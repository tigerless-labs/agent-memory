Steps to begin DJing as a hobby:

1. **Learn the basics** — beatmatching, mixing tracks, equipment operation via online tutorials and articles
2. **Invest in equipment** — DJ controller or turntables + mixing software; research options within budget
3. **Practice** — dedicate time at home, with friends, or at local events to develop skills
4. **Network** — connect with other DJs at events, online forums, collaborate on projects
5. **Share** — record and upload mixes online or perform at local events

Key point: DJing requires sustained practice but is rewarding as a hobby. Experimentation and personal style development are essential.
