Selected: Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite, 12.5 oz, 2.8 inches thickness. Good balance of comfort (2.8 inches) and weight (12.5 oz).

Alternatives considered:
- Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite: 11.8 oz, 2.5 inches (lighter but less comfort)
- Sea to Summit Ether Light XT: 13.9 oz, 3.5 inches (more comfort, slightly heavier)
- Big Agnes Insulated AXL: 9.8 oz, 2.5 inches (ultralight option)
- NEMO Equipment Astro: 10.5 oz, 2.5 inches (ultralight option)
