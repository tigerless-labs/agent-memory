Presented research on AI-powered adaptive learning systems and their impact on student learning outcomes at a research conference.

**Reception:**
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers
- Many appreciated the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities
- Developers reached out afterward, interested in learning more about the findings and how to apply them to their own work

**Key Discussion Point:**
An area of disagreement emerged around the role of teachers in AI-powered adaptive learning systems—some educators concerned these systems would replace teachers, others saw them as valuable support tools. The research emphasized that AI should augment rather than replace teaching.
