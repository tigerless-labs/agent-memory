Before the next bike ride, needs to:
1. Oil the bike chain - use appropriate chain lube for current conditions, clean chain first
2. Adjust the derailleur - check limit screws and alignment with cassette/chainrings

Also should check tire pressure, brake pads, and general bolts/quick releases before each ride.
