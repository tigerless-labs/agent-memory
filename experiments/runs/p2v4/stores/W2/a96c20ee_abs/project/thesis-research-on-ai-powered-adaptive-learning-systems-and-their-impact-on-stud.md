Explored the effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension.

**Key Findings:**
- AI-powered adaptive learning systems can be effective in improving student learning outcomes
- Implementation and integration into the classroom must be carefully considered
- Identified consistent challenges across case studies:
  - Teachers lack necessary skills and knowledge for effective use
  - Clear alignment with curriculum and learning goals is critical for adoption
- Systems have potential to promote equity and inclusion but risk algorithmic bias exacerbating inequities

**Recommendations:**
- Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support
- Developers should work closely with educators to ensure systems meet teacher and student needs
- Provide clear guidance on curriculum alignment
- Conduct ongoing evaluation and assessment of systems
- Address algorithmic bias concerns proactively

**Approach:**
Took a broader concept-focused approach using several specific platforms/systems as case studies to illustrate implementation challenges and opportunities.
