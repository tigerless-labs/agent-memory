- Local culture and traditions (Ethiopian Orthodox heritage, coffee ceremonies, markets in Addis Ababa)
- Religious festivals: Timkat (Jan 19), Meskel (Sept 27), Enkutatash/New Year (Sept 11), Fasika/Easter (April)
- Cuisine: Injera, Doro Wot, Tibs, Kitfo, Shiro, traditional Ethiopian coffee
- Historical sites: Lalibela rock-hewn churches, Axum, Gondar castles
- Part of broader interest in off-the-beaten-path African destinations (also mentioned: Namibia, Madagascar, Mozambique, Djibouti, Mauritania, Senegal)
