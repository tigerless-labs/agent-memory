**Trip Plan:**
- Route: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp via Tuolumne Meadows trailhead
- Duration: 3–4 days
- Distance: ~20 miles
- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Camp elevation: 10,300 feet

**Accommodations:**
- 4-person tent cabins with bunk beds and mattresses
- Canvas walls with solar-powered lighting
- Full-service meals included (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Modern restrooms and showers
- Camp store available

**Rationale:**
Chose over High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft gain) as more manageable introduction to high-elevation backpacking. User concerned about fitness level for extreme elevation and managing pack weight (40–50 lbs).
