Mather Campground: 327 sites with electric hookups at South Rim. Amenities: flush toilets, showers, laundry. Reserve at recreation.gov. Trailer Village RV Park: 84 sites with electric hookups, Wi-Fi, laundry, camp store. Reserve at grandcanyonlodges.com. Desert View Campground: 50 sites with electric hookups at South Rim, flush toilets, potable water. Reserve at recreation.gov. All fill quickly during peak season; reserve well in advance. Dispersed camping outside park boundaries available without hookups.
