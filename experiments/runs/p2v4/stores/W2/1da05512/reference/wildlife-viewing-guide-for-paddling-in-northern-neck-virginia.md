Common wildlife to expect: Bald eagles, ospreys, blue herons, egrets, waterfowl, river otters, muskrats, beavers, turtles, striped bass, largemouth bass, bluegill, catfish, deer, foxes, raccoons.

Best times for wildlife spotting: Early morning and late afternoon/early evening (when animals are most active). These times also have less wind and boat traffic for more pleasant paddling. Maintain safe distance from wildlife and avoid disturbing natural habitats.

Source: Wildlife observation guide for Hague, Virginia area paddling, 2023
