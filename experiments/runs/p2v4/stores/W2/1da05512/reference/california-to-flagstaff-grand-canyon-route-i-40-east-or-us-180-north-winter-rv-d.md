Route options: I-40 East (direct but less scenic) or US-180 North from I-40 (more scenic through San Francisco Peaks). Driving tips: leave early to avoid rush hour, avoid peak holiday travel days, use Waze/Google Maps for traffic updates, take regular breaks. Winter prep: winterize plumbing, ensure heating system works, check tires/consider snow tires or chains, pack winter gear and emergency kit, fill propane and fuel tanks, check weather before departure. High elevation brings freezing temps and snow. Agricultural checkpoints when entering Arizona—declare fruits/vegetables. Bring warm clothing, blankets, flashlights, first aid kit, shovel, ice scraper, sand/cat litter for traction.
