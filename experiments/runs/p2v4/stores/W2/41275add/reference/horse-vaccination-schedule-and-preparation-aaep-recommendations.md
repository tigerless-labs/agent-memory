**Core Vaccinations (annual):**
- Eastern and Western Encephalomyelitis (EEE/WEE) — typically spring
- Tetanus — annually (or every 5–7 years if robust immune response)
- West Nile Virus (WNV) — typically spring

**Risk-Based Vaccinations:**
- Influenza — annually or bi-annually for competing/traveling horses
- Rabies — annually or every 3 years depending on local regulations
- Strangles — annually or bi-annually for high-risk horses

**Foals/Young Horses:** Initial series at 6–8 weeks, boosters at 10–12 weeks, and 14–16 weeks. May need additional boosters through age 1–2.

**Pre-Vaccination Preparation:**
1. Schedule appointment well in advance
2. Confirm vaccination history with veterinarian
3. Clean/disinfect vaccination area
4. Separate horses if needed to reduce stress
5. Ensure horses are healthy (not showing illness)
6. Have medical history and vaccination records ready
7. Have horse haltered, groomed, and ready on appointment day
8. Be present during vaccination process
9. Follow post-vaccination instructions for any reactions

Consult veterinarian for customized schedule based on horse's age, health status, and local disease risks.
