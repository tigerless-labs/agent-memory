**National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP)** — Guidance on nutrition, health, management, and vaccination protocols for sheep producers.

**American Sheep Industry Association (ASI)** — Information on sheep health, vaccination guidelines, parasite management, and nutrition.

**University Extension Services** — Local extensions (Purdue, Cornell, Texas A&M, etc.) provide fact sheets on vaccinations, nutrition, and health management specific to regional conditions.

**Sheep 101** — Comprehensive online resource covering all aspects of sheep production including health, nutrition, and management.
