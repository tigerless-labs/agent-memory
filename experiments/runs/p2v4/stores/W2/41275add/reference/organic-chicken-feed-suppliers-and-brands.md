**Online Stores:**
- Amazon (including Amazon Elements brand)
- Tractor Supply Co.
- ChickensForBackyards (specializes in backyard chicken supplies)
- Meyer Hatchery (family-owned, also sells chicks and pullets)

**Feed Brands/Producers:**
- Organic Valley (farmer-owned cooperative)
- Blue Seal (certified organic and non-GMO options)
- Modesto Milling (family-owned, organic and non-GMO)
- Scratch and Peck Feeds (family-owned, certified organic, locally sourced ingredients)

**Tips:** Check local feed stores and co-ops for organic options. Look for USDA Organic, Non-GMO, and Certified Humane certifications. Transition gradually to new feed to prevent digestive upset.
