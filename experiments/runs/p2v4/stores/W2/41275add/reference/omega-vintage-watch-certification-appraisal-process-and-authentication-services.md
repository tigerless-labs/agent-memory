## Omega's Vintage Watch Certification Services

**What Omega provides:**
- Authentication: verify watch is genuine Omega, not replica
- Certificate of Origin: production date, model, serial number for provenance
- Condition Report: assessment highlighting restoration/repair needs
- Service and Restoration: skilled watchmakers service to original condition using original parts

**Certificate details:**
- Unique certificate number for verification
- Watch details: serial number, model, production date
- Signature of authorized Omega representative and Omega stamp

**Benefits:**
- Increased value: certified watches command higher prices
- Enhanced credibility for buyers
- Increased resale value with documentation
- Peace of mind on authenticity and condition

**Process:**
- Contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service
- Submit watch for examination
- Cost and timeline vary by model, age, condition
- Keep all documentation: certificates, service records, correspondence

## Currency/Banknote Appraisal Process

**Finding qualified appraiser:**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG)
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)

**Appraisal types:**
- Verbal: casual, informal assessment
- Written: more detailed formal report
- Formal appraisal report: comprehensive analysis with research

**Documentation received:**
- Appraisal report with description, authenticity opinion, estimated value
- Historical/provenance information
- Certificate of Authenticity (optional)

**Tips:**
- Choose reputable appraiser with currency expertise
- Provide accurate information about history and provenance
- Keep appraisal report safe for insurance/selling
- Consider multiple appraisals for accuracy
