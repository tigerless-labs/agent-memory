**Vaccination expenses:**
- CD-T vaccine (Clostridium perfringens type C & D, plus tetanus): $1.50–$3.00 per dose
- CDT with selenium: $2.50–$5.00 per dose  
- Pneumonia vaccines (Pasteurella, Mannheimia): $3.00–$6.00 per dose

**Feed expenses:**
- Hay (alfalfa or grass): $100–$200 per ton
- Grain (corn, oats): $150–$300 per ton
- Minerals and supplements: $20–$50 per month

Costs vary by region and market conditions. Veterinarian consultation recommended for flock-specific budgeting.
