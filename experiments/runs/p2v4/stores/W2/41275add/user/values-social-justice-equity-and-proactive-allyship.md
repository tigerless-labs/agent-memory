Values and interests around social justice and reconciliation:

- Deeply interested in understanding historical harms (colonialism, slavery) and their lasting impacts
- Believes in acknowledging personal biases and privilege as a foundation for change
- Values proactive calling-out of harmful behavior rather than waiting until harm is done
- Interested in practical, daily actions that contribute to social justice (education, amplifying marginalized voices, direct action, checking privilege)
- Sees personal reflection and continuous learning as essential to becoming a better ally
- Recognizes that change feels overwhelming but believes consistent individual actions have cumulative positive impact
- Values both individual and collective effort toward building a more inclusive, equitable world

Practical strategies valued:
- Self-education on history and marginalized communities
- Speaking up and using one's platform
- Supporting inclusive policies and organizations
- Being an active listener to underrepresented voices
- Volunteering and taking direct action
- Regular reflection on personal attitudes and behaviors
