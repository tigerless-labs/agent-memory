## Decision

Selling vintage collectibles online to reach wider audiences of collectors and enthusiasts. Selected approach: use multiple specialized platforms based on item type.

## Platforms by collectible type

**Watches (Omega Seamaster)**
- eBay (with Authenticity Guarantee)
- Chrono24 (watch-specialist marketplace)
- 1stdibs (luxury marketplace)
- The RealReal (consignment model)
- WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums
- Local watch dealers
- Auction houses: Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions

**Guitar (Fender Jazzmaster)**
- Reverb (music instrument specialist)
- eBay
- The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI forums
- Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange
- Facebook Marketplace

**Comics (Avengers #1)**
- ComicConnect (comic specialist)
- eBay
- Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, r/comicbooks
- Local comic book stores

**Action Figures (Batman)**
- eBay
- Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk forums
- ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room

**Electronics (Sony Walkman)**
- eBay
- Gearslutz, Resonance FM, vintage audio forums
- Facebook Marketplace

**Currency (Cuban peso note)**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG) appraiser
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
