Planning a future trip to Yellowstone National Park with family of five. Key interests include:

**Geysers & Hot Springs:**
- Old Faithful (must-see)
- Grand Geyser, Castle Geyser, Daisy Geyser in Upper Geyser Basin
- Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine formations)
- Norris Geyser Basin
- Boiling River (natural hot spring near Mammoth)

**Wildlife Viewing:**
- Bears (especially grizzly bears)
- Best areas: Lamar Valley, Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area
- Practice safe distance (100+ yards with binoculars)

**Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone:**
- Interested in geology
- Hiking trails: Brink of Lower Falls (3/4 mi), Seven Mile Hole (10 mi), South Rim Trail (6 mi)
- Scenic drives: Grand Loop Road

**Accommodations:**
- Canyon Lodge & Cabins is preferred option

**Other considerations:**
- Tips for keeping kids occupied during long RV drives discussed
