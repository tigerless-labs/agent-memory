## Reading Preferences

### Genres and Themes
- **Historical fiction** with strong female protagonists (especially WWII-era settings)
- **Mythology and folklore retellings** from diverse cultures — strong preference for non-Western cultures (African, Asian, Latin American)
- **Dystopian fiction with feminist themes** (e.g., *The Power* by Naomi Alderman)
- Character-driven narratives with emotional depth and complexity
- Women's perspectives, agency, and resilience during conflict
- Rich, lyrical prose with historical authenticity

- **Kristin Hannah** — *The Nightingale*
- **Madeline Miller** — *The Song of Achilles*
- **Kate Quinn** — *The Alice Network*
- **R.F. Kuang** — *The Poppy War* (Chinese mythology/history)
- **Roshani Chokshi** — *The Star-Touched Queen* (Indian mythology)
- **Naomi Alderman** — *The Power* (feminist dystopian)

### Format & Reading Habits
- **Strong preference for audiobooks** — actively seeks audiobook versions
- Reads in short sessions before bed
- Enjoys getting absorbed in emotional arcs and complex character relationships

### Specific Interests (from Jan 2023 conversation)
- Actively seeking audiobooks combining: strong female leads + historical setting + non-Western mythologies
- Particularly interested in African, Asian (Chinese, Japanese, Mongolian, Southeast Asian, Indian, Vietnamese), and Latin American mythology retellings
