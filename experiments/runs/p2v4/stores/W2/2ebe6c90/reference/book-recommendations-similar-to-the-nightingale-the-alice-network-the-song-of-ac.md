Recommendations for historical fiction with emotional depth and character development similar to The Nightingale:

**Top recommendations user showed interest in:**
- The Alice Network by Kate Quinn (WWI-era, dual narrative, female spies, highly praised)
- The Song of Achilles by Madeline Miller (retelling of Trojan War from Patroclus' perspective, lyrical prose)
- The Kitchen House by Kathleen Grissom (1800s American South plantation, character-driven, emotional)

**Other recommendations provided:**
- All the Light We Cannot See by Anthony Doerr (WWII, Pulitzer Prize)
- The Lost Girls of Paris by Pam Jenoff (WWII resistance network)
- The Women in the Castle by Jessica Shattuck (WWII aftermath)
- Those Who Save Us by Jenna Blum (WWII mother-daughter story)
- The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society by Mary Ann Shaffer (post-WWII)
- The Postmistress by Sarah Blake (WWII, multiple female perspectives)
- The Girl You Left Behind by Jojo Moyes (WWI France)
- The Invisible Bridge by Julie Orringer (WWII Hungarian family)
- We Were the Lucky Ones by Georgia Hunter (WWII Polish family)
- The Huntress by Kate Quinn (WWII, three women pursuing Nazi criminal)
- The Red Tent by Anita Diamant (biblical retelling)
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (fantasy, Chinese history/mythology inspired)
- The Gilded Wolves by Roshani Chokshi (historical fantasy, late 1800s Paris)
- The Ten Thousand Doors of January by Alix E. Harrow (historical fantasy, magical elements)
