Getting to Tortuguero from San Jose:

Option 1: Domestic flight from Tobias Bolaños International Airport (SYQ) to Tortuguero Airport (TTQ, ~1 hour), then boat transfer to lodge (1-2 hours).

Option 2: Shuttle bus San Jose to La Pavona (~4-5 hours), then boat to park (1-2 hours).

Option 3: Private transfer to La Pavona, then private boat.

Tips: Book in advance, pack light for boat transfer, check weather before traveling, many lodges offer transportation packages.

Wildlife in Tortuguero: howler monkeys, white-faced capuchin monkeys, spider monkeys, two-toed and three-toed sloths, toucans, quetzals, macaws, American and Morelet's crocodiles, green and spiny-tailed iguanas, various snakes (fer-de-lance, boa constrictor), jaguars (rare), caimans, 1,200+ butterfly species.

Best wildlife spotting: early morning and late afternoon, guided tours recommended, be patient, respect animals.
