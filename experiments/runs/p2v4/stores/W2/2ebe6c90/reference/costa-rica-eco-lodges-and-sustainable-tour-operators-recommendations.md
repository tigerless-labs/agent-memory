Recommended eco-lodges: Lapa Rios Ecolodge (Osa Peninsula), Monteverde Lodge (Monteverde), Arenal Observatory Lodge (Arenal Volcano), Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero National Park), El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula).

Sustainable tour operators: Costa Rica Expeditions, Osa Conservation, Tortuguero Canals Tours, Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve, Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours.

Look for certifications: CST (Certification for Sustainable Tourism), TIES (International Ecotourism Society), ICT (Costa Rican Tourism Board). Evaluate environmental policies, community involvement, conservation support.
