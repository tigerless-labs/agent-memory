Recommendations for bitter liqueurs to substitute for Campari in Negroni:\n\n- **Cynar**: Italian artichoke bitter. Vegetal, slightly sweet. Try 1/2 oz Cynar + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica.\n- **Aperol**: Italian bitter, sweeter and more orange-forward than Campari. Use 1 oz Aperol + 1 oz Carpano Antica.\n- **Luxardo Bitter**: Italian bitter, more intense and herbal. Try 1/2 oz Luxardo Bitter + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica.\n- **Suze**: French gentian-based bitter, crisp and earthy. Try 1 oz Suze + 1 oz Carpano Antica.\n- **Meletti**: Italian bitter, sweeter and citrus-forward. Use 1 oz Meletti + 1 oz Carpano Antica.\n\nMay require adjusting ratio of bitter to vermouth depending on liqueur intensity.
