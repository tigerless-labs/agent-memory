## Recipe\n- 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin (or preferred gin)\n- 0.5 oz Suze (or dry bitter like Luxardo Bitter)\n- 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth\n- Orange or lemon twist\n\n## Technique\n1. Fill mixing glass with ice\n2. Add gin, Suze, dry vermouth\n3. Stir 20-30 seconds\n4. Strain into chilled coupe glass\n5. Garnish with twist\n\n## Balancing notes\n- Use dry bitter liqueurs (Suze, Luxardo) not sweet\n- Hendrick's floral/herbal notes pair well with Suze's dry herbaceous character\n- Ratio flexibility: some prefer 2:1:1, others 3:1:1 gin:bitter:vermouth—experiment\n- Don't over-dilute; preserve the flavors\n- Great for warm weather, crisp refreshing option
