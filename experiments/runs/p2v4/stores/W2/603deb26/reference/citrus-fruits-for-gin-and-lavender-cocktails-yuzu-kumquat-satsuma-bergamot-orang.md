Recommended citrus pairings with gin and lavender:\n\n- **Orange**: Classic pairing with lavender; sweetness complements floral notes. Blood orange adds depth.\n- **Yuzu**: Japanese citrus (grapefruit-orange cross), tart and subtly sweet.\n- **Kumquat**: Small, sweet citrus. Use kumquat syrup or muddle fresh fruit.\n- **Satsuma**: Mandarin-type orange, sweet and tart, balances gin and lavender well.\n- **Bergamot**: Subtle citrus flavor adds complexity to gin and lavender.\n\n**Tips**: Use fresh citrus, start with small amounts (citrus can overpower), use peels/twists for added aroma.
