During peak campaign seasons, work hours increase by approximately 10 hours per week, resulting in 50-hour workweeks to accommodate additional workload. This demonstrates flexibility and commitment to meeting tight deadlines during high-volume periods. This ability should be highlighted in job applications as evidence of adaptability and dedication to project success.
