Decision: Use a hybrid approach for job applications.\n\n**Core version:** Create a master version of resume and LinkedIn profile highlighting overall skills, experience, and achievements to serve as foundation for all applications.\n\n**Tailored versions:** Create customized versions for each specific job application, tailoring to job description requirements.\n\n**Tailoring tactics:**\n- Use keywords from job description\n- Emphasize skills and experience aligned with job requirements\n- Use specific examples and quantified achievements\n- Adjust summary/objective statement to match role\n\n**Rationale:** Shows genuine interest, helps pass ATS systems, and increases chances of landing interviews while maintaining authenticity.\n\n**To mention in all versions:**\n- Certifications in Google Analytics and HubSpot Inbound Marketing\n- Experience with Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Google Analytics, Adobe Creative Cloud\n- Specific metrics for social media growth and engagement
