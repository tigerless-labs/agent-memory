Planned dinner party for 8-10 guests accommodating vegetarians and one gluten-free guest.

**Menu:**
- Appetizer: Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus
- Main: Vegetarian sushi rolls with grilled portobello mushrooms and carrot-ginger tempura bits, plus cucumber and avocado; lemon-ginger dipping sauce
- Side: Lemon-herb quinoa with toasted pine nuts and fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro, or basil)
- Dessert: Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available)

**Presentation details:**
- Garnish naan and quinoa with fresh herbs for color
- Garnish sushi with thinly sliced lemons or edible flowers (violas, pansies)

**Timing approach:**
- Prep as much as possible ahead
- Cook components in batches during event
- Serve courses 30-45 minutes apart
- Allow 30-60 minutes between main and dessert
- Stay relaxed; communicate with guests if running behind
