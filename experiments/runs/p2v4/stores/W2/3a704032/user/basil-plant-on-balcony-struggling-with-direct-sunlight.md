Balcony basil plant has been struggling with excessive direct sunlight. Using general-purpose potting mix. Considering moving to shadier spot (east- or west-facing, or using shade cloth). Discussed ideal basil soil: 2 parts peat moss/coco coir, 1 part perlite/vermiculite, 1 part compost/worm castings, 1 part general-purpose soil.
