Enjoys character-driven narratives with complex relationships and interconnected storylines.

**Graphic novels:** Drawn to sci-fi/fantasy adventures similar to *Saga* by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples — appreciates stunning art, complex characters, and moral ambiguity.

**Contemporary fiction:** Prefers novels with strong female protagonists, complex relationships, and identity exploration. Resonates with themes of public vs. private self, intersectionality (particularly women's experiences as immigrants, people of color), and complex portrayals of relationships between women. Enjoys narratives that explore how personal experiences shape identity and relationships.

**Examples enjoyed:** *The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo* by Taylor Jenkins Reid (started 2023-01-29; particularly appreciates character development, Cuban immigrant backstory, and interconnected storylines)

**Recommended graphic novels:** Monstress (Liu/Takeda), The Wicked + The Divine (Gillen/McKelvie), Black Hammer (Lemire/Ormston), The Umbrella Academy (Way/Bá), Descender (Lemire/Nguyen), The Goddamned (Aaron/Guéra), Y: The Last Man (Vaughan/Guerra)

**Recommended contemporary fiction:** The Devil Wears Prada, The Mothers, The Girls, The Interestings, Conversations with Friends, The Female Persuasion, The Last Romantics, The Age of Light
