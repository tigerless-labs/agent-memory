Made kimchi for the first time around May 14, 2023. First batch was a disaster:
- Added way too much gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) — made it far too spicy
- Added too much salt — made it overly salty

Rescue plan: rinse under cold water to remove excess salt, then rebalance flavors.

Key learning: careful measurement and tasting as you go are essential for balanced kimchi.
