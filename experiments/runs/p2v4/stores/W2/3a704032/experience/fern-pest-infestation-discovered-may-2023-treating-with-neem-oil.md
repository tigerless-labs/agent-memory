Fern has visible pest infestation—tiny moving dots on leaves and stems, likely spider mites or springtails. Treatment plan: neem oil spray per product instructions, plus increased misting 2-3 times per week with distilled/filtered water. Misting in morning to allow leaves to dry before nightfall. Avoided misting crown of plant to prevent rot. Isolated fern from other plants.
