Decided to use gochugaru (standard Korean chili flakes) for kimchi going forward, with careful measurement:

**Chili flakes (gochugaru):**
- Start with 1/4 to 1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables for mild heat
- Can adjust up to 1-2 tsp per 2 cups for hot, 2+ for extremely hot
- Measure by weight with digital scale when possible (more accurate than volume)
- Different brands vary in heat — start low, taste as you go

**Garlic & Ginger combo:**
- 2-3 cloves fresh garlic per 2 cups vegetables (minced or crushed)
- 1-2 inches fresh ginger per 2 cups vegetables (peeled, grated or sliced thin)
- Mix both with gochugaru, fish sauce, and gochujang for balanced flavor

**Process:**
- Taste continuously while making to catch imbalances early
- Rinse vegetables after salting to remove excess salt
- Monitor fermentation time — shorter ferment mellows heat/salt, longer intensifies flavor
