**Watering:** Check soil 1 inch deep; water when dry. Every 7–10 days in spring/summer, every 10–14 days in fall/winter.

**Misting:** 1–2 times weekly in average humidity; more often in dry climates. Mist in morning to prevent fungal issues. Avoid getting water on the crown.

**Temperature:** Ideal 68–72°F (20–22°C); acceptable 65–75°F (18–24°C). Avoid below 55°F or above 85°F. Keep away from drafts and vents.

**Fertilization:** Every 1–2 weeks during growing season (spring/summer); monthly during dormant season (fall/winter). Use balanced fertilizer (20-20-20). Half-strength to avoid root burn.

**Light:** Bright, indirect light. Avoid direct sun (leaf scorch). East or west-facing windows ideal.

**Humidity:** Thrives with 40–50% humidity. Can place pot on tray with water and pebbles.

**General:** Well-draining potting mix (tropical/orchid mix works). Repot if outgrown or nutrient deficient. Remove dead leaves/flowers. Check for pests (spider mites, mealybugs, scale).
