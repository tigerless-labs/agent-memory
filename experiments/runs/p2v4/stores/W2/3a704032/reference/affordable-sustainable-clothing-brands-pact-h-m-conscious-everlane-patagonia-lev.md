# Affordable Sustainable Clothing Brands

Recommended as meeting both sustainability/ethical production AND affordability criteria:

- **Pact** — Organic cotton for men, women, children; fair labor practices
- **H&M Conscious** — Sustainable collection by H&M using organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel
- **Everlane** — Transparent sustainable brand; minimalist aesthetic; detailed material sourcing information; good men's selection (t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, shoes)
- **Patagonia** — Outdoor clothing; mid-range to affordable options; organic cotton, recycled polyester, hemp
- **Levi's** — Denim brand with affordable sustainable line using recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton

**Note:** Everlane specifically highlighted for men's clothing with focus on timeless, classic designs made to last.
