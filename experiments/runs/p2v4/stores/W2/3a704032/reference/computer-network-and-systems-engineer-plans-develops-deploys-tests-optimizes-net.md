Computer Network and Systems Engineer role focuses on planning, developing, deploying, testing and optimizing network and system services. Key responsibilities: configuration management; overall operational readiness of network systems; handling multiple operating systems and diverse configurations; troubleshooting and fault-finding for network problems. Required experience: deep understanding of networking protocols and system architecture; experience designing and implementing scalable, secure, highly available infrastructure; deployment and optimization experience; multi-OS support including Windows, Linux and others; ability to minimize downtime and impact on users through rapid problem identification.
