**During blooming season (spring/summer):**
- Deadhead wilted flowers anytime to encourage new blooms
- Light pruning to shape, promote air circulation, remove weak/crossing growth
- Don't do extensive pruning while actively blooming

**After blooming season:**
- More extensive pruning to rejuvenate plant or control size
- Prune during dormant season (winter/early spring) for best results

**Pruning technique:**
- Make clean cuts at 45-degree angle
- Remove weak or diseased wood
- Can prune 1 month after previous pruning, monitor for stress

Rose bush pruned in April 2023 was producing new buds by May 2023.
