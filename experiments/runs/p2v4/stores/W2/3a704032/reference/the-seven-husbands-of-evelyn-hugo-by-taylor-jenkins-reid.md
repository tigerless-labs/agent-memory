*The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo* by Taylor Jenkins Reid

A contemporary fiction novel exploring identity, celebrity, fame, and complex relationships between women.

**Plot:** Follows Evelyn Hugo, a Cuban immigrant who became a famous Hollywood actress, as she tells her life story to journalist Monique through interviews. The narrative weaves together Evelyn's glamorous public persona with her vulnerable private self, revealing mysteries surrounding her seven marriages and relationships.

**Key themes:** 
- Identity (public vs. private self; intersectionality)
- Immigration and cultural identity
- Relationships between women (both supportive and competitive)
- Power and privilege in celebrity culture
- Gender and patriarchal systems in Hollywood

**Structure:** Dual narrators (Evelyn and Monique); Monique serves as active participant uncovering secrets, not passive listener

**What makes it compelling:** Rich character development, interconnected storylines, nuanced exploration of how personal experiences shape identity, strong female relationships with depth and complexity
