**Tools for facilitating student reflection and discussion in digital literacy classes:**\n\n- **Padlet** — collaborative platform for students to share thoughts and ideas in a virtual space\n- **Nearpod** — interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions to encourage participation\n- **Flipgrid** — video discussion platform where students respond with short video responses\n- **Google Jamboard** — virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual thinking and idea-sharing\n\n**Discussion methods to use with these tools:**\n- Ask open-ended questions (not yes/no)\n- Paraphrase and summarize student responses\n- Encourage active listening among peers\n- Use visual aids and graphic organizers
