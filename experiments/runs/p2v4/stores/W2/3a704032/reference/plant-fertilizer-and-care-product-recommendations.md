Fertilizer recommendations by plant type:

Snake Plants & Succulents: Miracle-Gro Cactus/Palm/Citrus Food (10-20-10), Espoma Organic Cactus (5-2-6), Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10)

Orchids: Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10), Espoma Organic Orchid (15-30-15), Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20)

General Indoor: Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20), Espoma Organic Indoor (2-2-2), Schultz All Purpose (20-20-20)

Humidifiers: Levoit LV600HH (6L, cool/warm, humidistat), Honeywell HUL520W (1L compact), Deneve Minimal Aroma (2.5L ultrasonic), Crane Drop (1L quiet)

Potting mix for Spider Plants: Miracle-Gro Indoor, Black Kow All Purpose, E.B. Stone Organics, Pro-Mix All Purpose
