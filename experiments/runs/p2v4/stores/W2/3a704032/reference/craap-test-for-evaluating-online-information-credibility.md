**CRAAP Test** — framework for evaluating credibility and reliability of online sources:\n- **C**urrency: Is the information current/up-to-date?\n- **R**elevance: Is it relevant to your research question?\n- **A**uthority: Who is the author? What are their credentials?\n- **A**ccuracy: Is the information supported by evidence?\n- **P**urpose: Why does the source exist? What is its intent?\n\nUseful for teaching 6th graders to critically evaluate online information sources.
