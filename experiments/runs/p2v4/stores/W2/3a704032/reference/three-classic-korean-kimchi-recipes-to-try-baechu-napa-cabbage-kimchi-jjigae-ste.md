Popular kimchi recipes to explore:

1. **Baechu Kimchi** — traditional recipe using napa cabbage, gochugaru, garlic, ginger, and fish sauce. This is the most common starting point.

2. **Kimchi Jjigae** — spicy fermented kimchi stew made with gochugaru, kimchi, pork, tofu, and vegetables. Warm comfort food variant.

3. **Danmuji Kimchi** — radish kimchi using daikon radish instead of cabbage, gochugaru, garlic, and ginger. Offers a different texture/flavor profile.

Key tip: Balance of sweet, sour, salty, and umami flavors is crucial in all versions. Experiment with fermentation time to adjust final flavor intensity.
