The user finds extensive descriptive passages about environments (like the moors in Wuthering Heights) to be distracting from plot and characters. They acknowledge that such descriptions serve literary purposes (atmosphere, symbolism, thematic depth) but personally prefer:

- Character development and plot movement over atmospheric descriptions
- Settings woven organically into narrative rather than listed in detail
- Narrative efficiency over poetic immersion through detailed environmental descriptions

This complements their existing interest in character-driven narratives. When recommending books or discussing literature, favor character-focused works or those that integrate setting subtly into the story.
