Strategy: Get professional appraisals for each item (prioritize necklace), then shop for specialty insurance.\n\nRecommended Insurance Companies: Jewelers Mutual (jewelry/heirlooms), Chubb (high-value items), AXA ART (antiques/collectibles), Hagerty (collectibles), State Farm (specialty department), Allstate (Valuable Items), Liberty Mutual (Valuable Possessions).\n\nAppraiser Selection: Look for NAJA/AGTA/ASA certification (jewelry) or AAA/ISA/NACA (antiques). Prefer independent professionals with experience in vintage/antique items.\n\nAppraisal factors: material type/quality, craftsmanship, condition, rarity, market demand, comparable sales. Documentation needed: proof of ownership, existing appraisals, repair records, photos, provenance.
