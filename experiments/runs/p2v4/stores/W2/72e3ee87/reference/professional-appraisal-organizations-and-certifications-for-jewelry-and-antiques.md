Jewelry Appraisers: National Association of Jewelry Appraisers (NAJA), American Gem Trade Association (AGTA), American Society of Appraisers (ASA).\n\nAntique & Collectible Appraisers: Appraisers Association of America (AAA), International Society of Appraisers (ISA), National Association of Collectible Appraisers (NACA).\n\nWhen selecting appraisers, prioritize certified members of these organizations. For jewelry (especially diamonds), prefer NAJA/AGTA/ASA. For antiques and collectibles, prefer AAA/ISA/NACA. Always verify certification and ask for references.
