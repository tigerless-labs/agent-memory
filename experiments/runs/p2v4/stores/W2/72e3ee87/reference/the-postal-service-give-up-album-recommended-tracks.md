**Band:** The Postal Service — collaboration between Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

**Album:** Give Up (2003)

**Standout tracks recommended:**
- "Such Great Heights" — dreamy, synth-driven anthem about new love; main melody on acoustic guitar
- "The District Sleeps Alone Tonight" — melancholy, introspective; about disconnection and insomnia
- "Clark Gable" — quirky, upbeat; charming lyrics about romantic pursuit
- "We Will Become Silhouettes" — somber, reflective; lyrics about nuclear apocalypse aftermath

**Other notable tracks:**
- "Recycled Air" — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar riff
- "Sleeping In" — vocal layering and harmonies, weaving vocal lines

**Distinctive features:** Collaboration blending indie rock and electronic; incorporation of acoustic instruments alongside synthesizers and electronic beats; vocal layering and harmonies; story-driven lyrics.
