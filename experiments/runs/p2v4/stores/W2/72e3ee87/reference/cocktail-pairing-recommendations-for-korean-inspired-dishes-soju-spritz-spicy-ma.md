## Drinks that pair well with Korean-inspired dishes (Bibimbap, kimchi tacos):\n\n### For Bibimbap:\n- **Soju Spritz**: Soju, lime juice, ginger beer — crisp and dry\n- **Cucumber-Lime Refresher**: Cucumber Gimlet variation with ginger liqueur\n- **Korean Iced Tea**: Black tea, sugar, citrus\n\n### For Kimchi Tacos:\n- **Spicy Mango Mule**: Mango puree, ginger beer, lime juice, chili flakes\n- **Ginger-Grapefruit Collins**: Ginger liqueur, grapefruit juice, soda water\n- **Cucumber-Kiwi Cooler**: Cucumber, kiwi puree, lime juice, sparkling water\n\n### General principle:\nWhen pairing with spicy dishes, look for drinks with sweet or creamy elements to balance heat. For savory dishes, use bright citrusy flavors to cut through richness.
