Recommendations for musical theater enthusiast who enjoys irreverent comedy musicals: Avenue Q, The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee, Something Rotten, Hamilton, The Producers, Urinetown, Be More Chill, Dear Evan Hansen, The Band's Visit, Hadestown. Avenue Q and Something Rotten share similar irreverent humor. Hadestown is a folk-opera retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice.
