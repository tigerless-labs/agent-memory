Recommended frozen brine shrimp brands for aquarium fish:

1. San Francisco Bay Brand - harvested from Great Salt Lake, offers plain, garlic, and omega-3 varieties
2. Hikari - offers plain, spirulina, and krill varieties
3. Argent - sustainable sourcing, offers plain, omega-3, and vitamin varieties
4. Omega One - specializes in nutrient-rich foods, high in omega-3 fatty acids

Selection criteria: sustainable sourcing, rich in nutrients and omega-3, no added preservatives, airtight packaging for freshness.

User's fish show strong preference for brine shrimp over other frozen foods.
