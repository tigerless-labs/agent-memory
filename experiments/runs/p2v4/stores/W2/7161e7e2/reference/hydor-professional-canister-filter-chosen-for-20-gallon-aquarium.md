Selected for 20-gallon tank upgrade.

Hydor Professional Canister Filter (150 model) - suitable for 20-40 gallon tanks, known for quiet operation with silent pump and noise-reducing system. Modular design for easy maintenance with built-in primer. Price: 150-200 dollars.

Compared to: Eheim Classic 2211 (100-150 dollars, quiet with some humming), Fluval 206 (100-150 dollars, slightly louder).

Biological media options: ceramic rings/noodles (clean 2-3 months, replace 6-12 months), bio-balls (similar), sintered glass (clean 3-6 months, replace 12-24 months).

Flow rate target: 100-200 GPH for 20-gallon tank.
