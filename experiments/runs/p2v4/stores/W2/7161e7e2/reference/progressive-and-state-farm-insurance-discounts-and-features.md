Progressive discounts: Snapshot usage-based rewards (up to 30%), accident forgiveness, good student discount, continuous insurance, online/paperless discounts. Safety features: advanced features discount (lane departure, blind spot, forward collision), vehicle safety rating discount. Coverage: loan/lease gap, custom equipment, disappearing deductible, deductible savings bank. Other: multi-car, bundle, roadside assistance, Name Your Price tool, price match. State Farm: Drive Safe & Save app-based program, Steer Clear (under 25 drivers), good student discount, defensive driving discount, vehicle safety features discounts, multi-car discount, bundling, ride-share insurance, local agents, efficient claims.
