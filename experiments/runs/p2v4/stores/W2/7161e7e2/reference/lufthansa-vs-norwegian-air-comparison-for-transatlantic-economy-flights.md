## Lufthansa Strengths
- Service quality: friendly, attentive cabin crew, personalized service
- Economy seat pitch: 31-32 inches (more spacious than Norwegian)
- Economy seat width: 17.5 inches
- Complimentary meal service on most flights with choice of meals
- Complimentary beverages including beer and wine
- In-flight entertainment: wide selection of movies, TV shows, music, games
- Checked baggage: 1 free bag (up to 23 kg) included in economy fare
- Adjustable headrests and good recline
- Strong frequent flyer program (Miles & More)
- Hub: Frankfurt Airport (FRA) - modern and efficient
- Price range: ~$850 return (New York to Frankfurt, July/August)

## Norwegian Air Weaknesses
- Financial instability: facing competitive and fuel cost pressures
- No complimentary meals (buy-on-board only)
- Economy seat pitch: 29-30 inches (cramped compared to Lufthansa)
- Economy seat width: 17 inches
- No adjustable headrests, limited recline
- Limited in-flight entertainment
- Charged checked baggage: $20-$30 per bag
- Hub: Oslo Airport (OSL)
- Risk factors: potential flight cancellations or schedule changes if financial situation worsens, bankruptcy risk

## Other Airlines Researched
- United Airlines: $820 return, non-stop from EWR to FRA
- TAP Air Portugal: $730 return, layover in Lisbon
- Swiss International Air Lines: $790 return, layover in Zurich
- Austrian Airlines: $810 return, layover in Vienna
- WOW Air: $620 return, layover in Reykjavik (budget option)
