Feeds fish twice daily with a varied diet:
- **Staple:** Tetra pellet food
- **Preferred treat:** Frozen brine shrimp (fish go crazy for it)
- **Secondary treat:** Frozen bloodworms (fish are more finicky about these)

Varies diet intentionally to provide balanced nutrition including proteins and omega-3 fatty acids. Fish show strong preference for brine shrimp over bloodworms.
