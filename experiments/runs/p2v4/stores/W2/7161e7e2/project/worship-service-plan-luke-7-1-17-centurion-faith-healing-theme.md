Service Structure:

Opening: 'Great Are You Lord' (All Sons & Daughters) — establishes God's sovereignty and power

Call to Worship & Prayer — invite hearts open, seeking healing and faith

Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17 (centurion and servant healing)

Sermon: Focus on faith, healing power of God, and inclusivity across all people

Response Hymn: 'O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing' (Charles Wesley) — gratitude for salvation and healing power

Congregation Testimony: Personal healing experience through faith

Faith Hymn: 'Amazing Grace' (John Newton) — forgiveness and healing through faith

Healing Prayer Time: Pastoral prayer for physical, emotional, and spiritual healing needs

Closing: 'Healer' (Kari Jobe) — God's restorative power and healing grace

Benediction: Reminder of God's presence and power to heal

Theological themes: Centurion's faith without proximity, healing through atonement (Isaiah 53:5), healing available to all regardless of status
