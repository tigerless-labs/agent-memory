Planning to create book content on Instagram:

**Post Strategy:**
- Title: 'Quarterly Faves & Wants'—asking followers for book recommendations of favorite books from the quarter
- Using hashtag: #bookrecs (12M+ uses, preferred over #bookrecommendations at 6M uses)

**Content Formats:**
- Instagram Stories: behind-the-scenes content showing #BookClubChat prep, thoughts during/after weekly Twitter chats, Q&A sessions
- IGTV: in-depth book reviews, author interviews, discussion videos
- Transitioning from using same hashtags for all posts to diversifying with relevant niche hashtags per post

**Engagement:**
- Planning Instagram Story poll asking followers which content type they prefer: behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, or book reviews
- Will use poll results to inform future content strategy

**Context:**
- Actively participates in weekly #BookClubChat on Twitter
- Reading interests align with historical fiction, mythology retellings, character-driven narratives
