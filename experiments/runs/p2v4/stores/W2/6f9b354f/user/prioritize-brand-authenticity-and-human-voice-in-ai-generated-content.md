Concerned about maintaining brand authenticity when using AI-generated content. Prefers AI-generated content to sound like it comes from the company and its team, not a robot. Approach: use AI as a starting point and research tool, then humanize and edit content with personal touches, anecdotes, and emotional connections before publishing. Requires human review and curation of all AI-generated content.
