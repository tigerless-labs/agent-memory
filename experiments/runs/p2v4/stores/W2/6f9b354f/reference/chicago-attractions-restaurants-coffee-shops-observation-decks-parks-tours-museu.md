## Coffee Shops (near Hilton downtown Chicago)
- **Intelligentsia Coffee**: 2-minute walk, expertly brewed coffee, minimalist vibe (53 E Randolph St)
- **Starbucks Reserve Bar**: 3-minute walk (646 S Michigan Ave)
- **La Colombe Coffee Roasters**: 5-minute walk, cozy atmosphere (1552 N Wells St)
- **Peet's Coffee**: 5-minute walk, artisanal beans (444 S LaSalle St)
- **The Coffee Studio**: Local independent, 7-minute walk (56 E Randolph St)

## Observation Decks & Viewpoints
- **Willis Tower (Sears Tower) Skydeck**: 103rd floor, stunning views, 10-minute walk (233 S Wacker Dr)
- **Millennium Park's Lurie Garden**: Great skyline views, especially at night (201 E Randolph St)
- **Navy Pier's Polk Bros Park**: Centennial Wheel for panoramic views (600 E Grand Ave)
- **360 CHICAGO Observation Deck**: 94th floor of John Hancock Center, 10-minute ride (875 N Michigan Ave)
- **Chicago Riverwalk**: 5-minute walk, evening lighting

## Parks & Running Routes
- **The Lakefront Trail**: 18.5-mile path along Lake Michigan, stunning views
- **Millennium Park**: 1.5-mile perimeter loop, Cloud Gate sculpture (The Bean), Jay Pritzker Pavilion
- **Grant Park**: Few blocks from Hilton, 1.5-mile loop
- **Maggie Daley Park**: 10-minute walk, 1.5-mile path, climbing wall, playgrounds
- **Chicago Riverwalk**: Scenic running/walking

## Tours
- **Chicago Architecture Riverwalk Tour**: 90-minute guided walk covering iconic architecture and history
- **Chicago's First Lady Cruises**: 90-minute architectural cruise along Chicago River (highly recommended)
- **Shoreline Sightseeing**: Various cruise options (30 min Lake Michigan, 2-hour architectural)
- **Chicago's Little Lady Cruises**: 1-hour cruise along Chicago River
- **Chicago Food Tours**: Guided walks through neighborhoods like Loop, Wicker Park, Lincoln Park
- **Chicago Gangster Tour**: 2-hour tour of gangster history and sites

## Museums & Galleries
- **The Art Institute of Chicago**: Top art museum, Impressionist/Post-Impressionist/American art, 10-minute walk (111 S Michigan Ave)
- **Field Museum**: Sue the T. rex, Egyptian artifacts, world cultures, 15-minute walk (1400 S Lake Shore Dr)
- **Museum of Contemporary Art**: Extensive modern/contemporary collection, 15-minute walk (220 E Chicago Ave)
- **The Richard H. Driehaus Museum**: Victorian mansion with late 19th-early 20th century art/design, 10-minute walk (40 E Erie St)
- **The Arts Club of Chicago**: Private club with rotating exhibitions (201 E Ontario St)
- **Carl Hammer Gallery**: Contemporary art by local/national/international artists (740 N Wells St)

## Restaurants
- **The Berghoff**: Classic since 1887, German-inspired, famous root beer, 5-minute walk (17 W Adams St)
- **The Gage**: Contemporary American, Millennium Park views, 10-minute walk (24 S Michigan Ave)
- **Girl & the Goat**: Top Chef winner Stephanie Izard, Mediterranean-inspired (809 W Randolph St)
- **Blackbird**: Modern American, seasonal Midwestern ingredients (619 W Randolph St)
- **Portillo's**: Chicago-style hot dogs and Italian beef (520 W Taylor St)
- **The Purple Pig**: Mediterranean small plates (500 N Michigan Ave)
- **Au Chavel**: Trendy diner, burgers and brunch (809 W Randolph St)
