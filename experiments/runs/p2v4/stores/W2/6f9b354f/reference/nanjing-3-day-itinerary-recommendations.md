Summer 3-day itinerary for couples:
- Day 1: Niushou Mountain (Buddhist park with temples, serene summer atmosphere), Qinhuai River (romantic boat rides, nighttime lighting)
- Day 2: Nanjing City Wall (ancient fortification, city views), Purple Mountain (Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Xuanwu Lake, botanical gardens, hiking)
- Day 3: Fuzimiao/Confucius Temple (historic district, temples, nightlife), Xuanwu Lake (tranquil walks, sculptures, playgrounds)

Winter 3-day itinerary for couples:
- Day 1: Niushou Mountain (serene, refreshing hike), Purple Mountain (snow-covered landscape, quiet atmosphere, Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum)
- Day 2: Nanjing City Wall (ancient military history), Confucius Temple Area (peaceful, romantic atmosphere, local restaurants)
- Day 3: Ming Palace Ruins (mysterious winter quality), Presidential Palace (historic residence, gardens, modern history)

Niushou Mountain appears in both itineraries as key attraction for spiritual/cultural experience.
