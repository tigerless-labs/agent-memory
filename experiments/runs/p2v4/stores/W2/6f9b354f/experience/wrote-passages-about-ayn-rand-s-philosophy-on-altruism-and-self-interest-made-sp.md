Worked through multiple iterations of writing about Ayn Rand's challenge to conventional altruism. She argues people must prioritize their own interests to survive since nature doesn't guarantee survival. Rand believes complete selflessness harms oneself and can be used to justify suppression of individual rights by dictators.

**English corrections made:**
- 'somewaht' → 'somewhat'
- 'Ayn Land' → 'Ayn Rand' (philosopher's name)

Note: The correct form is 'Ayn Rand believed that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests in order to live, which some might consider a somewhat selfish way of thinking.'
