# Building Contract Repudiation Content Strategy

## Project Overview
Creating a comprehensive content resource on building contract repudiation targeting multiple personas: developers, homeowners, builders, and contractors.

## Content Framework
Developed 15 article topics across 4 personas:

### Developers (3 articles)
- Understanding the Repudiation Process in Building Contracts
- How to Protect Your Business from Repudiating a Building Contract  
- The Risks and Consequences of Repudiating a Building Contract

### Homeowners (3 articles)
- When Can You Repudiate a Building Contract?
- The Legal Consequences of Repudiating a Building Contract
- Steps to Take if Your Builder Repudiates the Building Contract

### Builders (3 articles)
- How to Avoid Repudiating a Building Contract
- The Cost of Repudiating a Building Contract for Builders
- How to Respond if a Homeowner Repudiates the Building Contract

### Contractors (3 articles)
- Understanding the Consequences of Repudiating a Building Contract
- The Importance of Complying with Building Contract Obligations
- How to Protect Your Business from Repudiating a Building Contract

### General/All Personas (3 articles)
- The Differences Between Breach and Repudiation in Building Contracts
- The Importance of Seeking Legal Advice in Building Contracts
- How to Draft a Building Contract to Minimize Repudiation Risk
