Creating a home office nook in the spare bedroom. Design includes:
- Floating desk in middle of room
- Built-in shelving for storage
- Minimalist aesthetic with natural wood accents
- Lighting: pendant light above desk + table lamps for task lighting, specifically a minimalist wooden desk lamp with simple shade
- Greenery: considering small herb garden or vase with fresh flowers (low-light plants like ZZ Plant or Snake Plant)
- Natural light + layered lighting approach
- Color temperature preference: warm white (2700K-3000K)
