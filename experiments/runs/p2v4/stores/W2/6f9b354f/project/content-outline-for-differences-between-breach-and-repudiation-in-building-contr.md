## Key Distinctions to Cover
1. Breach is partial or temporary non-performance
2. Repudiation is showing intent to no longer be bound by contract or intent to perform in substantially inconsistent way
3. Test for repudiation is objective - based on conduct demonstrating clear intention to walk away
4. Repudiation requires compelling evidence vs mere breach
