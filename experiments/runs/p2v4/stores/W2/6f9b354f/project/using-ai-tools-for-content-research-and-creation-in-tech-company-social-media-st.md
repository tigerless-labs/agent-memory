**Decision**: Implement AI-powered research and content creation tools to improve company's social media strategy and online presence in tech industry.

**Tools chosen for exploration:**
- **BuzzSumo**: For topic suggestion, trending content analysis, and keyword insights
- **Ahrefs**: For content explorer, keyword research, competitor analysis, and topic ideation
- **Answer Engine**: For AI-powered Q&A insights to uncover audience questions and interests
- **Quora**: For community-driven Q&A insights and content inspiration

**Key concerns addressed:**
- Ensuring AI-generated content maintains brand authenticity and sounds human, not robotic
- Quality control and human review of all AI-generated content before publishing

**Implementation approach:**
1. Define brand voice and tone first
2. Train AI tools with existing company content
3. Use AI as research and ideation tool, not just content generation
4. Always review and humanize AI-generated output
5. Blend AI-generated content with human-created content

**Next steps:**
- Establish clear brand voice and tone guidelines
- Experiment with the selected tools
- Monitor content performance and adjust strategy
