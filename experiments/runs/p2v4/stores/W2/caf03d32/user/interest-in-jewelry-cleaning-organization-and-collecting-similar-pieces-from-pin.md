**Jewelry interests:**
- Active in organizing and maintaining a jewelry collection
- Uses proper cleaning methods: soft cloths, soap and water, specialized solutions for different materials
- Prefers organized storage with dividers and compartments
- Interested in finding similar pieces to items seen on Pinterest
- Values both new finds and sentimental/heirloom pieces

**Preferred sources for jewelry shopping:**
- Pinterest (for inspiration and discovery)
- Etsy (handmade/vintage)
- Specialty brands: BaubleBar, Mejuri, Aurate, Catbird
- Also checks Amazon, ASOS, Net-a-Porter
- Open to local jewelry stores for custom work

**Jewelry care habits:**
- Regular cleaning (every 1-2 months ideally)
- Stores jewelry in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- Keeps valuable pieces in secure storage
