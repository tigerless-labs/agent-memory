Interested in trout fishing in rivers. Prefers fishing without strike indicators to develop sensitivity to takes. Interested in streamer fishing, particularly Woolly Bugger and Muddler Minnow. Plans to start with Woolly Bugger, relying on its marabou tail for detecting takes. Will vary retrieves to imitate fleeing baitfish and fish at various depths in river structure (boulders, weed beds, drop-offs).
