Netflix music documentaries similar to Taylor Swift's 'Miss Americana':

- **Homecoming: A Film by Beyoncé** (2019) — Concert film and documentary about preparation for 2018 Coachella performance
- **Quincy** (2018) — Life and career of Quincy Jones; features interviews with Frank Sinatra, Michael Jackson, and others
- **Travis Scott: Look Mom I Can Fly** (2019) — Travis Scott's rise to fame, Astroworld tour, and personal life
- **Gaga: Five Foot Two** (2017) — Lady Gaga's life, music, struggles with chronic pain, creation of 'Joanne' album
- **The Black Godfather** (2019) — Music executive Clarence Avant's life and career
- **The Apollo** (2019) — History of Apollo Theater in Harlem; features Aretha Franklin, James Brown, and others
- **The Remix: Hip Hop x Fashion** (2019) — Intersection of hip-hop and fashion; features Kanye West, Pharrell Williams
- **I'll Sleep When I'm Dead** (2016) — DJ Steve Aoki's life and career
- **What Happened, Miss Simone?** (2015) — Nina Simone's life and career
- **The Art of Rap** (2012) — History and evolution of hip-hop; features Ice-T, Dr. Dre, Eminem
