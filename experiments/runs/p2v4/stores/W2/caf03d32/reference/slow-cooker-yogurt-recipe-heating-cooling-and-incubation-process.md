**Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 1/2 gallon milk (whole, low-fat, or nonfat; or vegan alternatives like soy, coconut, almond, oat)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures (as starter)
- Optional: sweetener (honey, maple syrup, sugar), flavorings (vanilla, cinnamon, fruit), probiotic powder

**Instructions:**

1. **Heat milk:** Pour milk into slow cooker, heat on low for 2-3 hours until it reaches 180-185°F (82-85°C). This kills bacteria and prepares environment for yogurt cultures.

2. **Cool milk:** Turn off cooker and let milk cool to 110-120°F (43-49°C) for 1-2 hours.

3. **Add starter culture:** Add yogurt with live cultures, whisk gently to combine.

4. **Incubate:** Cover slow cooker and wrap with towel/blanket for insulation. Incubate 8-12 hours or overnight. Longer incubation = thicker and tangier yogurt.

5. **Chill and store:** Refrigerate yogurt to chill, then transfer to airtight containers.

**Tips:**
- Temperature monitoring with a thermometer is highly recommended (more precise than time alone)
- For thicker yogurt, use less liquid or strain through cheesecloth (Greek yogurt style)
- Can use unflavored gelatin or pectin to thicken
- Add flavorings after cooling, not during incubation
- Use vegan yogurt starter or probiotic powder for plant-based versions
