Recommended YouTube channels for beginner guitar learners:

- **Justin Guitar** (Justin Sandercoe) — Encouraging approach, covers basics to advanced techniques, free online beginner course
- **Marty Music** (Marty Schwartz) — Learn popular songs quickly, breaks down complex songs into simple lessons; '5-Minute Guitar Lessons' playlist
- **GuitarLessons365** (Bill) — Comprehensive beginner's course, focus on acoustic guitar, well-structured
- **GuitarJamz** (Martyn) — Learn popular songs and riffs, fun engaging style; 'Beginner Guitar Lessons' playlist
- **Andy Guitar** (Andy Crowley) — Good for electric guitar, engaging teaching style; 'Guitar for Absolute Beginners' series

Tips: Start with acoustic guitar, practice regularly (10-15 min daily), use a metronome, find a practice buddy.
