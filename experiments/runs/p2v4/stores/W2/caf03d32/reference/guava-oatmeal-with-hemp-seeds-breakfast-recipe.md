High-protein, high-fiber breakfast formula:\n\n**Base (1 serving):**\n- 1/2 cup rolled oats\n- 1/4 cup diced guava (ratio: 1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal)\n- 1–2 tbsp hemp seeds (start with 1 tbsp if new)\n- 1/2 cup almond milk\n- 1 tbsp chia seeds\n- Honey/maple syrup optional\n\n**Nutritional breakdown:**\n- Oats (1 cup cooked): 6g protein, 4g fiber\n- Guava (1/2 cup): 2g protein, 4g fiber\n- Hemp seeds (1–2 tbsp): 5–10g protein, 1–2g omega-3s\n- Chia seeds (1 tbsp): 2g protein, 4g fiber\n\n**Toppings:** sliced banana, walnuts/almonds/pecans, toasted coconut flakes, cinnamon\n\n**Tips:**\n- Toast nuts to enhance flavor\n- Grind hemp seeds if texture is dense; mix with liquid first\n- Store hemp seeds in fridge/freezer (prone to rancidity)\n- Use fresh guava for best flavor; frozen/canned acceptable\n- Experiment with coconut milk instead of almond milk for variety
