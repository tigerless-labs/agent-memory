**Breakfast Ideas:**\n- Scrambled eggs with dehydrated veggies and sausage\n- Overnight oats with nuts and dried fruits\n- Campfire skillet with potatoes, bell peppers, onions\n\n**Lunch Ideas:**\n- Sandwiches with dehydrated meats and cheeses\n- Trail mix with nuts, seeds, dried fruits\n- Campfire-roasted vegetables with hummus or guacamole\n\n**Dinner Ideas:**\n- One-pot pasta with dehydrated sauce and veggies\n- Grilled meats (steak, chicken, fish) with roasted vegetables\n- Lentil or chili soup with crusty bread\n\n**Snacks:**\n- Trail energy balls (oats, nuts, dried fruits)\n- Jerky with cheese and crackers\n- Campfire-roasted marshmallows or s'mores\n\n**Cooking Tips:**\n- Pack non-perishable and dehydrated/freeze-dried ingredients\n- Cook in bulk to save time\n- Store food in airtight containers, secure location to avoid wildlife\n- Check campsite regulations for food storage and cooking rules
