Woolly Bugger: versatile streamer that imitates baitfish, leeches, or crawdad. Effective in moderate to fast currents with sink-tip line or weighted leader. Best fished with varied retrieves (stripping quickly, pausing, or slow steady retrieve) cast upstream and across, allowing swing downstream.\n\nMuddler Minnow: excellent for faster water, slim profile, weighted head, deer hair collar gives lifelike action. Can imitate baitfish, sculpin, or crayfish. Best in rocky or boulder-strewn bottoms.\n\nTechnique: Fish at various depths in structure (boulders, weed beds, drop-offs). Use 9-10 foot leader with 1-2X tippet. Set hook quickly.
