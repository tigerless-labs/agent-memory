Recommended psychedelic rock bands with mellow/atmospheric sound similar to Tame Impala:

- **Pond**: Album 'Man It Feels Like Space Again' (2015); song '30000 MEGATONS'
- **Unknown Mortal Orchestra**: Album 'Multi-Love' (2015); song 'Can't Keep Checking My Phone'
- **Temples**: Album 'Sun Structures' (2014); song 'Shelter Song'
- **Mild High Club**: Album 'Timeline' (2015); song 'Windowpane'
- **King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard**: Song 'Am I in Heaven?'
- **Foxygen**: Album '...And Star Power' (2014); song 'How Can You Really'
- **The Black Angels**: Album 'Phosphene Dream' (2010); song 'Black Grease'
- **The Horrors**: Album 'Skying' (2011); song 'Still Life'
- **Moon Duo**: Album 'Circles' (2012); song 'Sleepwalker'
- **A Place to Bury Strangers**: Album 'Worship' (2012); song 'You Are the One'
