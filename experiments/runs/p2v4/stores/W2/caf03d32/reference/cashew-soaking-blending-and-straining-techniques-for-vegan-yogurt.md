**Soaking Cashews**

1. Rinse 1 cup of cashews in a fine-mesh strainer under cold water
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight
3. After initial soak, drain and rinse again
4. Optional: Soak in fresh water for another 4 hours or overnight for extra creaminess

**Blending Cashews**

1. Drain and rinse the soaked cashews
2. Add 1/2 cup fresh water to blender with drained cashews
3. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
4. Stop and scrape down sides as needed

**Straining Options**

All three methods work; choose based on preference and availability:

- **Cheesecloth** - Pros: High filtration, catches smallest particles. Cons: Messy to use, requires squeezing
- **Nut Milk Bag** - Pros: Designed for this purpose, fine-mesh. Cons: Can be pricey, quality varies by brand
- **Fine-Mesh Sieve** - Pros: Easy to use and clean. Cons: May not catch all particles, slightly grainy result

**Straining Tips**
- Use clean, sanitized straining tool
- Squeeze mixture through tool to extract maximum liquid
- For cheesecloth: twist and squeeze to get more liquid out
- For nut milk bag: squeeze gently to avoid pushing particles through mesh

**Consistency Tips**
- Adjust cashew-to-water ratio to preference: 1:1 is standard
- Use less water for thicker yogurt, more for thinner
- Shorter blending time = thicker consistency
- Chill for at least 2 hours to allow thickening
