Options for vegan/plant-based yogurt bases to use in slow cooker yogurt making:

1. **Soy Yogurt** - Classic vegan option. Brands: Silk, So Delicious
2. **Coconut Yogurt** - Creamy and rich. Brands: So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo
3. **Almond Yogurt** - Low-calorie, low-fat. Brands: Almond Breeze, Silk, So Delicious
4. **Cashew Yogurt** - Creamy and indulgent. Brands: So Delicious, or homemade
5. **Oat Yogurt** - Good for nut allergies/intolerances. Brands: Oatly, So Delicious
6. **Rice Yogurt** - Hypoallergenic, easy to digest. Brands: Rice Dream, So Delicious
7. **Hemp Yogurt** - High in protein and omega-3s. Brands: Good Karma, Elmhurst 1925
8. **Flax Yogurt** - Low-calorie, high-fiber. Brands: Good Karma, or homemade

**Selection Tips:**
- Choose unflavored and unsweetened varieties to avoid added sugars
- Add your own flavorings and sweeteners later to taste
- Check ingredient lists and nutrition labels for dietary fit
