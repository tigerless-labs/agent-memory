**Flavorings**

- Vanilla (extract or bean)
- Cinnamon (powder)
- Nutmeg (powder)
- Fresh ginger (grated)
- Lemon (juice or zest)
- Cocoa (powder or melted chocolate)
- Fresh or frozen fruit (berries, mango, peaches)

**Sweeteners**

- Honey (drizzle)
- Maple syrup (small amount)
- Coconut sugar
- Date syrup (sweet, molasses-like flavor)
- Monk fruit sweetener (use sparingly - 150-200x sweeter than sugar)
- Stevia (pinch of powder)

**Add-ins for Texture**

- Granola (store-bought or homemade)
- Nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds)
- Cocoa nibs
- Dried fruit (cranberries, raisins, apricots)

**Notes**

- Experiment with different combinations to find your perfect blend
- Can add flavorings and sweeteners during or after cooking to taste
