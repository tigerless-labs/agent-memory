Starting strength training focused on core and leg exercises to improve performance in tennis and running.\n\n**Frequency:** 2-3 times per week with 48-72 hours recovery between sessions.\n\n**Core exercises:** Planks (30-60s, 3-5 sets), Russian twists (3×10-15), leg raises (3×10-15), bicycle crunches (3×10-15).\n\n**Leg exercises:** Squats (3×10-15, 2-3x/week), lunges (3×10-15 per leg, 2-3x/week), calf raises (3×10-15, 2x/week), deadlifts (3×10-15, 1-2x/week).\n\n**Approach:** Progressive overload (increase 2.5-5 lbs every 2 weeks), focus on proper form over weight, incorporate multi-muscle group exercises for efficiency. Balance with cardio (running, tennis) and flexibility work (stretching, yoga).
