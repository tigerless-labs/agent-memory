## Project Scope

- **Document count:** 90,000-100,000 reports
- **Document length:** 1-5 pages each
- **Topics:** International crime, terrorism, politics
- **Data structure:** Very little structured data; mostly unstructured text

## Recommended Organization Approach

Six-step methodology for organizing and analyzing:

1. **Text Cleaning** — Remove irrelevant characters, special characters, punctuation, stop words; normalize to lowercase
2. **Document Parsing** — Extract constituent parts: title, author, date, body text
3. **Entity Extraction** — Identify and extract names, organizations, locations using NER, POS tagging, dependency parsing
4. **Topic Modeling** — Identify main topics and themes to organize documents by common themes and identify patterns
5. **Sentiment Analysis** — Understand tone and emotions to track changes in public opinion or political sentiment
6. **Document Classification** — Classify by content (crime, terrorism, politics) for quick search and filtering

Note: Exact approach depends on specific requirements and nature of data.
