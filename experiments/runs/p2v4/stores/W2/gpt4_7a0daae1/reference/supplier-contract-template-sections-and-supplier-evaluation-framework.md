Key contract sections: Introduction, Scope of Work, Pricing and Payment Terms, Delivery and Shipping, Quality and Warranty, IP Rights, Term and Termination, Liability and Indemnification, Dispute Resolution, Confidentiality, Governing Law, Amendments, Entire Agreement, Signatures.

Key supplier evaluation factors: Product/Service Quality, Reliability and Consistency, Pricing and Cost, Customer Service and Support, Financial Stability and Reputation, Supply Chain Visibility, Certifications and Compliance, Innovation and Continuous Improvement.

Red flags: Poor communication, lack of transparency, no references provided, history of late deliveries or defects, inadequate insurance, unprofessional staff, unclear contract terms, unwillingness to adapt.
