Genghis Khan Yaki - grilled lamb or mutton with onions and vegetables. Kaisendon - rice bowl with fresh sashimi (salmon, tuna, squid). Jingisukan - Japanese Mongolian barbecue grilled lamb with soy sauce. Uni Ikura Don - rice bowl with sea urchin and salmon roe. Ishikari Nabe - savory salmon and vegetable hot pot, eaten in winter. Local dairy products including ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors).
