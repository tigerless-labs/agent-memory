Linkin Park integrated mental health advocacy and philanthropic work throughout their career. Their music dealt with depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation themes.

**Music for Relief (2015)**: Non-profit for disaster relief aid and mental health awareness campaigns. Worked with American Foundation for Suicide Prevention and Campaign to Change Direction.

**Awards**: Lifesaver Award (2017) from AFSP; Change Award (2018, inaugural) from Unite the United Foundation recognizing band members' work.

**Key History**: Lead singer Chester Bennington died 2017; remaining members continued advocacy. Also worked on environmental sustainability and education initiatives.

User appreciates the band's music and was impressed by their philanthropy involvement.
