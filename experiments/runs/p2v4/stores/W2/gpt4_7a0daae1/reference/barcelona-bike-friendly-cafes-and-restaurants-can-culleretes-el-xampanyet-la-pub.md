**Bike-friendly cafes and restaurants along Barcelona bike tour routes:**\n\n1. **Can Culleretes** — Historic café in the Gothic Quarter; outdoor seating and bike parking; good for coffee/snacks\n2. **El Xampanyet** — Popular spot in Barceloneta; outdoor seating and bike parking; famous for pan con tomate (bread with fresh tomato)\n3. **La Pubilla** — Cozy café in Eixample neighborhood; bike-friendly atmosphere and delicious pastries\n4. **Bicioci** — Bike-themed café in Poblenou neighborhood; healthy snacks and drinks, bike parking, repair station\n5. **Can Solé** — Family-run restaurant in Barceloneta; outdoor seating and bike parking; traditional Catalan cuisine\n\n**Recommendations:**\n- Check opening hours and menus before stopping\n- Many cafes have outdoor seating where you can lock your bike\n- Ask tour guide for insider recommendations along the route\n- Bring reusable water bottle and refill at water fountains throughout the city
