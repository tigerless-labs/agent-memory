**Getting from Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN) to Eixample:**

1. **Aerobús** (most direct)
   - Duration: 35-40 minutes
   - Cost: 5.90 EUR (approximately)
   - Route: Direct bus to Plaça Catalunya (short walk to Eixample)
   - Frequency: Every 5-10 minutes
   - Booking: Online or at airport

2. **R2 Nord Commuter Train** (fastest)
   - Duration: 25-30 minutes
   - Cost: 4.10 EUR (approximately)
   - Route: Airport to Passeig de Gràcia station (short walk to Eixample)
   - Booking: At airport or train station

3. **Taxi**
   - Duration: 20-30 minutes (traffic dependent)
   - Cost: 30-40 EUR approximately
   - Best for: Convenience or group travel

4. **Metro (L9 + transfer to L3)**
   - Duration: 40-50 minutes
   - Cost: 2.40 EUR (most economical)
   - Route: L9 from airport to Zona Universitària, transfer to L3 to Passeig de Gràcia

5. **Private Transfer**
   - Most expensive option
   - Best for: Group travel or those preferring personalized service
