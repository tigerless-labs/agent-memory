Car rental - excellent road network for independent exploration. JR Hokkaido trains - comfortable way to travel between major cities. Bus network - connects major tourist spots and cities. Bicycle - scenic routes available, good for rural areas and small towns. Guided tours - include food tastings, sightseeing, and transportation.
