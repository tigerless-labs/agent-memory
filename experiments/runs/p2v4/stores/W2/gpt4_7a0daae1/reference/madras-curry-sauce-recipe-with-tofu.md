Ingredients:
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon curry leaves (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon Madras curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onions and cook until translucent (about 5 minutes).
2. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, curry leaves, turmeric, Madras curry powder, garam masala, salt, and cayenne pepper. Cook for 1-2 minutes until spices are fragrant.
3. Add diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, and coconut milk. Stir well to combine.
4. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens.
5. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
6. Add cooked tofu (or protein of choice) and simmer for 5-7 minutes until flavors are well combined.
7. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve over rice, with naan, or with roti.

Tips: Can add potatoes/carrots; substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream; use different proteins like chicken, shrimp, or paneer; add lemon juice or vinegar for brighter flavor.
