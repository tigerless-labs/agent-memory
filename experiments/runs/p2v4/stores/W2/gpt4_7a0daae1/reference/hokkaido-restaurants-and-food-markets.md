Markets & Casual Dining: Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) - fresh seafood. Sapporo Ramen Yokocho - ramen alley. Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) - local food and souvenirs. Restaurants: Daruma (Sapporo) and Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) for Jingisukan. Yurigakuro (Sapporo) and Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru) for Ishikari Nabe.
