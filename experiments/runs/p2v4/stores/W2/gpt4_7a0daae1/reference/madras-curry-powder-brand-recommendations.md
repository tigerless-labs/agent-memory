Three brands recommended for Madras curry powder:

1. **Penzeys Spices** — 12-spice blend, popular choice among Indian cuisine enthusiasts
2. **The Spice House** — 15-spice blend with unique additions like fenugreek and fennel
3. **McCormick** — More accessible and affordable option, good balance of spices
