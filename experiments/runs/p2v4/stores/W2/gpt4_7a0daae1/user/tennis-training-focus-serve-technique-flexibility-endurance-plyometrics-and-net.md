Actively working on improving multiple aspects of tennis game for Saturday lessons:

**Serve Technique**
- Continental grip (index on second groove)
- Shoulder-width stance, body turned slightly right
- Ball toss 6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front, slightly right
- Contact at peak of toss, wrist and forearm for power
- Proper follow-through close to body
- Body rotation for power generation
- Serve variation: flat serves, topspin serves, slice serves

**Flexibility & Recovery**
- Stretching after Saturday lessons (15+ minutes)
- Focus areas: neck, shoulders, chest, arms, calves, hamstrings, quads, hip flexors, forearms, rotator cuff, glutes
- Hold stretches 30 seconds, breathe deeply, don't bounce
- Make stretching a daily habit, not just post-lesson

**Cardiovascular & Endurance**
- Currently doing running and cycling
- Adding structured cardio: 2-3 sessions per week
- HIIT workouts: 30-second high-intensity bursts with 30-second rest
- Aerobic steady-state: 20-30 minutes jogging or cycling
- Goals: faster recovery between points, maintain energy throughout matches

**Plyometric Exercises** (2-3 times per week)
- Box jumps (3 sets of 10-15 reps, start with 12-18 inch box)
- Depth jumps (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
- Lateral bounds (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
- Tuck jumps (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
- Medicine ball throws (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
- Rest 30-60 seconds between sets
- Focus on proper technique and explosive power

**On-Court Strategy**
- Come to net more often after weak opponent returns
- Develop stronger volleys
- Read opponent's return patterns
- Improve positioning at net
- Mix up strategies to keep opponent guessing
- Practice approach shots and net play in drills first
