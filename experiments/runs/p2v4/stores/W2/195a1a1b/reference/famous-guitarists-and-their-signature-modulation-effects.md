## Notable Guitarists Using Modulation Effects

- **David Gilmour** (Pink Floyd): Chorus and phaser effects on 'Comfortably Numb' and 'Shine On You Crazy Diamond'
- **Eddie Van Halen**: Flanger and phaser effects created his signature 'brown sound' tone
- **The Edge** (U2): Delay and chorus effects for his signature ethereal guitar sound
- **Andy Summers** (The Police): Chorus and flanger effects for complex, layered guitar parts
- **Jimi Hendrix**: Uni-vibe pedal to create phaser-like effects on 'Machine Gun' and 'Star Spangled Banner'
