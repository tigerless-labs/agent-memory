Best historic landmarks in Granada for views of the city:

1. **The Alhambra** — Ancient fortress and palace complex (Moorish reign, 13th-14th centuries). Built as fortress then used as royal palace by Nasrid dynasty. After 1492 Christian conquest, Catholic monarchs made additions. Restored 19th-20th centuries. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Offers breathtaking views of city and Sierra Nevada Mountains.

2. **Mirador de San Nicolas** — Viewpoint on Albaicín hill. Panoramic views of city, Alhambra, and Sierra Nevada Mountains.

3. **Sacromonte Abbey** — Monastery on hilltop with panoramic views of Granada and surroundings.

4. **El Salvador Church** — Bell tower provides bird's eye view of city center.

5. **Albaicín Quarter** — Historic quarter with narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings, multiple vantage points for city views.

The Alhambra is most popular with travelers and offers best overall views.
