**Paul Strand** (1890-1976) was an American photographer and pioneer of modernist and straight photography.

**Early career:**
- Born in New York City, largely self-taught
- Influenced by Alfred Stieglitz
- Joined Photo-Secession group in 1915
- Exhibited work in Stieglitz's gallery

**Major work:**
- Shifted to documentary photography in the 1920s
- Photographed rural communities, urban landscapes, industrial sites
- Extensively traveled documenting everyday life and social issues
- **Mexico series (1932+):** Famous project documenting Mexican people and landscapes; considered some of the most important examples of documentary photography from the period

**Philosophy:**
- Believed photography could be a powerful tool for social change
- Aimed to raise awareness of social injustices and inspire action
- Work characterized by simplicity, directness, and social purpose

**Legacy:** Established as a leading photographer of his generation; continued influence on modern photography.
