Evidence-based natural remedies and lifestyle modifications for pollen allergy management:

1. **Avoid outdoor activities during high pollen count days** — check pollen counts online to plan accordingly
2. **Keep indoor air clean and pollen-free** — wash hair and clothes after coming indoors, shower frequently, keep windows and doors closed
3. **Use air filters** — install filters in heating/AC systems and use portable air purifiers
4. **Saline nasal rinse/spray** — clear allergens from nasal passages
5. **Consume anti-inflammatory foods** — include omega-3s, fruits, and vegetables in a balanced diet
6. **Stay hydrated** — drink plenty of water to reduce skin irritation and clear nasal passages

Note: These remedies may not work for everyone and may not replace medical treatment. Always consult with a doctor before using natural remedies or making significant lifestyle changes.
