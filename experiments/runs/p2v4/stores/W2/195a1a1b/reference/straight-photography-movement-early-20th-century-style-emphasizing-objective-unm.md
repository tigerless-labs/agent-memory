**Straight photography** emerged in the 1920s-1930s as a photography style emphasizing straightforward, objective representation without manipulation or artistic effects.

**Key figures:**
- Alfred Stieglitz (coined the term, pioneered the movement)
- Edward Weston
- Paul Strand
- Ansel Adams

**Characteristics:**
- Truthful, honest representation of the world
- Rejection of artificial effects or manipulation
- Influenced by Pictorialism and documentary tradition
- Focus on clarity, precision, attention to detail
- Simple, everyday subjects (landscapes, still lifes, portraits)

**Legacy:** Continues to influence street, landscape, and portrait photography; celebrated for its commitment to objectivity and the power of photographic images.
