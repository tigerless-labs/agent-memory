Over-the-counter treatment options for pollen allergy symptoms:

1. **Antihistamines** — reduce sneezing, itching, and runny nose
2. **Decongestants** — relieve nasal congestion
3. **Nasal corticosteroid sprays** — reduce inflammation and swelling in nasal passages
4. **Cromolyn sodium nasal spray** — prevent allergy symptoms by stopping histamine release
5. **Saline nasal rinses** — clear mucus and irritants from nose and sinuses

Important: Read and follow instructions carefully; be aware of potential side effects or interactions with other medications. Consult healthcare professional if symptoms persist or worsen despite OTC treatments.
