Meditation Apps recommended:

- **Headspace**: Guided meditations for sleep, stress, focus. Free trial, then subscription. Contains 'Sleep' meditation (10 min) - found effective by user.
- **Calm**: Guided meditations, sleep stories, relaxing music. Free trial, then subscription. Contains 'Deep Sleep' (20 min).
- **Insight Timer**: Free app with large library including sleep and relaxation meditations. Contains 'Body Scan' meditation (10 min) - user planning to try.
- **Meditation Studio**: Variety of guided meditations including sleep. Free with in-app purchases.
- **Buddhify**: Guided meditations for different times of day, including bedtime. One-time purchase.

Free Online Resources: Andrew Weil guided meditations (YouTube), Rainy Mood website, Sleep With Me podcast, Meditation Oasis, UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center
