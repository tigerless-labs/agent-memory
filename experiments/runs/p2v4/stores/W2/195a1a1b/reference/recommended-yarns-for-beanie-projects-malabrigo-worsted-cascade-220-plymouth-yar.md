**Yarn recommendations for beanies:**\n\n- **Malabrigo Worsted**: Soft, worsted weight with subtle sheen and great stitch definition; wide color range\n- **Cascade 220**: Classic, affordable worsted weight; soft, warm, easy to work with; extensive color options\n- **Plymouth Yarn Company Worsted**: Soft with subtle texture and great stitch definition\n- **Berroco Ultra Alpaca**: Lightweight with subtle sheen; good for sensitive skin\n\n**Key tips:**\n- Always swatch before starting to check gauge\n- Worsted weight is ideal for beanies (beginner-friendly)\n- Pattern resources: Purl Soho's Simple Hat (free), Ravelry's Beginner Patterns collection\n- Beanie recommended over slouchy hat for beginners due to easier shaping and fewer stitches
