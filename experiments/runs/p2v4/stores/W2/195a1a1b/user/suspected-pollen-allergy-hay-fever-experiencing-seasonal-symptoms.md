Suspects pollen allergy based on experiencing these symptoms:
- Sneezing
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Itchy nose, throat, or ears
- Watery, red or swollen eyes (allergic conjunctivitis)
- Cough
- Postnasal drip
- Fatigue or weakness
- Headache

Plans to consult doctor or allergist for proper diagnosis. Interested in both OTC medications and natural remedies for management.
