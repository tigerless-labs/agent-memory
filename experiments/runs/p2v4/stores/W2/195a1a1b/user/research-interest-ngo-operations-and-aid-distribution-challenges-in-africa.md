Researched challenges NGOs face when distributing humanitarian aid in Africa, with particular focus on:

**Key areas of interest:**
- Accountability and transparency systems in aid distribution
- Impact of manual processes vs. automated systems
- Infrastructure limitations and their effect on operations
- Corruption prevention and mismanagement of resources
- Operational costs and resource limitations
- Donor reporting and accountability requirements
- Remote area distribution challenges

Engaged with rewriting content about humanitarian aid platforms and best practices for NGOs serving vulnerable communities in Africa.
