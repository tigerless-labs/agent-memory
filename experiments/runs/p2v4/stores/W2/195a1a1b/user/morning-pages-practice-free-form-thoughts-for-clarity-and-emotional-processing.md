Uses morning pages in journal for:
- Clearing mind and getting thoughts down on paper
- Processing emotions and reflecting on experiences
- Capturing ideas that later become poetry or short story scenes

Practice: lets thoughts flow freely onto the page. Sometimes has specific topics to explore, but mostly just starts writing and follows where it leads. Finds this helpful for tapping subconscious mind and discovering ideas organically.
