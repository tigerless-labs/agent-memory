Interested in writing science fiction romance stories, particularly those exploring themes of identity, self-discovery, and what it means to be human. Drawn to complex character arcs where protagonists undergo significant emotional and psychological growth. Enjoys world-building with advanced technology or speculative elements that force characters to question their sense of self. Interested in slow-burn romantic relationships built on mutual respect and emotional intimacy rather than insta-love. Appreciates stories where external sci-fi elements (technology, alien encounters, memory manipulation) serve as vehicles for exploring internal character conflicts.
