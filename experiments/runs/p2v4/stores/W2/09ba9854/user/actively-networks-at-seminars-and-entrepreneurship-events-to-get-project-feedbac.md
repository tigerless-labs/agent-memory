Regularly attends seminars and networking events to promote projects and gather feedback. Attended entrepreneurship seminar at co-working space near apartment.

Approach to asking for feedback:
- Be respectful of others' time; keep asks concise
- Show genuine interest in their work before requesting help
- Be clear about what type of feedback is needed
- Offer value in return when possible (skill swap, connection, acknowledgement)
- Have ready elevator pitch and answers about project
- Follow up after events to maintain connections

Avoids being pushy; respects boundaries and gracefully accepts declines.
