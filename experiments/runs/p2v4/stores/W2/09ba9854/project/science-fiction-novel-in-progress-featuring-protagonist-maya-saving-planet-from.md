## Project: Untitled Science Fiction Novel

**Protagonist:** Maya

**Central Conflict:** Maya must save her planet from an alien invasion. The story explores her motivations, goals, and internal/external conflicts as she fights to protect her world.

**Development Focus:** Character development using the Reedsy character profile template. Currently brainstorming Maya's:
- Short-term and long-term goals
- Motivations and driving forces
- Internal and external conflicts
- Character flaws and how they impact decision-making
- Relationships and how goals create tension with other characters

**Resources Used:**
- Reedsy character profile template (12-section structure)
- Character development exercises and brainstorming techniques
- Scene writing and character journaling methods
