Recommendations for protecting camera lenses during transport and storage:

**Lens Cases:**
- Think Tank Photo Lens Case: Excellent protection, durability, organization; soft padded interior; sizes for specific lens types
- Lowepro Lens Case: High-quality materials, durable construction, excellent protection; various sizes and styles available
- Nanuk Lens Case: Designed for extreme protection and durability; perfect for rugged outdoor use; excellent shock absorption

**Lens Pouches:**
- Op/Tech USA Lens Pouch: Soft and padded; excellent protection; various sizes; Velcro closure
- Lenscoat Lens Pouch: Designed for specific lens sizes; soft padded interior; water-resistant and breathable
- Tamrac Lens Pouch: Protection and convenience; various sizes; zippered closure

**Features to Consider When Choosing:**
- Size: Choose case/pouch that fits lens snugly to prevent movement and damage
- Material: Durable, high-quality materials resistant to scratches and dings
- Padding: Thick, soft padding to absorb shocks and protect lens
- Closure: Secure closure (zippered or Velcro) to prevent lens from falling out
- Breathability: Important if storing in humid environment to prevent moisture buildup

**Storage Tips:**
- Always store lenses with caps on to prevent scratches and damage
- Keep lenses upright to prevent oil from lens elements flowing onto mounts
- Avoid stacking lenses to prevent pressure and damage
- Clean lenses before storage to prevent dirt and dust accumulation
