## Reedsy Character Profile Template

**URL:** https://reedsy.com/character-profile-template

**Description:** A free, downloadable character profiling template with 12 sections covering comprehensive character development.

**Template Sections:**
1. Basic Info (name, age, occupation)
2. Physical Appearance
3. Personality (traits, values, goals, flaws)
4. Backstory (childhood, family, education, significant life events)
5. Motivations (what drives the character)
6. Conflict (internal and external)
7. Relationships (family, friends, romantic, enemies)
8. Skills and Abilities
9. Fears and Phobias
10. Habits and Quirks
11. Emotional Landscape
12. Evolution (character growth throughout story)

**Use:** Helpful for creating well-rounded, believable characters. Provides balance between brevity and depth. Can be customized and expanded as needed.
