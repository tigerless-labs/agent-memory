The Royal Affair: A sci-fi romance story concept exploring forbidden interspecies love. A commoner or forbidden match falls for a prince betrothed to a powerful alien princess in a futuristic intergalactic kingdom. The story navigates complexities of intergalactic politics, duty versus love, and the consequences of choosing personal happiness over royal obligation. Key themes: identity tied to royal heritage, questioning one's role and responsibilities, prioritizing personal desires while confronting duty and expectations. The protagonist must make difficult choices that challenge their sense of identity and purpose.
