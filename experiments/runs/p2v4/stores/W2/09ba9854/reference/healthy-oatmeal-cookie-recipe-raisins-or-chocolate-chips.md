**Ingredients:**\n- 2 cups rolled oats\n- 1 cup almond meal\n- 1/2 tsp baking powder\n- 1/2 tsp baking soda\n- 1/2 tsp salt\n- 1/2 tsp cinnamon\n- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted\n- 1/4 cup honey\n- 1 egg\n- 1 tsp vanilla extract\n- 1/2 cup raisins (or substitute with chocolate chips)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease.\n2. Whisk together oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.\n3. Whisk together melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla.\n4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients; mix until just combined.\n5. Gently fold in raisins.\n6. Drop dough onto baking sheet using cookie scoop or spoon, spacing 2 inches apart.\n7. Bake 10-12 minutes until cookies are golden brown.\n8. Cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.\n\n**Note:** Raisins keep cookies healthier; chocolate chips add sweetness and sugar but can substitute for different preference.
