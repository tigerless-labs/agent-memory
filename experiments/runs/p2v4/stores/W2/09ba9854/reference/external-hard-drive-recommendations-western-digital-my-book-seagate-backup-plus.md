Recommendations for external hard drive storage for photo backup:

**Primary Options:**
- Western Digital My Book: Popular and reliable, up to 16TB capacity, compact, built-in power management, compatible with Mac and PC
- Seagate Backup Plus: Well-known option, up to 5TB capacity, sleek design, 2-year warranty
- LaCie Rugged: Rugged and durable option for rough handling, up to 5TB, compatible with Mac and PC
- G-Technology G-Drive: High-performance option, up to 10TB, designed for creative professionals, fast transfer speeds

**Key Selection Factors:**
- Capacity: 2-3x current storage needs
- Compatibility: Mac vs PC
- Interface: USB 3.0, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C
- Speed: 7200rpm or SSD options
- Durability: Consider rugged or waterproof if portable
- Power: Bus-powered vs external power
- Security: Built-in encryption, password protection
- Warranty and support
- Portability: Size and weight
- Price range: $100 to $1000+

**Backup Strategy:** Follow the 3-2-1 rule: three copies of files, two different types of media, one copy offsite. Use automatic backup software and periodically verify backups can be restored.
