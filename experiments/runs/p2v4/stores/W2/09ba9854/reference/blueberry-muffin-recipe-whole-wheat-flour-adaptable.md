**Ingredients:**\n- 2 cups all-purpose flour (can substitute with whole wheat for healthier option)\n- 1/2 cup granulated sugar\n- 1 tablespoon baking powder\n- 1/2 teaspoon salt\n- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled\n- 2 large eggs\n- 1 cup milk\n- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract\n- 1 1/2 cups blueberries (fresh or frozen)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C). Line 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners or grease with cooking spray.\n2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.\n3. Whisk together melted butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla until smooth.\n4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients; mix until just combined (do not overmix).\n5. Gently fold in blueberries.\n6. Divide batter among muffin cups; top with extra blueberries if desired.\n7. Bake 20-25 minutes until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.\n8. Cool in pan 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.\n\n**Note:** Whole wheat flour substitution results in denser, darker muffins; may need adjusted baking time and added sweetness (honey or maple syrup).
