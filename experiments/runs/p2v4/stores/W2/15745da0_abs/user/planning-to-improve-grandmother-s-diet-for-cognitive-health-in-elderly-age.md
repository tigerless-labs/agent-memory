The user is working to enhance their grandmother's diet to support cognitive function as she ages. Key foods/supplements discussed for elderly cognitive health:

- Omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, tuna, mackerel)
- Antioxidants (berries, spinach, kale)
- B vitamins: B6, B9 (folate), B12 (found in leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals)
- Moderate caffeine (coffee, tea)
- Curcumin (turmeric)

The user is interested in making these healthy foods more appealing to their grandmother through:
- Creative recipes
- Incorporating into dishes she already enjoys
- Attractive presentation
- Considering texture (soft/easy-to-chew options)
- Involving her in meal planning

Easy healthy snack ideas to prepare for her:
- Apple/celery with nut butter
- Roasted chickpeas
- Greek yogurt with berries
- Hummus with vegetables
- Trail mix with nuts and dark chocolate
- Whole-grain crackers with avocado
- Smoothies with fruit and greens
- Energy balls with dates and nuts
