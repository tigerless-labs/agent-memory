Started learning printmaking as a beginner. Key interests identified:

- Basic techniques: relief printing and foundational methods
- Materials and their effects: ink types (water-based vs oil-based), paper thickness, printing plates (copper, zinc, linoleum), brayers, presses
- Brayer selection factors: ink type, printing surface texture, size of surface, level of detail in design, personal preference
- Approach: experimental learning, willingness to try different materials and make mistakes
- Would benefit from structured learning (classes/workshops) and consistent practice

Realized there's significant complexity to printmaking beyond initial expectations.
