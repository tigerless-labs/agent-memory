When revising team guidance and formal documentation:

- Replace casual phrases with formal equivalents (e.g., "automatically make it so" → "conclusively establish")
- Use formal transitions: "It is essential to keep in mind" vs. casual "remember"  
- Avoid colloquial language; maintain impeccable professional register throughout
- Phrases to replace: "look for any reason" (too casual) → "search for justifications"
- Structure for imperative, formal tone suitable for team protocols and policy documents
