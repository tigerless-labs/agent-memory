Frustrated with true crime stories that sensationalize crimes, glorify perpetrators, and ignore victims' experiences. Believes narratives should:
- Respect victims and families' dignity and privacy
- Focus on justice and prevention rather than exploitation
- Address broader social issues that enable crimes
- Prioritize victims' lived experiences over graphic details

Views consumers as responsible for holding media outlets accountable for ethical storytelling. Believes true crime has potential to inform and inspire positive social change when told responsibly. Emphasizes remembering the real people and real tragedies behind each story.
