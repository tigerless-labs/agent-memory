Critical principle for appraisal team review process:

An appraiser's assessment of a property as complex does not conclusively establish its complexity. Although appraisers possess expertise and experience in evaluating property complexities, it is imperative to rigorously consider their opinion and accompanying evidence in conjunction with independent research and analysis.

Appraisers have a vested interest in securing the highest fee possible, and may therefore be inclined to search for justifications of complexity. The team must exercise caution in judgment and ensure decisions are informed by objective criteria and comprehensive examination of the property and market conditions—not solely by the appraiser's assessment.
