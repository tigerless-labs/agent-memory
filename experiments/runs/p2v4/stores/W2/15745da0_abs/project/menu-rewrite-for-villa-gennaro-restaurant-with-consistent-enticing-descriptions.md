## Villa Gennaro Menu Rewrite

**Project**: Rewrote menu descriptions for Villa Gennaro restaurant across multiple sections.

**Sections completed**:
- Part 2: Classics (chicken and veal dishes) — Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese variants
- Part 3: Entrees (seafood, veal, lamb, risotto) — Fra Diavolo, Zuppa Di Pesce, Braised Short Ribs, Seafood Risotto, Rack of Lamb
- Part 4: Pasta (risotto, ravioli, pappardelle) — Vodka sauce, Mushroom Ravioli, Lobster Ravioli, Siciliana

**Style established**:
- Enticing, descriptive language highlighting ingredients and flavors
- Each item includes sauce/preparation details (wine reductions, herb notes, etc.)
- Consistent pricing format included
- Side options noted (pasta or vegetable and potato where applicable)
- Examples: 'rich and savory flavors,' 'succulent and tender,' 'bold and hearty'

**Dates**: Referenced parts 1 & 2 prior to this session; work continued on parts 3-4 on 2023-05-22.
