Recommended weather apps for checking trail conditions and forecasts:

1. **Dark Sky** — highly accurate, minute-by-minute forecasts, especially useful for hikers predicting precipitation
2. **Mountain Weather Forecast** — specifically designed for mountain weather, includes snowfall, avalanche risk, wind
3. **Weather Underground** — detailed hourly and 10-day forecasts, weather radar, satellite imagery, personal weather station network
4. **OpenSnow** — ski-focused but provides detailed mountain forecasts including temperature, precipitation, wind
5. **The Weather Channel** — trusted source with hourly/10-day forecasts, radar, satellite imagery
6. **AccuWeather** — minute-by-minute forecasts, weather radar, satellite imagery
7. **National Weather Service (NWS)** — official government app with detailed forecasts, warnings, radar

User indicated particular interest in trying Dark Sky and Mountain Weather Forecast.
