1. Workplace Simulation - Students manage teams to complete projects, practicing decision-making, communication, conflict resolution

2. Leadership Styles Role Play - Students explore different leadership styles and their advantages/disadvantages

3. Boardroom Discussion - Students take executive roles (CEO, CFO, CMO) to practice leadership, contribution, negotiation, defending positions

4. Decision-Making Scenarios - Students assume leader role, debate problems, determine positions and make decisions

5. Conflict Resolution - Students role-play as mediator or conflict participant, learning healthy conflict management
