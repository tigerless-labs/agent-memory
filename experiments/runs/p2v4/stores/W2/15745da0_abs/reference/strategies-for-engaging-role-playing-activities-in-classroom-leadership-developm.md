Make role-playing activities more engaging:

1. Use real-life examples students can relate to or that interest them
2. Provide clear objectives and goals from the beginning
3. Encourage creativity and imagination in role interpretation
4. Allow flexibility for students to take control and change course
5. Provide feedback on what went well and areas for improvement
