## Hamlet: Tragedy about Prince Hamlet of Denmark avenging his father's murder by uncle Claudius. Known for complex characters and themes of revenge, power corruption, and self-delusion. ## The Tempest: Romance about Prospero, Duke of Milan, stranded on an island using magic to manipulate shipwrecked characters including King Alonso, spirit Ariel, and monster Caliban. Explores power, control, forgiveness and reconciliation. ## The Winter's Tale: Tragicomedy about King Leontes' destructive jealousy believing his wife Hermione is unfaithful to King Polixenes. Results in deaths and banishment. Second half shows reunification and Hermione's innocence. Themes: jealousy's destruction, redemption and forgiveness.
