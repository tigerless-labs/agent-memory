Ensure all students participate equally in group work:

1. Set clear expectations for participation and contribution at the start of assignments
2. Provide individual assignments that contribute to the overall project
3. Assign group roles such as leader, recorder, and timekeeper
4. Encourage active listening so everyone's voice is heard
5. Monitor each student's participation and provide feedback as needed; address unequal participation early
