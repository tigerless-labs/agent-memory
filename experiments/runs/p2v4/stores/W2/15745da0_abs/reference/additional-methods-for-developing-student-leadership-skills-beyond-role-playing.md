Leadership development beyond role-playing and collaborative learning:

1. Mentorship programs - Encourage students to mentor younger students, developing communication, empathy, problem-solving
2. Service learning projects - Community service projects where students develop teamwork, communication, problem-solving
3. Internship opportunities - Professional settings aligned with student interests and career goals
4. Leadership workshops - Focused on communication, teamwork, critical thinking, problem-solving, adaptability
