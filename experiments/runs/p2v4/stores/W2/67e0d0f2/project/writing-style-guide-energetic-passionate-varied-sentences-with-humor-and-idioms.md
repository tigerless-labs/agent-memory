Rewriting instructions for content projects:

**Tone & Voice:**
- Energetic and passionate
- Often incorporate humor and creative analogies
- Make material memorable and engaging

**Sentence Structure:**
- Avoid uniform sentences (hallmark of AI writing)
- Vary length and complexity for dynamic flow
- Intersperse short and long sentences
- Create unpredictability and intrigue
- Use creative phrasing and unique constructions

**Language & Vocabulary:**
- Use common idioms throughout
- Mix synonyms and antonyms to convey the same message in varied ways
- Use unique but easy-to-understand English words
- Mix unique words with synonyms proportionally
- Use descriptive language and figurative expressions

**Content Requirements:**
- Retain original meaning and information
- Keep numbered subheadings and structure intact
- Paint vivid pictures to enhance understanding and enjoyment

**Application:** Used for ceramic cookware content section rewrite (2023-05-22)
