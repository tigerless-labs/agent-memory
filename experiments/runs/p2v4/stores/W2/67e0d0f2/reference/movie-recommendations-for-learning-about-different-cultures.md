## Films exploring different cultures and perspectives:

- **'Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon'** – Ancient China, themes of loyalty and honor
- **'The Lives of Others'** – East Berlin Cold War, surveillance and human connection
- **'Lion'** – Indian boy lost and adopted by Australian family, true story
- **'The Motorcycle Diaries'** – Che Guevara travel across South America, social injustice
- **'City of God'** – Brazilian favela, poverty and survival
