Toronto cost of living breakdown (as of 2023):

**Rent (per month):**
- Bachelor/1-bed shared: 1200-1600
- 1-bedroom apartment: 1600-2000
- 2-bedroom: 1800-2500
- 3-bedroom: 2500-3500

**Food:**
- Groceries: 500-750/month
- Casual dining: 10-15/meal
- Mid-range restaurants: 20-30/meal

**Transportation:**
- TTC monthly pass: 156
- Single fare: 3 (cash), 2.90 (Presto)
- Bike Share Toronto: 15-20/month

**Utilities:** 100-200/month (hydro, water, internet)
**Phone/Internet:** 50-100/month

**Ontario minimum wage:** 15/hour (as of 2022)
