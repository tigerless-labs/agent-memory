AFS Intercultural Programs (https://afs.org/) - Non-profit offering youth exchanges, study abroad, volunteering in 50+ countries. Experiment in International Living (https://www.experiment.org/) - Immersive programs for high school/college students with host families and community service. Youth for Understanding (https://www.yfu.org/) - Exchange programs in 40+ countries (year-long, semester, summer options). Cross-Cultural Solutions (https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/) - Volunteering in Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru; community development focus. Fulbright Program - International scholarships for research/study in US. Peace Corps - Government volunteers for community development projects abroad. Preparation tips: Research destination customs and history; learn local language basics; pack appropriately; stay open-minded; develop local relationships; follow safety practices.
