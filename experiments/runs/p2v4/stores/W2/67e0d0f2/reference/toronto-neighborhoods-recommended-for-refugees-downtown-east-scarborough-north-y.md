Recommended neighborhoods in Toronto for refugees/newcomers based on diversity, community resources, and support services:\n\n1. **Downtown East** (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown) — high concentration of newcomer communities, refugee organizations, community centers, language schools\n2. **Scarborough** — large immigrant population, Scarborough Village Recreation Centre offers newcomer services\n3. **North York** — significant refugee population, North York Community House, Centre for Information and Community Services\n4. **Etobicoke** — Etobicoke Newcomer Centre, West Toronto Newcomer Services, close to Toronto Pearson International Airport\n5. **Parkdale** — welcoming to refugees/immigrants, strong sense of community, vibrant arts scene\n\nConsider: proximity to community centers/language schools, public transportation, safety, affordable housing, cultural diversity.
