## Tips for Spotting Fake News
- Check the source; verify against trusted media outlets with proven accuracy
- Check tone; if sensational, divisive, or designed to create anger/fear, likely fake
- Verify facts through independent sources (government statistics, academic research, reputable journalism)
- Check photos and videos for manipulation
- Be skeptical of social media platforms as they're the primary breeding ground for unverified, biased content

## Critical Thinking Skills for Media Literacy
- Be curious; keep open mind and ask questions, seek different perspectives
- Check your own biases; expose yourself to diverse viewpoints
- Verify source credibility
- Check facts against reputable sources
- Pay attention to tone and language; avoid clickbait and sensational language
- Search for alternative viewpoints and sources
- Consider context surrounding the information
- Consult field experts for deeper understanding

## Finding Unbiased Information
- Consult multiple sources (government websites, various news outlets, academic sources)
- Check source credentials and reputation
- Look for sources attempting balanced coverage
- Seek expert opinions and analysis in the field
