High-demand sectors in Toronto job market (as of 2023):\n\n1. **Technology/IT** — software development, data science, cybersecurity; Toronto is a tech hub with many startups\n2. **Healthcare** — nurses, doctors, allied health professionals; growing need due to aging population\n3. **Finance** — banking, accounting, financial analysis; Toronto is a major financial hub\n4. **Skilled Trades** — electricians, plumbers, carpenters; shortage of skilled tradespeople in Canada\n5. **Renewable Energy** — growing sector as Canada transitions to sustainable energy\n\n**Job search resources:**\n- Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca)\n- Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor\n- Volunteer Toronto, Idealist, LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace\n\n**Language requirement:** Most jobs require CLB 5-6 (basic fluency); professional roles may require CLB 9-10
