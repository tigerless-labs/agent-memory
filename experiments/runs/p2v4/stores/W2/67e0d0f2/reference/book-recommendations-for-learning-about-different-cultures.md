## Character-driven narratives exploring different cultures:

- **'The Namesake' by Jhumpa Lahiri** – Bengali-American family identity and culture
- **'The Kite Runner' by Khaled Hosseini** – Afghan childhood friends, friendship and redemption  
- **'The Joy Luck Club' by Amy Tan** – Chinese-American women and immigrant mothers
- **'Things Fall Apart' by Chinua Achebe** – Nigerian tribe before/after colonization
- **'Persepolis' by Marjane Satrapi** – Graphic novel, Iranian girl during Islamic Revolution

These align with existing reading interests in character-driven narratives and historical/cultural storytelling.
