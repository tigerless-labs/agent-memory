**Genre:** Landscape and nature photography

**Camera preferences:**
- Compact form factor (vari-angle touchscreen LCD valued)
- Mirrorless format
- Not concerned about weight/size tradeoffs
- Weather sealing: nice-to-have, not required
- Values good image quality over sensor size (full-frame vs crop indifferent)

**Lens approach:** Open to starting with kit lens and building collection over time

**Learning interests:** Interested in understanding camera technology—autofocus systems, battery life, features that matter for the genres they shoot
