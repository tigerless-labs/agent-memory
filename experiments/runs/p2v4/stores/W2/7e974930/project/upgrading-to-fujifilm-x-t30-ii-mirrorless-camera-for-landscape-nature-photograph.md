Budget: $1,500 for camera body + lens

**Camera chosen:** Fujifilm X-T30 II
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- 425 phase-detection autofocus points (0.02s response time)
- Compact size with vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)
- Expected battery life: 300–400 frames per charge

**Kit lens plan:** Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ ($499), planning to add more lenses later
- No lenses yet; open to building lens collection over time

**Why chosen:** Compact form, advanced autofocus, excellent for landscape/nature work
