Collaborative creative writing on a story with MK (human protagonist) imprisoned by Wukong (Monkey King from mythology, now cruel captor searching for the diamond snare). MK doesn't know the snare's location and must convince Wukong to keep him alive as insurance. Key scenes: interrogation and torture, Wukong's mercy decision, MK's failed hunger pleas, minimal food deliveries, MK's identity crisis reconciling childhood hero-stories with captor-cruelty, failed escape attempts, starvation negotiations. Wukong is pragmatic-cruel, MK is desperate but resourceful. Magic barrier is unbreakable; diamond snare is key plot device; hunger/thirst drive desperation.
