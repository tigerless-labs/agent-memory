Key artists: Phoebe Bridgers, Julien Baker, Lucy Dacus, Bon Iver, Fleet Foxes, Sufjan Stevens, Courtney Marie Andrews, Vagabon, Adrianne Lenker, Sharon Van Etten, The Tallest Man on Earth. Recommended albums: Bon Iver - For Emma Forever Ago, Phoebe Bridgers - Stranger in the Alps, Julien Baker - Turn Out the Lights, Fleet Foxes - Fleet Foxes (self-titled), Sufjan Stevens - Carrie & Lowell. Genre characteristics: introspective lyrics, soaring vocals, storytelling-driven songwriting, mix of acoustic and indie-rock elements.
