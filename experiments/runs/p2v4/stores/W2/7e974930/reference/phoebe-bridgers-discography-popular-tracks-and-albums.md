Popular tracks: Scott Street, Killer, Motion Sickness, Funeral, Kyoto. Albums: Stranger in the Alps (2017, debut), Punisher (2020, sophomore), Boygenius (2018, EP with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus). Collaborations: Better Oblivion Community Center (2019) with Conor Oberst. Known for emotive live performances including NPR Tiny Desk Concert and KCRW sessions.
