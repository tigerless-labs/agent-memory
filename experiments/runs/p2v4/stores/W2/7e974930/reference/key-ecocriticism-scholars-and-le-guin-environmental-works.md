Ecocriticism scholars: Lawrence Buell (environmental imagination), Cheryll Glotfelty, Jonathan Bate, Eric D. Wilson. Ursula K. Le Guin major works: The Dispossessed (1974), The Left Hand of Darkness (1969), The Word for World is Forest (1976), The Lathe of Heaven (1971), Always Coming Home (1985). All explore environmental themes, political systems, and human-nature relationships.
