Recommended Bass Lures: Topwater (Poppers, Spooks, Frogs), Soft Plastics (Curly Tail Grubs, Worms, Lizards), Crankbaits (Shad-imitating, Square-bill, Deep-diving), Jigs (Jig and Pig, Swim bait, Finesse jigs), Spinnerbait, A-rig.

Fly Patterns for Bass: Streamers (Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, Baitfish Streamer), Poppers/Sliders (Poppers, Sliders, Gurgler), Nymphs (Crayfish Patterns, Mayfly Nymphs, Leech Patterns), Dry Flies (Frog Patterns, Terrestrials).

Knots: Improved Clinch Knot, Palomar Knot, Loop Knot (Perfection Loop).

Fly Rod Setup: 9-10 ft rod (6-8 weight), Weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking line, 9-12 ft leader (15-20 lb monofilament/fluorocarbon), 1-2 ft tippet (12-15 lb breaking strength).

Presentation Techniques: Strip retrieve, twitch retrieve, drag-free retrieve. Vary depth and pause between strips. Cast near structure (rocks, weeds, logs). Look for transitional zones and baitfish schools.
