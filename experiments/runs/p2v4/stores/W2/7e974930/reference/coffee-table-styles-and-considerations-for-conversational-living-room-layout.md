**Popular coffee table styles for conversational layout:**\n\n- **Ottoman coffee tables:** Storage ottoman base; comfortable footrest with hidden storage.\n- **Nesting tables:** 2-3 tables that stack together; ideal for small spaces, can be arranged to suit conversation circle.\n- **Reclaimed wood tables:** Adds warmth and character; look for unique pieces or distressed finishes to hide minor scratches.\n- **Industrial-chic tables:** Metal or reclaimed wood bases with rustic/distressed finish; adds edgy, modern touch.\n\n**Selection factors:**\n- **Size:** Measure space; consider distance between sofa/chairs and traffic flow.\n- **Shape:** Rectangular/square provide surface area; round encourages interaction.\n- **Material:** Wood, metal, glass, reclaimed wood; choose to complement existing furniture.\n- **Style:** Match living room aesthetic (modern, rustic, industrial, traditional).\n- **Functionality:** Storage, lift-top, lower shelf for display.\n\n**Retailers:** West Elm, Crate & Barrel, Wayfair.
