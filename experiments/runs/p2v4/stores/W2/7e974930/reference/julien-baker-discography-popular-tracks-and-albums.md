Popular tracks: Appointments, Turn Out the Lights, Rejoice, Souvenir, Hurt Less, Red Door, Favor. Albums: Sprained Ankle (2015, debut), Turn Out the Lights (2017, sophomore), Boygenius (2018, EP with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus). Known for introspective songwriting and soaring vocals. Known for emotive live performances including NPR Tiny Desk Concert and KCRW sessions.
