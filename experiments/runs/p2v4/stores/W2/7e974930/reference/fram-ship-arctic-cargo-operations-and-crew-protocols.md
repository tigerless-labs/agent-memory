## Arctic Cargo Handling on the Fram

**Loading process:**
- Cargo transported via helicopter, cargo planes, or specialized boats
- Secured with ropes, chains, or straps before loading
- Lifted using ship-mounted cranes or davits
- Stowed per cargo management plan and ship stability requirements

**Unloading process:**
- Cargo retrieved from storage using cranes
- Secured to equipment and lifted out using mechanical methods
- Transported to final destination via available transport modes

**Safety measures:**
- Detailed cargo management plans and procedures
- Specialized protective gear (hardhats, gloves, goggles, heavy-duty clothing)
- Clear crew communication protocols
- Regular training, assessments, and emergency drills
- Continuous weather monitoring (wind, waves, visibility)
- High-standard navigation equipment for ice/hazard avoidance
- Operations halted if weather becomes unsafe

**Crew duration:**
- Typical expeditions: weeks to several months
- Rare cases: up to one year
- Crew works long hours in rotating shifts
- Emphasis on physical and mental well-being, survival training, isolation management

**Operating challenges:**
- Unpredictable Arctic weather patterns
- Rough sea conditions
- Limited daylight in winter months
- Icebergs and navigation hazards
