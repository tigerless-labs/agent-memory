**Office chair brands to consider:**\n\n- **Herman Miller:** High-quality, ergonomic designs; range from budget-friendly to high-end models.\n- **Steelcase:** Well-established; offers ergonomic, task, and executive chairs.\n- **Flash Furniture:** Affordable office chairs with modern designs and decent ergonomics; good for budget-conscious shoppers.\n- **Autonomous:** Newer brand; affordable, ergonomic chairs with sleek designs and advanced features (adjustable lumbar support, cooling/ventilation).\n- **ERGOhuman:** (User's current brand) ERGOhuman has additional models: High-Back Executive Chair (adjustable lumbar, comfortable cushion), Mid-Back Mesh Chair (breathable mesh, adjustable armrests), Big & Tall Chair (extra-wide, sturdy frame).\n\n**Selection criteria:** Ergonomics (adjustable features), material/build quality, comfort, style, budget.
