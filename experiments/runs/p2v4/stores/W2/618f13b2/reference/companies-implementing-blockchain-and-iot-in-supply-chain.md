## Examples discussed:\n- **Walmart**: Using blockchain to track food product origin and improve traceability/safety\n- **Coca-Cola**: Using blockchain to track production and delivery of bottling equipment, reducing costs and enhancing transparency\n- **IBM & Maersk**: Developed TradeLens, a blockchain platform connecting global shipping industry players, improving visibility and reducing paperwork/delays\n- **Nestle**: Using IoT sensors to monitor product quality and safety during transportation and storage\n- **Provenance**: Blockchain-based platform allowing consumers to trace product origins, building transparency and consumer trust
