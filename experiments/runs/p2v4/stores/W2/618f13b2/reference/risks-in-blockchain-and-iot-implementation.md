## Key risks identified:\n- **Security**: Vulnerable to hacking and cyber-attacks; requires proper security measures and protocols\n- **Interoperability**: Different systems developed by different companies difficult to integrate; impedes data sharing and collaboration\n- **Regulatory**: Early-stage technologies with few established regulations; companies must ensure compliance with evolving legal environment\n- **Privacy**: Systems gather large amounts of sensitive/personal data; requires clear rules around data management and adherence to privacy protection laws\n\nConclusion: Risks manageable with proper safeguards; benefits outweigh downsides
