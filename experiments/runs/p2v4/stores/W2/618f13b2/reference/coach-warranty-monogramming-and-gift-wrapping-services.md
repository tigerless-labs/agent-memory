Coach offers 1-year standard warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Coach Promise extended warranty (~$20) extends coverage to 2 years total with priority customer service. Monogramming available on most styles including Parker: 2-3 week turnaround, ~$10-30 cost, multiple font/color options. Gift wrapping offered free or ~$5-10, includes signature gift box, satin ribbon, and personalized message option.
