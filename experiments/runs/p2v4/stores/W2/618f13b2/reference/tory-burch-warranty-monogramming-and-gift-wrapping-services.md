Tory Burch offers 2-year standard limited warranty on handbags covering defects in materials and workmanship. Tory Burch Care program (~$30) extends coverage to 3 years total with priority customer service, exclusive offers, and dedicated concierge service. Monogramming available on most styles including Robinson: 2-3 week turnaround, ~$10-30 cost, multiple font/color options. Gift wrapping services similar to Coach offerings.
