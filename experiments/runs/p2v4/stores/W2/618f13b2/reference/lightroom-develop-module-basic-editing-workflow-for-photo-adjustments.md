Key Develop module adjustments for enhancing photos:\n\n**Basic Panel:**\n- Exposure: Brighten (right) or darken (left) image\n- Contrast: Increase depth and definition\n- Shadows: Recover details in dark areas\n- Blacks: Darken blacks to separate from shadows\n- White Balance: Auto-adjust or use presets\n\n**Tips:**\n- Compare Before/After views frequently\n- Use histogram to optimize tone distribution\n- Make subtle adjustments to avoid over-processing\n- Use Gradient Filter or Adjustment Brush for localized edits\n- Sync adjustments across multiple photos\n\n**Other panels:** Tone Curve, Details (sharpening/noise reduction), Lens Corrections
