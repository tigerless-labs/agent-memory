Wants healthy lunch ideas that don't cause afternoon drowsiness. Prefers: protein-packed meals (lean proteins, legumes), whole grains/complex carbs, light and easy-to-digest foods. Recommended approaches: salads with lean protein, whole grain wraps with veggies, lentil/vegetable soups, grilled protein with quinoa, bean bowls. Avoids: heavy cheeses, croutons, and foods that cause sluggishness.
