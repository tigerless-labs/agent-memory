Planning to create interactive and immersive art installations exploring themes of identity, memory, and the human experience. Key concepts: using sound, light, and texture to recreate specific environments and memories; exploring collective memory and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts. Project ideas include installations simulating forest environments with multi-sensory elements (bird sounds, rustling leaves, scent of damp earth), and assemblages incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods. Interested in technologies like spatial audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback, AR, and interactive storytelling platforms for these installations.
