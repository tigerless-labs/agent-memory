Attended a guided tour at the Modern Art Museum on 2023-02-20 led by Dr. Patel, featuring the exhibition 'The Evolution of Abstract Expressionism'.

Key insights gained:
- **Pollock and Rothko**: Dr. Patel's explanation of their work was particularly enlightening — learned about the nuances in how emotion and meaning are conveyed through abstract art
- **Anselm Kiefer**: The large-scale installation by Kiefer was deeply moving and broadened appreciation for contemporary art's relationship to abstract expressionism
- **Personal impact**: The tour significantly deepened understanding of abstract expressionism and reinforced the value of guided tours for accessing deeper context and meaning in artworks

This experience reinforced preference for guided tours as an effective learning method.
