**Artists & Collectives**: Carsten Höller (interactive installations with slides, Ferris wheels); Anicka Yi (immersive, scent-based installations); Rafael Lozano-Hemmer (interactive installations using biometrics, robotics). **Exhibition Spaces**: Meow Wolf (Santa Fe); The Color Factory (San Francisco); TeamLab (Japan). **Technologies**: 3D audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback, augmented reality, interactive storytelling, collaborative platforms. **Software**: Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino. **Online resources**: Artspace, The Creators Project, Hyperallergic.
