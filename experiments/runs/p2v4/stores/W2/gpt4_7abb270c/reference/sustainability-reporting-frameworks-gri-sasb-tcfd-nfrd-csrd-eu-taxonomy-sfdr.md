## Key Frameworks

| Framework | Body | Focus | Applicability |
|-----------|------|-------|----------------|
| **GRI Standards** | Global Reporting Initiative | Sustainability | Voluntary |
| **SASB Standards** | Sustainability Accounting Standards Board | Sustainability (industry-specific) | Voluntary |
| **TCFD** | Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures | Climate-related risks/opportunities | Voluntary (increasingly adopted) |
| **IR Framework** | International Integrated Reporting Council | Integrated Reporting | Voluntary |
| **NFRD** | EU | Non-financial disclosure for large companies | Mandatory for large public-interest entities (>500 employees, €20M balance sheet or €40M turnover) |
| **CSRD** | EU | Sustainability reporting (expanded scope) | Proposed/mandatory for all large EU companies; format: ESEF/Inline XBRL |
| **EU Taxonomy** | EU | Classification system for sustainable activities | Mandatory for NFRD/SFDR subjects |
| **SFDR** | EU | Sustainability of financial products | Mandatory for financial market participants |
| **CDP** | Non-profit | Climate disclosure | Voluntary; complements TCFD |

**NFRD vs CSRD**: NFRD has no prescribed format but recommends GRI, SASB, TCFD standards. CSRD introduces mandatory ESEF format (Inline XBRL) for comparability.
