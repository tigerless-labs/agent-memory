Recommended brands for Dell XPS laptop protective cases and sleeves:
- Incipio
- Speck
- Tom Bihn
- Targus
- Case Logic

Advice: Look for durable materials like hardshell plastic, neoprene, or nylon; ensure fit matches Dell XPS model specifically; balance protection with portability; available on Amazon or Dell website.
