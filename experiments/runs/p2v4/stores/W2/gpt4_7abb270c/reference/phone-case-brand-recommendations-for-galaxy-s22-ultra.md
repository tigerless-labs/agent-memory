Recommended brands for Galaxy S22 Ultra protective cases:
- OtterBox Defender Series (40-50 dollars, triple-layer with built-in screen protector, excellent drop protection)
- Spigen Rugged Armor (15-25 dollars, sleek design with raised edges)
- Case-Mate Tough Stand (30-40 dollars, built-in kickstand)
- Tech21 Evo Check (25-35 dollars, checkered design, shock-absorbing)
- Samsung Official Case (20-30 dollars, sleek design with precise cutouts)

User chose OtterBox Defender Series.
