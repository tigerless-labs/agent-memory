## Museums & Galleries (London area)\n\n**Feminist Art Focus:**\n- **Tate Modern** — collection of feminist art from 1970s (Judy Chicago, Mary Kelly, Sylvia Sleigh)\n- **Institute of Contemporary Arts (ICA)** — strong history of showcasing feminist art; hosted research projects on 1970s feminist art history\n- **Whitechapel Gallery** — supports women artists; "Women in Revolt" exhibition (2018) on Women's Liberation Movement\n- **Barbican** — exhibitions on feminist art and activists (e.g., Nancy Spero, 2020)\n- **Photographers' Gallery** — feminist photography from 1970s; hosted "Feminist Avant-Garde of the 1970s" exhibition (2016-2017)\n\n**Space/Science Focus:**\n- **Royal Observatory, Greenwich** — astronomy, navigation, space exploration history\n- **National Space Centre** (Leicester, ~90 min from London) — dedicated space museum, Soyuz spacecraft, moon surface pieces\n- **Science Museum** — permanent Space Galleries with Soyuz T-1, Eagle lunar module replica; "Cosmonauts: Birth of the Space Age" exhibition\n\n## Key Feminist Artists (1970s)\n- Judy Chicago\n- Mary Kelly\n- Sylvia Sleigh\n- Nancy Spero\n- Jo Spence\n- Alexis Hunter\n- Guerrilla Girls (collective)\n\n## Publications\n- **Heresies: A Feminist Publication on Art and Politics**\n- **Chrysalis: A Magazine of Women's Culture**\n\n## Experts\n- **Dr. Maria Rodriguez** — renowned expert on feminist art, particularly 1970s\n\nSource: Assistant recommendations from museum consultation conversation, 2023-01-15.
