Art Movements and Artists to Explore

Based on user's interest in abstract expressionism and desire to explore beyond Pollock and Rothko.

ARTISTS SIMILAR TO POLLOCK AND ROTHKO:
- Willem de Kooning: Dutch-American abstract expressionist with bold expressive brushstrokes and vibrant colors
- Franz Kline: American painter known for large scale works with bold colors and gestural brushstrokes
- Clyfford Still: American painter with large abstract forms and muted color palette
- Ad Reinhardt: Rothko contemporary
- Barnett Newman: Abstract expressionist, Rothko contemporary
- Robert Motherwell: Abstract expressionist

IMPRESSIONISM: Claude Monet, Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Mary Cassatt — dreamy light-filled paintings capturing fleeting moments

CUBISM: Pablo Picasso, Georges Braque — fragmented objects into geometric shapes

SURREALISM: Salvador Dalí, René Magritte, Max Ernst — explores the irrational, blurs reality and fantasy

POP ART: Andy Warhol, Roy Lichtenstein, Jasper Johns — celebrates popular culture and consumerism

FAUVISM: Henri Matisse, André Derain, Maurice de Vlaminck — vibrant expressive colors and energetic brushstrokes

MINIMALISM: Donald Judd, Dan Flavin, Agnes Martin — simplicity, clean lines, calm and contemplation

BAROQUE: Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Vermeer — dramatic lighting, intense emotions, exquisite detail

ART NOUVEAU: Alphonse Mucha, Gustav Klimt, Charles Rennie Mackintosh — sinuous organic forms and ornate patterns

CONTEMPORARY: Anselm Kiefer — large-scale installations exploring history, mythology, and human condition
