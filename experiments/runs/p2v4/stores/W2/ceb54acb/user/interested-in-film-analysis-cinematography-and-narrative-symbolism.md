Appreciates how filmmakers use visual symbolism and cinematography to enhance storytelling. Values subtlety—symbolism should serve the narrative and emotional depth, not distract or feel heavy-handed. Interested in films like *The Godfather*, *The Shining*, *Blade Runner*, *Get Out*, *The Matrix*, *Casablanca*, *The Shawshank Redemption*, and *Star Wars*.

Recognizes that symbolism, while powerful when used well, is not necessary for a film to achieve greatness—great cinema can succeed through compelling performances, direction, storytelling, or unique visual style alone. Dislikes contrived or pretentious symbolism that prioritizes technique over story.
