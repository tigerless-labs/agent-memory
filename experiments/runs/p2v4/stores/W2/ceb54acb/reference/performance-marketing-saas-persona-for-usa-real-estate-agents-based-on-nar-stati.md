# Susan Green — Real Estate Agent Persona

Source: NAR (National Association of Realtors) statistics | Created: May 22, 2023

## Demographics
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female
- Location: Suburban or rural area
- Income: Median $61,140 (2019)
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

## Personality
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable and continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with local market knowledge

## Work Habits
- Works independently with brokerage support
- Builds strong client and colleague relationships
- Active in local real estate community
- Maintains current knowledge of trends and regulations

## Pain Points
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking and analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents and brokerages
- Keeping up with latest real estate technology

## Goals
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Stay ahead of competition
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

## Technology Usage
- CRM software for managing clients and properties
- Email marketing and communication tools
- Social media for personal and professional use
- Open to adopting new technology

## Marketing Channels
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking events and referrals
- Direct mail and email marketing
- Social media advertising

## Budget Approach
- Balances cost-effectiveness with quality results
- Continuously monitors and adjusts based on performance
- Willing to invest in solutions that demonstrate ROI
- Leverages brokerage marketing resources and support
