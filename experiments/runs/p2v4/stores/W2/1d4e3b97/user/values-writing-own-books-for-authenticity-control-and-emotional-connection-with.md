Prefers to write books themselves rather than use ghost-writers or AI. Values:

- **Personal fulfillment** from the writing process
- **Complete ownership and control** over content, tone, and message  
- **Authenticity** — genuine representation of their voice and ideas
- **Skill development** from the writing process
- **Emotional connection** with readers through direct authorship

Ghost-writing and AI lack the personal authenticity and fulfillment that self-authored work provides.
