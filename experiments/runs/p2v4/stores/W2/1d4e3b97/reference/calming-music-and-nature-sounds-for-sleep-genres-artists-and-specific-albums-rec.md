**Nature Sounds for Sleep:**
- Rainfall (gentle rain)
- Ocean waves
- Forest sounds (birdsong, rustling leaves, gentle wind)
- Babbling brook
- Wind chimes

**Music Genres:**
- Classical music (Mozart, Chopin, Bach)
- Instrumental piano (slow, gentle)
- Acoustic guitar (soft, slow)
- Ambient music (soft, minimal melodies)
- New age music (flutes, harps, gentle percussion)

**Specific Artists & Albums:**
- Brian Eno - Ambient 1: Music for Airports
- Max Richter - Sleep (8-hour album designed for sleep)
- Ludovico Einaudi - Nightbook
- Enya - Watermark
- Steve Reich - Music for 18 Musicians
