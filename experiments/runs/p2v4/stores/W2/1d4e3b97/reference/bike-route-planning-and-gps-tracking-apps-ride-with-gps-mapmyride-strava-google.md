Popular cycling apps for route planning, GPS tracking, and finding bike-friendly roads:\n\n**Ride with GPS** (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Route creation and customization\n- Turn-by-turn directions\n- Bike-friendly road finding\n\n**MapMyRide** (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Route planning with GPS tracking\n- Performance analysis\n- Large community-driven route database\n\n**Strava** (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Social network for cyclists\n- Route planning and GPS tracking\n- Browse popular routes, compete with friends\n\n**Google Maps** (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Bicycling mode preferred\n- Finds routes avoiding highways\n- Highlights bike paths and lanes\n\nAll support custom route creation and help avoid busy roads with rough surfaces.
