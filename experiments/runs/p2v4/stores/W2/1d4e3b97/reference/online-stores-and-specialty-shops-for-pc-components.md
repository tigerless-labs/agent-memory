**Major retailers:**
- Amazon.com
- Newegg.com
- BestBuy.com
- MicroCenter.com (physical + online, in-store pickup available)
- B&H Photo Video (bhphotovideo.com)

**Specialty/niche shops:**
- PCPartPicker.com (price comparison, availability tracking)
- Superbiiz.com (hard-to-find and discontinued items)
- Reddit r/buildapcsales (deals and discussion)
- Performance-PCs.com (high-performance, water cooling, modding parts)
- FrozenCPU.com (custom parts, modding supplies)

Tips: Compare prices across retailers, check reviews, verify return policies and warranties before purchase.
