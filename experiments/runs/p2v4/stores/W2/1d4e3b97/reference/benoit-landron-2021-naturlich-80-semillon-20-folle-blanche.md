**Wine:** Benoit Landron 2021 Naturlich\n\n**Composition:** 2021 vintage is 80% Semillon from organic growers in Bergerac (due to frost damage) + 20% Folle Blanche\n\n**Notes:** The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit character. Described as a spring wine.\n\n**Venue:** Opened by the glass at 161 (natural wine bar in Sydenham, South East London)\n\n**Context:** Original vintages were made with Folle Blanche, but Benoit Landron was hit by terrible frost in 2021, necessitating the Semillon addition from Bergerac.
