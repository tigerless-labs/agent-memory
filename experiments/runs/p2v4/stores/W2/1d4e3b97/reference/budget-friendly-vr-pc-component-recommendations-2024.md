Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580
CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K
RAM: 8 GB DDR4 with high speeds and low latency
Motherboard: MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, Gigabyte GA-A320M-H
Storage: 500 GB SSD (WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, SanDisk Ultra 3D)
PSU: EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

Note: Dated 2024; newer budget options likely available now.
