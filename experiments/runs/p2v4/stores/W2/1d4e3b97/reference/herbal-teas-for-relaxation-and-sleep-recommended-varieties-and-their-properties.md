**Herbal Teas for Sleep & Relaxation:**

1. **Chamomile** - Classic choice, calming effect on nervous system, reduces anxiety
2. **Lavender** - Known for calming properties, reduces stress and anxiety
3. **Valerian root** - Natural sedative, improves sleep quality
4. **Peppermint** - Calms mind and body despite energizing reputation
5. **Tart cherry** - Contains melatonin, regulates sleep-wake cycles
6. **Passionflower** - Natural sedative, reduces insomnia and anxiety
7. **Lemon balm** - Reduces stress and anxiety, improves sleep quality
8. **Rooibos** - Caffeine-free, contains antioxidants, promotes relaxation
9. **Hibiscus** - Lowers blood pressure, promotes relaxation
10. **GABA tea** - Contains GABA neurotransmitter, regulates sleep and relaxation

**Tips:**
- Choose high-quality, organic brands
- Avoid added sugars and artificial flavorings
- Brew 30-60 minutes before bedtime
- Experiment with blending different herbs
