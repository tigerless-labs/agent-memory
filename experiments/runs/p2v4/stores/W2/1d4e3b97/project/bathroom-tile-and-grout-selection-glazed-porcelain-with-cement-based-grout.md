## Materials Selected
- **Shower walls and floor:** Glazed porcelain tile
- **Grout:** Cement-based grout

## Decision Rationale
- Glazed porcelain chosen for:
  - Glossy finish aesthetic
  - Added protection against scratches and stains
  - Easy to clean and maintain
  - High water resistance for shower application
  
- Cement-based grout chosen for:
  - All-purpose versatility
  - Relatively affordable
  - Suitable for most tile installations
  - Plan to apply grout sealer for stain/water protection
