## Campaign: Lost City of Eldorado

A tabletop RPG campaign centered on exploring a lost city with treasure to claim. The campaign emphasizes puzzle-solving with a mix of logic, observation, and physical challenges designed to test players of different skill levels.

## Unique Puzzle Ideas Created

### 1. Mirror Puzzle
Players find a large mirror with symbols etched on its surface. Their reflection appears mirrored and reversed. They must use the symbols and position themselves in a specific way to reveal a hidden passage.

### 2. Elemental Puzzle
A room with levers and buttons that control elemental forces (earth, fire, air, water). Players must activate the correct sequence to manipulate elements and reveal a hidden passage. Example: air lever + water button creates mist revealing a path.

### 3. Musical Puzzle
A room filled with musical instruments and sheet music. Symbols on the sheet music correspond to different instruments. Players must play the correct sequence (e.g., piano notes → drum beat → trumpet blast) to unlock a hidden door.

### 4. Weight Puzzle
Players find weights of different sizes/shapes and a set of scales with weight markings. They must weigh each object and place them in a specific order on the scales to reveal a hidden passage.

### 5. Memory Puzzle
Players memorize object locations in a lit room, then the lights go out. They must navigate the dark room to retrieve a specific object and bring it to a designated spot to unlock a hidden passage.

## Puzzle Design Principles
- Mix of different challenge types (logic, observation, physical manipulation)
- Scalable difficulty for different player experience levels
- Each puzzle has a unique solution to progress the campaign
- Puzzles can be modified or combined as needed
