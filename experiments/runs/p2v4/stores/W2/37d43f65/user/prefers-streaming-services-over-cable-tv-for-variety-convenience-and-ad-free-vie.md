**Media consumption habit**: Hasn't watched cable TV in months. Strong preference for streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) over traditional television networks.

**Key reasons for preference:**
- **Variety & catalog**: Access to wide range of content including original programming, international shows, and classic movies not available on cable
- **Ad-free viewing**: Uninterrupted watching without commercials
- **Convenience features**: Can pause, rewind, fast-forward at will; picks up shows exactly where left off even after days away
- **Device flexibility**: Watches on phone, tablet, or other devices while on the go using mobile apps
- **Release model**: Prefers binge-watch model (full seasons released at once) over weekly episode waits required by cable TV
- **Personalized recommendations**: Enjoys streaming service features that suggest content based on viewing history

Describes streaming as having 'so much more variety and convenience' compared to cable, and appreciates not having to deal with 'all the commercials' or remember what happened in previous episodes.
