Planning a living room workspace/reading nook near big windows. Preferences:

**Furniture & Layout:**
- Small desk or console table (wall-mounted, narrow console, drop-leaf, or foldable)
- Position near big windows for natural light
- Prioritize compact, stylish options that don't clutter

**Styling Elements:**
- Small circular rug (3-4 ft diameter) under desk to define space and add warmth
- Small circular mirror (12-18 in diameter) above desk for visual interest and to make space feel larger
- Artwork/prints above desk - interested in botanicals, landscapes, abstract, or typography
- Frame style should complement overall room aesthetic

**Plants:**
- Low-maintenance plants for bright indirect light (big windows)
- Interested in: snake plant, ZZ plant, spider plant, pothos, succulents, dracaena, peace lily, bromeliads

**Overall Approach:**
- Recently decluttered living room successfully
- Values organization and minimalist aesthetic
- Prefers multi-functional, space-saving solutions
