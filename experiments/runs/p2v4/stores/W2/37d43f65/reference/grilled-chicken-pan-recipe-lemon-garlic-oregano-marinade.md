Pan-grilled chicken recipe using grill pan or cast-iron skillet:

**Marinade:**
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup lemon juice  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**Method:**
1. Marinate 4-6 boneless skinless chicken breasts for 20-30 min
2. Heat grill pan/cast-iron skillet to medium-high
3. Cook 6-7 min per side until internal temp reaches 165°F
4. Rest 5-10 min before serving

Pairs well with grilled vegetables, salads, corn on the cob, sweet potatoes, or quinoa salads.
