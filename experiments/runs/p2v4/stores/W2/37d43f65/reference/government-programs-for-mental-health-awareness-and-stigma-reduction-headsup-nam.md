## Examples of successful government mental health programs

- **HeadsUp** (Australia, launched 2016) — awareness campaign for mental health literacy; supports mentally healthy workplaces with resources and tools
- **NAMI** (United States) — grassroots mental health organization; largest in US; provides awareness, education, resources for affected individuals and communities  
- **ReachOut** (Ireland) — online support service for ages 16-25; information, advice, tools for mental health management
- **Bell Let's Talk** (Canada) — multi-year program raising awareness and combating mental illness stigma; supports innovative programs and treatments
- **Dealing with Depression** (New Zealand) — government program combining public awareness campaigns with online resources and face-to-face counseling

All cited as successful examples combining public awareness, accessibility, and community support.
