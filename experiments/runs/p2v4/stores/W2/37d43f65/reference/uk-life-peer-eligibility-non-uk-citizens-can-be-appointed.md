Non-UK citizens can be appointed as life peers in the House of Lords, provided they are:\n- British citizens, OR\n- Citizens of Commonwealth countries or the Republic of Ireland\n- And have permanent residence in the UK\n\nAppointments of non-UK-origin peers (and those from underrepresented backgrounds) have increased in recent years, reflecting efforts to diversify the House of Lords' membership and bring wider perspectives to legislation.
