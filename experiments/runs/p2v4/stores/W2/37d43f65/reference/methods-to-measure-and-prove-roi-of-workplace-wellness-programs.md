Five measurement approaches for wellness program effectiveness:

1. **Employee surveys** - Anonymous feedback on engagement and perceived value
2. **Health metrics tracking** - Blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI before/after comparison
3. **Absenteeism analysis** - Correlate wellness participation with reduced sick days
4. **Productivity measurement** - Track output/performance changes related to program participation  
5. **Cost calculations** - Quantify savings from reduced insurance claims, fewer sick days, improved productivity

When properly designed and measured, wellness programs can show positive impact on employee health, productivity, retention, and healthcare costs.
