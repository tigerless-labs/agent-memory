Google Calendar setup: separate calendar for gaming, recurring events, partner's time zone display, 'Find a time' and 'Propose a new time' features, reminders 30min-2hrs before. Zoom integrates via add-on for one-click scheduling and joining. Also supports Hangouts Meet and other video conferencing links. Invite partner by email; calendar sends meeting links and invitations.
