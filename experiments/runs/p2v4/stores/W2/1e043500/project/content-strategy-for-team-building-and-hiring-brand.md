Multi-format content strategy to establish thought leadership around hiring humans first, great technologists second.

Content Formats:
- Blog posts: articles on identifying key traits, building collaborative teams, psychometric testing
- Podcasts/Videos: interviews with industry experts and successful team leaders
- Webinars/Workshops: hands-on training on team building and soft skill evaluation
- E-books/Guides: deep-dive into psychometric testing, soft skills assessment, collaborative culture
- Social media: case studies, team profiles, brand values highlighting

Distribution Channels:
- Social media promotion and brand awareness building
- Email marketing
- Industry partnerships and collaborations
- Community engagement through comments and feedback

Measurement:
- Track content performance via analytics
- Adjust strategy based on engagement, reach, and conversion metrics
