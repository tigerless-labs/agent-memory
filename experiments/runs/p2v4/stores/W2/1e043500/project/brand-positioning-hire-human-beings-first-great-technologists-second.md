Building a brand around prioritizing soft skills, EQ, and psychometric testing over pure technical ability.

Core Ethos: hire great human beings first, then great technologists second. Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just coders. Value EQ (emotional intelligence) over IQ when building software development teams. Focus on collaboration, communication, and team dynamics.

Why This Matters: creates differentiation in hiring/team-building space, addresses gap in tech industry that over-indexes on technical skills, positions thought leadership in people-first engineering culture.
