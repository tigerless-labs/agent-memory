Six core content niches identified for thought leadership brand:

1. Remote Work Best Practices - staying productive, communication, time management, work-life balance

2. Managing People Remotely - effective communication, team dynamics, conflict resolution, culture building

3. Career Development and Advancement - career navigation, skill building, role progression in software development

4. Entrepreneurship and Business - starting/growing businesses, attracting/retaining clients, building strong teams

5. Emerging Technologies - latest trends, incorporation into development, staying current

6. Remote Work and Work-Life Balance - achieving balance, setting boundaries, avoiding burnout, healthy habits

All niches feed into core brand message of prioritizing human-centered, EQ-driven team building.
