Research examines the impact of social media usage patterns on depression and anxiety symptoms in young adults. Key areas of inquiry include:

- **Primary question**: Association between social media usage patterns and symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults
- **Mediating variables**: Exploring how self-esteem and social support mediate the relationship between social media use and mental health outcomes
- **Intervention focus**: Effectiveness of social media-based interventions and policies aimed at promoting mental health
- **Applications**: Mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, peer-to-peer support initiatives, and leveraging social media platforms for mental health promotion and stigma reduction
