## Visual Art Forms
- **Batik printing** — wax-resist dye technique for traditional patterns
- **Woodcarving** — statues, masks, utensils representing cultural traditions
- **Pottery** — coiling and throwing techniques for ceremonial and everyday utensils
- **Photography** — documents weddings, naming ceremonies, daily life, cultural practices
- **Painting** — depicts traditional clothing, food, dance, historical events

## Contemporary Developments
- Contemporary art forms (installations, performance, conceptual)
- Digital technology use (digital paintings, animations exploring social/climate themes)
- Pan-African artist collaborations
- Youth art programs for cultural heritage preservation
