## Trip Details
- **Duration:** 3 full days
- **Timing:** Early April
- **City choice:** Lisbon selected over Porto

## Day 1: Alfama & Baixa
- **Morning:** Alfama neighborhood → Lisbon Cathedral → Castle of São Jorge → tram rides through narrow streets
- **Afternoon:** Baixa district → Praça do Comércio → Rossio Square → Chiado streets → Café A Brasileira (coffee & pastel de nata)

## Day 2: Sintra Day Trip & Belém
- **Morning:** Train to Sintra → Pena Palace → Moorish Castle → gardens and hilltop views
- **Afternoon:** Return to Lisbon → Belém neighborhood → Jerónimos Monastery → Monument to the Discoveries → Pastéis de Belém pastries

## Day 3: Tagus River & Bairro Alto
- **Morning:** Tagus River walk → MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology) → river views
- **Afternoon:** Tram to Bairro Alto → Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara viewpoint → narrow streets → traditional restaurants

## Additional Recommendations
- Museums: National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum
- Other districts: LX Factory (art and culture district)
