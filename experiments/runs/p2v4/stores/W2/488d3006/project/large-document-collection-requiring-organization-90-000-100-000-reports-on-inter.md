## Collection Details
- **Volume:** 90,000-100,000 documents
- **Document length:** 1-5 pages each
- **Data structure:** Very little structured data; mostly unstructured text
- **Subject matter:** International crime, terrorism, and politics

## Recommended Organization Approach
Methodology suggested for organizing this collection:
1. **Text Cleaning** — Remove irrelevant characters, punctuation, stop words; normalize to lowercase
2. **Document Parsing** — Extract constituent parts (title, author, date, body text)
3. **Entity Extraction** — Identify and extract names, organizations, locations via NER, POS tagging, dependency parsing
4. **Topic Modeling** — Identify main topics and themes; organize by common themes; identify patterns
5. **Sentiment Analysis** — Understand tone and emotions; track changes in sentiment over time
6. **Document Classification** — Classify by content type (international crime, terrorism, politics); enable quick search/filter

This approach enables structured querying and analysis of a very large unstructured document corpus.
