As of May 2023, planning a hiking trip to Zion National Park in the near future.

Equipment: Selected Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof hiking boots. These offer excellent ankle support, traction, waterproofing (Gore-Tex), and breathability for desert terrain. Plans to break in gradually: wear around town first, then short easy hikes, before longer challenging trails.

Target Attractions:
- The Narrows: scenic hike along Virgin River through narrow canyon
- Angels Landing: iconic rock formation with spectacular canyon views
- Emerald Pools: series of three pools and waterfalls
- Weeping Rock: natural formation with dripping springs and vegetation

Planned Hikes:
- Angels Landing: 5-mile strenuous hike with steep drop-offs and narrow ridges
- West Rim Trail: 10-mile hike along west rim of Zion Canyon
- Observation Point Trail: 8-mile hike with spectacular views
- Kayenta Trail: 2-mile hike to Emerald Pools and Weeping Rock
- Watchman Trail: 3-mile hike with good views

Trip builds on experience from Big Sur hiking.
