### Tools recommended for improving time management and calendar use:

1. **Google Calendar** — free, syncs across all devices, shareable, reminders, multi-attendee events
2. **Outlook Calendar** — widely used in corporate settings, meeting scheduling, group calendars, email integration  
3. **Apple Calendar** — built into Apple devices, iCloud sync, color coding, drag-and-drop events
4. **Any.do** — combines to-do list management with calendar, reminders, task linking
5. **Trello** — visual collaboration tool with boards, cards, deadline tracking, calendar integration

Also useful: to-do lists prioritized daily, SMART goals, blocking time on calendar, eliminating notifications/distractions, regular breaks, automation tools.
