Resources recommended for learning abstract art and printmaking:\n\n**Abstract Art:**\n- The Art of Abstract (website guide)\n- Abstract Art Academy (online course platform)\n- YouTube: The Art Sherpa, Artists Network, Abstract Art with Deb Watson\n\n**Printmaking:**\n- Printmaking 101 (website guide)\n- The Printmaking Council of New Jersey\n- YouTube: Printmaking with Zeke, The Printmaking Studio\n\n**Online Platforms:**\n- Skillshare (abstract art and printmaking courses)\n- Craftsy (printmaking and abstract art classes)\n- Creativebug (various art techniques)\n\n**Communities:**\n- Reddit: r/AbstractArt and r/Printmaking\n- Facebook groups for art communities\n\n**Publications:**\n- The Jealous Curator (blog)\n- Printmaking Today (magazine)\n- Abstract Art Magazine (digital)\n\n**Art Supply Stores (online):**\n- Dick Blick Art Materials\n- Jerry's Artarama\n- Cheap Joe's Art Stuff\n- Hyatt's All Things Art
