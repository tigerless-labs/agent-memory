**Weeks 1-5: Foundation**
- Week 1: Introduction to Bot Trading (basics, benefits, risks, bot types)
- Week 2: Setting up Bot Trading System (accounts, platforms, parameters)
- Week 3: Backtesting and Optimization (importance, techniques, performance tuning)
- Week 4: Live Trading with Bots (backtesting vs live, setup, monitoring, adjustments)
- Week 5: Advanced Bot Trading Strategies (scalping, arbitrage, market making)

**Weeks 6-10: Advanced Topics**
- Week 6: Risk Management (stop-loss, position sizing, diversification)
- Week 7: Advanced Technical Analysis (indicators, chart patterns, performance improvement)
- Week 8: Trading Psychology (overcome fear/greed, disciplined mindset)
- Week 9: Advanced Trading Strategies (hedging, mean reversion, strategy combination)
- Week 10: Advanced Platforms and Tools (API integrations, latest trends in bot trading)
