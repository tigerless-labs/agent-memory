**Must-see attractions in Pittsburgh:**

Museums & Culture:
- Andy Warhol Museum (largest single-artist museum in US)
- Carnegie Science Center (interactive exhibits, planetarium, submarine tour)
- Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens

Viewpoints:
- Mount Washington via Duquesne Incline
- Monongahela Incline

Neighborhoods:
- Strip District: street food, ethnic markets, Primanti Bros. sandwiches
- North Shore: PNC Park, Andy Warhol Bridge, restaurants like The Foundry
- Oakland: cultural hub near University of Pittsburgh
- Shadyside: shops and restaurants, upscale area

Food & Drink:
- Primanti Bros. sandwiches (famous Strip District establishment)
- Craft breweries: Penn Brewery, East End Brewing Company, Grist House Brewing

Activities:
- Three Rivers Heritage Trail (24-mile trail system with scenic views)
- Brewery tours
- Biking via Healthy Ride bike-share

**Transportation:**
- Port Authority Day Pass: $5.50, One-way: $2.50
- Free Transit Zone in downtown Pittsburgh (light rail and buses)
- Healthy Ride bike-share available
- Uber/Lyft widely available
- Pittsburgh International Airport (PIT) is main airport
