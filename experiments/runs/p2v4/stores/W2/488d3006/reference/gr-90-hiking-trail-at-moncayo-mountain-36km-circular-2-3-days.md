Circular trail highlights: Moncayo mountain summit (highest peak), Fuenfría waterfall, meadows, forests, rock formations. Passes through small towns offering cultural/historical landmark access. Recommended best seasons: spring (wildflower bloom) and autumn (fall foliage, less crowded than summer).
