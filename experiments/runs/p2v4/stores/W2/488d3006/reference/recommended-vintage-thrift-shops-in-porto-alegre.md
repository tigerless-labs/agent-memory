Shops with Vinyl Records: Armazém do Passado (vintage clothing, accessories, décor, vinyl records including Brazilian music/rock/pop) and Casa da Sogra (retro home décor, furniture, accessories, rare vinyl records).

Other shops: Brechó Municipal (affordable used clothes/shoes/accessories), Bazar Vintage (fashion-forward vintage/retro), Pinga do Brechó (clothing/accessories/kitchenware, slightly higher prices).

Price ranges vary by item condition and rarity. Check websites/social media before visiting for current inventory.
