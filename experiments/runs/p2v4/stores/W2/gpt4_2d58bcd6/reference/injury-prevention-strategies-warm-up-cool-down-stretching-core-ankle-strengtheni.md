Key injury prevention strategies recommended:
- Always warm up 5-10 minutes with light cardio and dynamic stretching before workouts
- Cool down 5-10 minutes with static stretches after workouts
- Regular stretching and foam rolling to improve flexibility
- Strengthen core and ankle muscles (planks, side planks, ankle circles, calf raises)
- Use proper footwear and appropriate gear
- Listen to body and rest when fatigued or experiencing pain
- Avoid sudden changes in workout intensity or frequency
- Gradually increase weight, reps, or sets over time

Ankle-specific prevention:
- Ankle circles, calf raises, single-leg balancing
- Use ankle braces during high-impact activities
- Improve balance and proprioception with exercises

Recovery (RICE protocol):
- Rest: stop activity immediately
- Ice: apply ice to reduce inflammation
- Compression: use wraps or sleeves
- Elevation: keep injured area elevated
