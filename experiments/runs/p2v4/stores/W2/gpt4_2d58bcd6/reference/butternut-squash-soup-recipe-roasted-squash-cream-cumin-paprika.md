## Butternut Squash Soup Recipe\n\n**Servings:** 4–6\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1 large butternut squash (2–3 lbs)\n- 2 tbsp olive oil\n- 1 onion, chopped\n- 3 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tsp ground cumin\n- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)\n- ½ tsp salt\n- ¼ tsp black pepper\n- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth\n- ½ cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)\n- Fresh parsley or sage for garnish\n\n**Method:**\n1. Roast squash at 400°F (200°C) for 45–50 minutes until tender\n2. Sauté chopped onion in olive oil until softened (~5 min), add minced garlic\n3. Add cumin, paprika, salt, pepper; cook 1 min, add broth and bring to boil\n4. Scoop roasted squash flesh into pot\n5. Blend with immersion blender until smooth\n6. Stir in cream if desired; adjust seasoning\n7. Serve garnished with fresh herbs\n\n**Variations:** Add diced apple or kale, use coconut cream or Greek yogurt for dairy-free, experiment with nutmeg or cinnamon\n\n**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–5 days in airtight container; freeze up to 6 months
