## Winter Landscape Photography Camera Settings\n\n**Core Settings:**\n- **Mode:** Manual (M) for full control\n- **ISO:** 100–400 (minimize noise)\n- **Aperture:** f/8–f/11 (large depth of field, entire landscape sharp)\n- **Shutter Speed:** 1/125s–1/250s\n- **White Balance:** Cloudy or Shade (capture cool tones)\n\n**Exposure & Light Techniques:**\n- Overexpose by +⅓ to +1 stop to capture snow detail (snow meter-reads as gray, not white)\n- Use graduated ND filter to balance sky/ground exposure\n- Shoot during golden hour (warm light on snow) or overcast days (even light)\n- Avoid harsh midday sun\n\n**Gear & Capture:**\n- Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility\n- Use polarizing filter to reduce glare and enhance colors\n- Bracket shots (multiple exposures) for HDR blending\n- Wide-angle lens (10–24mm) for landscape vistas\n- Keep spare batteries warm in pocket (cold drains them quickly)\n\n**Composition:**\n- Shoot from low angle to emphasize texture and intimacy\n- Capture snowflakes with fast shutter (1/1000s) and wide aperture (f/2.8)\n- Look for animal tracks and winter patterns to add visual interest\n- Use leading lines, minimal compositions, and textures for depth
