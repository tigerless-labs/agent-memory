Japanese manufacturer of high-voltage fuses for electric vehicles developing strategy to enter Indian market (2023).

## Key Strategic Pillars

1. **Market Research** — Understand unique needs of Indian EV industry: prevalent models, charging infrastructure, local manufacturing, competitors. India differs from other markets due to price sensitivity, diverse demographics, and infrastructure variance.

2. **Indian Standards Compliance** — Ensure high-voltage fuses meet India-specific regulatory and quality standards; acquire necessary certifications for regulatory compliance and customer trust.

3. **Localized Production/Collaboration** — Establish local production or partner with Indian company to reduce costs, ensure timely delivery, and gain competitive pricing edge. Local partnership aids regulatory navigation.

4. **Price Competitive Strategy** — Develop competitive pricing that reflects India's price sensitivity while maintaining quality standards, essential for product adoption.

5. **Distribution Channels** — Build robust network through established automotive parts distributors and e-commerce platforms to ensure maximum reach across India's vast, diverse market.

6. **Targeted Marketing** — Highlight benefits (safety, reliability, performance) via digital marketing, social media, and local industry events. Messaging must cater to diverse Indian consumer needs and preferences.

7. **After-Sales Service** — Establish service center network and train local technicians. Critical for building brand loyalty and trust in Indian market.

## Key Differentiation from Other Markets

Price sensitivity is the primary constraint differentiating India from developed markets. Strong after-sales service infrastructure and regulatory compliance are higher priorities than in mature markets.
