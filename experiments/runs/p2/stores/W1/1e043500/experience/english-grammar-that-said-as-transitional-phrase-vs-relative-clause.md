**Distinction between two uses of 'that said':**\n\n1. **Transitional phrase**: 'That said' used to introduce a contrasting or qualifying statement\n   - Example: "I love the park, but that said, I don't like crowds."\n   - Meaning: Nevertheless, however, in contrast\n\n2. **Relative clause construction**: 'that said' where 'that' is a relative pronoun and 'said' is a verb\n   - Example: "I saw a tweet that said the TV show was interesting."\n   - Meaning: A tweet which expressed/stated that...\n   - This is NOT the transitional phrase\n\n**More natural versions:**\n- "I just saw a tweet saying the TV show was interesting."\n- "I just saw a tweet that said the TV show was interesting."\n\nThe relative clause version (that + verb) is often more natural than the transitional phrase in this context.
