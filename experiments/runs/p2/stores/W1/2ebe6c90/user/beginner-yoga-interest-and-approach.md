Recently interested in starting yoga practice as a complete beginner. Initial concerns: worried about flexibility level (concern that yoga is 'only for flexible people') and ability to keep up with class pace.

Receptive to beginner-friendly approaches: looking for beginner-focused classes, using props (blocks, straps) for support, modifying poses, finding a supportive teacher.

Understands that yoga is not a competition and focuses on listening to body rather than performance. Open to practicing at home first to build confidence before attending in-person classes.
