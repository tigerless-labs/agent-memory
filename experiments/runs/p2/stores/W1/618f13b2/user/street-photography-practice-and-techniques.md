Attended a street photography workshop on May 1-2, 2023 where I learned specific techniques for approaching strangers and asking permission to photograph. Since then I've been incorporating street photography into my daily routine, taking camera with me to work to capture candid shots of people on morning commute.

Key equipment:
- 50mm f/1.8 prime lens (purchased April 10, 2023)
- Captured sunset photos at Red Rock Canyon on April 12, 2023

Practices:
- Interested in candid street photography and building rapport with subjects
- Learning techniques for approaching strangers respectfully
- Experimenting with angles, compositions, anticipating moments
- Working on 'decisive moment' concept (Henri Cartier-Bresson)
- Shooting in burst mode for better chance of capturing perfect expressions
- Focus on shallow depth of field with 50mm lens
