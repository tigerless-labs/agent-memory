## Thesis Research

**Topic:** Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension.

**Key findings:**
- These systems can be effective in improving learning outcomes
- Implementation and integration into classrooms must be carefully considered
- Teacher professional development is critical — many teachers lack skills/knowledge to use systems effectively
- Clear curriculum alignment increases teacher adoption and effectiveness
- Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support
- Concerns about algorithmic bias that could exacerbate existing inequities

**Recommendations made:**
- Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support
- Developers should work closely with educators to align systems with curriculum and learning goals
- Systems need to be transparent and explainable
- Ongoing evaluation and assessment of system effectiveness

## Presentation & Feedback

- Presented thesis research as a poster at Harvard University research conference, summer 2023 (first research conference)
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers
- Several developers reached out afterwards, interested in applying findings

## Research Interests

- Personalized learning and how AI can tailor instruction to individual needs
- Real-time feedback and its impact on student learning outcomes  
- How AI can develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills
- Collaborative problem-solving facilitated by AI
- Ensuring equity and inclusion in AI-powered systems
