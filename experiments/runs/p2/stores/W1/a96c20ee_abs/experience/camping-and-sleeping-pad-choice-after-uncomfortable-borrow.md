## Past camping trip

Borrowed an uncomfortable sleeping pad from friend Alex on a previous camping trip. Learned that having the right gear makes a significant difference.

## Sleeping pad decision (May 2023)

After researching options, decided to purchase **Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite** sleeping pad:
- 2.8 inches (7.1 cm) thick
- 12.5 oz (354g) weight
- Chose this model to balance weight and comfort
- Prioritized comfort over ultralight weight
