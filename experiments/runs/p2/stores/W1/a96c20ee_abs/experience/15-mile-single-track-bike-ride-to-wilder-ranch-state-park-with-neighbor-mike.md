## Bike maintenance plan

After the ride, planning to perform maintenance:
- Oil the chain
- Adjust derailleur

**Additional maintenance tips to consider:**
- Use appropriate chain lube for conditions
- Clean chain before applying lube
- Check derailleur limit screws and alignment
- Check tire pressure
- Inspect brake pads
- Check all bolts and quick releases
