## Mission Peak hike — April 10, 2023

**Type:** Solo day hike

**Location:** Mission Peak, Fremont

**Outcome:** Enjoyed the hike greatly; planning to return soon

**Trip details:**
- Beautiful trail with stunning Bay Area views
- Had the biggest breakfast burrito of life on way back, at a diner in Fresno

## Planning next hike — May 2023

Planning another solo day hike to Mission Peak with similar preparation:
- Check weather forecast before going
- Bring 2-3 liters of water
- Pack snacks, map/compass, first aid kit, sunscreen
- Bring layers and headlamp
- Plan to hike during daylight hours
- Let someone know route and expected return time
- Wear bright/reflective clothing
