**Project:** Multi-day mountain biking trip summer 2023 to nearby mountains.

**Goals:**
- Explore moderate-difficulty mountain biking trails
- Ride 20-30 miles per day
- Get started/gain experience with mountain biking

**Plans:**
- Purchase new mountain bike before trip
- Research trails using Trailforks, MTB Project, TrailLink apps
- Consider guided mountain bike tours or rentals for skill-building
- Check local bike shops for recommendations and guided tour options
- Look for mountain bike festivals or events in the area
- Popular destinations to explore: Park City Utah, Moab Utah, Bend Oregon, Asheville North Carolina, Lake Tahoe

**Preparation:**
- Test ride bikes at local shops
- Research trail conditions before heading out
- Plan to bring water, snacks, sunscreen
- Need protective gear: helmet, gloves, knee pads
