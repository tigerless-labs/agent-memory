Genius Voucher is a platform designed to manage voucher-based humanitarian projects for NGOs. Features include: real-time management and control of distribution activities, hardware (POS machines for vendors, smart cards for beneficiaries), software portal for project team to create and monitor distributions. Benefits: accountability and transparency, remote management capability (works offline in areas with poor connectivity), user-friendly redemption process. Addresses NGO challenges in Africa including lack of reliable accountability systems, high operational costs, and infrastructure limitations.
