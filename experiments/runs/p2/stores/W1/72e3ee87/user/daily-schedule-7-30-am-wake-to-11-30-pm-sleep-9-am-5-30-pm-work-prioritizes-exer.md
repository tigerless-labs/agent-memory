## Daily routine (May 2023)

### Fixed schedule
- Wake: 7:30 AM
- Sleep: 11:30 PM (midnight may differ slightly)
- Work hours: 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM
- Lunch break: 1 hour

### Daily priorities
1. **Exercise**: 30 minutes daily (morning preferred; flexible activity: yoga, jogging, weightlifting, home workouts)
2. **Reading**: 30 minutes daily (dedicated study time)
3. **Crash Course**: 1 episode daily (typically during lunch break)

### Time allocation strategy
- Morning (7:30-9:00 AM): Exercise, shower, breakfast, preparation
- Midday (noon-1:00 PM): Lunch break + Crash Course episode
- Evening (5:30-11:30 PM): Reading, dinner, free time, wind-down routine

### Philosophy
- Structured yet flexible approach to personal development
- Combines health (exercise) with intellectual growth (reading, videos, courses)
- Uses natural break points (lunch, commute) for learning
- Values consistency and sustainable daily practice
