**Weekly tasks:** Vacuuming, tidying living room.

**Weekly deep clean:** Rotates tasks like organizing closet, cleaning fridge, bathroom.

**Pattern:** Finds evening tidying challenging when exhausted after work, but persists because it creates a sense of calm and relaxation before bed. Notes the positive impact on mental clarity overall.

Started noticing these benefits a few weeks into establishing the habit (as of May 2023).
