Prefers energetic, passionate writing with humor and creative analogies. Values engaging tone that avoids sounding AI-generated. Specific requirements for content rewrites:
- Varied sentence length and complexity (mix short and long)
- Rich use of idioms and figurative expressions
- Descriptive, vivid language
- Synonyms and antonyms for varied phrasing
- Unique but accessible vocabulary
- Dynamic, unpredictable flow rather than uniform patterns

Retains original meaning while transforming delivery for reader engagement and memorability.
