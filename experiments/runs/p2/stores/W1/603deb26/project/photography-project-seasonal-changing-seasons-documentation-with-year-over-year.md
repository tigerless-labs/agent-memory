Decided to start a long-term seasonal photography project (Aug 2023).

Goal: Document transformation of a specific location or natural setting throughout the year, capturing how light and colors change across seasons.

Plan: 
- Choose a specific location (park, trail, forest, or wetland)
- Visit regularly to capture seasonal changes
- Attempt to capture the same scene at the same time each year to track year-over-year changes
- Combine with themed wildlife/nature photography focus

This project will provide opportunity to experiment with different techniques, compositions, and stay motivated on long-term work.
