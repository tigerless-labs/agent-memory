Planning to organize and improve living room with big windows. Plans include:\n\n**Plants**: Adding low-maintenance houseplants that thrive in bright light — considering snake plant, ZZ plant, spider plant, pothos, succulents, dracaena, peace lily, or bromeliads.\n\n**Workstation**: Small desk or console table near the window for workstation/reading nook. Prefers compact and stylish options like wall-mounted desk, narrow console table, drop-leaf table, or foldable desk.\n\n**Accessories**: Small circular rug (3-4 feet diameter) under desk to define the workspace and add warmth; small circular mirror (12-18 inches diameter) above desk to add visual interest and make the space feel larger; artwork or prints above desk to add personality and visual interest.\n\n**Style preferences**: Prefers compact, stylish options that won't clutter the room. Favors brands including IKEA, West Elm, Crate & Barrel, CB2, AllModern, and Amazon. Uses neutral color palettes and quality materials to create a cohesive, organized look.
