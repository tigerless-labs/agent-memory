Sci-fi romance novel in development featuring protagonist Lyra, a skilled memory thief. Story set in dystopian future where memories can be extracted, stored, and sold on black market using advanced neurotechnology. Lyra is hired to steal from powerful CEO but has fragmented memories and no clear recollection of their own past. Key plot elements: Lyra uncovers clues about their true past while stealing the CEO's memories; questions arise about the authenticity and fabrication of identity; major plot twist involves discovery of Lyra's true past and reason for memory loss; another twist concerns CEO's true intentions. Central themes: identity formation, self-discovery, vulnerability, trust, and authenticity. Story involves slow-burn romance and character growth as Lyra learns to trust and form connections.
