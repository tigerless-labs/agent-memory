Starting a YouTube channel to record and upload gameplay footage from PC games. 

Decision: Chose OBS Studio for screen recording because:
- Free and open-source
- Large community and plugin ecosystem
- Highly customizable (important for creating unique channel look)
- Well-optimized for RTX 3080 + Ryzen 9 5900X

Also considering investing in good microphone and webcam for audio/video quality.
