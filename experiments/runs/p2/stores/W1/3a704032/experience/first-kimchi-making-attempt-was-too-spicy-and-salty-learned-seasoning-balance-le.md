**Date:** May 2023 (weekend before May 21)

**What happened:** First attempt at making kimchi failed—result was way too spicy and too salty.

**What went wrong:**
- Added too much gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
- Added too much salt overall

**Solutions attempted/planned:**
- Rinse the over-salted kimchi under cold running water, gently massaging to release salt
- Planned to add more fresh vegetables to dilute the salt (if still in early fermentation)
- Decided to start fresh batch with careful measurement

**Key learnings about kimchi seasoning:**
- Gochugaru measurement guide: Start small (1/4–1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables for mild; adjust from there)
- Measure chili flakes by weight on digital scale for accuracy, not volume
- Taste as you go—easier to add heat than remove it
- Proper salt technique: lightly salt vegetables, rinse thoroughly before adding seasoning paste
- Balance comes from combination of gochugaru + garlic + ginger + fish sauce (umami)

**Next attempt plan:** Use gochugaru carefully measured, add minced garlic (2–3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables), fresh ginger (1–2 inches grated, per 2 cups), and taste frequently during assembly.
