Fern experiencing pest infestation with tiny moving dots observed crawling on leaves and stems, likely spider mites or springtails.

Problem identification:
- Tiny moving dots visible on plant
- Pests on leaves and stems
- Suspected spider mites or springtails

Treatment plan:
- Use neem oil spray following product label instructions
- Wait several days for neem oil to take effect
- Begin misting fern 2-3 times per week after treatment
- Use distilled or filtered water for misting
- Mist in morning to allow drying before nightfall
- Mist leaf undersides thoroughly but gently
- Avoid wetting the crown of the plant

Prevention ongoing:
- Monitor closely after treatment
- May need to repeat neem oil treatment
- Good air circulation helps prevent fungal disease
- Maintain humidity 40-60 percent for fern health
- Avoid overwatering which can attract pests
