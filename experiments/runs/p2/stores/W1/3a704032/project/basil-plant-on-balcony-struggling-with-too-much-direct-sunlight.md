Basil plant on balcony is struggling due to excessive direct sunlight exposure.

Problem: Too much direct sunlight causing plant stress. Basil typically thrives in partial shade, especially in warmer climates.

Solution in progress:
- Move to shadier location receiving indirect sunlight
- Consider east or west-facing balcony position
- Use sheer curtain or shade cloth to filter intense sunlight

Ongoing adjustments needed:
- Soil in shadier spot dries slower, adjust watering schedule
- Check soil moisture with finger to first knuckle depth
- Water only when soil feels dry to touch
- Maintain humidity around plant by placing pot on tray with water and pebbles
- Fertilize sparingly with balanced water-soluble fertilizer at half recommended strength
- Prune regularly, pinch off flower buds to encourage leaf growth
- Avoid leggy stems, maintain bushy shape
- Maintain daytime temperatures between 65-75 degrees Fahrenheit
- Avoid drafty areas or temperature fluctuations
