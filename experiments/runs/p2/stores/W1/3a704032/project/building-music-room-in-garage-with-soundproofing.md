Planning to set up a music room in the garage. Key considerations:
- Needs soundproofing to avoid disturbing others
- Will need to address both sound dampening (absorption) and sound isolation (prevention of escape)
- Plans to seal gaps/cracks, add mass, decouple walls/ceilings
- Considering acoustic panels, resilient channels, floating floor options
- Budget will be a factor; prioritizing critical areas first
