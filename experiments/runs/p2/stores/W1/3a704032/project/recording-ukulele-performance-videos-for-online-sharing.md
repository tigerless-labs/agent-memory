Planning to record videos of ukulele playing to share online.

Instrument: Kala KA-CE15S ukulele

Initial video plan:
- Location: new music room in garage
- Recording equipment: phone camera
- Recommendation: Start with cover song rather than tutorial (less intimidating, more engaging)
- Target length: 2-3 minutes
- Include self-introduction, name the song
- Focus on relaxation and having fun

Technical setup for recording:
- Audio: Position microphone 6-12 inches from ukulele, slightly off-center, angled toward sound hole
- Lighting: Use natural light or softbox lighting, good lighting reduces harsh shadows
- Video: Phone on tripod or stable mount to reduce shaky footage
- Audio quality: Consider external microphone for better sound beyond phone microphone

Post-production:
- Edit with software like iMovie or DaVinci Resolve
- Add captions and tags for discoverability on YouTube
- Edit for brightness, contrast, saturation
