Loves narratives that explore character development and complex relationships. Drawn to books featuring strong female protagonists, especially ones that examine identity, personas (public vs. private self), and power dynamics between women. Examples: **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo** by Taylor Jenkins Reid (started Jan 29, 2023; kept them up at night).

Also interested in graphic novels with similar depth—looking for recommendations like Saga by Brian K. Vaughan.

Values:
- Intersectional narratives (race, gender, class, sexuality)
- Vulnerable inner lives beneath performed public personas
- Complex relationships between women (supportive AND competitive/painful)
- Literary exploration of fame, identity, and authenticity
