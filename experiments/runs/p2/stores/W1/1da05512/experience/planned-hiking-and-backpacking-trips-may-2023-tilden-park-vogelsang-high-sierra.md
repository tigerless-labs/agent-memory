May 20, 2023: Completed Mount Tamalpais hike. Recently returned from Yosemite camping trip (early May 2023). Planned solo day hike at Tilden Regional Park (Berkeley Hills, Bay Area) - was deciding between Nimitz Way Trail (4.5 mi, 3-4 hrs) and Wildcat Gorge Trail (5.5 mi, 4-5 hrs). Both offer panoramic Bay Area views.\n\nSeptember 2023: Planning 3-4 day backpacking trip to Sierra Nevada. Initially considered High Sierra Camp Loop (40 mi, 7,000 ft elevation) but decided it was too challenging. Instead chose Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route (~20 miles, 3,000 ft elevation gain, moderate difficulty). Vogelsang sits at 10,300 ft with full amenities (tent cabins, meals, showers). Requires permit through Yosemite Conservancy.
