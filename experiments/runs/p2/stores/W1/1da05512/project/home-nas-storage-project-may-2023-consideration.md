In May 2023, considering NAS device purchase to replace external hard drive backup system. Goal: centralized backup for laptop and phone with improved security and redundancy. Key requirements: AES-256 encryption, granular access control, 2FA support, RAID capability, mobile app backup support. Evaluated range from budget 2-bay (Synology DS218j ~150 USD) to mid-range 4-bay (QNAP TS-453D ~350 USD) to high-end options. Concerned about data security and wanted automatic scheduled backups.
