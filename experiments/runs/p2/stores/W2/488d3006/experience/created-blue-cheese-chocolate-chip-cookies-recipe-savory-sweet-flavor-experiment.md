Experimented with blue cheese chocolate chip cookie recipe, inspired by a blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from a local chocolate shop. Initial attempts were too pungent but refined recipe to balance salty blue cheese with sweet chocolate. Final recipe uses crumbled blue cheese (1/2 cup) mixed into standard chocolate chip cookie dough with semisweet chocolate chips. Bakes at 375°F for 12-15 minutes. High-quality blue cheese recommended. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer. Son (picky eater) was skeptical at first but loved the unexpected flavor combination.
