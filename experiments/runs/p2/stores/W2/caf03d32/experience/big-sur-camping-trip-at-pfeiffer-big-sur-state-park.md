Completed a 2-night camping trip to Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. Hiked to McWay Falls on first day and Pfeiffer Beach on second day. Used a portable power bank successfully to keep devices charged throughout the trip, which was helpful for navigation and capturing memories. Attempted astrophotography but results weren't as desired - still learning night photography skills. Experienced beautiful natural scenery and dark skies ideal for stargazing.
