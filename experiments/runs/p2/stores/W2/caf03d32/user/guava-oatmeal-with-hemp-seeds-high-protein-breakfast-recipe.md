Developing a high-protein breakfast combining guava and oatmeal to support fitness goals.

**Recipe ratio:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds (for protein and omega-3s boost)
- Honey or maple syrup (optional)
- Toppings: cinnamon, sliced banana, chopped nuts

**Notes:**
- Hemp seeds provide 5-10g protein per 1-2 tablespoons serving
- Can grind hemp seeds for better incorporation or mix with liquid first
- Store hemp seeds in airtight container in fridge/freezer to preserve freshness
