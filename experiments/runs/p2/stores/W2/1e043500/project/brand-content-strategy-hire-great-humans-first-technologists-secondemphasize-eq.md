Building a brand and content strategy around a hiring philosophy that prioritizes hiring great human beings first, talented technologists second. Key principles: EQ valued more than IQ when hiring software developers; use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just skilled coders; focus on team building and how to develop strong teams.

Content strategy focuses on:
- Training people on how to build effective teams
- Blog posts, podcasts, videos, webinars exploring importance of soft skills
- Guides on psychometric testing and evaluating soft skills
- Interviews with successful leaders
- Social media content highlighting case studies

Target audience: hiring managers, team leaders, software development organizations interested in better hiring practices. Positioning as thought leader in building effective teams by prioritizing human factors over pure technical ability.
