Twisted ankle while playing basketball with friends around May 16, 2023 (two weeks before May 30). Took a few days off from gym to rest and recover following RICE principle. Returned to normal workout routine by the following Monday with no setbacks. This incident sparked interest in injury prevention strategies and ankle strengthening exercises. Learned importance of warm-up, gradual intensity increase, proper footwear, and targeted ankle exercises like ankle circles, calf raises, and balance work.
