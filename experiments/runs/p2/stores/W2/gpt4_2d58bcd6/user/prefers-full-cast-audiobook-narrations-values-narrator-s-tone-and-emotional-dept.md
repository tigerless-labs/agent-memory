When listening to audiobooks with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes, strongly prefers full-cast narrations as they create a more immersive experience and help keep track of who's speaking. Also appreciates full-cast narrations that convey emotional depth and complexity of characters' experiences, which helps feel more connected to the story. While single narrators with unique tone and style can also be compelling, full-cast is the clear preference for fiction.
