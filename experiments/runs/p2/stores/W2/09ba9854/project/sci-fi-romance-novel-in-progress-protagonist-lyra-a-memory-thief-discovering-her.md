Sci-fi romance novel with protagonist Lyra, a skilled memory thief hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO. Lyra has no memory of her own past and fragmented identity. Story explores themes of identity and self-discovery through advanced neurotechnology for memory extraction and manipulation. Central plot mysteries: discovery of Lyra's true past and reason for memory loss; CEO's hidden agenda. Includes slow-burn romance between Lyra and the mark, built on trust and communication. Worldbuilding features memory trading as commodified good creating class divisions. Philosophical questions about authenticity of experience and nature of identity drive the narrative.
