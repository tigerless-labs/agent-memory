## Bathroom Renovation Project

**Planned scope:**
- Room size: 5x8 feet
- Design style: Spa-Inspired Retreat
- Key features:
  - Freestanding tub (keeping existing tub)
  - Large walk-in shower with linear drain
  - Double sink vanity
  - Spa-like ambiance

**Materials decisions:**
- Tile: Glazed porcelain for both walls and floor
  - Chosen for glossy finish, durability, stain resistance, ease of cleaning
  - Planning to use sanded grout for high-traffic areas
- Grout: Cement-based (all-purpose, affordable option)
  - Planning to seal after installation
  
**DIY approach:**
Planning to handle renovation work themselves, leveraging recent DIY experience (faucet repair, cabinet rearrangement). Will hire professionals for specialized plumbing and electrical work.

**Budget considerations:**
- Freestanding tub: $1,000-$3,000
- Walk-in shower components: $500-$2,000 per major item
- Tile and grout materials (variable by quantity)
- Professional labor for plumbing/electrical

**Project status:** Planning phase (as of 2024-02-20)
