Owns or works at a natural wine bar called **161** located in Sydenham, South East London. Also involved in marketing for a natural wine importer/distributor that imports wines like Benoit Landron's Naturlich.

Recently featured Naturlich 2021 pet-nat by the glass for spring service — the 2021 vintage is 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac and 20% Folle Blanche (due to frost damage that year). Notes describe it as having fleshy stone-fruit notes.
