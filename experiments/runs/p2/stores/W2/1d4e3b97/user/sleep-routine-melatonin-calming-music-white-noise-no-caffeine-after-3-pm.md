Established bedtime routine to address sleep issues:
- Takes melatonin 30 minutes before bed (started ~2 weeks before 2024-02-20)
- Falls asleep within 15–20 minutes of lying down with melatonin
- Reads 20–30 minutes before bed
- Listens to calming music or nature sounds while reading
- Uses white noise machine to block outside noise
- Gets ready for bed earlier than before

Sensitive to afternoon caffeine — used to drink coffee/tea around 4–5 PM but realized it affected sleep quality. Now limits caffeine intake in afternoons.

Target bedtime: 10 pm on weekdays, 10:30 pm on weekends. Typical wake time: ~7 am.
