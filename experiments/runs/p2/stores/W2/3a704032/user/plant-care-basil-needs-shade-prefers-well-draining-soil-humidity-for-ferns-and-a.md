Basil plant: on balcony, struggling with direct sunlight, needs shadier spot with filtered/indirect light, prefers well-draining potting mix with peat moss, perlite, compost. Ferns and African violets: need 50-60 percent humidity, planning to use humidifier (considering Levoit LV600HH, Honeywell HUL520W, or similar). Snake plants: low-maintenance, do well with sparse watering. Orchids: need high phosphorus fertilizer, no general-purpose fertilizer.
