Fern has tiny moving dot pests (likely spider mites or springtails). Treatment plan: 1) Apply neem oil spray per product instructions. 2) Mist fern 2-3 times weekly with distilled/filtered water in morning. 3) Mist undersides of leaves. 4) Maintain humidity 40-60 percent. 5) Monitor closely after treatment. May need to repeat treatment if pests persist. Isolate plant to prevent spread to other plants.
