Setting up music room in garage. Key tasks: 1) Soundproofing (seal gaps, add mass-loaded vinyl, acoustic panels, decouple walls/ceilings, consider drop ceiling). 2) Organization (vertical storage, wall-mounted racks for guitars, shelves for equipment, maximize corner space). 3) Video recording setup (improve lighting, audio microphone positioning, use phone camera on tripod). First video will be ukulele cover song in new music room.
