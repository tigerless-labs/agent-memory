Online platforms to explore for emerging artists and contemporary art: Artsy (vast collection, dedicated emerging artist section), Saatchi Art (focuses on emerging artists), Artspace (curated contemporary art), Uprise Art (emerging artists focus), Behance (artist portfolios), Juxtapoz (art magazine with artist profiles), Hyperallergic (art blog with emerging artist coverage), New American Paintings (US emerging painters), ArtNet (artist news and profiles). Features: personalized art advisory, curated collections, artist spotlights, online events and webinars.
