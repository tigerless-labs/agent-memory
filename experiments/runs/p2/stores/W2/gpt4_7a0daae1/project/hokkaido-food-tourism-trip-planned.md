User planning a food-focused trip to Hokkaido, Japan (2023).

**Food interests:**
- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton)
- Kaisendon (fresh sashimi rice bowls)
- Jingisukan (grilled lamb with soy sauce)
- Local dairy products (milk, butter, cheese, ice cream)
- Uni (sea urchin) ikura (salmon roe) don
- Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)

**Places to visit:**
- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) - fresh seafood
- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho - local ramen varieties
- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) - local cuisine and souvenirs
- Restaurants: Daruma (Sapporo), Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa), Yurigakuro (Sapporo), Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru)

**Transportation options being considered:**
- Rental car
- JR Hokkaido trains
- Bus network
- Bicycle for rural areas
- Guided food tours
