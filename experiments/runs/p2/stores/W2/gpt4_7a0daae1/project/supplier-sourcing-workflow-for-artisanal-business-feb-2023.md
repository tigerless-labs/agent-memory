In late February 2023, initiated formal supplier sourcing process for artisanal products. Developed supplier contract templates covering: scope of work, pricing/payment, delivery/shipping, quality standards, IP, term/termination, liability, dispute resolution, confidentiality. Also established processes for:\n\n- **Price negotiation**: Research market rates, know requirements, build relationships, be transparent about budget, look for value-added services, use competition as leverage\n- **Supplier evaluation**: Assess quality, reliability, pricing, customer service, financial stability, supply chain visibility, certifications, innovation\n- **Onboarding**: Create supplier profile, establish communication channels, conduct training, perform testing/QA, set up payment terms, establish KPIs\n- **Quality control**: Define quality standards, identify critical control points, implement regular inspections, establish corrective action process, monitor KPIs\n\nWas in touch with multiple supplier candidates as of late February 2023.
