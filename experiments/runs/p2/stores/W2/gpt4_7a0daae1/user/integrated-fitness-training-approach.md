Building a comprehensive fitness routine combining: strength training (squats, lunges, deadlifts, core work), cardio (HIIT, steady-state running/cycling), and flexibility (stretching, yoga). Prefers progressive overload with lighter weights increasing gradually. Does post-lesson stretching after Saturday tennis. Incorporates cross-training (tennis, running, cycling) for balanced fitness and injury prevention. Focus on proper form and technique over quantity.
