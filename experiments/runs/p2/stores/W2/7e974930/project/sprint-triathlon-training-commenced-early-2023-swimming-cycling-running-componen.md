**Progress tracking**: Used spreadsheet to log workouts, metrics, and analysis

- Resting heart rate decreased by 10 beats per minute
- Felt stronger, faster, and more confident

**Tools used**: Strava, Swim.com, Garmin for tracking

The training combined endurance sets, interval training, pyramid workouts, and swim-specific drills. Also incorporated strength training and technique work.
