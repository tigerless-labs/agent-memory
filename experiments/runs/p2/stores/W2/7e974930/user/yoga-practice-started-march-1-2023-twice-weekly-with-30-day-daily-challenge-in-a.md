Adopted regular yoga practice for triathlon training support. 

**Timeline**:
- Started: March 1, 2023 (twice weekly, 30-45 minute sessions)
- April 2023: Completed 30-day yoga challenge (daily practice, including on triathlon rest days)

**Benefits experienced**:
- Improved flexibility and range of motion
- Enhanced recovery
- Better body awareness
- Reduced muscle tension
- Improved hip and shoulder mobility (key areas for triathletes)

**Approach**: Focus on beginner-friendly styles (Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin) with poses targeting shoulders, hips, IT band. Used yoga as active recovery on easy training days.

The 30-day challenge was described as 'tough but really helpful with overall recovery and flexibility.'
