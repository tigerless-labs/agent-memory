Maintains daily cleaning routine: making bed, wiping down kitchen counters each morning. Weekly tasks: vacuuming, tidying living room. One deeper cleaning task per week (e.g., organizing closet, cleaning fridge). Reports noticeable improvement in mental clarity and feeling more calm/relaxed before bed. Evening tidying challenging when exhausted after long day, but persists because of mental health benefits. Emphasizes importance of creating good habits for long-term well-being.
