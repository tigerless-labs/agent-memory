**Design Plan:**
- Floating desk positioned in middle of room (maximizes space and creates openness)
- Built-in shelving for storage and organization
- Minimalist aesthetic with emphasis on natural wood accents
- Incorporates greenery (small herb garden or fresh flower vase for visual appeal and air quality)

**Lighting:**
- Pendant light above desk (sleek, modern design for sophistication)
- Wooden desk lamp with simple shade (minimalist, complements wood accents)
- Task lighting for focused workspace
- Prefers warm color temperature for calming atmosphere

**Furnishings & Materials:**
- Natural wood accents throughout
- Clean, simple lines for minimalist look

**Motivation:** Designed to maximize productivity and comfort for working from home; also enhances the bedroom's ambiance.
