Recently completed (as of December 2022) a 10-day road trip to Yellowstone National Park from California by RV with family of five. Described as 'quite the adventure' and 'a blast.'

**Attractions and interests:**
- Geothermal features: Old Faithful, Grand Geyser, Castle Geyser, Daisy Geyser, Mammoth Hot Springs, Norris Geyser Basin, Boiling River
- Wildlife viewing: Bears (especially grizzly bears), focus on Lamar Valley, Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area
- Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone River: Upper and Lower Falls, trails, scenic drives
- Geology and natural formations

**Travel style:**
- RV as both transportation and accommodation for family road trip
- Family-friendly activities suitable for children
- Interest in hiking and scenic viewpoints
- Willingness to stay multiple days in one area

**Outcomes:**
- Positive family experience mentioned (fond memories, planning return trip)
- Generated interest in repeat visit to Yellowstone
