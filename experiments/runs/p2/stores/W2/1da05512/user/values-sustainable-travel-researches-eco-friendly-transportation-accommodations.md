Actively researches eco-friendly options including: sustainable transportation (public transit, bike-share, carpooling over ride-hailing), certified green hotels and eco-lodges, carbon offset programs with airlines, reef-safe sunscreen, reusable travel gear (water bottles, straws, cloth towels), sustainable travel apps for offsetting and finding zero-waste options. This reflects broader environmental values and commitment to sustainable tourism practices.
