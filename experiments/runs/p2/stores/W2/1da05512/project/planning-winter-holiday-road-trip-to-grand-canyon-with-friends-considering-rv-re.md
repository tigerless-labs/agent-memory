# Grand Canyon Road Trip

**Timeline**: Winter holidays (likely December 2023)

**Trip details**:
- Traveling with friends (group trip)
- Route: California → Flagstaff, AZ → Grand Canyon
- Planning to rent an RV for the journey
- Want to experience scenic drive from Flagstaff to Grand Canyon

**Accommodation/Camping**:
- Interested in reserving campsite with electric hookups at Grand Canyon
- Want to camp overnight and wake up to sunrise at the Grand Canyon
- Considering Mather Campground, Trailer Village RV Park, or Desert View Campground
- Need to reserve well in advance as sites fill quickly during peak season

**Preparation considerations**:
- Will need to winterize RV (check heating, plumbing, tires, fuel, propane)
- Plan winter emergency kit (shovel, ice scraper, chains, blankets, food/water)
- Check weather forecasts and road conditions regularly
- Be aware of agricultural checkpoints when entering Arizona
- May need America the Beautiful annual pass for national parks

**Recommendations for trip**:
- Restaurant options in Flagstaff: Late for the Train Cafe, Proper Meats + Provisions, Fat Olives, The Toasted Owl, Big Foot BBQ
- Hotel options in Flagstaff: Little America, Hotel Monte Vista, Flagstaff Inn and Suites, The Inn at Flagstaff

**Concerns**:
- Previously experienced heavy traffic on Highway 101; concerned about traffic for this drive
- Winter weather preparedness for high elevation travel
