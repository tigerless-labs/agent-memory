Currently uses external hard drive for backups. Evaluating NAS options for centralized backup of laptop and phone files. Priorities: automatic backups, data redundancy, strong encryption and access control, two-factor authentication support. Researched Synology, QNAP, Netgear, Asustor, and Western Digital NAS models in range from 2-bay to 8-bay configurations. Considering trade-offs between storage capacity, price, and redundancy features.
