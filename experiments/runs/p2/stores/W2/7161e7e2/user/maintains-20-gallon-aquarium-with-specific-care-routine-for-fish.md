## 20-gallon aquarium setup

**Tank status:**
- Set up approximately 3 months ago (started ~late February 2023)
- Spent significant time setting up tank and cycling water when first acquired
- Tank cycled and established for 3 months as of May 2023

**Feeding routine:**
- Feeds fish twice daily
- Primary diet: Tetra pellet food (staple)
- Supplements with frozen brine shrimp and frozen bloodworms
- Fish strongly prefer frozen brine shrimp (go crazy for it)
- Fish less responsive/finicky about frozen bloodworms
- Intentionally varying diet to ensure balanced nutrient intake

**Equipment:**
- Considering upgrading to canister filter
- Researching options: Hydor Professional, Eheim Classic 2211, Fluval 206
- Prioritizing: quiet operation, easy maintenance, adequate flow rate for 20-gallon tank

**Water care:**
- Focused on maintaining good water quality
- Familiar with importance of regular water changes after 3 months of experience
