Thesis on AI-powered adaptive learning systems in math and reading comprehension. Key findings: systems effective but implementation requires careful classroom integration. Main implementation challenges: teacher professional development needs, importance of curriculum alignment, concerns about algorithmic bias. Recommendations: prioritize teacher support, developers work with educators, ongoing evaluation. Presented at Harvard conference summer 2023, received positive feedback. Some developers interested in findings.
