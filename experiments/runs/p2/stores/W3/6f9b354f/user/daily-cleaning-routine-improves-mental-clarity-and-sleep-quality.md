Maintains daily cleaning routine: making bed and wiping kitchen counters daily. Weekly: vacuuming and tidying living room. One deeper task per week (e.g., organizing closet, cleaning fridge).

Notices significant mental clarity improvement and feels calm and relaxed before bed as a result.

Struggles with tidying in evenings when exhausted, but persists because of the mental health benefits.
