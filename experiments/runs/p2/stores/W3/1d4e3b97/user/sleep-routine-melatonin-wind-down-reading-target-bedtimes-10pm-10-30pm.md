Sleep routine established as of February 2024:
- Takes melatonin supplement 30 minutes before bed (falls asleep within 15-20 minutes)
- Reads for 20-30 minutes before bed as part of wind-down routine
- Listens to calming music or nature sounds while reading
- Uses white noise machine to block outside noise
- Target bedtime: 10 pm on weekdays, 10:30 pm on weekends
- Wake time: typically 7 am
- Herbal tea preference for relaxation in evenings (chamomile, lavender, valerian root)
- Limited caffeine intake in afternoons (stopped having coffee/tea around 4-5 pm)

This routine was developed to address sleep difficulties and screen time issues, with positive results.
