Friend named Amanda Van West. Created multiple 1920s Prohibition-era gangster-themed nicknames for them as a creative exercise, exploring gin and speakeasy themes.

Final preferred nickname: **Amanda 'The Westside Whisperer' Van Hooch, Queen of the Speakeasy and Gin-Running Racket.**

Also created variations including: The Gin-Running Vixen, Moonshine Mandy, The Duchess of Bootlegging and Danger, The Queen of the Prohibition Racket, and others using period vocabulary (hooch, moonshine, bathtub gin, bootlegger, rum-runner, speakeasy, etc.).
