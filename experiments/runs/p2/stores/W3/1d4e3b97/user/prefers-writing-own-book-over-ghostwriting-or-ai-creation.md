Considers writing one's own book preferable to ghostwriting or AI-generated content. Values:

- **Personal fulfillment** — Writing as empowering, shared experience
- **Ownership and control** — Complete control over content, tone, and message
- **Authenticity** — True representation of self; builds credibility and trust with readers
- **Skill development** — Improves writing ability and refines personal voice
- **Emotional connection** — Creates deeper, more meaningful reader engagement

Views ghostwriting and AI alternatives as reducing authenticity, personal fulfillment, and audience connection.
