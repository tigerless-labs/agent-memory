161 is a natural wine bar located in Sydenham, South East London that the user owns/operates. They serve dishes like Mushroom & Oloroso paté with pickled cabbage, pickled cucumber and fried bread. They import and distribute natural wines, including Benoit Landron's 'Naturlich' pet-nat. In February 2024, they began serving the 2021 Naturlich by the glass as a spring wine.
