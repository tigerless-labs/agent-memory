Developing a game campaign featuring the lost city of Eldorado. Designed puzzle types include:

- **Mirror Puzzle**: Large mirror with etched symbols; players must position themselves/symbols to reveal hidden passages
- **Elemental Puzzle**: Levers and buttons controlling earth/fire/air/water elements; correct sequence reveals hidden passages
- **Musical Puzzle**: Sheet music with instrument symbols; playing correct note sequence unlocks hidden doors
- **Weight Puzzle**: Series of weights and scales; proper arrangement reveals hidden passages
- **Memory Puzzle**: Memorize object locations before lights go out; navigate dark room to retrieve specific items

Puzzles designed to challenge different skill levels and keep players engaged.
