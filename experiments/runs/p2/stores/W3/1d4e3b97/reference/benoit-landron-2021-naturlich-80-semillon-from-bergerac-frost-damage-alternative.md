Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat wine details:
- Due to severe frost in 2021, 80% is made with Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac (normally would be Folle Blanche)
- Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the blend
- Described as a 'spring wine'
- Served by the glass at 161 wine bar starting February 2024

User notes: 'it's pretty delicious' and appreciated as 'a fun variation on a theme'
