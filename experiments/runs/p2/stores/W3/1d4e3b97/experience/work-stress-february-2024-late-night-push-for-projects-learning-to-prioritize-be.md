Work experience and learning, February 2024:

Situation: Had to stay up until 1 am for several nights in a row to finish work projects, which disrupted sleep schedule and evening routines.

Response: Recognized this as unsustainable and began working on better task prioritization to prevent future late-night work marathons.

Strategies adopted:
- Using Eisenhower Matrix for task categorization
- Breaking large tasks into smaller, manageable chunks
- Focusing on impact-driven task selection
- Avoiding multitasking
- Setting realistic deadlines with 10-20% buffer
- Learning to say no to tasks that don't align with priorities
- Using time-blocking for focused work

Reflection: Recognizes that work-life balance is essential for maintaining good sleep habits and overall wellness. Working to establish boundaries around work hours.
