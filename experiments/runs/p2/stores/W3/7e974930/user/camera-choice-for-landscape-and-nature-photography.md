Chose Fujifilm X-T30 II for landscape and nature photography after researching Sony and Fujifilm mirrorless options. Drawn to compact size, vari-angle touchscreen LCD, and Intelligent Hybrid AF system. Budget was $1,500 for body and lens. Prioritizes landscape/nature genres, doesn't have existing lenses, not concerned about weather sealing (moisture-resistant is sufficient), compact size preferred.
