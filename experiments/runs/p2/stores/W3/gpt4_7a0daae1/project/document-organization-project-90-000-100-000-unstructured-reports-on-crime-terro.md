Project involves organizing and analyzing 90,000-100,000 unstructured documents (1-5 pages each) with minimal structured data. Topics: international crime, terrorism, politics. Approach: text cleaning (removing special characters, stop words, normalization), document parsing (title, author, date, body), entity extraction (NER for names/organizations/locations), topic modeling to identify themes and patterns, sentiment analysis to track tone and emotions, document classification by content category. Goals: enable quick search/filtering by topics and themes, identify patterns and trends, track changes in sentiment over time.
