Building comprehensive strength training routine to improve tennis and running performance. Core exercises (2-3 times/week): planks (30-60 sec holds), Russian twists, leg raises, bicycle crunches. Leg exercises (2-3 times/week): squats, lunges, calf raises, deadlifts. Plyometric exercises (2-3 times/week): box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds, tuck jumps, medicine ball throws. Committed to: starting with lighter weights and progressing gradually, focusing on proper form and technique, incorporating multi-muscle exercises for efficiency, allowing 48-72 hours recovery between sessions, combining with cardio (running/tennis) and flexibility work (stretching/yoga). Plans to increase weight by 2.5-5 lbs every two weeks as progress allows.
