Owns and maintains a 20-gallon aquarium. Tank has been established for approximately 3 months (as of May 2023). Spent significant time on initial setup and water cycling.

**Filtration:** Upgraded to Hydor Professional Canister Filter. Familiar with importance of regular water changes and maintaining water quality.

**Fish diet & feeding:**
- Feeds fish twice daily
- Staple: Tetra pellet food
- Supplements: Frozen brine shrimp (fish favorite), frozen bloodworms (less preferred)
- Intentionally varies diet for balanced nutrient intake
- Fish most enthusiastic about frozen brine shrimp

**Biological media:** Uses biological media basket in canister filter for beneficial bacteria colonization. Cleans/maintains media regularly.
