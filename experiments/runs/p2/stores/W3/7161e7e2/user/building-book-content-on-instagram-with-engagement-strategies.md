Creating book-themed Instagram content focused on:
- Quarterly book recommendations post (title: 'Quarterly Faves & Wants')
- Using varied, genre-specific hashtags (prefers #bookrecs over #bookrecommendations)
- Planning Instagram Stories for behind-the-scenes content and chat preparation
- Planning IGTV for in-depth book reviews and author interviews
- Using Instagram polls to gauge follower preferences for content types (behind-the-scenes, Q&A, book reviews)
- Regularly asking followers engagement questions about reading preferences and habits
- Strategy: mix popular and niche hashtags rather than repeating same hashtags across all posts
