Values reducing carbon footprint while traveling. Interested in:
- Eco-friendly transportation alternatives (public transit, cycling, electric/hybrid options, carpooling)
- Sustainable accommodations and eco-lodges
- Airlines offering carbon offset programs
- Eco-friendly travel gear (reusable water bottles, sustainable fabrics, solar chargers)
- Waste reduction while traveling (zero-waste travel apps, refill stations)
- Offsetting carbon emissions from flights and transportation

Uses travel apps to plan sustainable routes and find green accommodations.
