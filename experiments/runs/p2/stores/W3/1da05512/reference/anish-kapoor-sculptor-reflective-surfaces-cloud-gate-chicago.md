Anish Kapoor is a renowned sculptor known for large scale abstract installations with reflective surfaces. His work Cloud Gate at Millennium Park in Chicago was installed in 2004. It is a stainless steel sculpture made of 168 panels weighing over 110 tons. The mirror like reflective surface creates interactive visual effects where the environment is distorted and warped.
