Recommended restaurants in Flagstaff: Late for the Train Cafe breakfast and lunch, Proper Meats and Provisions deli, Fat Olives Italian restaurant, The Toasted Owl brunch spot, Big Foot BBQ. Accommodations: Little America Flagstaff hotel with pool, Hotel Monte Vista historic hotel, Flagstaff Inn and Suites budget option, The Inn at Flagstaff B and B, or camping at Flagstaff KOA.
