Decided on Vogelsang High Sierra Camp for first high-elevation backpacking trip in September 2023 instead of more difficult High Sierra Camp Loop.

**Trip details:**
- Location: Yosemite National Park, Sierra Nevada
- Elevation: 10,300 feet
- Distance: 20 miles
- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate (good intro to high-elevation backpacking)

**Access:**
- 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows trailhead
- Shuttle bus option from Tuolumne Meadows Visitor Center

**Accommodations:**
- Four-person tent cabins with wooden floors, canvas walls, bunk beds with mattresses
- Full-service dining program (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Modern restrooms with flush toilets and showers
- Camp store available
- Solar-powered lighting in cabins

**Logistics:**
- Requires permit and reservations through Yosemite Conservancy
- Need to book early (spots fill quickly)
- Pack should be ~40-50 lbs for 3-4 day trip
- Weather: potential snow/ice on higher passes in September, prepare for thunderstorms
