## Creative Project: Black Mirror Scene

**Concept**: Girl sits at computer, slowly disappears into it before speaking into microphone.

**Visual style**: Dark, unsettling—Black Mirror aesthetic

**Elements of interest**:
- Glitchy/distorted visual effects on computer interface
- Eerie music and sound design (haunting melody, discordant sounds, white noise)
- Disorienting camera movements, flashes of light
- Twist revealing darker side of technology (trapped in digital realm, malevolent computer)

**Status**: Early concept phase (as of May 21, 2023)
