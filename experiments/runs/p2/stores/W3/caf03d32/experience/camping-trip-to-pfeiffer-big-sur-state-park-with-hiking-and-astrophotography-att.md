Camped at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park for two nights. Hiked to McWay Falls on day one and Pfeiffer Beach on day two. Tried cooking camping food for the first time during the trip (reported as okay). Attempted astrophotography during the trip but results weren't as good as hoped—still learning night photography skills and plans to improve for next trip. Brought a portable power bank which was helpful for device charging, navigation, and capturing photos/memories.
