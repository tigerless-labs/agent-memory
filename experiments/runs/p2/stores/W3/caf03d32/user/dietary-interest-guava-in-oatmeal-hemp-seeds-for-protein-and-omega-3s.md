Looking to boost protein intake to support strength training routine.

**Guava:**
- Planning to add to morning oatmeal for protein and fiber boost
- Suggested ratio: 1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal
- Interested in using fresh guava when available

**Hemp seeds:**
- Recommended amount: 1-2 tablespoons per serving
- Provides ~5-10g protein and 1-2g omega-3s per serving
- Stores in airtight container in fridge or freezer to prevent rancidity
- Can grind before adding to oatmeal for better mixing

**Other protein/nutrition interests:**
- High-protein lunch ideas (40g+ protein targets)
- Interested in exploring guavas and durian as protein sources
- Open to creative oatmeal toppings: walnuts, almonds, pecans, chia seeds, flaxseeds, coconut flakes, cinnamon, cocoa nibs
