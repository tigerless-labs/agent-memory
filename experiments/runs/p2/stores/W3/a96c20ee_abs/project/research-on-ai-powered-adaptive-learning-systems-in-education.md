Thesis research focused on effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension. Key findings: systems can be effective in improving outcomes but implementation and integration into the classroom require careful consideration.

Common implementation challenges identified:
- Teacher professional development and training gaps
- Need for curriculum alignment
- Teacher concerns about job replacement
- Potential algorithmic bias affecting equity

Also explored systems' potential to reduce achievement gaps and support struggling students, with recommendations for educators and policymakers to prioritize ongoing teacher professional development, have developers work closely with educators on system design, and conduct ongoing evaluation/assessment of systems.

Presented as poster at first research conference (summer 2023). Received positive feedback from educators, policymakers, and developers.
