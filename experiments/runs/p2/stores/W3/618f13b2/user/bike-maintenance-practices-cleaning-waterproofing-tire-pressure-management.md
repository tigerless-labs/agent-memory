**Bike maintenance approach** (as of May 2023):

- **Cleaning**: Plans regular cleaning of bikes, especially wheels; prefers gentle, pH-neutral bike wash soap (brands: Finish Line, Pedro's, Muc-Off); uses soft-bristled brushes and microfiber cloths to avoid scratching
- **Waterproofing**: Plans to apply waterproofing to chain and gears using products like chain lubes with waterproofing properties or specialized waterproofing sprays
- **Tire pressure**: For Trek FX 2 hybrid bike on rough trails, sets pressure to approximately **65 PSI**; general range for trail riding 60–80 PSI depending on terrain
