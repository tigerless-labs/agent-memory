Listens to audiobooks during their daily commute. Strongly prefers full-cast narrations for dialogue-heavy novels with multiple characters, as it helps them keep track of who is speaking and adds to the immersive experience. Also enjoys single narrators with unique tone and style.

Has completed two audiobooks as of late May 2023:
- "Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind" by Yuval Noah Harari
- "Daisy Jones and The Six" by Taylor Jenkins Reid (full-cast narration recommended)
