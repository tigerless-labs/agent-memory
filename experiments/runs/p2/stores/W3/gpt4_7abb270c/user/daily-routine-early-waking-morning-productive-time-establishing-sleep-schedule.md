**Wake time**: Wakes up early, typically around 5:30-6:00 am. Values the extra morning time before work.

**Morning routine**: Uses morning hours to get personal projects/errands done. Finds this helpful for setting productive tone for the day.

**Sleep goals**: 
- Working to establish consistent sleep schedule to improve time management
- Developing wind-down routine (30-60 minutes before bed)
- Interested in meditation, deep breathing exercises, and reading as relaxation techniques
- Concerned about post-lunch energy levels; seeking lunch options to avoid afternoon sleepiness

**Afternoon energy**: Researching healthy lunch ideas (protein-rich, complex carbs, avoiding heavy foods) to prevent post-lunch slump and maintain afternoon productivity.

**Consistency preference**: Attempting to build sustainable habits but with flexibility; not rigid about daily adherence.
