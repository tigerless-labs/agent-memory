Listens to audiobooks via Libro.fm during daily commute (~1 hour each direction). Satisfied with narrator quality on the platform. Experiments with playback speed: typically starts at 1x (normal) for non-fiction that requires reflection, willing to adjust to 1.25x-1.5x for narrative-driven fiction. Notes that slower speed better for absorbing and applying concepts from self-improvement books.
