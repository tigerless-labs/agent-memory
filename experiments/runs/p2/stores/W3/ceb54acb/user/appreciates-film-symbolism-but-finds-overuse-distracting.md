Interested in cinematography and film symbolism. Appreciates how filmmakers use symbolic imagery to convey deeper meaning (e.g., color, recurring objects). However, believes symbolism can become overused and distracting when filmmakers try too hard to be symbolic. Thinks heavy-handed or excessive symbolism can detract from the story and make films seem contrived or pretentious.
