Observed personal pattern: prefers to shop on weekends when having more free time. Recently became aware that online shopping has been adding up, leading to a decision to cut down spending. Plans to:
- Track expenses more closely
- Set a budget
- Implement a 'one in, one out' policy: get rid of an old item every time bringing a new one into their life

This approach aims to be more mindful of purchases and avoid clutter.
