Made first attempt at kimchi around May 14-15, 2023. The batch was unsuccessful—too salty and too spicy. Learning from the mistake and planning adjustments: Use less gochugaru (Korean chili flakes); rinse finished kimchi under cold water to reduce salt; add garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables) and ginger (1-2 inches per 2 cups) to balance flavors. Key lesson: start with small amounts and taste as you go. Chose gochugaru as the preferred chili type for balanced, medium-to-hot heat and good flavor profile.
