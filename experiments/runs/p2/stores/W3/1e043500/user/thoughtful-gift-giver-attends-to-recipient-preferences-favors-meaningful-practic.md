## Gift-Giving Style
- **Approach**: Thoughtful and intentional; researches recipient preferences before deciding
- **Budget consciousness**: Flexible budgets ($70-$75 typical range for meaningful gifts)
- **Selection criteria**: Combination of sentimentality and practicality

- **Budget**: ~$70
- **Outcome**: Recipient loved it; felt special
- **Insight**: Best friend appreciates silver jewelry

## Decision-Making Process
1. Gathers information about recipient's style, interests, and preferences
2. Researches options with detailed material and quality information
3. Chooses items that combine utility with personalization
4. Considers long-term value and meaningfulness, not just initial impression
