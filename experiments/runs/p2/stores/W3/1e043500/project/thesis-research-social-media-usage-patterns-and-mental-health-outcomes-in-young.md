## Research Focus
Studying the impact of social media on mental health in young adults.

### Primary Research Question
How social media usage patterns are associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in the young adult population.

### Key Variables
- **Mediators explored**: Self-esteem and social support as potential mediating factors in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes
- **Outcomes studied**: Depression symptoms, anxiety symptoms, psychological distress, mental wellbeing
- **Target population**: Young adults (18-25 years), emerging adults, adolescents

### Research Scope
- Investigating effectiveness of social media-based interventions and policies for mental health promotion
- Exploring mental health awareness campaigns on social media
- Examining online support groups and peer-to-peer support initiatives
- Potential implications for mental health interventions and policies

### Status & Feedback
Submitted thesis proposal in early May 2023 and received positive feedback from advisor shortly after (~May 13, 2023), providing strong motivation to proceed.
