Core brand ethos: hiring great human beings first, then great technologists second, rather than pure technical capability.

Key differentiators:
- Emphasis on critical thinking and psychometric testing in hiring
- Value EQ (emotional intelligence) over IQ for software developers
- Focus on soft skills, collaboration, and team dynamics
- Content strategy targets managers, team leads, and hiring decision-makers

Planned content formats:
- Blog posts on hiring practices and team building
- Podcasts/videos with industry experts and successful leaders
- Webinars and workshops on hiring and team development
- In-depth guides and e-books on psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation
- Social media presence for thought leadership and community engagement

Target niches identified:
1. Remote work best practices
2. Remote team building
3. Remote leadership
4. Remote hiring and onboarding
5. Team dynamics and collaboration
6. Psychometric testing and soft skills assessment
