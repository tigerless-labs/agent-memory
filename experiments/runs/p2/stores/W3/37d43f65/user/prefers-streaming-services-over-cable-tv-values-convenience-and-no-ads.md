As of May 2023, hasn't watched cable TV in months. Prefers streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) for:
- Variety of content and access to shows not available on cable
- No commercials/ad-free viewing
- Ability to pause, rewind, fast-forward and resume where they left off
- Mobile viewing on phone/tablet while on the go
- Binge-watching entire seasons at once rather than waiting weekly
- Personalized recommendations based on viewing history

Values convenience and flexibility in entertainment consumption.
