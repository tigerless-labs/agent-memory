**Date:** 2023-05-28

**Situation:** After upgrading Dell Inspiron 15 5000 RAM from baseline to 16GB, battery life decreased from 6-7 hours to 4-5 hours.

**Investigation:** Additional RAM appears to increase power consumption. May need battery calibration (drain to 0%, charge to 100%, repeat 2-3 times) to resolve.

**Lesson:** RAM upgrades improve performance but may have trade-offs in power efficiency on older laptops. Battery calibration is a diagnostic step before assuming hardware issue.
