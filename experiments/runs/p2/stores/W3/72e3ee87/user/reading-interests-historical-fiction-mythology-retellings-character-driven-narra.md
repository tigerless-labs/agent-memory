## Reading Preferences

### Genres and Themes
- **Historical fiction** with strong female protagonists (especially WWII-era settings)
- **Mythology and folklore retellings** from diverse cultures (Greek, Chinese, Indian, Vietnamese, Latin American, African)
- Character-driven narratives with emotional depth and complexity
- Women's perspectives, agency, and resilience during conflict
- Intimate character relationships and inner lives
- Rich, lyrical prose with historical authenticity and careful research

### Style Preferences
- **Dislikes excessive descriptive passages** that slow the narrative—ornate description of setting/nature should serve the characters and plot, not distract from them (observed during discussion of *Wuthering Heights*, Apr 2023)
- Prefers prose that balances lyricism with storytelling momentum
- Values poetic language when it deepens character insight or mood, not when it reads like a "travel guide"

### Favorite Authors
- **Kristin Hannah** — *The Nightingale* (completed Jan 31, 2023)
- **Madeline Miller** — *The Song of Achilles* (classical knowledge, lyrical prose, character intimacy)
- **Kate Quinn** — *The Alice Network* (historical depth, overlooked women's stories)
- **R.F. Kuang** — *The Poppy War* (cultural depth, complex mythology)
- **Roshani Chokshi** — *The Star-Touched Queen* (lyrical cross-cultural storytelling)

### Books Read (as of Jan 2023)
- *The Nightingale* by Kristin Hannah (finished Jan 31, 2023)
- *The Poppy War* by R.F. Kuang
- *The Star-Touched Queen* by Roshani Chokshi
- *The Song of Achilles* by Madeline Miller (in progress mid-Jan 2023, halfway through)

### Reading Habits
- Prefers reading in short sessions before bed to unwind
- Values lyrical, evocative prose and character investment
- Enjoys getting absorbed in emotional arcs and complex relationships
