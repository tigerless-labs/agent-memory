Experimenting with cocktail creation using fresh garden herbs (basil, thyme, mint). Interested in lavender syrup recipe (1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + 1/4 cup dried lavender buds, steeped 10-15 min, refrigerated up to 2 weeks). Exploring citrus options for gin cocktails including: orange/blood orange, yuzu, kumquat, satsuma, bergamot. Learning to balance citrus so it doesn't overpower other flavors.
