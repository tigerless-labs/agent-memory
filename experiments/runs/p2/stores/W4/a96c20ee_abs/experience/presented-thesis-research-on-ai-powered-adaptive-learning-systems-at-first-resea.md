Presented poster research at first research conference (over summer 2023). The research explored the effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes.

**Research Focus:**
- Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in math and reading comprehension
- Implementation and integration challenges in classrooms
- Broader concept exploration with specific platforms and systems as case studies

**Key Findings:**
- These systems can be effective in improving student learning outcomes
- Implementation and integration need careful consideration
- Common challenge: need for ongoing teacher professional development and training
- Importance of clear curriculum alignment with system design
- Potential to reduce achievement gaps and support struggling students
- Concerns about algorithmic bias potentially exacerbating inequities

**Recommendations Made:**
- Prioritize teacher professional development and ongoing support
- Developers should work closely with educators on curriculum alignment
- Need for ongoing evaluation and assessment of systems

**Conference Reception:**
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers
- Many appreciated the nuanced approach to challenges and opportunities
- Developers reached out afterward interested in applying findings
- Encountered some disagreement about teacher replacement concerns
