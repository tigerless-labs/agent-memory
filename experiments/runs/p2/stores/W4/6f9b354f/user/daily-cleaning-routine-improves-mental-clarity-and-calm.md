Maintains a daily cleaning routine (making bed, wiping down kitchen counters) and weekly tasks (vacuuming, tidying living room). Also tackles one deeper cleaning task per week (organizing closet, cleaning fridge). Finds it challenging to tidy in evenings when exhausted, but the routine helps create a sense of calm and relaxation before bed. Recognizes that building good habits and systems makes daily life easier long-term.
