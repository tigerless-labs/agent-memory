Decided to incorporate AI into social media strategy after learning at a recent industry conference that AI will play a significant role in the tech industry in coming years. Plans to: 1) Define brand voice and tone first, 2) Experiment with AI-generated content tools while maintaining human curation, 3) Use AI for research and topic generation. Planning to try BuzzSumo and Ahrefs for topic suggestion and keyword research, and explore Answer Engine and Quora for insights and inspiration. Concerned about maintaining authenticity and brand voice with AI-generated content.
