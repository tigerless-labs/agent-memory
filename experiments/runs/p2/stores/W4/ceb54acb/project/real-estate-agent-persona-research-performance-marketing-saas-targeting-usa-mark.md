**Project:** Real estate agent persona development for performance marketing SaaS

**Market Focus:**
- USA real estate agents
- Data source: NAR (National Association of Realtors) research and statistics

**Target Persona (median profile):**
- Age: mid-50s
- Gender: Female representation significant  
- Location: suburban or rural areas
- Income: $61,140 median (2019)
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

**Personality Profile:**
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable and continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with local market knowledge

**Pain Points:**
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking/analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents and brokerages
- Keeping current with real estate technology

**Goals:**
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Differentiate from competition
- Streamline marketing efforts

**Technology Usage:**
- CRM software
- Email marketing tools
- Social media (personal and professional)
- Open to new technology adoption

**Marketing Channels:**
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking events and referrals
- Direct mail and email marketing
- Social media advertising

**Budget Behavior:**
- Cost-conscious but quality-focused
- ROI-driven investment decisions
- Leverages brokerage marketing resources
