Collects across multiple categories:

**Vintage cameras** — Started collecting in ~Feb 2023; had 17 cameras as of May 2023, including a Brownie Hawkeye acquired May 2023. Values proper storage at stable temperature (~65-70°F) and humidity (~50%), acid-free containers, regular maintenance/cleaning, and organized display. Interested in cataloging and preserving condition.

**Funko POP figures** — Large collection including the complete Marvel Cinematic Universe set. Prefers display in tiered/compartmentalized shelving with dust protection, organized by series/theme.

**Vinyl records** — Collector with focus on rare pressings. Owns a 1978 pressing of Fleetwood Mac's Rumours (acquired by May 2023). Stores upright, handles by edges, values clean/dry environment.

**Posters and art prints** — Collects Mondo and fantasy art posters, including a Hogwarts castle (Harry Potter) Mondo poster acquired May 2023. Prefers acid-free storage in tubes/flat files, framing with UV-filtering glass.

**General approach:** Values proper storage, maintenance, organization, and themed/accessible display across all collections. Interested in preservation and presentation.
