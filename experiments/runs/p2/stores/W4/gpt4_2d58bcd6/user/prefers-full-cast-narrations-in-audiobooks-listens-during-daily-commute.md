Listens to audiobooks during daily commute. Prefers full-cast narrations, especially for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes, as they add to the immersive experience and help track who's speaking. Also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style to stories.

Recently completed audiobooks:
- Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind by Yuval Noah Harari
- Daisy Jones and The Six by Taylor Jenkins Reid
