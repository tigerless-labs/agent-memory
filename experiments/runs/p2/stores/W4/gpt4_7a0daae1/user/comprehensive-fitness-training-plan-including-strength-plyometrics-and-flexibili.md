Building integrated fitness routine combining:\n- **Strength training**: Core (planks, Russian twists, leg raises, bicycle crunches) and legs (squats, lunges, calf raises, deadlifts) 2-3 times/week\n- **Plyometric exercises**: Box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds, tuck jumps, medicine ball throws 2-3 times/week\n- **Stretching and flexibility**: Regular post-lesson stretching, focusing on neck, shoulders, chest, calves, hamstrings, quads, hip flexors, forearms, rotator cuff\n- **Cardio**: HIIT, steady-state, hill sprints incorporated 2-4 times/week\n- **Recovery**: 48-72 hours rest between strength sessions\n\nApproach: gradual progression with lighter weights, focus on proper form, consistent practice.
