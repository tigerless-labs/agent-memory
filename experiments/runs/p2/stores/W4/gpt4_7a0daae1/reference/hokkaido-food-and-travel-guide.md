Hokkaido regional dishes: Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb), Kaisendon (fresh sashimi over rice), Jingisukan, Uni and Ikura don (sea urchin and salmon roe), Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot), local ice cream. Recommended food locations: Nijo Fish Market, Sapporo Ramen Yokocho, Tanukikoji Shopping Street, Daruma and Kitaichi Hall for jingisukan. Transportation: trains via JR Hokkaido, renting car, or cycling.
