Aquarium setup: 20-gallon tank established for 3 months (as of May 2023). Water cycle fully established. Fish diet: Tetra pellet food as staple, supplemented with frozen brine shrimp (fish favorite) and frozen bloodworms. Feeding schedule: twice daily. Fish are more finicky about bloodworms than brine shrimp. Planning upgrade: Hydor Professional Canister Filter for improved biological and mechanical filtration. Interested in San Francisco Bay Brand frozen brine shrimp and other high-quality food supplements.
