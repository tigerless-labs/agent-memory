Established sleep routine works well: Takes melatonin 30 minutes before bed (started ~2024-02-06), falls asleep within 15-20 minutes. Reads for 20-30 minutes before bed, listens to calming music or nature sounds. Uses white noise machine to block external noise. Bedtime targets: 10 pm on weekdays, 10:30 pm on weekends. Wake time typically 7 am. Avoids caffeine after 4 pm. Drinks herbal tea in evenings (chamomile, lavender preferred for relaxation). Successfully reduced evening screen time.
