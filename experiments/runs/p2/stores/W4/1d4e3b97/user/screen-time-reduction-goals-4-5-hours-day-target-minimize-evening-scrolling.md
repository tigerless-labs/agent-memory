Working to reduce screen time and manage usage mindfully. Current average: 4-5 hours daily (smartphone and laptop). Main challenge: mindless scrolling in evenings, especially 7-8 pm and before bed. Strategy: designate screen-free dinner time, establish wind-down routine without screens, alternate Netflix with screen-free activities (reading, journaling, stretching). Top apps used: social media, email, Netflix (uses Netflix as unwinding tool after work).
