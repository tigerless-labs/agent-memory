Ryan owns and operates a natural wine bar called 161 located in Sydenham, South East London. Signature dish: Mushroom & Oloroso paté with pickled cabbage, pickled cucumber, and fried bread. Carries natural wines including Benoit Landron's Naturlich pet-nat (2021, made 80% Semillon from organic Bergerac grower due to frost damage). Also imports and distributes natural wine.
