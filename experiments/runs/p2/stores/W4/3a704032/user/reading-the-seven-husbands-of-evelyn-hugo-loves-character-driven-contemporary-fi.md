Started reading **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo** by Taylor Jenkins Reid on 2026-01-29. Very engaged with the story.

**What draws them in:** Character development and interconnected storylines. Evelyn's backstory as a Cuban immigrant and how it shaped her. Exploration of public vs. private self/persona. Complex multifaceted relationships between women (Evelyn, Celia, Monique, Lucy). Intersectional storytelling about identity. Appreciation for Monique as an active narrator whose biases influence the story.

**Author:** Loves Taylor Jenkins Reid's writing — balances glamour with thought-provoking themes about celebrity, power dynamics, and human identity.

**Related interests:** Seeking contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists, complex relationships, exploration of identity, and character-driven narratives.
