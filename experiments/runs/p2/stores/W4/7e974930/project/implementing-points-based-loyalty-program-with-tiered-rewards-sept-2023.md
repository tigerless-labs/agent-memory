Decision: Implement a customer loyalty program to reward repeat customers and increase retention.

Program structure:
- **Type**: Points-based system where customers earn points for each purchase
- **Tiers**: Multiple tier levels with increasing exclusive rewards for frequent customers
- **Sign-up bonus**: Planning to offer incentive (e.g., welcome points, discount on first purchase, or free product) to encourage enrollment
- **Redemption**: Points redeemable for discounts or free products

Promotion strategy:
- Social media campaigns
- Email newsletters
- In-store promotions at market events
- Train staff to mention program at checkout with clear join instructions

Considering software: Square Loyalty, LoyaltyLion, Swell, or Belly for tracking and management.
