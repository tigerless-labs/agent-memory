Two expansion initiatives under consideration as of Sept 2023:

**Gift Baskets Initiative**:
- Create themed gift basket product line featuring best-selling soaps and candles
- Planned theme: "Downtown Farmers Market" basket to capitalize on brand recognition and customer nostalgia from popular market venue
- Target audience: Both locals with emotional connection to the market and tourists
- Value proposition: Thoughtful, curated collections that tell a story and evoke sense of place
- Planned extras: Personalized notes, market mementos (buttons/stickers), custom packaging, discount codes

**Artisanal Soap Expansion**:
- Expand product line to include more artisanal soap varieties
- Rationale: Recent sales strong, customer feedback positive, loyal customer base appreciates homemade quality
- Considerations to evaluate: customer demand, market trends, competitor analysis, production capabilities, pricing/margins

Both initiatives aligned with brand identity and existing customer base.
