When reading fiction like *Wuthering Heights*, prefers that setting be woven organically into plot and character development rather than described in extended detail. Tends to skim over lengthy descriptive passages about landscape/environment. Finds overly poetic or elaborate scene-setting distracting from the story and characters themselves. Appreciates that setting can enhance mood and reflect character emotion, but prefers subtle integration over explicit cataloguing of details.
