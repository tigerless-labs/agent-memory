Comfortable working extended hours during peak campaign/project seasons (typically adds ~10 hours per week to normal schedule). Views this as demonstrating:
- Adaptability and flexibility
- Strong time management and prioritization
- Ability to thrive in fast-paced environments
- Commitment to project success

Prefers phrasing work flexibility in terms of value delivery rather than just hours worked (e.g., 'managed high-volume workloads during peak seasons while maintaining high-quality results').
