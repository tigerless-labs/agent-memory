Enjoys making cocktails at home and hosting dinner parties where they serve cocktails. Interests include:

- **Spirit infusions**: Experimenting with infusing gin with flavors like lavender, grapefruit, cucumber, and mint. Prefers infusing ingredients separately then combining to control flavor intensity.
- **Classic gin cocktails**: Negroni (started practicing after Emma showed them, made 10+ times as of Nov 2023), Gin Martini, French 75, Bee's Knees (with homemade honey syrup), Pimm's Cup, Gin Rickey, Gin-Gin Mule, Aviation, Last Word.
- **Specialty cocktails**: Lavender Gin Fizz, Grapefruit Basil Martini, Grapefruit Rosemary Sparkler, Lavender Grapefruit Spritz, Grapefruit Lavender Collins, Cranberry Sparkler.
- **Syrups and infusions**: Makes homemade honey syrup (1:1 ratio honey:water), lavender syrup, basil syrup, rosemary syrup. Interested in variations like honey-ginger syrup.
- **Gin preferences**: Recently picked up Hendrick's gin (enjoyed as of Nov 2023). Interested in exploring different gin types (London Dry, Plymouth, New Western/New World styles).
- **Dinner party host**: Hosted dinner party where Cranberry Sparkler was very well-received.

Approach: starts with core recipes, experiments with ratios and garnishes, tests new spirits and ingredients methodically.
