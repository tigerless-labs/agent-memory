Creating multi-platform skincare content:
1. YouTube video on sustainable skincare with eco-friendly products and zero-waste routines
2. Instagram post highlighting natural and organic skincare products
3. Instagram Stories with tips on customizing skincare routines by skin type

Social media platforms: Pinterest has steady follower growth. Facebook has low engagement. Focus on skincare trends, sustainable beauty, K-Beauty and J-Beauty.
