Enjoying the book greatly — keeps them up at night. Appreciates the character development, especially Evelyn's portrayal as a Cuban immigrant who shaped her identity through Hollywood. Enjoys the dual narrative with Monique (journalist) as narrator, bringing outside perspective and active participation in uncovering Evelyn's secrets. Finds the exploration of public vs. private identity relatable, including intersectional identity as a woman, Latina, and immigrant. Interested in complex relationships between women and how they both empower and limit individuals. Likes how author shows female relationships as multifaceted, supportive yet competitive.
