Has basil plant growing on balcony that was receiving too much direct sunlight, causing it to struggle. Solution: move to shadier spot with filtered/indirect light or use shade cloth. Basil thrives in partial shade. Prefers temperatures 65-75F, humid environment, well-draining soil with good drainage. Soil pH 6.0-7.0 (slightly acidic to neutral). Prune regularly to encourage leaf growth.
