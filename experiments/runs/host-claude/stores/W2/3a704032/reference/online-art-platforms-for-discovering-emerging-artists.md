Recommended platforms for discovering contemporary art and emerging artists:\n\n- **Artsy**: Leading platform with curated section for emerging artists, filterable by medium and style\n- **Saatchi Art**: Online gallery focused on emerging artists\n- **Artspace**: Contemporary art platform with curated artist profiles and exhibitions\n- **Uprise Art**: Focuses on emerging artists with curated collections and exhibitions\n- **Behance**: Creative portfolio platform for artists and designers\n- **Instagram**: Many emerging artists showcase work; search hashtags like #emergingartist, #artoftheday\n- **Hyperallergic**: Online art blog covering news and emerging artist profiles\n- **ArtNet**: Platform with emerging artist section\n- **New American Paintings**: Features emerging painters from USA\n\nAll offer personalized recommendations and curated collections.
