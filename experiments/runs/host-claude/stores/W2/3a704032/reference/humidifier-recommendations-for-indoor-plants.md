Humidifier types and recommendations for humidity-loving plants (May 2023):\n\n**Types:**\n- Cool mist: most common, releases fine water vapor\n- Warm mist: warmer output, more expensive\n- Ultrasonic: quiet, energy-efficient, high-frequency sound waves\n\n**Key features to consider:**\n- Built-in humidistat for automatic control\n- Adjustable mist output\n- Tank size (larger = less frequent refilling)\n- Low noise level\n- Easy to clean (prevents bacterial growth)\n\n**Recommended models:**\n- Levoit LV600HH: hybrid (cool/warm), adjustable humidistat, 6L tank\n- Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate: compact, 1L tank, easy to use\n- Deneve Minimal Aroma: ultrasonic, 2.5L tank, built-in humidistat\n- Crane Drop Ultrasonic: compact, 1L tank, whisper-quiet\n\n**Usage tips:**\n- Place within 3-5 feet of plants\n- Monitor humidity with hygrometer (target 40-60% for ferns, 50-60% for African violets)\n- Clean regularly to prevent bacterial growth\n- For African violets: extra caution with overhumidification to prevent root rot
