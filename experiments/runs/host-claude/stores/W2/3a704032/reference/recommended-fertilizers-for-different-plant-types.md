Plant-specific fertilizers recommended (May 2023):\n\n**Snake Plant & Succulents:**\n- Balanced 20-20-20 for snake plants\n- Low-nitrogen cactus/succulent formulas (10-20-10) for succulents\n- Examples: Miracle-Gro, Espoma Organic, Schultz brands\n- Dilute to half strength; feed spring/summer only\n\n**Orchids:**\n- High-phosphorus content (10-20-10 or 15-30-15 or 30-10-10)\n- Low-nitrogen to prevent weak growth\n- Orchid-specific brands: Miracle-Gro Orchid Food, Espoma Organic Orchid, Schultz Orchid\n- Feed monthly only; dilute to half strength\n- Avoid general-purpose fertilizers\n\n**General tips:**\n- Always dilute to half strength to avoid root burn\n- Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) only\n- High-quality, well-draining potting mix is crucial for effectiveness
