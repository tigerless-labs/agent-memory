# Peace Lily Care Guide\n\n## Lighting\n- Prefers bright, indirect light; avoid direct sunlight\n- East- or west-facing windows ideal\n- Avoid low light to encourage blooming\n\n## Watering\n- Water when top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry (check by sticking finger to first knuckle)\n- Every 7-10 days during spring/summer growing season\n- Every 10-14 days during fall/winter dormant season\n- Ensure pot drains well to prevent waterlogging\n- Overwatering is the most common cause of death\n\n## Humidity & Misting\n- Prefers humid environment (40-50% humidity or higher)\n- Mist 1-2 times per week in average indoor conditions\n- Mist more often (every 3-4 days) in dry climates\n- Mist in the morning to allow leaves to dry during the day\n- Use room-temperature water\n- Avoid misting soil or crown (where leaves meet stem)\n\n## Temperature\n- Ideal range: 68°F–72°F (20°C–22°C)\n- Acceptable range: 65°F–75°F (18°C–24°C)\n- Avoid placing below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C)\n- Avoid drafts from windows, AC vents, heaters, fireplaces\n\n## Fertilization\n- Use balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20 or similar)\n- Fertilize every 1-2 weeks during growing season (spring/summer)\n- Reduce to once a month during fall/winter\n- Dilute to half recommended strength to avoid burning roots\n\n## Potting Mix\n- Use well-draining potting mix (tropical or orchid mix recommended)\n- Repot in spring/summer only if plant outgrows container or shows nutrient deficiency signs\n\n## Pruning\n- Remove dead or dying leaves and flowers to prevent disease spread\n- Use clean, sharp scissors to avoid spreading disease\n\n## Pest Control\n- Check regularly for spider mites, mealybugs, scale\n- Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if pests appear\n\n## General\n- Normal for new plants to lose some leaves while adjusting to environment\n- Monitor plant response and adjust care as needed\n- Yellowing or drooping leaves may indicate watering issues
