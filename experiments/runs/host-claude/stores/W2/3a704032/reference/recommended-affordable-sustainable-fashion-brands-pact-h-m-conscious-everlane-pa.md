List of affordable sustainable clothing brands recommended for ethical and eco-conscious shopping:\n\n- **Pact** - Organic cotton clothing for men, women, and children with fair labor practices\n- **H&M Conscious** - H&M's sustainable collection with organic cotton, recycled polyester, and Tencel\n- **Everlane** - Transparent brand offering affordable, high-quality clothing made from recycled synthetic fibers and organic cotton; good selection for men's clothing with minimalist aesthetic\n- **Patagonia** - Sustainable outdoor clothing brand with mid-range and affordable options; organic cotton, recycled polyester, and hemp\n- **Levi's** - Denim brand offering affordable sustainable options made from recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton\n\nOther brands mentioned: Stella McCartney, Eileen Fisher, Honest By
