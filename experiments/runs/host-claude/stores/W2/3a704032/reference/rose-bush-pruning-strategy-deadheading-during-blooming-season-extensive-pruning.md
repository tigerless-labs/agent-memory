# Rose Bush Pruning Guide\n\n## During Blooming Season\n- **Deadheading**: Remove dead or wilted flowers to encourage plant to focus energy on new blooms\n- **Light pruning**: Shape bush and promote air circulation by removing weak, spindly growth\n- Remove stems that cross or rub against each other\n- Do NOT do extensive pruning during active flowering\n\n## After Blooming Season\n- Do extensive pruning to rejuvenate plant or control size\n- Wait until blooming season is completely over before major pruning\n- Allows plant to complete flowering cycle and store energy\n\n## Pruning Technique\n- Prune during dormant season (winter/early spring) for most rose varieties\n- Make clean cuts at 45-degree angle\n- Remove any weak or diseased wood\n- Use clean tools to prevent disease spread\n- Monitor plant response to pruning and adjust strategy if needed\n\n## Notes\nPlants typically respond well to pruning and produce new buds quickly (within a month)
