Recommended tools for facilitating reflection and discussion in educational settings:\n- Padlet: collaborative platform for sharing thoughts and ideas\n- Nearpod: interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions\n- Flipgrid: video discussion platform for short video responses\n- Google Jamboard: virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual sharing\n\nAll support student engagement and interactive learning.
