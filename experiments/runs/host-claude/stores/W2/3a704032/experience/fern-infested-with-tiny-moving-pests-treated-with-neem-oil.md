Fern developed infestation of tiny moving dots (likely spider mites or springtails). First observed around late May 2023. Treatment plan: use neem oil spray and increase misting 2-3 times per week with distilled water to maintain 40-60% humidity. Misting in mornings helps prevent fungal issues. Will monitor closely and repeat treatment as needed.
