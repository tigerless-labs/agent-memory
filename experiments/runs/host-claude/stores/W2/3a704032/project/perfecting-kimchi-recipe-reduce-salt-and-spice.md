First batch (mid-May 2023) was too salty and spicy. Planned adjustments: rinse under cold water to remove excess salt, use gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) in smaller amounts (start with 1/4-1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables), add fresh garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups) and ginger (1-2 inches grated per 2 cups). Key tips: taste as you go, measure by weight not volume, consider brand variation in heat levels, sauté aromatics to mellow flavors.
