Decided to order 100 business cards as a starting point. Rationale:
- Good starting quantity that doesn't overcommit inventory
- Can always reorder if cards run out quickly
- More cost-effective than a small batch
- Avoids waste if demand is lower than expected
- Plan to monitor usage rate and adjust future orders accordingly
