Decided to launch a loyalty program featuring:
- Points-based system where customers earn points per purchase
- Tier-based structure with exclusive rewards for frequent customers
- Sign-up bonus to encourage enrollment
- Promotion through social media, email newsletters, in-store promotions
- Staff training at checkout to inform customers about the program
- Rewards structure: discounts on future purchases and free products
- Considering tier names like Bronze/Silver/Gold or VIP/Premium/Elite
