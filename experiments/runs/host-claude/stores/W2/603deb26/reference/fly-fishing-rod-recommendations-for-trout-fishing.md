Fly fishing rods to explore for lighter trout fishing: Orvis Clearwater (entry-level), Sage Igniter (high-performance), Winston Pure (premium), Redington Crux (budget), TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series (high-quality). Look for rods with 2-5 weight lines, 9-foot or shorter length, graphite material.
