**Lavender Gin Fizz**

Ingredients:
- 1.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz lavender syrup
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Soda water

Instructions:
1. Combine gin, lime juice, lavender syrup, and simple syrup in a shaker
2. Shake and strain into a Collins glass
3. Top with soda water
4. Garnish with lavender sprig and lime wheel

**Lavender Syrup:**
- Steep 0.25 cup dried lavender buds in 1 cup granulated sugar and 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes
- Strain and store in fridge for up to 2 weeks
