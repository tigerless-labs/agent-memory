Recommended vegetarian sushi fillings: roasted sweet potato, grilled portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura bits, spinach and tofu, pickled shiitake mushrooms, crispy fried tofu. These provide variety in texture and flavor for vegetarian sushi rolls.
