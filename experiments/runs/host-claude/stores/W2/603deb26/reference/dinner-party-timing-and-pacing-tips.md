Tips for dinner party timing: create timeline with realistic duration estimates, prep ahead, cook in batches, serve courses sequentially (30-45 min between appetizer and main, 30-60 min between main and dessert), stay relaxed, communicate with guests. Key: keep atmosphere convivial and don't stress about exact timing.
