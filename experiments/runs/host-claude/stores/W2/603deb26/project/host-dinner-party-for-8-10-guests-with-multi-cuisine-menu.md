Planning dinner party for 8-10 guests with diverse menu featuring: lemon-herb naan (Indian), vegetarian sushi rolls with portobello mushrooms and carrot-ginger tempura bits (Japanese), lemon-herb quinoa side dish, and French croissants. Accommodations: vegetarian options and one gluten-free guest. Garnishes include fresh herbs, thinly sliced lemons, and edible flowers (violas/pansies). Focus on lemon-based flavors and beautiful presentation.
