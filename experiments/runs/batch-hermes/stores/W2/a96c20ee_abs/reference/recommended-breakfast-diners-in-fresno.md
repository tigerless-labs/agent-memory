Recommended diners in Fresno for breakfast:
- Max's Bistro and Bar (signature "Max's Breakfast" burrito with scrambled eggs and chorizo)
- The Breakfast Club (retro-style diner with breakfast tacos, waffle sandwiches, and omelets)
- Pete's Breakfast House (giant pancakes, omelets, breakfast combos)
- Frank's Place (no-frills diner with "Frank's Favorite" breakfast plate)
- Denny's
