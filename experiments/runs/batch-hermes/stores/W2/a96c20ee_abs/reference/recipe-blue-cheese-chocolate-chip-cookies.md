Recipe for Blue Cheese Chocolate Chip Cookies inspired by dark chocolate and blue cheese truffles:

Ingredients:
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter (room temperature)
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C), line sheet with parchment.
2. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt.
3. Cream butter, sugar, and blue cheese with mixer until fluffy.
4. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
5. Mix in flour mixture; fold in chocolate chips.
6. Drop by tablespoonfuls onto sheet.
7. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges are golden. Cool 5 min on sheet, then transfer to wire rack.
