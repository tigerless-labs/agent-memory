Guidelines for kimchi flavoring:
- Gochugaru heat guide per 2 cups vegetables: Mild (1/4 to 1/2 tsp), Medium (1/2 to 1 tsp), Hot (1 to 2 tsp), Extremely Hot (2+ tsp).
- Rescuing over-salted kimchi: Rinse under cold running water, add more fresh salted vegetables, add desalting agents (rice vinegar, gochujang, water splash), or soak overnight in cold water (salt-breaking / geom-sul).
- Aromatics: 2-3 cloves minced garlic per 2 cups vegetables; 1-2 inches grated or sliced ginger per 2 cups vegetables.
