Notable award-winning country albums:
- Golden Hour by Kacey Musgraves (Grammy Album of the Year 2019; CMA/ACM Album of the Year 2018)
- The Weight of These Wings by Miranda Lambert (ACM/CMA Album of the Year 2017, Grammy nominee 2018)
- Traveller by Chris Stapleton (Grammy Best Country Album 2016, ACM/CMA Album of the Year 2015)
- Same Trailer Different Park by Kacey Musgraves (Grammy Best Country Album 2014, ACM/CMA 2013)
- Fearless by Taylor Swift (Grammy Best Country Album 2010, ACM/CMA 2009)
