Protein content per serving:
- Avocado: ~4g per medium
- Guavas: ~4.2g per cup
- Dried Apricots: ~3.9g per cup
- Bananas: ~2.6g per medium
- Durian: ~3.6g per cup
- Spinach (cooked): ~5.4g per cup
- Brussels Sprouts (cooked): ~4.1g per cup
- Asparagus (cooked): ~2.9g per cup
- Broccoli: ~2.6g per cup
- Kale (cooked): ~2.5g per cup
