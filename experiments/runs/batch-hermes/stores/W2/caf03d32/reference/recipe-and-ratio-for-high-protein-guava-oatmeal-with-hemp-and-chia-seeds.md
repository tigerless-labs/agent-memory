Guava Oatmeal Recipe:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1:2 ratio with oats)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1-2 tbsp hemp seeds (5-10g protein, 1-2g omega-3s)
- Optional: honey/maple syrup, nuts (walnuts, almonds, pecans), pinch of salt, cinnamon
