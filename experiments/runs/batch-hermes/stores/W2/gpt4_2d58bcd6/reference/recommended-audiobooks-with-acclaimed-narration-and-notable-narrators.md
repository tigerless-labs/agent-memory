Recommended audiobooks:
- "The Hate U Give" by Angie Thomas (narrated by Bahni Turpin)
- "The Night Circus" by Erin Morgenstern (narrated by Jim Dale)
- "The Power" by Naomi Alderman (narrated by Adjoa Andoh)
- "The Song of Achilles" by Madeline Miller (narrated by Frazer Douglas)
- "The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August" by Claire North (narrated by Peter Kenny)

Notable narrators: Juliet Stevenson, Scott Brick, George Guidall, Khristine Hvam.
