Key strategic factors for Indian EV automotive component market entry:
1. Market research on EV models, infrastructure, price sensitivity, and demographic diversity.
2. Adaptation to Indian regulatory and automotive quality standards and certifications.
3. Localized production or local Indian partnership for cost efficiency and regulatory compliance.
4. Value-driven competitive pricing.
5. Multi-channel distribution (automotive parts distributors, retailers, e-commerce).
6. Targeted marketing highlighting safety, reliability, and performance.
7. Local service network and technician training for after-sales support.
