Camera settings:
- Mode: Manual (M)
- ISO: 100-400
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (for large depth of field) or f/2.8 (for snowflakes)
- Shutter speed: 1/125s-1/250s (or ~1/1000s to freeze snowflakes)
- White balance: Cloudy or Shade
- Exposure: Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop for bright snow
- Format: RAW (bracket shots for HDR)
- Filters: Polarizing filter (reduce glare) and graduated ND filter
- Lens: Wide-angle (10-24mm) for landscape, macro for snow texture
- Best light: Golden hour or overcast skies; keep spare batteries warm.
