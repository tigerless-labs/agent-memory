Recipe:
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs), roasted at 400°F (200°C) for 45-50 min
- Sauté 1 chopped onion, 3 cloves minced garlic in 2 tbsp olive oil
- Add 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, 4 cups broth
- Blend with roasted squash, stir in 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream

Storage:
- Cool within 2 hours; store in airtight glass/plastic container.
- Refrigeration: 3 to 5 days.
- Freezing: 3 to 6 months in portioned containers.
- Reheat to minimum 165°F (74°C).
