Recommended titles:
- "Dear Martin" by Nic Stone
- "The Poet X" by Elizabeth Acevedo
- "Long Way Down" by Jason Reynolds
- "All American Boys" by Jason Reynolds and Brendan Kiely
- "The Sun is Also a Star" by Nicola Yoon

Recommended authors: Jason Reynolds, Nicola Yoon, Renee Watson ("Piecing Me Together", "Betty Before X"), Ibi Zoboi ("American Street", "Pride"), Dhonielle Clayton ("The Belles", "Shattered Midnight"), Tomi Adeyemi ("Children of Blood and Bone"), Sabaa Tahir ("An Ember in the Ashes"), Nnedi Okorafor ("Who Fears Death", "Binti").
