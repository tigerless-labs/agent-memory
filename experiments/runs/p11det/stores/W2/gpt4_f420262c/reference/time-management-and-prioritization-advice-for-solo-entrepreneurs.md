Strategies discussed for managing time as a solo founder:

1. Set SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) for agency and partnerships

2. Eisenhower Matrix - prioritize tasks into 4 quadrants:
   - Urgent and important (Do first)
   - Important but not urgent (Schedule)
   - Urgent but not important (Delegate)
   - Not urgent or important (Eliminate)

3. Task management tools: Trello, Asana, ClickUp

4. Time blocking by category:
   - Business development (networking, partnerships)
   - Client work (project management, delivery)
   - Marketing and growth (social media, content creation)
   - Admin and operations (accounting, HR)

5. Focus on high-leverage activities first (biggest impact on growth)

6. Learn to say no to things not aligned with goals

7. Delegate and outsource non-core tasks

8. Take breaks and prioritize self-care

9. Regularly review progress and adjust approach

10. Seek support from mentors, peers, coaches, or mastermind groups

User found Eisenhower Matrix and time blocking especially helpful for addressing overwhelm from doing too much personally.
