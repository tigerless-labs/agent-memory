JetBlue offers free live TV (36 channels: sports, news, entertainment), free Wi-Fi, and Fly-Fi in-flight entertainment system accessible via personal devices. Rated more reliable and user-friendly than American Airlines. Complimentary snacks included. 32-inch seat pitch with adjustable headrests. Even More Space upgrade (37-inch legroom) available for 10-25 dollars. Free seat selection during 24-hour check-in. BOS-FLL pricing approximately 148 dollars for morning departure.
