## Bear-Resistant Containers\n\n1. **Garcia Bear-Resistant Container** — Durable plastic, meets IGBC standards\n2. **BearVault** — Heavy-duty plastic, easy to use and secure\n3. **Ursack** — Lightweight, puncture-resistant material, good for backpackers\n\n## Bear-Resistant Bags\n\n1. **Ursack Major** — Durable, puncture-resistant material for food and scented items\n2. **Counter Assault Bear Bag** — Heavy-duty material, easy to use and secure\n3. **Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag** — Lightweight, compact, durable and puncture-resistant\n\n## Selection Criteria\n- Made of durable, puncture-resistant material\n- Specifically designed to be bear-resistant\n- Certified by reputable organization (e.g., IGBC)\n- Easy to use and secure\n- Right size for your needs\n\n## Food Storage Best Practices\n- Store food 100 yards away from campsite in:\n  - Hard-sided vehicle (windows/doors closed)\n  - Bear locker/bear box\n  - Tree 10 feet high, 4 feet out from trunk\n- Hang food from tree using sturdy rope if needed\n- Keep all food in airtight containers\n- Store scented items (toothpaste, soap, lotion) with food\n- Pack out all trash\n- Clean cooking area and dishes immediately
