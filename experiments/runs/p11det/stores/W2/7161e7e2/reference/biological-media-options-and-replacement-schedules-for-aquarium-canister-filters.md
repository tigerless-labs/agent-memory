**Biological Media Types:**\n\n1. **Ceramic rings or noodles**: Large surface area for bacteria colonization, long-lasting, cleanable/reusable. Replace every 6-12 months or when clogged/degraded.\n2. **Bio-balls**: Small spheres providing large surface area. Replace every 6-12 months or when clogged/degraded.\n3. **Sintered glass media**: Made from sintered glass particles, long-lasting and easy to clean. Replace every 12-24 months or when clogged/degraded.\n4. **Biological filter pads**: Pre-colonized with beneficial bacteria. Replace every 1-3 months per manufacturer instructions.\n\n**General Guidelines:**\n\n- Clean biological media every 2-3 months (or 3-6 months for sintered glass)\n- Fill biological basket to 2/3 to 3/4 capacity for good water flow\n- Rinse new media in old tank water before adding\n- Monitor ammonia/nitrite to determine replacement timing\n- Keep spare media on hand for smooth transitions
