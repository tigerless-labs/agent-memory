## Cocktails\n- **Witch's Brew**: gin, blue curaçao, lemon-lime soda, served in a cauldron with dry ice\n- **Graveyard Dew**: vodka, peach schnapps, cranberry juice, pineapple juice, sugar rim, crushed Oreo crust\n- **Mummy's Revenge**: tequila, lime juice, ginger beer, salt rim\n- **Zombie Apocalypse**: rum, pineapple juice, orange juice, grenadine\n- **Vampire's Kiss**: vodka, cranberry juice, lime juice, blood-red sugar rim\n\n## Games\n- Bobbing for Apples\n- Pin the Wart on the Witch\n- Mummy Wrap (team toilet paper wrapping game)\n- Haunted House Scavenger Hunt\n- Monster Mash Karaoke\n- Costume Contest\n- Haunted Photo Booth\n- Tarot Card Readings\n\n## Music Playlists\n- Classic Horror Themes (Halloween, Exorcist, Addams Family)\n- Spooky Soundtracks (The Shining, The Conjuring, Get Out)\n- Darkwave/Goth (The Sisters of Mercy, The Cure, Bauhaus)\n- Halloween Party Anthems (Thriller, Monster Mash, Werewolves of London)\n\n## Desserts\n- Witches' Hat Cookies (black icing)\n- Mummy Brownies (frosting strips)\n- Graveyard Cake (fondant tombstones)\n- Ghostly Marshmallow Treats (white chocolate, edible markers)\n- Bat Wing Chocolate Chip Cookies
