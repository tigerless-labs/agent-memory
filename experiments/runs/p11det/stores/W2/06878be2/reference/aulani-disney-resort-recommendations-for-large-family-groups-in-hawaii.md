Aulani, a Disney Resort & Spa (Oahu) - family-friendly resort for groups. Room options: 3-bedroom villas sleep up to 12, connecting villas available. Standard connecting rooms (4-5 people each) can connect up to 4 rooms for 16-20 capacity. Features: private beach, water park, lazy river, kids' pool with volcano-themed play area, Aunty's Beach House kids club (ages 3-12). Group rates available for 10+ rooms. Long-stay discounts for 5+ nights (10-20% off). Complimentary Disney's Magical Express shuttle from airport. Rates typically $600-700/night. Book 450 days in advance for best availability.
