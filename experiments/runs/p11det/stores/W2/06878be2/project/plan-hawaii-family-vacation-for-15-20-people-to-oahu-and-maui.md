Trip Details: 15-20 people (kids 5-15), Oahu and Maui, $500-700/night budget. Interested in Aulani Disney Resort with connecting rooms/villas. Contact Aulani for group discounts (10+ rooms) and long-stay discounts (5+ nights). Book 450+ days in advance. Activities: snorkeling, kids clubs, water parks, Road to Hana. Plan family dinners at restaurants seating 20-40+. Attend traditional luau. Recommended: Paradise Cove/Germaine's (Oahu), Old Lahaina/Feast at Lele (Maui). Consider off-season rates (Apr-Jun, Sep-Dec).
