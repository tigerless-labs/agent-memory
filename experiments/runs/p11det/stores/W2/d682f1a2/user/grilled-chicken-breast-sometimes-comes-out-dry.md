When grilling chicken breasts, they can turn out dry. Interested in cooking techniques to improve: marinating, brining, pounding to even thickness, medium-low heat (325-350°F), not pressing down, using meat thermometer (165°F internal temp), and letting meat rest 5-10 minutes before serving.
