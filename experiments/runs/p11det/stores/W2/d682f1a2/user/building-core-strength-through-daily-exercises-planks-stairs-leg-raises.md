Plans to incorporate core work into daily routine:
- Plank holds during TV commercial breaks (starting 30-60 seconds)
- Taking stairs instead of elevators
- Leg raises while brushing teeth (2 minutes, 2-3x daily)

Goal is to improve overall core strength and posture.
