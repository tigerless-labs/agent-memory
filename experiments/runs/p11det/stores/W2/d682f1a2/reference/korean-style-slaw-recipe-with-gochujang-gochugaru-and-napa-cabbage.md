**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups napa cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1 cup carrots, peeled and grated
- 1/2 cup cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)
- 2 tbsp Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Sesame seeds and chopped green onions for garnish (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine napa cabbage, carrots, and cucumber in a large bowl
2. Whisk together chili flakes, Gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, and ginger
3. Pour dressing over slaw and toss
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes

**Tips:**
- Add more chili for spicier slaw
- Can substitute napa cabbage with other types or shredded kale
- Keeps for up to 2 days refrigerated
- Pairs well with Korean BBQ pork belly
