Key books recommended for learning about Abstract Expressionism: 'The New York School' by Irving Sandler, 'Pollock' by Kirk Varnedoe, 'Mark Rothko: The Decisive Decade' by Todd Herman, 'Abstract Expressionism' by David Anfam, 'Art Since 1940' by Fred Kleiner. For Anselm Kiefer: 'Heaven and Earth' and 'The High Priestess' monographs. For art conservation: 'The Conservator's Handbook' by Valerie Dorge and Florian Horenburg, 'Art Conservation: Principles and Practice' by Andrew Oddy and Steven Smith, 'The Art of Conservation' by David Bomford. Key online resources: International Institute for Conservation (IIC), American Institute for Conservation (AIC), Getty Conservation Institute (GCI). Documentaries: PBS 'The Conservation Lab' (2019), BBC 'The Art of Conservation' (2017), MoMA's 'Conservation: Behind the Scenes' (YouTube).
