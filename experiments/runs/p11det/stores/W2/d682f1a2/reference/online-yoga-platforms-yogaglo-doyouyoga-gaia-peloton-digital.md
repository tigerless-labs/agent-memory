Recommended online platforms for beginner yoga classes:
- **YogaGlo**: Wide range including beginner flows, Hatha, Vinyasa, Restorative
- **DoYouYoga**: Beginner-focused with Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin yoga
- **Gaia**: Variety of beginner flows, meditation, yoga philosophy
- **Peloton Digital**: Yoga classes including beginner flows and meditation

Tips: Start with 20-30 minute classes, focus on alignment, use props like blocks and straps, find a consistent instructor.
