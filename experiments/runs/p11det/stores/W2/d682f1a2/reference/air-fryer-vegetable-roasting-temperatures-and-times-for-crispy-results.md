**General approach:**
- Reduce oven temp by 25-50°F compared to traditional oven
- Reduce cooking time by 30-50% vs oven
- Shake/stir halfway through
- Don't overcrowd basket
- Pat vegetables dry to remove excess moisture
- Toss with small amount of oil (1-2 tsp) and seasonings

**Specific settings for restaurant-style crispiness:**

**Brussels Sprouts:**
- Cut in half, pat dry
- 400°F for 10-12 min, shake halfway
- Increase to 420°F for final 2-3 min for extra crispiness

**Sweet Potatoes:**
- Cut into wedges/sticks
- Soak in cold water 30+ min, pat dry
- 400°F for 12-15 min, shake halfway  
- Increase to 420°F for final 2-3 min for extra crispiness

**Other vegetables tested:**
- Daikon: 375°F, 10-12 min
- Carrots: 400°F, 12-15 min
- Zucchini: 375°F, 8-10 min
- Mushrooms: 375°F, 10-12 min
- Broccoli: 400°F, 10-12 min

**Key for crispy results:**
- Use the two-stage cooking (regular temp, then bump to 420°F at end)
- Ensure thorough drying before cooking
- Minimal oil (1-2 tsp only)
- Single layer, no crowding
