Top cookbook stands: OXO Good Grips (sturdy, adjustable, non-slip base), KitchenAid (comfortable, sturdy base, adjustable height, soft-grip surface), Cuisinart (compact, space-efficient). Budget options: Amazon Basics (affordable, sturdy, adjustable, non-slip base), Umbra (budget-friendly, compact, non-slip base). Features to consider: adjustable height, non-slip base, page hold/clip, folding design for storage, sturdy material. Choose based on cookbook size, kitchen space, and weight capacity needs.
