Closing questions to ask during interviews for promotion to management position:

1. Is there anything that concerns you or any reservations you may have about my qualifications or experience for this role?
2. How does this role fit into the broader goals and objectives of the department and the organization as a whole?
3. Can you describe any new or upcoming initiatives that the manager will be involved in, and how will they contribute to the success of the department and the college?
4. How can I continue to develop my skills and abilities to excel in this role and contribute to the success of the department?
5. Are there any challenges or opportunities that you see in the near future for the department that the manager should be aware of and prepare for?
6. How does the department measure success for the manager, and what are the key performance indicators for this role?
7. What kind of support and resources will be available to me as the new manager to help me succeed in this role?
8. How can I best communicate and collaborate with other departments and stakeholders to ensure that the department is meeting the needs of the organization?
9. What are the most important qualities that you are looking for in the manager, and how do my strengths align with these qualities?
10. What is the timeline for making a decision on this role, and when can I expect to hear back from you regarding next steps in the process?
