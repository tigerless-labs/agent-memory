## Revision and Editing Process:\n\n**In Evernote:**\n- Revision template with: revision goals, changes, feedback\n- Separate note per revision with tagged changes (e.g., 'added scene', 'changed character motivation')\n\n**In Trello:**\n- Revision board with lists for: revision, editing, proofreading stages\n- Cards tracking revisions with changes, feedback, next steps\n- Labels for revision type (character development, plot twist, grammar)\n- Progress tracking via checklist\n\n**Revision Techniques:**\n1. Take a break — gain fresh perspective before revisions\n2. Get feedback — share with beta readers or writing groups\n3. Revise in layers — focus on one aspect at a time (character, plot, pacing)\n4. Edit in passes — multiple passes for specific elements (grammar, punctuation, dialogue)\n5. Seek help — writing communities, editors, mentors\n\n**General Approach:** Be patient, stay organized, celebrate progress along the way.
