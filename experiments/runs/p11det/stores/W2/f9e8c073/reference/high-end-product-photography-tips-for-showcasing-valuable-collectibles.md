Professional photography guidelines for photographing and editing high-end collectible items for sale:

**Lighting Techniques:**
- Use natural light when possible (soft, flattering)
- Position items near windows with diffused light
- Shoot during golden hour for warm, soft lighting
- Use softbox or DIY diffuser (translucent umbrella or white fabric)
- Avoid direct flash that creates harsh shadows
- Layer multiple light sources for depth (softbox + sidelight)

**Composition:**
- Use plain, neutral backgrounds (white, gray, or black)
- Apply rule of thirds for balanced framing
- Experiment with unique angles (low, high, Dutch angles)
- Photograph close-ups of specific details (watch face, fretboard, etc.)
- Vary angles and perspectives for visual interest

**Technical Setup:**
- Use tripod for steady, sharp images
- Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility
- Use shallow depth of field to isolate subject
- Ensure sharp focus on the item
- Consider lifestyle shots showing item in use

**Post-Processing/Editing:**
- Color correction: adjust temperature and saturation for accuracy
- Noise reduction to minimize grain
- Sharpening to enhance details (avoid over-sharpening)
- Retouching: remove dust, scratches, minor imperfections
- Maintain consistent look/feel across all images
- Establish cohesive visual brand

**General Principles:**
- Establish consistency across all product images
- Avoid over-retouching that looks unnatural
- Balance detail photography with overview shots
- Take multiple angles showing condition and quality
- Create professional appearance for higher perceived value
