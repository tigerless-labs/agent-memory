Business Asset Disposal Relief (BADR) reduces Capital Gains Tax to 10% on qualifying gains.

Lifetime Limit: £1 million per individual shareholder (not per company). Each shareholder has separate allowance covering all qualifying disposals in lifetime.

Qualifying Conditions - shareholder must meet ALL:
1. Company must be a trading company or holding company of trading group
2. Shareholder holds at least 5% of ordinary shares AND voting rights for at least 24 months before disposal
3. Shareholder is an officer or employee for at least 24 months before disposal

Key Points:
- Can dispose of shares and claim BADR even if business continues trading
- Company Secretary position qualifies as 'officer' status
- Each shareholder's disposal operates independently
- Tax advisor consultation recommended for specific circumstances
