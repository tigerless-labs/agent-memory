## Plot and Story Structure Guidance:\n\n**Structural Approaches:**\n- Three-act structure with clear goals and conflicts per act\n- Character-driven plot: character goals/motivations drive narrative\n- Consistent scene and chapter structure with purpose, conflict, resolution\n- Pacing and tension management (ebbing and flowing for reader engagement)\n\n**In Evernote:**\n- Plot template with: plot points, character arcs, conflicts, themes, setting\n- Scene lists breaking story into individual scenes with setting, characters, plot points\n\n**In Trello:**\n- Plot board with lists for story stages (exposition, rising action, climax, resolution)\n- Cards for plot points, scenes, chapters with details and labels\n- Track plot twists and reveals\n\n**Brainstorming Techniques:**\n1. Mind mapping — visualize plot, characters, themes\n2. What-if questions — explore alternative plot directions\n3. Scene brainstorming — imagine different scenarios\n4. Reverse outlining — write summary then break into scenes to find gaps
