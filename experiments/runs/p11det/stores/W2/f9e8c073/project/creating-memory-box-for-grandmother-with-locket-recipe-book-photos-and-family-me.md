Started creating a memory box to honor grandmother's memory and keep her close. The box will contain:

**Items collected:**
- Grandmother's locket
- Grandmother's handwritten recipe book
- Family photos
- Mementos from other family members

**Planned organization:**
- Gather items that remind of grandmother
- Organize in a personally meaningful way
- Involve other family members in contributing items and mementos
- Create focal point with special items (locket or recipe book)

**Care and use:**
- Store safely away from sunlight, moisture, extremes
- Visit regularly (weekly, monthly, or on special occasions)
- Add new items over time
- Possibly create rituals around visits
- Consider passing down to future generations
- May digitize contents for family archive

**Additional considerations:**
- Keep journal in box for writing memories
- Scan grandmother's recipe book and photos
- Share stories with family members
- Display box in special place
