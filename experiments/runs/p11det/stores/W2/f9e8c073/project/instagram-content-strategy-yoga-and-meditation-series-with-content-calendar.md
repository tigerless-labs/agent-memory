Plans to create an Instagram content series focused on yoga and meditation, including:

- Morning yoga routine reels
- Meditation exercises and guided meditations
- Tips and tutorials on poses and breathing techniques
- Inspirational quotes and motivational messages
- Personal stories about benefits of yoga and meditation
- Q&A sessions

Strategy includes:
- Creating a content calendar for consistency (aim for 3-5 posts per week)
- Using relevant hashtags (#yogaeverydamnday #meditation #mindfulness #yogalife #wellness)
- Sharing behind-the-scenes content (workspace, yoga mat setup, planning process, bloopers)
- Engaging with followers through comments and responses
- Posting when audience is most active
- Including calls-to-action in captions
- Collaborating with other yoga enthusiasts
- Being authentic and consistent with brand voice

Initial performance:
- Morning yoga routine reel: 50 views, 10 likes
- Recognized that behind-the-scenes content (desk photo) got 20 likes and comments from friends
