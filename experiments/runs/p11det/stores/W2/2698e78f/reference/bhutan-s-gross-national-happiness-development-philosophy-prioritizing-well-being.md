**Gross National Happiness (GNH)** is a development philosophy proposed by Jigme Singye Wangchuck (fourth king of Bhutan) in 1972. It measures progress through citizens' well-being rather than economic growth.

## Four Pillars
1. Sustainable and equitable socioeconomic development
2. Environmental conservation
3. Preservation and promotion of culture
4. Good governance

## Key Policies & Commitments
- Ban on plastic bags
- Commitment to maintain at least 60% forest cover
- Uses a GNH index to measure progress

## Relationship to Social Stability
Research suggests countries with higher well-being scores show:
- Higher levels of social trust
- More effective democratic systems
- Lower propensity for social and political unrest

Differs from GDP-focused growth by emphasizing holistic well-being: health, education, community vitality, rooted in Buddhist principles of interdependence and non-materialism.
