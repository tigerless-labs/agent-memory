Believes that emotional concepts like setsunai should be more widely known in English-speaking cultures, not to 'own' them but because humanity universally experiences these emotions.

Sees potential for awareness through:
- Self-reflection and being in-tune with emotions
- Mental health specialists, anthropologists, and sociologists helping articulate these concepts
- Translation and cultural exchange (anime, manga, literature)
- Online communities and educational curriculums
- Cross-cultural understanding that enriches emotional vocabulary

Views the increasing popularity of Japanese culture globally as an opportunity to bring nuanced emotional concepts into broader awareness.
