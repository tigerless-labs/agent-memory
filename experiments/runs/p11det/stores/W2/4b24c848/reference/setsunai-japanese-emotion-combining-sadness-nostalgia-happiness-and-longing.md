Setsunai (切ない) is a Japanese adjective describing a complex emotion with elements of both sadness and joy. It describes bittersweet nostalgia, heartache, and longing that often contains traces of fondness or happiness for past experiences. The concept is related to 'mono no aware' (物の哀れ), which reflects the beauty of transience.

Similar emotions exist in other languages: 'saudade' in Spanish/Portuguese describes a deep sense of melancholy and longing for someone or something absent.

While the word is uniquely Japanese, the emotion itself is universal to humanity — all cultures experience this blend of sadness and joy in memory and reflection. The Japanese language simply named and articulated it explicitly earlier.
