Popular platforms for finding vintage clothing and second-hand items:

- **eBay** — established marketplace with vast selection of vintage, second-hand, pre-owned items
- **ThredUp** — online consignment store specializing in gently used women's and kids' clothing
- **Poshmark** — social commerce platform for buying/selling gently used clothing and accessories
- **Depop** — platform for vintage clothing, accessories, and collectibles
- **Etsy** — marketplace for handmade, vintage, and unique items from independent sellers worldwide
- **The RealReal** — luxury consignment store with authenticated designer goods and vintage items
- **ASOS Marketplace** — platform for independent sellers to list vintage and second-hand items
- **Vestiare Collective** — luxury consignment specializing in pre-owned designer clothing and accessories
- **Ruby Lane** — online marketplace for vintage and antique items
- **Local online selling groups** — Facebook groups and local platforms for second-hand items in your community
