Standard vaccination schedule for dogs against parvo and common canine diseases:

**Puppy schedule:**
- Start: 6-8 weeks old
- Boosters: every 2-4 weeks
- Continue until: 16 weeks old

**Adult maintenance:**
- Booster at 1 year after initial series completion
- Then every 3 years thereafter

**Important notes:**
- The length of vaccine protection varies by individual dog and vaccine type
- Some vaccines provide protection for up to 3 years
- Parvovirus and distemper virus can survive in the environment for extended periods
- These viruses can be transmitted indirectly through contaminated surfaces, food/water bowls, clothing, and shoes
- Puppies are especially vulnerable due to immature immune systems
- Rabies is a zoonotic disease (transmissible between animals and humans)
- Preventing disease through vaccination is typically much more cost-effective than treating an infection
- Individual risk factors should be discussed with a veterinarian to customize the schedule
