When planning family trips, user prioritizes destinations that satisfy multiple family members' different interests. Recognized from Hawaii family trip with parents and younger brother (April 2023) how important it is to consider everyone's preferences.

**Balancing approach:**
- Father prefers outdoor activities (hiking, mountain pursuits)
- Mother prefers cultural attractions (museums, historical sites, galleries)
- User enjoys both plus film/cultural events
- Seeks destinations offering mix of hiking, cultural venues, and festivals

Has successfully used this balanced approach to select destinations like Park City, Utah (outdoor + film festival + cultural scene) and earlier considered Asheville NC, Santa Fe NM, Portland OR, Barcelona, Queenstown NZ, Copenhagen, and other hybrid destinations.
