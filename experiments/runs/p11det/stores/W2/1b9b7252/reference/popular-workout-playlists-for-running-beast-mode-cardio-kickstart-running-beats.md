**Popular Workout Playlists by Platform:**

1. **Beast Mode** (Apple Music) — High-energy mix of hip-hop, rock, and pop
2. **Cardio Kickstart** (Spotify) — Upbeat tracks across genres
3. **Running Beats** (Nike) — Curated for runners with tracks for different paces
4. **Fitness Blender** (Spotify) — Mix of genres and tempos
5. **Pulse** (Amazon Music) — Energetic tracks from popular artists

**Tips for Creating Your Own Playlist:**
- Choose tracks with consistent BPM to maintain running pace
- Mix genres and tempos to keep engagement
- Include motivational tracks with uplifting lyrics
- Experiment and adjust to personal taste and running style
