Family trip with parents and younger brother (4 people total) to attend Sundance Film Festival in Park City. All family members are film buffs. Decided to stay in Park City itself despite higher accommodation costs to maximize festival immersion and convenience.

**Family interests balanced:**
- Father: loves outdoor activities (hiking, mountain activities)
- Mother: interested in museums and historical sites
- User and family: all film enthusiasts

**Chosen destination attractions:**
- Hiking: Jordanelle Reservoir Trail (3.5 mi), Mid-Mountain Trail (13.5 mi), Guardsman Pass (4.5 mi), Rob's Trail (4.5 mi)
- Cultural/Film: Sundance Film Festival (January), Park City Museum, Kimball Arts Festival (August), Egyptian Theatre, Park City Institute
- Other: three ski resorts (Park City Mountain, Deer Valley, Canyons Village), mountain biking, historic Main Street

**Accommodation research completed:**
Hotels: The Grand Summit Hotel, The Chateau at Deer Valley, The Park City Peaks Hotel
Vacation rentals: Park City Mountain Resort Condos, Deer Valley Resort Condos, Park City Vacation Rentals
