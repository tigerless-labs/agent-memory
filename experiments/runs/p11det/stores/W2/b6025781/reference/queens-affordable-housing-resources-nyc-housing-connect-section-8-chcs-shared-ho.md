**NYC Housing Connect**: Online portal for affordable housing opportunities in NYC; search, create profile, apply for lotteries

**NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD)**: Various rental assistance and homeownership programs (visit website for details)

**Queens Community House (QCH)**: Affordable rental apartments, homeownership programs, case management and job training services

**Local Housing Authorities**:
- Queens Housing Authority
- Jamaica Housing Authority
- Flushing Willets Point Corona Local Development Corporation

**Section 8 Housing Program**: Federal rental assistance for low-income individuals/families; apply through NYC Housing Authority (NYCHA) or local housing authorities

**Shared Housing Options**: Consider roommate situations or shared apartments on Craigslist, Facebook Groups, or local classifieds for more affordable options

**Note**: Always research landlord, property, and neighborhood before applying; verify lease agreements in person; be cautious of scams and deposit/rent fraud
