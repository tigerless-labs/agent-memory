Modern sculptors recommended for study:\n\n- **Anish Kapoor**: Large-scale abstract installations, reflective surfaces, voids, geometric forms\n- **Rachel Whiteread**: British sculptor, intricate large-scale installations, space and memory\n- **Richard Serra**: Large-scale site-specific installations, massive steel plates, spatial perception\n- **Jeff Koons**: Large-scale hyper-realistic sculptures, popular culture themes, reflective surfaces\n- **Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen**: Large-scale playful installations, everyday objects transformed\n- **Ursula von Rydingsvard**: Polish-American, large-scale organic abstract forms, nature and human experience\n- **Barbara Hepworth**: British modernist sculptor, biomorphic forms, space and materiality\n- **Tony Cragg**: British sculptor, abstract colorful surreal works, found objects and industrial materials\n- **Eva Rothschild**: Irish-British, large-scale installations, space and materiality\n- **Phyllida Barlow**: British, large-scale playful installations, found objects and fabric
