**Online Learning Platforms & Websites:**\n\n- Craftsy: Online courses on pottery, ceramics, coiling, scoring, and glazing\n- Pottery Making Illustrated: Step-by-step tutorials, articles, videos on pottery techniques\n- The Potter's Council: Tutorials, articles, videos on various ceramic techniques\n- Ceramic Arts Network: Community and resources for ceramic artists\n- The Ceramic School: Tutorials and pottery instruction from experienced artists\n\n**YouTube Channels:**\n\n- Simon Leach Pottery: Renowned potter with tutorials on pottery techniques and coiling\n- Pottery By Osa: Various pottery tutorials including coiling, scoring, and other techniques\n- The Ceramic School: Range of pottery tutorials taught by experienced ceramic artists\n\n**Recommended Books:**\n\n- "The Potter's Bible" by Marylin Scott: Comprehensive guide to ceramics\n- "Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter" by Ian Gregory: Detailed guide to glaze formulation and application\n\n**Learning Topics:**\n- Coiling technique\n- Scoring and slipping technique\n- Coiling and scoring a vase\n- Glaze formulation and composition\n- Glaze application methods (brushing, pouring, dipping, spraying)\n- Firing types and temperatures\n- Achieving food-safe finishes\n- Creating custom glaze recipes
