Carbon offset apps: Carbon Footprint, Offset, EcoPassenger. Accommodation: Ecobnb, Eco Hotels of the World, BookDifferent, Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb. Airlines: Qantas (Fly Carbon Neutral), Air New Zealand (FlyNeutral), KLM (CO2ZERO), Lufthansa (Compensaid), SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United. Certifications: LEED, EarthCheck, Bluesign, GOTS, OEKO-TEX. Transportation apps: Citymapper, Moovit, Waze Carpool. Waste reduction: Refill, iRecycle, Zero Waste Travel. Other apps: HappyCow, EcoChallenge, Susty, TripIt, Rome2rio, Google Trips. Gear: refillable water bottles, beeswax wraps, rechargeable power banks, solid toiletries, solar chargers, reusable straws, reef-safe sunscreen, sustainable luggage.
