User is planning a museum visit with their niece to the Natural History Museum, focused on dinosaur exhibits. Key activities planned:
- Dino Dig exhibit: interactive fossil casting station where kids create and take home a plaster fossil cast (can be decorated at home with paint/markers)
- Dino-Mite Tour: guided tour for ages 5-12, lasts about 45 minutes to 1 hour, covers major exhibits (Diplodocus, T. rex, Triceratops)

User is a member of the History Museum (separate institution), so won't receive Natural History Museum member discounts. Plans to book both activities separately at standard rates.

Exhibits and tours should be booked in advance, especially during peak seasons.
