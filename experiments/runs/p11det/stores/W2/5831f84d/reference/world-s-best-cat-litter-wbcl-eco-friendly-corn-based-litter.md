Made from 100% natural corn kernels. Biodegradable, non-toxic, dust-free. Excellent odor control and clumping. Varieties: Original, Multiple Cat Clumping, Zero Mess, Forest. Available at Petco. More expensive than clay; prone to mold if not maintained; some cats may not accept texture. Eco-friendly alternative for cat litter.
