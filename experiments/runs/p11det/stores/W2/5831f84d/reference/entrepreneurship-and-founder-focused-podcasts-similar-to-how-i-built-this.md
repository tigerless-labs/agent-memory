Recommended podcasts: The Tim Ferriss Show (habits and routines of successful people), Masters of Scale by Reid Hoffman (scaling businesses), The GaryVee Audio Experience (business and marketing), How I Raised It (fundraising stories), The Hustle (daily stories of entrepreneurs and innovators), StartUp (startup building stories), The Entrepreneur's Journey (founder interviews), Mixergy (Andrew Warner deep-dive interviews), The Foundr Podcast (founder interviews), The School of Greatness with Lewis Howes (entrepreneurs and thought leaders), Wisdom From The Top by Guy Raz (founder stories). All feature entrepreneurial narratives focused on building companies, overcoming challenges, and business insights.
