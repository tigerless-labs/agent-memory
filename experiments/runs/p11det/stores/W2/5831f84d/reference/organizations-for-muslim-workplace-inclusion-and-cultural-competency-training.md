Key organizations for workplace inclusion and cultural competency training:
- ISNA (Islamic Society of North America) — workplace inclusion toolkit
- CAIR (Council on American-Islamic Relations) — employer resource guide
- MPAC (Muslim Public Affairs Council) — workplace inclusion initiative  
- ICNA (Islamic Circle of North America)
- ISPU (Institute for Social Policy and Understanding) — research-based training
- Local Muslim organizations and mosques — tailored offerings for specific needs

Also: Muslim Women's Alliance, American Muslim Women's Empowerment Council
