The Hideout (Logan Square): Weekly open mic Tuesday - music, comedy, poetry. The Whistler (Logan Square): Weekly singer-songwriter showcase Wednesday. Uncommon Ground (Wrigleyville): Coffee shop with open mics and singer-songwriter events. The Elbo Room (Lakeview): Open mics and singer-songwriter showcases. Lincoln Hall (Lincoln Park): Mid-sized venue for indie folk/rock. Schubas Tavern (Lakeview): Great indie folk and rock shows.
