Recommended plants: Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) - removes formaldehyde/xylene, easy to propagate; Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) - removes formaldehyde/benzene/trichloroethylene, tolerates low light; Peace Lily - removes ammonia/benzene; Dracaena - removes multiple toxins, 40+ species; Pothos - removes formaldehyde/xylene/toluene, grows to 8 feet; Rubber Plant - removes formaldehyde/benzene/trichloroethylene; Aloe Vera - medicinal properties; Bamboo Palm - grows to 12 feet. All remove common air pollutants. Prefer bright indirect light, well-draining soil, temperatures 65-75F (18-24C).
