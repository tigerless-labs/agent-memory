Recommended Toronto neighborhoods for refugees and newcomers:\n\n1. **Downtown East** (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown) - High concentration of newcomer communities, refugee organizations, community centers, language schools; close to city center\n\n2. **Scarborough** - Large immigrant population from Africa, Asia, Middle East; Scarborough Village Recreation Centre offers newcomer services, language classes, cultural programs\n\n3. **North York** - Significant refugee population; North York Community House and Centre for Information and Community Services provide support; close to York University with strong refugee support program\n\n4. **Etobicoke** - Growing refugee community; Etobicoke Newcomer Centre and West Toronto Newcomer Services offer support; close to Toronto Pearson International Airport\n\n5. **Parkdale** - History of welcoming refugees/immigrants; strong sense of community; many social services; close to city center; vibrant arts and cultural scene\n\n**Factors to consider when choosing**: proximity to community centers/language schools/support services, access to public transit/grocery/healthcare, safety and walkability, affordable housing availability, cultural/linguistic diversity
