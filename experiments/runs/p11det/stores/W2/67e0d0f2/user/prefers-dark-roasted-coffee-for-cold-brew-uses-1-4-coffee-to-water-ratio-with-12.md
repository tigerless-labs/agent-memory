Prefers dark roasted coffee for cold brew brewing. Key specifications:
- **Ratio**: 1 part coffee (120-140g) to 4 parts water (960ml)
- **Grind**: Coarse grind
- **Steep time**: 12-24 hours
- **Reasoning**: Dark roasts have lower acidity and bold, rich flavor that works well with cold brew's low-acidity extraction method; produces smooth, mellow flavor with chocolate/caramel notes instead of the brighter, more acidic profile of light roasts.
