Five blockchain-based proposal concepts explored for Cardano's Project Catalyst funding from Ghana:

1. **Cardano-based remittance system** — Decentralized system for Ghanaians abroad to send money home with fast, low-cost transactions, real-time tracking, mobile integration.

2. **Cardano-based microfinance platform** — Peer-to-peer lending/borrowing for Ghanaian small businesses and entrepreneurs, with credit scoring and investment tracking.

3. **Cardano education and awareness in Ghana** — Workshops, meetups, online tutorials, partnerships with local organizations to build Cardano knowledge.

4. **Cardano-based voting system for Ghana** — Decentralized voting using blockchain for secure, transparent elections to improve turnout and accountability.

5. **Cardano-based agricultural supply chain solution** — Blockchain system to improve transparency, traceability, and efficiency in Ghana's agricultural sector (farm to table). Includes logistics optimization, international market access, smart contracts for cross-border trade, impact metrics, stakeholder involvement, budget planning, and community benefits (transparency, reduced fraud, improved efficiency, financial inclusion, community development).
