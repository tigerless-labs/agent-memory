Interested in trying chicken fajita recipe at home for the first time. Familiar with eating fajitas but hasn't cooked them before. Basic recipe includes: marinated chicken strips, sautéed onions and bell peppers, lime juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, cumin, paprika. Variations include sumac sprinkle, spicy jalapeños, avocado salsa, and lime crema toppings.
