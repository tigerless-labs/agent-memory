## Key Concepts\n\n**4 Laws of Behavior Change:**\n1. Make it Obvious — increase awareness of habits and their cues\n2. Make it Attractive — create environment supporting desired habits\n3. Make it Easy — reduce decisions, minimize friction\n4. Make it Satisfying — celebrate wins to reinforce habit\n\n**Core Strategies:**\n- Aggregation of Marginal Gains: 1% improvement daily compounds to remarkable results\n- 2-Minute Rule: Make first step take <2 minutes to reduce activation energy\n- Habit Stacking: Layer new habits onto existing ones\n- Don't Miss Twice: Return to habit immediately after missing to prevent broken chains\n- Identity-Based Habits: Build habits aligned with values and identity\n- Systems Over Goals: Focus on processes, not just endpoints\n\n**Reader Applications:**\n- Create habit trackers to monitor progress\n- Focus on small, achievable goals for momentum\n- Recognize environment and community influence\n- Develop growth mindset around setbacks
