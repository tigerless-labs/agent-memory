Snake plant was dropping leaves. Root cause: overwatering every 4 days. Solution: adjusted watering to every 7-10 days during hot weather. Key learning: use finger test—water only when top 1-2 inches of soil are dry. Snake plants are drought-adapted and thrive on neglect. Always water at soil level (not leaves) and ensure pot drains well. Next: monitor plant recovery.
