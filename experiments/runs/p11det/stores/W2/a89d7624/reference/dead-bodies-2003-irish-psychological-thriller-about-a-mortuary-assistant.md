**Dead Bodies** is a 2003 Irish psychological thriller directed by Robert Quinn.

**Plot**: Centers on Tommy (played by Andrew Scott), a hospital mortuary assistant. He discovers a dead body with money in its mouth, which sets him on a course of sudden and dangerous events.

**Themes**: Explores grief, mental health, and desperation. The narrative is intense and has a grim edge.

**Genre**: Psychological thriller
