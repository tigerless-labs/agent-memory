Started a daily gratitude practice: writing down three things grateful for each day in a dedicated physical journal. Inspired by similar gratitude sharing on Instagram. Tips for practice:\n\n- Be specific about gratitudes (e.g., 'sister's listening ear' not just 'family')\n- Mix big and small things\n- Reflect on why each thing is important\n- Add a 'because' statement to each gratitude\n\nThis supports mental well-being and positivity.
