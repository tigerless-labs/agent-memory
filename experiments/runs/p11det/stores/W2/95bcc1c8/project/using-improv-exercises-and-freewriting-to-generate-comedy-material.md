Approach to comedy writing and material development:
- Incorporate improvisational exercises and freewriting into writing process
- Draw from personal experiences and find universal themes in specific stories
- Use exaggeration and embellishment to amplify real-life situations
- Self-deprecation as a relatable comedic device
- Focus on authenticity and vulnerability to connect with audience
- Be specific rather than general in joke details

Guiding principle: be open to new experiences and creative ideas, avoiding overthinking during the generative phase.
