Recommended Beatles albums to start with when exploring their work:

**Revolver** (1966) — Marked a turning point in the band's career. Features psychedelic rock ('Tomorrow Never Knows'), Indian-influenced music ('Love You To'), and experimental sounds.

**Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band** (1967) — Landmark concept album exploring a wide range of musical styles and themes. Includes 'Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds', 'With a Little Help from My Friends', and the title track.

**Abbey Road** (1969) — The Beatles' final album, widely regarded as a masterpiece. Includes 'Come Together', 'Something', and 'Here Comes the Sun'.

**Rubber Soul** (1965) — Marked a significant departure from straightforward rock and roll toward experimentation and new sounds. Includes 'Norwegian Wood', 'Drive My Car', and 'In My Life'.

**The White Album** (1968) — A sprawling, eclectic double album spanning many styles, from gentle balladry ('Julia') to heavy rock ('Helter Skelter').

These recommendations were given as starting points for someone wanting to appreciate the Beatles' classical influences and experimental approach.
