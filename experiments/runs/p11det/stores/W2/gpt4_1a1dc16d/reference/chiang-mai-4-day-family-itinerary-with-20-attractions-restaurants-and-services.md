Detailed 4-day Chiang Mai itinerary recommended May 2023 for children ages 6-13. Includes Doi Suthep-Pui (4.6★, 30-10 THB), Elephant Nature Park (4.8★, 3500-1750 THB), Chiang Mai Zoo (4.1★, 150-70 THB), Art in Paradise (4.3★, 400-200 THB), Doi Inthanon National Park (4.6★, 300-150 THB), Mae Sa Elephant Camp (4.3★, 2400-1200 THB), and Baan Tawai Village. Restaurants include Khao Soi Mae Sai (4.5★), Dash! Teak House (4.4★), SP Chicken (4.4★), The Riverside (4.3★), The Salad Concept (4.5★), The Good View (4.3★). Massage options: Lila Thai (4.6★, 250 THB), Fah Lanna (4.6★, 300 THB). Desserts: Mango Tango (4.2★), Bake & Bite (4.4★). All venues within 1-hour drive, rated 4+, with adult/child pricing in THB.
