Recommended posting frequencies and peak engagement times (EST):

**Facebook:**
- Frequency: 3-5 times per week
- Peak times: 12 pm, 3 pm, 9 pm
- Note: Algorithm prioritizes friends/family content; overposting reduces visibility

**Twitter:**
- Frequency: 5-10 times per day
- Peak times: 12 pm, 3 pm, 5 pm
- Note: Fast-paced platform allows frequent posting

**Instagram:**
- Frequency: 3-5 times per week
- Peak times: 12 pm, 5 pm, 8 pm
- Note: Visually-oriented; prioritize quality over quantity

**LinkedIn:**
- Frequency: 2-3 times per week
- Peak times: 10 am, 12 pm, 2 pm
- Note: Professional platform; focus on valuable, informative content

General best practices: Space posts to avoid feed flooding, mix content types (videos, images, links, text), use automation tools (Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social) for scheduling, analyze performance data to adjust frequency
