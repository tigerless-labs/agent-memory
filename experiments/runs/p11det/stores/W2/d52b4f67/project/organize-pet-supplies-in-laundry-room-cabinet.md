User stores pet supplies in a laundry room cabinet that is becoming cluttered. Items include: food and treats, toys and chews, grooming supplies, medications and supplements, flea and tick prevention, collars, and leashes.

**Organization strategy:**
- Categorize supplies by type
- Assign each category to a specific shelf or section
- Use labeled storage bins and containers
- Maximize vertical space with stackable shelves
- Keep frequently used items accessible
- Add labels/signs for easy identification
- Monthly purge and maintenance to prevent clutter buildup
- Store heavy items (pet food) on bottom shelf
- Consider using turntables/lazy Susans for back-of-cabinet items
