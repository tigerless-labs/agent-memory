## Current economic sectors
- **Manufacturing**: Textiles, electronics, advanced technologies
- **Retail**: Mix of independent boutiques and chain stores
- **Healthcare**: Medical facilities and providers, significant employment
- **Tourism & Hospitality**: Driven by historic sites, museums, beaches
- **Information Technology**: Growing sector (software development, data analysis, cybersecurity)

## Growth opportunities
- **Renewable Energy**: Offshore wind potential given coastline location
- **Advanced Manufacturing**: 3D printing, automation, additive manufacturing
- **Healthtech & Biotech**: Building on strong healthcare sector
- **Creative Industries**: Vibrant existing art/creative scene with expansion potential

## Recent initiatives supporting growth
- **Infrastructure investment**: High street renovation, Northern Gateway mixed-use development
- **Enterprise Zone**: Business rates relief, simplified planning, commercial property discounts
- **Skills & Training**: Colchester Institute vocational programs (engineering, construction, hairdressing, etc.)
- **Business Support**: Colchester Borough Council support teams for entrepreneurs

## Preservation & development balance strategy
- **Adaptive reuse**: Converting historic buildings to modern uses (e.g., Colchester Savings Bank → North Hill Noodle Bar, Queen Street Brewery → Queen Street Brewhouse)
- **New development**: Modern buildings that pay homage to historic character while incorporating modern design/technology
