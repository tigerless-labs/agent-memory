**Natural Food Sources:** salmon, tuna, mackerel, beef liver, egg yolks, cheese, mushrooms.

**Fortified Foods:** milk, yogurt, orange juice, cereals, plant-based milk alternatives.

**Sunlight Exposure:** Aim for 15–30 minutes daily, preferably at midday when UVB rays are strongest. Avoid sunscreen during this exposure to maximize vitamin D synthesis, but balance with sun protection (sunscreen, hats, protective clothing) to prevent skin damage.

**For People Who Work Indoors:** 
- Take a 10–15 minute walk outside during lunch break
- Eat vitamin D-rich foods regularly
- Consider supplements (available as capsules, tablets, gummies)
- Use a light therapy box if sunlight access is very limited
- Monitor vitamin D levels with a healthcare provider

**Supplements:** Follow doctor's advice on dosage; excessive vitamin D can be harmful. Check vitamin D levels before supplementing, especially in areas with limited winter sunlight.

**Factors Affecting Vitamin D Production:** skin tone, age, season, time of day, amount of skin exposed.
