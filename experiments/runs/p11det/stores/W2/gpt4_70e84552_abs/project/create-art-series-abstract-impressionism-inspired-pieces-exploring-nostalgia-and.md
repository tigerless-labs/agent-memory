**Series Project: Nostalgia & Impermanence**

Creating a series of abstract pieces inspired by impressionism but with personal twist.

**Themes:**
- Nostalgia and memory
- Impermanence—how things are lost or forgotten over time
- Recontextualizing vintage posters (originally meant for advertising/promotion)
- Blend of old and new elements

**Techniques being tested:**
- Layering paint and ink
- Using gel medium to create texture and depth
- Layering colors and textures for luminosity
- Decoupaging vintage posters onto canvas
- Using vintage posters as base for abstract pieces or in collages

**Inspiration:**
- Impressionist paintings (especially lighting and color use)
- Museum exhibition reference book with close-up shots

**Status:** In experimentation phase with ideas developing
