Planning a farm open house event featuring:

**Kids activities:**
- Petting zoo (goats, sheep, rabbits)
- Farm-themed crafts (paper plate chickens, cow masks, flower crowns)

**Products for sale:**
- Fresh eggs
- Farm-fresh baked goods (quiche, cookies, muffins)
- Fresh produce (seasonal)

**Pricing strategy decided:**
- Leafy greens: $2–3 per bunch
- Tomatoes: $3–5 per pound
- Cucumbers: $1–2 each
- Carrots: $2–3 per bunch
- Dozen eggs: $4–6
- Half-dozen eggs: $2–3
- Quiche: $15–20 per pie
- Cookies: $2–3 each or 6 for $10
- Muffins: $2–3 each or 6 for $12

**Marketing plans:**
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram with hashtags)
- Email newsletter to customer list
- Local event listings
- Influencer partnerships with food bloggers
- Flyers and brochures in local cafes
- Word-of-mouth referrals with incentives
- Collaborations with nearby farms
- Pre-event farm tours
- Press coverage outreach
- Clear on-farm signage
- Product samples at event
- Pre-registration discounts

Event is planned for soon, date TBD.
