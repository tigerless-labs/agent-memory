1920s slang phrases for incorporating into Mary Sunshine character dialogue:\n\n**Greetings:**\n- "The berries!" (excellent, great)\n- "Hello, sweetie!" or "Hello, sport!" (informal greetings)\n- "What's the haps, old sport?" (what's happening)\n\n**Expressions:**\n- "The cat's meow" (something excellent or fashionable)\n- "The bee's knees" (something excellent or impressive)\n- "23 Skidoo" (to leave quickly or take advantage of opportunity)\n- "All wet" (someone who is foolish or gullible)\n\n**Insults:**\n- "You're a regular flat tire" (someone who is boring)\n- "You're a pill" (someone who is annoying)\n- "You're a wet blanket" (someone spoiling the fun)\n\n**Approval:**\n- "I'm all for it, kiddo!" (I'm in agreement)\n- "You're the cat's pajamas!" (you're excellent)\n- "That's the spirit, old sport!" (good attitude or effort)\n\n**Miscellaneous:**\n- "Flapper" (a fashionable young woman)\n- "Sport" (term of endearment for friend)\n- "Joint" (a nightclub or speakeasy)\n- "Giggle water" (champagne or bubbly drink)\n\n**Usage tip:** Use slang sparingly, with playful and carefree spirit suited to Mary Sunshine's cheerful personality.
