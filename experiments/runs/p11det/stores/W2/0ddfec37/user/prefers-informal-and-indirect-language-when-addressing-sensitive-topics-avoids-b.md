When communicating about potentially sensitive topics or redirecting behavior, prefers:\n- Informal tone over formal\n- Indirect language over direct statements (e.g., 'maybe not so much about the promotion or advertisement' rather than 'don't do a sales pitch')\n- Gentle redirection rather than explicit prohibition\n\nHelped with: asking a speaker to avoid sales-focused content at community event (2026-09-02)
