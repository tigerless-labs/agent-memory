**Small Batch Dill Pickle Recipe (1-2 jars)**

Ingredients:
- 1-2 cups sliced cucumbers (about 1/2 to 1 lb)
- 1/8 cup kosher salt
- 1/8 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/8 cup white vinegar
- 1 tsp dried dill weed
- 1/2 tsp whole black peppercorns
- 1/8 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)

Instructions:
1. Create the brine: combine water, salt, sugar, and vinegar in saucepan; bring to boil
2. Pack cucumbers: slice into 1/4-inch rounds/spears, pack tightly into jar leaving 1 inch space
3. Add dill and spices: sprinkle over cucumbers
4. Pour the brine: pour hot brine over cucumbers until covered
5. Seal and ferment: close jar loosely; ferment at room temperature (70-75°F) for 3-5 days until desired tanginess
6. Store: refrigerate to slow fermentation; keeps several months

Uses lacto-fermentation method where lactic acid bacteria produces preservative acid.
