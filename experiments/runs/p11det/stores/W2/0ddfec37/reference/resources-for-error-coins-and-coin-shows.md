Resources for error coin collecting and finding coin shows:
- **American Numismatic Association (ANA)** (money.org) - Has "Coin Shows" section where you can search by state, city, or zip code
- **CoinWeek.com** - Comprehensive coin show calendar listing upcoming shows across the United States; can filter by state or month
- **Local coin dealers and shops** - Often have information on upcoming shows and events not listed online
- **Online communities** - Coin Collecting subreddit, Facebook groups dedicated to error coins; members share information on upcoming shows and events
- **Google Search** - Search for "coin shows near me" or "coin shows in [city/state]" for online event calendars and local coin clubs

Tips: Verify dates, times, and locations of shows as they can be subject to change
