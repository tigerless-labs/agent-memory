When preparing for mild, rainy winter climate, prefers waterproof jackets with breathable membranes (Gore-Tex quality), insulated layers, waterproof boots with good grip, and moisture-wicking accessories. Values quality materials and considers second-hand/thrift options for cost-effectiveness.
