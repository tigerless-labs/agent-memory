Complete warm-up takes 15-30 minutes before cycling:

Light Cardio (5-10 min):
- Light pedaling on stationary bike, trainer, or easy road spin
- Gradually increase heart rate and blood flow to muscles

Dynamic Stretching (5-10 min):
- Leg swings: forward and backward, alternating legs
- Hip circles: clockwise and counterclockwise rotation
- Knee lifts: lift one knee toward chest, alternate legs
- Ankle rotations: both clockwise and counterclockwise

Muscle Activation (5-10 min):
- Glute bridges: lie on back with knees bent, lift hips, squeeze glutes
- Calf raises: stand on edge of curb/step with heels off, raise onto toes
- Core engagement: sit on bike with hands on hoods, draw belly button toward spine

Final Spin (5-10 min):
- Gradually increase intensity to moderate pace
- Focus on pedaling technique, cadence, and breathing
- Prepare mind and body for the ride
