Maintains a reading log to track reading progress and write down thoughts on each book. Finds this practice helpful for staying motivated and retaining information.

Uses reflection prompts when reading, especially for non-fiction self-help books. These include:
- Main takeaways and key ideas
- Emotional impact and inspiration
- Memorable quotes and passages
- Relation to current life situation or goals
- Recommended next actions
- Critical thinking on author's claims
- Inspirational and motivational aspects

Started this practice to help retain more information, gain deeper insights, and apply lessons to life.

Established by 2023-05-30.
