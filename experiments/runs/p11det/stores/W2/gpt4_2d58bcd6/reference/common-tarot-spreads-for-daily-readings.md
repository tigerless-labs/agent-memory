**Single Card Spread:** One card for insight into the day.

**Three-Card Spread:** Past, present, future.

**Celtic Cross Spread:** Ten-card complex spread, adaptable for daily readings.

**Daily Focus Spread:** Overall energy (card 1), what to focus on (card 2), outcome (card 3).

**Mind, Body, Spirit Spread:** Mind (card 1), body (card 2), spirit (card 3) — useful for holistic daily balance.
