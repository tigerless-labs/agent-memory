## Books similar to 'The Hate U Give'
- 'Dear Martin' by Nic Stone
- 'The Poet X' by Elizabeth Acevedo
- 'Long Way Down' by Jason Reynolds
- 'All American Boys' by Jason Reynolds and Brendan Kiely
- 'The Sun is Also a Star' by Nicola Yoon

## Authors for diverse voices and social justice
- Jason Reynolds
- Nicola Yoon
- Renee Watson ('Piecing Me Together', 'Betty Before X')
- Ibi Zoboi ('American Street', 'Pride')
- Dhonielle Clayton ('The Belles', 'Shattered Midnight')
- Tomi Adeyemi ('Children of Blood and Bone')
- Sabaa Tahir ('An Ember in the Ashes')
- Nnedi Okorafor ('Who Fears Death', 'Binti')

## Audiobooks with excellent narration
- 'The Night Circus' (Erin Morgenstern), narrated by Jim Dale
- 'The Power' (Naomi Alderman), narrated by Adjoa Andoh
- 'The Song of Achilles' (Madeline Miller), narrated by Frazer Douglas
- 'The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August' (Claire North), narrated by Peter Kenny
- 'The Hate U Give' (Angie Thomas), narrated by Bahni Turpin
