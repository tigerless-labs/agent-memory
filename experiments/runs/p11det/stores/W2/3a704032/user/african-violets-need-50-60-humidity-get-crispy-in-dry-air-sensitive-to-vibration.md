African violets thrive in relative humidity of 50-60%. Without adequate humidity, leaves become crispy and brittle. Sensitive to vibrations and prone to fungal diseases, so require: quiet humidifier (low noise operation), good air circulation to prevent disease, and placement away from water standing on leaves. Position humidifier within 1-2 feet for effectiveness. Requires monitoring to avoid over-humidification which causes root rot.
