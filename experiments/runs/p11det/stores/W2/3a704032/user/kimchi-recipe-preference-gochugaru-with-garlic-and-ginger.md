Prefers to make kimchi using gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) as the base spice, combined with minced garlic and grated fresh ginger. Target measurements: 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon gochugaru per 2 cups of vegetables (starting conservative), 2-3 cloves of garlic per 2 cups vegetables, 1-2 inches of grated ginger per 2 cups vegetables. Learned to measure by weight when possible and taste as going along to avoid over-seasoning.
