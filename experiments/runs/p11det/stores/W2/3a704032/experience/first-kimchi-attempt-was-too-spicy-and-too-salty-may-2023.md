Attempted to make kimchi for the first time on approximately May 14-15, 2023. The result was over-spiced and over-salted, making it unpalatable. Learned that too much gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) and excessive salt in the brine were the main issues. Planned to rescue the batch by rinsing under cold running water and adjust seasoning for future attempts.
