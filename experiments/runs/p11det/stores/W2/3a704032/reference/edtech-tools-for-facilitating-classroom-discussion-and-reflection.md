Four collaborative platforms recommended for promoting student engagement through discussion and reflection activities:

1. **Padlet** — Collaborative platform for sharing thoughts and ideas in a virtual space
2. **Nearpod** — Interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions for student participation
3. **Flipgrid** — Video discussion platform where students respond to prompts with short videos
4. **Google Jamboard** — Virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual idea-sharing

All suitable for facilitating discussion and reflection in engaging, interactive ways for K-12 classrooms.
