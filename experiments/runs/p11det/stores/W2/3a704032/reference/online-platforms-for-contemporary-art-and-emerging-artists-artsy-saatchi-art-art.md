Recommended platforms for discovering contemporary art and emerging artists:

- **Artsy**: Leading platform featuring emerging and established artists, with dedicated emerging artist section
- **Saatchi Art**: Online art gallery focused on emerging artists  
- **Artspace**: Contemporary art platform with curated selection and artist profiles
- **Uprise Art**: Platform focusing on emerging artists with curated exhibitions
- **Behance**: Showcases creatives including artists and designers
- **Juxtapoz**: Online art magazine featuring emerging and established artists
- **Hyperallergic**: Art blog with news, exhibitions, and artist profiles
- **ArtNet**: Platform with news, exhibitions, and dedicated emerging artist section
- **1stdibs**: Marketplace with curated contemporary art by leading galleries
- **Minted**: Platform featuring emerging artists and designers
- **Collecteurs**: Contemporary art platform with curated selection
- **New American Paintings**: Features emerging painters from the US
- **The Artist's Institute**: Emerging artists through exhibitions and essays
- **Platform**: Showcases emerging artists from around the world

These platforms offer personalized recommendations, online exhibitions, virtual events, artist talks, and opportunities to purchase artworks.
