Netflix serves as primary unwinding mechanism after long work days. Rather than eliminating, making usage intentional and balanced: choose specific show/movie in advance rather than browsing, set timer to prevent marathon sessions, make social when possible with friends/family. Exploring alternative screen-free relaxation activities: reading books, journaling, writing, drawing/painting/crafting, board games/puzzles, musical instruments, cooking/baking, exercise/yoga/meditation, time with others (phone-free), board games. Concerned about replacing one screen habit with another - mitigating with screen-free zones and alternative activity substitution.
