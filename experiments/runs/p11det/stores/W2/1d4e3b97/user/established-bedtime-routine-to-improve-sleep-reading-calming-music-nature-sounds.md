Has been establishing a bedtime routine to address recent sleep troubles:

- **Reading**: 20-30 minutes of reading before bed
- **Calming sounds**: Listening to calming music or nature sounds on phone while reading
- **White noise machine**: Using a white noise machine to block out outside noise, which helps with sound sleep
- **Timing**: Getting ready for bed earlier

These changes have been helping improve sleep quality.
