Popular herbal teas known for calming and relaxing properties that can help with sleep:

1. **Chamomile tea**: Classic choice for relaxation; soothes the nervous system and promotes peaceful sleep
2. **Lavender tea**: Known for calming properties; reduces stress and anxiety
3. **Valerian root tea**: Natural sedative; improves sleep quality and helps with staying asleep
4. **Peppermint tea**: Calms the mind and body; menthol helps ease falling asleep
5. **Tart cherry tea**: Contains melatonin; helps improve sleep quality and duration
6. **Passionflower tea**: Natural sedative; reduces anxiety and insomnia
7. **Lemon balm tea**: Calming herb; reduces stress and anxiety; improves sleep quality
8. **Rooibos tea**: Caffeine-free; contains antioxidants; promotes relaxation
9. **Hibiscus tea**: Calming tea; helps lower blood pressure and promote relaxation
10. **GABA tea**: Contains GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid); helps regulate sleep and reduce anxiety

**Tips for selecting and brewing**:
- Choose high-quality, organic brands free of added sugars and artificial flavorings
- Follow package instructions for brewing
- Enjoy 30-60 minutes before bedtime
- Can blend different herbs to create unique flavors and effects
