## Labels following in Blue Note Records' footsteps

- **ECM Records** — German label (founded 1969), focuses on innovative jazz and classical music, known for artistic freedom and experimentation. Notable artists: Keith Jarrett, Pat Metheny, Jan Garbarek

- **Ropeadope Records** — US-based label (founded 1999), committed to supporting creative and innovative musicians with eclectic roster. Notable artists: Christian McBride, Robert Glasper, Snarky Puppy

- **Revive Music Group** — US music company (founded 2006), began as concert promotion, expanded to record label and artist management. Known for promoting young and emerging jazz artists with showcase opportunities

All three prioritize creativity and innovation over commercial success, mirroring Blue Note Records' founding philosophy.
