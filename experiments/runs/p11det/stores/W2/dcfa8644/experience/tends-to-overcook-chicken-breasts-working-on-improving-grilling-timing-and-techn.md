**Issue:** Recently overcooked chicken breasts while testing sweet and smoky BBQ sauce. Needs to improve timing.

**Techniques to improve:**
- Use a meat thermometer to reach 165°F internal temp (no guessing)
- Preheat grill to medium-high (400-425°F)
- Pat chicken dry before grilling to prevent moisture
- Oil grill grates before and during cooking
- Don't press down on chicken with spatula (squeezes out juices)
- Flip every 4-5 minutes for even cooking
- Place chicken at an angle for diagonal grill marks, then rotate 90° for crisscross pattern
- Let chicken rest a few minutes after cooking for juiciness

**Target:** Cook until internal temp is 165°F; 5-6 minutes per side typically
