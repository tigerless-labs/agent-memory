**Recipe plan to try:**

**Marinade:**
- Lime juice
- Olive oil
- Cumin
- Chili powder
- Sweet and smoky BBQ sauce

**Instructions:**
- Marinate chicken breasts in marinade
- Grill or bake until cooked through
- Shred the chicken
- Toss with BBQ sauce, lime juice, and cilantro

**Serving:**
- Serve in tacos with favorite toppings:
  - Avocado
  - Sour cream
  - Salsa
  - Cilantro
  - Other taco toppings
