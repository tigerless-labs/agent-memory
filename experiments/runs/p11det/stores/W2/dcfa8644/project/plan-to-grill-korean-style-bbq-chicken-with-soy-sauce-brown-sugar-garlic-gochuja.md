Recipe plan to try:

**Marinade:**
- Soy sauce
- Brown sugar
- Garlic
- Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- Sesame oil
- Optional: gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) for extra heat

**Instructions:**
- Marinate chicken breasts for 30 min to 1 hour (or overnight for more intense flavor)
- Grill over medium-high heat
- Cook for 5-6 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F
- Brush with sweet and smoky BBQ sauce during last 2 minutes of grilling
- Top with toasted sesame seeds and chopped green onions

**Notes:**
- Marinade promotes caramelization and creates a sticky, sweet glaze
- Adding gochugaru gives authentic Korean kick
- Pairs well with sweet and smoky BBQ sauce
