Has a close friend who has been supportive throughout anxiety and depression journey. Asked this friend to be an accountability buddy - they plan to check in with each other regularly and share progress. Finds it valuable to have someone who understands what it's like to live with anxiety and depression, rather than relying only on friends/family who may not fully understand. Accountability buddy arrangement helps stay motivated and supported. Also plans to connect with friends and family more often to reduce feelings of isolation.
