# Back Pain & Glute Activation

From a book chapter titled 'RULE #5: Use the Power in Your Posterior' (author: Jeremy).

## Key Concept: Gluteal Amnesia
Weak glutes from excessive sitting allow the spine to absorb forces meant for the glutes. This causes micro-damage over time that accumulates into macro damage. The spine and tiny paraspinal muscles are not designed to carry those loads.

## What Healthy Glutes Do
- Help maintain erect posture
- Keep you from falling forward when walking/running  
- Stabilize pelvis during single-leg stance or walking
- Assist in rising from a seated position
- Support picking things up, climbing stairs, running, rowing

## Solution: Three Steps
1. Wake up the glutes
2. Make them strong
3. Learn to use them at appropriate times (as designed)

## Exercises Mentioned
- **Bridge exercise** — activates glutes
- **Clamshell exercise** — critical for waking up glutes
  - Lie on side with hips/knees bent
  - Use fingers to feel glute activation (establish brain-muscle connection)
  - Find neutral spine and brace core
  - Often poorly performed but highly effective

## Connection to Back Health
High correlation between sleeping/weak glutes and back pain. Activating glutes relieves strain on spine and reflexively loosens tight psoas muscles.
