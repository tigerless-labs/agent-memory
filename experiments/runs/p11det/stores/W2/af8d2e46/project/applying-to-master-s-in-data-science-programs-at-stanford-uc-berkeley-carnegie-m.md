**Target Programs:**
- Stanford University - Master of Science in Data Science (MS-DS)
  - Acceptance rate: 5-7% (extremely competitive)
  - Emphasizes: machine learning, AI, data-driven research
- University of California, Berkeley - Master of Science in Data Science (MS-DS)
  - Acceptance rate: 10-15% (highly competitive)
  - Collaborative program between Statistics, Computer Science, and Electrical Engineering
  - Focus: practical applications, social impact
- Carnegie Mellon University - Master of Science in Data Science (MS-DS)
  - Acceptance rate: 10-15% (highly competitive)
  - Emphasizes: machine learning, AI, data-driven research, business value

**Application Status (as of 2023-05-20):**
- GRE: Completed, score 320 (competitive)
- Personal statement: In progress
- Timeline: Application materials being prepared

**Common Requirements across all three programs:**
- GRE (competitive scores ~320-325+)
- 3 letters of recommendation
- Personal statement (2-3 pages)
- Resume/CV
- Transcripts
- Prerequisite courses: programming, statistics, linear algebra

**Key Skills to Emphasize:**
- Data manipulation (Pandas, NumPy)
- Data visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Tableau, D3.js)
- Machine learning (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Python and R programming
- Statistical modeling and analysis
