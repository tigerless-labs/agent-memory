Portable power banks recommended for laptop charging on-the-go:\n\n**Compact options:**\n- **Anker PowerCore 10000**: 10,000mAh, 220g\n- **Mophie Powerstation Mini**: 5,000mAh, compact design\n- **Jackery Titan 20000**: 20,000mAh, sleek design\n\n**Laptop-specific:**\n- **Anker PowerCore Fusion**: 20,000mAh, designed for laptops\n- **Mophie Powerstation AC**: 22,000mAh, includes AC outlet\n- **Eggtronic Power Bank**: 20,000mAh, multiple USB ports including USB-C\n\n**Key features**: Capacity, size/weight, charging speed (QuickCharge, PowerDelivery), multiple USB ports, safety features (overcharge/short-circuit protection).\n\nRelible brands: Anker (known for quality and compact designs), Mophie (stylish), Jackery (high-capacity options).
