**Tokyo Restaurants for Traditional Japanese Cuisine:**\n\n**Sushi:**\n- Sushi Saito (3-Michelin-starred)\n- Sushi Dai (famous for traditional Edomae-style sushi)\n\n**Ramen:**\n- Ichiran Shibuya (unique, rich tonkotsu broth)\n- Totto Ramen (classic, flavorful shoyu ramen)\n\n**Tempura:**\n- Tempura Kondo (high-end, seasonal ingredients)\n- Tempura Tsunahachi (classic, affordable)\n\n**Yakitori:**\n- Toriki (wide variety of skewers, lively atmosphere)\n- Yakitori Alley (Omoide Yokocho - small alley with multiple yakitori shops)
