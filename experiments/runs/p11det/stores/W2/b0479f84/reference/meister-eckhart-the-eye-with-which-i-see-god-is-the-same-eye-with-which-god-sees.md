Meister Eckhart quote used to bridge Christian and contemplative/mystical approaches to God. The quote suggests perception of divine is relational and participatory—not objective knowledge of an external object, but mutual awareness. Used in teaching to reconcile Christianity with Eastern-influenced spirituality.
