Weekday morning structure example: 6:30 wake/stretch, 6:45-7:15 exercise, 7:15-7:45 shower/ready, 7:45-8:15 breakfast, 8:15 leave. Allocate 15-30 min wake ritual, 30 min exercise, 30-45 min shower, 30 min breakfast, 15-30 min buffer.

Exercise tips: set specific goals, warm up 5-10 min, cool down 5-10 min, vary routine, incorporate strength training, track progress, listen to body.

Commute usage: podcasts/audiobooks for learning, plan daily tasks, respond to urgent emails, meditation, journaling, catch up on news.

Early arrival to office (5-15 min): prioritize tasks, respond to urgent emails, review schedule, plan breaks, get head start on first task, organize workspace, meditate/stretch, review goals, learn something new.

Weekend approach: 8:30 AM wake, 9:00 meditate/yoga, 9:30 shower, 10:00 leisurely breakfast, 11:00+ plan day. Focus on relaxation and self-care rather than rushing.
