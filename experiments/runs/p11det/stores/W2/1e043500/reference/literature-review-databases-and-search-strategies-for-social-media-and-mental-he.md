## Academic Databases
- **PsycINFO** (American Psychological Association) — premier database for psychology and mental health research
- **PubMed** (National Library of Medicine) — comprehensive database for biomedical and life sciences literature
- **Scopus** (Elsevier) — large interdisciplinary database covering STEM, social sciences, humanities
- **Web of Science** (Clarivate Analytics) — multidisciplinary database covering science, social sciences, humanities
- **Google Scholar** (Google) — search engine for scholarly literature across disciplines

## Recommended Search Operators & Techniques
- Use Boolean operators: AND, OR, NOT
- Use wildcards: * (e.g., "mental health*")
- Use quotes for exact phrases: "social media usage patterns"
- Combine keywords strategically: ("social media" AND "mental health" AND "young adults")
- Use "cited by" feature to find recent articles citing influential studies
- Check reference lists of relevant articles for discovery
- Filter by date: focus on recent studies (2015-2022 or later)
- Look for reviews, meta-analyses, systematic reviews for comprehensive overviews

## Sample Search Strings
- ("social media" AND "mental health" AND "young adults") OR ("social media" AND "depression" AND "anxiety" AND "young adults")
- ("social media usage patterns" AND "symptoms of depression" AND "young adults")
- ("mediating role" AND "self-esteem" AND "social media" AND "mental health")
- ("social media-based intervention" AND "mental health" AND "young adults")
- ("online support groups" AND "mental health") OR ("peer support" AND "online communities")
