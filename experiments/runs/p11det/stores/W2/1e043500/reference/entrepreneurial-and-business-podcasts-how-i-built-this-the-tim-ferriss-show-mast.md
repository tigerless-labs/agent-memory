Recommended entrepreneurial stories and business-related podcasts:\n\n**Entrepreneurial Stories:**\n- **How I Built This** (Guy Raz): In-depth interviews with successful entrepreneurs\n- **The Tim Ferriss Show**: Habits and strategies of successful people\n- **Masters of Scale** (Reid Hoffman): How companies grow and scale\n- **The GaryVee Audio Experience** (Gary Vaynerchuk): Marketing, business, and personal development\n- **The School of Greatness** (Lewis Howes): Interviews with entrepreneurs and thought leaders\n\n**Business & Finance:**\n- **The Hustle**: Daily business, entrepreneurship, and tech news\n- **Planet Money** (NPR): Economics and finance\n- **The $100 MBA**: Practical business advice\n- **HBR IdeaCast** (Harvard Business Review): Business management and innovation\n- **The Profit First Podcast** (Mike Michalowicz): Entrepreneurship and profitability\n- **The Growth Lab** (HubSpot): Business growth and sales strategies
