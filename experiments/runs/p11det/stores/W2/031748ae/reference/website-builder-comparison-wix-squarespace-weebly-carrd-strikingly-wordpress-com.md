Popular website builders for freelancers, compared for ease of use:

**Wix**: Drag-and-drop editor, wide template selection, user-friendly interface. Plans start from USD 14/month (Connect Domain) to USD 35/month (VIP). All include custom domain, Wix ads removed, priority support.

**Squarespace**: Stylish templates, popular among creatives. Plans: Personal (USD 14/month), Business (USD 23/month), Basic Commerce (USD 27/month), Advanced Commerce (USD 49/month).

**Weebly**: Drag-and-drop editor, intuitive interface, great for beginners.

**Carrd**: Simple, free one-page website builder. Good for landing pages or minimal sites.

**Strikingly**: Specializes in one-page websites and portfolios. Easy to use.

**WordPress.com**: Hosted version of WordPress. Free plan available, with paid upgrades. Wide range of templates and plugins.

Common features across most platforms:
- Drag-and-drop editors
- Mobile-responsive designs
- SEO optimization tools
- Social media integration
- Affordable pricing with free trials or free plans
