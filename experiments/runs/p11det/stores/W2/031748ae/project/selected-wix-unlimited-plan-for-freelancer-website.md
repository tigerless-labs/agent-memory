Chose Wix Unlimited plan (USD 22/month billed annually) for professional freelancer website. Includes:
- 10GB storage (sufficient for freelancer portfolio)
- 5GB bandwidth  
- Custom domain
- Site analytics
- Email marketing tools
- Priority customer support
- 1-hour video hosting

Decided after comparing Wix, Squarespace, Weebly, Carrd, and Strikingly. Wix selected for good balance of features, price, and ease of use.
