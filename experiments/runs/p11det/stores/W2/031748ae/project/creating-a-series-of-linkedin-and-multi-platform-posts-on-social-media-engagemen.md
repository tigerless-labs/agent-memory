Starting platform: LinkedIn. Content series will expand to Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook.

Content approach: Share personal experiences and success stories, behind-the-scenes content about planning and scheduling posts, tips on engaging with audience, include social media analytics.

Key strategy: Focus on lessons learned from analytics, use visuals, share successes and failures, keep content concise, end posts with engagement questions.

Rationale: Personal experiences build authenticity and credibility. Behind-the-scenes content and thoughtfully-presented analytics add transparency and demonstrate results.
