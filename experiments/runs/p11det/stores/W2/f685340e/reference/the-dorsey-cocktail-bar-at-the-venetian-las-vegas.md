Stylish cocktail bar at The Venetian Las Vegas, 5-minute walk from Bellagio.

Specialty Cocktails: The Penicillin (blended Scotch, honey, ginger, lemon, Islay mist), The Vieux Carre (rye whiskey, Cynar, sweet vermouth, Benedictine), The Italian Sunset (gin, Aperol, lemon, grapefruit, soda)

Happy Hour 5-7pm: 10 dollar cocktails, 5 dollar beers, 7 dollar wine

Small plates: truffle popcorn, burrata, meat and cheese board, fried cauliflower, sliders

Open until 1am. Features live music and seasonal menu updates.
