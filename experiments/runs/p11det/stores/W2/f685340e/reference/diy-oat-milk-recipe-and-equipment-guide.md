**Equipment needed:**\n- High-speed blender (Vitamix or Blendtec)\n- Cheesecloth or nut milk bag\n- Large bowl or container\n- Measuring cups and spoons\n- Optional: flavorings and sweeteners\n\n**Process:**\n1. Rinse 1 cup rolled oats; soak 8+ hours or overnight\n2. Drain and rinse oats\n3. Blend soaked oats with 2 cups fresh water for ~2 minutes until smooth\n4. Pour through cheesecloth/nut milk bag into bowl; squeeze to extract milk\n5. Optional: filter through fine-mesh sieve for smoother texture\n6. Chill and store in refrigerator for 3-5 days\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use 1:2 ratio (oats:water) for creamier milk; 1:3 for lighter\n- Can add vanilla, cinnamon, dates for flavor\n- Try different oat types (steel-cut, Irish) for varying textures\n- Add pinch of sea salt to enhance flavor
