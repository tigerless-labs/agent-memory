## Maintenance Tips\n\n- Clean regularly with soft dry cloth after use to remove dirt and sweat\n- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and humidity  \n- Use racket cover to prevent scratches and damage\n- Check strings regularly for wear; re-string as needed\n- Use appropriate string tension for your playing style (avoid over/under-tightening)\n- Avoid extreme temperatures (hot car, freezing conditions)\n- Get professional servicing from stringer or tennis shop when needed
