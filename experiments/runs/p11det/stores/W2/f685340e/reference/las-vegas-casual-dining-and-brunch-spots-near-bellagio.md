Casual dining and brunch options near Bellagio:

**Casual Eats:**
- **Eataly** (Park MGM, adjacent to Bellagio) - Italian market and restaurant
- **Carson Kitchen** (Downtown Container Park, 10-minute Uber) - Gastropub with creative American comfort food

**Breakfast and Brunch:**
- **Mon Ami Gabi** (Paris Las Vegas, 5-minute walk) - French bistro with Strip views
- **The Bacchanal Buffet** (Caesars Palace, 10-minute walk) - Luxurious buffet
- **Eggslut** (The Cosmopolitan, 10-minute walk) - Popular breakfast spot with creative egg dishes

**Celebrity Chef Restaurants:**
- **Gordon Ramsay Hell's Kitchen** (Caesars Palace, 10-minute walk) - Hell's Kitchen-themed
- **Giada** (The Cromwell, 10-minute walk) - Italian by Giada De Laurentiis
