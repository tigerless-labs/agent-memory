Has been diagnosed with a herniated disc and is actively managing the condition through:
- Gentle exercise routines (stretching and core strengthening)
- Acupuncture sessions with a licensed acupuncturist for pain management and stress relief
- Progressive muscle relaxation (PMR) practice 2-3 times per week, which has proven helpful for managing pain and anxiety
- Exploring back surgery as a potential treatment option

Prefers low-impact exercises like cycling and water-based activities. Plans to continue acupuncture after surgery pending surgeon's clearance. Follows guidelines to wait for initial recovery period (2-4 weeks) before resuming intensive treatments post-operatively.

As of May 2023, using this multi-modal approach to avoid over-reliance on pain medication while maintaining activity during recovery.
