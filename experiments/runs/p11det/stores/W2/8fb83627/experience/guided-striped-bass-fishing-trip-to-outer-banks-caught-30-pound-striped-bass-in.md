Took a guided striped bass fishing trip to the Outer Banks. Weather was challenging: howling wind and rough seas. Despite conditions, the captain's expertise enabled successful fishing. Caught a particularly memorable striped bass estimated at 30 pounds, which the captain noted was one of the bigger fish he'd seen that season. The fish put up a strong fight. Trip reinforced the value of experienced guides and adaptive fishing techniques.
