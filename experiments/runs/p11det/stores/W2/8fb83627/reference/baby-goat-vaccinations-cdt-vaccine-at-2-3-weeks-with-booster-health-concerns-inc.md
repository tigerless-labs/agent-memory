Vaccination Schedule: CDT vaccine at 2-3 weeks of age, booster at 2-3 weeks later. Pasteurella multocida at 2-3 months with booster 2-3 weeks later.

Health Concerns: Scours (diarrhea) - monitor stool quality; Pneumonia - watch for coughing, rapid breathing, nasal discharge; Hypothermia - keep kids warm and dry; Parasite control - check regularly for ticks, lice, mites, and internal worms; Nutritional deficiencies - ensure balanced diet with milk/milk replacer and starter feed; Umbilical cord care - keep clean and dry, apply antiseptic daily; Disbudding/dehorning - consult vet on timing and method.
