**Location:** 50 Bowery, between Canal and Hester Streets, Chinatown. About 10-15 min walk to Times Square, 20-25 min walk or 5-10 min taxi to Central Park.

**Features:** 
- 229 modern guest rooms (King, Queen, Double, Suites available)
- Rooftop bar 'The Crown' with craft cocktails and city views
- Fitness center
- 24-hour front desk
- Complimentary continental breakfast

**Rating:** 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor

**Pros:** Unique Chinatown location with authentic restaurants, stylish modern rooms, affordable price, rooftop bar amenity.

**Cons:** Not directly in Midtown (need extra travel time), some rooms small, lively area at night.

**Amenities:** City views available in some rooms, courtyard/building views in others.
