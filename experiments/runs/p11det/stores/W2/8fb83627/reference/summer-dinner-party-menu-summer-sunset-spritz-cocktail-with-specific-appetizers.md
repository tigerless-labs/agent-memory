Menu pairing for Summer Sunset Spritz cocktail dinner party. Appetizers: Honeyed Goat Cheese Crostini (toasted baguette with creamy goat cheese, honey drizzle, fresh thyme) and Fresh Fruit and Prosciutto Skewers (grapes, berries, melon with prosciutto). Main course: Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, and rosemary. User selected these for being easy to prepare in advance, offering variety in textures and flavors, and complementing the light, refreshing spritz without overpowering it.
