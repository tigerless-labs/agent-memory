Evaluating trade-offs between:

**Potential benefits of prestigious institution:**
- Strong employer perception of education quality
- Brand equity and work ethic association  
- Extensive alumni network for job opportunities
- Perception of exclusivity/competitive admissions
- Reputation boost for career prospects

**Financial considerations:**
- Higher cost of education
- Increased debt burden
- Need to research salary differentials of graduates
- Explore scholarships and grants
- Consider ROI long-term

**Alternate options:**
- Attend lower-cost institution
- Pursue internships and work experience
- Consider graduate studies to enhance prospects
