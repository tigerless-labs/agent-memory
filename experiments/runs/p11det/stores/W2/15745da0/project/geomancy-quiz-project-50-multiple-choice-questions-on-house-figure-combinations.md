## Project Goal
Create a 50-question geomancy quiz at intermediate/advanced difficulty level.

## Format
- Multiple-choice questions (4 options each)
- Correct answer provided for each question
- Explanation for each answer choice
- Focus: house and figure combinations

## Key Geomancy Figures Covered
**Positive/Success:** Fortuna Major, Fortuna Minor, Albus, Laetitia, Acquisitio, Caput Draconis
**Negative/Challenge:** Rubeus, Tristitia, Amissio, Carcer, Cauda Draconis
**Neutral:** Populus, Via, Puer, Puella

## Houses and Their Meanings
1. House 1: Individual identity, personal goals, appearance
2. House 2: Wealth, material possessions, finances
3. House 3: Communication, siblings, short travel
4. House 4: Home, family, emotional security
5. House 5: Creativity, children, romance, fertility
6. House 6: Work, health, service, employment
7. House 7: Relationships, partnerships, marriage
8. House 8: Inheritances, transformation, shared resources
9. House 9: Higher learning, spirituality, travel, hopes and fears

## Sample Interpretations
- Fortuna Major in House 1: Prosperity and personal success
- Caput Draconis in House 2: Financial gain and stability
- Rubeus in House 3: Communication problems and misunderstandings
- Amissio in House 4: Loss or instability in home/family
- Tristitia in House 6: Physical illness and poor health
- Populus in House 7: Indecision in relationships
