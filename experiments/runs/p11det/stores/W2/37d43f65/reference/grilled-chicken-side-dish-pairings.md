Popular side dishes to serve with grilled chicken:

- **Grilled vegetables** — bell peppers, zucchini, eggplant seasoned with salt, pepper, and olive oil
- **Fresh salads** — Caesar, Greek, or other vegetable-based salads for balance
- **Corn on the cob** — grilled, with butter, salt, and paprika  
- **Baked sweet potato** — topped with butter, cinnamon, and brown sugar
- **Quinoa salad** — mixed with chopped vegetables (cucumber, tomato, bell pepper) and lemony vinaigrette for a nutritious option
