Best time to book: 54 days before departure (Skyscanner study). Prime booking window: 21-121 days before departure with prices within 5% of lowest. Avoid Tue/Wed/Sat booking; prefer Mon/Thu/Fri. Off-peak seasons cheaper: mid-Jan to mid-Mar, Apr-May, Sep-Nov. Red-eye and early morning flights often have lower fares. Use fare alerts (Airfarewatchdog, The Flight Deal, Secret Flying). Check multiple airports, clear cookies to avoid price tracking. Look for error fares.
