Display design for handmade candles uses 3-4 tier wooden crate tiered display. Decorative elements: eucalyptus greenery from prior Artisan's Fair, pinecones, decorative stones, ribbons or twine on candles, decorative lanterns with LED lights. Combines rustic wood with natural elements and warm lighting for inviting aesthetic.
