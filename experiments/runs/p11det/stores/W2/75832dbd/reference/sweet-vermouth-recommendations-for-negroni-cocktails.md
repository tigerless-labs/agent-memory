## Sweet Vermouth Options for Negroni

- **Cinzano** — Italian; sweet, fruity with vanilla and spice notes; classic choice
- **Carpano Antica Formula** — Italian; rich, complex with cherry, orange, spices; favorite among bartenders
- **Dolin Sweet Vermouth** — Chambery style; lighter, delicate, subtle sweetness and herbal notes; refined option
- **Martini & Rossi Sweet Vermouth** — Italian; sweeter than Cinzano, pronounced fruit flavor; pairs well with bold, juniper-forward gins
- **Punt e Mes** — Italian; bitter-sweet profile; slightly bitter notes enhance Campari, sweetness balances gin
- **Cocchi Sweet Vermouth** — herbs and spices blend; slightly sweet, spicy, herbal; adds complexity
- **Noilly Prat Sweet Vermouth** — French; rich, fruity with vanilla and orange notes; indulgent option

**Selection guide:**
- For traditional, bitter Negroni → Punt e Mes or Cocchi
- For sweeter Negroni → Carpano Antica Formula or Martini & Rossi
- For balanced Negroni → Dolin or Cinzano
