## Classic Negroni Recipe

**Ratio:** 1 part gin : 1 part Campari : 1 part sweet vermouth (equal parts)

**Measurement:** 1.5 oz (45 ml) of each ingredient

**Method:**
1. Stir all three ingredients with ice
2. Strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass filled with ice
3. Garnish with an orange twist

**Alternative Ratios:**
- 2:1:1 — more gin-forward, stronger botanical presence
- 1:2:1 — more Campari-forward, bolder and more bitter
- 1:1:2 — more sweet vermouth-forward, richer and sweeter

**Bitters:** A few dashes (2-3) of bitters add depth but are subtle compared to main ingredients. Angostura bitters are traditional; citrus and other specialty bitters can add complexity.
