During a weight loss discussion (2023-05-21), expressed:
- Initial desire for quick weight loss, but came to accept that gradual (1-2 lbs/week) is healthier
- Struggles with motivation when seeing no immediate results
- Finds it frustrating to put in effort without visible changes
- Misses indulging in favorite foods
- Values enjoying life alongside healthy habits
- Receptive to finding healthier alternatives and moderation rather than complete deprivation
- Appreciated advice on celebrating non-scale victories and being patient with the process

Key insight: Prefers sustainable lifestyle changes over strict dieting; needs motivation strategies that allow for enjoyment and flexibility.
