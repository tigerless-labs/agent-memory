## Plot Summary

Writing collaborative fanfiction featuring:
- **MK**: escaped from his father, conflicted, learning magic (fire spell from Wukong)
- **Macaque**: rightful king, weakened from imprisonment, prefers to be called 'Mac' (not 'Your Majesty') until restored to throne
- **Mei & Tang**: loyal companions helping with the resistance
- **Wukong (Sun Wukong)**: MK's father, antagonist, leads the golden-haired monkey invasion

## Established Story Elements

- The mountain was peaceful with a council of elders before invasion
- Council was betrayed by one of their own who allied with Wukong's golden-haired monkeys
- Macaque imprisoned for centuries; now free and determined to reclaim kingdom
- Campsite scene: Macaque tells stories of pre-invasion mountain to Mei and Tang
- Character preference: Macaque chuckles at being called 'Your Majesty' - says no one's called him that in centuries, asks them not to use titles until he's king again
- MK has nightmare that Wukong finds the campsite

## Writing Style Notes

- Emphasis on dialogue and character interactions
- Focus on storytelling within the narrative (Macaque recounting history)
- Detail and emotional depth in scene development
- Character voice is important (Macaque's speech patterns, MK's internal conflict)
