## Singapore Intelligent Transport System (ITS)
- Uses AI and data analytics to optimize traffic flow
- Network of cameras, sensors, and data analytics tools for real-time monitoring
- Adjusts traffic signals in real-time based on current conditions
- Result: Up to 20% reduction in travel times

## Melbourne AI-powered Traffic Management System
- Uses machine learning algorithms and real-time data
- Predicts traffic patterns and optimizes signal timing
- Result: Up to 50% reduction in vehicle wait times at intersections, shorter travel times, reduced emissions

## Los Angeles ATSAC (Automated Traffic Surveillance and Control)
- Collects real-time data from traffic sensors and cameras
- Uses AI algorithms to adjust traffic signals
- Reduces congestion and improves traffic flow

## Parking Solutions
- Smart Parking Systems: cameras, sensors, and data analytics to monitor available spaces and notify drivers in real-time
- Dynamic Pricing: adjust rates based on demand to incentivize parking in less crowded areas, freeing up high-demand spaces
- Autonomous Valet Parking: AI-enabled vehicles that can self-park using sensors and machine learning
