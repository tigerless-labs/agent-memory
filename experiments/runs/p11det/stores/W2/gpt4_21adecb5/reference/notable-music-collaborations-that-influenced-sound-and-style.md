Music collaborations discussed during research into The Elected:

**Cross-genre successes:**
- Run-DMC & Aerosmith - 'Walk This Way' (rap/rock blend)
- Daft Punk with Pharrell Williams & Nile Rodgers - 'Random Access Memories' (EDM/funk/soul/disco fusion)
- Paul Simon - 'Graceland' album with Ladysmith Black Mambazo (Western/African music)
- Jay-Z & Linkin Park - 'Collision Course' EP (rap/rock)
- Santana's collaborations with Rob Thomas, Everlast (Latin rock)

**Influential producer collaborations:**
- David Bowie & Brian Eno - 'Low', 'Heroes', 'Lodger' (electronic/experimental pioneering)
- The Traveling Wilburys (Dylan, Harrison, Lynne, Orbison, Petty) - Americana blend
- Björk with Arca, Matmos, Mark Bell (genre-shifting electronic)
- The xx with Jamie xx (indie rock/electronic)
- Kanye West with Rick Rubin, No I.D., Mike Dean (hip-hop production)

**Notable failed collaboration:**
- Lou Reed & Metallica - 'Lulu' (2011) - widely panned, though Reed himself defended it
