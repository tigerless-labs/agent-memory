## Presales Engineer Role

In SaaS software and cloud computing, a presales engineer typically:
- Provides technical expertise to sales teams during the sales process
- Conducts product demos, proof-of-concepts, and technical presentations
- Collaborates with sales to understand customer requirements
- Bridges gap between technical capabilities and customer needs

## Presales-Oriented Titles
- Pre-Sales Solutions Engineer
- Technical Pre-Sales Consultant
- Pre-Sales Technical Specialist
- Pre-Sales Cloud Architect
- Pre-Sales Support Engineer

## Sales Engineer-Oriented Titles
- Sales Solutions Engineer
- Technical Sales Consultant
- Sales Technical Specialist
- Sales Cloud Architect
- Sales Support Engineer

## Other Related Titles
- Solutions Engineer
- Technical Sales Engineer
- Cloud Solutions Architect
- Sales Consultant
- Technical Account Manager
- Business Development Engineer
