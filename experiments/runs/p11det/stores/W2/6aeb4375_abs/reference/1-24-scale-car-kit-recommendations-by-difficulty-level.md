## Easier to Moderate (suitable at 6 months experience):\n- **Revell '69 Chevrolet Camaro SS (kit #85-4231)** — well-engineered, simple construction, few parts, forgiving design. *Primary choice for competition.*\n- **Revell '68 Shelby Mustang GT500 (kit #85-4241)** — well-designed, moderate part count, more complex curves than Camaro but still accessible\n\n## Moderate to Challenging:\n- **Tamiya Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7 (kit #24343)** — higher part count, intricate details, excellent engineering and instructions\n- **Aoshima Honda NSX (kit #05035)** — moderate parts, complex curves, good for practicing bodywork and paint\n\n## Challenging (more experience needed):\n- **Tamiya Nissan GT-R R35 (kit #24355)** — high part count, complex engine and chassis, requires advanced techniques\n- **Italeri Ferrari 250 GT California Spyder (kit #3644)** — high part count, complex curves, photo-etch, delicate assembly\n\n## Classic Muscle Cars (recommended list):\n- **AMT '70 Dodge Challenger R/T (kit #AMT1151)** — iconic muscle car, great details, challenging build\n- **Revell '68 Shelby Mustang GT500** (see above)\n\n## European Sports:\n- **Tamiya Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7** (see above)\n- **Italeri Lamborghini Miura SV (kit #3646)** — beautifully detailed, challenging\n\n## Japanese Performance:\n- **Tamiya Nissan GT-R R35** (see above)\n- **Aoshima Honda NSX** (see above)\n\n## Racing Cars:\n- **Tamiya Ford GT40 Mk II (kit #24350)** — legendary racing car, highly detailed, challenging\n- **Revell Lola T70 Mk III (kit #85-4221)** — nicely detailed, moderate complexity
