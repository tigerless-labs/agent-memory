Popular hiking apps recommended for trail discovery, GPS tracking, and progress monitoring:

1. **AllTrails** - Comprehensive trail database, GPS tracking, reviews from other hikers (iOS/Android)
2. **MapMyHike** - GPS tracking route/distance/elevation/calories, custom route creation (iOS/Android)
3. **Gaia GPS** - Topographic maps, trail data, route planning, popular with backpackers (iOS/Android)
4. **Hiking Project** - Free app from REI with trail database, GPS tracking, reviews (iOS/Android)
5. **Strava** - Social fitness app, track hikes, compete with friends, discover new trails (iOS/Android)
6. **Fitbit** - Sync with Fitbit device to track hikes and overall fitness progress (iOS/Android)
7. **Hiking Journal** - Log hikes, track progress, add photos and notes, reflect on experiences (iOS)
8. **PeakFinder** - Identify mountains and peaks using camera and GPS (iOS/Android)

Other tools: GPS watches (Garmin, Suunto, Fitbit Ionic), paper maps and guides, hiking blogs and resources.
