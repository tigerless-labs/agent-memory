Recommended trails within 30-minute drive of Denver:

1. **Chautauqua Trail**, Chautauqua Park, Boulder (20-min drive): 2.1-mile loop, 400 ft elevation gain. Gentle, well-maintained trail with scenic views of the Flatirons and Boulder.

2. **Bear Creek Lake Park - Owl's Perch Trail**, Lakewood (25-min drive): 2.5-mile loop, 200 ft elevation gain. Peaceful trail around Bear Creek Lake with wildlife spotting and scenic views.

3. **Dinosaur Ridge - Triceratops Trail**, Morrison (25-min drive): 2.3-mile out-and-back, 300 ft elevation gain. Unique dinosaur fossil site with educational signs along the way.

4. **Lookout Mountain - Windy Saddle Trail**, Golden (25-min drive): 2.5-mile out-and-back, 400 ft elevation gain. Scenic trail with views of Rocky Mountains and Great Plains.

5. **South Table Mountain - Mesa Trail**, Golden (20-min drive): 2.5-mile loop, 300 ft elevation gain. Gentle, well-marked trail with panoramic views of Denver metro area and Rocky Mountains.

Preparation tips: Check trail conditions beforehand, bring water, snacks, sun protection, comfortable hiking shoes/boots, map/GPS, and let someone know your plans.
