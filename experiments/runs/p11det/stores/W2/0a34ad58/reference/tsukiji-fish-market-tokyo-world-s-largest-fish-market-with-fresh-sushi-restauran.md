## Tsukiji Fish Market, Tokyo\n\n**Overview:** One of the largest fish markets in the world, handling over 2,000 tons of seafood daily. Located near Ginza.\n\n**Areas:**\n- Inner market (jonai-shijo): Wholesale fish auction, limited public viewing, requires early arrival (~5 AM)\n- Outer market (jogai-shijo): Open to public with restaurants, sushi bars, seafood shops, kitchenware\n\n**Popular Sushi Restaurants:**\n- Sushi Dai: Legendary, high-quality Edomae-style sushi\n- Sushi Daiwa: Wide range of options, lively atmosphere\n- Tsukiji Fish Market Sushi: Fresh, affordable sushi bar\n\n**Best Time to Visit:** 5:00 AM - 7:00 AM for freshest sushi and peak market activity\n\n**Tips:**\n- Arrive early for best experience\n- Expect large crowds, especially peak seasons\n- Wear comfortable shoes, dress in layers (chilly mornings)\n- Bring cash (many shops cash-only)\n- Respect market traditions; avoid photos of fish/seafood\n\n**Transportation from Shinjuku:** JR Yamanote Line to Shimbashi → Tokyo Metro Ginza Line to Tsukijishijo Station (~30-40 minutes, ~¥420-450)
