**AI and Machine Learning:** The AI Alignment Newsletter (weekly, AI safety/alignment from MIRI), The AI Newsletter (AI Impact), Machine Learning Weekly (ML papers/tutorials), AI in Industry (Emerj, applications).

**Technology & Innovation:** The Exponential View (Azeem Azhar, tech/politics/society), The Technosociology Newsletter (social implications), IEEE Spectrum Newsletter (tech advancements), MIT Technology Review Newsletter (trends/innovations).

**Data Science:** Data Science Weekly, KDnuggets Newsletter (ML/AI/data science), DataCamp's Data Science Newsletter, FlowingData Newsletter (Nathan Yau, visualization/statistics).

**Industry-Specific:** The AI in Healthcare Newsletter, The AI in Finance Newsletter, The Robotics Newsletter.

**Honorable Mentions:** The Gradient (research/applications), AI Business News, Machine Learning Mastery.

**General News Sources:** The Washington Post, The Wall Street Journal (business/finance), NPR (in-depth reporting), The Guardian (global perspective), Politico (US politics).

**General Newsletters:** The Skimm (daily summary), The Morning (NYT daily briefing), Axios (politics/business/tech), NextDraft (curated stories), The Daily (NYT podcast).
