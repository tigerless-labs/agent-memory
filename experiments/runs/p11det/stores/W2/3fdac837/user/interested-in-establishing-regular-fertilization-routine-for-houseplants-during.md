Plans to expand plant care practices to include consistent fertilization:

Preferences and approach:
- Wants to fertilize plants more regularly but carefully
- Prefers balanced, water-soluble fertilizers (20-20-20 standard)
- Plans to dilute fertilizers to half recommended strength to avoid root burn
- Interested in fertilizing during spring/summer growing seasons
- Considering foliar sprays as supplementary nutrient source
- Open to compost additions for slow-release nutrients
- Monitors plants closely to avoid over-fertilization

Plant-specific applications:
- Peace lily: fertilize every 1-2 weeks during growing season
- Spider plant, dracaena, snake plant, basil: similar regular schedules as appropriate

Philosophy: patient, cautious approach with close monitoring rather than heavy fertilization.
