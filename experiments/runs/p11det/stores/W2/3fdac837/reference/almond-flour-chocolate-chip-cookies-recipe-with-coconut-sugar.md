Recipe: 1.5 cups almond flour, 0.5 cup coconut sugar, 0.25 cup softened unsalted butter, 2 large eggs, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 0.5 cup dark chocolate chips, pinch of salt. Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until lightly golden. Gluten-free, can be customized with different add-ins.
