**Ticket to Ride: Europe** — Recommended as first expansion. Introduces tunnels, ferries, new maps and gameplay mechanics. More complex than North America with more routes and connections.\n\n**Ticket to Ride: Asia** — Adds new maps, team play option, and new train car type.\n\n**Ticket to Ride: Rails & Sails** — Introduces ships as new transportation type, allows building ocean routes.\n\n**Ticket to Ride: Germany** — Adds new map of Germany with new route-building through cities mechanic.
