Emily Chen is an old acquaintance who works as a product manager at a fintech company. User is planning to reach out to reconnect and explore potential collaboration opportunities.

**Outreach approach:**
- Keep initial message brief and casual (not a pitch)
- Show genuine interest in her work and progress at fintech company
- Frame as reconnecting with open-ended collaboration possibilities
- Offer casual call to catch up and discuss mutual areas of interest
- Avoid high-pressure sales language
- Be open to her suggestions for collaboration areas
