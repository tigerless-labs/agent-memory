**SWIFT**: Messaging system for payment instructions between banks. Does NOT transfer money directly; transmits payment instructions and settlement messages to facilitate fund transfers.\n\n**Western Union**: Person-to-person money transfer service. Specializes in cross-border money transfers, bill payments, and money orders through a global agent network.\n\nBoth facilitate money movement but operate very differently.
