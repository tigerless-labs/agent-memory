Techniques for summarizing books and taking effective notes while reading:\n\n**Summarizing:**\n- Identify main plot points, key events, characters and themes\n- Determine author's purpose and message\n- Note character motivations and how they change\n- Note setting, atmosphere, tone and mood\n- Focus on climax and resolution\n\n**Taking Notes:**\n- Use a dedicated notebook or note-taking app\n- Create a consistent system (colors, symbols, headings for characters/themes/plot)\n- Record quotes with page numbers for reference in discussions\n- Ask questions and make connections as you read\n- Summarize each chapter or section\n- Use sticky notes or tabs for important pages\n- Create character maps showing relationships\n- Research author background and historical context\n\n**Benefits:** Helps prepare for book club discussions and develop deeper understanding of the book.
