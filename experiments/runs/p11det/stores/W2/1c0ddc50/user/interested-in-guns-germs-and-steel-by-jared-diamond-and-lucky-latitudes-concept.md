Book: "Guns, Germs, and Steel" by Jared Diamond (1997, Pulitzer Prize winner)

Specific interest in the "lucky latitudes" concept (20° to 40° north of equator) - Diamond's argument about how geography, climate, soil, water access, and biodiversity in this band of latitudes favored development of complex civilizations.

Also interested in related concepts: the "axis of history" (east-to-west development), Fertile Crescent, Indus Valley Civilization, Yellow River Valley, Mediterranean region.
