## BJJ Training Programs

### 20-Week White Belt to Blue Belt Program
- Week 1: Guard position
- Week 2: Side control position
- Week 3: Closed guard position
- Week 4: Half guard position
- Week 5: Knee-on-belly position
- Week 6: Mount position
- Week 7: Mount position
- Week 8: Back control
- Week 9: Takedowns
- Week 10: Passes
- Week 11: Submissions
- Week 12: Transitions
- Week 13: Drilling
- Week 14: Sparring
- Week 15: Conditioning
- Week 16: Mount position
- Week 17: Back control
- Week 18: Takedowns
- Week 19: Passes
- Week 20: Submissions

### 4-Week Submission-Focused Program
- Week 1: Fundamentals of submissions, including top 5 most effective: armbar, triangle choke, rear-naked choke, guillotine choke, kimura
- Week 2: Gi grips and control
- Week 3: Kimura attacks and defenses
- Week 4: Theory and visualization

### Home Drills
- Shrimping
- Knee cutters
- Bridge and roll
- Guard retention
- Dynamic stretching
- Jumping jacks
- Lunges

### Notes
- Always warm up before training
- Listen to your body and take regular breaks to avoid injury
- Continue learning and improving skills throughout training journey
