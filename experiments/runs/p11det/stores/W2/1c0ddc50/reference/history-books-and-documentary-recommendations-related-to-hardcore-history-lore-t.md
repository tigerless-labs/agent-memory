BOOKS:

For Hardcore History themes: 'The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich' by William L. Shirer; 'Guns, Germs, and Steel' by Jared Diamond (Pulitzer Prize); 'The Devil in the White City' by Erik Larson

For Lore themes: 'The Witches: Salem, 1692' by Stacy Schiff (Pulitzer Prize); 'The Devil's Gentleman' by Harold Schechter; 'The Ghost Map' by Steven Johnson

For The Dollop themes: 'A People's History of the United States' by Howard Zinn; 'The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks' by Rebecca Skloot; 'Killers of the Flower Moon' by David Grann

DOCUMENTARIES:

For Hardcore History themes: 'The World at War' (1973 series); 'The Apocalypse' (2000 series on Mongols); 'Death and the Civil War' (2002, PBS)

For Lore themes: 'The Salem Witch Trials' (2002, History Channel); 'The Real Wolfman' (2012, Smithsonian); 'The Dark Ages' (2007, History Channel)

For The Dollop themes: 'The Untold History of the United States' (2012); 'The True Story of the Bridge on the River Kwai' (2001); 'The Men Who Built America' (2012, History Channel)
