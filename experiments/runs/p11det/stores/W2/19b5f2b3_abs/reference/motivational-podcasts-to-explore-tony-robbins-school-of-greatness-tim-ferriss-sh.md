Recommended podcasts to check out:
- **The Tony Robbins Podcast** — by Tony Robbins, covers business, health, and personal development
- **The School of Greatness** — hosted by Lewis Howes, interviews with entrepreneurs and celebrities on personal growth and leadership
- **The Tim Ferriss Show** — author of The 4-Hour Work Week, interviews with successful people discussing habits and routines
- **TED Talks Daily** — talks on motivation, innovation, and personal growth
- **Happier with Gretchen Rubin** — by Gretchen Rubin (author of The Happiness Project), strategies for happiness and life improvement
