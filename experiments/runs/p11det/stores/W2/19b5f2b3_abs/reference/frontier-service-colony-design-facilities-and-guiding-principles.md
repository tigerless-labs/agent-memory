Sports & Recreation: Big Bear's Boxing, Wrestling, and Mixed Martial Arts Academy (fitness facility founded by Kodian martial artist Big Bear); The Playing Field Multisport Arena (nearby sports complex with basketball, volleyball, soccer, baseball, running track, tennis courts, swimming pool); Public Onsen (natural mineral hot spring with therapeutic properties, separate areas for men and women); Karaoke Bar (entertainment venue by izakaya). Community Design: Colony structured to encourage off-duty rangers, workers, and children to spend leisure time in community engaging in active lifestyles and socialization. Facilities centrally located, accessible by walking, cycling, or people mover. Regular events foster social interaction. Food Policy: Highly processed foods not imported or manufactured; focus on fresh, locally sourced nutritious ingredients. Medical: State-of-the-Art Hospital designed by Chief Medical Officer of Frontier Service; features passive solar heating/cooling, geothermal systems, solar panels, green roofs, water recycling, eco-friendly materials, patient-centered design, holistic health approach.
