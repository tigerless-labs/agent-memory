Completed a two-week solo backpacking trip through Japan in early 2023 (approximately Feb–Mar). Visited Fushimi Inari Shrine. Stayed in small hostels and guesthouses throughout the trip, which was a great way to meet other travelers. Also visited an onsen (hot spring) in the mountains, which was an amazing experience. This trip was a positive solo travel experience and helped develop comfort with independent travel.
