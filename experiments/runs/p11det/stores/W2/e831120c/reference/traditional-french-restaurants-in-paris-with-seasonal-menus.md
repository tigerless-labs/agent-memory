## Recommended Restaurants\n- Le Comptoir du Relais — 6th arrondissement, classic French dishes, famous steak tartare and escargots\n- Chez L'Ami Jean — 11th arrondissement, organic and locally sourced, seasonal menu, duck confit recommended\n- Le Grand Vefour — historic restaurant since 1784, foie gras, wild game, roasted scallops, seasonal focus\n- La Réserve — 8th arrondissement, intimate, seasonal menu changes regularly\n- Le Petit Châtelet — 5th arrondissement, cozy, roasted chicken and vegetables, seafood platter\n- Breizh Café — organic and locally sourced crepes with seasonal ingredients\n- Le 6 Paul Bert — 11th arrondissement, cozy bistro, creative seasonal menu\n- La Dame de Pic — 11th arrondissement, Michelin-starred, creative seasonal menu, cozy and intimate\n\n## Tips\n- Make reservations in advance\n- Consider lunch for menu du jour (daily menu) pricing\n- Ask servers for seasonal recommendations\n- Expect leisurely meals in French dining tradition
