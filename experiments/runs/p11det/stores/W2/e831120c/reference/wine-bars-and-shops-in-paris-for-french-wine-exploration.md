## Wine Bars\n- La Closerie des Lilas — 6th arrondissement, historic, extensive selection of French wines including rare and vintage\n- Le Barav — 11th arrondissement, curated natural and organic wines from small producers, knowledgeable staff\n- Septime La Cave — 11th arrondissement, run by team behind Michelin-starred Septime, rotating natural and organic wines, small plates\n\n## Wine Shops\n- La Maison du Vin — multiple locations, impressive selection from various French regions, knowledgeable staff\n- Nicolas — chain with several locations, wide selection including organic and natural options\n- Le Wine Shop — 6th arrondissement, curated selection including rare and vintage, offers tastings and workshops\n\n## Wine Markets\n- Marché aux Vins — 12th arrondissement, wide selection from various regions, taste and purchase directly from producers\n- Marché d'Aligre — 12th arrondissement, variety of French wines including natural and organic options\n\n## Tips\n- Ask staff for help and recommendations\n- Look for wine flights or tastings to sample multiple wines\n- Be prepared for higher prices than wine shops
