## Options considered for Lola

1. **Frontline Plus**: Topical, kills fleas/ticks/chewing lice, $30-$35 for 3-month supply
2. **Advantage II**: Topical, kills fleas/ticks/chewing lice, fast-acting, $30-$35 for 3-month supply
3. **Seresto Flea and Tick Collar**: 8 months protection from single collar, odorless, no monthly application needed, $30-$35
4. **Capstar**: Oral tablet once monthly, kills adult fleas/ticks, $25-$30 for 3-month supply
5. **Cheristin**: Topical, kills fleas/ticks, gentle non-greasy formula, $30-$35 for 3-month supply

All available OTC at Petco and online. Important: Consult veterinarian before starting new medication. Follow product instructions carefully.
