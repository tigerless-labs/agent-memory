Safeguard Global's self-service applications are organized into four main sections:\n\n1. **Attract and Hire** - Tools for attracting candidates, managing applications, scheduling interviews, and onboarding new employees\n2. **Manage** - Employee data and HR processes including time/attendance tracking, benefits enrollment, employee records, performance management\n3. **Pay** - Global payroll processing, tax withholding, payroll reports, benefits and compensation management\n4. **Analyse** - HR and payroll data analysis, real-time reporting, trend identification, predictive analytics, benchmarking
