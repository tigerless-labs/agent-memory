## Research Focus (2023-05-24)

Investigated government-funded apprenticeship opportunities in:
- Healthcare (medical assistants, nursing assistants)
- Culinary arts (professional cooking, pastry, hospitality)

Key comparison questions explored:
- Eligibility requirements for government-funded programs
- Industry availability and variety of programs
- Benefits vs. traditional post-secondary education
- Government-funded vs. private apprenticeships

This indicates interest in alternative career development paths with practical, hands-on training and financial accessibility.
