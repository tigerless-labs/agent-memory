Mathematical and computational approaches for generating new musical patterns and compositions:\n\n- **Markov Chains**: Generate note sequences based on probability of transitions between notes in training data\n- **Genetic Algorithms**: Evolve musical phrases through selection, mutation, and crossover operations\n- **Neural Networks**: Train on music datasets to generate new melodies, chord progressions, drum patterns\n- **Cellular Automata**: Generate patterns based on simple rules governing cell interactions in a grid\n- **L-Systems**: Create fractal patterns of notes through recursive rule application\n- **Spectral Analysis**: Analyze harmonic content of audio to generate new sounds\n\n**Fractals in Music**: Fractal algorithms create repeating patterns that are similar but not identical, producing constantly-evolving compositions. Can be used to:\n- Generate novel melodic and rhythmic patterns\n- Manipulate synthesizer parameters over time\n- Control generative visual displays that drive sonic parameters\n\nThese approaches combine music theory with computer science to explore algorithmic composition.
