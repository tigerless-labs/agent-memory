Planning to cook Massaman curry with beef and vegetables for a dinner party. Has received a detailed recipe with:
- 2 lbs beef (chuck or round), cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 medium onions, 3 garlic cloves
- 2 medium potatoes, 1 large carrot, 1 large red bell pepper
- 2 tbsp Massaman curry paste
- 2 cups beef broth, 1 cup coconut milk
- Spices: ground cinnamon, cardamom, cumin, turmeric
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
