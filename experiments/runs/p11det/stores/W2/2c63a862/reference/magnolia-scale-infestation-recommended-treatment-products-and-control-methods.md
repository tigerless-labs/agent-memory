# Magnolia Scale Treatment & Control

## Recommended Horticultural Products
- **Neem oil**: Natural oil extracted from neem tree; suffocates and repels scales; mix with water and spray per label
- **Insecticidal soap**: Potassium salts of fatty acids; suffocates and repels scales; rinse tree with water after application to prevent leaf damage
- **Dormant oil**: Heavier horticultural oil; used in dormant season (winter/early spring) before buds break to smother eggs
- **All Seasons Spray Oil**: Can be used year-round; mix with water and spray per label

## Prevention & Control Methods
- Regularly inspect tree for scale insects and sticky sap
- Prune and dispose of infested branches immediately
- Encourage natural predators (ladybugs, lacewings)
- Provide proper balanced nutrition
- Avoid over-fertilizing (creates favorable conditions for scales)
- Water deeply and infrequently to prevent water stress
- Use insecticides as last resort; follow label instructions carefully
- Rotate treatment methods to prevent resistance

## Important Facts
- Magnolia scales excrete honeydew that attracts ants
- **Ants do NOT help control infestation** — they protect scales from predators and transport them to other trees
- Infestation can spread to nearby trees via ant transport, wind-blown crawlers, or insect vectors
- If untreated: can last indefinitely, causes yellowing leaves, premature leaf drop, branch dieback, and ultimately kill the tree
- Easiest to control if caught early before reproduction spreads
