Planning a road trip to San Francisco for early June 2023 (a few weeks from late May). Decided to use all-season tires instead of purchasing dedicated rain tires for the trip. Driving cautiously on wet roads will be the approach, given San Francisco's rainy season (winter months see 15-20 inches of rain) and foggy conditions. Tire condition was verified (tread depth in good shape) before committing to this tire choice.
