Plan to insure entire collectibles collection. Will look into specialized collectible insurance options from providers like Chubb, State Farm, or Collectibles Insurance Services. Alternative options: adding a rider/endorsement to existing homeowners/renters insurance, or purchasing a separate stand-alone policy for collectibles. Proper documentation will support accurate valuation and coverage.
