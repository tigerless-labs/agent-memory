Looking to add low-light indoor plants to home. Particularly interested in:

- **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)**: Low-maintenance, can tolerate extreme low light and infrequent watering
  - Watering: Every 1-2 weeks in summer, every 4-6 weeks in winter (only when soil feels dry)
  - Fertilization: Balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) once a month during growing season (spring/summer), diluted to half strength
  - Prefers well-draining soil, low to medium indirect light
  - Toxic to pets and humans if ingested

- **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)**: Excellent air purifier, survives in low light, can go weeks without watering

Has a vase with fresh greenery on kitchen countertop and considering adding these plants to bedroom and home office spaces.
