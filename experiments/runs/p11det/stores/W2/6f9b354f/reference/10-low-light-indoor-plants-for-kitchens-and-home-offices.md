Recommended indoor plants that thrive in low-light conditions:

1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) - air purifier, low-maintenance, survives weeks without water
2. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) - easy care, can grow long, trainable to climb trellis
3. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) - low-maintenance, tolerates extreme low light and infrequent watering
4. Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii) - elegant white blooms, air purifier
5. Dracaena (Dracaena spp.) - 40+ varieties, different shapes and sizes
6. Philodendron (Philodendron spp.) - grows in low light, various shapes and sizes
7. Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema modestum) - variegated leaves, low-maintenance
8. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) - easy care, air purifier, white flowers
9. Parlor Palm (Chamaedorea elegans) - slender and elegant, low to medium light
10. Bromeliad (Bromeliaceae family) - includes pineapples and air plants, low-maintenance

General tips: All can tolerate low light but benefit from some indirect sunlight. North-facing window or grow lights help them thrive.
