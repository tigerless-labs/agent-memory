Tableau and Power BI are the most suitable tools. Tableau: user-friendly drag-and-drop interface, free trial, excellent interactivity, publishing to Tableau Online/Server, Story feature for narrative. Power BI: Microsoft analytics tool, interactive visualizations, free version, cloud-based sharing, Q&A feature. Alternatives: Google Data Studio, Plotly, D3.js, Easel.ly, Sisense, QlikView, Matplotlib/Seaborn, Visme.
