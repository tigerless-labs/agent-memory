Financial metrics: revenue growth rate, profit margin, ROI, break-even analysis, cash flow projections, cost savings, financial ratios. Operational: production capacity utilization, cycle time, throughput, defect rate, inventory turnover, supply chain efficiency, employee productivity. Customer: CAC, retention rate, CLV, satisfaction ratings, NPS, conversion rate, demographics. Market/Competitive: market share, market size/growth, competitive landscape, pricing analysis, switching costs, brand awareness. Innovation/Growth: R&D investment, patents, time-to-market, pipeline metrics, adoption rate, new revenue. Chart types by use case: company performance dashboard, industry landscape (scatter/bubble), market analysis (map/heatmap), problem tree (treemap), SWOT (quadrant), customer segmentation (clustering/heatmap), recommendation summary, cost-benefit (waterfall/bar), impact analysis (Sankey), financial performance (dashboard), break-even (scatter/line), sensitivity (tornado/spider), process flowchart, efficiency metrics (dashboard), resource allocation (heatmap/treemap), innovation roadmap (timeline/Gantt), growth strategy (matrix/quadrant), customer journey (map/flow).
