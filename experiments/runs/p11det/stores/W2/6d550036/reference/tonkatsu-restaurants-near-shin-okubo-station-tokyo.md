Recommended tonkatsu options within 10-minute walk of Shin-Okubo Station:\n\n1. **Tonkatsu Maisen Shin-Okubo** (3-minute walk) — ¥1,500-¥2,500 per person. Popular chain, crispy tonkatsu with shredded cabbage, rice, and miso soup.\n\n2. **Katsu-Ya Shin-Okubo** (5-minute walk) — ¥1,000-¥2,000 per person. Wide variety including unique flavors like curry and mentaiko. Lunch menu around ¥1,000.\n\n3. **Tonkatsu Tanaka Shin-Okubo** (7-minute walk) — ¥1,500-¥2,500 per person. Family-run, cozy atmosphere, high-quality pork.\n\n4. **Tonkatsu Botejyu Shin-Okubo** (10-minute walk) — ¥1,000-¥2,500 per person. Osaka-style, unique flavors like garlic and yuzu. Lunch menu around ¥1,000.
