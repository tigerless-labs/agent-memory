Snow Mountain Capital develops a sophisticated software platform designed to address disadvantages faced by Limited Partners (LPs) in venture capital. The platform assists with deal sourcing, evaluation, monitoring, and exit strategy.

Platform mission: Create technology that enhances traditional VC methodologies and provides LPs with better insights and control over their investments.
