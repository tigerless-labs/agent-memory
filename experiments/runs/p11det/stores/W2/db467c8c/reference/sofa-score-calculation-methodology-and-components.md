Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (SOFA) - six organ systems scored 0-4 each (max 24). Respiratory (PaO2/FiO2), Cardiovascular (MAP with/without vasopressors), Hepatic (bilirubin), Coagulation (platelets), Renal (creatinine/urine output), Neurologic (GCS). Example: PaO2/FiO2=200, MAP=65 no vasopressors, bilirubin=2.0, platelets=150k, creatinine=1.5, GCS=12 yields component scores 2+1+2+0+0+2 = SOFA 7. Score ≥2 indicates organ dysfunction per Sepsis-3 definition.
