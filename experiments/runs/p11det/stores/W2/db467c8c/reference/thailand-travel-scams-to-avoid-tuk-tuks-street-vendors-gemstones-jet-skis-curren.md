## Scam types to watch for in Thailand

1. **Tuk-Tuk scams** — Drivers offer cheap city 'tours' but take you to overpriced shops where they get commission. Always agree on price before boarding.

2. **Street Vendor Scams** — Fake goods or non-functional products sold at inflated prices. Inspect items carefully and compare prices elsewhere.

3. **Gemstone Scams** — Fake or low-quality gems sold at inflated prices. Only buy from reputable jewelers.

4. **Jet-Ski Scams** — Rental operators charge for pre-existing damage. Take photos before and after use, and clarify rental terms in advance.

5. **Currency Scams** — Check conversion rates and commission rates before exchanging. Don't accept torn or damaged banknotes.

## General protection tips

- Research beforehand and know current exchange rates
- Use reputable services (banks, established businesses)
- Avoid carrying large amounts of cash
- Keep valuables in hotel safe
- Trust your instincts if something seems questionable
- Report suspected fraud to local authorities immediately
