## Core Street Photography Techniques

**Capturing moments:**
- Use burst mode when interesting action unfolds to increase chances of capturing perfect expression
- Zone focusing: pre-focus on specific area and wait for subject to enter frame  
- Henri Cartier-Bresson's **'decisive moment'** - the peak moment of action or expression where subject is most revealing
- Anticipate movements and behavior patterns in public spaces

**Composition & visual:**
- Focus on the eyes - they carry emotion and depth
- Experiment with layers (foreground, middle ground, background) for depth
- Use negative space strategically
- Incorporate environment and context
- Look for interesting gestures and interactions

**Light & technical:**
- Soft, natural light brings out facial expression subtleties
- Avoid harsh mid-day sun with unflattering shadows
- Work with 50mm prime lens - get close but respect personal space

**Approach & discretion:**
- Shoot from hip or chest level for candid captures
- Blend in with neutral, casual clothing
- Respect subjects' privacy and personal boundaries
- Be aware of surroundings and keep belongings secure
