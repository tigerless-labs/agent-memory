## Techniques for Approaching & Building Rapport

- Show genuine interest in person's story or appearance
- Make eye contact and smile - establishes connection and shows respect
- Use open, confident body language (uncrossed arms, upright posture)
- Introduce yourself briefly and explain photography project/intentions
- Be prepared to answer questions about your work
- Show your portfolio/work on phone to help them understand your style
- Keep interaction brief - respect their time
- Be mindful of cultural and personal differences
- Start with small talk (weather, traffic, commute experience)
- Look for common ground
- Be respectful if someone declines and move on gracefully
- Follow up by sharing photos if you promised to
