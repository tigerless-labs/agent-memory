## Shoe Cleaning & Conditioning Kits
- **Saphir Medaille d'Or** — Premium brand, gentle leather cleaning and conditioning
- **Kiwi Shoe Care** — Well-known general shoe care brand with multiple product options
- **Jason Markk** — Eco-friendly shoe cleaner, suitable for various shoe types
- **Lexol** — Popular choice for leather cleaning and conditioning

## Waterproofing Sprays
- **Scotchgard** — Broad waterproofing spray for various materials
- **Crepe Protect** — Specifically good for suede and nubuck shoes
- **Nikwax** — Water-based spray, suitable for canvas and suede shoes

## Shoe Maintenance Tools
- **Cedar shoe trees** — Recommended for leather boots; cedar naturally repels insects and absorbs moisture
- **Leather-lined shoe trees** — Extra protection for leather shoes to maintain shape

## Finding Cobblers
Search online (Google Maps, Yelp, NSRA directory), ask friends/family for personal recommendations, check with local shoe stores for referrals.
