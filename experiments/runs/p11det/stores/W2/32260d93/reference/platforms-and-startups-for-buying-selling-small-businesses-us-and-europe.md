## United States
- **BizBuySell** — Online marketplace for buying and selling small businesses; offers business valuation, marketing, and negotiation assistance
- **ExitAdviser** — Platform for selling small businesses; provides business valuation, legal document preparation, and marketing
- **Sunbelt Business Brokers** — National network of business brokers with offices across many US states
- **Transworld Business Advisors** — Business brokerage firm offering business valuation, marketing, and negotiation assistance
- **Empire Flippers** — Online marketplace specializing in e-commerce, software, and content-based businesses

## Europe
- **BusinessesForSale.com** — UK-based online marketplace for buying and selling businesses
- **Rightbiz** — UK-based online marketplace with business valuation, legal document preparation, and marketing services
- **Enterprise Europe Network** — EU initiative providing business brokerage and transfer services for SMEs
- **BusinessesBuySell** — Platform for buying and selling small and medium-sized businesses in Europe
- **Companymarketplace.de** — German online marketplace for buying and selling businesses

Context: User researched options for selling small businesses in both US and Europe (May 2023).
