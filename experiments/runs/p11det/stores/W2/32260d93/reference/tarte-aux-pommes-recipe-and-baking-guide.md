A traditional apple tart from Normandy with crisp apples and buttery flaky pastry crust. Best apple varieties: Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, Braeburn. Baking tips: Keep butter and ingredients cold for a flaky crust, chill mixing bowl before starting. Roll out dough carefully and handle gently to avoid developing gluten and toughening. Layer apple slices evenly and tightly, fanning in spiral pattern for best presentation. Brush with egg wash (1 egg + 1 tablespoon water) before baking for golden color and shine. Drink pairings: Non-alcoholic (hot tea, cold milk, iced tea), Alcoholic (Calvados, hard cider, apple cider mimosa). User chose apple cider mimosa as pairing.
