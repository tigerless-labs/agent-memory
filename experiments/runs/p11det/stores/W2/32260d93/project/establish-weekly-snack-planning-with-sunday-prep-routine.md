Implement a weekly snack planning system with 30-60 minute Sunday prep sessions. Prep items include: cut fresh fruit (apples, carrots, bell peppers), energy balls, granola bars, boiled eggs, yogurt parfaits, smoothie packs, and portioned nuts and seeds. Goal is to have healthy options available throughout the week to reduce impulse snacking.
