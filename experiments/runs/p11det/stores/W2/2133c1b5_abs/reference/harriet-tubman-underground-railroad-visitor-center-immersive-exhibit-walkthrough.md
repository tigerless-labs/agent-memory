Detailed sensory-rich walkthrough of the museum exhibits and visitor experience:

**Opening**: Heavy air, weight of history, sounds of chains, transported to UGRR era

**Early life section**: Interactive replicas of slave quarters, tools, period objects; Maryland origins; personal escape narrative

**Navigation exhibits**: Maps of UGRR routes, safe houses, secret routes, codes used by conductors

**Conductor section**: Harriet Tubman's work and disguises (headscarf, soldier's uniform); risks, dangers, realities of enslavement (chains, whips, brutal punishments)

**Civil War section**: Weapons, uniforms, Black soldiers in Union army, Emancipation Proclamation impact

**Legacy section**: Civil Rights Movement influence, continued inspiration

**Design philosophy**: Interactive and immersive; engages all senses throughout; designed for visitors of all ages
