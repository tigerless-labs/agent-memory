When asking for descriptions—especially of places, experiences, or narratives—values:

- **Sensory detail**: sight, sound, smell, touch, even taste when relevant
- **Show, don't tell**: experiences conveyed through action and sensation rather than explanation
- **Emotional resonance**: details that evoke feeling, not just information
- **Specificity**: rejects brevity in favor of important details

Reference style: 'the last museum of the first grinch series' (the 2000 film's immersive, fantastical museum scenes)

When content feels too thin or summary-like, will ask for expansion and deepening.
