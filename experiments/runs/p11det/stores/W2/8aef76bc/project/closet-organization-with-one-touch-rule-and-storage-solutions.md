## One-Touch Rule\nWhen taking something out of closet, put it back in its designated spot immediately rather than leaving it on the floor or chair.\n\n## Storage Solution Recommendations\n1. **Stackable Storage Bins**: For socks, underwear, accessories, out-of-season clothing\n2. **Shoe Organizers**: Over-the-door or freestanding racks for shoes and accessories\n3. **Closet Shelves with Baskets**: For folded items like t-shirts, jeans, sweaters\n4. **Hanging Organizers**: With pockets or shelves for dresses, jackets, bags\n5. **Drawer Dividers**: To separate tops, bottoms, accessories\n6. **Tiered Closet Systems**: Comprehensive solution with shelves, rods, drawers\n\n## Considerations When Choosing Storage\n- Measure closet dimensions\n- Identify specific storage needs and frequency of use\n- Choose versatile, moveable options\n- Select solutions that match personal style
