For exploration/adventure similar to Sea of Thieves:
- Minecraft (exploration, creativity, multiplayer)
- Destiny 2 (cooperative play, exploration, FPS)
- Monster Hunter: World (co-op, open world)
- Sunless Skies (narrative exploration)
- Subnautica (underwater survival, discovery)

For story-driven games similar to The Last of Us Part II:
- What Remains of Edith Finch
- Life is Strange 2
- Oxenfree
- Firewatch
- Tell Me Why

For post-apocalyptic with fast-paced action:
- Days Gone (post-apocalyptic Oregon, zombie-like Freakers, action-focused)
- Horizon Zero Dawn (post-apocalyptic with robotic creatures, action-RPG)
- Control (third-person shooter, supernatural world, atmospheric tension)
- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (action-RPG, deep story, open world)
- Far Cry 5 (FPS, cult conflict)
- Resident Evil 7: Biohazard (survival horror)
- State of Decay 2 (open-world survival, zombie combat)

All available on Xbox Game Pass.
