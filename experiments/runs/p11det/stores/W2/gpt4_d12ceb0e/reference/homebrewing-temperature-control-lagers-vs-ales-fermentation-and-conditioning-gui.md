Fermentation & Conditioning Temperatures:

Lagers: fermentation 45-55°F (7-13°C), conditioning 32-40°F (0-4°C) - longer, produces crisp/clean/smooth beer

Ales: fermentation 60-75°F (15-24°C), conditioning 55-65°F (13-18°C) - produces fruity/spicy/complex flavors

Temperature Control Methods: fermentation chambers, temperature controllers with heating/cooling, suitable brewing location, match yeast to maintainable temperature

Key: temperature affects flavor/aroma/mouthfeel; fermenting outside range causes under/over-fermentation; yeast performance temperature-dependent; check yeast expiration dates
