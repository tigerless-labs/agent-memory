Planning two-part gift for Emma's high school graduation:

**Gift 1: Personalized Necklace**
- Engraved with Emma's name and graduation year (2022)
- Sources to check: Etsy, Zales, Things Remembered, Personalization Mall, Amazon

**Gift 2: Handwritten Heartfelt Letter**
- Include specific memories and anecdotes about Emma
- Express pride in her achievements and academic awards
- Offer words of wisdom and hopes for her future
- Chosen closing message: "Remember, your dreams are within reach, and I'll be cheering you on every step of the way."

**Special touches for the letter:**
- High-quality paper (cotton, linen, or handmade with texture)
- Fountain pen or calligraphy pen with archival-quality ink
- Optional embellishments: personal photo, small memento, wax seal
- Decorative envelope with special stamp
- Leave whitespace for visual appeal
