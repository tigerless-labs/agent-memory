Decided to sign up for a marathon in 2022 and chose Hal Higdon's Novice Marathon Training Plan (18-week plan). This plan:
- Assumes ability to run 3 miles without stopping
- Includes 4 runs per week
- Has one longer run on weekends
- Incorporates rest days and cross-training

Training approach:
- Using cycling for cross-training to improve endurance
- Incorporating strength training 2-3 times per week targeting core and legs
- Planning to add hill repeats (1-3 minute climbs) once or twice per week
- Focus on exercises: squats, lunges, deadlifts, planks, Russian twists, leg raises, side planks, calf raises, step-ups, glute bridges, leg press

Goals:
- Build on sprint triathlon endurance achieved Feb 2022
- Gradually increase weekly mileage
- Improve running efficiency and reduce injury risk
