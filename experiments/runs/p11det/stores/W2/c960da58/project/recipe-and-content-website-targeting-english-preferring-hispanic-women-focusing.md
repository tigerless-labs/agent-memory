Developing content strategy and recipe ideas for a website targeting English-preferring Hispanic women. Focus areas:

- **Cultural identity expression through food**: Recipes that help readers express multifaceted cultural identity
- **Multicultural Latino cuisines**: Featuring recipes from Mexico, Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Peru, Chile, and other countries
- **Fusion recipes**: Both creative/outlandish fusions (e.g., buffalo chicken tacos, mac and queso burritos) and healthy sustainable fusions
- **Time-saving elements**: Include quick preparation tips, hacks, and time-efficient recipes (30-minute meals)
- **Cesar Chavez values alignment**: Sustainable ingredients, fair labor practices for farmworkers, recipes featuring ingredients produced by Mexican farmworkers in the US
- **Content for May**: Cinco de Mayo, Mother's Day, and other May events

Generated 60 recipe ideas across three categories: general May content ideas, unexpected American-Mexican fusion recipes, and healthy sustainable recipes aligned with labor and sustainability values.
