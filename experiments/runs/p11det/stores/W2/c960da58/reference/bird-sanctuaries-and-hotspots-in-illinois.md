Illinois Audubon Society: Warbler Ridge Sanctuary (Alton), Prairie Ridge Sanctuary (Galesburg), Montelle Winery Sanctuary (Staunton). State/Federal areas: Champaign County Forest Preserve, Emiquon National Wildlife Refuge, Hennepin & Hopper Lakes State Fish and Wildlife Area. Other locations: Shawnee National Forest (southern Illinois, 280+ species documented), Starved Rock State Park, Morton Arboretum, Chicago Botanic Garden, Brookfield Zoo. Spring (April-May): Warblers, tanagers, orioles. Summer: breeding season, birds of prey. Fall (Sept-Oct): migration south, waterfowl. Winter: Snow Bunting, American Tree Sparrow.
