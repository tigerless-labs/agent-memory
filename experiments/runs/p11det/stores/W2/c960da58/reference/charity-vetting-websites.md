Platforms to research and verify charities before donating or participating:\n\n**Rating/Research Sites:**\n- Charity Navigator (charitynavigator.org)\n- GuideStar (guidestar.org)\n- BBB Wise Giving Alliance (give.org)\n- GreatNonprofits (greatnonprofits.org)\n- Charity Watch (charitywatch.org)\n\n**Due Diligence Steps:**\n- Verify 501(c)(3) status via IRS website\n- Review Form 990 financial reports\n- Research programs, services, and stated impact\n- Investigate governance and leadership team\n- Check online reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)\n- Contact the charity directly with questions\n- Look for accreditation from BBB or Independent Charities of America\n- Trust your instincts if something feels off
