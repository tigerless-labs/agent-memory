Recommended waterproof hiking boots featuring good traction and ankle support:

1. **Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof** - Popular choice with excellent waterproofing, breathability. Vibram TC5+ outsole for superior grip on various terrain.
2. **Salomon Quest 4D 3 GTX** - Features Gore-Tex waterproof/breathable membrane, Contagrip sole for wet and dry surfaces.
3. **Keen Targhee II Mid WP** - Comfortable waterproof design with rugged outsole for uneven terrain.
4. **The North Face Hedgehog Fastpack GTX** - Designed for fast-paced hiking. Gore-Tex membrane with grippy Vibram outsole for various trail conditions.
5. **La Sportiva Raptor GTX** - Comfortable waterproof design with aggressive outsole for rocky, wet, and dry terrain.

Key features: Gore-Tex or eVent waterproofing, deep-lugged outsole for grip, ankle support, cushioned midsole, breathable upper.
