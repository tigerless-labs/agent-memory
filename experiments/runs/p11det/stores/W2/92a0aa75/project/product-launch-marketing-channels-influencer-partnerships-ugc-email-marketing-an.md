## Selected Marketing Channels

Influencer Partnerships: $100-$1,000 per post (micro), $1,000-$5,000 (mid-tier), $5,000-$20,000 (top-tier); 10-20% agency fees. KPIs: reach, engagement, conversions, engagement rate, brand mentions.

User-Generated Content: Content creation $10-$100 per submission; campaign promotion $500-$5,000; moderation $500-$2,000/month. KPIs: participation rate, engagement, conversions, sentiment analysis, content quality.

Email Marketing: Tools $50-$500/month; content creation $500-$2,000; list building $500-$2,000. Activities: pre-launch list, teasers, launch announcement, post-launch nurturing.

Account-Based Marketing: Platform $1,000-$5,000/month; content $1,000-$5,000; advertising $1,000-$10,000. Activities: identify accounts, personalized content, targeted ads.

Overall allocation: Influencer+UGC 10-20% of budget; Email+ABM 15-30% of budget.
