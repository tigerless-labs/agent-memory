Philadelphia venues suited for intimate concerts with good proximity to stage:\n\n- **The Fillmore Philadelphia** (2,500 capacity) — mid-sized rock/pop venue\n- **The Foundry at The Fillmore** (450 capacity) — intimate setting\n- **Union Transfer** (1,000 capacity) — indie/alternative focus\n- **Theatre of Living Arts (TLA)** (1,100 capacity) — rock and pop shows\n- **First Unitarian Church** (600 capacity) — diverse music genres\n- **Boot & Saddle** (150 capacity) — very intimate, small indie acts\n\nAll offer closer connection to performers than large arenas. Tickets tend to sell out quickly at smaller venues. Arrive early for good standing room near stage.
