Websites: Justin Guitar (extensive fingerpicking section), Guitar Tricks (dedicated course), Fingerpicking.net (techniques and tabs). YouTube: Marty Music, Fingerstyle Guitar, Andy Guitar. Mobile apps: Fender Play, Guitar Tricks App. Songs recommended: Blackbird (Beatles), Fire and Rain (James Taylor), Stairway to Heaven (Led Zeppelin), Skinny Love (Bon Iver), Fast Car (Tracy Chapman), Wish You Were Here (Ed Sheeran), Hallelujah (Buckley), The Scientist (Coldplay), Landslide (Fleetwood Mac).
