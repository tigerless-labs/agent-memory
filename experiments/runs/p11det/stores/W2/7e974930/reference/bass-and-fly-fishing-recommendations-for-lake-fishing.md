Bass lures: Topwater (Popper, Spook, Frog), Soft plastics (Curly Tail Grub, Worm, Lizard), Crankbaits (Shad, Square-bill, Deep-diving), Jigs (Jig-and-Pig, Swim bait, Finesse), Spinnerbait, A-rig. Fly patterns: Streamers (Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, Baitfish), Surface (Poppers, Sliders, Gurgler), Nymphs (Crayfish, Crawdad, Hare's Ear, Leech), Dry (Frog, Ant, Beetle). Fly rod: 9-10 ft 6-8 weight. Reel: 150-200 yd backing. Line: Weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking. Leader: 9-12 ft 15-20 lb. Tippet: 1-2 ft 12-15 lb. Knots: Improved Clinch, Palomar, Loop. Retrieves: strip, twitch, drag-free. Target: rocks, weeds, logs; cast parallel to structure.
