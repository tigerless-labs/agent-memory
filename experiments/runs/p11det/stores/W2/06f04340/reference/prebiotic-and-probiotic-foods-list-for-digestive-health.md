## Prebiotic-rich foods\n- Chicory root\n- Garlic\n- Onions\n- Asparagus\n- Bananas\n- Apples\n- Oats\n- Jerusalem artichoke\n\n## Probiotic-rich foods\n- Yogurt (with live and active cultures)\n- Kefir\n- Fermented vegetables (sauerkraut, kimchi, pickles)\n- Tempeh (fermented soybeans)\n- Miso (fermented soybean paste)\n- Kombucha (fermented tea)\n- Natto (fermented soybean)\n\n## Meal incorporation ideas\n- Add sliced bananas or berries to oatmeal or yogurt for breakfast\n- Roast garlic and onions for sandwiches or pasta sauce\n- Use asparagus as side dish or in omelets\n- Make salads with mixed greens, apples, onions, or artichokes\n- Use oats as base for breakfast and lunch meals\n- Make salads with marinated or pickled vegetables\n- Add sweet potato to meals (baked, mashed, roasted)\n- Make green smoothies with banana, spinach/kale, oats, and milk
