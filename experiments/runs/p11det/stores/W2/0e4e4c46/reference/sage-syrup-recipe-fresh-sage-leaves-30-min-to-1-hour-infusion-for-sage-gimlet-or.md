**Sage Syrup Recipe**

Ingredients:
- Water and sugar for simple syrup
- Fresh sage leaves (preferred for vibrant flavor)

Instructions:
1. Combine water and sugar in saucepan
2. Bring to boil, then simmer 5-7 minutes until sugar dissolves
3. Remove from heat and add fresh sage leaves
4. Let steep for 30 minutes to 1 hour (taste to adjust flavor intensity)
5. Strain through fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth
6. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks

Uses:
- Sage Gimlet (Hendrick's gin, dry vermouth, sage syrup, black pepper)
- Sage Spritz (Hendrick's gin, lemon juice, simple syrup, soda water, garnished with sage/thyme)
