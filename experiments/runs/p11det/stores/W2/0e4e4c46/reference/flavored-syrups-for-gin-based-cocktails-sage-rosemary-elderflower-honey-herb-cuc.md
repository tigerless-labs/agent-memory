**Gin-Based Cocktail Syrup Pairings**

1. **Sage Syrup** - Savory, herbaceous; try in Sage Gimlet or Sage Spritz
2. **Rosemary Syrup** - Piney, herbaceous; use in Rosemary Gin Fizz or Rosemary Collins with lemon
3. **Elderflower Syrup** - Delicate, floral; try in Elderflower Gin Fizz or Elderflower Spritz with soda
4. **Honey Syrup with herbs** (thyme, rosemary, lavender) - Sweet, savory; use in Honey Gin Old Fashioned or Spritz
5. **Cucumber Syrup** - Refreshing, light; try in Cucumber Gimlet or Cucumber Collins
6. **Parsley Syrup** - Bright, green; use in Parsley Gin Fizz or Parsley Spritz
7. **Lemon Verbena Syrup** - Citrusy, herbaceous; try in Lemon Verbena Gin Fizz or Spritz
8. **Grapefruit Syrup** - Bitter, citrusy; use in Grapefruit Gin Fizz or Grapefruit Collins
9. **Mint Syrup with variations** (basil, lemongrass, cilantro) - Crispness and botanicals
10. **Spiced Apple Syrup** (cinnamon, nutmeg, allspice) - Fall-inspired; try in Spiced Apple Gin Fizz or Spritz
