Recommended energy bars for sports snacks:\n\n- **Clif Bar**: $1.50–$2.50 per bar. Classic choice among athletes, made with wholesome ingredients like nuts, seeds, and dried fruits.\n- **RXBAR**: $2–$3 per bar. Known for simple, natural ingredients and high protein content.\n- **Quest Bar**: $2–$3 per bar. Popular choice offering high-protein, low-sugar option.\n- **PowerBar**: $1.50–$3 per bar. Tried-and-true option available for decades.\n\nAll widely available at sports stores, online retailers, and grocery stores. Consider buying in bulk for better value.
