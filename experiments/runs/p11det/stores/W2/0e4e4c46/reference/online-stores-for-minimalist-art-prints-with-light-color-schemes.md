Online platforms recommended for finding minimalist abstract art prints with light color schemes:

1. **Society6** — Large collection of minimalist art, can filter by light/pastel color palettes
2. **Minted** — Curated minimalist designs with 'Light and Airy' and 'Soft and Soothing' categories
3. **Etsy** — Independent artists selling unique handmade prints; search 'minimalist art print' + 'light color scheme'
4. **Desenio** — Scandinavian-focused minimalist prints with 'Light and Airy' category
5. **AllPosters** — Large collection with filters by style, color, and size
6. **20x200** — Affordable limited-edition prints from emerging and established artists
7. **The Poster Club** — Curated selection with 'Light and Minimal' category
8. **Art.com** — Vast collection with style, color, and size filters
9. **Museum online shops** — MoMA and Guggenheim offer high-quality reproductions with minimalist designs
10. **Independent artists' websites** — Behance and DeviantArt for unique custom pieces

**Tips when purchasing:**
- Check print quality, paper type (look for acid-free, premium options), and framing availability
- Consider paper weight (300gsm+ for quality feel) and finish (matte preferred for office glare reduction)
- UV-filtering glass protects from fading
