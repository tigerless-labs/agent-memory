Recommended Australian artists to check out:

1. **G Flip** — Melbourne-based singer, songwriter, and producer; catchy pop-rock anthems
2. **The Teskey Brothers** — Melbourne blues and roots band; vintage sound
3. **Genesis Owusu** — Ghanaian-Australian rapper/songwriter; blends hip-hop, funk, soul
4. **Tia Gostelow** — Singer-songwriter from Far North Queensland; indie pop
5. **A.GIRL** — Sydney-based R&B artist; soulful vocals and emotional songwriting

All have been receiving critical acclaim in the Australian music industry.
