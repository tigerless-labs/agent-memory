Strategy to create a cohesive wardrobe using a hybrid approach:

**Foundation (Capsule):**
- Select 5-7 graphic t-shirts from H&M as core pieces
- Build outfit foundation around these graphic tees

**Mixing & Matching:**
- Layer graphic tees under thinner sweaters from Madewell
- Combine with winter pieces (coat, bottoms) from Zara
- Use fitted bottoms to balance bulky tops
- Add simple accessories (hats, gloves) to tie looks together

**Styling Principles:**
- Avoid overwhelming or bulky silhouettes by balancing voluminous pieces with fitted ones
- Use belts to cinch waist and create defined silhouette
- Mix textures and proportions for visual interest
- Choose coats with fitted silhouettes (tailored or pea coat style)

**Recent Inventory:**
- Graphic t-shirts from H&M (primary foundation pieces)
- Winter clothing items from Zara (including new winter coat)
- Sweaters from Madewell (layering pieces)
- Fitted jeans and bottoms (various stores)

This approach balances wardrobe structure with flexibility and personal style expression.
