## Ways to find local museums and science centers\n- **Google Maps** — Search for museums or science centers, filter by rating and distance\n- **Google Search** — Search "museums near me" or "science centers near me"\n- **Local Tourism Websites** — Visit local tourism bureau or visitor websites\n- **Social Media** — Follow museums and science centers on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram\n- **Ask Around** — Friends, family, coworkers often have recommendations\n\n## Museum directories\n- **MuseumsUSA** — Comprehensive directory of U.S. museums\n- **Association of Science-Technology Centers (ASTC)** — Directory of science centers and museums worldwide\n- **Local Cultural/Science Organizations** — Many cities provide local listings\n\n## Tips\n- Check museum websites directly to confirm hours, admission fees, exhibit schedules\n- Call museums to ask about upcoming events\n- Look for behind-the-scenes tours and special events\n- Many museums have newsletters highlighting upcoming exhibits
