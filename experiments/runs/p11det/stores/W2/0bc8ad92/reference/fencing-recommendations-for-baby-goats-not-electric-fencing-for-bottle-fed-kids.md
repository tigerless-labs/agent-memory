**Fencing height and specs for baby goats:**
- Minimum 4 feet; 5+ feet recommended (baby goats are excellent climbers)
- Mesh size: 2-inch x 4-inch minimum; 1-inch x 2-inch for added security
- Wire gauge: minimum 12.5 gauge

**Recommended fencing materials:**
- Woven wire fencing: 2-inch x 4-inch mesh prevents head/horn entanglement
- High-tensile fencing: Strong and durable but less effective for containment
- Cattle panels: Sturdy, durable, easy to install
- Field fencing: Designed for larger areas
- Goat-specific panels: Smaller mesh, more durable

**Infrastructure details:**
- Posts: Sturdy, spaced 8-10 feet apart
- Corner posts with anchor system to prevent sagging
- Gates: Sturdy, secure, self-closing recommended
- Visibility: Add strips or flags for boundary visibility

**Electric fencing for baby goats:**
NOT recommended while bottle-fed. Baby goats:
- Don't understand the shock/fence connection
- May experience excessive fear and stress
- Can be harmed by shocks at this vulnerable stage
- Better to use once weaned and older (3-4 months+)

**Predator protection:**
- Consider additional coyote-proof measures if needed
- Livestock guardian dogs or other guard animals
- Sturdy secure gates
