## Video-based resources\n- **Crash Course** — YouTube channel with video series on chemistry, physics, and sciences\n- **3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)** — Animated videos explaining physics and chemistry concepts\n- **Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell** — Animated videos on scientific topics including chemistry and physics\n- **Physics Girl** — Dianna Cowern's YouTube channel exploring physics through experiments\n- **Veritasium** — Focuses on experiments and demonstrations of physics concepts\n\n## Article-based resources\n- **Scientific American** — Popular online magazine covering science developments\n- **Physics Today** — Magazine-style website for physics research and news\n- **Chemistry World** — Chemistry news, research, and applications\n- **American Chemical Society (ACS) Central Science** — News and educational resources\n- **Physics.org** — Articles, news, and educational materials on physics\n\n## Online courses\n- **edX** — MOOC platform with chemistry and physics courses from top universities\n- **MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW)** — Free online courses and resources\n- **Coursera** — MOOC platform with chemistry and physics courses\n- **Khan Academy** — Free video lectures and exercises\n- **iTunes U** — Free educational lectures from top universities\n\n## Other resources\n- **r/learnchemistry and r/Physics** — Reddit communities for questions and discussions\n- **Sciencedaily, Phys.org, Chemistry News** — Science news blogs\n- **Science podcasts** — The Science Podcast, Physics Podcast, Chemistry in its Element
