Non-profit organization dedicated to protecting marine ecosystems and ocean wildlife. Website: www.oceanconservancy.org

Key programs: Trash Free Seas (plastic pollution reduction), Ocean Acidification research, Marine Wildlife Protection (sea turtles, sharks, dolphins), Ocean Policy advocacy.

Volunteer opportunities: Beach cleanups, coastal advocacy, research and monitoring, event support, online activism, citizen science projects.

Trash Free Seas page: www.oceanconservancy.org/trash-free-seas

Related conservation organizations include The Nature Conservancy, Wildlife Conservation Society, Audubon Society, Sierra Club, and others focused on specific ecosystems and species.
