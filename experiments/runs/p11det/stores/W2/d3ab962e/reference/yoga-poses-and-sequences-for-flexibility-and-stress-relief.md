Flexibility poses: Downward-Facing Dog, Cobra Pose, Seated Forward Fold, Pigeon Pose, Tree Pose. Stress relief poses: Child's Pose, Legs Up The Wall Pose, Seated Twist, Sphinx Pose, Savasana. Sequences include Sun Salutations, Yin-Yang Flow, and Restorative Yoga.
