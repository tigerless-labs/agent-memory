## Summer Cocktail Recipes

1. **Pimm's Cup** — British classic with Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit
2. **Watermelon Margaritas** — Watermelon puree, tequila, lime juice, agave nectar
3. **Summer Sangria** — White wine, peaches, strawberries, citrus, sparkling water
4. **Cranberry Lime Sparkler** — Cranberry juice, lime juice, vodka, sparkling water, mint
5. **Gin-Gin Mule** — Ginger beer, lime juice, gin (summer Moscow Mule variation)
6. **Spicy Pineapple Collins** — Pineapple juice, tequila, lime juice, cayenne pepper
7. **Rosé Spritz** — Rosé wine, lemon-lime soda, grenadine
8. **Blackberry Basil Martini** — Blackberry puree, vodka, lime juice, basil
9. **Citrus-Ginger Fizz** — Orange, lemon, grapefruit juices, ginger liqueur, sparkling water
10. **Elderflower Collins** — Elderflower liqueur, gin, lemon juice, soda water

All pair well with summer entertaining and grilled foods.
