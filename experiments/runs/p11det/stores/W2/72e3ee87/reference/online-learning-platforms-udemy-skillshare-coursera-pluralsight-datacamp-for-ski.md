**Online course platforms:**
- **Udemy**: Wide range of Photoshop/Illustrator courses; filter by rating, price, duration
- **Skillshare**: Creative projects and practical skills focus for design tools
- **Coursera**: University partnerships; graphic design, digital art, visual design courses
- **Pluralsight**: Courses on Photoshop, Illustrator, and other creative tools
- **DataCamp**: Interactive Data Science with Python courses and tutorials

**Free resources:**
- Adobe Creative Cloud tutorials (official)
- Phlearn (in-depth tutorials)
- Tutvid (free and paid tutorials)
- Creative Bloq (design blog with tutorials)
- YouTube channels: Tutvid, Phlearn, Nathaniel Dodson

**Practice communities:**
- Dribbble, Behance, Reddit r/design (design challenges)
- Reddit r/photoshop and r/illustrator (communities)
