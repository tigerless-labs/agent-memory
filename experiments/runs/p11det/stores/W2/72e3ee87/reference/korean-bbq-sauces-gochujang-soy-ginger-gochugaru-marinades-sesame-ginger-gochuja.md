Korean BBQ Sauces: (1) Gochujang - gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil; (2) Soy-Ginger - soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice vinegar; (3) Gochugaru - Korean chili flakes, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil. Marinades: (1) Sesame-Ginger - sesame oil, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar for chicken/beef/pork; (2) Korean-Style with Gochujang - gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil; (3) Cilantro-Lime - cilantro, lime juice, garlic, olive oil for chicken/shrimp/vegetables. Tip: balance bold flavors with acidity like lime or vinegar.
