Jewelry: NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers), AGTA (American Gem Trade Association), ASA (American Society of Appraisers). Antiques/collectibles: AAA (Appraisers Association of America), ISA (International Society of Appraisers), NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers). Select certified, experienced appraisers with no jewelry store affiliations. Get referrals, check reputation. One appraisal usually sufficient unless item is extremely rare. Provide proof of ownership, provenance, maintenance records, previous appraisals, and high-quality photos. Factors affecting value: diamond/gemstone quality, metal type, craftsmanship, condition, rarity, market demand, comparable sales.
