## Major Irish Cultural Festivals

- **Galway International Arts Festival**: July (two weeks), features theater, dance, music, and visual arts from around the world
- **Clifden Arts Festival**: September, showcases Irish traditional and contemporary art, music, poetry, theater, and film
- **Connemara Mussel Festival**: May, celebrates local mussel industry with food tastings, music, and family events
- **Rose of Tralee International Festival**: August, celebrates Irish culture and heritage with focus on women's accomplishments and empowerment
- **St. Patrick's Day**: March 17th national holiday, celebrated with parades, music, dancing throughout the country

All festivals occur in Connacht region or throughout Ireland.
