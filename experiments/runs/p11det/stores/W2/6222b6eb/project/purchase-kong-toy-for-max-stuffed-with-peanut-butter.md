Decided to get Max a Kong Toy as an interactive toy to keep him engaged while away during the day. Will stuff with peanut butter since Max loves it.

Tips for use:
- Start with small amount of peanut butter
- Mix in kibbles or treats for variety
- Can freeze for added challenge and cooling effect on hot days
- Rotate with other toys to prevent boredom
- Helps reduce anxiety and destructive behavior from boredom
