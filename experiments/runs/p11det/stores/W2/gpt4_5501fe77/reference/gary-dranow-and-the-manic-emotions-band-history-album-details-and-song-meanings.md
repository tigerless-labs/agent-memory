Band History: Gary Dranow is a musician/performer who plays for The Manic Emotions, based in Salt Lake City area. He returned to music in 2008 and re-established the band in 2021. Previously, he spent 10 years (starting after late 1990s) on ski racing and coaching.

Album Destiny Road: Written in the 1990s with intended release in summer 1998. Recorded on Jefferson Airplane's old 2 inch 16 Track machine owned by producer Jerry Manfredi (the machine is legendary for being on Jefferson Airplane's private airplane). Tracks were recorded in late 1990s but never mastered due to Gary's move to Park City, Utah. Eventually finished and released to critical acclaim. Autobiographical journey chronicling struggles, triumphs, and everything in between.

Music and Themes: Gary Dranow's struggles with manic depression fuel his creativity and inspire music that speaks to human experience.

Song Made it Another Day: Speaks to experience of personal and professional life disrupted by sensitivity to others. Waking up each day with weight of world on shoulders, then finding strength to face daily struggles. Key lyric 'Like a rat in a wheel' represents never-ending struggle to get personal life in order, only to be dissuaded at end of each day. Deeper meaning: making progress amid inevitable headwinds that buffet life.

Performance: Consummate performer. Live sets showcase energy, charisma, and unwavering passion for music. Band started playing shows immediately after reformation in 2021.
