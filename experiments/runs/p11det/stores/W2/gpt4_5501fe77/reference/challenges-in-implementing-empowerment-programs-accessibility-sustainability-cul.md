Key challenges when implementing empowerment programs: (1) Accessibility — ensuring reach to those most in need (physical access, transportation, funding for participation), (2) Sustainability — finding ongoing funding, building partnerships, developing long-term impact plans, (3) Cultural sensitivity — understanding and responding to community values, beliefs, and practices, (4) Resistance to change — overcoming resistance from those invested in status quo (requires public awareness, stakeholder partnerships), (5) Measuring success — determining if programs achieve intended outcomes, tracking participation, inequality reduction, community engagement. Addressing these challenges systematically is critical for program effectiveness.
