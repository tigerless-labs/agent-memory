## Key Attractions\n\n**The Alamo** (300 Alamo Plaza)\n- Free admission, 9am-5:30pm daily (extended hours March-October)\n- Historic Spanish mission, 1836 battle site\n- Alamo Church, Long Barrack Museum, Alamo Gardens\n- 17-minute documentary available\n- No photography inside Alamo Church\n- Visit early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds\n\n**River Walk (Paseo del Río)**\n- 15-mile network of walkways along San Antonio River\n- Open 24/7, lined with restaurants, shops, bars, hotels\n- Boat tour companies available\n- Pearl District at north end\n\n**Pearl District**\n- Historic Pearl Brewery complex transformed into upscale neighborhood\n- Pearl Farmers Market: Saturdays 9am-1pm (fresh produce, baked goods, prepared foods, handmade crafts, specialty foods, flowers, local brews)\n- Notable restaurants: La Gloria (Mexican), The Boiler House (gastropub)\n- Hotel Emma, The Twig Book Shop, Elise boutique\n\n**San Antonio Missions National Historical Park**\n- Four Spanish colonial missions along the river\n- Mission Concepción (1716), Mission San José (1720 - "Queen of the Missions"), Mission San Juan (1716), Mission Espada (1731)\n- Free admission, 9am-5pm daily\n- Ranger-led and self-guided tours available\n- Mission Trail: scenic drive connecting all four missions
