Smoked meat sandwich: cured beef brisket smoked and steamed, on rye with mustard. Poutine: fries, gravy, cheese curds. Montreal bagels: smaller and sweeter than NY, boiled in honey water, wood-fired oven. Tourtière: meat pie with pork and potatoes. Maple syrup treats: taffy, sugar pie, glazed bacon. Key markets: Atwater Market, Jean-Talon Market.
