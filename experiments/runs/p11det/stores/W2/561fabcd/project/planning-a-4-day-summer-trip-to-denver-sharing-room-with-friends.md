Trip planned for summer 2023 (June–August), duration 4 days. Sharing a room with friends.

**Weather context:** Denver summers are warm (84–86°F daytime, 29–30°C), mild evenings. Expect sudden weather changes and possible rain showers.

**Packing plans:**
- Portable charger, 5,000mAh+ capacity (to fully charge device at least once); keep with cables in small pouch
- Considering travel-sized power strip (for shared room, so multiple people can charge devices simultaneously)
- "Just in case" bag with: pain relievers (acetaminophen/ibuprofen), antacids (Tums/Rolaids), band-aids, antibacterial ointment, antiseptic wipes, antihistamines (Benadryl), motion sickness medication
- Lightweight, breathable clothing; layers (lightweight sweater/jacket) for temperature swings; rain gear; hiking boots (if mountain activities planned)
- Sunscreen with high SPF, lip balm with SPF, hat, sunglasses
- Will photograph "just in case" bag before trip

**Outlets:** Standard US Type A and B plugs, 120V/60Hz; universal adapter not needed.
