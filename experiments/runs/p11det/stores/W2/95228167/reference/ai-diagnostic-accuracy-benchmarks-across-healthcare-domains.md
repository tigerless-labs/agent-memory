**Disease diagnosis accuracy - AI vs human doctors:**

1. **Cancer diagnosis (mammography)**
   - AI accuracy: 97-99%
   - Human radiologists: 87-96%
   - Note: Higher false positive rate in some AI systems

2. **Diabetic retinopathy detection**
   - AI accuracy: 92-95%
   - Comparable to human ophthalmologists

3. **Cardiac diagnosis (arrhythmias, heart conditions)**
   - AI accuracy: 95-98%
   - Human cardiologists: 80-90%

**Key considerations:**
- AI systems require high-quality, diverse datasets for training
- Explainability remains a challenge in clinical adoption
- Real-world validation differs from controlled studies
- Hybrid human-AI approaches often outperform either alone
- Bias in training data leads to inaccurate outcomes

**Source:** Referenced study from The Lancet on breast cancer diagnosis
