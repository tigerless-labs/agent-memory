Recommended pieces to explore after fingerpicking fundamentals:

1. **Fernando Sor** - Etude in B minor (Op. 35, No. 22) — arpeggios, finger independence, expressive playing
2. **Francisco Tárrega** - Recuerdos de la Alhambra — intricate arpeggios, tremolos, expressive technique
3. **Joaquín Rodrigo** - En los trigales (from "Por los campos de España") — rhythm, dynamics, phrasing
4. **Heitor Villa-Lobos** - Prelude No. 1 in E minor — expressive guitar capabilities, phrasing, dynamics
5. **Isaac Albéniz** - Asturias (Leyenda) — demanding finger independence, arpeggio control
6. **Mauro Giuliani** - Etude in A major (Op. 60, No. 1) — finger independence, arpeggios, chordal playing
7. **Luigi Boccherini** - Fandango — finger independence, arpeggio control, rhythmic precision

All pieces benefit from slow practice, metronome work, breaking into sections, recording for self-feedback, and seeking teacher guidance for interpretation.
