Strategies for expanding beyond familiar progressions:

1. **Chord families** — Focus on chords within specific key/family (major, minor, dominant, augmented, diminished) to understand relationships
2. **Analyze favorite songs** — Figure out chord progressions from songs you love; use Ultimate-Guitar, Songsterr, Guitar Pro
3. **Chord substitutions** — Replace familiar chords with others sharing similar functions/tones to discover new sounds
4. **Use chord charts/diagrams** — Visualize relationships and see how chords fit together
5. **Practice in small sections** — Master 2-3 chords, then gradually add more to full progression
6. **Identify 'glue' chords** — Find connective chords that provide tension/release (e.g., V-I progressions)
7. **Learn chord functions** — Understand tonic, dominant, subdominant roles to create logical progressions
8. **Modal interchange** — Explore chords from parallel modes or keys for added complexity
9. **Record and listen** — Track progress and identify areas needing refinement
10. **Learn from others** — Study favorite artists, attend workshops, take lessons, join communities

Bonus: Keep a chord journal/spreadsheet to organize knowledge and identify patterns.
