Best practices for storing and displaying rare vinyl records to maintain condition and value:\n\n**Storage:**\n- Keep records upright to prevent warping\n- Use acid-free, archival-quality sleeves (polypropylene or polyethylene)\n- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight\n- Maintain temperature 50-70°F and low humidity\n- Avoid stacking (max 5-6 records per stack)\n- Use sturdy record storage boxes or crates\n\n**Display:**\n- Use sturdy, adjustable record stands\n- Avoid direct sunlight or use UV-filtering glass/acrylic\n- Handle records by edges only, never center label\n- Use clean, dry hands or gloves\n- Consider record frames for wall display with UV protection\n\n**Maintenance:**\n- Clean regularly with soft, dry cloth or record brush\n- Avoid touching vinyl surface (oils from skin attract dust)\n- Catalog and grade records to track condition and value\n- Preserve original packaging, inserts, and materials for resale value
