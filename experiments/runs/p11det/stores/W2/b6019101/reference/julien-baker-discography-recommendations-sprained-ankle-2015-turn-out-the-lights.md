## Julien Baker Discography

**Albums to explore:**
- **Sprained Ankle** (2015) — Debut album, critically acclaimed for introspective songwriting, soaring vocals, and indie-rock/folk elements.
- **Turn Out the Lights** (2017) — Sophomore album, more experimental and introspective. Explores themes of love, loss, and introspection.
- **Boygenius** (2018) — Self-titled EP collaboration with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus. Features harmony-rich vocal arrangements.

**Popular tracks:**
- Appointments
- Turn Out the Lights (title track)
- Rejoice
- Souvenir
- Hurt Less

**Other work:**
- "Red Door" (2019) — Single
- "Favor" (2020) — Single

Known for emotive and introspective live performances (NPR Tiny Desk, KCRW sessions available on YouTube).
