Neighborhoods recommended for one-bedroom apartments in Toronto, budget 1200-1600/month:

The Danforth (East York): 1400-1600/month, Danforth subway station, mix of old and new buildings

Riverdale: 1400-1600/month, Donlands subway station, gentrified and trendy

Leslieville: 1300-1500/month, Queen Street East streetcar, artsy feel

The Junction: 1300-1500/month, Junction subway station, up-and-coming

Scarborough Village: 1200-1400/month, Scarborough Village subway station

North York Centre: 1200-1400/month, North York Centre subway station

All have public transit, restaurants, and shops. Visit neighborhoods day and evening. Check Toronto Police Service Crime Map.
