**Lemon Tetras:**
- Can school peacefully with neon tetras
- May form mixed school  
- Fast/agile enough to avoid gouramis but risk of fin-nipping if gouramis are aggressive
- Too small to threaten pleco

**Zebra Danios:**
- Active and hardy, can coexist peacefully with neon tetras
- May chase playfully
- Too fast/active for gouramis to catch but risk of fin-nipping
- Ignore pleco

**Both require:** School of 6-10+ to reduce stress, plenty of hiding places, adequate tank space, regular water changes.
