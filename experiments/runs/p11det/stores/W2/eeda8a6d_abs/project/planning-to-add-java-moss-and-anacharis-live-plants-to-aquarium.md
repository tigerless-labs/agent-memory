**Java Moss:**
- Low to moderate lighting (1-2 watts/gallon)
- Temp: 72-82°F (tolerates as low as 65°F)
- pH: 5.5-8.5
- Attach to rocks/driftwood/decorations using plant weight or thread
- Gentle water flow preferred
- Fertilize weekly with 5-5-5 NPK
- Provides hideout for neon tetras
- Golden honey gouramis may nibble, pleco may graze but not preferred

**Anacharis:**
- Moderate to high lighting (2-4 watts/gallon)  
- Temp: 65-85°F (prefers 72-82°F)
- pH: 6.5-8.5 (tolerates wide range)
- Floating plant, no substrate needed
- Moderate to strong water flow
- Fertilize weekly with 5-5-5 NPK
- Provides food source for pleco
- Neon tetras and gouramis may nibble but not preferred

**Care notes:** Both sensitive to copper-based medications. Clean decaying plant matter regularly. Prune anacharis for bushy growth.
