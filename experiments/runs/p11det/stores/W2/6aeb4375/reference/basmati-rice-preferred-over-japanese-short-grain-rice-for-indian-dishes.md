For Indian-inspired dishes like saag aloo, basmati rice is the better choice over Japanese short-grain rice. Basmati has long slender grains with a nutty flavor that pairs well with Indian spices, while Japanese short-grain rice is stickier and better suited for sushi and other Japanese dishes.
