Marinate flank steak (or ribeye/sirloin) 2+ hours in lime juice, garlic, oregano, cumin, paprika, salt, pepper. Grill and slice thin against the grain. French fries: use high-starch potatoes (Russet/Idaho), cut thin, soak 30 min in cold water. Double-fry method: 325°F for 3-4 min (blanching), cool completely, then 375°F for 2-3 min until golden and crispy. Assemble fries on plate, top with steak strips, grated cheese (Monterey Jack or Cheddar), radishes, lime wedges, sour cream. Use thermometer to maintain oil temperature.
