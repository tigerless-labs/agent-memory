Avoiding screens for at least 1 hour before bedtime to minimize blue light exposure and reduce stimulation.\n\nImplementation:\n- Sets phone reminder at 10:15 PM to put phone away\n- Created comfortable reading/relaxation spot in bedroom to avoid temptation to check phone or watch TV\n- Has accountability partner (friend or family member) who checks in every few days to monitor progress\n- Focuses on alternative activities: reading, meditation, calming music, gentle stretches, yoga, gratitude journaling\n\nRationale: Blue light suppresses melatonin, screens are stimulating, and screen-free time improves sleep quality and wind-down effectiveness.
