Expressed strong interest in hiking the Andes, with particular focus on the Inca Trail to Machu Picchu. Plans to do multiple trips to explore various routes.

**Primary interest:** Inca Trail to Machu Picchu (Cusco, Peru)

**Lesser-known routes interested in exploring:**
- **Colca Canyon Trek** (southern Peru) — less crowded than Inca Trail, stunning canyon and mountain views
- **Huayhuash Circuit** (northern Peru) — challenging multi-day hike with high mountain passes, glacial lakes, panoramic views
- **Ausangate Trek** (southern Peru) — through small Andean communities, circling Ausangate mountain, snow-capped peaks and turquoise lakes
- **Salkantay Trek** (remote Andean villages) — alternative route to Machu Picchu, includes high passes and Salkantay glacier

Interested in interacting with local Andean cultures during these treks.
