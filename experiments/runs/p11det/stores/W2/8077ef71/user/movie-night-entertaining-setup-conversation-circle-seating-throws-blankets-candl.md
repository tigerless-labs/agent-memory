Prefers to set up movie nights with cozy atmosphere including:\n\n- **Seating**: Conversation circle layout with couch, armchair, and extra chairs/ottomans arranged to encourage socializing\n- **Ambiance**: Dimmed lights, scented candles (vanilla, cinnamon, lavender, caramel, or woodland berries recommended), fireplace for warmth\n- **Comfort**: Multiple throws and blankets available for guests to snuggle up with\n- **Snacks**: Mini quiches and fruit skewers (mess-free finger foods)\n- **Beverages**: Light to medium-bodied red wines like Pinot Noir, Beaujolais, or Valpolicella\n\nPlans to accommodate larger groups for future movie nights.
