Five organizations recommended for supporting Amazon rainforest and indigenous rights protection:

1. **Amazon Watch** — Works with indigenous leaders and environmental organizations to protect the Amazon rainforest, defend indigenous rights, and address climate change.

2. **Rainforest Trust** — Conserves tropical forests, including the Amazon, by partnering with local organizations and communities to establish protected areas.

3. **WWF (World Wildlife Fund)** — Conserves biodiversity and protects the environment, including efforts to conserve the Amazon rainforest.

4. **Cultural Survival** — Defends the rights, cultures, and lands of indigenous peoples around the world, including the Amazon region.

5. **Greenpeace** — Has campaigned for the protection of the Amazon rainforest and works to promote sustainable practices and protect biodiversity.
