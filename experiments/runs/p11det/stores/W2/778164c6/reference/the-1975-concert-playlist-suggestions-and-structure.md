**Sample playlist structure:**
- 3-4 songs from The 1975
- 2-3 songs from similar artists
- 2-3 upbeat, energetic tracks
- 1-2 concert anthems
- 1-2 personal favorite songs
- 1-2 mood-booster tracks
- 1 concert-themed track (optional)

**The 1975 tracks to include:**
- "The Sound"
- "Girls"
- "Give Yourself a Try"
- "Somebody Else"
- "Robbers"

**Similar artists to consider:**
- Pale Waves
- The Neighbourhood
- COIN

**Other recommendations:**
- "Mr. Brightside" by The Killers
- "Best Day of My Life" by American Authors
- "I Gotta Feeling" by The Black Eyed Peas
- "Uptown Funk" by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars
- "Can't Stop the Feeling!" by Justin Timberlake
- "Happy" by Pharrell Williams
