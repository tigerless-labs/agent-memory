Created a composite image combining a photo of niece dressed as a witch with a PNG illustration of a spooky castle. Faced challenges matching lighting and colors between the two images. Used GIMP techniques including:

- Levels and Color Balance adjustments
- Color grading/colorization
- Shadow and highlight effects using Drop Shadow, Dodge and Burn filters
- Layer blending modes (Multiply, Overlay, Soft Light)
- Lighting Effects filter for ambient lighting

Key learning: merging layers should be done once satisfied with changes, but keeping copies of original layers before merging is recommended for flexibility.
