Replacing old Manfrotto BeFree tripod. Comparing two premium brands:

Gitzo: Italian design, $300-1500, carbon fiber available, G-Lock mechanism, lightweight for travel

Really Right Stuff (RRS): American-made, $500-2500, lever-release locks, modular, excellent stability for heavy lenses

Tradeoff: Gitzo better for portability; RRS better for stability with telephoto lenses like the 70-200mm.
