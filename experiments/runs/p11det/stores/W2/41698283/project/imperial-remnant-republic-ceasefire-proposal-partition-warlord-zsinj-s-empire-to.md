**Plot Status: Ceasefire Proposal (In Motion)**

**Antagonist: Warlord Zsinj**
- Broke away from Empire to form independent splinter empire
- Headquarters: Serenno (non-Sith planet)
- Controls: the old Sith Worlds (core of his empire, held since early days of splinter formation)
- Threat: actively threatening both the Imperial Remnant and the New Republic

**Proposal Summary:**
Lizzie proposed to the Council of Moffs a temporary ceasefire with the New Republic to jointly partition Zsinj's empire. Goals:
- Eliminate Zsinj as a mutual threat
- Reclaim the Sith Worlds for the Imperial Remnant
- Divide Zsinj's remaining territory between the two powers

**Current Status:**
- Council of Moffs: **Approved** the plan
- New Republic: **Under consideration**
  - Lizzie contacted Mon Mothma (Republic Chancellor) via holoprojector
  - Mon Mothma agreed to discuss with her council and provide decision
  - Meeting took place at the formerly-Imperial Palace on Coruscant

**Key Locations:**
- Bastion: temporary Imperial capital (industrial planet)
- Coruscant: old Imperial capital, currently New Republic-controlled
- Serenno: Zsinj's power base
