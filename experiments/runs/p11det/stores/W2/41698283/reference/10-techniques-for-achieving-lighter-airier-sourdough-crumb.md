Comprehensive tips for improving sourdough texture, shared after user noted they'd been overmixing their dough.

1. **Use a healthy, active sourdough starter**: Robust, bubbly starter is essential. Feed regularly and use at peak activity.

2. **Use the right flour**: Bread flour (12-14% protein) produces more open, airy crumb. Can experiment with all-purpose but adjust water content and proofing time.

3. **Maintain gentle mixing and folding technique**: Avoid overmixing. Mix ingredients just until shaggy mass forms, then use gentle folding to develop dough.

4. **Keep dough at right temperature**: Ideal range 75°F–78°F (24°C–25°C) for fermentation. Helps yeast and bacteria produce more open crumb.

5. **Use the right water content**: Sourdough typically needs 70-80% hydration for open, airy crumb. Balance is key.

6. **Proof slowly and gently**: Slow, gentle proofing develops more even, open crumb. Avoid over-proofing (leads to dense, flat crumb).

7. **Score the bread correctly**: Proper scoring before baking controls how bread expands, resulting in more open, airy crumb.

8. **Bake with steam**: Steam allows bread to expand more during baking, creating more open crumb. Use steamy oven or pan of water.

9. **Monitor dough's progress**: Watch carefully during fermentation and proofing. Adjust techniques if dough becomes too dense or tight.

10. **Practice makes perfect**: Takes time and repeated attempts. Keep experimenting and adjusting until skills develop.
