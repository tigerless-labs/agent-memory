## Vegan High-Protein Sources for Salads\n\nNutritional info per 3-ounce serving (unless noted):\n\n### High-Protein, Low-Carb Options\n- **Seitan**: 21-22g protein, 4-5g carbs | Made from wheat gluten, meat-like texture\n- **Tempeh**: 15-16g protein, 6-7g carbs | Fermented soybean product, nutty flavor, rich in probiotics\n- **Tofu** (extra-firm): 9-10g protein, 2-3g carbs | Versatile, neutral flavor, highest protein when extra-firm or super-firm\n\n### Moderate-Carb but Protein-Rich\n- **Lentils** (1/2 cup cooked): 9-10g protein, 20-22g carbs | High in fiber\n- **Chickpeas** (1/2 cup cooked): 5-6g protein, 20-22g carbs | High in fiber\n\n### Other Options\n- **Vegan Protein Powder**: Convenient boost, look for low-carb options made from pea, hemp, or brown rice protein\n- **Mushrooms** (1 cup): 2-3g protein, 1-2g carbs | Adds meaty texture\n- **Vegan Meat Alternatives**: Check nutrition labels, look for pea protein, rice protein, or soy protein bases\n\n### Recommendations\n- Seitan, tempeh, and tofu are the best high-protein, low-carb options\n- Monitor portion sizes to keep carb content in check\n- Experiment with combinations to meet daily protein goals
