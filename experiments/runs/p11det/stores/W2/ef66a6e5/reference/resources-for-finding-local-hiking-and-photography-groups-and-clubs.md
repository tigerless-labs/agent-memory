**Primary platform:** Meetup.com — search for hiking/photography groups in local area\n\n**Hiking groups:** Sierra Club, Appalachian Mountain Club, REI outdoor adventure groups, local park districts, conservation organizations\n\n**Photography groups:** Meetup.com, local photography clubs, camera stores (B&H Photo, Camera World — offer workshops/classes), Flickr, Facebook photography groups, local colleges/community centers offering photography classes\n\n**General networking:** Local outdoor festivals, photography exhibitions, nature walks, volunteer opportunities with park/conservation organizations
