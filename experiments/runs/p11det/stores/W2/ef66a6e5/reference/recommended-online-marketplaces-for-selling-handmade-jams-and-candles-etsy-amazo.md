**Primary platforms (recommended for Sweet Delights):**
- **Etsy** — Well-established for handmade, vintage, unique items
- **Amazon Handmade** — Amazon's artisan platform (requires application)

**Additional marketplaces:**
- **eBay** — Popular general marketplace
- **Bonanza** — Online marketplace for handmade goods
- **Food52** — Artisanal foods focus
- **UncommonGoods** — Unique/handmade products (submit for consideration)
- **Gift Shop Now** — Specialty food and gift vendor platform

**E-commerce platforms:**
- **Shopify** — Has Shopify Marketplace feature
- **WooCommerce** — WordPress e-commerce plugin with marketplace feature

**Key considerations:**
- Review fees, commissions, and rules for each
- Create professional seller profiles with high-quality images
- Handle customer service, shipping, returns
- Optimize product listings for search
- Maintain consistent branding across platforms
- Keep website as central business hub
