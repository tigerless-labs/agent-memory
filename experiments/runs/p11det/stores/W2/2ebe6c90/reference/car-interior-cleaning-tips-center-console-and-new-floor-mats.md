Car interior cleaning plan for weekend of 2022-12-15:\n\n**Center Console Cleaning:**\n1. Vacuum first with handheld or brush attachment\n2. Damp microfiber cloth (not soaking wet) for gentle wiping\n3. Dry with clean cloth section to prevent water spots\n4. Gentle automotive interior cleaner for stubborn stains (apply to cloth, not console)\n5. Compressed air for tight spaces and crevices\n\n**Floor Mat Cleaning (new set):**\n1. Vacuum to remove loose dirt and debris\n2. Mild soap and warm water with soft-bristled brush\n3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water\n4. Dry with clean towel or air dry (avoid high heat)\n5. Consider specialized automotive floor mat cleaner if needed\n\n**Prevention:**\n- Use dust-repelling product or fabric protector\n- Regular vacuuming prevents buildup\n- Consider center console organizer to reduce clutter and make cleaning easier
