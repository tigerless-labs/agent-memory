Instagram Live: 10-30 minutes, visually-focused, behind-the-scenes, mobile-first vertical format. YouTube Live: 30-60 minutes, in-depth content, high production quality, SEO optimized. Facebook Live: 30-90 minutes, personal stories, community-focused, event promotion.
