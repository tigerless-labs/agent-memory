Decided to launch a live makeup tutorial on YouTube as a way to provide in-depth content and leverage audience interest in skincare/beauty routine. Audience has been asking questions about makeup and beauty tips. Platform choice: YouTube for its longer-form content capability (30-60 min streams), evergreen value, and SEO benefits.

Key considerations:
- Will need good lighting, sound, and camera equipment for professional quality
- Will prepare specific theme/focus (e.g., everyday look, evening look, or product-specific)
- Plans to encourage real-time interaction and answer audience questions during stream
- Opportunity to differentiate from skincare routine content on Instagram and Q&A sessions on Facebook
