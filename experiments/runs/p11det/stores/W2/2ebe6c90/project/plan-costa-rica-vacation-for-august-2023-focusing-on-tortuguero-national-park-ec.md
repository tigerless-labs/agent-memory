Planned August 2023 summer vacation to Costa Rica with focus on:
- **Tortuguero National Park** — multi-day eco-lodge stay
- **Wildlife spotting** — monkeys (howler, capuchin, spider), sloths, birds (toucans, macaws, quetzals), crocodiles, iguanas, jaguars
- **Transportation from San José to Tortuguero:**
  1. Domestic flight (1 hour) via Tobias Bolaños (SYQ) to Tortuguero Airport (TTQ), then boat transfer (1-2 hours)
  2. Shuttle bus to La Pavona (4-5 hours), then boat transfer (1-2 hours)
  3. Private transfer to La Pavona, then private boat

**Recommended eco-lodges:**
- Lapa Rios Ecolodge (Osa Peninsula)
- Monteverde Lodge (Monteverde)
- Arenal Observatory Lodge (Arenal Volcano)
- Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero)
- El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula)

**Recommended sustainable tour operators:**
- Costa Rica Expeditions
- Osa Conservation
- Tortuguero Canals Tours
- Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve
- Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours

**Packing tips:** lightweight gear, insect repellent, hiking shoes, refillable water bottle, binoculars, camera
