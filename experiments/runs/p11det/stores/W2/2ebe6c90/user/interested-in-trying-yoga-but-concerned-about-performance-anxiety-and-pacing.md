User wants to start a yoga practice but has two main concerns:

1. **Performance anxiety** — worried about not being good at yoga or falling behind while learning
2. **Pacing concerns** — anxious about being able to keep up with class speed

Key reassurances from exploration:
- Yoga is for all bodies and flexibility levels
- No competition, focusing on listening to body matters more than pace
- Beginner classes, props, and modifications are designed to help
- Taking breaks and practicing at home builds confidence
- Finding a supportive teacher makes a difference

Next step when ready: look for beginner-friendly classes with patient instructors who offer modifications.
