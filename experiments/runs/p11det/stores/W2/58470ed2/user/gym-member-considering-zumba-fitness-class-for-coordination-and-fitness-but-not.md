Gym member. Considering attending Zumba fitness class at the gym.\n\nZumba is good for:\n- Overall fitness and cardiovascular endurance\n- Coordination and rhythm development\n- Flexibility and strength\n- Confidence building\n\nHowever: Zumba is simplified fitness-focused dance and won't specifically improve salsa or Afro-Cuban rhythms skills. Recommendation: do both Zumba for fitness and take specific salsa/Afro-Cuban classes for dance technique improvement.
