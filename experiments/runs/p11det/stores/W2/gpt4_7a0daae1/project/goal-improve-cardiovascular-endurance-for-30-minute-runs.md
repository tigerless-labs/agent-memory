Working to improve cardiovascular endurance for 30-minute running workouts.

**Training Methods:**
- High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT): 2-3x/week, 20-30 min sessions
- Steady-state cardio: 30-45 min at moderate intensity, 3-4x/week
- Hill sprints: 30-90 sec uphill at max effort with recovery
- Interval training: 4-6 x 800m at fast pace with 400m active recovery
- Tempo runs: 20-30 min at challenging pace with warm-up/cool-down
- Long Slow Distance (LSD) runs: Once weekly at comfortable pace for 30-60 min

**Supporting habits:**
- Proper warm-ups and cool-downs
- Hydration and fueling before/during/after runs
- Mix up routes and terrain
- Balance with strength training and flexibility work
