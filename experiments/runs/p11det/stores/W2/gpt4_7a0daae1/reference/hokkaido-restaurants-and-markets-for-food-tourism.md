Recommended spots in Hokkaido for food exploration:

## Markets
- **Nijo Fish Market** (Sapporo) — fresh seafood including crabs, sea urchins, squid
- **Tanukikoji Shopping Street** (Sapporo) — local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs

## Ramen
- **Sapporo Ramen Yokocho** — alley with multiple local ramen shops

## Jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- **Daruma** (Sapporo)
- **Kitaichi Hall** (Asahikawa)

## Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)
- **Yurigakuro** (Sapporo)
- **Kakizaki Shokudo** (Otaru)

## Transportation options
- Rent a car (good road network)
- JR Hokkaido trains (connect major cities)
- Buses (extensive network covering tourist spots and cities)
- Bicycle (scenic biking routes, rural areas and small towns)
- Guided tours (food tastings, sightseeing, transportation packages)
