Barbecue recommendations for casual entertaining:

**Side Dishes (Easy Classics):**
- Coleslaw (creamy or vinegar-based)
- Baked beans (sweet and smoky)
- Grilled vegetables (peppers, zucchini, onions with oil and seasonings)
- Potato salad (with mayo and herbs)
- Corn on the cob (grilled or boiled with butter)
- Chips and dips (guacamole, salsa, queso)

**Desserts (Crowd-Pleasing):**
- Watermelon (refreshing, light)
- Brownies or cookies (easy to bake ahead)
- Fruit salad (colorful, healthy)
- S'mores station (interactive, always a hit)
- Ice cream sandwiches (DIY with multiple flavors)

**Beverages (Refreshing Options):**
- Lemonade, iced coffee, fruit-infused water
- Sparkling water with fruit slices
- Popsicles, agua fresca, virgin piña coladas
- Cucumber lime refresher, ginger ale spritzers
- Beer and wine (for adults)

**General Tips:** Prepare ahead, keep it simple, use DIY stations for interactive fun, don't stress about perfection.
