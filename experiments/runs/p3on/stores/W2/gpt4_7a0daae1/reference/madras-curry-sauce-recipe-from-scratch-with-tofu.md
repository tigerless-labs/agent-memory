**Madras Curry Sauce Recipe**\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 2 tbsp vegetable oil\n- 1 onion, diced\n- 2 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger\n- 1 tsp ground cumin\n- 1 tsp curry leaves (optional)\n- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder\n- 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder\n- 1/2 tsp garam masala powder\n- 1/2 tsp salt\n- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)\n- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes\n- 1 cup vegetable broth\n- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional, for creaminess)\n- Fresh cilantro for garnish\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Heat oil in large pan over medium heat. Add onions and cook until translucent (5 minutes).\n2. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, curry leaves, turmeric, Madras curry powder, garam masala, salt, and cayenne. Cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant.\n3. Add diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, and coconut milk. Stir well.\n4. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens and flavors meld.\n5. Taste and adjust seasoning.\n6. Add cooked tofu and simmer 5-7 minutes more.\n7. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve over rice, naan, or roti.\n\n**Variations:**\n- Add potatoes, carrots, or other vegetables\n- Substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream\n- Add lemon juice or vinegar for brightness\n- Experiment with different proteins (chicken, shrimp, paneer)
