When an ambiguous compensation clause leads to litigation in Indiana, courts consider:

**General contract interpretation principles:**
- Ambiguity construed against the drafter (typically the employer)
- Preference for reasonable interpretation favoring the employee
- Entire contract examined for context and intent

**Evidence and interpretation methods:**
- Evidence of intent from written/oral communications, negotiations, industry practices
- Parol (extrinsic) evidence allowed to clarify ambiguity if contract alone insufficient
- Course of performance: how parties acted under the contract historically
- Custom and usage: industry standards and practices

**Indiana-specific factors:**
- Indiana follows standard contract interpretation principles but has established case law
- Indiana wage payment statutes regulate timing and manner of wage payments to employees
- State-specific precedents and case law may influence interpretation of ambiguous provisions

These factors help determine outcomes when employees sue employers over compensation disputes involving unclear contract language.
