Recommended Madras curry powder blends:\n\n- **Penzeys Spices**: 12-spice blend, popular choice among Indian cuisine enthusiasts (includes turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom)\n- **The Spice House**: 15-spice blend with unique additions like fenugreek and fennel\n- **McCormick**: More accessible and affordable option, good balance of spices
