Recommended board games for multi-age family gatherings:

- **Ticket to Ride** (8+): Train-themed strategy, easy to learn, fun to play
- **Pictionary** (8+): Classic drawing game, great with groups
- **Settlers of Catan** (10+): Strategy with depth for older players
- **Clue** (8+): Detective mystery game
- **Uno** (6+): Simple card game, quick to play
- **Forbidden Island/Desert** (10+): Cooperative games where players work together
- **Apples to Apples** (10+): Party game for word matching
- **Scattergories** (10+): Creative word game with categories
- **Cranium** (10+): Multi-activity combining drawing, acting, trivia, wordplay
- **Guess Who** (6+): Classic guessing game

Most play with 2-6 players, easy to learn, adaptable difficulty.
