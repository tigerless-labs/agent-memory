## Eco-friendly accommodations in Barcelona city center

- **Hotel Omm**: Solar panels, energy-efficient lighting, recycling program, locally-sourced breakfast
- **Hotel Casa Fuster**: Biosphere Responsible Tourism certified, sustainable practices
- **Yök Casa + Cultura**: Solar panels, green roof, composting system, vegan breakfast
- **Hotel España Ramblas**: Energy-efficient lighting, waste reduction, sustainable breakfast
- **Hostal Orleans**: Budget hostel with green roof, recycling, locally-sourced breakfast

## Bike infrastructure

- **BiciLlanes**: 200+ km of designated bike lanes with physical barriers
- **Barcelona Bike Path** (Passeig de la Mar): 7 km seaside route
- **Diagonal Bike Lane**: 11 km across city
- **Ciclopeatonal**: Network connecting parks
- **Superblocks**: Traffic-limited neighborhoods
- **Bicing**: Bike-sharing system

## Bike tours & companies

- **Barcelona Bike Tours**, **Fat Tire Tours**: 3-hour guided tours
- **Bicitours Barcelona**, **Donkey Republic**: Rentals with baskets/racks
- Rental requests: comfortable seat, upright handlebars, basket/rack for water bottle

## Restaurants with bike parking

- **Can Culleretes**: Gothic Quarter cafe
- **El Xampanyet**: Pan con tomate
- **Bicioci**: Bike-themed cafe with repair station (Poblenou)
- **Can Solé**: Catalan cuisine (Barceloneta)

## Eco-tours

- **Barcelona Sail**: Solar-powered boats
- **Huerto Urbano**: Urban gardening workshops
- **Barcelona Eco Tours** / **Green Tours Barcelona**: Ecological walks
- **Montserrat Mountain & Wine Country**: Full-day tours
