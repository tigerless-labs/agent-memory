**Simple Naan Bread Recipe**\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour\n- 1/4 tsp salt\n- 1/4 tsp sugar\n- 1/2 tsp active dry yeast\n- 1/2 cup lukewarm water\n- 2 tbsp olive oil\n- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, for tenderness)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Combine flour, salt, sugar, and yeast in large bowl.\n2. Gradually add lukewarm water and mix until shaggy dough forms.\n3. Add olive oil and mix until dough comes together.\n4. Knead for 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.\n5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth, and let rise in warm place for 1-2 hours until doubled.\n6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat.\n7. Divide dough into 6-8 portions and shape into balls.\n8. Roll each into thin circle, 6-7 inches diameter.\n9. Cook in skillet for 1-2 minutes until puffed and edges curl.\n10. Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.\n11. Brush with melted butter or ghee if desired.\n12. Serve hot with curry.\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use stand mixer with dough hook to simplify kneading\n- Add flavorings like garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds\n- Use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crisper crust\n- Experiment with different flour types (whole wheat, higher protein)
