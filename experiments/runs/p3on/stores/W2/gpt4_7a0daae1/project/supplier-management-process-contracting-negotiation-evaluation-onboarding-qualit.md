Comprehensive systematic approach to supplier management established. Key areas: (1) Contracts - scope, specs, quality standards, pricing, payment terms, delivery, IP protection, warranty, dispute resolution. (2) Price Negotiation - research rates, build relationships, be transparent on budget, ask for discounts, use competition leverage, consider tiered/cost-plus pricing. (3) Evaluation - assess quality, reliability, pricing, customer service, financial stability, supply chain visibility, compliance; watch for poor communication, lack of transparency, late deliveries. (4) Onboarding - finalize contract, gather documentation, establish contacts, conduct meeting, provide training, set up QA and payment terms, monitor with KPIs. (5) Quality Control - define standards, identify critical points, create QC plan with inspections/testing, use Six Sigma, establish corrective actions, conduct audits. Quality management tools and ISO 9001/AS9100 standards considered.
