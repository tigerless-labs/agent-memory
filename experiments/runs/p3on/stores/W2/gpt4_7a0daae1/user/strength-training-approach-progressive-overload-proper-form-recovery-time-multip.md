Implements strength training with focus on:\n- Starting with lighter weights and progressing gradually\n- Prioritizing proper form and technique over heavy weight\n- Targeting multiple muscle groups (squats, deadlifts, lunges)\n- Allowing 48-72 hours recovery between sessions\n- Balancing with cardio (running, tennis) and flexibility exercises (stretching, yoga)\n- Increasing weight by 2.5-5lbs every two weeks\n\nGoal: Improve performance in tennis and running while maintaining overall fitness and reducing injury risk.
