## Research Question
How are social media usage patterns associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults?

## Research Areas of Interest
1. Mediating roles: exploring self-esteem and social support as potential mediators in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes
2. Interventions: examining the effectiveness of social media-based interventions and policies aimed at promoting mental health in young adults
3. Awareness and support: investigating how social media platforms can be leveraged for mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, and peer-to-peer support initiatives
