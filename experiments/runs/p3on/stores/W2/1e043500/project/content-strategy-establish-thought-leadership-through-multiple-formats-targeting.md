Content strategy to build brand around hiring philosophy:

**Content formats:**
- Blog posts: explore importance of hiring great humans, identify key traits, team collaboration
- Podcasts/videos: interviews with industry experts and successful team leaders
- Webinars/workshops: practical tips on building teams and hiring effectively
- E-books/guides: in-depth exploration of psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation, building collaborative culture
- Social media: share content, connect with audience, highlight case studies

**Approach:**
- Build brand as thought leader in effective team building
- Create valuable content providing practical insights and advice
- Engage with audience through social media and other channels
- Collaborate with other industry experts
- Track analytics and adjust strategy based on performance

**Target audience:** Team leads, hiring managers, engineering directors, entrepreneurs building teams
