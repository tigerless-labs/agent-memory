## Influential Studies to Start With

1. Burke, M., Marraccini, M. E., & McGannon, K. R. (2010). The relationship between social network use and depression in emerging adults. Computers in Human Behavior, 26(6), 1465-1473.

2. Király, O., Potenza, M. N., Stein, D. J., King, D. L., Hodgins, S. C., Saunders, J. B., ... & Demetrovics, Z. (2019). Mental health and addictive behaviors in young people: A systematic review of clinical and neurobiological findings. Journal of Behavioral Addictions, 8(3), 537-553.

3. Valkenburg, P. M., Peter, J., & Schouten, A. P. (2006). Friend networking sites and their relationship to adolescents' well-being and social self-esteem. CyberPsychology & Behavior, 9(5), 584-590.

4. Kim, J., & Lee, Y. (2015). The impact of social media on self-esteem: A systematic review. Computers in Human Behavior, 48, 118-125.

5. Lee, S. J., & Kim, B. (2017). The effects of social media on mental health: A systematic review. Journal of Behavior and Information Technology, 36(10), 1056-1066.

6. Hilty, D. M., Ferrer, D. C., Parish, M. B., Johnston, B., Callahan, E. J., & Yellowlees, P. M. (2017). The effectiveness of telemental health: A 2013 review. Telemedicine and e-Health, 23(2), 130-144.

7. Naslund, J. A., Aschbrenner, K. A., Marsch, L. A., & Bartels, S. J. (2017). The future of mental health care: Peer-to-peer support and social media. Epidemiology and Psychiatric Sciences, 26(2), 140-144.

8. Horgan, A., & Sweeney, J. (2017). Young people's online social networks and mental health: A systematic review. Journal of Adolescent Health, 61(3), 349-355.

9. Berry, N., & Lobban, F. (2017). Social media and mental health: A review of the literature. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing, 26(2), 141-153.

## Search Strategy Tips
- Use Boolean operators: AND, OR, NOT for combining keywords
- Use wildcards (*) for word variations
- Use exact phrases in quotes for targeted searches
- Apply date filters (e.g., 2015-2022) for recent studies
- Use citation tracking features (Google Scholar "Cited by", Scopus "Citation Tracker")
- Check reference lists of relevant articles for additional sources
- Look for meta-analyses and systematic reviews for comprehensive overviews
