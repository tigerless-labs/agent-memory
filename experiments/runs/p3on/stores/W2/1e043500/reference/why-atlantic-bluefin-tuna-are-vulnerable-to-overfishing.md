## Biological Factors
- **Slow growth rate and reproductive maturity**: Takes long time to reach reproductive age, slowing population recovery after decline

## Market/Economic Factors
- **High commercial value**: Highly prized in sushi market, creating intense fishing pressure

## Geographic/Migration Factors
- **Long-distance migrations**: Cross multiple jurisdictions with different regulations, leading to inconsistent protection
- **Wide distribution**: Found across Mediterranean and Atlantic Ocean

## Fishing Method Factors
- **Non-selective fishing methods**: Longlines, purse seines, and gill nets cause high bycatch of other species and juvenile Bluefin
- **Unregulated and illegal fishing**: Particularly prevalent in Mediterranean Sea, contributing significantly to overfishing
