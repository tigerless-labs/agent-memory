## Biological Challenges
- **Complex life cycle in captivity**: Reproductive and early life stages difficult to replicate; breeding in captivity is challenging and requires expensive facilities
- **Genetic diversity concerns**: Captive breeding could weaken species resilience to environmental changes

## Economic Challenges
- **High production costs**: Requires large expensive facilities, high-quality feeds, and ongoing maintenance—far more expensive than wild-caught

## Environmental Challenges
- **Feed sourcing**: High-quality feed often made from wild-caught fish, leading to overfishing of other species
- **Waste discharge**: Farmed fish operations produce significant waste and nutrient discharge affecting local environments

## Market Challenges
- **Consumer preference**: Many consumers prefer wild-caught Bluefin tuna over farmed, making market adoption difficult
