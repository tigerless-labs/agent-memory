Day 1 Morning: Alfama neighborhood, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, tram ride through narrow streets. Day 1 Afternoon: Baixa district (Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square), Chiado, Café A Brasileira for coffee and pastel de nata. Day 2 Morning: Day trip to Sintra with Pena Palace, Moorish Castle, beautiful gardens. Day 2 Afternoon: Belém neighborhood with Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém pastries. Day 3 Morning: Tagus River walk, MAAT Museum. Day 3 Afternoon: Bairro Alto neighborhood, Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara views, traditional restaurants. Other attractions: National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum, LX Factory.
