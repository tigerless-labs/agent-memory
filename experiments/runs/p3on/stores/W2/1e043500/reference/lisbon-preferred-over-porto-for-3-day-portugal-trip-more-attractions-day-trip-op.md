## Decision Rationale

When choosing between Lisbon and Porto for a 3-day Portugal trip, Lisbon is recommended because:

- **Wider range of attractions**: historical sites (Belem Tower, Jerónimos Monastery, National Museum of Ancient Art), cultural venues, museums
- **Vibrant nightlife**: many bars, restaurants, clubs in Bairro Alto neighborhood  
- **Day-trip destinations nearby**: Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica beaches
- **Porto consideration**: Beautiful but smaller, can be explored in less time

For a short 3-day window, Lisbon better maximizes activities and experiences.
