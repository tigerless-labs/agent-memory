## Life Cycle
- Spawn in Gulf of Mexico April-June
- Eggs hatch into transparent larvae, carried by Gulf Stream to eastern Atlantic
- Juvenile stage: feed on plankton and small fish in Mediterranean and Atlantic
- Adults: long-distance migrations across Atlantic from west to east for breeding and feeding
- Lifespan: 25-30 years typically, up to 40 years maximum
- Size: grow to 10+ feet long, over 1,000 pounds

## Conservation Status
- Listed as endangered species
- Have been significantly overfished
- Critical habitats: spawning grounds (Gulf of Mexico), migration routes, feeding areas
