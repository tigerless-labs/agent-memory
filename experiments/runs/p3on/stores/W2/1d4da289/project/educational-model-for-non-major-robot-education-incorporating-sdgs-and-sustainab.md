## Model Overview

Developed an educational model using robot programming (Convergence D-SteamRobot/DSR) to enhance engineering education for non-majors while incorporating Global Citizenship Education (GCED) and addressing climate/ecological change needs.

## Key Components

- **Vehicle**: Robot programming blends humanities and engineering
- **Empathy Building**: Books presented to increase understanding of real-world problems related to SDGs
- **Learning Method**: Simple mechanical mechanisms integrated into curriculum, prototyping to solve self-defined problems, students select SDGs topics to address

## Learning Outcomes

Computational thinking skills, problem-solving and critical thinking, deeper understanding of technology/engineering's role in sustainability, divergent thinking.

## Pedagogical Framework

Constructivist learning environment emphasizing empathy/exploration, immersion/peer-learning, hands-on learning (ideating → prototyping → sharing/improving), with sustainability and SDGs integrated throughout.

## Conclusion

Non-majors develop computational thinking and become confident problem-solvers and critical thinkers. Students learn how robots contribute to creating a more sustainable future while understanding real-world problems.
