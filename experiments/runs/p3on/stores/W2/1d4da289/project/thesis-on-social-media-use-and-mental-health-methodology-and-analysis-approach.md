Topic: How social media use affects mental health outcomes in young adults

**Completed:** Literature review on 2023-02-10

**Current Stage:** Methodology chapter in progress

**Data Collection Methods:**
- Online surveys for quantitative data
- Semi-structured interviews via video conferencing for qualitative data
- Target population: Young adults aged 18-25
- Planned sample size: 500 participants
- Sampling strategy: Online convenience sampling through social media platforms

**Qualitative Analysis:** Thematic analysis selected
- Expected themes: Social media habits, mental health experiences, social comparison/envy, social support and connection, self-presentation, FOMO, cyberbullying, self-regulation, coping mechanisms
