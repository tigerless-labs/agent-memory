**Proxima Centauri Facts:**
- Closest star to our Solar System
- Distance: ~4.24 light-years
- NASA's Parker Solar Probe (fastest spacecraft) would take 6,000+ years to reach it

**Current Technology Limitations:**
- No current spacecraft capable of reaching Proxima Centauri within a human lifetime
- Would require breakthrough propulsion systems to travel at speeds close to light

**Theoretical Future Propulsion Concepts:**
- Fusion-based propulsion
- Antimatter engines
- Space-time warping
- Wormholes

These concepts remain theoretical and require significant development before viable for human space travel.
