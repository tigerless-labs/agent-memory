**Calculation:** VAT is calculated as item total × rate (decimal). Example: PHP 1,000 × 0.12 = PHP 120 VAT; total = PHP 1,120. **VAT-registered businesses** (annual sales over PHP 3M): must charge and include VAT in invoices; must file VAT returns to Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR). **Non-VAT-registered businesses** (annual sales below PHP 3M): not required to charge or include VAT. Note: Information from May 2023; tax laws may have changed.
