Down Dog, Yoga Studio, and Pocket Yoga offer comprehensive classes, tracking, and goal-setting. Fitbit Coach integrates with Fitbit devices for yoga workouts. Strava tracks yoga practice and offers challenges. Mindbody connects with local studios. Online: Yoga International, DoYouYoga, and Yoga Journal all offer classes, tutorials, and practice logging.
