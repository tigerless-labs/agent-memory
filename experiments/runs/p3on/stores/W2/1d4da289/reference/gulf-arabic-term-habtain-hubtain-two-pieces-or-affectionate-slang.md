**حبتين** (habtain/hubtain) in Gulf countries can mean:

1. **Two pieces** - quantity indicator, often used for items like two tablets of medicine or two candies
2. **Affectionate slang** - informal term of endearment between close friends or family (like 'dear', 'buddy', 'sweetheart')

Context determines the exact meaning.
