**Traditional Tamara Dishes:**
- Harees — porridge-like dish made of wheat and meat
- Machboos — spiced rice dish with vegetables and meat or seafood
- Luqaimat — sweet dessert of dough balls drizzled with honey
- Shawarma — wrap of pita bread filled with grilled chicken, lamb, or beef with vegetables and sauces
- Balaleet — sweet vermicelli pudding with cardamom and saffron

**Vegetarian Options in Tamara:**
- Aloo Gobi — spiced cauliflower and potato curry
- Biryani — vegetarian rice dish with vegetables and spices
- Daal Makhani — creamy lentil curry with kidney beans and black lentils
- Chana Masala — spicy chickpea curry
- Stuffed Eggplant — eggplant filled with spiced rice and nuts, baked in tomato-based sauce
