**Film Details:**
- Released: November 12, 2021 (Netflix)
- Director: Lin-Manuel Miranda (directorial debut)
- Based on: Jonathan Larson's semi-autobiographical rock monologue
- Plot: Follows Jonathan Larson approaching his 30th birthday, struggling to create something meaningful while working as a waiter in NYC. Explores themes of creativity, ambition, and pressure to succeed.

**Cast:**
- Andrew Garfield as Jonathan Larson
- Alexandra Shipp as Susan (Jonathan's girlfriend)
- Robin de Jesús as Michael (Jonathan's best friend) - Tony nominee
- Vanessa Hudgens as Karessa (performer in Jonathan's workshop)
- Joshua Henry as Roger (fellow artist/rival) - Tony nominee
- MJ Rodriguez as Carolyn (actress/friend)
- Ben Ross as Freddy (fellow artist/friend)
- Judith Light as Clara (Jonathan's agent) - two-time Tony winner

**Music:**
- Based on Jonathan Larson's rock score from the original musical
- Features songs like '30/90', 'Boho Days', 'Louder Than Words'
- Combines themes of creativity and legacy similar to Hamilton
