# Spinach and Tomato Omelette

Ingredients:
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup diced onion
- 1/4 cup diced tomato
- 1/2 cup chopped spinach
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions:
1. Whisk the eggs and season with salt and pepper
2. Heat olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat
3. Add diced onion and sauté for 2 minutes until softened
4. Add chopped spinach and sauté for another 2 minutes until wilted
5. Pour whisked eggs over the onion and spinach mixture, cook for 1-2 minutes until edges set
6. Add diced tomato on top and continue cooking until eggs are set
7. Fold the omelette in half and serve hot
