Platforms offering classes in traditional textile production techniques:

- **Slow Fiber Studios** — Classes on weaving, natural dyeing, Japanese shibori; all skill levels
- **Maiwa School of Textiles** — Vancouver-based; online classes in natural dyeing, block printing, textile design; taught by artisans worldwide
- **Fibershed** — Nonprofit focused on sustainable/regenerative textile practices; classes in spinning, weaving, natural dyeing; all skill levels
- **Udemy** — Wide range from traditional weaving to digital textile design
- **Skillshare** — Classes in natural dyeing, embroidery, quilt making; all skill levels
