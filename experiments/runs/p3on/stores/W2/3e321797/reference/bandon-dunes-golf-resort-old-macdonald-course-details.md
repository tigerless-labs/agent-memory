**Old Macdonald Course at Bandon Dunes**\n\n- Designed by Tom Doak and Jim Urbina, opened 2010\n- Tribute to Charles Blair Macdonald (the 'Father of American Golf')\n- Retro-style links design inspired by Scottish courses\n- Features template holes inspired by famous Scottish links (e.g., 3rd hole inspired by 11th at St. Andrews, 14th inspired by 17th at North Berwick)\n- Key features: blind shots, deep revetted bunkers, undulating fast greens\n- Five sets of tees: 4,275 to 6,944 yards\n- Playable for all skill levels with multiple tee options\n- Built on sandy, gently sloping site with natural drainage\n\n**Bandon Dunes Resort Amenities:**\n- Range of accommodations from rooms to cottages and suites\n- Multiple dining options including McKee's Pub and Gallery Restaurant\n- Practice facility with driving range, putting green, short-game area\n- Junior golf programs and lessons for kids\n- Private and group lessons available\n- Guided course tours\n- Non-golfing activities: hiking, beachcomming, exploring town of Bandon\n- Resort shuttle service\n- Book tee times and accommodations well in advance, especially June-September peak season\n- Prepare for unpredictable coastal weather
