Organizations working to preserve and promote traditional textiles globally:

- **UNESCO** — Recognizes traditional textile production techniques as Intangible Cultural Heritage
- **Ethical Fashion Initiative** — Works with artisans in Africa, Asia, Latin America; connects to global market; partners with designers, retailers, NGOs
- **Mayan Women's Weaving Cooperative** — Guatemala-based; preserves indigenous backstrap weaving techniques
- Various fashion brands and designers working directly with artisans and cooperatives to create ethical fashion pieces
