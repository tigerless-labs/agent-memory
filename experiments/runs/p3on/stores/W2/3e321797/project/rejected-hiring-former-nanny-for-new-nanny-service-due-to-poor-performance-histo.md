A former nanny who previously worked for the user's own children applied to join the user's newly launched nanny service. The user decided not to hire her for the following reasons:

- The user's own kids did not like working with this nanny
- Multiple other past clients reported they would not hire this nanny again

The user sought help drafting a polite, professional rejection letter. Two versions were provided:
1. A formal version emphasizing business focus and team-building goals
2. A less formal version with a warmer tone while still being professional

The rejection emphasized the company's need to explore options that better align with business objectives, without directly citing performance concerns.
