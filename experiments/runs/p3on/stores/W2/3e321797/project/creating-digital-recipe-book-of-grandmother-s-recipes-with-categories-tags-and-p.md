Planning to create a digital recipe book of grandmother's recipes with the following organization:\n\n**Categorization:**\n- By course: Appetizers, Soups, Main Dishes, Sides, Desserts, Beverages\n\n**Tags:**\n- Ingredients\n- Cooking time (Quick and Easy under 30 min, Weeknight, Special Occasion)\n- Difficulty level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)\n- Servings\n\n**Content:**\n- Recipe title following naming convention (e.g., 'Grandma's Chicken Soup')\n- Serving sizes and yields\n- Cooking methods and techniques\n- Photos and videos when possible\n- Dedicated section for notes and stories including:\n  - Grandmother's cooking philosophy\n  - Favorite ingredients and why they mattered to her\n  - Memories of cooking with her\n  - Family traditions and role of food in celebrations\n  - Personal anecdotes about her\n  - Vintage photos from the kitchen\n\n**Goal:** Create a treasured family heirloom that preserves grandmother's culinary legacy and traditions for future generations.
