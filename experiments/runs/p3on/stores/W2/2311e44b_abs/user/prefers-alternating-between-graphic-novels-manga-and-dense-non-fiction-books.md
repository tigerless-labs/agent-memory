Alternating between lighter graphic novels/manga and denser non-fiction maintains reading momentum and keeps books interesting. Graphic novels and manga are quicker to get through, helping boost page count and providing mental breaks from dense material. Supports reading a mix of formats like 'The Nightingale' and 'Sapiens'.
