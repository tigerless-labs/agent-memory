Recommendations for adding visual interest to plant shelf:

**Plant Stands:**
- Mid-century modern: sleek, minimalist wooden or metal base
- Tiered stand: multiple levels to showcase several plants with depth
- Macramé stand: bohemian-inspired, adds cozy organic feel

**Trellises:**
- Wooden trellis: natural finish, rustic touch, simple minimalist design preferred
- Metal trellis: sleek modern design, industrial chic aesthetic
- Obelisk trellis: sculptural element, provides support for climbing plants

**Selection criteria:** Material (wood/metal/macramé), proportional size, style matching existing decor, sturdy/stable construction to prevent toppling.
