Recommended science-backed self-help and personal development books for evidence-based practical advice (May 2023):

1. **Atomic Habits** by James Clear — Building good habits and breaking bad ones, backed by psychology and neuroscience research

2. **Deep Work** by Cal Newport — Strategies for improving productivity and focus in a distracting world; backed by psychology and neuroscience

3. **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen Covey — Personal and professional development based on decades of research

4. **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck — About growth mindset and motivation, backed by Dweck's research

5. **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle — Mindfulness and meditation for living in the present and reducing stress

6. **Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience** by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi — About flow states in optimal experience, backed by decades of positive psychology research

7. **The Happiness Advantage** by Shawn Achor — Explores connection between happiness and success, backed by positive psychology research

8. **Mini Habits: Smaller Habits, Bigger Results** by Stephen Guise — Unique approach to habit formation based on research and experiments

9. **The Productivity Project** by Chris Bailey — Comprehensive guide to productivity backed by Bailey's research and experiments

10. **The Willpower Instinct** by Kelly McGonigal — Explores science of willpower with practical strategies for self-control and goal achievement

**Particular interest:** Atomic Habits and Deep Work for improving reading speed and retention.
