Recommended accessible, well-researched non-fiction books as alternatives to dense works like 'Sapiens' (May 2023):

1. **The Devil in the White City** by Erik Larson — Historical true crime about the 1893 World's Fair in Chicago and serial killer H.H. Holmes, reads like a thriller

2. **Bad Blood: Secrets and Lies in a Silicon Valley Startup** by John Carreyrou — About Theranos, a biotech startup's rise and fall

3. **The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks** by Rebecca Skloot — Compelling story about the intersection of medicine, ethics, and humanity

4. **Just Mercy: A Story of Justice and Redemption** by Bryan Stevenson — Powerful exploration of the US justice system, racial inequality, and criminal defense work

5. **The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business** by Charles Duhigg — Exploration of habits, how they're formed, and how to change them; mix of science, psychology, and real-life examples

All recommended as engaging, relatively easy reads without overwhelming academic density.
