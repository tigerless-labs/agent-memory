Mpix is an online professional-grade printing service known for high-quality prints with fast turnaround. Key paper options include Lustre (subtle sheen, warm tone), Metallic (high-gloss, cool tone, ideal for vibrant colors), Matte (non-reflective, good for portraits/landscapes), Canvas (1.5 or 2.5 inch wrap), Metal Prints (aluminum/steel glossy or matte), and Wood Prints (sustainably sourced, natural or white finish). Offers borderless prints and custom sizes. Shipping: Standard 4.95 USD (3-5 days production plus 3-5 days shipping), Priority 7.95 USD (2-3 days production plus 2-3 days shipping), Express 14.95 USD (1 day production plus 1 day shipping). Usually offers 20-30 percent discount for first-time customers.
