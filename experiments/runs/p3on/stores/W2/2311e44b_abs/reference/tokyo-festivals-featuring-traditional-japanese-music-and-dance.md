Recommended festivals in Tokyo with traditional music and dance:

- **Tokyo National Museum Summer Festival** (August) - traditional Japanese music and dance performances, workshops and exhibitions
- **Kanda Matsuri** (mid-May, biennial) - floats and costumed participants including traditional musicians and dancers  
- **Shunju Matsuri** (late September, annual) - Japanese cultural celebration with traditional music and dance, food, handicrafts
- **Setagaya Hachimangu Shrine Festival** (late September, annual) - taiko drumming and kagura (traditional Shinto performance)
- **Sanno Matsuri** (June, biennial) - one of Tokyo's three major festivals with ornate floats and costumed participants

Also of note (mixed traditional/modern): **Asakusa Samba Carnival** (late August) includes both samba and traditional Japanese performances.
