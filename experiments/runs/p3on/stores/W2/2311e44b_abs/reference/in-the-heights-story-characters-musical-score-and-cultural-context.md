Lin-Manuel Miranda's first Broadway musical, set in Washington Heights, Upper Manhattan. Protagonist: Usnavi, Dominican-American bodega owner. Explores gentrification, identity, belonging among Latinx community over three days.

Main characters: Abuela Claudia (matriarch), Nina Rosario (Stanford student), Benny (taxi worker), Vanessa (beauty salon worker, Usnavi's love interest), Sonny (cousin, activist), Kevin and Camila Rosario (taxi company owners).

Score: Fusion of salsa, merengue, bachata, Latin jazz, hip-hop, R&B, show tunes. Bilingual (English/Spanish). Each character has distinct musical voice reflecting personality.

Standout songs: In the Heights (opening), 96,000 (lottery/dreams), Breathe (Nina), Paciencia y Fe (Abuela), Alabanza (community), Carnaval del Barrio (celebration).

Cultural significance: Miranda grew up in Washington Heights; show celebrates Latinx culture, strong community/family ties, immigrant experience. Addresses gentrification and neighborhood displacement. Emphasizes authenticity through collaboration with Latino consultants and artists.
