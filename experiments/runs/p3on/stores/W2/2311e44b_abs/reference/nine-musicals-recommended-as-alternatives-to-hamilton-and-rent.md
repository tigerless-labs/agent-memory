Spring Awakening, Passing Strange, Next to Normal, Dear Evan Hansen, The Last Five Years, American Idiot, Fun Home, The Wild Party, Bat Out of Hell — all share themes of identity, community, emotional intensity, and innovative storytelling similar to Hamilton and Rent.
