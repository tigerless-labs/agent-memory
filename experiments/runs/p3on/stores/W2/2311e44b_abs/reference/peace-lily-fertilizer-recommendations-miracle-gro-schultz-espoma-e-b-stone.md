Recommended gentle fertilizers for peace lilies (prefer high phosphorus and potassium):

1. **Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food**: N-P-K ratio 24-8-16, water-soluble, specifically formulated for indoor plants
2. **Schultz All Purpose Plant Food**: N-P-K ratio 20-20-20, balanced and gentle
3. **Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer**: N-P-K ratio 2-2-2, organic with slow-release formula
4. **E.B. Stone Organics Bloom!**: N-P-K ratio 5-3-4, designed to promote blooming

Tips: Always follow packaging instructions; start with diluted solution to avoid burning roots; fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) only; consider skipping fertilization in dormant season (fall/winter).
