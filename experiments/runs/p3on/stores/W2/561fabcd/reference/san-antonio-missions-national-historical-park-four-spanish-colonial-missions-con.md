Collection of four Spanish colonial missions along the San Antonio River, preserving largest collection of Spanish colonial architecture in the US. Connected by scenic Mission Trail.

**Missions:** Concepción (1716, stunning frescoes) • San José (1720, 'Queen of the Missions', ornate stone carvings) • San Juan (1716, tranquil atmosphere) • Espada (1731, southernmost, beautiful chapel)

**Visit:** 9am-5pm daily (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's), Free admission, Ranger-led tours available, Self-guided brochures available. Bring comfortable shoes, sunscreen, water.
