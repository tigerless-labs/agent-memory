**National Eisteddfod of Wales** — Annual festival celebrating Welsh language, literature, music, and folk traditions; held in different location each year; attracts thousands of visitors. 2021 dates: August 30–September 4 in Llantwit Major, Vale of Glamorgan, South Wales.

**Conwy Feast** — Annual food festival in Conwy (historic coastal town in North Wales); celebrates local food, drink, music, arts, and crafts.

**Royal Welsh Show** — Major annual agricultural show in Llanelwedd (near Builth Wells, mid-Wales); showcases Welsh farming, rural life, livestock competitions, agricultural exhibits, and traditional crafts.

**Llangollen International Musical Eisteddfod** — Annual music and dance festival in Llangollen (North Wales); attracts artists and performers from around the world; celebrates diverse musical and cultural traditions.

**Anglesey Agricultural Show** — Two-day annual agricultural show on the Isle of Anglesey; showcases Welsh livestock and farm produce; includes family-friendly entertainment and activities.
