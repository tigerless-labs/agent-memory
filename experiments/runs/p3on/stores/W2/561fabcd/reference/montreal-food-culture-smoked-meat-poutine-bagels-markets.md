Montreal specialties and recommendations:
- Smoked meat sandwich: cured beef brisket on rye bread
- Poutine: fries with gravy and cheese curds
- Montreal-style bagels: smaller, sweeter than NYC style, boiled in honey water, wood-fired oven
- Tourtière: meat pie with ground pork and potatoes (holiday dish)
- Maple syrup treats: taffy, sugar pie, glazed bacon
- Markets: Atwater Market and Jean-Talon Market
- Diverse ethnic cuisines: Greek, Lebanese, Vietnamese
