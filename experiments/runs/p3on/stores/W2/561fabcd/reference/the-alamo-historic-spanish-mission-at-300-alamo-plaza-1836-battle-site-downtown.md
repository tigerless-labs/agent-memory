Former Spanish mission and iconic Texas landmark at 300 Alamo Plaza. Historic 1836 battle where Texan rebels (Davy Crockett, Jim Bowie) defended against Mexican army; inspired continued fight for independence.

**Visit Details:** 9am-5:30pm daily (extended March-October), Free admission, Ranger-led tours available, includes Alamo Church, Long Barrack Museum, Alamo Gardens. 17-minute documentary film available. Photography allowed in gardens/outside, not in Alamo Church.

**Tips:** Visit early morning or late afternoon, wear comfortable shoes, bring sunscreen/water. Walkable from downtown hotels.
