Weekly market at Pearl Brewery with: fresh produce (organic/heirloom), artisanal baked goods, prepared foods (tacos, barbecue, sandwiches), handmade crafts (jewelry, pottery, textiles), specialty foods (cheeses, charcuterie, honey), flowers/plants, local brews and wines.

**Tips:** Arrive early for best selection, bring reusable bags, bring cash, pet-friendly, check website for special events and live music. Parking available in Pearl's parking garage.
