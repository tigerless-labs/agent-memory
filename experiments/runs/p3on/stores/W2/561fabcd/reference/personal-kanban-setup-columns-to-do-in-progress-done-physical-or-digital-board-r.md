## Personal Kanban Setup Steps

1. **Determine scope** — decide what tasks/projects to manage (work, personal, or combined)
2. **Choose board** — physical (whiteboard, corkboard) or digital (Trello, Asana)
3. **Define columns** — typical: "To-Do", "In Progress", "Done" (customize as needed)
4. **Create tasks** — write each on a card and place in "To-Do"
5. **Move tasks** — move to "In Progress" when working, "Done" when complete
6. **Review regularly** — adjust columns and task placement as priorities shift

**Key principle**: Keep it simple to avoid creating more work; goal is workflow visualization and management.
