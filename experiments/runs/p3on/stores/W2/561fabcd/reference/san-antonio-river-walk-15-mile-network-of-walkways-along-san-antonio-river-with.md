The River Walk (Paseo del Río) is open 24/7 with a 15-mile path stretching from Pearl District to Missions Trail. Features diverse dining (La Gloria, Boudro's, The Esquire Tavern), shopping, and guided boat tours. The Pearl District sits at the north end with upscale shops, restaurants, and a Farmers Market (Saturdays 9am-1pm). Major events: Ford Holiday River Parade (late November), Fiesta San Antonio (April), Jazz & Blues Festival (September). Free shuttle service available.
