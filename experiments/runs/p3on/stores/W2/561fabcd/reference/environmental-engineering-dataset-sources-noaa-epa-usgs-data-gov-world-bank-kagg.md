Key sources for finding environmental engineering datasets:

1. **NOAA** — National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration: climate, weather, oceanography datasets
2. **EPA** — Environmental Protection Agency: air quality, water quality, hazardous waste, environmental topics
3. **USGS** — United States Geological Survey: water resources, geology, natural hazards
4. **Data.gov** — Federal government portal with broad dataset access, including environmental data
5. **World Bank** — Environmental and development datasets: water, sanitation, air pollution, climate change
6. **Kaggle** — Public dataset hosting including environmental engineering and science datasets
