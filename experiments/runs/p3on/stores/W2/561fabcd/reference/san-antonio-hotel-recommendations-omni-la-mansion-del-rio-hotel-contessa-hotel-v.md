### Recommended San Antonio Hotels

**Omni La Mansión del Rio** — 4-star, River Walk location. Spanish colonial-style architecture, outdoor pool, fitness center, Tex-Mex restaurant and poolside bar.

**Hotel Contessa** — 4-star, River Walk. Rooftop pool, fitness center, contemporary American cuisine.

**Hotel Valencia Riverwalk** — 4-star, downtown near River Walk. Rooftop pool, fitness center, gourmet restaurant.

**Hotel Emma** — 4-star boutique, restored 19th-century brewhouse. Courtyard, outdoor pool, farm-to-table restaurant.
