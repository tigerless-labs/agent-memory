## Overview
Fissionator is a slow, lumbering zombie threat that causes stress through passive radiation emission and zombie transformation on death rather than direct lethality.

## Visual Design
- Host body: bloated, disfigured with tumors/blisters, sickly green tint
- Radiation suit: bulky, outdated design melted/fused into host flesh (traps parasite inside)
- Green-ooze parasite: visible writhing under skin, amorphous and shifting
- Large gash in neck: parasite probes violently out, seeking new host
- Eyes: milky/glazed or glowing eerie green
- Movement: hunched, lurching gait; jerky, spasmodic movements (appears in pain)
- Vocalization: low guttural growl or silent except heavy breathing
- Environment: leaves trail of green glowing slime; parasite interacts violently with radiation

## Gameplay Mechanics
**AoE Radiation Damage:**
- Proximity-based: closer to Fissionator = more damage over time
- Vision distortion as warning to not approach too closely (not just visual hindrance)

**Attacks:**
- Radiation poisoning effect on player
- Parasite worms into player's mouth, slowing movement while trying to break free

**Counterplay:**
- Cosmic Cleanse ability negates AoE damage but only partially negates vision effect

**Death Trigger:**
- Radioactive burst transforms nearby zombies into radioactive variants
- Transformed zombies: light green glow, increased damage, spread radiation effect on attacks
- Visually: slime-parasite reacts violently to sudden radiation exposure

## Lore
- Host infected with parasite while working in radiation-contaminated environment
- Was wearing protective radiation suit
- Suit melted into flesh due to uncontrolled radiation exposure
- Fused suit traps parasite inside, preventing escape to new host
- Parasite's violent probing through neck gash is desperation to find new host/escape radiation prison
