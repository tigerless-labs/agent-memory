Takes a photo of luggage and its contents before zipping it up. Helps remember what's packed and where items are located in the suitcase. Also plans to photograph the contents of a 'just in case' emergency bag to quickly identify what's inside without digging through it. Finds this helpful for saving time, reducing stress, and avoiding overpacking.
