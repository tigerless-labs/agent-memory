User requested a comprehensive list of 50 girls/young women who played important roles in history before turning 18, with emphasis on global representation across cultures and time periods (not just European/American-centric).

Partial list compiled (conversation cut off at ~33 entries):

1. Hatshepsut (1508–1458 BCE) — Egyptian pharaoh
2. Cleopatra VII (69–30 BCE) — Last pharaoh of ancient Egypt
3. Artemisia I of Caria (5th century BCE) — Queen, fighter in Greco-Persian Wars
4. Joan of Arc (1412–1431 CE) — French military leader, age 19 when executed
5. Anne Frank (1929–1945 CE) — Holocaust diarist
6. Claudette Colvin (1939–present) — Civil rights activist, age 15 bus protest
7. Malala Yousafzai (1997–present) — Education activist, shot by Taliban at 15
8. Maria Gaetana Agnesi (1718–1799 CE) — Italian mathematician, calculus textbook at 19
9. Joan Miró (1893–1983 CE) — Spanish painter, first solo exhibition at 19
10. Anne Baxter (1923–1985 CE) — American actress, Academy Award at 23
11. Sarah Chang (1980–present) — American violinist, NYC Philharmonic debut at 8
12. Darya Saltykova (1730–1801 CE) — Russian noblewoman (crimes began at 25, note: older)
13. Clelia Duel Mosher (1863–1940 CE) — American physician, sexuality research at 17
14. Asmaa Mahfouz (1985–present) — Egyptian activist (2011 revolution at 26, note: older)
15. Tegla Loroupe (1973–present) — Kenyan runner, world records before 18
16. Sappho (7th century BCE) — Ancient Greek poet
17. Hildegard of Bingen (1098–1179 CE) — German abbess, mystic, composer, founder
18. Anne Bonny (1700–1782 CE) — Irish pirate, piracy career at 16
19. Mary Shelley (1797–1851 CE) — Frankenstein written at 18
20. Sacagawea (1788–1812 CE) — Shoshone guide for Lewis & Clark Expedition
21. Sybil Ludington (1761–1839 CE) — American Revolutionary War heroine, age 16
22. Nancy Wake (1912–2011 CE) — NZ-born Australian spy, 'White Mouse', WWII
23. Nzinga of Ndongo and Matamba (1583–1663 CE) — Angolan queen, resistance to Portuguese
24. Mira Bai (1498–1547 CE) — Indian princess, devotional poet
25. Pocahontas (1596–1617 CE) — Native American, early English colonization
26. Mary Anning (1799–1847 CE) — English fossil hunter, discoveries at 12
27. Margaret Bourke-White (1904–1971 CE) — American photographer, first female war correspondent
28. Anne Sullivan (1866–1936 CE) — American teacher, Helen Keller's mentor (started at 20, note: older)
29. Maud Stevens Wagner (1877–1961 CE) — American first known female tattoo artist, age 18
30. Frieda Belinfante (1904–1995 CE) — Dutch cellist, WWII resistance
31. Wilma Rudolph (1940–1994 CE) — American track athlete, 3 Olympic golds at 20 (note: older)
32. Mariya Oktyabrskaya (1905–1944 CE) — Soviet tank driver, Hero of Soviet Union
33. Boudica (died 60/61 CE) — Iceni queen, led uprising against Rome
34. Ann Lee (1736–1784 CE) — English-American religious leader, founded Shakers

Note: Some entries included were 18+ despite the <18 request; conversation emphasized need for diverse global representation after assistant initially presented Eurocentric list.
