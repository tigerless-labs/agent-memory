Omega offers authentication, Certification of Origin (with production date, model, serial number), condition reports, and service/restoration using original parts. Certificate of Authenticity includes unique certificate number, watch details, serial number, production date, and authorized representative signature. Benefits: increased resale value, enhanced credibility with buyers, guaranteed authenticity. Process requires contacting authorized Omega dealer or Customer Service. Cost varies by model, age, and condition. Keep all documentation including certification and service records in safe location.
