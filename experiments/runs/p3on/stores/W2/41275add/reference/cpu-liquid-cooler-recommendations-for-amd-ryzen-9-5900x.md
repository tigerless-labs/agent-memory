Recommended liquid coolers for Ryzen 9 5900X: NZXT Kraken X73 (360mm, popular, RGB), Corsair Hydro Series H115i RGB Platinum (280mm, excellent performance, customizable RGB), EKWB EK-AIO 360 D-RGB (high-performance, 360mm), Thermaltake Floe Riing 360 (high-performance, modern design). Air cooler alternative: Noctua NH-U14S TR4-SP3 (highly-regarded, more affordable). Considerations: radiator size and case clearance, fan noise levels, RGB customization, budget, warranty and manufacturer support.
