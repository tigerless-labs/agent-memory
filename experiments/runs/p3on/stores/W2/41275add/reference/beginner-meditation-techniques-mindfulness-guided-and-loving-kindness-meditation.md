Meditation types for beginners:

**Mindfulness Meditation** — Focus on the present moment without judgment. Sit quietly, close eyes or soften gaze, become aware of breath, thoughts, and emotions as they arise. Refocus attention on breath when distracted.

**Guided Meditation** — Listen to recorded guided meditation. Helpful for beginners who struggle with focus. Recommended apps: Headspace, Calm.

**Loving Kindness Meditation** — Cultivate feelings of love and compassion toward self and others. Sit comfortably, visualize someone you love or appreciate, silently repeat phrases: "may you be happy, may you be healthy, may you be safe, may you be at peace."

**Recommendations**
- Start with 5-10 minutes daily for beginners
- Gradually increase duration as comfortable  
- Consistency matters more than duration
- Even a few times weekly provides benefits
- Find the type that works best through experimentation
