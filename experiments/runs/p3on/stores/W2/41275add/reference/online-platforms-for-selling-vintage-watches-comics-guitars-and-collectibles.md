## Platforms by item type

**Vintage watches**: Reverb, eBay (with Authenticity Guarantee), Chrono24, 1stdibs, specialized watch forums (WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums), local watch dealers, auction houses (Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions)

**Comic books**: ComicConnect, eBay (dedicated comics category), Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, Reddit r/comicbooks, local comic book stores. Recommend certification from CGC (Certified Guaranty Company)

**Vintage action figures**: eBay, Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk, ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room, local toy stores. Consider certification from Wata Games or Professional Sports Authenticator (PSA)

**Guitars and musical instruments**: Reverb (primary platform), eBay (Vintage category), The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI, Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange, Facebook Marketplace

**Vintage electronics**: eBay, Gearslutz, Resonance FM, vintage audio forums, Facebook Marketplace, local online selling groups

**Currency**: Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG), International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
