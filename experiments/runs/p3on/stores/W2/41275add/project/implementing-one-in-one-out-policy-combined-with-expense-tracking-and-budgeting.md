Strategy to control online shopping spending and maintain mindful consumption:\n\n1. **One in, one out policy**: Whenever bringing a new item into life, must discard or donate an old item. Forces intentional purchasing decisions and prevents clutter buildup.\n\n2. **Expense tracking**: Tracking expenses more closely using a budgeting tool (app or spreadsheet) to monitor spending and maintain awareness.\n\n3. **Budgeting**: Setting a strict budget for spending and staying within means.\n\n4. **Additional strategies to consider**:\n   - Unsubscribe from promotional emails\n   - Delete shopping apps\n   - Avoid browsing online stores when bored or stressed\n   - Implement 30-day rule for non-essential purchases\n   - Categorize expenses and set limits per category\n\nGoal: Develop healthier relationship with shopping and reduce clutter.
