**Date:** June 2023 (planned conversation date: 2023-05-20)

**Activities:**
- Scavenger hunt (nature items for kids)
- Outdoor games (badminton, cornhole, tag, hide-and-seek)
- Impromptu jam session with siblings bringing instruments
- Shaker instruments (maracas, tambourines) for kids to play along
- Musical games and call-and-response activities

**Attendees:**
- Extended family
- Kids
- Cousin Emma with new baby

**Baby accommodations:**
- Designated quiet, shaded area for feeding and nap time
- Plan activities around baby's nap schedule
- Provide baby-friendly toys (soft blocks, rattles, teething toys)
- Assign adult baby buddies to help Emma

**What to bring:**
- Shaker instruments for kids
- Baby toys and entertainment
- Props for photo booth
- Chalk or art supplies

**Outfit:** Yellow shirts and light brown pants (chose light brown over white to avoid stains)
