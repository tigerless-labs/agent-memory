**Decision:** Use OBS Studio for recording gameplay footage for YouTube channel.

**Rationale:**
- Free and open-source
- Large community with extensive plugin ecosystem
- Highly customizable for creating unique channel branding
- Well-optimized for user's NVIDIA RTX 3080 and AMD Ryzen 9 5900X hardware
- Hardware acceleration support via NVIDIA GPU

**Alternative considered:** XSplit (subscription-based, but more polished UI; ultimately decided against it in favor of free option and community support)
