Plants that attract hummingbirds, particularly Ruby-throated Hummingbirds:

**Tubular/red/orange flowers:**
- Trumpet vine (Campsis radicans)
- Honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.)
- Fuchsia (Fuchsia spp.)
- Lantana (Lantana camara)
- Salvias (Salvia spp.)
- Bee balm (Monarda didyma)
- Cardinal flower (Lobelia cardinalis)
- Coral bells (Heuchera spp.)

**Seed-producing flowers:**
- Coneflower (Echinacea spp.)
- Black-eyed susan (Rudbeckia hirta)
- Zinnias (Zinnia spp.)

Hummingbirds prefer tubular, nectar-rich flowers in red, orange, and purple. Plant varieties with different bloom times to provide nectar throughout the growing season. Native plants are preferred.
