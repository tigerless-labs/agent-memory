Netflix specials recommended for studying storytelling in stand-up comedy: Hasan Minhaj Homecoming King (2017), Ali Wong Hard Knock Wife (2018), Bo Burnham Make Happy (2016), Hannah Gadsby Nanette (2018), Taylor Tomlinson Quarter-Life Crisis (2020), Mike Birbiglia My Girlfriend's Boyfriend (2013), Maria Bamford Lady Dynamite (2016), Demetri Martin Live At the Time (2018), Wanda Sykes Not Normal (2019), Brian Regan Nunchucks and Flamethrowers (2017). John Mulaney Kid Gorgeous cited as excellent example of narrative structure.
