Headspace free: Take10 (10-day intro), Everyday Headspace (3–5 min daily), SOS sessions (stress relief), Basics pack (Focus/Calm/Clarity/Motivation/Rest, 10–20 min each). Calm free: 7 Days of Calm (intro), Daily Calm (3–10 min), Sleep Stories (limited), Music/nature sounds (limited). Other options: Insight Timer (free library), Mindfulness Studio (various sessions), Buddhify (time-of-day meditations), 10% Happier (beginner-friendly), Simple Habit (5-min meditations).
