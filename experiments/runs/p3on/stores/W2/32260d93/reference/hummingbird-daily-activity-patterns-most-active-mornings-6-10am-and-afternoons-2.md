Ruby-throated Hummingbird daily activity patterns:

- **Morning peak (6-10am):** Most active, fueling up for the day and defending territory
- **Midday lull (11am-1pm):** Less active due to heat, rest and groom
- **Afternoon activity (2-5pm):** Active again as day cools, foraging for nectar
- **Evening (5-7pm):** Social behavior, gathering at feeders, courtship displays

Hummingbirds have high metabolism and consume up to twice their body weight in nectar and insects daily (equivalent to 100-200 lbs for humans). They visit flowers multiple times throughout the day.

Activity times vary by location, season, and food availability.
