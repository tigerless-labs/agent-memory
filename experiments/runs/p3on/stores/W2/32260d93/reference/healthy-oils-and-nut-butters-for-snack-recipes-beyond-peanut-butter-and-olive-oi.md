## Healthy Oils\n- **Avocado oil**: mild, buttery, high smoke point\n- **Grapeseed oil**: light, neutral, high smoke point\n- **Coconut oil**: rich in MCTs, great for baking and energy balls\n- **Sesame oil**: nutty, anti-inflammatory\n- **Walnut oil**: rich omega-3 content\n\n## Healthy Butters & Pastes\n- **Almond butter**: nutty, protein-rich\n- **Cashew butter**: smooth, creamy, high magnesium\n- **Coconut butter**: rich, MCTs\n- **Sunflower seed butter**: nut-free alternative\n- **Tahini**: nutty, protein-rich\n\n## Tips\n- Choose cold-pressed, expeller-pressed, or unrefined options\n- Store in cool, dark place\n- Use in moderation as part of balanced diet
