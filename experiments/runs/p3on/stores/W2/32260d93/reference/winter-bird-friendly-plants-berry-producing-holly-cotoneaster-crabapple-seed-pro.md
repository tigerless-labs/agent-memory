Bird-friendly plants beneficial during winter months:

**Berry-producing plants:**
- Holly (Ilex spp.) — eaten by robins, bluebirds, cedar waxwings
- Cotoneaster (Cotoneaster spp.) — robins, bluebirds, cedar waxwings
- Winterberry Holly (Ilex verticillata) — bright red berries, robins, cedar waxwings
- Crabapple (Malus spp.) — robins, bluebirds, woodpeckers

**Seed-producing plants:**
- Pine (Pinus spp.) — goldfinches, pine siskins, redpolls
- Coneflower (Echinacea spp.) — goldfinches, indigo buntings, downy woodpeckers
- Black-eyed Susan (Rudbeckia hirta) — goldfinches, buntings, juncos
- Sunflower (Helianthus annuus) — goldfinches, cardinals, blue jays

**Nectar-rich winter bloomers:**
- Winter-flowering shrubs: Camellia, Mahonia, Gaultheria (attract hummingbirds)
- Winter honeysuckle (Lonicera fragrantissima) — ruby-throated hummingbirds

Choose native plants suitable for local climate. Mix plant types for variety of food sources throughout winter.
