**Recipe Tips for Tarte aux Pommes:**

1. Use tart, firm apples like Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, or Braeburn
2. Keep butter and ingredients cold for a flaky crust; chill mixing bowl in fridge first
3. Roll out pastry gently on lightly floured surface; minimize handling to avoid developing gluten
4. Layer apple slices evenly and tightly; fanning in spiral pattern works well
5. Brush with egg wash (1 egg + 1 tbsp water) before baking for golden color and shine

**Drink Pairings:**
- Non-alcoholic: hot tea (Earl Grey, English Breakfast), cold milk, iced tea
- Alcoholic: Calvados (Normandy apple brandy), hard cider, apple cider mimosa
