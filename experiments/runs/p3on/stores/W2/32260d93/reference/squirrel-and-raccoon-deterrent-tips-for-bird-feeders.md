Methods to deter squirrels and raccoons from bird feeders:

**Squirrel-proofing:**
- Weight-activated perches that collapse or spin under squirrel weight
- Cage-like enclosures allowing birds but excluding larger animals
- Squirrel-resistant feeders (plastic/metal, hard to chew)
- Baffles above or below feeder
- Position feeder 10+ feet from structures squirrels can jump from

**Raccoon-proofing:**
- Sturdy pole (6+ feet tall, anchored in ground)
- Raccoon-resistant feeders with secure lids and weight-activated perches
- Raccoon-specific baffles around pole
- Move feeder to difficult-to-access areas (courtyards, away from water)
- Take down feeder at night (raccoons nocturnal)

**General tips:**
- Keep area clean of spilled seed/debris
- Use safflower seeds or hot pepper-infused seeds (less appealing to rodents)
- Hang shiny reflective objects (aluminum foil, CDs) near feeder
- Rotate feeder type/location periodically
