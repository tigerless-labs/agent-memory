Key books for comedy writing and performance: Comedy Writing Secrets by Mark Shatz, The Comedy Bible by Judy Carter, And Here's the Kicker edited by Mike Sacks, The Hidden Tools of Comedy by Steve Kaplan, Story by Robert McKee, Zen and the Art of Stand-Up Comedy by Jay Sankey, I'm Dying Up Here by William Knoedelseder. Also recommended: Building a Brand as a Comedian by Steve Hofstetter, Comedy Goes Digital by Chris Mancini, The Comedian's Way by Barry Friedman, Crush It! by Gary Vaynerchuk, Influencer by Brittany Hennessy.
