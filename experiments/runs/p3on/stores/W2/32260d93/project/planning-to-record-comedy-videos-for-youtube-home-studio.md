Planning to set up home recording studio for recording stand-up comedy videos for YouTube. Key setup considerations: lighting (natural light, softbox lights), audio (USB microphone like Blue Yeti or Rode NT-USB positioned 6-8 inches off-center, pop filter for noise reduction), camera (smartphone or DSLR with image stabilization), tripod for stability, simple neutral background, recording software (OBS Studio free or Adobe Premiere Pro for editing).
