**Oahu Restaurants (large groups):**\n- The Buffet at Hyatt Regency Waikiki (private room)\n- Duke's Waikiki (beachfront, private room seats 30)\n- The Old Spaghetti Factory (private room seats 40+)\n- Alan Wong's Honolulu (Hawaiian cuisine, private room seats 24)\n- Helena's Hawaiian Food (traditional Hawaiian, private room seats 30)\n\n**Maui Restaurants (large groups):**\n- Mama's Fish House (family-owned, private room seats 40)\n- The Fish Hopper (seafood, private room seats 30)\n- The Plantation House (contemporary Hawaiian, private room seats 24)\n- Castaway Cafe (Ka'anapali beachfront, private room seats 30)\n- Da Kitchen (Hawaiian comfort food, private room seats 20)\n\nTip: Reserve well in advance for large groups. Confirm group menus ahead of time. Ask about kid-friendly options.
