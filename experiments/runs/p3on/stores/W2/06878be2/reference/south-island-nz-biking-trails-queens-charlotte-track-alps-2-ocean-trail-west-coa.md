New Zealand South Island Biking Trails

Alps 2 Ocean Cycle Trail: 301 km, passes through Southern Alps, lakes, rivers, picturesque farming. Higher challenge level.

Queen Charlotte Track: 70 km along Marlborough Sounds with coastal and native bush scenery.

West Coast Wilderness Trail: 132 km of rugged untouched scenery with historic gold-mining heritage.

Otago Rail Trail: 150 km following old railway line between Middlemarch and Clyde through Otago countryside and mining towns. Good for longer, challenging rides.

St James Cycle Trail: 64 km remote biking experience through wild high country scenery.

Great Taste Trail: 175 km through Nelson-Tasman region with charming towns, vineyards, orchards. Good for foodies.

Bike Rentals: Widely available throughout South Island at tourist areas. Options include mountain bikes, road bikes, electric bikes plus helmets and accessories. Advance reservations recommended in peak season.

Biking Tips: Use quality helmet and padded shorts; bring water and snacks; plan route with rest stops; be aware of traffic; respect environment; carry first aid kit.
