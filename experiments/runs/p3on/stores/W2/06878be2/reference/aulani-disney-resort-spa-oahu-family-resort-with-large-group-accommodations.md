Aulani, a Disney Resort & Spa (Oahu, Hawaii). Chosen for large family vacation. 3-bedroom villas sleep 12 (connect for 24+); connecting standard rooms available (4 rooms = 16-20 total). Rates 600-700 per night. Group rates available for 10+ rooms (contact group sales team). Long-stay discounts 10-20% off for 5+ nights. Amenities: private beach, water park with volcano water play, multiple pools, lazy river, Aunty's Beach House kids club (ages 3-12). Complimentary Magical Express shuttle from Honolulu airport. Book 450 days in advance for best availability. Consider Disney-authorized travel agent for group planning.
