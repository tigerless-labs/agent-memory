**Oahu Activities:**\nHanauma Bay Snorkeling (kids 5+), Waikiki Beach, Dole Plantation (maze, pineapple train), Pearl Harbor/USS Arizona Memorial (kids 8+), Sea Life Park Hawaii (dolphin/sea lion shows, sea turtle feeding), Kualoa Ranch (horseback riding, ATV tours, movie tours)\n\n**Maui Activities:**\nMolokini Crater Snorkeling (kids 5+), Road to Hana (scenic drive with waterfalls, beaches, stops at Twin Falls/Ho'okipa), Whale Watching (Dec-May, kids 5+), Iao Valley State Park (botanical garden, hiking, Iao Needle), Maui Ocean Center Aquarium (all ages), surfing/paddleboard lessons (kids 8+)\n\nTips: Book in advance. Be flexible with weather. Bring sunscreen, hats, water. Consider guided tours for better navigation.
