Three popular budgeting apps compared in May 2023:

**Mint** - Free, links to 15,000+ financial institutions, auto-categorizes transactions, mobile app, user-friendly. Recommended for free users wanting straightforward expense tracking.

**YNAB (You Need a Budget)** - Freemium (6.99 per month or 83.99 per year after trial). Focuses on assigning jobs to every dollar, strong debt repayment planning, methodology-focused with workshops. Better for those willing to pay for comprehensive budgeting system.

**Personal Capital** - Free, investment-focused, tracks net worth, retirement planning tools, investment analysis. Better for those focused on investment tracking and financial planning rather than budgeting.

User chose Mint due to free tier and user-friendly interface.
