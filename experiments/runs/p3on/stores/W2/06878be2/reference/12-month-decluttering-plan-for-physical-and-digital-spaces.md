Month-by-month breakdown for year-long decluttering project:

**Month 1:** Create decluttering plan and set SMART goals. Determine areas needing work (home, office, devices, online accounts).

**Month 2:** Declutter clothing and accessories. Keep only items worn and loved; donate/sell/recycle unwanted items.

**Month 3:** Declutter books, magazines, papers. Keep essentials and sentimental items. Organize or digitize important documents.

**Month 4:** Declutter kitchen and dining. Remove broken, unused, or duplicate items. Organize for efficiency.

**Month 5:** Declutter living room and common areas. Keep items that serve purpose or bring joy. Donate/sell unwanted.

**Month 6:** Declutter bedroom and bathroom. Keep essentials and items contributing to peaceful atmosphere. Organize for comfort.

**Month 7:** Declutter workspace/home office. Keep productivity-supporting items. Organize for efficiency and focus.

**Month 8:** Organize digital files and folders on computer and external storage. Delete/archive unnecessary files and duplicates. Create maintainable file system.

**Month 9:** Declutter email inbox. Unsubscribe from unwanted newsletters. Delete/archive old emails. Create folder/label system.

**Month 10:** Evaluate social media accounts. Unfollow/unfriend misaligned accounts. Update privacy settings. Curate presence.

**Month 11:** Declutter smartphone and devices. Delete unused apps/games. Organize into folders. Update settings for performance/privacy.

**Month 12:** Assess progress. Identify areas needing further work. Establish maintenance routines and habits.
