Practical techniques discussed for managing online shopping impulses:

1. Spending journal - Track every purchase (date, item, price, store)
2. Budgeting app - Use tools like Mint to track and categorize spending
3. Set spending budget - Determine monthly limit and stick to it
4. 30-day rule - Wait 30 days before buying to separate impulse from genuine need
5. Unsubscribe from promotional emails - Reduce exposure to tempting offers
6. Remove saved payment methods - Make purchases more friction-filled
7. Address root causes - Identify if shopping is response to boredom, stress, or FOMO
8. Set boundaries - Only shop on certain days or specific websites
9. One-in, one-out policy - Get rid of old item for every new purchase to maintain balance
10. Review and adjust - Regularly check spending journal and budget for patterns

Emphasis on: tracking spending, understanding patterns, finding alternative coping mechanisms, and being patient with the habit-change process.
