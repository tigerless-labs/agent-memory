10 strategies for maintaining healthy work-life balance:

1. **Set a schedule:** Define working hours and stick consistently to fixed start/end times with designated breaks.
2. **Create dedicated workspace:** Set up separate work space to create mental boundary between work and personal life.
3. **Establish transition routine:** Use walk, meditation, or hobby to signal to brain it's time to switch gears between work and personal time.
4. **Limit work-related technology:** Turn off work email notifications, don't check work messages during personal time, keep work devices out of bedroom.
5. **Communicate boundaries:** Clearly tell colleagues, supervisor, and family about your availability during non-work hours.
6. **Prioritize personal time:** Schedule personal activities and self-care as non-negotiable commitments, not afterthoughts.
7. **Learn to say no:** Develop ability to decline work tasks/commitments that interfere with personal time or renegotiate deadlines.
8. **Practice time management:** Maximize productivity during work hours so work doesn't spill into personal time.
9. **Develop support network:** Cultivate friends, family, and colleagues who understand and respect your boundaries for encouragement and advice.
10. **Review and adjust:** Regularly assess work-life balance and adjust boundaries as life circumstances and work demands shift.
