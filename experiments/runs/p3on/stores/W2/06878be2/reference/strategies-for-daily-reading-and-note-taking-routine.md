10 strategies for establishing consistent daily reading and note-taking practice:

1. **Schedule daily reading time:** Block specific time daily when most alert and focused (early morning, lunch break, before bed).
2. **Set daily reading goal:** Establish goal like specific number of pages or time duration to maintain consistency and motivation.
3. **Choose reading format:** Select format(s) that work best (physical books, e-books, audiobooks) or mix depending on circumstances.
4. **Create comfortable reading environment:** Set up quiet, distraction-free space conducive to focus and engagement.
5. **Keep a reading list:** Maintain curated list of books/articles to read to ensure always having something and prevent decision fatigue.
6. **Take notes while reading:** Use system that works (highlighting, annotating, separate notebook, digital tool) to improve retention and understanding.
7. **Review notes regularly:** Schedule time to review notes to reinforce learning and identify patterns/themes in materials.
8. **Apply what you've learned:** Look for opportunities to use knowledge and insights gained from reading in daily life, work, or projects.
9. **Join book club or reading group:** Connect with others sharing reading interest for motivation, accountability, and community.
10. **Track progress:** Keep record of books/articles read and notes taken to monitor progress, stay motivated, and identify areas for further exploration.
