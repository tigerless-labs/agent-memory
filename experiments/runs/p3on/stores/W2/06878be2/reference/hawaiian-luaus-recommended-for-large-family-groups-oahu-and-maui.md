Luau recommendations for large groups:\n\n**Oahu:**\n- Germaine's Luau (Waikiki)\n- Paradise Cove Luau (west coast, oceanfront, seats 50, private tables)\n- Waikiki Starlight Luau (seats 30)\n\n**Maui:**\n- Old Lahaina Luau (oceanfront, seats 50, private tables)\n- Feast at Lele (seats 30, private tables)\n- Royal Lahaina Luau (oceanfront, seats 40, private tables)\n\nFeatures: live music, Polynesian dancing, buffet feasts. All offer private table/room options. Book in advance, especially for groups. Check dress codes. Arrive early for pre-luau activities (hula dancing, Hawaiian crafts, live music).
