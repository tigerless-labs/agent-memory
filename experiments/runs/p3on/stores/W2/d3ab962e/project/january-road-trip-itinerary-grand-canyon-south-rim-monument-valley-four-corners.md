Planned multi-destination road trip:

**Route:**
- Grand Canyon South Rim → Monument Valley: 200 miles, ~4 hours
- Monument Valley → Four Corners: 100 miles, ~2 hours
- Four Corners → Grand Canyon: 220 miles, ~4.5 hours

**Planning notes:**
- Add 1-2 extra days to avoid rushing and fully experience each destination
- Book accommodations well in advance, especially Monument Valley
- South Rim open year-round; North Rim closed in winter months
- Expect cold winter weather in January; pack warm layers
- Respect Native American land (Navajo Nation Tribal Park)
- Limited accommodations in area; plan lodging in advance
- Rough roads possible; ensure vehicle is in good condition

**Activities considered:**
- Monument Valley: guided tours, scenic 17-mile loop drive, hiking, sunrise/sunset photography
- Four Corners: photo at state borders, visitor center, surrounding mesas/buttes
