Specific crops successfully modified using CRISPR-Cas9 technology:

- **Soybean**: herbicide-resistant varieties developed
- **Wheat**: modified for drought and temperature stress resistance
- **Rice**: increased iron content to address nutrient deficiencies
- **Tomatoes**: modified ripening process for extended shelf life
- **Maize (corn)**: modified for increased yield

These represent practical applications of the technology as of 2022. The modifications demonstrate diverse use cases: disease/stress resistance, nutritional improvement, and yield enhancement.
