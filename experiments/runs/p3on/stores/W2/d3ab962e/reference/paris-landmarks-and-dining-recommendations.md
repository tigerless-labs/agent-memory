**Top landmarks for short visit (1-2 days):** Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum (close together), Champs-Élysées and Arc de Triomphe. **Complete list:** Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame, Champs-Élysées, Arc de Triomphe, Montmartre/Sacré-Cœur, Versailles, Sainte-Chapelle, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, Fontainebleau, Pont des Arts, Les Invalides, Place des Vosges, Panthéon. **Cafes near Eiffel Tower:** Café de l'Homme (tower views, creative French cuisine), Café Gustave (15th arr., brunches), Carette (multiple locations, pastries). **Restaurants near Louvre:** Le Grand Véfour (Michelin-starred, French cuisine), Le Comptoir du Louvre (classic dishes), Verjus (1st arr., tasting menus). Info from 2022; verify before visiting.
