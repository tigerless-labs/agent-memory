Researched two camera bags:\n\n**Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW** (professional-grade):\n- Holds DSLR/mirrorless + 400mm lens + 7-8 additional lenses/flash\n- Modular design, weather-resistant 420D nylon with AW coating\n- 4.3 lbs, 13.8 x 10.2 x 19.7 inches\n- Multiple carry options (backpack/shoulder/waist)\n\n**Think Tank Urban Disguise 40L** (urban/travel focused):\n- Holds DSLR/mirrorless + 24-70mm lens + 2-3 additional lenses/flash\n- Water-resistant 420D nylon, low-profile urban aesthetic\n- 2.6 lbs, 11.4 x 7.3 x 17.3 inches\n- Multiple carry options (backpack/shoulder/cross-body)\n\nProTactic offers greater capacity and durability; Urban Disguise offers compact, stylish travel option.
