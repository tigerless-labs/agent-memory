Decided on Merkur HD safety razor (~$40) as a beginner-friendly option for wet shaving. Uses double-edge (DE) blades, which typically cost $5–20 per pack of 10–20 blades ($0.25–$1 per blade). Blades need replacement every 5–10 shaves, or roughly every 1–2 weeks depending on shaving frequency. Attracted to the product for environmental sustainability and wallet-friendliness.
