## Foundational Texts

**Saussure**  
Arbitrary signs; signifier/signified distinction; langue vs. parole; synchronic analysis; structural analysis

**Ogden & Richards (1923) - The Meaning of Meaning**  
Meaning as relationship between words and referents; symbols (arbitrary, conventional) vs. signals (direct); contextual definition

**Bloomfield (1933) - Introduction to the Study of Language**  
English version publication: 1933

**Begriffsschrift**  
Translates to: concept-script / concept notation

**Chomsky (1957) - Syntactic Structures**  
Generative grammar theory; universal grammar; deep structure vs. surface structure; innate language faculty

**Lakoff & Johnson (1972) - Metaphors We Live By**  
Conceptual metaphor theory; metaphorical language reflects how we understand the world; language and thought interconnected
