## Recommended Les Paul Features\n- Coil-tap or coil-splitting for tonal flexibility (Gibson Les Paul Standard, Traditional, Custom models offer these)\n\n## Open D Tuning Setup for Electric Guitar\n### Strings: Use heavier gauge (0.012-0.052 or 0.013-0.056) for fuller tone\n### Pickup: Use neck pickup for warmer, mellower tone\n### Amp Settings:\n- Gain: 5-6 to prevent mud\n- Bass: +100-150 Hz for warmth\n- Midrange: -500-800 Hz to reduce brightness\n- Treble: Reduce 5k-8k Hz to prevent harshness\n\n## Playing Tips\n- Lighter, more delicate right-hand technique\n- Finger slides and vibrato for expression\n- Consider compression pedal for even dynamics
