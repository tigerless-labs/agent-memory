Recommended resources for learning about AI and healthcare AI applications:

1. **The AI Alignment Podcast** - Podcast delving into latest AI research, applications, and implications; hosted by experts
2. **AI in Industry** (Emerj blog) - In-depth articles, research, and insights on AI applications across industries
3. **The AI Now Institute** - Research center focused on social implications of AI; features research papers and event recordings
4. **Machine Learning Mastery** - Comprehensive online resource for ML and AI tutorials, articles, and courses
5. **KDNuggets** - Popular AI and machine learning news site with articles, tutorials, and industry updates
6. **AI for Everyone** - Free online course by Andrew Ng covering AI basics and applications
7. **arXiv** - Repository of electronic preprints for AI and machine learning research papers
8. **NeurIPS, IJCAI conferences** - Major AI conferences for networking and learning latest developments
9. **AI Subreddit** - Active community discussing AI topics, news, and research
10. **Coursera, edX, Udemy** - Platforms offering AI and machine learning courses from top institutions
