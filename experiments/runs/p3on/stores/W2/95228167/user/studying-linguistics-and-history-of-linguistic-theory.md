Actively studying foundational linguistic theories and the history of how linguists have understood word meaning and language structure. Areas of interest include structural linguistics (Saussure), early theories of meaning (Ogden & Richards), generative grammar (Chomsky), and conceptual metaphor theory (Lakoff).
