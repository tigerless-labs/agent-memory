## Dinosaur-Themed Art\n- Paleoart: paleontological illustrations and sculptures bringing dinosaurs to life\n- Gary Staab: artist and paleontologist using fossilized bones to create dinosaur models\n- Michael Whelan: dinosaur-inspired paintings combining science and art\n\n## Natural History Museum Exhibitions\n- American Museum of Natural History (NYC): "Hall of Saurischian Dinosaurs" - dinosaur fossils and artifacts\n- Field Museum of Natural History (Chicago): "Sue, the Largest T. Rex" - largest and most complete T. Rex skeleton\n- Natural History Museum (London): "Dino Jaws" - evolution of dinosaur teeth and jaws\n\n## Art and Natural History Collaborations\n- Art Evolves: brings together scientists, artists, and natural historians\n- The Art of Nature: art pieces by scientists and artists exploring the natural world\n- SciArt Initiative: has featured exhibitions on natural history and dinosaurs\n\n## Online Resources\n- DinoPit: dinosaur-themed art, illustrations, and sculptures\n- Paleoart community: showcases paleontological illustrations\n- Natural History Museum's Picture Library: vast collection of natural history images
