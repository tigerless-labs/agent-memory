Key multi-day hiking trail in Natural Park of Moncayo:\n\n**Route details:**\n- Distance: 36 kilometers\n- Duration: 2-3 days\n- Type: Circular route through the park\n- Passes through small towns and villages\n\n**Highlights:**\n- Moncayo summit (highest peak in park)\n- Fuenfría waterfall\n- Meadows, forests, and rock formations\n- Cultural and historical landmarks along the route\n\n**Best season:** Autumn (spring also good; summer too hot, winter has snow)
