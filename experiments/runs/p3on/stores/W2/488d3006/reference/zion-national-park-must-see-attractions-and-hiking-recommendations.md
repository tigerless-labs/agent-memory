**Must-see Attractions:**\n1. The Narrows - scenic hike along Virgin River through narrowest part of Zion Canyon (get wet)\n2. Angels Landing - iconic rock formation with steep, narrow ascent; spectacular views\n3. The Emerald Pools - three pools and waterfalls on Kayenta Trail\n4. Weeping Rock - unique rock formation with dripping springs and lush vegetation; short easy hike\n\n**Popular Hikes:**\n1. Angel's Landing - 5 miles, strenuous, steep drop-offs and narrow ridges\n2. West Rim Trail - 10 miles, stunning canyon views and Virgin River\n3. Observation Point Trail - 8 miles, spectacular views of canyon and landscape\n4. Kayenta Trail - 2 miles, connects to Emerald Pools and Weeping Rock\n5. Watchman Trail - 3 miles, views of Springdale and surrounding landscape\n\n**Essentials:**\n- Use free shuttle service throughout park\n- Check park website for permits and restrictions\n- Bring plenty of water and sunscreen (intense Utah sun)\n- Wear sturdy hiking shoes with good traction\n- Stay on designated trails, respect wildlife, pack out all trash
