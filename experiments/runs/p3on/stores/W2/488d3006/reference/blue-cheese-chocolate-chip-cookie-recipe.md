## Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheet with parchment.
2. Whisk together flour, baking soda, salt.
3. Cream butter, sugar, and blue cheese until light and fluffy.
4. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
5. Gradually mix in flour mixture.
6. Fold in chocolate chips.
7. Drop by tablespoonfuls onto baking sheet.
8. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges are lightly golden.
9. Cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.

**Notes:** Crumble blue cheese finely for even distribution. Use high-quality blue cheese. The blue cheese flavor should be subtle and intriguing, not overpowering. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer.
