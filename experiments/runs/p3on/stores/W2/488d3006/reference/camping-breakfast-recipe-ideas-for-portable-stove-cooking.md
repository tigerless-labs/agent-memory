Recommended camping breakfast ideas for portable stove:\n1. Campfire Skillet Breakfast (eggs, sausage/bacon, peppers, onions, cheese)\n2. Trail Mix Oatmeal (instant oats, nuts, dried fruits, cinnamon)\n3. Camping Breakfast Burrito (eggs, cheese, salsa, sausage/bacon/beans/avocado)\n4. French Press Coffee and Campfire Muffins\n5. Breakfast Tacos (tortillas, scrambled eggs, chorizo, cheese, tomatoes, salsa)\n6. Instant Campfire Hash Browns (shredded potatoes, onions, peppers)\n7. Camping Quiche (eggs, milk, cheese, bacon/sausage/vegetables)\n8. Yogurt Parfait (Greek yogurt, granola, berries/banana/mango)\n9. Campfire Pancakes (with syrup, honey, or fresh fruits)\n10. Egg and Cheese Wrap\n\nTips: pack light with non-perishable ingredients, prep at home, keep cooking gear simple.
