Inspired by a blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle tasted at a local chocolate shop. The flavor combination was unexpected and delicious, so decided to recreate it in cookie form.

First attempt overwhelmed the other flavors with too much blue cheese, resulting in a cookie that was too pungent and salty. After several more tries and recipe adjustments, found the perfect balance of sweet and savory.

Son (picky eater) was skeptical but loved the cookies on first bite, requesting multiple servings. This became a blog post celebrating the recipe.
