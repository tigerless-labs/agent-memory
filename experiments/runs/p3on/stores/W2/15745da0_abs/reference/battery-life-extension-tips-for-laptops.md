## Tips to extend laptop battery life

1. **Adjust screen brightness** — dimming or using auto-brightness saves significant power
2. **Turn off unnecessary features** — disable Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, location services when not in use
3. **Use Power Saver/Low Power Mode** — limits CPU performance, reduces brightness, turns off unnecessary features
4. **Close unnecessary apps and background processes** — prevents power drain from unused applications
5. **Update OS and drivers** — newer versions often include power-saving improvements
6. **Use battery-saving browsers** — Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome have built-in power-saving features
7. **Turn off keyboard backlight and peripherals** — saves power when not in use
8. **Clean laptop vents** — dust blockage causes excess heat and power consumption
9. **Avoid extreme temperatures** — keep between 32°F–95°F (0°C–35°C)
10. **Monitor battery health** — use Windows Battery Report or third-party tools
11. **Limit resource-intensive tasks** — avoid prolonged video editing, gaming, or streaming
12. **Turn off unnecessary notifications** — reduces wake cycles
13. **Update BIOS** — newer versions may include power-saving improvements

**RAM upgrade impact:** Adding RAM from 8GB to 16GB has minimal direct battery impact; power consumption increase is only 1–2 watts, but can enable slightly faster task completion and earlier entry into low-power states.
