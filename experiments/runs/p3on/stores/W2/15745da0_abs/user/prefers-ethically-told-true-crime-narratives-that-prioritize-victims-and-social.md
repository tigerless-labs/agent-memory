Values true crime stories that:
- Prioritize victims' dignity and experiences over graphic details
- Address systemic issues enabling violence, not just perpetrator focus
- Promote justice and prevention, not glorification of criminals
- Respect victims' families and avoid re-traumatization

Believes consumers bear responsibility for ethical engagement with media and should hold creators accountable for sensationalization and exploitation. Sees true crime narratives as having potential for positive social change when told responsibly.
