## Context
Restaurant menu rewrite project for Villa Gennaro, completed May 2023.

## Work completed
Rewrote three menu sections with enhanced item descriptions and maintained pricing:

**Part 2 – THE CLASSICS** ($22–$26)
- Chicken dishes: Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese
- Veal dishes: Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese
- All served with vegetable and potato or pasta

**Part 3 – ENTREES** ($20–$38)
- Veal Toscano ($24), Eggplant Parmigiana ($20)
- Seafood: Shrimp Fra Diavolo ($28), Broiled Salmon ($26), Zuppa Di Pesce ($34), Salmon Horseradish ($28), Seafood Risotto ($26)
- Meat: Braised Short Ribs ($38), Chicken Giambotte ($26), Rack of Lamb ($38)
- Vegetarian: Risotto Primavera ($20)

**Part 4 – PASTA** ($20–$32)
- Penne a la Vodka ($20), Capellini Primavera ($20)
- Seafood pasta: Rigatoni alla Gennaro ($30), Lobster Ravioli ($24), Roasted Pepper Pappardelle ($32)
- Other: Portobello Mushroom Ravioli ($24), Lasagna ($21), Braised Short Ribs Pappardelle ($30), Siciliana ($22)

## Approach
Each description emphasizes flavor profile (savory, zesty, hearty, etc.), key ingredients, and sauce/preparation method to make items more appealing to diners.
