**Books:**
- 'Anselm Kiefer: Heaven and Earth' by Kiefer & Dennison
- 'Anselm Kiefer: The High Priestess' by Kiefer & Dermutz

**Galleries:** Gagosian (NY/London) represents Kiefer since the 1980s

**Museums with Kiefer collections:** MoMA (NYC), Centre Pompidou (Paris), Serpentine Galleries (London)

**Notable installations:** 'Seven Heavenly Bodies' (2005-2015) at MoMA

**Documentaries:** 'Anselm Kiefer: The Seven Heavenly Bodies' (2015); Louisiana Channel artist profile (2017)
