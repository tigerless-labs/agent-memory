**Spider Plant winter care:**\n- Lighting: bright indirect light; move to south-facing window or use grow lights\n- Watering: reduce watering; water when soil feels dry\n- Humidity: place on pebble-water tray or use humidifier\n- Temperature: 65-75°F (18-24°C), avoid heating vents and drafts\n\n**Peace Lily winter care:**\n- Lighting: tolerates low light but benefits from indirect sun; north-facing or grow lights\n- Watering: reduce watering; let top 1-2 inches dry between waterings; avoid overwatering\n- Humidity: place on pebble-water tray or use humidifier\n- Temperature: 65-80°F (18-27°C), avoid heating vents and drafts\n\n**General tips:**\n- Fertilize less; skip or reduce to every 4-6 weeks\n- Prune to maintain shape and remove dead leaves\n- Monitor for pests (spider mites, mealybugs, scale)
