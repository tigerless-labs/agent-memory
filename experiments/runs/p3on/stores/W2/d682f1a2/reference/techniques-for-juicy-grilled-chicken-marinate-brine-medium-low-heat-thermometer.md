Prevent dry grilled chicken:\n- Marinate: olive oil + acid (lime/vinegar) + spices, 30+ min\n- Brine: 1 tbsp salt per 1 cup water, 30-60 min\n- Pound to even thickness\n- Grill medium-low (325-350°F)\n- Don't press down with spatula\n- Check thermometer: 165°F internal\n- Rest 5-10 min after cooking\n\nAlternatives: pan-sear, oven-roast, or poach for juicier results.
