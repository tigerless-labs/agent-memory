**Abstract Expressionism books:**
- 'The New York School' by Irving Sandler
- 'Pollock' by Kirk Varnedoe (former MoMA curator)
- 'Mark Rothko: The Decisive Decade: 1940-1950' by Todd Herman
- 'Abstract Expressionism' by David Anfam
- 'Theories and Documents of Contemporary Art' ed. Stiles & Selz
- 'Art Since 1940: Strategies of Being' by Fred S. Kleiner
- 'Theories of Modern Art' ed. Herschel B. Chipp
