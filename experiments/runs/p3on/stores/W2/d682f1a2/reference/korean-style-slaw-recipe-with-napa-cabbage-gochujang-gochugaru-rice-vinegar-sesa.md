**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups napa cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1 cup carrots, peeled and grated
- 1/2 cup cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup Korean chili flakes (gochugaru)
- 2 tablespoons Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Sesame seeds and chopped green onions for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Combine napa cabbage, carrots, and cucumber in large bowl
2. Whisk together chili flakes, gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, and ginger in small bowl
3. Pour dressing over slaw and toss
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes
6. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions before serving

**Notes:** Can adjust spice level, add nuts/seeds for crunch, keeps up to 2 days refrigerated
