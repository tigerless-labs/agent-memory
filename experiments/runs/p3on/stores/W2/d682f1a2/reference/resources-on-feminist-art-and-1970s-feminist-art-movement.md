**Key books:**
- 'The Feminist Art Movement' by Broude & Garrard (covers 1960s-1990s)
- 'Feminist Art and the Maternal' by Andrea Liss
- 'WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution' ed. Butler & Mark (2007 MoCA LA exhibition catalog)

**Online archives:** Feminist Art Base (Brooklyn Museum), Women's Art Library/MAKE (London), Guerrilla Girls collective work

**Key scholars:** Linda Nochlin ('Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists?'), Lucy Lippard, Griselda Pollock, Cornelia Butler

**Courses:** Coursera 'Feminist Art' (U Michigan), edX 'Gender and Art' (Harvard), Feminist Art Project (Rutgers)
