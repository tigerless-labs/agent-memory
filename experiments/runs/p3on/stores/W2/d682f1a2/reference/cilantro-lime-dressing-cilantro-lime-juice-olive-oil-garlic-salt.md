Fresh dressing for salads and grain bowls.\n\n**Ingredients**:\n- Freshly chopped cilantro\n- Lime juice\n- Olive oil\n- Minced garlic\n- Salt\n\nMix together. Bright, refreshing flavor—pairs well with quinoa and black bean salads.
