**Books:**
- 'The Conservator's Handbook' by Dorge & Horenburg
- 'Art Conservation: Principles and Practice' by Oddy & Smith
- 'The Art of Conservation' by David Bomford

**Museums offering tours:** Getty Museum, Metropolitan Museum of Art, Smithsonian Museum Conservation Institute

**Organizations:** International Institute for Conservation (IIC), American Institute for Conservation (AIC), Getty Conservation Institute (GCI)

**Online courses:** Coursera 'Art Conservation' (UPenn); Getty GCI online workshops
