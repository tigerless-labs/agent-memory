**Pork Belly Prep for Korean BBQ:**

1. **Scoring:** Make parallel cuts 1/4 inch deep and 1 inch apart on the fat side to help marinade penetrate
2. **Remove excess fat:** Trim thick fat layers but leave about 1/4 inch on surface to keep meat moist
3. **Tenderize:** Use meat mallet or back of knife to gently pound, focusing on thicker areas
4. **Cut:** Cut into 1-2 inch cubes or slices for even marinade penetration
5. **Dry brine:** Rub with kosher salt, brown sugar, and black pepper; let sit 30 min to 1 hour, then rinse and pat dry

**Cooking:** Marinate minimum 2 hours to overnight. Cook to internal temp 160°F (71°C). Let rest 5-10 minutes before serving. Use hot grill/grill pan for nice char.
