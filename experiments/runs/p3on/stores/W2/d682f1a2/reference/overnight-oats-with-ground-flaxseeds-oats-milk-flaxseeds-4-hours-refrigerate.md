**Ingredients** (1 serving):\n- 1/2 cup rolled oats\n- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk (or milk of choice)\n- 1 tbsp ground flaxseeds\n- 1 tbsp honey/maple syrup (optional)\n- Pinch of salt\n- Toppings: banana, berries, shredded coconut, cinnamon (no nuts)\n\n**Method**: Mix all in jar, refrigerate 4+ hours overnight, stir and serve in morning.\n\n**Notes**: Grind whole flaxseeds with coffee grinder, blender, or food processor (just before use). Pre-ground available at health food stores. Add more milk if too thick.
