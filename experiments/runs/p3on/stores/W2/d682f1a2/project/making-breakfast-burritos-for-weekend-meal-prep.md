Decided to use Breakfast Burritos as meal prep breakfast. Ingredients: scrambled eggs, sausage or bacon, black beans, cheese, veggies. Freezes individually wrapped for up to 3 months. Prefers oven reheating (350°F for 15-20 min) over microwave. Interested in trying unique fillings: roasted vegetables, chorizo, mushroom duxelles, avocado salsa, pickled onions, cilantro lime crema, kimchi, fresh cilantro.
