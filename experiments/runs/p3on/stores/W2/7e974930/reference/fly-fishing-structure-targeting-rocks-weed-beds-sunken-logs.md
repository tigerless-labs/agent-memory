Strategies for targeting structure when fly fishing for bass in lakes:

**Rocks**:
- Cast parallel to rock face (not directly at them)
- Use streamers for quick, aggressive retrieves
- Vary retrieve by depth: slower at base of drop-off, faster at top

**Weed Beds**:
- Use weedless flies (frog, weedless streamer)
- Cast into edges of weed bed, not thick center
- Use slow, deliberate retrieve to sink into weeds
- Imitate natural baitfish or insect movement in weed structure

**Sunken Logs**:
- Cast to structure as primary holding areas
- Vary presentation angle for natural approach
- Streamers effective near logs
- Use appropriate sink rates for depth

**General Structure Tactics**:
- Bass hold near structure for ambush feeding
- Transitional zones (drop-offs, weed lines, structure changes) most productive
- Adjust presentation speed and depth based on conditions
- Pay attention to seasonal patterns and water temperature
- Look for natural food sources and feeding signs
