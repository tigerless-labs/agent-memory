Effective bass lures by category:

**Topwater**: Popper (classic, creates surface commotion), Spook (walk-the-dog style, wounded baitfish), Frog (soft hollow-body for lily pads and heavy cover)

**Soft Plastics**: Curly tail grub (versatile with jighead or rig), worm (Texas or Carolina rig), lizard (crawdad imitation, good for heavy cover)

**Crankbaits**: Shad-imitating crankbait (medium-diving, open water), square-bill crankbait (shallow-diving around cover), deep-diving crankbait (10-20 feet depths)

**Jigs**: Jig and pig (curly tail or chunk trailer, heavy cover), swim bait (imitates baitfish), finesse jig (tight spaces, clear water)

**Other**: Spinnerbait (spinning blade with trailer), A-rig (multi-lure school imitation)

**Selection factors**:
- Water temperature: warm = baitfish/crawdad imitation; cool = shad or slower prey
- Water clarity: clear = natural presentation; murky = strong vibration or color
- Structure: adjust for rocks, weeds, logs
