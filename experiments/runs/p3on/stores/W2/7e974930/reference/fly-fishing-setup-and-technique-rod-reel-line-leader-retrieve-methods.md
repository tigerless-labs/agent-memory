Fly fishing lake bass setup and technique:

**Rod & Reel**:
- 9-foot 6-inch to 10-foot rod, medium to fast action
- 6-8 weight fly rod
- Reel with 150-200 yards of 20-pound backing
- Reliable drag system

**Line & Leader**:
- Weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking line (6-8 weight, 90-foot standard)
- 9-12 foot leader
- Monofilament or fluorocarbon, 15-20 pound breaking strength
- 1-2 foot tippet, 12-15 pound breaking strength
- Use tippet ring to prevent line twist
- Consider fluorocarbon for low visibility in clear water

**Retrieve Methods**:
1. Strip Retrieve: Steady, consistent strip; vary speed and length
2. Twitch Retrieve: Short quick movements (poppers, sliders, dry flies)
3. Drag-Free Retrieve: Allow fly to sink/float naturally
4. Incorporate pauses to let bass react

**Presentation Tips**:
- Cast near structure (rocks, weeds, logs)
- Vary depth to match feeding patterns
- Cast at 45-degree angles for natural presentation
- Adjust for water clarity (subtle in clear, aggressive in murky)
- Look for baitfish schools
- Focus on transitional zones (drop-offs, weed lines)
- Be prepared to move and adjust as conditions change

**Knots**: Practice Blood Knot and Surgeon's Knot for connections
