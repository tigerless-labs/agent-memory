Leading figures in ecocriticism: Lawrence Buell (environmental imagination, cultural restoration), Cheryll Glotfelty (literature and environment representation), Jonathan Bate (culture and nature relations), Eric D. Wilson (literature and natural world). Research methods: close reading, historical/cultural context analysis, environmental science analysis, comparative analysis, ethnographic fieldwork, discourse analysis. Focus: how literature represents nature and shapes societal attitudes toward the environment.
