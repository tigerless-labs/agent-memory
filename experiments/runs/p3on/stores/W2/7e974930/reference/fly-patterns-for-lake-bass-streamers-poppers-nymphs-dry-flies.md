Effective fly patterns for lake bass fishing:

**Streamers** (baitfish/leech imitation):
- Woolly Bugger (black, olive, brown)
- Muddler Minnow (baitfish, crawdad, frog)
- Baitfish Streamer (silver, gold, chartreuse for shad/shiner)

**Poppers and Sliders** (surface/subsurface):
- Poppers (white, yellow, chartreuse for disturbance)
- Sliders (slow steady retrieve)
- Gurgler (creates gurgling sound)

**Nymphs**:
- Crayfish patterns (Crayfish Hopper, Crawdad Nymph)
- Mayfly nymphs (Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail)
- Leech patterns (Woolly Leech)

**Dry Flies**:
- Frog patterns (Frog Fly, Poppers with Frog Legs)
- Terrestrials (Ant Fly, Beetle Fly for ants, beetles, grasshoppers)

**Setup**: 9-foot 6-inch or 10-foot fly rod, weight-forward floating line, 9-12 foot leader

**Tactics**: Match flies to lake's natural food; vary retrieve to match fly action and bass mood; experiment with patterns and presentations
