Camera Choice: Fujifilm X-T30 II

Specs:
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- Compact size with vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- Autofocus: 425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time, 99% accuracy
- Battery life: 380 frames per charge (EVF), 440 frames (LCD)
- Weather resistance: Moisture-resistant but not fully sealed

Budget: 1500 dollars for body and kit lens
- Body: 899 dollars
- Kit lens options considered: XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 (499 dollars) or XF 18-55mm f/2.8-4 (699 dollars)

Reason for choice: Compact size, vari-angle touchscreen, excellent autofocus system, good value for landscape and nature photography.
