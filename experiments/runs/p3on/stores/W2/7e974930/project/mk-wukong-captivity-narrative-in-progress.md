## Characters
- **MK**: Protagonist, imprisoned. Knows only old stories about the diamond snare. Slowly starving, confused by the contrast between legendary Wukong (heroic savior from childhood stories) and actual Wukong (cruel captor).
- **Wukong**: Monkey King antagonist. Seeks the diamond snare. Initially considers killing MK but keeps him alive as 'insurance.' Grows increasingly annoyed with MK's pleas and needs.

## Plot Elements
- MK is imprisoned behind a magical barrier
- Wukong interrogates him about the location of the diamond snare
- MK convinces Wukong to let him live (not fully convincingly)
- MK suffers from hunger/thirst; Wukong reluctantly provides minimal food/water
- MK attempts escape during a monkey feast party (fails)
- Central tension: Wukong debating whether MK's value as insurance outweighs his inconvenience

## Key Scenes Developed
- Initial interrogation and barrier testing
- MK's failed attempts to convince Wukong to release him
- Brief mercy: another monkey brings minimal food/water
- MK's internal conflict: reconciling heroic legend with cruel reality
- Party scene: MK hears feasting, attempts escape
- MK begs for food; Wukong reluctantly provides stale bread and water

## Themes
- Legendary figures vs. their corrupted reality
- Survival desperation
- Power imbalance and psychological manipulation
