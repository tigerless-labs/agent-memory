Brainstorming approaches focused on uncommon and novel methods to increase creative output:

1. **Dedicated time** — Block out distractions, create comfortable and inspiring environment
2. **Collaboration** — Working with others stimulates new ideas and approaches
3. **Mindfulness & meditation** — Clears mind and improves ability to generate ideas
4. **Regular breaks** — Allows recharging and approaching problems with fresh perspective
5. **Learn new skills & experiences** — Expands knowledge and provides new sources of inspiration
6. **Idea quotas with time constraints** — Forces quick thinking and generates higher volume of ideas
7. **Prompts and constraints** — Stimulates creativity by thinking outside comfort zone or using specific words/phrases as inspiration
8. **Journal or sketchbook** — Captures ideas as they occur and serves as future inspiration source

**Emphasis:** unconventional and novel approaches prioritized over standard techniques.
