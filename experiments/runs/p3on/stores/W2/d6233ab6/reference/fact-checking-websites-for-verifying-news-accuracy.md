## Recommended Fact-Checking Websites

1. **Snopes** — https://www.snopes.com/
2. **PolitiFact** — https://www.politifact.com/
3. **FactCheck.org** — https://www.factcheck.org/
4. **The Washington Post Fact Checker** — https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/fact-checker/
5. **Reuters Fact Check** — https://www.reuters.com/fact-check
6. **AP Fact Check** — https://apnews.com/hub/ap-fact-check

These tools verify accuracy of news stories. Tips for spotting fake news:
- Check the source for reputation/trustworthiness
- Read beyond sensational headlines
- Verify supporting evidence and quotes
- Compare multiple sources
- Check author credentials
- Assess overall tone and bias
