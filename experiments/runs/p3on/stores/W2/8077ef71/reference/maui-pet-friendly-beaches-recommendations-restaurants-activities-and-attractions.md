**Napili Beach (Morning):**
- The Gazebo: Pet-friendly restaurant with outdoor seating area overlooking Napili Bay. Serves pancakes, omelets, and Hawaiian-style dishes.

**Kaanapali Beach (Afternoon):**
- Hula Grill: Pet-friendly restaurant with outdoor seating overlooking the ocean. Offers fresh seafood, island-inspired cocktails, and live music.
- Whalers Village: Shopping and dining destination with pet-friendly restaurants, boutiques, and cultural events.

**Lahaina (Evening):**
- Fleetwood's on Front St: Pet-friendly upscale restaurant owned by Mick Fleetwood (Fleetwood Mac). Features rooftop bar, live music, gourmet seafood, meat, and vegetarian options.
- Kimo's: Iconic seafood restaurant with live music and happy hour menu.
- Captain Jack's Island Grill: Tiki-style pub with fun and festive vibe.
- Lahaina Banyan Tree: Historic landmark surrounded by park, hosts events and festivals, pet-friendly gathering spot.
