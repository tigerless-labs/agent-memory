Uses a digital blood pressure monitor to track readings regularly. Observed elevated systolic pressure readings. Working on lifestyle modifications to lower blood pressure: increasing potassium-rich foods (leafy greens, fruits, legumes, nuts/seeds), whole grains, fiber; reducing sodium; aerobic exercise (150 min/week); resistance training 2-3x/week; stress reduction (meditation, yoga, deep breathing); getting 7-8 hours sleep per night.
