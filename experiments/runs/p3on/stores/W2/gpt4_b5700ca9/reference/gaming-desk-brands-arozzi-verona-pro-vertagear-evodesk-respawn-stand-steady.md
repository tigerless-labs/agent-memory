Gaming desk recommendations evaluated (2023):

Arozzi Verona Pro: Spacious surface, ergonomic design, excellent cable management. Popular and well-regarded brand in gaming desk market.

Vertagear: Newer brand, sleek design, sturdy construction, innovative features like wireless charging pad.

EvoDesk: Strong on ergonomics and customization, height and tilt adjustment, split keyboard trays, good for comfort and productivity.

Respawn: Designed specifically for gamers, sturdy construction, ergonomic, built-in cup holder and headphone hook.

Stand Steady: Focuses on standing desks, offers sitting and standing options, sturdy construction, good cable management.

Selection criteria: space for full setup, ergonomic design, durability, cable management, style compatibility with gaming chair, budget.
