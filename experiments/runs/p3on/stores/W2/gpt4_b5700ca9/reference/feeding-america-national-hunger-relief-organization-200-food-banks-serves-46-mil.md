**Feeding America**\n\n- Largest hunger-relief organization in the United States\n- Established in 1979, headquartered in Chicago, Illinois\n- Network of 200 food banks across the US\n- Serves over 46 million people through 60,000+ food pantries, meal programs, and agencies\n- 501(c)(3) non-profit organization\n- Website: feedingamerica.org\n\n**Mission**: To feed America's hungry through a nationwide network of food banks and engage the country in the fight to end hunger.\n\n**Support to food banks**:\n- Food distribution sourcing\n- Fundraising resources\n- Advocacy for hunger-related policies\n- Programs and services (food safety training, inventory management, nutrition education)\n- Research and analysis\n\n**Key initiatives**:\n- Child hunger programs\n- Senior hunger programs\n- Disaster relief\n\n**Services provided by food banks**:\n- Emergency food assistance\n- Nutrition education\n- Childhood hunger programs\n- Senior hunger programs\n- Food rescue programs
