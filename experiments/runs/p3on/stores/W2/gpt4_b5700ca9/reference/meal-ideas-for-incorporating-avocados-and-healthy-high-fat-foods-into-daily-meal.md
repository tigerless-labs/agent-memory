**Meal incorporation ideas:**

1. Add avocado slices to salads or grain/buddha bowls
2. Use mashed avocado instead of mayonnaise on sandwiches/wraps
3. Make homemade guacamole (avocado, diced tomatoes, red onions, lime juice)
4. Include nuts or seeds in homemade trail mix, smoothie bowls, or oatmeal bowls
5. Top Greek yogurt with fresh berries, honey, and chopped nuts or seeds
6. Cook salmon or fatty fish with olive oil and herbs for dinner
7. Use olive oil to dress salads or cook vegetables
8. Add avocado to breakfast toast or eggs
9. Melt nut butter over heated whole grain bread for breakfast
10. Snack on dark chocolate with nuts, berries, or fruit

**Healthy high-fat foods:**
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, chia, flaxseeds, pumpkin seeds) — protein, fiber, healthy fats
- Salmon and fatty fish — omega-3 fatty acids, anti-inflammatory, heart-health benefits
- Olive oil — Mediterranean diet staple, linked to reduced heart disease/stroke/cancer risk
- Full-fat yogurt — healthy fats, protein, probiotics for digestive health
- Dark chocolate — antioxidants, heart health, brain function, mood benefits (in moderation)

Key principle: moderation and variety are essential; consuming in balance as part of a varied diet avoids weight gain.
