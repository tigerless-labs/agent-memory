Decided to get either a spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum) or snake plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) to improve indoor air quality. Both are chosen for ease of care and air-purifying properties.\n\n**Planned Setup:**\n- Planter with drainage holes (terracotta or ceramic preferred)\n- Well-draining potting mix designed for indoor plants\n- Monthly fertilization during growing season (spring/summer) with diluted balanced fertilizer\n- Avoid overwatering to prevent root rot\n\n**Why These Plants:**\n- Spider plant: Removes formaldehyde and xylene; easy to propagate\n- Snake plant: Almost impossible to kill; removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene; tolerates low light
