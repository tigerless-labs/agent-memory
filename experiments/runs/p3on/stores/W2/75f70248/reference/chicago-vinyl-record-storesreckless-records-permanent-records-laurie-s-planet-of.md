Chicago independent record stores with indie folk and singer-songwriter vinyl selections:

**Primary Stores:**
- Reckless Records (multiple locations: Wicker Park, Lakeview, Logan Square) – extensive new and used vinyl, strong indie folk section
- Permanent Records (Ukrainian Village) – vast selection of new and used records, indie/folk focus
- Laurie's Planet of Sound (Lincoln Square) – curated new and used vinyl, indie/folk/singer-songwriter
- Saki (Logan Square) – record store and art gallery with indie folk selection

**Used Record Stores:**
- The Exchange (Lakeview)
- Crossroads Music (Avondale) – large selection with indie/folk focus
- Dave's Records (Lincoln Square)

**Specialty:**
- The Vinyl Conflict (Logan Square) – punk, indie, underground, carries indie folk albums
- Bric-a-Brac Records (Avondale) – record store and vintage shop with indie folk selection
