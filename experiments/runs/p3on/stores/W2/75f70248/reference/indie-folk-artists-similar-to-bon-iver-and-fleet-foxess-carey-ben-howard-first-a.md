Indie folk and singer-songwriter artists recommended as similar to Bon Iver and Fleet Foxes:

**Similar to Bon Iver:**
- S. Carey (former Bon Iver band member)
- Volcano Choir (featuring Bon Iver's Justin Vernon)
- The Antlers
- James Blake
- Ben Howard (British singer-songwriter, introspective acoustic sound)

**Similar to Fleet Foxes:**
- Beirut (eclectic folk/world/indie rock blend)
- The Tallest Man on Earth (Swedish singer-songwriter, folk-rock)
- Grizzly Bear (harmony-rich indie rock)
- The Lumineers (folk-rock, catchy hooks)
- First Aid Kit (Swedish duo, lush harmonies, melancholic tone)

**General Indie Folk:**
- The Head and the Heart (Seattle-based, lush harmonies)
- The Avett Brothers (folk-rock, storytelling focus)
- Mumford & Sons (British folk-rock)
- The Decemberists (Portland-based, literary lyrics)
- Lord Huron (Michigan-based, dreamy atmospheric sound)
