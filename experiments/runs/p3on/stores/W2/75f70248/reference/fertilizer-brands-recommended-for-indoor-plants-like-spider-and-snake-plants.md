**Recommended Indoor Plant Fertilizers:**\n\n1. **Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food** — Water-soluble, specifically formulated for indoor plants, easy to apply.\n\n2. **Schultz Indoor Plant Food** — Balanced formula that promotes healthy growth and development, good for general indoor plant care.\n\n3. **Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer** — Organic formula with slow-release nutrients providing sustained nutrition over time.\n\n**Application Guidelines:**\n- Fertilize only during growing season (spring and summer)\n- Apply once per month\n- Dilute to half-strength to avoid root burn\n- Skip fertilizing during fall and winter dormant season\n- Use balanced fertilizer with N-P-K ratio like 20-20-20\n- Start with small amounts and observe plant response before increasing dose
