**Eight Air-Purifying Indoor Plants:**\n\n1. **Spider Plant** — Removes formaldehyde and xylene; easy to propagate; thrives in various lighting.\n\n2. **Peace Lily** — Elegant white blooms; removes ammonia and benzene; prefers moderate watering, indirect light.\n\n3. **Dracaena** (40+ species) — Removes trichloroethylene, formaldehyde, benzene; grows to 10 feet (prunable).\n\n4. **Snake Plant** — Nearly indestructible; removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene; tolerates low light.\n\n5. **Pothos** — Low-maintenance trailing plant (up to 8 feet); removes formaldehyde, xylene, toluene; tolerates various light.\n\n6. **Rubber Plant** — Stylish dark green glossy leaves; removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene; needs bright indirect light.\n\n7. **Aloe Vera** — Has medicinal properties; removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene; prefers bright indirect light, infrequent watering.\n\n8. **Bamboo Palm** — Feathery palm (grows to 12 feet); removes trichloroethylene, formaldehyde, benzene; needs bright indirect light.\n\n**General Care:** Most prefer bright indirect light. Research specific watering needs to avoid overwatering. Maintain 65-75°F temperatures. Group plants to increase humidity. Some need frequent pruning; others are low-maintenance.
