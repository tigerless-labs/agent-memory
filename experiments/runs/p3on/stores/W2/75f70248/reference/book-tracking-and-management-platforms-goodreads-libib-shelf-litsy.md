Platforms for tracking books and connecting with other readers:

- **Goodreads** — Virtual bookshelves, ratings, reviews, groups, reading challenges, friend imports, personalized recommendations
- **Libib** — App for cataloging personal book collection and creating reading lists
- **Shelf** — Social book discovery platform; create shelves, follow friends, get personalized recommendations
- **Litsy** — Book tracking app with virtual bookshelf, reading progress tracking, reader connections
- **Amazon Wish List** — Add books to wishlists for purchasing later
- **Library apps** — Check holds, track reading via library app if available

Goodreads chosen as primary platform for tracking and connecting with Book Lovers Unite community.
