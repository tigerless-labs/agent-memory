Certified Financial Modeling (CFM) offered by Financial Modeling Institute for finance professionals with bachelor's degree and 2+ years experience. Recommended courses: Wall Street Prep Financial Modeling, Coursera Financial Markets (Yale), edX Business Foundations (UPenn), Harvard Business Review Online Courses. Books: John Tjia's Financial Modeling and Forecasting, Forecasting Principles and Practice by Hyndman/Athanasopoulos. Other certifications of interest: CFA, CAIA, CIPM. Industry publications: Financial Times, Bloomberg, The Economist. Transition to portfolio manager requires investment knowledge, portfolio management skills, leadership experience, and professional network development.
