Chicago venues recommended for indie folk music and open mics:

**Live Music Venues:**
- The Hideout (Logan Square) – weekly open mic Tuesdays
- Schubas Tavern (Lakeview) – indie folk shows  
- The Whistler (Logan Square) – weekly singer-songwriter showcase Wednesdays
- The Empty Bottle (Ukrainian Village)
- Lincoln Hall (Lincoln Park)
- Uncommon Ground (Wrigleyville) – open mics and singer-songwriter events
- The Elbo Room (Lakeview) – open mics and singer-songwriter showcases

**Music Schools:**
- Old Town School of Folk Music (Lincoln Square) – guitar classes and folk workshops
