Portfolio Manager: manages investment portfolios for clients, responsible for portfolio construction, risk management, performance measurement, research/analysis, client communication, team leadership. Goal: maximize returns while minimizing risk aligned with client objectives. Financial Analyst: analyzes financial data to support business decisions, conducts financial analysis, develops forecasts and models, creates reports, participates in budgeting/planning. Key differences: PMs focus on investment decisions and portfolio management; FAs provide analytical insights to support business decisions. PMs work with broader scope across multiple clients/portfolios; FAs typically focus on specific business units. Transition to PM requires building investment knowledge, portfolio management expertise, leadership skills, and developing personal investment philosophy.
