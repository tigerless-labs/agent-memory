**Fiction:**
- The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo by Taylor Jenkins Reid
- The Nightingale by Kristin Hannah (historical fiction set in WWII France)
- The Silent Patient by Alex Michaelides (psychological thriller)
- The Family Upstairs by Lisa Jewell
- Red, White, and Royal Blue by Casey McQuiston (romantic comedy)

**Debut Novels:**
- The Last House Guest by Megan Miranda
- The Age of Light by Whitney Scharer (about photographer Lee Miller)
- The Farm by Joanne Ramos

All recommended as popular novels from 2022-2023.
