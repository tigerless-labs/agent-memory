Handmade rug that appropriates the IKEA logo with the type changed to 'MERDA' (Italian/Spanish for excrement). The piece is a commentary on and critique of capitalist and consumerist culture, corporate interests, waste, and the dynamics of consumer desire.

As part of the project, researched related contemporary and conceptual artists working with similar motifs of corporate appropriation and anti-consumerist critique.
