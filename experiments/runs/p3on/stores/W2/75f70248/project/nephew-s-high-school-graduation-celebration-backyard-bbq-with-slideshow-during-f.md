Planned celebration format for nephew's high school graduation coinciding with family reunion.

## Format
- Backyard BBQ with slideshow

## Slideshow Specifications
- Duration: 5-10 minutes max
- Mix of photos and short video clips
- Chronological structure from childhood to high school graduation
- Captions and quotes (kept understated, not cheesy)
- High-quality, clear, well-lit photos

## Soundtrack
- Mix of instrumental tracks and popular songs with sentimental value to nephew
- Keep it mellow and concise so it doesn't overpower photos
- Consider artists like Ludovico Einaudi, Max Richter, Olafur Arnalds for instrumental pieces
- Use royalty-free music or properly licensed tracks

## Photo/Milestone Types to Include
- Sports achievements (team photos, action shots, trophies)
- School life (prom, homecoming, events, photos with influential teachers/friends)
- Family memories (vacations, holidays, everyday moments)
- Personal milestones (hobbies, volunteer work, personal interests)
- Humorous moments (funny photos, inside jokes)
- Graduation-specific (cap and gown, diploma, celebration photos)

## Design Approach
- Use animations and transitions sparingly to enhance without overwhelming
- Keep captions concise and clear
- Vary pace and photo types to keep audience engaged
- Include personal touches and inside jokes
- Get feedback from family members before the event
