Java Moss: 1-2 watts/gallon lighting, 72-82°F, pH 5.5-8.5, attach to rocks/driftwood, weekly fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK). Good hideout for neon tetras; gouramis may nibble but not preferred food; pleco may graze. Sensitive to copper-based medications.

Anacharis: 2-4 watts/gallon lighting, 65-85°F (prefers 72-82°F), pH 6.5-8.5, floats on surface, prefers moderate to strong water flow, weekly fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK). Natural food source for pleco; neon tetras and gouramis may nibble. Prune regularly for bushy growth. Sensitive to copper-based medications.
