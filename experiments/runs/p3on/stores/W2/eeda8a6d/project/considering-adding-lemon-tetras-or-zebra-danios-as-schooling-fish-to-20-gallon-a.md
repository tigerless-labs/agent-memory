Lemon Tetras: compatible with neon tetras (can school together), compatible with gouramis (too fast to catch but risk of fin-nipping), compatible with pleco. Zebra Danios: compatible with neon tetras (active, hardy, coexist peacefully), compatible with gouramis (too fast but risk of fin-nipping), compatible with pleco. Introduction: use a school of 6-10 or more, provide hiding places, ensure adequate swimming space.
