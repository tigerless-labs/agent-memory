Adding a treasure chest near existing artificial coral and rocks to create a themed 'city' area for fish exploration. Plan to:
- Place treasure chest at an angle or slightly buried in substrate for depth
- Add live or artificial plants around the structures for lush, natural environment
- Vary heights using rocks and coral for different levels
- Leave open swimming space around the decorated area
- Create multiple hiding and exploration opportunities for neon tetras and other fish
