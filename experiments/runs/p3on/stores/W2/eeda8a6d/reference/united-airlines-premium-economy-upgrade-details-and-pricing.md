Seat pitch: 38 inches. Seat width: 19 inches. Screen size: 13.3 inches HD. Includes noise-cancelling headphones, priority check-in and boarding, priority baggage handling for 2 checked bags at 50 lbs each, priority security screening, Polaris Lounge access at JFK, priority deplaning, upgraded meal service with complimentary wine and beer, and pre-landing snack. Upgrade costs from New York to San Diego: approximately 450 dollars per person JFK to SAN one-way, 420 dollars per person SAN to JFK one-way. Total round-trip approximately 870 dollars per person. Premium Economy seats are limited and sell out quickly.
