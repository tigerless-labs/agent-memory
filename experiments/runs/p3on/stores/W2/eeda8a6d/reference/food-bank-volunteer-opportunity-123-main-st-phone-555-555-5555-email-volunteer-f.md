## Food Bank Volunteer Program

**Location:** 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345

**Contact:**
- Phone: 555-555-5555
- Email: volunteer@foodbank.org
- Website: foodbank.org

**Shift Times:** 4:00 pm - 7:00 pm, weekdays

**Tasks:** Sorting and packaging donations, inventory management, food distribution, special events

**Commitment Options:**
- One evening per week
- Two evenings per week
- Flexible (minimum 2 hours per month)

**Requirements:**
- At least 18 years old
- Complete volunteer application
- Brief orientation session (~30 minutes)
- Sign waiver/release form
- Background check

**Group Volunteering:** Food bank has a Group Volunteer Program for groups of 5-20 people

**Adoptable Projects:**
- Food drives
- Special event support
- Community garden
- Backpack program for children
- Food sortation events
- Fundraising campaigns
