Observed that the golden honey gouramis in the 20-gallon aquarium exhibit territorial aggression toward each other during feeding times. Attempted mitigation strategies: extra food portions and additional tank decorations have had limited effectiveness. Considering adding a school of fast-moving fish (lemon tetras or zebra danios) to serve as distractions and reduce aggression through competition and visual stimulation.
