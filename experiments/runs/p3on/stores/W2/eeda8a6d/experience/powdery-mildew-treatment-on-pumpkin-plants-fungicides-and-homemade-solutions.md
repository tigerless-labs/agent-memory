## Treatment Methods

**Remove infected material:**
- Remove infected leaves and plants early to prevent spread

**Fungicide options:**
- Potassium bicarbonate
- Neem oil
- Sulfur
- Copper-based fungicides
- Always read and follow label instructions carefully

**Homemade remedy:**
- 1 tablespoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- Few drops of dish soap
- Mixed in 1 gallon of water
- Test with expert advice before applying

**Best practices:**
- Avoid spraying during bloom to protect beneficial insects and honeybees
- Improve air circulation: space plants, remove weeds, use trellis system
- Seek expert advice before choosing any treatment
