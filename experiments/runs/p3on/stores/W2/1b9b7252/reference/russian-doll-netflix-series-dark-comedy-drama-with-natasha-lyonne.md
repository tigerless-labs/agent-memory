**Show:** Russian Doll (Netflix)

**Genre:** Dark comedy-drama

**Protagonist:** Nadia Vulvokov, a game developer who keeps reliving the same night (similar to Groundhog Day)

**Main performer:** Natasha Lyonne (praised for her performance)

**Season 1 details:**
- 8 episodes
- ~25-30 minutes each
- Well-paced introduction to story and characters

**Themes:**
- Mortality and meaning of life
- Identity and self-awareness
- Human connection and relationships
- Trauma and mental health
- Consequences of one's actions

**Tone:** Dark and witty humor mixed with introspection; compared to 'Groundhog Day' meets 'Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind'

**Visual/audio:** Muted color palette, indie rock and electronic music, haunting atmospheric score

**Supporting cast:** Greta Lee, Charlie Barnett, Elizabeth Ashley
