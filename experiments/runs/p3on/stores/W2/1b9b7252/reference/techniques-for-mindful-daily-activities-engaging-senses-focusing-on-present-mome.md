Specific techniques for being mindful during routine daily activities like showering or doing dishes:\n\n1. **Engage your senses**: Notice temperature of water, feel of soap, sounds, smells, textures\n2. **Focus on present moment**: Avoid multitasking; give full attention to the activity at hand\n3. **Slow down**: Take your time instead of rushing; fully experience and appreciate the moment\n4. **Be non-judgmental**: Use an open, non-judgmental attitude; don't label activity as good or bad\n5. **Use breath as an anchor**: Take deep, intentional breaths to stay centered and maintain mindfulness\n\nAlso useful: start small with short practices (few breaths after waking/before bed), set phone reminders throughout day, notice when mind wanders and gently bring attention back.
