Platforms: Training Peaks, Coach.me, Runcoach, Vdot Coaching, Nike Run Club Coaching. Services: McMillan Running (Greg McMillan), Hanson Coaching Services (Hanson Brothers), FIRST. Certified coaches via USATF and RRCA websites. Selection criteria: experience with similar pace and goals, communication style, customization, cost, multi-sport experience.
