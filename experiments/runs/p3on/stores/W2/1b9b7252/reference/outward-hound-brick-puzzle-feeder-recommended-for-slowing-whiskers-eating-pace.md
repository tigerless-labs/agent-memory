Product: Outward Hound Brick Puzzle Feeder\n\nRecommended to slow down Whiskers' eating pace and provide mental stimulation. Features a brick design that separates food, requiring the cat to work to get the meal out. User was considering trying this for their kitten. Introduction approach: start with easy mode (treats easily accessible), gradually increase difficulty, supervise mealtime, be patient as kittens may take time to understand the puzzle.
