Slow-feeder bowls and puzzle feeders recommended for slowing eating pace:\n\nSlow-feeder bowls:\n- Omega Paw Tricky Treat Ball (treat-dispensing)\n- Pet Zone Treat Wobbler (wobbles as cat eats)\n\nPuzzle feeders:\n- Kong Wobbler (classic, requires rolling/pushing)\n- Nina Ottosson Dog Brick Puzzle (multiple compartments, can be used for cats)\n- PetSafe SlimCat Interactive Toy and Feeder (multiple levels, hidden compartments)\n- Outward Hound Brick Puzzle Feeder (brick design, separates food)\n\nIntroduction tips: start with regular bowl, gradually transition; use high-value treats initially; supervise to ensure comfort.
