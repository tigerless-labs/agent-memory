Recommended interactive toys for cats (especially those who like feather wands and laser pointers):\n- Wand toys with varied attachments (fabric strips, ribbons, small balls)\n- LED light toys (LED balls, cubes)\n- Interactive toy mice with feathers/strings/attachments\n- Tunnels and tents (can attach toys to ends)\n- Crinkle toys (bags, balls with crinkling sounds)\n- Food puzzle toys (challenges problem-solving)\n- Catnip toys and catnip-infused scratching pads\n- Motorized toys (spinning tops, self-propelled)\n\nRotate toys regularly to prevent boredom. Hide toys around house to encourage hunting behavior.
