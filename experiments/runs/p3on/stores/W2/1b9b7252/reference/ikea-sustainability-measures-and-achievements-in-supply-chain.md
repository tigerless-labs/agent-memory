IKEA's major sustainability initiatives include:

**Sustainable sourcing:** 100% cotton from sustainable sources (organic or Better Cotton Initiative); wood, paper, and cardboard from sustainable sources

**Energy-efficient products:** LED light bulbs, induction cooktops, water-saving taps

**Renewable energy:** Solar and wind power installations at stores and production facilities; nearly doubled renewable energy production since 2009

**Circular economy:** Sustainable and recyclable materials; products designed for disassembly; take-back programs

**Transportation:** Targets to reduce carbon emissions by 15% per product (achieved by 2020); optimized logistics and efficient transport modes

**Achievements:**
- 43% reduction in carbon footprint from stores and distribution centers
- 89% waste recycling rate at stores and warehouses
- Named one of World's Most Ethical Companies by Ethisphere Institute
- Topped 2020 Sustainable Brand Index in Europe
