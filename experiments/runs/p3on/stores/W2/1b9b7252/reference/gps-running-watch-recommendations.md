Recommended models: Garmin Forerunner 945 (high-end with music and payments), Apple Watch Series 7 GPS (stylish with Apple Health integration), Polar Vantage V (multisport with power meter), Suunto 3 Fitness (mid-range), Fitbit Ionic (wellness focus). Key features to consider: GPS accuracy with GLONASS support, optical heart rate monitoring, water resistance 50 meters or more, battery life 8 plus hours in GPS mode, comfortable design, app integration with running platforms.
