Implemented a healthy social media strategy:\n- Set specific times of day to check social media accounts\n- Use website blockers to limit access during work hours\n- Unfollow or mute accounts that cause stress or negative feelings\n- Designate Sundays as completely social-media-free to focus on reading and other enjoyable activities\n\nThis strategy supports their goal to maintain a healthy online presence without endless scrolling.
