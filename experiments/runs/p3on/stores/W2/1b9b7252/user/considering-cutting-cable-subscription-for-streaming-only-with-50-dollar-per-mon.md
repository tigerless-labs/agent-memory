**Status:** Actively researching, not yet decided

**Motivation:** Save approximately 50 dollars per month by switching from cable to streaming-only services

**Main concern:** Losing access to favorite sports channels — currently undecided whether sports access is worth the cost

**Research approach:**
- Making a list of sports channels currently watched
- Prioritizing by importance and regularity
- Researching streaming services with those channels
- Looking into online communities (such as r/CordCutting on Reddit, streaming forums) for insights from others who have cut the cord

**Considered streaming services for sports:**
- Sling TV (ESPN, NFL Network, NBA TV)
- YouTube TV (ESPN, FS1, NBC Sports)
- Hulu with Live TV (ESPN, FS1, NBC Sports)
- DAZN (boxing, MMA)
- NFL Sunday Ticket

**Other considerations:**
- Internet connection speed and reliability
- Streaming quality
- Multiple subscriptions cost
- Live TV and news access
- Free trials to test services
