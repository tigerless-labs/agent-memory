Interested in both books after asking for historical fiction recommendations. Both are WWII-era and WWI-era novels with strong characters and emotional depth. 'All the Light We Cannot See' is described as lyrical and introspective with deliberate pacing. 'The Alice Network' is faster-paced and more plot-driven.
