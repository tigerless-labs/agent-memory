**Current:** Think Tank Photo Urban Disguise 40L (40-liter capacity)

**Reason for upgrade:** Growing gear collection outgrowing current bag capacity

**Primary candidate:** Think Tank Photo Airport Advantage
- Larger capacity with multiple compartments and pockets
- Better organization and gear accessibility
- TSA-approved laptop sleeve and tablet pocket
- Weather-resistant design, reinforced seams
- Comfortable shoulder strap and sturdy handle
- Pros: More space, better organization, travel-friendly
- Cons: Larger size may be less portable, heavier weight

**Alternative options:**
- Think Tank Photo Airport Essentials: Similar to Airport Advantage but smaller capacity
- Think Tank Photo Urban Disguise 60L: Larger version of current bag, maintains similar design

**Decision factors to consider:**
- Travel frequency
- Total gear volume needed
- Organization needs vs. size/weight concerns
- Specific features required

**Status:** Weighing options, not yet decided.
