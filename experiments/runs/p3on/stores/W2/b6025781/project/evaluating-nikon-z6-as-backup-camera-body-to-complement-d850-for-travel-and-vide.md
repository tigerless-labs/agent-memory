**Decision context:** Looking for a backup camera body to complement Nikon D850 (owned ~1.5 years). Primary use case: travel (smaller/lighter). Secondary interest: video work.

**Z6 advantages:**
- Smaller and lighter than D850, ideal for travel
- Superior video: 4K UHD full-frame at 30p (vs D850's 4K only in DX crop mode)
- 5 stops in-body image stabilization (IBIS)
- More autofocus points (273 vs 153), covering 90% of frame
- Advanced eye-detection AF
- Lower cost than D5
- Compatible with F-mount lenses via FTZ Adapter

**Z6 concerns:**
- Z-mount vs F-mount incompatibility (requires FTZ Adapter, slight autofocus/IBIS trade-offs)
- Different lens ecosystem (potential for lens duplication)

**Alternatives under consideration:**
- Nikon D500: APS-C, shares F-mount, excellent autofocus, but no 4K video
- Nikon D5: Flagship autofocus, excellent low-light, same F-mount, but larger/heavier and more expensive

**Next steps:** Needs further research and ideally hands-on testing before final decision.
