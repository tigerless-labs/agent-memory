Initial historical fiction recommendations given based on enjoyment of 'The Nightingale' by Kristin Hannah:\n\n1. **'All the Light We Cannot See' by Anthony Doerr** - Pulitzer Prize-winning WWII novel about blind French girl and German boy. Lyrical, emotionally resonant, with deliberate pacing and episodic chapters.\n2. **'The Alice Network' by Kate Quinn** - Dual perspectives of socialite and spy during WWI and post-war years. Fast-paced, plot-driven with historical detail and suspense.\n3. **'The Song of Achilles' by Madeline Miller** - Reimagining of Trojan War from Patroclus's perspective. Stunning writing, heart-wrenching love story.\n4. **'The Red Tent' by Anita Diamant** - Based on biblical story of Dinah, explores ancient Mesopotamian women's lives. Powerful and emotional.\n5. **'The Historian' by Elizabeth Kostova** - Sweeping novel spanning centuries, exploring Dracula legend. Richly detailed, atmospheric.\n6. **'Those Who Save Us' by Jenna Blum** - Mother-daughter WWII story exploring complex relationships and family secrets.\n7. **'The Lost Girls of Paris' by Pam Jenoff** - Based on real events, WWII resistance network. Gripping story of courage and sacrifice.
