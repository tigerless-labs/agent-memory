WWI-era historical fiction recommendations similar to 'The Alice Network':\n\n1. **'The Soldier's Wife' by Pam Jenoff** - Story of a young German woman living in France during WWI\n2. **'The War Bride' by Pam Jenoff** - American woman travels to marry British officer during WWI, caught up in war intrigue\n3. **'The Winter Guest' by Pam Jenoff** - Set in Poland during WWI, woman embroiled in Polish resistance\n4. **'Those Who Save Us' by Jenna Blum** - Mother-daughter story in Nazi Germany with context of events after WWI\n5. **'The Poppy War' by R.F. Kuang** - Fantasy novel inspired by Chinese history and WWI events\n\nThese all explore human experiences during wartime, women's roles in war, and moral complexities of conflict.
