## Online Housing Portals & Programs\n- **NYC Housing Connect** - Search affordable housing opportunities and apply for lotteries online\n- **NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD)** - Rental assistance and homeownership programs\n- **Section 8 Housing Program** - Federal rental assistance through NYC Housing Authority (NYCHA)\n\n## Community Housing Organizations\n- **Queens Community House** - Affordable rental apartments and homeownership programs\n- **Queens Housing Authority**\n- **Jamaica Housing Authority**\n- **Flushing Willets Point Corona Local Development Corporation**\n\n## Alternative Housing Options\n- Shared housing/roommate situations on Craigslist, Facebook Groups, and local online classifieds\n\n## Search Tips\nAlways research landlord and property before signing lease. Be cautious of scams. Never pay deposit without seeing apartment in person and signing lease.
