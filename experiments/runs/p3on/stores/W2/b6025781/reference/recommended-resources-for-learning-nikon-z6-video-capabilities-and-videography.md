**YouTube Channels:**
- **Peter McKinnon**: Popular photography/videography creator with extensive Z6 series covering features, settings, and techniques
- **Tony Northrup**: Well-known photographer/videographer with in-depth tutorials and reviews on Z6
- **Steve Perry** (Nikon Ambassador): Expert in video production with Z6-focused video series
- **Nikon USA YouTube channel**: Official tutorials, product demos, tips from Nikon Ambassadors

**Online Learning Platforms:**
- **Udemy**: Range of courses on Nikon Z6 photography and videography (beginner to advanced)
- **CreativeLive**: Online learning platform with Z6-specific courses and workshops on photography/videography
- **Lynda.com**: Courses and tutorials on photography, videography, and post-production including Z6 content

**Communities & Forums:**
- **Nikon Rumors** (nikon rumors website/forum): Dedicated Nikon camera community including Z6 users
- **Reddit r/Nikon**: Active Nikon enthusiast community for questions, experience sharing, and feedback

**Books & eBooks:**
- **Nikon Z6: The Complete Guide** by David Busch: Comprehensive guide covering features, settings, and techniques
- **Mastering the Nikon Z6** by Darrell Young: Detailed guide with focus on video capabilities and autofocus

**Learning approach:** Practice and experimentation is key to mastery. Consider local photography/videography communities, workshops, or online classes to learn from professionals.
