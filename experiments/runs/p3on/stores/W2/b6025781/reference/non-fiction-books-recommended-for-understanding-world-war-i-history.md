Books recommended to understand WWI context for 'The Alice Network':\n\n1. **'The Guns of August' by Barbara W. Tuchman** - Pulitzer Prize-winning account of events leading up to WWI and the first month\n2. **'A World Undone: The Story of the Great War, 1914-1918' by G.J. Meyer** - Comprehensive history covering causes, course, and consequences\n3. **'The First World War: A Complete History' by Martin Gilbert** - Thorough and accessible history covering all aspects\n4. **'Women in the War Zone: Hospital Service in France, 1914-1918' by Sharon Ouditt** - Explores experiences of women nurses, ambulance drivers, and support roles in WWI\n\nAudiobook versions also available, particularly the G.J. Meyer books narrated by Malcolm Hillgartner and Simon Vance.
