Simple roasted sweet potatoes with fresh rosemary:

**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, chopped (about 2 tbsp)
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss sweet potato cubes with olive oil, rosemary, salt, pepper until evenly coated
3. Spread in single layer on parchment-lined baking sheet
4. Roast 20-25 minutes until tender and caramelized, flipping halfway through
5. Cool slightly before serving

**Notes:** Adjust rosemary amount to taste. Can add thyme or parsley. Good for meal prep and pairs well with lentil bolognese and quinoa.
