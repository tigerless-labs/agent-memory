Simple lentil and mushroom bolognese recipe:

**Ingredients (Servings: 4-6, Time: 30-40 min):**
- 1 cup dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup red wine (optional)
- 2 tbsp olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté onion, garlic, carrot, celery in olive oil until tender (5-7 min)
2. Add mushrooms, cook until they release liquid and brown (5 min)
3. Add lentils, basil, oregano; cook 1-2 min stirring
4. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, pepper, wine if using; stir well
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer covered 20-25 min until lentils tender
6. Taste and adjust seasoning

**Tips:** Mix different lentil types for complex flavor. Add tomato paste for richness. Add bell peppers or zucchini in last 10 min. Freezes well for 3 months.

Pairs well with quinoa and roasted vegetables.
