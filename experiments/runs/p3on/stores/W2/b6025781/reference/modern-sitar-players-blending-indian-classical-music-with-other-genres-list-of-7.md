**Modern Sitar Fusion Players**

1. **Anoushka Shankar** - Electronica, flamenco, jazz (see separate entry)

2. **Ravi Shankar Jr.** - Blends traditional Indian with electronic and rock music

3. **Shivnath Mishra** - Fuses Indian classical with Western classical music

4. **Pt. Vishwa Mohan Bhatt** - Grammy Award-winning artist who blends Indian classical with Western classical and contemporary music

5. **Jaywant Naidu** - Incorporates various musical styles from around the world to create unique compositions

6. **Niladri Kumar** - Fuses traditional Indian with jazz, flamenco, and rock

7. **Purbayan Chatterjee** - Blends Indian classical with jazz, blues, and Latin styles
