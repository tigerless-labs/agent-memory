**Anoushka Shankar** (born 1981)
- Daughter of legendary sitar player Ravi Shankar
- Half-sister of singer Norah Jones  
- Began performing with her father at age 14
- Trained in sitar from childhood

**Musical Style**
Fusion of traditional Indian classical, electronica, jazz, and flamenco

**Albums**
- *Traveller* (with electronic artist Karsh Kale)
- *Rise* (features jazz musician collaborations)
- *Land of Gold* (explores themes of migration and displacement)

**Collaborations**
- M.I.A. - 'Inside Me' (sitar + electronic beats + political lyrics)
- Bombino - 'Cler Achel' (Indian ragas + West African guitar)
- Braille/Praveen Sharma - 'Kerala' (complex rhythms, hypnotic melodies)
- Pepe Habichuela - 'Lasya' (Indian ragas + flamenco rhythms)
- Joshua Redman (jazz saxophonist)
- Karsh Kale (percussionist)
- Tanmoy Bose (tabla)

**Recognition**
- Multiple Grammy nominations
- Social activist: promotes music education and women's rights
