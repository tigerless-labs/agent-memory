Resources recommended during spiritual exploration and grief processing conversation:

**Grief and Spirituality:**
- "A Grief Observed" by C.S. Lewis
- "The Dark Night of the Soul" by St. John of the Cross
- "When Bad Things Happen to Good People" by Harold S. Kushner

**Values-based Living and Mindfulness:**
- "The Power of Now" by Eckhart Tolle
- "The Gifts of Imperfection" by Brené Brown
- "The Art of Possibility" by Rosamund Stone Zander and Benjamin Zander
- "The Undefended Heart" by Jett Psaris

**Exploring Different Perspectives:**
- "The God Delusion" by Richard Dawkins (atheism perspective)

**Podcasts:**
- The RobCast (faith, doubt, spirituality)
- The Happiness Lab with Dr. Laurie Santos
- The Daily Boost
