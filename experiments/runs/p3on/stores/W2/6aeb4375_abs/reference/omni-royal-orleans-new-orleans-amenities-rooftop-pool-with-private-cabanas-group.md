## Omni Royal Orleans Amenities & Services

### Pool & Leisure
- Rooftop pool with private cabanas and VIP service
- Bar serving cocktails

### Guest Services
- Select Guest loyalty program (free to join; offers exclusive rates, upgrade priority, early check-in/late check-out, free Wi-Fi, complimentary pressing service)
- 24/7 concierge team providing personalized itineraries, local insider tips, restaurant reservations

### Group Services
- Group reservations with special rates for room blocks
- Private event spaces for welcome parties, farewells, group dinners
- Group catering and audiovisual equipment available

### Spa
- The Rioux House spa; massages, facials, body wraps; group spa packages available

### Transportation
- Airport shuttle from Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY)
- Town car/sedan service for airport and city transportation
- Valet parking
- Assistance with taxi and ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft)
- Bike rentals

### Location & Access
- Centrally located; walkable French Quarter
- Close to St. Charles Avenue Streetcar line
- Partnerships with tour companies (e.g., New Orleans Tours)
