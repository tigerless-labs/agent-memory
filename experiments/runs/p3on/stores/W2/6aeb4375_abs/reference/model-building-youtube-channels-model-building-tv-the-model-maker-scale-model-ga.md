YouTube channels for model building tutorials and inspiration:
- **Model Building TV** — watching regularly, found helpful
- **The Model Maker** — watching regularly, found helpful  
- **Scale Model Garage** — focuses on car kits, weathering, painting, and detailing
- **Weathering with Wes** — dedicated to weathering techniques
- **Model Car Guy** — tutorials, reviews, and builds of car kits

Also recommended online communities: Scalemates, Model Mayhem, Hyperscale, The Modeling News
Magazines: Scale Auto Magazine, FineScale Modeler Magazine
