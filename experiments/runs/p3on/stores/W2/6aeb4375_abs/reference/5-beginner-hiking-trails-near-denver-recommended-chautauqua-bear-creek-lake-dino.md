**Chautauqua Trail** (Chautauqua Park, Boulder, 20-min drive): 2.1-mile loop, 400 ft elevation gain. Gentle, scenic views of Flatirons and Boulder.\n\n**Bear Creek Lake Park - Owl's Perch Trail** (Lakewood, 25-min drive): 2.5-mile loop, 200 ft elevation gain. Peaceful trail with wildlife spotting opportunities.\n\n**Dinosaur Ridge - Triceratops Trail** (Morrison, 25-min drive): 2.3-mile out-and-back, 300 ft elevation gain. Unique fossil site with educational signs.\n\n**Lookout Mountain - Windy Saddle Trail** (Golden, 25-min drive): 2.5-mile out-and-back, 400 ft elevation gain. Views of Rockies and Great Plains.\n\n**South Table Mountain - Mesa Trail** (Golden, 20-min drive): 2.5-mile loop, 300 ft elevation gain. Panoramic views of Denver metro and Rockies.
