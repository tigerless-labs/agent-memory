## Recommended 1/24 scale car kits

**Easier to Moderate (good for intermediate modelers):**
- Revell '69 Chevrolet Camaro SS (kit #85-4231) — well-engineered, simple construction, few parts, forgiving design
- Revell '68 Shelby Mustang GT500 (kit #85-4241) — well-designed, moderate part count
- Aoshima Honda NSX (kit #05035) — moderate part count, good for practicing bodywork

**Moderate to Challenging:**
- Tamiya Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7 (kit #24343) — higher part count, intricate details, excellent engineering
- Tamiya Nissan GT-R (R35) (kit #24355) — high part count, complex engine/chassis details, advanced techniques

**More Challenging:**
- Italeri Ferrari 250 GT California Spyder (kit #3644) — high part count, complex curves, photo-etch, delicate assembly
- Tamiya Ford GT40 Mk II (kit #24350) — legendary racing car, more challenging build
- Italeri Lamborghini Miura SV (kit #3646) — beautifully detailed supercar, challenging build

**Tips for selection:** Consider skill level, available time, desired detail level. Research reviews and built examples online before deciding.
