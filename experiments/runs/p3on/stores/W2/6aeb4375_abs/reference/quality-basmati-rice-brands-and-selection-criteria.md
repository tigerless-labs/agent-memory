Recommended brands: Tilda, India Gate, Daawat, Zebra, Lundberg

When selecting basmati rice, look for:
- **Aroma**: Distinctive nutty and buttery aroma
- **Grain length**: Long, slender grains that are separate and not clumpy
- **Texture**: Light and fluffy when cooked, with slightly firm bite
- **Age**: Older basmati rice develops better flavor and quality over time

Cooking technique:
- Use the correct water-to-rice ratio for your recipe
- Soak rice before cooking to ensure even cooking and prevent mushiness
