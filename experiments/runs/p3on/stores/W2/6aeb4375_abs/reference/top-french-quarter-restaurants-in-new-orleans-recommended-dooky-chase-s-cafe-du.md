## Top French Quarter Restaurants Recommended

1. **Dooky Chase's Restaurant** - NOLA institution since 1941; try gumbo z'herbes; has adjacent art gallery
2. **Cafe du Monde** - Iconic cafe, open 24/7; famous for beignets and coffee
3. **Coop's Place** - Casual Cajun restaurant; red beans and rice, gumbo, fried chicken; lively atmosphere with live music
4. **Galatoire's** - Historic restaurant since 1905; famous shrimp remoulade; upscale, lively dining
5. **Willie Mae's Scotch House** - James Beard Award-winning; Southern and Creole cuisine; fried chicken, gumbo, shrimp and grits
6. **GW Fins** - Upscale; exceptional seafood (scallops, lobster, oysters)
7. **Johnny's Po-Boys** - Casual; serving since 1950; fried oyster or shrimp po'boys
8. **Brennan's Restaurant** - NOLA institution; Creole cuisine; famous Bananas Foster dessert
9. **Cafe Maspero** - Cozy cafe; breakfast and lunch including beignets, omelets, sandwiches
10. **Port of Call** - Casual; burgers, sandwiches, salads; fun atmosphere with outdoor seating
