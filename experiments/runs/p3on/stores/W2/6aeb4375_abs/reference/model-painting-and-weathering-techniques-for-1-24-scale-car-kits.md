Painting tips for 1/24 scale cars: Use acrylic or enamel paints from Tamiya, Vallejo, or Testors. Apply gray or white primer first, then high-gloss base coat (e.g., Candy Apple Red for '69 Camaro), and finish with clear coat. Use masking tape for sharp lines. Weathering techniques include dust/dirt effects with washes on lower body, tire wear with graphite pencil, chips and scratches with lighter paint, rust on undercarriage/wheel wells, and chrome effects. Key principles: study reference images, practice on test pieces first, layer effects gradually starting subtle, use magnifying glass for detail work, and remember less is more to avoid over-weathering.
