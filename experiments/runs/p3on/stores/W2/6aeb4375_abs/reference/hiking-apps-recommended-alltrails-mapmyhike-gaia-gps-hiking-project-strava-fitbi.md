**AllTrails** (iOS/Android): Comprehensive trail database, GPS tracking, reviews\n\n**MapMyHike** (iOS/Android): GPS tracking (route, distance, elevation, calories), trail database, custom routes\n\n**Gaia GPS** (iOS/Android): Topographic maps, trail data, route planning; popular for backpackers\n\n**Hiking Project** (iOS/Android): Free REI app with trail database, GPS tracking, reviews\n\n**Strava** (iOS/Android): Social fitness app, track hikes, compete with friends, discover trails\n\n**Fitbit** (iOS/Android): Track hikes and sync with Fitbit account for overall fitness monitoring\n\n**Hiking Journal** (iOS): Log hikes, track progress, add photos and notes\n\n**PeakFinder** (iOS/Android): Identify mountains and peaks using camera and GPS\n\nOther tools: GPS watches (Garmin, Suunto, Fitbit Ionic), paper maps, hiking blogs and guides.
