Working on perfecting falafel technique:\n\n**For crispy exterior:** High-quality soaked (not canned) chickpeas drained well, good spice blend (cumin, coriander, paprika, cayenne), coarse chop texture, 30+ min chilling, double-fry method (325°F then 375°F), drain on paper towels\n\n**For fluffy interior:** Don't over-mix, use egg/breadcrumbs/oats as binders, avoid overcrowding, don't overcook\n\nServes with tahini sauce, hummus, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles. Planning to make for dinner party.
