User values personal space but strongly prefers handling boundary invasions through indirect, non-confrontational methods:

- Avoids direct confrontation due to concern about escalation and safety
- Prefers not to cause scenes or draw attention to the situation
- Favors indirect approaches: moving away, creating barriers with belongings, using body language
- Inclined to give people benefit of the doubt (assumes they may not realize they're invading space)
- Conflict-avoidant but recognizes need to maintain boundaries
- Values harmony and 'live and let live' approach where possible

Willing to move or create distance rather than verbally address the issue.
