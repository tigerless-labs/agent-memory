Basmati rice is better for Indian cuisine than Japanese short-grain rice. Basmati has long, slender grains with nutty flavor that pairs well with Indian spices. Japanese short-grain rice is sticky and clumpy, unsuitable for Indian dishes. Keep basmati on hand for Indian cooking projects.
