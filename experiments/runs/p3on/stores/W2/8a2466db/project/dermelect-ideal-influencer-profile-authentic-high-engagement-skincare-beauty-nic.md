Ideal Dermelect influencer characteristics:

1. **Authenticity** — Genuine passion for skincare/nail care; shares honest, unbiased opinions; content reflects real experiences
2. **Engaging Personality** — Charismatic, relatable; creates compelling content that keeps audience interested
3. **High Engagement Rate** — Strong connection with audience; followers value opinions and take action on recommendations
4. **Relevant Niche** — Beauty, skincare, or self-care focus; audience already interested in these products
5. **Creative Storyteller** — Conveys brand message through mix of images, videos, stories; makes products appealing and informative
6. **Professionalism** — Meets deadlines, follows communication guidelines, maintains positive working relationship
7. **Brand Alignment** — Shares commitment to cruelty-free, eco-friendly practices; can effectively communicate these values

This profile ensures both campaign effectiveness and authentic brand representation.
