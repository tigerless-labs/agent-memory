Compensation structure for Dermelect influencer marketing campaign:

1. **Product Gifting** — Tailored selection of Dermelect products for influencers to experience and share genuine opinions
2. **Monetary Compensation** — Fixed fee based on reach, engagement rate, and content quality; negotiated individually
3. **Affiliate Program** — Personalized promo codes; influencers earn commission per sale (e.g., INFLUENCER10 for 10% off)
4. **Performance-based Bonuses** — Rewards when KPI milestones are met (sales targets, engagement levels)

Aim: establish long-term relationships while motivating high-quality content creation.
