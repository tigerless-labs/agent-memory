Past Dermelect influencer campaigns that informed current strategy:

1. **#DermelectDetox** — Detoxifying skincare products; daily routines, skin clarity/texture improvements, health results. Multi-platform (Instagram, stories, YouTube, blogs). Result: significant website traffic increase, product sales boost.

2. **#DermelectNailRevolution** — Nail care line (strengthening treatments, polishes); tips, tutorials, before-and-after transformations. Multi-platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Result: boosted brand awareness, generated buzz for nail products.

3. **#DermelectSelfCare** — Brand values and self-care emphasis; personal routines featuring Dermelect products, well-being investment messaging. Multi-platform (Instagram, stories, YouTube). Result: sparked conversations around self-care, encouraged product exploration and well-being prioritization.

Learnings from these campaigns inform strategy for new influencer partnerships.
