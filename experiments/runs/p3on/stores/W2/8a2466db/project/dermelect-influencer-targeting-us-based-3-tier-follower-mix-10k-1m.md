Influencer targeting for Dermelect campaign:

**Geography:** US-based only
- Primary market is US audience
- Simplifies shipping logistics
- Better cultural resonance with target audience
- Easier management of localized promos

**Follower breakdown (tiered approach):**
- **Micro:** 10,000–50,000 — highly engaged niche audiences, personal connections
- **Mid-tier:** 50,000–250,000 — balance of reach and engagement
- **Macro:** 250,000–1,000,000 — broad brand awareness

Mixed tier strategy maximizes reach across different customer segments.
