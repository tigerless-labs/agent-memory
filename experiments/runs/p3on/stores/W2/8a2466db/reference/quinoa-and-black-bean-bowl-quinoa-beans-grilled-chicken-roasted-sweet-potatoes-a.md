## Quinoa and Black Bean Bowl

**Components:**
- Cooked quinoa
- Cooked black beans
- Grilled chicken breast with lime-cumin marinade (see separate recipe)
- Roasted sweet potatoes with diced onions and minced garlic
- Diced bell peppers, grilled with chicken
- Avocado sauce (see separate recipe)

**Assembly:**
Combine all components in a bowl. Quick to prepare and protein-packed. User plans to try this combination.
