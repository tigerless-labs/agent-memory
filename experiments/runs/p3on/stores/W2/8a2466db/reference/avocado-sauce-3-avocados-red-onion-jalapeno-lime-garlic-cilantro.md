## Avocado Sauce Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 3 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine avocado, red onion, jalapeño, lime juice, garlic, salt, and pepper in blender or food processor
2. Blend until smooth and creamy
3. Taste and adjust seasoning
4. Stir in cilantro if using
5. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving

**Tips:**
- Use ripe avocados for best result
- Adjust jalapeño for desired heat level
- Can add Greek yogurt or sour cream for tangier flavor
