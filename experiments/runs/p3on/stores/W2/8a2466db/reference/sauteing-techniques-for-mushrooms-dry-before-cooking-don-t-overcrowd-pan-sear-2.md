**Key mushroom sautéing steps:**\n- Clean mushrooms gently; trim stems\n- **Dry mushrooms thoroughly** with paper towels to promote browning instead of steaming\n- Use large hot skillet (stainless steel or cast iron ideal)\n- Preheat to medium-high heat (~4-5 on scale of 1-10)\n- Add 1-2 tablespoons oil, let shimmer before adding mushrooms\n- **Don't overcrowd**—cook in batches if needed\n- Sear 2-3 minutes first side to develop golden-brown color\n- Stir and cook another 5-7 minutes until tender\n- Add aromatics (onion, garlic, thyme) after searing\n- Cook until liquid evaporates and mushrooms caramelize\n- Remove before they become mushy
