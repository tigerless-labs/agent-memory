## Roasted Sweet Potatoes Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (1-inch)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Diced onions
- Minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss sweet potatoes with oil, onions, garlic, salt, pepper, cumin, paprika
3. Spread in single layer on parchment-lined baking sheet
4. Roast 20-25 minutes until tender and caramelized, flipping halfway
5. Cool slightly before adding to bowl

**Tips:**
- Can roast at 450°F for crisper exterior (watch carefully)
- Add cayenne or red pepper flakes for spicy kick
