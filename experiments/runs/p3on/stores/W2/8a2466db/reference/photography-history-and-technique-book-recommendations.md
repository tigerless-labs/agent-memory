**Recommended books on photography history and technique:**

- *The History of Photography* by Beaumont Newhall — comprehensive, beautifully illustrated, covers invention to mid-20th century
- *The Camera: A History* by Giles Hudson — concise, engaging, explores camera development 19th century to present
- *Photography: A Very Short Introduction* by Steve Edwards — accessible introduction covering technical, artistic, social developments
- *The Oxford Handbook of the History of Photography* edited by Michael Pritchard — comprehensive collection of essays by leading historians
- *The Photography Book* by Ian Jeffrey — visually stunning with iconic images and commentary
- *The Art of Photography* by Bruce Bernard — explores artistic development from 19th century to 1980s
- *The Photographer's Eye* by John Szarkowski — classic, explores aesthetic aspects with focus on Atget, Stieglitz, Weston
- *Photography and the Art of Seeing* by Freeman Patterson — explores aesthetic aspects and photographer's vision
