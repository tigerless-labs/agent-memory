**Proteins that pair well with Creamy Mushroom Truffle Sauce pasta:**\n\n- **Grilled Chicken**: Season with thyme, rosemary, parsley. Grill 5-7 min per side until 165°F internal temp. Let rest before slicing.\n- **Pan-Seared Scallops**: Season with salt, pepper, lemon. Sear 2-3 min per side on high heat until golden. Serve immediately.\n- **Crispy Bacon/Pancetta**: Cook until crispy, chop into pieces, drain excess fat before combining with pasta.\n- **Sautéed Shrimp**: Cook 2-3 min per side in butter or oil over medium heat until pink. Season with salt, pepper, parsley.\n- **Pan-Seared Steak** (filet mignon, ribeye): Sear 3-5 min per side on high heat. Let rest before slicing thinly.\n- **Roasted Turkey/Chicken Sausage**: Roast at 400°F, slice or crumble into pasta.\n\n**Tips**: Balance protein flavor with sauce (don't overpower), mix textures, don't use too much protein—let sauce be the star.
