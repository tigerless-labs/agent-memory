## Grilled Chicken Breast Recipe (Lime-Cumin Marinade)

**Ingredients:**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400°F)
2. Whisk together oil, lime juice, cumin, paprika, salt, pepper
3. Brush marinade on both sides of chicken
4. Marinate 15-20 minutes (or up to 30 minutes refrigerated)
5. Grill 5-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F
6. Rest a few minutes before slicing
7. Slice thinly against the grain

**Optional:**
- Grill diced bell peppers alongside chicken
- Substitute lime with lemon or orange juice
- Marinate 2-3 hours or overnight for deeper flavor

**Note:** User typically uses a grill pan or cast-iron skillet instead of traditional grill.
