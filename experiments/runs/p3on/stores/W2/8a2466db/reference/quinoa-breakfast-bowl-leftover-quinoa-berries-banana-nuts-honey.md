## Quinoa Breakfast Bowl Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup leftover cooked quinoa
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 1/2 banana, sliced
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
- 1/4 cup chopped nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia, flax)
- Pinch of salt
- Splash of milk or yogurt (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix quinoa, berries, and banana in small bowl
2. Add honey if desired
3. Sprinkle nuts or seeds on top
4. Add pinch of salt
5. Add milk or yogurt for creamier texture if desired
6. Serve immediately

**Tips:**
- Customize with any favorite fruits, nuts, or seeds
- Add cinnamon, vanilla powder, or cocoa powder for extra flavor
- Mix in scrambled eggs or bacon for more protein
- Try different milk alternatives (almond, soy, coconut)

Quick way to use dinner leftovers for a nutritious, filling breakfast.
