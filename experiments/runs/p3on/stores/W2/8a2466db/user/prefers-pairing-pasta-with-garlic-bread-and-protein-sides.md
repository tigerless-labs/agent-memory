When serving pasta with cream-based sauces like Creamy Mushroom Truffle Sauce, enjoys serving it alongside simple garlic bread made with softened butter, minced garlic, and fresh or dried herbs (parsley, thyme, or oregano). Also interested in adding protein to pasta dishes to make them more substantial.
