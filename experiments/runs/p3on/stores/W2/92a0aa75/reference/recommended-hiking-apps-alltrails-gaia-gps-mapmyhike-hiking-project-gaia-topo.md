Recommended hiking apps with features:

AllTrails — Most popular hiking app; vast trail database, GPS tracking, offline maps (iOS/Android)

Gaia GPS — Powerful app with detailed topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning (iOS/Android)

MapMyHike — User-friendly; tracks route, distance, elevation gain; trail recommendations and reviews (iOS/Android)

Hiking Project — Free app from REI; comprehensive trail database, GPS tracking, route planning (iOS/Android)

Gaia Topo — Advanced option; detailed topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning (iOS/Android)

Key features to look for: GPS tracking and offline maps, comprehensive trail database, route customization, reviews and ratings from other hikers.
