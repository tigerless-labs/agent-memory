**Key Principles:**\n1. Simplify - avoid jargon, use plain language\n2. Use analogies and metaphors to relate to everyday situations\n3. Visualize data with charts, graphs, diagrams, icons\n4. Focus on "so what?" - implications and benefits, not technical details\n5. Use storytelling to make data relatable and memorable\n6. Provide context about why data matters and how it was collected\n7. Break down complex information into smaller chunks\n8. Use interactive elements - polls, quizzes, gamification\n9. Practice delivery and anticipate questions\n10. Follow up with additional resources or training sessions\n\n**Application:** Especially effective when working with sales teams to align on strategy and drive data-driven decisions.
