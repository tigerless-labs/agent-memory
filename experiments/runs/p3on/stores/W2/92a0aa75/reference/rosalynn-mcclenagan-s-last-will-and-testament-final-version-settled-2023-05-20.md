# Rosalynn McClenagan's Last Will and Testament

**Settled:** 2023-05-20

## Key Structure
- **Executor:** Warren McClenagan (primary), Brian McClenagan (alternate)
- **Beneficiaries:** Warren McClenagan, Brian McClenagan, and Benjamen McClenagan (three sons, equal shares)
- **Successor Guardians:** The three sons (for any minor children at time of death)
- **Contingency:** If any son predeceases, his share passes to his surviving children in equal shares

## Estate
- **Home:** ~$120,000, owned outright (addressed: 305 Chase Ave, Gruver, TX 79040 or 401 Main St., Gruver, TX)
- **Car:** ~$11,000
- **Annuity:** to be divided among sons
- **Life Insurance Policy:** to be divided among sons  
- **Farmland:** One-third (1/3) interest in Ayres farmland to be divided among sons
- **Tangible Personal Property:** household items, keepsakes, photo albums, pets divided equally among sons

## Distribution Plan
All property distributed equally to three sons in equal shares, to be divided as they agree. Residuary estate also goes to three sons equally.

Additional provisions:
- Contest clause: any beneficiary who contests forfeits their share
- Estate taxes paid from residuary estate
- Governed by Texas law
