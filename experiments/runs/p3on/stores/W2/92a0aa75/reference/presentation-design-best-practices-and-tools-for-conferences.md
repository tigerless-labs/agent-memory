**Recommended Tools:**\n- Canva (web-based, user-friendly, extensive templates)\n- PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Haiku Deck as alternatives\n\n**Design Best Practices:**\n1. Keep simple and concise - one message per slide\n2. Consistent design theme throughout\n3. High-quality, relevant images\n4. Visual aids (charts, graphs, diagrams) for complex data\n5. Strong contrast for visual hierarchy\n6. Generous white space\n7. 2-3 fonts maximum\n8. Minimal text (3-5 sentences per slide)\n9. Sparing use of transitions/animations\n10. Practice delivery multiple times\n\n**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**\n- Too much text or cluttered slides\n- Poor font choices or inconsistent design\n- Low-quality images\n- Overusing animations\n- Lack of white space\n- Color combinations problematic for color-blind viewers\n- Spelling/grammar errors\n- Not considering audience needs
