Recommended waterproof hiking boots for fall/wet conditions:

Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof — Popular choice; excellent waterproofing, breathability, traction. Features Vibram TC5+ outsole for superior grip on various terrain.

Salomon Quest 4D 3 GTX — Waterproof and breathable Gore-Tex membrane, grippy Contagrip sole that excels on wet and dry surfaces.

Keen Targhee II Mid WP — Comfortable, waterproof design with rugged outsole for excellent traction on uneven terrain.

The North Face Hedgehog Fastpack GTX — Designed for fast-paced hiking. Waterproof Gore-Tex membrane and grippy Vibram outsole for various trail conditions.

La Sportiva Raptor GTX — Comfortable, waterproof design with aggressive outsole for excellent traction on rocky, wet, and dry terrain.

Key features: waterproof/breathable membrane (Gore-Tex), rugged outsole with deep lugs, ankle support, comfortable cushioned midsole, appropriate weight for durability/support balance. Always break in boots before hitting trails.
