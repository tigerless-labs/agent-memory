Had been wanting to see 'Hamilton' for years. Finally watched it on Disney+ and loved it so much that watched it twice in one week. On the second viewing, convinced family to watch it with them. The innovative use of hip-hop and R&B to tell American history, combined with the cast's energy and performances, resonated deeply with both user and family. Particularly appreciated the show's themes of ambition, love, and legacy, and how it presented American history as complex and multifaceted rather than sanitized.
