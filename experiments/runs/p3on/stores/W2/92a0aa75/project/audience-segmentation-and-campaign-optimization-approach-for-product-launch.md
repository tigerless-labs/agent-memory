**Audience Segmentation:**\n- CRM data segmented by job function, industry, and company size\n- Separate email campaigns created per job function for relevance\n- Marketing automation tool used for personalization and customization\n\n**Social Media Strategy:**\n- Combination of organic and paid advertising\n- Relevant content creation with hashtags for visibility\n- Social media listening tools tracking engagement and sentiment\n\n**Campaign Optimization:**\n- A/B testing subject lines, CTAs, and creative assets\n- Data and analytics inform messaging and creative tailoring\n- Regular progress reviews and adjustments via bi-weekly meetings
