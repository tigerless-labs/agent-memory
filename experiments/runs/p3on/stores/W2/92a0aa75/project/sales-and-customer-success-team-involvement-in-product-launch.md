## Sales Team\n- Training and enablement period: 2-3 months\n- Budget allocation: 5-10% of overall marketing budget\n- Focus: product knowledge, target account identification, pilot programs, demo handling\n\n## Customer Success Team\n- Training and enablement period: 2-3 months\n- Budget allocation: 5-10% of overall marketing budget\n- Key role: identify early adopters and advocates among existing customers\n- Engagement activities: exclusive access, beta testing, customer reference programs
