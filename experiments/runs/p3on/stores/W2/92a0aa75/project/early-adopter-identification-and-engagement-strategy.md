Plan to identify and engage early adopters and advocates among existing customers. Timeline: 1-2 months. Budget: 2-5% of marketing budget.\n\n**Identification methods:**\n- Customer segmentation by adoption rate and satisfaction\n- Behavioral analysis of feature adoption and feedback\n- Customer health scoring\n\n**Engagement tactics:**\n- Exclusive early access to new product\n- Beta testing participation\n- Personalized communication\n- Incentives: discounts, VIP support, recognition, swag, influence on roadmap
