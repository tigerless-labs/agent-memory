Ryan wants to time fall hikes to experience peak foliage colors. Peak foliage season in Northern Hemisphere typically occurs mid-September to mid-October, earlier in northern states (New England, Upper Midwest), later in southern states (mid-October to early November). Factors affecting timing: tree species, latitude/climate, temperature, moisture, weather conditions. Daylight hours shortening (around late August) signals the start of seasonal change but doesn't precisely predict peak foliage.
