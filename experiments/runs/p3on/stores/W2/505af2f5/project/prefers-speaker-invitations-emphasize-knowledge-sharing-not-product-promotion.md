When inviting speakers—especially from companies building open-source technology—frame the focus around knowledge sharing, expertise, and community discussion rather than product promotion. Use indirect language like 'we'd love for you to share your experiences and insights, but maybe not so much about the promotion or advertisement of your products' rather than direct terms like 'sales pitch'.
