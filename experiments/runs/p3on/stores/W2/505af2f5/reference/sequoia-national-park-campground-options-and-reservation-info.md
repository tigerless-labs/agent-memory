Sequoia campground reservation process:\n- Some sites first-come, first-served; others reservable via recreation.gov\n- Peak season (June-August): reserve 6 months in advance\n- Off-peak season (Sept-May): more likely first-come, first-served\n\nPopular campgrounds:\n- **Lodgepole Campground** — Giant Forest area, year-round, reservations via recreation.gov\n- **Dorst Creek Campground** — near Giant Forest, mid-May to mid-Oct, reservations available\n- **Buckeye Flat Campground** — Giant Forest area, mid-May to mid-Oct, first-come, first-served\n- **Potwisha Campground** — near Ash Mountain entrance, year-round, first-come, first-served\n\nCheck park website for closures and restrictions.
