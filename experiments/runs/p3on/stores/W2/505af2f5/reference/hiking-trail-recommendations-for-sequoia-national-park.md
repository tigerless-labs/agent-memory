Recommended trails for scenic views in Sequoia National Park:\n\n1. **High Sierra Trail to Hamilton Lakes** (~12 miles round-trip) — stunning views, Kern River crossing, picturesque alpine lakes\n2. **Alta Peak Trail** (~14 miles round-trip) — panoramic views from 11,200 ft summit, Great Western Divide, Kaweah Range, Sierra Nevada views\n3. **Marble Falls Trail** (~4 miles round-trip) — beautiful waterfall, lush forest, Crystal Cave Road\n4. **Big Trees Trail to Moro Rock** (~2 miles round-trip) — giant sequoia groves including General Sherman Tree, Moro Rock granite dome with panoramic mountain views\n5. **Congress Trail** (~2 miles round-trip) — beautiful giant sequoia forest\n\nNote: Check trail conditions and permits before heading out.
