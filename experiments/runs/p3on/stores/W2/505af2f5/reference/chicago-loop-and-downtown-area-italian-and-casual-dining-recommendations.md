**Italian Restaurants in Loop:**
- Carmine's (1043 N Rush St) — family-style Italian
- The Berghoff Italian Restaurant (17 W Adams St) — classic Italian in historic building
- Il Poggiolo (205 N Michigan Ave) — upscale, contemporary Italian with handmade pastas
- Monteverde Restaurant & Pastificio (1020 W Madison St) — house-made pastas, famous cacio e pepe
- Bavette's Bar & Boeuf (218 W Kinzie St) — Italian-inspired menu with handmade pastas
- Formento's (925 W Randolph St) — Italian-American, retro vibe

**Lunch spots near Millennium Park:**
- Park Grill (237 E Randolph St) — casual, quick-serve in Millennium Park
- The Gage (24 S Michigan Ave) — seasonal American with Millennium Park views
- Così (233 S Wacker Dr) — fast-casual bowls and salads
- Potbelly (222 S Wabash Ave) — Chicago sandwich shop
- Bertucci's (233 S Wacker Dr) — Italian-inspired sandwiches and pizza
- The Purple Pig (500 N Michigan Ave) — Mediterranean small plates

Reservations recommended for sit-down restaurants, especially weekends.
