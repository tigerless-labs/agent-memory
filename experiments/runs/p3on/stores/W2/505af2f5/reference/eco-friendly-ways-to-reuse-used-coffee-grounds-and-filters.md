**Coffee Grounds:**
- Compost for nutrient-rich soil (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium)
- Natural pest repellent for ants, snails, slugs
- Natural fertilizer for plants
- Natural dye for fabric or paper
- Exfoliant in face masks or skincare scrubs

**Coffee Filters:**
- Paper towel alternative for cleaning
- DIY air fresheners (add essential oils)
- Fire starter (shred first)
- Biodegradable garden markers
- Craft project materials (flowers, lanterns)
