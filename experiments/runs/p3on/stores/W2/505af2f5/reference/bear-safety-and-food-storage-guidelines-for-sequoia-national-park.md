Sequoia has higher bear activity than Yosemite — requires extra precautions.\n\n**Food Storage:**\n- Use bear-resistant containers (BRCs) — available for rent\n- If no BRC: hang food 10 ft high, 4 ft from trunk, 100 yards from campsite\n- Keep food in airtight containers\n\n**Bear Safety Hiking:**\n- Make noise (talk, sing, wear bear bells) to avoid surprising bears\n- Stay on designated trails\n- Watch for tracks, scat, digging\n- Keep campsite clean\n- Carry bear spray; know how to use it\n\n**Additional:**\n- Check with rangers for current bear activity in your area\n- Know park regulations\n- Respect bears as wild animals — never approach or feed
