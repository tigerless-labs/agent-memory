**Major Museums & Attractions:**
- Art Institute of Chicago (111 S Michigan Ave) — world-class art museum with 300,000+ works; iconic pieces include American Gothic, Nighthawks, A Sunday on La Grande Jatte, Monet's Water Lilies, Van Gogh's The Bedroom
- Field Museum (1400 S Lake Shore Dr) — natural history; home to Sue (largest T. rex skeleton)
- Shedd Aquarium (1200 S Lake Shore Dr) — marine life, dolphins, beluga whales
- Adler Planetarium (1300 S Lake Shore Dr) — astronomy, stargazing on Lake Michigan shores
- Maggie Daley Park (337 E Randolph St) — innovative playground with rock climbing wall, suspension bridges, winter ice skating
- Chicago Cultural Center (78 E Washington St) — free admission, rotating exhibitions, concerts; beautiful Tiffany glass dome in Preston Bradley Hall

**Art Institute Visit Strategy:**
- Half-day (2-3 hours): Focus on most famous works
- Full-day (4-6 hours): Explore multiple departments (European Painting, American Art, Impressionist, Modern/Contemporary, Photography)
- Arrive early to beat crowds
- Use audio guides or mobile app
- Check website for current exhibitions and special events
