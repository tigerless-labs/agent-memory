Car rental companies near Sequoia:\n- **Enterprise** in Visalia (closest; SUVs/vans available)\n- **National** at Fresno Yosemite International Airport (~1.5 hrs away; hybrid/electric options)\n- **Budget** in Visalia\n\nMountain driving tips:\n- Check road conditions before entering park\n- Drive slowly on narrow, winding roads with steep drop-offs\n- Fill up gas before or early in park (limited stations)\n- Prepare for high elevation effects on vehicle\n- Consider 4WD for remote/dirt road areas\n- Bring map and GPS (cell service limited)\n- Watch for wildlife at dawn/dusk
