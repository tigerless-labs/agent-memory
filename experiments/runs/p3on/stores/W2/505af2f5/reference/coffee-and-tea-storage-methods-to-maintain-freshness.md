**Coffee Storage:**
- Store in airtight containers (glass or ceramic jars with tight-fitting lids)
- Keep in dark, cool place away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture
- Store at room temperature (60-70°F / 15-21°C)
- Freeze in airtight containers for longer storage (extends freshness weeks)
- Follow 'first in, first out' rule

**Tea Storage:**
- Store in airtight containers (tin or ceramic canisters)
- Keep in cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat
- Store away from strong-smelling foods (tea absorbs odors)
- Keep away from light (preserves flavor and aroma)
- Follow 'first in, first out' rule

**General Tips:**
- Clean containers regularly to prevent old flavors
- Label and date containers
- Store coffee and tea separately to prevent flavor transfer
