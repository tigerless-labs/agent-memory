**Inside park:**
- El Tovar Dining Room (~1 mi): Upscale Southwestern cuisine, El Tovar Hotel, reservations recommended
- Bright Angel Restaurant (~1.5 mi): American comfort food and breakfast, Bright Angel Lodge
- Canyon Café (~2 mi): Casual quick-service café with convenience store, breakfast/sandwiches/snacks
- Grand Canyon Village Marketplace (~2.5 mi): Grocery store with deli counter

**Grand Canyon Village area:**
- We Cook Pizza (~3 mi): Pizza, salads, sandwiches
- Canyon Coffee and Café (~3 mi): Coffee, pastries, light bites

**Tusayan (outside park, ~7 mi):** Mexican, Italian, American cuisine options

**Self-catering:** Camper Services at Mather Campground has kitchen facilities.
