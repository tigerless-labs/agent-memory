Quick Chicken Recipes: Fajitas, Tenders, Quesadillas, Lemon Garlic, Korean-Style Fried, Caesar Wraps, Veggie Kabobs, Buffalo Tenders, Avocado Salad, Tikka Masala. See detailed fajita recipe below.

Detailed Fajita Recipe (serves 4):
Marinade: 1 lb chicken breast strips + 1/2 cup lime juice + 1/4 cup olive oil + 2 cloves garlic + 1 tsp oregano + 1/2 tsp cumin + 1/4 tsp paprika + salt/pepper. Marinate 30-120 min.
Cooking: Sauté chicken 5-7 min until done. Remove to plate. Sauté 1 onion + 2 bell peppers 5-7 min until charred. Combine with chicken.
Serving: Warm 8 tortillas. Top with chicken mixture and desired toppings (avocado, sour cream, salsa, cheese, cilantro, sumac).

Ingredient Tips:
- Use smoked paprika for deeper flavor, sweet paprika for mild
- Sumac adds tangy flavor and red color - sprinkle on chicken or veggies during cooking
- For extra kick add jalapeños/serrano peppers to the skillet
- Make lime crema: mix sour cream + lime juice + salt
- Make avocado salsa: mash avocados + lime juice + salt + red pepper flakes
