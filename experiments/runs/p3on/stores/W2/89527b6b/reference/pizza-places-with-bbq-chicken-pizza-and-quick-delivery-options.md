## Recommended Pizza Chains with BBQ Chicken Options

1. **California Pizza Kitchen (CPK)** - Features grilled chicken, red onion, cilantro, and BBQ sauce on crispy crust
2. **Mellow Mushroom** - Offers "Holy Shiitake Pie" with grilled chicken, shiitake mushrooms, and BBQ sauce; also has "BBQ Chicken" with tangy sauce and red onion
3. **Papa Murphy's** - Take-and-bake model; BBQ Chicken with grilled chicken, red onion, BBQ sauce (can be cooked at home for crispy crust)
4. **Marco's Pizza** - "BBQ Chicken" with grilled chicken, red onion, BBQ sauce on fresh never-frozen crust; also "Chicken Bacon BBQ" with crispy bacon and caramelized onions
5. **Jet's Pizza** - "BBQ Chicken" pizza on square-cut crust, known for flavorful sauce
6. **Pieology** - Fast-casual with customizable "BBQ Chicken" pizza on choice of crust

Also explore local independent pizzerias for unique BBQ chicken variations.
