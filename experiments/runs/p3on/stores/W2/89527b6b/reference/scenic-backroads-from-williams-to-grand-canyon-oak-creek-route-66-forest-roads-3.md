**Oak Creek Canyon Scenic Byway (Hwy 89A):** Paved, well-maintained but narrow/winding. Red rock formations.

**Old Route 66 (Hwy 180):** Historic Route 66 paralleling main highway. Passes through Valle and Tusayan. Narrower, more rustic than main highway.

**Forest Road 305 (FR 305):** Unpaved through Coconino National Forest. Stunning mountain and valley views. Rough in areas; high-clearance vehicle recommended.

**Babbit Ranch Road (FR 307):** Unpaved through historic Babbit Ranch ranching area. Scenic countryside views. Rough in areas; high-clearance vehicle recommended.

**Desert View Drive (Hwy 64):** Scenic paved drive through Kaibab National Forest. Leads to Desert View Watchtower.

**Precautions for all routes:**
- Check road conditions before leaving (weather affects accessibility)
- Full tank of gas, spare tire, emergency supplies
- Bring plenty of water, snacks, sun protection
- Limited services on these routes
- Respect private property and wildlife
- Follow all traffic laws and regulations
