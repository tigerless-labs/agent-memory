**Primary Route (most direct):**
Highway 64 East → Highway 89 North (toward Page) → Highway 163 North to Monument Valley. Total: 4-5 hours from Grand Canyon.

**Scenic Alternative (through Navajo Nation):**
Highway 64 → Highway 89 → Tuba City → Highway 160 East (to Kayenta) → Highway 163 North. Passes through heart of Navajo Nation with red rock formations and traditional dwellings. Includes Cameron Trading Post stop (historic, operating since 1916).

**Things to Do:**
- Guided tours with Navajo guides (recommended to learn history and culture)
- Scenic loop road through valley with popular viewpoints (Forrest Gump Point, The Mittens)
- Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park Visitor Center
- Photography of iconic rock formations

**Important:**
- Monument Valley is sacred Navajo land
- Be respectful of land and Navajo people
- Follow park rules and regulations
- Guided tours recommended for cultural understanding
- Plan full gas tank before entering area
- Bring water, snacks, sun protection
