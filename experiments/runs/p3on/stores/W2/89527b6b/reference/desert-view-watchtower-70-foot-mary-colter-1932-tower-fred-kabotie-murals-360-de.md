**Architecture & Design:**
- 70-foot tall (21 meters) stone tower
- Designed by architect Mary Colter in 1932
- Built by Fred Harvey Company
- Native American-inspired architecture
- Located at Desert View, South Rim of Grand Canyon

**Interior & Exhibits:**
- Museum with Native American art and cultural exhibits
- Murals and paintings by Hopi artist Fred Kabotie
- Displays on indigenous history and traditions

**Amenities:**
- Gift shop with Native American crafts, books, souvenirs
- Small café with coffee, snacks, sandwiches
- Restrooms (expect long lines during peak season)
- Ranger programs during peak season

**Views & Photography:**
- 360-degree views of Grand Canyon and Colorado River
- Ideal for sunset and sunrise photography
- Binoculars recommended for wildlife (condors, eagles, ravens)

**Accessibility:**
- Wheelchair accessible areas
- Observation deck not fully wheelchair accessible
- Accessible viewpoints on lower level

**Visitor logistics:**
- Parking can be limited; may use overflow lot with shuttle
- Plan for crowds during peak season
