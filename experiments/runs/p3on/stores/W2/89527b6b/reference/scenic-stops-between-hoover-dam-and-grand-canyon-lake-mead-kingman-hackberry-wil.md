**Lake Mead National Recreation Area:** Largest US reservoir with panoramic views. Lake Mead Overlook and Boulder Harbor (picnic tables, boat launch).

**Kingman, Arizona:** Charming Route 66 town. Route 66 Museum (learn Mother Road history), historic downtown area.

**Hackberry General Store:** Old-fashioned general store on Route 66. Great for stretching legs, photos, snacks.

**Valentine, Arizona:** Small historic town. Keepers of the Wild Nature Park and Sanctuary (wildlife sanctuary).

**Williams, Arizona:** 'Gateway to the Grand Canyon.' Historic downtown area with restaurants and shops. Williams Depot (former train station, now museum). Bill Williams Monument (north of town, commemorates mountain man/trapper).

**Oak Creek Canyon:** Stunning gorge with red rock formations. Oak Creek Vista Point (panoramic views). Slide Rock State Park (swimming, picnicking).

**Flagstaff, Arizona:** Charming college town. Lowell Observatory (world-renowned astronomical observatory).
