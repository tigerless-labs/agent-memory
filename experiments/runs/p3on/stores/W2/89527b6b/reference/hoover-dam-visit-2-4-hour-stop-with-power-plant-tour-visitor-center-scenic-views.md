**Why visit:** Iconic engineering marvel and National Historic Landmark. Built during Great Depression. Historic significance to American West development.

**What to do:**
- Hoover Dam Power Plant Tour (30 minutes): Guided tour with elevator descent into dam
- Visitor Center: Exhibits on history, construction, hydroelectric power generation
- Viewpoints: Arizona and Nevada sides, stunning Colorado River views
- Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge: Walk or viewpoint
- Riverwalk Trail: Hike down toward Colorado River

**Tips:**
- Plan 2-4 hours total
- Security screening required (metal detectors, bag checks)
- Parking can be limited; arrive early or use remote lot shuttle
- Very hot in summer; bring sun protection, water, snacks
- Limited food/snacks on-site; bring own to avoid lines
- Restrooms available
- Time zone change from Grand Canyon (Pacific vs Mountain)
