**Proposal Overview**: Decentralized supply chain solution using Cardano blockchain to improve transparency, traceability, and efficiency in Ghana's agricultural sector.

**Key Components**:
- Blockchain-based product tracking from farm to consumer
- Logistics platform with real-time tracking, invoicing, route optimization
- Smart contracts for cross-border payments and trade compliance
- Financial services integration (lending, insurance)

**Implementation Strategy**:
- Partnerships with local organizations and educational institutions
- Capacity building and training programs
- Integration with wider agricultural development strategy
- Infrastructure development component

**Expected Benefits**:
- Reduced fraud and increased product quality assurance
- Improved market access for farmers (local and international)
- Enhanced efficiency and lower transportation costs
- Greater financial inclusion for farmers and traders
- Community development and improved livelihoods
- Increased transparency and trust in supply chain

**Evaluation Metrics**:
- Number of farmers/traders using platform
- Volume of products traded
- Fraud reduction rates
- Product quality improvements
- Revenue increases for participants
- Scalability potential to other West African regions
