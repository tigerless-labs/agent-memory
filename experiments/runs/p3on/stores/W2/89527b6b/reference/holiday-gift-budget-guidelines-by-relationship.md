Budget guidelines recommended for holiday gift shopping:\n\n- Close family members (spouse, children, parents): 50 to 200\n- Extended family members (siblings, aunts, uncles, cousins): 20 to 100\n- Close friends: 20 to 50\n- Coworkers or acquaintances: 10 to 20\n\nRecommended budgeting tools: Mint (free), YNAB (6.99 per month or 83.99 per year), Google Sheets (free), or a custom Excel spreadsheet.
