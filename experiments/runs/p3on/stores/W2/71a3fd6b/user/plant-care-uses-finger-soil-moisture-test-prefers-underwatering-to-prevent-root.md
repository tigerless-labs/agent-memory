**Soil moisture testing method:**
- Insert finger 1-2 inches into soil
- Water only if soil feels dry at that depth
- If soil feels damp or wet, wait several more days

**Watering philosophy:**
- Better to err on side of underwatering than overwatering
- Most houseplants are more susceptible to damage from overwatering/root rot

**Typical watering schedules:**
- Snake plant: 7-10 days in summer (adjusted from every 4 days to prevent leaf drop)
- Aloe vera and other succulents: 1x weekly, with soil moisture test
- Generally: water thoroughly when needed, let top 1-2 inches of soil dry between waterings

**Other plant care interests:**
- Considering humidifiers for tropical plants (increases humidity in dry indoor environments)
- Proper repotting technique and timing (spring/early summer preferred)
- Using balanced, diluted fertilizers during growing season
