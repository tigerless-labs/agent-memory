Plan to address reduced egg production through nutrition:

**Primary feed:** Currently using layer pellets; committed to reviewing label to verify calcium and nutrient levels.

**Supplements to add:**
- **Oyster shells:** Free-choice crushed oyster shells (1-2 cups per 10 hens per week) for calcium carbonate to support eggshell production
- **Dark leafy greens:** Kale, spinach, collard greens as treats or mixed into feed
- **Plain yogurt:** Once per week mixed with feed for probiotics and gut health
- **Fermented grains:** Fermented oats for natural probiotics
- **Fruits and vegetables:** Apples (seeds/cores removed), carrots, blueberries, berries, sweet potatoes (cooked), pumpkin/squash (cooked), broccoli, cauliflower — as treats not exceeding 10% of daily diet

**Implementation notes:**
- Introduce new supplements gradually to prevent digestive upset
- Monitor flock behavior, droppings, and egg production to assess response
- Keep treats under 10% of total diet; maintain balanced nutrition
