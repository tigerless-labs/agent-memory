## Reading + Writing Reflection Habit Stack\n\n**Anchor Habit**: Reading for at least 20 minutes daily  \n**New Habit**: Writing book review or reflection for 5-10 minutes  \n**Stack**: After finishing 20-minute reading session, write reflection for 5-10 minutes in reading journal\n\n### Reflection Questions to Guide Writing\n- What were the main takeaways?\n- What resonated with me, and why?\n- What challenged my assumptions?\n- How can I apply what I learned to my life?\n- What actions will I take as a result?\n\n### Recommended Formats\n- Free writing without stopping\n- Structured reflection with intro/takeaways/applications/questions/conclusion\n- Mind mapping visual connections\n- Journal entry format\n- Top 5 takeaways list\n- Letter to author or character\n\n### Benefits\n- Enhanced retention and understanding\n- Develops critical thinking skills\n- Applies learning to personal life\n- Sense of accomplishment and accountability\n- Creative self-expression outlet
