**Transportation from Frankfurt to Speyer:**
- By car: ~1 hour 10 minutes via A67 and A61 highways (rental available at Frankfurt airport)
- By train: ~1 hour 20 minutes from Frankfurt am Main Hauptbahnhof to Speyer Hauptbahnhof (direct and indirect options throughout the day)
- By bus: ~1 hour 30 minutes (multiple bus operators available)
- By taxi: ~1 hour, expensive option
