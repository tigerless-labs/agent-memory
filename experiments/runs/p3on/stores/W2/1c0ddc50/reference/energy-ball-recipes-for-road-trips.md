Five energy ball recipes recommended for road trip snacks:

1. **Peanut Butter Banana Bites** — rolled oats, peanut butter, mashed banana, honey
2. **Chocolate Chip Energy Bites** — rolled oats, almond butter, chocolate chips, honey
3. **Coconut Lime Energy Balls** — rolled oats, coconut flakes, lime zest, honey
4. **Cranberry Orange Energy Balls** — rolled oats, dried cranberries, orange zest, honey
5. **Nutty Energy Bites** — rolled oats, peanut butter, chopped nuts (walnuts or almonds), honey

All recipes are customizable, require simple mixing, shaping into balls, and refrigerating until firm. Store in airtight container to keep fresh during travel.
