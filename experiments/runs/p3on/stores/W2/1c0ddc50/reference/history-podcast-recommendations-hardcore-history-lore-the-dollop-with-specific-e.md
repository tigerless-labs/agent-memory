**History Podcasts Recommended:**

1. **Hardcore History** by Dan Carlin
   - Start with: "The Wrath of the Khans" (Episode 1), "The American Peril" (Episode 5), "Ghosts of the Ostfront" (Episode 10)
   
2. **Lore** by Aaron Mahnke
   - Start with: "They Made a Tonic" (Episode 1), "The Bloody Pit" (Episode 5), "Echoes" (Episode 20)
   
3. **The Dollop** by Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds
   - Start with: "The Radium Girls" (Episode 255), "The Donner Party" (Episode 134), "The Astor Place Riot" (Episode 201)

**Listening advice:**
- Episodes are 30-60 minutes, good fit for 40-minute commutes
- Alternate between podcasts to avoid burnout
- Hardcore History often features longer multi-part series
