**Book:** Guns, Germs, and Steel by Jared Diamond

**Topic:** Explores how geography, technology, and biology shaped human societies over 13,000 years.

**Key concepts:**
- Three main factors: guns (military power), germs (diseases), steel (technology)
- Geography's role in societal development
- Technology drives progress
- Diseases shaped history
- The "lucky latitudes" (20-40° north) as regions conducive to complex civilizations
- Located in regions including Fertile Crescent, Indus Valley, Yellow River Valley, Mediterranean

**Awards:** Pulitzer Prize for General Nonfiction (1998)

**Reader:** User is particularly interested in the "lucky latitudes" concept and wants to understand it in detail.
