Research on 19th century Korea covering religious persecution and Western influence.

**Key Events:**
- 1866 Byeong-in Persecution: widespread government crackdown on Catholicism, thousands killed
- 1871 Shinmiyangyo: American sailors killed by Korean soldiers; first armed confrontation between US and Korea
- 1886 Gaeseong massacre: hundreds of Korean Catholics killed by government

**Key Figures:**
- Father Thomas Hur: French priest instrumental in spreading Catholicism in Korea; killed during massacre
- Kim Ok-gyun: prominent intellectual and government official critical of government persecution; exiled

**Context:** Western powers (US, France, Great Britain), China, and Japan competed for influence in Korea. Government saw Catholicism and Western influence as threats to Confucian order and state power. Despite persecution, Catholicism became one of Korea's largest religious communities by 20th century.
