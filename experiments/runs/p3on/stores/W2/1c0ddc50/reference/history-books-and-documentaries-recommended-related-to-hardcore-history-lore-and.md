**Books Recommended:**

*Hardcore History inspired:*
- "The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich" by William L. Shirer
- "Guns, Germs, and Steel" by Jared Diamond (Pulitzer Prize winner)
- "The Devil in the White City" by Erik Larson

*Lore inspired:*
- "The Witches: Salem, 1692" by Stacy Schiff (Pulitzer Prize winner)
- "The Devil's Gentleman" by Harold Schechter
- "The Ghost Map" by Steven Johnson

*The Dollop inspired:*
- "A People's History of the United States" by Howard Zinn
- "The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks" by Rebecca Skloot
- "Killers of the Flower Moon" by David Grann

**Documentaries Recommended:**

*Hardcore History inspired:*
- "The World at War" (1973)
- "The Apocalypse" (2000) - about the Mongols
- "Death and the Civil War" (2002) - PBS

*Lore inspired:*
- "The Salem Witch Trials" (2002) - History Channel
- "The Real Wolfman" (2012) - Smithsonian
- "The Dark Ages" (2007) - History Channel

*The Dollop inspired:*
- "The Untold History of the United States" (2012)
- "The True Story of the Bridge on the River Kwai" (2001)
- "The Men Who Built America" (2012) - History Channel
