Organizing a camping trip with adult siblings near parents' house (countryside location; aunt's house also nearby in same area).

Plan includes:
- Involve all siblings in planning process
- Bring scanned family photos and documents organized around storytelling theme: family reunions and vacations
- Organize camping cook-off where each sibling brings their favorite camping recipe
- Mix of activities (hiking, nature walks, campfire games like capture-the-flag/frisbee/badminton, stargazing, campfire stories and singing) and downtime
- Assign task responsibilities: setup, cooking, cleaning
- Optional tech-free bonding time
- Capture photos/memories for shared album/scrapbook afterward
- Display photos around campfire using portable album, tablet, or DIY stand
