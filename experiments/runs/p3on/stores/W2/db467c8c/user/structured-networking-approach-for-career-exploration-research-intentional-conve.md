Before events: research attendees, prepare open-ended questions, clarify intentions. During conversations: strong introduction, active listening, follow-up questions, share experiences. After: thank-you emails, LinkedIn connection, periodic check-ins with shared resources/articles, ask about follow-up conversations or meetings. Values authenticity, respect for people's time, and taking notes for later reference.
