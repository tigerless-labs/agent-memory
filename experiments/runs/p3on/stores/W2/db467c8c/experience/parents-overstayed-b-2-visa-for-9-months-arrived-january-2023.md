User's parents entered the U.S. on a B-2 tourist visa and have been staying with the user for nine months (approximately January–October 2023). B-2 visas typically allow 6-month stays; the 9-month overstay exceeds this limit.

**Concerns:**
- Inadmissibility due to unlawful presence
- Bars to re-entry
- Potential negative impact on user's green card application
- May require Form I-601 waiver of inadmissibility

**Plan:** Consult immigration attorney about addressing the overstay, potential waivers, and alternative options for parents' immigration status to minimize impact on user's green card application.
