**Waiver of Inadmissibility (Form I-601)** is used to overcome grounds of inadmissibility, particularly unlawful presence from visa overstay.

**Eligibility:**
- Unlawful presence for 180 days to 1 year
- Material misrepresentation or immigration benefit fraud

**Key requirements:**
- **Extreme hardship** to qualifying relative (U.S. citizen or permanent resident spouse, parent, or child)
- Demonstrate rehabilitation; show applicant no longer poses risk

**Process steps:**
1. File Form I-601 with USCIS with supporting documentation
2. Biometric services appointment (fingerprints, photos)
3. USCIS adjudication (several months)
4. Possible interview at USCIS office or U.S. embassy/consulate
5. Discretionary decision based on extreme hardship evidence

**Alternative:** Form I-601A (provisional waiver) may be available in family-based immigration cases

**Important notes:**
- Complex process; success depends heavily on strength of extreme hardship argument
- Consult experienced attorney for strategy and document preparation
- Attorney can help assess likelihood of success and explore alternative options
