**Junmai selection:** Premium sake with rich, full-bodied flavor. Pairs well with sushi and other Japanese dishes. Alternatives for future: Ginjo (light, crisp, floral/fruity) and Daiginjo (high-end, complex, elegant).\n\n**Sake Etiquette:**\n- Serve in small ceramic cups (ochoko or sakazuki)\n- Pour sake for guests, not yourself (sign of respect)\n- Receive with both hands (sign of respect)\n- Never leave cup empty\n- Pour with right hand only\n- Serve at room temperature or slightly warmed\n- Some premium sakes best served chilled\n- Store in refrigerator to preserve flavor\n- Consider traditional masu (wooden box) for festive touch
