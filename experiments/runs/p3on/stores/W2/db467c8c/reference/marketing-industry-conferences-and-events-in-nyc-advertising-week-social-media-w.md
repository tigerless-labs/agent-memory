Marketing conferences and events to monitor for networking and learning:

- **Advertising Week** (September): Premier event for advertising and marketing industry, keynote speakers, panels, networking
- **Social Media Week** (February): Conference focused on latest trends in social media marketing
- **Digital Marketing Summit**: Series of events focused on digital marketing, expert speakers, networking
- **NYC Marketing Week** (October): Week-long event featuring marketing conferences, workshops, networking
- **MozCon**: Leading SEO and digital marketing conference with expert speakers, networking opportunities

Also consider: NYC Marketing Professionals meetup group, American Marketing Association (AMA) - NYC chapter, The Marketing Society global network.

Search platforms: Eventbrite, Lanyrd, Meetup for additional events.
