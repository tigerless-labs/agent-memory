**Professional directories for immigration attorneys:**

- **AILA (American Immigration Lawyers Association):** www.aila.org - search 'Find an Immigration Lawyer'; look for State Bar certified Immigration Law Specialists
- **National Immigration Project:** www.nationalimmigrationproject.org - directory of National Lawyers Guild member attorneys
- **Immigration Advocates Network (IAN):** www.immigrationadvocates.org - 'Find an Immigration Attorney' tool
- **Avvo:** www.avvo.com - ratings by experience, client reviews, peer endorsements
- **Martindale-Hubbell:** www.martindale.com - ratings and reviews by practice area

**Other approaches:**
- Personal referrals from friends, family, coworkers
- Online reviews (Yelp, Google Reviews, Facebook)

**When evaluating:**
- Verify state bar license and immigration law experience
- Ask about success rate with green card cases
- Inquire about experience with visa overstay situations
- Most offer free initial consultations
- Watch for red flags: guarantees of success, unrealistic promises, lack of transparency on credentials/experience
