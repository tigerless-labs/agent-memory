Visitors to Barcelona can see a range of art and heritage displays:

**Major sites:**
- **Sagrada Familia cathedral** — designed by Antoni Gaudí
- **Park Güell** — designed by Gaudí
- **Street art and murals** — found in El Raval and Poble Nou neighborhoods
- **La Rambla** — lined with historic buildings, statues, and fountains
- **Plaça de Catalunya** — historic plaza with cultural significance

The city is known for its vibrant cultural heritage and flourishing arts scene reflected throughout its public spaces. Work ranges from contemporary to historical, cultural to abstract, representing the city's identity and natural surroundings.
