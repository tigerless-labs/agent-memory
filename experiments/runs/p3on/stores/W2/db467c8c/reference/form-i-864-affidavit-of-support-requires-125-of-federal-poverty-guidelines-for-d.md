**Form I-864: Affidavit of Support**

When applying for a green card with dependents (including parents), you must complete Form I-864, which is a legally binding contract between you and the US government.

**Key Requirements:**
- Demonstrate income at least 125% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines (FPG)
- Include all dependent household members' expenses (food, housing, utilities, health insurance, medical expenses)
- If self-employed or unable to meet income alone, can use joint sponsors

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
1. Inaccurate or incomplete information across forms
2. Insufficient or missing documentation
3. Inconsistent information between applicant and sponsor
4. Failure to disclose criminal history
5. Not understanding eligibility for your category
6. Failure to respond to Requests for Evidence (RFEs)
7. Missing interview appointments or deadlines
8. Not preparing adequately for interviews

**Processing Timelines (general estimates):**
- Immediate Relative Petitions: 5-10 months
- Family-Based Petitions: 6-12 months  
- Employment-Based Petitions: 6-12 months

**Application Steps After Submission:**
1. USCIS receipt notice with case number
2. Background checks
3. Biometric services appointment (fingerprints, photo, signature)
4. Possible Request for Evidence (RFE) if additional documents needed
5. Medical examination by designated civil surgeon
6. Interview (if required)
7. Approval and oath ceremony
8. Green card received
