West Village coffee shop recommendations for NYC revisit:

- **La Colombe** (275 Bleeker St): Popular spot with beautiful outdoor seating, great for people-watching
- **Joe Coffee** (141 W 4th St): One of the original Joe Coffee locations, cozy atmosphere, expertly brewed coffee
- **Think Coffee** (250 Bleecker St): Socially responsible coffee shop, high-quality coffee and healthy food
- **Culture Espresso** (72 Greenwich Ave): Specialty coffee with high-end, small-batch beans, beautiful atmosphere
- **Grove Cafe** (137 7th Ave S): Charming cafe with cozy atmosphere, coffee, tea, and healthy food
