**Maki Rolls:**\n- Use nori sheets designed for sushi\n- Spread thin layer of sushi rice on nori, leaving 1-inch border at top\n- Place fillings in middle of rice\n- Roll using sushi mat or parchment paper with gentle pressure\n- Refrigerate at least 30 minutes to set\n- Slice into 8 equal pieces using sharp knife with gentle sawing motion\n\n**Nigiri:**\n- Shape small sushi rice balls (about 1 inch diameter)\n- Place fish or topping on rice\n- Gently press topping onto rice to secure\n- Serve immediately or refrigerate up to 2 hours\n\n**Serving Tips:**\n- Garnish with thinly sliced daikon, shiso leaves, or pickled ginger\n- Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger on the side\n- Serve immediately after assembly for best flavor and texture\n- Label creations with small flags or cards\n- Handle gently to avoid damaging\n- Keep refrigerated until serving
