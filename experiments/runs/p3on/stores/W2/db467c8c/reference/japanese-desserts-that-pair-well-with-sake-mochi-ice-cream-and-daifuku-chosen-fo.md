**Chosen for dinner party:** \n- Mochi Ice Cream (popular, creamy texture, subtle sweetness)\n- Daifuku (glutinous rice cake filled with sweet bean paste or fruit)\n\n**Other Japanese dessert options:**\n- Manju: steamed bun with sweet bean paste\n- Castella: traditional sponge cake (light, fluffy)\n- Yokan: jellied red bean paste dessert (palate cleanser)\n- Sakura Mochi Cake: modern twist with cherry blossom flavors\n- Green Tea Crème Brûlée: Japanese-inspired classic\n- Yuzu Panna Cotta: infused with yuzu citrus\n\n**Serving tips:**\n- Serve small portions to balance sweetness and richness with sake\n- Use traditional Japanese servingware (ceramic or wooden)\n- Garnish with seasonal ingredients (cherry blossoms, autumn leaves)
