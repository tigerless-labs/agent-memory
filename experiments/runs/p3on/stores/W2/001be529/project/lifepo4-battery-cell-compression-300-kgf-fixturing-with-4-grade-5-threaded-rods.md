**Cell specifications:**
- LiFePO4 battery cell (rectangular)
- Dimensions: 205 mm (height) × 174 mm (width) × 72 mm (thickness)

**Compression setup:**
- Total fixturing force: 300 kgf
- Compression applied to two largest faces (205 mm × 174 mm)
- Steel compression plates: 205 mm × 174 mm
- Four Grade 5 steel 1/4" threaded rods (UNC, 20 TPI)
- Rods installed at each corner, running straight through connecting opposite plates

**Calculated design parameters:**
- Force per rod: 37.5 kgf
- Pressure per face: ~41,234.5 Pa
- Required torque per rod: 17.27 ft-lbs
- Mechanical advantage (1/4" UNC): 15.7
