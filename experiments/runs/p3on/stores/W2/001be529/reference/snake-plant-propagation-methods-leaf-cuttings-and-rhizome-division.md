**Method 1: Leaf Cuttings**
1. Choose healthy mature leaf with long petiole
2. Cut 1-2 inches from base using sharp, clean knife
3. Remove lower leaves, leaving top 2-3 inches
4. Root in water or well-draining potting mix (roots develop 1-2 weeks)
5. Transplant to larger pot once rooted

**Method 2: Rhizome Division**
1. Choose mature plant with well-developed rhizome
2. Dig up entire plant and separate rhizome into sections
3. Ensure each section has at least one growing point
4. Replant divided sections in individual pots with well-draining mix

**Tips:**
- Use sharp, sterile tools for clean cuts
- Keep area bright, indirectly lit, 65-75°F
- Water sparingly until roots develop (prevent root rot)
- Can propagate anytime, but spring/summer optimal
- Snake plants are very easy to propagate
