## Must-see attractions

- **The Strip District**: Lively neighborhood known for street food, ethnic markets, unique shops. Try Primanti Bros. sandwiches (famous local sandwich).
- **Andy Warhol Museum**: Largest museum in US dedicated to single artist; great for art enthusiasts.
- **Mount Washington**: Take Duquesne Incline or Monongahela Incline for breathtaking city skyline and three-rivers views.
- **Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens**: Stunning gardens, greenhouses, art installations.

## Other attractions

- Carnegie Science Center: Interactive exhibits, planetarium, submarine tour
- PNC Park: One of best ballparks in country, on North Shore with great restaurants
- Three Rivers Heritage Trail: 24-mile scenic trail system

## Neighborhoods to explore

- Oakland: Home to University of Pittsburgh, mid-range hotels/Airbnbs $80–120/night
- Shadyside: Shopping and restaurants on Walnut Street and Ellsworth Avenue, $100–150/night

## Getting around

- Public transit: Port Authority buses/light rail, Day Pass $5.50
- Free Transit Zone: Downtown light rail (T) and buses are free
- Healthy Ride: Bike-share system
- Walkable neighborhoods; good for biking
- Ride-hailing: Uber/Lyft widely available

## Craft breweries

- Penn Brewery
- East End Brewing Company  
- Grist House Brewing
