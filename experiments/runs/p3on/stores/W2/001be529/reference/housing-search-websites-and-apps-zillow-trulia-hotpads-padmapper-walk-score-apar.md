Housing rental search resources recommended for affordable apartment hunting:\n\n**Websites:**\n- **Zillow** — Popular real estate site with rental filters by price/location/amenities, Rent Zestimate feature\n- **Trulia** — Similar to Zillow, includes crime maps and neighborhood insights\n- **HotPads** — Map-based search with Commute Time feature showing travel time to specific locations\n- **PadMapper** — Aggregates listings from multiple sources including Craigslist, includes Safety Score for neighborhoods\n- **Walk Score** — Evaluates walkability and public transit access of neighborhoods/addresses\n- **Apartments.com** — Comprehensive rental search with Ratings & Reviews from residents\n- **Rent Jungle** — Aggregates listings, includes Neighborhood Guide feature\n\n**Mobile Apps:**\n- Zillow Rentals (iOS/Android)\n- Trulia Rentals (iOS/Android)\n- PadMapper (iOS/Android) — emphasizes map-based searching\n\n**Currently Using:**\n- Zillow (primary)\n- HotPads (primary)\n\n**Search Tips:**\n- Filter by 800/month budget\n- Use location filters for public transit proximity\n- Read resident reviews before applying\n- Contact landlords directly to ask questions and schedule viewings\n- Stay organized and take notes
