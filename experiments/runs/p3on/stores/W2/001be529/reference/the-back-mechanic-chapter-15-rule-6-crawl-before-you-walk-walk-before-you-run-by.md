## Core Message
Progressive learning: master each movement level before advancing. Healing cannot be rushed; it has its own rhythm.

## Key Movements
1. Marching in place with neutral spine (crawl)
2. Walking with neutral spine (walk) - swing arms from shoulders, opposite to leg
3. Hip-hinging - bend forward from hips, not spine; keep lower back protected
4. Full-body rotation - chest, navel, hips move together; no spine twisting
5. Torso rotation with hip-hinge and squat - combined 3D movement for everyday tasks

## Safety Protocol
Return to previous pain-free level if any movement causes pain. Build gradually through small movements → increased range over time. Movements must become automatic habits.

## Core Principle
Daily exercises are more important than anything else for permanent pain reduction and building strength to support the spine during movement.
