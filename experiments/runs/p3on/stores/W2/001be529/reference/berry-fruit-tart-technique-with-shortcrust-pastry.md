Key techniques for preventing soggy crust: blind bake shortcrust pastry for 10-12 min until lightly golden before adding fruit; use hot oven (400F) to crisp crust quickly; don't overfill tart, leave border without fruit.

Preparing berries: choose firm berries like raspberries, blueberries, blackberries rather than soft strawberries; pat dry with paper towel; toss with 1-2 tbsp sugar per cup berries and 1/4 tsp cornstarch per cup; let sit 15-30 minutes to allow cornstarch to absorb excess juice; gently strain through sieve before arranging on tart.

Serving suggestions: top with whipped cream flavored with vanilla bean, lemon zest, or honey; or vanilla ice cream with lavender, bourbon, mint, or candied ginger.
