**Lighting:** Bright indirect light. East or west-facing window ideal. Avoid direct sunlight (causes scorching and fading).

**Watering:** Water when potting medium (not plant) feels dry. Generally once a week, or every 10-14 days. Over-watering is most common cause of death.

**Humidity:** Thrives at 40-70% relative humidity. Increase with water trays with pebbles, humidifiers, or grouping plants together. Aim for slightly higher than average home humidity.

**Temperature:** Most orchids prefer:
- Daytime: 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- Nighttime: 5-10°F (3-6°C) lower
- Phalaenopsis tolerate warmer; Cattleya prefer cooler

**Fertilizing:** Feed monthly with balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) or orchid-specific formula.

**Potting Medium:** Use sphagnum moss, fir bark, or bark/sphagnum/perlite mix. Must be well-draining.

**Pest Control:** Check regularly for mealybugs, spider mites, scale. Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if found.

**Rest Period:** Some species (like Phalaenopsis) need cooler temps (~50°F/10°C) and reduced watering to induce blooming.

**Pruning:** After blooming, cut flower spike 1 inch above emergence node to encourage new bloom. Trim dead/dying leaves and stems.
