**Phalaenopsis (Moth Orchid)** — Most common, adaptable, small, thrives in various lighting conditions.

**Dendrobium** — Long-lasting flowers, low-maintenance, hardy, tolerates some neglect.

**Oncidium (Dancing Lady Orchid)** — Small, easy care, bright showy flowers, forgiving of inconsistent watering.

**Cattleya** — Large showy flowers, relatively easy, prefers bright indirect light and moderate watering.

**Paphiopedilum (Venus Slipper Orchid)** — Unique slipper-shaped flowers, low-maintenance, prefers bright indirect light and consistent watering.

Choose pots around 4-6 inches for easier care. Look for species adaptable to home conditions without extreme temperature, humidity, or light requirements.
