**When to Repot:** Every 2-3 years when potting medium breaks down, when plant outgrows pot, or shows nutrient deficiency signs.

**Pot Selection:** Choose slightly larger than previous (orchids prefer slight root-bound). Must have good drainage holes.

**Potting Medium:** Use sphagnum moss, fir bark, or mix of bark/sphagnum/perlite. Avoid regular potting soil (retains too much water).

**Repotting Steps:**
1. Best done in morning to allow recovery
2. Gently remove plant from pot (rock gently if stuck)
3. Inspect roots—white = healthy, trim dead/rotting ones with sterile scissors
4. Remove old potting medium from roots and rhizome
5. Place small amount new medium in pot bottom
6. Position orchid with rhizome above medium, roots spread evenly
7. Fill sides with medium, gently firm as you go
8. Water thoroughly to settle medium

**Key Tips:**
- Handle roots gently—easily damaged
- Don't bury rhizome too deeply (causes rot)
- Don't over-pot (leads to root rot)
- Monitor after repotting, adjust watering/fertilizing as needed
