## Recommended restaurants in Chicago (city center/Loop area):\n\n**Classic Chicago:** Portillo's (Chicago-style hot dogs, Italian beef), Lou Mitchell's (comfort food, breakfast since 1923), Gene & Georgetti (Italian steakhouse)\n\n**Trendy:** Girl & the Goat (Stephanie Izard, Mediterranean), Au Chavel (burgers), The Purple Pig (small plates, cocktails)\n\n**Fine Dining:** Alinea (Michelin 3-star), Next (Michelin 3-star), Gibsons Italia\n\n**Casual:** Xoco (Mexican), Eataly (Italian market)\n\n**Breakfast/Brunch:** The Original Pancake House, Wildberry Cafe, Maison Parisienne\n\n## Museums of interest:\n- Field Museum (ancient Egyptian collection, Sue the T-rex)\n- Museum of Contemporary Art (MCA)\n- Art Institute of Chicago (visited previously, loved Impressionist/Post-Impressionist collection)
