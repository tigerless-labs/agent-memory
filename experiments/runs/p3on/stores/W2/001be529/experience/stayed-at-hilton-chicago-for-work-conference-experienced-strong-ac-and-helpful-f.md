User attended a work conference in Chicago and stayed at the Hilton Chicago (city center location). Positive notes: Front desk staff were very helpful. Negative notes: Room had strong air conditioning that needed adjustment every night. User forgot charger in room after checkout, and front desk held it until user returned to pick it up. Location was convenient for exploring the area on foot.
