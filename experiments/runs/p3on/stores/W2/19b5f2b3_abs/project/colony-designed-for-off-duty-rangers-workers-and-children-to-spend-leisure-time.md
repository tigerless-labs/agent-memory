The colony's structure and layout intentionally encourage residents (off-duty rangers, workers after shifts, children after school) to spend the majority of their leisure time engaged in active lifestyles and socialization within the community rather than remaining at home. Supported by numerous amenities: sports facilities, recreational centers, parks, cultural institutions. Easily accessible by walking, cycling, or people mover. Regular events (festivals, performances, community gatherings) draw people together. This design approach builds strong social bonds, fosters belonging, and contributes to physical and mental well-being.
