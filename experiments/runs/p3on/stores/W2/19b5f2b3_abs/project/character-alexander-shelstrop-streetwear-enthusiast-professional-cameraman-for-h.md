**Alexander Shelstrop** is a streetwear enthusiast and professional cameraman for Hollywood movies. He has a chaotic, devil-may-care attitude about nearly everything except his work (both professional as a cameraman and personal interests in aliens and cryptids). Primarily cares about the prosperity of himself and those close to him, regardless of broader impact. Values style over function - believes looking good means feeling good. Passionate about fashion and self-expression through clothing choices.
