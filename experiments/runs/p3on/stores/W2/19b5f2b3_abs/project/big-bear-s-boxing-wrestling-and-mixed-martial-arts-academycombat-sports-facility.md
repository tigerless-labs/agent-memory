A premier fitness and training facility founded by a renowned Kodian martial artist known as Big Bear. Offers classes in boxing, wrestling, and mixed martial arts for all skill levels. Features expert instruction, safety focus, workshops with guest instructors, in-house competitions, multiple training areas (boxing rings, wrestling mats, padded areas), fully equipped gym and weight room, locker rooms and shower facilities. Located near The Playing Field Multisport Arena.
