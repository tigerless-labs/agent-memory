The colony avoids importing or manufacturing highly processed foods. Instead, prioritizes fresh, locally sourced, and nutritious ingredients. Benefits: improved health and nutrition, reduced environmental impact from transportation, supports local economy and job creation, better taste, and increased food security through local reliance. This policy fosters a culture of healthy eating and creates a strong foundation for residents' physical and mental well-being.
