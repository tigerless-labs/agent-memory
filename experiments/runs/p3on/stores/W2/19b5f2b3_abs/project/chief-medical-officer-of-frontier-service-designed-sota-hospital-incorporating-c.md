The Chief Medical Officer (CMO) of the Frontier Service personally designed the state-of-the-art (SOTA) hospital in the colony, incorporating the colony's guiding principles of sustainability and well-being. Key features: energy efficiency (passive solar, geothermal, solar panels), green spaces (green roofs and walls), water management (greywater and blackwater recycling), sustainable materials (eco-friendly, locally sourced, recyclable), patient-centered design (spacious rooms, natural light, access to communal areas and green spaces), and holistic health approach (preventive care, mental health services, promotion of healthy lifestyles). The hospital exemplifies how healthcare can operate harmoniously within a sustainable, eco-conscious society.
