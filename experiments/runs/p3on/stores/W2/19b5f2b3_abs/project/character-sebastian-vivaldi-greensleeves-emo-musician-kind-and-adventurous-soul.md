**Sebastian Vivaldi Greensleeves** is an emo at heart musician with a kind and adventurous soul. His emotion-filled ballads and fierce riffs contrast sharply with his quiet, nervous everyday demeanor. He prioritizes people, always looking for ways to support friends through his music. Not physically strong, but loves harder than most. Uses music as his main outlet for self-expression and emotional expression. Initially more function-focused in style (comfortable clothes, stage 'armor'), but open to exploring more adventurous fashion choices.
