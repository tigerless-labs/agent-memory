Spent two weeks traveling solo around Japan, a few months before May 2023.

## Experiences
- Visited Fushimi Inari Shrine in Kyoto
- Visited an onsen (hot spring) in the mountains - found it amazing
- Stayed in small hostels and guesthouses, which was a great way to meet other travelers

## Preferences formed
- Prefers budget accommodations (hostels, guesthouses) for social connection with other travelers
- Enjoys onsen experiences and mountain settings
