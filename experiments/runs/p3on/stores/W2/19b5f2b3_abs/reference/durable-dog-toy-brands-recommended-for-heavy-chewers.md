Recommended brands and products for durable dog toys that withstand heavy chewing:

- **GoughNuts**: Made from proprietary rubber compound, extremely durable, resistant to chewing
- **Kong Wobbler**: Weighted rubber toy with unpredictable movement, treats dispense unpredictably
- **Nylabone DuraChew**: Made from tough nylon material, designed for aggressive chewers
- **West Paw Design Toys**: Made from Zogoflex material, extremely durable and resistant to chewing
- **Ever Pet Natural Rubber Toys**: Made from 100% natural rubber, durable and long-lasting
- **Outward Hound Brick Puzzle**: Durable plastic puzzle toy with adjustable difficulty levels
