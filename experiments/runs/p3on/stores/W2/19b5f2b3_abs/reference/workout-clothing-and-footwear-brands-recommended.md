Recommended brands for upgrading gym wardrobe:\n\n**Leggings/Athleisure:**\n- Lululemon (Align, Wunder Under)\n- Nike Pro\n- Athleta (Salutation, Elation)\n\n**Tops:**\n- Nike\n- Under Armour\n- Lululemon\n- Athleta\n- Champion\n- Hanes\n\n**Sports Bras:**\n- Nike\n- Under Armour\n- Lululemon (Enlite)\n- Athleta (Stash and Go)\n- Champion\n- Hanes\n\n**Running Shoes:**\n- Nike (Air Zoom Pegasus, Vaporfly)\n- Adidas (Ultraboost, Supernova)\n- Brooks (Ghost, Launch)\n\n**Cross-Training Shoes:**\n- Nike (Metcon, Vapormax)\n- Reebok (CrossFit Nano, Legacy)\n- Under Armour (Micro G Pursuit, HOVR Phantom)\n\nLook for: good arch support, cushioning, breathability, moisture-wicking fabrics
