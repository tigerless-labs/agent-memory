## Must-try Korean dishes
- Bibimbap: mixed rice bowl with vegetables, meat, fried egg
- Jeyuk bokkeum: stir-fried pork with vegetables and kimchi
- Bulgogi: thinly sliced marinated beef, grilled
- Kimchi Stew: spicy stew with kimchi and pork
- Tteokbokki: chewy rice cakes in sweet and spicy sauce
- Naengmyeon: cold noodles with spicy sauce
- Mandu: Korean dumplings (steamed, fried, or boiled)

## Recommended restaurants and markets in Seoul
- Gwangjang Market: famous for bindaetteok and makgeolli
- Myeong-dong Kyoja: affordable naengmyeon and Korean dishes
- Tosokchon Samgyetang: ginseng chicken soup
- Jinju Hoegwan: traditional Korean dishes
- Bicena: modern Korean bistro with creative dishes
- Myeong-dong Night Market: street food
- Hongdae Food Street: variety of food and cafes

## Tips
- Eat at local markets for authentic Korean cuisine
- Learn basic Korean phrases
- Be prepared for spicy dishes
