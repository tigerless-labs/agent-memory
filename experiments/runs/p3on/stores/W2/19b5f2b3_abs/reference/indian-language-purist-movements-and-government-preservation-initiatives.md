## Language Purist Movements

- **Tamil Purists Movement** — advocates for pure Tamil words, discourages foreign loanwords
- **Hindi Rakshak Samiti** (Hindi Protection Committee) — for Hindi preservation  
- **Bangla Bhasha Bachao Samiti** (Save Bengali Language Committee) — for Bengali preservation
- Similar movements exist for Kannada, Telugu, Marathi, and Gujarati

## Government Initiatives (India)

- **Tamil Virtual University** — offers courses on classical Tamil literature and language
- **Tamil Nadu government mandates** Tamil in official communication and public spaces
- **National Translation Mission** — promotes native and classical languages
- **Central Institute of Indian Languages** — documents and preserves regional/cultural diversity

## Key Challenges

- Widespread English use in education, media, administration
- Speaker unfamiliarity with classical/pure forms among educated/urban populations
- Risk of excluding speakers of dialects or non-standard varieties
- Tension between purity and linguistic inclusion/diversity
