Common English loanwords in contemporary Tamil:
- computer
- telephone
- coffee
- pencil
- camera

Technical/scientific terms borrowed from Sanskrit; religious, legal, and administrative terms from Urdu. These borrowings have become naturalized into contemporary Tamil vocabulary despite purist movement efforts.
