Services and platforms for finding dog walkers and pet sitters:

- **Rover.com**: Popular platform connecting pet owners with local dog walkers, sitters, and boarding services. Allows search by area, reading reviews, and scheduling meet-and-greets
- **Care.com**: Well-known platform offering pet care services including dog walking and sitting
- **National Association of Professional Pet Sitters (NAPPS)**: Professional organization with database of certified pet sitters searchable by location
- **Pet Sitters International (PSI)**: Professional organization with directory of member pet sitters
- **Google Search**: Local search for 'dog walkers near me' or 'dog sitters in [city]'
- **Word of Mouth**: Personal referrals from friends, family, or neighbors

When interviewing potential walkers/sitters: ask for references, verify experience with dogs similar to your dog's breed/size/temperament, and conduct a meet-and-greet before booking.
