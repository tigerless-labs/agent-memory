Recommended restaurants and cafes in Nikko area serving local specialties:\n\n**Senjogahara Rest House**: Near trailhead, grilled trout and yuba, has luggage storage (¥500-¥1,000/day), souvenir shop and picnic areas\n\n**Lake Chuzenji Restaurant**: Near the lake with mountain views, grilled trout, yuba, freshwater fish\n\n**Kegon Falls Restaurant**: Near Kegon Falls trailhead, grilled trout, yuba, soba noodles, souvenir shop and picnic areas\n\n**Yuba no Sato**: Chuzenji town, specializes in yuba dishes (soups, salads, hot pots)\n\n**Tofuya Ukai**: Chuzenji town, tofu dishes including yuba, vegetarian/vegan options\n\n**Luggage storage**: Lake Chuzenji Bus Terminal (¥500-¥1,000/day), Nikko Chuzenji Tourist Information Center (free, limited space), Odai Bus Terminal coin lockers (¥300-¥500/day)
