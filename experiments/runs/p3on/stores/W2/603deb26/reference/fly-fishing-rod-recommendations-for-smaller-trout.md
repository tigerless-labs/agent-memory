Recommended rods for trout: Orvis Clearwater, Sage Igniter, Winston Pure, Redington Crux, TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series. Look for 2-5 weight rods, 9-foot or 7-8 feet length. Graphite rods are lighter and more sensitive than fiberglass. Try rods at a local fly shop before purchasing.
