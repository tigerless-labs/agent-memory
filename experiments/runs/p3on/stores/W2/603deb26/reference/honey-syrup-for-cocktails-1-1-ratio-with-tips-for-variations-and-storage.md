Basic Honey Syrup: 1:1 ratio honey to water (classic starting point).

Instructions: Combine honey and water in saucepan. Heat over low heat, whisking until honey dissolves. Do not boil or burn. Cool before using. Store in fridge up to 2 weeks.

Honey Types to Try: Clover honey, manuka honey, acacia honey.

Variations:
- Infuse with citrus peel, herbs, or spices during dissolving
- Honey-ginger syrup: add sliced ginger during dissolving
- Adjust ratio to customize sweetness level
- Different honeys create distinct flavor profiles

Best for cocktails like Bee's Knees and Pimm's Cup.
