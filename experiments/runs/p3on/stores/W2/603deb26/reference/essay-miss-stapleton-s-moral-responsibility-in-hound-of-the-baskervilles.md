Five-paragraph essay analyzing whether Miss Stapleton should be held responsible for crimes in *The Hound of the Baskervilles*.

**Conclusion:** Miss Stapleton should be held responsible but punishment should be tempered by circumstance. She was an active accomplice (lured Sir Henry into danger, helped brother) but also a victim of an abusive, controlling brother with few escape options. Her actions were motivated by desperation rather than malice.

**Recommended approach:** Charges could range from conspiracy to attempted murder depending on severity of her role, but punishment should prioritize reform and rehabilitation rather than retribution.
