Pimm's Cup: Made with Pimm's No. 1 (gin-based liqueur), lemonade, garnished with cucumber, mint, and fruit. User interested in experimenting.

Bee's Knees: Gin, honey syrup, fresh lemon juice. Prohibition-era cocktail. User loves gin and honey combination.

Gin Rickey: Simple refreshing cocktail with gin, lime juice, soda water.

Gin-Gin Mule: Twist on Moscow Mule with gin, lime juice, ginger beer in copper mug.

Aviation: Gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, lemon juice.

Last Word: Gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, fresh lime juice.

Other classics: Classic Gin Martini (2:1 or 3:1 gin to dry vermouth with olive or lemon twist), French 75 (gin, lemon juice, sugar, champagne), Corpse Reviver No. 2 (gin, Lillet Blanc, Cynar, Cointreau, absinthe).
