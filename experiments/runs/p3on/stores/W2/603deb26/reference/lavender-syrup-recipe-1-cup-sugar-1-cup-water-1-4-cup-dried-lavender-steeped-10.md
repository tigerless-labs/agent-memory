# Lavender Syrup Recipe for Cocktails

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds

**Instructions:**
1. Combine sugar and water in a saucepan and heat, stirring until sugar dissolves
2. Add dried lavender buds and remove from heat
3. Let steep for 10-15 minutes until mixture cools to room temperature
4. Strain through fine-mesh sieve into clean glass bottle; discard lavender buds
5. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks

**Suggested cocktails:**
- Lavender Gin Fizz
- Lavender Dream
- Lavender Bee's Knees
