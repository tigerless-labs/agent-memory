Cucumber-Infused Gin: 1-2 cups sliced cucumber (English or hothouse, thin-skinned) to 1 liter gin. Infuse 2-5 days in cool dark place. Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter.

Mint-Infused Gin: 0.25-1 cup fresh mint leaves (peppermint or spearmint) to 1 liter gin. Infuse 2-5 days in cool dark place. Strain through cheesecloth.

Lavender-Infused Gin: 0.25 cup dried lavender buds to 1 liter gin. Infuse 2-3 days in cool dark place. Strain and filter.

Grapefruit-Infused Gin: 1-2 sliced grapefruits to 1 liter gin. Infuse 2-3 days in cool dark place. Strain and filter.

General tips: Use neutral spirits (gin, vodka, rum). Start with 1:4 ratio of flavor to spirit. Taste regularly to avoid over-infusion. Store in cool dark place. Infuse separately then combine to control intensity.
