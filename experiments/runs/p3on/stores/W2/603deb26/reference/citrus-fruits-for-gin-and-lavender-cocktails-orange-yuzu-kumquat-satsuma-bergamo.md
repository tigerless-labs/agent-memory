# Citrus Pairings for Gin and Lavender Cocktails

**Orange**
- Sweet complement to lavender, bitterness balances gin
- Tip: Use blood oranges for deeper, more complex flavor

**Yuzu**
- Japanese citrus (cross between grapefruit and orange)
- Flavor: tart and subtly sweet
- Adds unique, exotic flavor that sets cocktails apart

**Kumquat**
- Small, sweet citrus fruits
- Particularly well-suited to pairing with lavender
- Try kumquat syrup or muddle fresh kumquats

**Satsuma**
- Type of mandarin orange, sweet and tart
- Easy-to-peel skin
- Good for bursts of citrus flavor; sweetness helps balance gin bitterness

**Bergamot**
- Often used in tea and perfumery
- Subtle citrus flavor adds complexity
- Helps cut through the richness of the gin

**Working Tips:**
- Use fresh citrus for brightest, most vibrant flavor
- Experiment with different varieties to find perfect pairings
- Balance citrus amounts—it can quickly overpower other flavors
- Start with small amounts and adjust to taste
- Consider using citrus peels/twists for aroma and added depth
