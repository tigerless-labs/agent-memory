# Negroni Bitter Liqueur Alternatives

**Cynar**
- Italian bitter from artichokes
- Flavor: vegetal, slightly sweet
- Ratio: 1/2 oz Cynar + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica

**Aperol**
- Italian bitter, lighter than Campari
- Flavor: sweeter, more orange-forward
- Ratio: 1 oz Aperol + 1 oz Carpano Antica

**Luxardo Bitter**
- Italian bitter, intense
- Flavor: herbal, bold and complex
- Ratio: 1/2 oz Luxardo + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica

**Suze**
- French gentian-based bitter
- Flavor: crisp, earthy, herbaceous
- Ratio: 1 oz Suze + 1 oz Carpano Antica

**Meletti**
- Italian bitter, slightly sweet
- Flavor: citrus-forward, smooth
- Ratio: 1 oz Meletti + 1 oz Carpano Antica

**Note:** May need to adjust bitter-to-sweet vermouth ratio depending on which liqueur is used to achieve desired balance.
