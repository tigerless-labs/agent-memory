# White Negroni Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 oz Hendrick's gin
- 1/2 oz Suze (or other dry bitter liqueur like Luxardo Bitter)
- 1/2 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth
- Orange or lemon twist for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Fill mixing glass with ice
2. Add gin, Suze, and Dolin Dry Vermouth
3. Stir for 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute
4. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass
5. Garnish with orange or lemon twist

**Balance Tips:**
- Use dry bitter liqueurs (Suze, Luxardo Bitter) to avoid sweetness
- Choose dry vermouth to keep the drink dry
- Adjust ratio (2:1:1 or 3:1:1) based on preference
- Hendrick's floral and herbal notes pair well with Suze's crisp herbaceous character
- Don't over-dilute—stir just long enough to chill and dilute while preserving flavors
- This variation showcases the gin and bitter liqueur better than the classic Negroni
