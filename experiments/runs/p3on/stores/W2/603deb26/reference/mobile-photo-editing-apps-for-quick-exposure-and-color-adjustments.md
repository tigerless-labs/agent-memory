Mobile photo editing apps recommended for quick adjustments before sharing on social media:

**Free/Freemium:**
- Lightroom Mobile (iOS, Android): Adobe app, syncs with desktop Lightroom. More limited than desktop version, lacks Tone Curve, Split Toning, advanced local adjustments.
- Snapseed (iOS, Android): Google's professional-grade app with selective adjustments, layers, masking.
- Adobe Photoshop Express (iOS, Android): Quick editing for exposure, color, contrast, filters, effects.
- VSCO (iOS, Android): Film presets, exposure, contrast, color adjustments, filters.
- PicsArt (iOS, Android): Exposure, color, contrast, filters, effects, text overlays.
- Polarr (iOS, Android): AI-powered exposure, color, contrast adjustments.

**Paid:**
- Filterstorm (iOS): Curves, layers, masking. Price: 2.99 USD
- Afterlight (iOS, Android): Filters, textures, frames, basic edits. Price: 2.99 USD (iOS), 1.99 USD (Android)
- TouchRetouch (iOS, Android): Remove unwanted objects. Price: 1.99 USD (iOS), 2.99 USD (Android)
- Facetune (iOS, Android): Portrait retouching. Price: 3.99 USD (iOS), 2.99 USD (Android)
