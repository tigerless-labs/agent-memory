**Dinner Party Details:**
- 8-10 guests
- Vegetarian guests + 1 gluten-free guest to accommodate

**Menu:**

**Appetizer:**
- Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus
- Garnish: fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint)

**Main Course:**
- Sushi rolls with vegetarian options: cucumber, avocado, grilled portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura bits
- Lemon-ginger dipping sauce
- Garnish: thinly sliced lemons, edible flowers (violas, pansies)
- Lemon-herb quinoa with toasted pine nuts
- Gluten-free naan/flatbread option

**Dessert:**
- Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available)
- Fresh fruit platter with whipped cream or coconut cream

**Key Recommendations:**
- Roast sweet potato cubes for additional vegetarian sushi filling
- Marinate portobello mushrooms in soy sauce, sake, sugar before grilling
- Toast pine nuts and add fresh herbs to quinoa before serving
- Handle edible flowers gently
- Prep ingredients ahead (chopping, marinating, sauce-making)
- Cook dishes in batches to keep hot; allow 30-45 min between appetizer and main, 30-60 min between main and dessert
