**Meditation Apps:**\n- Headspace: Guided meditations for sleep (10 min), stress, focus. Has effective visualization components.\n- Calm: Sleep stories and relaxing music; guided meditations\n- Insight Timer: Free app with large library; Body Scan meditation available\n- Meditation Studio: Guided meditations with in-app purchases\n- Buddhify: Meditation for different times of day (one-time purchase)\n\n**Key Meditations:**\n- Headspace 'Sleep': 10 min, effective for relaxation and unwinding\n- Insight Timer 'Body Scan': Body awareness and tension release\n\n**Resources:**\n- Andrew Weil guided meditations (YouTube)\n- Rainy Mood: Rain sounds with meditation\n- Sleep With Me: Podcast with sleep meditations\n- UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center: Free guided meditations
