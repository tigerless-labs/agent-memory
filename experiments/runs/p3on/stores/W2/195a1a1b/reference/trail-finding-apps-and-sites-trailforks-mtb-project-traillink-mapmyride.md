Resources for discovering and tracking mountain biking trails:

- **Trailforks**: Trail search with difficulty ratings and tracking
- **MTB Project**: Trail database and mobile app
- **TrailLink**: Trail directory across regions
- **MapMyRide**: GPS tracking and route logging

Also check local bike shops for insider knowledge, national forests/parks for maintained trails, and IMBA Epic designations for must-ride trails.
