NGOs face multiple challenges when distributing aid in Africa:

- **Infrastructure**: Lack of roads, bridges, ports makes transport to remote areas difficult
- **Political instability**: Conflict and instability make operations unsafe and ineffective
- **Corruption**: Pervasive bribery and corruption in aid distribution
- **Language & cultural barriers**: Communication challenges with local communities
- **Limited resources**: Funding, personnel, and equipment constraints
- **High operational costs**: Staff, transport, and imported goods are expensive; limits aid capacity
- **Lack of reliable accountability systems**: Donors require transparent fund usage, but many NGOs lack systems to track and report activities; difficult to demonstrate impact and build trust; hard to ensure aid reaches intended recipients
