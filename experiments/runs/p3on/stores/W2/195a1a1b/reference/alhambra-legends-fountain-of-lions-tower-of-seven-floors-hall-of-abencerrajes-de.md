Four notable legends and myths associated with the Alhambra add to its mystique:

1. **Fountain of Lions**: Drinking from this fountain in the Palace of the Lions is said to make you fall in love with Granada and never want to leave.

2. **Tower of Seven Floors**: Allegedly haunted by the ghost of a princess imprisoned there by her father due to her forbidden love for a Christian knight.

3. **Hall of Abencerrajes**: Said to be the site of a massacre of the Abencerraje family; legend claims their bloodstains can still be seen on the walls.

4. **Devil's Megaphone**: A spot in the Generalife palace gardens with perfect acoustics where it's said the devil can be heard speaking through the echo.

These legends reflect the beliefs and values of the people who created them and contribute significantly to the cultural and historical heritage of the Alhambra.
