Top Granada landmarks offering panoramic views:

- **The Alhambra** (most popular): Ancient fortress/palace complex, best overall views of the city and Sierra Nevada Mountains
- **Mirador de San Nicolas**: Viewpoint on Albaicín hill with panoramic views of the Alhambra, city, and mountains
- **Sacromonte Abbey**: Hilltop monastery with panoramic views of Granada and surroundings
- **El Salvador Church**: Bell tower provides bird's eye view of Granada's city center
- **Albaicín Quarter**: Labyrinth of narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings with multiple vantage points for stunning views
