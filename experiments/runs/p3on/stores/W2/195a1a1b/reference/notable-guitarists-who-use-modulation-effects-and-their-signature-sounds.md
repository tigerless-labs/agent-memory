## Guitarists known for modulation effects:

- **David Gilmour** (Pink Floyd) — Uses chorus and phaser on classic solos like 'Comfortably Numb' and 'Shine on You Crazy Diamond'
- **Eddie Van Halen** — Uses flanger and phaser to create his signature 'brown sound' tone
- **The Edge** (U2) — Uses delay and chorus effects for his signature ethereal guitar sound
- **Andy Summers** (The Police) — Frequently uses chorus and flanger effects for complex, layered guitar parts
- **Jimi Hendrix** — Used uni-vibe pedal to create a phaser-like effect on songs like 'Machine Gun' and 'Star Spangled Banner'
