Key factors when selecting a mountain bike:

**Budget**: Range from hundreds to thousands; set range and stick to it

**Suspension Types**:
- Hardtail (front only): Good for smooth trails and cross-country
- Full-suspension (front and rear): Better for technical/rough terrain

**Wheel Sizes**:
- 26" (tight trails)
- 27.5" (versatile)
- 29" (fast and stable)

**Frame Materials**:
- Aluminum (lightweight, affordable)
- Carbon fiber (high-performance)
- Steel (durable)

**Brakes**: Disc brakes essential; choose mechanical or hydraulic

**Gearing**: Wide range for climbing and descending

**Tires**: Vary tread pattern and width (1.9-2.5") by terrain preference

**Fit**: Ensure adjustable seatpost, handlebars; comfortable standover height

**Components**: Evaluate fork, rear shock, wheels, and possible dropper post upgrade

Always test ride before purchasing.
