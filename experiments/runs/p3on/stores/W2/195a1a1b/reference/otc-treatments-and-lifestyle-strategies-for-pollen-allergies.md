**OTC Medications**
- Antihistamines: reduce sneezing, itching, runny nose
- Decongestants: relieve nasal congestion
- Nasal corticosteroid sprays: reduce inflammation and swelling
- Cromolyn sodium nasal spray: prevent symptoms by blocking histamine release
- Saline nasal rinses: clear mucus and irritants

**Lifestyle Changes**
- Avoid outdoor activities during high pollen count days (check online forecasts)
- Keep indoor air clean by showering and changing clothes when coming indoors
- Keep windows and doors closed during high pollen season
- Use air filters in HVAC system and consider a portable air purifier
- Consume anti-inflammatory foods (omega-3s, fruits, vegetables)
- Stay well-hydrated

Consult doctor before using natural remedies or lifestyle changes for allergy management.
