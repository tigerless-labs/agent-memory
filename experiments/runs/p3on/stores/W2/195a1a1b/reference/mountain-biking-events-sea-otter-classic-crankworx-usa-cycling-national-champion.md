Major mountain biking festivals and events:

- **Sea Otter Classic** (California): Four-day festival with racing, product demos, and exhibitions
- **Crankworx** (Whistler, BC + other locations): Series of mountain bike festivals featuring racing, slopestyle, and skill events
- **USA Cycling National Championships** (various locations): National competitive championships across multiple cycling disciplines

Search event websites and follow local bike shops on social media for updates.
