For niece Olivia's 6th birthday party in 2023, chose Art Studio Party theme over more traditional princess party or other unique themes. Planned art stations: paint station (sponge/stamping), marker station (rainbow drawing), glue station (collage making), paper station (folding/sculpture). Display ideas: mini gallery on clipboards/easels, art walk on clothesline, collaborative mural, take-home art book. Party favors: art supplies, coloring books. Kids to wear smocks/aprons for protection.
