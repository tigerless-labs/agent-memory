For twin nephew Jackson and niece Julia's first birthday party (February 2024), considering two neutral themes suitable for twins. Woodland Wonderland: nature walk, woodland creatures craft, acorn hunt, fairy ring toss. Little Explorer Party: sensory bin exploration, baby-friendly obstacle course, treasure hunt, music/movement. Party favors: seed bombs/potted plants (Woodland) or teething toys/soft blocks (Explorer).
