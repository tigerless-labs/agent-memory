## Recommended Yoga Wear Brands

**Prana**: Sustainable/eco-friendly focus; uses organic cotton, recycled materials, hemp, Tencel. Fair labor practices, carbon offset, water conservation, supply chain transparency. Styles: Halle Pants, Jamie Top.

**Athleta**: Wide range of yoga apparel with focus on comfort and flexibility. Styles: Salutation Pants, Elation Tops. Semi-annual sales in January and July with 20-50% off.

**Lululemon**: Known for high-quality, sweat-wicking fabrics and four-way stretch. Popular for yoga. Styles: Align Pants, Wunder Under Pants.

**Nike**: Dri-FIT technology for moisture-wicking. Styles: Pro Bra, Yoga Pants.

**Beyond Yoga**: Soft, stretchy, sustainable-focused wear. Styles: Spacedye Leggings, Cozy Top.

## Key Features for Yoga Wear
- Moisture-wicking fabric
- Four-way stretch
- Breathable mesh panels
- Flat seams
- High-waisted designs
- Popular styles: leggings, capris, supportive tops
