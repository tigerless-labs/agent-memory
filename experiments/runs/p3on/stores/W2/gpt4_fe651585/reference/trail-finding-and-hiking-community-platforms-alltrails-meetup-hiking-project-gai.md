## Trail Finding Apps

- **AllTrails**: Website and app for finding trails by location, distance, difficulty, and features. Filters available for difficulty level (Easy to Difficult), distance ranges (under 1 mile to 10+ miles), elevation gain, user ratings, features (waterfalls, scenic views, lakes), trail type, and accessibility. Allows downloading maps for offline use.

- **Hiking Project**: App providing trail information with social features to connect with hikers, join groups, and participate in discussions.

- **Gaia GPS**: Hiking app with community features for connecting with users, sharing routes, and joining discussions.

## Community & Social Platforms

- **Meetup**: Platform for finding and joining local hiking groups. Filters available by activity level (Casual to Extremely Active) and difficulty (Easy to Strenuous). Members can send direct messages, view profiles, and comment on group pages.

- **Facebook Groups**: Many local and national hiking-focused groups available.

- **Instagram**: Use hashtags like #hiking, #backpacking, #outdoorlovers, #naturephotography to connect with hikers.

- **Reddit's r/hiking**: Community for sharing advice, stories, and photos.

- **Hiking Forums**: Whiteblaze, Hiking Forum, Backpacking.net offer discussion boards for connecting with hikers.

- **Outdoor Clubs**: Sierra Club, Appalachian Mountain Club, NOLS chapters offer group hikes and events.

- **Local Outdoor Gear Stores**: Often host events, workshops, and group hikes.
