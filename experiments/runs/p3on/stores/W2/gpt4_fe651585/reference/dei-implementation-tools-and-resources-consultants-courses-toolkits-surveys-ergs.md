Resources for implementing workplace diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives:
- DEI consultants: professionals who develop strategies, provide training, advise on policies
- Online courses: cultural competency, diversity, inclusion (many free or low-cost)
- DEI toolkits: available online with resources for implementing initiatives
- Surveys and assessments: understand current DEI level
- Employee resource groups (ERGs): groups by shared identity/interest, build community
- Training programs: tailored diversity and inclusion training

Each organization should find what works best for their context.
