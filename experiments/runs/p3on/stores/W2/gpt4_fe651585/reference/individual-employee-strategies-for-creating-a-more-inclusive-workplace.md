Personal actions to contribute to workplace inclusion:
1. Educate yourself: read and learn about experiences of people from different backgrounds
2. Speak up: report discrimination, harassment, or bias respectfully and constructively
3. Listen and learn: understand colleagues' perspectives on diversity and inclusion
4. Be open-minded: listen to ideas that differ from your own
5. Use inclusive language: be mindful of language sensitive to different backgrounds
6. Support diversity initiatives: participate in DEI events and training
7. Create supportive atmosphere: through ERGs, events, or respectful interactions

Recognizes that creating inclusive workplace requires collective effort.
