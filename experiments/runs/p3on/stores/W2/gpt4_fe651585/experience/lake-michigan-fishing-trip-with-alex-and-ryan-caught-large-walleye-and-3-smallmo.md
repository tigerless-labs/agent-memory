Fished at Lake Michigan last weekend (2023-03-11). Caught one large walleye and three smallmouth bass (two were keepers). Fished near a drop-off using a jig with curly tail. Used St. Croix Mojo Bass rod. Weather was sunny with light breeze and calm water — ideal conditions. Grilled the fish on shore afterward with friends Alex and Ryan, who were impressed with the catch. The walleye put up a good fight; the rod handled it well.
