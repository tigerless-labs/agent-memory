Broadway-style musicals and theaters in Rome (shows/schedules subject to change - verify before visit):

**Popular Shows:**
- **The Lion King** (Teatro Brancaccio) - Disney classic, huge hit in Rome
- **Aladdin** (Teatro Sistina) - Disney favorite, beloved characters and songs
- **Mamma Mia!** (Teatro Olimpico) - ABBA-inspired, crowd-pleaser
- **We Will Rock You** (Teatro Brancaccio) - Queen-inspired, high-energy
- **La Cage aux Folles** (Teatro Sistina) - Italian adaptation of classic musical comedy

**Historic Theaters to Visit:**
- **Teatro dell'Opera di Roma** - Grand opera house with rich history
- **Teatro di Roma** - Historic theater with diverse program
- **Teatro Argentina** - Beautiful 18th-century theater

**Tip:** Check official websites or Vivaticket for current shows and book tickets in advance.
