Oven toasting: Preheat to 350°F (180°C), spread walnuts in single layer, toast 5-7 minutes until fragrant and lightly browned, stirring halfway through. Cool completely before using.

Benefits: Brings out natural oils for enhanced fragrance and flavor, reduces bitterness, improves texture making walnuts crispier.

For bread baking: Chop toasted walnuts coarsely (~1/4 inch), add during mixing phase after autolyse and before bulk fermentation, use gentle mixing to avoid crushing. Can combine toasted and untoasted walnuts for complex flavor. Consider adding walnut oil or extract to dough.

Alternative methods: Pan-toasting in dry skillet over medium heat with frequent stirring; Microwaving in 30-45 second intervals with stirring between (monitor to avoid burning).
