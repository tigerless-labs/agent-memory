Creating a starter: Keep environment warm (75-80°F / 24-27°C), use high-protein flour (bread or all-purpose), use filtered/bottled water (chlorine inhibits growth), maintain clean workspace. Mix 1/2 cup flour + 1/2 cup water, cover with cloth, rest 24-48 hours at room temperature.

Feeding schedule: Feed once or twice daily with 1:1 flour-to-water ratio. Discard half before feeding. Adjust frequency based on starter activity. Peak activity occurs 4-6 hours after feeding. Common schedules: once daily (24-hour), twice daily (12-hour).

Refrigerator storage: Store in airtight glass/ceramic container at 39-45°F (4-7°C). Feed once before storing. Feed once weekly while refrigerated.

Revival after storage: Allow 24-48 hours at room temperature (75-80°F / 24-27°C). Feed once or twice during revival. Starter is ready when active, bubbly, and frothy.

Long-term storage: Freeze as thick paste with equal parts water and flour at 0°F (-18°C) or below. Alternatively dehydrate at 150°F for 1-2 hours, store dried starter in airtight container. Maintain backup starter as insurance.

Troubleshooting: Slow/inactive = increase feeding frequency, check temperature/environment, ensure oxygen access. Too active/frothy = reduce feeding frequency, consider refrigerating. Bad smell/mold = discard and start fresh. Not rising = check temperature, environment, feeding schedule.
