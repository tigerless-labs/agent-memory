Wine establishments in Paris for French wine exploration:

**Wine Bars:**
- **La Closerie des Lilas** (6th arr.) - Extensive French wine selection, rare/vintage bottles, charming courtyard/salon
- **Le Barav** (11th arr.) - Natural and organic wines from small producers, knowledgeable staff
- **Septime La Cave** (11th arr.) - Rotating natural/organic wine selection, small plates, run by Septime restaurant team

**Wine Shops:**
- **La Maison du Vin** - Multiple Paris locations, extensive French wine selection by region, knowledgeable staff
- **Nicolas** - Wine shop chain with multiple Paris locations, French wines including organic/natural options
- **Le Wine Shop** (6th arr.) - Curated French wines including rare/vintage, offers tastings and workshops

**Tips:** Ask staff for pairing recommendations, try wine flights/tastings, prices vary significantly.
