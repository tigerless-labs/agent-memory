Broadway-style musicals and theaters in Paris (shows/schedules subject to change - verify before visit):

**Popular Shows:**
- **Moulin Rouge! The Musical** - Grandeur, music, dance; must-see cabaret-inspired production
- **The Phantom of the Opera** (Théâtre Mogador) - French adaptation, permanent residence
- **Les Misérables** (Théâtre du Châtelet) - Classic musical, rotating cast, stunning sets
- **Chicago** (Théâtre Marigny) - Kander and Ebb classic
- **Hamilton** (Théâtre du Châtelet) - French adaptation of the hit musical
- **We Will Rock You** - Queen-inspired, high-energy show

**Historic Theaters to Visit:**
- **Opéra Garnier** - Stunning opera house with guided tours
- **Théâtre du Châtelet** - Beautiful 19th-century theater
- **Comédie-Française** - Historic theater with rich history
- **Théâtre Mogador**
- **Théâtre Marigny**

**Tip:** Check official websites or Ticketmaster/Vivaticket for current shows and book tickets in advance.
