Curated list of Paris restaurants serving traditional French cuisine with seasonal ingredients and cozy atmosphere:

- **Le Comptoir du Relais** (6th arr.) - Classic French dishes, steak tartare, escargots, fresh seasonal ingredients
- **Chez L'Ami Jean** (11th arr.) - Organic/locally sourced, seasonal menu, duck confit specialty
- **Le Grand Vefour** (since 1784) - Historic, foie gras, wild game, roasted scallops, seasonal focus
- **La Réserve** (8th arr.) - Seasonal menu, fresh ingredients, creative dishes, intimate
- **Le Petit Châtelet** (5th arr.) - Seasonal ingredients, roasted chicken, seafood platter
- **Breizh Café** - Crepes with seasonal ingredients, organic/locally sourced
- **Le 6 Paul Bert** (11th arr.) - Seasonal menu, fresh ingredients, creative dishes
- **La Dame de Pic** (11th arr., Michelin-starred) - Seasonal menu, fresh ingredients, cozy/intimate

**Tip:** Make reservations in advance, especially for popular spots. Try menu du jour for better prices at lunch.
