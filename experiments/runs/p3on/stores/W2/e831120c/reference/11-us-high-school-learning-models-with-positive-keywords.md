## Learning Models Used in US High Schools

A curated list of 11 pedagogical models popular in US high schools, each with associated positive keywords:

1. **Traditional Classroom Model** — lecture-based, structured, teacher-centered, rigorous, discipline
2. **Flipped Classroom Model** — interactive, technology-based, flexible, student-centered, collaborative
3. **Personalized Learning Model** — self-directed, individualized, autonomous, adaptive, goal-oriented
4. **Blended Learning Model** — hybrid, technology-infused, flexible, customizable, engaging
5. **Mastery-Based Learning Model** — competency-based, goal-driven, self-paced, formative assessment, feedback-rich
6. **Project-Based Learning Model** — real-world, problem-based, collaborative, student-driven, innovative
7. **Experiential Learning Model** — hands-on, practical, authentic, reflective, immersive
8. **Cooperative Learning Model** — collaborative, team-based, interdependent, supportive, social
9. **Gamification Learning Model** — interactive, engaging, fun, competitive, rewarding
10. **Competency-Based Learning Model** — skill-based, performance-focused, mastery-oriented, outcome-driven, feedback-rich
11. **Design Thinking Learning Model** — creative, innovative, empathetic, user-centered, problem-solving

Each model has distinct strengths; schools may combine multiple models to meet diverse student needs.
