Museums, guided tours, and historical sites in Paris focused on French Resistance during WWII:

**Museums:**
- **Musée de la Résistance** - Comprehensive history of French Resistance movement, leaders, actions
- **Musée Jean Moulin** - Life and legacy of Jean Moulin, key Resistance unifier
- **Musée de l'Armée** (Army Museum) - Extensive Resistance section and Liberation of Paris
- **Musée Carnavalet** - Paris history including Occupation and Resistance section
- **Archeological Crypt of Notre-Dame** - Gallo-Roman history perspective

**Memorials:**
- **Mont Valérien Memorial** - Honors executed Resistance fighters
- **Place de la République** - Monument dedicated to Resistance and Liberation
- **Cimetière de Montparnasse** - Final resting place of many Resistance fighters including Jean Moulin

**Guided Tours:**
- Paris Resistance Tour (2 hours)
- WWII in Paris Tour (3 hours) - occupation, Resistance, Liberation
- French Resistance and Liberation Tour (4 hours) - deeper history including women's role

**Tips:** Many offer audio guides/tours in English. Visit mornings/early afternoon to avoid crowds. Check websites for temporary exhibitions.
