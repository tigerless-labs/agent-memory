Designed and began populating a spreadsheet to organize movie watching history.

**Core Columns:**
1. Movie Title
2. Release Year
3. Watch Date
4. Rating
5. Notes
6. Franchise/Series
7. Genre
8. Type (Sequel, Remake, Standalone, Prequel, Spin-off, Reboot)
9. Platform (Theaters, Amazon Prime, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Blu-ray/DVD, YouTube, iTunes)
10. Format (Theater, Streaming, Blu-ray)
11. Re-watch (Yes/No)
12. Month Watched

**Optional Columns:**
- Director
- Cast
- Runtime
- Language

Plan to populate via copy-paste from existing movie list in note-taking app.
