Plan to dedicate 5-10 hours per week to administrative tasks for candle business:

**Bookkeeping (1-2 hours/week):**
- Recording income and expenses
- Managing invoices and payments
- Reconciling bank statements
- Updating financial reports

**Marketing (2-4 hours/week):**
- Social media management
- Email marketing
- Content creation (photography, etc.)
- Marketing strategy research

**Other admin (1-2 hours/week):**
- Order fulfillment and shipping
- Customer service
- Supply chain/inventory management
- Business planning

Example weekly schedule:
- Monday: 2 hours (bookkeeping)
- Tuesday: 2 hours (marketing)
- Wednesday: 1 hour (order/shipping)
- Thursday: 2 hours (customer service)
- Friday: 1 hour (business planning)
