Planning to improve display and marketing materials for candle business after SummerFest market.

Display improvement tips to implement:
- Unify brand identity (logo, colors, aesthetic)
- Use clean/simple backdrop
- Group candles by type, size, or scent
- Create focal point with best-selling candles
- Utilize vertical space with risers/shelves
- Add greenery for natural appeal
- Use strategic lighting (spotlights, string lights)
- Clear signage with business name, product names, prices
- Mix candle sizes and shapes
- Consider 'candle of the month' featured section
- Keep display tidy and well-stocked

Status: Seeking feedback on current setup to identify improvement areas.
