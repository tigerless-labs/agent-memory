Decided on simple tiered pricing structure for wholesale candles:
- 1-11 candles: $4.40 each (base price)
- 12-24 candles: $4.00 each (10% discount)
- 25-49 candles: $3.60 each (18% discount)
- 50+ candles: $3.20 each (27% discount)

Pricing is based on cost calculation of:
- Material costs: $5 per candle
- Labor costs: $3 per candle
- Overhead costs: $1 per candle
- Total cost: $9 per candle before profit margin

Formula used: Wholesale Price = (Material + Labor + Overhead) × (1 - Desired Profit Margin)
With 60% markup, resulting in the tiered prices above.
