Methods to achieve perfectly cooked, juicy chicken breasts:

1. **Use a meat thermometer** — remove from heat at 165°F (74°C) internal temperature
2. Don't overcrowd grill — cook breasts individually to allow proper searing (not steaming)
3. Don't press down with spatula — squeezes out juices and makes chicken dry
4. Use medium-high heat — allows nice exterior sear while interior cooks through
5. Flip frequently every 4-5 minutes — ensures even cooking and prevents burning
6. Let rest 3-5 minutes after cooking — allows juices to redistribute for tenderness

**Common mistake:** Overcooking from longer grilling. Use thermometer to avoid this.
