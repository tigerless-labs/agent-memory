## Must-try dishes\n- **Undhiyu**: Mixed vegetable dish with variety of vegetables, beans, spices\n- **Khaman**: Sweet and sour snack made from chickpea flour; served with green chutney\n- **Handvo**: Savory cake made from lentils and rice; served with butter\n- **Gujarati Thali**: Traditional meal plate with dal, rice, roti, vegetables\n- **Local street food**: Pani puri, sev usal, masala chaas\n\n## Recommended restaurants\n- **Vishalla**: Traditional Gujarati cuisine in rustic setting\n- **Agashiye**: Vegetarian restaurant; wide range of Gujarati dishes with innovative options\n- **Gordhan Thal**: Traditional Gujarati snacks and meals\n- **Toran Dining Hall**: Gujarati and Indian dishes in traditional setting
