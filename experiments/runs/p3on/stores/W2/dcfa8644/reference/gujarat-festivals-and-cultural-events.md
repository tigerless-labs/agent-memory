## Major festivals\n- **Navratri** (Sep/Oct): Nine nights of traditional garba and dandiya raas dances, music, feasting. Popular in Ahmedabad and Vadodara.\n- **Uttarayan/Makar Sankranti** (January 14th): Beginning of sun's northern journey; kite-flying, traditional food, family gatherings\n- **Modhera Dance Festival** (January): At Sun Temple in Modhera (UNESCO site); traditional dance, music, cultural performances\n- **Rann Utsav** (November-February): White salt desert of Rann of Kutch; cultural performances, folk music, traditional handicraft exhibitions\n- **Vibrant Gujarat** (January, biennial): Business and cultural summit celebrating Gujarat's heritage and entrepreneurial spirit\n- **Bhavnath Mahadev Mela** (February): Fair at Bhavnath Mahadev Temple in Junagadh; traditional music, dance, folk performances\n- **Gujarat Festival** (November): Music, dance, theater, handicraft exhibitions
