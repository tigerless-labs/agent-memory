Finding a good cobbler: Ask friends, family, colleagues for recommendations. Check local shoe stores for referrals. Search online via Google for cobblers near you, check Yelp and Facebook reviews. Look for certifications from Shoe Service Institute of America or National Shoe Repair Association. Inspect their workshop for cleanliness and organization. Ask about their experience with leather boots and similar repairs. Get written quotes. Red flags: unclear pricing, poor communication, unprofessional workspace.
