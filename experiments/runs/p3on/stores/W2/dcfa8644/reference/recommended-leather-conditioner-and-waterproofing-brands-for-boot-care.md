Recommended leather conditioner brands: Leatherology, Chambers Leather Milk, Bickmore, Kiwi. Recommended waterproofing brands: Nikwax, Sno-Seal, Scotchgard, Kiwi. Oil-based conditioners provide more nourishment, water-based are better for waterproofing. Test products on small inconspicuous area first. Reapply regularly as needed.
