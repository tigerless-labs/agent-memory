Steps to achieve symmetrical, caramelized grill marks:

1. Clean grill grates and brush with oil before heating
2. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400-425°F)
3. Pat chicken dry with paper towels after marinating (excess moisture prevents searing)
4. Brush grates with oil again just before placing chicken
5. Place chicken at an angle to create diagonal marks from grill lines
6. Let chicken cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes (don't press down with spatula)
7. **Rotate 90 degrees** after 3-4 minutes on each side to create crisscross pattern
8. Cook to internal temperature of 165°F — avoid overcooking
9. Let rest 3-5 minutes after cooking to redistribute juices

**For browning/char:** Use marinade with sugar and sesame oil; brush with BBQ sauce during last 2 minutes of grilling.
