Home cleaning methods: Use soft-bristled toothbrush to gently scrub dirt and stains with mild soap and water. Avoid soaking. For tough stains, use baking soda and vinegar paste, let sit 30 minutes, rinse with cold water. Air dry away from direct sunlight. Never use machine wash or heat dryer. Consider professional cleaner for delicate materials like suede or nubuck, waterproof membranes, or tough stains. Act quickly as dirt and stains set over time.
