Emerging jazz artists worth listening to:\n\n1. **Nubya Garcia** — British saxophonist and composer from London; incorporates jazz, soul, and afrobeat; known for strong improvisational skills and powerful tone\n\n2. **Joel Ross** — American vibraphonist; innovative approach to composition and performance; debut album 'KingMaker' on Blue Note Records (2019)\n\n3. **Ashley Henry** — British pianist and composer from London; blends jazz, hip hop, and R&B; praised for unique sound and melodic sensibility\n\n4. **Georgia Anne Muldrow** — American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist; incorporates jazz, funk, and experimental elements; known for soulful vocals\n\n5. **Marquis Hill** — Chicago-based trumpeter; innovative approach to composition and performance; released albums on independent labels
