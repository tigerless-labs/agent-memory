Modern jazz record labels that prioritize artistic creativity and innovation like Blue Note Records:\n\n1. **ECM Records** — German label founded in 1969; known for innovative approach to recording jazz and classical music; artists include Keith Jarrett, Pat Metheny, Jan Garbarek\n\n2. **Ropeadope Records** — US-based label founded in 1999; eclectic roster supporting creative musicians; artists include Christian McBride, Robert Glasper, Snarky Puppy\n\n3. **Revive Music Group** — US company founded in 2006; began as concert promotion, expanded to record label and artist management; focused on promoting emerging jazz artists
