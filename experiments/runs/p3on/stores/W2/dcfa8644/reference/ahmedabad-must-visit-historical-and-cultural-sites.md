## Historical landmarks\n- **Sabarmati Ashram**: Former residence of Mahatma Gandhi; symbol of India's independence struggle\n- **Akshardham Temple**: Stunning Indian architecture with intricate carvings, ornate pillars, beautiful lake, evening light and sound show\n- **Jama Masjid**: One of India's largest mosques; Indo-Islamic architecture with intricate carvings\n- **Bhadra Fort**: Historic fort dating back to 15th century\n- **Sarkhej Roza**: Mosque and tomb complex; example of Islamic architecture\n\n## Cultural experiences\n- **Old City Heritage Walk**: Explore narrow lanes, hidden gems like Teen Darwaja and historic pols (neighborhoods)\n- **Navratri Celebrations**: Traditional garba and dandiya raas dances\n- **Kite Festival (Uttarayan)**: January 14th celebration with kite flying\n\n## Markets & food areas\n- **Lal Darwaja Market**: Traditional clothing, handicrafts, souvenirs\n- **Manek Chowk**: Historic square for local snacks, drinks, souvenirs\n\n## Other attractions\n- **Kankaria Lake**: Boating, walking trails, sunset views\n- **Science City**: Interactive exhibits, planetarium, IMAX theater\n- **Ahmedabad Museum**: City history, art, culture
