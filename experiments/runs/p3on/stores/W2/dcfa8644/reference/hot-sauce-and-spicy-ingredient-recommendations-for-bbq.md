Hot sauces and spicy ingredients that pair well with BBQ:

**Ready-made sauces:**
- Frank's RedHot — classic balance of heat and tanginess
- Sriracha — especially good with sweet and smoky BBQ sauces
- Cholula — Mexican hot sauce (arbol peppers), slightly sweet and smoky heat
- Tabasco
- Louisiana Hot Sauce
- Valentina

**Fresh ingredients:**
- Diced jalapeños or serrano peppers — add to marinades or BBQ sauce
- Chipotle peppers in adobo sauce — smoked and potent, use sparingly

**Fermented/Asian:**
- Gochujang (Korean chili paste) — sweet, savory, and spicy; works in marinades and sauces

**Strategy:** Start with small amounts and taste as you go. Easier to add more heat than to remove it.
