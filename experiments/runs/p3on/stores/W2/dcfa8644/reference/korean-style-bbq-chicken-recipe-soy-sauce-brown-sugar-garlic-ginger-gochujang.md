Korean-inspired BBQ chicken marinade:
- Soy sauce
- Brown sugar
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- Optional: Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) for extra heat

Marinate chicken breasts for 30 minutes to 1 hour (or overnight for more intense flavor). Grill 5-6 minutes per side, finish with sweet and smoky BBQ sauce glaze during last 2-3 minutes for char.
