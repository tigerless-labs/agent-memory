**Recommendations for educators and policymakers:**

1. **Prioritize teacher professional development** with ongoing support as systems are implemented
2. **Ensure curriculum alignment** by working with developers to demonstrate how systems align with curriculum and learning goals
3. **Engage developers with educators** to ensure systems are designed with teachers' and students' needs in mind
4. **Provide clear guidance** on how AI systems align with curriculum and learning goals
5. **Implement ongoing evaluation and assessment** to ensure systems are effective and continuously improved
6. **Position systems as augmentative, not replacive** to address teacher concerns and clarify the role of AI in supporting (not replacing) teaching and learning
