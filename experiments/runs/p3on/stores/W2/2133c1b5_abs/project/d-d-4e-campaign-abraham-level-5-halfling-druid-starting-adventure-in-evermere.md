## Character
- **Name:** Abraham
- **Race:** Halfling
- **Class:** Druid
- **Level:** 5

## World
- **Setting:** Evermere
- **Starting Location:** Oakwood village, at the edge of Whispering Woods

## Quest Hook
Summoned by Eadric (village elder, old friend) to investigate corruption in Whispering Woods. Creatures becoming aggressive with unnatural behavior; forest itself turning hostile. Ancient ruin called Moonstone Spire believed to house an artifact that can bend nature.

## NPCs Encountered
- **Eadric**: Village elder who summoned Abraham
- **Alaric**: Innkeeper at Silver Stag Inn
- **Mabel**: Villager who witnessed aggressive wolves with unnatural glow
- **Thom**: Woodsman who saw trees acting aggressively, uprooting and blocking paths
- **Lysander**: Traveling merchant who spotted mysterious shrouded figure with powerful magical energy in the forest

## Mystery to Investigate
- Mysterious figure spotted deep in forest surrounded by strange lights and eerie sounds
- Possible connection to rogue wizard or magical cult
- Moonstone Spire as likely source of disturbance
