Conducted research examining the effectiveness and implementation challenges of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension.

**Key focus areas:**
- Effectiveness of these systems in improving student learning outcomes
- Implementation and integration challenges in classroom settings
- Potential for promoting equity and inclusion
- Risks of algorithmic bias

**Research approach:** Broader conceptual analysis with several case studies to illustrate real-world challenges and opportunities.

**Presented:** First research conference as a poster presentation (summer 2022), received positive feedback from educators, policymakers, and developers.
