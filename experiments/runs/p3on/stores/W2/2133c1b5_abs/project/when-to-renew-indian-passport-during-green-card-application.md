User is deciding whether to renew Indian passport now or wait until after obtaining green card.\n\n**Decision factors:**\n- If passport expires within 6-12 months or upcoming travel planned: renew now\n- If passport valid for longer period with no travel: can wait until after green card\n\n**Process notes:** Will need to provide green card application receipt notice (Form I-797C) to Indian embassy/consulate. Consult with Indian embassy/consulate and immigration attorney for best approach.\n\nDecision date: 2023-03-29
