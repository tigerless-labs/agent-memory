**Common implementation challenges identified across case studies:**

1. **Teacher Professional Development:** Lack of ongoing training and support was a major barrier. Many teachers felt unprepared to effectively integrate AI-powered adaptive learning systems into their teaching practices.

2. **Curriculum Alignment:** Clear understanding and alignment with curriculum and learning goals was essential for adoption. Teachers were significantly more likely to adopt and use systems effectively when this alignment was explicit and clear.

3. **Algorithmic Bias Concerns:** While these systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students, there are risks that algorithmic bias could exacerbate existing inequities if not carefully addressed.

4. **Teacher Role Debate:** Stakeholders disagreed about whether AI systems would replace teachers or augment their work—this tension reflected different visions of the technology's proper role in education.
