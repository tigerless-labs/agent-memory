Volunteered at the Children's Museum during their 'Family Art Day' event on March 22, 2023, from 10 am to 4 pm.

**Activities supervised:**
- Collaborative mural project
- Sculpture station

**Reflections:**
Found the experience heartwarming, especially seeing the children's creativity shine through. The collaborative nature of the projects helped kids work together, share ideas, and build on each other's strengths.

**Impact:**
The experience reinforced interest in art education and working with children on creative projects. This volunteering informed thinking about mixed media collage and abstract expressionism projects suitable for kids in small group settings.
