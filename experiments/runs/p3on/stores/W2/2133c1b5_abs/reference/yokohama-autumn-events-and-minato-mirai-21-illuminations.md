## Seasonal Events (typically October-December)\n\n**Yokohama Oktoberfest** - German-style beer festival with food, drinks, and live music. Usually runs mid-October to early November.\n\n**Yokohama Autumn Leaves Festival** - Late November event featuring beautiful autumn foliage, food stalls, and traditional Japanese performances.\n\n**Minato Mirai 21 Autumn Illuminations** - Typically runs late October to mid-December, best viewed 5-10 PM. Features:\n- Maple Leaf Tunnel: 100-meter tunnel with illuminated maple trees in shades of red, orange, yellow\n- Harvest Moon Garden: Glowing moon surrounded by autumn flowers\n- Cosmo World Illuminations: Spectacular light shows with projection mapping on Ferris wheel\n- Waterfront Illuminations: Reflections on water creating shimmering effects\n\nIllumination themes typically incorporate fall season elements like maple leaves and chrysanthemums combined with modern lighting technology.
