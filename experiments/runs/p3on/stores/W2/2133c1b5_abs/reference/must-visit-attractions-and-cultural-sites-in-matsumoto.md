Key attractions in Matsumoto for traditional culture and history:

**Castles & Architecture:**
- **Matsumoto Castle** — National Treasure of Japan, 16th century, unique blend of Japanese and Western styles, well-preserved
- **Nawate Street** — Charming traditional street with shops, tea houses, restaurants; old Japan atmosphere
- **Nakamachi Street** — Historic street with traditional warehouses and shops from Matsumoto's mercantile past

**Museums & Cultural Sites:**
- **Matsumoto Folkcraft Museum** — Traditional Japanese crafts (woodcarvings, pottery, weaving), located in historic old house
- **Kaichi School Museum** — Meiji period school building, history of Japanese education
- **Matsumoto City Museum** — City history, art, and culture exhibits

**Temples & Shrines:**
- **Zenko-ji Temple** — Beautiful garden and traditional architecture, founded 7th century, famous for cherry blossom viewing
- **Matsumoto Shrine** — Shinto shrine near castle, god of war and agriculture, beautiful torii gate

**Parks & Nature:**
- **Agata no Mori Park** — Peaceful atmosphere, pond, walking trails, plants and flowers

**Local Specialties to Try:**
- Shinshu salmon
- Hakuba apples
- Matsumoto coffee
