## Must-See Attractions\n\n**Minato Mirai 21** - Futuristic district with stunning harbor views. Features the iconic Landmark Tower with observation deck offering panoramic city views.\n\n**Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse** - Historic warehouse complex with beautiful harbor views, now converted into a shopping and dining destination with charming atmosphere.\n\n**Yamashita Park** - Scenic waterfront park with excellent harbor views, good for leisurely walks or picnics.\n\n**Sankeien Garden** - Traditional Japanese garden featuring historic buildings, walking paths, and tranquil setting. Especially beautiful during fall foliage season.\n\n**Cup Noodles Museum** - Unique museum dedicated to instant ramen history. Visitors can create their own custom Cup Noodles.\n\n**Yokohama Museum of Art** - Modern art museum with diverse collection of Japanese and international artwork.
