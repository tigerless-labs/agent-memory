Recommended onsen facilities in Matsumoto area:

- **Asama Onsen** — Popular resort area ~30 min from Matsumoto City by bus/taxi. Multiple public and private baths.
- **Utsukushigahara Onsen** — Scenic resort with Japanese Alps views, ~45 min from Matsumoto City.
- **Hirata Onsen** — Historic facility with traditional atmosphere, indoor and outdoor baths, ~20 min from Matsumoto City.
- **Matsumoto Onsen Hotel** — Hotel with public onsen in central Matsumoto City with mountain views.

Notes: Some onsen have rules against tattooed individuals or may not accept foreigners. Private baths available at many facilities. Always wash before entering.
