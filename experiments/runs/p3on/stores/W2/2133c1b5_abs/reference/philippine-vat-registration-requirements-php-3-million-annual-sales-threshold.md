In the Philippines, VAT registration requirements:

**VAT-Registered Businesses** (annual sales/gross receipts > PHP 3 million):
- Required to charge VAT on sales
- Must include VAT in invoices
- Must file VAT returns with Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- Must pay collected VAT to BIR

**Non-VAT-Registered Businesses** (annual sales < PHP 3 million):
- Not required to charge VAT
- Not required to include VAT in invoices
- Do not file VAT returns

VAT registration status determines invoicing requirements.
