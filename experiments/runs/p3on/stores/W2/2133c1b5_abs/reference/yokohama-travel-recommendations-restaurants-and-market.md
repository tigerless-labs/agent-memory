## Recommended Restaurants\n\n**Kanemasa** - Kanimeshi (crab rice bowl) specialist in Yokohama's Chinatown. Operating for over 30 years with consistently fresh, high-quality crab. Popular spot with local visitors; expect queues.\n\n**Tsukiji Itadori Uogashi Nihon-Ichi** - Sushi restaurant located near the Yokohama Fish Market. Known for fresh seasonal ingredients. Namashirasu (raw sardine sushi) is a must-try. Gets busy and often sells out of the daily catch early.\n\n**Yokohama Fish Market** - Casual, affordable food stalls offering fresh seafood including kanimeshi and namashirasu at reasonable prices.\n\n**Yokohama Central Market** - Bustling marketplace near the Fish Market with multiple food stalls and restaurants. Offers fresh seafood (sushi, sashimi, grilled fish) and local goods.
