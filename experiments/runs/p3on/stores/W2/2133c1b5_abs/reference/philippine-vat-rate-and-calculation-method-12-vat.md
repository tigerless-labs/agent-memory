The standard VAT rate in the Philippines is 12%.

To calculate VAT, multiply the total cost by 0.12:
- Example: PHP 1,000 × 0.12 = PHP 120 VAT
- Total with VAT: PHP 1,000 + PHP 120 = PHP 1,120

This rate is used by VAT-registered businesses.
