Options for traveling from Tokyo to Nagano:

**Trains:**
- **Hokuriku Shinkansen** — Tokyo Station to Nagano Station, ~1 hour 45 minutes
- **Local trains and buses** available from Nagano Station to destinations

**Buses:**
- Highway buses from Tokyo Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal or Tokyo Station to Nagano City, ~3-4 hours
- **Nagano Snow Shuttle** — Tokyo Shinjuku area to Hakuba Valley, ~4-5 hours

**Flight + Train:**
- Flights from Haneda or Narita airports to Matsumoto Airport, then local train/bus connections

**Tips:**
- Book train tickets in advance during peak season for best availability and prices
- Consider Japan Rail Pass if planning multiple train trips
- Narita Express (N'EX) connects Narita Airport to Tokyo Station for shinkansen transfers
