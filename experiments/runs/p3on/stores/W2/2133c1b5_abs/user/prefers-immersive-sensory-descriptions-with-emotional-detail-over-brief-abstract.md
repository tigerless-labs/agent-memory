When requesting descriptions or narratives, prefers writing that:
- Engages multiple senses (sight, sound, smell, touch)
- Includes emotional resonance and specific details
- Shows rather than tells
- Avoids brevity in favor of rich, lived experience

Reference point: appreciates the immersive, environmental storytelling style of the Jim Carrey Grinch film (How the Grinch Stole Christmas) — visual and narrative richness that transports the reader into the scene.
