**Initial puppy series:** Start at 6-8 weeks old, with booster shots every 2-4 weeks until approximately 16 weeks old.\n\n**Adult schedule:** One booster at 1 year of age, then every 3 years thereafter.\n\n**Key diseases protected against:** Parvovirus, distemper, rabies.\n\n**Why important even for indoor dogs:** Viruses like parvovirus can survive in the environment and be transmitted indirectly through contaminated surfaces, food/water bowls, clothing, and shoes brought into the home. Indoor-only dogs are still at risk.\n\n**Cost-benefit:** Preventative vaccination is significantly cheaper than treating a preventable disease, which can require intensive and prolonged veterinary treatment.
