**Vinaigrette recipes to complement spiced lentils:**

Basic ratio: 2 tbsp acid (vinegar or citrus) + 1 tbsp oil + 1 tsp flavor ingredients + salt/pepper

**Cilantro Lime Vinaigrette:** lime juice, olive oil, minced cilantro, salt, pinch of cumin

**Ginger-Turmeric Vinaigrette:** apple cider vinegar, olive oil, grated ginger, turmeric powder, salt, black pepper

**Cumin-Coriander Vinaigrette:** red wine vinegar, olive oil, ground cumin, ground coriander, salt, cayenne pepper

**Lemon-Garlic Vinaigrette:** lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, salt, black pepper

Tips: Use mild oil (olive or avocado), adjust acid to taste, don't over-dress the lentils to let flavors complement rather than overpower.
