**Paris per night:**
- Hostels: €20–€40 (private €30–€50)
- Budget hotels: €60–€100 (branded €80–€120)
- Mid-range: €120–€200 (3-star €150–€250)
- Luxury: €300–€600+
- Airbnb: studio €30–€60, full apartment €80–€150

**Rome per night:**
- Hostels: €20–€40 (private €30–€60)
- Budget hotels: €50–€100 (branded €70–€120)
- Mid-range: €100–€200 (3-star €120–€250)
- Luxury: €250–€500+
- Airbnb: room €25–€50, full apartment €60–€120

**Booking tips:** Shoulder season (April–May, September–October) has lower prices. Book in advance for better rates. Look for breakfast inclusion or kitchen facilities to reduce costs.
