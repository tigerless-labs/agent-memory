**Spiced Lentils (Dal) Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 1 cup brown or green lentils, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups water
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Optional: cayenne pepper or red chili flakes

Instructions:
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium heat
2. Sauté diced onion, garlic, and ginger until onion is translucent
3. Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, and garam masala; cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant
4. Add lentils and water; season with salt
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat to low and simmer 20-25 minutes until lentils are tender
6. Puree to desired consistency using immersion blender or regular blender
7. Adjust seasoning as needed; add heat with cayenne pepper if desired

Variations: Add diced bell peppers or carrots, use red/yellow lentils instead, add coconut milk or yogurt for creaminess, adjust spice level to taste.
