**Cost:** Typically 5–10% of total trip cost.

**Worth considering if:**
- Pre-existing medical conditions
- Extended travel duration
- High-risk activities planned
- Expensive bookings or arrangements
- Traveling to high-risk destinations for natural disasters/instability

**Coverage includes:**
- Trip cancellation/interruption (illness, injury, emergencies)
- Medical emergencies abroad
- Flight delays and lost luggage
- Theft or loss of personal belongings
- 24/7 emergency assistance

**Best practices:**
- Purchase soon after booking trip for cancellation coverage
- Read fine print and exclusions carefully
- Compare policies from multiple providers
- Buy from reputable, established providers
- Check what's excluded before purchasing
