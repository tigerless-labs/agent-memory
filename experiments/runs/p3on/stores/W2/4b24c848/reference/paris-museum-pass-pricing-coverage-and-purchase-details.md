**Paris Museum Pass Benefits:**
Grants entry to 60+ museums and attractions including Louvre, Orsay Museum, Rodin, Arc de Triomphe, Palace of Versailles. Skip-line access, unlimited visits during validity period, free temporary exhibitions, additional discounts.

**Pricing:**
- 2-day pass: €48 adult, €24 child (ages 4-17)
- 4-day pass: €62 adult, €31 child (ages 4-17)
- 6-day pass: €74 adult, €37 child (ages 4-17)

**Value Example:** Louvre €18 + Orsay €12 + Arc de Triomphe €12 + Versailles €20 = €62 (equals cost of 4-day pass alone)
