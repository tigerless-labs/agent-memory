**Ways to repurpose spiced lentils (dal) throughout the week:**

1. **Lentil Salads:** Mix with chopped tomatoes, cucumbers, bell peppers + vinaigrette
2. **Lentil Wraps:** Fill whole wheat tortillas or naan with lentils, greens, avocado, hummus, cucumber
3. **Lentil Soups:** Use as base with diced veggies and broth; add coconut milk or cream for creamier version
4. **Lentil Cakes:** Mix with breadcrumbs, egg, spices; pan-fry until crispy; serve with yogurt sauce or chutney
5. **Stuffed Bell Peppers:** Fill peppers with lentils, cooked rice, chopped veggies; bake until tender
6. **Lentil Quesadillas:** Fill tortillas with lentils, cheese, diced veggies; cook until crispy
7. **Lentil Bolognese:** Use as meat substitute in pasta sauce
8. **Lentil Fritters:** Mix with chickpea flour, egg, spices; fry until crispy; serve with yogurt sauce
9. **Lentil Curry:** Add to curry sauce with coconut milk, tomato puree, spices; serve with rice or naan
10. **Buddha Bowls:** Top lentils with roasted veggies, avocado, fried egg

Key: Treat lentils as versatile base ingredient; experiment with seasonings to vary flavor profiles.
