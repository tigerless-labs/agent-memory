Explored the Japanese word 'setsunai' (切ない) — a bittersweet emotion mixing sadness, nostalgia, and joy. Argued that while the word is Japanese, the emotion itself is universal to humanity, not culturally exclusive. Interested in how such concepts could gain wider awareness in English-speaking cultures through translation, cultural exchange, online communities, and education. Values self-reflection and professional support (mental health specialists, anthropologists, sociologists) for developing emotional awareness across cultures.
