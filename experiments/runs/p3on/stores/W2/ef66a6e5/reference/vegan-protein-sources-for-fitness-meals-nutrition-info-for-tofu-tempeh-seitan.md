High-protein vegan options researched for fitness meal prep:

**Tofu** (3-ounce serving)
- Protein: 9-10g
- Carbs: 2-3g
- Best type: extra-firm or super-firm for highest protein

**Tempeh** (3-ounce serving)  
- Protein: 15-16g
- Carbs: 6-7g
- Note: fermented soybean product, rich in probiotics, nutty flavor

**Seitan** (3-ounce serving)
- Protein: 21-22g (highest protein of the three)
- Carbs: 4-5g
- Note: made from wheat gluten, meat-like texture

Also noted: lentils and chickpeas are good protein sources but higher in carbs (~20-22g per 1/2 cup cooked).
