Marketplaces where Sweet Delights can sell handmade products:\n\n**Primary options (handmade focus):**\n- Etsy (etsy.com) - Established platform for handmade items, artisanal goods\n- Amazon Handmade - Amazon's handmade section, requires approval\n\n**General marketplaces:**\n- eBay - Can set up seller store\n- Bonanza - Online marketplace for handmade goods\n\n**Specialty platforms:**\n- Food52 - Artisanal food platform, vendor application required\n- UncommonGoods - Unique handmade/creative products, submission-based\n- Gift Shop Now - Specialty food and gift vendors\n\n**E-commerce platforms with marketplace features:**\n- Shopify - Has Shopify Marketplace\n- WooCommerce - WordPress plugin with marketplace features\n\n**Key considerations:** Review platform fees/commissions, create professional seller profile, high-quality product images, handle customer service/shipping/returns, optimize listings for search, maintain brand consistency across platforms, keep website as central hub.
