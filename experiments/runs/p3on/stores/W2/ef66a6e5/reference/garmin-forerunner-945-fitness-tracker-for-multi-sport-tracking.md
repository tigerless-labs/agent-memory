Recommended for tracking runs, soccer games, and tennis matches. Features GPS, heart rate monitoring, stress tracking, performance condition, and recovery time estimates. Does not auto-detect tennis; requires manual activity selection. Does not track serve speed or racket swing directly. Can track duration, distance, calories, and intensity during tennis play.
