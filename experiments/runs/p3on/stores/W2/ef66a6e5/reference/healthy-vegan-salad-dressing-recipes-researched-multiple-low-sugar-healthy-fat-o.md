Vegan salad dressing and sauce options researched for low-sugar, healthy-fat meals:

1. **Lemon-Tahini Dressing** — Lemon juice + tahini + garlic + olive oil. Creamy, tangy, rich in healthy fats.

2. **Avocado-Lime Dressing** — Blended ripe avocado + lime juice + cilantro + salt. Creamy, refreshing, packed with healthy avocado fats.

3. **Ginger-Turmeric Vinaigrette** — Apple cider vinegar + olive oil + grated ginger + turmeric. Zesty, anti-inflammatory.

4. **Hummus-Based Dressing** — Hummus + lemon juice + garlic + water + optional herbs (parsley, cilantro). Creamy, protein-rich.

5. **Nutritional Yeast Cheese Sauce** — Nutritional yeast + lemon juice + garlic + olive oil. Nutty, cheesy flavor, addictive.

6. **Miso-Ginger Dressing** — Miso paste + apple cider vinegar + grated ginger + olive oil. Savory, umami-rich.

7. **Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette** — Fresh orange/grapefruit juice + olive oil + chopped herbs (parsley, basil, dill). Light, refreshing.

**Tips for all dressings:**
- Use healthy oils: olive oil, avocado oil, or grapeseed oil
- Natural sweeteners: dates, maple syrup, coconut sugar (avoid refined sugar)
- Use quality vinegars: apple cider or balsamic
- Experiment with spices/herbs instead of relying on salt and sugar
- Make from scratch to control ingredients
