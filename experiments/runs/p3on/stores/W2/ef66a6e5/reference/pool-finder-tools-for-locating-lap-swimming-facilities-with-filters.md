**Three main pool finder tools for locating lap swimming facilities:**

1. **Swim.com**
   - Filter by: Hours (Morning), Pool Length (25 yards), Water Temperature (78-80°F)
   - Can filter by: Programs & Teams (Masters Swimming, Swim Team, USMS)
   - Shows: Pool amenities, lane availability

2. **PoolFinder** 
   - Filter by: Hours of Operation (Morning), Pool Characteristics (Length, Temperature)
   - Can filter by: Programs & Activities (Masters Swimming, Swim Team)
   - Shows: Lap Swimming Hours availability

3. **USA Swimming Pool Finder**
   - Filter by: Pool Hours, Pool Characteristics (Length)
   - Can filter by: Programs & Teams (Masters Swimming, Swim Team, USMS affiliation)
   - **Note:** Does not have specific water temperature filter; requires direct contact

**Key search keywords to use:**
- 'morning lap swim', 'early bird swim', 'dawn patrol'
- '25-yard pool', 'short course', 'competition pool', 'lap pool'
- 'masters swimming', 'USMS'

**Information to confirm by direct contact:**
- Water temperature (not always listed online)
- Equipment policy (kickboards, pull buoys, availability)
- Lane sharing and circle swimming policies
- Lane etiquette rules and passing signals
- Swimmer density limits per lane
- Masters program details and coaching
