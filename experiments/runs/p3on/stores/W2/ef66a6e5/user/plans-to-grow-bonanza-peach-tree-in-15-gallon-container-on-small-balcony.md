Decided to grow Bonanza Peach (dwarf variety, 5-6 ft tall) in 15-gallon container with drainage on small balcony. Needs well-draining potting mix (perlite/vermiculite), full sun 6+ hrs, frequent watering and fertilizing. Annual late-winter pruning to open center/vase shape. Monitor for pests/diseases. Repot every few years.
