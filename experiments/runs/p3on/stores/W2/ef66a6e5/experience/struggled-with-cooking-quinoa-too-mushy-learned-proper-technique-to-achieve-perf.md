Previously had issues with quinoa becoming mushy when cooked. Learned and tested proper quinoa cooking method:
- Rinse quinoa thoroughly (removes bitter saponin coating) 
- Use 2:1 water-to-quinoa ratio
- Cook on medium heat (not high, which causes uneven cooking)
- Cook for 15-20 minutes until water is absorbed
- Quinoa should be tender but still slightly crunchy in center
- Fluff with fork after cooking to separate grains and prevent clumping

Results: perfectly cooked quinoa with ideal texture for salads and meal prep.
