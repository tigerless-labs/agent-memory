Authors and books recommended for readers who enjoy slow-burning psychological thrillers with unreliable narrators:

- **Shari Lapena**: The Couple Next Door, A Stranger in the House
- **Fiona Barton**: The Widow
- **Clare Mackintosh**: I Let You Go, The Farm
- **Mary Kubica**: The Good Girl, Don't You Cry
- **Tana French**: Dublin Murder Squad series
- **Laura Lippman**: What the Dead Know, And When She Was Good
- **Megan Abbott**: The Fever, You Will Know Me

User expressed specific interest in trying Shari Lapena and Fiona Barton first.
