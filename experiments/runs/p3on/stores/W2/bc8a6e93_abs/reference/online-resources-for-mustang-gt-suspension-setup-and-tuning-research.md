**Mustang Forums:**
- MustangForums.com — large community with suspension/handling section
- Mustang6G.com — focused on 2015+ Mustang, suspension upgrades and tuning
- TrackMustangsOnline.com — track enthusiasts, suspension setup/tuning

**Track-Focused Forums:**
- NASAForums.com — National Auto Sport Association, track driving and suspension setup

**YouTube Channels:**
- AmericanMuscle — Mustang suspension tutorials and reviews
- CJ Pony Parts — Mustang suspension info and installation guides
- TrackDayDiaries — track driving and suspension tuning with Mustang focus
- Traction Circle — track driving and suspension setup tutorials

**Manufacturer Resources:**
- KW Suspension website — suspension guides, technical documents
- Ohlins Racing website — technical resources, suspension guides

**Magazines/Blogs:**
- Mustang Monthly — suspension upgrade features
- Mustang Performance Magazine — high-performance Mustang focus
- The Track Edge — track driving and suspension tuning articles
- Speedhunters — motorsport topics including suspension setup
