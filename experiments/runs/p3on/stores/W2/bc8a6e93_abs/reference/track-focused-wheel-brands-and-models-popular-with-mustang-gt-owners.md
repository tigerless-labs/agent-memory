Wheel recommendations for Mustang GT track driving:

**Popular Brands & Models:**

1. **Forgiato** — Forgiato Tecnica, Forgiato Veloce — popular among track-focused Mustang enthusiasts

2. **OZ Racing** — OZ Superforgiata, OZ Ultraleggera — motorsport-grade wheels designed for high-performance driving

3. **HRE** — HRE P101, HRE R101 — premium, lightweight, high-strength wheels for track demands

4. **Enkei** — Enkei RPF1, Enkei TS-5 — Japanese manufacturer, known for strength, low weight, aggressive design

5. **APEX** — APEX EC-7, APEX FL-5 — US-based, lightweight and durable for track driving

**Selection Criteria:**
- Width/diameter: 18+ inches diameter, 9-10 inches wide
- Offset: lower offset improves handling, may require spacers
- Material: forged wheels (stronger, lighter) preferred over cast
- Weight: lighter improves handling and acceleration
- Hub bore: 72.56mm for Mustang GT
- Pair with high-performance track tires for optimal results
