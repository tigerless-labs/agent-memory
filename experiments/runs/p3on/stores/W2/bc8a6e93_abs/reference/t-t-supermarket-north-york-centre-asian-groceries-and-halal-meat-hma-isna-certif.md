**Location:** T&T Supermarket, 5300 Yonge St, North York Centre

**International Ingredients:**
- Extensive Asian selection: rice, noodles, grains, canned goods, sauces, condiments (soy sauce, fish sauce, sriracha), spices, herbs, frozen foods (dumplings, buns, spring rolls)
- Asian cuisines covered: Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Thai, Southeast Asian
- Also carries: Indian and Pakistani spices/sauces, Middle Eastern ingredients (sumac, pomegranate molasses), Latin American items, European products

**Halal Meat Section (Certified):**
- Certification: Halal Monitoring Authority (HMA) and Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)
- Products: fresh beef, chicken, lamb, goat; frozen burgers/sausages/kebabs; poultry (wings, drumsticks); seafood (shrimp, fish, squid)

**Other Features:**
- Large fresh produce section with Asian specialties (bok choy, Chinese broccoli, Korean chili peppers)
- In-store bakery: Asian-style breads, pastries, desserts
- Food court: Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese restaurants

**Practical Notes:**
- Can get crowded on weekends; bring shopping list due to vast selection
- Staff available for ingredient assistance and recommendations
- Offers loyalty program and sales (check website/newsletter)
