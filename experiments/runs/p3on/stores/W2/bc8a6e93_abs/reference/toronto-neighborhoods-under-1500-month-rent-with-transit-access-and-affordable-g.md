## Recommended one-bedroom neighborhoods under $1500/month with transit access:

**The Danforth** (East York) - $1400–1600/month
- Charming mix of old and new buildings, vibrant downtown on Danforth Avenue
- Transit: Danforth subway station
- Stores: Loblaws (1555 Danforth Ave), Metro (1441 Danforth Ave), independent grocers on Danforth Ave for international foods

**Riverdale** - $1400–1600/month
- Gentrified neighborhood with new condos
- Transit: Donlands subway station
- Stores: No Frills (545 Queen St E), Food Basics (655 Queen St E), Riverdale Farm Market (201 Winchester St)

**Leslieville** - $1300–1500/month
- Artsy small-town feel, vibrant Queen Street East
- Transit: Queen Street East streetcar
- Stores: Loblaws (1411 Queen St E), FreshCo (1421 Queen St E), Leslieville Market (1133 Queen St E)

**The Junction** - $1300–1500/month
- Up-and-coming, vibrant Dundas Street West
- Transit: Junction subway station
- Stores: No Frills (2995 Dundas St W), Food Basics (3055 Dundas St W), Junction Farmers' Market

**Scarborough Village** - $1200–1400/month
- Most affordable, mix of high-rise and low-rise buildings
- Transit: Scarborough Village subway station
- Stores: Loblaws (1900 Eglinton Ave E), FreshCo (2555 Eglinton Ave E), Scarborough Village Market (1921 Eglinton Ave E) for African and Caribbean foods

**North York Centre** - $1200–1400/month
- Most affordable, good Yonge Street amenities
- Transit: North York Centre subway station
- Stores: Loblaws (5095 Yonge St), T&T Supermarket (5300 Yonge St), P.A.T. Central Market (5555 Yonge St)
