## Tools\n- **YNAB** (You Need a Budget): Zero-based budgeting app\n- **Mint**: Tracks spending, creates budgets, sets goals\n- **Personal Capital**: Tracks income, expenses, investments\n- **Spendee**: Simple expense tracker with categorization\n\n## Frameworks\n- **50/30/20 rule**: Allocate 50% to needs, 30% to discretionary, 20% to debt & savings\n- **Sinking funds**: Separate accounts or buckets for specific irregular expenses\n- **Irregular income handling**: Estimate average monthly and track separately, set aside for tax liability
