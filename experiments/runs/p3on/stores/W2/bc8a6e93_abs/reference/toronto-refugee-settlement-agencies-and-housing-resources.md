Settlement Agencies: Toronto Refugee Resettlement Centre, COSTI Immigrant Services, YMCA of Greater Toronto, WoodGreen Community Services, Neighbourhood Group. Housing Programs: Housing Connections portal for rent-geared-to-income applications; Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) for affordable housing. Contact: City of Toronto 311 service; Housing Help Centre 416-285-9666. Finding safe neighborhoods: check Toronto Police Service Crime Map online, visit in person day and evening, talk to local residents. Required documentation: proof of income (sponsor letter, pay stub), ID (refugee claimant document, passport), proof of status (COPR or refugee protection claimant document).
