Future plan: Considering a performance camshaft upgrade after suspension work.

Key considerations for camshaft selection:
- Define goals: horsepower, torque, power band (low-end vs high-rpm), use case (daily/track/drag)
- Understand specs: Lift (valve opening distance), Duration (valve open time), Overlap (both valves open), LSA (lobe separation angle)
- Choose type: Hydraulic flat tappet (daily), Hydraulic roller (improved performance), Solid roller (high-performance, high-maintenance)
- Select profile: Stock replacement, Performance, or Aggressive
- Consider compatibility with other mods (headers, intake, exhaust)
- Research and compare before deciding
- Consult with professional engine builder/tuner for final selection

This is a future consideration pending completion of suspension and wheel upgrades.
