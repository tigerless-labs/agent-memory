Recommendations for games similar to Ticket to Ride (strategy-focused):\n- Settlers of Catan (resource management, trading, building)\n- Carcassonne (tile-laying, route-building)\n- El Grande (area control, strategy)\n- 7 Wonders (card-drafting, civilization building)\n- Pandemic (cooperative, resource management)\n- Azul (tile-laying, mosaic building)\n- Splendor (card-collecting, resource management)\n- Elfenland (route-building, similar to Ticket to Ride)\n- Russian Railroads (train-themed, more complex)\n- Trains (deck-building, train routes)\n\nGames suitable for large groups (6-8 players):\n- Wits & Wagers (betting/trivia)\n- Codenames (word-guessing, party)\n- Taboo (word-describing)\n- Deception: Murder in Hong Kong (social deduction, investigator role)\n- Two Rooms and a Boom (social deduction, team-based)\n- The Resistance (social deduction, mission-based)\n- Monikers (party/guessing)\n- Junk Art (dexterity)\n- Spyfall (social deduction)\n- What Do You Meme (party/caption cards)
