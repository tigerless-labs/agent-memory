Resources for finding minimalist art prints with light color schemes:

- **Society6**: Vast collection with filtering by color palette
- **Minted**: Focus on simplicity and elegance with "Light and Airy" category
- **Etsy**: Independent artists, search 'minimalist art print', 'light color scheme', 'pastel art'
- **Desenio**: Scandinavian design focus with light and airy options
- **AllPosters**: Filters by style, color, and size
- **20x200**: Affordable limited-edition prints from established and emerging artists
- **The Poster Club**: Curated selection with "Light and Minimal" category
- **Art.com**: Extensive collection with style and color filtering
- Museum sites (MoMA, Guggenheim): High-quality art reproductions from collections
