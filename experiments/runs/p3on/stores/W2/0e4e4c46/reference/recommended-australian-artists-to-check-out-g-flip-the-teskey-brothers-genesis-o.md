Recommended up-and-coming Australian artists:

- **G Flip** — Melbourne-based singer, songwriter, and producer known for catchy pop-rock anthems and impressive live performances
- **The Teskey Brothers** — Melbourne soulful blues and roots band with a vintage sound
- **Genesis Owusu** — Ghanaian-Australian rapper and songwriter blending hip-hop, funk, and soul
- **Tia Gostelow** — Singer-songwriter from Far North Queensland, indie pop with poignant lyrics
- **A.GIRL** — Sydney-based R&B artist with soulful vocals and emotional songwriting
