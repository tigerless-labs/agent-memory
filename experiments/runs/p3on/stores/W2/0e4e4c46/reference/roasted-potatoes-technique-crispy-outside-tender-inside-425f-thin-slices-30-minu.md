Technique for roasted potatoes with crispy exterior and tender interior:

**Preparation:**
- Use Russet or Idaho potatoes
- Cut thin (~1/4 inch wedges/slices)
- Soak in cold water 30+ minutes to remove starch
- Drain and pat dry thoroughly

**Cooking:**
- Preheat baking sheet in 425°F oven for 5–7 minutes
- Use high-heat oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- Add potatoes in single layer; don't overcrowd
- Flip and rotate at 20–25 minutes
- Check for crispiness at 30–35 minutes
- Optional: broil 2–3 minutes for extra crispiness

**Seasoning:**
- Mix minced garlic, fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme, parsley), salt, pepper with oil
- Optional: top with grated Parmesan and fresh parsley
