## Recommended stays near Glenfinnan Viaduct:

1. **The Glenfinnan Sleeping Car** — luxury train carriage, up to four guests
2. **Glenfinnan House Hotel** — historic hotel with views of Loch Shiel and surrounding mountains
3. **The Prince's House Hotel** — four-star hotel in the Highlands with luxury accommodations and dining
4. **Lochailort Inn** — traditional Scottish inn, short distance from viaduct
5. **Corran Bunkhouse** — budget-friendly option, less than 5 miles from viaduct, dormitory and private rooms available
