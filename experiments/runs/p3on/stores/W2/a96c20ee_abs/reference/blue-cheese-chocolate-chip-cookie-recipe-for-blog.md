Recipe uses 2 1/4 cups flour, 1 cup butter, 1 cup sugar, 1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese, 2 eggs, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips, 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt. Bake at 375F for 12-15 minutes. Inspired by blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local shop. Took multiple attempts to balance flavors so blue cheese is subtle but intriguing rather than overpowering. Pairs with red wine or dark beer.
