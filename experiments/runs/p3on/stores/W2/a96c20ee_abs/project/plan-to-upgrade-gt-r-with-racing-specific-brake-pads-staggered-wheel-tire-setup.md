Decided on the following GT-R modifications:

1. **Brake pads**: Racing-specific pads chosen for handling repeated heavy braking and high-temperature conditions during drifting
2. **Wheels & Tires**: Staggered setup (wider rear wheels) for aggressive stance and improved drifting capability, inspired by professional drifters
3. **Exhaust system**: Full exhaust (not cat-back) to unlock engine potential, provide more power gains, and improve throttle response

Will also consider engine tuning/management system to optimize performance with new exhaust.
