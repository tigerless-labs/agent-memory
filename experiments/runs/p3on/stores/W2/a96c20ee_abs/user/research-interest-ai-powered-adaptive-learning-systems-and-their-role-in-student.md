Researching AI in education with focus on:

- **Personalized learning**: How AI tailors instruction to individual students' needs, abilities, and learning styles
- **Adaptive learning systems**: Effectiveness of real-time difficulty adjustment and real-time feedback
- **Real-time feedback mechanisms**: How immediate feedback affects student learning outcomes and motivation
- **Critical thinking and problem-solving**: AI's role in developing metacognitive skills and complex reasoning
- **Collaborative problem-solving**: AI-facilitated team collaboration across locations and learning styles
- **Intelligent tutoring systems (ITS)**: One-on-one personalized support using NLP and cognitive models

Key interest areas: student engagement, metacognition development, personalized scaffolding, multi-modal feedback.
