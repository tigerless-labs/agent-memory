Values how nature creates peacefulness, solitude, and escape for story characters. Recognizes nature as both setting and metaphorical character in literature. Interested in literary analysis of themes like personal identity, trauma, relationships, cultural identity, growth, mental health, and social issues as they appear in character-driven narratives.
