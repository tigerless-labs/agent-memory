Toulouse is a major European aerospace hub with key companies: Airbus, ATR, and Liebherr. Recent developments:

- Airbus A320 wing production based in Toulouse
- French government's 'Plan Aéro' initiative (launched June 2020) invests €15 billion over three years in the aerospace sector
- Industry focus on sustainable technologies: hydrogen-powered aircraft, electric propulsion systems, lightweight materials
- Thousands of highly skilled jobs in aerospace and related sectors (logistics, transportation, research, development)
- Attracts foreign investment from US, Asia, and Middle East

Toulouse positioned as leader in sustainable aviation technology development and a model for how industries can balance economic growth with environmental responsibility.
