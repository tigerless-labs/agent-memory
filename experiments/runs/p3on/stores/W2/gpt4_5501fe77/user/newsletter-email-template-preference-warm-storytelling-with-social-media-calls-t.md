Preferred format for newsletter emails to subscribers:

OPENING: 'Dear [Subscriber],'

BODY STRUCTURE: 
- Tell a personal or biographical story in 2-3 substantial paragraphs
- Include direct quotes when available
- Weave in specific details, dates, and facts
- Create emotional connection through narrative rather than hard sell

CALL-TO-ACTION:
- Encourage following on social media for latest shows, releases, and behind-the-scenes content
- Emphasize community and gratitude for subscriber support

CLOSING:
- Signature line: 'Rock on!'
- Sign-off: 'Best regards, [Artist/Band Name]'

TONE:
- Warm and engaging
- Personalizes the artist's journey and struggles
- Grateful toward the subscriber community
- Inspirational without being preachy

Used successfully for Gary Dranow and the Manic Emotions newsletter series (2023).
