Attended a workshop on social media marketing where I met a woman who works for a non-profit organization using social media to promote social change.

**What stood out:** The effectiveness of their social media strategy in raising awareness about critical issues and mobilizing people to take action. Particularly impressed by their use of Instagram stories for personal testimonials and highlighting impact.

**Outcome:** Exchanged contact information with the woman and have been following her organization's social media accounts since then. This experience reinforced my belief in social media's power to break silence and stigma around important issues like mental health.

**Application:** The strategies and inspiration from this workshop directly informed the #BreakTheSilenceMH campaign I'm creating.
