Interactive stickers to use in Stories:
- Poll stickers: Yes/no or multiple choice questions
- Quiz stickers: Test follower knowledge
- Chat stickers: Encourage direct messages
- Slider stickers: Rate something on a scale
- Question sticker: Ask open-ended questions (already effective for user)

Other strategies:
- Swipe-up feature: Drive traffic to website, blog, or exclusive content
- Story series: Multi-part tutorials, day-in-the-life, behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions
- Story Highlights: Curate stories into themed collections (Behind Scenes, Tutorials, Testimonials, etc.)
- Collaborations: Partner with influencers or friends for fresh content
- Exclusive deals: Share limited-time discounts and early access
- Instagram Story Ads: Target specific audiences to drive traffic
- Time strategically: Post during peak hours, maintain consistent presence
- Encourage UGC: Ask followers to share using branded hashtag
- Monitor performance: Use Instagram Insights to track and adjust strategy
