Gary Dranow is a musician and founder of The Manic Emotions band based in Salt Lake City.

DESTINY ROAD ALBUM:
- Initially written in the 90s with intention to release summer 1998
- Recorded on Jefferson Airplane's old 2-inch 16 Track machine owned by producer Jerry Manfredi
- Recording interrupted: Gary moved to Park City, Utah and never mastered the tracks
- Spent 10 years focusing on ski racing and coaching (approximately 1998-2008)
- Album remained unfinished and unreleased during this period

CAREER TIMELINE:
- 2008: Dranow got back into music
- 2021: Re-established The Manic Emotions with band members in Salt Lake City area
- Immediately began performing live shows after reforming the band

PERSONAL & CREATIVE INFLUENCES:
- Struggles with manic depression, which has fueled his creativity
- Music speaks to the human experience and personal struggles
- Known as a consummate performer with high energy, charisma, and passion
- His song 'Made it Another Day' addresses the experience of personal sensitivity and daily struggles with the metaphor 'Like a rat in a wheel'

OUTLOOK:
As of 2023: Thrilled to be performing and sharing music with fans again, with plans to continue into 2023 and beyond.
