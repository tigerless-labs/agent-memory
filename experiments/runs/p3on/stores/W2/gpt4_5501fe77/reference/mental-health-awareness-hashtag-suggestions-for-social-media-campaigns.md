Collection of hashtags for mental health awareness campaigns:

1. #MentalHealthMatters — Simple, straightforward
2. #BreakTheSilenceMH — Encourages people to speak up (chosen for campaign)
3. #MindOverStigma — Playful, tackles stigma
4. #YouAreNotAloneMH — Reassuring, community-focused
5. #SpeakYourMind — Empowering
6. #MentalHealthUnfiltered — Promotes authenticity
7. #HopeInDarkness — Offers hope and support
8. #MentalHealthJourney — Acknowledges ongoing process
9. #Let'sTalkMH — Approachable, conversation-starter
10. #RiseAboveMH — Motivational

**Selection tips:**
- Easy to remember and spell
- Unique and not already heavily used
- Aligns with campaign tone/message
- Short and concise
- Use consistently across all platforms
