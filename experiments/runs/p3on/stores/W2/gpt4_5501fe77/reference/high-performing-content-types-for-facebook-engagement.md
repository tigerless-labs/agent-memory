Content types that perform well on Facebook:

Videos: Short, engaging, informative videos showcasing products/services/company culture
Listicles: Bite-sized lists providing valuable information or tips
Behind-the-scenes: Exclusive looks at events, product development, employee spotlights
User-generated content (UGC): Customer/fan content showcasing brand experiences
How-to guides and tutorials: Step-by-step educational content
Inspirational content: Quotes, stories, images that motivate audience
Humorous content: Memes, GIFs, videos that entertain
Exclusive offers: Limited-time deals, discounts, freebies

Key principles: Know audience interests/pain points; be authentic; use high-quality visuals; keep captions concise; ask questions to spark conversation; provide value; use storytelling; leverage trends; make it interactive; optimize for mobile; be consistent.
