Online writing resources:\n\n**Writing Prompt Sites:**\n- Writing.com — community-driven platform with writing prompts, exercises, activities\n- The Daily Post — WordPress blog with daily writing prompts on various topics\n- Reddit r/WritingPrompts — subreddit with daily prompts and supportive community\n- The Write Practice — offers writing prompts, exercises, and craft resources\n- 750 Words — encourages daily 750-word writing with optional prompts and motivation tools\n\n**Poetry Resources:**\n- The Poetry Foundation — vast online collection of poetry, articles, resources\n- Poets.org — poetry resources including prompts, exercises, poetry dictionary\n- NaPoWriMo — National Poetry Writing Month challenge (April) with daily prompts and community support
