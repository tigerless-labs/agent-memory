Instagram Posting Times: Peak engagement 12-1pm (lunch) and 5-6pm (post-work); Best days: Tue-Wed-Thu; Avoid 5-7am and 9-11pm.

Facebook Posting Times: Peak engagement 12-3pm (lunch/afternoon); Best days: Thu-Fri-Sun; Avoid 5-7am and 9-11pm.

Test different times with your audience using Instagram Insights and Facebook Page Insights analytics to find your specific optimal schedule.
