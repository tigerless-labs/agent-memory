Recommended books for poetry and writing development:\n\n- **The Artist's Way** by Julia Cameron — 12-week program to discover creative self; includes Morning Pages technique\n- **Writing Down the Bones** by Natalie Goldberg — Collection of writing exercises and prompts\n- **The 3 A.M. Epiphany** by Brian Kitely — Writing exercises to generate new ideas and perspectives\n- **The Ode Less Travelled** by Stephen Fry — Explores art of writing poetry with focus on form and technique\n\nKey techniques mentioned: freewriting, stream-of-consciousness writing, sense memory exercises, character development exercises.
