Key TikTok growth strategies:

Content development: Identify and focus on specific niche; develop consistent visual style/tone; post high-quality videos with good camera, editing, and sound; use catchy, relevant music; leverage hashtags (mix popular + niche).

Community engagement: Interact with other users (comments, likes); respond to comments on own videos; collaborate with other creators; use Duet feature; feature user-generated content.

Posting strategy: Post consistently on regular schedule; time videos for peak audience activity (after school, lunch breaks, evenings); post when audience is most active.

Growth tactics: Run contests, dance challenges, or Q&A sessions; share exclusive deals and behind-the-scenes content; cross-promote on other social platforms; offer promo codes or exclusive content.

Analytics and optimization: Use TikTok Analytics to track performance; identify what works; stay up-to-date with trends and algorithm changes; be authentic and creative; express personality and passions.
