Organizations that provide music therapy services to children's hospitals and other healthcare settings:

- **Music Therapy Inc.**: Non-profit providing music therapy services to children's hospitals, hospices, and healthcare settings
- **The Children's Music Fund**: Non-profit providing music therapy to children with serious illnesses, disabilities, or in need of emotional support
- **The Nathaniel Anthony Ayers Foundation**: Non-profit providing music therapy and education to children and adults with mental and physical disabilities
- **Melodic Caring Project**: Non-profit providing music therapy and live music performances to children's hospitals and hospices
- **Songs for Kids**: Non-profit providing music therapy and songwriting programs to children's hospitals, schools, and communities

These organizations often develop and implement music therapy programs with children's hospitals and may offer volunteer opportunities or donation programs.
