Campaign goal: Raise awareness about mental health issues and encourage people to share personal stories to break silence and reduce stigma.

**Hashtag:** #BreakTheSilenceMH

**Platforms:** Instagram and Twitter

**Content Strategy:**
- Mix of personal stories, statistics, mental health resources, quotes, and community engagement
- 4-week content calendar:
  - Week 1: Introduction and awareness
  - Week 2: Personal stories and experiences
  - Week 3: Resources and support
  - Week 4: Action and community building

**Key tactics:**
- Share personal experiences to create emotional connection
- Feature user-generated content
- Provide calls-to-action encouraging followers to share their own stories
- Partner with mental health influencers/advocates
- Track engagement with branded hashtag

Inspired by workshop experience and examples from non-profit organizations using social media for social change.
