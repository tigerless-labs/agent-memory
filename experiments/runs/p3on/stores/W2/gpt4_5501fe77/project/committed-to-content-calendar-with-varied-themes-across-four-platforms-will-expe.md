Started building social media content calendar with mix of:
- Behind-the-scenes content showing daily life
- Hobby-related posts and tips
- TV show/movie updates and discussions
- Interactive content (Q&A sessions, sneak peeks)
- Personal achievements and wrap-ups

Platforms: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok

Plan to optimize posting times by:
- Using Instagram Insights to see when followers are most active
- Experimenting with different times and tracking performance
- Considering audience demographics and schedules (students vs. professionals)
- Using third-party tools like Hootsuite, Sprout Social, or Buffer for detailed analytics
- General guidelines: Facebook/Twitter peak 12-3pm; TikTok peak 3-5pm

Aim for 3-5 stories per day, spaced out to avoid audience fatigue. Will maintain consistent posting schedule to build expectations.
