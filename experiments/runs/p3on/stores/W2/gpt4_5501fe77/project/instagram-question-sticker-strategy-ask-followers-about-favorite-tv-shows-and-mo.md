Plan to use Instagram's Question sticker feature with open-ended questions about TV shows and movies:
- Example prompts: 'What's your favorite TV show to binge-watch on a lazy Sunday? 📺' and 'What's the best movie you've seen recently? 🎥'
- Keep questions open-ended and easy to answer
- Engage with followers who respond by liking/commenting on their answers
- Use relevant hashtags to increase discoverability
- Pin questions to top of story to keep them visible
- Monitor responses using Instagram Insights to understand audience interests
