As of May 29 2023: Twitter account grew from 420 to 540 followers in one month. Current posting frequency is 5-7 tweets per day with a mix of educational and personal content. Actively engages by responding to comments and DMs but lacks a formal content calendar. Previous experience with Twitter Chats found valuable. Plans to host Q&A sessions to boost engagement, targeting 2-3% engagement rate minimum. Promotion tactics include countdown tweets, graphics, email newsletter, influencer collaboration, and tracking via Twitter Analytics and Bitly.
