In May 2023, the user was actively searching for a postdoctoral position but reported that no one was offering positions. This created emotional strain, leading to questions about overcoming regret and seeking comforting support.

Recommended strategies discussed:
- Networking with colleagues, professors, and researchers at conferences
- Expanding search to related fields/industries and international opportunities
- Tailoring CV and cover letter; seeking mentor feedback on application materials
- Reaching out to interviewers for feedback after rejections
- Persistence through a long application process

The user also sought advice on processing difficult emotions (regret, uncertainty) during the job search.
