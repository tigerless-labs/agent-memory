## Brands & Models

### Kala
- KA-15S Mahogany Soprano: $70-$100 - warm, rich sound
- KA-C Mahogany Concert: $100-$150 - 15" scale, 1.375" neck width, balanced mid-range

### Fender
- Seaside Soprano: $50-$80 - bright crisp sound
- Montecito Tenor: $150-$200 - larger body, full sound

### Lanikai
- LU-21 Soprano: $70-$100 - well-balanced, warm sound
- LU-21T Tenor: $100-$150 - deeper sound, 1.5" neck width

### Cordoba
- **20TM Tenor: $150-$200** - all-mahogany, 17" scale, rich full-bodied sound, excellent setup
- 20CM Concert: $100-$150 - bright clear sound

### Mahalo
- MR1 Soprano: $30-$50 - budget-friendly, bright
- MR3 Concert: $50-$80 - fuller sound than soprano

## Tonewoods
- **Mahogany**: warm, rich, balanced mid-range tone
- **Koa**: brighter, trebly, high-end presence, beautiful curly grain

## Ukulele Sizes
- **Soprano**: smallest (13-14" scale), brightest, tightest tone, easier for small hands
- **Concert**: medium (15-16" scale), warmer than soprano, good balance
- **Tenor**: largest (17" scale), fullest sound, deepest tone, best for large hands
