**Recommended citrus bitters brands for Negroni:**

1. **Regans' Orange Bitters No. 6** — Classic choice; blend of citrus peels (orange, lemon, grapefruit) adds subtle, warm citrus
2. **The Bitter Truth Orange Bitters** — German-made; vibrant and citrusy from orange, lemon, grapefruit peels
3. **Fee Brothers Orange Bitters** — Well-respected brand; slightly sweeter with citrus-forward profile
4. **Peychaud's Orange Bitters** — New Orleans-based (since 1800s); citrusy and slightly sweet with spice and herb hints
5. **Scrappy's Bitters Orange Bitters** — Craft brand; small-batch with bold, citrusy flavor from blend of citrus peels

**Usage:** Start with 2-3 dashes and adjust to taste. Bitters are potent; a little goes a long way.

**Effect on Negroni:** Citrus-forward bitters add brightness and depth to the drink, particularly when paired with citrus-forward gin. They're considered a subtle accent rather than a primary flavor component.
