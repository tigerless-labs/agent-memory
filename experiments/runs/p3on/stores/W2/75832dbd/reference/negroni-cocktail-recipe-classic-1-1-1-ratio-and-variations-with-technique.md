**Classic Negroni Recipe**

**Standard 1:1:1 ratio (most widely accepted):**
- 1 part Gin
- 1 part Campari
- 1 part Sweet Vermouth

**Measurement example:**
- 1.5 oz (45 ml) Gin
- 1.5 oz (45 ml) Campari
- 1.5 oz (45 ml) Sweet Vermouth

**Preparation:**
1. Stir all three ingredients with ice
2. Strain into chilled coupe or rocks glass filled with ice
3. Garnish with orange twist

**Ratio variations for personal preference:**
- **2:1:1** — Gin-forward, stronger botanical presence
- **1:2:1** — Campari-forward, bolder and more bitter
- **1:1:2** — Sweet vermouth-forward, richer and sweeter

**Key principle:** The 1:1:1 ratio is the classic starting point. Adjust ratios to find your preferred balance and flavor profile.
