A recommended balanced meal plan for weight loss emphasizing nutrient-dense foods:

**Breakfast:** 2 scrambled eggs with spinach, mushrooms, red peppers
**Snacks:** Small apple with 1 tbsp almond butter; 1 cup mixed berries with ½ cup non-fat Greek yogurt; 1 piece dark chocolate (optional)
**Lunch:** Grilled chicken breast with mixed greens, cucumbers, tomatoes, balsamic vinaigrette
**Dinner:** Baked salmon with roasted asparagus and sweet potato

Key principles: portion control, nutrient-dense low-calorie foods, adequate water intake. Safe and sustainable weight loss target is 1-2 pounds per week.
