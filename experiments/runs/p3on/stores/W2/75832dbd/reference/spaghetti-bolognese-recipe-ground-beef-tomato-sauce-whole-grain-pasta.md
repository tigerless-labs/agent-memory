- Ground beef
- Onions
- Garlic
- Tomato sauce
- Whole-grain spaghetti
- Fresh herbs (optional)
- Side: steamed broccoli

**Method:** Cook ground beef with onions, garlic, and tomato sauce. Serve with whole-grain spaghetti and broccoli.
