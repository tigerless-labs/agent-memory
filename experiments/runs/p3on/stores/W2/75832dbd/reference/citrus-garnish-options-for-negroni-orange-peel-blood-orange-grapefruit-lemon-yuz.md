**Recommended citrus garnish options for Negroni:**

1. **Orange peel** — Classic pairing; express oils over drink and use as garnish
2. **Blood orange peel** — Deeper, complex flavor; adds subtle sweetness and color
3. **Grapefruit twist** (pink or red) — Pairs with Campari's bitterness and gin's botanicals; tangy and citrusy
4. **Lemon twist** — Bright citrusy note; choose thick-skinned lemons for maximum flavor
5. **Yuzu peel** — Japanese citrus with tart and sweet profile; fascinating twist pairing with gin and Campari
6. **Meyer lemon twist** — Cross between lemon and orange; sweeter and less acidic than regular lemon

**Technique:**
- Express oils from peel by gently squeezing between fingers or using citrus press
- Rub peel around glass rim to coat with citrus oils
- Place peel in glass on top of ice or as final garnish

**Key principle:** Different citrus options complement different aspects of the Negroni. Experiment with combinations to create unique flavor profiles.
