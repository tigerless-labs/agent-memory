**Recommended gin brands for Negroni:**

1. **Plymouth Gin** — Slightly sweeter, subtle flavor; fruit and spice notes complement Campari's bitterness
2. **Bombay Sapphire** — Crisp, citrusy London Dry; moderate juniper presence cuts through Campari
3. **Beefeater London Dry** — Bold juniper with subtle fruit notes; stands up to Campari's bitterness
4. **G'Vine Floraison** — French grape-based gin with floral notes (grapeflower, jasmine) for unique twist
5. **Sipsmith London Dry** — Juniper-forward with sweetness and citrus; well-rounded balance
6. **The Botanist** — 22-botanical blend offering complexity; subtle fruit and floral notes
7. **Death's Door Gin** (Wisconsin) — Crisp and clean with juniper, coriander, citrus; modern take
8. **Ransom Old Tom Gin** — Sweeter, botanical-driven for historical twist on Negroni

**Key principle:** The gin should balance with Campari without overpowering it. Experiment with different styles to find preferred flavor profile.
