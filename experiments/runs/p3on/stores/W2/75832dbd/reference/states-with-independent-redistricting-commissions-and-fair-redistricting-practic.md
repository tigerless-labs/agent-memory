**California (2010):** 14-member independent redistricting commission, members selected through public application process, must be impartial.

**Arizona (2000):** 5-member independent redistricting commission, selected through public application process, members cannot be elected officials or political party officials.

**Iowa:** Nonpartisan redistricting process in place for decades, led by nonpartisan legislative services agency using population data and objective criteria, legislature votes on proposals.

**Colorado (2018):** 12-member independent redistricting commission, members selected by panel of retired judges, must be impartial.

**Key strategies for fair redistricting:**
- Independent commissions staffed by nonpartisan/impartial members
- Transparent processes with public input opportunities
- Objective criteria focused on equal population distribution and Voting Rights Act adherence
- Avoidance of partisan criteria (voting history, party affiliation)
- Judicial review to ensure constitutionality and fairness
