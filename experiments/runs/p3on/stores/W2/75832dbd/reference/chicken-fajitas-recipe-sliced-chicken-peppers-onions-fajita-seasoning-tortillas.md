Chicken Fajitas recipe (wanted to make):

**Ingredients:**
- Chicken breast, sliced into strips
- Bell peppers
- Onions
- Fajita seasoning
- Whole-grain tortillas
- Avocado
- Salsa

**Method:** Sauté chicken strips with peppers, onions, and fajita seasoning. Serve in tortillas with avocado and salsa.
