**Recommended sweet vermouth brands for Negroni:**

1. **Cinzano** — Classic Italian; sweet with vanilla and spice notes
2. **Carpano Antica Formula** — Favorite among bartenders; rich and complex with cherry, orange, spice
3. **Dolin** — Chambery style; lighter and more delicate, won't overpower other ingredients
4. **Martini & Rossi** — Italian; sweeter than Cinzano with pronounced fruit flavor, works well with bold gins
5. **Punt e Mes** — Italian; bitter-sweet profile enhances Campari while balancing gin
6. **Cocchi** — Herbal and spicy blend; adds complexity to the drink
7. **Noilly Prat** — French; rich and fruity with vanilla and orange, slightly sweeter

**Selection guide:**
- For **traditional/bitter Negroni**: Punt e Mes or Cocchi
- For **sweeter Negroni**: Carpano Antica Formula or Martini & Rossi
- For **balanced Negroni**: Dolin or Cinzano

**Key principle:** Sweet vermouth balances the bitterness of Campari and botanicals of gin. Choose based on desired flavor intensity.
