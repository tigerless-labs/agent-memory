Contemporary Romance with Strong Female Protagonists: The Proposal by Jasmine Guillory, The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo by Taylor Jenkins Reid, Eleanor Oliphant Is Completely Fine by Gail Honeyman, The Flatshare by Beth O'Leary, Normal People by Sally Rooney, Red White and Royal Blue by Casey McQuiston. Detective/Crime Romance: In Death series by J.D. Robb, The Rizzoli and Isles series by Tess Gerritsen. Steamy/Spicy Romance: Fifty Shades of Grey by E.L. James, Bared to You by Sylvia Day, Beautiful Disaster by Jamie McGuire, The Crossfire series by Sylvia Day, Gabriel's Inferno by Sylvain Reynard, Crash Into You by Katie McGarry, Kushiel's Dart by Jacqueline Carey.
