Beginner ukulele purchase in active consideration. After researching soprano vs concert sizes and mahogany vs koa characteristics, leaning toward the Cordoba 20TM Tenor Ukulele ($150-$200) for its warm, full-bodied sound, strong low-end presence, and excellent build quality/setup. Also evaluated: Kala KA-C Mahogany Concert ($100-$150), Lanikai LU-21T Tenor ($100-$150). Currently deciding between hardcase (maximum protection, weather-resistant, durable, but heavy/expensive) vs softcase (lightweight/portable/affordable, but limited protection/not weather-resistant). Cordoba 20TM specs: Tenor body, 17" scale length, 1.5" neck width, all-mahogany with ornate rosette.
