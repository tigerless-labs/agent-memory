Meal preps regularly using reusable containers from Thrive Market. Storage practice:
- Cools meals to room temperature within 2 hours after cooking
- Refrigerates within 30 minutes
- Labels containers with date and reheating instructions
- Stores in coldest part of refrigerator, organized by meal type
- Freezes meals for 3-4 month storage when needed
- Reheats to internal temp 165°F before eating
- Discards refrigerated meals after 5 days
