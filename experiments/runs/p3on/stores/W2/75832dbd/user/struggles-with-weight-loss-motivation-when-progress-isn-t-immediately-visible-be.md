User finds it hard to stay motivated during weight loss when scale doesn't show immediate changes. Responds well to celebrating alternative measures of progress: better energy, improved mood, clothes fitting better, better sleep, reduced stress. Benefits from supportive people, small achievable goals, tracking progress, and regular rewards. Finds it helpful to focus on the process and lifestyle changes rather than just the final outcome.
