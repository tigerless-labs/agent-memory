Shows interest in integrating insights from:
- Buddhist philosophy (specifically Jhana meditation practice and the 8 levels of concentration)
- Christian mysticism (particularly Meister Eckhart's teaching on the eye-seeing-God paradox and immanence of the divine)
- Using these traditions to explore themes of resilience, curiosity, and the limits of technology

Seeks to bridge these traditions without belonging exclusively to either, finding common ground in direct experience of the sacred.
