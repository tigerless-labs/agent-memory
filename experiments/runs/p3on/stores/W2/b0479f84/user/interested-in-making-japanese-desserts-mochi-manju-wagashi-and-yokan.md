Particularly interested in learning to cook and make traditional Japanese sweets/desserts:
- Mochi (glutinous rice cakes with sweet fillings)
- Manju (steamed buns with sweet bean paste)
- Wagashi (traditional Japanese confections)
- Yokan (jellied dessert made from red bean paste, agar, and sugar) - wants to try making this

Learned about wagashi through a discussion in the Book Club Facebook group while reading a novel set in Japan. Interested in the aesthetic and cultural aspects of these desserts, not just the taste.
