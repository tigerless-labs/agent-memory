Actively pursues running fitness with emphasis on:
- Consistent weekly schedule (3-mile jogs)
- Proper running form and technique
- Comprehensive warm-up and cool-down routines
- Hydration strategy during runs
- Injury prevention (particularly shin splints)
- Progressive training (hill repeats, increased mileage)
- Targeted strength training: glutes, hamstrings, quads, core, ankles
- Cross-training and low-impact alternatives

Prefers structured, evidence-based approaches to fitness with attention to detail on form, recovery, and injury prevention.
