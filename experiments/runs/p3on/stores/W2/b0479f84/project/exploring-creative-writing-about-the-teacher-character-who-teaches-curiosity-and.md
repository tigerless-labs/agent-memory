Working on or exploring philosophical/creative storytelling involving a character called 'the Teacher' who:
- Teaches about curiosity and resilience as core values
- Engages with people from different worldviews (journalists, computer scientists, religious practitioners)
- Challenges assumptions and invites deeper thinking
- Draws on wisdom from multiple traditions without being limited to any single one

Episodes explored:
1. Teacher's dialogue with a journalist about seeking untold perspectives
2. Teacher's dialogue with an LLM researcher about limits of technology in fostering human qualities
3. Teacher's dialogue with fundamentalist Christians, using Meister Eckhart to bridge Eastern and Western spiritual insight

Central tension: Technology vs. human resilience and curiosity; Eastern philosophy meets Christian mysticism.
