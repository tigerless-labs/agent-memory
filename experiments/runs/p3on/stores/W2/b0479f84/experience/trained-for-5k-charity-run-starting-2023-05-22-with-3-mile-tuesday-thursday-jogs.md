Started training for a 5K charity run on 2023-05-22. Established a regular running routine of 3-mile jogs every Tuesday and Thursday morning. Focused on:
- Running technique: proper posture, midfoot footstrike, cadence 160-170 steps/min, arm swing, core engagement
- Warm-up/cool-down: light cardio, leg swings, hip circles, knee lifts, calf raises, arm circles, static stretches
- Hydration: 16-20 oz water before run; sports drink/water every 10-15 min on longer runs
- Injury prevention: shin splints, proper footwear, ankle strengthening, running form, lower-impact surfaces
- Hill repeats: 1-3 min climbs, 3-5 repeats initially, focusing on form and consistent pace
- Strength training: glute bridges, squats, lunges, deadlifts, leg curls, leg press, leg extensions, step-ups

Goal: build endurance and strength for 5K performance.
