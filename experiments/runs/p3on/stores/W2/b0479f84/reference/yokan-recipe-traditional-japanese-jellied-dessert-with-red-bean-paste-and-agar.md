**Yokan Recipe:**

Ingredients:
- 1 cup red bean paste (available at Asian markets or online)
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 cups water
- 2 tablespoons agar powder (available at Asian markets or online)
- Food coloring (optional)

Instructions:
1. Prepare the red bean paste (drain/rinse if canned, or use homemade smooth paste)
2. In a medium saucepan, combine sugar and water, heat over medium heat, stirring until sugar dissolves
3. Mix agar powder with a little water to make a smooth paste, add to sugar syrup and stir well
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for about 10 minutes until mixture thickens and agar is fully dissolved
5. Add red bean paste to agar mixture and stir well to combine
6. Add food coloring if desired
7. Pour into rectangular mold or individual molds, let cool and set at room temperature
8. Refrigerate until chilled, cut into cubes or slices and serve

Tips:
- Use high-quality agar powder specifically designed for making yokan
- Adjust sugar amount to taste (yokan can be quite sweet)
- Experiment with flavors like green tea, sesame, or yuzu for variations
