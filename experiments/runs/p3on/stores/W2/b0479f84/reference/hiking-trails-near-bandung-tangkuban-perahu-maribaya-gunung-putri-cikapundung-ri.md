## Bandung Hiking Trails\n\n**Tangkuban Perahu Volcano** (25km N) — 1.5 hours to crater, lush rainforests, panoramic views, sulfuric smoke\n\n**Maribaya Waterfall** (15km NE) — 45 min, moderate difficulty\n\n**Gunung Putri** (20km out) — 4-5 hours, challenging, 360° views of city + volcano + forests\n\n**Cikapundung River** — 5km leisurely river walk, local favorite\n\n**Ciwidey Valley** (1hr drive S) — 2-3 hours, tea plantations, rice paddies, waterfall
