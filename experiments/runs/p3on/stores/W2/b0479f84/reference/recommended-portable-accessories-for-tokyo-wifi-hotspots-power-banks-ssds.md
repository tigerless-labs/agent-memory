Portable accessories recommendations for Tokyo-based mobile work:

**Portable WiFi Hotspot options in Japan:**
- Major carriers: NTT Docomo (Wi-Fi STATION), au KDDI (Wi-Fi WALKER), SoftBank (4G LTE Wi-Fi)
- MVNOs: UQ WiMAX, Y!mobile, LINE Mobile
- Rental: Japan WiFi (¥540/day), Wi2 (¥648/day), Pupuru (¥698/day)
- Purchase locations: Yodobashi Camera, Bic Camera, Amazon Japan

**Portable Power Bank recommendations:**
- Anker PowerCore 10000 - Slim, 10,000mAh, 220g
- Mophie Powerstation Mini - Compact, 5,000mAh
- Jackery Titan 20000 - 20,000mAh
- Anker PowerCore Fusion - Designed for laptops
- Mophie Powerstation AC - 22,000mAh with built-in AC outlet
- Eggtronic Power Bank - 20,000mAh with multiple USB ports

**Portable SSD recommendations:**
- Samsung T5 - 2TB max, 51g, sleek design
- WD My Passport SSD - Up to 2TB, lightweight
- SanDisk Extreme Portable - Up to 1TB, rugged
- Seagate Backup Plus - Up to 5TB
- G-Technology G-Drive Mobile - Up to 4TB
- LaCie Rugged SSD - Up to 2TB, extreme durability

Considerations: Capacity, speed (USB 3.2/Thunderbolt), durability, security, compatibility, warranty.
