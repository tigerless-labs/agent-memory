## Market Locations Recommended

### Dar es Salaam
- **Mwenge Woodcarvers Market** — Known for woodcarvings and skilled craftspeople
- **Tingatinga Arts Cooperative Society** — Founded by Edward Tingatinga; features distinctive Tingatinga-style paintings with stylized animals and plants native to Tanzania

### Arusha
- **Cultural Heritage Center** — Large selection of art and crafts from different regions of Tanzania
- **Arusha Maasai Market** — Specializes in Maasai beadwork and other crafts by Maasai artisans

### Zanzibar
- **Zawadi House** (Stone Town) — Boutique offering high-quality crafts including wood carvings, textiles, and jewelry

## Bargaining Tips
- Be friendly and polite; start with small talk to build rapport
- Research comparable prices from other vendors before negotiating
- Start with a lower offer to create room for negotiation
- Be willing to walk away; vendors may call back with better prices
- Approach with positive attitude and remember vendors rely on sales for livelihood
