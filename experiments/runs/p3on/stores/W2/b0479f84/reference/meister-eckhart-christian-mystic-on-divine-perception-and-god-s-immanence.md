Meister Eckhart (c. 1260–1327), medieval Christian mystic and theologian.

Key teaching referenced:
> The eye with which I see God is the same eye with which God sees me.

This teaching emphasizes:
- The intimate connection between human perception and divine perception
- God not as an external object to grasp but as a mystery experienced through awareness
- The Kingdom of God as immanent (within each person) rather than distant
- Resonates with Jesus's teaching: 'The kingdom of God is within you'

Eckhart bridges Christian orthodoxy with experiential mysticism, making him a useful reference for integrating Eastern and Western spiritual insights.
