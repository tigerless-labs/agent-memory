Why coral reefs are crucial to ocean ecosystems:\n\n**Biodiversity**: Despite covering only 0.1% of ocean surface, reefs support 25% of all marine species, 1/3 of fish species, 800+ species of coral, endangered species (sea turtles, sharks, rays)\n\n**Shoreline Protection**: Act as natural barriers; absorb wave energy, reduce coastal erosion, mitigate storm impact, trap sediment\n\n**Nursery Grounds**: Vital for juvenile marine species to find shelter, food, and protection\n\n**Food Security**: Support 500+ million people; provide 15% of global catch (lobster, shrimp, grouper, etc.)\n\n**Economic Value**: Multi-billion-dollar tourism industry; support local economies and jobs\n\n**Medicine & Research**: Source of new drugs (cancer treatments, painkillers); unique research environment\n\n**Carbon Sequestration**: Help regulate ocean chemistry; absorb carbon dioxide; mitigate climate change effects\n\n**Indicator Species**: Reef changes signal broader environmental issues (climate change, pollution)\n\n**Major Threats**: Rising sea temperatures, ocean acidification, land-based pollutants (fertilizers, sewage)
