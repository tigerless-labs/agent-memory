**Mount Tamalpais State Park** (30-40 min from SF)\n- Steep Ravine Trail: Moderate 3.5-mile hike with scenic views of ocean and waterfalls\n- Great for team outings with mixed ability levels\n\n**Other Bay Area options within 30-45 min:**\n1. **Muir Woods National Monument** - Dipsea Trail (moderate, 4.5 miles, redwoods + ocean views)\n2. **Purisima Creek Redwoods Open Space Preserve** - Whittemore Gulch Trail (moderate, 4.5 miles)\n3. **Wunderlich County Park** - Bear Gulch Trail (moderate, 4.5 miles, redwoods)\n4. **Edgewood Park and Natural Preserve** - Edgewood Trail (moderate, 4.5 miles, grassland views)\n5. **Pulgas Ridge Open Space Preserve** - Cordilleras Trail (moderate, 4.5 miles, redwoods)\n\nAll offer moderate trails, scenic views, and suitable parking/facilities for groups.
