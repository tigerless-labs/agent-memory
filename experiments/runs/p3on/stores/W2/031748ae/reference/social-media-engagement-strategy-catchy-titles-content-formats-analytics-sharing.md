## Title Strategy
- Ask questions to engage audience
- Use numbers (e.g., "5 Digital Marketing Trends to Watch in 2023")
- Be specific, avoid generic titles
- Use action words (Discover, Learn, Grow, Transform)
- Keep under 60 characters

## Content Types That Work
- Visuals: high-quality images, infographics, videos
- Scannable format: headings, bullet points, short paragraphs
- Actionable tips and expert advice
- Post length: 300-500 words
- End with engagement question

## Sharing Personal Experiences
- Be vulnerable (share struggles and lessons learned)
- Be specific with details (metrics, timelines, challenges)
- Focus on lessons rather than just success
- Use storytelling techniques (beginning, middle, end)
- Keep concise but detailed enough for relatability

## Analytics Sharing Best Practices
- Focus on key metrics only (engagement rates, reach, traffic)
- Use visuals (charts, graphs, infographics)
- Provide context (timeframe, audience demographics, strategy changes)
- Focus on lessons learned, not just numbers
- Be honest and humble about both successes and failures
- Share general metrics without overwhelming audience

## Additional Tactics
- Consistent posting schedule
- Engage with others' content (like, comment, share)
- Use relevant hashtags
- Tag relevant connections
- Try different formats (LinkedIn Live, Stories, Document posts)
- Analyze LinkedIn analytics for performance insights
- Experiment with posting times
