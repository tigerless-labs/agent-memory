Platform workflow:

1. **YouTube outreach** → YouTube viewers discover the platform
2. **AI Health Assessment** → Personalized health risk assessment via AI chatbot for cancer, depression, anxiety, chronic kidney disease (from Diabetes/Hypertension), etc.
3. **Music Therapy** → Free access to music therapy songs for anxiety, depression, and anger management
4. **NFT Fundraising** → Buy NFT tokens to support cancer patients and fund innovations
5. **Mental Health Screening** → PHQ-2 or PHQ-10 screening questionnaires
6. **Group Counseling** → Join group counseling with counselors and specialists via Calendly platform or custom EHR booking calendar
