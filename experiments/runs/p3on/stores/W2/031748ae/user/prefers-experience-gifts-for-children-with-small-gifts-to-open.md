Favors gifting experiences like amusement parks, activity classes, and outdoor adventures to nieces and nephews over purely physical gifts. Likes pairing the experience with a small (5-10 dollar) tangible gift to open on the gift-giving day. Values experiences for creating lasting memories, strengthening bonds, and encouraging kids to try new things.
