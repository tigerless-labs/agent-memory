Ryan deeply appreciates how Wes Anderson uses color grading in *The Grand Budapest Hotel* to create mood and atmosphere. Particularly loves the bright, candy-colored palette of the hotel scenes (pinks, purples, warm yellows) that create a playful, whimsical tone. Also appreciates the contrast with darker, desaturated colors used in tense/suspenseful scenes to add depth.

Interested in visual storytelling, cinematography, and how color palettes contribute to overall film tone and emotion.
