Recommended platforms and resources for learning new skills:

General Learning Platforms: Coursera (university courses), Udemy (courses and tutorials), edX (MOOCs from top universities), Skillshare (creative skills), LinkedIn Learning (business, tech, creative)

Specialized Learning: Codecademy (programming), DataCamp (data science and statistics), HubSpot Academy (marketing and sales), Duolingo (languages), Khan Academy (free courses and lessons)

Podcasts: The Tim Ferriss Show, How I Built This, TED Radio Hour, The GaryVee Audio Experience, The School of Greatness with Lewis Howes

Other Resources: Medium (articles and essays), Quora (Q&A platform), Open Culture (free online courses and resources), The Muse (career development)
