Healthcare Programs: National Apprenticeship Program for Medical Assistants (U.S. Department of Labor), Health Career Advancement Program (multiple states), Nursing Assistant Apprenticeship Program (Wisconsin Department of Workforce Development).

Culinary Arts Programs: American Culinary Federation (ACF) Apprenticeship Program, Culinary Institute of America (CIA) Apprenticeship Program, ProStart Apprenticeship Program (National Restaurant Association Educational Foundation).

General Programs: Registered Apprenticeship Program, Pre-Apprenticeship Program, Youth Apprenticeship Program.

Key Benefits: Hands-on training in real work environments, earning while learning to avoid debt, building industry connections, flexible customizable learning, nationally recognized credentials.
