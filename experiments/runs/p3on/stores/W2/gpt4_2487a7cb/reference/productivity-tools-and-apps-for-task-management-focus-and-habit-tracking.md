Tools recommended for organizing tasks, minimizing distractions, and building focus habits:

Task Management: Todoist, Trello, Asana, Wunderlist

Focus and Distraction Blocking: RescueTime, Focus@Will, StayFocusd, Pomodoro Timer, Freedom, SelfControl, Cold Turkey, LeechBlock, uBlock Origin, News Feed Eradicator

Goal-Setting and Habit-Tracking: Habitica, Strides, Coach.me, Moment

Other: Evernote for notes, Google Calendar for scheduling
