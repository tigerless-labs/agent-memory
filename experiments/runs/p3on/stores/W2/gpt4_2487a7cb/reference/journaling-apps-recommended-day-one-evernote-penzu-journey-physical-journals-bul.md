**Digital Journaling Apps:**\n- Day One (iOS, Android) - popular, clean interface, photo integration, reminders\n- Evernote (iOS, Android) - versatile, tagging, searching, organization\n- Penzu (iOS, Android) - habit tracking, prompts, mood tracking\n- Moodfit (iOS, Android) - mood tracking with journaling\n- Journey (iOS, Android) - minimalistic, focus on daily writing\n\n**Physical Journaling Tools:**\n- Bullet Journal - customizable, goal tracking\n- Leuchtturm1917 - high-quality, dedicated sections for goals\n- Moleskine - popular, various styles\n- Traveler's Notebook - flexible, customizable\n- Prompt Cards - journaling prompts and exercises
