Has successfully implemented productivity strategies:
- Prioritizing tasks to focus on most important items first
- Breaking tasks into smaller chunks to reduce overwhelm and build momentum
- Using website blockers during focus periods
- Scheduling specific times for social media checks rather than constant access
- Setting small rewards for completing focused work sessions
- Maintaining a 'done list' to track accomplishments and stay motivated

These strategies are showing positive results for staying on top of work, though maintaining discipline around phone-checking remains a work in progress.
