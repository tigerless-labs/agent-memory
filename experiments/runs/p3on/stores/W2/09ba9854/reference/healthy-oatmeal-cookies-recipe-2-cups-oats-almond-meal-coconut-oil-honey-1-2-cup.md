## Healthy Oatmeal Cookies Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins

**Directions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease.
2. Whisk together rolled oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in large bowl.
3. In another bowl, whisk together melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla extract.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold in raisins.
6. Using cookie scoop or spoon, drop dough onto prepared baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
7. Bake 10-12 minutes, or until cookies are golden brown.
8. Cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely.

**Note:** Can substitute chocolate chips for raisins; dark chocolate chips (higher cocoa percentage) are healthier option with less added sugar than regular chocolate chips.
