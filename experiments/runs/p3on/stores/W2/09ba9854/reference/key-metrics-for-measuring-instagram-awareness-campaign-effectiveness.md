Metrics to track for Instagram 'Awareness' objective ad campaigns:

**Primary Metrics:**
- **Reach** — Total number of unique people who saw the ad
- **Impressions** — Total number of times the ad was displayed
- **Frequency** — Average number of times each person saw the ad (watch for ad fatigue)

**Engagement Metrics:**
- **Engagement Rate** — Likes, comments, saves, shares on the ad
- **Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)** — Cost efficiency of reaching 1,000 people

**Lift Metrics:**
- **Ad Recall Lift** — Measured via surveys; how many people remember the ad/brand
- **Brand Awareness Lift** — Measured via surveys/polls; increase in brand awareness in target audience

**Secondary Metrics:**
- **Follower Growth** — Increase in Instagram followers during campaign
- **Organic Engagement** — Likes, comments, shares on organic posts (indicates spillover awareness)

**Analysis Questions:**
- Is reach and impressions increasing over time?
- Is engagement rate improving?
- Is CPM decreasing (efficiency improving)?
- Are people retaining memory of the brand after seeing ads?
