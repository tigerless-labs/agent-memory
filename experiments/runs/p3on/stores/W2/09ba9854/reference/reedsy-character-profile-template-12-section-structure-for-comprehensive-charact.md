## Reedsy Character Profile Template

Free downloadable template organized in 12 sections:

1. **Basic Info** — name, age, occupation, brief description
2. **Physical Appearance** — height, weight, hair/eye color, distinguishing features
3. **Personality** — traits, values, goals, flaws
4. **Backstory** — childhood, family, education, significant life events
5. **Motivations** — what drives them, goals, obstacles
6. **Conflict** — internal and external conflicts, how they respond to challenges
7. **Relationships** — family, friends, romantic interests, enemies, social dynamics
8. **Skills and Abilities** — talents, expertise, strengths/weaknesses
9. **Fears and Phobias** — what scares them, how they react
10. **Habits and Quirks** — mannerisms, peculiarities, humanity markers
11. **Emotional Landscape** — emotional strengths and weaknesses, how they process emotion
12. **Evolution** — potential growth and change throughout story

**Level of detail:** Balanced between brevity and depth; can be expanded or customized per character needs.

**Approach:** Use as starting point; create multiple profiles for protagonist, antagonists, love interests, supporting characters.
