External hard drive recommendations for backing up photos:\n\n**Products:**\n- Western Digital My Book — popular and reliable; capacities up to 16TB; compact; built-in power management; Mac and PC compatible\n- Seagate Backup Plus — well-known option; capacities up to 5TB; compact sleek design; 2-year warranty\n- LaCie Rugged — rugged and durable; designed to withstand rough handling; capacities up to 5TB; Mac and PC compatible\n- G-Technology G-Drive — high-performance option; capacities up to 10TB; designed for creative professionals; fast transfer speeds\n\n**Factors to consider when choosing:**\nCapacity (2-3x current needs), OS compatibility (Mac or PC), interface (USB 3.0, Thunderbolt 3, USB-C), speed (7200rpm or SSD), durability/ruggedness, power (external or bus-powered), built-in security/encryption, warranty and support, portability (size/weight), price.\n\n**Backup best practice:** Use the 3-2-1 backup rule — three copies of files, two different media types, one copy offsite. Set up automatic backups and periodically verify backups can be restored.
