Tools recommended for tracking and managing social media ad performance beyond platform-native analytics:

- **Hootsuite** — Multi-platform social media management and analytics
- **Sprout Social** — Social media management with detailed analytics and reporting
- **Agorapulse** — Social media management platform with performance tracking

Native analytics also available through:
- Facebook Ads Manager (detailed campaign metrics)
- Instagram Insights (organic performance metrics)
