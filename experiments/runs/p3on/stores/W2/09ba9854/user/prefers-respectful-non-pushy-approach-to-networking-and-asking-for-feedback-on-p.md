When networking and seeking feedback or collaboration, prefers:

1. **Respect time** — Be concise; remember everyone's schedule is valuable
2. **Be clear** — Explicitly state what feedback/help/collaboration is needed
3. **Show genuine interest** — Learn about the other person's work first
4. **Stay open to criticism** — Welcome constructive feedback and show willingness to improve
5. **Offer value** — Think about what can be offered in return (skill swap, introductions, etc.)
6. **Be prepared** — Have clear answers about project goals, vision, timeline ready
7. **Respect boundaries** — Accept when people decline without pushing further

**Example approach:** 'Hi [Name], I really enjoyed your talk/project. I'm working on something similar and would value your feedback. Would you be open to a quick chat or coffee meeting?'

**Why this matters:** Non-pushy networking builds genuine connections and increases likelihood of positive responses and real collaborations.
