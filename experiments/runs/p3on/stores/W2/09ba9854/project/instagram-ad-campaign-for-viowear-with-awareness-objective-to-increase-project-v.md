Decided to promote Viowear via Instagram ads using the 'Awareness' objective to increase brand visibility and reach new audiences. Setup involves:
- Creating Instagram Business Account linked to Facebook Page
- Using Facebook Ads Manager for campaign management
- Targeting audience based on demographics, interests, behaviors
- Initial focus on 'Awareness' objective (not Consideration or Conversion)
- Plan to measure success via reach, impressions, engagement, and other metrics

Inspired by Rachel Lee's insights from a digital marketing workshop.
