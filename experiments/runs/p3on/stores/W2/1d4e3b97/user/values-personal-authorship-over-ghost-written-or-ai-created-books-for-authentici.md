When considering writing a book, prefers personal authorship over ghost-writing or AI-generated alternatives. Values:
- Personal fulfillment and empowerment from the writing process
- Complete ownership and control over content, tone, and message
- Authenticity as a true representation and credibility builder
- Developing and refining writing skills and voice
- Forming emotional connections with readers

Connected to existing interests in reading character-driven narrative-focused historical fiction and mythology retellings.
