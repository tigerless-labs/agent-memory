**Major Retailers:**
- Amazon.com — wide range of PC components and accessories
- Newegg.com — popular hardware retailer
- BestBuy.com — PC components, peripherals, and pre-built gaming PCs
- Microcenter.com — computer and electronics retailer with online and in-store pickup
- B&hphotovideo.com (B&H Photo Video) — computer hardware and accessories

**Specialty/Niche Shops:**
- PCPartPicker.com — component selector with price comparison across retailers and compatibility checking
- Superbiiz.com — specializes in hard-to-find and discontinued items
- r/buildapcsales (Reddit) — community dedicated to finding and sharing PC component deals
- Performance-PCs.com — specializes in high-performance hardware, water cooling systems, modding parts
- FrozenCPU.com — specializes in hardware and modding parts for custom PC builds

**Purchasing Tips:** Compare prices and reviews across retailers, check return policies and warranties, use PCPartPicker to verify compatibility before purchasing.
