Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat is made with 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac and 20% Folle Blanche. The blend changed due to severe frost in 2021 that damaged the Folle Blanche crop. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the wine. It was opened by the glass at 161 for spring service.
