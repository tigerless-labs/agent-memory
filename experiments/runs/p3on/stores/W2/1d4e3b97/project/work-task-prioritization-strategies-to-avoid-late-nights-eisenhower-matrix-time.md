## Goal
Prioritize work tasks more effectively to avoid staying up late to finish projects.

## Key Strategies

**1. Eisenhower Matrix**
Categorize tasks into four quadrants:
- Urgent & Important (do first)
- Important but Not Urgent (schedule)
- Urgent but Not Important (delegate/minimize)
- Not Urgent or Important (eliminate)

**2. Prioritize by Impact**
Identify which tasks will have the most significant impact. Ask: "What task will make the most difference if I complete it today?"

**3. Break Down Large Tasks**
Divide large projects into smaller, manageable chunks to avoid feeling overwhelmed and prevent last-minute crunch.

**4. Time-Blocking**
Schedule specific times for specific tasks. Use calendars, planners, or apps to stay organized. This eliminates distractions during designated work blocks.

**5. Realistic Deadlines**
Estimate how long tasks will actually take and pad by 10-20% for unexpected interruptions or challenges.

**6. Task List & Tracking**
Write down all tasks and break them into subtasks. Cross off as completed to visualize progress and stay motivated.

**7. Avoid Multitasking**
Focus on one task at a time. Multitasking leads to distractions, mistakes, and decreased productivity.

**8. Learn to Say No**
Be mindful of taking on too much. Decline tasks not aligned with priorities or that can't fit in the schedule.

**9. Regular Breaks**
Take breaks to recharge and avoid burnout. Use breaks for something enjoyable or relaxing.

**10. Review and Adjust**
Regularly review task list and adjust priorities as circumstances change.
