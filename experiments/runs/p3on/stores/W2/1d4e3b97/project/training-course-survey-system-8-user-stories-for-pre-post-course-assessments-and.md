Functional Requirements:
- Trainees answer survey at course start and end
- Trainees see own progress (before/after comparison)
- Trainees see whole class results at course end

User Stories Generated:

Trainee-Facing:
1. Access survey at beginning to provide initial feedback
2. Access same survey at end to provide final feedback
3. See progress over course to track improvement and identify development areas
4. Compare end-of-course with initial results to see attitude/opinion changes
5. View entire class results at course end to compare progress with peers

Instructor-Facing:
6. Collect survey responses at start/end to track progress and evaluate training effectiveness
7. View individual trainee results to provide personalized feedback
8. View class-wide results to evaluate overall effectiveness and identify improvements
