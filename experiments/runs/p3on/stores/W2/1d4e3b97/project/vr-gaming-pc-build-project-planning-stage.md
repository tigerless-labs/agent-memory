User is planning to build a PC for virtual reality gaming. Two component tiers identified:

**High-End Components:**
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, 3070, or 3060 Ti; AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT or 6800
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, or i7-11700K
- RAM: 16+ GB DDR4 with high speed and low latency
- Motherboard: ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, or Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX
- Storage: Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 SSD
- PSU: EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X (minimum 750W)

**Budget-Friendly Components:**
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K
- RAM: 8 GB DDR4 with high speed and low latency
- Motherboard: MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, or Gigabyte GA-A320M-H
- Storage: WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D (500GB+)
- PSU: EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

**Assembly notes:** Ground yourself before handling components, install PSU first, ensure proper cable management, test POST before installing OS, install latest drivers.
