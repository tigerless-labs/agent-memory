## Daily Routine

**Dinner (7-8 pm)**: Designate as screen-free zone. Focus on conversations, reading, or listening to music instead of scrolling or watching TV.

**Evening Relaxation Window (8-9 pm)**: 30-60 minute block where you can choose between Netflix or screen-free alternative activities.

**Pre-Sleep Wind-Down (30 minutes before bed)**: Engage in calming, screen-free activities to signal to brain it's time to sleep.

## Alternative Relaxation Activities
Instead of defaulting to Netflix, try:
- Reading books or magazines
- Journaling or reflecting on the day
- Yoga or gentle stretches
- Meditation or mindfulness
- Listening to calming music or nature sounds
- Puzzles or board games
- Taking a warm bath or shower

## Tracking Tools
- Use built-in phone features: Screen Time (iOS) or Digital Wellbeing (Android)
- Set time limits for social media (suggested: 30 minutes/day) and Netflix
- Use website/app blockers if needed during designated screen-free times

## Screen-Free Zones
- Bedroom: screen-free to associate with sleep and relaxation
- Dinner table: keep devices away during meals
- Create a "screen-free sanctuary" like a reading nook or meditation corner
