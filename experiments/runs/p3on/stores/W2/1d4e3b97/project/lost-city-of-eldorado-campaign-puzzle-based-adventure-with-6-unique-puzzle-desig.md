Campaign: Lost City of Eldorado — adventure centered on navigating lost city with treasure to claim.

**Puzzle Categories:** Logic (deduction), Physical (block/lever manipulation), Pattern Recognition (symbol alignment), Word (unscramble/riddles), Visual (spotting hidden objects/patterns), Combination (mixed types).

**Six Unique Puzzles:**

1. **Mirror Puzzle** — Large mirror with etched symbols. Players position themselves and symbols to reveal hidden passage through mirrored/reversed reflection.

2. **Elemental Puzzle** — Levers/buttons controlling earth, fire, air, water. Players determine correct sequence to manipulate elements and reveal passage (e.g., air + water creates revealing mist).

3. **Musical Puzzle** — Room with instruments and sheet music (symbols correspond to instruments). Play correct sequence (e.g., piano → drum → trumpet) to unlock door.

4. **Weight Puzzle** — Differently-sized weights and scales with marked weights. Weigh items and arrange in specific order on scales to reveal passage.

5. **Memory Puzzle** — Players memorize object locations, lights go out, navigate dark room to retrieve specific object and bring to designated spot (e.g., find key in dark to unlock passage).

6. **Design Notes** — Scalable for different skill levels, unique solutions, mix of logic/observation/physical challenges, keeps players engaged throughout campaign.
