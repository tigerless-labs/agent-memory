Has been struggling with sleep quality. Started establishing a comprehensive sleep improvement strategy around early February 2024, including:\n\n- Melatonin supplementation (30 min before bed)\n- Consistent bedtime routine with reading, calming music/nature sounds\n- White noise machine for noise blocking\n- Afternoon caffeine elimination\n- Earlier bedtime\n\nResults have been positive — now falling asleep within 15-20 minutes consistently. Continues to experiment with additional strategies for further improvement (herbal teas, environment optimization, exercise, etc.).
