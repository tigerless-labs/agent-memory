The user prefers flexibility in how they organize their day rather than strict time-blocking or structured schedules. They benefit more from a framework-based approach (like Must-Dos/Should-Dos/Nice-To-Dos prioritization) combined with a simple checklist that they can review daily and adjust as needed.
