## Prioritization Framework\n\n**Must-Dos:** Top 3-5 essential tasks that are non-negotiable and impact work, relationships, or well-being. Examples: meeting deadlines, responding to urgent emails, exercising, quality time with loved ones.\n\n**Should-Dos:** Important but flexible tasks that bring joy, help growth, or maintain relationships. Examples: reading, learning, socializing.\n\n**Nice-To-Dos:** Non-essential tasks that are relaxing or entertaining. Examples: TV, gaming, social media.\n\n## Boundary-Setting Checklist\n\n**Daily Priorities:**\n- List top 3-5 essential Must-Dos for the day\n- Schedule time for self-care (meditation, journaling)\n\n**Boundary Reminders:**\n- Before committing to new tasks, ask: Does this align with my Must-Dos or priorities?\n- Communicate priorities to colleagues, friends, and family\n- Leave buffer time in schedule for flexibility and self-care\n\n**Weekly Review:**\n- Review and adjust Must-Dos\n- Assess commitments and say no to non-essential tasks\n- Celebrate self-care accomplishments\n\n## Implementation Tips\n- Post checklist somewhere visible (fridge, desk, mirror)\n- Review daily and adjust as needed\n- Be kind to yourself — boundary-setting is a process\n- Review progress with Dr. Smith in therapy sessions
