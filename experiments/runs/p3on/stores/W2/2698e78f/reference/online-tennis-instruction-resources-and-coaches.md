## Platforms & Websites\n- Tennis.com: instructional content, video lessons, articles, podcasts\n- Essential Tennis: video lessons, drills, exercises (technique focused)\n- Tennis Warehouse University: video lessons and tutorials\n- CoachTube: online coaching platform with one-on-one lessons\n- Tennis Coach Pro: online coaching platform with personalized lessons\n\n## YouTube Channels\n- Tennis Lessons 365\n- Tennis Coach 365\n- The Tennis Vault\n\n## Instructors\n- Brett Hobden: expert in technique and strategy\n- Jeff Salzenstein: former ATP player, focuses on technique, strategy, mental toughness\n- Ian Westermann: expert in technique, strategy, junior development\n\n## Recommended approach for improvement\nFocus on serve improvement first (currently inconsistent), but maintain balance with groundstrokes. Aim for 20-30% practice time on serve, rest on groundstrokes and other shots.
