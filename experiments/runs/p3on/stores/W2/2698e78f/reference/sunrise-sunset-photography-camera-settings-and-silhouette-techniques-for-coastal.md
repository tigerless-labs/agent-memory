Camera Settings: RAW format, ISO 100-400, White Balance Cloudy/Shade, Aperture f/8-f/16, Shutter 1/15s-1/30s, bracket exposures. Composition: Rule of thirds, leading lines, foreground interest, negative space. Silhouettes: Position sun behind subject, meter for sky with spot metering, use graduated ND filters, expose-blend multiple shots. Foreground elements: wide-angle lens 10-24mm, surfers use 1/1000s-1/4000s shutter to freeze action, structures benefit from off-center placement. Location scouting: tide pools at low tide, quiet neighborhoods, lesser-known piers, coastal hiking trails, abandoned structures, parks and reserves.
