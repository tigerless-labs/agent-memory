Winery: Caymus Vineyards, family-owned since 1972. Composition: 100 percent Cabernet Sauvignon from Napa Valley, including Special Selection Vineyard in Rutherford. Aged 20 months in French oak barrels (90 percent new oak). Tasting notes: dark cherry, blackberry, black currant, dark chocolate, mocha, spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, clove), vanilla, caramel, velvety tannins. Recognition: multiple Wine Spectator Top 100 rankings, Wine Enthusiast Wine of the Year 2012, 95+ ratings. Food pairing: grilled steak, roasted lamb or beef, pan-seared duck, game meats, mature cheeses. Price: 150-180 USD per bottle.
