**Foundational drum pattern tips:**\n- Start with kick drum on beats 1 & 3, snare on 2 & 4\n- Add rhythmic interest with hi-hats on off-beats and snare variations\n- Focus on feel/groove rather than complexity\n- Choose drum sounds matching the track's genre/style\n- Keep it simple—less is often more\n- Loop and repeat for continuity\n- Experiment with swing/shuffle for human feel\n- Listen to ukulele/saxophone tracks and match their rhythmic feel\n\n**Ableton Live-specific tools:**\n- Drum Rack: Create foundation and experiment with rhythms\n- Max for Live: Create custom patterns and effects\n- Arrangement View: Visual drum pattern creation and variations\n\n**Key principle:** Solid foundational drum pattern works better than overly complex patterns. Build from simple base and add variations.
