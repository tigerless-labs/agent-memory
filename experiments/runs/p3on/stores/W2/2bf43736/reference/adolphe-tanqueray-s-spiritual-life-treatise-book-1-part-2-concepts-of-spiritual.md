User studied Adolphe Tanqueray's *Spiritual Life* treatise, specifically Book 1 of the Second Part.

**Chapters reviewed:**
- Chapter 2: "The Purgative Way" — first stage of spiritual life, purification from sin and worldly attachment
- Chapter 3: "The Active Purgative Way" — soul actively works to detach from sin through mortification, virtue cultivation, prayer, and sacraments
- Chapter 4: "Vocal Prayer and Meditation" — structured prayer for beginners, using traditional vocal prayers and meditation on Scripture

**Key concepts:**
- **Spiritual beginner:** someone in early purgative way, desires to please God, struggles with temptations, needs guidance from spiritual director; characterized by need for structure and humility
- **Prayer of beginners:** focuses on vocal prayer (Our Father, Hail Mary) and mental prayer/meditation, requires discipline and set times for prayer, guided by spiritual director

**Tanqueray's emphasis:** importance of spiritual director/mentor, perseverance through long process of purification, humble obedience, regular structured prayer, use of sacraments (confession, Eucharist) for grace
