Creating a dedicated music room with optimized acoustics for recording.\n\n**Acoustic setup considerations:**\n- Rectangular room preferred (1.5-2x width:length ratio)\n- Acoustic panels and sound-absorbing materials for walls, ceiling, corners\n- Diffusers (bookshelves, irregular shapes) to scatter sound\n- Bass traps in corners for low-frequency control\n- Area rugs or carpet underlayment to reduce floor reflections\n- Ideal ceiling height: 10-12 feet\n- Windows positioned away from listening area\n\n**Recording equipment:**\n- Microphone: Rode NT1-A (~$230)\n- Audio interface options: Focusrite Scarlett or PreSonus AudioBox (~$100-300)\n- Software: Audacity (free), GarageBand (free on Mac), or FL Studio (~$99)\n- Studio monitors: KRK RP5 G3 (~$150) or Yamaha HS8 (~$200)\n- DAW: Ableton Live (for beat/melody creation)\n\n**Instruments to record:** ukulele, saxophone, and drum patterns/beats
