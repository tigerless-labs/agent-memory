## Verde Fashion Co.

**Brand**: Verde Fashion Co. — sustainable fashion brand

**Status**: Website launched ~mid-2023-05, first email subscribers acquired

**Mission**: Make sustainable fashion accessible and stylish; counter fast fashion with high-quality, timeless pieces that respect the planet and resources.

**Sustainable practices**:
- Uses eco-friendly materials: organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel
- Reduces waste in production: pattern-making optimization, fabric scraps recycling
- Commitment to transparency and accountability in supply chain

**Founder's motivation**: Personal story involving grandmother's handmade dress from family trunk, which sparked realization about impact of fast fashion. Decided to build brand that merges passion for fashion with commitment to sustainability.

**Target audience**: Subscribers interested in sustainable fashion, eco-conscious consumers
