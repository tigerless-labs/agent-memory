Selected Rode NT1-A as versatile condenser microphone for home recording studio. Chosen because:\n- Flat frequency response (20 Hz - 20 kHz) captures both instruments well\n- Sensitive enough for ukulele details, can handle saxophone's higher SPL\n- Cardioid pattern reduces ambient noise\n- Warm, detailed sound works for both acoustic instruments\n\nPlans to use with: Focusrite Scarlett audio interface, Audacity/GarageBand/FL Studio recording software.\n\nMic placement tips: 6-12 inches from ukulele sound hole or saxophone bell, slightly off-axis. Will need to adjust gain staging and EQ for each instrument.
