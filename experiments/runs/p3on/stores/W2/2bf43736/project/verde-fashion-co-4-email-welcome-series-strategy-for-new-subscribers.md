## Welcome Email Series for Verde Fashion Co.

**Strategy**: 4-email automated welcome series to introduce brand, build connection, and encourage first purchase

**Email 1 (sent immediately)**: Introduction and Brand Story
- Subject: Welcome to Verde Fashion Co.!
- Content: Brief intro to brand mission, personal founder story, expression of excitement
- CTA: Explore website and social media

**Email 2 (sent 3-4 days later)**: Our Sustainable Approach
- Subject: Our Sustainable Approach to Fashion
- Content: Highlight sustainable practices (eco-friendly materials, waste reduction, transparency), introduce fabric options (organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel)
- CTA: Learn more about sustainable materials on website

**Email 3 (sent 7-10 days later)**: Featured Products and Collections
- Subject: Explore Our Sustainable Fashion Collections
- Content: Showcase 2-3 best-selling/newest products with sustainable features, introduce product categories, share customer testimonials
- CTA: Shop website and explore collections

**Email 4 (sent 10-14 days later)**: Exclusive Offer and Community Invitation
- Subject: Join Our Sustainable Fashion Community
- Content: Offer exclusive discount (15% off first purchase), invite to social media, sneak peek of upcoming products/collaborations
- CTA: Shop with discount, follow social media

**Founder's personal story theme**: Include personal connection to brand (grandmother's dress, reaction to fast fashion) in Email 1 to build emotional connection
