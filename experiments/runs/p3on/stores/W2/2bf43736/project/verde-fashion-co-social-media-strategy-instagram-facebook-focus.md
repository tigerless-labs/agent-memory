## Social Media Strategy for Verde Fashion Co.

**Primary platforms**: Instagram and Facebook

**Instagram strategy**:
- Focus: Visual content, behind-the-scenes (BTS) content, customer showcase
- Content pillars:
  - Sustainability series: eco-friendly materials, waste reduction, sustainable practices
  - Behind-the-scenes: design process, material sourcing, production
  - Customer showcase: photos of customers wearing products (encourage with incentives)
  - Educational content: sustainable fashion tips and practices
- Tactics: Use hashtags, engaging captions, Stories and IGTV for sneak peeks and exclusive content, polls/quizzes
- Aesthetic: Consistent, high-quality visuals
- Posting frequency: Regular/consistent

**Facebook strategy**:
- Focus: In-depth content, blog posts, promotions, events
- Content pillars:
  - Long-form educational content
  - Blog posts
  - Promotions and sales
  - Event announcements
- Tactics: Targeted ads, Facebook Groups for community building, Facebook Live for Q&A, Events for launches
- Mobile optimization: Ensure content works on mobile devices

**Cross-promotion**: Share Instagram content to Facebook; drive Facebook users to Instagram profile

**Influencer partnerships**: Collaborate with sustainable fashion influencers aligned with brand values
