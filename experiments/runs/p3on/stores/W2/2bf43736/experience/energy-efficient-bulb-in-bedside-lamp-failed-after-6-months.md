Installed an energy-efficient bulb in bedside lamp approximately 6 months before May 2023. The bulb failed/burned out much sooner than expected, causing disappointment. Led to interest in higher-quality LED bulbs from brands like Philips, LIFX, or GE with 15,000+ hour lifespans and longer warranties.
