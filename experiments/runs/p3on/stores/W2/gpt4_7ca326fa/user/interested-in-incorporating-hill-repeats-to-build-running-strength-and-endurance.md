Plans to add hill repeats to running routine for improving endurance and leg strength. Approach:\n\n- Find a hill that takes 1-3 minutes to climb\n- Start with shorter repeats (2-3 minutes) and increase gradually\n- Perform 1-2 times per week with at least one rest day between\n- Example workout: 10-15 min warm-up, 3-5 sets of 2 min hill sprints with walking/jogging recovery, 10-15 min cool-down\n- Focus on proper running form while climbing\n- Expected benefits: improved anaerobic endurance, increased leg and glute strength, better running efficiency, and mental toughness
