Incorporates strength training 2-3 times per week targeting core and legs. Key exercises include:\n\n**Core:** Planks (30-60 sec), Russian twists (10-15 reps), leg raises (10-15 reps), side planks (each side 30-60 sec)\n\n**Legs:** Squats, lunges, deadlifts, calf raises, step-ups, glute bridges, leg press (all 3 sets of 10-15 reps)\n\nApproach: Start with lighter weights and progress gradually, focus on proper form, allow at least one rest day between sessions.
