Considering outdoor activities and photo booth for a graduation party:

**Outdoor Games:**
- Backyard obstacle course
- Water balloon toss
- Badminton game

**DIY Photo Booth Setup:**
- Multiple backdrops (colored paper, balloons, customized banner)
- Props: hats, glasses, mustaches, inflatable guitars, silly accessories
- Smartphone or digital camera with tripod
- Printing station with printer and paper
- Signage for the booth
- Encourage guests to share photos on social media with branded hashtag

These activities create interactive fun and personalized memories.
