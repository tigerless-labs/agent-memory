### LED Strip Lifespan\n\n**Typical lifespan ranges:**\n- Standard LED strips: 20,000-30,000 hours (approximately 10-15 years at 8 hours daily use)\n- High-quality LED strips: 30,000-50,000 hours (approximately 20-25 years at moderate use)\n- Premium LED strips: 50,000+ hours (approximately 30+ years)\n\n**Factors affecting lifespan:**\n- High temperatures reduce lifespan\n- Moisture/humidity affects performance\n- Frequent on/off switching can reduce lifespan\n- Extreme brightness settings reduce lifespan\n\n**Tips for longevity:**\n- Choose high-quality strips from reputable manufacturers\n- Follow installation and usage guidelines\n- Keep strips clean and dry\n- Avoid extreme temperatures and humidity\n- For under-cabinet lighting, choose strips with high CRI (80+) for good color accuracy\n\n### Recommended Table Lamp Brands for Warm Lighting\n- Lumens\n- Crate & Barrel\n- Pottery Barn\n- West Elm
