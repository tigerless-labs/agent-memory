Recommended stores for custom/personalized jewelry:

- **Etsy**: Unique, handmade, and customized jewelry from various sellers
- **Zales**: Well-known jewelry retailer with customization options
- **Things Remembered**: Specializes in personalized jewelry and gifts
- **Personalization Mall**: Wide range of customized gifts including jewelry
- **Amazon**: Various customized jewelry options from multiple sellers

When shopping: check return/refund policies and production/shipping times.
