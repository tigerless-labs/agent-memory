Industry-specific gender pay gaps based on Bureau of Labor Statistics data:

Technology and IT: 14.3% gap
Finance and Insurance: 17.6% gap
Healthcare: 12.2% gap
Law: 24.4% gap (largest gap)
STEM fields: 18.1% gap
Business and Management: 21.4% gap
Education: 12.5% gap
Arts and Design: 15.4% gap

Global gender pay gap: approximately 15.6% (World Economic Forum)

Key causes include occupational segregation, gender stereotypes, direct pay discrimination, caregiving responsibilities, and lower salary negotiation rates among women.

Solutions being promoted: equal pay enforcement, salary transparency, mentorship programs, flexible work, and negotiation training.
