Exploring eco-friendly cat litter alternatives for Luna. World's Best Cat Litter (WBCL) is a leading option: 100% natural corn-based, biodegradable, 99.9% dust-free, good odor control and clumping. Available varieties: Original (corn), Multiple Cat Clumping (multi-cat), Zero Mess (reduced dust), Forest (affordable blend). Cons: pricier than clay, may develop mold if not scooped regularly. Available at Petco and most pet retailers.
