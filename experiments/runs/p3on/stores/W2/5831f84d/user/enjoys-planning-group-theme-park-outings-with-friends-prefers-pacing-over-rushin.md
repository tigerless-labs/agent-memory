Plans group outings to theme parks with friends. When visiting complex events like Knott's Scary Farm, prefers to prioritize and fully immerse in a few key attractions (e.g., Pumpkin Eater, The Depths mazes) rather than rushing to experience everything in one night. Recognizes that a focused, well-paced experience is better than an exhausting rush through all options.
