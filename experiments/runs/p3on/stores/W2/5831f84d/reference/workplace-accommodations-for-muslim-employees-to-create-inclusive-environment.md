Reasonable accommodations companies can provide to support Muslim employees:

**Prayer and Worship:**
- Designate a quiet prayer room or prayer space
- Allow flexible scheduling to accommodate prayer times
- Provide Friday prayer accommodations (longer lunch breaks or on-site prayer space)

**Dietary:**
- Offer halal food options in cafeteria
- Allow employees to bring their own food
- Ensure kitchen areas are clean and free from cross-contamination

**Seasonal/Holiday:**
- Flexible scheduling or reduced workload during Ramadan
- Day off or flexible scheduling for Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha
- Time off for Hajj or Umrah pilgrimages

**Dress Code & Facilities:**
- Allow modest clothing including hijabs, abayas, beards
- Provide gender-segregated facilities (restrooms, locker rooms)

**Support & Community:**
- Establish employee resource group (ERG) for Muslim employees
- Provide cultural sensitivity training for all staff
- Use inclusive language and avoid scheduling during prayer times or Ramadan

**Implementation tips:**
- Consult with Muslim employees to understand specific needs
- Communicate accommodations clearly to all staff
- Ensure accommodations don't create undue hardship
- Review and update regularly for effectiveness
