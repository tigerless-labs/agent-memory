## Smoky Baked Beans Recipe (8-10 servings)\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1 lb dried navy beans, soaked overnight\n- 1 lb smoked bacon, 1-inch pieces\n- 1 medium onion, chopped\n- 2 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 cup ketchup\n- 1/4 cup brown sugar\n- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar\n- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce\n- 1 tsp ground mustard\n- 1 tsp smoked paprika (recommended)\n- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)\n- 1/4 cup bourbon (optional)\n\n**Method:**\n1. Cook bacon until crispy, set aside, cook onion in drippings until soft (~5 min)\n2. Add garlic (1 min), then add beans, ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire, mustard, paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper\n3. Add bacon and bourbon, transfer to 9x13 baking dish\n4. Cover with foil, bake 6 hours at 250°F\n5. Uncover, bake additional 30-60 minutes until tender and thickened\n6. Rest 30 minutes before serving\n\n**Tips:** Can add chipotle peppers for extra smokiness; works in slow cooker on low 8-10 hours
