Guidelines for recognizing and celebrating Eid holidays in the workplace:

**Eid al-Fitr (end of Ramadan):**
- Offer day off or flexible scheduling
- Host cultural event with traditional food and decorations
- Send 'Eid Mubarak' greeting messages to Muslim employees
- Give small gifts (box of dates, halal restaurant gift card)
- Organize interfaith dialogue

**Eid al-Adha (end of Hajj):**
- Offer day off or flexible scheduling
- Host cultural event with traditional food and decorations
- Send 'Eid Mubarak' greeting messages
- Consider organizing Qurbani/Udhiyah service (animal sacrifice arrangement)
- Organize charitable donation drive/fundraiser for food banks

**Best Practices:**
- Educate employees about significance of holidays
- Involve Muslim employees in planning
- Ensure all food is halal and respects dietary restrictions
- Avoid stereotyping or tokenization
- Make it company-wide—encourage all employees to participate
- Focus on genuine understanding and respect, not performative inclusion
