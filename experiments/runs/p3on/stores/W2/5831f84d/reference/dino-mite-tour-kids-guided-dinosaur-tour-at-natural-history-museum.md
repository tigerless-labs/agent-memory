Guided tour for children aged 5-12 at the Natural History Museum. Duration is approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour. Guide leads visitors through dinosaur galleries, sharing facts and stories about various dinosaurs. Includes interactive elements and opportunities for kids to ask questions and participate in discussions.
