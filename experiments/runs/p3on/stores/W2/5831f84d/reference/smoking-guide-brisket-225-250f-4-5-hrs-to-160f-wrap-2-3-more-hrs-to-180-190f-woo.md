## Brisket Smoking Technique\n\n- Temperature: 225-250°F (110-120°C)\n- Fat side up\n- Smoke 4-5 hours until internal temp reaches 160°F\n- Wrap in foil ("Texas Crutch") to prevent drying\n- Continue smoking 2-3 more hours until 180-190°F internal temp\n- Rest 1-2 hours before slicing\n- Slice against the grain\n- Spritz every hour with mop sauce/beef broth\n\n## Wood & Meat Pairings\n\n**Hickory** (strong, sweet, smoky): ribs, brisket, bacon, sausages\n**Oak** (earthy, mellow, sweet): pulled pork, chicken, turkey, vegetables\n**Mesquite** (strong, earthy, bitter): beef steaks/burgers, lamb, game meats\n**Applewood** (fruity, sweet, mild): poultry, pork tenderloins, baby back ribs, sausages\n**Cherrywood** (fruity, sweet, mild): poultry, pork tenderloins, game meats\n\n**Tips:** Start with mild wood if new to smoking; mix different woods for complex flavors; soak wood 30+ minutes before use
