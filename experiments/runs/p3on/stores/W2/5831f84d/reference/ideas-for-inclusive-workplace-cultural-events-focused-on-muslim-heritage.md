Ideas for organizing cultural events and workshops to promote Muslim heritage and interfaith understanding:

**Event Ideas:**
- Muslim Cultural Competency Training sessions
- Ramadan and Eid celebrations
- Hijab-Friendly Workspaces workshop
- Islamic art and calligraphy event
- Cultural potluck or cooking demonstration featuring traditional Muslim dishes
- Interfaith dialogue panel with Muslim speakers and other faith representatives
- Employee storytelling event where Muslim employees share personal stories and traditions
- Iftar dinner (breaking of fast) during Ramadan
- Eid celebrations (events or company recognition)

**Resources to Create:**
- Resource guide or primer on Muslim culture, holidays, and dietary restrictions
- Training materials on inclusive language and avoiding microaggressions

**Partnership:**
- Collaborate with local Muslim organizations and mosques to host events and provide speakers

**Key Principles:**
- Engage Muslim employees and community leaders throughout planning
- Ensure events are respectful and culturally sensitive
- Avoid tokenization or stereotyping
- Make it company-wide (all employees participate)
- Prioritize genuine understanding over surface-level gestures
