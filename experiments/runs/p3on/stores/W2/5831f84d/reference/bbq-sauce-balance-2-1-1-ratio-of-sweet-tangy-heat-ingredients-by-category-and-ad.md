## BBQ Sauce Flavor Balancing\n\n**Holy Trinity:**\n- **Sweet:** Brown sugar, honey, molasses, maple syrup\n- **Tangy:** Apple cider vinegar, white wine vinegar, balsamic, lemon/orange juice, yogurt/sour cream\n- **Heat:** Hot sauce, jalapeños/serrano peppers, cayenne, smoked paprika\n\n**Ratio:** Start with 2:1:1 (sweet:tangy:heat), adjust to taste\n\n**Building Flavor:**\n1. Layer ingredients - add sweet first, then tangy, then heat\n2. Too sweet? Add vinegar or citrus\n3. Too acidic? Add more sweetener\n4. Too spicy? Add dairy (sour cream/yogurt)\n5. Not spicy enough? Add hot sauce or peppers\n6. Umami depth: Add soy sauce, Worcestershire, or mushroom ingredients\n7. Thickeners: Tomato paste, ketchup, or cornstarch\n8. Rest overnight to let flavors meld\n\n**Regional Styles:**\n- Kansas City: tomato-based, sweet, thick\n- Texas: beef-based, thin, spicy\n- Carolina: vinegar-based, tangy, peppery\n\n**Variations:** Try fruit preserves (peach/apricot), unique spices (smoked paprika, coriander, cinnamon), bourbon/beer
