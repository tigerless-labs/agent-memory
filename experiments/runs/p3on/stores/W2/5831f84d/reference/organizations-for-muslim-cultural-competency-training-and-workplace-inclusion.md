Organizations that offer Muslim cultural competency training, workshops, and workplace inclusion resources:

1. **CAIR (Council on American-Islamic Relations)** — cultural competency training and workshops for employers
2. **ISNA (Islamic Society of North America)** — cultural competency training and workplace inclusion resources
3. **MPAC (Muslim Public Affairs Council)** — cultural competency training and Workplace Inclusion Initiative
4. **ICNA (Islamic Circle of North America)** — cultural events and resources
5. **ISPU (Institute for Social Policy and Understanding)** — research-based cultural competency training
6. **Local mosques and Muslim organizations** — often offer customized training and resources

**Topics typically covered in Muslim cultural competency training:**
- Demographics and diversity within Muslim community (Sunni, Shia, Sufi, different ethnicities)
- Islamic beliefs and practices (Five Pillars, Ramadan, Eid, daily prayers)
- Cultural nuances and customs (greetings, hospitality, communication styles)
- Common misconceptions and stereotypes about Muslims
- Workplace accommodations
- Microaggressions and implicit bias
- Effective communication strategies
- Inclusive language and practices
- Real-life case studies and scenarios

**Selection criteria:** Look for trainers with expertise, experience, positive reviews, and recommendations from previous clients.
