According to McKinsey & Company and LeanIn.Org (2022):\n- Women hold 47% of US workforce\n- 38% of manager-level positions\n- 24% of senior manager positions\n- 18% of executive-level positions\n- 5% of CEO positions (S&P 500)\n\n**By industry:**\n- Technology: 28% in leadership (senior manager+)\n- Finance: 22% in leadership\n- Healthcare: 34% in leadership\n\nCommon challenges for women in tech: gender bias, lack of representation, work-life balance, sexual harassment, pay gap, limited advancement, imposter syndrome.
