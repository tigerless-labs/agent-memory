**Teams adopting more cautious spending:** New York Yankees, Los Angeles Dodgers, Boston Red Sox - all shifting from big free agent investments to young talent development and staying under luxury tax threshold.

**Teams that continued investing:** New York Mets (Trevor Bauer record contract), San Diego Padres (Blake Snell trade, Yu Darvish signing).

**Success examples:** Houston Astros and Chicago Cubs built strong teams through youth-focused model.

**Key drivers:** Analytics enabling cost-effective talent identification; luxury tax penalties; COVID-19 pandemic revenue loss; recognition that homegrown cores build sustainable success.

**Trends discussed:** Increasing emphasis on player development over free agents; potential for more league parity if reduced big-market spending allows smaller-market teams to compete; question of whether caution continues post-pandemic.
