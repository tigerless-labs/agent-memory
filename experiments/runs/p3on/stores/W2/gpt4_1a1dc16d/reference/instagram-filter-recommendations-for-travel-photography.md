Clarendon: Brightens and intensifies colors, best for sunny outdoor shots. Juno: Adds warmer tone and enhances pinks and blues, ideal for beach or sunset shots. Valencia: Adds soft vintage look, great for cityscapes or architecture. Gingham: Adds subtle vintage look with cooler tone, ideal for nature shots. Lark: Enhances blues and greens, brightens darker areas, great for landscape shots. Recommendation: Use filters sparingly, avoid relying too heavily on them. Focus on showcasing unique aspects of destinations and personal perspective.
