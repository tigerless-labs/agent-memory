Trip parameters: 4 days, children ages 6-13, all places within 1-hour drive from Chiang Mai, Google-rated 4+. Each day includes shopping, lunch, dinner (non-vegetarian with 3 recommended dishes), foot massage, and dessert.

DAY 1: Doi Suthep-Pui National Park (4.6, 30/10 THB adult/child) | Huay Tung Tao Lake (4.5) | Chiang Mai Night Bazaar (4.1). Lunch: Khao Soi Mae Sai (4.5) - Khao Soi, Fried Noodles with Pork, Fried Rice with Chicken. Dinner: Dash! Teak House (4.4) - Grilled Pork Neck, Stir-Fried Pork with Basil, Tom Yum Kung. Foot massage: Lila Thai Massage (4.6, 250 THB). Dessert: Mango Tango (4.2) - Mango Sticky Rice.

DAY 2: Elephant Nature Park (4.8, 3500/1750 THB) | Chiang Mai Zoo (4.1, 150/70 THB) | Art in Paradise (4.3, 400/200 THB). Lunch: SP Chicken (4.4) - Grilled Chicken, Fried Chicken, Tom Yum Noodle Soup. Dinner: The Riverside (4.3) - Pad Thai, Massaman Curry, Grilled Pork Ribs. Foot massage: Fah Lanna Spa (4.6, 300 THB). Dessert: Bake & Bite (4.4) - Chocolate Lava Cake.

DAY 3: Doi Inthanon National Park (4.6, 300/150 THB) | Baan Tawai Village (4.3) | Mae Sa Elephant Camp (4.3, 2400/1200 THB). Lunch: The Salad Concept (4.5) - Caesar Salad, Tom Yum Soup, Pad Thai. Dinner: The Good View (4.3) - BBQ Pork Ribs, Pad Thai, Tom Yum Kung.

Day 4 not completed in original request. Note: From 2023 - verify current hours and prices.
