Managed product line launch project as Senior Marketing Manager leading team of 10 people for major retail client.

Project Details:
- Launch Date: June 1, 2023
- Team Size: 10 people
- Scope: Marketing strategy, creative assets, product packaging, e-commerce development, content creation, social media and email campaigns, retail coordination

Key Phases:
1. Kickoff and Planning (March 15-28)
2. Product Development (March 29 - April 11)
3. Creative Assets Development (April 12-25): packaging design, POS materials, product photography/video, marketing collateral
4. Content Creation (April 26 - May 9): product descriptions, social media content calendar, email campaigns, website content
5. E-commerce and Testing (May 10-23)
6. Launch Preparations (May 24 - June 1)

Used structured project management approach with WBS, dependency mapping, critical path analysis, and resource allocation matrix. Implemented project charter, communication protocols, and stakeholder management.

Team Structure Included: Graphic Designers, Copywriters, Social Media Specialist, Email Marketing Specialist, E-commerce Developer, Product Manager, Creative Director.
