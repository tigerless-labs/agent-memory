## Core composition principles for cityscapes:\n\n- **Rule of thirds**: Place horizon along one of the horizontal third lines for balance\n- **Leading lines**: Use roads, sidewalks, shorelines, or architectural lines to guide viewer's eye to focal point\n- **Depth and layers**: Incorporate foreground (streets, parks), middle ground, and background (skyline) for dimensionality\n- **Symmetry**: Look for symmetrical elements—buildings, bridges, reflections—to create harmony\n- **Reflections**: Use windows, puddles, water surfaces to add layered interest\n- **Distraction management**: Simplify by removing unnecessary elements; watch edges of frame\n- **Light as subject**: Capture neon signs, streetlights, building highlights as compositional elements\n\n## For nighttime/crowds:\n\n- Position people/crowds in foreground with vibrant lights blurred in background\n- Shoot from low angles to immerse in scene and convey human perspective\n- Capture moments of interaction (gestures, conversations, movement) for humanity\n- Use burst mode to catch peak moments of activity\n- Respect subjects' privacy—avoid vulnerable or compromising situations
