## Recommended focal lengths for wide-angle cityscape photography:\n\n**Full-frame cameras:**\n- 14-24mm\n- 16-35mm  \n- 14mm or 15mm prime lenses\n\n**APS-C cameras:**\n- 10-22mm\n- 12-24mm\n\n## Key techniques for wide-angle cityscape work:\n\n- Keep important lines (building edges) near center to minimize distortion\n- Shoot from low angles to exaggerate height and drama\n- Watch edges of frame to minimize distracting elements\n- Use lens's depth of field for sharp focus throughout scene\n- Converging lines create drama when positioned strategically\n- Golden hour or overcast conditions reduce distortion impact\n\n## Night photography settings:\n\n- ISO 100-400 (lower is better for quality, experiment to find camera's sweet spot)\n- Aperture f/2.8-f/4 for depth, or f/5.6-f/8 for full focus\n- Shutter speed 1/15s-1/30s for motion blur, or 1/100s-1/200s to freeze action\n- Use tripod essential for low-light stability\n- Bracket exposures for HDR merge\n- Shoot RAW for post-processing flexibility
