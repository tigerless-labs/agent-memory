Theme Parks near Virginia Beach:
- Busch Gardens Williamsburg (~1.5 hours): offers private room bookings for events
- Kings Dominion, Doswell VA (~2.5 hours): offers private room bookings for groups and birthday parties

Halloween Attractions:
- Haunted Hunt Club Farm (Virginia Beach): haunted house, hayride, may offer private event spaces
- Nightmare Mansion (Virginia Beach): haunted house, may offer group bookings  
- Spooky Acres (Chesapeake, VA): haunted house, trail, may offer group packages

Contact venues directly for current availability and private event options.
