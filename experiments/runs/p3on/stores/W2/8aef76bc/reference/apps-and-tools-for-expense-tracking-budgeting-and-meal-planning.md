## Expense & Budget Tracking Apps\n\n- **Mint** — comprehensive personal finance app; tracks spending by category, budgeting tools, alerts\n- **You Need a Budget (YNAB)** — budgeting-focused; tracks income/expenses, automated savings, investment tracking\n- **Personal Capital** — financial management; tracks income/expenses, investment tracking, financial planning\n- **Grocery IQ** — grocery-specific; create/manage lists, track prices, stay within budget\n- **Out of Milk** — grocery lists, price tracking, sale alerts\n- **Flipp** — aggregates weekly store ads, shopping list, price comparison\n- **AnyList** — grocery lists, price tracking, recipe organization\n- **ShopSavvy** — barcode scanning, price comparison, shopping lists\n- **Spendee, Wally, Toshl Finance** — general expense tracking with grocery features\n\n## Cashback & Coupon Apps\n\n- **Ibotta** — cashback on grocery purchases\n- **Fetch Rewards** — cashback on purchases\n- **Checkout 51** — cashback on specific items\n- **RetailMeNot, Coupons.com** — digital coupons\n\n## Meal Planning Apps\n\n- **Plan to Eat** — meal planning with grocery list integration\n- **Yummly** — recipe discovery and meal planning\n- **Mealime** — meal planning and grocery list generation\n\n## Spreadsheet/Template Options\n\n- Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel (custom or template-based)\n- Dave Ramsey, Budgeting Binder, NerdWallet, The Balance (printable templates)
