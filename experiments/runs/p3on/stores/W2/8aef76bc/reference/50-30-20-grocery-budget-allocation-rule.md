A simple framework for allocating a grocery budget across spending tiers:\n\n- **50%** Staples: produce, meat, dairy, whole grains\n- **30%** Semi-luxury items: specialty cheeses, artisanal breads\n- **20%** Luxury/splurge items: truffles, high-end chocolates\n\nAllows for treating yourself while maintaining overall budget discipline. Can be adapted to personal preferences.
