Chose Chicken Fajitas as the first slow cooker recipe to try. Includes boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced bell peppers, onions, fajita seasoning, diced tomatoes. Cook on low for 6-8 hours. Serve with warm tortillas and toppings like avocado, sour cream, salsa, shredded cheese, cilantro. Wants to complement with sides like Mexican street corn, cilantro lime rice, black beans, or avocado salad.
