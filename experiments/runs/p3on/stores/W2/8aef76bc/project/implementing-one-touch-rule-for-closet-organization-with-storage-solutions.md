Plans to implement a 'one touch rule' for closet: when taking something out, will put it back in designated spot immediately rather than leaving on floor or chair. Considering storage bins or baskets for clothing organization. Storage solutions of interest: stackable storage bins (for socks, underwear, accessories, out-of-season clothing), over-the-door shoe organizers, closet shelves with baskets, hanging organizers with pockets, drawer dividers, and tiered closet systems.
