# American Flag Tabbard Design Prompts

The user requested prompts for image generation tools (Midjourney) to visualize four tabbard designs based on the American flag.

## The Four Designs

1. **Classic Design** — Simple tunic with sleeves. American flag painted/embroidered on front: red and white stripes on bottom half, blue field with white stars on top half.

2. **Heraldic Design** — Simple tunic with sleeves. Divided into quadrants, each featuring different American heritage symbols (eagle, liberty bell, Statue of Liberty, etc.), painted or embroidered.

3. **Medieval-Inspired Design** — Tunic with long sleeves, V-shaped neckline, and diagonal split down the center. Features American flag alongside medieval-style symbols: bald eagle, shield, banner with motto, painted or embroidered.

4. **Modern Design** — Simple tunic with short sleeves. Bold geometric shapes and colors in patriotic theme (red, white, blue); abstract flag motifs using squares, triangles, circles.

All designs feature sleeves (short on Classic/Heraldic/Modern, long on Medieval-inspired).
