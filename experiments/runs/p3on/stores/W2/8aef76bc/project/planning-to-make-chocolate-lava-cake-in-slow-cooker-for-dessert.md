Planning to try a slow cooker chocolate lava cake as a dessert experiment. Recipe uses chocolate cake mix, milk, oil, eggs, vanilla extract. Cook on low for 2-3 hours. Key tips: use high-quality chocolate cake mix (30%+ cocoa), don't overmix batter, use 2-quart slow cooker, cook on low and slow, check doneness after 2 hours with toothpick, let rest 10-15 minutes, serve warm with vanilla ice cream. Can enhance with extra chocolate chips, Nutella, or peanut butter for gooier center.
