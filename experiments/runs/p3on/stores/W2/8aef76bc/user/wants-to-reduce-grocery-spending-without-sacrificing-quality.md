Seeking to save money on groceries while maintaining quality and not eating worse. Currently tries to buy in bulk and use coupons, but still occasionally splurges on luxury food items. Key strategies that appeal:\n\n- Meal planning and prep\n- Shopping sales and stocking up on non-perishables\n- Smart bulk buying (only what can be consumed before expiration)\n- Discount stores like Aldi and Lidl\n- Cashback apps (Ibotta, Fetch Rewards, Checkout 51)\n- Shopping in season and at farmers' markets/CSA\n- Store brands instead of name brands\n- Cooking from whole ingredients instead of processed foods\n- Freezing leftovers to reduce waste\n- Using unit prices for comparison\n\nInterested in using the 50/30/20 budget rule to balance staples, semi-luxury, and splurge items.
