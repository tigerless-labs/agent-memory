Developed a practice strategy for home-based dance improvement:

Session structure:
- Start with short 10-15 minute practice sessions
- Gradually increase duration over time

Focus areas:
- Footwork patterns (forward/back, side to side)
- Arm movements and waves
- Body isolations (hips, torso)
- Turns

Methods:
- Practice to actual salsa music for rhythm and timing
- Record yourself to identify areas for improvement and track progress
- Use online resources and YouTube tutorials for guidance
- Warm up before practicing and listen to body for breaks

Learning progression:
- Master basics first before moving to choreographies
- Once comfortable with basics, learn simple choreographies
- Gradually increase complexity over time

Philosophy: Be patient, have fun, don't rush, celebrate small progress
