## Platforms and Organizations Mentioned\n\n**Africa:**\n- Literary Africa — online platform for African literature, features quarterly magazine, writer directory, book reviews, writing resources, mentorship\n- Ake Arts and Book Festival — annual festival celebrating African/Nigerian literature\n- Nigeria Literature Society — promotes Nigerian literature\n- Lagos Book Club — focuses on Nigerian authors\n\n**Asia:**\n- Jaipur Literature Festival — major Asian literary festival celebrating Indian and international literature\n- The Sahitya Akademi — India's national academy of letters\n- The Indian Book Club — online book club exploring Indian literature\n- Singapore Book Club — Southeast Asian literature focus\n\n**Note:** These were mentioned in research discussion; verify current status and details before recommending.
