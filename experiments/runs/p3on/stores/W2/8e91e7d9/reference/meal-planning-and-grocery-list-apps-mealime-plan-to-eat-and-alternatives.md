Popular meal planning tools recommended:\n\n**Chosen:**\n- **Mealime**: meal planning app with healthy recipes, automated grocery lists, special diet options (vegan, gluten-free)\n- **Plan to Eat**: comprehensive meal planning with recipe saving, meal scheduling, grocery list generation\n\n**Alternatives:**\n- Yummly — recipe discovery and meal planning\n- Out of Milk — grocery list and pantry inventory tracking\n- Paprika Recipe Manager — recipe organization and meal planning\n- AnyList — custom lists for meal planning and grocery shopping\n- Google Keep — simple note-taking for meal plans\n- Allrecipes — recipe website with meal planning\n- Trello — project management tool for meal planning\n- Pinterest — visual recipe discovery and meal boards\n\nKey features to consider: recipe discovery, meal planning/scheduling, grocery list generation, pantry management, nutritional tracking, dietary customization, mobile app, third-party integrations.
