Podcast listening interests and habits

**Currently listening to:**
- How I Built This (entrepreneurship and business stories)
- Stuff You Should Know (general knowledge and science)

**Interested in exploring:**
Educational podcasts covering science, history, economics, and culture.

**Recommended educational podcasts:**
- **Planet Money** (NPR) — economics, business, and finance topics
- **Radiolab** — science, philosophy, human experience; thought-provoking and humorous
- **Hardcore History** — Dan Carlin's deep dives on historical events (long, in-depth episodes)
- **StarTalk Radio** — Neil deGrasse Tyson; science, pop culture, and comedy with guests
- **The TED Radio Hour** — based on TED Talks; wide range of topics
- **Ologies** — various scientific disciplines in engaging, accessible format
- **Hidden Brain** — unconscious patterns driving human behavior
- **The Daily** (New York Times) — daily news, politics, social issues
- **Science Vs** — debunks myths, explores science behind various topics
- **The Tim Ferriss Show** — interviews with successful people; habits, routines, strategies
- **Freakonomics Radio** — economics applied to unusual topics
- **The TED-Ed Podcast** — based on educational videos; science, history, art, culture
- **60-Second Science** (Scientific American) — daily science news and discoveries
- **The Conversation** — latest research and ideas in science, technology, society
- **Lexicon Valley** — language, linguistics, and communication
