Requested Cronus Zen scripts with 10 game automation mods for Call of Duty Mobile on Xbox One controller:

1. **Rapid Fire** — LT trigger rapid fire
2. **Auto Sprint** — Sprint on forward movement  
3. **Quick Scope** — Quick scope in/out with RT
4. **Auto Heal** — Use medkit when health < 50
5. **Jump Shot** — Jump and shoot simultaneously
6. **Auto Reload** — Auto reload at 0 ammo
7. **Auto Swap** — Swap weapon at 0 ammo
8. **Quick Revive** — Revive teammate with LB
9. **Auto Mantle** — Mantle over objects
10. **Quick Equip** — Swap equipped weapon with RB

Uses Xbox One controller buttons (LT, RT, A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, D-Pad) for scripting.
