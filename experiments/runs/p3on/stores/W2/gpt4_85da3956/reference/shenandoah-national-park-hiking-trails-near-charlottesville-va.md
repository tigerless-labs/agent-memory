Recommended trails for hiking in Shenandoah National Park (north of Charlottesville):

- **Old Rag**: 9 miles, challenging, beautiful views and unique rock formations
- **Hawksbill Mountain**: 2.9 miles, easiest option, leads to tallest peak in Shenandoah with stunning views
- **Dark Hollow Falls**: 1.4 miles, easy walk to a beautiful waterfall, good for summer dip
- **Marys Rock**: 3.7 miles, hike to summit with stunning views of Shenandoah Valley
- **Stony Man**: 1.6 miles, relatively easy, second-highest peak in park, panoramic views

Check weather conditions and park regulations before hiking.
