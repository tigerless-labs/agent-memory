# Pig Design Specifications (June 2023)

## Overview
Pig is a 6-rotor electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft designed for passenger transport.

## Key Specifications
- **MTOW:** 3200 kg
- **Configuration:** 6-rotor hexacopter
- **Capacity:** 6 people (1 pilot/engineer + 5 passengers, or similar mix)
- **Fuel:** Hydrogen (30 kg)

## Detailed MTOW Breakdown
- Empty weight: 1550 kg
  - Structure: 950 kg
  - Avionics and systems: 450 kg
  - Other equipment: 150 kg
- Payload: 630 kg (6 people at 75 kg each + 40 kg baggage per person)
- Fuel: 30 kg hydrogen
- **Total: ~2210 kg** (design target was 3200 kg)

## Dimensions
- Length: 10.5 m
- Wingspan: 15.6 m
- Overall height: 4.0 m
- Cabin dimensions: 2.5 m (length) × 1.5 m (width) × 1.9 m (height)
  - Cabin height specifically required to allow passengers to stand upright

## Performance
- Range: 400 km (mentioned in earlier discussions)

## Design Status
Design parameters established but further detail work needed on component selection, aerodynamic optimization, and control algorithms.
