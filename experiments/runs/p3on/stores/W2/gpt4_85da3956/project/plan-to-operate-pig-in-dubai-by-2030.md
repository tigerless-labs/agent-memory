# Pig Dubai Operations Plan

## Goal
Operate Pig aircraft in Dubai by 2030.

## Context
This goal was discussed in June 2023 during early design phases. Success will depend on:
- Regulatory approval and airworthiness certification
- Safety certifications meeting UAE requirements
- Infrastructure availability in Dubai
- Market demand validation
- Completion of design, manufacturing, testing phases

## Requirements
- Significant technical effort and investment needed
- Full regulatory compliance with UAE aviation authority
- Passenger safety certifications
