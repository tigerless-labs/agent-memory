Hosting approach:\n- Creates warm and welcoming atmosphere that encourages conversation and connection\n- Prefers simple but elegant—avoids clutter, keeps focus on food and company\n- Uses warm lighting (candles, string lights), fresh flowers or greenery as centerpieces\n- Keeps table settings clean and minimal—placemats, napkin rings, but avoids overcrowding\n- Considers guests' dietary restrictions and preferences\n- Offers both alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages (wine, sparkling water with lemon/flavored seltzer)\n- Prepares appetizers and components ahead of time to minimize day-of stress
