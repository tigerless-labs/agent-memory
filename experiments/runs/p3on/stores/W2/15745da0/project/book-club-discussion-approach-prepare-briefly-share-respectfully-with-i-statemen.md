Decided strategy for participating in book club discussions:

**Before discussion:**
- Arrive early to meet others
- Ask others about the book to start conversations

**During discussion:**
- Share thoughts and opinions freely
- Use 'I' statements ('I felt...', 'I think...') instead of absolute claims
- Say 'I see it differently' instead of 'you're wrong'
- Provide specific examples from the book to support opinions
- Ask open-ended questions to encourage deeper discussion
- Listen actively and respond thoughtfully to others

**Preparation approach:**
- Prepare 2-3 key points or questions beforehand
- Keep notes brief and concise
- Use notes as a flexible guide, not rigid requirements
- Stay open to natural flow of conversation
- Avoid dominating discussion
- Show respect for differing opinions even when disagreeing
