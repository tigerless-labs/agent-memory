Val Renberg, a human, met R'Kesh at age 21 while working on a massive container ship. At the time, Val was lonely and rejected the casual hookup lifestyle, orgies, and group marriages prevalent among her human peers. R'Kesh, an alien of unknown species, provided companionship and mutual respect that evolved into romantic attraction.

Despite their species differences and cultural stigma against mixed-species relationships, they formed a deep bond and became mates. Val appreciated R'Kesh's calm and dignified presence contrasted with the chaotic ship culture. R'Kesh valued Val's intelligence and individuality.

Their relationship is characterized by:
- Mutual respect and trust transcending species differences
- Shared values and sense of adventure
- Exploration of the galaxy together
- Enriched perspectives from their different backgrounds
