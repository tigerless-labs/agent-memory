Wants to integrate yoga into daily routine as non-negotiable habit. Prefers: starting with short practices (10-15 minutes minimum) on busy days; scheduling practice at same time daily; quiet dedicated space at home; combining physical poses with breathwork and meditation; using online resources/apps (YouTube, dedicated apps) for convenience and guided instruction; focusing on breathwork/meditation when time is very limited; creating habit loop by attaching yoga to existing daily routine (after waking, before bed); being flexible about missed days while quickly returning to practice; celebrating small wins for motivation. Interests span both flexibility (particularly hamstrings) and stress relief. Open to both physical and meditation-focused sessions.
