Interested in starting a journaling habit to reflect on progress, growth, and learnings at daily, weekly, or monthly intervals. Prefers: starting with manageable frequency; designated quiet comfortable space for journaling; journal style that appeals personally (experimented with lined/unlined/dot-grid); incorporation into daily/weekly routine (e.g., after morning routine or before bed); short initial entries (few sentences) building to longer reflection; ritualistic pre-journaling routine (tea, candles, music); flexibility/grace for missed days; guided prompts like gratitude, learnings, challenges, future plans; freedom to write without editing for grammar; periodic review of entries to track patterns and growth.
