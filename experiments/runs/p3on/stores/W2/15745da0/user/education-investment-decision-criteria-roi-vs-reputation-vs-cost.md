When evaluating whether to attend a more prestigious institution despite higher debt, Ryan considers:

1. **Return on investment**: Potential salary difference between graduates of prestigious vs. lower-cost institutions
2. **Reputation advantage**: Career boost from the institution's brand and alumni network
3. **Personal career goals**: Whether prestige is essential for specific career aspirations
4. **Financial burden**: Total debt load vs. available scholarships/grants
5. **Alternate paths**: Considering lower-cost institution + internships, work experience, or graduate studies as alternatives to boost career prospects

Approach: Research-driven, weighs pros and cons, aligns decision with personal goals and financial situation.
