## Korean-Style BBQ Tacos: Toppings & Sauces

Created for using leftover Korean-inspired BBQ chicken in tacos.

**Toppings**:
- Kimchi Slaw (spicy, sour, crunchy)
- Cilantro (fresh brightness)
- Green Onions (punch of flavor, crunch)
- Toasted Sesame Seeds (nutty, texture)
- Pickled Ginger (acidity, pop of flavor)
- Crushed Peanuts (texture, nutty flavor)

**Sauces**:
- **Gochujang Salsa**: Gochujang, diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro — spicy and sweet
- **Sriracha-Lime Crema**: Sriracha, lime juice, sour cream — creamy with kick
- **Korean Chili Flake Aioli**: Korean chili flakes, garlic, lemon juice — rich and creamy

**Pro Tips**:
- Use leftover Korean-inspired BBQ sauce directly as taco sauce
- Crumble and fry kimchi bits until crispy for extra texture
- Balance bold flavors with fresh, crunchy, and tangy components
