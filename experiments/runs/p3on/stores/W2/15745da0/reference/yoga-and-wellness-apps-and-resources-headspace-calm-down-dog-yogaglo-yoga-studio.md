Wellness and yoga apps recommended for building consistent practice, especially for quick/convenient sessions on busy days:\n\n- **Headspace** — meditation, mindfulness, and yoga guidance app; good for calm/focus\n- **Calm** — meditation and relaxation app with breathing exercises and sleep content  \n- **Down Dog** — yoga app with customizable practices of various lengths and difficulty levels\n- **YogaGlo** — online yoga classes and practice videos from instructors\n- **Yoga Studio** — guided yoga practice app with various pose sequences\n- **YouTube** — free yoga videos and classes (searchable by duration and focus area)\n\nAll useful for integrating yoga into daily routine without need for in-person studio classes. Allow flexibility for busy days and provide guided instruction.
