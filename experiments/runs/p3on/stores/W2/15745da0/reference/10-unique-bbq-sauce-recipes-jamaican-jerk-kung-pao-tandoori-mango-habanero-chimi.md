## BBQ Sauce Recommendations

1. **Jamaican Jerk**: Allspice, thyme, scotch bonnet peppers, lime juice — spicy and aromatic
2. **Kung Pao**: Soy sauce, hoisin sauce, rice vinegar, garlic, Szechuan peppercorns — sweet, spicy, and numbing
3. **Indian Tandoori**: Yogurt, garam masala, cumin, coriander, cayenne pepper, lemon juice — creamy and aromatic
4. **Mango Habanero**: Mango sweetness with habanero heat, cinnamon, allspice — tropical and spicy
5. **Chimichurri**: Parsley, oregano, garlic, red pepper flakes, red wine vinegar, olive oil — fresh and tangy
6. **Sriracha Honey**: Sriracha, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar — sweet and spicy
7. **Cajun**: Paprika, cayenne pepper, thyme, oregano, garlic, Worcestershire sauce — spicy and smoky
8. **Pineapple-Bourbon**: Pineapple juice, bourbon, brown sugar, Dijon mustard — sweet, tangy, and rich
9. **Chipotle Lime**: Chipotle peppers in adobo, lime juice, cumin — smoky and zesty
10. **Gochujang**: Korean chili paste, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, rice vinegar — sweet and savory
