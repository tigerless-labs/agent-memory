## Side Dish Pairings for Korean-Inspired BBQ

Perfect complements to Korean BBQ sauce on chicken and grilled asparagus:

1. **Kimchi Coleslaw**: Creamy coleslaw with spicy kick from kimchi — fermented flavors complement the sauce
2. **Cilantro Lime Rice**: Flavorful base with cilantro, lime, toasted sesame seeds, green onions
3. **Spicy Pineapple Slaw**: Sweet and spicy slaw with pineapple, red cabbage, gochujang
4. **Roasted Gochujang Sweet Potatoes**: Sweet potatoes with gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar — balances spicy chicken
5. **Quick Pickled Carrots**: Thinly sliced carrots in vinegar, sugar, Korean chili flakes — adds crunch and acidity
6. **Cucumber Kimchi Salad**: Thinly sliced cucumbers with kimchi, soy sauce, sesame oil — refreshing contrast
7. **Grilled Bok Choy**: Bok choy with soy sauce, garlic, sesame oil — Asian-inspired greens

**Serving tip**: Use mix of textures, temperatures, and flavor profiles to create complete meal experience.
