## Wood Chips for Smoking Pork Shoulder

- **Hickory**: Strong, sweet, savory flavor with notes of bacon and vanilla — classic choice
- **Apple Wood**: Fruity, sweet, mild flavor — complements richness of pork well
- **Cherry**: Mild, fruity flavor with hints of tartness — slightly sweeter and complex
- **Pecan**: Rich, nutty, slightly sweet flavor — adds depth and complexity
- **Post Oak**: Strong, smoky flavor with notes of vanilla and caramel — Texas-style BBQ favorite

**Tips**: Soak wood chips in water for 30+ minutes before smoking. Can combine different chips for unique flavor. Monitor temperature and adjust chip amount for consistent smoke flavor.
