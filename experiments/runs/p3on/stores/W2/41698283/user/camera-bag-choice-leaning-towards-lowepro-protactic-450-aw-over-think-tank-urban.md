Choosing between two camera bags for portrait and low-light photography work:

- **Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW** (leaning towards this)
  - Pros: better weather protection, more rugged, customizable compartments, larger capacity (fits 15" laptop), comfortable harness
  - Cons: bulkier, heavier, more utilitarian styling
  - Load capacity: can fit D5600 + 50mm prime + 18-55mm kit lens + additional gear

- Think Tank Urban Disguise 40L (alternative)
  - Pros: sleek urban design, lightweight, compact, multiple compartments (fits 13" laptop)
  - Cons: smaller capacity, less weather protection, less customizable

**Decision factor:** prioritizes weather protection and durability for protecting camera gear in varied conditions
