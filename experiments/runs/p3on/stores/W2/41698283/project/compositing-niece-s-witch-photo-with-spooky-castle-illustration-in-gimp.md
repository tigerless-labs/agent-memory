User is combining a PNG illustration of a spooky castle with a photo of their niece dressed as a witch. Already completed initial layer mask work to remove background from niece's image. Main issue: color and lighting mismatch between the two images. Techniques applied to solve this:

- Adjust Levels and Color Balance (Colors menu) to match castle illustration's tone
- Apply Colorize filter for unified color palette
- Add shadow/highlight effects using Drop Shadow and Dodge/Burn filters
- Layer blending modes tested: Multiply, Overlay, Soft Light
- Apply Lighting Effects filter for ambient light matching
- Merge down layers once satisfied with all adjustments

Status: In progress, learning GIMP compositing workflow.
