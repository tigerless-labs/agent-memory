Warlord Zsinj controls the old Sith Worlds at the core of his splinter empire, headquartered on non-Sith planet Serenno. Poses threat to both Imperial Remnant (ruled by Lizzie) and the New Republic (under Chancellor Mon Mothma). Lizzie proposed temporary ceasefire with the Republic to partition Zsinj's empire and reclaim the Sith Worlds for the Empire. Republic council is considering the proposal.
