Narrowed choice to two camera bags:

Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW (leaning toward)
- Weather resistant with rain cover
- Customizable compartments
- 450 capacity fits 15 inch laptop plus D5600, 50mm prime, 18-55mm kit lens
- Comfortable shoulder harness
- Cons: bulkier, heavier, more utilitarian style

Think Tank Urban Disguise 40L (alternative)
- Sleek urban design
- Lightweight, compact
- 40L capacity fits 13 inch laptop
- Cons: smaller capacity, less weather protection

Decision factor: prioritizes weather protection and durability for portrait and low-light work
