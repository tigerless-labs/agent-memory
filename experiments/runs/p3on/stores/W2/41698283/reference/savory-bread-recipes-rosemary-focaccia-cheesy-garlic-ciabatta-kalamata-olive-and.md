## Recommended Savory Bread Recipes

1. **Rosemary and Olive Oil Focaccia** (detailed recipe provided)
   - Ingredients: 1.5 cups warm water, 2 tsp active dry yeast, 3.5 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, coarse sea salt
   - Bake at 425°F for 20-25 minutes
   - Can substitute bread flour for all-purpose flour

2. **Cheesy Garlic Ciabatta** - airy bread with garlic and grated cheese

3. **Kalamata Olive and Thyme Bread** - briny olives with earthy thyme

4. **Sundried Tomato and Basil Bread** - sweet sun-dried tomatoes with fresh basil

5. **Rye and Caraway Seed Bread** - earthy rye with nutty caraway

## King Arthur Flour's Classic Sourdough Recipe
- Ingredients: 1 cup active sourdough starter, 3 cups bread flour, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp water
- Ferment 4-5 hours, proof 2-3 hours, bake at 450°F for 25-30 minutes in Dutch oven with steam
- Works well with bread flour substitution
