User interested in adding wide-angle lens to complement 70-200mm zoom.

14-24mm: Ultra-wide, dramatic perspective, good for sprawling landscapes, architecture, interior shots. More extreme distortion.

16-35mm: More versatile, wider range of applications. Good for cityscapes, landscapes, environmental portraits. Less extreme than 14-24mm.

Both complement telephoto lens nicely for creative range. Wide-angle shooting requires attention to distortion, vignetting, and flare. Practice needed to master technique.
