**Gitzo tripods: USD 300-1500**
Italian design, wide range of models, carbon fiber options, G-Lock system. Lighter weight, good for travel. Strengths: sleek design, portability.

**Really Right Stuff (RRS): USD 500-2500**
US-made, exceptional stability, lever-release locks, highly modular. Best for heavy lenses and demanding environments. Strengths: durability, precision, customization.

**Tripod weight impact:** Heavier tripods resist wind and support heavy lenses better; lighter tripods more portable.

**Key tripod head features:**
- Ballhead or panhead
- Quick-release plate
- Adjustable friction control
- Pan/tilt locks
- Bubble level
- Adequate load capacity
- Smooth drag

Popular brands: Arca-Swiss, Really Right Stuff, Gitzo, Manfrotto, Kirk Enterprises
