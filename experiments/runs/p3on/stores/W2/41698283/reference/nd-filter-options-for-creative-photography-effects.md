Considering neutral density filters for silky water effects, motion blur, and depth of field control.

High-quality options reviewed:
1. B+W 10-Stop ND Filter: well-respected brand, available in multiple sizes (52-72mm)
2. Hoya PRO1 Digital 10-Stop ND: quality brand, includes circular polarizer
3. Tiffen 10-Stop ND Filter: reputable brand, known for quality glass
4. NiSi 10-Stop ND Filter: newer brand, gained popularity, good price
5. Singh-Ray 10-Stop ND Filter: high-end brand, exceptional quality

Selection factors: filter size (must match lens diameter), stop value (3, 6, 10, or variable), glass quality, anti-reflective coatings, price
