Sheep breed options for small farms—Dorset (medium, excellent meat), Shetland (small, excellent wool, cool climate), Rambouillet (medium, excellent meat/wool, docile), Jacob (small, good meat/wool, hot climate), Ouessant (small, excellent wool, cool climate), Babydoll (small, good meat/wool, adaptable), Karacul (medium, excellent meat/wool, hot climate).
