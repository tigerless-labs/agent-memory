## Fresh Rosemary Tips
- Choose fragrant sprigs with vibrant green color; avoid wilted or brownish ones
- Store in plastic bag or wrap in refrigerator; lasts up to 1 week
- Chop just before using to release oils and flavors
- For Rosemary and Olive Oil Focaccia: use 2-3 sprigs (about 2-3 tbsp chopped leaves)
- Mix into dough during initial mixing for even distribution
- Rosemary is potent—start with less, taste as you go
- Can pair with other herbs like thyme, oregano, or parsley

## Bread Flour Substitution Tips
- Higher protein (12-14%) produces chewier crumb, larger holes, stronger gluten
- Imparts slightly sweeter, nuttier flavor
- Absorbs more water than all-purpose—may need to increase water
- Monitor dough progress; it may develop more quickly
- Be gentle when shaping to avoid excessive tension

## Sourdough Tips for Lighter, Airier Crumb
- Use healthy, active sourdough starter at peak activity
- Use bread flour (12-14% protein) or experiment with all-purpose
- Gentle mixing and folding technique—avoid overmixing
- Keep dough at 75-78°F for ideal fermentation
- Use higher water content: 70-80% hydration
- Slow, gentle proofing process; avoid over-proofing
- Score correctly before baking
- Bake with steam (Dutch oven or water pan)
- Monitor dough progress during fermentation and proofing
