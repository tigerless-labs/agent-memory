**Muffin Tin Frittata Recipe**

Ingredients (makes 6 frittatas):
- 6-8 eggs
- 1/2 cup diced veggies (user prefers bell peppers and onions)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or feta)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cooking spray or oil

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Whisk eggs with salt
3. Add diced veggies and cheese, mix well
4. Spray 6-cup muffin tin with cooking spray
5. Divide egg mixture evenly among cups
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until edges golden and centers set
7. Cool a few minutes before serving

Storage:
- Fridge: up to 3 days
- Freezer: up to 2 months

Reheating options:
- Microwave: 20-30 seconds
- Toaster oven: 5-7 min at 350°F
- Conventional oven: 10-12 min at 350°F
- Pan: 2-3 minutes per side

Other vegetable options: mushrooms, spinach, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, broccoli, asparagus, carrots
