Reputable jewelry and antique appraiser organizations to look for when searching for certified appraisers:

Jewelry Appraisers: National Association of Jewelry Appraisers (NAJA) - certifies jewelry appraisers and sets industry standards. American Gem Trade Association (AGTA) - certifies gemologists and appraisers, focusing on gemstones and jewelry. American Society of Appraisers (ASA) - multidisciplinary appraisal organization certifying appraisers in various fields including jewelry.

Antique and Collectible Appraisers: Appraisers Association of America (AAA) - certifies appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles. International Society of Appraisers (ISA) - non-profit certifying appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles. National Association of Collectible Appraisers (NACA) - certifies appraisers specializing in collectibles, antiques, toys, and memorabilia.

When selecting an appraiser, verify they hold certification from one of these organizations, have experience with similar items, are impartial/not affiliated with particular dealers, have professional affiliations, and are recognized by insurance companies.
