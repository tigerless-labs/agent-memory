Popular sunrise viewpoints in Rocky Mountain National Park:

**Foot-accessible or short-walk options:**
- **Bear Lake area** — popular spot for sunrise photography
- **Dream Lake** — short trail from Bear Lake with views of Hallett Peak and Flattop Mountain reflecting in water
- **Nymph Lake** — nearby summit with 360-degree views
- **Emerald Lake trail** — great sunrise views
- **Moraine Park** — open meadow accessible by short car ride + short walk, or accessible by short hiking trails to nearby viewpoints

**Drive-to viewpoint (longer drive):**
- **Trail Ridge Road summit** — requires ~1 hour drive; offers Alpine Visitor Center with amenities; spectacular alpine tundra views and surrounding peaks; highly recommended for sunrise photography

Also mentioned: Sprague Lake
