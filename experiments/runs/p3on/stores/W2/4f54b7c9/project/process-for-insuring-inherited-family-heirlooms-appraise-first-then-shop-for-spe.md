Appraisal Process: Get each item appraised by certified appraiser (NAJA, AGTA, or ASA certified). Gather documentation: proof of ownership, provenance, repair records, existing appraisals, photos/videos. Start with necklace first (most valuable), then music box and glassware. One appraisal per item initially; can get multiples only if needed.

Appraiser Selection: Look for certification from NAJA (jewelry), AGTA (gemstones), ASA (general), AAA, ISA, or NACA (antiques/collectibles). Search online for certified appraisers in area. Ask friends and family for referrals. Choose appraiser with experience in similar items. Music box may need specialized mechanical/musical items appraiser. Glassware appraiser should specialize in antique glass/ceramics.

Insurance Considerations: Use appraisal reports to shop for insurance. Look for agreed value coverage, no/low deductible, worldwide coverage, mysterious disappearance coverage. Recommended insurance companies: Jewelers Mutual (jewelry), Chubb, AXA ART (antiques/collectibles), Hagerty, State Farm, Allstate, Liberty Mutual.
