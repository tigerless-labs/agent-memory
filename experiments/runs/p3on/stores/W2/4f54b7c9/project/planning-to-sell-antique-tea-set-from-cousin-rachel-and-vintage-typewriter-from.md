## Items & Provenance

- **Antique tea set**: from cousin Rachel
- **Vintage typewriter**: belonged to dad

## Goal

Make extra cash while decluttering the apartment.

## Research & Sales Strategy

### Identify market value
Research online marketplaces (eBay completed listings, Etsy, Reddit r/antiques/r/vintage/r/typewriters) using targeted keywords:
- Tea set: antique/vintage tea set, material (porcelain, bone china), style (Victorian, Art Deco), brand, decorative details
- Typewriter: vintage/antique typewriter, brand (Smith Corona, Underwood, Olivetti), era, portable/manual/electronic

### Professional resources
- Certified appraisers: ISA (International Society of Appraisers), ASA (American Society of Appraisers)
- Online services: ValueMyStuff, WorthPoint, JustAnswer

### Local dealers
Contact antique dealers who specialize in tea sets and typewriters for buy offers or referrals.

## Approach When Contacting Dealers

1. Research and prepare: clean items, high-quality photos from multiple angles
2. Provide information: condition, materials, date if known, any restoration history
3. Highlight provenance: mention items came from family (cousin Rachel, dad), share relevant stories or documentation
4. Focus conversation on item features and value—briefly mention decluttering if asked, but don't emphasize personal motivation
5. Be prepared to negotiate
6. Get any sale agreement in writing
