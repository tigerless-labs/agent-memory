Maintains a manure compost pile from goat waste and is concerned about minimizing odor.

**Key odor-reduction strategies:**
- Maintain 2/3 brown materials (straw, hay) to 1/3 green materials (manure)
- Regularly turn pile to introduce oxygen
- Keep moisture consistent (like damp sponge)
- Keep pile 3-4 feet diameter, 3-5 feet tall
- Add bulking agents (straw, hay, shredded newspaper)
- Cover with carbon-rich material like straw or tarp
- Position downwind from living areas and away from water sources
- Maintain temperature 130-140°F (54-60°C)
- Add odor reducers: lime, dolomitic limestone, or zeolite
- Regular maintenance to prevent anaerobic pockets
