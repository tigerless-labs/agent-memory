Committed to controlling blood pressure by: maintaining healthy weight, eating a diet rich in fruits/vegetables/whole grains/lean proteins, exercising regularly (150+ min/week moderate intensity), quitting smoking, limiting alcohol (1 drink/day max), taking prescribed medications as directed, and regular healthcare follow-ups. Understands hypertension is often asymptomatic but serious if untreated. Plans to work closely with healthcare provider on ongoing management.
