User intends to expand the herd beyond Luna and Leo by adding more goats at a future date.

**Approach discussed:**
- Set up quarantine area for new goats before introduction
- Source from reputable breeders or rescues
- Ensure new goats are healthy, vaccinated, and dewormed
- Introduce gradually starting with visual contact through separate pens
- Use neutral area for meetings after 2-3 days
- Supervise behavior closely for signs of aggression/stress
- Gradually increase interaction time
- Provide multiple feeding/water stations and shelter to reduce competition
- Consider introducing goats of similar age, size, and temperament
- Avoid introducing during times of stress (weather, vaccinations, environment changes)
