**Timeline:** Next month from 2023-03-25 (approximately late April or early May 2023)

**Purpose:** Vaccinate Luna and Leo

**Recommended vaccinations per vet discussion:**
- CDT (Clostridium perfringens type C and D, and tetanus) — typically given at 2-3 weeks of age with booster 2-3 weeks later
- Pasteurella multocida (P. multocida) — typically given at 2-3 months of age with booster 2-3 weeks later
- Regional vaccines may include: Coccidiosis, Giardiasis, Leptospirosis

**Prep steps to take before visit:**
- Acclimate goats to handling (ears, legs, underside)
- Get them used to containers/carriers
- Desensitize to noise
- Practice halter breaking if using one
- Practice being weighed
- Consider a mock vet visit with friend/family
- Choose sturdy, well-ventilated carrier and introduce weeks before
- Discuss health concerns and medical history with vet beforehand
