Planning a dinner party featuring a signature cocktail and complementary summer dishes.

**Menu**
- Cocktail: Summer Sunset Spritz
- Appetizers: Honeyed Goat Cheese Crostini (toasted baguette, goat cheese, honey, thyme) and Fresh Fruit and Prosciutto Skewers
- Main Course: Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken (marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, thyme, rosemary; served with roasted vegetables or salad)

**Planning Approach**
- Preps ingredients and marinades in advance
- Appreciates dishes that can be prepared ahead of time
- Values smooth execution and timing
- Plans to delegate tasks and create inviting atmosphere
- Focus on light, refreshing flavors that complement each other
