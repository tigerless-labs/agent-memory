Website builders evaluated for digital marketing agency website:

1. **Wix** - drag-and-drop editor, wide range of templates, user-friendly, suitable for agencies
2. **Squarespace** - stylish, user-friendly, focus on design and UX, perfect for creative agencies
3. **Weebly** - drag-and-drop editor, various templates, quick and easy setup
4. **WordPress.com** - hosted CMS, range of templates, user-friendly, wide integrations
5. **Strikingly** - specializes in one-page websites and portfolios, visually appealing
6. **Format** - designed for creatives, focus on showcasing visual content
7. **Carrd** - simple one-page website builder and portfolios
