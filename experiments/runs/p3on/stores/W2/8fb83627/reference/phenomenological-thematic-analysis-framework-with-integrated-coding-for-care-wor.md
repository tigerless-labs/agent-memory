Developing a phenomenological analysis approach using thematic coding to understand care work experiences. Key codes:
- CM: Impact of comments from residents on mood and work
- IA: Interoceptive abilities and empathy in care work  
- FC: Fear of not being able to connect with residents; desire to prove worth
- RES: Role of resilience in elder care
- MC: Mindfulness and compassion approaches to care work
- AI: Potential impact of artificial intelligence on elder care context

Methodology draws on principles of phenomenological research: bracketing (setting aside preconceptions), phenomenological reduction (focusing on essential characteristics), and phenomenological interpretation (faithful reflection on lived meanings). Organized codes into categories that show how themes relate and patterns emerge.
