A gin-based aperitif cocktail created for dinner parties.

**Ingredients**
- 1.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz basic simple syrup (1:1 water:sugar)
- 0.5 oz lemon juice
- 2 oz Prosecco
- Splash of soda water
- Garnish: edible flowers or lavender sprig

**Notes**
The user adapted the traditional Lavender Honey Spritz recipe by substituting their homemade basic simple syrup (no flavor infusion) in place of the lavender-infused version. The drink remains light and refreshing, with honey providing sweetness instead of the syrup.

Named by the user as 'Summer Sunset Spritz' to evoke warm summer evenings.
