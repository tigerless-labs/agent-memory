A powerful first-person account by Yanomami shaman Davi Kopenawa (with French anthropologist Bruce Albert). Shares the perspective of the Yanomami people, their spiritual beliefs, traditions, and struggles with colonization, disease, and modernization. Highly recommended for understanding Yanomami culture and worldview.
