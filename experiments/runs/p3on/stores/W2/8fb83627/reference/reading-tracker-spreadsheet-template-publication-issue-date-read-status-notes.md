Spreadsheet template created April 2023 to organize reading progress. Structure includes sections for Magazines, Online Publications, and Newspapers. Columns: Publication name, Issue/Date, Read (Y/N or checkbox), Notes. Can expand with Rating (1-5 stars), Favorite Articles, and Topics columns. National Geographic example: Issue 1-3 marked complete, Issue 4 in progress.
