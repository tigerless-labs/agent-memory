Effective techniques for striped bass fishing (learned from Outer Banks guided trip and successfully applied to Lake Norman):\n\n- Use slower, more deliberate retrieve, especially effective when water is calm\n- Pay close attention to structure (submerged humps, points, drop-offs) where bass congregate\n- Look for areas with active baitfish activity (schools of shad, herring, shiners)\n- Fish depths ranging from 5-20 feet depending on conditions\n- Use medium to heavy action rod and reel for handling larger fish\n- Effective lures include jigs (1-2 oz white or chartreuse), spoons (1-2 oz silver/gold/copper), and topwater baits\n- Live bait (shad, herring, shiners) can be very effective\n- Best fishing during dawn, dusk, and night, especially summer months\n- Striped bass prefer water temperatures between 50-75°F (optimal 60-70°F)
