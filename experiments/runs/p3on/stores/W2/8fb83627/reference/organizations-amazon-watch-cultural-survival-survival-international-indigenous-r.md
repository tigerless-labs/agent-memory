Three key organizations working on indigenous rights and conservation:\n- **Amazon Watch**: Protects the Amazon rainforest and indigenous community rights\n- **Cultural Survival**: Advocates for indigenous peoples' rights globally, including Amazon Basin communities\n- **Survival International**: Campaigns for indigenous and tribal peoples' rights, including Amazon rainforest inhabitants\n\nAll provide news, articles, reports, and updates on indigenous struggles and victories.
