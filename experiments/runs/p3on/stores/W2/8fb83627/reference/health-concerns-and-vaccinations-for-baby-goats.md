**Core Vaccinations:**
- **CDT** (Clostridium perfringens C & D, tetanus): 2-3 weeks old, booster 2-3 weeks later
- **P. multocida** (Pasteurella multocida): 2-3 months old, booster 2-3 weeks later
- Regional vaccines may include Coccidiosis, Giardiasis, Leptospirosis

**Critical Health Concerns:**

1. **Neonatal care:** Adequate colostrum (first milk) within first hours of life
2. **Scours (diarrhea):** Common, can cause dehydration/electrolyte imbalance — monitor stool quality
3. **Pneumonia:** Watch for coughing, rapid breathing, nasal discharge
4. **Hypothermia:** Provide warm, dry shelter; monitor body temperature, especially in cold/wet
5. **Parasite control:** Check for ticks, lice, mites; internal worms — consult vet on timing
6. **Nutritional deficiencies:** Balanced milk/replacer + starter feed; monitor growth
7. **Umbilical cord care:** Keep clean/dry; apply antibacterial ointment or iodine daily
8. **Disbudding/dehorning:** Consult vet on timing and method

**Regular monitoring:** Schedule health checks with vet for development, vaccinations, parasite control, and any concerns.
