**Height:** Minimum 4 feet (1.2 m), preferably 5+ feet. Goats are climbers.

**Materials:**
- **Woven wire fencing:** 2-inch × 4-inch mesh, 12.5 gauge wire minimum — prevents head/horn entanglement
- **High-tensile fencing:** Strong and durable but less effective at preventing escape
- **Cattle panels:** Sturdy, easy to install
- **Field fencing:** Woven wire for larger areas
- **Goat panels:** Smaller mesh (1-inch × 2-inch), designed specifically for goats

**Design details:**
- Posts spaced 8-10 feet apart
- Sturdy corner posts with anchor system
- Gates must be secure, self-closing preferred
- Add visibility strips/flags to help goats see boundaries
- If predators present, add coyote-proof layer or livestock guardian

**Important:** Electric fencing NOT recommended for bottle-fed baby goats—they don't understand the concept and may experience repeated shocks causing stress/harm. Only consider for older goats (3-4 months+) after weaning.
