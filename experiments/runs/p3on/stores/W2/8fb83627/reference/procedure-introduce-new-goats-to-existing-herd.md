Before introduction:

Set up separate quarantine area for new goats

Ensure new goats are healthy from reputable sources

Verify vaccinations and deworming complete

Introduction process:

Visual introductions first: place new goats adjacent to existing herd for 2-3 days, allow sight/sound/scent contact without physical contact

Move to neutral area: after 2-3 days, meet in separate paddock or pen under close supervision

Monitor behavior: watch for aggression, fear, or stress. Separate immediately if issues arise.

Gradually increase interaction time over several days

Provide multiple resources: feeding stations, water sources, shelter to reduce competition

Factors for success:

Introduce goats of similar age and size

Choose similar temperaments

Avoid stressful times like weather changes or vaccinations

Supervise constantly and be ready to intervene

Be patient as process takes time
