Preparation steps weeks before visit:

Acclimate to handling: gentle petting, touching ears/legs, practice lifting legs

Comfort with containers: practice in dog crate or pen

Desensitize to noise: gradually increase volume of sounds

Practice halter breaking with treats and praise

Practice being weighed at home

Mock vet visit with friend or family

Introduce carrier in advance and make positive

Day of visit preparations:

Fast 12-24 hours before

Keep calm and relaxed

Bring favorite treats and hay

Consider companion animal for support

Stay calm yourself since goats sense your energy

Vet communication:

Discuss health concerns in advance

Share vaccination and medical history

Inform of behavioral issues
