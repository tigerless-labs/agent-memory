**Hotel 50 Bowery**\nLocation: 50 Bowery, between Canal and Hester Streets, Chinatown, NYC\n\nAmenities:\n- 229 modern guest rooms\n- Rooftop bar called 'The Crown' with city views and craft cocktails\n- Fitness center\n- 24-hour front desk\n- Complimentary continental breakfast\n- Flat-screen TVs and sleek bathrooms\n\nRoom types: King, Queen, Double, Suites (some with city views, some facing courtyard)\n\nLocation details:\n- ~10-15 min walk to Times Square\n- ~20-25 min walk to Central Park\n- Near Little Italy, SoHo, Brooklyn Bridge area\n- Trendy neighborhood with lively atmosphere\n\nRating: Average 4.5/5 stars on TripAdvisor\n\nPros: Unique Chinatown location, stylish modern rooms, affordable price point, rooftop bar\nCons: Not directly in Midtown (need extra travel time to reach certain attractions), some rooms small, can be noisy at night
