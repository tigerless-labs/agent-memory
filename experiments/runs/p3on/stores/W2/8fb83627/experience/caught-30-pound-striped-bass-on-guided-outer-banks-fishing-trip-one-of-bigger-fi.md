Took a guided striped bass fishing trip to the Outer Banks. Weather was challenging with strong winds and rough seas. Despite difficult conditions, the knowledgeable captain was able to find and land several striped bass. Caught one particularly memorable striper weighing approximately 30 pounds—the captain noted it was one of the largest fish he'd seen that season. The fish put up a strong fight. This trip was formative for learning striped bass techniques that were later successfully applied to Lake Norman fishing.
