Resident comments like 'you speak to say nothing' trigger a state of self-related thought processes and 'experiential withdrawal'—a disconnect from shared experience. This state affects interoceptive abilities situationally and impacts capacity to help and extend empathy. The user has learned to mentalize and sometimes verbalize these responses in the moment, and can acknowledge what's happening, but the long-term emotional effects remain unclear and sometimes surprising in their persistence.
