Regular success fishing for largemouth bass on Lake Norman using soft plastic worms (4-6 inch worms in natural colors like green pumpkin, watermelon, or junebug). Uses Texas rig, Carolina rig, or wacky rig for presentation. Focuses on structures like submerged vegetation, rocks, sunken logs, and drop-offs. Fishes depths of 5-20 feet with slow, steady retrieves or hop-and-stop action. Best results during spring and fall when water temperatures are 60-80°F, especially at dawn, dusk, and early morning.
