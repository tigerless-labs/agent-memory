Planning to purchase a new laptop with the following specifications:

**Required specs:**
- Minimum 8GB RAM
- SSD storage
- Processor: not yet decided (considering Intel Core i5/i7 or AMD Ryzen 5/7)

**Form factor considerations:**

*2-in-1 laptops* (hybrid tablet/laptop)
- Pros: versatility, lighter weight, touchscreen capabilities
- Cons: compromised keyboard/trackpad, uneven weight distribution, shorter battery life

*Traditional clamshell laptops*
- Pros: better keyboard/trackpad comfort, longer battery life, more affordable
- Cons: less versatile, bulkier design, limited tablet functionality

**Decision pending:** Whether to prioritize productivity features (keyboard/trackpad quality, battery life) or versatility/portability.
