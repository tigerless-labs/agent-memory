Actively working on improvisation skills through:\n\n- Solo practice exercises (free writing, stream-of-consciousness monologues, character development, scene work, sound and movement, status games, emotional exploration)\n- Recording and reflecting on practice sessions\n- Watching plays like *A Midsummer Night's Dream* for inspiration and character study\n- Applying improvisation techniques to audition preparation\n\nFocuses on core principles: listening, responding truthfully, 'yes, and...', staying present in the moment, not overthinking, and playing to the top of intelligence.
