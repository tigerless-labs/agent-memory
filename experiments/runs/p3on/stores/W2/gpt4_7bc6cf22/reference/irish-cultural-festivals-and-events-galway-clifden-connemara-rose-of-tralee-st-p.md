Recommended Irish cultural festivals and celebrations:

- **Galway International Arts Festival** — July, 2 weeks. Features theater, dance, music, and visual arts from around the world.
- **Clifden Arts Festival** — September. Showcases Irish traditional and contemporary art and culture, with music, poetry, theater, and film.
- **Connemara Mussel Festival** — May. Celebrates local mussel industry with food tastings, music, and family events.
- **Rose of Tralee International Festival** — August. Celebrates Irish culture and heritage, with focus on women's accomplishments and empowerment.
- **St. Patrick's Day** — March 17th. National holiday with celebrations and parades throughout Ireland, featuring music, dancing, and green celebrations.

All events are annual and primarily located in Connacht or wider Ireland.
