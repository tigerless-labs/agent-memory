Recommended trackers: Fitbit Charge 3 (affordable, tracks steps/distance/calories/intensity, 50m water-resistant); Garmin Vivosport (GPS, heart rate, 50m water-resistant); Apple Watch Series 5 (advanced, dance tracking, GPS, 50m water-resistant); Samsung Galaxy Fit (heart rate, sleek, 50m water-resistant); Polar M430 (high-end, heart rate/GPS/running power, 30m water-resistant).
