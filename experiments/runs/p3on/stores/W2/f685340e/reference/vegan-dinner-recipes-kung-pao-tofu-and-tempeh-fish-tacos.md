## Kung Pao Tofu
Stir-fry cubed tofu with peanuts, vegetables, and chili peppers in a savory sauce. Serve with steamed rice or noodles.

Marinating tips:
- Press tofu first to remove excess liquid (30+ minutes)
- Use mixture of soy sauce, rice vinegar, and cornstarch
- Add minced garlic, ginger, and scallions
- Marinate 30 minutes to 1 hour (don't over-marinate)
- Fry in peanut or avocado oil for depth

## Tempeh Fish Tacos
Marinate tempeh in lime juice, chili powder, cumin, then pan-fry until crispy. Serve in tacos with slaw and avocado.

Marinating tips:
- Crumble tempeh into small pieces
- Soak in citrus-based marinade (lime juice) with chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika
- Add small amount of nutritional yeast or miso paste for umami
- Marinate 30 minutes to 1 hour (don't over-soak)
- Pat dry and pan-fry or bake until crispy
