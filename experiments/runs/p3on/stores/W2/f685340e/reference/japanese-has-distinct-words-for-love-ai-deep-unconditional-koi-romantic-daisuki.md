Japanese language has multiple words for 'love' that reflect cultural values about expressing emotion and context-dependent intensity:\n- **ai** (愛): deep and unconditional love\n- **koi** (恋): romantic love\n- **daisuki** (大好き): liking or affection\n\nThe existence of these distinct terms suggests cultural emphasis on nuance, context, and appropriate expression based on the type and intensity of feeling.
