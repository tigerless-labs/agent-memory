## Recommended psychological thrillers (March 2023):

1. Gone Girl by Gillian Flynn — Classic twisty marriage thriller
2. Sharp Objects by Gillian Flynn — Journalist returns home to cover murders
3. The Girl on the Train by Paula Hawkins — Commuter obsessed with perfect couple
4. The Woman in the Window by A.J. Finn — Modern 'Rear Window' about recluse witnessing crime
5. The Couple Next Door by Shari Lapena — Baby goes missing, secrets revealed
6. **The Last Mrs. Parrish by Liv Constantine** — Debut novel; woman manipulates way into wealthy family's life; praised for dark humor and social commentary
7. The Wife Between Us by Greer Hendricks and Sarah Pekkanen — Marriage secrets told through unreliable narrators
8. The Poison Tree by Erin Kelly — Dark family secrets and tragedy
9. The Devil in the White City by Erik Larson — Historical thriller about H.H. Holmes and 1893 World's Fair
10. The 7 1/2 Deaths of Evelyn Hardcastle by Stuart Turton — Man wakes in different body each day to solve murder

User particularly interested in **The Last Mrs. Parrish** (planned to read next) for its dark humor and debut author's approach to the genre.
