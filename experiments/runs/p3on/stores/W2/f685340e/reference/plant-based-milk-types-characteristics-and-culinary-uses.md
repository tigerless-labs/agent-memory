## Almond Milk
- Made from: almonds and water
- Nutrition: Low calories, rich in vitamins, naturally low fat (often fortified)
- Flavor: Neutral, slightly nutty
- Best for: Cereal, smoothies, desserts, baking (cakes, muffins, cookies)

## Soy Milk
- Made from: soybeans and water
- Nutrition: High in protein and isoflavones, fortified varieties available
- Flavor: Slightly nutty, earthy
- Best for: Savory dishes (stir-fries, soups), creamy sauces, Asian-inspired dishes

## Cashew Milk
- Made from: cashews and water
- Nutrition: Creamy, rich in magnesium and vitamins, often fortified
- Flavor: Mild, slightly sweet
- Best for: Creamy sauces (Alfredo, hollandaise), desserts (cheesecakes, ice cream)

## Oat Milk
- Made from: oats and water
- Nutrition: Low calories, rich in fiber, naturally lactose-free, often fortified
- Flavor: Neutral, slightly sweet
- Best for: Cereal, smoothies, desserts, breakfast dishes, baking (bread, muffins, cakes)

## Selection Factors
1. Allergy concerns (nuts, soy, gluten)
2. Flavor profile compatibility with recipe
3. Nutritional needs (protein, calcium, vitamins)
4. Desired texture (creamy vs light)
5. Environmental impact considerations
