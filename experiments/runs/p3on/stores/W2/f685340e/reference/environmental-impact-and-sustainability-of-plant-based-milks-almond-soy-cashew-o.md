## Almond Milk
Water usage: 1.1 gallons of water per almond (high)
Issues: California drought impact, pesticide use, long-distance transportation
Flavor/uses: Neutral, slightly nutty; good for cereal, smoothies, baking

## Soy Milk
Water usage: Moderate (less than almond)
Issues: Deforestation in Amazon, fertilizer/pesticide pollution, transportation
Flavor/uses: Slightly nutty, earthy; good for savory dishes, creamy sauces, Asian-inspired recipes
Nutritional: High in protein and isoflavones

## Cashew Milk
Water usage: Moderate
Issues: Pesticide use, fair labor/environmental concerns in developing countries, transportation
Flavor/uses: Mild, slightly sweet; ideal for creamy sauces (Alfredo, hollandaise), desserts
Characteristics: Creamy texture, rich in magnesium

## Oat Milk
Water usage: Low (most water-efficient option)
Issues: Minimal pesticide use, good for soil health
Advantages: Crop rotation practices, fewer synthetic fertilizers, potential for local production
Flavor/uses: Neutral, slightly sweet; great for cereal, smoothies, baking, breakfast dishes

## How to Choose
- Look for eco-certifications (Fairtrade, Organic, Regenerative Organic Certified)
- Support local brands to reduce transportation
- Check brand's environmental policies
- Consider DIY options to reduce packaging waste
