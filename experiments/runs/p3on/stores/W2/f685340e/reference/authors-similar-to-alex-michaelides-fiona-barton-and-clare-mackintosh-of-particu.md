Authors similar to Alex Michaelides for psychological thrillers.

HIGHEST INTEREST: Fiona Barton (The Widow, The Child, The Suspect) - dark, atmospheric writing with complex characters; Clare Mackintosh (I Let You Go, The Farm, The Lie of You, The Other Mrs. Miller) - former police officer bringing authenticity to thrillers.

ALSO RECOMMENDED: B.A. Paris (Behind Closed Doors, The Breakdown), Mary Kubica (The Good Girl, Pretty Baby), Renee Knight (Disclaimer, The Good Liar), Louise Candlish (Our House, Those People), C.J. Tudor (The Chalk Man, The Taking of Annie Thorne), Sandie Jones (The Other Mrs. Jones).
