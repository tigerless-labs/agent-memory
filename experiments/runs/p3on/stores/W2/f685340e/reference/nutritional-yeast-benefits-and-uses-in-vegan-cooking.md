Nutritional yeast is a type of yeast grown on sugarcane or beet molasses, then harvested, washed, and dried.

## Nutritional Benefits
- High in protein (good source for vegan diets)
- Rich in B vitamins, including vitamin B12 (often lacking in plant-based diets)
- Good source of fiber (both soluble and insoluble)
- Mineral-rich: selenium, zinc, and copper

## Flavor Profile
- Nutty, cheesy, and slightly sweet flavor
- Often described as umami
- Potent - start with about 1/2 teaspoon and adjust to taste

## Common Uses
- Cheese substitutes and vegan cheese alternatives
- Savory dishes like soups, stews, and sauces
- Vegan meat alternatives (tempeh, tofu, seitan)
- Snacks (popcorn, vegan pizza, crackers)

## Use in Tempeh Fish Tacos
Adds savory umami flavor that enhances the "fishy" profile, balances the acidity of lime juice, and adds richness/complexity to the dish.
