## Equipment Needed
- High-speed blender (Vitamix or Blendtec)
- Cheesecloth or nut milk bag
- Large bowl or container
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Optional: flavorings and sweeteners (vanilla, cinnamon, dates)

## Process
1. Soak 1 cup rolled oats in water for 8 hours or overnight
2. Drain and rinse oats
3. Add soaked oats to blender with 2 cups fresh water
4. Blend on high speed for 2 minutes until smooth
5. Pour through cheesecloth/nut milk bag into bowl
6. Squeeze to extract all milk from pulp
7. Optional: filter through fine-mesh sieve for smoother milk
8. Store in refrigerator, use within 3-5 days

## Tips
- Use 1:2 ratio (oats:water) for creamier milk, 1:3 for lighter milk
- Add flavorings like vanilla, cinnamon, or dates
- Experiment with different oat types (steel-cut, Irish oats)
- Add pinch of sea salt to enhance flavor
- Oat pulp can be saved for oat flour or animal feed
