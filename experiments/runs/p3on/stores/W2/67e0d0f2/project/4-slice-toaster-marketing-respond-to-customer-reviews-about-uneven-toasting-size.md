Customer feedback on 4-slice toaster product includes recurring issues: uneven toasting (top doesn't toast evenly), size/weight/short cord, loud jarring eject function, ineffective crumb tray. Customers appreciate sleek design, wide slots, 4-slice capacity, digital settings. Marketing approach: acknowledge feedback, highlight relevant features, promise design improvements, create promotional blog post focusing on benefits.
