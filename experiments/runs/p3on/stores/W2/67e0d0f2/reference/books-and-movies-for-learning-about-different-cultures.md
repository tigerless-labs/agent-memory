## Books for Cultural Learning

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri
   - Explores themes of identity, family, and culture
   - Follows a Bengali-American family

2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini
   - Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan
   - Themes: friendship, betrayal, and redemption

3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan
   - Follows lives of four Chinese-American women
   - Explores relationships with immigrant mothers

4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe
   - Explores life of a Nigerian tribe before and after British colonization
   - Perspectives on imperialism and cultural change

5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi
   - Graphic novel format
   - Story of a young Iranian girl growing up during the Islamic Revolution

## Movies for Cultural Learning

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon**
   - Martial arts epic set in ancient China
   - Themes: loyalty and honor

2. **The Lives of Others**
   - German film set in East Berlin during the Cold War
   - Themes: surveillance, loyalty, and human connection

3. **Lion**
   - True story of an Indian boy who gets lost and adopted by Australian family
   - Journey to find birth family

4. **The Motorcycle Diaries**
   - Based on travel diaries of Che Guevara and Alberto
   - Travel across South America, explores injustices faced by the poor

5. **City of God**
   - Brazilian film
   - Follows various characters living in a favela (slum)
   - Themes: poverty, violence, and survival
