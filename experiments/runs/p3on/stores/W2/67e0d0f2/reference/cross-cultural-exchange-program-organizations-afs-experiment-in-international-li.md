## Exchange Program Organizations

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** (https://afs.org/)
   - One of the largest non-profits providing intercultural learning opportunities for students of all ages
   - Programs: student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering opportunities
   - Coverage: 50+ countries

2. **Experiment in International Living** (https://www.experiment.org/)
   - Nonprofit offering immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students
   - Format: host families, attend local schools, participate in community service projects

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** (https://www.yfu.org/)
   - International educational organization providing exchange programs for students and young adults
   - Programs: year-long exchanges, semester programs, summer programs
   - Coverage: 40+ countries

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** (https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/)
   - Nonprofit providing volunteering opportunities for people of all ages
   - Locations: Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
   - Focus: community development projects and cross-cultural exchange
