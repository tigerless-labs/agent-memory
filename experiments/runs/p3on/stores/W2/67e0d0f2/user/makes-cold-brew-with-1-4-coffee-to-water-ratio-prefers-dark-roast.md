Prefers cold brew (steep 12-24 hours) with dark roast coffee. Standard ratio: 1 part coarsely ground coffee to 4 parts water (e.g., 1 cup coffee to 4 cups water). Dark roasts give bold, rich flavor with chocolate/caramel notes and lower acidity—better for cold brew than light roasts.
