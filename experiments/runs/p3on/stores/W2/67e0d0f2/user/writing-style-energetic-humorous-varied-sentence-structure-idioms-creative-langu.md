Prefers rewrites with:
- Energetic and passionate tone with humor and creative analogies
- Varied sentence length and complexity (short and long mixed)
- Common idioms woven throughout
- Descriptive language and figurative expressions
- Synonyms and antonyms for variety
- Unique but easy-to-understand English words
- Avoids uniform sentence structure (hallmark of AI writing)
- Maintains original meaning and information while enhancing engagement
