Writing a song on ukulele with the concept of a morning commute, observing the city waking up. The song aims to capture:\n\n**Sensory details:**\n- Sounds: birds chirping, cars starting, people chatting\n- Smells: coffee, fresh bread, exhaust fumes\n- Visuals: light changes as sun rises, golden light on skyscrapers\n- Atmosphere: mix of people rushing to work and lingering in cafes\n\n**Themes to explore:**\n- Excitement of new day full of possibilities\n- Monotony and routine of daily life\n- Community and connection with fellow commuters\n- Personal emotional journey during morning hours\n\n**Musical approach:**\n- Using vivid sensory language and imagery\n- Experimenting with rhythm to mirror the city's awakening\n- Incorporating harmonica parts for texture and flavor\n- Keeping structure simple and focused\n\nThis is an active, in-progress project.
