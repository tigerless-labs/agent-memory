Root rot signs: soft/mushy roots, brown/black roots, yellowing or browning leaves, shriveling, wilting, black leaf spots, foul soil smell. Prevention: use well-draining soil, water only when soil is completely dry, avoid over-watering in winter, ensure drainage holes, use succulent-specific potting mix, don't let water sit in trays, monitor and repot as needed.
