**How to Caramelize Onions**

Key tips:
- Use sweet onions like Vidalia or Maui
- Slice thinly, about 1/8 inch thick
- Use large pan to avoid overcrowding
- Cook on low heat around 275F for even cooking
- Takes 30 minutes to over an hour
- Stir occasionally every 10-15 minutes to prevent burning
- Add pinch of salt to bring out natural sugars
- Use small amount of butter or oil
- Watch carefully as onions can quickly go from perfect to burnt

Basic recipe:
- 1 large sweet onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons butter or oil
- Pinch of salt
- Cook 15 minutes, add salt, cook another 15-30 minutes, stir every 10-15 minutes until desired caramelization
