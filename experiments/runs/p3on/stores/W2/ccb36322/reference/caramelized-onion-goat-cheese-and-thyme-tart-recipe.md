**Caramelized Onion, Goat Cheese, and Thyme Tart**

For the filling:
- 1/2 cup caramelized onions
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese  
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

Use with a basic savory tart crust (flour, butter, salt, ice water). Bake at 375F for 35-40 minutes until golden.
