**Savory Tart Ideas**

Other recipes mentioned:

1. **Spinach, Feta, and Sun-dried Tomato Tart** - Mediterranean-inspired with flaky crust
2. **Roasted Garlic and Rosemary Tart** - Roasted garlic mashed with olive oil, rosemary, blend of Parmesan, Cheddar, and mozzarella
3. **Caramelized Shallot, Blue Cheese, and Fresh Parsley Tart** - Rich pairing of sweet shallots with pungent blue cheese
4. **Asparagus, Goat Cheese, and Chive Tart** - Spring tart with blanched asparagus, crumbled goat cheese, chives

**Basic Savory Tart Crust:**
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1/4 cup ice-cold water
- Salt to taste
- Mix until dough comes together, refrigerate 30+ minutes, bake at 375F
