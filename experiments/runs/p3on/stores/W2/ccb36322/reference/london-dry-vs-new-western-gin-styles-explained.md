London Dry Gin: Traditional style, juniper-forward and dry, with botanicals like coriander, angelica, citrus peels. Examples: Beefeater, Bombay Sapphire (owned), Tanqueray, Gordon's. Best for classic cocktails like Martini, Negroni, Gin & Tonic. New Western/New World Gin: Modern style emerged 1990s-2000s, more complex with softer, nuanced flavors. Features wider range of botanicals including citrus, flowers, herbs, spices. Examples: Hendrick's (owned), The Botanist, Aviation. Better for modern cocktails and those preferring subtle gin flavor.
