Key recipes recommended for exploration with homegrown herbs: Rosemary Gin Spritz (gin, prosecco, rosemary syrup, lemon, soda water), Basil Gimlet (gin, lime juice, basil syrup), and cucumber-based cocktails like Cucumber Gimlet and Hendrick's Cucumber Collins. Rosemary and basil syrups made by steeping fresh herbs in sugar-water for 10-15 minutes.
