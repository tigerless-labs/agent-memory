# NJ Local News Community Research Findings

Study of three New Jersey communities examined local news information needs.

## Communities Studied
- Blairstown (Ridge View Echo)
- Paterson  
- Trenton

## Universal Information Needs (All Three Communities)
- **Service Journalism**: Information about municipal government meetings, service providers, contact information for local leaders
- **Facebook Presence**: People rely heavily on Facebook as first place for news updates and expect publishers to be visible there
- **Feel-good News**: Want more solutions-oriented, positive local news stories

## Specific Community Needs
- **Paterson & Trenton**: Demand for more local news about crime and safety
- **Paterson & Blairstown**: Content in languages other than English would grow audience

## Methodology Lessons Learned
- Difficult to 'parachute' into communities without deep roots
- Even with local publisher assistance, more face time and creative outreach needed
- Publishers appreciated understanding audiences at formative early stage

## Recommendations for Continued Engagement
- Conduct similar research again in 6 months or 1 year
- Establish iterative feedback loop with audiences
- Implement improvements based on findings
