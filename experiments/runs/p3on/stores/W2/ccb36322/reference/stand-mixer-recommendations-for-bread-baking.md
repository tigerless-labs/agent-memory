**Stand Mixer Recommendations**

High-end options:
- KitchenAid Professional 600 Series: 6-quart bowl, 575-watt motor
- Bosch Universal Plus: 6.5-quart bowl, 800-watt motor
- Cuisinart SM-50: 5-quart bowl, 500-watt motor
- Breville Bakery Chef: 5-quart bowl, 550-watt motor

Beginner-friendly options:
- **KitchenAid Classic Plus**: 4.5-quart bowl, 275-watt motor - chosen option for consideration
- Cuisinart FP-14DC: 3.5-quart bowl, 250-watt motor
- Sunbeam 2594: 3-quart bowl, 200-watt motor

Look for: planetary mixing action, durable construction, appropriate motor power for dough type
