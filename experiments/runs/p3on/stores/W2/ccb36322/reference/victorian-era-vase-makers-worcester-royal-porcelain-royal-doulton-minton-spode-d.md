## Major Victorian-era Vase Makers\n\n**Worcester Royal Porcelain** (est. 1751)\n- Known for high-quality, ornate vases\n- Japanese-inspired designs (1860s-1880s): cherry blossoms, chrysanthemums, birds\n- Pale blue/celadon glaze\n- Aesthetic Movement vases (1870s-1890s): curvilinear lines, botanical motifs\n- Renaissance Revival (1850s-1880s): ornate designs, acanthus leaves\n- Moorcroft-style (1880s-1900s): hand-painted designs\n- Notable artists: Charles Baldwyn, George Owen, William Moorcroft, William Moore\n- Date codes on base; crown mark with "Worcester" or "Royal Worcester"\n\n**Royal Doulton** (est. 1815)\n- Beautifully decorated vases with figurative and floral designs\n\n**Minton** (est. 1793, Stoke-on-Trent)\n- Ornate, highly decorated vases with intricate patterns\n\n**Spode** (est. 1767)\n- Famous for blue-and-white underglaze printing technique\n\n**Derby Porcelain** (est. 1750)\n- Ornate, decorative vases with floral and figurative designs\n\n## How to Identify\n\n- Examine base for maker's marks, signatures, stamps\n- Note style, pattern, design elements (florals, figures, geometric)\n- Determine material (porcelain, earthenware, ceramic)\n- Assess condition and any repairs\n- Look for artist signatures\n- Research through eBay, Etsy, auction catalogs, museum collections (V&A London, Metropolitan Museum NY)\n- Consult with appraisers specializing in antique ceramics
