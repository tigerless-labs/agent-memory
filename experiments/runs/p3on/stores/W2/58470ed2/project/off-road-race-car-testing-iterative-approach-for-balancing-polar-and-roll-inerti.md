Steps for on-track testing to find optimal inertia balance:

1. **Gather baseline data** on the track(s) — layout, surface conditions, other variables
2. **Create mathematical model** of vehicle and track to predict behavior and identify improvements
3. **Design experiments** with different inertia levels via suspension, body shape, component changes
4. **Test on actual track** with each variant to measure real-world performance
5. **Iterate** based on results until achieving best compromise between smooth and rough track performance

The goal is a vehicle that performs acceptably on both smooth (needs lower inertia agility) and rough (needs higher inertia stability) terrain.
