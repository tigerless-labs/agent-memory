When designing off-road race cars, roll and polar inertia requirements differ by track type:

- **Smooth tracks**: Lower roll and polar inertia improve agility and maneuverability
- **Rough tracks**: Higher inertias improve stability and control at speed

The optimal balance depends on track conditions and driver preferences. Finding a compromise requires iterative on-track testing with variations in suspension, body shape, and other components.
