Meals on Wheels America (www.mealsonwheelsamerica.org) offers multiple volunteer programs including meal delivery, kitchen support, administrative roles, fundraising/events, and client visitor roles. Event types include charity walks/runs, golf tournaments, food festivals, wine and dine events, bake sales, restaurant partnerships, and online fundraising campaigns. Volunteer roles for fundraising and events include event planning committee members, sponsorship outreach, marketing/promotion, volunteer recruitment, day-of-event support, photography/videography, social media ambassadors, auction coordinators, and registration/check-in roles. Most programs welcome volunteers 18+ with flexible scheduling.
