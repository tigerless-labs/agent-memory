## Methods for organizing YouTube subscriptions:

1. **Create custom playlists** — Organize videos by topic (Science, History, Programming) and add videos from favorite channels to these playlists for centralized access.

2. **Use YouTube's subscription management** — Go to Subscriptions tab → three dots → Manage → Sort by category to organize subscriptions into groups like Education, Entertainment, etc.

3. **Create a "Watch Later" playlist** — Add videos you want to watch but don't have time for immediately to track them for later viewing.

4. **Prioritize subscriptions** — Move favorite educational channels to the top of subscription list so their videos appear first in the feed.

5. **Use YouTube's "Topic" feature** — Subscribe to specific topics (Machine Learning, Astronomy, etc.) to discover new videos across channels.

6. **Third-party tools:**
   - Video Manager (browser extension for categorization)
   - TubeBuddy (subscription management and filtering)
   - SubscribeStar (organize into categories with filtering)

7. **Regular cleanup** — Periodically review and remove channels that no longer interest you or post infrequently to keep subscription feed clean and manageable.
