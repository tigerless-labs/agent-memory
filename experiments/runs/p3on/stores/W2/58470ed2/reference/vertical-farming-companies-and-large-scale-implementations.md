## Vertical Farm Examples

**Spread (Tokyo, Japan)**
- World's largest vertical farm
- Produces 10,000 heads of lettuce per day
- Facility size: 25,000 square feet

**Sky Greens (Singapore)**
- 9-meter tall vertical farm
- Produces 1 ton of vegetables per day

**Bowery Farming (New York City, USA)**
- 50,000 square foot vertical farm
- Uses LED lights and hydroponic technology
- Produces leafy greens and herbs year-round
- Claims 95% less water than traditional farming

## Other Cities with Vertical Farms
Amsterdam, South Korea, London, plus smaller-scale implementations in restaurants, grocery stores, and homes
