Extensive demon entries: Abaddon, Abigor, Astaroth, Asmodeus, Azazel, Bael, Baphomet, Barbatos, Beelzebub, Belial, Belphegor, Lilith, Lucifer, Moloch, Satan, Sariel, and 30+ others. Also covers demons in Christianity, Islam, Judaism. Authoritative reference for occult and paranormal studies.
