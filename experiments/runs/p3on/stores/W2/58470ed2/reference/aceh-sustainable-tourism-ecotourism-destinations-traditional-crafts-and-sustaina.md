Key destinations: Gayo Highlands (trekking), Sabang beaches, Leuser Ecosystem (jungle trekking, bird watching, wildlife). Transportation via Sultan Iskandar Muda International Airport (Banda Aceh) and Meulaboh Airport. Traditional crafts include woven textiles, woodcarving, silverwork promoted via social media. Aceh Tourism Board promotes responsible tourism, community participation, environmental protection. Ecotourism model emphasizes low-impact tourism with wildlife conservation and cultural preservation. Sustainability initiatives include waste management, water conservation, and prevention of cultural commodification.
