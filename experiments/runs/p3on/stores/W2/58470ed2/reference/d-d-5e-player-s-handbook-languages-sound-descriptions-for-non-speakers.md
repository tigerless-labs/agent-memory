Standard Languages: Common (familiar), Dwarvish (harsh/guttural, German/Nordic), Elvish (flowing/melodic, Romance/Welsh), Giant (slow/heavy, Old Norse/English), Gnomish (lively/quick, Dutch/Irish), Goblin (crude/sharp, Slavic with staccato), Halfling (warm/friendly, English with lilting tone), Orc (brutal/forceful, aggressive Germanic), Draconic (ancient/powerful, Latin/Greek with guttural hissing), Abyssal (dark/twisted, Semitic with sinister tone), Celestial (lofty/harmonious, melodic Latin/Greek), Infernal (precise/menacing, cold Latin/Greek), Sylvan (whimsical/enchanting, Gaelic/Irish lilting), Undercommon (secretive/muffled). Exotic Languages: Aarakocra (melodic bird-like with whistles/trills), Auran (breathy/airy wind whispers), Aquan (fluid/flowing watery sounds), Ignan (crackling/fiery with hissing), Terran (deep/rumbling earth sounds), Deep Speech (alien/unsettling clicks/pops), Primordial (ancient elemental mix), Thieves' Cant (coded Common with double meanings), Druidic (natural/earthy forest sounds).
