Learning resources for Afro-Cuban rhythms and Latin dance:\n\nYouTube Channels:\n- Salsa with Silvia (tutorials and challenges)\n- Afro-Cuban Dance Academy (rumba, son, folkloric dance)\n- Cuban Salsa Academy (Cuban salsa, rumba)\n- DanceWithMe (various styles including salsa, bachata, Afro-Cuban)\n- Joanna's Dance Studio (salsa, bachata, Afro-Cuban)\n\nOnline Platforms:\n- MasterClass (world-renowned instructors)\n- DanceClass (classes and tutorials)\n- SalsaAddiction (community, lessons, reviews)\n- Afro-Cuban Rhythms website (lessons and resources)\n- Latin Dance Academy (various Latin styles)\n\nApps:\n- Just Dance Now\n- Dance!\n\nRecommendation: Find qualified instructors for feedback on technique.
