Organizations that work on both social isolation and food insecurity:\n- Meals on Wheels America: meal delivery + social interaction for seniors\n- Feeding America: hunger-relief with social services, counseling, case management\n- Senior Corps: volunteer opportunities for seniors + meal delivery services\n- Little Brothers - Friends of the Elderly: companionship + food delivery for seniors\n- Catholic Charities USA: food assistance + social services (counseling, case management)\n- The Salvation Army: food assistance + housing support + social services\n\nLocal non-profits and faith-based organizations (like churches) also often combine meal provision with companionship and support services.
