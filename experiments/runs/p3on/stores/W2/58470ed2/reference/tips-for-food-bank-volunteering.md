**Tips for making the most of food bank volunteering:**\n- Be prepared to work: lifting, bending, and moving around\n- Wear comfortable, suitable clothing and shoes\n- Arrive 10-15 minutes early for orientation\n- Be flexible and adaptable to changing needs\n- Follow all instructions, guidelines, procedures, and safety protocols\n- Work as a team; communicate effectively\n- Treat clients with respect, kindness, and dignity\n- Follow food safety and handling procedures\n- Take initiative and ask questions when unsure\n- Be patient and understanding with clients, staff, and volunteers\n- Learn about food insecurity in your community\n- Provide feedback after volunteering to help improve the program
