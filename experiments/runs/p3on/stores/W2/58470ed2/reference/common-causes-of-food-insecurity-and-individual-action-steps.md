**Common Causes of Food Insecurity:**\n- Poverty and limited financial resources\n- Unemployment or underemployment\n- Food deserts (limited access to grocery stores/healthy food)\n- Lack of access to healthcare\n- Food waste and supply chain inefficiencies\n- Climate change and natural disasters\n- Social isolation and lack of support\n- Systemic and structural barriers (discrimination, racism)\n\n**Individual Action Steps:**\n- Volunteer at food banks or pantries\n- Donate food and funds\n- Advocate for policy change\n- Educate self and others\n- Support local food initiatives (community gardens, farmers markets, food co-ops)\n- Mentor or befriend someone in need\n- Reduce personal food waste\n- Participate in community events/food drives\n- Support organizations addressing food insecurity\n- Listen and amplify marginalized voices
