Must-see: Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (free observation deck), Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (mix of Japanese, French, English landscaping), Shinjuku District (neon streets, Kabukicho, Golden Gai).\n\nHidden gems: Omoide Yokocho (tiny alley with bars and shops, old-school Tokyo), Nonbei Yokocho (unique alley atmosphere), Shinjuku Ni-chome (Tokyo's largest LGBTQ+ district), Hanazono Shrine (peaceful Shinto shrine).\n\nFood and drink: Shinjuku's Oden Alley (fish cake vendors, street food), T's Restaurant (tonkatsu and comfort food), Golden Gai's Tachinomiya (standing bar), depachika (department store food floors).\n\nTip: Get a Suica or Pasmo card for public transport. Expect crowds at Shinjuku Station and Kabukicho.
