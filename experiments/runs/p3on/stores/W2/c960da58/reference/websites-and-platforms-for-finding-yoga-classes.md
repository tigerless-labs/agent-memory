**Search methods:**\n- Google: search 'yoga studios near me' or 'beginner yoga classes near me'\n- Yoga-specific directories: YogaFinder, Yoga International, DoYouYoga\n- Social media: Facebook and Instagram business pages for local studios\n- Local gyms: often offer yoga classes as part of group fitness programs\n\n**What to check:** Schedule, prices, class levels, studio philosophy, teaching style, amenities, and reviews.
