Fushimi Inari Shrine: famous for thousands of vermilion torii gates forming tunnels up the mountain.\n\nKinkaku-ji Temple (Golden Pavilion): stunning gold-leaf covered temple with beautiful gardens.\n\nArashiyama Bamboo Grove: serene area with towering bamboo stalks.\n\nKiyomizu-dera Temple: UNESCO World Heritage site, one of Kyoto's most famous temples with panoramic city views.\n\nGion District: traditional quarter famous for geisha culture and historic Japanese architecture.\n\nHidden gems: Philosopher's Path (scenic cherry blossom-lined walk), Nijo Castle (with nightingale floors), Sanjusangendo Temple (1,001 Kannon statues), Kyoto Imperial Palace, Ginkaku-ji Temple (Silver Pavilion).
