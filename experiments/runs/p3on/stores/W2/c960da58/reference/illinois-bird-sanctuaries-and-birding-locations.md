**Illinois Audubon Society Sanctuaries:**
- Warbler Ridge Sanctuary (Alton)
- Prairie Ridge Sanctuary (Galesburg)
- Montelle Winery Sanctuary (Staunton)

**Illinois Department of Natural Resources:**
- Champaign County Forest Preserve
- Emiquon National Wildlife Refuge
- Hennepin & Hopper Lakes State Fish and Wildlife Area

**Other Locations:**
- Shawnee National Forest (southern Illinois, 280+ species documented)
- Morton Arboretum
- Chicago Botanic Garden
- Brookfield Zoo
- Starved Rock State Park (good for Osprey sightings)
- Lake Michigan (good for Osprey sightings)
- Illinois River (good for Osprey sightings)
