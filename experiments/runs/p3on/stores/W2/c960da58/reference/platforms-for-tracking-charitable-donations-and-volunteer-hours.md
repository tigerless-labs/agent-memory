**Donation and volunteer tracking platforms:**\n- GivingGrid: track donations, volunteer hours, fundraising efforts; discover new charities\n- DonorPerfect: donor management software with reminders and notifications\n- Network for Good: donate to multiple charities, track donations, get receipts\n- VolunteerMatch: connects with nonprofits, track volunteer hours, earn badges\n- Bright Funds: single platform for multiple charities, track donations, earn rewards\n- Charity Navigator's Giving Basket: track donations, set recurring gifts, get receipts\n\n**Features to look for:** Centralized tracking, tax deduction receipts, recurring gift options, impact visualization.
