Must-see: Osaka Castle (majestic, great city views), Dotonbori (entertainment district with neon lights and street food), Universal Studios Japan (theme park with Harry Potter and Mario areas), Umeda Sky Building (observation deck with city views).\n\nHidden gems: Osaka Museum of History (rooftop garden with city views), Kuromon Ichiba Market (lively marketplace with fresh seafood and local snacks), Spa World (onsen theme park), Amerikamura (trendy shopping district).\n\nFood: takoyaki (octopus dumplings), okonomiyaki (savory pancakes), kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers).
