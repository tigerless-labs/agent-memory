Top sushi spots in Tokyo. Sukiyabashi Jiro (3 Michelin, Ginza) offers traditional Edomae-style omakase. Sushi Saito (3 Michelin, Roppongi) known for exquisite pieces and intimate atmosphere. Tsukiji Fish Market's Sushi Dai offers fresh sushi at reasonable prices (inside the market).
