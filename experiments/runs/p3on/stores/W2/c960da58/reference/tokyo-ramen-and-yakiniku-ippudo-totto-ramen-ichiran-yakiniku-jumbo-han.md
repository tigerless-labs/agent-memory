Ramen: Ippudo serves popular tonkotsu (pork bone) broth, multiple locations. Totto Ramen (Meguro) specializes in Hakata-style ramen. Ichiran (Shibuya) offers high-tech ramen experience with customizable toppings.\n\nYakiniku: Han no Daidokoro Bettei (Ginza) is upscale with premium meats. Yakiniku Jumbo Han (Shinjuku) is popular with lively atmosphere and wide variety of meats.
