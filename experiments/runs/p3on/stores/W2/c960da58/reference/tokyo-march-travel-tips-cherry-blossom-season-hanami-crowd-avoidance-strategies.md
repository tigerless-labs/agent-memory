March is cherry blossom season in Tokyo — beautiful but crowded. To avoid crowds: visit weekdays during morning (before 9am) or late afternoon, take lunchtime breaks (12-1pm), visit popular areas on weekday evenings.\n\nSpecific strategies: Tokyo Skytree (early morning or evening), Tsukiji Fish Market (before 7am for tuna auction, or later), Meiji Shrine (morning or late afternoon), Asakusa Senso-ji Temple (morning or late afternoon), Shibuya Crossing (morning or late evening).\n\nPlan ahead: book tickets and make reservations in advance, use public transportation, take breaks regularly.
