**Spring migration (April-May):**
- Warblers, tanagers, and orioles abundant
- Yellow Warbler, American Goldfinch, Baltimore Oriole

**Summer (June-August):**
- Breeding season with birds raising young
- American Robin, Red-winged Blackbird, Common Grackle
- Good for spotting birds of prey (hawks, eagles)

**Fall migration (September-October):**
- Species moving south for winter
- Ruby-throated Hummingbird, Broad-winged Hawk, Nashville Warbler
- Good for waterfowl (ducks, geese)

**Winter (December-February):**
- Good for off-season species: Snow Bunting, American Tree Sparrow
- Birds attracted to feeders: Dark-eyed Junco, American Goldfinch

**Osprey Season:** Late March/early April through September/October; look near large bodies of water (lakes, rivers)
