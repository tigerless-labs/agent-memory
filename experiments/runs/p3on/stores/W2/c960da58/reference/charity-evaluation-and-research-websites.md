**Charity rating sites:**\n- Charity Navigator (charitynavigator.org)\n- GuideStar (guidestar.org)\n- BBB Wise Giving Alliance (give.org)\n- GreatNonprofits (greatnonprofits.org)\n- Charity Watch (charitywatch.org)\n\n**Government verification:**\n- IRS website (irs.gov): verify 501(c)(3) status\n\n**Research steps:**\n- Review financial reports (Form 990)\n- Check leadership and governance\n- Understand programs and impact\n- Read online reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)\n- Contact charity directly with questions\n- Check for accreditations (BBB Wise Giving Alliance, Independent Charities of America)
