Major tour operators: Intrepid Travel and G Adventures offer structured group tours with experienced guides. Both have pros (convenience, meeting travelers) and cons (limited flexibility, potentially expensive).\n\nAlternatives: JNTO (Japan National Tourism Organization), Japan Guide Agency (JGA), Japan Experience, Inside Japan Tours, Japan Unmasked, Rising Sun Tours. Local operators offer more personalized experiences. Private guides available through Japan-Guide.com, Tokyo Private Guide, Kyoto Private Guide.
