**Earthenware:**\n- Strengths: soft and pliable (beginner-friendly), low firing temperature (~1800°F/980°C), wide range of glaze options\n- Weaknesses: brittle and prone to breakage, not suitable for functional ware, lower durability\n\n**Air-Dry Clay:**\n- Strengths: no kiln required, fast results, easy to repair if re-moistened\n- Weaknesses: limited durability, prone to cracking, not for outdoor use, best for small/simple projects\n\n**Stoneware:**\n- Strengths: very strong and durable, high-temperature firing (~2400°F/1300°C), food-safe and watertight, suitable for functional ware and outdoor use\n- Weaknesses: denser and less pliable (harder for beginners), requires high-temperature kiln access, limited glaze options (must withstand high temps)\n\nCurrently working with earthenware but interested in trying air-dry and stoneware.
