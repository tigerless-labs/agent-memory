**Bags:** Reusable, durable bags from recycled polyester or canvas; packable lightweight daypacks. **Hydration/Food:** Refillable water bottles (BPA-free, durable), beeswax wraps (replace plastic wrap), travel-sized toiletries in refillable containers, solid shampoo/toothpaste bars. **Power:** Rechargeable power banks, solar-powered chargers. **Textiles:** Merino wool clothing (sustainable, breathable, moisture-wicking), quick-drying fabrics (nylon, polyester, Tencel), second-hand or sustainable fashion brands. **Miscellaneous:** Lightweight quick-drying cloth towels, reusable straws (bamboo, stainless steel, glass), eco-friendly laundry sheets, mineral-based reef-safe sunscreen, travel journals. **Certifications:** Bluesign (sustainable textile), GOTS (organic textiles), OEKO-TEX (textile safety/sustainability).
