When personalizing a cheese knife with engraving:\n\n**Wood types for handles:**\n- Maple: light, fine grain, scratch/stain resistant\n- Walnut: dark and rich, distinctive grain, durable\n- Cherry: reddish-brown, fine grain, resists warping\n- Olive wood: exotic Mediterranean flair, very durable\n\n**Engraving font styles:** Script (elegant), Block (bold/modern), Serif (classic), Sans-Serif (clean)\n\n**Font sizes:** Small (3-4mm for initials), Medium (6-8mm for most names), Large (12-15mm for prominence)\n\n**Vendors for customized items:** Etsy, Zazzle, Personalization Mall, Mark and Graham, UncommonGoods\n\nConsider timeframe, budget, vendor personalization options, and material quality when ordering.
