**Hotel Sustainability Programs:** IHG Green Engage, Marriott Sustainability (energy/water reduction, waste), Hilton Travel with Purpose (environmental and social responsibility). **Eco-Lodge Platforms:** National Geographic Unique Lodges of the World, EcoLodges.com, The International Ecotourism Society, Glamping.com, Ecobnb. **Major Booking Sites with Filters:** Booking.com (Sustainable Travel filter), Expedia (Sustainable Tourism), Airbnb (Eco-Friendly Stays). **Sustainability Certifications:** LEED (green building standards), EarthCheck (environmental/social/economic assessment), Ecotourism Kenya, Bluesign (textile production), GOTS (organic textiles), OEKO-TEX (textile safety/sustainability).
