Explored for weekend day hike (May 2023). Options considered:\n\n- **Tilden Regional Park, Berkeley Hills**: Nimitz Way Trail (~4.5 mi out-and-back, 1,000 ft gain, 3-4 hrs) and Wildcat Gorge Trail (~5.5 mi out-and-back, 1,200 ft gain, 4-5 hrs)\n- **Muir Woods**: Dipsea Trail with redwoods\n- **Mount Diablo State Park**: 3,849 ft elevation, 360-degree views, Summit Trail or Falls Trail (4-10 miles)\n- **Sam McDonald County Park**: 4.5-mile Heritage Grove Trail through redwoods\n- **Purisima Creek Redwoods**: 4.5-6.5 mile options\n- **Mission Peak Regional Preserve**: 6.5-mile out-and-back, challenging\n\nUser chose Tilden Regional Park.
