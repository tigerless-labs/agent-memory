**Carbon Offset Programs:** Qantas (Fly Carbon Neutral), Air New Zealand (FlyNeutral), KLM (CO2ZERO), Lufthansa (Compensaid), SAS (Carbon Offset), Virgin Atlantic (Carbon Offset), United Airlines (Carbon Offset). **Sustainable Practices Leaders:** Norwegian Air (fuel-efficient aircraft, reduced plastics), TAP Air Portugal (sustainability strategy, biofuels), Finnair (50% emissions reduction by 2025, 10% sustainable fuel by 2025), Singapore Airlines (fuel efficiency, sustainable fuels). **Emerging:** Electric/hybrid-electric aircraft development, sustainable aviation fuels reducing emissions up to 80%, single-engine taxiing to reduce ground fuel use.
