**Wildlife in Hague, Virginia kayaking areas**\n\nBirds: bald eagles, ospreys, blue herons, egrets, waterfowl\n\nMammals: river otters, muskrats, beavers, deer, foxes, raccoons\n\nAquatic: turtles, striped bass, largemouth bass, bluegill, catfish\n\n**Best viewing times**: early morning and late afternoon/early evening (most active wildlife periods, less wind, less boat traffic)
