"Cloud Gate" is an iconic stainless steel sculpture by Anish Kapoor, installed in Millennium Park, Chicago in 2004. Also known as "The Bean." Made of 168 polished stainless steel panels. Dimensions: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long. Weighs over 110 tons. Famous for its mirror-like reflective surface that distorts and warps the surrounding environment. Interactive piece—visitors can walk around, touch, and photograph themselves in the reflections. The concave shape creates an intimate viewing experience. Surface is cleaned daily. One of Chicago's most photographed landmarks with over 10,000 photos taken daily.
