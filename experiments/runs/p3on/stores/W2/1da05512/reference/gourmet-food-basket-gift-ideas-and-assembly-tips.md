Gourmet food basket categories for gifts:\n\n**Savory:** artisanal cheeses (truffle gouda, brie, goat), cured meats (prosciutto, salami), artisanal crackers, gourmet nuts\n\n**Sweet:** gourmet chocolates/truffles, dried fruits (apricots, cranberries, dates), cookies/biscotti\n\n**Spreads:** artisanal jams (fig, quince, strawberry-balsamic), honey, maple syrup, chutneys/tapenades\n\n**Beverages:** specialty tea, gourmet coffee beans, wine/champagne\n\n**Extras:** fresh herbs, gourmet olives, antipasto\n\n**Assembly tips:**\n- Consider recipient's dietary restrictions\n- Source from local/small-batch producers for uniqueness\n- Add personal handwritten note or gift card\n- Pair with customized items (personalized cutting board, monogrammed cheese knife, engraved wine stopper)\n- Theme around specific cuisine (Italian, French, Spanish) if desired\n- Focus on visual appeal and complementary flavors
