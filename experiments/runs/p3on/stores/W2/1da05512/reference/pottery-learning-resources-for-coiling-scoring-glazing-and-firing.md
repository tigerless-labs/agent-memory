**Online Courses & Websites:**\n- Craftsy (pottery courses)\n- Pottery Making Illustrated (tutorials, articles, videos on pottery techniques)\n- The Potter's Council (tutorials on pottery techniques)\n- Ceramic Arts Network (tutorials, articles, videos, online courses on ceramics)\n\n**YouTube Channels:**\n- Simon Leach Pottery (pottery tutorials including coiling techniques)\n- Pottery By Osa (tutorials on coiling, scoring, and other techniques)\n- The Ceramic School (pottery tutorials taught by experienced ceramic artists)\n\n**Recommended Books:**\n- "The Potter's Bible" by Marylin Scott (comprehensive guide to ceramics including glazing and firing)\n- "Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter" by Ian Gregory (detailed guide to glaze formulation and application)\n\n**Best Practices:**\nKeep a glaze journal to record tests and results. Test glazes on small non-functional pieces first. Join ceramic communities online or in-person for learning and feedback.
