## Major Festivals
- **Timkat** (January 19): Marks baptism of Jesus with processions, music, dancing, water splashing
- **Meskel** (September 27): Commemorates finding of the True Cross with bonfires, feasting, traditional dancing
- **Enkutatash** (September 11): Ethiopian New Year with feasting, dancing, gift exchange
- **Fasika** (April): Ethiopian Orthodox Easter with fasting, prayer, colorful processions, feasting

## Must-Try Dishes
- **Injera**: Sourdough flatbread used as utensil to scoop stews
- **Doro Wot**: Spicy chicken stew with berbere spice (chili, ginger, coriander)
- **Tibs**: Spicy sautéed beef or lamb with onions and peppers
- **Kitfo**: Raw beef seasoned with spices and chili powder
- **Shiro**: Thick stew made from ground chickpeas or lentils
- **Coffee Ceremony**: Traditional roasting, grinding, and brewing ritual (Ethiopia is famous for coffee)

## Cultural Sites
- Rock-hewn churches of Lalibela
- Castles of Gondar
- Blue Nile Falls
- Markets in Addis Ababa
