**Public Transit:** Buses, trains, subways with planning apps like Moovit, Transit. **Human-Powered:** Walking (zero-emission), cycling and bike-sharing systems, e-bike rentals. **Taxi Alternatives:** Green Taxi, Electric Cab (electric/hybrid fleets in some cities). **Car Sharing:** Zipcar, Car2Go, Hertz (offer eco-friendly/hybrid options). **Airport/City Shuttles:** Shared shuttle services (more efficient than individual rides). **Micro-Mobility:** Bird, Lime, Spin (electric scooters and skateboards for short distances). **Ride-Sharing:** Waze Carpool, Carpool Buddy, BlaBlaCar (connect drivers with shared routes), Via, Juno, Gett (electric/hybrid ride-hailing in select cities). **Carbon Offset:** If using traditional services, offset through Carbon Offset or TerraPass platforms.
