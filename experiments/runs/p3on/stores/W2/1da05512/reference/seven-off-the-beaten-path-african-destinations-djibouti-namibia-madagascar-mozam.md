Recommended off-the-beaten-path African destinations for exploration:

- **Djibouti**: Otherworldly landscapes with volcanic craters, salt flats, stunning beaches
- **Namibia**: Vast deserts, sand dunes, unique wildlife (desert-adapted elephants and lions)
- **Madagascar**: Island nation off southeast Africa, diverse flora and fauna including lemurs, baobab trees, spiny forests
- **Mozambique**: Coastal country with pristine beaches, tropical islands (Bazaruto), Ilha de Mozambique with African, Arab, and Portuguese heritage
- **Ethiopia**: 3,000-year-old history, rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, castles of Gondar, Blue Nile Falls
- **Mauritania**: One of world's least populated and most unchanged landscapes, sweeping dunes, desert oases, nomadic tribes
- **Senegal**: West African country with vibrant cultural heritage, music scene in Dakar, Djoudj National Bird Sanctuary, pink lake of Retba
