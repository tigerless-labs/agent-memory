Decided on Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route for September 2023 backpacking trip in Sierra Nevada. Route details: ~20 miles, 3,000 ft elevation gain, moderate difficulty. Chose this over High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft gain) after reassessing abilities. Access: 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows or shuttle bus from Tuolumne Meadows Visitor Center. Requires reservations through Yosemite Conservancy (book early). Camp at 10,300 ft elevation with tent cabins, full meals, and modern amenities.
