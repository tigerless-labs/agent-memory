Scene concept: Girl about to sit down at computer, then slowly disappears into the computer (right before or as she speaks into the microphone). Recommended visual effects: distortion/glitch effects on computer interface, flickering lights and static noise, flashes of light or swirling motion, quick cuts for jarring effect, cut to computer interface shots after disappearance. Audio: low hum, distant whisper, haunting melody, discordant sounds, white noise. Camera: slow deliberate movements to build tension, zoom on face/hands before disappearance, disorienting camera movements. Narrative twist: person trapped in digital realm, computer itself is alive/malevolent, darker side of technology revealed. Goal: create unease and tension leaving viewer unsettled and curious.
