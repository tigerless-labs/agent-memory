Wants to transition from using pre-mixed glazes to creating custom glaze recipes from scratch. Goals include: learning glaze formulation and material selection, achieving specific colors and effects (blues, reds, matte finishes), mastering application techniques (brushing, pouring, spraying), and ensuring consistent food-safe finishes on earthenware pieces.
