## For dogs (Max):

**Flea & Tick Prevention:**
- Frontline Gold (topical)
- Advantix II (topical, repels biting flies/mosquitoes)
- K9 Advantix (topical)
- Activyl (fast-acting topical)
- Nexgard (oral chewable)
- Comfortis (oral chewable)
- Trifexis (combination, covers heartworms and parasites)
- Seresto collar (8 months protection)

**Sensitive Skin Shampoos:**
- Earthbath All-Natural Oatmeal & Aloe Shampoo
- Nature's Specialties Oatmeal Dog Shampoo
- Aloe vera Shampoo by Nature's Gate
- Aloe Vera Dog Shampoo by Earthbath
- Epi-Soothe Sensitive Skin Shampoo by Virbac
- Douxo Sensitive Skin Shampoo
- Royal Canin Gentle Skin Shampoo
- Nature's Gate Coconut Oil Dog Shampoo
- Earthbath Coconut Oil Shampoo
- Nature's Gate Shikakai Dog Shampoo

## For rabbits (Benny):

**Toys:**
- Untreated wood toys (apple, willow, birch)
- Hay balls
- Cardboard rolls
- Tunnels and tents
- Food puzzle toys
- Bell toys

**Brands:** Kaytee, Oxbow, Pet Zone, Small Pet Select, Ware Manufacturing

**Hideaways:**
- Kaytee Rabbit Hideaway (removable roof)
- Oxbow Rabbit Hut (ventilated, sloping roof)
- Ware Manufacturing Rabbit Tunnel (collapsible, multiple entrances)
