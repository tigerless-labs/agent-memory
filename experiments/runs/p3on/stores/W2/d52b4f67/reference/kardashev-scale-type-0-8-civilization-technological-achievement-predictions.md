Type 0.8 civilization harnesses ~80% of available planetary energy.

**Energy**: Efficient solar panels, wind turbines, advanced energy storage, fusion power, geothermal power, biofuels

**Transportation**: Advanced electric vehicles with longer range/faster charging, high-speed trains (300+ mph), magnetic levitation systems

**Information Technology**: AI that learns from humans, sophisticated VR systems, advanced quantum computing

**Biotechnology**: Gene editing to cure genetic diseases and enhance human performance, new drugs and therapies for wider disease range

**Space Exploration**: Permanent moon settlements, Mars terraforming, propulsion technologies including possible warp drives or wormholes

**Environmental Science**: Carbon dioxide capture and storage, geoengineering (atmospheric seeding with reflective particles, artificial clouds)
