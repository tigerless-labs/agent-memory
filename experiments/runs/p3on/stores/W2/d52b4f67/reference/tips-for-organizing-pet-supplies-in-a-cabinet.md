## Organization Steps:

1. **Purge and Categorize**: Sort into categories - food/treats, toys/chews, grooming supplies, medications/supplements, flea and tick prevention, miscellaneous (collars, leashes)

2. **Assign a Home**: Give each category a specific shelf or section

3. **Use Storage Containers**: Use bins, baskets, or containers for small items; label each

4. **Maximize Vertical Space**: Use stackable shelves or baskets

5. **Keep Frequently Used Items Accessible**: Store on top shelf or in convenient front location

6. **Label and Sign**: Label each shelf/section and add sign to cabinet door

7. **Keep it Clean**: Monthly tidying to remove expired or unused items

## Additional Tips:

- Use turntable/lazy Susan for back-of-cabinet access
- Store heavy items (pet food) on bottom shelf
- Keep donation bin for items to give to shelters
