The constitution needs to address seven key areas:

1. **Purpose & objectives** — the group's mission and activities
2. **Membership criteria** — who can join, rights and obligations
3. **Governance structure** — decision-making bodies, roles, responsibilities
4. **Meetings & decision-making** — frequency, conduct, voting procedures
5. **Finances** — income sources (e.g., membership fees), fund management and spending rules
6. **Amendments** — how the constitution can be changed
7. **Legal compliance** — ensure alignment with UK legal requirements for community groups

This represents the framework needed to create a clear, effective, and legally compliant constitution.
