**Will Harris** owns White Oak Pastures, a 3,200-acre farm in Georgia that produces grass-fed beef, lamb, and poultry using regenerative practices. Harris transitioned to regenerative farming in the 1990s and has since seen increases in soil fertility, biodiversity, and animal health. White Oak Pastures is a model for regenerative agriculture in the United States and has won numerous awards for environmental and social impact.
