Admission Requirements: No minimum GPA requirement; middle 80% typically 3.2-3.8. Average work experience 5-7 years (range 2-12). Average GMAT score ~710. Requires GMAT/GRE, essays, letters of recommendation, resume, and interview (by invitation). Program values leadership potential, teamwork experience, and strong work ethic.

Curriculum: 20.5 total credits with core and elective courses. Marketing Major requires 5-6 courses in marketing. Marketing courses include Marketing Strategy, Brand Management, Consumer Behavior, Digital Marketing, and Marketing Analytics. Other majors available: Finance, Strategy, Operations, Entrepreneurship, Management and Organizations.

Program Focus: More extensive range of marketing courses than peer programs with focus on both B2B and B2C marketing.
