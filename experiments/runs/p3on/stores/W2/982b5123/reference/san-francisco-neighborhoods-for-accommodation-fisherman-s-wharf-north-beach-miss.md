**Fisherman's Wharf** — Touristy, waterfront, Alcatraz access, Pier 39

**North Beach** — Italian-American culture, dining, walkable to Fisherman's Wharf

**Mission District** — Street art, trendy bars, Mexican food, younger crowd, transit accessible

**Pacific Heights** — Upscale, Bay views, Victorian homes, pricier, laid-back

**Hayes Valley** — Boutique shopping, galleries, hip restaurants, near City Hall

**The Castro** — LGBTQ+ community, Victorian homes, nightlife, character-rich

**Russian Hill** — Upscale, Bay Bridge/Alcatraz views, quieter, near Fisherman's Wharf

**Tip:** Book in advance, especially June-August peak season
