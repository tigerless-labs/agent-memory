When shopping at Walmart, these cashback and loyalty programs can help maximize savings:

- **Ibotta** — Cashback app that partners with Walmart for rewards on purchases
- **Fetch Rewards** — Cashback app that earns points on Walmart purchases (redeemable for cash or rewards)
- **Walmart+** — Paid membership program offering unlimited free delivery, fuel discounts, mobile checkout, and exclusive offers

Apps can often be stacked with digital coupons and store promotions for additional savings.
