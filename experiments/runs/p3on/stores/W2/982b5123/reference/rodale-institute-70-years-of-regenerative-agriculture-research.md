Nonprofit research organization conducting research on regenerative farming practices and providing education and training to farmers. Their research has shown that regenerative farming practices can improve soil health, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and increase crop yields. Has helped many farmers transition to organic and regenerative practices, and demonstrated that these approaches can be more profitable long-term.
