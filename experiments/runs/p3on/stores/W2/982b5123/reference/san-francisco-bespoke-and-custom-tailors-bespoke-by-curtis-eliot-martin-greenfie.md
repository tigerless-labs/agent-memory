- **Bespoke by Curtis Eliot** — Financial District, modern bespoke, high-quality fabrics

- **The Tailor's Keep** — Mission District, bespoke and alterations, variety of fabrics
- **Al's Attire** — Haight-Ashbury, bespoke/custom with eco-friendly focus, locally sourced
