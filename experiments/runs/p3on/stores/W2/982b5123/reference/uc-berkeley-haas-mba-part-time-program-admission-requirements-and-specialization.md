Admission Requirements: No minimum GPA requirement; middle 80% typically 3.3-3.7. Average work experience 5-7 years (range 2-10). Average GMAT score ~690. Requires GMAT/GRE, essays, letters of recommendation, resume, and interview (by invitation). Program values strong work history, leadership skills, and clear career path.

Curriculum: 42 total credits with core and elective courses. Marketing Concentration requires 12-15 credits. Marketing courses include Marketing Strategy, Brand Management, Consumer Behavior, Digital Marketing, and Marketing Research. Other concentrations available: Finance, Strategy, Operations, Entrepreneurship, Sustainability.

Program Strengths: Strong reputation for entrepreneurship and sustainability programs; may be good fit if interested in social impact or startup marketing.
