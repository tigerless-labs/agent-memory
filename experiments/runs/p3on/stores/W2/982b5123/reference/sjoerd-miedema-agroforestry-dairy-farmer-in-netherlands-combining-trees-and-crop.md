Dairy farmer in the Netherlands who has transitioned to agroforestry practices, combining trees and crops on the same farmland. By using this form of sustainable agriculture, he can now produce more crops and trees on the same amount of land than when he only had cows. This practice also helps retain more water in the soil, which saves costs on irrigation.
