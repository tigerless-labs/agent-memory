Admission Requirements: No minimum GPA requirement; middle 80% typically 3.2-3.8. Average work experience 5-7 years (range 2-15). Average GMAT score ~680. Requires GMAT/GRE, essays, letters of recommendation, resume, and interview (by invitation). Program values progressive work experience, leadership skills, and clear career path.

Curriculum: 57 total credits with core and elective courses. Marketing Concentration requires 12-15 credits. Marketing courses include Brand Management, Marketing Strategy, Consumer Behavior, Digital Marketing, and Marketing Research. Other specializations available: Finance, Strategy, Operations, Entrepreneurship, Sustainability.

Experiential Learning: Stronger focus on experiential learning than peer programs. Michigan Ross Consulting Club (MRCC) provides pro-bono consulting with major companies. MAP (Multidisciplinary Action Projects) offers 7-week consulting projects. Ross Marketing Consulting Program (RMCP) matches students with semester-long marketing projects from top companies. Industry-sponsored projects available. Strong alumni network with 47,000+ alumni worldwide.
