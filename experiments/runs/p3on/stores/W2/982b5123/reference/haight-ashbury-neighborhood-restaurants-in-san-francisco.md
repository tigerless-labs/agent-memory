Restaurant recommendations for Haight-Ashbury, San Francisco:\n\n1. **Zuni Cafe** — SF institution since 1979; famous brick oven roasted chicken, house-made bread, Mediterranean-inspired dishes\n2. **The Pork Store Cafe** — Casual breakfast/brunch spot; famous for 'Pork Store Sandwich' (eggs, bacon, cheddar on sourdough); serves breakfast all day; hours: M-F 8am-3pm, Sat-Sun 8am-4pm\n3. **Nopalito** — Organic, farm-to-table Mexican; tacos, burritos, grilled meats\n4. **The Alembic** — Gastropub with creative farm-to-table American cuisine, craft cocktails and beers\n5. **Magnolia Gastropub** — Craft beer spot; burgers, sandwiches, salads\n6. **The Pork Store BBQ** — Casual BBQ with in-house smoked ribs, brisket, sausages\n7. **Cafe Cole** — Cozy family-owned cafe; coffee, sandwiches, salads, baked goods
