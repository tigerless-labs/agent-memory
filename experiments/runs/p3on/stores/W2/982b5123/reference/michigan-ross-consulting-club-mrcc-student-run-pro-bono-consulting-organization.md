Overview: MRCC is a student-run consulting organization that provides pro-bono consulting services to top companies, non-profits, and startups. One of the largest and most prestigious consulting clubs in the country with 200+ members.

Project Types: Strategy Development (market analysis, competitive analysis, growth recommendations); Operational Improvement (process improvements, cost savings, efficiency gains); Marketing and Sales (marketing strategies, market research, sales growth); Organizational Development (organizational design, change management, leadership development); Innovation and Entrepreneurship (business plans, market research, fundraising guidance).

Client Base: Works with diverse companies ranging from startups to Fortune 500 firms. While they work with local Ann Arbor and Detroit companies, they also partner with companies nationwide and internationally. Notable clients include: Ford, GM, Fiat Chrysler, Google, Amazon, Microsoft, IBM, Henry Ford Health System, Michigan Medicine, Pfizer, Procter & Gamble, Kellogg's, Meijer, Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, Charles Schwab, and various startups in TechArb.

Industry Focus: Automotive/Mobility (strong due to Michigan presence); Healthcare and Biotech (leveraging University of Michigan reputation); Technology and Software; Sustainability and Energy (growing focus).

Client Matching: Clients pitch projects to MRCC; students indicate interest based on skills and interests; teams of 4-6 students with diverse expertise are formed; team meets with client for kickoff; project execution includes research, analysis, recommendations; final presentation to client.

Benefits: Practical hands-on experience; networking with top companies and peers; leadership development opportunities; resume building with tangible results; access to job opportunities and career advancement through MRCC network.
