## Platforms for artists\n- **Tumblr**: Tag posts with #art, #illustration, #painting; follow other artists; participate in artist communities\n- **Instagram**: Popular for sharing photos/videos; use hashtags; connect with other artists\n- **Behance**: Portfolio platform for creatives; showcase artwork; connect with professionals\n- **DeviantArt**: Online community for artists; share work; receive feedback; sell artwork\n- **Pinterest**: Visual search engine; curate collections; increase exposure\n- **ArtStation**: Portfolio platform for entertainment industry artists\n\n## Key strategies for visibility\n1. Consistency: post regularly, maintain consistent style\n2. Engage with audience: respond to comments, show interest in others' work\n3. Use hashtags: research relevant hashtags in your niche\n4. Collaborate: work with other artists to reach new audiences\n5. Experiment: try different formats, time-lapses, behind-the-scenes content\n6. Tailor content: customize approach for each platform\n7. High-quality visuals: use high-resolution images and attention-grabbing thumbnails\n8. Show personality: be authentic; share interests beyond just artwork\n\n## Handling criticism\n- Take time to cool off before responding\n- Respond professionally; avoid defensiveness\n- Distinguish trolls from constructive feedback\n- Don't take criticism personally\n- Seek support from artist communities
