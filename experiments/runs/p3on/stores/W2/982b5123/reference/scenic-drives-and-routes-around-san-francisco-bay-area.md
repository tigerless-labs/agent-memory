Recommended scenic drives around San Francisco Bay Area:\n\n1. **Pacific Coast Highway (Hwy 1)** — South from SF to Santa Cruz (~1.5 hours); stunning coastline, beaches, overlooks; stops: Half Moon Bay, Pescadero State Beach, Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk\n2. **17-Mile Drive** — Through Pebble Beach; coastal scenery, famous golf courses, Lone Cypress tree\n3. **Golden Gate Bridge to Muir Woods and Sausalito** — North from SF across Golden Gate Bridge; Muir Woods (~30 min, towering redwoods); Sausalito (~15 min, charming waterfront town with Bay views)\n4. **Napa Valley Wine Country** — East from SF (~1 hour); rolling hills, vineyards, charming towns (St. Helena, Calistoga); wine tours, gourmet dining\n5. **Skyline Boulevard (Hwy 35)** — South through Santa Cruz Mountains; breathtaking views of Bay Area, Pacific Ocean; stops: Wunderlich County Park, Thomas Fogarty Winery\n6. **Mount Tamalpais** — North from SF (~1 hour); Mount Tamalpais State Park with scenic summit views of Bay Area; hiking, biking available
