Prefers a multi-method approach to expense tracking: saves digital receipts from online retailers, reviews bank and credit card statements regularly, and uses budgeting apps (like Mint or YNAB) to categorize and track spending. Finds this effective for managing finances and making informed purchasing decisions, particularly for online shopping.
