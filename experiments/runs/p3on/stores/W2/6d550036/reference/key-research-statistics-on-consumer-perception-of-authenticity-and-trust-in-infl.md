**Stackla study:**
- 86% of consumers say authenticity is important when deciding what brands to support

**Influencer Marketing Hub survey:**
- 61% of consumers are more likely to trust influencer recommendations if they believe the influencer is authentic

**Edelman research:**
- 70% of consumers believe that authentic brands are more reliable
- 63% of consumers believe authentic brands are more trustworthy

**Implication:** Authenticity is a critical success factor in influencer marketing, with strong consumer preference for genuine influencers.
