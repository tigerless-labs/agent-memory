## Starting a Web Design Company

1. Gain necessary skills (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Adobe Creative Suite)
2. Develop a business plan
3. Create a portfolio
4. Establish pricing strategy
5. Register business
6. Build a team
7. Market services

## Setting Up a Web Design Company

1. Define business structure (sole proprietorship, LLC, corporation)
2. Develop business plan
3. Register business
4. Develop brand identity (logo, website, marketing materials)
5. Define services (design, development, maintenance, SEO, e-commerce)
6. Build team
7. Establish pricing strategy
8. Develop portfolio
9. Market services
