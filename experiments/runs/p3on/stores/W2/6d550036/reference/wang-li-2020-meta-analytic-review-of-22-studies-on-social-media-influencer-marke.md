**Full citation:** Wang, Y., & Li, M. (2020). The Effects of Social Media Influencer Marketing on Consumer Purchase Intention: A Meta-Analytic Review. *Journal of Interactive Marketing*, 49, 100269.

**Key findings:**
- Examines relationship between social media influencer marketing and consumer purchase intention
- Identifies significant moderators: influencer credibility, content quality, and social media platform type
- Provides quantitative synthesis of effects across 22 studies

**Relevance:** Addresses direct link between influencer characteristics and purchasing decisions.
