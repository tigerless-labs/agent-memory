**Full citation:** Kapoor, K. K., & Kumar, V. (2019). The Impact of Influencer Marketing on Consumer Purchase Decisions: A Study of Millennials. *Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services*, 47, 274-283.

**Key findings:**
- Investigates influencer marketing impact specifically on millennial consumers
- Identifies key factors: trust, credibility, and social norms
- Demonstrates that influencer marketing can:
  - Positively influence millennials' attitudes toward brands
  - Increase likelihood of purchase decisions
- Age-demographic insights for understanding consumer segments

**Relevance:** Provides demographic-specific research on purchase decision mechanisms.
