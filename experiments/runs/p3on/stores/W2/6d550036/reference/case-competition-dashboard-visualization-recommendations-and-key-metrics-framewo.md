Key visualization categories: Problem trees and SWOT analysis for problem identification; cost-benefit waterfall charts for recommendations; financial performance dashboards; operational efficiency metrics dashboards; customer acquisition cost and retention rate tracking; competitive landscape analysis and market share visualization; innovation roadmaps using Gantt charts.

Essential metrics to track: Revenue growth rate, profit margin, ROI, break-even analysis, cash flow projections. Operational: production capacity utilization, cycle time, throughput, defect rates, inventory turnover. Customer: CAC, retention rate, CLV, satisfaction ratings, NPS, conversion rates. Market/Competitive: market share, market size growth rate, competitive positioning, pricing analysis.

Best practices: Focus on few key metrics that tell a coherent story, use mix of leading and lagging indicators, ensure metric consistency across presentation, provide context and benchmarks for metrics, select actionable and measurable metrics.
