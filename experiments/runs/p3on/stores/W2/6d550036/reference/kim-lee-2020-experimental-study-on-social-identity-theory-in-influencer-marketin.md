**Full citation:** Kim, J., & Lee, Y. (2020). Understanding the Role of Social Identity in Influencer Marketing: An Experimental Study. *Journal of Advertising*, 49(2), 249-264.

**Key findings:**
- Explores how social identity theory explains effectiveness of social media influencer marketing
- Consumers sharing social identity with influencer are more likely to:
  - Engage with influencer's sponsored content
  - Exhibit positive attitudes toward the brand
- Demonstrates causal link between identity alignment and marketing effectiveness

**Relevance:** Directly supports interest in social identity theory as mechanism for influencer marketing success.
