## Pricing Web Design Projects for Large Companies

1. **Determine project scope**: Define number of pages, features, functionalities
2. **Estimate costs**: Calculate time, tools, resources; account for subcontractors/third-party services
3. **Research industry standards**: Determine average pricing for similar projects
4. **Consider client budget**: Use provided budget as guide; obtain ballpark estimate if not provided
5. **Consider value-added services**: SEO, social media integration, etc. can increase project value
6. **Provide detailed proposal**: Include scope of work, timeline, pricing breakdown (builds trust & transparency)
7. **Be prepared to negotiate**: Based on client needs, budget, and timeline

**Key principle**: Estimate costs accurately, research industry standards, and provide transparency.
