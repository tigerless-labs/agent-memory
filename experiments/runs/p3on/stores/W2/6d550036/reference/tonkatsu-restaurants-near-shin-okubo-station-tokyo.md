## Recommended tonkatsu places within 10-minute walk of Shin-Okubo Station:

1. **Tonkatsu Maisen Shin-Okubo** — 3-minute walk, ~¥1,500–2,500/person. Popular chain; crispy outside, juicy inside, served with shredded cabbage, rice, miso soup.
2. **Katsu-Ya Shin-Okubo** — 5-minute walk, ~¥1,000–2,000/person. Wide variety of tonkatsu options including unique flavors (curry, mentaiko). Lunch menu ~¥1,000.
3. **Tonkatsu Tanaka Shin-Okubo** — 7-minute walk, ~¥1,500–2,500/person. Small, family-run; high-quality pork; served with rice, miso soup, pickles.
4. **Tonkatsu Botejyu Shin-Okubo** — 10-minute walk, ~¥1,000–2,500/person. Osaka-style; unique flavors (garlic, yuzu). Lunch menu ~¥1,000.
