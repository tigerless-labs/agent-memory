Presented poster on this research at an academic conference on consumer psychology. Currently expanding the research with focus on:

- Credibility and trust factors in influencer-consumer relationships
- Authenticity in influencer marketing (how influencers establish and demonstrate authenticity)
- Social identity theory applied to influencer marketing
- Leveraging social identity to increase brand awareness and drive sales

Key areas of interest:
- How influencers establish credibility (expertise, relevance, authenticity, consistency, social proof)
- Consumer perception of authenticity in influencer marketing
- Using social identity theory to explain why audiences are drawn to influencers
- Practical strategies for influencers to build connections through social identity
