# Third Child Pregnancy Article (2023)

## Project Details
- **Topic (Indonesian):** hamil anak ketiga (third pregnancy)
- **Target audience:** 23-30 year old females, inexperienced
- **Publication:** Column in major media
- **Author role:** Pregnancy health & nutrition expert, mother of 3

## Writing Requirements
- **Style:** Informative, bias to short sentences, active voice, third person point of view
- **Voice:** Neutral and objective  
- **Tone:** Friendly but professional
- **Special instruction:** Write with high perplexity and burstiness (varied sentence length/complexity, not uniformly AI-like) — include longer/complex sentences alongside shorter ones

## Article Outline (5 Sections)

### I. Introduction
- Briefly introduce topic: how third pregnancy differs from first two
- Mention importance of recognizing pregnancy signs for healthy pregnancy/delivery

### II. Common Signs of Third Pregnancy (2000 words planned)
- Physical symptoms: morning sickness, frequent urination, breast tenderness
- How mother's body has adjusted after two prior pregnancies
- Potential risks: gestational diabetes, high blood pressure, difficult delivery

### III. Emotional Changes During Third Pregnancy (2000 words planned)
- Pregnancy blues easier to manage due to prior experience
- Mix of emotions and family dynamic changes with third child

### IV. Differences in Delivering Third Child (2000 words planned)
- How labor/delivery differs from previous pregnancies
- How having more children affects the birthing process

### V. Conclusion (500 words planned)
- Summarize signs, emotional changes, differences
- Emphasize importance of medical attention/support
- Provide resources for support
