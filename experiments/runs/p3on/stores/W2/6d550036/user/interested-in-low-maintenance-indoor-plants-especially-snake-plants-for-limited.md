Looking to add greenery to Tokyo apartment with easy-to-care-for houseplants. Specifically interested in snake plants (Sansevieria Trifasciata) because they tolerate low light (50–75 foot-candles), require minimal water, survive with neglect, and are nearly impossible to kill. Also considers ZZ plants, pothos, dracaena, succulents, peace lilies, and air plants. Prefers plants that work in rooms with limited natural light; willing to use grow lights if needed.
