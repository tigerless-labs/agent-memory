Particularly interested in how Christianity reconciles belief in a loving, omnipotent God with the existence of evil and suffering in the world.

Key questions explored:
- Whether monotheism's singularity of God limits spiritual diversity compared to polytheism
- The tension between God as a loving entity vs. as an authoritarian ruler
- How suffering and evil can exist under an all-powerful, all-loving God
- Whether God's sovereignty is compatible with human freedom

Seeks nuanced engagement with the question rather than single-answer theodicy.
