For embroidered/personalized baby blankets and gifts:

- **Etsy**: Handmade and customized items; search for 'custom baby blanket with name' or 'personalized baby blanket'; check reviews and seller policies
- **Amazon**: Customized baby blankets with embroidery; search for 'customized baby blanket with name' or 'personalized baby blanket'
- **Personalization Mall**: Wide range of personalized gifts including baby blankets; multiple font/color/design options; preview design before ordering
- **CafePress**: Customized gifts including baby blankets; variety of designs and fonts; option to upload custom designs
- **Zazzle**: Marketplace for customized products including baby blankets; choose designs, fonts, colors, or upload images

When shopping, check: turnaround time, material quality, embroidery method/durability, customer reviews.
