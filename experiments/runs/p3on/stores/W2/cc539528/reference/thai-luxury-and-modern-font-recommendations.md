## Recommended Thai Fonts
1. **Kanit** — contemporary sans-serif by Cadson Demak; geometric, clean lines
2. **Sarabun** — modern serif by Suppakit Chalermlarp; elegant, highly legible
3. **Maitree** — Thai serif by Cadson Demak; modern, balanced, versatile weights
4. **Krub** — geometric sans-serif by Cadson Demak; sleek, luxurious appearance
5. **Chonburi** — contemporary sans-serif by Cadson Demak; condensed, bold, contemporary

All by Cadson Demak or contemporary Thai designers; suitable for high-end Thai branding and design projects.
