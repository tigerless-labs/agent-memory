## Recommended Platforms

1. **STEEZY Studio** — Online platform with beginner-friendly hip-hop dance classes and tutorials focused on footwork
2. **DancePlug** — Online dance classes including hip-hop with personalized feedback on technique
3. **Hip Hop International** — Global organization offering online tutorials and workshops on different hip-hop styles

## Recommended YouTube Channels

1. **Hip Hop Dance Tutorial** — Free hip-hop dance tutorials for beginners, detailed breakdown of footwork-specific moves
2. **Matt Steffanina** — Dancer and choreographer with free hip-hop tutorials breaking down basic movements

## Beginner-Friendly Styles to Start With

- Hip-hop freestyle: Improvisational style with no strict rules, good for experimentation
- New Jack Swing: Late 1980s/early 1990s style with syncopated rhythms and simple footwork
