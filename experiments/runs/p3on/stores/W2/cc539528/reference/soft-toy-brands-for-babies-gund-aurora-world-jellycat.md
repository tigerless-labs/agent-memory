Popular brands for soft toys and plush animals suitable for babies:

- **GUND**: Known for soft, high-quality plush toys
- **Aurora World**: Variety of soft animals and themed toys
- **Jellycat**: Designer plush toys with imaginative designs

When selecting soft toys for infants, consider: soft plush material, size (6-10 inches for newborns/infants), safety (no detachable small parts), washability. Available on Amazon and specialty toy stores.
