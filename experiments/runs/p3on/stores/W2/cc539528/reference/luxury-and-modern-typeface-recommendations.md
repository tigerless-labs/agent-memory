## Recommended Fonts
1. **Avenir** — clean, elegant sans-serif by Adrian Frutiger; versatile for luxury branding
2. **Futura** — geometric sans-serif by Paul Renner; crisp, minimalist, upscale
3. **Didot** — classic serif by Firmin Didot; high contrast, refined; ideal for fashion/editorial
4. **Gotham** — contemporary sans-serif by Tobias Frere-Jones; geometric simplicity, multiple weights
5. **Proxima Nova** — modern sans-serif by Mark Simonson; clean lines, large x-height

All suitable for luxury branding, logo design, and print materials.
