Top national parks for families with kids:\n\n1. Yellowstone (Wyoming) - geysers, hot springs, wildlife, Junior Ranger program (ages 4-13)\n2. Yosemite (California) - granite cliffs, waterfalls, Mariposa Grove\n3. Grand Canyon (Arizona) - iconic natural wonder, hiking and camping\n4. Zion (Utah) - unique rock formations, Emerald Pools Trail, Narrows\n5. Acadia (Maine) - coast, mountains, forests, hiking and biking\n6. Great Smoky Mountains (Tennessee/North Carolina) - fall foliage, hiking, wildlife\n7. Olympic (Washington) - mountains, rainforests, 70+ miles of coastline\n8. Rocky Mountain (Colorado) - 60+ peaks above 12,000 feet, hiking, wildlife\n9. Grand Teton (Wyoming) - mountain vistas, lakes, rivers, Junior Ranger program\n10. Arches (Utah) - 2,000+ natural arches, scenic drives\n\nTips: Check park websites, book accommodations early, engage kids with Junior Ranger programs, be flexible with weather.
