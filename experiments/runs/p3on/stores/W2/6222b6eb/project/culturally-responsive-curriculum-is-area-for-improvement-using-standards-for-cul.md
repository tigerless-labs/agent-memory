Identified cultural responsiveness as an area needing improvement at Thornton Township High School. Interested in using the Standards for Cultural Responsiveness checklist as a tool for curriculum development and revision. Key practices to implement: connecting student perspectives and experiences to topics, incorporating student identities (family traditions, cultural backgrounds), encouraging use of first language, cross-cultural interaction via technology, community research and engagement, differentiated learning modes. Referenced Eugene Butler Jr. (2019) article on how culturally responsive instruction positively impacts academic outcomes for minority students.
