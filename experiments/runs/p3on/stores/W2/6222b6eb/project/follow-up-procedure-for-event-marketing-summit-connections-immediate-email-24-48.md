Procedure for converting Event Marketing Summit contacts into meaningful connections and partnerships:\n\n**Immediate Follow-up (Within 24-48 hours):**\n- Send personalized email referencing conversation and topics\n- Connect on social media (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)\n- Share relevant content (blog posts, articles)\n\n**Short-term Follow-up (Within 1-2 weeks):**\n- Schedule call or meeting to continue conversation\n- Introduce contacts to existing network\n- Share additional resources (whitepapers, webinars)\n\n**Long-term Follow-up (Within 1-3 months):**\n- Check-in and provide value through insights/advice/connections\n- Explore collaboration opportunities (joint webinars, content, sponsorships)\n- Invite to exclusive events or beta access\n\n**Best Practices:**\n- Use CRM to track interactions\n- Personalize approach based on conversation\n- Be consistent with follow-ups\n- Look for mutually beneficial opportunities\n- Be patient—focus on value rather than immediate results
