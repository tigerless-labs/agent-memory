Teaches Yearbook class (~25 students) at Thornton Township High School in Harvey, Illinois. School student population: 67.5% Black, 29% Latino, 2.5% Asian (Indians of Muslim Faith), <1% White. 17.3% classified as English Language Learners. Teacher demographics (district-wide): 53.8% White, 36.3% Black, 6.8% Latino, 1.8% Asian. In Yearbook class: 3 of ~25 students speak mostly Spanish. Concerned about cultural responsiveness—announcements only in English, excluding ELL students. Letters and report cards available in English and Spanish, but more outreach needed for Spanish-speaking families.
