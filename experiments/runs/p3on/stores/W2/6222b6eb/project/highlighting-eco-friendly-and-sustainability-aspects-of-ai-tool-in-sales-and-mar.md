Decision to emphasize sustainability and eco-friendly benefits of AI-powered project management tool in sales conversations and marketing materials, based on webinar learnings on sustainability in trade show management.\n\nIn sales conversations:\n- Emphasize environmental benefits (reduced carbon footprint, minimize waste)\n- Use eco-friendly language ('green', 'sustainable', 'eco-friendly')\n- Quantify environmental impact (reduced paper, energy consumption)\n- Highlight cost savings from sustainable practices\n- Share customer testimonials on environmental benefits\n\nIn marketing materials:\n- Create dedicated sustainability webpage\n- Use eco-friendly visuals (leaves, trees, recycling symbols)\n- Highlight sustainability certifications (ISO 14001, etc.)\n- Develop sustainability-focused whitepaper/e-book\n- Share eco-friendly tips on social media\n- Create sustainability-focused customer success stories\n- Make sustainability part of core brand identity
