Wine and cheese gift baskets are well-received gifts. Suggested components:
- A quality wine (see wine pairing guide)
- Gourmet cheese selection
- Gourmet crackers or breadsticks
- A nice wine opener (high-quality, compact options work best)
- Optional: gift card to local wine and cheese shop (allows recipient to choose favorites)

Presentation matters - consider gift wrapping if available. Total budget can be in the 40 dollar range.
