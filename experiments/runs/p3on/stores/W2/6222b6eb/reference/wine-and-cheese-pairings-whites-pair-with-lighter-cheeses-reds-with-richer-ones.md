Wine and Cheese Pairing Guide

White Wines:
- Chardonnay: Pairs well with rich, creamy cheeses (Brie, Camembert)
- Pinot Grigio: Crisp and refreshing; pairs with lighter cheeses (Goat Cheese, Feta)
- Sauvignon Blanc: Zesty and citrusy; cuts through strong cheeses (Blue Cheese, Parmesan)

Rose:
- Dry Rose: Versatile; pairs well with soft-ripened cheeses (Brie, Chevre)

Red Wines:
- Merlot: Smooth and approachable; pairs with a variety (Cheddar, Gouda, Provolone)

Popular Brands:
- Apothic
- La Crema
- Kim Crawford
- Cupcake Vineyards
- Masciarelli

Selection Tips:
- Price range: 10-30 per bottle for good quality
- Look for wines from established regions: Bordeaux, Burgundy, Tuscany, Napa Valley
- Consider label design for gift presentation
