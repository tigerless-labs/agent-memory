Book exploring how five key Christian words have been distorted from their original biblical meanings through modern religious usage.

**Details:**
- Author: Michael D. Halsey
- ISBN-10: 0979963729
- ISBN-13: 978-0979963728

**Five key words discussed:**
- Grace
- Faith
- Sanctification
- Righteousness
- Salvation

**Chapter 2 title:** Newspeak: Finished Is Incomplete

The book aims to help readers gain a deeper understanding of these core Christian concepts and their true meanings in theological context, arguing they have been distorted by cultural and religious attitudes into modern 'religious newspeak.'
