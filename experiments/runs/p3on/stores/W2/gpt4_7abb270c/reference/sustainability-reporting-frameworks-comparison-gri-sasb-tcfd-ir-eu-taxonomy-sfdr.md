Comparison of 7 major sustainability reporting frameworks:

| Framework | Regulatory Body | Focus | Applicability | Required Format |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| GRI Standards | Global Reporting Initiative | Sustainability | Voluntary | None (recommends GRI Standards) |
| SASB Standards | SASB | Sustainability | Voluntary | None (recommends SASB Standards) |
| TCFD Recommendations | Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures | Climate risks/opportunities | Voluntary, increasingly adopted by governments/investors | None (recommends disclosure in mainstream financial filings) |
| IR Framework | International Integrated Reporting Council | Integrated Reporting | Voluntary | None (recommends integrated reporting framework) |
| EU Taxonomy Regulation | European Union | Climate risks/opportunities | Mandatory for NFRD/SFDR subject companies | Must align with EU Taxonomy classification system |
| SFDR | European Union | Sustainability of financial products | Mandatory for financial market participants and advisers | Must disclose in pre-contractual documents and periodic reports |
| CSRD | European Union | Sustainability reporting | Proposed mandatory for all large EU companies | ESEF format with Inline XBRL (iXBRL) |

CDP's climate disclosure framework is voluntary, aligned with TCFD, and can complement other frameworks.
