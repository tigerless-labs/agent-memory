Plants suitable for bright natural light with ability to tolerate indirect light:\n- Snake Plant (Sansevieria): Low-maintenance, tolerates range of light\n- Pothos (Epipremnum aureum): Versatile, easy-to-grow\n- Dracaena: Over 40 species, minimal care, low to bright light\n- Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum): Elegant blooms, prefers bright indirect light\n- Spider Plant (Chlorophytum): Easy care, produces white flowers\n- Calathea: Colorful leaves, bright indirect light\n- Bromeliads: Tropical, bright indirect light, vibrant flowers\n- ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas): Extremely low-maintenance\n- Fiddle Leaf Fig: Requires bright indirect light, consistent moisture, high humidity, regular pruning. More finicky but stunning aesthetic.\n\nGeneral care tips: Monitor soil moisture, fertilize regularly, prune/repot as needed, watch for pests/diseases.
