**Timing:**
- Optimal ~54 days before departure (Skyscanner research)
- Prime window: 21-121 days out with prices within 5% of lowest (CheapAir)

**Best booking days:** Avoid Tue/Wed/Sat; book Mon/Thu/Fri instead

**Off-peak seasons:** Mid-Jan–mid-Mar, Apr–May, Sep–Nov

**Flight selection options:** Early morning, red-eye, or alternative airports

**Price monitoring tools:** Skyscanner alerts, Google Flights trends, Airfarewatchdog, The Flight Deal, Secret Flying

**Cost-saving tactics:**
- Clear cookies to prevent price tracking increases
- Search for error fares (mistake fares, significantly cheaper)
- Check alternative nearby airports
- Use budget airlines
- Maintain date flexibility
- Book group tickets for discounts
