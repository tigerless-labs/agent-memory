The Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) applies to large companies that are public-interest entities meeting these criteria:
- More than 500 employees AND
- Balance sheet total over €20 million OR net turnover over €40 million

Public-interest entities include companies with securities admitted to trading on EU regulated markets, credit institutions, and insurance undertakings.

NFRD does not prescribe a specific reporting format, but encourages use of internationally recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) for structuring disclosures.
