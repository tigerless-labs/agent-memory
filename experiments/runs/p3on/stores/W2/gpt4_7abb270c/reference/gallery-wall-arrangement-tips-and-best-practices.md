Tips for creating a visually appealing gallery wall:\n\n1. **Start with a theme:** Find a common thread in colors, shapes, subjects\n2. **Choose a unifying element:** Frame style, color palette, or shape consistency\n3. **Create a focal point:** Designate a central/statement piece to draw attention\n4. **Experiment with layouts:** Use graph paper or digital mood board tools\n5. **Balance and harmony:** Distribute prints to create visual equilibrium\n6. **Consider wall architecture:** Use molding, windows, doors as design features\n7. **Play with scale:** Mix large and small prints for visual interest and depth\n8. **Leave breathing room:** Avoid overcrowding; leave space between prints\n9. **Step back and adjust:** Evaluate overall look and make adjustments\n10. **Have fun:** Gallery wall is personal expression, experiment freely\n\nNo one-size-fits-all solution; find balance that works for your space.
