## Organizations\n- **AIC (American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works)**: Professional organization offering online courses, webinars, and publications on art conservation. Website: aic.stanford.edu\n- **IIC (International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works)**: Features articles, news, and events related to art conservation.\n- **Conservation Wiki**: Collaborative online platform with information on conservation techniques, materials, and best practices.\n\n## YouTube Channels\n- Art Conservation\n- Conservation Framing\n- The Getty Conservation Institute\n\n## Recommended Books\n- "The Conservation of Art and Architecture" by James C. Y. Watt\n- "The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory" by Caroline K. Keck\n- "Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide" by John H. Stubbs\n\n## Online Learning Platforms\n- Coursera, edX, and Udemy offer courses on art conservation, cultural heritage, and related topics.\n- Many local museums and galleries offer workshops and classes on art conservation.
