Interactive & Immersive Installation Artists:
- Carsten Höller: German artist known for interactive installations featuring slides, Ferris wheels, and playful elements
- Anicka Yi: Korean-American artist creating immersive, scent-based installations exploring art, science, and technology
- Rafael Lozano-Hemmer: Mexican-Canadian artist creating interactive installations using biometrics, robotics, exploring surveillance and control

Exhibitions & Venues:
- Meow Wolf: Santa Fe-based collective creating immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, and storytelling
- The Color Factory: San Francisco exhibition exploring emotional and psychological effects of color through interactive installations
- TeamLab: Japanese collective creating stunning immersive exhibitions using technology and light

Online Platforms:
- Artspace: Digital platform featuring interactive art experiences, articles, and artist interviews
- The Creators Project: Platform showcasing interactive and immersive art, music, and technology projects
