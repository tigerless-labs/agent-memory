The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) mandates use of European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) based on Inline XBRL (iXBRL) for sustainability information in annual reports. Format expected to be introduced in 2024, with reporting required from 2026 onwards. CSRD expands scope to all large companies operating in the EU (not just listed companies). Replaces NFRD.
