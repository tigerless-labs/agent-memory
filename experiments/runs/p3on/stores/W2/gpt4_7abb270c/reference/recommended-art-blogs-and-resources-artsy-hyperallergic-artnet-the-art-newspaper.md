## Art News & Exhibition Resources

- **Artsy**: Comprehensive platform for art news, exhibitions, artist profiles, and market trends
- **Hyperallergic**: Leading art blog covering contemporary art news, reviews, and opinion pieces (NYC-focused)
- **Artnet**: Art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists, trends
- **The Art Newspaper**: Daily international art news, exhibitions, and market analysis
- **Contemporary Art Daily**: Discoveries of new artists and exhibitions (contemporary focus)
- **Art in America**: Magazine with in-depth features, reviews, and interviews on contemporary art
- **Frieze**: Leading art magazine covering contemporary art, culture, politics, international exhibitions
- **Apollo Magazine**: British magazine covering art, architecture, collecting (historical and contemporary)
- **Art21**: Non-profit producing documentaries and online content on contemporary art
- **Culture Trip**: Art, culture, and travel coverage with focus on emerging artists and underground scenes
