**Feminist artists from the 1970s:**\nJudy Chicago (The Dinner Party), Miriam Schapiro, Carolee Schneemann, The Guerrilla Girls, Barbara Kruger, Cindy Sherman, Laurie Anderson\n\n**Key movement:** The Feminist Art Program, founded 1970 at California Institute of the Arts — one of the first platforms for women artists to explore feminist themes and challenges to traditional art world power structures.
