Books on Feminist Art:
- 'The Feminist Art Movement' by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard: Comprehensive history of feminist art from 1960s to 1990s
- 'Feminist Art and the Maternal' by Andrea Liss: Explores relationship between feminist art and motherhood, featuring Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles
- 'Women, Art, and Power' by Linda Nochlin: Essay collection on women's representation in art and feminism in art history
- 'The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact' by Broude and Garrard: Detailed examination of 1970s US feminist art movement
- 'Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly' by Guerrilla Girls: Essays and artworks by the anonymous feminist art collective

Documentaries on Feminist Art:
- '!Women Art Revolution' (2010) by Lynn Hershman Leeson: Interviews with 40+ feminist artists including Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, Carolee Schneemann
- 'The Heretics' (2009): About the Heresies Collective, featuring Nancy Azara and Miriam Schapiro
- 'Womart: The Women's Art Movement' (1991) by Kate Horsfield: Features Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, Yvonne Rainer
- 'Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations' (2010): Panel discussion with Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell, moderated by Huey Copeland
- 'Art:21 - Judy Chicago' (2014): PBS series episode featuring in-depth interview with Judy Chicago
