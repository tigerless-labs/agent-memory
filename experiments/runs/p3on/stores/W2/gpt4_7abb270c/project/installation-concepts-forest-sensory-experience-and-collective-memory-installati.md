Developing two main installation concept directions:

1. **Forest Sensory Installation**: Simulate walking through a forest using sound (birds chirping, leaves rustling), light, texture, and scent (damp earth) to recreate memories of specific environments and engage multiple senses.

2. **Collective Memory Installation**: Explore shared human experience across cultures and time periods by incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and historical periods to create a sense of shared identity and collective memory.

Interested in technologies: 3D audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback, data visualization, AR, interactive storytelling, and collaborative platforms for viewer participation.
