Intends to reorganize kitchen cabinets to make it easier to put things away and optimize storage efficiency. Discussed strategies including: purging and decluttering, categorizing items, assigning homes for each category, using vertical space, installing adjustable shelves, utilizing cabinet door space, storing heavy items low, keeping frequently used items accessible, and labeling shelves.
