When booking hotels near Miami International Airport, prefers the Hyatt Regency Miami Airport because:
- Connected to airport via skybridge (convenient access)
- Offers free shuttle service to Port of Miami cruise terminal
- Also provides shuttle to/from MIA airport

Useful for trips involving cruise departures from Port of Miami.
