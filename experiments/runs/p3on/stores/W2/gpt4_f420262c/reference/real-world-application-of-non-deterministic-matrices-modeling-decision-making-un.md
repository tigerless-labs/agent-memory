Non-deterministic matrices can model real-world decision-making scenarios where there is uncertainty about outcomes.

Example: A person deciding whether to go to a party

Truth values can represent:
- 0: certainty that a proposition is false
- 1: possibility that the proposition is true
- 2: possibility that the proposition is false

This allows modeling situations where:
- The person is uncertain whether to go or stay
- Different actions have multiple possible outcomes
- Each proposition can take on different truth-values depending on context

The negation and conjunction/disjunction operations then capture how these uncertainties combine when reasoning about compound propositions.
