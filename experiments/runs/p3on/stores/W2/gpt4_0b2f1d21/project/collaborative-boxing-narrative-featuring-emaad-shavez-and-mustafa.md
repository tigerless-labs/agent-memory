Story developed through collaborative storytelling:

**Main characters:**
- Emaad: professional boxer who retires at 46 after losing matches, returns to fight his brother Shavez
- Shavez: Emaad's younger brother, becomes world-dominant boxer with 7 Pakistan Boxing World Cup trophies
- Mustafa: Emaad's arch-nemesis who escapes prison and eventually attempts to kill Shavez
- CrustiChezz: Romanian fighter who trains under Emaad

**Story arc:**
- Mustafa escapes prison and challenges Emaad, who wins and retires
- Shavez nearly dies in car accident while planning Eid surprise for Emaad
- Emaad trains CrustiChezz before resuming boxing career
- Emaad defeats Shavez in 8th Boxing World Cup final
- Mustafa shoots Shavez at the celebration; Shavez survives despite cardiac arrest
- Emaad pursues and defeats Mustafa in final confrontation

**Themes:** family rivalry, redemption arcs, persistence despite aging, unexpected survival, revenge resolution
