**Mysterium** is a cooperative social deduction board game where one player is a Ghost communicating visions to Psychic Mediums who solve a murder mystery.

**Gameplay:** 7 rounds, Ghost plays abstract vision cards, Mediums interpret and vote on suspect/location/weapon. All Mediums must correctly identify the solution to win.

**Player count:** 2-7 players. Suitable for 3 players with slightly less dynamic play.

**Playtime:**
- First time: 60-90 minutes
- Average/experienced: 45-60 minutes
- Very experienced: 30-45 minutes

**Age:** Suitable for 12-year-olds with guidance; promotes imagination, creativity, teamwork, but may require help interpreting abstract visions.
