**Recommended books with similar themes to The Power:**\n1. The Handmaid's Tale by Margaret Atwood - dystopian, patriarchal society\n2. The Girls by Emma Cline - cult-like community, teenage girls in 1960s California\n3. The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang - fantasy inspired by Chinese history/mythology, military academy, slower burn than The Power\n4. The Fifth Season by N.K. Jemisin - epic fantasy, power, oppression, social hierarchy\n5. The Women's War by Jenna Glass - women discover power to control fate of world\n6. The Bear and the Nightingale by Katherine Arden - medieval Russia, mythology, power, feminism\n7. Ancillary Justice by Ann Leckie - sci-fi, AI seeking revenge, identity and justice\n\nNote: The Poppy War has different pace and style from The Power - slower burn, more traditional fantasy, more introspective.
