**Ticket to Ride** (8+, 30-60 min): Train-themed, build railroad routes. Easy to learn, develops strategic thinking.

**Sushi Go!** (8+, 15-30 min): Fast-paced card game, collect sushi dishes. Quick to play.

**Forbidden Island/Forbidden Desert** (10+, 20-30 min): Cooperative, retrieve treasures before island sinks/desert buries. Promotes teamwork.

**Carcassonne** (10+, 30-45 min): Tile-laying, build medieval landscape. Easy but strategic.

**Azul** (8+, 30-45 min): Create beautiful mosaic with tiles. Simple but deep.

**Qwirkle** (6+, 15-30 min): Tile-matching game, spatial awareness.

**Codenames** (10+, 15-30 min): Word-guessing, teamwork-focused.

All are age-appropriate, develop strategic thinking and problem-solving skills.
