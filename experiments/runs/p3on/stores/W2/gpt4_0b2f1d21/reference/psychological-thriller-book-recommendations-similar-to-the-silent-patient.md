**Recommended psychological thrillers:**\n1. Gone Girl by Gillian Flynn - twisty, explores dark secrets of marriage\n2. Sharp Objects by Gillian Flynn - journalist returns home, confronts dark past\n3. The Girl on the Train by Paula Hawkins - commuter becomes obsessed with perfect couple\n4. The Woman in the Window by A.J. Finn - modern Rear Window, recluse witnesses crime\n5. The Last Mrs. Parrish by Liv Constantine - obsession, marriage, deceit with dark humor\n6. The Couple Next Door by Shari Lapena - baby goes missing, secrets emerge\n7. The Witch Elm by Tana French - atmospheric, slow-burn about family secrets\n8. The 7 1/2 Deaths of Evelyn Hardcastle by Stuart Turton - mind-bending mystery, Groundhog Day-ish format\n9. Big Little Lies by Liane Moriarty - complex characters, multiple narrators\n10. The Devil in the White City by Erik Larson - historical true crime\n11. The Poison Tree by Erin Kelly - complex plot, multiple timelines\n\nAll work well as audiobooks. Gone Girl narrated by Julia Whelan and Kirby Heyborne.
