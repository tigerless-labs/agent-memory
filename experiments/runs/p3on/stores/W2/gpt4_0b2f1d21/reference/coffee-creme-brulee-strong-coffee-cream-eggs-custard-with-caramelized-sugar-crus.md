## Coffee Crème Brûlée Recipe\n\n**Custard:** Brew strong coffee, mix with cream, sugar, and eggs. Pour into ramekins and chill until set.\n\n**Caramelized Sugar Topping:** Use granulated sugar. Heat skillet over medium-high heat until almost smoking. Sprinkle sugar thinly in even layer. Let dissolve undisturbed for 2-3 minutes (don't stir much; gently tilt skillet for even cooking). Look for deep amber/reddish-brown color. Remove from heat immediately—don't overcook or it becomes bitter. Cool and set completely. Apply thin layer to chilled custard just before serving to create crunchy, glass-like crust.
