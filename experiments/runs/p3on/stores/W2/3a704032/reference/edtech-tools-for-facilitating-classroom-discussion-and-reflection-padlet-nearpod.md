**Edtech platforms recommended for classroom discussion and reflection activities:**

1. **Padlet** — Collaborative platform allowing students to share thoughts and ideas in a virtual space; enables visual wall-based collaboration

2. **Nearpod** — Interactive platform with features including:
   - Polls
   - Open-ended questions
   - Student participation prompts

3. **Flipgrid** — Video discussion platform where students respond to prompts or questions with short video responses; asynchronous discussion capability

4. **Google Jamboard** — Virtual whiteboard enabling collaborative ideation and visual sharing of thoughts and ideas

**Discussion facilitation methods to use alongside these tools:**
- Ask open-ended questions requiring more than yes/no answers
- Paraphrase and summarize student responses to show their ideas are valued
- Encourage active listening through peer summarization and follow-up questions
- Use visual aids (diagrams, graphic organizers) to help organize thinking
