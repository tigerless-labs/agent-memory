## Rose Bush Pruning Guidance

**Deadheading:** Remove dead or wilted flowers at any time to encourage plant to produce more blooms. This is a gentle pruning technique.

**Promoting Blooms:** Light pruning to shape and promote air circulation. Remove weak/spindly growth and crossing/rubbing stems. Encourages plant to direct energy toward flower production.

**Timing for Extensive Pruning:** Best done during dormant season (winter/early spring) to rejuvenate plant or control size. This allows plant to complete flowering cycle and store energy for next season.

**Technique:** Make clean cuts at 45-degree angle. Remove weak or diseased wood to prevent disease spread and encourage healthy growth.
