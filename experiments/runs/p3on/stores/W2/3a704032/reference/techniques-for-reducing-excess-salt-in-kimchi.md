**Methods to remove excess salt from over-salted kimchi:**

1. **Rinse method:** Gently rinse under cold running water while massaging, then drain. Works best early in fermentation.

2. **Dilution:** Add more vegetables (massaged with a bit of salt) to dilute the salt content.

3. **Desalting agents:** Add small amounts of rice vinegar or gochujang to help balance saltiness (be cautious with liquids—affects fermentation).

4. **Salt-breaking (geom-sul):** Soak in cold water for several hours or overnight, rinse, repeat multiple times. Note: may affect texture and flavor.

5. **Prevention:** Use correct salt type/amount, taste as you go, rinse vegetables thoroughly after salting.
