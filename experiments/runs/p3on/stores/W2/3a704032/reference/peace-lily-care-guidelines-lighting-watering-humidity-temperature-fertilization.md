## Peace Lily Care Guidelines

**Lighting:** Prefer bright, indirect light (east- or west-facing windows ideal). Avoid direct sunlight which causes leaf scorch. Low light prevents blooming.

**Watering:** Check soil moisture by inserting finger up to first knuckle. Water only when top 1-2 inches feel dry. Typical frequency: every 7-10 days in spring/summer, every 10-14 days in fall/winter. Overwatering is the most common cause of death. Ensure good drainage.

**Humidity:** Prefer humid environments. Can adapt to average humidity (40-50%). Use water/pebble trays or humidifier to increase humidity.

**Temperature:** Ideal range 68°F-72°F (20°C-22°C). Acceptable range 65°F-75°F (18°C-24°C). Avoid temperatures below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C). Keep away from drafts and heating/cooling vents.

**Fertilization:** Feed with balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) diluted to half strength during growing season (spring/summer). Fertilize every 1-2 weeks during growing season, monthly during fall/winter.

**Potting Mix:** Use well-draining mix designed for tropical plants or orchids.

**Pruning:** Remove dead/dying leaves and flowers regularly. Use clean, sharp tools.

**Repotting:** Generally not frequent; repot in spring/summer when plant outgrows container or shows nutrient deficiency.

**Pest Control:** Check regularly for spider mites, mealybugs, scale. Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil as needed.

**Misting:** 1-2 times per week for peace lilies (less than ferns). Mist in morning. Avoid misting soil or crown.
