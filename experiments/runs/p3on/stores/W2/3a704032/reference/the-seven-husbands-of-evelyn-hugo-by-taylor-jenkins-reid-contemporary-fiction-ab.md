Character-driven novel exploring themes of identity, fame, and relationships. Protagonist Evelyn is a Cuban immigrant whose identity is deeply tied to her celebrity persona but reveals a vulnerable inner self. The narrative uses Monique (a biographer) as an active participant, not passive observer. Praised for rich portrayal of intersectional identity and complex relationships between women—simultaneously supportive and competitive, loving and complicated.
