Recommended series with strong character development, blended genres, and complex narratives:\n\n- **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda — fantasy epic with supernatural abilities, stunning art, morally complex characters\n- **The Wicked + The Divine** by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie — mythology meets pop culture, gods as pop stars\n- **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston — superhero deconstruction, heroes stranded on farm, explores humanity of superheroism\n- **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá — dysfunctional superhero family, mystery and apocalypse\n- **Descender** by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen — sci-fi epic about robot boy hunted by humans, explores identity and humanity\n- **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra — biblical and mythological figures in dark, gritty reality\n- **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra — last human male on Earth, explores gender and power
