Languages used for music production and audio synthesis: Sonic Pi (beginner-friendly live coding), ChucK (designed for music, simple syntax), Max/MSP (visual, for custom instruments/effects), Pure Data (open-source visual alternative to Max/MSP), SuperCollider (real-time synthesis and algorithmic composition), Csound (software synthesis system, 30+ years old), Python (general-purpose with music libraries), Swift (for iOS/macOS music apps). Each has different strengths; choice depends on learning style and specific goals.
