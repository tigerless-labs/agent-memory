Gochugaru measurements per 2 cups of vegetables:
- Mild: 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon
- Medium: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon  
- Hot: 1 to 2 teaspoons
- Extremely Hot: 2+ teaspoons

Notes: Start with small amounts as it's hard to remove excess heat. Gochugaru has medium-to-hot heat with balanced flavor. Different brands vary in potency.
