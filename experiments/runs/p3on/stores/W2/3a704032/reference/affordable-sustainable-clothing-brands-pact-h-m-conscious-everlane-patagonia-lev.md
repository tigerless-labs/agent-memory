**Pact** — affordable organic cotton clothing for men, women, and children, made with fair labor practices.

**H&M Conscious** — sustainable collection by H&M offering affordable clothing made from organic cotton, recycled polyester, and Tencel.

**Everlane** — transparent sustainable brand with affordable, high-quality clothing from recycled synthetic fibers and organic cotton. Good men's selection (t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, shoes). Minimalistic aesthetic with timeless, classic designs made to last. Known for providing detailed information on how items are made and materials used.

**Patagonia** — sustainable outdoor clothing brand with mid-range and affordable options made from organic cotton, recycled polyester, and hemp.

**Levi's** — denim brand offering affordable sustainable clothing made from recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton.

Other leaders in sustainable fashion: Stella McCartney, Eileen Fisher, Honest By.
