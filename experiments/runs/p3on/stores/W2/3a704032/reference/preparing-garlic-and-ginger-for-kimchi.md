**Garlic:**
- Use 2-3 cloves per 2 cups of vegetables
- Mince or crush to release oils and flavor
- Always use fresh garlic
- Can combine with gochugaru and fish sauce

**Ginger:**
- Use 1-2 inches (grated or sliced) per 2 cups of vegetables
- Peel first, then grate or slice thinly
- Use fresh ginger (either young or mature)
- Sauté in oil beforehand if you want to mellow the pungency

**Tips:** Use separate cutting board/knife to avoid flavor transfer. Ginger is quite potent, adjust to taste.
