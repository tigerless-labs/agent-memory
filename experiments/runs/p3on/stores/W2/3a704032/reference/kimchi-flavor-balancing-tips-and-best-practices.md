**Balancing kimchi flavors (sweet, sour, salty, spicy):**

1. **Taste as you go** — catch imbalances early
2. **Use balanced seasoning paste** — adjust garlic, ginger, fish sauce, sugar proportions
3. **Add aromatics** — sauté onions, garlic, ginger in oil before adding to paste to bring out flavors
4. **Experiment with fermentation time** — shorter fermentation mellows strong flavors, longer develops complexity
5. **Start simple** — focus on key flavors rather than overcomplicate

**General tips:**
- Use fresh, high-quality ingredients
- Measure by weight when possible (more accurate than volume)
- Store chili flakes in airtight container in cool, dark place
- Kimchi is personal—experiment and adjust to taste preferences
