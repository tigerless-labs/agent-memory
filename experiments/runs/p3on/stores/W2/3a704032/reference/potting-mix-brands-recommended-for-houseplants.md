**Recommended brands for spider plants and general houseplants:**
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix — well-balanced general-purpose
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix — organic, balanced blend with peat moss/compost/perlite
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix — nutrient-rich, eco-friendly, includes compost/peat/perlite
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix — professional-grade, balanced blend

**Key ingredients to look for:** peat moss or coconut coir for moisture retention, compost/humus for nutrients, perlite/vermiculite for drainage and aeration, worm castings optional for extra nutrients.
