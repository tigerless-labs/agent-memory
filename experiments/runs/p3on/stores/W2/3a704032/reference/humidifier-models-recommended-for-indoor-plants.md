**Popular models:**
- **Levoit LV600HH Hybrid:** Affordable, cool/warm mist settings, humidistat, 6-liter tank
- **Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate:** Compact, 1-liter tank, suitable for small-medium spaces
- **Deneve Minimal Aroma:** Ultrasonic, 2.5-liter tank, humidistat, sleek design
- **Crane Drop Ultrasonic:** Compact, 1-liter tank, whisper-quiet, easy to clean

**Key features to prioritize:** Humidistat for auto control, adjustable mist output, tank size (1-6L), noise level, ease of cleaning. Place within 1-5 feet of plants; use hygrometer to monitor humidity levels.
