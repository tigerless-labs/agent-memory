Recommended novels with complex characters and relationships:\n\n- **The Devil Wears Prada** by Lauren Weisberger — demanding boss-assistant dynamic, strong female focus\n- **The Mothers** by Brit Bennett — coming-of-age, motherhood and identity in Southern California community\n- **The Girls** by Emma Cline — 1960s, teenage girls in Manson-esque cult, female relationships and power dynamics\n- **The Interestings** by Meg Wolitzer — friend group from summer arts camp, relationships over decades\n- **Conversations with Friends** by Sally Rooney — complex relationships between two women and a couple, power and desire\n- **The Female Persuasion** by Meg Wolitzer — young woman's relationships with famous feminist mentor\n- **The Last Romantics** by Tara Conklin — four siblings, complex family relationships over time\n- **The Age of Light** by Whitney Scharer — based on Lee Miller (model/photographer), Parisian avant-garde 1920s-30s, complex relationships
