**Snake Plant & General:** Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20), Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer (2-2-2), Schultz All Purpose Plant Food (20-20-20)

**Succulents & Cacti:** Miracle-Gro Cactus, Palm & Citrus Food (10-20-10), Espoma Organic Cactus! Fertilizer (5-2-6), Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10)

**Orchids:** Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10), Espoma Organic Orchid! Fertilizer (15-30-15), Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20)

**General tips:** Dilute to half strength, fertilize during growing season (spring/summer only), avoid high-nitrogen fertilizers for succulents and orchids.
