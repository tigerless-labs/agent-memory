Curated platforms for exploring emerging artists and contemporary art:

**Primary recommendations:**
- **Artsy** — leading platform with dedicated emerging artists section, filterable by medium/style
- **Saatchi Art** — online gallery focused on emerging artists
- **Artspace** — contemporary art platform with curated artworks, artist profiles, purchases
- **Uprise Art** — emerging artists focus with curated selections and purchases

**Discovery & browsing:**
- **Behance** — creatives showcase work by medium/style/location
- **Instagram** — hashtags: #emergingartist, #artoftheday, #contemporaryart, #artistsofinstagram
- **1stdibs** — curated contemporary art marketplace with galleries/dealers
- **Minted** — contemporary art and design from emerging artists
- **Collecteurs** — contemporary art and emerging artists

**Magazine & news:**
- **Juxtapoz** — online art magazine with artist profiles and interviews
- **Hyperallergic** — online art blog covering exhibitions and emerging artists
- **ArtNet** — news, exhibitions, artist profiles with emerging artists section
- **New American Paintings** — bi-monthly magazine and archive for emerging US painters
- **The Artist's Institute** — emerging artists via exhibitions and essays

**Features to look for:**
- Online exhibitions and virtual events (artist talks, panel discussions, live tours)
- Personalized recommendations based on interests
- Direct artist engagement through comments/messages
- Art fairs and festivals
- Curated collections by theme/medium/topic
