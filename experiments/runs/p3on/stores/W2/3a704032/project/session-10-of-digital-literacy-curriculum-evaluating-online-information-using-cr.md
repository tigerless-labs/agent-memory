Session 10 of a 6th-grade elementary school digital literacy curriculum.

**Focus:** Teaching students to evaluate credibility and reliability of online information

**Method:** CRAAP Test
- Currency: Is the information current/up-to-date?
- Relevance: Does it relate to the topic?
- Authority: Who is the author? Are they credible?
- Accuracy: Is the information correct and supported by evidence?
- Purpose: Why does the source exist? What is its intent?

**Activities:**
- Students practice CRAAP Test on sample articles
- Individual research projects where students evaluate 2 sources each using the worksheet
- Class presentations explaining credibility reasoning
- Group brainstorming of tips for evaluating online information

**Assessment:** Student worksheets, quiz or discussion, participation monitoring
