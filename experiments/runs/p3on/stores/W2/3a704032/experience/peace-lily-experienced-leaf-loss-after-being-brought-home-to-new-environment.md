Recently acquired peace lily was losing leaves, likely from transplant shock. Recommended care adjustments: bright indirect light (avoid direct sun), careful watering (check soil moisture to knuckle depth, ensure good drainage), increase humidity to 50%+, maintain 65-80°F, half-strength balanced fertilizer in growing season, remove dead/dying leaves, inspect for pests, ensure well-draining tropical plant potting mix, allow 2-3 weeks for acclimation.
