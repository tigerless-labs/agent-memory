Noticed tiny moving dots crawling on fern leaves and stems. Suspected pests are spider mites or springtails. Plans to treat with neem oil spray per product instructions. Will increase misting frequency to 2-3 times per week using distilled/filtered water in mornings to help maintain 40-60% humidity. Will monitor fern closely after treatment and repeat neem oil application as needed.
