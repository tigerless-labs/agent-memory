Has basil plant on balcony that struggles with too much direct sunlight. Currently using general-purpose potting mix. Planning to relocate to shadier spot with filtered/indirect sunlight. Will implement care tips: less frequent watering (check soil moisture), increased humidity via misting or water tray, balanced fertilizer at half strength, regular pruning of flower buds, optimal temps 65-75°F.
