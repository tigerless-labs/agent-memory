Received snake plant from sister in April 2023. Plant is thriving with current watering and fertilizer routine—no immediate repotting needed. Snake plants tolerate some root-bound conditions but will appreciate more space if they show significant growth. Prefers well-draining potting mix to prevent root rot. Will monitor before considering repot to 1-2 sizes larger pot.
