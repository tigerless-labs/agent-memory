Exploring Zapier for further workflow optimization. Interested in specific automation patterns:
- Creating project management tasks (Trello) from email triggers (Gmail) with specific subject lines
- Automating follow-up emails to clients after set periods (using Gmail delay)
- Creating tasks when clients submit web forms (Google Forms → Trello)
- Automating social media posting (Buffer → Facebook)
- Syncing CRM data (Stripe → HubSpot) and notifying team of new leads (Forms → Slack)

Views Zapier as advantageous over Microsoft Power Automate for its broader third-party integrations and ease of use for non-technical users, despite Power Automate's deeper Microsoft product integration.
