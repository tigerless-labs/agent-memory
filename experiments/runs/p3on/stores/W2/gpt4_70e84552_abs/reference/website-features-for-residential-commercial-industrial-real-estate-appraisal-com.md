Modern real estate appraisal company websites should include:

1. **Clean, professional design** - Brand-aligned, easy navigation
2. **Responsive design** - Optimized for mobile and various screen sizes
3. **Services page** - Residential, commercial, industrial appraisals, inspections, market analysis
4. **About us page** - Company history, values, team, industry experience
5. **Testimonials section** - Client testimonials for social proof and credibility
6. **Contact page** - Phone, email, contact form
7. **Online ordering system** - Streamlines client requests for appraisals/services
8. **Blog** - Establishes expertise, provides valuable industry information
9. **Property search tools** - Search by location, price range, property type
10. **Industry news/updates** - Demonstrates expertise and market knowledge

These features support both client acquisition and service delivery for appraisal firms.
