Professional appraisers evaluate properties using these key factors:

1. **Location** - Proximity to schools, parks, public transportation, shopping, amenities
2. **Size and layout** - Number of bedrooms/bathrooms, square footage, overall layout
3. **Condition** - Age of property, repairs/renovations made, overall condition
4. **Comparable sales** - Recent sales of similar properties (sales comparison approach)
5. **Income potential** - For commercial properties: rental income or business revenue potential
6. **Zoning** - Current and potential future zoning restrictions
7. **Environmental factors** - Contamination, proximity to hazardous waste sites
8. **Market trends** - Supply/demand, interest rates, economic factors
9. **Unique features** - Swimming pools, views, or other distinguishing characteristics
10. **Legal issues** - Pending litigation, easements, or other use restrictions

These factors apply across residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
