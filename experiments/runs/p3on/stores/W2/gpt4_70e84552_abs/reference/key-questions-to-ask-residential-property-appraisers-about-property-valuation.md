When consulting with property appraisers, relevant questions include:

**Valuation methodology:**
- What is the current market value?
- How did you determine market value?
- What factors were considered?
- What comparable properties were used?

**Property-specific factors:**
- How does property condition impact value?
- How does location impact value?
- How does size impact value?
- How does age impact value?
- How does property type (single-family, townhouse, condo) impact value?
- Are there unique features or characteristics affecting value?

**Market and external factors:**
- How does current real estate market impact value?
- What market trends apply, and how do they affect this property?
- Are there external factors (zoning, environmental issues) impacting value?

**Improvement and valuation updates:**
- What improvements/renovations could increase value?
- How often should the property be appraised for accurate, up-to-date value?

These questions help clients understand appraisal methodology and value drivers.
