When marketing training workshops (e.g., E&M coding), create a series of social media posts with different angles:

**Five post types:**
1. Teaser announcing workshop and key topics
2. Historical perspective (e.g., evolution of methodology)
3. Deep dive on specific practical topic
4. Real-world application examples
5. FAQ addressing trainee concerns

**Key finding:** Emotional, benefit-focused versions significantly outperform straightforward promotional posts. Frame posts around:
- Solving pain points ("tired of feeling unsure")
- Empowerment ("take control of your career")
- Progress ("transform your skills")
- Confidence-building ("gain the confidence you need")
- Recognition ("get the recognition you deserve")

Each post should include a clear call-to-action (register link or promotional code) and relevant hashtags.
