## Recipe: Korean-Style BBQ Chicken\n\nMarinate chicken breasts in:\n- Soy sauce\n- Garlic\n- Ginger\n- Brown sugar\n- Gochujang\n\nGrill or pan-fry and serve with kimchi and steamed rice.\n\n## Recommended Side Dishes\n\n- **Kimchi**: Spicy, fermented; adds crunch and umami\n- **Japchae**: Sweet potato starch noodles with beef and vegetables\n- **Bokkeumbap**: Korean fried rice with vegetables and optional meat\n- **Sigeumchi**: Blanched spinach with garlic and sesame oil\n- **Gyeranjjim**: Steamed eggs with sesame oil and soy sauce\n\nGreat for meal prep; reheats well and stores 3-4 days in fridge or can be frozen.
