**Garmin Forerunner 245 Music**

A mid-range GPS running watch with music storage and route navigation capabilities.

**Core Specs:**
- Price: ~$300
- Storage: 3.6 GB internal
- Battery: 7 hours GPS mode + music playback
- Satellite Systems: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo

**Running Features:**
- GPS tracking for accurate distance and pace
- Heart rate monitoring (built-in)
- Supports runs up to 20 km
- Audio pace/distance alerts
- Lap tracking

**Music:**
- Storage: Up to 500 songs
- Formats: MP3, AAC, FLAC
- Transfer via USB/Garmin Express software
- Bluetooth audio streaming to headphones
- Max file size: 10 MB per song
- Up to 10 playlists

**Routes:**
- Create/follow up to 100 courses
- Max 10,000 waypoints per course
- Turn-by-turn navigation
- Off-course alerts
- Good for 6-8 km lake runs with 10-20 waypoints

**Advantages:** Good balance of features and price for recreational runners tracking distance, pace, and music.
