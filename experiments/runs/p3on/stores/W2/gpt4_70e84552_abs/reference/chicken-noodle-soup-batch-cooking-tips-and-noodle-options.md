## Batch Cooking Tips\n\n- Use a large pot, slow cooker, or Instant Pot\n- Vegetables: carrots, celery, onions\n- Protein: boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs\n- Aromatics: garlic, ginger, bay leaves\n- Storage: 3-4 months frozen, 5-7 days refrigerated\n\n## Noodle Options\n\n1. **Egg Noodles**: Classic, traditional; soft and tender; absorb broth flavors well\n2. **Rice Noodles**: Gluten-free option; lighter, more delicate texture\n3. **Udon Noodles**: Thick and chewy, Japanese-style; hearty element\n4. **Homemade Noodles**: Custom texture, flavor, and shape\n\n## Reheating Toppings & Sides\n\n- Crusty bread or crackers\n- Grated cheese (cheddar, parmesan)\n- Sour cream or Greek yogurt\n- Fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro)\n- Side salad or fruit
