- Petting zoo (goats, sheep, etc.)
- Guided farm tours
- Farm-to-table section selling fresh produce and baked goods
- Demonstrations and workshops
- Kids activities and photobooth

- Enclosure design and animal safety for petting zoo
- Sanitation and hand-washing stations
- Staff/volunteer supervision
- Liability insurance coverage
- Weather contingency plan
- Parking and restroom facilities
