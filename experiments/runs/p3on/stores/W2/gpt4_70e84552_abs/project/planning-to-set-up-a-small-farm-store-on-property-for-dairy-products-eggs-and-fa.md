Future project to establish a farm store on the property.

**Products to sell:**
- Dairy products (milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, ice cream)
- Eggs (various sizes and types)
- Farm-fresh goods (produce, meats, baked goods)
- Value-added products (jams, honey, sauces)
- Possibly local partner products

**Key considerations discussed:**
- Accessible, visible location with parking
- Clear layout with signage and product categorization
- Attractive display cases and warm lighting
- POS system for efficient transactions
- Inventory management and staffing
- Health and safety standards compliance
- Potential for events, loyalty programs, and online presence
