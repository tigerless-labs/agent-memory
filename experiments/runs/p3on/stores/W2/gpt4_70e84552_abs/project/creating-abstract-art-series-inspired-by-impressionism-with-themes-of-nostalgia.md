In experimental phase creating a series of abstract pieces inspired by impressionist style with personal twist. Themes: nostalgia, memory, and impermanence. Incorporating vintage posters as symbols of lost/forgotten things, contrasting old and new elements. Techniques being explored: layering paint and ink, gel medium for texture and depth, decoupage of vintage posters onto canvas, using posters as bases for abstract work.
