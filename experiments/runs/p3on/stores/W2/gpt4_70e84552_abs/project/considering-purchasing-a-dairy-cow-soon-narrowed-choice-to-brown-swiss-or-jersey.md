Planning to purchase a dairy cow soon for dairy production and cheese-making at the farm.

**Breed decision:** Narrowed choice between:
- **Brown Swiss**: Gentle, hardy, adaptable; good for small-to-medium operations
- **Jersey**: High protein/calcium milk, rich and creamy; friendly and curious; often used for cheese production

**Current status:** Has been researching online and talking to other farmers in the area. Ready to make the investment soon.

**Intended products:** Will produce milk and cheese products to sell at farm open house event and future farm store.
