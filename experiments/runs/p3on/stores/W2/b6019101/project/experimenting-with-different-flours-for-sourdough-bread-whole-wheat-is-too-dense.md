Currently experimenting with different types of flour for sourdough bread baking:\n\n- **All-purpose flour (week of 2023-04-07)**: Turned out amazing, excellent results\n- **Whole wheat flour (week of 2023-04-14)**: Batch turned out too dense; need to adjust technique\n\nLearning that whole wheat flour absorbs more water, requires higher hydration rates, longer proofing, and may benefit from blending with all-purpose flour. All-purpose flour batches are currently the baseline for success.
