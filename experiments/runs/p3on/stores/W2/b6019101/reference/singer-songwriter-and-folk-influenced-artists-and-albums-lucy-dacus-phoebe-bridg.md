Lucy Dacus Albums: No Burden (2016), Historian (2018), Home Video (2021). Popular tracks: Night Shift, Addictions, Nonbeliever.

Phoebe Bridgers Albums: Stranger in the Alps (2017), Punisher (2020), Boygenius (2018), Better Oblivion Community Center (2019). Popular tracks: Scott Street, Killer, Motion Sickness, Funeral, Kyoto.

Julien Baker Albums: Sprained Ankle (2015), Turn Out the Lights (2017), Boygenius (2018). Popular tracks: Appointments, Turn Out the Lights, Rejoice, Souvenir, Hurt Less.

Other Similar Artists: Bon Iver, Fleet Foxes, Courtney Marie Andrews, Vagabon, Adrianne Lenker (Big Thief), Sharon Van Etten, The Tallest Man on Earth.

Note: Boygenius (2018) is a collaborative EP featuring all three artists (Phoebe Bridgers, Julien Baker, and Lucy Dacus).
