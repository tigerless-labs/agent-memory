- **International Campaign for Tibet (ICT)**: Promotes human rights and democracy for Tibetans in China; works with policymakers and grassroots supporters\n- **Human Rights Watch (HRW)**: International NGO investigating human rights abuses worldwide, including labor rights violations\n- **Free Tibet**: Non-profit promoting human rights of Tibetans in China and cultural preservation\n- **China Labor Watch (CLW)**: Improves working conditions and labor rights in China through investigations and supply chain advocacy\n- **Fair Trade USA**: Promotes ethical practices in international trade, working with producers on sustainability and social justice\n\nThese were recommended as effective alternatives to blanket boycotts.
