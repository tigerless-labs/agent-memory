**Additional refugee job search resources mentioned:**

**International Rescue Committee (IRC) Job Readiness Program**
- Job training, resume building, job placement services for refugees
- Local office locations available nationwide

**Local Refugee Resettlement Agencies**
- Catholic Charities
- Lutheran Social Services
- Offer job placement services and career counseling
- Vary by location

**Online Resources:**
- **Idealist**: Job board with refugee-friendly postings
- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking and job board
- **Indeed**: Job postings, some refugee-friendly
- **Resume-io, Resume Genius, Resume Companion**: Online resume review platforms (some charge fees)

**Volunteer Organizations:**
- **SCORE**: Free business and career coaching
- **MicroMentor**: Free business and career coaching

**Employer Programs:**
- Some companies like Chobani and Starbucks have programs specifically designed to hire and support refugees

**Education Support:**
- Career counseling services at local colleges and universities often offer free resume review and job search assistance
- Professional associations in specific fields may offer resume review and job search support
