## Lesser-known filmmakers with unique styles and visions:

1. **Anna Rose Holmer** — Known for coming-of-age drama "The Fits," exploring identity and conformity through a young female boxer.

2. **Barry Jenkins** — Known for "Moonlight" and "If Beale Street Could Talk," exploring themes of race, sexuality, and family.

3. **Chloé Zhao** — Known for soulful character explorations in "The Rider" and "Nomadland," with poetic visual style.

4. **Céline Sciamma** — Known for emotionally rich dramas: "Portrait of a Lady on Fire" and "Water Lilies," exploring desire, identity, and coming-of-age.

5. **Robert Eggers** — Known for horror films "The Witch" and "The Lighthouse," featuring distinct visual style and exploring isolation, madness, and the supernatural.
