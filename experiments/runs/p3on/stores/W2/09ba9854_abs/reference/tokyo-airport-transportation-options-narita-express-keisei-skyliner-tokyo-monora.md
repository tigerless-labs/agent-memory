**From Narita Airport (NRT):**
- N'EX (Narita Express): 60 min, 3000-4000 yen → Tokyo Station
- Keisei Skyliner: 45 min, 2500-3000 yen → Ueno Station  
- Airport Limousine Bus: 1 hour, 2000-3000 yen → various Tokyo locations
- Taxi: 1-2 hours, 15,000-20,000 yen

**From Haneda Airport (HND):**
- Tokyo Monorail: 15 min, 500-600 yen → Hamamatsucho Station
- Keikyu Railway: 30-40 min, 400-600 yen → Shinbashi Station
- Airport Limousine Bus: 30-60 min, 1000-2000 yen → various Tokyo locations
- Taxi: 30-60 min, 6000-10,000 yen

**Tips:**
- Suica/Pasmo prepaid cards work on trains, buses, subways throughout Tokyo
- For large luggage, bus or taxi preferred over train (stations crowded, limited storage)
- Consider advance booking for private car services for potential discounts
- Hotel staff often have partnerships with transportation providers for discounts
- Late night surcharges apply to taxis (10 PM - 5 AM)
