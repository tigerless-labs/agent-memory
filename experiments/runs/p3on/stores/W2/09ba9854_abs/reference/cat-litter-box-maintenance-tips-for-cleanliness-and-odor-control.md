Best practices for keeping a cat litter box clean and fresh:

1. **Daily scooping**: Remove solid waste and clumps daily to prevent bacteria growth
2. **Litter changes**: Replace litter completely every 7-10 days even with daily scooping
3. **Litter selection**: Choose high-quality, clumping litter designed to control odor and moisture
4. **Box cleaning**: Wash with mild detergent and warm water every 1-2 weeks, rinse thoroughly
5. **Disinfection**: Use 1 part white vinegar + 9 parts water solution, let sit 10-15 minutes before rinsing
6. **Odor control**: Sprinkle thin layer of baking soda at bottom of box or mix into litter
7. **Location**: Place in well-ventilated area away from food/water bowls and high humidity
8. **Monitor behavior**: Watch for signs cat is avoiding the litter box, which indicates cleaning needs
