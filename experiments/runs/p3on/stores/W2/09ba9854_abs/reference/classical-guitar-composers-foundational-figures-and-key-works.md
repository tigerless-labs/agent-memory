Francisco Tárrega (Spain): *Recuerdos de la Alhambra*, *Capricho Árabe*. Johann Sebastian Bach: lute and keyboard pieces adapted for guitar. Fernando Sor (Spain, early 19th c.): numerous works including operas and symphonies. Heitor Villa-Lobos (Brazil): Brazilian rhythms and folk melodies; wrote widely-used études. Mauro Giuliani (Italy): prolific in sonatas, concertos, études; collaborated with Paganini. Popular pieces: *Romance* (anonymous), *Asturias* (Albéniz), *Prelude No. 1* (Villa-Lobos). Modern composers: Leo Brouwer, Roland Dyens, Carlo Domeniconi.
