**Ingredients:**

*Dough:*
- 1 cup active, bubbly sourdough starter
- 2 cups bread flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

*Chocolate Filling:*
- 1 cup dark chocolate chips (at least 60% cocoa)
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

**Instructions:**
1. Mix dough ingredients until shaggy dough forms
2. Knead 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic
3. Proof in oiled bowl, covered, at room temperature 2-3 hours until doubled
4. Prepare filling: melt chocolate (microwave or double boiler), stir in softened butter, sugar, and vanilla until smooth
5. Punch down proofed dough and roll into large rectangle (1/4-inch thick)
6. Spread chocolate filling evenly over dough, leaving 1-inch border
7. Roll dough into tight log starting from long side
8. Fold log in half lengthwise (like folding a letter) and press edges to seal
9. Place in greased loaf pan, cover with plastic wrap
10. Proof at room temperature 1-2 hours until puffed
11. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
12. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden brown
13. Cool in pan 10 minutes
14. Optional: drizzle with glaze (powdered sugar mixed with milk)
15. Transfer to wire rack to cool completely

**Tips:**
- Use high-quality dark chocolate with 60%+ cocoa minimum
- Chill dough and filling 30 minutes before rolling/folding to firm up
- Mix dough and filling gently—don't overmix
- Be gentle when folding to avoid crushing ingredients
- Babka can be finicky; be patient with your first attempt
- Variations: add chopped nuts or dried fruit to filling
- Flavor experiments: pinch of salt, espresso powder (1 tsp), or liqueur (1 tbsp) in dough or filling
