## Key Traditional Buildings

**Alvar Aalto designs:**
- Paimio Sanatorium (early 20th century) – curved wooden facade
- Church of Three Crosses (1958, Imatra) – wooden interior
- Finnish Pavilion at Paris World Expo (1937)

**Heritage:**
- Suomenlinna Sea Fortress (UNESCO, off Helsinki, mid-18th century)

## Regional Styles

- **Savonia** (E Finland): wooden church villages, 17th–18th centuries
- **Karelia** (E Finland): traditional farmsteads, centuries of wood tradition
- **Northern Finland**: Sami wooden architecture
- **Cities**: Helsinki, Turku, Rauma (preserved historic neighborhoods)

## Modern (sustainable approach)

- Helsinki Central Library Oodi (2018, Ala) – Finnish spruce, green roof
- Kilden Performing Arts Center (Ala) – sourced pine facade
- Kesäpuisto Chapel (Oopeaa) – wooden 'cloak' design
- Helsinki University Think Corner (JKMM) – wood panels, geothermal
- Metsä Pavilion (Tokyo 2020, Helin & Co) – Kerto® LVL structure

## Nordic Comparison

Sweden/Norway: similar wood traditions. Iceland: turf houses. Estonia/Latvia: carved wooden details.
