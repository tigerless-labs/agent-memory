**Upwardly Global** — https://www.upwardlyglobal.org

Nonprofit providing job search assistance and career coaching to refugees and immigrants in the US.

**Services:**
- Resume and cover letter review
- Interview preparation and practice
- Job matching and placement
- Career coaching (one-on-one sessions)
- Career assessments and skills evaluation
- Online courses and certifications in IT, healthcare, finance, business, and digital marketing
- English language training and accent reduction
- Soft skills development
- Job fairs and networking events
- Industry-specific panels and mentorship opportunities
- Webinars and workshops

**Eligibility:** Refugees and immigrants authorized to work in the US with a high school diploma or equivalent, actively seeking employment.

**Course formats:** Self-paced, 1-6 months typical duration. Financial assistance and scholarships available for eligible applicants.
