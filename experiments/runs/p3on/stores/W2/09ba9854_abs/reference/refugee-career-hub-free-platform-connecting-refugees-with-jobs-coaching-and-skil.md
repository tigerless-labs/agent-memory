**Refugee Career Hub** — https://www.refugeecareerhub.org

Free online platform connecting refugees with job opportunities, career coaching, and skill-building resources.

**Services:**
- Job postings across various industries
- Entry-level positions (customer service, warehouse, food service)
- Skilled positions (software developers, nurses, accountants)
- Internship opportunities
- Remote work options
- Career coaching (one-on-one sessions)
- Skill-building resources: online courses, webinars, workshops
- Job readiness training: resume building, interview preparation, job search strategies
- Networking events (in-person and online)
- Resume review services

**Eligibility:** Resettled refugees in the US authorized to work (with valid work permit or green card), actively seeking employment.

**Registration:** Sign up at website → select 'Refugee' as registration type → provide personal information → verify email → complete profile with work experience, education, skills, language proficiency.
