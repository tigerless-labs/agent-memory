Popular flea and tick prevention medications for cats, all available OTC:

- **Frontline Plus**: Topical treatment, kills fleas/ticks/chewing lice, ~$30-35 for 3-month supply
- **Advantage II**: Topical treatment, kills fleas/ticks/chewing lice, fast-acting, ~$30-35 for 3-month supply
- **Seresto Flea and Tick Collar**: Odorless collar, provides 8 months protection, no monthly applications, ~$30-35 per collar
- **Capstar**: Oral medication (tablet), kills adult fleas/ticks, monthly application, ~$25-30 for 3-month supply
- **Cheristin**: Topical treatment, kills fleas/ticks including American dog tick, gentle non-greasy formula, ~$30-35 for 3-month supply

All are safe for cats. Always consult veterinarian before starting new medication.
