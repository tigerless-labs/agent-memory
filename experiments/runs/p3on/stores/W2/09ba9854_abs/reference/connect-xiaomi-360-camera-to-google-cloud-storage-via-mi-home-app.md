Steps to connect Xiaomi 360 camera to Google Cloud Storage: (1) Create a bucket in Google Cloud Storage Console; (2) Install Mi Home app and sign in with Xiaomi account; (3) Connect camera to Wi-Fi and Mi Home app; (4) Enable Cloud Storage in camera settings and sign in with Google account; (5) Configure bucket and recording settings; (6) Camera will automatically record and upload to GCS bucket. Requires Google Cloud Platform account; may have associated costs.
