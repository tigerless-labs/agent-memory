Uses five social media platforms regularly:

- **Facebook**: Engages with hobby-related groups; follows and posts
- **Instagram**: Keeps up with friends and family abroad; follows ~150 accounts; scrolls feed daily
- **Twitter**: Follows news and trends; scrolls at least 1 hour daily
- **LinkedIn**: For professional networking
- **WhatsApp**: For personal and work communication

Struggles to stick to daily time limits, especially on Twitter. Has set daily time limits on social media apps but regularly exceeds them.
