Specific sourdough recipes interested in trying:

**Fig and Rosemary Sourdough:**
- Rehydrate dried figs in water, orange juice, or honey water for 30+ minutes
- Chop rehydrated figs into small pieces for even distribution
- Combine with finely chopped fresh rosemary
- Mix into dough during autolyse or first fold
- Enhance with honey and orange zest

**Cranberry Orange Pecan Sourdough:**
- Rehydrate dried cranberries in water, orange juice, or apple cider for 30+ minutes
- Combine with chopped toasted pecans
- Add freshly grated orange zest (apply during final shaping or before baking)
- Mix into dough during autolyse or first fold
- Use blend of all-purpose and whole wheat flours

**Sourdough Chocolate Babka:**
- Rich, buttery babka with dark chocolate (60%+ cocoa) filling
- Features crumbly streusel topping
- Made with active sourdough starter
