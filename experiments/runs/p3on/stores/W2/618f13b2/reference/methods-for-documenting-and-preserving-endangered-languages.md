Key approaches to documenting and preserving endangered languages include: language documentation (recording audio/video of native speakers, creating dictionaries and grammars, transcribing oral stories); language revitalization programs (teaching to new learners, supporting daily use, promoting cultural pride); digital preservation (digital archives, mobile apps, online learning resources); language immersion schools (supportive environments with cultural activities); and community-driven efforts (preserving heritage and traditional practices). Success requires close collaboration with language speakers and communities.
