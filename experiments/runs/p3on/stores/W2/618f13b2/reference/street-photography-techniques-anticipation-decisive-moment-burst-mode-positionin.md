Key techniques for street photography discussed:\n\n- **Decisive Moment**: Henri Cartier-Bresson's concept of capturing the peak moment of action or expression\n- **Anticipation**: Study behavior patterns, anticipate movements and moments before they happen\n- **Burst Mode**: Use continuous shooting to increase chances of capturing perfect expressions\n- **Positioning**: Shoot from hip/chest level to remain discreet; find unique vantage points like staircases and escalators\n- **Low Profile**: Blend in with neutral clothing, know equipment settings, keep camera unobtrusive\n- **Focus on Eyes**: Eyes convey emotion and depth in candid shots\n- **Lighting**: Soft natural light reveals facial subtleties; avoid harsh midday sun\n- **Rapport Building**: Show genuine interest, be respectful, introduce yourself, be prepared to share work\n- **Respect & Ethics**: Always respect privacy boundaries, decline requests, people's discomfort, and cultural differences
