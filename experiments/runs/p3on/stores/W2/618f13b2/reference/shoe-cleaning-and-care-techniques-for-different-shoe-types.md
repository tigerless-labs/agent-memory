## Converse Chuck Taylors\n- Use soft-bristled brush to remove dirt\n- Mild soap and warm water (diluted dish soap)\n- Wipe with damp cloth, wring out thoroughly\n- Air dry at room temperature (no direct sunlight)\n- Consider rubber conditioner for toe cap\n- Optional: waterproofing spray for protection\n\n## Leather Boots\n- Regular conditioning with leather conditioner or oil\n- Waterproofing spray or cream\n- Clean with soft dry cloth regularly\n- Store in cool dry place with shoe trees\n- Avoid extreme temperatures\n- Leather protector on high-wear areas (toes, heels)\n- Professional maintenance by cobbler for polishing\n\n## Adidas Superstars (white sneakers)\n- Remove loose dirt with soft-bristled brush\n- Mix 1 tbsp mild soap with 1 cup warm water OR equal parts water and white vinegar\n- Use microfiber cloth, wring out thoroughly\n- Gentle scrubbing for scuff marks\n- Rinse with clean water\n- Blot with dry cloth\n- Air dry at room temperature\n- Consider sneaker cleaner products like Jason Markk for tough stains\n- Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners\n- Fabric protector or waterproofing spray for prevention
