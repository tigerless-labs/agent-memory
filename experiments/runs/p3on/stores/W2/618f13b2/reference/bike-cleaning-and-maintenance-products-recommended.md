Bike wash soaps: Finish Line, Pedro's, Simple Green, Muc-Off. Wheel cleaners: Pedro's Wheel Cleaner, Finish Line Wheel Cleaner, Muc-Off Wheel Cleaner. Chain lubes: Squirt Long Lasting, Finish Line Wet Lube, Pedro's Chain Lube, CeramicSpeed UFO, Muc-Off Ceramic. Waterproofing sprays: WD-40 Bike Water Repellent, Finish Line Waterproofing, Muc-Off Waterproofing. Key tips: use pH-neutral soap, soft-bristled brushes, microfiber cloths, test on inconspicuous areas first, dry thoroughly after cleaning.
