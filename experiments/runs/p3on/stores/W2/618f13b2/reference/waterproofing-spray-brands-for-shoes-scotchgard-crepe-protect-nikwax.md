**Waterproofing spray options:**
- Scotchgard — well-known brand with range of waterproofing sprays for different materials
- Crepe Protect — popular for suede and nubuck shoes
- Nikwax — water-based spray suitable for canvas and suede shoes

**When applying waterproofing spray:**
- Choose spray designed for the material (canvas, suede, nubuck)
- Check brand compatibility and customer reviews
- Always follow manufacturer instructions
- Test on small, inconspicuous area first before full application
- Consider impact on breathability and appearance
