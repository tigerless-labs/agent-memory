**General shoe cleaning kits:**
- Saphir Medaille d'Or — high-end, premium leather option
- Kiwi Shoe Care — well-known brand with range of kits for different shoe types
- Jason Markk Shoe Cleaner — popular, eco-friendly option for various shoe types
- Dr. Martens Shoe Care — for Dr. Martens or similar heavy-duty shoes

**Leather-specific cleaning kits:**
- Saphir — high-quality, gentle products for leather shoes
- Kiwi — offers kits for suede and nubuck
- Lexol — popular for leather cleaning and conditioning

When choosing, consider: type of material (canvas, suede, nubuck), kit contents (cleaner, conditioner, protector, tools), brand reputation, and always test on inconspicuous area first.
