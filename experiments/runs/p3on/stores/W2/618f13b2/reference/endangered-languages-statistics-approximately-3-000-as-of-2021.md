Approximately 3,000 languages are classified as endangered (as of 2021). Classification criteria include: number of remaining speakers, age of speakers (elderly speakers at higher risk), intergenerational transmission (whether parents pass language to children), language prestige relative to dominant languages, and political/economic factors like war, displacement, and globalization.
