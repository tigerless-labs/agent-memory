Handbag options and brands recommended for classic, elegant style with neutral color preference:

**Top Recommendations (USD 200-300 budget):**
- **Coach Parker**: USD 250-300 - Sophisticated daily driver, available in neutral colors
- **Kate Spade Cameron**: USD 200-250 - Stylish, functional, neutral palette, organized pockets
- **Tory Burch Robinson**: USD 250-300 - Chic, understated, magnetic closure, interior pockets
- **Longchamp Le Pliage**: USD 200-250 - Classic design, known for durability and versatility
- **Michael Kors Jet Set**: USD 200-250 - Sophisticated, multiple pockets for organization

**Other Brands to Consider:**
- Frye (high-quality leather, classic designs)
- Vince Camuto (sophisticated, neutral-focused)
- Rebecca Minkoff (fashionable, functional, classic twist)

**Services Available:**
- Monogramming: Both Coach and Tory Burch offer (USD 10-30, 2-3 weeks)
- Warranties: Coach 1-year plus optional extension; Tory Burch 2-year plus optional extension
- Gift wrapping: Available at most retailers (free to USD 10)
- Protection plans: Available for accidental damage (USD 20-50)

**Style categories:** totes, crossbody, satchels, backpacks depending on use.
