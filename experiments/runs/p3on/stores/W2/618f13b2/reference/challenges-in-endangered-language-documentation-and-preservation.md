Major challenges in documenting and preserving endangered languages include: limited resources (funding, expertise, time); lack of written materials in many languages; social and cultural factors (low perceived status, difficulty accessing some communities); language attrition (few or no native speakers, incomplete command); and cultural sensitivity concerns (sacred practices, risk of misappropriation). Some languages face unique challenges: complex grammar, difficult transcription, or specific community needs that require tailored approaches.
