## Korean-Style BBQ Marinade Recipe

For 1-2 pounds of meat:

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons gochujang
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) (optional)
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar (optional)
- Black pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Blend all ingredients until smooth
2. Taste and adjust to preference
3. Place meat in ziplock bag or shallow dish
4. Pour marinade over meat and massage for even coating
5. Refrigerate minimum 2 hours or overnight (6-8 hours recommended)

**Variations:**
- Adjust heat: add/reduce gochugaru
- Add aromatics: sliced onions, green onions, or grated carrots
- Sweetener options: honey, maple syrup, or Korean rice syrup
- Umami additions: fish sauce, doenjang (soybean paste), mushroom ingredients
- Extended marinating: 24-48 hours for more intense flavors

**Common styles:** Bulgogi (sweet/savory soy-sugar focused), Galbi (spicy/savory gochujang-garlic focused), Dak galbi (spicy/sweet gochujang-sugar focused)
