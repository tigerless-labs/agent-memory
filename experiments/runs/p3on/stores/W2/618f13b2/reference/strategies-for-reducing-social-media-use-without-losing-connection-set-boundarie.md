Recommendations for limiting social media while staying connected to friends:\n\n**Boundary-setting strategies:**\n- Decide on specific times to check social media (e.g., once daily)\n- Use website blockers or apps (Freedom, SelfControl, StayFocusd)\n- Remove social media apps from phone home screen\n- Designate phone-free zones/times (meals, conversations)\n- Unfollow or mute accounts that trigger negative feelings or FOMO\n- Create a social media-free day weekly or monthly\n\n**Combat FOMO:**\n- Reframe thinking: "I'm choosing to focus on my priorities" instead of "I'm missing out"\n- Recognize that social media is a highlight reel, not reality\n- Focus on building meaningful in-person relationships\n\n**Stay connected without social media:**\n- Phone calls and video calls (scheduled or spontaneous)\n- Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage) for more personal communication\n- Meetups and outings (coffee dates, walks, restaurants)\n- Email newsletters or creating newsletters\n- Snail mail (letters, postcards, care packages)\n- Group chats and forums around shared interests\n- Game nights and potlucks\n- Volunteer work together
