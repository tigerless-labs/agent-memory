Prefers Adobe Lightroom for managing photos. Organizes using:
- Hierarchical folder structure by trip/date
- Collections and Smart Collections for categorizing photos (landscapes, wildlife, flora, etc.)
- Keywords and tags for searchability
- Star ratings and flags to identify favorites
- Develop module for editing: exposure, contrast, highlights/shadows, white balance, tone curve, lens corrections

Already comfortable with Adobe ecosystem.
