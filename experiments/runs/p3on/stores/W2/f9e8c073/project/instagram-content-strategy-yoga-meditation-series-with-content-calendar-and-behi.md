User is building an Instagram presence focused on yoga and meditation content. Strategy includes:

- Creating a content calendar for consistency (aiming for 3-5 posts/week)
- Focusing on yoga/meditation series with morning routines, tutorials, and meditation guides
- Incorporating behind-the-scenes content to build authenticity and connection
- Sharing personal stories and challenges related to yoga/meditation journey
- Using relevant hashtags (#yogaeverydamnday, #meditation, #mindfulness, #yogalife, #wellness)
- Engaging with followers through comments, questions, and community building

Preference: authentic, personal, relatable content that shows vulnerability and real creative process.
