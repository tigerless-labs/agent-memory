Looking to buy a peridot necklace to mark an upcoming anniversary. Shopping strategy: check online retailers (Blue Nile, James Allen, Brian Gavin Diamonds) and local jewelry stores. Quality criteria: vibrant green color, minimal inclusions, good cut, metal type that complements skin tone (white gold, yellow gold, silver), GIA or ICA certification. Consider design and setting matching personal style.
