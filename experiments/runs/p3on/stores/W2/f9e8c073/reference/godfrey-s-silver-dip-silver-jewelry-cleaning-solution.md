Godfrey's Silver Dip is an easy-to-use solution for removing tarnish from silver jewelry. How to use: dip jewelry in solution, rinse with warm water, dry with soft cloth. Also considered: Weiman Silver Jewelry Cream, Hagerty Silver Foam, Connoisseurs Silver Jewelry Cleaner. DIY alternative: baking soda paste (1 tablespoon baking soda + 2 tablespoons water, apply 15-30 minutes, rinse).
