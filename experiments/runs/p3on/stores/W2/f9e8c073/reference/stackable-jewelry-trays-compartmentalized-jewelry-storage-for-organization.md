Stackable Jewelry Trays (examples: Songmics, Whitmor Deluxe Stackable Jewelry Tray) organize jewelry by type with individual compartments for earrings, rings, necklaces. Key features: velvet or soft fabric lining to prevent scratching, stackable design for space efficiency. Also available: jewelry armoires, anti-tarnish jewelry boxes (Stackers, Mele & Co), travel cases, over-the-door organizers. Look for high-quality materials, good compartmentalization, and size matching collection.
