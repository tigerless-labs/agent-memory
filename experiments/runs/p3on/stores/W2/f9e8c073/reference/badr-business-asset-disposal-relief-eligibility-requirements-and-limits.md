## Eligibility Requirements\n1. Company must be a trading company or holding company of a trading group\n2. Shareholder must hold at least 5% of ordinary shares and voting rights for at least 24 months before disposal\n3. Shareholder must be an officer or employee of company (or its trading group) for at least 24 months before disposal\n\n## Key Provisions\n- £1 million lifetime limit per INDIVIDUAL shareholder for reduced CGT rate (10% vs standard rate)\n- Limit is per individual, not company-wide; each shareholder has own separate allowance\n- Individual can dispose of shares even while business continues trading\n- Company secretary role qualifies as an officer for BADR purposes\n- Consultation with qualified tax advisor required for individual circumstances
