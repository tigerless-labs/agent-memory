Has a collection of silver jewelry including a silver chain. Pieces are prone to tarnishing. Jewelry care plan: clean with Godfrey's Silver Dip, organize with Stackable Jewelry Trays, store in cool/dry place with anti-tarnish lining, polish regularly with silver polishing cloths. General care: dry thoroughly after cleaning, avoid harsh chemicals/hot water, keep away from direct sunlight/chlorine/bleach.
