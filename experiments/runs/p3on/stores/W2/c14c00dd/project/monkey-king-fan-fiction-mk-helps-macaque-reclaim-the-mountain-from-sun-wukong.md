Working on a creative narrative featuring MK (Sun Wukong's son), Mei, Tang, and Macaque.

**Main plot:** MK escapes his father's control and helps the imprisoned King Macaque escape and plan to reclaim their mountain kingdom from the invading golden-haired monkeys led by Sun Wukong.

**Key world elements:**
- The mountain had a council of elders and lived in peace/harmony before invasion
- Golden-haired monkeys invaded and enslaved the original inhabitants
- Macaque was imprisoned and tortured for centuries
- A traitor among the original monkeys allied with the invaders
- MK has learned magic from his father, including fire spells
- The four protagonists establish a jungle campsite to plan their strategy

**Scenes written:**
- MK finding the others in the jungle
- Setting up campsite; Macaque tells stories of pre-invasion mountain
- Mei addresses Macaque as 'Your Majesty'; Macaque asks not to use titles until he's king again
- MK has a nightmare where Wukong finds the campsite and threatens his friends

**Character dynamics:**
- MK torn between loyalty to father and doing what's right
- Macaque determined to reclaim his kingdom despite weakness
- Mei and Tang supportive, eager to help restore the mountain
