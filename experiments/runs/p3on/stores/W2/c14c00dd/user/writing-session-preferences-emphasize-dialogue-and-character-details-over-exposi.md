When developing creative writing scenes:

- **Emphasize storytelling and dialogue** over abstract exposition
  - Instead of summarizing Macaque's history, have him tell stories around the campfire with vivid details
  - Use character reactions and interactions to reveal world-building
  
- **Include detailed descriptions and sensory details**
  - Fire crackling, flickering shadows, physical states (Macaque's wounds, chains)
  - Emotion on faces and in dialogue
  
- **Favor character moments and conversations** over plot summary
  - Let scenes breathe; allow characters to interact naturally
  - Dialogue reveals personality, history, and relationships simultaneously

- **When asked for rewrites, focus on deepening what's already there** rather than adding new plot points
  - Example: rewrite scene with more emphasis on Macaque's stories rather than changing the campfire scene structure
