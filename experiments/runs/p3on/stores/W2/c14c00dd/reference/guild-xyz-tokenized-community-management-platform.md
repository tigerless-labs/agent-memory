Guild.xyz is a platform for creating private, tokenized communities (called 'guilds') across different communication platforms, management tools, and games.

**Key features:**
- Token-enabled membership levels with blockchain assets
- Cross-chain token gating support (1M+ tokens, 100K+ NFT projects, 10+ chains indexed)
- Web3 captcha for phishing protection and bot filtering
- Real-time query engine with built-in indexer
- Composable membership requirements with logic gates
- Guild creation is free and gasless
- Open-source frontend and platform connectors
- API/SDK available for developers
- No-code tool, user-friendly

**Popular example guilds:** ZetaChain (243K members), Arbitrum (227K), Galxe (91K), Sui Ecosystem, and others.

Used by DAOs and Web3 communities to manage membership and access control.
