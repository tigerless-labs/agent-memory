Laxmi is the Hindu goddess of wealth, prosperity, and good fortune. Her worship involves: Diwali (five-day festival with offerings), Laxmi Puja (prayer ceremony on Diwali with lamps, flowers, sweets, mantras), Havan (sacred fire ritual), Aarti (lamp waving ritual with devotional songs), and Vrat (fasting for devotion).

Lamps and candles symbolize light dispelling darkness, invite the goddess into homes, honor the fire element, and create spiritual atmosphere. Flowers (favorite: lotus, marigold, rose, hibiscus, jasmine, chrysanthemum) represent beauty, purity, and vitality, with fragrance purifying the atmosphere. Fasting is an act of devotion that purifies mind and body, develops self-discipline, facilitates spiritual awareness, and is believed to bring prosperity and abundance.
