Recommended tracking apps for fitness and nutrition goals:\n\n**Workout Tracking:**\n- MyFitnessPal: exercises, sets, reps, weight tracking with large database\n- Strava: popular for runs, rides, cardio activities with social features\n- Fitbit Coach: personalized workouts based on goals and fitness level\n\n**Meal/Nutrition Tracking:**\n- MyFitnessPal: also tracks daily food intake, macronutrients, calories\n- Lose It!: calorie tracking app with barcode scanning\n- YAZIO: nutrition app with macronutrient and calorie tracking\n\nUser interested in tracking progress to stay motivated and measure results.
