Key techniques for rich and tangy pasta sauce:\n\n- **Caramelize onions and garlic**: Cook until dark to develop deep, sweet-savory flavor and break down sugars\n- **Balance flavors**: Combine sweet (tomatoes), acidic (lemon juice, balsamic vinegar, red wine), and savory elements\n- **Layer flavors**: Add ingredients at different stages to allow flavors to meld\n- **Reduce and simmer**: Cook slowly over low heat to concentrate flavors\n- **Acidity adjustment**: Taste regularly and adjust with lemon juice or vinegar\n- **Add fat**: Use olive oil, butter, or pancetta for richness and velvety texture\n- **Use quality tomatoes**: San Marzano or cherry tomatoes for natural tanginess\n\nAllows 30+ minutes simmering time for flavors to develop.
