**Authors Similar to Asimov:**
- Arthur C. Clarke: 'Childhood's End' (1953), 'Rendezvous with Rama', '2001: A Space Odyssey'
- Ray Bradbury: 'Fahrenheit 451', 'Dandelion Wine', 'The Martian Chronicles'
- Greg Egan: 'Diaspora', 'Permutation City', 'Incandescence'
- Kim Stanley Robinson: 'Mars', '2312', 'Aurora'
- Alastair Reynolds: 'Revelation Space', 'Chasm City'

**Recommended Books:**
- 'Dune' by Frank Herbert
- 'The Three-Body Problem' by Liu Cixin
- 'The Expanse' series by James S. A. Corey
- 'The Culture' series by Iain M. Banks
- 'The Long Earth' series by Terry Pratchett and Stephen Baxter
- 'Ancillary Justice' by Ann Leckie
- 'Old Man's War' by John Scalzi
- 'The Collapsing Empire' by John Scalzi
