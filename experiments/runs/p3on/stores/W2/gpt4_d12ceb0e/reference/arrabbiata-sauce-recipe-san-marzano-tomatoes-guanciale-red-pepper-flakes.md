Classic Italian arrabbiata sauce recipe and techniques:\n\n**Key ingredients (per 2 cups San Marzano tomatoes):**\n- 6 slices guanciale or pancetta, diced\n- 3 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 small onion, finely chopped\n- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)\n- 2 tablespoons olive oil\n- Salt and pepper to taste\n- 1 tablespoon fresh basil\n- Optional: pinch of sugar to balance acidity\n\n**Method:**\n1. Cook guanciale/pancetta in olive oil until crispy\n2. Sauté garlic and onions in same pan until softened\n3. Add crushed San Marzano tomatoes, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper\n4. Return meat to sauce, combine\n5. Simmer 30+ minutes to meld flavors\n6. Taste and adjust seasoning\n7. Serve with fresh basil and parmesan\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use high-quality San Marzano tomatoes (sweeter, less acidic)\n- Guanciale preferred over pancetta for authentic flavor\n- Use Calabrian or Korean chili flakes for better quality\n- Can roast tomatoes beforehand for deeper flavor\n- Add baking soda if sauce is too acidic
