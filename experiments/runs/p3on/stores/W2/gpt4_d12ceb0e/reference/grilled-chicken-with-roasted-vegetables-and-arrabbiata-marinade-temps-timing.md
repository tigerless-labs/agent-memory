Technique for grilled chicken and roasted vegetables to pair with arrabbiata sauce:\n\n**Grilled Chicken:**\n- Marinade: olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, thyme, rosemary (30+ minutes)\n- Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400°F / 200°C)\n- Grill 5-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C)\n- Let rest few minutes before slicing thinly against the grain\n\n**Roasted Vegetables:**\n- Choose: zucchini, bell peppers, eggplant, cherry tomatoes\n- Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried oregano\n- Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 20-25 minutes until tender and lightly caramelized\n- Optional: brush with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, and honey glaze in last 10 minutes\n\n**Assembly with Arrabbiata:**\n1. Slice grilled chicken thinly\n2. Toss pasta with arrabbiata sauce\n3. Add sliced chicken and roasted vegetables\n4. Top with grated parmesan and fresh basil\n\nThis combination creates balanced, well-rounded pasta dish with multiple flavor and texture layers.
