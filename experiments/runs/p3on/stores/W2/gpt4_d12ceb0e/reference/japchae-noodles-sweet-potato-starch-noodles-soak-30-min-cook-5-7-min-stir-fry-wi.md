Guide to making japchae noodles with proper texture:\n\n**Choosing noodles:**\n- Look for 100% sweet potato starch noodles (dangmyeon)\n- Alternative: combination of sweet potato and potato starch\n- Avoid wheat flour-based noodles\n\n**Preparation:**\n1. Soak noodles in cold water for at least 30 minutes to rehydrate evenly\n2. Cook in boiling water for 5-7 minutes per package instructions\n3. Drain and rinse in cold water to stop cooking\n4. Stir-fry with 1-2 tablespoons oil to prevent sticking and develop chewy texture\n\n**Seasoning (basic blend):**\n- 2 tablespoons soy sauce\n- 1 tablespoon sugar\n- 1 teaspoon sesame oil (add near end to preserve aroma)\n- 1 teaspoon gochujang (optional, for depth)\n- Minced garlic and ginger\n\n**Additions for texture/flavor:**\n- Sautéed onions, garlic, ginger as aromatics\n- Vegetables: spinach, carrots, bell peppers (provide crunch)\n- Toasted sesame seeds, chopped nuts\n- Balance sweet and savory flavors\n- Sesame oil used sparingly to avoid overpowering
