Uses panko breadcrumbs and grated parmesan for chicken parmesan coating, experimenting with Italian seasoning to enhance flavor. Preferred ratio: 1 cup panko breadcrumbs, 1/2 cup grated parmesan, 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning (per pound of chicken), plus salt and pepper to taste. Prefers freshly grated parmesan for best results.
