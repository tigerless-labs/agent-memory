Believes AI development should be cautious and that risks of creating superintelligent AI are too great to ignore. Thinks AI safety and value alignment should be prioritized to ensure AI systems are designed with human well-being in mind. Supports active government regulation and oversight of AI development to set standards, protect citizens' rights, address job displacement, and ensure transparency and accountability.
