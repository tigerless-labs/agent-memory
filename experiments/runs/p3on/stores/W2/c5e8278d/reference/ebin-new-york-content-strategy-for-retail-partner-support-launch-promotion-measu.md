Comprehensive content strategy plan for Ebin New York to support retail partners:

**Launch Phase:**
- Product images and videos showcasing features/benefits
- Step-by-step how-to guides for in-store signage or packaging
- Clear, concise product descriptions highlighting features/benefits

**Promotion Phase:**
- Social media content (images, videos) for retail partner sharing
- Targeted social media ads driving traffic to partner websites/stores
- Email marketing campaigns with product images, descriptions, purchase links
- In-store signage promoting products and highlighting key benefits

**Measurement Phase:**
- Track sales before, during, and after campaign to determine impact
- Monitor social media engagement (likes, shares, comments)
- Track email open and click-through rates to measure effectiveness
- Use data to make adjustments for future campaigns
