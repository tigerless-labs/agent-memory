## Foam Rolling Exercises

All exercises: roll slowly and smoothly, 30-60 sec per area, 2-3 times. Start with light pressure.

### IT Band Roll
- Position: Side-lying with foam roller just above knee
- Roll from knee toward hip, applying moderate pressure
- Focus: Outside of thigh where IT band is located
- Duration: 30-60 sec, 2-3 times

### Lateral Leg Roll  
- Position: Side-lying with foam roller just above ankle
- Roll toward knee, applying moderate pressure
- Focus: Outside of leg (IT band and lateral muscles)
- Duration: 30-60 sec, 2-3 times

### Hip Flexor Roll
- Position: Kneeling with foam roller under hip flexor (front of hip)
- Roll forward and backward
- Focus: Where hip flexor meets quadriceps
- Duration: 30-60 sec, 2-3 times

### Piriformis Roll
- Position: Seated with foam roller under glutes
- Cross affected leg over opposite leg (ankle on knee)
- Roll under glutes, applying moderate pressure
- Focus: Piriformis muscle (under glutes)
- Duration: 30-60 sec, 2-3 times

## Targeted Stretches (Hold 30 sec each, 2-3 reps per side)

### IT Band Stretch
- Stand with affected side near wall
- Cross opposite leg over affected leg
- Bend knee and lean toward wall
- Feel stretch on outside of leg

### Hip Flexor Stretch (Kneeling)
- Position: All fours
- Bring one knee forward, foot flat on ground
- Lean forward, stretching front of hip
- Hold 30 sec per side

### Hip Flexor Stretch (Lying)
- Position: Lying on back, legs straight
- Lift affected leg toward ceiling, keep knee straight
- Hold 5 sec, repeat 10-15 times per leg

### Lateral Leg Stretch
- Stand, feet shoulder-width apart
- Step to one side, bend front knee, keep back straight
- Lower body down, stretching outside of leg
- Hold 30 sec per side

### Piriformis Stretch
- Sit on floor with affected leg crossed over other leg
- Pull crossed knee toward opposite shoulder
- Feel stretch in back of leg
- Hold 30 sec per side

**Tips:** Breathe deeply; don't bounce or force stretches; stop if experiencing pain; consistency is key for recovery.
