Three major redevelopment areas in Hull with mixed impacts:

**Fruit Market** — transformed into cultural, residential, and commercial hub; new apartments, galleries, bars and restaurants; attracted young professionals and students; created jobs.

**St. Stephen's** — large shopping center built on former Hull Royal Infirmary site; retail, food, and entertainment opportunities; generated jobs and boosted local economy.

**Humber Enterprise Park** — industrial estate on city outskirts; former industrial buildings converted to modern office spaces; created business opportunities and jobs.

**Mixed impacts**: new investment and job opportunities, but displaced some long-standing residents; rising house prices; complaints about homogenized character and negative effects on small local businesses.
