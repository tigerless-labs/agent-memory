For candles and soaps (visually appealing products):\n\n**Facebook**: Use high-quality visuals, product showcases, behind-the-scenes content, customer testimonials, seasonal offers, Facebook Live, and engagement through comments/messages.\n\n**Instagram**: Curate visually appealing feed with consistent aesthetic, use Stories for behind-the-scenes content, product showcases, carousel posts, relevant hashtags (5-10), Instagram Shopping feature.\n\n**Captions**: Keep conversational tone, include CTAs, use 2-5 hashtags on Facebook; keep brief on Instagram with 2-3 emojis and 5-10 hashtags.\n\n**Tools**: Planoly or Later for scheduling Instagram posts.
