Strategy plan for measuring in-store launch campaign effectiveness:

**Step 1: Define KPIs**
- Sales of new product during launch
- Foot traffic to store during campaign
- Customer engagement (trials, questions about product)

**Step 2: Set Baseline**
- Establish baseline measurements of sales, foot traffic, and engagement pre-campaign
- Enables comparison to determine campaign impact

**Step 3: Implement Campaign**
- Eye-catching in-store signage promoting new product
- Staff demonstrations and customer questions
- Free product samples

**Step 4: Measure Results**
- Sales analysis: compare campaign period to baseline
- Foot traffic analysis: count customers during campaign vs. baseline
- Engagement analysis: track customer trials and inquiries

**Step 5: Evaluate and Adjust**
- Assess signage messaging and visuals
- Adjust product demonstrations or sampling approach
- Target specific customer segments with personalized promotions
- Make data-driven adjustments for future campaigns
