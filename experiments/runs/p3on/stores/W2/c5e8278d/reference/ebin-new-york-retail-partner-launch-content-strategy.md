Content strategy for retail partners launching Ebin New York products:

**Key components:**
- Highlight key benefits (natural ingredients, long-lasting results, unique features)
- Showcase product features (demonstrations, how-to videos, signage)
- Provide product information (ingredients, instructions, pricing)
- Create social media content (images, videos, customer reviews, UGC)
- Incentivize purchase (discounts, promotions)
- Provide expert advice (workshops, tutorials, in-store demos)

**Channels:** In-store displays, social media, email marketing, product labels, online pages
