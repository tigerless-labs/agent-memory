## Recommended 5K Interval Training Workouts

### Fartlek Intervals
- Warm up: 10-15 min easy running
- Alternate 1-2 min fast running (5K pace) with 2-3 min active recovery
- Repeat for 20-30 min total
- Cool down: 10-15 min easy running

### 400m Interval Workout
- Warm up: 10-15 min easy running
- Run 400m at fast pace (1-2 min) + 200m jog recovery
- Repeat 3-5 sets
- Cool down: 10-15 min easy running

### 800m Interval Workout
- Warm up: 10-15 min easy running
- Run 800m at fast pace (2-3 min) + 400m jog recovery
- Repeat 2-3 sets
- Cool down: 10-15 min easy running

### Hill Repeats
- Find gradual incline (1-2 miles)
- Warm up: 10-15 min easy running
- Run uphill at high intensity (1-2 min) + jog/walk downhill recovery
- Repeat 3-5 sets
- Cool down: 10-15 min easy running

### Pyramid Intervals
- Warm up: 10-15 min
- Ascending: 200m → 400m → 600m at fast pace with equal recovery
- Descending: 400m → 200m at fast pace with recovery
- Cool down: 10-15 min

### Proportional Intervals
- Warm up: 10-15 min
- 1 min hard / 2 min easy → 2 min hard / 4 min easy → 3 min hard / 6 min easy
- Cool down: 10-15 min

**Tips:** Start with 1-2 workouts per week; warm up/cool down thoroughly; mix workouts to prevent plateaus.
