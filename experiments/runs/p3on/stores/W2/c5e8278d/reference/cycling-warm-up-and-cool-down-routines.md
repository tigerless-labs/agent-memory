## Pre-Ride Warm-Up (15-30 minutes)

### Light Cardio (5-10 min)
- Start with easy pedaling on stationary bike, trainer, or flat road
- Gradually increase heart rate and blood flow

### Dynamic Stretching (5-10 min)
- Leg swings: forward/backward, each leg
- Hip circles: clockwise and counterclockwise
- Knee lifts: toward chest, alternating legs
- Ankle rotations: circular motion, both directions

### Muscle Activation (5-10 min)
- Glute bridges: 3 sets, squeeze at top
- Calf raises: on edge of curb/step
- Core engagement: engage core while seated on bike

### Final Spin (5-10 min)
- Gradually increase to moderate pace
- Focus on pedaling technique, cadence, breathing

## Post-Ride Cool-Down (15-30 minutes)

### Easy Pedaling (5-10 min)
- Continue at easy pace to bring heart rate down gradually
- Light spinning helps flush waste products

### Static Stretching (10-15 min)
- Quad stretch: hold 15-30 sec each leg
- Hamstring stretch: lean forward reaching toes, hold 15-30 sec
- Hip flexor stretch: kneeling position, hold 15-30 sec per side
- Calf stretch: heel hanging off step, hold 15-30 sec per leg

### Foam Rolling (5-10 min)
- Focus on areas that feel tight: IT band, quads, hamstrings
- Apply moderate pressure, roll slowly and smoothly

### Hydration & Refueling (10-15 min)
- Drink water or sports drink immediately
- Consume carbs + protein within 30-60 minutes post-ride
- Warm bath/shower for muscle relaxation

**Recovery tips:** Prioritize rest days; use compression garments if needed; listen to body and adjust intensity accordingly.
