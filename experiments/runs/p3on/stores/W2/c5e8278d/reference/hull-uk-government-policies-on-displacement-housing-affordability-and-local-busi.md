Government measures to address redevelopment-related displacement and economic impacts:

**Addressing Resident Displacement:**
- Affordable housing requirements on all new developments (percentage mandated)
- Tenancy protection for long-term residents, especially in social housing; special eviction protections
- Community engagement in regeneration project planning and decision-making
- Compulsory purchase orders with compensation when needed for regeneration projects

**Ensuring Housing Remains Affordable:**
- Rent caps/limits on affordable housing to significantly undercut market rates
- Long-term leaseholds and contracts to prevent future rent increases
- Quality standards for minimum habitability (insulation, heating, living standards)
- Monitoring and enforcement to prevent landlord violations

**Supporting Local Businesses:**
- Business support programs (mentorship, training, funding)
- Start-up support programs (funding, space, resources)
- Local procurement policy encouraging public sector to buy from local businesses
- Grant schemes for small businesses meeting criteria (local hiring, sustainability)

**Concerns Noted:** Community raised concerns that "affordable" housing definitions may not serve lowest-income groups effectively.
