Working on a document chapter covering technology in South African road freight.

**Subtopics completed:**
1. How technology helps to optimize routes and reduce costs — focuses on GPS tracking for route planning, TMS systems for real-time data, EDI for streamlined communication
2. How technology facilitates compliance and regulatory requirements — covers EDI for regulatory submissions, GPS for location tracking/reporting, TMS integration with compliance systems
3. How technology improves visibility and communication in transportation management — emphasizes real-time monitoring via TMS and GPS, stakeholder communication (shippers, carriers, drivers, customers), enhanced customer experience
4. How technology can be integrated with other supply chain functions — covers integration with inventory management, order management, warehouse management, financial management, and CRM systems

**Key technologies covered across all sections:**
- Transportation Management Systems (TMS) — central software for managing transportation operations
- GPS tracking — real-time vehicle and shipment location monitoring
- Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) — data exchange between systems, regulatory submissions, and communication

**Geographic/industry focus:**
South African road freight transportation sector, with emphasis on practical applications for efficiency, cost reduction, compliance, and supply chain integration.
