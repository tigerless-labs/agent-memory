User plans and shops for winter clothing strategically. Priorities: waterproof jacket with breathable membrane (Gore-Tex or equivalent), insulated/fleece layers, waterproof pants, warm sweaters, moisture-wicking socks, gloves, hat/scarf in water-resistant materials, waterproof boots with grip. Favors quality over quantity, looks for thrift/second-hand options. Understands importance of layering for variable weather. This preference emerged when planning for Vancouver's wet, mild winters (3–8°C, 12–15 rainy days/month Nov–Mar).
