Field guides recommended for bird identification:

1. Birds of Costa Rica by Richard Garrigues and Robert Dean - comprehensive guide covering 850+ species with detailed descriptions, range maps, and illustrations; primary recommendation for Costa Rica trips

2. The Sibley Guide to Birds by David Allen Sibley - covers North and Central American birds including Costa Rica; valued for detailed illustrations, range maps, and identification tips

Mobile apps for field identification:

1. Merlin Bird ID (free) - developed by Cornell Lab of Ornithology; uses AI-powered identification tools; available iOS and Android

2. iBird Pro (paid) - comprehensive coverage of North and Central American birds; includes detailed descriptions, range maps, and high-quality images

3. Birds of Costa Rica (paid) - developed by Audubon Guides; covers 850+ species with descriptions, range maps, images, and audio recordings of bird calls

4. eBird Mobile (free) - allows recording and submitting bird sightings; useful for tracking observations and contributing to citizen science projects

Selection factors:
- Regional coverage (must include target area)
- Species coverage completeness
- Image and audio quality
- User interface ease of use
- Regular updates
