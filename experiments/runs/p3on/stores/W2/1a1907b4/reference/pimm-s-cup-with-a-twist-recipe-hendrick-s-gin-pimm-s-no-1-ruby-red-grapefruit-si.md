A customized version of the classic British summer drink using Hendrick's gin with 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin, 0.5 oz Pimm's No. 1, 0.5 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz Ruby Red Grapefruit simple syrup, muddled cucumber slices, and a splash of ginger beer. Serve in a Collins glass with garnishes of cucumber, grapefruit, mint, and optional edible flowers. Muddled cucumber provides intense flavor and texture compared to cucumber juice. Ruby Red Grapefruit variety chosen for balanced sweet-tart flavor and pink hue.
