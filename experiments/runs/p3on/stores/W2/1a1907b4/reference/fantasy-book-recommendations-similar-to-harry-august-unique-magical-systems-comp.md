Comprehensive list of fantasy recommendations compiled from 2023-05-21 conversation.

**Similar to 'The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August' (philosophical, reincarnation, thought-provoking):**
- *The City & The City* by China Miéville — parallel universes, police procedural fantasy
- *The Night Circus* by Erin Morgenstern — magical realism, competition between magicians
- *The Gone-Away World* by Nick Harkaway — identity, reincarnation, adventure blend
- *The Lies of Locke Lamora* by Scott Lynch — complex story, unique magic, morally complex
- *The First Law* trilogy by Joe Abercrombie — morally complex, thought-provoking themes
- *The House of Shattered Wings* by Aliette de Bodard — post-apocalyptic Paris, fallen angels
- *The Golem and the Jinni* by Helene Wecker — mythology, identity, early 20th-century NYC
- *The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet* by Becky Chambers — identity, community, sci-fi
- *The Buried Giant* by Kazuo Ishiguro — memory, aging, post-Arthurian England
- *The Song of Achilles* by Madeline Miller — Trojan War, mythology, character-driven

**With unique/complex magical systems (similar to 'The Poppy War'):**
- *The Fifth Season* by N.K. Jemisin — orogeny magic, Broken Earth trilogy #1
- *The Gilded Wolves* by Roshani Chokshi — alchemy, mythology, YA
- *The Black Tides of Heaven* by J.Y. Yang — gods and monsters, novella
- *The Bone Shard War* by Andrea Stewart — bone shard magic system
- *The Library of the Unwritten* by A.J. Hackwith — stories as living magic
- *The Ten Thousand Doors of January* by Alix E. Harrow — interdimensional doors
- *The Outside* by Ada Hoffmann — mathematics-based magic, sci-fi/fantasy blend
- *The Obsidian Tower* by Melissa Caruso — obsidian magic, land manipulation
- *The Belles* by Dhonielle Clayton — beauty and flowers as magic, YA

**Complex world-building and diverse characters (series):**
- *The Malazan Book of the Fallen* by Steven Erikson — epic, complex mythology
- *The Gentlemen Bastards* series by Scott Lynch — heist fantasy, unique world
- *The Shadow Campaigns* series by Django Wexler — fantasy + military fiction, Napoleonic inspiration
- *The Lightbringer* series by Brent Weeks — unique magic system, action-packed
- *The Powder Mage* trilogy by Brian McClellan — gunpowder fantasy, French Revolution inspiration
- *The City of Brass* by S.A. Chakraborty — 18th-century Cairo, trilogy (can read standalone)
