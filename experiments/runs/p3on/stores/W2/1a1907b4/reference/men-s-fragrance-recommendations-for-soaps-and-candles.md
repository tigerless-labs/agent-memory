Soap fragrances: Cedarwood, Sandalwood, Pine (woodland); Black Pepper, Bergamot, Smoked Vanilla (spicy/smoky); Eucalyptus, Mint, Grapefruit (citrus); Leather, Tobacco, Whiskey (leather/tobacco). Candle fragrances: Woodsmoke/Campfire; Cinnamon, Cardamom, Frankincense (spiced); Moss, Sage, Forest Floor (earthy); Bourbon, Oak (luxury). Blend examples: Cedarwood+Bergamot (fresh, earthy), Leather+Tobacco (rich, sophisticated). Use high-quality phthalate-free fragrance oils.
