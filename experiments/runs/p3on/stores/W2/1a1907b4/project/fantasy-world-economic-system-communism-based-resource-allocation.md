Chose communism-based resource allocation system over monetary system.

Characteristics:
- Government/collective owns and controls means of production
- Resources allocated based on needs of community
- Goal: reduce inequalities and ensure basic needs met for all

Trade-offs considered:
- May stifle innovation without profit incentives
- Can limit personal motivation to work without wealth accumulation potential
- Difficult to adapt to rapidly changing economic environment
- Central planning may not allocate resources as efficiently as markets

Resource allocation approach: baseline for basic needs + additional incentives/resources for those who contribute to community through work
