Approach to allocate resources meeting basic needs while incentivizing work:

1. **Baseline allocation** based on individual factors: age, health, occupation
   - Example: physically demanding jobs may require more resources than sedentary work

2. **Incentive structure** - additional resources/privileges for community contribution
   - Agricultural work: extra resources for crop surplus
   - Factory work: extra resources for meeting production quotas
   
3. **Tracking system** - manual (ledger) + digital (computer program) to record:
   - Resource use and allocation
   - Individual contributions and work
   - Effectiveness of allocation system

This balances ensuring everyone's needs are met while motivating effort and productivity.
