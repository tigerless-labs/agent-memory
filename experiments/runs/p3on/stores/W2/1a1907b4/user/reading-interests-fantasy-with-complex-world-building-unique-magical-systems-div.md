Enjoys fantasy novels with complex world-building, diverse characters, and imaginative magical systems. Particularly drawn to:
- Stories exploring themes of identity, reincarnation, morality, and the human condition
- Character-driven narratives with emotional depth
- Richly detailed worlds and non-linear storytelling
- Both standalone novels and series
- Works with lyrical prose and introspective tone
- Historical fiction, mythology retellings, and fantasy that blends multiple genres

Appreciates darker, more mature fantasy that tackles challenging themes like trauma and oppression.
