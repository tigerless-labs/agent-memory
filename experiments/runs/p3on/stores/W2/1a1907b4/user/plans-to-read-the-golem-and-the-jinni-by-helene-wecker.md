Interested in reading *The Golem and the Jinni* by Helene Wecker, a historical fantasy novel set in early 20th-century New York City.

**Plot**: Follows two supernatural creatures—Chava (a golem made of clay) and Ahmad (a jinni of fire and air)—as they navigate their new lives in America and form an unlikely friendship. Explores their struggle with identity, the constraints of their supernatural natures, and the danger of being discovered by humans.

**Themes**: Identity, community, morality, cultural heritage (Jewish and Middle Eastern traditions and mythology).

**Style**: Lyrical, evocative prose; character-driven; melancholic and introspective tone; moderate pacing.

**Recognition**: 
- Finalist for the National Book Award (2013)
- Won Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature (2014)
- New York Times Notable Book (2013)
