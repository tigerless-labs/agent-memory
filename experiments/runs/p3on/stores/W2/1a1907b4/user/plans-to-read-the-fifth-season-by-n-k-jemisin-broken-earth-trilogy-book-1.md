Interested in reading *The Fifth Season* by N.K. Jemisin, the first book in the Broken Earth trilogy.

**Why**: Features complex world-building with the Stillness (a world suffering catastrophic Fifth Seasons), unique magic system based on orogeny, diverse characters (Essun, Damaya, Syenite), and explores mature themes including trauma and oppression. Won the Hugo Award for Best Novel (2016).

**Notes**: Non-linear narrative structure. Part of a trilogy; can be read as standalone but leads to *The Obelisk Gate* and *The Stone Sky*.
