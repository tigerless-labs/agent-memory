Actively improving bird identification skills through multiple methods:

- **Listening to bird calls**: Spending consistent time learning vocalizations of different species online (started ~April 2023)
- **Studying field marks**: Focusing on bird shapes, silhouettes, plumage patterns, colors, and markings
- **Birding journal**: Maintaining detailed records for the past 6+ months (since ~November 2022) including:
  - Date, location, and habitat information
  - Species observations with distinctive features
  - Behavioral notes
  - Sketches and photos when possible
  - Identification challenges to focus on
- **Regular practice**: Consistent birding trips using new binoculars to improve observation skills
