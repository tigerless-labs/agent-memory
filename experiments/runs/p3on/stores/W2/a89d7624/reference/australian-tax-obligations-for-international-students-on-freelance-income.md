Tax-free threshold (2022-2023): 18,201 AUD. Non-residents taxed at 32.5 percent on income 0-90,000 AUD, 37 percent on 90,001-180,000 AUD, 45 percent above 180,000 AUD. Must file tax return NAT 2541 by October 31. May need PAYG quarterly instalments. Recommend consulting tax professional familiar with international student obligations.
