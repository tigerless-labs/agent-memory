**BBQ Restaurants:**
- GQue BBQ (Golden) — 10-15 min from Red Rocks, brisket/ribs/sausage
- Dinosaur Bar-B-Que (Lakewood) — 20-25 min, smoked on-site
- Moe's Original BBQ (Wheat Ridge) — 15-20 min, Southern-style
- Big B's Delicious BBQ (Arvada) — 20-25 min, family-owned
- Wayne's Smoke Shack (Morrison) — few minutes from Red Rocks, food truck turned BBQ joint

**Dining Near Red Rocks:**
- The Ship Rock Grille — across from Red Rocks, American classics, outdoor patio with views of formations, partially covered and heated, pet-friendly
- The Village Tavern — 5 min walk, pub fare
- Morrison Inn — 10 min walk, American comfort food
- The Fort — 2 miles, Southwestern cuisine
- Bear Creek Tavern — 2.5 miles, American classics
