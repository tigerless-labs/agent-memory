Irish psychological thriller released in 2003. Centers on Tommy (played by Andrew Scott), a hospital mortuary assistant who discovers a dead body with money in its mouth, setting him on a course of dangerous events. Explores themes of grief, mental health, and desperation. Described as intense with a grim edge.
