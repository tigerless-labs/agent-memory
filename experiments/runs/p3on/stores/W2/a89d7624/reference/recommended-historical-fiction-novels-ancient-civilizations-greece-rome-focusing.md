Recommended Historical Fiction: Ancient Civilizations

Ancient Greece - Daily Life & Mythology:
- Circe by Madeline Miller (Circe's life and agency beyond Homer's narrative)
- The Song of Achilles by Madeline Miller (Trojan War from Achilles' lover's perspective)
- The Penelopiad by Margaret Atwood (Odyssey retold from Penelope's viewpoint)
- The Golden Mean by Annabel Lyon (Aristotle's life and relationship with Alexander the Great)

Ancient Rome - Social Life & Women:
- Daughters of Rome by Kate Quinn (four women's lives in ancient Rome, includes Vestal Virgin)
- The Memoirs of Hadrian by Marguerite Yourcenar (introspective life of Emperor Hadrian)
- Romanitas by Sophia McDougall (two sisters exploring daily life, politics, and hierarchies)
- Lustrum by Robert Harris (rise of Cicero during tumultuous Roman period)

Ancient Mesopotamia:
- The Red Tent by Anita Diamant (retelling of Dinah's story in ancient Mesopotamia)
- The Queen's Governess by Karen Harper (governess in King Nebuchadnezzar's court, Babylon daily life)
- The Gilded Cage by Kaitlyn Davis (young Babylonian princess, daily life and politics)

Other Ancient:
- The Egyptian by Mika Waltari (Egyptian physician during Pharaoh Akhenaten's reign, 14th century BCE)
