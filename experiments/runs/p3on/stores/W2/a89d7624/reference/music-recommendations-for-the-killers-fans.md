Bands recommended as similar to The Killers:

- **The 1975** — blend indie rock with catchy pop hooks, strong live presence
- **Imagine Dragons** — fellow Las Vegas natives, anthemic arena-ready sound
- **Arctic Monkeys** — British rock, similar energy and swagger, witty lyrics
- **The Black Keys** — blues-rock duo, gritty high-energy sound
- **Walk the Moon** — indie rock/pop blend, synth-infused upbeat tracks
- **The Neighbourhood** — California indie rock, darker atmospheric sound
- **Foals** — British indie rock, catchy hooks with math-rock influences
- **Panic! At The Disco** — Las Vegas band, theatricality and pop sensibilities
- **The Vaccines** — British indie rock, raw garage-rock sound
- **Two Door Cinema Club** — Irish indie rock, catchy hooks with synth-infused indie
