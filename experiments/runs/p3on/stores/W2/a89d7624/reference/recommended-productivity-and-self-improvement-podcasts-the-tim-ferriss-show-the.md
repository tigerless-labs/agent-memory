## Podcasts Recommended for Productivity & Self-Improvement\n\n### Specific Episodes:\n\n**The Tim Ferriss Show:**\n- Episode #214: Jamie Foxx on morning routine (30-minute workout, meditation, journaling)\n- Episode #193: Neil Strauss on focus and strategies including 'stop doing' list\n\n**The Happiness Lab with Dr. Laurie Santos:**\n- Episode: The Power of Small Wins (motivation and productivity)\n- Episode: The Benefits of a 'Stop Doing' List (eliminating energy-draining tasks)\n\n**Productivity Show:**\n- Episode 143: How to Create a Morning Routine That Works for You\n- Episode 135: The Power of Focus and Prioritization\n\n**Other Podcasts:**\n- The 5 AM Miracle Podcast - Episode 144: How to Create a Morning Routine That Lasts\n- The Achieve Your Goals Podcast - Episode 134: How to Boost Your Productivity with a Morning Routine\n\n### Time Tracking & Focus Tools Recommended:\n- Toggl (time tracking)\n- RescueTime (automated time tracking)\n- Harvest (time tracking and invoicing)\n- Pomodoro Timer, Pomofocus, Tomato Timer\n- Freedom (website blocker)\n- StayFocusd (Chrome extension)\n- Todoist (task management)\n- Any.do (task management)\n- Habitica (habit tracking, gamified)\n- Streaks Workout (fitness tracking)
