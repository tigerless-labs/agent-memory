**Venues:**
- Red Rocks Park and Amphitheatre — iconic venue, diverse genres
- The Ogden Theatre — historic LoDo venue, indie/rock/electronic
- The Bluebird Theater — historic, diverse lineup
- The Fillmore Auditorium — legendary venue, rock to hip-hop
- Larimer Lounge — laid-back, discover new talent
- Twist & Shout Records — legendary record store
- The hi-dive — punk, indie, alternative focus
- The Marquis Theater — mid-sized venue, diverse acts

**Festivals:**
- Grandoozy — diverse lineup rock/pop/hip-hop/electronic
- Denver Folk Festival — folk, bluegrass, roots
- Denver Jazz Festival — jazz all forms
- Westword Music Showcase — one-day festival, local focus
- Riot Fest Denver — punk, rock, alternative (September)
