Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) - air purifier, thrives in bright indirect light, can go weeks without watering. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) - tolerates variable lighting and infrequent watering, air purifier. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) - easy care, white flowers, prefers bright indirect light, propagates easily. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) - versatile, low-maintenance, works in various lighting, air purifier. Succulents (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) - extremely low-maintenance, tolerate bright light, require sparse watering. Dracaena - versatile, air purifier, can grow tall. Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii) - elegant blooms, air purifier, tolerates neglect. Bromeliads (Aechmea, Guzmania, Nidularium) - low-maintenance, beautiful flowers, bright indirect light. Care: water when soil is dry, fertilize sparingly, prune regularly. Try IKEA, West Elm, Amazon.
