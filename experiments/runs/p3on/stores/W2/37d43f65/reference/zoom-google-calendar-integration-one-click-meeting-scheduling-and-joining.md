Zoom offers a Google Calendar add-on enabling seamless meeting scheduling:\n- One-click Zoom meeting creation from calendar events\n- Automatic meeting invitations sent to guests with meeting link and details\n- One-click joining for attendees directly from calendar invite\n- Customizable settings: duration, audio/video options, meeting recording\n- Additional features: virtual backgrounds, breakout rooms, polls\n- Also integrates with Microsoft Outlook and iCal calendars\n\nBenefits: Reduces no-shows through automatic reminders and invitations; streamlines meeting scheduling; improves remote collaboration. Useful for coordinating online gaming sessions and other regular video calls.
