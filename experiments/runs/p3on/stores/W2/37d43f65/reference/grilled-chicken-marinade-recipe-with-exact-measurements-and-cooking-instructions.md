Olive oil (1/4 cup), lemon juice (1/4 cup), garlic (2 cloves minced), dried oregano (2 tsp), salt (1 tsp), black pepper (1/2 tsp). Whisk together, add chicken, marinate 20-30 min. Cook 6-7 min per side in grill pan or skillet at medium-high heat until 165F internal temp. Rest 5-10 min before serving.
