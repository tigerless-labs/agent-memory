Furniture and home decor: IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, Amazon. Art and print retailers: Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, Saatchi Art. For workspace furniture, look for wall-mounted desks, narrow console tables, drop-leaf tables, ottomans with trays, or foldable desks. For circular rugs and mirrors, consider proportional sizing (3-4 feet diameter rug, 12-18 inch mirror), durable materials like wool or jute, and colors/frames that complement the room.
