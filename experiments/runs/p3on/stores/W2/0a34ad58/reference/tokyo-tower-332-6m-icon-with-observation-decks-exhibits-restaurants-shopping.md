**Tokyo Tower** - iconic Tokyo landmark

**Key Facts:**
- Height: 332.6 meters (1,091 feet)
- Design inspired by Eiffel Tower with modern twist
- One of world's tallest towers

**What to Do:**
- View from observation decks with panoramic city views
- Spot landmarks: Imperial Palace, Meiji Shrine, Mount Fuji (clear days)
- Take elevator to observation decks or climb stairs for workout
- Wax museum exhibits
- Pirate-themed play area
- Guinness World Records museum

**Amenities:**
- Souvenir shops
- Restaurants
- Interactive exhibits about Tokyo's history and culture

**Tips:**
- Visit early morning or evening to beat crowds
- Buy tickets online to avoid queues
- Wear comfortable shoes (lots of walking)
- Pack snacks/water (options can be limited)
- Great for photos of Tokyo skyline
