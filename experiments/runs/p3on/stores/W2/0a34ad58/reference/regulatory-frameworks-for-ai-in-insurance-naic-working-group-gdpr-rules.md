## NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners)\n- Established working group on artificial intelligence\n- Focus areas: ethical standards, data privacy protection, transparency, independent third-party oversight\n\n## GDPR (European Union)\n- Strict rules on data collection, storage, processing, and use for AI\n- Individuals have right to know when AI makes decisions about them\n- Individuals can challenge AI-based decisions\n\n## Key Regulatory Concerns\n- Algorithmic bias from biased training data\n- Reinforcement of societal biases in risk assessment\n- Lack of transparency and interpretability in AI decision-making
