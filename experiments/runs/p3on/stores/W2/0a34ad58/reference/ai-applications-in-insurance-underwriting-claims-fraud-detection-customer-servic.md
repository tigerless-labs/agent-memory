## AI Use Cases in Insurance\n\n### Automated Underwriting\nAnalyze large data volumes to identify patterns and make predictions for faster, more accurate underwriting decisions\n\n### Claims Processing\nAutomate damage assessment and fraud detection for faster payouts and lower costs\n\n### Risk Assessment\nSupplement traditional statistical models to identify new risk factors and predict trends more accurately\n\n### Fraud Detection Techniques\n- **Anomaly detection**: flag unusual claim patterns (e.g., multiple claims for same injury, unusual time gaps)\n- **Social media monitoring**: check social media for contradictions (e.g., claiming disability but shown doing strenuous activity)\n- **Network analysis**: detect collusion patterns between individuals involved in claims\n- **Image/voice recognition**: detect fabrication or manipulation in submitted images or recordings\n\n### Customer Service\nAI-powered chatbots providing 24/7 support\n\n## Key Conclusions\n- AI won't completely replace actuaries; they'll complement each other\n- Potential for cheaper policies through more accurate risk assessment and automated processing\n- Competition and market expansion could drive down prices
