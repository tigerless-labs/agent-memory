**Tokyo Shopping Districts and Malls**

**Shinjuku (5-min walk from Park Hyatt)**
- Shinjuku Isetan: massive department store
- Shinjuku Odakyu Mylord: shopping mall
- Shinjuku Lumine: fashion/lifestyle focus
- Tokyu Hands: 10-min walk, varied products
- Shinjuku Takashimaya: large department store

**Harajuku (10-15 min by taxi/subway)**
- Fashion-forward district
- Trendy boutiques and vintage shops
- Unique souvenir stores

**Omotesando (15-20 min by taxi/subway)**
- Stylish shopping street
- High-end designer boutiques
- Art galleries and restaurants

**Ginza (20-25 min by taxi/subway)**
- Luxury shopping district
- High-end designer brands
- Jewelry stores and upscale department stores

**Ikebukuro Parco (20-25 min)**
- Trendy shopping mall
- Fashion, entertainment, lifestyle focus
