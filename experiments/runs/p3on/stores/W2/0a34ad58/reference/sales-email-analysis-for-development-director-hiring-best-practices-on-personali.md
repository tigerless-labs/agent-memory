Subject: Don't hire a Development Director

WHAT THE EMAIL DOES WELL:

1. Personalization — Addresses recipient by name, shows research on organization and specific position

2. Demonstrates expertise — Shows knowledge of sector and the specific hiring challenge

3. Problem identification — Presents concrete data about fundraiser retention: average tenure 16 months, replacement cost 127650 dollars

4. Challenges conventional thinking — Positions unusual but effective approach of postponing hire to build internal structure

5. Builds urgency and curiosity — Frames it as solution other executive leaders are adopting

6. Soft sell — Ends with 'It may not be a fit' but asks if conversation is worth justifying

7. Outcome focus — Emphasizes that internal work enables future Development Director to be empowered, focused, and ready to execute

KEY INSIGHT: Email uses problem to pattern to alternative to conversation framework. Opens with prospect's stated need (hiring dev director), introduces data showing why conventional approach fails, suggests alternative, invites exploratory conversation.
