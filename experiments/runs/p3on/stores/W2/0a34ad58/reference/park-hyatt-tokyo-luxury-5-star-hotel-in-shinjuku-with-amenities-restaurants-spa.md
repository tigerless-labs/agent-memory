**Park Hyatt Tokyo** — luxury 5-star hotel located in Shinjuku, Tokyo

**Key Amenities:**
- Spacious rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows and city views
- State-of-the-art fitness center
- Spa (47th floor) with massages, facials, body wraps, sauna, steam room
- Indoor pool (47th floor) with city views
- Multiple restaurants: New York Grill (American), Girandole (French-Japanese fusion), Peacock Alley (lounge)
- The Peak Bar (52nd floor, sophisticated bar with views)
- 24-hour room service, laundry/dry cleaning
- Business center, concierge
- Free Wi-Fi throughout
- Airport shuttle service (fee applies)
- Luggage storage

**Location:** Shinjuku district, central Tokyo with abundant shopping and dining nearby
