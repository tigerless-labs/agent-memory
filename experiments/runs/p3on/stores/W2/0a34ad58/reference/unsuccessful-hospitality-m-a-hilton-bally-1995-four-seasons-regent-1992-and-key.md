## Failed Mergers & Acquisitions

**Hilton Hotels & Bally Entertainment Corporation (1995)**
- Hilton acquired gaming company with casinos/hotels in Las Vegas and Atlantic City
- Expected gaming industry foothold; acquisition failed within years
- Failure factors: high debt levels, cultural clashes, increased competition in gaming industry

**Four Seasons & Regent Hotels & Resorts (1992-1997)**
- Acquired luxury brand with properties in Asia to expand Asian market presence
- Sold Regent in 1997; deemed unsuccessful
- Failure factors: high integration costs, lack of synergies, cultural differences

## Key Factors for M&A Success

**Critical for Success:**
- Strategic fit and compatible business models/objectives
- Effective integration planning for operations, cultures, systems, processes
- Reasonable valuation; avoiding overpaying
- Regulatory approval and compliance
- Anticipating market changes

**Advantages for Smaller Companies:**
- Can leverage agility and innovation
- More nimble approach to partnerships
- Technology sharing and joint marketing alternatives
- Access to capital through strategic partners/investors

**Overall Success Drivers:**
- Strategic fit alignment
- Cultural alignment
- Effective integration planning
- Due diligence and strategic planning
