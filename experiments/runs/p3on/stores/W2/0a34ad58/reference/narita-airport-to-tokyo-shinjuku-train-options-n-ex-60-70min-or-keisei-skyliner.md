**Getting from Narita Airport to Shinjuku, Tokyo**

**Option 1: JR Narita Express (N'EX)**
- Direct to Shinjuku Station
- Time: 60-70 minutes
- Cost: 3,200-3,500 yen (about 30-35 USD)
- Most convenient but more expensive

**Option 2: Keisei Skyliner + JR Yamanote Line**
- Keisei Skyliner: Narita to Ueno (40-50 min)
- Transfer to JR Yamanote at Ueno
- JR Yamanote to Shinjuku (20-30 min)
- Total: 50-60 minutes
- Cost: 2,500-3,000 yen (25-30 USD)
- More budget-friendly but requires transfer

**Option 3: Keisei Limited Express + Transfer**
- Time: 70-80 minutes
- Cost: 2,000-2,500 yen (20-25 USD)
- Most economical

**Notes:**
- All work with Suica IC card
- Touch card at entry/exit gates
- Check schedules ahead
- Keisei Skyliner is fastest to central Tokyo
