**Tsukiji Fish Market** — one of the world's largest fish markets in Tokyo

**Overview:**
- Handles over 2,000 tons of seafood daily
- Divided into inner market (jonai-shijo, wholesale, viewing only) and outer market (jogai-shijo, public access)

**For Visitors:**
- Best time to visit: 5:00–7:00 AM when market is busiest and fish is freshest
- Outer market has many sushi restaurants and seafood shops
- Popular sushi spots: Sushi Dai, Sushi Daiwa, Tsukiji Fish Market Sushi (small, unassuming bar)
- Fresh breakfast sushi is a must-do experience

**Tips:**
- Arrive early (5-7 AM) for best experience
- Expect large crowds, especially peak seasons
- Wear comfortable shoes, dress in layers (cold early morning)
- Bring cash (many shops/restaurants cash-only)
- Respect market rules, avoid touching displays or excessive photography
