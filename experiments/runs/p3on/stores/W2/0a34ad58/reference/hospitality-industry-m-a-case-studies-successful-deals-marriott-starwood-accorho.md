## Successful Mergers & Acquisitions

**Marriott International & Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide (2016)**
- Largest hotel company created; 30 brands added (St. Regis, W Hotels)
- 1.1M rooms in 100+ countries; 140M+ loyalty members
- Integration successful; improved financial results

**AccorHotels & Fairmont Raffles Hotels International/FRHI (2016)**
- Acquired luxury brands: Fairmont, Raffles, Swissôtel
- Access to North America and Asia markets; 6M+ loyalty members
- Strong post-acquisition financial results

**InterContinental Hotels Group/IHG & Kimpton Hotels (2015)**
- 60+ boutique properties in US
- Tapped into growing boutique hotel demand
- Successful integration; strong financial results

## Recent Deals (2018-2019)

**AccorHotels & Orbis (2019)** — Expanded presence in Central & Eastern Europe

**IHG & Six Senses Hotels Resorts Spas (2019)** — Expanded luxury market presence across Asia, Middle East, Europe, Americas

**Marriott & Elegant Hotels Group (2019)** — Luxury hotels & resorts in Barbados; Caribbean market foothold

**AccorHotels & sbe Entertainment Group (2018)** — 50% stake in lifestyle hospitality (SLS, Delano, Mondrian brands); North America expansion
