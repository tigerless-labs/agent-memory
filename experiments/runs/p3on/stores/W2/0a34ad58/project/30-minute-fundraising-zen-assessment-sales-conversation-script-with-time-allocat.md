**Total: 30 minutes**

**1. Introduction (5 min)**
Introduce yourself and organization. Brief overview of Fundraising Zen Assessment and its purpose. Ask about recipient's current goals/challenges.

**2. Listen Intently (10 min)**
Allow recipient to share fundraising goals, challenges, and past efforts in detail. Take notes. Ask clarifying questions to understand needs and priorities.

**3. Present Fundraising Zen Assessment (10 min)**
Explain scope and three focus areas: Relationship Based Giving, Mass Market Fundraising, Employer Enabled Support. Outline deliverables including historical giving analysis, KPI development, and new revenue growth plan. Emphasize value of professional assessment for deeper understanding and improvement identification.

**4. Upsell (5 min)**
Discuss benefits of assessment and how it helps achieve fundraising goals. Offer follow-up conversation. If interested, provide next steps (schedule follow-up or send additional info).

**5. Conclusion (5 min)**
Thank recipient. Ask for final questions. Confirm next steps and express hope to work together.

**Design principle:** Sales approach emphasizes listening over pitching. Sales person gathers customer needs/pain points early, then positions assessment as data-driven solution.
