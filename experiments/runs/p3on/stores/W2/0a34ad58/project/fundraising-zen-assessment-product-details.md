Professional assessment providing data-driven visual deliverable with contextual giving history and three-component revenue analysis.

**Three Focus Areas:**
A. Relationship Based Giving (key funder engagement, giving clubs, honor/final/tribute giving)
B. Mass Market Fundraising (monthly giving, paid ads, content strategy, conversion optimization, journey mapping)
C. Employer Enabled Support (employer patrons, employee champions, workplace campaigns)

**Assessment includes:** performance evaluation, revenue analysis, systems review, wealth screening/predictive modeling, stakeholder insights, documentation.

**Deliverables:** historical giving segments and risk assessment, KPI development and operational strategy, new revenue growth plan, performance campaign design, functional delegation calendar for relationship actions.
