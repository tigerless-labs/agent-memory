**Confirmation Email Structure**
Reaffirm conversation purpose. Request attendee preparation. Clarify time commitment. Confirm next steps.

**Information to Request from Attendee**
- Current fundraising goals and objectives
- Data/reports on past fundraising efforts (successes and challenges)
- List of current fundraising staff and their roles/responsibilities

**Pre-conversation Questions for Attendee to Mull Over**
- What specific challenges are you facing with fundraising and retention of fundraisers?
- What has your organization done in the past to try to address these challenges?
- What long-term goals do you have for your fundraising efforts?

**Purpose of Pre-work**
Preparation makes conversation more productive and focused. Attendee reflection on challenges/goals allows sales person to listen intently and position assessment solution effectively.
