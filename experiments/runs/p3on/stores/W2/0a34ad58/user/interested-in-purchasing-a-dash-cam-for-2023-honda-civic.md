Planning to buy a dash cam for vehicle protection and incident recording
Key features to look for:
- HD or 4K video resolution
- 140-160° field of view
- Night vision/low-light performance
- Motion detection
- Loop recording
- GPS tracking
- Audio recording capability
- Wi-Fi connectivity and smartphone app (worth considering but adds cost)
- Cloud storage options

Popular brands to research: BlackVue, Garmin, Thinkware, Rexing, Aukey, Cobra, Viofo

Considerations:
- Budget vs. features
- Subscription costs for cloud storage
- Ease of installation and use
- Privacy concerns with cloud storage
