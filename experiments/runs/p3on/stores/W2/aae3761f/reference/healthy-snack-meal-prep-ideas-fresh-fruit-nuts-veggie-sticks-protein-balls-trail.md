## Healthy Snack Meal Prep Ideas

**Fresh Fruit**
- Cut and store in airtight containers (apples, oranges, berries, grapes)
- Prepare fruit salad with seasonal mix and lemon juice

**Nuts and Seeds**
- Roast almonds, walnuts, pecans with olive oil and salt
- Mix pumpkin, sunflower, chia seeds for crunchy blend

**Veggie Sticks**
- Carrot, celery, cucumber with hummus or guacamole
- Roasted sweet potato or beet sticks with olive oil and seasonings

**Protein-Rich**
- Energy balls with rolled oats, nuts, dried fruits
- Hard-boiled eggs stored in fridge
- Greek yogurt parfait with granola, berries, honey

**Whole Grain**
- Homemade whole grain crackers with oats, nuts, seeds
- Store-bought crackers with avocado, hummus, or peanut butter

**Other Options**
- Trail mix: nuts, seeds, dried fruits, dark chocolate chips
- Roasted edamame or chickpeas
- Homemade granola with oats, nuts, seeds
- Veggie tray with bell peppers, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes and ranch dip

**Storage tip:** Label containers with date and ingredients for organization and freshness tracking.
