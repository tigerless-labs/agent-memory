## Best Potatoes for Beef Stew

All hold shape well when cooked. Choose based on preference:

- **Yukon Gold**: Buttery yellow flesh, slightly waxy, creamy flavor—excellent for stew
- **Red Bliss**: Waxy texture, sweet earthy flavor, retains shape, adds color
- **Russet**: High starch content, holds shape well, mild slightly sweet flavor
- **Idaho**: Light fluffy texture, mild flavor, good if you prefer lighter potato taste

**Prep tips:**
- Choose potatoes of similar size for even cooking
- Peel and chop into 1-2 inch cubes
- Optional: soak 30 min in cold water to reduce starchiness
- Add to slow cooker in last 1-3 hours depending on desired tenderness preference
