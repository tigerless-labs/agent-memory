## Browning Beef for Stew

**Why:**
- Caramelizes natural sugars, adds depth and richness
- Develops brown color for visual appeal
- Creates crust that locks in juices

**Best practices:**
- Use chuck roast or round cuts (good fat-to-lean ratio)
- Cut into bite-sized pieces for even browning
- Season with salt, pepper, herbs (thyme, rosemary)
- Use heavy-bottomed skillet or Dutch oven
- Heat pan 2-3 min over medium-high heat until hot but not smoking
- Add 1-2 tbsp oil, let shimmer 1 min
- Sear beef in single layer (batch if needed) 2-3 min per side
- Don't stir frequently—prevents brown crust formation
- Aim for deep brown, not burnt
- Transfer to slow cooker once browned
