## Slow Cooker Recipes

**Beef Recipes:**
- Chili Con Carne: ground beef, beans, tomatoes, spices
- Beef Stew: beef, potatoes, carrots, onions in broth (cook 8-10 hours low or 4-6 hours high)
- Pot Roast: beef with carrots, potatoes, onions in savory gravy

**Chicken Recipes:**
- Creamy Chicken Tacos: shredded chicken, tomatoes, onions, taco seasoning in cream sauce
- Chicken Fajitas: chicken, bell peppers, onions with fajita seasoning
- Honey Garlic Chicken: breasts with honey, garlic, soy sauce; serve with rice

**Pork Recipes:**
- Pulled Pork: pork shoulder with barbecue sauce
- Carnitas: pork shoulder with lard, orange juice, spices
- Pork Chili: ground pork, beans, tomatoes

**Vegetarian:**
- Lentil Curry: lentils, onions, garlic, Indian spices; serve with rice or naan
- Black Bean Chili: black beans, tomatoes, cumin
- Roasted Vegetable Stew: sweet potatoes, carrots, zucchini in vegetable broth
