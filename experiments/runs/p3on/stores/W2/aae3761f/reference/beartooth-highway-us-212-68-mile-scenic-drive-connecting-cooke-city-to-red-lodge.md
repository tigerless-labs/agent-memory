**Beartooth Highway** (US-212):\n- 68 miles connecting Cooke City, MT to Red Lodge, MT through Beartooth Mountains\n- Paved road, two lanes with occasional passing lanes and pull-outs\n- Reaches elevations over 10,000 feet\n- Summer conditions (late May-early October): usually mild (60s-70s°F), occasional thunderstorms\n- Speed limits 45-65 mph; watch for wildlife (bears, elk, deer, bighorn sheep)\n- Gas stations limited - refuel in Cooke City or Red Lodge before driving\n- One of the most scenic drives in the US
