## Side Dish Pairings for Beef Stew

**Salads**
- Green Salad: mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, light vinaigrette (refreshing contrast)
- Winter Greens Salad: kale, spinach, arugula with sliced apples, cranberries, tangy vinaigrette
- Roasted Beet Salad: beets, mixed greens, goat cheese, balsamic vinaigrette

**Bread** (classic pairing for dipping into stew juices)
- Crusty Bread: baguette, ciabatta, or rustic bread
- Garlic Bread: toasted garlic bread, homemade or store-bought
- Cornbread: warm cornbread with butter

**Other Options**
- Roasted Vegetables: carrots, Brussels sprouts, broccoli
- Mashed Potatoes: with garlic, butter, milk
- Sautéed Spinach: cooked with garlic and lemon
- Braised Greens: kale, collard greens, or mustard greens with garlic and lemon
