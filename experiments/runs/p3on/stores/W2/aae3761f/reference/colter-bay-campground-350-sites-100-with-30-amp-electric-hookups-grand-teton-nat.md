**Colter Bay Campground**, Grand Teton National Park area:\n- 350 total campsites; 100 have 30-amp electric hookups ("Electric Loop" section)\n- Accommodates RVs/trailers up to 45 feet\n- Amenities: flush toilets, showers, potable water\n- Potable water stations throughout, dump station near entrance (free with donations)\n- Reservations through recreation.gov with 6-month advance booking window\n- Peak season: June-August (book early)\n- Water conservation encouraged (limited supply, water-sensitive area)
