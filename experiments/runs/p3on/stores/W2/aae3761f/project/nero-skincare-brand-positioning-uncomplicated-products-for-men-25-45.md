Nero is a men's skincare brand targeting men ages 25-45 who are conscious of appearance but lack time for complex routines.

**Market opportunity:** Global men's grooming market projected at $166 billion by 2022.

**Key positioning:** Uncomplicated, easy-to-use skincare products specifically formulated for men's skin.

**Product line:** Cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen (made with natural ingredients).

**Funding:** Seeking $500,000 to develop and launch.

**Financial projections Year 1:** $1 million revenue, $500,000 net profit.

**Go-to-market:** Online and offline channels - social media advertising, influencer marketing, events, retail partnerships.
