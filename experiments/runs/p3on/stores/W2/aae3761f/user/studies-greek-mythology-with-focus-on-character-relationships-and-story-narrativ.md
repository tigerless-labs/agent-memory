Actively learning Greek mythology, particularly character-driven stories. Specifically engaged with:

**Stories & Cycles:**
- Golden Fleece (Medea, Jason, Pelias)
- Hercules/Alcides (Herakles) cycle including the 12 Labors
- Character origin stories and genealogies

**Key Characters:**
- Hercules/Alcides: demigod son of Zeus and Alcmene, known for strength; performs 12 Labors as atonement
- Medea: uses trickery, kills her own children
- Jason: seeker of the fleece, ultimately the villain
- Hera: jealous goddess, orchestrates Hercules' trials
- Deianeira/Deianera: Hercules' wife, inadvertently harms him
- Nessus: centaur antagonist
- Theseus: prevents Hercules from suicide

**Learning style:** Takes detailed notes, organizes information into definitions and character relationships, studies mythological genealogies and causality between events.
