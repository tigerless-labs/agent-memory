**When:** Occurs annually in October/November, on the full moon day in the Hindu month of Kartik. Exact dates vary yearly based on the Hindu lunar calendar. Typically lasts about a week.\n\n**Location:** Pushkar, Rajasthan, India\n\n**What to expect:** Camel races, traditional music and dance performances, local cuisine, cultural events. One of the most popular festivals in Rajasthan, attracting international visitors.\n\n**Planning note:** Large crowds—book travel arrangements well in advance.
