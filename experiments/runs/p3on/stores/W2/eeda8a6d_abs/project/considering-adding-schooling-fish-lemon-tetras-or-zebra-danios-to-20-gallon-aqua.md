Considering two options for schooling fish to add to the existing community:

**Lemon Tetras**
- Similar in size and temperament to neon tetras
- Can school peacefully with neon tetras, may form mixed schools
- Compatible with golden honey gouramis (too fast for them to catch)
- Compatible with pleco (fish ignore each other)
- Recommended to add in a school of 6-10+

**Zebra Danios**
- Active, hardy fish that can hold their own
- Compatible with neon tetras (may chase playfully but peaceful)
- Compatible with golden honey gouramis (too fast and active for them)
- Compatible with pleco (too small/agile to bother, ignore each other)
- Recommended to add in a school of 6-10+

General notes for adding schooling fish:
- Introduce in a school of 6-10 or more to reduce stress and territorial behavior
- Provide plenty of hiding places and visual barriers
- Ensure tank is large enough for additional biomass
- Monitor water parameters and perform regular water changes
