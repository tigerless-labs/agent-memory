As of 2023-05-22, planning to replace toilet flapper due to improper flushing.

**Status**: Ready to proceed
- Already purchased replacement flapper compatible with toilet model
- Confident with DIY approach

**Steps to follow**:
1. Turn off water supply (shut-off valve behind toilet)
2. Flush to drain tank
3. Disconnect chain from flapper and flush handle
4. Remove old flapper from overflow tube
5. Inspect and clean overflow tube and area for mineral buildup
6. Install new flapper, ensuring proper seating
7. Reconnect chain, adjusting length as needed
8. Restore water supply

**Tips**:
- Work carefully to avoid damaging overflow tube or components
- Refer to YouTube tutorial if needed during process
- Take time and be patient
