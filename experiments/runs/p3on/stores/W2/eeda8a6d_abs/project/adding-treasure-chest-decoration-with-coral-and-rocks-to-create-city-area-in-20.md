Decoration plan for the 20-gallon aquarium:

**New addition:**
- Treasure chest decoration

**Existing decorations:**
- Artificial coral
- Rocks

**Arrangement:**
- Creating a 'city' area by placing the treasure chest near the artificial coral and rocks
- Treasure chest positioned to create hiding places for neon tetras and other small fish
- Coral and rocks provide natural textured backdrop and additional hiding spots

**Design principles being followed:**
- Create depth by placing treasure chest at angle or slightly buried in substrate
- Vary heights using rocks and coral to create different levels
- Add greenery (live or artificial plants) around the structures
- Leave open swimming space around the structures for fish to move freely
- Treasure chest provides enclosed, cozy hiding space for small fish
