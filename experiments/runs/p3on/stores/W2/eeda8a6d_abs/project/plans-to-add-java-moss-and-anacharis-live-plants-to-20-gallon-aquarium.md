Decided to add two live plant species to the aquarium:

1. **Java Moss**
   - Low-maintenance carpeting plant
   - Will be attached to rocks or driftwood
   - Prefers low to moderate lighting (1-2 watts/gallon)
   - Temperature: 72-82°F (can tolerate as low as 65°F)
   - pH: 5.5-8.5 (prefers slightly acidic to neutral)
   - Water flow: prefers gentle flow
   - Fertilization: balanced fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK) weekly
   - Fish compatibility: provides hiding places for neon tetras; gouramis may nibble but not significantly; pleco may graze occasionally

2. **Anacharis**
   - Fast-growing floating plant
   - Floats on surface (no substrate needed)
   - Moderate to high lighting required (2-4 watts/gallon)
   - Temperature: 65-85°F (prefers 72-82°F)
   - pH: 6.5-8.5 (tolerates wide range)
   - Water flow: prefers moderate to strong
   - Fertilization: balanced fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK) weekly
   - Fish compatibility: provides natural food source for pleco; neon tetras may nibble occasionally; gouramis may eat but not significantly
   - Requires regular pruning to encourage bushy growth

Both plants are sensitive to copper-based medications, so avoid using them in the tank.
