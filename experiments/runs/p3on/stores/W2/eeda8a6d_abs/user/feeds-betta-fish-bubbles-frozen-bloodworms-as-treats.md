Plans to feed Bubbles frozen bloodworms 1-2 times per week as treats. Bettas are carnivorous and love protein-rich foods. Thaw bloodworms before feeding, offer 2-3 at a time, remove uneaten ones to maintain water quality. Can mix with other frozen foods like brine shrimp or daphnia for variety.
