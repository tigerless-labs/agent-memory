Beginner-friendly aquatic plants for 20-gallon tanks: Java Moss, Anacharis, Water Wisteria, Amazon Swords, Cryptocorynes. Plants need nutrient-rich substrate, adequate lighting, good water circulation. Anchor plants using weights, planting tools, or by burying roots deeply. Quarantine plants before adding to main tank.
