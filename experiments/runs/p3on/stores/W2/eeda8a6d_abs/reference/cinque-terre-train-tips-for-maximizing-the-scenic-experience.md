**Seating:** Left side of train from La Spezia to Levanto offers best views of villages and coastline.

**Timing:** Take train during off-peak hours to avoid crowds; consider late afternoon or evening for sunset views over the sea.

**Exploration:** Get off at different villages to hike and walk around, experiencing each village's unique character.

**Planning:** Trains in this area can be delayed; plan accordingly to avoid rushing.

**Camera:** Bring one—the coastal and village views from the train are exceptional.

**Food & drink:** No on-board catering available; bring snacks and water for the journey.
