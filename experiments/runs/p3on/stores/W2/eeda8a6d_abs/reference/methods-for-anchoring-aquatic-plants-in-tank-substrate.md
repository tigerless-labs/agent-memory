Plant anchoring techniques: use plant weights or anchors attached to stems; use planting tools like long-handled tweezers to push plants into substrate; bury roots deeply into substrate; place rocks or gravel around plant base; use soft plant tie-downs to tie stems to decorations; use high-density substrate; create plant pockets by digging holes and firming substrate around roots. Handle plants gently to avoid root or stem damage.
