Research-tested 50mm f/1.8 lenses for Sony A7III. Sony FE 50mm f/1.8 (about 200 dollars): compact and affordable. Sony Zeiss Planar T* FE 50mm f/1.4 ZA (about 1500 dollars): premium. Sigma 50mm f/1.4 DG HSM Art (about 500 dollars): excellent quality. Tamron 45mm f/1.8 Di VC USD (about 400 dollars): has image stabilization. Consider renting before buying to test fit.
