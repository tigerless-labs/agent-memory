## Top recommendation
**Kanelbullar** (Swedish cinnamon buns) — classic, warm, fluffy. Found in almost every bakery.
- Fabrique, Bullar och Bröd, Café Saturnus

## Other Swedish desserts to explore
- **Kladdkaka** (chocolate cake): Vete-Katten, Bageri Petrus
- **Pannkakor** (thin pancakes with whipped cream & lingonberry): Pom & Flora, Café Pascal
- **Semla** (almond pastry, seasonal around Lent): Most bakeries
- **Chokladbollar** (chocolate-oat-coconut balls): Drop Coffee, Kafé Esaias
- **Marängsviss** (chocolate, cream, meringue): Pelikan, Tennstopet
- **Lingonberry pie**: Riche, Operakällaren
