Lemon tetras require groups of 10-15, temperature 72-82F, pH 6.0-7.0, omnivorous diet. Zebra danios require groups of 6-10, temperature 65-75F, pH 6.5-8.0, hardy and tolerant species. Both are good for 20-gallon community tanks with hiding places.
