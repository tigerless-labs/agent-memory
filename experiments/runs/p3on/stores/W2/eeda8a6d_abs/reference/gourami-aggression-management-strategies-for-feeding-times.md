Gouramis can be territorial and aggressive, especially during feeding. Strategies to reduce aggression: introduce schooling fish in groups (10-15 lemon tetras or 6-10 zebra danios) to distract them; provide plenty of hiding places and visual barriers with plants and decorations; use separate feeding areas or feeding rings; increase water change frequency; monitor tank dynamics closely for stress signs.
