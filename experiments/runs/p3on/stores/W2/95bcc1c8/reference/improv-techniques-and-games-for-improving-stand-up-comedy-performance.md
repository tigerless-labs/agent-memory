## Core Improv Principles\n- **Yes, And...** principle: Agree with your partner's statement and add to it\n- Listen actively to partner's words, body language, and tone\n- Be in the moment; don't overthink or plan responses\n- Embrace failure and mistakes as learning opportunities\n- Play to the top of your intelligence (don't dumb down)\n\n## Improv Games for Practice\n- Yes, And...\n- Freeze\n- Scenes from a Hat\n\n## Application to Stand-up\n- Use improv to generate new jokes and material\n- Incorporate improv into writing process\n- Record improv sessions to review and track progress\n- Try different formats: shortform, longform, musical improv\n- Practice improv regularly with friends, family, or fellow comedians\n- Observe people and situations in daily life for material
