Recommended Madewell styles as mid-range quality handbags that balance affordability with durability:

**Transport Tote** ($50-$70)
- Material: sturdy cotton-blend canvas with water-resistant coating
- Trim/straps: vegan leather
- Style: classic, structured tote
- Care: spot clean with damp cloth, use fabric conditioner every 2-3 months, store in cool dry place

**Mini Transport Crossbody** ($50-$70)
- Material: pebbled leather
- Hardware: high-quality metal
- Lining: sturdy cotton-blend fabric
- Style: smaller, more compact than Transport Tote
- Care: condition regularly with leather conditioner, protect from water and extreme temperatures, handle gently to avoid scratches

Both represent good entry point to Madewell quality at accessible price point.
