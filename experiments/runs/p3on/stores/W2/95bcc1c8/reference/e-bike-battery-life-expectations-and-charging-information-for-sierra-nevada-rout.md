Battery life varies with terrain and assist level: 20-40 miles on flat with minimal assist, 10-20 miles on hills with moderate assist, 5-10 miles on steep terrain with high assist. Charging available at campgrounds, RV parks, lodges, resorts, general stores, cafes, motels and hotels. Recommendations: charge whenever stopping, consider spare battery, plan charging stops in advance, communicate with rental shop, ask about assist modes. Good bike types: Hardtail e-MTB with front suspension for rough terrain, or comfort e-bike with upright geometry.
