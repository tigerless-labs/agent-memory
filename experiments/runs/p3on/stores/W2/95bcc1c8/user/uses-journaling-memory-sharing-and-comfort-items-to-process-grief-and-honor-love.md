When processing grief, prefers to:\n- Bring comfort items and personal mementos (photos, letters) to share with family\n- Use journaling/notebooks to capture thoughts, feelings, and memories in the moment\n- Be physically prepared for emotional moments (e.g., tissues)\n- Share and discuss memories with family as part of honoring their legacy\n- Allow themselves to feel and express emotions openly rather than hide them
