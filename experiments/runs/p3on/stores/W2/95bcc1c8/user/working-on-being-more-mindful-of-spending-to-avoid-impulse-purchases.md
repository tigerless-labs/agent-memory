- Setting budgets before purchases
- Waiting 24 hours before buying to avoid impulse decisions
- Researching products and reading reviews

- Avoiding overloading on trendy items that won't last

Goal: Find better value for money and focus on quality items that will last longer, rather than buying on impulse.
