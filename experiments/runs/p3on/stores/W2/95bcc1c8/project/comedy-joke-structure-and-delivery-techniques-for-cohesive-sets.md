## Joke Structuring\n- Group jokes into clusters/themes for coherence\n- Use transitions between jokes (phrases, words, ideas)\n- Vary pace and tone to keep audience engaged\n- Build toward a climax or final punchline\n- Use callbacks and running gags for continuity\n- Create narrative arc (personal story, theme, or character)\n- Use segues to connect jokes and tags to add punchlines\n\n## Incorporating Personal Experiences\n- Draw from own life, observations, and emotions\n- Be honest and vulnerable with audience\n- Find universal truths in specific personal experiences\n- Exaggerate and embellish real-life experiences for comedic effect\n- Use self-deprecation to show relatability\n- Make jokes specific to own life (not general statements)\n- Use storytelling techniques with descriptive language and vivid details\n- Find humor in mundane everyday experiences\n- Be authentic but focus on the humor (not therapy)\n- Test jokes with different audiences and refine based on feedback
