Developed written content covering Edmonton's alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals, specifically:

- SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production (300 words) - covered waste management via Edmonton Waste Management Centre, Zero Waste Framework, food systems via Edmonton Food Council, sustainable procurement practices
- SDG 15: Life on Land (300 words) - covered natural ecosystems like North Saskatchewan River Valley, The Way We Grow plan, Biodiversity Action Plan, Roots for Trees program (2 million trees by 2050), Urban Forest Management Plan
- General local context (500 words) - covered Edmonton's geography, climate, economy (energy/manufacturing/services), multicultural demographics, governance structure, and major sustainability challenges (GHG emissions, urban sprawl, transportation)

Research drew from City of Edmonton official sources and municipal plans.
