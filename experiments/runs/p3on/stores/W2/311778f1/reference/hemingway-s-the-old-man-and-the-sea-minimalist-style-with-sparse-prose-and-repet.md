Ernest Hemingway's *The Old Man and the Sea* exemplifies his concise, direct writing style. Key characteristics:\n- Short sentences and minimalist approach\n- Uses repetition for rhythm (e.g., the fisherman's mantra 'I'll kill him, I'll kill him')\n- Sparse and understated prose\n- Allows readers to fill gaps with imagination\n- Raw and unvarnished realism\n- Focuses on surface-level events of the story
