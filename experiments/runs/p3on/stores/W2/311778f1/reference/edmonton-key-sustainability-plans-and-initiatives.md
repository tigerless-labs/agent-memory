Key City of Edmonton sustainability frameworks and programs to reference for any local sustainability work:

**Waste Management & Circular Economy:**
- Edmonton Waste Management Centre - state-of-the-art facility with composting, anaerobic digestion, materials recovery
- Zero Waste Framework - roadmap targeting 90% waste diversion from landfills
- Sustainable Purchasing Guide (2017) - guidance for procurement with sustainability criteria

**Biodiversity & Urban Forest:**
- Biodiversity Action Plan (2012) - conservation and restoration priorities
- Roots for Trees program - goal to plant 2 million additional trees by 2050
- Urban Forest Management Plan (2012) - carbon sequestration, climate adaptation
- North Saskatchewan River Valley & ravine system - 7,400+ hectares of parks and wildlife habitat

**Urban Planning & Climate:**
- The Way We Grow (2010) - Municipal Development Plan emphasizing natural area preservation, ecological corridors, environmental sensitivity
- Community Energy Transition Strategy - GHG emissions reduction, renewable energy expansion
- City Plan - 30-year growth and development vision

**Community & Food Systems:**
- Edmonton Food Council - supports resilient local food system, urban agriculture, community gardens, farmer's markets

**Geographic & Demographic Context:**
- Population: over 1 million
- Cold continental climate (long winters, short summers)
- Susceptible to extreme weather (drought, floods, heatwaves)
- Multicultural, diverse population; one of Canada's largest Indigenous populations
- Economy transitioning from oil/gas dependence to diversified sectors (manufacturing, services, technology, renewables)
- Historically high GHG emissions due to fossil fuel reliance
- Challenges: urban sprawl, transportation, affordable housing
