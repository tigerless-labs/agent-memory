# Interview Accommodations & Inclusive Hiring Practices

1. **Communicate beforehand** — Ask applicants if they require accommodations (wheelchair accessibility, sign language interpretation, extra time). Follow up to ensure needs are met.

2. **Be aware of biases** — Ensure selection is based on job-relevant skills, knowledge, and experience, not disability status.

3. **Provide reasonable accommodations** — If requested, provide assistive technology, interpreters, or other necessary supports.

4. **Create welcoming environment** — Offer comfortable space, clear directions, water, snacks, and breaks as needed.

5. **Train interviewers** — Educate interview team on disability etiquette and how to communicate respectfully with disabled candidates.

6. **Request feedback** — Ask candidates about their interview experience and use their suggestions to improve for future candidates.

These practices create more inclusive workplaces and better access to talent from people with disabilities.
