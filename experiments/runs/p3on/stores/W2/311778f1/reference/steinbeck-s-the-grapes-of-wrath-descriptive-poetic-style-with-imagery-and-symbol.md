John Steinbeck's *The Grapes of Wrath* exemplifies his descriptive, poetic writing style. Key characteristics:\n- Imagery and symbolism to evoke emotion\n- Vivid, detailed descriptions that paint pictures in reader's mind\n- Dense and immersive prose\n- Uses dialogue to reveal character and advance plot\n- Polished and refined realism\n- Delves deeper into underlying themes and emotions\n- Depicts struggles of Oklahoman farmer families during the Great Depression
