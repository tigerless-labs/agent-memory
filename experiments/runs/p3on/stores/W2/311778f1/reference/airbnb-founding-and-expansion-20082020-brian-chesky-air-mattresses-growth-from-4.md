**Founding (2008)**: Brian Chesky and a friend rented their apartment to guests for a local convention using air mattresses, calling it 'Air Bed & Breakfast.'

**Growth trajectory**:
- **2018**: ~190 countries, 4+ million listed properties, $31B valuation
- **2020**: 220+ countries, 7+ million locations

**Market challenges**:
- City officials and traditional lodging operators (hotels, motels, B&Bs) complained Airbnb hosts circumvented regulations and taxation
- Hosts faced legal issues due to city/state ordinances governing hotels and apartment leases
- **COVID-19 crisis**: Many hosts dependent on Airbnb revenue for mortgage payments or property income; shelter-in-place and travel restrictions created existential threat to peer-to-peer model

**Context**: Planned IPO during 2020, but pandemic threatened to derail growth.
