Notable examples discussed:
- **Dystopian portrayals** (1960s-1980s): 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968), Blade Runner (1982) — reflected society's concerns about technology surpassing human control
- **Nuanced/ethical portrayals** (2010s): Westworld (2016), Ex Machina (2014) — explore sentience, self-awareness, and ethics of creating intelligent machines
- **Optimistic portrayals**: Her (2013) — depicts AI as helpful and ubiquitous part of daily life

**Trend**: As technology became more integrated into everyday life and more familiar (Siri, Alexa), media portrayals became more complex and less uniformly dystopian.
