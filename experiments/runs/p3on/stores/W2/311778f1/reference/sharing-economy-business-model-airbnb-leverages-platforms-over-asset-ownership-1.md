**Business model shift** (Airbnb vs. traditional):
- **Traditional**: ownership-based (hotels own properties, high capital expenditure)
- **Sharing economy**: platform-based (individuals offer spare capacity with low friction)

**Key enablers**:
- Ubiquitous smartphone adoption
- Decreasing technology costs
- Easy sign-up and instant listing

**Revenue structure**:
- Airbnb takes ~11% fee per room stay
- Revenue decoupled from real-estate operating expenses
- Operating costs = tech company expenses (not property/utilities/staffing)
- Margin structure = online marketplace, not lodging establishment

**Financial performance**:
- **2016**: First profitable year
- **2017**: ~$3B revenue, $90M+ profit

**Competitive advantage**:
- Significantly lower operating costs than hotels, motels, B&Bs
- Faster expansion (no physical asset construction needed)
- Scalable marketplace model without capital-intensive property acquisition
