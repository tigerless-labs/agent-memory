**Market segments** (by property type):
- **Hotels**: larger facilities with amenities (spa, fine dining, views); heavy investment in operating costs
- **Motels**: smaller rooms with direct parking access; laundry facilities; positioned near highways for travelers; often used as stops on longer road trips
- **Bed & Breakfasts**: owner-operated, typically 1–2 rooms in owner's home; cozy, home-like ambiance; often in rural areas where hotel investment prohibitive; competed on personalization and community connection

**Industry cost structure** (2020, % of revenue):
| Cost Category | Hotels/Motels | B&Bs |
|---|---|---|
| Wages | 26% | 28% |
| Purchases (food, supplies) | 18% | 12% |
| Depreciation | 9% | 5% |
| Marketing | 2% | 2% |
| Rent & Utilities | 7% | 10% |
| Other | 27% | 40% |

**Total controllable costs**: ~78% of revenue (wages, property, utilities, purchases)

**US hotel market by segment** (2020):
- Recreation: 70%
- Business: 18%
- Other/Meetings: 12%

**Regulatory burden**: All properties subject to city, state, federal regulations (physical property, food safety, disability access, alcohol); required licensing fees; subject to state/federal taxation; slow expansion due to need for physical locations.
