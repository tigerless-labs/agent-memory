**Goal:** Transition Luna to new cat food brand for better digestive health

**Procedure (7-10 day gradual transition):**
1. Start: Mix 25% new food with 75% current food
2. Days 2-3: Increase to 33% new food, 67% current food
3. Days 4-5: Increase to 50% new food, 50% current food
4. Days 6-7: Increase to 66% new food, 34% current food
5. Days 8-10: 100% new food

**Monitoring:**
- Watch Luna's appetite and behavior daily
- Monitor stool quality for changes or digestive upset
- Watch for signs of upset: diarrhea, vomiting, lethargy
- Keep regular feeding schedule, no overfeeding/underfeeding

**If issues arise:**
- Slow down the transition process
- Consult with veterinarian if digestive upset occurs
- Every cat is different; adjust pace as needed

**Food details:**
- Maintaining similar texture (dry food to dry food in this case)
- Designed to improve digestive health
