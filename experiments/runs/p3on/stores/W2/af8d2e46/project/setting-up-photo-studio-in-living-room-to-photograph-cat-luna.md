Decided to set up a dedicated photo studio in the living room with:

**Setup & Props:**
- Simple background
- Luna's favorite toys: laser pointer toy, sisal rope scratching post
- Various props tested for cat comfort

**Photo Goals:**
- Capture Luna posing naturally (especially on scratching post)
- Action shots during laser pointer play
- Get Luna to look directly at camera
- Candid moments during play sessions

**Techniques to use:**
- Fast shutter speed (1/1000+) for action shots
- Continuous autofocus and burst mode for movement capture
- Treats and squeaky toys to direct gaze toward camera
- Low-angle shots from Luna's perspective
- Keep sessions short (5-10 min) and frequent
- Reward attention with praise and treats
- Follow laser pointer movement in shots
- Shoot from multiple angles

Plan to start with simple background and toys as props.
