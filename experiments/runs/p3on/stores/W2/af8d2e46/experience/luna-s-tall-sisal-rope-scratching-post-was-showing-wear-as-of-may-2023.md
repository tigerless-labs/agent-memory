**What happened:**
Luna's favorite scratching post—a tall sisal rope model—was noticeably wearing out by early May 2023. The wear suggested heavy use and affection from Luna.

**Response planned:**
- Replace the worn sisal rope post, or
- Add multiple new scratching surfaces to keep things interesting

**Options being considered:**
- New sisal rope post (her favorite style)
- Carpeted scratching post
- Corrugated cardboard scratcher
- Cat tree with multiple perches and scratching surfaces
- Placing new posts near windows for outdoor viewing

**Learning:**
Cats need sturdy, multiple scratching surfaces for:
- Nail health maintenance
- Muscle stretching and exercise
- Regular enrichment (rotating different types)

Luna's worn post indicates she actively uses scratching surfaces when provided—a good sign for overall enrichment.
