## From back pain treatment book (Chapter 13: Rule #5)

**Gluteal amnesia**: glutes atrophy from prolonged sitting, losing neural connectivity with the brain. This forces the spine to handle loads it's not designed for, causing back pain.

**Why glutes matter**: 
- Glutes are the largest muscles in the body and should handle most lifting/movement loads
- When inactive, the small spinal muscles take over and get strained
- Over time ("geologic time"), micro-irritations become macro damage to discs, joints, ligaments

**3-step solution**: 
1. Wake up sleeping glutes
2. Strengthen them
3. Learn to use them correctly

**Clamshell exercise**:
- Lie on side with hips and knees bent
- Place thumb inside upper hip bone, four fingers across outside hip
- Middle finger should lie on gluteal muscles
- Poke fingers in to reestablish brain-muscle connection
- Find neutral spine and brace core

**Key insight**: Psoas muscles reflexively tighten when glutes are weak, putting more pressure on spine. Activating glutes causes psoas to loosen.
