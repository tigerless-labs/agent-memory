Album: **Give Up** (2003)
- Collaboration between Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)
- Indie-electronic sound blending alternative rock with synthesizer-driven production

**Standout tracks recommended:**
- Such Great Heights — dreamy synth-driven anthem about new love
- The District Sleeps Alone Tonight — melancholic, introspective
- Clark Gable — quirky, upbeat love song
- We Will Become Silhouettes — somber, reflective
- Recycled Air — prominent drums and acoustic guitar
- Sleeping In — vocal layering and harmonies

**Notable contemporaries:** Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada
