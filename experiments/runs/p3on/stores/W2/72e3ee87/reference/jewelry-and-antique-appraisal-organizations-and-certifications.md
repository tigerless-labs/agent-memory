Jewelry Appraisers:
- NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers) — certifies jewelry appraisers
- AGTA (American Gem Trade Association) — certifies gemologists and appraisers
- ASA (American Society of Appraisers) — multidisciplinary, certifies appraisers in jewelry and other fields

Antique and Collectible Appraisers:
- AAA (Appraisers Association of America) — certifies appraisers in antiques, art, collectibles
- ISA (International Society of Appraisers) — certifies appraisers in antiques, art, collectibles
- NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers) — specializes in collectibles including antiques, toys, memorabilia

Appraiser Selection Criteria:
- Certification from reputable organizations (NAJA, ASA, AAA, ISA, NACA)
- Experience with similar items (vintage jewelry, antique music boxes, depression-era glassware)
- Impartial, not affiliated with particular jewelry stores or dealers
- Member of professional associations and committed to industry standards
- Recognized by insurance industry
- Strong referrals and reputation (check reviews and testimonials)
