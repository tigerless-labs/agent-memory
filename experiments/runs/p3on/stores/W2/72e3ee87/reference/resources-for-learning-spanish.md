Spanish learning resources recommended to supplement Duolingo:

**Language Learning Platforms:**
- SpanishDict (grammar guides, verb conjugations, dictionary)
- Babbel (paid, interactive lessons, conversational focus)
- Rosetta Stone (paid software with audio/visual aids and speech recognition)

**Online Courses:**
- Coursera's Spanish for Beginners (5-course specialization by Universidad Carlos III de Madrid)
- edX's Spanish Language and Culture (4-course series by Universidad de Salamanca)
- Open Culture's free Spanish language courses

**Video & Audio:**
- SpanishDict YouTube Channel (grammar, vocabulary, conversation)
- SpanishPod101 (YouTube and podcast)
- Coffee Break Spanish (podcast and YouTube)
- News in Slow Spanish (podcast)

**Language Exchange:**
- Conversation Exchange (find exchange partners)
- Tandem (app connecting with native speakers)
- HelloTalk (app with language exchange feature)

**Vocabulary & Study Apps:**
- Anki (flashcard app)
- Memrise (language learning with grammar/vocab)
- Quizlet (flashcards, tests, study games)
