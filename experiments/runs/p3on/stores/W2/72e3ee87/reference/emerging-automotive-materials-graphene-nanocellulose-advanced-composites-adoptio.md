Materials being researched for automotive body applications:
- **Advanced composites** (including nanocellulose, graphene)
- Potential advantages: lighter weight, greater strength, easier to recycle
- Current mainstream materials: steel (strong but heavy), aluminum, carbon fiber

**Adoption factors:**
- Requires rigorous testing and regulatory approval
- Cost-effectiveness concerns slow mainstream adoption
- Example: EV makers already using lightweight materials to increase range
- High-end sports cars already using advanced composites for performance
