Specialty Companies:
- Jewelers Mutual — specializes in jewelry, including vintage and antique pieces
- Chubb — offers high-value item insurance for jewelry, art, and collectibles
- AXA ART — specializes in art, antiques, and collectibles
- Hagerty — offers policies for collectibles including jewelry, art, and antiques

Standard Companies with Specialty Departments:
- State Farm — has specialized department for high-value items
- Allstate — offers Valuable Items policy covering jewelry, art, and collectibles
- Liberty Mutual — offers Valuable Possessions policy for jewelry, art, and antiques

Recommended Policy Features:
- Agreed value coverage (pays insured amount in case of loss/damage)
- No deductible or low deductible
- Worldwide coverage (if traveling with items)
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance or loss (not just theft or damage)

Tips: Read policy exclusions and limitations carefully. Get detailed appraisal reports for each item. Consider independent insurance broker specializing in high-value items.
