Appraisal priority order: 1) Grandmother's diamond necklace (likely most valuable), 2) Great-aunt's antique music box, 3) Mom's depression-era glassware.

**Strategy:**
- Get one professional appraisal per item initially; can obtain multiple if insurance requires or if values are uncertain
- Search online for certified appraisers in local area
- Ask friends and family for referrals
- Look for appraisers certified by organizations like NAJA, ASA, AAA, ISA, or NACA
- Provide appraisers with proof of ownership, provenance, repair records, photos
- Shop around with multiple insurance companies after appraisals are complete
- Keep all appraisal reports and insurance policies organized
