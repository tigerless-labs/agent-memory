Decision: Implementing regular virtual coffee breaks to improve remote team connection and reduce isolation from working from home.\n\nGround Rules:\n- No work talk allowed\n- Keep conversations light and casual\n- Respect time limits (30 minutes)\n- Use video for face-to-face interaction\n- Minimize distractions during calls\n\nImplementation Strategy:\n1. Frame as collaborative suggestion in team meeting\n2. Start with open-ended question about staying connected\n3. Gather team feedback before finalizing schedule\n4. Choose convenient time for majority\n5. Use reliable video platform (e.g., Zoom)\n6. Make participation optional\n7. Rotate conversation starters to keep fresh\n\nKey Principle: Avoid top-down mandate; ensure team feels heard and consulted.
