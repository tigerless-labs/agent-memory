Has an active basil plant used regularly in cooking. Watering schedule: check soil daily (water when dry to first knuckle), water thoroughly every 2-3 days depending on weather. Water at base to avoid fungal disease. Needs 4-6 hours direct sunlight daily, regular fertilization (10-10-10 NPK weekly), and pinching off flower buds to encourage bushy growth.
