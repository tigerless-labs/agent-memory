Recommendations given for films with all-female or predominantly female casts:

**Already watched:**
- Ocean's Eight — heist film with badass female characters
- Set It Off (1996) — crime thriller with four female friends turning to robbery
- Mean Girls (2004) — comedy about new girl and popular group
- Hidden Figures (2016) — drama about African-American female mathematicians in space program

**Recommended to watch:**
- Little Women (2019) — modern retelling of Louisa May Alcott's classic; nominated for six Academy Awards including Best Picture; heartwarming and poignant with outstanding performances
- Annihilation (2018) — visually stunning science fiction thriller about female scientists on dangerous expedition to mutated region called The Shimmer; thought-provoking and pushes boundaries
- The Women (1939) — classic Hollywood film with all-star female cast about high-society women dealing with infidelity and betrayal
- Bridesmaids (2011) — raunchy comedy about female friends navigating wedding-related conflicts
- A League of Their Own (1992) — comedy-drama about first professional all-female baseball league in 1940s; features Tom Hanks, Geena Davis, Madonna; perfect blend of comedy and drama
- The Heat (2013) — action-comedy about FBI agent and Boston detective teaming up against drug lord
- The Help (2011) — powerful drama about African-American maids in white households in Jackson, Mississippi in early 1960s
