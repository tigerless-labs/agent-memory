Three humidifier models recommended for living room (to support orchid plant care):\n\n1. **Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate**: Affordable, 1-gallon tank, runs 24 hours per fill, quiet, built-in humidistat\n2. **Levoit LV600HH Hybrid**: More advanced, 6-liter tank, runs 36 hours per fill, adjustable mist output, built-in humidistat\n3. **Deneve Minimal Aroma**: Compact/stylish, 1.5-liter tank, runs 16 hours per fill, includes night light, easy to clean\n\nConsiderations when choosing: tank capacity, mist output, humidistat quality, ease of cleaning, noise level. Orchids prefer humid environments (ideal pairing with humidifier for living room setup).
