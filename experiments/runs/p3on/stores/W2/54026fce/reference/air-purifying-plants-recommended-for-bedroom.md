Seven easy-to-care-for air-purifying plants recommended for bedroom:\n\n1. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria)**: Thrives in low light, removes formaldehyde and benzene, nearly impossible to kill\n2. **Spider Plant**: Easy to propagate, removes formaldehyde/xylene/toluene\n3. **Peace Lily**: White blooms, removes ammonia/benzene/acetone, prefers moderate watering\n4. **Dracaena** (40+ varieties): Low-maintenance, various light conditions, removes trichloroethylene/formaldehyde/benzene\n5. **Philodendron**: Easy care, multiple light conditions, removes formaldehyde/benzene/trichloroethylene\n6. **Pothos**: Low-maintenance trailing plant, removes formaldehyde/benzene/trichloroethylene\n7. **ZZ Plant**: Survives low light and infrequent watering, removes benzene/trichloroethylene/formaldehyde\n\nGeneral care: bright indirect light, well-draining soil, moderate watering, 65-75°F, occasional fertilization.
