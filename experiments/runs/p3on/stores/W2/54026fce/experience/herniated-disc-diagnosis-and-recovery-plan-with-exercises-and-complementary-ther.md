## Herniated Disc Condition

Dealing with a herniated disc and exploring active recovery approaches. Current management includes:

- **Exercise routines**: Gentle stretching (knee to chest, pelvic tilt, cat-cow, hamstring stretch), strengthening (plank, bridge, bird dog), and low-impact aerobics (stationary cycling, water-based exercises, modified yoga/Pilates)
- **Acupuncture sessions**: Regular acupuncture for pain and stress management, planning to continue post-operatively with surgeon clearance
- **Progressive muscle relaxation (PMR)**: Practicing 2-3 times per week, finding it very helpful for reducing muscle tension and managing pain and anxiety
- **Core strengthening**: Focus on strengthening core muscles to support back
- **Posture awareness**: Maintaining proper posture during exercises

Planning herniated disc surgery, will resume acupuncture post-operatively after medical clearance.

## Stress and Anxiety
Also managing stress and anxiety through relaxation techniques alongside physical recovery.
