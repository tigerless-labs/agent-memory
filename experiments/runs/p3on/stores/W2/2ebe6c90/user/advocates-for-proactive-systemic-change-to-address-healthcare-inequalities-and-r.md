Frustrated with reactive government action (crisis-driven) vs. proactive measures. Believes healthcare disparities are reflections of broader social/economic issues that must be tackled at systemic level. Values addressing root causes like poverty, discrimination, and education gaps as prerequisites for equitable healthcare access. Emphasizes that vulnerable communities shouldn't have to wait for crises to trigger change.
