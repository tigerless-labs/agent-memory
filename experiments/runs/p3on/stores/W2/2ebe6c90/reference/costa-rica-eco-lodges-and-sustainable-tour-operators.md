Lapa Rios Ecolodge (Osa Peninsula): pioneer in sustainable tourism with luxury accommodations and community engagement. Monteverde Lodge (Monteverde): cloud forest setting with strong sustainability focus. Arenal Observatory Lodge (Arenal Volcano): near volcano with conservation commitment. Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero National Park): accessible by boat/plane with wildlife focus. El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula): luxury accommodations with reforestation program. Tour operators include Costa Rica Expeditions, Osa Conservation, Tortuguero Canals Tours, Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve, and Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours. Look for CST, TIES, or ICT certifications.
