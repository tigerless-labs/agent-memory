Tortuguero is accessible only by boat or plane - no road access. Transportation options from San José: 1) Domestic flight to Tortuguero Airport (1 hour) plus boat transfer (1-2 hours); 2) Shuttle bus to La Pavona (4-5 hours) plus boat transfer (1-2 hours); 3) Private transfer to La Pavona plus private boat. Major wildlife includes howler monkeys, white-faced capuchins, spider monkeys, two and three-toed sloths, toucans, quetzals, macaws, American crocodiles, iguanas, jaguars. Over 300 bird species and 1200+ butterfly species. Best wildlife viewing times: early morning and late afternoon. Pack light due to boat transfers, bring insect repellent.
