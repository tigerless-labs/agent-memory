Engagement strategies aligned to each persona's functional value drivers. Revolver: low rates, balance transfers, financial education - provide dedicated support, email/text updates, targeted promotions. Big Spender: high limits, exclusive rewards, concierge - personalized offers, luxury partnerships, targeted increases. Cashback Extender: cashback rewards, retailer partnerships - personalized promotions, maximize tools, targeted offers. High Default Risk: debt programs, counseling - financial education, dedicated team, payment tracking, management programs. Balance Payer: card benefits, rewards, lower rates - personalized rewards, benefit tools, rate incentives. Minimum Payer: debt programs, counseling - similar to High Default Risk. Partial Payer: flexible plans, personalized rewards - flexible arrangements, debt tools, payment tracking. Low-Income: credit-building, education, lower fees - financial counseling, dedicated support, building products. High-Income: luxury benefits, concierge, exclusive access - personalized rewards, luxury partnerships, premium designs. Underutilized: engagement incentives, reward tools, higher limits - targeted promotions, benefit education, usage encouragement.
