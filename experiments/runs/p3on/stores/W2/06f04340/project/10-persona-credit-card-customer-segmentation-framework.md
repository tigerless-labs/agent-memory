10 credit card customer personas: Revolver (Medium-High risk, High profitability), Big Spender (Medium, High), Cashback Extender (Low-Medium, Medium-High), High Default Risk (Very High, Low-Medium), Balance Payer (Low, High), Minimum Payer (Medium, Low), Partial Payer (Medium-High, Medium), Low-Income Borrower (Medium-High, Low), High-Income Borrower (Low-Medium, High), Underutilized Borrower (Low, Low). Each requires tailored engagement strategies and value delivery aligned to their functional needs.
