Novel by Kevin Barry featuring two aging Irish gangsters (Maurice Hearne and Charlie Redmond) waiting for a boat to Tangier to find Maurice's missing daughter.

Key themes: sin and guilt from criminal past; fate and inability to escape consequences; tragedy and emotional scars from loss; attempts at redemption that ultimately fail; hope mixed with despair; existential confrontation with mortality and meaninglessness. Explores the human condition through aging criminals grappling with their inescapable past.
