Safety equipment recommendations for urban bike commuting, especially low-light conditions.

## Front and rear lights
Rechargeable lights with high lumens recommended for visibility to motorists:
- **Cygolite**
- **Planet Bike**
- **NiteRider**

These significantly increase visibility in low-light conditions, essential for early morning or evening commutes.

## Helmets
Must meet CPSC or Snell safety certification:
- **Bell Helmets**
- **Specialized**
- **Giro**

## Reflective gear
- Reflective ankle straps or arm bands
- Reflective stickers or decals for bike frame
- Bright, reflective clothing
- High-visibility accessories

## Priority
Lights and reflective gear are essential for urban commuting to increase visibility to motorists.
