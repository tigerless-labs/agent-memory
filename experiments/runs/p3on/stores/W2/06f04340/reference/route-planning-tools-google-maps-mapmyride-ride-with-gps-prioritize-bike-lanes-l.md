Tools and strategies for planning efficient, safe commute routes by bike.

## Online mapping and route planning tools
- **Google Maps** — routes, distance, and time estimates
- **MapMyRide** — bike-specific route planning and tracking
- **Ride with GPS** — elevation gain and bike-friendly route analysis

## Route planning priorities for safety and efficiency
- Prioritize bike lanes and dedicated bike paths
- Choose routes with good lighting (important for early morning or evening commutes)
- Minimize high traffic volume areas during peak commute times
- Reduce number of stops, turns, and traffic signals for efficiency
- Check for road closures or construction before commute

## Testing and execution
- Test ride planned route on weekend to evaluate road conditions and traffic patterns
- Stay alert and aware of surroundings
- Obey all traffic laws and signals
- Maintain high visibility with lights and reflective gear
- Build flexibility for weather changes
