Six restaurants recommended in Shinjuku for traditional Japanese cuisine, all within walking distance of Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building:

1. **Tofuya Ukai** (2-14-3 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku) - Tofu specialties including tofu hot pot, skewers, and kaiseki; 5-min walk from hotel

2. **Kyubey Ginza** (3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku) - High-quality Edomae-style sushi at reasonable prices; 10-min walk

3. **Toriki** (2-14-1 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku) - Family-run tempura, soba, and udon noodles; intimate atmosphere; 5-min walk

4. **Kappo Nakajima** (3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku) - High-end kappo-style restaurant with seasonal omakase course; 10-min walk

5. **Han no Daidokoro Bettei** (3-2-12 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku) - Luxurious traditional Japanese kaiseki with seasonal multi-course meals; 10-min walk

6. **Tsurutontan** (3-28-10 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku) - Traditional Japanese noodles (udon, soba, tempura soba) with lively atmosphere; 15-min walk

Reservations strongly recommended. Can book via Chope, TableCheck, or hotel concierge.
