User is actively learning Japanese phrases for upcoming travel to Tokyo.

**Learned:**
- **Konnichiwa** (こんにちは) - Formal daytime greeting meaning 'good day' or 'good afternoon'
  - Pronunciation: kon-nee-CHEE-wah
  - Used when entering stores, restaurants, or meeting someone during the day

User motivated by practical use during Tokyo travels. Good candidate for continued learning and conversational practice.
