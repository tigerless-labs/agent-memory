Recommended gaming keyboard models for FPS gaming (especially for Overwatch):

- Razer BlackWidow Chroma V2 — Customizable backlighting, macro keys, Razer Romer-G switches
- Logitech G512 Mechanical Gaming Keyboard — Customizable backlighting, macro keys, Logitech Romer-G switches
- SteelSeries Apex 7 TKL — Tenkeyless design, customizable backlighting, macro keys, SteelSeries QX2 switches
- Corsair K95 RGB Platinum — Premium option with customizable backlighting, macro keys, Cherry MX switches
- HyperX Alloy Elite — Compact design, customizable backlighting, macro keys, Kailh Silver Speed switches

Price range: 50 to 200+ dollars. Benefits for FPS gaming include programmable macro keys for abilities/ultimates, customizable backlighting for immersion, N-key rollover, and durable construction.
