Key techniques for achieving crispy pizza crust:\n- Use high-protein flour (12-14%): bread flour or all-purpose with high protein content\n- Temperature: preheat oven to 550°F (290°C) with baking stone/steel if available\n- Dough hydration: aim for 60-65% water for crispy outside, chewy inside\n- Stretching: create thin, even layer\n- Pre-baking: par-bake crust 2-3 minutes before toppings\n- Topping balance: avoid overloading (causes sogginess)\n- Cooking: 10-12 minutes until golden brown, use pizza peel for last 2-3 minutes\n- Air circulation: ensure even cooking in oven\n- Patience: don't open oven door frequently
