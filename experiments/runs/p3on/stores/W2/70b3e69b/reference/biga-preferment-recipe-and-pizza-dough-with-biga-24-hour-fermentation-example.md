**Biga Preferment Recipe:**\n- 100g bread flour\n- 100g water\n- 0.1g active dry yeast (optional)\n- Ferment at room temperature (75-78°F) for 24 hours\n\n**Pizza Dough with Biga (1000g flour base):**\n- 1000g bread flour\n- 650g water\n- 20g salt\n- 10g active dry yeast\n- 100g biga (after 24 hours fermentation)\n\nMix ingredients, add biga, mix until shaggy, then let rest and develop over time. Biga benefits: more complex flavor, better crust texture, improved dough strength.
