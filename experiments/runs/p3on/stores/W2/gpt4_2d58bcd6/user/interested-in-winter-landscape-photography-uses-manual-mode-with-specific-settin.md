## Winter Photography Preferences

Enjoys capturing winter landscapes with snowshoeing. Key interests and techniques:

**Camera Settings for Snowy Landscapes:**
- Mode: Manual (M) for full control
- ISO: 100-400 (keep low to minimize noise)
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (medium to small for large depth of field)
- Shutter Speed: 1/125s-1/250s (slow speed for motion blur effect)
- White Balance: Cloudy or Shade mode (captures cool tones of snow)
- Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop (compensate for bright snow)

**Techniques:**
- Shoot during golden hour (just before sunset) or overcast days for soft, even light
- Use polarizing filter to reduce glare from snow
- Experiment with composition using leading lines, patterns, textures
- Focus on details like snowflakes (fast shutter ~1/1000s, wide aperture f/2.8) and frost
- Bracket shots for HDR processing to capture wider dynamic range
- Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility
- Use wide-angle lens (10-24mm) to capture landscape vastness
- Get low angle to capture snow texture and create intimacy
- Look for animal tracks as compositional elements

**Additional Tips:**
- Bring spare batteries (cold drains them quickly)
- Dress warmly to stay comfortable and focused
- Use graduated ND filter to balance sky and land exposure
- Be patient and flexible with unpredictable weather
- Take breaks to review photos and adjust approach
