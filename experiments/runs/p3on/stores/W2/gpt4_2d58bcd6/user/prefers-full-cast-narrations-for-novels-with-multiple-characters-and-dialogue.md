Especially prefers full-cast narrations for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes—they enhance immersion and help track speakers. Also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style. Believes good narration is crucial to audiobook experience. Narrator's tone, pace, and inflection greatly impact how the listener interprets the story and emotional response.
