## Popular Winter Soup Recipes

1. **Creamy Tomato Soup** - Fresh tomatoes, onions, garlic, heavy cream; classic comfort food
2. **Butternut Squash Soup** - Roasted butternut squash, onions, garlic, stock, finished with cream swirl (favorite)
3. **French Onion Soup** - Rich and flavorful with caramelized onions, beef broth, croutons, melted cheese
4. **Minestrone Soup** - Hearty Italian soup with vegetables, beans, tomatoes, pasta in vegetable broth
5. **Roasted Carrot and Ginger Soup** - Vibrant and refreshing with roasted carrots, ginger, broth, topped with sour cream
6. **Spicy Black Bean Soup** - Flavorful and filling with black beans, diced tomatoes, onions, jalapeños, served with crusty bread
7. **Cream of Broccoli Soup** - Comforting and healthy with blanched broccoli, onions, garlic, cream or coconut milk
8. **Lentil Soup** - Nutritious and comforting with red or green lentils, onions, carrots, aromatic spices
9. **Chili con Carne** - Spicy and satisfying with ground beef, beans, tomatoes, blend of chili spices
10. **Potato and Leek Soup** - Creamy and comforting with potatoes, leeks, garlic, broth, finished with sour cream swirl
11. **Escarole and White Bean Soup** - Hearty and flavorful with escarole, cannellini beans, garlic, broth
12. **Spicy Pumpkin Soup** - Seasonal and comforting with roasted pumpkin, onions, garlic, kick of cayenne pepper

**General Tips:**
- Adjust recipes to suit taste preferences and dietary needs
- These soups freeze well for meal prep
- Perfect for warming up after outdoor winter activities
