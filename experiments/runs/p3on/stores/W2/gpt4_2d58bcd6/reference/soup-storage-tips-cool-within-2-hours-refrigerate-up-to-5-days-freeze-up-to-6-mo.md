## Soup Storage and Reheating Guide

**Cooling and Refrigeration:**
- Cool soup to room temperature within 2 hours to prevent bacterial growth
- Refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking in covered, airtight containers
- Label containers with date and contents

**Storage Containers:**
- Use airtight, leak-proof containers with tight-fitting lids
- Glass or BPA-free plastic preferred (non-reactive)

**Freezing:**
- Divide soup into individual portions (1-2 cups each) for easy thawing
- Use freezer-safe containers or freezer bags, removing as much air as possible
- Label with date and contents

**Storage Duration:**
- Refrigerator: 3-5 days
- Freezer: 3-6 months

**Reheating:**
- Thaw frozen soup overnight in refrigerator, or reheat from frozen
- Heat to minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)
- Reheat gently over low to medium heat, whisking occasionally to prevent scorching
- Pour into pot rather than thawing in microwave for better results
