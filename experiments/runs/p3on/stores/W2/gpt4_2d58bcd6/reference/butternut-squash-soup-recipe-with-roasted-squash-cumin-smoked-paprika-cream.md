## Simple Butternut Squash Soup

**Servings:** 4-6

**Ingredients:**
- 1 large butternut squash (about 2-3 lbs)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs (parsley or sage) for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Roast squash: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut butternut squash in half lengthwise, scoop out seeds, place cut-side up on baking sheet with olive oil. Roast 45-50 minutes until tender and caramelized.
2. Sauté: In large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add chopped onion and cook 5 minutes until softened. Add minced garlic and cook 1 minute more.
3. Season: Add cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook 1 minute, stirring constantly.
4. Add broth: Pour in chicken or vegetable broth and bring to boil.
5. Add squash: Once roasted and cooled slightly, scoop flesh into pot.
6. Blend: Use immersion blender or regular blender to puree soup until smooth.
7. Add cream (optional): Stir in heavy cream or coconut cream for richness.
8. Taste and adjust: Season with additional salt, pepper, or spices as needed.
9. Serve: Ladle into bowls, garnish with fresh herbs.

**Tips & Variations:**
- Use pre-cut butternut squash to save time
- Add diced apple or chopped kale for added flavor
- Swap heavy cream for coconut cream or Greek yogurt for dairy-free option
- Experiment with nutmeg or cinnamon for different flavor profiles
