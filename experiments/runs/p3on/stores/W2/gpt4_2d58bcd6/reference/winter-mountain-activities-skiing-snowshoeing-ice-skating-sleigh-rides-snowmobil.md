## Winter Mountain Activities

Popular winter activities for mountain trips:

1. **Skiing/Snowboarding** - Hit the slopes; many resorts offer lessons and rentals
2. **Snowshoeing** - Low-impact way to explore winter landscape; easy to learn with rental options
3. **Ice Skating** - Outdoor rinks in mountain towns; fun exercise in crisp air
4. **Sleigh Rides** - Horse-drawn sleigh through snowy woods; romantic and festive
5. **Snowmobiling** - Adrenaline option with guided tours or backcountry rentals
6. **Ice Climbing** - For the adventurous; requires specialized gear, training, and guide services
7. **Winter Hiking** - Serene experience with snow-covered landscapes and fewer crowds
8. **Dog Sledding** - Mush through snowy mountains with husky teams; some outfitters let you drive
9. **Snow Tubing** - Speed down snowy hills; most resorts offer designated lanes and tow ropes
10. **Stargazing** - Mountains offer clear skies with minimal light pollution for crystal-clear views

**General Tips:**
- Dress warmly and check weather forecast before heading out
- Respect the mountain environment
- Check trail conditions before starting
- Rent equipment or hire guide if new to activities
