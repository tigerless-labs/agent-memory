## Company Profile
- Manufactures and sells high-voltage fuses for electric vehicles
- Currently based in Japan

## Indian Market Entry Strategy (2023-05-30)

Recommended 7-point strategy from Indian EV marketing expert perspective:

1. **Market Research**: Study Indian EV industry, prevalent models, charging infrastructure, local manufacturing, competitors
2. **Adapt to Indian Standards**: Meet regulatory and quality requirements; acquire necessary certifications
3. **Localization**: Establish local production or partner with Indian company to reduce costs and ensure timely delivery
4. **Pricing**: Develop competitive pricing strategy accounting for Indian market's price sensitivity
5. **Distribution**: Build robust network through established automotive parts distributors, retailers, and e-commerce platforms
6. **Marketing**: Target digital/social channels and industry events; tailor messaging to diverse Indian demographic
7. **After-Sales Service**: Establish service center network and train local technicians for product support and brand loyalty

## Key Market Differences - India vs Other Markets
- **Price sensitivity**: Much higher than developed markets
- **Diverse demographic**: Varied customer needs and preferences
- **Infrastructure variation**: Uneven development across regions
- **Regulatory environment**: Specific Indian standards and certifications required
