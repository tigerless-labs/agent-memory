Modern and classic hymns and worship songs for faith healing theme based on Luke 7:1-17 and biblical theology of divine healing. Modern: Healer (Kari Jobe), Great Are You Lord (All Sons & Daughters). Classic: Amazing Grace (John Newton), O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing (Charles Wesley), There is Power in the Blood (Lewis E. Jones), Heal Me O Lord (William Walsham How).
