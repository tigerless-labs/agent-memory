**Soapstone-focused:**
- @soaperstar (Rachel Brooks) — intricate, whimsical, surreal creations
- @soapstonecarvings (Michael Maddy) — detailed, realistic, abstract pieces
- @stonecarverkaren (Karen Rossi) — figurative, abstract, symbolic works

**Stone sculptors (general technique inspiration):**
- @brianrussellsculpture (Brian Russell) — abstract organic forms
- @tomhirschsculpture (Tom Hirsch) — figurative to abstract, texture focus
- @jasonkrugman (Jason Krugman) — intricate, detailed, surreal

**Mixed media & abstract:**
- @alicja_pw (Alicja Pęciak-Włoch) — stone, wood, abstract forms
- @sculpturebylisa (Lisa Stevens) — mixed media, found objects
- @kristin_gaudioendacott (Kristin Gaudio-Endacott) — stone, wood blends

**Other sculptors:**
- @jennifermaestre (Jennifer Maestre) — intricate, detailed, colorful
- @heathermattoon (Heather Mattoon) — figurative to abstract, storytelling
- @kevinbox (Kevin Box) — figurative, abstract, surreal, symbolic
