## Popular Hashtags for Book Content\n\n**Primary recommendation**: #bookrecs (12M+ uses) over #bookrecommendations (6M+ uses) — shorter, more concise, higher engagement.\n\n### General Book Hashtags\n#booklover #bookstagram #books #reading #bookworm #bookaddict #booknerd #bookish #readersofinstagram #bookclub\n\n### Genre-Specific\n#fantasybooks #romancebooks #mysterybooks #thrillerbooks #scifi #horrorbooks #nonfictionbooks #youngadultbooks #historicalfiction #contemporarybooks\n\n## Content Strategy Tips\n\n- Mix popular hashtags with niche hashtags for optimal reach\n- Vary hashtags across posts rather than using the same set repeatedly\n- Track hashtag performance using Instagram Insights\n- Use 5-10 hashtags per post\n- Post polls in Instagram Stories to gather audience preferences\n- Create behind-the-scenes content for intimacy and exclusivity\n\n## Engagement Questions to Ask Followers\n\nExamples: favorite book of all time, e-books vs physical copies, favorite author, reading spot, genre preferences, most underrated book, book discovery methods, memorable quotes, book signing attendance.\n\n## Post Title Ideas\n\n**Recommended**: "Quarterly Faves & Wants" — highlights favorites while inviting recommendations from followers.
