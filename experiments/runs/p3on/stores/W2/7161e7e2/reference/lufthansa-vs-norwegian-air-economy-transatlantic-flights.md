Lufthansa: Full-service airline with friendly crew, comfortable seats at 31-32 inches pitch, complimentary meals, one free checked bag, strong loyalty program (Miles & More), financially stable.

Norwegian Air: Budget carrier with basic service, cramped seats at 29-30 inches pitch, pay-for-food model, charges for checked baggage (20-30 per bag), financially unstable with risk of cancellations.

Price comparison: Lufthansa around 850 for NY-Frankfurt round-trip in summer. Norwegian Air 200-230 cheaper but carries financial risk.
