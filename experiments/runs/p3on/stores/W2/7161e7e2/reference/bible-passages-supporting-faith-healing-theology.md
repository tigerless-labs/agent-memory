Key Bible passages supporting biblical theology of faith healing: Matthew 8:10 (faith not limited by nationality), Hebrews 11:1 (faith definition), Mark 9:23 (all things possible for believers), John 14:12-14 (greater works through faith), James 2:14-26 (faith with works), 1 Kings 17:17-24 (Elijah healing), 2 Kings 5:1-14 (Elisha healing Naaman), Mark 2:1-12, John 9:1-7, Mark 5:34 (Jesus healing miracles), Matthew 9:29 (according to your faith), Isaiah 53:5 (by his wounds we are healed), Acts 3:1-10 and Acts 9:32-35 (early Christian faith healing). Also connects to centurion story in Luke 7:1-10.
