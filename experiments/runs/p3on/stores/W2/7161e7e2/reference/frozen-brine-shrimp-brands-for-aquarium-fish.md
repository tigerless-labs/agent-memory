Recommended frozen brine shrimp brands:

1. San Francisco Bay Brand: Harvested from Great Salt Lake, rich in nutrients. Offers plain, with garlic, and with omega-3 options.

2. Hikari: Well-known aquarium brand. Offers plain, with spirulina, and with krill options.

3. Argent: High-quality, sustainably harvested. Offers plain, with omega-3, and with vitamins options.

4. Omega One: Specializes in nutrient-rich foods. Great Salt Lake source, high omega-3 content.

Selection criteria: sustainable harvesting, nutrient-rich, omega-3 content, no artificial preservatives, airtight packaging.
