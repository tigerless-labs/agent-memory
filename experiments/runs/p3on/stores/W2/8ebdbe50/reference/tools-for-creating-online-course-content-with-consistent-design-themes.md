Recommended tools for creating course content while maintaining consistent design/themes:\n\n1. **PowerPoint** — Microsoft's slide tool with design templates and themes\n2. **Google Slides** — Web-based presentation tool with design templates, real-time collaboration\n3. **Canva** — Graphic design tool with templates, graphics, and fonts for quick professional content\n4. **Articulate Storyline** — E-learning authoring tool for interactive content, quizzes, assessments, simulations\n5. **Adobe Creative Cloud** — Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects for high-quality visuals, animations, video\n\nAll support rapid content creation with consistent visual themes.
