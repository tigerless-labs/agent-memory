**4-Day Chiang Mai Itinerary**

Day 1:
- 9:00 am: Pickup from hotel
- Doi Inthanon (Google Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 1.5 hours)
- Wachirathan Waterfall (Rank: 4.5, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Trek to waterfall and Karen village with viewpoints (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Lunch at local Karen restaurant (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- King and Queen Pagoda (Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Hmong market (Rank: 4.1, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Return to Chiang Mai (Drive time: 1.5 hours)

Day 2:
- 9:00 am: Pickup from hotel
- Mae Wang Elephant Sanctuary (Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 1 hour)
- Lunch (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Bamboo rafting on Mae Wang River (Rank: 4.5, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Return to Chiang Mai (Drive time: 1 hour)

Day 3:
- 9:00 am: Pickup from hotel
- Wat Chiang Man Temple (Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 15 minutes)
- Bai Orchid Butterfly Farm (Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Huay Kaew Waterfall (Rank: 4.5, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Lunch (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden (Rank: 4.6, Drive time: 1 hour)
- Night Safari (Rank: 4.2, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Return to hotel (Drive time: 30 minutes)

Day 4:
- 9:00 am: Pickup from hotel
- Huay Tung Tao Sculpture Park (Rank: 4.4, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Pong Yang Jungle Coaster & Zipline (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Lunch (Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Baan Kang Wat Creative Village (Rank: 4.4, Drive time: 30 minutes)
- Return to hotel (Drive time: 30 minutes)

Key modification: Sticky Waterfall was replaced with Chiang Mai Night Safari at the end of Day 3.
