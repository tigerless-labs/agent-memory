**Recommended Products:**
- Meguiar's Quik Detailer (classic, gentle, non-streaking, safe for all finishes)
- Chemical Guys Detailing Spray (versatile, works on paint/wheels/trim)
- Autoglym High Definition (premium, deep glossy finish)
- Griot's Garage Detailing Spray (easy to use, all-around)
- P21S Concours Detailing Spray (high-end, showroom shine)

**Selection Factors:**
- Look for gentle, non-streaking formula
- Consider ease of use
- Check versatility (works on multiple surfaces)
- Choose reputable brands
