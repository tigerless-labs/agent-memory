**Ingredients:**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups chopped fresh tomatoes (or 1 can crushed)
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup oat milk
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: 1/4 tsp paprika, 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

**Method:** Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil (5 min). Add tomatoes, broth, herbs, salt, pepper. Simmer 20-25 min. Blend smooth with immersion blender. Stir in oat milk and heat through. Adjust seasoning.

**Customizations:** Add roasted vegetables, spinach, or nutritional yeast for extra flavor and nutrition. Serve with crusty bread or dairy-free sour cream topping.
