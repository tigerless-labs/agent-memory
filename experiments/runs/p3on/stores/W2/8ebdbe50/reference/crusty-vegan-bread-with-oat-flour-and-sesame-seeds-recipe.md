**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.5 cup oat flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 packet active dry yeast (vegan)
- 1 cup warm water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds

**Method:** Mix flours, salt, sugar, yeast. Add warm water gradually, mix until shaggy dough. Add olive oil, knead 10-12 min until smooth and elastic. First rise in oiled bowl, covered, 1 hour until doubled. Preheat oven to 425°F. Shape into round or oblong loaf on parchment paper. Brush with water, sprinkle sesame seeds. Bake 25-30 min until golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped.

**Notes:** Oat flour adds nutty flavor and tender crumb. Sesame seeds provide crunchy texture. Can add other toppings (nuts, herbs). For crustier bread, bake additional 5-10 min. Perfect with tomato soup.
