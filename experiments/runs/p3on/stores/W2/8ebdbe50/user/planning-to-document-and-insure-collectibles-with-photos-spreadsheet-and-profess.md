Plans to: (1) photograph each item from multiple angles, (2) create a detailed spreadsheet catalog with descriptions, serial numbers, condition, and estimated values, (3) get significant items professionally appraised, (4) obtain insurance coverage for the collection through specialized collectibles insurers like Chubb or State Farm. Interested in joining local collector clubs and connecting with online communities (Reddit, Facebook groups, forums) to learn more about appraisal and collecting.
