Workload: Full-time 15-20 hours/week, part-time less weekly but longer duration. Faculty support via discussion boards, email, group projects, real-time chat (Slack/Zoom/WebEx), social media, office hours. Quality indicators: university rankings, accreditations, faculty credentials, alumni reviews. Key advantage: flexibility to complete coursework at own pace while balancing other commitments.
