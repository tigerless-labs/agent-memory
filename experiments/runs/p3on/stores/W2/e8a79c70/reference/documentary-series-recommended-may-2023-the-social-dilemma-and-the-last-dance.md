From May 2023 recommendations:\n\n**The Social Dilemma**\n- Platform: Netflix\n- Release Date: September 9, 2020\n- Episodes: 7\n- Runtime: ~45-60 minutes each (total ~5.5 hours)\n- Description: Explores consequences of social media on our lives, featuring interviews with tech industry insiders and whistleblowers. Examines how social media is designed to be addictive and manipulate behavior.\n\n**The Last Dance**\n- Platform: Netflix (originally ESPN)\n- Release Date: April 19, 2020\n- Episodes: 10\n- Runtime: ~45-60 minutes each (total ~9.5 hours)\n- Description: Documentary series about Michael Jordan's life and career, using never-before-seen footage from the 1997-1998 Chicago Bulls season. Explores his rise to fame, competitive drive, and personal challenges.
