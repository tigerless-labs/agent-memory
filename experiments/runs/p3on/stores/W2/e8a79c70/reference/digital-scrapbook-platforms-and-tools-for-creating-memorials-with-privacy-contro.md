**Recommended platforms for memorial scrapbooks:**
- Google Photos: free, user-friendly, supports private albums with invite-only sharing
- Adobe Spark: free web-based design tool, offers private sections
- Canva: graphic design platform with scrapbook templates  
- Adobe Photoshop Elements: advanced features for photo editing
- Scrapbook Factory: specialized scrapbooking software
- Forever Artisan: digital scrapbooking software

**Audio integration:** Voice recordings can be captured on smartphone, edited with Audacity (free) or GarageBand (Mac), exported as MP3, then embedded or linked in scrapbook platforms.

**Privacy measures:** Password protection, private albums, invite-only sharing, custom permissions per person, two-factor authentication, link expiration options.

**Organization approach:** Tagging/keywords for searchability, thumbnails/previews, clear consistent naming conventions (e.g., YYYYMMDD - Event/Person), starred/favorite items, search function.
