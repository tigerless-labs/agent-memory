From May 2023 recommendations:\n\n**Ratched**\n- Platform: Netflix\n- Release Date: September 18, 2020\n- Episodes: 8 (Season 1)\n- Runtime: ~45-60 minutes per episode\n- Description: Psychological thriller prequel to "One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest," exploring origins of Nurse Ratched. Stars Sarah Paulson.\n\n**The Flight Attendant**\n- Platform: HBO Max\n- Release Date: November 26, 2020\n- Episodes: 8 (Season 1)\n- Runtime: ~45-60 minutes per episode\n- Description: Dark comedy-thriller starring Kaley Cuoco about a flight attendant who wakes up in a hotel room with no memory, only to find a dead body. Explores themes of trauma, addiction, and mystery.
