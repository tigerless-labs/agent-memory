**Classic French Omelette Recipe**

**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 eggs
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- Optional: 1 tablespoon finely chopped herbs

**Steps:**
1. Whisk eggs with salt until well combined and frothy
2. Heat nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until butter melts without sizzling
3. Add butter and swirl to coat entire surface
4. Pour egg mixture in and spread evenly with fork or spatula
5. Cook ~30 seconds until eggs begin to set
6. Gently lift one edge and fold to center
7. Fold opposite side to center
8. Tilt skillet and slide omelette onto plate
9. Garnish with herbs if desired

**Tips for Fluffy & Light Texture:**
- Whisk eggs thoroughly until frothy for even incorporation
- Use nonstick skillet to prevent sticking/tearing
- Cook over medium-high heat (not too hot/cool) to avoid dryness
- Don't overfill with toppings
- Fold gently with spatula—don't press down
- Serve immediately to prevent getting cold

**Popular Filling Combinations:**
- Cheese (Swiss, cheddar, or Gruyere)
- Ham and cheese (classic)
- Sautéed mushrooms (earthy flavor)
- Mushroom and cheese
- Cooked spinach with sautéed onions/garlic
- Spinach and feta (healthy, flavorful)
- Diced tomatoes (juicy burst)
- Tomato and basil (summer flavor)
- Fresh herbs (parsley, thyme, chives)
- Smoked salmon and dill (elegant)

*Note: Keep filling light—just a few tablespoons needed.*
