Organizations providing music therapy for seniors:\n- American Music Therapy Association (AMTA)\n- Music Therapy Association of America (MTAA)\n- AARP, National Council on Aging (NCOA), LeadingAge\n- Disability-focused organizations (Autism Society, Alzheimer's Association, National Parkinson Foundation)\n\nCommon music therapy activities with seniors: singing sessions, instrument playing, music-based exercise, memory lane sessions, lyric analysis, relaxation, reminiscence, improvisation, music and movement.\n\nBenefits for seniors: improved mood, memory recall, pain management, cognitive stimulation, social connection, physical rehabilitation, emotional expression, sleep improvement, reduced agitation, improved quality of life.
