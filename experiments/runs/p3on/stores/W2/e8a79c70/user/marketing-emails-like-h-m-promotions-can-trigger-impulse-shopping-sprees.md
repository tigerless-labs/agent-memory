User is aware that promotional emails from retailers can trigger shopping impulses and lead to unplanned spending sprees. In May 2023, an H&M email promotion triggered extended shopping activity across multiple retailers (H&M, Sephora, ASOS, Adidas) over several weeks.

User recognizes this pattern and is working to be more mindful and intentional about purchases rather than reactive to marketing triggers.
