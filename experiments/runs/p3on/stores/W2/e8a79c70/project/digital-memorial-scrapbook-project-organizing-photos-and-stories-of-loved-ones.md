Creating a digital memorial scrapbook to preserve and share memories of important people. Planned albums: 'Memories of Grandma', 'Memories of Alex', 'Memories of Mrs. Johnson' (former English teacher). Key preferences: private/password-protected albums, organized by person and theme, incorporating audio recordings of voices and stories. Content sources: photos from grandmother's funeral, photos and stories from aunt's hospital visits, classroom memories. Organization strategies recommended: by event and theme, chronological ordering, tagging for searchability, mix of photos/stories/audio/memorabilia. Wants to share selectively with trusted family and friends.
