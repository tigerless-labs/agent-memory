Makes a batch of quinoa salad jars on Sundays for quick, healthy lunches throughout the week. Customizes each with different vegetables (bell peppers, cucumbers, carrots, cherry tomatoes) and protein sources (grilled chicken, tofu, beans, edamame) to keep flavors varied and interesting. Pairs with vinaigrette dressing. Describes this routine as a 'lifesaver' for maintaining consistent healthy eating.
