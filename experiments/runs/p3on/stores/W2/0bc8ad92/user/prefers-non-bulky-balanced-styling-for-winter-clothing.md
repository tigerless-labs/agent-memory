Favors styling approaches that balance volume—pairing bulky pieces with fitted items, using belts to cinch waist, choosing lightweight fabrics (merino wool, cashmere, cotton blends), and strategic layering. Dislikes oversized or puffy coats and opts for fitted silhouettes like tailored or pea coats.
