Decided to build wardrobe using a hybrid approach: create a capsule around 5-7 favorite graphic t-shirts from H&M, then mix and match with winter pieces from Zara and other pieces from Madewell. Strategy includes layering graphic tees under thin sweaters from Madewell and topping with winter coat from Zara to avoid overwhelming bulk.
