Top-rated golf courses in Las Vegas for resort golf: Shadow Creek (Tom Fazio design, private, 15 min from Strip, lush landscaping and rolling hills); Wolf Creek Golf Club (30 min from Strip, dramatic elevation changes and ravines); Rio Secco Golf Club (Rees Jones design, 20 min from Strip, rolling hills and dramatic elevation); TPC Las Vegas (PGA Tour quality, Bobby Weed and Raymond Floyd design, 20 min from Strip, undulating greens and water features); Angel Park Golf Club (36-hole facility, 20 min from Strip, Palm and Pine courses). Most offer club rentals, lessons, and food services.
