Rinse and dry canned chickpeas. Toss with olive oil and seasonings: salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika. Spread on baking sheet and roast at 400°F (200°C) for 30–40 minutes until crispy. Alternative flavor combinations: lemon zest with dried herbs (thyme, rosemary); cumin and chili powder for spice; smoked paprika and sea salt for umami.
