# Ocean Conservancy

Primary organization of interest for marine ecosystem conservation.\n\n## Trash Free Seas Program\n\nFocus on reducing plastic pollution in ocean through:\n- Reducing single-use plastics\n- Improving waste management infrastructure\n- Advocating for policy changes on plastic pollution\n- Community education and engagement\n\n## Volunteer Opportunities\n\n- Beach cleanups\n- Citizen science projects (marine debris tracking)\n- Policy advocacy campaigns\n- Community education and outreach\n- Data collection on plastic pollution patterns\n\n**Website**: www.oceanconservancy.org\n**Program page**: www.oceanconservancy.org/trash-free-seas
