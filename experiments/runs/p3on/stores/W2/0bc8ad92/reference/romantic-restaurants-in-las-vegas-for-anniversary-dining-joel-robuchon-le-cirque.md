Top romantic restaurants in Las Vegas: Joël Robuchon (MGM Grand, 3 Michelin stars, French, intimate upscale); Le Cirque (Bellagio, Michelin-starred, French-Italian, luxurious); Picasso (Bellagio, 2 Michelin stars, French-Spanish contemporary, Bellagio fountain views); Carbone (Aria Resort, retro Italian-American, sophisticated vintage vibe); Eiffel Tower Restaurant (Paris Las Vegas, 11th floor, French cuisine, Strip views); Twist by Pierre Gagnaire (Mandarin Oriental, French, intimate, seasonal); Honey Salt (off-Strip, farm-to-table, seasonal); Old Homestead Steakhouse (Caesars Palace, classic steakhouse); Bardot Brasserie (Aria, French-inspired bistro); Savor (Cosmopolitan, contemporary American). Reserve well in advance, especially weekends.
