Helicopter Tour Operators: Maverick Helicopters and Sundance Helicopters both offer private tours with champagne. Tour options: Neon Lights Tour focuses on Strip landmarks (Bellagio fountains, Caesars Palace, Eiffel Tower); Grand Canyon Tour for longer flights with Colorado River views; Vegas Strip and Downtown Tour includes Fremont Street Experience and Golden Nugget; Sunset/Nighttime tours for romantic experiences. Key questions when booking: flight duration, aircraft type and capacity, pilot experience, safety features, amenities.
