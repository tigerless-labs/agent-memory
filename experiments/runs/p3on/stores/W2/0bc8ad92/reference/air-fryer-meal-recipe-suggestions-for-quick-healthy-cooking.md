## Air Fryer Chicken Thighs with Quinoa and Roasted Vegetables
- Season chicken thighs with garlic, ginger, cumin, and paprika
- Cook chicken thighs in air fryer until crispy
- Cook quinoa according to package instructions
- Toss mixed veggies in olive oil, salt, and dried thyme
- Roast vegetables in air fryer
- Serve chicken with quinoa and roasted veggies

## Air Fryer Ground Beef and Veggie Stir-Fry
- Cook ground beef in air fryer until browned
- Add mixed veggies and cook until tender
- Season with garlic, ginger, and soy sauce
- Serve with brown rice

## Air Fryer Peach and Berry Crisp
- Mix frozen peaches and berries in a bowl
- Top with mixture of oats, almonds, and dark chocolate chips
- Cook in air fryer until bubbly and crisp
