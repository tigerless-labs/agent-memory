## Fencing Requirements for Baby Goats

**Height:** 4-5 feet minimum (baby goats are excellent climbers)

**Material Options:**
- **Woven wire fencing:** 2-inch x 4-inch mesh with minimum 12.5 gauge wire (resistant to chewing)
- **Electric fencing:** Not recommended for bottle-fed baby goats; better for weaned kids 3-4 months old
- **High-tensile fencing:** Strong but less effective at preventing escape
- **Cattle panels:** Sturdy and durable, easy to install
- **Goat panels:** Specifically designed with smaller mesh size

**Mesh Size:** Smaller is better to prevent heads/legs getting stuck. 2x4 is standard; 1x2 for added security.

**Posts:** Use sturdy posts spaced 8-10 feet apart with secure corner post anchors

**Gates:** Make sturdy, secure, and easy to operate; consider self-closing gates

**Safety:** Ensure visibility to goats; add flags if needed. Supervise during enclosure introduction.
