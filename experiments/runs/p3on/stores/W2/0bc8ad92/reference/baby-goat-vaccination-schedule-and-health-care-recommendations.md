## Recommended Vaccinations for Baby Goats

**CDT Vaccine** (Clostridium perfringens type C and D, and tetanus)
- Core vaccination for goats
- Given at 2-3 weeks of age
- Booster 2-3 weeks later

**Pasteurella multocida (P. multocida)**
- Given at 2-3 months of age
- Booster 2-3 weeks later
- Protects against pneumonia and respiratory diseases

**Key Health Concerns for Baby Goats**
- Scours (diarrhea) - monitor stool quality, can lead to dehydration
- Pneumonia - watch for coughing, rapid breathing, nasal discharge
- Hypothermia - keep warm and dry, monitor body temperature
- Parasite control - both external (ticks, lice, mites) and internal (worms)
- Nutritional deficiencies - ensure balanced diet with adequate milk and starter feed
- Umbilical cord care - keep clean and dry, apply antibacterial ointment
- Colostrum intake - ensure adequate colostrum within first few hours of life
