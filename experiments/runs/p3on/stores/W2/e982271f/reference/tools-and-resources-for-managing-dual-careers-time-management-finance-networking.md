**Time Management:**
- Trello — task organization and prioritization for multiple projects
- Asana — task and deadline management

**Financial Management:**
- Mint — spending tracking and budget goals
- QuickBooks — financial management, especially for side businesses

**Networking:**
- LinkedIn — connect with professionals in multiple industries
- Meetup — attend networking events in relevant communities

**Learning & Skill Development:**
- Skillshare — online classes for skill improvement
- Udemy — expert-taught courses on various topics
