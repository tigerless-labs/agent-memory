## Restaurants

### Poutine & Diners
- **La Banquise**: 24-hour diner famous for poutine

### Smoked Meat
- **Schwartz's Deli**: Smoked meat sandwiches at reasonable prices
- **The Main**: Smoked meat
- **Lester's Deli**: Smoked meat

Montreal smoked meat is cured corned beef, hot smoked and hand-sliced.

### Bagels
- **St-Viateur Bagel**: Fresh Montreal-style bagels with various toppings, reasonable prices
- **Fairmount Bagel**: Montreal-style bagels

Montreal-style bagels are smaller and denser than traditional bagels, boiled in honey-sweetened water, baked in wood-fired oven.

### Bakery & Casual
- **Olive & Gourmando**: Sandwiches, salads, pastries at affordable prices
- **Marché des Éclusiers**: Farmer's market-style eatery with fresh produce and homemade dishes
- **Pizzeria Gema**: Neapolitan-style pizza, affordable
- **Patati Patata**: Casual diner with burgers and fries

## Must-Try Foods
1. **Montreal-style bagels** — Smaller, denser; boiled in honey-sweetened water; wood-fired oven baked
2. **Smoked meat** — Cured corned beef, hot smoked, hand-sliced
3. **Maple syrup** — Sample at cabane à sucre (sugar shack) outside the city
4. **Tourtière** — French-Canadian meat pie with pork, beef, and spices
5. **Montreal-style steak seasoning** — Garlic, salt, pepper, and other spices for grilled meats
6. **Pâté chinois**
7. **Pouding chômeur**
8. **Butter tarts**

## Neighborhoods
- **Plateau-Mont-Royal** — Colorful houses, hip cafes, bars, vibrant arts scene
- **Mile End** — Cafes, bars, boutiques, bakeries, music venues, galleries
- **Old Montreal** — Historic district, cobblestone streets, churches, museums, galleries
- **Little Italy** — Italian restaurants, cafes, specialty shops, Italian Week festival
- **Saint-Henri** — Stylish restaurants, cafes, bars, Atwater Market
- **Quartier des Spectacles** — Cultural heart with theaters, music venues, galleries, festivals

## Budget Activities
- **Mount Royal Park** — Hiking trails, picnic areas, city views
- **Markets** — Jean-Talon Market, Atwater Market, Lachine Market
- **Museums** — Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, Museum of Contemporary Art, Pointe-à-Callière Museum (many have reduced admission days)
- **Street art** — Plateau and Mile End neighborhoods
- **Festivals** — Montreal Jazz Festival, Just for Laughs, Montreal Fireworks Festival (some free programming)
- **Old Montreal** — Explore historic architecture and cobblestone streets

## Transportation & Getting Around
- **Public transit** — Buses, metro trains, commuter trains; tickets/passes at metro stations, convenience stores, online
- **Bixi** — Bike-sharing system with stations throughout city
- **Walking** — Montreal is walkable with attractions close together
- **Car-sharing** — Car2Go and Communauto for short-term rentals
- **Taxis/Uber** — Available but expensive during peak hours
- **Driving** — Parking difficult and expensive

Note: Montreal has excellent, efficient, affordable public transportation with an efficient metro system making it easy to navigate on budget.
