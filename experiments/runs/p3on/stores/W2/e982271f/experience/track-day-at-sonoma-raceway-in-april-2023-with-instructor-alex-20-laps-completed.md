Attended a track day at Sonoma Raceway in April 2023 with instructor Alex. Successfully completed 20 laps without any incidents. Alex provided valuable feedback on cornering and braking techniques. By the end of the day, lap times had improved significantly, and was able to carry more speed through turns and feel more confident in the car's abilities. Especially enjoyed the carousel turn and esses section. Noted that the track has great flow and elevation changes add challenge.
