**How to Make Flavored Syrups for Cocktails**

**Base Syrups:**
- **Simple syrup:** 1:1 sugar to water
- **Rich syrup:** 2:1 sugar to water (thicker, more concentrated)

**Infusion Methods:**

1. **Hot infusion** (for delicate flavors like fruits and herbs)
   - Steep flavor ingredient in hot water (180-190°F)
   - Duration: 10-30 minutes

2. **Cold infusion** (for robust flavors like spices and roots)
   - Steep flavor ingredient in cold water
   - Duration: several hours or overnight

**Ratio Guidelines:**
- Start with 1 part flavor ingredient to 2 parts syrup
- Experiment to find your preference

**Processing:**
- Strain through fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth
- Optional: filter through coffee filter or paper towels for clarity

**Storage:**
- Keep in airtight container in refrigerator
- Typical shelf life: 2-4 weeks
- Always label and date

**Popular Flavored Syrups for Cocktails:**
- Fruit syrups: raspberry, strawberry, orange
- Herbal syrups: mint, basil, lemongrass
- Spiced syrups: cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger
- Tea syrups: earl grey, green tea, chai
- Nut syrups: almond, hazelnut, pecan

**Example: Cardamom-Honey Syrup** (for Old Fashioned)
- Infuse crushed cardamom pods in hot honey syrup (1:1 honey and water)
- Steep 10-15 minutes
- Strain and filter
- Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks
