**Cardamom Old Fashioned** — twist on the classic with cardamom bitters.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz gin-based whiskey or botanical bourbon
- 1/2 oz orange liqueur (Cointreau or Grand Marnier)
- 1/2 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey and water)
- 2 dashes cardamom bitters
- Large ice cube
- Orange peel (optional garnish)
- Dried fruit or spiced cherry (optional garnish)

**Method:**
1. In a mixing glass, combine whiskey, orange liqueur, and honey syrup
2. Add cardamom bitters
3. Stir for about 30 seconds to combine and chill
4. Strain into a rocks glass with a large ice cube
5. Garnish with orange peel, dried fruit, or spiced cherry if desired

**Why these ingredients work:**
- Cardamom and orange are a classic pairing (Indian desserts)
- Honey syrup adds subtle sweetness, complements spicy/herbal cardamom
- Orange liqueur enhances citrus notes
- Dried fruit or spiced cherry adds fruity contrast to savory cardamom flavors
