## Key Attractions
- **The Blue Lagoon** — geothermal spa with turquoise waters, mineral-rich
- **The Golden Circle** — Geysir geysers, Gullfoss waterfall, Thingvellir National Park
- **Vatnajokull Glacier** — Europe's largest glacier, ice caves
- **Jokulsarlon Glacier Lagoon** — icebergs from Breidamerkurjokull
- **Seljalandsfoss & Skogafoss** — major waterfalls (60+ meters)
- **Reynisfjara Beach** — black sand beach with basalt columns
- **Kerid Crater Lake** — bright blue crater with red cliffs
- **Skaftafell National Park** — glacier-tipped mountains, hiking
- **Hverir** — geothermal area with mud pots
- **Askja Caldera** — remote highland crater lake

## Best Times to Visit
- **Summer (June-Aug)** — milder weather, longest daylight hours; most crowded/expensive
- **December-February** — Northern Lights viewing; shorter daylight, colder, fewer tourists
- **May & September** — mild weather, smaller crowds, lower prices; good for hiking

## Travel Tips
- **Car rental** — popular for flexibility; challenging driving conditions and weather
- **Tours** — great for learning more; many companies offer day/multi-day excursions
- **Budget accommodation** — camping or hostels cheaper than hotels; book in advance
- **Packing** — layers, waterproof/windproof clothing, sturdy hiking shoes, hat, gloves
- **Itinerary** — plan ahead but leave room for spontaneity and off-road discoveries
- **Respect nature** — leave no trace, follow trails, respect wildlife and local communities

## Infrastructure Notes
- Majority of population has access to geothermal heating and reliable electricity
- Power outages are rare and quickly fixed
- Well-developed emergency services for extreme weather, avalanches, volcanic eruptions
