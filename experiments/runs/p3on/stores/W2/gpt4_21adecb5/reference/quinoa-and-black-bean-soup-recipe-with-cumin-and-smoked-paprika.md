Ingredients: olive oil, onion, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, black beans, quinoa (1/2 cup). Heat oil, sauté onion and garlic, add spices, add broth, tomatoes, beans, and quinoa. Simmer 15-20 minutes until quinoa is cooked. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with cilantro.
