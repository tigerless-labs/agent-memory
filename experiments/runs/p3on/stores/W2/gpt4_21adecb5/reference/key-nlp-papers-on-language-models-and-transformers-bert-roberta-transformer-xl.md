Language Models and Transformers papers: BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding (Devlin et al., 2019) — seminal paper on BERT. RoBERTa: A Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach (Liu et al., 2019) — improved BERT variant. Transformer-XL: Attentive Language Models beyond a Fixed-Length Context (Dai et al., 2019) — extends transformer context length. Also recommended: Attention is not Explanation (Jain et al., 2019), The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis (Frankle et al., 2019), Adversarial Examples Are Not Bugs, They Are Features (Ilyas et al., 2019).
