Brands offering unique, lesser-known bitters for gin cocktails:

- **The Bitter Truth**
- **Bittercube**
- **The Bitter End**
- **Fee Brothers**
- **House of Botanicals**

Recommended varieties to explore:
- Sarsaparilla Bitters (sweet, spicy, herbal)
- Yuzu Bitters (bright, citrusy, Japanese)
- Cardamom Bitters (sweet, spicy, herbal—especially good for Old Fashioned)
- Cherry Blossom Bitters (delicate, floral)
- Smoked Hickory Bitters (smoky, savory)
- Celery Bitters (fresh, herbal)
- Grapefruit-Scallion Bitters (bright citrus with savory pungency)
- Black Truffle Bitters (luxurious, earthy)

Tip: Start with 2-3 dashes when trying new bitters, then adjust to taste.
