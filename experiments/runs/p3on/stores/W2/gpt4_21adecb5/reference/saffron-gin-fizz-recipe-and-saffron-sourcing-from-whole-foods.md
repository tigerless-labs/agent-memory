**Saffron Gin Fizz** — unique gin cocktail with exotic flavor profile.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz saffron-infused simple syrup
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey and water)
- Egg white
- Dash of orange bitters
- Saffron thread for garnish

**Method:**
1. Combine gin, saffron syrup, lemon juice, and honey syrup in a shaker
2. Dry shake (without ice) to emulsify the egg white
3. Add ice and shake again
4. Strain into a Collins glass
5. Garnish with a saffron thread

**Saffron Infusion:**
- Steep 1/2 teaspoon saffron threads in 1 cup hot water for 10-15 minutes

**Where to buy saffron:**
- Whole Foods (specialty/gourmet section)
- Specialty spice stores (Middle Eastern, Indian, Spanish)
- Online: Amazon, Penzeys Spices, The Spice House, Sahadi's

**What to look for:**
- Deep red-orange color
- Strong, sweet, earthy aroma
- Threads preferred over powder (powder can be adulterated)
- Check country of origin: Spain, Iran, or Kashmir (Spanish highest quality)
