**Gin Cocktails with Accessible Ingredients**

**Cucumber-Lime Refresher**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 oz cucumber juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- Muddle cucumber slices in simple syrup first
- Shake and strain into Collins glass

**Blackberry-Basil Martini**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz blackberry puree
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- Muddle blackberries and basil leaves in syrup first
- Shake and strain into chilled martini glass
- Garnish with basil leaf

**Grapefruit-Rosemary Fizz**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz grapefruit juice
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey and water)
- Top with soda water
- Garnish with rosemary sprig

**Spiced Apple Cider Gin Toddy** (warm serve)
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz apple cider
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz cinnamon syrup
- Shake and serve warm in mug
- Garnish with cinnamon stick and apple slice

Note: All use common ingredients easily found at grocery stores. Good for experimentation and building cocktail skills.
