Ingredients: olive oil, onion, garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, tomatoes, broth, peanut butter (1/2 cup), sweet potato, kale. Sauté onions, add spices, add tomatoes and broth, stir in peanut butter, add sweet potato (15-20 min), add kale (5 min), season and serve. Optional toppings: peanuts, cilantro, lime.
