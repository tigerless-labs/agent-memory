Recommended Spanish-language shows for language learning and late-night viewing:

**Drama/Thriller:**
- La Casa de Papel (Money Heist) — 5 seasons total, 41+ episodes (Season 5 divided into Volume 1: Sept 3, 2021 and Volume 2: Dec 3, 2021)
- Narcos — crime drama about Pablo Escobar
- Velvet — 1960s romantic drama about fashion designer and seamstress

**Comedy:**
- Paquita Salas — comedy about talent agent and team
- Los Hombres de Paco — sitcom about police officers
- El Ministerio del Tiempo — fantasy comedy about time travelers

**Telenovelas:**
- Yo Soy Betty, la Fea — Colombian telenovela about woman in fashion company
- La Usurpadora — Mexican telenovela about identical twins

**Documentaries:**
- ¿Cómo andamos? — explores Spanish culture and society
- Planeta Futuro — innovative projects and entrepreneurs

**General recommendations for watching:**
- Start with La Casa de Papel for engaging thriller with clear Spanish
- Features characters from different regions of Spain (various accents)
- Watch with English subtitles for language learning
- Pause and rewind as needed
- Take notes on new vocabulary
- Focus on comprehension over perfect understanding
