## Learning Recommendations & Study Tips

**Approach:**
1. Start with fundamentals before diving into transformers (neural networks, RNNs, word embeddings)
2. Focus on **intuition** behind attention mechanisms rather than just math
3. **Implement yourself** — implementing transformers and attention mechanisms solidifies understanding
4. Practice with hands-on projects (image classification, sentiment analysis, question answering)
5. Read research papers critically — understand motivations, methodologies, limitations

**Study Progression:**
1. Master basics: neural networks, backpropagation, optimization
2. Learn NLP fundamentals: text preprocessing, embeddings, language models
3. Study transformers: self-attention, multi-head attention, encoder-decoder architecture
4. Apply to specific tasks: classification, generation, Q&A, translation, dialogue
5. Extend to vision: Vision Transformers, object detection, image generation

**Community Engagement:**
- Join online forums (Kaggle, Reddit r/MachineLearning, r/DeepLearning)
- Participate in competitions and challenges
- Stay updated with latest research papers
- Contribute implementations and projects to GitHub

**Key Insight:**
With solid CS background, focus on understanding *why* transformers work better than previous approaches, not just the mechanics.
