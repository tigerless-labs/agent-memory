Recommended TV shows for late-night Friday viewing (suitable for watching until ~12:30 AM):

**Drama/Thriller:**
- Ozark (Netflix) — financial advisor launders money for drug cartel, relocates to Missouri Ozarks. 3 seasons with 33 episodes total (Season 4 announced for 2022). Season breakdown: S1=10 ep (July 21, 2017), S2=10 ep (Aug 31, 2018), S3=11 ep (Mar 27, 2020). User chose to start with this series.
- Breaking Bad (Netflix) — chemistry teacher turned meth kingpin
- Killing Eve (BBC America/AMC) — MI6 agent vs psychopathic assassin

**Science Fiction/Fantasy:**
- Stranger Things (Netflix) — kids battling supernatural forces in small town
- The Expanse (Amazon Prime) — sci-fi epic about colonized solar system
- Westworld (HBO) — explores AI, consciousness, ethics

**Comedy:**
- The Good Place (Netflix) — woman mistakenly ends up in the 'good place' after death
- Schitt's Creek (Netflix) — wealthy family rebuilds life in small town
- What We Do in the Shadows (FX/Hulu) — mockumentary about vampires in NYC

**Reality TV/Cooking:**
- The Great British Baking Show (Netflix) — feel-good baking competition
- Top Chef (Bravo) — competitive cooking show
- Queer Eye (Netflix) — makeover show with the Fab Five
