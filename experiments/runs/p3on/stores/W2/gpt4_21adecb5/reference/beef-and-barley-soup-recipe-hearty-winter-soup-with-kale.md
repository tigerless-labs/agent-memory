Ingredients: olive oil, onion, garlic, carrots, celery, beef stew meat (1 pound), pearled barley (1/2 cup), beef broth (6 cups), kale, parsley. Heat oil, sauté aromatics and vegetables, brown beef, add barley, add broth. Simmer 1-1.5 hours until beef is tender and barley cooked. Add kale and cook 5 minutes. Season and serve with parsley.
