Ingredients: olive oil, onion, garlic, ginger, Thai red curry paste, chicken broth, coconut milk (13.5 oz can), red bell pepper, carrots, cooked chicken, lime juice. Sauté aromatics and curry paste, add broth and coconut milk, add vegetables (15-20 min), add chicken (5 min), finish with lime juice. Serve with cilantro and lime wedges.
