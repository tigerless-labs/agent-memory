Communities where camera enthusiasts buy, sell, trade, and discuss vintage equipment:

**Forums:**
- Fred Miranda (dedicated Buy/Sell section)
- Photo.net (marketplace section)
- Rangefinder Forum (rangefinder cameras and lenses)
- Vintage Camera Collectors (Facebook group)
- r/photography, r/cameras, r/vintagecameras (Reddit)

**Strategy:** Can post Want to Buy ads or search existing listings; consider reaching out to local photography schools/colleges and their photography clubs for leads.
