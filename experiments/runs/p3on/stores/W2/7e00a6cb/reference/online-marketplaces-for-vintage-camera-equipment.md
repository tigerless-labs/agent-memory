Places to find and buy vintage camera lenses and equipment:

**Marketplaces:**
- eBay
- KEH Camera (specializes in used camera gear)
- B&H Photo (used department)
- Amazon (third-party sellers, use caution)
- Facebook Marketplace
- Letgo
- Craigslist (local)

**Tips:** Check seller ratings and reviews; research condition and authenticity; verify seller reputation before purchasing.
