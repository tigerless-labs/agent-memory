## Project Overview
- **Genre:** 2D escape game, deserted island setting
- **Game engine:** Construct 3 (chosen for beginner-friendliness, drag-and-drop interface, no coding required)
- **Final goal:** Building a raft by collecting 8 materials

## Chapter 1: Game Design - Core Mechanic
Players explore the island, find 8 materials, solve riddles and door puzzles, and craft these materials into a raft to escape.

## 8 Materials for Raft & Their Hiding Locations
1. Wood planks — hidden in nearby forest among trees
2. Rope — in abandoned shipwreck, tangled in debris
3. Fabric — in ruined building, torn from old curtain or tablecloth
4. Vines — in jungle area, hanging from tree or climbing wall
5. Rocks — on beach, hidden in sand or near water
6. Shells — on beach, washed up on shore or buried in sand
7. Tar — in tar pit or oil spill, extracted with right tools
8. Plastic bottles — in trash pile or littered on beach

## Riddle Examples for Puzzles
1. Q: "I am a word of three letters, but add two more and I become fewer." A: Few
2. Q: "I am not alive, but I grow; I don't have lungs, but I need air; I don't have a mouth, but I need water to live." A: Fire
3. Q: "I am light as a feather, yet the strongest man cannot hold me for much more than a minute." A: Breath

## Door Unlock Puzzle Example
- Locked door requires combination to open
- Combination hidden on nearby statue or signpost
- Player uses code-breaking tool (decoder ring or cipher wheel) to decipher

## Chapter 3: Visual & Audio Development Tasks
1. Develop visual style (concept art, storyboards, sketches)
2. Create artwork (backgrounds, characters, objects, UI) using Photoshop, GIMP, or Inkscape
3. Add animations and visual effects
4. Develop audio style (mood board, sound references)
5. Create audio elements (background music, sound effects) using Ableton, Logic Pro, or Audacity
6. Integrate visuals and audio into Construct 3
7. Test and refine for seamless integration and immersive atmosphere
