## Project: African Geomancy Quiz (50 questions)

**Format:** Multiple-choice questions with correct answers and explanations

**Content covered:**
1. **Yoruba/Ifa geomancy system** (Questions 1-12):
   - 16 Odu figures: Iwori, Oyeku, Obara, Osa, Odi, Ogbe, Otura, Ogunda, Irete
   - Four elements: Earth, Water, Light, Darkness
   - Celestial bodies: Sun (Ogbe), Moon (Odi), Stars (Otura)

2. **Western geomancy figures** (Questions 26-35):
   - Figures: Albus, Populus, Fortuna Minor/Major, Carcer, Caput Draconis, Acquisitio, Rubeus, Laetitia, Tristitia, Amor, Cauda Draconis, Via, Puer
   - Associated planets and themes (wealth, success, love, spirituality, obstacles)

3. **Bambara geomancy system** (Questions 21-30+):
   - Figures: Kanyi, Tugu, Yenye, Teme, Tulu
   - Houses: Faden, Basen, Sisi, Wurude
   - Themes: solutions, progress, movement, change, completion, health, love, protection, agriculture
