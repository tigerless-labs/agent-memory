Storage organization solutions for bathroom cabinets:\n- Stackable baskets or containers for grouping items\n- Adjustable shelves or drawer dividers\n- Over-the-door storage racks or hooks\n- Adhesive pockets or bins\n- Lazy Susans or turntables for corner access\n- Labeling systems for easy identification\n- Repurpose existing items (old baskets, containers)\n\nKey principles: create functional system based on habits, keep it visually appealing, be flexible to adjust over time.
