## Recommended 3-day winter itinerary for Nanjing, Jiangsu Province

### Day 1
- **Niushou Mountain** — Buddhist cultural park with pavilions, temples, pagodas, and statue of the Buddha; serene and peaceful in winter with refreshing air
- **Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan)** — Snow-covered landscape in winter; includes Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum and Xuanwu Lake boat rides

### Day 2
- **Nanjing City Wall** — Ancient military fortifications; one of the largest defense systems in the world; cool weather makes for comfortable exploration
- **Confucius Temple Area (Fuzimiao)** — Historic cultural district with temples, gardens, markets; takes on peaceful and romantic atmosphere in winter, ideal for couples

### Day 3
- **Ming Palace Ruins (Minggugong)** — Remains of Ming Dynasty imperial palace; mysterious quality in winter; cool weather comfortable for exploration
- **Presidential Palace (Zongtong Fu)** — Historic residence of Sun Yat-sen; serene and tranquil in winter; explore various buildings and gardens

**Seasonal note:** Winter visits offer peaceful, serene atmospheres; snow-covered landscapes; cool but comfortable weather for walking and exploring.
