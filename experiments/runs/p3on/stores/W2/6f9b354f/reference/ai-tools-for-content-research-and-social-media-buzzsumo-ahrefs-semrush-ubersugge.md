For content research and topic suggestion: BuzzSumo (trending topics, content performance), Ahrefs (content ideas based on competitors), SEMrush (topic research and content gaps). For keyword research: Google Keyword Planner (free), Moz Keyword Explorer, Ubersuggest (free). For content optimization: Acrolinx, Content Blossom, Conductor. For Q&A insights: Answer Engine (AI-powered Q&A platform), Quora (community questions), Google Trends (search trends). Other tools for engagement: Chatbots (ManyChat, Dialogflow), Sentiment analysis (Brandwatch, Hootsuite Insights), Personalization (AgilOne, Sailthru), Influencer ID (AspireIQ, Upfluence), Social listening (Sprout Social, Brand24).
