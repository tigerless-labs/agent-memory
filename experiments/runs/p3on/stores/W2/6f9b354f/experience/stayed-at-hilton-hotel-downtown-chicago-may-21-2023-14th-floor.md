Checked out on May 21, 2023. Room was on the 14th floor with a balcony. Enjoyed amazing views, especially at night. Used the fitness center twice during the stay and was impressed by the range of equipment available. Visited coffee shops (Intelligentsia), Willis Tower Skydeck, took the Lakefront Trail, and Chicago's First Lady Cruises architectural cruise.
