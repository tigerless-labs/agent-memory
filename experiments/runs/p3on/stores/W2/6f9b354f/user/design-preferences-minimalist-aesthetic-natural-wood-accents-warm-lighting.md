- Pendant lights for ambient lighting
- Task lighting with table lamps and desk lamps
- Dimmer switches for adjustable lighting levels
- LED lighting for energy efficiency

**Greenery:**
- Incorporates plants into interior spaces
- Interested in small herb gardens or fresh flower arrangements
- Uses plants to bring nature and freshness into home office spaces

**General approach:**
- Browses online for inspiration and design ideas
- Pays attention to how design elements (like color, light, materials) affect mood and productivity of spaces
