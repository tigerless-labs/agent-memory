When using AI tools for content creation, prioritizes maintaining brand voice and authenticity. Approach: human curation of all AI-generated content, editing for personal tone, blending AI suggestions with human-written content. Views AI as a starting point for content, not a final product. Also interested in using AI for research and topic inspiration rather than direct content generation.
