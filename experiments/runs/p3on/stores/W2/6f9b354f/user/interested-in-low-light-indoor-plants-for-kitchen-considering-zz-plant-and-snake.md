Looking to add low-light indoor plants to kitchen countertop where there's a vase with fresh greenery already.

Specifically interested in:
- **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)** - low-maintenance, tolerates extreme low light
  - Watering: every 1-2 weeks in summer, every 4-6 weeks in winter
  - Fertilization: balanced 20-20-20 fertilizer once a month during spring/summer (dilute to half strength)
  - Prefers well-draining soil, low to medium indirect light
  - Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
  - Prune to maintain shape; remove dead/damaged leaves
- **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)** - air-purifying, low-maintenance, can go weeks without watering

Also considering these for kitchen: Pothos, Peace Lily, Dracaena, Philodendron, Chinese Evergreen, Spider Plant, Parlor Palm, Bromeliads.
