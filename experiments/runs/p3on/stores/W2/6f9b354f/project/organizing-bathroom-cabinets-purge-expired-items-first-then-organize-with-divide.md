Bathroom cabinet organization project:\n- First step: purge and declutter (remove expired products, items not used, donate/recycle)\n- Next step: use dividers and baskets to separate categories (skincare, haircare, oral care, etc.)\n- Utilize back of cabinet door for additional storage\n- Label compartments for easy identification\n- Keep frequently used items in front and center\n- Use lazy Susan for corner items\n- Place heavy items on lower shelves\n\nThis is part of larger bathroom deep clean planned for week of 2023-05-21.
