Decided to use AI tools for content creation and social media research after consultation with marketing expert. Starting approach: define brand voice and tone first, then experiment with AI-generated content. Tools to explore: BuzzSumo and Ahrefs for topic suggestion and keyword research; Answer Engine and Quora for insights and content inspiration. Prioritizing human curation of all AI-generated content to maintain brand authenticity and avoid robotic-sounding content.
