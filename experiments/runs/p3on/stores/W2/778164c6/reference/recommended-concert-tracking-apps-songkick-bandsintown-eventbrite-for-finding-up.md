When looking for upcoming concerts and festivals in your area, use these resources:\n\n- **Songkick**: Track favorite artists and receive notifications when they tour your area. Includes concert calendar browsing.\n- **Bandsintown**: Similar to Songkick, tracks favorite artists, sends notifications, has mobile app and website.\n- **Eventbrite**: Search for local events, concerts, and festivals by category or location.\n- **Local venue websites**: Check theaters, clubs, and arenas directly for their event calendars.\n- **Social media**: Follow favorite artists, venues, and local promoters for event announcements.\n- **Festival websites**: Research specific festival lineups and ticket sales directly.\n\nThese are better alternatives to resale sites for finding events and often offer better pricing.
