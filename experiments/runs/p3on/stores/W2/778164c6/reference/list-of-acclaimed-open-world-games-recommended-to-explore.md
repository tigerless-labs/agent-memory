Popular and critically acclaimed open-world games:

1. **The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** — Classic fantasy RPG with vast explorable world
2. **Grand Theft Auto V** — Modern urban open-world with sandbox gameplay
3. **Red Dead Redemption 2** — Story-driven western with detailed open world
4. **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild** — Action-adventure with complete exploration freedom
5. **Fallout 4** — Post-apocalyptic RPG with extensive world to explore
6. **Assassin's Creed Odyssey** — Historical open-world action game
7. **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt** — RPG with detailed world and compelling narrative
8. **Horizon Zero Dawn** — Action RPG set in post-apocalyptic world with machine creatures; features innovative gameplay and stunning graphics
9. **Minecraft** — Sandbox building and exploration game
10. **Far Cry 5** — First-person action game with open world to explore

All offer vast, detailed worlds with freedom to explore and interact in various ways. Each has unique attributes appealing to different playstyles.
