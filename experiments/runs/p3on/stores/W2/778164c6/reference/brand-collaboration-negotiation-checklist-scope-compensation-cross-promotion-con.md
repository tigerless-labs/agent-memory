## Key Negotiation Points for Brand Collaborations

**1. Know your worth & research the brand**
- List your strengths and unique value proposition
- Research brand's products, services, previous collaborations
- Understand their goals and KPIs

**2. Define scope of work**
- Type of content (specific posts, series, or all content)
- Frequency of posts
- Specific requirements from brand

**3. Compensation & expectations**
- Monetary payment, free products, or combination
- Specific deliverables expected
- Content creation, promotion, and engagement requirements

**4. Cross-promotion clause (reciprocal promotion)**
Example language:
> The Brand agrees to promote the Influencer's content on their social media channels [specify platforms: Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, etc.], at least [number] times during the collaboration period. The Brand will use reasonable efforts to ensure promoted content reaches a minimum of [number] impressions/engagements.

**5. Content control & approval**
- Brand cannot edit/modify content without prior written approval
- Brand must adhere to creator's brand voice, tone, messaging guidelines
- Creator retains ownership of all content
- Brand's usage limited to collaboration period

**6. Exclusivity & conflicts of interest**
- Understand terms and limitations if exclusivity is requested
- Ensure no conflicts with other collaborations

**7. Content calendar & strategy**
- Request brand's content calendar
- Clarify how your content integrates into their overall strategy
- Discuss timing for maximum visibility and engagement
- Identify peak engagement times (events, holidays, trending topics)

**8. Engagement & comment management**
- Brand's strategy for responding to comments/messages on promoted content
- Response timeline (e.g., within 2-24 hours)
- Tone/voice consistency guidelines
- Escalation process for complex/sensitive comments

**9. Measuring success - KPIs & metrics**
- Engagement rate (engagements ÷ reach)
- Reach (total viewers)
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversion rate
- Return on investment (ROI)
- Set specific benchmarks for success
- Schedule regular check-ins to review progress

**10. Timeline & deadlines**
- Start/end dates
- Content delivery schedule
- Milestones and review points

**11. Contract & legal terms**
- Request formal written agreement
- Review carefully, negotiate unclear clauses
- Clarify content ownership beyond collaboration period

**12. Communication best practices**
- Be professional and responsive
- Maintain clear communication plan (frequency and method)
- Keep records of all agreements and communications
- Set clear boundaries from the outset
- Be open to negotiation but don't compromise brand integrity
- Be prepared to walk away if terms aren't favorable
