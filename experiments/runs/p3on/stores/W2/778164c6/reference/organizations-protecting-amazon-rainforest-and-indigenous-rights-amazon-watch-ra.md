Five organizations recommended for supporting Amazon rainforest and indigenous community protection:\n\n1. **Amazon Watch** — Works with indigenous leaders and environmental organizations to protect the Amazon, defend indigenous rights, and address climate change\n2. **Rainforest Trust** — Conserves tropical forests by partnering with local organizations to establish protected areas\n3. **WWF** (World Wildlife Fund) — Conserves biodiversity and environment, including Amazon rainforest\n4. **Cultural Survival** — Defends rights, cultures, and lands of indigenous peoples worldwide\n5. **Greenpeace** — Campaigns for Amazon protection and promotes sustainable practices
