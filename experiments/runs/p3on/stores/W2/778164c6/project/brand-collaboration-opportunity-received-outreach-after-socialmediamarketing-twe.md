**Situation:**
- Brand reached out after seeing user's tweets with #socialmediamarketing hashtag
- User is in early negotiation phase with brand

**User's content areas:**
- Tweets about social media marketing
- Book reviews
- Fitness-related posts

**Key concerns in negotiation:**
- Fair compensation (monetary, products, or combination)
- Reciprocal cross-promotion (brand promotes user's content on their channels)
- Content ownership and usage rights
- User maintains control over brand voice, tone, visual identity
- Content approval process before brand promotion
- Content calendar coordination and timing for maximum visibility
- Brand engagement strategy for comments/messages on promoted content
- Clear scope of work and timelines
- Exclusivity and conflict of interest terms

**Framework elements discussed:**
- Request content calendar from brand
- Define specific metrics for success (engagement rate, reach, CTR, conversion rate, ROI)
- Schedule regular check-ins with brand
- Establish response timeline for comments/messages
- Set benchmarks for collaboration performance
