Researching smart door lock options for home security. Key considerations:\n\n**Deadbolt locks**: Higher security, better resistance to break-ins, adds peace of mind. Downside: more expensive, harder to install, less convenient (harder for people with mobility issues).\n\n**Latch locks**: Easier installation, more convenient to use, less expensive. Downside: less secure, easier to break, less durable.\n\nDecision factors to evaluate: priority (security vs convenience), location in home, budget, and household needs (mobility considerations).
