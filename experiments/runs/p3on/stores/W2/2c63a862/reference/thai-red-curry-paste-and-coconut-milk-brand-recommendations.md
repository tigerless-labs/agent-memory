Red Curry Paste brands: Maesri (deep, rich flavor, moderate heat), Thai Kitchen (accessible, slightly sweeter), Pantai (premium, complex and earthy). Full-fat Coconut Milk: Chaokoh (popular Thai brand, rich and creamy), Native Forest (US organic option), Aroy-D (Thai brand, slightly sweeter). Recommended pairings: Maesri + Chaokoh for classic deep/spicy flavor; Thai Kitchen + Native Forest for sweeter balanced profile; Pantai + Aroy-D for complex earthy notes.
