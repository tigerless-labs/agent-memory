Itinerary Structure: 2 nights in Heraklion (Nikos Kazantzakis Int'l Airport arrival), then 3+ nights in Chania (northwest coast). Restaurants: Heraklion - Peskesi (traditional Cretan), Karavitis Taverna (grilled meats/seafood), Erganos café (breakfast), Arismari (organic/vegan). Chania - Ta Chalkina, Tamam (modern), Koukouvaya café, Almyra (beachfront), Salis, Tafarnabu, To Spitiko, Chrisostomos, Enetiko, Thalassino Ageri. Attractions: Heraklion Archaeological Museum, Palace of Knossos, Venetian Fortress Koules, Agios Titos Church, Cretaquarium, Watercity Waterpark. Chania - Maritime Museum, Venetian Harbor, Firkas Fortress, Chania Lighthouse, Botanical Park, Agia Triada Monastery, Samaria Gorge, Elafonisi beach, Ancient Aptera. April timing aligns with Greek traditions and festivals.
