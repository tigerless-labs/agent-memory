Five Indian cookbooks recommended: (1) The Essentials of Indian Cooking by Madhur Jaffrey - comprehensive guide to Indian cuisine, clear and easy to follow; (2) Indian Cooking by Suneeta Vaswani - modern take on traditional recipes, vegetarian/vegan options, detailed instructions; (3) 5 Spices, 50 Dishes by Ruta Kahate - focuses on five essential spices (turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom) and their versatility, engaging writing; (4) The New Indian Slow Cooker by Neela Paniz - slow cooker recipes including chana masala and saag paneer options; (5) Indian-ish by Priya Krishna - modern, approachable take on Indian cuisine blending traditional flavors with international twists.
