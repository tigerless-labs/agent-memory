**Glenbow Museum** — Calgary, Canada; extensive collection of Blackfoot art, artifacts, and culture; Blackfoot Gallery features traditional and contemporary art including beadwork, paintings, and sculptures.

**Museum of Indigenous People** — Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA; showcases contemporary Blackfoot art alongside works from other Indigenous artists.

**Blackfoot Crossing Historical Park** — Alberta, Canada; cultural center celebrating Blackfoot culture and history with various exhibits and showcases of Blackfoot artistry.
