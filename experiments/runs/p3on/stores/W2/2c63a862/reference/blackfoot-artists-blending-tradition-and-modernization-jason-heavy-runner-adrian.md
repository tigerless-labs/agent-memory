**Jason Heavy Runner** — Blackfoot fashion designer; incorporates traditional Blackfoot beadwork into contemporary fashion pieces (dresses, jackets, accessories with modern cuts and fabrics).

**Adrian Stimson** — Blackfoot artist; combines traditional Plains Indian ledger art with modern pop culture images (example: 'Parked Pow Wow Princess' depicts a young woman in traditional regalia on tires in a parking lot).

**Lauren Crazybull** — Works in painting, embroidery, and digital art; incorporates traditional Blackfoot motifs and designs; series 'Everything is a Ceremony' explores spirituality and mindfulness in the modern world.
