Working on an academic paper addressing long-term storage of EBSI (European Blockchain Service Infrastructure) verifiable credentials using TR-ESOR middleware from German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI).

Paper uses W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model standard (https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/) and TR-ESOR recommendations (https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Publikationen/TechnischeRichtlinien/TR03125/BSI_TR_03125_V1_2_1.pdf).

Three key challenges addressed: (1) Long-term storage of EBSI verifiable credentials, (2) Privacy preservation of VC holders, (3) TR-ESOR middleware integration. Storage includes cloud, local, and decentralized database options. Follows ACM and IEEE guidelines for influential computer science research.
