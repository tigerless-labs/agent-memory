Multi-part backyard improvement project in progress (as of March 2022):\n\n1. **Perimeter fencing** - Planning 6-8 foot fence with burial 1-2 feet deep. Options explored: wooden (maintenance-heavy), vinyl (low-maintenance), metal (chain link/wrought iron), composite. Will include secure self-latching gates.\n\n2. **Luna's shelter** (lamb) - 4x4 ft minimum, insulated, ventilated, water-resistant. Sloped roof, raised foundation, dry bedding (straw/hay), integrated with main fencing.\n\n3. **Chicken coop** (Rhode Island Reds) - Predator-proofed with hardware cloth, 1-inch wire mesh, buried foundation, secure latches, roosting bars 2-3 feet high, covered run.\n\nAll structures include predator-proofing as priority (coyotes, foxes, raccoons, hawks, owls). Considering electric fencing for additional deterrent around critical areas.
