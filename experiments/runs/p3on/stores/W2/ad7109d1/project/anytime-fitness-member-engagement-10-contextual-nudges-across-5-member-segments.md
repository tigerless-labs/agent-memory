## Project Summary

Created personalized contextual nudge strategy for Anytime Fitness member engagement, segmented across 5 distinct member types with mapping to Behavior Change Techniques (BCT) framework.

## Anytime Fitness Member Context

**Member Base:** Young adults to middle-aged individuals (students, professionals, retirees)

**Key Gym Features:** Convenience, accessibility, extended hours, worldwide access under single membership, smaller gym sizes for busy lifestyles

**Member Motivations:**
- Improve overall health and achieve personal fitness goals
- Maintain existing fitness level
- Social connection and community relationships with trainers/members
- Group workout participation (Zumba, Bootcamp, HIIT classes)
- Quiet environment with ample equipment (some segments)

## 5 Member Segments

1. Young Adults/Students
2. Professionals
3. Retirees
4. Quieter Environment Seekers
5. Group Workout Enthusiasts

## 10 Contextual Nudge Types Mapped to BCT

1. Instruction on how to perform behavior
2. Prompts/cues
3. Social support (emotional)
4. Behavioral substitution
5. Action planning
6. Social comparison
7. Salience of consequences
8. Reward (outcome)
9. Instructional tooltips
10. Progress highlighting

Each nudge type has specific, personalized implementations tailored to segment needs and preferences.
