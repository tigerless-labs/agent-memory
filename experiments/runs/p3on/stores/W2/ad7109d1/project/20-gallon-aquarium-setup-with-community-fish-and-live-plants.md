## Tank Setup

**Tank Size:** 20 gallons

**Current Fish:**
- 10 neon tetras
- 5 golden honey gouramis
- 1 small pleco catfish

**Live Plants (planned additions):**
- **Java Moss** — low to moderate lighting (1-2 watts/gallon), temp 72-82°F, attached to rocks/driftwood, low-maintenance
- **Anacharis** — moderate to high lighting (2-4 watts/gallon), temp 65-85°F, floating plant, fast-growing

**Current Decorations:**
- Artificial coral
- Rocks

**Planned Decorations:**
- Treasure chest — to create a "city" area with the coral and rocks
- Plan to arrange treasure chest near existing coral/rocks to create themed hiding area

**Possible Future Additions:**
- Lemon Tetras (compatible with existing community)
- Zebra Danios (compatible with existing community)
- Both would school with neon tetras

**Care Notes:**
- Both Java Moss and Anacharis are sensitive to copper-based medications
- Regular pruning needed for Anacharis
- Need balanced water-soluble fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK) weekly for plants
