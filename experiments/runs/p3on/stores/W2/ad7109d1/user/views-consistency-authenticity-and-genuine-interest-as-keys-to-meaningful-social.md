Values three core elements when building relationships on social media:

1. **Consistency** — demonstrates commitment to engaging with others and staying active in the community
2. **Authenticity** — people can tell when sincerity is lacking; genuine engagement matters
3. **Genuine interest** — showing care about what others are saying and valuing their opinions

Prefers having real conversations (e.g., discussing dog breeds with a friend) over leaving surface-level comments. Believes taking time to respond to others shows they stand out, especially with people they admire.
