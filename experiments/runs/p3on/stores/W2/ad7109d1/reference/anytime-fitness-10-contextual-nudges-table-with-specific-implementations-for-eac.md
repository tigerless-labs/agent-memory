## Nudge Implementation Details by Member Segment

| Nudge | Young Adults/Students | Professionals | Retirees | Quieter Environment Seekers | Group Workout Enthusiasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1: Welcome tour instruction | Introduce affordable student plans & study-break workouts | Highlight time-efficient workouts & extended hours | Showcase low-impact exercises & age-appropriate activities | Emphasize quieter hours & ample equipment | Feature Zumba, Bootcamp, HIIT classes |
| 2: Prompts/cues notifications | Student discounts, study-break workouts, youth events | Time-efficient workouts, peak hours, work break suggestions | Low-impact exercises, senior events, age-appropriate activities | Less busy hours, equipment availability | Upcoming classes, schedules, special events |
| 3: Social support banners | Themed workout nights, social gatherings for young adults | Lunchtime classes, efficient workout routines | Age-specific activities, low-impact classes | Equipment availability, quieter hours | Group classes, community events |
| 4: Behavioral substitution | Stress-relief exercises, campus fitness events | Routines fitting busy schedules | Age-appropriate workouts for retirees | Less crowded hours, equipment-focused workouts | Group classes, social workout activities |
| 5: Action planning reminders | Student events, group classes, social gatherings signed up for | Lunchtime classes, after-work events, appointments | Age-appropriate events, classes, social gatherings | Less crowded hours, equipment maintenance updates | Class schedules, schedule changes |
| 6: Social comparison | Share workout achievements, events attended, social connections on social media | Share time-efficient workouts, fitness milestones | Share age-appropriate workouts, social activities | Share workout routines, equipment achievements | Share class experiences, achievements, social connections |
| 7: Salience of consequences | Event countdowns for student events, popular group classes | Countdowns for lunchtime classes, after-work events, challenges | Countdowns for age-specific events, group classes | Countdowns for equipment maintenance, quieter hours | Countdowns for group classes, special events, challenges |
| 8: Reward badges | Badges for student events, campus fitness challenges | Badges for time-efficient workouts, lunchtime classes | Badges for age-appropriate activities, low-impact exercise participation | Badges for equipment-focused milestones | Badges for class attendance, social engagement |
| 9: Instructional tooltips | Study-break ideas, campus fitness resources | Time-management tips, efficient workouts, networking opportunities | Age-appropriate fitness advice, low-impact ideas, senior resources | Quiet workout times, equipment usage, peaceful routines | Class schedules, popular workouts, community tips |
| 10: Progress highlighting | Fitness goals, stress-relief achievements, social connections | Time-efficient workouts, work-life balance, fitness milestones | Age-appropriate workouts, health improvements, social connections | Equipment-focused workouts, quiet times, fitness goals | Class participation, social connections, fitness achievements |

## Key Design Principles

- Personalization by member segment and motivation
- Multiple touchpoints (welcome, notifications, banners, badges, tooltips, progress displays)
- Behavior change framework ensures psychological effectiveness
- Each nudge aligned with specific member needs and preferences
- Progression from awareness (welcome) through engagement (actions) to maintenance (progress tracking)
