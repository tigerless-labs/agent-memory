## Restaurants near Mather Campground, Grand Canyon:\n- **El Tovar Dining Room**: Upscale, specializes in prime rib and wild king salmon, ~1 mile from campground. Reservations highly recommended.\n- **Bright Angel Restaurant**: American comfort food with patio canyon views, walking distance from campground. Reservations recommended.\n- **Canyon Café**: Casual no-frills café, omelets/sandwiches/salads, short walk away.\n- **Maswik Food Court**: Quick bites including deli, pizza, grill options, ~10-minute walk.\n- **Yavapai Lodge Restaurant**: American cuisine with steak and seafood focus, patio canyon views, ~1.5 miles away.\n\n## Scenic stops from San Francisco to Grand Canyon:\n- **Lake Tahoe** (3.5 hours from SF): Alpine lake with crystal-clear waters and Sierra Nevada peaks.\n- **Mono Lake** (2.5 hours past Tahoe): Unique saline lake with limestone tufa tower formations.\n- **Bodie State Historic Park**: California Gold Rush era ghost town with abandoned buildings and mines.\n- **Hoover Dam** (selected): Iconic engineering marvel with guided tours and bridge views.\n- **Valley of Fire State Park** (selected): Red rock formations, ancient petroglyphs, petrified trees.\n- **Hackberry, Arizona**: Route 66 historic district with shops and photo opportunities.\n- **Wigwam Motels, Holbrook, Arizona**: Quirky 1950s accommodation landmark with concrete wigwams.\n- **Desert View Watchtower & Drive**: Historic tower designed by Mary Colter, viewpoints along final approach to Grand Canyon.
