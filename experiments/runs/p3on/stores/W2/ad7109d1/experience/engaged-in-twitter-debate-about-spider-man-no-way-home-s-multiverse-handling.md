Discussed the movie with a friend who thought the multiverse concept was well-executed, while feeling it was rushed with unresolved plot threads.

**Strengths they appreciated:**
- Emotional resonance and character moments (especially scenes between the three Spider-Men)
- Strong themes of responsibility, duty, and consequences of power
- Tom Holland's Spider-Man's emotional arc was well-handled

**Weaknesses identified:**
- Multiverse concept felt rushed and overshadowed character development
- Plot threads left unresolved
- Supporting characters (MJ and Ned) lacked emotional development and felt sidelined by the multiverse plot
- Some scenes felt like 'greatest hits' compilations rather than cohesive narrative
- Possible over-reliance on nostalgia and fan service

Despite these concerns, recognized the movie's overall quality and appreciates discussing it with others.
