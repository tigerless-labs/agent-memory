Watched Hannah Gadsby's 'Nanette' and found it inspirational for comedy crafting. Key takeaways for own material:
- Exceptional use of storytelling with personal anecdotes, historical context, and observations
- Emotional honesty and willingness to share vulnerabilities creates audience connection
- Subverts audience expectations with surprising twists on familiar comedic premises
- Careful pacing and tension control through pauses, tone, and body language
- Authenticity and vulnerability make material relatable
- Built around a clear theme (tension between comedy and tragedy)
- Tightly edited with each joke/story serving the overall narrative

Planning to study how other comics structure their sets and develop own thematic framework.
