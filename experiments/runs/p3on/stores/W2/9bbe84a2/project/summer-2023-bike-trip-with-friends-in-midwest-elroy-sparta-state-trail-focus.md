Planned a summer 2023 bike trip with friends, targeting Wisconsin and Michigan for mixed paved/dirt trail riding. Primary interest: Elroy-Sparta State Trail in Wisconsin (32 miles, mix of paved and crushed stone surfaces - user's first crushed stone experience). 

Selected trail features: relatively flat terrain (aligns with existing back comfort needs), scenic views through woodlands/prairies/wetlands, historic trestle bridge over Baraboo River, rest stops every 5-7 miles, small towns with food/water/restroom amenities along route.

Trip preparation: planning to pack basic bike tools, pump, spare tubes, first-aid kit, reusable water bottle.
