Bike trail preferences: likes mix of paved and unpaved surfaces (dirt/crushed stone), scenic views with wildlife and natural features, frequent rest stops (every 5-7 miles), accessible amenities (food/water/restrooms), and relatively flat terrain without steep grades.

Packing approach: brings basic bike tools (multi-tool, pump, spare tubes, chain maintenance tools), first-aid kit, sunscreen, insect repellent, reusable water bottle. Prefers to be self-sufficient but also relies on trail shops and services when needed.

Aligns with eco-conscious travel preferences and existing back comfort requirements.
