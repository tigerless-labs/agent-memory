Leaning toward HyperX Cloud II for gaming setup due to comfort and microphone quality. Concerned about reports of inconsistent virtual 7.1 surround sound in some games, particularly whether it would affect competitive Apex Legends play. Other headset options considered: SteelSeries Arctis 7, Turtle Beach Recon 200, Logitech G Pro X, Sennheiser GSP 670.\n\nNote: Pro players often use stereo or disable surround sound, so the surround sound limitation may not be a dealbreaker for competitive play.
