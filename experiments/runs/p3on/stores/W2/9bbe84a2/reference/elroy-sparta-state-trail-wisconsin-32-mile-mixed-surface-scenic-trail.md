## Elroy-Sparta State Trail, Wisconsin

**Route & Surface:** Elroy to Sparta; 32 miles total (~10 miles paved, ~22 miles compacted crushed stone); relatively flat with few gentle hills.

**Scenery:** Passes through wooded areas, prairies, wetlands. Features historic trestle bridge over Baraboo River. Wildlife: deer, turkeys, birds.

**Amenities:** Rest stops every 5-7 miles with parking, restrooms, picnic areas, basic bike repair stations. Small towns along route with restaurants, cafes, convenience stores. Campgrounds and hotels nearby.

**Bike Shops:** Sparta Bike Shop (Sparta) · Elroy Bike Shop (Elroy) · Tunnel Trail Cycles (Wilton) · Kendallville Cycles (Kendall)

**Resources:** Wisconsin DNR website (trail maps) · Elroy-Sparta State Trail website (conditions, events, attractions)

**Notes:** Good for riders new to crushed stone; suitable for hybrid/mountain/cyclocross bikes. Some weather exposure—prepare for variable conditions.
