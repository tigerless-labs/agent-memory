Recommended stand-up specials to study (as of July 2023):

**Recent/Key**
- John Mulaney - The Comeback Kid (2020)
- Taylor Tomlinson - Deal with It (2020)
- Hannah Gadsby - Douglas (2020) — follow-up to 'Nanette'
- Tom Segura - Ball Hog (2020)
- Dave Chappelle - Sticks & Stones (2019)
- Hasan Minhaj - Homecoming King (2017)
- Ali Wong - Baby Cobra (2016)
- Bo Burnham - Make Happy (2016)
- Ricky Gervais - Humanity (2018)
- Trevor Noah - Son of Patricia (2018)
- Aditi Mittal - Things They Wouldn't Let Me Say (2017)
- Deon Cole - Cole Blooded Seminar (2016)
- Wanda Sykes - Not Normal (2019)
- Jim Gaffigan - Quality Time (2020)
- Mike Birbiglia - The New One (2019)
- Sarah Silverman - A Speck of Dust (2017)

Using these for studying structure, storytelling, pacing, and developing own comedic voice.
