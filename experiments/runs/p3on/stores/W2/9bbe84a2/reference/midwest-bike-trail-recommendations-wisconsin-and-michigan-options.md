Wisconsin Trails:
- Wiouwash State Trail: 22 miles (Oshkosh to Hortonville), mostly paved with crushed stone sections, Fox River Valley views
- Lake Wissota State Park: 20 miles near Chippewa Falls; 6-mile lake loop
- Blue Mounds State Park: 15 miles; 4-mile Pleasure Valley Trail through prairies and forests

Michigan Trails:
- Pere Marquette State Trail: 30 miles (Midland to Clare), paved, Pere Marquette River views
- Leelanau Trail: 17 miles (Traverse City to Suttons Bay), paved/crushed stone mix, Lake Michigan views
- Sleeping Bear Heritage Trail: 27 miles (Traverse City to Sleeping Bear Dunes), paved, lakeshore views
- North Country Trail: 460 miles spanning Michigan (including UP), mixed paved/dirt, forest/lake/river scenery

Sources: Wisconsin DNR and Michigan DNR websites
