## Platforms for Industrial Skills Training

Explored as learning options in July 2023:

1. **Udemy** — courses in industrial automation, PLC programming, industrial maintenance
2. **Coursera** — engineering and manufacturing courses (supply chain, CAD, manufacturing processes)
3. **MIT OpenCourseWare** — free courses in supply chain management fundamentals, materials science, manufacturing
4. **Alison** — industrial automation, electrical systems, manufacturing processes, welding
5. **OSHA** — free occupational health and safety training

User was considering starting with Udemy's industrial automation course specifically.
