Research comparable prices on multiple platforms before setting prices. Provide detailed descriptions with high-quality photos from multiple angles. Be honest about condition assessment. For jewelry, books, and coins, consider professional appraisal/grading/certification first to increase value. Package carefully to prevent damage during transit. Include certificates of authenticity or provenance documentation when available.
