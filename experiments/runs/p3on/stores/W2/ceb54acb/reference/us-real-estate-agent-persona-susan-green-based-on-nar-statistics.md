## Real Estate Agent SaaS Persona

Name: Susan Green

Demographics:
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female
- Location: Suburban or rural area
- Income: Median 61,140 (per NAR 2019)

Profile:
- Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field
- Detail-oriented, organized, strong customer focus
- Works independently with brokerage support
- Builds local market relationships

Pain Points:
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking marketing performance
- Competition from other agents/brokerages
- Keeping up with real estate tech trends

Goals:
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Streamline marketing efforts

Tech Stack:
- CRM software for client/property management
- Email marketing tools
- Social media (personal and professional)
- Open to new tools for efficiency

Marketing Channels:
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking events and referrals
- Direct mail and email
- Social media advertising

Budget: Cost-conscious, ROI-driven, leverages brokerage resources

Source: NAR statistics https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics
