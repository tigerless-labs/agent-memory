Finds symbolism in cinema intriguing and enjoys analyzing how directors use visual symbols to convey deeper meaning. Values thoughtful, sparing use of symbolism that adds depth without distracting from the narrative. Dislikes when filmmakers overuse symbolism or make it too obvious, as this feels contrived and takes away from the story.

Discussed films including The Godfather (oranges as foreshadowing), The Shining, Blade Runner, Get Out, and The Matrix.
