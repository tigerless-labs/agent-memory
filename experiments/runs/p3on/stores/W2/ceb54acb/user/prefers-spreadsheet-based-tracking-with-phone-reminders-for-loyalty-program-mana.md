User's strategy for organizing loyalty programs:

- **Tracking method**: Spreadsheet with columns for program name, points/rewards type, current earned, needed for reward, expiration date, and notes
- **Reminders**: Phone-based alerts timed to specific stores or times of day  
- **Habit-building**: Incorporate loyalty app/card use into daily routine (e.g., always check app before shopping)
- **Staying informed**: Subscribe to newsletters, follow social media, download apps, check websites regularly, opt in for text messages
- **Motivation**: Tracking progress toward rewards (e.g., milestone tracking) helps maintain consistency

Prefers actionable, specific reminders over vague ones.
