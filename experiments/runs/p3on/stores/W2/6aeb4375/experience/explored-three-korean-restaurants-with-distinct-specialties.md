Recently tried three Korean restaurants:

1. **Bibimbap restaurant** — Known for exceptional bibimbap with crispy rice, savory vegetables, and runny egg
2. **Kimchi Jjigae restaurant** — Specializes in very spicy kimchi stew (Kimchi Jjigae)
3. **Creative Japchae restaurant** — Unique take on Japchae using sweet potato noodles, praised for the sweetness and texture of the noodles

Each location had its own unique flavor profile and style. User was impressed by the diversity in Korean cuisine across these establishments.
