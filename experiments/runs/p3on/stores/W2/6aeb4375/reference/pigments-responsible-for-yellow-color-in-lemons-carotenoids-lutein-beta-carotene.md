Lemons get their yellow color primarily from **carotenoids**, especially lutein and beta-carotene, not limonin. Carotenoids are a family of plant pigments responsible for yellow, orange, and red colors in fruits and vegetables. Limonin is a different compound—a bitter limonoid—present in the fruit but not the primary pigment. Flavonols also contribute to the coloration.
