Recommended basmati rice brands:\n\n1. **Tilda** — UK brand, trusted, offers premium and value options\n2. **India Gate** — Indian brand, popular, known for high-quality basmati\n3. **Daawat** — Indian brand, offers premium and value varieties\n4. **Zebra** — US brand, distinctive black/white striped packaging, premium and value options\n5. **Lundberg** — US brand, organic and non-organic options, known for quality and sustainability\n\n**Selection criteria:**\n- **Aroma:** Distinctive nutty and buttery scent\n- **Grain length:** Long, slender, separate grains (not clumpy)\n- **Texture:** Light and fluffy with slightly firm bite\n- **Age:** Older basmati is often superior (more mature flavor)\n\n**Cooking tip:** Use correct water-to-rice ratio and soak rice before cooking for even, fluffy results.
