Ingredients: 2 lbs pork bones (neck or rib), 4 cups chicken broth, 2 cups water, 2 tbsp sake, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp white pepper, noodles, toppings (pork belly, boiled egg, green onions). Bone roasting: 400°F for 30 min then 350°F for 1-2 hours. Can also use slow cooker (8-10 hours) or Instant Pot (30-40 min high pressure). Strain broth and assemble with noodles and toppings.
