Ingredients: 1 lb flank steak (or ribeye/sirloin), 1/4 cup lime juice, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/4 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp paprika, salt/pepper, 2 tbsp vegetable oil, 2 cups french fries, 1 cup grated cheese (Monterey Jack or Cheddar), radishes, lime wedges, sour cream. Marinade steak minimum 2 hours. Double-fry potatoes: 325°F for 3-4 min (blanching), cool, then 375°F for 2-3 min (crisping). Use Russet or Idaho potatoes, soak in cold water 30 min first. Cook steak to medium-rare, slice thin against the grain. Assemble fries with steak, cheese, and toppings.
