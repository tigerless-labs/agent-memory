Four lesser-known Andes hiking options recommended for exploration:

1. **Colca Canyon Trek** - Located in southern Peru, less crowded than the Inca Trail, offers stunning views of the canyon and surrounding mountains.

2. **Huayhuash Circuit** - Challenging multi-day hike in northern Peru, features high mountain passes, glacial lakes, and panoramic views of the Andes.

3. **Ausangate Trek** - Hike through small Andean communities in southern Peru, circling around the Ausangate mountain, offers views of snow-capped peaks and turquoise lakes.

4. **Salkantay Trek** - Trek through remote Andean villages and high mountain passes, features the Salkantay glacier, a lesser-known alternative to the Inca Trail that also leads to Machu Picchu.
