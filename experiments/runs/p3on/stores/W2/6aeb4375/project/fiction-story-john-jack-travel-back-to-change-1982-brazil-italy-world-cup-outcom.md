Creative fiction project: John (young football coach) and Jack (time traveler) go back to the 1982 World Cup Brazil vs Italy match (Italy won 3-2 historically) to make Brazil win instead. John handles tactical strategy planning; Jack uses a device to individually manage key Brazilian players - preventing them from drinking wine, accompanying them to gym, solving emotional relationship issues, and keeping them focused. Story explores time travel rules: only visit specific past points, cannot stay long, must avoid changing too much to prevent unintended present consequences.
