User is planning a dinner party combining Korean BBQ, Indian, and Middle Eastern cuisines. Date: TBD

**Korean BBQ options:**
- Bulgogi (thinly sliced marinated beef)
- Galbi (short ribs in sweet and spicy sauce)
- Dak Galbi (spicy chicken BBQ with gochujang)
- Pork Belly BBQ (marinated in sweet and spicy sauce)
- Japchae (stir-fried glass noodles with vegetables/meat)

**Indian options:**
- Chana Masala (chickpeas in tomato-based sauce)
- Palak Paneer (spinach curry with paneer)
- Samosas (spiced potato/pea pastries)
- Tandoori Chicken
- Vegetable Biryani
- Rogan Josh (lamb in spicy sauce)
- Sag Aloo (mustard greens with potatoes)

**Middle Eastern options:**
- Falafel (crispy fried chickpea balls)
- Shawarma (marinated lamb/chicken wraps)
- Hummus (chickpea dip with tahini)
- Tabbouleh (bulgur salad with parsley, tomatoes, mint)

**Planning approach:**
- Marinate meats in bulk beforehand
- Grill in batches
- Prepare sides (kimchi, rice, Japchae) in advance
- Set up DIY BBQ station with toppings
- Offer banchan (Korean side dishes) like kimchi, pickled radish, seasoned spinach
