User has been experimenting with different types of noodles and wants to try making popular Korean noodle dishes at home.

Specific dishes of interest:
- **Naengmyeon** — Cold noodle dish with buckwheat/starch noodles, spicy sauce, cucumber, pear, boiled egg
- **Ramyeon** — Korean instant noodles, customizable with vegetables, meat, or kimchi
- **Jjajangmyeon** — Chinese-Korean fusion with black bean sauce and noodles
- **Japchae** — Stir-fried sweet potato starch noodles with beef and vegetables
- **Bibim Naengmyeon** — Spicy cold noodles with vegetables and sweet/spicy sauce
- **Kalguksu** — Handmade noodle soup with vegetables and rich broth
- **Haemultang** — Spicy seafood noodle soup

User appreciates using high-quality Korean noodles and experimenting with gochujang, soy sauce, and sesame oil seasonings.
