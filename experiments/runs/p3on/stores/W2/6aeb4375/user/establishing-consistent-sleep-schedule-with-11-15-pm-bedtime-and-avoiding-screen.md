Working on establishing a consistent sleep schedule. 

**Current pattern:**
- Starts getting ready for bed around 11:15 PM
- Recently went to bed as late as 11:45 PM (later than preferred)

**Bedtime routine preferences:**
- Likes incorporating reading (10-15 minutes) into routine but tends to get engrossed and stay up late
- Prefers physical books over e-books due to blue light concerns
- Wants to follow reading with 5 minutes of quick relaxation techniques (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, or guided imagery)

**Screen management:**
- Plans to avoid screens for at least 1 hour before bed
- Will set a phone reminder at 10:15 PM to put phone away
- Plans to find a comfortable reading spot in bedroom to reduce temptation to check phone/watch TV

**Accountability:**
- Plans to ask a friend or family member to hold them accountable with check-ins every few days

**Other strategies discussed:**
- Dim lights 30 minutes before bed
- Keep bedroom temperature between 60-67°F (15-19°C)
- Use relaxation techniques (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, guided imagery, mindfulness meditation)
