User plans to make falafel for a dinner party and wants to achieve the perfect balance of crispy exterior and fluffy interior.

Key tips recalled:
- Use high-quality, dry chickpeas soaked overnight and drained
- Pulse chickpeas in food processor until coarsely chopped (not mushy)
- Use cumin, coriander, paprika, cayenne pepper for spice blend
- Chill mixture for at least 30 minutes before frying
- Double-fry technique: 325°F for 2-3 min, then 375°F for 2-3 min
- Don't over-mix the chickpeas with spices and herbs
- Use egg, breadcrumbs, and/or oats as binding agents
- Fry in batches to avoid overcrowding
- Drain on paper towels after frying
- Serve hot with tahini sauce, hummus, lettuce, tomatoes, pickles
