Owns Japanese short-grain rice which is good for sushi and Japanese dishes, but prefers traditional Indian basmati rice for Indian cuisine. Basmati's long, slender grains and nutty flavor pair better with Indian spices like garam masala and cumin than short-grain rice's stickier texture. Will use basmati for saag aloo and other Indian-inspired dishes.
