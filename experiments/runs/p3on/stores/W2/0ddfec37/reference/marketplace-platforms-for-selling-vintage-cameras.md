**Fred Miranda**: Popular forum for photography enthusiasts with dedicated marketplace for cameras and equipment.\n\n**Rangefinder Forum**: Community focused on rangefinder cameras including Leica, with 'For Sale' sections.\n\n**KEH Camera**: Reputable dealer in used cameras that may purchase or help consign vintage cameras.\n\n**WestLicht Auctions**: Leading auction house for photography equipment including vintage cameras, holds regular auctions.\n\nAlso useful: eBay, Etsy, Facebook Groups for vintage cameras, Instagram hashtags (#vintagecameras, #leica, #filmphotography), Reddit communities (r/photography, r/vintagecameras, r/leica).
