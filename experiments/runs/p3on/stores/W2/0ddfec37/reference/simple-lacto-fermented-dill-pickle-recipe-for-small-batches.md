**Small Batch Dill Pickle Recipe (1-2 jars)**\n\n**Ingredients (for 1-2 cups sliced cucumbers):**\n- 1-2 cups sliced cucumbers (about 1/2 to 1 lb)\n- 1/8 cup kosher salt\n- 1/8 cup granulated sugar\n- 1/4 cup water\n- 1/8 cup white vinegar\n- 1 tsp dried dill weed (or fresh dill heads for better flavor)\n- 1/2 tsp whole black peppercorns\n- 1/8 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)\n- Garlic cloves or parsley sprigs optional\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Create brine: Boil water, salt, sugar, and vinegar until dissolved\n2. Pack cucumbers into 1-pint or 1/2-pint jars, leaving 1 inch space at top\n3. Add dill and spices to jar\n4. Pour hot brine over cucumbers to cover completely\n5. Close jar loosely to allow fermentation gases to escape\n6. Ferment at room temperature (70-75°F) for 3-5 days until desired tanginess\n7. Store in refrigerator to slow fermentation; keeps several months\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use less table salt than kosher salt (it's more dense)\n- Check fermentation daily\n- Scale ingredients proportionally for different batch sizes\n- Variations: add jalapeños for spice, mustard or coriander seeds, bay leaves
