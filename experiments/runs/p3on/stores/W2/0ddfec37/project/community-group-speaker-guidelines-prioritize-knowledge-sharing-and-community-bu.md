The user hosts a community-based group where members learn from each other and exchange ideas.

**Speaker expectation:** Speakers are asked to focus on sharing knowledge, facilitating discussions, and building community — not promoting or advertising their products or companies, even if they represent open source projects.

**Communication approach:** The user prefers to communicate this tactfully and informally. Rather than directly saying 'avoid sales pitches,' rephrase it as requesting focus on 'sharing insights and experiences' or avoiding 'promotion/advertisement of products.' This maintains a respectful tone while clearly setting expectations.

This is core to how the group operates and should be mentioned to every speaker during the invitation/briefing.
