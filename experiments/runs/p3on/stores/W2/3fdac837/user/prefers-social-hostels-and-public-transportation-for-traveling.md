Based on Japan travel experience:
- Stays in mix of hostels and hotels; finds hostels excellent for meeting other travelers and maintaining social connections
- Prefers accommodations with social atmosphere (common areas, bars, organized events)
- Relies on public transportation which was efficient and convenient in Japan
- Solo travel is empowering and confidence-boosting but can involve challenges navigating unfamiliar streets and transportation systems
