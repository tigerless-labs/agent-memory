Traditional Italian dessert made with mascarpone cheese, ladyfingers, coffee, cocoa powder. Ingredients: 12-16 ladyfingers, 1 cup mascarpone cheese, 1/2 cup granulated sugar, 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder, 2 tbsp strong espresso/coffee, 2 tbsp rum or liquor (optional), cocoa powder for dusting. Method: dip ladyfingers in coffee for 3-5 seconds per side until soft but not too wet, beat mascarpone with sugar and cocoa powder until smooth and creamy, layer ladyfingers then mascarpone mixture alternately in serving dish, dust top with cocoa powder, refrigerate at least 3 hours or overnight before serving. Optional: drizzle with rum for adult version. This is a no-bake dessert.
