**Simple Sourdough Starter Recipe**\n\nIngredients:\n- 1/2 cup warm water (90-100°F)\n- 1/2 cup flour\n\nInstructions:\n1. Mix water and flour in clean glass/ceramic container until smooth\n2. Cover with cloth, let sit in warm draft-free place for 24-48 hours\n3. Feed starter with more flour and water every 24 hours\n4. After 7-10 days, starter should be bubbly, frothy, and nearly doubled in size—ready to use\n\nNote: Regular feeding and clean environment are key to maintaining healthy starter.
