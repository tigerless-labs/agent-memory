Flexible tour operators: Intrepid Travel (Flexi option), G Adventures (Independent Style and Spontaneous), Contiki (Free Time/Flexi), Busabout (hop-on hop-off bus), Eurail (train passes). Social hostel chains: Generator (Berlin, Rome, Paris), Meininger (London, Paris, Amsterdam), St. Christopher's Inns (London, Paris, Barcelona), Safestay (London, York, Edinburgh), Wombats (Vienna, Munich, Budapest). Booking sites: Hostelworld, Booking.com, Airbnb, HI Hostels, Couchsurfing, GetYourGuide, Viator. Budget transportation: FlixBus, Eurolines.
