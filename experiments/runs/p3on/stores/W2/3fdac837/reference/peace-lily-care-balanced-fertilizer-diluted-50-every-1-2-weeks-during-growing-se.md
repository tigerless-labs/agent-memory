Peace lily fertilization and care guidelines:\n\n**Fertilization:**\n- Use balanced fertilizer (20-20-20) or flowering plant formula\n- Always dilute to 50% recommended strength to avoid root burn\n- Fertilize every 1-2 weeks during growing season (spring/summer)\n- Avoid over-fertilization (causes yellow/brown leaves)\n- Apply fertilizer to soil only, not leaves\n- Water thoroughly after fertilizing\n\n**Additional care:**\n- Prefer high humidity (above 50%) - use pebble tray or humidifier\n- Bright, indirect light (can tolerate low light)\n- Avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)\n- Repot every 2-3 years\n- Sensitive to over-fertilization
