Classic Italian seafood pasta. Ingredients: spaghetti, fresh or canned clams (2 lbs), garlic (3 cloves), white wine (1/4 cup), parsley, olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon zest. Preparation tips: scrub fresh clams, remove from shells and rinse to remove grit. Cook spaghetti al dente, reserve 1 cup pasta water. Sauté garlic, add wine, add clams, cook until open, add pasta water, toss with spaghetti. Garnish with parsley and lemon zest. Can add cherry tomatoes or red pepper flakes for variation.
