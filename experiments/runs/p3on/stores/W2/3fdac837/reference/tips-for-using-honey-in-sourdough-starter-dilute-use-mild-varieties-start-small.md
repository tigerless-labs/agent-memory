**Using Honey in Sourdough Starter**\n\n- Start with small amount: replace only 10-20% of sugar with honey initially\n- Choose mild honey: clover or alfalfa; avoid strong dark honeys like buckwheat or manuka\n- Dilute the honey: mix with water since honey is sweeter than sugar, aim for similar consistency to sugar-water ratio\n- Adjust feeding schedule: honey ferments faster, so may need to modify feeding frequency; monitor starter activity\n- Be patient: honey can slow fermentation process, give starter extra time to develop\n\nExample: replace 1 tbsp sugar with 1 tbsp honey diluted with 1-2 tsp water in the starter recipe.
