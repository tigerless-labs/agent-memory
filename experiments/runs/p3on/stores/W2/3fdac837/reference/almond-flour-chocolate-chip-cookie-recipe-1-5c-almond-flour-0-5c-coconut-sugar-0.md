**Almond Flour Chocolate Chip Cookies** (gluten-free)\n\nIngredients:\n- 1 1/2 cups almond flour\n- 1/2 cup coconut sugar\n- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened\n- 2 large eggs\n- 1 tsp vanilla extract\n- 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips\n- Pinch of salt\n\nInstructions:\n1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper.\n2. Mix almond flour, coconut sugar, and salt in a bowl.\n3. Add softened butter, eggs, and vanilla extract. Mix until dough forms.\n4. Fold in dark chocolate chips.\n5. Scoop tablespoon-sized balls onto baking sheet with space between each.\n6. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until lightly golden.
