## Recommended Shows

1. **The Haunting of Hill House** (Netflix) — Horror with supernatural experiences in haunted house
2. **The X-Files** (Hulu, Amazon Prime) — Sci-fi horror, FBI agents investigating paranormal cases
3. **The OA** (Netflix) — Mind-bending, interdimensional travel and mysterious events
4. **The Twilight Zone** (CBS All Access) — Anthology series with sci-fi/horror twists, hosted by Jordan Peele
5. **The Umbrella Academy** (Netflix) — Superhero family with special powers, action and nostalgia
6. **Locke & Key** (Netflix) — Horror-fantasy with magical keys and supernatural elements
7. **The Passage** (FOX, Hulu) — Vampire horror series based on novels
8. **Colony** (USA Network, Netflix) — Dystopian sci-fi, occupied Los Angeles
9. **The Man in the High Castle** (Amazon Prime) — Alternate history, Nazi-occupied America
10. **Electric Dreams** (Amazon Prime) — Philip K. Dick anthology with sci-fi/horror twists

Recommended for Stranger Things fans.
