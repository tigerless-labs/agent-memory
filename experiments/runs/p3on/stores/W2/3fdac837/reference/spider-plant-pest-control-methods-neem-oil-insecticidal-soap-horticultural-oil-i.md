Comprehensive spider plant pest elimination approach:\n\n- Continue neem oil sprays every 3-4 days on all plant parts (undersides of leaves, stems, soil)\n- Supplement with insecticidal soap or horticultural oil mixed per label instructions\n- Prune heavily infested areas and dispose in sealed bags\n- Isolate plant from other plants for 4-6 weeks\n- Improve air circulation around plant\n- Increase humidity (spider mites thrive in dry conditions) using pebble tray or humidifier\n- Monitor for eggs regularly and remove immediately\n- Repeat treatments for 4-6 weeks minimum\n- If severe, quarantine and repot in fresh sterilized soil\n- Monitor plant response to treatments
