Spicy Korean seafood soup. Ingredients: vegetable oil, onion, garlic, Korean chili flakes (gochugaru - 1 cup), seafood/anchovy broth, water, gochujang (Korean chili paste - 1/2 cup), soy sauce, fish sauce (optional), sugar, black pepper, mixed seafood (clams, mussels, squid, shrimp, scallops - 2 cups), noodles (jjamppong, udon, or rice noodles), green onions, sesame seeds, kimchi for garnish. Method: sauté onion and garlic, add chili flakes, add broth/water/gochujang/soy sauce, simmer 10-15 min, add seafood, cook noodles separately, combine and cook 2-3 min more. Serves 4-6. Tips: adjust spice level, can add vegetables like zucchini/carrots/potatoes.
