Use unflavored unsweetened brands: Silk, So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo, Almond Breeze, Oatly, Rice Dream, Good Karma, Elmhurst 1925. Oat yogurt best for nut allergies. Rice yogurt is hypoallergenic. Hemp and flax high in protein/omega-3s/fiber respectively. Always check ingredient labels.
