**Recommended bands:**
- Pond: "Man It Feels Like Space Again" (2015), try "30000 MEGATONS"
- Unknown Mortal Orchestra: "Multi-Love" (2015), try "Can't Keep Checking My Phone"
- Temples: "Sun Structures" (2014), try "Shelter Song"
- Mild High Club: "Timeline" (2015), try "Windowpane"
- King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard: try "Am I in Heaven?" and "Sleep Drifter"
- Foxygen: "...And Star Power" (2014), try "How Can You Really"
- The Black Angels: "Phosphene Dream" (2010), try "Black Grease"
- The Horrors: "Skying" (2011), try "Still Life"
- Moon Duo: "Circles" (2012), try "Sleepwalker"
- A Place to Bury Strangers: "Worship" (2012), try "You Are the One"
