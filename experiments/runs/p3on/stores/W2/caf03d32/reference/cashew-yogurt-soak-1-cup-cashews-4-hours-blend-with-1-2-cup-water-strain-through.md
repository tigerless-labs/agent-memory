Soak rinsed cashews for 4+ hours or overnight, drain and rinse. Blend soaked cashews with 1/2 cup water (1:1 ratio) on high for 2 minutes until smooth. Strain through cheesecloth or nut milk bag, squeezing to extract all liquid. Chill strained mixture 2+ hours. Less water makes thicker yogurt; more water makes thinner. Use as base for slow cooker yogurt method.
