**Beginner-friendly YouTube channels:**
- Justin Guitar (Justin Sandercoe): Free online beginner course, gentle and encouraging
- Marty Music (Marty Schwartz): Great for learning popular songs quickly
- GuitarLessons365 (Bill): Comprehensive beginner course focused on acoustic guitar
- GuitarJamz (Martyn): Popular songs and riffs, engaging teaching style
- Andy Guitar (Andy Crowley): Electric guitar focus, engaging teaching style

**Tips:**
- Start with acoustic guitar (easier than electric)
- Practice regularly, even 10-15 minutes daily
- Use a metronome for rhythm development
- Find a practice buddy for accountability
