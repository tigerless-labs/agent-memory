Heat milk to 180-185F for 2-3 hours on low (kills bacteria). Cool to 110-120F over 1-2 hours. Add 1/4 cup plain yogurt starter per 1/2 gallon milk. Incubate covered 8-12 hours (overnight). Chill and refrigerate. Temperature monitoring with thermometer ensures consistent results; without it, heat until simmering at edges then cool before starter.
