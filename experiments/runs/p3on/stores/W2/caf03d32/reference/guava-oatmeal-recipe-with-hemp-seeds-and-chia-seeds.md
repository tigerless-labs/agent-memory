Breakfast recipe combining guava with oatmeal for protein and fiber boost:

**Base ratio:** 1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal

**Ingredients per serving:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (about 1-2 guavas)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds (complete protein source, all 9 amino acids)
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Optional toppings:** sliced banana, chopped nuts (walnuts, almonds, pecans), shredded coconut, cinnamon, cocoa nibs, or protein powder

**Preparation tips:**
- Use fresh guava for best flavor; frozen or canned works too
- Grind hemp seeds in coffee grinder for better mixing, or mix with liquid first to create paste
- Toast nuts before adding for enhanced flavor
- Store hemp seeds in airtight container in fridge/freezer to prevent rancidity

**Nutritional benefits:**
- 1 cup cooked oatmeal: 6g protein, 4g fiber
- 1/2 cup guava: 2g protein, 4g fiber
- 1 tbsp hemp seeds: ~5-10g protein, 1-2g omega-3s
- 1 tbsp chia seeds: 2g protein, 4g fiber
