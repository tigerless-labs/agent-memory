Flavorings: vanilla extract, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, fresh/dried fruit, lemon, cocoa. Sweeteners: honey, maple syrup, coconut sugar, date syrup, monk fruit, stevia. Add-ins: granola, nuts (almonds, walnuts), seeds (chia, flax), cocoa nibs, dried fruit. Add flavorings during or after incubation to customize.
