## Ukulele Purchase Research (May 21, 2023)

**Status:** Beginner researching first ukulele purchase.

Popular beginner brands:
- Kala (KA-15S Soprano, KA-C Concert)

- Cordoba (20TM Tenor, 20CM Concert)
- Mahalo (MR1 Soprano, MR3 Concert)

## Size Decision: Concert vs Tenor

**Soprano ukulele:** Brighter, more trebly (13-14" scale), easier for small hands, "plinky" sound
**Concert ukulele:** Warmer, more balanced (15-16" scale), fuller sound, comfortable for larger hands
**Tenor ukulele:** Deep, full-bodied (17" scale), more resonant, spacious feel

Initially leaned toward concert, but now considering tenor for fuller sound.

## Three Models Under Consideration

1. **Kala KA-C Mahogany Concert** ($100-$150)
   - Warm, balanced mid-range sound
   - 1.375" nut width
   - Compact, suitable for smaller hands

2. **Lanikai LU-21T Tenor** ($150-$200)
   - Deep, fuller sound
   - 1.5" nut width, 17" scale
   - Ornate rosette and binding

3. **Cordoba 20TM Tenor** ($150-$200)
   - Rich, full-bodied sound with strong low-end
   - 1.5" nut width, 17" scale
   - Excellent factory setup and attention to detail
   - Known for high quality
   - Ornate rosette and binding
   - **Currently favored option**

## Wood Type Comparison

**Mahogany:** Warm, rich, balanced; strong mid-range; traditional look; more affordable
**Koa:** Brighter, trebly; clear, crisp tone; beautiful curly grain; exotic high-end look; more expensive; longer sustain

Cordoba 20TM uses all-mahogany construction.

## Case Consideration

**Hardcase:** Maximum protection, durable, weather-resistant, but heavier and more expensive
**Softcase:** Lightweight and portable, affordable, but less protection

Leaning toward hardcase given the investment in a quality Cordoba instrument.

## Decision Point

Likely to purchase Cordoba 20TM Tenor Ukulele ($150-$200 range) with hardcase.
