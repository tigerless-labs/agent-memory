## Recommended Weight Loss Meal Plan

Example daily meal plan provided in May 2023:

**Breakfast:**
- 2 scrambled eggs with spinach, mushrooms, red peppers

**Snack 1:**
- 1 small apple with 1 tablespoon almond butter

**Lunch:**
- Grilled chicken breast with mixed greens, cucumbers, tomatoes, balsamic vinaigrette

**Snack 2:**
- 1 cup mixed berries with 1/2 cup non-fat Greek yogurt

**Dinner:**
- Baked salmon with roasted asparagus and sweet potato

**Optional Evening Snack:**
- 1 piece dark chocolate

## Key Principles
- Focus on portion control
- Choose nutrient-dense, low-calorie foods
- Drink plenty of water throughout day
- Gradual weight loss of 1-2 lbs/week is safe and sustainable
- Can include occasional treats like dark chocolate in moderation
