## Negroni Recipe Optimization — Gin, Vermouth, Bitters & Garnish Experiments

### Classic Ratio
- **1:1:1** (equal parts): 1.5 oz gin + 1.5 oz Campari + 1.5 oz sweet vermouth
- Variations tested: 2:1:1 (gin-forward), 1:2:1 (Campari-bold), 1:1:2 (vermouth-rich)

### Gin Options Recommended
1. **Plymouth Gin** — slightly sweeter, subtle, fruit & spice notes
2. **Bombay Sapphire** — crisp, citrusy, moderate juniper
3. **Beefeater London Dry** — bold juniper, subtle fruit
4. **G'Vine Floraison** — French, grape-based, floral (grapeflower, jasmine)
5. **Sipsmith London Dry** — juniper-forward, sweet, citrus
6. **The Botanist** — 22-botanical blend, complex, fruit & floral
7. **Death's Door Gin** — Wisconsin, crisp, clean, juniper/coriander/citrus
8. **Ransom Old Tom Gin** — sweeter, botanical, historical style

### Sweet Vermouth Options Recommended
1. **Carpano Antica Formula** — Italian favorite, rich/complex, cherry/orange/spices
2. **Cinzano** — classic Italian, sweet, fruity, vanilla/spice
3. **Dolin Sweet Vermouth** — Chambery style, lighter, subtle, herbal
4. **Martini & Rossi** — Italian, sweeter, pronounced fruit
5. **Punt e Mes** — Italian, bitter-sweet balance (enhances Campari)
6. **Cocchi Sweet Vermouth** — herbs/spices, slightly sweet/spicy/herbal
7. **Noilly Prat Sweet Vermouth** — French, rich, fruity, vanilla/orange

### Bitters
- **Angostura bitters** — traditional choice, warm/spicy/slightly sweet (2–3 dashes)
- Citrus-forward bitters tested:
  - **Regans' Orange Bitters No. 6** — orange/lemon/grapefruit peels
  - **The Bitter Truth Orange Bitters** — German-made, vibrant, citrusy
  - **Fee Brothers Orange Bitters** — slightly sweeter, citrus-forward
  - **Peychaud's Orange Bitters** — New Orleans, citrus + spice/herbs
  - **Scrappy's Bitters Orange Bitters** — craft, small-batch, bold citrus

### Citrus Garnishes Tested
1. **Orange peel** — classic choice, express oils
2. **Blood orange peel** — deeper flavor, subtly sweet, visual pop
3. **Grapefruit twist** — pink/red varieties, tangy complement to Campari
4. **Lemon twist** — bright citrus note, use thick-skinned lemons
5. **Yuzu peel** — Japanese citrus, tart/sweet, unique twist
6. **Meyer lemon twist** — lemon-orange cross, sweeter, less acidic

### Technique Notes
- Express citrus oils over drink by squeezing between fingers
- Rub peel around rim to coat with oils
- Place peel in glass on ice or as garnish
- Bitters: start 2–3 dashes, adjust to taste
- Stir with ice, strain into chilled glass with fresh ice
