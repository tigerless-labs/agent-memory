## Weight Loss Goals & Challenges

User is working on weight loss with these specific concerns:

- **Impatience with pace:** Initially wanted rapid results; learned that 1-2 lbs/week is healthy/sustainable
- **Motivation struggles:** Becomes frustrated when not seeing immediate results; tempted to give up
- **Food cravings:** Misses indulging in favorite foods; questions whether deprivation is worth being healthy
- **Emotional state:** Expresses feelings of doubt, frustration, wondering "what's the point"

## Key Strategies Discussed

1. Focus on non-scale victories (fitting clothes, energy, mood)
2. Keep food/exercise diary to track patterns
3. Vary workouts to prevent plateaus
4. Avoid comparing to others' progress
5. Practice moderation, not complete deprivation
6. Balance healthy foods with occasional treats
7. Celebrate small successes
8. Find support system
9. Think long-term (habits now = lasting impact)

User understands sustainable change takes time but struggles with emotional aspects of the process.
