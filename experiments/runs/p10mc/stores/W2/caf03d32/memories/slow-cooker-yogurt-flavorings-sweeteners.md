## Yogurt Flavorings and Sweeteners

**Flavorings:**
1. Vanilla — vanilla extract or vanilla bean
2. Cinnamon — pinch of cinnamon powder
3. Fruit — berries, mango, peaches
4. Nutmeg — pinch of nutmeg powder
5. Ginger — fresh grated ginger
6. Lemon — fresh lemon juice or zest
7. Cocoa — cocoa powder or melted chocolate

**Sweeteners:**
1. Honey — drizzle of pure honey
2. Maple syrup — small amount for rich sweetness
3. Coconut sugar — subtle, caramel-like sweetness
4. Date syrup — sweet, molasses-like flavor
5. Monk fruit sweetener — 150-200 times sweeter than sugar (use sparingly)
6. Stevia — pinch of stevia powder

**Other Add-ins:**
1. Granola — store-bought or homemade
2. Nuts or seeds — almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds
3. Cocoa nibs — chocolatey, crunchy texture
4. Dried fruit — cranberries, raisins, apricots

Can experiment with different combinations.
