## Cashew Milk Preparation

**Soaking the Cashews:**
1. Rinse 1 cup of cashews in fine-mesh strainer under cold water
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight
3. After soaking, drain and rinse again
4. Optional: change water and soak another 4 hours or overnight for extra cleaning

**Blending the Cashews:**
1. Drain and rinse soaked cashews again
2. Add 1/2 cup fresh water to blender with drained cashews
3. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
4. Stop and scrape down sides as needed during blending

**Straining the Mixture:**
1. Strain through cheesecloth, nut milk bag, or fine-mesh sieve into a bowl
2. Squeeze cheesecloth or bag to extract as much cream as possible
3. Discard solids
4. Optional: chill in refrigerator for at least 2 hours to allow thickening

**Consistency Tips:**
- **Ratio:** 1 cup cashews to 1/2 cup water (adjust for preference)
- **Thicker yogurt:** Use less water or blend shorter time
- **Thinner yogurt:** Use more water or blend longer
- Straining is crucial for smooth consistency without solids

**Straining Tools Comparison:**
- **Cheesecloth:** High filtration, catches small particles; can be messy; requires squeezing
- **Nut Milk Bag:** Designed for plant-based milks; effective filtration; can be pricey; gentle squeezing
- **Fine-Mesh Sieve:** Easy to use and clean; may not catch all particles; slight graininess possible
