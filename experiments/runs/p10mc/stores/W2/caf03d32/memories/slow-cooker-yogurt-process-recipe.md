## Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe

**Process:**
1. Heat milk to 180-185°F (82-85°C) for 2-3 hours to kill bacteria and denature proteins
2. Cool milk to 110-120°F (43-49°C) — takes 1-2 hours depending on slow cooker and room temperature
3. Add plain yogurt with live cultures (1/4 cup per 1/2 gallon milk) and whisk gently
4. Cover slow cooker, wrap with towel or blanket to maintain temperature
5. Incubate 8-12 hours or overnight — longer incubation = thicker and tangier yogurt
6. Chill in refrigerator before transferring to airtight containers

**Thermometer vs. No Thermometer:**
- **With thermometer:** Precise temperature control, faster heating, reduced risk of overheating, consistent results
- **Without thermometer:** Heat on low for 2-3 hours until milk simmers or bubbles form around edges; less precise, inconsistent results possible
- Thermometer is highly recommended but not strictly necessary

**Optional ingredients:**
- Sweetener (honey, maple syrup, sugar)
- Flavorings (vanilla, cinnamon, fruit)
- Probiotic powder for additional beneficial bacteria
- Can use different milk types: almond, soy, coconut, oat
- To make Greek yogurt: strain through cheesecloth to remove excess liquid

**Key temperatures:**
- Heating: 180-185°F (82-85°C)
- Cooling/starter addition: 110-120°F (43-49°C)
