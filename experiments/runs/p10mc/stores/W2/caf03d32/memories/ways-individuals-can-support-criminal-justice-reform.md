## Ways Individuals Can Support Criminal Justice Reform

1. **Get educated**
   - Learn about the history of the war on drugs
   - Understand racial disparities in the criminal justice system
   - Study the impact on communities of color

2. **Speak up**
   - Advocate for change through advocacy efforts
   - Support candidates who prioritize criminal justice reform
   - Speak out against racial injustice

3. **Support organizations**
   - Donate to criminal justice reform organizations
   - Volunteer with organizations focused on drug policy, police reform, and racial justice
   - Spread awareness about their work

4. **Vote**
   - Cast ballots in local, state, and national elections
   - Elect officials who prioritize criminal justice reform and equitable policies

5. **Practice allyship**
   - Listen to experiences of those impacted by the war on drugs
   - Amplify voices of affected communities
   - Work together to create change
