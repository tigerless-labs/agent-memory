## Psychedelic Rock Recommendations (Mellow/Atmospheric)

2. **Unknown Mortal Orchestra** (New Zealand)

   - Song: "Can't Keep Checking My Phone"

3. **Temples** (British)

4. **Mild High Club** (American)

5. **King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard** (mellow tracks)

6. **Foxygen** (American indie duo)
