## Beginner Guitar Learning

User is a beginner guitarist looking for tutorials and YouTube channels.

### Recommended YouTube Channels

1. **Justin Guitar** (Justin Sandercoe) — Free beginner course "Guitar for Beginners"; gentle, encouraging approach
2. **Marty Music** (Marty Schwartz) — Great for learning popular songs quickly; "5-Minute Guitar Lessons" playlist
3. **GuitarLessons365** (Bill) — Comprehensive acoustic guitar beginner's course; well-structured, patient teaching
4. **GuitarJamz** (Martyn) — Learning popular songs and riffs; fun, engaging style
5. **Andy Guitar** (Andy Crowley) — Perfect for electric guitar beginners; "Guitar for Absolute Beginners" series

### Recommended Starting Courses

- **"Guitar for Beginners"** by Justin Guitar — Covers basics: chords, strumming, technique
- **"Acoustic Guitar for Beginners"** by GuitarLessons365 — Comprehensive acoustic fundamentals
- **"Beginner Guitar Lessons"** by GuitarJamz — Range of beginner-friendly topics

### Practice Tips

- Start with acoustic guitar for easier skill development
- Practice regularly, even 10-15 minutes daily
- Use a metronome for rhythm and timing development
- Find a practice buddy for motivation
- Be patient — learning takes time and consistency
