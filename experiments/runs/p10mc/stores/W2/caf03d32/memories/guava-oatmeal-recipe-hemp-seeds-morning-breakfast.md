**Guava Oatmeal Base Recipe**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal ratio)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- Honey or maple syrup (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Hemp Seeds Addition**
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds per serving
- Provides ~5-10g protein and 1-2g omega-3s
- Can grind them or mix with liquid first
- Start with 1 tbsp if new to hemp seeds
- Store in airtight container in fridge/freezer to prevent rancidity

**Optional Toppings**
- Sliced banana, chopped nuts (walnuts, almonds, pecans), shredded coconut, cinnamon, protein powder

**Nutritional Summary**
- 1 cup cooked oatmeal: 6g protein, 4g fiber
- 1/2 cup diced guava: 2g protein, 4g fiber
- 1 tbsp chia seeds: 2g protein, 4g fiber
- 1-2 tbsp hemp seeds: 5-10g protein, omega-3s

User planning to make this regularly as high-protein, high-fiber breakfast option.
