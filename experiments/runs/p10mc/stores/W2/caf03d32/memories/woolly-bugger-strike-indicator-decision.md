## Woolly Bugger Strike Indicator Decision (May 2023)

**Decision:** Will fish Woolly Bugger without strike indicator initially

**Reasoning:**
- Marabou tail has lots of movement and action in water, effective for detecting takes
- Strike indicator can affect streamer action and look unnatural
- Strike indicator adds bulk to leader, harder to cast
- Wants to develop sensitivity as an angler
- Will focus on feel of streamer in water for subtle takes

**When strike indicators are useful:**
- Fast water (easier detection)
- Low-light conditions (visibility)
- Beginners developing skills

**When to skip strike indicators:**
- Slow water (makes streamer unnatural)
- Clear water (visible to fish)
- Experienced anglers relying on feel

**Technique notes:**
- Feel for slight tap or aggressive tug when trout takes
- Set hook quickly and firmly
- Be prepared to adjust presentation (retrieve speed, depth, angle) if needed
