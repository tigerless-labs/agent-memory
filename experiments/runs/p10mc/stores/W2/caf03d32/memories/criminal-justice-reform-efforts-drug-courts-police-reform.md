## Reform Efforts to Address War on Drugs Impact

Current approaches being pursued:

1. **Treatment-focused programs**
   - Expansion of drug courts
   - Diversion programs allowing treatment instead of jail
   - Shift from punishment to rehabilitation for addiction

2. **Sentencing reform**
   - Calls to end mandatory minimum sentences for drug offenses
   - Reduced sentences for nonviolent drug offenses

3. **Police reform**
   - Community policing strategies that foster positive relationships
   - Recruitment of officers from the communities they serve
   - Training on implicit bias and cultural sensitivity
   - Aim to increase trust and reduce tension with communities of color
