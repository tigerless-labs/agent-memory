## Trout Lure Recommendations (May 2023)

**Dry Flies:**
- Elk Hair Caddis
- Stimulator
- Blue Wing Olive

**Nymphs:**
- Hare's Ear Nymph
- Pheasant Tail Nymph
- Copper John

**Streamers:**
- Woolly Bugger — versatile, imitates baitfish/leeches/crawdad; marabou tail has lots of movement
- Muddler Minnow — slim profile, weighted head, imitates baitfish/sculpin/crayfish
- Black Stonefly

**Spinners & Spoons:**
- Mepps Aglia
- Rooster Tail
- Acme Kastmaster

**Soft Plastics:**
- Curly Tail Grub
- PowerBait

**Popular brands:** Simms, Orvis, Umpqua, Rio Products, Mepps, Acme

## River Streamer Fishing Notes
- Work well in moderate to fast currents
- Fish at various depths in deeper pools
- Look for structure (boulders, weed beds, drop-offs)
- Vary retrieve to imitate fleeing/injured prey
- Use 9-10 ft leader with 1-2X tippet
- Cast upstream/across, let swing downstream
