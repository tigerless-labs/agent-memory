## Hotels Recommended (May 22, 2023)

### The Cosmopolitan
- Vitality Fitness Center (24/7), 2,000 sq ft with state-of-the-art equipment
- Rooftop pool, Marquee Dayclub & Nightclub, Qua Baths & Spa
- Price: Deluxe $150–$300, City View $200–$400, Strip View $300–$600, Suites $500–$1,500

### Bellagio
- Fitness Center 6,000 sq ft (24/7), Canyon Ranch SpaClub (6,000 sq ft)
- Mediterranean pool complex, famous fountains
- Price: Deluxe $180–$350, City View $250–$450, Strip View $350–$700, Suites $600–$2,000, Fountain View $400–$1,000

### Wynn Las Vegas
- Fitness Center 6,000 sq ft (5am–8pm weekday, 5am–9pm weekend)
- Private golf course, luxury pool with cabanas, Spa at Wynn
- High-end shopping esplanade

### Encore at Wynn Las Vegas
- Fitness Center 5,000 sq ft (5am–8pm weekday, 5am–9pm weekend)
- Luxury pool with cabanas, Spa at Encore
- XS nightclub with pool and daybeds

### The Venetian
- Venice-themed decor, suites with canal views
- Option for romantic gondola rides through indoor canals
- Floor-to-ceiling windows, private balconies

### Palazzo
- Modern suites from Venetian Resort Hotel Casino group
- Floor-to-ceiling windows with Strip views

### Mandarin Oriental
- Located at CityCenter complex
- Modern rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows and private balconies

### Aria Resort & Casino
- Modern hotel at CityCenter complex
- Stylish rooms with Strip views or CityCenter complex views

## Booking Tips Given
- Check hotel websites or Expedia, Booking.com, Hotels.com for current rates
- Peak season (Fri–Sun, holidays, summer): 20–50% higher prices
- Off-peak (Mon–Thu, winter): 10–30% lower prices
- Book in advance for better rates
- Look for package deals (show tickets, dining credits, spa)
- Consider loyalty programs for upgrades and discounts
