## Bread-Baking Journey

Started baking bread from scratch around March 2023. Experimenting with thick, doughy bread recipes and decided to invest in a stand mixer to make the process easier.

## Stand Mixer Selection

After considering options, chose **KitchenAid Classic Plus**:
- 275-watt motor
- 4.5-quart bowl
- Good balance of features and price for a beginner
- Uses dough hook attachment for kneading

### Tips for thick doughs:
- Start with smaller batches
- Use correct dough hook attachment
- Use lower speed (2-3) initially, increase gradually
- Pause and scrape bowl sides occasionally  
- Monitor performance; stop if mixer struggles

## Cooking Interests

Interested in making savory tarts, particularly **caramelized onion, goat cheese, and thyme tart**. Has had issues caramelizing onions in the past—learned technique: use sweet onions (Vidalia/Maui), slice thin (1/8"), large skillet, low heat (~275°F/135°C), 30 min–1+ hour, stir every 10–15 min, avoid overcrowding.

Also exploring other cheese/herb combinations: goat cheese with chives/parsley, Parmesan and rosemary, feta with Mediterranean herbs.
