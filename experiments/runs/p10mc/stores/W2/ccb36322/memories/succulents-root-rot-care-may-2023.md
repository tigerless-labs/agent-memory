## Succulent care commitment

User has multiple succulents (May 20, 2023). Initially expressed not caring much for them and asked if they could survive without much attention. After learning about root rot risks and care requirements, agreed to try taking care of them properly.

### Signs of root rot in succulents
- Soft, mushy roots
- Brown or black roots
- Yellowing or browning leaves
- Shriveling or wilting
- Black spots on leaves
- Foul smell from soil

### Prevention measures
1. Use well-draining soil that allows excess water to run off quickly
2. Water only when soil is completely dry
3. Avoid over-watering, especially in winter when succulents go dormant
4. Ensure pots have drainage holes for excess water escape
5. Use potting mix specifically designed for succulents
6. Don't allow water to sit in saucers or trays under pots
7. Monitor plant health regularly and repot as necessary
