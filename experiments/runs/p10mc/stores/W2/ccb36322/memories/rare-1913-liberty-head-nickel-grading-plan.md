## Rare 1913 Liberty Head Nickel — Certification Plan

**Status:** Planning to get graded and certified (as of May 22, 2023)

**Item:** Rare 1913 Liberty Head nickel

**Planned action:** Submit for professional grading and certification to establish authenticity and value

**Recommended coin grading services:**
- **PCGS (Professional Coin Grading Service)** — one of largest and most respected services
- **NGC (Numismatic Guaranty Corporation)** — top-tier service, 30+ years experience; detailed reports on condition and authenticity
- **ANACS (American Numismatic Association Certification Service)** — non-profit, 40+ years experience; grading, authentication, conservation
- **ICG (Independent Coin Graders)** — established service, 20+ years experience; grading, authentication, appraisal

**Pre-submission checklist:**
- Research grading service reputation and credentials
- Understand their grading scale and criteria
- Compare fees and turnaround times
- Confirm experience with rare and high-value coins
- Follow submission guidelines carefully
- Store coin in safe, secure location until ready to submit
- Consider getting pre-submission appraisal from professional numismatist
