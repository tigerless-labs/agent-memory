## Rare 1980s Vinyl Record — Appraisal Plan

**Status:** Planning to get appraised (as of May 22, 2023)

**Item:** Rare vinyl record from the 1980s

**Planned action:** Get professional appraisal to determine value

**Recommended appraisal services:**
- **Discogs** — comprehensive online database with collector community feedback
- **Vinyl Me, Please** — subscription service with appraisal expertise
- **The Vinyl Factory** — UK-based appraisal and valuation services
- **MusicStack** — online marketplace with expert team for valuation
- **Local record stores** — specialty vintage/rare vinyl dealers in local area

**Appraisal tips:**
- Provide high-quality photos of cover art, labels, and any inserts/sleeves
- Document record condition, rarity, and provenance
- Research fees and terms (some charge flat fees, others take commission on sale)
