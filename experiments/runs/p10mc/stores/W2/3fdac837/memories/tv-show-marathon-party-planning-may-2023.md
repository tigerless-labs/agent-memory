## TV Show Marathon Party Project

**Timeline:** Planning stage, May 2023

**Venue:** Considering renting a larger space instead of hosting at home (considerations: parking, accessibility, noise restrictions)

### Theme Ideas
1. **Retro Sci-Fi Bash** — vintage sci-fi elements, neon lights, retro-futuristic decor, guests dress in retro outfits
2. **The Upside Down Invasion** — Hawkins, Indiana atmosphere, dark/eerie, dim lighting, 80s nostalgia
3. **Mystery Mansion Soiree** — sophisticated mysterious ambiance (inspired by Haunting of Hill House/Twilight Zone), dark colors, candelabras, eerie lighting
4. **Superhero Training Academy** — inspired by The Umbrella Academy, training facility theme

### Decorations
- Neon balloons, streamers, banners (pink, green, blue)
- TV show posters and prints
- Eerie lighting, fog machines, strange artifacts
- "Command center" with old computers and retro tech
- Caution tape, police lights, crime scene markers
- Obstacle course area for superhero training

### Snacks & Drinks
**Retro/Themed Treats:**
- Classic popcorn, candy, chips
- Pixelated cupcakes, Dungeons & Dragons-inspired cookies
- Stranger Things-themed ice cream
- "Poison" apples, "mystery meat" sandwiches, "otherworldly" energy drinks
- Superhero trail mix, power punch energy bars
- Themed drinks: "Eleven's Eggos Cereal Milk," "The Upside Down Punch," "Superhero Soda"

### Activities & Contests

**1. Superhero Training Obstacle Course**
- Mimic The Umbrella Academy training exercises
- Agility tests, strength challenges, problem-solving
- Balloons to pop, "laser beams" to navigate through
- User very excited about this element

**2. TV Show-Themed Costume Contest**
- **Open to ALL TV shows** (not just featured shows)
- Categories:
  - Best Overall Costume
  - Best Group Costume (3+ people)
  - Best TV Show Representation
  - Most Creative Use of Materials
  - People's Choice (guest voting)
- Integrate into trivia contest for bonus points

**3. Photo Booth**
- TV show-themed backdrops and props
- Capture fun and creativity
- Include costume displays

**4. TV Show Trivia Contest**
- Team-based (3-5 people per team)
- Multiple rounds by show/genre
- Question types:
  - Character identification (from screenshots/descriptions)
  - Quote identification
  - Episode summaries
  - Behind-the-scenes questions (creators, production, guest stars)
- Speed round option (time-limited questions)
- "Steal" option (steal points from incorrect answers)
- Prizes for winning team (TV show merchandise, gift cards, binge-watching kit)
- Charismatic host for entertainment
- Bonus points for teams incorporating costumes into answers

**5. TV Show-Themed Playlist**
- Background music throughout party
- Include: iconic theme songs, instrumental scores, popular songs from shows
- Examples: Game of Thrones, The Office, Stranger Things themes; Westworld, Narcos, Haunting of Hill House scores
- Mix upbeat and slow tracks
- Reasonable volume for conversation
- Can curate from streaming platforms or create collaborative playlist with guests

### User Excitement Level
- Very excited about decorations, obstacle course, costume contest, photo booth, trivia
- Looking forward to finding out "ultimate TV show buff" among friends
- Enthusiastic about layering fun and creativity across activities
