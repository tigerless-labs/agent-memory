## Imperial Radch Trilogy

- **Ancillary Justice** (2014) — AI named Breq seeks revenge against Radchaai Empire ruler Anaander Mianaai who destroyed her ship. Explores themes of power, identity, justice, imperialism, and what it means to be human. Won Hugo, Nebula, and Arthur C. Clarke awards.
- **Ancillary Sword** (2014) — Second book continuing Breq's story in Radchaai politics
- **Ancillary Mercy** (2015) — Final book concluding Breq's journey against Anaander Mianaai

Also:
- **Ancillary Stories** (2015) — Collection of short stories set in Imperial Radch universe
- **Consolation** (2018) — Novella set in Imperial Radch universe

## Other Works

- **Provenance** (2017) — Standalone novel set in different universe. Follows Ingray investigating her brother's theft of valuable artifact. Explores power, identity, inheritance in galaxy-spanning civilization. More introspective than Ancillary Justice, with greater focus on character development and relationships.
- **The Raven Tower** (2019) — Fantasy novel (departure from sci-fi), about young transgender man in conflict between gods and mortals
