## Bike Maintenance Tips

Key maintenance practices to follow:
1. Check tire pressure regularly (improves efficiency, safety, and tire longevity)
2. Lubricate chain every 100-200 miles (reduces wear and tear)
3. Inspect brake pads regularly, replace when worn
4. Check for loose bolts and signs of damage
5. Clean bike regularly to prevent dirt accumulation

## Finding Bike Shops

Methods to find reputable bike shops:
- Google search and Yelp reviews
- Ask friends, colleagues, fellow cyclists for recommendations
- Use online directories: BikeShopFinder, USA Cycling
- Look for certified mechanics (NBDA or PBMA certified)
