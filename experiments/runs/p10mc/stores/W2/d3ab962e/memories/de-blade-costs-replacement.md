## DE Blade Economics & Lifespan

**Cost:** $5–$20 per pack of 10–20 blades = $0.25–$1 per blade

**Lifespan:** 5–10 shaves per blade
- Daily shaver: replace every 5–7 shaves
- Less frequent shaver: replace every 10–14 shaves

**Factors affecting lifespan:**
- Shaving frequency
- Hair thickness (coarser hair wears blades faster)
- Shaving technique (improper technique burns through blades faster)
- Blade brand/quality (higher quality lasts longer)

**Typical schedule:** Replace every 1–2 weeks for daily shavers

**Tips:**
- Buy blades in bulk to save money
- Handle with care (very sharp)
- Store safely away from children/pets
- Experiment with different brands to find best match
