## Planned Itinerary

Road trip planned for January 2026 with the following route:
1. Grand Canyon South Rim (primary destination)
2. Monument Valley (Navajo Tribal Park, Arizona-Utah border)
3. Four Corners (Arizona, New Mexico, Utah, Colorado borders meet)
4. Return trip

## Driving Distances and Times

- Grand Canyon South Rim to Monument Valley: 200 miles, ~4 hours
- Monument Valley to Four Corners: 100 miles, ~2 hours
- Four Corners back to Grand Canyon South Rim: 220 miles, ~4.5 hours

## Notes

- January is winter; prepare for cold weather and potential road closures
- South Rim is open year-round; North Rim closes in winter
- Should add extra days to itinerary to avoid feeling rushed
- Book accommodations in advance (limited options in the area)
- Important: Respect Native American lands and culture (Monument Valley and Four Corners on Native American reservations)
