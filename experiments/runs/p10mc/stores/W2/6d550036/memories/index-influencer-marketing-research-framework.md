## Research Framework: Influencer Marketing & Consumer Purchasing Decisions

This index connects the research areas discussed for expanding the 2023 conference poster on influencer effects on consumer purchasing decisions.

### Core Framework Areas

**[research-conference-poster-influencer-purchasing-decisions]**  
Starting point: 2023 consumer psychology conference poster; established research focus on influencer effects on purchasing decisions

**[recommended-papers-influencer-marketing-2019-2020]**  
Five key peer-reviewed papers (2019-2020) providing empirical foundation for research expansion; includes systematic reviews, meta-analyses, experimental studies

**[influencer-credibility-trust-factors-framework]**  
Five credibility factors and four trust factors from Hussain & Ali systematic review; strategies influencers use to establish credibility affecting consumer decisions

**[authenticity-influencer-marketing-consumer-perception]**  
Eight authenticity strategies for influencers; consumer perception research showing 86% value authenticity; links authenticity to trust and purchasing likelihood

**[social-identity-theory-influencer-marketing-effectiveness]**  
Theoretical framework (Tajfel & Turner) explaining influencer effectiveness through five identity mechanisms; strategies for leveraging social identity for brand awareness and sales

### Research Integration Path

1. Foundation: Credibility and trust factors
2. Mechanism: Authenticity as credibility builder
3. Theory: Social identity as persuasion mechanism
4. Outcomes: Brand awareness and sales drivers
5. Empirical support: Five recommended peer-reviewed studies
