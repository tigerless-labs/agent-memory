## Quiz Session — May 20, 2023

**Philosophers & Key Ideas:**

- **Anthropology**: study of man/human beings (social science)
- **Sigmund Freud**: "Man is a composite of mechanical and material impulses"
- **Viktor Frankl**: "Man has the autonomy of a spiritual existence; a responsible being with self-determination"; believed science and religion together provide complete picture of human person
- **Second Vatican Council (Gaudium et Spes)**: described Man as "a mystery"; described Jesus Christ as "the perfect man who restored humanity's dignity by restoring its image of God"
- **Albert Einstein**: "Science without religion is lame, and religion without science is blind"
- **Karl Marx**: "religion is the opium of the people"
- **Friedrich Nietzsche**: The Will to Power; Übermensch (Superman); rejection of traditional morality and objective truth
- **Auguste Comte**: "The idea of rights should be completely wiped out"; believed social progress required subordination of individual to collective
- **Richard Dawkins**: argues complete opposition between religion and science; wrote "The God Delusion"
- **Stephen Jay Gould**: proposed NOMA thesis (Non-Overlapping Magisteria) — science and religion are separate domains that don't overlap or conflict
- **Pope Benedict XVI**: referenced Heidegger's two planes of thought — "calculating thought" (measurement/prediction) and "reflective thought" (meaning/significance)

**Science-Religion Integration:**
- NOMA thesis: science addresses WHAT/HOW, religion addresses WHY/WHO
- Integrated view: both are facets of same reality, providing complementary understanding
