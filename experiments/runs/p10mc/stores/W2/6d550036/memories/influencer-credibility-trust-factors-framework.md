## Credibility Factors in Influencer Marketing

1. **Expertise** — Influencers demonstrating knowledge in their niche/industry
2. **Relevance** — Strong connection to brand or product being promoted
3. **Authenticity** — Genuine, transparent, honest in content and interactions
4. **Consistency** — Consistent messaging, tone, and quality of content
5. **Social proof** — Showcasing followers, engagement, collaborations

## Trust Factors in Influencer Marketing

1. **Reliability** — Consistently delivering quality content and following through on promises
2. **Integrity** — Adhering to moral and ethical standards (e.g., disclosing sponsored content)
3. **Benevolence** — Demonstrating genuine interest in audience's well-being, providing value beyond product promotion
4. **Communication** — Open, responsive communication with audience

## Strategies for Establishing Credibility

1. High-quality, engaging, consistent content
2. Niche expertise and authority building
3. Transparency about sponsored content and FTC compliance
4. Active engagement and responsive interaction with followers
5. Collaborations with other influencers, brands, experts
6. Featuring user-generated content
7. Personal storytelling and vulnerability
8. Consistent tone and brand across platforms
