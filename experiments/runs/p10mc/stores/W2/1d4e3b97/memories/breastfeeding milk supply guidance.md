## Signs of Adequate Milk Supply
- Baby gains 5-7 oz/week
- 6-8 wet diapers daily
- 6-8 dirty diapers weekly
- Feeds every 2-3 hours
- Appears content after feeding

## Increasing Milk Supply
- Pump between feedings (in addition to, not replacing, direct breastfeeding)
- Ensure proper latch — lactation consultant can help assess
- Breastfeed/pump frequently to stimulate production

## Common Causes of Low Supply
1. Infrequent breastfeeding or pumping
2. Poor latch
3. Stress and fatigue
4. Health conditions: hypothyroidism, PCOS, previous breast surgery
5. Medications

## Diet and Hydration
- No universal foods to avoid; some babies sensitive to dairy, soy, wheat, nuts, caffeine
- Include: fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein, healthy fats
- Drink to thirst; pale yellow urine indicates good hydration (Institute of Medicine)
- Dehydration can affect milk production
- Balanced diet supports milk production

## Supplements That May Help
- Fenugreek (capsule or tea)
- Blessed thistle (capsule or tea)
- Milk thistle
- Brewer's yeast (B vitamins and minerals; capsule or powder)
- Fennel (tea or supplement)
- Always consult healthcare provider or lactation consultant before use

## Stress Impact
- Increases cortisol, which inhibits oxytocin release (needed for let-down)
- Changes milk composition: higher cortisol, lower lactose
- Occasional stress unlikely to significantly impact supply
- Management: exercise, adequate sleep, family support, professional counseling if needed
