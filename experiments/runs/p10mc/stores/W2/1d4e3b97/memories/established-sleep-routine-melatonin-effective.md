## Sleep Improvement Progress

**Established:** February 2024 (as of Feb 20, 2024)

**Current Bedtime Routine:**
- Reading for 20-30 minutes before bed
- Listening to calming music or nature sounds on phone while reading
- Using white noise machine to block outside noise
- Getting ready for bed earlier than before

**Melatonin Supplementation:**
- Started: ~early February 2024 (about 2 weeks before Feb 20)
- Dosage: 1 melatonin supplement
- Timing: 30 minutes before bed
- Results: **Very effective** — falls asleep within 15-20 minutes of lying down

**Caffeine Changes:**
- Previously drank coffee or tea at 4-5 PM
- Realized afternoon caffeine was disrupting sleep
- Now limiting afternoon caffeine intake

**Outcome:** Sleep quality has improved significantly; routine is working well
