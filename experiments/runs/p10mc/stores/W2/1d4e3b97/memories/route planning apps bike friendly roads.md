## Route Planning & Bike-Friendly Road Apps

**Primary Apps Recommended:**

1. **Ride with GPS**
   - Create and customize routes, find bike-friendly roads, turn-by-turn directions
   - Platforms: Web, iOS, Android

2. **MapMyRide**
   - Route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, community-driven route database
   - Platforms: Web, iOS, Android

3. **Strava**
   - Route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, browse popular routes
   - Social network for cyclists, compete with friends
   - Platforms: Web, iOS, Android

4. **Google Maps**
   - Use "Bicycling" mode to prefer bike paths/lanes, avoid highways
   - Platforms: Web, iOS, Android

**Route Planning Best Practices:**
- Avoid busy roads with high traffic
- Choose roads with bike lanes, shoulders, or designated paths
- Check road surface conditions (avoid rough/gravel/construction)
- Learn local bike laws and regulations
- Consult local bike shops for route recommendations
- Join online cycling forums and social groups

**Additional Resources:**
- Municipal and county bike maps on local government websites
- Local cycling clubs
- Online cycling communities
