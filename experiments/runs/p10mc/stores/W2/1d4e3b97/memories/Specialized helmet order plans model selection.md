## Specialized Helmet Order Plan

**Decision Context:**
- Current helmet outdated and needs replacement
- Planning to order soon from Specialized brand

**Specialized Helmet Features to Look For:**
- Tri-Fix web splitter fit system for secure, comfortable fit
- MIPS (Multi-Directional Impact Protection System) technology
- Adequate padding and comfort design
- Visor for sun/wind protection
- Good ventilation to keep head cool

**Sizing & Fit Process:**
- Measure head circumference above eyebrows and ears with flexible tape/string
- Check Specialized size chart for correct size
- Try on before ordering if possible (in-store or authorized dealer)
- Ensure snug fit with adjustable fit system

**Safety Standards to Verify:**
- CPSC certification (US standard)
- Snell certification (higher standard)
- EN 1078 certification (European standard)

**Alternative Brands & Models Researched:**
- Bell Zephyr (high-end road helmet, MIPS, excellent ventilation)
- Specialized Tactic 3.0 (versatile, MIPS, comfortable)
- Giro Synthe (premium road helmet, MIPS, aerodynamic)
- Trek Vapor 3 (lightweight, ventilated, In-Mold construction)

**Budget Ranges:**
- Entry-level: $30-50
- Mid-range: $50-100
- High-end: $100-200
- Premium: $200-300

**Ordering Options:**
- Specialized website direct
- Local authorized dealers
- Register helmet for warranty and support after purchase
