## Retailers Recommended for PC Components

### Major Retailers
1. **Amazon.com** — wide range of PC components and accessories
2. **Newegg.com** — popular computer hardware and software retailer
3. **Bestbuy.com** — PC components, peripherals, pre-built gaming PCs
4. **Microcenter.com** — physical stores in several US locations, online ordering with in-store pickup
5. **B&hphotovideo.com** — B&H Photo Video, computer hardware and accessories

### Specialty & Niche Shops
1. **PCPartPicker.com** — component selection and price comparison across retailers
2. **Superbiiz.com** — specializes in hard-to-find and discontinued items
3. **Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales** — subreddit for finding deals on PC components
4. **Performance-PCs.com** — high-performance hardware, water cooling, modding parts
5. **FrozenCPU.com** — computer hardware and modding parts for custom builds

## Purchasing Tips
- Compare prices and check customer reviews
- Check return policies and warranties
- Niche shops may have limited availability, higher prices, or longer delivery times
- Always research before purchasing
