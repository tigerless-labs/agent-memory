## Watering

- **Growing season (spring/summer):** Every 7-10 days
- **Dormant season (fall/winter):** Every 10-14 days
- **Check:** Soil dry to touch at 1-2 inches depth before watering
- **Avoid:** Overwatering and waterlogged soil; don't water the crown

## Misting

- **Frequency:** 1-2 times per week
- **Best practice:** Mist in the morning
- **Water temperature:** Room temperature
- **Note:** Peace lilies need less misting than ferns; ferns prefer every other day

## Temperature

- **Ideal range:** 68°F to 72°F (20°C to 22°C)
- **Acceptable range:** 65°F to 75°F (18°C to 24°C)
- **Avoid:** Below 55°F or above 85°F
- **Critical:** Avoid drafts and sudden temperature changes

## Humidity

- **Preferred level:** 40-50% relative humidity
- **Methods:** Pebble tray with water or humidifier nearby

## Fertilization

- **Type:** Balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20 or similar)
- **Growing season:** Every 1-2 weeks at half-recommended strength
- **Dormant season:** Once monthly
- **User's approach:** Using nursery-recommended water and fertilizer mixture (verify NPK ratio and concentration per label)

## Light

- **Preference:** Bright, indirect light
- **Avoid:** Direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)
- **Ideal placement:** East or west-facing windows

## General care

- **Potting mix:** Well-draining soil, tropical plant or orchid mix preferred
- **Pests:** Monitor for spider mites, mealybugs, scale; treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if needed
- **Pruning:** Remove dead/dying leaves and flowers with clean, sharp tools
- **Repotting:** Not frequently needed; repot only if root-bound or showing nutrient deficiency (spring/summer preferred)

## Adjustment period

- Leaf loss is normal when adjusting to new environment
- May take time to settle in after purchase
