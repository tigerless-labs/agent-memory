## Humidifier Recommendations

Provided 2026-09-02 for humidity-loving plants (fern, African violets):

### Recommended Models

1. **Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Humidifier**
   - Both cool and warm mist settings
   - Adjustable humidistat
   - Large 6-liter tank
   - Affordable

2. **Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier**
   - Compact, easy-to-use
   - 1-liter tank
   - Suitable for small to medium spaces

3. **Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier**
   - Stylish ultrasonic design
   - 2.5-liter tank
   - Built-in humidistat
   - Adjustable mist output

4. **Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier**
   - Compact, modern design
   - 1-liter tank
   - Whisper-quiet operation
   - Easy to clean

### Key Features
- Cool mist preferred for most plants
- Adjustable mist output
- Humidistat for automatic control
- Low noise level (important for African violets — sensitive to vibrations)
- Easy to clean and maintain

### Placement & Use
- Place within 3-5 feet of plants (1-2 feet for African violets)
- Use hygrometer to monitor humidity level
- Regular cleaning to prevent bacterial growth
- Target humidity: 50-60% for African violets; 50%+ for ferns

### Caution
- Avoid over-humidification (risk of root rot, fungal disease)
- Provide good air circulation around plants
