## Snake Plant

**Received:** Last month from sister

**Current status:** Doing well with existing care routine

**Watering & fertilizer:** Current routine is working well; not changing at this time

**Repotting considerations:**
- Decision: wait before repotting (plant is doing well, not urgently needed)
- When needed: only if root-bound (roots circling or growing out of drainage holes) or soil depleted
- Pot size: only 1-2 sizes larger (snake plants prefer slightly root-bound)
- Best timing: spring or summer when actively growing
- Soil: must be well-draining to prevent root rot
- Post-repotting: water sparingly to avoid shock

**Care notes:**
- Low-maintenance plant
- Susceptible to root rot if overwatered
- Tolerance for root-bound conditions is high
