## Kimchi Recipe Adjustment Plan

**Problem (May 21, 2023 - first attempt):**
- Too much chili flakes (overseasoned with heat)
- Too much salt

**Solution approach:**
1. Rinse the kimchi under cold running water to remove excess salt
2. Prepare new seasoning paste with measured ingredients

**Chosen ingredient: Gochugaru**
- Medium to hot heat level with balanced flavor
- More common and versatile than other Korean chili flake varieties

**Recommended measurements per 2 cups vegetables:**

| Heat Level | Gochugaru Amount |
|------------|-----------------|
| Mild | 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon |
| Medium | 1/2 to 1 teaspoon |
| Hot | 1 to 2 teaspoons |
| Extremely Hot | 2+ teaspoons |

**Garlic preparation:**
- Use fresh garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables)
- Mince or crush to release oils
- Combine with other ingredients for balanced flavor profile

**Ginger preparation:**
- Use fresh ginger (1-2 inches of grated or sliced ginger per 2 cups vegetables)
- Peel and grate or slice thinly
- Optional: sauté in oil to mellow flavors

**Key techniques:**
- Taste as you go and adjust
- Use separate cutting board for garlic/ginger to prevent flavor transfer
- Measure by weight for accuracy (digital kitchen scale)
- Be cautious with additions—easier to add than remove excess salt/spice
