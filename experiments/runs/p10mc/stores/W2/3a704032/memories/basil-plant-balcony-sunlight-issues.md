## Basil Plant Care

**Situation:**
- Location: balcony
- Problem: struggling with direct sunlight
- Solution: moving to shadier spot

**Current setup:**
- Soil: general-purpose potting mix
- Ideal mix: 2 parts peat moss or coco coir, 1 part perlite or vermiculite, 1 part compost or worm castings, 1 part general-purpose potting soil

**Care needs:**
- Light: filtered indirect sunlight (east- or west-facing), or use shade cloth
- Water: only when soil feels dry to touch; shadier spots need less frequent watering
- Humidity: high humidity preferred; can use pebble tray with water
- Temperature: 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- Fertilizer: balanced, water-soluble at half strength
- Maintenance: pinch off flower buds, trim leggy stems regularly

**Ideal soil conditions:**
- Well-draining (perlite/vermiculite important)
- pH 6.0-7.0 (slightly acidic to neutral)
- Rich in organic matter
- Loose structure for aeration
