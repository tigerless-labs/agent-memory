## Fertilizer Recommendations by Plant Type

Provided 2026-09-02:

### Snake Plant
- Balanced fertilizer with N-P-K ratio 20-20-20, slightly acidic pH (6.0-7.0)
- Recommended products:
  - Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20)
  - Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer (2-2-2)
  - Schultz All Purpose Plant Food (20-20-20)
- Dilute to half strength to avoid root burn

### Succulents
- Low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus (cactus/succulent-specific) formula like 10-20-10
- Recommended products:
  - Miracle-Gro Cactus, Palm & Citrus Food (10-20-10)
  - Espoma Organic Cactus! Fertilizer (5-2-6)
  - Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10)
- Dilute to half strength

### Orchid
- High-phosphorus formula like 30-10-10 or 15-30-15 for blooming
- Low nitrogen to prevent weak growth
- Recommended products:
  - Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10)
  - Espoma Organic Orchid! Fertilizer (15-30-15)
  - Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20)
- Feed monthly only (sparse fertilization)
- Dilute to half strength

**General rule**: Apply during growing season (spring/summer); skip fall/winter. Read labels carefully for plant-specific formulations.
