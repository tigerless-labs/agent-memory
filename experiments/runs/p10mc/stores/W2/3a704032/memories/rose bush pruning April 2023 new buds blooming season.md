## Pruning recommendations given

### Light pruning approach (current season)
- Can do light pruning to shape the bush and promote air circulation
- Remove weak or spindly growth
- Remove crossing or rubbing stems
- Directs energy toward producing more flowers

### Deadheading
- Remove dead or wilted flowers anytime to encourage new blooms
- Gentle technique that won't harm the plant

### Extensive pruning
- Wait until blooming season is over
- Better for rejuvenating plant or controlling size
- Allows plant to complete flowering cycle and store energy

## General rose pruning practices

- **Best timing:** During dormant season (winter or early spring)
- **Technique:** Clean cuts at 45-degree angle
- **Priority:** Remove weak or diseased wood to prevent disease spread
- **Monitoring:** Watch for stress or disease signs and adjust strategy accordingly
