## Potting Mix for Spider Plant

Provided 2026-09-02 for repotting spider plant:

### Key Ingredients
- Peat moss or coconut coir — moisture retention, slightly acidic pH
- Compost or humus — nutrients and soil structure
- Perlite or vermiculite — drainage and aeration
- Worm castings or worm compost — rich nutrients and beneficial microorganisms

### Recommended Potting Mix Products

1. **Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix** — balanced, general-purpose
2. **Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix** — high-quality organic blend with peat moss, compost, perlite
3. **E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix** — eco-friendly with compost, peat moss, perlite
4. **Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix** — professional-grade with peat moss, vermiculite, perlite

### Repotting Steps

1. Choose pot only 1-2 sizes larger (spider plants prefer slight root-binding)
2. Gently remove plant from old pot without damaging roots
3. Inspect and trim any rotten/damaged roots
4. Fill new pot with fresh potting mix
5. Gently firm mix around roots as you fill
6. Water thoroughly after repotting
7. Keep soil consistently moist but not waterlogged during establishment

### Spider Plant Preferences
- Well-draining potting mix
- Rich in organic matter
- Slightly acidic pH preferred
