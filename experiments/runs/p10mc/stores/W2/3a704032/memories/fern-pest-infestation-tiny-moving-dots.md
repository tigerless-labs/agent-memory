## Fern Pest Problem

**Pest identification:**
- Appearance: tiny, moving dots on leaves and stems
- Likely pests: spider mites or springtails
  - Spider mites: eight-legged, feed on plant sap, cause yellowing/stippling, spin fine webs
  - Springtails: wingless, feed on algae/fungi/decaying matter, found in moist environments, harmless but annoying

**Treatment plan:**
- Using neem oil as primary treatment (gentle, natural insecticide)
- Apply according to product instructions
- Wait a few days for effect before misting

**Prevention & maintenance:**
- Misting schedule: 2-3 times per week
- Water type: distilled or filtered (not tap water)
- Timing: mist in morning so leaves dry before nightfall
- Technique: fine mist only; mist leaf undersides where pests hide
- Avoid: misting the crown (where leaves meet stem) to prevent rot
- Air circulation: maintain good airflow to prevent fungal diseases

**General pest control approach:**
- Isolate plant from other plants
- Prune heavily infested leaves/stems
- Monitor closely; repeat treatment as needed
- Other options if severe: insecticidal soap, horticultural oil, or chemical pesticides (last resort)
