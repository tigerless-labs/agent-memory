User does meal prep on Sundays and makes large batches of quinoa salad in mason jars for quick, customizable lunches throughout the week (lasts up to 5 days in fridge).

Likes to customize the base quinoa with:
- Different colored veggies (bell peppers, carrots, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, avocado)
- Protein sources (grilled chicken, salmon, tofu, edamame, canned beans like black beans or chickpeas)
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, walnuts, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds)
- Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, cilantro)
- Dressings (olive oil + lemon juice vinaigrette, or Greek yogurt + dill)

Appreciates the flexibility to experiment and find new favorite combinations.
