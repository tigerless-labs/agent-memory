## T&T Supermarket — North York Centre

**Location:**
- Address: 5300 Yonge St, North York Centre

**Halal Meat Section:**
- Certified by Halal Monitoring Authority (HMA) and Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)
- Fresh meat: beef, chicken, lamb, goat
- Frozen meat: burgers, sausages, kebabs
- Poultry: chicken wings, drumsticks
- Seafood: shrimp, fish, squid

**International Ingredients:**

Asian focus (widest selection):
- Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Thai, Southeast Asian cuisine
- Rice, noodles, grains
- Canned goods (coconut milk, bamboo shoots)
- Sauces and condiments (soy sauce, fish sauce, sriracha)
- Spices and herbs (ginger, garlic, lemongrass)
- Frozen foods (dumplings, buns, spring rolls)

Other cuisines:
- Indian and Pakistani spices and sauces
- Middle Eastern (sumac, pomegranate molasses)
- Latin American (tortillas, salsa, queso fresco)
- European (Italian olive oil, French cheese)

**Additional Features:**
- Large produce section: Asian specialties (bok choy, Chinese broccoli, Korean chili peppers)
- In-store bakery: Asian-style breads, pastries, desserts
- Food court with Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese restaurants
- Loyalty program available
- Promotions listed on website/newsletter

**Tips for Visiting:**
- Prepare for crowds, especially weekends
- Bring shopping list due to vast selection
- Ask staff for assistance with unfamiliar ingredients
- Check website for current promotions
