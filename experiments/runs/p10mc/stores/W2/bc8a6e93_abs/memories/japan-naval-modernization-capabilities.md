## Naval Modernization Examples

Japan has modernized its navy's capabilities to address maritime security threats:

1. **Maritime surveillance** — Advanced network of surveillance and reconnaissance systems monitoring coastal and beyond waters. Acquisition of advanced unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and maritime patrol aircraft.

2. **Anti-submarine warfare (ASW)** — Developed capabilities to counter North Korean submarine threats. Includes new sonar systems, advanced aircraft, and submarines equipped with advanced sensors and weapons.

3. **Amphibious assault capability** — Created new amphibious force with amphibious assault vehicles and aircraft. Intended to defend remote islands in the East China Sea against potential takeover.

4. **Ballistic missile defense** — Advanced system to defend against North Korean missile threats. Includes deployment of Aegis-equipped destroyers and ground-based Patriot Advanced Capability (PAC-3) system.

5. **Cybersecurity** — Established cyber defense unit to protect naval assets from cyber attacks. Conducted joint cyber exercises with the U.S. to enhance cybersecurity capabilities.
