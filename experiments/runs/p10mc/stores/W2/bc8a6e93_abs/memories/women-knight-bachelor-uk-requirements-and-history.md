## Women and Knight Bachelor Appointments in the UK

**Eligibility & Requirements:**
- Women can be appointed as Knight Bachelor
- Requirements identical to men: must be a British citizen or Commonwealth citizen
- Must have made significant contribution to their field (arts, sciences, humanities, public service, or charity work)

**Historical Timeline:**
- Title created: 12th century
- Women eligible: early 20th century
- First women appointed: 1970s
- As of 2021: over 100 women appointed as Knight Bachelor
- Recent years: roughly equal numbers of men and women receiving the honor

**Process:**
- Recommendations made by Prime Minister's honours committee
- Final decision made by the Queen (or King)

**Title Usage:**
- Female equivalent of "Sir" is "Dame"
- Women receive title "Dame" before their name
