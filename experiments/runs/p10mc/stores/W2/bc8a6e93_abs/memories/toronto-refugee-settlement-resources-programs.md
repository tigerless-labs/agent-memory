## Toronto Refugee Settlement Resources & Programs

**Government Programs:**
- Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)
- Ontario Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing
- City of Toronto's Housing Connections portal (RGI housing applications)
- Toronto Community Housing Corporation (TCHC) - affordable housing options

**Financial Assistance Organizations:**
- United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR)
- Canadian Red Cross

**Housing Programs:**
- **Rent-Geared-to-Income (RGI)**: Subsidized rent based on income; apply through City of Toronto's Housing Connections portal
- **TCHC**: Toronto Community Housing Corporation; can apply online or visit office in person
- **Non-profit housing**: Toronto Non-Profit Housing Association, WoodGreen Community Services, Neighbourhood Group

**Settlement Agencies (Housing Assistance & Support):**
- Toronto Refugee Resettlement Centre
- COSTI Immigrant Services
- YMCA of Greater Toronto

**Housing Help Resources:**
- City of Toronto's 311 service
- Housing Help Centre: 416-285-9666

**Cultural Orientation Program:**
- Available upon arrival in Canada
- Typically lasts several weeks with sessions multiple times per week
- Format: group sessions, workshops, one-on-one meetings
- Content includes: Canadian culture/customs, settlement info, job search strategies, language support, community connections
- Benefits: understand Canadian culture, find employment, build social network, receive resources and services

**Online Resources:**
- Ontario Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing website
- City of Toronto's Affordable Housing Office
- Toronto Housing Market Analysis
