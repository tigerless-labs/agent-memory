## Flour Preferences for Lemon Desserts

User has experimented with different types of flour and prefers **all-purpose flour** for lemon-based desserts:

- **All-purpose flour** is preferred because:
  - Produces tender, delicate crumb that showcases bright citrusy flavor
  - Better for pastry — produces flaky, tender crust
  - Less likely to become tough or dense
  
- **Bread flour** is less ideal because:
  - Higher protein content (12-14%) produces more gluten
  - Results in chewier, denser texture
  - Better suited for yeast-based recipes, not delicate pastries

User is also aware of alternatives like cake flour (even lower protein for more delicate results) and pastry flour (between all-purpose and bread flour).
