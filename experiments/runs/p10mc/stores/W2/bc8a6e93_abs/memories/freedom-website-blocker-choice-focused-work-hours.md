## Freedom Website Blocker (May 2023)

**Decision:** Will use Freedom as primary website/app blocker during focused work hours (2:00 pm - 4:00 pm).

**Why Freedom:** Recommended after comparing options including StayFocusd, Cold Turkey, SelfControl, BlockSite, and LeechBlock. Has heard good things about it.

**Planned setup tips:**
- Set clear goals for what to block
- Customize blocklist for distracting websites and apps
- Schedule blocks in advance for work hours
- Use "Locked Mode" to prevent circumventing blocks
- Monitor productivity reports to refine blocklists
- Combine with Pomodoro Technique for maximum focus

**Alternative blockers evaluated:**
- StayFocusd: Free Chrome extension
- Cold Turkey: Windows/Mac app
- SelfControl: Free, open-source Mac app
- BlockSite: Chrome extension
- LeechBlock: Firefox add-on
