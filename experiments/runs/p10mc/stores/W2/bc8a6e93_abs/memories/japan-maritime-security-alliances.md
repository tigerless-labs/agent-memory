## Japan's Alliances in Maritime Security

Japan's alliances play a crucial role in safeguarding maritime interests through diplomatic and military support, intelligence sharing, training, and access to advanced technology.

### Most Important Ally: United States

- Close security partnership with stationing of U.S. military forces in Japan
- Provides access to advanced military technology
- Intelligence sharing and powerful military deterrent against potential aggressors

### Regional Security Partners

- **Australia** — Important security partnership
- **Canada** — Security partnership
- **ASEAN members** — Multiple security partnerships
- **India** — Partnership through Quadrilateral Security Dialogue (Quad)

## Multilateral Framework

**Quadrilateral Security Dialogue (Quad)** — Includes United States, India, and Australia. Strengthens cooperation in defense, maritime security, and counterterrorism.

## Diplomacy with Neighbors

Despite ongoing territorial disputes:
- **China** — High-level diplomatic meetings, joint economic projects, efforts to address historical grievances
- **South Korea** — Military and intelligence sharing, joint exercises with US, efforts to address historical issues

Strategy seeks to balance deterrence with diplomacy to maintain regional peace and stability.
