## Month-Long Social Media Break (May 2023)

**Decision:** Taking a one-month break from social media to address feeling overwhelmed.

**Reasons for break:**
- Overwhelmed by amount of information and news on social media
- Need to clear head and refocus on priorities
- Opportunity to reduce information overload and recalibrate

**Expected benefits:**
- Reduced information overload and ability to process/prioritize info
- Increased focus on priorities and tasks
- Improved mental health (reduced anxiety, FOMO, comparison)
- Enhanced creativity and reflection

**Planned approach:**
- Set clear goals for what to achieve during break
- Use website blockers (Freedom) to prevent access
- Replace social media time with joy-bringing activities (reading, exercise, time with friends/family)
- Reflect and journal about social media patterns
- Plan re-entry strategy: set boundaries, unfollow draining accounts, establish healthier habits

**Context:** Already successfully limiting social media to 30 min morning + 30 min evening, which has proven helpful for productivity. This month-long break is an intensification of that strategy.
