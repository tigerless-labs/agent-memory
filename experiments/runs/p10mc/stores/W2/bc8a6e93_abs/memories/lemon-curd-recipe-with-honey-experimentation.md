## Lemon Curd Recipe & Method

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup (120ml) freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1/4 cup (60g) unsalted butter, softened
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1 tsp (5g) grated lemon zest
- Salt (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Whisk sugar, lemon juice, and salt until sugar dissolves
2. Add softened butter, whisk until incorporated
3. Temper egg yolks by slowly pouring warm lemon mixture into eggs, whisking constantly
4. Return to saucepan, cook over medium heat whisking constantly until thickens and coats back of spoon (10-12 min)
5. Remove from heat, stir in lemon zest, cool to room temperature
6. Strain through fine-mesh sieve to remove egg solids

**Storage:** Refrigerate up to 2 weeks, freeze up to 3 months
