## Grocery Stores by Toronto Neighborhood

### The Danforth
- **Loblaws** (1555 Danforth Ave) - Greek, Italian, Asian ingredients
- **Metro** (1441 Danforth Ave) - Indian, Pakistani, Middle Eastern ingredients
- **Kensington Market-style stores** (various on Danforth Ave) - Latin American, Caribbean, Asian ingredients

### Riverdale
- **No Frills** (545 Queen St E) - Indian, Pakistani, Middle Eastern ingredients
- **Food Basics** (655 Queen St E) - Latin American, Caribbean ingredients
- **Riverdale Farm Market** (201 Winchester St) - Local, organic produce + international ingredients

### Leslieville
- **Loblaws** (1411 Queen St E) - International foods
- **FreshCo** (1421 Queen St E) - Indian, Pakistani, Middle Eastern ingredients
- **Leslieville Market** (1133 Queen St E) - Local, organic produce + international ingredients

### The Junction
- **No Frills** (2995 Dundas St W) - International foods
- **Food Basics** (3055 Dundas St W) - International foods
- **Junction Farmers' Market** (2960 Dundas St W) - Local, organic produce + international ingredients

### Scarborough Village
- **Loblaws** (1900 Eglinton Ave E) - International foods
- **FreshCo** (2555 Eglinton Ave E) - International foods
- **Scarborough Village Market** (1921 Eglinton Ave E) - African, Caribbean foods

### North York Centre
- **Loblaws** (5095 Yonge St) - International foods
- **T&T Supermarket** (5300 Yonge St) - **Asian specialty store** with halal certification
- **P.A.T. Central Market** (5555 Yonge St) - African, Caribbean foods

**Store Types:**
- Loblaws: Large chain stores, diverse selection
- Metro: Large chain, wide international selection
- No Frills / Food Basics: Discount grocers
- FreshCo: Discount grocers
- Neighborhood markets: Small independent stores, local/organic focus
