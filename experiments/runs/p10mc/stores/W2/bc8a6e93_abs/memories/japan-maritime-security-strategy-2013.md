Japan's current maritime security strategy (announced 2013) has three core components:

1. **Active contribution to peace** — Japan aims to contribute to regional peace and stability through diplomacy, promoting economic development, and participation in international peacekeeping operations.

2. **Protecting Japan's territorial integrity and territorial waters** — Japan safeguards sovereignty by strengthening defense capabilities, enhancing maritime surveillance, and developing robust amphibious defense capability.

3. **Open and stable maritime order** — Japan promotes rules-based maritime order through cooperation with regional countries, emphasizing international norms like freedom of navigation while addressing piracy and terrorism.

This strategy seeks to balance deterrence and diplomacy while promoting regional cooperation and peace.
