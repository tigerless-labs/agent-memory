## Perceived Threats to Japan's Maritime Interests

### Territorial Disputes

- **China** — Disputes over Senkaku/Diaoyu Islands in East China Sea
- **South Korea** — Disputes over Takeshima/Dokdo Islands
- **Risk** — Disputes can escalate into conflicts threatening Japan's maritime interests

### North Korean Threats

- Nuclear and missile programs pose significant threat to Japan's security
- Could disrupt maritime trade and flow of goods
- Negative impact on Japan's economy

### Natural Disasters

- Japan located in earthquake-prone region
- Vulnerable to tsunamis and other natural disasters
- Could affect maritime infrastructure and navy's ability to respond to emergencies

## Response Strategy

Japan has addressed these threats through:
- Modernizing navy capabilities
- Strengthening alliances with United States and other allies
- Increasing maritime surveillance and defense capabilities
- Participating in joint military and training exercises with other countries
- Developing comprehensive maritime security strategy
