## Recent Female Knights (2018-2021)

1. **Dame Mary Beard**
   - Background: Classicist and television presenter
   - Appointed: 2018 Queen's Birthday Honours

2. **Dame Sarah Gilbert**
   - Background: Oxford University professor
   - Achievement: Co-created the Oxford-AstraZeneca COVID-19 vaccine
   - Appointed: 2021 New Year Honours

3. **Dame Theresa Marteau**
   - Background: Professor of behaviour and health at Cambridge University
   - Appointed: 2019 Queen's Birthday Honours

4. **Dame Rosemary Squire**
   - Background: Theatre producer and founder of Ambassador Theatre Group
   - Appointed: 2018 New Year Honours

**Note:** These women represent diverse fields including humanities, sciences, health, and the arts.
