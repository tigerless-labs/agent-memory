## Joint Military Training Exercises

Japan participates in several recurring joint military exercises to enhance capabilities and interoperability:

1. **RIMPAC (Rim of the Pacific Exercise)** — Biennial joint military exercise hosted by the United States, attended by navies worldwide. Japan aims to enhance maritime interoperability and strengthen relationships with participating countries.

2. **Cope North** — Joint exercise between US, Japan, and Australia focusing on coordination of air operations in the Pacific theater. Includes air-to-air combat, air-to-ground attacks, and air-to-sea integration scenarios.

3. **Keen Sword** — Biennial joint military exercise between Japan and the United States involving ground, naval, and air forces. Aims to enhance interoperability and readiness to respond to maritime and air-based aggression threats.

4. **Blue Flag** — Joint air force exercise held in Israel with participants from multiple countries including Japan. Focuses on air-to-air combat, air-to-ground attacks, and coordinated multi-national air operations.

5. **Malabar** — Naval exercise between the United States, Japan, and India. Focused on maritime security, freedom of navigation, and counterterrorism operations.

These exercises help Japan increase military capabilities, improve interoperability with allies, and build strong relationships.
