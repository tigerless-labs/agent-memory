## Economic Development Projects for Regional Stability

Japan has pursued various economic development projects across Asia-Pacific to promote regional peace and stability:

1. **Partnership for Quality Infrastructure** — Japanese initiative providing high-quality infrastructure throughout the Asia-Pacific region. Includes funding for port development and improvement, benefiting Japan's maritime trade and interests.

2. **Asian Development Bank (ADB)** — Japan is a significant contributor. Uses its influence to promote development projects improving connectivity and stability in the region, boosting regional trade and Japan's economic interests.

3. **Greater Mekong Subregion Economic Cooperation Program** — Japan supports this program promoting economic integration among Southeast Asian countries. Funds infrastructure projects including ports, roads, and bridges. Enhances Japan's economic interests in the region.

4. **Japan-China-South Korea Free Trade Agreement** — Japan has been a driving force behind negotiations. Expected to lower trade barriers and promote economic integration, improving Japan's access to Chinese and South Korean markets.

## Impact on Maritime Interests

These projects improve connectivity and infrastructure, allowing Japan to expand trade and strengthen its commercial presence in regional seaports. Simultaneously, they contribute to regional peace and security, reducing risk of maritime conflicts affecting Japan's maritime interests.
