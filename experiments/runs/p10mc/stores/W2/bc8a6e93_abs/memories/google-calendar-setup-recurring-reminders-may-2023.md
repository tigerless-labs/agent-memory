## Google Calendar Setup (May 2023)

Using Google Calendar to manage recurring daily events and reminders:

**Recurring Events:**
- Morning Social Media Check: 7:15 am - 7:45 am (30 minutes)
- Exercise: 7:30 am - 8:00 am (30 minutes)
- Lunch Break: 12:00 pm - 1:00 pm (1 hour)
- Focused Work: 2:00 pm - 4:00 pm (2 hours)
- Evening Social Media Check: 9:00 pm - 9:30 pm (30 minutes)

**Reminders:** Set 15-30 minutes before each event to ensure on track.

**Notification method:** Digital calendar notification via Google Calendar (pop-ups, emails, or mobile notifications).

Chose Google Calendar over task management apps like Todoist or Trello for this purpose.
