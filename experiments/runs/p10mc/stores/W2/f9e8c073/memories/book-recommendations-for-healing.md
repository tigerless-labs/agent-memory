## Starting Book

**Wild: From Lost to Found on the Pacific Crest Trail** by Cheryl Strayed
- Powerful memoir exploring grief, healing, and self-discovery
- Matches interest in memoirs
- Recommended for someone processing difficult emotions

## Second Priority

**Daring Greatly** by Brené Brown
- User familiar with Brené Brown's TED talks and finds her work inspiring
- Focuses on vulnerability and courage
- Great for personal growth and self-reflection

## Other Recommendations Given

**Memoirs and Inspirational Stories**
- The Glass Castle by Jeannette Walls
- Man's Search for Meaning by Viktor E. Frankl

**Fiction**
- The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho
- The Nightingale by Kristin Hannah
- The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North

**Self-Help**
- The Seven Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck

## Reading Style

- Preference for memoirs
- Interest in personal growth and self-help
- Enjoys stories about resilience, hope, overcoming adversity
- Open to fiction for reflection and escapism
