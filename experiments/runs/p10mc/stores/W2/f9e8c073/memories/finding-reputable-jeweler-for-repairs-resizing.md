## Finding a Good Jeweler for Resizing

**Research methods:**
1. Ask for referrals from friends, family, coworkers
2. Check online reviews on Yelp, Google, Facebook Reviews
3. Check credentials: AGTA (American Gem Trade Association), JBT (Jewelers Board of Trade), NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers)
4. Verify experience with ring resizing, specifically with your metal type
5. Confirm they have proper equipment and technology

**Questions to ask during consultation:**
- What is the resizing process?
- What materials will be used?
- How long will it take?
- Can you see examples of previous work?
- Do you offer warranty or return policy?

**What to verify:**
- Detailed quote and timeline
- Clarity about process and pricing
- Warranty or return policy offered

**General approach:**
Schedule consultation to assess expertise and communication style. Ask questions. Be patient with the process. A good jeweler will be patient, knowledgeable, and willing to address concerns.
