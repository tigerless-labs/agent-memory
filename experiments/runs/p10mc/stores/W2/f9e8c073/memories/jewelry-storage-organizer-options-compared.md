## Jewelry Storage and Organization Options

**Stackable Jewelry Trays (user selected this option)**
- Separate compartments for earrings, rings, necklaces
- Look for velvet or soft fabric lining to prevent scratching
- Examples: Songmics, Whitmor Deluxe Stackable Jewelry Tray

**Jewelry Armoires**
- Large multi-drawer cases with separate compartments
- Often have lock and key for security
- Examples: Mirrored Jewelry Armoire, Standing Jewelry Armoire by SEI

**Anti-Tarnish Jewelry Boxes**
- Special lining that absorbs moisture and prevents tarnishing
- Best for silver jewelry storage
- Examples: Stackers brand, Mele and Co brand

**Travel Jewelry Cases**
- Compact, portable with individual compartments
- Good for small collections or travel

**Over-the-Door Organizers**
- Hang organizers with hooks, pockets, compartments
- Good for small spaces

**Selection criteria:**
Material (velvet, soft fabric, anti-tarnish lining), number of compartments, size relative to collection, portability needs, security features
