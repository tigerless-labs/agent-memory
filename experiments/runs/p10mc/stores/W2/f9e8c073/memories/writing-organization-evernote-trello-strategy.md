## Writing Organization Setup

**User Writing Activities:**
- Writes short stories and poetry regularly
- Participates in a weekly writing challenge with social media prompts
- Week 1 prompt: "Forgotten Memories" → wrote piece titled "The Smell of Old Books"

**File Organization Strategy:**
- **Evernote notebooks:** Separate notebooks for short stories, poetry, and writing challenge submissions
  - Writing challenge notebooks will have separate notebooks with subfolders for each prompt
- **Trello boards:** One board per project
  - Writing challenge board will have lists for each prompt, with cards for ideas and drafts

**Tools & Apps Selected:**
- **Evernote:** Notebooks, web clipper for research/inspiration, custom templates for characters and plots
- **Trello:** Visual boards, lists by stage/category, labels for organizing cards
- Tools mentioned but not yet selected: Scrivener, MindMeister, Simplenote, Bear, Workflowy

**Planned Evernote Features:**
- Character templates with fields: traits, backstory, motivations, goals, conflicts, relationships
- Plot templates with fields: plot points, character arcs, conflicts, themes, setting
- Revision template with fields: revision goals, changes, feedback
- Scene lists and character/plot outlines
- Web clipper to save research articles and inspiration

**Planned Trello Features:**
- Character boards with lists for main/supporting/antagonist characters
- Plot boards with lists for story stages (exposition, rising action, climax, resolution)
- Revision board with stages: revision, editing, proofreading
- Labels to categorize cards by trait, relationship, role, or revision type
- Calendar Power-Up for scheduling writing sessions

**Brainstorming Techniques Discussed:**
- Character interviews, character mapping, scenario brainstorming
- Three-act structure, what-if questions, scene brainstorming, reverse outlining
- Revision layers (focus one aspect at a time), editing passes (one element per pass)
- Getting feedback from beta readers/writing groups
