## Silver Jewelry Cleaning Options

**Commercial solutions recommended:**

1. Godfrey's Silver Dip — quick and easy, dip and rinse method
2. Weiman Silver Jewelry Cream — non-abrasive cream, apply with soft cloth
3. Hagerty Silver Foam — ammonia-free foam, gentle application
4. Connoisseurs Silver Jewelry Cleaner — non-toxic solution, dip method

**DIY option:**
Mix 1 tablespoon baking soda and 2 tablespoons water into a paste. Apply, let sit for 15 to 30 minutes, rinse with warm water, dry thoroughly.

**General silver care tips:**
Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive materials, hot water. Dry thoroughly to prevent water spots. Use silver polishing cloths or storage bags with anti-tarnish properties. Consider jewelry box with anti-tarnish lining (brands: Stackers, Mele and Co).
