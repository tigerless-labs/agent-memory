## Peridot Necklace for Anniversary

**Reason:** Upcoming anniversary; peridot is user's birthstone

**Shopping options:**

1. Online jewelry retailers: Blue Nile, James Allen, Brian Gavin Diamonds
2. Local jewelry stores: Search on Yelp or Google Maps
3. Custom designers: Etsy or referrals
4. Local jewelry associations/guilds: Member directories

**What to look for in peridot quality:**
- Vibrant green color
- Minimal inclusions
- Good cut quality
- High-quality stones have more intense color and sparkle

**Setting considerations:**
- Type: Prong settings, bezel settings, pendant designs
- Metal: White gold, yellow gold, or silver (choose to complement skin tone)
- Design: Match personal style

**Verification:**
- Ensure peridot is certified by GIA (Gemological Institute of America) or ICA (International Colored Gemstone Association)

**Approach:**
Take time, do research, don't hesitate to ask questions. Compare options from multiple retailers.
