## Lens Mount and Surrounding Area Cleaning

### Safe Cleaning Methods

1. Soft dry cloth: Use microfiber cloth or soft cotton cloth (avoid paper towels/tissues that leave lint)
2. Gentle circular motions: Hold cloth by corners, wipe gently in circular motion; avoid excessive pressure
3. Blower brush: Soft-bristled brush or small blower can gently sweep loose debris from mount
4. Distilled water (optional): For stubborn dirt, dampen cloth with distilled water—never spray liquids directly
5. Lens cleaning pen: Can be useful tool with soft, flexible tip

### What NOT to Do

* Never use compressed air on lens mount or surrounding area
* Never spray cleaning solutions directly on mount
* Avoid using paper towels or tissues (leave lint residue)

### Regular Maintenance

* Clean lens mount regularly, especially after changing lenses or shooting in dusty/sandy environments
* Always clean camera mount as well when changing lenses
* Inspect after cleaning to ensure debris is fully removed

### Prevention

* Avoid changing lenses in dusty environments
* Use lens hood for protection
* Use UV filter on lens
* Store in protective camera bag when not in use
