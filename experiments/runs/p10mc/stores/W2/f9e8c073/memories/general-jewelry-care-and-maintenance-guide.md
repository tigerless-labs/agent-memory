## General Jewelry Care and Maintenance

**Storage:**
Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use jewelry box or case with separate compartments to prevent scratching and tangling.

**Cleaning:**
Clean regularly using soft cloth and mild soap solution. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive materials, or hot water.

**Chemical avoidance:**
Avoid exposure to bleach, ammonia, or chlorine. These can damage or discolor metal or stones.

**After wear:**
Dry thoroughly to prevent water spots and tarnishing.

**Water exposure:**
Avoid wearing jewelry in water, including swimming pools, hot tubs, or activities involving harsh chemicals.

**Specialized cleaning:**
Use jewelry cleaning solutions specifically designed for the metal or stone type. Follow instructions carefully.

**Regular polishing:**
Polish regularly to maintain shine and prevent tarnishing. Use soft cloth and gentle polishing compound designed for the metal type.

**Damage inspection:**
Regularly inspect for loose stones, broken clasps, worn-out prongs. Address issues promptly.

**Professional care:**
Consider taking jewelry to professional jeweler for maintenance (cleaning, polishing, repairs). Jewelers can provide guidance for specific pieces.

**Safety:**
Keep jewelry away from children and pets to prevent accidental damage or loss.

**Metal-specific notes:**
Some metals like silver are more prone to tarnishing. Silver: store away from sunlight, use silver polishing cloth or solution, apply clear coat of wax/varnish for protection, use anti-tarnish lined jewelry box.
