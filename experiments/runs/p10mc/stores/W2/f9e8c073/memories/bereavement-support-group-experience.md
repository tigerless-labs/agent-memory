## Bereavement Support Group Experience

**Attendance:**
- Completed 3 sessions of bereavement support group
- Attended while going through a difficult time with multiple losses

**Impact:**
- Found the experience very helpful
- Helped process emotions related to grief and loss
- Provided community support during grieving process

**Related grief coping strategies explored:**
- Allow yourself to feel emotions
- Take care of physical health
- Seek social support (support groups, therapy, friends/family)
- Find healthy ways to express emotions (writing, art, creative outlets)
- Create memorials or rituals to honor loved ones
- Be patient and kind to yourself during the grieving process
- Consider professional help if grief becomes overwhelming
