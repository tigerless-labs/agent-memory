## Travel Writing Preferences

During a Fiji attractions article rewrite (April 2023), user requested:

1. **Remove clichés**: "secluded paradise," "ideal place," "perfect spot," "crystal clear waters," "tropical ambiance," "take in the natural beauty"
2. **Tone**: Friendlier, more conversational than formal tourism copy
3. **Content changes**: Removed GVI volunteer program reference

### Example revision direction
Rewrote Cola Island section from generic ("This secluded paradise offers visitors beautiful beaches...") to more specific ("The Cola Island is a small island in the Yasawas known for its secluded beaches and clear waters...").

User wants travel copy that describes actual attractions concretely rather than relying on overused descriptors.
