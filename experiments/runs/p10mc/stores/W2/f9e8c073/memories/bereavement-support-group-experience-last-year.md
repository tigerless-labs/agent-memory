## The Experience

**Duration & Format:**
- Attended 5 sessions total (last year, prior to October 2023)
- Bereavement support group focused on processing grief and loss

**What Worked:**

1. **Sense of community** — shared experiences with other group members all going through grief; feeling less alone
2. **Facilitator qualities** — kind, understanding, and skilled at creating safe space
3. **Safe space** — could express emotions and share story without fear of judgment
4. **Active listening** — opportunity to listen to others' stories without pressure to offer solutions; just being heard was healing

**Key Insight:**
The combination of community support + skilled facilitation + judgment-free space created powerful healing environment. The model has proven effective.

## Future Application

Plans to build on this success by:
- Seeking therapy or counselor for continued guidance
- Joining online forums and social media communities focused on grief, loss, mental health
- Journaling as complementary therapeutic outlet
- Reaching out to friends/family who have similar experiences
