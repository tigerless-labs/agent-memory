## Project: Tono Folk Legend Rewrites

**Goal:** Rewrite folk legend passages in Roald Dahl's style — chatty, fun, easy to understand. Remove dryness while preserving all specific details (dates, names, locations, measurements).

**Style criteria:**
- Conversational, engaging tone
- Remove academic/dry language
- Child-friendly but engaging for all ages
- Maintain every factual detail from original

**Completed rewrites (6 pieces):**

1. **Gonzo** — fisherman, catches full bamboo basket of trout, sees growing ghost/monster near stone marker on mountain road
2. **Tetsuzo's tales** — Taimagura Pond (Mt. Hayachine), red-faced old man/tengu, young girl, ancient circular rock dwellings, rooster crow
3. **Yamada Bay narrative** — hairy-faced man killed → fish decline; Dutch ship Breskens landed June 10, 1643; crew arrested, imprisoned 4 months by Tokugawa Iemitsu
4. **Kikuchi Eisaku** — hunter, Mt. Hayachine, winter 1913, encountered round-eyed man with wisteria vine bag in dim evening light
5. **Mt. Nishinai child** — around 1920, September/October, large man with bag of squirming snakes in wisteria container
6. **Cloth dyer's wife** — 1870s, chased by dainyudo monster (3m+ tall priest-like creature), legs swelled permanently from running

**Source:** Folk Legends from Tono (academic translation)
