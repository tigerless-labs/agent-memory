## Healing Strategies in Development

**Professional Support**
- Seeking therapy or counselor for ongoing guidance and support

**Journaling Practice**
- Writing has always been a therapeutic outlet
- Plans to use journaling to process emotions, reflect on experiences, gain clarity, work through challenging feelings
- Approach: judgment-free zone, write freely without worrying about grammar or spelling

**Community Connections**
- Exploring online forums focused on grief and loss
- Exploring social media communities focused on mental health
- Goal: connect with others who understand the experience and can offer support and advice

**Personal Relationships**
- Reaching out to friends and family members who have gone through similar experiences
- Recognizing value of having someone to talk to who understands

## Overall Mindset

- Taking things one step at a time
- Celebrating small wins
- Being patient with setbacks or difficult days
- Grateful for support received so far
- Hopeful about continuing healing journey
- Recognizing that healing is a process, not a destination
