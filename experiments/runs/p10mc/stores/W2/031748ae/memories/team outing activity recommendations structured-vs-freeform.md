## Activity Categories Discussed

**Outdoor Games:**
- Giant Jenga (primary interest)
- Cornhole
- Ring Toss
- Giant Chess
- Board games and card games

**Food & Beverage Strategy:**
- Light snacks: veggie platters, cheese/charcuterie, sweet treats
- Beverages: mix of soft drinks, beer, wine, water
- Complimentary drink tickets per guest (if included in venue package)

**Venue Options:**
- Rooftop bars/restaurants with game rentals
- Dedicated event spaces (can accommodate 5-20+ people)
- Platforms for venue rental: Peerspace, Eventbrite, Rooftop Republic

## Event Format Guidelines

**Agenda Structure:**
- **Option 1 (Structured):** Use icebreaker games, team trivia, or workshops to encourage participation and relationship-building
- **Option 2 (Freeform):** Minimal structure; provide games/snacks and let team mingle naturally

**Recommended Hybrid Approach:**
- 30-45 minutes of structured activities
- 30-45 minutes of free mingling
- Repeat cycle as needed

**Timing:**
- **Optimal Duration:** 2-3 hours (enough time without feeling rushed)
- **Day/Time:** Weekday afternoon or early evening (less crowded)

**Optional Enhancements:**
- Hire photographer or set up selfie station
- Themed activities (scavenger hunt, team trivia)
- Catering or food/beverage packages

## Corporate Packages Typically Include:
- Private rooftop/venue rental (2-3 hours)
- Outdoor game selection
- Complimentary drink ticket per guest
- Optional food/beverage add-ons
- Customizable elements based on team needs
