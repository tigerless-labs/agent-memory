## Budget & Context
- **Budget per gift**: $20–$50
- **Recent spending**: ~$120 on gifts last month (July 2023)
- **Goal**: More budget-friendly this time around
- **Season**: Upcoming holidays (planning started August 4, 2023)

## Recipients
- **Nieces and nephews**: Ages 5–12 years old
- **Friends**: Photography enthusiasts, mostly beginners

## Gift Strategy
Decided to pursue **experience gifts + small token gifts**:
- Main gift: Experience (e.g., amusement park tickets, activity class, sporting event, concert/show, outdoor adventure, cooking class, zoo/aquarium visit)
- Small gift to open on gift day: Inexpensive ($5–10) item related to the experience theme, personalized item, small game/puzzle, treats, or memory keeper (journal/scrapbook)

## Small Gift Ideas by Experience Type
- Amusement park: Stuffed animal, candy bag, keychain
- Art class: Washable markers, small art kit, sketchbook
- Sporting event: Team sticker/magnet, foam finger, popcorn

## Rationale
Experiences create lasting memories, allow bonding, encourage trying new things, and often bring more joy than material gifts.
