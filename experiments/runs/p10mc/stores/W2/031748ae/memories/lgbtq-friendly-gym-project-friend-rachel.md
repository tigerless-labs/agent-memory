## Context
Friend **Rachel** — LGBTQ+ individual who experienced marginalization within the LGBTQ+ community. This conversation sparked the user's commitment to creating safer, more inclusive fitness spaces.

## Project Goal
Create welcoming gym environments for LGBTQ+ individuals through systemic changes to language, facilities, policies, and programming.

## LGBTQ+-Focused Fitness Classes & Workshops

**Classes:**
- Rainbow Yoga — mindfulness, self-love, community
- Queer Fitness — high-energy, LGBTQ+ culture celebration
- TransFit — designed for transgender/non-binary individuals
- Pride Pilates — body positivity, self-acceptance, strength
- LGBTQ+ Dance Fitness — music/moves from LGBTQ+ culture

**Workshops:**
- LGBTQ+ Mental Health and Wellness
- Gender-Neutral Fitness and Nutrition
- LGBTQ+ Self-Defense (empowerment-focused)
- Queer Body Positivity

## Language & Communication Best Practices
- Use gender-neutral language ("everyone," "folks," "members")
- Ask for preferred pronouns on introduction
- Avoid gendered language ("guys," "girls" → "athletes," "participants")
- Be aware of microaggressions
- Use inclusive signage ("All Gender Restroom," "LGBTQ+ Friendly Gym")
- Apologize and learn if wrong pronouns are used

## Gender-Neutral Facilities & Inclusive Policies

**Facilities:**
- All-gender restrooms
- Gender-neutral locker rooms or private changing areas
- Private shower stalls

**Policies:**
- Non-discrimination policy including gender identity, expression, sexual orientation
- Gender identity policy respecting preferred names and pronouns
- Inclusive dress code (not targeting transgender people)
- Inclusive membership policies

## Implementation Strategy
- Staff training on LGBTQ+ issues, gender identity, sexual orientation
- Phased approach (low-cost high-impact first: signage, policy updates)
- Clear communication of changes to members/staff
- Establish feedback mechanisms for members
- Partner with local LGBTQ+ organizations (GLAAD, The Trevor Project, HRC)
- Create LGBTQ+ advisory board from community members
- Consider discounts/scholarships for addressing barriers to access

## Promotion & Community Engagement
- Social media campaigns (#LGBTQFitness, #QueerFitness, #PrideMonth)
- Partner with LGBTQ+ organizations and community centers
- Reach out to LGBTQ+ fitness influencers/bloggers
- Host Pride-themed events and fundraisers
- Create marketing materials with LGBTQ+-inclusive imagery
