## Platform Workflow

User requested diagram design for medical/wellness platform combining:

1. **Entry point**: YouTube Viewers
2. **AI Chatbot Assessment**: Personalized health risk evaluation for:
   - Cancer
   - Depression
   - Anxiety
   - Chronic kidney diseases (from diabetes/hypertension)
3. **Free Music Therapy**: Listening to music therapy songs for anxiety, depression, anger management
4. **Fundraising**: Buy NFT tokens to support cancer patients & innovations
5. **Mental Health Screening**: PHQ 2 or PHQ 10 screening questionnaires
6. **Professional Support**: Group counseling with counselors and specialists, booked via:
   - Calendly platform
   - EHR (Electronic Health Record) booking calendar platform

## Notes
- Workflow designed for website and presentation use
- Music therapy is free tier
- NFT tokens serve as fundraising mechanism
- Integration with established screening tools (PHQ assessments)
- Uses both external (Calendly) and internal (EHR) booking systems
