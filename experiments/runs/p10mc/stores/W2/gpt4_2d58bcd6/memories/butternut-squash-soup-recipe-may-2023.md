## Butternut Squash Soup Recipe

Planning to make this weekend starting May 30, 2023.

**Ingredients (4-6 servings):**
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs for garnish

**Method:**
1. Roast butternut squash halves at 400°F for 45-50 minutes
2. Sauté onion in olive oil until softened, add garlic
3. Add spices, pour in broth
4. Scoop roasted squash into pot
5. Blend until smooth
6. Stir in cream if desired
7. Season to taste

**Storage:**
- Cool to room temperature within 2 hours
- Refrigerate in airtight container: 3-5 days
- Freeze in portions: 3-6 months
- Reheat to 165°F minimum

**Variations:**
- Add diced apple or kale
- Use coconut cream or Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream
- Try nutmeg or cinnamon
