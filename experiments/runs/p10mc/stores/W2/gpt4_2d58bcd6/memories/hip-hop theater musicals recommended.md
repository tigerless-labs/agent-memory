## Musicals Recommended (Hip-Hop & Theater Fusion)

**Primary Recommendations:**
- **Hamilton** — Lin-Manuel Miranda, hip-hop/jazz/blues/R&B blend, American founding father story
- **In the Heights** — Lin-Manuel Miranda, hip-hop/salsa/Latin, Washington Heights Latino community
- **Freestyle Love Supreme** — Lin-Manuel Miranda/Thomas Kail/Anthony Veneziale, improvised hip-hop musical, identity/community themes
- **Passing Strange** — Stew & Heidi Rodewald, rock/hip-hop, self-discovery story in 1970s Europe
- **Holler If Ya Hear Me** — Inspired by Tupac Shakur music, Todd Kreidler book, urban setting, justice/redemption themes

**Also Suggested:**
- Dear Evan Hansen, Rent, The Bomb-itty of Errors (hip-hop adaptation of The Comedy of Errors), Stagger Lee (hip-hop/R&B thriller), The Great Comet, Hadestown

All emphasize storytelling, social themes, and innovative genre blending.
