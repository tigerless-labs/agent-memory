Recently started listening to audiobooks during daily commute, around late May 2023.

**Audiobooks completed:**
- Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind by Yuval Noah Harari
- Daisy Jones and The Six by Taylor Jenkins Reid

**Narration preferences:**
- Strongly prefers full-cast narrations, especially for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes — adds immersive experience and helps track who is speaking
- Also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style to the story

**Views on audiobook narration:**
- Believes narration is crucial and can make or break the listening experience
- Good narrator can elevate the story and make it more engaging; poor narrator detracts
- Narrator's tone, pace, and inflection greatly impact how story and characters are interpreted
- Skilled narrators can amplify emotional impact and convey emotional depth and complexity
- Narration creates collaborative experience with listener's imagination — narrator provides interpretation, listener builds upon it with own experiences and perspectives
