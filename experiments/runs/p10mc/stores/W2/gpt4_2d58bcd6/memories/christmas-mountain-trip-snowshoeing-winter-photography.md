## Christmas Mountain Trip Plans

Planned for Christmas 2023 (winter season).

**Activities:**
- Snowshoeing
- Winter landscape photography

**Photography Goals:**
- Capture winter landscape beauty during snowshoeing
- Document snow-covered terrain and natural scenery

**Recommended Camera Settings for Winter Landscapes:**
- Mode: Manual (M)
- ISO: 100-400 (low to minimize noise)
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (medium to small for depth of field)
- Shutter speed: 1/125s-1/250s
- White balance: Cloudy or Shade

**Photography Techniques:**
- Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop to capture snow details
- Use polarizing filter to reduce glare
- Shoot during golden hour for soft, warm light
- Shoot in RAW format
- Bracket shots for HDR merging
- Use wide-angle lens (10-24mm) for landscape vastness
- Look for leading lines, patterns, textures
- Shoot from low angles
- Capture snowflakes at f/2.8, 1/1000s
- Look for animal tracks in snow

**Preparation:**
- Bring spare batteries (cold drains them quickly)
- Weatherproof camera gear
- Pack warm clothing, first aid kit, snacks, thermos
- Take breaks to review and adjust approach

**Related equipment:**
- Sony A7III camera with 100-400mm Sony E-mount lens
