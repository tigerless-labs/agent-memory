## Artists Blending Hip-Hop & Theater

**Key Artists:**
- **Kendrick Lamar** — socially conscious, storytelling-driven hip-hop with musical theater sensibilities
- **The Roots** — legendary hip-hop group, blends hip-hop/R&B/rock, collaborated on Hamilton Mixtape
- **Lauryn Hill** — pioneering blend of hip-hop, R&B, soul, reggae with theatrical elements
- **A Tribe Called Quest** — influential in hip-hop theater development, identity/community themes
- **J. Period** — hip-hop artist/producer, worked on hip-hop adaptation of Romeo and Juliet (R&J: The Hip-Hop Version)

**Also Mentioned:**
- Vulfpeck (funk-rock eclecticism), Ben Folds (piano-driven pop-rock storytelling)

**Theater Organizations:**
- The Hip-Hop Shakespeare Company (UK, led by Akala) — fuses hip-hop with Shakespearean verse
- The Remix Project — collaboration between hip-hop artists and musical theater composers
- Rhymes Over Reason — hip-hop-infused productions including The Realness (Tupac-inspired)
- Kennedy Center Hip-Hop Theater Festival (annual)
