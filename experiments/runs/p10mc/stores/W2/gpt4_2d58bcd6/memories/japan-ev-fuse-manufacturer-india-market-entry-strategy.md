## Company Profile
- **Products**: High-voltage fuses for electric vehicles
- **Current base**: Japan
- **Target market**: India

## India Market Entry Strategy

### 1. Market Research
- Understand unique needs of Indian EV industry
- Study prevalent EV models, charging infrastructure, local manufacturing capabilities
- Analyze existing competitors
- Key distinction: Indian market is price-sensitive, demographically diverse, has varying infrastructure development

### 2. Indian Standards & Compliance
- Ensure fuses meet India's specific regulatory and quality standards
- Acquire necessary certifications
- Critical for legal compliance and building customer trust

### 3. Localized Production or Partnership
- Consider establishing local production or partnering with Indian company
- Reduces costs and ensures timely delivery
- Competitive pricing advantage essential in Indian market
- Local partnership helps navigate regulatory environment

### 4. Pricing Strategy
- India market is highly price-sensitive
- Develop competitive pricing while maintaining quality
- Price directly impacts product adoption

### 5. Distribution Network
- Develop robust distribution given vast and diverse Indian market
- Partner with established automotive parts distributors and retailers
- Explore e-commerce platforms for visibility and availability

### 6. Marketing & Promotion
- Highlight benefits: improved safety, reliability, performance
- Use digital marketing and social media
- Participate in local industry events
- Tailor messaging to diverse Indian consumer needs and preferences

### 7. After-Sales Service & Support
- Establish network of service centers
- Train local technicians
- Critical for brand loyalty and long-term customer relationships in India

## Key Market Differences
Indian market vs. other markets:
- Higher price sensitivity
- Diverse demographic requiring localized approach
- Varying infrastructure development levels
- Specific regulatory environment requiring compliance
- After-sales service plays larger role in brand trust and loyalty
