## Movie Musicals Recommended

**Jazz-Influenced:**
- Chicago (2002) — Renee Zellweger, Richard Gere, Catherine Zeta-Jones, jazz-infused score
- Moulin Rouge! (2001) — Nicole Kidman, Ewan McGregor, Paris setting, jazz/pop/rock blend
- The Great Debaters (2007) — Denzel Washington, Wiley College debate team, jazz-infused score

**Pop-Influenced:**
- Hairspray (2007) — John Travolta, Queen Latifah, Zac Efron, 1960s setting, pop score
- Mamma Mia! (2008) — Meryl Streep, Amanda Seyfried, Pierce Brosnan, ABBA songs
- Rock of Ages (2012) — Tom Cruise, Julianne Hough, Diego Boneta, pop-rock 1980s

**Hip-Hop/Rap-Influenced:**
- Get Rich or Die Tryin' (2005) — 50 Cent biopic, Terrence Howard, Ludacris
- Hustle & Flow (2005) — Terrence Howard, Taraji P. Henson, Ludacris, small-time pimp-to-rapper story
- Wild Style (1983) — Early hip-hop film, considered one of the first hip-hop movies

**Multi-Genre Blend:**
- La La Land (2016) — User recently rewatched and loves; jazz/pop/contemporary musical
- The Bodyguard (1992) — Whitney Houston, Kevin Costner, pop-infused romantic thriller
- Enchanted (2007) — Amy Adams, Patrick Dempsey, pop-infused fantasy
- Scottsboro (2001) — Blair Underwood, Charles S. Dutton, blues/jazz/gospel blend
