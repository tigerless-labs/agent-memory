## Card Overview
**Issuer:** Citi
**Airline:** American Airlines (AAdvantage program)

## Key Benefits
- Sign-up bonus: 50,000 AAdvantage miles after 5000 dollar spend in first 3 months
- Earning: 2x miles on American Airlines purchases, 1x on all other purchases
- Admirals Club lounge access: For cardholder plus up to 2 guests or family
- Priority check-in, security, and boarding on American Airlines
- Global Entry or TSA PreCheck credit: 100 dollars
- In-flight savings: 25% off food, beverages, headsets on American Airlines
- Anniversary bonus: 10,000 miles when spending 40,000 dollars in prior year
- Travel insurance and trip protection

## Costs and Requirements
- Annual fee: 450 dollars
- Credit score needed: 700+ FICO (good to excellent)
- Foreign transaction fees: None

## Considerations Before Applying
- High annual fee requires justifying with travel habits
- Requires meeting 5000 dollar spending in 3 months for bonus
- Consider 40000 dollar annual spend target for anniversary bonus
- Check if AAdvantage program aligns with travel plans
- Compare with other American Airlines card options with lower annual fees
