**Project:** Reorganizing kitchen cabinets to optimize storage and make it easier to maintain the new tidying habit.

**Strategies recommended:**
- Purge and declutter items not used in past year
- Categorize items (baking, cooking, dinnerware, etc.)
- Assign specific homes for each category
- Use vertical space with stackable containers/baskets
- Install adjustable shelves if needed
- Utilize cabinet door backs for spices/utensils
- Store heavy items on lower shelves
- Keep frequently used items front and center
- Label shelves and cabinets
- Keep the system flexible for future changes

**Purpose:** To make putting away dishes and appliances quicker and easier, supporting the "one-touch rule" strategy for kitchen counter tidiness.
