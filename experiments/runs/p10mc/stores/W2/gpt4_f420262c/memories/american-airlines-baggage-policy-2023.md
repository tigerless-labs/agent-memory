## American Airlines Baggage Policy (February 2023)

**Checked Bags:**
- First bag: $30 online, $40 at airport (Main Cabin)
- First bag: Free for Premium Economy, Business, First Class
- Second bag: $40 online, $50 at airport
- Size limit: 62 inches (L+W+H combined), 50 pounds max
- Overweight/oversized bags incur additional fees

**Carry-on Bags:**
- Size: Max 22" tall × 14" wide × 9" deep
- Plus one personal item (purse, briefcase, laptop bag)

**Baggage Delivery Guarantee:**
- Delayed baggage defined as: not available at claim within 4 hours of arrival
- If not delivered within 24 hours: $25 voucher for future travel
- Lost baggage reimbursement: up to $3,500 for contents

**Baggage Insurance Options:**
- Available through Allianz Global Assistance
- Baggage Protection: $12-20 per person per trip
- Coverage: up to $1,000 per bag
- Covers loss, damage, theft, and essentials reimbursement for delays

**Tips mentioned:**
- Pack essentials and valuables in carry-on
- Label bags with name, address, contact info
- Keep receipts for emergency purchases while bag is delayed
- Check credit card benefits for baggage insurance
- Check travel insurance policy
