## Travel Insurance Types and Pricing

Approximate prices for 4-day domestic USA trip per person:

1. **Trip Cancellation Insurance:** 20-50 dollars
   - Covers cancellation due to illness, injury, natural disasters

2. **Trip Interruption Insurance:** 15-30 dollars
   - Covers interruption and early return due to unforeseen circumstances

3. **Travel Medical Insurance:** 10-25 dollars
   - Covers emergency medical expenses and evacuation

4. **Travel Delay Insurance:** 10-20 dollars
   - Covers flight delays/cancellations requiring new flight/accommodation

## How to Purchase
- Direct from provider websites
- Through online travel agencies: Expedia, Orbitz, Travelocity

## Recommendation
Comprehensive policy covering trip cancellation, interruption, and travel delay provides best protection against unforeseen circumstances.
