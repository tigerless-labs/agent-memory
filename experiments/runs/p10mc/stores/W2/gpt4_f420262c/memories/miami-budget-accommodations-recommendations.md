## Budget-Friendly Options

**Hostels (30-40 per night):**
- Jazz Hostel: Centrally located, dorms and private rooms
- Miami Beach Hostel: Well-reviewed, dorms and private rooms

**Budget Hotels (60-90 per night):**
- Red Roof Inn Miami Airport: No-frills option
- Super 8 by Wyndham Miami: Basic amenities

## Mid-Range Options

**Hotels (100-140 per night):**
- Hampton Inn & Suites Miami Midtown: Comfortable accommodations
- Hyatt Place Miami Airport East: Modern hotel

**Vacation Rentals:**
- Airbnb: Private rooms or apartments, 80-120 per night
- VRBO: Apartments and houses, 100-150 per night

## Booking Tips
- Book in advance for better rates
- Consider off-season (May-September) for lower prices
- Watch for promotions on Expedia, Booking.com, Hotels.com
- Read reviews on TripAdvisor, Yelp, Google Reviews
- Check cancellation policies and location before booking
- Hostels and Airbnb often cheaper than traditional hotels, especially solo travelers
