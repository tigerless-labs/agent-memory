## JetBlue Amenities

**Included Free:**
- Live TV: 36 channels of sports, news, entertainment
- Wi-Fi: Fast, free throughout flight
- In-Flight Entertainment: Fly-Fi system on personal devices (phone, tablet, laptop)
- Snacks: Terra Chips, animal crackers, peanuts
- Drinks: Complimentary (additional items for purchase)
- Standard seat pitch: 32 inches with adjustable headrests
- Pause/resume content across flights feature

**Paid Options:**
- Even More Space seats: 37-inch pitch for 10-25 dollars per seat
- Additional snacks and drinks (sandwiches, salads, beer, etc.)

## Seat Selection Process
- Automatic random assignment at booking
- Free seat selection: choose at check-in (24 hours before flight)
- Availability subject to how quickly you check in
- Popular seats (aisle, window) book quickly

## In-Flight Entertainment System
- Fly-Fi system uses personal devices via free Wi-Fi
- More modern and reliable than American Airlines systems
- No seat-back screens (device-based instead)
- User-friendly interface
