Lost earbuds previously. Interested in Samsung Galaxy Buds Pro as replacement. Key features valued:
- Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) with three modes (High, Medium, Low) and ambient sound mode
- Up to 5 hours playtime with ANC on, 13 hours total with charging case
- Wireless charging support via Qi-compatible pads
- Power Share feature allows charging via Samsung phone battery
- Quick charge: 1 hour playtime in 5 minutes
- Comparable ANC quality to Sony WF-1000XM4 and Apple AirPods Pro
