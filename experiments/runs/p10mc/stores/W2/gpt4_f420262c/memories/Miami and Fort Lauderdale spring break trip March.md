## Trip Overview

- **Destination:** Fort Lauderdale/Miami area for spring break
- **Travel dates:** March (spring break)
- **Outbound flight:** Boston to Fort Lauderdale on Spirit Airlines
- **Return flight:** Fort Lauderdale to Boston on Spirit Airlines
- **Hotel:** Considering Hyatt Regency Miami Airport
- **Post-trip:** Cruise departing from Port of Miami cruise terminal

## Spirit Airlines Baggage Policy

- **Personal item:** Free, maximum size 18 x 14 x 8 inches
- **Carry-on bag:** $35–$45 if paid online during booking or up to 24 hours before flight; $45 if paid during online check-in; $55 at airport
- **First checked bag:** $30–$40 when paid online during booking
- **Oversized/overweight fees:** Apply for bags exceeding limits
- **Note:** User plans to double-check luggage dimensions before the trip

## Hotel and Cruise Transfer Details

- **Hotel considered:** Hyatt Regency Miami Airport
- **Shuttle service to Port of Miami cruise terminal:**
  - Available on cruise departure days
  - Typical departures: ~10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM (subject to change)
  - Travel time: approximately 20–30 minutes
  - Reservation required via hotel concierge: (305) 871-1234
  - First-come, first-served basis; recommend reserving early
  - Luggage space may be limited
