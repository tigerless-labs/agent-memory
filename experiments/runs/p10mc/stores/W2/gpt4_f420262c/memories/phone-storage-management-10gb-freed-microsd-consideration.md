Used Google's Storage Manager on Samsung S23 Ultra to organize and free up storage. Achievements:
- Freed 10GB of storage space using Storage Manager
- Familiar with identifying large files, duplicate files, unused apps, and cached data

Considering microSD card expansion. Pros discussed:
- Expanded storage capacity
- Cost-effective vs. new phone
- Flexibility to swap cards

Cons discussed:
- Slower read/write speeds than internal storage
- Potential compatibility issues
- Requires additional hardware

Decision: Not immediately needed given 10GB freed, but may be useful for future storage needs or data transfer between devices. If pursuing, looking for high-capacity (128GB-256GB), reputable brands like Samsung, SanDisk, or Western Digital.
