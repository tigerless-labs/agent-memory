## Entrepreneurial Journey — Time Management & Growth Strategy

**Challenge:** Juggling multiple responsibilities as solo founder — business development, accounting, marketing, client work all at once; overwhelmed by trying to do too much alone

**Key Realization:** Need to focus on high-leverage activities and delegate or outsource tasks that consume too much time

### Time Management Strategy (Adopting)
- **Eisenhower Matrix:** Prioritize tasks by urgency and importance (Do first / Schedule / Delegate / Eliminate)
- **Time Blocks:** Schedule dedicated blocks for:
  - Business development (networking, partnerships)
  - Client work
  - Marketing and growth
  - Admin and operations
- **High-Leverage Focus:** Identify tasks with greatest impact on agency growth
- **Delegation/Outsourcing:** Free up time by outsourcing lower-impact tasks as agency grows
- **Self-Care:** Maintain breaks, exercise, physical and mental well-being

### Mindset Shift
User recognizing that time is valuable resource; optimization needed for sustainable growth and reduced stress
