## American Airlines Customer Service & IFE (February 2023)

**Customer Service Ratings:**
- Skytrax: 3/5 stars (22,000+ reviews)
- TripAdvisor: 3.5/5 stars (33,000+ reviews)
- Consumer Reports: 64/100 (18,000+ passenger survey)
- Overall: Mixed reputation but improving in recent years

**Flight Disruption Handling:**
- Rebooking: On next available or partner airline
- Compensation: Vouchers, hotel, meals (varies by circumstances)
- Notification: Proactive via email, text, mobile app

**Customer Service Channels:**
- Phone: 1-800-433-7300 (expect wait times in peak periods)
- Online: Chat, email, feedback form on website
- Social media: @AmericanAir (Twitter), Facebook

**In-Flight Entertainment Systems:**
- Most aircraft: Seat-back touchscreens (Boeing 737-800, 777-200, A321T)
- Some aircraft: Tablet-based entertainment (Boeing 737 MAX, A321)
- Select routes: Stream to personal device via app
- Content: Movies, TV shows, music, games, live TV on some flights

**Recommendations made:**
- Download American Airlines app for flight updates and mobile boarding pass
- Sign up for flight alerts (email/text)
- Download content to device as backup
- Know DOT passenger rights
