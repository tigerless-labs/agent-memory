## Trip Details
- Destination: Fort Lauderdale
- Trip type: Spring break
- When: March 2026
- Airline: Spirit Airlines

## Spirit Airlines Baggage Policies
**Carry-on bags:** 22x14x9 inches max; not included in base fare; 30-45 dollars online, 40-55 dollars at airport

**Checked bags:** 62 linear inches max; 40 lbs limit; not included in base fare; 25-40 dollars online, 35-50 dollars at airport

**Overweight bags:** 75-100 dollars surcharge per bag exceeding 40 lbs

**Personal item:** Free (purse, small backpack, briefcase fitting under seat)

## Tips to Minimize Baggage Fees
- Pack light to fit under seat as personal item only
- Book baggage during initial booking (cheaper than airport)
- Look for Spirit bundle deals
- Pre-measure and weigh bags before travel date
