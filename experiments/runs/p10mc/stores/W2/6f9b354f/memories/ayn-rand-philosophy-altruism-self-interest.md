Ayn Rand was an American philosopher who challenged conventional views of altruism and selfishness.

**Her key arguments:**

- Nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests to survive
- Being completely selfless and giving everything to others harms oneself and society in the long run  
- Selflessness can be manipulated to justify suppression of individual rights by dictators
- Prioritizing one's own interests is not only necessary but also moral (her "philosophy of self-interest")

**Significance:**
Her ideas reverse the traditional view of selfishness as negative and altruism as positive, contributing to ongoing debates about balancing individual rights with collective good.
