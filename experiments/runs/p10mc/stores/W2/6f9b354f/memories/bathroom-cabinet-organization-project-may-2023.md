## Project Overview

Planning to tackle bathroom deep clean in May 2023, with bathroom cabinets as the main focus area.

## Current State

Bathroom cabinets are disorganized and chaotic; user has been putting this off for a while but is determined to complete it.

## Organization Strategy

**Phase 1: Purge & Declutter**
- Remove expired beauty products and medications
- Get rid of items no longer used
- Donate gently used items
- Recycle packaging

**Phase 2: Organize Remaining Items**
- Use dividers and baskets to create compartments
- Group items by category (skincare, haircare, oral care, medications, etc.)
- Label each compartment
- Store frequently used items in easy-to-reach locations
- Place heavier items (hair dryers, curling irons) on lower shelves

**Key Ideas**
- Utilize back of cabinet door with over-the-door storage racks or hooks
- Consider lazy Susan/turntable for hard-to-reach corners
- Keep system functional and easy to maintain

## Timeline

Starting with purge/declutter first, then moving to layout and product experimentation.
