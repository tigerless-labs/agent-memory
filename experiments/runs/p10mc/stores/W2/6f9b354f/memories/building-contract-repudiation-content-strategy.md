## Project Overview
Content strategy for building contract repudiation educational materials targeting multiple personas in construction industry.

## Target Personas
- Developers
- Homeowners
- Builders
- Contractors

## Key Concepts Covered
- Repudiation definition: party shows through conduct it no longer intends to be bound, or intends to fulfill only in substantially inconsistent way
- Breach vs. Repudiation distinction
- Rights to terminate when other party repudiates
- Wrongful termination consequences
- Legal remedies and damages

## SILO Support Pages
- Understanding Breach of Contract — "what is breach of contract?"
- Homeowner's Rights to Terminate — "can homeowners terminate a building contract?"
- Builder's Rights to Terminate — "can builders terminate a building contract?"
- Wrongful Termination — "what is wrongful termination of a building contract?"
- Contract Termination Process — "how to terminate a building contract?"
- Contract Specialist Services — "when to hire a construction lawyer?"
- Damages for Repudiation — "what damages can be claimed for repudiation?"
