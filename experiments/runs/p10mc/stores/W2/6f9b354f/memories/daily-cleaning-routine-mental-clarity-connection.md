## Daily & Weekly Cleaning Routine

Established a structured cleaning routine with three levels:

**Daily tasks:**
- Make bed
- Wipe down kitchen counters

**Weekly tasks:**
- Vacuum
- Tidy up living room

**Deep cleaning (one per week):**
- Rotating focus: organizing closet, cleaning fridge, bathroom, etc.

## Mental Health Connection

Reports improved mental clarity and calm/relaxed feeling before bed after evening tidying. This positive connection motivates her to maintain routine despite evening exhaustion after long days.

## Approach to Tasks

- Breaks larger projects into manageable chunks over time
- Takes time with projects rather than rushing
- Focuses on creating sustainable habits
- Motivated by cumulative long-term benefits
