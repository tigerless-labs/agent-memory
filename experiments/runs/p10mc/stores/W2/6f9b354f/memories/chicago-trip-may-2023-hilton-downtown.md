## Chicago Trip (May 2023)

**Hotel:** Hilton Hotel, downtown Chicago
- Checked out: May 21, 2023
- Room: 14th floor
- Amenities: small balcony (wished for more time to enjoy), fitness center (visited twice, impressed with equipment range)
- Nearby: about 5-10 min walk to various attractions

**Plans/interests mentioned:**
- Grab casual coffee at Intelligentsia Coffee (53 E Randolph St, 2-min walk)
- Visit Willis Tower (Sears Tower) Skydeck (103rd floor, 233 S Wacker Dr)
- Run on Lakefront Trail (18.5-mile path with city/lake views)
- Take Chicago's First Lady Cruises architectural cruise (90 minutes, Chicago River)

**Coffee shops recommended nearby:**
- Intelligentsia Coffee (53 E Randolph St, 2-min walk) — chose this one
- Starbucks Reserve Bar (646 S Michigan Ave, 3-min walk)
- La Colombe Coffee Roasters (1552 N Wells St, 5-min walk)
- Peet's Coffee (444 S LaSalle St, 5-min walk)
- The Coffee Studio (56 E Randolph St, 7-min walk)

**Other viewpoints/observation decks suggested:**
- Millennium Park's Lurie Garden (201 E Randolph St, 5-min walk)
- Navy Pier's Polk Bros Park (600 E Grand Ave)
- 360 CHICAGO Observation Deck (94th floor, 875 N Michigan Ave, 10-min ride)

**Museums/galleries near Hilton:**
- The Art Institute of Chicago (111 S Michigan Ave, 10-min walk)
- Field Museum (1400 S Lake Shore Dr, 15-min walk)
- Museum of Contemporary Art (220 E Chicago Ave, 15-min walk)
- Richard H. Driehaus Museum (40 E Erie St, 10-min walk)

**User interests demonstrated:** fitness centers, city views from high up, running/jogging, casual dining, architecture
