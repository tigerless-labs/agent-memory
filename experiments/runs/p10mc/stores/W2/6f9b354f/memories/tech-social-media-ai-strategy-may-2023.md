## Strategy: Define Brand Voice First
Before using AI-generated content tools, plan to:
1. Create explicit brand voice and tone guide
2. Train AI with existing company content (blog posts, social posts, website copy)
3. Always review/curate AI output before publishing
4. Edit and refine with personal anecdotes, humor, emotional connections
5. Use AI as starting point, not final product
6. Blend AI-generated content with human-created content
7. Monitor performance and iterate
8. Prioritize AI for research rather than pure generation
