## Stop Doing List - Strategy to Implement

**Concept Origin:**
- From Neil Strauss episode of The Tim Ferriss Show (Episode #193)
- Also discussed in The Happiness Lab podcast

**Purpose:**
- Reduce distractions during peak productivity hours (8:30 AM-12:00 PM)
- Free up time and mental energy for high-priority tasks
- Eliminate activities that drain energy or don't align with goals

**Expected Benefits:**
- Increased focus on important tasks
- More time for productivity
- Better mental clarity
- Improved energy conservation

**Potential Items to Consider for Stop Doing List:**
- Checking social media frequently
- Excessive TV or YouTube watching
- Gossip or negative conversations
- Procrastination habits
- Non-essential meetings
