## Abortion Procedures: Timing and Side Effects

### Medication Abortion
- **Timing:** 24-48 hours to complete (two doses)
- **Common side effects:** Cramping, bleeding, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headache, fever
- **Serious complications (rare):** Heavy bleeding, infection, incomplete abortion

### Vacuum Aspiration (Suction/Aspiration Abortion)
- **Timing:** 5-10 minutes
- **Common side effects:** Cramping, bleeding, mild pain or discomfort
- **Serious complications (rare):** Heavy bleeding, infection, injury to uterus or surrounding organs

### Dilation and Curettage (D&C)
- **Timing:** 10-20 minutes
- **Common side effects:** Cramping, bleeding, discomfort
- **Serious complications (rare):** Infection, uterine perforation

### Dilation and Evacuation (D&E)
- **Timing:** Several hours (depends on pregnancy size and other factors)
- **Common side effects:** Cramping, bleeding, discomfort
- **Serious complications (rare):** Injury to uterus or surrounding organs, heavy bleeding, infection

## Considerations When Choosing Procedure Type
- Consult healthcare provider for personalized information
- Stage of pregnancy determines available options (medication abortion up to ~10 weeks; D&E for later pregnancies)
- Personal preference, medical history, age, previous deliveries, health conditions
- Plan for aftercare, follow-up appointments, emotional support
- Understand legal rights and local abortion laws
