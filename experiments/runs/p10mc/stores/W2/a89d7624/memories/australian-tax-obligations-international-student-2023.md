## Tax Situation for International Students in Australia

**Tax Status:** Non-resident for tax purposes (taxed on Australian-sourced income only)

**Tax-Free Threshold:** 18,201 AUD for 2022-2023 financial year
- Below threshold: no tax owed (but may still need to file return)
- Above threshold: must pay taxes

**Tax Rates (Non-Resident):**
- 32.5% on income 0–90,000 AUD
- 37% on income 90,001–180,000 AUD
- 45% on income above 180,000 AUD

**Required Tax Forms:**
- NAT 2541: Tax return (annual deadline: October 31st)
- NAT 1432: Tax File Number (TFN) application (if needed)

**Payment Obligations:**
- Self-employed (freelancer/tutor) responsible for own tax payments
- May require PAYG (Pay As You Go) quarterly instalment payments to ATO

**Freelance/Tutoring Income:**
- Considered Australian-sourced income
- Fully taxable regardless of student status
- Recommendation: consult tax professional or accountant familiar with international student tax laws

**Resources:**
- Australian Taxation Office (ATO) website for latest rates and requirements
- Tax laws subject to change annually
