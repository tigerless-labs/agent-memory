## Recommended Audiobooks and Books

### Highly Recommended (Multiple Recommendations)
- **The City of Brass** by S.A. Chakraborty (Middle Eastern mythology, 18th century Cairo, strong female protagonist)
- **The House of Shattered Wings** by Aliette de Bodard (Vietnamese mythology, post-apocalyptic Paris, dark fantasy)
- **The Black Tides of Heaven** by J.Y. Yang (Chinese mythology, novella, strong female protagonist)
- **The Tiger's Daughter** by K. Arsenault Rivera (Japanese and Mongolian mythology, East Asian inspired world)
- **The Bone Shard War** by Andrea Stewart (Chinese and Southeast Asian mythology, strong female protagonist)
- **The Library of the Unwritten** by A.J. Hackwith (Multicultural mythology, diverse cast from various cultures)

### African Mythology and Folklore
- **Anansi Boys** by Neil Gaiman (African folklore, spider trickster Anansi)
- **The Fisherman** by John Langan (West African mythology, modern-day New York horror)
- **The House of Whispers** by Laura Purcell (African and Caribbean mythology, gothic atmosphere, strong female protagonist)
- **The Deep** by Rivers Solomon (African diasporic mythology, strong female protagonist)

### Asian Mythology
- **The City of Brass** by S.A. Chakraborty (Middle Eastern, 18th century Cairo setting)
- **The Star-Touched Queen** by Roshani Chokshi (Indian mythology, YA fantasy)
- **The Gilded Wolves** by Roshani Chokshi (Indian and French mythology, 19th-century Paris, YA)
- **The Black Tides of Heaven** by J.Y. Yang (Chinese mythology)

### Latin American/Mesoamerican Mythology
- **The Obsidian Tower** by Melissa Caruso (Mesoamerican-inspired cultures, historical setting, strong female protagonist)

### Greek Mythology Retellings (Similar Historical Treatment)
- **The Penelopiad** by Margaret Atwood (Penelope's perspective on the Odyssey)
- **The Silence of the Girls** by Pat Barker (Briseis' perspective on the Iliad)

### Dystopian with Feminist Themes (Similar to "The Power")
- **The Handmaid's Tale** by Margaret Atwood (narrated by Claire Danes)
- **The Testaments** by Margaret Atwood (sequel to Handmaid's Tale)
- **The Girls** by Emma Cline (Manson-like cult, female relationships and power dynamics)
- **The Belles** by Dhonielle Clayton (fantasy, magic-wielding women forced to serve ruling class)

### Historical Fiction with Strong Female Leads (Similar to "The Nightingale")
- **The Alice Network** by Kate Quinn (female spies WWI/WWII, two protagonists)
- **The Lost Girls of Paris** by Pam Jenoff (1946, resistance network women)
- **The Women in the Castle** by Jessica Shattuck (WWII Germany, three women)
- **Those Who Save Us** by Jenna Blum (mother-daughter relationship, WWII/Holocaust backdrop)
- **The Soldier's Wife** by Pam Jenoff (Nazi-occupied France)
- **The Velvet Hours** by Alyssa Palombo (Belle Époque Paris, based on true story of courtesan Marthe de Florian)
