## Audience Interests

## Platform Strategy

- **Instagram Live**: 10–30 min streams, visually-focused, behind-the-scenes, interactive polls/questions, mobile-first (vertical filming)
- **YouTube Live**: 30–60 min in-depth content, high-quality production, evergreen value, SEO-optimized, community building; live tutorials perform well here
- **Facebook Live**: 30–90 min community-focused, personal stories, events, call-to-action buttons
