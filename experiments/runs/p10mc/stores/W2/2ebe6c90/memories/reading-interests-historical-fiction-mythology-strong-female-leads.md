## Reading Preferences

### Genres and Themes
- **Historical fiction** with strong female leads (especially WWII era settings)
- **Mythology and folklore retellings** from diverse cultures, particularly non-Western sources
- **Feminist themes** and perspectives
- **Dystopian settings** with strong character development
- **Character-driven narratives** exploring complex relationships

### Geographic/Cultural Interests
- Greek mythology
- Chinese mythology and history
- Indian mythology
- Russian folklore
- Middle Eastern mythology with historical settings
- Vietnamese mythology
- Japanese and Mongolian mythology
- African and Caribbean folklore
- West African mythology
- Latin American and Mesoamerican cultures and folklore

### Preferred Format
- Audiobooks (at least for some reads)

### What Resonates
- Retellings from alternative perspectives (e.g., Penelope in the Odyssey, Briseis in the Iliad)
- Stories that reimagine classic tales with fresh viewpoints
- Rich, atmospheric prose
- Layered storytelling with historical detail
