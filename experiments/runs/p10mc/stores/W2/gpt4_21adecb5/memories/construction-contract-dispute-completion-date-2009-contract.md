## Case: Construction Contract Dispute — Completion Date

**Contract date:** 2009  
**Parties:** Builder vs. Owners  
**Key dispute:** When did practical completion of building works occur?

### Tribunal's Original Finding
- Practical completion occurred 20 October 2010 when FOC (Final Occupation Certificate) was issued
- Interpreted FOC as part of "building works" definition
- Reasoned that FOC is essential planning/building approval enabling legal occupation

### Appeal Court's Reversal (Final Decision)
- Tribunal erred in contract interpretation
- FOC is **not** part of physical building works; it is a separate planning approval
- Clause 7.1 makes builder responsible for obtaining approvals, but obtaining approval ≠ building works
- "Practical completion" defined in contract: physical works complete except minor defects not preventing usual purpose
- FOC receipt is not necessary condition for practical completion of physical works

### Key Contract Clauses (HIA Standard Form)
- **Clause 1 — "Building works":** Physical work to be carried out, completed, handed over per contract documents including variations
- **Clause 1 — "Practical completion":** Works complete except minor omissions/defects that do not prevent works from being reasonably usable for usual purpose
- **Clause 7.1 — Planning and Building Approvals:** Party named in Item 11, Schedule 1 (the builder) must obtain and pay for all building and planning approvals
- **Clause 26.2(c) — Progress Payments:** Final progress claim due on practical completion; must include occupancy certificate if required by law
- **Clause 26.7 — Final Payment:** Payable within 5 working days after practical completion and receipt of required documentation

### Tribunal Decision Date
16 March 2020

### Appeal Outcome
- Appeal allowed in part
- Tribunal's 16 March 2020 orders set aside
- Matter remitted to tribunal for rehearing
- Owners to pay builder's costs of appeal
