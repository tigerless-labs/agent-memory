## Chicken Flock Egg Production Issue

**Date:** May 22, 2023

**Problem:** Flock laying fewer eggs; uncertain if due to seasonal change or calcium deficiency.

**Recent Action Taken:**
- Cleaned chicken coop recently
- Replaced old bedding with fresh straw (not the cause)

**Planned Interventions:**

1. **Review Current Feed:** Check layer pellet label for calcium content (should be 3-4%)

2. **Supplements to Add:**
   - **Oyster shells:** 1-2 cups per 10 hens per week (free-choice) — source of calcium carbonate for eggshell production
   - **Dark leafy greens:** Kale, spinach, collard greens as treats — rich in calcium, vitamins, minerals

3. **Probiotics:**
   - Plain yogurt: Once per week mixed with feed
   - Fermented grains: Fermented oats

4. **Fruits & Vegetables:** (Keep under 10% of diet)
   - Apples (remove seeds/cores)
   - Carrots
   - Berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
   - Sweet potatoes (cooked, diced)
   - Pumpkin/squash (cooked, diced)
   - Broccoli, cauliflower

**Target Feed Composition for Laying Hens:**
- Protein: 16-18%
- Fat: 2-3%
- Carbohydrates: 40-50%
- Fiber: 2-4%
- Calcium: 3-4%
- Phosphorus: 0.5-1%

**Approach:** Introduce changes gradually; monitor behavior, droppings, and egg production; consult veterinarian if concerns persist.
