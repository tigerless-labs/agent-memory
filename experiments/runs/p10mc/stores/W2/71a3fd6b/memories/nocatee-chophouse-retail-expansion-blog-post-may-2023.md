## Blog Post: Nocatee Retail Expansion with Chophouse Nocatee

**Date**: May 22, 2023  
**Project**: Nocatee Town Center Expansion  
**Size**: 17,000 square feet  
**Anchor Tenant**: Chophouse Nocatee (high-end steakhouse + rooftop bar)

### Key Details
- **Location**: Nocatee, Florida — Town Center
- **Restaurant**: Chophouse Nocatee
  - High-end steakhouse with focus on steak and seafood
  - Features a rooftop bar with views of Nocatee landscape
  - Owned and managed by the same team behind Chophouse Thirteen in Mandarin
  - Expected to offer craft cocktails and dining experience similar to parent restaurant

### Stakeholder Quotes
**Michael O'Steen, Managing Director at The PARC Group** (Nocatee's master developer):
- "We are excited for these unique additions to our vibrant Town Center"

**Riley Skinner, Vice President of Skinner Brothers Realty**:
- "This next phase of retail will tie everything together to create the walkable Town Center we've been working hard to achieve over the past several years"
