User is implementing a habit stack that combines their existing 20-minute daily reading habit with a new 5-10 minute reflection writing habit.

**The Habit Stack:**
Anchor Habit: Reading for 20 minutes daily
New Habit: Writing a book review or reflection for 5-10 minutes
Formula: After finishing reading 20 minutes, write reflection in reading journal

**Reflection Formats Being Explored:**
- Free Writing: unstructured thoughts without editing
- Structured Reflection: using template (Introduction, Key Takeaways, Applications, Questions, Conclusion)
- Mind Mapping: visual map of concepts and connections
- Journaling: personal, introspective reflection
- Conversational: book club discussion style

**Reflection Questions:**
- What were the main takeaways?
- What resonated with me and why?
- What challenged my assumptions?
- How can I apply this to my life or work?
- What actions will I take?
- How does this relate to my goals?

**Implementation Approach:**
- Start with brief reflections (5-10 minutes)
- Use prompts or templates to guide writing
- Incorporate quotes from the book
- Try different perspectives
- Create Top 5 lists of insights
- Use digital tools like Goodreads or note-taking apps
