## Omega's authentication and certification services

### Services offered
- **Authentication** — verify genuine Omega timepiece, not replica or counterfeit
- **Certificate of Origin** — states production date, model, serial number to establish provenance
- **Condition Report** — assess watch condition, highlight restoration or repair needs
- **Service and Restoration** — skilled watchmakers service to original condition using original parts

### Certificate of Authenticity details
- **Unique Certificate Number** — used to verify watch authenticity
- **Watch Details** — includes serial number, model, production date, relevant information
- **Signature and Stamp** — authorized Omega representative signature and Omega stamp

### Benefits
- **Increased Value** — Omega-certified watches command higher prices due to guaranteed authenticity
- **Enhanced Credibility** — certification from manufacturer adds assurance for buyers
- **Improved Resale Value** — certification increases resale value
- **Peace of Mind** — assurance of professional service and authentication

### Process and cost
- Contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service
- Cost varies depending on model, age, and condition
- Timeline varies

### Tips
- Keep all documentation (certification, service records, correspondence)
- Store certification safely in secure location
- Consider additional documentation (appraisal, provenance report) to further enhance value
