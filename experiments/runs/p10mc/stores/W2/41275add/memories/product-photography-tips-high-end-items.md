## Lighting techniques

- **Natural light** — soft and flattering; position near window or shoot during golden hour
- **Softbox or diffused light** — softens harsh shadows, reduces hotspots
- **Avoid direct flash** — creates harsh shadows; use diffuser or bounce light if needed
- **Layered lighting** — combine softbox for overall illumination with sidelight for texture detail

## Composition

- **Simple backgrounds** — use plain neutral colors (white, gray, black) to isolate subject
- **Rule of thirds** — divide image into thirds both horizontally and vertically, place item along lines
- **Experiment with angles** — low angle, overhead, Dutch angle for visual interest
- **Show details** — close-up shots of specific features (watch face, guitar fretboard, comic cover)

## Editing in post-production

- **Color correction** — adjust temperature and saturation to accurately represent item
- **Noise reduction and sharpening** — minimize grain, enhance details (avoid over-sharpening)
- **Retouching** — remove dust, scratches, minor imperfections (avoid over-retouching)
- **Consistency** — establish cohesive look across all images for professional appearance

## General tips

- **Use tripod** — maintains steady camera, enables sharper images
- **Shoot in RAW** — more flexibility in editing, better noise reduction and color correction
- **Focus control** — ensure sharp focus on item; consider shallow depth of field to isolate subject
- **Lifestyle shots** — show item in use (watch on wrist, guitar being played) to help buyers imagine owning it
