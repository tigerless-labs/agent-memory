## Watch selling platforms

- **Chrono24** — specializes in luxury watches, large collector community
- **1stdibs** — high-end luxury marketplace, curated dealers
- **eBay** — use Authenticity Guarantee program, detailed photos
- **The RealReal** — consignment model for luxury watches
- **WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums** — specialized communities
- **Local watch dealers and collectors**
- **Auction houses** — Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions (commission-based)

## Comic book selling platforms

- **ComicConnect** — specializes in comic books, use CGC grading certification
- **eBay** — dedicated comics category
- **Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, Reddit r/comicbooks** — communities
- **Local comic book stores**

## Toy/action figure platforms

- **eBay** — large toy collector community, use Wata Games or PSA certification
- **Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk** — specialized forums
- **ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room** — toy marketplaces
- **Local toy and collectible stores**

## Guitar selling platforms

- **Reverb** — music instrument marketplace, large musician community
- **eBay** — use Vintage/Collectible categories
- **The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI** — guitar forums and communities
- **Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange** — specialized marketplaces
- **Facebook Marketplace** — local musicians and collectors

## Currency/numismatic platforms

- **Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG)** — for appraisers
- **International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)** — for appraisers
- General platforms: eBay, Facebook Marketplace for local sales
