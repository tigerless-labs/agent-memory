## Situation

**Date**: May 20, 2023
**Concern**: Noticed online shopping habits adding up; spending too much money
**Motivation**: Save money, reduce clutter, avoid impulse buying, develop mindful consumption

## Planned Strategies

1. **Expense Tracking**: Track expenses more closely with budgeting app or spreadsheet
2. **Budget Setting**: Set monthly/category budgets to stay within means
3. **One-in-One-Out Policy**: Get rid of an old item every time bringing a new one in
   - Forces intentionality about purchases
   - Maintains balanced level of possessions
   - Reduces clutter
4. **Cooling-off Period**: Implement waiting period before non-essential purchases
5. **Categorize Purchases**: Divide expenses by category with limits per category
6. **Find Alternatives**: Borrow from friends or find free alternatives before buying

## Observed Patterns

- Shops more on weekends (more free time)
- Tends to shop on Amazon and Best Buy
- Recognizes impulse buying tendency
