## Vinaigrette Options for Spiced Lentil Salads

**Basic Ratio:** 2 tbsp acid (vinegar or citrus) + 1 tbsp oil + 1 tsp flavor ingredients + salt & pepper

**1. Cilantro Lime Vinaigrette**
- Lime juice, olive oil, minced cilantro, salt, pinch of cumin
- Bright, citrusy flavor

**2. Ginger-Turmeric Vinaigrette**
- Apple cider vinegar, olive oil, grated ginger, turmeric powder, salt, black pepper
- Warm, spicy flavor

**3. Cumin-Coriander Vinaigrette**
- Red wine vinegar, olive oil, ground cumin, ground coriander, salt, cayenne pepper
- Smoky, earthy flavor

**4. Lemon-Garlic Vinaigrette**
- Lemon juice, olive oil, minced garlic, salt, black pepper
- Tangy, savory flavor

**Tips:**
- Use mild oil like olive or avocado oil
- Adjust acid amount to taste
- Don't over-dress; let flavors complement rather than overpower
