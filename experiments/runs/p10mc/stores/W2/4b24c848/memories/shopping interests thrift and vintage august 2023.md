1. **eBay** — vast selection of vintage, use keywords "vintage," "second-hand," "pre-owned"
2. **ThredUp** — women's and kids' clothing, consignment, dedicated vintage section
3. **Poshmark** — social commerce, gently used, search by hashtags
4. **Depop** — vintage clothing, accessories, collectibles
5. **Etsy** — handmade, vintage, unique items from independent sellers worldwide
6. **The RealReal** — luxury consignment, authenticated designer vintage
7. **ASOS Marketplace** — independent sellers listing vintage/second-hand

9. **Ruby Lane** — vintage and antique items, clothing, accessories
10. **Local Facebook groups** — buy/sell second-hand in community
