## Recommended Vaccination Schedule
- **Puppies**: Start at 6-8 weeks old
- **Initial series**: Boosters every 2-4 weeks until approximately 16 weeks
- **First booster after initial series**: At 1 year
- **Maintenance**: Every 3 years thereafter
- Individual schedules may vary based on age, health, lifestyle, and local disease outbreaks; consult veterinarian for specific dog

## Key Disease Information
- **Parvovirus and canine distemper**: Can survive in environment and contaminate surfaces, food/water bowls, clothing; can be transmitted indirectly into homes
- **Rabies**: Zoonotic disease (transmissible between animals and humans)

## Cost Considerations
- Prevention (vaccinations) is more cost-effective than treatment
- Single veterinary treatment visit for preventable disease can cost more than several years of vaccinations
- Some diseases like parvovirus require intensive, prolonged treatment
- Untreated preventable diseases can be fatal

## Why Indoor Dogs Still Need Vaccination
- Even indoor dogs can contact pathogens indirectly through owner's shoes/clothing, contaminated surfaces
- Puppies especially susceptible due to immature immune systems
- Natural immune system alone may not provide adequate protection against serious canine diseases
