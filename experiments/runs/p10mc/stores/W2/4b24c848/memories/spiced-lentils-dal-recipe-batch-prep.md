## Recipe: Spiced Lentils (Dal)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup brown or green lentils, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups water
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- Salt, to taste
- Optional: cayenne pepper or red chili flakes for heat

**Instructions:**
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil over medium heat
2. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until onion is translucent
3. Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, and garam masala; cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant
4. Add lentils and water; season with salt
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat to low, simmer 20-25 minutes until tender
6. Blend to desired consistency (partial or full blending)
7. Adjust seasoning; add cayenne or red chili flakes if desired

**Variations:**
- Add diced bell peppers or carrots
- Use red or yellow lentils for different texture
- Add coconut milk or yogurt for creaminess
- Spice with extra cayenne or red chili flakes

**Meal prep uses:** salads, wraps, soups, cakes, stuffed peppers, quesadillas, bolognese, fritters, curry, buddha bowls
