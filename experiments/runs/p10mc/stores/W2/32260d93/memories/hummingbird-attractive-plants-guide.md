## Recommended Plants for Hummingbirds

**Flower preferences:**
- Tubular/trumpet-shaped blooms
- Red, orange, and purple colors
- Nectar-rich varieties
- Multiple blooms for steady nectar supply
- Native plants for local populations

**Specific plants recommended:**
- Trumpet vine (Campsis radicans)
- Honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.)
- Fuchsia (Fuchsia spp.)
- Lantana (Lantana camara)
- Salvias (Salvia spp.)
- Bee balm (Monarda didyma)
- Cardinal flower (Lobelia cardinalis)
- Coral bells (Heuchera spp.)
- Coneflower (Echinacea spp.)
- Black-eyed susan (Rudbeckia hirta)
- Zinnias (Zinnia spp.)

**Strategy:** Plant varieties that bloom at different times for constant nectar availability throughout growing season. Choose native plants for your region.
