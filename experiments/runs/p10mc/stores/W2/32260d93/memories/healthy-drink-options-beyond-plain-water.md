## Healthy Drink Alternatives

User getting bored with plain water and wants more interesting drink options to pair with snacks.

### Recommended Healthy Drinks
1. **Infused Water**: Lemon, lime, cucumber, mint, or berries
2. **Herbal Teas**: Peppermint, chamomile, hibiscus (aid digestion, calm mind, antioxidants)
3. **Green Tea**: Rich in antioxidants; can boost metabolism; add lemon or honey
4. **Kombucha**: Fermented tea with probiotics; look for low-sugar options
5. **Coconut Water**: Natural electrolytes for hydration
6. **Non-Dairy Milk Alternatives**: Almond, soy, oat milk (protein + calcium)
7. **Cranberry Juice Spritzer**: Cranberry juice + sparkling water (antioxidants)
8. **Ginger Ale/Ginger Tea**: Anti-inflammatory properties; aids digestion; low-sugar ginger ale
9. **Vegetable Juice**: Cucumber, carrots, beets (nutrient-dense)
10. **Kefir**: Fermented milk with probiotics and protein

### Popular Pairings
- Apple slices with cinnamon + warm chamomile tea
- Carrot sticks with hummus + infused water (mint and lemon)
- Energy balls + cold coconut water
- Fresh berries with yogurt + green tea

User expressed excitement about trying **infused water flavors** and **herbal teas** specifically.
