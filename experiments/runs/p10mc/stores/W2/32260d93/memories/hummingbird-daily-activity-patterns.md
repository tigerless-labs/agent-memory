## Hummingbird Activity Patterns

**Morning (6:00 AM - 10:00 AM):** Peak activity, fueling up for the day and replenishing energy reserves, defending territories

**Midday (11:00 AM - 1:00 PM):** Activity lull when sun is high and temperatures warmest; birds rest, groom, or engage in social behaviors

**Afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM):** Increased activity as day cools; busy foraging for nectar and energy

**Evening (5:00 PM - 7:00 PM):** Social behavior increases, gathering at feeders and flower patches, courtship displays

**Feeding frequency:** Hummingbirds need to eat frequently to fuel high metabolism. They consume up to twice their body weight in nectar and insects daily (equivalent to human eating 100-200 pounds of food per day).

**Note:** Patterns vary by location, season, and food availability. Individual observations will vary.
