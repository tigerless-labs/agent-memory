## Game Rules & Mechanics

**Combat System:**
- When a quest involves combat, stop and indicate before proceeding
- Use a combat system to determine success or failure
- Losing combat results in losing items from inventory
- Losing combat can result in death
- Track HP during quests and indicate if HP needs to decrease
- The outcome is determined by the mechanics, not arbitrary narration

**Inventory System:**
- Items can be lost when defeated in combat
- Death is a possible consequence of losing combat

**Quest Structure:**
- Quests are presented with clear objectives and rewards
- Combat quests require mechanical resolution
