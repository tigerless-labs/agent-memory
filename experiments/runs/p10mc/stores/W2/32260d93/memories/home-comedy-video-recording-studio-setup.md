## Home Comedy Video Recording Studio Setup

The user is planning to record comedy performance videos for YouTube and needs guidance on setting up a home recording studio.

### Key Setup Recommendations

**Lighting:**
- Natural light near a window with soft, indirect light (avoid harsh shadows)
- Basic lighting kit with key light, fill light, and backlight (affordable options available online)
- Softbox lights ideal for even, soft lighting

**Audio:**
- USB microphone recommended: Blue Yeti or Rode NT-USB
- Position mic 6-8 inches from mouth, slightly off-center
- Use pop filter or DIY noise reduction (hoop with mesh screen)
- Monitor audio with headphones during recording

**Camera:**
- Smartphone camera or dedicated camera (DSLR/mirrorless)
- Look for good low-light performance and image stabilization
- Use a tripod for steady shots
- Frame options: medium shot (waist-up) or close-up (head and shoulders)

**Background:**
- Simple, neutral-colored background (white or gray wall)
- Avoid busy patterns or distracting colors
- Optional: add simple colored background or props for visual interest

**Recording Software:**
- Free: OBS Studio (PC) or QuickTime (Mac)
- Paid: Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve

**Recording Tips:**
- Practice before recording
- Aim for single-take recordings to maintain natural flow
- Use external hard drive for storage
- Monitor audio levels throughout
- Keep editing simple; avoid over-editing
- Add captions for accessibility
- Optimize for YouTube with relevant keywords and tags

### Recording Strategy
- Record set in one take for natural flow
- Monitor audio to catch issues during recording
- Use external storage to protect files
