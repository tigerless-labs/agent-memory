## Squirrel-Proofing Strategies

- Weight-activated perches that collapse or spin with heavier animals
- Cage-like enclosures allowing birds but excluding larger animals
- Squirrel-resistant plastic or metal materials
- Squirrel baffles as physical barriers above or below feeder
- Position feeder at least 10 feet away from jumping structures (trees, fences)

## Raccoon-Proofing Strategies

- Secure feeder pole at least 6 feet tall and anchored in ground
- Heavy-duty construction with secure lids and weight-activated perches
- Raccoon-specific baffles around pole or feeder to prevent climbing
- Move feeder to difficult-to-access area like courtyard, away from water sources
- Take feeder down or cover with baffle at sunset (raccoons most active at night)

## General Tips

- Keep area clean - regularly remove spilled seed and debris
- Use less appealing seed types: safflower seeds or hot pepper-infused seeds
- Visual deterrents: shiny reflective objects, aluminum foil, CDs near feeder
- Rotate feeder type or location periodically

**Note:** No method is 100% effective; patience and creativity needed.
