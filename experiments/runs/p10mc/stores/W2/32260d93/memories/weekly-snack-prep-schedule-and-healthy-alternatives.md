## Storage Tips for Freshness
- Airtight containers (glass jars, plastic, zip-top bags)
- Cool, dry place away from sunlight/heat
- Layer with parchment/wax paper
- Freeze for longer storage (energy balls, granola, nuts)
- Add silica gel or uncooked rice to absorb moisture (especially for trail mix to prevent sogginess)
- Label and date containers
- Rotate snacks regularly
- Monitor for staleness/mold regularly
