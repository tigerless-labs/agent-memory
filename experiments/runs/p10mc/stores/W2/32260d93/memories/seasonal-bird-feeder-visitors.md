## Winter Visitors (December - February)

- Winter finches: American Goldfinch, Common Redpoll, Purple Finch (Nyjer seed, suet)
- Sparrows: American Tree Sparrow, Dark-eyed Junco, White-throated Sparrow
- Woodpeckers: Downy, Hairy, Red-bellied (suet, sunflower seeds)
- Blue Jays: frequent during winter when natural food scarce

## Spring/Fall Migrants (March-May, August-November)

- Warblers: Yellow Warbler, Yellow-rumped Warbler, American Goldfinch
- Hummingbirds: Ruby-throated Hummingbird (nectar feeders)
- Orioles: Baltimore Orioles, Orchard Orioles (orange slices, jelly)

## Year-Round Residents

- Northern Cardinals (sunflower seeds, suet)
- Black-capped Chickadees, Carolina Chickadees (daily visitors - seeds, suet)
- Tufted Titmice, Bridled Titmice (sunflower seeds, peanuts)

**Note:** Patterns vary by region, climate, and local species. Research bird species common to your area and tailor feeder offerings accordingly.
