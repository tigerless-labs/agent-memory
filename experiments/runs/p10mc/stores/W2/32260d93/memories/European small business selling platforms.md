## European Small Business Selling Platforms

1. **BusinessesForSale.com** — UK-based online marketplace for buying and selling businesses; offers valuation, marketing, negotiation assistance
2. **Rightbiz** — UK-based online marketplace for buying and selling businesses; provides valuation, legal document preparation, marketing
3. **Enterprise Europe Network** — European Union initiative providing support to SMEs, including business brokerage and transfer services
4. **BusinessesBuySell** — European platform specializing in small and medium-sized businesses; provides valuation, marketing, negotiation assistance
5. **Companymarketplace.de** — German online marketplace for buying and selling businesses; offers valuation, legal document preparation, marketing

These platforms provide various services to support small business owners in Europe selling their businesses.
