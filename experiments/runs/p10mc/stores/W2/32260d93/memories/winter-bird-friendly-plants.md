## Berry-Producing Plants for Winter

- Holly (Ilex spp.) - eaten by robins, bluebirds, cedar waxwings
- Cotoneaster (Cotoneaster spp.) - robins, bluebirds, cedar waxwings
- Winterberry Holly (Ilex verticillata) - bright red berries for robins, cedar waxwings
- Crabapple (Malus spp.) - robins, bluebirds, woodpeckers

## Seed-Producing Plants for Winter

- Pine (Pinus spp.) - American Goldfinch, Pine Siskin, Common Redpoll
- Coneflower (Echinacea spp.) - American Goldfinch, Indigo Bunting, Downy Woodpecker
- Black-eyed Susan (Rudbeckia hirta) - American Goldfinch, Indigo Bunting, Dark-eyed Junco
- Sunflower (Helianthus annuus) - American Goldfinch, Cardinal, Blue Jay

## Nectar-Rich Plants for Winter

- Winter-flowering shrubs: Camellia, Mahonia, Gaultheria (attract hummingbirds like Anna's)
- Winter honeysuckle (Lonicera fragrantissima) - Ruby-throated Hummingbird and other species

## Planting Strategy

Choose plants native to your region and suitable for local climate. Mix different food sources (berries, seeds, nectar) to attract variety of bird species. Winter is challenging time for birds with scarce natural food sources.
