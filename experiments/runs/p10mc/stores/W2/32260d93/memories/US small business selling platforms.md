## US Small Business Selling Platforms

1. **BizBuySell** — online marketplace with business valuation, marketing, and negotiation assistance
2. **ExitAdviser** — platform for selling small businesses; provides valuation, legal document preparation, marketing
3. **Sunbelt Business Brokers** — national network of business brokers with offices across many US states
4. **Transworld Business Advisors** — business brokerage for buying/selling; offers valuation, marketing, negotiation assistance
5. **Empire Flippers** — online marketplace specializing in online businesses (e-commerce, software, content-based)

These platforms provide various services to support small business owners in selling their businesses.
