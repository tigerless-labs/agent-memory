## Contract Focus Areas

### Intellectual Property (IP) Clause Requirements
- Define IP ownership clearly (who owns work product)
- Specify IP license scope, duration, and territory
- Identify exclusions (pre-existing IP, proprietary methods/tools)
- Include warranties that work doesn't infringe third-party rights

### Confidentiality Clause Requirements
- Define what constitutes confidential information (business strategies, customer data, trade secrets)
- Specify obligations for maintaining confidentiality
- Identify exceptions (public domain info, legally required disclosures)
- Define duration of confidentiality obligations (may extend beyond contract term)
