## Catalan Literature and Music — Political Themes

### Authors and Works
- Mercè Rodoreda, Joanot Martorell, Josep Pla — early works on identity and nationalism
- Albert Sánchez Piñol, Quim Monzó — contemporary writers on political situation
- Javier Cercas — "The Impostor" explores identity and memory with nuanced perspective
- Joseph Piqué — "On The Line" on Catalan family struggles with national identity
- Fernando Aramburu — "Patria" offers pro- and anti-separatist perspectives (Basque setting, relevant context)

### Musicians
- Joan Manuel Serrat, Lluís Llach — promote Catalan culture and independence
- Marina Rossell — combines Catalan identity with themes of humanity and poetry
- Manolo García — supports Spanish unity

### Artist
- Francesc Torres — multimedia artist exploring history, memory, identity during Franco era

### Key Points
- Dominant narrative in mainstream Catalan literature/music supports independence
- Pro-Spanish and nuanced perspectives less prevalent but exist in independent outlets
- Artistic tradition emphasizes exploring difficult, complex issues
- Future representation of diverse viewpoints depends on political situation evolution
