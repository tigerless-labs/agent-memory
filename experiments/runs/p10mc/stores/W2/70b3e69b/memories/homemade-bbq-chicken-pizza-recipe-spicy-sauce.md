## BBQ Chicken Pizza with Spicy Twist

**Timeline**: May 20, 2023 — planning to make this pizza this weekend

**Recipe components:**
- Spicy BBQ sauce base (high-quality, not overly sweet; can add diced jalapeños or serrano peppers for extra kick)
- Grilled chicken: marinate in olive oil, lime juice, and favorite spices before grilling
- Red onion: thinly slice and caramelize before adding (brings out natural sweetness, balances heat)
- Fresh cilantro on top; can also mix chopped cilantro into the BBQ sauce for added flavor
- Optional additions: crumbled bacon, diced bell peppers for smokiness and crunch

**Preparation notes:**
- Pre-bake crust for 2-3 minutes before adding toppings
- Don't overload with toppings to keep crust crispy
- Balance toppings to ensure even cooking

## Dough experiments planned

**Flour comparison**: planning to test bread flour (12-14% protein) vs all-purpose flour (10-12% protein) — bread flour expected to produce crisper crust due to stronger gluten network

**Preferment (biga) plan**: 
- Considering using a biga preferment for more complex flavor and crispier crust
- Deciding between 24-hour biga (more complex flavor, deeper taste, better crust texture, but time-consuming) vs 12-hour biga (faster, easier to manage, still beneficial)
