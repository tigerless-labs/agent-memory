## Popular Pizza Topping Combinations

**Classic Favorites**

1. **Margherita**: Fresh tomatoes, mozzarella, basil, salt, olive oil - lets crispy crust shine
2. **Quattro Formaggi**: Parmesan, mozzarella, gorgonzola, ricotta - rich, creamy, savory
3. **Hawaiian**: Ham/Canadian bacon and pineapple - sweet and salty contrast

**Meat Lovers**

1. **Pepperoni and Sausage**: Classic combo with onions or bell peppers optional
2. **Meatball Madness**: Homemade meatballs, mozzarella, marinara - hearty and satisfying
3. **Bacon Cheeseburger**: Ground beef, crispy bacon, mozzarella, tangy BBQ sauce

**Veggie Delights**

1. **Roasted Veggie**: Artichoke hearts, roasted red peppers, olives, feta cheese
2. **Mushroom and Truffle**: Sauteed mushrooms, truffle oil, mozzarella - earthy, umami taste
3. **Pesto and Veggies**: Fresh pesto, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella - light and refreshing

**Gourmet Inspirations**

1. **Prosciutto and Arugula**: Thin prosciutto slices, arugula, shaved parmesan - salty, peppery
2. **Shrimp Scampi**: Shrimp, garlic, parsley, mozzarella - seafood twist
3. **Fig and Prosciutto**: Caramelized figs, prosciutto, arugula - sweet and savory

**Spicy Options**

1. **Jalapeno Popper**: Jalapenos, cream cheese, mozzarella - spicy kick
2. **Spicy Italian**: Pepperoni, banana peppers, red pepper flakes - bold, spicy
3. **Korean BBQ**: Marinated bulgogi beef, kimchi, green onions - spicy, savory, sweet

**Tips:**
- Pizza is highly customizable - experiment with different combinations
- Balance flavors and textures for best results
- Don't overload with toppings (keeps crust crispy)
