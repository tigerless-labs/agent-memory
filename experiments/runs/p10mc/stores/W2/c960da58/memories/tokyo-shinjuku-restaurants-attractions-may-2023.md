## Restaurants in Shinjuku

### Primary Recommendation
- **Yakiniku Jumbo Han** — yakiniku (Japanese BBQ), high-quality, popular, planned visit

### Also Researched
- T's Restaurant (tonkatsu and Japanese comfort food, Shinjuku-sanchome)
- Various ramen spots (Totto Ramen, Ichiran in Shibuya nearby)
- Tempura Tsunahachi (multiple locations)
- Shinjuku Oden Alley (street food, affordable)
- Golden Gai's Tachinomiya (standing bar)

## Attractions in Shinjuku

### Must-See
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building (free observation deck, Tochomae Station)
- Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden (beautiful in cherry blossom or autumn season)
- Shinjuku District (Shinjuku-dori, Kabukicho, Golden Gai)

### Hidden Gems
- Omoide Yokocho (tiny alleyway, small bars/restaurants, old Tokyo feel)
- Nonbei Yokocho (hidden alleyway, bars and restaurants)
- Shinjuku Ni-chome (LGBTQ+ district, vibrant atmosphere)
- Hanazono Shrine (serene Shinto shrine, peaceful escape)

### Nearby
- Tokyo Toy Museum (Iidabashi Station)
- Department store food floors (depachika)

## Visiting During Cherry Blossom Season (March)

### Crowd-Avoidance Strategy
- Visit weekdays instead of weekends
- Early morning visits (before 9 AM) for best experience
- Take lunchtime breaks (12-1 PM, fewer crowds)
- Weekday evenings less crowded than days

### Transit
- Purchase Suica or Pasmo card for convenient subway/transport
- Be prepared for crowded Shinjuku Station
