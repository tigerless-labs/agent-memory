Examples of businesses using AR to enhance customer experience:

1. Timberland - AR boot try-on: customers see how boots look on their feet before purchasing
2. Dulux - AR paint color app: visualize different paint colors on walls before painting
3. MoMA (Museum of Modern Art) - AR museum app: additional artwork information, background, audio guides
4. IKEA Place - AR furniture visualization: try furniture in home before purchase

These show AR's versatility across retail, home décor, and museum/cultural sectors. Small businesses can use AR creatively to differentiate from larger competitors and build customer loyalty.
