## 'Lost in the Woods' Short Story Project

**Original Challenge**
- Prompt: "Lost in the Woods"
- Completed 500-word challenge submission

**Expansion Goals**
- Deepen the story beyond the original submission
- Explore multi-layered themes of being lost (physical, emotional, mental)

**Central Concept**
- **Character struggle**: Finding identity and purpose after major life change
- **Symbolism**: Woods represent the character's inner journey and quest for self-discovery
- **Themes**: Resilience, self-acceptance, navigating uncertainty, personal growth

**Character Development**
- Backstory connects to a major life change (around February 10)
- Character feels adrift, uncertain about their path in life
- Seeking to understand identity and purpose
- Woods become a metaphor for internal turmoil and emotional complexity

**Brainstorming considerations**
- Character's motivations for entering the woods
- How journey triggers self-discovery and growth
- Use of symbolism (paths, clearings, light, water as emotional/spiritual elements)
- Potential encounters with other characters
- Character's evolution and what they learn

**Status**: Active development; drawing from personal experiences for authenticity
