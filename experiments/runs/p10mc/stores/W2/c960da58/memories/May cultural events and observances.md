## Key Events in May

1. **May Day / Labor Day (May 1)** — Celebration of working class and labor movements
2. **Cinco de Mayo (May 5)** — Commemorates Mexican Army's victory over French Empire at Battle of Puebla (1862)
3. **Mother's Day (Second Sunday)** — Celebration and honor of mothers and motherhood
4. **Memorial Day (Last Monday)** — U.S. federal holiday honoring military personnel who died in service
5. **Teacher Appreciation Week (First full week)** — Recognition of teachers' work and dedication
6. **National Nurses Week (May 6-12)** — Honors nurses' contributions and sacrifices
7. **National Police Week (May 15-21)** — Honors law enforcement officers who died in line of duty
8. **Asian American and Pacific Islander Heritage Month** — Month-long celebration of contributions and achievements
