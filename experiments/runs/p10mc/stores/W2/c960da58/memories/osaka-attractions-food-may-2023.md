## Must-See Attractions

- **Osaka Castle** — majestic, played key role in Japan unification, city views
- **Dotonbori** — entertainment district, neon lights, street food, unique attractions
- **Universal Studios Japan** — theme park with Harry Potter, Mario areas
- **Umeda Sky Building** — observation deck, good sunset views
- **Shitennoji Temple** — one of Japan's oldest (founded 593 AD), garden, treasure hall

## Hidden Gems

- **Osaka Museum of History** — showcases Osaka history and culture, rooftop garden
- **Sumo Spring Tournament** — if visiting in March, at Osaka Prefectural Gymnasium
- **Kuromon Ichiba Market** — lively marketplace, fresh seafood, local snacks and souvenirs
- **Spa World** — onsen (hot spring) theme park, various baths and saunas, relaxation
- **Amerikamura (American Village)** — trendy district, international restaurants, bars, shops

## Food and Cuisine

- **Takoyaki** — octopus dumplings
- **Okonomiyaki** — savory pancake
- **Kushikatsu** — deep-fried skewers

## March Tip
- Sumo Spring Tournament at Osaka Prefectural Gymnasium if visiting in March
