- **Equipment:** New turntable

## Beginner Tips Received

1. Start with albums you already love
2. Invest in good turntable, phono preamp, and speakers
3. Handle records by edges only, store upright in protective sleeves
4. Regular cleaning with soft cloth and gentle solution
5. Explore different genres and eras
6. Check condition when buying used records (look for scratches, warping)
7. Join vinyl communities (online forums, record store groups)
8. Be patient hunting for records
9. Attend record stores, swaps, Discogs, eBay
10. Enjoy the tactile experience, artwork, and nostalgia
