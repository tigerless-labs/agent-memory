## Cesar Chavez & Food Systems

**Who:** American labor leader and civil rights activist (Mexican and Filipino descent farmworker advocate)

**Main work:**
- Co-founded United Farm Workers (UFW) union
- Led strikes and boycotts (1960s-1970s)
- Fought for improved working conditions and wages for farmworkers

**Connection to food:**
- Not a cook/chef, but major advocate for healthy, sustainable food systems
- Believed everyone deserved access to fresh, nutritious food regardless of income
- Promoted organic and sustainable farming practices as part of labor advocacy
- Used consumer boycotts strategically: non-union grapes and lettuce became powerful labor movement symbols

**Philosophy:** "La Causa" (the cause) linked farmworker rights with fair labor practices in the entire food industry
