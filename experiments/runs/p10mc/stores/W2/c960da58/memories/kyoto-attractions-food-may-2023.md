## Must-See Attractions

- **Fushimi Inari Shrine** — thousands of vermilion torii gates forming tunnel up mountain
- **Kinkaku-ji Temple (Golden Pavilion)** — gold leaf covered, beautiful gardens
- **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** — serene, towering bamboo stalks
- **Kiyomizu-dera Temple** — UNESCO World Heritage, panoramic city view
- **Gion District** — traditional geisha quarter, Japanese architecture

## Hidden Gems

- **Philosopher's Path** — scenic walking route, cherry blossoms, temples
- **Nijo Castle** — UNESCO World Heritage, nightingale floors (alert guards to intruders)
- **Sanjusangendo Temple** — 1,001 life-size statues of Kannon (Buddhist goddess of mercy)
- **Kyoto Imperial Palace** — former Emperor residence, imperial history
- **Ginkaku-ji Temple (Silver Pavilion)** — peaceful, beautiful gardens, subtle beauty

## Food and Cuisine

- **Kaiseki** — multi-course traditional meals
- **Shojin-ryori** — Buddhist vegetarian cuisine
- **Yudofu** — boiled tofu dishes

## Best Times to Visit
- Early morning or late afternoon for fewer crowds
- Weekdays better than weekends
