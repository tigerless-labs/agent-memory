## Project Brief

**Target audience:** English-preferring Hispanic women

**Goal:** Help them express and share multifaceted cultural identity through food

**Timeline:** May (focused on May events)

**Key themes:**
- Fusion recipes blending different Latino cultures with each other and non-Latino cultures
- Time-saving tips and cooking hacks
- Countries of origin emphasis: Mexico, Puerto Rico, Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Peru, Chile, and others

**Content pillars for May:**
- Cinco de Mayo celebrations (May 5)
- Mother's Day brunch (second Sunday)
- Party hosting tips and hacks
- Fusion recipes (e.g., Mexican sushi, Peruvian quinoa risotto)
- Latin American breakfasts, summer salads, desserts
- Caribbean cocktails
- Time-saving 30-minute meals
