User wants to track their charitable activities in a centralized location to monitor donations and volunteer hours.

**Platforms recommended and to be evaluated:**
- **GivingGrid** — track donations, volunteer hours, fundraising; discover new charities
- **DonorPerfect** — donor management software; track donations, receipts, charitable activities; reminders and notifications
- **Network for Good** — donate to favorites, track donations, receive receipts and tax deductions
- **VolunteerMatch** — connect with non-profits; track volunteer hours, log activities, earn badges/rewards
- **Bright Funds** — donate through single platform, track donations, earn rewards and badges
- **Charity Navigator's Giving Basket** — track donations, set up recurring gifts, receive receipts and tax deductions

User plans to research these options to find the best fit for their needs.
