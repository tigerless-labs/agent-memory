User is committed to doing due diligence on charities before donating or participating in their events.

**Research methods and resources:**
1. Check charity website for mission, goals, programs, financials, leadership transparency
2. Use charity rating sites:
   - Charity Navigator (charitynavigator.org)
   - GuideStar (guidestar.org)
   - BBB Wise Giving Alliance (give.org)
   - GreatNonprofits (greatnonprofits.org)
   - Charity Watch (charitywatch.org)
3. Verify 501(c)(3) status through IRS website (irs.gov)
4. Review financial reports including Form 990
5. Research programs, services, goals, and intended impact
6. Examine governance, leadership, board members, staff
7. Check online reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)
8. Contact charity directly with questions
9. Check for accreditations (BBB Wise Giving Alliance, Independent Charities of America)
10. Trust instincts if something feels off

User plans to apply this process to organizations they've already participated in (Walk for Cancer event from May 15, 2023).
