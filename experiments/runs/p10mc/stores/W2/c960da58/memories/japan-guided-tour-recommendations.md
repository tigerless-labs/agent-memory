## Major Tour Companies

### Group Tour Companies
- Intrepid Travel (comprehensive, structured)
- G Adventures (reputable, group experience)

### Customized Tour Companies
- Inside Japan Tours (flexibility, experienced guides)
- Japan Unmasked (bespoke, cultural immersion)
- Rising Sun Tours (customized, history and culture focus)

### Alternative Options
- Local Japanese tour operators
- Private guides (highest flexibility)
- Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Read reviews from multiple sources
- Check sustainability practices
- Industry association membership (JATA)
- Look for built-in flexibility and free time
- Group size and guide ratio
- Communication clarity on needs and expectations

## Pros and Cons

### Group Tours
Pros: structured guides, convenient booking, social, pre-arranged activities

Cons: limited flexibility, fixed itinerary, potentially expensive

### Private Tours/Guides
Pros: complete control, personalized experience

Cons: higher cost, requires more planning
