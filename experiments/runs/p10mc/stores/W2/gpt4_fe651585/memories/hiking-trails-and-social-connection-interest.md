## Hiking Interest & Trail Discovery

- Looking for new hiking trails in local area

- Interested in connecting with other hikers and outdoor enthusiasts

## AllTrails Features

**Difficulty filters:**
- Easy
- Easy to Moderate
- Moderate
- Moderate to Difficult
- Difficult

**Distance filters:**
- Under 1 mile
- 1-3 miles
- 3-5 miles
- 5-10 miles
- 10+ miles

**Other filters available:** elevation gain, user ratings, features (waterfalls, scenic views, lakes), trail type (out and back, loop, point to point), accessibility (wheelchair accessible, dog-friendly)
