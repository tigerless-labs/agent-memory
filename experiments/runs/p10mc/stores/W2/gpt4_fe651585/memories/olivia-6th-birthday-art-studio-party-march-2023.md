## Party Theme
Art Studio Party for Olivia's 6th birthday (party date approaching as of March 17, 2023)

## Art Stations Planned
1. **Paint Station** — Abstract Art with Sponges and Paint
   - Materials: paper, paint, sponges, paintbrushes
   - Kids create textured art by stamping sponges

2. **Marker Station** — Rainbow Drawing
   - Materials: paper, markers, colored pencils
   - Vibrant multi-colored drawings

3. **Glue Station** — Collage Making
   - Materials: paper, glue, scissors, old magazines, fabric scraps
   - Kids create collages from cut shapes

4. **Paper Station** — Paper Folding and Sculpture
   - Materials: paper, scissors, glue, tape
   - 3D sculptures from folded paper

## Display Ideas for Kids' Artwork
- Art gallery with clipboards/easels
- Art walk on clothesline or wall
- Collaborative mural project
- Take-home art book (binding individual artwork)

## Party Favors
- Art supplies (crayons, markers, paint sets)
- Art-themed stickers or coloring books

## Party Logistics Notes
- Provide smocks/aprons for kids
- Plan art show at end of party for kids to showcase work
- Encourage experimentation and messy creativity
