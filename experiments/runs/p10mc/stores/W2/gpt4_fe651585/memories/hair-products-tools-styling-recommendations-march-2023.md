## Hair Styling Products and Tools

**Styling products (recommended March 2023):**

*Texture and volume:*
- Moroccanoil Dry Shampoo
- Bumble and bumble Hairdresser's Invisible Oil Dry Shampoo
- Sea salt sprays: Bumble and bumble Surf Spray, Oribe Texturizing Spray
- Volumizing mousse (already using, recommended by stylist)

*Hold and definition:*
- Oribe Matte Waves Texture Lotion
- Moroccanoil Moisture Repair Styling Cream
- Styling creams for lightweight hold

*Nourishment and shine:*
- Moroccanoil Argan Oil
- Kerastase Discipline Fluidissime
- Hair serums for smoothing and adding shine

**Styling tools:**

*For waves and curls:*
- Beachwaver S1 (curling wand)
- T3 Whirl Trio (creates beachy waves, tight curls, or loose waves)
- Diffuser attachment for blow dryer

*For sleek looks:*
- Dyson Corrale (flat iron)
- GHD Platinum+ (flat iron)

*Drying and brushing:*
- Dyson Supersonic (hair dryer)
- T3 Featherweight Luxe 2i (hair dryer with multiple heat settings)
- Round brushes: T3 Featherweight Luxe 2i, Moroccanoil Moisture Repair Round Brush
- Paddle brush for detangling and volume

**Best practices:**
- Always use heat protectant spray/cream before using heat tools
- Experiment with different temperatures
- Limit heat styling to 1-2 times per week to prevent damage
- Use round brush for smooth, sleek hair
- Use paddle brush for detangling and adding volume
