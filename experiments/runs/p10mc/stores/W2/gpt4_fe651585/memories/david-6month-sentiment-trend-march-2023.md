## David's 6-Month Sentiment Responses — "How do you feel about work?"

**Month 1:** Feeling good, excited about projects, making progress.

**Month 2:** Overwhelmed, lots on plate, having trouble keeping up.

**Month 3:** Things going well, handling stress better, getting into better rhythm.

**Month 4:** Feeling disconnected from work, not as engaged as before.

**Month 5:** Positive, making progress on long-term goals, contributing meaningfully to team.

**Month 6:** Burnt out, needs time off to recharge, but still satisfied with job overall.

**Trend Analysis:**
Started positive and enthusiastic in Month 1, with some fluctuation through the middle months. Overall impression: slight decline in satisfaction by Month 6. Despite positivity spikes (Months 3, 5), underlying burnout and disconnection issues appear by end of period. Recommend investigating specific factors causing the decline and addressing burnout concerns.
