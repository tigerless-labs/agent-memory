## Recommended Yoga Brands

**Lululemon**
- High-quality, sweat-wicking fabrics
- Four-way stretch
- Recommended products: Align Pants, Wunder Under Pants
- Offers: "We Made Too Much" section (up to 50% off), Sale section (up to 30% off)
- Email signup: 15% off first purchase

**Athleta**
- Focus on comfort, flexibility, and style
- Wide range of yoga pants and tops
- Recommended products: Salutation Pants, Elation Tops
- Semi-annual sale: January and July, typically 20-50% off
- Email signup: 20% off first purchase

**Prana**
- Sustainable and eco-friendly brand
- Comfortable, breathable, affordable yoga wear
- Recommended products: Halle Pants, Jamie Top
- See: prana-sustainable-practices

**Nike**
- Dri-FIT moisture-wicking technology
- Recommended products: Pro Bra, Yoga Pants
- Modern, technical approach to yoga wear

**Beyond Yoga**
- Comfortable, soft, stretchy yoga wear
- Sustainability focus
- Recommended products: Spacedye Leggings, Cozy Top
