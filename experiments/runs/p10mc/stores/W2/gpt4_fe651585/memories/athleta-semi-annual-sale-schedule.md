## Athleta Semi-Annual Sale

**Timing:**
- **January Sale**: Usually starts early January, lasts 2-3 weeks
- **July Sale**: Typically begins early July, lasts 2-3 weeks

- Up to 20% off: newer styles, best-sellers, popular items
- 20-30% off: seasonal items (winter/summer collections)

- Special promo codes or bundle deals (sometimes stackable on sale prices)

- Sign up for Athleta email list: exclusive promo codes and early access

- Use price comparison tools: CamelCamelCamel, Keepa track price history
