## Transposing in Reason
- Can transpose entire song using Pitch Shifter effect on master channel
- Can use transpose function in sequencer
- Can use pitch shifter on individual tracks/instruments
- Can use Transpose tool in sequencer to shift pitch of notes in MIDI clip

## Pitch Shifter Location in Reason
- Located in Pitch Shift module
- Found in Pitch section of Combinator device
- Can shift pitch up or down by semitones
- Also available in RV7000 mk2 module
- Can be used on any audio signal (vocals, instruments)
