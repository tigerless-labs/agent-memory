## Prana's Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Practices

**Sustainable Materials:**
- Organic cotton: reduces toxic pesticides and synthetic fertilizers, promotes healthier soil, air, water
- Recycled polyester and nylon: reduces waste and need for virgin materials
- Hemp: highly durable, sustainable crop requiring minimal water and pesticides
- Tencel: sustainable fabric from wood pulp cellulose with low environmental impact

**Responsible Manufacturing:**
- Fair labor practices: safe working conditions and fair wages throughout supply chain
- Carbon offset: invests in renewable energy projects to offset manufacturing emissions
- Water conservation: implements water-conserving measures in manufacturing, reduces wastewater

**Supply Chain Transparency:**
- Supply chain mapping: ensures transparency and accountability
- Third-party audits: regular audits for compliance with sustainability standards

**Design & Production:**
- Design for recyclability: uses materials that can be easily recycled or upcycled
- Zero-waste design: minimizes waste using digital printing and other techniques
- Repair and repurpose: encourages customers to repair and repurpose products

**Giving Back:**
- Environmental partnerships: partners with Conservation Alliance for conservation efforts
- Community development: supports Fair Trade USA program for fair labor and community development
