## Hair Type and Face Shape

**Hair characteristics:**
- Round face shape
- Medium-thick hair
- Wavy and slightly curly texture
- Recently had haircut and color (February 2023)
- Feeling more confident about trying new hairstyles after recent cut/color

**Current products (recommended by stylist, using regularly):**
- Sea salt spray
- Volumizing mousse
- Both making a noticeable difference in how hair looks and feels

**Recommended hairstyles for round face + wavy-curly hair:**
- Long, loose waves (adds movement, creates slimming effect)
- Asymmetrical bob (chin-length or longer, creates illusion of length/angles)
- High ponytail (elongates face, draws attention upward)
- Updo with volume at crown (creates illusion of height and length)

**Styling approach:**
- Embrace natural texture, don't hide waves/curls
- Use products that enhance natural texture (sea salt sprays, curl activators, styling creams)
- Avoid heavy greasy products that weigh hair down
- Get regular trims to maintain healthy bouncy curls
- Use heat protectant before styling tools
- Limit heat styling to 1-2 times per week

**Hair inspiration influencers:**
- Hailey Baldwin (loose effortless waves, beachy vibes)
- Chrissy Teigen (volume at crown, asymmetrical angles)
- Camila Coelho (bouncy curly hair)
