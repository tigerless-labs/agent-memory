## Composition Goals
- Genre blend: orchestral pop + adult contemporary + EDM context
- Production software: Reason
- Inspiration: Sia's "California Dreamin'" cover
- Desired characteristic: dynamic, constantly evolving compositions

## Recommended Song Structure Template
- **Intro**: simple melody (orchestral instrument or synth) building anticipation
- **Verse**: complex melody, focus on vocals, simple beat/rhythm
- **Pre-chorus**: main chord progression, momentum building toward chorus
- **Chorus**: energetic beat, catchy memorable melody
- **Bridge**: contrasting section with different chord progression/melody
- **Outro**: final restatement of main melody, closing the song

## Instrumentation Approach
- Live instruments: piano, guitar, drums
- Orchestral instruments: strings, horns, woodwinds
- Electronic elements: synthesizers, samplers, processors
- Production details: panning, EQ, compression, reverb for cohesive sound

## Production Techniques
- Experiment with different chord progressions
- Focus on vocal production: EQ, compression, reverb
- Listen to other artists in adult contemporary and orchestral pop for inspiration
