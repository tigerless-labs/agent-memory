## Yoga & Athletic Wear Interests

**Background:**
- Getting back into yoga (March 2023 conversation)
- Loves finding deals and discounts
- Wants to upgrade workout wardrobe
- Prioritizes comfort, breathability, and range of motion
- Interested in sustainable and eco-friendly practices

**Yoga Wear Preferences:**
- Comfortable and breathable fabrics
- Full range of motion
- Four-way stretch materials preferred
- Interest in moisture-wicking technology
- Flat seams to reduce chafing
- High-waisted designs for coverage and support

**Yoga Wear Styles:**
- Leggings (popular choice, provides compression)
- Capris (shorter option)
- Tops with built-in support or separate sports bra
- Jackets/kimonos for layering

**Care & Maintenance:**
- Wash yoga wear in cold water
- Avoid fabric softener
