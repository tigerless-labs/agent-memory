## Plan to Advance Workplace DEI

User plans to suggest steps to their HR department to combat systemic racism and discrimination in the workplace (March 2023).

### Recommended Steps to Propose
1. Hire a diverse workforce with inclusive hiring processes
2. Provide cultural competency training
3. Develop and enforce anti-discrimination policies with clear consequences
4. Conduct regular diversity and inclusion assessments
5. Encourage open communication without fear of retaliation
6. Offer mentorship and sponsorship programs for underrepresented employees
7. Hold leadership accountable for equity and inclusion goals
8. Be transparent and report regularly on DEI progress

### Tools and Resources to Explore
- DEI consultants: professionals who develop and implement DEI strategies
- Online courses: cultural competency, diversity, and inclusion topics (many free or low-cost)
- DEI toolkits: online resources for implementing workplace initiatives
- Surveys and assessments: measure current diversity, equity, and inclusion levels
- Employee resource groups (ERGs): create community and support for underrepresented employees
- Diversity and inclusion training programs: tailored to organization needs

### Individual Employee Contributions
User is committed to personal actions:
1. Educate self on experiences of people from different backgrounds
2. Speak up respectfully against discrimination, harassment, and bias
3. Listen to colleagues' perspectives on diversity and inclusion
4. Remain open-minded to different viewpoints
5. Use inclusive, culturally-sensitive language
6. Support and participate in company DEI initiatives and events
7. Foster supportive atmosphere through ERGs, events, and respectful interactions

User acknowledges workplace culture change is challenging but believes positive impact is possible through proactive implementation.
