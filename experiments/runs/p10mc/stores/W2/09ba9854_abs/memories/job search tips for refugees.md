## Key Strategies Discussed

### Resume and Online Presence
- Tailor resume to US job market with transferable skills
- Create professional LinkedIn profile
- Connect with industry contacts
- Get professional resume review

### Leverage Your Strengths
- Highlight language skills for translation/interpretation roles
- Gain US work experience through entry-level positions
- Use internships/volunteering to build references and network
- Build professional reputation

### Networking Approaches
- Attend job fairs and industry events
- Join professional associations in your field
- Conduct informational interviews via LinkedIn
- Build relationships with mentors

### Companies Known to Hire Refugees
- Chobani
- Starbucks
- Check Idealist, LinkedIn, Indeed for refugee-welcoming postings

### Interview Success
- Research company and position thoroughly before interview
- Practice answering common interview questions
- Dress professionally
- Prepare to discuss skills experience and goals clearly

### Understand the US Market
- Research job market and industry trends in target field
- Learn about salary negotiation expectations
- Understand US job application processes
- Study field-specific requirements

### Mindset
- Job search takes time, be persistent
- Celebrate small wins along the way
- Seek support from mentors and professionals
- Stay positive and motivated
