## Problem
- Exceeding daily time limits on Twitter
- Spending 1+ hour per day scrolling news and trends
- Struggling to maintain boundaries despite setting limits

## Recommended Strategies

1. **Schedule Twitter sessions** — allocate specific times (e.g., lunch break, after completing tasks) rather than scrolling throughout the day

2. **Use Twitter lists** — create lists for different topics/interests to focus on specific content and reduce mindless scrolling

3. **Implement 2-minute rule** — when catching yourself scrolling, take 2-minute break to assess if genuinely interested or can move on

4. **Remove from home screen** — move Twitter app to a folder or remove entirely to reduce temptation for habitual checking

5. **Find alternative news sources** — explore newsletters, podcasts, or online publications to reduce reliance on Twitter for news

## Context
- User is actively following news and trends closely
- Difficulty with impulse control on the platform
- Other social media platforms have better adherence to time limits
