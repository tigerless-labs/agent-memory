## Modern Finnish Architecture Incorporating Traditional Materials

### Helsinki Central Library Oodi
- Opened 2018
- Designed by Ala Architects
- Exterior made of Finnish spruce wood
- Green roof blending with surrounding parkland
- Enhanced energy efficiency and environmental sustainability

### Kesäpuisto Chapel
- Located in Kärsämäki, Northern Ostrobothnia
- Designed by OOPEAA Architects
- Distinctive wooden cloak encircling concrete core
- Asymmetrical shape
- Continues centuries-old wooden construction tradition

### Helsinki University Think Corner
- Designed by JKMM Architects
- Academic study space, library, and cafe
- Wood panel exterior
- Central atrium bringing natural light
- Green roof and geothermal heating system
- Prioritizes sustainability and energy efficiency

### Metsá Pavilion
- Built for Tokyo Olympics 2020
- Designed by Helin and co Architects
- Unique wooden structure made of Kerto LVL laminated veneer lumber
- Focus on sustainability and energy efficiency
- Uses environmentally friendly materials

## Context
Modern Finnish architects continue to explore innovative ways to incorporate traditional materials into contemporary designs, emphasizing sustainability and environmental responsibility.
