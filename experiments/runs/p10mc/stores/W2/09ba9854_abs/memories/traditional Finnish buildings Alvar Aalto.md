## Notable Traditional Finnish Buildings

### Suomenlinna Sea Fortress
- UNESCO World Heritage site
- Off the coast of Helsinki
- Mid-18th century
- Wooden structures for housing and military purposes

### Paimio Sanatorium
- Designed by Alvar Aalto
- Early 20th century
- Curved wooden facade
- Reflects organic architecture approach

### Church of the Three Crosses
- 1958, Imatra
- Designed by Alvar Aalto
- Wooden walls and ceilings
- Creates intimate, peaceful atmosphere

### Finnish Pavilion at Paris World Expo
- 1937
- Designed by Alvar Aalto
- Showcased Finnish design and wood usage

## Architect
Alvar Aalto: influential Finnish architect integrating wood and organic design
