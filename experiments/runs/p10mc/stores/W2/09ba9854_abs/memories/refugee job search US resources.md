## User Situation
Refugee in the US seeking employment. Needs help with job search strategy, resume review, and skill development.

## Primary Resources

### Refugee Career Hub
- Website: www.refugeecareerhub.org
- Free platform connecting refugees with jobs, career coaching, skill-building
- Registration: Sign up, select "Refugee" type, verify email, complete profile
- Job opportunities: Entry-level, skilled positions, internships, remote work
- Example jobs: Customer service (Amazon), warehouse work (UPS), software developer, medical interpreter
- Services: Career coaching, skill-building, networking events, job readiness training

### Upwardly Global
- Website: www.upwardlyglobal.org
- Nonprofit providing job search assistance to refugees and immigrants
- Services: Resume/cover letter review, interview prep, job matching, career coaching, online courses, networking events, job fairs
- English training: Language improvement and accent reduction available
- Eligibility: Authorized to work in US, high school diploma or equivalent, actively seeking work

### Online Certifications (via Upwardly Global)
- IT: CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+; Cisco CCNA/CCNP; Microsoft Office Specialist
- Healthcare: Medical billing/coding, Medical Assistant, Phlebotomy Technician
- Finance: Bookkeeping, QuickBooks, Financial Analysis
- Business: Project Management Professional, Lean Six Sigma
- Digital Marketing: Google Analytics, Facebook Blueprint
- Duration: Self-paced, typically 1-6 months
- Cost: Financial assistance and scholarships available

### Other Organizations
- IRC (International Rescue Committee): Job readiness, resume, placement
- Catholic Charities: Job placement and career counseling
- Lutheran Social Services: Job training and placement
- SCORE/MicroMentor: Free career coaching and resume review
