## Major Classical Guitar Composers

### Foundational Figures
- **Johann Sebastian Bach** — keyboard compositions with many pieces for lute now performed on classical guitar
- **Fernando Sor** — Spanish guitarist and composer, early 19th century, important figure in classical guitar development
- **Francisco Tárrega** — Spanish composer and guitarist, considered one of the most important figures in classical guitar history; famous works: "Recuerdos de la Alhambra" and "Capricho Árabe"
- **Mauro Giuliani** — Italian composer and guitarist, prolific composer of sonatas, concertos, and études; collaborated with violinist Niccolò Paganini
- **Heitor Villa-Lobos** — Brazilian composer, known for innovative use of Brazilian rhythms and folk melodies; wrote études widely used as teaching material

### Modern Composers
- **Leo Brouwer** — contemporary composer incorporating various musical traditions
- **Roland Dyens** — contemporary composer
- **Carlo Domeniconi** — contemporary composer creating unique diverse sounds from different musical traditions
