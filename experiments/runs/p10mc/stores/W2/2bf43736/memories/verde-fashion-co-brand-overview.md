## Brand Overview

**Verde Fashion Co.** is a sustainable fashion brand founded by the user (ruihanlee1030@gmail.com).

### Timeline
- Website launched: May 2023 (a few weeks before this conversation)
- First email subscribers: acquired during launch month

### Brand Origin Story
The founder discovered a handmade dress in her grandmother's attic that sparked the brand idea. The dress was high-quality, impeccably stitched, and timeless. This inspired her to create an alternative to fast fashion's throwaway culture.

### Sustainability Commitment
- **Materials**: Uses organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel, and other eco-friendly fabrics
- **Production**: Implements pattern-making optimization and fabric scraps recycling to minimize waste
- **Philosophy**: Emphasizes slow fashion, quality over quantity, durability, and timeless design

### Current Marketing Focus
1. Email marketing: 4-email welcome series for new subscribers
2. Social media: Instagram (visual/sustainability content) and Facebook (long-form/blog content)
