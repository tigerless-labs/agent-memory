## Desk Lamp Glare Issue

**Problem:**
- Desk lamp causing glare on computer screen
- Light positioned directly in line of sight or reflecting off screen

**Solution Plan:**
- Move desk lamp to the other side of the desk
- Adjust lamp's arm and shade to direct light indirectly onto work surface
- If glare persists: consider glare-reducing screen protector or monitor brightness/contrast adjustment

**Status:**
- Planning to implement this change
