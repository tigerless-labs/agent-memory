## Adolphe Tanqueray's Spiritual Life — Second Part, Book 1

The second part, Book 1 of Tanqueray's Spiritual Life treatise focuses on the purgative way and the early stages of spiritual development.

**Structure:**
- Chapter 2: "The Purgative Way" — introduces the first stage of spiritual life
- Chapter 3: "The Active Purgative Way" — deeper exploration of active detachment
- Chapter 4: "Vocal Prayer and Meditation" — prayer practices for beginners

**Key themes:**
- Purification of the soul from sin and worldly attachments
- Role of cardinal virtues (prudence, justice, temperance, fortitude)
- Importance of prayer, sacraments (confession, Eucharist), and spiritual reading
- Guidance from spiritual directors
- The journey of spiritual beginners

**References drawn from:** St. John of the Cross, St. Teresa of Avila
