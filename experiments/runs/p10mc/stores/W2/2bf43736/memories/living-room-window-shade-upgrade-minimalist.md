## Living Room Window Shade Project

**Goal:**
- Upgrade to minimalist window shades
- Allow more natural light into living room
- Maintain privacy

**Options Considered:**
1. **Roller Shades** — sleek, simple, various light-filtering fabrics
2. **Solar Shades** — block UV/heat while allowing light, various opacity levels
3. **Cellular/Honeycomb Shades** — air-trapping design, insulating, light-filtering options
4. **Plantation Shutters** — clean, simple look, allow light filtering
5. **Sliding Panels** — modern, minimalist, large adjustable panels

**Recommended Brands:**
- Hunter Douglas
- Levolor
- Bali
- Graber
- Somfy

**Selection Criteria:**
- Opacity level (light-filtering vs. blackout)
- Material (fabric, vinyl, aluminum, wood)
- Operating system (manual vs. motorized)
- Color/pattern compatibility with room décor
- Professional measurement and installation recommended
