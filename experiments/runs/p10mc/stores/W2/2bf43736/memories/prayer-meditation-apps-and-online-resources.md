## Prayer and Meditation Apps

### Meditation/Prayer Apps
1. **Pray As You Go** - Daily prayer app with guided meditations, Scripture readings, reflections
2. **Headspace** - Meditation app with Christian-themed meditations for stress, anxiety, sleep
3. **Calm** - Meditation app with Christian-focused section, sleep stories, relaxing music
4. **Abide** - Christian meditation app with guided meditations, Scripture readings, prayers
5. **Hallow** - Catholic prayer app with guided meditations, prayers, reflections; focus on Ignatian spirituality

## Online Prayer Resources
1. **The Upper Room** - Website offering daily devotions, prayers, meditations, prayer wall for sharing requests
2. **Prayercast** - Guided prayers, Scripture readings, reflections with focus on global prayer needs
3. **The Gospel Coalition** - Daily devotionals, prayers, meditations with Reformed theology focus
4. **Christian Meditation** - Guided meditations, Scripture readings, reflections on Christian spirituality
5. **Sacred Space** - Guided meditations, prayers, reflections with Ignatian spirituality focus

## Online Communities and Courses
1. **The Bible App** - Online Bible studies, devotionals, courses with community engagement
2. **RightNow Media** - Online Bible studies, courses, devotionals focused on Christian living and discipleship
3. **Church Online** - Online church services, Bible studies, courses with community focus
4. **Seedbed** - Online courses, devotionals, resources with Wesleyan theology and spiritual formation focus
5. **Renovaré** - Online courses, devotionals, resources focused on spiritual formation and Christian living
