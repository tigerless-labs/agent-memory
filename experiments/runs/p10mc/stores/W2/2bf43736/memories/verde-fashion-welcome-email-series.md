## Welcome Email Series

### Email 1: Introduction and Brand Story
**Timing**: Sent immediately after subscription

**Subject**: Welcome to Verde Fashion Co.!

**Content**:
- Brief introduction to Verde Fashion Co. and mission to make sustainable fashion accessible and stylish
- Personal story about why the founder started the brand and passion for sustainability
- Warm, personal message expressing excitement to welcome new subscribers
- CTA: Explore website and social media channels

### Email 2: Our Sustainable Approach
**Timing**: Sent 3-4 days after Email 1

**Subject**: Our Sustainable Approach to Fashion

**Content**:
- Highlight sustainable practices (eco-friendly materials, waste reduction, slow fashion)
- Commitment to supply chain transparency and accountability
- Introduce sustainable fabric options: organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel
- CTA: Learn more about sustainable materials and practices on website

### Email 3: Featured Products and Collections
**Timing**: Sent 7-10 days after Email 2

**Subject**: Explore Our Sustainable Fashion Collections

**Content**:
- Showcase 2-3 best-selling or newest products with sustainable features highlighted
- Introduce product categories (tops, dresses, outerwear, etc.)
- Include customer testimonials or reviews for social proof
- CTA: Encourage shopping and exploration

### Email 4: Exclusive Offer and Community Invitation
**Timing**: Sent 10-14 days after Email 3

**Subject**: Join Our Sustainable Fashion Community

**Content**:
- Exclusive discount: 15% off first purchase
- Invitation to join social media communities (behind-the-scenes content, tips, exclusive promotions)
- Sneak peek into upcoming products, collections, or collaborations
- CTA: Shop with exclusive discount and follow social media channels
