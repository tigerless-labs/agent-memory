## Social Media Strategy

### Platform Choice
Verde Fashion Co. will prioritize **Instagram** and **Facebook** as primary social media channels.

### Instagram Strategy
**Content focus**: Visual-centric, sustainability series

**Key tactics**:
- High-quality, well-lit photos and videos with consistent aesthetic
- Behind-the-scenes (BTS) content: design process, material sourcing, production
- Customer showcase: encourage customer photos, re-share with incentives
- Hashtag research: mix of popular and niche hashtags for reach
- Engaging captions: tell stories, ask questions, provide value
- Stories and IGTV for sneak peeks and exclusive deals
- Active engagement: respond to comments and DMs, use polls and quizzes
- Influencer collaborations aligned with brand values
- Consistent posting schedule

### Facebook Strategy
**Content focus**: Long-form, educational, community-building

**Key tactics**:
- Blog posts and in-depth content
- Targeted ads to reach specific demographics and interests
- Facebook Groups or community building for discussions
- Promotions, events, and new product launches
- Facebook Live for real-time connection and Q&A
- Mobile-optimized content
- Analytics tracking via Facebook Insights

### Cross-Platform Strategy
- Share Instagram content on Facebook for broader reach
- Use Facebook to drive traffic to Instagram profile
- Facebook-exclusive content to encourage Instagram visits

### General Best Practices
- Track analytics regularly and adjust strategy accordingly
- Prioritize consistency and engagement over volume
- Focus on authentic storytelling and community connection
