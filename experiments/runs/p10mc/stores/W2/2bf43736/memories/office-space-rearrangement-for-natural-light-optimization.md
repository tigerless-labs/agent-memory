## Office Space Rearrangement Project

**Goal:**
- Optimize office layout for maximum natural light
- Create productive workspace with better lighting

**Key Strategies:**

1. **Identify Light Source** — determine direction and intensity of natural light
2. **Desk Placement** — position near window (not directly in front) to reduce glare/reflections on screen
3. **Desk Orientation** — angle desk so light comes from side or at angle for soft, indirect light
4. **Clear Obstructions** — keep area in front of windows clear of furniture/plants/shelving
5. **Use Mirrors** — strategically place opposite windows to reflect and bounce light deeper into room
6. **Light Color Scheme** — use light colors on walls, floors, furniture to reflect and amplify natural light
7. **Rethink Furniture** — consider circular or U-shaped desk layout to reduce light-blocking furniture
8. **Window Treatments** — hang sheer curtains/blinds to filter harsh direct sunlight and reduce glare
9. **Task-Oriented Zones** — create different zones (reading nook, collaboration area) for different light levels
10. **Experiment and Adjust** — observe how natural light changes throughout day and make ongoing adjustments

**Bonus:**
- Use plants of varying heights/textures for visual interest and air purification
