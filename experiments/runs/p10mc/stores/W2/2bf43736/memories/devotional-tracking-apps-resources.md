## Tracking Apps for Daily Devotionals

### Recommended Apps

1. **YouVersion** - Popular Bible app with variety of devotionals including Lenten options; set reminders, track progress, share reflections with friends/groups

2. **Lectio 365** - Daily readings, reflections, prayers; allows tracking and adding notes

3. **Our Daily Bread** - Daily devotionals plus Lenten series; set reminders, track progress, audio devotionals available

4. **Daily Office** - Daily prayers, readings, reflections from Book of Common Prayer; customizable, reminders, progress tracking

5. **Hallow** - Catholic prayer app with Lenten series; reminders, progress tracking, guided meditations

6. **Daybreak Fellowship** - Daily devotionals plus Lenten series; reminders, community connection

7. **Bible Gateway** - Website offering variety of devotionals including Lenten options; reminders, personalized reading plans

## Usage Note

User should explore options to find the best fit for their needs; finding a system that promotes consistency is important.
