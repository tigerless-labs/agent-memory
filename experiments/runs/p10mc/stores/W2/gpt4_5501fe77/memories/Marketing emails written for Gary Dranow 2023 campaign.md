## Gary Dranow Newsletter Email Campaign (2023)

**Purpose:** Newsletter email campaign for subscriber base  
**Emails Written:** Three (numbered as 4th, 5th, and 6th in a larger series, though referred to as sixth and seventh in request)

### Email 4 — Destiny Road Album Story
**Focus:** Album history and creation story

- Explains the delay: Dranow's move to Park City, Utah for ski racing/coaching
- Positions album as culmination of a decade-long journey

### Email 5 — Comeback & Current Band

- Frames challenges as inspiration for connecting with fans

### Email 6 — Final Conclusion & "Made it Another Day"
