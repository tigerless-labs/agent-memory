Using Instagram Question sticker to drive engagement:

Planned questions:
- "What's your favorite TV show to binge-watch on a lazy Sunday?"
- "What's the best movie you've seen recently?"

Best practices for Question sticker:
- Ask open-ended questions (not yes/no)
- Keep questions relevant to content and audience
- Use attention-grabbing language and emojis
- Make questions easy to answer
- Encourage followers to share experiences
- Respond to answers to show engagement value
- Use consistently in regular content strategy
- Place prominently and pin to top of story
- Monitor responses via Instagram Insights

Expected benefits:
- Learn audience interests and tastes
- Start conversations around shared topics
- Build community and sense of belonging
- Increase engagement metrics
