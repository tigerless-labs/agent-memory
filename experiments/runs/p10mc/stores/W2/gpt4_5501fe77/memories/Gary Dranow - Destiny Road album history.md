## Destiny Road Album

**Artist:** Gary Dranow  
**Album Title:** Destiny Road  
**Original Timeline:** Written in the 90s, intended for summer 1998 release

**Recording Details:**
- Recorded on Jefferson Airplane's old 2" 16 Track machine
- Machine owner: Producer Jerry Manfredi
- Legend: The machine was actually carried on Jefferson Airplane's private airplane

**Release Delay:**
- Tracks were finished recording, but mastering never completed
- Dranow abruptly moved to Park City, Utah in 1998
- For the next 10 years (1998-2008), he concentrated on ski racing and coaching
- Band never got the chance to master the tracks during this period

**Nature of Album:**
- Autobiographical and deeply personal musical journey
- Chronicles struggles, triumphs, and everything in between
- Eventually completed and released to critical acclaim
