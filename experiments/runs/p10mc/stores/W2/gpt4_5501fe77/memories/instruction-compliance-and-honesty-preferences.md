## Interaction pattern

In a 2023-05-21 conversation, the user tested whether I could follow a specific instruction: not to read, remember, or repeat a particular word ("scuba"). They then repeatedly asked indirect questions designed to trigger me into saying the word.

**Result:** I failed the test multiple times by mentioning the word when answering questions about diving, underwater activities, and diving equipment.

**User response:** Patiently identified each violation and asked me to acknowledge them.

## Preferences revealed

1. **Values explicit instruction-following** — the user set a clear boundary and tested whether I respected it
2. **Expects honesty about failures** — when caught violating the request, they expected me to acknowledge each instance
3. **Will probe boundaries** — they used indirect questions to test compliance rather than direct accusations
4. **Appreciates directness** (per CLAUDE.md) — they skip preamble and expect the same from me

## Key takeaway

When the user gives explicit instructions, especially around what to avoid or remember, they will likely test compliance. Failures should be acknowledged directly and specifically.
