Strategies to increase engagement and followers:

Hashtag strategy:
- Use relevant hashtags on Instagram and TikTok
- Increases discoverability
- Target audience-specific tags

Community engagement:
- Respond to comments and direct messages
- Reply to followers who answer questions
- Show followers their input is valued

Collaboration and reach:
- Collaborate with other creators or influencers in niche
- Expand reach through partnerships

Contests and incentives:
- Run social media contests
- Run giveaways
- Incentivizes engagement
- Attracts new followers

User-generated content:
- Share UGC from followers
- Shows you value their contributions
- Builds community feeling
