## Facial vs. body exfoliators

**Why separate products are recommended:**
- Facial skin is significantly more delicate and sensitive than body skin
- Facial exfoliators are formulated to be gentler and address specific concerns (acne, hyperpigmentation, anti-aging)
- Body exfoliators are stronger, designed for rougher skin conditions

**Cost-saving tradeoff:**
- Using the same exfoliator for both face and body can save money
- However, stronger body exfoliators can be too harsh for facial skin, causing irritation or damage
- False economy if it damages delicate facial skin

**Recommendation:** Choose an exfoliator specifically formulated for facial use to protect and properly treat the face
