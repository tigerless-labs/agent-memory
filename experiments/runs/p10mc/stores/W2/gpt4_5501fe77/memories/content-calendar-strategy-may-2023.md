Content calendar strategy with variety across platforms:

Instagram: Stories, hobby-related photos, sneak peeks
TikTok: Quick tips, day-in-life vlogs, get-ready-with-me, Q&A videos
Facebook: TV/movie updates, behind-the-scenes, weekly wrap-ups, achievements
Twitter: Quotes, fun facts, entertainment commentary

Content themes rotate through:
- Behind-the-scenes glimpses
- Hobby and interest content
- TV shows and movies
- Interactive engagement
- Personal milestones

Posting guidelines:
- 3-5 stories daily (spaced out)
- Maintain consistent schedule
- Avoid overposting (audience fatigue)
