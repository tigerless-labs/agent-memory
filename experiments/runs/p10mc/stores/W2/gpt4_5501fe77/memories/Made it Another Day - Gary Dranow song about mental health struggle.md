## "Made it Another Day" — Gary Dranow

**Song Title:** Made it Another Day  
**Artist:** Gary Dranow  
**Album/Context:** The Manic Emotions

**Song Meaning:**
The song speaks directly to the experience of personal and professional life being disrupted by sensitivity to others around you, setting you back every time you think progress is made.

**Lyrical Analysis:**

*Core Concept:*
"You go to sleep with the weight of the world on your shoulders and then wake the next morning free of those feelings and being surprised you've made it another day to take on your daily struggle once again."

*Key Lyric:* "Like a rat in a wheel"
- Brings to life the never-ending struggle to get your personal life in order
- Only to be dissuaded at the end of every day
- Represents the cycle of making progress in the face of inevitable headwinds that buffet your life

**Artist's Explanation:**
Gary Dranow explains: The metaphor of "like a rat in a wheel" captures the repetitive nature of the daily struggle — working toward stability and personal growth, only to have circumstances conspire against progress by day's end, forcing you to start over the next morning.

**Connection to Gary's Experience:**
- Reflects his own struggles with manic depression
- Deeply personal and relatable to fans facing similar mental health challenges
- Represents the human experience of resilience and survival despite ongoing difficulties
