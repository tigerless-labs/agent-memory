## Gary Dranow and The Manic Emotions

**Artist:** Gary Dranow  
**Band Name:** The Manic Emotions

**Timeline:**
- **2008:** Dranow got back into music after 10-year hiatus (ski racing/coaching)
- **2021:** Re-established The Manic Emotions with band members located in Salt Lake City area
- **2021+:** Almost immediately started playing shows after re-establishment

**Performance Style:**
- Consummate performer
- Live sets are a testament to his energy, charisma, and unwavering passion for music

**Musical Inspiration:**
- Struggles with manic depression have fueled his creativity
- Music speaks to the human experience
- Connects with fans from all walks of life
- Inspires fans to embrace their own struggles and triumphs

**Future:**
- Thrilled to be performing and sharing music with fans again
- Unwavering spirit and determination
- Planned performances and releases in 2023 and beyond
