## Recommendations for facial exfoliators targeting acne and fine lines

**Ingredients to look for:**
- **Salicylic acid** (BHA) — unclogs pores, helps prevent acne
- **Glycolic acid or lactic acid** (AHAs) — minimize fine line appearance, improve skin texture

**What to avoid:**
- Physical exfoliants with nut shells — can cause micro-tears and worsen acne/fine lines
- Overly strong formulations designed for body use — facial skin is more delicate and sensitive

**Best practices:**
1. Choose gentle, non-abrasive formulations specifically formulated for facial use
2. Patch test before full application to check compatibility with your skin
3. Follow product instructions carefully
4. Consider consulting a dermatologist for personalized recommendations based on your unique skin concerns
