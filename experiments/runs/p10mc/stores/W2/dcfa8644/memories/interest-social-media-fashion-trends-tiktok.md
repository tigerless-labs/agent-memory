## Topic: Social Media & Fashion Trends

User explored how social media, particularly TikTok, shapes fashion trends among teenagers.

**Key observations the user engaged with**:
- TikTok may have bigger impact than Instagram/Pinterest due to short-form video format and democratized content creation (anyone can create, not just established influencers)
- Viral challenges, influencer collaborations, and "sound trends" drive fashion adoption
- TikTok trends emphasize comfort and practicality over flashy looks (e.g., oversized clothing, baggy pants)
- Smaller/niche clothing brands gain visibility through TikTok
- TikTok's younger demographic (teens/young adults) are early adopters

**User's perspective**: Noted the significance of individual diversity in trend-setting — "harder to predict what will become popular next" when content creation is democratized rather than top-down from established influencers.
