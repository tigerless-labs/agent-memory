User wants to track and pay off credit card debt using their Google Sheets financial tracker.

Plans to create a dedicated sheet for credit card debt tracking with columns for:
- Credit Card name
- Current Balance
- Interest Rate (APR)
- Minimum Payment
- Total Interest Paid
- Payoff Goal date

User is interested in creating a debt repayment plan that prioritizes cards by highest interest rate or smallest balance, and track progress toward becoming debt-free.

This is part of their comprehensive financial tracking system that includes income, expenses, and financial goals.
