## Fitness Tracking & Motivation Strategy

Committed to tracking progress and staying motivated through cycling and triathlon training.

### Tracking Methods
- Using fitness app to log workouts (duration, intensity, notes on how felt)
- Monitoring heart rate with monitor or perceived exertion scale (1-10)
- Will take progress photos and measurements periodically
- Tracking workout log to see progress over time and identify improvement areas

### Motivation Strategies
- Set specific, measurable goals
- Celebrate small victories along the way
- Reward system (e.g., new workout outfit after milestones)
- Stay positive and patient with progress
- Mix up workouts to avoid plateaus
- Consider finding workout buddy/accountability partner

### Goals Being Set
- Increasing endurance through cycling
- Improving interval training performance
- Building toward triathlon in June 2023
- Improving speed and overall fitness
