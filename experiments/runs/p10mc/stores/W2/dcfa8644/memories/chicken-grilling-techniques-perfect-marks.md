## Perfect Grill Marks & Cooking Tips

### Temperature & Heat
- Preheat grill to medium-high heat: 400-425°F
- Cook to internal temp of 165°F (74°C) using meat thermometer
- Don't overcook — prevents dry, tough chicken

### Grill Preparation
- Clean grates thoroughly before heating
- Brush grates with oil before preheating
- Oil grates again just before placing chicken

### Cooking Technique
- Pat chicken dry with paper towels (especially if marinated) to prevent excess moisture
- Place chicken at an angle to create diagonal grill marks
- Rotate 90 degrees after 3-4 minutes per side for crisscross pattern
- **Don't press down** on chicken with spatula — squeezes out juices
- Cook 5-6 minutes per side (varies by thickness)
- Flip frequently every 4-5 minutes for even cooking

### Post-Cooking
- Remove from grill and let rest a few minutes before serving
- Allows juices to redistribute, making chicken more tender and juicy

### Marinades
- Marinate for at least 30 minutes to 1 hour
- Can marinate overnight in fridge for more intense flavor
- For Korean-Style: use sugar, garlic, sesame oil in marinade to promote caramelization

### BBQ Sauce Application
- Brush sauce on during last 2 minutes of grilling for glaze
- Avoid applying too early or it can char/burn
