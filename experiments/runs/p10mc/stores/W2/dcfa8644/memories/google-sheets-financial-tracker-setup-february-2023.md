User set up a comprehensive Google Sheets financial tracker starting February 3, 2023 for online shopping and overall budget management.

Expense tracker sheet includes columns:
- Date
- Category (Electronics, Clothing, Entertainment, Home and Garden, Health and Beauty, Food and Beverages, Travel, Gifts, Subscriptions, Miscellaneous)
- Item Description
- Cost
- Total Spend
- Notes
- Optional: Store, Payment Method, Tags

Separate sheets for:
- Income tracking (Date, Source, Amount, Total Income, Notes)
- Financial Goals (Goal, Target Amount, Current Progress, Deadline, Monthly Contribution)
- Credit Card Debt (Credit Card, Current Balance, Interest Rate, Minimum Payment, Total Interest Paid, Payoff Goal)

User prefers Google Sheets over other budgeting apps like Mint, Personal Capital, or YNAB due to familiarity.
