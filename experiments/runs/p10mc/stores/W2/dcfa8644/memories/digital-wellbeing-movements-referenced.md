## Digital Wellbeing Frameworks & Movements

Concepts introduced in conversation about breaking unhealthy social media cycles:

**Time Well Spent movement**
- Aims to promote responsible design and use of technology that supports human well-being
- Focuses on intentionality in technology consumption

**Slow Tech movement**
- Advocates for slower, more intentional approach to using technology
- Philosophy of deliberate, conscious engagement rather than constant connectivity

**Individual strategies mentioned**:
- Digital detoxes
- Mindfulness practices
- Prioritizing real-life connections and experiences over virtual ones
- Setting healthy boundaries
- Seeking support when needed
- Prioritizing self-care

**Context**: These exist as responses to growing awareness of social media addiction and negative mental health effects (pressure to conform to beauty/fashion standards, impact on self-esteem).
