## Interval Training in Spin Classes

Starting to implement interval training in spin classes to improve cycling endurance beyond current progress.

### Modified Routine (Fitted to Fitness Level)
**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Easy spinning

**Interval 1: Modified Sprints (3 minutes)**
- Ride at moderate intensity for 20 seconds
- Recover at easy pace for 40 seconds
- Repeat for 3 minutes

**Interval 2: Modified Hill Climbs (5 minutes)**
- Increase resistance to simulate gentle hill
- Ride at moderate intensity for 1 minute
- Recover at easy pace for 1 minute
- Repeat for 5 minutes

**Interval 3: Modified Endurance Intervals (7 minutes)**
- Ride at moderate intensity for 1.5 minutes
- Recover at easy pace for 1.5 minutes
- Repeat for 7 minutes

**Cool-down (5 minutes)**
- Easy spinning

### Training Approach
- Willing to modify intervals to suit fitness level and prevent exhaustion
- Focus on perceived exertion rather than exact targets
- Listen to body and adjust as needed
- Target cadence: 80-100 RPM for smooth, efficient pedaling
- Plans to gradually increase intensity and duration as endurance builds
