## Values & Beliefs

User emphasizes the importance of prioritizing personal mental health and well-being over fleeting validation from social media (likes, followers, comments).

**Core principle**: Individual responsibility matters. While acknowledging that social media is a powerful cultural force shaping fashion trends and society, the user believes breaking free from unhealthy cycles requires personal action — mindfulness, setting boundaries, seeking support, prioritizing self-care.

**Concern**: Worry that society has "lost touch with reality and become slaves to our screens" and that people mindlessly follow influencers.

**Stance**: Not against technology itself, but advocates for **balance** and **intentionality** — using platforms as tools for connection and self-expression while protecting mental health.
