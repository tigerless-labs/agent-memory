## Sweet & Smoky BBQ Sauce Experimentation

Created and tested a sweet and smoky flavored BBQ sauce at home over the weekend of January 28-29, 2023. Used it to grill chicken breasts, which turned out well but were slightly overcooked — needs work on timing.

### Recipes to try next:
- **Korean-Style BBQ Chicken**: soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, gochujang marinade; interested in adding Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) for extra heat
- **BBQ Chicken Tacos**: lime juice, olive oil, cumin, chili powder base with the BBQ sauce

### Grilling lessons learned:
- Overcooked first attempt with chicken breasts
- Needs improvement on timing and temperature management
- Plans to implement thermometer-based cooking (target 165°F internal temp)

### Future experiments:
- Different heat levels and spice additions to the base sweet and smoky sauce
- Korean chili flakes in marinades
- Other spicy ingredients: Frank's RedHot, Sriracha, Cholula, chipotle peppers in adobo, gochujang
