Purchased on January 10, 2023 at an outlet mall with the user's sister for $80. User noted this was slightly over their initial budget but liked the design. Wants to maintain them carefully with proper cleaning and conditioning.

Care recommendations provided:
- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- Use shoe trees or stuff with paper to maintain shape
- Avoid extreme temperatures
- Use soft brush for gentle cleaning
- Damp cloth for wiping (avoid soaking)
- Mild soap if needed (diluted dish soap or sneaker cleaner)
- Apply water-resistant spray for protection
- Always air dry, never use heat/dryer

Key: Never put running shoes in washing machine.
