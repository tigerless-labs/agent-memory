## 1970s Feminist Movement Impact on Marriage and Family

### Key Changes Advocated
- Challenged traditional gender roles: male breadwinner / female homemaker model
- Pushed for equitable division of household labor and childcare
- Advocated for equal financial responsibilities between partners

### Reproductive Rights
- Fought for and achieved legalization of abortion
- Gave women greater control over reproduction, enabling career focus
- Supported access to birth control

### Expanded Definition of Family
- Advocated recognition of same-sex relationships and families
- Moved beyond traditional nuclear family model

### Lasting Impacts to Present Day
- Couples more likely to share domestic responsibilities
- Joint financial decision-making more common
- Evolution of fatherhood—more active parental involvement
- Expanded reproductive rights continue to affect family planning

### Remaining Gender Equality Issues
- Gender pay gap persists; women earn less on average
- Women still carry disproportionate burden of domestic labor and childcare, limiting career/educational opportunities
- Unequal access to reproductive healthcare and family planning options
- Legal recognition gaps for non-traditional families (same-sex couples, non-biological parents)
