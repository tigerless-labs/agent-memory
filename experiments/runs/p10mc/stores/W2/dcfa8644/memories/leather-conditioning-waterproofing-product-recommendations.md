User interested in leather conditioning and waterproofing for boots (especially brown leather boots from office party).

**Leather Conditioning:**

Purpose: Maintains natural moisture balance, prevents drying, cracking, brittleness

Types: Oil-based (more nourishment) vs. water-based (better for waterproofing)

Recommended brands:
- Leatherology
- Chamber's Leather Milk
- Bickmore
- Kiwi

Application: Small amount on cloth, gentle circular motions, avoid over-conditioning (leaves sticky residue)

**Waterproofing:**

Purpose: Protects from water, snow, liquids; prevents damage and staining

Types: Sprays (convenient), creams, waxes (more protection)

Recommended brands:
- Nikwax
- Sno-Seal
- Scotchgard
- Kiwi

Application: Follow product instructions, thin even layer, cover all surfaces

**General Tips:**
- Test on small inconspicuous area first
- Avoid over-conditioning
- Reapply regularly per product instructions
- Consider leather protector (like Scotchgard) for additional stain protection
- Choose products based on leather type
- Always read reviews and follow instructions carefully

These are not one-time treatments; regular maintenance needed.
