User wore sneakers to play basketball with friends on February 1, 2023, and they got very dirty. Wanted to take them to a professional cleaner but hasn't had time.

Plan: Try home cleaning first before going professional.

Home cleaning methods discussed:
- Check care label first for specific instructions
- Mild soap and water with soft-bristled toothbrush
- Damp cloth to wipe (avoid soaking)
- Baking soda and vinegar paste for tough stains (apply, let sit 30 min, rinse)
- Always air dry away from direct sunlight
- Use cleaning solution specifically for sneakers, not household cleaners
- Test on small area first

When to take to professional:
- Tough stains or scuff marks
- Delicate materials like suede/nubuck
- Specialized cleaning needed (waterproof/Gore-Tex membranes)

Act quickly as dirt/stains set over time.
