## Bookstagram Content Strategy

The user is actively building book lover content on Instagram, with a focus on engaging followers through curated posts and questions.

### "Quarterly Faves & Wants" Post
- **Title:** "Quarterly Faves & Wants" (chosen from suggestions)
- **Purpose:** Share favorite books of the quarter + ask followers for recommendations
- **Hashtag:** #bookrecs (preferred over #bookrecommendations; 12 million+ uses)
- **Call-to-action:** Encourage followers to comment with their own recommendations

### Hashtag Strategy
Realized they were using the same hashtags repeatedly across all posts. Now mixing:
- Popular hashtags: #booklover, #bookstagram, #books, #reading
- Genre-specific: #fantasybooks, #romancebooks, #historicalfiction, etc.
- Niche hashtags: More targeted by post topic
- Ratio: 5-10 hashtags per post

### Planned Book-Related Questions for Followers
- Favorites: favorite book of all time, favorite author, best recent read, life-changing books, favorite series
- Reading habits: e-books vs. physical, books per month, favorite reading spot, one or multiple books at once
- Genre questions: favorite fantasy/romance, scariest book, go-to genre in slumps
- Preferences: happy vs. realistic endings, format (hardcover/paperback/audiobook), complex characters vs. plot-driven, cover style
- Discovery: how they find books, best recommendations received, trust reviews vs. word-of-mouth, discovered authors on social media
- Miscellaneous: dinner with fictional character, longest books, book signings attended, reading tracking, memorable quotes
