## Instagram Sculptors to Follow

**Soapstone Sculptors**
- @soaperstar (USA) — Rachel Brooks; intricate, whimsical, surreal soapstone creations
- @soapstonecarvings (USA) — Michael Maddy; detailed, realistic, abstract pieces
- @stonecarverkaren (USA) — Karen Rossi; figurative, abstract, symbolic works

**Stone Sculptors (General)**
- @brianrussellsculpture (USA) — abstract, organic forms; clever texture use
- @tomhirschsculpture (USA) — figurative to abstract; texture and form focus
- @jasonkrugman (USA) — intricate, detailed, surreal creations

**Mixed Media & Abstract Sculptors**
- @alicja_pw (Poland) — stone, wood, other materials; abstract forms
- @sculpturebylisa (USA) — abstract sculptures; mixed media, found objects, bold colors
- @kristin_gaudioendacott (USA) — stone, wood blend; thought-provoking pieces

**Other Notable Sculptors**
- @jennifermaestre (USA) — intricate, detailed, colorful creations
- @heathermattoon (USA) — figurative to abstract; texture, form, storytelling
- @kevinbox (USA) — figurative, abstract, surreal; storytelling and symbolism focus
