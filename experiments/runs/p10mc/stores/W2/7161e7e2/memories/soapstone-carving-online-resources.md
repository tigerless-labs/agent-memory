**Online Course Platforms**

- **The Sculpture School** (thesculptureschool.com) — courses on soapstone carving; beginner to intermediate
- **Craftsy** (craftsy.com) — search "soapstone carving" or "stone carving"
- **Udemy** (udemy.com) — multiple soapstone carving courses available
