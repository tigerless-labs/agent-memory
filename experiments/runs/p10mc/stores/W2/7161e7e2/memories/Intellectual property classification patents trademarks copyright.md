## Classification of Intellectual Property

**1. Patent**
- Exclusive right to an invention for typically 20 years
- Requires public disclosure of the invention
- Granted for: Machines, processes, chemical compositions
- Requirements: New, useful, non-obvious

**2. Trademark**
- Identifies and distinguishes company's products or services
- Includes: Words, logos, symbols, distinctive marks
- Protection enforced through first-to-use system in India
- Prevents consumer confusion

**3. Copyright**
- Protects original creative works
- Covers: Books, plays, songs, paintings, films, photographs, software
- Automatic protection (no registration required in India)
- Gives author exclusive right to reproduce and distribute

**4. Trade Secrets**
- Unregistered business information providing competitive advantage
- Includes: Formulas, patterns, compilations, programs, processes, techniques, methods
- Protection not through registration but through confidentiality measures
- Examples: Manufacturing processes, customer lists

**Other Forms**
- Geographical indication
- Industrial design
- Layout-design of integrated circuits
- Plant variety protection
