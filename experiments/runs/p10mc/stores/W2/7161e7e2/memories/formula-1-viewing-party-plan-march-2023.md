## Formula 1 Viewing Party Plan
**Event**: First race of 2023 F1 season  
**Race**: Bahrain Grand Prix  
**Date**: March 20, 2023

### Decorations
- Checkered flags, banners, tablecloths
- Team/driver racing colors (Ferrari red, Mercedes silver, Red Bull blue+red)
- F1-themed balloons (cars, helmets, tires)
- Racing-themed signs ("Pit Stop," "Start/Finish Line," "Grand Prix")
- F1 memorabilia display (model cars, helmets, racing suits)

### Food & Drinks
- **Snacks**: "Pit Stop Pretzels," "Racing Wings," "Fuel-up Nachos"
- **Cuisine**: Bahrain-inspired or international dishes
- **Cocktails**: "Checkered Flag" (black/white), "Pit Stop Punch," "Formula 1 Fuel"
- **Desserts**: Checkered flag cake, F1 car-shaped cookies

### Activities
- F1 trivia contest
- "Predict the Podium" — guests predict top 3 finishers, win prizes
- F1-themed games (Pin the Number on F1 Car, F1 Bingo)
- Fantasy F1 league — guests pick drivers, earn points
- Photo booth with F1 props (helmets, racing suits, flags)

### Technical Setup
- Large screen with quality sound system
- Comfortable seating
- Encourage guests to wear favorite team/driver gear
- Time party around race schedule (pre/post-race coverage)
