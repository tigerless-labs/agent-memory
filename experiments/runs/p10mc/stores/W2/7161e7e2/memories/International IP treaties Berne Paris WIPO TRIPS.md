## International IP Treaties

**I. Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works**
- Adopted: 1886, Berne, Switzerland
- Protects: Literary, musical, and artistic works
- Key provision: Member countries recognize copyright of works from other members as if they were their own
- Revised multiple times since adoption

**II. Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property**
- Adopted: 1883, Paris, France
- Protects: Patents, trademarks, and industrial designs
- Key provision: Right of priority for patent and trademark applications from other member countries
- Revised multiple times since adoption

**III. WIPO Convention (World Intellectual Property Organization)**
- Adopted: 1967, Entered force: 1970
- Established WIPO as intergovernmental organization
- Purpose: Promote IP protection worldwide
- Function: Administers Berne Convention, Paris Convention, and other IP treaties

**IV. TRIPS Agreement (Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights)**
- Adopted: 1994, Entered force: 1995
- Covers: Patents, trademarks, copyrights, and other IP forms
- Applies to: All WTO member countries
- Requires: Minimum standards for IP protection and legal enforcement procedures
