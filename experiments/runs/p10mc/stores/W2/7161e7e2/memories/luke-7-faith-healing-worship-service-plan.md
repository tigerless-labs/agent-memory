## Sermon Topic: Luke 7:1-17 (Centurion and Servant)

Passage focuses on a Roman centurion whose servant was dying. The centurion sent Jewish elders to ask Jesus to heal the servant, then sent friends to tell Jesus "say the word and my servant will be healed." Jesus marveled at the centurion's faith and healed the servant at that hour.

Key themes:
- Faith transcends cultural/national boundaries (centurion was a foreigner, yet had greater faith than Israelites)
- Authority of Jesus—he understood the centurion's metaphor about authority and obedience
- Power of faith alone—servant healed without Jesus visiting

## Worship Service Structure

1. **Opening Hymn:** "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters
   - Modern worship, establishes God's sovereignty and power
   
2. **Call to Worship and Prayer**
   - Invite congregation into faith and openness to healing

3. **Scripture Reading:** Luke 7:1-17
   
4. **Sermon** on Luke 7:1-17
   - Focus: faith, healing, power of God, faith transcending boundaries
   
5. **Hymn of Response:** "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley
   - Classic hymn expressing gratitude for Jesus's healing power
   
6. **Testimony**
   - Congregation member shares personal healing testimony through faith
   
7. **Hymn of Faith:** "Amazing Grace" by John Newton
   - Classic expressing forgiveness and healing through faith in Jesus
   
8. **Healing Prayer Time**
   - Pastor/leader leads prayers for physical, emotional, spiritual healing
   
9. **Closing Hymn:** "Healer" by Kari Jobe
   - Modern worship affirming God's power to heal and restore
   
10. **Benediction**
    - Reminder that God is present and believers can trust His healing power

## Biblical Theology of Faith Healing

Healing is integral to biblical salvation and atonement. God demonstrated healing power throughout OT (prophets Elijah, Elisha) and NT (Jesus's ministry, early church through apostles). Key principle: healing connected to faith—not purely God's will alone but requires belief/trust from individual or intercessors. Matthew 9:29 shows Jesus linked healing directly to faith. Isaiah 53:5 connects physical healing to Christ's atonement. Available to all believers through faith, not limited by ethnicity/status (demonstrated by centurion example).
