## Comparison Summary
Evaluated Lufthansa vs Norwegian Air for New York to Frankfurt flights, with decision pending on trade-off between price and airline stability.

## Norwegian Air
**Pros:**
- Lowest price: $650 return via Oslo (OSL)

**Cons:**
- Financial difficulties due to competition, rising fuel costs, long-haul investments
- Risk of flight cancellations or schedule changes if financial situation worsens
- Bankruptcy risk (worst case)
- Limited customer service if financial problems worsen
- Layover adds time (Oslo)

## Lufthansa
**Advantages:**
- Higher quality service with friendly, experienced cabin crew
- Personalized service experience
- Better amenities:
  - Complimentary meal service (choice of two meals, drinks including beer/wine)
  - Economy seats: 31-32 inches pitch, 17.5 inches width, adjustable headrests
  - Wide in-flight entertainment selection
  - Includes 1 free checked bag (up to 23 kg)
- Non-stop flights available
- More stable airline with strong financial foundation
- Better hub airport (Frankfurt is modern and efficient)
- Established frequent flyer program (Miles & More)
- Price: $850 return (non-stop option)

## Norwegian Air Technical Details
- Budget airline
- Limited amenities
- Economy seats: 29-30 inches pitch, 17 inches width, no adjustable headrests, limited recline
- No complimentary meals (buy-on-board or pre-order online)
- Limited in-flight entertainment
- Charges extra for checked baggage ($20-$30 per bag)

## Decision Factors
User prioritizes: airline stability and reliability over lowest price
Main concern: Risk tolerance for Norwegian Air's financial situation
Secondary consideration: Comfort and service quality on long-haul flight
