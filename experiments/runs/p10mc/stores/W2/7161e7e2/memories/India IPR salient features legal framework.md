## Key Features of IPR in India

1. **Legal Framework**: Comprehensive laws for patents, trademarks, copyrights, and geographical indications
2. **Patents**: First-to-file system with strict criteria (novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability)
3. **Trademarks**: First-to-use system governed by Trade Marks Act, 1999, administered by Trade Marks Registry
4. **Copyright**: Automatic protection (no registration required), governed by Copyright Act, 1957
5. **Enforcement**: Civil and criminal remedies; specialised courts and tribunals for IPR disputes
6. **International Treaties**: Signatory to TRIPS Agreement and Paris Convention
7. **IPAB**: Intellectual Property Appellate Board hears appeals from Registrar of Trade Marks, Controller of Patents, Registrar of Copyrights
8. **National Policy**: 2016 National IPR Policy provides framework for system development and strengthening

India balances creator/owner rights with public interest through this robust multi-layered system.
