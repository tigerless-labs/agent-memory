## Famous Soapstone Sculptors

**Inuit Tradition (Canada, Greenland, Alaska)**
- **Kenojuak Ashevak** (1927–2013) — pioneering Inuit artist; colorful prints and soapstone sculptures
- **Kiawak Ashoona** (1933–2014) — sculptor and printmaker; soapstone, whalebone, other materials

**African Sculptors (Zimbabwe)**
- **Nicholas Mukomberanwa** (1940–2002) — prominent Zimbabwean sculptor; figurative and abstract pieces
- **Joram Mariga** (b. 1927) — Zimbabwean sculptor; intricate figurative works

**Contemporary Western Sculptors**
- **Susan Taylor Glasgow** (USA, b. 1950) — delicate, intricate carvings of flowers, leaves, organic forms
- **Mark Lundeen** (USA, b. 1958) — Colorado-based; realistic, detailed animals, humans, abstract
- **Tim Lewis** (USA, b. 1952) — abstract, figurative, surreal pieces
- **Clare Leighton** (UK, 1898–1989) — sculptor, printmaker, illustrator; figurative and abstract
