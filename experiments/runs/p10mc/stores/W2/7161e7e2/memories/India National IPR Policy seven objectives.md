## Seven Objectives

1. Create and exploit IP assets for the benefit of the Indian economy, society, and public
2. Create awareness and promote IP culture in India
3. Strengthen the IP legal and judicial system in India
4. Encourage R&D, innovation and creativity in India
5. Provide enabling environment for development of IP industry in India
6. Promote balance between public interest and private rights in the IP system
7. Promote international cooperation in IP matters

Adopted in 2016, this policy framework aims to strengthen India's IP system while balancing innovation incentives with public interest protection.
