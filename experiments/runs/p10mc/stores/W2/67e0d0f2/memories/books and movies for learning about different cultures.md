## Books and Movies for Learning About Different Cultures

### Recommended Books

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri - Explores identity, family, and culture through a Bengali-American family story

2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini - Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan; themes of friendship, betrayal, and redemption

3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan - Follows four Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers

4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe - Explores life of a Nigerian tribe before and after British colonization

5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi - Graphic novel about a young Iranian girl growing up during the Islamic Revolution

### Recommended Movies

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** - Martial arts epic set in ancient China; themes of loyalty and honor

2. **The Lives of Others** - German film set in East Berlin during the Cold War; explores surveillance, loyalty, and human connection

3. **Lion** - True story of an Indian boy lost and adopted by an Australian family; journey to find birth family

4. **The Motorcycle Diaries** - Based on Che Guevara's travel diaries across South America; explores injustices faced by the poor

5. **City of God** - Brazilian film about life in a violent favela; themes of poverty, violence, and survival
