## In-Demand Sectors

Technology and IT
- Toronto tech startup hub
- Demand for software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity specialists

Healthcare
- Aging population drives growth
- Nurses, doctors, allied health in demand

Finance
- Major financial hub
- Banking, accounting, financial analysis opportunities

Skilled Trades
- Shortage of electricians, plumbers, carpenters

Renewable Energy
- Growing sector as Canada shifts to sustainable energy

## Credentials and Experience

- International credentials may need recognition steps
- Volunteering recommended to gain Canadian experience
- Professional associations and industry events build networks
- Volunteer.ca, Idealist.org, LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace as sources

## Wages and Cost of Living

Minimum wage Ontario: $15/hour (2023)
Entry-level salaries: $35,000-$50,000/year

Toronto monthly costs:
- 1-bedroom rent: $1,200-$2,000
- Groceries: $500-$750
- TTC pass: $156
