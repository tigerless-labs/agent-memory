## Recommended Neighborhoods

**Downtown East** (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown)
- High concentration of newcomer communities
- Many refugee organizations, community centers, language schools
- Close to city center for services and amenities

**Scarborough**
- Large immigrant population from Africa, Asia, Middle East
- Scarborough Village Recreation Centre offers newcomer services, language classes, cultural programs

**North York**
- Significant refugee population
- North York Community House and Centre for Information and Community Services
- Close to York University (refugee support program available)

**Etobicoke**
- Growing refugee community
- Etobicoke Newcomer Centre and West Toronto Newcomer Services
- Near Toronto Pearson International Airport

**Parkdale**
- Long history of welcoming refugees and immigrants
- Strong community sense and social services
- Close to city center with vibrant arts scene

## Key Organizations

- COSTI Immigrant Services
- WoodGreen Community Services
- The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO)
- Toronto Refugee Centre
- North York Community House
- Refugee Sponsorship Support Program (RSSP)
- Toronto Affordable Housing Office

## Selection Criteria

When choosing neighborhood, prioritize: community centers and language schools nearby, public transit access, grocery stores and healthcare, walkability and safety, affordable housing, parks and recreation.
