Current desk lamp is too dim and causes headaches. Saw a lamp at IKEA last weekend (around May 13-14, 2023) but didn't have time to purchase it. 

**Priority criteria:**
1. **Brightness** (primary) - targeting 500-800+ lumens minimum to reduce eye strain and headaches
2. **Adjustability** (secondary) - flexible arm, swivel head, or tilting shade to direct light

**Additional features being considered:**
- USB port or built-in USB hub for device charging convenience
- LED with high color rendering index (CRI) for better color accuracy
- Warmer color temperature (2700K-3000K) if sensitive to blue light

Decision approach: Get brighter lamp first, then look for adjustability and USB features as add-ons.
