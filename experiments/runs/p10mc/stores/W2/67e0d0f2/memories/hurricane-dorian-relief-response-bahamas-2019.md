## Hurricane Dorian Relief Task Force

**Incident:** Hurricane Dorian devastated the Bahamas in September 2019.

**Organizations involved:**
- United Nations
- World Health Organization (WHO)
- International Red Cross
- Various national governments

**Relief activities:**
- Food, water, and medical supplies distribution
- Search and rescue teams deployed
- Temporary shelters for displaced individuals
- Damage assessment and recovery planning

**Safety measures for volunteers:**
- Safety briefings on risks (contaminated water, heat exhaustion, debris injuries)
- Protective equipment (helmets, gloves, safety glasses)
- Security checkpoints and safe zones
- Coordination with local authorities for security
- Psychological support and counseling for workers

**Main challenges:**
1. Scale of disaster across multiple islands—difficulty reaching remote areas
2. Logistical coordination across many organizations
3. Communications infrastructure destroyed
4. Security threats including looting and criminal activity
