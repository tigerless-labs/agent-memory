## Cross-Cultural Exchange Program Organizations

Resources for finding and learning about cross-cultural exchange programs:

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** - https://afs.org/
   - One of the largest non-profit organizations
   - Programs: student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering
   - Operates in 50+ countries

2. **Experiment in International Living** - https://www.experiment.org/
   - Offers immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students
   - Students live with host families, attend local schools, participate in community service

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** - https://www.yfu.org/
   - International educational organization for students and young adults
   - Programs: year-long exchanges, semester programs, summer programs
   - Operates in 40+ countries

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** - https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/
   - Volunteering opportunities for people of all ages
   - Countries: Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
   - Focus: community development projects and cultural exchange
