## Flipped Classroom Design Consultation

**Date:** March 10, 2023  
**Professor:** Dr. Smith (engineering, basic algebra course)  
**Consultant:** Instructional designer with experience flipping science and social science courses

## Current Course Structure
- Traditional lecture format (presenting concepts on board, students take notes)
- Problem sets assigned for homework
- In-class time spent reviewing problem sets together

## Proposed Flipped Model
- Create short video lectures for pre-class viewing
- Develop online quizzes and activities for outside-class practice
- Use class time for interactive and collaborative activities
- Free up lecture time for active learning

## Consultant Strengths
- Experience flipping courses across sciences and social sciences
- Currently focused on technology integration in teaching
