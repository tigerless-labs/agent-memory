## Interest & Context
User interested in whether avocados can be eaten daily without weight gain, and how to incorporate avocados and other healthy high-fat foods into meals.

## Calorie & Nutritional Data (Avocado vs. Alternatives)
- **Avocado**: 1 oz ≈ 50 calories; rich in monounsaturated fats (healthier), fiber, potassium, vitamin C
- **Nuts**: 1 oz = 160–200 calories
- **Olive oil**: 1 tbsp ≈ 120 calories

Key difference: avocado has monounsaturated fats (healthier) vs. saturated fats in other high-fat foods.

## Healthy High-Fat Foods Recommended
1. **Nuts & seeds** — almonds, cashews, chia seeds, flaxseeds, pumpkin seeds; high in protein and fiber
2. **Salmon** — omega-3 fatty acids, anti-inflammatory, reduces heart disease risk
3. **Olive oil** — Mediterranean diet staple, heart disease/stroke prevention
4. **Full-fat yogurt** — probiotics, supports healthy gut digestion
5. **Dark chocolate** (moderation) — antioxidants, heart/brain health benefits

## Meal Ideas
- Avocado slices in salads or buddha bowls
- Mashed avocado as mayo substitute on sandwiches/wraps
- Homemade guacamole
- Nuts/seeds in trail mix, smoothie bowls, oatmeal
- Greek yogurt with berries, honey, chopped nuts
- Salmon with olive oil and herbs
- Olive oil for salad dressing or cooking vegetables
- Avocado on breakfast toast or with eggs
- Nut butter on whole grain bread
- Dark chocolate with nuts, berries, or fruit

**Key principle**: Moderation and variety, balanced diet.
