## Goal
Learning Russian language, starting from beginner level.

## Recommended approach

1. **Start with Cyrillic alphabet** — learn letters and corresponding sounds (foundation)
2. **Take a course** — options include:
   - In-person classes
   - Online courses
   - Language exchange programs
3. **Practice daily** — consistency matters more than duration; even brief daily sessions build skill
4. **Immerse in the language:**
   - Listen to Russian music
   - Watch Russian movies
   - Find native speakers for conversation practice
5. **Online resources:**
   - Language-learning apps
   - Online dictionaries
   - Grammar guides
6. **Embrace mistakes** — speaking practice without perfectionism is essential for fluency

## Key principles
- Learning a language is time-intensive but rewarding with dedication
- Regular practice is more important than perfect grammar at early stages
- Immersion accelerates learning significantly
