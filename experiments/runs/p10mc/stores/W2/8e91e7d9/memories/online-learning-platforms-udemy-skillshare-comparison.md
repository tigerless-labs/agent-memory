## Online Learning Platform Evaluation

**Udemy:**
- **Strengths:** In-depth, comprehensive courses; structured learning format; theory and foundations focus; diverse topics
- **Pricing:** $20-200+ per course
- **Format:** Video lectures, quizzes, assignments; structured path
- **Best for:** Building solid foundational knowledge, theoretical understanding

**Skillshare:**
- **Strengths:** Project-based learning; practical, hands-on approach; creative approach to topics; more affordable
- **Pricing:** $10-30 per course
- **Format:** Project-focused, creative skills orientation
- **Best for:** Practical skill application, creative projects, quick skill acquisition

**Evaluation criteria for choosing courses:**
1. Research instructor credentials and background
2. Read reviews from other students
3. Define clear learning goals
4. Create a schedule and commit to completion
5. Practice and apply learnings to real projects

**Considerations:**
- Quality varies significantly across courses
- Many courses lack traditional accreditation
- Limited direct instructor interaction on most platforms
- Support quality varies by course and instructor
- Overwhelming number of course choices

**Best practices:**
- Supplement with books, tutorials, real-world experience
- Choose based on learning style (structured vs project-based)
- Budget considerations (Skillshare $10-30 vs Udemy $20-200+)
- Look for recently updated courses reflecting latest trends
