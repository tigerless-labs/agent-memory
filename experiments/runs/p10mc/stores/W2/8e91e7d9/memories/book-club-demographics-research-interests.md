User is interested in book club demographics research. Key topics explored:

**Gender trends:**
- Pew Research Center (2019): 74% women, 26% men in book clubs
- BookMovement (2020): 85% of book clubs have more women than men; average ratio 7:3
- Little data on non-binary/gender-nonconforming participation

**Age participation:**
- Pew Research Center (2019): Women aged 40-49 most likely (45%); women 50-64 (42%)
- Book of the Month (2020): 62% of members aged 25-44
- AARP (2019): 71% of Americans 65+ read for pleasure; 44% participate in book clubs

**Education and income effects:**
- Pew Research Center (2019): 53% with bachelor's degree+ participate vs 34% some college vs 23% high school or less
- Literary Guild (2020): 62% of members have household income above $50,000
- Higher education correlates with literary fiction/non-fiction; lower income with popular fiction/romance

**Geographic variations:**
- US: Northeast/West Coast prefer literary fiction; South/Midwest prefer popular/genre fiction
- UK: Literary heritage focus; London more diverse than rural areas
- Canada: Bilingual influence; Indigenous literature growing
- Australia/NZ: Strong literary focus; Indigenous literature emphasis
- Asia/Africa: Emerging literary scenes, focus on local authors
