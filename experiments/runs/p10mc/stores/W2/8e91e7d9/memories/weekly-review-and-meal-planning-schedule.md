## Weekly Review & Meal Planning Schedule

**Day & Time**: Sunday evenings, 8:30 pm - 9:30 pm (1 hour total)

**Availability Constraint**: Not available Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7:00 pm to 8:00 pm

**Agenda**:
1. Review tasks and goals (15-20 minutes)
   - Reflect on accomplishments and challenges
   - Review progress toward goals
   - Adjust priorities for upcoming week
2. Meal planning for next week (20-25 minutes)
   - Plan 3-5 dinner meals
   - Make ingredient list
   - Consider schedule and dietary preferences
3. Grocery shopping planning (10-15 minutes)
   - Create shopping list
   - Check pantry/fridge/freezer inventory
   - Look for sales

**Purpose**: Reflect on week, prepare for next week, stay organized with meal prep
