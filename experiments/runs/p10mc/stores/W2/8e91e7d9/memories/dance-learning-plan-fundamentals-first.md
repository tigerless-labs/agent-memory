## Dance Learning Strategy

**Approach:** Focus on mastering fundamentals first before attempting choreographies

**In-class:**
- Stick with beginner-level classes (not all-levels yet)
- Goal: build solid foundation in salsa with instructor Alex

**Home practice:**
- Short sessions: 10-15 minutes initially
- Focus on specific skills: footwork and arm movements
- Use online resources (YouTube tutorials)
- Practice to salsa music for rhythm and timing
- Possibly record self to track progress

**YouTube resources:** Watching DanceWithMe and other choreography videos for inspiration, but prioritizing basics first

**Progression plan:**
1. Master basic footwork, posture, alignment
2. Learn simple choreographies focusing on specific skills
3. Gradually increase complexity of choreographies

**Rationale:** Building good habits and strong technique first prevents bad habits and injuries; provides foundation for learning choreographies more efficiently.
