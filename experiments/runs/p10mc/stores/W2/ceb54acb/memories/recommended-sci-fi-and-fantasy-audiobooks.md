## Sci-Fi and Fantasy Audiobook Recommendations

### Science Fiction
- **Ready Player One** by Ernest Cline (narrated by Wil Wheaton) — dystopian future, virtual reality, fast-paced, nostalgic
- **The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet** by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude) — character-driven, diverse crew, galaxy travel, blend of sci-fi and character studies

### Fantasy
- **The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August** by Claire North (narrated by Peter Kenny) — reincarnation, time travel, complex plot
- **The Lies of Locke Lamora** by Scott Lynch (narrated by Simon Vance) — con artists, fantasy world, witty dialogue, character-focused
- **The Goblin Emperor** by Katherine Addison (narrated by Kyle McCarley) — court politics, magic, character-driven

### Fantasy Similar to "The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet"
- **The House of Shattered Wings** by Aliette de Bodard (narrated by Anne Flosnik) — post-apocalyptic Paris, atmospheric, character-driven
- **The Golem and the Jinni** by Helene Wecker (narrated by George Guidall) — historical fantasy, early 20th-century NYC, immigrant experience, identity
- **The City of Brass** by S.A. Chakraborty (narrated by Soneela Nankani) — con artist, magic-wielders, Middle Eastern and South Asian mythology
- **The Black Tides of Heaven** by J.Y. Yang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) — gods and monsters, orphan, character development
- **The Poppy War** by R.F. Kuang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) — inspired by Chinese history and mythology, military academy, power and war themes
