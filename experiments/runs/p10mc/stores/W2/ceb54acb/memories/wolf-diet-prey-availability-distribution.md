Wolf Diet and Prey:

Prey Preferences:
- Wolves do not have favorite prey
- Diet entirely based on availability, not preference
- Primary prey: elk, deer, bison, moose, caribou (large herbivores)
- Secondary prey: rabbits, rodents, birds (when larger prey scarce)

Seasonal Variations:
- Winter: large herbivores harder to find, wolves shift to smaller prey
- Winter diet may include hares, voles, other small animals
- Prey selection depends on season and availability

Carnivorous Diet:
- Wolves are apex predators with digestive systems specialized for meat
- Carnivorous diet is primary nutrition source
- May occasionally eat vegetation (fruits, berries, grasses) as supplemental nutrients
- Would not survive on vegetarian/plant-based diet alone

Food Distribution After Hunt:
- Distribution based on multiple factors, not single rule
- Alpha pair typically gets first pick
- Other dominant individuals may also get priority
- Size of prey affects distribution: larger kills allow everyone to eat; smaller kills require sharing selectively
- Pack size affects distribution: larger packs must be more selective or take turns
- Social dynamics and individual personalities influence who gets what
- Individual assertiveness affects food claims
