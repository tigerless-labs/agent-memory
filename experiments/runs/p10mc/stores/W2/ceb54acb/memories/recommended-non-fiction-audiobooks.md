## Non-Fiction Audiobook Recommendations

### Self-Improvement
- **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen Covey (narrated by Stephen Covey) — personal development and effectiveness, classic self-help
- **Daring Greatly** by Brené Brown (narrated by Brené Brown) — vulnerability, shame, wholehearted living
- **Mindset** by Carol S. Dweck (narrated by Bernadette Dunne) — growth mindset, overcoming obstacles, achieving success

### Productivity
- **Deep Work** by Cal Newport (narrated by Jeff Bottoms) — focused success in distracted world
- **Essentialism** by Greg McKeown (narrated by Greg McKeown) — prioritizing importance, eliminating non-essential tasks
- **Atomic Habits** by James Clear (narrated by James Clear) — building good habits and breaking bad ones

### Other Non-Fiction
- **Sapiens** by Yuval Noah Harari (narrated by Derek Perkins) — brief history of humankind, insights into past, present, future
- **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle (narrated by Eckhart Tolle) — living in present moment, spiritual guide
- **Shoe Dog** by Phil Knight (narrated by Norbert Leo Butz and Phil Knight) — Nike memoir, entrepreneurship, perseverance
