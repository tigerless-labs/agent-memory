Wolf Pack Structure:

Hierarchy System:
- Packs have structured hierarchy based on dominance and submission
- Alpha male and female lead the pack
- Lower-ranking individuals submit to higher-ranking ones
- Ranking maintained through physical posturing, vocalizations, nipping, biting

Establishing Dominance:
- Wolves establish hierarchy through physical posturing
- Vocalizations signal rank and status
- Physical contact like nipping establishes dominance without serious injury
- Most conflicts resolved without serious injury due to submission behaviors

Roles During Hunting:
- Alpha pair and dominant individuals typically lead hunts
- Less dominant wolves wait their turn to participate or eat share of kill
- Each wolf assigned different role based on strength and position in hierarchy
- Dominance structure minimizes conflict during coordinated hunts

Food Distribution:
- Alpha male and female typically get first pick of kill
- Distribution also depends on individual personalities and assertiveness
- More dominant/assertive wolves claim more food for themselves
- More submissive wolves wait their turn
