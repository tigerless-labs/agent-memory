## 30 Specific Use Cases for Generative AI

1. **Player scouting**: Analyze stats (goals, assists, plus-minus) to identify top junior league performers for recruitment.
2. **Game strategy**: Identify opposing team power-play patterns; suggest defensive adjustments for penalty-killing.
3. **Player performance analysis**: Track goaltender save percentage; suggest positioning improvements.
4. **Injury prediction**: Detect knee injury risk from training load; recommend preventive measures.
5. **Fan engagement**: Personalized in-game recommendations (food, merchandise, seats) based on preferences.
6. **Social media**: Generate pre-game hype posts with player stats and matchup previews.
7. **Ticket pricing**: Dynamically adjust prices based on opponent popularity and weather.
8. **Merchandise**: Predict demand for player jerseys after standout performances; manage inventory.
9. **Sponsorships**: Identify local sports retailers as potential sponsors; create tailored proposals.
10. **Game scheduling**: Avoid conflicts with major local events; maximize attendance.
11. **Travel planning**: Find cost-effective flights/accommodations for road trips.
12. **Nutrition**: Create high-protein, low-carb meal plans for muscle-building and agility.
13. **Fitness monitoring**: Track VO2 max; recommend personalized cardio for endurance.
14. **Fan profiling**: Segment fans ("die-hard," "casual," "youth hockey families") for targeted marketing.
15. **Venue management**: Schedule ice resurfacing and maintenance; maximize efficiency.
16. **Virtual coaching**: Real-time feedback sessions for wrist shot technique practice.
17. **Fan sentiment analysis**: Monitor social media during losing streaks; address concerns.
18. **Financial forecasting**: Predict playoff ticket revenue for facility upgrades/player development.
19. **Competitive analysis**: Analyze rival teams to identify competitive advantages.
20. **Marketing optimization**: A/B test promotional graphics and messaging.
21. **Contract negotiation**: Analyze defenseman performance and comparable contracts for fair market value.
22. **Community outreach**: Identify local charity events for positive publicity and support.
23. **Media relations**: Draft and distribute press releases for player signings.
24. **Mental health**: Monitor player stress; recommend relaxation techniques and resources.
25. **E-sports**: Develop virtual hockey tournament for fan engagement.
26. **Content generation**: Create blog posts recapping games with player quotes and highlights.
27. **Event planning**: Coordinate fan meet-and-greet events; manage logistics and promotions.
28. **Brand development**: Analyze fan data to design logos appealing to new and loyal fans.
29. **Video analysis**: Analyze game footage for key moments and coaching patterns.
30. **Sustainability**: Recommend sustainable practices for operations and facilities.
