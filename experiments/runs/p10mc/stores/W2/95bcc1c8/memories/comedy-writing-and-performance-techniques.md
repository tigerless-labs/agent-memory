## Comedy Writing Techniques

1. **Start with observations** — mundane, relatable, absurd observations from daily life
2. **Identify unique perspective** — what sets you apart; explore quirks, flaws, strengths
3. **Find the surprise/twist** — subvert expectations, clever connections, wordplay
4. **Keep it concise** — economy of words, cut unnecessary details
5. **Make it relatable** — tap into shared experiences and emotions
6. **Play with language** — wordplay, alliteration, clever turns of phrase
7. **Be authentic and honest** — share true thoughts and feelings
8. **Test and iterate** — try on audiences, be open to feedback and revision
9. **Find your voice** — develop unique tone, style, persona
10. **Write regularly** — treat comedy writing like a muscle to exercise

## Set Structure

- **Opening:** Strong, attention-grabbing joke that sets tone
- **Flow:** Group jokes into clusters or themes
- **Transitions:** Use phrases or ideas to bridge between jokes
- **Pacing:** Vary pace and tone; mix fast-paced with slower jokes
- **Build to climax:** Structure toward a final punchline or strong ending
- **Callbacks and running gags:** Create continuity and show clever writing
- **Edit and curate:** Remove jokes that don't fit
- **Narrative arc:** Create personal story or theme to connect audience
- **Segues and tags:** Use phrases to connect jokes and add punchlines

## Making Jokes Relatable & Authentic

1. Start with your own life — draw from experiences, observations, emotions
2. Be honest and vulnerable — share true feelings, fears, insecurities
3. Find the universal in the specific — identify common truths people relate to
4. Exaggerate and embellish — amplify emotions and situations for effect
5. Use self-deprecation — make fun of yourself
6. Make it specific — specific to your own life
7. Use storytelling techniques — descriptive language and vivid details
8. Find humor in the mundane — everyday experiences from unique perspective
9. Be authentic, not autobiographical — draw from life but stay funny
10. Practice and refine — test on audiences, refine based on feedback

## Improv Skills

**Core Principles:**
- Yes, And principle — agree with partner and add to it
- Listen actively — focus on words, body language, tone
- Don't overthink — be in the moment, react truthfully
- Embrace failure — take risks, learn from mistakes
- Observe life — people, situations feed character creation
- Play to your intelligence — bring your unique perspective
- Have fun — improv is enjoyable

**Practice Methods:**
- Take improv classes
- Practice regularly with friends and fellow comedians
- Play improv games: Yes And, Freeze, Scenes from a Hat
- Record yourself to identify improvements
- Try different formats: shortform, longform, musical improv

**For Comedy Writing:**
- Use improv to generate new material
- Incorporate improv into writing process
- Improv develops spontaneity and creativity
