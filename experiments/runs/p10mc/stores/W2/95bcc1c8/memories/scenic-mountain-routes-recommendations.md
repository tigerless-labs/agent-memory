## Scenic Mountain Routes

**Blue Ridge Parkway** (North Carolina and Virginia)
- Distance: 469 miles
- Features: winding through Blue Ridge Mountains, breathtaking views, hiking trails, picturesque towns

**Going-to-the-Sun Road** (Montana)
- Distance: 50 miles
- Location: Highway 83 through Glacier National Park
- Features: stunning mountain vistas, glaciers, alpine lakes

**Beartooth Highway** (Montana and Wyoming)
- Distance: 68 miles
- Features: scenic mountain views, glaciers, lakes, towering peaks

**Million Dollar Highway** (Colorado)
- Distance: 25 miles
- Route: US-550 between Silverton and Ouray
- Features: San Juan Mountains scenery, valleys, waterfalls

**Kancamagus Highway** (New Hampshire)
- Distance: 34 miles
- Features: White Mountains setting, waterfalls, hiking trails, fall foliage

## Mountain Park Attractions

- **Grand Teton National Park** (Wyoming): towering peaks, pristine lakes, wildlife
- **Asheville, North Carolina**: mountain town with arts scene, craft breweries, hiking, whitewater rafting
- **Glacier National Park** (Montana): glaciers, alpine lakes, hiking, scenic boat tours, historic lodges
- **Estes Park, Colorado**: mountain town at Rocky Mountain National Park gateway, scenic drives, hiking, wildlife viewing
- **Mount Rainier National Park** (Washington): glaciers, wildflowers, mountain vistas, hiking trails, scenic drives
