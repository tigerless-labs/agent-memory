## Rosalynn McClenagan — Last Will and Testament

**Executor:** Warren McClenagan (primary); Brian McClenagan (alternate)

**Beneficiaries:** Three sons in equal shares
- Warren McClenagan
- Brian McClenagan
- Benjamen McClenagan

**Estate Property:**
- Home at 305 Chase Ave, Gruver, TX 79040 — approximately $120,000, owned outright
- Car — approximately $11,000
- Annuity
- Life insurance policy
- One-third (1/3) interest in Ayres farmland

**Distribution Terms:**
- All tangible personal property (household items, keepsakes, photo albums, pets) to three sons in equal shares
- Specified property (annuity, home, insurance, car, farmland) to three sons in equal shares
- Remainder property to three sons in equal shares
- Three sons appointed as guardians of any minor children

**Succession:** If any son predeceases Rosalynn, his share passes to his surviving children in equal shares

**Other provisions:**
- Executor to pay debts, funeral expenses, estate administration costs
- Taxes levied against estate paid from residuary estate
- Forfeiture clause: any beneficiary contesting the will forfeits their share
