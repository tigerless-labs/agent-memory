## Maternal Changes at 27 Weeks Pregnant

**Physical Changes**
- Belly: prominent, uterus ~2 inches above navel
- Shape and size vary by body type and baby's position

**Common Symptoms**
- **Braxton Hicks contractions**: occasional, irregular, usually painless practice contractions
- **Shortness of breath**: expanding uterus presses on diaphragm
- **Swelling**: fluid retention in legs, ankles, feet — relieve by elevating legs, wearing comfortable shoes
- **Leg cramps**: especially at night — ease with gentle stretching and massage
- **Hemorrhoids**: swollen veins from pelvic pressure — use over-the-counter creams and warm baths
- **Varicose veins**: enlarged veins on legs from increased blood flow and pressure — supportive stockings help

**Emotional Changes**
- Mix of excitement, anxiety, and anticipation
- Mood swings, irritability, increased sensitivity
- Often driven by hormonal changes, physical discomfort, and fatigue

**Coping Strategies**
- **Exercise**: walking, prenatal yoga (low-impact, improves mood, alleviates discomfort, boosts energy)
- **Relaxation**: deep breathing, meditation, progressive muscle relaxation (manage stress and anxiety)
- **Self-care**: adequate rest, proper nutrition, hydration
- **Support**: connect with other pregnant women via support groups for shared experiences and emotional support

**Important Notes**
- Acknowledge and express emotions
- Seek professional counseling if needed
