## Podcast Recommendations (March 2023)

User currently enjoys The Daily and Pod Save America; seeking additional recommendations.

### News and Politics
- **The Ben Shapiro Show** - Daily podcast on news, politics, culture (conservative perspective)
- **The Axe Files with David Axelrod** - In-depth interviews with newsmakers and thought leaders
- **Podcast for America** - Weekly podcast on politics, policy, culture (hosted by four former Obama aides)
- **The Daily Zeitgeist** - Daily podcast on news, politics, pop culture (humorous approach)

### In-Depth Interviews and Conversations
- **How I Built This** (Guy Raz) - Interviews with entrepreneurs and innovators
- **The Tim Ferriss Show** - Deep dives into habits, routines, and strategies of successful people
- **TED Radio Hour** - Based on TED Talks, explores big ideas and perspectives
- **Armchair Expert with Dax Shepard** - Conversations with celebrities and public figures

### Culture and Entertainment
- **The Watch** - Weekly discussion of TV, movies, pop culture (NY Times/Ringer)
- **The Ringer's The Big Picture** - Movies, TV, and pop culture analysis
- **Vulture's The Watchlist** - Latest in TV, movies, pop culture
- **The Read** - Pop culture, social justice, personal stories (Kid Fury and Crissle West)

### Science and Technology
- **Radiolab** - Big questions and ideas through storytelling
- **Stuff You Should Know** - General knowledge on wide range of topics
- **The TED Talk Daily** - Daily TED Talks on various subjects
- **StarTalk Radio** (Neil deGrasse Tyson) - Science, pop culture, and more
