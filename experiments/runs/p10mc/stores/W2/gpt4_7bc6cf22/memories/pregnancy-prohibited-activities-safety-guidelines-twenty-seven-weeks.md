## Prohibited Activities and Safety at 27 Weeks Pregnant

**Activities to Avoid**
- **Contact sports**: basketball, soccer, hockey (risk of abdominal injury or falls)
- **High-impact activities**: running, jumping, high-intensity aerobics (strain on joints and ligaments)
- **Heavy lifting**: strain on back and pelvic muscles — ask for help
- **Hot tubs and saunas**: high temperatures raise core body temperature, potentially harming baby; avoid prolonged heat exposure
- **Amusement park rides**: roller coasters and thrill rides cause sudden acceleration/deceleration, posing risks

**Safety Guidelines**
- **Wear seatbelt**: always in car, position lap belt below belly, shoulder belt across chest
- **Maintain posture**: proper standing and sitting alleviates back pain and pregnancy-related discomfort
- **Practice hygiene**: frequent handwashing and food safety prevent infections
- **Vaccinations**: consult healthcare provider about flu shot and Tdap vaccine

**Sexual Activity Safety**
Generally safe during pregnancy unless healthcare provider advises otherwise

**Communication with Partner**
- Discuss any concerns or discomforts
- Adjust activities accordingly

**Positioning and Technique**
- Choose comfortable positions accommodating growing belly
- Gentle and shallow penetration prevents discomfort or cervical injury
- Avoid forceful or deep penetration

**Protection**
- Use protection if partner has STI or woman at risk of contracting one
- Prevents transmission and potential pregnancy complications
