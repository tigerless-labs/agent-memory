## Chase Freedom Unlimited
- 3% cashback all purchases first year (up to $20,000), then 1.5%
- No annual fee
- $150 bonus after $500 spend in first 3 months

## Citi Double Cash Card
- 2% cashback on all purchases
- No rotating categories
- No annual fee

## Discover it Cash Back
- 5% cashback on rotating categories
- 1% cashback on all other purchases
- Dollar-for-dollar match of cashback in first year

## Capital One Quicksilver
- Unlimited 1.5% cashback on all purchases
- No rotating categories
- $150 bonus after $500 spend in first 3 months

User evaluated these as alternatives to Amazon Prime Rewards Visa Signature Card.
