## Router Info
- **Model**: TP-Link Archer AX50
- **Purchase**: Amazon, approximately $150
- **Purchased**: Before March 4, 2023
- **Initial impressions**: "Huge improvement" in Wi-Fi signals

## Problem
- **Symptom**: Slow internet speeds, especially in evenings when multiple devices connected
- **Started**: A few days before March 4, 2023
- **Scope**: All devices affected
- **Temporary fix**: Restarting router temporarily resolves issue
- **ISP status**: No outages or maintenance reported in area (checked March 4, 2023)

## Troubleshooting Steps Recommended

**Firmware Update:**
- Download latest .bin file from tp-link.com Support section
- Access router at 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1
- Default credentials: admin / admin
- Upload firmware through Management/Advanced Settings
- Do not interrupt update or turn off router during process
- Reboot after update completes

**Wireless Configuration:**
- Dual-band router: 2.4GHz and 5GHz
- Channel width: 20/40 MHz or Auto
- Channel: Consider Auto or specific channels (1, 6, 11 for 2.4GHz)
- Check SSID is unique

**Other Troubleshooting:**
- Use Wi-Fi Analyzer (Android/iOS) to check for channel congestion
- Check for device interference: cordless phones, microwaves, neighbors' networks
- Router placement: central location, elevated, away from obstacles
- Quality of Service (QoS): prioritize critical devices
- Contact ISP: check for throttling
- Temporarily disconnect devices to test if congestion is cause
