## Fetal Development at 27 Weeks

**Size and Appearance**
- Length: ~14.4 inches (36.6 cm)
- Weight: ~1.9 pounds (875 grams)
- Covered in soft downy hair (lanugo)
- Protected by waxy substance (vernix caseosa)

**Organ Development**
- Brain: rapid growth with pronounced grooves and indentations
- Lungs: maturing, producing surfactant for breathing
- Eyes: wide open, fully formed eyelids, can distinguish light/dark (vision still blurry)
- Liver: functional, processing waste products
- Spleen: producing red blood cells
- Kidneys: fully developed, actively producing urine

**Movement and Position**
- Baby can move freely within ample womb space
- Common positions: head-down, feet-first (breech), sideways (transverse)
- Most babies settle head-down by end of pregnancy

**Signs of Healthy Fetus**
- Regular movements: kicks, rolls, hiccups
- At least 10 movements detected within 2 hours
- Hearing well-developed
- Recognizes mother's voice and external sounds
- Each baby is unique with varying activity levels

**Nutrition Requirements**
- Protein, iron, calcium, vitamins (folic acid, vitamin D)
- Key foods: lean meats, fish, dairy, whole grains, fruits, vegetables
- Prenatal vitamins recommended

**Prenatal Monitoring at 27 Weeks**
- Measure belly size
- Check baby's heartbeat
- Ultrasound to monitor growth
- Glucose screening test for gestational diabetes
