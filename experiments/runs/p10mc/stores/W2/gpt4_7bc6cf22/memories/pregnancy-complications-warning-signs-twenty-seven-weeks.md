## Complications at 27 Weeks Pregnant

**Signs of Possible Miscarriage**
- Severe abdominal pain or cramping
- Vaginal bleeding or spotting
- Sudden decrease or absence of fetal movement
- Fluid or tissue passing from vagina
- Action: seek immediate medical attention

**Signs of Early Labor (Premature Birth)**
Premature birth defined as delivery before week 37; risks to baby's health
- Regular contractions increasing in frequency and intensity
- Lower back pain or pelvic pressure
- Abdominal cramps similar to menstrual cramps
- Sudden gush or slow leak of amniotic fluid from vagina
- Changes in vaginal discharge (increased mucus or blood-tinged)
- Action: contact healthcare provider immediately

**Gestational Diabetes**
- High blood sugar developing during pregnancy
- Usually diagnosed between weeks 24-28
- Management: proper diet, exercise, regular prenatal care

**Preeclampsia**
- Pregnancy complication with high blood pressure and potential organ damage
- Usually occurs after week 20
- Signs: severe headaches, vision changes, upper abdominal pain
- Action: early detection and medical intervention vital

**Placental Issues**
- **Placenta previa**: low-lying placenta
- **Placental abruption**: premature separation of placenta
- May cause bleeding and require medical intervention or early delivery

**General Warning**
Seek immediate medical attention for any concerning symptoms.
