## Serve Fundamentals

### Grip & Setup
- **Grip**: Continental grip (index finger on second groove)
- **Stance**: Feet shoulder-width apart, body faces net, turn slightly right (right-handers), weight on back foot
- **Toss**: 6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front of body, slightly right; use non-dominant hand

### Execution
- **Racket position**: Hold at 90-degree angle to body, face pointing toward net
- **Contact**: Hit at peak of toss; use wrist and forearm for power
- **Follow-through**: Keep racket close to body for control and power
- **Body rotation**: Uncoil body as you contact, transfer weight from back foot to front foot

### Serve Variations
- **Flat serve**: Fast, straight, minimal spin
- **Topspin serve**: Forward spin, dips down into court
- **Slice serve**: Sidespin, curves away from opponent

### Practice Tips
- Find the racket's sweet spot with new racket
- Adjust technique if new racket has different power level
- Experiment with string tension if adjustable
- Record yourself and analyze technique
- Practice regularly to develop muscle memory
