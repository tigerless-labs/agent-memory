## Barcelona Airport (BCN) to City Center

**Airport:** Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN)

**Transportation options to Eixample:**

1. **Aerobús** — €5.90 (~$6.70 USD), 35–40 min
   - Direct bus, runs every 5–10 minutes
   - Destination: Plaça Catalunya (short walk to Eixample)
   - Tickets: online or at airport

2. **R2 Nord Train** — €4.10 (~$4.60 USD), 25–30 min
   - Commuter train from airport
   - Destination: Passeig de Gràcia station (short walk to Eixample)
   - Tickets: airport or train station

3. **Taxi** — €30–40 (~$35–45 USD), 20–30 min
   - Depends on traffic

4. **Metro** — €2.40 (~$2.70 USD), 40–50 min
   - L9 line from airport → Zona Universitària
   - Transfer to L3 line → Passeig de Gràcia station
   - Cheapest option, short walk to Eixample

5. **Private Transfer** — more expensive but convenient for groups

**Recommendation context:** Best value for solo travelers is metro (cheapest) or Aerobús (fast, direct); train is middle option.
