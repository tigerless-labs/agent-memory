## Barcelona Neighborhoods Overview

**Central/Touristy:**
- **La Rambla & Gothic Quarter (Ciutat Vella)** — heart of action, street performers, shopping; crowded and noisy
- **El Raval** — trendy, multicultural, mix of boutique hotels and restaurants; good for foodies

**Quieter/Central:**
- **Eixample** — modernist architecture, wide avenues, laid-back; close to city center without hustle; abundant restaurants, cafes
- **Sant Antoni** — hip neighborhood, mix of modern/vintage shops, restaurants; good for foodies and local culture

**Beach-Focused:**
- **Barceloneta** — lively, seafood restaurants, beach access
- **Port Vell** — quieter, more upscale

**Trendy/Local:**
- **Poblenou** — former industrial area, modern buildings, restaurants, boutiques; good for local vibe, close to beach
- **Gràcia** — charming, bohemian, village-like atmosphere, street festivals, independent shops; further from center but accessible by transit

**Factors for choosing:**
- Distance to landmarks (Sagrada Familia, Park Guell, beach)
- Noise tolerance
- Budget
- Preference for local vs tourist atmosphere
