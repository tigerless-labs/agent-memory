## Simple Naan Bread Recipe

**Ingredients:**
* 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
* 1/4 teaspoon salt
* 1/4 teaspoon sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon active dry yeast
* 1/2 cup lukewarm water
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, but recommended for more tender crumb)

**Instructions:**
1. In large mixing bowl, combine flour, salt, sugar, and yeast.
2. Gradually add lukewarm water to dry ingredients and mix until shaggy dough forms.
3. Add olive oil and mix until dough comes together in sticky ball.
4. Knead dough for 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth, and let rise in warm, draft-free place for 1-2 hours or until doubled in size.
6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat.
7. Divide dough into 6-8 equal portions and shape each into ball.
8. Roll out each ball into thin circle, about 6-7 inches in diameter.
9. Place naan in preheated skillet and cook for 1-2 minutes until it starts to puff and edges curl.
10. Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
11. Brush with melted butter or ghee if desired.
12. Serve hot with Madras curry.

**Tips and Variations:**
* Use stand mixer with dough hook attachment to simplify kneading
* Add flavorings like garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds to dough for extra flavor
* Use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone in oven to cook naan for crisper crust
* Experiment with whole wheat or higher protein all-purpose flour for more robust flavor
