## Tennis Training Schedule

- **Saturday lessons**: Regular tennis lessons on Saturdays (March 2023)
- **New tennis racket**: Received new racket (March 17, 2023)

## Training Components

### Cardio & Endurance
- Running and cycling to improve endurance
- Planning 2-3 cardio sessions per week
- HIIT, aerobic, and plyometric-based training

### Plyometric Exercises
Recently added or planning to add:
- **Box jumps**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps, start with 12-18" box
- **Depth jumps**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps, start with 12-18" box
- **Lateral bounds**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps, sideways jumping for agility
- **Tuck jumps**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps, knees to chest
- **Medicine ball throws**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps, explosive rotational power

### Stretching
Post-Saturday lesson routine includes:
- Neck, shoulder, chest, arm stretches (warm-up)
- Calf, hamstring, quad, hip flexor stretches (lower body)
- Forearm, rotator cuff, glute stretches (tennis-specific)
- Hold each 30 seconds, breathe naturally, no bouncing

## Court Strategy Development
Focus on net play to exploit opponent's weaker returns:
- Read opponent's return patterns
- Position at optimal angle to volley
- Use footwork to adjust positioning
- Slightly closed racket face for topspin control
- Mix up game to avoid predictability
