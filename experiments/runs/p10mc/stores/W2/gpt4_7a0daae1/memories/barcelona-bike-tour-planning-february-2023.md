## Barcelona Bike Tour Details

**Trip purpose:** Eco-friendly exploration of Barcelona by bike (February 2023)

**Bike requirements:**
- Comfortable seat with extra padding or gel inserts
- Upright handlebars (Dutch-style) to reduce back/neck strain
- Basket or rack to carry reusable water bottle and essentials
- Consider comfort bikes, hybrid bikes, or e-bikes with suspension and ergonomic grips
- Past back issues require ergonomic setup for pain-free ride

**Recommended bike rental shops:**
- Barcelona Bike Tours (offers comfort bikes with baskets/racks)
- Fat Tire Tours (comfortable well-maintained bikes with baskets/racks)
- Bicitours Barcelona (range of bikes, accommodates special requests)
- Donkey Republic (bike-sharing platform with baskets/racks, hourly or daily rental)

**Barcelona bike infrastructure:**
- BiciLlanes: 200+ km of designated bike lanes (often physically separated from traffic)
- Diagonal Bike Lane: 11 km dedicated lane across city
- Barcelona Bike Path: 7 km seaside path along Mediterranean coast (Passeig de la Mar)
- Ciclopeatonal: network of bike paths connecting parks and green areas
- Superblocks (Superilles): neighborhoods with limited traffic, pedestrian/cyclist priority
- Bicing bike-sharing system: day passes and subscriptions available
- Helmets recommended but not mandatory

**Bike-friendly cafes/restaurants along tour routes:**
- Can Culleretes — historic café in Gothic Quarter, outdoor seating, bike parking
- El Xampanyet — popular for quick bites, outdoor seating, bike parking, pan con tomate
- La Pubilla — cozy café in Eixample, bike-friendly, pastries
- Bicioci — bike-themed café in Poblenou, healthy snacks, bike parking + repair station
- Can Solé — family-run Catalan restaurant in Barceloneta, outdoor seating, bike parking

**Eco-friendly activities booked in advance:**
- Park Güell and Horta Labyrinth guided visit (4-hour tour)
- Montserrat Mountain and wine country tour (full-day)
- Urban Gardening Workshop (Huerto Urbano community garden)
- Ecological Walking Tour
- Barcelona Hop-on Hop-off Bus (eco-friendly engines)

**Transportation tips:**
- Hola BCN card: unlimited travel on buses, metro, tram lines
- Refill reusable water bottle at public fountains throughout city
- Check café opening hours and menus before stopping

**Related interests:**
- Carries reusable water bottle in carry-on luggage (reduces plastic waste, saves money on airport water)
- Requires ergonomic bike setup due to past back issues
- Interest in eco-friendly travel accommodations and activities
