## Madras Curry Sauce Recipe (from scratch)

**Ingredients:**
* 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
* 1 onion, diced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
* 1 teaspoon ground cumin
* 1 teaspoon curry leaves (optional, but recommended for authentic flavor)
* 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
* 1/2 teaspoon Madras curry powder
* 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
* 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
* 1 cup vegetable broth
* 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional, for creaminess)
* Fresh cilantro, for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in large pan over medium heat. Add onions and cook until translucent (about 5 minutes).
2. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, curry leaves (if using), turmeric, Madras curry powder, garam masala, salt, and cayenne pepper (if using). Cook for 1-2 minutes, until fragrant.
3. Add diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, and coconut milk (if using). Stir well to combine.
4. Bring to simmer and cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens and flavors meld.
5. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
6. Add cooked tofu (or protein of choice) and simmer for additional 5-7 minutes.
7. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve over rice, with naan, or with roti.

**Tips:**
* Use mixture of spices to create custom Madras curry powder blend
* Add potatoes, carrots, or other vegetables to make curry more substantial
* Substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream for creamier sauce
* Experiment with different proteins: chicken, shrimp, or paneer
* Add splash of lemon juice or vinegar for brighter flavor
