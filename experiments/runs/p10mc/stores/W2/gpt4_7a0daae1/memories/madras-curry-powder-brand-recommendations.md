## Recommended Madras Curry Powder Brands

**Penzeys Spices**
- Popular choice among Indian cuisine enthusiasts
- Blend of 12 spices including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, and cardamom

**The Spice House**
- Madras Curry Powder made with blend of 15 spices
- Includes unique additions like fenugreek and fennel

**McCormick**
- More accessible and affordable option
- Still offers good balance of spices
