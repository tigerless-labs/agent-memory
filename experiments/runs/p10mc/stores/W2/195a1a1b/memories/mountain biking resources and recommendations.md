Mountain biking resources and recommendations discovered for summer vacation planning:

**Trail finding tools**:
- Websites: Trailforks, MTB Project, TrailLink
- Apps: Trailforks, MapMyRide
- Search for national forests, national parks, state parks
- IMBA (International Mountain Bicycling Association) Epics designation

**Popular mountain biking destinations in United States**:
- Park City, Utah
- Moab, Utah
- Bend, Oregon
- Asheville, North Carolina
- Lake Tahoe, California/Nevada

**Trail characteristics for 20-30 mile moderate difficulty rides**:
- Mix of smooth singletrack, rocky sections, technical features
- Elevation gain: around 1,000-2,000 feet
- IMBA difficulty rating system: blue or green trails (moderate)

**Bike shop chains offering guided tours and rentals**:
- REI (Recreational Equipment Inc.)
- Performance Bicycle
- Trek Bicycle Stores
- Giant Bicycle
- Local bike shops (often have insider trail knowledge)

**Resources for finding events**:
- USA Cycling website (events, races, cyclocross)
- Popular festivals: Sea Otter Classic (California), Crankworx (Whistler and other locations), Mountain Bike Festivals (various)
- Local cycling organizations and clubs
- Social media of bike shops and cycling groups
