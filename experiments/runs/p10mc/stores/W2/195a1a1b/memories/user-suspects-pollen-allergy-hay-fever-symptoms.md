## Pollen Allergy Suspicion and Management Approach

**Timeline:** May 20, 2023

**Symptoms Reported:**
- Sneezing
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Itchy nose, throat, or ears
- Watery, red, or swollen eyes
- Cough
- Postnasal drip
- Fatigue or weakness
- Headache

**Plans:**
- Will consult with doctor or allergist for proper diagnosis and treatment

**Over-the-Counter Treatment Options Considered:**
1. Antihistamines (reduce sneezing, itching, runny nose)
2. Decongestants (relieve nasal congestion)
3. Nasal corticosteroid sprays (reduce inflammation in nasal passages)
4. Cromolyn sodium nasal spray (prevent symptoms by stopping histamine release)
5. Saline nasal rinses (clear mucus and irritants)

**Natural Remedies and Lifestyle Changes:**
- Avoid outdoor activities during high pollen count days (check online pollen forecasts)
- Keep indoor environment pollen-free: wash hair and clothes after coming indoors, shower to reduce exposure
- Keep windows and doors closed during high pollen season
- Use air filters in home (HVAC systems and portable air purifiers) — **user found this tip particularly useful**
- Use saline nasal rinse or spray
- Consume balanced, healthy diet with anti-inflammatory foods (omega-3s, fruits, vegetables) — **user found this tip particularly useful**
- Stay hydrated with plenty of water
