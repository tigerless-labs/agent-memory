## Francis Benjamin Johnston (1864-1952)

American photographer known for portraits of prominent figures and pioneering work in architectural photography. One of the first women to establish a professional photography career in the United States.

**Life:**
- Born into prominent Virginia family
- Educated at private schools
- Born 1864
- Died 1952

**Career:**
- Started photography career in 1890s as apprentice to several photographers
- Opened own studio in Washington, D.C. in 1894
- Quickly established reputation as one of city's most talented photographers
- Received wide range of commissions

**Major Contributions:**

**Architectural Photography:**
- One of the first photographers to recognize importance of documenting America's historic buildings
- Traveled extensively throughout country to photograph historic structures
- Photographs of colonial-era buildings and other historic structures
- Helped raise awareness of importance of preserving America's architectural heritage

**Portraiture:**
- Well-known for portraits of prominent figures including:
  - Theodore Roosevelt
  - Mark Twain
  - Susan B. Anthony
  - Alice Roosevelt Longworth
  - Many others
- Portraits characterized by elegance, simplicity, attention to detail
- Established reputation as leading photographer of her time

**Philosophy:**
- Committed to using photography as powerful tool for social change
- Believed photography could raise awareness of social issues and inspire action

**Legacy:**
- Celebrated for technical excellence, artistic innovation
- Enduring influence on development of American photography
