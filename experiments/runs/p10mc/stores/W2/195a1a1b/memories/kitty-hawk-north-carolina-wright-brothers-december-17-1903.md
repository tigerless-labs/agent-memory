## Kitty Hawk, North Carolina

Town located on the Outer Banks of North Carolina. Known for historical significance as site of Wright brothers' first powered flight.

**Historical Significance:**
- Site of Wright brothers' first powered flight on **December 17, 1903**
- Orville and Wilbur Wright chose location because of:
  - Consistent winds
  - Sandy terrain
- **Wright Brothers National Memorial** located in Kitty Hawk to commemorate achievement and pioneering spirit

**Natural Attractions:**
- Beautiful beaches popular for:
  - Swimming
  - Sunbathing
  - Water sports
- Kitty Hawk Woods Coastal Reserve — nature preserve with:
  - Walking trails
  - Birdwatching
  - Kayaking opportunities

**Recreation & Activities:**
- Watersports:
  - Surfing
  - Kayaking
  - Paddleboarding
  - Fishing
- Several golf courses in and around area
- Popular destination for golf enthusiasts

**Culture & Dining:**
- Part of Outer Banks, chain of barrier islands along North Carolina coast with rich history and cultural heritage
- Visitors can explore:
  - Historic lighthouses
  - Museums
  - Other attractions celebrating region's history
- Numerous restaurants and shops
- Local cuisine and seafood
- Souvenirs

**Character:**
- Small-town charm
- Beautiful beaches
- Outdoor recreational activities
