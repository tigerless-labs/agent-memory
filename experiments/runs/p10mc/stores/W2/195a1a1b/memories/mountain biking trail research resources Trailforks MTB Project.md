Recommended resources for finding mountain biking trails:

**Websites and Apps:**
- Trailforks
- MTB Project
- TrailLink
- MapMyRide
- USA Cycling website

**Popular Mountain Biking Destinations:**
- Park City, Utah
- Moab, Utah
- Bend, Oregon
- Asheville, North Carolina
- Lake Tahoe, California/Nevada

**Key Considerations for Trail Selection:**
- Distance: 20-30 mile loops or out-and-back routes
- Difficulty: Moderate level (blue trails on IMBA scale)
- Elevation gain: Around 1,000-2,000 feet
- Trail surface: Mix of smooth singletrack, rocky sections, technical features

**Safety Reminders:**
- Check trail conditions and regulations before heading out
- Bring water, snacks, sunscreen
- Wear protective gear (helmet, gloves, knee pads)
- Ride within ability level and take regular breaks
