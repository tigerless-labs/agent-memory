## Violent Incidents During the Civil Rights Movement

1. **Murder of Emmett Till** (1955)
   - 14-year-old African American boy
   - Brutally beaten, shot, and thrown into a river in Mississippi
   - Accused of whistling at a white woman

2. **16th Street Baptist Church Bombing** (1963)
   - Location: Birmingham, Alabama
   - Killed four young African American girls
   - Many others injured

3. **Freedom Summer Murders** (1964)
   - Three civil rights activists killed
   - Two of the victims were white
   - Perpetrators: members of the Ku Klux Klan
   - Location: Mississippi

4. **Selma to Montgomery March / "Bloody Sunday"** (1965)
   - Date: 1965
   - Location: Edmund Pettus Bridge in Selma, Alabama
   - Peaceful civil rights marchers brutally beaten by Alabama state troopers

5. **Murder of Viola Liuzzo** (1965)
   - White civil rights activist
   - Killed by members of the Ku Klux Klan
   - Driving home from a voting rights march in Alabama

6. **Mississippi Burning** (1964)
   - Three civil rights workers murdered
   - Two of the victims were white
   - Perpetrators: members of the Ku Klux Klan
   - Location: Mississippi

**Context:** These incidents, along with many others, demonstrate the systemic racism and violence that was pervasive throughout American society during this period. They illustrate the immense courage and sacrifice of civil rights activists who risked their lives to fight for equality and justice.
