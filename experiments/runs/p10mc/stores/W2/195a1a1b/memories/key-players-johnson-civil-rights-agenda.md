## Key Players in President Johnson's Civil Rights Agenda

1. **Martin Luther King Jr.** — Influential civil rights activist and leader who advocated for non-violent protests to end racial discrimination

2. **Robert F. Kennedy** — Attorney General under Johnson who worked closely with President Johnson to advance civil rights legislation

3. **Hubert Humphrey** — Vice President under Johnson and staunch advocate for civil rights legislation

4. **Thurgood Marshall** — First African American appointed to the Supreme Court; key figure in advancing civil rights through legal means

5. **James Meredith** — First African American student admitted to the University of Mississippi; his enrollment was facilitated by the federal government under Johnson's direction

6. **Roy Wilkins** — Executive Director of the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP); key advocate for civil rights legislation

7. **A. Philip Randolph** — President of the Brotherhood of Sleeping Car Porters; leading voice in the fight against racial discrimination in the workforce

8. **Ella Baker** — Civil rights activist and organizer who played a key role in the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee (SNCC) and other organizations

9. **Whitney Young Jr.** — Executive Director of the National Urban League; influential advocate for civil rights and economic equality

10. **John Lewis** — Civil rights activist and leader who played a key role in organizing protests and advocating for voting rights legislation
