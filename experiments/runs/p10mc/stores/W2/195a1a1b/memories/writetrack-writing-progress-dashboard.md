## WriteTrack Tool Recommendation

**Tool**: WriteTrack  
**Type**: Web-based writing progress tracker  
**Cost**: Free  
**Features**:
- Tracks daily word count
- Tracks time spent writing
- Shows streaks
- Customizable writing goals
- Reminders

**User's decision**: Planning to try out WriteTrack for tracking overall writing progress across projects (short story, poetry, journal entries).

**Why recommended**: Simple and easy to use, good for seeing overall progress (total words written, poems finished, etc.) across multiple writing tools and projects.
