Planning to purchase a new mountain bike for summer mountain biking vacation. Key considerations:

**Process**: Will visit local bike shops, do test rides before purchasing.

**Wheel sizes**: 26" (tight trails), 27.5" (versatile), 29" (fast, efficient, stable)

**Suspension types**: Hardtail (front only, smooth trails) vs full-suspension (front and rear, technical trails)

**Frame materials**: Aluminum (lightweight, affordable), carbon fiber (lightweight, high-performance), steel (durable, comfortable)

**Gearing**: Wide range (1x or 2x chainring) for climbing and descending

**Brakes**: Disc brakes (mechanical or hydraulic)

**Tires**: Aggressive tread for technical trails, smoother for cross-country; widths 2.3-2.5" for traction or 1.9-2.2" for speed

**Accessories**: Dropper post, pedals, saddle

**Dropper post details** (specifically interested):
- Hydraulic or cable-actuated
- Lowers saddle 100-150mm via lever/button
- Benefits: improved control and confidence, better clearance on rough terrain, enhanced descending capability, efficient climbing
- Considerations: adds weight, requires maintenance, must be compatible with frame

**Beginner recommendation**: Hardtail or entry-level full-suspension with forgiving geometry (relaxed head angle, lower standover height, shorter top tube)
