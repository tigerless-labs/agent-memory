Recommendations for exploring multiverse concept with better execution than Spider-Man No Way Home:

**Loki** (Disney+ series, 2021)
- Explores branching timelines and consequences of altering reality
- Introduces TVA (Time Variance Authority) which regulates multiverse
- Offers deeper multiverse exploration with better pacing

**What If...?** (Disney+ series, 2021)  
- Animated anthology of alternate MCU scenarios
- Showcases infinite possibilities of multiverse
- Explores consequences of different choices and outcomes

**Doctor Strange** (2016)
- First MCU introduction of multiverse concept
- Excellent explanation of alternate dimensions and timelines
- Praised for visuals and mystic arts representation

**Spider-Man Into the Spider-Verse** (2018)
- Not MCU but highly acclaimed multiverse film
- Introduces Miles Morales Spider-Man
- Explores parallel universes with different Spider-People
- Praised for visuals, style, and storytelling quality

**Your interest:** Planning to watch Loki and What If? on Disney+ (already subscribed); curious how MCU continues developing multiverse in future projects.
