## Laptop Battery Status

**Current Issue:**
- Experiencing poor battery life
- Has tried various software and hardware optimization tips
- Battery likely old and degraded

**Battery Lifespan Guidelines:**
- New laptop battery: 80-90% capacity after 300-500 charge cycles
- After 1-2 years: capacity drops to 70-80%
- After 2-3 years: capacity drops to 50-60%
- After 3-5 years: capacity drops to 30-40%

**Next Steps:**
- Check warranty status before replacing
- Consider manufacturer replacement or third-party battery
- Look into proper battery disposal/recycling options

**Disposal Tips:**
- Tape terminal ends with clear tape
- Contact local waste management for recycling facilities
- Check manufacturer take-back programs
- Retailers like Best Buy often have recycling programs
- Never throw in regular trash (fire hazard)
