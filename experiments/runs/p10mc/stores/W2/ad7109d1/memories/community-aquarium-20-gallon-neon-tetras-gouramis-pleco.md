## Tank Stock

- **Size:** 20 gallons
- **Fish:** 10 neon tetras, 5 golden honey gouramis, 1 small pleco catfish
- **Considering adding:** Schooling fish like lemon tetras or zebra danios (compatible with existing stock)

## Plants of Interest

Expressed particular interest in:
- **Java Moss:** Attach to rocks/driftwood; low-moderate lighting; temp 72-82°F; fertilize weekly; provides hiding for small fish; pleco may graze but not significantly
- **Anacharis:** Floating plant; moderate-high lighting (2-4 watts/gallon); temp 72-82°F; fertilize weekly; pleco will graze on it as food source

## Decorations

Currently has:
- Artificial coral
- Rocks

Planning to add:
- Treasure chest decoration
- Will arrange treasure chest, coral, and rocks together to create a "city" area for fish to explore
- Will place at angles/slight angles in substrate; vary heights with rocks/coral; leave open swimming space

## Care Notes

- Both Java Moss and Anacharis are sensitive to copper-based medications
- Treasure chest chosen over sunken ship because it's more compact for 20-gallon size
- Goals: create hiding places, stimulate fish exploration, enhance aesthetics
