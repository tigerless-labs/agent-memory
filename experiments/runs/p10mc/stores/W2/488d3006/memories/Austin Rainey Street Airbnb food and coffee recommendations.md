## Austin Rainey Street Stay

- **Accommodation**: Airbnb on Rainey Street, Austin
- **Dates**: April 2023
- **Trip Purpose**: Attended Austin Film Festival

## Breakfast Spots (Walking distance from Rainey Street)
1. Banger's Sausage House & Beer Garden — breakfast tacos, quiche, eggs benedict
2. Craft Pride — weekend brunch, breakfast burritos, avocado toast, chilaquiles
3. Container Bar / Café No Sé — coffee shop with pastries, quiche, sandwiches
4. Lustre Pearl — weekend brunch, eggs benedict, breakfast tacos, omelette station
5. Emmer & Rye — farm-to-table, seasonal breakfast menu with eggs, pancakes, sandwiches
6. Café José — traditional breakfast, omelettes, pancakes, Mexican-inspired breakfast tacos
7. Magnolia Café (10-15 min walk) — vast menu, omelettes, pancakes, sandwiches
8. The Frisco Shop (10-15 min walk) — classic diner, eggs, pancakes, burgers
9. La Condesa (15-20 min walk) — weekend brunch, breakfast tacos, chilaquiles, eggs benedict

## Coffee Shops (Walking distance)
1. Cuvee Coffee — high-quality beans, pour-overs, lattes, cold brew, popular with locals
2. Once Over Coffee Bar — expertly pulled espresso, pastries, sandwiches (5-min walk)
3. Veracruz All Natural Coffee — amazing coffee, pastries, Mexican-inspired breakfast items
4. Café No Sé — stylish, specialty drinks, local roaster beans (in Container Bar complex)
5. The Brew & Brew (10-min walk) — coffee, beer, wine, pastries

## Notes
- All recommendations within walking distance
- Rainey Street is described as vibrant neighborhood with plenty of options
- Cuvee Coffee and Once Over Coffee Bar recommended for quick coffee on the way out
