## Big Sur Camping Experience

Solo trip to Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park campground in May 2023. First use of portable camping stove on this trip. Interested in expanding outdoor cooking skills, particularly breakfast recipes for camping.

### Food and Wildlife Management
- Stores food in airtight containers and keeps them in car while camping
- Packs out all trash when leaving campsite
- Values minimizing environmental impact and reducing wildlife encounters
- Properly informed about bear-resistant food storage practices

### Camping Breakfast Recipe Ideas Discussed
- Campfire skillet breakfast (eggs, sausage, peppers, onions, cheese)
- Trail mix oatmeal with nuts and dried fruits
- Camping breakfast burritos
- French press coffee and campfire muffins
- Breakfast tacos
- Instant campfire hash browns
- Camping quiche
- Yogurt parfait
- Campfire pancakes
- Egg and cheese wraps

### Bear-Resistant Storage Options Recommended
- Garcia Bear-Resistant Container
- BearVault containers
- Ursack containers and bags
- Counter Assault Bear Bag
- Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag
