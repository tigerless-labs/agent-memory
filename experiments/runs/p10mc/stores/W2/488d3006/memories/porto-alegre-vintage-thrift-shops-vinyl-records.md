## Recommended Shops

### Armazém do Passado
- Vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor items
- **Has vinyl records**: variety ranging from Brazilian music to rock and pop from various decades
- Curated and trendy vintage styles

### Casa da Sogra
- Thrift store specializing in retro and vintage home decor, furniture, accessories
- **Has vinyl records**: selection includes some rare and hard-to-find titles

### Brechó Municipal
- Municipal marketplace selling used clothes, shoes, accessories
- Known for **affordable prices**
- May have small vinyl record selection

### Pinga do Brechó
- Vintage and thrift store with clothing, accessories, kitchenware
- **Prices can be higher but still reasonable**
- May have small vinyl record selection

### Bazar Vintage
- Stylish, fashion-forward store selling vintage and retro clothing and accessories
- Curated trendy vintage styles

## Note
Always check with shops (website, social media, or call ahead) for current inventory and vinyl record availability before visiting.
