## Zion National Park Trip

### Trip Planning
- Planning a trip to Zion National Park in the near future (after May 2023)
- Has relevant hiking experience from Big Sur trip
- Interested in exploring must-see attractions and popular hikes

### Must-See Attractions and Hikes Discussed
- The Narrows (scenic hike along Virgin River)
- Angels Landing (iconic rock formation, steep final ascent)
- The Emerald Pools (series of pools and waterfalls on Kayenta Trail)
- Weeping Rock (rock formation with dripping springs)
- West Rim Trail (10-mile hike)
- Observation Point Trail (8-mile hike)
- Watchman Trail (3-mile hike)

### Hiking Boot Purchase
- Decided to purchase **Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof boots**
- Boot features needed for Zion terrain: waterproofing, ankle support, traction, breathability
- Break-in strategy: wear around town first, start with short hikes, gradually increase distance/difficulty before tackling Zion's more challenging trails
- Will bring blister care items (moleskin, bandages)

### Zion Logistics
- Free shuttle service throughout park
- Check for permits and restrictions before hiking
- Bring plenty of water and sunscreen (intense Utah sun)
- Sturdy hiking shoes essential for steep, uneven terrain
