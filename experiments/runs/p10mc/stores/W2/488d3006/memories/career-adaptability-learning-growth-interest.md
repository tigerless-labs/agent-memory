User engaged in discussion about adaptability as universal skill across industries (tech, healthcare, education, retail, hospitality, marketing, consulting, finance, startups, logistics). User's perspective: "It's true, there's always something new to learn and explore when you're adaptable. It can be daunting at times, but it's also an exciting way to keep growing in your career."

Core concern: balancing the excitement of constant learning and career growth with avoiding burnout and overwhelm. User found following tips helpful:
1. Prioritize self-care (sleep, exercise, healthy food, meditation/yoga)
2. Practice time management
3. Learn to say "no" and recognize limits
4. Seek support from coworkers/mentors
5. Celebrate successes

User recognizes adaptability doesn't mean being constantly available, but finding right balance between change management and self-care.
