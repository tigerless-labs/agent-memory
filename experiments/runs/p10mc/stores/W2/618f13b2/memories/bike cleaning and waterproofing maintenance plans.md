User is planning to clean Trek FX 2 hybrid bike this weekend, especially the dirty wheels.

Bike washing products recommended:
- Mild pH-neutral soap: Finish Line, Pedro's, Simple Green, Muc-Off
- Soft-bristled brush and microfiber cloths
- Wheel cleaners: Pedro's Wheel Cleaner, Finish Line Wheel Cleaner, Muc-Off Wheel Cleaner
- Rinse with water and dry thoroughly to prevent rust and water spots

Chain and gears waterproofing products:
- Chain lube: Squirt Long Lasting Chain Lube, Finish Line Wet Lube, Pedro's Chain Lube with XRP
- Waterproofing sprays: WD-40 Bike Water Repellent Spray, Finish Line Waterproofing Spray, Muc-Off Waterproofing Spray
- Ceramic coatings: CeramicSpeed UFO Chain Treatment, Squirt Ceramic Chain Lube, Muc-Off Ceramic Lube

Always follow product instructions and test on small area first.
