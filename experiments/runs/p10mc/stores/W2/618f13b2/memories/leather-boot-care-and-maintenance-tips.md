## Leather Boot Care & Maintenance

**Regular conditioning:**
- Use high-quality leather conditioner or leather oil regularly
- Keeps leather soft, supple, and protected from drying out
- Use sparingly and follow manufacturer instructions (over-conditioning leaves greasy residue)

**Waterproofing:**
- Apply waterproofing spray or cream to protect from water and stains
- Especially important in rainy or snowy climates
- Reapply periodically as needed

**Cleanliness:**
- Clean regularly with soft, dry cloth to remove dirt and debris
- Use soft-bristled leather brush to remove surface dirt
- Never use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners (can damage leather)

**Storage & shape maintenance:**
- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
- Use cedar shoe trees or stuff with paper to maintain shape and prevent creasing
- Prevents leather from drying out or cracking

**Temperature protection:**
- Avoid exposure to extreme temperatures (near heaters, freezing cold)
- Extreme heat/cold causes leather to dry out and crack

**Careful handling:**
- Handle with care to avoid scratches, scuffs, or tears
- Avoid wearing in harsh weather or during rough outdoor activities
- Can apply leather protector to high-wear areas (toes, heels) for extra protection

**Professional maintenance:**
- Take boots to professional cobbler or leather repair service for periodic maintenance
- Services include: polishing, conditioning, and other professional care
- More cost-effective than replacing damaged boots
