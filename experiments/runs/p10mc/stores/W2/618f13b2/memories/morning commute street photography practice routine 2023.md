## Morning Commute Street Photography Practice

**Started:** May 2023, following the street photography workshop (May 1-2)

**Current routine:**
- Takes camera to work daily during morning commute
- Focuses on capturing candid shots of commuters
- Experimenting with different angles and compositions
- Practicing anticipation of moments and gestures

**Key practice areas:**
- Capturing perfect expressions and gestures in candid shots
- Building rapport with strangers
- Asking permission ethically and respectfully
- Keeping a low profile while photographing
- Using the 50mm f/1.8 lens for street work

**Equipment used:**
- 50mm f/1.8 prime lens (purchased April 10, 2023)
- Works on burst mode for candid moments

**Goals:**
- Improve composition skills through daily practice
- Become more comfortable approaching strangers
- Capture meaningful, candid moments
- Develop a cohesive street photography style
