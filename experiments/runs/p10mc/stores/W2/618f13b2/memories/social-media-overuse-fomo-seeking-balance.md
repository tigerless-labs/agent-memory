## Social Media Challenge

The user has been:
- Scrolling social media feeds endlessly
- Feeling FOMO (fear of missing out) seeing friends' seemingly fun activities
- Recognizing that social media is contributing to their sense of disconnection, not helping
- Feeling pressure to stay constantly connected to avoid missing out

## Goals
- Limit social media use without feeling left out
- Stay connected to friends and acquaintances through non-social-media means
- Develop a healthier relationship with social media
- Prioritize real-world, meaningful interactions

## Suggested Strategies That Resonated
- Phone/video calls instead of just following social media
- Regular in-person meetups (coffee dates, outings)
- Snail mail (letters, postcards)
- Group chats/forums around shared interests
- Game nights and potlucks
- Scheduled, intentional social media check-ins (not mindless scrolling)
- Unfollowing/muting accounts that trigger FOMO
