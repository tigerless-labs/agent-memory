## Shoe Rack Organization System

**Method chosen:** Hybrid approach
- Primary: group by type (heels, flats, boots, sandals, sneakers)
- Secondary: sort by color within each type group

**Storage approach:**
- Most frequently worn: at eye level or front center
- Out-of-season shoes: stored in storage bins
- Shoe trees: used for boots to maintain shape

**Benefits:**
- Quick access to needed pair
- Reduced decision fatigue
- Protected off-season storage

## Confirmed May 30, 2023

User confirmed preference for type + color system as primary method. This hybrid approach makes it significantly easier to find needed shoes.

**Additional organization tips discussed:**
- Can combine type + color with other secondary categories if needed (occasion, season, frequency of use)
- Store shoes on racks, shelves, or cubbies to maximize vertical space
- Use shoe trees or stuff with paper to maintain shape and prevent creasing
- Keep frequently worn shoes in easy-to-reach locations
- Place less frequently worn shoes toward back or on higher shelves

**Detailed categorization options for future refinement:**
- By Occasion: work/school, formal events, casual outings, outdoor activities, date night
- By Season: winter (boots, warm shoes), spring/fall (transitional), summer (sandals)
- By Frequency: daily wear, occasional wear, special occasions
