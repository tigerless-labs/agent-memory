## Canvas & Sneaker Shoe Cleaning

**Materials needed:**
- Soft-bristled brush or soft clean cloth (microfiber or cotton preferred)
- Gentle cleaning solution
- Clean, damp cloth for rinsing
- Dry cloth for final blotting

**Gentle cleaning solution options:**
- 1 tablespoon mild soap (diluted dish soap or hand soap) + 1 cup warm water
- Equal parts water and white vinegar
- Specialized products: Sneaker Cleaner, Jason Markk (test small area first)

**Step-by-step cleaning process:**
1. Remove loose dirt with soft-bristled brush or cloth
2. Dip cloth in cleaning solution and wring thoroughly
3. Wipe entire surface (tongue, laces, sole, canvas)
4. For stubborn scuff marks: gentle scrubbing motion with damp cloth (avoid excessive pressure)
5. Rinse with clean damp cloth to remove soap residue
6. Blot with dry cloth to remove excess moisture
7. Air dry at room temperature (away from direct sunlight, heat, or hair dryer)

**Important DON'Ts:**
- Never soak shoes or use excessive water
- Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners
- Don't use rough cloths
- Don't expose to direct sunlight or heat (causes material degradation)
- Don't use toothbrush method (can be too abrasive)

**Rubber maintenance:**
- Clean rubber toe caps and soles separately
- Can use rubber conditioner or silicone-based lubricant to prevent drying and cracking

**Preventive care:**
- Apply fabric protector or waterproofing spray after cleaning
- Helps repel water and stains
- Makes future cleaning easier
- Reapply periodically

**Lace care:**
- Don't forget laces when cleaning
- Wipe with mild soap and water
- Air dry
