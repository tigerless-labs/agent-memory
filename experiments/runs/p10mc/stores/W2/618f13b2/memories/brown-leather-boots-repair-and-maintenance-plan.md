## Brown Leather Boots — Repair & Maintenance Plan

**Status:** Scuffed at office party on February 5, 2023; seeking repair and ongoing maintenance.

**Plans decided:**

- Get leather shoe cleaning kit (considering: Saphir, Kiwi, or Lexol brands)

**Cleaning kit priorities:**
- Must be specifically designed for leather
- Should include: gentle cleaner, conditioner, protector, tools (brush, cloth)
- Brands recommended: Saphir (high-quality, gentle), Kiwi (range of leather types), Lexol (popular for conditioning)

**Storage approach:**
- Will store with cedar shoe tree when not in use
- Will be organized on shoe rack by type, then by color
- Out-of-season storage in storage bins
