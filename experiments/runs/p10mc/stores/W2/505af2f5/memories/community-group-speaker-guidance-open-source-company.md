## Context

User hosts a community-based group focused on knowledge sharing, discussions, and community building. Members bring interesting ideas and learn from each other.

## Speaking Request

An upcoming speaker from a company that is the main driver for building an open source technology has been invited.

## Key Request

The speaker should focus on:
- Knowledge and expertise
- Discussions and insights
- Community building

The speaker should avoid:
- "Sales pitch" language (but use alternative phrasing like "promotion or advertisement of your products")

## Communication Style Preference

- Informal tone (not email-like)
- Indirect wording (don't use "sales pitch" directly; mention "promotion" or "advertisement" instead)
