## Luna's Food Transition Plan

**Date:** May 20, 2023

**Plan:** Transitioning Luna to a new food brand specifically chosen for better digestive health

**Transition method:** Gradual 7-10 day transition
- Start with 25% new food mixed with current food
- Gradually increase proportion every few days
- Monitor behavior, appetite, and stool quality
- Watch for signs of digestive upset (diarrhea, vomiting, lethargy)
- Consult veterinarian if any adverse reactions occur
