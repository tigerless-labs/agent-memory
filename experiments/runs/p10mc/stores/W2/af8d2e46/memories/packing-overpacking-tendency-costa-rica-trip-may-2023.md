## Overpacking Pattern

User has a tendency to overpack on trips. On a recent Costa Rica trip:
- **Packed:** 7 shirts and 5 pairs of shorts
- **Actually wore:** 3 shirts and 2 pairs of shorts
- **Insight:** Brought too many "just in case" items that went unused

## Goal
User is actively working to improve packing habits by focusing on essentials rather than contingency items. Recognizes the problem and is seeking strategies to pack more strategically.

## Recommendations discussed
- Aim for 2-3 versatile tops instead of 7
- Pack 2-3 bottoms instead of 5
- Focus on multipurpose, mix-and-match pieces
- Write activity-based packing lists
- Avoid overpacking "nice to have" items
