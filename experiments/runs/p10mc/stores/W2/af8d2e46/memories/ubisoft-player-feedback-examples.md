## Ubisoft Player Feedback Response Examples

### Positive Examples (Responsive)

1. **Assassin's Creed Odyssey** - Level scaling patch; addressed complaints about level gating system; allowed easier open world exploration

2. **Rainbow Six Siege** - Operator rebalancing after feedback about underpowered characters

3. **The Division 2** - Increased high-quality gear drops following criticism about punishing loot system

4. **Ghost Recon Wildlands** - Added option to turn off AI squadmates in response to player requests for more immersion

5. **Far Cry 5** - Added new gameplay elements (fishing, hunting) in response to criticism about linear storytelling; enabled more free world exploration

### Negative Example (Delayed Response)

**Ghost Recon Breakpoint** - Launched with significant player criticism including weak storytelling, aggressive microtransactions, poor loot system, problematic AI companions, and technical glitches. Ubisoft acknowledged the feedback but took considerable time releasing fixes, causing players to feel ignored. Eventually made several improvements.

### Communication Improvements

- Increased social media engagement
- Regular fan interaction and feedback responses  
- Feedback mechanisms: surveys, forums, beta testing programs
- Transparent development updates
- Frequent sharing of development insights
