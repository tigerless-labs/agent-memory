## Photography Tips for Photographing Luna

**Date:** May 20, 2023

**Getting Luna to pose:**
- Choose time after meal or play session (most active)
- Use positive reinforcement (treats, praise, affection)
- Let her explore the photo studio setup first
- Keep sessions short (5-10 minutes) and frequent
- Use comfortable props (toys, scratching posts, favorite blanket)
- Create calm atmosphere (calming music, comfortable temperature, minimal noise)
- Shoot from her level (low-angle, intimate shots)
- Use "treat trick" — hold treat near lens or desired pose, slowly move toward spot
- Capture candid moments during natural behavior
- Be patient; cats are unpredictable

**Getting Luna to look directly at camera:**
- Use treats near lens
- Make eye contact first
- Use squeaky toys near camera
- Get on her level
- Call her name or use familiar voice
- Keep camera still
- Reward attention with praise
- Use reflector if distracted
- Don't force posed shots; capture candid looks

**Action shot tips (laser pointer play):**
- Use fast shutter speed (at least 1/1000th second)
- Enable continuous autofocus (AI Servo or AF-C)
- Use burst mode for multiple quick shots
- Anticipate Luna's behavior
- Ensure good lighting, avoid harsh shadows
- Shoot from low angle/side to emphasize speed
- Follow laser pointer with camera
- Experiment with different angles
- Keep background simple
- Practice frequently

**Props and ideas:**
- Cardboard boxes or tunnels
- Feather wands
- Catnip toys
- Funny hats or costumes
- Cozy blankets/beds
- Puzzle or treat-dispensing toys
- Cat cafe setup (cups, saucers, fake food)

**Incorporating sisal rope scratching post:**
- Use as perch for surveying
- Move laser pointer around post for jumping/pouncing
- Capture climbing movements
