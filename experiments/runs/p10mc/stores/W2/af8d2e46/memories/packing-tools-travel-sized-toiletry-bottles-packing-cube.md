## Packing Investments (May 2023)

User has invested in travel organization tools:
- **Travel-sized toiletry bottles** — refillable containers for decanting toiletries; saves space and weight
- **Packing cube** — helps organize and compress items, keeps toiletries in one place for quick access

## Effectiveness
User reports these tools have "really helped me stay organized" and are making a noticeable difference in trip preparation.

- Decanting favorite toiletries into smaller containers
- Using multi-purpose products (shampoo + conditioner)

- Labeling containers to easily identify contents
