## Enrichment and Toy Recommendations for Luna

**Date:** May 20, 2023

**Recommended toys and activities to encourage exercise and engagement:**

1. **Wand toys with feathers or fabric** — allows natural hunting behavior, encourages pouncing and chasing
2. **Interactive toy mice** — small, furry with bells or noise-makers inside
3. **Tunnels and tents** — provide new environments to explore and play
4. **Food puzzle toys** — mental stimulation, cat works for treats by rolling/pushing
5. **Catnip toys or mice** — filled with dried catnip or coated with catnip oil
6. **Cardboard boxes** — cut holes in different shapes and sizes for enrichment
7. **Window perches** — observe outdoors, exercise jumping and stretching
8. **Hide-and-seek play** — hide treats or toys around house for hunting
9. **Sensory play** — different textures (paper bags, tissue, crinkly toys)
10. **Toy rotation** — switch out toys regularly to prevent boredom

**Benefits of placing scratching posts near windows:**
- Viewing pleasure (watch birds, trees, creatures)
- Natural light (maintains circadian rhythms)
- Ventilation (gentle breeze if window openable)
- Multi-functional scratching, stretching, and observing

**Safety considerations for window placement:**
- Avoid direct sunlight (can cause overheating)
- Beware of strong drafts
- Secure window so cat can't escape
- Ensure scratching post is stable and won't topple
