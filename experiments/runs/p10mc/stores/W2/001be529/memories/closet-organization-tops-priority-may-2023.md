## Closet Organization Project (May 20, 2023)

### Problem
- Closet getting cluttered from recent shopping
- Need systematic organization approach
- Focusing specifically on organizing tops (many new acquisitions)

### Recent Top Acquisitions (from thrift haul)
**Casual (70%)**
- Bright coral pink t-shirt with fun graphic print
- Multiple t-shirts and tank tops

**Dressy (30%)**
- Soft pastel blue sweater with subtle cable knit pattern
- Black blouse with bold abstract floral pattern

**Casual/Layering**
- White tank top with delicate lace trim

**Patterns noted**
- Stripes, polka dots, florals, solid colors

### Organization Methods Recommended
1. **Purge approach**: KonMari Method (keep what sparks joy), FlyLady Method, or 4-Box Method
2. **Top-specific system**:
   - Categorize by type: short-sleeve, long-sleeve, dressy, activewear
   - Sort by occasion: everyday, work, date night, lounging
   - Organize by season: current season accessible, out-of-season in bins
   - Use color-coded system or color grouping
   - Hang frequently worn items at eye level
   - Use double hangers to maximize space

3. **Integration approach** (recommended): 
   - Integrate new tops into existing categories rather than separate section
   - Sort new items by color/pattern within each group
   - Leave space between items to avoid overcrowding
   - Assess gaps and opportunities for new outfit combinations

### Key Takeaway
Integration of new pieces into existing system helps visualize how they fit into overall wardrobe and makes daily outfit creation easier.
