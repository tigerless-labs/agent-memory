## Excel Conditional Formatting: Highlight Min/Max

To highlight highest and lowest values in a range:

For the MAX value, use: MAX dollar-sign A dollar-sign 1 colon dollar-sign A dollar-sign 10 equals A1

For the MIN value, use: MIN dollar-sign A dollar-sign 1 colon dollar-sign A dollar-sign 10 equals A1

Usage: Apply via Conditional Formatting → New Rule → Use formula to determine which cells to format. Replace A1:A10 with your range and A1 with the current cell reference.
