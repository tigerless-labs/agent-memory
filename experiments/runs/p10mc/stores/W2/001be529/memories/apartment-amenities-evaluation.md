## Amenities to Consider

**High Priority Options**
- In-unit laundry - saves time and convenience
- Gym or fitness center - for fitness activities
- Proximity to public transportation - essential for commute

**Secondary Amenities**
- Pool - relaxation and hot weather relief
- Community garden - for growing own food
- Parking - if you have a car
- Security features - gated parking, cameras, on-site security
- Pet-friendly policies - if you have pets

## Selection Strategy
- Prioritize your own needs; don't pay for unnecessary amenities
- Ask questions during apartment viewings
- Balance amenities with budget constraints
- Safety and cleanliness matter more than luxury amenities
