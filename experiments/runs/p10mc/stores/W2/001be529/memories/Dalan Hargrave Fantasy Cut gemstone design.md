**Design Details:**
- Designer: Dalan Hargrave, Copyright May 23, 2003
- Indexes: 72 (5 degrees apart)
- Refractive Index: 1.76
- Face-up brilliance: ISO 89.5%, COS 78.1% (at 10° tilt: ISO 89.2%, COS 78.6%; at 20° tilt: ISO 75.9%, COS 63.9%)

**Application:**
- Optimized for CREATIVE GEMS SC-33 Kashmir Blue Synthetic flame-fusion corundum
- Designed for 8mm ring stone
- Works well in most light to medium material with RI 1.7+ in sizes 8mm and larger

**Design Notes:**
- 72-index teeth spacing (5 degrees apart) means adjacent indices are less problematic for design than finer resolution
- Includes TCP (Top Crown Point), GMP (Great Main Point), PCP (Pavilion Crown Point), and level girdle angles
