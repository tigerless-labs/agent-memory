## Orchid Purchase Plans

**Decision date:** May 21, 2023

**Status:** Considering purchase; ready to handle most care requirements but concerned about humidity

**Humidity solution chosen:** Tray with pebbles and water method
- Place orchid pot on tray filled with water and pebbles
- Water evaporates to increase humidity around plant
- Refill water as needed
- Cost: $0 (using existing household items)
- Alternative methods noted: saucer with water, grouping with other plants, misting, humidifier ($20-50)

**Recommended beginner orchid species:**
- Phalaenopsis (Moth Orchid) — most common, adaptable, thrives in various lighting
- Dendrobium — long-lasting flowers, low-maintenance, relatively hardy
- Oncidium (Dancing Lady Orchid) — small, forgiving if watering is inconsistent
- Cattleya — large showy flowers, prefers bright indirect light
- Paphiopedilum (Venus Slipper Orchid) — unique slipper-shaped flowers, low-maintenance

**Key care points to monitor:**
- Bright indirect light (east/west-facing window, avoid direct sun)
- Water when potting medium feels dry (weekly or every 10-14 days)
- Target humidity 40-70%
- Daytime temps 65-75°F, nighttime 5-10°F cooler
- Well-draining potting media (bark, sphagnum moss, perlite mix)
- Repot every 2-3 years

**Repotting timeline:** Every 2-3 years as potting medium breaks down
