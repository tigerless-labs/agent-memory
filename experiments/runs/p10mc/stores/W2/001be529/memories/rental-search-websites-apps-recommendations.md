## Recommended Rental Search Websites

1. **Zillow** — Popular real estate website with rental filters by price, location, amenities; includes "Rent Zestimate" feature; mobile app available (iOS/Android)

2. **Trulia** — Similar to Zillow with price/location/amenity filters; provides crime maps and neighborhood insights; mobile app available (iOS/Android)

3. **HotPads** — Map-based search engine with filters for price, location, amenities; "Commute Time" feature shows travel time to specific locations; useful for evaluating transportation access

4. **PadMapper** — Aggregates listings from Craigslist and local sources; filters by price, location, amenities; provides "Safety Score" for neighborhoods; mobile app available (iOS/Android)

5. **Walk Score** — Not a rental site but useful for evaluating neighborhood walkability and public transportation access; enter address/neighborhood to get proximity scores to amenities

6. **Apartments.com** — Comprehensive rental search with price/location/amenity filters; "Ratings & Reviews" feature shows resident feedback about properties

7. **Rent Jungle** — Aggregates listings from various sources; filters by price, location, amenities; provides "Neighborhood Guide" with local area insights

## Search Tips
- Set budget filter to $800 or less to avoid seeing properties outside price range
- Use location filters to focus on areas near public transportation and work/volunteer locations
- Read reviews and ratings from current/former residents
- Contact landlords/property managers directly to ask questions and schedule viewings
- Stay organized, take notes, and prioritize needs
