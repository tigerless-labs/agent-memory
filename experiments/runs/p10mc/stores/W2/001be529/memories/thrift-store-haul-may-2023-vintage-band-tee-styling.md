### Styling Tips Received
For vintage band t-shirts:
- Pair with modern pieces for contrast
- Try with distressed denim, flowy skirts, or leather jacket
- Layer with denim jacket, leather jacket, or cardigan
- Use accessories to bring outfit into present day
- Tuck into high-waisted pants or skirt

For high-waisted distressed jeans:
- Can cuff/roll hem to show shoes
- Tuck or untuck top for different vibes
- Pair with fitted tops or cropped jacket
- Add belt to accentuate waist
- Layer with denim or leather jacket
