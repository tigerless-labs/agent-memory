## Snake Plant Propagation Project

**Date started:** May 21, 2023

**Background:** User has had significant past success propagating snake plants (Sansevieria Trifasciata)

**Status:** Active project — planning to propagate from existing healthy plant

**Propagation methods:**

### Method 1: Leaf Cuttings
1. Choose mature, healthy leaf with long stem (petiole)
2. Cut leaf 1-2 inches from base using sharp, clean knife
3. Remove lower leaves, leaving top 2-3 inches
4. Root in water or well-draining potting mix
5. Roots develop within 1-2 weeks
6. Transplant to larger pot once rooted

### Method 2: Rhizome Division
1. Dig up mature snake plant with well-developed rhizome
2. Separate rhizome into sections (each must have at least one growing point/eye)
3. Replant divided sections in individual pots with well-draining potting mix
4. Water thoroughly

**Optimal conditions:**
- Bright, indirect light
- Temperature: 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- Water sparingly until roots develop (prevent root rot)
- Can propagate any time of year; spring/summer optimal for growth

**Key tips:**
- Make clean cuts with sterile tools
- Keep area bright and consistently warm
- Avoid overwatering during rooting stage
- Take multiple cuttings to increase success rate
