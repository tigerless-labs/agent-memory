## Chapter 15: Rule #6 - Crawl Before You Walk, Walk Before You Run

### Core Principles
- Essence: lifelong behavioral change via movement pattern retraining and progressive exercise
- Progressive overload: master each level before advancing
- Daily exercises (primary tool for permanent pain reduction)
- "Be still to heal": minimize lumbar spine movement during activities
- Cannot rush healing; follow its natural rhythm

### Progression Sequence
1. **Marching in place** (neutral spine, no lumbar movement) — baseline
2. **Walking with neutral spine** (weight-bearing, glute/lat engagement)
3. **Hip-hinging** (bend forward using hip hinge axis, protected neutral spine)
4. **Full-body rotation** (chest, navel, hips move together; no waist/spine movement)
5. **Torso rotation + hip-hinge + squat** (combined 3D movement for daily tasks)

### Walking Mechanics (Step-by-Step)
- Step 1: Good posture (tall, shoulders back, head centered, chest out, pelvis neutral)
- Step 2: Core locks neutral spine in place
- Step 3: Maintain lower-back position; arm swing from shoulders (opposite arm to leg)
- Step 4: Take sitting breaks when pain threatens; gradually extend walking duration

### Key Failures & Fixes
- **Knee pain**: Pivot feet lightly, increase squat depth at bottom
- **Back pain**: Verify hip-hinge, rotation, and squat individually; master each before combining; use mirror feedback; increase range gradually
