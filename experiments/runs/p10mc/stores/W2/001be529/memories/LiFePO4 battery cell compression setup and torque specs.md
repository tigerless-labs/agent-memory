## Battery Cell Specifications
- Type: LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate)
- Dimensions: 205mm (height) × 174mm (width) × 72mm (thickness)
- Shape: Rectangular cube

## Compression Fixture Design
- **Compression plates:** Two steel plates, each 205mm × 174mm
- **Fasteners:** Four Grade 5 steel 1/4" threaded rods (UNC, 20 TPI)
  - One rod at each corner of the plates
  - Rods run straight through the cell, connecting opposite plates
- **Total compression force target:** 300 kgf

## Force Distribution & Calculations
- **Force per rod:** 75 kgf (300 kgf ÷ 4 rods)
- **Force per rod adjusted for two-face compression:** 37.5 kgf per rod
- **Total force (in Newtons):** 1,471.5 N (4 × 367.875 N)
- **Compression area:** 35,670 mm² (205mm × 174mm)
- **Pressure on each face:** ~41,234.5 Pa (Pascals)

## Thread Specifications (1/4" UNC)
- Pitch: 0.05 inches
- Threads per inch (TPI): 20
- Mechanical advantage: ~15.7

## Required Torque
- **Target torque per rod:** ~17.27 ft-lbs
- Conversion factor used: 1 Nm = 0.73756 ft-lbs
- Note: Calculation assumes no friction losses and perfect force transmission; actual application should account for friction and use proper torque wrenches with appropriate lubrication
