## Recommended Shenandoah Hikes
- Old Rag: 9 miles, challenging, beautiful views and rock formations
- Hawksbill Mountain: 2.9 miles, tallest peak in park, stunning views
- Dark Hollow Falls: 1.4 miles, waterfall, summer swimming
- Marys Rock: 3.7 miles, Shenandoah Valley views
- Stony Man: 1.6 miles, easy, second-highest peak, panoramic views
