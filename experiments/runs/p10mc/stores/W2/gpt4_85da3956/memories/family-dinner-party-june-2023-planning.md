## Dinner Party Plan — June 24-25, 2023

**Event Details:**
- 6-8 family members
- Weekend of June 24-25, 2023

**Menu Decisions:**
- **Appetizers (confirmed):** Bruschetta (toasted baguette with tomatoes, garlic, basil, mozzarella, balsamic glaze) and Spinach & Artichoke Dip (with tortilla chips or pita)
- **Drinks before main:** Sparkling water with lemon or flavored seltzer (low-calorie alternative to wine)
- **Main course options discussed:** Lasagna, Grilled Chicken Fajitas, Baked Salmon with Lemon and Herbs, One-Pot Pasta
- **Dessert options:** Fruit Salad, Brownies/Cookies, Cheesecake Bars

**Atmosphere & Setup:**
- **Music:** Playlist with acoustic and soft rock songs (artists: Jack Johnson, Jason Mraz, Ed Sheeran, James Taylor, Fleetwood Mac, The Eagles); kept at low volume for conversation
- **Lighting:** Warm lighting with candles, string lights, or table lamps
- **Table setting:** Tablecloth/runner, simple centerpiece (fresh flowers, candle, or fruit bowl), placemats, napkin rings; keep simple and uncluttered
- **Decorations:** Fresh greenery/flowers, photo display of family and friends, optional dessert table

**Wine Pairing Notes (if selected):**
- Lasagna: Chianti, Valpolicella, or Pinot Grigio
- Grilled Chicken Fajitas: Sauvignon Blanc, Grenache Rosé, or Tempranillo
- Baked Salmon: Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio, or Gewürztraminer
- One-Pot Pasta: Chianti, Montepulciano, or Pinot Grigio

**Tips Applied:**
- Buffet-style serving considered for ease
- Ask guests about dietary restrictions in advance
- Prep dishes ahead of time (lasagna, roasted vegetables)
- Keep volume low so conversation can flow
- Offer non-alcoholic options for all guests
