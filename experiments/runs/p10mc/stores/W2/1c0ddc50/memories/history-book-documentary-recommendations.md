## Books

### Hardcore History-inspired
- **The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich** by William L. Shirer - comprehensive history of Nazi Germany
- **Guns, Germs, and Steel** by Jared Diamond - Pulitzer Prize winner exploring how geography, technology, and biology shaped human societies
- **The Devil in the White City** by Erik Larson - gripping account of 1893 World's Fair in Chicago and serial killer H.H. Holmes

### Lore-inspired
- **The Witches: Salem, 1692** by Stacy Schiff - Pulitzer Prize winner about the Salem witch trials
- **The Devil's Gentleman** by Harold Schechter - lesser-known true crime story from early 20th century
- **The Ghost Map** by Steven Johnson - narrative about 1854 cholera outbreak in London

### The Dollop-inspired
- **A People's History of the United States** by Howard Zinn - non-traditional, bottom-up approach to American history
- **The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks** by Rebecca Skloot - story about HeLa cells and their impact on medicine and science
- **Killers of the Flower Moon** by David Grann - true crime story about Osage murders and birth of FBI

## Documentaries

### Hardcore History-inspired
- **The World at War** (1973) - classic documentary series covering World War II from multiple perspectives
- **The Apocalypse** (2000) - documentary series exploring history of the Mongols and their impact on the world
- **Death and the Civil War** (2002) - PBS documentary about how American Civil War changed understanding of death and mourning

### Lore-inspired
- **The Salem Witch Trials** (2002) - History Channel documentary exploring the Salem witch trials and their significance
- **The Real Wolfman** (2012) - Smithsonian Channel documentary examining the true story behind the werewolf legend
- **The Dark Ages** (2007) - History Channel documentary series on European history from fall of Roman Empire to Renaissance

### The Dollop-inspired
- **The Untold History of the United States** (2012) - documentary series offering alternative perspective on American history
- **The True Story of the Bridge on the River Kwai** (2001) - History Channel documentary on true events behind famous film
- **The Men Who Built America** (2012) - History Channel documentary series on influential American entrepreneurs and inventors
