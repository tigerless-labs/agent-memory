Recommended snack options to combat the 2:00 pm energy dip:
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, pumpkin seeds, chia seeds) — high in healthy fats and protein
- Fresh fruit (apples, bananas, berries) — fiber, vitamins, antioxidants
- Homemade energy balls — rolled oats, nuts, seeds, dried fruit
- Protein-rich snacks — Greek yogurt, hard-boiled eggs, cottage cheese
- Whole grain crackers with avocado or peanut butter
- Edamame — high protein and fiber
- Trail mix — nuts, seeds, dried fruit
- Dark chocolate (70%+ cocoa) — natural caffeine and flavonoids
- Apple slices with almond butter
- Smoothies — frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, nuts/seeds

Key principle: Choose nutrient-dense, low-sugar options with whole grains, fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and healthy fats.
