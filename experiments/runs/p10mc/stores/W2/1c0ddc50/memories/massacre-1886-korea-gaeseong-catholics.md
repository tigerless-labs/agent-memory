## 1886 Massacre in Gaeseong, Korea

**Event:** Massacre of hundreds of Korean Catholics in the city of Gaeseong.

**Context:** Occurred during period of growing Western influence and Catholic missionary activity in Korea during 19th century.

**Cause:** Korean government saw Catholicism as threat to traditional Confucian order and as method for Western powers to gain foothold in country. Government determined to suppress religious expression and resist foreign influence.

**Key Figures:**
- **Father Thomas Hur** — French priest instrumental in spreading Catholicism in Korea; killed during massacre
- **Kim Ok-gyun** — Prominent intellectual and government official critical of government's handling; exiled from country

**Consequences:**
- Heightened tensions between Korean government and Western powers
- Demonstrated government's power to suppress religious expression
- Had profound impact on spread of Catholicism in Korea
- Remains important part of Korea's history
- Marked turning point in 19th century Korean history

**Context:** Part of broader pattern of violence and persecution against Westerners and Catholics in Korea during 19th century
