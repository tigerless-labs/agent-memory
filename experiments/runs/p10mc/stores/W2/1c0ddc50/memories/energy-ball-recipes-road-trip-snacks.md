## Energy Ball Recipe Recommendations

Five recommended energy ball recipes for road trip preparation. All are easy to make with simple ingredients, customizable to taste, and have long shelf life. Store in airtight container to keep fresh.

### 1. Peanut Butter Banana Bites
Ingredients: rolled oats, peanut butter, mashed banana, honey
Classic combination, good energy source.

### 2. Chocolate Chip Energy Bites
Ingredients: rolled oats, almond butter, chocolate chips, honey
Sweet treat option.

### 3. Coconut Lime Energy Balls
Ingredients: rolled oats, coconut flakes, lime zest, honey
Refreshing flavor combination.

### 4. Cranberry Orange Energy Balls
Ingredients: rolled oats, dried cranberries, orange zest, honey
Fruity and tangy option.

### 5. Nutty Energy Bites
Ingredients: rolled oats, peanut butter, chopped nuts (walnuts or almonds), honey
For nut lovers.

## Selection Criteria
When choosing a recipe, consider:
- Nutrition: balance of complex carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats
- Shelf life: recipes with dried fruits or nuts stay fresher longer
- Texture: mix of crunchy and chewy options keeps snacks interesting
