## History Podcasts

**Hardcore History** by Dan Carlin
- Starting episodes: "The Wrath of the Khans" (ep 1), "The American Peril" (ep 5), "Ghosts of the Ostfront" (ep 10)
- In-depth, engaging, well-researched historical stories

**Lore** by Aaron Mahnke
- Starting episodes: "They Made a Tonic" (ep 1), "The Bloody Pit" (ep 5), "Echoes" (ep 20)
- Dark, mysterious, and fascinating tales from history

**The Dollop** by Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds
- Starting episodes: "The Radium Girls" (ep 255), "The Donner Party" (ep 134), "The Astor Place Riot" (ep 201)
- Comedic, entertaining, and informative looks at weird historical events

## Science Podcasts

**StarTalk Radio** by Neil deGrasse Tyson
- Starting episodes: "The Universe in a Glass of Wine" (ep 1), "The Science of Sports" (ep 4), "A Conversation with Buzz Aldrin" (ep 13)
- Explore the universe, science, and pop culture with humor

**Radiolab** by Jad Abumrad and Robert Krulwich
- Starting episodes: "The Sepulveda" (ep 1), "The Trust Engineers" (ep 13), "The Bad Show" (ep 23)
- Mind-bending stories and conversations about science, philosophy, and human experience

**Stuff You Should Know** by Josh Clark and Chuck Bryant
- Starting episodes: "How the Internet Works" (ep 1), "The Science of Dreams" (ep 23), "The Short History of the Guillotine" (ep 54)
- Engaging and informative explanations of various scientific and technological topics
