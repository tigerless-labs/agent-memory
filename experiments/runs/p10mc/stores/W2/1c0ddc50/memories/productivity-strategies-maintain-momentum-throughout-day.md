User wants to maintain their strong morning productivity (9am-12pm) throughout the entire day. Key recommendations:

1. Prioritize most important tasks during 9am-12pm peak hours
2. Use Pomodoro Technique — 25 min focus + 5 min break, 15-30 min break after 4 cycles
3. Break large tasks into smaller chunks
4. Schedule breaks in advance for recharge
5. Stay hydrated and maintain healthy snack routine
6. Minimize distractions (social media, email during peak hours)
7. Leverage energy peaks — match task difficulty to energy level
8. Take brief outdoor walks for fresh air and sunlight
9. Maintain organized workspace
10. Review and adjust strategy regularly

For afternoon slump specifically: power nap (20-30 min), creative activities, phone calls/meetings, light stretching/exercise.
