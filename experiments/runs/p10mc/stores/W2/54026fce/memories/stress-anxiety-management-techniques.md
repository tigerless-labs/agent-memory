## Stress & Anxiety Management Strategy

**Primary Techniques Being Used:**
1. **Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)** - practicing 2-3x per week
   - Tense muscle groups for 5-7 seconds
   - Release and relax for 10-15 seconds
   - Repeat 3-5 times per group
   - Progress from toes up to face
   - Duration: 10-15 minutes, increasing gradually
   - Benefits noticed: reduced muscle tension, better relaxation

2. **Deep Breathing Exercises**
   - 4-7-8 pattern: inhale 4 sec, hold 7, exhale 8
   - Use during stressful moments

3. **Mindfulness Meditation**
   - Focus on present moment
   - Pay attention to breath and body sensations

4. **Visualization**
   - Imagine peaceful environments (beach, forest)

5. **Yoga & Gentle Stretching**
   - Combination with back pain exercises

**Relaxation Apps (Interested/Using):**
- Headspace (guided sessions, progress tracking)
- Calm (meditation, sleep stories, music)
- Insight Timer (free option, various meditations, sounds)
- Breethe (personalized sessions, breathing exercises)
- Happify (games, meditations, stress management)

**Additional Tools:**
- Essential oils (lavender, chamomile, bergamot)
- Nature sounds (rain, ocean, forest)
- Journaling for processing thoughts
- Grounding techniques (5 senses focus)

**Current Status:**
- Dealing with stress and anxiety related to herniated disc recovery
- Finding PMR particularly effective
- Committed to regular practice and self-care
