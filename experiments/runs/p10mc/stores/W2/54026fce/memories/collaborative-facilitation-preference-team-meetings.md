## Collaborative Facilitation Style

### Preferred Approach
User values collaborative facilitation and wants to ensure all team members feel comfortable sharing their thoughts. Dislikes dominating conversations or forcing ideas on the team.

### Techniques Used
- Ask open-ended questions
- Use non-verbal cues to show active listening
- Avoid interrupting team members
- Paraphrase and summarize to confirm understanding
- Specifically invite quieter team members to contribute
- Take notes during discussions
- Show willingness to adapt ideas based on feedback
- Lead by example with authentic engagement

### Values
- Respect for team autonomy and boundaries
- Creating safe space for diverse perspectives
- Valuing all voices equally
- Flexibility and responsiveness to feedback
- Inclusive decision-making
