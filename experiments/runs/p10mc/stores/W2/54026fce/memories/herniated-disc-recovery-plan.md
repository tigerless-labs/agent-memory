## Herniated Disc Recovery Journey

**Condition & Timeline:**
- Diagnosed with herniated disc, causing back pain
- Planning surgery (date TBD, pending decision)
- Need to consult surgeon/healthcare provider for post-operative guidelines

**Exercise Routine (Current):**
- Stretching: knee-to-chest, pelvic tilt, cat-cow, hamstring stretch
- Strengthening: plank, bridge, bird dog exercises
- Low-impact cardio: cycling, water-based exercises, yoga/Pilates
- Warm up 5-10 min before exercise

**Complementary Therapies:**
- **Acupuncture**: Seeing acupuncturist for pain and stress management; planning to continue post-surgery with doctor clearance; wait 2-4 weeks post-op before resuming
- **Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)**: Practicing 2-3x per week; finding it very helpful for reducing muscle tension and promoting relaxation; using systematic tense/release pattern starting from toes moving up body

**Pain Management:**
- Focus on gentle, low-impact activities
- Listen to body; stop if pain occurs
- Use proper posture during exercises
- Strengthen core muscles
- Balance exercise with rest and recovery

**Educational Resources Used:**
- AAOS (www.aaos.org)
- APTA (www.moveforwardpt.com)
- Mayo Clinic (www.mayoclinic.org)
- Book: "Treat Your Own Back" by Robin McKenzie
- Relaxation apps: Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer, Breethe, Happify

**Goals:**
- Recovery and return to normal activity
- Reduce reliance on pain medication
- Improve overall well-being through exercise and relaxation
