## Healthy Snack Options

**Fresh Fruits and Veggies**
- Baby Carrots with Hummus
- Fresh Fruit Salad (grapes, berries, citrus)
- Cucumber Slices with Guacamole

**Protein-Packed Snacks**
- Hard-Boiled Eggs (prepare batch on weekend)
- Greek Yogurt with Nuts and Berries
- Apple Slices with Peanut Butter

**Whole Grain Options**
- Whole Grain Crackers with Avocado
- Oatmeal Energy Balls (rolled oats, nuts, dried fruits)
- Whole Grain Toast with Banana and Honey

**Granola and Other Options**
- Homemade Trail Mix (nuts, seeds, dried fruits)
- Air-Popped Popcorn
- Dark Chocolate-Dipped Fruit (cranberries, apricots, bananas)

## Portion Control
- Keep snacks to 100-200 calories per serving
- Prepare proteins in batches on weekends for easy grab-and-go access
