## Recommended Cookbook Stands

**Top-Tier Options**
- **OXO Good Grips Cookbook Stand** — sturdy, adjustable, non-slip base
- **KitchenAid Cookbook Stand** — comfortable, durable, adjustable height, soft-grip surface
- **Cuisinart Cookbook Stand** — compact and space-efficient

**Budget-Friendly Options**
- **Amazon Basics Cookbook Stand** — affordable, sturdy, adjustable, non-slip base
- **Umbra Cookbook Stand** — compact, easy to clean, non-slip base

## Key Features to Consider
- Adjustable height to read recipes without neck strain
- Non-slip base to keep cookbook stable
- Page hold or clip to keep page open
- Folding design for compact storage (if counter space is limited)
- Sturdy enough to hold large or heavy cookbooks
