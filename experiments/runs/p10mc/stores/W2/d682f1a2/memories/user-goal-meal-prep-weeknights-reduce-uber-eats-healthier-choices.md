## User Goals — Meal Prep and Healthy Eating

**Primary Motivation**
- Reducing reliance on Uber Eats (currently using for weekends)
- Making healthier food choices
- Saving time with quick weeknight recipes

**Implementation Plans**
1. Start meal prepping for the week using organized meal plans and grocery lists
2. Invest in meal prep containers for storing prepared meals
3. Get a cookbook stand for easier recipe following in the kitchen
4. Organize recipe collection (cookbooks + online recipes) using recipe app
5. Plan meals weekly with grocery list generation

**Recipe Interests**
- Quick and easy weeknight options
- Healthy snacking alternatives
- One-pot meals
- 30-minute meals
- Pantry staples dishes

**Tools Needed**
- Recipe organizing app (considering Paprika, Yummly, BigOven)
- Meal prep containers (glass, stainless steel, or BPA-free plastic)
- Cookbook stand for kitchen counter
