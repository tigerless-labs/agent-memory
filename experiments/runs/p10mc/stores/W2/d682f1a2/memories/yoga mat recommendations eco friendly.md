## Mat Recommendations

1. Lululemon The Mat - high-quality, eco-friendly, excellent grip and cushioning
2. Manduka eKO SuperLite - lightweight, eco-friendly, textured surface
3. Alo Yoga Warrior Mat - thick, cushioned, non-slip surface
4. Gaiam Print Yoga Mat - affordable, eco-friendly, fun design
5. Jade Yoga Mat - high-quality, eco-friendly, natural rubber

## Selection Criteria

- Material: Natural rubber, jute, or recyclable polyurethane (non-toxic, PVC-free)
- Thickness: 4-5mm for cushioning; 1-2mm for portability
- Grip: Textured surface for safety
- Size: Standard 24 inches x 68 inches
- Weight: Light for portability, heavy for stability
