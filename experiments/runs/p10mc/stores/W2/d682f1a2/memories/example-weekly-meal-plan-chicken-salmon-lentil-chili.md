## Example Weekly Meal Plan

**Monday** — Chicken Fajitas with Bell Peppers and Onions

**Tuesday** — Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables and Quinoa

**Wednesday** — Lentil Soup with Crusty Bread

**Thursday** — Grilled Chicken Breasts with Roasted Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

**Friday** — Pizza Night with Homemade Crust and Toppings

**Saturday** — Breakfast for Dinner (Scrambled Eggs, Sausage, Pancakes)

**Sunday** — Slow Cooker Chili with Ground Beef and Vegetables

## Corresponding Grocery List Organization

**Produce**
- Bell peppers, onions, mixed greens, sweet potatoes, green beans, tomatoes, fresh herbs (cilantro, parsley)

**Meat/Protein**
- Boneless skinless chicken breasts, salmon fillets, ground beef, sausage

**Dairy**
- Shredded cheese, Greek yogurt, eggs

**Grains**
- Quinoa, whole wheat bread, pizza crust, pancake mix

**Pantry**
- Canned black beans, canned diced tomatoes, olive oil, spices (cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper), baking supplies

## Meal Planning Tips
- Plan for leftovers and use creatively
- Consider meal prepping or cooking in bulk
- Swap ingredients based on preferences
- Use cashback apps or digital coupons to save money
