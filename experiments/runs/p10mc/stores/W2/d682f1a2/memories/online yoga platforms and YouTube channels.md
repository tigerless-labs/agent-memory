## Online Platforms

- YogaGlo - wide range of beginner classes (Hatha, Vinyasa, Restorative)
- DoYouYoga - beginner focus (Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin yoga)
- Gaia - variety of classes, meditation, yoga philosophy
- Peloton Digital - cycling and yoga classes including beginner flows

## YouTube Channels

1. Yoga With Adriene - gentle beginner-friendly, Hatha and Vinyasa
2. Dylan Werner Yoga - focus on alignment, breathwork, mindfulness
3. Sarah Beth Yoga - gentle, slow-paced, Hatha and Restorative
4. Fightmaster Yoga (Candace) - challenging but accessible, Vinyasa and Power yoga
5. Yoga Girl (Rachel Brice) - inspiring, gentle, Hatha and Vinyasa

## Tips for Online Classes

- Start with shorter classes (20-30 minutes)
- Focus on alignment and instructor cues
- Use props for modifications (blocks, straps, blankets)
- Find consistent instructor that resonates
- Supplement, don't replace in-person classes
