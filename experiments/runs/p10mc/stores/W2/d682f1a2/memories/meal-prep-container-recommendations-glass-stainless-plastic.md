## Recommended Meal Prep Container Options

**Glass Containers** — non-toxic, eco-friendly, work for hot/cold foods
- Pyrex
- Anchor Hocking
- OXO

**Stainless Steel** — durable, rust-resistant, keeps food hot/cold for hours
- S'well
- Hydro Flask
- Klean Kanteen

**BPA-Free Plastic** — lightweight and affordable (Tritan or Polypropylene materials)
- Rubbermaid
- Glad
- Ziploc

**Budget-Friendly Alternatives**
- Mason jars
- Reused takeout containers (sanitized)

## Key Features to Look For
- Airtight seals to keep food fresh and prevent spills
- Stackability to save fridge space
- Microwave-safe if reheating
- Dishwasher-safe for easy cleaning
- Portion control markings or dividers
