## Project: Living Room Workspace & Reading Nook

**Status:** Planning phase

### Space Details
- Recently tidied living room
- Large windows providing bright natural light
- Planning to add workspace/reading nook area

### Planned Elements
1. **Desk/Console Table**
   - Compact, stylish option
   - Positioned near the big window
   - Looking for narrow, minimalist design
   - Considering: wall-mounted, drop-leaf, or narrow console options
   - Multi-functional (storage preferred)

2. **Circular Rug**
   - Purpose: Define workspace, add warmth
   - Planned size: 3-4 feet diameter
   - Looking for durable, low-maintenance material (wool, jute, or synthetic)
   - Color: Neutral or complementary to room palette
   - Thickness: ~1/4 inch

3. **Circular Mirror**
   - Purpose: Add visual interest, make space feel larger
   - Planned size: 12-18 inches diameter
   - Will hang above desk/console table
   - Hang height: 57-60 inches from floor

4. **Artwork/Prints**
   - Multiple pieces to add personality
   - Size: 1/3 to 2/3 width of desk/table
   - Color palette should complement room
   - Considering styles: botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, pop art
   - Budget-conscious (prints and reproductions)

5. **Plants**
   - Low-maintenance species preferred
   - Can tolerate bright indirect light (big windows)
   - Considering: snake plant, ZZ plant, spider plant, pothos, succulents, dracaena, peace lily, bromeliads

- IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, Amazon
- Art prints: Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, Saatchi Art
