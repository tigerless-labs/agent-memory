## Streaming Service Preferences

**Date:** May 20, 2023  
**Key Preference:** Streaming services over traditional cable TV

### Usage Pattern
- Hasn't watched cable TV in months
- Primary entertainment: streaming services

### Preferred Streaming Platforms
- Netflix
- Amazon Prime
- Hulu

### Why Streaming Over Cable
1. **Variety** — Much wider variety of content than traditional TV
2. **No Commercials** — Ad-free viewing experience; no repetitive or time-consuming ads
3. **Continuity** — Can pick up shows right where they left off, even after days of not watching
4. **Content Library** — Access to original programming, international shows, classic movies, and previously aired episodes not available on traditional TV
5. **Mobile/Multi-device** — Can watch on phones and tablets while on the go; accessible anywhere with internet
6. **Binge-watch Model** — Entire seasons released at once rather than waiting a week between episodes
7. **User Control** — Can pause, rewind, fast forward as desired; watches at own pace
8. **Personalization** — Streaming services offer recommendations based on viewing history

### Context
Discussion occurred during broader conversation about how streaming services have impacted traditional television networks, leading to decline in cable/satellite subscriptions and forcing traditional networks to launch their own streaming services (CBS All Access, Peacock).
