## Jesse Owens — 1936 Olympics

**Historical achievement:**
- First African-American to win Olympic gold medal in the 200m sprint
- Achieved this at the 1936 Berlin Olympics
- Won 4 gold medals total at the 1936 Olympics (100m, 200m, long jump, 4x100m relay)

**Historical context:**
- Competed during Adolf Hitler's attempt to use the 1936 Berlin Olympics as a platform to showcase Nazi ideology of Aryan supremacy
- Owens' victories directly challenged and undermined these racist narratives

**Personal resilience:**
- Demonstrated exceptional mental toughness and composure under intense racism and discrimination
- Had strong support system: family, friends, community, and coach who provided guidance
- Was a devout Christian; his faith provided strength and motivation during adversity
- Never lost sight of his goals despite facing numerous obstacles

**Historical significance:**
- Paved the way for future generations of athletes
- Became an icon of civil rights and symbol of fight against racism
- Proved that dedication, talent, and hard work can overcome significant barriers
