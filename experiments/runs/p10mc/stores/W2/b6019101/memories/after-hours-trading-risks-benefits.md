## After-Hours Trading: Risks and Benefits

### Why Use After-Hours Trading

1. News or market events - Earnings reports, M&A announcements after market close may warrant immediate action
   - Example: Merger announced after close; stock may spike at open; investors can pre-position orders

2. Economic/political events - Data releases outside regular hours

3. Limited availability - Investors in different time zones or with scheduling constraints

### Key Risks vs Regular Trading Hours

1. Volatility - Fewer market participants and lower liquidity mean small orders significantly impact price. Wider bid-ask spreads result in unfavorable prices.

2. Limited information - Fewer buyers/sellers; harder to make informed decisions or execute at desired price.

3. Gapping risk - Stock may open at significantly different price from expected; unfavorable order execution possible.

4. Order execution delays - Lower liquidity may mean orders not filled, or filled much later than expected.

5. Higher fees - Some brokers charge more for after-hours trading or impose minimum balance requirements.

### Choosing a Broker for After-Hours Trading

- Confirm broker offers after-hours trading for securities of interest
- Compare trading fees and minimum balance requirements
- Evaluate platform: ease of use, mobile app, research tools, market data
- Ensure quality customer support available during after-hours
- Read reviews and research thoroughly before deciding
