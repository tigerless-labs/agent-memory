## Stock Order Types

1. Market Order - Buy/sell at current market price (simplest, most common)
2. Limit Order - Buy/sell at specific price equal to or better than market price
3. Stop Order - Sell when stock reaches trigger price below current market (safeguard against losses)
4. Stop-Limit Order - Stop order with price limit once stock hits trigger
5. Trailing Stop Order - Dynamic stop that adjusts with stock price movements (percent or dollar amount)
6. After-Hours Trading Order - Placed outside regular hours (9:30 AM-4:00 PM EST), executed when market opens next day

Selection depends on investment goals and risk tolerance.
