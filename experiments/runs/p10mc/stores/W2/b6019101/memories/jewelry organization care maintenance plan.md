## Jewelry Organization and Care Plan

### Organization Strategy
- **Designate jewelry station:** Specific place to keep jewelry when not wearing
- **Use jewelry organizer:** Compartments to prevent tangling and loss
- **Put jewelry on last:** When getting ready to avoid misplacing pieces
- **Create launching pad:** Small tray near front door for pieces being removed
- **Photo inventory:** Take photos of jewelry collection for identification and documentation

### Silver Jewelry Care
- Use silver polishing cloth to remove tarnish and maintain shine
- Avoid bleach and ammonia (damages silver)
- Store in cool, dry place to prevent tarnishing
- Keep separate to prevent scratching or tangling

### General Maintenance
- Dry thoroughly after cleaning to prevent water spots
- Store each piece separately
- Avoid extreme temperatures
- Check regularly for loose settings or prongs
- Consider professional cleaning for delicate pieces
- Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials
- Use soft, lint-free cloth to wipe after wearing
- Use gentle cleaning solutions (soap and warm water or jewelry cleaner)
- Avoid ultrasonic cleaners for gemstones or pearls

### Additional Protection
- Consider jewelry box with lock for valuable/sentimental pieces
- Keep special pieces in designated travel case when traveling
- Document collection with photos and detailed descriptions
