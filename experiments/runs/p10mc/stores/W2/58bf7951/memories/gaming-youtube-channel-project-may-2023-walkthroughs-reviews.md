## Gaming YouTube Channel Project (May 2023)

### Project Setup

- **Start date:** May 2023
- **Content type:** Walkthroughs and game reviews
- **Platform:** YouTube (new account created)
- **Target game:** Horizon Zero Dawn (first gameplay footage)

### Learning Path

- Watching tutorials on video editing
- Studying gameplay recording techniques
- Goal: High-quality, consistent content

### Recording Equipment & Software

**Screen recording software options (comparing):**
- OBS Studio (free, highly customizable) — **recommended for starting**
- ShadowPlay (free for NVIDIA GPU owners, great for this PC)
- XSplit (popular, feature-rich)
- Bandicam (user-friendly, free trial)
- Camtasia (paid, advanced features + built-in editing)

**Audio equipment to acquire:**
- Quality USB microphone (examples: Blue Yeti, HyperX QuadCast Gaming Microphone)
- Pop filter (reduce plosive sounds)

**Optional:**
- Webcam (for facecam footage)

### Recording Settings & Targets

- **Target resolution:** 1080p (1920×1080)
- **Target frame rate:** 60fps
- **Bitrate/codec:** To be optimized per test runs
- **Audio:** Clear, crisp quality with noise reduction

### Video Editing Software Under Consideration

- Adobe Premiere Pro
- Final Cut Pro
- DaVinci Resolve

### Content Strategy

- **Differentiation:** Unique perspective, authentic enjoyment
- **Content focus options:** Combat strategies, exploration, puzzle-solving, or general walkthroughs
- **Engagement:** Respond to comments, ask for feedback, plan content in advance
- **Thumbnails & titles:** Planned with thumbnails and SEO optimization
- **Consistency:** High-quality content produced regularly

### Technical Optimization

- Close unnecessary programs during recording
- Adjust graphics settings for optimal balance between quality and performance
- Test record before full recordings
- Monitor PC performance during recording (CPU, GPU, RAM)

### Content Calendar Considerations

- Horizon Zero Dawn: First major title (planning-stage)
- Potential follow-up: Other narrative-driven games from PC library
