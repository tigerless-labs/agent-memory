Toulouse is a major aerospace center with leading companies: Airbus, ATR, and Liebherr.

**Recent developments:**
- Airbus transferred A320 wing production to Toulouse site (2010s)
- Significant expansion of aircraft production and supplier industries
- Engine manufacturing, parts supply, and associated services growth

**Economic impact:**
- Thousands of skilled jobs in aerospace and related fields (logistics, transport, hospitality)
- Major contributor to local and regional GDP
- Attracts foreign investment from US, Asia, Middle East

**Future innovation:**
- Airbus developing hydrogen-powered aircraft
- Research into electric propulsion systems and lightweight materials
- Sustainable aviation focus

**Policy support:**
- French government "Plan Aéro" initiative: €15 billion investment over three years, launched June 2020
- Positions Toulouse as leader in aerospace sector and innovation
