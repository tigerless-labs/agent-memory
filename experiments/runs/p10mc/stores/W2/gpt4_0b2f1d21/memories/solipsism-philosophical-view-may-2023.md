User inquiry: "What is the philosophical view that the only person you trust is conscious is yourself?"

Answer: **Solipsism**

Definition: Solipsism is the philosophical position that only one's own mind is certain to exist. All external realities—including other people, objects, and their consciousness—are uncertain and may not exist. They could be illusions or figments of one's own imagination.

Key concept: The only mind/consciousness you can truly trust to exist is your own.

This reflects an interest in epistemology and philosophy of mind.
