## Music Festival Attendance

### Festival Experience Level
- Regular attendee at music festivals
- Planning to attend upcoming festivals soon
- Values safety and organization while enjoying the experience

### Safety Practices

The user is mindful of belongings safety at festivals and implements these practices:
- Keeping valuables secure with pouches or wallets with zippers
- Labeling bag with luggage tag or contact information for recovery
- Keeping essentials in separate pouch (ID, cash, credit card) in case main bag lost
- Keeping bag in front and zipped in crowded areas
- Staying mindful of surroundings, especially in crowded areas (stages, food vendors, bathrooms)
- Avoiding leaving bag unattended or out of sight
- Using portable charger to stay connected and contact authorities if needed
- Designating meeting spots with group if attending with others
- Storing bag securely at night (in tent, with trusted friend, or at bag-check area)

### Packing Strategy

Essential items for festivals:
- Fjallraven cross-body bag (primary carry)
- Phone with secure attachment (phone strap or lanyard)
- Wallet or small valuables pouch
- Reusable water bottle
- Portable charger
- Sunscreen (travel-sized in pouch)
- Hat
- Temporary ID/contact information tag

### Organization Approach

- Using small pouches to keep items organized and prevent loss
- Rolling clothes to save space
- Packing multipurpose items
- Keeping small trash bag to collect waste
