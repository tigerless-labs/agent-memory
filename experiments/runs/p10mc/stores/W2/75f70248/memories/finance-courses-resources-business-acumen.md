## Business Acumen Courses
1. **Coursera** - Financial Markets by Yale University (covers basics of financial markets, instruments, institutions)
2. **edX** - Business Foundations by University of Pennsylvania (business principles, finance, accounting, marketing)
3. **LinkedIn Learning** - Business Skills platform (wide range on finance, strategy, leadership)
4. **Harvard Business Review** - Online Courses and Certifications (finance, strategy, innovation)

## Domain Expertise in Finance
1. **CFA Institute** - Online Learning (Chartered Financial Analyst program and various courses)
2. **Financial Times** - Masterclasses (investment, risk management, financial markets)
3. **Wall Street Prep** - Financial Modeling (financial modeling, investment banking, private equity)
4. **Global Association of Risk Professionals (GARP)** - Online Courses (risk management, credit risk, operational risk)

## Industry Insights and News Sources
- The Financial Times (global financial news and analysis)
- Bloomberg (financial news, data, analysis, articles, videos, podcasts)
- The Economist (global news, politics, business, finance, economics)
- Khan Academy - Finance and Capital Markets (free online courses)

## Podcasts
1. The Finance Industry Podcast (interviews with finance professionals)
2. Planet Money (NPR podcast on economics, finance, business)
3. The Financial Diet (personal finance, investing, career development)
4. The Motley Fool's Money Podcast (investing, finance, business)

## Recommended Books
1. "A Random Walk Down Wall Street" by Burton G. Malkiel (investing and stock market introduction)
2. "The Intelligent Investor" by Benjamin Graham (value investing and wealth creation)
3. "The Essays of Warren Buffett: Lessons for Corporate America" edited by Lawrence Cunningham (investor insights)
4. "Flash Boys: A Wall Street Revolt" by Michael Lewis (high-frequency trading and market structure)

## Financial Modeling Resources
1. **Wall Street Prep** - Financial Modeling (Excel skills, forecasting, valuation)
2. **Coursera** - Financial Modeling by University of Michigan
3. **edX** - Financial Modeling and Forecasting by New York Institute of Finance
4. **LinkedIn Learning** - Financial Modeling (Excel skills, forecasting, data analysis)

## Recommended Books on Financial Modeling
1. "Financial Modeling and Forecasting" by John Tjia (comprehensive guide)
2. "Financial Modeling for Business Owners and Entrepreneurs" by Tom Y. Sawyer (practical guide)
3. "Forecasting: Principles and Practice" by Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos (forecasting and time series analysis)
