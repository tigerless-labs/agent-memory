## CFM Certification Overview
The Certified Financial Modeler (CFM) Certification is offered by the Financial Modeling Institute (FMI), founded in 2015. It validates expertise in financial modeling.

## Eligibility Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in relevant field
- 2+ years work experience in finance or related field
- Strong understanding of financial modeling concepts

## Exam Details
- Format: 4-hour, 120-question exam
- Content areas: Financial Modeling Fundamentals, Modeling Techniques, Model Building, Financial Analysis, Model Review and Audit
- Pass requirement: Must pass to receive Certified Financial Modeler designation

## Fees
- Application Fee: $200
- Exam Fee: $1,200
- Annual Maintenance Fee: $200

## Benefits
- Demonstrates expertise in financial modeling
- Enhances career prospects and job opportunities
- Develops advanced financial modeling skills
- Access to community of certified professionals
- Requires ongoing professional development to maintain certification
- Includes adherence to FMI code of ethics

## FMI Mission
The Financial Modeling Institute promotes excellence in financial modeling by establishing standards, best practices, and certifications. Founded to elevate financial modeling practice through standardized approaches.
