In May 2023, the user decided to adopt Goodreads as their primary platform for tracking books they want to read. They plan to:
- Create virtual bookshelves (Want to Read, Currently Reading, Read)
- Import friends from Facebook, especially from Book Lovers Unite
- Join Goodreads groups to connect with like-minded readers
- Track reading progress and set reading goals
- Use the "Currently Reading" shelf to track multiple books in progress

They were initially choosing between Goodreads, Amazon Wish List, library apps, Libib, Shelf, and Litsy, but selected Goodreads based on recommendations from friends in Book Lovers Unite.
