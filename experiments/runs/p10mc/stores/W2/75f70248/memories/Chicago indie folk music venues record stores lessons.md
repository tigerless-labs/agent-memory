## Chicago Music Venues (Indie Folk Focus)

- The Hideout (Logan Square) — hosts weekly Tuesday open mics
- The Whistler (Logan Square) — Wednesday singer-songwriter showcase
- Schubas Tavern (Lakeview) — indie folk/rock shows; outdoor concert series
- The Empty Bottle (Ukrainian Village) — indie rock, folk, electronic acts
- Lincoln Hall (Lincoln Park) — mid-sized indie folk and rock venue
- Uncommon Ground (Wrigleyville) — coffee shop with open mic nights
- The Elbo Room (Lakeview) — open mics and singer-songwriter showcases
- The Riviera Theatre, United Center, The Vic Theatre

## Chicago Music Festivals

- **Chicago Folk Festival** (June 11-13) — free festival in Grant Park
- **Windy City Smokeout** (July 16-18) — country/folk/Americana at United Center
- **Taste of Randolph** (June 18-20) — West Loop street festival with indie/folk acts
- **Lollapalooza** (July 29-August 1) — features indie folk alongside other genres

## Record Stores (Vinyl - Indie Folk/Singer-Songwriter Focus)

- **Reckless Records** (Wicker Park, Lakeview, Logan Square) — extensive new/used vinyl, indie folk section
- **Permanent Records** (Ukrainian Village) — vast selection, indie/folk/singer-songwriter focus
- **Laurie's Planet of Sound** (Lincoln Square) — curated indie folk and singer-songwriter vinyl
- **Saki** (Logan Square) — record store/art gallery with indie folk selection
- **The Exchange** (Lakeview) — used vinyl store with indie folk albums
- **Crossroads Music** (Avondale) — large used vinyl selection, indie/folk focus
- **Dave's Records** (Lincoln Square) — used vinyl with indie folk
- **The Vinyl Conflict** (Logan Square) — punk/indie/underground with some indie folk
- **Bric-a-Brac Records** (Avondale) — record store/vintage shop with indie folk vinyl
- Chicago Music Exchange (online retailer)

## Guitar Lessons & Music Instruction (Indie Folk Focus)

- **Old Town School of Folk Music** (Lincoln Square) — wide range of folk/acoustic guitar classes
- **The Music Gallery** (Lakeview) — private lessons, indie and folk focus
- **The Guitar Workshop** (Logan Square) — indie/folk/rock focus
- **Fretboard Music** (Avondale) — folk, rock, indie lessons
- **The Music Room** (Ravenswood) — folk/indie/acoustic focus
- **Chicago Guitar Lessons** (multiple locations)
- **Folk Guitar Lessons Chicago** (North Center) — indie/folk specific
- Columbia College Chicago and DePaul University (degree/course programs)
