User is planning to acquire either a spider plant or snake plant for their living room to help purify air and manage dust/pet dander.

Plan:
- Get either spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum) or snake plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)
- Use a planter with drainage holes in the bottom
- Use well-draining potting mix specifically designed for indoor plants
- Interested in understanding fertilization schedules and best practices

Key learnings:
- Both plants are low-maintenance and excellent for beginners
- Good drainage is critical to prevent overwatering and root rot
- Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) once monthly at half-strength with balanced fertilizer
- Can skip fertilizing in dormant season (fall/winter)
