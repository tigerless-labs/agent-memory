## Brown Fjallraven Cross-Body Bag

The user owns a brown Fjallraven cross-body bag that serves as their go-to carry bag for music festivals and general use. It's reliable and comfortable for carrying phone, wallet, and water bottle.

### Using at Music Festivals

Planning to pack the following essentials for upcoming music festivals:
- Travel-sized sunscreen (in small pouch to prevent leakage)
- Hat (can be folded/rolled to save space)
- Portable charger (compact size, stored in designated pocket)
- Phone, wallet, water bottle (regular items)

### Organization Strategy

- Decluttering to carry only essentials
- Using small pouches or bags to store small items
- Assigning a "home" for each item in specific compartments
- Keeping front pocket for quick access items
- Utilizing water bottle pocket if available
- Regular cleaning to maintain bag condition

### Cleaning and Maintenance

- Wipe exterior regularly with soft dry cloth
- Spot clean stains with appropriate cleaning product
- Air out occasionally to prevent moisture buildup
