## #Endgame Token Project

**Date**: May 22, 2023

### Treasury Management
- Uses calculated sells and buys to maintain steady growth
- Re-balances validator investments to generate consistent buybacks
- Buys back tokens for appreciation or burns them to maintain sustainable growth rate

### Referral System
- 7-year program
- Referred users receive discount for 7 years on all buys and sells
- Referrer receives rewards for any buys/sells the referred friend makes for 7 years
- Non-referral users pay full price and taxes
- dApp URL: dapp.endgame.black

### Airdrop Raffles
- Occasional airdrops for certain activities or milestones within Endgame
- Prizes include reward tokens
- Keeps project fresh and engaging for newcomers

### Sell Tax Breakdown: 20% Base (with Referral Discounts)

**Full breakdown:**
- Base: 20% sell tax
- Referral system discount: -10% (if using referral)

**Allocation with referral discounts:**
- 3% → Reward token reflection (-1% if referred → 2% if referred)
- 14% → Liquidity, RFV, Treasury, #Endgame Champion, Algorithmic Chart Management System, and Burning System (-8% if referred → 6% if referred)
- 2% → Battle Royal Champion System (-1% if referred → 1% if referred)
- 1% → Rebaser wallet

### Dynamic Whale Tax
- 1% to 10% of transaction allocated towards purchasing reward tokens
- Designed to incentivize larger transactions

### Long-term Vision: Approaching Zero Taxes
- Ultimate goal: minimal or zero tax structure
- Early stage requires funds to build infrastructure
- Tax adjustments will be made over time in collaboration with community
