## Portfolio Manager Role
**Primary Goal**: Maximize returns while minimizing risk

**Key Responsibilities**:
1. Portfolio Construction - selecting securities and asset allocation
2. Risk Management - monitoring and managing risk exposure
3. Performance Measurement - tracking and analyzing portfolio performance
4. Research and Analysis - researching markets, economies, securities
5. Client Communication - providing regular updates and investment strategies
6. Team Management - leading and managing investment professionals

## Financial Analyst Role
**Primary Goal**: Provide insights and recommendations for business decisions

**Key Responsibilities**:
1. Financial Analysis - analyzing financial statements and data
2. Forecasting and Modeling - developing financial models and forecasts
3. Reporting and Visualization - creating reports and visualizations
4. Budgeting and Planning - participating in budgeting and planning processes
5. Ad Hoc Analysis - performing analysis to support specific business decisions

## Key Differences
- **Focus**: Portfolio Managers manage investment portfolios; Financial Analysts analyze financial data
- **Responsibility**: Portfolio Managers make investment decisions; Analysts provide recommendations
- **Scope**: Portfolio Managers work with multiple portfolios/clients; Analysts focus on specific business units
- **Skills Required**: Portfolio Managers need investment knowledge, risk management, client communication; Analysts need strong analytical skills and attention to detail
