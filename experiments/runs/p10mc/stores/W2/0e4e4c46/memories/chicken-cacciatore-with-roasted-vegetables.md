Plans to make Chicken Cacciatore (Italian-inspired dish with chicken, onions, bell peppers, mushrooms, and tomatoes in a rich tomato sauce) paired with:
- Roasted Broccoli (toss with olive oil, salt, pepper; roast until tender and caramelized)
- Garlic and Herb Roasted Potatoes (thin slices with olive oil, minced garlic, fresh herbs like parsley/rosemary/thyme, salt, pepper)

Tips for crispy roasted potatoes (outside) and tender inside:
- Use high-starch potatoes (Russet or Idaho)
- Cut into thin slices (~1/4 inch thick)
- Soak in cold water for 30+ minutes to remove excess starch; pat dry
- Use high-heat oil (avocado or grapeseed oil)
- Preheat oven to 425°F; heat pan in oven 5-7 minutes before adding potatoes
- Single layer, don't overcrowd
- Flip and rotate after 20-25 minutes
- Check crispiness after 30-35 minutes; continue in 5-10 minute increments as needed
- Optional: broil for 2-3 minutes at the end for extra crispiness
