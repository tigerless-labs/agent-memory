## Gender Equality Policy Changes in Japan

### Timeline of Recent Policies

**2015**: Japanese government introduced a five-year plan to promote gender equality
- Target: increase percentage of women in leadership positions to 30% by 2020
- Included: improving access to childcare, increasing women in science and technology fields

**2018**: Law passed requiring large companies to disclose gender equality efforts
- Must report: percentage of female employees and managers
- Must show: steps taken to close gender pay gaps

**April 2019**: Law introduced requiring both public and private-sector companies with more than 100 employees to conduct annual surveys on employees' experiences with sexual harassment

**September 2020**: Legislation passed to criminalize sexual harassment
- Previously not considered a criminal offense in Japan

## Context
Japan also experienced:
- Women granted voting rights in 1945 (postwar)
- Women now make up over half of all college students
- Shift from manufacturing to service-based economy opened professional opportunities for women
- Gender pay gap remains one of the highest in the world
- Low percentage of women in leadership positions compared to other developed countries
