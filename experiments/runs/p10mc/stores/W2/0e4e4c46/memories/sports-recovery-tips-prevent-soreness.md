Tips to prevent soreness after playing sports (especially basketball):

1. **Warm up and cool down** - Light cardio and stretching before playing; static stretches after to help body recover
2. **Stay hydrated** - Drink plenty of water or sports drinks before, during, and after playing
3. **Stretch and foam roll** - Regular stretching and foam rolling improve flexibility, reduce muscle tension, promote blood flow; focus on muscle groups used during play
4. **Use compression gear** - Compression clothing (tights, sleeves) improve blood flow and reduce muscle oscillation
5. **Ice and heat therapy** - Apply ice for 15-20 minutes after playing to reduce inflammation; use heat later to promote blood flow and relaxation
6. **Rest and recovery** - Allow 1-2 rest days per week between games/intense activities for muscles to repair and rebuild
7. **Proper nutrition** - Balanced diet with protein, complex carbohydrates, and healthy fats to help recovery and muscle rebuilding
8. **Massage and self-myofascial release** - Use massage, lacrosse ball, or tennis ball to release muscle tension
9. **Listen to your body** - If fatigued or experiencing pain, take extra days off or modify activities
10. **Gradually increase intensity** - Avoid sudden changes in intensity/duration; gradually increase activity level to reduce soreness and injury risk

Consult medical professional if experiencing persistent or severe pain.
