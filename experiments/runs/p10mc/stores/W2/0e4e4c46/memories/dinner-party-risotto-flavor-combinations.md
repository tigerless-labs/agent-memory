## Risotto Flavor Combinations for Dinner Parties

Suggested on May 23, 2023. 10 unique flavor combinations to impress dinner guests:

1. **Saffron Risotto with Smoked Salmon and Pickled Fennel** — subtle bitterness of saffron, rich smoked salmon, tangy crunchy pickled fennel
2. **Mushroom and Truffle Risotto with Caramelized Onions and Thyme** — earthy mushrooms, truffle oil, sweet caramelized onions, herbaceous thyme
3. **Lemon and Asparagus Risotto with Parmesan and Prosciutto** — bright citrusy lemon zest, tender asparagus, salty prosciutto, umami Parmesan
4. **Roasted Beet and Goat Cheese Risotto with Walnuts and Fresh Mint** — sweet roasted beets, tangy goat cheese, crunchy walnuts, fresh mint brightness
5. **Spicy Shrimp and Chorizo Risotto with Smoked Paprika and Manchego** — spicy chorizo, succulent shrimp, smoky paprika, nutty Manchego cheese
6. **Butternut Squash and Sage Risotto with Browned Butter and Amaretti** — sweet butternut squash, earthy herbaceous sage, nutty browned butter, crunchy amaretti
7. **Pear and Gorgonzola Risotto with Walnuts and Fresh Thyme** — sweet ripe pears, creamy tangy gorgonzola, crunchy walnuts, fresh thyme
8. **Cauliflower and Turmeric Risotto with Crispy Capers and Pine Nuts** — subtle cauliflower, warm spicy turmeric, crispy capers, crunchy pine nuts
9. **Duck Confit and Cherry Risotto with Port Wine and Fresh Thyme** — rich gamey duck confit, fruity sweet cherries, luxurious velvety port wine, fresh thyme
10. **Pumpkin and Sage Risotto with Amaretti and Parmesan** — comforting pumpkin, earthy herbaceous sage, crunchy amaretti, nutty Parmesan
