## Home Office Minimalist Update

**Project Goal:** Refresh home office with minimalist wall decor

**Chosen Artwork:** 
- **"Soft Blooms" by Rachel Austin**
  - Description: Delicate, watercolor-inspired piece featuring soft pink and white blooms on a creamy background
  - Print quality: High-resolution digital print with archival inks
  - Paper: 100% cotton rag, 300gsm weight
  - Finish: Soft matte (reduces glare)
  - Features: 1-inch white border, signed and dated by artist, ships in protective tube

**Framing Decision:**
- Simple white frame with subtle profile (1-2 inches wide)
- Rationale: Creates clean, minimalist look without distracting from artwork
- Will provide contrast to soft pastel colors while maintaining cohesive aesthetic

**Other Decor Ideas Considered:**
- Simple art prints (black and white, minimalist color schemes)
- Typography (motivational quotes in elegant fonts)
- Floating shelves with curated items
- Mirrors (sleek, minimalist frames)
- Geometric patterns
- Nature-inspired botanical prints
- Wall-mounted planters
- LED light installations

**Online Stores for Art Prints:**
- Society6, Minted, Etsy, Desenio, AllPosters, 20x200, The Poster Club, Art.com, museum collections (MoMA, Guggenheim), independent artist websites
