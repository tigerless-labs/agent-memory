## Cooking Oil Pairings for Risotto

Suggested May 23, 2023 to pair with risotto flavor combinations.

### Oil for Mushroom and Truffle Risotto

**Recommendation:** Avoid truffle oil if already using truffle in the dish — it will be overpowering.

**Better options:**
- **Mushroom-Infused Oil** — adds deep earthy flavor without overpowering truffles
- **Pumpkin Seed Oil** — nutty, slightly sweet flavor pairs well with earthy mushrooms and truffles
- **Hazelnut Oil** — rich nutty flavor adds depth and complexity without overpowering

### Oil Pairings for All Risotto Flavors

1. Saffron Risotto with Smoked Salmon — Avocado Oil or Grapeseed Oil (light, neutral)
2. Mushroom and Truffle — Mushroom Oil, Pumpkin Seed Oil, or Hazelnut Oil
3. Lemon and Asparagus — Lemon-Infused Olive Oil or Blood Orange Oil (citrusy)
4. Roasted Beet and Goat Cheese — Pumpkin Seed Oil or Hazelnut Oil (mild, nutty)
5. Spicy Shrimp and Chorizo — Smoked Olive Oil or Chili-Infused Oil (smoky)
6. Butternut Squash and Sage — Walnut Oil or Pecan Oil (nutty)
7. Pear and Gorgonzola — Grapeseed Oil or Almond Oil (light, fruity)
8. Cauliflower and Turmeric — Canola Oil or Sunflower Oil (neutral)
9. Duck Confit and Cherry — Duck Fat or Bacon Fat (rich, gamey)
10. Pumpkin and Sage — Chili-Infused Oil or Cinnamon-Infused Oil (warm, spicy)
