## Recommended Weekly Strength Training Routine

**Date Recommended:** May 23, 2023

### Monday
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes brisk walking, jogging, or cycling
- Resistance: 3 sets × 10-12 reps each of squats, lunges, push-ups, rows, bicep curls
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes interval training (treadmill or stationary bike, high/moderate intensity alternating)

### Wednesday
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes jumping jacks, high knees, or mountain climbers
- Resistance: 3 sets × 10-12 reps each of deadlifts, bench press, pull-ups, tricep extensions, lateral raises
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes steady-state cardio (elliptical or stair climber)

### Friday
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes dynamic stretching and mobility exercises
- Resistance: 3 sets × 10-12 reps each of step-ups, leg press, dumbbell chest fly, lat pulldowns, shoulder press
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes circuit training (alternating resistance + cardio)

**Key Points:**
- Rest at least 1 day between workouts for muscle recovery
- Choose weights/exercises appropriate for fitness level
- Soreness (DOMS) is normal but not an indicator of progress
- Overtraining can hinder results and increase injury risk
