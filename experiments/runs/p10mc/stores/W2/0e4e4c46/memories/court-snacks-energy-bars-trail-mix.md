For bringing snacks to basketball games:

**Preferred options:**
- Energy bars (looking for affordable options)
- Trail mix (can make homemade mix of nuts, seeds, dried fruits)

**Not preferred:**
- Jerky
- Sandwiches or wraps (too inconvenient to eat during games)

**Affordable energy bar recommendations (as of April 2023):**
- Clif Bar: $1.50-$2.50 per bar
- RXBAR: $2-$3 per bar
- Quest Bar: $2-$3 per bar
- PowerBar: $1.50-$3 per bar

All widely available at sports stores, online retailers, or grocery stores. Can also buy in bulk or variety packs to save money and try different flavors.
