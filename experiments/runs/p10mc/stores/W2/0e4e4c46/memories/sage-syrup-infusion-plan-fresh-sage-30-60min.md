## Sage Syrup Infusion Plan

**User's Decision:** May 23, 2023 — Starting with Sage Syrup first (before Spiced Apple Syrup).

### Sage Syrup Recipe Details

- **Fresh sage leaves** — will use fresh (not dried) for vibrant, bright flavor
- **Infusion time:** 30 minutes to 1 hour (user confirmed this duration)
- **Intended use:** Sage Gimlet or Sage Spritz with Hendrick's gin
- **Storage:** Refrigerated for up to 2 weeks

### General Syrup Infusion Technique

**Fresh vs. Dried:**
- Fresh herbs: vibrant, bright flavor; infuse faster (30 min - 1 hour)
- Dried herbs: more concentrated, more intense flavor; infuse slower (2-3 hours)

**Method:**
1. Combine water and sugar in saucepan, bring to boil
2. Reduce heat, simmer 5-7 minutes until sugar dissolves
3. Remove from heat, add fresh sage leaves
4. Steep for 30 minutes to 1 hour
5. Strain through fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth
6. Store in refrigerator

**Tips:**
- Use higher herb ratio for stronger flavor, lower for subtle flavor
- Add citrus slices (lemon, orange) for brightness and depth
- Experiment with different base liquids (tea, coffee) instead of water
