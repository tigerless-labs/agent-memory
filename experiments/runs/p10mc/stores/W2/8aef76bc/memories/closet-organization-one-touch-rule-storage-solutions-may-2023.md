## Goal

Organize closet and bedrooms more efficiently. User wants to expand cleaning routine beyond living room/dining room to include bedrooms and bathrooms starting May 2023.

## Strategy: One Touch Rule

When taking something out of the closet, immediately put it back in its designated spot rather than leaving on floor or chair. This approach prevents clutter buildup and maintains organization.

## Storage Solutions Being Considered

1. **Stackable Storage Bins** — for socks, underwear, accessories, out-of-season clothing (with lids to keep dust out)
2. **Shoe Organizers** — over-the-door or freestanding to maximize vertical space
3. **Closet Shelves with Baskets** — for storing folded items like t-shirts, jeans, sweaters
4. **Hanging Organizers** — with pockets or shelves for dresses, jackets, bags (maximizes vertical space)
5. **Drawer Dividers** — to separate tops, bottoms, accessories
6. **Tiered Closet Systems** — comprehensive solution with shelves, rods, drawers (if major investment justified)

## Key Considerations

- Measure closet dimensions before purchasing
- Choose versatile options that can be rearranged as needs change
- Select based on personal style and closet aesthetic
- Match storage solutions to specific items being stored and frequency of use
