## Apps & Tools Recommended for Budgeting & Grocery Tracking

### Budgeting Apps
- **Mint** — tracks spending by category, budgeting tools, alerts
- **YNAB (You Need a Budget)** — income/expense tracking, automated savings, investment tracking
- **Personal Capital** — income/expense tracking, investment tracking, financial planning
- **Spendee, Wally, Toshl Finance** — alternative budgeting apps with grocery tracking

### Grocery-Specific Apps
- **Grocery IQ** — create/manage lists, track prices, stay within budget
- **Out of Milk** — create/manage lists, track prices, sale alerts
- **Flipp** — aggregates weekly ads from local stores, creates shopping list, tracks prices
- **AnyList** — create/manage lists, track prices, organize recipes
- **ShopSavvy** — scan barcodes, compare prices, create shopping list

### Spreadsheet Templates
- Google Sheets budgeting templates
- Microsoft Excel budgeting templates
- Printable worksheets (Dave Ramsey, Budgeting Binder, etc.)
- Custom spreadsheet columns: Date, Category, Item, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Cost, Running Total
