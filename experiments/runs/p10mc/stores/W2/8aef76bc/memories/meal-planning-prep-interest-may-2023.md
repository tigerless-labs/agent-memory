## Meal Planning & Prep Interest

- **Motivation**: reduce grocery spending, reduce food waste, save time
- **Challenges**: worried about time commitment, meal ideas, food waste, cooking skills
- **Approach**: wants to start small (plan 1-2 meals/week, gradually increase)
- **Resources**: meal planning apps (Plan to Eat, Yummly, Mealime), cookbooks (The Meal Prep Cookbook, The Full Helping), online communities (Facebook groups, Reddit forums)
- **Strategy**: 
  - Find inspiration from social media, cookbooks, blogs
  - Create grocery lists based on meal plans
  - Shop for ingredients usable in multiple meals
  - Practice cooking skills with simple recipes
  - Budget rule considered: 50/30/20 (50% staples, 30% semi-luxury, 20% luxury/splurge items)
