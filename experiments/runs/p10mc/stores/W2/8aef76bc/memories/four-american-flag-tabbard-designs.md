## Four American Flag–Based Tabbard Designs

**Date:** May 21, 2023  
**Context:** Designer with knowledge of marketing, heraldry, and the Middle Ages

### 1. Classic Design
Simple tunic with sleeves. American flag centered on front. Red and white stripes on bottom half, blue field with white stars on top half. Painted or embroidered.

**Midjourney Prompt:**  
Generate an image of a tabbard with sleeves featuring the American flag on the front. The tabbard should be a simple tunic with short sleeves, and the American flag should be painted or embroidered onto the tabbard. The bottom half of the tabbard should feature the red and white stripes of the flag, while the top half should feature the blue field with white stars.

### 2. Heraldic Design
Simple tunic with short sleeves. Divided into four quadrants. Each quadrant features a different American heritage symbol: bald eagle, liberty bell, Statue of Liberty, and one more emblem. Painted or embroidered.

**Midjourney Prompt:**  
Generate an image of a tabbard with sleeves featuring a heraldic design inspired by the American flag. The tabbard should be a simple tunic with short sleeves, and should be divided into quadrants featuring different symbols and emblems representing American heritage and values. The symbols can be painted or embroidered onto the tabbard, and can include emblems such as an eagle, a liberty bell, or the Statue of Liberty.

### 3. Medieval-Inspired Design
Tunic with long sleeves, V-shaped neckline, diagonal split down center. American flag alongside other symbols: bald eagle, shield, banner with motto. Inspired by medieval knight coats of arms. Painted or embroidered.

**Midjourney Prompt:**  
Generate an image of a tabbard with sleeves featuring a medieval-inspired design inspired by the American flag. The tabbard should be a tunic with long sleeves, a V-shaped neckline, and a diagonal split down the center. The design should include the American flag alongside other symbols and emblems representing American values and ideals, such as a bald eagle, a shield, or a banner with a motto. The motifs can be painted or embroidered onto the tabbard.

### 4. Modern Design
Simple tunic with short sleeves. Abstract approach to the flag. Bold geometric shapes and colors. Elements: squares, triangles, circles. Patriotic color palette (red, white, blue). Painted or embroidered.

**Midjourney Prompt:**  
Generate an image of a tabbard with sleeves featuring a modern, abstract design inspired by the American flag. The tabbard should be a simple tunic with short sleeves, and should feature bold geometric shapes and colors that evoke the spirit of contemporary design. The abstracted flag motifs can be painted or embroidered onto the tabbard, and can feature shapes such as squares, triangles, or circles in patriotic colors such as red, white, and blue.
