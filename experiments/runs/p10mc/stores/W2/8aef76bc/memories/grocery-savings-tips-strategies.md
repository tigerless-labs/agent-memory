## Grocery Savings Strategies

### Shopping Methods
- Meal planning and prep to avoid unnecessary purchases
- Shop sales and stock up on non-perishables when cheap
- Buy in bulk wisely (avoid waste, split with friends if needed)
- Shop at discount stores (Aldi, Lidl) for 50% savings on staples
- Shop in season and at farmers markets/CSA programs
- Avoid shopping when hungry (reduces impulse buys)
- Check unit prices for best deals

### Cost-Saving Tools
- Cashback apps: Ibotta, Fetch Rewards, Checkout 51
- Digital coupons: RetailMeNot, Coupons.com
- Store brands and generics (similar quality, lower price)

### Budget Strategies
- Avoid processed and pre-packaged foods
- Cook from scratch with whole ingredients
- Freeze leftovers to reduce waste
- Set aside "treat yourself" budget (weekly or monthly) for luxury food items
- 50/30/20 rule for grocery budget:
  - 50% staples (produce, meat, dairy, whole grains)
  - 30% semi-luxury (specialty cheeses, artisanal breads)
  - 20% luxury/splurge (truffles, high-end chocolates)
