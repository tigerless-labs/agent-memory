## Project Overview

Active project as of May 21, 2023 to reorganize kitchen cabinets for maximum space efficiency and reduced clutter.

## Approach
- Purge and declutter: sort into keep/donate/discard, ruthless with items unused for 1+ years
- Categorize items: baking, cooking, dinnerware, utensils, etc.
- Assign designated homes for each category
- Use vertical space with stackable containers, baskets, shelves
- Place frequently-used items on accessible shelves
- Use over-the-door storage, adhesive hooks, or pegboard
- Install dividers or inserts to organize within shelves
- Store heavy items (pots/pans) in lower cabinets
- Maintain organization with 6-12 month refresher
