## User Interest
The user wants to experience traditional Japanese culture, specifically traditional Japanese music and dance performances in Tokyo.

## Recommended Festivals for Traditional Music & Dance

1. **Tokyo National Museum Summer Festival** (August, Taito-ku)
   - Features traditional Japanese music and dance performances
   - Includes workshops and cultural exhibitions

2. **Kanda Matsuri** (mid-May, biennial, Chiyoda-ku)
   - Features parade with musicians and dancers performing traditional Japanese music and dance
   - Dazzling floats and elaborately costumed participants

3. **Shunju Matsuri** (late September, Higashi-cho)
   - Annual celebration of Japanese culture
   - Includes traditional music and dance performances, food, and handicraft booths

4. **Setagaya Hachimangu Shrine Festival** (late September, Setagaya-ku)
   - Features traditional Japanese music and dance
   - Includes taiko drumming and kagura (traditional Shinto performance)

5. **Asakusa Samba Carnival** (late August, Asakusa)
   - Colorful parade with both samba and traditional Japanese performances
   - More contemporary but blends traditional elements

## Additional Cultural Attractions Mentioned
- Tokyo National Museum - largest Japanese art and artifact collection
- Meiji Shrine - Shinto shrine in forested area in heart of Tokyo
- Senso-ji Temple - oldest temple in Tokyo, famous red lantern
- Tsukiji Fish Market - largest fish market in world, popular for foodies
