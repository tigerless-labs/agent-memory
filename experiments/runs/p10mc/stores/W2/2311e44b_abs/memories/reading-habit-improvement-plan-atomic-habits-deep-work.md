## Reading Habit Improvement Plan

Created May 2023 using principles from **Atomic Habits** by James Clear and **Deep Work** by Cal Newport.

### Scheduling Reading Sessions

**Set a specific goal**: 30-40 pages or 30-60 minutes daily

**Choose consistent time**:
- Early morning (30 min before work)
- Lunch break (30-60 min)
- Evening (30-60 min before bed)

**Schedule as non-negotiable**: Calendar entries or phone reminders

### Eliminating Distractions

1. Create conducive environment: quiet corner, library, outdoor spot
2. Turn off notifications: silence phone, TV, electronic devices; use Freedom/SelfControl/Moment apps
3. Minimize multitasking: focus solely on reading

### Practicing Active Reading

1. **Take notes**: Keep reading journal or note-taking app (Evernote, Simplenote)
   - Key quotes/passages
   - Character developments
   - Themes/motifs
   - Questions/thoughts

2. **Summarize and reflect**: After each session
   - Summarize main events/plot points
   - Reflect on resonances and forward-looking anticipation

3. **Ask questions**: As you read
   - What is author conveying?
   - How do character actions relate to plot?
   - What themes are emerging?

### Example Weekly Schedule

**Monday-Friday**: 30 min lunch break reading (12:00-12:30 PM) + notes/summary

**Weekends**: 60 min morning reading (9:00-10:00 AM) + active reading with questions

### Implementation Tips

- Start small, gradually increase
- Track progress with reading log or habit app (Habitica, HabitBull)
- Set small rewards for milestones
