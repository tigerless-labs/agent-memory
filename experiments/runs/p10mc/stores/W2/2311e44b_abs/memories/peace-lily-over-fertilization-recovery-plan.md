## Situation
User's peace lily has droopy leaves due to over-fertilization.

## Recovery steps recommended
1. Stop fertilizing for now
2. Check soil moisture and flush soil thoroughly with water to leach out excess fertilizer salts
3. Provide good air circulation (consider fan)
4. Prune damaged yellow/droopy leaves at the base
5. Monitor temperature (65–80°F / 18–27°C) and humidity
6. Repot in fresh soil if salts are heavily contaminated

## Ongoing care
- Humidity tray setup: shallow tray with pebbles, water level below pot bottom
- Consider switching from balanced fertilizer to one with higher phosphorus/potassium
- Monitor progress over time
