## Recommended Accessible Non-Fiction Books

User found Sapiens too slow and dense; requested engaging, accessible non-fiction recommendations.

**Recommended titles:**

1. **The Devil in the White City** by Erik Larson — historical true crime about 1893 World's Fair in Chicago and serial killer H.H. Holmes; reads like thriller
2. **Bad Blood: Secrets and Lies in a Silicon Valley Startup** by John Carreyrou — rise and fall of Theranos biotech startup
3. **The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks** by Rebecca Skloot — medicine, ethics, and humanity through story of Henrietta Lacks and her immortal cells
4. **Just Mercy: A Story of Justice and Redemption** by Bryan Stevenson — US justice system, racial inequality, lawyer's work on death row cases
5. **The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business** by Charles Duhigg — science, psychology, real-life examples of habit formation

All are well-researched, engaging, relatively easy to read without being overwhelming.
