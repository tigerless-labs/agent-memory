Example Weekday Morning Routine (for 8 AM work start):

6:30 AM - Wake up, stretch, meditate (15 minutes)
6:45 AM - Exercise: 30 min yoga or jog (30 minutes)
7:15 AM - Shower and get ready (30 minutes)
7:45 AM - Breakfast and meal prep (30 minutes, can prep lunch)
8:00 AM - Ready to head out the door

General allocation guidelines discussed:
- Waking up and morning routine: 15-30 minutes
- Exercise: 30 minutes
- Shower and get ready: 30-45 minutes
- Breakfast and meal prep: 30 minutes
- Buffer time: 15-30 minutes (for unexpected delays)

Tips for efficiency:
- Prepare breakfast and lunch the night before
- Be flexible and adjust as needed
- Find balance between personal needs and schedule
- Key: find routine that leaves you feeling energized, focused, ready for day

Variations:
- Can start with shorter exercise (20 minutes) and gradually increase
- Adjust wake time based on commute length
- Be flexible if need more sleep
