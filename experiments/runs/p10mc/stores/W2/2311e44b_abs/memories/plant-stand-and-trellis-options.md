## Plant stand options

### Mid-century modern plant stand
- Sleek, minimalist design
- Wooden or metal base
- Complements fiddle leaf fig aesthetic
- Adds sophistication

### Tiered plant stand
- Multiple levels
- Showcases multiple plants
- Creates sense of depth
- Adds visual interest

### Macramé plant stand
- Bohemian-inspired
- Cozy, organic feel
- Unique contrast to modern fiddle leaf fig

## Trellis options

### Wooden trellis
- Natural finish
- Blends with shelf
- Rustic touch
- Simple, minimalist design recommended

### Metal trellis
- Sleek, modern design
- Industrial chic aesthetic
- Must have sturdy base

### Obelisk trellis
- Provides plant support
- Sculptural element
- Good for climbing plants like air plants

## Selection criteria
- Material: wood/metal (natural or industrial), macrame/woven (bohemian)
- Size: proportional to plants and shelf, allow space for growth and air circulation
- Style: complement existing decor and personal taste
- Stability: sturdy, won't topple (important with pets/children)
