Based on conversation from May 20, 2023, recommended tools for management and onboarding:

**Task & Project Management**
- Trello: visualize tasks with boards, lists, cards
- Asana: organize tasks with deadlines and assignments
- Todoist: create and manage to-do lists
- Wrike: team collaboration and task prioritization
- Smartsheet: spreadsheet-like interface for projects

**Documentation & Knowledge Base**
- Confluence: knowledge base and project documentation
- Notion: flexible workspace for notes and documents
- Microsoft SharePoint: centralized platform for sharing project info
- Google Docs/Microsoft Word Online: collaborative document editing

**Scheduling & Calendar**
- Google Calendar: schedule meetings and set reminders
- Microsoft Outlook: email, calendar, and task integration
- Calendly: schedule meetings with others

**Training & Learning Management**
- Litmos: learning management system
- TalentLMS: training platform
- Docebo: training and learning platform

**Checklists & Progress Tracking**
- Checkli: dedicated checklist app
- Excel/Google Sheets: spreadsheets for tracking progress

**Mentorship & Feedback Methods**
- OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for goal-setting
- SMART Goals framework
- MentorNet or Chronus for structured mentorship
- START method for feedback (Specific, Timely, Actionable, Relevant, Transparent)
- Regular bi-weekly or monthly check-ins for progress review
