## Fertilizers recommended

### Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food
- N-P-K ratio: 24-8-16
- Water-soluble
- Formulated for indoor plants like peace lilies
- Higher phosphorus for blooming and root growth

### Schultz All Purpose Plant Food
- N-P-K ratio: 20-20-20
- Balanced, gentle on plants
- Good general-purpose option
- Won't cause excessive growth or burning

### Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer
- N-P-K ratio: 2-2-2
- Organic (bone meal, alfalfa meal, feather meal)
- Slow-release formula
- Gentle on peace lilies

### E.B. Stone Organics Bloom!
- N-P-K ratio: 5-3-4
- Designed to promote blooming
- Natural ingredients (fish bone meal, alfalfa meal)
- Good for peace lilies

## Usage notes
- Peace lilies prefer fertilizers with higher phosphorus (P) and potassium (K)
- Start with diluted solution to avoid root burn
- Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer)
- Skip fertilization during dormant season (fall/winter)
- User's African violets doing well on balanced water-soluble fertilizer
