## Metal Print Image Preparation

### Required Settings
- **Color Space:** Adobe RGB (1998) — recommended for metal prints
- **Bit Depth:** 16-bit or 8-bit
- **Resolution:** Minimum 300 dpi
- **File Format:** TIFF or PSD (compatible with Mpix metal print process)

### Lightroom Editing Techniques
- **Color Grading:** Use Color Grading tool to create balanced, natural color palette
- **Contrast & Exposure:** Metal prints accentuate drama; add punch without overdoing it
- **Sharpening:** Use Detail panel; metal reveals softness, so careful sharpening needed
- **Noise Reduction:** Critical if shot in low light or high ISO; use Noise Reduction tool
- **Local Adjustments:** Use Adjustment Brush for local contrast fine-tuning

### Photoshop Alternatives
- Gradient Map for color grading
- Unsharp Mask for sharpening
- Noise Reduction filter
- Spot Healing Brush for local adjustments

### Recommended Plugins
- **Nik Collection** — Color Efex Pro and Viveza plugins excellent for landscape depth/contrast
- **Luminar** — landscape-specific filters and presets
- Custom presets in Lightroom/Photoshop for consistent look

### Key Note
Metal prints accentuate colors and local contrast; prioritize careful color management and sharpening to avoid revealing unwanted artifacts.
