## Show Overview

**Setting:** Washington Heights, Upper Manhattan (predominantly Latinx community)  
**Timeframe:** 3 days  
**Creator:** Lin-Manuel Miranda (first Broadway musical before Hamilton)

## Main Characters

- **Usnavi de la Vega** — Protagonist; Dominican-American bodega owner; narrator; dreams of returning to Dominican Republic
- **Abuela Claudia** — Community matriarch; raised Usnavi after his parents passed; wise, protective
- **Nina Rosario** — High school student; just back from first year at Stanford; balancing identity and family expectations
- **Benny** — Works at taxi dispatch; loves Nina; lacks education/opportunities
- **Vanessa** — Usnavi's love interest; beauty salon worker; wants to escape neighborhood
- **Sonny** — Usnavi's cousin; passionate about social justice and activism
- **Kevin and Camila Rosario** — Nina's parents; taxi company owners; struggling business

## Major Themes

1. **Identity** — Complexities of Latinx identity, cultural heritage, immigrant experience
2. **Community** — Tight-knit neighborhood; neighbors as family
3. **Dreams** — Characters' aspirations drive narrative
4. **Gentrification** — Rising rents and displacement threats

## Musical Score

**Styles:** Latin rhythms (salsa, merengue, bachata, Latin jazz) + hip-hop + R&B + jazz + show tunes  
**Lyrics:** Complex rhyme schemes, wordplay, English/Spanish blending

**Character-Specific Musical Voices:**
- Usnavi: Hip-hop influenced
- Nina: Introspective, R&B-infused (Breathe, Everything I Know)
- Vanessa: Sassy, pop-infused (It Won't Be Long Now, Gossip)
- Sonny: High-energy hip-hop (96,000, The Club)
- Abuela Claudia: Traditional Latin (Paciencia y Fe)

**Standout Numbers:**
- In the Heights (opening; sets tone)
- 96,000 (ensemble; lottery/American Dream)
- Breathe (Nina's ballad)
- Piragua (Reprise) (summer, community joy)
- Alabanza (tribute to community resilience)
- Carnaval del Barrio (full community celebration/dance)

## Community Aspects

- Bodega as gathering place
- Characters rally for Abuela Claudia when ill
- Deep relationships between characters
- Celebration of neighborhood vibrancy

## Cultural Context

- Influenced by Lin-Manuel Miranda's own Washington Heights childhood
- Predominantly Dominican population with other Latino groups
- Strong immigrant experience themes
- History of salsa, merengue, bachata music scene
- Recent gentrification pressures affecting neighborhood
- Emphasis on cultural authenticity
