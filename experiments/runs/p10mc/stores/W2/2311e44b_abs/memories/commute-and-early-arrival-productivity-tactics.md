How to maximize morning commute:

Audio content:
- Listen to industry-specific or hobby/personal development podcasts
- Audiobooks (Audible, Scribd, library digital collections)
- Language learning apps (Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone)

Productivity activities:
- Plan daily tasks and priorities
- Respond to emails and check schedule
- Brainstorm ideas or reflect on challenges
- Catch up on news

Relaxation options:
- Guided meditations or calming music
- Daydreaming/quiet reflection
- Journaling
- People-watching
- Personal music playlist

How to use early office arrival (5-15 min):

Productivity-focused:
- Prioritize tasks by importance/urgency
- Scan and respond to urgent emails
- Review day's schedule and meetings
- Get head start on a task (10-15 min focused work)
- Organize workspace and gather needed materials

Wellness-focused:
- Meditate or stretch
- Plan breaks throughout day
- Review short/long-term goals
- Learn something new (tutorial, article, podcast)

Relaxation option:
- Simply relax, enjoy quiet morning, have coffee/tea, mentally prepare

Key: Stay safe and aware during commute, especially if listening to audio or distracted
