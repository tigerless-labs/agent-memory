## Science-Backed Self-Help & Personal Development

User requested non-fiction backed by scientific research, not just inspirational stories; interested in practical advice for habits and productivity.

**Recommended titles:**

1. **Atomic Habits** by James Clear — building good habits and breaking bad ones, backed by psychology and neuroscience research
2. **Deep Work** by Cal Newport — productivity and focus strategies, backed by psychology and neuroscience
3. **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen Covey — classic, decades of research behind it
4. **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck — growth mindset, backed by Dweck's own research
5. **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle — mindfulness and meditation, rooted in research, practical advice for present moment living
6. **Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience** by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi — seminal work on flow concept, decades of positive psychology research
7. **The Happiness Advantage** by Shawn Achor — connection between happiness and success, backed by positive psychology research
8. **Mini Habits: Smaller Habits, Bigger Results** by Stephen Guise — habit formation based on author's research and experiments
9. **The Productivity Project** by Chris Bailey — comprehensive productivity guide, backed by author's research
10. **The Willpower Instinct** by Kelly McGonigal — science of willpower, practical strategies for self-control

User particularly interested in **Atomic Habits** and **Deep Work** for improving reading habits specifically.
