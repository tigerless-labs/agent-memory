Weekend Morning Routine Approach (as of May 21, 2023):

Example Weekend Schedule (sleeping in later):
8:30 AM - Wake up, stretch, enjoy coffee in bed
9:00 AM - Meditate or practice yoga (relaxation-focused)
9:30 AM - Take longer shower and get ready
10:00 AM - Enjoy bigger breakfast or try new recipe
11:00 AM - Plan out day (errands, meetings, projects)
11:30 AM - Start day, feeling refreshed and relaxed

Key principles for weekends:
- Adjust wake-up time to accommodate sleeping in (aim for 8:00-8:30 AM if weekday is 6:30 AM)
- Prioritize relaxation and self-care over productivity
- More flexibility - don't be hard on self if skip activities
- Focus on enjoyment and rejuvenation
- Still get natural light exposure (circadian rhythm regulation)
- Can make bigger/leisurely breakfast, try new recipes
- More meditation/yoga/self-care focused
- Still helpful to plan day, but with more freedom

Tips:
- Be kind to self - weekends are meant as break
- Listen to body - take extra sleep if needed
- Create special weekend rituals to look forward to (favorite coffee, special breakfast)
- Balance relaxation with some light productivity
