## Successful mergers and acquisitions in hospitality

### Marriott International and Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide (2016)
- Created the largest hotel company in the world
- Added 30 hotel brands to Marriott's portfolio
- Included high-end brands: St. Regis, W Hotels
- Combined company: over 1.1 million rooms in over 100 countries
- Expanded loyalty program to over 140 million members
- Integration went smoothly; improved financial results post-merger

### AccorHotels and Fairmont Raffles Hotels International (FRHI) (2016)
- Acquired luxury brands: Fairmont, Raffles, Swissôtel
- Expanded into new markets in North America and Asia
- Gained access to FRHI's loyalty program (6+ million members)
- Successful integration; strong financial results reported

### InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG) and Kimpton Hotels & Restaurants (2015)
- Acquired boutique hotel brand with 60+ US properties
- Expanded into growing boutique hotel market
- Positioned for luxury market expansion
- Strong financial results; loyalty program expansion; new customer base
