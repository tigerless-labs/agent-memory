## Unsuccessful mergers and acquisitions in hospitality

### Hilton Hotels and Bally Entertainment Corporation (1995)
- Hilton acquired Bally Entertainment, which owned casinos and hotels in Las Vegas and Atlantic City
- Expected to provide foothold in gaming industry and diversify Hilton's business
- Deemed a failure; Hilton sold Bally's a few years later
- Failure factors: high debt levels of Bally, cultural clashes between companies, increased competition in gaming industry

### Four Seasons and Regent Hotels & Resorts (1992; sold 1997)
- Four Seasons acquired Regent Hotels & Resorts, luxury brand with properties in Asia
- Expected to expand Four Seasons' presence in Asian market and increase luxury portfolio
- Sold in 1997; considered a failure
- Failure factors: high integration costs, lack of synergies between companies, cultural differences
