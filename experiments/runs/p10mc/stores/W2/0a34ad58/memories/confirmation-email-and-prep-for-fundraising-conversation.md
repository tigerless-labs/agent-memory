## Confirmation Email & Conversation Prep

**Timing**: Send immediately after booking

**Email Structure**:
1. Thank you for booking
2. Reaffirm purpose of conversation
3. Information to have on hand (three categories)
4. Questions to consider beforehand (three questions)
5. Looking forward to conversation

### Data to Gather (Have on Hand)

- Current fundraising goals and objectives
- Any data or reports on past fundraising efforts, including successes and challenges
- List of current fundraising staff and their roles and responsibilities

### Questions for Prospect to Consider

- What specific challenges are you facing with fundraising and retention of fundraisers?
- What has your organization done in the past to try to address these challenges?
- What long-term goals do you have for your fundraising efforts?

### Purpose of Prep
- Warms prospect on topic before conversation
- Gets them thinking about their situation (higher quality conversation)
- Signals professionalism and intentionality
- Ensures they come prepared to talk (no vague hand-waving)
- Creates natural transition into listening phase of 30-minute call
