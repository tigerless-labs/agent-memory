## Route Options with Suica Card

### Option 1: JR Narita Express (N'EX) Direct
- **Travel Time:** 60-70 minutes
- **Cost:** Approximately ¥3,200-3,500 (around $30-35 USD)
- **Details:** Most convenient and comfortable; direct to Shinjuku Station, final stop on N'EX line
- **Procedure:** Follow signs to JR East Train Station at Narita Airport, board N'EX bound for Shinjuku, get off at Shinjuku Station

### Option 2: Keisei Skyliner + JR Yamanote Line Transfer
- **Travel Time:** 50-60 minutes total
- **Cost:** Approximately ¥2,500-3,000 (around $25-30 USD)
- **Details:** Budget-friendly option with one transfer
- **Procedure:** Follow signs to Keisei Train Station at Narita, take Skyliner to Ueno Station (40-50 min), transfer to JR Yamanote Line bound for Shinjuku (20-30 min)

### Option 3: Keisei Limited Express + JR Yamanote Line Transfer
- **Travel Time:** 70-80 minutes total
- **Cost:** Approximately ¥2,000-2,500 (around $20-25 USD)
- **Details:** Most budget-friendly option; longer travel time
- **Procedure:** Follow signs to Keisei Train Station at Narita, take Limited Express to Ueno Station, transfer to JR Yamanote Line bound for Shinjuku

## Using Suica Card

1. Touch card on reader at ticket gate when entering Narita Airport train station
2. Board chosen train
3. Touch card on reader at ticket gate when exiting Shinjuku Station
4. Balance deducted from card automatically

## Tips

- N'EX is more expensive but more convenient, comfortable ride
- Keisei options more budget-friendly but require transfer at Ueno
- Check train schedules and plan accordingly
- Costs and times are approximate; may vary by time of day and traffic
- Inform your bank/credit card company of travel plans
