## Tower Overview

- **Height:** 332.6 meters (1,091 feet)
- **Design:** Inspired by Eiffel Tower with modern, sleek twist
- **Status:** One of the tallest towers in the world
- **Location:** Tokyo

## What to See & Do

### Observation Decks
- Breathtaking panoramic views of Tokyo's skyline
- Can see Imperial Palace and Meiji Shrine
- Mount Fuji visible on clear days
- High-speed elevator access

### Exhibitions & Attractions
- Wax museum
- Pirate-themed play area
- Guinness World Records museum

### Other Activities
- Shopping at souvenir shops
- Dining at restaurants within tower complex
- Stair climbing option (great workout alternative to elevator)
- Interactive exhibits and displays about Tokyo's history, culture, landmarks

## Visitor Tips

- **Timing:** Visit early morning or later evening to beat crowds
- **Tickets:** Purchase online in advance to avoid long queues
- **What to Wear:** Comfortable shoes and clothes for walking/climbing
- **Bring:** Snacks and water (limited options within tower)
- **Experience:** Unforgettable attraction with something for everyone
