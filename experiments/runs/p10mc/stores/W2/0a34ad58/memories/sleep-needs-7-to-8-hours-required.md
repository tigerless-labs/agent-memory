## Sleep Requirements & Impact

**Key finding:** Established May 2023 while working on consistent wake-up time routine

**Sleep need:** 7-8 hours per night

**With sufficient sleep (7-8+ hours):**
- Wake up feeling refreshed
- Energized throughout the day
- Better mental clarity

**With insufficient sleep (less than 7 hours):**
- Wake up groggy and disoriented
- Harder to maintain morning routine
- Reduced mental performance during work

**Sleep quality factors explored:**
- Importance of consistent sleep schedule
- Impact on ability to establish consistent wake-up time
- Relationship between sleep duration and morning energy levels

**Strategies discussed:**
- Consistent bedtime and wake time (including weekends)
- Calming pre-sleep routine
- Optimized sleep environment (dark, quiet, comfortable temperature)
- Limiting screens before bed (blue light suppresses melatonin)
- Avoiding caffeine and alcohol in evening hours
- Morning sunlight exposure to regulate circadian rhythm
- Regular exercise (but not close to bedtime)
- Limiting naps to 20-30 minutes
