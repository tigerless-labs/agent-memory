## Fundraising Zen Assessment

**Purpose**: A professional, data-driven and visual deliverable that provides contextual history on giving and results with deep dive and action plan for an agreed-upon focus area.

### Three Focus Areas

**Focus Area A: Relationship Based Giving**
- Key Funder Engagement and Advancement
- Community Giving Clubs and Societies
- Honor Giving
- Final Giving
- Tribute Giving

**Focus Area B: Mass Market Fundraising**
- Monthly Giving
- Traffic Engineering and Paid Advertising
- Content Strategy
- Conversion, Offer, and Call-To-Action Optimization
- Journey Mapping and Persona Development

**Focus Area C: Employer Enabled Support**
- Employer Patrons
- Employee Champions
- Workplace Campaigns

### Assessment Components

The assessment evaluates:
- Performance and analysis of fundraising and revenue results
- Objective feedback on current efforts
- Data analysis to surface relevant insights and answers to prioritized questions/obstacles
- Financial relationships prioritized through predictive modeling and wealth screening
- Fundraising systems and processes (technical and non-technical)
- Value proposition positioning to the philanthropic landscape
- Insights collected from staff, board members, and key funders
- Situational history, archiving, and documentation

### Deliverables

a) Review of historical giving segments and concentration risk assessment
b) Development of key performance indicators and data entry/collection operational strategy for reports and dashboards
c) New revenue growth plan
d) Upcoming performance campaign design and results forecasting
e) Functional delegation calendar of internal relationship and publishing actions with related work sessions and upcoming agenda schedules
