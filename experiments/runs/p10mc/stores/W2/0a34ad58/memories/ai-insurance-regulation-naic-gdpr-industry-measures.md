## Regulatory Measures for AI in Insurance

### Government/Regulatory Bodies

1. **NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners) — United States**
   - Established working group on artificial intelligence
   - Developing regulatory guidance for AI use in insurance
   - Focus areas: ethical standards, data privacy protection, transparency, independent third-party oversight

2. **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) — European Union**
   - Imposes strict rules on how companies collect, store, process, and use personal data
   - Applies to data used to train AI algorithms
   - Gives individuals right to know when AI decisions are used about them
   - Gives individuals right to challenge those decisions

### Industry Self-Regulation & Company Initiatives

- **Transparency and explainability measures**: making AI-based decisions more understandable and challengeable
- **Data diversity efforts**: ensuring training data is diverse and unbiased
- **Independent oversight**: third-party reviews of AI systems

### Current Status

- These measures are a step forward but much work remains
- Important for policymakers, regulators, industry leaders, and consumers to work together
- Must address emerging risks and ensure AI respects individual rights
- Must promote social and economic well-being
