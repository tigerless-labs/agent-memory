## Shinjuku District (Walking Distance)

- **Shinjuku Isetan** (5-minute walk) — Massive department store, fashion, electronics, wide range of products
- **Shinjuku Odakyu Shinjuku Mylord** (5-minute walk) — Bustling shopping mall, fashion, beauty, souvenir shops
- **Shinjuku Lumine** (5-minute walk) — Stylish shopping mall, fashion, accessories, lifestyle goods

## Nearby Districts (Short Taxi/Subway Ride)

- **Tokyu Hands** (10-minute walk) — Popular department store, daily essentials to unique souvenirs
- **Shinjuku Takashimaya** (10-minute walk) — Large department store, fashion, beauty, food
- **Harajuku** (10-15 minutes by taxi or subway) — Fashion-forward district, trendy boutiques, vintage shops, unique souvenirs
- **Omotesando** (15-20 minutes by taxi or subway) — Stylish shopping street, high-end boutiques, art galleries, restaurants
- **Ikebukuro Parco** (20-25 minutes by taxi or subway) — Trendy shopping mall, fashion, entertainment, lifestyle goods
- **Ginza** (20-25 minutes by taxi or subway) — Luxurious shopping district, high-end designer brands, jewelry stores, upscale department stores

## Shopping Approach

- Easily explore areas on foot or by short taxi ride from Park Hyatt Tokyo
- Wide variety of options from budget-friendly to luxury brands
