## Recent mergers and acquisitions in hospitality (2018–2019)

### AccorHotels and Orbis (2019)
- Acquisition of Orbis, leading hotel company in Central and Eastern Europe
- Expanded AccorHotels presence in region and access to new markets

### InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG) and Six Senses Hotels Resorts Spas (2019)
- Acquired luxury hotel and spa brand with properties in Asia, Middle East, Europe, and Americas
- Expanded IHG's presence in luxury market segment
- Gained access to new geographic markets

### Marriott International and Elegant Hotels Group (2019)
- Acquisition of luxury hotel collection in Barbados
- Expanded portfolio of luxury hotels
- Foothold in Caribbean market

### AccorHotels and sbe Entertainment Group (2018)
- AccorHotels acquired 50% stake in sbe Entertainment Group
- sbe brands: SLS, Delano, Mondrian
- Positioned AccorHotels in fast-growing lifestyle hospitality market
- Expanded presence in North America
