## Nonprofit Fundraising Sales Funnel

Complete sales process for Fundraising Zen Assessment upsell targeting nonprofits planning Development Director hires.

### Components in Sequence

1. **dont-hire-development-director-sales-email-hook** — Initial outreach email positioning internal capacity-building as prerequisite to hiring; cites fundraiser retention crisis (16-month average tenure, $127,650 replacement cost); leads to booking decision

2. **confirmation-email-and-prep-for-fundraising-conversation** — Post-booking email requesting prospect gather specific data and consider reflective questions before the conversation; three data categories and three questions

3. **thirty-minute-conversation-script-zen-assessment-close** — 30-minute conversation workflow structured as: Introduction (5m), Listen (10m), Present Assessment (10m), Upsell (5m), Conclusion (5m); emphasis on listening to surface pain before presenting solution

4. **fundraising-zen-assessment-service-overview** — Complete description of the Fundraising Zen Assessment service itself; three focus areas, assessment components, and five key deliverables

### The Core Problem
- Nonprofits hiring fundraisers face high turnover (avg 16 months)
- Root cause: organization lacks established culture of philanthropy and internal systems
- Solution: Build capacity first, then hire, resulting in empowered and successful new hire

### Success Metric
Close the prospect on conducting the Fundraising Zen Assessment within the 30-minute conversation
