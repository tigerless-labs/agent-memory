## 30-Minute Conversation Script: Fundraising Zen Assessment Close

**Time Allocation**: 5-10-10-5-5 breakdown

### Segment 1: Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introduce yourself and your organization
- Provide brief overview of Fundraising Zen Assessment and its purpose
- Ask opening question: "Do you have specific goals or challenges you're currently facing with your fundraising efforts?"

### Segment 2: Listen Intently (10 minutes)
**Goal**: Let prospect talk; take detailed notes
- Listen to their current fundraising goals and challenges in detail
- Ask clarifying questions to better understand their needs and priorities
- Encourage them to share past efforts they've made to address these challenges
- Identify pain points, bottlenecks, and gaps in their current process
- Do NOT interrupt; let them finish their thoughts
- Surface the specific obstacles they're facing

### Segment 3: Present Fundraising Zen Assessment (10 minutes)
**Positioning**: "Based on what you've shared, here's how we can help..."
- Explain scope of assessment and the three focus areas:
  - Relationship Based Giving
  - Mass Market Fundraising
  - Employer Enabled Support
- Outline key deliverables:
  - Review of historical giving segments and concentration risk
  - KPI development and data collection strategy
  - New revenue growth plan
  - Performance campaign design and forecasting
  - Functional delegation calendar
- Emphasize value: "A professional assessment gives you clarity and a roadmap, so when you do hire, they inherit a strong foundation"

### Segment 4: Upsell (5 minutes)
- Connect the assessment directly to their stated pain points
- Discuss specific benefits as they relate to this prospect's challenges
- Offer to schedule follow-up to discuss assessment details
- If interest: provide next steps (scheduling timeline, pricing conversation, etc.)
- If hesitation: ask "What would need to happen for this to make sense?"

### Segment 5: Conclusion (5 minutes)
- Thank them for their time and insights
- Summarize what you heard (builds rapport and confirms understanding)
- Recap next steps
- Express genuine interest in working together
- End with soft commitment: "Let's talk next week about moving forward"

### Key Principles
- The listen phase is 1/3 of the call — do not rush it
- Every claim in the present phase should tie back to something they said
- Frame assessment as prerequisite to hiring, not as alternative to hiring
- Listen for budget concerns but do not discuss pricing in this call
- Goal: Get to yes on the assessment in this 30 minutes
