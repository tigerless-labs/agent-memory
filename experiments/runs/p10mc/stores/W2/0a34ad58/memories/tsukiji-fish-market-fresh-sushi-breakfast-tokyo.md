## About Tsukiji Fish Market

- One of the largest fish markets in the world
- Handles over 2,000 tons of seafood every day
- Divided into two main areas:
  - **Inner Market (jonai-shijo):** Wholesale fish auction (not open to general public)
  - **Outer Market (jogai-shijo):** Seafood restaurants, sushi bars, shops, souvenirs, kitchenware

## Fresh Sushi for Breakfast

Top recommended restaurants in outer market:
- **Sushi Dai** — Legendary sushi restaurant, high-quality fish, traditional Edomae-style sushi
- **Sushi Daiwa** — Popular sushi restaurant, wide range of options, lively atmosphere
- **Tsukiji Fish Market Sushi** — Small unassuming sushi bar, freshest and most affordable sushi in area

## Getting There from Shinjuku

1. From Shinjuku Station, take JR Yamanote Line towards Tokyo Station (10 stops)
2. Get off at Shimbashi Station
3. Transfer to Tokyo Metro Ginza Line towards Asakusa (2 stops)
4. Get off at Tsukijishijo Station
5. Follow signs to Tsukiji Fish Market

- **Total Travel Time:** 30-40 minutes
- **Approximate Cost:** ¥420-450 with Suica card

## Tips for Visit

- **Best Time:** 5:00 AM to 7:00 AM for freshest sushi and busiest market
- **Dress Code:** Wear comfortable shoes; dress in layers (can be chilly in early morning)
- **Payment:** Many restaurants/shops accept cash only — bring yen
- **Behavior:** Respect market rules, avoid touching fish on display, be mindful of photo restrictions
- **Inner Market:** Can observe auction from viewing area if arriving by 5 AM, but expect crowded and fast-paced environment
