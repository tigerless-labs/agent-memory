## Currency Exchange Options Near Park Hyatt Tokyo

### Convenient Locations at Shinjuku Station (5-minute walk)

- **Travelex** (B1 floor, near Odakyu Shinjuku Line gates) — Well-known service, competitive rates
- **Seven Bank** (B1 floor, near JR East gates) — Offers currency exchange at Shinjuku Station branch

### Walking Distance

- **Lawson Bank** (5-minute walk, Shinjuku-dori Street) — Convenience store chain, offers currency exchange

### Nearby Options

- **Tokyo Shinjuku Post Office** (10-minute walk, Shinjuku-dori Street, near Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden) — Offers currency exchange, including some less common currencies
- **Shinjuku Mitsui Building** (10-minute walk) — Currency exchange counter on 1st floor, operated by Mitsubishi UFJ Trust and Banking Corporation

## Tips for Exchange

- Compare exchange rates among options for best deal
- Prepare to show passport and/or identification
- Some services may have limited hours or minimum exchange amounts
- ATMs often offer better exchange rates than currency exchange counters

## Alternative: ATM Withdrawal

- **7-Eleven** (multiple locations near Park Hyatt Tokyo) — ATMs accept international cards, convenient yen withdrawal
- **Shinjuku Station** (5-minute walk) — Multiple bank ATMs inside station
- Credit cards and debit cards widely accepted in Japan; may not need large cash amount
