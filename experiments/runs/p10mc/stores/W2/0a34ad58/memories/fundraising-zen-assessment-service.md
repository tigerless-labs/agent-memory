## Overview
Professional assessment providing data-driven and visual deliverables on giving history, results, and insights for all three components of revenue programs, with deep dive and action plan for agreed focus area.

## Three Focus Areas
**A. Relationship Based Giving** — Key Funder Engagement and Advancement, Community Giving Clubs and Societies, Honor Giving, Final Giving, Tribute Giving

**B. Mass Market Fundraising** — Monthly Giving, Traffic Engineering and Paid Advertising, Content Strategy, Conversion, Offer, Call-To-Action Optimization, Journey Mapping and Persona Development

**C. Employer Enabled Support** — Employer Patrons, Employee Champions, Workplace Campaigns

## Assessment Components
- Evaluation of performance and revenue results
- Objective feedback on current efforts
- Data analysis and insights into prioritized questions and obstacles
- Financial relationship prioritization through predictive modeling and wealth screening
- Review of fundraising systems and processes (technical & non-technical)
- Value proposition positioning assessment to philanthropic landscape
- Insights collection from staff, board members, and key funders
- Situational history writing, archiving, and documentation

## Deliverables
a) Review of historical giving segments and concentration risk assessment
b) Development of key performance indicators and data entry/collection operational strategy for reports and dashboards
c) New revenue growth plan
d) Upcoming performance campaign design and results forecasting
e) Functional delegation calendar of internal relationship and publishing actions, work sessions, and agenda schedules
