## Regulatory Framework

**NAIC (National Association of Insurance Commissioners)**: Established a working group on artificial intelligence in the United States focused on developing ethical standards, data privacy protection, transparency, and independent third-party oversight for AI use in insurance.

**EU GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**: Imposes strict rules on how companies collect, store, process, and use personal data, including data used to train AI algorithms. Grants individuals the right to know when AI decisions are used about them and to challenge those decisions.

## Industry Initiatives

Many insurance companies are taking additional steps beyond regulations:
- Investing in transparency and explainability measures so AI decisions can be understood and challenged
- Ensuring training data is diverse and unbiased
- Developing internal guidelines and oversight mechanisms

## Current Status

These regulatory and industry measures represent progress, but significant work remains to ensure AI is used responsibly and ethically in insurance while respecting individual rights and promoting social and economic well-being.
