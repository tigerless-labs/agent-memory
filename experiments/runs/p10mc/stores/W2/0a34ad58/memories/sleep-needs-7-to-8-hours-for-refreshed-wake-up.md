## Sleep Requirements and Patterns

**Sleep duration needed**: 7-8 hours minimum

**Effects of adequate sleep (7-8 hours)**:
- Wakes up feeling refreshed and energized
- Better daytime alertness and focus

**Effects of insufficient sleep (less than 7 hours)**:
- Wakes up groggy and disoriented
- Increased mental fatigue during work hours
- Reduced ability to concentrate on long work tasks

**Current challenges**:
- Establishing consistent wake-up time (struggle with late-night work commitments)
- Example: Wednesday late-night work project kept up until 1:00 AM, slept until 8:00 AM
- Mental exhaustion and concentration difficulties during long work hours

**Goals**:
- Maintain 7-8 hour sleep window consistently
- Establish reliable bedtime routine
- Improve sleep quality
