## Baby Carrier Types & Recommendations

### Soft-Structured Carriers
- **Ergobaby Omni 360** — versatile, multiple positions (front, back, hip), popular choice
- **Lillebaby Complete** — six carrying positions including forward-facing, built-in lumbar support
- **Tula Explore** — stylish, wide seat, adjustable panel for customizable fit

### Wrap-Style Carriers
- **Moby Wrap** — soft, stretchy, easy to use, comfortable for newborns and older babies
- **Solly Baby Wrap** — sleek design, range of colors and patterns
- **Boba Wrap** — soft, machine-washable, simple design

### Ring Sling Carriers
- **Maya Wrap** — comfortable, adjustable, padded shoulder, range of fabric options
- **Sleeping Baby Productions (SBP) Ring Sling** — highly-rated, adjustable, simple design
- **Tula Ring Sling** — stylish, padded shoulder, range of fabric options

### Mei Tai Carriers
- **Babyhawk Mei Tai** — comfortable, structured waistband, padded straps
- **DidyTai** — soft, adjustable, range of fabric options

## Key Considerations When Choosing
- **Comfort** — padded straps, comfortable waistband, supportive seat
- **Adjustability** — fits both parents comfortably
- **Ease of use** — simple, intuitive design with clear instructions
- **Breastfeeding support** — allows easy breastfeeding with comfortable, adjustable design
- **Weight range** — suitable for newborn through toddler
- **Machine washability** — practical for busy parents

## Tips Before Purchasing
- Try out different carriers before buying if possible
- Read online reviews from other parents
- Check warranty or return policy
- Consider what features matter most to your lifestyle
