## Japanese Cuisine Recommendations

- **Nihonryori Ryugin** (3-minute walk) — Three-Michelin-starred restaurant, innovative modern Japanese cuisine using seasonal ingredients
- **Sushi Saito** (5-minute walk) — Three-Michelin-starred sushi restaurant, traditional Edomae-style sushi
- **Yakiniku Jumbo Han no Daidokoro Bettei** (10-minute walk) — High-end yakiniku (Japanese BBQ) restaurant, premium meats, exceptional service
- **Tofuya Ukai** (10-minute walk) — Popular tofu restaurant, wide variety of tofu dishes, serene atmosphere
- **Toriki** (10-minute walk) — Yakitori (grilled chicken skewers), lively atmosphere, delicious affordable options
- **Ichiran Shinjuku** (15-minute walk) — Famous tonkotsu (pork bone) ramen chain, unique rich broth

## International Cuisine (In Hotel)

- **New York Grill** — Located within Park Hyatt Tokyo, stunning views of Tokyo, classic American grill-style cuisine
- **Girandole** — Located within Park Hyatt Tokyo, modern French restaurant, focus on seasonal ingredients and creative dishes

## French-Japanese Fusion

- **Shunbou** (5-minute walk) — Contemporary French-Japanese fusion, cozy atmosphere

## Booking Tips

- Reservations highly recommended, especially during peak travel seasons
- Online reservation platforms: Chope, TableCheck
- Can also ask Park Hyatt Tokyo's concierge team for assistance
- Wide range of price points from affordable to very expensive
- Some restaurants have dress codes or require credit card for reservation
