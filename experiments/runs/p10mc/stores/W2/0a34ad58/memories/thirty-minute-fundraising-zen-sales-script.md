## 30-Minute Sales Conversation Script Workflow

### 1. Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introduce yourself and your organization
- Provide brief overview of the Fundraising Zen Assessment and its purpose
- Ask if the recipient has specific goals or challenges with their fundraising efforts

### 2. Listen Intently (10 minutes)
- Allow recipient to share current fundraising goals and challenges in detail
- Take notes and ask clarifying questions to understand their needs and priorities
- Encourage them to share past efforts they've made to address challenges
- Listen for pain points around staff retention, fundraising performance, revenue gaps

### 3. Present the Fundraising Zen Assessment (10 minutes)
- Explain scope of assessment and three focus areas:
  - Relationship Based Giving
  - Mass Market Fundraising
  - Employer Enabled Support
- Outline deliverables:
  - Review of historical giving segments
  - Development of key performance indicators
  - New revenue growth plan
  - Performance campaign design
  - Functional delegation calendar
- Emphasize value of professional assessment to gain deeper understanding and identify improvement areas

### 4. Upsell (5 minutes)
- Discuss benefits of Fundraising Zen Assessment for achieving their fundraising goals
- Connect their stated challenges back to what the assessment addresses
- Offer to schedule follow-up conversation to discuss assessment details
- If interested, provide next steps (follow-up scheduling, additional information)

### 5. Conclusion (5 minutes)
- Thank recipient for their time
- Ask for final questions
- Confirm next steps
- Express interest in working together

## Key Messaging Thread
Build from initial email premise: the problem is not just hiring a Development Director, but building internal fundraising infrastructure and culture first. The assessment uncovers where their current efforts are working and where they need to build before bringing in new hires.
