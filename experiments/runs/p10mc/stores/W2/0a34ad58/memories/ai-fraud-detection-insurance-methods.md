## AI Fraud Detection in Insurance

Four main AI-based approaches used to detect insurance fraud:

### 1. Anomaly Detection
- AI algorithms analyze large volumes of claims data to identify suspicious patterns
- Flags: multiple claims for the same type of injury, unusual time gaps between incident and claim

### 2. Social Media Monitoring
- AI monitors social media platforms for posts/updates that contradict claimant's reported injury or illness
- Example: person claiming debilitating injury but seen playing sports in social media videos

### 3. Network Analysis
- AI analyzes connections between individuals in claims (family members, doctors, agents)
- Identifies patterns of collusion or suspicious activities
- Detects fraud rings

### 4. Image and Voice Recognition
- Analyzes images/recordings submitted as part of claims
- Looks for signs of fabrication or manipulation
- Example: analyzing damaged vehicle/property photos for evidence of staged accidents or tampering

## Benefits
- Automates and speeds up detection process
- Reduces investigation costs
- Improves accuracy of fraud detection
