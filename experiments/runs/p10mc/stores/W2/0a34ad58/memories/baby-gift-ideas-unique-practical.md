## Baby Gift Ideas: Unique & Practical

### Unique Gifts
- **Personalized Baby Blanket** — Soft, cozy blanket with baby's name or initials embroidered. Thoughtful keepsake.
- **Baby Carriers/Slings** — Ergobaby, Lillebaby, or other brands allow hands-free parenting and bonding. See baby-carriers note for specific models.
- **Baby Book Subscription** — Book of the Month, Literati, or similar. Delivers curated books monthly for literacy development.
- **Sensory Play Kit** — Textured toys, rattles, teething toys. Stimulates senses and encourages exploration.

### Practical Gifts
- **Diaper Caddy Organizer** — Storage for diapers, wipes, creams. Keeps essentials organized and within reach.
- **Baby Bathtub Support** — Bathtub insert or support for safe, comfortable bathing.
- **Breast Pump Accessories** — Storage bags, breast shields, nursing pillows (if parents plan to breastfeed).
- **Baby Feeding Essentials** — Baby food maker, silicone bibs, sets of baby spoons and forks for easier mealtimes.

### Multipurpose Gifts
- **Baby Gym or Playmat** — With hanging toys and attachments for comfortable play, tummy time, development.
- **Baby Carrier with Storage** — Built-in storage for diapers, toys, essentials. Convenient for outings.
- **Baby Monitor with Camera** — Camera and temperature sensor for remote monitoring.
- **Nursery Organizer** — Storage for toys, clothes, diapers. Keeps nursery tidy.

## Before Purchasing
- **Check the registry** — ensure not duplicating gifts
- **Consider grouping with others** — pool resources for larger/more expensive items
- **Think about practicality** — does the gift solve a real parenting need or create lasting memories?
