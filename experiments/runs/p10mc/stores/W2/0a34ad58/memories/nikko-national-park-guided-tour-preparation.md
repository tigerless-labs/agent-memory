## What to Prepare for Nikko National Park Guided Tour

### Clothing & Footwear
- **Weather-Appropriate Clothing:** Check weather forecast for Nikko; dress in layers
- **Waterproof Jacket:** Bring if rain expected
- **Comfortable Footwear:** Wear sturdy, comfortable shoes for walking and exploring park's trails

### Supplies to Bring
- **Hydration:** Reusable water bottle for eco-friendly travel
- **Snacks:** Pack some, especially if tour doesn't provide refreshments
- **Sun Protection:** Sunscreen and hat to protect from sun during outdoor activities
- **Photography:** Camera or smartphone for capturing landscapes and memories

### Documentation & Organization
- **Tour Booking Confirmation:** Have confirmation and all necessary documents ready
- **TripIt App:** Use to keep all travel details organized
- **Tour Details:** Know exact location of meeting point for tour

### Money
- **Cash:** Bring small amount of yen for purchases or emergencies (backup)
- Suica card for transportation to tour meeting point

## Getting to Meeting Point Using Suica Card

1. Check exact address of tour meeting point
2. Use Suica card for train or bus travel
3. Use transit app or Google Maps to plan best route
4. Account for transfers and check schedules

## Route to Nikko from Tokyo

- Take JR Tohoku Shinkansen from Tokyo to Utsunomiya Station
- Transfer to JR Nikko Line to reach Nikko Station
- Use Suica card for these segments
- Use local buses or taxis if needed from Nikko Station to meeting point

## Travel Approach

- Tour desk at Park Hyatt Tokyo can help with activity planning and booking
- Allow travel time to meeting point; don't underestimate journey duration
