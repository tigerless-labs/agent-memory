## AI-Powered Fraud Detection Techniques

**Anomaly detection**: AI analyzes claims data to identify suspicious patterns such as multiple claims for the same injury type, unusual time gaps between incident and claim, or other statistical outliers that might indicate fraudulent activity.

**Social media monitoring**: AI monitors social media platforms for posts or updates contradicting a claimant's reported condition. Example: flagging someone claiming disability who appears in videos playing sports.

**Network analysis**: AI examines connections between individuals involved in claims (family members, doctors, insurance agents) to identify patterns of collusion or suspicious relationships.

**Image and voice recognition**: AI analyzes submitted images or recordings for signs of fabrication or manipulation, such as evidence of staged accidents or tampering in vehicle or property damage photos.

## Benefits

These AI applications automate and speed up fraud detection, reduce investigation costs, and improve accuracy compared to manual review processes.
