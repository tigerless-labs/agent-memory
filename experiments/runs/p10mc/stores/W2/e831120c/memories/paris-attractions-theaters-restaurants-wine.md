## Historical Sites & Museums
- **The Louvre Museum**: Mona Lisa, ancient civilizations to 19th century
- **Notre-Dame Cathedral**: 12th-century Gothic cathedral
- **Arc de Triomphe**: Honors French soldiers, views from top
- **Palace of Versailles**: Opulent royal château, gardens, Hall of Mirrors (day trip)
- **Catacombs of Paris**: Underground ossuary with remains of millions (18th century)
- **Musée Carnavalet**: Paris history from prehistoric times to present, 17th-century mansion
- **Archeological Crypt of Notre-Dame**: Gallo-Roman artifacts

## WWII & French Resistance Sites
- **Musée de la Résistance**: Comprehensive look at Resistance movement and leaders
- **Musée de l'Armée**: Extensive section on French Resistance and Liberation
- **Musée Jean Moulin**: Dedicated to Jean Moulin, key Resistance figure
- **Mont Valérien Memorial**: Honors Resistance fighters executed by Nazis
- **Place de la République**: Monument to Resistance and Liberation
- **Cimetière de Montparnasse**: Burial site of many Resistance fighters including Jean Moulin

## Broadway-Style Musicals & Theaters
**Shows**:
- Moulin Rouge! The Musical (iconic cabaret adaptation)
- The Phantom of the Opera (Théâtre Mogador, in French)
- Les Misérables (Théâtre du Châtelet)
- Chicago (Théâtre Marigny)
- Hamilton (French adaptation, Théâtre du Châtelet)

**Historic Theaters**:
- Opéra Garnier (opera house with guided tours)
- Théâtre du Châtelet (19th-century)
- Comédie-Française (historic theater)

## Restaurants (Traditional French, Seasonal Ingredients)
1. **Le Comptoir du Relais** (6th arr.): Steak tartare, escargots
2. **Chez L'Ami Jean** (11th arr.): Organic/local ingredients, duck confit, seasonal menu
3. **Le Grand Vefour**: Historic (since 1784), foie gras, wild game, roasted scallops
4. **La Réserve** (8th arr.): Intimate, seasonal menu, creative dishes
5. **Le Petit Châtelet** (5th arr.): Roasted chicken, seafood platter
6. **Breizh Café**: Crepes with seasonal, organic ingredients
7. **Le 6 Paul Bert** (11th arr.): Seasonal menu, creative cuisine
8. **La Dame de Pic** (11th arr.): Michelin-starred, seasonal focus, creative and intimate

## Wine Bars
- **La Closerie des Lilas** (6th arr.): Extensive selection, rare and vintage bottles, courtyard
- **Le Barav** (11th arr.): Natural and organic wines from small producers
- **Septime La Cave** (11th arr.): Run by Michelin-starred Septime team, natural wines, small plates

## Wine Shops & Markets
- **La Maison du Vin**: Multiple locations, knowledgeable staff
- **Nicolas**: Chain with multiple Paris locations
- **Le Wine Shop** (6th arr.): Curated selection, rare bottles, tastings and workshops
- **Marché aux Vins** (12th arr.): Wine market with producer tastings
- **Marché d'Aligre** (12th arr.): Variety of natural and organic wines

## Tips
- Make reservations in advance for popular restaurants
- Visit restaurants at lunch for more affordable menu du jour
- Ask servers for seasonal recommendations
- Expect leisurely meals (French pace)
- Tours and museums often have English audio guides
- Visit museums in morning/early afternoon to avoid crowds
- Check websites for temporary exhibitions and special events
