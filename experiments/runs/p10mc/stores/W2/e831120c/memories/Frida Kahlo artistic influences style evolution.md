## Artistic Influences and Style Development

**Key Artistic Influences**
- Jose Maria Velasco (19th-century Mexican painter)
- European modernism, especially Impressionism
- Surrealists: Salvador Dali and Andre Breton
- Mexican folk art and traditional textiles and pottery
- Mexican mythology and Aztec/Mayan heritage

**Artistic Style Periods**

Early Work (1920s-1930s)
- Influenced by European modernism and Surrealism
- Gentle, dreamy colors
- Focus on portraiture
- Experimentation and exploration phase

Mexican Folk Art Influence (1930s-1940s)
- Incorporation of vibrant colors and symbolism from traditional Mexican folk art
- Bold, expressive use of color
- Symbolic imagery drawn from Mexican mythology and folklore
- Significant shift incorporating personal and cultural references

Autobiographical Period (1940s-1950s)
- Increasingly personal and introspective
- Rich symbolism with recurring motifs
- More muted and somber palette reflecting suffering
- Later 1950s work featured looser brushstrokes and more vibrant, expressive colors

**Themes Throughout Career**
- Physical and emotional pain
- Mexican identity and cultural heritage
- Relationships and love (especially marriage to Diego)
- Femininity, motherhood, and female experience
- Female sexuality and desire
- Mortality and the human condition
