## Creating a Sourdough Starter

**Environment & Setup:**
- Warm, draft-free place: 75-80°F (24-27°C)
- High-protein flour (bread flour or all-purpose with high protein)
- Filtered or bottled water (chlorine inhibits growth)
- Clean, sanitized equipment

**Initial Process:**
- Mix 1/2 cup flour + 1/2 cup water in clean glass/ceramic container
- Cover with cloth, rest 24-48 hours at room temperature
- This is the beginning of your starter

## Maintenance

**Daily Feeding:**
- Feed once per day using 1:1 flour:water ratio
- Discard half of starter before feeding
- Keep at room temperature during active use
- Feed once a week if storing in refrigerator

**Monitoring:**
- Observe activity, smell, appearance
- If too active: reduce feeding frequency
- If too sluggish: increase feeding frequency
- Watch for bad smell or mold → discard and start fresh

## Feeding Schedule Options

**Understanding the cycle (3 phases):**
1. Peak activity: 4-6 hours after feeding (bubbly, frothy)
2. Decline: 2-4 hours (less bubbly)
3. Dormancy: several hours or overnight (less active)

**Schedule examples:**
- **Once daily (24-hour):** Feed at 8 am, then 8 am next day
- **Twice daily (12-hour):** Feed 8 am and 8 pm
- Adjust based on starter's activity and baking schedule

**Tips:**
- Be flexible; adjust based on needs
- Use starter at peak activity
- Maintain consistent environment
- Track with calendar

## Storage Methods

**Refrigeration (short-term):**
- Store in clean, airtight container
- Temperature: 39-45°F (4-7°C)
- Feed before storing
- Feed once weekly when stored
- Revive: allow 24-48 hours at room temperature (75-80°F)
- Feed 1-2 times during revival before using

**Freezing (long-term):**
- Mix starter with equal parts water and flour (thick paste)
- Store in airtight container or freezer bag
- Temperature: 0°F (-18°C) or below

**Dehydration (long-term):**
- Mix starter with flour (thick paste)
- Spread on parchment-lined baking sheet
- Dehydrate at 150°F (65°C) for 1-2 hours
- Store dried starter in airtight container

**Backup Starter:**
- Maintain a backup in case primary becomes contaminated or dies
