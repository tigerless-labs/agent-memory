## Frida's Artistic Process

**Techniques**
- Layering: built up multiple layers of paint, paper, and materials for textured three-dimensional effect
- Mixed media: combined oil paint, watercolor, and collage for unique textures and visual interest
- Rich symbolism drawing on Mexican mythology, folk art, and personal experiences
- Blended traditional Mexican folk art with surrealist and realist elements

**Materials Used**
- Small, portable canvases (allowed painting in comfort and convenience)
- Oil paint, watercolor, and gouache
- Mixed her own colors to achieve specific hues and textures
- Collage elements: paper, fabric, and found objects for texture and visual interest
- Traditional Mexican folk art motifs and techniques

**Inspirations**
- Mexican folk art as connection to cultural heritage and emotional expression
- Surrealism to explore the subconscious and inner world
- Realism to depict the world with precision and accuracy
- Personal experiences: physical pain, emotional suffering, relationships

**Creative Process**
- Intuitive and spontaneous, reflecting emotional state
- Sketched ideas in notebooks or on scraps of paper, refining concepts and compositions
- Transferred sketches to canvas, working quickly to capture essence of ideas
- Frequently revised paintings making changes and adjustments while working through emotions and ideas
- Process was deeply personal and emotional journey
