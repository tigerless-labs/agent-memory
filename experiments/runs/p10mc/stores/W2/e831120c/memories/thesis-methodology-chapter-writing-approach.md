## Methodology Chapter Writing Strategy

### Standard Chapter Sections
1. Introduction to the methodology
2. Research design (explain type and justify choice)
3. Data collection methods (instruments, procedures, sampling strategy)
4. Data analysis procedures (statistical tests, software, justification)
5. Ethical considerations (consent, data protection, confidentiality)
6. Limitations (biases, assumptions, methodological weaknesses and impact)

### Writing Approach
1. **Start with outline** — Organize structure before writing
2. **Restate research questions** — Provide context for methodology
3. **Be specific** — Detail challenges faced and how addressed
4. **Use clear language** — Avoid overly technical jargon
5. **Include visual aids** — Tables, figures, diagrams to break up text
6. **Address assumptions** — Discuss limitations' impact on results
7. **Seek feedback** — Share draft with supervisor/peers for improvement

### Key Principles
- Ensure methods align with research questions/objectives
- Provide justification for methodological choices
- Be transparent about limitations and biases
- Use specific language over vague generalities
- Make complex concepts accessible to readers

### Before Submitting
- Review for clarity and conciseness
- Verify all methods are clearly explained
- Ensure ethical considerations are addressed
- Get feedback from supervisor or peers
- Revise based on feedback
