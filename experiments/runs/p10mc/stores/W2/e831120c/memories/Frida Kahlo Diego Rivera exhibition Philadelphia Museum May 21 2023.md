## Exhibition Experience

**Exhibition Details**
- Exhibition title: "Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera"
- Location: Philadelphia Museum of Art
- Event: Opening reception
- Date: May 21, 2023 (Sunday)
- Featured collection: Jacques and Natasha Gelman Collection (on loan)

**About the Gelman Collection**
- Jacques and Natasha Gelman were a Mexican couple
- Close friends and patrons of Frida and Diego
- Collection includes many works by Frida and Diego
- Also features other prominent Mexican artists
- Provides unique insight into Frida's artistic evolution and her relationship with Diego Rivera

**User's Impressions**
- Struck by stunning works on loan
- Particularly impressed by Frida's vibrant colors and symbolism in her paintings
