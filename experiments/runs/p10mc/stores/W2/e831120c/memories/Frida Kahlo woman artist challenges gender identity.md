## Frida as a Woman Artist and Gender Identity

**Challenges Faced**
- Gender bias: women artists overlooked, undervalued, marginalized
- Her work often dismissed as folk art or naive rather than legitimate art
- Limited access to education, training, and exhibition opportunities
- Stereotyping as Mexican or folk artist limited opportunities

**How She Navigated Challenges**
- Defiance and determination — continued to paint despite obstacles
- Networking and collaboration with women artists like Tina Modotti and men like Diego
- Subverting expectations through art, incorporating themes of femininity and sexuality
- Refused to be typecast or deterred

**Gender's Impact on Her Art**
- Explored feminine identity, motherhood, female experience
- Conveyed deep emotional intimacy, vulnerability, and sensitivity
- Incorporated erotic and sensual themes subverting traditional female sexuality
- Challenged traditional gender roles and representations of women in art
- Blurred lines between masculinity and femininity

**Relationships and Gender**
- Sister Cristina
- Friends: Dolores del Rio and Maria Felix
- Rumored affair with Josephine Baker
- These relationships suggest possible queer identity and influenced her art
- Complex power dynamics with Diego Rivera (mentor role) but she asserted own artistic identity
