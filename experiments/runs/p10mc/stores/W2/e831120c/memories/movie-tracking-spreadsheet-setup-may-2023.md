## Movie Tracking Spreadsheet Template

**Core Columns (12):**
1. Movie Title
2. Release Year
3. Watch Date (e.g., 2023-02-15)
4. Rating (e.g., 4/5 or 8/10)
5. Notes (e.g., "Loved the action scenes!")
6. Franchise/Series (e.g., "Marvel Cinematic Universe")
7. Genre (e.g., "Superhero", "Action", "Comedy")
8. **Type** (Sequel, Remake, Standalone, Prequel, Spin-off, Reboot)
9. **Platform** (Theaters, Amazon Prime, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Blu-ray/DVD, YouTube, iTunes)
10. Format (Theater, Streaming, Blu-ray)
11. Re-watch (Yes or No)
12. **Month Watched** (e.g., "February", "02")

**Optional Columns:**
- Director
- Cast
- Runtime (minutes)
- Language
