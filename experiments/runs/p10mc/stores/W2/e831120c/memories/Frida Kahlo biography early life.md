## Early Life and Family

**Birth and Family Background**
- Born: July 6, 1907 in Coyoacán, Mexico City
- Father: Wilhelm Kahlo (Hungarian-Jewish immigrant, photographer)
- Mother: Matilde Calderón y González (devout Catholic, indigenous and Spanish descent)

**Childhood**
- Contracted polio at age 6, left right leg shorter and weaker than left
- Physically and emotionally challenging childhood
- Father introduced her to photography and encouraged artistic pursuits
- Mother instilled appreciation for Mexican folk art and culture
- Exposed to intellectual and artistic circles

**Education**
- Attended prestigious Escuela Nacional Preparatoria in Mexico City
- One of few female students
- Became interested in politics
- Drawn to Mexican muralist movement and work of Diego Rivera
