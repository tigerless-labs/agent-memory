## Notable Works and Symbolism

**Early Works (1920s-1930s)**
- Self-Portrait with Velvet Dress (1926) — gentle dreamy colors, portraiture focus

**Mexican Folk Art Period (1930s-1940s)**
- The Two Fridas (1939) — bold vibrant colors, symbolic imagery
- Self-Portrait with Thorn Necklace and Hummingbird (1940) — depicts complexities and contradictions of love with Diego

**Autobiographical and Symbolic Period (1940s-1950s)**
- The Wounded Table (1940) — intense emotions through symbolism
- The Broken Column (1944) — self-portrait showing physical pain
- Without Hope (1945) — conveys suffering through art
- The Circle of Life (1950) — looser brushstrokes, vibrant expressive colors
- Still Life with Parrot and Fruit (1951) — continued use of symbolism

**Recurring Motifs and Symbolism**
- Thorn necklace representing physical and emotional pain
- Broken column representing spinal injuries and suffering
- Tears or blood flowing from eyes representing emotional trauma
- Self-portraits as primary vehicle for expression
- Blended realism, symbolism, and fantasy
