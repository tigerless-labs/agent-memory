## Whole Grain Bread Recipes Recommended

1. **Multigrain Bread with Kamut and Rye** — combines Kamut, rye, whole wheat, oats, barley; complex flavor
2. **Spelt and Walnut Bread** — ancient grain paired with walnuts; lovely crumb, slightly sweet flavor [**PRIORITY — user planning to try next**]
3. **Whole Wheat Ciabatta with Oats and Sunflower Seeds** — Italian-style; whole wheat, oats, sunflower seeds; hearty, crusty, good for sandwiches/toast
4. **Rye and Caraway Seed Bread** — traditional Scandinavian; distinctive caraway flavor with earthy rye
5. **Ancient Grain Bread with Quinoa and Amaranth** — protein-rich combination of quinoa, amaranth, whole wheat, Kamut, spelt
6. **100% Whole Wheat Sourdough** — only whole wheat flour with natural starter; tangy, chewy
7. **Bulgur and Herbs Bread** — Middle Eastern-inspired; bulgur (cracked wheat) with fresh parsley, thyme, rosemary
8. **Oatmeal and Honey Bread** — sweet bread with rolled oats and honey; soft, comforting; good for toast
9. **Einkorn and Sesame Seed Bread** — ancient grain einkorn with nutty sesame seeds; soft, tender crumb, aromatic
10. **Seven-Grain Bread** — hearty combination of whole wheat, rye, oats, barley, Kamut, spelt, triticale; complex, satisfying

## General Advice
- Experiment with different flours, seeds, flavor combinations
- Spelt flour: more delicate than other flours; absorbs more liquid; may produce denser crumb
