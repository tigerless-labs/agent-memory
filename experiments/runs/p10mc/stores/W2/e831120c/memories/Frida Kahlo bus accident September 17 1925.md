## The Bus Accident and Its Impact

**The Accident**
- Date: September 17, 1925
- Location: Mexico City
- Frida's age: 18 years old
- Event: Bus collided with a streetcar

**Injuries**
- Spinal fractures
- Pelvis fractures
- Leg fractures
- Internal injuries (including damaged uterus affecting ability to carry pregnancy to term)
- Over 30 surgeries in following years

**Lifelong Impact**
- Chronic pain (constant companion)
- Limited mobility and independence
- Reliance on painkillers (leading to addiction)
- Emotional trauma: anxiety, depression, deep-seated fear of death
- Became a driving force in her artistic development and themes
