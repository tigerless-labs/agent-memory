## Sports & Fitness Documentaries — Recommended List

**Climbing & Extreme Sports**
- "Free Solo" — soloing El Capitan
- "The Dawn Wall" (2017) — Tommy Caldwell and Kevin Jorgeson's El Capitan climb
- "Meru" (2015) — three climbers attempt Shark's Fin route on Mount Meru
- "Valley Uprising" (2014) — rock climbing counterculture in Yosemite 1960s-70s
- "The Rescue" (2020) — 2018 Thai cave rescue

**Plant-Based Diet & Health**
- "The Game Changers" (2018) — plant-based diet for athletes
- "What the Health" (2017) — health industry, plant-based benefits
- "Forks Over Knives" (2011) — plant-based diet and health impact
- "The Plant-Based Diet" (2019) — Netflix series on plant-based diet and environment
- "The Magic Pill" (2017) — ketogenic diet and health

**Bodybuilding & Strength**
- "Pumping Iron" (1977) — classic bodybuilding documentary, features Arnold Schwarzenegger
- "Bigger, Stronger, Faster" (2008) — steroid use in sports and bodybuilding
- "Generation Iron" (2013) — modern bodybuilding, Phil Heath and others
- "The Redemption of the Iron Giant" (2016) — strongman Eddie Hall's World's Strongest Man journey

**Running & Endurance**
- "Born to Run" (2016) — Tarahumara people and endurance science
- "The Barkley Marathons: The Race That Eats Its Young" (2014) — toughest ultramarathon
- "UltraMarathon Man: 50 Marathons 50 States 50 Days" (2008) — Dean Karnazes' feat
- "Unbreakable: The Western States 100" (2012) — elite ultrarunners attempt Western States Endurance Run

**Cycling**
- "The Armstrong Lie" (2013) — Lance Armstrong doping scandal
- "The Program" (2015) — doping culture in professional cycling

**CrossFit & Functional Fitness**
- "Generation Iron" (2013) — modern bodybuilding world
- "Fittest on Earth" (2016) — CrossFit athletes preparing for Games
- "The Redeemed and the Dominant: Fittest on Earth" (2018) — CrossFit Games

**Combat Sports & MMA**
- "Fightville" (2011) — MMA world
- "The Notorious" (2017) — Conor McGregor's rise to UFC champion
- "Choke" (1999) — Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and its MMA influence

**Obstacle Course Racing**
- "Spartan: Ultimate Team Challenge" (2016) — teams compete in Spartan Races
- "Rise of the Sufferfests" (2016) — obstacle course racing rise and community

**For Beginners / Inspiration**
- "Fittest on Earth" (2016) — inspiring CrossFit athlete stories
- "The Human Experiment" (2013) — fitness and exercise science introduction
- "Unbreakable: The Western States 100" (2012) — mental toughness and perseverance
- "Spartan: Ultimate Team Challenge" (2016) — teamwork and fitness inspiration
- "Rise of the Sufferfests" (2016) — obstacle racing for all fitness levels

**Sports & Mental Health**
- "The Last Dance" (2020) — Michael Jordan's final Bulls season
- "The Weight of Gold" (2020) — Olympic athletes' mental health challenges
- "Take Every Wave: The Life of Laird Hamilton" (2017) — surfer Laird Hamilton
