## Session: Literary Analysis of Hound of the Baskervilles

**Date:** May 21, 2023  
**Work:** Five-paragraph analytical essay

### Assignment
User requested an essay analyzing whether Miss Stapleton in "The Hound of the Baskervilles" (Conan Doyle) should be held responsible for her role in:
- Murder of Sir Charles Baskerville
- Attempted murder of Sir Henry Baskerville

Key angles requested:
- Whether she should be held responsible
- If yes: why and how severely should she be punished
- If no: why not

### Analysis Provided
The essay examined:
- **Active culpability:** She pretended to be Sir Henry's ally, lured him into danger, helped brother conceal identity
- **Mitigating circumstances:** Victim of abusive brother's control, desperation to escape, limited options
- **Conclusion:** Should be held responsible but with tempering for coercion; punishment should enable reform rather than purely punitive
