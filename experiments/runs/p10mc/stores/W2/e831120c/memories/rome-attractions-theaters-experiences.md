## Historical Sites & Museums
- **Colosseum**: Ancient amphitheater built in 80 AD, iconic symbol of Rome
- **Roman Forum**: Heart of ancient Rome with ruins, temples, basilicas
- **Pantheon**: Magnificently preserved ancient temple built in 126 AD
- **Vatican City**: Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel, St. Peter's Basilica

## Broadway-Style Musicals
- **The Lion King** (Teatro Brancaccio): Disney classic
- **Aladdin** (Teatro Sistina): Disney favorite
- **Mamma Mia!** (Teatro Olimpico): ABBA-inspired musical
- **We Will Rock You** (Teatro Brancaccio): Queen-inspired, high-energy
- **La Cage aux Folles** (Teatro Sistina): Italian adaptation of classic musical

## Historic Theaters
- **Teatro dell'Opera di Roma**: Grand opera house with rich history
- **Teatro di Roma**: Diverse program
- **Teatro Argentina**: Beautiful 18th-century theater

## Cultural Experiences
- **Trastevere**: Charming neighborhood with narrow streets, piazzas, lively nightlife
- **Gelato Tasting**: Famous gelato shops include Gelato di San Crispino, Fatamorgana
- **Italian Cuisine**: Authentic pasta, pizza, risotto at trattorias and osterias
- **Opera or Concert**: Teatro dell'Opera di Roma or Auditorium Parco della Musica

## Booking Tips
- Check official websites or ticketing sites (Ticketmaster, Vivaticket) for current schedules
- Book tickets in advance
- Shows and schedules subject to change
