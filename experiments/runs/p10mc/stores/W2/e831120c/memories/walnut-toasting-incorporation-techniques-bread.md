## Why Toast Walnuts?

- Brings out natural oils → more fragrant and flavorful
- Reduces bitterness and astringency → sweeter, nuttier taste
- Improves texture → crisper, more satisfying

## Toasting Methods

**Oven Method (PREFERRED for bread):**
- Preheat to 350°F (180°C)
- Spread in single layer on baking sheet with space between pieces
- Toast 5-7 minutes
- Stir halfway through
- Cool completely before chopping
- Watch for fragrance and light browning

**Pan-toasting (Stovetop):**
- Dry skillet over medium heat
- Stir frequently
- Toast until fragrant and lightly browned

**Microwaving:**
- Short intervals: 30-45 seconds
- Stir between intervals
- Caution: can quickly go from toasted to burnt

## Incorporating into Dough

**Preparation:**
- Chop toasted walnuts coarsely (~1/4 inch / 6mm pieces)
- Ensures even distribution throughout dough

**Mixing Phase:**
- Add after autolyse, before bulk fermentation
- Use gentle mixing (stand mixer with dough hook or by hand)
- Avoid overmixing (prevents breaking down walnuts)

**Optional Enhancement:**
- Preferment or soaker (biga, poolish, walnut soaker)
- Helps walnuts absorb moisture and distribute evenly
- Can use mixture of toasted + untoasted for complex flavor

**During Fermentation:**
- Fold gently, especially in first few folds (dough still delicate)
- Distribute walnuts evenly
- Don't over-proof (can cause uneven distribution or sinking)

**Shaping:**
- Use gentle touch to avoid crushing walnuts
- Round or oblong shapes help even distribution

**Scoring:**
- Use gentle, shallow cuts
- Avoid cutting into walnuts
- Helps bread open nicely without exposing walnuts

## For Spelt-Specific Notes
- Spelt flour is more delicate → handle gently
- Avoid overdeveloping gluten
- Spelt absorbs more liquid than other flours → monitor hydration
- Spelt breads tend to be denser than others
