**Niece's interests:** Dinosaurs

- **Dino Dig exhibit**: Interactive hands-on fossil casting activity where kids mix plaster, pour into molds, and remove their own fossil cast to take home. Can be decorated at home with paint, markers, stickers, or used in dioramas.

**Booking logistics:**
