## Trip Overview

**Trip Duration:** 14 days
**Travelers:** All adults (user, parents, siblings)
**Last family trip:** ~February 2023 (6 months before July 31, 2023 conversation)

## Selected Itinerary

**Destination:** Italy, Greece, Croatia

1. **Rome, Italy (Days 1-3)**
   - Colosseum, Roman Forum, Pantheon
   - Vatican City, Sistine Chapel, St. Peter's Basilica
   - Neighborhoods: Trastevere and Monti
   - Local cuisine and gelato

2. **Santorini, Greece (Days 4-6)**
   - Flight from Rome (~1.5 hours)
   - Oia and Imerovigli whitewashed houses
   - Black sand beaches (Perissa)
   - Village of Emporio
   - Sunset viewing
   - User particularly excited about Santorini (bucket list)

3. **Split, Croatia (Days 7-14)**
   - Flight from Santorini (~1.5 hours)
   - Diocletian's Palace (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
   - Mestrovic Gallery
   - Plitvice Lakes National Park (optional day trip)
   - Waterfront Riva

## Budget-Friendly Tips Discussed

**Accommodation:**
- Airbnb, VRBO, or Booking.com vacation rentals instead of hotels
- More space and amenities for families

**Flights:**
- Book well in advance
- Flexible with dates
- Consider budget airlines or error fares
- Fly Rome → Santorini → Split to avoid backtracking

**Transportation:**
- Use public transit, taxis, shuttles instead of car rental
- Walk or bus in Santorini
- Walk or affordable public transit in Split

**Food:**
- Eat at local tavernas with traditional cuisine
- Buy groceries at local markets for some meals
- Try street food and gelato (budget-conscious selections)

**Santorini-specific tips:**
- Visit Oia during day to avoid sunset crowds/high prices
- Walk to nearby Imerovigli for views without Oia premium prices
- Traditional Greek lunch at local taverna instead of fancy restaurants

**Split-specific tips:**
- Explore Diocletian's Palace on own (no guided tour)
- Mestrovic Gallery: free admission on Sundays
- Enjoy Riva waterfront free atmosphere

**Activities:**
- Prioritize free/low-cost activities (historic centers, markets, beaches)
- Consider discounted guided tour packages

## Travel Timing

**Best seasons:** Spring (April-May) and autumn (September-October)
- Mild weather, fewer tourists, lower prices

**Avoid:** Peak season June-August (hot, crowded, expensive)

**Avoid:** Winter December-February (cool, rainy)

**Planned timing:** Shoulder season (April-May or September-October)

## Key Packing Items

**Clothing:** Lightweight breathable clothes, comfortable walking shoes, sandals, dress shoes, light jacket/sweater, dressier outfit

**Toiletries:** Travel-sized essentials, sunscreen, insect repellent, after-sun lotion

**Electronics:** Universal power adapter (Type F/E/C), portable power bank, camera with chargers and memory cards

**Other essentials:** Reusable water bottle, travel umbrella/rain jacket, small backpack/daypack, first-aid kit, travel pillow, eye mask

**Documents:** Valid passport (6+ months), travel insurance, visa documentation, digital copies stored with trusted contact

**Currency:** Euro (Italy, Greece), Croatian kuna (Croatia)

## Additional Notes

- Many locals speak some English, but learning basic phrases in Italian, Greek, and Croatian helpful
- Santorini Jazz Festival in July; Ifestia Festival in August (if traveling mid-summer)
- Athens Festival June-September; Split Summer Festival July-August; Dubrovnik Summer Festival July-August
