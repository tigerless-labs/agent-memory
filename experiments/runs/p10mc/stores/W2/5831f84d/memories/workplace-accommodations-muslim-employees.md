## Prayer and Worship Accommodations
- **Prayer rooms or quiet spaces**: Designate private or quiet area for daily prayers
- **Flexible scheduling**: Allow adjustments to accommodate prayer times, especially during Ramadan when fasting and prayer times change daily
- **Friday prayer accommodation**: Longer lunch break on Fridays to attend Jumu'ah at nearby mosque, or provide quiet space on-site
- **Gender-segregated facilities**: Restrooms or locker rooms for those who prefer separate facilities

## Ramadan-Specific Support
- Flexible scheduling during Ramadan fasting
- Reduced workload or additional breaks to manage energy levels
- Understanding during month of fasting

## Dietary Accommodations
- Halal food options in cafeteria
- Allow employees to bring own food
- Keep kitchen and dining areas clean and free from cross-contamination with non-halal foods

## Holiday and Observance Recognition
- **Eid al-Fitr**: Day off or flexible scheduling; cultural event/celebration; gifts (dates, halal restaurant gift card); Eid Mubarak greetings
- **Eid al-Adha**: Day off or flexible scheduling; cultural event/celebration; Qurbani/Udhiyah service; charitable donations/fundraiser; Eid Mubarak greetings
- **Hajj/Umrah**: Allow time off for pilgrimage (important religious obligation)

## Dress Code and Personal Expression
- Allow modest clothing including hijabs, abayas, beards (within health and safety standards)

## Community and Support
- **Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)**: Establish faith-based ERGs for Muslim employees to provide platform for support, networking, cultural exchange
- **Cultural sensitivity training**: For all employees to promote understanding and respect

## General Guidelines for Implementation
- Consult with Muslim employees and community leaders about specific needs
- Communicate accommodations clearly and transparently to all employees
- Ensure accommodations do not create undue hardship or burden on business
- Review and update accommodations regularly for effectiveness
- Involve Muslim employees in planning and evaluation
