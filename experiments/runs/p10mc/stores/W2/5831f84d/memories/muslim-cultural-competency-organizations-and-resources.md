## Recommended Organizations

**Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR)**
- Offers cultural competency training, workshops, Employer Resource Guide

**Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)**
- Provides cultural competency training, workshops, Workplace Inclusion Toolkit

**Muslim Public Affairs Council (MPAC)**
- Offers cultural competency training, workshops, Workplace Inclusion Initiative

**Institute for Social Policy and Understanding (ISPU)**
- Research-based cultural competency training and workshops

**Islamic Circle of North America (ICNA)**
- Cultural events and resources

**Local mosques and Islamic centers**
- May offer culturally tailored training

**Harvard Pluralism Project**
- Resources on Muslim culture and traditions

## Topics Typically Covered in Training
- Demographics and diversity within Muslim community (ethnicities, cultures, Sunni/Shia/Sufi denominations)
- Islamic beliefs and practices (Five Pillars, Ramadan, Eid, daily prayers)
- Cultural nuances (greetings, hospitality, communication styles)
- Common misconceptions and stereotypes about Muslims
- Workplace accommodations for Muslim employees
- Microaggressions, bias, and Islamophobia recognition
- Effective communication strategies
- Inclusive language and practices
- Case studies and real-life scenarios
- Action planning and resources

## Selection Criteria
- Expertise and experience
- Reputation and client reviews
- Involve Muslim employees in planning and evaluation process
