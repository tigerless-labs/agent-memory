## Preservation Efforts & Tourism Impact in Yerevan

### Key Historical Sites
- **Armenian Genocide Memorial** — major cultural landmark
- **Republic Square** — central historical site  
- **Matenadaran Museum** — ancient manuscripts museum
- **Old Town** — traditional architecture preservation, primary tourist attraction shown on tours
- **Erebuni Fortress** — restored historical monument, draws history-focused visitors

### Positive Impacts on Tourism
- **Infrastructure Development** — hotels, restaurants, souvenir shops created to serve tourists
- **Increased Arrivals** — measurable growth in tourist numbers from restoration efforts
- **Cultural Promotion** — easier to market Armenian history and heritage globally
- **Visitor Engagement** — restoration creates meaningful connections to city's past

### Challenges to Preservation
1. **Financial Resources** — restoration and preservation require significant investment; government funding often limited
2. **Political Instability** — regional tensions can jeopardize ongoing preservation work and tourism industry
3. **Vandalism & Theft** — human-caused damage to cultural heritage reduces attractions
4. **Lack of Awareness** — lesser-known sites underpromoted, resulting in fewer visitors and less preservation attention
5. **Skilled Professional Shortage** — need for archaeologists, historians, architects; regional shortage leads to suboptimal restoration quality

### Path Forward
Collaborative approach needed across government, NGOs, private companies, and tourists to address challenges and strengthen preservation status.
