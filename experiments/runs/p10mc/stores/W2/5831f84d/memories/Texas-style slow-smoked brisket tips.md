**How to achieve tender, fall-apart Texas-style slow-smoked brisket at home:**

**Cut Selection:**
- Whole packer brisket (10-12 lbs) has more marbling and flavor
- Flat cut (5-6 lbs) is leaner and more forgiving
- Look for thick fat cap (at least 1/4 inch) to keep meat moist

**Preparation:**
- Trim excess fat if necessary
- Season generously with dry rub, coat all surfaces
- Let sit at room temperature 1-2 hours for rubs to penetrate
- Optional: inject with marinade or mop sauce for extra flavor

**Smoking:**
- Set smoker to 225-250°F
- Place brisket fat-side up
- Smoke for 4-5 hours until internal temperature reaches 160°F
- Wrap in foil ("Texas Crutch") to prevent drying and promote tenderization
- Continue smoking 2-3 more hours until internal temp reaches 180-190°F

**Resting:**
- Remove from smoker and rest 1-2 hours
- Wrap in towel/blanket to keep warm
- Allows juices to redistribute, makes meat more tender

**Serving:**
- Slice against the grain with sharp knife
- Serve with favorite BBQ sauce or variety of sauces

**Additional Tips:**
- Monitor smoker temperature, adjust as needed
- Spritz with mop sauce or beef broth every hour
- Requires patience and attention to detail
