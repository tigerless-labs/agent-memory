**Wood and Meat Pairing Guide for Smoking**

**Wood Types and Best Pairings:**

1. **Hickory** — Strong, sweet, smoky
   - Pairs with: ribs (beef/pork), brisket, bacon, sausages

2. **Oak** — Earthy, mellow, slightly sweet
   - Pairs with: pulled pork, chicken, turkey, vegetables

3. **Mesquite** — Strong, earthy, bitter
   - Pairs with: beef (steaks, burgers), lamb, game meats (venison, elk)

4. **Applewood** — Fruity, sweet, mild
   - Pairs with: poultry (chicken, turkey, duck), pork (tenderloins, baby back ribs), sausages

5. **Cherrywood** — Fruity, sweet, mild
   - Pairs with: poultry (chicken, turkey, duck), pork (tenderloins, baby back ribs), game meats (venison, elk)

**Meat to Wood Pairings:**
- Beef: hickory, oak, mesquite
- Pork: hickory, applewood, cherrywood
- Poultry: applewood, cherrywood, oak
- Sausages: hickory, oak, applewood
- Game Meats: mesquite, oak, cherrywood

**General Tips:**
- Start with mild wood if new to smoking (applewood, cherrywood) to avoid overpowering meat
- Experiment with wood blends for unique flavors
- Soak wood chips/chunks in water 30+ minutes for efficient burning and cleaner smoke
- Monitor smoker temperature consistently
- Practice and experiment to find perfect pairings for your taste
