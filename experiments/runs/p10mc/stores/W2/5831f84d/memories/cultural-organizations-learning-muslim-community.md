## Ways to Find Cultural Events

**Local Organizations**
- Local mosques and Islamic centers (ask about events, lectures, workshops open to public)
- Community centers (often host cultural events and activities)
- Local universities and colleges (Middle Eastern or Islamic Studies departments may have public events)

**Social Media and Online Resources**
- Follow local Muslim organizations on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram for event announcements
- Search online event calendars focused on cultural events
- Check university/college websites for public lectures

**Interfaith Organizations**
- Local interfaith organizations often host events promoting dialogue between religious communities

## Key Organizations to Contact

- **Islamic Society of North America (ISNA)**
- **Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR)**
- **Muslim Public Affairs Council (MPAC)**
- **Islamic Circle of North America (ICNA)**
- **Local Muslim women's organizations** (Muslim Women's Alliance, American Muslim Women's Empowerment Council)

## Approach Guidelines
- Approach with respect, open mind, willingness to learn
- Be mindful of cultural sensitivities and traditions
- Engage genuinely with different communities
