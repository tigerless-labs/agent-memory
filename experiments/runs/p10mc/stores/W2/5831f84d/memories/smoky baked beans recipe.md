**Smoky Baked Beans Recipe** (Serves 8-10)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pound dried navy beans, soaked overnight and drained
- 1 pound smoked bacon, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup ketchup
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon ground mustard
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup bourbon (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 250°F
2. Cook bacon until crispy, set aside, reserve drippings
3. Sauté onion in drippings until soft (~5 min), add garlic
4. Add beans, ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, Worcestershire, mustard, paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper
5. Add bacon and bourbon, stir to combine
6. Transfer to 9x13" baking dish
7. Cover with foil, bake 6 hours
8. Uncover, bake 30-60 minutes more until tender and liquid thickens
9. Rest 30+ minutes before serving

**Tips:** Can add chipotle peppers for more smokiness; can make in slow cooker on low 8-10 hours; add bell peppers, jalapeños, or hot dogs for variation.
