**Backyard BBQ Party Entertainment Ideas**

**Classic Outdoor Games:**
- Lawn Twister (use spray paint to draw board)
- Cornhole (can build DIY or purchase)
- Giant Jenga (build DIY or purchase)
- Badminton or Volleyball

**Unique Activities:**
- Water Balloon Toss (two-line teams, step back after each catch)
- Lawn Scavenger Hunt (list items to find)
- Outdoor Movie Trailer or BBQ-themed movie
- DIY Photo Booth with outdoor backdrop and props (hats, sunglasses, mustaches)

**Food-Related Entertainment:**
- Grill-Off Competition (guests showcase grilling skills)
- BBQ Sauce Tasting with voting
- Watermelon Eating Contest

**Other Ideas:**
- Upbeat summer playlist or live band
- Lawn Games Tournament (horseshoes, ladder toss, KanJam)
- Kids' Zone with sidewalk chalk, bubbles, ball pit

**Tips:**
- Keep activities simple, safe, enjoyable for all ages
- Keep guests entertained while food is being prepared
