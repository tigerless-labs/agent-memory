## Contacts Organization Project

**Status:** Planning stage (as of May 21, 2023)

**Issue:**
- Large contacts list with many unknown numbers and names
- Unclear who many contacts belong to
- Part of broader phone storage decluttering effort

**Plans:**
1. Merge duplicate contacts
2. Categorize contacts (friends, family, work, acquaintances, businesses, services)
3. Use consistent naming convention
4. Add context notes (how they met, relationship, important dates)
5. Delete unknown or unused contacts
6. Organize by frequency of contact
7. Regular review and update (every 3-6 months)
