## Research Interests in AI & Education

### Primary Focus Areas

1. **Personalized Learning & Adaptive Systems**
   - How AI tailors instruction to individual student needs, abilities, and learning styles
   - Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student outcomes

2. **Real-Time Feedback**
   - How feedback is provided through adaptive systems: automated grading, error analysis, hints, scaffolding
   - Impact of immediate feedback on student learning outcomes
   - Mechanisms for personalized feedback through NLP

3. **Critical Thinking & Problem-Solving**
   - How AI can help develop critical thinking skills
   - AI's role in developing problem-solving abilities
   - Argumentation-based systems, inquiry-based learning, simulations, and games

4. **Collaborative Problem-Solving**
   - How AI facilitates collaboration among students
   - Virtual collaboration platforms and intelligent facilitation
   - Benefits include enhanced collaboration, personalized learning, increased engagement, scalability

### Key Research Findings

- Meta-analysis of 22 studies on adaptive learning systems yielded effect size of 0.34 (moderate to large positive impact)
- Real-time feedback improves student accuracy, motivation, metacognition, and reduces anxiety
- AI-powered systems show promise in developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills
