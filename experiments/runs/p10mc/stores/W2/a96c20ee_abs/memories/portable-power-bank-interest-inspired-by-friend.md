## Portable Power Bank Interest

**Context (as of May 21, 2023):**
- Friend has a portable power bank
- Friend uses it when traveling and attending festivals
- Finds it very helpful

**Status:**

**Key factors to consider:**
- Capacity (5,000-20,000mAh or more)
- Output voltage/current compatibility
- Input charging speed (Quick Charge, USB-C Power Delivery)
- Size and weight balance
- Material and build quality
- Safety features (overcharge, short-circuit, temperature control)
- Brand reputation and certifications (UL, CE)
- Additional features (LED flashlight, solar panels, wireless charging)
