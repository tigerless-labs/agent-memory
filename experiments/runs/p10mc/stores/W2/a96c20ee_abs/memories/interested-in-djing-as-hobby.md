### What DJing is (as explained)
- Art of mixing and manipulating music tracks to create unique sound/atmosphere
- Uses equipment: turntables, CD players, digital controllers
- DJs select/play pre-recorded tracks and may create remixes
- Typically performed live at parties, clubs, and events
- History dates back to 1920s-1930s (radio broadcasting and dance clubs)

### Steps to Get Started with DJing
1. **Learn the basics**: beatmatching, mixing tracks, using equipment (online tutorials/articles)
2. **Invest in equipment**: DJ controller or turntables + software to control/mix (various price points available)
3. **Practice**: spend time honing skills, experimenting with techniques and styles (at home, with friends, or at local DJ events)
4. **Connect with other DJs**: attend DJ events, join online forums/communities, collaborate
5. **Share your music**: record and upload mixes online, perform at local events/parties
