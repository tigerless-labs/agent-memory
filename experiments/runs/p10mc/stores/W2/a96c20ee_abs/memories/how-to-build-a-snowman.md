## Materials
- 3 snowballs: large (base), medium (middle), small (head)
- Sticks for arms
- Carrot or similar for nose
- Coal or similar for eyes, mouth, buttons
- Hat and scarf (optional)

## Steps
1. Find spot with packed snow and adequate space
2. Roll large snowball for base body
3. Roll medium snowball, place on top of large
4. Roll small snowball for head, place on top
5. Insert sticks for arms (can use snow as glue)
6. Add carrot for nose (stick or glue with snow)
7. Add coal for eyes, mouth, and buttons
8. Add hat and scarf if available

**Pro tip:** Add more snow as you roll to reach desired size.
