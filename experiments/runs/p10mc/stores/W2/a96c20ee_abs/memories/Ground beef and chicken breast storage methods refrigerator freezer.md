## Refrigeration Guidelines

**Ground Beef (Cooked):**
- Store in airtight, shallow containers at 40°F (4°C) or below
- Use within 3 to 4 days
- Cool to room temperature before refrigerating

**Chicken Breasts (Cooked):**
- Store in covered containers at 40°F (4°C) or below
- Use within 3 to 4 days
- Cool to room temperature before refrigerating

## Freezing Guidelines

**Ground Beef (Cooked):**
- Wrap tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil
- Place in freezer-safe bag or airtight container
- Label and date
- Storage time: 3 to 4 months
- Thaw in refrigerator or reheat to 165°F (74°C)

**Chicken Breasts (Cooked):**
- Place in airtight container or freezer bag (remove excess air)
- Label and date
- Storage time: 4 to 6 months
- Thaw in refrigerator or reheat to 165°F (74°C)

## Best Practices

- **Portioning:** Divide into smaller portions (1/4 cup or 1/2 cup) for easier future use
- **Shredding/Dicing:** Pre-shred or dice chicken for easier use in wraps, soups, and salads
- **Cooling:** Always cool to room temperature before storing to prevent bacterial growth
- **Labeling:** Always include date, contents, and reheating instructions
- **Freezer Organization:** Store in single layer to prevent cross-contamination and ease retrieval
- **Reheating Safety:** Always reach minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)
