## Healthy Snack Ideas with Chicken

**Chicken Salads:**
- Classic: diced chicken, low-fat mayo, celery, apple (serve on whole-grain bread/crackers)
- Greek: diced chicken, Greek yogurt, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, feta cheese (serve on whole-grain pita/crackers)
- Spicy: diced chicken, low-fat mayo, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice (serve on whole-grain tortilla chips/crackers)

**Chicken Wraps:**
- Caesar wrap: diced chicken, romaine, avocado, low-fat Caesar dressing in whole-grain tortilla
- Hummus wrap: hummus spread, diced chicken, mixed greens, cucumber in whole-grain tortilla
- Chicken and avocado wrap: diced chicken, mashed avocado, mixed greens, red pepper flakes in whole-grain tortilla

**Other Healthy Snacks:**
- Chicken and veggie kabobs (chicken, bell peppers, onions, mushrooms; brush with olive oil, grill or bake)
- Chicken and quinoa bites (cooked quinoa, diced chicken, chopped herbs, lemon juice; form into bite-sized balls)
- Chicken and spinach pinwheels (cream cheese spread, diced chicken, spinach, bell peppers on whole-grain tortilla; slice into pinwheels)
- Chicken and mango skewers (alternate diced chicken and mango; brush with olive oil, grill or bake)
- Chicken and cucumber roll-ups (cream cheese spread, diced chicken, sliced cucumber, dill on whole-grain tortilla; slice into rounds)

## Meal Prep Notes

- Prepare in batch on weekends for week-long use
- Shred or dice chicken for easier use in wraps, salads, soups
- Store prepared snacks in airtight containers for up to 3 days
- Can freeze many items for longer storage (see storage guide)
