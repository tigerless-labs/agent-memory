## 1/24 Scale Car Kit Recommendations

**Easier to Moderate (Good for 6-month experience):**
- Revell 1969 Chevrolet Camaro SS (kit 85-4231) - simple construction, forgiving design, well-engineered
- Revell 1968 Shelby Mustang GT500 (kit 85-4241) - moderate part count, more complex lines than Camaro

**Moderate (Step up in complexity):**
- Tamiya Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7 (kit 24343) - higher part count, intricate details, excellent Tamiya engineering
- Aoshima Honda NSX (kit 05035) - moderate part count, complex curves, practice for bodywork and paint

**Challenging (Advanced):**
- Tamiya Nissan GT-R R35 (kit 24355) - high part count, complex engine and chassis, advanced techniques needed
- Italeri Ferrari 250 GT California Spyder (kit 3644) - high part count, complex curves, requires photo-etch
- Italeri Lamborghini Miura SV (kit 3646) - beautifully detailed supercar
- Tamiya Ford GT40 Mk II (kit 24350) - legendary racing car
- Revell Lola T70 Mk III (kit 85-4221) - classic racing car, moderate difficulty

**Selection Tips:**
- Check competition rules for kit eligibility
- Research kits online and read reviews
- Look at built examples for sense of complexity
