## Recommended Restaurants in French Quarter

1. **Dooky Chase's Restaurant** — NOLA institution since 1941; gumbo z'herbes, jambalaya, Creole classics; adjacent art gallery
2. **Cafe du Monde** — iconic 24/7 cafe; beignets and coffee; people-watching
3. **Coop's Place** — casual Cajun; red beans and rice, gumbo, fried chicken; live music
4. **Galatoire's** — historic since 1905; shrimp remoulade; upscale dining
5. **Willie Mae's Scotch House** — James Beard Award-winner; fried chicken, gumbo, shrimp and grits
6. **GW Fins** — upscale seafood; scallops, lobster, oysters
7. **Johnny's Po-Boys** — casual since 1950; fried oyster or shrimp po'boys with gumbo
8. **Brennan's Restaurant** — NOLA institution; Creole cuisine; bananas Foster dessert
9. **Cafe Maspero** — cozy; breakfast/lunch with beignets, omelets, sandwiches
10. **Port of Call** — casual; burgers, sandwiches, salads; outdoor seating

Also explore street food vendors, beignet cafes, po'boy shops throughout the Quarter
