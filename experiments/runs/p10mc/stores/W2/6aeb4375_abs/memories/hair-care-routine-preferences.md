## Hair Care Learnings (April 2023)

**When to brush:**
- Wait until hair is dry before brushing
- Wet hair is weakest, most susceptible to breakage and damage
- If brushing wet hair, use wide-tooth comb only
- Start brushing at ends, work up to roots (don't pull down from roots)

**Products planned:**
- Protein-based conditioners: strengthen hair strands, prevent breakage
- Leave-in conditioners: nourish, protect, provide moisture
- Hair masks: deep hydration for damaged/weakened hair
- Heat protectants: if using heat styling tools

**Specific brand recommendations to investigate:**
1. Olaplex No. 3 Hair Perfector
2. Kerastase Resistance Length Strengthening Scalp Serum
3. Redken Extreme Anti-Snap Leave-In Treatment
4. Briogeo Don't Despair, Repair! Strengthening Treatment Oil

**Other prevention habits:**
- Reduce heat styling tools
- Avoid tight hairstyles
- Get regular trims for split ends
- Use gentle hair care routine
