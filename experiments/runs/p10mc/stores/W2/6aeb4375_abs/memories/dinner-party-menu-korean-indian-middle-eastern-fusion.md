## Dinner Party Menu — Multi-Cuisine Fusion

**Date:** May 26, 2023
**Concept:** Korean, Indian, and Middle Eastern cuisine blend

### Main & Proteins
- Korean-style BBQ beef
- Middle Eastern falafel

### Side Dishes
- **Kimchi** — spicy fermented Korean cabbage
- **Bokkeumbap** — Korean-style fried rice
- **Samosas** — Indian fried pastries with spiced potatoes and peas
- **Saag aloo** — spinach with potatoes, garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, garam masala

### Rice
- **Basmati rice** (traditional Indian) — decided against using Japanese short-grain rice due to texture differences (short-grain is too sticky for Indian dishes)
- Recommended brands: Tilda, India Gate, Daawat, Zebra, Lundberg
- To purchase from store (May 26, 2023)

### Basmati Rice Selection Criteria
- Distinctive nutty and buttery aroma
- Long, slender grains that are separate, not clumpy
- Light and fluffy texture with slightly firm bite
- Prefer older rice (better maturity and flavor)
- Cook with correct water ratio and soak before cooking
