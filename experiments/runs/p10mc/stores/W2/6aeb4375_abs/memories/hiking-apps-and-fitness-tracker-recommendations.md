## Recommended Hiking Apps

### GPS & Trail Tracking
1. **AllTrails** — Comprehensive trail database, GPS tracking, hiker reviews (iOS/Android)
2. **MapMyHike** — GPS tracking, route/distance/elevation/calories, custom routes (iOS/Android)
3. **Gaia GPS** — Topographic maps, trail data, route planning; popular for backpacking (iOS/Android)
4. **Hiking Project** — Free app from REI, vast trail database, GPS tracking, reviews (iOS/Android)
5. **PeakFinder** — Identify mountains/peaks using camera and GPS (iOS/Android)

### Social & Motivation
6. **Strava** — Track hikes, compete with friends, join challenges, discover trails (iOS/Android)

### Fitness Integration
7. **Fitbit app** — Sync with Fitbit device to track hikes and overall fitness progress (iOS/Android)
8. **Hiking Journal** — Simple app to log hikes, track progress, add photos and notes (iOS only)

### Other Tools
- **GPS watches**: Garmin, Suunto, Fitbit Ionic (GPS tracking, heart rate, fitness features)
- **Paper maps and guides**: Traditional backup for navigation
- **Hiking blogs/books**: Inspiration and recommendations for new trails

## Recommended Fitness Trackers

### Budget-Friendly
1. **Fitbit Charge** — $70
   - Steps, distance, calories burned, sleep tracking
   - User-friendly, entry-level option
   
2. **Samsung Galaxy Fit** — $100
   - Activity, sleep, stress monitoring
   - Built-in GPS
   - Integration with Samsung Health
   - Sleek design

### Mid-Range
3. **Fitbit Inspire** — $100
   - Steps, distance, calories, sleep
   - GPS tracking
   - Guided breathing sessions
   - Female health tracking

4. **Garmin Vivosport** — $200
   - GPS-enabled for running/cycling/activities
   - Sleep, stress, heart rate monitoring
   - Guided breathing sessions

### Premium
5. **Apple Watch Series 3** — $250
   - Wide range of features
   - GPS tracking
   - Heart rate monitoring
   - Apple Health integration

## Fitness Tracker Selection Criteria
- **Accuracy**: Step tracking, distance measurement, heart rate monitoring
- **Comfort**: Wearable without skin irritation
- **Water resistance**: 50m+ if planning water activities
- **Battery life**: At least 5 days per charge
- **App compatibility**: Works with iOS or Android
- **Additional features**: GPS, sleep, stress, breathing sessions
- **Price**: Budget $70-$250

## Recommendation
Suggest trying different apps first before investing in hardware. AllTrails and Hiking Project are good free starting points. For fitness tracking, Fitbit Charge ($70) offers good value for beginners.
