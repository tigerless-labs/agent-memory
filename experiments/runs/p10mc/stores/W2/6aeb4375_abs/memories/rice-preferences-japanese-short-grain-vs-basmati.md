## Rice Preferences

**Current rice:** Japanese short-grain rice (recently adopted)
- Best suited for sushi and Japanese dishes
- Not recommended for Indian cuisine due to stickiness

**Decision (May 26, 2023):** Will use traditional Indian basmati rice for saag aloo and other Indian dishes instead of short-grain rice, because:
- Basmati has long, slender grains (separate, not clumpy)
- Basmati is less sticky and clingier than Japanese short-grain
- Basmati pairs better with Indian spices and seasonings
- Different water ratios and cooking times required for each rice type

**Action:** Purchase basmati rice from store (May 26, 2023)
