## Party Details

**Confirmed Stations:**
1. **Basketball** – hoop area with activities
2. **Baseball** – throwing/hitting area
3. **Horse Racing** – photo booth with jockey props
4. **Formula 1** – racing simulator, Monaco-inspired cocktail bar with upscale drinks ("Pit Stop," "Checkered Flag"), F1 car cutouts, pit stop challenge, F1 trivia, checkered flag decor
5. **Tennis** – mini tennis court or "hit the target" game, tennis ball toss, tennis-themed trivia
6. **Premier League Soccer** – mini soccer field, penalty shootout, soccer ball toss game, Premier League trivia
7. **Fantasy Football** – mock draft station using draft software, draft board on large screen, fantasy football experts to facilitate, creative team names, prizes for winners

**Decoration & Atmosphere Ideas:**
- Stadium backdrop with scoreboard
- Balloon arches in sport colors
- Sports equipment hanging decor
- Jersey garland from different teams
- Sport-specific banners
- Trophy centerpieces
- Scoreboard-themed snacks and drinks
- High-energy, multi-sport music playlist
