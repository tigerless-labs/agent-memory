## Recommended Beginner-Friendly Trails Near Denver
1. **Chautauqua Trail**, Chautauqua Park, Boulder
   - Distance: 20-minute drive from Denver
   - Length: 2.1-mile loop
   - Elevation gain: 400 ft
   - Features: Well-maintained, scenic views of Flatirons and Boulder
   
2. **Bear Creek Lake Park - Owl's Perch Trail**, Lakewood
   - Distance: 25-minute drive
   - Length: 2.5-mile loop
   - Elevation gain: 200 ft
   - Features: Peaceful trail around lake, wildlife spotting opportunities
   
3. **Dinosaur Ridge - Triceratops Trail**, Morrison
   - Distance: 25-minute drive
   - Length: 2.3-mile out-and-back
   - Elevation gain: 300 ft
   - Features: Dinosaur fossil site with educational signs
   
4. **Lookout Mountain - Windy Saddle Trail**, Golden
   - Distance: 25-minute drive
   - Length: 2.5-mile out-and-back
   - Elevation gain: 400 ft
   - Features: Scenic views of Rocky Mountains and Great Plains
   
5. **South Table Mountain - Mesa Trail**, Golden
   - Distance: 20-minute drive
   - Length: 2.5-mile loop
   - Elevation gain: 300 ft
   - Features: Gentle, well-marked, panoramic Denver metro and Rocky Mountain views

## Hiking Safety Reminders Provided
- Check trail conditions before heading out
- Bring water, snacks, sun protection
- Wear comfortable hiking shoes/boots with good traction
- Carry map, compass, or GPS device
- Let someone know hiking plans and estimated return time
