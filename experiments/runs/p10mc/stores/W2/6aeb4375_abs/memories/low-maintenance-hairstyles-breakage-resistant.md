## Low-Maintenance Hairstyles for Hair Health

User priority: low-maintenance styles that minimize breakage (April 2023)

**Recommended styles:**

1. Low ponytail — keeps hair away from face, prevents tangles, classic and easy
2. Messy bun — stylish, effortless, keeps hair off neck and face when on the go
3. Braids — secures hair, minimizes tangles and breakage (three-strand, fishtail, Dutch)
4. Bun with scrunchie — scrunchies more gentle than elastic hair ties
5. Wet bun — gather fully wet hair into low bun with pins or clips, reduces friction-caused breakage

**Key principle:**
- Avoid tight styles that pull hair or stress scalp
- Avoid regular hair elastics; prefer scrunchies
