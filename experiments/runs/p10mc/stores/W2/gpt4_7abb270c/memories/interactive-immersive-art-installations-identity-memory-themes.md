## Artistic Direction (January 2023)

User is exploring interactive and immersive art installations as their medium for artistic expression.

### Themes of Interest:
- Identity, memory, and the human experience
- Collective memory and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts
- Creating empathy and connection through immersive experiences

### Specific Installation Concept:
Creating installations that incorporate sound, light, and texture to recreate the sensation of being in a specific environment or memory.

**Example project:** A forest installation with:
- Bird sounds (chirping)
- Rustling leaves (auditory)
- Scent of damp earth
- Multi-sensory immersion of being in nature

### Secondary Concept:
An installation incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods to explore shared human experience and collective memory.

This represents the user's current creative focus and artistic vision.
