## TV Shopping Research

**LG OLED Strengths**
- Panel quality: Perfect blacks, infinite contrast, wide viewing angles
- webOS platform: User-friendly smart TV interface
- Wide color gamut and multiple HDR support (Dolby Vision, HDR10, HLG)
- Low input lag for gaming
- Price: Generally more affordable than Sony
- Popular models: C9 (2019), B9 (2019), CX (2020), GX (2020)

**Sony OLED Strengths**
- Panel quality: Excellent blacks, wide viewing angles, color accuracy
- X1 Ultimate processor: Advanced picture processing with HDR remastering
- Acoustic Surface Audio: Screen used as speaker for immersive audio
- Android TV platform: Google Assistant built-in, wide app range
- Advanced gaming features: HDMI 2.1, variable refresh rate (VRR)
- Popular models: A9G (2019), A8G (2019), A1E (2020), A9S (2020)

**Key Comparison Factors**
- Panel quality: Both excellent; LG slight edge in color accuracy
- Smart platform: LG webOS generally more user-friendly
- Gaming: Sony more advanced features
- Price: LG typically more affordable
