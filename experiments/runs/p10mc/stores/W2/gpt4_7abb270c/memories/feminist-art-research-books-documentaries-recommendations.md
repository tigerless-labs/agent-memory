## Recommended Books
- "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard - comprehensive history 1960s-1990s
- "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss - explores relationship between feminist art and maternal, features Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles
- "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin - essays on women in art, feminism in art history
- "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard - detailed US 1970s focus
- "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls - essays and artworks from anonymous feminist collective

## Recommended Documentaries

## Key Artist: Judy Chicago
- Born Judith Sylvia Cohen, 1939, Chicago Illinois
- Education: University of Illinois, Art Institute of Chicago, UCLA MFA 1964
- Early work influenced by Abstract Expressionism
- Shifted to feminist themes in late 1960s
- **The Dinner Party** (1974-1979) - monumental installation with 39 place settings celebrating women from history/mythology; now in Brooklyn Museum permanent collection
- **Birth Project** (1980-1985) - paintings, weavings, sculptures on birth and motherhood
- Taught at CalArts, UCLA, Duke University
- 2018 National Medal of Arts recipient
- Continues inspiring contemporary artists
