## Art Conservation Learning Goals

**Primary Interest:** Pursuing art conservation as a hobby with focus on preserving historical artifacts and cultural heritage

**Materials & Techniques to Learn:**

**Adhesives:**
- Acrylic polymers (PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV)
- Animal glues (hide glue, bone glue)
- Cellulose-based adhesives (rice starch paste, wheat starch paste)
- Epoxy resins (Araldite, Epon)
- Natural waxes (beeswax, paraffin wax)

**Consolidants:**
- Acrylic polymers (Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98)
- Silanes
- Parylene
- Natural waxes

**Conservation Papers:**
- Cotton paper (acid-free, archival-quality)
- Linen paper
- Rag paper
- Kozu paper (Japanese, for Asian artworks)
- Gampi paper (Japanese, for Asian artworks)
- Key properties: acid-free, lignin-free, high alpha-cellulose content

**Mounting & Framing Techniques:**
- Hinging (reversible adhesive, rice starch or PVA)
- Window mounting
- Float mounting
- Conservation framing (acid-free, lignin-free, UV-filtering)
- Reversible framing
- Glazing (glass or acrylic)

**Coatings & Protective Materials:**
- Varnishes (dammar, acrylic)
- Lacquers
- Waxes
- Acrylic polymers

**Safety & Considerations:**
- Work in well-ventilated areas
- Wear protective gear (gloves, goggles, masks)
- Prioritize reversibility in all techniques
- Test material compatibility before use
- Follow proper handling procedures
- Match materials to artwork's age and composition

**Resources Mentioned:**
- American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC)
- International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC)
- Conservation Wiki
- YouTube channels: Art Conservation, Conservation Framing, The Getty Conservation Institute
- Books: "The Conservation of Art and Architecture" (Watt), "The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory" (Keck), "Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide" (Stubbs)
- Workshops at local museums and galleries
- Online courses on Coursera, edX, Udemy
