## Marathon Training Plan (May 2022)

**Plan Selected:** Hal Higdon's Novice Marathon Training Plan (18-week plan)
- Assumes ability to run 3 miles without stopping
- 4 runs per week + cross-training
- Includes long weekend runs, rest days, cross-training integration

**Previous Endurance Experience:**
- Sprint triathlon completed in San Diego in late February 2022
- Building marathon training on existing triathlon endurance base

**Current Training Elements:**
- **Cycling:** Using cycling for endurance improvements; reports it's helping with running performance
- **Cross-training frequency:** 1-2 times per week
- **Strength training:** 2-3 sessions per week targeting core and legs
- **Hill repeats:** Planning to incorporate; 1-2x per week with at least one rest day between

**Cross-Training Options Being Used:**
- Cycling (stationary or outdoor)
- Strength training (core, glutes, legs)
- Considering: swimming, elliptical, yoga/pilates

**Sample Weekly Structure:**
- Monday: Easy run (3 miles)
- Tuesday: Cycling (30 min, easy)
- Wednesday: Speed workout (4 miles)
- Thursday: Strength training (30 min)
- Friday: Easy run (3 miles)
- Saturday: Long run (variable distance)
- Sunday: Rest or active recovery
