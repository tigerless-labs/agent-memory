## Real-World Scenarios: CFO Goals in Action

### 1. Renewable Energy Tech Company — Revenue Forecasting
**Scenario:** CFO forecasting Q-next revenue and profits for renewable energy technology startup
**Goal:** Improve demand prediction for products/services
**Benefit:** Identify high-demand products, allocate resources strategically, capitalize on opportunities, increase revenue/profits

### 2. Software & Patents Company — Intangible Asset Valuation
**Scenario:** CFO valuing company's intangible assets (software IP, patents)
**Goal:** Accurately value patents and software IP
**Benefit:** Understand true company value, make better investment decisions, inform partnership discussions

### 3. Product Launch — Balancing Short & Long-term
**Scenario:** CFO planning to launch new product while maintaining R&D investments
**Goal:** Balance quarterly financial goals with long-term strategic investments
**Benefit:** Secure resources for product launch AND sustain R&D pipeline, ensure company longevity

### 4. Market Expansion — Liquidity Management
**Scenario:** CFO planning expansion into new geographic market
**Goal:** Maintain high liquidity reserves for expansion
**Benefit:** Capitalize on expansion opportunities, ensure resources available for successful market entry

### 5. Multi-Project Portfolio — Resource Allocation
**Scenario:** CFO managing multiple concurrent projects and product lines
**Goal:** Efficiently allocate resources to most profitable initiatives
**Benefit:** Maximize ROI, ensure resources flow to highest-value projects, improve overall company profitability

### 6. Performance Measurement — High-Tech Metrics
**Scenario:** CFO measuring financial performance using traditional metrics (which don't fit high-tech reality)
**Goal:** Develop high-tech-specific financial performance metrics
**Benefit:** Accurately measure company performance, enable better strategic decisions

### Context
All scenarios assume CFO needs better FP&A tools and data capabilities to achieve goals effectively.
