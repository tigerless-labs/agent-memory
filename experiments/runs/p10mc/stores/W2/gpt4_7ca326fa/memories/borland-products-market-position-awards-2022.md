## Borland Software Platform — Products, Market Position, and Awards

### Target Market
- Software developers
- IT managers
- Business professionals

### Key Products and Services

**Application Lifecycle Management (ALM)**
- Requirements management
- Project management
- Test management
- Version control
- Emphasizes agile methodologies
- Strong focus on collaboration tools for dev/ops teams

**Software Testing**
- Silk Test
- Silk Central
- Known for test automation capabilities
- Seamless integration with other development tools

**Continuous Delivery & DevOps**
- Silk Performer
- Silk Central (also used for continuous delivery)
- Speeds up software delivery cycles
- Reduces error and failure risks
- Automates build, test, and deployment processes

### Market Differentiation
- Emphasis on continuous delivery and DevOps
- Strong focus on agile methodologies
- Extensive automation capabilities
- Collaboration between development and operations teams
- Process modernization for businesses moving away from waterfall methods

### Main Competitors
- Microsoft
- IBM
- Oracle

### Industry Awards & Recognition
- **SD Times 100** — 2019, 2020 (recognized for innovation and leadership)
- **Gartner Magic Quadrant** — Leader in Software Test Automation
- **Forrester Wave** — Q3 2018, Strong Performer for Omnichannel Functional Test Automation Tools
- **European Software Testing Awards** — 2017 (Best Agile Project, Best Test Automation Project)
- **DevOps Excellence Awards** — 2018 (Best DevOps Automation Project)

### Summary
Borland's competitive advantage lies in its integrated approach to agile development, continuous delivery, and DevOps practices, with particular strength in test automation and team collaboration.
