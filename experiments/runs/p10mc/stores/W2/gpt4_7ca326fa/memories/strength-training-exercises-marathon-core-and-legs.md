## Marathon Strength Training Exercises

**Core Exercises (3-5 sets each):**
1. **Plank** - 30-60 seconds, 3-5 sets (targets abs, obliques, lower back)
2. **Russian twists** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps (lie on back, knees bent, twist torso)
3. **Leg raises** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps (lie on back, raise legs toward ceiling without touching floor)
4. **Side plank** - 30-60 seconds each side, 3-5 sets (targets obliques and glutes)

**Leg Exercises (3 sets, 10-15 reps each unless noted):**
1. **Squats** - feet shoulder-width apart, lower down keeping back straight, knees behind toes
2. **Lunges** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps per leg (front knee behind toes, back knee near ground)
3. **Deadlifts** - using dumbbells/barbell, lift to hip level, squeeze glutes
4. **Calf raises** - on step/platform with heels off edge, 3 sets of 10-15 reps
5. **Step-ups** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps per leg (step onto platform, bring other foot up)
6. **Glute bridges** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps (lie on back, lift hips squeezing glutes)
7. **Leg press machine** - 3 sets of 10-15 reps

**Implementation Tips:**
- Start with lighter weights, progress gradually
- Focus on proper form to target correct muscles and prevent injury
- Prioritize glute-targeting exercises (squats, lunges, deadlifts) for running
- Aim for 2-3 strength training sessions per week with at least one rest day between
- Allow muscles to repair and rebuild with rest/recovery days

**Progression:** Start lighter, increase load as strength builds
