## Hill Repeats Training

**What They Are:**
Running up a hill at high intensity, then walking/jogging back down to recover. Cycle is repeated multiple times.

**Benefits:**
1. **Improved endurance** - builds anaerobic endurance (body's ability to sustain high-intensity efforts)
2. **Increased strength** - targets legs, glutes, core for strength and power building
3. **Enhanced running efficiency** - improves running form and efficiency through leg/arm propulsion
4. **Mental toughness** - challenging workouts build confidence and mental resilience

**How to Incorporate:**

**Hill Selection:**
- Find a hill taking 1-3 minutes to climb
- Steep enough to challenge but maintain good form
- Avoid hills so steep running form breaks down

**Programming:**
- Frequency: 1-2 times per week with at least one rest day between
- Start with shorter repeats (2-3 minutes), gradually increase duration
- Mix up length and intensity to prevent plateaus

**Workout Structure:**
1. Warm up: 10-15 minutes easy running
2. Run up hill at high intensity for 2 minutes
3. Walk or jog down to recover
4. Repeat for 3-5 sets
5. Cool down: 10-15 minutes easy running

**Form Tips:**
- Maintain strong posture, quick turnover
- Focus on driving legs and arms
- Adjust intensity/duration based on how body feels
