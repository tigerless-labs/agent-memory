## Overview
4-day family trip to Chiang Mai with children aged 6–13. Requirements: attractions within 1-hour drive, all rated ≥4 on Google, lunch/dinner daily (non-vegetarian, ≥4 rating), foot massage + dessert each evening.

## Day 1

### Attractions
1. **Doi Suthep-Pui National Park**
   - Description: Mountain peak with temple, hiking trails, waterfalls, scenic views
   - Address: Tambon Su Thep, Amphoe Mueang Chiang Mai, Chang Wat Chiang Mai 50200, Thailand
   - Google Rating: 4.6
   - Hours: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
   - Entry: Adult 30 THB, Child 10 THB

2. **Huay Tung Tao Lake**
   - Description: Serene lake, picnic spot, paddle boat rentals
   - Address: Mae Rim, Chiang Mai, Thailand
   - Google Rating: 4.5

3. **Chiang Mai Night Bazaar**
   - Description: Bustling market with souvenirs, clothes, street food
   - Address: Chang Khlan Rd, Tambon Chang Khlan, Amphoe Mueang Chiang Mai, Chang Wat Chiang Mai 50100, Thailand
   - Google Rating: 4.1
   - Hours: 6:00 PM – 12:00 AM

### Lunch: Khao Soi Mae Sai
