South China Sea Dispute — Negotiation Barriers and Strategies

**Conversation date:** May 9, 2023

## Barriers Identified
- Cultural differences in priorities (China's historical claims vs Southeast Asian stability focus)
- Linguistic barriers causing miscommunication
- Lack of mutual understanding between parties

## Successful Negotiation Strategies
1. Professional interpreters — relatively successful
2. Cultural mediators familiar with both cultures
3. Building personal relationships and trust
4. Patient, persistent approach
5. Focusing on shared values like regional stability

## Strategy Effectiveness
- Interpreters: working well for linguistic barriers
- Personal relationships: slower but valuable for trust-building
- Shared values: mixed results due to disagreements on territorial claims and resources
