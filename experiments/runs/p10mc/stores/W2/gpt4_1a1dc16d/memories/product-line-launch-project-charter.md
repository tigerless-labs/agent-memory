## Project Charter: New Product Line Launch

**Project Title:** Launch of New Product Line for Major Retail Client

## Project Objective

Successfully launch a new product line for major retail client, meeting their business objectives and revenue targets. Comprehensive marketing strategy will drive sales, increase brand awareness, and enhance customer engagement.

## Project Scope

The project includes:
- Comprehensive marketing strategy development
- Product packaging, point-of-sale materials, and marketing collateral creation
- E-commerce functionality design and development on client's website
- Product content creation (descriptions, videos, images)
- Social media and email marketing campaign development and execution
- Coordination with retail client for in-store displays and promotions
- New product line launch execution

## Project Timeline

- **Week 1-2 (March 15-28):** Project kickoff and planning
- **Week 3-4 (March 29-April 11):** Product line development and creative assets creation
- **Week 5-6 (April 12-25):** Content creation and e-commerce development
- **Week 7-8 (April 26-May 9):** Social media and email marketing campaign development
- **Week 9-10 (May 10-23):** Launch preparations and testing
- **Week 11-12 (May 24-June 1):** Launch and post-launch activities

## Project Stakeholders

- Senior Marketing Manager (Project Lead)
- Graphic Designer(s)
- Copywriter(s)
- Photographer/Videographer
- Social Media Specialist
- Email Marketing Specialist
- E-commerce Developer
- Creative Director
- Product Manager (Retail Client)
- 10 Project Team Members total

## Key Deliverables

- Comprehensive marketing strategy document
- Product packaging and point-of-sale materials
- Marketing collateral (brochures, flyers, etc.)
- E-commerce functionality on client's website
- Product content (descriptions, videos, images)
- Social media and email marketing campaigns
- Launch plan and post-launch report

## Identified Risks and Assumptions

**Risks:**
- Delays in creative assets development may impact launch timeline
- Technical issues with e-commerce functionality may impact launch

**Assumptions:**
- Retail client will provide timely feedback and approvals on marketing materials
- Project team will have access to necessary resources and tools

## Purpose

Ensure all project team members are aligned and working toward the same goal with clear understanding of objectives, scope, deliverables, and responsibilities.
