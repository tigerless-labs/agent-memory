## Project Overview

**Project:** Launch of New Product Line for Major Retail Client
**Launch Date:** June 1, 2023
**Role:** Senior Marketing Manager (project lead)
**Team Size:** 10 people
**Client:** Major retail client

## Project Timeline

- **Week 1-2 (March 15-28):** Project kickoff and planning
- **Week 3-4 (March 29-April 11):** Product line development and research
- **Week 5-6 (April 12-25):** Creative assets development
  - Product packaging design (deadline April 18)
  - Point-of-sale materials design (deadline April 20)
  - Product image/video creation (deadline April 22)
  - Marketing collateral design (deadline April 24)
- **Week 7-8 (April 26-May 9):** Content creation
  - Product descriptions (deadline May 1)
  - Social media content calendar (deadline May 3)
  - Email marketing campaigns (deadline May 5)
  - Website content (deadline May 8)
- **Week 9-10 (May 10-23):** E-commerce development and testing
- **Week 11-12 (May 24-June 1):** Launch preparations and soft launch
- **June 1:** Launch day and post-launch activities

## Key Team Roles

- Senior Marketing Manager (project lead)
- Graphic Designer(s) — packaging, POS materials, marketing collateral
- Copywriter(s) — product descriptions, website content
- Photographer/Videographer — product images and videos
- Social Media Specialist — content calendar and campaigns
- Email Marketing Specialist — email campaign development
- E-commerce Developer — website functionality and integration
- Creative Director
- Product Manager (Retail Client representative)

## Project Scope

- Comprehensive marketing strategy development
- Product packaging and point-of-sale materials design
- Marketing collateral (brochures, flyers, etc.)
- E-commerce functionality on client's website
- Product content (descriptions, videos, high-quality images)
- Social media and email marketing campaigns
- In-store displays and promotions coordination
- Launch plan and post-launch evaluation

## Key Stakeholders

All roles listed above, plus senior leadership team members
