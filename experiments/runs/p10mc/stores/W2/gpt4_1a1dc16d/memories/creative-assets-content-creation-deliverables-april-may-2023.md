## Creative Assets Development Phase

**Timeline:** April 12-25, 2023

### Task 1: Product Packaging Design
- **Assignee:** Graphic Designer
- **Deadline:** April 18
- **Deliverables:**
  - 3-4 design concept options
  - Finalized packaging design files (print-ready)

### Task 2: Point-of-Sale Materials Design
- **Assignee:** Graphic Designer
- **Deadline:** April 20
- **Deliverables:**
  - Design concepts for posters, flyers, banners
  - Finalized design files for POS materials

### Task 3: Product Image & Video Creation
- **Assignee:** Photographer/Videographer
- **Deadline:** April 22
- **Deliverables:**
  - High-quality product images (minimum 10 per product)
  - Product videos (minimum 3 per product)

### Task 4: Marketing Collateral Design
- **Assignee:** Graphic Designer
- **Deadline:** April 24
- **Deliverables:**
  - Design concepts for brochures, flyers, etc.
  - Finalized design files

## Content Creation Phase

**Timeline:** April 26-May 9, 2023

### Task 1: Product Descriptions
- **Assignee:** Copywriter
- **Deadline:** May 1
- **Deliverables:**
  - E-commerce product descriptions (minimum 500 words per product)
  - Product benefits and technical specifications

### Task 2: Social Media Content
- **Assignee:** Social Media Specialist
- **Deadline:** May 3
- **Deliverables:**
  - Social media content calendar (minimum 4 weeks)
  - Post assets (images, videos, etc.)

### Task 3: Email Marketing Campaigns
- **Assignee:** Email Marketing Specialist
- **Deadline:** May 5
- **Deliverables:**
  - Minimum 2 email campaign concepts
  - Finalized creative assets (subject lines, copy, images)

### Task 4: Website Content
- **Assignee:** Copywriter
- **Deadline:** May 8
- **Deliverables:**
  - Website content (product pages, About Us sections, etc.)
  - SEO-optimized meta tags and descriptions

## Key Considerations

- Assign tasks based on team members' strengths and expertise
- Set up regular check-ins and progress updates
- Encourage collaboration and feedback for consistency across assets
- Consider shared document or project management tool for progress tracking
- Break down into smaller, actionable items for team accountability
