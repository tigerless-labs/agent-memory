## Overview
Discussion about how big market MLB teams have reacted to rising free agent salaries and how the COVID-19 pandemic affected spending patterns.

## Teams and Spending Patterns
- **New York Yankees**: Historically big spenders; became more cautious in recent years, focusing on young talent development and staying under luxury tax threshold
- **Los Angeles Dodgers**: Used to be biggest spenders; shifted to more efficient spending; can build strong teams without breaking the bank on top free agents
- **Boston Red Sox**: Became more cautious after winning World Series in 2018; traded high-priced players to get under luxury tax threshold
- **Houston Astros & Chicago Cubs**: Success through developing young talent core rather than signing expensive free agents

## Specific Player Signings Mentioned
- **Trevor Bauer** to New York Mets: record-breaking contract
- **Blake Snell** to San Diego Padres: high-profile trade
- **Yu Darvish** to San Diego Padres: free agent signing

## Key Factors Shaping Team Decisions
1. **Analytics Revolution**: Teams can now more accurately analyze player performance and identify young talent potential; allows salary savings
2. **Luxury Tax Threshold**: Penalizes teams for exceeding spending limits; makes multiple high-priced free agent signings financially risky
3. **Sustainable Success Model**: Recognition that building homegrown player core leads to long-term success
4. **COVID-19 Pandemic Impact**: Reduced revenue from lack of fans and TV deal impacts; made teams more cautious with spending
5. **Financial Stability**: Increased emphasis post-pandemic on ensuring long-term viability

## Trends Identified
- Shift from high-priced free agent signings to farm system development
- Preference for shorter, more flexible contracts over long-term deals worth hundreds of millions
- Continued advantage for big market teams despite spending caution (larger revenue sources, fan bases)
- Potential for increased parity if smaller market teams benefit from big market caution, though this remains uncertain

## Uncertainty
- Long-term impact of pandemic on team spending post-recovery unknown
- Whether parity will increase or if big market teams will maintain structural advantages
