## Organizations Addressing Social Isolation + Food Insecurity

1. **Meals on Wheels America**: Delivers meals to seniors; focuses on combating social isolation through friendly visits and social interaction during delivery

2. **Feeding America**: Largest hunger-relief organization in US; partners to provide social services (counseling, case management) alongside food assistance

3. **Senior Corps**: Corporation for National and Community Service program; connects seniors with volunteer opportunities to combat isolation; partners for meal delivery

4. **Little Brothers - Friends of the Elderly**: Focuses on alleviating loneliness among seniors through companionship, food delivery, and services

5. **Catholic Charities USA**: Food banks, meal delivery, social services (counseling, case management) supporting individuals/families in need

6. **The Salvation Army**: Food assistance, housing support, and social services addressing multiple needs

7. **Local Non-profits and Faith-based Organizations**: Many recognize importance of addressing both issues; provide meals, companionship, support services
   - User's church: Organized charity event focused on collecting donations; potential for future involvement

## Key Insight

Social isolation is a root cause of food insecurity; addressing both together creates more holistic support for vulnerable populations.
