Historical figure: Erik Jan Hanussen
- Birth name: Hermann Steinschneider
- Dates: 1889–1933
- Born in Vienna, Austria
- Performed in Berlin during 1920s–early 1930s
- Famous for mind reading, prediction, stage magic, hypnotism
- Published: "Hanussen's Bunte Wochenschau" newspaper with astrological predictions and political commentary
- Claimed connections to high-ranking Nazi officials including Hitler
- Reportedly helped members of Jewish community escape persecution
- Found dead in forest outside Berlin in 1933; death circumstances still unclear
- Remembered as enigmatic figure who mixed entertainment with historical influence
