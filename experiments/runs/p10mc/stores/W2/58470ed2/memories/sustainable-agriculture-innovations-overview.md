## Five Sustainable Agriculture Innovations

1. **Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA)**
   - Includes hydroponics, aquaponics, and vertical farms
   - Year-round production independent of weather
   - Reduces pest and disease impact
   - Increases productivity and reduces land use

2. **Precision Agriculture**
   - Uses sensors, drones, and data analytics
   - Monitors soil conditions, moisture, crop health
   - More efficient resource use
   - Reduces water and fertilizer use; improves yields

3. **Regenerative Agriculture**
   - Focuses on soil health, biodiversity, ecosystem resilience
   - Practices: crop rotation, cover crops, reduced tillage
   - Increases soil organic matter
   - Reduces erosion; promotes biodiversity

4. **Plant-Based Protein**
   - Alternative to traditional animal protein
   - Requires significantly less water and land
   - Reduces greenhouse gas emissions
   - Reduces zoonotic disease risk

5. **Food Waste Reduction**
   - Prevents wastage during production, transportation, storage, consumption
   - Reduces emissions; conserves resources
   - Improves food security
