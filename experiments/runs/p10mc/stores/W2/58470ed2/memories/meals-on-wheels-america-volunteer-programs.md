## Meals on Wheels America Volunteer Programs

**Primary Volunteer Roles:**
1. **Meal Delivery Volunteers**: Pick up meals and deliver to seniors/individuals with disabilities
2. **Kitchen Volunteers**: Assist with meal preparation, packaging, labeling
3. **Administrative Volunteers**: Phones, data entry, special projects
4. **Fundraising and Event Volunteers**: Organize and participate in fundraising events (charity walks, golf tournaments, galas, etc.)
5. **Client Visitor Volunteers**: Provide social interaction and companionship during/outside meal delivery

**How to Get Involved:**
1. Visit www.mealsonwheelsamerica.org and use "Find a Program" tool to locate local program
2. Contact local program directly
3. Complete application (may include background check, references)
4. Attend orientation (policies, procedures, expectations)
5. Start volunteering

**Typical Requirements:**
- Age 18+ (varies by program)
- May require minimum time commitment (1 hour/week or 1 day/month)
- May need to provide transportation for delivery
- Flexible scheduling often available
- Some roles may require food safety training or additional certification

## Fundraising and Event Volunteer Opportunities

**Types of Events:**
- Charity walks/runs
- Golf tournaments
- Food festivals
- Wine and dine events
- Bake sales
- Restaurant partnerships
- Online fundraising campaigns

**Common Event Volunteer Roles:**
1. **Event Planning Committee**: Venue, catering, entertainment, logistics (needs event planning experience, organizational skills)
2. **Sponsorship Outreach**: Secure sponsorships from local businesses (needs sales/fundraising experience, strong communication)
3. **Marketing/Promotion**: Social media, email, press releases (needs marketing experience, social media skills)
4. **Volunteer Recruitment and Management**: Recruit and manage event volunteers (needs leadership, communication skills)
5. **Day-of-Event Support**: Setup, registration, logistics, teardown (needs physical capability, fast-paced environment tolerance)
6. **Photography/Videography**: Capture event content (needs photography/videography experience)
7. **Social Media Ambassadors**: Share updates and engage audience (needs social media expertise, writing skills)
8. **Auction/Raffle Coordinators**: Solicit donations, organize displays, manage bidding (needs event planning, attention to detail)
9. **Registration/Check-in**: Manage registration and customer service (needs communication skills, detail orientation)
10. **Logistics and Supply**: Event setup and teardown support
