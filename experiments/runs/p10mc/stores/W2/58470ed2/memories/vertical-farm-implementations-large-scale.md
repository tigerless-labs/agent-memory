## Real-World Large-Scale Vertical Farm Implementations

### Tokyo, Japan
- **Company**: Spread
- **Status**: World's largest vertical farm
- **Production**: 10,000 heads of lettuce per day
- **Facility size**: 25,000 square feet

### Singapore
- **Company**: Sky Greens
- **Facility height**: 9 meters tall
- **Production**: 1 ton of vegetables per day
- **Design**: Vertical tower system

### New York City, USA
- **Company**: Bowery Farming
- **Facility size**: 50,000 square feet
- **Crops**: Leafy greens and herbs, year-round production
- **Water efficiency**: Uses 95% less water than traditional farming methods

### Other Cities with Large-Scale Vertical Farms

### Market Trend
Vertical farming technology is becoming increasingly accessible to various scales of operation, from large commercial facilities to smaller residential implementations.
