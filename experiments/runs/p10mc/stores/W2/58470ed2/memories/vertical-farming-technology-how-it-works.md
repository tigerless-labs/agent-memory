## Vertical Farming Technology

### Basic Mechanism
- Crops grown in vertically stacked layers using high-tech equipment
- Plants grown in nutrient-rich water solutions (hydroponics)—no soil needed
- LED lighting systems mimic natural sunlight for indoor growth
- Computer-controlled environments regulate conditions

### Environmental Controls
- Sensors monitor and regulate:
  - Temperature
  - Humidity
  - Lighting conditions
- Automation manages daily operations

### Key Advantages
- **Water efficiency**: Uses 95% less water than traditional farming methods
- **Year-round production**: Independent of seasons and weather
- **Chemical reduction**: Minimal need for pesticides and herbicides
- **Urban agriculture**: Enables crop production in areas with limited arable land
- **Local sourcing**: Reduces long-distance transportation needs
- **Food freshness**: Produce harvested and consumed locally

### Best Applications
- Urban areas with limited space
- Regions with harsh climates or extreme seasonality
- Leafy greens and herbs (most successful crops)
- Indoor/controlled facility settings
