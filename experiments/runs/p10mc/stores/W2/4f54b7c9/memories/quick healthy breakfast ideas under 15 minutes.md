Quick Healthy Breakfast Ideas

1. Avocado Toast (5 min)
   Toast whole-grain bread, mash avocado, add sliced egg, salt, pepper

2. Overnight Oats (5 min)
   Mix rolled oats, milk, nuts, seeds, or fruit in jar night before
   Top with fresh fruit and honey in morning

3. Yogurt Parfait (5 min)
   Layer Greek yogurt, berries, granola, cinnamon

4. Peanut Butter Banana Toast (3 min)
   Toast whole-grain bread, spread peanut butter, top with banana slices

5. Breakfast Smoothie (5 min)
   Blend fruits, yogurt, milk

6. Scrambled Eggs with Spinach and Feta (10 min)
   Scramble eggs, add spinach and feta, serve with toast or tortilla

7. Muffin Tin Frittatas (10 min when prepped)
   Make batch ahead, reheat in morning

8. Cottage Cheese and Fruit (5 min)
   Mix cottage cheese with berries or peaches, add cinnamon or honey

9. Green Smoothie Bowl (10 min)
   Blend spinach, avocado, milk, fruit; top with granola, nuts, fruit

10. Hard-Boiled Eggs and Toast (5 min)
    Boil eggs night before, store in fridge
    Quick protein-packed option
