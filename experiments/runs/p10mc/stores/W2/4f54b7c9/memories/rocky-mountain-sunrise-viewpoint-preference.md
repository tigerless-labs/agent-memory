## Sunrise Viewpoint Preferences
- User wants sunrise views in Rocky Mountain National Park
- Prefers locations accessible by foot or short drives (dislikes long drives)
- Interested in sunrise photography

## Recommended Viewpoints
**Most Accessible (preferred by user):**
- **Dream Lake** — short trail from Bear Lake area; views of Hallett Peak and Flattop Mountain reflecting in calm waters
- **Nymph Lake** — hike to summit; offers 360-degree views of surrounding mountains
- **Moraine Park** — accessible by car with short walk to open meadow; can also reach via short trail hikes to nearby viewpoints
- **Emerald Lake trail** — good sunrise mountain views

**Alternative (requires longer drive):**
- **Trail Ridge Road summit** — approximately 1 hour drive; stunning alpine tundra and surrounding peak views; Alpine Visitor Center at summit with restrooms and snacks
