## Portable Power Bank

**Use & Motivation**
- Purchased after realizing phone battery would die frequently during long days out
- Carries in Fjallraven Kanken backpack daily
- Keeps multiple devices charged while away from outlets

**Storage**
- Stores in small pouch or case with its cables and adapters
- Keeps in easily accessible pocket (front or side pocket) or top-loading pocket
- Uses carabiner attachment option to keep it secure and within reach

**Maintenance**
- Charges regularly to ensure it's always ready when needed
- Cleans periodically to prevent dust/dirt accumulation
