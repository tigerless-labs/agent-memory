Muffin Tin Frittatas Recipe

Ingredients (6 frittatas):
- 6-8 eggs
- 1/2 cup diced vegetables (bell peppers and onions recommended)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or blend)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cooking spray or oil

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Whisk together eggs with salt
3. Add diced veggies and cheese, mix well
4. Spray 6-cup muffin tin with cooking spray
5. Divide egg mixture evenly among muffin cups
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until edges golden and centers set
7. Cool for a few minutes before serving

Recommended Vegetables:
- Bell peppers (any color; red or yellow for sweetness)
- Onions
- Mushrooms
- Spinach
- Cherry tomatoes
- Zucchini
- Broccoli
- Asparagus
- Carrots (grated)

Reheating Options:
1. Microwave: 20-30 seconds in microwave-safe container
2. Toaster oven: 350°F for 5-7 minutes on baking sheet
3. Conventional oven: 350°F for 10-12 minutes
4. Pan: 2-3 minutes per side over medium heat

Storage:
- Refrigerator: up to 3 days in airtight container
- Freezer: up to 2 months

Tips:
- Add splash of water or milk when reheating to keep moist
- Frozen frittatas need extra reheating time
- Can wrap in paper towel or cloth to keep warm while getting ready
