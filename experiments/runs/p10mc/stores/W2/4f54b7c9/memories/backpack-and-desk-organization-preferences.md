## Organization Preferences & Tips

**General Approach**
- Empty and sort items into categories before organizing
- Assign a specific home for each item
- Keep frequently used items in easy-to-reach pockets
- Use labels and color-coding where helpful

**Cable & Electronics Management**
- Uses cable organizers, velcro straps, or silicone wraps
- Labels cables with colored markers or tags to identify devices
- Stores all cables and chargers together in dedicated pouches
- Wraps cables neatly to prevent tangling and kinking
- Keeps chargers separate from cables when possible

**Storage Tools & Containers**
- Small pouches or bags for organizing by category
- Packing cubes for separating items by type
- Carabiners or clips for attaching items to backpack straps
- Separate breathable bags for gym clothes and shoes
- Storage containers or bins for desk organization

**Accessibility & Maintenance**
- Top-loading pockets for quick-access items
- Regularly cleans backpack and equipment
- Airs out backpack to prevent moisture and odors
- Uses odor-controlling products when needed
- Creates designated "stations" (cable station, to-do station, etc.)

**Desk Organization**
- Clears desk completely before reorganizing
- Groups similar items together
- Creates storage for small items (paper clips, pushpins)
- Establishes to-do station with notebook and pen
