## Gym Supplies Organization

**Items Carried**
- Water bottle
- Towel  
- Gym clothes (tops, bottoms, socks)
- Gym shoes

**Organization System**
- Water bottle: dedicated side pocket for easy access
- Towel: rolled to save space, stored in small pouch or clipped to backpack straps
- Gym clothes: separate breathable bag, rolled, packing cubes by type (tops/bottoms/socks)
- Dirty clothes: stored in separate bag to prevent mixing with clean items
- Gym shoes: separate shoe bag to keep clean and prevent damage

**Maintenance**
- Shoes cleaned regularly to prevent dirt buildup and odors
- Uses odor-controlling products (deodorizers, fabric refresher sprays)
- Backpack aired out regularly to prevent moisture and odors
