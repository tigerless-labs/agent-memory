## Human Role vs Unmanned Systems in Military

**Why Humans Remain Essential**
- Creativity, intuition, and critical judgment cannot be fully replicated by machines
- Complex and unpredictable combat scenarios require human ability to adapt to new situations
- Decision-making authority and coordination remains vital in combat situations

**Expected Role of Unmanned Systems**
- Primary applications: reconnaissance, surveillance, and logistics
- Tools to support and enhance human efforts rather than replace them
- Reduce risk to human life and increase operational efficiency
- Complement rather than substitute human capabilities

**Key Principle**
Unmanned vehicles and robots will play increasingly important roles in modern warfare, but will continue to function as tools supporting human decision-making and command authority rather than autonomous replacements for human soldiers.
