## Australian Army Future Technology Investments

The Army is exploring development and acquisition of:

**Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs)**
- Increased robotic capabilities for reconnaissance, surveillance, and logistics tasks

**Directed Energy Weapons (DEWs)**
- Laser weapons and similar systems for defense against aerial threats

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
- Enhanced operational decision-making and situational awareness
- Real-time data processing and analysis for tactical decision support
- Processing large amounts of data to assist in battlefield decisions

**Smart Munitions**
- Smart grenades with advanced targeting and detonation capabilities
- Precision-guided missiles for improved accuracy and reduced collateral damage

**Strategic Goals**
- Maintain competitive edge in modern warfare
- Meet challenges of future conflicts through advanced technology adoption
- Continue shift toward unmanned support systems while maintaining human decision-making authority
