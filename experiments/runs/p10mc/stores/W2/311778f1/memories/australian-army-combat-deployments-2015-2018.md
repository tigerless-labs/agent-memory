## Australian Army Combat Deployments

**2015 - Iraq Operations**
- Deployed UAVs for intelligence gathering and enemy movement monitoring
- Special forces used unmanned systems to gather critical information without exposing personnel

**2016 - Iraq Operations**  
- First combat deployment of M1A1 Abrams tanks
- Tanks provided effective fire support to ground troops in urban combat situations

**2018 - Afghanistan Operations**
- Deployed Hawkei protected vehicles
- Provided enhanced mobility and protection for soldier transport and resupply operations

These modernized platforms reduced risk to personnel while improving operational effectiveness.
