## Airbnb in 2020

**Founding & Early History**
- Founded 2008 when Brian Chesky and friend rented apartment to guests for local convention
- Used air mattresses, called it "Air Bed & Breakfast"
- Proof of concept: peer-to-peer room-sharing business model viability

**Growth Through 2020**
- 2018: ~190 countries, 4M+ listed properties, $31B estimated valuation
- 2020: 220+ countries, 7M+ locations
- Planned IPO during 2020

**Business Model: Sharing Economy**
- Technology platform enabling individuals with spare capacity to offer services/resources
- Leverages smartphones and declining technology costs
- Homeowners rent spare rooms; car owners offer taxi services
- Hosts sign up on platform, offer services; company charges small transaction fee

**Revenue & Cost Structure**
- Receives ~11% fee per room stay
- Revenue decoupled from operating expenses of traditional lodging
- Significantly lower operating costs than hotels/motels/B&Bs
- Expenses structured as technology company, not real estate operator
- Margin structure mirrors online marketplace, not lodging establishment
- Achieved profitability in 2016 (first time)
- 2017: ~$3B revenue, $90M+ profit

**COVID-19 Crisis**
- Global pandemic in first half of 2020 threatened Airbnb's success
- Many hosts relied on Airbnb revenue for mortgage subsidies or property-dependent income
- Shelter-in-place and travel restrictions created uncertain market
- Business model depended on individuals sharing private homes
