## Campaign Overview
- Brand: Dermelect (skincare and nail care products)
- Focus: US-based influencers only
- Target market: US audience
- Key values: cruelty-free, eco-friendly

## Past Campaigns (Proof of Concept)
1. **#DermelectDetox** — detoxifying skincare products; Instagram posts, stories, YouTube videos, blog posts; increased website traffic and product sales
2. **#DermelectNailRevolution** — nail strengthening treatments and polishes; nail care tips, tutorials, before-and-after transformations; Instagram, TikTok, YouTube; boosted brand awareness
3. **#DermelectSelfCare** — self-care emphasis; personal routines featuring Dermelect products; Instagram, stories, YouTube videos

## Influencer Tiers by Follower Count
- **Micro-influencers**: 10,000–50,000 followers (highly engaged niche audience)
- **Mid-tier**: 50,000–250,000 followers (balance of reach and engagement)
- **Macro**: 250,000–1,000,000 followers (broad reach and awareness)

## Compensation Structure
1. **Product gifting** — tailored selection of products
2. **Fixed monetary fees** — negotiated individually based on reach, engagement, quality
3. **Affiliate program** — personalized promo codes; commission per sale
4. **Performance-based bonuses** — awarded for reaching KPI milestones

## Ideal Influencer Qualities
1. Authenticity — genuine passion, honest unbiased opinions
2. Engaging personality — charismatic, relatable, compelling content
3. High engagement rate — strong connection with followers
4. Relevant niche — beauty, skincare, or self-care focused
5. Creative storyteller — mix of images, videos, stories
6. Professional — meets deadlines, communicates well
7. Brand alignment — committed to cruelty-free, eco-friendly practices
