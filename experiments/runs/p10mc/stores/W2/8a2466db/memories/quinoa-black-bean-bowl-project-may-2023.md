## Quinoa and Black Bean Bowl — May 2023 Project

User plans to make this protein-packed bowl combining:

### Base Components
- Cooked quinoa
- Cooked black beans
- Diced chicken breast (grilled)

### Grilled Chicken Marinade
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro (optional)
- Grill 5-7 min per side until 165°F internal temp
- **Addition**: Diced bell peppers grilled alongside chicken

### Roasted Sweet Potatoes
- 2-3 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (1-inch)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin (optional)
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- **Additions**: Diced onions and minced garlic for extra flavor
- Roast at 425°F for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway

### Avocado Sauce
- 3 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
- Blend smooth, refrigerate 30+ min

### Other Optional Toppings
- Sliced avocado
- Shredded Monterey Jack, Cheddar, or Pepper Jack cheese
- Cotija cheese or Queso Fresco
- Salsa
- Sliced radishes
- Black olives
- Pickled onions
- Toasted pumpkin seeds

### Assembly
Top the quinoa and black beans with grilled chicken, roasted sweet potatoes, avocado sauce, and desired toppings.
