## Overview

User's photography learning journey spanning from understanding their own vintage cameras (1920s-1950s) back through photography history to the origins in the 1800s. Integration of technical understanding with artistic and aesthetic appreciation.

## Learning Path

1. **Personal Collection Context**
   - 1950s Rolleiflex TLR camera (recently acquired, active learning)
   - 1920s Kodak Vest Pocket Camera (collection piece)
   - Both represent later evolution of photography technologies

2. **Early Photographic Processes**
   - Daguerreotypy (1839): Daguerre's metal plate process
   - Calotypy (1841): Talbot's paper-based negative-positive process
   - Understanding technical steps, chemistry, and practical limitations

3. **Pioneer Photographers**
   - Louis Daguerre (1787-1851): invented and popularized first commercial process
   - William Henry Fox Talbot (1800-1877): developed negative-positive approach
   - Professional photographers who used later cameras: Cartier-Bresson, Capa, Arbus

4. **Artistic & Aesthetic Development**
   - How artistic approaches evolved with technology
   - Differences in subjects: Daguerre focused on portraiture, Talbot on landscape/still life
   - Evolution of photographic aesthetics 19th-early 20th century
