## Creamy Mushroom Truffle Sauce Recipe

**Mushroom selection**: Planning to use a mix of cremini and shiitake mushrooms (decided against pure button mushrooms).

### Sautéing mushrooms (for optimal flavor):
- Clean gently, trim stems, slice uniformly
- Pat dry with paper towels to enable browning instead of steaming
- Use large skillet (stainless steel or cast iron) with 1-2 tbsp oil
- Heat to medium-high (4-5/10 heat level) until oil shimmers
- Don't overcrowd; work in batches if needed
- Sear first side 2-3 minutes until golden brown
- Stir and continue 5-7 minutes more until tender, liquid mostly evaporated, caramelized
- Add aromatics: onions, garlic, thyme during cooking
- Watch for overcooking (mushrooms go from perfect to mushy quickly)

### Truffle oil technique:
- Choose high-quality oil with real truffle essence, avoid artificial additives
- Use sparingly — start with 1-2 teaspoons, taste and adjust
- **Add toward the end** of sauce-making to preserve delicate flavors (high heat destroys the aroma)
- Infuse in the sauce for 1-2 minutes before serving
- Balance with cream, Parmesan, and herbs (truffle oil is potent)

### Full sauce assembly:
1. Sauté mushrooms + onions
2. Add garlic + thyme, cook 1 minute
3. Deglaze with white wine, reduce almost completely
4. Add heavy cream, bring to simmer
5. Reduce heat to low, simmer 2-3 minutes until slightly thickened
6. Stir in truffle oil, infuse 1-2 minutes
7. Season with salt, pepper, grated Parmesan, pinch of nutmeg

## Garlic Bread Side Pairing

**Recipe**:
- 1 rustic Italian bread or baguette, sliced 1-inch thick
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp softened butter
- 1 tsp dried or fresh herbs (parsley, thyme, or oregano)
- Salt and pepper

**Preparation**:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
2. Mix softened butter + minced garlic + herbs
3. Spread thin layer on each bread slice
4. Toast 8-10 minutes until lightly browned and crispy
5. Season with salt and pepper
6. Optional: top with Parmesan or fresh herbs

## Protein Additions Being Considered

User wanted to make the pasta more substantial by adding protein. Options discussed with cooking techniques:

1. **Grilled Chicken** — Season with herbs, grill 5-7 min per side until 165°F internal temp, rest and slice
2. **Pan-Seared Scallops** — High heat skillet, season with salt/pepper/lemon, sear 2-3 min per side until golden
3. **Crispy Bacon or Pancetta** — Cook over medium heat until crispy, chop, drain excess fat
4. **Sautéed Shrimp** — Medium heat, butter/oil, cook 2-3 min per side until pink
5. **Pan-Seared Steak** (filet mignon or ribeye) — High heat, sear 3-5 min per side, rest and slice thin
6. **Roasted Turkey or Chicken Sausage** — Slice and roast at 400°F or crumble into dish

**Guiding principle**: Protein should complement the sauce's earthy flavors, not overpower it; start with small amounts and adjust to taste.
