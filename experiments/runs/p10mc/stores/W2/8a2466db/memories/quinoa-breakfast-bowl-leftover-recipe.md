## Quinoa Breakfast Bowl — Leftover Recipe

Quick and nutritious breakfast using leftover quinoa from dinner.

### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup leftover cooked quinoa
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 1/2 banana, sliced
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (optional)
- 1/4 cup chopped nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, or flaxseeds)
- Pinch of salt
- Splash of milk or yogurt (optional)

### Instructions
1. Mix quinoa, mixed berries, and sliced banana in a bowl
2. Add honey/maple syrup for sweetness (optional)
3. Sprinkle nuts or seeds on top
4. Add pinch of salt
5. Add milk or yogurt for creamier texture (optional)
6. Serve immediately

### Flavor Variations
- Add cinnamon, vanilla powder, or cocoa powder for extra flavor
- Mix in scrambled eggs or crumbled bacon for added protein
- Use different types of milk: almond milk, soy milk, coconut yogurt
- Customize with favorite fruits and nuts

### Benefits
- Complete protein from quinoa
- Fiber and natural sweetness from fruits
- Crunchy texture from nuts/seeds
- Quick to prepare using leftovers
