## Piano Study Resumption

Considering restarting piano lessons as an adult after childhood lessons.

**Considerations for getting started:**
- Defining personal goals (hobby renewal, specific styles, personal fulfillment)
- Finding adult-friendly piano teacher with adult learner experience
- Choosing lesson frequency (considering preference for weekly or bi-weekly)
- Acquiring piano/keyboard for home practice

**Expectations as adult learner:**
- Likely need to review basics (hand position, finger numbers, basic chords)
- Focus on developing proper technique (finger independence, hand-eye coordination, pedaling)
- Tailored lessons based on personal interests and goals
- Regular practice commitment: 20-30 minutes, 3-4 times weekly for progress
- Potential performance opportunities in recitals or informal gatherings

**Mindset:**
- Patient with learning process — progress takes time
- Open communication with teacher about goals and concerns
- Focus on enjoyment and personal fulfillment rather than rushing progress
