## News Consumption Preferences

**Current habits:**
- Stays informed primarily through Twitter
- Feels overwhelmed by volume and speed of information sometimes
- Wants to engage more deeply with news, not just consume headlines
- Cares about both staying informed AND maintaining mental health

## Balance Strategies Discussed

**Managing information overload:**
- Create boundaries: check news at specific times, avoid before bed/upon waking
- Use Twitter tools: lists, hashtags, filters to organize content
- Follow verified news outlets rather than all tweets
- Take breaks when overwhelmed

**Deeper engagement with news:**
- Follow verified news sources and comment/share their content
- Join online groups and forums for discussion
- Attend public events and workshops on topics of interest
- Read in-depth articles for full context, not just headlines
- Share thoughts on social media to add voice to conversation

**Mental health practices:**
- Meditation or relaxation techniques when stressed
- Understand context and history of stories, not just surface-level consumption
- Participate in conversations to broaden perspectives
