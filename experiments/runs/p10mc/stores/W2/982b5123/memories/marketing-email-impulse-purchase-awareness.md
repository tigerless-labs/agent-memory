## Awareness of Marketing Triggers

User has identified that promotional/sale emails from retailers (like H&M) are a personal trigger for impulse shopping and increased spending beyond actual needs.

In May 2023, an H&M promotional email led to a shopping spree that included purchases from multiple retailers (Sephora, ASOS, Adidas) over the following weeks, totaling over $500+ in discretionary spending.

User demonstrates metacognition about this pattern and recognizes:
- Marketing tactics are designed to trigger purchases
- Being aware of these triggers helps make more intentional financial decisions
- Need to distinguish between actual needs and sale-driven impulse purchases

This insight may be useful for:
- Email management strategy (unsubscribing from promo lists, using filters)
- Budget planning (accounting for seasonal sales events)
- Intentional spending practices
