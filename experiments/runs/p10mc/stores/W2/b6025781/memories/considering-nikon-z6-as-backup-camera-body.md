## Considering Nikon Z6 as Backup Camera

**Current Situation (May 21, 2023):**
- Primary camera: Nikon D850 (owned ~1.5 years, still primary for most shoots)
- New lens: Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG OS HSM Art Lens
- Seeking backup camera body for travel (smaller/lighter) and video work

**Z6 Advantages Being Considered:**
- Smaller and lighter than D850 (ideal for travel)
- Superior video capabilities:
  - 4K UHD at 30p/25p/24p (100 Mbps max bitrate)
  - 1080p at up to 120p for slow-motion
  - In-body image stabilization (IBIS): up to 5 stops
  - Advanced autofocus: 273 phase-detection points (90% frame coverage)
  - Faster readout speed (reduces rolling shutter)
  - Better moiré reduction
- Advanced features: eye-detection AF, interval timer, 4K time-lapse
- Advanced audio system with 3.5mm jack

**Z6 Autofocus vs D850:**
- Z6: 273 points vs D850's 153 points
- Z6: Better low-light performance (-2 EV to +19 EV)
- Z6: Faster overall autofocus speed
- Z6: Advanced eye-detection AF
- D850: Slightly faster in very low light with certain lenses

**Lens Compatibility Concerns:**
- Sigma 24-70mm is F-mount (DSLR) lens
- Requires Nikon FTZ Adapter for Z6 use
- Potential drawbacks with adapter: slightly slower AF, IBIS unavailable, possible lens correction inaccuracy
- Existing F-mount investment makes direct Z-mount transition expensive
- Considering dual-lens ecosystems vs compatibility adapter approach

**Alternative Backup Options Considered:**
- **D500**: Same F-mount ecosystem as D850; excellent AF for sports/wildlife; APS-C sensor (lower image quality); no 4K video
- **D5**: Flagship AF; excellent low-light; same F-mount; expensive; larger/heavier

**Decision Status:** Undecided - needs more research, considering test/try-before-buying approach
