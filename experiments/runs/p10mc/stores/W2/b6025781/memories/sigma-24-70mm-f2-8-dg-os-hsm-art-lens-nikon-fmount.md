## Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG OS HSM Art Lens

**Lens Details:**
- Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG OS HSM Art series lens
- F-mount (DSLR mount) - designed for Nikon D850
- Recently purchased (May 2023)
- Features optical stabilization (OS)

**Primary Use:**
- D850 (primary camera)

**Potential Secondary Use - Z6 Compatibility:**
- Not native Z-mount
- Requires Nikon FTZ Adapter for Nikon Z6 use
- Compatibility confirmed: FTZ Adapter supports Sigma Art series lenses

**FTZ Adapter Considerations:**
- Autofocus: Slightly slower and less accurate than native Z-mount lenses; phase-detection AF still reliable
- Image Stabilization: Lens OS (optical stabilization) works; camera's IBIS not available with adapter
- Aperture Control: Electronic aperture control supported
- Lens Corrections: Available but may be less accurate than with native lenses

**Part of Larger Decision:**
- Contributing factor in Z6 backup camera body consideration
- Trade-off: Adapter convenience vs. cost of native Z-mount lenses
- Heavy investment in F-mount ecosystem makes direct Z-mount transition expensive
