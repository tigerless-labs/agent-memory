## Career Paths Discussed

1. **Solar Design Engineer** — designs solar power systems; requires bachelor's in mechanical or electrical engineering
2. **Wind Turbine Technician** — installs, maintains, repairs wind systems; requires associate degree or technical training
3. **Energy Efficiency Specialist** — advises on energy conservation; degree in environmental science or engineering preferred
4. **Biomass Scientist** — researches biofuel conversion; degree in biochemistry or chemical engineering preferred
5. **Geothermal Engineer** — designs ground/water heat extraction systems; degree in mechanical or civil engineering required
6. **Hydropower Engineer** — designs hydropower systems; degree in mechanical, civil, or electrical engineering required

## Salary Comparisons (mentioned)

- Wind turbine technician: $56,230 median annual (vs. oil & gas rotary drill operator: $54,940)
- Solar PV installer: $44,890 median annual (vs. natural gas plant operator: $69,910)
- Renewable energy salaries generally competitive with traditional energy

## Job Growth & Investment Outlook

- Renewable energy occupations projected to have faster than average growth rates vs. traditional energy
- IRENA employed 11.5 million people globally in renewable energy in 2019, expected to increase
- Government support and increased climate awareness driving investment
- Cost competitiveness of renewables improving
- New technologies increasing efficiency and scalability

## Getting Started

- Obtain relevant degree/certification in engineering, science, or technology
- Seek internships or apprenticeships
- Network with professionals, attend conferences
- Stay current with industry news and trends
