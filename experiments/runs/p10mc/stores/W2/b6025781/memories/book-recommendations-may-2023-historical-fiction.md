## Recommended Historical Fiction Books (May 2023)

### Primary Interest

**"All the Light We Cannot See" by Anthony Doerr**
- Pulitzer Prize winner
- Setting: World War II
- Characters: blind French girl, German boy; intersect during war
- Pace: deliberate and measured; lyrical writing; short episodic chapters alternating between characters; emotionally resonant; contemplative read with slower burn
- Good for: daily commute reading

**"The Alice Network" by Kate Quinn**
- Setting: World War I and post-war years
- Characters: two women (socialite and spy); gripping story of friendship, love, survival
- Based on: real Alice Network spy operation
- Pace: fast-paced, plot-driven; shorter concise chapters; page-turner with twists; energetic and engaging

### Secondary Recommendations
- "The Song of Achilles" by Madeline Miller
- "The Red Tent" by Anita Diamant
- "The Historian" by Elizabeth Kostova
- "Those Who Save Us" by Jenna Blum
- "The Lost Girls of Paris" by Pam Jenoff

- Enjoys historical fiction with emotional depth and strong storytelling
- Reads on daily commute
