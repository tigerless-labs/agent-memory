## World War I Historical Fiction (May 2023)

Similar to "The Alice Network" by Kate Quinn - focus on human experience during wartime, women's roles, moral complexities

### Recommended Novels

**By Pam Jenoff**
1. "The Soldier's Wife" - young German woman in France during WWI; survival and resistance
2. "The War Bride" - American woman marries British officer during WWI; caught up in war effort and British aristocracy intrigue
3. "The Winter Guest" - set in Poland during WWI; woman embroiled in Polish resistance movement

**Other**
- "The Poppy War" by R.F. Kuang - fantasy world inspired by Chinese history and WWI events; orphan in military academy during war between rival nations

### Themes
- Focus on human experience during wartime
- Role of women in war effort
- Moral complexities of living in conflict
- Women's agency and resistance
