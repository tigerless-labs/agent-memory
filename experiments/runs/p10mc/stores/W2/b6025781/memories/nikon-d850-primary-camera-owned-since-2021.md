## Nikon D850 Primary Camera

**Ownership:**
- Owned for approximately 1.5 years as of May 2023
- Acquired around late 2021 or early 2022
- Still primary camera body for most shoots

**Role:**
- Primary full-frame DSLR for photography
- Praised for high-resolution 45.7MP sensor
- Excellent low-light performance
- Superior autofocus (153 phase-detection points)
- Strong build quality and weather sealing

**Lens Ecosystem:**
- F-mount (Nikon DSLR mount)
- Currently using: Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG OS HSM Art Lens (F-mount)
- Heavy investment in F-mount lens ecosystem

**Companion Gear:**
- Camera bag: Think Tank Photo Urban Disguise 40L

**Backup Camera Consideration:**
- Exploring Nikon Z6 as backup/travel camera
- Z6 offers smaller, lighter package with better video capabilities
- Lens compatibility via FTZ Adapter for F-mount lenses
- Different lens ecosystem makes full investment in Z-mount expensive

**Video Capabilities:**
- Can record 4K UHD at 30p but only in cropped DX mode
- Full-frame limited to 1080p at 60p
- Lacks in-body image stabilization
