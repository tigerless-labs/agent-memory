## Hiking Groups/Organizations
- **Meetup.com** — find local hiking groups by searching area
- **Local clubs** — Sierra Club, Appalachian Mountain Club, Hiking Club of [local area]
- **Outdoor gear stores** — REI, Dick's Sporting Goods offer adventure groups and organized hikes
- **Park districts** and conservation organizations — guided hikes and outdoor programs
- **Facebook groups** — local hiking groups

## Photography Groups/Organizations
- **Meetup.com** — photography meetups and groups in area
- **Local photography clubs** — Photography Club, National Camera Club
- **Camera stores** — B&H Photo, Camera World offer workshops, classes, and groups
- **Online communities** — Flickr, Facebook groups dedicated to photography
- **Local colleges and community centers** — classes and workshops

## Other Connection Opportunities
- **Volunteer opportunities** — park cleanups, conservation efforts
- **Local events** — outdoor festivals, photography exhibitions, nature walks
