## Quinoa Cooking Tips

User previously had issues with quinoa being too mushy.

### Perfect Quinoa Method:
- **Rinse thoroughly**: Remove bitter saponin coating with cold water in fine-mesh strainer (~30 seconds)
- **Water ratio**: 2 parts water per 1 part quinoa
- **Heat level**: Medium heat (not high, to prevent uneven cooking and mushiness)
- **Cooking time**: 15-20 minutes until water is absorbed
- **Doneness test**: Tender but still slightly crunchy in center
- **Finish**: Fluff with fork after cooking to separate grains and cool
- **Optional soaking**: 30 minutes to 1 hour before cooking (then drain and rinse again)
- **Pan type**: Heavy-bottom pot for even heat distribution
- **Seasoning**: Pinch of salt in cooking water

## Quinoa and Black Bean Salad Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed and drained
- 2 cups water
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 small red onion, diced
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Cook quinoa using technique above
2. Combine cooked quinoa, black beans, red bell pepper, red onion in large bowl
3. Whisk together olive oil and lime juice in small bowl
4. Pour dressing over salad, toss to combine
5. Stir in cilantro, season with salt and pepper
