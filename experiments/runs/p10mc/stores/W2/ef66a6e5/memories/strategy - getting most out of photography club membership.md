## Participating in Discussions
- Be active listener — pay attention to others' thoughts/experiences
- Ask questions — seek clarification on unfamiliar topics
- Share your own experiences — contribute to discussions without dominating
- Respect differing opinions — photography is subjective, be open-minded

## Giving & Receiving Feedback
- Be constructive when giving feedback — focus on specific aspects (composition, lighting, editing)
- Provide actionable suggestions for improvement
- Be respectful receiving feedback — avoid defensiveness, feedback helps growth
- Ask for feedback on own work — be specific about what you want feedback on
- Learn from feedback given to others

## Building Relationships with Members
- Attend meetings regularly — build relationships and stay connected
- Participate in activities — photo walks, workshops, club outings to bond
- Be approachable — talk to new members, make them feel welcome
- Follow up after meeting someone — continue conversation and build connection

## Additional Tips
- Be open-minded — try new genres, techniques, equipment
- Share your knowledge — if you have expertise, share with others (builds reputation and community)
- Respect club rules and guidelines
- Have fun — enjoy learning and growing with like-minded individuals
