## Focus on Process, Not Outcome
- Celebrate small wins — attending event, good conversation, sending follow-up
- Set realistic expectations — building relationships takes time, no immediate results
- Emphasize journey not destination — focus on learning, people met, skills developed

## Track Progress
- Keep journal/log of events attended, people met, follow-ups made
- Use habit tracker for daily/weekly habits
- Reflect and adjust strategy based on observations

## Stay Positive & Persistent
- Remind yourself why you started — reflect on original goals
- Focus on positive interactions instead of negative experiences
- Don't take lack of response personally — not reflection of your worth

## Find Accountability & Support
- Find accountability partner — share goals and progress
- Join community with others sharing similar goals
- Celebrate successes together

## Self-Care & Energy Management
- Prioritize self-care — exercise, reading, hobbies that bring joy
- Manage energy — don't overcommit to too many events/responsibilities
- Take breaks and rest when needed
- Stay organized with calendars, to-do lists, project management tools
- Be willing to learn from feedback and adjust approach
- Stay flexible — pivot strategy if something isn't working
