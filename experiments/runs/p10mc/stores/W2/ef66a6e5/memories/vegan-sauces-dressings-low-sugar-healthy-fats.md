## Vegan Sauces & Dressings

User wants low-sugar, low-unhealthy-fat options for salads.

### Dressing Recipes:

**Lemon-Tahini Dressing**
- Mix: lemon juice, tahini, garlic, olive oil
- Creamy, tangy, low sugar, rich in healthy fats from tahini

**Avocado-Lime Dressing**
- Blend: ripe avocado, lime juice, cilantro, pinch salt
- Creamy, refreshing, packed with healthy fats

**Ginger-Turmeric Vinaigrette**
- Whisk: apple cider vinegar, olive oil, grated ginger, turmeric powder
- Zesty, anti-inflammatory

**Hummus-Based Dressing**
- Mix: hummus, lemon juice, garlic, water
- Creamy, protein-rich
- Optional: add chopped herbs (parsley, cilantro)

**Nutritional Yeast Cheese Sauce**
- Blend: nutritional yeast, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil
- Nutty, cheesy flavor, creamy, addictive

**Miso-Ginger Dressing**
- Whisk: miso paste, apple cider vinegar, grated ginger, olive oil
- Savory, umami-rich

**Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette**
- Mix: fresh orange or grapefruit juice, olive oil, chopped herbs (parsley, basil, dill)
- Light, refreshing

### General Healthy Dressing Tips:
- Use healthy oils: olive, avocado, grapeseed (avoid processed vegetable oils)
- Natural sweeteners: dates, maple syrup, coconut sugar (instead of refined sugar)
- Vinegars: apple cider or balsamic (instead of white vinegar)
- Add flavor with spices and herbs instead of salt and sugar
- Make from scratch to control ingredients and portions
