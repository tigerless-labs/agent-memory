## Time Management
- Create calendar — schedule events, follow-ups, tasks with reminders/notifications
- Batch similar tasks — group follow-up messages, calls together
- Set time blocks — allocate specific time slots for events/follow-ups/tasks
- Leave buffer time — account for delays and tasks taking longer
- Learn to say no — be selective, don't commit to everything

## Energy Management
- Prioritize self-care — activities that energize you
- Identify energy peaks — schedule events/tasks during your best energy times
- Take regular breaks — rest and recharge
- Set boundaries — communicate them to others to avoid overcommitting
- Delegate tasks if possible — free up time/energy for important activities

## Event Selection Strategy
- Be strategic — choose events aligned with goals/values/interests
- Evaluate ROI — assess potential benefits of each event
- Limit quantity — attend manageable number per month to avoid burnout
- Focus on quality over quantity — build meaningful connections vs. attend many events

## Follow-up Strategy
- Prioritize follow-ups — focus on people most likely to lead to meaningful connections
- Use templates — create message templates to save time
- Use automation — schedule/automate follow-ups when possible
- Space out follow-ups — avoid overwhelming yourself and others
- Track progress — identify areas for improvement

## Ongoing Review & Maintenance
- Regularly review and adjust strategy
- Use tools for organization (to-do lists, project management, apps)
- Communicate availability/boundaries with others
- Prioritize physical and mental health
