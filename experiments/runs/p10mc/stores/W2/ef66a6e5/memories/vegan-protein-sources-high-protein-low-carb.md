## High-Protein, Low-Carb Vegan Protein Sources

User interested in adding to salads; planning to try seitan and tempeh.

### Options with Nutritional Info (per 3-oz serving unless noted):

**Seitan** (User interested)
- Protein: 21-22g
- Carbs: 4-5g
- Made from wheat gluten, meat-like texture

**Tempeh** (User interested)
- Protein: 15-16g
- Carbs: 6-7g
- Fermented soybean product, rich in probiotics, nutty flavor

**Tofu**
- Protein: 9-10g
- Carbs: 2-3g
- Look for extra-firm or super-firm for highest protein

**Vegan Protein Powder**
- Convenient boost for salads
- Look for: low-carb, pea/hemp/brown rice protein base

**Lentils** (per 1/2 cup cooked)
- Protein: 9-10g
- Carbs: 20-22g
- Higher carb but good protein and fiber

**Chickpeas** (per 1/2 cup cooked)
- Protein: 5-6g
- Carbs: 20-22g
- Higher carb but good protein and fiber

**Mushrooms** (per 1 cup)
- Protein: 2-3g
- Carbs: 1-2g
- Adds meaty texture, lower protein option

**Vegan Meat Alternatives**
- Look for pea/rice/soy protein bases
- Veggie burgers, vegan sausages
- Can be high protein, low carb
