## Before the Event
- Prepare ahead: think about goals and what you hope to get from the group
- Review group's purpose and rules
- Plan your introduction: name, interests, why you're attending

## At the Event
- **Arrive early** — mingle before event starts, meet people as they arrive
- **Find common ground** — shared interests, gear (e.g., camera model)
- **Start with small talk** — light casual conversations about event/location/weather
- **Listen actively** — pay attention, show interest
- **Ask open-ended questions** — encourage conversation and sharing (not yes/no)
- **Be yourself** — authenticity is key; don't pretend to have different interests
- **Take small steps** — start with one person or comment if overwhelmed

## Icebreaker Conversation Starters
- "Hi, I'm [Name]. What brings you to this event?"
- "I love your [camera/gear]. What kind of photography do you like?"
- "Have you been on any exciting hikes recently?"
- "What do you think of [recent news/event related to group interest]?"
- "I'm new to this group. Can you tell me a little about it?"

## Tips for Shy/Introverted People
- Find a buddy — attend with friend who shares interests
- Take breaks when overwhelmed — step away to recharge
- Focus on listening instead of contributing
- Celebrate small wins — acknowledge accomplishments like striking up conversation
