## Application for Senior Advisor — Te Puni Kōkiri

**Position:** Senior Advisor, Te Tai Tokerau Rohe, Regional Partnerships and Operations Puni  
**Organization:** Te Puni Kōkiri (Ministry of Māori Development)  
**Candidate:** Liam  
**Date:** May 21, 2023

### Key Responsibilities
- Lead work with agencies and providers to improve outcomes for Māori
- Assist whānau, hapū, iwi, and Māori organisations to develop long-term economic and social initiatives
- Act as conduit for information between Māori communities and agencies
- Manage local-level contractual arrangements
- Provide mentoring and coaching to regional Advisors
- Inform investment decisions
- Reports to Manukura - Team Leader, Te Tai Tokerau

### Required Qualifications
- Experience working with whānau, hapū, iwi, Māori organisations and communities
- Proven project management skills
- Understanding of government and public sector processes
- Understanding of Treaty of Waitangi relevance
- Excellent communication skills, including conversational te Reo Māori
- Ability to build and manage significant relationships with diverse perspectives
- Clean full motor vehicle driver's licence

### Organization Values
- Excellence
- Valuing people and relationships
- Working collectively
- Creative and innovative
- Work/life balance
- Employee wellbeing and learning and development

### Application Outcome
Application letter submitted May 21, 2023 addressing project management, government/public sector knowledge, Treaty of Waitangi understanding, relationship building, and organizational values alignment.
