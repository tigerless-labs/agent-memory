## Campaign Planning Framework

**Campaign Context:**
- Target audience: 18-35 year olds
- Budget: $50,000
- Team size: 3 people
- Timeline: 6 weeks for launch/promotion; 3-month sales period
- Goals: Increase brand awareness by 20%; drive $100,000 in revenue within first 3 months

**SMART Goals Example:**
- Specific: Increase brand awareness by 20% among 18-35 year olds in 6 weeks; $100k revenue in 3 months
- Measurable: 10% social media follower growth, 15% website traffic increase, 500+ leads in 2 months, $50k sales in first month, 10% average order value increase
- Achievable: Based on market research and past campaign data with stated budget and team
- Relevant: Direct impact on revenue and market share
- Time-bound: 6-week campaign with milestones at weeks 1-2 (planning/content creation), weeks 3-4 (launch/promotion), weeks 5-6 (optimization/evaluation)

**Content Calendar Setup:**
1. Identify channels: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, email, blog, video, paid ads, influencer partnerships
2. Determine themes: product features, customer testimonials, industry news, educational content, behind-the-scenes
3. Plan content types: social posts, blog articles, emails, videos, infographics, podcasts
4. Assign to channels and dates with columns for: date, channel, content type, theme, title, keywords/tags, target audience
5. Add: content status, owner, engagement metrics, budget allocation
6. Review and refine regularly

**Social Media Posting Frequency:**
- Facebook: 3-5 times per week; peak engagement 12pm, 3pm, 9pm EST
- Twitter: 5-10 times per day; peak engagement 12pm, 3pm, 5pm EST
- Instagram: 3-5 times per week; peak engagement 12pm, 5pm, 8pm EST
- LinkedIn: 2-3 times per week; peak engagement 10am, 12pm, 2pm EST

**Key Metrics to Track:**

Social Media: engagement rate, reach, impressions, follower growth rate, conversion rate

Email Marketing: open rate, click-through rate (CTR), conversion rate, bounce rate, unsubscribe rate

Content Marketing: page views, unique visitors, bounce rate, average time on site, conversion rate

Paid Advertising: cost per click (CPC), cost per conversion (CPA), return on ad spend (ROAS), CTR, conversion rate

Overall Campaign: ROI, customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (CLV), conversion rate, net promoter score (NPS)

**ROI Calculation:**
ROI = (Gain from Investment - Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment
Example: ($1,500 revenue - $1,000 spend) / $1,000 = 50% ROI

**CAC Calculation:**
CAC = Total Marketing and Sales Expenses / Number of New Customers Acquired
Example: $10,000 spend / 100 new customers = $100 CAC
