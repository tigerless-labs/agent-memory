New social media boundaries plan established May 2023 to maintain healthy habits after Feb 2023 recovery break.

Implementation plan:
- Set specific times of day to check social media accounts (vs. all-day access)
- Use website blockers or apps (e.g., Freedom, SelfControl, StayFocusd) to restrict access during work hours
- Unfollow accounts that make them feel bad about themselves or others
- Designate Sundays as completely social media-free day
- Use Sunday time for reading (20-book 2023 goal) and other enjoyable activities

Motivation: Prevent relapse into mindless scrolling; maintain positive online presence and mental wellbeing
