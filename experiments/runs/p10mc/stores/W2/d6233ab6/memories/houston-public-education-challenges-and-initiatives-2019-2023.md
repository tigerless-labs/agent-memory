## Challenges
1. **Funding** — Reliant on local property taxes, creating inconsistencies across state
2. **Achievement Gap** — Large disparities between minority/low-income students and peers in academic performance, graduation rates, college readiness
3. **English Language Learners (ELL)** — Over one-third of Houston ISD students are ELLs, straining resources
4. **Teacher Shortage** — Particularly in science and math

## Major Efforts & Measurable Impact

### School Finance
- 2019: Texas lawmakers passed school finance reform bill increasing funding by $6.5 billion
- Directed toward: teacher pay, full-day pre-K for eligible families, low-income student support

### Achievement Gap Progress (2018-2019 school year)
- 1% decrease in achievement gap between white and Hispanic students
- 2% decrease in gap between white and Black students

### ELL Program Success
- Houston ISD bilingual education program recognized for success
- 2018: 94% graduation rate among ELL students (vs. 67% statewide average)

### Teacher Hiring
- 2018-2019: Houston ISD hired 2,000+ new teachers through alternative certification programs

## Successful Innovative Programs

### EMERGE (started 2009)
- College-readiness program for high-achieving students from low-income backgrounds
- Helps with college applications and financial aid navigation
- Outcome: 1,300+ students placed in selective colleges/universities

### Early Matters
- Coalition of Houston business and civic leaders advocating for high-quality early childhood education
- Successfully pushed for pre-K expansion
- 2018: Houston voters approved ballot initiative to fund free full-day pre-K for eligible Houston ISD children

### Energized for STEM Academy (EFSA)
- Network of public charter schools specializing in STEM education
- 2019: Named National Blue Ribbon School by U.S. Department of Education

### Houston A+ Challenge
- Non-profit supporting innovation and improvement in public education
- Provides grants and training to teachers and schools for implementing innovative practices
