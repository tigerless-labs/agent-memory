Starting daily gratitude journal habit as of May 2023.

Format: Three things per day, written in a dedicated physical journal (not digital)

Key tips to implement:
- Write at consistent time each day (choose morning or before bed)
- Be specific, not generic (e.g., "sister's listening ear" not just "family")
- Mix big things with small things (job change + good coffee)
- Add reflection: write brief sentence on why each thing is important
- Variety is key to sustain the habit

Sample prompts user liked:
- Today I'm grateful for...
- Someone who made my day...
- Something I often take for granted...
- A skill or talent I'm grateful for...
- A memory or experience I cherish...

User also shares gratitude on Instagram but prefers physical journal for deeper reflection.
