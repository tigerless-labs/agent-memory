## Historical Evolution

### Post-Independence Cooperation
- Both countries shared similar colonial experiences and independence struggles
- Relationship strengthened when both became ASEAN founding members in 1967

### Period of Tension
- Mid-2000s: Maritime border dispute emerged in Mindanao Sea over EEZ and oil exploration
- This created tensions and prompted Philippine formal protest against Indonesia

### Recent Improvements
- Working towards resolving territorial disputes through peaceful means
- Strengthened partnerships in:
  - Trade
  - Tourism
  - Education
  - Culture

## Regional Cooperation Initiatives
- **Coral Triangle Initiative (CTI)** — Joint effort to preserve and protect marine biodiversity
- **ASEAN Economic Community** — Joint efforts toward sustainable and inclusive economic growth in Southeast Asia

## Overall Trajectory
Relationship has evolved from cooperation and mutual understanding → territorial tensions → renewed cooperation across multiple sectors (as of March 2023).
