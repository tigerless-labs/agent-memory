## Yokohama Day Trip (October 2023)

### Key Sites
- **Minato Mirai 21**: Futuristic district, harbor views, Landmark Tower observation deck
- **Red Brick Warehouse**: Historic warehouse, shopping/dining destination
- **Sankeien Garden**: Japanese garden with maple trees, beautiful in autumn
- **Yamashita Park**: Waterfront park
- **Cup Noodles Museum**, **Yokohama Museum of Art**: Also available

### Restaurant Recommendations

**Kanemasa** (Chinatown)
- Specialty: Kanimeshi (crab rice bowl)
- Operating 30+ years
- Fresh, high-quality crab
- Popular spot — expect queue

**Tsukiji Itadori Uogashi Nihon-Ichi** (near Yokohama Fish Market)
- Specialty: Namashirasu (raw sardine sushi)
- Fresh seasonal ingredients
- Sells out quickly; arrive early

**Yokohama Central Market**
- Casual and affordable
- Grilled squid, sushi, sashimi
- Multiple food stalls

### User Interests
- Try kanimeshi, namashirasu, grilled squid
- Beer exploration
- Fall foliage viewing
