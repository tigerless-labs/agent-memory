## Dispute Overview
Indonesia and Philippines dispute a small area in the Mindanao Sea involving overlapping exclusive economic zone (EEZ) claims. Dispute emerged mid-2000s when Philippines protested Indonesia's oil exploration activities in the disputed area.

## Resolution Steps Taken

1. **Joint Technical Working Group (JTWG)** — Established 2015 for Maritime and Ocean Concerns; held several meetings; recommended formation of joint boundary committee

2. **Joint Patrols** — Agreed 2017 as confidence-building measure to prevent illegal fishing and maritime crimes in disputed area

3. **UN Commission on the Limits of the Continental Shelf (CLCS)** — Both countries submitted claims to CLCS under UNCLOS to determine outer continental shelf limits beyond 200 nautical miles

4. **Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA)** — Agreed 2018 to settle dispute through arbitration process in The Hague

## Status
Both governments committed to peaceful resolution through dialogue and negotiations, though as of March 2023 dispute remained unresolved.
