## Yokohama Autumn Events (Late October - December 2023)

### Yokohama Oktoberfest
- **When**: Mid-October to early November
- **Type**: German-style beer festival
- **Features**: Beer, food, drinks, live music
- **Good for**: Beer enthusiasts

### Yokohama Autumn Leaves Festival (Yokohama Koyo Matsuri)
- **When**: Late November
- **Features**: Autumn foliage, food stalls, traditional Japanese performances
- **Good for**: Fall season enjoyment

### Minato Mirai 21 Autumn Illuminations
- **When**: Late October to mid-December
- **Best viewing times**: 5 pm to 10 pm
- **Theme**: Autumn-themed, incorporates fall season with traditional Japanese motifs
- **Highlights**:
  - Maple Leaf Tunnel: 100-meter tunnel with illuminated maple trees (red, orange, yellow)
  - Harvest Moon Garden: Large glowing moon, autumn flowers, romantic atmosphere
  - Cosmo World Illuminations: Colorful lights, projection mapping on Ferris wheel
  - Waterfront Illuminations: Lights reflecting off water creating shimmering effect

**Note**: Dates and details subject to change; check official websites or local tourism for confirmation
