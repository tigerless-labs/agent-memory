## Campaign: Oakwood Forest Corruption

**Player Character:**
- Name: Abraham
- Race: Halfling
- Class: Druid
- Level: 5

**Setting:**
- **Oakwood**: Small village at the edge of Whispering Woods; simple farmers and artisans
- **Whispering Woods**: Ancient forest with magical and mundane creatures (wolves, bears, deer, fey, treants, elementals)
- **Moonstone Spire**: Ancient ruin deep in forest; said to house powerful artifact that can bend nature to one's will; believed cursed, long avoided

**Quest Hook:**
Summoned by Eadric (old friend, village elder) to investigate growing darkness threatening Oakwood. Creatures becoming increasingly aggressive; plants and trees acting hostile. Fear that corruption will spread beyond the forest.

**NPCs & Information Gathered:**

1. **Eadric** (village elder, old friend)
   - Summoned Abraham via letter
   - Creatures attacking village for weeks
   - Reports of mysterious figure deep in forest
   - Strange lights and eerie sounds from that area
   - Suspects magical cult or rogue wizard
   - Mentioned Moonstone Spire as possible source

2. **Alaric** (Innkeeper, Silver Stag Inn)
   - Accommodating, helpful
   - Expected Abraham's arrival
   - Tavern serves as information hub

3. **Mabel** (older villager)
   - Encountered pack of wolves near village
   - Wolves unusually aggressive with unnatural glowing eyes
   - Never seen such behavior in lifetime

4. **Thom** (burly woodsman)
   - Trees behaving unnaturally, lashing out with branches
   - Trees uprooting themselves and blocking paths
   - Too dangerous for villagers to gather wood or hunt

5. **Lysander** (traveling merchant)
   - Glimpsed mysterious figure from distance
   - Figure shrouded in darkness
   - Sensed powerful magical energy around figure
   - Did not approach closer

**Progress:**
- Abraham arrived at Oakwood at sunset
- Met Eadric at his home
- Gathered initial information about forest disturbances
- Checked into Silver Stag Inn (room prepared)
- Gathered information from tavern patrons (completed)
- Currently at bar with whiskey, considering next move

**Unresolved:**
- Identity of mysterious figure
- Nature of Moonstone Spire artifact
- Root cause of forest corruption
- Connection between figure and disturbances
- Whether disturbance is magical curse, artifact activation, or hostile entity
