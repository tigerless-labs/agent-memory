## Harajuku Shrines and Temples

### Meiji Shrine
- Located in Harajuku
- Described as serene oasis in midst of bustling area
- Major historical site

### Kawai Shrine
- Smaller, lesser-known shrine in quiet alleyway
- Dedicated to: Agriculture, fertility, and industry gods
- Traditional belief: Brings good luck and prosperity
- Notable features:
  - Beautiful gardens (stunning in cherry blossom season March-April and autumn foliage November-December)
  - "Lucky Bell" — legend says ringing it and making a wish causes it to come true
  - Small souvenir shop: omamori (lucky charms) and ema (wooden prayer tablets)
  - Visitors can write wishes on ema and hang them at the shrine
- Good for: Escaping crowds of main streets, exploring historical Harajuku
