## Research Topic
- **Field**: Education Technology (EdTech), specifically AI in education
- **Focus**: Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes
- **Subjects**: Math and reading comprehension
- **Approach**: Broader conceptual research with specific platforms/systems as case studies

## Key Findings
1. AI-powered adaptive learning systems **can be effective** in improving student learning outcomes in math and reading comprehension
2. However, **implementation and integration into the classroom need careful consideration** — success depends heavily on how systems are deployed
3. Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students (equity/inclusion angle)
4. Concern: **algorithmic bias** in these systems could exacerbate existing inequities if not addressed

## Common Challenges Across Case Studies
1. **Teacher professional development**: Many teachers lacked necessary skills and knowledge to effectively use systems; hindered successful implementation
2. **Curriculum alignment**: Clear understanding of how systems aligned with curriculum and learning goals was crucial; when alignment was clear, teachers were more likely to adopt and use systems effectively
3. **Algorithmic bias**: Risk of perpetuating or worsening existing educational inequities

## Recommendations Made
1. Educators and policymakers should **prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support** as teachers implement systems
2. Developers should **work closely with educators** to ensure systems are designed with needs of teachers and students in mind
3. Developers should provide **clear guidance on curriculum alignment and learning goals**
4. **Ongoing evaluation and assessment** of systems to ensure effectiveness

## Conference Presentation
- Presented poster at first research conference (summer 2022)
- Received **positive feedback** from educators and policymakers
- Appreciated the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities
- Some developers reached out afterwards expressing interest in findings and how to apply them

## Stakeholder Perspectives
- **Area of disagreement**: Role of teachers in AI-powered adaptive learning systems
  - Some educators concerned systems would replace teachers
  - Others saw them as valuable tools to support teaching and learning
  - Research emphasizes systems should augment/support teaching, not replace it
