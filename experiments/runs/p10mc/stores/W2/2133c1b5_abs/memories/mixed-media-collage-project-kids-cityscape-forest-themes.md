---

**Project Vision:**
Combining mixed media collage and abstract expressionism styles for a collaborative kids' art project.

**Group Structure:**
- Group size: 3–4 kids per group (balances collaboration with individual contributions)
- Consider mixing ages, skill levels, and personalities
- Assign a group leader/facilitator

**Project Format:**
- Large-scale mixed media collage
- Theme options: cityscape or forest (provides direction while allowing imagination)

**Materials:**
- Papers: construction paper, scrapbook paper, newspaper, magazine cutouts, tissue paper, cardboard
- Adhesives: glue sticks, white glue, tape, staples, decoupage medium
- Paints and inks: tempera, acrylic, watercolor, markers, colored pencils, stamp pads
- Textures: fabric, yarn, felt, burlap, natural materials (leaves, twigs)
- Found objects: buttons, bottle caps, cardboard tubes, recyclable items
- Additional: glitter, stickers, stencils

**Teaching Approach:**
- Demonstrate basics but avoid over-instruction
- Encourage experimentation with materials
- Provide examples of mixed media collages for inspiration
- Set up materials stations for rotation
- Emphasize process over final product
- Use open-ended questions to spark brainstorming

**Theme Introduction Examples:**
*Cityscape:* Show images, ask "What makes this a city?" Brainstorm buildings, cars, people, streets. Ask about futuristic vs. historical vs. imaginary cities.
*Forest:* Show images, ask "What makes this a forest?" Brainstorm trees, animals, layers (canopy, forest floor). Ask about dense jungle vs. magical forest vs. backyard forest.

**Group Activities:**
1. Collaborative brainstorming
2. Quick sketching/rough outline
3. Material exploration
4. Collaborative goal-setting

**Supervision Tips:**
- Time limit: 30–60 minutes
- Use smocks/aprons for protection
- Document process and final products with photos
- Circulate to provide guidance and encouragement
- Encourage collaboration and material sharing
