## Overview
The Coral Triangle Initiative (CTI) is a regional partnership established 2007 to promote conservation and sustainable use of marine and coastal resources in the Coral Triangle region. The region encompasses parts of six countries and is home to incredibly diverse marine species and habitats—considered the epicenter of marine biodiversity and a global conservation priority.

## Indonesia and Philippines Role
Both are founding members and their participation was instrumental in developing and implementing the CTI Regional Plan of Action, which outlines shared vision, goals, and strategies for conservation, sustainable use, and equitable benefits sharing.

## Key Initiatives

1. **Marine Protected Areas (MPAs)** — Indonesia and Philippines have established and managed significant number of MPAs within Coral Triangle region; serve as breeding and feeding grounds; protect critical ecosystems (coral reefs, seagrass beds, mangroves)

2. **Sustainable Fisheries** — Both promoting sustainable fishing practices and improved fisheries management:
   - Capacity building for local fisher communities
   - Alternative livelihoods
   - Traceability systems

3. **Climate Change Adaptation** — Building resilience through:
   - Ecosystem-based adaptation strategies
   - Climate-resilient fisheries management frameworks
   - Marine monitoring and data collection systems

## Significance
CTI serves as vital platform for regional cooperation between Indonesia and Philippines toward conservation and sustainable use of marine resources.
