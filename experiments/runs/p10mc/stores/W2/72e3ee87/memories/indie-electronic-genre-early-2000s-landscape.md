## Indie-electronic genre — Early 2000s context

**The era**: The Postal Service's "Give Up" (2003) coincided with broader explosion of indie-electronic experimentation. Many artists were blending electronic and indie rock during this period, making the genre less novel than it might initially appear.

**Key artists of the era**:
- Death Cab for Cutie
- The Shins
- Modest Mouse
- Aphex Twin
- Four Tet
- Boards of Canada

**Genre trajectory**: Indie-electronic evolved from niche experimentation to a mainstream genre during and after the early 2000s. What seemed innovative at the time was part of a broader wave of genre-blending across the indie and electronic music scenes.
