## Inherited Heirlooms to Insure

Three family items requiring appraisal and insurance coverage:

1. Grandmother's vintage diamond necklace (highest priority)
2. Antique music box from great-aunt (second priority)
3. Depression-era glassware from mother (third priority)

All recently inherited with sentimental and monetary value.

## Recommended Insurance Companies

Specialty insurers for valuables:
- Jewelers Mutual (jewelry, vintage and antique pieces)
- Chubb (high-value items including jewelry, art, collectibles)
- AXA ART (art, antiques, collectibles, jewelry)
- Hagerty (collectibles including jewelry, art, antiques)

Mainstream companies with specialty divisions:
- State Farm (high-value items department)
- Allstate (Valuable Items policy)
- Liberty Mutual (Valuable Possessions policy)

Also consider independent insurance brokers specializing in high-value items.

## Appraisal Process

Key steps:
1. Find certified appraiser
2. Provide documentation (ownership proof via will/trust/inheritance papers, provenance, repair records, photos/videos)
3. Get detailed written report with description, value estimate, methodology, supporting images

Appraiser certifications to look for:

Jewelry appraisers:
- NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers)
- AGTA (American Gem Trade Association)
- ASA (American Society of Appraisers)

Antique and collectible appraisers:
- AAA (Appraisers Association of America)
- ISA (International Society of Appraisers)
- NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers)

Factors affecting appraisal value:
- Diamond specifications (carat weight, cut, color, clarity, number)
- Metal type and purity
- Craftsmanship and design
- Condition (damage, wear, repair needs)
- Rarity and uniqueness
- Market demand
- Comparable sales

## Strategy

1. Appraise and insure necklace first (likely most valuable)
2. Then appraise music box (find specialist in antique mechanical/musical items)
3. Finally appraise glassware (find specialist in antique glassware/ceramics)

One appraisal per item is sufficient for most cases. Multiple appraisals only if: high-value or rare item, complex or unusual piece, or required by insurance company.

## Insurance Policy Preferences

Look for policies offering:
- Agreed value coverage (pays stated amount if lost or damaged)
- No deductible or low deductible
- Worldwide coverage option
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance or loss

Keep all appraisal reports and insurance policies organized and accessible.
