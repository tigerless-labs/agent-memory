## Analysis Points Discussed

**Setting's role in novel:**
- Moors symbolize characters' wild passions (esp. Heathcliff/Catherine)
- Wuthering Heights vs. Thrushcross Grange contrast represents social class differences and character temperaments
- Setting creates gothic atmosphere, isolation, confinement that enhances themes of obsession
- Detailed descriptions intended to reflect characters' inner emotional landscapes

**Characters' importance:**
- Heathcliff and Catherine's intense love/obsession is primary driver of plot
- Secondary characters (Edgar, Isabella, Linton) contribute through relationships and their own obsessions

**User's perspective:** Finds detailed setting descriptions excessive; questions whether novel could convey themes equally well with less emphasis on landscape description.
