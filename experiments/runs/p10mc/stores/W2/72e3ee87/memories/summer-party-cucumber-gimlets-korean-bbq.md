## Summer Party Setup

**Core Drinks:**
- **Cucumber Gimlets** — served successfully at recent BBQ, friends loved them
- Grilled pineapple as garnish — tried at last BBQ, worked really well

**Food:**
- Korean-inspired dishes: Bibimbap, kimchi tacos
- BBQ items (sauces/marinades recommended: gochujang BBQ, soy-ginger, Korean-style with gochugaru)
- Sides: kimchi, spicy pickled carrots, kimchi coleslaw, gochujang roasted vegetables, Korean-style corn, cucumber kimchi salad

**Drink Pairings:**
- For Bibimbap: Soju Spritz, Cucumber-Lime Refresher, Korean Iced Tea
- For Kimchi Tacos: Spicy Mango Mule, Ginger-Grapefruit Collins, Cucumber-Kiwi Cooler
- General rule: spicy dishes need sweet/creamy balance; savory dishes need bright citrusy flavors

**DIY Cocktail Bar Essentials:**
- Mixers: ginger beer, lime juice, grapefruit juice, cucumber-lime juice, soy sauce-infused simple syrup
- Garnishes: cucumber slices, lime wedges, mint, kimchi, pickled ginger, sesame seeds, fresh herbs
- Equipment: ice, various glasses, muddler, jigger, garnish picks
