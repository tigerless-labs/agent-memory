## The Postal Service — Give Up (2003)

**Band**: Collaboration between Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel).

**Standout tracks**:
- "Such Great Heights" — dreamy synth-driven anthem about new love; features acoustic guitar main melody
- "The District Sleeps Alone Tonight" — melancholy, introspective; about disconnection and insomnia
- "Clark Gable" — quirky, upbeat; charming lyrics about romance
- "We Will Become Silhouettes" — somber, reflective; about coping with nuclear apocalypse aftermath
- "Recycled Air" — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar riff; propulsive yet dreamy
- "Sleeping In" — vocal lines weave in and out; lush chorus with almost orchestral complexity

**Distinguishing features**:
- Acoustic instruments (guitar, drums) layered with electronic beats and synthesizers
- Extensive vocal layering and harmonies across multiple tracks
- Postal-related imagery woven throughout discography
- Genre-blending collaboration representing bold fusion of indie rock and electronic music

**Contemporaries**: Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada.
