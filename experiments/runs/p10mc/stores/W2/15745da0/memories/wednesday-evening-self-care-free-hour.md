## Wednesday Evening Self-Care

User has a free hour on Wednesday evenings and is interested in self-care activities to unwind and recharge.

## Suggested Self-Care Activities
- Relaxing bath with essential oils (lavender, chamomile) and candles
- Reading time (fiction, self-help, magazines)
- Yoga or stretching (apps like Down Dog)
- Creative expression (painting, drawing, knitting, writing)
- Meditation or guided imagery (Headspace, Calm, YouTube)
- Listening to favorite music or discovering new playlists
- Face mask or skincare routine (facial, exfoliate, moisturize)
- Journaling (reflect on day/week, gratitude, accomplishments, goals)
- Walking or light movement (neighborhood walk, tai chi, qigong)
- Phone-free time (one hour without screens)
- Aromatherapy (diffuser, candles; scents: lavender, chamomile, bergamot)
- Tea time (herbal tea: chamomile, peppermint, lemon balm)
- Laughing/humor (funny videos, articles, memes)
- Nature connection (window gazing, balcony/patio time, observe natural world)
- Power napping (20-minute nap for refresh)

## Key Principle
Self-care is about making time for yourself and doing what feels nourishing and enjoyable. Experiment to find what works best individually.
