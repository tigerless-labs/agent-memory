## Yoga for Flexibility and Stress Relief

User interested in yoga poses that improve flexibility and reduce stress.

## Flexibility Poses
- Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana): stretches hamstrings, calves, spine
- Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana): opens chest and shoulders; stretches upper back
- Triangle Pose (Trikonasana): stretches hips, thighs, spine
- Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana): stretches entire back side from shoulders to heels
- Pigeon Pose (Eka Pada Rajakapotasana): stretches hips, glutes, lower back; releases tension

## Stress Relief Poses
- Child's Pose (Balasana): gentle, calming; stretches back and hips; promotes relaxation
- Cat-Cow Pose (Marjaryasana-Bitilasana): flowing sequence; releases tension in neck, shoulders, spine
- Seated Twist (Bharadvajasana): gentle twist releasing tension in spine, shoulders, hips
- Sphinx Pose (Salamba Bhujangasana): gentle backbend; opens chest and shoulders; reduces stress
- Legs Up The Wall Pose (Viparita Karani): inverted pose; calms nervous system; reduces stress; promotes relaxation

## Breathing Exercises
- Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana): calming; balances nervous system; reduces stress
- 4-7-8 Breathing (Relaxation Breathing): simple yet effective; calms mind and body

## Practice Tips
- Listen to body; modify or exit if experiencing discomfort or pain
- Practice regularly for consistent improvement in flexibility and stress relief
- Combine with other stress-reducing activities (meditation, deep breathing)
- Consult healthcare professional or yoga therapist before starting, especially with existing conditions
- Remember: yoga is a journey; be patient, gentle, and kind to yourself
