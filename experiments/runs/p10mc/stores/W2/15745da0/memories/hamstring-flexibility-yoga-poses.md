## Hamstring Flexibility Challenge

User reports struggling with hamstring flexibility and is interested in yoga poses that target hamstrings specifically.

## Yoga Poses for Hamstring Flexibility

**Standing Poses**
- Standing Forward Fold (Uttanasana): stretches hamstrings, back of body
- Standing Hamstring Stretch (Prasarita Padottanasana): gentle stretch targeting hamstrings, calves, glutes
- Warrior Pose (Virabhadrasana): powerful pose stretching hamstrings, glutes, hips

**Seated Poses**
- Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana): deeper stretch for hamstrings, calves, spine
- Seated Hamstring Stretch (Akarna Dhanurasana): gentle stretch for hamstrings and calves
- Janu Sirsasana (Head-to-Knee Pose): stretches hamstrings, glutes, hips

**Supine Poses**
- Supine Hamstring Stretch (Supta Padangusthasana): gentle stretch for hamstrings and glutes
- Legs Up The Wall Pose (Viparita Karani): inverted pose stretching hamstrings, calves, glutes

## Practice Guidelines
- Start slow and gentle, especially if new to yoga or have tight hamstrings
- Hold each pose for 3-5 breaths, breathing deeply and slowly
- Engage core and maintain long spine to avoid lower back pressure
- Don't bounce or force stretch; focus on relaxing into pose
- Practice regularly for consistent improvement
- Stretch after workout or end of day when muscles are warm
- Use props (blocks, straps, wall) to modify and make accessible

## Complementary Strategies
- Incorporate strength-building exercises (squats, lunges)
- Flexibility takes time and patience
- Be gentle with self; don't push past discomfort or pain
- With consistent practice, improvement comes over time
