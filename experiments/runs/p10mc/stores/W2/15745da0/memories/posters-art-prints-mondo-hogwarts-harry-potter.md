## Collection Overview
- **Recent acquisition**: Mondo poster featuring Hogwarts castle from Harry Potter (May 21, 2023)
- **Goal**: Display nicely to showcase artwork

## Storage Options
- Sturdy acid-free poster tubes or cardboard tubes to prevent creasing
- Flat files or portfolios with individual compartments for large quantities
- Acid-free folders or sleeves to prevent moisture/UV damage

## Display Ideas
- Frame with acid-free mats and UV-filtering glass/acrylic
- Poster hangers or clips for walls/doors (good for renters)
- Art print racks or stands on shelf/desk
- Shadow box displays for depth and dimension
- Group by theme (e.g., Harry Potter or fantasy)

## Care Instructions
- Handle with care to prevent creasing, tearing, or scratching
- Use acid-free materials (mats, folders, frames)
- Periodic rotation to keep display fresh
- Store in clean, dry environment
