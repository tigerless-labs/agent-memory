## Summer Sandals — May 2023

User seeking new sandals for summer with:
- A bit of a heel
- Fun, decorative straps
- Trendy style
- Must be comfortable

### Recommended brands and styles:
1. Steve Madden Sandy — low heel, strappy details, buckle closure
2. Vince Camuto Brynn — moderate heel, tassel on toe strap
3. Sam Edelman Penny — low heel, ankle strap with buckle closure
4. Tory Burch Miller — moderate heel, signature gold buckle and tassel

### Selection criteria:
- Prioritize cushioning and arch support
- Consider heel height and strap width for comfort
- Breathable materials for all-day wear
