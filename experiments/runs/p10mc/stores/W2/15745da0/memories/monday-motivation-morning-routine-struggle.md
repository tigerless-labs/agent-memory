## Monday Motivation Challenge

User reports struggling to get motivated on Mondays and is interested in developing a morning routine to:
- Boost energy levels
- Set a positive tone for the day
- Improve Monday productivity

## Suggested Morning Routine Framework (6:00 AM - 8:00 AM)
- 6:00 AM: Wake up, stretch, natural sunlight exposure (10-15 min)
- 6:15 AM: Cold shower
- 6:30 AM: Mindfulness meditation (10 min)
- 6:40 AM: Gratitude practice (5 min)
- 6:45 AM: Light physical activity (10 min)
- 7:00 AM: Healthy breakfast (complex carbs, protein, healthy fats)
- 7:20 AM: Plan day and review schedule
- 7:30 AM: Get ready, listen to uplifting music
- 7:45 AM: Fresh air time
- 8:00 AM: Start day refreshed

## Key Components
- Morning sunlight and stretching
- Cold shower for alertness and circulation
- Mindfulness/meditation (10-15 min, apps like Headspace, Calm)
- Gratitude writing (three things grateful for)
- Physical activity (yoga, jumping jacks, short walk)
- Nourishing breakfast (avoid sugary foods)
- Day planning and task prioritization
- Uplifting music or motivational podcasts
- Small reward after routine completion

## Principles
- Consistency is essential; stick to the same time daily
- Start with small, achievable goals and build up
- Flexibility important—don't stress if a day is missed
- Adjust based on what works for the individual
