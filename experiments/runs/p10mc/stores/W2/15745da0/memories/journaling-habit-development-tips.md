## Journaling as Habit

User wants to start a journal to reflect on day, week, or month and is seeking strategies to make it a consistent habit.

## Tips for Consistent Journaling

**Setup & Space**
- Set specific goal: decide frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and start manageable
- Choose dedicated, quiet, comfortable space without distractions
- Journal in same spot each time
- Select journal type that appeals (lined, unlined, dot-grid)

**Routine & Ritual**
- Incorporate into daily/weekly routine (after waking, before bed, lunch break)
- Develop pre-journaling ritual (tea, candle, soothing music)
- Make it a habit loop: attach to existing habit (after brushing teeth, before coffee)

**Writing Practice**
- Start small: even a few sentences; gradually increase depth
- Write without editing: journal is for eyes only; ignore grammar/spelling
- Be flexible: don't stress if missing days; get back ASAP
- Write freely, let thoughts flow

**Guidance & Reflection**
- Use guided prompts:
  - What am I grateful for today?
  - What did I learn today?
  - What am I looking forward to?
  - What challenges am I facing; how to overcome?
- Periodically review entries to reflect on progress, growth, patterns
- Identify areas for improvement and celebrate achievements

**Format**
- Physical journal, note-taking app, or digital tools (Evernote, Day One)
- Experiment to find what works best

## Key Mindset
Be patient, kind, and gentle with yourself. Don't be hard on self for missing days. Celebrate small wins along the way.
