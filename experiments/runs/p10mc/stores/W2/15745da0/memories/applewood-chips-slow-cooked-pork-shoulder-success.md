## Applewood Success

Recently used applewood chips with a pork shoulder slow-cooked on the grill. The flavor was incredible — the sweetness of the applewood complements the richness of the pork perfectly, and the smoky flavor adds nice depth.

Slow-cooking pork shoulder on a grill breaks down connective tissues, making it fall-apart tender and juicy.

## Wood Chip Experimentation

User is exploring different wood chip types for smoking meats, particularly for pork shoulder.

**Recommendations considered:**
- Hickory — classic choice, strong sweet and savory flavor with notes of bacon and vanilla
- Apple wood — fruity, sweet, mild flavor (✓ user confirmed success)
- Cherry wood — mild, fruity flavor with hints of tartness
- Pecan wood — rich, nutty, slightly sweet
- Post oak — strong, smoky flavor with notes of vanilla and caramel

**Tips:** Soak wood chips for 30+ minutes; can mix types for unique profiles; monitor temperature for consistent smoke.

## Enhancement Techniques

- Dry brining before cooking
- Injecting with flavor (apple cider, bourbon, herbs)
- Finishing with sweet and sticky glaze (honey, brown sugar, Dijon mustard)
