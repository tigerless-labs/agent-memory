## Collection Overview
- **Notable piece**: Rare 1978 pressing of Fleetwood Mac's Rumours (acquired May 21, 2023)
- **Goal**: Prominent display of rare pressing

## Storage Options Being Considered
- Sturdy acid-free record crates or boxes with dividers
- Record storage cubes for small spaces
- LP storage shelves with individual slots and lips
- Records stored upright to prevent warping

## Display Ideas
- Record display wall using grid/hooks or clips
- Display shelves with dedicated area for rare finds
- Rotating displays for different angles
- Acrylic stands for individual showcase
- Grouped display by artist (e.g., Fleetwood Mac discography)

## Maintenance
- Handle records by edges only (never center label)
- Regular cleaning and conditioning
- Store in clean, dry environment to prevent damage
- Consider obtaining record player for enjoyment
