## Brown Dress Shoes — May 2023

User seeking brown dress shoes suitable for work or formal events.

### Recommended brands:
- Allen Edmonds — Park Avenue or Dalton styles
- Johnston Murphy — Conrad or Melville styles
- Clarks — Cloud or Tuscany styles
- Ted Baker — unique brogue details
- Magnanni — Spanish handcrafted brand

### Recommended styles:
- Oxfords: classic, suitable for work and formal events, elegant
- Derbies: open lacing system, often more comfortable than Oxfords, good for formal events
- Loafers: more casual yet elegant, dress up or down

### Features to look for:
- Cushioning in insole and midsole
- Breathable materials (leather or mesh panels)
- Soft, supple leather that molds over time
- Consider custom fitting or alteration for precise fit
