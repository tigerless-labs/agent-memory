## Finding Coin Shows and Error Coin Resources

**Official Resources**
- American Numismatic Association (ANA) website (money.org): "Coin Shows" section with searchable directory by state/city/zip
- CoinWeek.com: Comprehensive coin show calendar, searchable by state or month

**Local Resources**
- Local coin dealers and coin shops: Often have information on nearby shows
- Community: Online forums and Facebook groups dedicated to coin collecting and error coins
- Reddit: r/CoinCollecting community

**Tips**
- Verify dates, times, locations as shows subject to change
- Join online communities to stay informed about upcoming events
- Connect with local dealers for insider information
