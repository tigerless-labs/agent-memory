## Finding Stamp Shows and Philately Resources

**Official Resources**
- American Philatelic Society (APS) website (stamps.org): "Calendar of Events" section lists upcoming shows and exhibitions across US, filterable by date/location/event type

**Local Resources**
- Local stamp clubs and philatelic organizations: Often have information on regional and national events
- Stamp dealers and auction houses: Participate in or sponsor events, can provide information

**Communities**
- Online forums and social media groups dedicated to stamp collecting
- Reddit: r/Stamps and philately subreddits
- Facebook groups for stamp collectors

**Finding Shows**
- Google search: "stamp shows near me" or "stamp exhibitions [city/state]"
- Verify dates and locations as subject to change
