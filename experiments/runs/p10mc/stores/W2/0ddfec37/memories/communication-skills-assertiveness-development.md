## Challenge

## Practice Approach

Role-playing meeting scenarios to build confidence and assertiveness

**Techniques being practiced:**
- Acknowledge other person's concerns first
- Provide specific data/evidence to support ideas
- Offer compromises that address resource concerns
- Take ownership of tasks to reduce risk
- Stay calm and professional under pushback

## Effective Patterns Observed

From June 13 email marketing proposal role-play:
- Starting with "I understand your point, but..." shows respect
- Following up with concrete numbers strengthens case
- Proposing scaled-down versions overcomes objections
- Volunteering for implementation reduces resistance
