## Online Platforms for Selling Vintage Cameras

**Specialized Forums (Recommended for Leica)**
- Fred Miranda: Popular photography forum with dedicated marketplace for cameras
- Rangefinder Forum: Community focused on rangefinder cameras including Leica
- Photography Stack Exchange: Q&A platform with buying/selling section

**General Marketplaces**
- eBay: Large collector base, requires detailed descriptions and quality photos
- Etsy: Focuses on vintage items, requires shop setup
- Amazon: Option available but higher fees

**Social Media & Communities**
- Facebook Groups: Join vintage camera/Leica enthusiast groups
- Instagram: Use hashtags like #vintagecameras, #leica, #filmphotography
- Reddit: r/photography, r/vintagecameras, r/leica

**Specialized Dealers & Auction Houses**
- KEH Camera: Can purchase cameras or help with consignment sales
- WestLicht Auctions: Leading auction house for photography equipment, holds regular auctions

## Selling Best Practices
- Research camera value using multiple sources (price guides, auction results, comparable listings)
- Include original lens and accessories to increase appeal and value
- Clean/prepare camera, necessary repairs/maintenance before listing
- Take high-quality photos from multiple angles showing condition and details
- Write detailed descriptions: condition, features, history, any repairs done
- Set competitive price based on market research
- Use secure payment methods (PayPal, Escrow)
