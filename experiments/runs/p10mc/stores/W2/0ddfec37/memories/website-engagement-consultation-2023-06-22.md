## Consultation Summary
**Date:** June 22, 2023  
**Topic:** Improving website user engagement and achieving viral reach

## User Constraints & Preferences
- Does not want to spend time with analytical tools
- Not interested in technical optimization details (page speed, HTTP requests, etc.)
- Prefers creative and innovative solutions
- Seeking something "never been done before" and truly distinctive
- Pattern: Consistently rejects suggestions, asks for "something better"

## Recommended Analytical Tools
1. **Google Analytics** — free tool tracking time on site, bounce rate, pages per session
2. **Hotjar** — heat maps, user recordings, feedback polls
3. **Crazy Egg** — heat maps and click tracking
4. **Qualaroo** — surveys and feedback forms
5. **Mixpanel** — advanced analytics for user behavior and conversions

## Recommended Strategies (by escalation level)

### Tier 1: Technical Optimization
- Compress images
- Minimize HTTP requests
- Use caching (browser or CDN)
- Optimize code
- Choose reliable web host

### Tier 2: Creative Content & Design
- User-centric design
- Interactive content (quizzes, polls, surveys, games)
- Storytelling and case studies
- Video content
- Personalization
- Customer reviews and testimonials

### Tier 3: Cutting-Edge Technologies
- Virtual Reality (immersive experiences)
- Augmented Reality (product visualization)
- AI (personalized recommendations, chatbots)
- Gamification
- Interactive timelines

### Tier 4: Campaign & Community Ideas
- Non-profit collaboration
- Live-streamed events and webinars
- Viral challenges with hashtags
- Unique contests (product commercials, reviews, stories)
- 360-degree videos
- AI-powered chatbots with natural language processing
- Social cause movements

## Observed Pattern
User repeatedly asks for suggestions "better than" previous rounds, indicating either:
- Unclear/unrealistic expectations about viral reach
- Specific vision not yet articulated
- Seeking external validation of ideas rather than implementation guidance

**Note for future conversations:** May need to explore underlying business goals, target audience, and realistic expectations before recommending specific tactics.
