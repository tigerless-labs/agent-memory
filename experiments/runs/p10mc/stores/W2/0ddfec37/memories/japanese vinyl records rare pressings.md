## Finding Rare Japanese Vinyl Records

**Online Marketplaces**
- Discogs: Vast database with country filter for Japanese pressings, wantlist feature
- eBay: eBay.co.jp for Japan access, eBay.com worldwide filter
- Yahoo! Japan Auctions: Popular for rare records
- Rakuten: Japanese e-commerce with auction section

**Specialty Online Stores**
- Jet Set Records (jetsetrecords.com): Tokyo-based specialty store
- HMV Japan (hmv.co.jp): Large retailer online store
- Disk Union (diskunion.net): Japanese store with rare and second-hand records

**Communities**
- Reddit r/vinyl and r/JapaneseMusic
- Japanese music forums and Facebook groups

**Physical Stores (Tokyo)**
- Tower Records Shibuya
- HMV Shibuya
- Disk Union Shibuya
- Meditations (meditations.jp)
- Shibuya and Shinjuku districts recommended

## Buying Tips
- Research condition and authenticity before purchasing
- Use Google Translate for Japanese sites
- Check shipping costs from Japan
- Be cautious with individual sellers and auctions
