## Chemical Options
- **Scrubbing Bubbles Shower Cleaner** — dissolves soap scum, mildew, hard water stains; fresh scent
- **Lysol Shower & Bathroom Cleaner** — kills mildew and mold; removes soap scum and hard water stains; EPA-registered to kill 99.9% bacteria/viruses
- **Clorox Shower Cleaner** — powerful bleach-based formula; tackles tough soap scum and mildew; whitens and brightens

## Natural/Eco-Friendly Options
- **Seventh Generation Shower & Tub Cleaner** — plant-based, biodegradable
- **Ecover Shower & Bathroom Cleaner** — non-toxic, free of phosphates and chlorine; cruelty-free, vegan

## Other Targeted Options
- **Tilex Mold & Mildew Remover** — specifically targets mold and mildew
- **Shine Bathroom & Shower Cleaner** — gentle, non-abrasive; removes soap scum without scratching

## Application Tips
- Wear gloves and ventilate
- For tough buildup, let cleaner sit a few minutes before scrubbing
- Regular cleaning prevents buildup
