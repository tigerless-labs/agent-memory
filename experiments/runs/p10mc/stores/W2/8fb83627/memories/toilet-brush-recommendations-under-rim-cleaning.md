## Standard Brushes
- **Clorox Toilet Brush** — sturdy bristles; curved head for under-rim reach
- **Scotch-Brite Toilet Brush** — spiral-bristle design; ergonomic handle
- **OXO Good Grips Toilet Brush** — soft-grip handle; compact head for under-rim access

## Long-Handled Options
- **Clorox Toilet Brush with Long Handle** — easier reach without bending
- **Libman Toilet Brush with Long Handle** — sturdy for people with mobility issues

## Angled Head Options
- **Mr. Clean Toilet Brush** — unique angled head for under-rim and tight spaces
- **Casabella Toilet Brush** — curved, angled head for hard-to-reach areas

## Eco-Friendly
- **Seventh Generation Toilet Brush** — bamboo handle, biodegradable bristles
- **Ecover Toilet Brush** — wooden handle, natural bristles

## Care Tips
- Regular disinfection of brush prevents bacterial spread
- Replace every 1–2 years when bristles wear/fray
