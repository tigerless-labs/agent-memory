## Bleach-Based Options
- **Lysol Grout & Mildew Remover** — powerful bleach formula; EPA-registered for 99.9% kill rate
- **Scrubbing Bubbles Grout Cleaner** — gentle, non-abrasive; specifically for grout lines

## Non-Bleach Options
- **OxiClean Grout & Tile Cleaner** — powerful, non-bleach; lifts dirt and grime

## Acid-Based Cleaners (Strong/Risky)
- **CLR Grout Cleaner** — dissolves mineral deposits and grout stains; **can etch certain tile surfaces**
- **Lime-A-Way Grout Cleaner** — acid-based; tackles tough stains; **test on small area first**

## Natural/Eco-Friendly
- **Seventh Generation Grout & Tile Cleaner** — plant-based, non-toxic; biodegradable
- **Ecover Grout & Tile Cleaner** — free of harsh chemicals; gentle on surfaces and environment

## Application Tips
- Always test small area first (especially acid-based)
- Wear gloves, ventilate area
- Use grout brush or old toothbrush to apply and scrub
- Let sit 10–15 minutes for tough stains before scrubbing
- Regular sealing prevents stains and eases future cleaning
