## User Context
- Works with residents in elder care setting (pilot phase project)
- Not formally trained as healthcare professional
- Not heavily involved in broader community
- Seeking to maintain authenticity while proving professional worth

## Central Experience Being Analyzed
Residents' critical comments ("you speak to say nothing") trigger:
- Mood disruption affecting work quality
- Self-related thought processes that disconnect from shared experience
- Situational impacts on interoceptive abilities
- Reduction in drive to help and empathy
- "Experiential withdrawal" (user's own term) — a coping mechanism they've learned to mentalize and verbalize
- Persistent emotional effects over time despite moment-to-moment awareness
- Growing fear of not being able to find the right words to connect with residents

## Thematic Codes Developed

- **T1-SC** (role of resilience in elder care)
- **T1-MI** (mindfulness/compassion approaches)
- **T1-IS** (artificial intelligence impact on elder care)
- **T2-AP, T2-MS, T2-VR** (previous text codes)
- **T3-IF, T3-WA** (previous text codes)

### From current analysis (T4):
- **T4-CM** (impact of comments on mood/work): comments trigger self-reflexivity that disconnects from presence
- **T4-IA** (interoceptive abilities and empathy): situational impact on ability to sense internal states and empathetic connection
- **T4-FC** (fear of connection and proving worth): fear of inadequacy fueled by lack of experience, community involvement, professional training

## Emerging Categories
1. **Impacts of interactions with residents on work and emotional state** — subsumes T4-CM, T4-IA, T4-FC
2. **Role of mindfulness/compassion in care work** — subsumes T1-MI, T4-IA
3. **Technological and systemic contexts** — subsumes T1-IS

## Phenomenological Methodology in Use
- **Bracketing**: Setting aside preconceptions to approach lived experience openly
- **Phenomenological reduction**: Identifying essential characteristics of experiential patterns
- **Phenomenological interpretation**: Reflecting on meanings faithful to lived experience
- **Style**: Edmund Husserl and Maurice Merleau-Ponty — focusing on embodied, situated consciousness and the intersubjective world of shared meaning

## Key Questions for Ongoing Analysis
- How do critical comments disrupt the intentional structure of care work?
- What is the relationship between interoceptive withdrawal and the fear of inadequacy?
- How does authenticity function as both a constraint and a source of meaning in this work?
- How can experiential withdrawal be understood not just as withdrawal *from* but as a meaningful reorientation of consciousness?
