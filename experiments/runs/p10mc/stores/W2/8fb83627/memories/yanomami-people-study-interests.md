## Who Are the Yanomami

The Yanomami are an indigenous people living in the Amazon rainforest across Venezuela and Brazil. They represent one of approximately 400+ distinct ethnic groups in the Amazon Basin.

## User's Specific Interests

- Daily life and cultural practices
- Spiritual beliefs and shamanic traditions
- Traditional ways of life
- How they are struggling with:
  - Disease introduction from outside contact
  - Colonization pressures
  - Deforestation and habitat loss
  - Mining and resource extraction
  - Climate change impacts

## Resources Recommended

**Books:**
- *The Falling Sky: Words of a Yanomami Shaman* by Davi Kopenawa and Bruce Albert — first-hand account from a Yanomami shaman about his people's perspective, beliefs, and struggles
- Works by Napoleon A. Chagnon on Yanomami ethnography and social structure
- *Yanomami: An Account* by Alcida Rita Ramos — history and culture

**Documentaries:**
- *The Yanomami: A Struggle for Survival* (1991) — classic documentary on their struggles
- *The Last Shaman* (2016) — follows a Yanomami shaman preserving traditions

**Articles:**
- National Geographic — Yanomami coverage
- The Guardian — articles on Yanomami land rights and survival
- The New Yorker — in-depth reporting on challenges they face
- Cultural Survival — ongoing news and updates
