## Story Concept: Time Travel Football Intervention

**Characters:**
- **John**: young football coach
  - Role: tactical strategy, game planning, analyzing opponent weaknesses/strengths
- **Jack**: experienced time traveler with device to boost player focus
  - Role: individual player development and lifestyle management

**Mission:**
- Travel to 1982 World Cup: Brazil vs Italy
- Historical result: Italy won 3-2
- Goal: reverse outcome, make Brazil win

**Role Division:**

*John's Tasks:*
- Design detailed tactical game plan
- Analyze Italian team's strengths/weaknesses
- Outline Brazilian team's field roles/responsibilities

*Jack's Tasks (using device with key Brazilian players):*
- Prevent wine drinking night before match
- Stop partying/night-before distractions
- Accompany players to gym for fitness prep
- Solve emotional problems with girlfriends
- Keep players mentally focused on match

**Story Setup:**
- Mysterious event: all stars go out one night
- John selected for journey
- Meets Jack (time traveler)
- Jack explains time travel rules: specific past points only, limited stay, careful not to change too much
- 1982 Brazil vs Italy chosen as first mission
