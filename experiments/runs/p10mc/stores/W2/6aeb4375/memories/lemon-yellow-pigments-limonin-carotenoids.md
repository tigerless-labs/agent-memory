## Lemon Yellow Pigmentation

When asked about the pigment that gives lemons their yellow color:

**Primary pigment:** Limonin — a flavonoid compound responsible for yellow, orange, and red coloring in fruits and vegetables. Located in the peel and outer layers of the lemon.

**Contributing pigments:**
- Carotenoids
- Flavonols

These compounds work together to produce the characteristic bright yellow appearance of lemons.
