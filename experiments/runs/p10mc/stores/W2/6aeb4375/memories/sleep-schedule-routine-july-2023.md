## Sleep Schedule and Bedtime Routine (July 2023)

**Current situation:**
- Having sleep troubles; trying to establish consistent sleep schedule
- Typically starts getting ready for bed around 11:15 PM
- Often ends up going to bed around 11:45 PM (30 minutes later than desired)
- Has been trying to read 20+ minutes before bed but gets too engrossed and stays up late

**Planned bedtime routine (11:15 PM – 11:45 PM):**
1. **11:15–11:20 PM (5 min)**: Deep breaths, dim lights
2. **11:20–11:30 PM (10 min)**: Physical book reading (with timer to prevent overengagement)
3. **11:30–11:35 PM (5 min)**: Quick relaxation (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, or guided imagery)
4. **11:35–11:40 PM (5 min)**: Get ready for bed (brush teeth, change into pajamas, turn off lights)
5. **11:40–11:45 PM (5 min)**: Get into bed, settle, final deep breaths

**Book choice strategy:**
- Prefers physical books over e-books to avoid blue light interference
- Avoids thrillers or page-turners; opts for calming fiction, poetry, or self-help
- Sets timer for 10 minutes to prevent reading overengagement

**Accountability strategies:**
- Set phone reminder for 10:15 PM daily to put phone away
- Find comfortable spot in bedroom for reading/relaxation (to reduce phone/TV temptation)
- Asked friend or family member to check in every few days for accountability

**Overall goal:** Create consistent wind-down routine that signals body it's time to sleep, improve sleep quality and duration
