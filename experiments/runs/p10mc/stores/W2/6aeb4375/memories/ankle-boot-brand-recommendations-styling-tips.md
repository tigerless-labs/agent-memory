## Ankle Boot Brand Recommendations

### High-Quality Options
1. **Sam Edelman** — known for quality, comfort, stylish designs; $150–$300 range
2. **Vince** — fashion-forward, high-quality, chic to edgy styles
3. **Stuart Weitzman** — comfort-focused, quality materials (leather, suede, nubuck), flat to heeled options
4. **By Far** — modern, sleek designs; popular with fashion influencers
5. **Acne Studios** — fashion-forward, edgy aesthetic, high-quality

### Budget-Friendly Option
- **Clarks** — comfortable, affordable (under $100), trendy designs; less durable than high-end options

## Black Boots + White Dress Styling Tips

1. **Balance volume** — pair with fitted dress hitting above the knee to elongate legs
2. **Add a blazer or cardigan** — tailored blazer/cardigan in neutral (beige, navy, black) for sophistication
3. **Play with textures** — mix smooth boots with textured dress (lace, eyelet, soft cotton)
4. **Keep accessories simple** — delicate necklace or stud earrings; avoid chunky pieces
5. **Consider neckline** — V-neck or scoop neckline draws eye upward; crew neck pairs with statement necklace
6. **Add a belt** — thin belt in neutral (tan, black, brown) defines silhouette and breaks up volume
7. **Keep vibe cohesive** — use neutral or monochromatic colors; avoid bright colors or bold patterns that clash

## Shopping Considerations

- **Material:** Leather, suede, nubuck all viable depending on weather and style
- **Heel height:** Choose height for comfort and lifestyle
- **Style:** From sleek/minimalist to bold/ornate — match personal taste
- **Comfort:** Always try on and walk around to test fit
