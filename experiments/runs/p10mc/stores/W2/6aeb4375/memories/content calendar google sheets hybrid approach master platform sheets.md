- Platform (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, etc.)
- Content Title/Description
- Hashtags (if applicable)
- Keywords (if applicable)
- Image/Video URL (if applicable)
- Follower Count (by platform)
- Follower Growth Rate
- Engagement Rate

- Top Performing Content
- Best Posting Times
- Hashtag Performance tracking

### Separate Platform Sheets
Create focused sheets for each platform with platform-specific columns (e.g., Instagram-specific hashtags, Facebook-specific ad targeting)

## Tracking Metrics
- Follower growth and engagement rates
- Hashtag performance (which ones drive most engagement)
- Tools: Instagram Insights + social media analytics tool
