## Tools Recommended (July 2023)

### Scheduling & Management Tools
1. **Hootsuite** — Social media management, built-in content calendar, multi-platform scheduling
2. **Buffer** — Social media scheduling, content calendar feature, free plan available
3. **CoSchedule** — Content marketing calendar, multi-channel scheduling, free trial + paid
4. **Trello** — Project management for content (boards, lists, cards), flexible
5. **Airtable** — Database-driven custom calendar, flexible but requires setup

### Templates
1. **Google Sheets** — User's choice; can duplicate/customize
2. **Microsoft Excel** — Free templates from templates.office.com
3. **Canva** — Free visually appealing content calendar templates

### Alternative: Paper Planner
- Physical notebook or paper planner option if preferred

## Selection Criteria
- Ease of use
- Customization options
- Integration with existing workflow
- Cost (free vs. paid)
- Scalability
