## Purchase Decision

Decided to invest in high-quality **Sam Edelman ankle boots** (block heel style) for summer.

### Why Sam Edelman over Clarks:
- Prioritizes quality materials, craftsmanship, and comfort
- Willing to invest for durability across multiple seasons
- Appreciates stylish, on-trend designs
- Boots will retain value over time

### Price consideration:
- Sam Edelman: $150–$300 range
- Clarks: Under $100 (considered but rejected)

## Styling Plans for the Boots

- **With dresses and skirts** — for elevated, sophisticated looks
- **Flowy maxi dress** — for bohemian chic vibe
- **High-waisted mom jeans** — for casual, everyday wear

The block heel provides height without sacrificing comfort.
