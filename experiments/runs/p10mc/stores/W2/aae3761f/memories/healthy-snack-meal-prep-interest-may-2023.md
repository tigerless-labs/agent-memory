## Meal Prep & Healthy Snacking (May 20, 2023)

User is revamping meal prep routine and wants to prepare healthy snacks in advance to:
- Curb unhealthy impulse snacking
- Stay fueled between meals
- Save time during the week

**Snack options being considered:**
- Fresh cut fruit (apples, oranges, bananas, grapes, berries) in airtight containers
- Roasted nuts (almonds, walnuts, pecans) with olive oil and salt
- Seed blends (pumpkin, sunflower, chia seeds)
- Veggie sticks (carrots, celery, cucumber) with hummus or guacamole
- Roasted sweet potato or beet sticks
- Energy balls (rolled oats, nuts, dried fruits)
- Hard-boiled eggs
- Greek yogurt parfaits with granola and berries
- Homemade whole grain crackers
- Trail mix (nuts, seeds, dried fruits, optional dark chocolate)
- Roasted chickpeas or edamame
- Homemade granola
- Veggies with homemade ranch dip

**Organization:** Store in airtight containers labeled with date and ingredients for freshness tracking
