## Beef Stew Plan (May 20, 2023)

**Preparation:**
- Brown beef (chuck roast or round, cut into bite-sized pieces) in Dutch oven
- Heat pan to medium-high (4-5 on scale) for 2-3 minutes
- Add 1-2 tablespoons oil, let shimmer
- Sear beef in single layer 2-3 minutes per side until deep brown
- Don't stir too much to preserve brown crust

**Potatoes:**
- Russet (most popular, high starch, holds shape well)
- Yukon Gold (buttery, waxy, retains shape)
- Red Bliss (waxy, sweet, earthy flavor, retains shape)
- Cut into 1-2 inch cubes
- Optional: soak 30 minutes in cold water to reduce starchiness
- Add to slow cooker in last 1-3 hours of cooking

**Cooking:**
- Cook on low 8-10 hours or high 4-6 hours
- Vegetables: potatoes, carrots, onions
- Liquid: mix beef broth and red wine (optional)
- Optional additions: tomato paste or diced tomatoes for richness and acidity

**Side dishes:**
- Crusty bread (baguette, ciabatta, or rustic) for dipping
- Simple green salad with mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, light vinaigrette

**Equipment:** Dutch oven for browning
