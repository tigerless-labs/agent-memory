## Rutgers Study Abroad Resources

**Program scope:**
- 320+ programs available
- 60+ countries covered
- Lengths from few weeks to full academic year
- Disciplines include language studies, anthropology, business, engineering

**Application steps:**
1. Research programs on Rutgers Global website
2. Meet with Rutgers Global advisor
3. Complete online application
4. Submit supporting documents
5. Await acceptance

**Financial aid options:**
- Federal aid (Pell Grants, Stafford Loans)
- State aid (NJ programs)
- Rutgers scholarships: Global Experience Scholarship, Study Abroad Need-Based Scholarship, International Public Service Award
- External scholarships: Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship, Fund for Education Abroad
- Work-study opportunities abroad

**Language considerations:**
- Requirements vary by program and location
- Rutgers offers Spanish language courses through Department of Spanish and Portuguese Studies
- Language proficiency tests may be required for some programs

**Support services:**
- Advising
- Pre-departure orientations
- Financial aid counseling
