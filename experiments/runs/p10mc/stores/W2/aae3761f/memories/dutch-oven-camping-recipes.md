**Breakfast recipes:**
- Dutch Oven Pancakes: Mix batter in Dutch oven, cook on portable grill, top with syrup/butter/fresh fruit
- Campfire Breakfast Casserole: Layer scrambled eggs, sausage, bell peppers, onions, potatoes in Dutch oven, cook on grill

**Lunch and dinner recipes:**
- Dutch Oven Chili: Brown ground beef and onions, add canned tomatoes, beans, spices, simmer on portable grill
- Grilled Chicken and Veggie Pot Pie: Mix cooked chicken, veggies, pot pie crust in Dutch oven, cook on grill, serve hot
- Campfire Lasagna: Layer lasagna noodles, meat sauce, ricotta cheese, mozzarella in Dutch oven, cook on grill

**Dessert recipes:**
- Dutch Oven Apple Cobbler: Mix sliced apples, sugar, cinnamon in Dutch oven, top with biscuit dough, cook on grill
- Campfire Peach Crumble: Mix sliced peaches, sugar, cinnamon in Dutch oven, top with crumble mixture, cook on grill

**Cooking tips for Dutch oven with portable grill:**
- Use a heat diffuser (cast-iron skillet or ceramic plate) between Dutch oven and grill for even heat distribution
- Monitor temperature to ensure safe internal temperature (usually 165°F for meat)
- Adjust cooking time based on recipe and grill heat output
- Keep Dutch oven and utensils clean to prevent cross-contamination and foodborne illness
