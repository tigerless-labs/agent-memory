**Pre-use checks:**
- Inspect grill for damage, rust, worn parts; ensure connections secure
- Check propane tank filled and in good condition; verify expiration date
- Follow manufacturer instructions for tank maintenance

**Setup and operation:**
- Place grill on level, stable surface away from flammable materials, overhanging branches, dry leaves
- Maintain safe distance - keep children and pets at least 3 feet away
- Use heat-resistant surface (grill mat or fireproof pad) to prevent damage to ground
- Monitor wind direction to avoid sparks/embers blowing toward flammable materials
- Never leave grill unattended while in use

**Safety during use:**
- Use long-handled utensils to avoid burns
- Keep fire extinguisher or bucket of sand nearby for emergencies
- Be cautious with hot grease/oil (can cause serious burns)
- Avoid overloading grill with too much food (causes flare-ups)

**Post-use precautions:**
- Turn off grill when not in use; ensure it cools before storing
- Store in well-ventilated area away from flammable materials, out of reach of children and pets
- Dispose of ash and waste properly per campsite regulations

**Camping-specific safety:**
- Follow campsite rules and regulations for portable grills and campfires
- Check with local authorities for fire restrictions or burn bans
- Keep bucket of water nearby, especially in wildfire-prone areas
