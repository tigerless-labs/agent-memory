**Breakfast recipes:**
- Campfire Skillet: Scrambled eggs, sausage, bell peppers, onions, potatoes in cast-iron skillet
- Grilled Pancakes: Pancake batter in portable container, grill, top with syrup/butter/fresh fruit
- Campfire Breakfast Burritos: Scrambled eggs, chorizo, cheese, veggies wrapped in tortilla, grilled

**Lunch and dinner recipes:**
- Grilled Chicken Fajitas: Marinate chicken breast, grill with bell peppers and onions, serve with tortillas, sour cream, salsa
- Campfire Burgers: Grilled to perfection, topped with lettuce, tomato, cheese, favorite toppings
- Shrimp and Veggie Skewers: Marinated shrimp and veggies on skewers, grilled, served with quinoa or rice
- Grilled Chicken and Veggie Wraps: Grilled chicken and veggies wrapped in tortilla with hummus, avocado, sprouts

**Snacks and desserts:**
- Grilled Pineapple: Pineapple slices brushed with brown sugar and cinnamon, grilled until caramelized
- Campfire S'mores: Classic - marshmallows, chocolate, graham crackers
- Trail Mix: Nuts, seeds, dried fruits in portable container

**Prep tips:**
- Pack smart: Use airtight containers to keep ingredients fresh and organized
- Prep ahead: Marinate meats before trip to save time
- Keep it simple: Choose recipes with minimal ingredients and cooking time
- Clean as you go: Regular grill/utensil cleaning prevents cross-contamination
