## Rutgers Language Exchange Programs

**Conversation Cafes**
- Casual language practice setting
- Available for Spanish and other languages
- Paired with native speakers
- Held on campus

**Language Exchange Program**
- Matches students fluent in one language with learners
- Regular meetings for practice and cultural exchange

**Global Ambassadors Program**
- Pairs international students with American students
- Practice languages and share cultures

**Benefits:** Improve language proficiency and cultural knowledge before studying abroad

**Relevant for:** Beginners wanting to practice Spanish before study abroad
