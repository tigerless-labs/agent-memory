### Jason and Medea Story
- Medea tricks Pelias's daughters into slicing him up
- Jason wants to be with Medea, but she kills their children
- Jason is characterized as the villain

### Hercules (Heracles/Alcides)
- Best of the demigods, known for great strength
- Son of Zeus and Alcmene
- Given Twelve Labors by King Eurystheus as punishment for killing his wife Megara and children in madness
- Labors designed by Hera to kill, humiliate, and exhaust him
- Completed all 12 labors and earned forgiveness
- Later story: protected Deianeira from centaur Nessus; Nessus gave her a love potion (actually acid) that poisoned Hercules

### Key Characters
- Theseus (cousin): stopped Hercules from killing himself
- Thespian: music teacher killed by Hercules when learning
- Admetus and Alcestis: king and queen Hercules helped
- Hera: goddess jealous of Alcmene, orchestrated Hercules' suffering
- Eurystheus: king who assigned the Twelve Labors
