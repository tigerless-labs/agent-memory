## Festival Strategy

**Event**: Downtown Art Festival (upcoming, date TBD)

**Planned Approach**:
- Mix of both smaller and larger pieces to appeal to different buyers
- Target audience: first-time buyers and serious collectors
- Plan to bring prints alongside original pieces

## Print Pricing

- **Strategy**: Price prints at 20-30% of the original piece price
- **Rationale**: Make art more accessible to wider audience, encourage multiple purchases, build brand awareness, attract new collectors
- **Quality focus**: High-quality prints with vibrant colors to maintain value perception
- **Branding**: Clearly brand prints with name, logo, contact info

## Early Bird Discount

- **Considering**: Fixed amount off (e.g., $10 off purchases before 12 pm) vs percentage off
- **Goal**: Create urgency, drive early-day foot traffic, increase sales volume

## Brand Goals
- Increase brand visibility and awareness through affordable entry points
- Build loyal following among collectors
- Establish premium brand presence while being accessible
