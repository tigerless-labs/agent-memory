## San Antonio Solo Trip - June 2023

### Trip Details
Solo traveler planning June 2023 visit to San Antonio. Previous positive experience attending wedding in Austin, TX; stayed at Omni Barton Creek Resort. Prefers Omni Hotels.

### Recommended Hotels
All on/near River Walk, walkable to attractions:

**Omni La Mansión del Rio** (4-star, River Walk)
- Spanish colonial-style architecture
- Outdoor pool, fitness center
- Dining: Tex-Mex restaurant, poolside bar
- Walking distance to Alamo, Rivercenter Mall, Tower of the Americas

**Hotel Contessa** (4-star, River Walk)
- Stylish modern rooms
- Rooftop pool, fitness center
- Contemporary American cuisine

**Hotel Valencia Riverwalk** (4-star, downtown)
- Chic sophisticated atmosphere
- Rooftop pool, fitness center
- Gourmet restaurant

**Hotel Emma** (4-star boutique)
- Restored 19th-century brewhouse
- Beautiful courtyard, outdoor pool
- Farm-to-table restaurant, Sternewirth bar

### The Alamo
- Address: 300 Alamo Plaza, San Antonio, TX 78205
- Hours: 9am-5:30pm daily, extended March-October
- Admission: Free (guided tours available for fee)
- Historical: 1836 battle for Texas independence, Davy Crockett, Jim Bowie, General Santa Anna
- Includes: Alamo Church (no photography inside), Long Barrack Museum, Alamo Gardens
- Documentary: "The Battle of the Alamo" (17 minutes)
- Website: www.thealamo.org
- Tips: visit early morning/late afternoon, sunscreen/hat/water, comfortable shoes

### River Walk (Paseo del Río)
- 15-mile pedestrian path, Pearl District to Missions Trail
- Hours: 24/7, free shuttle service
- Activities: strolling, dining, shopping, boat tours
- Notable restaurants: La Gloria, Boudro's, The Esquire Tavern
- Shopping: River Walk Mall, boutiques, galleries
- Events: Ford Holiday River Parade (late November), Fiesta San Antonio (April), San Antonio Jazz & Blues Festival (September)
- Website: www.visitsanantonio.com

### Pearl District
Former brewery complex at north end of River Walk, shopping/dining/entertainment hub.

**Pearl Brewery** - shops, restaurants

**Pearl Farmers Market** - Saturdays 9am-1pm
- Vendors: fresh produce, baked goods, prepared foods, handmade crafts, specialty foods, flowers/plants, local beer & wine
- Pet-friendly
- Tips: arrive early, bring reusable bags, cash recommended

**Restaurants & Venues**
- Hotel Emma (historic, courtyard, Sternewirth bar)
- La Gloria (Mexican, craft cocktails, outdoor seating)
- The Boiler House (gastropub, beer selection)
- Jasmine Thai (authentic Thai, outdoor seating)
- Elise (women's clothing, accessories, home decor)
- The Twig Book Shop (independent bookstore, book clubs)

Parking: Pearl garage or street. Tip: check for weekend brunch crowds, live music events.

### San Antonio Missions National Historical Park
Largest Spanish colonial architecture collection in US, four missions along San Antonio River.

- Mission Concepción (founded 1716, frescoes, ornate)
- Mission San José (1720, "Queen of the Missions", stone carvings)
- Mission San Juan (1716, tranquil, picturesque)
- Mission Espada (1731, southernmost, chapel)

Hours: 9am-5pm daily (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's)
Admission: Free
Tours: ranger-led available, self-guided with maps
Activity: scenic Mission Trail drive

Tips: comfortable shoes, sunscreen/water, respect historical sites
