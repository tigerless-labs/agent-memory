## Montreal Food Culture

### Must-Try Dishes
1. **Smoked meat sandwich** — thinly sliced beef brisket cured in spices, smoked and steamed, served on rye bread with mustard and pickle
2. **Poutine** — French fries with gravy and cheese curds; variations include bacon or pulled pork
3. **Montreal-style bagels** — smaller and sweeter than New York bagels, boiled in honey-sweetened water, baked in wood-fired oven, served with cream cheese or smoked salmon
4. **Tourtière** — meat pie with ground pork, potatoes, and spices; traditionally served during holidays
5. **Maple syrup treats** — maple taffy, maple sugar pie, maple-glazed bacon (Quebec is major maple syrup producer)

### Food Markets
- Atwater Market
- Jean-Talon Market

### Restaurant Scene
Abundant restaurants serving traditional French cuisine and ethnic cuisines (Greek, Lebanese, Vietnamese)
