## Montreal Cultural Profile

**Geographic & Linguistic Significance**
- Second-largest French-speaking city in the world after Paris
- Located at confluence of Saint Lawrence and Ottawa rivers
- Major transportation hub connecting eastern Canada and US

**Cultural Attractions**
- Montreal Museum of Fine Arts
- Canadian Centre for Architecture
- Montreal Museum of Contemporary Art

**Major Festivals**
- Montreal International Jazz Festival
- Just For Laughs Festival
- Montreal International Film Festival

**Architecture**
- Historic Old Montreal district (oldest area of city with beautiful buildings and cobblestone streets)

**Connection to Food Culture**
Montreal's cultural diversity directly supports its vibrant food scene with access to diverse cuisines and local specialties.
