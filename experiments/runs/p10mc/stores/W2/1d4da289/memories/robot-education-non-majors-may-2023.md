Robot Education Model for Non-Majors with GCED and SDGs

Date: May 20, 2023
Context: Study to enhance engineering education for non-majors using robot programming.

Problem
Non-majors struggle to produce beyond motionless forms due to lack of mechanism and coding understanding.

Model Components
- Tool: Convergence D-SteamRobot (DSR) blends humanities and engineering
- Pathway: books for empathy, understand real-world problems, select SDGs topic, learn simple mechanisms, create prototypes
- Environment: Constructivist learning with empathy and exploration focus

Learning Outcomes
Students develop:
- Computational thinking
- Critical thinking
- Problem-solving skills
- Understanding of technology role in sustainability
- Personal agency in solving SDG problems
- Enhanced creativity through hands-on learning and peer collaboration

Implementation
Proceeds through ideating, prototyping, sharing phases with sustainability and SDGs emphasis throughout.
