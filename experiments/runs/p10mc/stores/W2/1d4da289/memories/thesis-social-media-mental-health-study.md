## Study Overview
- **Topic**: How social media use affects mental health outcomes in young adults
- **Research questions**:
  - How does social media use affect mental health outcomes in young adults?
  - What are the specific social media behaviors associated with negative mental health outcomes?

## Thesis Progress
- Literature review completed: February 10
- Currently working on: Methodology chapter
- Next: Data collection plan development

## Methodology
- **Study design**: Mixed-methods
- **Target population**: Young adults aged 18-25
- **Planned sample size**: 500 participants
- **Sampling strategy**: Online convenience sampling through social media platforms

## Data Collection Methods
- **Quantitative**: Online surveys with validated measures of social media use and mental health outcomes
  - Platform: Secure online survey platform
- **Qualitative**: Semi-structured interviews on social media behaviors and mental health experiences
  - Platform: Video conferencing software

## Data Analysis
- **Qualitative analysis method**: Thematic analysis
  - Steps: familiarization, initial coding, coding framework development, coding and theme identification, theme refinement and naming, theme mapping and diagramming, theme interpretation and reporting
  - Expected themes: social media habits, mental health experiences, social comparison and envy, social support and connection, self-presentation and identity, fear of missing out (FOMO), cyberbullying and online harassment, self-regulation and boundaries, parental or peer influence, coping mechanisms and strategies

## Software Evaluation
- **NVivo**: 14-day free trial; user-friendly interface, robust coding and analysis tools, excellent data visualization
- **Atlas.ti**: 30-day free trial; powerful for large datasets, advanced coding, memoing, and visualization features

## Data Quality and Management
- Data will be cleaned and coded using statistical software
- Data will be stored on a secure server with restricted access
