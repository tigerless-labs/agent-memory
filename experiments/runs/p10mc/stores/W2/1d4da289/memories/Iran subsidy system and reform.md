## Iran's Subsidy System

Iran has long used subsidies as a tool to control prices and make essential goods and services affordable:
- **Coverage**: Food, energy, transportation, housing
- **Criticisms**: System criticized as inefficient; led to underinvestment in subsidized industries

## 2010 Subsidy Reform Plan

The government implemented a plan to phase out subsidies:
- **Goal**: Reduce budget deficit and redirect funds to targeted social welfare programs
- **Replacement**: Direct cash transfers to households instead of subsidies

## Challenges to Reform

- High inflation
- Decline in value of national currency (Iranian Rial)
- Public protests as cost of living rose
- US economic sanctions limiting access to international markets
- Shortage of goods and increased prices making subsidy reform more difficult
