## Career Exploration Plan

**Decision point (May 21, 2023):** Considering pursuing a creative career while working part-time in technology simultaneously.

**Concerns addressed:**
- Time management challenges
- Career trajectory and advancement
- Financial planning across two income streams
- How to explain diverse experience to employers

**Tools to explore:**

**Time Management:**
- Trello — task organization and prioritization
- Asana — project and deadline management

**Financial Management:**
- Mint — spending tracking and budgeting
- QuickBooks — streamlined financial management

**Networking:**
- LinkedIn — connect with professionals in both industries
- Meetup — attend events in both communities

**Learning:**
- Skillshare — skill development courses
- Udemy — expert-taught courses

**Approach:** Explore both fields to see where it leads; maintain balance and plan for recharge time.
