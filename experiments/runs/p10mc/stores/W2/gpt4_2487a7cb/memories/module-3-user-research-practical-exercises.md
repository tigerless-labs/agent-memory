## Module 3: User Research — Practical Exercises

### 1. Conduct User Interviews
- Identify a target audience for a digital product
- Develop a set of questions to ask during the interview
- Conduct the interview and take notes on user responses
- Analyze responses to identify pain points and areas for improvement
- Use insights to inform digital product design

### 2. Create User Personas
- Research the target audience for a digital product
- Identify common characteristics and behaviors
- Create fictional user personas representing different audience segments
- Use personas to inform design, ensuring it meets needs of each segment

### 3. Conduct Usability Testing
- Create a prototype of a digital product
- Recruit users to test the prototype
- Develop a set of tasks for users to complete
- Observe users and take notes on usability issues and improvements
- Use insights to make design iterations

### 4. Develop User Journey Maps
- Map out user journey from discovery to purchase or sign-up
- Identify pain points and areas for improvement
- Develop a plan to address pain points
- Use insights to inform digital product design

### 5. Conduct Competitive Analysis
- Research competitors in the digital product's market
- Analyze their strengths and weaknesses
- Identify differentiation opportunities
- Use insights to inform digital product design

**Goal**: Learner gains hands-on experience with user research methods and develops strong foundation to create digital products meeting target audience needs and preferences.
