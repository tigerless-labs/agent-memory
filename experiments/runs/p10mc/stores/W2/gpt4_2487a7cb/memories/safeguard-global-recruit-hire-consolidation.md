## Overview
Safeguard Global restructured self-service HR applications by combining separate Recruit and Hire sections into one unified section.

## Original Structure
- **Recruit**: sourcing and identifying candidates, posting job openings, tracking applications, scheduling interviews, skills assessments
- **Hire**: onboarding and managing new employees, offer letters, employee paperwork, training modules, orientation checklists

## New Unified Structure
1. **Attract and Hire**: complete hiring solution from candidate sourcing through onboarding
2. **Manage**: employee data, HR processes, time/attendance, benefits, performance management
3. **Pay**: global payroll processing, tax withholding, benefits management
4. **Analyse**: HR/payroll analytics, reporting, trend identification, predictive analytics

Whether you're looking to streamline your hiring process or enhance your onboarding procedures, we've got you covered."
