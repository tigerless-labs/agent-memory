## Homebrewing Interest

User is interested in homebrewing beer with focus on temperature control and yeast management.

## Temperature Ranges

**Lagers:**
- Fermentation: 45-55°F (7-13°C) — produces crisp, clean, smooth beer
- Conditioning: 32-40°F (0-4°C) — longer process, reduces harsh flavors

**Ales:**
- Fermentation: 60-75°F (15-24°C) — produces fruity, spicy, complex flavors
- Conditioning: 55-65°F (13-18°C) — allows beer to clear, reduce chill haze

## Yeast Selection Tips

- Different styles need different strains: American ale, British ale, Belgian, German Lager
- Yeast health essential: use healthy strains, avoid contamination, ensure proper oxygenation
- Create a yeast starter before pitching to grow active cells
- Yeast performance highly temperature-dependent — stay within strain-specific range to avoid under/over-fermentation
- Fermenting outside optimal range: alters taste/aroma, creates off-flavors
- Check yeast packaging date; old yeast less active, prolongs fermentation, creates off-flavors
- Temperature control during fermentation ensures efficient yeast performance and desired flavor profile
