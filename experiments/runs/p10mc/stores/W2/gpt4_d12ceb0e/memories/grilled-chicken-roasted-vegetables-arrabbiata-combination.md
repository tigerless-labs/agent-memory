**Grilled Chicken Technique:**
- Marinate chicken breasts in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, thyme, rosemary for minimum 30 minutes
- Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400°F/200°C)
- Grill 5-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C)
- Let rest for few minutes before slicing thinly against the grain

**Roasted Vegetables:**
- Vegetables: zucchini and bell peppers (primary choices mentioned)
- Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, dried oregano
- Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 20-25 minutes until tender and lightly caramelized
- Optional: brush with glaze of olive oil, balsamic vinegar, honey during last 10 minutes

**Assembly with Arrabbiata:**
- Slice grilled chicken and combine with roasted vegetables
- Toss pasta with arrabbiata sauce, chicken, and vegetables
- Top with parmesan cheese and fresh basil
