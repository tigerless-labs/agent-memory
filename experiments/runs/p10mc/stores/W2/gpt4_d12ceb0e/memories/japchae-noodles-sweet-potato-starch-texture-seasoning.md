User exploring Japchae noodles (Korean sweet potato starch noodles) recipe development.

**Texture Technique:**
- Using 100% sweet potato starch noodles (dangmyeon)
- Soaking noodles in cold water for minimum 30 minutes to rehydrate
- Cooking in boiling water for 5-7 minutes
- Rinsing in cold water to stop cooking
- Stir-frying with oil (1-2 tablespoons) to develop chewy texture and prevent sticking

**Seasoning Profile:**
- Balanced sweet and savory flavors (adjust sugar and soy sauce to taste)
- Soy sauce base
- Sugar for sweetness
- Sesame oil used sparingly (can be overpowering)
- Optional gochujang for added depth

**Aromatics and Texture:**
- Sautee onions, garlic, ginger before adding noodles
- Mix in vegetables like spinach, carrots, bell peppers
- Toasted sesame seeds or chopped nuts for added crunch
- Garnish with green onions

User approaching recipe with attention to both texture and flavor balance.
