## Springfield, Illinois — Comfort Food Restaurants

Recommended for classic American comfort food with cozy atmosphere:

1. **Augie's Front Burner**
   - Family-owned
   - Serves classic American comfort food
   - Known for meatloaf and mashed potatoes
   - Warm, inviting, nostalgic atmosphere

2. **Obed & Isaac's Microbrewery and Eatery**
   - Located in a historic church building
   - Rustic, cozy atmosphere
   - Menu features: meatloaf sandwich, mashed potato options
   - Also has craft beer selection

3. **The Feed Store**
   - Casual, family-friendly
   - Serves classic American comfort food: meatloaf, mashed potatoes, burgers, sandwiches
   - Rustic, down-home, relaxed atmosphere

4. **Luti's Tavola**
   - Italian-American restaurant
   - Notable dish: meatloaf with mashed potatoes and marinara sauce
   - Cozy, intimate, family-owned atmosphere

**Tip:** Call ahead to confirm Wednesday hours and availability before visiting.
