User is perfecting pasta sauce recipes using fresh crushed tomatoes as the foundation ingredient. Key findings and techniques:

**Fresh Crushed Tomatoes:**
- Describes as "game-changer" for bright, vibrant flavor
- Primary ingredient in all pasta sauce experiments

**Flavor Development Techniques:**
- Caramelize onions and garlic for deep, rich flavor
- Add acidity (lemon juice, vinegar, red wine) to balance sweetness
- Use San Marzano tomatoes when possible for better quality
- Balance sweet, acidic, and savory elements

**Arrabbiata Sauce Experiments:**
- San Marzano tomatoes with guanciale or pancetta
- Red pepper flakes for spicy kick
- Combining with grilled chicken and roasted vegetables
- Roasted vegetables include zucchini and bell peppers
- Finish with parmesan cheese and fresh basil

User takes methodical approach to ingredient ratios and cooking technique.
