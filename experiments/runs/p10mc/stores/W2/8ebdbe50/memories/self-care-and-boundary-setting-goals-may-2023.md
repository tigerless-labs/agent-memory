## Current Situation

User is experiencing burnout and has identified self-care and boundary-setting as priorities. They feel overwhelmed with commitments and responsibilities.

## Self-Care Focus Areas

**Morning Routine:**
- Start small with 5-10 minute sessions
- Gradually incorporate activities like journaling, reading, yoga
- Focus on enjoyable activities like listening to music or drinking tea

**Self-Care Essentials:**
- Hydration (water throughout the day)
- 7-8 hours of sleep per night
- Nourishing whole foods
- Regular physical activity (walking, dancing, yoga)

**Mindfulness and Relaxation:**
- Meditation apps (Headspace, Calm)
- Deep breathing exercises
- Progressive muscle relaxation
- Grounding techniques (engaging five senses)

**Creative Expression:**
- Journaling for thoughts and gratitudes
- Drawing, painting, crafting
- Music or sound therapy
- Reading and writing

## Boundary-Setting Goals

User wants to learn to:
- Say no to commitments that drain energy
- Set clear boundaries with others
- Take breaks throughout the day
- Prioritize alone time for self-reflection
- Recognize personal capacity and limits

**Specific strategies discussed:**
- Practice saying no starting with low-stakes requests
- Use gentle but firm tone without apology
- Avoid over-explaining or justifying
- Stay firm when facing pushback
- Reframe boundary-setting as self-love, not selfishness

## Mindset Reminder

"You can't pour from an empty cup" — prioritizing own needs first enables better functioning overall.
