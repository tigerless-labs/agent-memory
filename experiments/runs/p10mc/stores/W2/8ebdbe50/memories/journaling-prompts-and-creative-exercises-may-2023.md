## Reflective Prompts for Daily Practice

1. **Gratitude practice** — Three things you're thankful for; reflect on their importance
2. **Emotional check-in** — Identify current feelings and their sources; explore solutions
3. **Daily accomplishments** — Celebrate successes, no matter how small
4. **Lessons learned** — Reflect on challenges and mistakes; how to apply lessons forward
5. **Self-care reflection** — How you cared for yourself; impact of practices

## Creative Exercises

1. **Stream-of-consciousness writing** — 10-15 minute timed freewriting without stopping; accesses subconscious
2. **Free writing with prompts** — Use starter phrases like "I wish..." or "I'm afraid of..."
3. **Drawing or doodling** — Incorporate art; represent current mood or emotions
4. **Dialogue with inner self** — Write conversation between present, past, or future self
5. **Sensory exploration** — Describe memory/experience linked to one sense; engage all five senses

## Exploratory Questions

- What am I looking forward to?
- What's been weighing on my mind?
- Are my actions aligned with my goals and values?
- How have I grown or changed this past year?
- What self-care practices should I prioritize next month?

## Themed Journaling Approaches

1. **Moon journaling** — Track lunar cycle and its effects on emotions/energy
2. **Seasonal reflections** — How current season influences mood, habits, goals
3. **Mindfulness journaling** — Focus on present moment; describe thoughts/feelings/surroundings without judgment

## Additional Creative Options

- Collage or scrapbooking
- Photography
- Music or sound art
- Poetry or short stories
- Dance or movement

## Key Principles

- No need for perfection; journal is private space
- Experiment with different formats
- Start small (5-10 minutes daily)
- Make it a consistent habit and ritual
- Don't edit or judge while writing
