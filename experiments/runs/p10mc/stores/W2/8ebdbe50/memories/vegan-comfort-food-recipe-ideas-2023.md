## Vegan Comfort Food Recipe Ideas

**10 Vegan Comfort Food Options:**
1. Vegan Mac and Cheese with non-dairy milks and vegan cheeses (cashew or soy-based)
2. Lentil Shepherd's Pie with lentils, mashed potatoes, vegan gravy
3. Vegan Chili with beans, vegetables, spices; serve with bread or over rice
4. Cauliflower Gnocchi with Tomato Sauce using cauliflower instead of potatoes
5. Vegan Chick'n Parmesan Sandwich with breaded tofu/tempeh, marinara, vegan mozzarella
6. Creamy Vegan Broccoli Soup with broccoli, potatoes, non-dairy milk
7. Vegan Meatball Subs with plant-based meatballs, marinara, vegan mozzarella
8. Roasted Vegetable Quinoa Bowl with sweet potatoes, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower
9. Vegan Lasagna with pasta, marinara sauce, dairy-free ricotta cheese
10. Stuffed Bell Peppers filled with rice, black beans, vegetables

**Popular Vegan Comfort Food Ingredients:**
* Non-dairy milks: almond, soy, oat, etc.
* Vegan cheeses: Daiya, Follow Your Heart, Kite Hill, etc.
* Protein sources: tofu, tempeh, seitan
* Fiber and protein: lentils, chickpeas, black beans
* Nutritional yeast for cheesy, nutty flavor
* Vegan Worcestershire sauce for depth of flavor
