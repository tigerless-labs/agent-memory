## 1893 Morgan Dollar Documentation

User recently purchased a rare 1893 Morgan dollar coin and is including it in collectibles documentation and insurance planning.

**Condition-based value ranges:**
- Good (G4): $200-$500
- Fine (F12): $500-$1,500
- Very Fine (VF20): $1,500-$3,500
- Extremely Fine (EF40): $3,500-$6,000
- About Uncirculated (AU50): $6,000-$10,000
- Mint State (MS60): $10,000-$20,000+

**Preservation guidelines:**
- Handle with clean, dry hands or gloves only
- Store in cool, dry place, away from sunlight and moisture
- Use high-quality album or folder designed for coins
- Avoid cleaning (can reduce value significantly)
- Avoid harsh chemicals (bleach, acid)
- Consider professional certification from PCGS or NGC
- Keep insured as part of overall collection

**Value factors:**
- Lower mintage makes it rare and valuable
- High demand from collectors
- Certification adds premium value
