## Bread Storage Tips for Freshness

**Room Temperature (2-3 days):**
* Store in paper bag or breathable container (canvas or cotton bag)
* Allows bread to "breathe" and maintain crustiness
* Let bread cool completely before storing to prevent moisture buildup

**Freezing (up to 3 months):**
* Wrap tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil
* Place in freezer-safe bag
* Thaw at room temperature when ready to eat, or reheat in oven

**What to Avoid:**
* Airtight containers trap moisture and make bread soggy

**Refrigeration (5-7 days):**
* Store in paper bag or breathable container
* Note: refrigeration can cause slight drying

**Slicing:**
* Slice just before serving to prevent drying out
* For sliced bread, store in paper bag or breathable container at room temperature or refrigerator

**Reviving Stale Bread:**
* Sprinkle water on bread
* Heat in oven at 350°F for 5-10 minutes
* Restores some crustiness and freshness
