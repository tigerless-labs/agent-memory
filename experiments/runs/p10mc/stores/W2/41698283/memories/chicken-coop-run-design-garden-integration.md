## Chicken Coop & Run Design + Garden Integration

**Coop Design:**
- Predator-proofing: hardware cloth, chicken wire, metal mesh
- Ventilation: windows, vents, screens to remove ammonia/moisture
- Insulation: regulate temperature for comfort
- Dry and clean: straw or pine shavings for bedding/waste absorption
- Easy cleaning: removable walls/floors
- Nesting boxes: 1 per 3-4 hens with perch and sloping roof
- Roosting bars: for night perching away from predators/drafts

**Run Design:**
- Sturdy fencing: chicken wire, hardware cloth, or electric netting
- Covered run portion: provides shade and weather protection
- Space: minimum 2-4 square feet per bird
- Ground cover: gravel, wood chips, or grass (prevents mud)
- Shade: trees, shrubs, or shade cloth
- Water/feed access: place in run or coop
- Secure gates: lockable to prevent predators

**Additional Coop/Run Tips:**
- Location: well-ventilated, protected from wind/sun
- Drainage: slight slope to prevent water accumulation
- Consider electrifying fence for predator deterrent
- Regular maintenance: inspect for damage
- Predator management: raccoons, foxes, hawks are threats

**Garden Integration Benefits:**
- Pest control: slugs, snails, insects naturally reduced
- Fertilizer: chicken manure valuable for plants
- Soil health: scratching and aerating soil
- Weed control: eating/scratching up weeds (reduces herbicides)
- Companion planting: certain plants provide shade, shelter, food
- Biodiversity: attracts beneficial insects, dynamic ecosystem
- Aesthetics: charming, rustic addition to garden

**Garden Integration Considerations:**
- Space: must accommodate coop/run plus garden
- Fencing: protect chickens from predators and prevent escape
- Predator protection: covered runs or electric fencing
- Noise/disturbance: consider neighbors
- Health/safety: clean water, food, shelter; prevent disease transmission
- Regulations: check local zoning, permits, health requirements
- Maintenance: coop cleaning, run maintenance, regular care

**Design Considerations:**
- Design coop to blend with garden using complementary materials
- Plan run size/layout for adequate exercise and foraging space
- Select plants compatible with chickens (provide shade, shelter, food)
- Manage water effectively to prevent flooding/erosion
