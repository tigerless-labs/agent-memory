## Birds in Synchronized Flight vs. Quantum Entanglement

Birds in synchronized flight are NOT an example of quantum entanglement, despite superficial similarities.

### Synchronized Bird Flight
- Birds coordinate movements while flying in close proximity
- Behavior results from local interactions between individual birds
- Mechanisms include following nearby birds, responding to wind/light changes
- This is classical behavior, not quantum

### Quantum Entanglement
- Occurs at quantum scale between particles
- Results from fundamental laws of quantum mechanics
- Cannot be explained by classical physics
- Involves instantaneous correlation across arbitrary distances

The key difference: bird flocking is emergent behavior from local classical interactions, while entanglement is a fundamental quantum phenomenon with no classical explanation.
