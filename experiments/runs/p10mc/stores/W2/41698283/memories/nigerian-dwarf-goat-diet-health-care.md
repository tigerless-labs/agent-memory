## Nigerian Dwarf Goat Care

**Diet and Nutrition:**
- High-quality hay: 80-90% of daily intake (Timothy hay, alfalfa, or mix)
- Grain: 1-2 cups per day, low protein (12-14%), high fiber (18-20%)
- Fresh water at all times
- Minerals: free-choice block formulated for goats
- Vitamins A, D, E needed; can add small amount of vegetable oil
- Treats limited to 5-10% of diet (apples, carrots, sweet potatoes, sunflower seeds)
- Avoid: chocolate, avocados, copperleaf (copper toxicity risk)
- Feeding schedule: twice daily, divided rations, free-choice hay
- Monitor weight—prone to obesity

**Common Health Issues & Prevention:**
- **Parasites (internal/external)**: Deworming every 4-6 weeks; rotational grazing; clean/dry environment
- **Respiratory issues**: Good ventilation, clean/dry environment, CDT vaccine
- **Urinary tract/calculi**: Clean water access; low calcium/phosphorus diet; apple cider vinegar in water
- **Hoof problems**: Trim every 4-6 weeks; clean/dry environment
- **Nutritional deficiencies**: Balanced diet with mineral/vitamin supplements
- **Copper toxicity/deficiency**: Monitor copper levels carefully; consult experienced breeder

**General Care:**
- Regular health monitoring and veterinary relationship
- Rotate pastures if possible
- Provide browse (branches, leaves) to mimic natural diet
- Friendly, intelligent animals—good for small-farm pets
