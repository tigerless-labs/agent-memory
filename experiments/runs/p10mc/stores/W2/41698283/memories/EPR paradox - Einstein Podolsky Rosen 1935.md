## EPR Paradox

The EPR paradox (Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paradox) is a thought experiment proposed in 1935 as a challenge to quantum mechanics principles, which were still relatively new and controversial at the time.

The paradox centers on entangled particles:
- When two entangled particles are separated by a large distance
- Measuring one particle's spin immediately determines the other's spin
- Einstein, Podolsky, and Rosen argued this violates the principle of locality (information cannot travel faster than light)
- They proposed that some hidden variable must explain the apparent non-locality

Historical significance:
- Played a major role in development of quantum mechanics
- Subject of extensive debate and study in subsequent decades
- Helped shape understanding of reality at the fundamental level
- Named "spooky action at a distance" by Einstein himself
