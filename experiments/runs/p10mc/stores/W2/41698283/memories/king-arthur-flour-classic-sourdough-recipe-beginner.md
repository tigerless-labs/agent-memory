## King Arthur Flour's Classic Sourdough Recipe

Recommended as a great beginner sourdough recipe — simple, well-written, flexible schedule.

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup sourdough starter (active and bubbly)
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp water

**Instructions:**
1. Mix the starter, flour, and salt in a bowl until just combined
2. Add the water and mix until the dough comes together in a shaggy mass
3. Knead the dough for 10 minutes, then place it in a lightly oiled bowl to rest
4. Ferment the dough for 4–5 hours, or until nearly doubled in size
5. Shape the dough into a round or oblong loaf, place on baking sheet or proofing basket
6. Proof the dough for 2–3 hours, or until nearly doubled in size again
7. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) with Dutch oven or steamy oven
8. Score the bread, then bake for 25–30 minutes until golden brown

**Why it's good for beginners:**
- Simple ingredients (flour, water, salt, starter only — no additives)
- Clear, well-written instructions
- Flexible schedule — can fit proofing/baking around daily routine
- Excellent for learning sourdough basics before experimenting

**User's planned experiment:**
- Considering using bread flour instead of all-purpose
- Bread flour will produce chewier, more robust crumb with higher protein (12–14%)
- Better gluten development, more open/airy crumb, more tender crust
- Slightly sweeter flavor
- May need more water as bread flour absorbs more
- Monitor dough progress and adjust as needed
- Be gentle when shaping to avoid excess tension
