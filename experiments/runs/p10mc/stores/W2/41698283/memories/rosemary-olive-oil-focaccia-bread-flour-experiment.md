## Rosemary and Olive Oil Focaccia Recipe & Experiment

**Recipe (as discussed):**

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups warm water
- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (user planning to use bread flour instead)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 sprigs of fresh rosemary, chopped (2–3 tablespoons of leaves)
- Coarse sea salt, for topping (optional)

Instructions:
1. Combine warm water and yeast in a bowl, let sit 5–10 minutes until frothy
2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil; mix until shaggy dough forms
3. Knead 10–12 minutes until smooth and elastic
4. Place in oiled bowl, cover, rise 1 hour (or doubled in size)
5. Preheat oven 425°F (220°C)
6. Punch down, shape into rectangle on parchment-lined baking sheet
7. Dimple with fingers, sprinkle rosemary and coarse sea salt
8. Drizzle with olive oil
9. Bake 20–25 minutes until golden brown

**Using Bread Flour:**
- Higher protein (12–14%) vs all-purpose (10–12%)
- Will produce chewier crumb with larger holes
- Enhances gluten development, more tender yet crispy crust
- Slightly sweeter, nuttier flavor that complements rosemary and olive oil
- May need to increase water slightly as bread flour absorbs more
- Monitor dough progress — may develop more quickly
- Handle gently when shaping to avoid too much tension

**Fresh Rosemary Tips:**
- Choose vibrant green, fragrant sprigs; avoid wilted or brownish
- Store in plastic bag/wrap in fridge (lasts ~1 week)
- Chop just before using to release oils
- Use 2–3 sprigs (2–3 tbsp chopped leaves) for this recipe
- Start conservative, can adjust up to taste — rosemary is potent
- Distribute evenly throughout dough during initial mixing
- Can combine with other herbs (thyme, oregano, parsley) for balance

**Other flour options mentioned:**
- Caputo "00" flour — ideal for focaccia (tender crumb, crispy crust)
- Whole wheat flour — nuttier flavor, coarser texture, needs water/proofing adjustments
- Ancient grains (Kamut, Spelt) — distinct flavors but require recipe adjustments

**High-quality olive oil** recommended for best flavor.
