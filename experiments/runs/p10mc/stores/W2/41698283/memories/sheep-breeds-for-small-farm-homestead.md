## Sheep Breeds for Small Farms & Homesteads

**Selection Factors:** Climate, available land, purpose (meat/milk/fiber), space/fencing requirements, local breeder availability, registration options

**Dorset**
- Origin: England | Size: 100-150 lbs | Coat: White with brown/black face
- Production: Excellent meat, fair milk, fair fiber
- Temperament: Friendly, easy to handle
- Climate: Hardy, adaptable to various climates

**Shetland**
- Origin: Shetland Islands, Scotland | Size: 70-100 lbs | Coat: Various colors
- Production: Good lean meat, fair milk, excellent fine wool
- Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, curious
- Climate: Hardy, adapted to cool, wet climates

**Rambouillet**
- Origin: France | Size: 100-150 lbs | Coat: White with pinkish face
- Production: Excellent meat, good milk, excellent fine wool
- Temperament: Docile, easy to handle
- Climate: Adaptable to various climates

**Jacob**
- Origin: Middle East | Size: 70-100 lbs | Coat: White with black/brown markings
- Production: Good lean meat, fair milk, good medium wool
- Temperament: Friendly, curious, energetic
- Climate: Hardy, adapted to hot and dry climates

**Ouessant**
- Origin: France | Size: 50-70 lbs | Coat: Black or brown
- Production: Good lean meat, fair milk, excellent fine wool
- Temperament: Friendly, gentle, curious
- Climate: Hardy, adapted to cool, wet climates

**Babydoll**
- Origin: United States | Size: 50-70 lbs | Coat: White with black/brown face
- Production: Good lean meat, fair milk, good medium wool
- Temperament: Friendly, gentle, curious
- Climate: Adaptable to various climates

**Karacul**
- Origin: Central Asia | Size: 100-150 lbs | Coat: Various colors
- Production: Excellent meat, good milk, excellent fine wool
- Temperament: Friendly, intelligent, curious
- Climate: Hardy, adapted to hot and dry climates

**Recommendations:** Visit breeders to experience breeds; research local demand; check American Sheep Industry Association for registration; start small and scale up with experience
