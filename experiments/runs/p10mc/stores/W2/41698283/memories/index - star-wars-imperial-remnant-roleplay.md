## Characters

**Lizzie Ó Muirgheasa** — Nekomimi (cat-like being), Grand Admiral and current leader of the Imperial Remnant, meditation chamber in privacy with holographic sculpture art, uniform with Grand Admiral badge

**Alyssa** — Dragonkin, Captain rank, taller than Lizzie, in romantic relationship with Lizzie (girlfriend)

**Mila** — Pantherkin, combat specialist, bodyguard, armed with combat gear

## Settings

- **Bastion** — industrial planet, temporary capital of the Imperial Remnant
- **Coruscant** — former Imperial capital, currently controlled by New Republic, location of formerly-Imperial Palace where Mon Mothma works
- **Serenno** — non-Sith planet, headquarters of Warlord Zsinj's splinter empire

## Factions & Politics

- **Imperial Remnant** — led by Lizzie Ó Muirgheasa as Grand Admiral
- **New Republic** — controls Coruscant, led by Chancellor Mon Mothma
- **Warlord Zsinj's Splinter Empire** — broke away from Empire, headquartered on Serenno; controls Sith Worlds at core of territory (long-standing possession since splinter began, not recent acquisition); poses threat to both Empire and Republic

## Key Events

- Lizzie asked Alyssa to be her girlfriend in the meditation chamber
- Lizzie presented plan to Council of Moffs: propose temporary ceasefire with New Republic to jointly partition Zsinj's empire and reclaim Sith Worlds for the Empire
- Council of Moffs approved the plan
- Lizzie contacted Chancellor Mon Mothma via holoprojector with ceasefire/partition proposal
- Mon Mothma considering proposal, will discuss with her council
