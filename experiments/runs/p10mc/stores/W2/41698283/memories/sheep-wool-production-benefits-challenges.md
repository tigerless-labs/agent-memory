## Sheep Wool Production: Benefits & Challenges

**Benefits:**
- Diversified income stream from wool and wool products
- Sustainable, renewable, biodegradable resource (annual harvest)
- Low overhead compared to other livestock
- Multi-purpose animals: meat, milk, AND wool simultaneously
- Soil conservation: help maintain pasture health, control weeds
- Value-added products: yarn, hats, scarves, blankets capture larger profit margin
- Unique marketing opportunities: sustainable, locally sourced, natural products appeal

**Challenges:**
- Initial investment: sheep, fencing, equipment
- Land and pasture management requirements
- Shearing and processing: time-consuming, labor-intensive, requires specialized equipment/skills
- Market fluctuations: wool prices depend on global trends; need diversified strategy
- Predator management: coyotes, wolves, foxes require effective protection
- Health and parasite management: diseases, parasites, regular veterinary care needed
- Time commitment: daily care, shearing, processing, marketing

**Is It Worth Considering?**

YES if you:
- Have adequate pasture/land for small flock
- Willing to invest time in shearing, processing, marketing
- Can manage predators, parasites, health issues
- Want diversified income streams and value-added products
- Value sustainable, renewable resources

**Getting Started:**
1. Research local market demand and prices for wool/wool products
2. Consult with experienced sheep farmers in your area
3. Develop business plan and budget
4. Start with small flock, scale gradually with experience
