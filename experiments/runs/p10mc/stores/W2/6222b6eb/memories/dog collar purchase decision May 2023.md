## Dog Collar Purchase Decision (May 2023)

### Selected: Yellow Dog Design Nylon Collar
- **Reason:** Known for high quality, wide range of styles and colors
- **Material:** Nylon
- **Advantages:** Durable, easy to clean, perfect for active lifestyle
- **Accessory:** Engraved name tag with Max's name and owner's contact information

### Collar Brands Researched
1. **Yellow Dog Design** - stylish, comfortable, durable; materials include nylon, leather, cotton
2. **Puppia** - soft, comfortable, adjustable; materials include cotton, polyester, nylon
3. **Coastal Pet Products** - high-quality; materials include nylon, leather, rubber; includes martingale and breakaway options
4. **Ruffwear** - for outdoor enthusiasts and active dogs; materials include nylon, polyester, rubber

### Collar Types Considered
- **Nylon Collars:** Durable, easy to clean, variety of colors and patterns
- **Leather Collars:** Stylish, durable, develops patina over time
- **Breakaway Collars:** Release if caught on something, reduces injury risk
- **Martingale Collars:** Tighten when dog pulls, prevent slipping out

### Name Tag Options
- **Engraved Name Tags:** Durable, easy to read, customizable with name and contact info
- **ID Tags with Rivets:** Stylish option, durable, customizable

### Selection Criteria Used
- Comfort and adjustability
- Durability for active lifestyle
- Style and personality fit
- Safety features
