## Sentinel-2 Level 2 Data Coverage and Characteristics

### Mission Overview
- **Operator:** European Space Agency (ESA)
- **Constellation:** Two satellites (Sentinel-2A and Sentinel-2B) in tandem configuration
- **Mission type:** Earth observation, optical imagery

### Coverage Specifications
- **Spatial coverage:** Global coverage of entire Earth's surface
- **Revisit time:** 5 days at the equator (due to dual satellite constellation)
- **Data availability:** Level 2 data available for global coverage

### Spatial Resolution
- **Visible and Near-Infrared (NIR) bands:** 10 meters
- **Shortwave-Infrared (SWIR) bands:** 20 meters

### Availability Constraints
- **Cloud cover impact:** Cloud cover can prevent data collection in certain areas
- **Temporal coverage:** Not all time periods may have data available due to cloud cover, sensor malfunction, or other issues
- **Data provider schedule:** Availability depends on acquisition schedule and data provider operations

### Data Access
- **Primary source:** European Space Agency's Copernicus Open Access Hub
- **Cost:** Free and open access
- **Format availability:** Level 2 (atmospherically corrected) and Level 1C products available

### Important Note
Level 2 data is **not always available** for every location and time period. Availability depends on data provider, sensor characteristics, temporal coverage, and environmental conditions. If Level 2 data unavailable, alternatives include other data providers or acquiring Level 1 data and applying atmospheric correction manually.
