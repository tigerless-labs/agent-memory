## Interactive Dog Toy Recommendations (May 2023)

### Selected: Kong Toy
- Reason: Max loves peanut butter
- Can stuff with peanut butter-filled treats
- Tips for use:
  - Start with small amount, gradually increase
  - Mix treats or peanut butter flavors
  - Freeze for extra challenge and cooling treat
  - Try layering: kibbles + peanut butter + treats
  - Always supervise
  - Rotate with other toys

### Alternative Options Presented
1. **Tricky Treat Ball** - adjustable difficulty, varied treat sizes
2. **Puzzle Toys** - Nina Ottosson Dog Brick, Outward Hound Brick Puzzle
3. **Interactive Feeders** - Outward Hound Sensory Maze, Pet Zone Treat Wobbler
4. **Hide-a-Squirrel** - plush with hidden squeakers and compartments
5. **West Paw Design's Wobbler** - weighted, treat-dispensing, wobbles and moves
6. **Sensory Mats** - Snuffle Mat, Pawfect Pets Sensory Mat
7. **Tug Toys with Treats** - Tug-A-Jug, Ever Pet Interactive Tug Toy
8. **Electronic Interactive Toys** - Petcube Bites, Furbo Interactive Dog Camera
9. **DIY Interactive Toys** - cardboard boxes, paper bags with treats

### Purposes
- Keeps dog engaged during owner's absence
- Reduces anxiety, stress, and destructive behavior
- Provides mental stimulation and problem-solving
