## Dog Walker Search (May 2023)

### Platforms to Check
1. **Rover** - popular platform for dog walkers and sitters, browse profiles, read reviews, book
2. **Care.com** - primarily childcare but also pet care services including dog walking
3. **Wag!** - dog walking and sitting platform, 30-minute or 1-hour walks available, real-time tracking
4. **Barkly Pets** - connects owners with local walkers and sitters, browse profiles and reviews

### Professional Organizations
1. **Pet Sitters International** - network of professional pet sitters and dog walkers across US
2. **National Association of Professional Pet Sitters (NAPPS)** - professional organization for local pet sitters and dog walkers

### Medication Administration Consideration
- Planning to ask if dog walker would be willing to help with flea and tick prevention medication application every two weeks
- Important factors to discuss with walker: experience with medication, certifications, comfort level, liability
- Alternative options if walker won't do medication: pet sitter, trusted neighbor/friend, automated medication dispensers
- Instructions to provide walker: dosage, frequency, potential side effects, written schedule

### Vetting Tips
- Check reviews from multiple sources
- Conduct interview before booking
- Verify insurance and certifications
- Get meet-and-greet with dog
