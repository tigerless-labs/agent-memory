## Bilingual Teaching Strategy

**Current Approach:**
- Provides directions in both English and Spanish in Yearbook class
- Uses Google Translate to prepare Spanish instructions before class
- Current Spanish proficiency level: upper beginner

**Goal:**
- Increase Spanish proficiency to better reach Spanish-speaking students and families
- Make ELL students feel more welcome in classroom

**Context:**
- 3 out of ~25 students in Yearbook class speak almost exclusively Spanish
- School has 17.3% English Language Learners overall
- Teacher recognizes need for better communication with Spanish-speaking students and families

**Opportunity for Growth:**
- Could leverage this strategy school-wide if culturally responsive curriculum is implemented
- School announcements in Spanish would help reach the 17% of ELL students currently excluded
