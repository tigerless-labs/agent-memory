## Key References for Culturally Responsive Teaching

**Primary Article:**
- **Butler, E. Jr.** (2019, April 05). Why a Culturally Responsive Curriculum Works. *Education Week*. https://www.edweek.org/leadership/opinion-why-a-culturally-responsive-curriculum-works/2019/04
  - Key finding: Culturally responsive instruction has positive impact on academic outcomes of minority students
  - Quotes Geneva Gay's definition: using cultural knowledge, prior experiences, frames of reference, and performance styles of ethnically diverse students
  - Notes: In culturally responsive classrooms, classroom climate is safe haven with respect and care; teachers establish trusting relationships
  - Example from Tucson Unified School District: after introducing culturally relevant courses, graduation rates and standardized test scores improved measurably

**Foundational Source:**
- **Gay, G.** (2000). *Culturally Responsive Teaching: Theory, Research, and Practice*. Teachers College Press.
  - Foundational definition: using cultural knowledge, prior experiences, frames of reference, and performance styles of ethnically diverse students to make learning more appropriate and effective

**Practical Tool:**
- **Standards for Cultural Responsiveness Checklist.** Brown University. https://www.brown.edu/academics/education-alliance/teaching-diverse-learners/strategies-0/culturally-responsive-teaching-0
  - Two sets: one for what lessons should align to, one for improving existing lessons
  - Covers: clear expectations, cooperative learning, student perspectives, knowledge building, self-assessment, evidenced reasoning, questioning beliefs, alternative viewpoints, cultural representation in images/examples
