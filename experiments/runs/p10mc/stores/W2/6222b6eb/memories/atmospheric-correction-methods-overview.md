## Atmospheric Correction Methods for Remote Sensing

Three main approaches for atmospheric correction:

1. **Dark Object Subtraction** — Relatively simple and fast; involves subtracting reflectance of a dark object (e.g., water body) to estimate and remove atmospheric effects; works with most remote-sensed data types

2. **Empirical Line Method** — Simple to implement; uses known reflectance characteristics of atmospheric gases (water vapor, ozone) to estimate and remove atmospheric effects; works with most remote-sensed data types

3. **Flux-based correction** — Uses atmospheric measurements of atmospheric constituents to estimate effects; simple if measurements available, more complex if measurements must be obtained separately

**Selection factors:** Algorithm choice depends on study characteristics, data format and resolution, study area properties, and availability of atmospheric measurement data.
