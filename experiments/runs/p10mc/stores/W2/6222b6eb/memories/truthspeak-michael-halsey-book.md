## Book: Truthspeak

**Author:** Michael D. Halsey

**Full Title:** Truthspeak: The True Meaning of Five Key Christian Words Distorted Through Religious Newspeak

**ISBN-10:** 0979963729
**ISBN-13:** 978-0979963728

### Overview
The book explores how five key Christian words have been distorted through "religious newspeak"—a form of language shaped by cultural and societal forces that has diluted these concepts from their original biblical meanings.

### Five Key Words Discussed
1. Grace
2. Faith
3. Sanctification
4. Righteousness
5. Salvation

### Chapter Titles
- Chapter 1: "Salvation: Rescued to Worship"
- Chapter 2: "Newspeak: Finished Is Incomplete" (or possibly "Sanctification: Finished and Being Finished")

### Purpose
Halsey aims to help readers gain a deeper understanding of these core Christian concepts by explaining their original meanings and showing how they can be applied to modern life. The book is written in an accessible style for both new believers and long-time followers of Christianity.

### Author's Goal
Not to criticize or condemn modern Christian culture, but to encourage a deeper understanding of these key concepts and their importance in Christian theology.
