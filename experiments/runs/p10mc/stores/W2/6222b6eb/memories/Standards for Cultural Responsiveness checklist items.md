## Standards for Cultural Responsiveness — Full Checklist

**Part 1: What All Lessons Should Align To**
- Are students told specifically what they are expected to know and do?
- Does the lesson use cooperative learning, especially for new material (book clubs, literature circles, role-playing, discussion groups, peer teaching, Jigsaw, Reciprocal Learning)?
- Does the lesson ask students to connect their perspectives and experiences to the topic?
- Do students participate in building knowledge (writing definitions, peer teaching, student-directed sharing time)?
- Is self-assessment included?
- Are students required to support their ideas by referencing text, pictures, reasoning?
- Does the lesson teach students to question/challenge their own beliefs and actions?
- Are alternative viewpoints acknowledged and discussed?
- Do lesson images and examples reference various cultures?
- Do images represent people as individuals, as stereotypes, or generically?
- Are there male and female protagonists (historic, biographical, fictional) representing varying economic and ethnic backgrounds?

**Part 2: Checklist for Improving Lessons**
- Could a poll be added for students to reflect on learning style preferences?
- Is there a way to incorporate students' identities (family traditions, cultural background research)?
- Could a poll or open-ended question enable students to question/challenge or review the lesson?
- Is there a way to teach about differences between individuals and show how diversity strengthens the class?
- Does the lesson encourage use of student's first language to enhance learning?
- Is there a way for students to choose reading material, project goals, or research topics?
- Can the lesson be made inquiry-based/discovery-oriented?
- Is there a way for students to research topics within their community (interviews, guest speakers, alternative problem-solving approaches)?
- Is there a way to differentiate learning modes (solo/group work, video/written submissions)?
- Can the lesson include cross-cultural interaction via technology?

**Source:** Standards for Cultural Responsiveness Checklist, Brown University, https://www.brown.edu/academics/education-alliance/teaching-diverse-learners/strategies-0/culturally-responsive-teaching-0
