## Strategies for Indoor Workers to Maintain Vitamin D

1. **Lunch break outside**: Get 10-15 minutes of sun exposure during the day when the sun is strongest. Even brief midday exposure helps increase vitamin D levels.

2. **Eat vitamin D-rich foods**: Incorporate salmon, tuna, egg yolks, cheese, mushrooms, and fortified products into regular diet.

3. **Vitamin D supplements**: Take if not getting enough from diet and sun exposure. Doctor may prescribe specific dosage based on individual needs.

4. **Light therapy box**: Consider using to simulate natural sunlight if access to sunlight is limited.

5. **Monitor with healthcare provider**: Get vitamin D levels checked, especially if at risk of deficiency or have limited sun exposure. Adjust strategy based on professional advice.

Balance vitamin D intake with skin protection: use sunscreen, wear hats, avoid prolonged unprotected sun exposure.
