## Colchester Historical Sites

1. **Colchester Castle** — Norman castle with museum; exhibits on Roman, medieval, and Civil War history
2. **St. Botolph's Priory** — Founded 11th century; important religious site with impressive ruins showing religious history
3. **Hollytrees Museum** — Georgian townhouse, 18th century; displays daily life of middle-class Georgian families including furniture, clothing, and decorative arts
4. **Tymperleys Clock Museum** — Grade I listed house; collection includes lantern clocks, long-case clocks, and a clock with garden features
5. **Firstsite Art Gallery** — Contemporary art gallery; modern art exhibitions and various events throughout the year

All located in or near town center.
