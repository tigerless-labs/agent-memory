## French Omelette Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 eggs
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped herbs (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Whisk eggs with salt in bowl until well combined and frothy
2. Heat nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add butter and swirl to coat surface
4. Pour egg mixture in and spread evenly with fork or spatula
5. Cook ~30 seconds until eggs begin to set
6. Gently lift one edge and fold over center
7. Fold opposite side over center
8. Tilt skillet and slide omelette onto plate
9. Garnish with herbs if desired

## Tips for Fluffy & Light Omelette

- **Whisk well** — eggs should become frothy with yolks and whites fully combined
- **Use nonstick skillet** — prevents sticking, tearing, and ensures even cooking
- **Medium-high heat** — avoids overcooking (dry/rubbery) or undercooking
- **Don't overfill** — keep filling light to maintain texture
- **Fold gently** — use spatula without pressing down; avoid flattening
- **Serve immediately** — prevents cooling and limpness

## Filling Recommendations

**Classic combinations:**
- Ham and cheese (any type)
- Mushrooms and cheese (sautéed mushrooms + grated cheese)
- Spinach and feta (cooked spinach + crumbled feta)
- Tomato and basil (diced tomatoes + fresh basil)
- Smoked salmon and dill (elegant option)

**Individual options:**
- Cheese: Swiss, cheddar, Gruyere
- Ham: cooked and diced
- Mushrooms: sautéed
- Spinach: cooked with sautéed onions and garlic
- Tomatoes: diced and cooked
- Herbs: parsley, thyme, chives (fresh or dried)

**Note:** Use just a few tablespoons of filling per omelette to maintain lightness.
