## Colchester Parks and Outdoor Areas

1. **Castle Park** — Colchester's largest public park in town center; features beautiful gardens, large pond, skateboard park, tennis courts, cricket ground, sports facilities

2. **Highwoods Country Park** — Large nature reserve on outskirts; over 350 acres with woodlands, open meadows, several ponds; good for hiking, wildlife watching, picnics

3. **Wivenhoe Trail** — Walking and cycling route along River Colne; approximately 5 miles long between Colchester and Wivenhoe; passes through woodlands, wildlife habitats, old industrial sites

4. **Cudmore Grove Country Park** — Located on Mersea Island, short drive from Colchester; features beach, woodlands, picnic areas, walking and cycling routes

5. **Castle Point Wildlife Reserve** — Located near coast; features salt marshes, mud flats, tidal creeks; excellent for bird watching (waterfowl, waders, other wildlife)

Suitable for hiking, cycling, picnicking, and wildlife watching.
