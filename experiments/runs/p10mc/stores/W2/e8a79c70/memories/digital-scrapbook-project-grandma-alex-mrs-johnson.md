## Digital Scrapbook Project

User is organizing photos from the past few months into a digital scrapbook to preserve memories of loved ones.

### Planned Album/Sections

1. **"Memories of Grandma"** — Photos and stories from grandmother's funeral; wants to centralize all memories of grandmother in one place
2. **"Memories of Alex"** — Photos and stories from hospital visits (relationship to user: appears to be family member, possibly aunt)
3. **"Memories of Mrs. Johnson"** — Photos and stories from English class; tribute to a former teacher who had significant impact on user's education

### Features & Requirements

- **Audio recordings**: Want to add voice recordings, stories, and audio memories to the scrapbook
- **Privacy/Security**: Password protection and private albums so only specific trusted people can access (especially important for funeral/hospital visit memories)
- **Organization**: By theme, event, person, and chronological order
- **Searchability**: Need easy-to-find specific memories with clear naming conventions and tagging
- **Interactivity**: Interest in adding audio, video, quotes, and interactive elements

### Platform Options Discussed

- Google Photos
- Adobe Spark
- Canva
- Digital scrapbooking software

### Technical Preferences

- MP3 format for audio files
- Cloud storage for accessibility and backup
- Transcripts for audio recordings (for accessibility)
- Strong passwords and two-factor authentication for security
