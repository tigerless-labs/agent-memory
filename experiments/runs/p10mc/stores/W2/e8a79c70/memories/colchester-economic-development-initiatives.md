## Colchester Economic Development Strategies and Initiatives

### Infrastructure Investment
- Renovation of Colchester's high street
- Northern Gateway: mixed-use development providing commercial and residential opportunities

### Enterprise Zone
- Financial incentives for businesses and investors
- Business rates relief
- Simplified planning processes
- Commercial property discounts

### Skills and Training
- Colchester Institute offers vocational training programs
- Programs include engineering, construction, hairdressing, and other sectors
- Focus on improving local workforce skills

### Business Support
- Colchester Borough Council actively supports businesses and entrepreneurs
- Dedicated business support teams assist with starting and growing businesses

### Historic Preservation Strategy
- Adaptive reuse of historic buildings maintains character while serving modern purposes
- Examples: Colchester Savings Bank converted to North Hill Noodle Bar; Queen Street Brewery converted to Queen Street Brewhouse mixed-use development
- New buildings incorporate modern design while paying homage to town's history
- Strategy balances historic charm with economic development
