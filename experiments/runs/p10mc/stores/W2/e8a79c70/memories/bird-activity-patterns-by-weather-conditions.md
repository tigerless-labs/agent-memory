Bird species exhibit increased activity in certain weather conditions:

**Rainy Days**
- American Robins, Northern Cardinals, Blue Jays more active
- Forage for earthworms and invertebrates brought to surface

**Windy Days**
- Hawks, eagles, vultures thrive in windy conditions
- Use wind currents to soar and glide while scanning for prey

**Overcast Days**
- Warblers, sparrows, finches more active
- Diffuse light makes foraging for insects and seeds easier

**Cold Fronts**
- Waterfowl and sparrows more visible
- Seek food sources to fuel up before cold weather

**Warm Fronts**
- Hummingbirds, orioles, tanagers more active
- Take advantage of increased insect activity and nectar availability

**Low-Pressure Systems**
- Raptors and songbirds may be more active
- Changing weather patterns trigger migratory instincts

Note: These are general trends and vary by specific species, location, and time of year. Always research the particular species and location.
