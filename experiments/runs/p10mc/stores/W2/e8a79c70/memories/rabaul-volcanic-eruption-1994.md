## Rabaul Volcanic Eruption, 1994

**Date & Location**: 1994, Rabaul, Papua New Guinea

**Casualties & Damage**:
- At least 5 people dead
- Over 1,000 injured
- Extensive damage to property and infrastructure in the town

**Warning Signs**: 
- Increased seismic activity for several weeks before eruption
- Formation of a new vent on the volcano's flank
- Local authorities had evacuation plans implemented

**Recovery & Current Status**:
- Town of Rabaul and surrounding communities were rebuilt after the eruption
- Many buildings and infrastructure were destroyed or damaged
- People still live near the volcano despite ongoing risk
- Residents rely on agriculture and fishing for livelihoods
- Local authorities continue to monitor the volcano with evacuation plans in place

**Community Perspective**:
- Many residents have lived there for generations and have adapted to volcanic risk
- Some acceptance that volcanic activity is part of the natural environment
- Residents remain concerned about safety during periods of increased volcanic activity
