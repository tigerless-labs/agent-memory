## Vitamin D Sources

### High Natural Food Sources
- Fatty fish: salmon, tuna, mackerel
- Beef liver, egg yolks, cheese, mushrooms

### Fortified Foods
- Dairy products (milk, yogurt)
- Orange juice
- Cereals
- Plant-based milk alternatives

### Sun Exposure
- Recommended: 15-30 minutes daily during midday (strongest UVB rays)
- UVB rays interact with skin cholesterol to produce vitamin D
- Varies by skin tone, age, and season
- Important to balance with sun protection (sunscreen, hats, protective clothing)

### Supplements
- Available in capsules, tablets, gummies
- Recommended for areas with limited sunlight (especially winter in northern regions)
- Must follow doctor's dosage advice — too much vitamin D can be harmful

### Monitoring
- Talk to healthcare provider about checking vitamin D levels
- Important for bone health
- Special attention if limited sun exposure or at risk of deficiency
