Pocahontas (also known as Matoaka and Amonute) married English colonist John Rolfe in 1614. Rolfe was a tobacco farmer who had settled in Jamestown, Virginia. The marriage was a diplomatic union meant to create peace between the Powhatan tribe and English colonists, and it ushered in a period of relative peace between the two groups.

Pocahontas and Rolfe had one son together: Thomas Rolfe, born in 1615. Pocahontas died in 1617 while traveling to England with her husband and son.

Note: Pocahontas had an earlier, well-known relationship with English colonist John Smith, but she married Rolfe, not Smith.
