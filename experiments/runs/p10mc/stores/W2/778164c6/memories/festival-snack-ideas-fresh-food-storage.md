## Festival Snacks and Food Storage

### Snack Suggestions

#### Protein and Healthy Fats
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, pumpkin seeds)
- Trail mix
- Jerky (beef, turkey, chicken)

#### Carbohydrates and Natural Energy
- Dried fruits (apricots, cranberries, raisins, dates)
- Energy bars (Clif Bars, Luna Bars, RX Bars - look for wholesome ingredients and minimal sugar)
- Granola or energy chews (Clif Shot Bloks)
- Fresh fruits (apples, oranges, bananas, avocados)

#### Protein-Rich Options
- Sandwiches or wraps with whole-grain bread, peanut butter, banana slices, or turkey
- Crackers and peanut butter combo
- No-bake energy balls (oats, peanut butter, honey)

#### Hydration
- Refillable water bottles
- Electrolyte drinks (coconut water, sports drinks)

### Keeping Sandwiches Fresh
- Use small insulated lunch bag
- Wrap sandwiches tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil
- Store in thermally-insulated container (vacuum-insulated lunch box)
- Pack in shaded area of backpack to avoid direct sunlight

### Keeping Fruits Fresh
- Choose less perishable fruits (apples, oranges, bananas, avocados)
- Store in breathable containers (mesh bags, containers with holes)
- Wrap individual fruits in plastic wrap or foil to prevent bruising
- Keep in shaded area of backpack

### General Food Preservation
- Pack snacks in small portions to prevent crushing
- Use reusable containers or bags
- Pack small cooler pack with ice packs
- Check on snacks regularly for spoilage
- Check festival website for specific food and drink rules/restrictions
