## Furniture Restoration Project

**Current Work:**
- Item: Old oak coffee table
- Status: In progress, making good progress
- Location: Part of restoration class at local workshop

**Learning & Skills:**
- Taking classes at local antique furniture restoration workshop
- Researching antique furniture restoration principles
- Learning and refining restoration skills
- Interest in staying true to original look and feel when restoring

**Challenge: Determining Original Finish**
- Struggling with deciding on the right finish to use
- Wants to determine what type of finish was originally used
- Needs to balance historical accuracy with practical durability

**Research Methods Applied:**
- Examining table's surface for remaining original finish, patina, worn areas
- Studying wood grain, color, texture
- Looking for hardware and fittings clues
- Researching similar tables from same era and style
- Consulting with furniture restoration experts

**Finish Considerations:**
- Arts and Crafts era: traditionally used shellac or dark, rich stain
- Oak typically looks great with darker stain
- Need to consider purpose (frequent use vs. display)
- Testing finish on inconspicuous area before full application
