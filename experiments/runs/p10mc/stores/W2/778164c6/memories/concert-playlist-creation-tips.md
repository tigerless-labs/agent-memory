## Concert Playlist Creation Guide

### Playlist Structure
- 3-4 songs from the headlining artist
- 2-3 songs from similar artists or bands
- 2-3 upbeat, energetic tracks
- 1-2 concert anthems
- 1-2 personal favorite songs
- 1-2 mood-booster tracks
- 1 concert-themed track (optional)

### Song Selection Strategies
- Include artist's popular tracks, fan favorites, and newer releases
- Add songs from similar artists with same genre/style for cohesive vibe
- Choose high-energy, upbeat songs easy to sing along to
- Include crowd-pleaser songs known for strong live performances
- Add personal favorite songs that resonate emotionally
- Choose feel-good tracks to get in positive mood
- Include songs with concert/festival/live music themes in lyrics

### The 1975 Concert Playlist Example
- The 1975 popular tracks: "The Sound", "Girls", "Give Yourself a Try"
- Similar artists: Pale Waves, The Neighbourhood, COIN
- Upbeat tracks: "Mr. Brightside" by The Killers, "Best Day of My Life" by American Authors
- Concert anthems: "I Gotta Feeling" by The Black Eyed Peas, "Uptown Funk" by Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars
- Personal favorites: "Somebody Else", "Robbers" by The 1975
- Mood-booster tracks: "Can't Stop the Feeling!" by Justin Timberlake, "Happy" by Pharrell Williams

### Key Principle
Create a playlist that gets you excited and pumped up for the concert. Experiment with different songs and genres to find the perfect mix.
