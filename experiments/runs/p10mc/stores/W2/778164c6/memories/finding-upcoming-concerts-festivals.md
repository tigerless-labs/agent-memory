## Finding Upcoming Concerts and Festivals

### Dedicated Concert Platforms
- **Songkick** - Track favorite artists and receive notifications when they come to your area, explore concert calendar
- **Bandsintown** - Track artists, get concert notifications, user-friendly app and website

### General Event Discovery
- Local event calendars and websites like Eventbrite
- Search "concerts near me" or "upcoming events in [your city]"
- Local newspapers and event sections

### Social Media
- Follow favorite artists on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram
- Follow venues, venues, and local promoters
- Monitor for upcoming shows and events

### Direct Sources
- Check websites of local venues (theaters, clubs, arenas)
- Research festival websites for lineups and ticket information
- Sign up for artist email lists and fan club notifications

### Research Best Practices
- Always double-check authenticity of event and ticket seller before purchasing
- Verify through official sources
