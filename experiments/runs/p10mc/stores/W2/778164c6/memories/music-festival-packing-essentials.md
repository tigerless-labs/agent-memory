## Music Festival Packing Essentials

### Critical Items
- Festival tickets, wristbands, necessary documents
- Refillable water bottle (many festivals have refill stations)
- SPF 30+ sunscreen, sun hat, sunglasses
- Comfortable shoes or sneakers suitable for festival terrain
- Portable charger for phone and devices
- Combination of cash and credit cards
- Small first-aid kit (band-aids, antiseptic wipes, pain relievers)

### Personal Care Items
- Small towel for sweat or drying off
- Travel-sized deodorant and wipes to stay fresh
- Any prescription medications
- Small umbrella or rain jacket for unexpected rain

### Snacks and Hydration
- Reusable snack containers
- Waterproof snack bags for items like sandwiches and chips
- Fresh fruits (apples, oranges, bananas)
- Energy bars or protein bars
- Electrolyte drinks like coconut water or sports drinks

### Technology and Protection
- Waterproof phone case and screen protector
- Earplugs for hearing protection during loud performances
- Printed or digital copy of festival map

### Optional Items
- Cashless wristband if festival offers one (convenient for purchases)

### Packing Tips
- Pack light to avoid heavy backpack
- Check festival website for prohibited or recommended items
- Designate meeting spot with friends
- Stay hydrated throughout the day
- Pack snacks in small portions to prevent crushing or spoiling
- Use reusable containers to reduce waste
