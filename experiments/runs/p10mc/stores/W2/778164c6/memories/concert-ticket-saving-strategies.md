## Concert Ticket-Saving Tips

### Official Purchasing
- Buy directly from official ticketing websites (Ticketmaster, AXS, venue websites)
- Avoid extra fees charged by resale websites
- Consider box office or retail outlet purchases for "no service fee" options

### Presale Opportunities
- Sign up for artist fan clubs
- Follow artists on social media and join email lists
- Get presale access before general sales

### Promotional Options
- Look for early bird discounts with limited-time offers
- Enter ticket lotteries for face value or free tickets
- Look for bundle deals (tickets + merchandise + experiences)

### Alternative Marketplaces
- Use StubHub, SeatGeek, or Vivid Seats for competitive pricing
- Read reviews and verify seller reputation before purchasing
- Beware of fake tickets

### Cost-Reduction Strategies
- Choose upper-level seats for lower prices
- Be flexible with weeknight or off-peak season concerts
- Use cashback apps (Rakuten/Ebates, TopCashback) for cashback on ticket purchases
- Check for student or military discounts

### Information Resources
- Follow local radio stations for giveaways and presale codes
- Monitor venue and artist social media
