Barack Obama is a former President of the United States who served two terms from 2009 to 2017.

**Birth & Background:**
- Born August 4, 1961 in Honolulu, Hawaii
- Grew up in Hawaii before attending college on the mainland
- Attended Harvard Law School

**Career before presidency:**
- Community organizer
- State senator
- U.S. senator

**Presidential term (2009-2017):**
- Key policy achievements: the Affordable Care Act and legalization of same-sex marriage
- Played significant role in international relations, particularly in the Middle East

**After presidency:**
- Remains active in politics and public life
- Continues to be an influential figure in the Democratic Party
