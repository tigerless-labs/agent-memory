## Interest in Monrovia's Economic Development

From May 2023 conversation:

**User's concerns about Monrovia:**
- Political changes (Ellen Johnson Sirleaf → George Weah presidency, 2018 peaceful transfer of power) created opportunity but economy remains unstable
- Persistent poverty, unemployment, infrastructure gaps, education/healthcare challenges
- Heavy reliance on foreign aid

**User's policy values:**
- Government should prioritize job creation and economic growth
- **Small businesses and local entrepreneurs are critical** — should be "backbone of the economy"
  - SMEs face: limited finance access, poor infrastructure, limited market access
  - Government should provide: finance access, infrastructure, business environment support, training/technical assistance
- **Sustainable development is essential** — balance economic, social, environmental goals
  - Support green technologies, reforestation, sustainable land use, renewable energy
  - Economic policies must account for environmental impact
- **Community collaboration is key** — government should work with local communities, NGOs, private sector to find innovative solutions

The user expressed optimism that Monrovia can achieve sustainable, inclusive growth with commitment from government, civil society, and private sector.
