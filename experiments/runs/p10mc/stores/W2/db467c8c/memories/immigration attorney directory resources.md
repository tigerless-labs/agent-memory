## Key Directory Resources

- AILA (American Immigration Lawyers Association) — Find an Immigration Lawyer tool at aila.org; look for State Bar certified specialists
- National Immigration Project — Directory at nationalimmigrationproject.org
- Immigration Advocates Network (IAN) — Find an Immigration Attorney search at immigrationadvocates.org
- Avvo — Attorney ratings at avvo.com; filter by experience and reviews
- Martindale-Hubbell — Attorney directory at martindale.com with ratings and practice area filters

## How to Evaluate

- Ask about experience with green card applications and overstay cases
- Verify state bar licensing in your state
- Inquire about success rate on similar cases
- Use free initial consultation to assess expertise and communication
- Check online reviews (Yelp, Google Reviews, Facebook)

## Red Flags

- Guarantees of successful outcome
- Unrealistic promises
- Unexplained high fees
- Lack of transparency about credentials
