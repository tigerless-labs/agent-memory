## Sushi Assembly Tips (for reference)

**Maki Rolls:**
- Use room-temperature sushi rice
- Lay nori sheet on sushi mat
- Spread thin rice layer, leaving 1-inch border at top
- Place fillings in middle
- Roll gently using mat
- Refrigerate 30+ minutes before slicing
- Cut into 8 pieces with gentle sawing motion

**Nigiri:**
- Shape small rice balls (~1 inch diameter)
- Top with fish or other topping
- Press gently to secure
- Serve immediately or refrigerate up to 2 hours
