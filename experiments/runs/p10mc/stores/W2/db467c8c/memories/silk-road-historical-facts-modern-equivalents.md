## Historical Silk Road Facts

**Origin of term:** German geographer Ferdinand von Richthofen coined "Silk Road" in the late 19th century.

**Nature of route:** Complex network of trade routes connecting Asia and Europe, not a single road.

**Goods traded:** Silk, spices, tea, porcelain, ivory, jade — not limited to silk.

**Impact:** 
- Facilitated exchange of goods, ideas, and culture
- Spread of Buddhism from India to China
- Exchange of art and technology across regions
- Created wealthy merchant classes and major trade centers

**Challenges:** Wars, conflicts, and bandits disrupted trade and threatened merchants.

## Modern Equivalents

- Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) — China's infrastructure project connecting Asia, Europe, Africa
- New Silk Road — Proposed by Turkey
- Trans-Asian Railway Network — Connects Europe and Asia
- Trans-Pacific Partnership (TPP) — Trade agreement shaping economic relationships

These share the original Silk Road's purpose of promoting trade, commerce, and cultural exchange.
