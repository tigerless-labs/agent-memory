## User's Situation

- Planning to apply for a green card soon
- Parents have been staying with the user for **9 months** (overstay of B-2 tourist visa)
- B-2 tourist visa limit: 6 months (general rule); 9-month stay violates this
- Parents' overstay may impact user's own green card application

## Key Immigration Issues

- Parents' unlawful presence (9-month overstay) makes them inadmissible
- Potential consequences for parents: inadmissibility, bars to re-entry, deportation
- Potential consequences for user: impact on green card application outcome
- May need to address parents' issue separately to avoid jeopardizing user's application

## Solution Under Consideration

- **Form I-601**: Application for Waiver of Grounds of Inadmissibility
- Requires proving **extreme hardship** to qualifying relative (the user, as their child)
- Requires demonstrating parents' **rehabilitation**
- Complex discretionary process; attorney guidance essential

## Next Steps

1. Find qualified immigration attorney with experience in:
   - Green card applications
   - Overstay situations
   - Waiver of inadmissibility process
2. Consult attorney about:
   - Parents' specific overstay circumstances
   - Likelihood of waiver approval
   - Impact on user's green card timeline
   - Alternative options for parents' immigration status
3. Prepare supporting evidence if pursuing waiver (medical records, financial docs, letters of support, community ties)
