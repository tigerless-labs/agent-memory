## What is a Waiver of Inadmissibility

A request to waive or forgive grounds of inadmissibility that would otherwise prevent entry or remaining in the US. For the user's parents' situation, used to overcome inadmissibility due to unlawful presence from overstaying their B-2 tourist visa.

## Eligibility Requirements

Parents must demonstrate inadmissibility due to:
- Unlawful presence: over 180 days but less than one year in the US without authorization (user's parents had 9-month stay, which qualifies)
- Or misrepresentation/fraudulent obtainment of immigration benefit

## Application Process

1. File Form I-601 (Application for Waiver of Grounds of Inadmissibility) with USCIS
2. USCIS schedules biometric services appointment for fingerprints and biographic information
3. USCIS adjudication of the application
4. Possible interview at USCIS office or US embassy/consulate

## Key Burden of Proof

Must demonstrate:
1. Extreme hardship to a qualifying relative (user, as their US citizen or lawful permanent resident child)
2. Rehabilitation and that applicant is no longer a risk to the US

## Supporting Evidence Needed

- Medical records
- Financial documents
- Letters from family members and friends
- Evidence of community ties

## Important Notes

- Waiver is discretionary
- Decision may take significant time
- Attorney strongly recommended due to complexity
- Failure to establish extreme hardship results in denial
