## Practice Plan with Rachel

**Learning Partner:** Rachel (Lebanese descent, classmate in beginner Arabic class)

**Phase 1 - Introductions (Starting Point)**
- Ask each other's names: "ما هو اسمك؟" (What's your name?)
- Introduce yourselves: "أنا [name]" (I am [name])
- Ask where from: "من أين أنت؟" (Where are you from?)
- Ask what they do: "ما هو عملك؟" (What do you do?)

**Phase 2 - Interests and Basic Conversations**
- Discuss hobbies: "ما هو هوايتك؟" (What's your hobby?)
- Share interests using "أنا أحب..." (I like...)
- Practice describing preferences and dislikes

**Phase 3 - Daily Routines**
- Ask about daily routines: "ما هو يومك؟" (What's your day like?)
- Discuss morning/evening habits
- Share favorite TV shows or movies
- Discuss weekend activities

**Phase 4 - Complex Topics (Later)**
- Traditional Lebanese cuisine
- Cultural events and festivals
- Travel experiences (Lebanon or other Arab countries)
- Current events and social issues in Lebanon/Arab world

**Practice Tips:**
- Set specific topic/activity for each session
- Use authentic materials (news, videos, music)
- Correct each other's mistakes
- Take turns speaking and listening
- Have fun and enjoy the process
- Practice regularly
