**User Interest**: Organizing a charity run or walk event

### Key Planning Steps:

1. **Define Your Goal** - Choose cause, fundraising target
2. **Choose Date and Location** - Consider accessibility, safety, amenities
3. **Obtain Permits** - Research local permits, licenses, approvals needed
4. **Create Budget** - Cover venue, marketing, equipment, supplies, contingencies
5. **Form Planning Committee** - Recruit volunteers, assign tasks
6. **Register Event** - Register with relevant charities and obtain insurance
7. **Design Course/Route** - Plan safe, enjoyable route considering distance and terrain
8. **Promote Event** - Use social media, email, flyers, local businesses, schools, community groups
9. **Secure Sponsors** - Reach out for sponsorships and donations
10. **Plan Logistics** - Arrange timing systems, water stations, first aid, parking
11. **Registration & Fundraising** - Set up online registration and fundraising platform
12. **Execute & Evaluate** - Run event smoothly and gather feedback
