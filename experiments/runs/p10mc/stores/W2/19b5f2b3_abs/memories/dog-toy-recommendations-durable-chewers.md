## Durable Dog Toy Recommendations

For dogs like Max who are heavy chewers, these toys are known for durability:

1. GoughNuts - Proprietary rubber compound, extremely durable, resistant to chewing
2. Kong Wobbler - Weighted rubber toy with unpredictable movement, puzzle engagement
3. Nylabone DuraChew - Tough nylon material for aggressive chewers
4. West Paw Design (Zogoflex) - Incredibly durable proprietary material, various toy types
5. Ever Pet Natural Rubber Toys - 100 percent natural rubber, durable and long-lasting
6. Outward Hound Brick Puzzle - Durable plastic puzzle toy with adjustable difficulty

**Usage Tips:**
- Always supervise when introducing new toys
- Rotate toys regularly to prevent boredom
- For treat-dispensing toys: start with softer treats, adjust difficulty as dog becomes experienced
- Clean puzzle toys regularly to prevent bacterial growth
