## Scene & Relationship

**Date:** May 20, 2023 (Saturday)

**Location:** Outside a pub

**Characters Present:** Sebastian Vivaldi Greensleeves, Alexander Shelstrop; waiting for friend Pond

**Relationship:** Close friends; Sebastian admires Alexander's style boldness; Alexander supports Sebastian's emotional depth

## Conversation: Style vs. Function

**Topic:** Aesthetics, fashion, personal expression, and practicality

**Key Exchange:**
- Sebastian admires Alex's bold style but questions practicality (e.g., uncomfortable-looking shoes)
- Alexander argues "style is everything" — looking good makes you feel good; clarifies he wears Yeezys for style, not function like hiking
- Sebastian reveals his philosophy: function over style; clothes are comfortable and help him focus on music; onstage presence feels like wearing armor
- Alexander challenges this: asks why Sebastian doesn't express himself through clothing like he does through music
- Sebastian acknowledges never considering it; sees music as his main outlet but opens to being more adventurous with clothing
- Alexander offers to take him shopping and teach him streetwear; suggests he wear something eye-catching for next performance
- Sebastian laughs but doubts he could pull off streetwear as well as Alex

**Outcome:** Agreement to shop together; Sebastian commits to trying more expressive clothing choices while maintaining his essential comfort-first approach

**How It Ended:** Pond arrives, they greet warmly and head into pub together
