## Strategies to Keep Dogs Engaged During Work Hours

**Interactive & Enrichment Activities:**
- Stuff treat-dispensing or puzzle toys with favorite treats, kibble, or canned dog food
- Provide long-lasting chews: bully sticks, rawhides, Kongs with peanut butter
- Create backyard obstacle courses with tunnels, cones, jumps
- Place bird feeder or squirrel feeder near window for entertainment
- Hide treats or toys around house and backyard for scavenger hunts

**Exercise & Routine:**
- Exercise dog before leaving for work: brisk walk, park play, fetch
- A tired dog is a happy dog
- Hire dog walker or sitter for midday visits
- Consider enrolling in doggy daycare for social interaction

**Mental Health & Anxiety:**
- Calming aids: pheromone diffusers, calming music, calming treats
- Helpful if dog has separation anxiety

**Toy Management:**
- Rotate toys every few days to keep things fresh
- Prevents boredom and encourages exploration

**Professional Services:**
- Dog walkers on platforms like Rover.com and Care.com
- Pet sitter resources: NAPPS and PSI
- Always interview providers, ask for references, research experience with dog breed and temperament
