## Tamil Language Preservation Efforts

### Purist Movement & Initiatives
- **Tamil Purists Movement** — advocates for use of pure Tamil words, discourages foreign loanwords
- **Classical Tamil promotion** — considered the purest form of Tamil
- **Tamil Virtual University** — established by Tamil Nadu government; offers courses on classical Tamil literature and language
- **Government mandate** — Tamil Nadu government requires Tamil in official communication and encourages use in public spaces
- **Organizations** — promote Tamil use in education, media, and literature

### Language Borrowings in Contemporary Tamil
Foreign borrowings commonly used in modern Tamil:
- From English: computer, telephone, coffee, pencil, camera
- From Sanskrit: technical and scientific terms
- From Urdu: words related to religion, law, and administration

### Challenges to Preservation Efforts
1. **Widespread English use** — in education, media, and administration
2. **Urban/educated speakers** — tendency toward loanwords, especially among educated and urban populations
3. **Lack of awareness** — many Tamil speakers unfamiliar with classical Tamil or recognize foreign words as such
4. **Risk of exclusion** — emphasis on purity may marginalize dialects and regional variations
5. **Balance needed** — must preserve language while promoting linguistic diversity and inclusion
