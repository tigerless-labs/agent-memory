## Online Resources for Arabic Learning

**Websites:**
- Duolingo - Interactive lessons, quizzes, exercises for beginners
- Al-Kitaab - Arabic textbook series with online resources, videos, exercises
- ArabicPod101 - Podcast-based course with audio, video, PDF materials, mobile app
- Madina Arabic - Online courses, tutorials, grammar, reading, listening exercises

**Language Exchange:**
- italki - Find tutors or language exchange partners (native Arabic speakers)
- Conversation Exchange - Find language exchange partners or practice with native speakers

**YouTube Channels:**
- Learn Arabic with Maha - Engaging video lessons, explanations, conversations
- Arabic Alphabet - Arabic alphabet, reading, writing focus
- Arabic with Nadia - Grammar, vocabulary, conversations

**Language Learning Apps:**
- Memrise - User-generated Arabic courses (vocabulary and grammar focus)
- Anki - Flashcard app for memorizing vocabulary and phrases
- HelloTalk - Connect with native Arabic speakers for language exchange, built-in correction feature

**Audio Resources:**
- Radio Sawa - Arabic radio station
- Monte Carlo Doualiya - Arabic radio station
- Coffee Break Arabic - Podcast with conversational lessons
- Arabic Pod - Podcast with conversational lessons

**Communities:**
- r/LearnArabic - Reddit community for learners
- r/Arabic - Reddit community
- Arabic Language Learners Facebook group
- Learn Arabic with Native Speakers Facebook group
