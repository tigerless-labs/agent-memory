## Philippine VAT Information

**VAT Rate:** 12% (as of May 2023)

**VAT Registration Threshold:**
- Businesses with annual sales or gross receipts exceeding PHP 3 million must be VAT-registered
- Businesses below this threshold are not required to charge VAT

**Invoice Requirements:**
- VAT-registered businesses: required to charge VAT and include it in invoices; must file VAT returns and pay collected VAT to Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- Non-VAT-registered businesses: not required to charge or include VAT

**Calculation Example:**
- Total cost: PHP 1,000
- VAT (12%): PHP 1,000 × 0.12 = PHP 120
- Total with VAT: PHP 1,120
