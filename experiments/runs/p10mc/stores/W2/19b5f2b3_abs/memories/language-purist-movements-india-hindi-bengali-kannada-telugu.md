## Language Purist Movements Across India

### Major Language Preservation Movements
Each major Indian language has its own language purist movement:

1. **Hindi** — Hindi Rakshak Samiti (Hindi Protection Committee)
2. **Bengali** — Bangla Bhasha Bachao Samiti (Save Bengali Language Committee)
3. **Kannada** — regional language purist movement
4. **Telugu** — regional language purist movement
5. **Marathi** — regional language purist movement
6. **Gujarati** — regional language purist movement

### Common Goals
- Promote use of purest form of each language
- Free languages from foreign influence
- Encourage use of native words instead of loanwords
- Promote linguistic purity and cultural identity

### Government Initiatives
- **National Translation Mission** — promotes use of native and classical languages
- **Central Institute of Indian Languages** — documents and preserves regional and cultural diversity

### Parallel to Tamil Efforts
Similar efforts to preserve Tamil are part of a broader trend across Indian languages seeking to maintain linguistic purity while promoting cultural identity.
