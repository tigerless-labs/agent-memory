## Story Overview

Fiction project exploring MK's imprisonment by Wukong (the Monkey King). MK is captured and interrogated about the location of a "diamond snare." MK doesn't know where it is.

## Key Plot Points

1. **Initial Interrogation**: Wukong demands MK reveal the diamond snare's location. MK insists he only knows old stories about it. Wukong tries torture; MK continues denying knowledge.

2. **Negotiation for Life**: Wukong questions why he should keep MK alive. MK argues he can serve as "insurance" — promising silence and future availability if needed. Flatters Wukong ("you're a legend"). Wukong reluctantly agrees to spare him, but warns it's temporary.

3. **Deprivation Cycle**: 
   - MK becomes extremely hungry and thirsty after days without food
   - Tries to get Wukong's attention by complaining — backfires, Wukong becomes annoyed
   - Another monkey delivers minimal food/water without explanation
   - MK too desperate to question it; drinks and eats greedily

4. **Moral Crisis**: MK struggles to reconcile childhood stories of heroic Wukong with the cruel captor he faces. Wonders: is there still good in him? Has he changed? Feels confused, scared, profoundly alone.

5. **Failed Escape Attempt**: Hears monkeys partying and feasting nearby. Desperately tries to break magical barrier and steal food. Fails completely—barrier too strong, he too weak. Returns to cage, still starving.

6. **Final Negotiation**: MK begs Wukong for food/water, using logic: "You can't use me as insurance if I starve to death." Wukong is visibly tired of MK's pleas. Eventually gives small bowl of water and stale bread—warns MK not to push his luck.

## Character Dynamics

- **MK's Strategy**: Uses flattery ("you're a legend"), logical appeals to self-interest, and emotional pleas. Tries manipulation but is ultimately powerless.
- **Wukong**: Motivated by need for diamond snare (reason unclear). Keeps MK alive but punishes through deprivation. Becomes increasingly annoyed by complaints but reluctantly provides minimum sustenance to keep MK alive.
- **The Legendary Gap**: MK knows stories of Wukong as hero/savior. Observes he's cruel, callous, seemingly without compassion. Questions whether goodness remains or if he's fundamentally changed.

## Setting & Atmosphere

- MK trapped behind magical barrier
- Wukong can summon items (snaps fingers to produce food/water)
- Other monkeys serve Wukong, carry out orders
- Monkeys have feasts/parties while MK starves—he can hear them
- Barrier impenetrable; MK too weak to break it
- Time passes in slow, painful isolation

## Themes

- Survival through persuasion and flattery
- Disillusionment with heroes and myths
- Power imbalance and captivity psychology
- Hunger as physical and psychological torment
- Question: can a cruel person still possess goodness inside?
