## Interdisciplinary vs Transdisciplinary Scholarship

### Interdisciplinary Scholarship
- Involves bringing together different disciplines to study a particular topic or problem
- Synthesizes insights and methods from multiple fields to gain more comprehensive understanding
- Example: interdisciplinary study of climate change draws on atmospheric science, economics, sociology, political science
- Focused on understanding a problem through multiple lenses

### Transdisciplinary Scholarship
- Goes beyond interdisciplinarity by actively involving stakeholders and practitioners from various fields in research process
- Collaborative approach with multiple disciplines working together to co-create knowledge and solve real-world problems
- Aims to bridge gap between academic research and practical application
- Goal is to achieve deeper understanding of problem and generate implementable solutions

### Distinction
In simple terms: interdisciplinary scholarship combines different disciplines to study a problem, while transdisciplinary scholarship combines different disciplines to solve a problem.

### Example: Ecocriticism
Ecocriticism is an interdisciplinary field (literature, environmental studies, cultural studies) that can also be seen as transdisciplinary if it aims to generate solutions and engage stakeholders to address environmental issues.
