## Loyalty Program Plan

The user is implementing a customer loyalty program with the following structure:

**Program Type:**
- Points-based system
- Customers earn points for each purchase
- Redeemable for discounts or free products

**Tier Structure:**
- Different tier levels for frequent customers to unlock exclusive rewards
- Tiered progression based on purchase frequency or amount spent

**Sign-Up Incentive:**
- Planning to offer sign-up bonuses to encourage program participation
- Considering options: welcome points, discount on first purchase, free product, or exclusive access

**Promotion & Implementation:**
- Social media promotion
- Email newsletters
- In-store promotions
- Staff training: checkout staff will mention the program to customers
- Clear instructions provided for how to join

**Goals:**
- Increase repeat sales
- Build customer loyalty
- Encourage word-of-mouth marketing
