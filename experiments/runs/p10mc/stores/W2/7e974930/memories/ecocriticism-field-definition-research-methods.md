## Ecocriticism

Ecocriticism is an interdisciplinary field of study that examines the relationship between literature and the environment. It explores the ways in which literature reflects, shapes, and challenges our perceptions of the natural world, and the ways in which our relationship to the environment is represented in literature. It also considers the environmental impact of literary production and consumption. Draws on literary theory, environmental science, cultural studies, and philosophy.

## Research Methods of Ecocritical Scholarship

1. **Close reading** — Detailed analysis of language, imagery, and literary techniques to explore representation of the natural world and human-environment relationship
2. **Historical and cultural context analysis** — Placing texts within historical/cultural contexts to understand how they reflect and shape societal attitudes toward the environment
3. **Environmental science and ecology analysis** — Drawing on environmental science knowledge to understand representation of the natural world in literature
4. **Comparative analysis** — Comparing texts across historical periods and cultural contexts to track how nature representation and human-environment relationships have evolved
5. **Ethnographic and fieldwork** — Studying relationship between people and environment through field observation and interviews
6. **Discourse analysis** — Analyzing language and discourse in literary texts and cultural productions to understand how they construct and reinforce views of the environment
