## Yoga Practice Integration

### Regular Practice
- Started yoga twice a week on **March 1, 2023**
- Found it helpful for injury prevention and flexibility

### 30-Day Yoga Challenge
- Completed daily yoga practice for 30 days in **April 2023**
- Practiced every single day, even on running rest days
- Described as tough but very beneficial
- Notable improvements: overall recovery and flexibility

### Recommended Yoga Styles & Resources
- Beginner-friendly: Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin
- Advanced: Ashtanga, Power Yoga
- Key poses for triathletes: Downward-Facing Dog, Warrior II, Tree Pose, Seated Forward Fold
- Recommended apps: YogaGlo, Peloton, Down Dog
- Recommendation: 1-2 sessions per week, 30-45 minutes, focus on shoulders, hips, IT band

### Benefits Observed
- Enhanced recovery
- Improved flexibility and range of motion
- Better mental resilience and focus
- Improved balance and body awareness
