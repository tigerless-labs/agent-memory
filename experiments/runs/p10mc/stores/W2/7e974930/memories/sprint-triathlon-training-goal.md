## Sprint Triathlon Training

User is currently training for a sprint triathlon. Has a background as a marathon runner with good aerobic base but needs to focus on swimming technique and efficiency for the triathlon.

### Key Recommendations Received
- Focus on technique: keep body horizontal, engage core, use hips for power
- Build endurance gradually: start with 25-50 meters, increase distances
- Incorporate drills: kicking drills, arm circles, catch-up drill
- Strength training: shoulder rotations, lat pull-downs, planks, Russian twists
- Practice open-water swimming (sighting, wetsuit comfort)
- Swim-specific workouts: sprint intervals (25-50m high intensity), endurance sets (200-400m moderate pace), brick workouts

### Sample Swim Workouts Recommended
- Warm-up: easy swimming mix + dynamic stretching (5-10 min)
- Drills: kicking (3×25m), arm circles (3×25m)
- Endurance: 200-400m moderate pace
- Sprint intervals: 25m high intensity, 15-25s rest, 8-12 reps
- Cool-down: easy swimming + static stretching (5-10 min)

### Tracking & Apps
Uses spreadsheet for progress tracking. Also recommended:
- Strava
- Swim.com
- Garmin
