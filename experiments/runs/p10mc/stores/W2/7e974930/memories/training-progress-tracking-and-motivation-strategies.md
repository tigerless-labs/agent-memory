## Training Progress Tracking & Motivation

### Tracking Method
- Uses personal spreadsheet to log workouts
- Records: date, distance, time, how felt during workout
- Allows pattern identification and progress visualization

### Recommended Tracking Apps
- Strava
- Swim.com
- Garmin

### Motivation Strategies Recommended
1. Set specific, achievable goals (break larger goals into smaller objectives)
2. Find a workout buddy or join swim groups
3. Reward yourself with non-food rewards (new swim cap, massage, etc.)
4. Visualize success (imagine crossing finish line)
5. Stay positive and patient with progress
6. Get adequate rest and recovery (sleep critical)
7. Follow triathletes and fitness enthusiasts on social media for inspiration
8. Celebrate small wins along the way
9. Maintain consistency (at least 2-3 swims per week)
10. Mix up workouts to avoid boredom and plateaus
11. Consider working with a swim coach or triathlon coach

### Key Insight
Tracking progress on spreadsheet proved to be "really helpful to see how far you've come"
