**Fujifilm X-T30 II Technical Specifications**

**Autofocus System:**
- Intelligent Hybrid AF (phase-detection + contrast-detection)
- 425 phase-detection points covering ~100% of frame
- Response time: 0.02 seconds
- Accuracy: ~99% success rate per Fujifilm testing
- Face and eye detection with AI subject tracking
- Low-light performance: minimum -3EV
- Customizable AF modes: zone focusing, wide/tracking, single-point

**Battery Life:**
- Official rating: 380 frames per charge (EVF), 440 frames (LCD)
- Real-world: 300-400 frames per charge
- Power-saving features: Power Save mode, Economy mode, Auto Power Off
- Can extend with USB-C power bank or external battery grip

**Weather Resistance:**
- NOT fully weather-sealed
- "Moisture-resistant" only (Fujifilm terminology)
- Protects against light rain/mist but less robust than X-T3/X-H1
- Requires regular cleaning in harsh conditions

**Kit Lens Option:**
- Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ
- Price: $499
- Compact and lightweight
- Versatile focal range

**Price:** $899 body only
