## Festival-Level Initiatives

### Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI)
- Major festivals (Sundance, Toronto, Berlin) have established DEI committees
- Ensure programming, juries, and staff reflect industry diversity

### Inclusive Programming
- Actively seeking films by women, people of color, LGBTQ+ filmmakers, and filmmakers with disabilities
- Showcasing underrepresented voices and perspectives

### Accessibility Initiatives
- Wheelchair-accessible venues
- Audio descriptions and closed captions
- Making events accessible to attendees with disabilities

### Mentorship and Development
- Sundance Institute's Native American and Indigenous Film Program
- Development programs supporting emerging filmmakers from underrepresented groups

## Production Company Initiatives

### Structural Changes
- Diversity and inclusion departments at major studios (Warner Bros., Universal, Netflix, Disney, HBO)
- Unconscious bias training programs

### Inclusion Riders
- Jordan Peele's Monkeypaw Productions incorporating inclusion riders into contracts
- Ensuring diverse hiring practices and on-set representation

### Diverse Hiring Initiatives
- Targeted recruitment programs
- Companies like Disney and HBO implementing specific diversity hiring practices

### Content Development
- Netflix's "Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion" development program
- Creating content by and featuring underrepresented voices

## Industry-Wide Movements

### The 4% Challenge
- Started by actress/producer Frances McDormand
- Goal to increase number of women directors hired in industry

### Time's Up
- Movement by women in entertainment to address sexual harassment, assault, gender inequality

### The Black List
- Annual list of best unproduced screenplays by writers of color
- Has helped launch careers of many underrepresented writers

### Sundance Institute's Inclusion Initiative
- Resources, support, and funding for underrepresented filmmakers
- Focus on women, people of color, and filmmakers with disabilities

## Key Advocacy Points
- Need for more representation behind camera, especially directors and writers
- Essential to provide film education and training opportunities for underrepresented groups
- Diverse voices telling diverse stories is crucial for industry growth
