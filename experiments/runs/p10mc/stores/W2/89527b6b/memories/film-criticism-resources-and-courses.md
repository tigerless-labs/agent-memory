## Online Resources (Favorite Publications)
1. **RogerEbert.com** — reviews, essays, criticism from renowned critics including Roger Ebert
2. **The A.V. Club** — reviews, interviews, features from experienced critics
3. **The Film Comment** — in-depth film criticism, interviews, essays
4. **Cinema Scope** — international cinema, critical essays and reviews
5. **The Guardian's Film Section** — reviews, interviews, features from film critics

## Online Courses
1. **Film Criticism 101** (Coursera) — University of Pennsylvania course on basics of film criticism
2. **Film Studies: An Introduction** (edX) — University of Toronto course on film history, theory, and criticism
3. **The Art of Film Criticism** (MasterClass) — taught by film critic Leonard Maltin
4. **Film Analysis and Criticism** (Udemy) — film analysis, criticism, and review writing
5. **Writing About Film** (FutureLearn) — University of East Anglia course on film criticism and writing

## Foundational Books on Film Criticism
1. **"I Lost It at the Movies"** by Pauline Kael — classic collection of reviews and essays
2. **"The Film Criticism of Otis Ferguson"** — collection of essays and reviews from influential critic
3. **"Film Criticism: A Very Short Introduction"** by Andrew Sarris — concise introduction to film criticism
4. **"The Critic's Craft"** by Gerald Peary — explores art of film criticism and critic's role

## Key Insight
- Film critics play vital role in helping audiences discover new films and emerging filmmakers
- Critics' opinions are subjective but significantly influence a film's success and box office performance
- In-depth analysis and thoughtful critique are valued over simplistic reviews
