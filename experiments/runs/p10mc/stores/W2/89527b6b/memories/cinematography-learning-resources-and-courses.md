## Online Resources
1. **American Cinematographer** — official ASC website with articles, interviews, tutorials
2. **No Film School** — articles, tutorials, interviews on cinematography and lighting
3. **Cinematography Database** — comprehensive database of cinematographers and techniques
4. **The Cinematography Podcast** — interviews with professional cinematographers
5. **Filmmaker IQ** — in-depth tutorials, articles, courses on cinematography

## Online Courses
1. **MasterClass: Cinematography** — taught by Oscar-winning cinematographer Roger Deakins (known for Blade Runner 2049, No Country for Old Men)
2. **Cinematography Fundamentals** (Udemy) — beginner's course on camera angles, lighting, composition
3. **Cinematography: Shooting for Film and Video** (Coursera) — UCLA course on cinematography principles
4. **The Art of Cinematography** (LinkedIn Learning) — creative and technical aspects
5. **Cinematography with a Camera** (Skillshare) — practical techniques using a camera

## YouTube Channels
1. **Every Frame a Painting** — cinematography and filmmaking technique analysis
2. **The Film Theorists** — film technique analysis
3. **Cinematography Tutorials 360** — practical cinematography tutorials and tips
4. **Indie Film Hustle** — tutorials, interviews, independent filmmaking insights
5. **Film Riot** — tutorials, gear reviews, filmmaking tips

## Learning Approach
- Watch favorite films multiple times, focusing on camera angles, lighting, composition
- Analyze how technical elements contribute to mood and storytelling
