## Quick Chicken Recipes Recommended (May 2023)

1. **Chicken Fajitas** - sauteed chicken with peppers, onions, fajita seasoning, flour/corn tortillas
2. **Chicken Tenders with Honey Mustard** - breaded and baked/fried, honey + Dijon mustard + lemon sauce
3. **Chicken Quesadillas** - shredded cooked chicken mixed with cheese in tortillas
4. **Lemon Garlic Chicken** - marinated in lemon, olive oil, garlic, herbs; grilled or baked with roasted vegetables
5. **Korean-Style Fried Chicken** - double-fried with gochugaru spicing, served with sweet/spicy sauce
6. **Chicken Caesar Wraps** - grilled chicken wrapped in tortilla with romaine, parmesan, Caesar dressing
7. **Chicken and Veggie Kabobs** - skewered chicken, peppers, onions, mushrooms; brushed with olive oil and grilled
8. **Buffalo Chicken Tenders** - breaded/fried, tossed in buffalo sauce, served with blue cheese and celery
9. **Chicken and Avocado Salad** - grilled chicken with avocado, red onion, citrus vinaigrette
10. **Chicken Tikka Masala** - yogurt-spice marinated chicken, grilled, with creamy tomato sauce and basmati rice

All recipes designed to be quick and easy for weeknight cooking.
