## Background
User once worked in a team where people kept interrupting each other during meetings. Looking for ways to prevent and manage this in future teams.

## Key concern
Particular challenge: how to address interruptions when the person interrupting is the team leader or manager, without creating tension or appearing to undermine their authority.

## Strategies discussed
- **Virtual meetings**: use mute button, hand-raise feature, visual cues for turn-taking
- **Ground rules**: establish clear expectations about not interrupting before meetings start
- **Active listening**: encourage team members to listen carefully
- **Addressing the behavior**: 
  - If peer: can address more directly in meeting
  - If manager/leader: address privately, be tactful, don't accuse, suggest alternatives like taking notes while others speak
  - Get support from colleagues if needed

## Notes
User mentioned they would try the mute button approach in virtual meetings. No concrete follow-up decision or outcome recorded.
