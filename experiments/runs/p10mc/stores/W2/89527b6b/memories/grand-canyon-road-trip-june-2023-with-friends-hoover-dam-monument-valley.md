## Grand Canyon Road Trip — June 2023

**Trip Details:**
- Planned for June 2023 (next month from May 21, 2023)
- Traveling with friends
- Base: Mather Campground

**Major Stops:**

### Hoover Dam (2-4 hours recommended)
- Power plant guided tour (30 min)
- Visitor Center
- Viewpoints on Arizona and Nevada sides
- Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge views
- Colorado River Riverwalk Trail
- Peak summer heat — bring water, sun protection
- Security screening required
- Time zone: Pacific (one hour behind Grand Canyon)

### Scenic Route: Williams to Grand Canyon

**Route Options:**
1. **Oak Creek Canyon Scenic Byway (Hwy 89A)** — paved, winding, red rock formations
2. **Old Route 66 (Hwy 180)** — historic, through Valle and Tusayan
3. **Forest Road 305** — unpaved, Coconino National Forest, high-clearance vehicle needed
4. **Babbit Ranch Road (FR 307)** — unpaved, historic ranching area
5. **Desert View Drive (Hwy 64)** — paved, Kaibab National Forest, best for canyon views

**Intermediate Stops:**
- Lake Mead National Recreation Area (Lake Mead Overlook, Boulder Harbor)
- Kingman, Arizona — Route 66 Museum
- Hackberry General Store — old-fashioned stop on Route 66
- Valentine, Arizona — Keepers of the Wild Nature Park
- Williams, Arizona — historic downtown, Williams Depot museum
- Flagstaff, Arizona — Lowell Observatory

### Desert View Watchtower
- **Design:** 70-foot stone tower designed by Mary Colter (1932), built by Fred Harvey Company
- **Views:** 360-degree panoramic views of Grand Canyon, Colorado River
- **Art:** Interior murals and paintings by Hopi artist Fred Kabotie, Native American artifacts
- **Amenities:** Gift shop, small café (coffee, snacks, sandwiches)
- **Accessibility:** Wheelchair accessible on lower levels; observation deck not accessible
- **Best for:** Sunset/sunrise viewing, photography
- **Crowds:** Limited parking in peak season — overflow lot with shuttle

### Monument Valley from Grand Canyon
- **Driving time:** 4–5 hours
- **Main Route:** Highway 64 East → Cameron → Highway 89 North → Page → Highway 163 North
- **Scenic Route (through Navajo Nation):** Highway 64 East → Tuba City → Highway 160 East → Kayenta → Highway 163 North
- **Cameron Trading Post:** Historic, operating since 1916 — worth stopping
- **Activities:** Navajo-guided tours, scenic loop road, Forrest Gump Point, the Mittens viewpoint, Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park Visitor Center
- **Respect:** Sacred Navajo land — be mindful of local culture and environment

## Restaurants near Mather Campground

**Inside Grand Canyon National Park:**
- El Tovar Dining Room (~1 mile) — upscale Southwestern, reservations recommended
- Bright Angel Restaurant (~1.5 miles) — American comfort food, breakfast
- Canyon Café (~2 miles) — casual, quick-service, breakfast/sandwiches

**Grand Canyon Village:**
- Grand Canyon Village Marketplace (~2.5 miles) — deli
- We Cook Pizza (~3 miles) — pizza, salads, sandwiches
- Canyon Coffee and Café (~3 miles) — coffee, pastries

**Tusayan (~7 miles)** — Mexican, Italian, American options
