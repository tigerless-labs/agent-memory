## Akkadian
- **Language family**: Semitic
- **Region**: Mesopotamia
- **Writing**: First Semitic language written in cuneiform
- **Important works**: Code of Hammurabi
- **Significance**: Widely used during Babylonian and Assyrian Empires

## Egyptian
- **Preservation**: One of the oldest and most well-preserved ancient languages
- **Writing system**: Hieroglyphics (symbolic writing)
- **Time period**: ~3200 BC to 400 AD
- **Text coverage**: Religious beliefs and practices, literature, history

## Hittite
- **Language family**: Indo-European (one of the oldest known Indo-European languages)
- **Region**: Anatolia (modern-day Turkey)
- **Time period**: Hittite Empire era
- **Writing**: Cuneiform

## Old Hebrew
- **Language of**: The Hebrews in early first millennium BC
- **Earliest inscriptions**: 10th century BC
- **Major text**: Hebrew Bible (important to Judaism and Christianity)

## Significance
These languages and their surviving texts offer a window into ancient cultures, beliefs, and ways of life, helping us understand the development of writing and history of the ancient Near East.
