User is exploring whether to hire a personal trainer after completing their earned session. Key criteria for selection:

- Nationally recognized certification (ACE, NASM, NSCA)
- Specialization matching their fitness goals
- Personality and communication style that motivates
- Compatible availability and scheduling
- Reasonable cost and package deals
- Offers free consultation or trial session
- Experience with similar fitness goals

User values trainer who makes them feel comfortable, motivated, and supported.
