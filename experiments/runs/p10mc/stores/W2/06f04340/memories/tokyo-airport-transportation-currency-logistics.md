## Airport Transportation

### From Narita Airport (NRT)
- **Narita Express (N'EX)**: ~60–70 min to Shinjuku, ¥3,000–4,000; then taxi (~¥1,000) or 10–15 min walk to hotel
- **Keisei Skyliner**: ~60–70 min (transfer at Nippori to JR Yamanote Line), ¥2,500–3,500
- **Airport Limousine Bus**: ~90–120 min to Shinjuku Station West Exit, ¥2,000–3,000
- **Taxi**: ~60–90 min, ¥15,000–20,000

### From Haneda Airport (HND)
- **Tokyo Monorail**: ~30–40 min to Hamamatsucho, then transfer to JR Yamanote to Shinjuku, ¥500–1,000
- **Keikyu Line**: ~30–40 min to Shinbashi, then transfer to JR Yamanote to Shinjuku, ¥500–1,000
- **Airport Limousine Bus**: ~30–60 min to Shinjuku Station West Exit, ¥1,000–2,000
- **Taxi**: ~30–60 min, ¥5,000–10,000

## Currency & Payment
- **Prepaid cards**: Suica or Pasmo (available at airport or train stations); works on trains, buses, subway throughout Tokyo
- **Late arrival/heavy luggage**: Taxi most convenient (though pricier)
- **ATM withdrawal**: Foreign transaction fees + ATM operator fees; daily withdrawal limits may apply

## Budget Breakdown (¥50,000)
- **Mid-range restaurant meals**: ¥2,000–5,000 per meal
- **Convenience store snacks/drinks**: ¥500–1,000 per item
- **Tokyo Metro/JR train fares**: ¥200–400 per ride
- **Souvenirs/small purchases**: ¥500–2,000 per item
- **Tip**: Keep smaller bills (¥1,000, ¥2,000, ¥5,000) for convenience stores, vending machines, small transactions
