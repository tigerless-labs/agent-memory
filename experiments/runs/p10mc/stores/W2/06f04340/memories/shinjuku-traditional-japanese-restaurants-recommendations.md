## Recommended Shinjuku Restaurants

### 1. Tofuya Ukai
- **Address**: 2-14-3 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 5-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: Tofu cuisine (hot pot, skewers, kaiseki multi-course)
- **Style**: Traditional

### 2. Kyubey Ginza
- **Address**: 3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 10-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: High-quality Edomae-style sushi
- **Price**: Reasonable

### 3. Toriki
- **Address**: 2-14-1 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 5-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: Tempura, soba, udon noodles
- **Atmosphere**: Cozy, family-run, intimate
- **Price**: Reasonable

### 4. Kappo Nakajima
- **Address**: 3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 10-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: Seasonal omakase (chef's selection) course
- **Style**: High-end kappo-style

### 5. Han no Daidokoro Bettei
- **Address**: 3-2-12 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 10-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: Traditional Japanese kaiseki (multi-course seasonal meal)
- **Style**: Luxurious, elegant setting

### 6. Tsurutontan
- **Address**: 3-28-10 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku
- **Distance**: 15-minute walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building
- **Specialty**: Traditional Japanese noodles (udon, soba, tempura soba)
- **Atmosphere**: Fun, lively
- **Price**: Wide range of options

## Booking Tips
- Reservations highly recommended, especially peak hours/weekends
- Online platforms: Chope, TableCheck
- Alternative: Ask hotel concierge for assistance
