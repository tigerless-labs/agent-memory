Freelance writing project for a client with a small business selling eco-friendly cleaning products.

**Key selling points developed:**
- Natural ingredients (safe, gentle)
- Environmental benefits (reduced carbon footprint)
- Health protection (family and employees safety)
- Effectiveness comparable to chemical-laden products
- Positioning as part of environmental movement

**Copywriting approach:** Contrast traditional harsh chemicals against eco-friendly alternative, positioning natural ingredients as both safer and equally effective.
