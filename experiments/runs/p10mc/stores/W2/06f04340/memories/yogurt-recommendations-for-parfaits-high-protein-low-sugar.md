## Yogurt Options for Parfaits

User criteria: high protein, low sugar, creamy texture for yogurt parfaits.

### Greek Yogurt (15-20g protein per 6oz)
- **Fage Total Greek Yogurt** (plain, unflavored)
- **Chobani Non-Fat Plain Greek Yogurt**
- **Siggi's Non-Fat Plain Greek Yogurt**

### Icelandic Yogurt / Skyr (17-20g protein per 6oz)
- **Siggi's Icelandic-Style Yogurt** (thick, creamy)
- **Icelandic Provisions Yogurt**

### Non-Dairy Coconut Yogurt (10-15g protein per 6oz)
- **So Delicious Coconut Yogurt** (lactose-free option)
- **Kite Hill Coconut Yogurt** (minimal added sugars)
- **Coconut Bliss Yogurt**

**Recommendation**: Check ingredient lists and nutrition labels; experiment to find preferred brand and flavor.
