## Lavender Cocktail Experimentation

**Lavender Syrup Recipe:**
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds

Instructions:
1. Combine sugar and water, heat until sugar dissolves
2. Add lavender buds, remove from heat
3. Steep for 10-15 minutes until cool to room temperature
4. Strain through fine-mesh sieve
5. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks

**Cocktail Recipes to Try:**

1. **Lavender Gin Fizz**
   - 2 oz Hendrick's gin
   - 1 oz lavender syrup
   - 1 oz lemon juice
   - 1/2 oz simple syrup
   - Top with soda water
   - Garnish: lemon wheel + lavender sprig

2. **Lavender Dream**
   - 2 oz Hendrick's gin
   - 1 oz lavender syrup
   - 1 oz lemon juice
   - 1/2 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey + water)
   - Strain into coupe glass
   - Garnish: lavender sprig

3. **Lavender Bee's Knees**
   - 2 oz Hendrick's gin
   - 1 oz lavender syrup
   - 1 oz honey syrup
   - Strain into rocks glass with ice
   - Garnish: lemon wheel + lavender sprig

**Usage Tips:**
- Start with small amount of syrup; lavender overpowers quickly
- Pair with citrus (lemon, grapefruit) to balance floral flavor
- Experiment with different lavender types (English vs French) for varying profiles
- Consider lavender bitters or lavender-infused spirits for depth
