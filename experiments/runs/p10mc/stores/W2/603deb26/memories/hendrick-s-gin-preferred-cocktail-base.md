## Hendrick's Gin — Preferred Cocktail Base

**Properties:**
- Unique blend of botanicals featuring rose and cucumber notes
- Excellent for pairing with fresh herbs from garden
- Versatile base for experimentation

**Cocktails Using Hendrick's:**
1. Negroni (with Carpano Antica vermouth, experimenting with various bitter liqueurs)
2. White Negroni (with Suze and Dolin Dry Vermouth)
3. Lavender cocktails (Lavender Gin Fizz, Lavender Dream, Lavender Bee's Knees)
4. Cucumber-Basil Refresher
5. Garden Gimlet (with mint)
6. Lemon-Thyme Fizz
7. Elderflower Collins
8. Mint to Be
9. Various citrus-based combinations

**Why Preferred:**
- Rose and cucumber notes complement lavender and fresh herb flavors
- Botanical complexity enhances floral pairings
- Works well with both light (citrus, herbs) and bold (bitter liqueurs) flavors
- User has invested significant time experimenting to maximize its potential

**Active Experiments:**
- Negroni variations with different bitter liqueurs
- White Negroni with dry vermouth and dry bitter liqueurs
- Lavender syrup cocktails with Hendrick's base
- Citrus and fresh herb combinations
