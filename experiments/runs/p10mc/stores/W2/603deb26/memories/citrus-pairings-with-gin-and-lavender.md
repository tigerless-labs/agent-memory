## Citrus Pairings with Gin and Lavender

**Recommended Citrus Fruits:**

1. **Orange** (especially blood oranges)
   - Classic pairing with lavender
   - Sweetness complements floral notes
   - Bitterness of gin provides balance
   - Blood oranges add deeper, more complex flavor

2. **Yuzu**
   - Japanese citrus (cross between grapefruit and orange)
   - Tartness + subtle sweetness pair beautifully with gin and lavender
   - Adds unique, exotic flavor profile

3. **Kumquat**
   - Small, sweet citrus fruit
   - Excellent with gin and lavender pairing
   - Try using kumquat syrup or muddle fresh kumquats
   - Sweetness balances bitterness of gin

4. **Satsuma**
   - Type of mandarin orange, sweet and tart
   - Easy-to-peel skin
   - Perfect for burst of citrus flavor
   - Nicely complements gin and lavender sweetness

5. **Bergamot**
   - Often used in tea and perfumery
   - More subtle citrus flavor than others
   - Adds lovely complexity to gin cocktails
   - Citrus flavor helps cut through gin richness

**General Tips:**
- Always use fresh citrus for brightest, most vibrant flavor
- Experiment with different varieties to find perfect fit
- Balance citrus carefully to avoid overpowering other flavors
- Use citrus twists and peels for added aroma and flavor depth
- Start small and adjust to taste
