Honey syrup recipe for cocktails:
- **Basic ratio**: 1 part honey to 1 water (can adjust to taste)
- **Process**: Heat over low heat, whisk until honey dissolves, avoid boiling/burning, cool before use
- **Storage**: Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks
- **Variations**: Can infuse with citrus peel, herbs, spices, ginger (try honey-ginger for spicy-sweet flavor)
- **Honey types**: Clover, manuka, or acacia honey work well

Used in cocktails like Bee's Knees and other recipes.
