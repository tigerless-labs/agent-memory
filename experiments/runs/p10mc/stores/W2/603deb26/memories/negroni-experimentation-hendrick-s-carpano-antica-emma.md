## Negroni Experimentation

**Base Recipe Preferences:**
- Hendrick's gin (preferred for its rose and cucumber notes)
- Carpano Antica vermouth (creates excellent flavor combination)
- Searching for perfect bitter liqueur balance

**History:**
- Emma taught the user how to make Negroni in 2023
- User has made it 5 times since then, getting closer to ideal recipe each time

**Bitter Liqueur Alternatives Explored:**
1. Cynar (artichoke-based, vegetal, slightly sweet) — try 1/2 oz Cynar + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica
2. Aperol (lighter, citrus-forward) — try 1 oz Aperol + 1 oz Carpano Antica
3. Luxardo Bitter (intense, herbal) — try 1/2 oz Luxardo + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica
4. Suze (French gentian, crisp, earthy) — try 1 oz Suze + 1 oz Carpano Antica
5. Meletti (slightly sweeter, citrus-forward) — try 1 oz Meletti + 1 oz Carpano Antica

**White Negroni Interest:**
- User wants to try this variation to showcase gin and bitter liqueur
- Recipe: 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin + 0.5 oz Suze + 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth, stirred over ice, garnished with orange/lemon twist
- Key tips: dry bitter liqueur, dry vermouth, balance gin:bitter:vermouth ratio, avoid over-dilution

**Next Steps:**
- Test different bitter liqueurs one at a time
- Experiment with White Negroni ratio variations (2:1:1 vs 3:1:1)
