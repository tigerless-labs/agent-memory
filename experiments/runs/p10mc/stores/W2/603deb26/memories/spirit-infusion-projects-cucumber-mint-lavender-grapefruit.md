User planning to infuse spirits with various flavors:
- **Cucumber and mint gin**: Planning to infuse these separately to control flavor intensity
- **Lavender gin**: Using dried lavender buds (1/4 cup per liter), 2-3 day infusion
- **Grapefruit gin**: Using sliced grapefruit, 2-3 day infusion
- **Lavender syrup**: 1/4 cup dried lavender buds, 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, steeped 10-15 minutes

All infusions to be stored in cool, dark place and strained through cheesecloth or coffee filter before use.
