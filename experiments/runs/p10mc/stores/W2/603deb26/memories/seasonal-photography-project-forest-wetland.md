## Seasonal Photography Project

**Decision Date**: August 2023

**Project Scope**:
- Document changing seasons in a natural setting (forest or wetland)
- Visit a specific location regularly throughout the year
- Capture the same scene at the same time each year to see transformation over time
- Combine with wildlife/nature photography theme

**Why This Project**:
- Loves how light and colors change throughout the year
- Great opportunity to experiment with different techniques and compositions
- Interest in capturing beauty of the natural world
- Long-term commitment provides motivation and skill development
