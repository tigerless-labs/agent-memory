## Night Cityscape Photography Tips

**Camera Settings**:
- Manual Mode for full exposure control
- ISO: 100-400 to minimize noise
- Aperture: f/2.8 or lower for shallow depth of field
- Shutter Speed: 15-30 seconds for long exposure
- White Balance: Daylight or Auto to capture warm city light tones

**Composition**:
- Find high vantage points (rooftops, hills, observation decks) for sweeping views
- Use leading lines (roads, sidewalks, building edges) to guide viewer's eye
- Exploit symmetry (reflections in water/glass) for visual interest
- Frame shots with architectural elements (archways, columns) for depth

**Techniques**:
- Long Exposure: Use remote shutter release or self-timer to minimize shake
- Bracketing: Multiple exposures merged with HDR software for wider dynamic range
- Panning: Follow moving objects for blurred background, sharp subject
- Intentional Camera Movement: Move camera during exposure for abstract effects

**Essential Equipment & Practices**:
- Tripod required for stability
- RAW format for maximum post-processing flexibility
- Scout location beforehand for best vantage points
- Shoot during blue hour (hour after sunset) when city lights turn on and sky has daylight hints - creates warm, beautiful atmosphere

**Post-Processing**:
- Adjust colors, contrast, mood during RAW processing
