## Mobile Photo Editing Apps for Quick Adjustments

**Top Picks for Exposure & Color**:

1. **Lightroom Mobile** (iOS, Android) - Free + subscription
   - Powerful Adobe tool, advanced editing
   - Non-destructive, syncs with desktop Lightroom
   
2. **VSCO** (iOS, Android) - Free + IAP
   - Film-inspired presets
   - Exposure, contrast, color adjustments
   
3. **Snapseed** (iOS, Android) - Free (Google)
   - Professional-grade
   - Selective adjustments, layers, masking
   
4. **Adobe Photoshop Express** (iOS, Android) - Free
   - Streamlined desktop version
   - Quick exposure, color, contrast fixes
   
5. **Polarr** (iOS, Android) - Free + IAP
   - AI-powered quick adjustments
   - Exposure, color, contrast

**For Specific Tasks**:

- **Object Removal**: TouchRetouch ($1.99 iOS, $2.99 Android)
- **Portrait Retouching**: Facetune ($3.99 iOS, $2.99 Android) - smooth skin, whiten teeth
- **Comprehensive Tools**: PicsArt (Free + IAP) - filters, effects, text overlays
- **Advanced Editing**: Filterstorm ($2.99 iOS) - curves, layers, masking
- **Filters & Presets**: Afterlight ($2.99 iOS, $1.99 Android)

**User-Friendly & Fast**: All are designed for quick, on-the-go editing before social media sharing.
