## Farm Social Media Expansion (August 2023)

**Platforms to consider:**
- Instagram (visual storytelling)
- Facebook (updates and events)
- Twitter (frequent short updates)

**Content strategy:**
- Share high-quality photos/videos of farm, products, activities
- Post farm stories, values, mission
- Share recipes, farming tips, behind-the-scenes content
- Use relevant hashtags
- Post 3-5 times per week
- Feature user-generated content from customers

**Engagement plan:**
- Respond to comments/messages/reviews promptly
- Interact with audience to build community
- Monitor analytics to track performance
- Use hashtags to increase visibility

**Setup steps:**
- Create business account
- Complete profile with farm info and logo
- Post introductory announcement
- Begin sharing content regularly
