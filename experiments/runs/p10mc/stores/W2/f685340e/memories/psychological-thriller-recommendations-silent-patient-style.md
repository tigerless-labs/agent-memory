**Core recommendations (strongest matches to "The Silent Patient" style):**
1. "Gone Girl" by Gillian Flynn - twisty marriage mystery
2. "The Girl on the Train" by Paula Hawkins - obsession and unreliable perception
3. "The Last Mrs. Parrish" by Liv Constantine - morally ambiguous protagonist, dark humor, social commentary on wealth/privilege
4. "The Wife Between Us" by Greer Hendricks and Sarah Pekkanen - unreliable narrators, marriage facade, expert twist
5. "The Couple Next Door" by Shari Lapena - baby goes missing, family secrets
6. "The Woman in the Window" by A.J. Finn - Hitchcock-inspired, recluse witnesses crime
7. "Sharp Objects" by Gillian Flynn - journalist, hometown murders, dark secrets
8. "The Poison Tree" by Erin Kelly - family secrets and lies
9. "The Devil in the White City" by Erik Larson - historical psychological thriller, H.H. Holmes
10. "The 7 1/2 Deaths of Evelyn Hardcastle" by Stuart Turton - unique premise, multiple bodies, murder mystery

**Why "The Last Mrs. Parrish" and "The Wife Between Us" stand out:**
- Complex, morally ambiguous characters
- Psychological mind games between characters
- Twisty plots with unexpected reveals
- Dark humor and social commentary (especially Parrish)
- Unreliable narrators (especially Wife Between Us)
