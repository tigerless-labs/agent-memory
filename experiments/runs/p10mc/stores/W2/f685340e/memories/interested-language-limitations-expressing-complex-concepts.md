User is interested in the philosophical question: do language limitations affect our ability to understand complex concepts or experiences?

Key observations:
- Some experiences are difficult to describe in words (falling in love, sensation of pain)
- Some concepts are hard to express in single words or phrases
- This can result in misunderstandings or misinterpretations

However, humans have developed ways to circumvent language limitations:
- Use metaphors and analogies to convey abstract ideas
- Use nonverbal cues and physical gestures to communicate meaning
- Language evolves continuously as speakers create or adapt new terms and expressions
- New words and expressions develop to better reflect nuances of experiences and ideas

User interested in ongoing evolution of language as a dynamic response to communicative needs and limitations.
