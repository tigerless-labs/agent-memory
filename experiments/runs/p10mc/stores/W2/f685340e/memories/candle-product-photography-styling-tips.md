## Candle Product Photography & Styling

### Lighting & Basics
- Use natural light near windows with soft, indirect light
- Shoot at different angles for visual interest (directly above, angled, slightly from above)
- Use shallow depth of field to blur background and emphasize candle
- Shoot during golden hour for warm, soft summer light
- High-resolution images (at least 1000px on longest side)

### Styling for Eco-Friendly Candles
**Props:**
- Nature-inspired: pinecones, shells, pebbles, dried leaves
- Botanicals: fresh/dried flowers, branches, eucalyptus, rosemary
- Reclaimed wood pieces
- Earth-toned fabrics (linen, cotton, burlap)
- Potted plants (succulents, rosemary sprigs)
- Vintage/repurposed items

**Backdrops:**
- Natural textures: wood, stone, woven baskets
- Kraft paper or recycled paper
- Natural fiber fabrics (linen, cotton)
- Reclaimed wood planks or old wooden door
- Nature-inspired wallpaper

**Color Palette:**
- Earth tones: sage green, sandy beige, mossy green
- Soft pastels: pale pink, baby blue, mint green
- Monochromatic color schemes

### Summer-Themed Candle Photo Shoot
**Color Palette:**
- Pastel hues (pale pink, baby blue, mint green)
- Warm neutrals (beige, sandy white, weathered wood)
- Vibrant accents (coral, yellow, orange)

**Summer Props:**
- Fresh/artificial summer flowers (daisies, sunflowers, gerbera daisies)
- Seashells, driftwood, beachy elements
- Summer fruits (lemons, limes, berries)
- Natural textiles (linen, cotton, canvas)

**Summer Backdrops:**
- Light-colored wood
- Pastel-colored paper/paint
- Lightweight cotton or linen fabrics

**Summer Styling Tips:**
- Keep it light and airy with minimal props
- Play with negative space for breathability
- Capture warm golden hour light
- For lavender/lemon specifically: dried lavender buds, fresh lavender sprigs, pinecones, branches, stones, vintage wooden elements

### General Tips
- Show candle details in close-ups (wax texture, wick, label)
- Include scale reference (ruler or coin)
- Establish consistent visual brand across all photos
- Add lifestyle shots (candle in real-world settings)
- Edit for enhanced colors/contrast without over-editing
