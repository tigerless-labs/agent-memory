## Tennessee River Watershed

### Geological Composition
- **Sedimentary rocks**: limestone, sandstone, shale
- **Metamorphic rocks**: gneiss, schist (Appalachian Mountains)
- **Igneous rocks**: granite, diabase
- **Mineral deposits**: coal, oil, natural gas
- Valley and ridge region: folded and faulted sedimentary rocks

### Ecosystem & Wildlife Impacts
- Nutrient-rich metamorphic rock areas support diverse plant species and wildlife
- Limestone areas affect water pH, increase aquatic species abundance
- Cumberland Plateau and Appalachian Mountains provide diverse habitats

### Endangered Species
- **Freshwater mussels**: sensitive to water quality, depend on clean water and stable river conditions
- **Fish**: yellowfin madtom, pygmy madtom
- **Other fauna**: bats, birds, mammals (especially in cave systems and rock formations)

### Conservation Organizations & Initiatives
1. **Tennessee River Basin Network** - multi-org collaboration (nonprofits, government, business) focused on water quality restoration, sustainable land use, habitat conservation
2. **Tennessee River Freshwater Pearl Mussel Conservation Fund** - research on mussel ecology, advocacy, habitat restoration
3. **Tennessee Wildlife Federation** - runs "Tennessee's Wild Side" project highlighting regional biodiversity
4. **Government agencies**: Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service

### Pollution & Regulatory Efforts
- **Tennessee River Basin Initiative (TRBI)** - multi-state program monitoring water quality, identifying pollution sources, implementing management practices
- **Effective to date**: reduced sediment and nutrient pollution, improved water quality and aquatic habitats
- **Regulations**: Clean Water Act (federal), Tennessee water quality standards
- **Ongoing issues**: industrial discharge, agricultural runoff, mining impacts on water quality and soil stability

### Key Challenges
- Pollution remains significant despite efforts
- Pollutant accumulation in food chains affects wildlife and human health
- Mining activities impact habitats and water resources
- Continued monitoring and research needed for long-term ecosystem health
