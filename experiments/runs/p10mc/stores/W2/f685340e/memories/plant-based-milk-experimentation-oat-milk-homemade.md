User has been experimenting with different plant-based milks:

- **Almond milk** — aware of water usage concerns
- **Soy milk** — aware of deforestation concerns
- **Cashew milk** — interested but concerned about fair trade and transportation

**Environmental Concerns:**
Prioritizes sustainability and environmental impact when choosing plant-based milk. Prefers oat milk due to:
- Lower water usage compared to almond and soy
- Fewer pesticides
- Can be produced locally
- Supports crop rotation practices

**Homemade Oat Milk Project:**
Interested in making oat milk at home to:
- Reduce packaging waste
- Reduce transportation emissions
- Control ingredients and quality

Equipment recommendations provided:
- High-speed blender (Vitamix or Blendtec preferred)
- Cheesecloth or nut milk bag
- Large bowl/container
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Optional: flavorings and sweeteners

Basic process: 1 cup rolled oats (soaked 8+ hours), blended with 2 cups fresh water, strained through cheesecloth. Yields milk that keeps 3-5 days refrigerated.
