## Kung Pao Tofu

**Key technique: Pressing and marinating**
- Press tofu for at least 30 minutes to remove excess liquid
- Marinade: soy sauce, rice vinegar, cornstarch, peanut or vegetable oil, minced garlic, ginger, scallions
- Marinate for 30 min to 1 hour (don't over-marinate or it becomes mushy)
- Fry in hot wok/skillet with flavorful oil (peanut or avocado oil preferred)

**Serve with:** steamed rice or noodles, peanuts, vegetables, chili peppers

## Tempeh Fish Tacos

**Key technique: Soaking and crisping**
- Crumble tempeh into small pieces
- Soak in citrus-based marinade: lime juice, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika
- Add umami element: nutritional yeast or miso paste (creates "fishy" flavor)
- Soak for 30 min to 1-2 hours (don't over-soak or becomes soggy)
- Pat dry with paper towels
- Pan-fry or bake until crispy and golden

**Serve with:** warm tortillas, slaw, avocado, salsa, sautéed onions

## Important ingredient: Nutritional yeast
- Adds savory, umami flavor that enhances "fishy" profile
- Balances acidity of citrus juice
- Start with small amount (about 1/2 teaspoon) and adjust to taste
- Rich in protein, B vitamins, minerals
