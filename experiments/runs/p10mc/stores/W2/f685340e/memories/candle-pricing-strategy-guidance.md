## Candle Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Process
1. **Calculate costs** — include wax, essential oils, fragrances, containers, labels, materials, labor, and overhead
2. **Research market** — check online marketplaces, craft fairs, local boutiques to find average artisanal candle prices
3. **Consider size and complexity** — larger/more intricate candles require higher material and labor costs
4. **Factor in unique selling points** — eco-friendly materials, handcrafted designs, bespoke fragrances allow premium pricing
5. **Start with base price and adjust** — offer pricing tiers or bundles for different customer segments
6. **Test and adjust** — gather customer feedback and sales data to refine prices
7. **Pricing psychology** — prices ending in .95/.99 appear more affordable; round numbers seem premium
8. **Be transparent** — explain pricing rationale on website and social media

### Suggested Price Ranges
- **Small candles (8-10 oz):** $15–$20
- **Medium candles (12-14 oz):** $20–$25
- **Large candles (16-18 oz):** $25–$30
- **Specialty candles (unique fragrances/ingredients):** $30–$40

### Notes
- Adjust based on unique features and materials used
- Pricing is iterative; refine as business grows
