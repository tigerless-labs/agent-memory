## Plant-Based Milk Comparison

### Almond Milk
**Profile:** Low calories, rich in vitamins, neutral slightly nutty flavor
**Uses:** Cereal, smoothies, desserts, baking (cakes, muffins, cookies)
**Environmental concerns:**
- Requires 1.1 gallons of water per almond
- Mostly grown in California during droughts
- High pesticide use
- Long transportation distances

### Soy Milk
**Profile:** High protein, slightly nutty earthy flavor, fortified options available
**Uses:** Savory dishes, stir-fries, soups, creamy sauces (vegan mac and cheese), Asian-inspired dishes
**Environmental concerns:**
- Major contributor to Amazon deforestation and habitat loss
- Requires significant irrigation water
- High synthetic fertilizer and pesticide use
- Long transportation distances

### Cashew Milk
**Profile:** Creamy texture, rich in magnesium, mild slightly sweet flavor
**Uses:** Creamy sauces (vegan Alfredo, hollandaise), desserts (cheesecakes, ice cream)
**Environmental concerns:**
- Requires irrigation water
- Pesticide use
- Fair trade and labor practice concerns in developing countries
- Long transportation distances

### Oat Milk
**Profile:** Low calories, naturally lactose-free, rich in fiber, neutral slightly sweet flavor
**Uses:** Cereal, smoothies, desserts, baking (bread, muffins, cakes), breakfast dishes
**Environmental advantages:**
- Relatively low water usage
- Often grown in crop rotation (promotes soil health)
- Fewer pesticides and synthetic fertilizers
- Can be produced locally, reducing transportation emissions

## Sustainability Tips
- Look for eco-certifications: Fairtrade, Organic, Regenerative Organic Certified
- Support local brands when possible
- Research brand environmental policies
- Consider making homemade milk to reduce packaging waste
