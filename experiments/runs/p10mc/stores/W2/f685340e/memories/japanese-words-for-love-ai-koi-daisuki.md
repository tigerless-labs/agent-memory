User mentioned example of how Japanese language reflects cultural values through emotional vocabulary:

- **ai** — deep, unconditional love
- **koi** — romantic love
- **daisuki** — liking or affection

This multiplicity of words for "love" in Japanese suggests:
- Japanese cultural norm of valuing and expressing emotion clearly
- Emphasis on importance of context and intensity level when expressing different types of love
- Language structure enables more nuanced distinctions in emotional expression

Used as example of how language can reveal and reinforce cultural values and norms about emotion and relationships. Contrasts with languages that have single word for "love" without such distinctions.
