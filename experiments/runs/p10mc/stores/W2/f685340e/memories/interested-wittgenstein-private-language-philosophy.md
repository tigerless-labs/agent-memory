User is interested in Wittgenstein's philosophical ideas, particularly:
- The concept that language is inherently social and cannot be truly "private" (only one person understanding it)
- How meanings are learned through social interaction and observation of language use in context
- The ambiguity of language and how meanings shift with context over time
- Implications for communication: not just conveying information, but also building relationships and negotiating meaning

Discussed how Wittgenstein's ideas relate to understanding communication as more than information transfer, and the need for deep understanding of language and social/cultural contexts.
