## 12-Month Decluttering Plan

A year-long plan to declutter both physical and digital spaces, broken into monthly tasks. Original plan started May 2023.

### Monthly breakdown:

- **Month 1 (May):** Create plan, set SMART goals
- **Month 2 (June):** Clothing and personal items
- **Month 3 (July):** Books, magazines, papers (digitize documents)
- **Month 4 (August):** Kitchen and dining area
- **Month 5 (September):** Living room and common areas
- **Month 6 (October):** Bedroom and bathroom
- **Month 7 (November):** Workspace/home office
- **Month 8 (December):** Digital files and folders
- **Month 9 (January):** Email inbox (unsubscribe, archive, organize)
- **Month 10 (February):** Social media accounts (unfollow, unfriend, privacy settings)
- **Month 11 (March):** Smartphone and devices (apps, organization, settings)
- **Month 12 (April):** Review progress, establish maintenance routines

### Key principles:

- One area per month makes process manageable
- Donate/sell/recycle unwanted items
- Create organization systems for remaining items
- End with routines to maintain decluttered spaces
- Adjust plan to fit personal circumstances

Plan emphasizes creating a more organized, peaceful environment.
