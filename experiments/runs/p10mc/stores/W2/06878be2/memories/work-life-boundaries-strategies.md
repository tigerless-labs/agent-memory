## Work-Life Boundaries

The user explored strategies to establish clear boundaries between work and personal life:

1. **Set a schedule** — Define working hours and stick consistently
2. **Dedicated workspace** — Physical separation creates mental boundaries
3. **Transition routine** — Walk, meditate, hobby to signal context switch
4. **Limit work technology** — Turn off notifications, avoid work messages during personal time
5. **Communicate boundaries** — Clearly set expectations with colleagues, supervisor, family
6. **Prioritize personal time** — Schedule activities and self-care as non-negotiable commitments
7. **Learn to say "no"** — Decline tasks that interfere with personal time
8. **Time management** — Maximize productivity during work hours to avoid bringing work home
9. **Support network** — Cultivate relationships that respect boundaries
10. **Review and adjust** — Regularly reassess balance as life circumstances change

Key principle: Flexibility over time as demands shift.
