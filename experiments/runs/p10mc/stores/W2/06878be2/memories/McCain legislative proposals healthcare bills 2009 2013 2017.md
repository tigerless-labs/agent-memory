McCain proposed several specific healthcare reform bills during his Senate career:

1. **Affordable Health Choices Act (2009)**: Introduced as an alternative to President Obamas healthcare reform package. Focused on expanding choice and competition in healthcare by establishing state-based insurance exchanges and allowing individuals to purchase insurance across state lines.

2. **Empowering Patients First Act (2013)**: Aimed to repeal the ACA and replace it with market-based reforms. Focused on giving consumers more control over their healthcare decisions and expanding access to high-quality, affordable care.

3. **Long-Term Care and Retirement Security Act (2017)**: Proposed as a replacement for the ACAs long-term care provisions. Aimed to provide affordable, comprehensive long-term care coverage for seniors and those with disabilities.

While not all of McCains proposals were enacted into law, his efforts helped shape the national conversation around healthcare policy and influenced future reform efforts.
