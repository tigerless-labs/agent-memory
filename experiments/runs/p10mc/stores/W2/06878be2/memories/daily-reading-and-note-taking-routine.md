## Daily Reading and Note-Taking Routine

User wants to establish consistent daily reading and note-taking practices for personal and professional growth.

### Core strategies:

1. **Schedule daily reading time** — Block specific time when most alert/focused (early morning, lunch, before bed)
2. **Set daily reading goal** — Number of pages or time duration
3. **Choose reading format** — Physical books, e-books, or audiobooks (mix formats as needed)
4. **Comfortable environment** — Quiet, distraction-free space
5. **Keep a reading list** — Curated list prevents decision fatigue
6. **Take notes while reading** — Highlighting, annotating, separate notebook/digital tool
7. **Review notes regularly** — Reinforce learning and identify patterns
8. **Apply what you've learned** — Use insights in daily life and work
9. **Join book club or reading group** — Community, motivation, accountability
10. **Track progress** — Record books/articles read and notes taken

Progress tracking helps maintain motivation and identify areas for deeper exploration.
