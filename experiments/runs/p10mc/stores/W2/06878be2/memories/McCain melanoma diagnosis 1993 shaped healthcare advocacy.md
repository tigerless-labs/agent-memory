McCain's 1993 melanoma diagnosis was formative to his healthcare positions. The cancer led to surgical removal of a significant portion of his left cheekbone and lymph nodes. His personal experience as a cancer patient made him a strong advocate for access to quality and affordable healthcare, especially for those with pre-existing conditions. This experience significantly shaped his approach to healthcare policy during his Senate career.
