## Todoist Quick Task Capture

User explored Todoist as a tool for capturing tasks while on-the-go and syncing across other organizing apps.

### Quick entry methods:

1. **Mobile app** — Tap "+", type or dictate task, auto-syncs
2. **Widgets** — Android/iOS home screen for entry without opening app
3. **Email** — Send task to unique email address (save in contacts for quick access)
4. **Voice assistants** — "Hey Google, add [task] to my Todoist" (works with Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa)
5. **Browser extension** — Quick entry from computer without opening Todoist

### Syncing with other apps:

1. **Zapier or IFTTT** — Create automated workflows between Todoist and other organizing apps (Skedpal, Sunsama, Routinely App)
2. **Native integrations** — Some apps have built-in Todoist support (e.g., Google Calendar, Trello)
3. **Manual syncing** — Transfer during daily/weekly reviews if automation unavailable

Todoist integrations support various organizing workflows and automation levels.
