## AI Disease Diagnosis Accuracy

### Breast Cancer Detection
- AI-powered accuracy: 97-99%
- Human radiologist accuracy: 87-96%
- Source: Multiple studies showing AI outperforms or matches specialists
- Caveat: Higher false positive rate compared to human radiologists (per The Lancet study)

### Diabetic Retinopathy Detection
- AI-powered accuracy: 92-95%
- Comparable to human ophthalmologists

### Cardiac Diagnosis (arrhythmias and heart conditions)
- AI-powered accuracy: 95-98%
- Human cardiologist accuracy: 80-90%

## System Types
1. Computer-aided diagnosis (CAD): Assists radiologists/clinicians by highlighting abnormalities and suggesting diagnoses
2. Deep learning-based systems: Use neural networks to analyze medical data and make autonomous diagnoses

## Challenges and Limitations
- Data quality and availability (need large, diverse, high-quality datasets)
- Explainability and transparency (difficult for clinicians to understand AI reasoning)
- Regulatory frameworks still developing
- Clinical validation needed in real-world settings
- Algorithmic bias from training data
- False positive/negative rates vary by system

## Future Directions
- Hybrid approaches combining human clinicians with AI (higher accuracy than AI alone)
- Explainable AI development for increased transparency
- Multi-modal data integration (EHRs, medical images, genomics)
