## Guitar Upgrade Consideration

Currently owns: Fender Stratocaster
Considering: Gibson Les Paul upgrade

### Interest Areas
- **Acoustic-like fingerpicking patterns**: wants to explore delicate, intricate fingerpicking styles (mentioned "Blackbird" intro as example)
- **Open D tuning**: interested in exploring DADF#AD tuning on electric guitar, particularly for warmer, resonant tone and slide guitar capability
- **Tonal flexibility**: wants more tonal options and variety in playing

### Les Paul Models Recommended (with coil-tap/coil-splitting)
1. **Gibson Les Paul Standard** — coil-tap function on tone controls for brighter, more agile sound
2. **Gibson Les Paul Traditional** — coil-splitting function allows access to single-coil sound from humbuckers
3. **Gibson Les Paul Custom** — coil-tap or coil-splitting depending on specific configuration

### Key Specs for Reference
- Les Paul scale length: 24.75"
- Fingerboard: 22 frets
- Body: mahogany with carved maple top
- Pickups: two humbuckers (bridge and neck)

### Notes for Open D Tuning on Les Paul
- Use heavier string gauge (0.012-0.052 or 0.013-0.056) for fuller, more resonant tone
- Neck pickup recommended for warmer, mellower tone suitable for open D
- Lower amp gain (5-6) to prevent muddiness
- Boost bass (100-150 Hz), cut midrange (500-800 Hz) and treble (5k-8k Hz)
- Use lighter touch and gentler right-hand technique for smoother tone
