## Trip Details
- **When**: Summer 2023 (planned)
- **Who**: Planning with friends
- **Location**: Midwest, chose Wisconsin specifically
- **Trail**: Elroy-Sparta State Trail
  - Length: 32 miles (Elroy to Sparta, Wisconsin)
  - Surface: ~10 miles paved, ~22 miles crushed stone
  - Terrain: Relatively flat, gentle hills, no steep grades
  - Features: Wooded areas, prairies, wetlands, historic trestle bridge over Baraboo River
  - Wildlife: deer, turkeys, birds

## Amenities
- Rest stops approximately every 5-7 miles
- Parking, restrooms, picnic areas at access points
- Bike shops along trail: Sparta Bike Shop, Elroy Bike Shop, Tunnel Trail Cycles (Wilton), Kendallville Cycles (Kendall)
- Food/drinks available in towns along the trail
- Campgrounds and hotels available

## What They're Bringing
- Basic bike tools (multi-tool with Allen wrenches, screwdrivers)
- Pump (for tire inflation)
- Refillable water bottles
- Energy snacks (nuts, dried fruits, energy bars)
- First-aid kit basics
- Sunscreen, insect repellent
