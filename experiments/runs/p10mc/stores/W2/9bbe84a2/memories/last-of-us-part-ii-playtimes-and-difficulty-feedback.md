## Playtime Summary
- **Normal difficulty**: 25 hours
- **Hard difficulty**: 30 hours
- Completed both playthroughs

## Difficulty Experience
- Found the difficulty curve well-balanced — challenging but not frustrating
- Died a few times, mostly when rushing through sections
- Learned to take a more strategic, deliberate approach to overcome tough encounters
- Playing strategically made a significant difference in overcoming difficult sections

## Overall Impression
- Very emotionally impactful game
- Strong storytelling, characters, and world-building
- Looking forward to trying other games in the future, but not immediately replaying on higher difficulties

## Interest in Related Content
- Interested in games with similar atmosphere to The Last of Us Part II but with faster-paced action
