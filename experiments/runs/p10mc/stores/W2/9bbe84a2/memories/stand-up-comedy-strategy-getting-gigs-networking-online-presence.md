## Strategy: Getting More Gigs and Open Mic Opportunities

**Networking and Relationships**
- Attend open mics, comedy shows, and festivals in your area
- Introduce yourself to other comedians, promoters, and bookers
- Be friendly, supportive, and respectful
- These connections lead to gig opportunities, feedback, and advice

**Online Presence**
- Develop professional website or profile on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram
- Showcase comedy style, upcoming gigs, and videos
- Easier for promoters/bookers to find and book you

**Online Communities**
- Facebook groups dedicated to stand-up comedy in your area
- Reddit forums (r/standupcomedy)
- Online forums for stand-up comedy in your region
- Share opportunities, provide feedback, offer advice

**Finding Open Mics and Gigs**
- Open Mic Finder
- Comedy Club Listings
- Local event calendars
- Meetup.com (search for comedy open mics)
- Facebook Events (search for comedy open mics or stand-up comedy events)
- Craigslist (verify legitimacy)

**Proactive Outreach**
- Reach out to comedy clubs, bars, venues hosting comedy nights
- Ask about open mic opportunities or booking processes
- Follow up with promoters, bookers, other comedians with gigs in area

**Press Kit and Promotion**
- Develop press kit with: bio, headshots, sample set (video or written)
- Helps promoters/bookers understand your style and material

**Professional Approach**
- Always be ready to perform with solid set
- Maintain professional demeanor
- Be respectful to venue, other comedians, audience

**Alternative: Run Your Own Show**
- If struggling to find gigs, organize your own open mic or show
- Gain experience, build following, attract other comedians

**Ongoing Development**
- Continuously write and improve material
- Try out new jokes and refine craft
- Stay motivated and confident when pursuing gigs

**Persistence**
- Getting more gigs takes time, patience, perseverance
- Don't get discouraged by rejections or setbacks
- Keep pushing forward; opportunities will arise
