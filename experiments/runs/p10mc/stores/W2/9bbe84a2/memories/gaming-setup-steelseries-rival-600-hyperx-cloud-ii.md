## Gaming Mouse
Interested in **SteelSeries Rival 600** as new mouse to pair with recently acquired keyboard.

Key features valued:
- TrueMove3 sensor (12,000 CPI)
- 1-to-1 tracking, no acceleration/deceleration
- Low latency (1ms)
- Liftoff distance 0.5mm
- High precision at high speeds (up to 350 IPS)
- Removable side panel for weight adjustment

Also considered: Logitech G Pro X Superlight, Razer DeathAdder Elite, HyperX Pulsefire Raid, Corsair Harpoon Wireless

## Gaming Headset
**Leaning towards HyperX Cloud II** despite some concerns.

Valued features:
- Comfort for long gaming sessions
- Great quality microphone
- 7.1 surround sound (though reported as inconsistent in some games)
- Detachable microphone
- Closed-back design

Known issue: Virtual surround sound can be wonky in some games, but user has accepted this as not a dealbreaker for Apex Legends play (pro players often use stereo anyway).

Also considered: SteelSeries Arctis 7, Turtle Beach Recon 200, Logitech G Pro X, Sennheiser GSP 670
