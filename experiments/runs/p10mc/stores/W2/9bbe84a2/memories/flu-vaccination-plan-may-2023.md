## Vaccination Plan (May 30, 2023)

**Flu history:**
- Previously had the flu and was prescribed Tamiflu (oseltamivir), which helped significantly

**Vaccine decision:**
- Decided to get the **quadrivalent flu vaccine** (protects against four flu strains: H1N1, H3N2, and two B strains Victoria/Yamagata)
- Did not choose high-dose vaccine
- Cost considered: quadrivalent ~$20-30 without insurance vs. high-dose ~$60-70
- Concerned about insurance coverage

**Implementation:**
- Planned to get vaccine at one of: doctor's office, pharmacy (CVS/Walgreens/Rite Aid), community health clinic, or health department
- Vaccine is typically covered as preventive service by health insurance
