## Core Brand Ethos

Build teams by hiring **great human beings first, then great technologists second**.

Key principles:
- Prioritize **soft skills, critical thinking, and character** over coding ability alone
- Use **psychometric tests** to identify critical thinkers and human qualities
- Value **EQ (emotional intelligence) over IQ** when hiring software developers
- Focus on collaboration, communication, and interpersonal skills

## Target Message

Establish thought leadership positioning: a brand that teaches teams how to build effectively by getting the hiring fundamentals right—selecting for character and mindset before technical prowess.

## Content Strategy Pillars

1. **Blog posts**: Articles exploring importance of hiring humans first; identifying key traits in candidates; building teams with collaboration focus
2. **Podcasts/videos**: Interviews with industry experts and successful team leaders on building effective teams with this philosophy
3. **Webinars/workshops**: Practical training on psychometric assessment, evaluating soft skills, fostering collaborative culture
4. **E-books/guides**: Deep-dive resources on psychometric testing, soft skill evaluation, culture-building
5. **Social media**: Case studies, team profiles, thought leadership content highlighting this approach
6. **Audience engagement**: Respond to community, build relationships, position as thought leader

## Target Audience

People responsible for hiring and team building in software development: CTOs, engineering managers, founders, tech leads who care about building sustainable, collaborative teams.

## Brand Voice

Educational, values-driven, practical, challenging conventional "hire the smartest coder" wisdom.
