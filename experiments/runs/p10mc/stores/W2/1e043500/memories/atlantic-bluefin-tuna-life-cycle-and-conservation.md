## Life Cycle

- **Size & lifespan**: grow up to 10 feet long, weigh over 1,000 pounds; live 25-30 years, can reach 40 years
- **Spawning**: occurs in the Gulf of Mexico between April and June
- **Larval stage**: eggs hatch into transparent larvae; carried by Gulf Stream from Gulf of Mexico to eastern Atlantic Ocean
- **Juvenile stage**: feed on plankton and small fish; found in Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean
- **Maturity**: migrate long distances to feed and spawn; observed crossing Atlantic from western to eastern Atlantic for breeding

## Conservation Status & Approach

- Listed as an **endangered species** due to overfishing
- Conservation efforts identify critical habitats: spawning grounds, migration routes, feeding areas
- Implemented measures: reduce commercial fishing, enforce regulations protecting spawning/juvenile habitats, reduce bycatch, restrict fishing methods
