## Content Prioritization Techniques

Use these methods to rank and prioritize content ideas:

1. **Content Audit**: Identify content gaps and opportunities aligned with business goals
2. **Customer Persona**: Develop detailed audience profiles to understand what content resonates
3. **Content Scorecard**: Evaluate ideas against criteria: relevance, timeliness, potential impact
4. **Audience Feedback**: Surveys, focus groups, social media listening to learn what audience wants
5. **Keyword Research**: Understand what topics people search for; create content meeting audience search intent
6. **Content Calendar**: Plan publication cadence to ensure consistent content stream

## Selection Criteria

When evaluating content ideas, consider:
- Alignment with business goals
- Value provided to target audience
- Relevance to current industry trends
- Potential traffic and engagement impact
- SEO opportunity from keyword demand
- Team capacity to produce quality content
