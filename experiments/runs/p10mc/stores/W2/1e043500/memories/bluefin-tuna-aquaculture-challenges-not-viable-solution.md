## Key Challenges

1. **Complex life cycle**: reproductive and early life stages difficult to replicate in captivity; requires large, expensive facilities
2. **High cost**: much more expensive than wild-caught Bluefin; requires large facilities, high-quality feeds, intensive maintenance
3. **Environmental impact**: discharge of waste and nutrients like all aquaculture; feed made from wild-caught fish leads to overfishing of other species
4. **Genetic diversity**: captive breeding results in loss of genetic diversity, weakening species' resilience to environmental changes
5. **Consumer preference**: many consumers prefer wild-caught over farmed Bluefin; difficult to shift market perception

## Conclusion

While aquaculture theoretically could reduce pressure on wild populations, it is **not a practical or straightforward solution**. Significant investment and research needed to overcome breeding/growing challenges; still environmentally problematic due to feed sourcing.
