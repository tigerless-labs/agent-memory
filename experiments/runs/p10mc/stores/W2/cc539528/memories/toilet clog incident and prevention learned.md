## Incident
Clogged toilet a few days before May 20, 2023 by flushing too much toilet paper at once; had to call a plumber.

## Prevention tips learned
- Use sewer-safe / clog-resistant toilet paper (single or 2-ply, breaks down faster)
- Avoid flushing non-biodegradable items (wet wipes, sanitary products, paper towels, cotton balls)
- Be cautious with food waste and grease
- Monthly maintenance: 1 cup baking soda (30 min) + 1 cup vinegar (30 min)
- Keep plunger and toilet auger on hand

## Brands for clog-resistant TP
- Scott 1000/1100 series
- Charmin Ultra Strong / Ultra Soft
- Quilted Northern Ultra Plush / Ultra Comfort
- Cottonelle Ultra Comfort / Ultra Clean
- Seventh Generation Recycled (100% recycled fibers)
