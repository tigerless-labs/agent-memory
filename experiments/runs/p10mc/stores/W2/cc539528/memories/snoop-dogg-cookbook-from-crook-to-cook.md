Snoop Dogg's book "From Crook to Cook: Platinum Recipes from Tha Boss Dogg's Kitchen" is widely popular and well-received.

## Content
- Recipes from childhood, fast-food chains, family entertaining
- West Coast and Southern recipe heritage
- Personal anecdote: cooked hot dogs in hotel room with clothes iron
- Tips on hosting dinner parties

## Reception
- Generally positive reviews from fans and food enthusiasts
- Praised for unique blend of recipes and personal stories
- Some criticism: recipes not health-focused, not suitable for dietary restrictions
- Overall emphasis on indulgence and authentic cooking
