## Luxury & Modern Design Palette

### Color Codes
- Green: #2E7D32 (deep and rich, sophisticated)
- Blue: #3F51B5 (deep elegant hue, refined atmosphere)
- Grey: #9E9E9E (neutral balanced shade, versatile)

### Western Fonts
1. Avenir - sans-serif by Adrian Frutiger; clean, elegant, versatile, timeless
2. Futura - geometric sans-serif by Paul Renner; crisp lines, minimalist aesthetic
3. Didot - serif by Firmin Didot; high contrast, refined elegance, good for fashion/editorial
4. Gotham - contemporary sans-serif by Tobias Frere-Jones; geometric simplicity, multiple weights
5. Proxima Nova - modern sans-serif by Mark Simonson; clean lines, geometric shapes, large x-height

### Thai Fonts
1. Kanit - sans-serif by Cadson Demak; geometric shapes, clean lines
2. Sarabun - serif by Suppakit Chalermlarp; clean, elegant, high legibility
3. Maitree - serif by Cadson Demak; modern and balanced, versatile weights
4. Krub - geometric sans-serif by Cadson Demak; sleek and luxurious
5. Chonburi - sans-serif by Cadson Demak; condensed and bold, contemporary

All recommended for branding, logo design, and print materials targeting luxury and modern aesthetic.
