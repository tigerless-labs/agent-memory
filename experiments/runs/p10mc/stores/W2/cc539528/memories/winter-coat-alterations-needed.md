## Cost reference
- Hemming: $10-$30
- Taking in or letting out seams: $20-$50
- Sleeve alterations: $15-$40
- Shoulder or collar adjustments: $20-$50
- Zipper or button replacement: $10-$30
- Lining repairs: $20-$100
- **Total average for multiple alterations**: $50-$200

## Finding tailors suggestions
- Google Search: "tailors near me"
- Yelp: Filter by rating, distance, hours
- Ask for personal referrals from friends/family/colleagues
- Check local dry cleaners (many offer alteration services)
- Check online directories (Thumbtack, TaskRabbit, Nextdoor)

## Questions to ask a tailor
- Experience with altering winter coats
- Pricing for the specific alterations needed
- Turnaround time
- See examples of their work/portfolio
- Ask about bundling discounts for multiple alterations
