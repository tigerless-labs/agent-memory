## Cleaning Habits & Vacuuming Schedule (May 21, 2023)

**Living situation:** Lives alone, no pets, small space with mix of low-pile and berber carpets in high-traffic areas (hallways and living room).

**Vacuuming schedule agreed upon:**
- 30 minutes per week total
- Divided into two 15-minute sessions:
  - Session 1: Hallways and adjacent high-traffic areas
  - Session 2: Living room, around furniture and high-traffic paths
- Bi-weekly deep cleaning: 15-30 minutes for stairs, under furniture, or specific dusty areas

**Robot vacuum interest:**
- Considering robot vacuum for daily maintenance (not replacement for weekly vacuuming)
- Small space makes it suitable for this investment
- Popular models mentioned: iRobot Roomba, Neato Botvac, Eufy RoboVac, Samsung Powerbot

**Smart plug for robot vacuum:**
- Plans to use smart plug to schedule vacuum remotely when away from home
- Brands mentioned: TP-Link Smart Plug, Belkin WeMo Insight, Amazon Smart Plug, iDevices Switch
- Wants to automate scheduling for convenience

**Daily "quick pick-up" routine:**
- 10-minute routine every morning before work
- Focus: putting away clutter and dirty dishes
- High-traffic areas to focus on: kitchen counter/sink, living room, entryway/hallway, bathroom counters
- Tip: use timer to stay on track
- Complements existing bed-making habit (started around May 7, 2023)
