## "The Wellerman" VC Parody

Full parody version where the whale hunt becomes a startup's search for venture capital. Key changes:
- Ship "Billy O' Tea" → Startup named "Venture Jerk"
- Whale hunt → Pitching to investors
- "Wellerman come with sugar and tea and rum" → "VC come to give us cash and freedom and fun"
- Captain's crew → CEO and team
- 40-day whale hunt → 100-day funding round

**Verse 1:**
There once was a startup that put to work
The name of the company was the Venture Jerk
The investors all said, "It's too risky now"
O blow, my startup boys, blow

**Chorus:**
Soon may the VC come
To give us cash and freedom and fun
One day, when the pitchin' is done
We'll take our leave and grow

**Full parody includes:** Verses 2-6, post-chorus sections, and outro — all following the original song's structure with startup/VC fundraising metaphors replacing whaling references.
