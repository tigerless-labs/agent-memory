## Online Expansion Plan

### Platforms
- Facebook business page
- Instagram account

### Content Strategy
1. **Product Showcases** — high-quality photos from multiple angles
2. **Behind-the-Scenes Content** — process videos, creative workspace, market events (Tuesday evening night market)
3. **User-Generated Content (UGC)**
   - Create branded hashtag to encourage customer photos
   - Feature customer photos/testimonials on feed and stories
   - Add CTA to packaging and website asking customers to share
   - Offer incentive (15% discount code) for sharing

### Engagement
- Respond promptly to comments and messages
- Concise, engaging captions with CTAs
- Use relevant hashtags (2-5 for Facebook, 5-10 for Instagram)
- Add emojis to increase visual appeal

### Contests & Giveaways
- Photo contest: followers share photos using products, winner chosen by creativity/popularity
- Promote across all platforms
- Behind-the-scenes content from successful market events to build excitement

### Partnerships
- Collaborate with micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) in home/wellness niche
- Partner with complementary small businesses (e.g., local soap makers, home decor)
- Joint promotions (bundles, exclusive discounts)
- Share each other's content

### Tools to Consider
- Planoly or Later for Instagram scheduling
- Instagram Shopping to tag products in posts/stories
- Facebook Live for real-time engagement
