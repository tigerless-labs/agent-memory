## Move to Vancouver

**Timeline:**
- Moving date: mid-August 2023
- Semester starts: September 1st, 2023
- Purpose: Attending university in Vancouver

**Neighborhood considerations:**
- Primary interest: Fairview neighborhood
- Fairview is in south-central Vancouver (Granville St to west, Cambie St to east, 16th Ave to south, Broadway to north)
- Close to UBC and Emily Carr University of Art + Design
- Housing options: apartments, condos, houses, basement suites available but competitive rental market
- Amenities: diverse restaurants, Save-On-Foods, Safeway, Whole Foods, Charleson Park, B-Line rapid transit on Broadway
- Safe neighborhood with low crime rates
- Strong sense of community with many students

**Weather planning:**

*August:*
- High: 23-25°C (73-77°F)
- Low: 13-15°C (56-59°F)
- 9 hours sunshine/day
- Humidity: 64%

*September:*
- High: 20-22°C (68-72°F)
- Low: 10-12°C (50-54°F)
- 7 hours sunshine/day
- Average 12 rainy days; light to moderate rainfall; beginning of wet season

*Winter (Dec-Feb):*
- Range: 3-8°C (37-46°F)
- 12-15 rainy days per month
- Snow rare but possible; more in nearby mountains

**Winter clothing to purchase before moving:**
- Waterproof/breathable jacket with hood (Gore-Tex or similar)
- Insulated jacket or fleece with water-resistant treatment
- Waterproof pants for outdoor activities
- Warm sweaters and thermal layers
- Moisture-wicking warm socks
- Warm gloves and hat (wool, fleece, or synthetic blends)
- Scarf
- Waterproof boots with good grip

**Neighborhoods to explore before September 1st:**
1. Gastown - oldest neighborhood, cobblestone streets, boutiques, restaurants; Steam Clock, Irish Heather bar
2. Yaletown - former industrial area, upscale shopping/dining; Yaletown Brewing Company
3. Granville Island - public market, artisan shops, restaurants, street performers
4. Chinatown - large Chinatown, culture/food/shops; Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden
5. Mount Pleasant - vintage shops, craft breweries, restaurants; street art, Vancouver Flea Market
6. Commercial Drive - eclectic, independent shops/restaurants/cafes; Rio Theatre, Farmers Market
7. Kitsilano - laid-back beachy vibe; Kitsilano Beach, Vanier Park, outdoor pool

**Public transit:**
- Get Compass Card (reusable transit card)
- Download Transit app for real-time info
- SkyTrain (Expo Line) is main rapid transit
- Bus system extensive throughout city
- Key stations: Waterfront, Granville, Stadium-Chinatown, Main Street-Science World, Commercial-Broadway
- Vancouver Attractions Pass available for discounted access to multiple attractions

**Additional exploration plans:**
- Day trips: Bowen Island, Squamish, Whistler
- Try local cuisine: seafood, sushi, poutine
- Explore street art in Mount Pleasant and other neighborhoods
- Visit breweries, wineries, farmers markets
