## Team Composition & Work

The user's data science team specializes in analyzing water sensor data and flood risk maps. They work during normal business hours and provide insights that help prepare for and mitigate water-related risks.

## User Preferences

- Prefers moderate, measured language ("like" rather than "love" or "adore")
- Wants professional tone that's appreciative but not hyperbolic

## Final Poem (May 21, 2023)

```
Roses are red, violets are blue,
I truly admire my data science crew.
They work with water sensors and flood risk maps,
Analyzing the data with their technical claps.

From river levels to rainfall and more,
They analyze it all to give us a score.
Their work keeps us informed, always on track,
My data science team, their skills are hard to match.

Their insights help us prepare and take action,
To mitigate the risks of water's reaction.
Their work is so crucial, and we owe them a lot,
My data science team, you're simply top-notch.

So here's to my team, with gratitude and pride,
For all the knowledge and insights they provide.
Their passion and expertise are simply the key,
My data science team, you're simply the best to me!
```

## Iteration History

1. Initial version used stronger language ("love," "divine," "adore")

3. Added specific domain context: water sensors and flood risk maps
4. Removed line about working hours (user clarified timing doesn't matter to the poem)
5. Fixed grammar: changed "their work will never disappoint" to "their skills are hard to match"
