## Pumpkin Powdery Mildew Treatment (May 22, 2023)

User has pumpkin plants affected by powdery mildew and sought treatment advice.

### Treatment approaches learned:
1. **Manual**: Remove infected leaves and plants early
2. **Fungicides**: Potassium bicarbonate, neem oil, sulfur, or copper-based options
3. **Environment**: Improve air circulation via plant spacing, weed removal, trellis systems
4. **Homemade**: 1 tbsp baking soda + 1 tbsp vegetable oil + few drops dish soap in 1 gallon water
5. **Timing**: Avoid spraying during bloom to protect beneficial insects and honeybees

User committed to careful fungicide selection and reading labels before application.
