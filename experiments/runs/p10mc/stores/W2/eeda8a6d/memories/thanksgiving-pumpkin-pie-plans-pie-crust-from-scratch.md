Plans to make pumpkin pie for Thanksgiving. Interested in making pie crust from scratch and learning proper techniques.

Key pie crust tips:
- Use cold ingredients (cold butter, water, flour)
- Don't overmix the dough
- Use 3:2:1 ratio of flour to fat to water
- Chill dough at least 30 minutes before rolling
- Roll gently to about 1/8 inch thickness
- Bake crust until lightly golden (15-20 minutes for single-crust pie)
- Use pie shield or weights to prevent shrinking/burning
- Can make dough ahead and refrigerate up to 2 days or freeze up to 2 months

Interested in pie crust variations and favorite spice combinations for pumpkin pie.
