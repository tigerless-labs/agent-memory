Wears favorite fall boots almost every day during the fall season. Interested in waterproofing boots to keep them dry and extend their lifespan.

Waterproofing products recommended:
- Nikwax Tech Wash (water-based, gentle cleaner and waterproofing)
- Scotchgard Fabric & Fabric Protector (repels water and stains)
- Kiwi Camp Dry (water-based spray)
- Granger's Performance Repel (high-performance, ideal for Gore-Tex membranes)

Maintenance approach: Clean boots first, choose product suitable for boot material, test on small area first, reapply regularly, condition leather boots regularly, store in dry cool place away from direct sunlight.
