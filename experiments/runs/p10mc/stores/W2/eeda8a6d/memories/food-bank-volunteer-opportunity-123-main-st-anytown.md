## Food Bank Volunteer Details

**Location:** 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345

**Contact Information:**
- Phone: 555-555-5555
- Email: volunteer@foodbank.org
- Website: foodbank.org

**Time Commitment:**
- Monday-Friday evenings
- Typical shifts: 4:00 pm - 7:00 pm
- Flexible options: one evening/week, two evenings/week, or as-needed (min 2 hrs/month)

**Tasks:**
- Sorting and packaging donated food items
- Inventory management (receiving, stocking)
- Food distribution setup and client assistance
- Special events and administrative support

**Volunteer Requirements:**
- Must be 18+ years old
- Complete volunteer application form (available on website or in person)
- Attend 30-minute orientation session
- Sign waiver and release form
- Pass background check (standard procedure, quick online or in-person process)
- On-the-job training provided

**Group Volunteering:**
- Group Volunteer Program available for groups of 5-20 people
- Dedicated staff supervision for groups
- Flexible scheduling for group needs

**Project/Program Adoption Options:**
- Food drives (collect specific items)
- Special event support (galas, charity runs, fundraising)
- Community garden project
- Backpack program (weekend food for children in need)
- Food sortation events
- Fundraising campaigns

## Application Process
1. Download/pick up application from website or in person
2. Complete and return via email or in person
3. Attend orientation
4. Complete background check
5. Begin volunteering

**Practical Tips:**
- Wear comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes
- Bring water bottle and snacks
- Contact volunteer coordinator with any questions
