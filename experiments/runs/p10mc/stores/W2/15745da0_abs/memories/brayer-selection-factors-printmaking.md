## Choosing the Right Brayer

When selecting a brayer for a printmaking project, consider:

1. **Ink type**: Water-based inks require softer brayer; oil-based inks require harder brayer
2. **Printing surface texture**: Smooth surfaces work better with harder brayer; rough surfaces need softer brayer
3. **Size of printing surface**: Smaller surfaces use smaller brayer; larger surfaces need larger brayer
4. **Design detail level**: Fine detailed designs work better with smaller, firmer brayer
5. **Personal experience**: Go with what you've had success with before, or experiment to find preference

**Note**: Brayer choice significantly affects final texture and quality of print. Requires experimentation and trial-and-error to master.
