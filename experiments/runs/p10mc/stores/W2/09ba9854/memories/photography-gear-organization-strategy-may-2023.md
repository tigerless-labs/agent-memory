## Organization Goals & Recommendations

**External Hard Drives for Photo Backup**
- Recommended options: Western Digital My Book, Seagate Backup Plus, LaCie Rugged, G-Technology G-Drive
- Key considerations: capacity (2-3× current needs), compatibility (Mac/PC), interface (USB 3.0/Thunderbolt 3/USB-C), speed, durability, portability
- Best practice: use 3-2-1 backup rule (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite); set up automatic backups

**Memory Card Organization**
- Label cards with capacity, type, and unique identifier (e.g., "128GB-1")
- Use protective cases or wallets to prevent loss/damage
- Create card inventory list to track in-use, available, and cards needing formatting
- Rotate cards regularly for even wear
- Format cards regularly before use

**Accessory Storage**
- Remote shutter release: small pouches, keychain storage, or bag compartments
- Lens cleaning kit: dedicated pouches or small storage boxes with dividers
- Extra batteries: individual battery cases or designated bag compartments

**Lens Protection**
- Recommended lens cases: Think Tank Photo, Lowepro, Nanuk
- Recommended lens pouches: Op/Tech USA, Lenscoat, Tamrac
- Key features: size fit, durable material, thick padding, secure closure, breathability
- Storage tips: always use lens caps, store upright, avoid stacking, clean before storage

**Lens Cleaning**
- Recommended kits: Canon, Nikon, Zeiss lens cleaning kits
- Use microfiber cloths (soft, lint-free)
- Apply solution to cloth, not directly to lens
- Clean in circular motion from center outward
- Test on small area first to ensure coating safety
