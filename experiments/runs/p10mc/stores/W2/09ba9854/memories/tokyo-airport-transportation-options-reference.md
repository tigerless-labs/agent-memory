## Narita Airport (NRT) Options

**Trains:**
- N'EX (Narita Express) to Tokyo Station: ~60 min, ¥3,000–4,000
- Keisei Skyliner to Ueno Station: ~45 min, ¥2,500–3,000

**Buses:**
- Airport Limousine Bus to various Tokyo locations: ~1–2 hours, ¥2,000–3,000

**Taxi:**
- Daytime (5 AM–10 PM): ¥15,000–20,000 (~$140–180)
- Late night (10 PM–5 AM): ¥18,000–25,000 (~$160–220)

## Haneda Airport (HND) Options

**Trains:**
- Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho: ~15 min, ¥500–600
- Keikyu Railway to Shinbashi: ~30–40 min, ¥400–600

**Buses:**
- Airport Limousine Bus: ~30–60 min, ¥1,000–2,000

**Taxi:**
- Daytime (5 AM–10 PM): ¥6,000–10,000 (~$55–90)
- Late night (10 PM–5 AM): ¥8,000–14,000 (~$70–125)

## Alternative Services

- **Private car services**: Japan Car Service, Tokyo Private Car — fixed pricing, ~$40–50, more accessible with luggage
- **Shared rides**: Go-Van, Japan Bus Online — ~$20–30
- **Suica/Pasmo cards**: Prepaid transit cards usable on trains, buses, subways throughout Tokyo

## Tips for Large Luggage

- Luggage carts or porter services available at train stations
- Buses/private services more comfortable than trains for multiple bags
- Hotels often have partnerships offering discounts or fixed rates
- Booking transportation in advance recommended, especially for large groups or luggage
