## 1946 Reorganization

Created Ministry of Civil Aviation for civilian aviation oversight while Air Ministry retained military aviation control.

## 1964 Merger

Air Ministry merged with Admiralty and War Office to form Ministry of Defence, reflecting post-WWII changes in warfare.

## Modernization Delays

Reasons for slow restructuring: technological change in aircraft and tactics, resource allocation, infrastructure updates, personnel training, coordination across multiple departments, competing interests between military branches, and need to reflect changing government priorities and international relations.
