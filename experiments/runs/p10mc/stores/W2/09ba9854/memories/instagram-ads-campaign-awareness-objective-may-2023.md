## Instagram Ad Campaign Setup

**Date**: May 20, 2023

**Objective**: "Awareness" - to increase visibility/reach for their project

**Platform**: Instagram, managed through Facebook Ads Manager

**Key metrics to track**:
- Reach (number of people exposed to the ad)
- Impressions (times ad was displayed)
- Frequency (average times per person)
- Engagement (likes, comments, saves, shares)
- Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)
- Brand awareness lift

**Tools for tracking**:
- Facebook Ads Manager (primary)
- Instagram Insights (organic performance)
- Possibly third-party tools: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse

**Status**: Ready to launch; new to social media advertising; working through setup process
