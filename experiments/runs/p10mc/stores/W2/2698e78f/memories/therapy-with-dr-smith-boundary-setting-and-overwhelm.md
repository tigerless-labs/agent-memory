## Therapy and Boundary Work

**Therapeutic relationship:**
- Therapist: Dr. Smith
- Session frequency: every two weeks (ongoing)
- Topics: anxiety management, overwhelm, boundary-setting

**Key guidance from Dr. Smith:**
- Learning to say "no" and set healthy boundaries is essential for managing overwhelm
- Discussed that boundaries are not selfish but necessary for mental/emotional well-being

**Current boundaries being worked on:**
- Overwhelmed with too many commitments
- Need to prioritize own needs and capacity
- Learning to identify personal limits and values

**Next step: Partner conversation about social boundaries**
- Partner tends to invite user to many social events
- User needs time to stay home and relax
- Plan: Have calm conversation using "I" statements to express feelings
- Goal: Limit social commitments to 1-2 events per week
- Approach: 
  - Express feelings without blame
  - Listen to partner's perspective
  - Find compromise that works for both
  - Offer alternatives (e.g., staycation nights, planned quality time)
- Prepared for potential pushback (e.g., "you're not fun," "you're abandoning me")
