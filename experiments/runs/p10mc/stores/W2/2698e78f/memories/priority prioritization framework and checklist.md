**Prioritization Framework Recommended (Nov 2023):**

**Three-Tier System:**
1. **Must-Dos:** Top 3-5 non-negotiable essential tasks (daily/weekly)
2. **Should-Dos:** Important but flexible tasks (reading, learning, socializing)
3. **Nice-To-Dos:** Non-essential but enjoyable tasks (entertainment, relaxation)

**Boundary-Setting Strategies Given:**
1. Use Must-Dos as a filter for incoming requests
2. Schedule buffer time between commitments
3. Communicate priorities clearly with colleagues, friends, family
4. Practice self-care regularly
5. Batch similar tasks for efficiency
6. Leave room for spontaneity

**Simple Checklist Created:**

*Daily:*
- Identify Must-Dos (3-5 tasks)
- Schedule self-care (meditation, journaling)
- Check requests against Must-Dos before committing
- Leave buffer time

*Weekly:*
- Review priorities and adjust
- Evaluate commitments and say no to non-essential tasks
- Celebrate self-care activities and progress

**User Preference:** Flexible scheduling approach preferred over rigid structures. User appreciated visual checklist format, plans to display it where visible daily.
