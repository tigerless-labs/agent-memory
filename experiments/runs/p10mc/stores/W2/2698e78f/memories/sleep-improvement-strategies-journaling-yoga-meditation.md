## Sleep Improvement Journey

**Current situation:**
- Struggling to fall asleep some nights despite maintaining journaling practice and consistent sleep schedule
- Using yoga and meditation as self-care practices to help with anxiety and sleep quality

**Strategies being tried:**
1. Sleep environment: dark, quiet, cool bedroom (60-67°F)
2. Bedtime routine: reading, soothing music, gentle stretches, warm bath
3. Screen avoidance: minimizing 1 hour before bed to protect melatonin
4. Exercise: regular activity but avoiding vigorous exercise 3-4 hours before bed
5. Caffeine/nicotine/alcohol: avoiding in afternoon and evening
6. Aromatherapy: lavender, chamomile scents
7. Morning sunlight exposure for circadian rhythm regulation

**Self-care activities:**
- Yoga: helps calm mind/body, regulates cortisol, improves sleep depth
- Meditation: increases melatonin, improves sleep stages (deep sleep and REM)
- Both practices helping reduce anxiety levels

- Discussing progress with Dr. Smith in therapy sessions (every two weeks)
