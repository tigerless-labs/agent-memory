## Caymus Special Selection Cabernet Sauvignon

**Winery**: Caymus Vineyards, family-owned since 1972 by Chuck Wagner

**Composition & Aging**
- 100% Cabernet Sauvignon
- Sourced from select vineyards in Napa Valley, including Special Selection Vineyard in Rutherford
- Aged 20 months in French oak barrels (90% new oak)

**Tasting Profile**
- Dark fruits: black cherry, blackberry, black currant, hints of dark chocolate and mocha
- Spice and oak: cinnamon, nutmeg, clove, vanilla, caramel
- Velvety smooth tannins with long, satisfying finish
- Well-balanced acidity

**Awards & Recognition**
- Wine Spectator's Top 100 Wines of the Year (multiple times)
- Wine Enthusiast's Wine of the Year (2012)
- 95+ ratings from Robert Parker, Wine Spectator, Vinous

**Price**: $150–$180 per bottle (current vintage 2018)

**Food Pairings**
- Grilled steak (ribeye, filet mignon)
- Roasted lamb or beef tenderloin
- Pan-seared duck breast or game meats
- Mature cheeses (cheddar, gouda) and charcuterie

**Availability**: Fine wine shops, online retailers, directly from the winery
