## Website Builder Options Reviewed

**Date:** March 2023

**Platforms recommended:**
- WordPress.com — hosted version with templates and plugins
- Wix — popular choice, drag-and-drop, extensive templates
- Squarespace — stylish, good for creatives
- Weebly — intuitive interface
- Carrd — simple one-page websites, free option
- Strikingly — one-page portfolio specialist

**Selection criteria discussed:**
- Ease of use and learning curve
- Customization flexibility
- Scalability for growing business
- Budget constraints
- Integration with other tools
- Storage and bandwidth requirements

**User's choice:** Wix Unlimited plan
- Reasoning: Good feature-to-price balance
- Influenced by positive reviews from online community members
- Appealed to user's need for user-friendly, drag-and-drop interface

**Key features evaluated:**
- Mobile responsiveness
- Analytics and reporting
- Email marketing integration
- SEO optimization tools
- Customer support quality
