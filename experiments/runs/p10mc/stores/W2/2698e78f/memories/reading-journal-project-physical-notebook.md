## Reading Journal Project

**Project Goal:**
Start a dedicated reading journal to track reading progress and jot down thoughts on books.

**Format Decision:**
- Starting with a generic/blank notebook that will be customized to fit individual needs
- Prefers physical journal over digital solution

**Rationale:**
- Found Notes app on phone was only an okay temporary solution
- Finds handwriting more intimate and personal than digital notes
- Enjoys being able to flip back through physical pages to see progress over time
- Wants a keepsake to reflect on reading journey in the future

**Potential Journal Elements to Explore:**
- Book-inspired art or quotes (to be decided)
- Tracking dates books were finished
- Recording thoughts and reflections
- Rating system for books
- Character analysis or thematic exploration

**Next Steps:**
- Acquire a generic notebook
- Develop personal templates and formats for entries
- Begin regular journaling habit
