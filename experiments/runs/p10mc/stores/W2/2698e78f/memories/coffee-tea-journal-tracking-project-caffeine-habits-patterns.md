The user wants to keep a coffee or tea journal to:
- Track daily caffeine intake
- Identify patterns and habits in consumption
- Explore flavor preferences
- Monitor energy levels and correlation with consumption
- Track mood and emotional changes related to caffeine
- Develop a consistent daily routine

**Journal details to track:**
- Date and time of consumption
- Type of coffee or tea (brand, flavor, brewing method)
- Amount consumed (cup size)
- Time of day (morning, afternoon, evening)
- Activity or situation (work, study, socializing, relaxation)
- Energy levels (before and after)
- Mood and emotions (before and after)

**Potential journal formats:**
- Simple grid layout with columns for date, time, type, amount, notes
- Daily spread with sections for morning, afternoon, evening
- Coffee/tea tracker with grid and rating system (1-5 stars)
- Habit tracker page
- Free-form journaling
- Combination hybrid approach

**Tools mentioned:**
- Bullet Journal
- Passion Planner
- Habitica
- Day One
- Evernote
