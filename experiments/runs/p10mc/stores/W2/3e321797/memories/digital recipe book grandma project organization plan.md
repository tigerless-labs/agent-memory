## Project Overview
User is creating a **digital recipe book** dedicated to preserving grandma's recipes as a family heirloom and tribute to her culinary legacy.

## Organization Structure

### Primary Categorization (by Course)
- Appetizers
- Soups
- Salads
- Main Dishes
- Sides
- Desserts
- Beverages

### Tags (Secondary Organization)
- **Ingredients:** Main ingredients (chicken, beef, vegetables, etc.)
- **Cooking Time:** Quick & Easy (under 30 min), Weeknight, Special Occasion
- **Difficulty Level:** Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
- **Special Diets:** Gluten-free, Vegan, etc. (if applicable)

### Recipe Entry Format
Each recipe includes:
- Course category
- Ingredient tags
- Cooking time
- Difficulty level
- Serving size and yields
- Cooking methods/techniques
- Photos of finished dish (if available)
- Personal notes and stories

## Content: Stories & Legacy Section
Dedicated section preserving grandma's culinary heritage:
- **Cooking Philosophy:** Her approach (fresh ingredients, experimentation, traditional techniques)
- **Favorite Ingredients:** Go-to herbs, spices, types of meat with stories about why they mattered
- **Cooking Memories:** Favorite recipes made together, special meal memories, lessons learned
- **Family Traditions:** Role of food in celebrations (holidays, special occasions, cultural heritage)
- **Personal Anecdotes:** Stories about her personality, humor, patience in kitchen, love of feeding others
- **Vintage Photos:** Pictures of grandma in kitchen, cooking with family, enjoying meals together

## Tools & Storage
- Considering recipe management apps (Paprika Recipe Manager, Yummly, BigOven)
- Or cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) for accessibility and sharing
- Consistent formatting and naming conventions for easy navigation
- Searchable design for quick recipe lookup

## Future Possibilities
- Collaborative family effort (invite relatives to contribute recipes and memories)
- Could expand to family cookbook with multi-generational recipes
