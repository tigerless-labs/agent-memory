## Birding Journal Practice

**Duration:** Started ~November 2022, ongoing as of May 2023 (6 months)

**Journal organization approach:**
- Date and location for each entry
- Habitat type (forest, wetland, backyard, etc.)
- Species recorded (common names, scientific names, or both)
- Distinctive features observed:
  - Plumage patterns, colors, markings
  - Beak shape and size
  - Leg and foot color
  - Size/shape comparisons to known birds
  - Behavioral notes (foraging, flocking, etc.)
- Sketches and doodles to aid memory of shapes and patterns
- Photos when possible
- Habitat and environmental notes (vegetation, water, weather)
- Identification challenges and confusing characteristics noted

**Purpose:**
- Track progress in identification skills
- Record sightings and observation history
- Identify patterns in bird populations and migration
- Reference for future identifications
- Foundation for improved birding knowledge
