## AI-Generated Poetry Platforms

1. **AI Poet** — generates poetry with options for different styles, themes, emotions
2. **PoetryBot** — chatbot that generates poetry based on user input, focuses on collaboration and conversation
3. **Deep Poetry** — uses deep learning for poetry generation, focuses on creativity and originality
4. **Verse By Verse** — generates poetry with focus on structured forms (sonnets, haikus)

## AI-Assisted Poetry Writing Tools

1. **Language is a Virus** — focuses on experimental and avant-garde poetry styles
2. **PoemPortraits** — generates personalized and introspective poetry based on user input
3. **WriteSonic** — focuses on music and sound-inspired poetry generation
4. **AI Writer** — assists writers and helps overcome writer's block

## Human-AI Collaboration Tools

1. **Poetry Forge** — enables humans and AI to co-create poetry with collaborative editing and feedback
2. **AI Writer's Assistant (AIA)** — features idea suggestion, word choice assistance, style guidance
3. **Lyrical AI (LAI)** — assists songwriters and poets with lyrics, melodies, and rhymes suggestions
4. **Collaborative Poetry Generation Tool (CPGT)** — research-based platform exploring human-AI creative potential

## Communities

1. **r/AIPoetry** — subreddit for discussing AI-generated poetry
2. **AI Poetry Discord** — community server for AI poetry enthusiasts with channels for sharing and feedback
3. **Poetry Generation Forum** — dedicated forum for poetry generation discussions
4. **AI-powered Poetry Tools Discord** — for designers and developers working on AI poetry tools

## Benefits of Human-AI Poetry Collaboration

- Overcomes writer's block through idea generation
- Assists with poem structure, meter, and rhyme scheme
- Enables collaborative learning between human writers and AI
- Creates opportunities for new and innovative poetry forms

## Challenges to Address

- Creative control and user satisfaction
- AI reliance on familiar patterns and clichés
- Limited emotional understanding by AI
- Difficulty evaluating quality of human-AI collaborative work
