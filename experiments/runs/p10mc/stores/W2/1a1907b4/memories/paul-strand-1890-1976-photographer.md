American photographer (1890-1976). Born NYC. Self-taught but influenced by Alfred Stieglitz. Joined Stieglitz's Photo-Secession group in 1915. Shifted to documentary photography in 1920s, capturing everyday life and social issues. Traveled extensively photographing rural communities, urban landscapes, industrial sites. Famous for Mexico photo series starting 1932—considered important documentary work. Believed photography was powerful tool for social change to raise awareness of injustices.
