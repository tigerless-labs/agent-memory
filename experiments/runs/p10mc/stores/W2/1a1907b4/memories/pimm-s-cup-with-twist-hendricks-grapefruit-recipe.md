## Pimm's Cup with a Twist — Hendrick's & Ruby Red Grapefruit

### Overview
User is experimenting with summer cocktails using Hendrick's gin, inspired by a recent mixology class. Decided on **Pimm's Cup with a Twist** as the weekend project.

### Recipe Components

**Ruby Red Grapefruit Simple Syrup:**
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1 cup (240ml) water
- 1/2 cup (120ml) freshly squeezed Ruby Red grapefruit juice
- Optional: 1–2 slices of grapefruit peel

Instructions: Combine sugar and water, heat until dissolved. Add grapefruit juice and optional peel. Remove from heat, steep 30+ minutes to meld flavors. Strain into clean glass bottle, refrigerate.

**Cocktail Base (from original Pimm's Cup with a Twist):**
- 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin
- 0.5 oz lemon juice (fresh)
- 0.5 oz grapefruit simple syrup (as prepared above)
- 0.5 oz Pimm's No. 1
- Splash of ginger beer

**Technique:**
- Muddle cucumber slices (not cucumber juice) to release intense, fresh flavors and add texture
- Use large, clear ice cubes or spheres

**Glassware & Presentation:**
- Collins glass (tall, slender shape showcases color layers and garnishes)
- Garnishes: cucumber slice/wheel, Ruby Red grapefruit slice/wedge, fresh mint sprig, optional edible flowers (violas, pansies, lavender)

### Why These Choices
- Ruby Red grapefruit variety chosen for balanced sweet-tart flavor and pink hue
- Muddling cucumber for pronounced fresh flavor vs. cucumber juice (more subtle)
- Grapefruit bitterness balances Pimm's sweetness; citrus complements Hendrick's botanicals
- Collins glass emphasizes visual appeal and pink/green color layers

### Context
- User recently attended mixology class, made classic Pimm's Cup and other classic cocktails
- Excited to experiment with new flavor combinations
- Weekend project (May 2023)
