## Bird Call Learning Program

**Start date:** ~April 2023 (approximately 1 month before May 21, 2023 session)

**Method:**
- Listening to bird calls online
- Finding this approach very effective for learning

**Strategy:**
- Learning calls of birds commonly seen in local area
- Also focusing on calls of target species of interest
- Plans to continue this practice

**Benefits recognized:**
- Improves species identification ability
- Complements visual identification from binoculars and field guides
- Helps narrow down possibilities when observing birds
