## Seminal Research Papers

1. **"Generating Poetry with Deep Learning"** by Zhang and Lapata (2014)
   - Introduces neural poetry concept
   - Foundational work on deep learning for poetry generation

2. **"Neural Poetry: Learning to Generate Poetic Language"** by Yi et al. (2018)
   - Explores neural networks for poetic language generation
   - Focuses on meter, rhyme, and sentiment

3. **"Poetry Generation with Recurrent Neural Networks"** by Ghosh et al. (2016)
   - Uses RNNs for poetry generation
   - Emphasizes language modeling and sequence generation

4. **"Style-Conditioned Poetry Generation"** by Wang et al. (2020)
   - Introduces style control for generated poems
   - Allows control over tone and style

5. **"AI-generated Poetry: A Survey"** by Ghosh et al. (2020)
   - Comprehensive survey covering technical and design aspects
   - Overview of the field

6. **"Designing AI-powered Poetry Tools"** by Mackay et al. (2020)
   - Focuses on UX and human-AI interaction for poetry tools

## Influential Articles

1. **"Can AI Write Poetry?"** by Janelle Shane (The Atlantic, 2019)
   - Explores capabilities and limitations of AI-generated poetry
   - Discusses intersection of AI and creativity

2. **"The Future of Poetry is AI-Generated"** by Emily Mackay (The Guardian, 2020)
   - Discusses how AI-generated poetry can augment human creativity
   - Explores how AI can assist writers

3. **"AI-Generated Poetry: A New Frontier in Creative Writing"** by Ross Goodwin (Medium, 2019)
   - Discusses new possibilities for creative writing
   - Explores human-AI collaboration

4. **"The AI Poetry Revolution"** by Allison Parrish (The New Yorker, 2020)
   - Explores rise of AI-generated poetry
   - Discusses implications for literary world

## Books

1. **"The Next Word: Where Minds and Machines Meet"** by Janelle Shane (2020)
   - Explores intersection of AI and creativity
   - Focus on language, poetry, and art

2. **"AI in the Humanities: A Critical Review"** edited by David M. Berry and Anders Fagerjord (2020)
   - Collection of essays on AI in humanities
   - Includes poetry and literature applications
