Town on Outer Banks of North Carolina. Known for Wright brothers' historic first powered flight on December 17, 1903. Wright brothers chose location for consistent winds and sandy terrain. Wright Brothers National Memorial commemorates achievement.

Attractions:
- Beaches: long stretch popular for swimming, sunbathing, water sports
- Kitty Hawk Woods Coastal Reserve: nature preserve with walking trails, birdwatching, kayaking
- Watersports: surfing, kayaking, paddleboarding, fishing
- Golf courses in area
- Outer Banks history: part of barrier island chain along NC coast with rich history; historic lighthouses, museums
- Dining and shopping: local cuisine, seafood, souvenirs
