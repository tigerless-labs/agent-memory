## Reading Interests

**Enjoyed:**
- "The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August" by Claire North — liked its unique blend of fantasy, reincarnation, and philosophical themes

**Specific interests:**
- Unique, well-developed magical systems (liked The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang)
- Complex world-building and diverse characters
- Prefers standalone novels over series

**Currently planning to read:**
- "The Fifth Season" by N.K. Jemisin (first book of Broken Earth trilogy) — Hugo Award winner 2016
  - Features orogeny magic system
  - Non-linear narrative structure
  - Multiple POV characters: Essun, Damaya, Syenite
  - Deals with mature themes: trauma, oppression, violence

**Currently interested in:**
- "The Golem and the Jinni" by Helene Wecker (2013)
  - Early 20th-century New York City setting
  - Follows Chava (golem made of clay) and Ahmad (jinni of fire/air)
  - Themes: identity, community, morality, cultural heritage
  - National Book Award finalist 2013, Mythopoeic Fantasy Award winner 2014
  - Lyrical writing style, character-driven, moderate pacing
