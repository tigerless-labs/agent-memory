American photographer (1865-1946). Born NYC. Studied at Art Students League. Started as portrait photographer but pioneered aerial photography in 1890s using cameras mounted on balloons, then airplanes (1900s). Worked with aviator Orville Wright. Used various cameras and techniques to capture landscape patterns from above—new artistic perspective.

Innovator in nighttime photography, unusual for his era. Experimented with artificial light sources: electric lights, gas lamps, torches. Developed equipment using flash powder and magnesium wire for bursts of bright light in darkness. Nighttime photographs captured urban scenes (city streets, buildings) with dramatic, moody atmosphere and sharp light-dark contrasts. Work influenced later photographers exploring night photography.
