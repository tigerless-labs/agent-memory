American photographer (1864-1952). Born into prominent Virginia family. Began photography career 1890s as apprentice. Opened own studio in Washington DC in 1894. Pioneer in architectural photography—documented historic American buildings to raise awareness of preservation. Known for elegant portraits of politicians, celebrities, socialites including Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, Alice Roosevelt Longworth. Work characterized by elegance, simplicity, attention to detail. Believed photography could be tool for social change.
