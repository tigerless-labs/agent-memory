## Social Media's Impact on Fashion Industry

**Key transformations:**
- Increased brand exposure through Instagram, Twitter, Facebook
- Direct consumer engagement with behind-the-scenes content
- Influencer marketing as major driver
- Real-time trend monitoring and response
- Fashion week live streaming
- Increased awareness of sustainable/ethical fashion

**Traditional advertising:** Still has a role but transformed; luxury brands use high-end magazines and billboards; social media more cost-effective and targeted for most brands

**Notable brand examples:**
- Burberry: live-streamed London Fashion Week 2010; uses Snapchat, Instagram, Facebook
- Nike: "Just Do It" campaign with shareable content and influencer partnerships
- Glossier: beauty brand built entirely through social media
- ASOS: major e-commerce success through engaged community; Instagram shopping feature
- Boohoo: established as major player through social media

**Influencers:** Kylie Jenner and Chiara Ferragni as major brand ambassadors
