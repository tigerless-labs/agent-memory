## Training Plan

**Start date:** May 22, 2023 (Monday)
**Goal:** Complete 5K charity run
**Current routine:** 3-mile jogs every Tuesday and Thursday morning
**Weekly plans:** Increase mileage, add hill repeats, incorporate strength training

## Running Technique Tips
- Posture: upright, ears/shoulders/hips/ankles aligned
- Footstrike: midfoot or forefoot (not heel strike)
- Cadence: 160-170 steps per minute
- Arm swing: 90-degree bend, close to body, relaxed
- Breathing: deep, rhythmic (2-3 steps inhale/exhale)
- Core engagement: pull belly button toward spine
- Hip/glute activation: squeeze glutes, push hips forward
- Ankle mobility: quick, light movements

## Warm-up Routine (10-15 minutes)
1. Light cardio: 5-10 min jogging/walking/jumping jacks
2. Leg swings: 30 sec-1 min per leg
3. Hip circles: 30 sec-1 min each direction
4. Knee lifts: 10-15 reps per leg
5. Calf raises: 10-15 reps
6. Arm circles: 30 sec-1 min

## Cool-down Routine (10-15 minutes)
1. Walking: 5-10 minutes to lower heart rate
2. Static stretches (hold 15-30 seconds each):
   - Hamstrings: forward bend
   - Quads: standing, lift leg behind
   - Calves: step back, heel on ground
   - Hip flexors: kneeling position
   - Glutes: lying down, cross legs, lift hips
3. Foam rolling: IT band, quads, hamstrings

## Hydration Strategy
- Before run: 16-20 oz water 1-2 hours prior
- During run: For runs >30-45 min, drink 4-6 oz every 10-15 min
- For runs >60 min: use sports drink with electrolytes
- Urine check: pale yellow = well-hydrated; dark yellow = dehydrated
- Post-run: 16-20 oz water/sports drink within 30 minutes
- Avoid: caffeine, sugary drinks

## Shin Splint Prevention
- Gradual progression: increase mileage slowly
- Proper footwear: replace every 300-400 miles or 3-4 months
- Ankle strengthening: calf raises, ankle circles, single-leg balance
- Running form: midfoot strike, upright posture, avoid overstriding
- Stretch and foam roll: calves, Achilles, shins
- Run on softer surfaces: trails, grass, treadmill (avoid concrete)
- Cross-training: cycling, swimming, elliptical to reduce repetitive stress
- Compression socks/sleeves for additional support

## Hill Repeats Technique
- Choose hill: 1-3 minute climb, steep but maintainable
- Warm up: 10-15 min easy running before starting
- Start with: 3-5 repeats, increase gradually
- Maintain form: upright posture, engaged core, quick turnover
- Recovery: walk/jog down hill between repeats
- Cool down: 10-15 min easy running after
- Frequency: once per week with 2-3 rest days between
- Types of hills:
  - Short/steep (1-2 min): explosive power
  - Medium (2-3 min): lactate tolerance
  - Long (3-5 min): endurance and stamina

## Strength Training Exercises

**Glutes:**
- Squats: feet shoulder-width, bend until thighs parallel to ground
- Lunges: large step forward, lower until back knee near ground
- Deadlifts: grab weight shoulder-width, lift to hip level
- Glute bridges: lie on back, lift hips up, squeeze glutes

**Hamstrings:**
- Deadlifts: (see above)
- Leg curls: curl legs up toward glutes
- Leg press: push platform away with feet
- Romanian deadlifts: hinge at hips, keep back straight

**Quads:**
- Squats: (see above)
- Leg press: (see above)
- Leg extensions: lift weight by straightening legs
- Step-ups: step up onto bench, alternate legs

**Recommendations:**
- Strength training: 2-3 times per week with 2-3 rest days
- Start light, progress gradually
- Focus on proper form
- Include core and calf exercises for overall running efficiency
