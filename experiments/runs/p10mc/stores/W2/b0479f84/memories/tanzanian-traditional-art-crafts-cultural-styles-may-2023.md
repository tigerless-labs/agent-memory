## Tanzanian Art & Crafts by Cultural Group (May 2023)

**Materials:**
- Northern regions (Maasai): Beadwork
- Coastal regions: Shell and coconut shell crafts
- Southern regions (Makonde): Wood carving

**Styles & Techniques:**
- **Maasai** (Northern): Intricate beadwork
- **Makonde** (Southern): Intricate wood carving  
- **Chaga** (Northern): Woven baskets and mats
- **Tingatinga** (originated in Tanzania): Stylized animals and plants native to the region

**Functional Items:**
- **Maasai Shuka**: Colorful cloth used as garment or blanket
- **Kanga**: Printed fabric used as wraparound skirt or dress

**Key Characteristic:**
Traditional art and crafts reflect Tanzania's cultural diversity through unique materials, styles, techniques, symbols, motifs, and functionality specific to different regions and cultural groups.
