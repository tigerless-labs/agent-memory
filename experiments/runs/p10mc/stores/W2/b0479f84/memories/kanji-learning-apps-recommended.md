User struggling to read kanji fluently and wants practice apps. Top recommendations:

**Primary:**
- **WaniKani** — comprehensive kanji platform using mnemonics, radicals, spaced repetition
- **Anki** — highly customizable spaced repetition flashcard app with pre-made kanji decks
- **Kanji Study** — dedicated kanji app with stroke orders, example sentences, large database
- **Memrise** — language learning app with large kanji courses and spaced repetition

**Additional options:**
- **Joyo Kanji** — free app focusing on standardized Joyo kanji for everyday use
- **KanjiQ** — gamified quizzes on kanji readings and meanings
- **Learn Japanese with Yomiwa** — comprehensive app with kanji dictionary, flashcards, exercises
- **Kanji Grid** — unique grid-based kanji reading practice
- **Kanji Senpai** — free app with large kanji database and flashcards
- **NHK WORLD-JAPAN Kanji Master** — free online tool by Japan's public broadcaster

Recommended approach: consistency with daily practice sessions.
