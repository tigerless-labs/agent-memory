## Recommended Tanzanian Art & Craft Markets (May 2023)

**Dar es Salaam:**
1. **Mwenge Woodcarvers Market** — Known for woodcarvings and skilled craftsmen
2. **Tingatinga Arts Cooperative Society** — Founded by the late Edward Tingatinga; known for distinctive painting style

**Arusha:**
3. **Cultural Heritage Center** — Large selection of art and crafts from different regions of Tanzania
4. **Arusha Maasai Market** — Specializes in Maasai beadwork and other crafts made by Maasai artisans

**Zanzibar:**
5. **Zawadi House** (Stone Town) — Boutique offering high-quality crafts including wood carvings, textiles, and jewelry

**Bargaining Notes:**
- Bargaining is a common practice in Tanzania
- Vendors rely on sales for a living; negotiate for fair price but pay reasonably
- Tips: Be friendly and polite, know the market value, start with low offer, be willing to walk away, approach with positive attitude
