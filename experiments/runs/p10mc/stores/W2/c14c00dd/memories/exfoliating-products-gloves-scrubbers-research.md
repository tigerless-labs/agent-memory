## Exfoliating Products Research

User has been using a loofah for exfoliation but is open to trying new products.

**Exfoliating Gloves Recommended:**
1. **Sugarbear Exfoliating Gloves** — gentle yet effective, soft plush material, suitable for daily exfoliation
2. **Dermalogica Daily Microfoliant Gloves** — removes dead skin cells effectively, gentle enough for daily use, suitable for most skin types
3. **Kiko Exfoliating Gloves** — more intense exfoliating surface, ideal for thicker skin or deeper exfoliation; be gentle with sensitive skin

**Scrubbers Recommended:**
1. **Konjac Sponge** — gentle daily exfoliator, natural fibers, suitable for all skin types including sensitive
2. **Dr. Dennis Gross Skincare alpha beta peel Body & Face Scrub** — physical and chemical exfoliants combined, deeper exfoliation, use cautiously if sensitive skin
3. **The Body Shop Himalayan Salt Scrub** — Himalayan salt and sweet almond oil, nourishing and exfoliating; use 1–2 times per week

**Key Difference from Loofah:**
- Loofahs can be harsh on skin, especially sensitive skin
- Gloves better for daily exfoliation
- Scrubbers better suited for 1–2 times per week
- Generally gentler and more effective than loofahs
