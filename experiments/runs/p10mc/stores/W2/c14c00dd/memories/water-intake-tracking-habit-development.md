## Water Intake Tracking Habit

User is developing a habit of drinking more water and has a refillable water bottle on bathroom counter.

**Tracking Methods Suggested:**
1. **Water tracking apps:** Waterlogged, Hydro Coach, Plant Nanny — send reminders, provide personalized goals based on activity level and climate
2. **Physical log:** Small notebook or sticky note on fridge to log time and amount daily
3. **Stickers/markers:** Mark consumed glasses/bottles on water bottle or chart

**Motivation Tips:**
1. Set specific daily goals and reminders
2. Make it a habit at set times (wake up, before meals)
3. Infuse water with lemon, lime, cucumber for taste
4. Keep water bottle accessible throughout day
5. Reward yourself when reaching daily goal
6. Monitor progress with photos or log
7. Get accountability from friend/family member
8. Educate self on benefits (skin health, energy, digestion)

**Additional Tips:**
- Drink full glass upon waking to rehydrate after sleep
- Sip throughout day rather than gulping
- Avoid drinking too close to bedtime to minimize nighttime awakenings
- Developing habit takes time and consistency; start with small goals and gradually increase
