User is planning to fix a leaky faucet in their bathroom themselves rather than immediately calling a plumber.

**User's Approach:**
- Has some DIY experience and feels comfortable attempting this
- Will call professional plumber if issues arise during repair

**DIY Repair Steps Given:**
1. Identify leak source (handle, O-ring, valve stem)
2. Turn off water supply at shut-off valves under sink
3. Disassemble faucet to access leaky component
4. Replace worn-out part (O-ring, gasket, or valve stem)
5. Reassemble faucet in reverse order
6. Turn on water slowly to check for leaks

**Resources Mentioned:**
- YouTube tutorials for specific faucet repair
- Home Depot, Lowe's, The Spruce websites for guides
- Faucet manufacturer's website or manual

**When to Call Plumber:**
- Complex repairs (cartridge or valve body issues)
- Multiple leaks or leaking from multiple areas
- No DIY experience or discomfort with repairs
- Water damage or mold around faucet

**Tools Needed:**
- Faucet repair kit or specific tools (basin wrench, adjustable pliers)
