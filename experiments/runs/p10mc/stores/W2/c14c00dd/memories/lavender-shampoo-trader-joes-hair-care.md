## Lavender Shampoo & Hair Care

User picked up a lavender scented shampoo at Trader Joe's on a whim and reports it has been doing wonders for their hair.

**Hair Health Tips Given:**
1. Moisturize regularly with hydrating conditioner, hair mask 1–2 times per week
2. Use sulfate-free products to avoid stripping natural oils
3. Protect from heat with heat protectant spray; limit heat styling to 1–2 times per week
4. Get regular trims every 6–8 weeks to prevent split ends
5. Be gentle when combing: use wide-tooth comb or detangling brush, start from ends working up
6. Avoid over-washing: wash every other day or every 2–3 days
7. Use microfiber towel or old t-shirt instead of regular towels to reduce friction and breakage
8. Eat balanced diet rich in omega-3s, vitamins, minerals (salmon, walnuts, spinach, eggs, nuts)
9. Reduce stress through meditation, yoga, deep breathing
10. Protect from sun with hat or SPF hair product

**Lavender Specific Tips:**
- Lavender oil soothes itchy scalp and promotes relaxation
- Mix lavender oil with olive oil for pre-shampoo treatment to nourish and calm hair
- Helps reduce dandruff and flakiness
- Look for lavender-infused shampoos/conditioners
