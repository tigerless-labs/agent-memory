## Key Facts
- **Depth:** ~36,070 feet (10,994 meters)
- **Location:** Western Pacific Ocean
- **Exploration history:** Trieste submersible reached the bottom in 1960
- **Current exploration method:** Unmanned underwater vehicles (UUVs)

## Species Discovered
- Mariana snailfish
- Amphipods
- Cusk eels
- Many with bioluminescence adaptations

## Exploration Coverage
- Approximately 90% of the ocean remains unexplored
- Deep-sea exploration technology continues to evolve
