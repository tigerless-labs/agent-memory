**Pea Growing Setup**

User planning to grow peas on a trellis structure.

**Trellis specifications:**
- Height: at least 6–8 feet tall
- Sturdy construction required
- Secure anchoring to ground to prevent wind toppling

**Support methods:**
1. Twine or string ties around trellis and stems (soft materials only)
2. Pea sticks at base (small thin sticks, branches, or bamboo skewers)
3. Nylon or polypropylene mesh netting draped over trellis
4. Individual stakes for varieties prone to flopping (e.g., snow peas)

**Care notes:**
- Prune regularly to encourage bushy growth
- Remove weak or spindly growth
- Trim vine tips to encourage branching
- Full sun location with good air circulation
- Regular watering (avoid overwatering to prevent leggy growth)
