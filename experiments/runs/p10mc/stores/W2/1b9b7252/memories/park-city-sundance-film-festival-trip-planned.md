## Park City, Utah — Sundance Film Festival Trip

**Trip Details:**
- **Destination:** Park City, Utah
- **Event:** Sundance Film Festival (January)
- **Duration:** Not yet specified
- **Travelers:** Family of four — user, both parents, younger brother
- **Accommodation:** Staying in Park City (chosen over Salt Lake City for immersion and atmosphere; willing to pay premium rates)

**Attractions & Activities:**

*Hiking Trails:*
- Jordanelle Reservoir Trail (3.5 miles, moderate)
- Mid-Mountain Trail (13.5 miles, scenic aspen forests)
- Guardsman Pass (4.5 miles, panoramic views)
- Rob's Trail (4.5 miles, good for biking and hiking)

*Cultural Events & Venues:*
- Sundance Film Festival (January) — largest independent film festival in US
- Park City Museum (mining history exhibits)
- Kimball Arts Festival (August) — 3-day festival with 200+ artists, live music, food
- Egyptian Theatre (concerts, plays, musicals)
- Park City Institute (concerts, theater, lectures)

*Other Activities:*
- Skiing/snowboarding: Park City Mountain, Deer Valley Resort, Canyons Village
- Mountain biking (400+ miles of trails)
- Alpine Coaster (thrilling mountain ride)
- Historic Main Street (shops, restaurants, galleries)

**Accommodation Options:**

*Hotels Discussed:*
- The Grand Summit Hotel — Indoor pool, free shuttle to festival venues; at Park City Mountain base
- The Chateau at Deer Valley — Luxury option, outdoor pool, free shuttle; at Deer Valley Resort base
- The Park City Peaks Hotel — Heart of Park City, indoor pool, free shuttle to venues

*Vacation Rentals:*
- Park City Mountain Resort Condos — kitchens, fireplaces, resort amenities
- Deer Valley Resort Condos — luxury, private hot tub, resort access
- Park City Vacation Rentals — range of properties (cabins to townhomes), many within walking distance to venues

**Booking Tips:**
- Book accommodation ASAP — prices increase closer to festival dates
- Look for properties with kitchens (more cost-effective for meals)
- Read reviews and check amenities carefully

**Festival Attendance Strategy:**
- Register Sundance account for updates and early ticket access
- Use waitlist for popular films/events
- Consider ticket packages (often go on sale before individual tickets)
- Individual tickets sell out fast — act quickly
- Volunteer option available for exclusive tickets and behind-the-scenes access
- Download official Sundance app for schedules, maps, and updates

**Tips for Family Festival Experience:**
- Plan ahead, research schedule in advance
- Prioritize must-see films (can't attend everything)
- Take breaks to rest and recharge
- Pack snacks and water
- Familiarize with venue locations beforehand
- Dress in layers (Park City is cold/snowy in January)
- Free bus system connects resorts and town (easy transportation)
- Stay flexible for schedule changes
