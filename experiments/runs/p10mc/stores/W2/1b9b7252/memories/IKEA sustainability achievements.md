## IKEA Sustainability Initiatives

### Materials & Products
- 100% cotton sourcing from sustainable sources (organic cotton, Better Cotton Initiative)
- Eco-friendly materials: bamboo, recycled plastic
- Energy-efficient products: LED bulbs, induction cooktops, water-saving taps
- Products designed for disassembly and recyclability

### Energy & Emissions
- Nearly doubled renewable energy production since 2009 (solar panels on stores and facilities)
- 43% carbon footprint reduction from stores and distribution centers
- Target: 15% reduction in carbon emissions from transportation per product by 2020

### Circular Economy
- Take-back programs for product returns
- 89% waste recycling rate at stores and warehouses

### Recognition
- Named one of World's Most Ethical Companies by Ethisphere Institute
- Topped 2020 Sustainable Brand Index in Europe
