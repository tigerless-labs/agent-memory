**Crop Rotation Interest**

User interested in implementing crop rotation to break pest/disease cycles and optimize soil health.

**Planned crop families for rotation:**
- Solanaceae: tomatoes, peppers, eggplants, potatoes
- Legumes: beans, peas, lentils (nitrogen-fixing)
- Cucurbitaceae: cucumbers, squash, melons
- Brassicas: broccoli, cauliflower, kale, cabbage
- Alliums: onions, garlic, leeks

**Rotation strategy being considered:**
- Move crops to different family beds each season
- Example sequence: Tomatoes (Year 1) → Beans (Year 2) → Cucumbers (Year 3)

**Additional practices:**
- Incorporate cover crops between cycles
- Use trap crops (marigolds, nasturtiums) to lure pests away
- Keep detailed records of crop performance and pest issues
- Consider nitrogen-fixing legumes to enrich soil for subsequent crops
