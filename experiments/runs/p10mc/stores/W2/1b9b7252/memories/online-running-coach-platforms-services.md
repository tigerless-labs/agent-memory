## Online Running Coach Platforms and Services

### Coaching Platforms (with marketplace)
1. **Training Peaks** — Well-established platform connecting athletes with certified coaches; filter by event (marathon), pace range, and multi-sport experience; has rigorous "Training Peaks Certified Coach" program
2. **Coach.me** — Personalized coaching with one-on-one coaching, customized training plans, progress tracking
3. **Runcoach** — Connects runners with experienced coaches; offers personalized plans, workout analysis, ongoing support
4. **Vdot Coaching** — Personalized plans, workout tracking, access to experienced coaches
5. **Nike Run Club Coaching** — Free coaching with personalized plans and workout tracking

### Dedicated Coaching Services
1. **McMillan Running** — Led by renowned coach Greg McMillan; offers personalized plans, online coaching, training camps
2. **Hanson Coaching Services** — Led by the Hanson Brothers; personalized plans, online coaching, training camps
3. **FIRST** (Furman Institute of Running and Scientific Training) — Science-based training with personalized plans, online coaching, training camps

### Certified Coach Organizations
- **USATF** (USA Track & Field) — Find certified coaches by area or online
- **RRCA** (Road Runners Club of America) — Search certified coaches by area or online

### Finding Right Coach
- Search by marathon specialty
- Filter by pace range (e.g., 3:00-4:00, 4:00-5:00)
- Look for coaches with multi-sport experience (especially soccer/tennis)
- Review bios for coaching philosophy and certifications
- Contact 2-3 candidates to compare approaches
