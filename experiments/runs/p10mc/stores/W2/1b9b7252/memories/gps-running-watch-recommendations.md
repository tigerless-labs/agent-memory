## GPS Running Watch Recommendations

### Top Models by Category

**Premium / Advanced:**
- **Garmin Forerunner 945** — High-end with music storage, Garmin Pay, performance monitoring, accurate GPS, long battery life, comfortable design

**Smartwatch / Style-Focused:**
- **Apple Watch Series 7 (GPS)** — Stylish, built-in GPS, heart rate monitoring, Apple Health integration, music storage, AirPods streaming

**Multisport / Serious Athletes:**
- **Polar Vantage V** — Advanced features: power meter, running power, stride detection, detailed analytics, customizable training plans

**Mid-Range / Budget-Conscious:**
- **Suunto 3 Fitness** — Accurate GPS, heart rate monitoring, customizable training plans, affordable
- **Fitbit Ionic** — Built-in GPS, heart rate monitoring, guided breathing sessions, comprehensive fitness tracking

### Key Features to Prioritize
1. GPS Accuracy — High-quality GPS chip + GLONASS support for accurate distance, pace, route
2. Heart Rate Monitoring — Optical HR monitoring essential for exercise tracking
3. Water Resistance — Suitable for wet conditions (50m, 100m, or higher)
4. Battery Life — Minimum 8 hours in GPS mode (or power-saving features)
5. Comfort & Design — Fits well, suits personal style
6. App Integration — Compatible with Strava, Runkeeper, Apple Health, etc.
7. Additional Features — Music storage, mobile payments, stress tracking (optional)

### Price Range
$200–$1,000+ depending on features and brand

### Compatibility Check
- Ensure compatibility with your smartphone OS (iOS or Android)
- Consider size, weight, and personal style preferences
