**DIY Potting Soil Experimentation**

User making own potting soil mix and seeking guidance for pea growing.

**Current experimental mix:**
- Peat moss
- Vermiculite
- Perlite

**Issues with current mix for peas:**
- Peat moss too dense, can cause waterlogging
- Vermiculite too dense, insufficient drainage
- Perlite too lightweight, insufficient structure

**Recommended improved mix for peas:**
- 20-30% Peat moss or coconut coir (moisture, acidity)
- 20-30% Compost or worm castings (structure, fertility, beneficial microbes)
- 20-30% Perlite or vermiculite (drainage, aeration)
- 10-20% Garden soil or topsoil (structure, nutrients)
- 10-20% Sand or grit (drainage, structure)

**Pea-specific requirements:**
- Well-draining soil (prevent root rot)
- Good aeration for healthy roots
- pH 6.0-7.0 (slightly acidic to neutral)
- Legume inoculants may be beneficial

**Preparation:**
- Sterilize before use (bake at 300F for 30 min, or solarize 4-6 weeks in clear plastic bag)
