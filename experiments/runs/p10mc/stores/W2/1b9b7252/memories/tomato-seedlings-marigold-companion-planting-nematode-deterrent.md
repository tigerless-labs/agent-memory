**Planting Plan (May 2023, Greenville SC)**

User planning to plant tomato seedlings outdoors this weekend, using **marigolds as companion plants** to deter nematodes (microscopic root-attacking worms).

**Spacing:** Plant marigolds 6–12 inches (15–30 cm) away from tomato plants.

**Marigold varieties recommended:**
- French marigold (Tagetes patula)
- African marigold (Tagetes erecta)

**Additional nematode management strategies:**
- Crop rotation
- Soil solarization
- Nematode-resistant tomato varieties

**Companion planting benefits:**
- Marigolds attract beneficial insects (bees, butterflies)
- Creates barrier against nematodes
- Can be planted at same time or weeks before tomatoes
