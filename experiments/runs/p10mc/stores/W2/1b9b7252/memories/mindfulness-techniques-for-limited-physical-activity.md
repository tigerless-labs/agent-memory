## Mindfulness Techniques for Limited Physical Activity

Discussed May 25, 2023 as alternatives for people unable to participate in strenuous physical activities.

### Core Techniques
1. **Mindful breathing** — Count as you inhale and exhale; focus attention entirely on breath
2. **Guided imagery and visualization** — Visualize positive, calming environments
3. **Grounding exercises** — Focus on physical sensations: feet on ground, texture surfaces, breath sounds
4. **Progressive muscle relaxation** — Tense and relax each muscle group from toes to head
5. **Mindful eating** — Pay attention to taste, texture, sensation while eating; stay present

### Recommended Resources & Apps
- **Headspace app** — guided meditations and visualization exercises for stress/anxiety relief
- **Mindful.org** — free guided imagery including "The Mountain Meditation" and "The Body Scan Meditation"
- **UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center** — free guided meditations for stress reduction, relaxation, resilience
- **Mindfulness Exercises website** — free range of mindfulness and visualization exercises
- **YouTube** — variety of free guided imagery exercises

### Tips for Daily Routine Integration
- Start small: short, simple practices (few deep breaths after waking or before bed)
- Set phone/computer reminders for mindful breaks throughout day
- Practice during daily activities: washing dishes, showering, walking
- Notice when mind wanders; bring attention back to present moment
- Practice guided meditations daily to build skills

### Task-Specific Mindfulness (Showering/Washing Dishes)
- **Engage senses** — notice temperature, texture of water/soap, smells, sounds
- **Focus on present moment** — avoid multitasking, don't get distracted
- **Slow down** — take time instead of rushing; fully experience the moment
- **Be non-judgmental** — approach with open attitude, don't label as good/bad
- **Use breath as anchor** — take intentional deep breaths; use breath as focal point

### Key Insight
Mindfulness can be practiced in any activity, no matter how small. Experimentation is needed to find what works best individually.
