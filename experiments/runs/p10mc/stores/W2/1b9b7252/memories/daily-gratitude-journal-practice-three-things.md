User decided to start a daily habit of writing down three things they are grateful for. This practice is inspired by what they already do on Instagram but will be done in a dedicated physical journal rather than online.

Method: User chose to use a physical dedicated journal (not digital) for this practice, which they feel will be more meaningful for reflection.

Tips discussed included:
- Making it a consistent daily habit at a set time
- Being specific rather than general
- Mixing big and small things to be grateful for
- Reflecting on why each thing is important

This practice is meant to improve mental well-being and cultivate positivity.
