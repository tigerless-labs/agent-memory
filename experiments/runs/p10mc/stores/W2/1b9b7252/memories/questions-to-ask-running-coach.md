## Key Questions for Potential Running Coaches

### About Experience & Expertise
1. **Multi-Sport Experience:** Can you share examples coaching athletes in multiple sports, especially soccer and tennis?
2. **Similar Pace Experience:** What experience do you have coaching runners with similar pace and goals as mine?
3. **Marathon Experience:** What's your track record with marathon-focused athletes?

### About Training Philosophy & Approach
4. **Coaching Style:** What's your coaching philosophy and how will you tailor it to my specific needs?
5. **Balancing Multiple Sports:** How will you help me balance training for running, soccer, and tennis without overtraining?
6. **Periodization:** How will you periodize my training to peak for marathons while maintaining soccer/tennis performance?
7. **Customization:** How will you customize my training plan to fit my schedule, goals, and available time?

### About Communication & Support
8. **Communication Frequency:** How often will we communicate, and what's the best way to reach you?
9. **Adaptability:** How will we adapt the training plan if my soccer/tennis schedule changes unexpectedly?
10. **Support During Training:** What kind of ongoing support can I expect (e.g., workout analysis, motivation, troubleshooting)?

### About Injury Prevention & Health
11. **Injury Prevention:** How will you help me prevent injuries that might affect multiple sports?
12. **Rehabilitation Approach:** What's your approach if I do get injured?
13. **Fatigue Monitoring:** How will you monitor my fatigue levels and adjust training accordingly?

### Certifications & References
14. **Certifications:** What coaching certifications do you hold? Are you Training Peaks Certified?
15. **References:** Can you provide references from multi-sport athletes you've coached?

### Key Red Flags
- Doesn't acknowledge the complexity of multi-sport training
- Dismisses concerns about overtraining
- Won't customize approach or offers generic plans
- Lacks experience with your specific sports combination
