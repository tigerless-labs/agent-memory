## Pinterest Overview

Pinterest is an online platform for saving and organizing visual content (images, videos) into collections called **boards**.

### Core Features
- **Search & Browse**: Users can search by keywords or browse categories (food, fashion, beauty, home decor, weddings, travel)
- **Save & Share**: Users can save content to their own boards and share with followers
- **Follow**: Users can follow other users and boards to discover new content
- **Community Boards**: Multiple users can contribute and collaborate on shared boards

### Saving Content
- Can save images and videos
- Can save links to articles, websites, and blog posts
- When saving links: add title, description, and relevant image

### Cost
- **Free to use**: No fees for basic functionality
- **Free business accounts**: Available with additional tools (analytics, ability to promote pins)
- **Paid promotion**: Promoting pins or ads through Pinterest Ads requires separate ad budget
- **Content creation costs**: High-quality images/videos for business use may require investment in equipment

### Use Cases
Commonly used for planning and inspiration: home decorating, weddings, travel, and project planning
