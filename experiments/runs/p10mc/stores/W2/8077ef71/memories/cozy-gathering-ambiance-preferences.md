## Cozy Gathering Ambiance Preferences

**Lighting**:
- Dimmed lights for movie nights
- Avoid harsh, bright overhead lighting
- Creates intimate, relaxed atmosphere

**Heat and Warmth**:
- Fireplace for ambiance and warmth
- Note: Wood smoke scent can interact with candle scents (keep fireplace at moderate level)
- Prefer warm, cozy feeling over cool environments

**Candle Scents** (tested or interested in):
- **Vanilla**: Warm, comforting, pairs well with sweet fruit
- **Cinnamon**: Classic, cozy, evokes winter/warmth
- **Lavender**: Calming, soothing, subtle
- **Caramel**: Sweet, comforting, inviting
- **Woodland Berries**: Evokes fireplace, cozy atmosphere

**Textiles**:
- Extra throws and blankets in soft materials (fleece, Sherpa, faux fur)
- Variety of sizes for different uses
- Neutral colors (beige, gray, navy) or accent colors
- Creates tactile, inviting atmosphere

**Overall aesthetic**:
- Emphasizes comfort and coziness
- Encourages social interaction and conversation
- Pays attention to sensory details (scent, texture, warmth)
- Balances ambiance elements so nothing overpowers
