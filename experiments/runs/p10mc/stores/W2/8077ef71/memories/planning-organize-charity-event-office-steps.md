## Planning Office Charity Event

User interested in organizing a charity event at their office to engage colleagues, raise funds, and boost morale.

**Steps to organize:**
1. Choose cause - select registered 501(c)(3) organization aligned with values
2. Get management approval - approach HR/management with details about charity, event concept, benefits
3. Form organizing committee - gather colleagues to help with planning, promotion, execution
4. Set date and time - pick time that works for most employees (lunch, happy hour, weekend)
5. Choose event type - options include:
   - Charity bake sale
   - Fundraising walk/run
   - Auction or raffle
   - Food drive
   - Volunteer day
   - Donation drive
6. Create budget - expenses for venue, materials, marketing; seek sponsorships from local businesses
7. Promote event - internal communications, social media, flyers to encourage participation
8. Plan logistics - arrange equipment, supplies, volunteers for setup, registration, execution
9. Follow up - regularly update colleagues, ensure tasks completed on time

Keep organized, be flexible, have fun. Can bring office together and positively impact community.
