## Wine Spill Incident (February 2022)

**What happened**:
- A friend spilled red wine on the carpet during a gathering
- User was able to clean it up quickly
- No permanent damage resulted

**Lessons learned**:
- Need to keep wine wipes or wine cleaning solution on hand
- Use wine glasses with wide base and narrow rim to reduce tipping risk
- Have extra throws and blankets available (can be used to quickly absorb spills)
- Keep napkins and cleaning supplies within reach during gatherings

**Prevention strategies**:
- Choose sturdy glassware (wide base, narrow rim)
- Place drinks on stable surfaces away from seating areas when possible
- Have cleanup supplies readily available
- Consider using coasters to prevent accidents
