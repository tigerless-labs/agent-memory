## Cast Iron Pan Ownership & Cleaning Preference

**User owns a cast iron pan** and has a strong preference for **ease and speed of cleaning over traditional seasoning maintenance**. They are willing to use non-traditional cleaning methods (soap, potentially dishwasher) if it means less effort.

### Cleaning Methods Discussed
- **Traditional (soap-free)**: hot water, soft-bristled brush, plastic scraper for food, dry thoroughly, optionally apply vegetable oil
- **User's preference**: willing to use soap despite it stripping seasoning, wants minimal cleanup effort
- **Not recommended**: dishwasher (harsh detergents and high heat damage the seasoning, cause rust and warping)

### Seasoning Process (if needed)
- Preheat oven to 375°F
- Apply thin layer of vegetable oil to entire surface (inside and out)
- Place pan upside down on baking sheet
- Bake 30-40 minutes until darker and oil absorbed
- Cool in oven
- Repeat as needed

### Key Preference
User stated: "I don't care about the seasoning or rust-resistance. I just want my pan to be clean and I don't want to put in a lot of effort."

This preference may affect future recommendations about cookware care or cooking methods.
