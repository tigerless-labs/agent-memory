## Vetting Charities Before Donating or Participating

User wants to research charities to ensure time and resources are used effectively.

**Rating websites:**
- Charity Navigator (charitynavigator.org)
- GuideStar (guidestar.org)
- BBB Wise Giving Alliance (give.org)
- GreatNonprofits (greatnonprofits.org)
- Charity Watch (charitywatch.org)

**Research steps:**
1. Check charity website for mission, goals, programs, financials, leadership
2. Use rating websites listed above for evaluations
3. Verify 501(c)(3) status through IRS website (irs.gov)
4. Review financial reports and Form 990
5. Research programs, services, goals, impact
6. Research governance, leadership, board members, staff diversity
7. Read online reviews (Google, Yelp, Facebook)
8. Contact charity directly with questions
9. Check for accreditation (BBB Wise Giving Alliance, Independent Charities of America)
10. Trust instincts — if something feels off, look for alternatives

Trust your instincts about organizations.
