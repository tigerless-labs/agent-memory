## Movie Night Setup (March 2022)

**When**: This weekend (early March 2022)

**Snacks**:
- Mini quiches
- Fruit skewers

**Beverages**:
- Red wine options: Pinot Noir, Beaujolais, or Valpolicella
- Keep wine glasses with wide base and narrow rim to prevent tipping

**Ambiance**:
- Dim the lights
- Light a fireplace for warmth and coziness
- Scented candles: Vanilla, Cinnamon, Lavender, Caramel, or Woodland Berries
- Extra throws and blankets scattered around for guests to use
- Soft, plush materials like fleece, Sherpa, or faux fur preferred

**Seating Arrangement**:
- Conversation circle layout (recent favorite after rearranging couch and armchair)
- Using existing extra chairs and ottomans to complete the circle
- Coffee table or console table in center for snacks and conversation
