## Dairy Cattle Breed Comparison

Information on dairy cattle breeds provided May 2023 when user was considering purchase.

**Key Selection Factors**:
1. Milk production volume per lactation and milk quality (fat/protein content)
2. Feed efficiency and conversion cost
3. Temperament and docility for regular handling
4. Hardiness and adaptability to local climate
5. Disease resistance profile (mastitis, lameness)
6. Reproductive performance and replacement frequency
7. Size and mobility for housing/pasture management
8. Specialized characteristics for cheese/butter production

**Popular Dairy Breeds**:

**Holstein**:
- High milk production volume
- Excellent feed efficiency
- Docile temperament
- Standard for large-scale dairy operations

**Jersey**:
- Rich, creamy milk
- High in protein and calcium
- Friendly and curious nature
- Excellent for cheese production
- Popular for smaller operations

**Guernsey**:
- Golden-colored milk
- Rich, creamy milk
- Often used in cheese production
- Premium quality reputation

**Brown Swiss**:
- Gentle and hardy nature
- Highly adaptable
- Good for small to medium-sized operations
- Strong reproductive performance

**Ayrshire**:
- Originating from Scotland
- High milk production
- Very hardy
- Distinctive red and white markings

**Selection Tips**:
- Research different breeds thoroughly
- Talk to other dairy farmers
- Visit farms with breeds of interest
- Choose based on specific needs, resources, and goals
