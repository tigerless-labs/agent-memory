## Etsy Shop Plans

**Decision:** Start with single Etsy shop combining both vintage art posters and original artwork. Can split into separate shops later if needed.

**Why Etsy:**
- Lower upfront costs compared to Shopify
- Platform is naturally geared toward unique, vintage, and handmade items
- Vintage posters will resonate well with Etsy's customer base

**Fee Structure (as of May 2023):**
- Listing fee: $0.20 per listing (valid 4 months or until sale)
- Transaction fee: 3.5% of sale price (including shipping)
- Payment processing fee: 3% + $0.25 per transaction (waived with Etsy Payments)
- Shipping label fee: optional, ~$0.50-$1.50

**Shop Status:** Planning phase; not yet launched

**Rationale for single shop:**
- Don't have huge collection of vintage posters yet
- Can cross-promote vintage posters to customers interested in original art and vice versa
- Can split into two shops later if business grows and categories need separation
