## Farm Store Plans

User is planning to establish a farm store on the property to sell farm products directly to customers.

**Location & Design Considerations**:
- Choose easily accessible, visible location with good parking
- Size based on product volume and expected customer volume
- "U"-shaped layout recommended for customer flow
- Natural lighting supplemented with warm, inviting lights
- Attractive display cases for products

**Products to Stock**:
- Dairy products: milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, ice cream
- Eggs: different sizes and types (organic, free-range)
- Fresh produce: seasonal vegetables
- Baked goods: items made with farm eggs and ingredients
- Value-added products: jams, honey, sauces
- Local products: partner with other farmers (honey, maple syrup, crafts)
- Strong branding: logo, packaging, signage reflecting farm values

**Operations**:
- Point of Sale (POS) system for transactions and inventory tracking
- Inventory management system for rotation and freshness
- Knowledgeable, friendly staff
- Health and safety compliance with sanitation schedules

**Additional Features**:
- Host events: farm tours, cheese-making classes, tastings
- Loyalty program for repeat customers
- Website and social media presence
- Regular engagement with community
