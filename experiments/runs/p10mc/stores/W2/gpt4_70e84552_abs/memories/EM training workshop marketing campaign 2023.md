## Workshop Overview
**Name:** E&M Training Workshop (2023 Guidelines)  
**Duration:** 2 days  
**Format:** Hands-on training with practical examples and case studies

## Curriculum Topics
- Introduction to 2023 Guidelines
- History of E&M MDM table from 1995 to 2023 changes overview
- Types of MDM table
- Detailed discussion on Data points — Amount and/or Complexity of Data to Be Reviewed and Analysed
- New changes in Hospital coding (observation, inpatient & consultation services)
- Time-based coding concepts – Total Time
- Prolonged Services
- How the new MDM table applies to ED coding
- ED Examples
- How the New MDM table applies to Hospital observation & inpatient coding
- OBS & IP Examples
- FAQs

## Marketing Strategy
Social media campaign with 5 post types, each created in two versions:
1. **Teaser post** — announcing workshop, highlighting 2023 guidelines and data points
2. **History/Evolution post** — MDM table changes from 1995 to 2023
3. **Specific topic post** — hospital coding for observation, inpatient, consultation services
4. **Practical applications post** — new MDM table for ED/hospital observation/inpatient coding
5. **FAQ post** — addressing trainee questions and concerns

**Tone evolution:** Regular → Emotional/Convincing
- Use emotional language: "tired of feeling unsure," "take control," "empower yourself"
- Address pain points: compliance worry, knowledge gaps, career advancement
- Focus on transformation and confidence-building
- Include clear call-to-action with registration links
- Hashtags: #EandM #Coding #2023Guidelines #HospitalServices #MDMtable

## Key Emotional Messaging Themes
- Empowerment and control over career
- Recognition for hard work
- Confidence and staying ahead
- Transformation through knowledge
- Addressing overwhelm and uncertainty

## Sample Posts Created
All sample posts emphasize practical relevance, career advancement, and staying current with latest guidelines. Audience: healthcare professionals in medical coding.
