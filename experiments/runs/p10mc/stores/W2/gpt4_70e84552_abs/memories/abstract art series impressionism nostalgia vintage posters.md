## Art Series Concept

**Inspiration:** Attended an impressionist art exhibit at local museum (May 2023); purchased a catalog/book at the gift shop. Inspired by the impressionists' use of lighting and color.

**Series Idea:** Create a series of abstract pieces inspired by impressionism but with personal twist. Blend old and new by incorporating vintage art posters into the work.

**Central Themes:**
- **Nostalgia and memory:** Recontextualize vintage posters (originally meant to advertise/promote) to make viewers reflect on how past relates to present
- **Impermanence:** Use worn, faded vintage posters as metaphor for loss and forgetting over time; worn quality evokes melancholy and introspection

**Artistic Techniques (experimentation phase):**
- Layering paint and ink for depth
- Using gel medium to create texture and depth; achieve luminosity
- Incorporating vintage posters via:
  - Decoupaging onto canvas
  - Using posters as a base for abstract pieces
  - Cutting up and reassembling posters into new forms
  - Collage approaches

**Project Status:** Experimentation phase; no completed pieces yet; multiple ideas and techniques being tested
