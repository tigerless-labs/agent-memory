## Music Storage and Capacity

- **Total Songs:** Up to 500 songs on watch
- **Internal Storage:** 3.6 GB
- **Supported Formats:** MP3, AAC, FLAC
- **Max File Size:** 10 MB per song
- **Playlists:** Up to 10 playlists, maximum 500 songs per playlist

## Music Transfer Process

1. Connect watch to computer with USB cable
2. Download and install Garmin Express software
3. Create or select music playlist on computer
4. Use Garmin Express to transfer music files to watch
5. Control playback using watch buttons and touchscreen

## Music Playback Features

- Shuffle and repeat functionality
- Volume control via buttons or Garmin Connect app
- Bluetooth audio streaming to compatible headphones/speakers
- Organize music by genre, tempo, or mood in separate playlists

## GPS and Route Navigation

- **Satellite Systems:** GPS, GLONASS, Galileo
- **Route Storage:** Up to 100 courses or routes on device
- **Waypoints per Route:** Maximum 10,000 waypoints/points per course
- **Route Following:** Turn-by-turn directions with off-course alerts
- **Real-Time Display:** Pace, distance, heart rate shown with route map
- **Route Creation:** Via Garmin Connect app on smartphone

## Battery and Performance

- **GPS Mode with Music:** 7 hours battery life
- **Accuracy:** Suitable for runs up to 20 km
- **Water Resistance:** Waterproof for running in various conditions

- **Price Point:** $300 USD
