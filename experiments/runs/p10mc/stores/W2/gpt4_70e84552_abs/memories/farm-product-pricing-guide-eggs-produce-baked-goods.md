## Farm Product Pricing Guide

Pricing recommendations provided May 2023 for farm open house event and farm store.

**Fresh Produce**:
- Leafy greens (lettuce, kale, spinach): 2-3 dollars per bunch
- Tomatoes: 3-5 dollars per pound
- Cucumbers: 1-2 dollars each
- Carrots: 2-3 dollars per bunch

**Pricing Strategy for Produce**:
- Research competitive pricing at local farmers markets
- Adjust for seasonal availability (peak season higher prices)
- Consider bundle deals and farmers basket discounts
- Factor in production costs (seeds, labor, expenses)

**Eggs**:
- Dozen eggs: 4-6 dollars
- Half-dozen eggs: 2-3 dollars
- Specialty eggs (duck, quail): 6-8 dollars per dozen

**Pricing Strategy for Eggs**:
- Compare to local grocery store and farmers market prices
- Premium pricing for heritage or specialty breeds
- Offer discounts for bulk purchases (multiple dozen)

**Baked Goods**:
- Quiche: 15-20 dollars per pie
- Cookies: 2-3 dollars each or 6 for 10 dollars
- Muffins: 2-3 dollars each or 6 for 12 dollars

**Pricing Strategy for Baked Goods**:
- Research local bakery pricing
- Factor in ingredient costs (eggs, flour, sugar)
- Price based on size and complexity
- Offer samples to encourage purchases

**General Tips**:
- Display prices clearly and prominently
- Be prepared for price negotiation, especially bulk orders
- Have clear payment system ready (cash, credit, mobile)
- Comply with local health and safety regulations for food sales
