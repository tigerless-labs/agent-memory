## Professional Property Valuation Factors

A residential property appraiser considers the following factors when determining property value:

1. **Location** — proximity to schools, parks, public transportation, shopping, and amenities
2. **Size and layout** — number of bedrooms and bathrooms, square footage, overall layout
3. **Condition** — age of property, repairs and renovations made, overall condition
4. **Comparable sales** — recent sales of similar properties in the area (sales comparison approach)
5. **Income potential** — rental income or business revenue potential (more relevant for commercial properties)
6. **Zoning** — current and potential future zoning restrictions
7. **Environmental factors** — contamination, proximity to hazardous waste sites
8. **Market trends** — supply and demand, interest rates, economic conditions
9. **Unique features** — swimming pools, views, or other distinctive characteristics
10. **Legal issues** — pending litigation, easements, or other constraints on property use

The **sales comparison approach** is one of the most common methods appraisers use.
