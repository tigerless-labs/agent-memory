## Farm Open House Event

User is planning a farm open house event to showcase farm operations and sell products.

**Timeline**: Planned for "soon" as of May 21, 2023 (date not yet finalized)

**Planned Activities**:

**For Kids**:
- Petting zoo with goats, sheep, rabbits
- Farm-themed crafts (paper plate chickens, cow masks, flower crowns)
- Scavenger hunt around farm
- Tractor or wagon rides
- Farm animal feeding opportunities

**For Adults**:
- Guided farm tours
- Wine and cheese tasting
- Farm-to-table cooking demonstrations
- Gardening workshops
- Live music and farm-fresh food

**For All Ages**:
- Farm photo booth with props
- Farm-themed lawn games (cornhole, giant Jenga, obstacle course)
- Farm market with fresh produce and artisanal goods
- Q&A session with farmer/agriculture expert
- Raffle or silent auction

**Products to Sell**:
- Fresh produce (greens, tomatoes, cucumbers, carrots, etc.)
- Fresh eggs from chickens
- Baked goods made with farm eggs (quiches, cookies, muffins)
- Milk and cheese products (from future dairy cow purchase)

**Marketing/Promotion Ideas**:
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram, relevant hashtags)
- Email marketing to customer list
- Local event listings and community calendars
- Influencer partnerships with food bloggers
- Flyers and brochures in local cafes and farmers' markets
- Word-of-mouth and referral incentives
- Collaboration with nearby farms
- Pre-event exclusive farm tours
- Press outreach to local media
- Clear farm signage
- Special offers for pre-registration or social media shares
- Product samples at event
