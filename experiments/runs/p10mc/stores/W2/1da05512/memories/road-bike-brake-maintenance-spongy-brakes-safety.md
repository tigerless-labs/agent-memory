## Road Bike Brake Issue

**Problem:** Road bike brakes have been feeling spongy — a safety concern that needs attention before long rides.

**Plan:** Schedule maintenance check at local bike shop

**Checklist prepared before shop visit:**
- Clean bike thoroughly (chain, derailleurs, brake calipers)
- Check tire pressure to spec
- Identify and list all issues: spongy brakes, any shifting problems, unusual noises, loose components
- Gather maintenance records from previous work

**Common brake issues that may need addressing:**
- Brake pad replacement or adjustment
- Cable adjustments
- Fluid replacement (if hydraulic)
- Bleed brake lines

**Priority:** Address before attempting 60-mile road ride to ensure safety
