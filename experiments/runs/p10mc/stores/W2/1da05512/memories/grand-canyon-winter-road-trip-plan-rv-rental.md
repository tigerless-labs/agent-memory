## Grand Canyon Winter Holiday Trip

**Trip Details:**
- When: Winter holidays (December/January)
- Who: User + friends
- Where: Grand Canyon, Arizona; Flagstaff base
- How: Renting an RV
- Key experience: Drive scenic route from Flagstaff to Grand Canyon, camp at Grand Canyon with electric hookups, wake up to sunrise

**Route:**
- Driving from California to Arizona
- Highway 101 previously had heavy traffic for San Francisco trip (May 2023)
- Route options to Flagstaff: I-40 East (direct, less scenic) or US-180 North (scenic, through San Francisco Peaks)

**RV Camping at Grand Canyon:**
Options with electric hookups and amenities (book well in advance):
1. Mather Campground (South Rim): 327 campsites with hookups, flush toilets, showers, laundry; reserve via recreation.gov
2. Trailer Village RV Park (South Rim): 84 sites with hookups, Wi-Fi, laundry, camp store; reserve via grandcanyonlodges.com
3. Desert View Campground (South Rim): 50 campsites with hookups, flush toilets, potable water; reserve via recreation.gov

**Flagstaff Base Recommendations:**
Restaurants: Late for the Train Cafe, Proper Meats + Provisions, Fat Olives, The Toasted Owl, Big Foot BBQ
Hotels: Little America Flagstaff, Hotel Monte Vista, Flagstaff Inn and Suites, The Inn at Flagstaff
Camping: Flagstaff KOA or nearby national forests

**Winter RV Preparation Needed:**
- Winterize plumbing (drain/insulate water hoses, use freeze-proof or heated hoses)
- Check/upgrade heating system (backup propane heater)
- Inspect/upgrade tires (snow tires or chains for snowy roads)
- Fill propane and fuel before trip
- Pack winter gear: warm clothing, blankets, emergency kit (flashlight, first aid, snacks, shovel, ice scraper, sand/cat litter for traction)
- Check weather forecast and road conditions before/during trip
- High elevation + winter weather = freezing temps, snow, icy roads likely

**Border/Checkpoint Info:**
- No international borders (California to Arizona is domestic)
- Agricultural checkpoints when entering Arizona—declare fruits/vegetables/plants
- May encounter highway patrol checkpoints; have license, registration, insurance ready
- RV parks may require inspection before hookup
- Consider America the Beautiful annual pass for national parks access
