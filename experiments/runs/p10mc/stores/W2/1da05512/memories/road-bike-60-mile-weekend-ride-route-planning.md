## 60-Mile Road Bike Ride (May 2023 weekend)

**Goal:** Complete a longer road bike ride (~60 miles) this weekend

**Route planning:**
- Primary tool: **MapMyRide** — allows filtering by road bike routes, terrain, distance
- Also useful: Strava, Ride with GPS, Bikemap
- For finding trails/paths: TrailLink, Rails-to-Trails Conservancy

**Pre-ride preparations:**
- Check road bike condition: tire pressure, brakes, gears
- Plan route in advance with map/GPS
- Pack essentials: water, snacks, basic tool kit
- Wear: comfortable breathable clothing, properly fitted helmet
- Notify someone of route and expected return time

**Bike status before ride:**
- Address spongy brakes at maintenance check (safety priority)
- Installing new Shimano Ultegra clipless pedals before ride (if time allows)
