## Ethiopian Cuisine Recommendations

Dishes to try during Ethiopia visit:

1. **Injera** — sourdough flatbread, staple of Ethiopian cuisine, used as utensil to scoop up stews and curries

2. **Doro Wot** — spicy chicken stew made with berbere spice (blend of chili powder, ginger, coriander, and other spices)

3. **Tibs** — spicy sautéed meat dish made with beef or lamb, onions, and peppers

4. **Kitfo** — raw beef seasoned with spices and chili powder

5. **Shiro** — thick stew made with ground chickpeas or lentils, often served with injera

6. **Ethiopian Coffee** — Ethiopia famous for coffee; traditional coffee ceremony includes roasting beans, grinding, brewing in special pot

Ethiopian cuisine noted for unique and flavorful dishes. Traditional coffee ceremony recommended as cultural experience.
