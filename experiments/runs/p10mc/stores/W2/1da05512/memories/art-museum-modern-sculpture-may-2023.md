## Modern Sculpture Inspiration from Museum Visit

**Date:** May 20, 2023 (Saturday)

**Event:** Visited art museum, attended modern sculpture exhibit. Inspired to explore contemporary sculptors.

**Primary Focus:** Anish Kapoor — particularly interested in his use of reflective surfaces in large-scale installations.

**Cloud Gate Information:**
- Located in Millennium Park, Chicago, Illinois
- Installed in 2004
- Made of 168 stainless steel panels
- Weighs over 110 tons
- Dimensions: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long
- Known as "The Bean" due to bean-like shape
- Mirror-like polished surface reflecting and distorting surroundings
- Interactive piece — viewers walk, touch, photograph
- Originally intended to be temporary installation, now permanent
- Surface cleaned daily
- One of most photographed Chicago landmarks (10,000+ photos daily)
