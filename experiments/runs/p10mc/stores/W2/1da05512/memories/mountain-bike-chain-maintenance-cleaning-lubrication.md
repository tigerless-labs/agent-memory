## Mountain Bike Chain Maintenance

**Recent work (May 2023):**
- Fixed flat tire: replaced inner tube
- Cleaned chain
- Bike has been running smoothly since

**Chain cleaning routine:**
- Clean every 1-2 weeks, or more often if riding in dirty/wet conditions
- Use bike-specific chain cleaner (avoid harsh chemicals)
- Tools: chain cleaning brush or chain scrubber
- Clean in well-ventilated area
- Rinse thoroughly to remove all dirt and cleaning residue

**Chain lubrication:**
- Choose lubricant for riding conditions: wet, dry, or all-weather
- Apply sparingly and evenly to clean chain
- Avoid over-lubrication (attracts dirt)
- Lubricate after cleaning to protect from corrosion
- Wipe excess lubricant from chain and components (chainrings, derailleur)

**Ongoing maintenance:**
- Regularly inspect chain for rust, stretching, broken links
- Use chain wear indicator tool to know when replacement needed
- Don't forget: clean/lubricate chainrings, cassette, derailleur pivot points
- Keep chain properly maintained for optimal performance and longevity
