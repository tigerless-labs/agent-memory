## Recommended African Destinations

Seven off-the-beaten-path destinations discussed:
1. **Djibouti** — volcanic craters, salt flats, stunning beaches
2. **Namibia** — vast deserts, sand dunes, desert-adapted elephants and lions
3. **Madagascar** — island nation, lemurs, baobab trees, spiny forests
4. **Mozambique** — pristine beaches, tropical islands (Bazaruto), UNESCO-listed Ilha de Mozambique with African, Arab, Portuguese heritage
5. **Ethiopia** — 3,000-year history, rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, castles of Gondar, Blue Nile Falls
6. **Mauritania** — least populated landscapes, sweeping dunes, desert oases, nomadic tribes
7. **Senegal** — vibrant music scene in Dakar, Djoudj National Bird Sanctuary, pink lake of Retba

**Ethiopia chosen as top recommendation** for experiencing local culture — ancient traditions, Ethiopian Orthodox Church, local food, music, coffee ceremonies, friendly/hospitable people.
