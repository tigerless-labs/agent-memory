## Scene Concept
Girl is about to sit down and speak into a microphone when she slowly disappears into the computer—Black Mirror aesthetic (dark, tech-horror, unsettling).

## Recommended Techniques for Black Mirror Effect

### Visual Effects
- Glitchy, distorted computer interface (malfunction look)
- Distortion effects, flashes of light, or swirling motion as she disappears
- Flickering lights and static noise
- Quick cuts to create jarring effect
- Close-ups of computer interface/buttons after disappearance

### Sound Design
- Eerie music or haunting melody
- Discordant sounds or white noise
- Low hum or distant whispers
- Distorted sound effects

### Cinematography & Lighting
- Slow, deliberate camera movements building anticipation
- Zoom in on face/hands before disappearance
- Dim lighting or backlighting for mood
- Shadows to create mystery and suspense

### Narrative Twist
Consider revealing darker implication: she's being trapped in a digital realm, or the computer itself is alive/malevolent.
