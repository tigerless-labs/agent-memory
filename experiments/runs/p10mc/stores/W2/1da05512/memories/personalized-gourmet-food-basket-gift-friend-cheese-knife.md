## Gourmet Food Basket Gift Project

**Friend profile**: Appreciates unique, handmade items

**Basket contents**:
- Artisanal cheeses (2-3 varieties)
- Cured meats (prosciutto, salami, pepperoni)
- Artisanal crackers or small baguette
- Gourmet nuts and seeds
- Gourmet chocolates (truffles, caramels)
- Exotic dried fruits
- Gourmet cookies or biscotti
- Artisanal jams
- High-quality honey or maple syrup

**Customized Cheese Knife (centerpiece)**:
- Handle material: Wooden (warm, rustic, character-building patina over time)
- Wood type options: maple, walnut, cherry, or olive wood
- Personalization: Friend's name engraved on handle
- Font style options: script, block, serif, sans-serif, italic, bold, uppercase
- Font size options: small (1/8 inch), medium (1/4 inch), large (1/2 inch)

**Recommended vendors**: Etsy, Zazzle, Personalization Mall, Mark and Graham, UncommonGoods

**Budget**: $30-$40 total
