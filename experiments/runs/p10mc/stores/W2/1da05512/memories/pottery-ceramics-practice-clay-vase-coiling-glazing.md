## Ceramics and Pottery Practice

**Current Project:** Clay vase using coiling and scoring techniques

**Clay Types Being Explored:**

1. **Earthenware Clay** (currently using)
   - Easy to work with, forgiving for beginners
   - Low firing temperature (~1800°F/980°C)
   - Wide glaze options
   - Weaknesses: brittle, prone to cracking, not suitable for functional ware

2. **Air-Dry Clay** (interested in trying)
   - No kiln required
   - Fast results
   - Easy to repair
   - Weaknesses: limited durability, not suitable for outdoor use, limited size/complexity

3. **Stoneware Clay** (interested in trying)
   - Strong and durable
   - High-temperature firing (~2400°F/1300°C)
   - Food-safe and watertight
   - Weaknesses: more difficult to work with, requires high-temperature kiln, limited glaze options

**Glazing & Firing Goals:**
- Achieve consistent, food-safe finish on functional ware
- Learn glaze formulation and create glaze recipes from scratch
- Master application techniques: brushing, pouring, spraying
- Achieve specific colors and effects: blues, reds, matte finishes
- Understand firing temperatures and techniques

**Skills Being Developed:**
- Coiling and scoring techniques
- Glaze composition and application
- Firing control and atmosphere
- Glaze testing and troubleshooting
