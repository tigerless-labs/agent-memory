## Ethiopian Festivals

Major festivals discussed for visiting Ethiopia:

1. **Timkat** — January 19
   - Religious festival marking baptism of Jesus in Jordan River
   - Features colorful processions, music, dancing, water splashing

2. **Meskel** — September 27
   - Religious festival commemorating the finding of the True Cross by St. Helena
   - Includes lighting of bonfires, feasting, traditional dancing

3. **Enkutatash** — September 11
   - Ethiopian New Year celebration
   - Features feasting, dancing, exchange of gifts

4. **Fasika** — April (typically)
   - Ethiopian Orthodox Easter celebration
   - Includes fasting, prayer, colorful processions, feasting, gift exchanges

Festivals recommended as excellent way to experience Ethiopian culture and meet locals eager to share customs.
