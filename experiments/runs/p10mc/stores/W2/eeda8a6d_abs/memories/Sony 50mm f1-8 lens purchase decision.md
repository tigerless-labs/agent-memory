Lens Consideration: Sony FE 50mm f/1.8

Reasons for interest:
- Versatile focal length
- Wide aperture for low light and shallow depth of field
- Good for portraits and street photography
- Compact and reasonably priced

Options researched:
- Sony FE 50mm f/1.8 ($200, excellent value)
- Sony Zeiss Planar T FE 50mm f/1.4 ZA ($1,500, premium)
- Sigma 50mm f/1.4 DG HSM Art ($500, excellent quality)
- Tamron 45mm f/1.8 Di VC USD ($400, has image stabilization)

Decision: Decided to wait and get more familiar with current 100-400mm lens before purchasing new gear.

May revisit this decision in future once more proficient with existing equipment.
