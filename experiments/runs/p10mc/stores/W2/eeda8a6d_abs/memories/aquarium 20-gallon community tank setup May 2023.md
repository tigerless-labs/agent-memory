## Current Tank Setup (May 2023)

**Tank Size**: 20 gallons

**Current Fish**:
- 10 neon tetras
- 5 golden honey gouramis
- 1 small pleco catfish

**Current Decorations**:
- Artificial coral
- Rocks

## Plans for Addition

### Live Plants (Confirmed Interest)

**Java Moss**:
- Lighting: 1-2 watts per gallon (low to moderate)
- Temperature: 72-82°F (can tolerate as low as 65°F)
- pH: 5.5-8.5, prefers slightly acidic to neutral
- Setup: Attach to rocks, driftwood, or decorations with plant weight or thread
- Fertilization: Balanced water-soluble fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK) once weekly
- Fish interaction: Neon tetras use as hideout; golden honey gouramis may nibble but not significant; pleco may graze but not significant
- Note: Sensitive to copper-based medications; prone to algae growth

**Anacharis**:
- Lighting: 2-4 watts per gallon (moderate to high)
- Temperature: 65-85°F, prefers 72-82°F
- pH: 6.5-8.5
- Setup: Floating plant, no substrate needed
- Fertilization: Balanced water-soluble fertilizer (5-5-5 NPK) once weekly
- Fish interaction: Pleco catfish will graze as natural food source; neon tetras may nibble occasionally; golden honey gouramis may attempt to eat but not significant
- Care: Prune regularly to encourage bushy growth; sensitive to copper-based medications

### Potential Schooling Fish (Under Consideration)

**Lemon Tetras**:
- Compatible with neon tetras (similar size and temperament, may form mixed school)
- Compatible with golden honey gouramis (too fast/agile to catch, unless gouramis fin-nip)
- Compatible with pleco (fish too small to bother pleco)

**Zebra Danios**:
- Compatible with neon tetras (active and hardy, may chase playfully but peaceful)
- Compatible with golden honey gouramis (too fast/active to catch, unless gouramis fin-nip)
- Compatible with pleco (too small and agile to bother pleco)

**Introduction tip**: Add schooling fish in groups of 6-10 to reduce stress and territorial behavior

### Decorations (New Addition)

**Treasure Chest**:
- Placement: Near artificial coral and rocks to create "city" area for fish exploration
- Benefits: Provides cozy, enclosed hiding space; creates visual interest
- Better choice than sunken ship for 20-gallon tank (smaller, more compact)

**Layout Tips**:
- Create sense of depth by angling treasure chest or burying slightly in substrate
- Add live plants around treasure chest and coral for lush environment
- Vary heights using rocks and coral for different exploration levels
- Leave open swimming space around decorations for fish to swim freely
