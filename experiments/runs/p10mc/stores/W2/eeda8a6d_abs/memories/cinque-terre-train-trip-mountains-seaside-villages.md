## Trip Decision

Considering scenic train rides in Europe, user narrowed choices to Bernina Express or Cinque Terre Train, then chose **Cinque Terre Train** because of preference for mountains mixed with seaside villages.

## Route & Viewing Tips

- **Best views**: Left side of train from La Spezia to Levanto
- **Timing**: Off-peak hours to avoid crowds; late afternoon/evening for sunset views over the sea
- **Stop at villages**: Get off and walk/explore each Cinque Terre village to experience the unique beauty
- **Plan for delays**: Trains in this area can be delayed; build buffer into day planning
- **Bring camera**: Views are breathtaking; essential for capturing coastline and villages
- **Bring snacks and water**: No on-board catering; bring supplies for the journey

## Preferences Noted

- Scenic train travel appeals to user
- Drawn to combination of mountain and coastal scenery
- Values photography opportunities during travel
