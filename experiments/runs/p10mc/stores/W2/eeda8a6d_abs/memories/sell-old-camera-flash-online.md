Plan to sell old camera flash that hasnt been used in a while.

Where to sell: Craigslist or eBay

Tips to follow:
- Research market prices on eBay, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace for similar flashes
- Gather make, model, and accessories information
- Clean flash thoroughly
- Take clear high-quality photos from multiple angles
- Write detailed description including condition, features, camera compatibility
- Set competitive price
- Consider selling as bundle with other unused gear
- Choose eBay for larger audience (but pay fees) or Craigslist for free listing
- Meet buyers in public place and be cautious of scams
