## Bathroom sink slow drainage issue

**Timeline:**
- Issue started: Late April 2023 (approximately 3 weeks before May 22)
- Tried drain cleaner: twice (before May 22, did not work)
- Tried plunger and plumbing snake: Sunday, May 21, 2023 (did not work)
- Conversation date: Monday, May 22, 2023

**Symptoms:**
- Water drains slowly after washing hands or face
- Bathroom has bad smell, possibly from trash can but could be drain-related

**Troubleshooting plan (weekend of May 27-28):**
1. Clean aerator: soak in hot water + white vinegar for 30-60 minutes, scrub with soft brush, rinse and reinstall
2. Check P-trap: ensure properly filled with water (dry trap causes sewer gas smells)
3. Baking soda treatment: pour 1 cup baking soda down drain, then 1 cup hot water, let sit several hours or overnight, rinse with hot water
4. Clean bathroom trash can; use baking soda and activated charcoal for odor absorption

**Next action:** If sink still slow after weekend, call professional plumber for deeper drain line inspection
