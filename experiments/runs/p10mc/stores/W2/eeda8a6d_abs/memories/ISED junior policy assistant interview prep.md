## Role Details
- **Position:** Junior Policy Assistant
- **Organization:** ISED (Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada)
- **Focus:** Life Sciences and Biomanufacturing Sector
- **Conversation date:** May 20, 2023

## Job Description Summary
Supporting government officials with research and analysis for project assessments evaluating company proposals to grow Canada's Life Sciences and Biomanufacturing Sector. Role emphasizes:
- Strong research skills
- Managing multiple priorities
- Quick turnaround on requests
- Curiosity about biomanufacturing industry
- Life sciences program experience (asset)

## Interview Questions to Prepare
1. Tell us about a time when you had to manage multiple priorities and how you handled it
2. How do you stay organized when working on multiple projects or tasks simultaneously
3. How do you approach researching a new topic and what resources do you use
4. Can you give an example of summarizing complex information for someone with limited knowledge
5. How would you prioritize and approach requests with quick turnaround times
6. What are the biggest challenges facing Canada's biomanufacturing industry and how could they be addressed
7. How do you stay up-to-date with industry trends in the life sciences sector
8. Tell us about research projects you've been involved in and how you contributed
9. How do you ensure accuracy and attention to detail
10. Describe a situation where you demonstrated curiosity about a new topic and how you learned more

## Follow-up Questions to Ask the Interviewer
1. What specific types of projects would I be supporting in this role
2. How does ISED approach supporting and growing the biomanufacturing industry, and what role does this position play
3. How does ISED collaborate with other government agencies and industry partners
4. What professional development opportunities are available to employees at ISED
5. How does ISED prioritize its initiatives and how would I be involved
6. Can you describe the culture and work environment at ISED and how the team approaches collaboration
7. How is success measured in this role and what key metrics would you be looking for
8. How does ISED stay informed about emerging trends in biomanufacturing and how would I contribute
