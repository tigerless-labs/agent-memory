User is experimenting with Neapolitan-style pizza and traditional Italian toppings.

**Traditional Neapolitan Pizza Principles:**
- Focus on simplicity and quality ingredients
- Avoid overloading with toppings
- Core flavors: San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, fresh basil

**Recommended Traditional Topping Combinations:**
1. Margherita: San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella (preferably buffalo mozzarella), extra-virgin olive oil, fresh basil
2. Quattro Formaggi: mozzarella, parmesan, gorgonzola, ricotta (often with arugula or cherry tomatoes)
3. Prosciutto e Funghi: thin prosciutto, sautéed mushrooms (cremini or shiitake)
4. Anchovies and Capers: salty anchovies, capers on tomato sauce and mozzarella base
5. Quattro Stagioni: four sections with artichokes, sun-dried tomatoes, olives, and ham/anchovies
6. Capricciosa: ham, mushrooms, artichokes, olives, anchovies
7. Salsiccia e Friarielli: spicy Italian sausage and friarielli (broccoli rabe)
8. Gorgonzola e Pere: creamy gorgonzola, caramelized pears
9. Zucchine e Fior di Latte: thinly sliced zucchini, fior di latte mozzarella
10. Puttanesca: olives, capers, anchovies, garlic

**Note on Truffle Oil:** Not traditional in Neapolitan pizza; it's a modern twist. Traditional pizzaiolos discourage its use. If experimenting with truffle, use fresh truffles (expensive, special occasions) rather than truffle oil.
