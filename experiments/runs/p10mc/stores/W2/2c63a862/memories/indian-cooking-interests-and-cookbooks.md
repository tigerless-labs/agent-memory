User considering trying Indian recipes, specifically chana masala or saag paneer, and looking for good Indian cookbooks.

**Recommended Indian Cookbooks:**

1. **"The Essentials of Indian Cooking" by Madhur Jaffrey**
   - Comprehensive guide to Indian cuisine
   - Covers chana masala and saag paneer
   - Clear, concise, easy-to-follow recipes
   - Author is renowned Indian cookbook writer

2. **"Indian Cooking" by Suneeta Vaswani**
   - Modern take on traditional Indian recipes
   - Includes vegetarian and vegan options
   - Detailed instructions
   - Recipes adaptable to different skill levels

3. **"5 Spices, 50 Dishes" by Ruta Kahate**
   - Focuses on five essential spices: turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom
   - 50 recipes showcasing spice versatility
   - Engaging writing
   - Easy-to-follow recipes

4. **"The New Indian Slow Cooker" by Neela Paniz**
   - Convenient slow cooker approach to Indian cooking
   - Includes chana masala and saag paneer recipes
   - Range of recipe options

5. **"Indian-ish" by Priya Krishna**
   - Modern, approachable take on Indian cuisine
   - Blends traditional flavors with international twists
   - Engaging writing
   - Good for experimenting with new flavors

**Additional Resources:**
- Food blogs and YouTube channels offer recipe tutorials and cooking guidance

**Key to Success:** Practice, experiment with different spices/ingredients/techniques, don't be afraid to try new things and adjust recipes to taste
