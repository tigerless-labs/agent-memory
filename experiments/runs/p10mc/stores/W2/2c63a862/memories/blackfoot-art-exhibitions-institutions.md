## Institutions Featuring Blackfoot Art

Places to see and experience Blackfoot art:

1. **Glenbow Museum** — Calgary, Canada
   - Extensive collection of Blackfoot art, artifacts, and culture
   - Blackfoot Gallery features traditional and contemporary Blackfoot art
   - Includes beadwork, paintings, sculptures

2. **Museum of Indigenous People** — Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA
   - Showcases contemporary Blackfoot art
   - Also features works by other Indigenous artists
   - Rotating exhibitions

3. **Blackfoot Crossing Historical Park** — Alberta, Canada
   - Cultural center celebrating Blackfoot culture and history
   - Features various exhibits and showcases of Blackfoot artistry
   - Educational and cultural programming
