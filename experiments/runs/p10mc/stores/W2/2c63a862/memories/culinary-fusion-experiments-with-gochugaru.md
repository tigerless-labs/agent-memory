**About Gochugaru (Korean Chili Flakes) in Curries:**

Gochugaru works well in curries for several reasons:
- **Heat level:** Distinct, slow-building heat that enhances overall spiciness without overpowering other flavors
- **Flavor profile:** Smoky, slightly sweet, nutty flavor complements warm aromatic spices in Massaman curry
- **Umami boost:** High concentration of umami flavor compounds adds depth and richness

**Tips for Using Gochugaru in Massaman Curry:**
- Start small: begin with 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon and adjust to taste (potent spice)
- Add at different stages: early additions help flavors meld; late additions preserve heat and flavor
- Balance with other spices: complement with cumin, coriander, cinnamon
- Choose appropriate type: varieties range from mild to extremely hot

**Types of Gochugaru Available:**
1. Standard Korean chili flakes: moderate heat, balanced flavor
2. Ghost pepper gochugaru: hotter variety, intense fiery heat
3. Smoked gochugaru: smoky flavor, slightly sweeter heat profile, adds depth

**General Principle:** When working with gochugaru, a little goes a long way. Taste as you go, adjusting heat and flavor to preference.
