User experimenting with different types of curry paste and coconut milk brands for Thai curry cooking.

**Recommended Thai Red Curry Paste Brands:**
1. **Maesri** — popular Thai brand, deep rich flavor, good balance of spices, not too sweet, moderate heat
2. **Thai Kitchen** — well-known US brand, more accessible, slightly sweeter than Maesri, good balance
3. **Pantai** — Thai brand, higher price, more complex flavor profile, deeper earthier tone

**Recommended Coconut Milk Brands:**
1. **Chaokoh** — popular Thai brand, full-fat, staple in Thai kitchens, rich and creamy, high fat content
2. **Native Forest** — US-based brand, high-quality organic, full-fat option with rich creamy texture, mild coconut flavor
3. **Aroy-D** — Thai brand, range of coconut milk products, rich creamy texture, slightly sweeter than Chaokoh

**Recommended Pairings:**
- Maesri + Chaokoh: classic combination, rich flavorful curry with deep slightly spicy flavor
- Thai Kitchen + Native Forest: slightly sweeter curry, creamy texture, more balanced flavor
- Pantai + Aroy-D: complex earthy curry, rich creamy texture, slightly sweeter flavor

**Key Principle:** Taste and adjust as you go; don't be afraid to add more curry paste, coconut milk, or spices to get flavor right
