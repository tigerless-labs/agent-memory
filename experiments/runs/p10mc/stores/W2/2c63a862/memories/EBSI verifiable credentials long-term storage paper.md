## Paper Focus
Decentralized application for long-term storage of EBSI-issued verifiable credentials, addressing trust challenges in decentralized systems.

## Key Standards & References
- **W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model**: https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/
- **TR-ESOR Middleware**: https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Publikationen/TechnischeRichtlinien/TR03125/BSI_TR_03125_V1_2_1.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2
  - Developed by German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI)
  - Provides recommendations for long-term digital document preservation

## Verifiable Credentials (VCs) Overview
- Digital credentials that can be cryptographically verified and authenticated
- Enable issuers to grant credentials to holders
- Holders present them to verifiers as proof of identity, qualification, or attributes
- Privacy-preserving and secure credential management per W3C standard

## Three Key Challenges Addressed
1. **Long-term storage**: Reliable and efficient storage of EBSI-issued VCs ensuring availability and integrity over extended periods
2. **Privacy preservation**: Methods protecting VC holder privacy while maintaining system trust and security
3. **TR-ESOR integration**: Implementing TR-ESOR middleware as long-term storage solution, exploring feasibility and benefits

## EBSI Context
- European Blockchain Service Infrastructure: decentralized platform for secure cross-border digital public services
- Utilizes verifiable credentials to establish trust and safeguard user privacy
