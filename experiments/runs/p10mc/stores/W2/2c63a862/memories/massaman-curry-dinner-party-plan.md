**Basic Recipe (Serves 4-6):**

Ingredients:
- 2 lbs beef (chuck or round), cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 medium onions, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1 large carrot, peeled and sliced
- 1 large red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 tbsp Massaman curry paste
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in Dutch oven/saucepan over medium heat; add onions, cook 5 min until softened/lightly browned
2. Add garlic, cook 1 min until fragrant
3. Add beef, cook until browned on all sides (~5 min)
4. Add potatoes, carrot, bell pepper; cook 5 min, stirring occasionally
5. Add Massaman curry paste, cook 1-2 min stirring constantly until fragrant
6. Add beef broth, coconut milk, cinnamon, cardamom, cumin, turmeric, salt, pepper; stir well
7. Bring to boil, reduce heat to low, simmer covered 1.5 hours until beef is tender
8. Taste and adjust seasoning
9. Garnish with cilantro if desired

**Tips:**
- Use high-quality Massaman curry paste with fresh aromatic ingredients
- Brown beef properly to develop rich deep flavor
- Can add/substitute other vegetables (sweet potatoes, zucchini, green beans)
- Adjust spice level for guests' preferences
- Serve with steamed rice or roti (Thai-style flatbread)
- Can make ahead; refrigerate or freeze (flavors meld further)

**Experimentation:** Interested in adding Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) for extra kick; recommended starting with 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon and adjusting to taste
