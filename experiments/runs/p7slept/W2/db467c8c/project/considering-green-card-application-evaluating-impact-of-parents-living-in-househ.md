As of 2023-07-16, actively exploring green card application process. Key concern: parents have been living in US household for 6 months (arrived ~2023-01-16) and are dependent on user for financial support.

Parents contribute to household: help with chores, cooking, and are taking English language classes.

Decision factors:
- Must demonstrate financial support at 125% of Federal Poverty Guidelines if parents are included as dependents
- Need to include parents' expenses in Form I-864 (Affidavit of Support) calculations
- Having parents present can strengthen family-ties argument but complicates financial documentation

Considering whether to include parents in sponsorship or keep them as separate dependents.
