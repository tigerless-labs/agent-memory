Eligibility categories: Family-based (spouse/parent/child), Employment-based (job offer/skills), Diversity visa lottery, Refugee/asylum status.

Process steps: (1) Determine eligibility; (2) Gather documents (passport, birth cert, police certs, medical exam, Form I-864); (3) File petition (I-130 or I-140); (4) Wait for processing; (5) Apply for green card (I-485 or DS-260); (6) Attend interview; (7) Receive decision and green card.

Processing timelines: Immediate relatives 5-10 months, Family-based 6-12 months to 1-5 years, Employment-based similar, DV lottery 6-12 months.

Form I-864 (Affidavit of Support): Legally binding contract, must demonstrate income at 125% Federal Poverty Guidelines, account for all dependents' expenses including parents' food/housing/utilities/health insurance/medical costs. Joint sponsors available if insufficient income alone.

Common mistakes: (1) Inaccurate/incomplete information (2) Insufficient documentation (3) Incorrect fees (4) Missing deadlines/RFE responses (5) Inconsistent info (6) Failure to disclose criminal history (7) Misunderstanding eligibility (8) Lack of interview prep (9) Unaccredited representatives (10) Not keeping copies (11) Not understanding medical exam (12) Not disclosing circumstance changes.

Post-submission: Biometric appointment, background checks, possible RFE, medical exam, interview, approval notice, oath ceremony, green card issuance.
