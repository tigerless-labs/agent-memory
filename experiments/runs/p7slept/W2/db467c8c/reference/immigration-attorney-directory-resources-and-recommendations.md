Resources for finding qualified immigration attorneys: (1) AILA (American Immigration Lawyers Association, www.aila.org) - search tool to find attorneys, look for State Bar certified Immigration Law Specialists; (2) National Immigration Project (www.nationalimmigrationproject.org) - directory of immigration attorneys; (3) Immigration Advocates Network (www.immigrationadvocates.org) - search by area; (4) Avvo (www.avvo.com) - attorney ratings and peer endorsements; (5) Martindale-Hubbell (www.martindale.com) - ratings and reviews. Also check personal referrals and online reviews (Yelp, Google Reviews, Facebook). Red flags: attorneys guaranteeing success, promising unrealistic results, charging exorbitant fees, or not transparent about credentials.
