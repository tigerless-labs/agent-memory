# Barcelona Public Art & Cultural Heritage

Barcelona features extensive public art and cultural heritage displays throughout the city.

## Major Architectural Works
- **Sagrada Familia cathedral** — iconic design by Antoni Gaudí
- **Park Güell** — famous public space designed by Gaudí

## Street Art & Neighborhoods
- Street art and graffiti murals in **El Raval** neighborhood
- Street art and graffiti murals in **Poble Nou** neighborhood

## Historic Public Spaces
- **La Rambla** — lined with historic buildings, statues, and fountains
- **Plaça de Catalunya** — historic plaza with buildings, statues, and fountains

## Overall Character
The city is known for its vibrant cultural heritage and flourishing arts scene, with works ranging from contemporary to historical, cultural to abstract.
