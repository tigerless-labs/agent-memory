Waiver of inadmissibility (Form I-601 or I-601A) may address grounds for deportation/inadmissibility due to unlawful presence. Key requirements: (1) Applicant must demonstrate extreme hardship to qualifying relative (US citizen or lawful permanent resident spouse, parent, or child); (2) Show rehabilitation; (3) File Form I-601 with USCIS. Process includes submitting application with supporting evidence, biometric services appointment, and adjudication (may include interview). Discretionary approval. Potential consequences of visa overstay: inadmissibility, bars to re-entry, and impact on family members' immigration applications. Unlawful presence >180 days but <1 year or misrepresentation can be grounds for waiver.
