## Blueberry Muffin Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups flour (all-purpose or whole wheat)
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups fresh or frozen blueberries

**Directions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C). Line 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
3. In another bowl, whisk melted butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla until smooth.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry, mix until just combined (do not overmix).
5. Gently fold in blueberries.
6. Divide batter among muffin cups. Top with additional blueberries if desired.
7. Bake 20-25 minutes until toothpick comes out clean.
8. Cool in pan 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.

**Note:** Whole wheat flour substitution results in denser, darker muffins. May need minor adjustments to baking time.
