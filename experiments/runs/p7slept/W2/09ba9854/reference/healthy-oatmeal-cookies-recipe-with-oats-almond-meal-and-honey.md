## Healthy Oatmeal Cookies Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins (or dark chocolate chips as substitute)

**Directions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
3. In another bowl, whisk melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla extract.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold in raisins.
6. Drop dough onto baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
7. Bake 10-12 minutes until golden brown.
8. Cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.

**Note:** Can substitute raisins with dark chocolate chips (higher cocoa %, less sugar) for a less healthy but still satisfying option.
