Recommended reading for developing characters:

1. **Characters & Viewpoint** by Orson Scott Card
   - Comprehensive guide to creating believable characters

2. **The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers** by Christopher Vogler
   - Explores the hero's journey and character archetypes

3. **How to Write Short Stories and Use Them to Further Your Writing Career** by James Scott Bell
   - Includes section on character development
