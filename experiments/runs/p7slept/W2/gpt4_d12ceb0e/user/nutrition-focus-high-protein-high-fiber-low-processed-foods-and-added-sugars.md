Diet priorities to support weight loss and muscle recovery:\n- High protein (1.6-2.2g per kg body weight) for muscle growth and maintenance\n- High fiber for satiety and digestive health\n- Minimize processed foods\n- Minimize added sugars\n- Focus on whole foods: lean meats, fish, eggs, legumes, whole grains, vegetables, fruits\n- Avoid: processed meats, refined sugars, fried foods, high-sodium foods, trans fats
