User deeply resonates with how literature explores the gap between public persona and private self. Appreciates how 'The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo' shows Evelyn performing a celebrity identity while a more vulnerable person exists beneath the surface.

Notes that this is a universal human experience — we all wear different masks in different situations, with public and private selves that sometimes feel in conflict. Particularly engaged by how intersectionality (gender, ethnicity, immigrant status) shapes identity and life experiences.

Values literary exploration of:
- How societal expectations constrain identity
- The complexity and depth of female relationships (supportive and competitive, loving and complicated simultaneously)
- How women are both empowered and limited by relationships with each other
- Nuanced character development showing the impact of systemic forces (patriarchy, marginalization) on individual lives
