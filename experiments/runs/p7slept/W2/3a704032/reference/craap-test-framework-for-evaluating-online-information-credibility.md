The CRAAP Test is a method for evaluating the credibility and reliability of online sources, particularly useful for digital literacy instruction.

**CRAAP** stands for:
- **C**urrency: Is the information current and up-to-date?
- **R**elevance: Is it relevant to your research topic?
- **A**uthority: Who is the author/publisher? Do they have credentials?
- **A**ccuracy: Is the information accurate and verifiable?
- **P**urpose: What is the purpose of the source? Is there bias?

Used as a structured worksheet and discussion framework for 6th-grade digital literacy education.
