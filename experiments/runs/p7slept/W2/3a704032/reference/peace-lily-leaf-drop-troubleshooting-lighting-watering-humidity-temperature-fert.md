**Peace Lily Leaf Drop Troubleshooting:**

Leaf drop is common when peace lilies transition to new environments. Recovery steps:

- **Light**: Bright indirect light preferred; tolerates low light; avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)
- **Watering**: Check soil moisture at first knuckle depth; water when dry (overwatering is main cause of leaf drop); ensure pot drains well
- **Humidity**: Prefers above 50%; increase with water tray + pebbles or humidifier
- **Temperature**: Optimal 65-80°F (18-27°C); avoid heating/cooling vents, fireplaces, drafty windows
- **Fertilization**: During growing season (spring/summer) with balanced, water-soluble fertilizer at half strength
- **Pruning**: Remove dead/dying leaves at base to prevent disease spread
- **Potting Mix**: Must be well-draining, slightly acidic pH; repot if current mix is depleted
- **Acclimation**: Allow 2-4 weeks for plant to adjust to new environment before expecting recovery

Monitor closely and adjust care based on plant response.
