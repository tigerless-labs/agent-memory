## Watering\n- Water when top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry\n- Growing season (spring/summer): every 7-10 days\n- Dormant season (fall/winter): every 10-14 days\n- Avoid overwatering; check soil moisture before watering\n\n## Temperature\n- Ideal range: 68-72°F (20-22°C)\n- Acceptable range: 65-75°F (18-24°C)\n- Avoid temperatures below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C)\n- Avoid drafts, heating/cooling vents, and sudden temperature changes\n\n## Humidity & Misting\n- Prefers humid environment but adapts to average humidity\n- Mist 1-2 times per week\n- Mist more frequently (every 3-4 days) in dry climates\n- Mist in morning; use room-temperature water\n\n## Light\n- Bright, indirect light preferred\n- East or west-facing windows ideal\n- Avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)\n\n## Soil & Potting\n- Use well-draining potting mix\n- Mix for tropical plants or orchids is suitable\n- Repot only when outgrown container or showing nutrient deficiency\n\n## Fertilization\n- Balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) during growing season\n- Dilute to half recommended strength\n- Fertilize every 1-2 weeks in spring/summer\n- Reduce to monthly in fall/winter
