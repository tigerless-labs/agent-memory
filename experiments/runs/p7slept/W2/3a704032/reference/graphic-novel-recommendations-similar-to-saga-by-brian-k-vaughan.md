User seeking graphic novels similar to Saga. Recommended titles:

- **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda — fantasy epic with stunning art, supernatural abilities, complex characters
- **The Wicked + The Divine** by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie — mythology meets pop culture, gods as reincarnated pop stars
- **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston — superhero deconstruction, heroes on a rural farm after cosmic event
- **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá — dysfunctional superhero family, action/drama/humor blend
- **Descender** by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen — sci-fi, young robot boy hunted by humans, themes of identity and humanity
- **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra — dark gritty world with biblical and mythological figures
- **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra — last human male on Earth after mysterious event, explores gender and power
