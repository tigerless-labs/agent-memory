Ryan is looking for a dog walker because he is away during the day. Needs to handle Max while away and potentially administer flea/tick medication every two weeks.

Recommended platforms:
- Rover (most popular, browse profiles, read reviews)
- Care.com (also offers pet care)
- Wag! (real-time route tracking)
- Barkly Pets
- Pet Sitters International
- NAPPS (National Association of Professional Pet Sitters)

Key tips for finding:
- Check multiple reviews
- Conduct an interview
- Verify liability insurance and certifications
- Get meet-and-greet with Max first
- Ask about medication administration comfort level
