Teacher at Thornton Township High School in Harvey, Illinois. Teaches Yearbook class with approximately 25 students. 3 of 25 speak Spanish as primary language.

School student demographics: 67.5% Black, 29% Latino, 2.5% Asian, <1% White; 17.3% English Language Learners.
Teacher demographics (district-wide): 53.8% White, 36.3% Black, 6.8% Latino, 1.8% Asian.

Current context: school-wide cultural responsiveness is low. Intercom announcements only in English. Letters/report cards in English and Spanish. Goal: increase accessibility for Spanish-speaking families and students.

Using Standards for Cultural Responsiveness checklist to develop curriculum that reflects student experiences and identities.
