Recommendations for highlighting eco-friendly aspects in sales and marketing:\n\n**Sales Conversations:**\n- Emphasize environmental benefits: reduced carbon footprint, minimized waste\n- Use eco-friendly language: "green," "sustainable," "eco-friendly"\n- Quantify impact: paper reduction, energy consumption savings\n- Highlight cost savings from sustainable practices\n- Share customer testimonials showing environmental benefits\n\n**Marketing Materials:**\n- Create dedicated sustainability page on website\n- Use eco-friendly visuals (leaves, trees, recycling symbols)\n- Highlight relevant certifications (ISO 14001, etc.)\n- Develop sustainability-focused whitepaper/e-book\n- Share eco-friendly tips on social media\n- Position brand as thought leader in sustainable project management\n\n**Additional Initiatives:**\n- Create sustainability-focused sales script\n- Offer free sustainability assessment/audit\n- Partner with eco-friendly organizations\n- Develop customer success stories focused on environmental benefits\n- Make sustainability core part of brand identity and messaging
