**Citation (APA 7):**
Butler, E. Jr. (2019, April 5). Why a culturally responsive curriculum works. *Education Week*, https://www.edweek.org/leadership/opinion-why-a-culturally-responsive-curriculum-works/2019/04

**Key insights:**
- Culturally responsive teaching has positive impact on academic outcomes of minority students
- Geneva Gay (2000) defines culturally responsive teaching as using cultural knowledge, prior experiences, frames of reference, and performance styles of ethnically diverse students
- Establishes trusting relationships; classroom becomes safe haven for students to take risks
- High-interest material that reflects student lives is essential for engagement
- Persistent disparities in graduation rates show current approaches do not provide equitable education

**Practical applications mentioned:**
- Infusing diverse authors into curriculum
- Students writing letters to historical figures
- Comparing student lives with scientists/inventors of different races/genders
- Embedding minority contributions across science, math, electives
- Districtwide implementation (Tucson USD) improved graduation rates and test scores
