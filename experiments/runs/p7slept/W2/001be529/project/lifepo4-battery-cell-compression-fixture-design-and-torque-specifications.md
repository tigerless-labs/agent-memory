Cell dimensions: 205mm height x 174mm width x 72mm thick. Compression applies to both largest faces (205mm x 174mm). Fixture: Two steel plates (205mm x 174mm) with four grade 5 steel 1/4-inch threaded rods at corners. Total fixturing force required: 300 kgf. Force per rod: 37.5 kgf (367.875 N). Pressure per face: 41,234.5 Pa. Torque specification for threaded rods: 17.27 ft-lbs. Rods are UNC threads with 20 TPI. Note: calculation assumes no friction losses and perfect force transmission; use proper torque wrench and lubrication for accurate application.
