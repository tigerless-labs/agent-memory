Sogginess Prevention: (1) Blind bake crust 10-12 min at 400F; (2) Use hot oven 400F; (3) Pat berries dry; (4) Don't overfill, leave 1-inch border; (5) Choose low-water berries (raspberries, blueberries, blackberries); (6) Add thin jam/frangipane base; (7) Use shortcrust pastry; (8) Bake until golden.

Berry Preparation (Macération): Pat berries dry → Mix with sugar (1-2 tbsp per cup) and cornstarch (1/4 tsp per cup) → Let sit 15-30 minutes for juice release → Strain through fine-mesh sieve → Arrange in single layer with border.

Serve with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.
