Whipped Cream Options: Vanilla Bean (vanilla extract or powder), Lemon Zest (bright citrusy complement), Honey (extra sweetness with floral notes).

Ice Cream Options: Vanilla Bean (classic, optionally add pinch of salt), Creamy Lavender (dried lavender buds or extract), Bourbon Vanilla (splash of bourbon for richness), Fresh Mint Churned (refresh complement to berries), Candied Ginger (spicy and intriguing flavor).
