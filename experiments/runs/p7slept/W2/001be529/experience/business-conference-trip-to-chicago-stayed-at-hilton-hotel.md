Attended work conference in Chicago (early 2023). Stayed at Hilton Chicago, centrally located in the Loop near Millennium Park and Art Institute. Positive experience: front desk staff were very helpful—when charger was forgotten at checkout, they held it for return. Room issue: air conditioning was too strong, required nightly adjustment.
