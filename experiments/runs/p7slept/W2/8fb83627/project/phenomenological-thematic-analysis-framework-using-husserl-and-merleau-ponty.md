Developing a descriptive phenomenological approach to thematic analysis of elder care texts and lived experience. Methodology includes:

- **Bracketing**: Setting aside preconceptions to approach data with openness
- **Phenomenological reduction**: Focusing on essential characteristics to understand lived experience holistically  
- **Phenomenological interpretation**: Reflecting on data to access deeper meanings

Current codes across texts (T1-T4):
- RES: Resilience in elder care
- MC: Mindfulness and compassion approaches to care work
- AI: Artificial intelligence impact on elder care
- CM: Impact of resident comments on author's mood and work
- IA: Interoceptive abilities and empathy in care work
- FC: Fear of not connecting with residents and desire to prove worth

Organizing into categories and seeking patterns of how themes interrelate.
