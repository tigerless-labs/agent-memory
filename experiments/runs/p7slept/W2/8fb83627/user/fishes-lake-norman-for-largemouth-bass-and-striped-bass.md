Lake Norman is a primary fishing destination. Successfully targets largemouth bass using soft plastic worm lures. Also fishes for striped bass on the lake, using techniques adapted from ocean fishing experience (slower, deliberate retrieve in calm water; focus on structure and baitfish activity).
