Key insight: focusing on progress rather than perfection helps stay motivated when struggling with anxiety and depression. Practices self-compassion by: celebrating small victories (taking walks, brief meditations, getting out of bed), being gentle on off days, acknowledging that setbacks are normal and part of the journey. Tends to be hard on herself when not meeting expectations, so reframing thoughts from 'I failed' to 'I'm doing my best' is important. Has a close friend who understands anxiety/depression struggles and serves as accountability buddy. Also practices daily gratitude by writing down three things grateful for each day.
