Daily meditation and yoga practice is important for managing anxiety and depression. Prefers starting small with 5-10 minute meditations and 10-20 minute yoga sessions. Uses apps like Headspace, Calm, or Insight Timer for guided meditations. Focuses on consistency over perfection, and celebrates small victories like taking walks or doing brief stretches. On busy days, incorporates quick flows, desk yoga, or gentle movements. Willing to adjust practice length based on energy and mental state.
