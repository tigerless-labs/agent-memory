Packing strategy recommendations for efficient travel:

**Clothing Packing:**
- 3-4-5 rule: aim for 3 tops, 4 bottoms, 5 socks/undergarments
- Create 3-5 outfits total from clothes brought
- Use packing cubes or small bags for organization
- Roll clothes to save space and reduce wrinkles
- Pack 1-2 versatile pieces that can be worn multiple times
- Focus on items that can be mixed and matched

**Decision Framework:**
- Check weather for destination
- Consider planned activities
- Plan outfits in advance
- Choose multipurpose items (e.g., scarves that work as blankets or accessories)
- Select clothes that work dressed up or down

**Toiletries:**
- Use travel-sized bottles and containers
- Decant full-size products into smaller containers
- Choose multi-purpose products
- Pack essentials only
- Keep liquids in clear quart-sized zip-top bag for security

**Carry-on vs Checked:**
- Carry-on: essentials, valuables, electronics, comfort items, change of clothes
- Checked: bulky items, extra toiletries, shoes, souvenirs
