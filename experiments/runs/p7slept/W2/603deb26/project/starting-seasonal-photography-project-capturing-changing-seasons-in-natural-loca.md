Decided to start a long-term photography project documenting the changing seasons in a natural setting (forest, wetland, park, or trail). Plans to visit the same location regularly throughout the year and capture it at consistent times to see how it transforms seasonally. Interested in combining this with wildlife/nature and street photography themes.

Attracted to this project because of interest in how light and colors change throughout the year, and opportunity to experiment with different techniques and compositions.

Related interests: night cityscape photography, wildlife and nature, street photography.
