Recommended mobile photo editing apps with quick adjustment capabilities for exposure, contrast, and color before social media sharing:

1. **Lightroom Mobile** (iOS, Android) - Free with optional subscription; advanced editing tools, exposure/contrast/color grading
2. **VSCO** (iOS, Android) - Film-inspired presets, free with in-app purchases
3. **Snapseed** (iOS, Android) - Professional-grade by Google, free; selective adjustments, layers, masking
4. **Adobe Photoshop Express** (iOS, Android) - Free; quick fixes for exposure, color, contrast
5. **TouchRetouch** (iOS, Android) - Remove unwanted objects; $1.99 (iOS), $2.99 (Android)
6. **Facetune** (iOS, Android) - Portrait retouching; $3.99 (iOS), $2.99 (Android)
7. **PicsArt** (iOS, Android) - Feature-rich; exposure, color, contrast; free with in-app purchases
8. **Filterstorm** (iOS) - Advanced tools (curves, layers, masking); $2.99
9. **Afterlight** (iOS, Android) - Intuitive filters, textures, frames; $2.99 (iOS), $1.99 (Android)
10. **Polarr** (iOS, Android) - AI-powered; quick exposure/color/contrast adjustments; free with in-app purchases
