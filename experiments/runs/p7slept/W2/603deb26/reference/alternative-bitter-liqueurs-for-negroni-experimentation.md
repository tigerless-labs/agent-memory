Instead of Campari: Cynar (artichoke-based, vegetal and slightly sweet, try 0.5 oz Cynar + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica); Aperol (sweeter, more orange-forward, use 1 oz Aperol + 1 oz Carpano Antica); Luxardo Bitter (intense herbal profile, use 0.5 oz + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica); Suze (French gentian-based, crisp and earthy, use 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica); Meletti (Italian, sweeter and citrus-forward, use 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica). Adjust ratios of bitter liqueur to vermouth as needed since potencies vary.
