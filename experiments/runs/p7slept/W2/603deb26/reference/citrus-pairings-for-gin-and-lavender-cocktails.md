Options beyond grapefruit and lemon: Orange (classic with lavender, try blood orange for depth); Yuzu (Japanese citrus, cross between grapefruit and orange, tart with subtle sweetness); Kumquat (small, sweet, use as syrup or muddle fresh); Satsuma (sweet mandarin, easy-to-peel, pairs nicely with gin and lavender); Bergamot (subtle, complex, often used in tea and perfumery). Tips: use fresh citrus for brightness, experiment with citrus peels for aroma, balance citrus carefully as it can overpower other flavors, consider citrus twists or peels as garnish.
