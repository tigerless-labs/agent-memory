### Lavender Cocktails\n**Lavender Gin Fizz:** 1.5 oz gin, 0.5 oz fresh lime juice, 0.5 oz lavender syrup, 0.5 oz simple syrup in shaker; top with soda water, garnish lavender sprig & lime wheel\n\n**Lavender Dream:** 2 oz vodka, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup in shaker; strain to coupe glass\n\n### Grapefruit Cocktails\n**Grapefruit Basil Martini:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, 0.5 oz lime juice, 0.5 oz basil syrup in shaker; garnish grapefruit twist & basil leaf\n\n**Grapefruit Rosemary Sparkler:** 2 oz tequila, 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, 0.5 oz lime juice, 0.5 oz rosemary syrup in shaker; top with soda water, garnish grapefruit slice & rosemary sprig\n\n### Mixed Flavor Cocktails\n**Lavender Grapefruit Spritz:** 2 oz prosecco, 1 oz grapefruit juice, 0.5 oz lavender syrup, splash soda water; garnish grapefruit slice & lavender sprig\n\n**Grapefruit Lavender Collins:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz grapefruit juice, 0.5 oz lavender syrup, 0.5 oz lemon juice in shaker; top with soda water, garnish grapefruit slice & lavender sprig\n\n**Pimm's Cup:** Pimm's No. 1 mixed with lemonade, garnished with cucumber, mint, and fruit\n\n**Bee's Knees:** Gin, honey (via honey syrup), and lemon juice\n\n**Cucumber Gimlet variants:** Combinations of gin, fresh cucumber juice/slices, lime juice, and fresh mint\n\n### Other Classic Gin Cocktails\n**Gin Rickey:** Gin, lime juice, soda water\n\n**Gin-Gin Mule:** Gin, lime juice, ginger beer, served in copper mug\n\n**Aviation:** Gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, lemon juice\n\n**Last Word:** Gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, fresh lime juice\n\n### Syrups & Infusions\n**Honey Syrup:** 1:1 ratio honey to water, heated until honey dissolves, cooled and stored in fridge up to 2 weeks\n\n**Lavender Syrup:** Steep 0.25 cup dried lavender buds in 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes, strain, store up to 2 weeks\n\n**Basil Syrup:** Steep 0.25 cup fresh basil leaves in 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes, strain
