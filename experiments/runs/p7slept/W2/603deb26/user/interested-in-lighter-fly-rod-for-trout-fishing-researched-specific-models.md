Looking to purchase a lighter fly rod designed for smaller fish and trout fishing. Prefers 2-5 weight lines, fast or medium-fast action, and 9-foot length (or shorter 7-8 feet for tight spaces).

Researched rod models and prices:
- Orvis Clearwater: 198-298 dollars, entry-level to intermediate
- Sage Igniter: 400-500 dollars, high-performance, fast action
- Winston Pure: 300-400 dollars, premium, smooth precise casting
- Redington Crux: 150-250 dollars, budget-friendly, good for backups
- TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series: 200-300 dollars, high-quality, smooth action

Prefers graphite (lighter, more sensitive) but recognizes fiberglass durability trade-off. Plans to try rods at local fly shop before purchase.
