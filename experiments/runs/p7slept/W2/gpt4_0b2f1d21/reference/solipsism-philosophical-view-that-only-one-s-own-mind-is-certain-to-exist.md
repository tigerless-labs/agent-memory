Solipsism is the philosophical idea that only one's own mind is certain to exist, and that all external realities—including other minds—are uncertain and may not exist. According to solipsism, the only person you can trust to have a truly conscious and real existence is yourself. Other people and external objects may be mere illusions or figments of your imagination.
