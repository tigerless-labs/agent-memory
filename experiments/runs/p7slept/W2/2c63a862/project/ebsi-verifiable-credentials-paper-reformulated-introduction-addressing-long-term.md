Research paper on decentralized application for long-term storage of EBSI-issued verifiable credentials.

**Key standards:**
- W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model: https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/
- TR-ESOR (BSI middleware for digital document preservation): https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/BSI/Publikationen/TechnischeRichtlinien/TR03125/BSI_TR_03125_V1_2_1.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=2

**Three challenges addressed in paper:**
1. Long-term storage: Reliable and efficient long-term storage for EBSI-issued verifiable credentials with guaranteed availability and integrity
2. Privacy preservation: Protecting credential holder privacy while maintaining system trust and security
3. TR-ESOR integration: Implementing TR-ESOR middleware as practical long-term storage solution for VCs

**Introduction structure:**
- Opens with trust element in decentralized systems vs traditional PKI limitations
- Explains shift from handwritten to digital documents requiring secure long-term storage
- Defines verifiable credentials (VCs): cryptographically verified digital credentials enabling issuer → holder → verifier flow
- Introduces EBSI as decentralized platform for cross-border digital services
- Positions paper as addressing the three specific challenges above
- Emphasizes contribution to robust, privacy-preserving credential management in decentralized systems

Refined through iterative reformulation to include smooth transitions between concepts.
