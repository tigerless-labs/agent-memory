## Overview
- Destination: Crete, Greece
- Duration: 7 nights, 6 days
- Stay: 2 nights Heraklion, 3 nights Chania
- Season: April
- Focus: Nature, food/dining, main tourist sites

## Day 1-2: Heraklion
**Day 1:**
- Arrival at Nikos Kazantzakis International Airport
- Lunch: Peskesi (traditional Cretan cuisine)
- Heraklion Archaeological Museum (Minoan artifacts)
- Walk old town: Venetian Fortress Koules, Agios Titos Church
- Dinner: Karavitis Taverna (grilled meats, seafood)
- Evening: Heraklion seafront stroll

**Day 2:**
- Breakfast: Erganos café (Cretan specialties)
- Palace of Knossos (largest Minoan palace)
- Lunch: Arismari (organic, vegetarian/vegan options)
- Cretaquarium (Mediterranean marine life)
- Optional: Watercity Waterpark
- Dinner: Peskesi (Dakos or Kalitsounia)

## Day 3-6: Chania
**Day 3:**
- Transfer to Chania (northwest coast)
- Lunch: Ta Chalkina (traditional taverna, old town)
- Maritime Museum of Crete
- Walk: Venetian Harbor, Firkas Fortress
- Dinner: Tamam (creative modern Cretan)
- Evening: Beachfront promenade walk

**Day 4:**
- Breakfast: Koukouvaya café
- Botanical Park and Gardens of Crete
- Lunch: Almyra (beachfront, fresh seafood, Cretan dishes)
- Agia Triada Monastery (17th-century)
- Dinner: Salis (Greek/Mediterranean)
- Walk: Old Venetian walls

**Day 5:**
- Breakfast: Tafarnabu (homemade baked goods)
- Samaria Gorge (Europe's longest gorge)
- Lunch: To Spitiko (family-run, traditional Greek)
- Elafonisi Beach (pink sand, crystal clear waters)
- Dinner: Chrisostomos (grilled meats and fish)
- Sunset beach walk

**Day 6:**
- Breakfast: Enetiko café
- Chania Lighthouse (Venetian-era, views)
- Lunch: Tamam
- Ancient Aptera (theater, Roman cistern, Venetian fortress)
- Dinner: Thalassino Ageri (seafood, sea views)
- Evening: Old town walk and nightlife

## Key Recommendations
- Restaurants cover traditional Cretan, modern fusion, seafood, and vegetarian options
- Mix of archaeological sites, natural attractions, and coastal activities
- Hourly structure includes meal breaks, sightseeing, beaches, and leisure walks
