Experimenting with authentic Italian pizza, specifically Neapolitan-style. Appreciates simplicity and high-quality ingredients. Interested in traditional topping combinations like Margherita (San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, olive oil, basil), Quattro Formaggi, Prosciutto e Funghi, Puttanesca, and others. Understands that truffle oil is modern/gourmet, not traditional Neapolitan, and prefers to focus on traditional quality ingredients when making Neapolitan-style pizza.
