User is preparing backyard infrastructure (fencing and shelter) to bring home Luna, a lamb. Fencing should be at least 48 inches tall with wire mesh no larger than 2 inches, buried 12-18 inches underground. Shelter should be at least 4x4 feet with insulation, ventilation, and predator-proofing (hardware cloth, secure doors/latches).
