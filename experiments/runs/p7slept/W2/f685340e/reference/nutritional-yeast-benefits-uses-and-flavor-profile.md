**What it is:** Yeast grown on sugarcane or beet molasses, harvested, washed, and dried.\n\n**Nutritional benefits:**\n- High in protein (good for vegan diets)\n- Rich in B vitamins, including B12 (often lacking in plant-based diets)\n- Good source of fiber (soluble and insoluble)\n- Mineral-rich: selenium, zinc, copper\n\n**Flavor profile:** Nutty, cheesy, slightly sweet; umami taste\n\n**Uses:**\n- Cheese substitutes and vegan cheese alternatives\n- Savory dishes (soups, stews, sauces)\n- Vegan meat dishes (tofu, tempeh, seitan)\n- Snacks (popcorn, pizza, crackers)\n\n**In tempeh marinades:** Adds savory umami flavor that complements citrus and spices; enhances 'fishy' flavor, balances acidity of lime juice, adds richness and complexity. Start with ~1/2 teaspoon and adjust to taste as it's potent.
