Developed approach to product photography for candle business:\n- Use natural light from windows with soft, indirect illumination\n- Neutral backgrounds (white, gray, beige, rustic wood) to focus on product\n- Style scenes with nature-inspired props: botanicals, dried flowers, reclaimed wood\n- Include earth-toned fabrics like linen and cotton\n- Photography approach emphasizes simplicity and avoids overcrowding\n- Edit photos for consistency in color, contrast, and brightness\n- High-resolution images for online applications and sales platforms
