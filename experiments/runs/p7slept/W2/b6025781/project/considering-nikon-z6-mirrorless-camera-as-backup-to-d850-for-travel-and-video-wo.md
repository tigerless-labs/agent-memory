Evaluating Nikon Z6 as backup camera body to complement D850.\n\n**Motivation:**\n- Smaller and lighter than D850, ideal for travel\n- Superior video: 4K UHD in full-frame (vs D850 cropped 4K), 100 Mbps bitrate, 1080p 120fps\n- In-body image stabilization (IBIS) — 5 stops\n- Advanced autofocus: 273 phase-detection points (vs D850's 153), 90% frame coverage\n- Eye-detection AF\n- Better low-light autofocus performance\n\n**Concerns:**\n- Different mount: Z-mount requires Nikon FTZ Adapter for F-mount lenses\n- Already heavily invested in F-mount lens ecosystem\n- Autofocus may be slightly slower using adapter on legacy lenses\n- Different lens ecosystem would eventually require native Z-mount investments\n\n**Alternatives considered:**\n- Nikon D500: APS-C, same F-mount, excellent autofocus, no 4K\n- Nikon D5: Flagship autofocus and low-light, heavier, more expensive\n\n**Deciding factors:**\n- Interest in expanding into video production makes Z6's video capabilities very appealing\n- Love D850's autofocus but Z6's system is arguably superior\n- Planning to test Z6 before final decision
