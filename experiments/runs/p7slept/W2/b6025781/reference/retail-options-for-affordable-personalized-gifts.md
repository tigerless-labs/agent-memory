Online and retail options for finding personalized items:

Online: Etsy, Amazon, Zazzle, Personalization Mall
Physical stores: Things Remembered, Target, Walmart

Etsy: handmade and personalized items from independent sellers, filters for personalized items, sort by price
Amazon: personalized jewelry, accessories, home decor with custom titles
Zazzle: create customized products, often has sales and discounts
Personalization Mall: specializes in personalized gifts across categories
Things Remembered: physical store with personalized items, jewelry, frames, accessories
Target and Walmart: limited selection of personalized items like monogrammed towels and mugs

Typical price range: 10 to 30 dollars

Tip: Check production and shipping times for timely delivery. Be flexible with design choices to keep costs lower.
