**Author:** Kate Quinn\n**Genre:** Historical Fiction (WWI)\n**Setting:** World War I and post-war period; based on the real Alice Network, a Belgian espionage ring led by Louise de Bettignies\n**Characteristics:** Faster-paced and plot-driven than 'All the Light We Cannot See'; page-turner with twists and turns; multiple perspectives; shorter chapters; well-developed characters; richly detailed historical context; explores women's roles in WWI espionage.\n**Historical basis:** The Alice Network was a real WWI spy network (1915-1918) that gathered German intelligence for the British.\n**Reader note:** More energetic pace; good for someone who enjoyed 'The Nightingale' and wants to learn about WWI.
