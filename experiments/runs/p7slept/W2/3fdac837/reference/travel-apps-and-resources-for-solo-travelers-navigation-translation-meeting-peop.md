Apps and platforms useful for solo travel:\n\n**Navigation & Planning**\n- Google Maps\n- TripIt\n- Rome2rio (route planning)\n\n**Language**\n- Google Translate (text, speech, camera translation)\n\n**Meeting People & Social**\n- Meetup (local events and meetups)\n- Bumble BFF (meet friends)\n- Travel Buddies (connect with travelers)\n- Facebook groups: Solo Travelers, Female Solo Travelers\n- Couchsurfing\n\n**Safety**\n- bSafe (emergency alerting)\n- Revolar (personal safety alerts)\n\n**Activities & Experiences**\n- GetYourGuide\n- Viator\n- Airbnb Experiences\n\n**Accommodation**\n- Hostelworld\n- Booking.com\n- Airbnb\n- HI Hostels\n\nOther tips: Share plans with hotel staff/friends/family before going out; keep phone charged and location shared; use official/reputable transportation services (licensed taxis, ride-sharing apps).
