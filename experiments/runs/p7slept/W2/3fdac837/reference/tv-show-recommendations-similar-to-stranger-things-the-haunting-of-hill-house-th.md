## Recommendations (May 2023)

Suggested shows with sci-fi/horror/mystery elements similar to Stranger Things:

1. **The Haunting of Hill House** (Netflix) - Horror series with atmospheric and emotional storytelling
2. **The X-Files** (Hulu, Amazon Prime) - Classic sci-fi horror following FBI agents investigating paranormal cases
3. **The OA** (Netflix) - Thought-provoking exploration of interdimensional travel and near-death experiences
4. **The Twilight Zone** (CBS All Access) - Jordan Peele reboot; anthology series with standalone sci-fi/horror episodes
5. **The Umbrella Academy** (Netflix) - Superhero series based on comics; dysfunctional family with special powers
6. **Locke & Key** (Netflix) - Horror-fantasy with magical keys and supernatural elements
7. **The Passage** (FOX, Hulu) - Vampire horror series based on novels
8. **Colony** (USA Network, Netflix) - Dystopian sci-fi set in occupied Los Angeles
9. **The Man in the High Castle** (Amazon Prime) - Alternate history; rebels fighting Nazi occupation
10. **Electric Dreams** (Amazon Prime) - Philip K. Dick anthology series with sci-fi and horror twists

All offer mix of adventure, mystery, and supernatural elements.
