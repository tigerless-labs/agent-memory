## Top Things to Do in Amsterdam

1. **Van Gogh Museum** — Largest collection of Vincent Van Gogh's artwork in the world
2. **Anne Frank House** — WWI hideout; secret annex; important historical museum
3. **Bike Tours** — Amsterdam is known for bike culture; popular way to explore the city
4. **Rijksmuseum** — Renowned art museum; features Dutch art including Rembrandt and Vermeer
5. **Canal Cruises** — Amsterdam known as 'Venice of the North'; unique perspective from water
6. **Jordaan Neighborhood** — Charming area; small streets, boutiques, restaurants and bars
7. **Traditional Dutch Food** — Bitterballen, stroopwafels, herring
8. **Flower Market** — Only floating flower market in the world; colorful and fragrant
9. **Heineken Experience** — History and brewing process of Heineken beer
10. **Red Light District** — Famous attraction; adult entertainment and vibrant nightlife

Note: Recommendations from May 2023; verify current hours, admission policies before visiting.
