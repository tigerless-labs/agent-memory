Plan to declutter apartment and sell antique tea set (from cousin Rachel) and vintage typewriter (from dad) for extra cash.

Strategy:
- Research online marketplaces (eBay, Etsy) to determine comparable values
- Contact local antique dealers specializing in tea sets and typewriters
- Emphasize provenance and family history when discussing items
- Focus on item features, condition, and documentation
- Be open to offers and negotiations

Approach: Will highlight the items' significance and history rather than emphasizing the decluttering motivation.
