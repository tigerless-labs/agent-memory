Popular foot-accessible sunrise viewpoints in Rocky Mountain National Park:

- **Bear Lake & Dream Lake**: Short trail to Dream Lake offers views of Hallett Peak and Flattop Mountain reflecting in calm water
- **Nymph Lake**: Hike to summit offers 360-degree views of surrounding mountains  
- **Emerald Lake Trail**: Good sunrise views
- **Moraine Park**: Accessible by car with short walk to open meadow, or by short trail hikes to other viewpoints

Also mentioned: Trail Ridge Road summit (offers alpine tundra views and Alpine Visitor Center amenities) but requires ~1 hour drive.
