Flagship wine from Caymus Vineyards (family-owned, founded 1972). 100 percent Cabernet Sauvignon from select Napa Valley vineyards, aged 20 months in French oak (90 percent new oak).

**Tasting Profile**
- Dark fruits: black cherry, blackberry, black currant with hints of dark chocolate and mocha
- Spices: cinnamon, nutmeg, clove
- Oak notes: vanilla and caramel
- Tannins: velvety smooth with long finish
- Well-balanced acidity

**Accolades**
- Wine Spectator's Top 100 Wines of the Year (multiple times)
- Wine Enthusiast's Wine of the Year (2012)
- 95+ ratings from Robert Parker, Wine Spectator, Vinous

**Pricing**
Average price: 150-180 dollars per bottle. Available at fine wine shops, online retailers, and winery direct.

**Food Pairings**
Grilled steak (ribeye, filet mignon), roasted lamb or beef tenderloin, pan-seared duck or game meats, mature cheeses (cheddar, gouda) and charcuterie.
