Italian bread recipe recommended for someone transitioning to savory baking after enjoying sourdough with butter.

**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 cups warm water
- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (or bread flour for chewier crumb)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 sprigs of fresh rosemary, chopped
- Coarse sea salt, for topping (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine warm water and yeast in a bowl. Let sit 5-10 minutes until frothy.
2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix until shaggy dough forms.
3. Knead 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Place in oiled bowl, cover, let rise 1 hour until doubled.
5. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
6. Punch down dough, shape into rectangle on parchment paper.
7. Dimple with fingers, sprinkle rosemary and coarse salt.
8. Drizzle with olive oil, bake 20-25 minutes until golden.

**Rosemary tips:**
- Use 2-3 sprigs fresh rosemary (2-3 tbsp chopped)
- Choose vibrant green sprigs with piney aroma
- Chop just before using to release oils
- Can combine with other herbs (thyme, oregano, parsley) for balance
- Rosemary is potent—start with less, adjust to taste

**Flour notes:**
- Bread flour produces chewier crumb with larger holes
- May need slightly more water with bread flour
- Alternative: Caputo "00" flour for tender, delicate crumb and crispy crust
