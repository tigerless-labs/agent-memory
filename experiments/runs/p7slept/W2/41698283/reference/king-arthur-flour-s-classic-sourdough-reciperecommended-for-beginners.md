Recommended as an excellent beginner sourdough recipe due to simple ingredients, clear instructions, flexible schedule, and great learning value.

**Why it's good for beginners:**
- Simple ingredients only: flour, water, salt, sourdough starter
- Clear, well-written instructions
- Flexible schedule fits daily routines
- Perfect for learning basics and experimenting with variations

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup sourdough starter (active and bubbly)
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp water

**Instructions:**
1. Mix starter, flour, and salt until just combined.
2. Add water and mix until shaggy mass.
3. Knead 10 minutes, then rest in oiled bowl.
4. Ferment 4-5 hours until nearly doubled.
5. Shape into round or oblong loaf on baking sheet or proofing basket.
6. Proof 2-3 hours until nearly doubled again.
7. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) with Dutch oven or steamy setup.
8. Score bread, bake 25-30 minutes until golden brown.

**Bread flour note:** Can substitute all-purpose flour but bread flour produces chewier crumb with better gluten development; may require slightly more water. Monitor dough's progress and adjust proofing time as needed.
