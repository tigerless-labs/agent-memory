Planning an art project combining mixed media collage and abstract expressionism techniques.

**Project structure:**
- Small group format: 3-4 kids per group
- Large-scale collaborative collages
- Theme-based (considering cityscape or forest themes)

**Materials approach:**
- Variety of papers (construction, scrapbook, newspaper, magazine cutouts, tissue, cardboard)
- Adhesives (glue sticks, white glue, tape, staples, decoupage)
- Paints and inks (tempera, acrylic, watercolor, markers, colored pencils, stamp pads)
- Textures (fabric, yarn, felt, burlap, natural materials)
- Found objects (buttons, bottle caps, cardboard tubes)
- Additional (glitter, stickers, stencils)

**Facilitation approach:**
- Introduce theme with visual inspiration and brainstorming
- Group discussion and sketching before creating
- Material exploration and experimentation
- Emphasize process over product
- Provide guidance while encouraging imagination

**Inspired by:** Recent museum and gallery visits with mixed styles and ideas
