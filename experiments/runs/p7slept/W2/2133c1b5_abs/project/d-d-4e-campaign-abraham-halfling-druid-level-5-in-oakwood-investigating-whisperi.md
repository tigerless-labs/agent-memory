## Campaign: Evermere Adventure

**Character:** Abraham, Halfling Druid, Level 5 (D&D 4th Edition)

**Setting:** Oakwood village at the edge of the Whispering Woods

**Plot Hook:** Village elder Eadric summoned Abraham to investigate mysterious disturbances:
- Forest creatures becoming unnaturally aggressive with glowing eyes
- Aggressive plant life, trees uprooting and blocking paths
- A mysterious shrouded figure with strong magical aura spotted in the forest

**Key Locations:**
- Silver Stag Inn (where Abraham is staying)
- Moonstone Spire: ancient ruin in the forest rumored to house a powerful artifact that can bend nature to one's will

**NPCs & Information:**
- **Eadric** (village elder, Abraham's old friend) — coordinating village defense, suggested investigating Moonstone Spire
- **Alaric** (innkeeper) — supportive, provided lodging
- **Mabel** (villager) — witnessed pack of wolves with unnatural glow, unusually aggressive
- **Thom** (woodsman) — reported trees lashing out and uprooting, becoming too dangerous to venture into forest
- **Lysander** (traveling merchant) — glimpsed the mysterious figure from afar, sensed powerful magical energy

**Current Status (after session):** Abraham has gathered initial intelligence at the inn and is preparing to investigate further. Next steps to consider: seek more information, rest before forest expedition, or venture toward Moonstone Spire.
