Feeds fish twice daily with rotation of:\n- **Tetra pellet food**: staple/regular meals (well-established brand, good acceptance)\n- **Frozen brine shrimp**: fish's favorite, goes crazy for it (excellent source of omega-3 and nutrients)\n- **Frozen bloodworms**: secondary supplement, fish are finickier about these but still eat them (rich in iron and protein)\n\nBrand recommendations explored:\n- San Francisco Bay Brand (brine shrimp from Great Salt Lake)\n- Hikari\n- Argent\n- Omega One\n\nApproach: varying diet to ensure balanced nutrient intake and mimic natural foraging behavior.
