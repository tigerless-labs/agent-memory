Creates book-focused social media content primarily on Instagram. Strategy includes:

**Hashtag approach**: Uses diversified, targeted hashtags rather than repeating the same set across posts. Prefers #bookrecs (12M+ uses) over #bookrecommendations (6M+ uses) for book recommendation content. Mixes popular hashtags (#booklover, #bookstagram) with niche genre-specific tags (#fantasybooks, #romancereaders).

**Content formats**: 
- Instagram Stories for behind-the-scenes content: prep work for #BookClubChat, posting thoughts on discussions, Q&A sessions
- IGTV for longer-form content: in-depth book reviews, author interviews, book-related challenges
- Posts asking followers for engagement: book recommendations, favorite books, reading preferences

**Engagement**: Runs polls on Stories to gauge follower preferences on content types (behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, book reviews).
