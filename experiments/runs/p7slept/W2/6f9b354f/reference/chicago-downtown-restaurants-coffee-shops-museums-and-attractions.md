Coffee: Intelligentsia (53 E Randolph, 2-min walk), La Colombe (1552 N Wells), Peet's (444 S LaSalle). Observation decks: Willis Tower Skydeck (233 S Wacker, 103rd floor, 10-min walk), 360 CHICAGO (875 N Michigan, 94th floor). Museums: Art Institute (111 S Michigan, 10-min), Field Museum (1400 S Lake Shore, 15-min). Tours: Chicago's First Lady Cruises (90-min architectural cruise). Running: Lakefront Trail (18.5 miles along Lake Michigan), Millennium Park loop (1.5 miles), Grant Park loop (1.5 miles).
