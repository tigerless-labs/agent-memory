## Summer 3-Day Couple Itinerary

**Day 1:**
- Niushou Mountain: Buddhist cultural park with temples, pagodas, Buddhist museum, giant Buddha statue; serene and peaceful
- Qinhuai River: Historic scenic waterway; romantic boat rides; especially beautiful at night with colorful lights

**Day 2:**
- Nanjing City Wall: Ancient military fortification; gates, watchtowers; views of city and countryside
- Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan): Historic sites including Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, gardens, hiking trails; features Xuanwu Lake and Botanical Garden

**Day 3:**
- Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple Area): Historic district with temples, shops, street vendors, local cuisine; beautiful at night with illuminated buildings and streets
- Xuanwu Lake: Tranquil natural area; sculptures and monuments; playgrounds for children

## Winter 3-Day Couple Itinerary

**Day 1:**
- Niushou Mountain: Serene and peaceful in winter; Buddhist cultural park with pavilions, temples, cultural attractions; cool weather makes for refreshing visit
- Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan): Snow-covered landscape creates serene environment; Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum; boat rides on Xuanwu Lake

**Day 2:**
- Nanjing City Wall: Ancient military defense system; unique quality in winter; cool weather for comfortable exploration; learn about China's ancient history and military tactics
- Confucius Temple Area (Fuzimiao): Peaceful and romantic atmosphere in winter; temples, gardens, markets; shops and restaurants for local culture

**Day 3:**
- Ming Palace Ruins (Minggugong): Remains of Ming Dynasty imperial palace destroyed in Taiping Rebellion; mysterious quality in winter; explore palace grounds and Ming Dynasty history
- Presidential Palace (Zongtong Fu): Historic residence of Sun Yat-sen, first president of Republic of China; serene and tranquil atmosphere in winter; explore buildings and gardens to learn modern Chinese history

**Key Note:** Niushou Mountain is the must-include attraction in both seasonal itineraries.
