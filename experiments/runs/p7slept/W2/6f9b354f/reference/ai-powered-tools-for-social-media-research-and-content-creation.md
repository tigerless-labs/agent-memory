**For Topic & Keyword Research:**
- BuzzSumo: trending topics, keywords, content performance, topic suggestions, influencer identification
- Ahrefs: content explorer, topic/keyword suggestions, competitor analysis, content gaps
- SEMrush: topic research, keyword analysis, competitors, content gaps
- Google Keyword Planner: free tool for keyword suggestions and content topics
- Moz Keyword Explorer: keyword analysis, competitors, content strategies
- Ubersuggest: free tool for keywords, content ideas, competitor analysis

**For Content Inspiration & Q&A:**
- Answer Engine: AI-powered Q&A to uncover audience questions and topics
- Quora: community-driven Q&A for insights and content inspiration

**For Content Optimization:**
- Acrolinx: content analysis, grammar, style improvements
- Content Blossom: content optimization for readability and engagement
- Conductor: AI-powered SEO and engagement optimization

**For Engagement:**
- Chatbots: ManyChat, Dialogflow, Microsoft Bot Framework for 24/7 support
- Sentiment Analysis: Brandwatch, Hootsuite Insights, NetBase
- Personalization: AgilOne, Sailthru
