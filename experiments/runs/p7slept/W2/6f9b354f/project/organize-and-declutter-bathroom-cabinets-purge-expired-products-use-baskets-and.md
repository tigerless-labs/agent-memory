**Objective:** Tidy and reorganize bathroom cabinets to maximize storage and keep items easily accessible

**Status:** Starting week of 2023-05-21

**Approach:**
1. **Purge and declutter** - Remove everything from cabinets; sort into keep/donate/discard; discard expired products and unused items
2. **Organize** - Use dividers, baskets, and stackable containers to separate items by category (skincare, haircare, oral care, medications, etc.)
3. **Utilize space** - Use back of cabinet door for additional storage with hooks, racks, or adhesive pockets
4. **Maintain** - Label each section for easy identification; keep frequently used items front and center

**Key tips to remember:**
- Heavy items (hair dryers, curling irons) go on lower shelves
- Consider lazy Susan for hard-to-reach corner spaces
- Store high-touch items (handles, faucets) appropriately
- Make system visually appealing and easy to maintain
