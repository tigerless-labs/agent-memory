Planning a home office nook in the spare bedroom with a minimalist aesthetic and natural wood accents. Design includes:

- Floating desk positioned in the middle of the room
- Built-in shelving for storage
- Minimalist aesthetic with natural wood accents
- Greenery: considering a small herb garden or vase with fresh flowers
- Lighting: pendant light above desk + table lamps for task lighting
- Minimalist wooden desk lamp with simple shade
- Sleek, modern pendant light design
- Warm color temperature lighting preferred (2700K-3000K)
- Considering brands like Muuto, Lumens, Schoolhouse Electric

Goals: maximize productivity and comfort, create a calming atmosphere.
