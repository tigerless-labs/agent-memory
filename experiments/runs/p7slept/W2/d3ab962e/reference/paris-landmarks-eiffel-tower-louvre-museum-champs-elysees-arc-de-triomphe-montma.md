For a 1-2 day Paris visit, prioritize Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum (located near each other, can see both in one day). Secondary landmarks include:
- Champs-Élysées 
- Arc de Triomphe (viewable from its top)
- Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur Basilica
- Palace of Versailles
- Sainte-Chapelle
- Musée d'Orsay
- Centre Pompidou
- Les Invalides
- Place des Vosges
- Panthéon

Crowd-avoidance strategy: Visit early morning (after opening) or late afternoon/evening. Weekdays less crowded than weekends. Off-season (fall/winter) has fewest crowds.
