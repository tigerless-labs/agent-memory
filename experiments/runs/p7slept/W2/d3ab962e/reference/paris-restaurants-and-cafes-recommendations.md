Cafes near Eiffel Tower: Cafe de l'Homme (views, creative cuisine), Cafe Gustave (15th arr, brunches), Carette (pastries, coffee). Restaurants near Louvre: Le Grand Vefour (Michelin-starred), Le Comptoir du Louvre (classic French), Verjus (1st arr, tasting menus).
