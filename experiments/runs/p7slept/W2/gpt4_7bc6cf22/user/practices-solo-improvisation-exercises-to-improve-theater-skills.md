Interested in regular solo improvisation practice to improve skills between group sessions and workshops.\n\nFavorite solo exercises:\n- Free writing (10-15 minutes without stopping or editing)\n- Stream-of-consciousness monologues\n- Character development and experimentation\n- Solo scene work with imagined scenarios\n- Sound and movement exercises for physicality\n- Status games (playing with high/low/neutral status)\n- Emotional exploration scenes\n- Improv games adapted for solo practice (e.g., Freeze, Sound Effects)\n- Recording and reflecting on sessions\n\nPractice habits:\n- Sets timer to stay focused\n- Practices with mirror to observe body language\n- Willing to experiment with props and costumes\n- Views mistakes as learning opportunities\n- Focuses on exploration and growth, not perfection\n- Aims for regular practice (even 15-20 minutes weekly)
