When substituting honey for granulated sugar in recipes, use a general ratio of 1/2 to 2/3 cup of honey for every 1 cup of sugar called for in the recipe. Honey is sweeter than sugar, so less is needed. Be prepared to adjust liquid content in recipe accordingly. Honey can help retain moisture in baked goods. Different honey varieties (clover, manuka, acacia) offer unique flavor profiles.
