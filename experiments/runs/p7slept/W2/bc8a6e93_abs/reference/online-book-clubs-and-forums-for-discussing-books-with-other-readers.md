Online communities for book discussion and recommendations: Goodreads (largest community with millions of members, groups, and discussions), Book of the Month Club (curated selections with forums), Reddit r/books (vibrant reader community), Book Riot (blog with online book clubs), LibraryThing (virtual bookshelf and group discussions), Popsugar Reading Challenge (community challenges and discussions), The Book Smugglers (blog focused on science fiction, fantasy, romance), Kirkus Reviews (publication hosting book clubs and discussions).
