Authors and books recommended for fans of slow-burning thrillers with unreliable narrators and plot twists similar to Gillian Flynn.

Primary recommendations the user will check out: Shari Lapena (The Couple Next Door, A Stranger in the House), Fiona Barton (The Widow).

Other recommended authors: Clare Mackintosh (I Let You Go, The Farm), Mary Kubica (The Good Girl, Don't You Cry), Tana French (Dublin Murder Squad series), Laura Lippman (What the Dead Know, And When She Was Good), Megan Abbott (The Fever, You Will Know Me), Paula Hawkins (The Girl on the Train, Into the Water), Ruth Ware (In a Dark, Dark Wood, The Woman in Cabin 10), B.A. Paris (Behind Closed Doors, The Breakdown).
