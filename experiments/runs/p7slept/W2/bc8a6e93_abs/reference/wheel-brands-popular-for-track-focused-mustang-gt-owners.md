Popular track-performance wheel brands and models for Mustang GT:\n\n1. **Forgiato** - Forgiato Tecnica, Forgiato Veloce\n2. **OZ Racing** - OZ Superforgiata, OZ Ultraleggera\n3. **HRE** - HRE P101, HRE R101 (premium, lightweight, high-strength)\n4. **Enkei** - Enkei RPF1, Enkei TS-5 (high strength, low weight)\n5. **APEX** - APEX EC-7, APEX FL-5 (US-based, lightweight and strong)\n\n**Wheel selection criteria:**\n- Minimum 18" diameter, 9-10" width\n- Hub bore: 72.56mm (Mustang GT standard)\n- Forged wheels preferred over cast (stronger, lighter)\n- Lower offset improves handling but may require modifications
