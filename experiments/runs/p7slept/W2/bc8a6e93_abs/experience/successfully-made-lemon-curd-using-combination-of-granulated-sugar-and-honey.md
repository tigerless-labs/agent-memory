Made a batch of lemon curd using a combination of granulated sugar and honey that turned out really well. Found that the rich, caramel-like flavor of the honey pairs beautifully with the brightness of lemon. Using honey in combination with other sweeteners helps balance out the flavor and texture of the dessert.
