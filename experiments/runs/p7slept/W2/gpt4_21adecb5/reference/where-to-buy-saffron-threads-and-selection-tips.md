**Where to buy saffron:**
- Specialty spice stores (Middle Eastern, Indian, Spanish cuisine)
- Gourmet markets (Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, upscale grocery stores)
- Online retailers: Amazon, Penzeys Spices, The Spice House, Sahadi's
- Some well-stocked supermarkets: Kroger, Safeway

**What to look for:**
- Deep red-orange color
- Strong, sweet, earthy aroma
- Whole threads (avoid powder — can be adulterated with fillers)
- High-quality indicator: Spanish origin (Crocus sativus), Iran, or Kashmir

**Saffron substitutes (imperfect):**
- Turmeric: 1/2 tsp per 1/2 tsp saffron (yellow color, no exotic flavor)
- Smoked paprika: 1/2 tsp per 1/2 tsp saffron (smoky, slightly sweet)
- Annatto seeds: 1/2 tsp per 1/2 tsp saffron (sweet, nutty, yellow-orange color)
