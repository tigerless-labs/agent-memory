Recommended Spanish-language TV shows for improving Spanish language skills:\n\n**Drama/Thriller**\n- **La Casa de Papel** (Money Heist) - Netflix: Thieves planning to rob Royal Mint of Spain\n- **Narcos** - Netflix: Biographical crime drama about Pablo Escobar\n- **Velvet** - Netflix: 1960s romantic drama about fashion designer and seamstress\n\n**Comedy**\n- **Paquita Salas** - Netflix: Hilarious comedy about talent agent and team\n- **Los Hombres de Paco** - Hulu/Amazon Prime: Spanish sitcom about police officers\n- **El Ministerio del Tiempo** - Hulu/Amazon Prime: Fantasy comedy about time travelers\n\n**Telenovelas**\n- **Yo Soy Betty, la Fea** - Hulu/Amazon Prime: Colombian telenovela about woman in fashion company\n- **La Usurpadora** - Hulu/Amazon Prime: Mexican telenovela about identical twin sisters\n\n**Documentaries**\n- **¿Cómo andamos?** - Netflix: Explores different aspects of Spanish culture and society\n- **Planeta Futuro** - Netflix: Showcases innovative projects and entrepreneurs\n\nTips: Adjust audio settings for English subtitles, start with shows that interest you, gradually progress to watching without subtitles.
