Decided to purchase a snake plant to decorate apartment. The room has limited natural light, which is a key reason for choosing snake plant—it thrives in low-light conditions and is very low-maintenance. Snake plant care: water sparingly (only when top 1-2 inches of soil are dry), keep 6-8 inches away from walls for air circulation, fertilize with balanced fertilizer during growing season (spring/summer).
