Recommended accommodations in Flagstaff, Arizona for Grand Canyon road trip:

**Hotels/Inns:**
- **Little America Flagstaff** - Mid-range, outdoor pool, free breakfast
- **Hotel Monte Vista** - Historic hotel with rich history, comfortable rooms, lively atmosphere
- **Flagstaff Inn and Suites** - Budget-friendly, clean rooms, convenient location
- **The Inn at Flagstaff** - Cozy family-run B&B, warm atmosphere, homemade breakfast

**Camping:**
- **Flagstaff KOA** - Good for tent/RV camping
- Various nearby national forest campsites

Book well in advance for peak winter holiday season.
