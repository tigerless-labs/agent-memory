Grand Canyon National Park campgrounds with electric hookups available for RV camping:

1. **Mather Campground** - Most popular, South Rim location
   - 327 campsites with electric hookups
   - Amenities: flush toilets, showers, laundry facilities
   - Reservations through recreation.gov

2. **Trailer Village RV Park** - South Rim location
   - 84 sites with electric hookups
   - Amenities: Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, camp store
   - Reservations through grandcanyonlodges.com

3. **Desert View Campground** - South Rim location
   - 50 campsites with electric hookups
   - Amenities: flush toilets, potable water
   - Reservations through recreation.gov

**Important:** Book well in advance as these fill up quickly during peak season, especially winter holidays. Alternative: dispersed camping outside park boundaries has no hookups but offers more rustic experience.
