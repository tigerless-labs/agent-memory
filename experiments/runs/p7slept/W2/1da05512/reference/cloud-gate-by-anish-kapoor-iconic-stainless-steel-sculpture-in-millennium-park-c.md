**Cloud Gate** by Anish Kapoor\n\n- Location: Millennium Park, Chicago, Illinois\n- Installed: 2004\n- Also known as: "The Bean"\n- Material: 168 stainless steel panels\n- Weight: Over 110 tons\n- Dimensions: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long\n- Surface: Mirror-like polished stainless steel\n- Key features:\n  - Highly reflective, distorts and warps surrounding environment\n  - Interactive piece; visitors can walk around, touch, and photograph\n  - Concave shape creates intimate reflection experience\n  - Daily surface cleaning maintains mirror-like finish\n- Symbolism: Connection to sky and clouds; Chicago's vibrant energy\n- Cultural impact: Originally temporary, became permanent icon; most photographed Chicago landmark (~10,000 photos daily)\n- Design philosophy: Explores relationship between individual and urban environment
