Recommended restaurants in Flagstaff, Arizona for road trip to Grand Canyon:

- **Late for the Train Cafe** - Flagstaff institution, breakfast and lunch, vegan and gluten-free options, signature Train Wrecks breakfast burritos
- **Proper Meats + Provisions** - Gourmet deli and meat market, sandwiches, salads, charcuterie boards, good for picnic supplies
- **Fat Olives** - Italian cuisine, pasta, pizzas, risottos, cozy atmosphere
- **The Toasted Owl** - Brunch spot, creative dishes including maple bacon waffles and breakfast tacos
- **Big Foot BBQ** - BBQ restaurant, tender meats, generous portions

All locations in Flagstaff. Book in advance during peak winter holiday season.
