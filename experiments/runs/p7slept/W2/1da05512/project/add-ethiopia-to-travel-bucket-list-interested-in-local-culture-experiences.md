After discussion about off-the-beaten-path African destinations, decided to add Ethiopia to travel bucket list. Drawn to Ethiopia specifically for:
- Rich cultural heritage spanning 3000+ years
- Unique traditions: Ethiopian Orthodox Church, coffee ceremonies
- Friendly and hospitable locals
- Ancient historical sites (Lalibela, Axum)
- Markets and authentic cuisine in Addis Ababa

Key Ethiopian experiences to pursue:
- **Festivals:** Timkat (Jan 19), Meskel (Sept 27), Enkutatash (Sept 11/Ethiopian New Year), Fasika (April/Orthodox Easter)
- **Cuisine:** Injera (flatbread staple), Doro Wot (spicy chicken), Tibs (sautéed meat), Kitfo (raw beef), Shiro (chickpea stew), traditional coffee ceremony
- **Cultural immersion:** Visit local markets, experience authentic food, meet locals at festivals
