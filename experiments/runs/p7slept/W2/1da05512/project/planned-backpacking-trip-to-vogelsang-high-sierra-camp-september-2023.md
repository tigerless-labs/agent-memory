Route Details:
- Route: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp  
- Distance: 20 miles
- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Elevation: 10,300 feet

Camp Amenities:
- Tent cabins (4-person with bunk beds, mattresses, pillows)
- Full-service dining (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Modern restrooms with flush toilets and showers
- Potable water access
- Camp store for snacks and supplies
- Solar-powered lighting in cabins

Access:
- 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows OR
- Shuttle bus from Tuolumne Meadows Visitor Center

Key Considerations:
- Permits required (apply through Yosemite Conservancy)
- Reservations book up quickly - apply early
- Pack layers for unpredictable high-elevation weather
- Bring own sleeping bag (cabins provide mattress/pillows)
- Possible thunderstorms, snow, high winds in September
- May encounter snow/ice on passes in early September

Decision Context:
User initially considered High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft gain) but reassessed ability level. Vogelsang route chosen as better introduction to high-elevation backpacking while offering stunning scenery.
