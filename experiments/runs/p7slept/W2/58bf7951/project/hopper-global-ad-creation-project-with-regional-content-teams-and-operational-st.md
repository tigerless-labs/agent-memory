**Client:** Hopper

**Challenge:** Creating ads on a global level with three independently managed regions (APAC, LatAM + Canada, Europe). Main issues: casting creators globally and establishing operational processes.

**Goal:** Identify a process through which high-quality content can be created for all regions.

**Team & Stakeholders:**
- **Jes** | Head of ops - Owns the process + ClickUp instance; ensures delivery on time with right quality standards
- **Etienne** | Creative strategist - Researches and scopes ad concepts; creates briefs; defines discovery criteria
- **Ali** | Paid Media Director - Responsible for account growth; ensures content aligns with targeting strategies
- **Daniel** | Content Production Manager - Handles voiceover and content operations
- **Kat** | Creator Community Manager & Senior Project Manager (Europe) - Manages Europe region
- **Flor** | Senior Project Manager (LatAM + Canada) - Manages LatAm and Canada regions

**Key Focus Areas:**
1. Global creator casting strategy
2. Operational process structure for content creation
3. Regional coordination and collaboration tools
4. Timeline and milestone definition
