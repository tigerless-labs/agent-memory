Font recommendations that convey luxury and modern style:

1. **Avenir** – clean and elegant sans-serif by Adrian Frutiger; versatile and timeless for luxury branding
2. **Futura** – geometric sans-serif by Paul Renner; crisp lines and minimalist aesthetic, popular for upscale brands
3. **Didot** – classic serif by Firmin Didot; high contrast strokes and refined elegance for luxury and fashion
4. **Gotham** – contemporary sans-serif by Tobias Frere-Jones; geometric simplicity with wide range of weights
5. **Proxima Nova** – modern sans-serif by Mark Simonson; clean lines and geometric shapes for luxury design
