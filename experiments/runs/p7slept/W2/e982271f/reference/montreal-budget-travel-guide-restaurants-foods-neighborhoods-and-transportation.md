## Affordable Restaurants
- La Banquise — 24-hour diner, known for poutine (fries, cheese curds, gravy)
- Schwartz's Deli — smoked meat sandwiches at reasonable prices
- St-Viateur Bagel — Montreal-style bagels, boiled in honey-sweetened water, wood-fired oven
- Fairmount Bagel — alternative Montreal bagel spot
- Olive & Gourmando — bakery café with sandwiches, salads, pastries
- Pizzeria Gema — affordable Neapolitan-style pizza
- Patati Patata — casual diner with burgers and fries

## Must-Try Foods
- Montreal Bagels — smaller and denser than traditional bagels
- Smoked Meat — cured in spices, hot smoked, hand-sliced; try at Schwartz's, The Main, Lester's Deli
- Poutine — fries, cheese curds, gravy
- Tourtière — traditional French-Canadian meat pie with pork and beef
- Maple syrup — try at cabane à sucre (sugar shacks outside city)
- Montreal-style steak seasoning — garlic, salt, pepper blend

## Neighborhoods to Explore
- Plateau-Mont-Royal — colorful houses, hip cafes, vibrant arts scene
- Mile End — cafes, boutiques, bakeries, music venues, galleries
- Old Montreal — historic cobblestone streets, old buildings, churches, museums
- Little Italy — Italian restaurants, cafes, specialty shops, Italian Week festival
- Saint-Henri — stylish restaurants, cafes, bars, Atwater Market
- Quartier des Spectacles — cultural heart with theaters, music venues, year-round festivals

## Transportation Options
- Metro — excellent and cheap public transit system
- Bixi — bike-sharing system throughout city
- Walking — city is very walkable with attractions close together
- Car2Go/Communauto — car-sharing for short-term rentals

## Budget Activities
- Mount Royal Park — hiking trails, picnic areas, city views
- Markets — Jean-Talon Market, Atwater Market, Lachine Market
- Museums with reduced admission days — Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, Museum of Contemporary Art, Pointe-à-Callière Museum
- Street art tours — self-guided walking tours in Plateau and Mile End
- Free festivals — Montreal Jazz Festival, Just for Laughs, Montreal Fireworks Festival (some free programming)
