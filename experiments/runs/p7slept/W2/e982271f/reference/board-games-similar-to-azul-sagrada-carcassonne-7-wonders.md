Games with similar accessibility and strategic depth to Azul:\n\n**Sagrada** — Tile-laying game with dice-drafting mechanic. Build a stained-glass window by drafting and placing dice.\n\n**Carcassonne** — Classic tile-laying game where players build a medieval landscape with roads, cities, and fields.\n\n**7 Wonders** — Card-drafting game where you build a civilization by collecting cards representing structures, technologies, and military units.\n\nOther party/word games: **Taboo** (describe words without taboo words), **Dixit** (guess cards based on clues), **Quelf** (answer bizarre questions creatively), **Wits & Wagers** (bet on trivia answers), **Bananagrams** (fast-paced word game with letter tiles).
