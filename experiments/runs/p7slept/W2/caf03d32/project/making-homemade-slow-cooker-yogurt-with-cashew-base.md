Technique for making yogurt: Soak 1 cup cashews for 4+ hours, blend with 1/2 cup water until smooth, strain through cheesecloth/nut milk bag/fine-mesh sieve. Heat cashew milk to 180-185F to pasteurize, cool to 110-120F, add vegan yogurt starter, incubate 8-12 hours covered with towel. Adjust cashew-to-water ratio for thickness. Can flavor with vanilla, cinnamon, fruit, nutmeg, ginger, lemon, cocoa. Sweeteners: honey, maple syrup, coconut sugar, date syrup, monk fruit, stevia. Add-ins: granola, nuts, seeds, cocoa nibs, dried fruit.
