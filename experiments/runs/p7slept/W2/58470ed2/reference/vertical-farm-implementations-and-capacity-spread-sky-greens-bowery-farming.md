Large-scale vertical farm implementations:

Spread (Tokyo, Japan): World's largest vertical farm; produces 10,000 heads of lettuce per day in 25,000 sq ft facility

Sky Greens (Singapore): 9-meter tall vertical farm; produces 1 ton vegetables per day

Bowery Farming (New York City): 50,000 sq ft facility using LED lights and hydroponic technology for leafy greens and herbs year-round; uses 95% less water than traditional farming

Other cities: Amsterdam, South Korea, London have large-scale implementations

Key insights: Vertical farms best for urban areas with limited arable land and harsh climates. Won't completely replace traditional farming. Some crops don't grow well hydroponically. Best as complement to traditional methods.
