Resources for learning Afro-Cuban rhythms and Latin dance:

**YouTube Channels:**
- Salsa with Silvia — tutorials, lessons, dance challenges
- Afro-Cuban Dance Academy — lessons on rumba, son, Afro-Cuban folkloric dance
- Cuban Salsa Academy — lessons on Cuban salsa, rumba, Afro-Cuban rhythms
- DanceWithMe — lessons on salsa, bachata, Afro-Cuban rhythms
- Joanna's Dance Studio — lessons on salsa, bachata, Afro-Cuban rhythms

**Online Resources:**
- MasterClass — online classes by world-renowned instructors
- DanceClass — platform with classes and tutorials
- SalsaAddiction — community and resource for salsa dancers
- Afro-Cuban Rhythms — lessons, tutorials, music, dance, and culture
- Latin Dance Academy — classes on salsa, bachata, Afro-Cuban rhythms

**Apps:**
- Just Dance Now — dance lessons on various styles
- Dance! — dance classes on various styles

Recommended for home practice to improve skills before attending dance festivals.
