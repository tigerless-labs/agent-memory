For acne: salicylic acid (beta-hydroxy acid) helps unclog pores and prevent acne.

For fine lines: glycolic acid or lactic acid (alpha-hydroxy acids) minimize fine line appearance and improve skin texture.

Avoid: physical exfoliants with nut shells (cause micro-tears, exacerbate acne and fine lines). Avoid over-exfoliation (causes irritation and damage).

Best practices: patch test new products, choose gentle non-abrasive formulations, consult dermatologist for personalized recommendations.
