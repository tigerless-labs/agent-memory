Interested in getting involved in empowerment programs and social justice work in the community. Possible avenues include: (1) Research community organizations doing empowerment and social justice work, (2) Volunteer time or expertise (program design, marketing, fundraising, mentoring), (3) Attend local events and rallies to connect with like-minded people and learn about local programs, (4) Make financial donations to support programs, (5) Advocate for policy changes through contacting elected representatives, attending public meetings, and participating in advocacy campaigns.
