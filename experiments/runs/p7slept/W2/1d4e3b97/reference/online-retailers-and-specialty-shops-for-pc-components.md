Major retailers: Amazon.com, Newegg.com, BestBuy.com, Microcenter.com (physical + online pickup), B&Hphotovideo.com.

Specialty/niche shops: PCPartPicker.com (price comparison, availability tracking), Superbiiz.com (hard-to-find and discontinued items), Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales (community deals), Performance-PCs.com (water cooling, modding parts), FrozenCPU.com (modding parts and custom builds).

Recommendation: Compare prices, check customer reviews, verify return policy and warranty before purchase. Niche shops may have limited availability, higher prices, or longer delivery times.
