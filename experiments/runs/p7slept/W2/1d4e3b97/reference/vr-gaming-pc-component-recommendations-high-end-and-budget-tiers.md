High-End: RTX 3080/3070/3060Ti or RX 6800XT/6800 GPU; Ryzen 9 5900X or i7-10700K/11700K CPU; 16GB+ DDR4 RAM; ASUS ROG B550-F or MSI MPG B550 or Gigabyte AORUS motherboard; Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 SSD; 750W+ PSU (EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X).

Budget-Friendly: GTX 1660 Super or RX 580 GPU; Ryzen 5 3600 or i5-10600K CPU; 8GB DDR4 RAM; MSI B450M-A PRO MAX or ASRock B450M-HDV or Gigabyte GA-A320M-H motherboard; 500GB SSD (WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D); 500-650W PSU (EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W).

Note: From 2024-02-20; components may be outdated.
