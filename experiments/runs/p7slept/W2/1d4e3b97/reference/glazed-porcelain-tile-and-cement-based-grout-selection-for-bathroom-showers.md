**Glazed Porcelain Tile Benefits:**
Glossy finish with design options, durable glaze protection, scratch and stain resistant, highly water resistant, easy to clean and maintain, affordable.

**Cement-Based Grout Choice:**
Most common all-purpose type, made from cement/sand/water, suitable for glazed porcelain, relatively affordable, requires sealing.

**Application Technique:**
Mix to toothpaste consistency, apply with float at 45-degree angle, work in 4 sq ft sections, clean continuously, allow 10-15 min set time before rinsing, apply sealer after curing.

**Alternatives Considered:**
Epoxy grout (durable but expensive), unglazed tile (slip resistant but high maintenance).
