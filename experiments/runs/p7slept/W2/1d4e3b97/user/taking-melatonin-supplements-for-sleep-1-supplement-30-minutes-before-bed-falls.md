Started taking melatonin supplements approximately 2 weeks ago to improve sleep.

- **Dosage**: 1 melatonin supplement
- **Timing**: Takes it 30 minutes before bed
- **Effect**: Usually falls asleep within 15-20 minutes of lying down
- **Assessment**: Melatonin has really helped with sleep quality

This is in addition to the bedtime routine (reading, calming sounds, white noise machine).
