## Simple Grilled Chicken Breast\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts\n- 2 tbsp olive oil\n- 1 tsp salt\n- 1 tsp black pepper\n- 1 tsp lemon zest (optional)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400°F / 200°C)\n2. Mix together olive oil, salt, and black pepper\n3. Brush mixture evenly onto both sides of chicken breasts\n4. Sprinkle lemon zest over both sides if using\n5. Grill for 5-6 minutes per side until internal temp reaches 165°F (74°C)\n6. Let rest for 5 minutes before slicing\n\nKeep simple seasoning to avoid overpowering lighter meals and herbal tea pairings. Do not overcook or chicken will dry out.
