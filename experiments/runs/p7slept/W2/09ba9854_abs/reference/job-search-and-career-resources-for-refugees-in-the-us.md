## Key Organizations

**Refugee Career Hub** (www.refugeecareerhub.org)
- Free online platform connecting refugees with job opportunities
- Offers career coaching and skill-building resources
- Provides resume review and job readiness training
- Registration: Sign up with personal info and complete profile with work experience, education, skills, language proficiency

**Upwardly Global** (www.upwardlyglobal.org)
- Nonprofit providing job search assistance, career coaching, and skills training
- Services: resume/cover letter review, interview prep, job matching, career coaching, online courses, networking events
- Online courses in IT (CompTIA, Cisco, Microsoft), Healthcare (medical billing, assistant), Finance, Business, Digital Marketing, English/Soft Skills
- Offers job fairs and networking events throughout the year
- Financial assistance and scholarships available
- Eligibility: authorized to work, HS diploma/equivalent, actively seeking employment

**Other Organizations**
- International Rescue Committee (IRC)
- Catholic Charities
- Lutheran Social Services
- SCORE (free business/career coaching)
- MicroMentor (free career coaching)
