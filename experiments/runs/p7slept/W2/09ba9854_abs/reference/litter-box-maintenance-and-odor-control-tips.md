Daily: Scoop solid waste and clumps daily. Weekly: Change litter completely every 7-10 days. Wash litter box with mild detergent and warm water every 1-2 weeks, rinse thoroughly. Odor control: Use high-quality odor-control litter, sprinkle thin layer of baking soda at bottom, add baking soda to litter itself. Disinfect with 1:9 white vinegar to water solution, let sit 10-15 minutes. Environment: Place in well-ventilated area away from food/water bowls and high-humidity areas.
