Recommended apps for checking weather forecasts before hiking:\n- **Dark Sky**: Highly accurate, minute-by-minute forecasts for exact location; especially useful for predicting when/where precipitation will occur\n- **Mountain Weather Forecast**: Specifically designed for mountain weather, includes snowfall, avalanche risk, wind conditions\n\nUser was interested in checking these out for planning 5-7 mile hikes.
