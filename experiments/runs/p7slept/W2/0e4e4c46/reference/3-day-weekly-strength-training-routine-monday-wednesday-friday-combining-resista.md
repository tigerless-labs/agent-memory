## Weekly Schedule

**Monday:**
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes brisk walking/jogging/cycling
- Resistance: 3 sets of 10-12 reps — squats, lunges, push-ups, rows, bicep curls
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes interval training (treadmill or stationary bike, alternating high/moderate intensity)

**Wednesday:**
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes jumping jacks, high knees, mountain climbers
- Resistance: 3 sets of 10-12 reps — deadlifts, bench press, pull-ups, tricep extensions, lateral raises
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes steady-state cardio (elliptical or stair climber)

**Friday:**
- Warm-up: 5-10 minutes dynamic stretching and mobility
- Resistance: 3 sets of 10-12 reps — step-ups, leg press, dumbbell chest fly, lat pulldowns, shoulder press
- Cardio: 20-30 minutes circuit training (alternating resistance and cardio exercises)

**Recovery:** Rest at least one day between workouts; rest days are essential for muscle repair and avoiding overtraining/injury.
