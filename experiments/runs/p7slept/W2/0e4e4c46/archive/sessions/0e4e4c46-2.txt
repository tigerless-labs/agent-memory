2. **Cut them correctly**: Cut the potatoes into thin wedges or thin slices (about 1/4 inch thick). This will help them cook evenly and get crispy.
3. **Soak the potatoes**: Soak the cut potatoes in cold water for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch. After soaking, drain and pat them dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture.
4. **Use the right oil**: Use a high-heat oil like avocado oil or grapeseed oil, which can handle high temperatures without burning or smoking.
5. **High heat and hot pan**: Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Heat a baking sheet or roasting pan in the oven for 5-7 minutes. Remove the hot pan from the oven and carefully add the potatoes in a single layer. This will help the potatoes sear and crisp up quickly.
6. **Don't overcrowd**: Make sure to leave some space between the potato slices or wedges. Overcrowding can lead to steaming instead of roasting, resulting in soft potatoes.
7. **Flip and rotate**: After 20-25 minutes, flip and rotate the potatoes to ensure even cooking and crisping.
8. **Check for crispiness**: Check the potatoes after 30-35 minutes. If they're not crispy enough, continue to roast in 5-10 minute increments until they reach your desired level of crispiness.
