Stayed at Hilton Los Angeles Downtown during a friend's birthday celebration. The staff were exceptionally friendly and helpful. Received a complimentary suite upgrade due to the special occasion. The room had stunning city skyline views from the upper floor. Upon Friday afternoon check-in, received a complimentary welcome drink at the lobby bar. Appreciated the personalized service touches and attention to detail. Overall positive experience that made the weekend memorable.
