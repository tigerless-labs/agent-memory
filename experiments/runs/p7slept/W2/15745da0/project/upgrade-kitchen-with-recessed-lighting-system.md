## Design decisions
- Trim finish: satin nickel (to match existing kitchen hardware)
- Aesthetic: modern kitchen design
- Lighting type: recessed lighting with slim/low-profile or baffle trims

## Technical specifications
- Color temperature: 5000K-5500K (daylight white) preferred, or 4000K-4500K (cool white)
- Lumen output: 800-1000 lumens (1000-1200 if larger kitchen)
- CRI: 80+ required for accurate color rendering
- Dimming: must have dimming capability
- Smart compatibility: considering Philips Hue integration (already using in living room)

## Optional features to consider
- Motion sensors for convenience and energy efficiency
- Timers for scheduled lighting scenes

## Brands researched
- Halo, Juno, Tech Lighting, WAC Lighting, LBL Lighting (recessed fixtures)
- Philips Hue, LIFX, GE Smart Bulbs, Sylvania Smart+, Cree (smart bulbs)
- Lutron, Leviton, Belkin (motion sensors/timers)
