## Popular wood chips for smoking pork shoulder:\n\n1. **Hickory** - Strong, sweet, savory flavor with notes of bacon and vanilla. Classic choice.\n2. **Apple Wood** - Fruity, sweet, mild flavor. Complements richness of pork.\n3. **Cherry Wood** - Mild, fruity flavor with hints of tartness. Slightly sweeter profile.\n4. **Pecan Wood** - Rich, nutty, slightly sweet. Adds depth and complexity.\n5. **Post Oak** - Strong, smoky flavor with vanilla and caramel notes. Texas-style BBQ favorite.\n\n## Tips:\n- Soak chips in water for 30+ minutes before smoking to prevent flare-ups\n- Mix different chips to create unique flavor combinations\n- Monitor temperature and adjust chip amount for consistent smoke\n- Choose intensity: Hickory/Post Oak for strong flavor, Apple/Cherry for milder\n\n## Success note:\nApplewood chips worked amazingly with slow-cooked pork shoulder on grill.
