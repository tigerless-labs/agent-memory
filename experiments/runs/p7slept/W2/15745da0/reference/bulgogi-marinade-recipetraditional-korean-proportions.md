Traditional bulgogi marinade: 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 2 tablespoons sugar, 2 cloves garlic (minced and sautéed), 1 tablespoon grated ginger (sautéed), 1 tablespoon sesame oil, 1 teaspoon Korean chili flakes (gochugaru), 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar (optional). Key tips: balance of sweet and savory; sauté garlic and ginger first to mellow flavors; sesame oil should be used sparingly due to strength; rice vinegar adds brightness; experiment with ratios to find preferred balance.
