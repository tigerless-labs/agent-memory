On busy days, prioritizes shorter, manageable yoga sessions rather than full-length practices. Strategy: start with 10-15 minute practices, schedule yoga at the same time daily, use online resources (YouTube, YogaGlo, Down Dog, Headspace, Yoga Studio apps) for flexibility, focus on breathwork and meditation when short on time, attach yoga to existing habits (after waking, before bed), be flexible with missing days and focus on getting back on track, celebrate small wins. Treats yoga as non-negotiable daily priority like brushing teeth or showering.
