User is committed to creating safe, welcoming environments for LGBTQ+ individuals, with specific focus on gym settings. Interested in implementing:
- Gender-neutral facilities (restrooms, locker rooms, private showers)
- Inclusive policies and non-discrimination commitments
- LGBTQ+-focused fitness classes and workshops
- Inclusive language and communication practices
- Staff training on LGBTQ+ issues and cultural competency
- Partnerships with local LGBTQ+ organizations

Key priorities: using inclusive language, respecting pronouns, avoiding gendered language, providing accessible resources, and fostering community belonging.
