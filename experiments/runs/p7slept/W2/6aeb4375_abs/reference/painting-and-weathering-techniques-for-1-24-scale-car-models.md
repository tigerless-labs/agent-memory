## Painting techniques:\n\n**Paint types:** Acrylic or enamel paints designed for models (Tamiya, Vallejo, Testors)\n\n**Process:**\n1. Prime with gray or white primer for uniform base\n2. Base coat: high-gloss paint (e.g., Tamiya TS-14 "Candy Apple Red" for '69 Camaro)\n3. Masking: use masking tape or film for sharp lines on stripes, emblems, trim\n4. Clear coat: protect base and create glossy finish\n\n## Weathering techniques:\n\n**General approach:** Build up effects in layers, subtlety is key (avoid over-weathering)\n\n**Specific effects:**\n- Dust and dirt: use washes or pigment/thinner mix on wheels, tires, lower panels\n- Tire wear: graphite pencil or dark gray marker on sidewalls\n- Chips and scratches: small brush with lighter shade of base color\n- Rust and corrosion: rust-colored pigments on undercarriage, wheel wells, hinges\n- Glass and chrome: silver/chrome marker for trim, clear acrylic for glass\n\n## Practice tips:\n- Always test on a test piece first\n- Use magnifying glass for detail work\n- Reference real cars for authenticity\n- Work in layers, starting subtle\n- Less is more
