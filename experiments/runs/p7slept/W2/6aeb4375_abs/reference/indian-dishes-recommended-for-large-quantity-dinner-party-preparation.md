Popular Indian dishes suitable for cooking in large quantities:
- Chana Masala (chickpeas in tomato sauce)
- Palak Paneer (spinach curry with paneer cheese)
- Samosas (fried pastries with spiced potato filling)
- Tandoori Chicken (marinated chicken, oven-cooked)
- Vegetable Biryani (rice dish with vegetables and spices)
- Rogan Josh (lamb in spicy sauce)
- Sag Aloo (mustard greens with potatoes)

Preparation tips for large quantities:
- Prepare sauces and spice blends in advance
- Use large pots and pans or slow cooker/Instant Pot
- Make extra rice and naan bread
- Pair with basmati rice or roti
