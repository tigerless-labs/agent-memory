Researching ukulele purchase options. Discussed various models:

**Concert size (preferred initially):**
- Kala KA-C Mahogany Concert: $100-$150
- Cordoba 20CM Concert: $100-$150

**Tenor size (later consideration):**
- Lanikai LU-21T Tenor: $100-$150
- Cordoba 20TM Tenor: $150-$200 (heard great things about this one)

**Other models researched:**
- Kala KA-15S Soprano: $70-$100
- Lanikai LU-21 Soprano: $70-$100
- Fender Seaside Soprano: $50-$80
- Fender Montecito Tenor: $150-$200
- Mahalo MR1 Soprano: $30-$50
- Mahalo MR3 Concert: $50-$80

**Key learnings:**
- Prefers warmer, balanced sound (leaning toward mahogany over koa)
- Considering case options for protection (hardcase vs softcase)
- Initially interested in concert size, later considering tenor for fuller sound and spacious feel
- Most recent focus: Cordoba 20TM Tenor as top contender
