Popular datasets for training deep learning models in medical image segmentation:

**Brain Segmentation**
- BRATS (Brain Tumor Segmentation Challenge): 750 MRI scans with T1, T2, FLAIR, T1ce modalities
- MRBrainS: 5 MRI scans with manual segmentations of brain structures

**Cardiac Segmentation**
- ACDC (Automatic Cardiac Diagnosis Challenge): 150 cardiac MRI scans
- LVSC (Left Ventricle Segmentation Challenge): 30 cardiac MRI scans

**Lung Segmentation**
- LUNA (Lung Nodule Analysis): 888 CT scans
- LOLA11 (Lung CT Segmentation Challenge): 55 CT scans

**Abdominal Segmentation**
- CHAOS (Computational Harmonization of Abdominal Organs Segmentation): 40 abdominal CT scans
- BTCV (Beyond the Cranial Vault): 50 abdominal CT scans

**General/Multi-Task**
- MedMNIST: 12 medical image segmentation tasks
- MSD (Medical Segmentation Decathlon): 10 medical image segmentation tasks

**Challenge Platforms**
- ISBI (International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging) Challenge
- MICCAI (Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention) Grand Challenge
