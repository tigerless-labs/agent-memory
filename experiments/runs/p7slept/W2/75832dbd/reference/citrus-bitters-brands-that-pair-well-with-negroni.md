## Citrus-Forward Bitters for Negroni

- **Regans' Orange Bitters No. 6** — classic choice; blend of orange, lemon, grapefruit peels; subtle, warm citrus flavor
- **The Bitter Truth Orange Bitters** — German-made; vibrant, citrusy; made with orange, lemon, grapefruit peels
- **Fee Brothers Orange Bitters** — well-respected brand; slightly sweeter, more citrus-forward than some alternatives
- **Peychaud's Orange Bitters** — New Orleans-based (since 1800s); unique citrusy and slightly sweet profile with hints of spice and herbs
- **Scrappy's Bitters Orange Bitters** — craft brand; high-quality, small-batch; bold, citrusy flavor from orange, lemon, grapefruit peels

**Usage:** Start with 2-3 dashes in a Negroni; adjust to taste. Citrus bitters add brightness and depth when paired with citrus-forward gins.
