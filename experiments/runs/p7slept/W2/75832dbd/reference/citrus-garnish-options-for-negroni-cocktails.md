## Citrus Peels and Twists for Negroni Garnish

- **Orange peel** — classic pairing with Campari and sweet vermouth; express oils over drink
- **Blood orange peel** — deeper, more complex flavor; adds subtle sweetness and color
- **Grapefruit twist** — pairs well with Campari's bitterness and gin's botanicals; use pink or red for visual appeal
- **Lemon twist** — bright, citrusy note; look for thick, oily skin for maximum flavor
- **Yuzu peel** — Japanese citrus; unique tart and sweet profile; complements herbal gin notes and Campari bitterness
- **Meyer lemon twist** — sweeter, less acidic than regular lemon; adds smooth, citrusy note

**Technique:**
1. Express oils from peel by gently squeezing or using a citrus press
2. Rub peel around rim of glass to coat with citrus oils
3. Place peel or twist in glass on top of ice or as garnish
4. Can combine different citrus peels for unique flavor profile
