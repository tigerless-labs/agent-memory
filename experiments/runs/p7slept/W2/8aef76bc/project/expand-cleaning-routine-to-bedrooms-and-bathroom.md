## Goal\nExtend cleaning and organization routine to bedrooms and bathroom after getting living room and dining room under control.\n\n## Bedroom Organization Tips\n- Make bed every morning\n- Designate a place for everything (specific drawers, shelves)\n- Use storage containers with labels\n- Organize clothing with closet organizers or dividers by type/season\n- Put away items after use immediately\n- Schedule 10-15 minute weekly tidying sessions\n- Implement 'one in, one out' policy for new items\n- Keep surfaces (dresser, nightstand, desk) clear\n- Regularly vacuum/sweep floors\n- Establish 'no shoes in bedroom' policy\n\n## Bathroom Tips\n- Clean sink and mirror daily\n- Wipe toilet seat and handle regularly\n- Use caddy or basket for toiletries\n- Clean shower and tub regularly\n- Clean high-touch areas (light switches, doorknobs)
