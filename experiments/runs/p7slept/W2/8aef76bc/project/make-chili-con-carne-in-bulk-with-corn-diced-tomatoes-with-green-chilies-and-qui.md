Plan to make a large batch of chili con carne for weekly meal prep (reheating at work). Specific ingredients to add:\n- Ground beef or turkey (browned with aromatics: onions, garlic, bell peppers)\n- Beans (kidney beans, black beans, or pinto beans)\n- Corn (frozen, thawed and dried, or canned/drained)\n- Diced tomatoes with green chilies\n- Quinoa or brown rice (cooked separately, then added)\n- Spices: chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, vinegar for acidity\n\nTips:\n- Brown meat well for caramelization\n- Simmer at least 1 hour for flavors to meld\n- Portion into individual containers\n- Can freeze up to 3 months if not eating within 3-4 days\n- Reheat in microwave or stovetop\n- Optional toppings: shredded cheese, sour cream, diced onions, avocado
