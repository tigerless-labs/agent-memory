## Expense Tracking Apps\n- **Mint**: Personal finance app with spending tracking and budgeting alerts\n- **YNAB (You Need a Budget)**: Detailed budgeting with automated savings features\n- **Personal Capital**: Income/expense tracking with investment tools\n\n## Grocery-Specific Tracking\n- **Grocery IQ**: Shopping lists and price tracking\n- **Out of Milk**: Shopping lists and sale alerts\n- **Flipp**: Aggregates weekly store ads, price comparison\n- **AnyList**: Lists, recipes, price tracking\n- **ShopSavvy**: Barcode scanning and price comparison\n\n## General Budgeting with Grocery Features\n- **Spendee**: Expense tracking with category features\n- **Wally**: Digital receipt tracking\n- **Toshl Finance**: Comprehensive expense tracking\n\n## Meal Planning Tools\n- **Plan to Eat**: Meal planning with grocery list integration\n- **Yummly**: Recipe discovery and meal planning\n- **Mealime**: Simplified meal planning and prep guidance\n\n## Spreadsheet Approach\n- Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel with custom templates\n- Columns: Date, Category, Item, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Cost, Running Total\n- Useful for hands-on control and custom categorization
