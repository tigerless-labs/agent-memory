Four design concepts for tabbards based on the American flag, ready for Midjourney image generation.

**1. Classic Design** — Simple tunic with short sleeves. American flag front (red/white stripes bottom half, blue field with white stars top half). Painted or embroidered.

**2. Heraldic Design** — Simple tunic with short sleeves, divided into four quadrants. Each quadrant features different American symbols: eagle, liberty bell, Statue of Liberty. Painted or embroidered.

**3. Medieval-Inspired Design** — Tunic with long sleeves, V-shaped neckline, diagonal split down center. Features American flag with medieval symbols (bald eagle, shield, banner with motto). Painted or embroidered.

**4. Modern Design** — Simple tunic with short sleeves. Abstract geometric approach with bold geometric shapes and colors (red, white, blue) in contemporary design style.

All designs include sleeves (added in revision). Prompts are ready for Midjourney image generation.
