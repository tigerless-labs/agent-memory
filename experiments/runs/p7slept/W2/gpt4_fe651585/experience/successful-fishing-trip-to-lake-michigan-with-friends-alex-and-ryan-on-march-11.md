Went fishing at Lake Michigan with friends Alex and Ryan. Used St. Croix Mojo Bass rod with curly tail jig near a drop-off. Caught one large walleye that put up a good fight and three smallmouth bass (two were keepers). Weather was perfect: sunny with light breeze and calm water. After fishing, grilled the caught fish on the shore with friends, who were impressed with the catch. Overall a great experience and already planning next fishing adventure.
