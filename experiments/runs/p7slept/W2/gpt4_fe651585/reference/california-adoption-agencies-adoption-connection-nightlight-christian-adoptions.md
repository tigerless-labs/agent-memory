**California-based agencies to research**:\n\n1. **Adoption Connection** — Emphasizes personalized service and support; offers comprehensive support services for birth mothers including counseling, case management, and advocacy\n\n2. **Nightlight Christian Adoptions** — Faith-based agency with strong emphasis on personalized care and guidance; provides birth mother counseling, support groups, and post-placement support\n\n3. **Family Builders** — Offers comprehensive and individualized approach to adoption; provides birth mothers with counseling, support groups, and resources\n\n**When researching agencies**:\n- Check website, social media, and reviews\n- Ask friends/family/online communities about their experiences\n- Verify licensure with California Department of Social Services (CDSS)\n- Verify accreditation with Council on Accreditation (COA)\n- Schedule consultations with multiple agencies\n- Ask about their approach to home study, communication frequency, and customization\n- Trust your instincts about which agency feels like the right fit
