**Timeline**: The home study process in California typically takes 3-6 months, though this can vary based on case complexity and agency workload.

**Cost**: 1500 to 3000 dollars depending on the agency or social worker, with possible additional fees for travel expenses.

**Home study requirements**:
- Identification and vital records
- Proof of income and employment
- Medical reports
- Criminal background checks (Live Scan and FBI)
- Child abuse clearance (California Department of Justice)
- 2-3 home visits by social worker
- Interviews with household members

**Recommended approach**:
- Choose a licensed agency or social worker licensed by California Department of Social Services (CDSS)
- Verify accreditation with Council on Accreditation (COA)
- Gather all documents beforehand to avoid delays
- Be transparent and open during assessment
