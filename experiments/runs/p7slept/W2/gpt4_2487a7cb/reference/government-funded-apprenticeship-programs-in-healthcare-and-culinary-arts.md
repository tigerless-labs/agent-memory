## Programs Researched (2023-05-24)

### Healthcare Industry Programs:
- **National Apprenticeship Program for Medical Assistants** (U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship) — On-the-job training + classroom instruction in anatomy, medical terminology, clinical skills
- **Health Career Advancement Program** (Health Career Advancement Program Council) — Career advancement for incumbent healthcare workers; available in several states
- **Nursing Assistant Apprenticeship Program** (Wisconsin Department of Workforce Development) — On-the-job training + classroom in patient care, infection control, communication

### Culinary Arts Programs:
- **American Culinary Federation (ACF) Apprenticeship Program** — On-the-job + classroom in baking/pastry, culinary arts, hospitality management
- **Culinary Institute of America (CIA) Apprenticeship Program** — On-the-job + classroom in food prep, kitchen management, culinary entrepreneurship
- **ProStart Apprenticeship Program** (National Restaurant Association Educational Foundation) — High school students; culinary arts + restaurant management experience

### General Programs:
- Registered Apprenticeship Program
- Pre-Apprenticeship Program
- Youth Apprenticeship Program

### Industries with Programs:
Construction, healthcare, IT, manufacturing, automotive, transportation, energy

## Key Findings:
Apprenticeships vs. traditional degrees: hands-on training + earn-while-learning vs. academic breadth + career flexibility. Government-funded benefits: accredited, standardized, lower cost, wider accessibility, support services (career counseling, job placement, mentoring).
