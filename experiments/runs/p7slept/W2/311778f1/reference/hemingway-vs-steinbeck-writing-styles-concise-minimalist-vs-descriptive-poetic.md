## Comparison

**Hemingway**: Concise, direct, minimalist style
- Short sentences, sparse prose, repetition for rhythm
- Example: *The Old Man and the Sea* — uses the fisherman's repeated refrain "I'll kill him" to build intimacy
- Raw, unvarnished approach focusing on surface-level events
- Allows readers to fill gaps with imagination

**Steinbeck**: Descriptive, poetic, figurative style
- Dense, detailed imagery and symbolism
- Example: *The Grapes of Wrath* — vivid depiction of Oklahoman farmers' struggles during the Great Depression
- Polished, refined prose that immerses readers
- Uses dialogue to reveal character and advance plot
- Delves into underlying themes and emotions

Both explore the human condition and common people's struggles with realism, but through opposite stylistic approaches.
