Interested in preparing healthy snacks weekly for portion control and convenience. Focus areas:

- Fresh fruit (cut apples, oranges, berries, grapes in airtight containers)
- Veggie sticks (carrot, celery, cucumber with hummus/guacamole)
- Protein-rich options (hard-boiled eggs, Greek yogurt parfaits, edamame)
- Nuts and seeds (roasted almonds, walnuts, seed mixes)
- Homemade trail mix and granola
- Energy balls with oats, nuts, dried fruits

Goal: Curb unhealthy impulse snacking and stay fueled between meals.
