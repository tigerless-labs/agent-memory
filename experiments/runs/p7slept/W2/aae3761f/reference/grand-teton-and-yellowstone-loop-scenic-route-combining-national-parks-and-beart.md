7-10 day road trip from US East combining Grand Teton, Yellowstone, and Beartooth Highway (US-212, 68 miles, 4 hours). Beartooth Highway fully paved, speeds 45-65 mph, 10000+ ft elevation, summer temps 60s-70s°F. Colter Bay Campground in Grand Teton: 350 sites (100 with 30-amp electric), flush toilets, showers, dump station, books via recreation.gov with 6-month advance window. All parks peak season June-August. Requires bear spray. Gas stations limited on highway.
