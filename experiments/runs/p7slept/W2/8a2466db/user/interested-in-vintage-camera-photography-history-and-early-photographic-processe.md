- Early photographic pioneers: Louis Daguerre, William Henry Fox Talbot
- Early photographic processes: daguerreotypy (1839), calotypy (1841)
- Artistic and aesthetic developments of photography in the 19th and early 20th centuries
- Camera maintenance and repair for vintage equipment
- Technical details of historical cameras and their operation

Prefers hands-on learning through using actual vintage cameras combined with historical research and study.
