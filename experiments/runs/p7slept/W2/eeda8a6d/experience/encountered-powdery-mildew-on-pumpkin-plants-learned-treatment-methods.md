Dealt with powdery mildew infection on pumpkins. Learned multiple treatment approaches:

**Fungicides:** potassium bicarbonate, neem oil, sulfur, or copper-based fungicides

**Non-fungicide methods:**
- Remove infected leaves and plants early to prevent spread
- Improve air circulation through spacing, weed removal, and trellising
- Homemade remedy: 1 tablespoon baking soda + 1 tablespoon vegetable oil + a few drops of dish soap in 1 gallon of water

**Best practices:**
- Avoid spraying when plants are in bloom (protects beneficial insects/bees)
- Always read and follow fungicide label instructions carefully
- Consult gardening experts before choosing treatment approach
