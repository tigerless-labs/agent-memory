Tank decoration plan: Adding treasure chest to create city-layout area with existing artificial coral and rocks. Treasure chest selected over sunken ship for 20-gallon tank size (more compact). Positioning: Place treasure chest near existing coral and rocks to form cohesive city area that encourages fish exploration (especially neon tetras). Design approach: Create visual depth with angled/buried treasure chest, add greenery around structures, vary heights with rocks and coral for different swimming levels, leave open swimming space. Treasure chest benefits: Provides cozy enclosed hiding spaces for small fish. Combined with coral and rocks creates stimulating exploration environment. City layout increases visual interest and provides multiple hiding spots for security.
