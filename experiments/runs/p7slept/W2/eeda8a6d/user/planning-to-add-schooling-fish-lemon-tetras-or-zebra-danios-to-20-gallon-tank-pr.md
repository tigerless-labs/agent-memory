User is considering adding schooling fish to their 20-gallon gourami tank to help distract gouramis and reduce feeding-time aggression.\n\n**Considerations:**\n- Will introduce schooling fish in groups (10-15 individuals)\n- Considering either lemon tetras or zebra danios\n- Plans to provide plenty of hiding places using plants and decorations\n- Aware of gourami compatibility concerns and will monitor tank dynamics\n- Plans to increase water changes to reduce stress and aggression\n\n**Fish research:**\n- Lemon tetras: 72-82°F, pH 6.0-7.0, soft to moderate hardness, need 10+ individuals\n- Zebra danios: 65-75°F, pH 6.5-8.0, soft to hard, need 6-10+ individuals\n- Both species are omnivorous and accept frozen/live foods like brine shrimp and bloodworms
