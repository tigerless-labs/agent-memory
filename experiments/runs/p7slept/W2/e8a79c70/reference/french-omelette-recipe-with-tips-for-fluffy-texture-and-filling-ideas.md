Ingredients: 2-3 eggs, pinch of salt, 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, 1 tablespoon finely chopped herbs (optional). Whisk eggs with salt until frothy. Heat nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, add butter and coat surface. Pour egg mixture in and spread evenly. Cook ~30 seconds until eggs set, then fold edges toward center. Tilt and slide onto plate. Tips: whisk eggs well until frothy, use nonstick skillet, cook at medium-high heat (not too high or low), don't overfill, fold gently without pressing, serve immediately. Filling ideas: cheese (Swiss/cheddar/Gruyere), ham, mushrooms, spinach with onions/garlic, tomatoes, fresh herbs, ham and cheese, spinach and feta, tomato and basil, smoked salmon and dill.
