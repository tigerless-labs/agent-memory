Recommended tools and resources for learning bird song identification:\n\n- **Merlin Bird ID** - Free app by Cornell Lab of Ornithology with Sound ID feature to identify songs\n- **Xeno-canto** - Online archive of 400,000+ bird sound recordings, searchable by species/location\n- **AllAboutBirds** - Cornell Lab of Ornithology website with bird songs, spectrograms, detailed descriptions\n- **eBird Audio Guide** - Free app with audio recordings, photos, and range maps\n- **BirdNote** - Daily radio show and podcast featuring bird sounds and stories\n\nTips: Start with common species, listen repeatedly, pay attention to pitch/rhythm/quality, practice in the field.
