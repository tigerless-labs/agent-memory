Government agencies involved in document verification for the Philippine system:\n\n- **BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue)** - Verifies and approves income tax return documents\n- **Office of Permits and Licenses** - Verifies and approves business permit documents\n\nBoth agencies can be integrated into the approval workflow for the document verification system.
