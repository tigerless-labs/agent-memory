Wants to organize and track loyalty programs using a spreadsheet template. Preferred tracking method combines:
- Spreadsheet (Google Sheets or Excel) with columns for program name, points/rewards type, amount earned, amount needed for reward, expiration date, and notes
- Phone reminders set at specific times or locations to remember to use loyalty apps/cards
- Making loyalty card use a daily habit/routine
- Staying informed about promotions and offers via newsletters, social media, and in-store information

Strategy: Set actionable reminders like 'Use FreshMart loyalty app when shopping' or 'Grab coffee at favorite coffee shop to earn stamps toward free drink'
