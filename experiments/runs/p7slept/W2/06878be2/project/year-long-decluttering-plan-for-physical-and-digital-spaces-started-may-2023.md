Comprehensive 12-month decluttering project covering both physical and digital spaces, with monthly focus areas:

**Months 1-6: Physical spaces**
- Month 1: Plan and set SMART goals
- Month 2: Clothing and personal items
- Month 3: Books, magazines, and papers
- Month 4: Kitchen and dining area
- Month 5: Living room and common areas
- Month 6: Bedroom and bathroom

**Months 7-12: Workspace and digital**
- Month 7: Workspace or home office
- Month 8: Digital files and folders on computer/external storage
- Month 9: Email inbox (unsubscribe, delete, organize)
- Month 10: Social media accounts (unfollow, privacy settings, curation)
- Month 11: Smartphone and other devices (apps, organization, settings)
- Month 12: Review progress and establish maintenance routines

**Methods:**
- Sort through items systematically
- Keep only what's essential, used, or brings joy
- Donate, sell, or recycle unwanted items
- Create organization systems
- Establish habits for maintaining decluttered spaces
