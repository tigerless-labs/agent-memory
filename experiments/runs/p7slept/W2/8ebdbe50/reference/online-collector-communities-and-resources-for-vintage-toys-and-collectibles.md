## Forums & Communities\n- Reddit: r/vintagestarwars, r/toycollectors\n- Facebook groups: Vintage Star Wars Collectors, Toy Collectors United\n- Star Wars Collector's Guild: Comprehensive forum with vintage figure sections\n- Toy Collectors Forum: General forum for appraisal, restoration, etc.\n\n## Specialized Websites\n- Vintage-Action-Figures.com: Vintage action figures including Star Wars, price guides\n- ToyArchive.com: Database of vintage toys with collectible resources\n\n## Social Media\n- Instagram: @vintagestarwars, @toycollector, @collectibletoys\n- Twitter: Hashtags #vintagetoys, #starwarscollectibles, #toyappraisal\n\nUse these to connect with experienced collectors, learn appraisal processes, and verify information across multiple sources.
