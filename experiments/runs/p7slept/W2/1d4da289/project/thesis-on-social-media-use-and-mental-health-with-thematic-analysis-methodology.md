**Thesis Topic**: Social media use and mental health outcomes

**Research Questions**:
- How does social media use affect mental health outcomes in young adults?
- What are the specific social media behaviors associated with negative mental health outcomes?

**Progress**:
- Literature review: Completed February 10, 2023
- Current work: Methodology chapter development
- Data collection planning: In progress

**Methodology Decisions**:
- Qualitative data analysis: Thematic analysis
- Data collection methods: Online surveys (quantitative) + semi-structured interviews (qualitative)
- Target population: Young adults aged 18-25
- Planned sample size: 500 participants

**Software Evaluation in Progress**:
- NVivo (14-day free trial available)
- Atlas.ti (30-day free trial available)
- Purpose: Select tool for qualitative data coding and analysis

**Expected Themes to Explore**:
- Social media use habits (frequency, duration, platform types)
- Mental health experiences (anxiety, depression, self-esteem, body image)
- Social comparison and envy
- Social support and connection
- Self-presentation and identity formation
- FOMO and online anxiety
- Cyberbullying and harassment
- Self-regulation strategies
- Coping mechanisms
