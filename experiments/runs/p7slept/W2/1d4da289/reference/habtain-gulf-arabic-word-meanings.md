## Definitions

**حبتين** (habtain/hubtain) has two main meanings in Gulf Arabic:

1. **Literal:** Two pieces, units, or items of something — commonly used with quantities (two tablets, two candies, etc.)

2. **Affectionate/Slang:** Term of endearment used informally between close friends or family members, similar to calling someone 'dear,' 'buddy,' or 'sweetheart.'

Meaning depends on context of conversation.
