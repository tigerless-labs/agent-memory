## Gramby Roll Technique

A rolling motion escape technique used in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu to escape from the bottom side control position.

### Overview
- Named after Marcelo Garcia, a Brazilian BJJ practitioner and instructor known for effective use of the technique
- Effective for escaping from bottom side control and taking the top position

### Execution
1. Start lying on your back with opponent in side control on top
2. Use hips and legs to create space and leverage
3. Roll your body over onto your side
4. Control opponent's head and posture with arms during the roll
5. Prevent opponent from re-establishing side control

### Requirements
- Good coordination
- Control and timing
- Practice to develop skills and confidence
- Can be challenging against a resisting opponent
