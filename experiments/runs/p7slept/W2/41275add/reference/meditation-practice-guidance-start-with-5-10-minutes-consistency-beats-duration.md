Start meditation with 5 to 10 minutes per session and gradually increase duration over time. Consistency matters more than duration, so regular part-time practice is better than sporadic longer sessions. Even meditating a few times per week provides measurable mental health benefits. The key is finding a type and time of day that works for your schedule and building a sustainable habit.
