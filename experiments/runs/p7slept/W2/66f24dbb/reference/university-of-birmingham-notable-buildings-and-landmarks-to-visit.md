Clock Tower ('Old Joe')

- 100 meters tall — tallest freestanding clock tower in the world
- Neo-Venetian Gothic design with red brick and ornate stonework
- Open to public during certain times; visitors can climb spiral staircase to the top
- Houses museum with exhibits on the clock tower and university history
- Features iconic bell 'Big Joe' — one of only two of its kind in the world

Other Notable Campus Buildings

- Aston Webb Building: Red brick and terracotta exterior, houses administrative offices and lecture theatres
- Barber Institute of Fine Arts: Art gallery with paintings, sculptures, and other artworks
- Winterbourne House and Garden: Historic house with beautiful gardens, tea room, and guided tours
- Chancellor's Court: Large open courtyard with fountains, sculptures, and seating
- Edgbaston Park Hotel: Accommodations, dining, and meeting facilities on campus
