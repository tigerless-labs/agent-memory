## Application Instructions

1. **Cucumber** — Place chilled cucumber slices on eyes for 10–15 minutes to reduce swelling and soothe skin.

2. **Tea bags** — Use cool tea bags on eyes for 10–15 minutes to reduce inflammation and swelling.

3. **Tomato** — Apply tomato juice mixed with lemon juice on under-eye area and wash off after 10 minutes with cold water for lightening dark circles.

4. **Almond oil** — Massage almond oil on affected area regularly for moisturization and lightening the skin.

5. **Rose water** — Dip a cotton ball in rose water and place it on eyes to soothe and hydrate the skin.

6. **Aloe vera** — Apply fresh aloe vera gel under eyes to reduce puffiness and lighten dark circles.

7. **Turmeric** — Mix turmeric powder with pineapple juice and apply for about 10 minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water.

8. **Coconut oil** — Massage coconut oil under eyes before sleeping, and in the morning wash it off with warm water for smooth and hydrated skin.

9. **Lemon juice** — Apply a mixture of lemon juice and honey on affected area for 15 minutes, then rinse with warm water for lightening dark circles.

10. **Mint leaves** — Crush fresh mint leaves and apply as a paste under eyes for about 10 minutes. Wash off with warm water.

Note: Consult a doctor before trying any new remedies, especially if you have pre-existing medical conditions.
