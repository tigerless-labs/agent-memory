Plan to bring parents to Chicago for a weekend getaway after the June 2023 conference. Parents have never visited Chicago before.

Recommended activities:
- Architecture River Cruise (highly preferred)
- Explore the Magnificent Mile (Michigan Avenue) — upscale shopping, dining, landmarks
- Willis Tower Skydeck (103rd floor views)
- Millennium Park and Cloud Gate sculpture
- Navy Pier boat cruises and amusement park
- Art Institute of Chicago
- Lincoln Park neighborhood and zoo
- Stroll along Lakefront Trail
- Classic Chicago food: deep-dish pizza, Chicago-style hot dogs

Guided city tour planned: Chicago Trolley Company 90-minute guided tour recommended. They offer senior discounts (5 dollars off for age 65 and over). Other tour options: Big Bus Chicago (hop-on hop-off), Chicago's First Lady Cruises, Free Tours by Foot.
