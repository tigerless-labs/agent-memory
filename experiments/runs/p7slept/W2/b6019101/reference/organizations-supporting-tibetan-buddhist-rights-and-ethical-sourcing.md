Organizations working to support Tibetan Buddhists in China and promote ethical practices in international trade:

- **International Campaign for Tibet (ICT)**: Promotes human rights and democracy for Tibetans; raises awareness of human rights situation in Tibet
- **Human Rights Watch (HRW)**: International NGO investigating human rights abuses, including in Tibet and labor rights globally
- **Fair Trade USA**: Promotes ethical practices in international trade, working with producers on environmental sustainability and social justice
- **China Labor Watch (CLW)**: Improves working conditions and labor rights in China; investigates supply chains and corporate practices
- **Free Tibet**: Promotes human rights of Tibetans and supports religious freedom and cultural preservation
- **Dalai Lama**: Spiritual leader of Tibetan people, advocate for Tibetan Buddhist rights and non-violence
