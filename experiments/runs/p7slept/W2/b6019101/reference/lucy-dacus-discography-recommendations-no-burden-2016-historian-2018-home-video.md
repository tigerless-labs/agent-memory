## Lucy Dacus Discography

**Albums to explore:**
- **No Burden** (2016) — Debut album, critically acclaimed for introspective songwriting and lo-fi production.
- **Historian** (2018) — Sophomore album, recommended starting point. More polished production, expands on her sound with popular tracks.
- **Home Video** (2021) — Latest album, explores themes of nostalgia, identity, and relationships.

**Popular tracks:**
- Night Shift (from Historian) — Critically acclaimed single showcasing storytelling ability and catchy hooks
- Addictions (from No Burden) — Introspective track exploring themes of relationships and personal growth
- Nonbeliever (from Historian) — Hauntingly beautiful song featuring soaring vocals and poignant lyrics

Known for:
- Introspective and poignant lyrics
- Catchy indie-rock sound
- Deep, storytelling-driven songwriting
- Energetic and emotive stage presence

**Recommendation:** Start with **Historian** as it's the best representation of her sound; then explore **No Burden** for lo-fi production, or **Home Video** to hear her most recent work and growth as an artist.
