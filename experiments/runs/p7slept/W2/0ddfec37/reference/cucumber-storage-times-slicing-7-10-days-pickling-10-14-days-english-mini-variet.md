**Refrigerator storage times:**
- Slicing cucumbers: 7-10 days
- Pickling cucumbers: 10-14 days
- English cucumbers: 10-14 days
- Mini cucumbers: 5-7 days

**Storage tips:**
- Refrigerate at consistent temperature below 40°F (4°C)
- Use breathable container (paper bag, mesh bag, ventilated plastic)
- Keep dry; gently pat dry before storing
- Store upright to prevent moisture at stem end
- Don't wash before storing; wash just before use
- Check regularly for soft spots, mold, sour smell
- Remove spoiled cucumbers to prevent spread
