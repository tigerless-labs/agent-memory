Recommended eco-friendly yoga mats for home practice:
- **Lululemon The Mat**: High-quality, eco-friendly, excellent grip and cushioning
- **Manduka eKO SuperLite**: Lightweight, eco-friendly, textured surface for grip
- **Alo Yoga Warrior Mat**: Thick, cushioned, non-slip surface, stylish design
- **Gaiam Print Yoga Mat**: Affordable, eco-friendly, fun designs, decent grip
- **Jade Yoga Mat**: High-quality, eco-friendly, excellent grip and cushioning, natural rubber

Recommendations to look for: natural rubber, jute, recyclable polyurethane; 4-5mm thickness; textured surface; standard size 24" x 68".
