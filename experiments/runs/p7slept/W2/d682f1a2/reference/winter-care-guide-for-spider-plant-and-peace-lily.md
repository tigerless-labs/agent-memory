Winter care tips for both Spider Plant and Peace Lily:

Spider Plant:
- Lighting: Prefers bright indirect light; may need south-facing window or grow lights in winter
- Watering: Reduce watering; check soil moisture, water only when dry
- Humidity: Place pot on tray with water and pebbles or use humidifier
- Temperature: Keep between 65-75°F; avoid heating vents and drafts

Peace Lily:
- Lighting: Tolerates low light but benefits from indirect sunlight; use north-facing window or grow lights
- Watering: Reduce watering; let top 1-2 inches dry between waterings; avoid overwatering to prevent root rot
- Humidity: Prefers humid environment; use tray with pebbles and water or humidifier
- Temperature: Keep between 65-80°F; avoid heating vents and drafts

General:
- Fertilize less frequently (every 4-6 weeks or skip entirely)
- Prune to maintain shape and remove dead/dying leaves
- Monitor for winter pests like spider mites, mealybugs, scale
