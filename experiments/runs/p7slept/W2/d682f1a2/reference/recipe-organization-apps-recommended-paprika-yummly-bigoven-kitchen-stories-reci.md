Paprika Recipe Manager (iOS, Android, Windows): comprehensive recipe organization with tagging and search, meal planning, grocery lists, nutritional analysis. Yummly (iOS, Android): discover and save recipes from various sources with meal planning and grocery lists. BigOven (iOS, Android): massive recipe database (200,000+ recipes), save/organize, create cookbooks, generate grocery lists. Kitchen Stories (iOS, Android): visually appealing with recipe organization, digital cookbooks, and cooking videos. Recipe Keeper (iOS, Android): simple recipe tagging, meal planning, grocery lists, nutritional analysis. Alternative options: Evernote, OneNote, and Google Keep for note-based recipe organization.
