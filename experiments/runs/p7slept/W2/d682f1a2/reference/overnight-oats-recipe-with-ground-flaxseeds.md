**Ingredients:**\n- 1/2 cup rolled oats\n- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk (or milk of choice)\n- 1 tablespoon ground flaxseeds\n- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)\n- Pinch of salt\n- Toppings: sliced banana, berries, shredded coconut, cinnamon\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Mix oats, milk, ground flaxseeds, sweetener, and salt in a jar\n2. Stir well to distribute flaxseeds evenly\n3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight (8-10 hours)\n4. Stir in the morning, add more milk if too thick\n5. Top with desired toppings and serve\n\n**Note:** Ground flaxseeds have a slightly nutty flavor and coarser texture than chia seeds. Grind fresh flaxseeds using a coffee grinder, blender, food processor, or mortar and pestle. Best to grind just before use.
