User had wanted to see *Hamilton* for years. Finally watched it on Disney+ and loved it so much they watched it twice in one week. On the second viewing, convinced family to watch it with them. The show exceeded expectations. User was particularly moved by the musical numbers, cast performances (including Leslie Odom Jr.'s 'Wait for It' and Renée Elise Goldsberry's 'Helpless'), and the overall energy of the production.
