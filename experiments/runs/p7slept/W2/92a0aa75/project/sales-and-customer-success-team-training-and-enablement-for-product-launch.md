Sales Team: Product knowledge, target account identification, pilot programs, sales enablement content, lead generation, demo handling, upselling. Training (2-3 months, 5-10% of budget): comprehensive product training, sales messaging, objection handling, role-playing, content access, feedback/coaching.

Customer Success Team: Customer feedback and insights, early adopter identification, pilot programs, onboarding planning, customer reference programs, support and training. Training (2-3 months, 5-10% of budget): product knowledge, customer-centric messaging, change management, communication planning, role-playing.
