## Ableton Live Music Production Learning

Experimenting with Ableton Live DAW to create drum patterns and melodies that complement ukulele and saxophone recordings.

### Drum Pattern Approach
- Foundation: kick on beats 1 & 3, snare on 2 & 4
- Add rhythmic interest with variations (hi-hats, altered snare patterns)
- Focus on groove and feel over complexity
- Match drum sounds to track style
- Keep patterns simple and effective
- Loop patterns for continuity
- Use swing/shuffle for natural feel

### Ableton Tools
- Drum Rack: foundation for pattern creation
- Max for Live: custom effects and patterns
- Arrangement View: visual editing
