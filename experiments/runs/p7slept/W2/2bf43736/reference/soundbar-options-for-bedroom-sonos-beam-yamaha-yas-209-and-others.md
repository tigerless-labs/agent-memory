## Recommended Soundbars\n\n- **Sonos Beam**: $399. Compact, excellent sound, Alexa/Google Assistant.\n- **Yamaha YAS-209**: $299. Clear Voice feature, built-in subwoofer, HDMI ARC.\n- **Bose Soundbar 700**: $799. High-end, exceptional sound.\n- **Samsung HW-Q90R**: $1,099. Object-based sound, wireless rear speakers.\n- **JBL Bar 5.1 Surround**: $499. Focus on surround, wireless subwoofer.\n- **Polk Command Bar**: $299. Voice control focused, built-in Alexa.\n- **Sony HT-Z9F**: $699. Dolby Atmos, DTS:X support.
