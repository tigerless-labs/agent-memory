Getting from Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN) to Eixample:\n\n1. **Aerobús**: Direct bus every 5-10 minutes to Plaça Catalunya, 35-40 min, 5.90 EUR\n2. **R2 Nord Train**: Commuter train from airport to Passeig de Gràcia station, 25-30 min, 4.10 EUR\n3. **Taxi**: 20-30 min depending on traffic, 30-40 EUR\n4. **Private Transfer**: More expensive, convenient for groups\n5. **Metro**: L9 line from airport to Zona Universitària, then transfer to L3 to Passeig de Gràcia, 40-50 min, 2.40 EUR\n\nTickets available online or at airport. Check airport website for current schedules.
