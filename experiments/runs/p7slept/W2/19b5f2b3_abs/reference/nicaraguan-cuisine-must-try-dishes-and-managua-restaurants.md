## Must-Try Nicaraguan Dishes
- **Gallo Pinto**: National dish of rice and beans mixed with onions and sweet peppers, served with sour cream, cheese, and fried plantains
- **Nacatamales**: Masa (corn dough) filled with pork or chicken, rice, and vegetables, wrapped in plantain leaves and steamed
- **Vigorón**: Street food with yuca (cassava) topped with crispy chicharrones (fried pork skins) and cabbage slaw in sour orange sauce
- **Quesillo**: Nicaraguan cheese wrapped in tortilla with onions, cream, and salt
- **Indio Viejo**: Stew with ground corn, chicken or beef, vegetables, and sour orange-based sauce
- **Tostones**: Fried green plantains served as side or appetizer with garlic sauce

## Recommended Restaurants & Vendors in Managua
- **Los Ranchos**: Local chain, steak, seafood, traditional dishes like gallo pinto and nacatamales
- **Mercado Carlos Roberto Huembes**: Bustling market for quesillo, vigorón, gallo pinto
- **Asados Don Pancho**: Family-owned, grilled meats, gallo pinto, plantains
- **La Cocina de Doña Haydée**: Near airport, nacatamales, indio viejo, tostones
- **El Caramanchel**: Fish dishes, fish soup, grilled fish, ceviche, seafood soup
