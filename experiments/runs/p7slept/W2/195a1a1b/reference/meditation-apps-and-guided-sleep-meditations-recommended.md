Headspace: Sleep meditation (10 min) with Andy Puddicombe. User found this very helpful and calming, especially the imagery and visualization sections.

Calm: Deep Sleep (20 min), Sleep Story: The Magical Forest (20 min).

Insight Timer: Free app. Body Scan meditation (10 min).

Meditation Studio: Sleep Meditation (15 min), Progressive Muscle Relaxation (10 min).

Buddhify: Guided meditations for different times of day (one-time purchase).

Free resources: YouTube (Andrew Weil Sleep Meditation, Herbert Benson Relaxation Response, Jack Kornfield Loving-Kindness), Rainy Mood, Sleep With Me podcast, Meditation Oasis, UCLA Mindful Awareness Center.

Tips: Start 5-10 min sessions, use headphones, quiet space, consistent habit, gentle redirection of wandering thoughts, experiment to find best fit.
