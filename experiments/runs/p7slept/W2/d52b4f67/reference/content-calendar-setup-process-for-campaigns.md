Steps to set up a campaign content calendar:

1. **Identify content channels:** Social media platforms, email, blog, video, paid ads, influencer partnerships, guest blogging

2. **Determine content themes:** Based on campaign goals and target audience (e.g., product features, customer testimonials, industry news, educational content, behind-the-scenes)

3. **Plan content types:** Social posts (images/videos/links), blog articles, email newsletters, video scripts, infographics, podcasts

4. **Create calendar template** with columns:
   - Date, Channel, Content Type, Theme, Content Title, Keywords/Tags, Target Audience, Status, Content Owner, Budget

5. **Assign content to dates and channels** across the campaign period (e.g., 6 weeks)

6. **Additional columns:** Content status (draft/in-progress/published), engagement metrics targets, budget allocation

7. **Review and refine:** Regularly check alignment with campaign goals

Tools: Google Sheets, Trello, CoSchedule (color-code by theme/channel for visualization)

Best practices: Plan evergreen 'content bank' for repurposing, build in contingency content, ensure consistent frequency across channels
