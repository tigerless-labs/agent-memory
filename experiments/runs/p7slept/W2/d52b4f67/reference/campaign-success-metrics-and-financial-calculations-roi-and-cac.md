Key metrics for measuring campaign performance:

**Return on Investment (ROI)**
- Formula: (Gain from Investment - Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment
- Example: $1,500 revenue - $1,000 spent = $500 gain; ROI = 500/1000 = 50%
- Why it matters: Justifies campaign spend, optimizes budget allocation, improves decision-making
- Shows return per dollar spent

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
- Formula: Total Marketing and Sales Expenses / Number of New Customers Acquired
- Example: $10,000 spent to acquire 100 customers = $100 CAC
- Why it matters: Identifies inefficiencies, informs pricing strategy, determines sustainability of acquisition

**Other metrics to track:**
- Social media: engagement rate, reach, impressions, follower growth, conversion rate
- Email: open rate, click-through rate, bounce rate, unsubscribe rate
- Content: page views, unique visitors, bounce rate, time on site
- Paid ads: cost per click (CPC), cost per conversion (CPA), return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Overall: customer lifetime value (CLV), net promoter score (NPS)
