Festivals in Tokyo with traditional Japanese music and dance:

1. **Tokyo National Museum Summer Festival** (Taito-ku, August) - Traditional music and dance performances, workshops, and exhibitions

2. **Kanda Matsuri** (Chiyoda-ku, mid-May, biennial) - Parade with musicians and dancers performing traditional Japanese music and dance

3. **Shunju Matsuri** (Higashi-cho, late September, annual) - Traditional music and dance performances, food, and handicraft booths

4. **Setagaya Hachimangu Shrine Festival** (Setagaya-ku, late September, annual) - Traditional music and dance including taiko drumming and kagura (traditional Shinto performance)

Senso-ji Temple (oldest temple in Tokyo with famous red lantern) and Meiji Shrine (Shinto shrine in forested area) are also major cultural attractions that host traditional performances.
