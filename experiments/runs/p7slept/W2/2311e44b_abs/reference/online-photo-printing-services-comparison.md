Services evaluated: Shutterfly ($3.99–$9.99 shipping), Snapfish ($2.99–$9.99), Mpix ($4.95–$14.95, professional-grade), Nations Photo Lab ($4.95–$14.95), Printique ($4.95–$14.95), Google Photos ($2.99–$4.99). Mpix chosen for wide paper options (Lustre, Metallic, Matte, Canvas, Metal, Wood). First-time discounts: 20–30% welcome offer, discount codes, free shipping, bundle deals, referral program. Metal print settings: Adobe RGB color space, 16-bit depth, 300 dpi minimum, TIFF/PSD format. Useful plugins: Nik Collection, Luminar.
