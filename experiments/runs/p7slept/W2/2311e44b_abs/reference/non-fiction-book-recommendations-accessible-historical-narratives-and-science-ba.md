## Accessible Non-Fiction Recommendations

For someone enjoying Sapiens but finding it slow-going:

1. **The Devil in the White City** by Erik Larson - Historical true crime about 1893 World's Fair in Chicago and serial killer H.H. Holmes (reads like a thriller)
2. **Bad Blood: Secrets and Lies in a Silicon Valley Startup** by John Carreyrou - Theranos rise and fall
3. **The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks** by Rebecca Skloot - Medicine, ethics, and humanity intersecting
4. **Just Mercy: A Story of Justice and Redemption** by Bryan Stevenson - US justice system, racial inequality, death row advocacy
5. **The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business** by Charles Duhigg - Science-backed habits book with real-life examples

## Science-Backed Self-Help Books (Habits & Productivity)

1. **Atomic Habits** by James Clear - Building habits, breaking habits (backed by psychology and neuroscience)
2. **Deep Work** by Cal Newport - Productivity and focus strategies
3. **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen Covey - Decades of research-based personal development
4. **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck - Growth mindset research
5. **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle - Mindfulness and meditation-based practices
6. **Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience** by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi - Flow state and positive psychology
7. **The Happiness Advantage** by Shawn Achor - Happiness and success connection (positive psychology)
8. **Mini Habits: Smaller Habits, Bigger Results** by Stephen Guise - Unique habit formation approach
9. **The Productivity Project** by Chris Bailey - Research-backed productivity strategies
10. **The Willpower Instinct** by Kelly McGonigal - Science of willpower and self-control
