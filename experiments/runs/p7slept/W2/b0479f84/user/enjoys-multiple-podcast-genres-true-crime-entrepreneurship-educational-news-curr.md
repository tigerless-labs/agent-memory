Listens to podcasts across multiple genres for different moods and purposes:

- **True Crime**: Started listening to Crime Junkie on January 15, 2023 (on a road trip to visit family). Can't get enough of the genre.
- **Entrepreneurship**: Interested in entrepreneurship and has been listening to 'How I Built This' with Guy Raz for months (as of May 2023).
- **Educational**: Enjoys educational podcasts that are engaging and informative. Listens to 'Stuff You Should Know' and 'Radiolab'.
- **News/Current Events**: Listens to 'The Daily' from The New York Times to stay up-to-date on world events.
- **Comedy**: Loves comedy podcasts for breaks from serious topics. Particularly enjoys 'My Brother, My Brother and Me' and 'Improv4Humans'. Seeks out comedy with absurdist humor, sibling/group dynamics, and playful tone.
