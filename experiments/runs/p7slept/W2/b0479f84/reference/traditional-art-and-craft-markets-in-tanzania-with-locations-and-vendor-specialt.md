## Tanzanian Traditional Art Markets

**Dar es Salaam:**
- Mwenge Woodcarvers Market — Known for woodcarvings and skilled craftsmen
- Tingatinga Arts Cooperative Society — Founded by late Edward Tingatinga; specializes in distinctive Tingatinga painting style featuring stylized animals and plants native to the region

**Arusha:**
- Cultural Heritage Center — Large selection of art and crafts from different regions of Tanzania
- Arusha Maasai Market — Specializes in Maasai beadwork and crafts made by Maasai artisans

**Zanzibar:**
- Zawadi House (Stone Town) — Boutique offering high-quality crafts including wood carvings, textiles, and jewelry
