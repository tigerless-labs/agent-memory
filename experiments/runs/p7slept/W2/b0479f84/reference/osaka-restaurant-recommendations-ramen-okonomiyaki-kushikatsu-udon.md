**Osaka Restaurants for Traditional Japanese Cuisine:**\n\n**Ramen:**\n- Ichiran Osaka (same chain as Tokyo, different broth recipe)\n- Ramen Kuroda (rich, savory tonkotsu ramen)\n\n**Okonomiyaki (savory pancakes):**\n- Okonomiyaki Mizuno (classic, savory okonomiyaki)\n- Okonomiyaki Chitanda (modern, creative variations)\n\n**Kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers):**\n- Kushikatsu Tanaka (wide variety)\n- Kushikatsu Daruma (classic, affordable)\n\n**Udon:**\n- Sanuki Udon (authentic, handmade noodles)\n- Tsurutontan (modern, creative udon dishes)\n\n**Local specialties to try:**\n- Takoyaki (octopus dumplings)\n- Kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers)\n\n*Note: Got ramen recommendation from Travel Enthusiasts Facebook group*
