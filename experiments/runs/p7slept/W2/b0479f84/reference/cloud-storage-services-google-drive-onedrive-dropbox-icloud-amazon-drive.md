Cloud storage and backup services recommended:\n\n- **Google Drive**: 15GB free storage, integrates with Google apps\n- **Microsoft OneDrive**: 5GB free, integrates with Office suite\n- **Dropbox**: 2GB free, seamless file sharing, many integrations\n- **Apple iCloud**: 5GB free for Apple users\n- **Amazon Drive**: 5GB free, unlimited photo storage for Prime members\n\n**Recommended backup strategy**: 3-2-1 rule — three copies of data, two different media types, one offsite.
