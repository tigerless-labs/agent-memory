Creating a series of teaching episodes centered on 'the Teacher,' a spiritual guide figure who emphasizes curiosity and resilience. Episodes explore the Teacher's engagement with different worldviews:
- Journalists skeptical of perspective-taking
- LLM researchers optimistic about technology
- Fundamentalist Christians concerned about non-Christian framing

Core philosophy: bridges Eastern (Buddhist/contemplative) and Western (Christian/mystical) traditions; critiques over-reliance on technology; advocates balance between innovation and human wisdom.
