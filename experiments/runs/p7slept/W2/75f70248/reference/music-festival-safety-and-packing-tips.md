Festival safety tips: keep valuables in small zipper pouch or wallet, attach phone to bag with strap/lanyard, label bag with contact info, keep bag in front in crowded areas, designate meeting spot with group, store bag securely at night/in tent. Packing essentials: travel-sized sunscreen, hat, portable charger with cord organizer, phone, wallet, water bottle. Bag organization: use separate compartments, front pocket for frequent-access items, main compartment for larger items, side pockets for water bottle. Festival-specific: avoid leaving bag unattended, monitor surroundings, use designated bag-check areas if available.
