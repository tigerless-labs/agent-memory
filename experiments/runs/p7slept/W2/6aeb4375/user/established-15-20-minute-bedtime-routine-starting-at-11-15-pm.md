Bedtime routine to help establish consistent sleep schedule:\n\n- **11:15–11:20 PM (5 min):** Deep breaths and dim bedroom lights\n- **11:20–11:30 PM (10 min):** Read physical book (non-engaging, avoid page-turners) with 10-minute timer set\n- **11:30–11:35 PM (5 min):** Quick relaxation (deep breathing, progressive muscle relaxation, or guided imagery)\n- **11:35–11:40 PM (5 min):** Get ready for bed (brush teeth, change into pajamas, turn off lights/electronics)\n- **11:40–11:45 PM (5 min):** Get into bed, take final deep breaths, settle for sleep\n\nGoal: Fall asleep closer to 11:45 PM consistently.
