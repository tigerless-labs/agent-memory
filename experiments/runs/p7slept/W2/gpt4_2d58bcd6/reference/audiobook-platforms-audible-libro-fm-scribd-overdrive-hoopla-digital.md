Audiobook subscription and access platforms reviewed in May 2023:

Subscription services: Audible (14.95/month, 500k+ titles), Libro.fm (14.99/month, indie bookstore partners), Scribd (8.99/month unlimited), Audiobooks.com (14.95/month), Hibooks (12.99/month), Kobo Audiobooks, Google Play Books

Free/Library options: OverDrive (borrow free with library card), Hoopla Digital (free with library card or student ID), Audiobooksync (free summer program - 2 audiobooks/week for 13 weeks)

All platforms offer mobile apps for on-the-go listening.
