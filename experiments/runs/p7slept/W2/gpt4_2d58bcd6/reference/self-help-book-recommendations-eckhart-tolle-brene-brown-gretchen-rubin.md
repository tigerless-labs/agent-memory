Recommended self-help and personal development books for reflection and personal growth:

Mindfulness: 'The Power of Now' by Eckhart Tolle - Guide to living in present moment

Brené Brown: 'Daring Greatly' and 'The Gifts of Imperfection' - Vulnerability, shame, imperfection, self-acceptance

Happiness: 'The Happiness Project' by Gretchen Rubin - Year-long happiness experiment

Relationships: 'Attached' by Levine & Heller (attachment styles), 'Nonviolent Communication' by Rosenberg, 'Hold Me Tight' by Sue Johnson

Psychology: 'Mindset' by Carol Dweck (growth mindset), 'Presence' by Amy Cuddy (confidence)

Memoirs: 'Wild' by Cheryl Strayed (self-discovery), 'The Year of Magical Thinking' by Joan Didion (grief), 'Man's Search for Meaning' by Viktor Frankl (resilience)
