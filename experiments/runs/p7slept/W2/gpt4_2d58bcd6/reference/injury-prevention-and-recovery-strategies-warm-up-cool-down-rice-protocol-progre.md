**Injury Prevention Strategies:**

1. Warm up and cool down (10-15 min each)
2. Stretch and foam roll regularly
3. Strengthen core and ankles (planks, side planks, ankle circles)
4. Use proper gear and equipment
5. Listen to body - stop if fatigued or experiencing pain
6. Gradually increase intensity and volume (avoid sudden changes)
7. Focus on compound exercises (squats, lunges, push-ups, rows, planks)
8. Incorporate progressive overload (increase weight, reps, or sets over time)
9. Use proper form and technique
10. Schedule workouts as non-negotiable appointments
11. Vary routine every 4-6 weeks to avoid plateaus

**Injury Recovery Strategies:**

1. RICE protocol - Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation for acute injuries
2. Gentle stretching and mobilization exercises
3. Compression wraps/sleeves to reduce swelling
4. Elevation to promote blood flow and reduce inflammation
5. Rest days and modified workouts
6. Professional help for chronic pain or severe injuries

**Specific for Ankle Sprains:**
- Strengthen ankles with ankle circles, calf raises, single-leg balancing
- Use ankle braces/supports during high-impact activities
- Improve balance and proprioception (single-leg squats, balance boards)
