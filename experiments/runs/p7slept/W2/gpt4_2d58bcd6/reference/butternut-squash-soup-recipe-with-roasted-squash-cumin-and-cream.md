Simple butternut squash soup recipe: roast 2-3 lb squash at 400°F for 45-50 min. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil, add cumin and paprika, broth, roasted squash. Blend smooth, add cream if desired. Serves 4-6. Stores 3-5 days refrigerated or 3-6 months frozen.
