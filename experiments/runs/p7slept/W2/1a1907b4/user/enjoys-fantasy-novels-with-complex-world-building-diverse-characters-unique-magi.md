User seeks fantasy novels with the following characteristics:

**Structural elements:**
- Complex world-building and mythology
- Unique, well-developed magic systems central to the story
- Non-linear or unconventional narrative structures (willing to invest time learning complex terminology)

**Character & theme focus:**
- Diverse, well-developed characters
- Morally complex themes and ambiguous morality
- Thoughtful exploration of identity, community, cultural heritage
- Character-driven storytelling over action-heavy plots
- Mature themes: trauma, oppression, philosophical questions

**Examples that resonate:**
- *The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August* by Claire North (reincarnation, identity, philosophical)
- *The Poppy War* by R.F. Kuang (unique magic system, complex characters, politics)
- *The Fifth Season* by N.K. Jemisin (geology-based magic, diverse characters, trauma)

**Preferences:**
- Willing to read both series and standalone novels
- Prefers character-driven, introspective tone over fast-paced action
- Appreciates beautiful, lyrical prose
- Values cultural depth and mythological/folkloric elements
- Likes books with awards recognition (Hugo, National Book Award, etc.)
