American photographer known for establishing one of the first professional photography careers for women in the United States.

**Life & Career:**
- Born 1864 in Virginia; died 1952
- Born into prominent Virginia family
- Educated at private schools
- Began photography career in 1890s as apprentice to several photographers
- Opened her own studio in Washington, D.C. in 1894
- Quickly established reputation as one of the city's most talented and innovative photographers

**Work & Contributions:**
- Pioneer in architectural photography, one of the first to recognize importance of documenting America's historic buildings
- Traveled extensively photographing colonial-era buildings and other historic structures
- Helped raise awareness of importance of preserving America's architectural heritage

**Notable Subjects (Portraits):**
- Theodore Roosevelt
- Mark Twain
- Susan B. Anthony
- Alice Roosevelt Longworth
- Other politicians, celebrities, and socialites

**Characteristics of Her Work:**
- Elegance, simplicity, attention to detail
- Established reputation as leading photographer of her time

**Legacy:**
- One of first women to establish professional photography career in US
- Work continues celebrated for technical excellence, artistic innovation, and enduring influence on American photography
- Committed to using photography as tool for social change and raising awareness of social issues
