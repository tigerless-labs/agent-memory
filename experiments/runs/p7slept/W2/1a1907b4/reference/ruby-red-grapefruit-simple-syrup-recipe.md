**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1 cup (240ml) water
- 1/2 cup (120ml) freshly squeezed Ruby Red Grapefruit juice
- Optional: 1-2 slices of grapefruit peel for extra citrus flavor

**Instructions:**
1. Combine sugar and water in a saucepan and heat until sugar is dissolved
2. Add grapefruit juice and optional grapefruit peel slices
3. Remove from heat and let steep for at least 30 minutes to allow flavors to meld
4. Strain the syrup into a clean glass bottle and store in refrigerator

**Storage:** Up to 2 weeks in refrigerator

**Usage:** ~0.5 oz (15ml) per cocktail; can adjust to taste

**Why Ruby Red Grapefruit:** Chosen for balanced sweet-tart flavor and the beautiful pink hue it adds to cocktails. The bitterness balances sweetness while citrus flavor complements botanical gin.
