Soap Fragrances: Woodland scents (cedarwood, sandalwood, pine), spicy/smoky (black pepper, bergamot, smoked vanilla), fresh/citrusy (eucalyptus, mint, grapefruit), leather and tobacco. Candle Fragrances: Woodsmoke and campfire, spiced and oriental (cinnamon, cardamom, frankincense), earthy and mossy (moss, sage, forest floor), bourbon and oak. Blending tips: combine cedarwood with bergamot for fresh earthy scent, or pair leather and tobacco for rich sophisticated aroma. Best practices: keep fragrances simple, consider desired mood/atmosphere, test and iterate on small batches, use high-quality phthalate-free fragrance oils.
