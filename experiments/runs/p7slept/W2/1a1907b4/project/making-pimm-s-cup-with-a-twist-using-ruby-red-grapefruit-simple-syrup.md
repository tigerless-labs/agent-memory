User is experimenting with making a Pimm's Cup with a Twist cocktail with specific modifications:

**Recipe:** Pimm's Cup with a Twist
- 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin (cucumber and floral notes)
- 0.5 oz lemon juice  
- 0.5 oz Ruby Red Grapefruit simple syrup (chosen for balanced flavor and pink hue)
- 0.5 oz Pimm's No. 1
- Splash of ginger beer
- Muddled cucumber slices (preferred over cucumber juice for more intense, fresh flavor and texture)

**Preparation:**
- Muddle cucumber slices in the mixing glass
- Serve in a Collins glass (tall, slender shape to showcase color and garnishes)

**Garnish options considered:**
- Cucumber slice/wheel
- Grapefruit slice/wedge
- Fresh mint sprig
- Edible flowers (violas, pansies, lavender)
- Fresh berries
- Large clear ice cubes

**Context:** User recently attended a mixology class and had made classic cocktails; this is a creative experimentation based on that learning.
