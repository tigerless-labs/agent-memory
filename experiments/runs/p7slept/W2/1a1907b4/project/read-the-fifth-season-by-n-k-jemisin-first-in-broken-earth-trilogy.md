User wants to read *The Fifth Season* by N.K. Jemisin, the first book in the Broken Earth trilogy.

**Why this book:**
- Introduces the world (the Stillness), magic system (orogeny), and three main characters: Essun, Damaya, and Syenite
- Complex world-building and unique magic based on controlling/manipulating energy
- Diverse characters and thought-provoking themes about trauma, oppression, and violence
- Won Hugo Award for Best Novel (2016)
- Non-linear narrative structure

**Series note:** Part of a trilogy (The Fifth Season → The Obelisk Gate → The Stone Sky), but designed to stand alone. If enjoyed, will want to continue with the sequels.

**Prepare for:** Complex terminology, emotional investment in characters, mature themes, patience with world-building.
