## Popular Neighborhoods\n\n**Gastown**: Historic with cobblestone streets, boutiques, restaurants, Gastown Steam Clock. Access: SkyTrain Waterfront, buses #4 or #7.\n\n**Yaletown**: Upscale trendy area with breweries, waterfront restaurants. Access: SkyTrain Yaletown-Roundhouse, buses #6 or #17.\n\n**Granville Island**: Public market, artisan shops, restaurants, street performers. Access: Bus #50, or ferry from False Creek.\n\n**Chinatown**: Large neighborhood with Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden, cultural center, diverse food. Access: SkyTrain Stadium-Chinatown, buses #3 or #8.\n\n**Mount Pleasant**: Hip with vintage shops, craft breweries, street art, flea market. Access: SkyTrain Main St-Science World, buses #3 or #9.\n\n**Commercial Drive**: Eclectic community with independent shops, cafes, street art, farmers market, Rio Theatre. Access: SkyTrain Commercial-Broadway, buses #9 or #20.\n\n**Kitsilano**: Beach neighborhood with outdoor recreation, Kitsilano Beach & Pool, Vanier Park. Access: Bus #4, or SkyTrain Granville then transfer.\n\n## Key Attractions\nStanley Park (1,000 acres), Capilano Suspension Bridge, Grouse Mountain, Museum of Anthropology, Vancouver Aquarium, VanDusen Botanical Garden (55 acres), English Bay.\n\n## Climate & Weather\n**August**: 23-25°C high, 13-15°C low, 9 hours daily sunshine, dry, 64% humidity.\n**September**: 20-22°C high, 10-12°C low, 7 hours daily sunshine, start of wet season (~12 rainy days).\n**Winter (Dec-Feb)**: 3-8°C average, 12-15 rainy days per month, snow rare in city but common in nearby mountains.\n\n## Transportation\nSkyTrain Expo Line, TransLink buses including #3, #4, #6, #7, #8, #9, #17, #20, #50. Use Compass Card and Transit app for planning. Well-developed bike infrastructure including Seawall and Central Valley Greenway.
