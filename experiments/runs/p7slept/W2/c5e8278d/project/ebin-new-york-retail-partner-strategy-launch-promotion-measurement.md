Strategy plans created for Ebin New York to support retail partners with in-store launches:

**Launch Strategy:** Product images/videos, how-to guides, product descriptions
**Promotion Strategy:** Social media content, email campaigns, in-store signage
**Measurement:** Track sales, foot traffic, engagement, social metrics, email metrics

Key KPIs: baseline comparison (pre/during/post), customer engagement, social engagement, email rates

Tactics: product demos, free samples, personalized promotions, eye-catching signage, influencer partnerships
