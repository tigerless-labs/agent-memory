# Pittsburgh Weekend Getaway

**Trip Parameters:**
- Duration: 2-3 days
- Budget: 500-700 USD (flights + accommodations)
- Neighborhoods: Oakland or Shadyside
- Accommodation target: 100-150 USD/night

**Interests:**
- Strip District: street food, ethnic markets, Primanti Bros. sandwiches
- Mount Washington: views via Duquesne Incline or Monongahela Incline
- Andy Warhol Museum
- Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens
- Carnegie Science Center
- Craft breweries: Penn Brewery, East End Brewing Company, Grist House Brewing
- Three Rivers Heritage Trail (24-mile trail system)
- North Shore area
- PNC Park

**Hotels Researched:**

Oakland:
- Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh East Liberty: 150 USD/night
- The Oaklander Hotel: 120 USD/night
- Airbnb: 80-120 USD/night

Shadyside:
- Hotel Shadyside: 120 USD/night
- The Inn on Walnut: 100 USD/night
- Airbnb: 100-150 USD/night

Also considered:
- The Inn on the Mexican War Streets: 120 USD/night
- The Mansions on Fifth Hotel: 180 USD/night

**Transportation:**
- Flights from NYC (JFK/LGA): 150-250 USD round-trip
- In-city: Port Authority transit (2.50 USD one-way, 5.50 USD day pass); Free Transit Zone downtown; Healthy Ride bike share; Uber/Lyft available

User is active traveler; recently returned from LA/San Diego and Chicago; visited Boston recently. Looking for accommodations in Oakland or Shadyside neighborhoods.
