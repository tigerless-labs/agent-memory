Rex'\n- Natural History Museum, London — 'Dino Jaws' (evolution of dinosaur teeth and jaws)\n\n## Online Communities & Platforms\n\n- ArtSci Salon (art, science, and technology intersection)\n- SciArt Initiative (non-profit promoting science and art)\n- Ars Electronica (festival and platform for art and science projects)\n- DinoPit (dinosaur-themed art and illustrations)\n- Paleoart community (paleontological illustrations)
