## Recommended Online Learning Platforms\n\n**Abstract Art:**\n- The Art of Abstract (comprehensive guide)\n- Abstract Art Academy (video lessons, beginner to advanced)\n- YouTube: The Art Sherpa, Artists Network, Abstract Art with Deb Watson\n\n**Printmaking:**\n- Printmaking 101 (comprehensive guide)\n- Printmaking Council of New Jersey (tutorials, videos, resources)\n- YouTube: Printmaking with Zeke, The Printmaking Studio\n\n**Multi-Medium Courses:**\n- Skillshare (abstract art and printmaking courses)\n- Craftsy (printmaking, abstract art, mixed media classes)\n- Creativebug (various art techniques)\n\n**Communities & Forums:**\n- Reddit: r/AbstractArt, r/Printmaking\n- Facebook groups for art communities\n\n**Blogs & Magazines:**\n- The Jealous Curator (abstract art, printmaking, mixed media)\n- Printmaking Today magazine\n- Abstract Art Magazine (digital)
