Recommended books: The Will to Change by bell hooks, Masculinity and Its Discontents by Michael Kimmel, The End of Male Dominance by Steven Pinker. Documentaries: The Mask You Live In (2015), The Bro Code (2011). Online: The Good Men Project, XY Online, The National Coalition for Men & Boys. Podcasts: The Man Enough Podcast, The Gender Podcast. Courses: Masculinity and Social Justice by Michael Kimmel (Coursera), Gender and Sexuality by University of Michigan (edX).
