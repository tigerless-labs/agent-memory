**Book:** Guns, Germs, and Steel: The Fates of Human Societies\n**Author:** Jared Diamond\n**Year:** 1997\n**Award:** Pulitzer Prize for General Nonfiction (1998)\n\n**Key concepts:**\n- Explores how geography, technology, and biology shaped human societies over 13,000 years\n- Argues Eurasian civilizations rose due to favorable geographic factors\n- Introduces "lucky latitudes" (20-40° N) where major civilizations emerged\n- Proposes "axis of history" running east to west\n\n**Topics covered:**\n- The Fertile Crescent, Indus Valley, Yellow River Valley, Mediterranean region\n- Role of natural resources, climate, soil, water, and biodiversity\n- Impact of technology and disease on civilizations\n\n**Note:** User is particularly interested in the "lucky latitudes" concept.
