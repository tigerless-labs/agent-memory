Considering purchasing a dairy cow in the near future to expand farm's dairy product offerings.

**Breed options under consideration:**
1. **Brown Swiss** — gentle, hardy, adaptable; good for small-to-medium operations
2. **Jersey** — rich, creamy milk; high in protein and calcium; friendly and curious

**Decision basis:**
- Has been researching breeds online
- Talking to other farmers in the area for advice
- Evaluating both breeds for milk production quality and suitability to farm

**Purpose:**
- Produce milk and cheese products to sell at farm open house event
- Expand farm's dairy product line beyond eggs and baked goods

**Next steps:**
- Finalize purchase decision soon
- Prepare farm infrastructure (fencing, pasture management, nutrition plans)
- Plan gradual introduction of new cow to farm
- Establish milking routine

Status: Ready to make investment soon, timeline pending final decision between breeds.
