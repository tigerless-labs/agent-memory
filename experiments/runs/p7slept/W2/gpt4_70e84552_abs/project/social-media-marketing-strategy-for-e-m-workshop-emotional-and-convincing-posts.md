Marketing approach for E&M training workshop using social media posts with emotional, convincing tone. Six post types developed: (1) Teaser announcing workshop with 2023 guidelines and data points focus, (2) Historical narrative of E/M MDM table evolution 1995-2023, (3) Deep dive into hospital coding for observation/inpatient/consultation services, (4) Practical applications showing how new MDM table applies to ED and hospital coding, (5) FAQ post addressing trainee concerns about workshop relevance, duration, and hands-on practice. Each post includes call-to-action for registration and relevant hashtags (#EandM #Coding). Strategy emphasizes empowerment, confidence-building, and career advancement messaging.
