1920s fashion research for Mary Sunshine character preparation:\n\n**Clothing:**\n- Flapper dresses with dropped waistline and loose fit\n- Beaded or fringe dresses\n- High-waisted pants\n\n**Accessories:**\n- Cloche hats (felt or straw with feathers/flowers/beads)\n- Pearl necklaces (single or multi-strand)\n- Art Deco jewelry with geometric patterns\n- Long gloves (silk or kid leather)\n- Mary Janes or T-strap shoes with low-to-moderate heel\n\n**Hair & Makeup:**\n- Bobbed hair with center parting and loose curls (Mary Sunshine's signature)\n- Red lipstick (bold, matte shade)\n- Rouge and powder for cheeks\n\n**Style Tips:**\n- Embrace androgynous look with oversized clothing and accessories\n- Mix and match patterns, textures, and styles\n- Experiment with different looks to find what feels right for the character
