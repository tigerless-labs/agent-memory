Gender pay gap data showing the difference between average male and female earnings by industry (women's earnings as percentage of men's).

Global: Gender pay gap approximately 15.6% (women earn 84.4 cents per dollar earned by men)

US Industry Breakdown:
- Technology and IT: 14.3% gap (women earn 85.7% of male earnings)
- Healthcare: 12.2% gap (women earn 87.8% of male earnings)
- Education: 12.5% gap (women earn 87.5% of male earnings)
- Arts and Design: 15.4% gap (women earn 84.6% of male earnings)
- Finance and Insurance: 17.6% gap (women earn 82.4% of male earnings)
- STEM fields: 18.1% gap (women earn 81.9% of male earnings)
- Business and Management: 21.4% gap (women earn 78.6% of male earnings)
- Law: 24.4% gap (women earn 75.6% of male earnings)

Key Causes:
- Occupational segregation (women underrepresented in high-paying roles)
- Gender stereotypes affecting role assignment
- Pay discrimination for same work
- Caregiving responsibilities and time off
- Lower salary negotiation rates

Solutions:
- Equal pay for equal work enforcement
- Salary transparency and disclosure
- Mentorship and sponsorship programs
- Flexible work arrangements for caregivers
- Education and negotiation skills training
