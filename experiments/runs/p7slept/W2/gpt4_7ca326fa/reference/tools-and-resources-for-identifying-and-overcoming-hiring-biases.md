Resources and tools to help identify and overcome implicit biases in hiring and job application review.

Online Assessment Tools:
- Harvard University's Project Implicit - Free online implicit bias test to become aware of personal biases
- GapJumpers - Blind hiring tool to remove identifiable information from resumes
- Blendoor - Blind hiring platform
- ZipRecruiter Blind Hiring Feature - Resume anonymization feature

Best Practices and Training:
- Structured interviews using standardized questions and evaluation criteria
- Blind review process to remove identifiable information from resumes and cover letters
- Diversity and inclusion scorecards for candidate evaluation based on skills and qualifications
- Unconscious bias training programs
- DEI (Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion) training through SHRM and other organizations

Process Improvements:
- Diverse interview panels to bring different perspectives
- Clear, skills-focused job descriptions
- Standardized evaluation criteria for consistency
- Atlassian Diversity and Inclusion in Hiring guide
- Pinterest Inclusive Hiring Toolkit
