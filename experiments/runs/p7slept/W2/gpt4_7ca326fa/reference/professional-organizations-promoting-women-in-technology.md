Organizations focused on women in tech and gender equality recommended for involvement in promoting gender equality in the industry.

Girls Who Code - Non-profit aiming to increase women in technology and close the gender gap

Women Who Code - Global network providing resources, support, and opportunities for women in tech

National Center for Women & Information Technology (NCWIT) - Non-profit working to increase women's participation in computing and IT

Women in Technology International (WITI) - Global organization empowering women in technology and promoting diversity and inclusion

AnitaB.org - Non-profit promoting women in technology and computing, named after Anita Borg

CodeChops - Community-driven organization providing resources, mentorship, and networking for underrepresented groups including women

TechGirlz - Non-profit inspiring and educating middle school girls about technology

Women in Tech Network (WITN) - Global network providing networking, mentorship, and career development

The Ada Lovelace Institute - UK-based organization promoting women in tech and computing

Society of Women Engineers (SWE) - Strong presence in tech industry with resources for women in engineering and technology
