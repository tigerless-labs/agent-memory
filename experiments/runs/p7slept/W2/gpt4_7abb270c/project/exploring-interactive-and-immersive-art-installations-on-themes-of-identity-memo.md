Currently developing artistic practice focused on interactive and immersive art installations.

**Key themes:**
- Identity, memory, and the human experience
- Creating sense of empathy and connection with audiences

**Installation concepts in development:**

1. **Sensory Environment Simulation**: Creating installations that recreate the sensation of being in a specific environment or memory through multi-sensory elements:
   - Sounds (birds chirping, leaves rustling)
   - Light
   - Texture
   - Scent (damp earth)
   - Example: simulating the experience of walking through a forest

2. **Collective Memory Installation**: Exploring how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts:
   - Incorporating found objects, images, and sounds
   - Drawing from different cultures and time periods
   - Creating sense of shared human experience

**Technologies and techniques of interest:**
- Spatial 3D audio (responds to viewer movement)
- Projection mapping
- Scent diffusion
- Haptic feedback (vibrating floors, walls, objects)
- Data visualization
- Augmented reality (overlaying historical images/sounds/texts)
- Interactive storytelling (branching narratives)
- Collaborative platforms for viewer contributions
- Unity (game engine)
- Max/MSP (visual programming)
- OpenFrameworks (open-source C++ library)
- Arduino (microcontroller)
