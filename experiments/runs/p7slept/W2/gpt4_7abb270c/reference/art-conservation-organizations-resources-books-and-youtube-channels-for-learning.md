## Organizations\n\n- **American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC)**: Professional organization with online courses, webinars, publications\n- **International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC)**: Prominent organization featuring articles, news, events\n- **Conservation Wiki**: Collaborative platform on conservation techniques, materials, best practices\n\n## YouTube Channels\n\n- Art Conservation\n- Conservation Framing\n- The Getty Conservation Institute\n\n## Recommended Books\n\n- "The Conservation of Art and Architecture" by James C. Y. Watt — comprehensive guide on principles and practices\n- "The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory" by Caroline K. Keck — foundational text on philosophy and ethics\n- "Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide" by John H. Stubbs — practical guide for objects, artwork, and architectural materials\n\n## Learning Opportunities\n\n- Local museums and galleries often offer workshops and classes\n- Online courses on Coursera, edX, and Udemy\n- Professional organizations offer workshops, conferences, training\n\n## Safety Resources\n\n- Work in well-ventilated areas when using chemicals\n- Use protective gear: gloves, goggles, masks\n- Follow proper handling procedures for all objects
