Interested in learning art conservation as a hobby, particularly in these areas:\n\n- **Adhesives and consolidants**: PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, acrylic polymers, epoxy resins, natural waxes, silanes, Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98\n- **Conservation papers**: Cotton paper, linen paper, rag paper, kozu paper (Japanese), gampi paper (Japanese); prioritizes acid-free, lignin-free, high alpha-cellulose content\n- **Mounting and framing techniques**: Hinging, window mounting, float mounting, conservation framing, reversible framing, specialized mounting for textiles/objects/Asian art\n- **Conservation materials properties**: Strength, flexibility, reversibility, chemical stability, UV resistance, water resistance\n\nEmphasis on reversibility, material compatibility, and safety protocols in all techniques.
