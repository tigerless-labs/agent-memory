## Tokyo Tower\n\n**Overview:**\n- Height: 332.6 meters (1,091 feet)\n- Iconic landmark inspired by Eiffel Tower but modern design\n- One of the world's tallest towers\n\n**What to See & Do:**\n- Multiple observation decks with panoramic 360° views\n- See Tokyo skyline, Imperial Palace, Meiji Shrine\n- Mount Fuji visible on clear days\n- Walk the stairs (alternative to elevator)\n- Wax museum\n- Pirate-themed play area\n- Guinness World Records museum\n- Souvenir shops\n- Restaurants and cafes\n\n**Tips:**\n- Visit early morning or evening to beat crowds\n- Buy tickets online in advance\n- Wear comfortable shoes\n- Bring snacks/water (limited options in tower)\n- Great for sunset or night views\n\n**Location:** Accessible from Shinjuku via short taxi ride or train transfer
