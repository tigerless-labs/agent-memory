Building a brand around the core ethos that when hiring software developers and building teams:

**Core philosophy:**
- Great human beings first, then great technologists second
- Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just amazing coders
- EQ should be valued more than IQ when working with software developers

**Content strategy pillars:**
- Blog posts on identifying key human traits in potential hires, building collaborative teams
- Podcasts/videos with industry experts on building successful teams
- Webinars/workshops with practical tips on team building and hiring
- E-books/guides on psychometric testing, evaluating soft skills, fostering collaboration
- Social media case studies and team member profiles embodying these values

**Brand positioning:**
Establish thought leadership as an expert in building effective teams by prioritizing human qualities alongside technical skills.
