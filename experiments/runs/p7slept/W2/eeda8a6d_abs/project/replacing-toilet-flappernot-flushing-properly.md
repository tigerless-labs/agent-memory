Purchased replacement flapper compatible with toilet model.

**Steps to replace:**
1. Turn off water supply (shut-off valve behind toilet, turn clockwise)
2. Flush to drain tank
3. Disconnect chain from flapper and flush handle
4. Remove old flapper from overflow tube (may need to wiggle)
5. Inspect and clean overflow tube area
6. Install new flapper, ensuring proper seat
7. Reconnect chain, adjust length as needed
8. Turn water supply back on (counterclockwise)
