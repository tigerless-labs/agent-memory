**Seating:** Left side of train from La Spezia to Levanto offers best views of villages and coastline

**Timing:** 
- Travel during off-peak hours to avoid crowds
- Consider afternoon/evening for sunset views over the sea

**At the villages:** Get off train at different villages to walk around and experience each one's unique beauty

**Planning:** Trains in area can be delayed, so plan day accordingly to avoid rushing

**What to bring:**
- Camera to capture coastline and village views
- Snacks and water (no on-board catering available)
