Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) biomanufacturing program: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/biomanufacturing/en

Government initiative focused on strengthening Canada's domestic biomanufacturing and life sciences industry, particularly for development and production of vaccines, therapeutics, and medicines. Accepts company proposals for growth support.
