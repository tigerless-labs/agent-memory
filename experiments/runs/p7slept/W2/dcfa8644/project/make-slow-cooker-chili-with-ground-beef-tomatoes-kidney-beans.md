## Planned Slow Cooker Chili

User planning to make a slow cooker chili with the following ingredients and method.

### Core Ingredients:
- Ground beef (user intends to brown with onions and garlic first)
- Canned tomatoes
- Kidney beans
- Chili powder
- Ground cumin
- Paprika (regular, not smoked, per user's preference)
- Salt and pepper
- Brown sugar (to balance acidity from tomatoes)

### Optional Additions (user considering):
- Bell peppers
- Jalapenos
- Extra diced onions
- Garlic
- Mushrooms
- Tomato paste
- Beer or beef broth

### Preferred Cooking Method:
1. Brown ground beef with onions and garlic in a skillet (5-7 minutes total, let develop crust)
2. Drain excess fat
3. Add browned mixture to slow cooker
4. Add canned tomatoes, kidney beans, and seasonings
5. Simmer in slow cooker for 6-8 hours

### Key Tips for This Recipe:
- Don't overcrowd when browning the beef
- Break up beef into small pieces during browning
- Let beef develop a crust before stirring (2-3 minutes)
- Cook until dark brown color
- Drain excess fat before adding to slow cooker
