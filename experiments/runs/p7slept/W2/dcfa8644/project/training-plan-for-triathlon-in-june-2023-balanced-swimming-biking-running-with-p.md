Committed to training for triathlon scheduled June 2023, starting February 2023.

**Training approach:** Balanced training across swimming, biking, running. Periodized training blocks with varying intensity. Incorporate brick workouts (two disciplines back-to-back). Focus on strengths and weakness areas. Maintain 3-4 training sessions per week with strength training for power/efficiency. Prioritize rest/recovery (7-9 hrs sleep).

**Accountability:** Use fitness app to track workouts. Set specific measurable goals. Monitor heart rate and perceived exertion. Consider working with triathlon coach. Celebrate small victories.

**Key principles:** Gradual progression to avoid injury. Flexibility to adjust plan. Listen to body. Build confidence through consistent training.
