**Recommended hot sauces for BBQ:**
- Frank's RedHot — classic sauce with nice balance of heat and tanginess
- Sriracha — spicy kick, especially good with sweet and smoky BBQ sauces
- Cholula — Mexican hot sauce from arbol peppers, slightly sweet and smoky heat
- Tabasco — classic option
- Louisiana Hot Sauce — classic option
- Valentina — classic option

**Spicy ingredients to add to BBQ sauce or marinades:**
- Diced jalapeños or serrano peppers — fresh heat
- Chipotle peppers in adobo sauce — smoked jalapeños with deep, smoky heat (use sparingly)
- Korean chili paste (gochujang) — fermented soybean paste with sweet, savory, and spicy flavor
- Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) — authentic Korean heat
- Korean chili flakes in marinades — pairs well with sweet and smoky flavors

**Tip:** Start with small amounts of spicy ingredients and taste as you go. Heat is easy to add but hard to remove.
