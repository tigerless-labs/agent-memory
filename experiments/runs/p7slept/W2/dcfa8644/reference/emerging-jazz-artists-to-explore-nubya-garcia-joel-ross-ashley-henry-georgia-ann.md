**Nubya Garcia** — British saxophonist/composer, London jazz scene, incorporates jazz, soul, afrobeat elements, known for improvisational skills and powerful tone

**Joel Ross** — American vibraphonist, innovative jazz composition/performance approach, debut album KingMaker (2019) on Blue Note Records, widely acclaimed

**Ashley Henry** — British pianist/composer, London jazz scene, blends jazz, hip hop, R&B elements, praised for unique sound and melodic sensibility

**Georgia Anne Muldrow** — American singer/songwriter/multi-instrumentalist, jazz and soul communities, incorporates jazz, funk, experimental music, known for soulful vocals and organic approach

**Marquis Hill** — Chicago-based trumpeter, innovative jazz composition/performance, released multiple albums on independent labels, collaborated with prominent jazz musicians

These artists distinguish themselves through willingness to experiment, blending influences from hip hop, R&B, and funk, and bringing fresh perspective to jazz.
