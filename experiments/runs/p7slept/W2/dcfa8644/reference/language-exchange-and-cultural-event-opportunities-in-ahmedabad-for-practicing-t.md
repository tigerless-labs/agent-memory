Options to find Telugu language exchange and cultural events in Ahmedabad:

**Language Exchange:**
- Ahmedabad Language Exchange: Meetup group organizing language exchange events, including Telugu
- Gujarat University's Language Department: May have language exchange programs or cultural events

**Cultural Resources:**
- Indian Institute of Sindhology: Dedicated to promoting Sindhi culture and language; may have events for other languages
- Local event calendars and newspapers: Keep track of cultural events, festivals, concerts during visit

These resources provide opportunities to practice Telugu language skills while meeting locals and experiencing cultural exchange.
