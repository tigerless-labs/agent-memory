Preparation guidance for cross-cultural exchange programs:

1. **Research the country and culture** - Learn about customs, traditions, history, and current events to have a more meaningful and respectful experience.

2. **Learn the language** - Learn some basics of the local language to communicate more effectively and show you care about their culture.

3. **Pack appropriately** - Pack clothes and items suitable for the local climate, culture, and activities.

4. **Be open-minded and flexible** - Living abroad can be challenging with new foods, social norms, and cultural expectations. Flexibility is essential.

5. **Develop relationships with locals** - Meet and develop relationships with locals to practice language skills, learn more about culture, and make friends.

6. **Stay safe** - Be aware of surroundings, follow local customs and laws, and use common sense.
