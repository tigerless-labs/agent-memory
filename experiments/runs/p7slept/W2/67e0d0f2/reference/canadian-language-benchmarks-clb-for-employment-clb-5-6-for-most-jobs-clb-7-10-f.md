**Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)** - Scale from 0 (beginner) to 12 (advanced proficiency)\n\n**CLB levels for employment**:\n- CLB 5-6: Basic fluency; everyday situations; limited vocabulary/grammar\n- CLB 6-7: Customer service, sales roles\n- CLB 7-8: Administrative, office work\n- CLB 5-6: Skilled trades, technical jobs\n- CLB 9-10+: Professional roles (healthcare, finance, law)\n\n**Language certificates/assessments**:\n- Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada (LINC)\n- International English Language Testing System (IELTS)\n- Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL)\n- Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program (CELPIP)\n\n**Strategies to improve English**:\n- Continue language classes (LINC programs)\n- Practice with native speakers (in-person or online)\n- Watch English TV shows/movies with subtitles\n- Listen to podcasts, radio, audiobooks\n- Read books, articles, news websites\n- Use language learning apps (Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone)\n\n**Job search resources**:\n- Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca) - lists jobs with language requirements\n- Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor\n- Settlement agencies provide job search support
