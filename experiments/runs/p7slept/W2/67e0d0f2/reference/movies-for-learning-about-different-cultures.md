Recommended movies for understanding different cultures and cross-cultural perspectives:

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** - Martial arts epic set in ancient China exploring themes of loyalty and honor.

2. **The Lives of Others** - German film set in East Berlin during the Cold War exploring surveillance, loyalty, and human connection.

3. **Lion** - True story of an Indian boy lost and adopted by an Australian family, journeying to find his birth family.

4. **The Motorcycle Diaries** - Based on Che Guevara's travel diaries exploring injustices faced by the poor across South America.

5. **City of God** - Brazilian film following characters in a violent favela exploring themes of poverty, violence, and survival.
