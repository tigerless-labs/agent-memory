Current desk lamp is too dim and causes headaches during work. Requirements for replacement lamp:
- **Brightness**: Minimum 500-800 lumens (higher preferred)
- **Color temperature**: Warmer tone (2700K-3000K preferred) to reduce blue light sensitivity
- **Priority features**: Brightness is the primary concern; adjustability (flexible arm, swivel head, tilting shade) is secondary
- **Nice-to-have features**: USB port or built-in USB hub for device charging/powering

**Context**: Looking to replace current lamp; considers desk lamp brightness a health/comfort issue due to headaches from inadequate lighting.
