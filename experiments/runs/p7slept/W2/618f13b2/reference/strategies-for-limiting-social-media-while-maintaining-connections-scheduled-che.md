## Key Strategies for Reducing Social Media Use\n\n1. **Set boundaries** — decide on specific times to check accounts\n2. **Use blockers** — apps like Freedom, SelfControl, StayFocusd\n3. **Remove apps from home screen** — reduce ease of mindless scrolling\n4. **Phone-free zones** — during meals, conversations, or time with others\n5. **Schedule social media checks** — instead of constant checking\n6. **Unfollow/mute accounts** — that trigger FOMO or negative feelings\n7. **Focus on meaningful interactions** — comments, DMs, video calls rather than passive scrolling\n8. **Alternative information sources** — newsletters, podcasts, news aggregators\n9. **Social media-free days** — designate regular breaks\n10. **Practice self-compassion** — remember that social media is a highlight reel\n\n## FOMO Reframing\n\nInstead of: "I'm missing out on fun" → "I'm choosing to focus on my own life and priorities"\nInstead of: "Everyone's having a better time" → "Social media is a highlight reel, not reality"\nInstead of: "I need to stay connected" → "I'm building meaningful connections through real interactions"
