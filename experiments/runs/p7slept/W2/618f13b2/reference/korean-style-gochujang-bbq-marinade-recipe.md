## Korean BBQ Marinade (1-2 lbs meat)

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons gochujang
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) — optional
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar — optional
- Black pepper to taste

**Process:** Blend all ingredients until smooth. Adjust to taste. Marinate meat in ziplock bag or dish for 2 hours minimum, or overnight (6-8 hours ideal).

**Variations:** Use honey/maple syrup/Korean rice syrup for sweetness. Add fish sauce, soybean paste, or mushroom ingredients for umami. Can marinate up to 48 hours for intense flavor.
