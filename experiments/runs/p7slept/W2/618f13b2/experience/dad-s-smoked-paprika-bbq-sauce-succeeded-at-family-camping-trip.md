During a family camping trip in April 2023, dad experimented with a new BBQ sauce recipe by adding smoked paprika to deepen the flavor. The sauce was used with grilled pork sausages and was a hit—everyone loved it. The meal also included grilled vegetables, potatoes, corn on the cob, and s'mores with homemade marshmallows made by mom.
