## Quinoa and Black Bean Salad\n\n### Ingredients\n- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed and drained\n- 2 cups water\n- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed\n- 1 red bell pepper, diced\n- 1 small red onion, diced\n- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro\n- 2 tablespoons olive oil\n- 1 tablespoon lime juice\n- Salt and pepper to taste\n\n### Instructions\n1. Cook the quinoa according to proper technique (2:1 water ratio, 15-20 minutes on medium heat)\n2. In a large bowl, combine the cooked quinoa, black beans, red bell pepper, and red onion\n3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil and lime juice\n4. Pour the dressing over the quinoa mixture and toss to combine\n5. Stir in the chopped cilantro and season with salt and pepper to taste\n\n**Notes:** High in protein (from quinoa and black beans), low in carbs (relatively), refreshing citrus flavoring. Great for meal prep and stores well in the fridge.
