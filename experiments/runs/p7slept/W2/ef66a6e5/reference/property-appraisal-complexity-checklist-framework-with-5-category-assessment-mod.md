Framework to determine whether property appraisals are complex and justify higher fees. 

Categories: (1) Location — remote/limited access, unique environmental features, crime/hazard rates, challenging street layout; (2) Property type and design — unusual/complex design, special features like pools/hot tubs/large decks, unique HVAC systems, outbuildings; (3) Age and condition — significantly older/newer than typical, extensive repairs, unusual wear/damage, major alterations; (4) Zoning and land use — unique zoning, floodplain/conservation area/historic district, complex regulations, variances; (5) Market conditions — rapidly appreciating/declining, vacation home concentration, high-end properties, economic trends, supply/demand factors.

Purpose: Help team consistently assess property characteristics, determine complexity, justify higher fees, provide accurate appraisals, reduce errors, and improve internal processes.
