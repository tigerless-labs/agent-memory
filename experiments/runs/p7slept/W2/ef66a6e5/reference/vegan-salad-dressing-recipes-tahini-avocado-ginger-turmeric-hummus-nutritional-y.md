## Healthy Vegan Salad Dressings\n\nAll low in sugar and unhealthy fats, high in healthy fats or protein.\n\n### Lemon-Tahini Dressing\n- Lemon juice, tahini, garlic, olive oil\n- Creamy, tangy, rich in healthy fats from tahini\n\n### Avocado-Lime Dressing\n- Ripe avocado, lime juice, cilantro, salt\n- Creamy, refreshing, packed with healthy fats\n\n### Ginger-Turmeric Vinaigrette\n- Apple cider vinegar, olive oil, grated ginger, turmeric powder\n- Zesty, anti-inflammatory benefits\n\n### Hummus-Based Dressing\n- Hummus, lemon juice, garlic, water (adjust for consistency)\n- Optional: chopped herbs (parsley, cilantro)\n- Creamy, protein-rich\n\n### Nutritional Yeast Cheese Sauce\n- Nutritional yeast, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil\n- Nutty, cheesy flavor, vegan substitute for cheese sauce\n\n### Miso-Ginger Dressing\n- Miso paste, apple cider vinegar, grated ginger, olive oil\n- Savory, umami-rich\n\n### Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette\n- Fresh orange or grapefruit juice, olive oil, chopped herbs (parsley, basil, dill)\n- Light, refreshing\n\n### Tips for Healthy Vegan Dressings\n- Use healthy oils: olive oil, avocado oil, or grapeseed oil\n- Sweeteners: dates, maple syrup, or coconut sugar (instead of refined sugar)\n- Use apple cider or balsamic vinegar instead of white vinegar\n- Experiment with spices/herbs for flavor instead of salt/sugar\n- Make from scratch to control ingredients and portions
