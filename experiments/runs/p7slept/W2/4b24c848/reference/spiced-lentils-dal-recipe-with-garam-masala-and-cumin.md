**Spiced Lentils (Dal)**

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 2 cups water
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, grated
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- Optional: cayenne pepper or red chili flakes

**Instructions:**
1. Heat 2 tbsp oil over medium heat
2. Sauté onion, garlic, ginger until onion is translucent
3. Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala; cook 1-2 min until fragrant
4. Add lentils and water; season with salt
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 20-25 min until tender
6. Blend to desired consistency (partial or full purée)
7. Adjust seasoning; add cayenne for heat if desired

**Variations:**
- Add bell peppers or carrots
- Use red/yellow lentils for different texture
- Add coconut milk or yogurt for creaminess
- Increase cayenne or chili flakes for spice
