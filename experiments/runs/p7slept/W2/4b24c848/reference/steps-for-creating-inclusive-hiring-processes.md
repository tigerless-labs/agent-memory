**Practical steps to overcome bias in hiring:**\n\n1. **Educate yourself** — Learn about different types of biases in hiring and recognize them in decision-making\n2. **Re-evaluate job descriptions** — Use inclusive language, focus on essential skills rather than demographic profiles\n3. **Use diverse sourcing methods** — Use diversity-focused job boards, community organizations, universities, recruitment events (not just referrals)\n4. **Implement unconscious bias training** — Provide training for everyone involved in hiring\n5. **Be transparent about the hiring process** — Clarify the application process, assessment criteria, interviewer names, and reference handling for all candidates\n\nThese steps help ensure equal opportunity for qualified candidates from diverse backgrounds.
