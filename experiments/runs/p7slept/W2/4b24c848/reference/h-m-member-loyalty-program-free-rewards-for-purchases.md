**H&M Member** is a free loyalty program that rewards customers for purchases and interactions with the brand.

Benefits:
- Exclusive offers and discounts
- Points earned for every purchase (online and in-store)
- Early access to sales and promotions before general public
- Birthday treats / special offers on birthday
- Personalized fashion recommendations and style inspiration

Sign-up methods:
- H&M website: click Member at top right corner, fill registration form
- H&M mobile app: sign up through the app
- In-store: ask a sales associate for help
