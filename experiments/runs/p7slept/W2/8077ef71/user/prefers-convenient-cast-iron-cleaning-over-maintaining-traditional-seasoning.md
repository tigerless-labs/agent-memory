Prioritizes ease and speed when cleaning cast iron pans over maintaining seasoning and rust-resistance. Willing to use soap or dishwasher if it simplifies cleaning, accepting that this will require more frequent re-seasoning or pan maintenance. Values practical convenience over traditional cast iron care methods.
