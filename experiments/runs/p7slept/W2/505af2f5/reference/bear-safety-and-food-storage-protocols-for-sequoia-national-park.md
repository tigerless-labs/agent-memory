Sequoia National Park has higher bear activity than Yosemite. Food storage methods: use bear-resistant containers (BRCs, available for rent at visitor centers), or hang food/trash 10 feet off ground and 4 feet from tree trunk, 100 yards from campsite. Store in airtight containers to minimize odors. Bear safety practices: make noise while hiking (talk, sing, wear bear bells), stay on designated trails, watch for tracks/scat/digging, keep clean campsite, carry bear spray and know how to use it. Contact park rangers for current bear activity updates in planned hiking areas. Sequoia requires more active bear precautions than Yosemite.
