Recommended trails in Sequoia National Park for scenic views similar to Yosemite's Vernal Falls: High Sierra Trail to Hamilton Lakes (12 miles round-trip, views of Hamilton Lakes and towering peaks), Alta Peak Trail (14 miles round-trip, challenging, 11,200 ft peak with views of Great Western Divide and Sierra Nevada), Marble Falls Trail (4 miles round-trip, waterfall similar to Vernal Falls), Big Trees Trail to Moro Rock (2 miles round-trip, giant sequoias and Moro Rock granite dome with mountain views), Congress Trail (2 miles round-trip, Congress Grove of giant sequoias). Recommended campgrounds: Lodgepole (year-round, reservable), Dorst Creek (mid-May to mid-October, reservable), Buckeye Flat (mid-May to mid-October, first-come/first-served), Potwisha (year-round, first-come/first-served). Peak season reservations recommended 6 months in advance via recreation.gov.
