Car rental companies near Sequoia National Park: Enterprise in Visalia (closest to park, offers SUVs/vans), National at Fresno Yosemite International Airport (about 1.5 hours away, offers hybrid/electric options), Budget in Visalia. Tips for navigating Sequoia's mountain roads: check road conditions before entry, drive slowly on narrow/winding roads, fill up gas in Visalia or at Ash Mountain Visitor Center (limited gas in park), be prepared for high altitude effects on vehicle performance, consider 4WD for remote areas, bring map and GPS, watch for wildlife especially at dawn/dusk.
