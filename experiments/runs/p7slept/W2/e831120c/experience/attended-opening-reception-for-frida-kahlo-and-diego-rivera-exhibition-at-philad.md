Visited the Philadelphia Museum of Art on May 21, 2023 for the opening reception of the 'Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera' exhibition featuring works from the Jacques and Natasha Gelman Collection. Was particularly struck by the stunning works on display and spent time learning about both artists' lives and artistic styles.
