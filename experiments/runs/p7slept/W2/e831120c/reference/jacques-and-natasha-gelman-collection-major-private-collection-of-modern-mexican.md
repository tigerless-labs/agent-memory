The Jacques and Natasha Gelman Collection is an extraordinary collection of modern and contemporary art, particularly strong in Mexican artists. The Gelmans were close friends and patrons of Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera. Their collection provides important insights into Frida's artistic evolution and her relationship with Diego. Featured at Philadelphia Museum of Art's 'Frida Kahlo and Diego Rivera' exhibition (May 2023).
