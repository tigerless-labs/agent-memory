Ten whole grain bread recipes recommended:

1. **Multigrain Bread with Kamut and Rye** — Nutty Kamut + earthy rye + whole wheat, oats, barley for complex flavor

2. **Spelt and Walnut Bread** — Ancient grain spelt paired with walnuts, lovely crumb and slightly sweet flavor. *User wants to try this.*

3. **Whole Wheat Ciabatta with Oats and Sunflower Seeds** — Italian-style, hearty crusty loaf good for sandwiches/toast

4. **Rye and Caraway Seed Bread** — Traditional Scandinavian, distinctive caraway seed flavor

5. **Ancient Grain Bread with Quinoa and Amaranth** — Protein-rich combination with whole wheat, Kamut, spelt

6. **100% Whole Wheat Sourdough** — Uses only whole wheat flour and natural starter, tangy and chewy

7. **Bulgur and Herbs Bread** — Middle Eastern-inspired with bulgur, parsley, thyme, rosemary

8. **Oatmeal and Honey Bread** — Sweet bread with rolled oats and honey, soft and comforting for toast

9. **Einkorn and Sesame Seed Bread** — Ancient grain einkorn with nutty sesame seeds, aromatic with soft tender crumb

10. **Seven-Grain Bread** — Combines wheat, rye, oats, barley, Kamut, spelt, triticale for complex satisfying loaf
