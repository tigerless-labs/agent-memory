Popular Fresno diners for breakfast:

- **Max's Bistro & Bar**: Generous portions, creative breakfast dishes, Max's Breakfast burrito
- **The Breakfast Club**: Retro-style, breakfast tacos, waffle sandwiches, omelets
- **Pete's Breakfast House**: 30+ year institution, giant pancakes, omelets
- **Frank's Place**: No-frills, since 1947, Frank's Favorite breakfast plate
- **Denny's**: Chain with multiple Fresno locations
