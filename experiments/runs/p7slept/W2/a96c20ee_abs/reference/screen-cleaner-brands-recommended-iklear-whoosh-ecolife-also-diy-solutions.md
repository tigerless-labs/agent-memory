**Recommended screen cleaner brands:**
- iKlear
- Whoosh!
- Ecolife

**Recommended solution types:**
- Water-based screen cleaning solutions (avoid harsh chemicals, ammonia, alcohol)
- Pre-moistened screen cleaning wipes
- DIY solutions: 50/50 water + white vinegar, or diluted dish soap

**Selection criteria:**
- Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage screen coating
- Check for streak-free formulas
- Verify safety for screen material (glass, OLED, etc.)
- Read user reviews before purchasing
