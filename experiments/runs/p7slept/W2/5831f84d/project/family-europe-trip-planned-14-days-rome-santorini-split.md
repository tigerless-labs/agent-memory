**Trip plan:** 14-day family trip with parents and siblings, all adults.

**Itinerary:**
- Rome (Days 1-3): Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican City, neighborhoods
- Santorini (Days 4-6): Whitewashed houses, black sand beaches, sunset viewing, Greek tavernas  
- Split (Days 7-14): Diocletian's Palace, Mestrovic Gallery, Plitvice Lakes day trip

**Preferences:**
- Mix of culture, history, and outdoor activities
- Moderate budget
- Relaxed pace with time to explore deeply
- Parents keen on Italy/Spain elements; user and siblings interested in Greece/Croatia
- Prefer to travel in shoulder season (April-May or September-October) to avoid crowds and high prices

**Budget tips adopted:**
- Vacation rentals or apartments instead of hotels (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com)
- Book flights in advance; fly Rome to Split to avoid backtracking
- Use public transportation, taxis, or shuttles rather than rental car
- Eat at local tavernas; buy groceries for some meals
- In Santorini: visit Oia daytime, stay in Imerovigli for better value
- In Split: explore Diocletian's Palace independently; check Mestrovic Gallery free Sundays

**Packing essentials:** Lightweight breathable clothing, comfortable walking shoes, light jacket, universal power adapter (Type F/E/C), reusable water bottle, sunscreen, portable power bank, travel insurance documents, basic first-aid kit
