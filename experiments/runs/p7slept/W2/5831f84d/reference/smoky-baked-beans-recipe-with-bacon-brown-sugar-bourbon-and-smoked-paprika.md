**Recipe Details:**\n- Servings: 8-10\n- Key ingredients: 1 lb dried navy beans, 1 lb smoked bacon, 1 cup ketchup, 1/4 cup brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, smoked paprika, optional cayenne pepper and bourbon\n- Cooking: Bake at 250°F for 6-7 hours total (covered 6 hours, uncovered 30 min-1 hour)\n- Bacon cooked first, then onion and garlic sautéed in drippings, beans and spices added\n- Can be made in slow cooker on low for 8-10 hours\n- Optional additions: chipotle peppers, diced bell peppers, jalapeños, hot dogs\n\nPerfect for summer cookouts, pairs well with grilled meats.
