Reasonable workplace accommodations to support Muslim employees:

**Prayer & Scheduling**
- Designate quiet prayer rooms or areas
- Flexible scheduling for prayer times, especially during Ramadan
- Friday prayer accommodations (longer lunch breaks or on-site prayer space)

**Dietary**
- Halal food options in cafeteria
- Allow personal food; prevent cross-contamination
- Ramadan-specific: flexible scheduling, reduced workload, additional breaks

**Dress & Facilities**
- Allow hijabs, abayas, modest clothing per health/safety standards
- Allow beards
- Gender-segregated facilities (restrooms, locker rooms)

**Recognition & Time Off**
- Recognize Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha (day off or flexible scheduling)
- Allow time off for Hajj or Umrah pilgrimages

**Inclusion**
- Cultural competency training for all employees
- Inclusive language (avoid scheduling during prayer times/Ramadan)
- Employee resource group (ERG) for Muslim employees

**Implementation**: Consult with Muslim employees, communicate transparently, ensure no undue business hardship, review regularly.
