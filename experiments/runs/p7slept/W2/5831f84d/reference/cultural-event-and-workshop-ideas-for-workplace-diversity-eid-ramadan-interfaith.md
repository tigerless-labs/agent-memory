Event and workshop ideas for workplace cultural programming:

**Ramadan & Eid Events**
- Iftar dinners during Ramadan (breaking of the fast)
- Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha celebrations
- Send Eid Mubarak messages to Muslim employees
- Small gifts (dates, halal restaurant gift cards)

**Cultural & Educational**
- Muslim Cultural Competency Training sessions
- Islamic art, calligraphy, or architecture workshops
- Traditional Muslim dishes potluck or cooking demonstrations
- Employee storytelling events (Muslim employees share personal experiences)

**Dialogue & Community**
- Interfaith panels or discussions with speakers from multiple faiths
- Interfaith dialogue/panel on Muslim experiences
- Faith-based employee resource groups

**Tips for Interfaith Dialogue**
- Create safe, private spaces
- Establish ground rules for respect and active listening
- Encourage personal storytelling to build empathy
- Use 'I' statements; avoid assumptions
- Avoid tokenization or stereotyping
- Ensure diverse representation
- Be aware of power dynamics
- Follow up on commitments

**Avoid**: Stereotyping, tokenization, insensitive language, intrusive questions, lack of representation, power imbalances.
