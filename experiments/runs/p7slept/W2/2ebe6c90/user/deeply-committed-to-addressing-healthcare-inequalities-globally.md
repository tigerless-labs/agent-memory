Prioritizes healthcare equity and believes systemic change is essential to address disparities. Advocates for:
- Prioritizing healthcare funding for marginalized communities
- Addressing root causes: poverty, discrimination, lack of education
- Investing in healthcare infrastructure in underprivileged areas
- Universal healthcare systems with equitable access regardless of socioeconomic status

Frustrated that governments often take action only during crises like COVID-19 rather than proactively. Believes bold, innovative approaches are needed to tackle long-standing healthcare inequalities in vulnerable communities.
