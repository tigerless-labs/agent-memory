Eco-Lodges: Lapa Rios (Osa Peninsula), Monteverde Lodge, Arenal Observatory Lodge, Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero), El Remanso Lodge (Osa). Tour Operators: Costa Rica Expeditions, Osa Conservation, Tortuguero Canals Tours, Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve, Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours. Look for CST (Certification for Sustainable Tourism) certification from Costa Rican Tourism Board (ICT). Selection criteria: environmental policies, community engagement, local conservation support, guest reviews.
