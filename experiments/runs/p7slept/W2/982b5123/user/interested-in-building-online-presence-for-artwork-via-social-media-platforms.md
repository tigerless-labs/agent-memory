Wants to promote artwork and connect with fellow artists on multiple platforms.

**Platforms of interest:**
- Tumblr (primary focus in conversation)
- Instagram
- Behance (portfolio for creatives)
- DeviantArt (art community with feedback/sales)
- Pinterest (visual curation)
- ArtStation (entertainment industry portfolio)

**Key strategies discussed:**
- Post regularly and maintain consistent style/brand
- Use relevant hashtags (#art, #illustration, #painting, etc.)
- Follow and engage with other artists
- Share behind-the-scenes/process content
- Collaborate with other artists
- Use high-quality visuals
- Respond to comments/messages
- Tailor content to each platform's audience

**Attitude toward feedback:**
- Values constructive criticism
- Willing to learn from feedback
- Understands importance of handling negative comments professionally
- Sees criticism as opportunity to improve
