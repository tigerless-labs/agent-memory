## Aphid Control Methods\n\n**Chemical/Organic Options:**\n- Neem oil (dilute 2-4 tsp per quart water; spray thoroughly; repeat every 7-10 days; apply early morning/evening to avoid phytotoxicity)\n- Insecticidal soap (gentle, non-toxic)\n- Horticultural oil (refined mineral oil that suffocates aphids)\n- Pyrethrin (natural pesticide from chrysanthemum flowers)\n- Diatomaceous earth (fossilized algae powder; dehydrates aphids)\n\n**Biological Control:**\n- Ladybugs/lady beetles (especially effective; release 1,000-2,000 for large infestations)\n- Lacewings (generalist predators; use eggs/larvae form)\n- Parasitic wasps (e.g., Aphidius ervi)\n- Hoverflies/flower flies\n\n**Physical/Cultural:**\n- Fine-mesh screens\n- Row covers\n- Remove weeds (can harbor aphids)\n- Improve air circulation\n- Water at base only (avoid wetting leaves)\n\n**Plant-Based Strategies:**\n- Plant aphid-resistant pepper/tomato varieties\n- Use companion plants: basil, marigold, chives, oregano, parsley, dill, borage, nasturtium, radish
