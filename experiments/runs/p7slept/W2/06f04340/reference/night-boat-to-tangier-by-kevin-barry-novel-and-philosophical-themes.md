Novel by Kevin Barry featuring two aging Irish gangsters (Maurice Hearne, Charlie Redmond) waiting for a boat to Tangier to find Maurice's missing daughter. Explores philosophical themes: sin and guilt from criminal past, fate and inescapability of consequences, tragedy and loss, redemption attempts, false hope, failure, and existential confrontation with mortality and meaninglessness.
