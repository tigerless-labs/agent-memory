**Bike Registration Platforms:**

BikeIndex
- Free online bike registration platform
- Searchable database for police and bike shops
- Helps with recovery if bike is stolen
- URL: bikeindex.org

National Bike Registry (NBR)
- Non-profit organization
- National database for registered bikes
- Police can search database for recovery

**Route Planning & Mapping Tools:**

Google Maps
- Basic route planning and distance estimation

MapMyRide
- Dedicated cycling route planning
- Shows elevation gain and terrain

Ride with GPS
- Detailed route planning for cyclists
- Community features

**Commute Route Criteria:**

- Designated bike lanes and bike paths
- Low-traffic roads and bike boulevards
- Well-lit routes (important for early morning/evening commutes)
- Minimal traffic signals and stops for efficiency
- Avoid peak traffic hours on busy roads
- Test ride recommended before committing to regular use

**Registration Information Needed:**
- Bike make, model, and serial number
- Color and distinctive features
- Photo of bike
- Contact information
