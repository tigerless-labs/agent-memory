Quick and healthy breakfast options (all under 15 minutes to prepare):

1. **Avocado Toast** (5 min)
   - Toast whole-grain bread
   - Mash avocado and spread on toast
   - Add sliced egg, salt, and pepper

2. **Yogurt Parfait** (5 min)
   - Layer Greek yogurt, fresh berries, and granola
   - Optional: chia seeds or honey

3. **Smoothie** (5 min)
   - Blend fruits, yogurt, and milk (dairy or non-dairy)

4. **Peanut Butter Banana Toast** (3 min)
   - Toast whole-grain bread
   - Spread with peanut butter
   - Top with banana slices

5. **Overnight Oats** (5 min prep night before)
   - Mix rolled oats, milk, nuts, seeds, or fruit in jar
   - Ready to eat in the morning

6. **Cottage Cheese and Fruit** (5 min)
   - Mix cottage cheese with berries or sliced peaches

7. **Green Smoothie Bowl** (10 min)
   - Blend spinach, avocado, banana, and milk
   - Top with granola, nuts, and fresh fruit

8. **Hard-Boiled Eggs** (5 min, prep night before)
   - Boil eggs the night before
   - Keep refrigerated for quick protein-packed breakfast
