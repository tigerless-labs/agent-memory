Bourbon glaze ingredients:
- 1/2 cup Bourbon Whiskey (higher-proof ~90-100 proof preferred)
- 1/4 cup Brown Sugar
- 2 tablespoons Honey
- 2 tablespoons Dijon Mustard
- 1 tablespoon Soy Sauce (optional)
- 1 teaspoon Garlic Powder (optional)
- Salt and pepper

Tips:
- Higher-proof bourbon gives more intense flavor and thicker glaze
- Balance sweetness by adjusting sugar and honey
- Reduce glaze over low heat for thicker consistency
- Brush on salmon during last 10-15 minutes of cooking
- Bake at 400°F for 12-15 minutes per inch of thickness

Dijon mustard adds tanginess, soy sauce adds umami depth.
