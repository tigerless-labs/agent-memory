Suggested gin-based cocktail recipes using homegrown or fresh herbs:

**Cucumber Cocktails:**
- Cucumber Gimlet: Hendrick's gin, lime, simple syrup, cucumber syrup
- Pimm's Cup with Cucumber Twist: Hendrick's, Pimm's No. 1, lemon-lime soda, cucumber syrup
- Cucumber Basil Martini: Hendrick's, lime, simple syrup, basil syrup
- Hendrick's Cucumber Collins: Hendrick's, lemon, simple syrup, cucumber syrup, soda water

**Herb-Forward Cocktails:**
- Rosemary Gin Spritz: Gin, Prosecco, rosemary syrup, lemon, soda water
- Basil Gimlet: Gin, lime, simple syrup, basil syrup

All recipes include instructions for making fresh herb syrups (10-15 minute steep time).
