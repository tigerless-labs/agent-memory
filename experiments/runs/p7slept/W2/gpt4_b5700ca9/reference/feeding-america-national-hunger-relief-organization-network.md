**Feeding America** is the largest hunger-relief organization in the US, founded 1979, headquartered in Chicago.\n\n- Network: 200 food banks across the US\n- Reach: Serves 46+ million people through 60,000+ food pantries and meal programs\n- Services: Food distribution, fundraising support, advocacy, nutrition programs, child/senior hunger initiatives, disaster relief\n- Website: feedingamerica.org (search by zip code to find local food bank)\n\nSupports local food banks through food sourcing, distribution, training, and advocacy on hunger-related policies.
