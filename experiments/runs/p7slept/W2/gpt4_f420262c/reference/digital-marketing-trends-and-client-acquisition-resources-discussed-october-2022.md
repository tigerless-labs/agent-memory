Key trends to monitor for Sparkle Media (as of Oct 2022):
1. AI and Machine Learning for personalization and automation
2. Voice search optimization (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant)
3. Video marketing and live streaming
4. Influencer marketing (especially micro-influencers 10k-100k followers)
5. Customer experience (CX) across touchpoints
6. Account-Based Marketing (ABM)
7. Social commerce (Instagram Shopping, Facebook Marketplace)
8. Content marketing and content hubs
9. Mobile-first strategy
10. Data-driven marketing with analytics tools
11. Podcast advertising
12. Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR/AR)
13. Customer Data Platforms (CDPs)
14. Omnichannel marketing
15. Sustainability and social responsibility marketing

Client acquisition resources:
- Upwork, Clutch, Capterra, Agency Spy (agency directories)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Referrals and word-of-mouth
- Industry events and conferences
- Online communities (Reddit r/digitalmarketing, Quora, Facebook groups)
- Google My Business
- Cold emailing (Hunter, Email Hunter)
- Local listings (Yelp, Bing Places)
- Case studies and testimonials
- Social media marketing
- Guest blogging
- Industry-specific directories
