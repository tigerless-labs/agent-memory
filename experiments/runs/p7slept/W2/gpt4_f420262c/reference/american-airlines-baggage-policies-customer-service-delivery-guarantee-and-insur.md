Baggage Policy:
- First checked bag: $30 online, $40 at airport (Main Cabin); Free (Premium Economy/Business/First)
- Second checked bag: $40 online, $50 at airport  
- Checked bag max: 62 inches combined, 50 lbs
- Carry-on max: 22 x 14 x 9 inches
- Basic Economy doesn't allow free checked bags

Customer Service:
- Phone: 1-800-433-7300 (reservations)
- Online support via website chat, email, feedback forms
- Social media: Twitter @AmericanAir, Facebook
- Ratings: Skytrax 3/5, TripAdvisor 3.5/5, Consumer Reports 64/100
- Flight disruption response: rebooking, compensation (vouchers/hotel/meals)

Baggage Delivery Guarantee:
- Delayed = not at claim within 4 hours of arrival
- Guarantee: delivery within 24 hours or $25 travel voucher
- Lost baggage: reimbursement up to $3,500

Baggage Insurance Options:
- Trip Insurance: through Allianz Global Assistance
- Baggage Protection: $1,000 per bag, $12-20 per person per trip
- Covers loss, damage, delayed bag essentials
- Claim requires documentation (receipts, proof of loss)
