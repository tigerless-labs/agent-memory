## Character Development Approaches:\n\n**In Evernote:**\n- Custom character template with: traits, backstory, motivations, goals, conflicts, relationships\n- Separate note per character with linked research and inspiration\n- Character arc tracking for emotional journey visualization\n\n**In Trello:**\n- Separate character board with lists for main/supporting characters and antagonists\n- Character cards with name, role, motivations, goals\n- Relationship cards tracking interactions and dynamics\n\n**Brainstorming Techniques:**\n1. Character interviews — imagine interviewing your character\n2. Research-based profiles — study real people and cultures\n3. Character mapping — visualize relationships and conflicts\n4. Scenario brainstorming — imagine challenging situations and reactions
