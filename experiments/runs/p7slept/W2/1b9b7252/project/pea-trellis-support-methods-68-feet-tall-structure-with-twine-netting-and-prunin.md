Growing peas on trellis (spring 2023 crop rotation plan).

**Trellis specifications:**
- 6–8 feet tall minimum
- Options: traditional trellis, teepee, or DIY bamboo + twine
- Must be securely anchored to ground

**Support methods:**
- Twine/string tied to trellis and pea stems
- Pea sticks (thin branches/bamboo skewers) at base  
- Netting (nylon or polypropylene mesh) draped over trellis
- Soft ties (cloth strips) to gently secure stems; avoid wire
- Prune regularly to encourage bushy growth; remove weak/spindly growth

**Growing conditions:** Full sun, good air circulation, regular watering (avoid overwatering).
