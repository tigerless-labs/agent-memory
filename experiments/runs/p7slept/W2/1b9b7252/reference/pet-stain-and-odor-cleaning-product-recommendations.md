## Enzyme-based cleaners\n- Nature's Miracle\n- Biokleen Bac-Out Stain & Odor Remover\n- Earthbath Enzyme-Based Stain & Odor Remover\n\n## Natural alternatives\n- Baking soda and vinegar paste\n- Hydrogen peroxide (equal parts with water)\n\n## Odor-neutralizing products\n- Zero Odor Pet Odor Eliminator\n- Outward Hound Oko Cat & Dog Odor Eliminator\n\n## Best practices\n- Act quickly on fresh stains\n- Blot, don't rub (work from outside in)\n- Rinse thoroughly after cleaning\n- Dry the area to prevent bacteria growth\n- Test product on small area first
