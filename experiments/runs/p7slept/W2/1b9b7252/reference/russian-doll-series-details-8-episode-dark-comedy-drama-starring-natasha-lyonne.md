**Series:** Russian Doll

**Format:** 8 episodes, 25-30 minutes each (Season 1)

**Genre:** Dark comedy-drama with existential themes

**Premise:** Nadia Vulvokov (played by Natasha Lyonne), a game developer, keeps reliving the same night at her 36th birthday party, dying and waking up repeatedly in a bathroom—similar to Groundhog Day but with deeper existential and emotional weight.

**Tone:** Darkly comedic and introspective, witty but tackling heavy topics: depression, anxiety, existential crisis, mortality, identity, trauma, and human connection.

**Themes:** Mortality and meaning of life, identity and self-awareness, human connection and relationships, consequences of actions.

**Production:** Distinct visual style with muted color palette; haunting, atmospheric score blending indie rock and electronic music.

**Performance:** Natasha Lyonne delivers excellent performance with perfect blend of humor, vulnerability, and intensity.

**Why it was recommended:** Similar to Stranger Things in exploring supernatural mysteries with emotional depth, good for viewers interested in thoughtful, character-driven narratives.
