## Slow-feeder bowls\n- **Outward Hound Brick Puzzle Feeder**: Brick design separates food, makes cat work for meal\n- **Omega Paw Tricky Treat Ball**: Ball design; requires rolling to dispense food\n- **Pet Zone Treat Wobbler**: Wobbles and moves as cat eats\n\n## Puzzle feeders\n- **Kong Wobbler**: Requires rolling, pushing, and problem-solving\n- **Nina Ottosson Dog Brick Puzzle**: Multiple compartments (can be used for cats)\n- **PetSafe SlimCat Interactive Toy and Feeder**: Multiple levels and hidden compartments\n\n## Introduction tips for kittens\n- Start with easy mode to build confidence\n- Mix with regular food bowl initially\n- Gradually increase difficulty\n- Supervise and encourage exploration\n- Use smaller kibble or favorite treats\n- Be patient; may take time to adjust\n- Benefits: slows eating pace, prevents gorging, provides mental stimulation
