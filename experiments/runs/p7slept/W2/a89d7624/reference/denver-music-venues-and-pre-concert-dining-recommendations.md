**Music Venues & Festivals:**
- Red Rocks Park and Amphitheater (iconic, diverse acts)
- The Ogden Theatre (indie, rock, electronic)
- The Bluebird Theater (historic, diverse lineup)
- The Fillmore Auditorium (rock, hip-hop)
- Larimer Lounge (new talent, cozy setting)
- Festivals: Grandoozy, Denver Folk Festival, Denver Jazz Festival, Westword Music Showcase, Riot Fest Denver

**BBQ Near Red Rocks:**
- GQue BBQ (Golden, 10-15 min drive)
- Dinosaur Bar-B-Que (Lakewood, 20-25 min)
- Moe's Original BBQ (Wheat Ridge, 15-20 min)
- Big B's Delicious BBQ (Arvada, 20-25 min)
- Wayne's Smoke Shack (Morrison, food truck, few minutes away)

**Dining Near Red Rocks:**
- The Ship Rock Grille (across street, outdoor patio with red rock views, heated patio, pet-friendly)
- The Village Tavern (5 min walk, pub fare)
- Morrison Inn (10 min walk, American comfort food)
