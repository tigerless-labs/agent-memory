## Core Exercises\n- Plank: 30-60 seconds, 3-4 sets\n- Russian twists: 3 sets of 12-15 reps with medicine ball or weight plate\n- Leg raises: 3 sets of 12-15 reps\n- Bicycle crunches: 3 sets of 12-15 reps\n\n## Leg Exercises\n- Squats: 3 sets of 12-15 reps\n- Lunges: 3 sets of 12-15 reps per leg\n- Calf raises: 3 sets of 12-15 reps\n- Deadlifts: 3 sets of 8-10 reps\n\nFor triathlon training: 2 sessions per week, focus on functional strength, endurance, and flexibility.
