## Healthier Dessert Ideas (Low Sugar)

### Homemade Recipes
- **Chocolate avocado mousse** — made with ripe avocados, unsweetened cocoa powder, milk, and honey
- **Fruit sorbet** — frozen fruit (berries or melon) blended with a little honey or agave syrup
- **Apple crisp** — fresh apples, oatmeal, almond flour, and a touch of maple syrup
- **Chia pudding** — chia seeds soaked in milk overnight with honey or maple syrup, topped with fresh fruit or nuts
- **Chocolate-covered strawberries** — strawberries dipped in melted dark chocolate
- **Baked fruit with honey and cinnamon** — apple, pear, or peach baked until soft and caramelized
- **Greek yogurt parfait** — layered Greek yogurt with fresh fruit and granola

### Store-Bought Low-Sugar Snacks
- Fresh fruit (apples, bananas, oranges, berries)
- Nut butter packs (almond or peanut butter single-serve)
- Roasted nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, sunflower seeds)
- Protein bars (less than 5g added sugar, whole food ingredients)
- Rice cakes (topped with nut butter or avocado)
- Dark chocolate (70% cacao or higher)

## Strategies for Managing Sugar Cravings
- Read nutrition labels before buying
- Practice portion control rather than complete elimination
- Gradually reduce sugar intake so taste buds adjust
- Stay hydrated—water reduces craving intensity
- Get adequate sleep—fatigue triggers sugar cravings
- Use spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger) instead of sugar for flavor
- When eating out: research menu beforehand, ask for modifications, choose grilled/steamed options
- Bring your own snacks to social gatherings
