## Abstract Expressionist Artists (Similar to Pollock & Rothko)

1. **Willem de Kooning** — Dutch-American; bold, expressive brushstrokes; vibrant color palette
2. **Franz Kline** — American; large scale; bold colors; gestural brushstrokes; sense of energy and spontaneity
3. **Clyfford Still** — American; large abstract forms; muted color palette; explores themes of sublime and unknown
4. **Ad Reinhardt** — contemporaneous with Rothko; abstract expressionist
5. **Barnett Newman** — contemporaneous with Rothko; abstract expressionist
6. **Robert Motherwell** — contemporaneous; abstract expressionist

## Related Artist
