## User Interest in Art Conservation

On **February 15, 2023**, the user participated in a behind-the-scenes tour of the Museum of History's conservation lab. During the tour, they:
- Saw meticulous artifact preservation processes
- Tried their hand at basic conservation techniques
- Were impressed by the dedication required to preserve cultural heritage

The user wants to pursue art conservation as a hobby and is interested in learning about:
1. Basic techniques and materials
2. Different adhesives and consolidants (PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98, etc.)
3. Conservation-grade papers (cotton, linen, rag, kozu, gampi paper)
4. Mounting and framing techniques (hinging, window mounting, float mounting, conservation framing)
5. Properties to consider: acid-free, lignin-free, alpha-cellulose content, reversibility
