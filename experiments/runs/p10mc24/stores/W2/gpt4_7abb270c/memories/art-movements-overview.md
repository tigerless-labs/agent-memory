## Art Movements and Representative Artists

1. **Impressionism** — Claude Monet, Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Mary Cassatt; dreamy, light-filled paintings; fleeting moments of modern life
2. **Cubism** — Pablo Picasso, Georges Braque; geometric shapes; reassemble objects innovatively
3. **Surrealism** — Salvador Dalí, René Magritte, Max Ernst; irrational; blur reality and fantasy
4. **Pop Art** — Andy Warhol, Roy Lichtenstein, Jasper Johns; popular culture, consumerism; bold colors, graphic imagery
5. **Fauvism** — Henri Matisse, André Derain, Maurice de Vlaminck; vibrant, expressive colors; energetic brushstrokes; evoke emotions
6. **Minimalism** — Donald Judd, Dan Flavin, Agnes Martin; simplicity, clean lines; calm and contemplation
7. **Baroque** — Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Vermeer; dramatic lighting; intense emotions; exquisite detail
8. **Art Nouveau** — Alphonse Mucha, Gustav Klimt, Charles Rennie Mackintosh; sinuous, organic forms; ornate patterns; luxury and elegance
