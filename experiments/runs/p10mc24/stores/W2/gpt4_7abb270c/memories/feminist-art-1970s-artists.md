## Notable Feminist Artists (1970s)

1. **Judy Chicago** — "The Dinner Party"; explores gender, identity, female experience
2. **Miriam Schapiro** — fabric, textiles; challenges traditional notions of art and femininity
3. **Carolee Schneemann** — multi-disciplinary (performance, film, installation); explores gender, sexuality, body
4. **Guerrilla Girls** — anonymous collective; provocative, humorous tactics; challenged male-dominated art world
5. **Barbara Kruger** — bold, graphic images; text-based works; critiques consumer culture and gender roles
6. **Cindy Sherman** — pioneering photographer/filmmaker; explores construction of identity, gender, representation
7. **Laurie Anderson** — performance artist, musician, filmmaker; storytelling, technology, multimedia; explores identity, politics, human experience

## Institution
- **Feminist Art Program** — founded 1970 at California Institute of the Arts; one of first of its kind; platform for women artists to explore feminist themes
