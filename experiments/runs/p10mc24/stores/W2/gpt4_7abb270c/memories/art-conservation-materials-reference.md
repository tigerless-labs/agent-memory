## Art Conservation Materials & Techniques

### Adhesives

**Acrylic Polymers (water/solvent-based)**
- Products: PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV
- Use: bonding paper, textiles, organic materials
- Properties: flexible, good chemical stability

**Animal Glues**
- Types: hide glue, bone glue
- Use: bonding wood, paper, organic materials
- Properties: strong, traditional, reversible

**Cellulose-Based Adhesives**
- Products: rice starch paste, wheat starch paste
- Use: paper conservation and repair
- Properties: acid-free, reversible

**Epoxy Resins**
- Products: Araldite, Epon
- Use: bonding metal, glass, inorganic materials
- Properties: two-part, strong permanent bond

**Natural Waxes**
- Types: beeswax, paraffin wax
- Use: consolidating ceramics, stone, fragile/porous materials
- Properties: reversible, good for fragile items

### Consolidants

- **Paraloid B-72**: for stone, ceramics, glass
- **Butvar B-98**: for fragile/powdery materials (wall paintings, textile fragments)
- **Parylene**: vapor-deposited, for metal/ceramics/inorganic materials
- **Silanes**: react with material surface, for stone/ceramics/glass
- **Acrylic polymers**: for wall paintings, sculptures

### Coatings

**Varnishes**
- Types: dammar varnish, acrylic varnish
- Properties: UV resistance, protects from moisture/pollutants

**Lacquers**
- Properties: protective layer, some may not be reversible

**Waxes**
- Types: beeswax, paraffin wax
- Properties: water-resistant, reversible

**Acrylic Polymers**
- Products: Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV
- Properties: UV resistant, chemical resistant

### Conservation Papers

**Cotton Paper**
- Properties: strong, durable, acid-resistant
- Use: archival-quality printmaking paper, conservation mounting boards

**Linen Paper**
- Properties: strong, lightweight, tear-resistant
- Use: conservation of historical documents, prints, drawings

**Rag Paper** (cotton or linen)
- Properties: strong, durable, acid-free
- Use: conservation of watercolors, prints, drawings

**Kozu Paper** (Japanese, mulberry bark)
- Properties: strong, translucent, aging-resistant
- Use: conservation of Asian artworks, scrolls, screens

**Gampi Paper** (Japanese, gampi plant)
- Properties: strong, lightweight, aging-resistant
- Use: conservation of Asian prints and drawings

**Paper Properties to Prioritize:**
- Acid-free or neutral pH (~7.0)
- Low lignin content (prevents yellowing/brittleness)
- High alpha-cellulose content (stronger, more durable)
- Surface texture (smooth for mounting; textured for artistic techniques)
- Transparency (for translucent artworks)

### Mounting Techniques

1. **Hinging**: reversible attachment using rice starch paste or PVA glue + thin paper/tape strip
2. **Window mounting**: create window in mount board for small/delicate artwork
3. **Float mounting**: attach with spacer/fillet for "floating" effect
4. **Conservation-grade adhesives**: BEVA 371, Lascaux 360 HV

### Framing Techniques

1. **Conservation framing**: acid-free, lignin-free, UV-filtering materials
2. **Reversible framing**: easily disassembled/reassembled without damage
3. **Frame design**: select profiles/colors/materials to complement artwork
4. **Glazing**: glass or acrylic to protect from dust, UV light, environmental factors

**Specialized techniques:**
- Textile mounting (tapestries, embroideries, costumes)
- Object mounting (sculptures, artifacts, 3D objects)
- Asian mounting (traditional techniques for scrolls, screens, fans)
- Illuminated manuscript mounting (medieval manuscripts, rare books)

### Key Principles

- **Reversibility**: all techniques should be reversible without damage
- **Material compatibility**: select materials compatible with artwork composition
- **Environmental control**: provide stable temperature/humidity/light environment
- **Safety**: work in well-ventilated area, wear protective gear (gloves, goggles, masks)
