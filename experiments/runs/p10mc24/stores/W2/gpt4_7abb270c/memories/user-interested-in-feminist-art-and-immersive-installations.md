## Interests

**Feminist Art**: Particularly interested in learning about feminist artists and movements, especially from the 1970s. Appreciates Judy Chicago's work and The Dinner Party as an iconic symbol of feminist art.

**Interactive & Immersive Art**: Wants to explore interactive and immersive art installations as a way to engage audiences and create empathy and connection.

## Artistic Vision

Working on creating installations that explore:
- **Identity, memory, and human experience**
- **Collective memory** and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts
- **Multi-sensory experiences**: incorporating sound, light, and texture

Example concept: Installation simulating walking through a forest with bird sounds, rustling leaves, and the scent of damp earth.

Interested in incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods.

## Recent Event & Networking

Attended lecture series at Museum of Contemporary Art on January 22, 2023. Dr. Maria Rodriguez spoke about feminist art in the 1970s.

**Connections made at reception:**
- Met several artists working on feminist art projects; exchanged contact information for potential future collaboration
- Met a curator interested in learning about user's artistic practice; offered valuable advice on getting work seen by a wider audience

- **Interactive storytelling**: Branching narratives, collaborative platforms for viewer contributions
