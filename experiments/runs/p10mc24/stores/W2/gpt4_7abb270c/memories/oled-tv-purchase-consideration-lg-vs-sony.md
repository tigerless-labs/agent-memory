## 4K OLED TV Purchase Consideration

**Status**: Researching, decision not yet made

**Options Being Considered:**
1. LG OLED TVs
2. Sony OLED TVs

**LG OLED TVs:**

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional OLED panels with perfect blacks and infinite contrast
- Wide color gamut with vivid, lifelike colors
- Multiple HDR support (Dolby Vision, HDR10, HLG)
- webOS platform - user-friendly smart TV platform
- Low input lag suitable for gaming
- Generally more affordable than Sony

**Popular Models:**
- LG C9 Series (2019)
- LG B9 Series (2019)
- LG CX Series (2020)
- LG GX Series (2020)

**Sony OLED TVs:**

**Strengths:**
- Excellent OLED panels with perfect blacks
- X1 Ultimate processor with advanced picture processing
- Acoustic Surface Audio (screen as speaker for immersive audio)
- Android TV platform with Google Assistant built-in
- Advanced gaming features: low input lag, HDMI 2.1, variable refresh rate (VRR)
- Better for next-gen gaming consoles

**Popular Models:**
- Sony A9G Series (2019)
- Sony A8G Series (2019)
- Sony A1E Series (2020)
- Sony A9S Series (2020)

**Key Comparison Points:**
- Panel quality: Both excellent; LG slightly better for color accuracy and viewing angles
- Smart TV platform: LG webOS considered more user-friendly than Sony Android TV
- Gaming: Sony has advantage with HDMI 2.1 and VRR support
- Audio: Sony's Acoustic Surface Audio is unique advantage
- Price: LG generally more affordable
