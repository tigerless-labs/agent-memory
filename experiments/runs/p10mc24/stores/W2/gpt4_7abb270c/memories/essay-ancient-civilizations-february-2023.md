## Essay Project — Ancient Civilizations

**Date Started**: February 10, 2023

**Topic**: Ancient civilizations, with focus on ancient Egypt

**Exhibition Visit**: Attended "Ancient Egyptian Artifacts" exhibition (location not fully specified; not at Metropolitan Museum of Art). Took photos of various artifacts for reference.

**Topics Researched**:
