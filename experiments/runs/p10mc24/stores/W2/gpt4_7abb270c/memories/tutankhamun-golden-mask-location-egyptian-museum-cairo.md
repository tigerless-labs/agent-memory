## Tutankhamun's Golden Mask

**Location**: Egyptian Museum in Cairo, Egypt (not at Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York)

**Discovery**: 1922, discovered by British archaeologist Howard Carter in tomb KV62, Valley of the Kings

**Physical Composition**:
- Solid gold: 11 kg (24 lbs)
- Adorned with precious stones: lapis lazuli, carnelian, turquoise
- Placed over the pharaoh's face to protect his identity in the afterlife

**Significance**:
- Represents divine kingship and pharaoh's connection to gods
- Symbol of protection for soul in afterlife
- Demonstrates wealth and power of Egyptian kingdom
- Exquisite example of ancient Egyptian artistry and craftsmanship

**Current Preservation**: Climate-controlled display case at Egyptian Museum in Cairo; seen by millions of visitors annually.
