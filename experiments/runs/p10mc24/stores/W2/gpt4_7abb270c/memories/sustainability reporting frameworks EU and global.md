## Frameworks and Directives

### Global/Voluntary Frameworks
- **GRI Standards** (Global Reporting Initiative): Voluntary; comprehensive sustainability reporting; no required format
- **SASB Standards** (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board): Voluntary; industry-specific sustainability reporting; no required format
- **TCFD Recommendations** (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures): Voluntary but increasingly adopted; climate-related risks/opportunities; recommends disclosure in mainstream financial filings
- **IR Framework** (International Integrated Reporting Council): Voluntary; integrated reporting; no required format
- **CDP** (Climate Disclosure Project): Voluntary; climate disclosure; complements GRI, SASB, TCFD, IR frameworks

### EU Regulatory Directives

**NFRD (Non-Financial Reporting Directive)** — Current
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for large companies; public-interest entities with >500 employees AND (>€20M balance sheet OR >€40M net turnover)
- **Format:** No prescribed format; encourages use of GRI, SASB, TCFD, IR frameworks
- **Scope:** Non-financial information (business model, policies, outcomes, risks, impacts on ESG, diversity policy)
- Member states can extend scope to more companies

**CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)** — Replacing NFRD
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for all large companies operating in EU (broader scope than NFRD)
- **Required Format:** ESEF (European Single Electronic Format) using Inline XBRL (iXBRL) for sustainability information in annual reports
- **Timeline:** Format introduction expected 2024; reporting required from 2026 onward (reporting periods starting Jan 1, 2024+)
- Status: Proposed, expected adoption 2022

**EU Taxonomy Regulation**
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for companies subject to NFRD and SFDR
- **Requirements:** Reporting must align with EU Taxonomy and use common classification system
- **Focus:** Climate-related risks and opportunities

**SFDR (Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation)**
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for financial market participants and financial advisers
- **Format:** Requires disclosure in pre-contractual documents and periodic reports
- **Focus:** Sustainability of financial products

## Key Relationships
- CSRD expands NFRD scope from large listed/credit/insurance entities to all large EU companies
- CSRD introduces first prescribed format (ESEF/iXBRL) for mandatory sustainability reporting
- All frameworks can be used in combination (e.g., CSRD companies can reference GRI/SASB/TCFD for detailed disclosures)
