## Printmaking Learning Interest

**Status**: Beginner, just starting out

**Key learning**: 
- Brayer choice affects texture of the print (was previously unaware of this)
- Brayer selection depends on: ink type (water-based = softer brayer, oil-based = harder brayer), printing surface texture, size of surface, level of detail in design, personal preference/experience

**Recommended approach**:
1. Start with relief printing (basic, minimal equipment)
2. Experiment with different materials (paper types, ink types) to understand their effects
3. Don't fear mistakes — part of the learning process
4. Consider taking a printmaking class or workshop
5. Practice regularly to build skill

**Attitude**: Open to learning, willing to experiment, wants practical tips for getting started
