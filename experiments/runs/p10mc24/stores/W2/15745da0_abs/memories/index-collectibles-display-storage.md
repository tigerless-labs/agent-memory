## Index: Collectibles & Display/Storage

User maintains multiple organized collections with attention to preservation and display aesthetics:

### Collections
- **Vintage Cameras** — 17 pieces; 3 months collecting; Brownie Hawkeye recent acquisition
- **Funko POP! Figures** — Large collection; complete MCU series
- **Vinyl Records** — Rare 1978 Fleetwood Mac *Rumours* as centerpiece
- **Posters & Art Prints** — Mondo Hogwarts (Harry Potter) recent acquisition

### Common Storage Principles
- Climate control (65–70°F, ~50% humidity for cameras)
- Acid-free storage materials across all collections
- Individual compartments/dividers to prevent damage
- UV protection where applicable
- Silica gel or dehumidification for moisture control

### Display Philosophy
- Highlight rare/signature pieces prominently
- Group items by series, theme, or era
- Create narrative or thematic displays
- Rotate displays periodically to refresh
- Balance accessibility with preservation

### Maintenance Approach
- Quarterly inspection schedules
- Regular dusting and gentle cleaning
- Dedicated tools per collection type
- Inventory/documentation for tracking
