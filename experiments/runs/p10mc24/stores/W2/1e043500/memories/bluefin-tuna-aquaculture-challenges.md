## Aquaculture Challenges for Bluefin Tuna

- **Complex reproduction in captivity**: Difficult to replicate reproductive and early life stages; breeding in captivity challenging and requires large, expensive facilities.

- **High cost**: Significant operational expense vs. cost of wild-caught fish; requires large facilities, high-quality feeds, and maintenance.

- **Environmental impact**: Discharge of waste and nutrients; farmed fish feed often sourced from wild-caught fish, driving overfishing of other species.

- **Genetic diversity**: Captive breeding weakens species resilience to environmental changes through loss of genetic diversity.

- **Consumer preference**: Market preference for wild-caught over farmed Bluefin; difficult to shift consumer behavior.

## Conclusion
Aquaculture has potential but requires significant investment and research; not a straightforward or practical near-term solution to wild overfishing.
