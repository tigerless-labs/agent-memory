## Atlantic Bluefin Tuna Life Cycle & Biology

- **Size & longevity**: Adults grow up to 10 feet long and weigh over 1,000 pounds. Typical lifespan 25–30 years; can live up to 40 years.

- **Spawning**: Occurs in the Gulf of Mexico between April and June. Eggs hatch into transparent larvae.

- **Larval dispersal**: Larvae carried by Gulf Stream from Gulf of Mexico to eastern Atlantic Ocean.

- **Juvenile stage**: Feed on plankton and small fish; found in Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean.

- **Adult migration**: Mature Bluefin make long-distance migrations across the Atlantic to feed and breed.

- **Conservation status**: Listed as endangered species. Critical habitats include spawning grounds in Gulf of Mexico, migration routes across Atlantic, and feeding areas.

- **Conservation approaches**: Reducing commercial fishing, enforcing spawning ground protection, protecting juvenile habitats, reducing bycatch, restricting fishing methods.
