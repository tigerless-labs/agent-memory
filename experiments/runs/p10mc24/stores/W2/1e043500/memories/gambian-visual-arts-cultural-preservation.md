## Gambian Visual Arts & Cultural Preservation

User asked about visual arts' role in preserving Gambian cultural traditions (May 20, 2023).

### Traditional Visual Art Forms
- **Batik printing** — wax-resist dyeing for traditional patterns
- **Woodcarving** — statues, masks, utensils representing cultural traditions
- **Pottery** — coiling and throwing techniques for everyday utensils
- **Photography** — documenting weddings, naming ceremonies, cultural events; capturing everyday life, clothing, food
- **Painting** — depicting traditional clothing, food, dance, historical events, significant places

### Photography's Role (user particularly interested)
- Photographers document cultural ceremonies in detail
- Create visual archives for future generations
- Record everyday life and daily activities
- Educate younger Gambians about cultural heritage

### Recent Developments (2023+)
- Emergence of contemporary art forms (installations, performance, conceptual art)
- Increased use of digital technology (digital paintings, animations on climate/social justice/human rights)
- Art collaborations with other African artists exploring Pan-African themes
- Youth programs teaching importance of cultural preservation and art as social change tool
