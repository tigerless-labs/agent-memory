## YA Recommendations for Diverse Reading (May 2023)

Given as recommendations to complement The Hate U Give and similar character-driven social justice narratives.

**Recommended Books:**
- Dear Martin by Nic Stone
- The Poet X by Elizabeth Acevedo
- Long Way Down by Jason Reynolds
- All American Boys by Jason Reynolds and Brendan Kiely
- The Sun is Also a Star by Nicola Yoon

**Recommended Authors:**
- Jason Reynolds - powerful voice in YA, social justice and identity themes
- Nicola Yoon - identity, family, social justice themes
- Renee Watson - experiences of Black girls/women, identity and social justice
- Ibi Zoboi - Haitian-American narratives, identity themes
- Dhonielle Clayton - fantasy/speculative fiction with identity and power themes
- Tomi Adeyemi - African mythology with fantasy and social commentary (Children of Blood and Bone)
- Sabaa Tahir - fantasy with social commentary (An Ember in the Ashes)
- Nnedi Okorafor - African culture and mythology with social commentary

All recommended for blending genres, experimenting with form/structure, and pushing boundaries in storytelling.
