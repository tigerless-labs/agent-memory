## Current Routine
- Doing light yoga poses in the morning
- Goal: improve flexibility and ankle strength
- Planning to incorporate more stretching into daily routine

## Recommended Yoga Poses for Ankle Flexibility & Strength

1. **Ankle Circles** — sit with legs straight, lift one foot, draw circles both directions, 5-10 reps each way
2. **Toe Spreads** — sit with legs straight, spread toes apart as far as possible, bring together, 10-15 reps
3. **Ankle Alphabet** — lift one foot and draw alphabet letters A-Z with toes
4. **Calf Raises** — stand on edge of stair/step with heels off, raise onto tiptoes then lower, 10-15 reps
5. **Downward-Facing Dog** — standard yoga pose, hold 30 sec to 1 min, engages ankles and core
6. **Tree Pose** — balance on one leg with other foot on inner thigh, hold 30 sec to 1 min per leg
7. **Eagle Pose** — stand, bend knees, cross one leg wrapping ankle around calf, hold 30 sec to 1 min
8. **Seated Forward Fold** — sit with legs straight, fold forward reaching toward toes/shins, hold 30 sec to 1 min

## Additional Ankle Mobility Exercises
- Ankle rotations (both directions, 5-10 reps)
- Toe curls (curl toes under then release, 10-15 reps)
- Heel raises (raise up onto heels then lower, 10-15 reps)

## Tips
- Warm up before yoga practice
- Listen to body, modify if discomfort
- Consistency important — results take time with regular practice
