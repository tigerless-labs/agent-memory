## Enabling Thrive on Higher Logic

**Date:** May 30, 2023

### 5-Step Activation Process
1. Log in to Higher Logic account and navigate to Admin interface
2. Click on "Features" tab
3. Search for "Thrive" and click "Activate" button
4. Follow prompts to complete activation (terms of service, billing, Thrive settings)
5. Start setting up community: create groups, add members, configure settings

### Important Caveats
- **Availability:** Not available to all Higher Logic customers
- **Restrictions:** May be limited to certain pricing tiers
- **Additional Fees:** May require additional fees beyond standard plan
- **Support:** Contact Higher Logic support if unsure about availability or need help

### Thrive Purpose
Thrive is a Higher Logic feature that enables organizations to create and manage online communities.
