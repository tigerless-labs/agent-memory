## Injury Prevention Strategies

1. **Warm up and cool down** — 10-15 minutes light cardio and dynamic stretching before, static stretches after
2. **Stretch and foam roll regularly** — improves flexibility, reduces muscle tension and soreness
3. **Strengthen core and ankles** — weak muscles lead to poor balance, higher injury risk
4. **Use proper gear and equipment** — appropriate shoes and gear for activity/sport
5. **Listen to your body** — if fatigued or in pain, stop and rest; don't push too hard
6. **Gradually increase intensity and volume** — avoid sudden changes in workout routine to prevent overuse injuries

## Injury Recovery Strategies

1. **RICE Principle** — Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation for acute injuries like ankle sprains
2. **Stretch and mobilize** — gentle stretching and mobilization promotes recovery and reduces stiffness
3. **Use compression and elevation** — compression wraps/sleeves reduce swelling; elevation improves blood flow
4. **Take rest days** — allow body time to recover with modified workouts or complete rest
5. **Seek professional help** — consult healthcare professional or sports medicine specialist for chronic pain or severe injuries

## Specific Ankle Sprain Recovery
- Strengthen ankles with targeted exercises (circles, calf raises, single-leg balance)
- Use ankle braces or supports during high-impact activities for stability
- Improve balance and proprioception with single-leg squats and balance boards

## General Principles
- Better to be proactive with prevention than deal with injury downtime
- Every bit of preventive care counts
