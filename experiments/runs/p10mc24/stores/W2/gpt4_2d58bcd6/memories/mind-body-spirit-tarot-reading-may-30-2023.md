## Mind, Body, Spirit Tarot Reading — May 30, 2023

### Cards drawn:
- **Mind:** Two of Pentacles reversed
- **Body:** Hierophant reversed  
- **Spirit:** Empress reversed

### Interpretation:
- **Two of Pentacles reversed (mind):** Feeling overwhelmed, mentally scattered, struggling to keep up, difficulty focusing and making clear decisions
- **Hierophant reversed (body):** Resistance to established routines/rules, disconnection from physical self, discomfort with conformity
- **Empress reversed (spirit):** Lack in nurturing/creativity/abundance, creative blockage, missing emotional nourishment, spiritual disconnection

### Overall reading:
Cards suggest imbalance across multiple life areas, with challenges in mind-body-spirit alignment. Reversed cards indicate obstacles but also opportunity for growth through self-awareness.
