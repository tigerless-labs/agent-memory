## Company Profile
- Manufactures and sells high-voltage fuses for electric vehicles
- Based in Japan
- Seeking to enter Indian market

## Market Entry Strategy (7 Points)

### 1. Market Research
- Understand unique needs of Indian EV industry
- Study prevalent EV models, charging infrastructure, local manufacturing
- Key distinction: Indian market is price-sensitive with diverse demographics and varying infrastructure development

### 2. Adaptation to Indian Standards
- Ensure high-voltage fuses meet Indian regulatory and quality standards
- Acquire necessary certifications
- Critical for compliance and customer trust

### 3. Localized Production or Collaboration
- Establish localized production OR partner with Indian company
- Benefits: reduced costs, timely delivery, competitive pricing advantage
- Navigates regulatory environment more efficiently

### 4. Pricing Strategy
- Indian market is price-sensitive — this is central to success
- Develop competitive pricing that offers value without compromising quality
- Direct impact on product adoption

### 5. Distribution Channels
- Build robust distribution network across vast, diverse market
- Partner with established automotive parts distributors and retailers
- Explore e-commerce platforms for visibility and availability

### 6. Marketing and Promotion
- Highlight benefits: improved safety, reliability, performance
- Use digital marketing, social media, local industry events
- Messaging must cater to diverse Indian consumer needs and preferences

### 7. After-Sales Service and Support
- Establish network of service centers
- Train local technicians
- Critical differentiator in Indian market — builds brand loyalty and trust

## Key Market Differences: India vs Other Markets
- **Price sensitivity:** Higher than other markets — major purchasing factor
- **Demographics:** More diverse
- **Infrastructure:** Development varies significantly by region
- **Regulatory environment:** Unique local standards required
- **Customer relationships:** After-sales service more critical for loyalty
