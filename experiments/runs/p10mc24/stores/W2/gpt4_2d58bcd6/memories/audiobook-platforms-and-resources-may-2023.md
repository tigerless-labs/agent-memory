## Audiobook Platforms (May 2023)

- Audible - subscription-based service with vast library
- Libro.fm - partners with independent bookstores
- Scribd - subscription access to e-books, audiobooks, documents
- Public libraries - free access via OverDrive or Hoopla

## Notable Narrators Mentioned

**Known for excellent performances:**
- Bahni Turpin - narrated The Hate U Give
- Jim Dale - narrated The Night Circus
- Adjoa Andoh - narrated The Power
- Frazer Douglas - narrated The Song of Achilles
- Peter Kenny - narrated The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August
- Juliet Stevenson - nuanced performances (The Girl on the Train, The Power)
- Scott Brick - distinctive voice (The Da Vinci Code, The Bourne Identity)
- George Guidall - veteran with 1000+ audiobooks (The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo, Lincoln Rhyme series)
- Khristine Hvam - youthful energy (The Hunger Games, The Divergent series)

Audie Awards recommended as source for discovering great narrations.
