## Tarot Trumps Card Game

### Overview:
A fictional card game created May 30, 2023 designed to be played with a full tarot deck.

### Basic rules:
- **Players:** 2-4
- **Deck:** Full 78-card tarot deck
- **Objective:** Win tricks and score points by taking tarot trumps

### Setup:
Deal entire deck one card at a time to players. Players receive equal cards; remaining cards form the "kitty."

### Core gameplay:
1. Player to dealer's left leads first trick with any card
2. Other players must play card of same suit if possible
3. Tarot trumps beat any other card (including other suits)
4. Highest trump wins the trick; winner leads next trick

### Trump card rankings (ascending):
The Fool (4 pts) through The World (25 pts) — total of 22 trumps

### Scoring:
- Takes containing tarot trump: score points based on trump rank
- Takes without tarot trump: 0 points
- Player with highest total score wins

### Trump point values:
Fool=4, Magician=5, High Priestess=6... up to World=25
