## Home Strength Training Plan

### Goal
Supplement gym routine with home workouts to increase frequency and accelerate progress

### Household Items as Weights
- Water bottles or cans (1-2 liters) used as dumbbells
- Backpack or bag filled with books or weights
- Resistance bands
- Bodyweight exercises

### Sample 3-Day Home Workout Split

**Monday - Upper Body**
- Push-ups: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Incline dumbbell press (water bottles/cans): 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Bent-over dumbbell rows (water bottles/cans): 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Tricep dips (using chair or bench): 3 sets of 10-12 reps

**Wednesday - Lower Body**
- Squats: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Calf raises (bodyweight or weights): 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Lunges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg
- Glute bridges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps

**Friday - Core**
- Plank: 3 sets of 30-60 second hold
- Russian twists (with weight): 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Leg raises (lying on back): 3 sets of 10-12 reps
- Bicycle crunches: 3 sets of 10-12 reps

### Key Principles
- Warm up 5-10 minutes before, cool down 5-10 minutes after
- Focus on compound exercises that work multiple muscle groups
- Use progressive overload - gradually increase weight/reps/sets
- Start with lighter weights and build up
- Proper form and technique crucial to avoid injury
- Consistency and patience important
