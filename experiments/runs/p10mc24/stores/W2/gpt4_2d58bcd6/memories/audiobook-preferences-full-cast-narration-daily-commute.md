## Audiobook Listening Habits

- Recently started listening to audiobooks during daily commute
- Completed audiobooks:
  - "Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind" by Yuval Noah Harari
  - "Daisy Jones and The Six" by Taylor Jenkins Reid (appreciated the full-cast narration format that matches the novel's oral history style)

## Narration Preferences

- **Strong preference for full-cast narrations**, especially for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes
- Finds full-cast narrations help with:
  - Keeping track of who is speaking
  - Creating immersive experience
  - Connecting emotionally with characters
  
- Also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style to stories

- Believes narration is **crucial to audiobook experience**: narrator's tone, pace, and inflection greatly impact emotional response and character interpretation
- Good narration can elevate the story and keep listener engaged; poor narration can detract significantly
- Values narrator's ability to convey emotional depth, complexity, and character nuances
- Recognizes that narrator's interpretation collaborates with listener's imagination to shape how characters are imagined
