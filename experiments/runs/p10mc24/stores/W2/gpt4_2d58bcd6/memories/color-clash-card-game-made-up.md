## Color Clash Card Game

### Overview:
A fictional card game created May 30, 2023 as a creative exercise in game design.

### Basic rules:
- **Players:** 2-6
- **Deck:** Standard 52-card deck
- **Objective:** Score most points by creating sets of cards with matching suits

### Core gameplay:
1. Deal 7 cards to each player; remainder forms draw pile
2. Players draw one card and play one card from hand
3. Player declares a suit
4. All players must play cards of that suit if they have them
5. Player who triggered suit match draws another card and plays

### Melds variant:
- Melds are sets of 3+ cards of same rank
- Players must lay down base meld before laying additional melds
- Players can add to opponent's melds with cards of same rank
- Game ends when draw pile depleted or no more melds possible

### Scoring:
Each card = 1 point. Bonus points for most cards of a suit or "rainbow" (all 4 suits).
