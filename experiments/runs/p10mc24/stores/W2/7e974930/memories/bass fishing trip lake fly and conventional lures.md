## Trip Details
- **When**: A few weeks from March 20, 2023
- **Where**: Nearby lake
- **Target**: Bass
- **Approach**: Both conventional lures and fly fishing

## Conventional Lure Recommendations
**Topwater**: Popper, Spook, Frog
**Soft Plastics**: Curly Tail Grub, Worm (Texas/Carolina rig), Lizard
**Crankbaits**: Shad-imitating (medium-diving), Square-bill (shallow), Deep-diving (10-20 ft)
**Jigs**: Jig and Pig, Swim bait, Finesse jig
**Other**: Spinnerbait, A-rig

## Knots for Lures
1. **Improved Clinch Knot** — easy, high breaking strength
2. **Palomar Knot** — strong and reliable
3. **Loop Knot (Perfection Loop)** — creates secure loop, won't slip
- Always moisten knot before pulling tight
- Trim excess tag end close to knot

## Fly Fishing Setup

### Fly Rod & Reel
- **Rod**: 9-foot 6-inch to 10-foot, 6-8 weight, medium to fast action
- **Reel**: Capacity for 150-200 yards of 20-lb test backing, reliable drag system

### Fly Line
- **Type**: Weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking line
- **Weight**: 6-8 range
- **Length**: 90 feet standard

### Leader & Tippet
- **Leader**: 9-12 feet, monofilament or fluorocarbon, 15-20 lb breaking strength
- **Tippet**: 1-2 feet, 12-15 lb breaking strength
- Use tippet ring to prevent line twist

## Fly Patterns for Bass
**Streamers**: Woolly Bugger (black/olive/brown), Muddler Minnow, Baitfish Streamer (silver/gold/chartreuse)
**Poppers & Sliders**: Poppers (white/yellow/chartreuse), Sliders, Gurgler
**Nymphs**: Crayfish Hopper, Crawdad Nymph, Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail Nymph, Woolly Leech
**Dry Flies**: Frog Patterns, Terrestrials (ants, beetles, grasshoppers)

## Presentation Techniques
- **Strip Retrieve**: Steady, consistent for streamers/nymphs; vary speed and length
- **Twitch Retrieve**: Short, quick movements for poppers, sliders, dry flies
- **Drag-Free Retrieve**: Let fly float/sink naturally
- Use **pauses** in retrieve to let bass react
- Cast at 45-degree angles for natural presentation
- Vary depth based on water temperature

## Structure Targeting
- **Rocks**: Cast parallel to rock face; use streamers; vary retrieve speed by depth
- **Weed Beds**: Use weedless flies (frogs, weedless streamers); cast to edges not thick center; slow, deliberate retrieve
- **Sunken Logs**: [strategy not yet discussed in detail]
- Focus on **transitional zones**: drop-offs, weed lines, structure changes
- Look for schools of baitfish

## Key Notes
- Check local fishing regulations and obtain necessary permits
- Practice casting and knot-tying before trip
- In clear water: subtle presentation; in murky water: aggressive retrieve
- Match flies to natural food sources in lake
