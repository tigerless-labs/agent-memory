## Camera Purchase Decision

**Decision**: Fujifilm X-T30 II (selected after comparing with Sony a6400 and Sony a7 II)

**Budget**: ~$1,500 for camera body and lens

**Reasoning**:
- Compact size (priority)
- Vari-angle touchscreen LCD (priority)
- Excellent autofocus system (425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time, 99% accuracy)
- Good battery life: 380 frames per charge (EVF), 440 frames (LCD)
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed, but adequate for outdoor use)

**Recommended Kit**:
- Camera body: Fujifilm X-T30 II ($899)
- Kit lens: Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ ($499)

**Future plans**: Open to investing in additional lenses later (X-mount ecosystem available)

**Photography focus**: Landscape and nature photography (primary interest)

**Camera background**: Currently transitioning from old DSLR; user prefers carrying larger gear
