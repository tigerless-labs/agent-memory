Essential Ethiopian dishes to experience:

- **Injera**: Sourdough flatbread that serves as both plate and utensil for scooping stews and curries.
- **Doro Wot**: Spicy chicken stew made with berbere spice (blend of chili powder, ginger, coriander, and other spices).
- **Tibs**: Spicy sautéed meat dish made with beef or lamb, onions, and peppers.
- **Kitfo**: Raw beef seasoned with spices and chili powder.
- **Shiro**: Thick stew made with ground chickpeas or lentils, served with injera.
- **Ethiopian Coffee**: Country is famous for coffee. Traditional coffee ceremony includes roasting beans, grinding them, and brewing in a special pot.

Ethiopian cuisine is known for unique, complex flavors and is highly satisfying.
