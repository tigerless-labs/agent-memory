Key Ethiopian festivals for travelers:

- **Timkat**: January 19th — commemorates baptism of Jesus. Features processions, music, dancing, and water splashing.
- **Meskel**: September 27th — commemorates the finding of the True Cross by St. Helena. Features bonfires, feasting, and traditional dancing.
- **Enkutatash**: September 11th — Ethiopian New Year celebration with feasting, dancing, and gift exchange.
- **Fasika**: April (typically) — Ethiopian Orthodox Easter with fasting, prayer, colorful processions, feasting, and gift exchange.

These festivals offer authentic experiences to meet locals and participate in cultural traditions.
