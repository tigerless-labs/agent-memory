User recently fixed mountain bike flat tire and cleaned chain; now interested in chain maintenance routine.

Key practices:
- Clean chain every 1-2 weeks (more often in dirty/wet conditions)
- Use bike-specific chain cleaner
- Use chain cleaning tool for effectiveness
- Rinse thoroughly
- Apply lubricant sparingly to clean chain
- Wipe excess lubricant
- Regularly inspect for wear, rust, stretching, broken links
- Consider chain wear indicator tool
- Also maintain other components: chainrings, cassette, derailleur pivot points
