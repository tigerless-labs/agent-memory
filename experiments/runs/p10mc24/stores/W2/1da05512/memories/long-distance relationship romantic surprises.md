## User Situation
In a long-distance relationship and wants to surprise partner with something special and romantic.

## Romantic Activity Ideas Discussed
- Cook same meal together over video call with wine or dessert
- Virtual movie night using Netflix Party or Zoom
- Play online games together (chess, trivia, puzzles, multiplayer games)
- Take virtual tours of museums, galleries, or attractions together
- Send surprise care packages with flowers, favorite food, or romantic items
- Plan surprise video calls

## Key Principle
Show partner you care and are committed through heartfelt, personal activities rather than generic gestures.
