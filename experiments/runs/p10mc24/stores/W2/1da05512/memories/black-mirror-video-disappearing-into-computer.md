## Scene Concept
**Date**: May 21, 2023  
**Project**: Black Mirror-style video scene

### Setup
- Girl is about to sit down at a computer
- Right before she speaks into microphone: camera shows her slowly disappearing into the computer
- Goal: dark, unsettling, dystopian sci-fi feel (Black Mirror aesthetic)

### Recommended Techniques

**Visual Effects**
- Glitchy, distorted computer interface as she disappears
- Distortion effects, flashes of light, or swirling motion to show her being pulled in
- Cut to computer screen/interface after disappearance to suggest something strange happening inside

**Sound Design**
- Low hum or distant whisper as she disappears
- Eerie music or discordant sounds to create foreboding
- Flickering lights or static noise

**Camera & Lighting**
- Dim lighting or backlighting to create mystery and suspense
- Zoom in on girl's face/hands as she approaches computer to build anticipation
- Slow, deliberate camera movements to build tension
- Quick cuts during disappearance for jarring, disorienting effect
- Close-ups of buttons/screens after disappearance

**Narrative Twist** (optional)
- Girl trapped in digital realm
- Computer itself is alive or malevolent
