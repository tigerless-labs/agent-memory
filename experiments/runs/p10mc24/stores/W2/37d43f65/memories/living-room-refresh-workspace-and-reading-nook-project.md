## Living Room Refresh Project

**Status:** Planning phase (May 20, 2023)

### Room details
- Recently tidied and organized
- Large windows providing bright indirect light
- Goal: maintain clutter-free aesthetic while adding warmth and personality

### Phase 1: Plants for bright windows
Low-maintenance options recommended:
- Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) — air purifier, tolerates neglect
- ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) — handles infrequent watering
- Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) — easy care, air purifier
- Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) — versatile, can climb or trail
- Succulents (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) — extremely low-maintenance
- Dracaena (Dracaena spp.) — tall, air purifier
- Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii) — elegant white blooms
- Bromeliads (Aechmea, Guzmania) — colorful flowers

Care: water when soil dry, minimal fertilizer, monitor temperature and humidity

### Phase 2: Workspace/reading nook near window

**Desk and console table options:**
- Wall-mounted floating desk (space-saving)
- Narrow console table (sleek, minimal design)
- Drop-leaf table (folds when not in use)
- Ottoman with tray (flexible, comfortable seating)
- Foldable desk (compact, portable)

### Phase 3: Circular rug
- **Size:** 3-4 feet in diameter (proportional to compact workspace)
- **Material:** wool, jute, or synthetic (durable, low-maintenance)
- **Color palette:** neutral (beige, gray, taupe) or bold color for personality
- **Pattern:** simple or subtle (Moroccan-inspired or minimalist geometric)
- **Thickness:** approximately 1/4 inch (won't obstruct chair movement)
- **Benefits:** defines space, adds warmth, hides cords, softens aesthetic

### Phase 4: Circular mirror above desk
- **Size:** 12-18 inches in diameter
- **Hanging height:** 57-60 inches from floor (comfortable viewing)
- **Frame style:** choose to complement surrounding decor (minimalist or ornate)
- **Glass type:** consider beveled edge or subtle texture
- **Benefits:** adds visual interest, makes space feel larger, reflects light and positive energy

### Phase 5: Artwork and prints above desk or console table
- **Size:** 1/3 to 2/3 the width of the desk (proportional scale)
- **Theme selection:** choose pieces that resonate with personal style and room theme
- **Color palette:** complement existing room colors for harmony
- **Consider lighting:** ensure piece stands out in room's light levels

**Popular styles:**
- Botanicals (plants, flowers, trees — elegant, serene)
- Abstracts (colorful, textured, energetic)
- Typography (inspirational quotes, playful sayings)
- Landscapes (photography or paintings, calming)
- Pop art (bold, colorful, fun)

**Budget tip:** prints, reproductions, and emerging artists offer affordable options

### Key maintenance habits
1. One-touch rule: put items away immediately after use
2. Designate landing strip for items to deal with later
3. Regular maintenance: 10-15 minutes daily or 30 minutes weekly
4. Home for everything system
5. In-out rule: remove old item when adding new
6. Keep surfaces clean to prevent dust buildup
