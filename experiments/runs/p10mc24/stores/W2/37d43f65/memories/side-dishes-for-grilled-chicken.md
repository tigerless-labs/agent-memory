## Recommended Side Dishes

**Grilled vegetables**
- Bell peppers, zucchini, eggplant
- Season with salt, pepper, and olive oil

**Fresh salad**
- Classic Caesar salad or Greek salad (cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, feta, olives)
- Balances the richness of grilled chicken

**Corn on the cob**
- Classic summer side dish
- Top with butter, salt, and paprika

**Baked sweet potato**
- Bake in oven
- Top with butter, cinnamon, and brown sugar for sweet and savory flavor

**Quinoa salad**
- Mix cooked quinoa with chopped vegetables (cucumbers, tomatoes, bell peppers)
- Dress with lemony vinaigrette
- Nutritious and tasty option
