## Non-UK Citizens as Life Peers in UK

**Eligibility requirements** (from conversation May 20, 2023):
- Must be British citizen, Commonwealth citizen, or Irish citizen
- Must have permanent residence in UK
- Rare for non-UK citizens to be nominated/appointed

**Examples provided:**
1. **Baroness Boothroyd** — born Yorkshire; father was Belgian soldier, mother Polish-Jewish refugee; Speaker of House of Commons 1992–2000; appointed to House of Lords 2000
2. **Baroness Hayman** — born Canada; first elected Speaker of House of Commons; appointed to House of Lords after stepping down in 2000
3. **Lord Patten of Barnes** — born Sudan; last British Governor of Hong Kong; appointed life peer 2005
4. **Baroness Manningham-Buller** — born Northamptonshire; Director General MI5 2002–2007; appointed to House of Lords 2008
