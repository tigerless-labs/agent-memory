## Cooking Method

After marinating chicken breasts for 20-30 minutes at room temperature, cook using one of these methods:

**With a grill or grill pan:**
1. Preheat to medium-high heat (400-450F if using a grill; or heat the pan until hot)
2. Oil the grates/pan to prevent sticking
3. Cook chicken 6-7 minutes per side
4. Use a meat thermometer to verify internal temperature reaches 165F
5. Rest for 5-10 minutes before serving (allows juices to redistribute)

**With cast-iron skillet:**
- Same cooking time and temperature check as grill pan method

## Why this works
- 6-7 minutes per side achieves thorough cooking without drying out the meat
- 165F is the safe minimum internal temperature for poultry
- Resting allows carryover cooking and juice retention, resulting in juicier, more flavorful chicken

## Equipment notes
- No actual grill needed; grill pan or cast-iron skillet works just as well
- The marinade is the same regardless of cooking method
