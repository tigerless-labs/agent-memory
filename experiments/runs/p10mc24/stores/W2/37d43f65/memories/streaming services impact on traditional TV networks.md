## Impact of streaming on traditional television

**Market shift:**
- Growing viewer migration from cable/satellite TV to streaming (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu)
- Decline in traditional TV viewership
- Decline in advertising revenue for traditional networks

**Network adaptation strategies:**
- Launching proprietary streaming services: CBS All Access, NBC's Peacock
- Offering shows on third-party platforms (Hulu, Netflix) for binge-watching
- Developing high-quality original programming to compete with streaming platforms

**Result:**
- Streaming services competing directly with traditional networks on content production
- Industry disruption affecting how networks operate, create content, and market themselves
