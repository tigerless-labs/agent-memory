## Schedule & Coordination

- Coordinates regular gaming sessions with a partner who lives in another city
- Target frequency: at least twice per week
- Time difference between locations creates scheduling complexity
- Looking for tools to manage calendar and coordinate availability

## Tools Explored

- **Google Calendar** (primary choice) — for scheduling and coordinating recurring gaming sessions
- **Zoom** — video conferencing for gaming sessions, integrates with Google Calendar via add-on
- **Google Hangouts** — alternative for video conferencing, integrated in Google Calendar
- **Calendly**, **World Time Buddy**, **Doodle** — alternatives for scheduling and time zone management
- **Microsoft Outlook** — alternative calendar platform with similar features

## Google Calendar Features of Interest

- Setting reminders (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or custom)
- "Find a time" feature — shows available slots for all attendees
- "Propose a new time" feature — suggests alternative times if initial slots don't work
- Adding multiple time zones to display both their time and partner's time
- Sharing calendar with gaming partner
- Creating recurring events for regular sessions
- Video conferencing integration (Hangouts, Zoom, custom links)
- Adding notes/descriptions with correct times in partner's time zone
