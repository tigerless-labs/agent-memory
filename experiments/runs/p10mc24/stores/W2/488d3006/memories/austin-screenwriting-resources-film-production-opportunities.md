## Screenwriting workshops & events in Austin
- Austin Film Festival's Screenwriting Conference (annual, panels, workshops, one-on-one meetings)
- Writers Guild of America (East/West) — occasional Austin workshops and seminars
- Austin Screenwriters Group (meetup group: script readings, writing exercises, industry panels)
- The Screenplay Workshop (Austin-based: character, dialogue, structure; one-on-one coaching available)
- Moody College of Communication at UT Austin (courses, workshops, lectures; some open to public)
- Austin Film Society (AFS) (screenings, workshops, lectures, filmmaking grants)
- SXSW Conference & Festivals (film program with panels, workshops, networking)

## Finding current film productions & getting involved
**Resources:**
- Austin Film Commission (website lists current/upcoming feature films, TV, commercials)
- Texas Film Commission (search by production type, location, job category)
- Film Austin (non-profit, posts productions, casting calls, job opportunities)
- Austin Film Society (posts productions, casting calls, behind-scenes events)
- Casting websites: Backstage, Casting Networks, Mandy.com
- Production Lists (aggregates film/TV productions by location, type, job category)
- Social media (follow local production companies, casting agencies, industry professionals)

**Tips for getting involved as extra/PA:**
- Create professional profile on casting websites
- Follow casting calls and apply
- Network at local film events
- Volunteer on local film sets
- Be prepared for flexible schedule and long hours
