## Pollock-Krasner Foundation & Grant

**Established:** 1985 by Lee Krasner (widow of Jackson Pollock)

**Purpose:** Support individual artists of all nationalities and backgrounds; promote legacy of Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner

**Eligibility:**
- Mid-career and established artists
- Must have been actively working for at least 5 years
- Must demonstrate artistic merit
- Must demonstrate financial need (provide proof of income, expenses, debts)

**Grant Details:**
- Monetary award (amount varies based on individual needs and selection committee evaluation)
- Meant to cover practical costs: art supplies, studio rent, other expenses
- Highly competitive
- Open to all art disciplines: painting, sculpture, printmaking, photography, multimedia, etc.
- No preference for specific styles, genres, or mediums

**Foundation Activities:**
- Awards financial support to qualifying artists
- Maintains extensive archive of information about Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner
- Supports research and scholarly activities related to Pollock and Krasner's work

**Notable Recipients (partial list):**
Mark Bradford, Kara Walker, Robert Gober, David Salle, Elizabeth Murray, Frank Stella, Chuck Close, Julie Mehretu, Kiki Smith, Jasper Johns, Joan Mitchell

**Context:** The grant helps support artists who pursue full-time creative work rather than supplementary jobs, allowing them to continue artistic practice while navigating the unpredictable art market.
