## Popular Austin food trucks
- Veracruz All Natural (authentic Mexican: migas tacos, tortas, breakfast burritos)
- Frank (gourmet hot dogs: "Chicagoan", "Bacon mac")
- Torchy's Tacos (originated as food truck, now chain; "Trailer Park" taco)
- East Side King (Asian-fusion: pork belly buns, kimchi fries, Thai chicken karaage)
- Chi'Lantro (Korean-Mexican fusion: BBQ tacos, burritos, kimchi fried rice)
- The Peached Tortilla (Asian-fusion: Korean BBQ tacos, banh mi, kimchi fried chicken)
- G'Raj Mahal (Indian-inspired: chicken tikka masala, saag paneer, naan wraps)
- Lulu's Fried Chicken (Southern: fried chicken, waffles, biscuits)
- Yume Burger (Japanese-inspired: "Tokyo Burger", "K-Town Burger")
- The Good Seed (plant-based/vegan/gluten-free: burgers, bowls, desserts)

## Resources for tracking food trucks

- Check social media/websites for current schedules and locations
