## Appraisal Documentation and Process

**General Appraisal Report Should Include**
- Item description including denomination series condition
- Appraiser opinion on authenticity and rarity
- Estimated current market value
- Historical and provenance information

**Types of Appraisals Available**
- Verbal appraisal (informal assessment, general value estimate)
- Written appraisal (detailed formal assessment with analysis)
- Formal appraisal report (extensive comprehensive document)

**Certification Services by Category**

**Comics**
- CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) grading standard
- Certifies condition and authenticity
- Certificate reflects grade and value

**Watches**
- Omega provides manufacturer certification
- Independent appraisals from recognized watch organizations
- Certificate of origin with production details

**Guitars**
- Guitar Appraisal Group offers professional appraisals
- Vintage Guitar Magazine can provide reference
- Service documentation from authorized repair shops

**Toys and Figures**
- Wata Games certification
- PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) grading
- Certificate reflects condition grade

**Currency and Paper Money**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG) members
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
- Numismatic appraisers specialize in currency authentication

## Appraisal Tips
- Research and select reputable appraiser with relevant expertise
- Provide accurate history and condition information
- Get multiple appraisals for accurate valuation
- Keep all appraisal reports and supporting documentation
- Use appraisals for insurance and resale purposes
- Maintain chain of custody documentation
