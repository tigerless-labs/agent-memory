## Omega Certification Services

**What Omega Offers**
- Authentication verification of genuine Omega timepiece
- Certificate of Origin stating production date model and serial number
- Condition Report assessing watch condition and restoration needs
- Professional service and restoration using original parts when possible

**Certificate of Authenticity Details**
- Unique certificate number for verification
- Watch details including serial number model and production date
- Signature of authorized Omega representative and Omega stamp
- Can be used to verify authenticity with potential buyers

**Benefits of Omega Certification**
- Increased resale value due to guaranteed authenticity
- Enhanced credibility with buyers
- Peace of mind on condition and provenance
- Professional documentation of watch history

**Getting the Service**
- Contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service
- Cost varies by model age and condition
- Timeline varies by watch condition and required work
- Keep all documentation including certification and service records in safe location

**Additional Value Enhancement**
- Can combine with independent appraisals for greater credibility
- Consider getting additional provenance documentation
- Professional appraisal from recognized organizations adds value
