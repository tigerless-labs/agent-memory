## Selling Platforms by Item Type

**Watches (Vintage/High-End)**
- Chrono24 (watch-specific marketplace, large collector community)
- 1stdibs (luxury items with curated dealers)
- The RealReal (consignment model for luxury items)
- eBay (with Authenticity Guarantee program)
- Watch forums and communities (WatchUseek Timekeeper's Forums Omega Forums)
- Local watch dealers and collectors
- Auction houses (Christie's Sotheby's Heritage Auctions)

**Comic Books**
- ComicConnect (specialized comic platform with large collector community)
- eBay (dedicated comics category)
- Comic Book Resources forums
- Comic Vine and Reddit communities
- Local comic book stores
- Certification: CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) grading

**Musical Instruments (Guitars)**
- Reverb (music-specific marketplace designed for instruments)
- eBay (vintage and collectibles categories)
- The Gear Page forums
- GuitarPlayer forums
- TDPRI (The Dallas Pedal and Guitar Ranch Internet Forum)
- Guitar Collector and Vintage Guitar Exchange
- Facebook Marketplace and local selling groups

**Toys and Action Figures**
- eBay (large toy collector community)
- Action Figure Insider forums
- The Fwoosh and ToyArk communities
- ToyCollector FigureCollector and Vintage Toy Room platforms
- Local toy and collectible stores
- Certification: Wata Games or PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator)

**Electronics (Audio Equipment)**
- eBay (vintage electronics category)
- Gearslutz forums
- Resonance FM forums
- Vintage audio equipment forums
- Facebook Marketplace and local audio enthusiast groups

**Currency (Paper Money)**
- Professional numismatic appraisers through PNG or IAPMD
- Specialized currency collectors
- eBay (rare currency category)
- Local currency dealers

## General Selling Tips (All Categories)
- Research platform fees before listing
- High-quality photos from multiple angles
- Detailed descriptions including condition
- Provide certification and documentation
- Set competitive prices based on research
- Be prepared to answer buyer questions
- Consider negotiation in pricing strategy
