## Product Photography Guidance Given

**Lighting**
- Use natural light when possible, especially near windows or during golden hour
- Softbox or DIY diffuser with translucent umbrella to soften harsh shadows
- Avoid direct flash; bounce light if necessary
- Layer lighting with softbox for overall illumination plus sidelight for texture

**Composition**
- Use plain neutral backgrounds (white, gray, black) to isolate subject
- Apply rule of thirds for balanced layout
- Experiment with unique angles and perspectives
- Take close-ups of specific details and features
- Consider lifestyle shots showing items in use

**Editing**
- Color correction for accurate representation
- Noise reduction and sharpening without overdoing
- Minor retouching to remove dust and scratches (avoid over-retouching)
- Establish consistent look across all images

**Technical Tips**
- Use tripod for sharp images and precise composition control
- Shoot in RAW format for maximum flexibility
- Ensure sharp focus with shallow depth of field for professional look
- Pay attention to item-specific details (watch face, guitar fretboard, comic book cover art)
