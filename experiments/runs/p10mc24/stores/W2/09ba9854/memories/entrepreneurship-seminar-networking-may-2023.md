## Entrepreneurship Seminar and Networking (May 20, 2023)

User is attending a morning entrepreneurship seminar at a co-working space near their apartment.

### Goals for the Seminar
- Network with like-minded individuals
- Spread the word about their project
- Ask attendees for feedback on their project idea
- Explore potential collaboration opportunities

### Networking Approach
- Have an elevator pitch ready about the project
- Be prepared to talk about what makes the project unique
- Ask questions and listen to others' experiences
- Be open to learning from others

### Asking for Feedback and Help
When approaching people for feedback or collaboration:
1. Be respectful of people's time - keep asks concise
2. Be clear about what you're asking for (feedback, collaboration, guidance)
3. Show genuine interest in their work before asking
4. Be open to receiving constructive criticism
5. Offer value in return (skill swap, network connection, etc.)
6. Be prepared to answer questions about the project
7. Don't be pushy - respect boundaries if someone declines

### Follow-up
- Send quick emails or LinkedIn requests to keep in touch with new contacts
- Build a strong network of potential collaborators and advisors
