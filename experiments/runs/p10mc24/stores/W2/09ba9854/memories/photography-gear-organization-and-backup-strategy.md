## Photography Gear Organization and Backup Strategy

Comprehensive initiative to organize photography equipment and establish reliable backup procedures.

**Key Components:**

**1. Camera Bag Organization (Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW)**
- Categorize gear by type (bodies, lenses, flashes, accessories)
- Prioritize most-used items
- Use adjustable dividers for separation
- Store valuables first (bodies, lenses)
- Utilize exterior pockets and tripod straps
- Heavy items at bottom, padding as needed

**2. Memory Card Management (SanDisk Extreme Pro)**
- Label cards with capacity, type, and unique identifier
- Store in protective cases or wallet
- Organize by capacity or usage
- Maintain card inventory
- Format regularly before use
- Rotate cards to ensure even wear

**3. Small Accessories Organization**
- Remote shutter release: small pouches, keychain storage, zippered compartments
- Lens cleaning kit: dedicated pouch or small storage box with dividers
- Extra batteries: individual cases or battery storage boxes
- General accessories: pouches, mini storage bags, travel organizers

**4. Lens Protection and Storage**
- Cases: Think Tank Photo, Lowepro, Nanuk
- Pouches: Op/Tech USA, Lenscoat, Tamrac
- Store with caps on, upright position
- Avoid stacking lenses
- Clean before storage

**5. Photo Backup Strategy**
- Recommended external hard drives: Western Digital My Book, Seagate Backup Plus, LaCie Rugged, G-Technology G-Drive
- Implement 3-2-1 backup rule: three copies on two different media types, one copy offsite
- Set up automatic backup software
- Verify backups periodically for integrity

**Selection Criteria for Hardware:**
- Capacity: 2-3x current storage needs
- Compatibility: Mac or PC
- Interface: USB 3.0, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C
- Speed: Consider transfer speed (7200rpm or SSD)
- Durability: Rugged/waterproof if needed for travel
- Security: Built-in encryption, password protection
- Warranty and support options
- Portability and price considerations
