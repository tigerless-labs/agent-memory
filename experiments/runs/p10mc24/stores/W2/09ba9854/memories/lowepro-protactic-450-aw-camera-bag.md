## Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW Camera Bag

Newly acquired camera bag used for photography gear organization and transport.

**Features:**
- Adjustable dividers and compartments for customizing internal layout
- Multiple exterior pockets with weather-sealed options
- Tripod attachment straps (compatible with Manfrotto BeFree tripod)
- Weather-sealed design for protection in wet conditions

**Organization Tips:**
- Pack camera bodies and lenses in main compartment first
- Use dividers to separate gear by type
- Store small items (batteries, memory cards, filters) in dedicated pouches and compartments
- Attach tripod to exterior using tripod straps
- Place heavy items (lenses, laptop) at the bottom for balance
- Roll cables to save space
- Leave some padding space for gear protection
