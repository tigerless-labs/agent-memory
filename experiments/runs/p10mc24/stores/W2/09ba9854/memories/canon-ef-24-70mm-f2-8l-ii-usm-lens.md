## Canon EF 24-70mm f/2.8L II USM Lens

Professional Canon EF mount lens owned for photography work.

**Lens Cleaning:**
- Recommended cleaning kits: Canon official kit, Zeiss Lens Cleaning Kit
- Use microfiber cloths (soft, lint-free) for safe cleaning
- Apply cleaning solution sparingly to cloth, not directly to lens
- Clean in circular motion from center outward
- Avoid paper towels or abrasive materials
- Inspect after cleaning and repeat if necessary

**Storage:**
- Consider lens cases from Think Tank Photo, Lowepro, or Nanuk for protection during transport
- Lens pouches from Op/Tech USA, Lenscoat, or Tamrac available in various sizes
- Always store with lens caps on to prevent scratches
- Keep lens upright to prevent oil migration
- Clean before storage to prevent dust accumulation
