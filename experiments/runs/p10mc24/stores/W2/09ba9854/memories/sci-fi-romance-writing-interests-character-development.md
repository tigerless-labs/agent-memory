## Writing Interests and Guidance

**Primary Interests:**
- Sci-fi romance genre
- World-building in sci-fi romance stories
- Character development and complex, believable characters
- Crafting compelling character arcs and relationships
- Themes of identity and self-discovery

**Key Writing Preferences:**

### World-Building Tips Valued:
- Establish clear vision and core concept
- Develop rich history with significant events and cultural developments
- Define consistent rules of world (physics, magic, technology)
- Build diverse, immersive environments with sensory details
- Develop unique language and slang specific to world
- Consider technology implications on daily life and relationships
- Balance detail with ambiguity to allow reader imagination
- Make it relatable with human emotions despite extraordinary circumstances
- Be consistent yet flexible as story evolves

### Character Development Principles:
- Give clear goals and motivations (specific, measurable, relatable)
- Create multi-dimensional characters with contradictions, flaws, strengths
- Develop authentic backstories shaping personality and actions
- Show emotional vulnerability for intimacy and connection
- Create conflicts stemming from character motivations
- Demonstrate emotional intelligence and empathy
- Build authentic relationships on mutual respect and trust
- Avoid info-dumping character details
- Maintain consistency in personality and traits
- Use unique skillsets and abilities integral to plot

### Character Arc Preferences:
- Show genuine emotional journey and growth
- Create character-driven plot
- Use contradictions to add depth
- Avoid clichés and overused tropes
- Develop deep emotional connections in romance
- Create tension and conflict from differences in values/goals
- Show emotional intimacy (comfort, support, understanding)
- Make romance partnerships, not one-way streets
- Prefer slow-burn over insta-love
- Focus on emotional intimacy and mutual respect

### Identity and Self-Discovery Themes:
- Characters questioning purpose and identity
- Blurring boundaries between human/non-human through sci-fi elements
- Characters confronting fears and motivations
- Embracing change and adapting to new circumstances
- Using symbolism to represent journey of self-discovery
- Exploring intersectionality of identity aspects
