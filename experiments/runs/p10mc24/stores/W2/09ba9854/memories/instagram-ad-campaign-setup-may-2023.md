## Instagram Ad Campaign Setup (May 20, 2023)

User is promoting their project on Instagram using an ad campaign with the **Awareness** objective to increase visibility.

### Campaign Metrics to Track
- **Reach**: Number of people who saw the ad
- **Impressions**: Number of times ad was displayed
- **Frequency**: Average times each person saw the ad (watch for ad fatigue)
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, saves, shares
- **Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)**: Cost efficiency metric
- **Ad Recall Lift**: How well people remember the ad and brand
- **Brand Awareness Lift**: Increase in brand awareness via surveys/polls
- **Social Media Engagement Metrics**: Followers, likes, comments, shares on organic posts

### Tools for Tracking
- Facebook Ads Manager (detailed performance metrics)
- Instagram Insights (organic performance)
- Third-party tools: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse

### Ad Campaign Setup Steps
1. Converted Instagram account to Business Account
2. Created Facebook Ads Manager account
3. Set up Ad Account with payment method
4. Chose "Awareness" objective
5. Defined target audience
6. Created ad with visual content
7. Set budget and schedule
8. Launched campaign

### Key Analysis Questions
- Are reach and impressions increasing over time?
- Is engagement rate improving?
- Is CPM decreasing?
- Are people remembering the brand and ad?
