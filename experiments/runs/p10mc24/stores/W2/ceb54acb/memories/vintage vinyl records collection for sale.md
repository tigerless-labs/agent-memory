## Recommended Selling Platforms
**Online Marketplaces:**
- **Discogs** — popular platform with price guide; create seller account
- **eBay** — use "completed listings" to research comparable prices
- **MusicStack** — dedicated music marketplace
- **Reverb LP** — specialty marketplace for rare/collectible records

**Specialty Record Stores:**
- **Vinyl Me, Please** — subscription club that also buys/sells
- **The Vinyl Factory** — UK-based, focus on rare/collectible

**Communities:**
- **Reddit r/vinylcollectors** — community for buying/selling/trading
- **Vinyl Collective** — dedicated forum
- **Local record stores** — may buy or consign

## Selling Tips
1. Research prices on Discogs, eBay for comparable records
2. Grade records accurately (condition affects value significantly)
3. Provide detailed descriptions and high-quality photos (record, sleeve, inserts)
4. Consider bundling records to increase appeal
5. Be open to negotiation
6. Package carefully to prevent shipping damage
