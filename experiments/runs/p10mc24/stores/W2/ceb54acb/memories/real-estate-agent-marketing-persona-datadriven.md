## Performance Marketing SaaS Persona: Susan Green

**Data source**: National Association of REALTORS (NAR) research-statistics — https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics

**Demographics:**
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female (reflects typical agent base)
- Location: Suburban or rural area
- Income: $61,140 median (2019, per NAR)
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

**Personality:**
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable and continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with good market knowledge

**Work Habits:**
- Works independently with brokerage support
- Builds strong client and colleague relationships
- Active in local real estate community
- Maintains current knowledge of trends and regulations

**Pain Points:**
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking/analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents and brokerages
- Keeping up with latest real estate tech and trends

**Goals:**
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Stay ahead of competition
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

**Technology Usage:**
- CRM software (managing clients/properties)
- Email marketing and communication tools
- Social media (personal and professional)
- Open to new technology for work efficiency

**Marketing Channels:**
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking and referrals
- Direct mail and email
- Social media advertising

**Marketing Budget:**
- Balances cost-effectiveness with quality
- Monitors and adjusts based on performance
- Willing to invest in solutions showing ROI
- Leverages brokerage marketing support where available

**Key insight:** This persona differs from tech-forward, high-income assumptions; real estate agent market skews toward mature, suburban-based professionals managing resource constraints and competing on service and local reputation, not pure tech differentiation.
