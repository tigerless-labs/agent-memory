## Selling Tips
1. Research prices on eBay, Etsy, Ruby Lane for comparable typewriters
2. Clean and restore to original condition to increase value
3. Provide detailed photos (typewriter, mechanisms, accessories) and descriptions
4. Test the machine and demonstrate functionality (test page or video)
5. Include original accessories if available (case, ribbons, manuals)
6. Bundle accessories to increase overall sale value
7. Package carefully to prevent damage during transit
