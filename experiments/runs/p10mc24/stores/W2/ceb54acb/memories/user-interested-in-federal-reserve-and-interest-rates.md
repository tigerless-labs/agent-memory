## Interest in Federal Reserve & Economics

User wants to understand:
- How the Federal Reserve uses interest rates to influence the economy
- FOMC meeting schedule (8 times per year) and decision-making process
- How Fed policy affects everyday personal finances
- How to stay informed about interest rate changes

## Key Mechanisms Discussed

**How Fed adjusts rates:**
- Lowers federal funds rate → cheaper bank borrowing → lower consumer loan rates → stimulates growth
- Raises federal funds rate → expensive borrowing → higher consumer loan rates → slows growth
- FOMC meets 8 times/year to review GDP, inflation, unemployment, consumer spending

**Personal finance impacts:**
- Mortgages: rising rates make homes less affordable; falling rates make homeownership more accessible
- Credit cards: rising rates increase borrowing costs
- Savings accounts: rising rates offer higher returns; falling rates offer lower returns
- Inflation: higher rates keep inflation in check; lower rates can fuel inflation

## Historical examples discussed
- Great Depression (1929-1933): initial rate increases worsened downturn; 1933 expansion policy
- 1970s-1980s: tightened monetary policy to control high inflation
- 2007-2009 Financial Crisis: near-zero rates, new lending facilities, large securities purchases
- COVID-19 2020: rate cuts, lending facilities, securities purchases

## User's Action Items
- Monitor financial news for interest rate changes
- Check bank account and mortgage rates
- Consider consulting financial advisor if needed
