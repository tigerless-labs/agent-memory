## Film Analysis Interests

Fascinated by how filmmakers use symbolism to add depth and meaning. Appreciates detailed analysis of iconic scenes and visual motifs.

Notable discussions:
- **The Godfather** — horse head scene cited as iconic moment; oranges discussed as symbol of impending danger/death
- Values balance between symbolism enhancement and story clarity
- Sometimes feels filmmakers overuse symbolism, making it contrived or pretentious rather than enhancing narrative

Prefers when symbolism is thoughtful and subtle rather than heavy-handed.
