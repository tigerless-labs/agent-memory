## Films with Effective Symbolism

### Heavy Symbolic Use
- **The Shining** (1980, Stanley Kubrick) — maze represents characters' minds; red symbolizes danger and violence
- **Blade Runner** (1982, Ridley Scott) — eyes, animals, and nature explore identity, humanity, existence
- **Get Out** (2017, Jordan Peele) — white symbolizes oppressive racism; deer imagery represents victimhood
- **The Matrix** (1999) — red and blue pills symbolize choice/free will; explores nature of reality

### Great Films Without Heavy Symbolism
- Casablanca (1942)
- The Shawshank Redemption (1994)
- Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope (1977)

**Key takeaway**: Symbolism enhances but isn't required for greatness; effective symbolism requires filmmaker skill and restraint.
