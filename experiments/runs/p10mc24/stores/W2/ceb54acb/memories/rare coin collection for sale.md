## Selling Strategy
1. Research comparable coin prices on eBay, NGC, CoinWeek
2. Get coins professionally graded and certified by NGC or PCGS (increases value)
3. Provide detailed photos (obverse, reverse) and high-quality descriptions
4. Document condition carefully, include any certificates of authenticity
5. Be prepared to provide provenance documentation (receipts, certificates, letters)
6. For extremely rare/valuable coins: consider consignment to Heritage Auctions or Stack's Bowers
7. Package securely to prevent damage during transit
