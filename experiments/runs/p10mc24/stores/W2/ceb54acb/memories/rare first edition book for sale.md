## Recommended Selling Platforms
**Online Marketplaces:**
- **AbeBooks** — leading platform for rare/used/out-of-print books
- **Biblio** — popular for rare books and first editions
- **Alibris** — marketplace for new, used, and rare books

**Specialty Bookstores:**
- **The Strand** — NYC independent bookstore with rare book department; buys/sells
- **Powell's City of Books** — Portland, Oregon; extensive rare book room
- **Peter Harrington Rare Books** — UK-based rare book dealer

**Communities & Auctions:**
- **Reddit r/rarebooks** — community for buying/selling/trading
- **Book Collectors' Forum** — dedicated forum
- **Christie's** — auction house with rare books department
- **Sotheby's** — prominent auction house; rare book department with regular auctions

## Selling Strategy
1. Consider professional book appraisal to establish value
2. Research comparable sales on AbeBooks, Biblio, Alibris
3. Provide detailed descriptions and high-quality photos (book, dust jacket, signatures, inscriptions)
4. Document condition carefully, including any flaws
5. Be prepared to provide provenance (receipts, letters, documentation of history)
6. For extremely rare/valuable books: consider consignment to auction house for higher price
7. Package carefully to prevent damage during shipping
