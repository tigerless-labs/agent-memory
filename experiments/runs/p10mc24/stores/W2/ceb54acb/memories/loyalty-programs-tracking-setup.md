## Loyalty Program Tracking Setup

- **FreshMart** — points-based system
- **The Daily Grind** — stamp-based system; needs 14 stamps for free drink; currently has 12 stamps (needs 2 more)
- **PetPalooza** — points-based system

**Tracking method:** Spreadsheet with columns for Program Name, Points/Rewards, Earned, Needed for Reward, Expiration Date, Notes

- Set phone reminders to use loyalty apps/cards when near specific stores or at certain times

**Goal:** Maximize rewards and points; particularly focused on earning the last 2 stamps at The Daily Grind for the free drink

**Next steps:**
- Grab coffee at The Daily Grind soon to earn the 2 remaining stamps
