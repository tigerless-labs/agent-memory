## Trail Finding Resources

### Websites and Apps
- **Trailforks**: Trail search and tracking
- **MTB Project**: Trail information and community
- **TrailLink**: Trail database
- **MapMyRide**: Route tracking and sharing
- **IMBA Epics**: International Mountain Bicycling Association's must-ride trails

### How to Find Trails
1. Use trail websites/apps with location and difficulty filters
2. Visit local bike shops for insider recommendations
3. Check national forests, national parks, state parks
4. Search for IMBA Epics in your area

### Trail Selection Criteria
- **Distance**: 20-30 mile loops or out-and-back routes (for moderate riders)
- **Difficulty**: Moderate (blue or green IMBA ratings)
- **Elevation gain**: 1,000-2,000 feet
- **Trail surface**: Mix of smooth singletrack, rocky sections, technical features

### Popular Mountain Biking Destinations in US
- Park City, Utah
- Moab, Utah
- Bend, Oregon
- Asheville, North Carolina
- Lake Tahoe, California/Nevada

## Bike Shops and Rentals

### Major Chains
- REI (Recreational Equipment Inc.)
- Performance Bicycle
- Trek Bicycle Stores
- Giant Bicycle

### Services to Look For
- Guided tours for beginners/intermediate riders
- Half-day, full-day, or multi-day rentals
- Lessons or clinics (beginner skills, cornering, descending)
- Bike maintenance and repair services

### How to Find Local Shops
1. Google search: "bike shops near me" or "mountain bike rentals near me"
2. Online directories: Yelp, TripAdvisor, Google Maps
3. Visit bike shop websites
4. Follow local shops on social media (Facebook, Instagram)
5. Check local outdoor organizations and recreation centers

## Mountain Biking Festivals and Events

### Major Events
- **Sea Otter Classic**: Four-day festival in California with racing, demos, exhibitions
- **Crankworx**: Series at multiple locations with racing, slopestyle, and other events (Whistler, BC and others)
- **Mountain Bike Festivals**: Various locations with group rides, demos, live music
- **USA Cycling National Championships**: Series of national-level events in different cycling disciplines

### How to Find Events
1. Google search: "cycling events near me" or "mountain bike festivals in [region]"
2. Local cycling clubs and advocacy groups
3. USA Cycling website
4. Social media: Follow local bike shops, cycling clubs, outdoor organizations
5. Event websites and local visitor centers

### Important Tips
- Register in advance; spots fill quickly
- Check trail conditions and regulations before heading out
- Bring water, snacks, sunscreen
- Wear protective gear: helmet, gloves, knee pads
- Ride within your ability level
