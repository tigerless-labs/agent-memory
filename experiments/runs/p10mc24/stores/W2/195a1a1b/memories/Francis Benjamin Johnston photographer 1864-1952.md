**Francis Benjamin Johnston** (1864-1952) was an American photographer and pioneering woman in professional photography.

**Background:**
- Born into prominent Virginia family
- Educated at private schools
- Opened studio in Washington, D.C. in 1894
- Quickly established reputation as one of the city's most talented and innovative photographers

**Major contributions:**
- **Architectural photography pioneer** — one of first photographers to recognize importance of documenting America's historic buildings; traveled extensively photographing colonial-era buildings and historic structures; helped raise awareness of preserving America's architectural heritage
- **Portrait photography** — well-known for portraits of politicians, celebrities, socialites

**Notable subjects portrayed:**
- Theodore Roosevelt
- Mark Twain
- Susan B. Anthony
- Alice Roosevelt Longworth
- Many others

**Style:**
- Portraits characterized by elegance, simplicity, attention to detail
- Established her reputation as one of leading photographers of her time

**Philosophy:**
- Committed to idea that photography could be powerful tool for social change
- Believed photography could raise awareness of social issues and inspire action
- Work celebrated for technical excellence, artistic innovation, enduring influence on American photography
