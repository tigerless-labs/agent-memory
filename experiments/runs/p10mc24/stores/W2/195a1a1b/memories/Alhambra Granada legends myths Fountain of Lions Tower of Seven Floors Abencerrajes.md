## Alhambra Legends and Myths

User was intrigued by the cultural and historical mythology associated with the Alhambra.

**Fountain of Lions**
- Legend: Drinking from the Fountain of Lions in the Palace of the Lions will make you fall in love with Granada and never want to leave

**Tower of the Seven Floors**
- Said to be haunted by ghost of princess
- Princess imprisoned by her father for forbidden love with Christian knight
- Adds mystique to the tower

**Hall of Abencerrajes**
- Believed to be site of massacre of the Abencerraje family
- Legend claims their bloodstains can still be seen on the walls
- Represents tragic historical event embedded in architecture

**Devil's Megaphone (Generalife Palace Gardens)**
- Specific spot with perfect acoustics
- Legend says devil can be heard speaking through the echo
- Related to supernatural folklore of the palace

**Cultural Significance**
- Legends add sense of mystery and magic to location
- Reflect beliefs and values of people who created them
- May contain kernel of truth or be entirely fictional
- Part of cultural and historical heritage value
