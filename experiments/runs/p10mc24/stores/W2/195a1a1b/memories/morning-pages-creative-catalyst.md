## Morning Pages as Creative Catalyst

**Practice:** Free-flowing morning pages where thoughts guide the writing. No strict rules—sometimes specific topics, usually spontaneous.

**Benefits for creative work:**
- Helps develop characters by exploring their thoughts, feelings, and motivations
- Generates story ideas and themes
- Serves as source material for poems
- Allows emotional processing and reflection

**Workflow:** Writes about themes or ideas in morning pages → Later revisits and develops into full creative work (poems, short story scenes, etc.)

**Evidence:** February 2, 2023 poem originated as morning pages entry about a winter park walk, later revised and expanded into complete poem

**Impact on writing practice:** Morning pages have contributed to consistency in creative output and deeper engagement with personal reflection
