## Accountability & Transparency

**Problem:** Manual systems have loopholes; corruption and mismanagement common when NGOs work with local institutions.

**Solution:** 
- Beneficiaries receive allocations from authorized vendors using smart card
- POS machine processes transaction, issues receipt to beneficiary
- All transactions monitored in real-time by project team
- Complete transparency in whole process
- Prevents corruption through automated, documented transactions

## Offline Capability for Remote Areas

**Problem:** Most remote African areas lack internet or have slow connectivity.

**Solution:**
- Redemption process works offline (no internet required at vendor)
- NFC cards hold beneficiary data locally
- Devices sync data locally during distribution
- After complete offline distribution, devices sync to database
- Enables monitoring in areas with no connectivity

## User-Friendly Redemption

**Process for beneficiary:**
1. Tap smart card on vendor's POS terminal
2. Choose items
3. Enter PIN code to finalize transaction

Simple process compared to manual alternatives; seamless experience for end users.
