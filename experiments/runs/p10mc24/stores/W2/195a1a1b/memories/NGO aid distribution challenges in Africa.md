## Key Challenges

1. **Lack of infrastructure** — roads, bridges, ports absent in many areas, making remote aid transport difficult
2. **Political instability & conflict** — unsafe operating environment for NGOs
3. **Corruption** — bribery and misappropriation common when distributing aid
4. **Cultural & language barriers** — difficulty communicating with local communities
5. **Limited resources** — insufficient funding, personnel, and equipment
6. **High operational costs** — importing goods/services, staff salaries, transportation elevated compared to other regions
7. **Lack of reliable and flexible accountability systems** — NGOs struggle to track, report transparently, demonstrate impact; donors lack confidence; difficult to adapt strategies to changing environments

## Impact
Without reliable accountability, NGOs cannot ensure aid reaches intended recipients or verify positive impact. Lack of flexible systems prevents adapting to community needs or operational changes.
