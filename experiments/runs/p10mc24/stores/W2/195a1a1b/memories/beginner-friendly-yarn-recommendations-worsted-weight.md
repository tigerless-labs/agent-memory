## Popular Beginner Worsted Weight Yarn Options

For beginner knitters looking for soft, warm yarn with good stitch definition:

**1. Malabrigo Worsted**
- Soft with subtle sheen
- Great stitch definition
- Wide range of colors
- Popular among knitters
- Good for beanies and scarves

**2. Cascade 220**
- Classic, affordable option
- Soft and warm
- Easy to work with
- Very wide color range
- Excellent for beginners
- Great value

**3. Plymouth Yarn Company Worsted**
- Soft with subtle texture
- Great stitch definition
- Good range of colors
- Traditional choice for knitters

**4. Berroco Ultra Alpaca**
- Soft, lightweight
- Subtle sheen
- Great stitch definition
- Good for sensitive skin
- Preferred by those wanting lighter weight

## Tips for Success

- Always swatch before starting (check gauge/tension)
- Choose needles suitable for yarn weight
- Maintain even tension while knitting
- Start with simple patterns to build confidence
