**Paul Strand** (1890-1976) was an American photographer and pioneer of modernist and straight photography movements.

**Early life & training:**
- Born in New York City
- Largely self-taught
- Strongly influenced by Alfred Stieglitz and modernist photographers
- Became member of Stieglitz's Photo-Secession group in 1915

**Career focus:**
- 1920s onward: focused on documentary photography
- Photographed everyday life, social issues, rural communities, urban landscapes, industrial sites
- Characterized by simplicity, directness, social purpose

**Major work:**
- Series of photographs of people and landscapes of **Mexico beginning in 1932** — considered some of the most important examples of documentary photography from the period; established his reputation as leading photographer of his generation

**Philosophy:**
- Deeply committed to using photography as a powerful tool for social change
- Believed photography could raise awareness of social injustices and inspire action
- Work known for innovative style, social relevance, enduring influence on modern photography
