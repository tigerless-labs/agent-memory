## Journal Practice

**Start date:** January 15, 2023

**Primary uses:**
- Morning pages (free-flowing thoughts)
- Occasional poetry
- Clearing mind and processing emotions
- Reflecting on progress and goals (especially for writing projects)

**Approach:** Lets thoughts flow freely onto the page. Sometimes has specific topics to write about, but usually lets the writing guide itself.

**Impact:** Has become a significant tool for personal reflection and creative development. Morning pages entries often evolve into poems and story scenes for other projects.
