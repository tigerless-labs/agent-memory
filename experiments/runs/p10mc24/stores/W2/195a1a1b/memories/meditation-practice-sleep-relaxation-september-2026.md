## Meditation Practice for Sleep and Relaxation

**Tried & Feedback:**

1. **Sleep by Headspace** (10 minutes)
   - Status: Completed, very positive experience
   - Effect: Very relaxing and calming, helped mind unwind, made falling asleep easier
   - Most liked: Imagery and visualization part (helped distract from stressful thoughts, focused on relaxation)
   - Structure: Introduction (1min) → Breathing and Relaxation (4min) → Imagery/Visualization (3min) → Final Relaxation (2min)
   - Plan to continue using this regularly

**Planning to Try:**

2. **Body Scan by Insight Timer** (10 minutes)
   - Purpose: Full-body awareness and tension release
   - Expected structure: Introduction → Body scan from toes to head → Breathing/relaxation → Conclusion
   - Focus: Notice sensations without judgment, release tension

**Meditation Apps Recommended:**

- **Headspace**: Free trial, then subscription; guided meditations for sleep, stress, focus
- **Calm**: Free trial, then subscription; sleep stories, relaxing music
- **Insight Timer**: Free app; vast library including sleep-specific meditations
- **Meditation Studio**: Free with in-app purchases; variety of sleep/stress/anxiety meditations
- **Buddhify**: One-time purchase; guided meditations for different times of day

**Other Sleep Meditations Recommended:**
- Deep Sleep by Calm (20 min)
- Sleep Meditation by Andrew Weil (10 min, available on YouTube)
- Sleep Story: The Magical Forest by Calm (20 min)
- Progressive Muscle Relaxation by Meditation Studio (10 min)
- Rainy Night by Rainy Mood (10 min)

**Best Practices:**
- Start with short meditations (5-10 min), increase as comfortable
- Use headphones to block external noise
- Find quiet, comfortable space
- Expect mind to wander; gently redirect attention
- Make meditation part of daily routine for consistency
- Goal: Use 20-30 min before bed as part of wind-down routine
