## Alhambra History

**Etymology & Name**
- Word "Alhambra" derived from Arabic "Al-Hamra" meaning "the red one"
- Named for reddish hue of walls and towers

**Construction & Medieval Period**
- Built 13th-14th centuries during Moorish reign in Spain
- Constructed as fortress to defend against Christian attacks
- Also served as royal palace for Nasrid dynasty
- Residence of sultan and his court
- Center of political and cultural activity during Nasrid period

**After Conquest**
- Christian conquest of Granada: 1492
- Used as royal palace by Catholic monarchs
- Several additions made to complex by Catholic rulers

**Modern Era**
- Fell into disrepair over centuries
- Restoration work: 19th and 20th centuries
- Today: One of Spain's most visited landmarks
- Status: UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Stands as testament to Moorish art, architecture, and culture in Spain
