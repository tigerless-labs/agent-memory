## Websites and Online Courses
- **Soapstone Carving Academy** (soapstonecarvingacademy.com): Comprehensive online course platform with tutorials, videos, beginner to intermediate
- **The Sculpture School** (thesculptureschool.com): Online courses and tutorials for various sculpting techniques including soapstone
- **Craftsy** (craftsy.com): Online learning platform with soapstone and stone carving courses
- **Udemy** (udemy.com): Courses on soapstone carving, stone carving, and related topics

## YouTube Channels
- **Soapstone Carving with Rachel** (youtube.com/soapstonecarvingwithrachel): Rachel Brooks teaching soapstone techniques, tools, projects
- **The Stone Carver** (youtube.com/thestonecarver): Tom Wolfe - stone carving including soapstone with tutorials and demonstrations
- **Stone Sculpture Tutorials** (youtube.com/stonesculpturetutorials): Beginner-friendly stone sculpture including soapstone
- **Carving Soapstone with Michael** (youtube.com/carvingssoapstonewithmichael): Michael Maddy sharing soapstone carving techniques

## Tutorials and Guides
- **Soapstone Carving for Beginners** - The Spruce Crafts (thesprucecrafts.com): Comprehensive guide on basics, tools, techniques, projects
- **Soapstone Carving Tutorial** - Instructables (instructables.com): Step-by-step tutorial on basic techniques and tools
- **Stone Carving 101** - Sculpture.org (sculpture.org): Beginner's guide to stone carving including soapstone, tools, techniques, safety
- **Soapstone Carving Tips and Techniques** - ArtMinds (artminds.com): Collection of tips, techniques, tutorials

- **Stone Carving Forum** (stonecarvingforum.com): Community forum for stone carvers including soapstone enthusiasts
