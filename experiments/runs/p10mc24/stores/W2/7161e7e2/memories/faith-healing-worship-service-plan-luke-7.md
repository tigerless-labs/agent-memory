## Worship Service Plan: Faith Healing (Luke 7:1-17 Theme)

**Structure:**

1. **Opening Hymn:** "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters
   - Sets tone: God's power, authority, sovereignty
   
2. **Call to Worship and Prayer**
   - Pastor/worship leader invites congregation to open hearts, come before God in faith
   - Ask for healing and guidance
   
3. **Scripture Reading:** Luke 7:1-17
   - Read by congregation member, pastor, or worship leader
   
4. **Message:** Sermon on Luke 7:1-17
   - Focus on faith, healing, God's power
   
5. **Hymn of Response:** "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley
   - Response to message: praise and gratitude
   
6. **Testimony**
   - Congregation member shares testimony of experiencing healing through faith in Jesus
   - Encourages others to have faith and trust in God's power to heal
   
7. **Hymn of Faith:** "Amazing Grace" by John Newton
   - Classic hymn affirming forgiveness and healing through faith
   
8. **Healing Prayer**
   - Pastor/worship leader leads congregation in prayer for those needing physical, emotional, or spiritual healing
   
9. **Closing Hymn:** "Healer" by Kari Jobe
   - Reminds congregation of God's power to heal and restore
   - Encourages trust in Him
   
10. **Benediction**
    - Pastor/worship leader reminds congregation: God is with you; have faith in His power to heal and restore

**Notes:** Adaptable to context and congregation preferences; different songs can substitute while maintaining the theme progression.
