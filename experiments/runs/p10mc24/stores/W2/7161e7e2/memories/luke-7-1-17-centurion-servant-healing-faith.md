## The Passage: Luke 7:1-17

**Summary:** A Roman centurion's beloved servant was dying. The centurion, through Jewish elders and friends, asked Jesus to heal his servant without Jesus needing to come to his house. The centurion expressed faith in Jesus's authority—"say the word, and my servant will be healed"—comparing Jesus's power to his own authority over soldiers. Jesus was amazed at this faith and healed the servant from a distance.

**Key themes:**
- Faith in Jesus's power and authority
- Faith transcending nationality and ethnicity (centurion was a foreigner, yet had more faith than the Jewish elders and Israel)
- Authority and the spoken word of Jesus

## Biblical Theology of Faith Healing

**Core principles:**
1. God has the power to heal through faith in Him
2. Healing is part of Christ's atonement (Isaiah 53:5) and available to all believers
3. Examples throughout OT (Elijah, Elisha) and NT (Jesus, apostles in Acts)
4. Faith is necessary but not the only factor; God's sovereignty remains
5. Healing connects to both physical restoration and spiritual salvation
