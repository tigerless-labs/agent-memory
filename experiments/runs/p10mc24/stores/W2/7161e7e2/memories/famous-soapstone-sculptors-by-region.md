## Inuit Soapstone Tradition
- **Kenojuak Ashevak** (Canada, 1927-2013): Pioneering Inuit artist known for colorful prints and soapstone sculptures
- **Kiawak Ashoona** (Canada, 1933-2014): Renowned Inuit sculptor and printmaker working with soapstone, whalebone

## African Soapstone Sculptors
- **Nicholas Mukomberanwa** (Zimbabwe, 1940-2002): Prominent Zimbabwean sculptor working with soapstone, wood; figurative and abstract pieces
- **Joram Mariga** (Zimbabwe, b. 1927): Zimbabwean sculptor creating intricate figurative sculptures in soapstone and wood

## Contemporary Soapstone Sculptors (USA)
- **Susan Taylor Glasgow** (USA, b. 1950): Known for delicate, intricate soapstone carvings of flowers, leaves, organic forms
- **Mark Lundeen** (USA, b. 1958): Colorado-based sculptor creating realistic, detailed soapstone sculptures of animals, humans, abstract forms
- **Tim Lewis** (USA, b. 1952): Works with soapstone, wood creating abstract, figurative, surreal pieces
- **Clare Leighton** (UK, 1898-1989): British sculptor working with soapstone, wood; figurative and abstract sculptures
