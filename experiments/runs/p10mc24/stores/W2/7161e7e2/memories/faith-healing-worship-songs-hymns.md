## Worship Songs & Hymns on Faith Healing Theme

**Modern worship songs:**
- "Healer" by Kari Jobe — expresses belief in God's power to heal and restore
- "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters — faith in God's power and authority, sovereignty over all things
- "I Believe in Miracles" by Jackson Browne — faith in God's power to heal and perform miracles (note: secular context but expresses this faith)

**Classic hymns:**
- "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley — joy and gratitude for salvation offered by Jesus; his power to heal and save
- "Amazing Grace" by John Newton — through faith in Jesus, we can be forgiven and healed; one of the most well-known hymns
- "There is Power in the Blood" by Lewis E. Jones — through faith in Jesus and his shed blood, we can be forgiven and healed
- "Heal Me, O Lord" by William Walsham How — through faith in Jesus, we can be healed and restored

All express belief in God's power to heal and restore through faith.
