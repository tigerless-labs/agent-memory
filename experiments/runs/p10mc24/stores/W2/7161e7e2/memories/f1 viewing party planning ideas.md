## Decorations
- Checkered flags, banners, or tablecloths
- Racing team colors (Ferrari red, Mercedes silver, Red Bull blue/red)
- F1-themed balloons (cars, helmets, tires)
- Racing-themed signs ("Pit Stop," "Start/Finish Line," "Grand Prix")
- F1 memorabilia (model cars, helmets, racing suits)

## Food & Drinks
- Pit stop snacks: "Pit Stop Pretzels," "Racing Wings," "Fuel-up Nachos"
- International cuisine (e.g., Middle Eastern for Bahrain)
- Racing-themed cocktails: "Checkered Flag," "Pit Stop Punch," "Formula 1 Fuel"
- Grid-themed desserts: checkered flag cake, F1 car-shaped cookies

## Activities
- F1 trivia contest
- "Predict the Podium" game (guess top 3 finishers, award prizes)
- F1-themed games ("Pin the Number on the F1 Car," "F1 Bingo")
- Fantasy F1 league (guests pick drivers, earn points)
- Photo booth with F1 props (helmets, racing suits, flags)

## Technical Setup
- Large screen with quality sound system
- Comfortable seating
- Encourage guests to wear favorite F1 team/driver gear
- Time party around race schedule (include pre- and post-race)
