## Peace Lily Care Setup

**Acquisition:** Purchased from nursery on May 6, 2023 (two weeks before May 20 conversation).

**Condition:** Has been losing some leaves, but this is normal for plants adjusting to new environment.

**Fertilization:** Using a water + fertilizer mixture purchased from the nursery where the peace lily was bought. Concentration and NPK ratio unknown; following nursery instructions.

**Watering & Fertilizing Schedule:**
- Uses a plant tracking app to track watering and fertilization reminders
- Water when top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry to touch
- Spring/summer: every 7-10 days
- Fall/winter: every 10-14 days
- Fertilize (as part of water mixture) every 1-2 weeks during growing season; once a month in dormant season

**Humidity & Misting:**
- Prefers humidity (peace lily can tolerate 40-50% relative humidity)
- Mist 1-2 times per week (not as frequently as fern)
- Mist in morning; use room-temperature water
- Avoid misting soil or crown

**Temperature:** Currently keeping at 65°F to 70°F (18°C to 21°C)
- Ideal range: 68°F (20°C) to 72°F (22°C)
- Acceptable range: 65°F (18°C) to 75°F (24°C)
- Avoid temperatures below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C)

**Care Tips:**
- Check soil moisture by finger test up to first knuckle
- Water thoroughly; ensure good drainage to prevent waterlogging
- Remove dead/dying leaves as needed
- Watch for pests (spider mites, mealybugs, scale)
