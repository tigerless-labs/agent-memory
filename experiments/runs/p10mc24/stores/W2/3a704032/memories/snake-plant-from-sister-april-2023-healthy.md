## Snake Plant — Thriving

**Origin:** Received from sister in April 2023 (May 2023 conversation date).

**Current status:** Doing very well with user's existing watering and fertilizer routine. User was considering repotting but decided to **wait and monitor** before taking action.

**Repotting decision:** Not currently needed. Snake plants tolerate being slightly root-bound and can handle some neglect. Only repot if:
- Roots are truly bound (circling, growing through drainage holes)
- Soil is depleted
- Visible significant growth needed

**If/when repotting:**
- Use pot only 1–2 sizes larger (prefers tight conditions)
- Use well-draining potting mix (susceptible to root rot)
- Pot in spring/summer during active growth
- Water sparingly after repotting (plant shock risk)
