## Fern Pest Problem & Treatment Plan

**Pest identification:** Tiny, moving dots on leaves and stems. Likely spider mites (feed on sap, cause yellowing/stippling, may web) or springtails (feed on algae/fungi, harmless but nuisance, attracted to moisture).

**Treatment approach (user's plan):**
1. Treat with neem oil per product instructions
2. Wait a few days for treatment to take effect
3. Start regular misting 2–3 times per week

**Misting guidelines (after neem oil treatment):**
- Use **distilled or filtered water** only (avoid tap water mineral buildup)
- Mist **early morning** so leaves dry before nightfall (prevents fungal disease)
- Mist **undersides of leaves** thoroughly where pests hide
- **Avoid the crown** (where leaves meet stem) to prevent rot
- Use fine mist only, no strong jets (jets spread pests)

**Humidity target:** 40–60% relative humidity (ideal for ferns).

User will monitor fern closely and repeat neem oil treatment if needed. If problem persists, consult local nursery expert.
