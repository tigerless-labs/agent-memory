## Gochugaru: Korean Chili Flakes

**What it is:**
- Made from dried and crushed Korean chili peppers
- Most common type used in kimchi-making
- Heat level ranges from mild to extremely hot depending on brand/batch

**Selection Factors:**
1. Heat level (start mild if new; adjust upward to taste)
2. Flavor profile (can be smoky, fruity, or sweet depending on pepper variety and region)
3. Texture (fine grind distributes evenly; coarse grind provides texture)
4. Brand/quality (look for reputable brands with good reputation)

**Common Variants:**
- Standard **Gochugaru**: medium-to-hot, balanced heat and flavor
- **Cheongyang gochugaru**: intense heat with fruity notes (very spicy)
- **Regional varieties**: Jeju Island or Gwangju brands may have unique profiles

**Storage:**
- Keep in airtight container
- Store in cool, dark, dry place away from light and moisture
- Preserves potency and flavor when stored properly

**Usage Tips:**
- Start with small amounts; heat varies by brand
- Mix with garlic, ginger, fish sauce, and gochujang for balanced seasoning paste
- Can combine with other chili pepper types (gochu, serrano) for added depth
- Adjust during fermentation based on taste testing
