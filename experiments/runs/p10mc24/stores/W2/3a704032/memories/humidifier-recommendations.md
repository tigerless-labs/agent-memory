## Humidifier Types
- **Cool mist** — best for most plants
- **Warm mist** — for heat-loving plants, more expensive
- **Ultrasonic** — quieter, energy-efficient

## Key Features to Look For
- Built-in humidistat for automatic humidity control
- Adjustable mist output
- Appropriate tank size (larger = less refilling)
- Low noise level (important for African violets, which are vibration-sensitive)
- Easy to clean (prevents bacterial growth and fungal diseases)

## Recommended Humidifiers
1. **Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Humidifier** — cool/warm settings, humidistat, 6-liter tank
2. **Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier** — compact, 1-liter tank, easy-to-use
3. **Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier** — ultrasonic, 2.5-liter tank, sleek design
4. **Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier** — whisper-quiet, 1-liter tank

## Tips for Use
- Place within 3-5 feet of plants (1-2 feet for African violets)
- Use hygrometer to monitor humidity
- Clean regularly to prevent bacterial/fungal growth
- African violets need 50-60% humidity, good air circulation, and no standing water
