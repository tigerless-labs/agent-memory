## Garlic and Ginger Preparation for Kimchi

**Garlic:**
- Use fresh garlic cloves (avoid old or sprouted)
- Preparation: mince or crush to release oils and flavor
  - Tools: garlic press, microplane, or fine knife chopping
- Amount: 2–3 cloves per 2 cups of vegetables (adjust to taste)
- Mix with gochugaru, fish sauce, and ginger for balanced flavor

**Ginger:**
- Use fresh ginger (young or mature, both work)
- Preparation: peel, then grate or slice thinly
  - Tools: microplane or box grater for grating
- Amount: 1–2 inches (grated or sliced) per 2 cups of vegetables (adjust to taste; potent ingredient)
- Mix with garlic, gochugaru, and fish sauce for seasoning paste

**Handling Best Practices:**
- Handle gently to avoid bruising or unnecessary crushing (can release bitterness)
- Use separate cutting board and knife to prevent flavor transfer to other ingredients
- Optional: sauté garlic and ginger in oil before adding to kimchi to mellow their potency

**Flavor Integration:**
- Garlic provides pungent, savory depth
- Ginger provides spicy, warming, complex notes
- Together they balance the heat from chili flakes and salt content
