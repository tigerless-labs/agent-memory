## Kimchi Recipe Experimentation

**First Attempt:** May 21, 2023 (Sunday)
- Over-salted and over-spiced; identified main issues as excessive chili flakes and salt
- Plan: rinse kimchi under cold running water to remove excess salt

**Next Attempt Strategy:**
- Using **gochugaru** (Korean chili flakes) for medium-to-hot heat with balanced flavor
- Adding **garlic and ginger** to improve flavor balance and depth
- Measuring by weight when possible to avoid over-adding

**Key Measurements for Next Batch:**
- **Gochugaru:** 
  - Mild: 1/4–1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables
  - Medium: 1/2–1 tsp per 2 cups vegetables
  - Hot: 1–2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables
  - (Start on lower end; can always add more)
- **Garlic:** 2–3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables, minced or crushed
- **Ginger:** 1–2 inches (fresh, peeled, grated or sliced) per 2 cups vegetables

**Preparation Approach:**
- Taste as you go throughout fermentation
- Consider short fermentation first to avoid compounding overly-salty/spicy flavors
- Monitor fermentation timeline to allow flavors to develop and mellow
