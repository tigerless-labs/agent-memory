Recommendations given (May 2023) for contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists and complex relationships, similar to The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo:

- **The Devil Wears Prada** by Lauren Weisberger — complex relationship between demanding boss and assistant, female-focused
- **The Mothers** by Brit Bennett — coming-of-age in Southern California community, themes of motherhood and identity
- **The Girls** by Emma Cline — 1960s, teenage girls in cult, explores female relationships and power dynamics
- **The Interestings** by Meg Wolitzer — friends from summer arts camp, complex relationships over years
- **Conversations with Friends** by Sally Rooney — two women and couple they become involved with, power/identity/desire
- **The Female Persuasion** by Meg Wolitzer — young woman's mentor relationship with famous feminist, identity and power themes
- **The Last Romantics** by Tara Conklin — four siblings, complex relationships and mutual influence over years
- **The Age of Light** by Whitney Scharer — based on true story of Lee Miller (model/photographer), 1920s-1930s Paris, complex relationships

User seeking narratives with complex female relationships and identity exploration.
