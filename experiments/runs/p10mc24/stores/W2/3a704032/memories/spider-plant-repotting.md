## Spider Plant Repotting

### Why Repot
- Old potting mix becomes depleted of nutrients
- Spider plants benefit from fresh mix every 1-2 years

### Potting Mix Requirements
- Well-draining formula
- Rich in organic matter
- Key ingredients: peat moss/coconut coir, compost, perlite/vermiculite, worm castings

### Recommended Potting Mixes
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix

### Repotting Steps
1. Choose pot only one-two sizes larger (spider plants prefer slightly root-bound)
2. Gently remove from current pot without damaging roots
3. Inspect roots; trim any signs of rot
4. Add fresh potting mix, firming gently
5. Water thoroughly; keep soil moist (not waterlogged)
