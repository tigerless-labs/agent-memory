## Plant Collection

- **Peace lily** — recently brought home, losing leaves; needs bright indirect light, careful watering, humidity (50%+), 65-80°F, balanced fertilizer at half strength
- **Snake plant** (Sansevieria Trifasciata) — prefers balanced fertilizer (e.g., 20-20-20), slightly acidic pH (6.0-7.0), water-soluble or organic options
- **Succulents** — need low-nitrogen, cactus/succulent-specific fertilizer (higher phosphorus, e.g., 10-20-10)
- **Orchid** — unique requirements; needs high-phosphorus fertilizer (10-20-10 or 15-30-15), low-nitrogen, once-monthly feeding at half strength, specific orchid potting mix
- **Fern** — humidity-loving plant; user considering humidifier
- **African violets** — thrive at 50-60% humidity, prone to crispiness in dry air, sensitive to vibrations, prefer cool mist and good air circulation
- **Spider plant** — planning to repot in fresh potting mix; prefers well-draining mix with organic matter, slightly root-bound

## Care Interests
- Learning species-appropriate fertilizers rather than general-purpose
- Humidity management for tropical plants
- Proper potting mixes and repotting techniques
