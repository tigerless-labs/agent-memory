## Fern Plant Care

**Misting Frequency:** Every other day (more frequently than other houseplants in collection).

**Humidity Requirements:** Ferns thrive in very humid conditions, often above 50% relative humidity—higher than peace lily's 40-50% tolerance.

**Notes:** Different humidity needs from peace lily, which only needs 1-2 misting per week.
