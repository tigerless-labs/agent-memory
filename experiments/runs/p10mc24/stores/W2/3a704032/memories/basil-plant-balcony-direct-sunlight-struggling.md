## Basil Plant Care — Direct Sunlight Problem

The user's basil plant was struggling with excessive direct sunlight on the balcony. Moved to a shadier spot (partial shade, east/west-facing if possible).

**Care plan:**
- Provide filtered sunlight, avoid intense direct sun
- Adjust watering: less frequent when in shade, soil dries slower
- Maintain humidity via pebble tray or humidifier
- Fertilize sparingly (half-strength balanced liquid fertilizer)
- Prune regularly, pinch off flower buds to encourage leaf growth
- Monitor temperature: ideal 65–75°F (18–24°C)

**Soil:** Currently using general-purpose potting mix. Basil prefers well-draining, nutrient-rich soil (pH 6.0–7.0). Consider custom mix if needed:
- 2 parts peat moss/coconut coir
- 1 part perlite/vermiculite
- 1 part compost/worm castings
- 1 part general-purpose soil
