User is getting back into reading graphic novels and likes **Saga** by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples (blend of sci-fi, adventure, complex characters).

Recommended similar titles:

- **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda — fantasy epic, stunning art, complex characters, moral ambiguity
- **The Wicked and The Divine** by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie — mythology/pop culture intersection, gods reincarnate as pop stars
- **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston — superhero deconstruction, stranded heroes, character-driven, mysterious plot
- **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá — dysfunctional superhero family, prevents apocalypse, action/drama/humor blend
- **Descender** by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen — sci-fi epic about young robot boy hunted in hostile universe, explores identity and humanity
- **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra — dark gritty reality, biblical/mythological elements
- **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra — explores gender, power, humanity after mysterious event kills all males

User appreciates character-driven narratives with complex relationships and moral ambiguity.
