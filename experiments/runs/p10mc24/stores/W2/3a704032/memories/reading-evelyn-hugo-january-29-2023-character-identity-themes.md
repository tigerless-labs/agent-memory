Started reading **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo** by Taylor Jenkins Reid on January 29, 2023 — keeping user up at night, deeply engaged.

## What resonates:

**Character & Story**
- Character development, especially Evelyn's backstory as Cuban immigrant
- How her background shaped who she became
- Mystery surrounding her seven husbands
- Monique as active narrator, not passive listener; brings outside perspective
- Monique's own biases/life influence her perceptions of Evelyn — adds complexity

**Identity themes**
- Exploration of public persona vs. inner/true self — Evelyn performs for public, vulnerable person beneath
- Resonates with universal human experience: we all wear masks/personas in different situations
- Conflict between public and private selves
- Intersectionality of Evelyn's identity: woman, Latina, immigrant — shapes experience profoundly
- Societal expectations place pressure on her identity

**Female relationships**
- Portrayal of relationships between women (Celia, Monique, Lucy) — one of book's strongest aspects
- Female relationships are complex, multifaceted, richly drawn
- Relationships can be supportive AND competitive simultaneously
- Sources of strength/inspiration BUT also pain/struggle
- Navigate realistic, relatable complexity
- Can be empowering and limiting at same time

**Broader themes**
- Power and privilege — Evelyn's access/lack of access shapes relationships and experiences
- Glamour of celebrity balanced with serious emotional themes
- Escapist yet thought-provoking

User enjoys character-driven contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists and complex relationships.
