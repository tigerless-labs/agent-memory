## ZZ Plant Care Guide

**Watering Schedule:**
- Water when soil feels dry to touch
- Summer: every 1-2 weeks
- Winter: every 4-6 weeks
- Avoid overwatering; can lead to root rot
- Water sparingly; avoid getting water on leaves to prevent fungal disease

**Fertilization:**
- Fertilize once a month during growing season (spring and summer)
- Use balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20)
- Dilute to half recommended strength to avoid burning roots
- Skip fertilizing during winter months when plant is dormant

**Light Requirements:**
- Prefers well-draining soil
- Can thrive in a variety of potting mixes
- Avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)
- Ideal: low to medium indirect light

**Maintenance:**
- Prune to maintain shape and encourage new growth
- Remove dead or damaged leaves to prevent disease spread

**Safety Note:**
- Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
- Keep out of reach of curious animals and children

**Ideal For:** Busy people or plant care beginners; excellent air purifier; can tolerate extreme low light and infrequent watering.
