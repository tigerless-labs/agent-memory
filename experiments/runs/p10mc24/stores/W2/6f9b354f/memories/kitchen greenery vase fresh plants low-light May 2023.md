## Kitchen Plant Setup

**Current Setup (May 2023):**
- Added vase with fresh greenery to kitchen countertop
- Wants to explore more low-light plant options for kitchen

**Interests:**
- Exploring low-light indoor plants suitable for kitchens with limited natural light
- Looking to expand from initial vase setup

**Recommended Low-Light Plants for Kitchen:**
- Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) — air purifier, low-maintenance, weeks without watering
- Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) — grows long, trainable, easy care
- ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) — extremely low-maintenance
- Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii) — elegant white blooms, air purifier
- Dracaena — 40+ varieties, shapes and sizes
- Philodendron — various shapes from compact to trailing
- Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema modestum) — variegated leaves, low-maintenance
- Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) — easy care, white flowers, air purifier
- Parlor Palm (Chamaedorea elegans) — slender, elegant for limited spaces
- Bromeliads (Bromeliaceae family) — low-maintenance, varied appearance

**Note:** Even low-light plants benefit from some indirect sunlight; north-facing windows or grow lights help.
