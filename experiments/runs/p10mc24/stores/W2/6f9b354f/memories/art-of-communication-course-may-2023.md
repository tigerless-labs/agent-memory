## Art of Communication Course (May 2023)

**Date completed:** May 23, 2023

**Topics learned:**
- Building self-confidence and self-esteem
- Overcoming glossophobia (fear of public speaking)
- Different confidence frameworks
- Understanding audience structure
- Articulation framework (speaking clearly and effectively)
- Persuasion techniques
- Delivering speeches with impact ("like a rockstar")
- Personal branding
- Maintaining an effective LinkedIn profile
- Confident communication strategies
- Art of listening / active listening
- Art of conversations
- Networking techniques

**Key takeaway:** Communication is a learned skill, not a natural talent. Effective communication is two-way (speaking + active listening), requires effort and practice, and involves both verbal and nonverbal elements (body language, tone of voice).

**Outcome:** Expanded understanding of effective communication and gained practical frameworks and techniques to enhance communication skills in personal and professional settings.
