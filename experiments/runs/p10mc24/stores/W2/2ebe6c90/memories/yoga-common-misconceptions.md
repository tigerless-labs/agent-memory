## Common Yoga Misconceptions

**Myth 1 - Only for Flexible People**
Yoga is designed to *build* flexibility, strength, and balance over time. You don't start flexible.

**Myth 2 - Just Stretching**
Yoga includes stretching, but also mindful movements, meditation, breathwork, and relaxation for total well-being.

**Myth 3 - It's Religious**
Despite Hindu roots, yoga is not inherently religious. Anyone of any faith can practice it.

**Myth 4 - Only for Women**
Yoga benefits everyone regardless of gender. Many athletes and professionals use it for flexibility, strength, and mental focus.

**Myth 5 - Too Slow and Boring**
Some styles are gentle and relaxing, but many are challenging, dynamic, and energizing.

**Myth 6 - Too Expensive**
While studio classes can be pricey, affordable or free options exist online and in communities.

**Myth 7 - Requires Equipment**
You need only your body and comfortable clothing. Props like mats are helpful but not required.
