User emphasizes that healthcare inequalities are a reflection of broader social and economic issues that require systemic change. Key points:

- Healthcare disparities are caused by underlying factors: poverty, discrimination, social exclusion, lack of education
- Governments must address these root causes, not just healthcare systems
- Prioritizing funding for marginalized communities is crucial
- Reducing poverty increases access to healthy food, housing, education
- Investing in education empowers individuals to manage their health
- Both moral imperative (fairness) and economic argument (productivity, cost reduction)

User is frustrated that change only happens during crises like COVID-19; advocates for proactive measures rather than waiting for tragedies.
