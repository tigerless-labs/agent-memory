Specific policy recommendations for reducing healthcare inequalities:

**Healthcare System Approaches:**
- Universal healthcare systems (Canada, UK, Spain are examples)
- Subsidizing healthcare services for marginalized communities
- Prioritizing vulnerable communities for health services
- Investing in healthcare infrastructure in marginalized/rural areas
- Equity lens in healthcare delivery

**Addressing Root Causes:**
- Addressing poverty, racism, discrimination, sexism
- Education programs on disease prevention and management
- Food security and nutrition assistance
- Social policy on health equity and intergenerational poverty

**Innovation:**
- Telemedicine and virtual consultations
- Mobile health clinics in remote areas
- Technology to improve healthcare access

**Key Insight:**
- Requires sustained collaborative effort from governments, healthcare institutions, community organizations, and individuals
- Proactive measures needed to prevent crises, not reactive crisis response
