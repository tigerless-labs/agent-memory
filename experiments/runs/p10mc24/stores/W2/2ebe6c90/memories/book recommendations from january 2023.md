## Historical Fiction with Strong Female Leads

1. **The Alice Network** by Kate Quinn
   - Female spies during WWI and WWII
   - Audiobook narrated by Saskia Maarleveld
2. **The Red Tent** by Anita Diamant - imagines life of Dinah from ancient Mesopotamia
3. **The Girl with the Pearl Earring** by Tracy Chevalier - 17th-century Holland, inspired by Vermeer
4. **The Other Boleyn Girl** by Philippa Gregory - Mary and Anne Boleyn
5. **The Paris Wife** by Paula McLain - Hadley Richardson, first wife of Ernest Hemingway, 1920s Paris
6. **The Women in the Castle** by Jessica Shattuck - three women in Germany during/after WWII
   - Audiobook narrated by Cassandra Campbell
7. **The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society** by Mary Ann Shaffer - post-WWII Guernsey
   - Audiobook narrated by Juliet Mills et al.
8. **The Heretic's Daughter** by Kathleen Kent - witch accusations in 17th-century England
9. **The Lost Queen** by Signe Pike - 6th-century Scottish queen Languoreth
10. **The Lost Girls of Paris** by Pam Jenoff - resistance network photographs, 1946
    - Audiobook narrated by Elizabeth Jasicki

## Feminist/Dystopian Audiobooks (similar to "The Power" by Naomi Alderman)

1. **The Handmaid's Tale** by Margaret Atwood - narrated by Claire Danes
2. **The Testaments** by Margaret Atwood (sequel) - narrated by Ann Dowd, Bryce Dallas Howard, Mae Whitman
3. **The Girls** by Emma Cline - Manson-like cult in 1960s California - narrated by Cady McClain
4. **The Belles** by Dhonielle Clayton - fantasy with magic-wielding women - narrated by Rosie Jones
5. **The Poppy War** by R.F. Kuang - Chinese-inspired fantasy - narrated by Emily Woo Zeller
6. **The Fifth Season** by N.K. Jemisin (Broken Earth trilogy) - narrated by Robin Miles
7. **The Disasters** by M.K. England - space explorer trainees - narrated by Bahni Turpin
8. **The Outside** by Ada Hoffmann - first contact with aliens - narrated by January LaVoy

## Mythology/Folklore Retellings from Various Cultures

1. **The Song of Achilles** by Madeline Miller - Trojan War from Patroclus' perspective
2. **The Penelopiad** by Margaret Atwood - Odyssey from Penelope's perspective
3. **The Gilded Wolves** by Roshani Chokshi - Indian and French mythology, 19th-century Paris
4. **The House of Shattered Wings** by Aliette de Bodard - Vietnamese mythology, post-apocalyptic Paris
5. **The Bear and the Nightingale** by Katherine Arden - Russian folklore, medieval Russia
6. **The Silence of the Girls** by Pat Barker - Iliad from Briseis' perspective
7. **The Library of the Unwritten** by A.J. Hackwith - multicultural mythology, unwritten stories

## Audiobooks from Non-Western Cultures' Mythologies

1. **Anansi Boys** by Neil Gaiman - African folklore
2. **The Black Tides of Heaven** by J.Y. Yang - Chinese mythology
3. **The Tiger's Daughter** by K. Arsenault Rivera - Japanese and Mongolian mythology
4. **The Fisherman** by John Langan - West African folklore, modern New York
5. **The City of Brass** by S.A. Chakraborty - Middle Eastern mythology, 18th-century Cairo
6. **The Bone Shard War** by Andrea Stewart - Chinese and Southeast Asian mythology
7. **The Obsidian Tower** by Melissa Caruso - Mesoamerican cultures mythology
8. **The House of Whispers** by Laura Purcell - African and Caribbean folklore
9. **The Deep** by Rivers Solomon - African diasporic mythology
