## Recommended Eco-Lodges

1. **Lapa Rios Ecolodge** (Osa Peninsula) — pioneer in sustainable tourism, luxurious accommodations, guided tours, community engagement
2. **Monteverde Lodge** (Monteverde) — surrounded by cloud forest, comfortable rooms, guided tours, strong sustainability focus
3. **Arenal Observatory Lodge** (Arenal Volcano) — near base of volcano, stunning views, guided tours, conservation commitment
4. **Tortuga Lodge** (Tortuguero National Park) — accessible only by boat/plane, wildlife focus, guided tours, conservation-oriented
5. **El Remanso Lodge** (Osa Peninsula) — luxurious, guided tours, reforestation program

## Recommended Sustainable Tour Operators

1. **Costa Rica Expeditions** — pioneer in eco-tourism, guided hiking, birdwatching, wildlife adventures
2. **Osa Conservation** — guided tours in Osa Peninsula, conservation programs, community engagement
3. **Tortuguero Canals Tours** — local, family-owned, canal exploration, sustainability and community focus
4. **Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve** — birdwatching, hiking, wildlife tours, local conservation support
5. **Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours** — Golfito Wildlife Refuge exploration, conservation support

## Certifications to Look For

- **CST** (Certification for Sustainable Tourism)
- **TIES** (International Ecotourism Society)
- **ICT** (Costa Rican Tourism Board)

Check for: environmental policies, community involvement, local conservation support, transparency, positive guest reviews
