## Wildlife in Tortuguero National Park

### Primates (Monkeys)
- **Howler monkeys** — loud, distinctive howls, common
- **White-faced capuchin monkeys** — agile, intelligent, often in groups
- **Spider monkeys** — long slender bodies, impressive canopy acrobatics

### Mammals
- **Two-toed sloths** — slow-moving, often hanging upside down
- **Three-toed sloths** — extra toe, similar behavior to two-toed

### Birds (300+ species total)
- **Toucans** — brightly colored beaks, vibrant plumage
- **Quetzals** — iridescent feathers, long tails
- **Macaws** — large, colorful parrots, flying overhead

### Reptiles
- **American crocodiles** — found in rivers and streams
- **Morelots crocodiles** — also in waterways
- **Green iguanas** — basking in sun or underbrush
- **Spiny-tailed iguanas** — smaller, quick-moving
- **Snakes:** Fer-de-lance (venomous), boa constrictor (harmless), various species

### Other Wildlife
- **Jaguars** — rare, requires luck and keen eyes
- **Caimans** — small alligator-like creatures, common in rivers
- **Butterflies** — 1200+ species, butterfly enthusiasts dream destination
- **Sea turtles** — park known for sea turtle nesting

## Wildlife Spotting Tips
- Join guided tours with knowledgeable guides
- Best times: early morning and late afternoon (animal activity peaks)
- Practice patience and careful observation
- Keep safe distance, do not feed, do not disturb natural behavior
- Bring binoculars and camera
- Guides can help locate specific species
