## Road Conditions in Costa Rica

### Road Types
- **Paved roads** — 30 percent, good condition, urban/highways/tourist routes
- **Unpaved roads** — 70 percent, rough/dusty/potholes, rural/national parks/remote
- **Mountain roads** — steep, winding, narrow, limited visibility, scenic but hazardous
- **Rainy season effects** — May-November brings mud, flooding, potential road closures

### Transportation Options
- **4x4 vehicle rental** — best for exploring remote/rural areas; use reputable companies
- **Shuttle services** — arranged through hotels/tours, convenient
- **Public buses** — affordable but crowded/slow
- **Taxis** — widely available, no meters standard; agree on price beforehand
- **Private transfers** — available through hotels/tour operators
- **Domestic flights** — fastest for longer distances/remote areas

## Driving Tips and Safety
- **Pack 4x4 vehicle** — highly recommended for off-road exploration
- **Drive defensively** — rough roads, steep inclines, unpredictable weather
- **Bring road map and GPS** — detailed maps essential
- **Vehicle maintenance** — check tire pressure, oil, brakes; carry spare tire/toolkit
- **Emergency kit** — snacks, water, first aid, flashlight
- **Learn Spanish** — basic phrases helpful for emergencies
- **Daylight driving only** — avoid night driving due to poor visibility/lighting
- **Watch for obstacles** — pedestrians, cyclists, livestock share roads
- **Use headlights** — increase visibility in low-light conditions
- **Avoid tired driving** — take regular breaks, rest when fatigued

## Language
Basic Spanish phrases helpful for communication and emergencies.
