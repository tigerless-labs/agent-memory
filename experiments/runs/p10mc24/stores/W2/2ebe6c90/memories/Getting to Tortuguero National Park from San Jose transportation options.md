## Transportation to Tortuguero National Park

**Option 1: Domestic Flight + Boat** (fastest)
- Fly from Tobias Bolaños International Airport (SYQ) in San José to Tortuguero Airport (TTQ)
- Flight time: ~1 hour
- Boat transfer from airport to eco-lodge: ~1-2 hours

**Option 2: Shuttle Bus + Boat**
- Shuttle from San José to La Pavona: ~4-5 hours
- Boat transfer from La Pavona to park: ~1-2 hours
- Eco-lodge usually arranges boat transfer

**Option 3: Private Transfer + Private Boat**
- Private transfer from San José to La Pavona: ~4-5 hours
- Private boat transfer to park: ~1-2 hours

## Important Considerations

- **Book in advance** — especially during peak season (December-April)
- **Check weather** — heavy rain or strong winds affect boat transfers
- **Eco-lodge packages** — many lodges include San José transportation in their packages
- **Pack light** — due to boat transfers, minimize luggage
- **Essential items:** insect repellent, comfortable shoes with traction, refillable water bottles, snacks

## Accessibility

- No roads to Tortuguero — only accessible by boat or plane, which preserves its remote jungle character
