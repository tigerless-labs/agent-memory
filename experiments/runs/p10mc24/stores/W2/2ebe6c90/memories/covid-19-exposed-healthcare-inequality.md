COVID-19 Pandemic Exposed Healthcare Inequalities:

**Access Disparities:**
- Low-income communities, marginalized populations, rural areas have limited healthcare access
- Higher rates of COVID-19 complications, hospitalizations, deaths among these groups

**Resource Disparities:**
- Wealthy nations could access and distribute medical supplies and equipment
- Developing nations struggled to provide healthcare workers with safety gear

**Race and Ethnicity:**
- Ethnic minorities disproportionately affected
- Contributing factors: higher poverty, inadequate healthcare access, systemic racism

**Communication and Education:**
- Limited access to accurate virus information
- Language barriers prevented diverse populations from learning protective measures

**Critical Observation:**
Governments often only prioritize healthcare equity during crises like COVID-19, rather than taking proactive measures. User advocates for sustained, non-crisis-driven action to address healthcare disparities.
