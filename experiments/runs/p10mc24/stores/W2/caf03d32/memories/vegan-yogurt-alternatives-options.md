## Vegan Yogurt Base Alternatives

User explored vegan yogurt bases for slow cooker yogurt making on May 30, 2023.

**Options researched:**

1. **Soy Yogurt:** Classic vegan option. Brands: Silk, So Delicious
2. **Coconut Yogurt:** Creamy and rich base. Brands: So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo
3. **Almond Yogurt:** Low-calorie, low-fat option. Brands: Almond Breeze, Silk, So Delicious
4. **Cashew Yogurt:** Creamy and indulgent (user's primary interest). Can blend cashews manually with water or use branded versions from So Delicious. User researched detailed cashew soaking/blending process.
5. **Oat Yogurt:** Good for nut allergies/intolerances. Brands: Oatly, So Delicious
6. **Rice Yogurt:** Hypoallergenic, easy-to-digest. Brands: Rice Dream, So Delicious
7. **Hemp Yogurt:** High protein, omega-3 rich. Brands: Good Karma, Elmhurst 1925
8. **Flax Yogurt:** Low-calorie, high-fiber. Brands: Good Karma; can also make at home

**Important selection criteria:**
- Choose unflavored and unsweetened varieties to avoid extra sugars and flavorings
- Can add own flavorings and sweeteners later to taste
- Check ingredient list and nutrition label to meet dietary needs and preferences
