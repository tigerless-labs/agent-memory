## Cashew-Based Vegan Yogurt Process

User specifically interested in making slow cooker yogurt with cashew base (asked twice on May 30, 2023).

**Soaking Cashews:**

1. Rinse 1 cup raw cashews under cold running water
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight
3. After soaking, drain and rinse cashews again
4. Soak in fresh water for another 4 hours or overnight
5. Drain and rinse one final time before blending

**Blending:**

1. Add drained/rinsed soaked cashews to blender with 1/2 cup fresh water
2. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
3. Stop blender and scrape down sides as needed to ensure even mixing

**Straining Options (user inquired about all three):**

1. **Cheesecloth:** High filtration level, catches smallest particles. Messy to work with, requires squeezing to extract all liquid.
2. **Nut Milk Bag:** Specifically designed for straining plant-based milks, fine-mesh material. Can be pricey, squeeze gently to avoid pushing particles through mesh.
3. **Fine-Mesh Sieve:** Easy to use and clean, catches most particles. May not catch all particles, resulting in slightly grainy texture.

**Tips for consistency:**
- Adjust water ratio: less water = thicker yogurt, more water = thinner yogurt. Base ratio is 1:1 (1 cup cashews to 1 cup water equivalent)
- Blending time affects consistency: shorter blend time = thicker result, longer = thinner
- Straining is crucial for smooth consistency
- Chill mixture in refrigerator for at least 2 hours to allow thickening and setting

**For slow cooker yogurt:**
- Use low heat setting to prevent scorching or burning
- Incubate for 8-12 hours or overnight
- Use vegan yogurt starter or probiotic powder designed for plant-based yogurts
