## Yogurt Customization Ideas

Explored on May 30, 2023 for slow cooker yogurt customization.

**Flavorings:**

1. Vanilla extract or vanilla bean
2. Cinnamon powder
3. Fresh fruit: berries, mango, peaches
4. Nutmeg powder
5. Fresh grated ginger
6. Fresh lemon juice or zest
7. Cocoa powder or melted chocolate

**Sweeteners:**

1. Honey
2. Maple syrup
3. Coconut sugar
4. Date syrup
5. Monk fruit sweetener (very potent, 150-200x sweeter than sugar)
6. Stevia powder

**Add-ins for texture:**

1. Granola (store-bought or homemade)
2. Nuts or seeds: almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds
3. Cocoa nibs
4. Dried fruit: cranberries, raisins, apricots

**User approach:** Experiment with different combinations to create perfect blend
