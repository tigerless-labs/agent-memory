## Guava Oatmeal with Hemp Seeds

**Base recipe:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1-2 guavas depending on size)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
- Pinch of salt

**Hemp seed addition:**
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds per serving
- Grind them up or mix with liquid first to distribute evenly
- Provides ~5-10g protein and 1-2g omega-3s (ALA)

**Storage:** Keep hemp seeds in airtight container in fridge or freezer to prevent rancidity.

**Optional toppings:** sliced banana, chopped nuts, shredded coconut, cinnamon, protein powder

**Ratio tip:** 1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal gives good flavor/texture balance.
