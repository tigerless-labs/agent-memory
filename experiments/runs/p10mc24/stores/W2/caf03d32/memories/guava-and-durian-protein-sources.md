## High-Protein Tropical Fruits

### Guava
- **Protein content:** 1 cup guava fruit = 4.2g protein
- **Ease of use:** Fresh guava preferred for best flavor/texture; frozen or canned work too
- **Ideas:**
  - Add to morning oatmeal (1/4 cup diced per serving)
  - Guava smoothie (1/2 cup frozen guava + 1/2 banana + 1/2 cup Greek yogurt + honey + almond milk)
  - Guava salsa (mix diced guava, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice)
  - Guava salad (mixed greens, diced guava, feta, pecans, balsamic vinaigrette)

### Durian
- **Protein content:** 1 cup durian = 3.6g protein
- **Note:** Pungent smell; start small and adjust to taste; frozen durian reduces smell and blends easier
- **Ideas:**
  - Durian smoothie (1/2 cup frozen + 1/2 banana + coconut milk + honey + cinnamon)
  - Durian fruit salad (mix with pineapple, kiwi, coconut flakes, lime)
  - Durian crepes (fill with durian, coconut cream, cinnamon)

**Tip:** While not as protein-dense as animal products or legumes, these fruits provide fiber, vitamins, and antioxidants alongside protein.
