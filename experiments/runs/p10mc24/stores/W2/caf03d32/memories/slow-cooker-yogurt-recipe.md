## Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 gallon milk (whole, low-fat, or nonfat)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures (for starter)
- Optional: honey, maple syrup, sugar for sweetness; vanilla, cinnamon, fruit for flavor; probiotic powder

**Process:**

1. Heat milk in slow cooker on low for 2-3 hours until it reaches 180-185F (82-85C). This temperature kills unwanted bacteria and denatures proteins.

2. Cool milk to 110-120F (43-49C) - takes approximately 1-2 hours. Temperature must be checked to ensure consistency.

3. Add 1/4 cup plain yogurt starter, whisk gently to combine.

4. Cover slow cooker and wrap with towel or blanket to maintain consistent temperature. Incubate for 8-12 hours or overnight. Longer incubation produces thicker, tangier yogurt.

5. Refrigerate to chill. Transfer to airtight containers for storage.

**Key points:**
- Using a thermometer ensures accurate temperature control
- Can substitute different milk types: almond, soy, coconut for non-dairy yogurt
- Can make Greek yogurt by straining through cheesecloth to remove excess liquid
- Add probiotic powder for additional beneficial bacteria
