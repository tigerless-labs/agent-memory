## Spice Rack Options

**Magnetic Spice Strips:**
- Adhesive magnetic strips for wall, cabinet, or fridge
- Example: IKEA SPICE STRIP, Amazon Magnetic Spice Strip

**Spinning Spice Rack:**
- Carousel-style rack for countertop or wall mount
- Example: Cuisinart Spice Rack, OXO Good Grips Spice Rack

**Tiered Spice Rack:**
- Vertical rack for countertop or cabinet interior
- Example: Rubbermaid Tiered Spice Rack, YouCopia SpiceStack

## Cabinet Organizer Options

**Shelf Dividers:**
- Adjustable dividers for different container sizes
- Example: The Container Store Adjustable Shelf Dividers, Whitmor Shelf Dividers

**Stackable Baskets:**
- Stack to store dry goods and supplies
- Example: Seville Classics Stackable Baskets, ClosetMaid Shelf Baskets

**Pull-Out Cabinet Organizers:**
- Slide-out systems for easy back-of-cabinet access
- Example: Rev-A-Shelf Pull-Out Cabinet Organizer, Knape & Vogt Pull-Out Shelf

## Other Storage Solutions

- **Over-the-Door Storage:** hooks, baskets, shelves (Whitmor Over-the-Door Storage Rack, Command Over-the-Door Hooks)
- **Drawer Organizers:** inserts and dividers (OXO Good Grips Drawer Organizer)
- **Countertop Containers:** airtight canisters (OXO Good Grips Canisters, Anchor Hocking Glass Canisters)

## Tips Before Purchasing

- Measure cabinet spaces and countertops for fit
- Consider items to store and frequency of use
- Choose durable, easy-to-clean materials
- Read reviews and check brand reputation
