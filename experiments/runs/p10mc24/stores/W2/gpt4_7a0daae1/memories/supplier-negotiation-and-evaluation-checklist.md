## Supplier Negotiation Tactics

**Research & Positioning**
- Gather market intelligence on product/service and competitor pricing
- Understand market rates and supplier competitors
- Have multiple quotes to use as leverage

**Communication Strategy**
- Build genuine relationships with suppliers
- Be transparent about budget constraints (when appropriate)
- Ask specific negotiation questions: "Is this the best price you can offer?" or "Can you do any better?"
- Justify discount requests with concrete reasons (bulk order, long-term contract)

**Leverage Points**
- Demonstrate you've researched alternatives and have competing quotes
- Ask suppliers to match or beat competitors' offers
- Explore value-added services: free shipping, priority delivery, technical support
- Consider total cost of ownership (including support, maintenance, warranty)
- Know your walk-away price and be prepared to use it

**Alternative Models**
- Tiered pricing for bulk orders
- Cost-plus pricing (supplier's cost + markup)
- Value-based pricing (tied to performance metrics)

## Supplier Evaluation Checklist

**Must-Have Criteria**
- [ ] Quality standards and certifications met
- [ ] On-time delivery track record
- [ ] Competitive pricing (verified via market research)
- [ ] Responsive communication
- [ ] Financial stability
- [ ] Relevant industry certifications (ISO, CE, FDA as applicable)

**Red Flags — Do Not Proceed If:**
- Poor or slow communication
- Lack of transparency in pricing/quality/supply chain
- Unwillingness to provide references
- History of late deliveries or quality defects
- Inadequate insurance or liability coverage
- Unprofessional staff
- Unfair contract terms
- Unwilling to adapt to your needs

**Evaluation Method**: Create weighted scoring system to objectively rank suppliers; use this before onboarding
