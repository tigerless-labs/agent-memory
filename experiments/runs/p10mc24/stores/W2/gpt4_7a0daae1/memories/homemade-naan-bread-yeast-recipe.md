## Homemade Naan Bread Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup lukewarm water
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, recommended for tender crumb)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, and yeast in large bowl
2. Gradually add lukewarm water and mix until shaggy dough forms
3. Add olive oil and mix until dough comes together
4. Knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth
6. Let rise in warm place for 1-2 hours until doubled
7. Divide into 6-8 equal portions, shape into balls
8. Roll each ball into thin circle (6-7 inches diameter)
9. Preheat non-stick skillet over medium heat
10. Cook naan 1-2 minutes per side until puffed and golden brown
11. Brush with melted butter or ghee

**Tips & Variations:**
- Use stand mixer with dough hook for easier kneading
- Add garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds for extra flavor
- Use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crisper crust
- Try whole wheat flour or higher-protein flour for different texture
- First batches may not be perfect — practice and adjust to preference
