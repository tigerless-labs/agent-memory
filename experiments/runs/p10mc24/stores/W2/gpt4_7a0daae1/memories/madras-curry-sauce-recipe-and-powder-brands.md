## Madras Curry Powder Brands Recommended

**Popular Brands:**
- **Penzeys Spices** — Madras Curry Powder (blend of 12 spices including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom)
- **The Spice House** — Madras Curry Powder (blend of 15 spices, includes fenugreek and fennel)
- **McCormick** — Madras Curry Powder (more affordable option, good balance of spices)

## Madras Curry Sauce from Scratch Recipe

**Key Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon curry leaves (optional, for authentic flavor)
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon Madras curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional, for creaminess)
- Fresh cilantro (for garnish)

**Basic Steps:**
1. Sauté onions in oil until translucent (~5 minutes)
2. Add garlic, ginger, and all dry spices; cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant
3. Add tomatoes, broth, and coconut milk
4. Simmer 10-15 minutes until sauce thickens
5. Add tofu or protein and simmer additional 5-7 minutes
6. Garnish with cilantro, serve over rice or with naan

**Tips:**
- Create custom spice blends
- Add potatoes, carrots, or other vegetables
- Substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream
- Add lemon juice or vinegar for brightness
