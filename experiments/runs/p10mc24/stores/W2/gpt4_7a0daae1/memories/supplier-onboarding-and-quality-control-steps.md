## Supplier Onboarding Checklist

**Pre-Onboarding**
- [ ] Create comprehensive onboarding checklist
- [ ] Finalize contract/agreement with signatures

**Information Gathering**
- [ ] Company profile and contact details
- [ ] Product/service specifications
- [ ] Certifications and compliance documentation
- [ ] Insurance and liability information
- [ ] Store all in centralized database/supplier management system

**Communication Setup**
- [ ] Designate primary point of contact (both sides)
- [ ] Establish channels: email, phone, project management tool
- [ ] Schedule regular check-ins

**Kickoff Meeting**
- [ ] Introduce key team members
- [ ] Review contract and expectations
- [ ] Confirm communication protocols
- [ ] Address questions/concerns

**Operational Setup**
- [ ] Provide business process training
- [ ] Clarify quality standards and specifications
- [ ] Conduct initial testing/quality assurance
- [ ] Set up payment terms and invoicing
- [ ] Establish performance KPIs

## Quality Control Process Implementation

**Phase 1: Standards Definition**
- Define clear quality standards and specifications
- Document requirements
- Communicate to supplier in writing

**Phase 2: Planning**
- Identify critical control points in supplier process
- Identify risks and potential improvements
- Develop QC plan with:
  - Monitoring procedures
  - Frequency of inspections/testing
  - Testing methods
  - Checks at each control point

**Phase 3: Implementation**
- Conduct regular inspections and testing
- Use statistical process control (Six Sigma methods)
- Verify supplier follows QC plan

**Phase 4: Issue Management**
- Develop corrective action process
- Require root cause analysis for defects
- Assign responsibility to supplier for fixes

**Phase 5: Monitoring**
- Track KPIs: defect rates, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction
- Monthly performance reviews
- Provide constructive feedback

**Checks & Balances to Maintain**
- Regular audits and inspections
- Product/service testing and validation
- Certification verification
- Third-party audits (periodic)
- Customer complaint monitoring

**Recommended Methodologies**
- ISO 9001:2015 — quality management systems
- Six Sigma — statistical process control
- Lean Manufacturing — process optimization
