## Tofu Recipe Ideas

**Breakfast:**
- **Tofu Scramble** — crumbled tofu with turmeric, nutritional yeast, spices; serve with whole-grain toast or wrap
- **Tofu Omelette** — marinate in soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar; pan-fry with veggies in tortilla

**Lunch/Dinner:**
- **Tofu Stir-Fry Wrap** — diced tofu sautéed with bell peppers, broccoli, carrots; serve in wrap with peanut sauce
- **Tofu Quesadilla** — marinate tofu in lime juice, cumin, chili powder; sauté with onions/peppers; cook in tortilla with cheese until crispy
- **Tofu Curry** — cook diced tofu in curry sauce with coconut milk, curry powder, turmeric; serve over rice or naan

**Snacks:**
- **Tofu Bites** — marinate tofu cubes in soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar; bake until crispy; serve with hummus or guacamole
- **Tofu Spring Rolls** — wrap tofu, shredded carrots, cilantro in rice paper; serve with sweet chili sauce

**Other:**
- **Tofu Skewers** — alternate tofu cubes with cherry tomatoes, mushrooms, bell peppers; brush with olive oil and grill/bake
- **Tofu Tacos** — season tofu with taco seasoning; cook in skillet; serve in tacos with salsa, avocado, sour cream
