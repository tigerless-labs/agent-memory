## Pantry Essentials Recommended

**Grains & Carbs:**
- Brown rice, quinoa, whole wheat pasta, oats

**Canned Goods:**
- Black beans, chickpeas, diced tomatoes, tuna (water or olive oil)
- Coconut milk, canned tomatoes for soups and curries

**Nuts & Seeds:**
- Almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds
- Provide healthy fats, protein, fiber

**Spices & Herbs:**
- Turmeric, cumin, basil, oregano, cayenne pepper
- Provide flavor and antioxidants

**Oils & Vinegars:**
- Olive oil, avocado oil, balsamic vinegar, apple cider vinegar
- For cooking and salad dressings

## Fridge Essentials Recommended

**Protein Sources:**
- Eggs (boiled, scrambled, omelettes)
- Greek yogurt (high protein, calcium; smoothies or snacks)
- Chicken breast, salmon fillets, tofu (plant-based)

**Produce:**
- Leafy greens (spinach, kale, collard greens)
- Fresh fruit (apples, bananas, berries for snacking, oatmeal, yogurt)

## Other Staples

**Beverages:**
- Water, unsweetened tea, coffee for healthy hydration

**Snacks:**
- Nuts, dried fruits, homemade energy balls (oats and nuts)

## Tips

- Check pantry/fridge before shopping to avoid duplicates and waste
- Focus on healthy, whole-food ingredients
