## Hendrick's Gin Preference

**Purchase and preference:**
- Recently purchased Hendrick's gin (August 2023)
- Really enjoying the unique botanical blend
- Preferred choice for cocktail experimentation

**Usage in cocktails:**
- Negroni: Pairs beautifully with Carpano Antica vermouth
- Lavender cocktails: Floral notes complement lavender syrup well
- Garden herb cocktails: Rose and cucumber notes work with fresh herbs (basil, mint, thyme, elderflower)
- White Negroni: Hendrick's floral/herbal notes pair well with dry bitter liqueurs like Suze

**Botanical profile:**
- Unique blend of botanicals
- Rose and cucumber notes
- Complements both fresh herbs and floral ingredients
- Works well with citrus pairings (orange, bergamot, yuzu)

**Experimentation focus:**
Using Hendrick's as the base for exploring:
- Different bitter liqueurs (Cynar, Aperol, Luxardo, Suze, Meletti)
- Lavender syrup and other floral ingredients
- Diverse citrus pairings
- Fresh garden herbs
