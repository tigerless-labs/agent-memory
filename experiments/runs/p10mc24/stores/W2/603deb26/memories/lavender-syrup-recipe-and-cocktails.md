## Lavender Syrup Recipe

**Simple Lavender Syrup:**
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds

**Instructions:**
1. Combine sugar and water in saucepan, heat until sugar dissolves
2. Add dried lavender buds and remove from heat
3. Steep for 10-15 minutes until cooled to room temperature
4. Strain through fine-mesh sieve into glass bottle
5. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks

## Lavender Cocktail Recipes

**Lavender Gin Fizz:**
- 2 oz Hendrick's gin
- 1 oz lavender syrup
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Top with soda water
- Garnish: lemon wheel + lavender sprig

**Lavender Dream:**
- 2 oz Hendrick's gin
- 1 oz lavender syrup
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 0.5 oz honey syrup (1:1 honey and water)
- Strain into coupe glass
- Garnish: lavender sprig

**Lavender Bee's Knees:**
- 2 oz Hendrick's gin
- 1 oz lavender syrup
- 1 oz honey syrup
- Strain into rocks glass with ice
- Garnish: lemon wheel + lavender sprig

**Tips:** Start with small amount of lavender syrup (can overpower); pair with citrus to balance floral notes; store at room temperature initially, then refrigerate.
