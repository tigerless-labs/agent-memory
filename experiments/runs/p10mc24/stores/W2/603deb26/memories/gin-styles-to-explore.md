# Gin Styles

**London Dry Gin**
- Bold, juniper-forward flavor
- Classic traditional style

**Plymouth Gin**
- Slightly sweeter, more approachable
- Good for beginners

**New Western or New World Gin**
- Modern style
- Experimental flavor profiles
- Creative botanicals
