## Dinner Party Overview
- **Date**: August 2023 (exact date not specified)
- **Guest count**: 8-10 people
- **Dietary accommodations**: vegetarian guests + 1 gluten-free guest
- **User background**: Has taken beginner's cooking, Indian, sushi-making, and pastry-making classes; family loved a pasta dish made after beginner's class
- **Theme**: Lemon-based dishes, multicuisine

## Menu Plan

### Appetizer
- Lemon-herb naan (infused with lemon zest, olive oil, garlic, parsley or cilantro)
- Served with spiced chickpeas or hummus

### Main Course
- **Vegetarian sushi rolls** with three filling options:
  1. Avocado and cucumber (classic)
  2. Grilled portobello mushrooms (marinated in soy sauce, sake, sugar; grilled until tender)
  3. Carrot-ginger tempura bits (shredded carrots mixed with ginger, light tempura batter, fried)
- Lemon-ginger dipping sauce (lemon juice, grated ginger, soy sauce, hint of sugar)
- Lemon-herb quinoa as side dish (light, refreshing; topped with toasted pine nuts or fresh herbs; squeeze fresh lemon juice before serving)

### Dessert
- Lemon curd or lemon bars (with gluten-free option available)
- Fresh fruit platter with whipped cream or coconut cream (gluten-free)

### Special consideration
- Gluten-free croissants or pastry alternative (fruit tart or lemon bars) for gluten-free guest

## Presentation & Garnishes
- Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint) as garnish for naan and quinoa
- Thinly sliced lemons and edible flowers (violas or pansies) as sushi garnish
- Table decoration: light, neutral tablecloth; fresh flowers or herb plant centerpiece; handwritten menu or place cards

## Timing Notes
- Prep ahead as much as possible (chop vegetables, marinate ingredients, make sauces)
- Cook in batches to keep courses hot and fresh
- Serve appetizer first, main course 30-45 minutes later, dessert 30-60 minutes after main
- Keep timeline flexible and communicate with guests if running behind
