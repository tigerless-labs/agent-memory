## Negroni Experimentation

**Progress:**
- Hendrick's gin + Carpano Antica vermouth = excellent combination
- Made 5 times at home since Emma taught the technique
- Getting progressively closer to perfecting the recipe

**Bitter liqueur alternatives to try:**
1. Cynar (artichoke-based, vegetal, slightly sweet) - try 0.5 oz + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica
2. Aperol (lighter, more orange-forward) - 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica
3. Luxardo Bitter (intense, herbal) - 0.5 oz + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica
4. Suze (French gentian, crisp, earthy) - 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica
5. Meletti (citrus-forward, slightly sweet) - 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica

**Note:** May need to adjust bitter-to-sweet vermouth ratio depending on liqueur intensity.
