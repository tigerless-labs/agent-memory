Seeking a lighter fly rod for smaller trout fishing.

Recommended models with approximate prices:
- Orvis Clearwater: 198-298 (entry-level, lightweight, sensitive)
- Sage Igniter: 400-500 (high-performance, light, fast action)
- Winston Pure: 300-400 (premium, smooth casting)
- Redington Crux: 150-250 (budget-friendly, beginner option)
- TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series: 200-300 (high-quality, lightweight)

Specifications recommended for trout:
- Line weight: 2-5 weight
- Action: Fast or medium-fast
- Length: 9 feet (standard) or 7-8 feet (tight spaces, smaller streams)
- Material: Graphite (lighter, more sensitive) or fiberglass (more durable)

Advice: Try rods at local fly shop before purchasing. Pair with matching reel and line.
