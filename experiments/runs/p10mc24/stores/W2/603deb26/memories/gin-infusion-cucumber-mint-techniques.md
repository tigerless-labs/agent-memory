# Cucumber-Infused Gin

- Use thin-skinned sweet cucumber (English or hothouse)
- Slice thinly to increase surface area
- Combine 1-2 cups sliced cucumber with 1 liter gin
- Infuse 2-5 days in cool, dark place
- Taste regularly to avoid over-infusion
- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter

# Mint-Infused Gin

- Use fresh peppermint or spearmint leaves
- Add 1/4 to 1 cup fresh mint leaves per 1 liter gin
- Infuse 2-5 days in cool, dark place
- Monitor flavor to avoid over-infusion
- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter

# Combining Method

- Infuse cucumber and mint separately (allows control of each flavor intensity)
- Combine infusions in 50/50 ratio initially
- Adjust proportions to taste
- Age the combined blend for a few days to allow flavors to meld
