# Lavender Cocktails

**Lavender Gin Fizz**
- 1.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.5 oz lavender syrup
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Top with soda water
- Garnish: lavender sprig, lime wheel

**Lavender Dream**
- 2 oz vodka
- 1 oz lavender syrup
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 0.5 oz honey syrup

**Lavender Syrup**: Steep 1/4 cup dried lavender buds in 1 cup sugar and 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes, strain.

# Grapefruit Cocktails

**Grapefruit Basil Martini**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz lime juice
- 0.5 oz basil syrup
- Garnish: grapefruit twist, basil leaf

**Grapefruit Rosemary Sparkler**
- 2 oz tequila
- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz lime juice
- 0.5 oz rosemary syrup
- Top with soda water

# Combined

**Lavender Grapefruit Spritz**
- 2 oz prosecco
- 1 oz grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz lavender syrup
- Splash of soda water

**Grapefruit Lavender Collins**
- 2 oz gin
- 1 oz grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz lavender syrup
- 0.5 oz lemon juice
- Top with soda water
