# Basic Honey Syrup

**Ratio**: 1:1 honey to water (can adjust to taste)

**Preparation**:
1. Combine honey and water in saucepan
2. Heat over low heat, whisking until dissolved
3. Do not boil or burn the honey
4. Cool before using in cocktails
5. Store in fridge up to 2 weeks

# Honey Types

- Clover honey
- Manuka honey
- Acacia honey
Each creates different flavor profiles

# Variations and Infusions

- Add citrus peel, herbs, or spices during dissolving
- Honey-ginger syrup: add sliced ginger during dissolving for spicy-sweet flavor
- Experiment with different honey-to-water ratios for preferred sweetness
