# To Explore Beyond Gin

**Old Fashioned**
Whiskey cocktail with bourbon or rye, sugar, bitters, citrus twist

**Daiquiri**
Rum-based with white rum, lime juice, simple syrup

**Sidecar**
Cognac, Cointreau, lemon juice in sugar-rimmed glass
