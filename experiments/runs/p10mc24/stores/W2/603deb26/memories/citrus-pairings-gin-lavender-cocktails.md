## Citrus Pairings with Gin and Lavender

**Current experimentation status:**
Previously experimented with grapefruit and lemon; seeking to expand citrus variety.

## Recommended Citrus Options

**Orange**
- Classic pairing with lavender
- Sweetness complements floral notes
- Bitterness balances gin
- Tip: Try blood oranges for deeper, complex flavor

**Yuzu**
- Japanese citrus (grapefruit-orange cross)
- Tart and subtly sweet
- Pairs beautifully with gin and lavender
- Adds exotic, unique flavor profile

**Kumquat**
- Small, sweet citrus fruits
- Well-suited to pairing with lavender
- Sweetness complements floral notes
- Use: kumquat syrup or muddle fresh fruits

**Satsuma**
- Type of mandarin orange
- Sweet and tart
- Easy-to-peel skin
- Sweetness helps balance gin bitterness

**Bergamot**
- Often used in tea and perfumery
- Subtle citrus flavor
- Adds complexity to gin cocktails
- Lovely pairing with lavender

## General Citrus Tips

- Use fresh citrus for brightest, most vibrant flavor
- Each variety has unique profile - experiment freely
- Citrus can overpower other flavors - start small and adjust
- Citrus peels add aroma and flavor - use twists or expressed peels for added depth
