# Spirit Infusion Tips

**Choose Neutral Spirit**
- Vodka, gin, or rum works best
- Allows infused flavor to shine through

**Flavor Ingredient Ratios**
- General starting point: 1 part ingredient to 4 parts spirit
- Experiment to find preferred balance

**Infusion Time**
- Depends on desired flavor strength
- Start shorter, taste regularly to avoid over-infusion
- Examples: lavender 2-3 days, grapefruit 2-3 days, mint 2-5 days

**Storage**
- Keep in cool, dark place
- Light and heat affect infusion process
- Strain and filter before using

**Flavor Combinations to Try**
- Citrus peels (lemon, orange, grapefruit)
- Herbs: basil, rosemary, thyme
- Spices: cinnamon, nutmeg, cardamom
- Fruits: strawberries, raspberries, cranberries
- Tea: earl grey, green tea, chai

**Safety**
- Follow proper sanitation procedures
- Store carefully to avoid contamination and spoilage
