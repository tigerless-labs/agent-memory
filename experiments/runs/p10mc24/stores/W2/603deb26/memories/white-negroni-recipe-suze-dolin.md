## White Negroni Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin (or preferred gin)
- 0.5 oz Suze (or other dry bitter liqueur like Luxardo Bitter)
- 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth
- Orange twist or lemon twist for garnish

**Preparation:**
1. Fill mixing glass with ice
2. Add gin, Suze, and Dolin Dry Vermouth
3. Stir for 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute
4. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass
5. Garnish with orange or lemon twist

## Flavor Balancing Tips

**Dry bitter liqueur:** Use Suze, Luxardo Bitter, or other dry options (avoids sweetness)

**Dry vermouth:** Dolin Dry Vermouth recommended to keep the drink crisp

**Ratio adjustments:** 
- Some prefer 2:1:1 ratio (gin:bitter:vermouth)
- Others prefer 3:1:1 ratio
- Experiment to find personal preference

**Gin consideration:** Hendrick's floral/herbal notes pair well with Suze's dry, herbaceous profile. Adjust bitter liqueur/ratio if using different gin.

**Dilution:** Stir just long enough to chill and dilute; avoid over-diluting.

**Best for:** Warm weather or when seeking crisp, refreshing cocktail.
