## Wilder Ranch State Park Ride

**Date:** Last weekend before May 21, 2023 (approximately May 14-15)

**Companion:** Neighbor Mike

**Trail details:**
- Location: Wilder Ranch State Park
- Distance: 15 miles
- Type: Single-track trails
- Assessment: Fun, but tough on bike

**Maintenance planned before next ride:**
1. Oil the chain
   - Clean chain first to remove dirt/grime
   - Use appropriate chain lube for conditions (wet vs dry)
   - Apply per manufacturer specs, wipe excess
2. Adjust derailleur
   - Check limit screws for proper adjustment
   - Check alignment with cassette/chainrings
   - Consider professional tune-up if uncomfortable doing it

**Other pre-ride checks recommended:**
- Tire pressure (recommended level)
- Brake pads (check for wear)
- Secure attachments and bolts
- Quick releases tightened properly

**Essentials to carry:**

- Water and snacks
- Notify someone of route and expected return time
- Consider carrying phone for emergencies
