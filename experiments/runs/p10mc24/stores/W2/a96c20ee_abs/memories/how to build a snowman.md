## Building a Snowman

### Materials needed
- Large snowball (base of body)
- Medium snowball (middle of body)  
- Small snowball (head)
- Sticks for arms
- Carrot or other material for nose
- Coal or other material for eyes, mouth, and buttons
- Hat and scarf (optional)

### Steps
1. Find a good spot with packed snow and enough space
2. Roll the large snowball on the ground until big enough for the base
3. Stack the medium snowball on top of the large one
4. Roll and stack the small snowball on top for the head
5. Insert sticks directly or use snow as "glue" to attach arms
6. Add carrot for the nose
7. Use coal to create eyes, mouth, and buttons
8. Add hat and scarf if available

The key is rolling the snowballs to get them big enough, then stacking from largest (bottom) to smallest (top). Accessories can be attached with or without snow as an adhesive.
