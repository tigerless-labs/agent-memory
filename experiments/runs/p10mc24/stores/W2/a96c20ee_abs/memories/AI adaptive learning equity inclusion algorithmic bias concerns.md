## Equity and Inclusion Potential

AI-powered adaptive learning systems have potential to help reduce achievement gaps and provide more personalized support to students who may be struggling or disadvantaged.

## Algorithmic Bias Concerns

Identified significant concerns around potential for bias in algorithms used to drive these systems. If not carefully addressed, algorithmic bias could exacerbate existing inequities rather than reduce them.

This is a critical area of ongoing research and development in EdTech that requires careful attention during system design and implementation.
