## Sleeping Pad Decision

**Choice:** Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite

**Specs:**
- Weight: 12.5 oz (354g)
- Thickness: 2.8 inches
- Insulation: Good R-value for cold weather

**Reasoning:** User prefers comfort over ultralight weight; willing to sacrifice ounces for better sleeping experience.

**Context:** User forgot sleeping pad on previous camping trip and had to borrow uncomfortable one from friend Alex. This new trip is a lesson learned — bringing own quality pad.

**Alternatives considered:**
- Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite: 11.8 oz, 2.5 inches — lighter but less comfortable
- Sea to Summit Ether Light XT: 13.9 oz, 3.5 inches — more comfort, slightly heavier
- Big Agnes Insulated AXL: 9.8 oz, 2.5 inches — ultralight
- NEMO Equipment Astro: 10.5 oz, 2.5 inches — ultralight
