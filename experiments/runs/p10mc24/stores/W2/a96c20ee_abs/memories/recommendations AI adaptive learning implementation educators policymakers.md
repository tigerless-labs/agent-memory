## Recommendations for Educators and Policymakers

1. **Prioritize Teacher Professional Development**: Educators and policymakers should prioritize teacher professional development and provide ongoing support for teachers as they implement AI-powered adaptive learning systems.

2. **Developer-Educator Collaboration**: Developers should work closely with educators to ensure their systems are designed with the needs of teachers and students in mind.

3. **Curriculum Alignment Guidance**: Developers should provide clear guidance on how their systems align with curriculum and learning goals.

4. **Ongoing Evaluation and Assessment**: Emphasized the importance of ongoing evaluation and assessment of AI-powered adaptive learning systems to ensure their effectiveness.

## Conference Reception

Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers at summer 2023 conference presentation. Many appreciated the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities. Some developers reached out afterwards, interested in learning more about findings and how to apply them to their own work.
