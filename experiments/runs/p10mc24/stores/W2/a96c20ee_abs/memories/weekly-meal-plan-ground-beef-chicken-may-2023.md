## Weekly Meal Plan — Ground Beef & Chicken

### Monday
- **Breakfast:** Breakfast Burritos (scrambled eggs, ground beef, cheese)
- **Lunch:** Chicken Caesar Salad Wraps (grilled chicken, romaine, parmesan)
- **Dinner:** Spaghetti Bolognese (ground beef, tomato sauce, vegetables)

### Tuesday
- **Breakfast:** Chicken and Avocado Toast (scrambled eggs, cherry tomatoes)
- **Lunch:** Tacos (seasoned ground beef, lettuce, cheese, salsa)
- **Dinner:** Baked Chicken Breast with roasted vegetables and quinoa

### Wednesday
- **Breakfast:** Breakfast Tacos (scrambled eggs, ground beef, salsa)
- **Lunch:** Chicken and Veggie Stir-Fry with brown rice
- **Dinner:** Meatball Subs (ground beef meatballs, marinara, mozzarella)

### Thursday
- **Breakfast:** Chicken and Cheese Omelette with hash browns
- **Lunch:** Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry with brown rice
- **Dinner:** Chicken Fajitas (grilled chicken, bell peppers, onions)

### Friday
- **Breakfast:** Breakfast Quesadillas (scrambled eggs, ground beef, cheese)
- **Lunch:** Chicken Caesar Salad (grilled chicken, romaine, parmesan)
- **Dinner:** Beef and Mushroom Gravy over egg noodles

### Saturday
- **Breakfast:** Chicken and Waffles with maple syrup
- **Lunch:** Beef Tacos (seasoned ground beef, lettuce, cheese, salsa)
- **Dinner:** Baked Chicken Breast with roasted potatoes and green beans

### Sunday
- **Breakfast:** Breakfast Burritos (scrambled eggs, ground beef, cheese)
- **Lunch:** Chicken and Avocado Wrap (mixed greens, whole wheat tortilla)
- **Dinner:** Spaghetti with Meat Sauce (ground beef) and garlic bread

## Usage Notes

- Plan ahead and prep on weekends to save time during the week
- Pack snacks in bags or cars for easy access
- Keep recipes simple and require minimal ingredients
- Experiment with different seasonings and flavors
- Make healthy snacking a habit by incorporating it into daily routine
