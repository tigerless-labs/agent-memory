## Mission Peak Hike (April 10, 2023)

**Date:** April 10, 2023

**Location:** Mission Peak, Fremont

**Type:** Solo day hike

**Assessment:** Really enjoyed it; planning to repeat

**Trail notes:** Beautiful with stunning views of the Bay Area

**Future plans:** Planning another solo day hike to Mission Peak

**Preparation tips for repeat:**
- Check weather forecast (can get hot in summer months)
- Bring 2-3 liters of water, sun protection (hat, sunglasses)
- Let someone know route, expected return time, contact info
- Pack daypack essentials: water, snacks, map/compass, first aid kit, sun protection, extra layers, headlamp, emergency shelter, multi-tool, fire starter, ID
- Hike during daylight hours, avoid peak sun (11am-3pm)
- Wear bright/reflective clothing
- Follow Leave No Trace principles
- Bring GPS device or app (fully charged)
