## Ground Beef & Chicken Recipes

### Ground Beef Burgers

1. **Classic Cheeseburgers** — Season with salt, pepper, spices; grill or pan-fry; serve with melted cheese, lettuce, tomato, condiments
2. **Sliders with Caramelized Onions** — Mix with minced onion; form small patties; serve on small buns with cheddar and ketchup
3. **Spicy Southwestern Burgers** — Add diced jalapeños, cumin, chili powder; top with pepper jack, avocado, fried egg
4. **Greek-Style Stuffed Burgers** — Mix with crumbled feta, kalamata olives, oregano; serve with tzatziki sauce

### Chicken Breast Sandwiches

1. **Classic Chicken Sandwiches** — Season and grill; serve on toasted bun with lettuce, tomato, mayo
2. **Buffalo Chicken Sandwiches** — Marinate in hot sauce and ranch; serve with shredded lettuce, celery, blue cheese
3. **Chicken Club Sandwiches** — Top with crispy bacon, lettuce, tomato, mayo on toasted bread
4. **Chicken Caesar Sandwiches** — Grill and serve with romaine, parmesan, Caesar dressing
