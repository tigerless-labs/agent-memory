## Storage & Food Safety for Cooked Ground Beef & Chicken

### Ground Beef Storage

**Refrigeration:**
- Use airtight, shallow containers with tight-fitting lids
- Store at 40°F (4°C) or below within 2 hours of cooking
- Use within 3–4 days
- Label and date containers

**Freezing:**
- Wrap tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil
- Place in freezer-safe bags or airtight containers
- Label and date the package
- Store for 3–4 months
- Thaw in refrigerator before reheating
- Reheat to internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)

**Portioning:**
- Divide cooked ground beef into smaller portions (1/4 cup or 1/2 cup)
- Store in individual airtight containers or freezer bags for easy grab-and-go access

### Chicken Breast Storage

**Refrigeration:**
- Store in covered container at 40°F (4°C) or below within 2 hours of cooking
- Use within 3–4 days
- Label and date containers

**Freezing:**
- Place in airtight container or freezer bag; remove excess air
- Label and date the package
- Store for 4–6 months
- Thaw in refrigerator before reheating
- Reheat to internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)

**Preparation for Freezing:**
- Shred or dice cooked chicken for easier portioning in future recipes
- Portion into meal-sized amounts in separate bags or containers

### General Best Practices

1. **Cooling:** Allow cooked meat to cool to room temperature before refrigerating/freezing to prevent bacterial growth
2. **Containers:** Use airtight, shallow containers to prevent moisture accumulation and bacterial growth
3. **Freezer Organization:** Store frozen items in a single layer to easily find what you need and prevent cross-contamination
4. **Labeling:** Always include date, contents, and reheating instructions
5. **Reheating:** Ensure food reaches minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C) for food safety
