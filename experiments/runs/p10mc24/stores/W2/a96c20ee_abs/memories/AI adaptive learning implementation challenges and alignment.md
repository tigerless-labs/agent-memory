## Implementation Challenges

**Teacher Professional Development**: One common challenge across all case studies was the need for ongoing teacher professional development and training to effectively integrate AI-powered adaptive learning systems. Many teachers felt they lacked the necessary skills and knowledge to use the systems effectively, which hindered successful implementation.

**Curriculum Alignment**: Another common theme was the importance of clear understanding of how AI-powered adaptive learning systems aligned with curriculum and learning goals. When alignment was clear, teachers were more likely to adopt and use the systems effectively.

## Concerns About Role of Teachers

Area of disagreement among stakeholders: Some educators were concerned that AI-powered adaptive learning systems would replace teachers, while others saw them as valuable tools to support teaching and learning.
