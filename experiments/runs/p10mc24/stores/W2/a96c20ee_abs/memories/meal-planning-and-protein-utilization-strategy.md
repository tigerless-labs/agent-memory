## Meal Planning & Protein Utilization Strategy

### Core Approach
The user is strategically planning meals around proteins purchased on sale (BOGO ground beef and chicken from Publix, May 2023) with emphasis on:
- **Value maximization** — Use all purchased meat efficiently
- **Food waste reduction** — Multiple recipes for various leftover uses
- **Sustainable habits** — Weekend meal prep enabling healthy daily choices
- **Flexibility** — Mix of breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack options

### Key Principles Applied
1. **Versatility** — Same proteins appear across breakfast (omelets, burritos), lunch (wraps, salads), dinner (stir-fries, pasta), and snacks (salads, wraps)
2. **Batch cooking** — Prepare proteins once, use in multiple recipes throughout the week
3. **Smart storage** — Understand refrigeration vs. freezing timelines to plan consumption
4. **Meal prep culture** — Weekend preparation of batches enables weekday convenience
5. **Nutritional balance** — Emphasis on healthy snacks with vegetables, whole grains, protein

### Timeline & Phases
- **Purchase:** ~May 20, 2023 (BOGO deal at Publix)
- **Initial cooking:** Weekend of May 20-21 (burgers and sandwiches)
- **Ongoing usage:** Week of May 22 (via meal plan)
- **Storage:** Freezing for 3-4 months (ground beef) and 4-6 months (chicken) for extended use
