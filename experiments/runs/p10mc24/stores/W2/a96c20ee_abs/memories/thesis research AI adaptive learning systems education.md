## Research Overview

**Conference Presentation**: Presented research poster at first research conference, summer 2023

**Research Focus**: Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes

**Specific Domains**: Math and reading comprehension

**Research Methodology**: Broader conceptual approach with specific platforms and systems explored as case studies to illustrate challenges and opportunities of implementation

## Key Finding

AI-powered adaptive learning systems can be effective in improving student learning outcomes, but implementation and integration into the classroom require careful consideration.
