## Incorporating Healthy Snacks into Daily Routine

### Breakfast Ideas
1. **Chicken and Avocado Wrap** — Prepare night before; grab-and-go; refrigerate overnight
2. **Chicken and Quinoa Bites** — Batch-make on weekend; store in airtight container up to 3 days
3. **Chicken Salad on Whole-Grain Toast** — Prep salad Sunday evening; store 3 days; serve with fried egg

### Post-Workout Snacks
1. **Chicken and Mango Skewers** — Batch-make on weekend; store in airtight container up to 3 days; refreshing option
2. **Chicken and Spinach Pinwheels** — Make batch on weekend; store up to 3 days; protein-packed
3. **Chicken Caesar Wrap** — Prepare immediately after workout; filling and satisfying

### Mid-Morning or Afternoon Snacks
1. **Chicken and Cucumber Roll-Ups** — Batch-make on weekend; store up to 3 days
2. **Chicken and Hummus Wrap** — Make batch on weekend; store up to 3 days; healthy and filling
3. **Chicken Salad on Crackers** — Prep salad Sunday; store 3 days; pair with carrot sticks or cherry tomatoes

### Meal Prep Strategy

**Planning & Preparation:**
- Prepare snacks on weekends or night before to save time during week
- Batch-cook and portion for easy weekly access
- Choose simple recipes requiring minimal ingredients
- Experiment with different seasonings and flavors to maintain variety

**Storage & Access:**
- Pack snacks in bags or car for easy access throughout day
- Airtight containers preserve freshness (up to 3 days refrigerated)
- Label containers with prep date
- Keep ingredients on hand for quick assembly

**Habit Building:**
- Make healthy snacking a consistent daily habit
- Rotate different snack types to prevent boredom
- Tie snacks to specific daily triggers (post-workout, mid-morning, etc.)
- Start weekends with meal prep to set up the week
