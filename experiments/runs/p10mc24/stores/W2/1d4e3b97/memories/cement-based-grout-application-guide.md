## Cement-Based Grout: Mixing and Application

**Mixing Steps:**
1. Follow manufacturer's mixing instructions (ratio varies by brand)
2. Use clean, potable water only
3. Mix in well-ventilated area
4. Target consistency: toothpaste-like or thick batter
5. Avoid over-mixing (weakens grout)
6. Mix in small batches to prevent waste

**Application Process:**
1. Prepare area: ensure tile is clean, dry, debris-free; remove spacers
2. Use grout float at 45-degree angle
3. Press firmly onto tile, spreading evenly
4. Fill joints completely but don't overfill
5. Work in small sections (about 4 square feet)
6. Clean grout float regularly during application
7. Allow 10-15 minute set time before cleaning
8. Clean with damp sponge or cloth, rinse thoroughly

**Post-Application:**
- Apply grout sealer once fully cured
- Protects from stains and water damage
- Use grout sponge for final cleanup

**Best Practices:**
- Work in well-lit area
- Clean as you go to prevent haze
- Avoid over-grouting (messy appearance)
