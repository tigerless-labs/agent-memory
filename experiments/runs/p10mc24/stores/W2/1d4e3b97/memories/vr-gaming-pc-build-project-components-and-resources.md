## Project: Build VR Gaming PC

**Started:** February 20, 2024

### High-End Component Recommendations

- **Graphics card:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, RTX 3070, RTX 3060 Ti; AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT or RX 6800
- **Processor:** AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, or i7-11700K
- **Memory:** 16 GB or more of DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency
- **Motherboard:** ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX
- **Storage:** Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 (SSDs for faster load times)
- **PSU:** EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X (at least 750W)

### Budget-Friendly Component Recommendations

- **Graphics card:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580
- **Processor:** AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K
- **Memory:** 8 GB of DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency
- **Motherboard:** MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, or Gigabyte GA-A320M-H
- **Storage:** 500 GB SSD — WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D
- **PSU:** EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

### Assembly Tips

1. Ensure all components are compatible before starting
2. Ground yourself to avoid electrostatic discharge (ESD)
3. Install PSU first for proper cable management
4. Install motherboard, CPU, memory, and M.2 SSDs carefully
5. Install graphics card firmly in PCIe slot
6. Keep cable management organized using cable ties
7. Connect peripherals (keyboard, mouse, VR headset)
8. Test build and check POST and BIOS
9. Install OS and drivers
10. Take time to avoid mistakes
