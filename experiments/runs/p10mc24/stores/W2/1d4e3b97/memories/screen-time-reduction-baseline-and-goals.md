## Current Screen Time Situation

**Baseline (as of February 2024):**
- Total daily screen time: 4-5 hours including work and leisure
- Primary devices: smartphone and laptop
- Top 3 most-used apps: social media, email, Netflix
- Work screen time: estimated 2-3 hours daily

**Weak Points:**
- Evening mindless scrolling: 7-8 pm (after dinner, watching TV) and before bed
- Most tempted during these times

## Sleep Schedule Goals

- **Weekdays:** Target 10:00 pm bedtime
- **Weekends:** Target 10:30 pm bedtime
- **Wake time:** Consistent 7:00 am

## Relaxation Needs

- Uses Netflix as primary unwinding mechanism after long workdays
- Values relaxation time and doesn't want to eliminate screen-based wind-down entirely
- Concerned about replacing Netflix with another screen (phone or laptop)

## Strategies Committed To

1. Screen-free dinner zone (7-8 pm)
2. Wind-down routine (30 min before bed, screen-free)
3. "Relaxation window" concept: 30-60 min daily choosing between Netflix OR screen-free activity
4. Designate screen-free zones (bedroom, dinner table, cozy reading nook)
5. Alternative activities: reading, journaling, yoga, meditation, stretching
6. Better task prioritization at work to avoid late-night project rushes
