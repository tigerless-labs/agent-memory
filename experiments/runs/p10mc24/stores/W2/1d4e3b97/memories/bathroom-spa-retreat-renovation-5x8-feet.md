## Bathroom Renovation Plan

**Scope:** Complete bathroom redo in Spa-Inspired Retreat style

**Size:** 5x8 feet

**Key Features:**
- Freestanding tub (keeping existing tub preference)
- Large walk-in shower with linear drain
- Double sink vanity
- Separate water closet area

**Material Choices:**

**Tile:**
- Glazed porcelain tile for both shower walls and floor
- Chosen for: glossy finish, durability, scratch/stain protection, ease of cleaning
- Considered but rejected: unglazed porcelain (better slip-resistance but less glossy aesthetic)

**Grout:**
- Cement-based grout
- Rationale: all-purpose option, relatively affordable, suitable for glazed porcelain
- Application notes: requires sealing, prone to staining but easy to apply

**Freestanding Tub Budget:** $1,000–$3,000 depending on size and brand

**User's DIY Involvement:** Open to tackling some renovation work themselves; has prior experience with home projects
