Route planning resources recommended for finding bike-friendly roads for long rides:

**Apps and Websites:**
- **Ride with GPS** — custom route creation, turn-by-turn directions (Web, iOS, Android)
- **MapMyRide** — route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, community routes (Web, iOS, Android)
- **Strava** — social network for cyclists, popular routes, create custom routes, compete with friends (Web, iOS, Android)
- **Google Maps** — use "Bicycling" mode to prefer bike lanes, paths, avoid highways (Web, iOS, Android)

**Tips:**
- Avoid busy roads; prefer dedicated bike lanes and paths
- Choose roads with good surface conditions
- Consult local bike shops for area-specific route recommendations
- Check municipal bike maps from local government websites
- Join online cycling forums and local clubs for route recommendations

**Safety Checklist:**
- Pre-ride: helmet check, bike condition (tires, brakes, gears), reflective gear
- On-road: follow traffic rules, be visible, use hand signals, ride defensively
- Watch for potholes, debris, avoid dooring hazard from parked cars
- Carry basic toolkit (pump, tire levers, Allen wrenches), phone, ID
- Stay hydrated: drink 20-24 oz water per hour of riding
- Consider sports drinks with electrolytes for rides over 1 hour
