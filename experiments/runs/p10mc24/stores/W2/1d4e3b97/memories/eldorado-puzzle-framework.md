## Lost City of Eldorado Campaign

Game campaign centered on players exploring a lost city and claiming treasure.

### Six Puzzle Types Framework

Designed to challenge players of different skill levels and maintain engagement:

1. **Logic Puzzles** — reasoning and deduction; e.g., figure out clues to unlock a door or find a hidden key
2. **Physical Puzzles** — manipulate physical objects in specific sequences; e.g., move blocks or activate levers to open a gate
3. **Pattern Recognition Puzzles** — identify patterns to solve; e.g., align symbols in specific order to open door or unlock chest
4. **Word Puzzles** — language-based challenges; e.g., unscramble letters to form a word or solve riddles
5. **Visual Puzzles** — observation skills required; e.g., spot hidden objects or identify patterns in murals/paintings
6. **Combination Puzzles** — hybrid challenges mixing multiple puzzle types; e.g., solve series of logic puzzles and physical puzzles to unlock a door

Each puzzle has unique solution; progression through puzzles leads deeper into lost city toward treasure.
