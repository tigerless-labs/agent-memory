161 is a natural wine bar located in Sydenham, South East London. 

**Dishes:**
- Mushroom & Oloroso pâté with pickled cabbage, pickled cucumber, and fried bread

**Wine service:**
- Benoit Landron 'Naturlich' pet-nat 2021, served by the glass in spring
  - Blend: 80% Semillon (from organic grower in Bergerac), 20% Folle Blanche
  - Frost damage in 2021 forced the change from previous vintages
  - Adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the wine
  - Described as "a spring wine" and "a fun variation on a theme"

**Operations:**
- Also imports and distributes natural wines
