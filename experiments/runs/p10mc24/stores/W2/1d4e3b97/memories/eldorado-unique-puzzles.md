## Unique Puzzle Ideas for Lost City of Eldorado Campaign

Five custom puzzle designs combining different puzzle types and mechanics:

### 1. Mirror Puzzle
- **Setting:** Large room with mirror surface etched with symbols
- **Mechanic:** Player reflection appears mirrored/reversed; must position themselves and manipulate symbols to match specific pattern
- **Outcome:** Reveals hidden passage
- **Types Combined:** Visual + Pattern Recognition

### 2. Elemental Puzzle
- **Setting:** Room with series of levers and buttons controlling elemental forces
- **Elements:** Earth, Fire, Air, Water
- **Mechanic:** Discover correct sequence to activate elements (e.g., Air lever → Water button = mist that reveals path)
- **Outcome:** Manipulate elements to reveal hidden passage
- **Types Combined:** Logic + Physical

### 3. Musical Puzzle
- **Setting:** Room filled with various musical instruments
- **Mechanic:** Sheet music with symbols corresponding to specific instruments; must play correct instrument sequence
- **Example Sequence:** Piano notes → Drum beat → Trumpet blast
- **Outcome:** Unlocks hidden door
- **Types Combined:** Pattern Recognition + Physical

### 4. Weight Puzzle
- **Setting:** Series of weights (different sizes/shapes) and scales marked with weight measurements
- **Mechanic:** Weigh each object and place in specific order on scales
- **Outcome:** Reveals hidden passage
- **Types Combined:** Logic + Physical + Observation

### 5. Memory Puzzle
- **Setting:** Dark room filled with various objects and items
- **Mechanic:** Players memorize object locations before lights go out; navigate darkness to retrieve specific object
- **Example:** Find key before lights off, navigate dark room, unlock hidden passage
- **Outcome:** Access to next area
- **Types Combined:** Memory + Observation + Physical

## Customization Notes
- All puzzles modifiable to suit player skill levels
- Can be combined or modified for increased difficulty
- Mix of logic, observation, and physical challenges
