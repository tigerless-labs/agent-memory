## Recommended Books

1. "The Namesake" by Jhumpa Lahiri
   - Explores identity, family, and culture through story of Bengali-American family

2. "The Kite Runner" by Khaled Hosseini
   - Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan
   - Themes: friendship, betrayal, redemption

3. "The Joy Luck Club" by Amy Tan
   - Follows four Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers

4. "Things Fall Apart" by Chinua Achebe
   - Explores life of Nigerian tribe before and after arrival of British colonizers

5. "Persepolis" by Marjane Satrapi
   - Graphic novel about young Iranian girl growing up during Islamic Revolution
