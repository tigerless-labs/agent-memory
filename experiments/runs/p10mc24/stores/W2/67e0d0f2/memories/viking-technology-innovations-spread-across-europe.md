Viking technological contributions that spread across Europe:

Shipbuilding: Clinker-built ships were stronger and more flexible than traditional European ships; technology widely adopted.

Metalworking: Vikings used bog iron from Scandinavia to create high-quality swords and axes; influenced medieval weapons development.

Woodworking: Intricate designs on ships and furniture influenced Scottish, British Isles, and Normandy craftsmanship.

Personal Hygiene: Vikings practiced cleanliness uncommon in Europe at the time; introduced combs, razors, and tweezers, raising hygiene standards across the continent.

Vikings established kingdoms in England, Ireland, and Normandy. Cultural integration especially in Ireland left lasting influence on language and local customs.
