## Recommended Movies

1. "Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon"
   - Martial arts epic set in ancient China
   - Themes: loyalty and honor

2. "The Lives of Others"
   - German movie set in East Berlin during Cold War
   - Themes: surveillance, loyalty, human connection

3. "Lion"
   - True story of Indian boy who gets lost
   - Adopted by Australian family, journeys to find birth family

4. "The Motorcycle Diaries"
   - Based on travel diaries of Che Guevara and Alberto
   - Travel across South America, explores injustices faced by poor

5. "City of God"
   - Brazilian movie set in violent favela (slum)
   - Themes: poverty, violence, survival
