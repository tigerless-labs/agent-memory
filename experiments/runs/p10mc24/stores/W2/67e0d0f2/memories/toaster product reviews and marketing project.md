## Toaster Product Overview

4-slice toaster with digital controls, wide slots, and timing function. Features:
- Capacity: 4 slices, 2 long slices, or 4 English muffins at once
- Digital settings with adjustable toasting time and temperature
- Timing function for meal preparation
- Clean-out tray at bottom for crumbs
- Sleek, modern matte black finish
- Compact yet wide slots

## Customer Reviews (May 20, 2023)

**Linzi Schwartz:**
- Likes sleek design
- Toasts unevenly (some spots burnt, others barely toasted)
- Cannot fit 4 slices if bread is larger than average loaf
- No bagel feature (not noticed before purchase)
- Digital settings have no in-between options
- Planning to donate and buy different brand

**Edward Farrey:**
- Bread doesn't toast evenly at top
- Digital controls neat but require "babysitting"
- Prefers old dial-type controllers for easier adjustment
- Looks nice and easy to operate
- Disappointed companies can't make good toasters

**Kindle Customer:**
- Large and lightweight/flimsy
- Gets very hot including sides
- Short cord length
- Eject is loud and jarring (startles people/animals)
- Likes 2-long-slice or 4-muffin capacity
- Would not necessarily recommend but will keep it

**Tim S.:**
- Likes wide slots and 4-slice capacity
- Timing function useful for meal prep
- Uneven toasting, top of bread undertoasted
- Requires flipping bread and re-toasting
- Clean-out tray doesn't catch all crumbs; spills on counter
- Cons outweigh pros
