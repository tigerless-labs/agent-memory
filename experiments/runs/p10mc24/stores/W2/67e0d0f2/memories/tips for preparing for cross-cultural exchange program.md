## Preparation Tips

1. Research the country and culture
   - Learn customs, traditions, history, current events
   - Understand cultural background of people you'll live with
   - Leads to more meaningful and respectful experience

2. Learn the language
   - Learn some basics before departing
   - Shows respect for local culture
   - Helps with communication

3. Pack appropriately
   - Pack clothes and items for local climate, culture, and activities
   - May need to leave behind favorite outfits
   - Bring items that wouldn't normally be needed at home

4. Be open-minded and flexible
   - Living in foreign country can be challenging
   - May encounter new foods, social norms, cultural expectations
   - Essential to maximize experience

5. Develop relationships with locals
   - Meet and develop relationships with local people
   - Practice language skills
   - Learn more about culture and make friends

6. Stay safe
   - Be aware of surroundings
   - Follow local customs and laws
   - Use common sense
