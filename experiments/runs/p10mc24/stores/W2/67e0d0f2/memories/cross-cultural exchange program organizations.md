## Recommended Organizations

1. AFS Intercultural Programs (https://afs.org/)
   - One of the largest non-profit organizations for intercultural learning
   - Programs include student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering
   - Available in 50+ countries

2. Experiment in International Living (https://www.experiment.org/)
   - Nonprofit offering immersive cultural exchange programs
   - Target: high school and college students
   - Features: live with host families, attend local schools, community service projects

3. Youth for Understanding (YFU) (https://www.yfu.org/)
   - International educational organization for exchange programs
   - Available in 40+ countries
   - Program types: year-long exchanges, semester programs, summer programs

4. Cross-Cultural Solutions (https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/)
   - Nonprofit volunteering opportunities for all ages
   - Countries: Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
   - Focus: community development projects and cross-cultural exchange
