Interested in landscape and nature photography, particularly sunset/seascape shots. Decided to upgrade to **Fujifilm X-T30 II** after researching Sony vs Fujifilm options. Budget: ~$1,500 for camera body and lens. Drawn to the X-T30 II for its compact size, vari-angle touchscreen LCD, and fast/accurate autofocus system (425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time). Camera has moisture resistance but is not fully weather-sealed. Does not yet own any interchangeable lenses. Preferences: APS-C sensor acceptable, weather sealing nice-to-have but not essential, not concerned about size/weight.
