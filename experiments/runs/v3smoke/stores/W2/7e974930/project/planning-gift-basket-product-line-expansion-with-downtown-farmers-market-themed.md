**Plan:** Add gift baskets to product line, starting with a themed concept.

**Specific idea:** Downtown Farmers Market themed gift basket featuring best-selling soaps and candles from that event.

**Strategic rationale:**
- Increases average order value per customer
- Appeals to local nostalgia and community connection
- Attracts new customer segment seeking thoughtful gifts
- Creates differentiated, unique offering

**Potential additions to baskets:**
- Personalized notes about the market and products
- Small mementos/tokens representing the market
- Custom-designed packaging reflecting market aesthetic
- Promotional codes or discounts to drive repeat visits

**Considerations before launch:**
- Start small with limited basket variations to test market
- Choose complementary products aligned with brand
- Price strategically to balance profit margins with value perception
- Assess impact on inventory management and complexity
