## Lucy Dacus\n- Albums: *No Burden* (2016, debut), *Historian* (2018), *Home Video* (2021)\n- Popular tracks: "Night Shift", "Addictions", "Nonbeliever"\n- Style: introspective songwriting, lo-fi production (earlier work), poignant lyrics\n\n## Phoebe Bridgers\n- Albums: *Stranger in the Alps* (2017, debut), *Punisher* (2020)\n- Collaborations: *Boygenius* EP (2018, with Julien Baker & Lucy Dacus), *Better Oblivion Community Center* (2019, with Conor Oberst)\n- Popular tracks: "Scott Street", "Killer", "Motion Sickness", "Funeral", "Kyoto"\n- Style: hauntingly beautiful, introspective, mix of indie-rock and electronic elements\n\n## Julien Baker\n- Albums: *Sprained Ankle* (2015, debut), *Turn Out the Lights* (2017)\n- Collaboration: *Boygenius* EP (2018, with Phoebe Bridgers & Lucy Dacus)\n- Popular tracks: "Appointments", "Turn Out the Lights", "Rejoice", "Souvenir", "Hurt Less"\n- Style: introspective, soaring vocals, mix of indie-rock and folk\n\n## Other recommended artists in this genre:\nBon Iver, Fleet Foxes, Sufjan Stevens, Courtney Marie Andrews, Vagabon, Adrianne Lenker (Big Thief), Sharon Van Etten, The Tallest Man on Earth
