Comprehensive guide to bass fishing with both conventional lures and fly fishing methods. Covers: effective lures (topwater, soft plastics, crankbaits, jigs), fly patterns (streamers, poppers, nymphs, dry flies), essential knots (Improved Clinch, Palomar, Loop), fly rod setup (9-10 ft 6-8 weight rod, floating/sinking line, 9-12 ft leader, 1-2 ft tippet), and retrieval techniques (strip, twitch, drag-free retrieves with pauses). Key presentation tips: target structure like rocks and weed beds, vary depth based on temperature, use 45-degree casting angles. Recommendations derived from fishing expertise.
