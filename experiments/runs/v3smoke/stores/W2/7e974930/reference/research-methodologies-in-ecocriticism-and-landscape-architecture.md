**Ecocritical research methods:**
- Close reading — detailed analysis of language, imagery, literary techniques representing nature
- Historical/cultural context analysis — place texts within their era to understand attitudes toward environment
- Environmental science/ecology analysis — apply scientific knowledge to understand nature representation
- Comparative analysis — compare texts across time periods and cultures to track changes in nature representation
- Ethnographic fieldwork — observe people and conduct interviews to understand human-environment relationships
- Discourse analysis — analyze language in literary and cultural texts to understand environmental views

**Landscape architecture methodologies:**
- Site Analysis — evaluate physical, ecological, cultural characteristics to understand opportunities and constraints
- Conceptual Design — iterative exploration of design ideas responsive to specific site context
- Master Planning — comprehensive plan for site development over time
- Detailed Design — specific drawings and specifications for construction
- Construction Management — oversee construction to ensure design implementation

**Interdisciplinary vs Transdisciplinary:** Interdisciplinary combines disciplines to study a problem; transdisciplinary involves stakeholders and practitioners in collaborative knowledge co-creation to solve real-world problems.
