## Story Setup
- **MK**: imprisoned behind magic barrier by Wukong
- **Wukong**: legendary Monkey King seeking 'the diamond snare'; cruel and manipulative captor
- **Core conflict**: MK must convince Wukong to keep him alive as 'insurance'; struggles with hunger/thirst

## Scenes Developed
1. Wukong questions why he needs MK alive; MK argues he's valuable as leverage—Wukong agrees to let him live (for now)
2. MK attempts to get Wukong's attention demanding food; backfires badly
3. Another monkey delivers meager rations (fruit, water); barely enough but MK takes it gratefully
4. MK reconciles his childhood memory of heroic Wukong with the cruel reality of his captor—confusion and fear
5. Monkeys hold loud feast/party; MK desperately tries to escape/steal food; fails
6. MK begs Wukong for sustenance, arguing he can't be used as insurance if he starves; Wukong reluctantly provides stale bread and water, annoyed by the pleas

## Thematic Elements
- Contrast between legendary hero and actual tyrant
- Survival desperation in captivity
- Psychological complexity of captor-captive dynamic
- Power imbalance used as leverage over time
