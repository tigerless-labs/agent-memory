Cargo Loading: (1) Crew establishes secure area; (2) Transport by helicopter/cargo planes/boats; (3) Secure with ropes/chains/straps; (4) Hoist aboard with ship cranes/davits; (5) Stow per cargo management plan. Unloading: (1) Identify secure deck area; (2) Retrieve cargo from storage; (3) Secure to equipment; (4) Transport to final destination. Safety: proper securing, detailed cargo plans, safety equipment (hardhats/gloves/goggles), clear crew communication, regular training/drills, continuous weather monitoring with operation abort if unsafe, high-standard navigation equipment. Arctic Challenges: unpredictable weather, rough seas, limited winter daylight. Crew Duration: typically weeks to several months, rarely up to one year. Crews trained in survival skills, isolation coping, extreme weather adaptation.
