For reclaimed wood dining tables:\n- Choose tables with durable, water-resistant finishes (polyurethane or wax-based coating)\n- Periodic sealing may be required; many manufacturers provide DIY guidance\n- Clean with gentle wood cleaner and soft cloth; avoid harsh chemicals\n- Use placemats, coasters, table runners to protect surface\n- Distressed/rustic finishes hide minor scratches and dents well\n- Consider tables with metal or wooden bases for easier maintenance\n\nAlternative: hybrid option with reclaimed wood top and metal/wooden base provides both character and durability.
