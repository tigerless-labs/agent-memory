Recommended weather apps for checking hiking trail forecasts:\n\n1. **Dark Sky** - Highly rated for accuracy, minute-by-minute forecasts\n2. **Mountain Weather Forecast** - Specifically designed for mountain regions, includes snowfall and avalanche risk\n3. **OpenSnow** - Mountain weather forecasts, includes wind conditions\n4. **Weather Underground** - 10-day forecasts, radar, satellite imagery\n5. **National Weather Service (NWS)** - Official service, detailed warnings\n6. **AccuWeather** - Minute-by-minute forecasts\n7. **The Weather Channel** - Detailed forecasts with radar\n\nUser specifically interested in Dark Sky and Mountain Weather Forecast.
