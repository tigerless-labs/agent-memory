Power consumption estimates for RAM upgrades:
- 8GB DDR4: ~1-2 watts
- 16GB DDR4: ~2-4 watts

Impact on battery life from RAM upgrade is minimal (~1-2W increase). Indirect benefits possible through improved task efficiency—laptop can complete tasks faster and enter low-power states sooner.
