Simple, nutritious snack ideas:

- Apple slices or celery sticks with almond or peanut butter
- Roasted chickpeas (seasoned with paprika, garlic powder, salt)
- Greek yogurt with fresh berries and honey drizzle
- Hummus with carrots, celery, or pita chips
- Trail mix with nuts, dried fruit, and dark chocolate chips
- Whole-grain crackers with avocado or guacamole
- Smoothies with frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, spinach or kale
- Energy balls with dates, nuts, and coconut

These snacks provide important nutrients without excess sugar, salt, or unhealthy fats—good as quick snacks between meals or healthy dessert alternatives.
