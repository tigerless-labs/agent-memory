Resources for finding hiking trails by location, distance, and difficulty:\n\n1. **AllTrails** - Popular website and app, search by location/distance/difficulty\n2. **TrailLink** - US trails, descriptions, maps, reviews\n3. **Hiking Project** - Comprehensive trail guides, maps, elevation profiles, user reviews\n4. **Local park websites** - Often list trails with distances and difficulty levels\n\nUser looking for 5-7 mile trails near their location.
