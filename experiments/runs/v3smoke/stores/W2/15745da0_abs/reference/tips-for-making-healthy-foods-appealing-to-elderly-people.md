Strategies for encouraging elderly individuals to eat nutritious foods:

- **Get creative with recipes**: Find interesting recipes incorporating healthy foods (fruit salads with berries, roasted vegetables with herbs and spices)
- **Incorporate into favorites**: Add healthy ingredients to foods they already enjoy (spinach in pasta sauce, berries in smoothies)
- **Presentation matters**: Use colorful, visually appealing plates; cut fruits/veggies into fun shapes; add garnishes
- **Consider texture**: Some elderly have difficulty chewing; try pureeing into smoothies or steaming/roasting until soft
- **Involve them**: Ask about food preferences and involve them in meal planning and preparation

Patience is key—introduce new foods in small amounts and allow time for adjustment.
