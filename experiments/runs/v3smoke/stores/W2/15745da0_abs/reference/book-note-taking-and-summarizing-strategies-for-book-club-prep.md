Strategies for preparing for book club discussions:\n\n**Summarizing approach:**\n- Identify main plot points and key events\n- Determine author's purpose and themes\n- Note key characters and their motivations\n- Focus on climax and resolution\n\n**Note-taking while reading:**\n- Use dedicated notebook or app\n- Create consistent system (colors, symbols, headings)\n- Write down impactful quotes with page numbers\n- Ask questions and make connections\n- Summarize each chapter or section\n\n**Additional tools:**\n- Use sticky notes or tabs for important pages\n- Create character maps showing relationships\n- Research author background and historical context\n- Discuss with others to gain different perspectives\n\nUser preparing for next Instagram book club discussion.
