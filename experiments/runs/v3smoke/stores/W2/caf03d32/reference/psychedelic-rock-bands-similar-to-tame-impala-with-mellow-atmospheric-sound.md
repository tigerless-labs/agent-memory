**Bands to explore:**
- **Pond** – Australian psychedelic rock, lush synths, dreamy vibe; album: *Man It Feels Like Space Again* (2015)
- **Unknown Mortal Orchestra** – NZ psych rock/funk/soul blend, atmospheric introspective sound
- **Temples** – British neo-psychedelia, rich atmospheric soundscapes, focus on melody
- **Mild High Club** – US psychedelic rock mixing jazz, folk, electronic; mellow and atmospheric
- **King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard** – AUS psych rock; "Am I in Heaven?" and "Sleep Drifter" for mellow tracks
- **Foxygen** – US indie rock duo blending psychedelia with folk and glam
- **The Black Angels** – US psychedelic rock, dark atmospheric sound
- **The Horrors** – UK post-punk/psychedelic, moody atmospheric approach
- **Moon Duo** – US psychedelic duo, hypnotic repetitive sound with krautrock influences
- **A Place to Bury Strangers** – US noise rock/psychedelic, dark atmospheric sound

**Key feature:** All share Tame Impala's experimental psychedelic approach with emphasis on atmospheric production rather than high-energy performance.
