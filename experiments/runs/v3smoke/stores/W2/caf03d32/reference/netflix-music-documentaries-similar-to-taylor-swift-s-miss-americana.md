**Recommended documentaries with behind-the-scenes and artist biography focus:**
- **Homecoming: A Film by Beyoncé** (2019) – Coachella preparation and performance
- **Quincy** (2018) – Life/career of Quincy Jones; interviews with Sinatra, Michael Jackson
- **Travis Scott: Look Mom I Can Fly** (2019) – Rise to fame and Astroworld tour
- **Gaga: Five Foot Two** (2017) – Lady Gaga's life, music, struggles with chronic pain, *Joanne* creation
- **The Black Godfather** (2019) – Clarence Avant's career; interviews with Quincy Jones, Snoop Dogg
- **The Apollo** (2019) – Apollo Theater history; performances and interviews with Aretha Franklin, James Brown
- **The Remix: Hip Hop × Fashion** (2019) – Hip-hop/fashion intersection; interviews with Kanye West, Pharrell
- **I'll Sleep When I'm Dead** (2016) – DJ Steve Aoki's life and tours
- **What Happened, Miss Simone?** (2015) – Nina Simone's life and career
- **The Art of Rap** (2012) – Hip-hop history and evolution; Ice-T, Dr. Dre, Eminem interviews

**Similar to Miss Americana:** All offer mix of music, personal story, and artist perspective.
