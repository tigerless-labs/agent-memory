## Data Privacy Interests

**Core interest:** Understanding how tech companies protect user data and emerging technologies that enhance privacy.

**Specific emerging technologies of interest:**
- Homomorphic encryption (encryption allowing computation on ciphertext without decryption)
- Differential privacy (noise injection to prevent individual re-identification)
- Federated learning (ML training on distributed data without centralization)
- Blockchain for decentralized data storage and access control

**Companies noted for strong privacy practices:**
- Apple (end-to-end encryption for iMessage/FaceTime, Safari privacy)
- Microsoft (privacy controls, 2FA, Windows Hello biometric auth)
- Google (third-party app restrictions, Privacy Checkup)
- Mozilla/Firefox (privacy-first browser, cookie blocking by default)
- ProtonMail (end-to-end encryption, zero-access encryption)

**Philosophy:** Recognizes that perfect data privacy solutions don't exist, but both companies and individuals must take proactive steps. Practices personal data protection through strong passwords, 2FA, and careful sharing online.
