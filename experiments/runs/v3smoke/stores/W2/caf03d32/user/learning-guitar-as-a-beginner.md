Beginner guitarist interested in learning. Prefers acoustic guitar as starting point to build foundational skills before progressing to electric.

**Recommended YouTube channels:**
- Justin Guitar (Justin Sandercoe) – comprehensive beginner course, encouraging approach
- Marty Music (Marty Schwartz) – focuses on learning popular songs quickly
- GuitarLessons365 (Bill) – structured acoustic guitar course
- GuitarJamz (Martyn) – popular songs and riffs
- Andy Guitar (Andy Crowley) – electric guitar with engaging style

Practice tip: consistency matters more than duration; 10-15 min daily beats irregular longer sessions.
