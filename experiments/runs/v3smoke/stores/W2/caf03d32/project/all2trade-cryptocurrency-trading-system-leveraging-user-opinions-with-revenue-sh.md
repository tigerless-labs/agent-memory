All2Trade is a cryptocurrency trading platform that uses collective user opinions to make automated trading decisions.

**Purpose**: Generate revenue by executing trades based on user insights into market trends, paying users a percentage of profits.

**Users**: Anyone who wants to contribute opinions and receive revenue share. Platform is free for users.

**Core Workflow**:
- Users provide trend opinions for specific cryptocurrencies via simple interface
- System aggregates opinions + applies deep learning analysis
- Automated trading executes based to combined signals
- Revenue from trades is distributed (percentage to opinion contributors, spread retained)

**Design**: Completely automated, minimal interface, free for users.
