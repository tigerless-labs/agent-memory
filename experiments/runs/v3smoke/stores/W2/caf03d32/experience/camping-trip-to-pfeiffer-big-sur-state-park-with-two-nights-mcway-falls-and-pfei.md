Spent two nights camping at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. Hiked to McWay Falls on the first day and did a shorter hike to Pfeiffer Beach on the second day. Used a portable power bank for navigation and photography, which was very helpful. Attempted astrophotography during the trip but results weren't as good as hoped — still learning night photography skills and wants to improve.
