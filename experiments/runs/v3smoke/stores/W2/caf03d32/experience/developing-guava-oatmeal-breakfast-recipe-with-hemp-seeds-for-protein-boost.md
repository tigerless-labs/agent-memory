Building a morning oatmeal routine with guava and hemp seeds to support fitness goals.

**Base Recipe Ratio:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1:2 guava-to-oatmeal ratio)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds (provides 5-10g protein + omega-3s)
- Optional: 1 tbsp chia seeds, honey/maple syrup, salt
- Toppings: banana, nuts, coconut flakes, cinnamon, protein powder

**Nutritional Profile:**
- Oatmeal (1 cup cooked): 6g protein, 4g fiber
- Guava (1/2 cup diced): 2g protein, 4g fiber
- Chia seeds (1 tbsp): 2g protein, 4g fiber
- Hemp seeds (1-2 tbsp): 5-10g protein, 1-2g omega-3s

**Tips:**
- Start with 1-2 tbsp hemp seeds; can grind them for easier mixing
- Mix ground hemp seeds with liquid first to create paste
- Store hemp seeds in fridge/freezer (go rancid quickly)
- Toast nuts/seeds at 350°F for 5-10 min to enhance flavor
