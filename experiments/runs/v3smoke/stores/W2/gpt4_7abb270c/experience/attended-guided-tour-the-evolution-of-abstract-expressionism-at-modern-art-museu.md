Attended a guided tour at the Modern Art Museum focused on the evolution of abstract expressionism. The tour was led by Dr. Patel and provided insights into the works of Jackson Pollock and Mark Rothko, highlighting the nuances of their artistic approaches and the emotional depth of abstract expressionism. Found the tour enlightening and appreciated gaining a deeper understanding of how much emotion and meaning could be conveyed through abstract art.
