Building consistent evening wind-down routine (30-60 minutes before bed) to improve sleep quality and time management. Activities include:\n- Meditation and deep breathing exercises\n- Reading (20-30 minutes, especially poetry and nature writing)\n- Avoiding screens 30-60 minutes before bed\n- Maintaining consistent sleep schedule\n\nFlexible approach: prioritize as routine but don't stress over missing days.
