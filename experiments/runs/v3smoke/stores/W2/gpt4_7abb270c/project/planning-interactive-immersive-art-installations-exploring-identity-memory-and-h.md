Currently planning to create interactive and immersive art installations with focus on identity, memory, and the human experience. Interested in using installations as a way to engage audiences and create empathy and connection.

Specific ideas being explored:
- Multi-sensory installations that incorporate sound, light, and texture to recreate the sensation of specific environments or memories (e.g., forest walk with bird sounds, rustling leaves, scent of damp earth)
- Explorations of collective memory and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts
- Installations using found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods to represent shared human experience
