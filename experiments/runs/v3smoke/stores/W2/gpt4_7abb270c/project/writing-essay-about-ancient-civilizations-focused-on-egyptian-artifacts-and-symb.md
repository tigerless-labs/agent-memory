Working on an essay about ancient civilizations with focus on ancient Egypt. Research topics include: Tutankhamun's golden mask (discovered 1922, housed at Egyptian Museum Cairo, made of 11kg solid gold with lapis lazuli, carnelian, and turquoise inlay), ankh symbol (represents eternal life, union of male/female principles), mummification process (70-day preservation process using natron and resin), and scarab beetles (symbolized regeneration and rebirth).

Visited an 'Ancient Egyptian Artifacts' exhibition where photographed various artifacts and saw mummified remains. Plans to use exhibition photos as reference material for essay.
