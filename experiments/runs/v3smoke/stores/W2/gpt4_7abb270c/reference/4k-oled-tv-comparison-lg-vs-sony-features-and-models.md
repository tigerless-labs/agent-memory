LG OLED TVs: Exceptional panel quality, perfect blacks, infinite contrast, wide color gamut, webOS smart platform, low input lag for gaming. Popular models: C9, B9, CX, GX series. Sony OLED TVs: Excellent panel quality, X1 Ultimate processor with object-based HDR, Acoustic Surface Audio, Android TV platform with Google Assistant, HDMI 2.1 support for gaming. Popular models: A9G, A8G, A1E, A9S series. Key difference: LG has slight edge in color accuracy, Sony has better gaming features; LG typically more affordable.
