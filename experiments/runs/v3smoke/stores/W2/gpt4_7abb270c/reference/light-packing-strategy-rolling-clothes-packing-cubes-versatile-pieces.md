**Space-saving:**
- Roll clothes instead of folding (less wrinkles, more space)
- Use packing cubes to organize and compress
- Decant toiletries into travel-sized containers

**Clothing:**
- Pack clothes that mix and match for multiple outfits
- Choose versatile pieces (scarves that work as blankets/accessories)
- Wear bulkiest items during travel instead of packing

**Productivity:**
- Download content for flights (movies, podcasts, audiobooks)
- Bring portable charger and work on projects during flight time
- Use layovers for meals, power naps, or exploring
