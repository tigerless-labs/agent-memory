Dell XPS: Premium design, InfinityEdge display (up to 4K OLED), Intel i9 processor, up to 64GB RAM, 2TB SSD, 18+ hour battery, multiple ports (Thunderbolt 3, USB-A, HDMI), good for gaming. MacBook Air: Aluminum unibody, Retina display, Apple M1 chip, up to 18 hour battery, two Thunderbolt 3 ports, integrates with Apple ecosystem. Key differences: OS (Windows vs macOS), gaming capability, price (XPS more affordable), ecosystem integration.
