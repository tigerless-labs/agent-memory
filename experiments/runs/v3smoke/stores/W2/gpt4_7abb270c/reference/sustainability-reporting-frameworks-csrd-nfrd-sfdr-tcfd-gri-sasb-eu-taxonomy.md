EU sustainability reporting frameworks and standards. CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) mandates ESEF/Inline XBRL format for large EU companies. NFRD (Non-Financial Reporting Directive) applies to large public-interest entities (>500 employees, >€20M balance sheet or >€40M turnover); recommends GRI, SASB, TCFD but no mandatory format. SFDR mandatory for financial market participants. EU Taxonomy requires common classification. TCFD, GRI, SASB are voluntary frameworks.
