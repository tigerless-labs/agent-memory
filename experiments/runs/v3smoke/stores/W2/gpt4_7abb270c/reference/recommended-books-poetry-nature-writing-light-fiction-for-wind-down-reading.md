**Poetry collections for relaxation:**\n- Mary Oliver's poetry collection (user's chosen starting point)\n- Emily Dickinson's Complete Poems\n- Walt Whitman's Leaves of Grass\n\n**Nature writing:**\n- Walden by Henry David Thoreau\n- Pilgrim at Tinker Creek by Annie Dillard\n- The Hidden Life of Trees by Peter Wohlleben\n\n**Light fiction (engaging but not stimulating):**\n- The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society by Mary Ann Shaffer\n- The Elegance of the Hedgehog by Muriel Barbery\n- The Unlikely Pilgrimage of Harold Fry by Rachel Joyce\n\nAll recommended for evening wind-down routines.
