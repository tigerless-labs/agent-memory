## Professional Organizations\n- **American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC)** — offers online courses, webinars, publications; https://www.conservation-us.org/\n- **International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC)** — features articles, news, events on art conservation\n- Conservation Wiki — collaborative platform for conservation techniques, materials, best practices\n\n## YouTube Channels\n- Art Conservation\n- Conservation Framing\n- The Getty Conservation Institute\n\n## Recommended Books\n- "The Conservation of Art and Architecture" by James C. Y. Watt\n- "The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory" by Caroline K. Keck\n- "Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide" by John H. Stubbs\n\n## Online Learning Platforms\n- Coursera\n- edX\n- Udemy\n\n## Learning Approach\n- Start with basic surface cleaning techniques on non-valuable items\n- Take workshops at local museums and galleries\n- Join professional conservation organizations for access to training and conferences\n- Practice material identification (paper, canvas, wood, pigments)\n- Invest in basic tools: tweezers, magnifying glasses, scalpels, brushes\n- Always work in well-ventilated areas with protective gear (gloves, goggles, masks)
