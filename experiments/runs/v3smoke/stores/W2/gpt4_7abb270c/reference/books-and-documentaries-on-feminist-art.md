Recommended books and documentaries for learning about feminist art history and theory:

**Books:**
1. "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard - comprehensive history 1960s-1990s
2. "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss - explores relationship between feminist art and maternal, featuring Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles
3. "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin - essays on representation of women in art and feminism in art history
4. "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard - detailed US 1970s examination
5. "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls - essays and artworks from the feminist collective

**Documentaries:**
1. "!Women Art Revolution" (2010) - Lynn Hershman Leeson, interviews with 40+ feminist artists (Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, Carolee Schneemann)
2. "The Heretics" (2009) - documentary on Heresies Collective (Nancy Azara, Miriam Schapiro)
3. "Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991) - Kate Horsfield series (Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, Yvonne Rainer)
4. "Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010) - panel discussion (Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell, moderated by Huey Copeland)
5. "Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014) - PBS series episode on Judy Chicago
