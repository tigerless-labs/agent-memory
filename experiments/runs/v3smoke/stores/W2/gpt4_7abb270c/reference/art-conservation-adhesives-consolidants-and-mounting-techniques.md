## Common Adhesives in Art Conservation\n- **Acrylic polymers** — water/solvent-based for paper, textiles (PVA, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV)\n- **Animal glues** — hide glue, bone glue for wood, paper, organic materials\n- **Cellulose-based** — rice starch paste, wheat starch paste for paper\n- **Epoxy resins** — two-part adhesives (Araldite, Epon) for metal, glass\n- **Natural waxes** — beeswax, paraffin for ceramics, stone\n- **Tissue adhesives** — gelatin, isinglass for delicate materials\n\n## Common Consolidants\n- **Acrylic polymers** — for fragile/powdery materials like wall paintings\n- **Paraloid B-72** — stone, ceramics, glass\n- **Butvar B-98** — ancient wall paintings, textile fragments\n- **Parylene** — metal, ceramics via vapor deposition\n- **Silanes** — stone, ceramics, glass by reacting with surface\n\n## Mounting Techniques\n- **Hinging** — reversible using rice starch or PVA with thin paper strips\n- **Window mounting** — creating window in mount board for small/delicate works\n- **Float mounting** — using thin spacer to create "floating" effect\n- **Conservation-grade adhesives** — BEVA 371, Lascaux 360 HV\n\n## Framing Considerations\n- Use acid-free, lignin-free, UV-filtering materials\n- Design for reversibility — easy disassembly without damage\n- Glazing options: glass or acrylic for UV/dust protection\n- Specialized techniques for textiles, objects, Asian art, illuminated manuscripts\n\n## Key Properties to Consider\n- **Reversibility** — must allow removal without damage\n- **Material compatibility** — must not react with artwork\n- **Flexibility** — accommodate natural movement over time\n- **Chemical stability** — resist degradation\n- **Penetrability** — for consolidants to be effective\n\n## Paper Selection for Conservation\n- **Acid-free/neutral pH** (around 7.0) preferred\n- **Lignin-free** to prevent yellowing/brittleness\n- **High alpha-cellulose** for strength/durability\n- **Cotton paper** — strong, acid-resistant, archival quality\n- **Linen paper** — strong, lightweight, tear-resistant\n- **Rag paper** — cotton/linen, acid-free, durable\n- **Kozu paper** — Japanese mulberry bark, used for Asian art\n- **Gampi paper** — Japanese fibers, lightweight, age-resistant
