According to Skyscanner research: cheapest flights book ~54 days before departure. Prime booking window: 21–121 days out. Set price alerts via Airfarewatchdog, The Flight Deal, or Secret Flying. Book mid-week (avoid Sat/Tue), clear cookies, consider alternative airports. Avoid booking during peak holidays.
