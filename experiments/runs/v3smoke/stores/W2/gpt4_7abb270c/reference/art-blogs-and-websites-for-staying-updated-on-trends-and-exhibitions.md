Recommended online resources for staying updated on art world news, trends, and exhibitions:

1. **Artsy** - comprehensive platform for art news, exhibitions, artist profiles, market trends
2. **Hyperallergic** - art blog with news, reviews, opinion on contemporary art (NYC focus)
3. **Artnet** - art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists
4. **The Art Newspaper** - daily international art news, exhibitions, market analysis
5. **Contemporary Art Daily** - discovering new artists and exhibitions (contemporary focus)
6. **Art in America** - contemporary art magazine with features, reviews, interviews
7. **Frieze** - contemporary art magazine covering art, culture, politics, international exhibitions
8. **Apollo Magazine** - British art magazine covering art, architecture, collecting
9. **Art21** - non-profit producing documentaries and online content on contemporary art
10. **Culture Trip** - art, culture, travel coverage focusing on emerging artists and underground scenes
