Blog post about Blue Cheese Chocolate Chip Cookies recipe featuring a story about a picky-eater son who loved the unexpected flavor combination.

**Inspiration:** Blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from a local chocolate shop.

**Recipe:**
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temp
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

**Method:** Cream butter, sugar, and blue cheese; add eggs and vanilla; mix in flour mixture; fold in chocolate chips. Bake at 375°F for 12-15 min.

**Development:** First attempts were too pungent and salty; recipe refined through iterations to balance sweet and savory. Blue cheese flavor should be subtle and intriguing, not overpowering.
