Investigated effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension. Found systems can be effective but implementation and classroom integration require careful consideration.

## Key Findings
- AI-powered adaptive learning systems can improve student learning outcomes
- Teacher professional development is critical for effective integration
- Clear curriculum alignment increases teacher adoption and effectiveness
- Systems have potential for equity/inclusion but algorithmic bias poses risks to existing inequities
- Implementation challenges common across platforms studied

## Recommendations Made
- Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support
- Developers should engage closely with educators to align with curriculum and learning goals
- Implement ongoing evaluation and assessment protocols
- Address algorithmic bias to ensure equity in outcomes
