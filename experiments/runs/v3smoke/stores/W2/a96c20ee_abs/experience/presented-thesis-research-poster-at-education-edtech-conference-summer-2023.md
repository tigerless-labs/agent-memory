Presented research on AI-powered adaptive learning systems at a research conference in summer 2023. This was first research conference presentation.

## Reception
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers on the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities
- Multiple developers reached out afterward, interested in learning more about findings and potential applications
- Notable point of discussion: some disagreement among stakeholders about role of teachers in AI systems (replacement vs. support tool)

## Stakeholder Perspectives Encountered
- Some educators concerned AI systems would replace teachers
- Others saw systems as valuable tool to support teaching and learning
