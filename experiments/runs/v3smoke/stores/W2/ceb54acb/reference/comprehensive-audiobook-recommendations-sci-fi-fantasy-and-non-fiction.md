From conversation on 2023-05-22, collected audiobook recommendations across multiple genres.

Science Fiction:
- Ready Player One by Ernest Cline (narrated by Wil Wheaton)
- The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude)

Fantasy:
- The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North (narrated by Peter Kenny)
- The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (narrated by Simon Vance)
- The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison (narrated by Kyle McCarley)

Fantasy similar to The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet:
- The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard (narrated by Anne Flosnik)
- The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker (narrated by George Guidall)
- The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty (narrated by Soneela Nankani)
- The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller)
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller)

Self-Improvement:
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey (narrated by Stephen Covey)
- Daring Greatly by Brene Brown (narrated by Brene Brown)
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck (narrated by Bernadette Dunne)

Productivity:
- Deep Work by Cal Newport (narrated by Jeff Bottoms)
- Essentialism by Greg McKeown (narrated by Greg McKeown)
- Atomic Habits by James Clear (narrated by James Clear)

General Non-Fiction:
- Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari (narrated by Derek Perkins)
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle (narrated by Eckhart Tolle)
- Shoe Dog by Phil Knight (narrated by Norbert Leo Butz and Phil Knight)
