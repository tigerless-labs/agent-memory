Films discussed for their use of symbolism:

**The Godfather** — Oranges symbolize impending danger and death; appear before violent events and character deaths (e.g., Don Corleone at fruit market, assassination attempt in garden).

**The Shining** — Maze symbolizes the mind; red symbolizes danger and violence.

**Blade Runner** — Eyes, animals, nature symbolize identity, humanity, and nature of existence.

**Get Out** — White symbolizes oppressive racism; deer imagery symbolizes victimhood.

**The Matrix** — Red and blue pills symbolize choice and free will; overall theme explores reality and human perception.

Films considered great without heavy symbolism: Casablanca, The Shawshank Redemption, Star Wars: Episode IV.
