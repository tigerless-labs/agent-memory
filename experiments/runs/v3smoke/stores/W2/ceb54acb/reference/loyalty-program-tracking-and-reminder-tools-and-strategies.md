## Tracking Options\n- **Apps**: AwardWallet, LoyaltyLion, Stocard\n- **Digital tools**: Google Sheets, Excel, Evernote, OneNote, Simplenote\n- **Physical**: Notebook, dedicated loyalty card tracker\n\n## Reminder Tools\n- Built-in phone reminders (location or time-based)\n- Habit tracking apps: Habitica, HabitBull, Loop Habit Tracker, AnyList\n- Sticky notes on fridge, car dashboard, wallet\n- Notes on phone lock screen\n\n## Staying Informed\n- Newsletter and email signup\n- Social media follows\n- Mobile app push notifications\n- Regular website checks\n- Text message opt-in\n- In-store promotion posters/flyers\n- Ask store employees\n- Google Alerts\n- Loyalty program forums and communities
