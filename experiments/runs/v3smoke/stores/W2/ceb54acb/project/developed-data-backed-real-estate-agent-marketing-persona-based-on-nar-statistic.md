Created performance marketing SaaS persona profile using NAR (National Association of Realtors) research data.

Persona profile: Susan Green, 55-year-old female agent in suburban/rural area with median income of 61,140 dollars (2019 data).

Key attributes: Detail-oriented, customer-focused, continuously learning. Uses CRM software, email marketing, social media. Main pain points are limited marketing resources, difficulty tracking performance, competition from other agents, staying current with technology.

Goals include boosting visibility and reputation, increasing lead generation and conversion, staying ahead of competition, and streamlining marketing efforts. Expects marketing solutions to demonstrate ROI.
