Actively works to build connections on social media with people who share similar interests. Implements engagement strategies: responding to comments promptly, asking questions to encourage discussion, sharing valuable content, using hashtags strategically, participating in online communities, and collaborating with others. Uses tools and systems—spreadsheets, reminders, batching similar tasks—to stay organized and keep track of multiple conversations and relationships across platforms.
