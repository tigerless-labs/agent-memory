Developing interest in printmaking techniques. Key learning areas:

**Materials & effects:**
- Ink type (water-based vs. oil-based) affects brayer selection
- Paper thickness impacts ink saturation and final appearance
- Printing plate materials (copper, zinc, linoleum) produce different textures
- Brayer type (hardness/size) affects ink distribution and detail capture

**Beginner approach:**
- Start with relief printing (lower barrier to entry)
- Experimentation with different materials is essential for learning
- Trial and error is normal; mistakes are learning opportunities
- Practice is key to mastering the craft

**Next steps:** Consider taking a printmaking class or workshop to learn hands-on techniques and connect with other artists.
