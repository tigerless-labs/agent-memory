Planning to improve grandmother's diet to support cognitive function and overall health.

**Target nutrients:**
- Omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, tuna, mackerel, flaxseed)
- Antioxidants (berries, spinach, kale)
- B vitamins (B6, B9/folate, B12 from leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals)
- Moderate caffeine (coffee/tea)
- Curcumin (turmeric)

**Approach strategies:**
- Involve grandmother in meal planning and preparation to increase engagement
- Find creative, interesting recipes using these foods
- Incorporate into dishes she already enjoys (e.g., adding spinach to pasta sauce)
- Consider texture preferences (some older adults may prefer steamed/roasted, pureed options)
- Make food visually appealing with colorful plates and garnishes
- Introduce small amounts gradually and be patient with her adjustment to new flavors
- Easy snack options: apple/celery with almond butter, hummus, Greek yogurt with berries, trail mix, smoothies, energy balls

**Status:** Planning phase (as of May 2023)
