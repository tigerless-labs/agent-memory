## Artistic Practice

**Current Focus:** Interactive and immersive art installations

**Themes of Interest:**
- Identity, memory, and human experience
- Collective memory and shared cultural/historical contexts
- Individual experiences shaped by cultural and historical context

**Installation Concepts in Development:**
- Multi-sensory environments recreating specific places/memories (sound, light, texture, scent)
  - Example: simulating forest walk with bird sounds, rustling leaves, damp earth scent
- Found objects, images, sounds from different cultures/time periods exploring shared human experience
- Incorporates personal narratives, historical events, and mythical stories

**Technologies/Techniques Exploring:**
- 3D spatial audio
- Projection mapping
- Scent diffusion
- Haptic feedback (vibrating floors/walls)
- Data visualization
- Augmented reality
- Interactive storytelling (branching narratives, choose-your-own-adventure)
- Collaborative platforms for viewer contributions
- Tools: Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino

**Influences & Study:**
- Studied 1970s feminist art, particularly works of Judy Chicago
- Attended Dr. Maria Rodriguez lecture on feminist art in 1970s
- Interest in how feminist art explores identity and female experience
