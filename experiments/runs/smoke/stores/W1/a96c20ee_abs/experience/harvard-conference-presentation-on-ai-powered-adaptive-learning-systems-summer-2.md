Presented thesis research as a poster at first research conference at Harvard University in summer 2023. Research focused on the effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension. Key findings: systems can be effective, but implementation and integration require careful consideration. Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers who appreciated the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities. Some developers reached out afterwards to discuss findings.
