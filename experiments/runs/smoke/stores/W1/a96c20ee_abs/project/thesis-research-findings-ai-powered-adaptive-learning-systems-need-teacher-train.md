Research on AI-powered adaptive learning systems identified three critical success factors:

1. **Teacher Professional Development**: Ongoing training and support essential for teachers to effectively integrate systems into teaching practices. Many teachers lack necessary skills and knowledge, which hinders successful implementation.

2. **Curriculum Alignment**: Clear understanding of how systems align with curriculum and learning goals is crucial. When alignment is clear, teachers more likely to adopt and use systems effectively.

3. **Algorithmic Bias**: Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students, but concerns exist about algorithmic bias potentially exacerbating existing inequities if not addressed.

Recommendations: Prioritize teacher professional development, ensure developers work closely with educators on alignment and needs, emphasize ongoing evaluation and assessment of system effectiveness.
