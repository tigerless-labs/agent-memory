Specific interests in AI-powered education systems:
- **Personalized learning**: How AI can tailor instruction to individual student needs, abilities, and learning styles
- **Real-time feedback**: How immediate, specific, and actionable feedback improves student learning outcomes
- **Critical thinking & problem-solving**: How AI facilitates development of argumentation, inquiry-based learning, and problem-solving skills through simulations and games
- **Collaborative learning**: How AI-powered systems facilitate collaborative problem-solving among students, including virtual collaboration platforms, intelligent facilitation, and communication analysis

Saw projects on these topics at Harvard University research conference.
