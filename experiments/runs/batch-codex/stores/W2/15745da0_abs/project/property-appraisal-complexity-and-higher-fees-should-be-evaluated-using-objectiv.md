Purpose: help the team consistently decide whether an appraisal order is complex and whether a higher fee is justified. The assessment should examine location, property type and design, age and condition, zoning and land use, and market conditions. An appraiser's assessment does not conclusively establish complexity; the team should weigh evidence against its own research because appraisers may have a vested interest in securing the highest fee possible.
