Recommended storing cameras around **65°F to 70°F** and **around 50% humidity**, away from direct sunlight, in original cases or acid-free boxes with padded separation and no direct stacking. Dust with a soft brush or blower bulb, clean lenses using lens-safe solution and microfiber, inspect seals, inventory make/model/serial number/condition/purchase and repair history, and schedule maintenance, such as quarterly. For display, use tiered or divided shelves or UV-filtering glass-front cases with soft indirect lighting.
