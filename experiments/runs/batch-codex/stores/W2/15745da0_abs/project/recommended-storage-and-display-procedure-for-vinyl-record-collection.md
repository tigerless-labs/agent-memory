Recommended acid-free record crates, storage cubes, or dedicated LP shelving; keep records upright, clean, and dry and handle them by the edges. Suggested highlighting the rare **1978 pressing of Fleetwood Mac's _Rumours_** in a central display case or individual acrylic stand, optionally with Fleetwood Mac records or related artwork and memorabilia.
