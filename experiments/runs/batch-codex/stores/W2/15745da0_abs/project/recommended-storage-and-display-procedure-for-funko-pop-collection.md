Recommended dedicated slotted shelves or glass-door cases; individual protector cases or labeled compartmented bins for storage; regular dusting and careful handling. For the Marvel Cinematic Universe set, group figures by movie or series, recreate scenes, highlight key characters, add props, and rotate the display.
