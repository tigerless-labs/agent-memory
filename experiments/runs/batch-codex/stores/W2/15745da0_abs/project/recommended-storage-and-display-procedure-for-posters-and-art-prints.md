Recommended storing posters in sturdy acid-free tubes, flat files, portfolios, folders, or sleeves. For display, use acid-free mats and UV-filtering glass or acrylic, poster hangers, print racks, or shadow boxes. Suggested placing the **Mondo Hogwarts castle poster** prominently or grouping it with Harry Potter or fantasy art, keeping it in a clean, dry environment, and rotating displays.
