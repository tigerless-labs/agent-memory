Brayer guidance: water-based ink generally calls for a softer brayer and oil-based ink for a harder brayer; smoother surfaces may suit harder brayers, rougher surfaces softer brayers; match brayer size to the printing surface; fine detail may benefit from a smaller, firmer brayer; experimentation matters. Beginner suggestions: start with relief printing, test papers and inks, accept mistakes, take a class or workshop, and practice.
