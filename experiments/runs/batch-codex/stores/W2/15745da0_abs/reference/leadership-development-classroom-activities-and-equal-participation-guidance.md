Suggested leadership activities included workplace simulations, trying different leadership styles, boardroom roles such as CEO/CFO/CMO, decision-making scenarios, conflict-resolution role play, mentorship, service learning, internships, workshops, and collaborative learning. Engagement suggestions: use relatable real-life examples, state clear objectives, encourage creativity, allow flexibility, and give feedback. Equal group participation suggestions: set expectations, give individual assignments, assign roles such as leader/recorder/timekeeper, encourage active listening, and monitor participation with early feedback.
