Suggestions included a social-media calendar; Hootsuite, Buffer, or Sprout Social; reminders; a conversation spreadsheet; prioritizing communities; strategic notifications; batching engagement; and a separate book-club note with discussion dates. Reading-note suggestions included chapter summaries, quotes with page numbers, questions and connections, character maps, tabs or sticky notes, and research into the author and historical context.
