The assistant advised reviewing all application details, bringing originals and photocopies requested in the interview notice, arriving at least 30 minutes early, dressing professionally, answering honestly, asking for clarification when needed, and promptly providing requested follow-up material. For a marriage-based interview it suggested reviewing relationship dates and shared-life details with the spouse, bringing evidence such as photos, joint financial statements, shared-residence records, and letters or cards, and preparing for possible separate questions about how the couple met, routines, free time, household chores, conflicts, and future plans. It also suggested understanding the husband’s job title, duties, education, field of study, and how the work uses related or transferable skills.
