For 4-6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, make a marinade from 1/4 cup olive oil, 1/4 cup lemon juice, 2 cloves garlic (minced), 2 teaspoons dried oregano, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Marinate the chicken for 20-30 minutes at room temperature. Heat a grill pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat, cook the breasts for 6-7 minutes per side until the internal temperature is at least 165°F, then rest for 5-10 minutes before slicing and serving.
