The assistant recommended anonymous employee surveys; tracking health measures such as blood pressure, cholesterol, and BMI before and after implementation; monitoring absenteeism; measuring productivity; and calculating savings from insurance claims, sick days, and productivity. It also stressed that effectiveness depends on program design and fit for the workforce.
