Suggestions for a living room with big windows were Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata), ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia), Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum), Pothos (Epipremnum aureum), succulents such as Aloe, Echeveria, and Crassula, Dracaena (Dracaena spp.), Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii), and bromeliads such as Aechmea, Guzmania, and Nidularium.
