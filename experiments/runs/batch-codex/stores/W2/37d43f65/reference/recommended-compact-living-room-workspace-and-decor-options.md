For a compact workspace, the assistant suggested a wall-mounted desk, narrow console table, drop-leaf table, ottoman with tray, or foldable desk. Suggested retailers were IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, and Amazon. For artwork, it suggested botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, and pop art, with Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, and Saatchi Art as sources. A circular rug around 3-4 feet in diameter and a circular mirror around 12-18 inches in diameter were suggested as proportional starting points.
