After upgrading the 2018 Dell Inspiron 15 5000 series laptop to 16GB RAM, the user reported a huge performance boost, but battery life declined from around 6-7 hours to around 4-5 hours. They reported no other recent hardware or software changes and planned to try battery calibration.
