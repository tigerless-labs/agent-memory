Suggested on 2023-05-23: Discogs, eBay, MusicStack, Reverb LP, Vinyl Me, Please, The Vinyl Factory, Reddit's r/vinylcollectors, Vinyl Collective, local record stores, and Half Price Books. Advice: research completed sales, grade accurately, provide detailed descriptions and photos, consider bundles, negotiate, and package records carefully.
