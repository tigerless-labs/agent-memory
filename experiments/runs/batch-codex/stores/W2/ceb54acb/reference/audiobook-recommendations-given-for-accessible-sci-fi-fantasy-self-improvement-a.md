Recommendations given on 2023-05-22:

Sci-fi: **The Three-Body Problem** by Liu Cixin, narrated by Luke Daniels; **Ready Player One** by Ernest Cline, narrated by Wil Wheaton; **The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet** by Becky Chambers, narrated by Rachel Dulude.

Fantasy: **The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August** by Claire North, narrated by Peter Kenny; **The Lies of Locke Lamora** by Scott Lynch, narrated by Simon Vance; **The Goblin Emperor** by Katherine Addison, narrated by Kyle McCarley; **The House of Shattered Wings** by Aliette de Bodard, narrated by Anne Flosnik; **The Golem and the Jinni** by Helene Wecker, narrated by George Guidall; **The City of Brass** by S.A. Chakraborty, narrated by Soneela Nankani; **The Black Tides of Heaven** by J.Y. Yang, narrated by Emily Woo Zeller; **The Poppy War** by R.F. Kuang, narrated by Emily Woo Zeller.

Self-improvement/productivity: **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen Covey, narrated by Stephen Covey; **Daring Greatly: How the Courage to Be Vulnerable Transforms the Way We Live, Love, Parent, and Lead** by Brené Brown, narrated by Brené Brown; **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck, narrated by Bernadette Dunne; **Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World** by Cal Newport, narrated by Jeff Bottoms; **Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less** by Greg McKeown, narrated by Greg McKeown; **Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones** by James Clear, narrated by James Clear.

Other non-fiction: **Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind** by Yuval Noah Harari, narrated by Derek Perkins; **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle, narrated by Eckhart Tolle; **Shoe Dog: A Memoir by the Creator of Nike** by Phil Knight, narrated by Norbert Leo Butz and Phil Knight.

Listening advice: begin around 1x or 1.25x, increase toward 1.5x if comprehension remains good, pause to take notes and reflect, revisit key sections, and optimize speed by topic.
