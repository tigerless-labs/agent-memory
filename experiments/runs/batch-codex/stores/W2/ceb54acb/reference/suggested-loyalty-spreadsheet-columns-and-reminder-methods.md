Suggested spreadsheet columns: Program Name, Points/Rewards, Earned, Needed for Reward, Expiration Date, and Notes. For The Daily Grind, suggested Program Name, Stamps, Expiration Date, and Notes. Reminder ideas included location/time-based phone reminders, associating loyalty use with shopping or morning coffee routines, keeping cards accessible, visual cues, linked payment cards, and aggregators such as AwardWallet and Stocard.
