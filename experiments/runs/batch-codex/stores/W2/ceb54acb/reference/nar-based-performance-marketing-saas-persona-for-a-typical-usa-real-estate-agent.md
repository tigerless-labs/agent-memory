Persona drafted using https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics: **Susan Green**, age 55, female, located in a suburban or rural area, real estate agent, median income of $61,140 (2019), with a bachelors degree in Real Estate or a related field. She is detail-oriented, organized, customer-focused, adaptable, tech-savvy, and knowledgeable about the local market. Key pains are limited marketing resources, difficulty measuring performance, competition, and keeping up with technology and trends. Goals are visibility and reputation, more leads and conversions, staying competitive, and streamlining marketing. Uses CRM, email, social media, online listings, referrals, direct mail, and brokerage resources; willing to invest where ROI is demonstrated.
