Suggested on 2023-05-23: eBay, NGC (Numismatic Guaranty Corporation), CoinWeek, APMEX (American Precious Metals Exchange), JM Bullion, Liberty Coin, Reddit's r/coincollecting, Coin Forum, CoinTalk, and CoinCommunity; for exceptionally rare or valuable coins, Heritage Auctions or Stack's Bowers. Advice: research comparable prices, consider grading/certification by NGC or PCGS, photograph obverse and reverse, document rarity and provenance, and package carefully.
