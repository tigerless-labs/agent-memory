Suggested on 2023-05-23: eBay, Etsy, Ruby Lane, The Typewriter Shop, Typewriter Collector, Gramercy Typewriter, The Typewriter Forum, and Reddit's r/typewriters. Advice: research comparable sales, clean or restore appropriately, provide detailed photos and descriptions, test functionality with a test page or video, include accessories, and package carefully.
