Suggested asking about reservations concerning the candidate, the role's fit with organizational goals, upcoming initiatives, skill development, near-term challenges, key performance indicators, available support and resources, cross-department collaboration, desired qualities and alignment with the candidate's strengths, and the decision timeline and next steps.
