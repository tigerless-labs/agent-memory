Suggested on 2023-05-23: Abebooks, Biblio, Alibris, The Strand, Powell's City of Books, Peter Harrington Rare Books, Reddit's r/rarebooks, Book Collectors' Forum, Christie's, and Sotheby's. Advice: obtain a professional appraisal, research comparable prices, document condition and distinguishing features with photos, consider auction consignment for an exceptionally valuable copy, and retain provenance.
