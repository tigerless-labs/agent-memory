Recommendations given on 2023-05-23: search the American Society of Appraisers (ASA), National Association of Jewelry Appraisers (NAJA), and International Society of Appraisers (ISA); select a certified specialist in vintage or antique jewelry and pearls; seek referrals from antique dealers, estate-sale companies, or auction houses; check reviews; interview 2-3 appraisers about process, written reports, fees, and conflicts of interest; bring provenance or receipts; and consider a second opinion. Expected fee stated: $100 to $500 or more.
