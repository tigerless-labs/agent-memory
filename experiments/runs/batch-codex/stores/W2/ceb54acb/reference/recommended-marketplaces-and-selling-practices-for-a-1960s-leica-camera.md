Suggested on 2023-05-23: eBay, Etsy, KEH Camera, Leica Store, Camera West, The Camera Shop, Reddit's r/vintagecameras, Leica Forum, Fred Miranda, Photography-on-the-Net, and DPReview. Advice: research comparable prices, clean the camera, document lenses/accessories/condition, test it with a roll or sample photos, consider professional servicing, and package carefully.
