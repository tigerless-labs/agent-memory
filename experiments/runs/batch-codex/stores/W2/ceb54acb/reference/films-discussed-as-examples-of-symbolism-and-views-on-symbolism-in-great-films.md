Examples supplied on 2023-05-23: The Godfather uses oranges as a commonly analyzed sign of impending danger or death, though Francis Ford Coppola and Gordon Willis had not confirmed or denied that interpretation; The Shining (1980) uses the maze and red; Blade Runner (1982) uses eyes, animals, and nature; Get Out (2017) uses white and deer imagery; The Matrix (1999) uses red and blue pills. Symbolism was described as capable of adding depth but not necessary for greatness; Casablanca (1942), The Shawshank Redemption (1994), and Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope (1977) were offered as examples. Heavy-handed or excessive symbolism can distract or feel contrived.
