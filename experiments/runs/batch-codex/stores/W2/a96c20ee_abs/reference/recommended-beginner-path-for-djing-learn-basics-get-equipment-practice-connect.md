The suggested beginner path was: learn beatmatching, track mixing, and equipment basics; research a DJ controller or turntables plus software suited to budget and skill level; practice at home, with friends, or at local DJ events; connect through events, forums, communities, and collaborations; and record/upload mixes or perform locally.
