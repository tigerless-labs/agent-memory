The research found that AI-powered adaptive learning systems can improve student outcomes, but success depends on careful classroom implementation and integration. Recurring case-study themes were the need for ongoing teacher professional development and training and clear alignment with curriculum and learning goals; teachers lacking relevant skills and knowledge struggled to implement systems successfully.
