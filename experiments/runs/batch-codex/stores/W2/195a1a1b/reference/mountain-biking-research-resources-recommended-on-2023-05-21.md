Recommended trail resources: Trailforks, MTB Project, TrailLink, MapMyRide, local bike shops, national forests and parks, and IMBA (International Mountain Bicycling Association) Epics. Suggested event resources: USA Cycling, local cycling organizations, and events including Sea Otter Classic and Crankworx. General target characteristics discussed were moderate trails, around 1,000-2,000 feet of elevation gain, and 20-30-mile loops or out-and-back routes.
