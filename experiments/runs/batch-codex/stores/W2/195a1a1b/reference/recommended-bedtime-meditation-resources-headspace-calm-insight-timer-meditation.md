Suggested meditation apps included **Headspace** (guided meditation for sleep, stress, and focus; free trial then subscription), **Calm** (guided meditations, sleep stories, and relaxing music; free trial then subscription), **Insight Timer** (free, large guided-meditation library), **Meditation Studio** (sleep, stress, and anxiety meditations; free with in-app purchases), and **Buddhify** (meditations for different parts of the day, including bedtime; one-time purchase). Other resources named were Guided Meditation by Andrew Weil on YouTube, Rainy Mood, Sleep With Me, Meditation Oasis, and UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center.
