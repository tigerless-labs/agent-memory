The beneficiary redemption flow is: tap the smart card on an authorized vendor's POS terminal, choose items, and enter a PIN to validate the transaction. The POS issues a receipt confirming the items or cash equivalent received, while the project team can monitor transactions and balances for accountability, transparency, and future card top-ups.
