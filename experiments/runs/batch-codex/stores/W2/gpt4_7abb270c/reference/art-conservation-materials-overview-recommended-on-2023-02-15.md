Adhesives discussed: PVA glue, Acryloid/Paraloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV, hide glue, bone glue, rice starch paste, wheat starch paste, Araldite, Epon, beeswax, paraffin wax, gelatin, isinglass, cyanoacrylates, and BEVA 371. Consolidants: acrylic polymers, Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98, Parylene, silanes, and natural waxes. Coatings: dammar varnish, acrylic varnish, lacquers, waxes, Acryloid B-72, and Lascaux 360 HV. Selection factors included reversibility, strength, flexibility, penetrability, chemical stability, UV/water resistance, aging, and compatibility.
