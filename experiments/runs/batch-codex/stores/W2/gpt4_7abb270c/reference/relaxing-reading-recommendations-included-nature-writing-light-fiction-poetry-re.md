Recommended bedtime reading: "Walden" by Henry David Thoreau; "Pilgrim at Tinker Creek" by Annie Dillard; "The Hidden Life of Trees" by Peter Wohlleben; "The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society" by Mary Ann Shaffer; "The Elegance of the Hedgehog" by Muriel Barbery; "The Unlikely Pilgrimage of Harold Fry" by Rachel Joyce; "Leaves of Grass" by Walt Whitman; "The Complete Poems of Emily Dickinson"; "The Poetry of Mary Oliver"; "The Power of Now" by Eckhart Tolle; "Mindset: The New Psychology of Success" by Carol S. Dweck; "The Miracle of Mindfulness" by Thich Nhat Hanh; "The Essays of George Orwell"; "The Complete Short Stories of Ernest Hemingway"; and "Me Talk Pretty One Day" by David Sedaris.
