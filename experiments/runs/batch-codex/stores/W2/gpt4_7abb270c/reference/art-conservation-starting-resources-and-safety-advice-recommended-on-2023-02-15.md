Recommended: American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC), International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC), Conservation Wiki, The Getty Conservation Institute, Coursera, edX, Udemy, and museum or professional-organization workshops. Advice included practicing only on non-valuable items, prioritizing reversibility and material compatibility, ventilation, protective gear, and seeking an experienced conservator when unsure.
