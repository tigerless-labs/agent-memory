Suggested: American Museum of Natural History including Milstein Hall of Ocean Life, Rose Center for Earth and Space, and Discovery Room; Children’s Museum of the Arts; New York Hall of Science; Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum; The Met Temple of Dendur and family programs; Guggenheim Museum; Whitney Museum of American Art; Museum of Arts and Design (MAD); Brooklyn Museum; and Studio Museum in Harlem. Interactive ideas included fossil digs, dinosaur play, water play, building zones, science experiments, mini-cities, and art studios.
