Books recommended: "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard; "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss; "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin; "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard; and "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls. Documentaries/resources recommended: "!Women Art Revolution" (2010), "The Heretics" (2009), "Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991), "Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010), and "Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014).
