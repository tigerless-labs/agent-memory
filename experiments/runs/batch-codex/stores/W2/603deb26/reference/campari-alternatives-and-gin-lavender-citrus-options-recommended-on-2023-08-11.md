Suggested Negroni bitter alternatives: Cynar, Aperol, Luxardo Bitter, Suze, and Meletti, adjusting bitter-to-vermouth ratios to taste. Suggested citrus pairings for gin and lavender: orange (especially blood orange), yuzu, kumquat, satsuma, and bergamot; use fresh citrus, begin with small amounts, and use twists or peels for aroma.
