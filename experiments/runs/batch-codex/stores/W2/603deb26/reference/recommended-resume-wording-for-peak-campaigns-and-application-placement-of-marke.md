Suggested wording included: “Demonstrated flexibility and adaptability by working extended hours (up to 50 hours/week) during peak campaign seasons to ensure timely and successful project delivery.” Other options emphasized high-volume workloads, tight deadlines, time management, prioritization, and thriving in fast-paced environments. Recommended listing Google Analytics and HubSpot Inbound Marketing in a resume Certifications or Skills section, LinkedIn Licenses & Certifications, and relevant cover letters.
