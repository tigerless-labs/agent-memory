In the five-paragraph essay, the assistant concluded that Miss Stapleton should be held responsible for actively helping the plot against the Baskervilles, potentially facing charges ranging from conspiracy to attempted murder, but that punishment should be tempered because her cruel and abusive brother controlled her and her conduct may have been driven by coercion and desperation. The proposed emphasis was rehabilitation and making amends rather than punishment for its own sake.
