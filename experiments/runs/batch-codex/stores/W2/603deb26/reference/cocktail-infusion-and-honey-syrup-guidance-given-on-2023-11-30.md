Lavender gin guidance: 1/4 cup dried lavender buds per 1 liter gin for 2-3 days. Grapefruit-infused gin: 1-2 sliced grapefruits per 1 liter gin for 2-3 days. Cucumber gin: 1-2 cups thinly sliced English or hothouse cucumber per 1 liter gin for 2-5 days. Mint gin: 1/4 cup to 1 cup fresh mint leaves per 1 liter gin for 2-5 days. Store infusions in a cool, dark place, taste regularly, then strain through cheesecloth or a coffee filter. Honey syrup: begin with a 1:1 honey-to-water ratio, dissolve over low heat without boiling, cool, and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.
