Lavender syrup: 1 cup granulated sugar, 1 cup water, and 1/4 cup dried lavender buds; dissolve sugar in water, add buds off heat, steep 10-15 minutes until room temperature, strain, and refrigerate up to 2 weeks. White Negroni: 1 1/2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1/2 oz Suze (or Luxardo Bitter), 1/2 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth; stir with ice for 20-30 seconds, strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass, and garnish with an orange or lemon twist. Suggested trying 2:1:1 or 3:1:1 ratios.
