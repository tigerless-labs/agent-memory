Recommended manual mode, ISO 100-400, aperture f/2.8 or lower for shallow depth of field, shutter speeds around 15-30 seconds, Daylight or Auto white balance, tripod, remote release or self-timer, RAW capture, exposure bracketing/HDR, panning, intentional camera movement, high viewpoints, leading lines, symmetry, architectural framing, location scouting, patience, and blue-hour shooting just after sunset.
