Start with a medium-weight 9-foot rod and matching reel, possibly rented or borrowed first. Learn the clinch knot, barrel knot, and blood knot. Beginner-friendly flies: Woolly Bugger, Elk Hair Caddis, and Hare’s Ear Nymph. Practice short casts in a safe open area and gradually increase distance; begin on calm small streams, lakes, or ponds. Suggested resources included Orvis, Fly Fishing 101, Fly Fishing with Tim Flagler, Fishidy, Fly Fishing Simulator, and Fishing Buddy.
