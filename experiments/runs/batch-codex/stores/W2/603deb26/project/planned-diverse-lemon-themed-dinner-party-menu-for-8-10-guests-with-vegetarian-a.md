The menu plan included lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus; vegetarian sushi rolls with avocado, cucumber, grilled portobello mushrooms, and carrot-ginger tempura bits; lemon-ginger dipping sauce; lemon-herb quinoa; croissants; and lemon curd or lemon bars with a gluten-free option. The party would have about 8-10 guests, including a few vegetarians and one gluten-free guest. Garnish ideas included parsley, basil, or mint for naan and quinoa, and thinly sliced lemons or edible violas or pansies for sushi.
