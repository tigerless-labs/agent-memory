The assistant advised that beginners can start with five or ten minutes and increase gradually; consistency matters more than occasional long sessions, and practicing a few times a week can still be worthwhile. Beginner-friendly types suggested were mindfulness meditation, guided meditation (including Headspace or Calm), and loving-kindness meditation.
