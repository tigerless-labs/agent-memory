Recommendations given on 2023-05-21:

- Omega Seamaster: eBay, Chrono24, 1stdibs, The RealReal, WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums, local dealers or collectors, Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions, Instagram, and Facebook Marketplace.
- Sony Walkman Professional: eBay, specialized audio/electronics forums including Gearslutz and Resonance FM, and Facebook Marketplace/local groups.
- The Avengers #1 (1963): ComicConnect, eBay, Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, Reddit's r/comicbooks, and local comic stores; CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) certification was suggested.
- Batman action figure: eBay, Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk, ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room, and local collectible stores.
- Fender Jazzmaster: Reverb, eBay, The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI (The Dallas Pedal and Guitar Ranch Internet Forum), Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange, and Facebook Marketplace/local groups.

General guidance was to research fees, use detailed descriptions and high-quality images, preserve appraisal/authentication documents, research comparable prices, and transact cautiously.
