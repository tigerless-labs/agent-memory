Recommended online stores and suppliers: Amazon, Tractor Supply Co., ChickensForBackyards, Meyer Hatchery, Organic Valley, Blue Seal, Modesto Milling, and Scratch and Peck Feeds. Advice included checking USDA Organic, Non-GMO, or Certified Humane certifications; reading nutrition labels; comparing prices; considering bulk orders; checking local feed stores and co-ops; and transitioning chickens gradually to new feed.
