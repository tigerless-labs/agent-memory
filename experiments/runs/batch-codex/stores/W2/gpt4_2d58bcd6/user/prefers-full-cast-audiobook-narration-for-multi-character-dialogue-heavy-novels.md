The user especially prefers full-cast narrations for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes because they are immersive and make it easier to track who is speaking. They also enjoy strong single narrators with a distinctive tone and style, and consider tone, pace, and inflection crucial to emotional engagement.
