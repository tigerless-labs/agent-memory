Recommendations given: "The Hate U Give" by Angie Thomas, narrated by Bahni Turpin; "The Night Circus" by Erin Morgenstern, narrated by Jim Dale; "The Power" by Naomi Alderman, narrated by Adjoa Andoh; "The Song of Achilles" by Madeline Miller, narrated by Frazer Douglas; and "The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August" by Claire North, narrated by Peter Kenny. Narrators also suggested: Juliet Stevenson, Scott Brick, George Guidall, and Khristine Hvam.
