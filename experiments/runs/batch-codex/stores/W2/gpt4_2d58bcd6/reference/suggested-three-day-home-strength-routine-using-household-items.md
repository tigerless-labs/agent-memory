Suggested routine: Monday upper body—push-ups, water-bottle/can presses or curls, bent-over rows, and chair/bench tricep dips, generally 3 sets of 10–12 reps. Wednesday lower body—squats, lunges, calf raises, and glute bridges, generally 3 sets of 10–12 reps. Friday core—planks for 30–60 seconds, Russian twists with a bottle/can, leg raises, and bicycle crunches, generally 3 sets. Warm up and cool down for 5–10 minutes, prioritize form, and progressively increase weight, reps, or sets.
