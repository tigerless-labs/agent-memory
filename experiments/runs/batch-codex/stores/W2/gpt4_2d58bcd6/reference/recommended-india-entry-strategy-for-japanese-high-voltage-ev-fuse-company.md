The recommended strategy was: conduct India-specific market research; adapt to Indian regulatory and quality standards and obtain certifications; localize production or partner with an Indian company; use competitive value-based pricing; build distribution through established automotive-parts distributors, retailers, and e-commerce; market safety, reliability, and performance through digital channels and local industry events; and build local after-sales service and technician capability. The rationale emphasized India's price sensitivity, diverse demographics, uneven infrastructure development, distinct regulatory environment, and importance of local trust and service.
