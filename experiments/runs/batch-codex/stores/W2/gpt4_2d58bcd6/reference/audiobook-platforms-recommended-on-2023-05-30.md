Recommended **Audible** (over 500,000 titles; stated price $14.95/month for one credit), **Libro.fm** ($14.99/month for one credit; supports a chosen independent bookstore), **Scribd** (stated price $8.99/month for unlimited access), **Hoopla Digital** and **OverDrive** (free borrowing with a library card; Hoopla was also described as supporting a student ID), **Google Play Books**, **Audiobooks.com** ($14.95/month for one credit), **Hibooks** ($12.99/month for one credit), **Kobo Audiobooks**, and **Audiobooksync** (described as offering two free audiobooks per week for 13 weeks in summer). These were 2023-era prices and claims and may have changed.
