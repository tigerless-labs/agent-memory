On 2023-05-30 the assistant suggested enabling Thrive in Higher Logic by logging in, opening the Admin interface, selecting **Features**, finding **Thrive**, and clicking **Activate**, then completing terms, billing, and configuration prompts. It cautioned that Thrive may be limited to certain pricing tiers or carry additional fees and recommended contacting Higher Logic support if it is unavailable or activation help is needed.
