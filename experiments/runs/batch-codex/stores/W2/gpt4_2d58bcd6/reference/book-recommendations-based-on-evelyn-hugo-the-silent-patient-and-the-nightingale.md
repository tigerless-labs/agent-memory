Recommended fiction: **Daisy Jones and The Six** by Taylor Jenkins Reid; **The Royal We** by Heather Cocks and Jessica Morgan; **The Address** by Fiona Davis; **Gone Girl** by Gillian Flynn; **The Girl on the Train** by Paula Hawkins; **The Last Time I Lied** by Riley Sager; **All the Light We Cannot See** by Anthony Doerr; **The Alice Network** by Kate Quinn; and **The Lost Girls of Paris** by Pam Jenoff. Suggested authors: **Colleen Hoover**, **Emily Giffin**, and **Liane Moriarty**.
