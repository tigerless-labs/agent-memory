Daily tarot spread options suggested on 2023-05-30:

1. Single Card: one card for insight into the day ahead.
2. Three-Card: past, present, future.
3. Celtic Cross: ten cards for an in-depth question or issue.
4. Daily Focus: overall energy, what to focus on, and outcome.
5. Mind, Body, Spirit: one card each for mind, body, and spirit.
