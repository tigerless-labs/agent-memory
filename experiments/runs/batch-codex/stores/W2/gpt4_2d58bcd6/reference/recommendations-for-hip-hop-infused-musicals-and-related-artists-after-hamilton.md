Musicals recommended: **In the Heights**, **Freestyle Love Supreme**, **Passing Strange**, **Holler If Ya Hear Me**, **The Bomb-itty of Errors**, and **Stagger Lee**. Artists/groups recommended: **Kendrick Lamar**, **The Roots**, **Lauryn Hill**, **A Tribe Called Quest**, and **J. Period**. Related projects/resources: **The Hip-Hop Shakespeare Company** led by Akala, **The Remix Project**, **Rhymes Over Reason**, **The Realness**, and **The Kennedy Center's Hip-Hop Theater Festival**.
