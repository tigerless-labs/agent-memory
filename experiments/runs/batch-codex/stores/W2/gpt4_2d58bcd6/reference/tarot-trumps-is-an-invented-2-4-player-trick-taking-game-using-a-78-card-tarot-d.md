Assistant-created game from 2023-05-30. **Tarot Trumps** deals the entire 78-card tarot deck to 2-4 players, with leftovers placed in a kitty. Players follow the led suit if possible or play a trump. The Major Arcana are ordered from The Fool through The World; tricks containing a tarot trump score by that trump's rank, described as 4 points for The Fool, 5 for The Magician, continuing up to 25 for The World. The highest total after all cards are played wins.
