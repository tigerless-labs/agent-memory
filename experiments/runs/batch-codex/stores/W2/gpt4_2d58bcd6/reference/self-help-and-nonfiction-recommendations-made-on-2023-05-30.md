Recommended **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle; **Daring Greatly** and **The Gifts of Imperfection** by Brené Brown; **The Happiness Project** by Gretchen Rubin; **Attached** by Amir Levine and Rachel Heller; **Nonviolent Communication** by Marshall B. Rosenberg; **Hold Me Tight** by Sue Johnson; **Mindset** by Carol S. Dweck; **Presence** by Amy Cuddy; **Wild** by Cheryl Strayed; **The Year of Magical Thinking** by Joan Didion; and **Man's Search for Meaning** by Viktor E. Frankl.
