Story canon established on 2023-05-30:

- During a mission to retrieve important data from a hostile ship, Odyl planned an infiltration in which Val allowed herself to be captured and tied up while Odyl posed as a double agent.
- Odyl leaned close, whispered "good girl" in Val's ear, called her "Dearie", then turned and kissed her before leaving. Though planned as part of the ruse, Val did not anticipate how it would make her feel. The incident set the tone for years of flirtation, attraction, and tension. DSES-4 was angry that they went outside protocol, but the retrieved data and Odyl's quick thinking smoothed things over.
- In a later brief encounter, they met in a hallway late at night; Odyl pulled Val into a more passionate kiss, then gave her a coy smile and disappeared into the shadows.
- They later reunited for the Unnamed City mission on the abandoned homeworld of the Xirin, Odyl's people. The mission was sad and powerful for Odyl. They worked closely together for a whole month, relied on one another in danger, shared hopes, fears, and vulnerability, and became genuinely close.
- Their feelings remained unspoken through the mission, which culminated in a fiery, passionate last night before they were separated for a few months.
