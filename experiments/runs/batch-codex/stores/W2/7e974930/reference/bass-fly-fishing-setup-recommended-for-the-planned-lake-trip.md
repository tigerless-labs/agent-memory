Recommended a 9-foot 6-inch to 10-foot, 6-8 weight, medium-to-fast-action fly rod; reel capacity of at least 150-200 yards of 20-pound-test backing with a reliable drag; a 6-8 weight, 90-foot weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking line; a 9-12 foot monofilament or fluorocarbon leader of at least 15-20 pounds; and a 1-2 foot tippet of at least 12-15 pounds.
