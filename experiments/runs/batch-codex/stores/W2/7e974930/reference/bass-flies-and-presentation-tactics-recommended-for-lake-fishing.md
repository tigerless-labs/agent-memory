Recommended Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, baitfish streamers, poppers, sliders, Gurgler, crayfish patterns, Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail Nymph, Woolly Leech, frog patterns, and terrestrials. Presentation guidance included strip, twitch, and drag-free retrieves; pauses; varying sink rate, depth, speed, and casting angle; targeting rocks, weed lines, drop-offs, sunken logs, and other transitions; and adapting to clarity and baitfish activity.
