Recommendations given on 2023-05-20: Pact for organic cotton and fair labor; H&M Conscious for organic cotton, recycled polyester, and Tencel; Everlane for transparent production and sustainable materials; Patagonia for organic cotton, recycled polyester, and hemp; Levi's for recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton. Everlane was described as having men's t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, and shoes, with a minimalistic, timeless aesthetic and production/material transparency.
