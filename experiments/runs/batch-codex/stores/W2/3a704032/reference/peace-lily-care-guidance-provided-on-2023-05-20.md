Guidance given: bright indirect light; water when the top 1-2 inches of soil are dry (often every 7-10 days in spring/summer and 10-14 days in fall/winter); use well-draining soil; mist 1-2 times weekly in average conditions or every 3-4 days in dry conditions, preferably mornings with room-temperature water; balanced water-soluble fertilizer such as 20-20-20 at half strength, while following the nursery product label; ideal temperature 68°F (20°C) to 72°F (22°C), acceptable 65°F (18°C) to 75°F (24°C), and avoid below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C).
