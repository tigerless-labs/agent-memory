The proposed 10th-session lesson is titled “Evaluating Online Information.” Its objective is to teach credibility and reliability evaluation using the CRAAP Test: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose. The plan includes instruction, paired sample evaluation, research of two sources, student presentations, a tips discussion, and assessment through worksheets, a quiz or discussion, and participation monitoring.
