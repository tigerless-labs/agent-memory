The user saw tiny moving dots crawling on their fern's leaves and stems, possibly spider mites or springtails, and decided to try neem oil. They were advised to isolate and inspect the fern, follow the product label, monitor and repeat as needed, and mist only 2-3 times a week using distilled or filtered water in the morning rather than over-humidifying.
