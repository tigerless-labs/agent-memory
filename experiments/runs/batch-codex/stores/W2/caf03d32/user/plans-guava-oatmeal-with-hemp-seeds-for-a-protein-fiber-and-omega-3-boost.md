The user is interested in making morning oatmeal with guava and adding hemp seeds for extra protein, omega-3s, crunch, and nutrition. The suggested starting formula was 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (a 1:2 guava-to-oatmeal ratio), and 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds per serving, starting with 1 tablespoon and adjusting to taste. Suggested additions included chia seeds, banana, walnuts, almonds, coconut, cinnamon, and optional protein powder.
