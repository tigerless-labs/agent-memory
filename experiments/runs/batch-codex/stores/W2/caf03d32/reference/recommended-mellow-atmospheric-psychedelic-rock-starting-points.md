Recommendations given for mellow, atmospheric psychedelic rock similar to Tame Impala included Pond, Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Temples, Mild High Club, King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard's mellow tracks, Foxygen, The Black Angels, The Horrors, Moon Duo, and A Place to Bury Strangers. Starting points included Pond — **Man It Feels Like Space Again** (2015), "30000 MEGATONS"; Unknown Mortal Orchestra — **Multi-Love** (2015), "Can't Keep Checking My Phone"; Temples — **Sun Structures** (2014), "Shelter Song"; Mild High Club — **Timeline** (2015), "Windowpane"; King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard — **I'm in Your Mind Fuzz** (2014), "Am I in Heaven?"; Foxygen — **...And Star Power** (2014), "How Can You Really"; The Black Angels — **Phosphene Dream** (2010), "Black Grease"; The Horrors — **Skying** (2011), "Still Life"; Moon Duo — **Circles** (2012), "Sleepwalker"; A Place to Bury Strangers — **Worship** (2012), "You Are the One".
