The assistant said a **Woolly Bugger** is versatile for river trout and can imitate baitfish, leeches, or a crawdad. Suggested a **9-10 foot leader with a 1-2X tippet**, a sink-tip line or weighted leader, casting upstream and across, letting it swing downstream, varying the retrieve, and setting the hook quickly. A small strike indicator may help in fast water, low light, or for beginners, but may reduce action or spook fish in slow or clear water.
