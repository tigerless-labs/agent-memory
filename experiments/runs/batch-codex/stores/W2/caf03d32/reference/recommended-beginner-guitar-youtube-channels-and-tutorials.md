Beginner guitar recommendations given: YouTube channels **Justin Guitar** (Justin Sandercoe), **Marty Music** (Marty Schwartz), **GuitarLessons365** (Bill), **GuitarJamz** (Martyn), and **Andy Guitar** (Andy Crowley). Specific tutorials: **Guitar for Beginners** by Justin Guitar, **Acoustic Guitar for Beginners** by GuitarLessons365, **5-Minute Guitar Lessons** by Marty Music, **Guitar for Absolute Beginners** by Andy Guitar, and **Beginner Guitar Lessons** by GuitarJamz. Advice included practicing 10-15 minutes daily and using a metronome.
