Selection factors discussed: image quality of at least **10MP**, detection range and trigger speed, battery life, weather resistance, video, time-lapse, and Wi-Fi. Suggested models included **Bushnell Core DS No Glow**, **Bushnell Prime L10**, **Moultrie A-20**, **Moultrie M-880i**, **Reconyx HyperFire 2**, **Reconyx UltraFire**, **Covert Code Black 12**, **Covert MP6**, **Browning Dark Ops HD Max**, **Browning Trail Camera**, **Spypoint Link-EVO**, **Spypoint Solar-Dark**, **Spartan GoCam**, **Covert Code Black 4G**, and **Reconyx Solar**.
