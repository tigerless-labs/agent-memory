The user wants to try making slow-cooker yogurt with a cashew base and sought guidance on soaking, blending, consistency, and straining. Guidance given: soak 1 cup cashews for at least 4 hours or overnight, drain and rinse, blend with about 1/2 cup fresh water for about 2 minutes, adjust water for thickness, and strain. A cheesecloth or nut milk bag was suggested for the smoothest result; a fine-mesh sieve is easier but may leave a slightly grainy texture.
