The proposed architecture combines user opinions, market data, and other relevant inputs in a deep-learning model that outputs probabilities for future cryptocurrency trends. Its prediction is combined with crowd opinions for automated trading, and the model is periodically retrained on the latest data. Suggested technologies included TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Keras; ccxt for exchange interaction; Pandas for data work; Flask-Login and Flask-JWT for authentication; and Celery or scheduled tasks for market fetching, trading, and retraining.
