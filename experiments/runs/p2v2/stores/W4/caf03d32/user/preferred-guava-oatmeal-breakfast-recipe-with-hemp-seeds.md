Guava Oatmeal Recipe (from 2023-05-21):
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- Optional: 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds for protein and omega-3s boost

Also interested in adding nuts (walnuts, almonds, pecans) or seeds for crunch. User planning to incorporate hemp seeds as complete protein source.
