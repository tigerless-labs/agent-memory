Attended lecture series at the Museum of Contemporary Art where Dr. Maria Rodriguez spoke about the role of feminist art in the 1970s. Found the lecture thought-provoking and enlightening.

During the reception after the lecture:
- Met several artists working on feminist art projects and exchanged contact information for potential future collaboration
- Met a curator who expressed interest in learning about my artistic practice and offered valuable advice on how to get work seen by a wider audience
- Built connections with other art enthusiasts

The experience reinforced the importance of community and shared passion in the art world.
