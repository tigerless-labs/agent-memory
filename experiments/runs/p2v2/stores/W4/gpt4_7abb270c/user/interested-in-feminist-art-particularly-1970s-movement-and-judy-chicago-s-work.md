Strong interest in feminist art history, especially the 1970s movement. Particularly fascinated by Judy Chicago's work, especially The Dinner Party installation (1974-1979), which explores female sexuality, creativity, and celebrates women's historical achievements.

Interested in:
- How feminist art challenged patriarchal structures in the art world
- Addressing issues like female sexuality, reproductive rights, erasure of women's contributions
- Historical context of feminist art and its continued relevance today
- Artists like Judy Chicago, Mary Kelly, Faith Ringgold, Miriam Schapiro, Carolee Schneemann, Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles

Resources engaged with:
- Art blogs: Artsy, Hyperallergic, Artnet, The Art Newspaper, Contemporary Art Daily, Art in America, Frieze, Apollo Magazine
- Books: 'The Feminist Art Movement' by Broude & Garrard, 'Women, Art, and Power' by Linda Nochlin, 'Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly'
- Documentaries: '!Women Art Revolution' (2010), 'The Heretics' (2009)
