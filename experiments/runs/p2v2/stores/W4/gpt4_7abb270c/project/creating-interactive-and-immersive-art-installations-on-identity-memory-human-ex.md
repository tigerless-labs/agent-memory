Exploring themes of identity, memory, and the human experience through interactive and immersive installations. Planning to incorporate sound, light, and texture to recreate sensations of specific environments or memories (e.g., walking through a forest). Also interested in exploring collective memory and shared cultural/historical contexts through found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods.

Technical interests: 3D audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback. Exploring tools like Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino.

Goal: Create installations that engage audiences and foster empathy and connection.
