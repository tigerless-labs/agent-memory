Artists creating interactive and immersive installations to study:
- **Carsten Höller** — German artist known for interactive installations with slides, Ferris wheels, playful elements
- **Anicka Yi** — Korean-American artist creating immersive, scent-based installations exploring art, science, technology
- **Rafael Lozano-Hemmer** — Mexican-Canadian artist creating interactive installations using biometrics, robotics exploring themes of surveillance and control
- **Judy Chicago** — Pioneer of feminist art, The Dinner Party installation
- **TeamLab** — Japanese collective creating immersive exhibitions using technology and light

Exhibition spaces and collectives:
- **Meow Wolf** (Santa Fe) — immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, storytelling
- **The Color Factory** (San Francisco) — interactive installations exploring emotional/psychological effects of color
- **TeamLab** — stunning immersive exhibitions using technology and light

Online resources for interactive art:
- Artspace (digital platform for interactive art experiences)
- The Creators Project (showcases interactive art, music, technology)
- Hyperallergic (reviews of interactive exhibitions)
