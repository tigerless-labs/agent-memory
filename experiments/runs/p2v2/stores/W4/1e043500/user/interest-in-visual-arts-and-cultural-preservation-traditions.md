Interested in how visual arts preserve and transmit cultural traditions, particularly in African contexts.

**Visual art forms for cultural preservation:**
- Batik printing — wax-resist dyeing for traditional patterns
- Woodcarving — traditional statues, masks, utensils
- Pottery — coiling and throwing techniques for everyday ceremonial pieces
- Photography — documenting cultural events (weddings, naming ceremonies, celebrations) to educate younger generations
- Painting — depicting traditional clothing, food, dance, and historical events

**Contemporary developments in visual arts (as of 2023):**
- Emergence of contemporary forms: installations, performance art, conceptual art
- Increased digital technology use: digital paintings, animations on contemporary issues (climate change, social justice, human rights)
- Cross-African collaborations exploring Pan-African themes
- Youth involvement through art programs focused on cultural heritage preservation and social change
