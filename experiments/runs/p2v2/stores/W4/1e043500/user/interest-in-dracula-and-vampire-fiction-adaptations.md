Interested in literary adaptations of Dracula and vampire stories across different media.

**Film recommendations explored:**
- 1979 *Dracula* directed by John Badham, starring Frank Langella — noted as staying closest to the original novel's characterization
- *Nosferatu* (1922) directed by F.W. Murnau — grotesque, rat-like portrayal
- *Dracula* (1931) with Bela Lugosi — romanticized, sophisticated portrayal
- *Dracula* (1992) directed by Francis Ford Coppola — romantic angle with Vlad the Impaler backstory
- *Van Helsing* (2004) — demon-like interpretation
- *Dracula* TV series (2013) — complex emotional, guilt-haunted portrayal
- *Dracula* series (2020) — shape-shifter who adapts through history
