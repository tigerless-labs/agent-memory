Interested in understanding conservation strategies and why certain species are vulnerable to overfishing.

**Topics explored:**
- Atlantic Bluefin tuna life cycle, migration patterns, and conservation
- Factors making species vulnerable to overfishing:
  - Slow growth rates and long time to reproductive age
  - High commercial value (creates market pressure)
  - Long-distance migrations across multiple jurisdictions
  - Non-selective fishing methods and bycatch
  - Unregulated/illegal fishing
- Aquaculture as alternative: challenges include complex breeding requirements, high costs, environmental impact (waste, wild-caught feed), genetic diversity loss, consumer preference for wild-caught
