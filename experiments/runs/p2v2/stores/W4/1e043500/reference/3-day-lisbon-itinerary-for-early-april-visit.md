**Trip duration:** 3 full days in Lisbon  
**Timing:** Early April  
**Reason for choice:** Lisbon selected over Porto for more attractions and day-trip options in limited time

**Day 1 – Historic areas & neighborhoods**
- Morning: Alfama neighborhood → Lisbon Cathedral → Castle of São Jorge → tram ride through Alfama streets
- Afternoon: Baixa district → Praça do Comércio → Rossio Square → Chiado streets → A Brasileira café (coffee & pastel de nata)

**Day 2 – Day trip to Sintra + Belém**
- Morning: Day trip to Sintra → Pena Palace → Moorish Castle → gardens and hill views
- Afternoon: Return to Lisbon → Belém neighborhood → Jerónimos Monastery → Monument to the Discoveries → Pastéis de Belém (traditional pastry)

**Day 3 – Modern Lisbon & viewpoints**
- Morning: Walk along Tagus River → MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology) → river views
- Afternoon: Tram to Bairro Alto → Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara viewpoint → explore Bairro Alto streets → dinner in traditional restaurant

**Nearby attractions not in itinerary (optional additions):**
- National Museum of Ancient Art
- Calouste Gulbenkian Museum  
- LX Factory (trendy art/culture district)
- Day trips: Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica beaches
