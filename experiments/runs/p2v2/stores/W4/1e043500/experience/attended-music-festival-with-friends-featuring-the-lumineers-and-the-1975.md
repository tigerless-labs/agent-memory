Went to a music festival with friends last month (approximately April 2023) and saw The Lumineers and The 1975 perform. Found it to be an amazing experience. Both bands have unique sounds - The Lumineers are known for their folk-infused rock with foot-stomping energy, and The 1975 for their blend of indie rock, pop, and electronica.
