Incorporating strength training into fitness routine to improve performance in tennis and running. Key exercises include squats, lunges, deadlifts, calf raises (legs) and planks, Russian twists, leg raises, bicycle crunches (core). Plans to use lighter weights with progressive overload, 2-3 times per week with 48-72 hours recovery between sessions.
