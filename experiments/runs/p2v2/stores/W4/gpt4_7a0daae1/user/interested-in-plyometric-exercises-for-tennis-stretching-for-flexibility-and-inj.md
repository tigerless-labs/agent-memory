Adding plyometric exercises to improve power, speed, and agility for tennis. Exercises include box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds, tuck jumps, and medicine ball throws (2-3 times per week). Also incorporating stretching routines before and after tennis lessons to improve flexibility and reduce injury risk.
