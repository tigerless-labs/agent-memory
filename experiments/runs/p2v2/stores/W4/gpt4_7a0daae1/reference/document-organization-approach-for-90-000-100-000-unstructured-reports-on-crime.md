Approach for organizing large volume (90,000-100,000) of unstructured documents (1-5 page reports) on international crime, terrorism, and politics:

1. **Text Cleaning** - Remove special characters, punctuation, stop words; normalize to lowercase
2. **Document Parsing** - Extract title, author, date, body text
3. **Entity Extraction** - Identify names, organizations, locations using NER, POS tagging, dependency parsing
4. **Topic Modeling** - Identify main topics/themes to organize by common themes and patterns
5. **Sentiment Analysis** - Understand tone/emotions to track opinion changes
6. **Document Classification** - Classify by content category (crime, terrorism, politics) for quick search/filter

The specific approach depends on requirements and data nature.
