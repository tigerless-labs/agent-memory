Hokkaido must-try dishes:\n- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton)\n- Kaisendon (sashimi over rice)\n- Jingisukan (Mongolian-style barbecue)\n- Uni and ikura don (sea urchin and salmon roe)\n- Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)\n- Local dairy: ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors)\n\nRecommended locations:\n- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) - fresh seafood\n- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho - local ramen\n- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) - local cuisine\n- Daruma (Sapporo) - Jingisukan\n- Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) - grilled lamb\n- Yurigakuro (Sapporo) - Ishikari Nabe
