**Honey Syrup for Cocktails**\n\nRatio: 1 part honey to 1 part water (1:1)\n\nMethod:\n1. Combine honey and water in saucepan\n2. Heat over low heat, whisking until honey dissolves\n3. Do not boil or burn the honey\n4. Let cool before using\n5. Store in fridge for up to 2 weeks\n\nVariations:\n- Infuse with citrus peel, herbs, or spices during dissolving\n- Try different honey types (clover, manuka, acacia) for distinct flavor profiles\n- Honey-ginger syrup: add sliced ginger during dissolving\n\nUses: Bee's Knees, and other gin-based cocktails
