**Spirit Infusion Methods**\n\n**Cucumber-Infused Gin**\n- Use thin-skinned cucumbers (English or hothouse, avoid waxed)\n- Slice thinly to increase surface area\n- Ratio: 1-2 cups sliced cucumber per 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-5 days in cool dark place\n- Avoid over-infusion (can become bitter)\n- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter\n\n**Mint-Infused Gin**\n- Use peppermint or spearmint (or other varieties: chocolate mint, apple mint)\n- Ratio: 1/4 to 1 cup fresh mint leaves per 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-5 days in cool dark place\n- Monitor regularly - mint is potent\n- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter\n\n**Combining Infusions**\n- Infuse cucumber and mint separately to control intensity\n- Combine infusions in 50/50 ratio initially, adjust to taste\n- Allow combined infusion to age a few days for flavors to meld\n\n**Other Options Tested**\n- Lavender Gin: 1/4 cup dried lavender to 1 liter gin, infuse 2-3 days\n- Lavender Vodka: 1/4 cup dried lavender to 1 liter vodka, infuse 1-2 weeks\n- Grapefruit Gin: 1-2 grapefruits sliced, infuse 2-3 days\n- Grapefruit Vodka: 1-2 grapefruits sliced, infuse 1-2 weeks\n\n**General Tips**\n- Store in cool, dark place\n- Strain and filter before using\n- Experiment with different flavor combinations\n- Follow proper sanitation and storage to avoid contamination
