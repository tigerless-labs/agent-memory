**Gin Cocktails of Interest**\n\n**Already expressed interest in trying:**\n\n*Lavender Gin Fizz*\n- 1.5 oz gin\n- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice\n- 0.5 oz lavender syrup\n- 0.5 oz simple syrup\n- Top with soda water\n- Garnish: lavender sprig, lime wheel\n\n*Grapefruit Basil Martini*\n- 2 oz gin\n- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice\n- 0.5 oz lime juice\n- 0.5 oz basil syrup\n- Garnish: grapefruit twist, basil leaf\n\n*Bee's Knees* (interested due to gin-honey combination)\n- Gin, honey, lemon juice\n- Prohibition-era classic\n\n*Pimm's Cup* (planning to experiment with Pimm's No. 1)\n- Pimm's No. 1 with lemonade\n- Garnish: cucumber, mint, fruit\n\n**Other classic gin cocktails recommended**\n- Gin Rickey: gin, lime juice, soda water\n- Gin-Gin Mule: gin, lime juice, ginger beer (in copper mug)\n- Aviation: gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, lemon juice\n- Monkey Gland: gin, orange juice, grenadine, absinthe\n- Last Word: gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, lime juice\n- Gin Martini: 2:1 or 3:1 gin to dry vermouth, olive or lemon twist\n- Corpse Reviver No. 2: gin, Lillet Blanc, Cynar, Cointreau, absinthe\n- French 75: gin, lemon juice, sugar, champagne
