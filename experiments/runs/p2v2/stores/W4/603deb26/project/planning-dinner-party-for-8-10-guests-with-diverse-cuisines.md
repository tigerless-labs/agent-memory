Organizing a dinner party for 8-10 guests featuring:\n- Lemon-herb naan\n- Vegetarian and non-vegetarian sushi rolls (portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura, cucumber, avocado)\n- Croissants\n- Lemon-herb quinoa\n- Lemon curd or lemon bars\n\nNeeds to accommodate vegetarians and one gluten-free guest. Timing and presentation are important considerations.
