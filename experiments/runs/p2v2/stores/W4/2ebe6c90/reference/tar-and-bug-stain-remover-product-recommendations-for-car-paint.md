**Context:** Recommendations for removing tar and bug splatters from vehicle paint.

**Recommended Products:**

1. **Meguiar's Tar and Bug Remover**
   - Popular and well-reviewed
   - Specifically designed for tar, bug splatters, overspray
   - Safe for most paint finishes
   - Easy to apply and remove

2. **Chemical Guys Tar and Bug Remover**
   - Favorite among detailing enthusiasts
   - Gentle yet effective formula
   - Breaks down contaminants without paint damage
   - Safe for wheels, trim, and exterior surfaces

3. **3M Tar and Bug Remover**
   - More aggressive formula
   - Removes tough tar, bug splatters, overspray, and stickers
   - Safe for most finishes but requires testing first
   - Use with caution

4. **Gtechniq Tar and Bug Remover**
   - Premium product
   - Specifically designed for tar and bug contaminants
   - Gentle yet effective
   - Safe for most paint finishes

**Application Tips:** Read instructions carefully, test on small area first, apply to microfiber cloth (not directly to paint), use gentle rubbing motion, follow with wash and wax
