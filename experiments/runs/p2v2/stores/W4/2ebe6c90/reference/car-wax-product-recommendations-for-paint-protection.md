**Context:** Recommended products for car wax application after detailing spray use.

**Product Recommendations:**

1. **Meguiar's Gold Class Carnauba Plus Premium Wax**
   - Higher price point
   - Deep, glossy finish
   - High-quality, recommended for those willing to invest

2. **Chemical Guys Car Wax**
   - Affordable option
   - Easy to apply and remove
   - High-gloss finish, safe for most paint finishes

3. **Turtle Wax Carnauba Premium Car Wax**
   - Well-known brand
   - Affordable
   - High-quality wax with deep glossy finish

4. **P21S Concours Carnauba Wax**
   - Premium option for enthusiasts
   - More expensive
   - Showroom-quality finish

**Application Tips:** Apply in thin layers, allow to haze before buffing, avoid direct sunlight, maintain regular waxing schedule every 2-3 months
