Decision point (Dec 2022): Choosing between Costa Rica and Iceland for summer vacation.

**Costa Rica interests:**
- Outdoor activities: hiking, wildlife watching, zip lining, whitewater rafting
- Eco-lodges and sustainable tourism (high priority)
- Tortuguero National Park - planning to stay at eco-lodge there for several nights
- Wildlife: monkeys, sloths, birds, tropical biodiversity
- Access to nature: rainforests, waterfalls, hot springs, beaches
- Road conditions: expects rough/unpaved roads, muddy conditions in rainy season

**Iceland interests:**
- 24-hour daylight in August
- Potential Northern Lights viewing
- Unique landscape and geology

**Key preference:** Sustainability and eco-tourism are important - interested in certified eco-lodges and responsible tour operators (CST certification, TIES association). Wants to support conservation and local communities.
