Recommended musicals with irreverent humor and catchy music:\n- Avenue Q (puppets in NYC, adult themes, similar to Book of Mormon)\n- The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee (quirky comedy)\n- Something Rotten! (Shakespeare comedy, original Broadway cast recording available)\n- Hamilton (hip-hop/jazz/blues/R&B blend)\n- The Producers (Mel Brooks classic)\n- Urinetown (satirical)\n- Be More Chill (sci-fi comedy)\n\nMore dramatic/beautiful scores:\n- Dear Evan Hansen (social media and mental health)\n- The Band's Visit (Egyptian police band)\n- Hadestown (folk-opera retelling of Orpheus myth, 8 Tony Awards 2019, original Broadway cast features Eva Noblezada, Reeve Carney, Patrick Page, Amber Gray)
