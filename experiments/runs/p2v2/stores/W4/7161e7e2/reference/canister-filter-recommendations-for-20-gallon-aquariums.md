Recommended canister filters for 20-gallon tanks:\n- Eheim Classic 2211 (reliable, mechanical and biological filtration)\n- Fluval 206 (designed for 20-40 gallons, compact, quiet)\n- Hydor Professional 150 (quiet, modular design, easy maintenance, ~$150-200)\n- API SuperClean (easy setup and maintenance)\n\nRecommended frozen brine shrimp brands:\n- San Francisco Bay Brand (Great Salt Lake, nutrient-rich)\n- Hikari (popular, spirulina and krill options)\n- Argent (sustainable, omega-3 fatty acids)\n- Omega One (omega-3 rich)\n\nOther fish food recommendations:\n- Tetra pellet food (good staple)\n- Frozen bloodworms (protein and iron)\n- Frozen daphnia (omega-3 rich)\n- Frozen mysis shrimp\n- Spirulina flakes/pellets\n- Algae-based foods (nori sheets, algae flakes)
