Light novel series featuring protagonist Cid Kagenou who dreams of being a mastermind controlling events from the shadows. Cid creates 'Shadow Garden', a secret organization with childhood friends to solve mysteries and carry out missions while hiding identities. Series blends action, adventure, and comedy with strong metafictional elements—references and parodies anime/manga tropes, includes fourth-wall breaks. Adapted into manga; anime adaptation in production as of 2023.
