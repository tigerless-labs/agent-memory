## Books Recommended\n1. **Dear Martin** by Nic Stone - Teenager writes letters to MLK after wrongful accusation\n2. **The Poet X** by Elizabeth Acevedo - Novel-in-verse about Afro-Latina teen, slam poetry, identity\n3. **Long Way Down** by Jason Reynolds - Teen dealing with brother's murder, cycle of violence\n4. **All American Boys** by Jason Reynolds & Brendan Kiely - Police brutality from two perspectives\n5. **The Sun is Also a Star** by Nicola Yoon - Jamaican-American and Korean-American teens, identity themes\n6. **Children of Blood and Bone** by Tomi Adeyemi - Fantasy with African mythology\n7. **An Ember in the Ashes** by Sabaa Tahir - Fantasy with Roman-inspired world-building\n8. **Who Fears Death** or **Binti** by Nnedi Okorafor - African culture and mythology in SF/fantasy\n\n## Recommended Authors\n- Jason Reynolds\n- Nicola Yoon  \n- Renee Watson (Piecing Me Together, Betty Before X)\n- Ibi Zoboi (American Street, Pride)\n- Dhonielle Clayton (The Belles, Shattered Midnight)\n- Tomi Adeyemi\n- Sabaa Tahir\n- Nnedi Okorafor\n\nThese authors are known for blending genres, complex characters, and social justice themes.
