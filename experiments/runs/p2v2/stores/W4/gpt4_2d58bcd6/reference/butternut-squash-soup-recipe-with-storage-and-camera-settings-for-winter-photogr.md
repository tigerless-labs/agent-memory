## Butternut Squash Soup Recipe\nSimple recipe for 4-6 servings:\n- Roast butternut squash (2-3 lbs) at 400°F for 45-50 minutes\n- Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil\n- Add spices (cumin, smoked paprika), broth, roasted squash\n- Blend until smooth\n- Optional: add heavy cream or coconut cream\n- Storage: refrigerate 3-5 days or freeze 3-6 months\n\n## Winter Photography Camera Settings\n- Mode: Manual (M)\n- ISO: 100-400\n- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (depth of field)\n- Shutter Speed: 1/125s-1/250s\n- White Balance: Cloudy or Shade\n- Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop for snow detail\n- Use polarizing filter to reduce glare\n- Shoot RAW format\n- Golden hour provides best lighting\n\n## Photography Tips for Snowshoeing\n- Bring extra batteries (cold drains them)\n- Use graduated ND filter for sky/land balance\n- Bracket shots for HDR merging\n- Low angle shooting captures texture\n- Look for animal tracks in composition
