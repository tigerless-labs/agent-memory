- Morning yoga practice, focusing on flexibility and ankle/ankle-specific poses
- Gym membership and regular workouts
- Lunch break walks at work
- Weekend cycling (bikes to grocery store instead of driving)
- Does home strength training with bodyweight exercises and household items as weights
- Interested in injury prevention and recovery strategies
