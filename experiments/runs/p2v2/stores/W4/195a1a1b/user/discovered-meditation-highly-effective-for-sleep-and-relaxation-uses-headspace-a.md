Recently started using meditation apps for sleep and relaxation before bed.

Positive experience with Headspace 'Sleep' meditation (10 minutes):
- Found it very relaxing and calming
- Helped quiet the mind and made falling asleep easier
- Particularly enjoyed the imagery and visualization components
- Gentle guidance by Andy Puddicombe

Currently exploring Insight Timer's 'Body Scan' meditation:
- Continues the meditation practice for sleep
- Interested in body-awareness and progressive relaxation techniques

Meditation preferences:
- Enjoys imagery and visualization components
- Uses headphones to block external noise
- Practices meditation before bedtime (part of wind-down routine starting ~8:30-9pm)
- Finds it helps with stress relief and mental quieting

Recommended apps:
- Headspace (subscription, has free trial)
- Insight Timer (free app with large library)
- Calm (popular for sleep stories)

Plans to make meditation a regular part of bedtime routine.
