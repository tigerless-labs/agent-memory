**Current factors in material selection:**\n- Weight (improves fuel efficiency, acceleration, handling)\n- Strength (crash safety, durability)\n- Cost and availability\n- Durability\n- Aesthetics\n\n**Common materials:**\n- Steel/titanium: strong but heavy\n- Aluminum/carbon fiber: lightweight, strong, expensive\n- Reinforced plastics: lightweight\n\n**Hybrid approach:** mix materials strategically (steel for structural areas, aluminum for panels)\n\n**Emerging materials for future vehicles:**\n- Advanced composites\n- Nanocellulose\n- Graphene\n- 3D printing enables complex designs with diverse materials\n\n**Adoption timeline:** Varies; high-end sports cars and EVs already using advanced materials. Mainstream adoption depends on testing, regulatory approval, cost-effectiveness, consumer demand, and manufacturing capabilities.
