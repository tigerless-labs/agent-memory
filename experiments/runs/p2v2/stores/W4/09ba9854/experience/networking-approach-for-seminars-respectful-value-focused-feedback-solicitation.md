Attending morning entrepreneurship seminars at co-working space near apartment for networking and project visibility. Key networking principles learned: be respectful of time, clearly define what you're asking for, show genuine interest in others' work, be open to feedback, offer value in return, be prepared to answer questions, respect boundaries (don't be pushy). Approach: create concise elevator pitch, engage in active listening, follow up with contacts via email/LinkedIn. Goal: get feedback on project and find potential collaborators.
