Basic recipe (preheat oven to 400°F, bake 20-25 minutes):

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (can substitute with whole wheat for healthier option)
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F and line muffin pan
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt
3. In separate bowl, whisk butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla until smooth
4. Add wet ingredients to dry, mix until just combined (don't overmix)
5. Gently fold in blueberries
6. Divide batter among muffin cups
7. Bake 20-25 minutes until toothpick comes out clean
8. Cool in pan 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack

**Notes on whole wheat flour substitution:**
- Results in denser and darker muffins
- May need to adjust baking time
- Consider adding honey or maple syrup for additional sweetness since whole wheat has deeper flavor
