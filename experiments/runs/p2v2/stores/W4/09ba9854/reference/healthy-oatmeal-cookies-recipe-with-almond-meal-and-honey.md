Healthy cookie recipe (preheat oven to 350°F, bake 10-12 minutes):

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins (can substitute with dark chocolate chips)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line baking sheet with parchment
2. In large bowl, whisk together oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon
3. In separate bowl, whisk together melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla
4. Add wet ingredients to dry and mix until combined
5. Gently fold in raisins
6. Drop spoonfuls on baking sheet about 2 inches apart
7. Bake 10-12 minutes until golden brown
8. Cool on sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack

**Notes:**
- Can substitute raisins with dark chocolate chips for different flavor
- Dark chocolate options are healthier than regular chocolate chips
- These are healthier alternatives to satisfy sweet tooth cravings
