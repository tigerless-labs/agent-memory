**Pittsburgh Weekend Getaway Recommendations**

**Must-See Attractions:**
- Strip District: lively neighborhood known for street food, ethnic markets, unique shops; famous for Primanti Bros. sandwiches
- Andy Warhol Museum: largest museum in US dedicated to a single artist
- Mount Washington: take Duquesne Incline or Monongahela Incline for city skyline and three rivers views
- Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens

**Other Activities:**
- Brewery tours (Penn Brewery, East End Brewing Company, Grist House Brewing)
- North Shore exploration: PNC Park, Andy Warhol Bridge
- Carnegie Science Center
- Three Rivers Heritage Trail (24-mile trail system)

**Accommodations:**
- Oakland and Shadyside neighborhoods recommended
- Options: Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh East Liberty, The Inn on the Mexican War Streets, Airbnb
- Budget: $100-150 per night for Airbnb
- Rates range $120-200 per night for hotels

**Transportation:**
- Port Authority: buses, light rail, inclined planes
- Free Transit Zone in downtown
- 1-Day Pass: $5.50, 3-Day Pass available
- Bike-share: Healthy Ride
- Uber/Lyft widely available

**Flights from NYC:** $150-250 round-trip, ~1.5 hours flight time
