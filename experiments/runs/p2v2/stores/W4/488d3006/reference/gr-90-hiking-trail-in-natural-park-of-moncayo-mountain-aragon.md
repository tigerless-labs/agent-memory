**GR-90 Hiking Trail - Natural Park of Moncayo, Aragón, Spain**

- Circular hiking trail: ~36 kilometers
- Duration: 2-3 days to complete
- Passes through small towns and villages with cultural/historical landmarks
- Highlights: summit of Moncayo mountain, Fuenfría waterfall
- Scenery: meadows, forests, rock formations
- Best time to visit: spring (wildflowers, mild climate) or autumn (colorful leaves, fewer crowds)
- Avoid: summer due to high temperatures; winter due to snowfall
