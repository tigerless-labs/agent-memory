**Bot Trading Course Outline (10 Weeks)**

Week 1: Introduction to Bot Trading
- Basics, benefits, risks, types of bots and strategies

Week 2: Setting up Bot Trading System
- Create trading account, connect to bot
- Choose platform, set up parameters (assets, risk level, strategies)

Week 3: Backtesting and Optimization
- Importance of backtesting, using historical data
- Optimization techniques

Week 4: Live Trading with Bot
- Differences between backtesting and live trading
- Setup, monitoring, management and adjustments

Week 5: Advanced Bot Trading Strategies
- Scalping, arbitrage, market making
- Risks and rewards

Week 6: Risk Management
- Managing risks, stop-loss orders, position sizing
- Diversification

Week 7: Advanced Technical Analysis
- Indicators and chart patterns
- Improving bot performance

Week 8: Advanced Trading Psychology
- Psychological aspects, overcoming fear and greed
- Disciplined trading mindset

Week 9: Advanced Trading Strategies
- Hedging and mean reversion
- Combining strategies

Week 10: Advanced Bot Platforms and Tools
- Trading platforms, API integrations
- Latest developments and trends
