**2021 Naturlich by Benoit Landron**\n\n- **Grape blend:** 80% Semillon (organic, from Bergerac), 20% other\n- **Reason for blend:** 2021 frost damage destroyed most of the Folle Blanche crop; Semillon sourced from organic grower in Bergerac as substitute\n- **Wine type:** Pet-nat (pétillant naturel)\n- **Tasting notes:** Fleshy stone-fruit character from the Semillon, citrus notes, minerality from Folle Blanche\n- **Style:** Described as a spring wine, fun variation on the original recipe\n- **Currently served:** By the glass at 161 natural wine bar in Sydenham
