Evaluating whether to write a book myself rather than using ghost-writers or AI generation.

Key reasons to self-write noted:
- Personal fulfillment and empowerment
- Complete ownership and control over content, tone, message
- Authenticity and credibility with readers
- Improvement of writing skills and voice
- Emotional connection with audience

Ghost-writing/AI risks: lack of authenticity, reduced personal fulfillment, weaker audience connection.
