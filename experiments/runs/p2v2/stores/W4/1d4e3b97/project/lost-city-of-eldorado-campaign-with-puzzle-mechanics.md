Working on 'Lost City of Eldorado' campaign involving multiple unique puzzle types:

- **Mirror Puzzle**: Uses symbols on mirror and player position/reflection
- **Elemental Puzzle**: Levers/buttons controlling earth, fire, air, water elements
- **Musical Puzzle**: Sheet music with instrument sequences to unlock doors
- **Weight Puzzle**: Scales and weights to solve, reveals hidden passages
- **Memory Puzzle**: Memorize object locations before darkness, retrieve specific items

Puzzles designed for varied skill levels, each with unique solution paths.
