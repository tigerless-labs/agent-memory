Science Fiction: The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin (Luke Daniels); Ready Player One by Ernest Cline (Wil Wheaton); The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (Rachel Dulude). Fantasy: The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North (Peter Kenny); The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (Simon Vance); The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison (Kyle McCarley); The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard (Anne Flosnik); The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker (George Guidall); The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty (Soneela Nankani); The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang (Emily Woo Zeller); The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (Emily Woo Zeller). Non-fiction: The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey; Daring Greatly by Brené Brown; Mindset by Carol S. Dweck; Deep Work by Cal Newport; Essentialism by Greg McKeown; Atomic Habits by James Clear; Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari; The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle; Shoe Dog by Phil Knight.
