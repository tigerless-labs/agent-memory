System Purpose: Online document verification and authentication for government documents. Key Features: (1) Online document submission. (2) Blockchain lookup - if document not on blockchain, request sent to authorized government agency for approval. (3) Document verification by concerned agency (BIR for income tax returns; Office of Permits and Licenses for business permits). (4) Automatic blockchain storage upon approval. Technology Stack: OCR for text extraction, NLP for feature identification, machine learning algorithms for authenticity comparison with reference documents. Fraud Detection: Machine learning to identify patterns and anomalies indicating fraud/errors; alerts government agency for investigation. Storage: Verified documents stored on blockchain for tamper-proof future access. Digital Security: Digital signatures and encryption prevent tampering. Government Agencies: BIR (Bureau of Internal Revenue), Office of Permits and Licenses.
