## Graphic novels recommended (similar to Saga)

- **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda — fantasy epic with stunning art
- **The Wicked + The Divine** by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie — mythology and pop culture intersection
- **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston — superhero deconstruction
- **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá — dysfunctional superhero family
- **Descender** by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen — sci-fi epic about a robot boy
- **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra — biblical/mythological noir
- **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra — last male human on Earth, by same author as Saga
