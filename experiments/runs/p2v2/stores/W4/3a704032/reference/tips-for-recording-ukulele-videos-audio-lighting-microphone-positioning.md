Audio recording tips: use dedicated condenser microphone (Blue Yeti, Rode NT-USB), position 6-12 inches from instrument slightly off-center angled at sound hole, use pop filter, record in quiet room with minimal echo, use DAW like Audacity or GarageBand. Video recording tips: use good lighting (natural or softbox), good camera with stabilization, mount on tripod, frame shot carefully. External microphone for video capture. Edit with iMovie, Adobe Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve. Add captions and tags for discoverability on YouTube.
