Recommended platforms for discovering emerging artists and contemporary art: Artsy, Saatchi Art, Behance, Instagram (using hashtags like #emergingartist #artoftheday #contemporaryart #artistsofinstagram), Juxtapoz, Hyperallergic, New American Paintings, ArtNet, The Artist's Institute, Platform, Artspace, 1stdibs, Uprise Art, Minted, Collecteurs. These platforms offer online exhibitions, artist showcases, virtual events, art fairs, and curated collections.
