Recommended fertilizer brands: Miracle-Gro (Indoor Plant Food 20-20-20, Cactus/Palm/Citrus 10-20-10, Orchid Food 30-10-10), Espoma (Indoor Plant Fertilizer 2-2-2, Cactus 5-2-6, Orchid 15-30-15), Schultz (All Purpose Plant Food 20-20-20, Cactus/Succulent 10-20-10, Orchid Food 20-20-20). Recommended humidifiers: Levoit LV600HH (6-liter tank, cool/warm mist), Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate (1-liter tank), Deneve Minimal Aroma (2.5-liter, ultrasonic), Crane Drop (1-liter, quiet operation). Recommended potting mixes: Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix, Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix, E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix, Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix.
