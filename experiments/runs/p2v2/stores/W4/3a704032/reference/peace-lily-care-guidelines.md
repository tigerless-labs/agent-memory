**Lighting:** Prefer bright, indirect light; east or west-facing windows ideal; avoid direct sunlight (causes scorch) and low light (prevents blooming)

**Watering:** Water when top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry to touch. Frequency: every 7-10 days during spring/summer growth; every 10-14 days in fall/winter. Overwatering is the most common cause of death. Ensure pot drains well. Avoid waterlogged soil.

**Humidity:** Prefer humid environment (40-50% relative humidity). Options: place pot on tray with water and pebbles, use humidifier, or mist. Misting frequency: 1-2 times per week (less frequent than ferns which need above 50%). Mist in morning to allow leaves to dry. Avoid misting soil or crown.

**Temperature:** Ideal range 68-72°F (20-22°C). Acceptable range 65-75°F (18-24°C). Avoid below 55°F or above 85°F. Keep away from heating/cooling vents, fireplaces, and drafty windows.

**Fertilization:** Balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20) during growing season (spring/summer) at half recommended strength. Frequency: every 1-2 weeks during growing season; reduce to once monthly in fall/winter dormancy.

**Soil:** Use well-draining potting mix; tropical or orchid-specific mixes work well.

**Pruning:** Remove dead or dying leaves and flowers to prevent disease spread. Use clean, sharp scissors/pruning shears.

**Repotting:** Not frequently needed; only when plant outgrows pot or shows nutrient deficiency signs. Best done in spring/summer.

**Pests:** Monitor for spider mites, mealybugs, scale insects. Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if needed.

**Note:** Normal to lose some leaves when adjusting to new environment. Monitor leaf condition (drooping/wrinkled = too dry air).
