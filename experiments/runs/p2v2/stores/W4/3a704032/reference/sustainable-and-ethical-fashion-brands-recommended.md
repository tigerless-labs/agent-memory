**Affordable sustainable brands:**
- **Patagonia** — outdoor clothing; mid-range/affordable options; uses organic cotton, recycled polyester, hemp
- **Everlane** — transparent sustainable brand; minimalist aesthetic; timeless designs made to last; detailed supply chain information; good men's selection (t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, shoes)
- **Pact** — organic cotton clothing for men, women, and children; fair labor practices
- **H&M Conscious** — H&M's sustainable collection; organic cotton, recycled polyester, tencel
- **Stella McCartney** — luxury sustainable fashion brand
- **Eileen Fisher** — sustainable fashion brand
- **Levi's** — denim brand; recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton

All these brands are committed to transparency and ethical production practices.
