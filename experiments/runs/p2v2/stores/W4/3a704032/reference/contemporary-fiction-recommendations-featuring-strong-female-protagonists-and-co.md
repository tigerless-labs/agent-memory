## Contemporary fiction novels recommended

- **The Devil Wears Prada** by Lauren Weisberger — demanding boss/assistant relationship
- **The Mothers** by Brit Bennett — coming-of-age in tight-knit Southern California community
- **The Girls** by Emma Cline — teenage girls caught in Manson-esque cult, 1960s
- **The Interestings** by Meg Wolitzer — friends from summer arts camp, decades-spanning relationships
- **Conversations with Friends** by Sally Rooney — complex relationships between young women and a couple
- **The Female Persuasion** by Meg Wolitzer — young woman with famous feminist mentor
- **The Last Romantics** by Tara Conklin — four siblings over years
- **The Age of Light** by Whitney Scharer — based on true story of Lee Miller, photographer in 1920s-30s Paris, Parisian avant-garde

All feature strong female protagonists and complex relationships between women.
