User is planning to set up a music room in their garage and wants to soundproof it to avoid disturbing others and improve sound quality. Key concerns: soundproofing (preventing sound escape/entry) vs sound absorption (reducing echo/reverberation), thin walls, ceilings, gaps around doors/windows. Materials needed: mass-loaded vinyl, acoustic panels, resilient channels, floating floor.
