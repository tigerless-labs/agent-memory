Dad created a BBQ sauce recipe during family summer camping trip in April 2023. Added smoked paprika to deepen the flavor profile. The sauce was used with grilled pork sausages and was well-received by the entire family. Smoked paprika is an effective ingredient for adding smoky depth to BBQ sauce without overpowering other flavors.
