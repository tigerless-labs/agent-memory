**Westmoreland State Park**\n- Location: 145 Cliff Road, Montross, VA\n- Offerings: Kayak, canoe, paddleboard rentals on Potomac River\n- Amenities: Provides life jackets with rentals\n\n**Northern Neck Kayaking & Outdoor Rentals**\n- Location: 126 Jessie duPont Memorial Highway, Burgess, VA\n- Offerings: Kayak, canoe rentals and tours\n- Waters: Great Wicomico River, Chesapeake Bay\n- Amenities: Provides life jackets with rentals\n\n**Wildlife to expect:** Bald eagles (user's favorite), ospreys, blue herons, egrets, waterfowl, river otters, muskrats, beavers, turtles, striped bass, largemouth bass, bluegill, catfish, deer, foxes, raccoons along shore\n\n**Best wildlife viewing times:** Early morning and late afternoon/early evening (when animals most active); also better paddling conditions with less wind and boat traffic
