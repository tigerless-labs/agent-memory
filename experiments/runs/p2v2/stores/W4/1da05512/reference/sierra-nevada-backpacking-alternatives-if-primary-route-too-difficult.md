**Rae Lakes Loop (Kings Canyon):**\n- Distance: ~45 miles | Elevation: 6,000 ft | Difficulty: Moderate\n- Start: Road's End trailhead | Permits: Kings Canyon NP\n- Features: Rae Lakes, Fin Dome, Kings River Canyon\n- Route: Road's End → Rae Lakes → Glen Pass → Paradise Valley → Road's End\n\n**Ansel Adams Wilderness - Thousand Island Lake:**\n- Distance: ~30 miles | Elevation: 4,000 ft | Difficulty: Moderate  \n- Start: Agnew Meadows | Permits: Ansel Adams Wilderness\n- Features: Minarets, Thousand Island Lake, Ritter Range\n\n**Emigrant Wilderness - Emigrant Lake:**\n- Distance: ~35 miles | Elevation: 3,500 ft | Difficulty: Moderate\n- Start: Crabtree trailhead | Permits: Emigrant Wilderness\n- Character: Lesser-visited, secluded experience\n\n**High Sierra Camp Loop (challenging baseline):**\n- Distance: 40 miles | Elevation: 7,000 ft | Difficulty: Moderate-challenging\n- Terrain: Gentle valleys → steep ascents to 11,000 ft → high traverses → descents\n- Trail surface: Rocky, uneven, some stream crossings\n- Season factors: Snow/ice possible at passes, exposed to wind/storms\n- Physical: 8-10 hour days, 40-50 lb packs, altitude adjustment needed
