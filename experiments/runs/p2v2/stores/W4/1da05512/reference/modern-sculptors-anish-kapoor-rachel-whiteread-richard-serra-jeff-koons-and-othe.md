Recommended modern sculptors to explore:
- **Anish Kapoor**: Known for large-scale, abstract, interactive installations with reflective surfaces and voids
- **Rachel Whiteread**: British sculptor creating intricate, large-scale installations exploring space and memory
- **Richard Serra**: American sculptor famous for large-scale, site-specific steel installations
- **Jeff Koons**: Known for large-scale, hyper-realistic sculptures exploring popular culture and consumerism
- **Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen**: Swedish-American duo creating large-scale, playful, humorous installations
- **Ursula von Rydingsvard**: Polish-American sculptor working with organic forms and various materials
- **Barbara Hepworth**: British modernist sculptor known for abstract, biomorphic forms
- **Tony Cragg**: British sculptor creating abstract, colorful, surreal works
- **Eva Rothschild**: Irish-British sculptor exploring space and materiality
- **Phyllida Barlow**: British sculptor known for large-scale, colorful, playful installations
