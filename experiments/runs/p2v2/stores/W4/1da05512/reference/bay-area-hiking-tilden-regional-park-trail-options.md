**Nimitz Way Trail:**\n- Distance: 4.5 miles out-and-back (9 miles round-trip)\n- Elevation: 1,000 feet gain\n- Difficulty: Moderate\n- Time: 3-4 hours\n- Terrain: Mix of forests and grasslands\n- Views: Panoramic Bay Area, SF, Golden Gate Bridge\n- Character: Well-marked, well-maintained, gentle graded trail\n\n**Wildcat Gorge Trail:**\n- Distance: 5.5 miles out-and-back (11 miles round-trip)\n- Elevation: 1,200 feet gain\n- Difficulty: Moderate to challenging\n- Time: 4-5 hours\n- Terrain: Lush shaded canyon along Wildcat Creek\n- Features: Stream crossings, potential mud (wet season)\n- Character: Steeper than Nimitz Way, more rugged terrain\n\nUser selected this park for solo day hike after discussing multiple Bay Area options.
