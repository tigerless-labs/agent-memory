**Carbon Offset:** Carbon Footprint, Offset, EcoPassenger\n\n**Sustainable Accommodations:** Ecobnb, Eco Hotels of the World, BookDifferent, Booking.com (Sustainable Travel), Expedia, Airbnb (Eco-Friendly filter)\n\n**Airlines with Carbon Offset:** Qantas (Fly Carbon Neutral), Air New Zealand (FlyNeutral), KLM (CO2ZERO), Lufthansa (Compensaid), SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United, Norwegian (fuel efficiency focus), TAP Air Portugal\n\n**Eco Transportation Alternatives:** Public transit (Citymapper, Moovit), bike-share, car-sharing (Zipcar, Car2Go), electric scooters (Bird, Lime, Spin), ride-sharing (Waze Carpool, BlaBlaCar)\n\n**Waste Reduction:** Refill (water bottle stations), iRecycle, Zero Waste Travel\n\n**Other Apps:** HappyCow (vegan/vegetarian restaurants), EcoChallenge, Susty (tips + hacks)\n\n**Eco Certifications:** LEED, EarthCheck, Ecotourism Kenya
