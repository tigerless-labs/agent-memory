Recommended restaurants in Flagstaff, Arizona:
- Late for the Train Cafe: Breakfast and lunch, including vegan/gluten-free options
- Proper Meats + Provisions: Gourmet deli with sandwiches and charcuterie
- Fat Olives: Italian cuisine with pasta, pizza, risotto
- The Toasted Owl: Brunch spot with creative dishes
- Big Foot BBQ: BBQ restaurant

Recommended accommodations:
- Little America Flagstaff: Mid-range hotel with pool and free breakfast
- Hotel Monte Vista: Historic hotel with lively atmosphere
- Flagstaff Inn and Suites: Budget-friendly option
- The Inn at Flagstaff: Cozy family-run bed and breakfast
- Flagstaff KOA: Camping option
