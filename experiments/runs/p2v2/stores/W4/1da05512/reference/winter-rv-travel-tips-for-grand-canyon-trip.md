Tips for winterizing an RV for winter travel:

**Plumbing & Water:**
- Drain and insulate water hoses
- Install freeze-proof or heated water hoses

**Heating:**
- Check RV heating system condition
- Consider portable or propane heater as backup

**Tires & Safety:**
- Check tire pressure and tread depth
- Consider snow tires or tire chains for snowy roads

**Supplies:**
- Pack winter clothing, blankets, emergency supplies
- Bring flashlights, first aid kit, snacks
- Fill propane tank before departure
- Include shovel, ice scraper, snow chains, sand/cat litter

**Route & Weather:**
- Check Arizona Department of Transportation website for road conditions
- Monitor weather forecasts
- Drive slowly and cautiously
- Use low gear when driving uphill or on snowy roads
- Consider winter-specific RV insurance

**Backup Planning:**
- Have alternative accommodation options in case of extreme weather
