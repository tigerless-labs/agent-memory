Resources for learning pottery and ceramic techniques:

**Online Courses & Websites:**
- Craftsy: Online courses and tutorials on pottery and ceramics
- Pottery Making Illustrated: Step-by-step tutorials, articles, and videos
- The Potter's Council: Tutorials, articles, and videos on pottery techniques
- Ceramic Arts Network: Online community with tutorials on various techniques

**YouTube Channels:**
- Simon Leach Pottery: Comprehensive tutorials on pottery techniques
- Pottery By Osa: Various pottery tutorials including coiling and scoring
- The Ceramic School: Range of pottery tutorials by experienced ceramic artists

**Books:**
- "The Potter's Bible" by Marylin Scott: Comprehensive guide to ceramics
- "Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter" by Ian Gregory: Guide to glaze formulation and application
