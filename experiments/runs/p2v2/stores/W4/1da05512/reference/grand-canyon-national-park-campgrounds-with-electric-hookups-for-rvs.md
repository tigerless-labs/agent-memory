RV campgrounds at Grand Canyon National Park with electric hookups:

**Mather Campground:**
- 327 campsites with electric hookups
- Located near South Rim
- Amenities: flush toilets, showers, laundry facilities
- Reserve through recreation.gov

**Trailer Village RV Park:**
- 84 sites with electric hookups
- Located near South Rim
- Amenities: WiFi, laundry facilities, camp store
- Reserve through grandcanyonlodges.com

**Desert View Campground:**
- 50 campsites with electric hookups
- Located near South Rim
- Amenities: flush toilets, potable water
- Reserve through recreation.gov

Note: Campgrounds fill quickly during peak season, especially winter holidays. Reserve well in advance.
