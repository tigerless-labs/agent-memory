**Folk Horror/Atmospheric (similar to Midsommar):**\n- The Wicker Man (1973) — classic paganism/ritual sacrifice\n- The Devil's Backbone (2001) — Spanish Civil War era haunting\n- Kill List (2011) — slow-burning, atmospheric\n- A Dark Song (2016) — occult rituals and folk magic\n- November (2017) — black-and-white, 19th century Estonia\n- Apostle (2018) — Netflix, cults and paganism\n- The Sacrament (2013) — found-footage, cult dangers\n\n**Slow-burn Atmospheric Horror:**\n- The Invitation (2015) — psychological tension\n- Martha Marcy May Marlene (2011) — cult aftermath\n- The Blackcoat's Daughter (2015) — boarding school supernatural\n- The Autopsy of Jane Doe (2016) — mysterious corpse secrets
