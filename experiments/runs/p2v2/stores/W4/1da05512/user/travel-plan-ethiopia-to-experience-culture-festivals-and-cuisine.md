Ethiopia added to travel bucket list. Interested in experiencing local culture and traditions. Wants to attend festivals: Timkat (Jan 19—baptism of Jesus), Meskel (Sept 27—finding of True Cross), Enkutatash (Sept 11—Ethiopian New Year), Fasika (April—Orthodox Easter). Wants to try Ethiopian dishes: Injera (flatbread staple), Doro Wot (spicy chicken stew), Tibs (sautéed meat), Kitfo (raw beef), Shiro (chickpea/lentil stew), and experience traditional coffee ceremony.
