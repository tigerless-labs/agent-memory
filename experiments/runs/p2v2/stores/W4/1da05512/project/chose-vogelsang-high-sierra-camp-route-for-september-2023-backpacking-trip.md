**Route Selected:** Vogelsang High Sierra Camp (Yosemite area)\n- Distance: ~20 miles\n- Elevation gain: ~3,000 feet  \n- Difficulty: Moderate\n- Duration: 3-4 days planned\n- Permits: Required from Yosemite\n\n**Why chosen:** User reassessed abilities and wanted more manageable option than High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft). This route described as 'great introduction to high-elevation backpacking' with amazing scenery and more relaxed pace.\n\n**Accommodations:** Tent cabins at 10,300 ft with bunk beds, mattresses, pillows, modern restrooms, showers, full-service dining.\n\n**Access:** 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows or shuttle from visitor center. Book through Yosemite Conservancy early as spots fill quickly.
