Thesis research focused on the effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in math and reading comprehension. Key findings: these systems can be effective but implementation and integration into the classroom need careful consideration. Common challenges identified: need for ongoing teacher professional development and training; importance of curriculum alignment with learning goals. Also explored potential to promote equity and inclusion, but identified concerns around algorithmic bias. Research used case studies to illustrate challenges and opportunities of implementation.
