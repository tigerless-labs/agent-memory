Hasn't watched cable TV in months. Values streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) for:
- Ad-free viewing
- Ability to pick up where they left off
- Diverse content library (originals, international, classic movies)
- Mobile access (phone/tablet)
- Binge-watch model (full seasons at once, no weekly waits)
- Overall convenience and flexibility
