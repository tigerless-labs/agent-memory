Recommended calendar/scheduling tools:\n- Google Calendar (web, iOS, Android) — integrates with Google services, supports recurring events, time zones, Find a time, Propose a new time features\n- Microsoft Outlook (web, iOS, Android)\n- Calendly (web) — good for sharing availability\n- Doodle (web) — polling for scheduling\n- World Time Buddy (web) — for coordinating across time zones\n\nVideo conferencing integration:\n- Google Calendar integrates with Google Hangouts/Meet\n- Zoom has add-on for Google Calendar for one-click meeting creation\n- Can add conferencing links from Discord, Skype, or other tools to calendar events
