- Interested in low-light indoor plants for various spaces
- Considering ZZ Plant specifically for ease of care

ZZ Plant care information learned:
- Watering: every 1-2 weeks in summer, 4-6 weeks in winter (drought tolerant)
- Fertilization: balanced 20-20-20 diluted fertilizer once monthly during growing season (spring/summer), skip in winter
- Prefers well-draining soil and low-to-medium indirect light
- Toxic to pets and children if ingested

Other low-light plant options researched: Snake Plant, Pothos, Peace Lily, Dracaena, Philodendron, Chinese Evergreen, Spider Plant, Parlor Palm, Bromeliad
