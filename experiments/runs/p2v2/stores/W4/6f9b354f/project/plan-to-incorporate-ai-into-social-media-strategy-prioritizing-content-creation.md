**Goal:** Incorporate AI into company's social media strategy

**Concerns:** Quality and authenticity of AI-generated content — wants it to sound like the company, not robotic

**Planned approach:**
1. Define brand voice and tone first
2. Experiment with AI-generated content tools
3. Use AI for research (topic suggestions, keyword research) rather than just content generation
4. Review and curate AI content before publishing
5. Blend AI content with human-created content

**Tools to explore:**
- BuzzSumo — topic suggestions, trending analysis
- Ahrefs — content explorer, keyword research
- Answer Engine — AI-powered Q&A insights
- Quora — community-driven Q&A for content inspiration

**Philosophy:** Think of AI as augmentation, not replacement of human creativity. AI should free up time for creative work.
