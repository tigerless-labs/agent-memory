**Content generation tools:**
- WordLift — generates blog posts, social posts, eBooks
- Content Blossom — AI content optimization
- Snoring Cat — content generation
- Adobe Fresco, Prisma, Deep Dream Generator — visual/image generation

**Analytics & insights:**
- Hootsuite Insights — social media performance, trends, predictive analytics
- Union Metrics — social analytics
- Brandwatch — sentiment analysis, customer concerns
- NetBase — sentiment analysis
- Sprout Social — social listening, brand monitoring
- Brand24 — brand mentions, trends
- Talkwalker — social listening

**Engagement & customer service:**
- ManyChat — AI chatbots
- Dialogflow — conversational AI
- Microsoft Bot Framework — chatbots

**Personalization:**
- AgilOne — personalized social content
- Sailthru — personalization

**Influencer & optimization:**
- AspireIQ — influencer identification
- Upfluence — influencer partnerships
- HYPR — influencer identification
- Acrolinx — content optimization
- ContentStudio — content optimization
- Conductor — content optimization

**Key recommendations for using AI:**
- Always include human review before publishing
- Maintain transparency about AI use
- Comply with GDPR and CCPA for data privacy
- Use as augmentation, not replacement
