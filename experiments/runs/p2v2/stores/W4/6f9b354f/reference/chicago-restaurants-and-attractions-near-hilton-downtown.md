**Coffee shops near Hilton downtown:**
- Intelligentsia Coffee (53 E Randolph St) — 2-min walk, local roaster, expertly brewed, minimalist vibe
- Starbucks Reserve Bar (646 S Michigan Ave) — 3-min walk
- La Colombe Coffee Roasters (1552 N Wells St) — 5-min walk
- Peet's Coffee (444 S LaSalle St) — 5-min walk
- The Coffee Studio (56 E Randolph St) — 7-min walk

**Observation decks & viewpoints:**
- Willis Tower Skydeck (233 S Wacker Dr) — 10-min walk, 103rd floor, views of city and Lake Michigan
- Millennium Park's Lurie Garden (201 E Randolph St) — 5-min walk, nice at night
- Navy Pier's Polk Bros Park (600 E Grand Ave) — short ride, Centennial Wheel
- 360 CHICAGO Observation Deck (875 N Michigan Ave) — 10-min ride, 94th floor
- Chicago Riverwalk — 5-min walk, views at night

**Running routes/parks:**
- Lakefront Trail — 18.5-mile path along Lake Michigan
- Millennium Park loop — 1.5 miles
- Grant Park — few blocks away, multiple paths

**Tours:**
- Chicago's First Lady Cruises — 90-min architectural cruise along Chicago River
- Chicago Riverwalk Tour — 90 min
- Chicago Food Tours
- Chicago Gangster Tour — 2 hours
