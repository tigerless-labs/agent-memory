**Tools user plans to explore:**

**BuzzSumo** — Topic suggestion and analysis
- Analyzes trending topics and keywords
- Content performance insights
- Influencer identification
- User is particularly interested

**Ahrefs** — Content explorer for SEO research
- Keyword research and optimization
- Competitor analysis
- Content gaps and suggestions
- User is particularly interested

**Answer Engine** — AI-powered Q&A insights
- Analyzes questions relevant to your niche
- Helps identify content gaps
- Inspection of audience interests

**Quora** — Community-driven Q&A
- Trending questions and topics
- Content inspiration and insights
- Understanding audience pain points

**Other research tools mentioned:**
- Google Keyword Planner (free)
- SEMrush — topic research, keyword analysis
- Moz Keyword Explorer
- Ubersuggest (free) — keywords, content ideas, competitor analysis
- Google Trends (free) — search trends and popular topics

**User's approach:** Start with BuzzSumo and Ahrefs for primary research, supplement with Answer Engine and Quora for content inspiration and validation.
