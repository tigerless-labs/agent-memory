Tier structure with multiple loyalty levels offering exclusive benefits. 

Program features:
- Points-based system: customers earn points for each purchase
- Tier-based progression: frequent customers unlock exclusive rewards
- Sign-up bonus to encourage enrollment (design TBD)
- Reward options: discounts on future purchases, free products, exclusive access, early access to sales

Promotion and rollout strategy:
- Promote through social media
- Email newsletters
- In-store promotions
- Train staff to mention program at checkout
- Provide clear joining instructions

Considering loyalty software: Square Loyalty, LoyaltyLion, Swell, or Belly
