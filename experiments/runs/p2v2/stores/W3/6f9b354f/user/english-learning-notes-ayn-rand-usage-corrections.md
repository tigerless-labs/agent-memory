Example sentence correction from May 2023 philosophy discussion.

Incorrect: Ayn Land thinks that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must take care of ourselves to live in a somewaht selfish manner.

Correct: Ayn Rand believed that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests in order to live, which some might consider a somewhat selfish way of thinking.

Key corrections: proper name spelling (Ayn Rand), past tense consistency (believed), spelling (somewhat), and phrasing clarity.
