Graphics card upgrade recommendations provided May 28, 2023

Affordable alternatives to NVIDIA RTX 3070:

Mid-range options:
- NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super: USD 200-250, good for 1080p and 1440p gaming
- AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT: USD 200-220, strong competitor to GTX 1660 Super

Entry-level options:
- NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 Super: USD 150-180, entry-level boost
- AMD Radeon RX 5500 XT: USD 150-180, entry-level with decent 1080p performance

Considerations when choosing:
- Monitor resolution (affects card suitability)
- Power supply capacity
- System motherboard and CPU compatibility

Additional money-saving strategies:
- Look for refurbished or open-box models
- Check for bundle deals and discounts
- Compare performance benchmarks before purchase
