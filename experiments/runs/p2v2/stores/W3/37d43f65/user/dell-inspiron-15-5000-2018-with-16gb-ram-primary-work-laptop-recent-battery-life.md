**Device:** Dell Inspiron 15 5000 series  
**Year:** 2018  
**Current RAM:** 16GB (upgraded recently, gave performance boost)  
**Current issue:** Battery life dropped from 6-7 hours to 4-5 hours after RAM upgrade to 16GB  
**Purpose:** Primary work laptop  

**Troubleshooting attempted/planned:**
- Battery calibration (drain to 0%, charge to 100%, repeat 2-3x)
- Check system resource usage in Task Manager
- Adjust power management settings
- Monitor for resource-hungry processes

**Possible causes identified:**
- Battery recalibration needed
- Additional power draw from higher RAM capacity
- Increased background resource usage
