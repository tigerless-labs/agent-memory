Strongly prefers streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) over traditional cable television.

**Reasons for preference:**
- Ad-free, uninterrupted viewing experience
- Watch on-demand at own pace; can resume where left off
- Broader content catalog than cable (originals, international shows, classics)
- Can watch on any device, including mobile while on the go
- Can binge entire seasons at once rather than waiting for weekly releases

**Dislikes about cable:**
- Repetitive commercials and interruptions  
- Forced to wait for weekly episode releases
- Limited content availability

As of May 2023, hasn't watched cable TV regularly in months.
