**Albums:**
- Sprained Ankle (2015) — debut album, introspective songwriting, soaring vocals, indie-rock and folk elements
- Turn Out the Lights (2017) — sophomore album, explores themes of love, loss, introspection; more experimental sound
- Boygenius (2018) — collaborative EP with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocal arrangements

**Popular/recommended tracks:**
- "Appointments" — critically acclaimed, showcases introspective songwriting and soaring vocals, indie-rock and folk mix
- "Turn Out the Lights" — title track from sophomore album, poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, hauntingly beautiful melody
- "Rejoice" — explores themes of love, loss, introspection; signature vocal delivery and introspective lyrics
- "Souvenir" — critically acclaimed, showcases ability to craft emotive songs with focus on storytelling
- "Hurt Less" — features poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic elements
- "Red Door" (2019) — features poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic mix
- "Favor" (2020) — showcases ability to craft emotive, introspective songs with storytelling focus

**Live performances:** Known for emotive live performances; check out NPR Tiny Desk Concert or KCRW Morning Becomes Eclectic performance
