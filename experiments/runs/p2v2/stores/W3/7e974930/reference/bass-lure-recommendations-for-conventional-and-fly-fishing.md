**Conventional Bass Lures:**

**Topwater Lures:**
- Popper — creates surface commotion, attracts from distance
- Spook — walk-the-dog style, imitates wounded baitfish, warm water active bass
- Frog — hollow-body lure, ideal for heavy cover and lily pads

**Soft Plastics:**
- Curly Tail Grub — versatile, jighead/Texas rig/Carolina rig options
- Worm — classic, Texas rig or Carolina rig
- Lizard — imitates crawdad, flipping and pitching in heavy cover

**Crankbaits:**
- Shad-imitating crankbait — medium-diving, open water and structure
- Square-bill crankbait — shallow-diving, shallow water and cover
- Deep-diving crankbait — 10-20 feet depth, drop-offs and humps

**Jigs:**
- Jig and Pig — effective in heavy cover and around structure
- Swim bait — jighead with soft plastic swim bait
- Finesse jig — small, tight spaces and clear water

**Other:**
- Spinnerbait — spinning blade with soft trailer
- A-rig — multi-lure rig imitating school of baitfish

**Fly Patterns:**
Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, Baitfish Streamer, Popper, Slider, Gurgler, Crayfish Hopper, Crawdad Nymph, Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail Nymph, Woolly Leech, Frog Fly, Ant Fly, Beetle Fly

**Selection considerations:**
- Water temperature: warm water = baitfish/crawdad imitations; cool water = shad imitations
- Water clarity: clear water = natural presentation; murky water = stronger vibration/action
- Structure: choose lures suitable for rocks, weed beds, sunken logs
