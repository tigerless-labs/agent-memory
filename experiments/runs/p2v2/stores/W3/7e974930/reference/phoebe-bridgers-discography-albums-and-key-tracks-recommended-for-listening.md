**Albums:**
- Stranger in the Alps (2017) — debut album, introspective songwriting, soaring vocals, indie-rock and folk elements
- Punisher (2020) — sophomore album, explores themes of love, loss, introspection; more experimental sound
- Boygenius (2018) — collaborative EP with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocal arrangements
- Better Oblivion Community Center (2019) — collaborative album with Conor Oberst

**Popular/recommended tracks:**
- "Scott Street" — hauntingly beautiful, showcases introspective songwriting and soaring vocals
- "Killer" — critically acclaimed, poignant lyrics with catchy indie-rock melody
- "Motion Sickness" — explores love, loss, introspection; signature vocal delivery
- "Funeral" — hauntingly beautiful, showcases ability to craft emotive songs with storytelling
- "Kyoto" — critically acclaimed, poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic elements
- "7" (2020) — features poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic elements

**Live performances:** Known for emotive live performances; check out NPR Tiny Desk Concert or KCRW Morning Becomes Eclectic performance
