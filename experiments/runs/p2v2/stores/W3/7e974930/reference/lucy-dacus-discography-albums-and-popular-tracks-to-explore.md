**Albums:**
- No Burden (2016) — debut album, lo-fi production, introspective songwriting
- Historian (2018) — sophomore album, expanded sound, polished production
- Home Video (2021) — explores themes of nostalgia, identity, relationships

**Popular/recommended tracks:**
- "Night Shift" (from Historian) — critically acclaimed, showcases storytelling and hooks
- "Addictions" (from No Burden) — introspective, explores themes of relationships and personal growth
- "Nonbeliever" (from Historian) — hauntingly beautiful, features soaring vocals and poignant lyrics

**Recommendation:** Start with Historian as representative of her sound; then explore No Burden for lo-fi style; Home Video for recent work and artistic growth.
