**Targeting Rocks When Fly Fishing**
- Cast parallel to rocks, not directly at them
- Allows fly to move naturally along rock face rather than bouncing off
- Use streamer patterns for quick, aggressive retrieves
- Vary retrieve speed: slower near base of rocky drop-off; faster near top

**Targeting Weed Beds**
- Use weedless flies (frog patterns, weedless streamers) to reduce hang-ups
- Cast into edges of weed bed rather than thick of it
- Allows targeting bass cruising the weed line edges
- Use slow, deliberate retrieve to sink fly into weeds
- Imitate natural crawling baitfish or nymph movement

**General Structure Tactics**
- Bass hold near and around rocks, weed beds, and logs
- These are transition zones and feeding areas
- Early morning and late evening often more productive
- Bass use structure for ambush points and shelter
- Accurate casting to specific targets increases success

**Weedless Fly Options**
- Frog patterns with weed guard
- Weedless streamer designs
- Soft plastic patterns rigged to avoid vegetation contact

**Retrieves Around Structure**
- Short, subtle movements in heavy cover
- Slightly more aggressive retrieves in open water near structure
- Pause after strikes to let fish take fly
