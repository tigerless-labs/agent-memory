Fujifilm X-T30 II specifications and features:
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- Hybrid AF system (phase-detection + contrast-detection): 425 phase-detection points covering ~100% of frame
- AF speed: 0.02 seconds response time, 99% success rate
- Advanced subject tracking with AI prediction
- Face and eye detection capabilities
- Low-light AF performance: -3EV minimum
- Battery life: 380 frames per charge (EVF), 440 frames (LCD); ~45 min video (EVF), ~60 min (LCD)
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)
- Vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- Compact, lightweight body
- Body only: approximately $899; with XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ kit lens: approximately $1,398 total

Also recommend considering:
- Sony Alpha a6400 (compact, $998 body)
- Fujifilm X-T3 (weather-sealed, $1,099 body)

Both offer excellent landscape and nature photography capabilities.
