High-end VR gaming components recommended: NVIDIA RTX 3080/3070/3060 Ti or AMD RX 6800 XT/6800 graphics; AMD Ryzen 9 5900X or Intel i7-10700K/i7-11700K processor; 16 GB DDR4 RAM; ASUS ROG Strix B550-F or MSI MPG B550 motherboards; Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 SSDs; EVGA SuperNOVA or Corsair RM750X PSU. Budget-friendly alternatives: NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super or AMD RX 580 graphics; AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel i5-10600K; 8 GB DDR4 RAM; MSI B450M-A PRO MAX or similar motherboards; WD Blue or Kingston A400 SSDs; EVGA 500W or Corsair CX 650W PSU. Online retailers: Amazon, Newegg, Best Buy, Micro Center, B&H Photo Video. Specialty shops: PCPartPicker, Superbiiz, Reddit r/buildapcsales, Performance-PCs, FrozenCPU.
