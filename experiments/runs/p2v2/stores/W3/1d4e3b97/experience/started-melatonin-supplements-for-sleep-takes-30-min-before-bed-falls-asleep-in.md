Started taking melatonin supplements around early February 2024 to address sleep troubles. Established bedtime routine:\n- Reads for 20-30 minutes before bed\n- Listens to calming music or nature sounds\n- Gets ready for bed earlier\n- Takes melatonin 30 minutes before bedtime\n- Usually asleep within 15-20 minutes\n\nResults: Melatonin has really helped and improved sleep quality significantly.\n\nAlso limiting caffeine:\n- Used to drink coffee/tea around 4-5 PM\n- Stopped because it was affecting sleep\n\nUsing white noise machine to block outside noise, which has further improved sleep.\n\nTarget bedtimes: 10 pm on weekdays, 10:30 pm on weekends.
