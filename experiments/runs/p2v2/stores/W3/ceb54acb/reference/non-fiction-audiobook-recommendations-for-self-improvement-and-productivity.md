## Self-Improvement & Productivity Audiobooks

**Self-Improvement:**
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey
- Daring Greatly by Brené Brown
- Mindset: The New Psychology of Success by Carol S. Dweck

**Productivity:**
- Deep Work by Cal Newport
- Essentialism by Greg McKeown
- Atomic Habits by James Clear

**Additional:**
- Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind by Yuval Noah Harari
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle
- Shoe Dog: A Memoir by Phil Knight (on Nike's early history)

**Tips:** For non-fiction, recommended starting with normal speed (1x or 1.25x) and adjusting upward, taking notes to improve retention, and revisiting key concepts for better application of ideas.
