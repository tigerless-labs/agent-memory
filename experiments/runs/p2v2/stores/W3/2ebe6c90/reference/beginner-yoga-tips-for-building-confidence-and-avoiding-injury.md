**Getting Started:**\n- Look for beginner-friendly classes with supportive instructors\n- Start gently and listen to your body—don't push too hard initially\n- Use props (blocks, straps) to support proper alignment\n- Take breaks when needed (child's pose is always an option)\n\n**Building Confidence:**\n- Yoga is not a competition; focus on your own practice\n- Everyone's body is different; modify poses as needed\n- Practice at home with videos before joining a class\n- Find an instructor whose teaching style resonates with you\n\n**Managing Pacing:**\n- Focus on breath to stay centered even when class feels challenging\n- Modifications make poses accessible for all levels\n- Regular practice (even brief sessions) builds strength and flexibility over time\n- Consistency matters more than intensity\n\n**Key Mindset:**\nYoga is a journey, not a destination. Be patient with yourself and celebrate small improvements.
