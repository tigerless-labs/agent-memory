**Key Engagement Strategies:**\n- Real-time interaction: respond to comments, use audience names\n- Engage with polls, quizzes, Q&A sessions\n- Mix content types (tutorials, behind-the-scenes, exclusive content)\n- Encourage subscriptions and follow-ups\n\n**Platform-Specific Recommendations:**\n- Instagram Live: 10-30 min, visually focused, mobile-first, use FOMO\n- YouTube Live: 30-60 min, high-quality production, evergreen content, SEO optimization\n- Facebook Live: 30-90 min, community-focused, personal stories, call-to-actions\n\n**Content Ideas for Beauty/Makeup:**\n- Theme-focused makeup tutorials (natural look, evening look, etc.)\n- Product feature streams\n- Q&A sessions on skincare/makeup questions\n- Behind-the-scenes product selection\n- Live challenges and interactive elements\n\n**Cross-Platform Strategy:**\n- Repurpose content into multiple formats\n- Use teasers/snippets to promote main streams\n- Maintain consistent schedule and branding
