Considering Costa Rica for August 2023 vacation. Deliberated between Costa Rica and Iceland; chose Costa Rica for warmer weather and tropical climate.\n\n**Main Interest: Tortuguero National Park Eco-Lodge**\n- Planning to stay at an eco-lodge in Tortuguero for a few nights\n- Accessible only by boat or plane from San José\n- Transportation options from San José: domestic flight (~1 hr) + boat, or shuttle to La Pavona + boat transfer\n- Need to book accommodations and transfers well in advance\n\n**Wildlife Interests:**\n- Monkeys: howler monkeys, white-faced capuchin monkeys, spider monkeys\n- Sloths: two-toed and three-toed\n- Birds: toucans, quetzals, macaws, 300+ species total\n- Reptiles: crocodiles, iguanas, snakes\n- Other: jaguars (rare), caimans, 1,200+ butterfly species\n- Prefers guided tours; best wildlife spotting in early morning and late afternoon\n\n**Other Outdoor Activities:**\n- Hiking in national parks (Corcovado, Manuel Antonio, Monteverde Cloud Forest)\n- Volcano hiking (Arenal Volcano)\n- Surfing and beaches\n- Whitewater rafting\n- Zip-lining/canopy tours\n- Waterfalls and natural hot springs\n- Birdwatching (850+ species)\n\n**Recommended Eco-Lodges:**\n- Lapa Rios Ecolodge (Osa Peninsula, luxury, conservation-focused)\n- Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero National Park)\n- Monteverde Lodge (cloud forest)\n- Arenal Observatory Lodge (near Arenal Volcano)\n- El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula)\n\n**Concerns:**\n- Road conditions: 30% paved, 70% unpaved; rough, muddy, prone to potholes\n- Mountain roads: steep, winding, narrow\n- Rainy season (May-November): muddy/flooded roads, potential closures\n- Recommends 4x4 vehicle if driving independently\n- Alternatively: shuttle services, private transfers, domestic flights\n\n**Other Recommended Operators:**\n- Costa Rica Expeditions\n- Osa Conservation\n- Tortuguero Canals Tours\n- Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve\n- Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours\n\n**Weather in August:**\n- Transition between rainy and dry seasons ("green season")\n- Temperatures: 77-87°F coast, cooler in mountains\n- Relatively dry month with ~12 rainy days (brief showers)\n- High humidity, lush landscapes, fewer tourists, lower prices
