Plans to return to Yellowstone for another family trip. Key interests for the visit:\n\n**Geysers and Hot Springs:**\n- Old Faithful (iconic, must-see)\n- Grand Geyser (Upper Geyser Basin, erupts every 7-10 hours, shoots water 200 feet)\n- Castle Geyser (erupts every 10-12 hours)\n- Daisy Geyser (erupts every 3-4 hours, shoots water 80 feet)\n- Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine formations, colorful algae)\n- Norris Geyser Basin (Porcelain Basin, Emerald Spring)\n- Boiling River (natural hot spring for soaking)\n\n**Wildlife Viewing:**\n- Especially interested in bear sightings (maintain 100+ yard distance)\n- Best areas: Lamar Valley ("Serengeti of North America"), Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area, Mammoth Hot Springs\n- Other wildlife: bison, elk, osprey, eagles\n- Early morning and late evening best for spotting\n\n**Hiking and Scenic Areas:**\n- Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone River area (fascinated by geology)\n- Brink of Lower Falls Trail (3/4 mile, easy, 308-foot waterfall)\n- Seven Mile Hole Trail (10 miles, challenging)\n- South Rim Trail (6 miles, easy)\n- Interested in stroller-friendly trails for kids\n\n**Accommodations:**\n- Interested in Canyon Lodge & Cabins (heart of park, near Grand Canyon area)\n- Book early (accommodations fill up, especially June-August peak season)\n\n**With Kids:**\n- Interested in strategies to keep children occupied during long drives: audiobooks, road trip bingo, car games, tablets, snacks, pit stops, scavenger hunts, car karaoke, travel journals
