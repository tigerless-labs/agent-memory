## Hemp seeds specs:\n- Recommended dose: 1-2 tablespoons per serving\n- Protein: 5-10g per serving\n- Omega-3s: 1-2g per serving\n- Complete protein with all 9 essential amino acids\n\n## Usage tips:\n- Grind in coffee grinder for better texture\n- Mix with liquid first to distribute evenly\n- Start with 1 tablespoon if new to them\n- Store in airtight container in fridge/freezer (can go rancid)\n- Nutty flavor - may be overpowering for some\n\nBenefits: fiber, magnesium, potassium, iron; supports heart health and inflammation.
