## High-protein fruits:\n- **Guava**: 4.2g protein per cup; fresh or in smoothies\n- **Durian**: 3.6g protein per cup; Southeast Asian tropical fruit\n\n## Suggested incorporations:\n- **Guava Smoothie**: 1/2 cup frozen guava, banana, Greek yogurt, honey, almond milk\n- **Guava Salsa**: diced guava with red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice\n- **Durian Smoothie**: frozen durian, banana, coconut milk, cinnamon, honey\n- **Morning oatmeal**: 1/4 cup diced guava per 1/2 cup oats (1:2 ratio)\n\nFrozen versions easier to work with; use in moderation due to strong flavors.
