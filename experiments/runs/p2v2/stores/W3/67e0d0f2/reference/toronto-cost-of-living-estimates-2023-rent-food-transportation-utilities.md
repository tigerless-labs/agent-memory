Rent (May 2023):
- 1-bedroom shared/bachelor: CAD $1,200-$1,600/month
- 1-bedroom high-rise: CAD $1,600-$2,000/month
- 2-bedroom: CAD $1,800-$2,500/month
- 3-bedroom: CAD $2,500-$3,500/month

Food:
- Groceries: CAD $500-$750/month for basics
- Casual dining: CAD $10-$15 per meal
- Mid-range restaurants: CAD $20-$30 per meal

Transportation:
- TTC monthly pass (adult): CAD $156.00
- TTC single fare (cash): CAD $3.00
- TTC single fare (Presto card): CAD $2.90

Utilities & Communications:
- Hydro, water, internet: CAD $100-$200/month
- Phone and internet: CAD $50-$100/month

Employment context (Ontario 2023):
- Minimum wage: CAD $15.00/hour
- Entry-level jobs: CAD $35,000-$50,000/year
