Organizations offering cross-cultural exchange and learning:

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** - https://afs.org/
   - Non-profit with 50+ country programs
   - Offers: student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering
   
2. **Experiment in International Living** - https://www.experiment.org/
   - Nonprofit focusing on immersive cultural exchange
   - Target: high school and college students
   - Includes: host family stays, local school attendance, community service
   
3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** - https://www.yfu.org/
   - International educational organization
   - Programs: year-long exchanges, semester programs, summer programs
   - Coverage: 40+ countries
   
4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** - https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/
   - Volunteering opportunities for all ages
   - Locations: Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
   - Focus: community development and cultural exchange

**Preparation tips for cross-cultural exchange:**
- Research country culture, customs, history, current events
- Learn basic local language phrases
- Pack climate/culture-appropriate clothing
- Maintain open-minded and flexible mindset
- Develop relationships with local people
- Prioritize safety and awareness

**Recommended books for cultural learning:**
- The Namesake by Jhumpa Lahiri (Bengali-American identity)
- The Kite Runner by Khaled Hosseini (Afghanistan, friendship and redemption)
- The Joy Luck Club by Amy Tan (Chinese-American women)
- Things Fall Apart by Chinua Achebe (Nigerian tribal life and colonization)
- Persepolis by Marjane Satrapi (Iranian girl during Islamic Revolution)

**Recommended films for cultural learning:**
- Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (ancient China, martial arts)
- The Lives of Others (East Berlin during Cold War)
- Lion (Indian boy adopted by Australian family)
- The Motorcycle Diaries (Che Guevara travels, social injustice)
- City of God (Brazilian favela life)
