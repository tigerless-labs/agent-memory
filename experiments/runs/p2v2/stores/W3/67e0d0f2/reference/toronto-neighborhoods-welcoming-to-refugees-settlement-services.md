Toronto Neighborhoods for Newcomers and Refugees (May 2023):

**Downtown East (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown):**
- High concentration of newcomer communities
- Refugee organizations, community centers, language schools
- Close to city center

**Scarborough:**
- Large immigrant population
- Communities from Africa, Asia, Middle East
- Scarborough Village Recreation Centre (newcomer services, language classes)

**North York:**
- Significant refugee population
- Support services: North York Community House, Centre for Information and Community Services
- Near York University (strong refugee support program)

**Etobicoke:**
- Growing refugee community
- Services: Etobicoke Newcomer Centre, West Toronto Newcomer Services
- Close to Toronto Pearson International Airport

**Parkdale:**
- History of welcoming refugees and immigrants
- Strong community sense and social services
- Vibrant arts and cultural scene
- Close to city center

**Settlement Organizations in Toronto:**
- The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO)
- COSTI Immigrant Services
- WoodGreen Community Services
- Refugee Sponsorship Support Program (RSSP)
- Toronto Refugee Centre

**Housing Resources:**
- Toronto Affordable Housing Office
- City of Toronto Rent Supplement Program
- YMCA subsidized housing
- Salvation Army subsidized housing

**Factors to consider when choosing neighborhood:**
- Proximity to community centers, language schools, support services
- Access to public transit, grocery stores, healthcare
- Safety and walkability
- Affordable housing availability
- Cultural and linguistic diversity
