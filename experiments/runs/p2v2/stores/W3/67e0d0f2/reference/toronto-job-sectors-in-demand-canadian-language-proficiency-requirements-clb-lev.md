In-Demand Job Sectors in Toronto (May 2023):

1. **Technology & IT:**
   - Software development, data science, cybersecurity
   - Toronto is a major tech hub with many startups

2. **Healthcare:**
   - Nurses, doctors, allied health professionals
   - Growth driven by aging population

3. **Finance:**
   - Banking, accounting, financial analysis
   - Toronto is a major financial center

4. **Skilled Trades:**
   - Shortage of electricians, plumbers, carpenters
   - Strong demand across Canada

5. **Renewable Energy:**
   - Growing sector as Canada transitions to sustainable energy

**Canadian Language Proficiency Requirements (CLB - Canadian Language Benchmarks):**

CLB Scale: 0 (beginner) to 12 (advanced)

**Typical minimum for most jobs:** CLB 5-6
- Basic fluency in reading, writing, conversation
- Able to understand/communicate in everyday situations
- Limited vocabulary and grammar

**By job category:**
- Customer service/sales: CLB 6-7
- Administrative/office: CLB 7-8
- Skilled trades/technical: CLB 5-6
- Professional roles (healthcare, finance, law): CLB 9-10+

**Language proficiency assessments:**
- Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada (LINC) certificate
- IELTS (International English Language Testing System)
- TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language)
- CELPIP (Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program)
