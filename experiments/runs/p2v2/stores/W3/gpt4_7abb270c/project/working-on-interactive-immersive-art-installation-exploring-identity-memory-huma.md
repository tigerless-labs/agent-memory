Planning to create interactive and immersive art installations with the following characteristics:

**Themes:** Identity, memory, human experience, collective memory, shared cultural and historical contexts

**Sensory approach:** Combining sound, light, texture, and scent to recreate specific environments or memories. Example: simulating experience of walking through forest with bird sounds, rustling leaves, scent of damp earth.

**Narrative approach:** Interested in exploring personal narratives, historical events, and mythical stories through installation.

**Technologies of interest:** Spatial audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback, augmented reality, interactive storytelling, data visualization.

**Tools:** Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino for creating interactive elements.
