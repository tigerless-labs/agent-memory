Boston: 1 hour flight (100-200), hotels 150-250/night. Pittsburgh: 1.5 hour flight (150-250), hotels 120-200/night. Nashville: 2.5 hour flight (200-300), hotels 180-280/night. Washington DC: 1 hour flight (100-200), hotels 180-280/night. All within 500-700 dollar budget for 2-3 day trip. User has recently visited Boston so considered Pittsburgh instead.
