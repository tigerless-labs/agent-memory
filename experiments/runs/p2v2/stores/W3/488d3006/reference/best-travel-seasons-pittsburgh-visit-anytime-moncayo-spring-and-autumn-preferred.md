Pittsburgh: Good year-round. Moncayo/Aragon: Spring (wildflowers, mild weather) and autumn (fall colors, wildlife viewing, avoid crowds) are best. Summer has high temperatures and crowds. Winter has snowfall, limiting hiking but offering skiing. Autumn specifically recommended for Moncayo hiking trips.
