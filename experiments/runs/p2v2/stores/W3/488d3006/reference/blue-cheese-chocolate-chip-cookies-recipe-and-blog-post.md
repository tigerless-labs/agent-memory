Recipe combines salty blue cheese with sweet chocolate for complex flavor. Inspiration: blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local chocolate shop. Son (picky eater) enjoyed the recipe. Recipe inspiration came from experiment trying to balance sweetness with savory element. Ingredients: 2.25 cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt, 1 cup butter, 1 cup sugar, 0.5 cup blue cheese (crumbled), 2 eggs, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375F for 12-15 min. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer. Good for sophisticated dessert or dinner party.
