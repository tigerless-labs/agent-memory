Port Authority: buses, light rail, inclines (Monongahela and Duquesne). Fares: 2.50 one-way, 5.50 day pass. Free Transit Zone in downtown. Healthy Ride bike-share available. Uber and Lyft widely available. Note: Monongahela and Duquesne inclines provide panoramic views of city skyline and three rivers.
