GR-90 is a circular hiking trail in Moncayo natural park in Aragón. Distance: approximately 36 kilometers. Duration: 2-3 days to complete. Route features: passes through small towns and villages, reaches highest peak (Moncayo summit), Fuenfria waterfall, beautiful meadows, forests, rock formations. Good opportunity to learn about local culture and history.
