For camping in bear country. Containers: Garcia Bear-Resistant Container (meets IGBC standards), BearVault (heavy-duty plastic), Ursack (lightweight, puncture-resistant). Bags: Ursack Major (puncture-resistant), Counter Assault Bear Bag (heavy-duty), Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag (lightweight, compact). Key criteria: durable puncture-resistant material, specifically bear-resistant design, IGBC certification, easy to use/secure, appropriate size. Alternative method: store food 100+ yards from campsite, 10 feet high in tree, 4 feet from trunk.
