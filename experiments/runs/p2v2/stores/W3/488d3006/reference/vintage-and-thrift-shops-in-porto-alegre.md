Shops recommended for vintage shopping in Porto Alegre: Brechó Municipal (affordable prices for used clothes/shoes/accessories), Armazém do Passado (clothing, accessories, decor - has vinyl records), Bazar Vintage (trendy vintage and retro items), Casa da Sogra (retro/vintage home decor, furniture, accessories - has vinyl records), Pinga do Brechó (vintage/thrift with clothing, accessories, kitchenware). Brechó Municipal known for affordability. Armazém do Passado and Casa da Sogra have vintage vinyl records including Brazilian music, rock, and pop from various decades.
