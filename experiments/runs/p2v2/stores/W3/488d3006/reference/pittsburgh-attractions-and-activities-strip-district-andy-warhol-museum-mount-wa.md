Strip District: street food, ethnic markets, Primanti Bros sandwiches. Andy Warhol Museum: largest single-artist museum in US. Mount Washington: Duquesne and Monongahela inclines for city views. Three Rivers Heritage Trail: 24-mile trail system. North Shore: PNC Park, restaurants. Phipps Conservatory botanical gardens. Carnegie Science Center. Craft breweries: Penn Brewery, East End Brewing, Grist House Brewing.
