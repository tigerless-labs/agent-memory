User set up 20-gallon aquarium approximately 3 months before May 23, 2023 (roughly Feb 2023). Spent full day setting up and cycling water initially.

Fish care routine:
- Feeds fish twice daily
- Primary food: Tetra pellet food (fish's staple)
- Supplementary foods: frozen bloodworms and frozen brine shrimp
- Fish strongly prefer frozen brine shrimp ("go crazy for it")
- Fish more finicky about bloodworms
- User intentionally varies diet to ensure balanced nutrient intake

Considering upgrading filtration system with canister filter (looking at Hydor Professional, Eheim Classic, or Fluval 206).
