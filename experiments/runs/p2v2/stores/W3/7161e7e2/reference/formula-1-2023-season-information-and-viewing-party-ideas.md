**2023 Formula 1 Season:**

Schedule: Bahrain Grand Prix (March 20) to Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (November 26).

Regulation changes: Cost cap set at 145 million; simplified front wings, reduced drag, increased downforce for closer racing; new Pirelli tire compounds.

Team news: Aston Martin debuts (was Racing Point); AlphaTauri debuts (was Toro Rosso); Sebastian Vettel joins Aston Martin; Sergio Perez joins Red Bull; Fernando Alonso returns to Renault; Daniel Ricciardo joins McLaren; Mick Schumacher and Nikita Mazepin debut with Haas.

Championship contenders: Mercedes (reigning champions with Hamilton/Bottas); Red Bull Racing; Ferrari (Leclerc/Sainz Jr).

Features: Sprint qualifying format continues; improved aerodynamics; sustainability goal of carbon neutral by 2030.

**Viewing Party Ideas:**

Decorations: Checkered flags, racing colors, F1 balloons, racing signs, memorabilia.

Food/Drinks: Pit stop snacks, international cuisine, racing-themed cocktails, grid-themed desserts.

Activities: F1 trivia, podium predictions, games, fantasy F1 league, photo booth.
