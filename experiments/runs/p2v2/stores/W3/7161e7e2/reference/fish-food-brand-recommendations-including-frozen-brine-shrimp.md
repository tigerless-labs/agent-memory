**Frozen Brine Shrimp Brands (May 2023):**

San Francisco Bay Brand: Popular, harvested from Great Salt Lake, rich in nutrients. Offers plain, brine shrimp with garlic, brine shrimp with omega-3 fatty acids.

Hikari: Well-known brand. Offers plain brine shrimp, brine shrimp with spirulina, brine shrimp with krill.

Argent: Reputable brand, sustainably sourced. Offers plain brine shrimp, brine shrimp with omega-3 fatty acids, brine shrimp with vitamins.

Omega One: Specializes in nutrient-rich foods. Brine shrimp harvested from Great Salt Lake, rich in omega-3 and essential nutrients.

**Selection Criteria:** Sustainable sourcing, nutrient-rich, omega-3 fatty acids, no preservatives or artificial ingredients, airtight packaging for freshness.
