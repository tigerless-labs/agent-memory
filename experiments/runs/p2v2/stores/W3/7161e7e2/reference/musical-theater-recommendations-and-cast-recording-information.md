**Recommendations given (May 2023):**

1. **Avenue Q** - Irreverent musical comedy with similar style to Book of Mormon. About puppets in NYC dealing with adult themes. Has Original Broadway Cast Recording (2003) and London Cast Recording (2006) available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music. Also Live at the Zipper Theater recording (2005) on YouTube and Amazon Prime.

2. **The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee** - Charming musical comedy about misfits in spelling bee. Catchy music, lovable characters.

3. **Something Rotten!** - Musical comedy set in 1590s about two brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare. Original Broadway Cast Recording released 2015, features Brian d'Arcy James, Christian Borle, John Cariani. 24 songs including "Welcome to the Renaissance," "I Love the Way," "A Musical," "Hard to Be the Bard," "Right Hand Man," "We Are the Brothers Wright." Available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Google Play, YouTube Music.

4. **Hamilton** - Groundbreaking musical blending hip-hop, jazz, blues, R&B. Tells American history story with diverse cast.

5. **The Producers** - Mel Brooks musical comedy about two scheming producers trying to create a Broadway flop.

6. **Urinetown** - Satirical musical poking fun at capitalism, government, social hierarchy.

7. **Be More Chill** - Sci-fi musical comedy about high school student taking pill to become popular. Quirky, electronic-pop score.

8. **Dear Evan Hansen** - Heartwarming musical about social media, mental health, human connection.

9. **The Band's Visit** - Poignant musical about Egyptian police band's visit to Israel. Quiet, introspective with stunning score.

10. **Hadestown** - Folk-opera retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice myth. 2019 Tony Awards: 8 wins including Best Musical, Best Book (Anaïs Mitchell), Best Original Score (Anaïs Mitchell). Original Broadway cast: Eva Noblezada (Eurydice), Reeve Carney (Orpheus), Patrick Page (Hades), Amber Gray (Persephone). Cast album released 2019. Premiered off-Broadway 2016, Broadway 2019, West End performances, US tour.
