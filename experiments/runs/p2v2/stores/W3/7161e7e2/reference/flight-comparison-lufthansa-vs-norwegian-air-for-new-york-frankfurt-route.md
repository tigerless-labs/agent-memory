Flight pricing and amenities comparison from May 2023:

**Lufthansa (non-stop)**
- Price: Around 850 USD return
- Seat pitch: 31-32 inches
- Amenities: Complimentary meal service, in-flight entertainment, 1 free checked bag up to 23kg
- Service: Friendly, personalized, attentive crew
- Hub: Frankfurt Airport (FRA)
- Frequent flyer: Miles and More program

**Norwegian Air (with layover in Oslo)**
- Price: 650 USD return (budget option)
- Seat pitch: 29-30 inches  
- Amenities: Buy-on-board food, limited in-flight entertainment, extra charge for checked baggage (20-30 USD per bag)
- Service: Efficient, functional, less personalized
- Note: Company had financial difficulties during this period

**Other alternatives compared:**
- United Airlines: 820 USD non-stop
- TAP Air Portugal: 730 USD with Lisbon layover
- Swiss International Air Lines: 790 USD with Zurich layover
