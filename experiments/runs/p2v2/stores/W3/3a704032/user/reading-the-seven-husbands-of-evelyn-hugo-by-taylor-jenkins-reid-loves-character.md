Started reading on 2023-01-29, still actively reading as of 2023-05-21.

**What draws the user:**
- Character development and complex relationships between women
- Exploration of identity (public persona vs. private self)
- Intersectional identity themes (woman, Latina, immigrant)
- Themes of power, privilege, and female empowerment

**Character observations:**
- Loves Evelyn's Cuban immigrant backstory and how it shaped her
- Appreciates Monique as narrator—brings outside perspective while being active participant in uncovering story
- Engages deeply with how Evelyn's relationships with women (Celia, Monique, Lucy) are central to her identity

**Resonance:**
- User identifies with themes about public vs. private personas—everyone has masks they wear in different contexts
- Appreciates how the book shows female relationships as both supportive and competitive, powerful and limiting

**Related reading interests:**
- Graphic novels similar to Saga: Monstress, The Wicked + The Divine, Black Hammer, The Umbrella Academy, Descender, The Goddamned, Y: The Last Man
- Contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists and complex relationships
