Noticed tiny moving dots on fern leaves and stems. Suspected spider mites or springtails. Decided to treat with neem oil spray according to label instructions. Monitoring closely for results. Planning to mist fern 2-3 times weekly with distilled water to maintain humidity and prevent pest return. Misting in morning, avoiding crown area to prevent rot.
