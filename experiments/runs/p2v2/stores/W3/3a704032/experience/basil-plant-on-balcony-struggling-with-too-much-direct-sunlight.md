Basil plant on balcony receiving too much direct sunlight and getting stressed. Decided to move to shadier spot with filtered sunlight (east or west-facing). Will adjust watering to account for less evaporation. Basil prefers partial shade, especially in warm climates. Monitor soil moisture before watering. Maintain humidity, fertilize sparingly at half strength. Pinch off flower buds to encourage leaf growth. Monitor temperature, maintain 65-75F.
