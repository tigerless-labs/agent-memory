On 2023/05/21, discussed failed first kimchi attempt from the prior weekend (approx 2023/05/14).

**What went wrong:**
- Too much salt
- Too much chili flakes (gochugaru)

**Plan to fix:**
- Rinse under cold running water to remove excess salt
- Re-season with measured gochugaru (starting with 1/4-1/2 teaspoon per 2 cups vegetables)
- Add garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups) and ginger (1-2 inches grated, per 2 cups)
- Taste as you go rather than adding all at once

**Key learnings:**
- Measure chili flakes by weight when possible, not volume
- Start conservative with heat level and adjust up
- Fresh garlic and ginger preferred
- Can use rinsing to salvage over-salted kimchi
