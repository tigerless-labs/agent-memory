Considering or evaluating a position as Computer Network and Systems Engineer with responsibilities including:
- Planning, developing, deploying, testing, and optimizing network and system services
- Configuration management and operational readiness
- Troubleshooting and fault-finding for network problems
- Supporting multiple operating systems and configurations

Background: Currently or previously held role as Escalation Engineer II at Amazon Web Services (AWS), troubleshooting complex customer issues.
