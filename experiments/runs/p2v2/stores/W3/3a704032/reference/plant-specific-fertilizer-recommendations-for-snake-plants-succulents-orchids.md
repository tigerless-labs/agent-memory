Snake plant: use balanced fertilizer (20-20-20), dilute to half strength. Options: Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food, Espoma Organic Indoor, Schultz All Purpose.

Succulents: use low-nitrogen cactus formula (10-20-10), dilute to half strength. Options: Miracle-Gro Cactus Palm Citrus, Espoma Organic Cactus, Schultz Cactus Succulent.

Orchids: use high-phosphorus formula (15-30-15 or 20-40-20), dilute to half strength. Options: Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10), Espoma Organic Orchid (15-30-15), Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20). Fertilize sparingly once per month.
