Audio recording: Invest in good microphone (Blue Yeti, Rode NT-USB). Position microphone 6-12 inches from instrument, angled toward sound hole, slightly off-center. Use pop filter. Record in quiet room with minimal echo. Use DAW software (Audacity free, GarageBand, Adobe Audition).

Video recording: Use good lighting (natural light preferred). High-quality camera with stabilization. Experiment with different angles and framing. Use tripod for stability. Consider external microphone for camera. Use gimbal to reduce shaky footage.

Additional: Plan content (tutorial vs cover). Edit using iMovie, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve. Add captions and tags. First video recommendation: cover song rather than tutorial (less intimidating, more engaging, less pressure).
