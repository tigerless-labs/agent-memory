Platforms for emerging artists and contemporary art: Artsy (curated artworks, artist profiles, exhibitions), Saatchi Art (online art gallery for emerging artists), Behance (creative portfolios), Instagram (hashtags: emergingartist, artoftheday, contemporaryart, artistsofinstagram), Juxtapoz (online art magazine), Hyperallergic (art news and profiles), New American Paintings (emerging painters), ArtNet (news and exhibitions), The Artist's Institute, Platform (global emerging artists).

More curated/personalized: Artspace (contemporary art), 1stdibs (contemporary art and antiques), Uprise Art (emerging artists), Minted (contemporary art and design), Collecteurs (contemporary art).
