Photography tips for selling instruments online:

Use natural light or invest in lightbox/softbox. Clean instruments thoroughly before shooting. Use high-quality camera or smartphone. Take angled shots: front, back, sides. Include close-ups of knobs, buttons, unique features. Show instrument in use if possible. Include accessories in photos. Be honest about flaws and cosmetic issues. Use plain neutral background. Edit photos for brightness/contrast/saturation. Resize per marketplace requirements.

Listing tips: Take clear photos, provide detailed condition description, list marketplaces: Reverb, eBay, Craigslist.
