Basil soil: requires well-draining mix with organic matter. pH 6.0-7.0. Include peat moss, perlite, compost. DIY mix: 2 parts peat/coconut coir, 1 part perlite, 1 part compost, 1 part general potting soil.

Spider plant: prefers well-draining mix rich in organic matter. Options: Miracle-Gro Indoor, Black Kow All Purpose, E.B. Stone Organics, Pro-Mix All Purpose. Repot to pot 1-2 sizes larger, gently firm mix around roots, water thoroughly after repotting.

Peace lily: prefers well-draining mix, slightly acidic pH. Use tropical plant mix.

Snake plant: requires well-draining potting mix to prevent root rot.
