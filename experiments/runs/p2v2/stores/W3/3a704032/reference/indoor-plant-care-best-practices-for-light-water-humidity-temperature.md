Lighting: Peace lilies prefer bright indirect light, tolerate low light. Avoid direct sunlight.

Watering: Check soil moisture by inserting finger to first knuckle. Water only when soil feels dry. Overwatering is common mistake causing leaf drop.

Humidity: Most tropical plants prefer above 50% humidity. Increase humidity with water tray and pebbles or humidifier. Mist in morning for moisture without fungal risk.

Temperature: Most tropical plants prefer 65-80F. Avoid heating vents, fireplaces, drafty windows.

Pests: Inspect for spider mites, mealybugs, scale. Treat with neem oil or insecticidal soap diluted to half strength.
