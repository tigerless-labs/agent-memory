# Basic Korean-Style BBQ Marinade Recipe\n\nFor 1-2 pounds of meat (beef, pork, chicken, or tofu).\n\n## Ingredients\n- 2 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste)\n- 2 tablespoons soy sauce\n- 2 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tablespoon grated ginger\n- 1 tablespoon brown sugar\n- 2 tablespoons sesame oil\n- 1 teaspoon Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) - optional\n- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar - optional\n- Black pepper, to taste\n\n## Instructions\n1. In a blender or food processor, blend all ingredients until smooth\n2. Taste and adjust to liking\n3. Place meat in ziplock bag or shallow dish\n4. Pour marinade over meat\n5. Massage to ensure even coating\n6. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight (6-8 hours)\n7. For more intense flavors, can marinate 24-48 hours\n\n## Key Variations\n- **Bulgogi**: Sweet and savory, focus on soy sauce and sugar\n- **Galbi**: Spicy and savory, focus on gochujang and garlic\n- **Dak galbi**: Spicy and sweet, focus on gochujang and sugar\n\n## Tips\n- Adjust heat level by adding more or less gochugaru\n- Add sliced onions, green onions, or grated carrots for depth\n- Use honey, maple syrup, or Korean rice syrup (yutjang) for varying sweetness\n- Add fish sauce, doenjang (soybean paste), or mushroom ingredients for umami\n- Gochujang provides depth, heat, and sweetness to the base\n\nSource: Suggested for trying at home (May 2023)
