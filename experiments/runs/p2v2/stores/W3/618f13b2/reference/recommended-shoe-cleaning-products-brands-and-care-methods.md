## Shoe Cleaning Kits & Products\n\n**Leather Shoe Cleaning Kits:**\n- Saphir Medaille d'Or — high-end, premium cleaning kit for leather\n- Kiwi Shoe Care — well-known brand, range of options for different shoes\n- Lexol — popular for leather cleaning and conditioning\n\n**Canvas/Sneaker Cleaners:**\n- Jason Markk Shoe Cleaner — popular, eco-friendly option\n- Sneaker Cleaner (unspecified brand)\n\n## Waterproofing Sprays\n\n- Scotchgard — well-known waterproofing spray for various materials\n- Crepe Protect — popular for suede and nubuck shoes\n- Nikwax — water-based spray suitable for canvas and suede\n\n## Storage & Maintenance\n\n- Cedar shoe trees — natural, aromatic wood; repels insects and absorbs moisture; ideal for leather boots\n- Leather-lined shoe trees — extra protection for maintaining boot shape\n\n## Cleaning Methods\n\n- Gentle solution: 1 tbsp mild soap + 1 cup warm water\n- Alternative: equal parts water and white vinegar\n- Clean with soft cloth, avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials\n- Air dry at room temperature, avoid direct sunlight or heat\n\n## Finding Cobblers\n\n- Google "cobblers near me" or "shoe repair near me" with location\n- Check Yelp, Google Maps, Bing Places for reviews\n- Ask friends, family, colleagues for recommendations\n- Contact local shoe stores; they often have repair services or referrals\n- Check National Shoe Repair Association (NSRA) or Shoe Service Institute of America (SSIA) member directories\n- Look for experience with specific repairs and materials\n- Get quotes and check turnaround times
