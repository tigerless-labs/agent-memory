## Approaching Strangers for Photography\n\n- Show genuine interest in the person and their story\n- Be direct and straightforward: "I'm a photographer and I love your [outfit/accessory]. May I take your photo?"\n- Use eye contact and friendly smile to establish connection\n- Read body language and respect boundaries\n- Be prepared for rejection; it's a learning opportunity\n- Show your work on phone/camera if asked\n- Keep interactions brief\n- Be aware of surroundings and context\n- Practice in low-stakes situations first\n\n## Building Rapport with Strangers\n\n- Make eye contact and smile\n- Use open, confident body language\n- Introduce yourself and explain your project/intent\n- Be prepared to answer questions\n- Show sensitivity to cultural and personal differences\n- Start with light small talk (weather, traffic)\n- Find common ground\n- Follow up and share photos if promised\n\n## Capturing Candid Moments\n\n- Observe and anticipate people's behavior and movements\n- Pay attention to facial expressions, hand movements, posture\n- Wait for the "decisive moment" (Henri Cartier-Bresson concept)\n- Shoot in burst mode to capture perfect expression\n- Focus on the eyes (windows to the soul)\n- Use soft, natural light; avoid harsh mid-day sun\n- Experiment with unusual angles (low/high vantage points)\n- Look for layers: foreground, middle ground, background\n- Experiment with negative space\n- Focus on gestures and interactions for storytelling\n\n## Keeping a Low Profile\n\n- Wear casual, neutral clothing to blend in\n- Know your equipment settings well\n- Shoot from hip or chest level rather than to eye\n- Use small camera or compact prime lens (like 50mm f/1.8)\n- Shoot from the hip literally for more candid moments\n- Zone focusing: pre-focus on area and wait for subject to enter frame\n- Capture mundane moments, not just extraordinary ones\n- Find unique vantage points (staircases, escalators, bridges)\n\n## Legal & Ethical\n\n- Be aware of local laws regarding public photography\n- Respect people's privacy and personal space\n- Be respectful and discreet when photographing in public\n- Be aware of surroundings and keep belongings safe
