Started learning street photography after attending a workshop on May 1-2, 2023. Focus areas include: building confidence and developing respectful approaches when asking strangers for permission to photograph them, reading body language and building rapport, capturing candid expressions and gestures, using 50mm f/1.8 lens effectively, composition techniques (layers, negative space, anticipation), and keeping a low profile. Practicing during morning commutes with camera in tow.
