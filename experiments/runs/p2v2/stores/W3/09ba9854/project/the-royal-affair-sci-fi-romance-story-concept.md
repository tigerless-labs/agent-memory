User is considering **The Royal Affair** as a sci-fi romance story concept.

**Setting:** Futuristic, intergalactic kingdom where humans and aliens coexist in fragile peace.

**Protagonist:** A duty-bound, noble-hearted prince torn between loyalty to kingdom and personal desires. Falling for a forbidden alien partner.

**Conflict:** Royal duties clash with growing feelings; interspecies relationships are forbidden; royal family must maintain pure bloodlines.

**Themes:** Identity, self-discovery, duty vs. personal happiness, navigating forbidden love.

**World-building:** Advanced technology for transportation, communication, energy; aliens possess ancient mystical knowledge.

**Character arc:** Prince begins duty-focused but gradually confronts own desires and flaws, learning to prioritize personal happiness.
