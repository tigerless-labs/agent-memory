User is working on a sci-fi romance story called **The Memory Thief**. 

**Protagonist:** Lyra — a skilled memory thief with fragmented memories and no clear recollection of their past. Hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO.

**Plot:** Lyra uncovers clues about their own past while stealing memories. A key plot twist involves the discovery of their true past and reason for memory loss, forcing identity re-evaluation. Another twist reveals the CEO's true intentions and consequences of the theft.

**Themes:** Identity, self-discovery, questioning authenticity of memories and self.

**World:** Dystopian future where memories can be extracted, stored, and sold on black markets. Government regulates through Memory Authority; corruption and underground markets thrive. Neurotechnology enables memory extraction/manipulation/transfer.

**Relationship:** Lyra develops slow-burn emotional connection with their mark, building trust and communication.
