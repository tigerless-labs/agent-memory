## Character Development Resources (Recommended May 2023)\n\n### Reedsy Character Profile Template\n- URL: https://reedsy.com/character-profile-template\n- Structure: 12 sections covering basic info, physical appearance, personality, backstory, motivations, conflicts, relationships, skills, fears, habits, emotional landscape, and character evolution\n- Described as detailed but balanced—not overly lengthy but encourages thoughtful character development\n\n### Recommended Books\n- **Characters & Viewpoint** by Orson Scott Card\n- **The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers** by Christopher Vogler (explores hero's journey and character archetypes)\n- **How to Write Short Stories and Use Them to Further Your Writing Career** by James Scott Bell (includes character development section)\n\n### Online Resources\n- Writers Digest: https://www.writersdigest.com/write-better-characters\n- The Creative Penn: https://www.thecreativepenn.com/character-development/\n- Helping Writers Become Authors: https://www.helpingwritersbecomeauthors.com/category/character-development/\n- The Write Practice: https://thewritepractice.com/character-development-exercises/\n- Novel Writing Help: https://novelwritinghelp.com/character-development-exercises/\n\n### Personality Assessment Tool\n- 16 Personalities test: https://www.16personalities.com/free-personality-test (useful for character insights)\n\n### Brainstorming Tips\n- Mind mapping character motivations and conflicts\n- Writing from character perspective (journaling, interviews)\n- Scene writing to test character reactions\n- Pomodoro technique for sustained writing focus
