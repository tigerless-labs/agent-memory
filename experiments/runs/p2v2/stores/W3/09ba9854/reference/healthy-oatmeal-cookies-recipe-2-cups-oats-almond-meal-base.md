Healthier cookie recipe provided and user expressed intent to make:\n\n- 2 cups rolled oats, 1 cup almond meal\n- 1/2 tsp each: baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon\n- 1/4 cup each: coconut oil (melted), honey\n- 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla\n- 1/2 cup raisins (user asked about substituting with dark chocolate chips)\n\nBake 350°F for 10-12 minutes.
