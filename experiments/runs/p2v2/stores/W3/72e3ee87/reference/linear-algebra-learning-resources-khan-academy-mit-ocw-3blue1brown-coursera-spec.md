**Free online resources:**
- **Khan Academy Linear Algebra Course** — comprehensive basics, free
- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Linear Algebra** — video lectures, notes, assignments, free
- **3Blue1Brown Linear Algebra Series** — animated video explanations on YouTube
- **Gilbert Strang materials** — free online textbook and video lectures (YouTube)

**Paid online courses:**
- **Coursera Linear Algebra Specialization** — 4-course specialization by University of Pennsylvania
- **edX Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory** — 12-week course by UC Berkeley
- **Udemy Linear Algebra for Machine Learning** — focused on ML/data science applications

**Practice:**
- Khan Academy practice problems and exercises
- MIT OpenCourseWare problem sets

**Recommended learning path:** Start with Khan Academy, then Coursera specialization
