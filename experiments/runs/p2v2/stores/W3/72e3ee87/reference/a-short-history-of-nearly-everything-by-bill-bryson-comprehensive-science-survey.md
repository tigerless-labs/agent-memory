**Title:** A Short History of Nearly Everything  
**Author:** Bill Bryson  
**Awards:** Aventis Prize for Science Books (2004), shortlisted for Samuel Johnson Prize

**Structure:** Six parts covering:
1. The Origins of the Universe (Big Bang, formation, life origins)
2. The Size of the Earth (geology, plate tectonics)
3. A New Age Dawns (life rise, ecosystems)
4. The Road to Us (human evolution)
5. The Stuff of Life (chemistry, physics, matter)
6. The Earth and Its Inhabitants (weather, oceans, life diversity)

**Writing style:**
- Engaging, witty, concise
- Uses anecdotes and vivid descriptions
- Conversational tone
- Accessible to non-experts
- No science background required

**Content:** Covers vast range of scientific topics, from Big Bang to modern biology, with stories about scientists themselves
