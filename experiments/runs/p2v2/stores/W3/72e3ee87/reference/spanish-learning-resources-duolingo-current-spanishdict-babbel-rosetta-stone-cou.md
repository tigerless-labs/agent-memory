**Currently using:**
- Duolingo (app-based learning)

**Language platforms:**
- **SpanishDict** — grammar guides, verb conjugations, dictionary (planned to start)
- **Babbel** — paid, interactive lessons, conversational focus
- **Rosetta Stone** — paid software, audio/visual, speech recognition

**Online courses:**
- **Coursera Spanish for Beginners** — 5-course specialization by Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (planned after SpanishDict)
- **edX Spanish Language and Culture** — 4-course series by Universidad de Salamanca
- **Open Culture** — free courses from universities worldwide

**Video resources:**
- SpanishDict YouTube channel
- SpanishPod101 YouTube channel
- Coffee Break Spanish (podcast and YouTube)

**Language exchange:**
- Conversation Exchange (find exchange partners)
- Tandem (language exchange app)
- HelloTalk (app with language exchange feature)

**Apps:**
- Anki (flashcards)
- Memrise (vocabulary and grammar)
- Quizlet (flashcards and study games)

**Podcasts:**
- Coffee Break Spanish
- Spanish Obsessed
- News in Slow Spanish

**Recommended path:** SpanishDict → Coursera Spanish for Beginners
