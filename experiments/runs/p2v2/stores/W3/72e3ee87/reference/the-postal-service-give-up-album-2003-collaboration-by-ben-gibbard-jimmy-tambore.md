**Album:** Give Up  
**Artist:** The Postal Service (indie-electronic duo)  
**Released:** 2003  
**Members:**
- Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie, indie rock)
- Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel, electronic)

**Standout tracks recommended:**
- *Such Great Heights* — synth-driven anthem, new love themes
- *The District Sleeps Alone Tonight* — melancholic, introspective, insomnia themes
- *Clark Gable* — quirky upbeat sound, romantic themes
- *We Will Become Silhouettes* — somber/reflective, post-apocalyptic lyrics
- *Recycled Air* — acoustic guitar, prominent drum beat

**Distinctive features:**
- Blends indie rock with electronic elements
- Uses acoustic instruments alongside synthesizers
- Rich vocal layering and harmonies
- Genre-defining indie-electronic sound
- Strong storytelling in lyrics
