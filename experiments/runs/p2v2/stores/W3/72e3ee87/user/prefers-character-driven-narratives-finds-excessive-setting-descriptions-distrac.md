Likes historical fiction, mythology retellings, and character-driven narratives. When discussing Wuthering Heights, noted that Emily Bronte's detailed descriptions of moors and settings feel like 'reading a travel guide' rather than engaging with the story. Understands settings serve thematic purposes but personally prefers narrative focus on characters and plot rather than exhaustive environmental detail.
