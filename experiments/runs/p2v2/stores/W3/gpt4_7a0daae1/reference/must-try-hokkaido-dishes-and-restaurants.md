## Dishes to try:\n- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton with onions and vegetables)\n- Kaisendon (rice bowl with fresh sashimi: salmon, tuna, squid)\n- Jingisukan (Japanese Mongolian barbecue, grilled lamb/mutton with soy sauce)\n- Uni (sea urchin) and ikura (salmon roe) don\n- Ishikari Nabe (savory hot pot with salmon and vegetables, eaten in winter)\n- Hokkaido ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors)\n\n## Restaurants and markets:\n- Nijo Fish Market, Sapporo: fresh seafood\n- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho: alley with ramen shops\n- Tanukikoji Shopping Street, Sapporo: local Hokkaido cuisine and souvenirs\n- Daruma, Sapporo: jingisukan (grilled lamb)\n- Kitaichi Hall, Asahikawa: jingisukan\n- Yurigakuro, Sapporo: Ishikari Nabe\n- Kakizaki Shokudo, Otaru: Ishikari Nabe\n\n## Getting around:\nOptions include rental car, JR Hokkaido trains, buses, bicycles, or guided food tours.
