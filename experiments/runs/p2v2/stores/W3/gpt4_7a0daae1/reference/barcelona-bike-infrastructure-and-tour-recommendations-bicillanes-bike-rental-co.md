**Barcelona Bike Infrastructure:**\n- BiciLlanes: 200+ km of designated bike lanes, often separated by physical barriers\n- Many pedestrianized or traffic-restricted streets in city center\n- Superblocks (Superilles): urban design zones prioritizing pedestrians and cyclists\n- Barcelona Bike Path (Passeig de la Mar): 7 km seaside path along Mediterranean\n- Diagonal Bike Lane (Carrer de la Diagonal): 11 km dedicated lane across the city\n- Ciclopeatonal: network of bike paths connecting parks and green areas\n- Bicing: bike-sharing system with day passes available\n- Helmets recommended (not mandatory)\n\n**Bike Tour and Rental Companies:**\n- Barcelona Bike Tours: 3-hour tours visiting La Sagrada Família, Park Güell, Gothic Quarter; offer bikes with baskets/racks\n- Fat Tire Tours: comfortable bikes with baskets/racks\n- Bicitours Barcelona: various bikes including ones with baskets/racks\n- Donkey Republic: bike-sharing with baskets/racks available\n\n**Eco-Friendly Activities:**\n- Barcelona Sail: eco-friendly sailing with solar-powered boats\n- Park Güell and Horta Labyrinth tours (4 hours): Book via GetYourGuide or Viator\n- Montserrat Mountain and Wine Country full-day tour: hiking and wine tasting\n- Huerto Urbano: urban gardening workshops in Barcelona\n- Barcelona Eco Tours / Green Tours Barcelona: walking tours on eco-friendly initiatives\n- Barceloneta Beach clean-up and snorkeling via Surf House Barcelona
