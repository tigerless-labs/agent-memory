**Key Supplier Evaluation Factors:**\n\n1. Product/Service Quality: Standards, certifications, compliance, QC processes\n2. Reliability and Consistency: On-time delivery, deadline meeting, acceptable lead times\n3. Pricing and Cost: Competitive rates, transparency, bulk discounts, hidden fees\n4. Customer Service and Support: Responsiveness, technical support, training, maintenance\n5. Financial Stability: Solvency, industry reputation, bankruptcy history\n6. Supply Chain Visibility: Transparent chain, risk management, disaster contingency\n7. Certifications and Compliance: ISO, regulatory compliance, compliance programs\n8. Innovation and Improvement: R&D investment, continuous improvement culture, adaptability\n\n**Red Flags to Watch:**\n- Poor communication or unresponsiveness\n- Lack of transparency in pricing/quality/supply chain\n- Unwilling to provide references or case studies\n- History of late deliveries, defects, quality issues\n- Inadequate insurance or liability coverage\n- Unprofessional behavior or unqualified staff\n- Unclear/unfair contract terms\n- Unwillingness to adapt to business needs\n\n**Price Negotiation Strategies:**\n1. Research market rates and competitor pricing\n2. Clearly define requirements and non-negotiables\n3. Build genuine business relationship with supplier\n4. Be transparent about budget constraints\n5. Ask directly for better pricing with specific phrases\n6. Look for value-added services (free shipping, priority delivery, support)\n7. Calculate total cost of ownership (support, maintenance, warranty)\n8. Be willing to walk away (know your max price)\n9. Use competitor quotes as leverage\n10. Explore alternative pricing models: tiered, cost-plus, value-based\n11. Get agreements in writing\n\n**Evaluation Method:**\n- Create weighted scoring system for each factor's importance\n- Use for objective comparison across suppliers
