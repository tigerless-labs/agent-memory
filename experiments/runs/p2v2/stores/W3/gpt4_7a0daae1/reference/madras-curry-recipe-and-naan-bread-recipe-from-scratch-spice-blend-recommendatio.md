**Madras Curry Powder Brands:**\n- Penzeys Spices: 12-spice blend including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom\n- The Spice House: 15-spice blend with unique additions like fenugreek and fennel\n- McCormick: More accessible and affordable option\n\n**Simple Madras Curry Sauce from Scratch:**\n- Heat 2 tbsp vegetable oil, sauté 1 diced onion until translucent\n- Add 2 minced garlic, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp cumin, curry leaves (optional), 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder, 1/2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)\n- Cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant\n- Add 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, 1 cup vegetable broth, 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional)\n- Simmer 10-15 minutes until thickened\n- Add cooked protein (tofu, chicken, etc.), simmer 5-7 minutes more\n- Garnish with fresh cilantro\n- Serve over rice, naan, or roti\n\nVariations: Add potatoes/carrots; substitute yogurt or cashew cream for coconut milk; add lemon juice/vinegar for brightness\n\n**Simple Naan Bread Recipe:**\n- Ingredients: 1.5 cups all-purpose flour, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp active dry yeast, 1/2 cup lukewarm water, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 cup yogurt (optional)\n- Mix dry ingredients, gradually add water; knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic\n- Let rise 1-2 hours in greased bowl until doubled\n- Divide into 6-8 portions, shape into balls, roll into thin circles (6-7 inches)\n- Cook in preheated non-stick skillet 1-2 minutes per side until puffed and golden\n- Brush with melted butter/ghee if desired\n- Tips: Use stand mixer for kneading; add garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds for flavor; cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crisper crust
