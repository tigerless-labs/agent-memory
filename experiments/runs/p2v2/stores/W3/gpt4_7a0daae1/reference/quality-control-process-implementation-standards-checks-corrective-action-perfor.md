**Quality Control Process Setup:**\n\n1. **Define Quality Standards**: Establish clear specs and requirements; document and communicate to supplier; ensure mutual understanding\n2. **Identify Critical Control Points**: Determine points that can impact quality; identify potential risks and improvement areas\n3. **Develop Quality Control Plan**: Outline procedures for monitoring/controlling quality; include checks at critical control points; define inspection frequency and methods\n4. **Implement Quality Control Checks**:\n   - Regular inspections and testing\n   - Statistical process control methods (e.g., Six Sigma)\n   - Verify supplier follows QC plan\n5. **Establish Corrective Action Process**:\n   - Process for addressing quality issues/defects\n   - Root cause analysis procedures\n   - Supplier responsible for implementing corrections\n6. **Monitor and Evaluate Performance**:\n   - Regularly evaluate against quality standards\n   - Use KPIs: defect rates, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction\n   - Provide feedback on performance and improvement areas\n\n**Checks and Balances:**\n- Regular audits and inspections for compliance\n- Product/service testing and validation\n- Industry certification verification (ISO, CE, FDA)\n- Supplier certification programs (ISO 9001, AS9100)\n- Third-party audits and inspections\n- Customer feedback and complaint monitoring\n\n**Quality Standards and Methodologies:**\n- ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management Systems)\n- AS9100 (Aerospace Industry QMS)\n- Six Sigma (Statistical Process Control)\n- Lean Manufacturing (Process Optimization)\n\n**Tools and Templates:**\n- Quality control plan template (Word/Docs)\n- Quality control checklist (Excel/Google Sheets)\n- Corrective action report template (Word/Docs)\n- Supplier performance evaluation template (Excel/Google Sheets)\n- Quality management software: Qualityze, ETQ\n\n**Key Principle:**\nClear quality standards with regular monitoring and documented corrective actions ensure supplier maintains consistent quality over time.
