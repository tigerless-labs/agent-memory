Recommended bike-friendly cafes and restaurants in Barcelona:\n\n1. **Can Culleretes**: Historic café in Gothic Quarter, outdoor seating, bike parking, good for coffee/snacks\n2. **El Xampanyet**: Popular spot for quick bites and drinks, outdoor seating, bike parking, known for pan con tomate (bread with fresh tomato)\n3. **La Pubilla**: Cozy café in Eixample neighborhood, bike-friendly, delicious pastries\n4. **Bicioci**: Bike-themed café in Poblenou neighborhood, healthy snacks and drinks, bike parking, repair station\n5. **Can Solé**: Family-run restaurant in Barceloneta neighborhood, traditional Catalan cuisine, outdoor seating, bike parking\n\nTips: Check opening hours and menus in advance; many have outdoor areas for bike parking. Tour guides typically have local recommendations.
