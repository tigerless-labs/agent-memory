Strategies for bookstagram success:

Content:
- Post high-quality photos with good lighting
- Diversify content: reviews, photos, quotes, bookshelves, reading nooks
- Post consistently (daily, weekly, or biweekly)
- Write engaging captions with thoughts and questions
- Use relevant book-related hashtags

Aesthetics:
- Choose visually appealing color scheme and theme
- Use props and accessories (bookmarks, booklights, coffee)
- Create consistent visual brand style

Engagement:
- Respond to comments and answer questions
- Host giveaways or reading challenges
- Collaborate with other bookstagrammers
- Share user-generated content from followers

Growth:
- Complete profile with picture, bio, contact info
- Participate in book-related events and reading challenges
- Promote account on other social media
- Use Instagram Insights to track performance

Additional:
- Be authentic and honest about books
- Ask permission for advanced reader copies (ARCs)
- Follow Instagram guidelines
