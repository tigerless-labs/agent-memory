Popular audiobook platforms:

Audible: 500k+ titles, 14.95/month for one credit per month. Largest selection.

Libro.fm: Partners with independent bookstores. 14.99/month for one credit.

Scribd: Subscription-based. 8.99/month for unlimited access to audiobooks, ebooks, and documents.

Hoopla Digital: Free with library card or student ID. Digital media platform with audiobooks, ebooks, music, movies, TV.

OverDrive: Free through public libraries with library card. Digital library for audiobooks and ebooks.

Google Play Books: Digital bookstore with audiobooks and ebooks. Often has deals and discounts.

Audiobooks.com: 14.95/month for one credit.

Hibooks: Newer platform with curated selection. 12.99/month for one credit.

Kobo Audiobooks: Digital bookstore with audiobooks and ebooks.

Audiobooksync: Free summer program offering two free audiobooks per week for 13 weeks.
