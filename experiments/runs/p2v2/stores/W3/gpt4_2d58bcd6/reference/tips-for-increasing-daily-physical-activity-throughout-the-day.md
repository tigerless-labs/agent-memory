Strategies for staying active throughout the day:

General:
- Take stairs instead of elevator
- Park farther away to walk more
- Use fitness tracker to set daily step goals
- Take breaks to stretch muscles

At work:
- Do desk exercises (chair squats, desk push-ups, leg lifts)
- Walk to coworkers desk instead of emailing
- Take walks during phone calls (when possible)
- Suggest walking meetings with colleagues
- Take lunchtime fitness classes if available

Home:
- Turn housework into workout with music
- Play active games with kids (tag, hide-and-seek, soccer)

General lifestyle:
- Try active commuting (bike, bus, train)
- Active shopping trips (biking to store instead of driving)
- Take walks during lunch break at work
