**Simple Butternut Squash Soup Recipe** (Serves 4-6)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs (parsley or sage) for garnish

**Method:**
1. Roast squash halves (cut lengthwise, seeds removed) at 400°F for 45-50 minutes until tender
2. Sauté onion in olive oil until soft (~5 min), add garlic
3. Add cumin, paprika, salt, pepper; cook 1 minute
4. Add broth, bring to boil
5. Scoop roasted squash flesh into pot
6. Blend until smooth (immersion or regular blender)
7. Stir in cream if desired
8. Season to taste, garnish with fresh herbs

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3-5 days or freeze 3-6 months in portions
