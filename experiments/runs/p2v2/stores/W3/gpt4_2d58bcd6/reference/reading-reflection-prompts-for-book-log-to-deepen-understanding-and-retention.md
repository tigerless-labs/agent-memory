Reflection prompts for reading log:

General:
- Main takeaway or key idea
- How did it make me feel? Inspire, motivate, challenge?
- Most memorable or impactful quotes/passages
- How does this relate to my current life or goals?
- Would I recommend it? Why?

Self-help specific:
- What changes do I want to make as a result?
- What practices, habits, or mindset shifts to adopt?
- How can I apply concepts to overcome specific challenges?
- Key actions or steps to implement the ideas?
- How will I hold myself accountable?

Action-oriented:
- One specific action to take this week/month/quarter?
- What obstacles might I face and how overcome them?
- What support systems or resources do I need?
- How will I measure success or progress?
- First step to get started?

Critical thinking:
- What did I disagree with or find unconvincing?
- How does this align with or challenge my beliefs/values?
- Potential limitations or biases in author approach?
- How does this relate to other books on similar topics?
- What questions or areas did it leave me exploring?

Inspirational:
- What inspired or motivated me most?
- How did it make me feel about myself or abilities?
- Which quotes, stories, anecdotes resonated most?
- How can I use inspiration to fuel my goals?
- What small wins can I celebrate?
