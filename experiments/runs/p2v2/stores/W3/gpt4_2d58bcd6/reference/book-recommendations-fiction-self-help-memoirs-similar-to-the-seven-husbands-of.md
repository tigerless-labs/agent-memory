Recommendations given based on enjoyed books:

Similar to "The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo":
- Daisy Jones and The Six (Taylor Jenkins Reid)
- The Royal We (Heather Cocks, Jessica Morgan)
- The Address (Fiona Davis)

Similar to "The Silent Patient" (psychological thriller):
- Gone Girl (Gillian Flynn)
- The Girl on the Train (Paula Hawkins)
- The Last Time I Lied (Riley Sager)

Similar to "The Nightingale" (WWII historical fiction):
- All the Light We Cannot See (Anthony Doerr)
- The Alice Network (Kate Quinn)
- The Lost Girls of Paris (Pam Jenoff)

Other authors recommended:
- Colleen Hoover (emotional, thoughtful)
- Emily Giffin (relationships, family, identity)
- Liane Moriarty (relatable characters, complex relationships)

Non-fiction and self-help:
- The Power of Now (Eckhart Tolle)
- Daring Greatly (Brene Brown)
- The Happiness Project (Gretchen Rubin)
- Attached (Amir Levine, Rachel Heller)
- Nonviolent Communication (Marshall Rosenberg)
- Hold Me Tight (Sue Johnson)
- The Gifts of Imperfection (Brene Brown)
- Mindset (Carol Dweck)
- Presence (Amy Cuddy)
- Wild (Cheryl Strayed)
- The Year of Magical Thinking (Joan Didion)
- Man Search for Meaning (Viktor Frankl)

Books on hip-hop and musical theater fusion:
- In the Heights (Lin-Manuel Miranda)
- Freestyle Love Supreme (Miranda, Kail, Veneziale)
- Passing Strange (Stew, Heidi Rodewald)
- Holler If Ya Hear Me (Tupac music)
