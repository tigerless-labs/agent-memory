Yoga and mobility exercises recommended for ankle flexibility and strength:

Yoga poses:
- Ankle Circles: Draw circles with ankle in both directions
- Toe Spreads: Spread toes apart as far as possible
- Ankle Alphabet: Draw alphabet with toes
- Calf Raises: Raise up on tiptoes and lower
- Downward-Facing Dog: Hold 30 sec-1 min
- Tree Pose: Balance on one leg for 30 sec-1 min
- Eagle Pose: Cross one leg over other for 30 sec-1 min
- Seated Forward Fold: Hold 30 sec-1 min

Ankle mobility exercises:
- Ankle rotations (5-10 reps each direction)
- Toe curls (10-15 reps)
- Heel raises (10-15 reps)

General injury prevention:
- Always warm up and cool down (5-10 min each)
- Stretch and foam roll regularly
- Strengthen core and ankles
- Use proper gear for activities
- Listen to body, rest when fatigued
- Gradually increase intensity
- Focus on proper form and technique

Recovery (RICE):
- Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation for acute injuries
- Gentle stretching for recovery
- Take rest days
- Consider compression wraps or braces
