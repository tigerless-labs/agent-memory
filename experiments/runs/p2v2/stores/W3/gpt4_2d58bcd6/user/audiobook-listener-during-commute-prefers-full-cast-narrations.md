Recently started listening to audiobooks during daily commute.

Audiobooks finished:
- Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind by Yuval Noah Harari
- Daisy Jones & The Six by Taylor Jenkins Reid (full-cast narration)

Strongly prefers full-cast narrations for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes - finds it more immersive and helps track who's speaking.

Also appreciates single narrators who bring unique tone and style to stories.

Recognizes narration quality is crucial to the audiobook experience - good narrator elevates the story, poor narrator detracts from it.
