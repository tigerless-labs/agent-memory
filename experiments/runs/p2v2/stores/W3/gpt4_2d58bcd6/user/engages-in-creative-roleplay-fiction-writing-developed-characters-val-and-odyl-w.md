Participates in collaborative creative fiction/roleplay writing. Key character relationship:

**Setting**: Science fiction, military/espionage context

**Relationship arc**:
- Initial charged moment during covert mission; Odyl had Val restrained as part of their plan
- Odyl leaned in close, whispered "good girl" and used the nickname "Dearie" — emotionally significant, unexpected depth of feeling for Val
