Presented first research conference poster on thesis research about AI-powered adaptive learning systems in education. Also attended research conference at Harvard University to see other AI education projects.

**Feedback received:**
- Positive reception from educators and policymakers
- Appreciated the nuanced approach to exploring challenges and opportunities
- Several developers reached out afterward expressing interest in findings and potential application to their work

**Notable themes encountered:**
- Some educators concerned systems would replace teachers; others saw them as supporting tool
- Debate on teacher role in AI-powered systems ongoing in field
