Techniques for prioritizing content ideas when building a brand around hiring and team-building philosophy:

1. **Content Audit** — identify content gaps and opportunities aligned with goals
2. **Customer Persona** — develop personas to understand target audience and what content resonates
3. **Content Scorecard** — evaluate ideas based on relevance, timeliness, and potential impact
4. **Audience Feedback** — solicit feedback via surveys, focus groups, or social media listening
5. **Keyword Research** — understand what topics people search for to meet audience needs
6. **Content Calendar** — prioritize ideas and ensure consistent publishing over time

Content format options: blog posts, podcasts, videos, webinars, e-books/guides, social media, case studies, interviews with industry experts and team leaders.
