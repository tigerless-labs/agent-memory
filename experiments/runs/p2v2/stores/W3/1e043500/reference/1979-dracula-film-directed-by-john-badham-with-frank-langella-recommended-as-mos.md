**Film:** Dracula (1979)  
**Director:** John Badham  
**Lead:** Frank Langella as Dracula

**Why recommended:** Literary scholars consider this the most accurate adaptation of Bram Stoker's novel. The portrayal is refined and seductive, capturing the character's charm and menace. Stays true to the novel's characterization, original plot, symbolism, and themes.
