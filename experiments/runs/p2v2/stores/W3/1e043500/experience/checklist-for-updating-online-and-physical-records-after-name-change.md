Process checklist for updating digital and physical presence after legal name change.

**Online accounts to update:**
- Email (address and signature)
- Social media (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest)
- Blogging and communities (WordPress, Blogger, Medium, Reddit, Quora)
- Cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud)
- Financial accounts (banking, credit cards, PayPal, Amazon, eBay)
- Subscription services (Netflix, Hulu, Spotify, Apple Music)
- Professional networks (LinkedIn, Academia.edu, ResearchGate)
- Gaming accounts (Xbox, PlayStation, Steam)
- Travel and loyalty programs
- Health services

**Physical records:**
- Social Security Card (Form SS-5)
- Passport (Form DS-11)
- Driver's license/state ID (check state DMV requirements)
- Voter registration
- Vehicle registration
- Property deeds
- Insurance policies
- Professional licenses
- Employer records

**Tips:**
- Update gradually, starting with most important accounts
- Keep certified copy of name change decree/divorce decree for documentation
- Use consistent naming format across platforms
- Check specific institutional requirements as they vary
