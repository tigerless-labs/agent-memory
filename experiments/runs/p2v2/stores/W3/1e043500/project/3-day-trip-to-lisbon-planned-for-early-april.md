Planning a short trip to Lisbon, Portugal in early April. Recommended Lisbon over Porto for a 3-day visit.

**Proposed itinerary:**

**Day 1:** Alfama neighborhood, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, Chiado district, café A Brasileira (pastel de nata)

**Day 2:** Day trip to Sintra (Pena Palace, Moorish Castle), Belém neighborhood (Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém pastry)

**Day 3:** Tagus River walk, MAAT museum, Bairro Alto neighborhood, Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara viewpoint, dinner in traditional restaurant

Other attractions mentioned as options: National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum, LX Factory art district.
