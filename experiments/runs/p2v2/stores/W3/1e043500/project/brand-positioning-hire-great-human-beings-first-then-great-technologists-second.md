Prioritize hiring and building teams around **great human beings first**, then technical excellence second.

Key principles:
- Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just skilled coders
- Value emotional intelligence (EQ) more highly than raw intelligence/coding ability (IQ) when hiring software developers
- Build teams on human qualities, collaboration, and communication skills as primary criteria
- Technical skills are secondary to human foundation

Content strategy focuses on remote work best practices, team building, remote leadership, hiring/onboarding, and tools. Goal: establish thought leadership in human-centered hiring and team development philosophy.
