**Photography interests:**\n- Cityscapes at night (twinkling lights, bustling energy, dramatic skyscrapers)\n- Landscape and nature photography\n- Light and color changes throughout seasons (primary interest)\n- Wildlife and nature photography\n- Capturing dynamic movement and motion\n\n**Night cityscape techniques learned:**\n- Manual mode shooting\n- ISO 100-400 for minimal noise\n- Wide aperture (f/2.8 or lower)\n- Slow shutter speed (15-30 seconds)\n- Daylight white balance for warm city light tones\n- High vantage points (rooftops, hills, observation decks)\n- Leading lines composition\n- Symmetry and reflection use\n- Long exposure techniques\n- HDR and bracketing\n- Panning techniques\n- Intentional camera movement for creative effects\n- RAW format shooting\n- Post-processing for enhancement\n- Blue hour shooting (hour after sunset)\n\n**Skill development focus:**\n- Staying motivated on long-term projects\n- Setting realistic goals and tracking progress\n- Journaling about shoots and learnings\n- Celebrating milestones\n- Learning from failures\n- Continuous experimentation with new techniques
