Dinner party menu planned for 8-10 guests (August 2023), accommodating vegetarians and gluten-free needs:

Appetizer: Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus

Main Course: Vegetarian sushi rolls with lemon-ginger dipping sauce
- Fillings: grilled portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura bits, avocado, cucumber
- Accompaniment: lemon-herb quinoa with toasted pine nuts and fresh herbs (parsley, basil, cilantro)

Garnish ideas: Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint), thinly sliced lemons, edible flowers (violas, pansies)

Dessert: Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available), fresh fruit platter

Key ingredients: lemon zest, fresh ginger, soy sauce, olive oil, avocado, portobello mushrooms, quinoa
