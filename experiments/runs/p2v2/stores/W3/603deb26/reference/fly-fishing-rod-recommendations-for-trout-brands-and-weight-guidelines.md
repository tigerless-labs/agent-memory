Recommended fly fishing rods for trout (from August 2023 conversation):

- Orvis Clearwater: entry-level to intermediate, 198-298 USD
- Sage Igniter: high-performance, 400-500 USD  
- Winston Pure: premium, 300-400 USD
- Redington Crux: budget-friendly/backup, 150-250 USD
- TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series: high-quality, 200-300 USD

Guidelines: Look for 2-5 weight lines, fast or medium-fast action, 9-foot length (or 7-8 feet for smaller streams). Graphite rods are lighter and more sensitive than fiberglass.
