Classic gin-based cocktails recommended for experimentation:

**Refreshing/Light:**
- Bee's Knees: Gin + honey syrup + lemon juice
- French 75: Gin + lemon juice + sugar + champagne
- Pimm's Cup: Pimm's No. 1 + lemonade + cucumber + mint + fruit

**Complex/Herbal:**
- Aviation: Gin + maraschino liqueur + crème de violette + lemon juice
- Last Word: Gin + green Chartreuse + maraschino liqueur + lime juice
- Corpse Reviver No. 2: Gin + Lillet Blanc + Cynar + Cointreau + absinthe

**Simple/Refreshing:**
- Gin Rickey: Gin + lime juice + soda water
- Gin-Gin Mule: Gin + lime juice + ginger beer (in copper mug)
- Monkey Gland: Gin + orange juice + grenadine + splash of absinthe

**Other classics:**
- Old Fashioned: Whiskey + sugar + bitters + citrus twist
- Daiquiri: White rum + lime juice + simple syrup
- Sidecar: Cognac + Cointreau + lemon juice

All recipes suggest using high-quality spirits and fresh ingredients.
