**Spirit Infusion General Guidelines:**

Ratio: Start with 1 part flavor ingredient to 4 parts spirit (can adjust)
Base spirits: Neutral-tasting vodka, gin, or rum work best

**By ingredient type:**

Citrus peels (lemon, orange, grapefruit): 2-3 days infusion
- 1-2 citrus fruits per 1 liter spirit

Lavender:
- Dried buds: 1/4 cup per 1 liter
- Infusion time: 2-3 days

Grapefruit slices: 1-2 per 1 liter, 2-3 days infusion

Cucumber slices:
- Use thin-skinned varieties (English/hothouse)
- Slice thinly to increase surface area
- 1-2 cups per 1 liter, 2-5 days infusion
- Taste regularly to avoid bitterness

Mint leaves:
- Fresh leaves only
- 1/4 to 1 cup per 1 liter
- 2-5 days infusion
- Monitor closely - mint can over-infuse

**Storage:** Cool, dark place (away from light and heat)

**Finishing:** Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter. Can rack (transfer to new container leaving sediment) for further clarification.

**Tips:**
- Taste regularly during infusion
- Experiment with different spirit types
- Can combine separate infusions after completion
- Avoid over-infusion which causes bitterness
