**Gin Styles to Explore:**

**London Dry Gin:**
- Classic, traditional style
- Bold, juniper-forward flavor
- Strong botanical profile
- Good for classic cocktails (Negroni, Martini, G&T)

**Plymouth Gin:**
- Slightly sweeter and more approachable
- Good for beginners or those wanting softer profiles
- Less aggressively juniper-focused
- Works well in cocktails requiring balance

**New Western or New World Gin:**
- Modern, experimental style
- Features diverse botanical profiles
- Less juniper-centric than London Dry
- Interesting for creative cocktails and infusions
- Unique flavor expressions

**Specific gin noted:**
- Hendrick's gin: Cucumber-forward flavor, features cucumber and rose notes, good for herbaceous cocktails and light drinks

**General recommendation:**
Explore different gin styles to find which flavor profiles work best for personal preferences and desired cocktail types.
