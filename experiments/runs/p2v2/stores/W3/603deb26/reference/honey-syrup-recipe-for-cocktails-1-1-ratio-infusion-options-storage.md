**Basic Honey Syrup:**

Ratio: 1:1 honey to water (standard starting point, can adjust)

**Preparation:**
1. Combine honey and water in saucepan
2. Heat over low heat, whisking until honey dissolves
3. Do not boil or burn the honey
4. Cool before using in cocktails
5. Store in fridge for up to 2 weeks

**Variations & Infusions:**

Add during dissolving process:
- Citrus peel (lemon, grapefruit, orange)
- Herbs (lavender, mint, basil, rosemary)
- Spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, cardamom)
- Ginger slices (for honey-ginger syrup - adds spice and warmth)

**Honey Type Selection:**
Different honeys create different flavor profiles:
- Clover honey (classic)
- Manuka honey (stronger flavor)
- Acacia honey (mild, delicate)

**Tips:**
- Choose high-quality, pure honey
- Adjust honey-to-water ratio for desired sweetness level
- Strain if using solid ingredients during infusion
- Use in Bee's Knees, French 75, and other cocktails requiring sweetness
