**Citrus fruits recommended for gin and lavender cocktails:**\n\n1. **Orange** - Classic pairing with lavender; sweetness complements floral notes; try blood oranges for deeper complexity\n2. **Yuzu** - Japanese citrus (grapefruit/orange cross); tartness + subtle sweetness; adds unique exotic flavor\n3. **Kumquat** - Small, sweet citrus; pairs well with lavender; can use kumquat syrup or fresh muddled fruit\n4. **Satsuma** - Type of mandarin; sweet and tart; loose, easy-to-peel skin; helps balance gin bitterness\n5. **Bergamot** - Subtle citrus flavor; often used in tea/perfumery; adds complexity to gin cocktails\n\n**Additional citrus already explored:**\n- Grapefruit\n- Lemon\n\n**Tips for working with citrus in cocktails:**\n- Always use fresh citrus over bottled/frozen juice\n- Experiment with different varieties\n- Don't let citrus overpower other flavors (start small, adjust to taste)\n- Use citrus peels for aroma and flavor (twists, peels)\n- Citrus balances floral and herbal notes in gin-based drinks
