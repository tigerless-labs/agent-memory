**Lavender Syrup Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water  
- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds

Instructions:
1. Combine sugar and water in saucepan, heat until sugar dissolves
2. Add dried lavender buds, remove from heat
3. Steep 10-15 minutes until cool to room temperature
4. Strain through fine-mesh sieve into clean glass bottle
5. Discard lavender buds
6. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks

**Cocktails using this syrup:**
- Lavender Gin Fizz: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, topped with soda water
- Lavender Dream: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup
- Lavender Bee's Knees: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz honey syrup

**Tips:** Start with small amount of lavender syrup (can overpower), pair with citrus like lemon, experiment with different lavender types (English vs French).
