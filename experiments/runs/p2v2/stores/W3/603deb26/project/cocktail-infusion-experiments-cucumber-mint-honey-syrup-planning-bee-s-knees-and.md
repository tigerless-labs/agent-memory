Planning series of cocktail experiments as of Nov 2023:

**Gin infusions:**
- Infuse cucumber and mint separately (not together initially) to control flavor intensity
- Combine infusions in 50/50 ratio initially, then adjust to preference

**Specific cocktails to make:**
- Bee's Knees (gin, honey syrup, lemon juice)
- Pimm's Cup (Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit)
- Lavender Gin Fizz
- Grapefruit Basil Martini

**Homemade ingredients:**
- Making honey syrup (1:1 ratio honey to water, can infuse with flavors)
- Cucumber infusion guidelines: 1-2 cups sliced cucumber per 1 liter gin, 2-5 days infusion
- Mint infusion guidelines: 1/4 to 1 cup fresh mint per 1 liter gin, 2-5 days infusion

Using Hendrick's gin as base for experiments.
