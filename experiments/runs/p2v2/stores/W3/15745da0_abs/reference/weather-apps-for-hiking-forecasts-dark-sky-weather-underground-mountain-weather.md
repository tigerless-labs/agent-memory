## Recommended Weather Apps for Hiking\n\nTop recommendations for checking trail weather forecasts:\n\n1. **Dark Sky** (iOS, Android) - Highly accurate with minute-by-minute forecasts for exact location; especially useful for predicting precipitation\n2. **Weather Underground** (iOS, Android) - Detailed forecasts including hourly and 10-day, radar, satellite imagery, and personal weather station network\n3. **Mountain Weather Forecast** (iOS, Android) - Specifically designed for mountain weather, includes snowfall, avalanche risk, and wind conditions\n4. **OpenSnow** (iOS, Android) - Primarily for skiing but also detailed mountainous region forecasts\n5. **The Weather Channel** (iOS, Android) - Trusted source with detailed forecasts, radar, and satellite imagery\n6. **AccuWeather** (iOS, Android) - Popular app with minute-by-minute forecasts, radar, and satellite imagery\n7. **National Weather Service (NWS)** (iOS, Android) - Official app with critical hazard information\n\n## Selection Criteria\n- Accuracy using multiple data sources\n- Location-specific forecasts\n- Detailed hourly and extended forecasts\n- Weather radar and satellite imagery\n\nUser selected: Dark Sky and Mountain Weather Forecast for personal use.
