In May 2023, actively concerned about grandmother's diet and nutrition. Researching and implementing ways to improve her cognitive health through dietary changes.\n\n**Specific approaches**:\n- Introducing omega-3 rich foods (fatty fish like salmon, tuna, mackerel)\n- Adding antioxidants (berries, spinach, kale)\n- B vitamins (B6, B9/folate, B12 from leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals)\n- Moderate caffeine via coffee/tea\n- Turmeric/curcumin for anti-inflammatory effects\n\n**Methods to increase appeal**:\n- Creating new recipes incorporating healthy foods\n- Blending healthier items into familiar dishes (e.g., spinach in pasta sauce)\n- Improving visual presentation and using appealing dishware\n- Considering texture preferences and potential swallowing difficulties (purees, steaming, roasting)\n- Involving grandmother in meal planning and preparation\n- Creating easy, attractive snacks like apple-almond butter, Greek yogurt with berries, hummus with vegetables, energy balls\n\nApproach emphasizes patience and small incremental changes rather than forcing major diet shifts.
