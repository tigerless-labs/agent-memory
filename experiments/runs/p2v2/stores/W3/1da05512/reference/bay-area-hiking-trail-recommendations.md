**Recently completed:**\n- Mount Tamalpais\n- Vernal Falls (Yosemite)\n\n**Recommended alternatives in Bay Area:**\n\n1. **Muir Woods & Dipsea Trail** - Redwood forests, connects to Mount Tamalpais option or Steep Ravine Trail\n\n2. **Tilden Regional Park (Berkeley Hills)** - Panoramic Bay Area views\n   - Nimitz Way Trail: 4.5 mi one-way, 1,000 ft gain, 3-4 hours, moderate\n   - Wildcat Gorge Trail: 5.5 mi one-way, 1,200 ft gain, 4-5 hours, moderate-challenging\n\n3. **Mount Diablo State Park** - Highest peak at 3,849 ft\n   - 360-degree views of Bay Area, Central Valley, Sierra Nevada\n   - Various trails, 4-10 miles\n\n4. **Sam McDonald County Park** (Santa Cruz Mountains) - Heritage Grove Trail (4.5 mi through redwoods)\n\n5. **Purisima Creek Redwoods** (San Mateo County) - Redwood gem with 4.5-6.5 mile trails\n\n6. **Mission Peak Regional Preserve** (Fremont) - 6.5 mi out-and-back with Bay Area and Diablo Range views\n\n**Trail conditions:** Always check park websites for conditions, permits, and restrictions before heading out.
