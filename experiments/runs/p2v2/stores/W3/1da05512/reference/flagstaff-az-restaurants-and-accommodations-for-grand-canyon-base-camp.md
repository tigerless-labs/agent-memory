Restaurants in Flagstaff, Arizona:
- Late for the Train Cafe - breakfast/lunch, vegan and gluten-free options, signature Train Wrecks breakfast burritos
- Proper Meats + Provisions - gourmet deli, sandwiches, salads, charcuterie
- Fat Olives - Italian cuisine, pasta, pizza, risotto
- The Toasted Owl - brunch, maple bacon waffles, breakfast tacos
- Big Foot BBQ - barbecue, tender meats

Accommodations:
- Little America Flagstaff - mid-range, outdoor pool, free breakfast
- Hotel Monte Vista - historic, rich history, lively atmosphere
- Flagstaff Inn and Suites - budget-friendly
- The Inn at Flagstaff - family-run B&B, homemade breakfast
- Flagstaff KOA - camping

Tips: Book in advance, especially during winter holidays. Check weather forecasts before Grand Canyon visit. Consider America the Beautiful annual pass for national park access.
