**Airlines with Carbon Offset Programs**\n- Qantas (Fly Carbon Neutral)\n- Air New Zealand (FlyNeutral)\n- KLM (CO2ZERO)\n- Lufthansa (Compensaid)\n- SAS - Scandinavian Airlines\n- Virgin Atlantic\n- United Airlines\n\nAirlines with sustainable practices: Norwegian Air, TAP Air Portugal, Finnair, Singapore Airlines\n\n**Eco-Accommodation Platforms**\n- Booking.com (sustainable travel filter)\n- Expedia (sustainable tourism)\n- Airbnb (eco-friendly stays filter)\n- Ecobnb (eco-lodges focus)\n- Glamping.com\n- National Geographic Unique Lodges of the World\n\nLook for certifications: LEED, EarthCheck, GOTS, Bluesign, OEKO-TEX\n\n**Carbon Offset & Sustainability Apps**\n- Carbon Footprint - calculate and offset flights, accommodation, transport\n- Offset - flight emissions offsetting\n- EcoPassenger - transportation emissions calculation\n- Refill - find water bottle refill stations\n- iRecycle - locate recycling centers\n- Zero Waste Travel - tips and resources\n- HappyCow - vegan/vegetarian restaurants\n\n**Transportation & Planning Apps**\n- Citymapper - public transit, walking, cycling routes\n- Moovit - bike-share and transit planning\n- Waze Carpool - carpooling\n- Rome2rio - multi-mode trip planning\n- TripIt - trip planning with carbon suggestions\n\n**Eco-Friendly Gear**\n- Reusable water bottles (BPA-free, durable materials)\n- Beeswax wraps (plastic wrap replacement)\n- Rechargeable power banks\n- Merino wool clothing\n- Reef-safe, mineral-based sunscreen\n- Sustainable luggage (recycled materials)\n- Travel-sized solid toiletries (shampoo bars, toothpaste tablets)
