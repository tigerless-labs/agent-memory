User interested in modern sculptors, particularly Anish Kapoor and his use of reflective surfaces. Recommended contemporary sculptors include:
- Anish Kapoor (large-scale installations, reflective surfaces)
- Rachel Whiteread (space, memory, human experience)
- Richard Serra (site-specific steel installations)
- Jeff Koons (hyper-realistic, popular culture themes)
- Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen (playful, everyday objects)
- Ursula von Rydingsvard (organic forms, nature)
- Barbara Hepworth (modernist, biomorphic forms)
- Tony Cragg (abstract, colorful, surreal)
- Eva Rothschild (space, materiality installations)
- Phyllida Barlow (colorful, playful installations)

Cloud Gate ('The Bean') in Chicago Millennium Park - stainless steel, 168 panels, 110+ tons, 33ft tall x 42ft wide x 66ft long, installed 2004. Mirror-like reflective surface, cleaned daily, most photographed landmark in Chicago.
