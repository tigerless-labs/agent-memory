**Folk Horror Films (similar to Midsommar)**\n- The Wicker Man (1973) - paganism, ritual sacrifice\n- The Devil's Backbone (2001) - haunting ghost story, Spanish Civil War\n- Kill List (2011) - slow-burning, occult\n- A Dark Song (2016) - occult rituals, folk magic\n- November (2017) - black-and-white, 19th-century Estonia\n- Apostle (2018) - Netflix, cults and paganism\n- The Sacrament (2013) - found-footage, cults\n\n**Horror-Comedy Films**\n- Shaun of the Dead (2004) - zombie balance of humor and horror\n- Zombieland (2009) - action-packed zombie comedy\n- Tucker & Dale vs Evil (2010) - hillbilly horror spoof\n- What We Do in the Shadows (2014) - mockumentary, vampires in Wellington, NZ\n- Cooties (2014) - dark comedy with zombie-like children\n- Tragedy Girls (2017) - high school serial killer social media\n- Gremlins (1984) - classic, small town chaos\n- Slither (2006) - alien parasite invasion\n\n**Horror TV Shows**\n- Ash vs. Evil Dead (2015-2018) - Bruce Campbell, Evil Dead franchise\n- What We Do in the Shadows (TV, 2019-present) - vampire mockumentary in NYC\n- Santa Clarita Diet (2017-2019) - suburban couple, zombie wife\n- iZombie (2015-2019) - zombie in coroner's office, procedural\n- Scream Queens (2015-2016) - college serial killer, Ryan Murphy\n\n**Recent Horror (2022 releases)**\n- Barbarian (2022) - Airbnb rental horror\n- X (2022) - remote farmhouse film shoot\n- Scream (2022) - fifth installment, new cast, meta\n- The Black Phone (2022) - boy communicates with dead via mysterious phone\n- Watcher (2022) - psychological, stalking premise
