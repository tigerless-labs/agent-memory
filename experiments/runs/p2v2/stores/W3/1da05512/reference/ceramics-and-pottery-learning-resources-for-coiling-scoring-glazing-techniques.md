Resources for learning ceramics:

Websites/Courses:
- Craftsy - online courses and tutorials on pottery and ceramics
- Pottery Making Illustrated - step-by-step tutorials and videos
- The Potter's Council - pottery tutorials and articles
- Ceramic Arts Network - online community with tutorials and videos

YouTube Channels:
- Simon Leach Pottery - renowned potter with comprehensive tutorials
- Pottery By Osa - variety of pottery tutorials
- The Ceramic School - pottery tutorials by experienced artists

Tips for success:
- Use clay with good plasticity and strength
- Keep coiling and scoring consistent (thickness, shape)
- Score deeply and apply slip evenly for strong bonds
- Keep a glaze journal to track experiments
- Practice patience - improvement comes over time
