**Paul Strand (1890–1976)** — American photographer and pioneer of straight photography. Born in NYC, largely self-taught but influenced by Stieglitz. Joined Photo-Secession in 1915. Known for documentary photography of social issues. Famous for Mexico series (begun 1932). Committed to photography as tool for social change.\n\n**Francis Benjamin Johnston (1864–1952)** — American photographer, one of first women to establish professional career. Known for portraits of politicians/celebrities (Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony) and pioneering architectural photography. Documented historic American buildings to preserve architectural heritage.\n\n**Charles Dudley Arnold (1865–1946)** — American photographer, pioneered aerial photography. One of first to use cameras on balloons and airplanes. Worked with Orville Wright. Also known for innovative nighttime photography using electric lights, gas lamps, flash powder, and magnesium wire to capture dramatic urban scenes.
