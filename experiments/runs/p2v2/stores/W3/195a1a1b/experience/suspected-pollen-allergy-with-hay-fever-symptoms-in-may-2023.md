Experienced symptoms consistent with hay fever/pollen allergy: sneezing, runny or stuffy nose, itchy nose/throat/ears, watery/red/swollen eyes, cough, postnasal drip, fatigue, headache. Was exploring over-the-counter treatments and lifestyle modifications (air filters, saline rinses, avoiding high pollen count days, anti-inflammatory diet). Planned to consult doctor for diagnosis.
