Started reading January 29, 2023. Deeply engaged with the book as of May 2023 — it keeps them up at night.

Loves the character development and the novel's exploration of identity, particularly:
- How Evelyn's identity is performative (public celebrity persona vs. vulnerable private self)
- Themes of intersectionality: as a woman, Latina, and immigrant navigating Hollywood
- Complex relationships between women (Celia, Monique, Lucy)
- Both empowering and limiting aspects of female relationships
- Resonates personally with the universal human experience of having public and private selves

Appreciates Monique as the narrator — she brings an outside perspective as a journalist writing Evelyn's biography and actively uncovers secrets. Her own biases and life experiences influence her perception of Evelyn's story, which adds complexity to the narrative.

Also engaged with broader themes: nature of celebrity and fame, power dynamics between women, societal expectations shaping identity.
