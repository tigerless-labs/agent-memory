Planning to record ukulele performance videos to share online. Using Kala KA-CE15S ukulele and phone camera. First video planned:
- Setting: new music room
- Will position microphone 6-12 inches away from ukulele, slightly off-center
- Will use good lighting (natural light or softbox)
- For first video: planning to do a cover song rather than tutorial (less intimidating, more engaging)
- Target length: 2-3 minutes
- Will include self-introduction

Equipment recommendations: dedicated microphone (e.g., Blue Yeti), pop filter, tripod for stability, external microphone for better audio quality. Will edit using software like iMovie, Adobe Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve.
