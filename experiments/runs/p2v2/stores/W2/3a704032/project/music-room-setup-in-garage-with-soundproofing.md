Setting up a dedicated music room in the garage. Planning to implement soundproofing including:
- Sealing gaps and cracks with acoustic sealant
- Adding mass-loaded vinyl and acoustic panels to walls and ceilings
- Installing solid core/soundproof doors
- Using resilient or hat channels for decoupling
- Considering drop ceiling with sound-absorbing materials
- Addressing floor isolation

Also planning instrument storage with wall-mounted shelves, hooks, and racks to maximize vertical space.
