**Museums Visited or Interested In:**

*The Metropolitan Museum of Art:*
- Large collection spanning 5,000 years of human history
- Over 2 million works of art
- Notable sections: Egyptian art (36,000+ objects), American Wing, Temple of Dendur
- Family-friendly: Audio guides for kids, Discovery Guides, Family Tours, Family Map

*Natural History Museum:*
- Visited for 'Dinosaur Fossils' exhibition
- Features life-sized T-Rex replica
- Hands-on activities section

*Modern Art Museum:*
- Large collection of modern/contemporary art
- Family-friendly activities: Family Tours (ages 5-12), Art Labs, Family Activity Guides

*Other Notable Art Museums:*
- The Guggenheim - Frank Lloyd Wright spiral architecture, modern/contemporary art
- Whitney Museum of American Art - American art from 20th-21st centuries
- Museum of Arts and Design (MAD) - intersection of art, craft, design (3,000+ objects)
- Brooklyn Museum - 5,000 years of human history, diverse collection
- Children's Museum of the Arts - ages 10 and under, interactive exhibits
- Studio Museum in Harlem - African American art and culture
- The Cloisters - medieval art and architecture, upper Manhattan location

*Museums for Lectures/Educational Programs:*
- Museum of History - offers behind-the-scenes conservation lab tours
- Museum of Contemporary Art - hosts lecture series (e.g., feminist art in 1970s)
