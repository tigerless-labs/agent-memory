Resources for learning about art conservation:

**Organizations:**
- American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC) - offers online courses, webinars, publications
- International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC) - features articles, news, events
- Conservation Wiki - collaborative platform with conservation techniques, materials, best practices

**YouTube Channels:**
- Art Conservation
- Conservation Framing
- The Getty Conservation Institute

**Books:**
- "The Conservation of Art and Architecture" by James C. Y. Watt
- "The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory" by Caroline K. Keck
- "Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide" by John H. Stubbs

**Common Adhesives & Consolidants:**
- Acrylic polymers (PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV)
- Animal glues (hide glue, bone glue)
- Cellulose-based adhesives (rice starch paste, wheat starch paste)
- Epoxy resins (Araldite, Epon)
- Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98
- Natural waxes (beeswax, paraffin wax)

**Conservation-Grade Papers:**
- Cotton paper - strong, acid-resistant
- Linen paper - lightweight, tear-resistant
- Rag paper - strong, acid-free
- Kozu paper - Japanese, strong, translucent, used for Asian art
- Gampi paper - Japanese, strong, lightweight
