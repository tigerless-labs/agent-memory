## Books\n- "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard — comprehensive history 1960s-1990s\n- "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss — relationship between feminist art and maternal\n- "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin — essays on representation of women in art\n- "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Broude and Garrard\n- "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls\n\n## Documentaries\n- "!Women Art Revolution" (2010) by Lynn Hershman Leeson — 40+ feminist artists interviewed\n- "The Heretics" (2009) — Heresies Collective documentary\n- "Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991) by Kate Horsfield\n- "Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010) — panel discussion\n- "Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014) — PBS series episode\n\n## Key Artists\nJudy Chicago, Mary Kelly, Miriam Schapiro, Carolee Schneemann, Faith Ringgold, Barbara Kruger, Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell, Nancy Spero, Yoko Ono
