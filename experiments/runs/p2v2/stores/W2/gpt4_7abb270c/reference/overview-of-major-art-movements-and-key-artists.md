**Abstract Expressionism:**
- Artists: Jackson Pollock, Mark Rothko, Willem de Kooning, Franz Kline, Clyfford Still, Ad Reinhardt, Barnett Newman, Robert Motherwell
- Characteristics: Large-scale, bold colors, expressive brushstrokes, emphasis on emotion and gesture

**Other Notable Art Movements:**

*Impressionism:* Claude Monet, Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Mary Cassatt - dreamy, light-filled paintings

*Cubism:* Pablo Picasso, Georges Braque - fragmented objects, geometric shapes

*Surrealism:* Salvador Dalí, René Magritte, Max Ernst - irrational, blurred reality/fantasy

*Pop Art:* Andy Warhol, Roy Lichtenstein, Jasper Johns - celebrates popular culture, consumerism

*Fauvism:* Henri Matisse, André Derain, Maurice de Vlaminck - vibrant colors, expressive brushstrokes

*Minimalism:* Donald Judd, Dan Flavin, Agnes Martin - simplicity, clean lines, essence of form

*Baroque:* Caravaggio, Rembrandt, Vermeer - dramatic lighting, intense emotion, exquisite detail

*Art Nouveau:* Alphonse Mucha, Gustav Klimt, Charles Rennie Mackintosh - sinuous forms, ornate patterns

**Contemporary Artists:**
- Anselm Kiefer - German artist known for large-scale installations exploring history, mythology, human condition
