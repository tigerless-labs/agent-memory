Key figures and movements in feminist art:

**Pioneer Feminist Artists:**
- Judy Chicago - Known for 'The Dinner Party'; explores gender, identity, female experience
- Miriam Schapiro - Incorporated fabric, textiles; challenged traditional notions of femininity
- Carolee Schneemann - Multi-disciplinary artist (performance, film, installation); explored gender, sexuality, body
- The Guerrilla Girls - Anonymous collective using provocative tactics to challenge male-dominated art world

**Foundational Programs:**
- The Feminist Art Program (founded 1970 at California Institute of the Arts) - first of its kind

**Other Notable Feminist Artists:**
- Barbara Kruger - Bold graphic images, text-based works critiquing consumer culture and gender
- Cindy Sherman - Pioneering photographer/filmmaker exploring identity, gender, representation
- Laurie Anderson - Performance artist, musician, filmmaker; explores identity, politics, human experience

**Key Topics in Feminist Art:**
- Challenging traditional representations of gender and identity
- Questioning power structures in the art world
- Exploring female experience and perspectives
- Critiquing consumer culture and social norms
- Performance, installation, and multimedia approaches
