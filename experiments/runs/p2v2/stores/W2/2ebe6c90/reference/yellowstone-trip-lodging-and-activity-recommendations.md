Accommodations recommended: Canyon Lodge & Cabins (heart of park, close to Grand Canyon), Grant Village (Yellowstone Lake shores), Yellowstone Gateway Inn (West Yellowstone, just outside park). Key geysers: Grand Geyser (Upper Geyser Basin, 200 ft eruptions, 7-10 hours), Castle Geyser (10-12 hours), Daisy Geyser (3-4 hours, 80 ft). Hot springs: Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine formations, stroller-friendly), Norris Geyser Basin, Boiling River. Wildlife: best in Lamar Valley, Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area. Keep 100+ yards from bears; early morning/evening best for viewing.
