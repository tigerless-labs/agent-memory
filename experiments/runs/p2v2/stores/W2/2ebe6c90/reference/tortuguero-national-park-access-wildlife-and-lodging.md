Access from San José: (1) Domestic flight to TTQ + boat transfer (1 hour flight + 1-2 hours boat); (2) Shuttle to La Pavona (4-5 hours) + boat transfer (1-2 hours); (3) Private transfer + boat. Book lodges in advance; check weather as boats affected by conditions. Wildlife: howler, capuchin, and spider monkeys; two- and three-toed sloths; toucans, quetzals, macaws; American crocodiles; green iguanas; 1,200+ butterfly species; rare jaguars. Best viewing: early morning/late afternoon with guides. Pack light due to boat transfer.
