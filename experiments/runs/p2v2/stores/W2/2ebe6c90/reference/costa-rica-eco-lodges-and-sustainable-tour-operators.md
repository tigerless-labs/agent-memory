Recommended eco-lodges: Lapa Rios (Osa Peninsula), Monteverde Lodge (cloud forest), Arenal Observatory Lodge (volcano area), Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero), El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula). Tour operators: Costa Rica Expeditions, Osa Conservation, Tortuguero Canals Tours, Selva Bananito Lodge, Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours. Look for CST (Certification for Sustainable Tourism), TIES membership, or ICT certification.
