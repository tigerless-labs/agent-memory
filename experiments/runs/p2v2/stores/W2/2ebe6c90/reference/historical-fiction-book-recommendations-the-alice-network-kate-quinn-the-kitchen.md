Book recommendations given based on interest in historical fiction similar to The Nightingale:\n\n**Specifically interested in:**\n- 'The Alice Network' by Kate Quinn: WWI/WWII-era novel about female spies, strong female protagonists\n- 'The Kitchen House' by Kathleen Grissom: Set in 1800s American South, focuses on complex relationships and character development\n\n**Other recommendations provided:**\n- 'All the Light We Cannot See' by Anthony Doerr: Pulitzer Prize-winning WWII novel\n- 'The Lost Girls of Paris' by Pam Jenoff: WWII-era, women resistance network\n- 'The Women in the Castle' by Jessica Shattuck: WWII, three women's stories\n- 'Those Who Save Us' by Jenna Blum: Mother-daughter story during WWII\n- 'The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society' by Mary Ann Shaffer: Post-WWII\n- 'The Huntress' by Kate Quinn: WWII, three women pursuing Nazi war criminal\n- 'We Were the Lucky Ones' by Georgia Hunter: Polish family during WWII\n\nUser appreciates authors like Madeline Miller and Kathleen Grissom for their lyrical/emotional prose and character-driven storytelling.
