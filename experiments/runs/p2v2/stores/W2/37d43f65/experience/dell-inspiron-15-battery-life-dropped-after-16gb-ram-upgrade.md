## Situation
Battery life on Dell Inspiron 15 5000 (2018) decreased from 6-7 hours to 4-5 hours immediately after upgrading RAM to 16GB.

## Root Cause (TBD)
Likely possibilities:
1. Battery calibration needed after hardware change
2. Increased power consumption from additional RAM
3. More intensive apps running with more available RAM

## Action Taken
User planned to try battery calibration by:
- Letting battery drain to 0%
- Charging to 100%
- Repeating 2-3 times

## Follow-up Needed
Check if battery calibration resolved the issue, and if not, investigate:
- System resource usage patterns
- Power management settings optimization
- Potential BIOS updates
- Actual battery health/degradation
