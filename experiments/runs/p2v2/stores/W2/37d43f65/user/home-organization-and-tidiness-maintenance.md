Prefers organized, clutter-free living spaces. Uses strategies to maintain organization:\n- One-touch rule (put items away immediately after use)\n- Designated landing strip for items that need later attention\n- Regular maintenance (10-15 min daily or 30 min weekly tidying)\n- Home for everything system (assign specific place for each item)\n- Uses storage containers and baskets with labels\n- In-out rule (remove/donate old item when bringing new one in)\n- Keeps frequently used items accessible\n- Manages paper clutter actively\n- Regular surface cleaning\n- Involves household members in maintaining organization
