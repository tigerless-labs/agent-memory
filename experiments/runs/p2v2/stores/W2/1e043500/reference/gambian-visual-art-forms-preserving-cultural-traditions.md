**Visual art forms in Gambian culture:**

- **Batik printing** — wax-resist technique creating traditional patterns  
- **Woodcarving** — statues, masks, utensils representing cultural traditions  
- **Pottery** — coiling and throwing techniques for everyday utensils  
- **Photography** — documenting cultural ceremonies (weddings, naming ceremonies), daily life, clothing, food  
- **Painting** — depicting traditional clothing, food, dance, cultural practices, historical events

Photography especially important for archiving ceremonies and educating younger generations about cultural heritage.

**Recent developments:** Contemporary art forms (installations, performance art, conceptual art), digital technology integration, Pan-African artist collaborations, youth involvement programs.
