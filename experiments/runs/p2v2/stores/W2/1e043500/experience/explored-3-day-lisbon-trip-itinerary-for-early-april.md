Asked about a 3-day Portugal trip, chose Lisbon over Porto. Received an itinerary covering:

**Day 1:** Alfama, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, Tram ride, Baixa, Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square, Chiado  
**Day 2:** Day trip to Sintra (Pena Palace, Moorish Castle), then Belém neighborhood (Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries)  
**Day 3:** Tagus River walk, MAAT museum, Bairro Alto, Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara

Also noted nearby attractions: National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum, LX Factory.
