Creating a content brand that emphasizes hiring great human beings first, then great technologists second. Core values: psychometric testing to identify critical thinkers, EQ valued over IQ in software developers, focus on team building and leadership.

Content niches:
- Remote Work Best Practices
- Remote Team Building  
- Remote Leadership
- Remote Hiring and Onboarding
- Remote Work Tools and Technology
- Software Development Best Practices
- Career Development
- Entrepreneurship and Business
- Emerging Technologies

Content strategy includes: blog posts, podcasts/interviews, webinars, e-books, social media, case studies of team members embodying the values.

Target audience: software development managers and leaders interested in building high-performing teams through human-centric hiring.
