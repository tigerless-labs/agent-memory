In discussion of *Wuthering Heights* (2023-04-28), expressed frustration with Emily Brontë's detailed setting descriptions—found themselves skimming over extensive moor/landscape passages. Stated preference for story and character over 'poetic' embellishment; sees overly ornate prose as distracting from narrative drive rather than enhancing it.

This refines the existing interest in character-driven narratives: likes the *content* but dislikes flowery prose that obscures plot momentum.
