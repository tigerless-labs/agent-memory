**Album:** Give Up by The Postal Service (2003)

**Artists:** Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) + Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

**Standout tracks:**
1. **Such Great Heights** — Dreamy, synth-driven anthem about hopeful new love
2. **The District Sleeps Alone Tonight** — Melancholy, introspective; about disconnection and insomnia
3. **Clark Gable** — Quirky, upbeat; charming lyrics about wooing a romantic interest
4. **We Will Become Silhouettes** — Somber, reflective; about aftermath of nuclear apocalypse

**Why it resonated:** Groundbreaking blend of indie rock and electronic elements. Fresh sound that appealed to both indie and electronic music scenes. Lyrics captured themes of love, loss, and uncertainty for a generation coming of age.
