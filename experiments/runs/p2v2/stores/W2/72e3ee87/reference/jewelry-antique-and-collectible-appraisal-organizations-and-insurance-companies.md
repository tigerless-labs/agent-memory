**Appraisal Organizations & Certifications:**

Jewelry Appraisers:
- NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers)
- AGTA (American Gem Trade Association)
- ASA (American Society of Appraisers)

Antique & Collectible Appraisers:
- AAA (Appraisers Association of America)
- ISA (International Society of Appraisers)
- NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers)

**Insurance Companies:**

Specialty:
- Jewelers Mutual (jewelry, heirlooms)
- Chubb (high-value items, jewelry, art, collectibles)
- AXA ART (art, antiques, collectibles, jewelry)
- Hagerty (collectibles including jewelry, art, antiques)

Standard with Specialty Departments:
- State Farm (high-value items, jewelry, antiques)
- Allstate (Valuable Items policy)
- Liberty Mutual (Valuable Possessions policy)

**Appraisal Report Should Include:**
- Detailed description of item
- Value opinion
- Methodology explanation
- Photos and diagrams
- Proof of ownership documentation
