User currently using Duolingo; seeking supplementary resources.

**Language Learning Platforms:**
- SpanishDict (comprehensive dictionary, grammar guides, verb conjugations)
- Babbel (paid, interactive lessons, conversational focus)
- Rosetta Stone (paid software, interactive lessons, speech recognition)

**Online Courses:**
- Coursera's Spanish for Beginners (5-course specialization, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid)
- edX's Spanish Language and Culture (4-course series, Universidad de Salamanca)

**Video Resources:**
- SpanishDict YouTube Channel
- SpanishPod101 (YouTube, audio and video lessons)
- Coffee Break Spanish (podcast and YouTube)

**Practice/Exchange:**
- Conversation Exchange (language exchange partners)
- Tandem (app for language exchange)
- HelloTalk (language learning app with exchange feature)

**Podcasts:**
- Coffee Break Spanish (conversational lessons, news, culture)
- Spanish Obsessed (lessons, stories, conversations)
- News in Slow Spanish (news in slow, clear Spanish)

**Apps for Practice:**
- Anki (flashcard app for vocabulary/phrases)
- Memrise (language learning with courses and lessons)
- Quizlet (flashcards, tests, study games)
