## Project Scope
- Size: 5×8 feet
- Style: Spa-Inspired Retreat
- Keeping existing tub; adding large walk-in shower with double sink vanity option

## Materials Decided
- Shower walls & floor: Glazed porcelain tile (chosen for glossy finish, scratch/stain resistance, easy cleaning)
- Grout: Cement-based (affordable, all-purpose, requires sealing)
- Waterproof membrane behind shower walls
- Grout sealer to protect against stains and water damage

## Homeowner DIY Level
- Fixed leaky faucet ~3 weeks prior
- Recently rearranged bathroom cabinets
- Willing to tackle renovation work themselves
- Will hire professionals for plumbing and electrical systems
