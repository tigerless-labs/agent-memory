User is weighing whether to write their own book versus hiring a ghost-writer or using AI-generated content. 

Reasons to write own book (from discussion):
- Personal fulfillment and empowerment
- Complete ownership and control over content, tone, and message
- Authenticity — true representation builds credibility and trust
- Improved writing skills and refined voice
- Emotional connection with readers for deeper engagement

Using ghost-writers or AI may result in lack of authenticity, reduced personal connection with audience, and reduced sense of fulfillment.
