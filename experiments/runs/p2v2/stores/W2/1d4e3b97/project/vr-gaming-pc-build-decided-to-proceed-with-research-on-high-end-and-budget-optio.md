## User Commitment
Expressed enthusiasm to start VR gaming PC build in late February 2024.

## High-End Configuration Recommended
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080/3070/3060 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT/6800
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, or i7-11700K
- RAM: 16+ GB DDR4 (high clock speeds, low latency)
- Motherboard: ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX
- Storage: Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 SSD
- PSU: EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X (750W min)

## Budget-Friendly Configuration
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K
- RAM: 8 GB DDR4 (high clock speeds, low latency)
- Motherboard: MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, Gigabyte GA-A320M-H
- Storage: 500GB SSD (WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, SanDisk Ultra 3D)
- PSU: EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

## Key Compatibility Steps
- Check target VR headset recommended specs
- Ensure motherboard supports latest CPUs/GPUs
- GPU + CPU meet or exceed headset requirements
- PSU has sufficient power for all components
- Case fits all components with adequate airflow
- All components compatible with intended OS
