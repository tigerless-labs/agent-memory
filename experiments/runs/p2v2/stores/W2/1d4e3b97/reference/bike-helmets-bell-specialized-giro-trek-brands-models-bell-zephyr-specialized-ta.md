**Bike Helmet Brands and Models**

**Top Brands:**
- **Bell Helmets** - High-quality with MIPS technology
- **Specialized** - Wide range with Tri-Fix web splitter for fit
- **Giro** - Popular with Roc Loc Air fit system and MIPS
- **Trek** - Known for comfort and ventilation with In-Mold construction

**Recommended Models:**
- **Bell Zephyr** - High-end road helmet with MIPS, excellent ventilation
- **Specialized Tactic 3.0** - Versatile for road/mountain/commuter, MIPS technology
- **Giro Synthe** - Premium road helmet with MIPS, aerodynamic design
- **Trek Vapor 3** - Lightweight, ventilated, In-Mold construction

**Price Ranges:**
- Entry-level: 30-50
- Mid-range: 50-100
- High-end: 100-200
- Premium: 200-300

**Selection Tips:**
- Ensure proper fit (snug but comfortable)
- Look for safety certifications (CPSC, Snell, EN 1078)
- Choose good ventilation for comfort
- Consider weight for longer rides
- Try on before purchasing if possible
