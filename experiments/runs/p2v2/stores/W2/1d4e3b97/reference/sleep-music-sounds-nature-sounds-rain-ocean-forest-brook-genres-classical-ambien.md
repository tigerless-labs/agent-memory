**Music and Sounds for Sleep**\n\n**Nature Sounds:**\n- Rainfall (gentle rain)\n- Ocean waves\n- Forest sounds (birdsong, rustling leaves, wind)\n- Babbling brook\n- Wind chimes\n\n**Music Genres:**\n- Classical (Mozart, Chopin, Bach)\n- Instrumental piano (slow, gentle)\n- Acoustic guitar (soft, slow)\n- Ambient (soft, calming textures, minimal melodies)\n- New age (flutes, harps, gentle percussion)\n\n**Artists and Albums:**\n- Brian Eno - Ambient 1: Music for Airports\n- Max Richter - Sleep (8-hour album designed for sleep)\n- Ludovico Einaudi - Nightbook (calming piano)\n- Enya - Watermark (soothing vocals and instrumentation)\n- Steve Reich - Music for 18 Musicians (gentle percussion and melodies)\n\nAvailable on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and other platforms.
