## Original Requirement
Trainees answer survey at course start and end. At course end, trainees view own progress and whole-class results.

## User Stories Generated

### Trainee Stories
1. Access survey at beginning to provide initial feedback/opinions
2. Access same survey at end to provide final feedback/opinions
3. View progress over course to track improvement and identify development areas
4. Compare end-of-course results with initial results to see opinion/attitude changes
5. View entire class survey results at end to compare progress with peers and see group performance

### Instructor Stories
6. Collect survey responses at course beginning and end to track progress and evaluate training effectiveness
7. View individual trainee survey results to provide personalized feedback and support
8. View entire class results to evaluate overall effectiveness and identify improvement areas

## Coverage
Addresses dual survey access, individual progress tracking, class comparison, and instructor oversight capabilities.
