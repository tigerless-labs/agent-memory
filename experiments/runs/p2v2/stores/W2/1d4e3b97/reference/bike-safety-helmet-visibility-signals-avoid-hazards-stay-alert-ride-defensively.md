**Bike Safety Tips for Long Rides**\n\n**Before Riding:**\n- Always wear properly fitted helmet\n- Check tire pressure, brakes, gears\n- Wear reflective clothing and gear\n\n**On the Road:**\n- Follow all traffic laws and signals\n- Ride in same direction as traffic\n- Use designated bike lanes when available\n- Use hand signals to turn, merge, change lanes\n- Maintain safe distance from vehicles and other cyclists\n- Ride defensively - anticipate others' actions\n\n**Hazard Avoidance:**\n- Watch for potholes, cracks, debris\n- Avoid parked cars (risk of "dooring")\n- Use caution in intersections\n- Reduce speed on wet roads\n- Avoid obstacles and construction zones\n\n**Visibility and Communication:**\n- Wear bright, reflective clothing\n- Make eye contact with motorists\n- Be extra cautious at intersections\n\n**Essentials to Carry:**\n- Phone and ID\n- Basic toolkit (pump, tire levers, Allen wrenches)\n- Water and snacks\n- Take regular breaks (hourly)\n\n**Best Practices:**\n- Ride with a buddy when possible\n- Stay alert, avoid phone use and music\n- Maintain consistent sleep and energy levels
