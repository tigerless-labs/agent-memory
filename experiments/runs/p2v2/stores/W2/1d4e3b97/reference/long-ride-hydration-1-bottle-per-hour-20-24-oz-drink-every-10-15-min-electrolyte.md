**Hydration for Long Bike Rides**\n\n**Pre-Ride Hydration:**\n- Drink 16-20 oz water or sports drink 1-2 hours before\n- Ensure pale yellow or clear urine\n\n**During Ride:**\n- Carry at least 1 water bottle (20-24 oz) per hour of riding\n- Sip water every 10-15 minutes\n- Use sports drinks (Gatorade, Nuun) with electrolytes for long/intense rides\n- Avoid caffeine and sugary drinks\n- Monitor urine color - dark yellow indicates dehydration\n\n**Timing:**\n- Take breaks every hour to drink, stretch, and rest\n- Eat hydrating foods pre-ride (watermelon, bananas, energy bars)\n\n**Post-Ride:**\n- Drink water or sports drink to replenish fluids and electrolytes\n- Helps with recovery and reduces muscle soreness\n\n**Weather Adjustments:**\n- Increase hydration in hot/humid conditions\n- More frequent and larger quantities may be needed
