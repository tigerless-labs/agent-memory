**Bike Route Planning and Tracking Apps:**\n\n1. **Ride with GPS** - Route planning and customization, find bike-friendly roads, turn-by-turn directions. (Web, iOS, Android)\n\n2. **MapMyRide** - Route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, large community-driven route database. (Web, iOS, Android)\n\n3. **Strava** - Social network for cyclists with route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, popular routes database. (Web, iOS, Android)\n\n4. **Google Maps** - Bicycle mode shows bike-friendly routes, avoids highways, prefers bike paths and lanes. (Web, iOS, Android)\n\n**Tips for finding bike-friendly routes:**\n- Avoid busy roads with high traffic\n- Choose roads with bike lanes, shoulders, or bike paths\n- Check road surface quality\n- Research local bike laws and regulations\n- Visit local bike shops for recommendations\n- Join online cycling forums and local cycling clubs
