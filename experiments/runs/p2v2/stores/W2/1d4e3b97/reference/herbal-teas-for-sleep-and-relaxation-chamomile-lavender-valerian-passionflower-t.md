**Herbal Teas for Sleep and Relaxation**\n\nRecommended teas with calming properties:\n- **Chamomile**: Soothes nervous system, reduces anxiety, promotes peaceful sleep\n- **Lavender**: Reduces stress and anxiety, promotes relaxation\n- **Valerian root**: Natural sedative, improves sleep quality, calms nervous system\n- **Peppermint**: Can calm mind and body despite association with energy\n- **Tart cherry**: Contains melatonin, helps regulate sleep-wake cycles\n- **Passionflower**: Natural sedative, reduces anxiety and insomnia\n- **Lemon balm**: Reduces stress and anxiety, improves sleep quality\n- **Rooibos**: Caffeine-free, contains antioxidants, promotes relaxation\n- **GABA**: Neurotransmitter that helps regulate sleep and relaxation\n\nBest served 30-60 minutes before bedtime. Choose high-quality, organic brands without added sugars or artificial flavors.
