**Benoit Landron Naturlich 2021**\n\n- Blend: 80% Semillon from organic grower in Bergerac + 20% Folle Blanche\n- Reason for blend: Frost damage in 2021 meant most Folle Blanche was lost, so Semillon substituted\n- Flavor profile: Adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the mix\n- Style: Pet-nat (pétillant naturel), sparkling wine\n- Service: By the glass at 161 wine bar, opened for spring service\n- Quality: User (wine bar owner) confirmed it's delicious, described as "a fun variation on a theme"\n\nReviewed in style of Jancis Robinson with notes of citrus, stone fruits, honeyed sweetness, lively bubbles, zesty acidity, and minerality.
