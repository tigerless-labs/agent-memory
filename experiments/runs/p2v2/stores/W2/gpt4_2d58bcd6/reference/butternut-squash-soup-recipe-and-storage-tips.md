**Simple Butternut Squash Soup Recipe (Serves 4-6)**\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)\n- 2 tbsp olive oil\n- 1 onion, chopped\n- 3 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tsp ground cumin\n- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)\n- 1/2 tsp salt\n- 1/4 tsp black pepper\n- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth\n- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)\n- Fresh herbs (parsley or sage) for garnish\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Roast squash at 400°F for 45-50 min (cut in half lengthwise, seeds removed, cut side up)\n2. Sauté onion in olive oil until softened (5 min), add garlic (1 min)\n3. Add spices, cook 1 min, pour in broth, bring to boil\n4. Add roasted squash flesh to pot\n5. Blend until smooth using immersion blender\n6. Add cream if desired\n7. Season to taste and serve\n\n**Storage Tips:**\n- Refrigerator: 3-5 days in airtight container\n- Freezer: 3-6 months in freezer-safe portions\n- Cool to room temp within 2 hours before refrigerating\n- Reheat to 165°F internal temperature\n- Freeze in individual portions for easy reheating\n\n**Variations:**\n- Add diced apple or chopped kale\n- Use coconut cream or Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream\n- Experiment with nutmeg or cinnamon\n- Use pre-cut/peeled squash to save time
