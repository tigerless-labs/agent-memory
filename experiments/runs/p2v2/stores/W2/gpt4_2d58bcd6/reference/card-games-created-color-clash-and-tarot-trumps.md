**Color Clash** — A card game for 2-6 players using a standard 52-card deck. Players draw from the draw pile and play one card, declaring a suit. Other players must play all cards of that suit from their hand. Scoring based on color/suit matching.\n\n**Tarot Trumps** — A trick-taking game for 2-4 players using a full 78-card tarot deck. Players must follow suit or play a trump. Tarot trumps (The Fool through The World) beat all other cards and are ranked in ascending order. Scoring based on tarot trump cards taken in tricks (The Fool worth 4 points up to The World worth 25 points).
