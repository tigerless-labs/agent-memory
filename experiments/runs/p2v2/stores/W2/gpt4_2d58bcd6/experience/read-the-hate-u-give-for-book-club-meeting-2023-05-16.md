Had to rush to finish 'The Hate U Give' by Angie Thomas for a book club meeting. Was the only person who hadn't finished it by the meeting date, but managed to complete it a few days before. Found the narration in audiobook format (by Bahni Turpin) powerful and emotive, which helped with engagement and connection to characters.
