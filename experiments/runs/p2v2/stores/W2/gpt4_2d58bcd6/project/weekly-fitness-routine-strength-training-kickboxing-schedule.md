Starting new fitness routine incorporating both strength training and existing kickboxing classes.\n\n**Weekly Schedule:**\n- Monday: Upper body strength training (push-ups, dumbbell chest press, rows, tricep dips, bicep curls)\n- Tuesday: Kickboxing class\n- Wednesday: Rest day\n- Thursday: Kickboxing class\n- Friday: Lower body strength training (squats, leg press, lunges, leg extensions, leg curls)\n- Saturday: Rest day\n- Sunday: Core and full-body exercises (planks, Russian twists, leg raises, burpees, mountain climbers)\n\n**Goals:**\n- Incorporate strength training 2-3 times per week\n- Maintain kickboxing on Tuesdays and Thursdays\n- Track progress using Strava app\n- Include outdoor weekend activities (bike rides/hikes with colleagues)\n- Build cardio endurance while improving overall fitness\n\n**Progress tracking:**\n- Using Strava mobile app to track bike rides and hikes\n- Considering creating Strava club for 'The Spikers' team
