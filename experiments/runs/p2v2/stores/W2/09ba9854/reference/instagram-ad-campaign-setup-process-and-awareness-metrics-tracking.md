## Instagram Advertising Reference

**Setup Steps**:
1. Convert Instagram account to Business Account (Settings > Account > Switch to Business Account)
2. Create Facebook Ads Manager account  
3. Set up Ad Account in Ads Manager with payment method
4. Choose Campaign Objective (Awareness, Consideration, or Conversion)
5. Define Target Audience in Ads Manager
6. Create Ad with chosen format (photo, video, carousel, stories)
7. Set Budget (daily or lifetime) and Schedule
8. Launch and monitor performance

**Ad Objective Choice**: 'Awareness' for increasing visibility and reach of new projects

**Key Awareness Campaign Metrics**:
- **Reach**: Total people who saw the ad
- **Impressions**: Total number of times ad was displayed
- **Frequency**: Average times each person saw the ad (monitor for ad fatigue)
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, saves, shares
- **CPM**: Cost per thousand impressions (efficiency metric)
- **Ad Recall Lift**: How well people remember the ad/brand
- **Brand Awareness Lift**: Increase in brand awareness (measured via surveys)
- **Social Media Engagement**: Followers, organic likes/comments/shares

**Tracking Tools**:
- Facebook Ads Manager (detailed performance metrics)
- Instagram Insights (organic performance)
- Third-party: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Agorapulse (additional insights)

**Analysis Questions**:
- Are reach and impressions increasing over time?
- Is engagement rate improving?
- Is cost per thousand impressions decreasing?
- Are people remembering the brand/ad?
