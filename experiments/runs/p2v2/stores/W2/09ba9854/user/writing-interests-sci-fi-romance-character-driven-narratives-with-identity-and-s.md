## Writing & Story Preferences

**Genres**: Sci-fi romance with emphasis on complex character development

**Key Themes**: 
- Identity and self-discovery
- Emotional vulnerability and authenticity  
- Slow-burn romantic relationships built on trust and mutual respect
- World-building with clear rules and technological/magical systems

**Character Development Priorities**:
- Multi-dimensional characters with contradictory traits
- Rich backstories influencing motivations
- Relatable emotional struggles despite extraordinary circumstances
- Character-driven plot (plot serves character arcs)
- Explores how characters navigate high-stakes situations with emotional intelligence

**Narrative Elements Preferred**:
- Plot twists that challenge character identity
- Symbolic elements representing character journeys
- Themes woven throughout via world-building, symbolism, and character arcs
- Avoid clichés and overused character tropes

**Story Ideas Interested In**:
- 'The Memory Thief' (completed character development)
- 'The Royal Affair' (interspecies romance with political complexity)
