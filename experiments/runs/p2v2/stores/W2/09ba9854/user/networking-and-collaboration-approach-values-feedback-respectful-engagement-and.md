## Networking & Collaboration Style

**Approach**:
- Attends seminars and workshops to learn and connect with like-minded individuals
- Prepares elevator pitch for projects
- Values receiving and giving constructive feedback
- Seeks collaboration opportunities while respecting others' boundaries and time

**Key Principles**:
- Be clear about what you're asking for (feedback, collaboration, guidance)
- Show genuine interest in others' work before asking for help
- Be open to criticism and value their input
- Offer value in return (skill swap, connections, genuine acknowledgment)
- Don't be pushy; respect people's decisions if they decline
- Always follow up with new connections to maintain relationships

**Preference**: Face-to-face networking at seminars and co-working spaces for feedback and collaboration
