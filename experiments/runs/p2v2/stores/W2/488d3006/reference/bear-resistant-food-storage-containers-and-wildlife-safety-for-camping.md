Wildlife safety for camping, particularly bear prevention:

**Bear-Resistant Containers Recommended:**
- Garcia Bear-Resistant Container - durable plastic, meets IGBC standards
- BearVault - heavy-duty plastic, easy to use and secure
- Ursack - lightweight, puncture-resistant material, good for backpackers
- Ursack Major - bear-resistant bag for food/scented items
- Counter Assault Bear Bag - heavy-duty material, easy and secure
- Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag - lightweight, compact, puncture-resistant

**Food & Trash Storage Rules:**
- Use airtight containers to seal odors
- Store food/scented items (toothpaste, soap, lotion) at least 100 yards from campsite
- Store in: vehicle with closed windows/doors, bear locker/box (if available), or tree 10+ feet high, 4+ feet from trunk
- Hang food from tree using sturdy rope if necessary (10+ feet up, 4+ feet out)
- Never leave trash or dirty dishes out overnight
- Pack all trash, keep trash sealed and clean
- Use bear-resistant trash cans if available

**Additional Wildlife Safety:**
- Keep campsite clean
- Clean cooking area and utensils immediately after use
- Check with park rangers about recent bear activity before camping
- Make noise while hiking to avoid surprising bears
