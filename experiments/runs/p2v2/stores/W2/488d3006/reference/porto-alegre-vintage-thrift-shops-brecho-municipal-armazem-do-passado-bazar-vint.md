Vintage and thrift shops in Porto Alegre recommended for exploration:

1. **Brechó Municipal** - Municipal marketplace with used clothes, shoes, accessories. Known for affordable prices.
2. **Armazém do Passado** - Vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor items. May carry vintage vinyl records (Brazilian music, rock, pop).
3. **Bazar Vintage** - Stylish, fashion-forward vintage/retro clothing and accessories.
4. **Casa da Sogra** - Thrift store specializing in retro/vintage home decor, furniture, accessories. May carry vintage vinyl records.
5. **Pinga do Brechó** - Vintage/thrift store with clothing, accessories, kitchenware. Prices somewhat higher but reasonable.

Note: Prices vary by condition and rarity. Brechó Municipal is most affordable. Armazém do Passado and Bazar Vintage offer more curated, trendy selections.
