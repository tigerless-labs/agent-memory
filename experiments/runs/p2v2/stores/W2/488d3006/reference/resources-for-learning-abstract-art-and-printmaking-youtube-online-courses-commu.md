Resources for learning abstract art and printmaking:

**YouTube Channels:**
- The Art Sherpa - step-by-step abstract art tutorials for beginners
- Artists Network - abstract art lessons and demonstrations
- Abstract Art with Deb Watson - abstract art tutorials and tips
- Printmaking with Zeke - printmaking tutorials and demonstrations
- The Printmaking Studio - printmaking lessons and tips

**Online Courses & Platforms:**
- Skillshare - courses on abstract art and printmaking
- Craftsy - classes on printmaking, abstract art, mixed media
- Creativebug - video classes on various art techniques including abstract art and printmaking

**Communities & Forums:**
- Reddit: r/AbstractArt and r/Printmaking
- Facebook groups dedicated to abstract art and printmaking

**Websites & Magazines:**
- The Art of Abstract - comprehensive guide to abstract art history, techniques, styles
- Abstract Art Academy - video lessons and tutorials
- Printmaking 101 - guide to printmaking techniques, materials, resources
- The Printmaking Council of New Jersey - tutorials, videos, resources
- The Jealous Curator - blog featuring abstract art, printmaking, mixed media
- Printmaking Today - magazine dedicated to printmaking

**Printmaking & Art Supply Brands:**
- Speedball, Dick Blick, Daniel Smith (printmaking)
- Golden Artist Colors, Liquitex (abstract art supplies)

**Online Art Supply Stores:**
- Dick Blick Art Materials
- Jerry's Artarama
- Cheap Joe's Art Stuff
- Hyatt's All Things Art
