Must-see attractions: Strip District (street food, Primanti Bros sandwiches), Andy Warhol Museum (largest single-artist museum in US), Mount Washington (Duquesne/Monongahela inclines for city views), Phipps Conservatory. Other: North Shore (PNC Park, restaurants), Three Rivers Heritage Trail (24-mile trail system), Carnegie Science Center. Best neighborhoods to stay: Oakland, Shadyside. Transportation: Port Authority transit ($2.50 one-way, $5.50 day pass), free transit zone downtown, Healthy Ride bike-share, Uber/Lyft. Flight from NYC: ~1.5 hours, $150-250. Nearby historic sites: Monastery of Veruela, Castle of Trasmoz.
