Zion National Park recommended attractions and hikes:

**Must-See Attractions:**
- The Narrows - scenic hike along Virgin River through narrowest part of Zion Canyon (get wet/wade)
- Angels Landing - iconic rock formation with spectacular views (steep, narrow final ascent)
- Emerald Pools - three pools/waterfalls along Kayenta Trail (relaxing, swimming opportunity)
- Weeping Rock - unique formation with dripping springs and vegetation (short, easy hike)

**Popular Hikes:**
- Angel's Landing: 5 miles, strenuous, steep drop-offs and narrow ridges
- West Rim Trail: 10 miles, stunning canyon and Virgin River views
- Observation Point Trail: 8 miles to viewpoint with panoramic views
- Kayenta Trail: 2 miles to Emerald Pools and Weeping Rock
- Watchman Trail: 3 miles to viewpoint overlooking Springdale

**Practical Tips:**
- Use free shuttle service throughout park
- Check park website for permits/restrictions
- Bring plenty of water and sunscreen (intense Utah sun)
- Wear sturdy hiking shoes with good traction
- Stay on designated trails, respect wildlife, pack out all trash
