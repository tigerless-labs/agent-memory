Circular hiking trail in Natural Park of Moncayo mountain, Aragón, Spain. Distance: ~36km. Duration: 2-3 days. Best seasons: autumn (colorful leaves) and spring (wildflowers). Highlights: Moncayo summit, Fuenfría waterfall, meadows, forests, rock formations. Also available: camping sites, rural cottages in nearby towns (Tarazona, Veruela, Borja). Activities: hiking, wildlife watching, mountain biking, skiing in winter.
