Adding Ethiopia to travel bucket list. Primary interest: experiencing local culture and traditions. Interested in Ethiopian festivals: Timkat (January 19th - baptism celebration with processions and water splashing), Meskel (September 27th - True Cross commemoration with bonfires and traditional dancing), Enkutatash (September 11th - Ethiopian New Year with feasting and dancing), Fasika (April - Orthodox Easter with processions and feasting). Food interests: Injera (flatbread staple), Doro Wot (spicy chicken stew), Tibs (sautéed meat dish), Kitfo (raw beef), Shiro (chickpea/lentil stew), traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony. Also interested in other African destinations: Djibouti, Namibia, Madagascar, Mozambique, Mauritania, Senegal.
