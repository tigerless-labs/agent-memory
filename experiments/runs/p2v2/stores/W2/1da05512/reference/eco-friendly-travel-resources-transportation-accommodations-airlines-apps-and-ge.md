**Sustainable Transportation:**\nPublic transit (Moovit, Transit apps), walking/cycling, electric taxis, car-sharing (Zipcar, Car2Go), ride-sharing (Waze Carpool, BlaBlaCar), electric scooters (Bird, Lime)\n\n**Eco-Friendly Accommodations:**\nBooking.com Sustainable Travel, Expedia Sustainable Tourism, Airbnb Eco-Friendly Stays, Ecobnb, Glamping.com, National Geographic Unique Lodges\nCertifications: LEED, EarthCheck\n\n**Airlines with Carbon Offset:**\nQantas, Air New Zealand, KLM, Lufthansa, SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United Airlines. Also: Norwegian Air, TAP Air Portugal, Finnair, Singapore Airlines\n\n**Eco-Friendly Travel Apps:**\nCarbon offset: Carbon Footprint, Offset, EcoPassenger\nAccommodations: Ecobnb, Eco Hotels of the World, BookDifferent\nWaste reduction: Refill, iRecycle, Zero Waste Travel\nTransportation: Citymapper, Moovit, Waze Carpool\nOther: HappyCow (vegan/vegetarian), EcoChallenge, Susty\n\n**Travel Gear:**\nReusable water bottles, beeswax wraps, solar chargers, merino wool clothing, reef-safe sunscreen\nCertifications: Bluesign, GOTS, OEKO-TEX
