Recommended trails in Bay Area for solo day hiking:\n- Tilden Regional Park (Nimitz Way Trail 4.5 mi, Wildcat Gorge Trail 5.5 mi)\n- Berkeley Hills with panoramic views\n- Mount Diablo State Park (3,849 ft, 4-10 mile options)\n- Sam McDonald County Park (Santa Cruz Mountains)\n- Purisima Creek Redwoods\n- Mission Peak Regional Preserve (6.5 miles)\n\nAll have varying difficulty levels and trail conditions to check before visiting.
