**Selected: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp**\n- Distance: 20 miles\n- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet\n- Difficulty: Moderate\n- Elevation: 10,300 feet\n- Starting point: Tuolumne Meadows\n- Amenities: tent cabins, meals, restrooms, showers, potable water\n- Access: 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows or shuttle from visitor center\n- Permits required through Yosemite Conservancy\n\n**Other Options Considered:**\n- High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft gain - more challenging)\n- Glen Aulin and May Lake (25 miles, 2,500 ft gain)\n- Emigrant Wilderness - Emigrant Lake (20 miles, 2,000 ft gain)\n- Rae Lakes Loop (45 miles, 6,000 ft gain)
