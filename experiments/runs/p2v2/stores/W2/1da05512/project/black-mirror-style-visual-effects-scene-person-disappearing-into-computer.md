Creative project exploring unsettling visual effects inspired by Black Mirror.

Scene concept: Girl sits down in front of computer and slowly disappears into the computer just before speaking into a microphone.

Technical approach for the effect:
- Use slow, deliberate camera movements for anticipation and tension building
- Add eerie sound effects (low hum, distant whispers) to create foreboding
- Use dim lighting, shadows, and backlighting for moody atmosphere
- Visual distortion effects: swirling motion, flashes of light, disorienting camera movements
- Glitchy interface effects on the computer screen
- Cut to close-ups of computer buttons/screens after disappearance to suggest something happening within

Goal: Create sense of unease and mystery that leaves viewers unsettled and curious.
