Board game recommendations given for family gatherings with mixed ages:

User chose:
- Ticket to Ride: train-themed strategy game, easy to learn, suitable age 8+
- Pictionary: drawing game, suitable age 8+

Other recommended games:
- Scattergories: creative word game, age 10+
- Cranium: drawing, acting, trivia, wordplay, age 10+
- Settlers of Catan: strategy game, age 10+
- Clue: detective mystery game, age 8+
- Uno: simple card game, age 6+
- Forbidden Island/Forbidden Desert: cooperative games, age 10+
- Apples to Apples: party/matching game, age 10+
- Guess Who: guessing game, age 6+
