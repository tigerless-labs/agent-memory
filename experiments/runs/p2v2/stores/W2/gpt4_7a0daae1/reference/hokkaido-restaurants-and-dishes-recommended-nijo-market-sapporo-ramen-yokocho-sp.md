Hokkaido dining recommendations for foodie visit:

Restaurants and Markets:
- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo): fresh seafood including crabs, sea urchin, squid
- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho: ramen shops and local ramen varieties
- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo): local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs
- Daruma (Sapporo): Genghis Khan (grilled lamb)
- Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa): Genghis Khan
- Yurigakuro (Sapporo): Ishikari Nabe (hot pot)
- Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru): Ishikari Nabe

Must-try dishes:
- Genghis Khan Yaki: grilled lamb/mutton with onions, vegetables, savory sauce
- Kaisendon: rice bowl with fresh sashimi (salmon, tuna, squid)
- Jingisukan: Japanese Mongolian barbecue with grilled lamb/mutton
- Uni ikura don: rice bowl with sea urchin and salmon roe
- Ishikari Nabe: hot pot with salmon and vegetables (winter specialty)
- Local ice cream flavors: lavender, melon, cheese (Hokkaido dairy specialty)
