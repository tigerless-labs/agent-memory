Recommended worsted weight yarns for knitting projects:

Malabrigo Worsted: soft with subtle sheen, great stitch definition, wide color range

Cascade 220: classic and affordable, soft and warm, easy to work with, wide color range

Plymouth Yarn Company Worsted: soft with subtle texture, great stitch definition

Berroco Ultra Alpaca: soft lightweight yarn with subtle sheen, good for sensitive skin, lighter weight option

Tips when working with yarn:
- Always swatch before starting to ensure correct gauge
- Choose appropriate needles for yarn weight and type
- Maintain even tension while knitting
