Dropper post: a seatpost that allows quick height adjustment while riding.

Function:
- Uses hydraulic or cable-actuated system  
- Lowers saddle typically by 100-150mm
- Controlled by lever or button
- Can be adjusted on the fly without stopping

Benefits for mountain biking:
- Increased control and confidence on technical descents
- Improved clearance from saddle to ground
- Better handling on rough terrain and tight corners
- Enhanced ability to ride aggressively on downhills
- Maintains pedaling efficiency when raised for climbing
- Convenient operation with remote lever

Considerations:
- Adds some weight to bike
- Requires regular maintenance and lubrication
- Must verify compatibility with bike frame
- Valuable upgrade but can be added later if budget constrained
