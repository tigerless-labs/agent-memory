Key factors to consider when purchasing a mountain bike:

Wheel sizes:
- 26 inches (older standard, tight trails)
- 27.5 inches (versatile all-around)
- 29 inches (fast and stable)

Suspension types:
- Hardtail (front suspension only, good for smooth trails)
- Full suspension (front and rear, better for technical trails)

Frame materials:
- Aluminum (lightweight, affordable)
- Carbon fiber (lightweight, high performance)
- Steel (durable, comfortable)

Components to consider:
- Gearing: wide range for climbing and descending
- Brakes: disc brakes recommended
- Tires: aggressive tread for technical, smooth for cross country
- Dropper post: allows quick saddle height adjustment for improved control

Beginner recommendations:
- Hardtail or entry-level full suspension
- Relaxed head angle geometry
- Lower standover height
- Shorter top tube

Important: Always test ride before purchasing and visit local bike shops for recommendations.
