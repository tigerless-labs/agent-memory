Resources for finding mountain biking trails:

Websites and apps:
- Trailforks
- MTB Project  
- TrailLink
- IMBA Epics designation system

Mountain bike festivals and events:
- Sea Otter Classic (California)
- Crankworx (Whistler and other locations)
- USA Cycling events

Local resources:
- REI (Recreational Equipment Inc.)
- Performance Bicycle
- Trek Bicycle Stores
- Giant Bicycle
- Local independent bike shops

Tips for trail selection:
- Search for loops or out-and-back routes
- Consider difficulty ratings and elevation gain
- Check trail conditions and regulations
