Historic landmarks in Granada offering city views:

Alhambra: Ancient fortress and palace complex built during Moorish period (13th-14th centuries). Offers breathtaking views of city and Sierra Nevada Mountains. Most-visited landmark. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Word derived from Arabic meaning the red one due to reddish walls.

Mirador de San Nicolas: Viewpoint on Albaicin hill with panoramic views of city, Alhambra, and Sierra Nevada Mountains.

Sacromonte Abbey: Monastery on hilltop with panoramic views of Granada and surroundings.

El Salvador Church: Bell tower offers bird's eye view of city center.

Albaicin Quarter: Narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings with various vantage points for city views.

Legends associated with Alhambra:
- Fountain of Lions: legend says drinking from it makes you love Granada
- Tower of Seven Floors: said to be haunted by ghost of imprisoned princess
- Hall of Abencerrajes: believed to be massacre site with bloodstains
- Devil's Megaphone: location with perfect acoustics in Generalife gardens
