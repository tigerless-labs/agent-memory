Cross-cultural exchange program organizations:\n\n1. AFS Intercultural Programs: Large non-profit providing intercultural learning for students. Offers student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering in 50+ countries. Website: https://afs.org/\n\n2. Experiment in International Living: Nonprofit offering immersive cultural exchange for high school and college students. Programs include host family stays, local school attendance, community service. Website: https://www.experiment.org/\n\n3. Youth for Understanding (YFU): International educational organization for exchange programs for students and young adults. Offers year-long exchanges, semester and summer programs in 40+ countries. Website: https://www.yfu.org/\n\n4. Cross-Cultural Solutions: Nonprofit providing volunteering for all ages in Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru. Focus on community development and cross-cultural exchange. Website: https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/\n\nThese organizations provide opportunities for personal growth, language learning, cultural understanding, and building global connections.
