Cross-cultural learning book recommendations:\n\n- The Namesake by Jhumpa Lahiri: explores identity, family, and culture through Bengali-American family story\n- The Kite Runner by Khaled Hosseini: childhood friends in Afghanistan, themes of friendship, betrayal, redemption\n- The Joy Luck Club by Amy Tan: Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers\n- Things Fall Apart by Chinua Achebe: Nigerian tribe before and after British colonization\n- Persepolis by Marjane Satrapi: graphic novel, young Iranian girl growing up during Islamic Revolution\n\nMovies:\n\n- Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon: martial arts epic set in ancient China, loyalty and honor\n- The Lives of Others: German film set in Cold War East Berlin, surveillance and human connection\n- Lion: true story, Indian boy lost and adopted by Australian family, journey to find birth family\n- The Motorcycle Diaries: travel diaries of Che Guevara, journey across South America, poverty and injustice\n- City of God: Brazilian film, favela life, poverty, violence, survival\n\nNote: these resources help build understanding of different cultural experiences and perspectives.
