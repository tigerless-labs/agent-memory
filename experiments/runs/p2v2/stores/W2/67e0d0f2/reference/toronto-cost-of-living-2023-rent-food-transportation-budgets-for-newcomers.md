Toronto cost of living estimates (May 2023):\n\nRent: 1-bedroom shared 1200-1600/month, 1-bedroom high-rise 1600-2000/month, 2-bedroom 1800-2500/month, 3-bedroom 2500-3500/month\n\nFood: Groceries 500-750/month, casual dining 10-15 per meal, mid-range 20-30 per meal\n\nTransportation: TTC monthly pass 156, single fare 3, bike share 15-20/month\n\nUtilities: Hydro/internet 100-200/month, phone/internet 50-100/month\n\nEmployment: Ontario minimum wage 15/hour, entry-level 35000-50000/year\n\nRecommendations: share housing to split costs, explore Toronto Affordable Housing Office, Rent Supplement Program, YMCA/Salvation Army subsidized housing
