User is interested in meditation for mental health benefits (stress reduction, focus, emotional regulation, resilience). Expressed concerns about having time and discipline for daily practice.

Discussion revealed:
- Even a few times per week can provide benefits
- Consistency matters more than duration
- Recommended beginner meditation types: mindfulness meditation, guided meditation (apps like Headspace or Calm), loving-kindness meditation
- Start with 5-10 minutes and gradually increase
- Key is finding a practice that works for individual schedule and preferences
