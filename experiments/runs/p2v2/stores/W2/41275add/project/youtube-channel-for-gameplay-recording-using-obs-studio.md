Planning to start YouTube channel recording gameplay footage. Evaluated screen recording software options including OBS Studio, XSplit, ShadowPlay, Bandicam, and others. Decided on OBS Studio due to:\n- Free and open-source\n- Large community with extensive plugin support\n- Highly customizable for creating unique channel aesthetic\n- Well-optimized for AMD Ryzen 9 5900X and NVIDIA RTX 3080 hardware\nPlanning to invest in quality microphone and webcam for audio/video quality.
