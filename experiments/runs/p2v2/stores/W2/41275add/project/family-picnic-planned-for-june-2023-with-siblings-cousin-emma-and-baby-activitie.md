## Planned Family Picnic (June 2023)

**Attendees:**
- User's mom
- User's siblings (bringing instruments for jam session)
- Cousin Emma (bringing new baby)
- Kids

**Planned Activities:**
- Scavenger hunt (finding nature items)
- Outdoor games (badminton, cornhole, tag)
- Family jam session with kid-friendly songs and activities
  - Call-and-response activities
  - Movement/dancing
  - Shaker instruments for kids to play along
  - Simple narrative storytelling through music
- Photo booth with props

**Clothing Plan:**
- Matching yellow shirts and light brown pants (adjusted from white for stain resistance)
- Comfortable shoes for outdoor activities

**Accommodations for Baby:**
- Create quiet, shaded area for feeding/soothing
- Plan quieter activities around baby's nap schedule
- Bring baby-friendly toys (soft blocks, rattles)
- Designate baby buddy to help cousin Emma
- Be flexible and prepared for unexpected needs
