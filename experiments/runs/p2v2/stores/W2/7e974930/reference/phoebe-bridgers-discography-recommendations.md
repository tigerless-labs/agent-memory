**Popular tracks:**\n- "Scott Street" - hauntingly beautiful\n- "Killer" - critically acclaimed\n- "Motion Sickness" - explores love/loss/introspection\n- "Funeral" - emotive storytelling\n- "Kyoto" - poignant lyrics with soaring vocals\n\n**Albums:**\n- Stranger in the Alps (2017) - debut, critically acclaimed introspective songwriting\n- Punisher (2020) - sophomore, more experimental and introspective\n- Boygenius (2018) - self-titled EP with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocals\n\n**Collaborations:**\n- Better Oblivion Community Center (2019) - with Conor Oberst\n\n**Recommendation:** Start with Stranger in the Alps for introduction to her work.
