**Popular tracks:**\n- "Appointments" - critically acclaimed, introspective songwriting with soaring vocals\n- "Turn Out the Lights" - title track, poignant lyrics and hauntingly beautiful\n- "Rejoice" - explores love/loss/introspection\n- "Souvenir" - emotive storytelling\n- "Hurt Less" - poignant lyrics with soaring vocals\n\n**Albums:**\n- Sprained Ankle (2015) - debut, critically acclaimed introspective songwriting and soaring vocals\n- Turn Out the Lights (2017) - sophomore, more experimental and introspective\n- Boygenius (2018) - self-titled EP with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocals\n\n**Other releases:**\n- "Red Door" (2019) - single\n- "Favor" (2020) - single\n\n**Live performances:** Known for emotive live performances, check NPR Tiny Desk Concert and KCRW Morning Becomes Eclectic.
