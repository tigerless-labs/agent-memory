After discussing pros and cons of using a strike indicator with Woolly Bugger streamers, decided to fish without one initially. Reasoning: will rely on the marabou tail's movement and action to detect takes, and wants to develop better feel and sensitivity as an angler. Plans to experiment and adjust presentation (retrieve speed, depth, cast angle) if not getting takes.
