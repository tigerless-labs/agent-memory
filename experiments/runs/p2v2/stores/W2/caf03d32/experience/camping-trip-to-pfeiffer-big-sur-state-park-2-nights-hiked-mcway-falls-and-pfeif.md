Multi-day camping trip to Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. Stayed 2 nights under the stars. 

Day 1: Hiked to McWay Falls
Day 2: Shorter hike to Pfeiffer Beach

Lessons learned:
- Tried cooking camping food for the first time (results were okay)
- Brought portable power bank (proved to be lifesaver for device charging, navigation, and photo capture)
- Attempted astrophotography at night but results didn't meet expectations; still learning technique
- Plans to improve astrophotography skills on future Big Sur trips

Overall: Found dark skies at Big Sur ideal for stargazing and astrophotography practice
