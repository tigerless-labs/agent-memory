Ways individuals can support criminal justice reform efforts:

1. Get educated: Learn about history of war on drugs, racial disparities in criminal justice system, impact on communities of color

2. Speak up: Advocate for change through supporting reform candidates, speaking out against racial injustice

3. Support organizations: Donate to, volunteer with, or spread awareness of criminal justice reform organizations focused on drug policy, police reform, and racial justice

4. Vote: Cast ballot in local, state, and national elections for officials who prioritize criminal justice reform

5. Practice allyship: If not from affected community, support those impacted by listening to experiences, amplifying voices, working together for change

Context: Criminal justice reform includes shifting from punitive measures toward rehabilitation and treatment, expansion of drug courts, diversion programs, ending mandatory minimums for drug offenses, and community policing strategies that build trust.
