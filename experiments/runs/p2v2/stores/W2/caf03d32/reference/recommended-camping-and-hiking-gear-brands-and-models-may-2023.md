Portable Stoves: MSR PocketRocket 2 (lightweight, compact, fuel-efficient), Jetboil Flash Cooking System (all-in-one: burner, pot, cooking vessel; fast and efficient)

Camping Lanterns: Black Diamond Apollo (bright, compact, battery-powered, red light mode), Coleman Classic Camp Lantern (classic, reliable, affordable, warm light)

Hiking Boots: Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof (recommended for day hiking, durable and comfortable)

Sleeping Pads: Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite, REI Co-op Trailbreak

Camping Chairs: REI Co-op Camp X Chair, Coleman Camping Chair

Water Filters: LifeStraw, Sawyer Mini

Trail Cameras (hunting): Bushnell Core DS No Glow, Bushnell Prime L10, Moultrie A-20, Reconyx HyperFire 2, Browning Dark Ops HD Max

Fishing Lures (trout): Woolly Bugger and Muddler Minnow recommended for river fishing with streamers
