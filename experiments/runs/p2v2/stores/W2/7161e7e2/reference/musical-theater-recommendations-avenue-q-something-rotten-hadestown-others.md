Recommended musicals with similar irreverent style to The Book of Mormon:\n- **Avenue Q** — irreverent, hilarious, about puppets in NYC, similar style to The Book of Mormon\n- **Something Rotten!** (2015 Broadway cast recording available on Spotify/Apple Music/Amazon Music)\n- **Hadestown** — folk-opera retelling of Orpheus & Eurydice myth, won 2019 Tony for Best Musical and Grammy for Best Musical Theater Album\n- **The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee** — charming, quirky musical comedy\n- **Hamilton** — groundbreaking, hip-hop/jazz/blues/R&B score\n- **The Producers** — classic Mel Brooks musical comedy\n- **Urinetown** — satirical musical comedy about capitalism and government\n- **Be More Chill** — sci-fi comedy with electronic-pop score\n- **Dear Evan Hansen** — heartwarming about social media and mental health\n- **The Band's Visit** — poignant, beautiful, Egyptian police band visits Israel\n\nUser planning to start with Avenue Q and check out Something Rotten! cast album.
