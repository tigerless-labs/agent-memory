Canister Filter Recommendations for 20-gallon tank:

Hydor Professional (150 model) — very quiet, easy maintenance, modular design, 150-200 dollars

Eheim Classic (2211 model) — well-respected, 100-150 dollars

Fluval 206 — compact, quiet, 100-150 dollars

Fish Food Brands:

San Francisco Bay Brand — frozen brine shrimp from Great Salt Lake, nutrient-rich

Hikari — well-known, range of frozen foods

Argent — sustainable sources, high quality

Omega One — specializes in nutrient-rich foods, omega-3 fatty acids

Tetra — reliable pellet food (user's current staple)

User chose Hydor Professional as their upgrade choice.
