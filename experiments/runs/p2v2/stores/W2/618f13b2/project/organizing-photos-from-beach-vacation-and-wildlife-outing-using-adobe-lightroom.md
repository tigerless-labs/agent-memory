In May 2023, organizing and editing photos from two recent trips:\n\n1. **Beach Vacation**: Photos taken during beach vacation, including sunrise and sunset shots\n2. **Wildlife/Scenery Outing**: Over 200 photos from a nature outing, with subject categories including landscapes, wildlife, and flora\n\n**Organization approach (Adobe Lightroom)**:\n- Created separate collections for each photo type (Landscapes, Wildlife, Flora)\n- Created Smart Collections for beach vacation with date range and location-based filtering\n- Using hierarchical folder structure for organization\n- Tagging photos with relevant keywords\n- Using star ratings to identify favorite photos\n\n**Editing**: Adjusting exposure, contrast, and recovering shadow details to enhance vibrancy.
