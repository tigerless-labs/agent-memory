**BBQ Sauce Sweet/Tangy Balance Tips**\n\n**Sweet Ingredients:**\n- Brown sugar, honey, molasses, maple syrup, pineapple, apples\n- Starting ratio: 1:1 to 1:2 (sweet to tangy)\n\n**Tangy Ingredients:**\n- Vinegar (apple cider, balsamic, white)\n- Citrus juice (lemon, lime, orange)\n- Tomatoes\n- Start small and adjust - they overpower quickly\n\n**Balancing Techniques:**\n1. Taste as you go and adjust\n2. Start with a base recipe and tweak ratios\n3. Consider BBQ style (Kansas City, Carolina, Texas) and meat type\n4. Experiment with sweet:tangy ratios (2:1, 3:1, 4:1)\n5. Be mindful of acidity levels\n6. Add umami depth with soy sauce, Worcestershire, or mushroom ingredients\n7. Let sauce sit for hours or overnight for flavors to meld\n\n**Grilling for Char:**\n- **High heat:** 350-400°F (175-200°C) for initial searing\n- **Initial sear:** 2-3 minutes per side depending on thickness\n- **Finish:** Move to cooler area (225-250°F) to prevent burning\n- **Technique:** Oil grates, don't press down, rotate and flip regularly\n- **Patience:** Char development takes time\n\nSmoked paprika adds excellent smoky depth to BBQ sauce without overpowering other flavors.
