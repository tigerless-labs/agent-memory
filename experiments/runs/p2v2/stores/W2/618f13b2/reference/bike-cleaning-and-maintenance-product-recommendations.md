Product brands recommended for bike maintenance (May 2023):

**Bike wash soap**: Finish Line, Pedro's, Simple Green, Muc-Off

**Wheel cleaners**: Pedro's Wheel Cleaner, Finish Line Wheel Cleaner, Muc-Off Wheel Cleaner

**Chain lube and waterproofing**: 
- Squirt Long Lasting Chain Lube
- Finish Line Wet Lube
- Pedro's Chain Lube with XRP
- CeramicSpeed UFO Chain Treatment
- Squirt Ceramic Chain Lube
- Muc-Off Ceramic Lube

**Waterproofing sprays**: WD-40 Bike Water Repellent, Finish Line Waterproofing, Muc-Off Waterproofing

**Bike storage solutions**: Saris, Thule, Topeak, Delta Cycle, Feedback Sports
