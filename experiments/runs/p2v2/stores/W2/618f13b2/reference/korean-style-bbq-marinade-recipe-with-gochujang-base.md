**Basic Korean-Style BBQ Marinade Recipe**\n\nFor 1-2 pounds of meat:\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 2 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste)\n- 2 tablespoons soy sauce\n- 2 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tablespoon grated ginger\n- 1 tablespoon brown sugar\n- 2 tablespoons sesame oil\n- 1 teaspoon Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) - optional\n- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar - optional\n- Black pepper to taste\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Blend all ingredients until smooth (using blender or food processor)\n2. Taste and adjust to preference\n3. Place meat in ziplock bag or shallow dish\n4. Pour marinade over meat and massage to coat evenly\n5. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, ideally 6-8 hours overnight\n\n**Variations:**\n- Use honey, maple syrup, or Korean rice syrup (yutjang) for different sweetness\n- Add sliced onions, green onions, or grated carrots\n- Add fish sauce, soybean paste (doenjang), or mushroom ingredients for umami\n- Marinate up to 24-48 hours for more intense flavor\n\n**Common Korean BBQ styles:**\n- **Bulgogi**: Sweet and savory, soy sauce and sugar focused\n- **Galbi**: Spicy and savory, gochujang and garlic focused\n- **Dak galbi**: Spicy and sweet, gochujang and sugar focused
