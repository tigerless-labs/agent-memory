Started street photography in May 2023. Attended photography workshop May 1-2, 2023 focused on street photography. Purchased 50mm f/1.8 prime lens on April 10, 2023. Took sunset photos at Red Rock Canyon on April 12, 2023. Incorporates photography into daily routine—takes camera to work, photographs people during morning commute. Interested in candid portrait photography and learning to approach strangers ethically to ask permission for photos. Wants to improve composition skills, especially capturing candid shots, gestures, and expressions in public spaces. Key interests: observation, anticipation of moments, understanding light, using negative space.
