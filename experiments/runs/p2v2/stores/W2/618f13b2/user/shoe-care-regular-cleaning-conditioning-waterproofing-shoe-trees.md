Prioritizes shoe maintenance and longevity. Uses or plans to use:

**Cleaning:**
- Mild soap and water solution for regular cleaning
- Soft cloths and soft-bristled brushes
- Specialized shoe cleaning products (Jason Markk, Sneaker Cleaner recommended)
- Gentle approach—avoids harsh chemicals and abrasive materials

**Conditioning & Protection:**
- Leather cleaning kits for leather shoes (interested in Saphir, Kiwi, Lexol brands)
- Waterproofing spray (Scotchgard, Crepe Protect, Nikwax) to protect from water and stains
- Leather conditioner or oil for leather boots to keep supple
- Cedar shoe trees for boots to maintain shape and absorb moisture

**General practices:**
- Cleans shoes regularly to prevent dirt/stain buildup
- Air dries shoes naturally at room temperature
- Tests new products on inconspicuous areas first
- Believes proper care extends shoe lifespan significantly

Interested in learning proper shoe care techniques to protect investments.
