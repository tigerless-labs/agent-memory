Completed a solo day hike to Mission Peak in Fremont on April 10, 2023. Enjoyed the experience and learned valuable lessons for preparation:

**Preparation needs for next hike:**
- Oil bike chain and adjust derailleur before rides
- Check tire pressure
- Inspect brake pads
- Ensure all bike bolts and quick releases are tightened
- Bring basic tool kit (pump/CO2, multi-tool, spare tubes)
- Pack sufficient water (2-3 liters) and snacks
- Bring map, compass, GPS
- First aid kit
- Sunscreen and sun protection (hat, sunglasses)
- Extra layers and jacket
- Headlamp/flashlight
- Emergency shelter (space blanket)
- Personal identification and emergency contacts

**Solo hiking safety:**
- Notify someone of planned route and expected return time
- Hike during daylight hours, avoid peak sun (11am-3pm)
- Wear visible/reflective clothing
- Follow Leave No Trace principles
- Stay alert and aware of surroundings
