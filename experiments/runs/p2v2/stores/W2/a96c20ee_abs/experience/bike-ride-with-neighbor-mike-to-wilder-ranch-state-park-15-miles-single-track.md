Went on a bike ride with neighbor Mike to Wilder Ranch State Park. Completed approximately 15 miles of single-track trails. The ride was challenging on the bike's components.

**Maintenance needed after this ride:**
- Oil the chain (single-track trails are tough on bike components)
- Adjust derailleur
- Check derailleur limit screws and alignment
- Inspect and clean components

**Preparation for future rides:**
- Consider taking bike to local shop for professional tune-up if not comfortable with adjustments
- Bring basic tool kit on rides
- Tire pressure check before rides
- Brake pad inspection before rides
