## Blue Cheese Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Inspiration:** Derived from a blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle discovered at a local chocolate shop. Recipe developed after experimenting with balance—initial attempts were too pungent before achieving the final sweet-savory blend.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup blue cheese, crumbled (use high-quality)
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat to 375°F (190°C), line baking sheet with parchment
2. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt
3. Cream butter, sugar, and blue cheese until fluffy
4. Beat in eggs and vanilla
5. Mix in flour gradually
6. Fold in chocolate chips
7. Drop by tablespoon onto sheet
8. Bake 12-15 min until edges golden
9. Cool 5 min on sheet, then transfer to rack

**Notes:** Fine crumble the blue cheese for even distribution. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer. Best served as sophisticated dessert or dinner party offering.
