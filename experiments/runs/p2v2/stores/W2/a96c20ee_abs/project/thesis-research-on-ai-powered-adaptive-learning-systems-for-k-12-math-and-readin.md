Presented poster on thesis research at first research conference (mentioned as being 'over the summer'). Research investigated effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student outcomes.

**Key Findings:**
- AI-powered adaptive learning systems can effectively improve student learning outcomes in math and reading
- Implementation and classroom integration require careful planning - this is critical to success

**Common Challenges Identified:**
1. **Teacher professional development**: Teachers need ongoing training to effectively use systems; many felt they lacked necessary skills and knowledge
2. **Curriculum alignment**: When systems aligned clearly with curriculum and learning goals, teacher adoption and effective use increased significantly
3. **Algorithmic bias**: Potential for bias in algorithms to exacerbate existing inequities if not addressed
4. **Equity concerns**: Systems may widen achievement gaps if not designed inclusively and without addressing access barriers

**Recommendations Made:**
- Educators and policymakers should prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support
- Developers should work closely with educators during design to ensure systems meet real classroom needs
- Clear guidance needed on how systems align with curriculum and learning goals
- Ongoing evaluation and assessment of system effectiveness is essential

**Stakeholder Feedback:**
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers, who appreciated the nuanced approach
- Developers expressed interest in findings and how to apply them
- Some disagreement encountered about teacher role: concerns that systems would replace teachers vs. viewing them as support tools

**Explored Areas:**
- Potential to promote equity and inclusion, reduce achievement gaps
- How systems could provide personalized support to struggling students
- Real-time feedback mechanisms and their impact on learning
- Critical thinking and problem-solving skill development
- Collaborative problem-solving facilitation via AI

**Conference Experience:**
- Attended research conference at Harvard University to present and explore AI in education projects
- Directly observed interesting projects at the conference
