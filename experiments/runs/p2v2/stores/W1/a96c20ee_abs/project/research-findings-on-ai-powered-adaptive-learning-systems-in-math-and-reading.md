Research focused on effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student outcomes in math and reading comprehension. Found systems can be effective but implementation needs careful consideration. Key challenges: need for ongoing teacher professional development, lack of teacher skills/knowledge, need for clear curriculum alignment. Success factor: clear curriculum alignment increases teacher adoption. Potential for reducing achievement gaps and supporting struggling students, but concerns about algorithmic bias potentially exacerbating inequities. Recommendations: prioritize teacher professional development, developers work closely with educators, provide curriculum alignment guidance, ongoing evaluation needed, address algorithmic bias.
