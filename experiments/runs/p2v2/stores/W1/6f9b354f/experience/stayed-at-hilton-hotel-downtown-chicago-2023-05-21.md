Checked out of Hilton Hotel in downtown Chicago on 2023-05-21. Room was on 14th floor with amazing night views and small balcony. Impressed by the fitness center (used twice during stay). Explored nearby restaurants (Intelligentsia Coffee, The Berghoff, Girl & the Goat, etc.), observation decks (Willis Tower Skydeck, 360 CHICAGO), running routes (Lakefront Trail, Millennium Park), and planned to take Chicago's First Lady Cruises architectural cruise.
