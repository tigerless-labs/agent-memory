Recently tidied living room and working on keeping it clutter-free using strategies like 'one-touch rule' and designated storage.\n\nPlans for living room:\n- Add low-maintenance plants in big windows (considering: Snake Plant, ZZ Plant, Spider Plant, Pothos, Succulents, Dracaena, Peace Lily, Bromeliads)\n- Install small desk or console table near window for workstation/reading nook (considering: wall-mounted desk, narrow console table, drop-leaf table, ottoman with tray, or foldable desk)\n- Add small circular rug (3-4 feet diameter) under desk to define workspace and add warmth\n- Add small circular mirror (12-18 inches diameter) above desk to add visual interest and make space feel larger\n- Add artwork or prints above desk—considering botanical, abstract, typography, landscape, or pop art styles from retailers like Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble\n\nGoal: maintain clutter-free, organized, warm, and visually interesting space.
