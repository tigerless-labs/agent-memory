Prefers streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) for:
- Ad-free viewing experience
- Ability to pause, rewind, fast-forward at will
- Resume feature (pick up exactly where you left off)
- Binge-watching full seasons at once
- Mobile access (phone/tablet)
- Larger content catalog (international shows, classic movies, shows that aired before)
- Convenience and flexibility

Does not watch cable TV. Values uninterrupted viewing without commercial breaks.
