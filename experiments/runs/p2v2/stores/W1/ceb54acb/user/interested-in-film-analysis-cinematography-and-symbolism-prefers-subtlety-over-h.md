Appreciates:
- Technical filmmaking elements (direction, cinematography, editing, music, acting)
- Meaningful symbolism in film (e.g., use of oranges in The Godfather)
- Examples: The Shining, Blade Runner, Get Out, The Matrix

View: Symbolism adds depth but isn't required for greatness. If overused or too obvious, it becomes distracting and makes films feel contrived or pretentious. Prefers thoughtful, sparing use that enhances rather than overshadows the story.
