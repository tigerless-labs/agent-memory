**Recommended by Claude on 2023-05-22 as character-driven fantasy/sci-fi with strong narration:**

- The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude) - Character-driven sci-fi following a diverse ship crew
- The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (narrated by Simon Vance) - Fantasy con artist tale with witty dialogue
- The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison (narrated by Kyle McCarley) - Court politics and magic
- The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard (narrated by Anne Flosnik) - Post-apocalyptic Paris with fallen angels
- The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker (narrated by George Guidall) - Historical fantasy in early 20th-century NYC
- The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty (narrated by Soneela Nankani) - Con artist fantasy with Middle Eastern/South Asian mythology
- The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) - Fantasy novella with god training narrative
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) - Chinese history-inspired military academy fantasy

All noted for strong character development, world-building, atmosphere, and complex themes.
