**Recommended by Claude on 2023-05-22 for self-improvement and productivity:**

**Self-Improvement:**
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey (narrated by Stephen Covey) - Classic personal development
- Daring Greatly by Brené Brown (narrated by Brené Brown) - Vulnerability and wholehearted living
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck (narrated by Bernadette Dunne) - Growth mindset concept

**Productivity:**
- Deep Work by Cal Newport (narrated by Jeff Bottoms) - Focused work in distracting world
- Essentialism by Greg McKeown (narrated by Greg McKeown) - Prioritization and eliminating non-essential
- Atomic Habits by James Clear (narrated by James Clear) - Building habits and breaking bad ones

**Other:**
- Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari (narrated by Derek Perkins) - History of humankind
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle (narrated by Eckhart Tolle) - Present moment mindfulness
- Shoe Dog by Phil Knight (narrated by Norbert Leo Butz and Phil Knight) - Nike memoir

User planning to start with The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People and Atomic Habits. Listening strategy: start at comfortable speed (1x or 1.25x) and adjust as needed, with note-taking for retention.
