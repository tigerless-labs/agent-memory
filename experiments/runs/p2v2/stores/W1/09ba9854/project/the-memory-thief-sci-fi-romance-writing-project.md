User is writing a sci-fi romance story titled **The Memory Thief**.\n\n**Protagonist**: Lyra, a skilled memory thief with fragmented or missing memories of their own past.\n\n**Plot**: Lyra is hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO. As they progress, they uncover clues about their own past, leading to self-discovery.\n\n**Key themes**: Identity, self-discovery, authenticity of memory and experience, nature of identity in world where memories can be manipulated.\n\n**Planned plot twist**: Revelation of Lyra's true past and the reason behind their memory loss, forcing them to re-evaluate their sense of self and decide their future path.\n\n**World-building**: Advanced neurotechnology enables memory extraction and manipulation. Memories can be extracted, stored, and sold on black market. Government regulates memory trade but corruption exists.
