Recipe collection shared:

Blueberry Muffins:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (or whole wheat flour for healthier option)
- 0.5 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 0.5 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen
- Bake at 400F for 20-25 minutes

Healthy Oatmeal Cookies:
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 0.5 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp baking soda
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp cinnamon
- 0.25 cup coconut oil, melted
- 0.25 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 0.5 cup raisins (or chocolate chips as substitute)
- Bake at 350F for 10-12 minutes

Notes: Whole wheat flour makes denser muffins; may need honey/maple syrup for added sweetness
