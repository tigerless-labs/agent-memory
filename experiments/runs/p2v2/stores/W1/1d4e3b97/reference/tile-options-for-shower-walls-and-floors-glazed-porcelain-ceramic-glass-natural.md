**Shower Wall Tiles:**
- **Glass tile**: Water-resistant, easy to clean, aesthetic appeal, various colors/textures
- **Ceramic/Porcelain**: Durable, water/stain resistant, affordable, wide range of styles
- **Natural stone** (marble, travertine, limestone): Luxurious but requires more maintenance and higher cost

**Shower Floor Tiles:**
- **Porcelain/Ceramic**: Slip-resistant, durable, matte/textured finish preferred for safety
- **Mosaic tile**: Slip-resistant due to small pieces, various materials available
- **Quarry tile**: Natural clay/shale, durable and slip-resistant for high-traffic areas

**Key factors:** Slip-resistance (especially for floors), water absorption rate, maintenance requirements, durability.

User chose: Glazed porcelain tile for both walls and floor.
