**Grout Types:**
- **Cement-based**: Most common, suitable for most glazed tile, prone to staining, requires sealing. Affordable.
- **Epoxy-based**: Two-part (resin + hardener), more durable, stain-resistant, more expensive, stronger odor
- **Acrylic**: Water-based, easy cleanup, dries quickly, flexible, resistant to cracks
- **Urethane**: Two-part, flexible and durable, stain/crack resistant, good for high-traffic areas
- **Silicone**: Flexible, used for joints/corners with movement, not for entire walls/floors

**Grout Color & Finish:**
- Choose color to complement or contrast tile
- Sanded finish: More durable, for high-traffic areas
- Unsanded finish: Better for walls, less wear

**Mixing Cement-Based Grout:**
1. Follow manufacturer instructions for water-to-powder ratio
2. Use clean, potable water
3. Mix in well-ventilated area
4. Achieve toothpaste/thick batter consistency
5. Mix in small batches to avoid waste
6. Avoid over-mixing (causes weakness)

**Applying Cement-Based Grout:**
1. Prepare area: clean, dry, no old adhesive
2. Remove spacers/wedges
3. Use grout float at 45° angle, press firmly
4. Fill joints completely without overfilling
5. Work in small sections (~4 sq ft)
6. Clean as you go
7. Allow 10-15 min to set
8. Clean with damp sponge, rinse thoroughly
9. Apply grout sealer after curing

**Key tip for glazed porcelain:** Use grout sealer to protect from stains and water damage.
