**Producer:** Benoit Landron\n**Wine:** Naturlich pet-nat\n**Vintage:** 2021\n**Blend:** 80% Semillon (from an organic grower in Bergerac), 20% Folle Blanche\n\nBackground: 2021 was a difficult frost year. Landron was hit by severe frost damage that destroyed approximately 80% of the expected Folle Blanche crop. He sourced organic Semillon from Bergerac to compensate, creating a variation on the typical blend.\n\n**Tasting notes:** Fleshy stone-fruit notes from the Semillon, crisp minerality from the Folle Blanche. Described as "a fun variation on a theme" and appropriate as a spring wine.\n\n**Service:** Served by the glass at 161 wine bar starting in spring (noted as of Feb 2024).
