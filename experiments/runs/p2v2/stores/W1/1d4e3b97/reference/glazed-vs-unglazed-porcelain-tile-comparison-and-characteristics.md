**Glazed Porcelain Tile:**
- Aesthetics: Shiny, glossy finish with colored/patterned options
- Durability: Glaze layer protects against scratches, stains, fading
- Cleaning: Smooth surface makes cleaning easy
- Moisture: Highly water/moisture resistant (ideal for showers)
- Cost: Generally more expensive than unglazed
- Slip-resistance: Varies by product; some options available

**Unglazed Porcelain Tile:**
- Aesthetics: Natural, matte finish with rustic/industrial look
- Durability: Less protected; more prone to staining/etching
- Cleaning: Requires more frequent cleaning but less prone to staining
- Traction: Rougher texture provides better grip
- Cost: Generally more affordable
- Slip-resistance: Better naturally due to texture

**When to choose each:**
- Glazed: For sleek, modern aesthetics; low maintenance preference; better stain protection
- Unglazed: For rustic look; prioritizing slip-resistance; budget constraints; willing to maintain more

**User's choice:** Glazed porcelain tile preferred for glossy finish, durability, stain resistance, and easy cleaning.
