Working to improve kimchi recipe after first attempt (weekend of 2023/05/21) was too spicy and overly salty.

**Adjustments in progress:**
1. Rinse finished kimchi under cold running water to remove excess salt
2. Switch to gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) for better flavor control and balance
3. Add garlic and ginger for depth and balance

**Measurement guidelines learned:**
- Gochugaru amounts (per 2 cups vegetables):
  - Mild: 1/4–1/2 tsp
  - Medium: 1/2–1 tsp  
  - Hot: 1–2 tsp
  - Extremely hot: 2+ tsp
- Garlic: 2–3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables (minced or crushed)
- Ginger: 1–2 inches per 2 cups vegetables (peeled, grated or sliced thinly)

**Key techniques:**
- Measure by weight when possible (more accurate than volume)
- Taste as you go — easier to add than remove
- Use fresh, high-quality ingredients
- Allow proper fermentation time; flavor develops over time

Chose gochugaru specifically for its medium-to-hot heat level with balanced flavor profile.
