Getting back into reading graphic novels. Particularly enjoys series combining science fiction/fantasy with character-driven storytelling and moral complexity.

Inspired by *Saga* by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples.

Recommended similar titles:
- Monstress (Marjorie Liu, Sana Takeda) — fantasy epic with supernatural abilities
- The Wicked + The Divine (Kieron Gillen, Jamie McKelvie) — mythology meets pop culture
- Black Hammer (Jeff Lemire, Dean Ormston) — superhero deconstruction
- The Umbrella Academy (Gerard Way, Gabriel Bá) — dysfunctional superhero family
- Descender (Jeff Lemire, Dustin Nguyen) — sci-fi with young robot protagonist
- The Goddamned (Jason Aaron, R.M. Guéra) — dark biblical/mythological setting
- Y: The Last Man (Brian K. Vaughan, Pia Guerra) — post-apocalyptic exploring gender and power
