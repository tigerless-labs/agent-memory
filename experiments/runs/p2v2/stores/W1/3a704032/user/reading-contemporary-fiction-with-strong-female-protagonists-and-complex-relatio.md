Enjoys contemporary fiction exploring identity, celebrity culture, and female relationships. Started reading *The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo* by Taylor Jenkins Reid on January 29, 2023. Particularly drawn to character-driven narratives that explore the tension between public personas and private selves, intersectional identity, and complex female friendships.

Appreciates stories featuring:
- Strong female protagonists with depth and agency
- Exploration of identity and performance
- Complex, nuanced relationships between women  
- Themes of power, privilege, and marginalization
- Character intersectionality (e.g., Latina immigrant experiences)

Recommended titles in this category:
- The Devil Wears Prada (Lauren Weisberger)
- The Mothers (Brit Bennett)
- The Girls (Emma Cline)
- The Interestings (Meg Wolitzer)
- Conversations with Friends (Sally Rooney)
- The Female Persuasion (Meg Wolitzer)
- The Last Romantics (Tara Conklin)
- The Age of Light (Whitney Scharer)
