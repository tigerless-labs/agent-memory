Brands recommended for sustainable, ethical, and affordable clothing:\n\n- **Pact** — organic cotton clothing for men, women, children; fair labor practices\n- **H&M Conscious** — sustainable collection with organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel\n- **Everlane** — transparent supply chain, affordable quality basics, recycled synthetics and organic cotton; good men's selection\n- **Patagonia** — outdoor clothing; organic cotton, recycled polyester, hemp; mid-range pricing\n- **Levi's** — denim made from recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton\n\nEach offers mid-to-affordable pricing with commitment to sustainability and ethical production.
