Prefers to organize shoes by type first, then by color within each type for easy access. Uses waterproofing sprays (Scotchgard, Crepe Protect, Nikwax) to protect shoes from water and mud. Uses cedar shoe trees for leather boots to maintain shape and prevent creasing. Uses leather-specific cleaning kits (brands like Saphir, Kiwi, Lexol) for brown leather boots and similar leather shoes. Stores out-of-season shoes in storage bins to free up rack space. Cleans shoes regularly to maintain their appearance and extend lifespan.
