Prefers to use gentle, pH-neutral bike wash soaps specifically designed for bikes to protect paint and components. For wheels, uses dedicated wheel cleaners or gentle degreasers. Prefers soft-bristled brushes and microfiber cloths for cleaning. Considers waterproofing products like Squirt Long Lasting Chain Lube, Finish Line products, Pedro's offerings, Muc-Off products, WD-40 Bike sprays, and CeramicSpeed UFO treatments for drivetrain protection against corrosion and water.
