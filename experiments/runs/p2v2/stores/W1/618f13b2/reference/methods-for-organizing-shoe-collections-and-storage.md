Popular shoe organization methods:

**By Type:**
- Heels (high, mid, low)
- Flats (ballet, loafers, sandals)
- Boots (ankle, knee-high, over-the-knee)
- Sneakers (running, basketball, casual)
- Sandals (flat, wedged, sporty)
- Dress shoes (formal, semi-formal)

**By Occasion:**
- Work/school
- Formal events
- Casual outings
- Outdoor activities
- Date night

**By Season:**
- Winter, Spring/Fall, Summer

**By Color:**
- Neutrals, Brights, Pastels, Metallics

**By Frequency of Use:**
- Daily wear
- Occasional wear
- Special occasion

**Hybrid approach:**
- Type + Color
- Type + Occasion

**Storage tips:**
- Store out-of-season shoes in storage bins or containers
- Use shoe racks, shelves, or cubbies to maximize vertical space
- Use shoe trees or stuff with paper to maintain shape
- Keep frequently worn shoes in easy-to-reach locations
- Store less-used shoes toward back or higher shelves
- Keep shoes in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
