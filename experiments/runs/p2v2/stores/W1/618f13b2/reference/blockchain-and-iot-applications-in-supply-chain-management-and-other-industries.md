Key applications of blockchain and IoT technologies:

**Supply Chain Management:**
- Blockchain for enhanced transparency and traceability of products throughout lifecycle
- IoT sensors for real-time tracking of goods (location, temperature, humidity)
- Combined use for improved supply chain sustainability and ethical sourcing verification
- Automation and digitization to reduce errors and lower costs

**Companies using these technologies:**
- Walmart: blockchain for food product origin tracking
- Coca-Cola: blockchain for bottling equipment tracking
- IBM and Maersk: TradeLens platform for global shipping visibility
- Nestle: IoT sensors for product quality and safety during transport
- Provenance: blockchain platform for product origin tracing

**Other industries with potential:**
- Healthcare: securing patient data and medical supply chain logistics
- Finance: secure and verifiable transactions
- Agriculture: traceability and waste reduction
- Energy: IoT for consumption monitoring and efficiency
- Manufacturing: inventory management and error reduction

**Risks and concerns:**
- Security vulnerabilities to hacking and cyber-attacks
- Interoperability issues between different systems
- Regulatory uncertainties (still early stage technologies)
- Privacy concerns with large data collection
- Need for proper security measures and protocols
