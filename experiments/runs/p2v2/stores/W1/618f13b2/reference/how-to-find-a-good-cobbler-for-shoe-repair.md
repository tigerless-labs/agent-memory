Methods to find a reliable cobbler for shoe repair:

**Online search:**
- Google 'cobblers near me' or 'shoe repair near me' with city/zip code
- Check Yelp, Google Maps, Bing Places
- Search Facebook, Twitter, Instagram for local cobblers

**Review sites:**
- Angie's List
- Thumbtack
- TaskRabbit

**Professional associations:**
- National Shoe Repair Association (NSRA)
- Shoe Service Institute of America (SSIA)
- These often have member directories

**Local recommendations:**
- Ask friends, family, colleagues
- Ask local shoe store associates
- Visit local shoe stores and ask for referrals

**Vetting a cobbler:**
- Check reputation through reviews and ratings
- Ask about experience with similar repairs (heel repair, leather work)
- Verify they work with your material (leather, suede, etc.)
- Get price quotes and compare with other cobblers
- Ask about turnaround time and rush services
- Look for warranty/guarantee on work
