Recommended shoe cleaning kit brands for different shoe types:

**General use:**
- Jason Markk: popular, eco-friendly, suitable for various shoe types
- Saphir Medaille d'Or: high-end premium option suitable for leather shoes
- Kiwi: well-known brand with range of cleaning kits for different shoe types

**Leather-specific:**
- Saphir: high-quality, gentle cleaning products for leather
- Kiwi: offers range of kits for suede, nubuck, and other leather types
- Lexol: popular for leather cleaning and conditioning

**Specialty:**
- Dr. Martens Shoe Care: for Dr. Martens and similar shoes
- Jason Markk: also works well for canvas and suede shoes

**Waterproofing spray brands:**
- Scotchgard: well-known for waterproofing various materials
- Crepe Protect: popular for suede and nubuck shoes
- Nikwax: water-based, suitable for canvas and suede
