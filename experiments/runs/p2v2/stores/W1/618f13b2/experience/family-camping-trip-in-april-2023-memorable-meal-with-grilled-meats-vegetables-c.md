Family camping trip (early April 2023) featured: grilled pork sausages with father's smoked-paprika BBQ sauce, grilled vegetables, potatoes, corn on the cob, and homemade marshmallow s'mores made by mother. The smoked paprika addition to the BBQ sauce was a highlight that enhanced the flavor profile of the grilled meats. Shared meal in nature with family was a memorable bonding experience.
