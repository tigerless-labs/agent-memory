Seeking strategy to enter the Indian market for high-voltage EV fuses. Key strategic considerations identified: (1) Price sensitivity - competitive pricing critical; (2) Regulatory standards - must meet Indian automotive standards and acquire certifications; (3) Localized production or partnerships - reduce costs, ensure timely delivery, gain competitive edge; (4) Distribution network - must be robust across diverse/vast market, leverage established distributors and e-commerce; (5) Marketing - highlight safety, reliability, performance; use digital/social media; (6) After-sales service - establish service centers, train local technicians, critical for brand loyalty. India market differs from Japan/other markets due to price sensitivity, diverse demographics, infrastructure development, and unique regulatory environment.
