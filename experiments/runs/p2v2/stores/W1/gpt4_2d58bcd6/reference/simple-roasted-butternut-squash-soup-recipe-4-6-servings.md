**Ingredients:** 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs), 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 onion (chopped), 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper, 4 cups broth (chicken or vegetable), 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional), fresh herbs for garnish.\n\n**Instructions:** (1) Roast squash at 400°F for 45-50 min; (2) Sauté onion/garlic in olive oil; (3) Add spices and broth, bring to boil; (4) Add roasted squash; (5) Blend until smooth; (6) Stir in cream if desired; (7) Season to taste; (8) Serve with fresh herbs.\n\n**Storage:** Refrigerate 3-5 days in airtight container, freeze 3-6 months. Cool quickly to room temperature within 2 hours before storing. Can add diced apple or kale for variation.
