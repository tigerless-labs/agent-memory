**Contemporary/YA Authors recommended for similar reading interests:**\n\n**Jason Reynolds** - 'Long Way Down' (lyrical, violence cycle), 'All American Boys' (police brutality), 'Look Both Ways' (varied forms)\n\n**Nicola Yoon** - 'The Sun is Also a Star' (identity, family, romance), 'Everything, Everything' (lyrical prose)\n\n**Renee Watson** - 'Piecing Me Together', 'Betty Before X' (Black female experiences, social justice)\n\n**Ibi Zoboi** - 'American Street', 'Pride' (Haitian-American, beautifully written)\n\n**Dhonielle Clayton** - 'The Belles', 'Shattered Midnight' (fantasy, identity, power)\n\n**Tomi Adeyemi** - 'Children of Blood and Bone' (African mythology, fantasy)\n\n**Sabaa Tahir** - 'An Ember in the Ashes' (Roman-inspired fantasy, social commentary)\n\n**Nnedi Okorafor** - 'Who Fears Death', 'Binti' (African culture, speculative fiction)\n\nThese authors excel at blending genres, character depth, social justice themes, and diverse perspectives.
