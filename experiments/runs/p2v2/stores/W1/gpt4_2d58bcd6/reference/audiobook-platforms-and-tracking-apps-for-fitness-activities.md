**Audiobook Platforms:**\n- **Audible** - subscription-based, vast library\n- **Libro.fm** - partners with independent bookstores\n- **Scribd** - subscription for e-books, audiobooks, documents\n- **Public libraries** - free access via OverDrive or Hoopla\n\n**Fitness Tracking Apps:**\n- **Strava** - popular for tracking bike rides and hikes, includes route mapping, distance, speed, time; can create clubs and track collective progress with friends\n- **MapMyRide** - route and distance tracking for cycling\n- **AllTrails** - trail tracking and hiking data\n\nStrava chosen for tracking outdoor activities with volleyball team colleagues.
