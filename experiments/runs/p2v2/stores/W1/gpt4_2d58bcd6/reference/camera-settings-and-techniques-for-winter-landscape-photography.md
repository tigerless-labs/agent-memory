**Recommended Camera Settings for Snow Landscapes:**\n\n**Mode & Exposure:** Manual (M) mode for full control. Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop to capture snow detail. ISO 100-400 (low to minimize noise). Aperture f/8-f/11 for good depth of field. Shutter speed 1/125-1/250s.\n\n**Color & Filtering:** Set white balance to Cloudy or Shade mode for cool tones. Use polarizing filter to reduce glare and enhance colors. Use graduated ND filter to balance sky/land exposure.\n\n**Technique:** Shoot during golden hour or overcast days for soft, even light. Avoid harsh midday sun. Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility. Bracket shots for HDR merging.\n\n**Composition:** Wide-angle lens (10-24mm) for landscape vastness. Low angle shots capture snow texture and intimacy. Look for animal tracks, snowflake patterns, leading lines. Fast shutter (1/1000s) with wide aperture (f/2.8) to freeze snowflakes.\n\n**Practical Tips:** Bring extra batteries (cold drains them). Warm batteries in pocket. Use weather-sealed camera or waterproof housing. Take breaks to review images. Keep hands warm for better handling.
