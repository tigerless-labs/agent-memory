Strongly prefers full-cast narrations in audiobooks, especially for dialogue-heavy novels with multiple characters. Finds that skilled narrators amplify emotional impact, help distinguish between characters, and create an immersive experience. Also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style to stories. Narrator choice significantly influences emotional engagement with audiobooks.
