Research focuses on how social media usage patterns are associated with depression and anxiety symptoms in young adults. Exploring potential mediating roles of self-esteem and social support in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes. Also interested in social media-based interventions and policies, mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, and peer-to-peer support initiatives on social media platforms.
