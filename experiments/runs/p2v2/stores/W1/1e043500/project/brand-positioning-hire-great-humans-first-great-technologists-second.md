Building brand around hiring philosophy emphasizing character and EQ over pure technical skill.

Core ethos:
- Hire great human beings FIRST, then great technologists second
- Use psychometric testing to identify critical thinkers, not just amazing coders
- Value EQ more than IQ when hiring software developers

Content strategy focus:
- Remote team building and management best practices
- How to build effective teams prioritizing character and soft skills
- Content formats: blog posts, podcasts, videos, webinars, e-books
- Target audience: team leaders, hiring managers, remote team builders
- Emphasize collaboration, communication, and team culture over pure technical excellence

Related niches to address:
- Remote Work Best Practices
- Remote Team Building  
- Remote Leadership
- Remote Hiring and Onboarding
- Remote Work Tools and Technology
