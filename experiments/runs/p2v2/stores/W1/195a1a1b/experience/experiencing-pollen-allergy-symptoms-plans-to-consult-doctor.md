User is experiencing hay fever-like symptoms consistent with pollen allergy: sneezing, runny/stuffy nose, itchy nose/throat, watery/red eyes, cough, postnasal drip, fatigue, headache. User plans to consult with a doctor or allergist for proper diagnosis and treatment. Over-the-counter options discussed include antihistamines, decongestants, nasal corticosteroid sprays, and saline rinses. Lifestyle modifications recommended: avoid high pollen days, keep windows closed, use air filters, maintain anti-inflammatory diet, stay hydrated.
