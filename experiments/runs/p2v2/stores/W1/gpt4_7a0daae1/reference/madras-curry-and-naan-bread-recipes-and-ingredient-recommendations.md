Madras Curry Recipe: Use Madras curry powder blends from Penzeys Spices, The Spice House, or McCormick. Base recipe includes onion, garlic, ginger, cumin, turmeric, garam masala, canned tomatoes, vegetable broth, and coconut milk. Cook for 10-15 minutes simmering. Add tofu or protein and simmer additional 5-7 minutes. Serve over rice or with naan bread. Naan Bread Recipe: Use all-purpose flour, salt, sugar, yeast, lukewarm water, olive oil, and yogurt. Knead 5-7 minutes, let rise 1-2 hours. Divide into 6-8 portions, roll thin (6-7 inches), cook 1-2 minutes per side in skillet until puffed and golden brown. Brush with butter/ghee. Variations: add garlic, cilantro, cumin seeds, or use cast-iron skillet for crispier crust.
