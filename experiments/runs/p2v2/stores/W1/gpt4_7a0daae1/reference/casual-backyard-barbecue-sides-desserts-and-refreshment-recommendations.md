**Classic Barbecue Sides:**
- Coleslaw
- Baked beans
- Grilled vegetables (peppers, zucchini, onions)
- Potato salad
- Corn on the cob

**Desserts:**
- S'mores station (user's favorite)
- Watermelon
- Brownies or cookies
- Fruit salad
- Watermelon patriotic parfaits
- Ice cream sandwiches

**Refreshments:**
- Lemonade (can add flavors: strawberry, raspberry)
- Iced coffee
- Fresh fruit infused water (cucumber, lemon, lime, berries)
- Sparkling water with fruit
- Popsicles
- Agua fresca (watermelon, cantaloupe, hibiscus)
- Virgin piña coladas
- Cucumber lime refresher
- Ginger ale spritzer

User prefers casual, laid-back vibes with simple, classic options rather than elaborate entertainment.
