Tennis serve tips: proper continental grip, shoulder-width stance with body turned slightly, toss 6-8 feet high and 6-8 inches in front, racket at 90-degree angle, contact at peak of toss with slight closed face. Follow-through close to body. Vary serves: flat (fast/straight), topspin (dips down), slice (curves away). Net play strategy: come to net to exploit opponent's weak returns, read return patterns, position at optimal angle, use quick feet adjustments, watch ball closely, adjust racket face, be prepared to react strongly, practice in drills before match play.
