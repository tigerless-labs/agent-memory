Core exercises: planks (30-60s, 3-5 sets), Russian twists (3x10-15), leg raises (3x10-15), bicycle crunches (3x10-15) - all 2-3x/week. Leg exercises: squats (3x10-15, 2-3x/week), lunges (3x10-15 per leg, 2-3x/week), calf raises (3x10-15, 2x/week), deadlifts (3x10-15, 1-2x/week). Cardio types: HIIT (20-30 min, 2-3x/week), steady-state (30-45 min, 3-4x/week), hill sprints (30-90s intervals). Stretching routine: neck rolls, shoulder rolls, chest stretch (30s each), calf stretch (30s per leg), hamstring stretch, quad stretch (30s per leg), hip flexor stretch, forearm stretch, rotator cuff stretch, glute stretch - do before and after activity. Plyometrics (2-3x/week): box jumps (3x10-15, start 12-18in), depth jumps (3x10-15), lateral bounds (3x10-15), tuck jumps (3x10-15), medicine ball throws (3x10-15). Focus on proper form, progressive overload (increase 2.5-5lbs every 2 weeks), 48-72h recovery between sessions.
