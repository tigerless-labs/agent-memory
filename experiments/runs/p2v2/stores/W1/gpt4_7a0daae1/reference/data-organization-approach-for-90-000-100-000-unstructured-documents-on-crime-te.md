Approach for organizing large document collection (90,000-100,000 reports, 1-5 pages each) on topics: international crime, terrorism, politics. Recommended steps: text cleaning (removing special chars, punctuation, stop words, normalization), document parsing (title, author, date, body), entity extraction (names, organizations, locations via NER/POS tagging), topic modeling (identify main themes/patterns), sentiment analysis (tone/emotions), document classification by content type. Goal: enable quick search and filtering by topic and themes.
