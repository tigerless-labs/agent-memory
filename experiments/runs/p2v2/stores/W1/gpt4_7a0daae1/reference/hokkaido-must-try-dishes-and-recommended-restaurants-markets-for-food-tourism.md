**Must-Try Dishes:**
- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton with vegetables)
- Kaisendon (rice bowl with fresh sashimi)
- Jingisukan (Japanese Mongolian barbecue style)
- Uni ikura don (sea urchin and salmon roe over rice)
- Ishikari Nabe (hot pot with salmon and vegetables, winter specialty)
- Local ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors)

**Recommended Locations:**
- **Nijo Fish Market, Sapporo** — Fresh seafood (crab, sea urchin, squid)
- **Sapporo Ramen Yokocho** — Alley with multiple ramen shops
- **Tanukikoji Shopping Street, Sapporo** — Local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs
- **Daruma, Sapporo** — Jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- **Kitaichi Hall, Asahikawa** — Jingisukan specialty
- **Yurigakuro, Sapporo** — Ishikari Nabe
- **Kakizaki Shokudo, Otaru** — Ishikari Nabe
