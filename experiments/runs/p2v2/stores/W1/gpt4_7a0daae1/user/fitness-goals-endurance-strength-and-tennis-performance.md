Primary goals: improve cardiovascular endurance for 30-minute runs, improve reaction time and hand-eye coordination, increase core and leg strength for tennis.

Schedule: Friday evenings solo cycling along river, Saturdays tennis lessons, strength training 2-3x weekly. Focus on proper form, progressive overload, and recovery (48-72 hours between sessions).
