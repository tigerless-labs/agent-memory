User employs structured note-taking methods when reading books for discussions: summarizing chapters, recording quotes with page numbers, creating character maps showing relationships and motivations, asking questions about the story, and making connections to personal life or other works. Uses sticky notes or tabs to mark important pages. Researches author background and historical context. Prefers having organized notes to reference during book club discussions and to develop deeper understanding of the material.
