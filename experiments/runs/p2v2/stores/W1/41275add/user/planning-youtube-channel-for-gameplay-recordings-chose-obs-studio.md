Starting a YouTube channel focused on gameplay recording.

Chose **OBS Studio** for screen recording software because:
- Free and open-source
- Large community and plugin ecosystem
- Highly customizable (important for creating unique channel look)
- Well-optimized for RTX 3080 and Ryzen 9 5900X hardware

Alternative considered: XSplit (more polished UI but subscription-based)
