Crowd favorites for sparking conversations:\n\n**Favorites/Habits**: What's your favorite book of all time? Do you prefer e-books or physical copies? What's your go-to genre? How many books do you read monthly?\n\n**Preferences**: Do you prefer happy or realistic endings? Standalone books or series? Complex characters or plot-driven stories?\n\n**Discovery**: How do you find new books? Best recommendation you've received? Do you trust reviews or word-of-mouth?\n\n**Engagement**: If you could have dinner with any fictional character, who? Most underrated book you've read? Longest book you've read?
