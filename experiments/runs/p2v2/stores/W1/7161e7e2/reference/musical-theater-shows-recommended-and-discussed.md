**Recommendations based on Book of Mormon interest:**
- Avenue Q (irreverent humor, catchy music)
- The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee (quirky comedy)
- Something Rotten! (1590s Shakespeare comedy) - Original Broadway Cast Recording (2015) features Brian d'Arcy James, Christian Borle, John Cariani
- Hamilton (groundbreaking, diverse music)
- The Producers (Mel Brooks musical comedy)
- Urinetown (satirical musical)
- Be More Chill (sci-fi musical comedy)
- Dear Evan Hansen (mental health, human connection)
- The Band's Visit (poignant, beautiful score)
- Hadestown (folk-opera retelling of Orpheus myth) - Won 8 Tony Awards in 2019 for Best Musical. Original Broadway cast: Eva Noblezada, Reeve Carney, Patrick Page, Amber Gray. Score by Anaïs Mitchell.

Available on streaming: Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music
