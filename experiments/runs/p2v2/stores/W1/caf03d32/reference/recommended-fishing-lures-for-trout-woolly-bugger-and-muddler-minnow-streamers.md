Fishing gear recommendations from May 2023 conversation:

**Primary interest**: Woolly Bugger and Muddler Minnow streamers for trout fishing in rivers

**Woolly Bugger characteristics**:
- Versatile streamer imitating baitfish, leeches, or crawdads
- Marabou tail and palmered hackle provide movement and action
- Works well in moderate to fast current
- Can be fished with sink-tip line or weighted leader

**Muddler Minnow characteristics**:
- Slim profile with weighted head
- Great for faster water in rivers
- Deer hair collar gives lifelike action
- Imitates baitfish, sculpin, or crayfish
- Good for rocky/boulder-strewn bottoms

**Fishing approach**:
- User prefers to fish without strike indicator
- Relies on detecting takes through marabou tail movement
- Plans to fish upstream/across with variation in retrieve patterns
- Cast upstream and across, allow streamer to swing downstream

Other recommended lures: Hare's Ear Nymph, Pheasant Tail Nymph, Copper John, Black Stonefly, Mepps Aglia, Rooster Tail, Acme Kastmaster

Brands: Simms, Orvis, Umpqua, Rio Products, Mepps, Acme
