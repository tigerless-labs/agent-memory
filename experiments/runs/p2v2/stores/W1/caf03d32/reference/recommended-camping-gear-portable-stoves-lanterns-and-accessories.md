Camping gear recommendations from May 2023 conversation:

**Portable Stoves**:
- MSR PocketRocket 2: lightweight, compact, reliable, excellent fuel efficiency
- Jetboil Flash Cooking System: all-in-one solution (burner, pot, cooking vessel), fast and efficient

**Camping Lanterns**:
- Black Diamond Apollo Lantern: bright, reliable, compact, powered by AA batteries, has red light mode
- Coleman Classic Camp Lantern: classic, reliable, affordable, easy to use, warm soft light

**Other camping gear discussed**:
- Sleeping pads: Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite, REI Co-op Trailbreak
- Camping chairs: REI Co-op Camp X Chair, Coleman Camping Chair
- Water filtration: LifeStraw, Sawyer Mini
- Camp shoes: Teva Terra-Float, Sanuk Beaumont
- Camp towels: MSR Trail Towel, REI Co-op Camp Towel

**Tips for Big Sur/similar camping**:
- Check weather forecast and pack warm layers (Big Sur is chilly mornings/evenings)
- Pack layers for temperature adjustments
- Bring bug repellent (mosquitoes present)
- Use portable power bank for device charging during trip
- Proper food storage to avoid wildlife
