User is interested in learning art conservation techniques as a hobby after participating in a behind-the-scenes tour of the Museum of History's conservation lab.

**Topics explored:**
- Mummification process in ancient Egypt
- Adhesives and consolidants used in art conservation (acrylic polymers, animal glues, cellulose-based adhesives, epoxy resins, natural waxes, Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98, Parylene, silanes)
- Paper types used in conservation (cotton paper, linen paper, rag paper, Japanese papers like kozu and gampi)
- Mounting and framing techniques (hinging, window mounting, float mounting, conservation framing, reversible framing)
- Conservation materials properties (acid-free, lignin-free, alpha-cellulose content, surface texture, transparency)

**Resources mentioned:**
- American Institute for Conservation (AIC)
- International Institute for Conservation (IIC)
- Conservation Wiki
- YouTube channels: Art Conservation, Conservation Framing, The Getty Conservation Institute
- Books: 'The Conservation of Art and Architecture', 'The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory', 'Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide'

**Approach:** Prefers learning through hands-on participation, local museum workshops, and professional guidance from experienced conservators.
