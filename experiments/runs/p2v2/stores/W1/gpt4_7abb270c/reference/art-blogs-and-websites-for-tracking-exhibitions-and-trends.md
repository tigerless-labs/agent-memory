Resources for staying updated on contemporary art, exhibitions, and trends:

- Artsy: comprehensive online platform for art, exhibitions, market trends, artist profiles
- Hyperallergic: leading contemporary art blog with focus on NYC
- Artnet: art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists
- The Art Newspaper: daily international art news and market analysis
- Contemporary Art Daily: emerging artists and exhibitions
- Art in America: contemporary art magazine with in-depth features and interviews
- Frieze: contemporary art and culture magazine, international focus
- Apollo Magazine: British art magazine covering historical and contemporary art
- Art21: non-profit with documentaries and online content on contemporary art
- Culture Trip: art, culture and travel coverage, underground scenes
