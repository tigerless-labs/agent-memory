Creative project: Creating a visual effect scene inspired by Black Mirror.

Scene concept: Girl sitting down at computer, and right before she speaks into microphone, camera shows her slowly disappearing into the computer interface.

Desired effects:
- Slow, deliberate disappearance with visual distortion
- Glitchy and distorted visual effects on computer interface
- Eerie sound design with low hums, distorted sounds, or whispers
- Lighting and shadows for moody atmosphere
- Use of flashes of light or swirling motion to create disorientation
- Close-up shots of computer elements to suggest something strange happening

Style influence: Black Mirror - dark, unsettling, technology-focused narrative.
