Product: Shimano Ultegra clipless pedals (SPD-SL)

Why recommended: Popular choice with high reviews, known for quality construction, smooth pedaling feel, reliable performance.

Key features:
- Road bike specific (lighter, more aerodynamic than mountain versions)
- Compatible with Shimano SPD-SL cleats
- Adjustable tension mechanism for smooth engagement
- Improved pedaling efficiency and power transfer
- Reduced fatigue on long rides

Installation notes:
- Requires pedal wrench, Allen wrenches, torque wrench
- Must use correct torque specification to avoid over-tightening
- Use proper cleats aligned correctly
- DIY installation possible; bike shop available if needed

Cost: Varies, generally mid to higher price range for road pedals
Compatibility: Ensure shoes have matching Shimano SPD-SL cleats
