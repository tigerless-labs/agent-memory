Planning to rent an RV for a road trip to Grand Canyon with friends during winter holidays (around Dec 2023-Jan 2024). Base in Flagstaff, Arizona. Plan to:
- Drive from California to Arizona
- Camp overnight at Grand Canyon National Park (interested in sites with electric hookups at Mather Campground, Trailer Village, or Desert View Campground)
- Watch sunrise from Grand Canyon
- Visit Flagstaff restaurants and accommodations

Preferences:
- Will need to winterize RV for cold weather and potential snow
- Concerned about winter driving conditions
- Interested in scenic drives and taking time to enjoy views

Resources to use:
- recreation.gov for campsite reservations
- grandcanyonlodges.com for Trailer Village
- America the Beautiful annual pass for national park access
- Flagstaff restaurants: Late for the Train Cafe, Proper Meats + Provisions, Fat Olives, The Toasted Owl, Big Foot BBQ
- Hotels: Little America Flagstaff, Hotel Monte Vista, Flagstaff Inn and Suites, The Inn at Flagstaff
