Committed to yoga practice for triathlon training and recovery. Started with twice-weekly sessions on March 1, 2023. Progressed to a 30-day daily yoga challenge in April 2023 where practiced every day even on running rest days. Benefits included improved flexibility, overall recovery, and reduced muscle tension. Practiced gentle styles initially (Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin). Focused on poses like Downward-Facing Dog, Warrior II, Tree Pose for triathlon-specific movements and injury prevention.
