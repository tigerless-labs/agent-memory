Yellowstone accommodations: Canyon Lodge & Cabins (heart of park, near Grand Canyon of Yellowstone), Grant Village (Yellowstone Lake, south loop base), Yellowstone Gateway Inn (West Yellowstone, outside west entrance). Top geysers: Old Faithful (iconic), Grand Geyser (largest, 7-10hr intervals, 200ft eruptions), Castle Geyser (Upper Geyser Basin, 10-12hr intervals), Daisy Geyser (3-4hr intervals, 80ft). Hot springs: Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine formations, family-friendly), Norris Geyser Basin, Boiling River (near Mammoth, soak opportunity). Wildlife viewing: Lamar Valley (best general wildlife), Slough Creek (grizzly bears), Tower-Roosevelt Area (grizzly bears), Mammoth Hot Springs area. Tips for kids: ranger programs, Junior Ranger activities, keep safe distance from wildlife (100+ yards from bears).
