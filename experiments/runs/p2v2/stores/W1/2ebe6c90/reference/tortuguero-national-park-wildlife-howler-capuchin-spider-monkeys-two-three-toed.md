Tortuguero National Park wildlife summary: Monkeys - howler (loud, distinctive howls), white-faced capuchins (agile, intelligent), spider monkeys (long limbs, acrobatic). Sloths - two-toed and three-toed. Birds - toucans (colorful beaks), quetzals (iridescent feathers), macaws, over 300 bird species total. Reptiles - American/Morelet's crocodiles, green/spiny-tailed iguanas, venomous fer-de-lance snakes, harmless boa constrictors. Other - jaguars (rare), caimans, 1,200+ butterfly species. Tips: join guided tours, look early morning/late afternoon, be patient, respect wildlife and keep safe distance.
