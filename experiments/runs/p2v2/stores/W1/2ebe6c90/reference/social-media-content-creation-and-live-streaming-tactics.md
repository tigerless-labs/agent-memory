**Live Streaming Platform-Specific Tips:**\n\n**Instagram Live:**\n- Keep streams 10-30 minutes (shorter attention span)\n- Visually-focused with high-quality visuals and props\n- Share behind-the-scenes and exclusive content\n- Use polls, questions, and quizzes\n- Mobile-first (film vertically)\n\n**YouTube Live:**\n- Aim for 30-60 minute in-depth, informative content\n- Invest in high-quality production (lighting, sound, editing)\n- Create evergreen content that remains valuable\n- Optimize with keywords, tags, descriptions for SEO\n- Focus on community building\n\n**Facebook Live:**\n- Longer-form: 30-90 minutes\n- Community-focused using Groups and Watch Parties\n- Share personal stories and behind-the-scenes content\n- Use call-to-action buttons to drive traffic\n- Promote events and webinars\n\n**Content Ideas:**\n- Keep ideas journal to track inspiration\n- Repurpose existing content into multiple formats\n- Host Q&A and AMA sessions\n- Share personal stories and "day in the life"\n- Collaborate with other creators\n- Ask audience for topic suggestions\n- Experiment with new formats (challenges, games, workshops)
