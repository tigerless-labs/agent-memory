Eco-lodges recommended for Costa Rica: Lapa Rios Ecolodge (Osa Peninsula, luxury with community engagement), Monteverde Lodge (cloud forest, strong sustainability focus), Arenal Observatory Lodge (near volcano, conservation-focused), Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero, wildlife focus), El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula, includes reforestation program). Sustainable tour operators: Costa Rica Expeditions (pioneer in eco-tourism), Osa Conservation, Tortuguero Canals Tours (family-owned), Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve, Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours. Look for certifications: Certification for Sustainable Tourism (CST), International Ecotourism Society (TIES), Costa Rican Tourism Board (ICT).
