Strategies for keeping kids occupied on long drives: Audiobooks/podcasts (Audible, Scribd, podcast apps). Road trip games - bingo (common sights), I Spy, 20 Questions, Would You Rather, License Plate Tag. Technology - tablets with educational apps/games, e-books, headphones. Snacks - healthy options, mystery snack box. Pit stops - frequent breaks at rest stops, parks, attractions. Scavenger hunts - find items on the road. Car karaoke/dance party. Travel journals - writing, drawing, collecting souvenirs (brochures, postcards, ticket stubs). Turn-taking - let each child choose music, game, or snack. Key principle: be flexible, adapt to kids' moods/interests, turn long drives into bonding experiences.
