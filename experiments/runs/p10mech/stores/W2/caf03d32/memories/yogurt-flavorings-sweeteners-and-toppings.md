## Yogurt Customization Options

User wants to flavor and sweeten slow cooker yogurt (May 30, 2023).

### Flavorings

* Vanilla: vanilla extract or vanilla bean
* Cinnamon: pinch of cinnamon powder for warm, spicy flavor
* Fruit: berries, mango, peaches for sweet, fruity flavor
* Nutmeg: pinch for warm, aromatic flavor
* Ginger: grated fresh ginger for spicy, invigorating flavor
* Lemon: fresh juice or zest for bright, citrusy flavor
* Cocoa: cocoa powder or melted chocolate for chocolatey yogurt

### Sweeteners

* Honey: pure honey drizzle for natural sweetness
* Maple syrup: small amount for rich, velvety sweetness
* Coconut sugar: subtle, caramel-like sweetness
* Date syrup: sweet, molasses-like flavor
* Monk fruit sweetener: 150-200 times sweeter than sugar; use sparingly
* Stevia: pinch of stevia powder for natural sweetness

### Other Add-ins

* Granola: store-bought or homemade for crunchy texture
* Nuts or seeds: chopped almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds for crunch and nutrition
* Cocoa nibs: for chocolatey, crunchy texture
* Dried fruit: cranberries, raisins, apricots for natural sweetness and chewiness

### Customization Tips

* Experiment with different combinations to find perfect slow cooker yogurt
* Can add flavorings and sweeteners after yogurt has chilled
* Adjust to taste preferences
