## Vegan Yogurt Alternatives

User has been trying to eat more plant-based meals (May 2023) and wants to use vegan yogurt as a base for slow cooker yogurt instead of dairy.

### Recommended Options

1. **Soy Yogurt:** Classic vegan base. Brands: Silk, So Delicious
2. **Coconut Yogurt:** Creamy and rich. Brands: So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo
3. **Almond Yogurt:** Low-calorie, low-fat. Brands: Almond Breeze, Silk, So Delicious
4. **Cashew Yogurt:** Creamy and indulgent. Can make homemade by blending cashews with water. Brands: So Delicious
5. **Oat Yogurt:** Good for nut allergies/intolerances. Brands: Oatly, So Delicious
6. **Rice Yogurt:** Hypoallergenic, easy-to-digest. Brands: Rice Dream, So Delicious
7. **Hemp Yogurt:** High in protein and omega-3 fatty acids. Brands: Good Karma, Elmhurst 1925
8. **Flax Yogurt:** Low-calorie, high-fiber. Brands: Good Karma (or make homemade)

### Selection Tips

- Choose **unflavored, unsweetened varieties** to avoid extra sugars and flavorings
- Can add your own flavorings and sweeteners later to taste
- Always check ingredient list and nutrition label for dietary needs and preferences
- Use as starter culture in slow cooker yogurt recipes
