## Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe

User made beef stew in slow cooker (May 30, 2023) and is interested in making more slow cooker recipes, including yogurt.

### Basic Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 gallon milk (whole, low-fat, nonfat, or plant-based alternative)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures (store-bought or previous batch)
- Optional: sweetener (honey, maple syrup, sugar), flavorings (vanilla, cinnamon, fruit), probiotic powder

### Process

1. **Heat the milk:** Pour milk into slow cooker and heat on low for 2-3 hours until it reaches **180°F to 185°F (82°C to 85°C)**. This kills unwanted bacteria and denatures proteins for proper yogurt setting.

2. **Cool the milk:** Turn off slow cooker and let milk cool to **110°F to 120°F (43°C to 49°C)**. Takes around 1-2 hours depending on slow cooker and room temperature.

3. **Add starter culture:** Once cooled, add plain yogurt with live cultures and whisk gently to combine.

4. **Incubate:** Cover slow cooker and wrap with towel/blanket to maintain temperature. Incubate for **8-12 hours or overnight**. Longer incubation = thicker and tangier yogurt.

5. **Chill and store:** Refrigerate yogurt to chill, then transfer to airtight containers.

### Temperature Monitoring

Using a **thermometer is highly recommended** to ensure:
- Accurate temperature control (precise readings)
- Faster heating (know exactly when desired temp reached)
- Reduced risk of overheating (damages proteins, affects texture/flavor)

Without a thermometer: Heat on low 2-3 hours until bubbles form around edges, but results will be less consistent.

### Tips

- Use a thermometer for consistent results
- Experiment with different milk types (dairy or plant-based)
- Greek yogurt: strain through cheesecloth or fine-mesh sieve to remove excess liquid
- For thicker yogurt: add unflavored gelatin or pectin before incubation
- For probiotic-rich yogurt: add probiotic powder before incubation
