## Cashew-Based Yogurt Process

User wants to make slow cooker yogurt with cashew base (May 30, 2023).

### Soaking the Cashews

1. Rinse 1 cup of raw cashews in fine-mesh strainer under cold running water
2. Soak in water for **at least 4 hours or overnight** in bowl or container with lid
3. After 4 hours: drain, rinse, and soak in fresh water for another 4 hours or overnight (optional but recommended for best results)
4. Final rinse before blending

### Blending the Cashews

1. Drain and rinse soaked cashews again
2. Add **1/2 cup fresh water** to blender with drained cashews
3. Blend on high speed for **about 2 minutes** or until smooth and creamy
4. Stop and scrape down sides as needed to ensure even mixing

### Straining the Mixture

Choose one of three methods:

**Cheesecloth:**
- Pros: High-level filtration, catches small particles
- Cons: Messy to use, need to squeeze mixture through cloth
- Method: Squeeze cheesecloth and twist to extract maximum liquid

**Nut Milk Bag:**
- Pros: Specifically designed for plant-based milks, fine-mesh catches particles well
- Cons: Can be pricey, effectiveness varies by brand
- Method: Gently squeeze bag to avoid pushing particles through mesh

**Fine-Mesh Sieve:**
- Pros: Easy to use and clean, catches most particles
- Cons: May not catch all particles, resulting in slightly grainy texture
- Best if: Don't have cheesecloth or nut milk bag

### Ratio & Consistency Tips

- **Basic ratio:** 1 cup cashews to 1/2 cup water (1:1)
- **For thicker yogurt:** Use less water, blend for shorter time
- **For thinner yogurt:** Use more water, blend for longer time
- **For smoothest texture:** Always strain the mixture before using

### Next Steps for Slow Cooker

After straining, use the cashew cream as base in slow cooker yogurt recipe:
- Heat to 180-185°F, cool to 110-120°F
- Add vegan yogurt starter or probiotic powder
- Incubate 8-12 hours or overnight
- Chill to set and thicken
