## Landscape Architecture Methodologies

1. **Site Analysis** — evaluate physical, ecological, cultural characteristics of site to understand opportunities and constraints; provides information for responsive design

2. **Conceptual Design** — create initial design ideas responsive to site context; iterative process exploring different options before final design

3. **Master Planning** — create comprehensive plan for site development; provides framework for implementation over time

4. **Detailed Design** — create detailed drawings and specifications for construction; provides information needed for project construction

5. **Construction Management** — oversee construction ensuring design is properly implemented

## Influence on Ecocriticism

### 1. Representation of Nature
- Landscape architects shape outdoor spaces people interact with
- Way nature is represented in these spaces shapes perceptions
- Can create spaces reflecting holistic, inclusive understanding of nature
- Influences how ecocritics study and interpret literature

### 2. Environmental Justice
- Landscape architects can promote environmental justice through accessible design
- Sensitivity to needs of marginalized communities
- Influences ecocriticism by highlighting how marginalized communities affected by environmental issues

### 3. Sustainability
- Design spaces that are more sustainable
- Promote holistic understanding of human-environment relationship
- Influences ecocriticism by highlighting how literature reflects/shapes sustainability perceptions

### 4. Role of Aesthetics
- Landscape architects concerned with aesthetics of outdoor spaces
- How nature is aesthetically represented shapes perceptions
- Ecocritics can study this aesthetic dimension in literature
