## Tracking Methods

### Manual Tracking (Currently Using)
- **Spreadsheet**: User is tracking progress on spreadsheet (effective method)
- Record for each workout: date, distance, time, notes on how you felt
- Identify patterns over time

### Training Apps (Recommended)
- **Strava**: Track swims including distance, time, pace
- **Swim.com**: Specialized for swim tracking
- **Garmin**: Comprehensive fitness tracking with metrics

## Key Metrics to Track
- Swim times
- Heart rate data
- Pace
- Distance
- Weight (user: currently down 5 lbs)
- Resting heart rate (user: decreased by 10 bpm)
- Progress photos (optional visual tracking)

## Goal-Setting Strategy
- Create structured training plan with specific daily workouts
- Set specific, achievable goals (not just general targets)
- Break larger goals into smaller, manageable objectives
- Gradually increase distance and intensity over time

## Motivation Strategies
- Find a workout buddy or join swim group for accountability
- Reward yourself with non-food rewards (new swim cap, massage, etc.)
- Visualize success (imagine crossing finish line)
- Celebrate small wins along the journey
- Mix up workouts to avoid boredom and prevent plateaus
- Seek inspiration from other triathletes/swimmers on social media
- Get professional guidance from coach if needed

## Consistency Target
- Aim to swim at least 2-3 times per week
- Include one or two rest days between sessions
