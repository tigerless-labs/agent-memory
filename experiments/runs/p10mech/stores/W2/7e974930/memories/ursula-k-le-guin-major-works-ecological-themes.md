## Major Works & Themes

### The Dispossessed (1974)
- Story of anarchists establishing a commune on a distant planet
- Explores anarchism as political/social system
- Notable for exploration of human-environment relationship
- Critiques capitalist ideologies

### The Left Hand of Darkness (1969)
- Set on planet where inhabitants are genderless
- Explores themes of gender, sexuality, power
- Notable for exploration of human-environment relationship
- Critiques societal attitudes toward nature

### The Word for World is Forest (1976)
- Story of conflict between human colonizers and native planet inhabitants
- Explores themes: imperialism, colonialism, human-environment relationship

### The Lathe of Heaven (1971)
- Intersection of science fiction and environmentalism
- Story of man whose dreams can change reality
- Critiques destructive human impact on environment
- Critiques misuse of science and technology

### Always Coming Home (1985)
- Set in post-apocalyptic California
- Explores themes: environmentalism, ecology, human-environment relationship
- Format: collection of stories, songs, and poetry

## Overall Impact
- Influential in science fiction and fantasy, and literature generally
- Known for compelling stories exploring complex social and political issues
- Critiques capitalist ideologies, societal attitudes toward nature
- Addresses destructive impact of humans on environment
