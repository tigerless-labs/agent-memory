## Interdisciplinary Scholarship
- Bringing together different disciplines to study a particular topic or problem
- Involves synthesizing insights and methods from multiple fields
- Goal: gain comprehensive understanding of topic
- Example: climate change study drawing on atmospheric science, economics, sociology, political science

## Transdisciplinary Scholarship
- Goes beyond interdisciplinarity
- Actively involves stakeholders and practitioners from various fields in research process
- Collaborative approach: multiple disciplines work together to co-create knowledge
- Bridges gap between academic research and practical application
- Goal: deeper understanding of problem and generate implementable solutions

## Simple Distinction
- **Interdisciplinary**: combining disciplines to study a problem
- **Transdisciplinary**: combining disciplines to solve a problem

## Note on Ecocriticism
Ecocriticism is an interdisciplinary field involving literary studies, environmental studies, cultural studies, etc. It can also be seen as transdisciplinary when it aims to generate solutions and engage stakeholders to address environmental issues.
