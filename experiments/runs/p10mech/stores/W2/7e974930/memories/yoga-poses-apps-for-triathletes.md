## Recommended Yoga Poses for Triathletes

### Key Target Poses
- **Downward-Facing Dog**: Improves shoulder flexibility and strength
- **Warrior II**: Opens hips and improves balance
- **Tree Pose**: Enhances balance and focus
- **Seated Forward Fold**: Stretches hamstrings and lower back

### Focus Areas
- Shoulders (prone to injury and tightness)
- Hips (critical for running and cycling)
- IT band (common issue for runners)

## Yoga Styles to Explore
- **Beginner-friendly**: Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin
- **More dynamic**: Ashtanga, Power Yoga
- **Recovery-focused**: Restorative Yoga

## Recommended Apps/Platforms
- YogaGlo
- Peloton
- Down Dog

## Practice Recommendations
- Frequency: 1-2 times per week (can start with 30-45 minutes, increase gradually)
- Timing: Schedule on rest days or easy training days
- Approach: Use yoga as active recovery after hard swim/bike sessions
- Mindset: Listen to body, modify poses as needed, not competitive

## Additional Tips
- Communicate with yoga instructor about being a triathlete
- Focus on consistency and regularity
- Pay attention to breathwork as a tool for body connection
- Engage core muscles for stability, balance, and posture
