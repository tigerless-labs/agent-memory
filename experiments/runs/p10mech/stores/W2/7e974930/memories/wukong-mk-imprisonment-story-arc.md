## Story Arc: MK & Wukong Imprisonment

**Setting & Conflict**
- MK is imprisoned behind a magical barrier by Wukong
- Wukong searches for a "diamond snare" — MK genuinely doesn't know where it is
- Wukong initially wants to kill MK; MK convinces him to keep him alive as "insurance"
- Agreement: Wukong lets MK live (for now), warns against escape attempts or he'll kill him

**Character Dynamics**
- **MK**: Desperate, hungry, scared; tries to reason with Wukong; appeals to his sense of strategy (insurance argument); attempts escape and theft fail
- **Wukong**: Cruel, impatient, easily annoyed; initially dismissive of MK's needs; not entirely unmovable (can be argued with); sends another monkey with minimal food rather than dealing with MK's pleas directly

**Key Scenes Developed**

1. **Interrogation + Deal** — Wukong interrogates MK about diamond snare; MK swears he doesn't know; Wukong considers killing him but is convinced by MK's flattery and insurance argument

2. **First Backfired Plea** — MK, hungry and thirsty, tries to get Wukong's attention to shut him up with food; backfires (Wukong gets annoyed)

3. **Minimal Sustenance** — Another monkey delivers a small bundle: a few pieces of fruit and small container of water; MK eats desperately, still hungry afterward

4. **Reconciliation Scene** — MK alone, trying to understand why the heroic Monkey King from childhood stories has become this cruel captor; confused, scared, searching for any explanation or remaining good

5. **Party Scene** — Monkeys have loud feast and party nearby; MK can hear feasting and laughter; desperately tries to break barrier and steal food but fails; left alone, starving

6. **Final Negotiation** — MK begs Wukong for food/water, arguing logically that "insurance" is worthless if he starves to death; Wukong, tired of pleas, eventually provides stale bread and a bowl of water via magic; warns MK not to push his luck; MK eats slowly and carefully

**Details**
- Magic barrier imprisons MK; strong enough to withstand his attacks
- Wukong uses magic (lightning bolts, food/water summoning)
- Wukong sends other monkeys to avoid dealing with MK directly sometimes
- Food provided: fruit, stale bread, water (minimal amounts)
- MK physically weak from hunger/thirst/stress
- Wukong's patience is finite; he grows annoyed with repeated pleas

**Story Themes**
- Powerlessness vs. clever argumentation (MK must survive through reason, not strength)
- Gap between legend and reality (heroic Wukong vs. cruel captor)
- Desperation and survival instinct driving MK's choices
- Wukong's arrogance mixed with practical constraints
