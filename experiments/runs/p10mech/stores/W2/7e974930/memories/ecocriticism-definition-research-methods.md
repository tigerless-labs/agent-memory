## Definition
Ecocriticism is an interdisciplinary field of study that examines the relationship between literature and the environment. It explores:
- How literature reflects, shapes, and challenges our perceptions of the natural world
- How our relationship to the environment is represented in literature
- The environmental impact of literary production and consumption
- Draws on: literary theory, environmental science, cultural studies, philosophy

## Research Methods
1. **Close reading** — detailed analysis of language, imagery, and literary techniques in texts to explore representation of nature and human-environment relationships
2. **Historical and cultural context analysis** — place literary texts within historical/cultural context to understand societal attitudes toward environment
3. **Environmental science and ecology analysis** — apply environmental science knowledge to understand representation of natural world in literature
4. **Comparative analysis** — compare texts from different periods/cultures to explore how nature representation has changed over time
5. **Ethnographic and fieldwork** — observe people in field settings and conduct interviews about their relationship to environment
6. **Discourse analysis** — analyze language and discourse in literary texts to understand how they construct and reinforce views of environment
