## "The Godfather" Analysis

**Film Credits:**
- Director: Francis Ford Coppola
- Cinematographer: Gordon Willis
- Composer: Nino Rota
- Screenwriter: Mario Puzo (with Coppola)
- Main actors: Marlon Brando, Al Pacino

**Iconic Moments:**
- Horse head scene — considered one of the most memorable and iconic scenes in cinematic history; represents the brutal nature of the Corleone family business
- Orange symbolism — analyzed by film critics as symbolizing impending danger and death; present in scenes leading up to violent events:
  - Don Corleone getting shot at the fruit market
  - Don Corleone's assassination attempt in the garden
  - Creates foreshadowing and sense of inevitability

## Films Using Symbolism

**With heavy/deliberate symbolism:**
- "The Shining" (1980, Stanley Kubrick) — maze symbolizing mind, color red for danger/violence
- "Blade Runner" (1982, Ridley Scott) — eyes, animals, nature symbolizing identity and humanity
- "Get Out" (2017, Jordan Peele) — white color for oppressive racism, deer imagery for victimhood
- "The Matrix" (1999) — red/blue pills symbolizing choice/free will

**Great films without heavy symbolism:**
- "Casablanca" (1942)
- "The Shawshank Redemption" (1994)
- "Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope" (1977)

## User's View on Symbolism

- Finds symbolism in films intriguing and fascinating
- Recognizes that symbolism is not *necessary* for a film to be great
- Believes symbolism can be **overused and become distracting** — filmmakers sometimes try too hard
- Prefers balance: symbolism should enhance story, not overshadow it or seem contrived/pretentious
