## Susan Green — Real Estate Agent Performance Marketing Persona

### Why This Persona
Initial attempt (Mark Smith) was generic and not data-backed. Second iteration incorporated actual NAR (National Association of Realtors) statistics for accuracy.

### Source
NAR Quick Real Estate Statistics at nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics

### Demographics
- Age: 55 (female)
- Location: Suburban or rural area (more typical than urban)
- Median Income: $61,140 (2019 NAR data)
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

### Personality & Work Style
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable, continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with good local market knowledge
- Works independently with brokerage support
- Active in local real estate community
- Maintains relationships with clients and colleagues

### Pain Points (Key for SaaS Positioning)
- Limited access to marketing resources (especially vs. larger brokerages)
- Difficulty tracking and analyzing marketing performance
- Heavy competition from other agents and brokerages
- Challenge keeping up with latest real estate technology and trends
- Time-consuming manual marketing processes

### Goals
- Boost visibility and reputation in local market
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Stay ahead of competition
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

### Technology Usage
- CRM software for clients/properties
- Email marketing and communication tools
- Social media (personal and professional)
- Open to new tech if it improves efficiency/ROI

### Marketing Channels
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking events and referrals
- Direct mail and email marketing
- Social media advertising

### Budget Approach
- Cost-conscious but willing to invest in solutions with proven ROI
- Continuously monitors and adjusts spend based on results
- Leverages brokerage marketing resources where available
- Expects clear return on marketing technology investments

### SaaS Value Props
For marketing automation/analytics tools targeting this persona: focus on simplicity (less learning curve), ROI clarity (tracking what works), and broker-independent solutions.
