## Persona Development: Use Data, Not Assumptions

### The Lesson
When creating buyer personas for SaaS products, especially in verticals with published industry statistics, use the actual data rather than assumptions.

### Example: Real Estate Agent Personas
- **First version (Mark Smith):** Generic male agent, age 43, urban Los Angeles, tech-savvy, $100k+ income — sounded plausible but wasn't grounded in data
- **Second version (Susan Green):** Female agent, age 55, suburban/rural area, $61,140 median income — based on actual NAR statistics

The second persona is:
- More authentic (reflects actual demographics in the industry)
- More credible for stakeholders
- More useful for targeting and positioning
- Better foundation for discovering real pain points

### Where to Find Data
For real estate: NAR (National Association of Realtors) publishes quick real estate statistics at nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics

### Pattern for Other Industries
Look for:
- Industry association reports (like NAR for real estate)
- Government labor statistics
- Published surveys and research from credible sources
- Analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester, etc.)

These beat anecdotes and assumptions every time.
