## Artists to Study

### Carsten Höller
- German artist
- Known for: interactive and immersive installations
- Signature elements: slides, Ferris wheels, playful interactive elements

### Anicka Yi
- Korean-American artist
- Known for: immersive, often scent-based installations
- Themes: relationship between art, science, and technology

### Rafael Lozano-Hemmer
- Mexican-Canadian artist
- Known for: interactive installations using biometrics, robotics, and technology
- Themes: surveillance, control, and human interaction with technology

## Exhibitions & Collectives to Experience

### Meow Wolf
- Location: Santa Fe (flagship location)
- Style: Immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, and storytelling

### TeamLab
- Origin: Japanese collective
- Style: Stunning immersive exhibitions using technology, light, and interactive environments

### The Color Factory
- Location: San Francisco
- Style: Interactive and immersive art installations exploring emotional and psychological effects of color
