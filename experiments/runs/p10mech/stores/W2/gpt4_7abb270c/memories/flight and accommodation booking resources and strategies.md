## Flight Booking Websites
- **Skyscanner** - compares multiple airlines and travel agencies
- **Google Flights** - price trends and exploration tools
- **Kayak** - searches hundreds of travel sites
- **Momondo** - specializes in error fares and discounts
- **Airfarewatchdog** - fare sale alerts, error fares, exclusive deals

## Accommodation Booking
- **Booking.com** - large selection including hotels, hostels, apartments
- **Airbnb** - unique locally-hosted accommodations
- **HotelsCombined** - meta-search comparing multiple sites
- **Trivago** - hotel price comparison
- **Hostelworld** - specialized for hostels and budget options

## Flight Booking Timing
- **Optimal:** 54 days before departure (on average)
- **Prime window:** 21-121 days before departure (typically within 5% of lowest price)
- **Strategies:** 
  - Set up fare sale alerts on preferred airlines
  - Use price trend tools to track changes
  - Be flexible with dates/airports
  - Book on off-peak days (Mon, Thu, Fri vs Tue, Wed, Sat)
  - Consider red-eye/early morning flights
  - Look for error fares (limited time availability)
  - Clear cookies to avoid price increases
  - Consider budget airlines

## Off-Peak Seasons for Lower Prices
- Mid-January to mid-March
- April to May
- September to November
