Online trail-finding resources for locating 5-7 mile hikes:

1. **AllTrails** (website and app) — search by location, distance, difficulty
2. **TrailLink** (website) — US trails with descriptions, maps, reviews
3. **Hiking Project** (website and app) — comprehensive guide, maps, elevation profiles, user reviews
4. **Local park websites** — many parks list their own trails with distances and difficulty levels

These resources allow filtering by distance (5-7 miles target), difficulty level, and other factors.
