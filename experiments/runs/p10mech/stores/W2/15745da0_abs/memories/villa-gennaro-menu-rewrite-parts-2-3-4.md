## Project: Villa Gennaro Menu Rewrite

**Date:** May 22, 2023

### Scope
Rewrote parts 2–4 of Villa Gennaro's menu with more enticing descriptions. Part 1 (presumably appetizers/starters) was not included in this conversation.

### Parts Completed

**Part 2: THE CLASSICS**
- Chicken dishes (Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese) — $22 each
- Veal dishes (Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese) — $24–$26
- All served with choice of vegetable & potato or pasta
- Added rich, sensory descriptions emphasizing tenderness, flavor, and saucing

**Part 3: ENTREES**
- Seafood: Shrimp Fra Diavolo ($28), Broiled Salmon ($26), Zuppa Di Pesce ($34), Salmon Horseradish ($28), Seafood Risotto ($26)
- Meat: Veal Toscano ($24), Braised Short Ribs ($38), Chicken Giambotte ($26), Rack of Lamb ($38)
- Vegetarian: Eggplant Parmigiana ($20), Risotto Primavera ($20)
- Price range: $20–$38

**Part 4: PASTA**
- Penne a la Vodka ($20), Capellini Primavera ($20), Rigatoni alla Gennaro ($30), Portobello Mushroom Ravioli ($24), Lobster Ravioli ($24), Lasagna ($21), Braised Short Ribs Pappardelle ($30), Siciliana ($22), Roasted Pepper Pappardelle ($32)
- Price range: $20–$32

### Rewrite Approach
- Enhanced dish descriptions with sensory language (e.g., "tender," "succulent," "bold," "zesty," "rich," "hearty")
- Maintained original structure and pricing
- Kept consistent framing: ingredient list → preparation style → sauce/accompaniments → side options
- Added context (e.g., "Maine lobster meat," "homemade ravioli," "sun-dried tomatoes")
- Standard disclaimer: "Note that prices are subject to change and may vary by location or time of day"

### Outcome
Three menu sections rewritten with more compelling language and maintained consistency across sections.
