## Printmaking Learning (May 21, 2023)

User expressed interest in learning printmaking and was new to the concept of how brayer choice affects final prints.

### Guidance on Brayer Selection
Factors to consider:
- **Ink type**: Water-based inks need softer brayers; oil-based need harder
- **Surface texture**: Smooth surfaces suit harder brayers; rough surfaces need softer
- **Surface size**: Smaller surfaces = smaller brayer; larger surfaces = larger brayer
- **Design detail**: Fine details benefit from smaller, firmer brayers
- **Personal preference**: Trial and error to find preferred tools

### Beginner Tips Recommended
1. Start with relief printing (most accessible)
2. Experiment with different papers and inks
3. Don't fear mistakes — adjust and retry
4. Consider taking a printmaking class or workshop
5. Practice regularly to build skill

Key principle: Printmaking is fundamentally about experimentation and creativity.
