Chose to use **Dark Sky** and **Mountain Weather Forecast** apps for checking weather conditions before hiking.

Other apps evaluated but not selected:
- Weather Underground (hourly/10-day forecasts, radar, satellite)
- OpenSnow (mountain weather, though ski-focused)
- The Weather Channel
- AccuWeather (minute-by-minute forecasts)
- National Weather Service (NWS) — official app with warnings and radar

Dark Sky: highly accurate, minute-by-minute forecasts, good for predicting precipitation timing
Mountain Weather Forecast: specialized for mountainous regions, includes snowfall/avalanche risk/wind
