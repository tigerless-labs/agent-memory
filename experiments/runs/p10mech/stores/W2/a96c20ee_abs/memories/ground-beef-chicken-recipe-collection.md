## Ground Beef Recipes
- **Classic Cheeseburgers:** Season with salt, pepper, spices; grill/pan-fry; serve on toasted bun with cheese, lettuce, tomato, condiments
- **Sliders with Caramelized Onions:** Mix with minced onion, salt, pepper; form small patties; serve on small buns with caramelized onions, cheddar, ketchup
- **Spicy Southwestern Burgers:** Add diced jalapeños, cumin, chili powder; top with pepper jack cheese, sliced avocado, fried egg
- **Greek-Style Stuffed Burgers:** Mix with crumbled feta cheese, chopped kalamata olives, oregano; serve with tzatziki sauce
- **Spaghetti Bolognese:** Ground beef with tomato sauce and vegetables over pasta
- **Beef Tacos:** Seasoned ground beef with lettuce, cheese, salsa
- **Meatball Subs:** Ground beef meatballs with marinara sauce and mozzarella cheese
- **Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry:** Over brown rice
- **Beef and Mushroom Gravy:** Over egg noodles

## Chicken Breast Recipes
- **Classic Chicken Sandwiches:** Grilled/pan-fried with salt, pepper, spices; serve on toasted bun with lettuce, tomato, mayo
- **Buffalo Chicken Sandwiches:** Marinate in hot sauce and ranch; serve with lettuce, celery, blue cheese crumbles
- **Chicken Club Sandwiches:** Topped with crispy bacon, lettuce, tomato, mayo on toasted bread
- **Chicken Caesar Sandwiches:** Served on bun with romaine lettuce, parmesan, Caesar dressing
- **Chicken Fajitas:** Grilled chicken breast with bell peppers and onions
- **Baked Chicken Breast:** With roasted vegetables and quinoa/potatoes
- **Chicken and Veggie Stir-Fry:** With brown rice
- **Chicken and Cheese Omelette:** With hash browns

## Leftover Chicken Soups
- **Chicken Noodle Soup:** Shredded/chopped chicken with vegetables and noodles
- **Creamy Chicken and Rice Soup:** Blended cooked chicken with broth, rice, heavy cream
- **Chicken Tortilla Soup:** Shredded chicken with tortillas, diced tomatoes, onions, spices

## Leftover Chicken Salads
- **Chicken Caesar Salad:** Diced chicken, romaine lettuce, croutons, parmesan, Caesar dressing
- **Greek Chicken Salad:** Diced chicken with Greek yogurt, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, feta cheese, lemon juice, olive oil
- **Chicken and Avocado Salad:** Diced chicken with ripe avocado, mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, zesty vinaigrette
- **Classic Chicken Salad:** Diced chicken with low-fat mayo, chopped celery, diced apple; serve on whole-grain bread/crackers
- **Spicy Chicken Salad:** Diced chicken with mayo, diced jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice

## Leftover Chicken Pasta Recipes
- **Chicken Fettuccine Alfredo:** With parmesan cheese and Alfredo sauce
- **Chicken Carbonara:** With bacon, eggs, parmesan, creamy sauce
- **Chicken Pesto Pasta:** With pesto sauce, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella

## Healthy Leftover Chicken Snacks & Light Meals
- **Chicken Wraps:** Caesar wrap, hummus wrap, avocado wrap (all with whole-grain tortillas)
- **Chicken and Veggie Kabobs:** Skewered chicken with bell peppers, onions, mushrooms; grilled/baked
- **Chicken and Quinoa Bites:** Mixed cooked quinoa with diced chicken, herbs, lemon juice; formed into bite-sized balls
- **Chicken and Spinach Pinwheels:** Cream cheese, spinach, bell peppers on whole-grain tortilla, rolled and sliced
- **Chicken and Mango Skewers:** Alternating diced chicken and mango chunks on skewers; grilled/baked
- **Chicken and Cucumber Roll-Ups:** Cream cheese, diced chicken, sliced cucumber, dill on tortilla
- **Chicken Quesadillas:** Shredded chicken with cheese, salsa, sautéed onions
- **Chicken and Rice Bowl:** Diced chicken over rice with roasted vegetables and soy sauce
- **Chicken Tacos:** Shredded chicken with salsa, avocado, sour cream, shredded cheese
- **Chicken and Veggie Wrap:** Diced chicken, mixed greens, cucumber, tomato, hummus
- **Chicken Pot Pie:** Leftover chicken with flaky crust and creamy filling

## Meal Plan Ideas (Weekly)
- Monday: Breakfast burritos with ground beef, chicken Caesar wraps, spaghetti Bolognese
- Tuesday: Chicken and avocado toast, ground beef tacos, baked chicken with roasted vegetables and quinoa
- Wednesday: Breakfast tacos with ground beef, chicken and veggie stir-fry with brown rice, meatball subs
- Thursday: Chicken and cheese omelette, beef and broccoli stir-fry with brown rice, chicken fajitas
- Friday: Breakfast quesadillas with ground beef, chicken Caesar salad, beef and mushroom gravy over egg noodles
- Saturday: Chicken and waffles, beef tacos, baked chicken with roasted potatoes and green beans
- Sunday: Breakfast burritos with ground beef, chicken and avocado wrap, spaghetti with meat sauce

## Storage/Preparation Tips for Snacks
- Prepare snacks on weekends or night before for week
- Pack in bags/car for easy access
- Store chicken salads and wraps in airtight containers for up to 3 days
- Prep chicken and quinoa bites, pinwheels, skewers on weekends; store up to 3 days
