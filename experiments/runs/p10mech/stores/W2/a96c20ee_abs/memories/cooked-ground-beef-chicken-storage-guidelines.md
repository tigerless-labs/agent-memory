## Cooked Ground Beef
- **Refrigeration:** Store in airtight shallow containers at 40°F (4°C) or below; use within 3–4 days
- **Freezing:** Wrap in plastic wrap or foil; store in freezer bags or airtight containers; lasts 3–4 months
- **Reheating:** Heat to internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)

## Cooked Chicken Breasts
- **Refrigeration:** Store in covered container at 40°F (4°C) or below; use within 3–4 days
- **Freezing:** Place in airtight container or freezer bag, remove excess air; lasts 4–6 months
- **Reheating:** Heat to internal temperature of 165°F (74°C)

## General Tips
- Cool to room temperature before refrigerating or freezing
- Always label containers with date and contents
- Use shallow airtight containers to prevent moisture and bacterial growth
- Consider portioning into smaller servings for easier future use (e.g., 1/4 cup or 1/2 cup portions)
