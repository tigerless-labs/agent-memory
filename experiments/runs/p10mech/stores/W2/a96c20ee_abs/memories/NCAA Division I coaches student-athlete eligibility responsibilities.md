## Coaches' Eligibility Monitoring Role

NCAA Division I coaches have significant responsibility for ensuring student-athletes remain eligible for competition. Key responsibilities include:

### Academic Monitoring
- Monitor athletes' grades and academic progress
- Ensure satisfactory progress toward degree completion
- Verify compliance with NCAA core course requirements

### Support & Compliance
- Provide or coordinate academic support (tutoring, study halls)
- Work with academic advisors and compliance officers
- Ensure athletes meet all NCAA rules and regulations

### Accountability
- Coaches are held accountable for violations of NCAA eligibility rules by their athletes
- Creates strong incentive to ensure compliance

This significant responsibility contributes to coaching being a challenging and demanding role, though coaches do receive recognition for their contributions to student-athlete success both athletically and academically.
