Planning bike maintenance before next ride:
- Oil the chain (use appropriate lube type for conditions)
- Adjust derailleur (check limit screws and alignment with cassette/chainrings)

Additional maintenance checks recommended: tire pressure, brake pad wear, bolts and quick releases, general inspection.
