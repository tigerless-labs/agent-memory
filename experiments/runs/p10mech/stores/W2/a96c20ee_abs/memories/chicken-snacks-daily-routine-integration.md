## Breakfast Ideas with Leftover Chicken
- **Chicken and Avocado Wrap:** Prepare night before, store in fridge, grab-and-go
- **Chicken and Quinoa Bites:** Make batch on weekend, store in airtight container up to 3 days
- **Chicken Salad on Whole-Grain Toast:** Prepare batch Sunday evening, store in fridge up to 3 days; serve with fried egg or on oatmeal topping

## Post-Workout Snacks
- **Chicken and Mango Skewers:** Make batch on weekend, store in airtight container up to 3 days; refreshing option
- **Chicken and Spinach Pinwheels:** Make batch on weekend, store in airtight container up to 3 days; protein-packed
- **Chicken Caesar Wrap:** Prepare after workout for satisfying, filling snack

## Mid-Morning or Afternoon Snacks
- **Chicken and Cucumber Roll-Ups:** Prepare batch on weekend, store in airtight container up to 3 days
- **Chicken and Hummus Wrap:** Make batch on weekend, store in airtight container up to 3 days; healthy and filling
- **Chicken Salad on Crackers:** Prepare batch Sunday evening, store in fridge up to 3 days; serve with carrot sticks or cherry tomatoes

## Tips for Integration
- Plan ahead: prepare snacks on weekend or night before to save time during week
- Pack snacks in bag/car for easy access throughout day
- Keep it simple: choose snacks requiring minimal ingredients
- Get creative: experiment with different ingredients and flavors to maintain interest
- Make it a habit: incorporate healthy snacking into daily routine consistently
