Fresno diner options recommended for breakfast stops (particularly on return trips from hikes/camping):

1. **Max's Bistro & Bar** — Known for generous portions and creative breakfast; signature "Max's Breakfast" burrito with scrambled eggs and chorizo
2. **The Breakfast Club** — Retro-style diner with classic breakfast fare; breakfast tacos, waffle sandwiches, omelets
3. **Pete's Breakfast House** — 30+ year Fresno institution; giant pancakes, omelets, breakfast combos
4. **Frank's Place** — Operating since 1947, no-frills diner; famous "Frank's Favorite" breakfast plate (eggs, bacon, sausage)
5. **Denny's** — Chain diner with multiple Fresno locations; wide variety of breakfast options including Grand Slam
