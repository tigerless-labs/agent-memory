## Brake Pad Decision

User considering upgrade for increased drifting frequency.

**Options evaluated:**
- **High-performance pads:** Good for spirited/track driving, more affordable, sufficient for casual drifting
- **Racing-specific pads:** Better heat tolerance, fade resistance, more expensive, handles repeated high-speed braking

**Decision pending:** Based on driving intensity and budget

## Exhaust System Decision

**Options evaluated:**
- **Cat-back exhaust:** Less expensive, easier install, moderate power gains, limited by stock downpipe/converter
- **Full exhaust system:** More expensive, custom tailoring possible, significant power gains especially mid-range/high-end, requires potential tune/engine management

**Decision made:** Going with **full exhaust system**
- Reason: Unlock full potential of GT-R's engine
- Next step: Research reputable manufacturers for GT-R-specific systems
- Complementary upgrade: Considering engine tune or management system to optimize new exhaust

## Material Preferences for Upgrades
- Look for high-quality exhaust materials: stainless steel or titanium for durability/corrosion resistance
- Check for performance warranty/guarantee on exhaust system
