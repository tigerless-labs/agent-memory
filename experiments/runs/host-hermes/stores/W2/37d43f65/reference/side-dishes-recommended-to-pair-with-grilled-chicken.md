Recommended side dishes for grilled chicken:
1. Grilled vegetables: bell peppers, zucchini, eggplant with salt, pepper, and olive oil.
2. Fresh salad: classic Caesar salad or Greek salad with cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, feta cheese, and olives.
3. Corn on the cob: grilled corn slathered with butter and sprinkled with salt and paprika.
4. Baked sweet potato: baked in the oven with butter, cinnamon, and brown sugar.
5. Quinoa salad: cooked quinoa with chopped cucumbers, tomatoes, bell peppers, and a lemony vinaigrette.
