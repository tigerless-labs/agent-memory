### Ingredients
- 4-6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

### Instructions for Grill
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (400-450°F).
2. In a large bowl, whisk olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
3. Coat chicken breasts in marinade; cover with plastic wrap and marinate 20-30 minutes at room temperature.
4. Oil the grill grates.
5. Place chicken smooth side down; cook 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches at least 165°F.
6. Rest chicken 5-10 minutes before slicing.

### Instructions for Grill Pan / Cast-Iron Skillet
1. Prepare marinade and coat chicken for 20-30 minutes at room temperature.
2. Heat grill pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Cook chicken breasts 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches at least 165°F.
4. Rest 5-10 minutes before slicing.
