The team uses an objective checklist to assess whether a property appraisal order is complex and whether a higher fee is justified, evaluating Location, Property Type/Design, Age/Condition, Zoning/Land Use, and Market Conditions. An appraiser's designation of complexity must not be taken at face value due to financial incentives; decisions must be based on objective criteria and independent research.
