## Summer 3-day itinerary for couples:

**Day 1:**
- **Niushou Mountain** — Buddhist cultural park with temples, pagodas, modern Buddhist museum, giant Buddha statue
- **Qinhuai River** — historic scenic waterway; romantic boat rides, especially beautiful at night with colorful lights

**Day 2:**
- **Nanjing City Wall** — ancient fortification, great city views, walkable/bikeable along wall with gates and watchtowers
- **Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan)** — Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, beautiful gardens, hiking trails, Xuanwu Lake, Botanical Garden

**Day 3:**
- **Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple Area)** — historic district with temples, shops, street vendors, beautiful nighttime lights
- **Xuanwu Lake** — tranquil natural area, scenic walks, boat rides, sculptures and monuments

## Winter 3-day itinerary for couples:

**Day 1:**
- **Niushou Mountain** — serene and peaceful in winter, refreshing hike, Buddhist cultural park
- **Purple Mountain** — quiet atmosphere, snow-covered landscape, Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Xuanwu Lake boat rides

**Day 2:**
- **Nanjing City Wall** — unique winter quality, cool weather, ancient military defense system
- **Confucius Temple Area (Fuzimiao)** — peaceful and romantic atmosphere, local culture and restaurants

**Day 3:**
- **Ming Palace Ruins (Minggugong)** — mysterious quality in winter, remains of Ming Dynasty imperial palace
- **Presidential Palace (Zongtong Fu)** — serene and tranquil, Sun Yat-sen's residence, gardens, China's modern history
