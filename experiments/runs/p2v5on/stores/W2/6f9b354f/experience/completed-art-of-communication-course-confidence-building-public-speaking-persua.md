Took an Art of Communication course covering:

**Core Skills:**
- Building self-confidence and self-esteem
- Overcoming glossophobia (fear of public speaking)
- Confidence frameworks for managing nervousness
- Articulation framework for clear, effective speaking
- Audience structure analysis and tailoring messages
- Persuasion techniques and impactful presentation
- Delivering speeches with engagement and energy
- Personal branding and LinkedIn profile maintenance
- Active listening and conversation techniques
- Professional networking

**Key Learnings:**
- Communication is a learnable skill, not an innate talent
- Effective communication is a two-way process requiring active listening and understanding
- Communication requires effort, patience, and practice
- Nonverbal communication (body language, tone of voice) is as important as verbal communication
- Communication effectiveness depends on tailoring to audience and context
