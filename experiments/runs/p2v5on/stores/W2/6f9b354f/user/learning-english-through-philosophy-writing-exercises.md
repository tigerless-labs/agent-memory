Session 2023/05/21: Working on passages about Ayn Rand's philosophy of altruism vs self-interest. Grammar and phrasing corrections:

**Errors → Corrections:**
- "Ayn Land" → "Ayn Rand" (proper noun)
- "somewaht" → "somewhat" (spelling)
- "Ayn Land thinks that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must take care of ourselves to live in a somewaht 'selfish' manner." → "Ayn Rand believes that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests in order to live, which some might consider a somewhat 'selfish' way of thinking." (verb tense consistency, more natural phrasing)

**Patterns learned:**
- Use "believes/believed" with philosophical figures for consistency
- "prioritize one's own interests" is more precise than "take care of ourselves"
- Qualify subjective terms: "which some might consider..." rather than assuming tone
