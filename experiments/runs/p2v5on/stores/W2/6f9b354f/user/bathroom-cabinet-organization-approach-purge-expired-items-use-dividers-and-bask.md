Strategy for organizing cluttered bathroom cabinets:

1. **Purge and declutter first** — Remove everything, sort into keep/donate/discard, get rid of expired products and unused items
2. **Use dividers and baskets** — Separate compartments by category to keep similar items together
3. **Store heavy items below** — Hair dryers, curling irons on lower shelves
4. **Utilize back of door** — Use over-the-door racks, hooks, adhesive pockets for storage
5. **Label everything** — Label cabinets, shelves, baskets so items are easy to find
6. **Keep frequently used items front and center** — Most accessible locations for daily items
7. **Consider lazy Susan** — For hard-to-reach corner spaces

This systematic approach helps create a functional, organized space and supports the overall cleaning routine and mental well-being.
