Planning to use AI tools for:\n- Content creation (humanizing AI-generated content while maintaining authenticity)\n- Research for topic ideas and keyword suggestions\n- Engagement through chatbots and sentiment analysis\n- Content personalization\n\nApproach: Define brand voice/tone guide, train AI on existing content, always review/curate AI output before publishing, treat AI as starting point not final product.\n\nTools to evaluate: **BuzzSumo** (trending, content ideas), **Ahrefs** (content explorer, keywords), **Answer Engine** (Q&A insights), **Quora** (community Q&A inspiration). Also considering: SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner, Moz, Ubersuggest.
