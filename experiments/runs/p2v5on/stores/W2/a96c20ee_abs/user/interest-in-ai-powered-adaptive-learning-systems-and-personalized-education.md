Interested in how AI can tailor instruction to individual students' needs, with specific focus on:

- Adaptive learning systems and their effectiveness (meta-analysis: effect size 0.34)
- Real-time feedback mechanisms and impact on student performance
- Development of critical thinking and problem-solving skills via AI
- Collaborative problem-solving facilitated by AI systems
- NLP-based feedback for writing and argumentation
- Addressing equity, access, and teacher buy-in challenges

This interest developed after attending first research conference at Harvard University (May 2023) where saw AI in education projects.
