## Ground Beef Burgers
- Classic Cheeseburgers: season with salt, pepper, spices; grill or pan-fry; serve with cheese, lettuce, tomato, condiments
- Sliders with Caramelized Onions: ground beef with minced onion; small patties on small buns
- Spicy Southwestern Burgers: add jalapeños, cumin, chili powder; top with pepper jack, avocado, fried egg
- Greek-Style Stuffed Burgers: ground beef mixed with feta, kalamata olives, oregano; serve with tzatziki

## Chicken Breast Sandwiches & Dishes
- Classic: season chicken breasts; grill/pan-fry; serve with lettuce, tomato, mayo
- Buffalo: marinate in hot sauce and ranch dressing; serve with celery, blue cheese
- Club: grilled chicken topped with bacon, lettuce, tomato, mayo
- Caesar: grilled chicken on bun with romaine, parmesan, Caesar dressing

## Soups
- Chicken Noodle: shred chicken, add to soup with noodles and vegetables
- Creamy Chicken and Rice: blend chicken with broth, rice, heavy cream
- Chicken Tortilla: shredded chicken with tortillas, diced tomatoes, onions, spices

## Salads
- Chicken Caesar: chopped chicken with romaine, croutons, parmesan, Caesar dressing
- Greek: diced chicken with Greek yogurt, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, feta, lemon juice, olive oil
- Chicken and Avocado: diced chicken with avocado, mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, vinaigrette

## Pasta
- Fettuccine Alfredo: cooked fettuccine with chicken, parmesan, Alfredo sauce
- Carbonara: spaghetti with chicken, bacon, eggs, parmesan, creamy sauce
- Pesto Pasta: cooked pasta with chicken, pesto sauce, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella

## Healthy Snacks
- Chicken Salad: classic (mayo, celery, apple); Greek (yogurt, cucumber, tomato, feta); spicy (mayo, jalapeño, cilantro, lime)
- Wraps: Caesar wrap (tortilla, chicken, lettuce, avocado, Caesar dressing); Hummus wrap (hummus, chicken, greens, cucumber); Avocado wrap (chicken, mashed avocado, greens, red pepper flakes)
- Chicken and Veggie Kabobs: skewered chicken, peppers, onions, mushrooms; brush with olive oil; grill/bake
- Chicken and Quinoa Bites: mix quinoa with diced chicken, herbs, lemon juice; form into balls
- Chicken and Spinach Pinwheels: spread cream cheese on tortilla; layer chicken, spinach, peppers; roll and slice
- Chicken and Mango Skewers: alternate chicken and mango chunks; brush with olive oil; grill/bake
- Chicken and Cucumber Roll-Ups: spread cream cheese on tortilla; layer chicken, cucumber, dill; roll and slice

## Storage Tips
- Refrigeration: cooked ground beef/chicken in airtight shallow containers; keep at 40°F or below; use within 3-4 days
- Freezing: wrap tightly or place in freezer bag; label and date; ground beef lasts 3-4 months; chicken lasts 4-6 months
- Portioning: divide into smaller portions for easier future use; shred or dice for versatility
- General: cool to room temperature before storing; use airtight containers; reheat to 165°F for safety
