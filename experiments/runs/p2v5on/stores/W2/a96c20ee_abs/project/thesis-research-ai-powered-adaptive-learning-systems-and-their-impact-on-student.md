## Research Focus

Effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in mathematics and reading comprehension. Research took a broad conceptual approach with specific platforms/systems as case studies.

## Key Findings

- AI-powered adaptive learning systems can be effective in improving student learning outcomes
- Implementation and integration into classrooms require careful consideration
- Potential to help reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students

## Common Challenges Identified (across case studies)

1. **Teacher Professional Development**: Lack of necessary skills and knowledge hindered effective implementation
2. **Curriculum Alignment**: Clear alignment with curriculum and learning goals was critical for teacher adoption
3. **Algorithmic Bias**: Systems carry risk of bias in algorithms, potentially exacerbating existing inequities if not addressed
4. **Teacher Role Tension**: Debate among educators about whether systems replace or augment teaching

## Recommendations Made

- Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support
- Developers should work closely with educators to ensure systems align with needs of teachers and students
- Ensure clear guidance on curriculum alignment and learning goals
- Implement ongoing evaluation and assessment of system effectiveness
- Address algorithmic bias concerns to promote equity and inclusion

## Presentation & Feedback

- Presented research as a poster at first research conference (summer 2022)
- Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers for nuanced approach
- Developers reached out afterward, interested in applying findings to their work
- Conference attendance was significant milestone in research career
