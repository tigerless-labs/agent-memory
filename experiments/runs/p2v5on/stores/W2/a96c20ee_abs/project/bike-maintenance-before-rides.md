**Chain:** Clean first to remove dirt/grime, then apply appropriate lube (wet or dry), wipe excess.\n\n**Derailleur:** Check limit screws are properly adjusted and aligned with cassette/chainrings. Take to bike shop if uncomfortable adjusting.\n\n**Other checks:** Tire pressure, brake pad wear, all components securely attached.
