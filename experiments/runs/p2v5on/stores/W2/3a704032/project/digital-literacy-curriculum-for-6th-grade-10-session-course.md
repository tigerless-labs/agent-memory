Creating a media education curriculum for elementary school 6th graders covering digital literacy topics.

**Structure**
- Multi-session course (at least 10 sessions planned)
- Taught as 'media education instructor'
- Lesson plans presented in tabular form

**Session 10: Evaluating Online Information**
Teaches evaluation of online source credibility using the CRAAP Test (Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose). Activities include practicing CRAAP Test on sample articles, student research with source evaluation, and creating tips lists.
