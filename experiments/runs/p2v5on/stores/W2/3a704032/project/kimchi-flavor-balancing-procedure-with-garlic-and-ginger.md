**For garlic (2–3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables):**
- Use fresh garlic only
- Mince or crush to release oils
- Mix into seasoning paste with gochugaru and other ingredients
- Optional: sauté in oil first to mellow intensity

**For ginger (1–2 inches grated per 2 cups vegetables):**
- Use fresh ginger, peeled
- Grate or slice thinly to release oils
- Combine with garlic, gochugaru, and fish sauce for balanced profile
- Optional: sauté to mellow intensity

**General:**
- Use separate cutting board/knife to prevent flavor transfer
- Taste and adjust incrementally
- Avoid bruising garlic/ginger as it releases bitterness
