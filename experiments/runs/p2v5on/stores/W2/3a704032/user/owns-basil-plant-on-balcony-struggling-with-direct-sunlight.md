Basil plant on balcony has been struggling with too much direct sun. Plan to move it to a shadier spot (east- or west-facing location with indirect light). Has been using general-purpose potting mix but considering switching to custom mix with better drainage (2 parts peat moss/coco coir, 1 part perlite/vermiculite, 1 part compost, 1 part general potting soil).
