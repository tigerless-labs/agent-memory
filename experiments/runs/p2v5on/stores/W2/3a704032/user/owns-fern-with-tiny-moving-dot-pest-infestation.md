Fern has tiny moving dot pests (likely spider mites or springtails) crawling on leaves and stems. Plan to treat with neem oil spray. Will mist fern 2-3 times per week with distilled/filtered water to maintain humidity (40-60% RH) and help prevent pest return. Misting in morning to allow leaves to dry before nightfall.
