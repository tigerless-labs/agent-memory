Enjoys:

**Contemporary fiction**
- Strong female protagonists with complex relationships
- Exploration of identity, power dynamics, and personal conflict
- Character-driven narratives over plot-driven
- Intersectional representation (race, class, gender, immigrant experience)
- Female relationships portrayed with nuance — supportive yet competitive, loving yet complicated

**Graphic novels**
- Similar to _Saga_ by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples
- Science fiction and fantasy with complex characters and moral ambiguity
- Rich worldbuilding

**Also enjoys**
- Mythology retellings
- Historical fiction

Drawn to stories exploring themes of identity, hidden selves, and how personal experiences shape who we become.
