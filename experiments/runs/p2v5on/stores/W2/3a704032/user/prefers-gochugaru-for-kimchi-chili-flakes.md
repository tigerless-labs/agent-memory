Chose standard Korean gochugaru (medium to hot heat level, balanced flavor) as the primary chili flakes for kimchi-making. Appreciates its versatility and the recommendation to start conservative (1/4–1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables for mild heat) and adjust upward by taste.
