Recommended digital tools for facilitating interactive reflection and discussion in digital literacy classes:

**Padlet** — Collaborative platform where students share thoughts and ideas in a virtual space

**Nearpod** — Interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions to encourage student participation

**Flipgrid** — Video discussion platform allowing students to respond to prompts with short videos

**Google Jamboard** — Virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual idea sharing

**Facilitation techniques:** Open-ended questions, paraphrasing and summarizing contributions, encouraging active listening, using visual aids and graphic organizers.
