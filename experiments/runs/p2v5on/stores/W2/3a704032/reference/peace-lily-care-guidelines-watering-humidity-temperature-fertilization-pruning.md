# Peace Lily Care Guidelines

**Lighting:**
- Prefers bright, indirect light
- Avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch)
- East or west-facing windows ideal
- Low light may prevent blooming

**Watering:**
- Check soil: water when top 1–2 inches feel dry to touch
- Growing season (spring/summer): typically every 7–10 days
- Dormant season (fall/winter): every 10–14 days
- Overwatering is most common cause of death
- Ensure pot has good drainage to prevent waterlogged soil

**Humidity:**
- Prefers humid environment but can tolerate average humidity (40–50%)
- Misting: 1–2 times per week sufficient for average conditions
- Mist more frequently (every 3–4 days) in dry climates
- Mist in morning so leaves dry during day (prevents fungal disease)
- Can place pot on water-filled tray with pebbles or use humidifier

**Temperature:**
- Ideal range: 68–72°F (20–22°C)
- Acceptable range: 65–75°F (18–24°C)
- Avoid temperatures below 55°F (13°C) or above 85°F (29°C)
- Avoid drafts, heating/cooling vents, fireplaces

**Fertilization:**
- Growing season (spring/summer): every 1–2 weeks
- Dormant season (fall/winter): once per month
- Use balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (e.g., 20–20–20)
- Dilute to half recommended strength to avoid root burn
- Prefer slightly higher nitrogen (e.g., 20–15–15)

**Pruning & Maintenance:**
- Remove dead/dying leaves and flowers to prevent disease
- Use clean, sharp scissors/pruning shears
- Generally doesn't need frequent repotting
- Repot in spring/summer if outgrown container or showing nutrient deficiency signs
- Use well-draining potting mix (tropical or orchid-specific good choice)

**Pests:**
- Check regularly for spider mites, mealybugs, scale
- Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if detected

**Leaf Loss:**
- Normal when plant adjusts to new environment
- Monitor overall plant health; adjust care as needed
