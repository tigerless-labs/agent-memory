## Recommended platforms for contemporary art discovery\n\n- **Artsy** — Features emerging and established artists; dedicated emerging artist section; filter by medium, style\n- **Saatchi Art** — Online art gallery focused on emerging artists; curated collections and exhibitions\n- **Artspace** — Focuses on contemporary art and emerging artists; curated selection; direct artist purchase\n- **Uprise Art** — Emerging artists and contemporary art; curated artworks, artist profiles, exhibitions; purchase available\n\nOther platforms mentioned: Behance, Instagram, Juxtapoz, Hyperallergic, New American Paintings, ArtNet, The Artist's Institute, Platform\n\nThese platforms typically use algorithms and human curation for personalized experiences, offer online exhibitions, artist showcases, virtual events, and curated collections.
