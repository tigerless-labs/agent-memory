## Fertilizer recommendations

**Snake Plant & Succulents:**
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20) for snake plants
- Miracle-Gro Cactus, Palm & Citrus Food (10-20-10) for succulents
- Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer (2-2-2) for snake plants
- Espoma Organic Cactus! Fertilizer (5-2-6) for succulents
- Schultz All Purpose Plant Food (20-20-20) for snake plants
- Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10) for succulents

**Orchids (need higher phosphorus):**
- Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10)
- Espoma Organic Orchid! Fertilizer (15-30-15)
- Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20)

General tip: Dilute all fertilizers to half strength to avoid burning roots. Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) only.

## Humidifier recommendations

**For general plant humidity:**
- Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Humidifier (6-liter tank, cool/warm mist, humidistat)
- Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier (1-liter tank, compact)
- Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier (2.5-liter tank, ultrasonic)
- Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier (1-liter tank, quiet operation)

**For African Violets (preferred):**
- Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier
- Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier
- Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier

African Violets prefer 50-60% humidity; position humidifier 1-2 feet away.

## Potting mix recommendations

**For Spider Plant:**
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix

Look for mixes with peat moss/coir, compost, and perlite or vermiculite for drainage. Spider plants prefer to be slightly root-bound.
