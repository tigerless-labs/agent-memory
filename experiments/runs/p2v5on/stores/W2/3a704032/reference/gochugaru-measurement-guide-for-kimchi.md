Heat level guidelines for gochugaru per 2 cups of vegetables:
- **Mild**: 1/4–1/2 tsp
- **Medium**: 1/2–1 tsp  
- **Hot**: 1–2 tsp
- **Extremely hot**: 2+ tsp

Tips: Measure by weight (grams/ounces) on a digital scale for accuracy; different brands vary in heat intensity; taste as you go since adding is easier than removing; store airtight in cool, dark place.
