Started reading _The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo_ by Taylor Jenkins Reid on January 29, 2023. Loving the character development and complex relationships. Particularly appreciates:

- The exploration of identity: resonates with themes of public vs. private selves, the different personas/masks we wear
- Evelyn's intersectional identity as a woman, Latina, and immigrant — how these shape her in Hollywood
- Monique as narrator — brings outside perspective while being an active participant in uncovering Evelyn's story
- The portrayal of relationships between women — both supportive and competitive, loving and complicated
- How the book balances glamour with deeper emotional themes and moral complexity

The character-driven narrative and nuanced exploration of female relationships especially resonates.
