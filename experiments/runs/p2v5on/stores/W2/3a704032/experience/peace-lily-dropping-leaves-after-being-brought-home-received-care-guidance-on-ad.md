Peace lily was losing leaves after recently being moved to a new home. Received comprehensive care guidance to help it adjust, including:

**Factors addressed:**
- Lighting: Needs bright, indirect light; avoid direct sun
- Watering: Overwatering is common cause of leaf drop; check soil moisture before watering
- Humidity: Prefers above 50%; can use water/pebble tray or humidifier
- Temperature: Prefers 65-80°F (18-27°C); avoid drafts/heat vents
- Fertilization: Use balanced fertilizer at half strength during growing season
- Soil: Ensure well-draining potting mix, possibly repot with tropical plant mix
- Acclimation: Takes weeks for recovery from transplant shock

Also noted: May have been exposed to pests or diseases from newly added plants; should monitor and isolate if issues appear.
