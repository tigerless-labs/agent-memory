Made first batch of kimchi last weekend. Failed — too much spice and salt. Has decided to:
- Rinse the kimchi under cold running water to remove excess salt
- Use gochugaru (standard Korean chili flakes) going forward
- Add garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables) and ginger (1-2 inches grated per 2 cups) to balance flavors

Plans to retry with corrected measurements and preparation techniques.
