During a family camping trip in April 2023, dad experimented with a BBQ sauce recipe by adding smoked paprika for deeper flavor. The sauce was served with classic pork sausages and was very successful—everyone loved it. The meal also included grilled vegetables, potatoes, corn on the cob, and homemade marshmallows made by mom for s'mores.
