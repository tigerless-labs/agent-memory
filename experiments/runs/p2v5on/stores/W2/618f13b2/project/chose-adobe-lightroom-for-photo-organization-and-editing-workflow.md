Selected Adobe Lightroom for managing photo collection from beach vacation and wildlife outing, motivated by existing comfort with Adobe ecosystem. Uses Lightroom's features including: Smart Collections for date range and location filtering, separate collections for photo types (landscapes, wildlife, flora), and Develop module for editing (exposure, contrast, shadow recovery adjustments).
