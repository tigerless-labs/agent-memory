Bike wash soaps: Finish Line, Pedro's, Simple Green, Muc-Off. Wheel cleaners: Pedro's Wheel Cleaner, Finish Line Wheel Cleaner, Muc-Off Wheel Cleaner. Use soft-bristled brushes and microfiber cloths; test on inconspicuous areas first; dry thoroughly to prevent water spots.
