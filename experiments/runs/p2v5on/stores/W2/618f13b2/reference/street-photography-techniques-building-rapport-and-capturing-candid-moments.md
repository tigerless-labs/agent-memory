Techniques for approaching strangers and capturing candid shots:

**Building Rapport:**
- Show genuine interest; ask questions and listen actively
- Make eye contact and smile; use open body language
- Introduce yourself and explain your photography project
- Be respectful of boundaries and personal space
- Respect decisions; no pressure if someone declines

**Capturing Candid Moments:**
- Anticipate moments and study behavior patterns
- Wait for the 'decisive moment' (Henri Cartier-Bresson concept)
- Shoot in burst mode to increase chances of perfect shot
- Focus on eyes and facial expressions
- Use soft, natural light; avoid harsh midday sun
- Experiment with unusual angles (low/high vantage points)
- Shoot from hip or chest level to stay inconspicuous
- Capture the mundane, not just extraordinary moments

**Practical Approach:**
- Blend in with neutral clothing
- Keep interactions brief and to the point
- Show your work to build comfort and trust
- Be prepared for rejection and move on gracefully
- Shoot in burst mode when interesting moment unfolds
