## Shoe cleaning techniques

### Converse Chuck Taylor All Stars
- Brush off loose dirt with soft-bristled brush
- Mix mild soap (diluted dish soap) with warm water
- Wipe down gently; avoid soaking
- For tough stains: baking soda + water or commercial stain remover
- Condition rubber toe cap with rubber conditioner or silicone lubricant
- Use waterproofing spray for protection
- Air dry at room temperature (avoid direct sunlight/heat)
- Clean laces separately with mild soap and water

### Adidas Superstars (white)
- Remove loose dirt with soft brush or cloth
- Gentle cleaning solution: 1 tbsp mild soap + 1 cup warm water (or equal water + white vinegar)
- Dampen cloth in solution, wring thoroughly, wipe entire surface
- For scuffs: gentle scrubbing motion, don't apply pressure
- Rinse with clean damp cloth to remove soap residue
- Blot with dry cloth, air dry at room temperature
- Consider fabric protector or waterproofing spray for prevention
- Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive cleaners, rough cloths
- Optional products: Sneaker Cleaner, Jason Markk (test on small area first)

## Finding a good cobbler

1. **Online search**: "cobblers near me" or "shoe repair near me" + city/zip
2. **Directories**: Yelp, Google Maps, Bing Places
3. **Word of mouth**: Ask friends, family, colleagues, shoe store associates
4. **Associations**: National Shoe Repair Association (NSRA), Shoe Service Institute of America (SSIA)
5. **Social media & reviews**: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Angie's List, Thumbtack, TaskRabbit
6. **Local shoe stores**: Ask about on-site repair or recommendations

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Check reputation and reviews
- Ask about experience with specific repairs (heel repair, leather)
- Get quote and compare prices
- Check turnaround time

## Leather boot care

- **Conditioning**: Regular high-quality leather conditioner/oil keeps leather soft and protected
- **Waterproofing**: Apply spray or cream, especially in rainy/snowy climates
- **Cleanliness**: Regular cleaning with soft dry cloth; avoid harsh chemicals
- **Storage**: Cool, dry place away from direct sunlight; use shoe trees or stuff with paper
- **Temperature**: Avoid extreme heat/cold that dries or cracks leather
- **Protection**: Apply leather protector to high-wear areas (toes, heels)
- **Brushing**: Use soft-bristled leather brush for regular maintenance
- **Professional care**: Consider periodic cobbler maintenance (polishing, conditioning)
- **Avoid**: Over-conditioning (causes greasy residue)
