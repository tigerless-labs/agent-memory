## Reviewed photo management tools:\n\n**Adobe Lightroom** - Chosen option. Offers robust cataloging, tagging, keywording, Smart Collections (with date range and location filters), collections, ratings/flags, develop module for editing.\n\n**Adobe Bridge** - Free companion to Creative Cloud. Provides media manager with tagging and keywording.\n\n**Google Photos** - User-friendly online platform with automatic organization and tagging.\n\n**Other options** - Apple Photos, Capture One, digiKam (varies by OS).\n\n## Key Lightroom features used:\n- Smart Collections: Auto-include photos by date range and location\n- Collections: Manual organization by photo type\n- Develop Module: Adjustments including exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, white balance\n- Sync: Apply edits across multiple photos\n- Grid/Loupe views: Efficient multi-photo and detail editing
