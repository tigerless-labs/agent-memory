Trek FX 2 recommended tire pressures: smooth roads and bike paths 80-100 PSI (5.5-6.9 bar); rough roads and dirt trails 60-80 PSI (4.1-5.5 bar). For trail riding, aim for 60-70 PSI (4.1-4.8 bar) for better traction and comfort on uneven terrain.
