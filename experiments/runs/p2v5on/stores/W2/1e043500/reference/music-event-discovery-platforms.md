## Platforms for Finding Concerts and Festivals\n- **Songkick**: Track artists, receive concert notifications for your area\n- **Bandsintown**: Similar to Songkick, with concert alerts and discovery\n- **Eventbrite**: Event ticketing, filter by location/genre/date\n- **Facebook Events**: Search events in your area\n- **Venue Websites**: Check local concert halls and amphitheaters\n- **Music Festival Websites**: Specific festival lineups and tickets\n- **Local Music Blogs**: Coverage of local music events\n- **Social Media**: Follow artists, promoters, and venues for updates
