Primary Visual Art Forms in The Gambia:

Batik Printing — Technique using wax to resist dye, creates traditional patterns and designs unique to Gambian culture.

Woodcarving — Traditional techniques producing statues, masks, and utensils representing cultural heritage.

Pottery — Rich history using coiling and throwing techniques to create culturally significant everyday utensils.

Photography — Documents ceremonies (weddings, naming ceremonies, cultural celebrations), everyday life, clothing, and food; creates archival records and educates younger generations.

Painting — Depicts cultural traditions, traditional clothing, food, dance, historical events, and significant places.

Contemporary Developments: Contemporary art forms (installations, performance, conceptual art) connecting to global art scene; digital technology use (digital paintings, animations addressing climate change, social justice, human rights); Pan-African collaborations exploring shared themes; youth engagement programs teaching cultural preservation through art.
