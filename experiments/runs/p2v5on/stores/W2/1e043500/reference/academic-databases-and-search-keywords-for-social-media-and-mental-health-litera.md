**Key Academic Databases:**
- PsycINFO (American Psychological Association) — premier database for psychology and mental health research
- PubMed (National Library of Medicine) — comprehensive biomedical and life sciences literature
- Scopus (Elsevier) — large interdisciplinary database covering STEM, social sciences, humanities
- Web of Science (Clarivate Analytics) — multidisciplinary database for science, social sciences, humanities
- Google Scholar — search engine for scholarly literature across disciplines

**Core Search Keywords:**
- Social media, mental health, young adults, depression, anxiety, symptoms
- Usage patterns, online social networking, digital media, psychological well-being
- Mediators: self-esteem, social support, self-concept, social connections, social capital
- Relationships: mediating, moderating, intervening, indirect effects
- Interventions: social media-based, online, digital, e-mental health, internet-based
- Policies: social media regulation, online safety, mental health policy
- Initiatives: mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, peer-to-peer support

**Boolean Search Examples:**
- ("social media" AND "mental health" AND "young adults") OR ("social media" AND "depression" AND "anxiety" AND "young adults")
- ("social media" AND "self-esteem" AND "depression") OR ("social media" AND "social support" AND "anxiety")
- ("social media-based intervention" AND "mental health" AND "young adults")
- ("social media" AND "mental health awareness" AND "campaigns")
- ("social media" AND "online support groups" AND "mental health") OR ("peer support" AND "online communities" AND "mental health support")
