## Streaming Services\n- **Bandcamp**: Discover artists in ambient/lo-fi, browse by tags/labels\n- **Apple Music**: 'Moods' section for relaxing music, 'New Music Mix' playlist updates regularly\n- **Tidal**: Human-curated recommendations, 'Rising' section for emerging artists\n- **Deezer**: AI-powered discovery, 'Mood' section\n\n## Key Spotify Playlists\n- Lo-Fi Girl (2M+ followers): Lo-fi hip-hop and chillhop\n- Ambient Universe: Ambient, drone, experimental\n- Chillhop Music: Lo-fi hip-hop and instrumental\n- Coffee Shop Jazz: Relaxing atmosphere\n- Discover Weekly and Release Radar: Personalized recommendations
