**Research Question:** How are social media usage patterns associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults?

**Thesis Focus Areas:**
1. Direct relationships between social media usage and mental health outcomes (depression, anxiety)
2. Mediating roles of self-esteem and social support in these relationships
3. Implications for mental health interventions and policies
4. Effectiveness of social media-based interventions
5. Using social media platforms for mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, and peer-to-peer support initiatives

**Status:** Thesis proposal submitted and received positive feedback from advisor (May 2023). Currently conducting literature review phase.
