Decided to build personal brand around human-first hiring for tech teams. Core philosophy: prioritize hiring great human beings first, then technologists second. Value EQ over IQ in software developers. Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just technical skill. Focus: thought leadership in team building emphasizing interpersonal skills and emotional intelligence as critical to high-performing tech teams.
