Developed content strategy to establish thought leadership around human-first hiring philosophy. Content formats: blog posts exploring importance of hiring great humans and identifying key traits; podcasts/videos with interviews from industry experts on building successful teams; webinars and workshops providing practical tips for team building and hiring; e-books and guides on psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation, fostering collaboration; social media for sharing content, case studies, and team member profiles embodying brand values. Strategy focuses on providing practical insights and engaging audience through multiple channels.
