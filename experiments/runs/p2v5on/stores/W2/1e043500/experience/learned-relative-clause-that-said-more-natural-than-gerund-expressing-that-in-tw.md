**Sentence improvement (2023-05-20):**

Less natural:
`I just saw a tweet expressing that the TV show was interesting.`

More natural:
`I just saw a tweet that said the TV show was interesting.`

**Key insight:** Using a relative clause (that + verb) is more natural than gerund + that construction when describing what a tweet or statement conveyed. The structure 'that said' (relative pronoun + past-tense verb) reads more like spoken English than 'expressing that'.
