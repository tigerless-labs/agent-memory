Conducted research into challenges NGOs face when distributing humanitarian aid in Africa, focusing on systemic barriers and technological solutions.

**Key Challenges Identified:**

Infrastructure:
- Lack of basic infrastructure (roads, bridges, ports) making remote aid transport difficult

Political & Security:
- Political instability and conflict limiting safe NGO operations

Corruption & Accountability:
- Widespread corruption and bribery during aid distribution
- Lack of reliable and flexible accountability systems
- Difficulty demonstrating impact to donors and communities
- Challenges tracking aid and ensuring it reaches intended recipients

Operational:
- Limited resources (funding, personnel, equipment)
- High operational costs due to poor infrastructure and import needs
- Reliance on manual data entry and tracking systems

Social:
- Cultural and language barriers in community communication and collaboration

**Solution Explored:**
Examined Genius Voucher platform as a potential solution to address accountability gaps and operational inefficiencies, particularly its offline-capable technology suited to Africa's connectivity constraints and its role in preventing corruption through transparent, automated tracking.
