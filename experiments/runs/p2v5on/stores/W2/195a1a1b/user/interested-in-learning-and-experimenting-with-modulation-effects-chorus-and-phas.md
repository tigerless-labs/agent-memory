User is exploring modulation effects for the first time and planning to experiment with chorus and phaser effects on guitar solos.

**Starting approach:**
- Experiment with both chorus and phaser to see which fits their solo better
- Try combining modulation effects with other pedals (overdrive, delay)
- Practice with a metronome to stay in time while experimenting
- Vary effect settings to find preferred sound

**Reference guitarists using modulation effects:**
- David Gilmour (Pink Floyd) — chorus and phaser on 'Comfortably Numb', 'Shine On You Crazy Diamond'
- Eddie Van Halen — flanger and phaser effects for 'brown sound' tone
- The Edge (U2) — delay and chorus for ethereal sound
- Andy Summers (The Police) — chorus and flanger for layered guitar parts
- Jimi Hendrix — uni-vibe pedal for phaser-like effect on 'Machine Gun', 'Star Spangled Banner'
