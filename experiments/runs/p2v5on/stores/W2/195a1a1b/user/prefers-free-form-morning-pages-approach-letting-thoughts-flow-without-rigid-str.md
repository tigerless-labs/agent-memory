Morning pages approach:
- Lets thoughts flow freely onto the page, usually without predetermined prompts
- Sometimes has specific topics to write about, but mostly follows the stream of consciousness
- Avoids strict structure or rigid prompts

This approach works well for clearing mind, processing emotions and experiences, and accessing creative ideas through the subconscious.
