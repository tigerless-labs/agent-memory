## Influencer Success Factors
- **Authenticity**: Sharing real struggles and strengths, not just polished content
- **Relatability**: Mirroring audience experiences and values to build trust
- **Social influence**: Power to shift opinions and purchasing behavior
- **User-generated content**: Building community through followers' participation
- **Consistency**: Regular posting and engagement creates stability/reliability

## Negative Impacts of Influencer Power
- Spread of misinformation and confusion
- Promotion of unrealistic body types and disordered eating patterns
- Materialism and consumption-focused messaging
- Burnout and mental health impacts on both influencers and followers
- Digital addiction, especially among young people

## Accountability Mechanisms
- **FTC guidelines** exist for sponsored content transparency, but enforcement is weak
- Individual actions: self-education, speaking up, purchasing power, supporting responsible creators, joining advocacy groups
- Public pressure and grassroots movements can force platform changes (e.g., Facebook's fake news removal efforts after scrutiny)
- Regulatory approaches through lawmakers
