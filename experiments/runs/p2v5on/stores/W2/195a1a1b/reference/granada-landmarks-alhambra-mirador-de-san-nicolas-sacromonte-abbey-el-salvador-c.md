Key Granada landmarks mentioned: Alhambra (Nasrid dynasty fortress/palace, 13th-14th centuries, UNESCO World Heritage Site), Mirador de San Nicolas (panoramic viewpoint), Sacromonte Abbey (hilltop monastery), El Salvador Church (bell tower views), Albaicín Quarter (old town vantage points). Also documented: Alhambra legends including Fountain of Lions, Tower of the Seven Floors, Hall of Abencerrajes massacre, and Devil's Megaphone acoustical phenomenon.
