American photographer (1890-1976) who pioneered modernist and straight photography. Born in New York City, largely self-taught but influenced by Alfred Stieglitz. Joined Stieglitz's Photo-Secession group in 1915. In 1920s shifted to documentary photography, capturing everyday life and social issues. Famous for his Mexico photograph series beginning in 1932, considered important examples of documentary photography. Believed photography could be a tool for social change.
