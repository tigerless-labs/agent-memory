Genius Voucher is a platform designed to manage voucher-based humanitarian projects, particularly well-suited for NGOs in Africa.

**Platform Overview:**
- Manages and controls distribution activities in real-time with periodic accountability reports
- Eliminates manual data entry for item/cash issuance
- All-in-one solution combining hardware and software

**Hardware Components:**
- POS machines issued to authorized vendors
- Smart cards (NFC) issued to beneficiaries for redemption

**Software Components:**
- Portal for project teams (NGOs) to create and monitor distributions in real-time
- Real-time balance monitoring for efficient card top-up planning
- Offline data syncing capability

**Key Advantages for Africa:**
- Works offline in areas with limited/no internet connectivity (critical for remote regions)
- Devices sync locally during distribution, then sync to database after completion
- Promotes accountability and transparency, reducing corruption and mismanagement
- User-friendly redemption: beneficiaries tap smart card on POS terminal, choose items, enter PIN to complete transaction
- Provides transaction receipts confirming received allocations

**Impact:**
- Prevents corruption and resource mismanagement
- Enables seamless beneficiary experience compared to manual processes
- Facilitates real-time monitoring and reporting
