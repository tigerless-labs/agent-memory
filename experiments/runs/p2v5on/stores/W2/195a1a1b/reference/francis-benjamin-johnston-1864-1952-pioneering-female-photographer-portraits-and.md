American photographer (1864-1952), one of the first women to establish professional photography career in the US. Born into prominent Virginia family. Opened studio in Washington D.C. in 1894. Pioneer in architectural photography, documenting America's historic buildings to raise awareness of preservation. Famous for elegant, simple portraits of prominent subjects including Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, and Alice Roosevelt Longworth. Used photography as tool for social change.
