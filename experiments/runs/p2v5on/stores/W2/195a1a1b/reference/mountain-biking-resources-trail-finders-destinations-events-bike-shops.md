## Trail Finding Resources
- **Trailforks** — search trails by location, difficulty, distance
- **MTB Project** — trail database and reviews
- **TrailLink** — multi-use trail finder
- **MapMyRide** — track routes and find trails

## Popular Mountain Biking Destinations (US)
- Park City, Utah
- Moab, Utah
- Bend, Oregon
- Asheville, North Carolina
- Lake Tahoe, California/Nevada

## Mountain Bike Events & Festivals
- **Sea Otter Classic** — California, four-day festival with racing and demos
- **Crankworx** — Whistler BC and other locations, racing and slopestyle events
- **USA Cycling National Championships** — various locations

## Mountain Bike Components: Dropper Post
A dropper post is a seatpost that quickly lowers/raises saddle height (typically 100-150mm) via hydraulic or cable system. Benefits: increased control on technical descents, improved clearance for obstacles, enhanced descending agility, efficient climbing, convenient on-the-fly adjustment.
