American photographer (1865-1946), pioneer of aerial photography. Studied at Art Students League of New York. In 1890s began experimenting with cameras mounted on balloons; in early 1900s used airplanes as photography platforms, worked with pioneering aviators including Orville Wright. Known for innovative aerial compositions capturing landscape patterns from above. Also pioneered innovative nighttime photography using artificial light sources (electric lights, gas lamps, torches, flash powder, magnesium wire). Nighttime work captured urban scenes with dramatic contrasts between light and dark.
