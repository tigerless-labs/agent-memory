**Self-Care & Boundaries**
- Prioritize physical and mental health (sleep, exercise, nutrition)
- Use relaxation techniques (meditation, yoga)  
- Learn to say no and recognize limits
- Seek support from coworkers/mentors
- Celebrate successes, big and small

**Time Management**
- Daily prioritized to-do lists
- SMART goals (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound)
- Calendar blocking for specific tasks
- Eliminate distractions (turn off notifications, limit email checking)
- Regular breaks to maintain focus
- Leverage technology for tracking, delegation, automation

**Key insight:** Being adaptable doesn't mean constant availability or excessive workload—it's about balancing change management with self-care.
