The GR-90 is a circular hiking trail in the Natural Park of Moncayo mountain, Aragón. **Distance & Duration:** Approximately 36 kilometers; requires 2-3 days to complete. **Highlights:** Summit of Moncayo (highest peak in the park), Fuenfría waterfall, beautiful meadows, forests, and rock formations. **Route:** Passes through small towns and villages offering cultural and historical learning opportunities. Recommended for breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding mountainside.
