# Bot Trading 10-Week Course Outline

## Week 1-5: Fundamentals & Live Trading
- Week 1: Introduction to Bot Trading (basics, benefits, risks, types of bots)
- Week 2: Setting up Bot Trading System (account setup, platform selection, parameter configuration)
- Week 3: Backtesting and Optimization (testing performance, optimizing settings)
- Week 4: Live Trading with Bot (backtesting vs live, monitoring, management)
- Week 5: Advanced Strategies (scalping, arbitrage, market making)

## Week 6-10: Risk Management & Advanced Topics
- Week 6: Risk Management (risk types, stop-loss, position sizing, diversification)
- Week 7: Advanced Technical Analysis (indicators, chart patterns, performance improvement)
- Week 8: Trading Psychology (emotional aspects, overcome pitfalls, discipline)
- Week 9: Advanced Strategies (hedging, mean reversion, combining strategies)
- Week 10: Advanced Platforms & Tools (API integrations, latest developments)
