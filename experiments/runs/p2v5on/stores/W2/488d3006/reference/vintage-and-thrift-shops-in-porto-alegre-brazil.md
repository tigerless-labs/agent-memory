Brechó Municipal: Municipal marketplace for used clothes, shoes, accessories. Affordable prices. | Armazém do Passado: Vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor, and vinyl records (Brazilian, rock, pop). Curated/trendy styles. | Bazar Vintage: Stylish fashion-forward vintage and retro clothing/accessories. | Casa da Sogra: Retro/vintage home décor, furniture, accessories, and rare vinyl records. | Pinga do Brechó: Clothing, accessories, kitchenware. Higher prices but reasonable. | Note: Check websites/social media before visiting to confirm current inventory.
