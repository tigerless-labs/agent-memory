The Pollock-Krasner Foundation was established in 1985 by Lee Krasner (widow of Jackson Pollock) to support individual artists of all nationalities.

**Eligibility:**
- Mid-career and established artists
- Must have been actively working for at least 5 years
- Must demonstrate commitment through work and exhibition history
- Must demonstrate financial need (proof of income, expenses, debts)

**What it supports:**
- Covers art supplies, studio rent, and other expenses
- Award amount determined by individual needs and artistic merit evaluation
- Open to all disciplines: painting, sculpture, printmaking, photography, multimedia

**Notable recipients mentioned:** Mark Bradford, Kara Walker, Robert Gober, David Salle, Elizabeth Murray, Frank Stella, Chuck Close, Julie Mehretu, Kiki Smith, Jasper Johns, Joan Mitchell

**Additional resources:**
The foundation maintains an archive of information about Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner, and supports research and scholarly activities related to their work.
