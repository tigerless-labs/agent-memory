Prefers reading that focuses on plot and character development over extensive environmental/landscape descriptions. Finds detailed poetic setting descriptions distractingly verbose. Values organic integration of setting within the narrative rather than standalone descriptive passages. Enjoys historical fiction, mythology retellings, and character-driven stories, but wants setting to serve the story rather than dominate it.
