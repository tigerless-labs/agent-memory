Album: Give Up (2003)

Artists: Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) + Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

Standout tracks:
- 'Such Great Heights' — synth-driven, dreamy, hopeful
- 'The District Sleeps Alone Tonight' — melancholy, introspective
- 'Clark Gable' — upbeat, quirky
- 'We Will Become Silhouettes' — somber, reflective
- 'Recycled Air' — prominent drums & acoustic guitar
- 'Sleeping In' — vocal layering, lush harmonies

Distinctive features:
- Blend of indie rock + electronic
- Acoustic instruments mixed with synthesizers
- Rich vocal layering and harmonies
- Storytelling lyrics with vivid characters
- Postal/mailing imagery throughout

Contemporaries: Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada
