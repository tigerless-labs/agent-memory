Summer cocktails: Pimm's Cup, Watermelon Margaritas, Summer Sangria, Cranberry Lime Sparkler, Gin-Gin Mule, Spicy Pineapple Collins, Rosé Spritz, Elderflower Collins. Fruit garnishes: lime wedges with mint, melon balls, strawberry, kiwi, grapefruit, orange, peach, mango, grilled pineapple. Korean BBQ sauces: Gochujang BBQ Sauce, Soy-Ginger BBQ Sauce. Marinades: Sesame-Ginger, Cilantro-Lime. Sides: Kimchi Coleslaw, Gochujang Roasted Vegetables, Cucumber Kimchi Salad, Pickled Ginger, Cilantro Lime Rice, Spicy Edamame. DIY Bar mixers: ginger beer, lime juice, grapefruit juice, soy sauce-infused simple syrup. Garnishes: cucumber, lime, mint, kimchi, pickled ginger, sesame seeds.
