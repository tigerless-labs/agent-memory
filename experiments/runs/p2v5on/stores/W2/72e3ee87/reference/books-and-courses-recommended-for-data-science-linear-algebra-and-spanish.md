Data Science: 'Data Science for Dummies' by Lillian Pierson - beginner-friendly introduction covering data preparation, analysis, machine learning, big data. Linear Algebra: Khan Academy free course, Coursera specialization by University of Pennsylvania. Spanish: SpanishDict platform, Coursera's Spanish for Beginners (Universidad Carlos III de Madrid), Coffee Break Spanish podcast, Tandem app for language exchange. Science: Crash Course Science series on YouTube.
