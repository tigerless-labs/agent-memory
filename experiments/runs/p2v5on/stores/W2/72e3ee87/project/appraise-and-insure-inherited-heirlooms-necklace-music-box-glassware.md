## Plan
1. Start with grandmother's diamond necklace appraisal (priority item)
2. Then appraise antique music box from great-aunt
3. Then appraise depression-era glassware from mom

## Appraisal Process
- Search for certified appraisers (NAJA, ASA, or AAA certified)
- Ask friends/family for referrals
- Provide proof of ownership, provenance, repair records, photos/videos
- Get one appraisal per item initially; get multiple appraisals later if needed

## Insurance Companies to Consider
**Jewelry specialists:**
- Jewelers Mutual (for jewelry/heirlooms)
- Chubb (high-value items)
- AXA ART (art, antiques, collectibles)
- Hagerty (collectibles including jewelry)

**Standard insurers with specialty departments:**
- State Farm (high-value items department)
- Allstate (Valuable Items policy)
- Liberty Mutual (Valuable Possessions policy)

## Key Appraisal Organizations
- NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers) - jewelry
- AGTA (American Gem Trade Association) - gemstones/jewelry
- ASA (American Society of Appraisers) - multidisciplinary
- AAA (Appraisers Association of America) - antiques/art/collectibles
- ISA (International Society of Appraisers) - antiques/art/collectibles
- NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers) - collectibles
