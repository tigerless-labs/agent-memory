For someone just starting printmaking:

1. **Start with basic techniques** — begin with relief printing (easier to learn, minimal equipment), advance to more complex techniques once basics are solid

2. **Experiment with materials** — try different paper types and inks to understand how they affect the final product

3. **Don't fear mistakes** — mistakes are part of learning; adjust and try again

4. **Take a class or workshop** — learn techniques, get feedback, connect with other artists

5. **Practice regularly** — skill improves with repetition

Key principles: Printmaking is about experimentation and creativity. The right brayer depends on ink type, printing surface texture, size of surface, and design detail level.
