## Nutrients & Foods
- **Omega-3 fatty acids**: salmon, tuna, mackerel — improves memory and cognitive function
- **Antioxidants**: berries, spinach, kale — reduce oxidative brain damage
- **B vitamins** (B6, B9/folate, B12): leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals
- **Caffeine**: moderate consumption via coffee or tea
- **Curcumin**: turmeric compound with anti-inflammatory properties

## Tips for elderly relatives
- Incorporate healthy foods into dishes they already enjoy
- Use attractive presentation with colorful plates
- Consider texture/chewing difficulty: puree, steam, or roast soft
- Involve them in meal planning and preparation
- Start with small amounts of new foods; be patient

## Easy healthy snacks
1. Apple/celery with almond or peanut butter
2. Roasted chickpeas with paprika, garlic, salt
3. Greek yogurt with berries and honey
4. Hummus with carrots, celery, pita chips
5. Trail mix (nuts, dried fruit, dark chocolate)
6. Whole-grain crackers with avocado/guacamole
7. Smoothies (frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, spinach/kale)
8. Energy balls (dates, nuts, coconut)
