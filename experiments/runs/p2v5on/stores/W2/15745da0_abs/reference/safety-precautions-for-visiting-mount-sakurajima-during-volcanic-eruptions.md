Essential precautions: Stay alert to local warnings; wear dust mask, goggles, long-sleeved clothing; avoid volcanic ash and gases; stay away from lava flows; avoid touching ash; drive carefully (tsunami risk); prepare for power outages; respect regulations. For photography: consult professional guides and use safe distances.
