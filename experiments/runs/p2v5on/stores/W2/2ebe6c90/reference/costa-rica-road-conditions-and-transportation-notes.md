Costa Rica road conditions: About 30 percent paved roads in good condition; 70 percent unpaved, rough, dusty roads in rural areas. Mountain roads can be steep and winding. During rainy season (May-November) roads become muddy and prone to flooding or washout. Transportation options: 4x4 rental vehicle recommended for rural areas; shuttle services from hotels; extensive public bus network; private transfers; domestic flights for remote areas. Avoid driving at night on rural roads.
