Approaches to address healthcare inequalities:

**Policy/System Level:**
- Universal healthcare systems (examples: Canada, UK, Spain)
- Subsidizing healthcare services for vulnerable communities
- Healthcare funding allocated based on need
- Prioritizing vulnerable communities for healthcare service allocation
- Investment in healthcare infrastructure in marginalized areas

**Addressing Root Causes:**
- Social determinants of health policies (poverty, racism, discrimination, intergenerational poverty)
- Education programs on disease prevention/management
- Food security/nutrition assistance programs
- Housing improvements

**Innovation:**
- Telemedicine and virtual consultations
- Mobile health clinics in remote areas
- Technology-enabled healthcare delivery

**Implementation:**
Requires sustained, collaborative effort from governments, healthcare institutions, community organizations, advocacy groups, and individuals.
