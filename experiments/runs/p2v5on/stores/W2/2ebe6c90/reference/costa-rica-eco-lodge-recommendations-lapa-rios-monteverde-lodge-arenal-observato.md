Recommended eco-lodges in Costa Rica: (1) Lapa Rios Ecolodge, Osa Peninsula - pioneer in sustainable tourism with luxurious accommodations; (2) Monteverde Lodge, Monteverde - surrounded by cloud forest, strong sustainability focus; (3) Arenal Observatory Lodge near Arenal Volcano - stunning views, guided tours, conservation commitment; (4) El Remanso Lodge, Osa Peninsula - luxurious with reforestation program. All offer guided tours and community engagement opportunities.
