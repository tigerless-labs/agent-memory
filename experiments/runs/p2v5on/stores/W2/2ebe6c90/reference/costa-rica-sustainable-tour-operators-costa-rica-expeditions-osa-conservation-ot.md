Recommended sustainable tour operators in Costa Rica: (1) Costa Rica Expeditions - pioneer in eco-tourism with guided hiking, birdwatching, wildlife adventures; (2) Osa Conservation - tours and conservation programs in Osa Peninsula with community engagement; (3) Tortuguero Canals Tours - family-owned local operator; (4) Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve - birdwatching, hiking, wildlife while supporting local conservation; (5) Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours - guided tours supporting conservation. Look for CST (Certification for Sustainable Tourism) certification.
