Costa Rica outdoor activities discussed: Hiking in Corcovado National Park (primary rainforest, diverse wildlife), Arenal Volcano, Monteverde Cloud Forest. Wildlife watching in Tortuguero National Park (sea turtles, monkeys, sloths, 300+ bird species), Manuel Antonio National Park, Osa Peninsula. Other activities: surfing at Tamarindo and other beaches, whitewater rafting (Class II-IV rapids), zip-lining through canopy, natural hot springs, waterfalls. Birdwatching available with over 850 bird species in country. Wear insect repellent, sunscreen, sturdy hiking shoes, bring binoculars and camera.
