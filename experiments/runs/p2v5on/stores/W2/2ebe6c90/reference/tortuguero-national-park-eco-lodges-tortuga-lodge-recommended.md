Tortuga Lodge in Tortuguero National Park offers guided tours and wildlife focus. Accessible only by boat or plane from San Jose. Park home to howler monkeys, white-faced capuchins, spider monkeys, two-toed and three-toed sloths, toucans, quetzals, macaws, crocodiles, iguanas, jaguars, caimans, and over 1200 butterfly species.
