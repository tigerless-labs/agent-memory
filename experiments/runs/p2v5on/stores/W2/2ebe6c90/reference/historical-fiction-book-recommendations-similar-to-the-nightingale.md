Books recommended for emotional depth and character development:

**WWII Era:**
- All the Light We Cannot See by Anthony Doerr (Pulitzer Prize winner)
- The Alice Network by Kate Quinn (explores lives of two women: socialite and spy during WWI and aftermath)
- The Lost Girls of Paris by Pam Jenoff (WWII-era, follows widow discovering photographs of resistance network women)
- The Women in the Castle by Jessica Shattuck (three women's experiences during WWII)
- Those Who Save Us by Jenna Blum (mother-daughter story during WWII)
- The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society by Mary Ann Shaffer (epistolary, post-WWII)
- The Postmistress by Sarah Blake (1940, interweaves stories of three women)
- The Invisible Bridge by Julie Orringer (Hungarian Jewish family during WWII)

**Other Periods:**
- The Red Tent by Anita Diamant (biblical, reimagines Dinah's story in ancient Mesopotamia)
- The Song of Achilles by Madeline Miller (retells Trojan War from Patroclus' perspective - CURRENTLY READING)
- The Kitchen House by Kathleen Grissom (1800s American South, complex relationships on plantation)
- The Girl You Left Behind by Jojo Moyes (France during WWI)

**Other:**
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (fantasy inspired by Chinese history)
- The Gilded Wolves by Roshani Chokshi (late 1800s Paris, thieves/scholars)
- The Ten Thousand Doors of January by Alix E. Harrow (magical book allowing travel between worlds)
