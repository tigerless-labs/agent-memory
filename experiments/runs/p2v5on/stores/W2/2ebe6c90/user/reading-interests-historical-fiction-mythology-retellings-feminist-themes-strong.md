## Reading Preferences

### Genres and Themes
- **Historical fiction** with strong female protagonists (especially WWII-era settings)
- **Mythology and folklore retellings** from diverse cultures — particularly fascinated by **non-Western cultures** (Greek, Chinese, Indian, Vietnamese, Latin American, African, Middle Eastern)
- Character-driven narratives with emotional depth and complexity
- Women's perspectives, agency, and resilience during conflict and hardship
- Intimate character relationships and inner lives
- Rich, lyrical prose with historical authenticity and careful research
- **Feminist themes** and explorations of power dynamics
- Stories that **reimagine classic tales from different perspectives** (especially from marginalized or female viewpoints)
- Interest in **dystopian settings** with social commentary

### Preferred Authors & Books Enjoyed
- **Kristin Hannah** — *The Nightingale* (WWII, intimate relationships)
- **Madeline Miller** — *The Song of Achilles* (classical retelling, LGBTQ+ perspectives)
- **Kate Quinn** — *The Alice Network* (WWI/WWII female spies)
- **R.F. Kuang** — *The Poppy War* (Asian-inspired fantasy, cultural depth)
- **Roshani Chokshi** — *The Star-Touched Queen* (Indian mythology retelling)
- **Naomi Alderman** — *The Power* (feminist dystopian)
- **Pat Barker** — *The Silence of the Girls* (Iliad retelling from female perspective)
- **Margaret Atwood** — *The Handmaid's Tale* (dystopian feminist themes)

### Format Preferences
- Enjoys both audiobooks and print books
- Values narration quality in audiobooks

### Current Research Interests
- Actively seeking mythology and folklore from African, Asian, and Latin American cultures
- Drawn to non-Western perspectives and mythologies
- Looking for works balancing historical authenticity with imaginative retellings
