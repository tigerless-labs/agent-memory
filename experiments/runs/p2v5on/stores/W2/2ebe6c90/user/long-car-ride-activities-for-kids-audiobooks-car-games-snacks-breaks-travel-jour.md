Prefers strategies for keeping kids occupied on long drives: audiobooks/podcasts, road trip bingo, car games (I Spy, 20 Questions, License Plate Tag), tablets/handheld games, snack stations, regular pit stops, scavenger hunts, car karaoke, travel journals, taking turns with choices.
