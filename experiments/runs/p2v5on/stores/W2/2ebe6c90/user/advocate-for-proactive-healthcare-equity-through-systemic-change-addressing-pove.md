Believes healthcare inequalities stem from deeper social/economic issues (poverty, discrimination, lack of education). Advocates for:

- Prioritizing healthcare funding for marginalized communities
- Systemic/proactive government action rather than waiting for crises
- Addressing root causes alongside healthcare infrastructure investment
- Collaborative effort from government, institutions, and communities

Frustrated that governments treat healthcare equity as priority only during crises (like COVID-19 pandemic), not proactively. Sees this as both moral imperative and economic necessity.
