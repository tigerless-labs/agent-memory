After comparing Sony and Fujifilm options, decided to pursue Fujifilm X-T30 II for landscape and nature photography.

Key features that influenced the decision:
- Compact size with vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- Advanced Intelligent Hybrid AF system (425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time)
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)
- Battery life: 380-440 frames per charge
- Fits within 1500 USD budget with kit lens

Was considering:
- Fujifilm X-T3 (more advanced, weather-sealed, but higher cost)
- Sony a6400 (compact, excellent AF, good for video)
- Sony a7 II (full-frame, weather-sealed)

Kit lens under consideration: Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ (compact option) or XF 18-55mm f/2.8-4 R LM OIS (higher quality option)
