Story premise: MK is held captive by Wukong (Monkey King from Journey to the West legend) in a magic barrier. Wukong seeks an artifact called the 'diamond snare' and initially thought MK knew its location.

**Plot established:**
- MK convinces Wukong not to kill him by offering to serve as 'insurance' and promising silence
- MK remains imprisoned despite not knowing the snare's location
- Wukong provides minimal food/water (fruit, stale bread, water) only when MK begs, showing annoyance and impatience
- MK struggles with the contrast between childhood stories of heroic Wukong and the cruel captor he faces
- MK hears monkeys feasting and attempts desperate escape; fails against the magic barrier
- MK's hunger and desperation intensify as he realizes Wukong is tired of his pleas

**Character dynamics:**
- Wukong: cruel, dismissive, strong, easily annoyed; uses minimal sustenance as control
- MK: desperate, scared, hungry, weak; tries logic and appeals to Wukong's legend; shows some sympathy for Wukong despite his cruelty
- Conflict: MK's idealized childhood version of Wukong vs. the brutal reality of his captor

Tone: Dark, exploring captivity, desperation, and disillusionment with legends.
