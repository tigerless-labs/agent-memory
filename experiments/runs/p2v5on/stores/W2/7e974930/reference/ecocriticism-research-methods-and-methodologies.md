Common research methods in ecocritical scholarship:

1. Close reading - Detailed analysis of language, imagery, literary techniques for nature representation and human-environment relationship.
2. Historical and cultural context analysis - Placing texts within context to understand societal environmental attitudes.
3. Environmental science and ecology analysis - Drawing on environmental science knowledge for understanding nature representation.
4. Comparative analysis - Comparing texts across periods/cultures to explore changes in nature representation and human-environment relationship.
5. Ethnographic and fieldwork - Observing people in field and conducting interviews about people-environment relationship.
6. Discourse analysis - Analyzing language in literary/cultural texts to understand how they construct and reinforce environmental views.

All methods aim to understand nature representation in literature and how representations shape/reflect societal environmental attitudes.
