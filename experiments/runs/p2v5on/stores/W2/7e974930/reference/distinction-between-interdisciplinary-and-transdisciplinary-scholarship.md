Interdisciplinary Scholarship: Combines different disciplines to study a topic/problem. Synthesizes insights and methods from multiple fields for comprehensive understanding. Example: Climate change study drawing on atmospheric science, economics, sociology, political science.

Transdisciplinary Scholarship: Goes beyond interdisciplinarity by actively involving stakeholders and practitioners from various fields in research. Collaborative knowledge co-creation aimed at solving real-world problems. Bridges gap between academic research and practical application.

Simple distinction: Interdisciplinary = combining disciplines to study a problem. Transdisciplinary = combining disciplines to solve a problem.

Ecocriticism can function as both: as interdisciplinary field combining literary studies, environmental studies, cultural studies; or as transdisciplinary approach when aiming to generate solutions and engage stakeholders to address environmental issues.
