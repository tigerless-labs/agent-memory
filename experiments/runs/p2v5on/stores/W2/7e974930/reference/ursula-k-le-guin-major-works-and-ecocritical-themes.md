Major Works:

1. The Dispossessed (1974) - Anarchists on distant planet exploring anarchism, human-environment relationship, capitalist critique.
2. The Left Hand of Darkness (1969) - Genderless planet exploring gender, sexuality, power, environment.
3. The Word for World is Forest (1976) - Colonizers vs natives exploring imperialism, colonialism, environment.
4. The Lathe of Heaven (1971) - Dreams change reality; critiques environmental destruction and science misuse.
5. Always Coming Home (1985) - Post-apocalyptic California with stories/songs exploring environmentalism and ecology.

Known for complex social/political exploration, capitalist critique, and human environmental impact.
