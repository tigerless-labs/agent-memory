Key figures and quotes in ecocriticism:

Lawrence Buell: 'The study of literature and environment can be seen as an act of cultural restoration, a way of returning to the world the richness of its own diversity, the voices of its own inhabitants, the agency of its own inhabitants.' Also: 'The environmental imagination is the capacity of literature to shape the way we think about the environment and the ways we relate to it.'

Cheryll Glotfelty: 'The ecocritic is concerned with the ways in which literature represents the relationship between humans and the environment, and the ways in which this representation reflects and shapes societal attitudes towards the environment.' Also: 'Ecocriticism is an interdisciplinary field of study that examines the relationship between literature and the environment, and the ways in which this relationship is represented in literature.'

Jonathan Bate: 'Ecocriticism is not just a matter of reading nature into literature, but of reading literature as an aspect of the relations between culture and nature.'

Eric D. Wilson: 'The study of literature and the environment is the study of the relationship between human culture and the natural world.'
