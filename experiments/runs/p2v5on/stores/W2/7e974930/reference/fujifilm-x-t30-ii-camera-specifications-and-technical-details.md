**Fujifilm X-T30 II Specifications:**

Sensor & Image:
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- Excellent dynamic range and film-like colors (Fujifilm's strength)
- Compact and lightweight body
- Vari-angle touchscreen LCD

Autofocus System (Intelligent Hybrid AF):
- 425 phase-detection points covering ~100% of frame
- 0.02-second response time
- ~99% accuracy rate
- Advanced AI-powered subject tracking
- Face and eye detection capabilities
- Excellent low-light performance (minimum -3EV working range)
- Customizable AF modes (zone focusing, wide/tracking, single-point)

Battery Life:
- Still images (EVF): up to 380 frames per charge
- Still images (LCD): up to 440 frames per charge  
- Movie (EVF): up to 45 minutes continuous
- Movie (LCD): up to 60 minutes continuous
- Real-world: 300-400 frames per charge typical
- Features: Power Save mode, Economy mode, Auto Power Off

Weather & Build:
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed like X-T3)
- Provides protection against light rain/mist
- Benefits from regular cleaning
- Less robust than higher-end models (X-T3, X-H1)

Video:
- Excellent video capabilities for a camera in this class
- Well-suited for landscape and nature content

Pricing:
- Body only: ~899 USD
- With kit lens options: fits within 1500 USD budget
