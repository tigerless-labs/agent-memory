Common methodologies in landscape architecture:

1. Site Analysis - Evaluating physical, ecological, cultural characteristics to understand project opportunities and constraints. Provides foundation for responsive design.

2. Conceptual Design - Creating initial design ideas responding to site context. Iterative exploration of options before final design.

3. Master Planning - Comprehensive plan for site development, providing framework for project implementation over time.

4. Detailed Design - Creating detailed drawings and specifications for construction. Provides information needed for project construction.

5. Construction Management - Overseeing construction to ensure design is properly implemented.

Landscape architecture is interdisciplinary, combining design, planning, and environmental science. Can influence ecocriticism by shaping nature representation in spaces, promoting environmental justice, advancing sustainability, and exploring aesthetic dimensions of human-environment relationships.
