- Laptop: Dell Inspiron 15 5000 series from 2018
- Primary work laptop
- RAM upgraded to 16GB (was working, caused performance boost)
- **Battery issue:** Before upgrade, 6-7 hours; after upgrade, now 4-5 hours
- Troubleshooting steps recommended: calibrate battery (drain to 0%, charge to 100%, repeat 2-3x)
