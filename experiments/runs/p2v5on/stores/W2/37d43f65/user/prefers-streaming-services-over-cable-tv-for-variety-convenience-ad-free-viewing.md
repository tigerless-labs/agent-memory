Hasn't watched cable TV in months. Values:
- Variety and convenience compared to traditional TV
- Ad-free, uninterrupted viewing experience
- Ability to resume shows where left off
- Access to wider content library (including international shows, classics)
- Mobile access (phone/tablet) for watching while on the go
- Entire seasons released at once for binge-watching, rather than weekly episode releases
