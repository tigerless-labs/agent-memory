### Workspace/Reading Nook Plan

Adding a workstation or reading nook near the big living room window.

**Furniture:**
- Small desk or console table (wall-mounted, narrow console, drop-leaf, or foldable options preferred)
- Compact, stylish, won't add clutter
- Height-comfortable for sitting or standing work

**Layout & Decor:**
- Small circular rug (3-4 feet diameter) underneath to define the space and add warmth
- Small circular mirror (12-18 inches diameter) above the desk to add visual interest and make space feel larger
- Artwork or prints above the desk for personality (botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, or pop art)

**Retailers mentioned:** IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, Amazon, Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble

**Key consideration:** Maintain clutter-free aesthetic while adding functional and visual elements.
