Examples discussed regarding non-UK citizens appointed as life peers:

- Baroness Boothroyd (born Yorkshire, father Belgian soldier, mother Polish-Jewish refugee; Speaker of House of Commons 1992-2000; appointed to House of Lords 2000)
- Baroness Hayman (born in Canada, first elected Speaker of House of Commons; appointed to House of Lords after 2000)
- Lord Patten of Barnes (born in Sudan; last British Governor of Hong Kong; appointed life peer 2005)
- Baroness Manningham-Buller (born Northamptonshire; Director General of MI5 2002-2007; appointed to House of Lords 2008)

Note: Requirements for non-UK citizens to become life peer are British citizenship or Commonwealth/Ireland citizenship with permanent UK residence.
