### Low-Maintenance Plants for Bright Windows

Plants recommended for a living room with big windows (bright indirect light):

1. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)** - Tolerates low light but thrives in bright indirect light; can go weeks without watering; air purifier
2. **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)** - Tolerates range of lighting; infrequent watering; air purifier; can survive with neglect
3. **Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)** - Easy care; white flowers; prefers bright indirect light; propagates easily
4. **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)** - Versatile; tolerates various lighting; air purifier; can climb walls or trellises
5. **Succulents** (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) - Extremely low-maintenance; bright indirect light; water sparingly to avoid overwatering
6. **Dracaena** - Tolerates range of lighting; air purifier; can grow tall
7. **Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii)** - Elegant white blooms; air purifier; prefers well-draining soil and moderate watering
8. **Bromeliads** - Low-maintenance; produce beautiful flowers; thrive in bright indirect light

**General care tips:**
- Water when soil feels dry to the touch (avoid overwatering)
- Fertilize sparingly
- Prune regularly to maintain shape
- Monitor temperature and humidity
