Key Big Five Personality Test research studies cited:

1. **Goldberg (1993)** — Structure of phenotypic personality traits
   - https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.48.1.26

2. **McCrae & Costa (1987)** — Validation of the five-factor model across instruments and observers
   - https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.52.1.81

3. **Roberts, Kuncel, Shiner, Caspi, & Goldberg (2007)** — Comparative validity of personality traits vs socioeconomic status vs cognitive ability for predicting life outcomes
   - https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2007.00047.x

4. **Soto & John (2017)** — Big Five Inventory-2 (BFI-2) with hierarchical model and 15 facets
   - https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000096

5. **Tackett et al. (2017)** — Meta-analytic review of NEO Personality Inventory-3 psychometric properties
   - https://doi.org/10.1177/1073191115588767
