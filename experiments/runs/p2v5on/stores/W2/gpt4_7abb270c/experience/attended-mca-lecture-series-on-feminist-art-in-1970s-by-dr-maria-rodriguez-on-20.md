Attended a lecture series at the Museum of Contemporary Art featuring Dr. Maria Rodriguez speaking about the role of feminist art in the 1970s. Found it enlightening to learn about the historical context of feminist art and its continued relevance today.

Also attended the reception following the lecture, where made several valuable connections:
- Met artists working on projects related to feminist art and exchanged contact information for potential future collaboration
- Met a curator who expressed interest in learning more about own artistic practice and offered valuable advice on how to get work seen by a wider audience

The event was enriching and provided good opportunity to connect with like-minded individuals in the art world.
