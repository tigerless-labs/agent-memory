Particularly interested in learning about and exploring feminist art, especially the work and significance of Judy Chicago and the feminist art movement of the 1970s.

Strong interest in:
- The Dinner Party (Judy Chicago's monumental installation celebrating women's historical achievements)
- Feminist artists and their contributions to challenging patriarchal structures in the art world
- How feminist art addresses issues like female sexuality, reproductive rights, and women's contributions to history

Resources appreciated:
- Art blogs and websites for staying updated on trends and exhibitions (Artsy, Hyperallergic, Artnet, The Art Newspaper, Contemporary Art Daily, Art in America, Frieze, Apollo Magazine, Art21, Culture Trip)
- Books on feminist art (works by Norma Broude, Mary Garrard, Linda Nochlin, Andrea Liss)
- Documentaries on feminist artists (including "!Women Art Revolution", "The Heretics", documentaries on Judy Chicago)
