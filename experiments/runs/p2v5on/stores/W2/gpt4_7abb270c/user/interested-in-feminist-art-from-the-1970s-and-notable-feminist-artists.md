Interested in feminist art movements and artists from the 1970s. Notable artists of interest include Judy Chicago (particularly 'The Dinner Party'), Miriam Schapiro, Carolee Schneemann, the Guerrilla Girls collective, Barbara Kruger, Cindy Sherman, and Laurie Anderson. Also interested in the Feminist Art Program founded in 1970 at CalArts. Values educational lectures and networking opportunities with fellow art enthusiasts.
