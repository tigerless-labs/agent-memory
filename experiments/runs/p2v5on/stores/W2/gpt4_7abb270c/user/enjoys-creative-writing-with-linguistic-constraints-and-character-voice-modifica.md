Requests creative text where character speech is transformed with specific rules: letter substitutions (r→w, l→w, th→ff at word end, th→d at word start), added speech quirks (uwuu endings). Finds humor and appeal in constraints that create distinct character voices. Applies transformations overtly to achieve aesthetic/comedic effect.
