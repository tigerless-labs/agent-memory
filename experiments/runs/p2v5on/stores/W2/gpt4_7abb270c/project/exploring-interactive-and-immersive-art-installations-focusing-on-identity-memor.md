Interested in creating interactive and immersive installations that explore themes of identity, memory, and the human experience. Wants to create work that engages audiences and fosters empathy and connection.

## Specific Installation Ideas

- **Sensory environment recreation**: Installation incorporating sound, light, and texture to recreate the sensation of being in a specific environment or memory. Example: forest installation with bird sounds, rustling leaves, and scent of damp earth.
- **Collective memory exploration**: Installations incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods to explore shared human experience and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts.

## Interested Technologies
- Spatial/3D audio
- Projection mapping
- Scent diffusion
- Haptic feedback
- Data visualization
- Augmented reality
- Interactive storytelling
- Collaborative platforms
- Tools: Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino
