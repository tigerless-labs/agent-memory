Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) proposes standardized reporting format based on European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) using Inline XBRL (iXBRL). Format expected introduction: 2024. Reporting requirement starts: 2026 onwards. Applies to all large companies operating in the EU.
