Key organizations: American Institute for Conservation (AIC) and International Institute for Conservation (IIC). Online resources: Conservation Wiki, YouTube channels Art Conservation/Conservation Framing/Getty Conservation Institute. Recommended books: The Conservation of Art and Architecture (Watt), The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory (Keck), Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide (Stubbs). Common adhesives: acrylic polymers (PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV), animal glues, cellulose-based adhesives, epoxy resins, natural waxes. Common consolidants: acrylic polymers (Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98), parylene, silanes. Conservation papers must be acid-free and lignin-free; types include cotton, linen, rag, kozu (Japanese), and gampi (Japanese).
