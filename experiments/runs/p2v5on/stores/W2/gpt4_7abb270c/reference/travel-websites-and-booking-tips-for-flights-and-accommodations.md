## Flight Search Engines\n- **Skyscanner**: Compares prices across airlines and travel agencies\n- **Google Flights**: User-friendly with price trends and fare comparisons\n- **Kayak**: Searches hundreds of travel sites for flights, hotels, car rentals\n- **Momondo**: Specializes in error fares and sales\n- **Airfarewatchdog**: Fare sale alerts and exclusive deals\n\n## Accommodation Booking\n- **Booking.com**: Large selection of hotels, hostels, apartments\n- **Airbnb**: Unique locally-hosted accommodations\n- **HotelsCombined**: Meta-search engine comparing multiple booking sites\n- **Trivago**: Hotel search engine with price comparisons\n- **Hostelworld**: Specialized for hostels and budget accommodations\n\n## Package Deals\n- **Expedia**: Package deals on flights, hotels, car rentals\n- **Orbitz**: Package and individual booking options\n- **Priceline**: Package deals and "Name Your Own Price" feature\n\n## Travel Inspiration\n- **TripAdvisor**: Destination insights and reviews\n- **Lonely Planet**: Destination guides and travel tips\n- **Travel + Leisure**: Travel inspiration and guides\n\n## Flight Booking Timing Tips\n- **54 days in advance**: On average, cheapest booking window (per Skyscanner)\n- **21-121 days in advance**: Prime booking window with prices within 5% of lowest (per CheapAir.com)\n- **Avoid peak booking days**: Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Saturdays are busiest; try Mondays, Thursdays, Fridays\n- **Off-peak seasons**: Mid-January to mid-March, April-May, September-November have lower prices\n- **Set price alerts** to track price changes\n- **Be flexible with dates** to find cheaper days\n- **Clear cookies** to avoid price increases\n- **Look for error fares** for significant savings\n- **Consider budget airlines** for cheaper regional flights
