Recommended artists working with interactive and immersive installations:

- **Carsten Höller**: German artist known for interactive installations featuring slides, Ferris wheels, playful elements
- **Anicka Yi**: Korean-American artist creating immersive, scent-based installations exploring art, science, and technology
- **Rafael Lozano-Hemmer**: Mexican-Canadian artist creating interactive installations using biometrics, robotics, and technology to explore surveillance and control

Notable exhibitions and collectives:

- **Meow Wolf**: Santa Fe-based collective creating immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, and storytelling
- **The Color Factory**: San Francisco-based exhibition space featuring interactive installations exploring emotional and psychological effects of color
- **TeamLab**: Japanese collective creating immersive exhibitions using technology and light for interactive environments

Online resources for discovering interactive art:
- Artspace
- The Creators Project
- Hyperallergic (often features interactive/immersive art reviews)
