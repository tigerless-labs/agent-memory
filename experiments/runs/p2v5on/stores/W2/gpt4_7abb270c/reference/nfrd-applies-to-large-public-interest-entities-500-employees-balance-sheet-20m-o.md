Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) applies to large public-interest entities meeting these thresholds:\n- More than 500 employees\n- Balance sheet total over €20 million, OR\n- Net turnover over €40 million\n\nPublic-interest entities include listed companies, credit institutions, and insurance undertakings. No mandatory reporting format required, but companies encouraged to use internationally recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) for consistency.
