## Voluntary Frameworks\n- **GRI Standards**: Global, comprehensive sustainability reporting (no mandatory format)\n- **SASB Standards**: Industry-specific sustainability disclosure (no mandatory format)\n- **TCFD Recommendations**: Climate-related financial disclosures, increasingly adopted (no mandatory format)\n- **IR Framework**: Integrated reporting (no mandatory format)\n\n## Mandatory EU Frameworks\n- **EU Taxonomy Regulation**: Mandatory for companies subject to NFRD and SFDR; requires alignment with EU Taxonomy classification system\n- **SFDR**: Mandatory for financial market participants and advisers; requires pre-contractual and periodic disclosure\n- **CSRD**: Mandatory for all large EU companies; requires ESEF/Inline XBRL format\n\nFrameworks can be used in combination for more comprehensive reporting. EU Taxonomy and CSRD complement the voluntary frameworks.
