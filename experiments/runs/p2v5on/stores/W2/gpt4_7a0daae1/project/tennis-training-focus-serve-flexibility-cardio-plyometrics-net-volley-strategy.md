Current tennis training focuses on multiple skill areas in preparation for Saturday lessons:

**Serve technique**: Continental grip, stance, toss (6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front), racket position, contact point, follow-through, body rotation, serve variations (flat, topspin, slice).

**Flexibility & injury prevention**: Post-lesson stretching routine including neck, shoulders, chest, arms, calves, hamstrings, quads, hip flexors, forearms, rotator cuff, glutes. Holding stretches 30 seconds, breathing deeply, no bouncing.

**Cardio for endurance**: Running and cycling to build cardiovascular endurance. Incorporating HIIT, steady-state aerobic work, agility drills 2-3x per week with proper rest days.

**Plyometric exercises**: Box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds, tuck jumps, medicine ball throws (3 sets of 10-15 reps). Starting with lower intensity, increasing gradually with proper technique focus. Combining with strength training (squats, lunges).

**Court strategy**: Coming to net more often, using volleys to take advantage of opponent's weaker returns. Focus on reading opponent patterns, positioning, footwork, racket face control, reaction time, and mixing up strategies.
