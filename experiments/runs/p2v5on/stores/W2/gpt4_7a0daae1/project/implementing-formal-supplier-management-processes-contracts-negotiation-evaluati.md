Committed to establishing formal supplier management processes across the full lifecycle:

- **Contracts**: Creating supplier contracts with clear terms on scope, pricing, delivery, quality, IP, liability, and dispute resolution
- **Price negotiation**: Using research, relationship-building, competition leverage, and alternative pricing models to negotiate best rates
- **Supplier evaluation**: Assessing quality, reliability, pricing, customer service, financial stability, compliance, and innovation; watching for red flags (poor communication, lack of transparency, late deliveries)
- **Onboarding**: Establishing supplier profiles, clear communication channels, designated points of contact, training, testing/QA, and payment terms
- **Quality control**: Defining quality standards, identifying critical control points, developing QC plans, implementing regular inspections, establishing corrective action processes, monitoring KPIs (defect rates, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction)

**Tools/Standards referenced:**
- Contract management: ContractWorks, Concord
- Supplier management systems: SAP Ariba, Coupa  
- Project/collaboration: Asana, Trello
- Quality management: Qualityze, ETQ
- Quality standards: ISO 9001:2015, AS9100, Six Sigma, Lean Manufacturing
