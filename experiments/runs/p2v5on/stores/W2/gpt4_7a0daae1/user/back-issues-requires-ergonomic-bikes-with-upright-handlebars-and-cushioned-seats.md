Has prior back issues and needs ergonomic accommodations when cycling or doing active travel. Requires bikes with:
- Upright handlebars to reduce strain on back and neck
- Comfortable, cushioned seats with gel inserts
- Optional: hybrid bikes or e-bikes with suspension

This is important when recommending bikes for tours or activities.
