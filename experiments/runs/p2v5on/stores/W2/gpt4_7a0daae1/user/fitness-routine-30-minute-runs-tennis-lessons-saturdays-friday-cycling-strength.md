Current fitness routine includes:
- Running: 30-minute runs (working on cardiovascular endurance)
- Tennis: Lessons on Saturdays
- Cycling: Solo Friday evenings, planning to join group rides
- Strength training: Core (planks, Russian twists, leg raises, bicycle crunches) and legs (squats, lunges, calf raises, deadlifts) 2-3x per week

Approach: Progressive overload with lighter weights, proper form focus, 48-72 hours recovery between strength sessions, balanced with flexibility/stretching and yoga.
