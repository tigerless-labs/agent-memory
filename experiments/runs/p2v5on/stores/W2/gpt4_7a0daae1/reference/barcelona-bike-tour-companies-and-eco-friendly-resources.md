Bike Tour & Rental Companies: Barcelona Bike Tours, Fat Tire Tours, Bicitours Barcelona, Donkey Republic — all offer bikes with baskets/racks and comfort features like upright handlebars and cushioned seats.

Barcelona Bike Infrastructure: BiciLlanes (200+ km dedicated lanes), Superblocks (traffic-restricted areas), Barcelona Bike Path (7 km seaside), Diagonal Bike Lane (11 km), Bicing (bike-sharing).

Bike-Friendly Stops: Can Culleretes (Gothic Quarter), El Xampanyet (tapas), La Pubilla (pastries), Bicioci (bike café with repair station), Can Solé (Catalan cuisine).

Eco-Friendly Accommodations: Hotel Omm, Hotel Casa Fuster, Yök Casa + Cultura, Hotel España Ramblas, Hostal Orleans.

Note: Information from Feb 2023; verify current availability before booking.
