## Madras Curry Sauce Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp curry leaves (optional)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional, for creaminess)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in large pan over medium heat; add onions until translucent (5 min)
2. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, curry leaves, turmeric, Madras curry powder, garam masala, salt, cayenne; cook 1-2 min until fragrant
3. Add diced tomatoes, vegetable broth, coconut milk; stir well
4. Simmer 10-15 minutes until sauce thickens
5. Add cooked tofu or protein and simmer 5-7 more minutes
6. Garnish with cilantro and serve over rice, naan, or roti

**Variations:** Add potatoes/carrots, substitute coconut milk with yogurt, add lemon juice or vinegar for brightness
