Recommended locations to explore Hokkaido's food culture:

**Markets:**
- **Nijo Fish Market** (Sapporo) — wide variety of fresh seafood including crab, sea urchin, squid

**Ramen:**
- **Sapporo Ramen Yokocho** — alley with multiple local ramen shops

**Shopping/Food:**
- **Tanukikoji Shopping Street** (Sapporo) — local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs

**Grilled lamb (Jingisukan/Genghis Khan):**
- **Daruma** (Sapporo)
- **Kitaichi Hall** (Asahikawa)

**Hot pot (Ishikari Nabe):**
- **Yurigakuro** (Sapporo)
- **Kakizaki Shokudo** (Otaru)
