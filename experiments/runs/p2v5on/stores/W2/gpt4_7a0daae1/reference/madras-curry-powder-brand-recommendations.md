Recommended brands for Madras curry powder blends:

1. **Penzeys Spices** — Madras Curry Powder blend of 12 spices (turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom)
2. **The Spice House** — 15-spice blend including fenugreek and fennel
3. **McCormick** — More affordable option, good balance of spices
