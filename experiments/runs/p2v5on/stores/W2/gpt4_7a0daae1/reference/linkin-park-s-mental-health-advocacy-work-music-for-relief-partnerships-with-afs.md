Linkin Park's music lyrics addressed depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation. Band created Music for Relief non-profit (2015) for disaster aid and mental health awareness. Partnerships included American Foundation for Suicide Prevention and Campaign to Change Direction. After Chester Bennington's death (2017), remaining members continued mental health advocacy. Received recognition for humanitarian efforts including work with mental health organizations.
