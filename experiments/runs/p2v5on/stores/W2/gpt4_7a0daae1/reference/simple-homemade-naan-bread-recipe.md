## Homemade Naan Bread Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup lukewarm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, for tenderness)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine flour, salt, sugar, yeast in mixing bowl
2. Gradually add lukewarm water and mix until shaggy dough forms
3. Add olive oil and mix until dough comes together
4. Knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth, let rise 1-2 hours in warm place until doubled
6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat
7. Divide dough into 6-8 portions, shape into balls
8. Roll each ball into thin circle (6-7 inches diameter)
9. Cook in skillet 1-2 min until puffs and edges curl; flip and cook another 1-2 min until golden brown
10. Brush with melted butter or ghee if desired; serve hot

**Tips:** Use stand mixer for dough, add garlic/cilantro/cumin seeds for flavor, use cast-iron skillet for crisper crust, experiment with whole wheat flour
