Popular skincare trends for content:
1. **Sustainable skincare** - eco-friendly products, zero-waste routines, recyclable packaging
2. **Customized skincare** - skin typing (normal, dry, oily, combination), personalized formulas
3. **Natural/organic ingredients** - CBD, green tea, turmeric; DIY exfoliants (sugar, salt, coffee)
4. **Skincare for specific concerns** - acne, anti-aging, hyperpigmentation
5. **K-Beauty & J-Beauty trends** - snail mucin, sheet masks, essences, sake, green tea
6. **Inclusive skincare** - diverse skin tones and types
7. **Skincare tools/devices** - microneedling, facial steamers, LED masks, facial rollers

**Content format strategies:**
- YouTube tutorials showing how to incorporate trends
- Product reviews with honest opinions
- Before-and-after transformations
- Collaborations with other influencers/brands
- Instagram Stories for quick tips
- Hashtag optimization for reach
- Swipe-up features to link to blog posts or product pages
- User-generated content campaigns
