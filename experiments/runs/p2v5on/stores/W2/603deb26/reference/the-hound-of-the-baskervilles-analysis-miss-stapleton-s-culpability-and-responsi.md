**Work:** 'The Hound of the Baskervilles' by Arthur Conan Doyle

**Character analysis:** Miss Stapleton is both perpetrator and victim in the plot:
- **Active role:** Accomplished in murder of Sir Charles and attempted murder of Sir Henry; lured him into danger; helped brother conceal identity; provided means
- **Mitigating circumstances:** Raised by cruel, abusive, controlling brother with few escape options; acted from desperation to gain independence, not malice

**Responsibility framework:**
- Should be held accountable for actions
- Punishment should be tempered by degree of coercion and circumstances
- Possible charges: conspiracy to attempted murder (depending on severity of role)
- Punishment should aim at reform/redemption rather than retribution alone

**Key insight:** Complex responsibility that considers both agency and constraint; accountability without dehumanization.
