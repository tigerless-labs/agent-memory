## Mobile Photo Editing Apps

Recommended for quick exposure and color adjustments before social media sharing:

**Free or freemium options:**
- **Lightroom Mobile** (iOS, Android) — Adobe's mobile version, non-destructive editing, presets, selective adjustments, syncs with desktop Lightroom
- **Snapseed** (iOS, Android) — Google's professional-grade app, selective adjustments, layers, masking, quick fixes
- **Adobe Photoshop Express** (iOS, Android) — Streamlined mobile version, quick tools for exposure/color/contrast
- **VSCO** (iOS, Android) — Film-inspired presets, advanced editing, free with in-app purchases
- **PicsArt** (iOS, Android) — Broad feature set, editing tools, filters, effects, text overlays
- **Polarr** (iOS, Android) — AI-powered, quick adjustments, filters, effects

**Paid options:**
- **TouchRetouch** (iOS $1.99, Android $2.99) — Object removal and touch-ups
- **Facetune** (iOS $3.99, Android $2.99) — Portrait retouching
- **Filterstorm** (iOS $2.99) — Advanced editing with curves, layers, masking
- **Afterlight** (iOS $2.99, Android $1.99) — Filters, textures, frames, basic editing

**Note on Lightroom Mobile vs Desktop:**
Mobile version has simplified interface but lacks advanced features like Tone Curve, Split Toning, Lens Corrections, and advanced local adjustments. Best used as complement to desktop version, not replacement.
