**Luxury brands** (high follower counts, lower engagement):
- Gucci (43.6M followers, 1.5% engagement)
- Chanel (42.4M followers, 1.3% engagement)
- Louis Vuitton (38.4M followers, 1.2% engagement)
- Prada (34.4M followers, 1.1% engagement)
- Dior (33.4M followers, 1.2% engagement)

**Affordable fashion**:
- Zara (34.6M, 2.5% engagement)
- H&M (32.4M, 2.1% engagement)
- Forever 21 (28.4M, 2.3% engagement)
- ASOS (24.4M, 2.5% engagement)
- Topshop (22.4M, 2.2% engagement)

**Streetwear/contemporary**:
- Supreme (14.4M, 3.5% engagement)
- Nike (13.4M, 2.5% engagement)
- Adidas (12.4M, 2.2% engagement)
- Converse (11.4M, 2.5% engagement)
- Vans (10.4M, 2.8% engagement)

**Collaboration approaches:**
1. Influencer partnerships via brand's marketing team/PR
2. Sponsored post opportunities
3. Affiliate marketing programs
4. Content collaborations (guest posts, video features, takeovers)
5. Product seeding (free products for reviews/tutorials)
6. Brand events, fashion shows, pop-ups
7. User-generated content (UGC) campaigns with branded hashtags
8. Brand ambassador programs
