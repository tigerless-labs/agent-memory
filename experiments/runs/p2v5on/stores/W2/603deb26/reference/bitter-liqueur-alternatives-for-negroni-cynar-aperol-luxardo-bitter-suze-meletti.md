Alternatives to Campari for Negroni: Cynar (Italian artichoke liqueur, vegetal and slightly sweet, use 0.5 oz Cynar + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica); Aperol (lighter, more orange-forward, use 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica); Luxardo Bitter (intense herbal, use 0.5 oz + 0.5 oz Carpano Antica); Suze (French gentian-based, crisp and earthy, use 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica); Meletti (Italian, citrus-forward and sweet, use 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica). Tip: Adjust ratio of bitter to sweet vermouth based on intensity; may need to experiment to find perfect balance.
