**Lavender Infusions:**\n\n*Lavender Gin:*\n- Add 1/4 cup dried lavender buds to 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-3 days in cool, dark place\n- Strain and filter before using\n\n*Lavender Vodka:*\n- Add 1/4 cup dried lavender buds to 1 liter vodka\n- Infuse 1-2 weeks in cool, dark place\n- Strain and filter before using\n\n**Grapefruit Infusions:**\n\n*Grapefruit-Infused Gin:*\n- Slice 1-2 grapefruits, add to 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-3 days in cool, dark place\n- Strain and filter before using\n\n*Grapefruit-Infused Vodka:*\n- Slice 1-2 grapefruits, add to 1 liter vodka\n- Infuse 1-2 weeks in cool, dark place\n- Strain and filter before using\n\n**Syrups:**\n- Lavender Syrup: steep 1/4 cup dried lavender in 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water for 10-15 min, strain\n- Basil Syrup: steep 1/4 cup fresh basil in 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water for 10-15 min, strain
