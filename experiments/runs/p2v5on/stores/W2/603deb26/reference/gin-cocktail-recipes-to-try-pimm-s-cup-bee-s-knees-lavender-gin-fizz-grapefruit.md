Recipes to experiment with:\n\n**Immediately interested:**\n- Pimm's Cup: Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit\n- Bee's Knees: gin, honey syrup, lemon juice\n- Lavender Gin Fizz: gin, fresh lime juice, lavender syrup, simple syrup, soda water\n- Grapefruit Basil Martini: gin, fresh grapefruit juice, lime juice, basil syrup\n\n**Other classics to explore:**\n- Gin Rickey: gin, lime juice, soda water\n- Gin-Gin Mule: gin, lime juice, ginger beer\n- Aviation: gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, lemon juice\n- Monkey Gland: gin, orange juice, grenadine, absinthe\n- Last Word: gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, lime juice
