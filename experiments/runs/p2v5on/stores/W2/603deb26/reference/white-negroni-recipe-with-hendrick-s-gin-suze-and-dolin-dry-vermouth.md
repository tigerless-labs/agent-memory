White Negroni Recipe: 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin, 0.5 oz Suze (or Luxardo Bitter), 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth. Fill mixing glass with ice, add ingredients, stir 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass. Garnish with orange or lemon twist. Tips: Start with dry bitter liqueur for crisp herbaceous flavor; choose dry vermouth to avoid sweetness; experiment with gin to bitter to vermouth ratios (try 2:1:1 or 3:1:1); stir just long enough to chill without over-diluting.
