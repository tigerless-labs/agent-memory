Heat 1 cup sugar and 1 cup water until sugar dissolves. Add 1/4 cup dried lavender buds, remove from heat, steep 10-15 minutes until cooled, strain through fine-mesh sieve, store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Use in cocktails like Lavender Gin Fizz, Lavender Dream, and Lavender Bee's Knees.
