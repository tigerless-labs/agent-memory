Citrus pairing recommendations for gin and lavender cocktails: Orange (especially blood oranges for deeper complex flavor, sweetness complements floral notes); Yuzu (Japanese citrus cross between grapefruit and orange, tart with subtle sweetness, adds exotic flavor); Kumquat (small sweet citrus, try kumquat syrup or muddle fresh); Satsuma (sweet mandarin with loose peel, adds burst of citrus); Bergamot (subtle citrus used in tea, adds complexity without overpowering). General tips: Use fresh citrus for brighter vibrant flavor; experiment with different varieties; start with small amounts to avoid overpowering other flavors; consider using citrus peels and twists for added aroma and depth.
