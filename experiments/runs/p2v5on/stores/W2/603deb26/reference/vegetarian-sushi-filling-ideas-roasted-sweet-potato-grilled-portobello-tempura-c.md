**Substantial vegetarian sushi filling options:**

1. **Roasted sweet potato** - Roast diced sweet potatoes with oil, salt, pepper until tender; cools before use
2. **Grilled portobello mushrooms** - Marinate in soy sauce, sake, sugar; grill until tender with slight char for meaty texture
3. **Carrot-ginger tempura bits** - Shred carrots mixed with ginger, light tempura batter, fried until crispy
4. **Spinach and tofu** - Sautéed spinach with crumbled tofu, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger and garlic
5. **Pickled shiitake mushrooms** - Slice thinly, pickle in vinegar, sugar, soy sauce for tangy umami flavor
6. **Crispy fried tofu** - Cube extra-firm tofu, light batter, fry until crispy, season with soy sauce and sesame oil

All provide variety of textures and flavors to complement cucumber/avocado rolls.
