Engages with classic literature through the lens of character accountability. Interested in analyzing how circumstances (coercion, control, desperation) mitigate or complicate moral responsibility for actions. Demonstrated with close analysis of 'The Hound of the Baskervilles' and Miss Stapleton's role: examining both her active participation in crimes and her circumstances as victim of her brother's control.

Analytical framework considers: degree of coercion, alternatives available to character, motivation (malice vs. desperation), and how punishment should balance accountability with rehabilitation potential.
