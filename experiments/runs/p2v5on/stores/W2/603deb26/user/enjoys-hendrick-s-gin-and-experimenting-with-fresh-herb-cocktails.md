Prefers Hendrick's gin for mixing cocktails, particularly with fresh herbs from garden. Has tried multiple summer cocktails including Cucumber-Basil Refresher, Garden Gimlet, and variations with lavender, lemon-thyme, and other fresh herbs. Also experimenting with Negroni variations using Hendrick's gin and Carpano Antica vermouth. Interested in trying different citrus fruits (yuzu, kumquat, satsuma, bergamot) and bitter liqueurs (Cynar, Aperol, Luxardo Bitter, Suze, Meletti) in cocktails.
