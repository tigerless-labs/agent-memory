Recipe for honey syrup:\n\n**Basic Recipe:**\n- Ratio: 1 part honey to 1 water (can adjust to taste)\n- Heat mixture over low heat, whisking until dissolved\n- Don't boil or burn\n- Cool before using\n- Stores in fridge for up to 2 weeks\n\n**Flavor Variations:**\n- Infuse with citrus peel, herbs, or spices during dissolving\n- Honey-ginger syrup: add sliced ginger while heating\n- Experiment with different honey types (clover, manuka, acacia)\n\n**Uses:**\n- Bee's Knees: gin, honey syrup, lemon juice\n- Lavender Dream: vodka, lavender syrup, lemon juice, honey syrup\n- Various other cocktails for sweetness and flavor
