Plan to create two separate gin infusions:\n\n**Cucumber-Infused Gin:**\n- Use English or hothouse cucumber (thin-skinned, sweet)\n- Slice thinly to increase surface area\n- Ratio: 1-2 cups cucumber to 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-5 days in cool, dark place\n- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter\n\n**Mint-Infused Gin:**\n- Use fresh peppermint or spearmint\n- Add 1/4 to 1 cup fresh leaves to 1 liter gin\n- Infuse 2-5 days in cool, dark place\n- Strain through cheesecloth or coffee filter\n\n**Combining:**\n- Start with 50/50 ratio of infusions\n- Allow combined blend to age a few days\n- Adjust ratios to taste preference
