**Date planned:** August 2023 for ~8-10 guests  
**Dietary accommodations:** Vegetarian and gluten-free options needed

**Menu components:**
- Appetizer: Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus
- Main: Vegetarian sushi rolls (avocado, cucumber, grilled portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura bits) with lemon-ginger dipping sauce
- Side: Lemon-herb quinoa with fresh herbs and pine nuts
- Dessert: Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option)
- Croissants (French pastry)

**Garnish ideas:** Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint), thinly sliced lemons, edible flowers (violas/pansies)

**Key recommendations:** Prep ahead where possible, cook in batches, serve courses sequentially (30-45 min between appetizer/main, 30-60 min main/dessert)
