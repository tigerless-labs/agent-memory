## Seasonal Photography Project

**Decision date:** 2023-08-04

User is committed to a photography project capturing the changing seasons in a natural setting (forest or wetland). Planning to:
- Visit a specific location (park or trail) regularly throughout the year
- Document light and color changes across seasons
- Potentially capture the same scene at the same time each year for year-to-year comparison
- Combine with wildlife/nature photography themes

**Motivation factors:**
- Interest in how light and colors change throughout the year
- Opportunity to experiment with different techniques and compositions
- Love for the beauty of the natural world

**Tools being used:**
- Desktop Lightroom for editing
- Considering mobile photo editing apps (Lightroom Mobile, VSCO, Snapseed)
