Campaign: Lost City of Eldorado - A game campaign featuring puzzles to challenge players of different skill levels. Puzzle Types: 1) Mirror Puzzle - large mirror with etched symbols players position to reveal hidden passages. 2) Elemental Puzzle - levers/buttons controlling earth, fire, air, water forces in specific sequences. 3) Musical Puzzle - instruments and sheet music requiring correct note sequence to unlock doors. 4) Weight Puzzle - different sized weights placed on scales in specific order to reveal passages. 5) Memory Puzzle - players memorize object locations before darkness, then navigate to retrieve specific items. Each puzzle combines logic, observation, and physical challenges.
