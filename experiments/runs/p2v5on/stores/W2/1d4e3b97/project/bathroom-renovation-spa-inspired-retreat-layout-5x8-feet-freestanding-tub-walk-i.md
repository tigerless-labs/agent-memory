Scope: Full bathroom renovation with spa-inspired retreat aesthetic in 5x8 ft space, keeping existing tub.

Design: Freestanding tub (est. 1000-3000 dollars) plus large walk-in shower with linear drain and double vanity.

Material decisions: Glazed porcelain tile for shower walls and floor (chosen for water resistance, durability, scratch/stain protection, easy cleaning). Cement-based grout with sealer for stain protection.

Budget ranges: Freestanding tub 1000-3000, tub filler 200-1000, shower enclosure 500-2000, shower pan 500-2000, plus tile, grout, flooring, walls. Professional plumbing and electrical required.

Installation approach: Will use grout float at 45-degree angle, work in small sections, apply grout sealer after curing.
