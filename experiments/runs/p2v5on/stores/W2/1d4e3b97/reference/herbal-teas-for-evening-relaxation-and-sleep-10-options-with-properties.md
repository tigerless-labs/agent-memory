Herbal teas recommended as caffeine-free alternatives for afternoon/evening relaxation:

1. **Chamomile** — classic for relaxation, calms nervous system
2. **Lavender** — reduces stress and anxiety
3. **Valerian root** — natural sedative, improves sleep quality
4. **Peppermint** — menthol calms mind and body
5. **Tart cherry** — contains melatonin, regulates sleep-wake cycles
6. **Passionflower** — natural sedative, reduces anxiety and insomnia
7. **Lemon balm** — reduces stress and anxiety, improves sleep quality
8. **Rooibos** — caffeine-free with antioxidants
9. **Hibiscus** — calming, can lower blood pressure
10. **GABA** — neurotransmitter for sleep and relaxation regulation

Tips: Choose organic, high-quality brands without added sugar or artificial flavorings. Brew 30-60 minutes before bed for best effect.
