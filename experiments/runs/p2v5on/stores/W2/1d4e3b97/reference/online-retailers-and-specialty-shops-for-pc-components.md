Recommended retailers for purchasing PC components:

**Major retailers**:
- Amazon.com
- Newegg.com  
- BestBuy.com
- Micro Center (microcenter.com) — physical stores + online with in-store pickup
- B&H Photo Video (bhphotovideo.com)

**Specialty shops and niche retailers**:
- PCPartPicker.com — component selection and price comparison across retailers
- Superbiiz.com — specializes in hard-to-find and discontinued items
- Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales — subreddit for PC component deals
- Performance-PCs.com — high-performance hardware, water cooling, modding parts
- FrozenCPU.com — computer hardware and custom PC modding parts

**Tips**: Compare prices and reviews before purchasing; check return policies and warranties. Niche shops may have limited availability, higher prices, or longer delivery times.
