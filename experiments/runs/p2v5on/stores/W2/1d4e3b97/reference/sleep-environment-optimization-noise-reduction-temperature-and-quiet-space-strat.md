Comprehensive strategies for creating an optimal sleep environment:

**Noise-Blocking Methods:**
- Earplugs (disposable or custom-made)
- Earmuffs
- Soundproofing curtains or acoustic panels
- Door sweeps or door seals (gaps under doors)
- Window inserts (for old/thin windows)
- Quiet fan for background masking

**Sleep Environment:**
- Bedroom temperature: 60-67°F (cool)
- Blackout curtains (if light is an issue)
- Sleep mask (for light blockage)
- Area rugs (reduce echo)
- Sound-absorbing materials: acoustic foam, fabric, plants
- Remove stimulating items (TVs, computers, exercise equipment)
- Weighted blanket or comfortable mattress topper

**Household Coordination:**
- Establish quiet hours
- Communicate sleep needs to household members
- Reduce noise during sleep hours

**Light & Circadian Support:**
- Morning sunlight exposure (helps regulate circadian rhythms)
- Consider sleep tracker device/app (monitors sleep duration, quality, stages)
