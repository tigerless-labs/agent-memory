Budget-friendly component recommendations for VR gaming PC build:

- **Graphics card**: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580
- **Processor**: AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K  
- **Memory**: 8 GB DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency
- **Motherboard**: MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, or Gigabyte GA-A320M-H
- **Storage**: 500 GB SSD such as WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D
- **Power Supply**: EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

These components offer good performance for VR gaming at more affordable prices than high-end options, with potential for later upgrades.
