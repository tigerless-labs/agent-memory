Audio recommendations for bedtime relaxation and sleep:

**Nature Sounds:**
- Rainfall (gentle rain)
- Ocean waves
- Forest sounds (birdsong, rustling leaves, wind)
- Babbling brook
- Wind chimes

**Music Genres:**
- Classical (Mozart, Chopin, Bach)
- Instrumental piano (slow, gentle)
- Acoustic guitar (soft, slow)
- Ambient music (soft textures, minimal melodies)
- New age (flutes, harps, gentle percussion)

**Specific Artists & Albums:**
- Brian Eno — *Ambient 1: Music for Airports*
- Max Richter — *Sleep* (8-hour album designed for sleep)
- Ludovico Einaudi — *Nightbook* (calming piano)
- Enya — *Watermark* (soothing vocals + gentle instrumentation)
- Steve Reich — *Music for 18 Musicians* (gentle percussion, soft melodies)

Available on: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, iTunes, Google Play Music
