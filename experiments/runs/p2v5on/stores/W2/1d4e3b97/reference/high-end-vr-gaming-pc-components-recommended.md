High-end component recommendations for VR gaming PC build:

- **Graphics card**: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, 3070, or 3060 Ti; AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT or 6800
- **Processor**: AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, or i7-11700K
- **Memory**: 16 GB+ DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency
- **Motherboard**: ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, or Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX
- **Storage**: SSD recommended for faster load times — Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850
- **Power Supply**: 750W+ high-quality PSU such as EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X

These are powerful components capable of handling demanding VR games with excellent performance.
