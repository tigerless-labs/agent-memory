### Recommended Route Planning Apps\n\n- **Ride with GPS** — Route creation, customization, turn-by-turn directions. Web, iOS, Android.\n- **MapMyRide** — Route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, community route database. Web, iOS, Android.\n- **Strava** — Social network for cyclists; route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis, popular routes, competition features. Web, iOS, Android.\n- **Google Maps** — Bicycling mode available; avoids highways, prefers bike paths and lanes. Web, iOS, Android.\n\n### Tips\n- Avoid busy roads; look for bike lanes or paths\n- Check road surface quality\n- Consider local bike laws and regulations\n- Local bike shops can recommend routes
