### Top Helmet Brands\n\n- **Bell** — High-quality helmets with MIPS technology. Model: Bell Zephyr (high-end road).\n- **Specialized** — Innovative features, Tri-Fix web splitter for fit. Model: Tactic 3.0 (versatile, MIPS).\n- **Giro** — Popular with cyclists, Roc Loc Air fit system, MIPS. Model: Synthe (premium road, aerodynamic).\n- **Trek** — Focus on comfort and ventilation, In-Mold construction. Model: Vapor 3 (lightweight, road/mountain).\n\n### Key Features to Look For\n- MIPS (Multi-Directional Impact Protection System)\n- Proper fit and adjustable system\n- Good ventilation for long rides\n- Lightweight construction\n- Safety certifications: CPSC, Snell, or EN 1078\n\n### Price Ranges\n- Entry-level: 30-50 USD (Bell Streak, Giro Fixture)\n- Mid-range: 50-100 USD (Specialized Chapeau, Trek Vapor 2)\n- High-end: 100-200 USD (Bell Zephyr, Giro Synthe)\n- Premium: 200-300 USD (Specialized Tactic 3.0, Trek Vapor 3)
