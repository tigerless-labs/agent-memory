### Pre-Ride\n- Drink 16-20 oz water or sports drink 1-2 hours before\n- Aim for pale yellow or clear urine\n\n### During Ride\n- Carry at least 1 water bottle (20-24 oz) per hour of riding\n- Sip every 10-15 minutes for steady fluid flow\n- Use electrolyte sports drinks (Gatorade, Nuun) to replace sodium/potassium lost to sweat—especially important for long/intense rides\n- Monitor urine color; dark yellow = drink more\n\n### Foods & Hydration\n- Eat high-water content foods: watermelon, bananas, energy bars with electrolytes\n- Avoid caffeine and sugary drinks (exacerbate dehydration)\n- Take breaks every hour to rest and rehydrate\n\n### Post-Ride\n- Drink water or sports drink to replenish fluids and electrolytes\n- Supports recovery and reduces muscle soreness
