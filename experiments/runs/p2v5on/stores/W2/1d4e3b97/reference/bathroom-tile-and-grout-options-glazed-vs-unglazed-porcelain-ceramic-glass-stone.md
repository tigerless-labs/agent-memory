Tile options for bathroom:

Shower walls: Glass tile (water-resistant, easy to clean), ceramic/porcelain (durable, affordable), natural stone (luxurious but high maintenance).

Shower floors: Porcelain/ceramic tile (slip-resistant, durable, look for matte/textured finish), mosaic tile (slip-resistant surface), quarry tile (natural, durable, slip-resistant).

Glazed vs Unglazed Porcelain: Glazed has shiny glossy finish with protection layer, easier to clean, better for walls. Unglazed has natural matte finish, better slip-resistance for floors, more budget-friendly, rougher texture.

Grout types:

Cement-based (most common): Affordable, suitable for most tile, requires sealing, prone to staining. Ratio varies by brand, mix to toothpaste consistency, apply with grout float at 45-degree angle, work in 4 sq ft sections, allow 10-15 min set time before cleaning.

Epoxy-based: Two-part, more durable and stain-resistant than cement, more expensive, strong odor.

Acrylic: Water-based, easy cleanup, quick-drying, flexible, resistant to cracks.

Urethane: Two-part, flexible, durable, stain and crack resistant.

Silicone: Flexible, used for joints and accents not full coverage.

Installation tips: Prepare substrate and ensure level, apply waterproof membrane to walls and floor, use tile adhesive suitable for wet areas, grout properly, apply grout sealer for protection. Work in well-lit area, use grout sponge for cleanup.
