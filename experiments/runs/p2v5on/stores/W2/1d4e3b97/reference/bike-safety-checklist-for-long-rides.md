### Pre-Ride Checks\n- Helmet: properly fitted, securely fastened, meets safety standards\n- Bike condition: tire pressure, brakes, gears all working\n- Reflective gear for low-light riding\n\n### On-Road Safety\n- Follow traffic laws and signals\n- Ride in same direction as traffic, use bike lanes\n- Wear bright/reflective clothing\n- Use hand signals for turns, merges, lane changes\n- Ride defensively; anticipate motorist and pedestrian actions\n- Maintain safe distance from vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists\n\n### Road Hazards to Avoid\n- Potholes, cracks, debris\n- Parked cars (risk of being doored)\n- Wet roads (reduce speed, increase distance)\n- Intersections (high accident risk)\n\n### Personal Preparedness\n- Carry basic toolkit: pump, tire levers, Allen wrenches\n- Bring phone and ID\n- Avoid distractions (phone, music)\n- Consider riding with a buddy\n- Take regular breaks to rest and stretch
