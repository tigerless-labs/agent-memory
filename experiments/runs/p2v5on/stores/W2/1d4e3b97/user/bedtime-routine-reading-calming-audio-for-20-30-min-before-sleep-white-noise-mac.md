Evening wind-down routine includes:
- Reading for 20-30 minutes before bed (on phone)
- Listening to calming music or nature sounds simultaneously  
- White noise machine running to block outside noise
- Getting ready for bed earlier than previous schedule

This combination has been effective for sleep quality improvement as of Feb 2024.
