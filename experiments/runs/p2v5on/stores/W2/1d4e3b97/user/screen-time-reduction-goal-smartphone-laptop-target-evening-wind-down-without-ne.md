**Devices:** smartphone and laptop (most used)

**Sleep schedule target:**
- Weekdays: 10 pm bedtime
- Weekends: 10:30 pm bedtime
- Wake-up: 7 am

**Current screen time:**
- 4-5 hours/day average
- Top 3 apps: social media, email, Netflix

**Problem areas:**
- Mindless scrolling 7-8 pm (after dinner/TV) and before bed
- Overreliance on Netflix for unwinding

**Strategies to implement:**
- Screen-free dinner zone (7-8 pm)
- 30-minute wind-down routine before bed (reading, journaling, stretching, music)
- Use Screen Time/Digital Wellbeing tracking
- Set intentional time limits on social media and Netflix (not bans, but scheduled)
- Replace some screen time with: reading, board games, puzzles, yoga, meditation, journaling
- Avoid replacing screens with screens (e.g., Netflix → phone scrolling)
- Bedroom as screen-free zone to encourage sleep

**Goal:** Reduce evening screen time while maintaining Netflix as occasional treat rather than default.
