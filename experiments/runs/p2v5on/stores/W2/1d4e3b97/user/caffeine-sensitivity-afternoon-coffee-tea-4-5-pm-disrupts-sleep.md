Discovered that regular afternoon caffeine consumption (coffee or tea around 4-5 PM, which was previously enjoyed) was negatively affecting sleep quality. Has now limited afternoon caffeine intake to improve evening wind-down.

Note: This was not previously recognized as a factor in sleep difficulties.
