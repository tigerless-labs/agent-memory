161 is a natural wine bar located in Sydenham, South East London. Serves dishes like Mushroom & Oloroso paté with pickled cabbage, pickled cucumber and fried bread. Carries natural wines including Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat (80% Semillon from organic grower in Bergerac, 20% Folle Blanche).
