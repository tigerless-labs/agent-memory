**Recent challenge (week of 2024-02-13):**
- Stayed up until 1 am for multiple nights in a row to finish work projects
- This disrupted bedtime routine and sleep schedule

**Current recovery plan:**
- Gradual bedtime adjustment: aim for target bedtime by making 15-30 min adjustments each night
- Re-establish calming pre-sleep routine this week
- Focus on task prioritization to avoid future late-night crunches

**Task prioritization strategies to implement:**
- Eisenhower Matrix (categorize by urgent/important)
- Break large tasks into smaller chunks
- Use task lists and time-blocking
- Avoid multitasking
- Set realistic deadlines (pad by 10-20% for interruptions)
- Learn to say no to overcommitment
- Take regular breaks to avoid burnout
- Review and adjust priorities regularly

**Work boundary goal:** Prevent work from encroaching on personal time; communicate workload concerns with manager to avoid committing to unrealistic deadlines.
