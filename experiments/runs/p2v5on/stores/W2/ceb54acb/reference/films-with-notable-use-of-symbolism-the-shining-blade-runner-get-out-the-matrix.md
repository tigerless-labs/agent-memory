## Films with rich symbolic imagery:\n\n- **The Shining** (1980): Stanley Kubrick's horror film uses maze symbolism and the color red for danger/violence\n- **Blade Runner** (1982): Ridley Scott's sci-fi uses eyes, animals, and nature imagery to explore identity and humanity\n- **Get Out** (2017): Jordan Peele's thriller uses white color symbolism for racism and deer imagery for victimhood\n- **The Matrix** (1999): The red/blue pills symbolize choice and free will\n\nDiscussed in context of symbolism adding depth without being necessary for a film to be great.
