Performance marketing SaaS persona profile for typical US real estate agent, developed using NAR statistics.

Persona: Susan Green
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female  
- Location: Suburban or rural area
- Income: Median 61,140 (2019 NAR data)
- Education: Bachelor's in Real Estate or related field

Characteristics:
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable and continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with good local market knowledge

Pain Points:
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking and analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents and brokerages
- Keeping up with latest real estate technology and trends

Goals:
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Stay ahead of competition
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

Key Insight: Using actual NAR data corrected initial assumptions. Agents are older, lower income, and more likely female than a generic stereotype suggested.
