Feels that when filmmakers try too hard with symbolism, it becomes distracting and detracts from the story. Believes the best use of symbolism is thoughtful and spare, enhancing the narrative rather than overwhelming it. Values storytelling that comes first, with symbolism as enrichment rather than the primary focus.
