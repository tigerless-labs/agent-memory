Decided to organize loyalty programs using:

**Tracking method:** Spreadsheet with columns for program name, points/rewards earned, points needed for reward, expiration date, and notes

**Reminder approach:** Phone reminders to use loyalty apps/cards when near specific stores or at certain times of day

**Habit formation:** Make using loyalty cards/apps part of daily routine (e.g., every time visiting a store or before purchase)

**Staying informed:** Intent to monitor promotions and new offers through newsletters, social media, mobile apps, in-store materials, and loyalty aggregators

Motivation: Wants to maximize rewards and not miss opportunities to earn points or special offers
