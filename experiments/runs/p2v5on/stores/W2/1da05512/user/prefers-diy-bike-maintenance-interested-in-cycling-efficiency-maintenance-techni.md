Ryan is comfortable and prefers to perform his own bike maintenance work when possible, including flat tire repair, chain cleaning and lubrication, and component installation. 

Interests:
- Cycling efficiency improvements (researches pedal types and their performance benefits)
- Proper bike maintenance techniques and checklists
- Route planning for long-distance rides
- Learning about clipless pedal systems and their advantages

He uses online tools like MapMyRide for planning routes and researches product reviews before making bike upgrades. Will take bike to a shop for complex work (like brake adjustments) when needed, but approaches it methodically by preparing the bike and documenting issues beforehand.
