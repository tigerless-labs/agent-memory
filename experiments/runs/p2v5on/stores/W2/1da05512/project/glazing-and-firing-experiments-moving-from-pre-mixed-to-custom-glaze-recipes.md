Currently experimenting with different glazes on earthenware pieces. Goal is to achieve consistent, food-safe finishes. Moving from using pre-mixed glazes to creating own glaze recipes. Specific interests: glaze formulation from scratch, selecting materials and understanding ingredient roles, achieving specific colors and effects (subtle blues, vibrant reds, textured matte finishes), mixing and testing glazes, and application techniques (brushing, pouring, spraying).
