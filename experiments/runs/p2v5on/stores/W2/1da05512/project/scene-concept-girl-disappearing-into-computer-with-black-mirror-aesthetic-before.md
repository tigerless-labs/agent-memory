**Scene concept:** Girl about to sit down and speak into a microphone slowly disappears into the computer before she can speak.

**Aesthetic goal:** Black Mirror-ish tone — dark, unsettling, dystopian

**Suggested techniques:**
- **Visual effects:** Glitchy/distorted computer interface, flickering lights, static noise, distortion effects, flashes of light, swirling motion to simulate being 'sucked' into the computer
- **Audio:** Eerie sound design — haunting melody, discordant sounds, white noise, low hum, distant whispers, unsettling atmosphere
- **Camera:** Slow deliberate movements to build tension; zoom in on face/hands for intimacy; disorienting camera movements during disappearance
- **Lighting:** Dim lighting, backlighting, shadows to create mood and mystery
- **Editing:** Quick cuts to jar the viewer; cut to computer interface after disappearance to suggest something strange happening within

**Status:** Creative exploration from 2023
