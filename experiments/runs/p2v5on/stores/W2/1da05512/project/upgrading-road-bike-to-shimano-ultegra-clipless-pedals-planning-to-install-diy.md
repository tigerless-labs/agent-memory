Ryan has decided to upgrade his road bike with Shimano Ultegra clipless pedals (SPD-SL system). He has researched options and decided on the Ultegra model due to strong reviews. Plans to:
- Order the pedals online
- Install them himself using a pedal wrench and torque wrench
- Adjust cleats and tension appropriately
- Test thoroughly before riding
- Can take to bike shop if installation proves difficult

Benefits he's seeking: improved pedaling efficiency, increased power transfer, reduced fatigue.
