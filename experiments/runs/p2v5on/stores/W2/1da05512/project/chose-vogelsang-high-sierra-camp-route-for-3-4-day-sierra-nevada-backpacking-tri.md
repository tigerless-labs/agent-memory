After initially considering the High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft elevation gain), reassessed ability level and chose the Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route instead:

- **Distance**: 20 miles
- **Elevation gain**: 3,000 feet  
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Base elevation**: 10,300 feet at camp
- **Access**: 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows, or shuttle from visitor center
- **Accommodations**: Tent cabins with mattresses, pillows, wooden floors
- **Amenities**: Full food service (breakfast, lunch, dinner), modern restrooms, showers, potable water, camp store
- **Permits**: Required through Yosemite Conservancy (book early, fills quickly)
- **Trip timing**: September 2023

This route offers an excellent introduction to high-elevation backpacking with stunning alpine scenery while being more manageable than the full loop.
