Sierra Nevada backpacking route options considered but not chosen (May 2023):

1. **High Sierra Camp Loop**
   - Distance: 40 miles
   - Elevation gain: 7,000 feet
   - Difficulty: Moderate to challenging
   - Route: Tuolumne Meadows → Glen Aulin → May Lake → Vogelsang → Tuolumne Meadows
   - Terrain: Alpine meadows, forests, high-elevation traverses above 10,000 ft, mountain passes (~11,000 ft)
   - Not chosen due to concerns about readiness for this level of challenge

2. **Rae Lakes Loop**
   - Distance: 45 miles
   - Elevation gain: 6,000 feet
   - Difficulty: Moderate
   - Route: Road's End (Kings Canyon) → Rae Lakes → Glen Pass → Paradise Valley → Road's End
   - Features: Rae Lakes, Fin Dome, Kings River Canyon

3. **Ansel Adams Wilderness - Thousand Island Lake**
   - Distance: 30 miles
   - Elevation gain: 4,000 feet
   - Difficulty: Moderate
   - Route: Agnew Meadows → Thousand Island Lake
   - Features: Minarets, Ritter Range

4. **Emigrant Wilderness - Emigrant Lake**
   - Distance: 35 miles
   - Elevation gain: 3,500 feet
   - Difficulty: Moderate
   - Route: Crabtree → Emigrant Lake
   - Character: Lesser-visited, more secluded experience

All routes require permits (apply early).
