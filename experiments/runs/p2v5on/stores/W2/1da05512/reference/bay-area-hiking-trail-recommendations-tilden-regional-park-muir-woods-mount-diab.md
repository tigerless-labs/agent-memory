Recommended Bay Area day hike options for solo hiking (as of May 2023):

1. **Muir Woods and Dipsea Trail**: Redwood forests, scenic Steep Ravine Trail
2. **Berkeley Hills: Tilden Regional Park** - Chose this option
   - Nimitz Way Trail: 4.5 mi out-and-back, 1,000 ft gain, moderate, 3-4 hours
   - Wildcat Gorge Trail: 5.5 mi out-and-back, 1,200 ft gain, moderate-challenging, 4-5 hours
   - Panoramic views of Bay Area, San Francisco, and Golden Gate Bridge
3. **Mount Diablo State Park**: East Bay's highest peak (3,849 ft), 360-degree views
   - Summit Trail or Falls Trail: 4-10 miles
4. **Sam McDonald County Park**: Santa Cruz Mountains
   - Heritage Grove Trail: 4.5 miles through redwood forest
5. **Purisima Creek Redwoods Open Space Preserve**: San Mateo County coastside
   - Purisima Creek Trail: 4.5 miles
   - Whittemore Gulch Trail: 6.5 miles
6. **Mission Peak Regional Preserve**: Fremont
   - 6.5-mile out-and-back to summit with Bay Area views

All trails recommend bringing plenty of water, sun protection, notifying someone of your itinerary.
