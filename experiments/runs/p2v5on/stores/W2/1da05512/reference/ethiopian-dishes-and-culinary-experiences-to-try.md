**Injera** — Sourdough flatbread; staple of Ethiopian cuisine, used as a utensil to scoop up stews and curries.\n\n**Doro Wot** — Spicy chicken stew made with berbere spice (blend of chili powder, ginger, coriander, and other spices).\n\n**Tibs** — Spicy sautéed meat dish made with beef or lamb, onions, and peppers.\n\n**Kitfo** — Raw beef dish seasoned with spices and chili powder.\n\n**Shiro** — Thick stew made with ground chickpeas or lentils, served with injera.\n\n**Coffee ceremony** — Traditional Ethiopian experience: roasting beans, grinding them, and brewing in a special pot. Ethiopia is famous for its coffee.
