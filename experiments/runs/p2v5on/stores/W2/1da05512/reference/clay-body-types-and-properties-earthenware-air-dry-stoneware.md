Earthenware: easy to work with, soft and pliable, low firing temperature (around 1800F/980C), colorful glaze options, but brittle/prone to cracking, not suitable for functional ware. Air-dry: no kiln required, fast results, easy to repair, but limited durability, not suitable for outdoor use, best for smaller simpler projects. Stoneware: strong and durable, high-temperature firing (2400F/1300C), food-safe and watertight, ideal for functional ware, but more challenging to work with, requires high-temperature kiln, limited glaze options.
