YouTube channels: Simon Leach Pottery, Pottery By Osa, The Ceramic School. Websites and courses: Craftsy, Pottery Making Illustrated, The Potter's Council, Ceramic Arts Network. Recommended books: 'The Potter's Bible' by Marylin Scott (comprehensive guide to ceramics including glazing and firing), 'Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter' by Ian Gregory (detailed guide to glaze formulation and application). Also recommended: keeping a glaze journal to record tests and firing conditions, joining ceramic communities, and experimenting with different glazes and techniques.
