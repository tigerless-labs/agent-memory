Cloud Gate is a large-scale stainless steel sculpture by Anish Kapoor, installed in Millennium Park, Chicago in 2004. Also known as The Bean. Made of 168 stainless steel panels, weighing over 110 tons. Dimensions: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long. Mirror-like surface polished to high shine creates reflective, curved effect that distorts surroundings. Interactive piece inviting viewers to walk, touch, and photograph. Surface cleaned daily. Symbolic of Chicago's connection to sky and clouds, and the city's vibrant energy.
