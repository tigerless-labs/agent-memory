- Address: 145 Cliff Road, Montross, VA
- Offers: kayak, canoe, and paddleboard rentals
- Location: Potomac River

**Northern Neck Kayaking & Outdoor Rentals**
- Address: 126 Jessie duPont Memorial Highway, Burgess, VA
- Offers: kayak and canoe rentals, tours
- Waters: Great Wicomico River, Chesapeake Bay, various Northern Neck waterways
- Includes life jackets with rentals
