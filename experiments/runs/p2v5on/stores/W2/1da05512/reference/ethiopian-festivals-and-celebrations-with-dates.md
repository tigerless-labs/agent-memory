**Timkat** — January 19th. Religious festival marking the baptism of Jesus. Features processions, music, dancing, and water splashing.\n\n**Meskel** — September 27th. Commemorates the finding of the True Cross. Includes bonfires, feasting, and traditional dancing.\n\n**Enkutatash** — September 11th. Ethiopian New Year. Time of joyous celebration with feasting, dancing, and gift exchange.\n\n**Fasika** — April (dates vary). Ethiopian Orthodox Easter celebration. Features fasting, prayer, colorful processions, feasting, and gift exchange.
