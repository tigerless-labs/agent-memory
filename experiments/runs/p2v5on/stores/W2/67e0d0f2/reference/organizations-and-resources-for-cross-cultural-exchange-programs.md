## Exchange Program Organizations

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** — https://afs.org/ — Non-profit offering student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, and volunteering in 50+ countries

2. **Experiment in International Living** — https://www.experiment.org/ — Immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students with host family placements

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** — https://www.yfu.org/ — Exchange programs for students and young adults including year-long exchanges, semester programs, and summer programs in 40+ countries

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** — https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/ — Volunteering opportunities in Costa Rica, Ghana, India, and Peru focused on community development and cultural exchange

## Preparation Tips

- Research country, culture, customs, history, current events
- Learn basics of local language
- Pack appropriately for climate and culture
- Be open-minded and flexible with new foods and social norms
- Develop relationships with locals
- Stay safe and follow local customs and laws
