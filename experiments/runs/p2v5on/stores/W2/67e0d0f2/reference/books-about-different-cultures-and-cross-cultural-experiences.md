## Recommended Books

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri — Explores identity, family, and culture through the story of a Bengali-American family

2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini — Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan, exploring themes of friendship, betrayal, and redemption

3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan — Follows the lives of four Chinese-American women and their relationships with their immigrant mothers

4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe — Explores the life of a Nigerian tribe before and after the arrival of British colonizers

5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi — Graphic novel about a young Iranian girl growing up during the Islamic Revolution
