Language Proficiency in Canada is measured by Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB), ranging from 0-12 (beginner to advanced).

**Typical Job Requirements:**
- Most jobs: CLB 5-6 (basic fluency, everyday communication)
- Customer service/sales: CLB 6-7
- Administrative/office: CLB 7-8
- Skilled trades/technical: CLB 5-6
- Professional roles (healthcare, finance, law): CLB 9-10 or higher

**Language Certifications:**
- LINC (Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada)
- IELTS (International English Language Testing System)
- TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language)
- CELPIP (Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program)

**Other Job Market Info:**
- Minimum wage in Ontario (as of 2022): CAD 15.00/hour
- Entry-level salary range: CAD 35,000-50,000/year
- Job search resources: Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca), Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor
