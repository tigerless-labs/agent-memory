Key organizations for refugee and newcomer support:\n\n- **The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO)**\n- **COSTI Immigrant Services**\n- **WoodGreen Community Services**\n- **Refugee Sponsorship Support Program (RSSP)**\n- **Toronto Refugee Centre**\n- **North York Community House**\n- **Etobicoke Newcomer Centre**\n- **West Toronto Newcomer Services**\n- **Toronto Affordable Housing Office**\n\nServices offered: settlement support, language classes, housing assistance, job search support, community integration programs.\n\nOther resources: City of Toronto Rent Supplement Program, YMCA housing, Salvation Army subsidized housing, Toronto Public Library.
