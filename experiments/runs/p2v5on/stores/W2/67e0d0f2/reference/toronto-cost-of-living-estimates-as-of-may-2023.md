**Monthly Rent (approximate):**\n- Bachelor/shared: CAD 1,200-1,600\n- 1-bedroom apartment: CAD 1,600-2,000 (high-rise), CAD 1,200-1,600 (other)\n- 2-bedroom: CAD 1,800-2,500\n- 3-bedroom: CAD 2,500-3,500\n\n**Food:**\n- Groceries: CAD 500-750/month\n- Casual dining: CAD 10-15/meal\n- Mid-range restaurants: CAD 20-30/meal\n- Food delivery/takeout: CAD 10-20/meal\n\n**Transportation:**\n- TTC monthly pass: CAD 156.00\n- TTC single fare: CAD 3.00 (cash), CAD 2.90 (Presto card)\n- Bike Share Toronto: CAD 15-20/month\n\n**Other Monthly Expenses:**\n- Utilities (hydro, water, internet): CAD 100-200\n- Phone and internet: CAD 50-100\n- Entertainment/leisure: CAD 200-500\n\n**Budgeting Tips:**\n- Create individualized budget\n- Prioritize needs over wants\n- Consider roommates to split costs\n- Take advantage of free community resources (libraries, parks)\n- Aim to save 10-20% of income
