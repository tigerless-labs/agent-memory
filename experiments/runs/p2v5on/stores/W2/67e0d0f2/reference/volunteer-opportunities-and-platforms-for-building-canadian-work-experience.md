Volunteering is valued by Canadian employers as relevant work experience and helps build professional networks.\n\n**Volunteer Platforms:**\n- Volunteer Toronto\n- Idealist\n- LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace\n\n**Benefits of volunteering:**\n- Gain Canadian work experience\n- Build professional network in field of interest\n- Demonstrate commitment and skills to future employers\n- Connect with community and gain cultural knowledge\n- Can lead to job opportunities\n\nNote: Particularly relevant for newcomers seeking to overcome barriers like unrecognized credentials or lack of Canadian work experience.
