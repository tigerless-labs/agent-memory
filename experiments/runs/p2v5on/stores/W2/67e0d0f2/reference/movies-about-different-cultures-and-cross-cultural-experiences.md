## Recommended Movies

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** — Martial arts epic set in ancient China exploring themes of loyalty and honor

2. **The Lives of Others** — German film set in East Berlin during the Cold War exploring themes of surveillance, loyalty, and human connection

3. **Lion** — True story of an Indian boy who gets lost and adopted by an Australian family, with a journey to find his birth family

4. **The Motorcycle Diaries** — Based on the travel diaries of Che Guevara and friend Alberto as they travel across South America, exploring injustices faced by the poor

5. **City of God** — Brazilian film following characters living in a violent favela (slum), exploring themes of poverty, violence, and survival
