Neighborhoods with strong support for refugees and newcomers in Toronto:\n\n1. **Downtown East** (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown) - High concentration of newcomer communities, refugee organizations, community centers, language schools\n2. **Scarborough** - Large immigrant population, refugee communities from Africa, Asia, Middle East; Scarborough Village Recreation Centre offers newcomer services\n3. **North York** - Significant refugee population; North York Community House and Centre for Information and Community Services; close to York University\n4. **Etobicoke** - Growing refugee community; Etobicoke Newcomer Centre and West Toronto Newcomer Services; convenient to Toronto Pearson Airport\n5. **Parkdale** - History of welcoming refugees and immigrants; strong community sense; close to city center; vibrant arts scene\n\nConsiderations: proximity to services, public transit access, safety, walkability, affordable housing, diversity.
