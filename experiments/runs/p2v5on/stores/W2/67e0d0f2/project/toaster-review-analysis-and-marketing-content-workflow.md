Workflow for turning customer reviews into marketing content:

1. Format reviews into bullet points - extract key points from each review and group by reviewer
2. Write positive response comments - address each bullet point with empathy and acknowledge feedback  
3. Create blog post - organize by categories, include positive quotes, acknowledge mixed reviews, lead with benefits

Key issues identified: uneven toasting, no bagel feature, digital controls require monitoring, large/lightweight, gets hot on sides, short cord, loud eject, poor crumb containment.

Positive features: sleek design, wide slots, 4-slice capacity, timing function for meal prep, easy to operate.

Marketing approach: frame trade-offs positively, acknowledge issues while emphasizing benefits, target busy families.
