Effective approach for managing intensive online learning (e.g., 11-week Coursera course): Create dedicated calendar, use structured note-taking system, break assignments into chunks, allocate specific study times. Recommended to work on personal projects during coursework to reinforce learning and build portfolio - start small with projects aligned to course topics. Join study groups for motivation and peer learning. Key: prioritize coursework completion while supplementing with practical application projects.
