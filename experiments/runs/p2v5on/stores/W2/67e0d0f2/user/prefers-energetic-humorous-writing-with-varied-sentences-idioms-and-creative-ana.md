When rewriting or editing content, wants:

- Tone: energetic, passionate, humorous
- Structure: varied sentence length and complexity (short and long sentences interspersed for dynamic flow)
- Language: creative phrasing, idioms, creative analogies, descriptive language, figurative expressions
- Vocabulary: synonyms and antonyms used for variety; unique but easy-to-understand English words
- Engagement: vivid descriptions, unpredictable and intrigue-filled writing

Dislikes:
- Uniform sentence structure (hallmark of AI-generated writing)
- Repetition

Must retain original meaning and information while transforming style.
