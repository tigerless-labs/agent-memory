Planning to try cold brew with dark roasted coffee beans.

Preferred ratio: 1:4 (coffee to water)  
Grind: coarse  
Steeping time: 12-24 hours  

Reasoning: Dark roasts provide bold, rich flavor with deep chocolate/caramel notes and lower acidity, which works well in cold brew's low-acidity method without being overpowered. Prefers to avoid over-extraction by using coarser grind and potentially shorter steeping times (12-18 hours for dark roasts).
