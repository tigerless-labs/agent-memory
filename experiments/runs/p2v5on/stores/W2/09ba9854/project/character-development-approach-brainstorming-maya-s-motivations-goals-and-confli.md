Approach to developing protagonist Maya: Using brainstorming exercises to understand her short-term and long-term goals, core motivations, what drives her to save the planet, and what she's willing to sacrifice. Focus areas include internal and external conflicts, how her goals create tension with other characters, her values and beliefs, past experiences shaping her motivations, and how she'll react to obstacles and moral dilemmas.
