## Project: Sci-Fi Romance Novel (Untitled)

**Protagonist: Lyra**
- Skilled memory thief
- Has no recollection of their own past; memories are fragmented and unclear
- Identity is shrouded in mystery, even to themselves

**Core Plot**
- Lyra is hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO
- While diving into the CEO's memories, Lyra uncovers clues about their own past
- This discovery challenges Lyra's sense of self and purpose

**Key Plot Twists**
1. Discovery of Lyra's true past and the reason for their memory loss
   - Forces Lyra to re-evaluate their sense of self and purpose
   - Decision point: continue as memory thief or forge new path
2. Revelation of CEO's true intentions and consequences of memory theft
   - Challenges Lyra's understanding of their role and relationships

**Themes Explored**
- Identity and self-discovery
- Nature of memory and authenticity of identity
- Questions about whether memories/identity can be fabricated or altered
- Authenticity of experiences and relationships in a world where memories can be manipulated

**Sci-Fi Elements**
- Advanced neurotechnology for memory extraction/manipulation
- Memory trade industry (black market and regulated)
- Possibility of fabricated or altered memories

**Inspiration Sources**
- Story prompt: "The Memory Thief" — exploring world where memories can be extracted and sold
- Also considering elements from "The Royal Affair" prompt (identity, forbidden connections, high-stakes romance)
