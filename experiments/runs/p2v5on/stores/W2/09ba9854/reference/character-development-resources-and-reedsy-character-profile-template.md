Resources for character development:

Websites: Writers Digest (writersdigest.com/write-better-characters), The Creative Penn (thecreativepenn.com/character-development/), Helping Writers Become Authors, The Write Practice (thewritepractice.com/character-development-exercises/), Novel Writing Help

Character Profiling Tools: Reedsy Character Profile Template (free, 12 sections: Basic Info, Physical Appearance, Personality, Backstory, Motivations, Conflict, Relationships, Skills/Abilities, Fears/Phobias, Habits/Quirks, Emotional Landscape, Evolution), 16 Personalities test

Books: 'Characters & Viewpoint' by Orson Scott Card, 'The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers' by Christopher Vogler, 'How to Write Short Stories and Use Them to Further Your Writing Career' by James Scott Bell
