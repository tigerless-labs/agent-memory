**External Hard Drives (for photo backup):**
- Western Digital My Book (up to 16TB, compact, Mac/PC compatible)
- Seagate Backup Plus (up to 5TB, sleek design, 2-year warranty)
- LaCie Rugged (rugged/durable, up to 5TB)
- G-Technology G-Drive (high-performance, up to 10TB, for professionals)

**Lens Cases:**
- Think Tank Photo Lens Case (soft padding, good protection, size-specific)
- Lowepro Lens Case (durable, various sizes and styles)
- Nanuk Lens Case (extreme protection, shock absorption, rugged)

**Lens Pouches:**
- Op/Tech USA Lens Pouch (soft padded, various sizes, Velcro closure)
- Lenscoat Lens Pouch (size-specific, water-resistant, breathable)
- Tamrac Lens Pouch (durable, various sizes, zippered closure)

**Lens Cleaning Kits:**
- Canon Lens Cleaning Kit (official, soft brush, microfiber cloth, solution)
- Nikon Lens Cleaning Kit (suitable for Canon lenses too)
- Zeiss Lens Cleaning Kit (high-quality microfiber cloth, solution, soft brush)

**Lens Cleaning Cloths:**
- Microfiber cloths (lint-free, safe for lenses)
- Pre-moistened lens cleaning tissues
- Sensor cleaning swabs (for more thorough cleaning)
