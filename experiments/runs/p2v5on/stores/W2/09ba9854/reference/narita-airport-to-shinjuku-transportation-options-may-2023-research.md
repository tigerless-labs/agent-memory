Direct trains: Narita Express (NX) costs 3020 yen (~28 USD), 60-70 min to Shinjuku Station. Keisei Skyliner plus Tokyo Metro Ginza Line costs 2520 plus 200-400 yen (~23-27 USD), 60-70 min total with transfer at Ueno. Budget option: Keisei Electric Railway costs 1000-1200 yen (~9-11 USD), 80-90 min with multiple transfers. Also: Airport Limousine Bus 3200 yen (~29 USD), 60-90 min. Taxi or ride-hailing 20000-30000 yen (~180-270 USD). Prices from May 2023 research.
