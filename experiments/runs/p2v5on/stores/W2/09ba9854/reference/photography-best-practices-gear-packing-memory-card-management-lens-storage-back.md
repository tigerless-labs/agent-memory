**Packing Camera Bag (Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW):**
1. Categorize gear by type; prioritize frequently-used items
2. Use adjustable dividers to separate and organize
3. Pack camera bodies and lenses in main compartment first
4. Store small items (batteries, cards, filters) in pouches
5. Utilize exterior pockets for tripod (via straps), bottles, accessories
6. Roll cables to save space and prevent tangles
7. Place heavy items at bottom for balance
8. Don't overpack; leave room for acquired items and padding
9. Use weather-sealed pockets for phone, wallet, essentials
10. Experiment with configurations to find optimal arrangement

**Memory Card Management:**
- Label cards with capacity, type, and unique identifier
- Use color-coding to differentiate cards
- Store in protective case or holder to prevent loss/damage
- Organize by capacity, type, or usage
- Maintain inventory list of cards (in-use, available, needs formatting)
- Format cards regularly before each use
- Develop file naming system by date, camera, or project
- Rotate cards regularly to prevent overuse
- Clean cards gently; avoid touching contacts

**Lens Storage & Protection:**
- Store lenses with caps on
- Keep lenses upright (prevents oil migration)
- Avoid stacking lenses
- Clean before storage
- Use protective cases/pouches with thick padding
- Look for breathable materials to prevent moisture buildup

**Photo Backup Strategy:**
- Use 3-2-1 backup rule: 3 copies, 2 different media types, 1 offsite
- Set up automatic backups
- Periodically verify backups are complete and restorable
- Consider USB 3.0, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C for speed
- Calculate capacity needs (at least 2-3x current storage)

**Lens Cleaning:**
- Use soft, lint-free cloths; avoid paper towels
- Handle lens by edges to prevent oil transfer
- Apply solution sparingly to cloth, not directly to lens
- Clean in circular motion from center outward
- Test small area first
- Read kit instructions; test before full cleaning
