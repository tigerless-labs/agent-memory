Blueberry muffin recipe: 2 cups all-purpose flour (can substitute with whole wheat flour), 1/2 cup sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 cup melted butter, 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1.5 cups blueberries. Bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes. Whole wheat flour makes denser muffins; may need honey/maple syrup for extra sweetness.
