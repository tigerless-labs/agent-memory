Healthy oatmeal cookie recipe: 2 cups rolled oats, 1 cup almond meal, 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 cup coconut oil, 1/4 cup honey, 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1/2 cup raisins (or dark chocolate chips for added sweetness). Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes. Dark chocolate chips offer healthier alternative with higher cocoa and less sugar.
