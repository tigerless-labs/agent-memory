**Lighting:**
- Use natural light when possible; position items near windows with diffused light
- Golden hour provides soft, warm light
- Use softbox or diffused light to soften harsh shadows
- Avoid direct flash; if using flash, diffuse or bounce off surface
- Consider layered lighting (softbox for overall + sidelight for texture)

**Composition:**
- Plain, neutral backgrounds (white, gray, black) to isolate item
- Rule of thirds: place item along horizontal/vertical thirds
- Experiment with unique angles (low, high, Dutch angles)
- Close-up shots of specific features (watch face, guitar fretboard, comic cover)
- Show details and condition clearly

**Editing:**
- Color correction: adjust temperature/saturation for accuracy
- Noise reduction and sharpening (avoid over-sharpening)
- Retouching: remove dust/scratches but avoid making images look unnatural
- Maintain consistent look across all images
- Never over-retouch or deceive about condition

**Technical tips:**
- Use tripod for steady, sharp images
- Shoot in RAW format for editing flexibility
- Ensure sharp focus on the item
- Consider shallow depth of field to isolate subject
- Include lifestyle shots showing item in use (watch on wrist, guitar being played)

**General strategy:**
- Establish consistent visual brand across listings
- High-quality, well-lit images are crucial for high-end items
- Multiple angles and close-ups build buyer confidence
