**Recommended selling platforms by item type:**

**Vintage Watches (e.g. Omega Seamaster):**
- eBay (use Authenticity Guarantee program)
- Chrono24 (specialized luxury watches)
- 1stdibs (high-end luxury items)
- The RealReal (consignment model)
- WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums (specialized communities)
- Local watch dealers/collectors
- Auction houses (Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions)

**Comics (e.g. First edition Avengers #1):**
- ComicConnect (specialized comics platform)
- eBay (dedicated comics category)
- Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, r/comicbooks (communities)
- Local comic book stores

**Toys/Action Figures (e.g. Batman figure):**
- eBay (large toy collector community)
- Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk (communities)
- ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room (specialized markets)
- Local toy/collectible stores

**Guitars (e.g. Fender Jazzmaster):**
- Reverb (instrument-focused marketplace)
- eBay (vintage/collectible categories)
- The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI (guitar communities)
- Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange
- Local music stores

**Electronics (e.g. Sony Walkman):**
- eBay (vintage electronics category)
- Gearslutz, Resonance FM, vintage audio forums
- Facebook Marketplace, local online selling groups

**Currency (e.g. Cuban peso note):**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG)
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
- Reputable numismatic experts/currency appraisers

**General tips:**
- Research fees for each platform
- Provide high-quality photos, detailed descriptions, documentation
- Set competitive prices based on market research
- Get professional appraisals/certifications (CGC for comics, Wata/PSA for toys)
- Keep all documentation and certificates
- Consider multiple appraisals for accuracy
