**Games & Activities**: Scavenger hunt, outdoor games (badminton, cornhole), nature crafts, treasure hunt, storytelling, picnic bingo, nature walk, kite flying, food-themed games, photobooth.

**Jam Session with Kids**: Kid-friendly songs (Twinkle Twinkle, Wheels on the Bus, pop songs like Happy), call-and-response, movement activities (marching, dancing, clapping), storytelling through music, improvisation, shaker instruments, musical games (Freeze Dance, Musical Chairs).

**Baby Accommodation**: Designated quiet shaded area, baby kit (diapers, wipes, formula), baby-friendly toys (soft blocks, rattles), regular breaks, assign adult buddy for help, plan around baby's nap schedule, create nap-friendly zone.
