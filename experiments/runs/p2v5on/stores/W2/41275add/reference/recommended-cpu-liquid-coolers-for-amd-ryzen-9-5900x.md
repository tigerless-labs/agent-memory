Top liquid cooler recommendations for Ryzen 9 5900X:

1. NZXT Kraken X73 - 360mm, 3-fan, RGB, specifically designed for AMD Ryzen
2. Corsair Hydro Series H115i RGB Platinum - 280mm radiator, excellent performance, RGB customization via iCUE
3. EKWB EK-AIO 360 D-RGB - High-performance, 360mm, 3 fans, customizable RGB, AMD-optimized
4. Thermaltake Floe Riing 360 - 360mm, 3 fans, RGB, sleek design
5. Noctua NH-U14S TR4-SP3 - Air cooler alternative (high-quality, quiet, socket-compatible)

Selection criteria: radiator size, fan noise levels, RGB compatibility/software, budget 100-200 dollars, warranty and support reputation
