**Apps recommended for beginners:**
- Headspace
- Calm

**Beginner meditation types:**
1. **Mindfulness Meditation** — Focus on present moment without judgment; sit quietly, observe breath/thoughts/emotions, refocus when distracted
2. **Guided Meditation** — Listen to recorded guided meditation (good for maintaining focus)
3. **Loving-kindness Meditation** — Cultivate love/compassion toward self and others; visualize someone you care about and silently repeat kindness phrases

**Key principle:** Consistency matters more than duration. Start with 5-10 minutes, even a few times per week is beneficial.
