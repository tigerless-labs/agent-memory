**Omega watch authentication/servicing:**
- Omega offers certification and authentication services for vintage watches
- Service includes: authenticity verification, certification of origin, condition report
- Omega issues Certificate of Authenticity with unique certificate number
- Certificate includes: serial number, model, production date, Omega signature/stamp
- Benefits: increased value, enhanced credibility for buyers, better resale value
- Contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service for process/cost/timeline
- Keep all documentation: certification, service records, correspondence

**General appraisal process:**
1. Find reputable expert specializing in the item type
2. Submit item with relevant documentation/provenance
3. Appraiser examines condition, authenticity, rarity
4. Appraiser researches item, verifies existence and market demand
5. Receive appraisal report with description, authenticity opinion, estimated value

**Appraisal documentation types:**
- Verbal appraisal: informal, general value idea
- Written appraisal: more detailed formal assessment
- Formal appraisal report: extensive document with thorough analysis

**Currency/numismatic appraisal:**
- Find appraiser through Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG) or International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
- Appraisal report should include: denomination, series, condition, authenticity, rarity, estimated value, historical info
- May receive separate Certificate of Authenticity

**Authentication services vary by item:**
- Comics: CGC (Certified Guaranty Company)
- Toys: Wata Games, Professional Sports Authenticator (PSA)
- Watches: Manufacturer certification or third-party watchmaker certification
- Currency: Professional numismatic appraisers

**Best practices:**
- Keep all appraisal reports and documentation safe
- Get multiple appraisals from different experts for accuracy
- Be honest about item history, condition, provenance
- Use formal appraisals for insurance and high-value sales
