**Cashew Milk for Yogurt**

Ratio: 1 cup cashews to 1/2 cup fresh water (adjust for preferred thickness)

**Soaking:**
1. Rinse 1 cup raw cashews under cold water
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight
3. After soaking, drain and rinse again

**Blending:**
1. Add soaked cashews to blender with 1/2 cup fresh water
2. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
3. May need to stop and scrape down sides

**Straining:**
- Use cheesecloth, nut milk bag, or fine-mesh sieve
- Strain to remove remaining solids for smooth consistency
- Squeeze/twist to extract as much liquid as possible

**Tips:**
- Less water = thicker yogurt; more water = thinner
- Fine-mesh sieve easier to clean but may allow some particles through
- Nut milk bag best for filtration but can be pricey
- Cheesecloth very effective but messier to use
- Chill mixture in refrigerator for at least 2 hours to allow further thickening
