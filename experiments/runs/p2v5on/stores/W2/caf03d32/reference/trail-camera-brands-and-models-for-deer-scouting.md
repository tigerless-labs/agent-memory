Budget-friendly: Bushnell (Core DS No Glow, Prime L10), Moultrie (A-20, M-880i). Mid-range: Reconyx (HyperFire 2, UltraFire), Covert (Code Black 12, MP6). High-end: Browning (Dark Ops HD Max), Spypoint (Link-EVO, Solar-Dark). Cellular options: Spartan GoCam, Covert Code Black 4G. Solar-powered: Spypoint Solar-Dark, Reconyx Solar. Key features: 10MP+, good low-light performance, fast trigger speed, long battery life, waterproof, video and time-lapse capability. Retailers: Amazon, Bass Pro Shops, Cabela's, Walmart, Trailcameratrader.
