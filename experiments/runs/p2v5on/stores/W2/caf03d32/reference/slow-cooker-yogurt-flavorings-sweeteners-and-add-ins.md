**Natural Flavorings:**
- Vanilla (extract or bean)
- Cinnamon (warm, spicy)
- Fresh ginger (spicy, invigorating)
- Lemon (juice or zest, bright citrusy)
- Nutmeg (warm, aromatic)
- Cocoa powder or melted chocolate

**Fruit Options:**
- Berries, mango, peaches, dried cranberries, raisins, apricots

**Natural Sweeteners:**
- Honey (pure, drizzle)
- Maple syrup (rich, velvety)
- Coconut sugar (subtle, caramel-like)
- Date syrup (sweet, molasses-like)
- Monk fruit (150-200x sweeter than sugar, use sparingly)
- Stevia (powder, natural)

**Texture Add-ins:**
- Granola (store-bought or homemade)
- Nuts/seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia, flaxseeds)
- Cocoa nibs (chocolatey crunch)
- Dried fruit

Experiment with combinations to create perfect flavor profile.
