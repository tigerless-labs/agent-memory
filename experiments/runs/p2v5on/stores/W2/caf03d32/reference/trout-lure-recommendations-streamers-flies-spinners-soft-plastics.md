Dry flies: Elk Hair Caddis, Stimulator, Blue Wing Olive. Nymphs: Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail, Copper John. Streamers: Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, Black Stonefly. Spinners and spoons: Mepps Aglia, Rooster Tail, Acme Kastmaster. Soft plastics: Curly Tail Grub, PowerBait. Brands: Simms, Orvis, Umpqua, Rio Products, Mepps, Acme. For river trout: use 9-10 foot leader with 1-2X tippet, sink-tip line or weighted leader, cast upstream and across, vary retrieve to imitate fleeing baitfish.
