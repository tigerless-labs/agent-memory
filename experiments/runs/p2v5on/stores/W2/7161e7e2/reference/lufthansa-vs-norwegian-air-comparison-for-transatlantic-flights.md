Lufthansa: Full-service with complimentary meals, 31-32 inch seat pitch, adjustable headrests, wide entertainment selection, 1 free checked bag, Frankfurt hub, Miles & More program.

Norwegian Air: Budget carrier with buy-on-board food only, 29-30 inch seat pitch, no adjustable headrests, limited entertainment, extra baggage charges (20-30 USD), Oslo hub, financial stability concerns.

Takeaway: Lufthansa better for comfort and reliability; Norwegian Air cheapest but sacrifices amenities.
