Recommendations for musical theater shows based on interest in irreverent humor and catchy music:

**Irreverent comedies similar to The Book of Mormon:**
- Avenue Q: Irreverent musical about puppets in NYC dealing with adult themes and relationships
- Something Rotten!: Set in 1590s, about two brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare; hilarious and witty
- The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee: Quirky musical about misfits in a middle school spelling bee
- The Producers: Mel Brooks musical about scheming producers; silly and outrageous
- Urinetown: Satirical musical poking fun at capitalism and social hierarchy
- Be More Chill: Sci-fi comedy about high school popularity, with electronic-pop score
- Hamilton: Groundbreaking musical with diverse cast; hip-hop, jazz, blues, and R&B music

**Other standout shows:**
- Dear Evan Hansen: Heartwarming musical about social media, mental health, and human connection
- The Band's Visit: Poignant musical about Egyptian police band's visit to Israel; quiet and introspective
