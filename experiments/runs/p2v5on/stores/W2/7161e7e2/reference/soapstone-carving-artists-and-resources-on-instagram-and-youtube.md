**Instagram sculptors to follow:**
- @soaperstar (Rachel Brooks, USA) - whimsical and surreal soapstone
- @soapstonecarvings (Michael Maddy, USA) - detailed, realistic, and abstract pieces
- @stonecarverkaren (Karen Rossi, USA) - figurative, abstract, and symbolic works
- @brianrussellsculpture (Brian Russell, USA) - abstract, organic forms

**YouTube channels:**
- Soapstone Carving with Rachel (Rachel Brooks)
- The Stone Carver (Tom Wolfe) - stone and soapstone carving tutorials
- Stone Sculpture Tutorials - beginner-friendly content
- Carving Soapstone with Michael (Michael Maddy)

**Online platforms:**
- Soapstone Carving Academy (soapstonecarvingacademy.com) - dedicated courses
- Craftsy (craftsy.com) and Udemy (udemy.com) - soapstone carving courses
- Instructables - step-by-step soapstone tutorials
- Sculpture.org - Stone Carving 101 guide

**Communities:**
- Reddit r/soapstonecarving for discussions and shared techniques
