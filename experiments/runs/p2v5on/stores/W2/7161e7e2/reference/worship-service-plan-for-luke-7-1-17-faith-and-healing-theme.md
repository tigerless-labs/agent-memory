Service structure: Opening with 'Great Are You Lord' by All Sons & Daughters, call to worship and prayer, scripture reading Luke 7:1-17, sermon on faith and healing, response hymn 'O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing' by Charles Wesley, member testimony, 'Amazing Grace' by John Newton, healing prayer, closing with 'Healer' by Kari Jobe, benediction. Related passages: Matthew 8:10, Hebrews 11:1, Mark 9:23, John 14:12-14, James 2:14-26, Isaiah 53:5. Additional songs: 'I Believe in Miracles' (Jackson Browne), 'There is Power in the Blood' (Lewis E. Jones), 'Heal Me O Lord' (William Walsham How).
