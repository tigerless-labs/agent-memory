**Story:** Folk-opera retelling of the ancient Greek myth of Orpheus and Eurydice set in the underworld. Orpheus travels to Hadestown to rescue Eurydice; charms Hades and Persephone with his music on condition he doesn't look back until they reach the surface. Explores themes of love, power, freedom, and redemption with intertwined character stories.

**Music:** Blend of folk, blues, jazz, and Americana with ancient Greek influences. Written by Anaïs Mitchell (book, music, lyrics). Hauntingly beautiful with soulful vocals. Each character has distinct musical voice—Orpheus upbeat and optimistic, Eurydice melancholic and introspective, Fates eerie and foreboding, Hades and Persephone dark and sensual.

**Awards & Reception:**
- 2019 Tony Awards: 8 wins (Best Musical, Best Book, Best Original Score)
- 2019 Grammy Awards: 2 wins (Best Musical Theater Album)
- 2019 Drama Desk Awards: 4 wins

**Casting (Original Broadway):**
- Reeve Carney as Orpheus
- Eva Noblezada as Eurydice
- Patrick Page as Hades
- Amber Gray as Persephone

Original Broadway cast album released 2019. Premiered off-Broadway 2016, transferred to Broadway 2019. Has been performed in London's West End and touring United States.
