Old Testament: Elijah restored life of boy (1 Kings 17:17-24), Elisha healed Naaman of leprosy (2 Kings 5:1-14). New Testament: Jesus healed paralyzed man (Mark 2:1-12), blind man (John 9:1-7), woman with flow of blood (Mark 5:34). Jesus affirmed healing authority and emphasized faith: 'According to your faith let it be done to you' (Matthew 9:29). Early church continued healing practice: Peter healed beggar at temple gate (Acts 3:1-10), Paul healed invalid (Acts 9:32-35). Key theological concept: healing connected to salvation and redemption, part of Christ's atonement. Isaiah 53:5 'by his wounds we are healed' refers to physical and spiritual healing through death and resurrection. Healing available to all believers through faith in Jesus.
