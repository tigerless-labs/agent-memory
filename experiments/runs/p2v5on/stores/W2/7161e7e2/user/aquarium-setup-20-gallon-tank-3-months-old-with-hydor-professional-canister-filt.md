**Tank specs:**
- Size: 20 gallons
- Setup time: 3 months (cycling complete)
- Filter: Hydor Professional Canister Filter (chosen after researching Eheim and Fluval)

**Feeding routine:**
- Frequency: Twice daily
- Staple: Tetra pellet food (fish appreciate as regular meal)
- Primary supplement: Frozen brine shrimp (fish love this the most, go crazy for it)
- Secondary supplement: Frozen bloodworms (fish enjoy but are more finicky about these)
- Philosophy: Varying diet to ensure balanced nutrient intake, mimicking natural foraging

**Dietary philosophy:**
Intentionally rotating foods to prevent nutritional deficiencies and promote healthy growth, coloration, and vitality. Considers this approach reduces stress and disease susceptibility.
