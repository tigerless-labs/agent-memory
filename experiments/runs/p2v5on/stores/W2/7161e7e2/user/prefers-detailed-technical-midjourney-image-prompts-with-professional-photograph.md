Likes image prompts written in rich, descriptive language that includes:
- Specific camera equipment (body, lens, focal length)
- Technical settings (aperture, ISO, shutter speed)
- Detailed lighting descriptions
- Camera positioning and composition details
- Aspect ratio and quality parameters (--ar, --q, --v)

Enjoys creative/whimsical subject matter (e.g., a female Shiba Inu dressed as a grandma gangster playing poker in a casino). Prefers prompts written from the perspective of a professional photographer, illustrator, and animator.

Example subjects worked with: Viking warrior in Scandinavian landscape, 1970s New York cafe, German Shepherd running through river, Japanese ski resort, robot assisting elderly woman, Roman gladiator hunting in forest, Shiba Inu grandma gangster at poker table.
