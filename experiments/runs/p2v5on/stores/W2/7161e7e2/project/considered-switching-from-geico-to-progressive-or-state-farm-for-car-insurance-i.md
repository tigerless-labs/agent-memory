Had been with Geico for approximately 3 years. Explored switching to Progressive after hearing they offer better rates for Honda owners. Key interest areas: loan/lease gap coverage, discounts for advanced safety features (lane departure warning, blind spot detection), multi-car discounts, and usage-based safe driver programs (Snapshot). Also compared State Farm's offerings (Drive Safe & Save program, similar safety feature discounts). Interested in mentioning vehicle safety features when requesting quotes to maximize discount eligibility.
