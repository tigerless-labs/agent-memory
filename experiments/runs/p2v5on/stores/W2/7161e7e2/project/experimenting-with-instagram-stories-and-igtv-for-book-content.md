Plan to use Instagram Stories for: behind-the-scenes content (prep for #BookClubChat, notes, questions), post-chat thoughts, Q&A sessions, and creative process snippets. Plan to use IGTV for: in-depth book reviews, author interviews, discussion videos exploring themes/characters, bookish challenges. Focus on engagement, visual appeal, good lighting and sound quality.
