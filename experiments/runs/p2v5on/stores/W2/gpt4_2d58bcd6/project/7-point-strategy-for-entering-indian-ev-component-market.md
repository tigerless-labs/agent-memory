Market entry approach for high-voltage fuse sales in India. Key points: (1) Market research on EV industry, (2) Adapt to Indian standards/certifications, (3) Localized production or partnership for cost/delivery, (4) Competitive pricing strategy given price sensitivity, (5) Robust distribution network with automotive partners and e-commerce, (6) Targeted marketing via digital channels and industry events, (7) After-sales service network with local technician training. India differs from other markets: higher price sensitivity, India-specific regulatory standards required, less developed charging infrastructure, requires larger dispersed distribution network.
