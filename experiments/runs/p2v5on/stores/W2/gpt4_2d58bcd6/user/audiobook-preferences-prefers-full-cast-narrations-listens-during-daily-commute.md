Prefers full-cast narrations for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes; finds them more immersive and easier to track speakers. Also enjoys single-narrator audiobooks with distinctive tone and style. Believes quality narration is crucial—a skilled narrator can amplify emotional impact, enhance character connection, and make the listening experience significantly more engaging. Currently listening to audiobooks during daily commute.
