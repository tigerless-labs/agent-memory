Strava is a popular mobile app for tracking bike rides and hikes. Features include tracking routes, distance, speed, heart rate, and time. Also allows creating clubs with friends for group tracking and competition. User plans to use it to track outdoor activities and consider creating a club for 'The Spikers' team.
