Popular audiobook platforms recommended: Audible ($14.95/month for one credit), Libro.fm ($14.99/month for one credit, supports independent bookstores), Scribd ($8.99/month unlimited access), Hoopla Digital (free with library card), OverDrive (free through public libraries), Google Play Books, Audiobooks.com ($14.95/month for one credit), Hibooks ($12.99/month for one credit), Kobo Audiobooks, Audiobooksync (free summer program with two free audiobooks per week for 13 weeks)
