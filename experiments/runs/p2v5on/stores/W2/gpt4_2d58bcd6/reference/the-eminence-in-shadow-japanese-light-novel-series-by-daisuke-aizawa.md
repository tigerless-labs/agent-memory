**Series:** The Eminence in Shadow (Kage no Jitsuryokusha ni Narou)

**Author:** Daisuke Aizawa

**Type:** Japanese light novel with manga and anime adaptations

**Plot:** Protagonist Cid Kagenou dreams of being a mastermind controlling things from the shadows, inspired by novels and games. He creates a secret organization called 'Shadow Garden' and recruits childhood friends to help him.

**Themes:**
- Action, adventure, and comedy
- Metafictional elements with references to anime, manga, and video game tropes
- Fourth-wall breaking and humor
- Friendship and trust among team members

**Adaptations:** Manga series and anime adaptation in production/available
