**Song:** JONY - Аллея (Alley)

**Artist:** JONY

**Language:** Russian

**Theme:** Lost love, memory, longing, and impermanence

**Key Elements:**
- Alley imagery representing a place of memory and loss
- Nature imagery: comparison of lost love to a withered flower
- Chorus phrase: 'тебя нет со мной' (tebya net so mnoy) — 'you're not with me'
- Emphasis on inability to move on and emotional intensity
- Shifts between past memories and present pain
- Use of 'ты' (ty — 'you') for direct, intimate address

**Musical Style:** 
- Piano and subtle electronic elements
- Dreamy, ethereal quality
- Emotional vocal delivery
- Notable track in the Russian music scene

**Themes:** Heartbreak, nostalgia, power of memories to evoke emotions
