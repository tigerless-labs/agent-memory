**Ingredients:**
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs (parsley or sage) for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Roast squash halves at 400°F (200°C) for 45-50 min until tender
2. Sauté onion in olive oil until soft, add garlic
3. Add spices, cook 1 min, pour in broth
4. Add roasted squash flesh and puree with immersion blender
5. Add cream if desired, season to taste
6. Serve with fresh herbs

**Tips:** Use pre-cut squash to save time; add apple or kale for variation; try coconut cream or Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream.
