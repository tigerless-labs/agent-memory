Yogurt recommendations for making parfaits:

**Greek Yogurt** (15-20g protein per 6-oz serving)
- Fage Total Greek Yogurt
- Chobani Non-Fat Plain Greek Yogurt
- Siggi's Non-Fat Plain Greek Yogurt

**Icelandic Yogurt (Skyr)** (17-20g protein per 6-oz serving)
- Siggi's Icelandic-Style Yogurt
- Icelandic Provisions Yogurt

**Coconut Yogurt (Non-Dairy)** (10-15g protein per 6-oz serving)
- So Delicious Coconut Yogurt
- Kite Hill Coconut Yogurt
- Coconut Bliss Yogurt

**Selection Tips:**
- Choose plain, unflavored varieties for flexibility
- Always check ingredient list for quality
- Compare nutrition labels for protein and sugar content
- Greek and Icelandic yogurts offer higher protein content
