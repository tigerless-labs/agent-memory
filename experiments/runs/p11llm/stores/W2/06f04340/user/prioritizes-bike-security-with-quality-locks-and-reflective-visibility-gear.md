When commuting by bike, prioritizes the following:

**Security:**
- Invests in high-quality U-lock or chain lock with high-security rating (brands: Kryptonite, Abus, OnGuard)
- Locking strategy: Secures frame and wheels to sturdy, fixed objects (bike racks, posts)
- Uses secondary cable lock for accessories (wheels, seat)
- Interested in bike registration for theft recovery

**Visibility:**
- Wears reflective clothing and accessories (strips, arm/ankle bands)
- Uses front and rear lights with high lumens and rechargeable battery
- Applies reflective stickers or decals to bike
- Prioritizes well-lit routes with good visibility

**Other accessories of interest:**
- Fenders (to stay dry in wet conditions)
- Rack and panniers (for carrying gear/groceries)
- Portable pump or CO2 inflator
- Multi-tool for basic repairs
- Water bottle and cage
- Kickstand

This reflects a practical, safety-conscious approach to urban cycling.
