Booked Dec 15 2022. Hotel near Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku. ¥50,000 JPY exchanged Feb 1 2023. Restaurants: Tofuya Ukai (tofu, 2-14-3 Yoyogi), Kyubey Ginza (sushi, 3-2-14 Shinjuku), Toriki (tempura/soba/udon, 2-14-1 Yoyogi), Kappo Nakajima (omakase, 3-2-14 Shinjuku), Han no Daidokoro Bettei (kaiseki, 3-2-12 Shinjuku), Tsurutontan (noodles, 3-28-10 Shinjuku). Transport: Haneda faster (Monorail/Keikyu/Limousine ¥500-2000). Narita N'EX direct (¥3000-4000). Recommend Suica/Pasmo card.
