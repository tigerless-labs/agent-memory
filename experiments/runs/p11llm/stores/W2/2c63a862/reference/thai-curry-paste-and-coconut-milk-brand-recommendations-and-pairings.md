**Thai Red Curry Paste Brands:**
- Maesri: Popular Thai brand, deep rich flavor, moderate heat, not too sweet
- Thai Kitchen: Accessible US brand, slightly sweeter than Maesri, good balance of spices
- Pantai: More expensive Thai brand, complex flavor profile, deeper earthier tone

**Coconut Milk Brands:**
- Chaokoh: Popular Thai brand, full-fat, rich and creamy, high fat content, perfect for curries
- Native Forest: US organic brand, high-quality, full-fat, rich creamy texture, mild coconut flavor
- Aroy-D: Thai brand, full-fat, rich creamy texture, slightly sweeter than Chaokoh

**Recommended Pairings:**
- Maesri Red Curry Paste + Chaokoh Full-Fat Coconut Milk: Classic combination, rich flavorful curry, deep slightly spicy flavor
- Thai Kitchen Red Curry Paste + Native Forest Full-Fat Coconut Milk: Slightly sweeter curry, creamy texture, balanced flavor profile
- Pantai Red Curry Paste + Aroy-D Full-Fat Coconut Milk: Complex earthy curry, rich creamy texture, slightly sweeter flavor
