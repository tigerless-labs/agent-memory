Decided to use a hybrid approach for home security cameras:
- **Wired cameras**: At front and back doors for reliability, higher video quality, and security
- **Wireless cameras**: Inside the house for flexibility and easy installation

This approach balances security benefits (wired at entrances) with convenience (wireless interior flexibility). Camera considerations include positioning for clear footage, motion detection/alerts, night vision, weather resistance for outdoor cameras, and sufficient storage capacity.
