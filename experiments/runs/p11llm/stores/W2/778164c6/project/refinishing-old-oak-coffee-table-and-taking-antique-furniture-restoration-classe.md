Taking classes at a local workshop to improve antique furniture restoration skills. Currently working on refinishing an old oak coffee table. 

Project goals:
- Research and determine the original finish used on the table
- Choose an appropriate finish that stays true to the table's original look and era
- Document the table's style and era (considering Arts and Crafts, Victorian, or Mid-Century Modern possibilities)
- Study the table's construction and materials before restoration

Process being applied:
- Researching similar oak coffee tables online (eBay, Craigslist, antique marketplaces)
- Consulting with furniture restorers and experts for guidance
- Testing finishes on inconspicuous areas before full application
- Considering durability needs based on table's intended use
- Documenting before, during, and after photos

Current challenge: Determining the original finish type and choosing the best matching finish for restoration.
