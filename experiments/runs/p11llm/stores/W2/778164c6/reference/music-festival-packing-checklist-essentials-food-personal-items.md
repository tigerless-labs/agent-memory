**Essentials:**
- Tickets, wristbands, ID
- Refillable water bottle
- Sunscreen (SPF 30+), sun hat, sunglasses
- Comfortable shoes/sneakers
- Portable charger
- Cash + credit cards
- Small first-aid kit (band-aids, antiseptic wipes, pain relievers)

**Food & Snacks:**
- Nuts and seeds (almonds, cashews, trail mix)
- Dried fruits (apricots, cranberries, raisins, dates)
- Energy bars (Clif, Luna, RX Bars)
- Jerky (beef, turkey, chicken)
- Fresh fruits (apples, oranges, bananas)
- Granola or energy chews
- Sandwiches or wraps
- Crackers and peanut butter
- No-bake energy balls (oats, peanut butter, honey)
- Electrolyte drinks (coconut water, sports drinks)

**Personal items:**
- Small towel
- Travel-sized deodorant and wipes
- Medications
- Small umbrella or rain jacket
- Waterproof phone case and screen protector
- Earplugs
- Festival map
- Washable refill containers for snacks

**Tips:**
- Pack light - only essentials
- Check festival website for prohibited items
- Designate meeting spot with friends
- Stay hydrated throughout day
- Keep sandwiches in insulated lunch bag, wrapped tightly
- Use breathable containers for fruits
- Pack snacks in small portions
- Use cooler pack with ice packs to keep items fresh
