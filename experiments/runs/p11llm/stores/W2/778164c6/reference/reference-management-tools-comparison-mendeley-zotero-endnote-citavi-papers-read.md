Reference management tool options evaluated for thesis:

**Mendeley** — Free reference manager; popular in academia; organizes, annotates, and cites papers; large community database; strong web importer

**Zotero** — Free, open-source; great citation style integration; known for ease of use and flexibility

**EndNote** — Commercial; advanced features; automated formatting; collaboration tools

**Citavi** — Comprehensive; assists with knowledge management, task planning, and writing

**Papers** — Designed for Mac/iOS; clean interface; seamless app integration

**ReadCube** — Free; focuses on article discovery and organization; desktop app for Windows/Mac

**RefWorks** — Web-based; popular with students and researchers; known for ease of use

Key selection criteria: citation style support, integration with writing software (Word, LaTeX, Google Docs), collaboration features, ease of use, cost.
