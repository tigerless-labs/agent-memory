**Places to buy tickets:**
- Official ticketing: Ticketmaster, AXS, venue websites (no extra fees)
- Presales: Fan clubs, social media, email lists
- Resale sites: StubHub, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats (check seller reviews)

**Cost-saving strategies:**
- Early bird discounts and promotional offers (limited time)
- Ticket lotteries (win at face value or free)
- Bundle deals (tickets + merchandise + experiences)
- Upper-level seats (cheaper than floor/lower level)
- No service fee options at box office or retail outlets
- Local radio station giveaways and presale codes
- Cashback apps: Rakuten, TopCashback
- Student or military discounts
- Flexible on weeknight/off-peak timing for lower prices

**Tracking upcoming events:**
- Songkick: Track artists, get concert notifications
- Bandsintown: Similar to Songkick with app/website
- Local event calendars: Eventbrite, local newspapers
- Social media: Follow artists, venues, promoters
- Venue websites: Theaters, clubs, arenas
- Festival websites: Check lineups and ticket info
