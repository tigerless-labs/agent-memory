**Tomato and Spinach Curry**

Ingredients:
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 large tomatoes, diced
- 2 cups spinach leaves, chopped
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 tablespoon oil
- Water as needed

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in pan and add cumin seeds, let them splutter
2. Add chopped onion and sauté until golden brown
3. Add diced tomato and cook until soft and mushy
4. Add coriander powder, turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt; mix well
5. Add chopped spinach leaves and sauté 1 minute
6. Add water as needed to adjust consistency and cook 5 more minutes
7. Serve hot with rice or roti

Aromatic Indian-style curry using the three base ingredients plus spices.
