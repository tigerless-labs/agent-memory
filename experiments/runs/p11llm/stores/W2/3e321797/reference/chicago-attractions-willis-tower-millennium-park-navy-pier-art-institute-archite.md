Chicago top attractions:

Major Landmarks:
- Willis Tower (formerly Sears Tower): Skydeck on 103rd floor for panoramic city views
- Millennium Park: Lurie Garden, Cloud Gate sculpture (The Bean), free outdoor events
- Navy Pier: Lakefront attraction with boat cruises, amusement park, Chicago Shakespeare Theater
- Art Institute of Chicago: Major museum with Impressionist, Post-Impressionist, American art

Tours:
- Architecture River Cruise: Guided boat tour showcasing Chicago's architecture and history (recommended for first-time visitors)
- Chicago Trolley Company: 90-minute guided tour of downtown, covering Willis Tower, Millennium Park, Navy Pier. Senior discounts available (5 dollars off for 65 plus).
- Big Bus Chicago: Hop-on hop-off bus tour covering multiple neighborhoods
- Chicago's First Lady Cruises: 90-minute lakefront tour
- Free Tours by Foot: Pay-what-you-wish downtown walking tour

Neighborhoods:
- The Magnificent Mile (Michigan Avenue): Upscale shopping, dining, Tribune Tower, Water Tower Place
- Lincoln Park: Beautiful park, zoo, Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum
- Wicker Park/Bucktown: Boutique shopping, street art, vibrant atmosphere
- Chinatown: Authentic cuisine, Ping Tom Memorial Park
- Old Town: Historic streets, boutiques, restaurants, bars

Activities:
- Lakefront Trail: Beautiful views of Lake Michigan and city skyline
- Rooftop bars: Signature Room at 95th, Roof at The Wit Hotel
- Theater: Steppenwolf Theatre Company, Chicago Shakespeare Theater

Dining:
- The Polo Cafe: 24-hour diner with American comfort food
- Baderbräu Brewing Company: German-inspired brewery and restaurant
- Lou Mitchell's: Beloved breakfast and lunch spot, famous for waffles
- The Berghoff: Historic German restaurant with schnitzel and sauerbraten
- Portillo's and Lou Malnati's: Classic Chicago deep-dish pizza and hot dogs
- Starbucks: Across street from Hyatt Regency McCormick Place

Area around Hyatt Regency McCormick Place:
- South Loop neighborhood is safe with foot traffic from convention attendees
- Walking distance to dining and attractions
