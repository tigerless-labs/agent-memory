Initial Email Variation 1 (Subject: Increase Revenue from Missed Calls): Highlights lost business from missed calls. Introduces Caller Intercept as solution. CTA: demo call.

Initial Email Variation 2 (Subject: Don't Miss Out on Potential Revenue): Acknowledges busy schedule. Emphasizes custom automations. Softer approach to CTA.

Follow-up Variation 1 (Subject: Quick Reminder): Direct reminder of first email. Reiterates benefits. Strong CTA for call.

Follow-up Variation 2 (Subject: Don't Miss Out on the Benefits): Focuses on benefits and custom automations. Offers flexibility in scheduling (softer ask).
