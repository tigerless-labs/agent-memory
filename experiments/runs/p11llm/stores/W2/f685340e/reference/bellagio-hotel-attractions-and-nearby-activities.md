Bellagio Hotel Activities:

- Fountains of Bellagio: iconic water show with music, free. M-F: 3-8pm every 30 min, 8pm-12am every 15-20 min. Sat: 12pm-8pm every 30 min, 8pm-12am every 15-20 min. Sun: 11am-7pm every 30 min, 7pm-12am every 15-20 min
- Conservatory and Botanical Gardens: seasonal displays changed 5 times per year, open 24/7, free admission, wheelchair accessible
- Gallery of Fine Art: rotating art exhibits, 15-20 dollar admission
- Spa and Salon: massages, facials, treatments
- Pool: Mediterranean-inspired design with private cabanas

Nearby Attractions:
- The Cosmopolitan (short walk): bars, clubs, restaurants
- High Roller Observation Wheel (10 min walk): 550-foot Ferris wheel at LINQ Promenade
- Caesars Palace: pools, Forum Shops, restaurants
- Mirage Volcano: outside The Mirage hotel, fire effects display
- Venetian: gondola rides (5 min walk)
- Mirage Secret Garden and Dolphin Habitat: dolphins, white tigers, exotic animals
- Crystals: luxury shopping at CityCenter
- Strip walk: view various hotels and attractions along Las Vegas Boulevard
