## Models with coil-tap/coil-splitting features\n\n- **Gibson Les Paul Standard**: Features coil-tap function on tone controls, provides brighter, more agile sound alongside traditional warm humbucker tones\n- **Gibson Les Paul Traditional**: Features coil-splitting function, allows access to single-coil sound from humbuckers\n- **Gibson Les Paul Custom**: Often features coil-tap or coil-splitting depending on configuration\n\n## Benefits for open D tuning\n\n- Provides brighter tones to cut through the mix when needed\n- Maintains warm, sustain-rich tones the Les Paul is known for\n- Offers tonal variety and flexibility for experimenting with different sounds and textures\n- Helps bridge gap between electric and acoustic-like playing styles
