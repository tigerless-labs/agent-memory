**Key factors when choosing a portable power bank:**

**Capacity:** 5,000mAh to 20,000mAh+ (higher = more charges, but bulkier)

**Output:** Check voltage and current (amps) for device compatibility; multiple USB ports useful

**Input charging speed:** Fast charging capabilities (Quick Charge/QC, USB-C Power Delivery/PD)

**Size & weight:** Balance capacity with portability; compact designs preferred for travel/festivals

**Build quality:** Durable casing (aluminum or plastic), sturdy construction for travel durability

**Safety features:**
- Overcharge protection
- Short-circuit protection  
- Temperature control to prevent overheating

**Certifications:** UL (Underwriters Laboratories) or CE (Conformité Européene) ensure safety standards

**Brand reputation:** Research manufacturer reliability; check reviews and ask for recommendations

**Additional features:**
- LED flashlight/lantern
- Solar panels
- Wireless charging
- Carrying cases

**Tradeoff:** May need to compromise on size/weight for higher capacity, or vice versa for budget options
