Values true crime narratives that:
- Prioritize victims' experiences and dignity over graphic details
- Promote justice and prevention rather than sensationalization
- Don't glorify perpetrators or exploit victims' families
- Challenge systemic issues that enable violence
- Respect privacy and avoid re-traumatizing families

As a consumer, believes people should hold themselves accountable for how they engage with true crime content, being mindful of impact on real people and their families. Wants to support creators and outlets that practice ethical storytelling over exploitation.
