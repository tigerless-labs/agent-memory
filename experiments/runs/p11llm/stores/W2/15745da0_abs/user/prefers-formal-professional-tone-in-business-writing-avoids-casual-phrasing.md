When writing business communications and documentation, prefers formal, professional language. Avoids casual phrasing and colloquialisms. Examples of revisions made:
- 'automatically make it so' → 'conclusively establish'
- 'look for any reason' → 'search for justifications'
- 'careful' → 'exercise caution'
- 'make it so' avoided in favor of more formal constructions

Values precise, refined language that conveys authority and objectivity.
