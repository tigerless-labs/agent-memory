## Cooking Method

**Grill or outdoor grill:**
- Preheat to 400-450°F
- Oil grates to prevent sticking
- Cook 6-7 minutes per side (smooth side down first)
- Internal temperature: 165°F (use meat thermometer)
- Rest 5-10 minutes after cooking to redistribute juices

**Grill pan or cast-iron skillet:**
- Heat over medium-high heat
- Cook 6-7 minutes per side
- Internal temperature: 165°F (use meat thermometer)  
- Rest 5-10 minutes after cooking
