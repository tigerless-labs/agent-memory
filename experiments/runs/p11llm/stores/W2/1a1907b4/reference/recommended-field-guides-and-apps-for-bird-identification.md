Field Guides:
- Birds of Costa Rica by Richard Garrigues and Robert Dean: Comprehensive guide covering 850+ species with detailed descriptions, range maps, and illustrations. Recommended for Costa Rica birding.
- The Sibley Guide to Birds by David Allen Sibley: Covers Central American birds including Costa Rica. Excellent for detailed illustrations and identification tips.

Mobile Apps:
- Merlin Bird ID (free, iOS/Android): AI-powered identification tool by Cornell Lab of Ornithology
- iBird Pro (paid): Comprehensive North and Central American bird coverage
- Birds of Costa Rica (paid, Audubon Guides): 850+ species with descriptions, range maps, and audio recordings
- eBird Mobile (free): Record and submit sightings, contributes to citizen science

Spotting Scopes (budget-friendly options):
- Celestron Ultima 80 Angled (around 200): Excellent image quality and comfortable eyepiece
- Vortex Optics Solo 10x25 Monocular (around 150): Lightweight and portable alternative
- Bushnell Legend Ultra HD 20-60x80 (around 250): Wide magnification range and 80mm objective
- Nikon 8245 ACULON 20-60x80 (around 250): High-quality optics and durable build
