User wants to read *The Golem and the Jinni* by Helene Wecker, a standalone historical fantasy novel.

**Plot:**
Set in early 20th-century New York City. Chava is a golem (creature of clay) created to serve as a wife for a rabbi in Poland; orphaned when her master dies en route to America. Ahmad is a jinni (being of fire and air) released from a copper flask by a tinsmith in New York. They navigate their new lives, confront their identities and supernatural natures, and eventually form an unlikely friendship.

**Why this book:**
- Complex characters and themes: identity, community, morality, cultural heritage
- Lyrical, evocative writing with emotional depth and introspection
- Weaves Jewish and Middle Eastern mythology and folklore
- Finalist for National Book Award (2013)
- Won Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature (2014)
- New York Times Notable Book (2013)

**Tone:** Melancholic, introspective, character-driven rather than action-heavy
