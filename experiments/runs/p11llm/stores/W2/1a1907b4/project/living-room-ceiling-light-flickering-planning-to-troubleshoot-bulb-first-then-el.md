Living room ceiling light flickers occasionally. 

**Troubleshooting plan** (in order):
1. Try replacing the bulb (may fix if worn out or loose connection)
2. Check for loose connections in fixture
3. If persists, consult licensed electrician to rule out electrical issues (loose wiring, faulty ballast, circuit problems)

**Safety note**: Always turn off power to fixture before any repairs or replacements.
