**Camera Settings for Night Cityscapes**

Manual Mode with full control over exposure, aperture, ISO. Low ISO (100-400) to minimize noise. Wide aperture (f/2.8 or lower) for shallow depth of field. Slow shutter speed (15-30 seconds) to capture movement and light trails. White balance on Daylight or Auto to capture warm tones.

**Composition Tips**

Find high vantage points like rooftops, hills, or observation decks. Use leading lines (roads, sidewalks, building edges) to guide viewer's eye. Take advantage of symmetry from reflections in water or glass. Frame shots using architectural elements like archways or columns.

**Techniques**

Long exposure with remote shutter release or self-timer to minimize camera shake. Bracketing at different exposures and HDR processing for wider dynamic range. Panning to follow moving subjects with blurred background. Intentional camera movement during long exposure for creative effects. Shoot during blue hour (hour after sunset) for warm atmosphere with hint of daylight still visible.

**Process**

Scout location beforehand. Use tripod for stability during long exposures. Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility. Be patient for the right moment with optimal lighting and conditions.
