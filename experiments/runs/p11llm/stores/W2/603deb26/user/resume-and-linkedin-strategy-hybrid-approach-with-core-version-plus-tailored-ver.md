Application material strategy: Create a strong core version of resume and LinkedIn profile highlighting overall skills and experience. Then tailor both to specific job descriptions by using keywords from job posting, emphasizing relevant skills/experience, and adjusting summary or objective statement. This shows genuine interest and increases chances with ATS and hiring managers.
