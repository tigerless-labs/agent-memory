Main rituals: Diwali festival (Oct/Nov, 5 days), Laxmi Puja (special ceremony on Diwali with lamps, flowers, sweets, mantras), Havan (sacred fire ritual), Aarti (oil lamp waving), Vrat (fasting). Lighting lamps symbolizes light dispelling ignorance and invites goddess into home. Favorite flowers: lotus, marigold, rose, hibiscus, jasmine, chrysanthemum—represent beauty, purity, life, vitality. Fasting is devotional self-discipline for purification, spiritual awareness, and seeking prosperity.
