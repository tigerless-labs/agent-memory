**Ingredients:**\n- Fresh cilantro, chopped\n- Lime juice\n- Olive oil\n- Garlic, minced\n- Salt to taste\n\nMix together for a bright, refreshing dressing. Pairs well with quinoa, black beans, grilled chicken, roasted vegetables, and avocado crema.
