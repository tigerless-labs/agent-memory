Recommended YouTube channels for beginner-friendly yoga:
- **Yoga With Adriene**: Gentle, beginner-friendly Hatha and Vinyasa flows
- **Dylan Werner Yoga**: Perfect for beginners, focus on alignment and breathwork
- **Sarah Beth Yoga**: Gentle, slow-paced, Hatha and Restorative
- **Fightmaster Yoga** (Candace): Challenging yet accessible Vinyasa and Power yoga
- **Yoga Girl** (Rachel Brice): Inspiring and gentle Hatha and Vinyasa flows

Tips: Start with shorter classes (20-30 min), listen to body, pay attention to alignment cues, use props for modifications.
