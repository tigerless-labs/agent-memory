Key books on feminist art: 'The Feminist Art Movement: Images and Ideologies' by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard, 'Feminist Art and the Maternal' by Andrea Liss, 'WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution' edited by Cornelia Butler and Lisa Gabrielle Mark (accompanies 2007 MoCA Los Angeles exhibition). Important feminist art scholars to explore: Linda Nochlin (especially 'Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists?' 1971), Lucy Lippard, Griselda Pollock, Cornelia Butler. Online resources: The Feminist Art Base (Brooklyn Museum), Women's Art Library MAKE (London), The Guerrilla Girls collective. Key exhibitions: WACK! (2007-2008 at MoCA LA), 'The Feminist Avant-Garde of the 1970s' (2016-2017, Hamburger Kunsthalle), 'Women Artists and Post-War Abstraction' (2020, MoMA).
