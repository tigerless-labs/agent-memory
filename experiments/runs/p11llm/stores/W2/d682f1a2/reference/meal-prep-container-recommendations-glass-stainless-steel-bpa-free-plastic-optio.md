Glass containers (Pyrex, Anchor Hocking, OXO): non-toxic, eco-friendly, suitable for hot and cold foods. Stainless steel containers (S'well, Hydro Flask, Klean Kanteen): durable, rust-resistant, keeps food hot/cold for hours. BPA-free plastic (Tritan, Polypropylene - Rubbermaid, Glad, Ziploc): lightweight, affordable, durable. Features to prioritize: portion control markings or dividers, airtight seals for freshness, stackable design, microwave-safe, dishwasher-safe. Budget option: mason jars or reusable takeout containers. Choose based on diet type, portability needs, and available storage space.
