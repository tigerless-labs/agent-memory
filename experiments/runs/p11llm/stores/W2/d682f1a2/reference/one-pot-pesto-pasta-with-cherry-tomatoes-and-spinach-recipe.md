Quick and easy dinner recipe (25 minutes total: 10 min prep, 15 min cook).

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup whole-grain pasta
- 1/4 cup pesto sauce
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup fresh spinach leaves
- 1/2 cup grated mozzarella cheese (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Boil pasta according to package instructions
2. Add pesto, cherry tomatoes, and spinach to the same pot
3. Cook 2-3 minutes until spinach wilts
4. Drain pasta and return to pot, toss everything together
5. Season with salt, pepper, and olive oil
6. Top with mozzarella if desired

**Nutritional benefits:**
- Whole-grain pasta: fiber and complex carbs
- Pesto: healthy fats and antioxidants
- Cherry tomatoes: vitamin C and lycopene
- Spinach: iron and vitamins A and K

Can be customized with grilled chicken, shrimp, or tofu for protein.
