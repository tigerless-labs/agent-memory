**Preparation steps:**

1. **Scoring the fat layer**
   - Use sharp knife to make parallel cuts
   - ~1/4 inch deep, 1 inch apart on fat side
   - Helps marinade penetrate deeper
   - Don't cut too deep into the meat

2. **Trim excess fat**
   - Leave about 1/4 inch of fat on surface for moisture
   - Helps meat cook more evenly

3. **Tenderizing**
   - Use meat mallet or back of heavy knife
   - Gently pound thicker areas
   - Breaks down connective tissues
   - Alternatively: tenderizer tool or rolling pin

4. **Cut into pieces**
   - 1-2 inch cubes or slices
   - Helps marinade penetrate evenly
   - Reduces cooking time
   - Achieves more tender texture

5. **Dry brine (optional)**
   - Salt rub with kosher salt, brown sugar, black pepper
   - Let sit 30 min to 1 hour
   - Rinse off excess, pat dry

**Marinating:**
- Minimum 2 hours to overnight for optimal flavor
- Adjust marinating time based on piece size

**Cooking:**
- Hot grill or grill pan for nice char
- Cook to internal temp of 160°F (71°C)
- Rest 5-10 min before slicing to redistribute juices
