As a team lead, working on improving delegation and task management, specifically:\n\n- Setting realistic deadlines and expectations for team members\n- Providing necessary resources and support for task completion\n- Establishing clear task definitions with objectives, scope, and deadlines\n- Understanding team members' strengths/weaknesses to assign appropriate tasks\n- Regular check-ins and feedback to monitor progress\n- Empowering team members to take ownership\n- Using task tracking systems (Jira, Trello, Asana) for transparency\n\nRecognizes delegation as an ongoing process requiring communication, trust, and adaptability.
