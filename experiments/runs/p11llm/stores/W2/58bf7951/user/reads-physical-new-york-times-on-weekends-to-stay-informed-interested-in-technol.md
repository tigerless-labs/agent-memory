Started buying physical copies of The New York Times on weekends in early October 2022. Enjoys the habit but wanted a better system to stay informed during the week. Interested in staying up-to-date on technology, AI, and machine learning developments and trends.

Also reads The Washington Post occasionally.

Prefers to create a structured reading routine by:
- Setting aside specific time for reading
- Prioritizing sections of interest
- Skimming headlines to identify key stories
- Using a news aggregator (e.g., Feedly, Apple News)
- Taking notes or bookmarking interesting articles
- Diversifying news sources beyond a single publication
