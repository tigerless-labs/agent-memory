Recommended screen recording software options for recording gameplay footage on PC:
- OBS Studio (free, highly customizable)
- XSplit (popular among streamers and YouTubers)
- ShadowPlay (NVIDIA GPU owners)
- Bandicam (easy to use, free trial)
- Camtasia (paid, advanced features)

Recording settings: 1080p at 60fps is a good starting point
