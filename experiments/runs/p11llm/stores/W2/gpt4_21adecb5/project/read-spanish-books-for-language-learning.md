Decided to read Spanish books as part of Spanish language learning strategy. Books recommended for beginners:\n\n1. **La Sombra del Viento** by Carlos Ruiz Zafón - Gothic mystery set in post-civil war Barcelona\n2. **La Casa de los Espíritus** by Isabel Allende - Family saga exploring Chile's history; clear and accessible language\n3. **Cien años de soledad** by Gabriel García Márquez - Magical realist masterpiece; rich poetic language\n4. **El Aleph** by Jorge Luis Borges - Short story collection exploring philosophy and metaphysics\n5. **La Casa de Papel** by Juan Gómez-Jurado - Fast-paced thriller (novel version of Netflix series)\n\nLearning approach: Start with comfortable language level, use dictionary for unfamiliar words, read at own pace, consider audiobooks for listening practice, and take notes on new vocabulary and phrases.
