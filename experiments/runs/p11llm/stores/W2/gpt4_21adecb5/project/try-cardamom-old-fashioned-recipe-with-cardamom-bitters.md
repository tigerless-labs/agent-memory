Plan to experiment with a new Old Fashioned variation using cardamom bitters and complementary ingredients.

**Recipe:** 2 oz gin-based whiskey, 1/2 oz orange liqueur (Cointreau or Grand Marnier), 1/2 oz honey syrup, 2 dashes cardamom bitters. Combine in mixing glass, stir for 30 seconds, strain into rocks glass with large ice cube. Garnish with orange peel, dried fruit, or spiced cherry.

**Complementary ingredients:** Orange liqueur (cardamom-orange is a classic pairing), honey syrup adds subtle sweetness, dried fruits like cranberries or cherries, spiced cherries with cinnamon/cloves/allspice.
