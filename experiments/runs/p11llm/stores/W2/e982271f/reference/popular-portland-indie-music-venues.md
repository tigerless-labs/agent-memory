Venues in Portland known for hosting indie music shows:

- Mississippi Studios
- Doug Fir Lounge
- Wonder Ballroom
- Crystal Ballroom
- Holocene
- Aladdin Theater
- The Old Church
- The Liquor Store
- Alberta Street Pub
- Revolution Hall

These venues host both local and national indie artists.
