Prefers to take notes on laptop during conference sessions. Strategy: quote key phrases verbatim for memorable insights, paraphrase main ideas in own words for better retention. Uses abbreviations and shortcuts for efficiency. Focuses on comprehension and understanding relationships between ideas rather than transcribing every word. Reviews and refines notes during breaks and after sessions to clarify points and add context.
