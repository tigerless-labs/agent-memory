## Korean BBQ Tacos Recipe Ideas\nUse leftover Korean BBQ chicken breasts as filling.\n\n## Toppings:\n1. Kimchi Slaw - Spicy, sour, crunchy contrast\n2. Cilantro - Fresh, cuts through bold flavors\n3. Green Onions - Thinly sliced for punch and crunch\n4. Toasted Sesame Seeds - Nutty flavor and texture\n5. Pickled Ginger - Adds acidity and bright flavor\n6. Crushed Peanuts - Nutty flavor and crunch\n7. Crispy Kimchi Bits - Fry kimchi pieces until crispy\n\n## Sauces:\n1. **Gochujang Salsa** - Gochujang, diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro. Spicy and sweet.\n2. **Sriracha-Lime Crema** - Sriracha, lime juice, sour cream. Creamy and tangy kick.\n3. **Korean Chili Flake Aioli** - Korean chili flakes (gochugaru), garlic, lemon juice. Rich and creamy.\n\n## Assembly Tips:\n- Use leftover Korean BBQ sauce as taco sauce\n- Balance bold flavors with fresh, crunchy, tangy toppings\n- Don't be afraid to experiment and adjust flavors\n\n## Side Pairings:\n- Cilantro lime rice\n- Kimchi coleslaw\n- Quick pickled carrots\n- Grilled bok choy
