## Unique BBQ sauce recommendations:\n\n1. **Jamaican Jerk BBQ Sauce** - Allspice, thyme, scotch bonnet peppers, lime juice\n2. **Kung Pao BBQ Sauce** - Soy sauce, hoisin sauce, rice vinegar, garlic, Sichuan peppercorns. Sweet, spicy, numbing.\n3. **Indian-Style Tandoori BBQ Sauce** - Yogurt, garam masala, cumin, coriander, cayenne, lemon juice\n4. **Mango Habanero BBQ Sauce** - Mango sweetness, habanero heat, hints of cinnamon and allspice. Tropical and spicy. Pairs well with grilled asparagus.\n5. **Chimichurri BBQ Sauce** - Parsley, oregano, garlic, red pepper flakes, red wine vinegar, olive oil. Fresh and herby.\n6. **Sriracha Honey BBQ Sauce** - Sriracha, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar. Sweet and spicy.\n7. **Cajun BBQ Sauce** - Paprika, cayenne, thyme, oregano, garlic, Worcestershire sauce\n8. **Pineapple-Bourbon BBQ Sauce** - Pineapple juice, bourbon, brown sugar, Dijon mustard. Sweet, tangy, rich.\n9. **Chipotle Lime BBQ Sauce** - Chipotle peppers in adobo, lime juice, cumin. Smoky and zesty.\n10. **Gochujang BBQ Sauce** - Gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, rice vinegar. Sweet and savory Korean-inspired.\n\n## Tips:\n- Experiment with sweetness, heat, and tanginess levels\n- Use as starting point and create own variations
