YouTube channels: Maangchi, Manjula's Kitchen (Indian), Pai's Kitchen (Thai), Korean Food TV, Show Me the Curry. Cookbooks: Maangchi's Big Book of Korean Cooking; The Essentials of Indian Cooking by Madhur Jaffrey; Thai Street Food by David Thompson.
