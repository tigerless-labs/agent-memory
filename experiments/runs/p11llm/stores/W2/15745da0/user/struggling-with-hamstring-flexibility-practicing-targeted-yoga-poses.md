Has tight hamstrings and is working on improving flexibility through yoga practice. Recommended hamstring-targeting poses include: Standing Forward Fold (Uttanasana), Standing Hamstring Stretch (Prasarita Padottanasana), Warrior Pose (Virabhadrasana), Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana), Seated Hamstring Stretch (Akarna Dhanurasana), Janu Sirsasana (Head-to-Knee Pose), and Supine Hamstring Stretch (Supta Padangusthasana). Practice tips: start slow, hold poses for 3-5 breaths, don't bounce/force stretches, practice regularly, incorporate strength-building exercises (squats, lunges), stretch after workouts when muscles are warm, use props (blocks, straps, wall) for modifications.
