## Home Music Room & Recording Studio Project

### Acoustic Treatment
- Rectangular room (target length 1.5-2x width)
- Acoustic panels on walls, ceilings, corners
- Diffusers using bookshelves or irregular shapes
- Bass traps in corners
- Area rugs/underlayment on hard floors
- Ceiling height: 10-12 feet ideal
- Windows positioned away from listening area

### Recording Equipment Selected
- **Microphone: Rode NT1-A** condenser ($230) — chosen for versatility with both ukulele and saxophone
- Audio Interface: Focusrite Scarlett or PreSonus AudioBox series ($100-300)
- Studio Monitors: KRK RP5 G3 ($150) or Yamaha HS8 ($200)
- Recording Software: Audacity (free), GarageBand, or FL Studio ($99)

### Recording Mic Placement
- **Ukulele**: 6-12 inches from sound hole, slightly off-axis
- **Saxophone**: 6-12 inches from bell, slightly off-axis; may require higher gain settings
