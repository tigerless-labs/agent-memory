**Westmoreland State Park**
- Address: 145 Cliff Road, Montross, VA
- Rentals: kayak, canoe, paddleboard
- Waters: Potomac River
- Includes life jackets with rentals

**Northern Neck Kayaking & Outdoor Rentals**
- Address: 126 Jessie duPont Memorial Highway, Burgess, VA
- Rentals: kayak, canoe (and tours available)
- Waters: Great Wicomico River, Chesapeake Bay
- Includes life jackets with rentals

Both locations provide safety equipment and are suitable for day trips.
