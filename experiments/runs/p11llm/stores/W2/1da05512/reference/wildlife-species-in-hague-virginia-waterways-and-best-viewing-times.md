**Waterfowl & Birds**
- Bald eagles
- Ospreys
- Blue herons
- Egrets

**Mammals**
- River otters
- Muskrats
- Beavers
- Deer
- Foxes
- Raccoons

**Fish**
- Striped bass
- Largemouth bass
- Bluegill
- Catfish

**Best viewing times:** Early morning and late afternoon/early evening (when animals are most active and wind/boat traffic is lower)
