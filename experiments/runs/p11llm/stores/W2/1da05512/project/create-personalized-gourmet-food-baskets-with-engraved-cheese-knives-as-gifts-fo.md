Creating gourmet food baskets as gifts for friends with a budget of $30-40 per basket. Each basket includes:

**Personalized item:**
- Customized cheese knife with wooden handle and friend's name engraved

**Suggested basket contents:**
- Artisanal cheeses (truffle gouda, brie, goat cheese)
- Cured meats (prosciutto, salami, pepperoni)
- Crackers and artisanal bread
- Gourmet nuts (truffle-infused almonds, flavored pumpkin seeds)
- Gourmet chocolates and truffles
- Dried fruits (apricots, cranberries, dates)
- Specialty teas or gourmet coffee
- Artisanal jams or chutneys
- Honey or maple syrup
- Olives or antipasto

**Friend preferences considered:** Friends appreciate unique, handmade items. Wooden handle chosen for its warmth, character, and unique grain patterns. The knife will develop a beautiful patina over time.
