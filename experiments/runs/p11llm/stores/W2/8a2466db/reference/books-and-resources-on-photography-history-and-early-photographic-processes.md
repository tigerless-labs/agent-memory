Photography History (General): The History of Photography by Beaumont Newhall, The Camera: A History by Giles Hudson, Photography: A Very Short Introduction by Steve Edwards, The Oxford Handbook of the History of Photography edited by Michael Pritchard, The Photography Book by Ian Jeffrey. Artistic & Aesthetic Development: The Art of Photography by Bruce Bernard, The Photographer's Eye by John Szarkowski, Photography and the Art of Seeing by Freeman Patterson. Technical: Camera Maintenance and Repair by Thomas Tomosy, The Rolleiflex TLR: A Guide to the Classic Twin-Lens Reflex Camera by John Phillips. Online: Metropolitan Museum of Art Heilbrunn Timeline, Getty Museum Photography Collection, National Gallery of Art Photography Collection, Luminous Lint, Daguerreian Society, The Talbot Catalogue Raisonné.
