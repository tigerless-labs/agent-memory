**Quinoa Breakfast Bowl Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup leftover cooked quinoa
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 1/2 banana, sliced
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
- 1/4 cup chopped nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, or flaxseeds)
- Pinch of salt
- Splash of milk or yogurt (optional)

Instructions:
1. In a small bowl, mix together cooked quinoa, mixed berries, and sliced banana.
2. Add drizzle of honey or maple syrup if desired for sweetness.
3. Sprinkle chopped nuts or seeds on top for crunch and nutrition.
4. Add pinch of salt to bring out flavors.
5. Add splash of milk or yogurt if desired for creamier texture.
6. Serve immediately and enjoy.

Tips: Use any combination of favorite fruits, nuts, or seeds. Add cinnamon, vanilla powder, or cocoa powder for extra flavor. Mix in scrambled eggs or crumbled bacon for added protein. Use leftover quinoa from dinner. Try different types of milk or yogurt (almond milk, soy milk, coconut yogurt) for non-dairy options.

Great way to use leftover quinoa and create a quick, nutritious breakfast.
