Decided to make a complete Quinoa and Black Bean Bowl with the following components:

**Bowl Base:**
- Cooked quinoa
- Black beans

**Proteins:**
- Grilled chicken breast (with lime, cumin, paprika marinade)

**Vegetables:**
- Roasted sweet potatoes (with diced onions and minced garlic)
- Grilled bell peppers (diced and grilled with the chicken)

**Sauce:**
- Avocado sauce (lime, jalapeño, cilantro, red onion, garlic)

**Additional Toppings (as desired):**
- Shredded cheese
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges
- Sliced avocado
- Salsa
- Black olives
- Diced tomatoes

This is a protein-packed, healthy meal prep option that aligns with preference for under-30-minute meals and experimenting with new recipes.
