**Spirit Airlines Baggage Policy (as of January 2023):**

**Personal Item:** Free. Maximum size: 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Must fit under seat in front.

**Carry-On Bag:** Charged separately. Maximum size: 22 x 14 x 9 inches.
- Online during booking / up to 24 hours before: $35-$45
- Online check-in (up to 1 hour before): $45
- At airport: $55

**Checked Bag:**
- First bag, online during booking / up to 24 hours before: $30-$40
- Additional bags: $40-$50 each
- At airport: $55 per bag

**Checked Bag Restrictions:** Max 62 linear inches (L+W+H). Max weight: 40 lbs.
- 41-50 lbs: $30 extra
- 51-70 lbs: $75 extra  
- 71-100 lbs: $100 extra

**Special Items:** Musical instruments, surfboards, bicycles subject to additional fees and restrictions.

**Tips:** Pay online during booking for lowest fees. Measure bags before trip to avoid fees.
