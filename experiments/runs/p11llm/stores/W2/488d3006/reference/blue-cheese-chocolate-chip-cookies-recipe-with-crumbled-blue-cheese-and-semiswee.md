Recipe developed by testing and adjusting to balance savory blue cheese with sweet chocolate. Ingredients: 2.25c flour, 1tsp baking soda, 1tsp salt, 1c butter, 1c sugar, 0.5c crumbled blue cheese, 2 eggs, 2tsp vanilla, 2c chocolate chips. Bake at 375F for 12-15 min. Inspired by blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local shop.
