## Easy Camping Breakfast Recipes\n\n1. **Campfire Skillet Breakfast** — Scrambled eggs, sausage/bacon, bell peppers, onions, shredded cheese in cast-iron skillet\n2. **Trail Mix Oatmeal** — Instant oats with nuts, dried fruits, cinnamon; add hot water with milk or honey\n3. **Camping Breakfast Burrito** — Scrambled eggs, cheddar cheese, salsa, sausage/bacon, black beans, avocado in tortilla\n4. **French Press Coffee & Campfire Muffins** — Fresh coffee with homemade muffins\n5. **Breakfast Tacos** — Warm tortillas, scrambled eggs, chorizo, cheese, diced tomatoes, salsa\n6. **Instant Campfire Hash Browns** — Shredded potatoes, onions, bell peppers with oil, salt, pepper, paprika\n7. **Camping Quiche** — One-skillet quiche with eggs, milk, cheese, bacon/sausage/vegetables\n8. **Yogurt Parfait** — Greek yogurt, granola, berries, banana, mango in layers\n9. **Campfire Pancakes** — Pancakes from mix or scratch, topped with syrup, honey, fresh fruits\n10. **Egg and Cheese Wrap** — Scrambled eggs, shredded cheese, sausage/bacon/avocado in tortilla\n\n## Camping Cooking Tips\n- Pack light: choose non-perishable items or room-temperature storage items\n- Use fuel-efficient portable stove\n- Keep cooking gear simple and easy to clean\n- Prep ingredients at home to save campsite time\n- Follow food safety and hand-washing guidelines
