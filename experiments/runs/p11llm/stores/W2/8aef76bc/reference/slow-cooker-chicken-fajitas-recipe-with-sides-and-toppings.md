**Slow Cooker Chicken Fajitas**

Ingredients:
- Boneless, skinless chicken breast
- Sliced bell peppers
- Onions
- Fajita seasoning
- Diced tomatoes
- Whole-grain tortillas

Instructions:
- Cook on low for 6-8 hours
- Serve with warm tortillas, avocado, and sour cream

**Recommended Sides:**
- Mexican Street Corn (corn with mayo, cotija cheese, chili powder, lime)
- Cilantro Lime Rice
- Roasted Vegetables (peppers, zucchini, carrots)
- Black Beans (with onion, garlic, cumin)
- Avocado Salad

**Recommended Toppings:**
- Sliced avocado
- Sour cream or Greek yogurt
- Diced fresh tomatoes
- Shredded cheese (Monterey Jack, Cheddar, or Pepper Jack)
- Salsa
- Fresh cilantro
- Sliced radishes
- Sliced jalapeños for extra heat

**Tip:** User wanted this as their first recipe to try from the slow cooker
