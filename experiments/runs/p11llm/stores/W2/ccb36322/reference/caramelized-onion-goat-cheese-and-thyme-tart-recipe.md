Basic Tart Crust: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup cold butter, 1/4 cup ice-cold water, salt. Mix, refrigerate 30 min. Filling: 1/2 cup caramelized onions, 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese, 1 tbsp fresh thyme, salt and pepper. Bake at 375F for 35-40 minutes until crust is golden. For caramelized onions: use sweet onions (Vidalia/Maui), slice thin (1/8 inch), cook over low heat (275F) for 30+ minutes, stir every 10-15 minutes, add pinch of salt, watch carefully to avoid burning.
