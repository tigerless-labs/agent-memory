Recommended bands based on interest in The 1975, Arctic Monkeys, and The Neighbourhood:\n\n**High energy with crowd interaction:**\n- The Libertines (British indie rock, crowd-surfing, stage-diving)\n- Kings of Leon (southern-tinged indie rock)\n- The Wombats (British indie rock, witty banter, infectious energy)\n- The Xcerts (Scottish indie rock)\n- Catfish and the Bottlemen (Welsh indie rock, frontman Van McCann)\n\n**Atmospheric/moody style:**\n- The Black Keys (blues-rock)\n- The Strokes (garage rock, post-punk influences)\n- Interpol (post-punk revival, dark atmosphere)\n\n**Modern indie/alternative:**\n- The Vaccines (British indie rock)\n- Foals (math rock with danceable rhythms)\n- Glass Animals (psychedelic rock, atmospheric)\n- Cage the Elephant (blues-rock)\n\nAll known for high-energy live shows and audience engagement. Good for smaller venue exploration.
