Has a dog named Max who has sensitive skin. Max requires gentle, hypoallergenic shampoos with soothing ingredients.

**Recommended shampoo options for Max:**
- Oatmeal-based: Earthbath All-Natural Oatmeal & Aloe, Nature's Specialties Oatmeal Dog Shampoo
- Aloe vera-based: Nature's Gate, Earthbath Aloe Vera
- Sensitive skin formulas: Epi-Soothe (Virbac), Douxo, Royal Canin Gentle Skin
- Natural options: Coconut oil or shikakai shampoos

Max also needs flea and tick prevention medication.
