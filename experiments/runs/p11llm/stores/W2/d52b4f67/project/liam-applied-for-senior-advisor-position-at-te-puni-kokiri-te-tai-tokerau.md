Liam applied for a Senior Advisor role in Te Puni Kōkiri's (Ministry of Māori Development) Te Tai Tokerau Rohe, Regional Partnerships and Operations Puni, reporting to Manukura - Team Leader, Te Tai Tokerau.

**Role responsibilities:**
- Lead work with agencies, organisations and providers to improve outcomes for Māori
- Act as conduit for information into/out of Māori communities
- Manage and analyse local-level information on Māori views, aspirations, experiences
- Assist whānau, hapū, iwi, and Māori organisations in developing long-term economic/social development initiatives
- Manage local-level contractual arrangements and stakeholder relationships
- Provide mentoring to regional Advisors
- Inform investment decisions with colleagues

**Required qualifications:**
- Experience working with whānau, hapū, iwi, Māori organisations and communities
- Project management discipline
- Understanding of government/public sector processes and Treaty of Waitangi relevance
- Excellent communication skills including conversational te Reo Māori
- Relationship-building and diverse perspective valuation skills
- Clean full motor vehicle driver's licence

**Te Puni Kōkiri values:** excellence, valuing people and relationships, working collectively, creative and innovative approach, work/life balance support.
