Roll and Polar Inertia in Off-Road Design

Roll inertia: resistance to rotational motion around the longitudinal axis. Affects rollover resistance and traction during cornering.

Polar inertia: resistance to rotational motion around the vertical axis. Affects stability and control.

Track Conditions Trade-off:
- Smooth off-road tracks: Lower inertias better (increases agility and maneuverability for tight sections)
- Rough off-road tracks: Higher inertias better (maintains stability and control over uneven terrain)
- CG height: Lower always preferred for stability and rollover resistance

Finding Optimal Compromise:
Best approach requires actual on-track testing with the vehicle. Gather track data, create mathematical model, experiment with different inertia levels via suspension and body shape changes, test performance, iterate until achieving best balance for both smooth and rough sections.
