Kayla Itsines is a fitness trainer and influencer on Instagram (@kayla_itsines) who specializes in:\n- High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) with short intense bursts followed by brief rest periods\n- Strength training using compound exercises\n- Functional exercises like squats, lunges, and step-ups\n- Bodyweight exercises requiring no special equipment\n\nHer workouts are typically 20-30 minutes long with intense intervals lasting 30-60 seconds. Rest periods are short (15-30 seconds). Her programs include BBG (Bikini Body Guide) and BBG Stronger, and she provides modifications for different fitness levels.
