Preparing for a speed networking event planned for the week of 2023-05-25. These events are 5-minute conversations with rotating partners.

**Key conversation starters:**
- Icebreakers: What brings you here? What do you do and are passionate about? How did you get into your field?
- Industry insights: What are the biggest challenges? Latest trends? Most exciting innovations? Industry evolution?
- Career development: Best career advice received? Most significant lessons? Professional development approach? Next career step?
- Collaboration: What partnership/collaboration opportunities interest you? How to stay connected? Valuable connections you've made?
- Lighthearted: Outside interests? Recent books/podcasts? Historical dinner guest? Unique background?
- Closing: Best way to stay in touch? Further discussion topics? Recommendations for others to meet?

**Success tactics:**
- Listen actively and genuinely
- Share your own experiences and insights
- Ask follow-up questions to go deeper
- Goal is building connections, not collecting cards
- Be authentic and show interest in others' responses
