Decided to visit Belize as an eco-tourism destination. Focus: snorkeling and diving at the Belize Barrier Reef (largest barrier reef in Northern Hemisphere, UNESCO World Heritage Site). Interested in:\n\n- Diving at the Blue Hole and other reef sites\n- Staying at eco-lodges like Hamanasi Adventure and Dive Resort\n- Exploring protected areas like Hol Chan Marine Reserve and Bacalar Chico National Park\n- Supporting sustainable tour operators like ReefCI\n- Learning about local Garifuna and Maya cultures through community-based tourism\n\nKey operators/resources researched: ReefCI, Sea's the Day, Belize Dive Haven, Victoria House Resort, Chabil Mar Resort.
