Compact and reliable portable SSDs recommended:\n\n**Compact options:**\n- **Samsung T5**: Up to 2TB, only 51g\n- **WD My Passport SSD**: Up to 2TB, lightweight\n- **SanDisk Extreme Portable SSD**: Up to 1TB, rugged design\n\n**High-capacity options:**\n- **Seagate Backup Plus**: Up to 5TB\n- **G-Technology G-Drive Mobile SSD**: Up to 4TB\n- **LaCie Rugged SSD**: Up to 2TB, extreme durability\n\n**Key features to consider**: Capacity, speed (USB 3.2 Gen 2 or Thunderbolt 3), durability, built-in security/encryption, compatibility with laptop.
