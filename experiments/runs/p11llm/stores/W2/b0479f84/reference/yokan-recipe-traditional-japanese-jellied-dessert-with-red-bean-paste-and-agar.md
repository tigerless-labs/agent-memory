**Yokan (羊羹)**\n\nTraditional Japanese jellied dessert made from red bean paste, agar, and sugar.\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1 cup red bean paste (canned or homemade)\n- 1 cup granulated sugar\n- 2 cups water\n- 2 tablespoons agar powder\n- Food coloring (optional)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Prepare red bean paste (drain/rinse if canned)\n2. Combine sugar and water, heat over medium until sugar dissolves\n3. Mix agar powder with water to form paste, add to syrup, stir well\n4. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 10 minutes until agar dissolves\n5. Add red bean paste, stir well\n6. Add food coloring if desired\n7. Pour into rectangular or individual mold\n8. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until set\n9. Cut into cubes or slices to serve\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use high-quality agar powder specifically for yokan\n- Adjust sugar to taste (yokan can be quite sweet)\n- Experiment with flavors: green tea, sesame, yuzu\n\nYokan is a classic wagashi (Japanese sweet) and good introduction to making them at home.
