Apps recommended for practicing Japanese kanji reading:\n\n- **WaniKani** (Web, iOS, Android): Uses mnemonics, radicals, and spaced repetition\n- **Anki** (Web, iOS, Android): Customizable spaced repetition flashcard app\n- **Kanji Study** (iOS, Android): Large kanji database with stroke orders and example sentences\n- **Memrise** (Web, iOS, Android): Language learning with user-generated kanji content\n- **Joyo Kanji** (iOS, Android): Free app focusing on standard kanji\n- **KanjiQ** (iOS): Gamified kanji quizzes\n- **Learn Japanese with Yomiwa** (iOS, Android): Comprehensive app with kanji dictionary and flashcards\n- **Kanji Grid** (Android): Grid-based kanji practice\n- **Kanji Senpai** (iOS): Large database with flashcards\n- **NHK WORLD-JAPAN's Kanji Master** (Web): Free interactive lessons from Japan's public broadcaster
