Decided to start with 10-15 minutes of bodyweight exercises, 3 times per week as a way to get back into a routine after a difficult period. Chose bodyweight exercises because they can be done anywhere and require no equipment. Specific exercises mentioned: squats, push-ups, lunges, planks, and bicycle crunches. Prefers starting small and building gradually. Values proper form over high volume.
