## Finding a Good Jeweler\n\n1. Ask for referrals from friends, family, coworkers\n2. Check online reviews on Yelp, Google, Facebook\n3. Verify professional credentials (AGTA, JBT, NAJA membership)\n4. Confirm experience with ring resizing and your metal type\n5. Verify they have proper equipment for accurate work\n6. Schedule a consultation to assess expertise and communication\n7. Get detailed quote and timeline\n8. Check warranty and return policies\n\n## General Jewelry Care\n\n1. Store in cool, dry place away from sunlight and moisture\n2. Clean regularly with soft cloth and mild soap\n3. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, ammonia, chlorine\n4. Dry thoroughly after cleaning or wearing\n5. Avoid wearing jewelry in water (pools, hot tubs, showers)\n6. Use jewelry cleaning solutions specific to metal/stone type\n7. Polish regularly with soft cloth and appropriate compounds\n8. Inspect regularly for damage (loose stones, broken clasps)\n9. Consider professional maintenance and cleaning\n10. Keep away from children and pets\n\n## Peridot Jewelry Selection\n\nWhen shopping for peridot jewelry:\n- Look for vibrant green color with minimal inclusions\n- Choose appropriate setting (prong or bezel)\n- Select metal that complements skin tone (white gold, yellow gold, silver)\n- Verify certification (GIA or International Colored Gemstone Association)
