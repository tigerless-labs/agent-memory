## Note-taking and writing organization tools reviewed:\n\n1. **Scrivener** — Novel-writing and screenwriting focus; corkboard outlining, character/setting tools, manuscript mode (free trial available)\n2. **Evernote** — Popular note-taking with web clipper, tagging, organization; free and premium versions\n3. **Trello** — Visual project management with boards, lists, cards; free and premium versions\n4. **MindMeister** — Mind mapping and brainstorming; free and premium versions\n5. **Simplenote** — Minimalist, distraction-free note-taking with tagging and sync across devices\n6. **Bear** — Note-taking combining Simplenote simplicity with Evernote power; tagging and focus mode\n7. **Workflowy** — Hierarchical note-taking with lists, sublists, and tags\n\nKey features to consider: organization system, collaboration needs, device sync, distraction-free interface, export options.
