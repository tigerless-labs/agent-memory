Rewriting folk legend flash pieces from *Folk Legends from Tono* in accessible, conversational voice inspired by Roald Dahl.

## Requirements
- **Voice**: chatty, fun, energetic, easy to understand
- **Remove**: academic dryness, formal tone
- **Keep**: all details, facts, place names, character names, dates exactly as presented

## Successful approach (demonstrated)
- Use exclamations and contractions for conversational tone
- Create emotional beats and dramatic pacing
- Personify characters and build narrative momentum
- Break up dense exposition into simpler sentences
- Lead with the most vivid/surprising elements
- Use 'Poor [name]' or similar human touches

## Source material includes
- Stories from Mt. Hayachine, Taimagura Pond, Yamada Bay, Shimohei region
- Mix of hunters, fishermen, villagers encountering supernatural figures
- Historical context (Dutch ship Breskens landing 1643, arrests by Tokugawa Iemitsu)
- Folklore figures: giant monsters (norikoshi, dainyudo), tengu, ghostly monks
