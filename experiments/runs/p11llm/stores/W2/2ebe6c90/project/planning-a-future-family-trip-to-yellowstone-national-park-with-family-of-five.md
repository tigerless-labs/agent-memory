**Attractions:**
- Old Faithful geyser
- Grand Geyser, Castle Geyser, Daisy Geyser
- Mammoth Hot Springs
- Norris Geyser Basin
- Boiling River
- Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone River
- Wildlife viewing, especially bears (Lamar Valley, Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area)

**Accommodation:** Interested in Canyon Lodge & Cabins

**Activities:** Hiking trails in the Grand Canyon area, scenic drives, ranger-led programs for kids
