Committed to a home-based bodyweight exercise routine with no equipment. 

**Schedule:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday mornings

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes per session (starting small, plan to increase as endurance builds)

**Exercises:** Push-ups, squats, lunges, and stretching for flexibility

**Motivation:** Getting healthier and setting a good example for aging parents (mom 55, dad 58)

**Approach:** Starting low-key at home, focusing on consistency over intensity. Will track progress and gradually increase frequency/duration as comfortable.
