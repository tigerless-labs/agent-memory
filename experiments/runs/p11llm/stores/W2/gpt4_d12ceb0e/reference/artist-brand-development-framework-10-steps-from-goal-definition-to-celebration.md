Framework for artist career development and success:

1. **Define Your Goals** — What impact/legacy do you want? Write them down and reference regularly
2. **Research Target Audience** — Who are they? Their interests? What resonates?
3. **Build Your Brand** — Reflect who you are and what you stand for; identify what makes you unique
4. **Network** — Attend events, join communities, engage on social media
5. **Develop Skills** — Take classes, attend workshops, collaborate with other musicians
6. **Create Marketing Plan** — Social media, online advertising, email marketing, digital tactics
7. **Collaborate with Other Artists** — Reach new audiences and expand brand
8. **Stay Focused and Consistent** — Dedicated time each day/week; stay motivated
9. **Measure Progress** — Regularly assess; track successes and challenges
10. **Celebrate Wins** — Recognize progress to maintain motivation

For artist categorization and discovery optimization:
- Identify genre, vocal style, lyrical content, production style
- Use keywords in artist name, song titles, and platform tags
- Research Spotify playlists by genre/audience
- Reach out directly to playlist curators for submissions
