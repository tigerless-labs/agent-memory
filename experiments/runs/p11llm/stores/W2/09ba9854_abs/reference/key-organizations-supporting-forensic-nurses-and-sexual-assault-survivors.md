## Organizations providing forensic nursing support and victim advocacy

- **International Association of Forensic Nurses (IAFN)** — Professional organization offering continuing education, certification, and networking for forensic nurses
- **Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner (SANE) programs** — Specialized training in sexual assault care and forensic examination; includes pediatric/adolescent programs (P/ASANE)
- **National Sexual Violence Resource Center (NSVRC)** — Resources, training, and information on sexual assault prevention, response, and treatment
- **National Children's Alliance** — Membership organization supporting child advocacy centers across the US
- **Rape, Abuse, and Incest National Network (RAINN)** — Crisis intervention, education, and resources for survivors of sexual violence
- **Employee Assistance Programs (EAP)** — Offered by many hospitals and health facilities; provides confidential counseling to staff

These organizations provide training, support, and resources to help forensic nurses serve victims and manage the emotional toll of their work.
