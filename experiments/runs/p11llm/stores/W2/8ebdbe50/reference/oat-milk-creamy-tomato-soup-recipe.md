**Oat Milk Creamy Tomato Soup Recipe**

Ingredients:
* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 onion, diced
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 cups chopped fresh tomatoes (or 1 can of crushed tomatoes)
* 2 cups vegetable broth
* 1 cup oat milk
* 1 teaspoon dried basil
* 1 teaspoon dried oregano
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* Optional: 1/4 teaspoon paprika, 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil, add onion and garlic, cook 5 minutes
2. Add tomatoes, broth, basil, oregano, salt, pepper. Simmer 20-25 minutes
3. Blend soup until smooth
4. Add oat milk, stir over low heat until incorporated
5. Adjust seasoning; serve hot with optional paprika or vegan sour cream

Can be customized with roasted vegetables, spinach, or nutritional yeast.
