Planning a comprehensive sports-themed party with the following stations and themes:

- **Basketball** - hoop and competitive activity
- **Baseball** - throwing area and equipment
- **Horse racing** - photo booth with jockey hats and props
- **Formula 1** - racing simulator, Monaco-inspired cocktail bar (champagne, martinis, upscale cocktails), F1 car cutouts, checkered flag decor
- **Tennis** - mini court or 'hit the target' game, tennis ball toss
- **Premier League** - mini soccer field or soccer ball toss, penalty shootout game
- **Fantasy football draft** - mock draft station with software to engage guests

General decoration ideas include: sport-specific balloon arches, sport equipment on walls/ceiling, jersey garlands, trophy centerpieces, sports field table runners, athlete cutouts, scoreboard-themed snacks and drinks, sport-themed music playlists, trivia stations, and photo booths.

Party setup emphasizes dividing the venue into different sport stations with interactive games and activities for guests.
