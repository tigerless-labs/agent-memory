## Channels already watched:\n- Model Building TV\n- The Model Maker\n\n## Recommended YouTube channels:\n- Model Builder's Guide (MBG) — wide range of tutorials, basic to advanced\n- Scale Model Garage — car kits focus, weathering and detailing emphasis, host Dave\n- Weathering with Wes — dedicated to weathering techniques for cars, trucks, military vehicles\n- Model Car Guy — tutorials, reviews, builds with detailing and weathering focus\n\n## Online communities:\n- Scalemates — comprehensive community and forum, extensive tutorials and resources\n- Model Mayhem — large tutorial and gallery collection\n- Hyperscale — well-known forum with car kit section\n- The Modeling News — news, reviews, tutorials\n\n## Magazines:\n- Scale Auto Magazine (bi-monthly) — dedicated to scale car modeling\n- FineScale Modeler Magazine (monthly) — various modeling including cars, advanced techniques
