Interested in trying hairstyles that are easy to maintain and reduce stress on hair:
- Low ponytail (keeps hair away from face)
- Messy bun (effortless, keeps hair off neck)
- Braids (three-strand, fishtail, or Dutch braid - minimizes tangles)
- Bun with scrunchie (more gentle than elastic)
- Wet bun (gathers wet hair into low bun to reduce friction)

Avoids tight styles that place undue stress on scalp.
