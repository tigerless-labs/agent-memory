In crowded events and public transportation, user prefers subtle, non-confrontational approaches to personal space invasion over direct confrontation. Concerned about:
- Conflict escalation or physical confrontation
- Drawing attention or 'making a scene'
- Freezing up in the moment
- Authority not being available

Recognizes the need for boundaries but experiences tension between respecting own comfort and avoiding confrontation. Favors indirect solutions (moving away, physical barriers, leaving) over direct verbal confrontation.
