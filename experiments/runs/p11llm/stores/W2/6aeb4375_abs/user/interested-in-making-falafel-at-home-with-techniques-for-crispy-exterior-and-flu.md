Wants to make falafel as part of Middle Eastern dishes for dinner parties. Key techniques for success:
- Use high-quality dry chickpeas soaked overnight
- Pulse in food processor until coarsely chopped, not mushy
- Chill mixture for at least 30 minutes
- Shape into small irregular shapes for more surface area
- Double-fry method: lower temp 325°F first (2-3 min), then higher temp 375°F (2-3 min)
- Drain on paper towels to prevent sogginess
- Avoid over-mixing to keep fluffy
- Use egg, breadcrumbs, and/or oats as binders

Plans to serve with tahini sauce, hummus, lettuce, tomatoes, and pickles.
