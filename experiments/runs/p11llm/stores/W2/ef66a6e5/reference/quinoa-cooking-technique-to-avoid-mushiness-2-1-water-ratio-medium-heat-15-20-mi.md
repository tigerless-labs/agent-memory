## How to Cook Quinoa Perfectly

**Key tips to avoid mushy quinoa:**

1. **Rinse thoroughly** - Use fine-mesh strainer, rinse under cold water for ~30 seconds to remove saponin (bitter coating)
2. **Water ratio** - Use 2:1 ratio of water to quinoa (1 cup quinoa = 2 cups water)
3. **Heat level** - Cook on medium heat, not high, to ensure even cooking
4. **Don't overcook** - Quinoa is done when water is absorbed and grains are tender but still slightly crunchy in center (~15-20 minutes)
5. **Fluff with fork** - After cooking, remove from heat and fluff to separate grains and cool down
6. **Use heavy-bottomed pot** - Helps distribute heat evenly

**Optional:** Some people soak quinoa for 30 minutes to 1 hour before cooking (drain and rinse again after soaking), but it's not necessary.
