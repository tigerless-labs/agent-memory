Decided to grow a Bonanza Peach tree in a container on a small balcony. Specific variety chosen: Bonanza Peach (dwarf variety, 5-6 feet tall, produces full-sized freestone peaches).

**Container & soil requirements:**
- Container: at least 15 gallons with good drainage
- Potting mix: well-draining (contains perlite, vermiculite, or coarse sand); can use equal parts potting soil, perlite, and compost
- Repot every few years to refresh growing medium

**Pruning (late winter while dormant):**
- Remove dead/diseased branches and crossing branches
- Thin center for open vase shape to improve air circulation
- Remove water sprouts
- Prune back 1/3 of last year's growth to promote fruiting
- Avoid heavy pruning that reduces fruit production

**Care requirements:**
- Full sun: at least 6 hours daily
- Frequent watering and fertilizing
- Use slow-release fertilizer 1-2 times per growing season
- Regular monitoring for pests/diseases
- Clean up debris and fallen fruit

**Pest & disease prevention:**
- Variety is disease-resistant
- Organic products available (neem oil, pyrethrin, sulfur)
- Regular pruning improves airflow to prevent fungal infections
