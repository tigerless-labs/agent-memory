Plan to make a large batch of chicken noodle soup this weekend to prep for the week ahead. Will use boneless chicken breast/thighs, variety of vegetables (carrots, celery, onions), aromatics (garlic, ginger, bay leaves), and noodles (considering egg noodles, rice noodles, or udon). Will portion into meal prep containers and either refrigerate for 5-7 days or freeze for up to 3-4 months.
