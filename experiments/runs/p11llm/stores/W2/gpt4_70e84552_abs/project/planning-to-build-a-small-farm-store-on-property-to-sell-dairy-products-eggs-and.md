Considering establishing a dedicated farm store on the property.

**Products to stock:**
- Dairy products (milk, cheese, yogurt, butter, ice cream) — from future dairy cow
- Eggs (various sizes and types: organic, free-range)
- Fresh seasonal produce
- Baked goods made from farm products
- Value-added products (jams, honey, sauces)
- Local partner products (honey, maple syrup, crafts from other farmers)

**Store design considerations:**
- Location: easily accessible, visible, convenient parking
- Layout: U-shaped flow, clear signage, product categorization
- Display: attractive cases showcasing products appealingly
- Lighting: natural light supplemented with warm, inviting fixtures
- Size: determined by product volume and expected customer flow

**Operations plan:**
- Point-of-sale system for efficient transactions and sales insights
- Inventory management system for tracking, restocking, rotation
- Knowledgeable staff for customer service
- Health and safety standards compliance with regular sanitation

**Additional revenue ideas:**
- Host events (farm tours, cheese-making classes, tastings)
- Loyalty program for repeat customers
- Online presence (website and social media)
- Sample offerings to encourage product trial

Status: Idea phase, planning concurrent with dairy cow acquisition.
