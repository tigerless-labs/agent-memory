**Goal:** Establish consistent wake-up time and reduce snooze button reliance.

**Approach:**
- Use the 10-minute rule: Give self 10 minutes to gradually wake up instead of getting out of bed immediately
- Set alarm for same time daily, including weekends
- Create engaging morning routine with meditation (15-20 min) and music to motivate getting out of bed
- Establish consistent bedtime routine for better sleep quality

**Progress:**
- Successfully integrated 15-minute morning meditation (confirmed effective on 2023-03-31)
- Chooses Upbeat Classics playlist (especially 'Walking on Sunshine') for morning energy
- Breakfast: oatmeal with fruit and nuts provides good sustained energy
- Morning snacks: almonds and pumpkin seeds

**Next steps:** Establish consistent bedtime routine (wind down 30-60 min before bed, avoid screens, keep bedroom cool/dark/quiet)
