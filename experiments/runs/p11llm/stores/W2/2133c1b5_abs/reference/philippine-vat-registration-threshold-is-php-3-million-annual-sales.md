In the Philippines, businesses whose annual sales or gross receipts exceed PHP 3 million are required to register for VAT. VAT-registered businesses must charge VAT (12%) on their sales and include it in their invoices. Businesses with annual sales below PHP 3 million are not required to be VAT-registered and do not need to charge VAT.
