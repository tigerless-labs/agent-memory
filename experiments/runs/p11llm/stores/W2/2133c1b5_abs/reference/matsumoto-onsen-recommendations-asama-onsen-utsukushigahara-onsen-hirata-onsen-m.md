Recommended onsen (hot springs) near Matsumoto for November 2023 trip:

- **Asama Onsen** - Popular resort area with public and private baths, ~30 min from Matsumoto City by bus/taxi
- **Utsukushigahara Onsen** - Scenic resort with Japanese Alps views, ~45 min from Matsumoto City by bus/taxi
- **Hirata Onsen** - Historic facility with traditional atmosphere, indoor and outdoor baths, ~20 min from Matsumoto City by bus/taxi
- **Matsumoto Onsen Hotel** - Located in heart of Matsumoto City with public onsen facility and mountain views

Tips: Many onsen have specific rules (wash before entering, no tattoos policy). Some offer private baths for rent. Check ahead for hours, prices, and specific requirements.
