## Restaurants\n- **Kanemasa** (Chinatown) - highly recommended for kanimeshi (crab rice bowl), 30+ years established, popular with locals\n- **Tsukiji Itadori Uogashi Nihon-Ichi** - near Yokohama Fish Market, recommended for namashirasu (raw sardine sushi), fresh seasonal ingredients, gets busy\n- **Yokohama Central Market** - casual option for grilled squid and other fresh seafood at reasonable prices\n\n## Attractions\n- Minato Mirai 21 - futuristic district with harbor views, Landmark Tower observation deck\n- Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse - historic complex with shopping/dining\n- Chinatown - one of world's largest\n- Yamashita Park - waterfront with harbor views\n- Sankeien Garden - beautiful in autumn foliage season\n- Cup Noodles Museum\n\n## Events (Oct-Dec 2023)\n- Yokohama Oktoberfest (mid-October to early November) - German-style beer festival\n- Yokohama Autumn Leaves Festival (late November)\n- Minato Mirai 21 autumn illuminations (late Oct-mid Dec) - features Maple Leaf Tunnel, Harvest Moon Garden, Cosmo World, waterfront lights\n\nUser planning to visit Minato Mirai 21 and Red Brick Warehouse, try local seafood specialties, enjoy harbor views and autumn foliage.
