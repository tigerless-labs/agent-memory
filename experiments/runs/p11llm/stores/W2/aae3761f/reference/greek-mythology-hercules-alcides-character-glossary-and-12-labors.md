Hercules/Alcides: The greatest of all demigods, known for strength and considered somewhat foolish by the Greeks. Son of Zeus and Alcmene. After killing his wife Megara and children in a fit of madness, he was assigned 12 Labors by King Eurystheus to atone for his guilt.

Jason: Seeker of the golden fleece, protagonist of the Argonaut myth. Wants to be with Medea. Later portrayed as the villain.

Medea: Tricks the daughters of Pelias into slicing him up. Kills her own children. Later uses an acidic love potion against Hercules through Deianira.

Eurystheus: The king who assigned Hercules the 12 Labors designed by Hera to kill, humiliate, and exhaust him.

Deianira: Hercules' wife who mistakenly sends him an acidic poison thinking it is a love potion, causing him immense pain.

Nessus: A centaur who attacks Deianira; killed by Hercules with a poison arrow. Before dying, gives Deianira the poisoned 'love potion'.

Theseus: Cousin of Hercules who stops him from killing himself after his fit of madness.

Hera: Queen of the gods, jealous of Alcmene and Hercules. Designs the 12 Labors with intent to kill, humiliate, and exhaust Hercules.

Alcmene: Mother of Hercules; target of Hera's jealousy.

Iphicles: Hercules' half-brother, son of Amphitryon and Alcmene.

Amphitryon: General in Greek mythology; Alcmene's husband and father of Iphicles.
