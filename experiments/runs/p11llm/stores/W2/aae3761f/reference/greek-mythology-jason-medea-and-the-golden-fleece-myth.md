Jason and the Argonauts - The Golden Fleece:

Jason seeks the Golden Fleece. Goes to Pelias. Medea tricks the daughters of Pelias into slicing him up.

Medea: A powerful figure in the myth who helps Jason obtain the fleece. Wants to be with Jason but becomes consumed by jealousy and revenge. Eventually kills her own children as an act of vengeance when Jason seeks to leave her for another woman.

The plot explores themes of love, betrayal, and revenge in Greek mythology.
