Located on Topsail Island, NC (sound side) with views of marshes and Intracoastal Waterway. Known for excellent seafood platters. Large covered outdoor deck. Good lunch stop when driving from Outer Banks to coastal Georgia destinations (about 2 hours from Outer Banks, then 4-5 more hours to Tybee).
