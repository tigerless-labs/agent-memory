8-slide pitch deck structure created for Nero:

**Slide 1: Cover slide**
- Brand name: Nero
- Tagline: 'Uncomplicated skincare for the modern man'

**Slide 2: Executive Summary**
- New skincare brand for men who want to look and feel their best without time-consuming processes
- Uncomplicated, specific products that enhance male self-esteem
- Seeking 500K funding for product development, launch, and brand building
- Year 1 projections: 1M revenue, 500K net profit

**Slide 3: Problem and Solution**
- Problem: Men struggle with skincare due to lack of time or knowledge
- Solution: Nero offers uncomplicated, easy-to-use products formulated for men's skin
- Market: Global men's grooming projected to reach 166 billion by 2022

**Slide 4: Product and differentiation**
- Product line: cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen
- All easy to use and effective
- Made with natural ingredients
- Differentiated by male-specific formulation and uncomplicated use

**Slide 5: Target Market**
- Men 25-45 who are appearance-conscious
- Want to look and feel their best
- Don't have time or knowledge for complex routines
- Personas: John, David, Michael

**Slide 6: Marketing and Sales**
- Strategies: social media, influencer marketing, events
- Retail partnerships for in-store availability
- Year 1 projected sales: 1M, net profit 500K

**Slide 7: Financial Projections**
- Revenue, gross margin, net income projections
- Breakdown of costs and expenses

**Slide 8: Team**
- Founders: John Doe (business/marketing), Jane Smith (skincare formulation)
- Sales manager, marketing manager
- Board advisors with skincare, retail, finance experience
