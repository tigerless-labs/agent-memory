Methods to block out noise and create a quiet sleep environment:

Noise-blocking: Earplugs (disposable or custom-made), earmuffs, soundproofing curtains, acoustic panels, door sweeps or door seals (for gap between door and floor), window inserts for old/thin windows.

Creating quiet environment: Gentle quiet fan for background noise, area rugs on bedroom floor to reduce echo, sound-absorbing materials (acoustic foam, fabric, plants), sleep mask for blocking light, removing stimulating items from bedroom (TVs, computers, exercise equipment).

Other tips: Establish quiet time in household during sleep hours, communicate sleep needs with household members, experiment with different solutions to find what works best.

A combination of solutions may be needed to achieve the desired quiet sleep environment.
