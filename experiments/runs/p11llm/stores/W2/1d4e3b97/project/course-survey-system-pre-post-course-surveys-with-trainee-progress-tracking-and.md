## Functional Requirements
Trainees can:
- Answer survey once at beginning of course
- Answer survey once at end of course
- View their own progress (survey results comparison)
- View whole class's survey results

## Generated User Stories
1. **Trainee**: Access survey at course beginning
2. **Trainee**: Access survey at course end
3. **Trainee**: See personal progress over course
4. **Trainee**: Compare end-of-course vs initial survey results
5. **Trainee**: View entire class's end-of-course survey results
6. **Instructor**: Collect survey responses at beginning and end
7. **Instructor**: View individual trainee survey results
8. **Instructor**: View class-wide survey results to evaluate training effectiveness

## Context
This was an exercise in converting functional requirements into user stories, covering both trainee and instructor perspectives.
