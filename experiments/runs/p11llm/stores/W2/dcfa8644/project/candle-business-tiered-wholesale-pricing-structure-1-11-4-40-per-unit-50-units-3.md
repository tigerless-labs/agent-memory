Decided 2023-02-03 to use simple tiered pricing for wholesale customers.

Cost breakdown per candle:
- Material: 5.00
- Labor: 3.00  
- Overhead: 2.63
- Total cost: 10.63

Tiered pricing tiers:
- 1-11 candles: 4.40 each
- 12-24 candles: 4.00 each (10% discount)
- 25-49 candles: 3.60 each (18% discount)
- 50+ candles: 3.20 each (27% discount)

Strategy: Encourage bulk orders while maintaining healthy profit margins.
