Discussed allocating dedicated weekly time for administrative tasks:

Bookkeeping/Accounting: 1-2 hours per week (recording income/expenses, invoices, reconciliation)

Marketing/Promotion: 2-4 hours per week (social media, email newsletters, content creation)

Other Administrative: 1-2 hours per week (order fulfillment, customer service, supply chain, business planning)

Total target: 5-10 hours per week, depending on business size and needs. Schedule these tasks consistently into weekly routine.
