Attended cousin's wedding which was a beautiful blend of traditional Indian customs and modern elements. Particularly enjoyed:
- Vibrant decorations and colors throughout the wedding
- Bride and groom in stunning traditional attire
- Traditional music and dancing
- Traditional wedding ceremony with priest performing rituals and chants
- Wide variety of delicious Indian dishes with 5-6 different types of sweets

Met distant relatives from Gujarat at the wedding, who told about the family's ancestral village. This sparked interest in connecting with roots and planning a trip to Gujarat.
