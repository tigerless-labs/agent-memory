## Basic Homemade Fajita Seasoning Blend

- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust to taste for heat level)
- Salt and pepper to taste

Mix ingredients together and store in airtight container.

## Notes
- User has successfully used chili powder + cumin alone as a fajita seasoning substitute in the past
- User dislikes smoky flavors, so should use regular paprika (not smoked paprika)
- Can substitute paprika with: coriander, oregano, Mexican oregano, or dried cilantro if preferred
- Optional additions for depth: small amounts of ground cinnamon or cloves
