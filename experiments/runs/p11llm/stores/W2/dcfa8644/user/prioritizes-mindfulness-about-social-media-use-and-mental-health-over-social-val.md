Values a balanced, intentional relationship with technology and social media. Recognizes that excessive screen time can negatively affect mental health and self-esteem. Believes individuals should take personal responsibility for limiting social media exposure, setting healthy boundaries, and prioritizing self-care. Concerned about losing touch with reality and becoming overly dependent on fleeting validation from likes and followers. Advocates for digital detoxes, mindfulness practices, and prioritizing real-life connections and experiences. Supports the idea that mental health and well-being should come before the pursuit of social validation.
