Interested in understanding how policy reforms are initiated and the various actors involved (political leaders, government agencies, interest groups, public opinion, international institutions). Examined examples like firearm regulation movement (Moms Demand Action, Everytown for Gun Safety, Brady Campaign) and #MeToo as examples of interest group and social media influence on policy. Skeptical that social media activism alone ('all talk and no action') can drive meaningful policy change without being combined with traditional forms of activism (lobbying, public demonstrations, direct action) and backed by thorough research and well-informed policy proposals.
