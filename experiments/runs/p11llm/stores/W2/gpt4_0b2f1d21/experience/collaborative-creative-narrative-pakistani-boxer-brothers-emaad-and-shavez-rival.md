## Story Arc

**Emaad's early career:**
- Defeated arch-nemesis Mustafa after Mustafa escaped from a 2-day prison sentence
- Initially retired from boxing after defeating Mustafa

**Family crisis & comeback:**
- Brother Shavez was in a car accident while planning an Eid surprise
- Shavez survived; Emaad continued training after initially retiring
- Took on CrustiChezz (a Romanian fighter) as a protégé

**Second career phase:**
- Emaad continued boxing despite reaching age 46
- Began losing matches due to age, decided to retire
- Meanwhile, Shavez rose to prominence and won 7 Boxing World Cup trophies for Pakistan

**The climax:**
- Eighth Boxing World Cup final: Emaad faced his brother Shavez in the ring
- Emaad defeated Shavez in 'the fight of the century'
- Immediately after victory, Mustafa reappeared and shot Shavez
- Shavez miraculously survived despite having no heartbeat; Emaad performed CPR
- Emaad pursued and defeated Mustafa, bringing him to justice for his brother

**Themes:** Family bonds, redemption arcs, revenge cycles, dramatic comebacks, sibling relationships
