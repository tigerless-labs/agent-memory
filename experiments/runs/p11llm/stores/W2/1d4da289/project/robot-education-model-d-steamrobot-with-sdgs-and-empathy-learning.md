Educational model for non-majors using Convergence D-SteamRobot (DSR) that blends humanities and engineering. Uses books to build empathy for real-world problems, hands-on prototyping of solutions aligned with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and simple machine mechanisms. Constructivist learning environment developing computational thinking, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Students understand technology's role in sustainability and learn to contribute to global challenges through robotics work.
