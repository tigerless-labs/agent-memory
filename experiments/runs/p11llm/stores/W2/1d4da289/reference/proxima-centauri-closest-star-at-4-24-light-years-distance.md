**Proxima Centauri** is the closest star to our Solar System.

Distance: 4.24 light years away.

Travel time: NASA's Parker Solar Probe (fastest spacecraft) would take over 6,000 years to reach it.

Current technology insufficient for interstellar travel within human lifetime.
