Researching online Master of Science programs. Key concerns:
- Faculty qualifications and how to verify them
- Whether faculty support matches on-campus programs
- Ways to connect with faculty outside virtual office hours
- Workload and time commitment while managing other responsibilities

Key info gathered:
- Online Master's typically requires 15-20 hours/week (full-time)
- Evaluate faculty via: university website profiles, accreditations, rankings (US News), current/former student feedback, credentials/certifications
- Communication channels: discussion boards, email, group projects, chat platforms (Slack/Zoom/WebEx), social media
- Online programs offer flexibility to balance with work/family commitments
- Faculty provide same support as on-campus: feedback, grading, virtual office hours, one-to-one support

Status: Actively researching, hesitant about enrollment due to support/time concerns.
