**Ratched**
- Release: September 18, 2020 (Netflix)
- Episodes: 8, ~45-60 min each
- Psychological thriller prequel to *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest*, starring Sarah Paulson

**The Flight Attendant**
- Release: November 26, 2020 (HBO Max)
- Episodes: 8, ~45-60 min each  
- Dark comedy-thriller starring Kaley Cuoco; follows flight attendant Cassie Bowden who wakes in hotel room with no memory and a dead body

**The Social Dilemma**
- Release: September 9, 2020 (Netflix)
- Episodes: 7, ~45-60 min each; total ~5.5 hours
- Documentary series on social media addiction, manipulation, and influence; features tech industry insiders

**The Last Dance**
- Release: April 19, 2020 (Netflix/ESPN)
- Episodes: 10, ~45-60 min each; total ~9.5 hours
- Documentary on Michael Jordan's career and 1997-1998 Chicago Bulls season
