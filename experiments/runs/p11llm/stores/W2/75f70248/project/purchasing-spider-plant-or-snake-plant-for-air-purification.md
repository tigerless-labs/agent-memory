Decided to purchase either spider plant (Chlorophytum comosum) or snake plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) for home air purification. Both are easy to care for and thrive indoors. Setup: use planter with drainage holes and well-draining potting mix. Fertilize once monthly during spring/summer with half-strength balanced fertilizer (20-20-20 N-P-K); skip fall/winter. Prefer infrequent watering. Spider plant removes formaldehyde and xylene. Snake plant removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene and tolerates low light.
