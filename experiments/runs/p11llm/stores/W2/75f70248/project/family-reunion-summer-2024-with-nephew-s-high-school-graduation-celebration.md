Planning a family reunion for summer 2024 that coincides with nephew's high school graduation. Decided on a backyard BBQ with a slideshow as the celebration format.

Slideshow Plan:
- Duration: 5-10 minutes maximum
- Soundtrack: Mix of instrumental tracks and popular songs that are sentimental to nephew; kept mellow and concise to not overpower
- Photo types: childhood memories, high school years, sports achievements, school events, family moments, personal milestones, humorous moments, graduation-specific photos
- Structure: Chronological from childhood through graduation
- Captions: Mix of personal, inspirational, and humorous quotes/captions (avoid overly cheesy tone)
- Animations: Keep minimal—simple fade-ins, dissolves, and transitions without overwhelming
- Quality: High-quality, well-lit, in-focus photos; vary pace to maintain engagement

Key details:
- Get feedback from trusted family members before event
- Practice presentation multiple times before the day
- Balance serious and playful tone throughout
- Include inside jokes and personal anecdotes for authenticity
