Planning to attend another music festival in the near future. Will pack: travel-sized sunscreen (1 oz), hat (fold/roll to save space), and compact portable charger with cable organizer. Strategy: use main compartment for sunscreen and hat, front pocket for frequently-accessed items (phone/wallet), side pockets for water bottle. Prioritizing light packing to avoid overloading Fjallraven bag.
