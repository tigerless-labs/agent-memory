Similar to Bon Iver: S. Carey (former member introspective), Volcano Choir (Justin Vernon project with electronic elements), The Antlers (emotive soundscapes), James Blake (soulful electronic indie), Ben Howard (introspective acoustic). Similar to Fleet Foxes: Beirut (eclectic folk and world music), The Tallest Man on Earth (Swedish folk rock), Grizzly Bear (harmony rich indie rock), The Lumineers (folk rock with catchy hooks), First Aid Kit (Swedish duo with lush harmonies). Others: The Head and the Heart (lush harmonies), The Avett Brothers (folk rock storytelling), Mumford and Sons (energetic folk rock), The Decemberists (indie folk with literary lyrics), Lord Huron (dreamy atmospheric)
