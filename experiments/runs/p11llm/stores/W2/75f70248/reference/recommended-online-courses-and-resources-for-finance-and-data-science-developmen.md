**Business Acumen Courses**
- Coursera - Financial Markets by Yale University
- edX - Business Foundations by University of Pennsylvania
- LinkedIn Learning - Business Skills courses
- Harvard Business Review (HBR) - Online Courses

**Domain Expertise & Finance**
- CFA Institute - Online Learning and CFA program
- Financial Times - Masterclasses
- Wall Street Prep - Financial Modeling and Investment Banking
- GARP (Global Association of Risk Professionals) - Online Courses on Risk Management

**Financial Modeling & Forecasting**
- Wall Street Prep - Financial Modeling course
- Coursera - Financial Modeling by University of Michigan
- edX - Financial Modeling and Forecasting by New York Institute of Finance
- LinkedIn Learning - Financial Modeling courses

**Recommended Books**
- "A Random Walk Down Wall Street" by Burton G. Malkiel
- "The Intelligent Investor" by Benjamin Graham
- "The Essays of Warren Buffett: Lessons for Corporate America" edited by Lawrence Cunningham
- "Flash Boys: A Wall Street Revolt" by Michael Lewis
- "Financial Modeling and Forecasting" by John Tjia
- "Financial Modeling for Business Owners and Entrepreneurs" by Tom Y. Sawyer
- "Forecasting: Principles and Practice" by Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos

**Industry News & Publications**
- The Financial Times
- Bloomberg
- The Economist
- Khan Academy - Finance and Capital Markets (free)

**Podcasts**
- The Finance Industry Podcast
- Planet Money (NPR)
- The Financial Diet
- The Motley Fool's Money Podcast
