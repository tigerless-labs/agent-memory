Old Town School of Folk Music (Lincoln Square): Wide guitar classes with folk/acoustic focus. The Music Gallery (Lakeview): Private lessons with indie/folk focus plus workshops. The Guitar Workshop (Logan Square): Private and group lessons for indie/folk/rock. Fretboard Music (Avondale): Private lessons and group classes for folk/rock/indie. The Music Room (Ravenswood): Private and group lessons for folk/indie/acoustic.
