When asking for feedback or collaboration, values being:\n- Respectful of others' time\n- Clear about what they're asking for\n- Genuinely interested in others' work\n- Open to constructive criticism\n- Offering value in return\n- Not pushy or aggressive\n\nPrepares an elevator pitch for their project ideas and follows up with contacts after networking events.
