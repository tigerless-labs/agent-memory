**Waterproofing products for fall/winter boots:**
- Nikwax Tech Wash — water-based cleaner and waterproofing agent, popular with outdoor enthusiasts
- Scotchgard Fabric & Fabric Protector — repels water and stains
- Kiwi Camp Dry — water-based waterproofing spray, easy to apply
- Granger's Performance Repel — high-performance spray, ideal for Gore-Tex or similar membranes

**Application tips:**
- Clean boots first before waterproofing
- Choose product suitable for boot material (leather, suede, nubuck, synthetic)
- Test on small inconspicuous area first
- Follow manufacturer instructions for drying time
- Reapply regularly to maintain effectiveness
- Condition leather boots to keep them supple
