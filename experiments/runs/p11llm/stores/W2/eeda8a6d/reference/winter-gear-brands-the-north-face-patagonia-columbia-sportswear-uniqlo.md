**Recommended brands for winter gear:**
- The North Face — quality winter jackets and outerwear
- Patagonia — insulated gear and outerwear
- Columbia Sportswear — winter clothing and footwear
- Uniqlo — affordable, high-quality thermal wear and layering pieces

Focus on layering with moisture-wicking base layers, insulating mid-layers, and waterproof outerwear. Look for thermal leggings, down or synthetic insulated jackets, and quality insulated boots.
