Indian energy company active in pipelines, natural gas marketing, LPG, petrochemicals, and power generation.

## Strategic Goal
Target: Among top 50 global energy companies by 2030

## Recent M&A Activity (2018–2019)

Acquisitions:
- March 2019: IEDCL (180 MW gas-based power plant, Tamil Nadu)
- May 2018: 5% stake in ONGC Tripura Power Company (726.6 MW gas-based power plant)

Divestitures:
- August 2018: 10.2% stake in Mahanagar Gas Limited
- June 2018: Entire stake in Central UP Gas Limited (to Adani Gas)

## Key Challenges
- Infrastructure development (land, permitting, environmental clearances, financing)
- Competition from domestic and international players
- Regulatory and policy changes
- Energy price volatility
- Geopolitical risks

## Strengths
- Diverse business portfolio
- Investment in R&D for new technologies
- Strong financial position with consistent revenues
- Experienced management team

## Sustainability Initiatives
- Expanding natural gas pipeline network as cleaner energy alternative
- Renewable energy: solar projects with Indian Railways
- Bio-CNG plants (converting agricultural/municipal waste)
- CO2 capture and utilization tech (joint venture with Carbon Clean Solutions Limited, 2020)
- Internal practices: energy reduction, water conservation, waste management
