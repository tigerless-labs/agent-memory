Bike trail recommendations for Midwest summer trip:\n\n**Wisconsin:**\n- **Elroy-Sparta State Trail**: 32 miles, paved and crushed stone mix, scenic countryside, rest stops\n- **Wiouwash State Trail**: 22 miles, mostly paved, Fox River Valley views, Oshkosh to Hortonville\n- **Lake Wissota State Park Trails**: 20 miles near Chippewa Falls, including 6-mile lake loop\n- **Blue Mounds State Park Trails**: 15 miles including 4-mile Pleasure Valley Trail through prairies and forests\n\n**Michigan:**\n- **Pere Marquette State Trail**: 30 miles, paved, Pere Marquette River and forest views\n- **Leelanau Trail**: 17 miles, paved/crushed stone mix, Traverse City to Suttons Bay, Leelanau Peninsula views\n- **Sleeping Bear Heritage Trail**: 27 miles, paved, Traverse City to Sleeping Bear Dunes, Lake Michigan views\n- **North Country Trail**: 460 miles total, spans Michigan including Upper Peninsula, mixed terrain\n\nAll feature rest stops, small towns with food/water, and bike repair services. Check DNR websites for current conditions.
