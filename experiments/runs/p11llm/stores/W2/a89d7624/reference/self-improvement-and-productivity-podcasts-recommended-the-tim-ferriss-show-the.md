Recommended podcasts for self-improvement and productivity (user interested in exploring these during lunch breaks):

**Primary Recommendations:**
- **The Tim Ferriss Show**: Interviews with successful people on habits, routines, success strategies
  - Episode #214: Jamie Foxx on morning routine (30-min workout, meditation, journaling)
  - Episode #193: Neil Strauss on focus strategies and 'stop doing' lists

- **The Happiness Lab with Dr. Laurie Santos**: Science of happiness and well-being
  - Episode on 'The Power of Small Wins': How small wins boost motivation
  - Episode on 'Benefits of a Stop Doing List': Eliminating energy-draining tasks

- **Productivity Show**: Time management, goal-setting, expert interviews
  - Episode 143: Creating an effective morning routine
  - Episode 135: Focus and prioritization for goal achievement

**Additional Recommendations:**
- **The 5 AM Miracle Podcast**: Hal Elrod's morning routine strategies (Episode 144)
- **Achieve Your Goals Podcast**: Hal Elrod's productivity tips with morning routines (Episode 134)

User was particularly interested in episodes about morning routines and 'stop doing' lists in relation to their peak productivity hours (8:30 AM - 12:00 PM).
