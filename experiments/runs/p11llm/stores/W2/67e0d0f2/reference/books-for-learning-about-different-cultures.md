Recommended books for understanding different cultures and cross-cultural perspectives:

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri - Explores identity, family, and culture through a Bengali-American family story.

2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini - Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan exploring friendship, betrayal, and redemption.

3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan - Follows four Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers.

4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe - Explores a Nigerian tribe's life before and after British colonization.

5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi - Graphic novel about a young Iranian girl growing up during the Islamic Revolution.
