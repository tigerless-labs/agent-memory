Four major organizations offering cross-cultural exchange programs:

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** - https://afs.org/ - One of the largest non-profit organizations providing intercultural learning for students of all ages. Offers student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, and volunteering in 50+ countries.

2. **Experiment in International Living** - https://www.experiment.org/ - Nonprofit offering immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students. Programs include living with host families, attending local schools, and community service projects.

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** - https://www.yfu.org/ - International educational organization providing exchange programs for students and young adults. Offers year-long exchanges, semester programs, and summer programs in 40+ countries.

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** - https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/ - Nonprofit providing volunteering opportunities for all ages in countries like Costa Rica, Ghana, India, and Peru. Focus on community development projects and cultural exchange.
