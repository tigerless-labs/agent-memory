**Albums:**
- *Golden Hour* - won Grammy Album of the Year (2019), CMA Album of the Year (2018), ACM Album of the Year (2018)
- *Same Trailer Different Park* - won Grammy Best Country Album (2014), ACM Album of the Year (2013), CMA Album of the Year (2013)

**Popular Songs:**
- Merry Go 'Round
- Follow Your Arrow
- Butterflies
- Rainbow

Known for blending traditional country music with modern pop elements.
