**Objective**: Automate translation scale, speed, and quality for large academic research database and information services company.

**Current state**: Manual, time-consuming, cost-prohibitive translation processes; lacks TMS; marketing/engineering teams burdened with low-value-add translation work.

**Ideal state**: Technology-powered translation strategy with TMS, machine translation, and NLP automation.

**Key Results**:
- Reduce manual translation work by 50%
- Increase translation speed by 30%
- Improve translation quality by 20%
- Reduce translation costs by 40%

**Implementation approach**: Implement TMS (centralized platform), machine translation/NLP for low-value-add tasks, translation memory for consistency and cost reduction.
