Collection of fun party activity ideas for celebrations:

**Outdoor Games:**
- Backyard obstacle course (hula hoops, cones, balance beams, tires, tunnels)
- Water balloon toss (classic game with players stepping back after each catch)
- Badminton game (setup badminton net)
- Lawn Twister (use spray paint to draw board)
- Scavenger hunt
- Cornhole, giant Jenga, ring toss

**DIY Photo Booth Setup:**
- Backdrops: colored paper, balloons, customized banners
- Props: hats, glasses, mustaches, inflatable guitars, silly accessories
- Camera: smartphone or digital camera with tripod
- Printing station: printer and paper for on-the-spot prints
- Signage: 'Photo Booth' or 'Grad's Glamour Shots'

**Tips:**
- Keep setup simple
- Designate a photo booth host
- Encourage social media sharing with branded hashtag
