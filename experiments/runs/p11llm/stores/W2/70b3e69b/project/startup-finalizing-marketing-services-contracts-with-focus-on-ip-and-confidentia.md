Currently working on finalizing contracts for marketing services startup. Key focus areas:

**Intellectual Property (IP) clauses:**
- Define ownership of work products and creative assets
- Specify scope, duration, and territory of licenses granted to clients
- Identify pre-existing IP and tools retained by the startup
- Include warranties that work doesn't infringe third-party IP rights

**Confidentiality clauses:**
- Define what constitutes confidential information (business strategies, customer data, trade secrets)
- Specify confidentiality obligations for clients
- Identify exceptions (public domain, legally required disclosures)
- Set duration of confidentiality obligations (may extend beyond contract term)

**Next steps:**
- Obtain contract templates from resources like Docracy, LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer, Startup Foundry
- Customize templates for specific startup needs
- Send contracts to lawyer contact (from Chamber of Commerce networking event) for professional review and feedback
- Negotiate final terms with clients

**Resources to reference:**
- Y Combinator Startup Law 101
- ABA Model Contract templates
- INTA Model Contract for trademark/copyright licensing
