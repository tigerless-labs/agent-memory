Planned pizza topping combination:\n- Spicy BBQ sauce (high-quality, not too sweet; optionally add diced jalapeños or serrano peppers)\n- Grilled chicken (marinated in olive oil, lime juice, and spices)\n- Red onion (thinly sliced and caramelized)\n- Fresh cilantro (sprinkled on top; can also mix into BBQ sauce)\n- Optional additions: crumbled bacon or diced bell peppers\n\nTechnique notes:\n- Pre-bake crust for 2-3 minutes before adding toppings (par-baking)\n- Balance toppings to prevent soggy crust\n- Cook for 10-12 minutes until crust is golden brown
