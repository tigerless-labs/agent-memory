## Madras Curry Powder Brands\n- **Penzeys Spices**: 12-spice blend (turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom)\n- **The Spice House**: 15-spice blend (includes fenugreek and fennel)\n- **McCormick**: Affordable, accessible option\n\n## Madras Curry Sauce Recipe (from-scratch)\n**Ingredients:**\n- 2 tbsp vegetable oil\n- 1 diced onion\n- 2 cloves garlic, minced\n- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger\n- 1 tsp ground cumin\n- 1 tsp curry leaves (optional)\n- 1/2 tsp turmeric\n- 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder\n- 1/2 tsp garam masala\n- 1/2 tsp salt\n- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)\n- 1 can diced tomatoes (14 oz)\n- 1 cup vegetable broth\n- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional)\n- Fresh cilantro for garnish\n\n**Instructions:** Sauté onion in oil until translucent. Add spices and cook 1-2 min until fragrant. Add tomatoes, broth, coconut milk. Simmer 10-15 min. Add cooked tofu/protein and simmer 5-7 min more. Garnish with cilantro. Serve over rice or with naan.\n\n**Tips:** Customize spice blend, add potatoes/carrots, use yogurt or cashew cream instead of coconut milk, add lemon juice for brightness.
