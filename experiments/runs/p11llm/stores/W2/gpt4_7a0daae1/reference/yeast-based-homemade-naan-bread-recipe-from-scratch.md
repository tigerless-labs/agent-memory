## Simple Naan Bread Recipe\n**Ingredients:**\n- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour\n- 1/4 tsp salt\n- 1/4 tsp sugar\n- 1/2 tsp active dry yeast\n- 1/2 cup lukewarm water\n- 2 tbsp olive oil\n- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, for tender crumb)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, yeast\n2. Gradually add water and mix\n3. Add olive oil and mix until sticky ball forms\n4. Knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic\n5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth, rise 1-2 hours until doubled\n6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat\n7. Divide into 6-8 portions, shape into balls\n8. Roll each into thin circle (6-7 inches diameter)\n9. Cook in skillet 1-2 minutes until puffing, flip\n10. Cook another 1-2 minutes until golden brown\n11. Brush with melted butter or ghee if desired\n12. Serve hot\n\n**Variations:** Add garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds to dough; use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crispier crust; try whole wheat or higher-protein flour.
