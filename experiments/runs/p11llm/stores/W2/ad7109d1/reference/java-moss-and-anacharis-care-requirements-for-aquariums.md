**Java Moss**
Lighting: Low to moderate (1-2 watts per gallon)
Temperature: 72-82°F (22-28°C)
Water Parameters: pH 5.5-8.5, slightly acidic to neutral
Attachment: Rocks, driftwood, or decorations with plant weight or thread
Water Flow: Gentle, but adapts to moderate currents
Fertilization: 5-5-5 NPK fertilizer once weekly
Fish Interaction: Great hideout for neon tetras; honey gouramis may nibble; pleco grazes but not significant food source
Cautions: Sensitive to copper medications; prone to algae

**Anacharis**
Lighting: Moderate to high (2-4 watts per gallon)
Temperature: 65-85°F (18-29°C)
Water Parameters: pH 6.5-8.5, wide tolerance
Type: Floating plant, no substrate needed
Water Flow: Moderate to strong
Fertilization: 5-5-5 NPK fertilizer once weekly
Fish Interaction: Primary food for pleco catfish; tetras/gouramis nibble but not preferred
Pruning: Prune regularly for bushy growth
