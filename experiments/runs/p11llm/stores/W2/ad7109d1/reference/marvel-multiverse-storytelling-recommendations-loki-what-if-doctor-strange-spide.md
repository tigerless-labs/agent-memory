Recommendations for exploring multiverse concepts in Marvel media:

**Disney+ Series:**
- **Loki** (2021) — Explores branching timelines and the Time Variance Authority (TVA) that regulates the multiverse. Recommended for deeper understanding of multiverse mechanics.
- **What If...?** (2021) — Animated anthology series showcasing infinite possibilities through alternate 'what if' scenarios. Shows consequences of different choices and outcomes.

**Films:**
- **Doctor Strange** (2016) — Introduced multiverse concept to MCU; explains alternate dimensions and timelines with acclaimed visuals.
- **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** (2018) — Non-MCU animated film; excellent example of multiverse storytelling with parallel universes and multiple Spider-People. Highly acclaimed for visuals, style, and storytelling.

User indicated interest in checking out Loki and What If...? on Disney+.
