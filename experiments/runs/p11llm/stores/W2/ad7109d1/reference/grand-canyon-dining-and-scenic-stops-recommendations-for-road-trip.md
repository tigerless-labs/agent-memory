## Restaurants near Mather Campground (Grand Canyon)\n- El Tovar Dining Room: upscale, contemporary American with regional ingredients, about 1 mile from Mather Campground. Recommended: prime rib, wild king salmon\n- Bright Angel Restaurant: American comfort food, breakfast/lunch/dinner, walking distance, patio with canyon views\n- Canyon Café: casual, great for breakfast/lunch, short walk\n- Maswik Food Court: quick bites/snacks, pizza, deli, grill, 10-minute walk\n- Yavapai Lodge Restaurant: American cuisine, steak and seafood, 1.5 miles away, patio with views\n\n## Scenic Stops SF to Grand Canyon\n- Lake Tahoe, CA/NV (3.5 hrs from SF)\n- Mono Lake, CA (2.5 hrs further)\n- Bodie State Historic Park, CA (ghost town)\n- Hoover Dam, AZ/NV (chosen stop)\n- Valley of Fire State Park, NV (chosen stop)\n- Hackberry, AZ (Route 66 area)\n- Wigwam Motels, Holbrook, AZ\n- Desert View Watchtower, Grand Canyon
