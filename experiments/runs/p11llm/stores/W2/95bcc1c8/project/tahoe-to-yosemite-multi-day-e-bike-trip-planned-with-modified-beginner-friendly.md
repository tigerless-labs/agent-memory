Multi-day e-bike trip from Carson City, Nevada to Tuolumne Meadows via South Lake Tahoe and Sierra Nevada. Modified 4-day itinerary for beginners: Day 1 Carson City to Gardnerville (20 mi, 1000 ft); Day 2 Gardnerville to Markleeville (25 mi, 1500 ft); Day 3 Markleeville to Bridgeport (30 mi, 2000 ft); Day 4 Bridgeport to Tuolumne Meadows (40 mi, 2500 ft). Planning first e-bike rental from Tahoe City Cycles, Tahoe Bike Rentals, South Lake Tahoe Bike Rentals, or Tahoe Sports Hub. Key concerns: battery life (10-40 miles depending on terrain), charging at campgrounds/lodges/cafes. Still need to book accommodations and research attractions.
