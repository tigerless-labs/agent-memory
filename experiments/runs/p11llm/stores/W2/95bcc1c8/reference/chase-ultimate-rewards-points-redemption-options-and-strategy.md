Three main ways to redeem Chase Ultimate Rewards points:

**1. Chase Travel Portal (Best for simplicity)**
- Book flights, hotels, car rentals directly
- 25 percent bonus value (1 point = 1.25 cents value for travel)
- Can earn additional points on bookings
- Compare prices across platforms to ensure best deal

**2. Transfer to Loyalty Programs (Best for maximum value)**
- Transfer at 1:1 ratio to: United, American Airlines, Delta, Marriott, Hyatt, others
- More flexible redemption options
- Better value if you have elite status or find award sweet spots
- Access to loyalty program award charts and blackout-date rules

**3. Book with Travel Partners**
- Redeem through Expedia, Airbnb, VRBO
- Earn points on partner bookings
- Receive travel credits or bookings

**Tips:**
- Compare Chase portal prices with external booking sites
- Check for elite status in loyalty programs for better redemption rates
- Review terms for restrictions and blackout dates
- Flexibility is a key advantage of Chase points vs airline-specific miles
