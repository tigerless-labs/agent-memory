During emotionally difficult times (like grief), the user employs several coping strategies:\n\n- **Journaling**: Writes down thoughts, feelings, and memories in a notebook to process emotions and capture moments\n- **Comfort items**: Brings mementos (photos, jewelry, or personal items) connected to loved ones\n- **Sharing memories**: Shares old photos, letters, and stories with family to honor the person's memory and feel connected\n- **Being prepared**: Brings practical comfort items (tissues) to be ready for emotional moments\n- **Reflection**: Uses writing and reflection as a therapeutic tool to gain insight and process grief\n\nThe user believes in acknowledging and validating emotions rather than hiding them, and finds that preparation and organization help reduce stress during difficult times.
