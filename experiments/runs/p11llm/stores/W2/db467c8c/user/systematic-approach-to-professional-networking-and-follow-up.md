Prefers thoughtful, intentional networking with meaningful connections. Follow-up approach includes:\n- Sending thank-you emails within days of meeting\n- Connecting on LinkedIn\n- Periodically checking in and sharing relevant resources/articles\n- Asking if contacts are open to follow-up conversations or meetings\n- Being specific about what they're looking for (job opportunities, advice, guidance)\n- Showing enthusiasm and passion in follow-up communications\n\nValues active listening, asking thoughtful questions, and being respectful of others' time.
