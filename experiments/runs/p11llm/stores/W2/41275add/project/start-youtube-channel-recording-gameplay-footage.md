Planning to start recording gameplay footage for a YouTube channel. Decided to use **OBS Studio** as the screen recording software because:\n\n- Free and open-source\n- Large community of users and extensive plugin library\n- Highly customizable for creating a unique channel look\n- Well-optimized for AMD Ryzen 9 5900X and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080 hardware\n- Supports hardware acceleration for improved performance\n\nConsiderations also included XSplit, but OBS Studio was chosen as the starting option.
