Professional photography guidance for documenting vintage items for sale:\n\n**Lighting:**\n- Use natural light when possible (soft, flattering)\n- Softbox or diffused light to soften shadows\n- Avoid direct flash; bounce light if needed\n- Consider layered lighting for depth\n\n**Composition:**\n- Plain, neutral backgrounds (white/gray/black)\n- Rule of thirds positioning\n- Experiment with unique angles and perspectives\n- Close-up shots of specific details\n\n**Editing:**\n- Color correction for accurate representation\n- Noise reduction and selective sharpening\n- Minor retouching (dust, scratches) without over-processing\n- Consistent style across all images\n\n**Additional tips:**\n- Use tripod for stability\n- Shoot in RAW format for editing flexibility\n- Ensure sharp focus; consider shallow depth of field\n- Include lifestyle shots showing item in use\n\nUser has photography background with Canon EOS 60D and Sony A7III, so starting with existing equipment advantage.
