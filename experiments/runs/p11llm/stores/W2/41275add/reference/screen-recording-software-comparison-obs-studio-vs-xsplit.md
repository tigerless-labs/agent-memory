Screen recording options for PC gaming and YouTube:\n\n**OBS Studio (Chosen)**\n- Free, open-source\n- Lightweight, lower resource usage\n- Large community, extensive plugin library\n- Highly customizable\n- Hardware acceleration support for NVIDIA GPT\n- Multi-source audio mixing, chroma keying, custom hotkeys\n\n**XSplit (Alternative)**\n- User-friendly interface, more polished\n- Subscription-based with free trial\n- Easier for beginners\n- Comprehensive customer support\n- Features: 4K recording, multi-streaming, built-in video editor\n\nOther options evaluated: ShadowPlay (NVIDIA GPU required), Fraps, Bandicam, Camtasia, Game Bar (Windows 10).\n\nBoth fully compatible with AMD Ryzen 9 5900X and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080.
