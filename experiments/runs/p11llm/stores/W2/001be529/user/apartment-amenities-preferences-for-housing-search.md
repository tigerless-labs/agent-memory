When searching for apartments, considers these amenities desirable (in priority order based on conversation):
1. Proximity to public transportation (primary need)
2. Safety and security features (gated parking, cameras, on-site security)
3. Cleanliness and good condition
4. In-unit laundry or laundry facilities
5. Gym or fitness center
6. Pool
7. Parking (if has a car)
8. Pet-friendliness (if applicable)
9. Community amenities like gardens

Priorities: affordability, safety, cleanliness, and accessibility to volunteer job location.
