# VFX Workflow Stages

1. **Pre-production** — Planning phase with directors, producers, cinematographers; determine VFX needs, look and feel
2. **Asset creation** — Create digital assets (characters, props, environments) using 3D modeling and texturing
3. **Animation** — Animate digital elements; includes keyframe animation, motion capture, simulations
4. **Compositing** — Combine visual elements (live-action, CG, VFX) into single cohesive image
5. **Rendering** — Generate final VFX output; render individual elements then composite together
6. **Review and approval** — Review work with director and stakeholders against creative vision
7. **Delivery** — Final stage; deliver finished VFX to client or integrate into final project

Understanding these stages is important for all roles (artists, producers, directors) to ensure final product meets creative vision and technical requirements.
