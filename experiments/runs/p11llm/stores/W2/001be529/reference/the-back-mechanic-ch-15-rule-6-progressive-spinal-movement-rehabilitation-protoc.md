## Core Principle
Crawl before you walk: master each movement level before progressing. Healing has its own rhythm and cannot be rushed.

## Movement Progression Sequence
1. Marching in place with neutral spine (no lower back movement)
2. Walking with neutral spine (weight-bearing, engages glutes and lats)
3. Hip-hinging (bending from hips only, lower back stays neutral)
4. Full-body rotation (chest, navel, hips move together, spine protected)
5. Compound movements (torso rotation plus hip-hinge plus squat for daily tasks)

## Scientific Basis
All movements keep the lower spine in neutral position with engaged core. Movement initiates from hips, pelvis, and larger muscle groups—never from spine flexion/rotation. This protects intervertebral discs and prevents long-term wear.

## Pain-Based Progression Rule
If any movement causes pain: regress to the previous level, maintain until pain-free, then attempt progression again. Indicates insufficient strength or improper form in one component.
