Snake plants (Sansevieria Trifasciata) are easy to propagate using two main methods:

**Method 1: Leaf Cuttings**
- Choose a mature, healthy leaf with a long petiole
- Cut leaf 1-2 inches from base using sharp, clean knife
- Remove lower leaves, leaving top 2-3 inches
- Root in water or well-draining potting mix
- Roots develop within 1-2 weeks
- Transplant once roots develop

**Method 2: Rhizome Division**
- Choose a mature plant with well-developed rhizome
- Dig up entire plant and separate rhizome into sections
- Ensure each section has at least one growing point (eye)
- Replant divided sections in individual pots with well-draining mix
- Water thoroughly

**Tips:**
- Use sharp, sterile tools to prevent disease
- Keep area bright, indirectly lit, 65-75°F
- Water sparingly until roots develop (prevent root rot)
- Can propagate any time, but spring/summer optimal
