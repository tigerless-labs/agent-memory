For article writing targeting 23-30 year old women (inexperienced audience):

**Style Requirements:**
- Informative, bias to short sentences
- Active voice, third-person POV
- Friendly but professional tone
- Neutral and objective voice

**Specific Instruction:**
Include 'perplexity' and 'burstiness' — write with good variation in sentence length and complexity (mix longer/complex sentences with shorter ones) to avoid uniform AI-like tone.

**Delivery Method:**
Prefers phased, step-by-step delivery with confirmation before moving to next section.

**Context:**
Used for article on third pregnancy health & nutrition targeting inexperienced audience.
