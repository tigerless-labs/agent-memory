Decided to use grouping and binning methods to handle high cardinality categorical variables in customer purchase data analysis. This approach will:
- Group similar categories based on business knowledge or clustering
- Reduce dimensionality and avoid curse of dimensionality
- Prevent overfitting and computational issues
- Improve feature engineering for pattern identification

Also considering other techniques like target encoding and feature extraction for the analysis.
