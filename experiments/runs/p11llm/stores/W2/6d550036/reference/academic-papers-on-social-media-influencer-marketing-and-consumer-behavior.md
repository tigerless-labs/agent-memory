Foundational papers for research on influencer marketing effects on consumer purchasing:

**Systematic Review & Meta-analyses:**
- Hussain, S., & Ali, M. (2020). Influence of Social Media Influencers on Consumer Purchase Decisions: A Systematic Review. Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, 55, 102083. [Comprehensive review of 35 studies identifying credibility, trust, and social identity as key themes]
- Wang, Y., & Li, M. (2020). The Effects of Social Media Influencer Marketing on Consumer Purchase Intention: A Meta-Analytic Review. Journal of Interactive Marketing, 49, 100269. [Meta-analysis of 22 studies; identifies influencer credibility, content quality, and platform type as significant moderators]

**Social Identity Theory & Influencer Marketing:**
- Kim, J., & Lee, Y. (2020). Understanding the Role of Social Identity in Influencer Marketing: An Experimental Study. Journal of Advertising, 49(2), 249-264. [Demonstrates that shared social identity increases engagement with sponsored content and brand attitudes]

**Millennial Consumer Behavior:**
- Kapoor, K. K., & Kumar, V. (2019). The Impact of Influencer Marketing on Consumer Purchase Decisions: A Study of Millennials. Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, 47, 274-283. [Explores trust, credibility, and social norms as drivers of purchase intent in millennial consumers]

**Deception & Manipulation:**
- Schouten, A. P., & Jansz, J. (2020). The Dark Side of Influencer Marketing: An Examination of Deception and Manipulation. Journal of Business Ethics, 161(2), 257-271. [Examines ethical concerns in influencer marketing practices]
