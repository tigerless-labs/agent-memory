Day 1: Alfama (Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge), Baixa (Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square, Chiado, Café A Brasileira). Day 2: Sintra day trip (Pena Palace, Moorish Castle), Belém (Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém). Day 3: Tagus River walk, MAAT Museum, Bairro Alto (Miradouro viewpoint, restaurants). Also consider: National Museum of Ancient Art, Gulbenkian Museum, LX Factory. Lisbon chosen over Porto for 3-day trip due to more attractions and nearby day trips (Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica).
