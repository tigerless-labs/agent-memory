**Source:** Brown University, Education Alliance
https://www.brown.edu/academics/education-alliance/teaching-diverse-learners/strategies-0/culturally-responsive-teaching-0

**Two components:**

**Part A: Checklist for Lesson Alignment**
- Clear expectations for what students know/do
- Cooperative learning for new material (book clubs, literature circles, role-playing, Jigsaw, Reciprocal Learning)
- Students connect perspectives/experiences to topic
- Students build knowledge (write definitions, peer teach, student-directed sharing)
- Self-assessment included
- Students support ideas with text/pictures/reasoning
- Teach students to question/challenge own beliefs and actions
- Alternative viewpoints acknowledged and discussed
- Lesson images/examples reference various cultures
- Images represent people as individuals (not stereotypes/generically)
- Protagonists (historic/biographical/main characters) vary by gender, economic and ethnic backgrounds

**Part B: Checklist for Improving Lessons**
- Polls for learning style preferences
- Incorporate student identities (family traditions, cultural background research)
- Polls/questions to question/challenge/review lesson
- Teach differences between individuals; show how diversity strengthens class
- Encourage use of first language to enhance learning
- Student choice in reading, project goals, research topics
- Inquiry-based/discovery-oriented approaches
- Research within community; interview/invite community members
- Differentiate learning modes (individual vs. group; video vs. written)
- Cross-cultural interaction via technology

**Use as:** curriculum development and revision tool
