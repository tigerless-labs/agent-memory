Three-phase approach for turning Event Marketing Summit contacts into meaningful connections and partnerships:\n\n**Immediate Follow-up (24-48 hours):**\n- Send personalized emails referencing conversations and topics discussed\n- Connect on social media (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)\n- Share relevant content aligned with discussion topics\n\n**Short-term Follow-up (1-2 weeks):**\n- Schedule calls or meetings to continue conversation\n- Introduce contacts to existing network via email or social media\n- Share additional resources (whitepapers, webinars)\n\n**Long-term Follow-up (1-3 months):**\n- Check-in and provide value through insights, advice, connections\n- Explore collaboration opportunities (joint webinars, content, sponsorships)\n- Send invitations to exclusive events (beta testing, early access)\n\n**Best Practices:**\n- Use CRM to track interactions and follow-ups\n- Personalize approach based on conversation and interests\n- Be consistent in follow-up cadence\n- Focus on mutually beneficial opportunities\n- Be patient: building meaningful connections takes time\n- Prioritize providing value over pushing for immediate results
