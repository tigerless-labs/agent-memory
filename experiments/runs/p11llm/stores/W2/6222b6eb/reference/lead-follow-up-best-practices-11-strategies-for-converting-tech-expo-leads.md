Effective lead follow-up recommendations for converting 200+ Tech Expo leads:\n\n1. **Respond quickly** (24-48 hours) - shows enthusiasm and keeps company top of mind\n2. **Personalize outreach** - address by name, reference specific conversations\n3. **Segment leads** - categorize by interest level, job function, company size\n4. **Multi-channel approach** - emails, phone calls, social media combined\n5. **Provide value** - share case studies, whitepapers, webinars\n6. **Offer demos/meetings** - personalized product demos and meetings\n7. **Set clear next steps** - outline follow-up calls or meetings\n8. **Use CRM software** - track interactions and ensure no leads fall through cracks\n9. **Phone call follow-up** - powerful for re-engaging unresponsive leads\n10. **Nurture leads** - develop strategy for long-term relationship building\n11. **Measure and optimize** - track effectiveness and adjust approach\n\nAdditional tactics: offer free trial, highlight competitive advantages, share success stories and testimonials.
