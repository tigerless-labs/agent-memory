User seeking contemporary fiction novels with strong female protagonists and complex relationships like 'The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo'. Recommended titles:

- **The Devil Wears Prada** by Lauren Weisberger — complex relationship between demanding boss and assistant, focus on female characters
- **The Mothers** by Brit Bennett — coming-of-age in Southern California, themes of motherhood and identity
- **The Girls** by Emma Cline — 1960s, teenage girls in Manson-esque cult, female relationships and power dynamics
- **The Interestings** by Meg Wolitzer — friends from summer arts camp, complex relationships over time
- **Conversations with Friends** by Sally Rooney — complex relationships between two young women and a couple
- **The Female Persuasion** by Meg Wolitzer — young woman's relationship with famous feminist mentor, themes of identity and power
- **The Last Romantics** by Tara Conklin — four siblings, complex relationships across time
- **The Age of Light** by Whitney Scharer — based on Lee Miller's life, Paris 1920s-1930s avant-garde, complex relationships with men and identity
