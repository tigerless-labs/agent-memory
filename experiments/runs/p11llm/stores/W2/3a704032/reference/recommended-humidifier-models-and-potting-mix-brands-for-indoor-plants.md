**Recommended Humidifiers for Indoor Plants:**
- Levoit LV600HH Hybrid: 6-liter tank, cool/warm mist, adjustable humidistat, affordable
- Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate: 1-liter tank, compact, easy to use
- Deneve Minimal Aroma: 2.5-liter tank, ultrasonic, built-in humidistat, sleek design
- Crane Drop Ultrasonic: 1-liter tank, whisper-quiet operation, modern design

Placement: within 3-5 feet of plants. Clean regularly to prevent bacterial growth.

**Recommended Potting Mixes for Spider Plants and General Use:**
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix (balanced, general-purpose)
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix (organic, peat/compost/perlite blend)
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix (eco-friendly, nutrient-rich)
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix (professional-grade, peat/vermiculite/perlite blend)

Spider plants prefer well-draining mix rich in organic matter, slightly acidic pH, and like being slightly root-bound.
