Fern developed an infestation of tiny, moving dots on leaves and stems—likely spider mites or springtails. Treatment plan: neem oil spray applied per product instructions. Follow-up: increase misting to 2-3 times per week (morning is best, with distilled/filtered water to avoid mineral buildup on leaves). Avoid misting the crown. Monitor closely after treatment and repeat as needed.
