Resources for finding stamp shows and exhibitions:
- **American Philatelic Society (APS)** (stamps.org) - Has "Calendar of Events" section listing upcoming stamp shows and exhibitions across the United States; can filter by date, location, and event type
- **Local stamp clubs** - Philatelic organizations in your area; often have information on regional and national events
- **Stamp collecting forums and online communities** - Stamp Collecting subreddit, Facebook groups dedicated to stamp collecting; members share information about upcoming shows and exhibitions
- **Stamp dealers and auction houses** - Contact local dealers or auction houses; they often participate in or sponsor events and have information on upcoming happenings
- **Google Search** - Search for "stamp shows near me" or "stamp exhibitions [city/state]" for online event calendars and local philatelic organizations

Tips: Verify dates, times, and locations of events as they can be subject to change
