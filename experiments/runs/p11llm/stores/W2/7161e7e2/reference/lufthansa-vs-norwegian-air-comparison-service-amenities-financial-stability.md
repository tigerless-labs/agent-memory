**Lufthansa**\n- Strong financial foundation, stable carrier\n- Service: Friendly, attentive crew, personalized experience\n- Food: Complimentary meal service on most flights (choice of 2 meals), snacks, drinks including beer/wine\n- Seats: 31-32 inch pitch, 17.5 inch width, adjustable headrests, more recline\n- In-flight entertainment: Wide selection of movies, TV, music, games\n- Baggage: 1 free checked bag (23kg) included in economy\n- Hub: Frankfurt Airport (FRA) — modern, efficient\n- Frequent flyer: Miles & More program\n\n**Norwegian Air**\n- Financial struggles due to competition, fuel costs, and long-haul investments\n- Risks: Possible flight cancellations, bankruptcy risk, limited customer service\n- Service: Functional, efficient but more impersonal\n- Food: Buy-on-board service (no complimentary meals)\n- Seats: 29-30 inch pitch, 17 inch width, limited headrest adjustment, less recline\n- In-flight entertainment: Limited selection\n- Baggage: Extra charge for checked bags ($20-30)\n- Hub: Oslo Airport (OSL)\n\nConclusion: Lufthansa offers better value for those prioritizing comfort and service; Norwegian Air better for budget-conscious travelers willing to accept trade-offs.
