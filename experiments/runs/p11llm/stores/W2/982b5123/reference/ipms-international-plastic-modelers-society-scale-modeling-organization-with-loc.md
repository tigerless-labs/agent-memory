**IPMS (International Plastic Modelers' Society)**

Founded in 1963 in the United Kingdom. Over 4,000 members worldwide with chapters in over 20 countries.

**Activities:**
- Local chapters meet regularly to share models and techniques
- Annual national convention in the US with contests, workshops, and vendor room
- Regional meets for specific geographic areas
- Model contests at local, regional, and national levels
- Online forums for discussion and knowledge sharing
- Public displays and exhibitions
- Quarterly magazine: IPMS/USA Journal

**Membership Benefits:**
- Access to local chapters
- Discounts on model kits, tools, and supplies
- Online forum access
- Contest eligibility
- Community of modelers

**Online Communities for Model Building:**
- Reddit: r/ModelBuilding (130,000+ members)
- Scalemates (100,000+ members)
- Hyperscale
- Model Mayhem
- Facebook groups (Model Building Enthusiasts, Scale Modeling, etc.)

**YouTube Channels for Tutorials:**

- Modeldaddy (weathering and detailing)
- The Scale Modeler
