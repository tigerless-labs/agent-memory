Experimenting with DIY potting soil. Original mix (peat moss + vermiculite + perlite) unsuitable for peas; too dense/waterlogged.

**Recommended recipe for peas:**
- 20–30% Peat moss or coconut coir (moisture retention, acidity)
- 20–30% Compost or worm castings (structure, fertility, microbes)
- 20–30% Perlite or vermiculite (drainage, aeration)
- 10–20% Garden soil or topsoil (structure, nutrients)
- 10–20% Sand or grit (drainage, structure)

**Key for peas:**
- Good drainage (prevent waterlogging/root rot)
- Aeration for healthy roots
- Slightly acidic to neutral pH (6.0–7.0)
- Consider legume-friendly fertilizers or inoculants

**Preparation:** Sterilize before use to prevent disease — either bake at 300°F for 30 min or solarize in clear plastic bag 4–6 weeks.
