Training Peaks is a well-established platform for connecting with certified running coaches.

**Finding a Coach on Training Peaks:**
1. Click "Find a Coach" and select event goal (e.g., Marathon)
2. Filter by pace range (e.g., 3:00-4:00 marathon pace)
3. Filter by sports (e.g., Multi-Sport with soccer, tennis)
4. Review coach profiles, bios, and certifications
5. Contact 2-3 coaches to discuss experience and approach

**Look for:**
- Training Peaks Certified Coach badge
- Experience with runners at your pace
- Multi-sport coaching background
- Customization and communication style
- Track record with similar athletes

**Questions to Ask:**
- Experience coaching multi-sport athletes
- Approach to balancing training across sports
- Periodization strategy to peak at right times
- Communication frequency and style
- Injury prevention and rehabilitation approach
- Cost and service offerings
