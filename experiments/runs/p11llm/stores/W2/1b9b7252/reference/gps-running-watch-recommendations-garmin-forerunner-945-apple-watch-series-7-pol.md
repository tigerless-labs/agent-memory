Top GPS Running Watch Recommendations:

1. **Garmin Forerunner 945** — High-end with music storage, Garmin Pay, performance monitoring, long battery life
2. **Apple Watch Series 7 (GPS)** — Built-in GPS, heart rate monitoring, Apple Health integration, music storage
3. **Polar Vantage V** — Advanced features including power meter, running power, stride detection, ideal for serious runners
4. **Suunto 3 Fitness** — Mid-range, accurate GPS, heart rate monitoring, customizable training plans
5. **Fitbit Ionic** — Focus on fitness and wellness, built-in GPS, guided breathing sessions

Key Features to Prioritize:
- GPS accuracy with GLONASS support
- Optical heart rate monitoring
- Water resistance for wet conditions
- Battery life of 8+ hours in GPS mode
- Compatibility with running apps (Strava, Runkeeper, Apple Health)
- Comfort and appropriate design for personal style

Price ranges from under 200 dollars to over 1000 dollars depending on features and brand.
