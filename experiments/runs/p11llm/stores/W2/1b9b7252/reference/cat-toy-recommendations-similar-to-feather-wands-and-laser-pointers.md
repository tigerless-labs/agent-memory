## Interactive wand toys\n- Wand toys with fabric strips, ribbons, or small ball attachments\n\n## Light toys\n- LED light toys (balls or cubes) as safer alternatives to laser pointers\n\n## Hunting toys\n- Interactive toy mice with feathers or strings\n\n## Exploration toys\n- Tunnels and tents (with feathers or attachments at ends)\n- Crinkle toys (bags or balls)\n\n## Mental stimulation\n- Food puzzle toys (fill with treats or kibble)\n- Catnip toys or catnip-infused mats\n- Motorized toys (spinning tops, self-propelled)\n\n## General tips\n- Rotate toys regularly to prevent boredom\n- Hide toys around house to encourage hunting\n- Supervise playtime to ensure safety
