Status: In evaluation

Potential monthly savings: 50 dollars by cutting cable and switching to streaming-only

Key concern: Access to sports channels; wants to keep ability to watch favorite sports and teams

Decision approach:
- Make list of all sports channels currently watched via cable
- Prioritize channels by importance
- Identify must-have teams and leagues
- Research streaming services with needed sports content
- Calculate total cost of streaming services vs current cable

Streaming options for sports:
- Sling TV (ESPN, NFL Network, NBA TV)
- YouTube TV (ESPN, FS1, NBC Sports)
- Hulu with Live TV (ESPN, FS1, NBC Sports)
- DAZN (boxing, MMA)
- NFL Sunday Ticket (standalone NFL service)

General streaming services: Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Hulu, Disney+, HBO Max, Apple TV+, YouTube TV

Next steps: Research online communities (Reddit: r/CordCutting, r/Streaming, r/Sports) for advice from others who have made the transition
