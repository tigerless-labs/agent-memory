In Reason (Propellerhead):\n\n**Transposing entire songs:**\n- Use Pitch Shifter effect on the master channel\n- Use the Transpose function in the sequencer\n- Apply pitch shifter to individual tracks for selective transposition\n- Use Transpose tool in the sequencer to shift pitch of MIDI clips by semitone\n\n**Pitch Shifter location:**\n- Found in the Pitch Shift module within the Combinator device (Pitch section)\n- Also available in the RV7000 mk2 module\n- Can shift audio signals (vocals, instruments) up or down by semitones\n\nThese tools allow flexible harmonic manipulation without re-recording.
