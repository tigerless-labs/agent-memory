**Album:** Give Up (2003)

**Artists:** Collaboration between Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

**Notable for:**
- Blending indie rock with electronic elements
- Use of acoustic instruments (guitar, drums) combined with synthesizers
- Vocal layering and harmonies throughout
- Themes of love, loss, and uncertainty

**Standout tracks:**
1. **Such Great Heights** — Dreamy, synth-driven anthem with acoustic guitar melody; hopeful spirit
2. **The District Sleeps Alone Tonight** — Melancholy, introspective; about disconnection and insomnia
3. **Clark Gable** — Quirky, upbeat; charming lyrics about romance
4. **We Will Become Silhouettes** — Somber, reflective tone; about post-apocalyptic aftermath
5. **Recycled Air** — Prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar riff
6. **Sleeping In** — Vocal lines weaving with lush, orchestral chorus

**Contemporary artists in the same space:** Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada

**Reception:** Critical acclaim; helped define indie-electronic as a mainstream genre in early 2000s. Successfully bridged indie rock and electronic music scenes.
