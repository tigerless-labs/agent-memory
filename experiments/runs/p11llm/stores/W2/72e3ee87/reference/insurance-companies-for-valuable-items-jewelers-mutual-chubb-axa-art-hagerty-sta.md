Specialty companies: Jewelers Mutual (jewelry/heirlooms), Chubb (high-value items), AXA ART (art/antiques/collectibles), Hagerty (collectibles). Standard with specialty depts: State Farm, Allstate (Valuable Items), Liberty Mutual (Valuable Possessions). Look for: agreed value coverage, low/no deductible, worldwide coverage, mysterious disappearance coverage, work with independent brokers.
