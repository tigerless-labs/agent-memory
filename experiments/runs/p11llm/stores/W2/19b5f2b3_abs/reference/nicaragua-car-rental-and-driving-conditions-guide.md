## Car Rental Options in Managua
- International companies: Avis, Hertz, Enterprise
- Local car rental companies also available

## Driving Conditions in Nicaragua
- Narrow roads, heavy traffic
- Poorly maintained roads
- Limited road signs
- Recommended for drivers with experience in developing countries

## Documentation Required
- Valid driver's license from home country (good for up to 90 days)
- Credit card
- Passport

## Tips for Driving
- Have experience driving in developing countries recommended
- Exercise caution while driving
- Rent a car with GPS or download reliable map app for navigation
- Check with specific rental company for their exact requirements
