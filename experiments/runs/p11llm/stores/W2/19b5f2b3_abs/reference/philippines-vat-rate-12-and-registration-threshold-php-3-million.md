## VAT Rate
The Philippine VAT rate is **12%**.

## Calculation
To calculate VAT on a purchase: multiply the total cost by 0.12.
Example: PHP 1,000 × 0.12 = PHP 120 VAT; total with VAT = PHP 1,120.

## Registration & Requirements
**VAT-registered businesses:**
- Annual sales/gross receipts exceeding PHP 3 million
- Required to charge VAT on sales
- Must include VAT in invoices issued to customers
- Must file VAT returns and pay collected VAT to the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)

**Non-VAT-registered businesses:**
- Annual sales/gross receipts below PHP 3 million
- Not required to charge VAT
- Not required to include VAT in invoices
