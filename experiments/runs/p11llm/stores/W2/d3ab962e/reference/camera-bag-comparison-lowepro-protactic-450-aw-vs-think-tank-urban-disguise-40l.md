Two camera bags under consideration:\n\nLowepro ProTactic 450 AW: 4.3 lbs, 13.8x10.2x19.7 in, weather-resistant, holds DSLR with 400mm lens plus 7-8 lenses, modular design, multiple carry options\n\nThink Tank Urban Disguise 40L: 2.6 lbs, 11.4x7.3x17.3 in, water-resistant, holds DSLR with 24-70mm lens plus 2-3 lenses, sleek urban design, lighter and more compact\n\nKey trade-off: ProTactic has larger capacity and modularity; Urban Disguise is lighter and more discreet.
