Ann Leckie books exploring power, identity, and social themes:

**Imperial Radch Trilogy:**
- *Ancillary Justice* (2013) — Hugo, Nebula, Arthur C. Clarke award winner. Explores imperialism, power structures, and AI identity through Breq's quest for revenge against Anaander Mianaai.
- *Ancillary Sword* (2014) — Continues Breq's story navigating Radchaai politics and consequences of her actions.
- *Ancillary Mercy* (2015) — Concludes the trilogy with Breq facing Anaander Mianaai.

**Standalone Novels:**
- *Provenance* (2017) — Explores power, identity, and inheritance in different universe. Focuses on Ingray uncovering secrets about valuable artifacts and complex web of political intrigue.
- *The Raven Tower* (2019) — Fantasy novel (departure from sci-fi), follows a transgender man in conflict between gods and mortals.

**Short Stories:**
- *Ancillary Stories* (2015) — Collection set in Imperial Radch universe.
- *Consolation* (2018) — Novella expanding Imperial Radch universe.

All known for thought-provoking exploration of power, identity, and justice.
