Merkur HD uses double-edge (DE) blades - thin rectangular blades with sharp edge on both sides, typically stainless steel or platinum-coated.

**Cost:** Pack of 10-20 DE blades averages 5-20 USD, works out to 0.25-1.00 USD per blade.

**Lifespan:** Depends on:
- Shaving frequency: daily shaving = replace every 5-7 shaves; less frequent = 10-14 shaves per blade
- Shaving technique: improper technique shortens blade life
- Hair type: coarser hair requires more frequent replacement
- Blade quality: premium blades last longer

Average: replace every 5-10 shaves (every 1-2 weeks depending on shaving frequency).

**Tips:** Handle with care (very sharp), store safely away from children/pets, buy in bulk to save money, experiment with different brands to find best match.
