Monument Valley: Located on Arizona-Utah border in Navajo Tribal Park. Known for iconic sandstone formations. Activities include guided tours, scenic 17-mile loop drive, hiking trails, photography especially at sunrise/sunset. Four Corners: Where Arizona, New Mexico, Utah, and Colorado meet. Highlights include photo opportunity standing in all four states, monument, Navajo Nation Visitor Center. Both locations on Native American reservations requiring respectful visitation.
