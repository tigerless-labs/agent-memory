Weekly newsletter outline for teaching startup founders about community importance.

## Structure:
- **Opening:** Welcome framing around community's role in startup success
- **Section 1 – Why community matters:** Feedback validation, customer connection, momentum
- **Section 2 – How to build community:** Social engagement, blogging, events; emphasize authenticity and approachability
- **Section 3 – Success stories:** Examples (Airbnb, Dropbox, Tesla) with insights on approach and gains
- **Section 4 – Leverage for growth:** Use feedback to improve product, encourage UGC, referral incentives
- **Conclusion:** Key takeaways, invitation to share experiences and join community
- **Closing:** Call to follow on social media

Generic template usable for any startup community-focused newsletter.
