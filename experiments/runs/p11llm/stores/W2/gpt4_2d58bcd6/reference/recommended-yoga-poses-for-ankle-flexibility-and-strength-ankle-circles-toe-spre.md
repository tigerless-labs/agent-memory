**Yoga poses recommended for ankle flexibility and strength:**

1. Ankle Circles - Draw circles with ankle, small to large, 5-10 reps each direction
2. Toe Spreads - Spread toes apart and release, 10-15 reps
3. Ankle Alphabet - Draw alphabet letters with toes from A-Z
4. Calf Raises - On edge of stair/step, raise onto tiptoes then lower, 10-15 reps
5. Downward-Facing Dog - Stretches calves and ankles, hold 30 sec-1 min
6. Tree Pose - Balance on one leg with other foot on inner thigh, 30 sec-1 min per side
7. Eagle Pose - Cross one leg over other and wrap ankle around calf, hold 30 sec-1 min per side
8. Seated Forward Fold - Reach toward toes, 30 sec-1 min

**Additional ankle mobility exercises:**
- Ankle rotations (both directions, 5-10 reps)
- Toe curls (10-15 reps)
- Heel raises (10-15 reps)
