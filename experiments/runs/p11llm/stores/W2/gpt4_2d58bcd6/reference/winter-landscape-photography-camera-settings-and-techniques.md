Recommended settings for winter landscape photography: ISO 100-400 (low for quality), aperture f/8-f/11 for depth of field, shutter speed 1/125-1/250s, white balance Cloudy or Shade. Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop to capture snow details. Use wide-angle lens (10-24mm). Shoot during golden hour or overcast days. Use polarizing filter to reduce glare. Bracket shots for HDR. Focus on leading lines, patterns, textures. Get low to ground for texture. Use fast shutter (1/1000s) and wide aperture (f/2.8) to capture snowflakes. Include animal tracks in composition. Cold tips: bring spare batteries (cold drains quickly), dress warmly, use weather-sealed camera, allow 2-hour cooling before storing.
