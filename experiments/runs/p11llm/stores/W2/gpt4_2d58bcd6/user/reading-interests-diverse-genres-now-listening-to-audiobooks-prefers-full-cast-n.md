Reads across genres: historical fiction, contemporary fiction, psychological thrillers, character-driven narratives, mythology retellings. Recent fiction reads (May 2023): 'The Hate U Give' by Angie Thomas, 'The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo' by Taylor Jenkins Reid, 'The Silent Patient' by Alex Michaelides, 'The Nightingale' by Kristin Hannah. Also reads non-fiction: 'Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind' by Yuval Noah Harari.

Recently started listening to audiobooks during daily commute. Prefers full-cast narrations for dialogue-heavy novels with multiple characters for immersive experience, but also enjoys single narrators who bring unique tone and style. Audiobooks completed: 'Sapiens' (Yuval Noah Harari), 'Daisy Jones and The Six' (Taylor Jenkins Reid).

Believes narrator's tone, pace, and inflection significantly impact emotional response and connection to story.

Part of a book club. Recently read 'The Hate U Give' for book club meeting; was initially the only member not finished but completed it before the meeting date.
