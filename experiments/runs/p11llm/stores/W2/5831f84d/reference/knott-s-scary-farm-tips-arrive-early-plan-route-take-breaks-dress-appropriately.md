**Tips for experiencing Knott's Scary Farm:**\n\n1. **Arrive early** — Get there before opening to beat crowds and hit popular mazes first.\n2. **Plan your route** — Prioritize mazes/attractions and use the Knott's Scary Farm app to reserve spots and check wait times.\n3. **Take breaks** — Event can be intense; take time to grab food, rest, and recharge.\n4. **Dress appropriately** — Wear comfortable shoes and dress in layers; it can be chilly in the evening.\n5. **Explore scare zones** — Take time to interact with scare actors for full immersive experience.\n6. **Pace yourself** — Focus on 3-4 priority mazes rather than trying to do everything in one night to avoid burnout.\n7. **Check schedules** — Review Knott's Scary Farm website/app for maze schedules and special performances like "Cursed!" musical or "The Hanging" comedy show.\n\n**Coordinate group outings:**\n- Set date/time that works for everyone\n- Create group chat for communication\n- Designate meeting spot outside park\n- Ensure everyone has tickets ready\n- Plan food/drink strategy\n- Consider splitting up to hit different attractions, with set reconvene times\n- Stay in groups of at least 2-3 for safety
