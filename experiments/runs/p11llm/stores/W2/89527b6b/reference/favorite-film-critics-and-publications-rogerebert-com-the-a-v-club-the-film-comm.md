Favorite film critics and publications for reviews and film analysis:

- **RogerEbert.com** — Film reviews, essays, and criticism from renowned critics
- **The A.V. Club** — Film reviews, interviews, and features from experienced critics
- **The Film Comment** — In-depth film criticism, interviews, and essays

Appreciates these sources for their in-depth analysis and thoughtful critiques that go beyond simple ratings.
