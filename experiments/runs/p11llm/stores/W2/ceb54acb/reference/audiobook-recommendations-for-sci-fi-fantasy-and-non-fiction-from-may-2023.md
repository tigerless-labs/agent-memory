Sci-Fi & Fantasy Recommendations

Starting point (already liked):
- The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin — Chinese sci-fi, Hugo Award winner

Character-driven sci-fi similar to interests:
- The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude)

Fantasy with similar tone and style:
- The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard
- The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker
- The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty
- The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang

Other fantasy recommendations:
- The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North
- The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch
- The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison

Non-Fiction: Self-Improvement & Productivity

Self-Improvement:
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey
- Daring Greatly by Brené Brown
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck

Productivity:
- Deep Work by Cal Newport
- Essentialism by Greg McKeown
- Atomic Habits by James Clear

Other Non-Fiction:
- Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle
- Shoe Dog by Phil Knight

Narrator Notes:
Rachel Dulude is versatile, excels at characterization, skilled with accents and pacing, well-suited to character-driven stories.
