Ursula K. Le Guin is known for exploring ecological, social, and political issues through SF/fantasy. Major works:

- **The Dispossessed** (1974) - Anarchist commune on distant planet; explores anarchism as political system and human-environment relationships; critiques capitalist ideologies.
- **The Left Hand of Darkness** (1969) - Genderless planet inhabitants; explores gender, sexuality, power, and human-environment relationships.
- **The Word for World is Forest** (1976) - Conflict between human colonizers and native inhabitants; themes of imperialism, colonialism, and human-environment relations.
- **The Lathe of Heaven** (1971) - Dreams that change reality; critiques destructive human impact on environment and science/technology misuse.
- **Always Coming Home** (1985) - Post-apocalyptic California; explores environmentalism, ecology, and human-environment relationships through stories, songs, poetry.

Le Guin is influential in SF/fantasy and literature generally; known for exploring complex social/political issues, critiquing capitalism and destructive human impact on environment.
