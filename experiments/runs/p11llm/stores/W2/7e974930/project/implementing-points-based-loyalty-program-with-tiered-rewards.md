Decided to launch a customer loyalty program with the following structure:

**Program Type:** Points-based system
- Customers earn points for each purchase
- Points redeemable for discounts or free products

**Tier Structure:** Multiple tiers to reward frequent customers with exclusive benefits

**Sign-up Incentive:** Offering sign-up bonus to encourage enrollment

**Promotion Strategy:**
- Social media announcements
- Email newsletters to existing customers
- In-store promotions and signage
- Staff training: checkout staff will mention program to all customers

**Goals:** Increase repeat purchases, build customer loyalty, drive word-of-mouth marketing
