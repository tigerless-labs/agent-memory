## Types of Chemical Peels

**Superficial Peels (AHA - Glycolic, Lactic, Mandelic Acid)**
- Exfoliate top layer of dead skin cells
- Boost collagen production
- Reduce fine lines, sun damage, uneven texture
- Downtime: Minimal (mild redness/peeling subsides within hours to 1-2 days)
- Results last: Few weeks (requires repeat treatments every few months)

**Medium Peels (BHA - Salicylic Acid, TCA)**
- Penetrate deeper into pores
- Treat acne, blackheads, oily skin
- Treat fine wrinkles, sun damage, mild discoloration
- Downtime: Up to 1+ week (redness, swelling, peeling)
- Results last: Several months to years

**Deep Peels (Phenol)**
- Strongest type; targets dermis layer
- Stimulate collagen production and skin tightening
- For severe wrinkles and sun damage
- Downtime: Several weeks (significant peeling, crusting, redness)
- Results last: Several years

## Key Recommendations
- Professional application is superior to at-home kits
- At-home kits may be less effective and risk skin damage if misapplied
- Plan peel timing carefully before important events
- During healing: avoid direct sunlight, wear sunscreen, avoid picking skin
- Don't apply makeup immediately after peel (risk of irritation, infection, scarring)
- Maintenance requires regular skincare routine with sun protection and gentle exfoliation
- Consult skincare professional for personalized recommendations
