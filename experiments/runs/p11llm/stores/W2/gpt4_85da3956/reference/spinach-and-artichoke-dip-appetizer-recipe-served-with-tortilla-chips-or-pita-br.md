## Spinach & Artichoke Dip Recipe

**About**: A classic party favorite that's easy to make and always serves a crowd well. Works great as an appetizer for 6-8 people.

**Serving**: Serve with tortilla chips or pita bread for dipping.

**Why This Works for Parties**:
- Can be made ahead of time
- Universally enjoyed
- Easy for guests to eat while mingling and having conversations
- Reheats well or can be served cold depending on preference

**Variations**: Can add cream cheese, sour cream, Parmesan cheese, or other toppings depending on personal preference.

**Notes**: A crowd-pleaser that pairs well with other appetizers like bruschetta.
