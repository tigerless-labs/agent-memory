1. **Communicate beforehand** — Ask applicants if they need accommodations (wheelchair access, sign language interpretation, extra time). Follow up to confirm.

2. **Awareness of bias** — Base decisions on job-relevant skills, knowledge, and experience, not on disability.

3. **Provide reasonable accommodations** — Offer assistive technology, interpreters, or other requested accommodations.

4. **Create welcoming environment** — Ensure comfortable setting, clear directions, offer water/snacks/breaks.

5. **Train interviewers** — Educate staff on disability etiquette and communication best practices.

6. **Request feedback** — Ask applicants about their interview experience and implement their suggestions for future candidates.
