Case study on Airbnb covering its founding in 2008, growth through 2020, and COVID-19 impact.

**Topics:**
- History: founded 2008, grew to 220 countries with 7M properties by 2020
- Business model: technology platform for peer-to-peer room sharing; charges transaction fees (approx 11 percent)
- Market context: competes with hotels, motels, bed and breakfasts
- Industry costs: wages 26 percent, purchases 18 percent, depreciation 9 percent, marketing 2 percent, rent/utilities 7 percent, other 27 percent
- Financial performance: 31 billion valuation by 2018; first profitability in 2016; 3 billion revenue and 90 million profit by 2017
- Challenge: COVID-19 pandemic threatened business model dependent on travel bookings
