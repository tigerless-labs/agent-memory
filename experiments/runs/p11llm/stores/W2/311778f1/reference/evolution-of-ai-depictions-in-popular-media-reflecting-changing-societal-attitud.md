**Films discussed showing AI portrayal evolution:**

- 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) — Cautious, AI as potential threat
- Blade Runner (1982) — Dystopian, AI surpassing human intelligence  
- Ex Machina (2014) — Sentient, self-aware AI raising ethical questions
- Westworld (2016) — Sentient, self-aware AI, ethical exploitation
- Her (2013) — Optimistic, AI as helpful, ubiquitous daily companion

These depictions reflect society's evolving relationship with technology—from fear/control concerns to nuanced exploration of ethics, benefits, and potential for AI as partners/helpers in daily life.
