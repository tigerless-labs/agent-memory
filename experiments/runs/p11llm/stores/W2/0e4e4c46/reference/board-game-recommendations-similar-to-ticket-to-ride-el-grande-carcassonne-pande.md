Games recommended as similar to Ticket to Ride in terms of strategy and complexity level:\n\n- **El Grande** - Area control and strategy\n- **Carcassonne** - Tile-laying game with medieval landscape building\n- **Pandemic** - Cooperative game with strategic thinking\n- **7 Wonders** - Card-drafting civilization builder\n- **Azul** - Tile-laying with resource management and planning\n- **Scythe** - Worker placement, resource management, area control (90-120 min, 1-5 players, plays well with 4-5)\n  - Designer: Jamey Stegmaier\n  - Expansions: Invaders from Afar, Wind Gambit, The Rise of Fenris\n- **Castles of Burgundy** - Resource management and tile-laying estate building
