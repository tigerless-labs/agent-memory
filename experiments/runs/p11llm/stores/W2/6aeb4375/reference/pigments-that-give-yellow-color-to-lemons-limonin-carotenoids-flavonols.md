The primary pigments responsible for lemon color are:

- **Limonin** — a flavonoid present in the peel and outer layers
- **Carotenoids** — contribute to yellow coloration
- **Flavonols** — additional pigments contributing to color

These pigments are concentrated in the lemon peel and outer layers, creating the bright yellow appearance.
