**Organizations that certify jewelry appraisers:**

- **NAJA** (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers) — Non-profit that certifies jewelry appraisers and sets industry standards
- **AGTA** (American Gem Trade Association) — Trade association certifying gemologists and appraisers; focuses on gemstones and jewelry
- **ASA** (American Society of Appraisers) — Multidisciplinary organization certifying appraisers in various fields including jewelry

**What to look for in a jewelry appraiser:**
- Certified by NAJA, AGTA, or ASA
- Experience with vintage/antique jewelry similar to your items
- Impartial (not affiliated with jewelry stores or dealers)
- Professional affiliations and membership in industry associations
- Recognition by insurance companies
- Strong referrals and online reputation

**Appraisal report should include:**
- Detailed description (materials, dimensions, characteristics)
- Appraised value (may include a range)
- Methodology used to determine value
- Supporting photos and diagrams

**Factors affecting diamond necklace appraisal value:**
- Type and quality of diamonds (carat weight, cut, color, clarity)
- Metal type and purity (gold, platinum, silver, etc.)
- Craftsmanship and design era
- Overall condition (damage, wear, needed repairs)
- Rarity and uniqueness
- Current market demand
- Comparable sales
