Evaluating whether installing solar panels is worth the investment. Key factors to weigh:

- **Cost**: Varies by home size, roof sunlight exposure, and panel type
- **Energy savings**: Depends on energy usage and local electricity rates
- **Environmental impact**: Reduces carbon footprint
- **Maintenance**: Requires regular upkeep due to weather exposure
- **Government incentives**: Tax credits and rebates may apply depending on location
