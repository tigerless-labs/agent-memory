## Cherokee Nation Language Program
- Developed robust online language resources during COVID-19 pandemic
- Offers virtual language classes, podcasts, videos, and social media pages
- Provides opportunities for learning Cherokee language globally

## Cultural Preservation Methods
- Online language classes and digital resources
- Virtual cultural events via video conferencing (e.g., Zoom)
- Social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) for showcasing culture
- Digital storytelling through online videos, virtual tours, podcasts
- Drive-through events for traditional practices with safe distancing
- Online versions of powwows and cultural celebrations

## Funding & Support
- Cherokee Nation Cultural Tourism Department provides grants to Cherokee artists and small cultural businesses

## Ways to Support Cherokee Culture from Outside
1. Learn about Cherokee history and culture (books, documentaries, cultural events)
2. Support Cherokee artists and businesses (purchase art, crafts, and items)
3. Donate to organizations dedicated to Cherokee culture preservation
4. Advocate for indigenous rights (land rights, environmental protection, healthcare access)
