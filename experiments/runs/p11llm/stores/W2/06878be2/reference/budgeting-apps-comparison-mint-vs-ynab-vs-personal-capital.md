**Mint**: Free, user-friendly, auto-categorization, bill tracking, credit score monitoring. Recommended for free, simple expense tracking.

**YNAB (You Need a Budget)**: $6.99/month or $83.99/year (34-day free trial). Focuses on assigning jobs to every dollar, prioritizing spending, debt repayment planning. Strong budgeting methodology with workshops and support.

**Personal Capital**: Free, investment-focused, net worth tracking, retirement planning, financial analysis. Best for those wanting comprehensive financial picture.

User chose Mint for its free cost and user-friendly interface.
