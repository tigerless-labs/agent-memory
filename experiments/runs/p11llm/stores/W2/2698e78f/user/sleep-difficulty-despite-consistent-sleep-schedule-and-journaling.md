Has difficulty falling asleep on some nights despite maintaining a consistent sleep schedule and using journaling. Received recommendations including bedroom environment optimization, relaxation routines, limiting screens before bed, stress management techniques, exercise timing, avoiding caffeine/nicotine/alcohol, aromatherapy, morning light exposure, and potential supplements like melatonin or magnesium.
