## Websites/Platforms\n- **Tennis.com** - Instructional content, video lessons, articles, podcasts\n- **Essential Tennis** - Video lessons, drills, exercises for forehand, backhand, serve, volleys\n- **Tennis Warehouse University** - Video lessons and tutorials on technique, strategy, equipment\n- **YouTube Channels**: Tennis Lessons 365, Tennis Coach 365, The Tennis Vault\n- **CoachTube** - Online coaching platform with one-on-one lessons\n- **Tennis Coach Pro** - Online coaching platform with one-on-one lessons\n\n## Notable Coaches\n- **Brett Hobden** - Expertise in technique and strategy\n- **Jeff Salzenstein** - Former ATP player; expertise in technique, strategy, mental toughness\n- **Ian Westermann** - Expertise in technique, strategy, junior development\n\n## Tips for Online Lessons\n- Set clear goals and communicate them\n- Be consistent with scheduling\n- Use video analysis to identify improvement areas\n- Communicate openly with instructors\n- Online lessons are best as supplement to in-person coaching, not replacement
