## 8 Engagement Strategies

1. **Identify Community Leaders** — Communicate with cultural group leaders to spread awareness and create interest
2. **Partner with Local Organizations** — Activate networks and resources through community institutions
3. **Offer Multilingual Communication** — Provide materials in community languages to demonstrate inclusivity
4. **Conduct a Needs Assessment** — Get input from community members to tailor programming to their needs
5. **Provide Food from Different Cultures** — Serve diverse cuisines to demonstrate inclusivity beyond main culture
6. **Cultural Celebrations** — Integrate different cultural performances (African drumming, Mexican mariachi, Chinese Lion dance, etc.)
7. **Create an Inclusive Environment** — Design safe, welcoming spaces for all backgrounds
8. **Cultivate Relationships** — Build long-term community relationships for deeper understanding of needs

## Example Events

1. **Culture Fest** (Spokane, Washington) — Annual celebration of cultural diversity with food, performances, traditional clothing
2. **National Night Out** — Community-building campaign bringing neighbors from diverse backgrounds together with law enforcement
3. **Chicago's World Music Festival** — Features global musical styles with master classes and interactive workshops
4. **Dia de los Muertos** (Santa Ana, California) — Mexican holiday celebration breaking cultural barriers through inclusive festivities
5. **Chinatown Street Fest** (San Francisco) — Celebrates Chinese culture through food, music, art, and dance
