Comprehensive strategies for improving sleep quality and setting healthy boundaries:

Sleep strategies: bedroom environment optimization (dark, quiet, cool 60-67F), relaxation routines (reading, music, gentle stretching, warm bath), screen time limits (1 hour before bed), stress management (deep breathing, progressive relaxation, meditation), exercise timing (none 3-4 hours before bed), avoiding caffeine/nicotine/alcohol in afternoon/evening, aromatherapy (lavender, chamomile, valerian), morning light exposure, supplements like melatonin or magnesium.

Boundary-setting strategies: recognize limits, identify values, practice saying no in low-stakes situations, use the 3Rs (Reflect, Respond, Renegotiate), offer alternatives, be firm but polite, remember that no is a complete sentence, prepare for pushback, celebrate successes, seek support from trusted people.
