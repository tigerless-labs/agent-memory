User's signature cocktail created for a summer dinner party. Based on Lavender Honey Spritz recipe but made with their basic simple syrup instead of lavender simple syrup. The name evokes warm, relaxing summer evenings. Recipe: 1.5 oz gin, 0.5 oz basic simple syrup, 0.5 oz lemon juice, 2 oz Prosecco, splash of soda water, garnished with edible flowers or lavender sprig. The neutral simple syrup allows the honey and gin flavors to shine through while keeping the drink light and refreshing.
