Key resources shared for learning about Yanomami: Books — The Falling Sky by Davi Kopenawa (shaman autobiography), Shadows of the Forest by Chagnon (ethnography), Yanomami: Account of Last Days by Ramos. Documentaries — The Yanomami: A Struggle for Survival (1991), The Last Shaman (2016), Embrace of the Serpent (2015). Organizations: Amazon Watch, Cultural Survival, Survival International.
