Teurgoule: A sweet rice pudding made with milk, cinnamon, and sugar, often baked in a ceramic dish. A creamy, comforting dessert option. Calvados Soufflé: A light and airy soufflé made with Calvados, the region's famous apple brandy. A sophisticated dessert option.
