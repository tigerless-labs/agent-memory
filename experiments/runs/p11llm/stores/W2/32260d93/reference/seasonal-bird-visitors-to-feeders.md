Winter (Dec-Feb): American Goldfinch, Common Redpoll, Purple Finch, American Tree Sparrow, Dark-eyed Junco, White-throated Sparrow, Downy/Hairy/Red-bellied Woodpeckers, Blue Jays. Spring/fall migrants (Mar-May, Aug-Nov): Yellow Warbler, Yellow-rumped Warbler, American Goldfinch, Ruby-throated Hummingbird, Baltimore/Orchard Orioles. Year-round: Northern Cardinals, Black-capped/Carolina Chickadees, Tufted Titmice, Bridled Titmice. Best offerings: Nyjer seed, suet, sunflower seeds, orange slices, jelly.
