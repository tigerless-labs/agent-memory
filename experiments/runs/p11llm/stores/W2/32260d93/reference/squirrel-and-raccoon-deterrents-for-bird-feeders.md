Squirrel-proofing: weight-activated perches, cage-like enclosures, squirrel-resistant materials, physical baffles, position feeder 10+ feet from trees/fences. Raccoon-proofing: sturdy 6-foot pole anchored in ground, heavy-duty feeders with secure lids, raccoon-specific baffles, move feeder away from water sources, take down feeder at night. General tips: keep area clean, use safflower or hot-pepper seeds, visual deterrents (reflective objects), rotate feeders.
