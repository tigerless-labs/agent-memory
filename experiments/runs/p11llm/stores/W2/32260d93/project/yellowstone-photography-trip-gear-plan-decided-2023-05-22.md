For Yellowstone trip planned for summer 2023, decided to bring:
- Nikon D500 camera body
- 70-200mm lens
- Compact polarizing filter (for glare reduction and color enhancement)
- Spare EN-EL15a battery

Also purchased Nikon MC-36a Multi-Function Remote Cord for remote shutter release capability. Confirmed these items after attending photography workshop where tested new equipment and tried remote shutter release, which convinced them of its value.
