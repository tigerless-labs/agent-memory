Decided to begin with Headspace's 'Take10' program—a 10-day guided meditation introduction designed for mindfulness beginners. Plan to integrate meditation into existing daily podcast routine. After Take10, interested in exploring the Basics Pack, particularly the Clarity meditations (for mental clarity and focus) and Motivation meditations (for building confidence and goal-setting). Tips for integration: meditate at same time daily, find quiet space, use app for tracking progress, combine with podcast sessions (before/after/between episodes).
