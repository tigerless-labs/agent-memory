Thai font recommendations that convey luxury and modern style:

1. **Kanit** – contemporary sans-serif by Cadson Demak; geometric shapes and clean lines for luxury and modern projects
2. **Sarabun** – modern serif by Suppakit Chalermlarp; clean, elegant, highly legible for luxury branding and editorial
3. **Maitree** – modern serif by Cadson Demak; balanced design with versatile weights for luxurious typography
4. **Krub** – geometric sans-serif by Cadson Demak; sleek and luxurious appearance for high-end branding
5. **Chonburi** – contemporary sans-serif by Cadson Demak; slightly condensed and bold, suitable for luxury design
