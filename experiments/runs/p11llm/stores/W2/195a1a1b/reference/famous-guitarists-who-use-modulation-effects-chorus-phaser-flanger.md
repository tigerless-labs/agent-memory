## Guitarists and modulation effects

- **David Gilmour** (Pink Floyd): Uses chorus and phaser effects on classic solos like 'Comfortably Numb' and 'Shine On You Crazy Diamond'
- **Eddie Van Halen**: Used flanger and phaser effects to create his signature 'brown sound' tone
- **The Edge** (U2): Frequently uses delay and chorus effects to create his signature ethereal guitar sound
- **Andy Summers** (The Police): Often uses chorus and flanger effects to create complex, layered guitar parts
- **Jimi Hendrix**: Used a uni-vibe pedal to create a phaser-like effect on songs like 'Machine Gun' and 'Star Spangled Banner'
