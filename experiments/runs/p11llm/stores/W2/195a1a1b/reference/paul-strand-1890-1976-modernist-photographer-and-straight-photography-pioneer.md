American photographer (1890-1976) who pioneered the modernist movement and straight photography.

**Career highlights:**
- Born in New York City; largely self-taught
- Joined Photo-Secession group under Alfred Stieglitz in 1915
- Transitioned to documentary photography in the 1920s
- Documented rural communities, urban landscapes, and industrial sites
- Famous for Mexico series beginning 1932 — considered some of the most important documentary photography of the period

**Philosophy:** Committed to using photography as a tool for social change and raising awareness of social injustices. His work characterized by simplicity, directness, and social purpose.

**Legacy:** Recognized as one of the leading photographers of his generation; continues to influence modern photography for innovative style, social relevance, and impact on modernist development.
