**The Goonies (1985)** - Action-adventure about kids on a treasure hunt. Suitable for ages 8+. Features Sean Astin, Josh Brolin, Corey Feldman. Mild language, brief violence, themes of friendship and teamwork.\n\n**The NeverEnding Story (1984)** - Fantasy about a boy discovering a magical book. Suitable for ages 7+, but contains mature themes (loss, fear, world ending) and some scary scenes. May be too intense for sensitive younger children.\n\n**Other recommendations given:**\n1. E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982)\n2. The Princess Bride (1987)\n3. Ghostbusters (1984)\n4. Home Alone (1990)\n5. Matilda (1996)\n6. The Sandlot (1993)\n7. The Muppet Movie (1979)\n8. Indiana Jones and the Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981)\n\nThese were suggested as classic family movies suitable for childhood nostalgia marathons.
