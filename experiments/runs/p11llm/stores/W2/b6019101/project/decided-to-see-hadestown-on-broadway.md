After comparing Hadestown with The Inheritance, chose to see Hadestown on Broadway based on its emotional resonance and thematic focus on love and sacrifice. Drawn to the folk-opera style and the retelling of the Orpheus and Eurydice myth in a contemporary setting. Interested in experiencing the integration of music and storytelling.
