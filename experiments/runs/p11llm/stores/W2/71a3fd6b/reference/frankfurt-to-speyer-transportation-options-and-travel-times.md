Multiple transport options available between Frankfurt and Speyer, Germany:

**By car:** ~1 hour 10 minutes
- Rent at Frankfurt Airport or city center
- Route: A67 and A61 highways

**By train:** ~1 hour 20 minutes
- Depart: Frankfurt am Main Hauptbahnhof
- Arrive: Speyer Hauptbahnhof
- Multiple direct and indirect trains available throughout the day

**By bus:** ~1 hour 30 minutes
- Several bus companies operate between the two cities

**By taxi:** ~1 hour
- Depart from Frankfurt Airport or city center
- Most expensive option
