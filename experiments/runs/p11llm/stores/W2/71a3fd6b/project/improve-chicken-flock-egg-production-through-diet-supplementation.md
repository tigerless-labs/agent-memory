## Current Situation
- Flock experiencing declining egg production
- Currently using layer pellets as primary feed
- Coop maintenance is good (clean bedding with fresh straw)

## Diet Improvement Plan
The following supplements and additions are being introduced:

### Calcium supplements
- Oyster shells (crushed, offered free-choice): 1-2 cups per 10 hens per week
- Dark leafy greens: kale, spinach, collard greens offered as treats

### Probiotics for gut health
- Plain yogurt (unsweetened): added once per week to feed
- Fermented grains (fermented oats): regular addition to diet

### Fresh produce for nutrition and variety
- Fruits: apples (seeds/cores removed), berries (blueberries, strawberries, raspberries)
- Vegetables: carrots, broccoli, cauliflower, cooked sweet potatoes, pumpkins, squash
- Guideline: treats should not exceed 10% of total diet

## Next Steps
1. Review current layer pellet label to verify calcium and nutrient content
2. Introduce supplements gradually to prevent digestive upset
3. Monitor flock behavior, droppings, and egg production for improvements
4. Consult veterinarian if concerns persist

## Key Nutritional Targets for Laying Hens
- Protein: 16-18%
- Fat: 2-3%
- Calcium: 3-4%
- Fiber: 2-4%
- Always provide fresh, clean water for optimal nutrient absorption
