Peace lily has been in current pot for ~2 years. Recently acquired new pot (1-2 sizes larger) and fresh potting mix to repot. Key repotting steps learned: water day before repotting, gently tease apart root-bound roots, place potting mix layer in new pot, spread roots, fill sides, water thoroughly after. Avoid over-potting to prevent root rot. Plan: repot in spring/summer during active growth.
