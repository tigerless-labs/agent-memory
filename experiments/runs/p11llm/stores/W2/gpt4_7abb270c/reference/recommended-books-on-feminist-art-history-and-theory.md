**Key books on feminist art:**

1. **"The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard**: Comprehensive history of feminist art from 1960s-1990s
2. **"Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss**: Explores relationship between feminist art and maternal; features artists Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles
3. **"Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin**: Collection of essays by renowned art historian; covers representation of women in art, feminism's role in art history
4. **"The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard**: Detailed examination of US feminist art movement during 1970s
5. **"Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls**: Collection of essays and artworks by anonymous feminist art collective known for bold, humorous critiques of art world

**Key documentaries:**

1. **"!Women Art Revolution" (2010)**: Lynn Hershman Leeson; features 40+ feminist artists including Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, Carolee Schneemann
2. **"The Heretics" (2009)**: Documentary on Heresies Collective; features Nancy Azara, Miriam Schapiro
3. **"Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991)**: Kate Horsfield documentary series; features Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, Yvonne Rainer
4. **"Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010)**: Panel discussion with Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell; moderated by Huey Copeland
5. **"Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014)**: PBS Art:21 episode; in-depth interview with Judy Chicago, pioneer of feminist art
