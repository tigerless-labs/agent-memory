**Popular art platforms for news, trends, and exhibitions:**

1. **Artsy**: Comprehensive platform; news, exhibitions, artist profiles, market trends
2. **Hyperallergic**: Leading art blog; news, reviews, opinion pieces on contemporary art; focus on New York City
3. **Artnet**: Go-to source for art market news, auction results, exhibitions; features emerging artists and trends
4. **The Art Newspaper**: Daily online publication; international art news, exhibitions, market analysis
5. **Contemporary Art Daily**: Resource for discovering new artists and exhibitions; focus on contemporary art
6. **Art in America**: Magazine; contemporary art features, reviews, interviews
7. **Frieze**: Leading art magazine; contemporary art, culture, politics; focus on international exhibitions and events
8. **Apollo Magazine**: British art magazine; art, architecture, collecting; focus on historical and contemporary art
9. **Art21**: Non-profit organization; produces documentaries and online content on contemporary art and artists
10. **Culture Trip**: Website; art, culture, travel coverage; focus on emerging artists and underground scenes
