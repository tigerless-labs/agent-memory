**Artists working in interactive/immersive installations:**

- **Carsten Höller**: German artist; known for interactive installations featuring slides, Ferris wheels, playful elements
- **Anicka Yi**: Korean-American artist; creates immersive, scent-based installations exploring relationships between art, science, and technology
- **Rafael Lozano-Hemmer**: Mexican-Canadian artist; creates interactive installations using biometrics, robotics, technologies exploring surveillance and control themes

**Exhibition spaces and collectives:**

- **Meow Wolf**: Santa Fe-based collective; creates immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, storytelling
- **The Color Factory**: San Francisco-based; interactive installations exploring emotional/psychological effects of color
- **TeamLab**: Japanese collective; immersive exhibitions using technology and light to create interactive environments

**Online resources:**
- Artspace: digital platform for interactive/immersive art experiences, articles, interviews
- The Creators Project: showcases interactive/immersive art, music, technology projects worldwide
- Hyperallergic: online art publication with articles/reviews of interactive/immersive exhibitions
