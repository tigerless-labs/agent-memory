Sample email identified as effective in nonprofit/fundraising sector. Key elements that work well: (1) Personalization by name showing research on organization/position, (2) Demonstrate knowledge of their specific context and challenges, (3) Present common problem (e.g., high turnover, retention challenges), (4) Offer solution positioning without hard sell, (5) Lead with unusual/effective approach positioning, (6) Clear CTA inviting short exploratory conversation, (7) Name recipient again in closing CTA. This structure led to successful booking.
