## Narita Airport to Shinjuku Station\n\n**Option 1: JR Narita Express (N'EX)**\n- Direct train to Shinjuku Station\n- Travel time: 60-70 minutes\n- Cost: ~3200-3500 yen (~30-35 USD)\n- More expensive but comfortable and convenient\n\n**Option 2: Keisei Skyliner plus Transfer**\n- Keisei Skyliner to Ueno Station (40-50 minutes)\n- Transfer to JR Yamanote Line to Shinjuku (20-30 minutes)\n- Total time: 50-60 minutes\n- Cost: ~2500-3000 yen (~25-30 USD)\n- Budget-friendly option\n\n**Option 3: Keisei Limited Express plus Transfer**\n- Travel time: 70-80 minutes\n- Cost: ~2000-2500 yen (~20-25 USD)\n- Most budget-friendly option\n\n**Using Suica Card:**\n- Touch card at ticket gate when entering station\n- Tap out at destination station  \n- Fare deducted automatically from card balance\n- Works on all train lines\n\n**Tips:**\n- Check train schedules before travel\n- Arrive at airport with time to purchase/charge Suica card\n- Keep belongings close on crowded trains\n- Ask station staff if assistance needed\n- Consider airport shuttle service if available (fee applies)
