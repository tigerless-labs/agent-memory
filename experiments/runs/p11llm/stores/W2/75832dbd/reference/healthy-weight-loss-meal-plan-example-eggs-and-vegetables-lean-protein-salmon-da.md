Example daily meal plan for weight loss with portion control and nutrient-dense foods:

**Breakfast**
- 2 scrambled eggs with spinach, mushrooms, and red peppers

**Snack**
- 1 small apple with 1 tablespoon almond butter

**Lunch**
- Grilled chicken breast with mixed greens, cucumbers, tomatoes, and balsamic vinaigrette

**Snack**
- 1 cup mixed berries with 1/2 cup non-fat Greek yogurt

**Dinner**
- Baked salmon with roasted asparagus and sweet potato

**Optional Snack**
- 1 piece dark chocolate

**Key principles:**
- Focus on portion control
- Choose nutrient-dense foods that are low in calories
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day
