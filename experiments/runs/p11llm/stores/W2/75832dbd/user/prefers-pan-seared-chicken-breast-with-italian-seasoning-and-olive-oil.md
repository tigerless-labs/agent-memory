For meal prep: pan-sears chicken breast at medium-high heat (400°F) for 5-7 minutes per side until internal temp reaches 165°F. Uses Italian-style seasoning (oregano, basil, thyme, rosemary) with olive oil. Had overcooked chicken previously; uses thermometer to avoid this mistake.
