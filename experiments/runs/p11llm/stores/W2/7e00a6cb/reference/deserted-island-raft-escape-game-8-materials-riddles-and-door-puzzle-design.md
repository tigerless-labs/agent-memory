2. Rope - Found in abandoned shipwreck, tangled among debris
3. Fabric - Found in ruined building, torn from old curtain or tablecloth
4. Vines - Found in jungle area, hanging from tree or climbing up wall
5. Rocks - Found on beach, hidden among sand or near water
6. Shells - Found on beach, washed up on shore or buried in sand
7. Tar - Found in tar pit or oil spill, carefully extracted with right tools
8. Plastic bottles - Found in trash pile or littered on beach
