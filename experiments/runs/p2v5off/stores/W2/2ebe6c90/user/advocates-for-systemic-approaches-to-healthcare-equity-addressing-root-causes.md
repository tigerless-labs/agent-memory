Believes healthcare inequalities reflect broader social/economic issues (poverty, discrimination, lack of education) and require proactive systemic change, not reactive crisis response. Supports innovative healthcare delivery approaches (telemedicine, mobile clinics) alongside policy changes. Frustrated that governments prioritize healthcare equity only during crises like COVID-19 rather than before problems become emergencies. Emphasizes that tackling root causes and working collaboratively across government, institutions, and community organizations is essential for sustainable progress.
