Comprehensive book recommendations (2023-01-31) for character-driven historical fiction with emotional depth similar to 'The Nightingale':

**Particularly interested:**
- 'The Song of Achilles' by Madeline Miller (Greek mythology retelling; intimate character focus)
- 'The Kitchen House' by Kathleen Grissom (1800s American South; complex relationships)

**WWII/Historical war fiction:**
- All the Light We Cannot See by Anthony Doerr
- The Alice Network by Kate Quinn (WWI; female spies; real historical Alice Network organization)
- The Lost Girls of Paris by Pam Jenoff
- The Women in the Castle by Jessica Shattuck
- Those Who Save Us by Jenna Blum
- The Huntress by Kate Quinn
- We Were the Lucky Ones by Georgia Hunter
- The Postmistress by Sarah Blake
- The Girl You Left Behind by Jojo Moyes

**Other periods:**
- The Red Tent by Anita Diamant (biblical/ancient Mesopotamia)
- The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society by Mary Ann Shaffer (post-WWII epistolary)

**Writing styles:**
- Madeline Miller: lyrical, poetic prose; intimate introspective narrative; classical scholar; character-driven focus on complex relationships
- Kathleen Grissom: emotionally authentic; character-driven; meticulously researched; accessible prose

Common appeal: strong character development, emotional depth, immersive historical settings.
