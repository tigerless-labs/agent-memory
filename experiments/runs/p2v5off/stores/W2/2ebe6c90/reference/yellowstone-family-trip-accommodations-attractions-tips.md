## Accommodations\n- **Canyon Lodge & Cabins**: Heart of park, close to Grand Canyon of Yellowstone River, general store, restaurants, visitor center\n- **Grant Village**: Shores of Yellowstone Lake, lakeside rooms and cabins\n- **Yellowstone Gateway Inn**: West Yellowstone, just outside west entrance\n\n## Key Attractions\n- **Geysers**: Old Faithful, Grand Geyser (200ft eruptions, every 7-10 hours), Castle Geyser (10-12 hours), Daisy Geyser (3-4 hours, 80ft)\n- **Hot Springs**: Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine formations, stroller-friendly), Norris Geyser Basin (Porcelain Basin, Emerald Spring), Boiling River (near Mammoth)\n- **Wildlife Viewing**: Lamar Valley ("Serengeti of North America"), Slough Creek (grizzly bears in early summer), Tower-Roosevelt Area, Mammoth Hot Springs\n- **Grand Canyon of Yellowstone**: Brink of Lower Falls Trail (3/4 mi), Seven Mile Hole Trail (10 mi), South Rim Trail (6 mi)\n\n## Tips\n- Book accommodations early (peak season June-August)\n- Keep 100+ yard distance from bears\n- Best wildlife viewing: early morning and late evening\n- Bring binoculars, layers, sunscreen, comfortable hiking shoes\n- Stay on designated trails and boardwalks\n- Plan regular breaks for kids during driving
