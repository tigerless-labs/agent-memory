## Entertainment\n- Audiobooks and podcasts (Audible, Scribd)\n- Road trip bingo (cows, billboards, rest stops, etc.)\n- Car games: I Spy, 20 Questions, Would You Rather, License Plate Tag\n- Tablets and handheld games with headphones\n- Car karaoke with kid-friendly playlists\n\n## Snacks & Breaks\n- Set up snack station with healthy options\n- Mystery snack box with surprise treats\n- Regular pit stops at rest stops, parks, or attractions to stretch and refuel\n\n## Engagement\n- Scavenger hunts (different car types, roadside attractions, cloud shapes)\n- Travel journals for writing, drawing, collecting souvenirs\n- Let each child take turns choosing music, picking games, selecting snacks\n\n## General Tips\n- Be flexible and adapt to kids' moods and interests\n- Regular breaks prevent boredom and restlessness
