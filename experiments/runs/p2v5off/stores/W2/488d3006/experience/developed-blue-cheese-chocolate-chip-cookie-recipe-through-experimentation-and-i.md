Created recipe by experimenting with chocolate chip cookie variations, aiming to add a savory element. Inspired by a blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle tasted at a local chocolate shop. Initial attempts failed (blue cheese flavor too strong and pungent). Through several refinements, found the right balance where the blue cheese is subtle and intriguing rather than overpowering, complementing the semisweet chocolate.
