Ingredients: 2.25c flour, 1tsp baking soda, 1tsp salt, 1c butter, 1c sugar, 0.5c blue cheese (crumbled), 2 eggs, 2tsp vanilla, 2c semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375°F for 12-15 min. Inspired by blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local chocolate shop. Took several iterations to balance flavors correctly. Final version: subtle blue cheese (not overpowering) paired with sweet chocolate.
