Recommended vintage and thrift shops in Porto Alegre:

- **Armazém do Passado**: Vintage clothing, accessories, décor, plus variety of vinyl records (Brazilian music, rock, pop from various decades)
- **Casa da Sogra**: Retro/vintage home décor and furniture, plus selection of vintage vinyl records including rare titles
- **Brechó Municipal**: Municipal marketplace with affordable used clothes, shoes, accessories (may have small vinyl selection)
- **Bazar Vintage**: Stylish, fashion-forward store with vintage/retro clothing and accessories
- **Pinga do Brechó**: Vintage/thrift with clothing, accessories, kitchenware (may have some vinyl, but not a specialty)

Armazém do Passado and Casa da Sogra noted as best for vinyl record selection.
