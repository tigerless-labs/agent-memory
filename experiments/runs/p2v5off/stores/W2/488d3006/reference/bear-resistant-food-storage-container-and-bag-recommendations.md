Bear-resistant container brands recommended: Garcia Bear-Resistant Container, BearVault, Ursack. Bear-resistant bags: Ursack Major, Counter Assault Bear Bag, Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag. Look for IGBC certification, durable puncture-resistant material, and secure design.
