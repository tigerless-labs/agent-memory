Must-see: The Narrows, Angels Landing, Emerald Pools, Weeping Rock. Popular hikes: Angels Landing (5 mi, strenuous), West Rim Trail (10 mi), Observation Point Trail (8 mi), Kayenta Trail (2 mi), Watchman Trail (3 mi). Tips: use free shuttle service, bring water and sunscreen, wear sturdy hiking shoes, check permits, respect trails.
