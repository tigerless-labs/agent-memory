Recommended boots for Zion-type terrain: Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof, Keen Targhee II Mid WP, Salomon Quest 4D 3 GTX, La Sportiva Raptor GTX. Look for: water resistance or waterproofing, ankle support, aggressive tread for traction, breathability. Break in new boots by wearing around town first, start with short hikes, gradually increase difficulty.
