Restaurants: Late for the Train Cafe (breakfast/lunch, Train Wrecks burritos), Proper Meats + Provisions (gourmet deli), Fat Olives (Italian), The Toasted Owl (brunch), Big Foot BBQ. Accommodations: Little America Flagstaff (mid-range, pool, free breakfast), Hotel Monte Vista (historic), Flagstaff Inn and Suites (budget), The Inn at Flagstaff (B&B). Also Flagstaff KOA camping. Book well in advance for winter holidays.
