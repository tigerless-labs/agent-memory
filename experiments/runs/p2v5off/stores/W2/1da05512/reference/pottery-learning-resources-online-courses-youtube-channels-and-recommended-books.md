## Online Platforms & Websites\n- **Craftsy**: Online courses on pottery and ceramics\n- **Pottery Making Illustrated**: Step-by-step tutorials, articles, and videos on pottery techniques\n- **The Potter's Council**: Website with pottery tutorials and community resources\n- **Ceramic Arts Network**: Online community and resource for ceramic artists with tutorials on coiling, scoring, glazing, and firing\n\n## YouTube Channels\n- **Simon Leach Pottery**: Tutorials on pottery techniques by renowned potter Simon Leach\n- **Pottery By Osa**: Pottery tutorials including coiling, scoring, and scoring/slipping techniques by Osa Atoe\n- **The Ceramic School**: Pottery tutorials on coiling, scoring, vase-making taught by experienced ceramic artists\n\n## Recommended Books\n- **The Potter's Bible** by Marylin Scott: Comprehensive guide to ceramics including glazing and firing\n- **Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter** by Ian Gregory: Detailed guide to glaze formulation and application\n\n## Forum Communities\n- **Ceramic Artists Network**: Community forum for ceramic artists with discussions on glazing and firing\n- **Pottery Making Illustrated**: Website forum for potters to discuss techniques
