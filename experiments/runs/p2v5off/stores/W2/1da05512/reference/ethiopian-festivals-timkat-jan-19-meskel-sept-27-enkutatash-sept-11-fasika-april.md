Timkat (Jan 19): Religious festival marking baptism of Jesus, with processions, music, dancing, and water splashing. Meskel (Sept 27): Commemorates finding of the True Cross, includes bonfires, feasting, and traditional dancing. Enkutatash (Sept 11): Ethiopian New Year with feasting, dancing, and gift exchange. Fasika (April): Ethiopian Orthodox Easter with fasting, prayer, processions, and feasting.
