Campgrounds with electric hookups at Grand Canyon South Rim: Mather Campground (327 sites, flush toilets, showers, laundry via recreation.gov), Trailer Village RV Park (84 sites, Wi-Fi, laundry, camp store via grandcanyonlodges.com), Desert View Campground (50 sites, flush toilets, potable water via recreation.gov). Book well in advance as these fill quickly in peak season. RV winterization checklist: drain/insulate water hoses, check heating system, inspect tires or use snow tires/chains, pack winter gear, fill propane and fuel tanks, check weather, drive cautiously, bring winter emergency kit (shovel, ice scraper, sand/cat litter), consider winter RV insurance, have backup hotel plan.
