Scene concept: Girl sitting down, about to speak into microphone, then slowly disappears into computer.

**Visual & Technical Techniques:**
- Glitchy, distorted interface effects; flickering lights, static noise
- Disorienting camera movements; quick cuts for jarring effect
- Distortion effects, flashes of light, swirling motion during disappearance
- Zoom in on face/hands during sitting to build anticipation
- Close-ups of computer buttons/screens after disappearance

**Sound Design:**
- Eerie, unsettling atmosphere (haunting melody, discordant sounds, white noise)
- Low hum or distant whisper for foreboding
- Sound effects suggesting something strange within the computer

**Lighting & Mood:**
- Dim lighting or backlighting for mystery/suspense
- Shadows to create moody atmosphere

**Narrative Twist Option:**
- Girl being trapped in digital realm, or computer itself is alive/malevolent
