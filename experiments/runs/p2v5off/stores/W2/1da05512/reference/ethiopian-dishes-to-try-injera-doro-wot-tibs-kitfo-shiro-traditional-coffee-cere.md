Injera: Sourdough flatbread staple, used as utensil to scoop up stews and curries. Doro Wot: Spicy chicken stew with berbere spice blend (chili powder, ginger, coriander, and others). Tibs: Spicy sautéed meat dish with beef or lamb, onions, and peppers. Kitfo: Raw beef dish seasoned with spices and chili powder. Shiro: Thick stew made from ground chickpeas or lentils, served with injera. Coffee: Ethiopia is famous for its coffee; traditional coffee ceremony involves roasting beans, grinding them, and brewing in a special pot.
