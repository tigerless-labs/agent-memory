Trail recommendations for Bay Area day hikes (provided May 2023):\n\n1. **Muir Woods and Dipsea Trail** — Redwood forests, connects to Mount Tamalpais or Steep Ravine Trail\n2. **Berkeley Hills: Tilden Regional Park** — Nimitz Way Trail or Wildcat Gorge Trail, panoramic views of Bay Area and Golden Gate Bridge, 5-7 mile moderate hikes\n3. **Mount Diablo State Park** — 3,849 ft peak, 360-degree views, Summit Trail or Falls Trail, 4-10 miles\n4. **Sam McDonald County Park** — Santa Cruz Mountains, Heritage Grove Trail (4.5 mi through redwoods)\n5. **Purisima Creek Redwoods Open Space Preserve** — San Mateo County, Purisima Creek Trail (4.5 mi) or Whittemore Gulch Trail (6.5 mi)\n6. **Mission Peak Regional Preserve** — Fremont, challenging 6.5-mile out-and-back to summit with Bay Area views\n\nUser selected Tilden Regional Park for the day hike.
