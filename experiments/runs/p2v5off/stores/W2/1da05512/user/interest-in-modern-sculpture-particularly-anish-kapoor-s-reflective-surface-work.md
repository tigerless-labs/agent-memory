Inspired by a modern sculpture exhibit visited at art museum on 2023-05-20. Particularly interested in Anish Kapoor's work, especially his use of reflective surfaces. Studied Cloud Gate in Millennium Park, Chicago (installed 2004, made of 168 stainless steel panels, weighs over 110 tons, 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long) as a key example of interactive, space-altering sculpture with mirror-like surfaces.
