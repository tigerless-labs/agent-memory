After evaluating multiple 3-4 day backpacking route options in the Sierra Nevada, user decided on Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route:\n\n- **Distance:** Approximately 20 miles\n- **Elevation gain:** 3,000 feet  \n- **Duration:** 3-4 days\n- **Difficulty:** Moderate\n- **Starting point:** Tuolumne Meadows\n- **Elevation of camp:** 10,300 feet\n- **Accommodations:** Tent cabins with bunk beds, mattresses, pillows, flush toilets, showers, meals provided\n- **Why chosen:** Better suited to user's moderate backpacking experience than more challenging options like High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft elevation gain)\n- **Requires:** Permit from Yosemite National Park; reservations through Yosemite Conservancy\n\nUser found this a good introduction to high-elevation backpacking with manageable difficulty while still offering stunning alpine scenery.
