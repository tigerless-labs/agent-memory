## Key Selection Criteria\n- **Capacity**: 5,000–20,000mAh or higher (higher = more charges but bulkier)\n- **Output**: Multiple USB ports, check voltage/amperage compatibility\n- **Input**: Fast charging support (Quick Charge/USB-C Power Delivery)\n- **Size/weight**: Balance portability with capacity\n- **Build quality**: Aluminum or durable plastic, solid construction\n- **Safety features**: Overcharge protection, short-circuit protection, temperature control\n- **Certifications**: UL or CE certified products\n- **Brand reputation**: Research for reliability\n- **Extra features**: LED flashlight, solar panel, wireless charging, carrying case\n\nPrioritize based on needs: compact high-capacity with fast charging, or budget-friendly with lower capacity.
