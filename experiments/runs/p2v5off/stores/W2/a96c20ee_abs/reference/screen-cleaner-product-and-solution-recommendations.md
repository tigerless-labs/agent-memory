## Product Recommendations\n- **Specialized microfiber cloths**: Soft texture with slightly sticky surface to pick up dirt\n- **Water-based cleaning solutions**: Avoid ammonia or alcohol; gentle and safe\n- **Pre-moistened screen wipes**: Convenient, streak-free formula\n- **Screen cleaning sprays**: Look for non-streaking, gentle formulas\n  - iKlear\n  - Whoosh!\n  - Ecolife\n  - DIY: 50/50 water + white vinegar, or diluted dish soap\n\n## Selection Tips\n- Check ingredients for safety\n- Read user reviews from reputable sources\n- Follow manufacturer instructions to avoid damage\n- Always clean gently; avoid paper towels or abrasive materials
