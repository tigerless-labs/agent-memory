Explored effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes in math and reading comprehension.

Key findings: Systems can effectively improve outcomes when properly implemented. Common implementation challenges include teacher professional development needs and importance of curriculum alignment. Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps but algorithmic bias poses risks. Presented as poster at first research conference (summer 2023), received positive feedback from educators and policymakers.

Main recommendations: (1) Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development, (2) Developers collaborate with educators on design, (3) Provide clear curriculum alignment guidance, (4) Establish systematic evaluation processes.
