In May 2023, decided to purchase the Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite inflatable sleeping pad for camping trips. This pad offers a good balance between comfort and weight:

- Thickness: 2.8 inches (7.1 cm)  
- Weight: 12.5 oz (354g)
- Type: Inflatable

Decision rationale: Willing to sacrifice some weight for extra comfort compared to lighter ultralight options. This was chosen over lighter alternatives like the Big Agnes Insulated AXL (9.8 oz) or NEMO Equipment Astro (10.5 oz), prioritizing a more comfortable sleep surface while remaining reasonably lightweight and packable for backpacking.

Previous experience: Had borrowed an uncomfortable sleeping pad from friend Alex on a previous camping trip, which motivated getting a higher-quality pad.
