Actively practicing drifting skills at a local parking lot with focus on:
- Controlling speed and angle during turns
- Smooth, gradual inputs on steering, throttle, and brakes
- Maintaining consistent, smooth drifts
- Experimenting with entry/exit speeds
- Transitioning between turns

Safety priority: always use proper safety protocols, wear helmet, maintain safe distance from obstacles, be aware of surroundings.
