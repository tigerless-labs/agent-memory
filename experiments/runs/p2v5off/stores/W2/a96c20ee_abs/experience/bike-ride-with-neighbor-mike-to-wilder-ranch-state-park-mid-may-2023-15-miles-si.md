In mid-May 2023, completed a bike ride with neighbor Mike to Wilder Ranch State Park covering approximately 15 miles of single-track trails. Single-track riding is challenging and tough on bike components.

Maintenance needs identified:
- Oil the chain (clean first, apply appropriate lube for conditions, wipe excess)
- Adjust derailleur (check limit screws and alignment with cassette/chainrings)
- Pre-ride checks: tire pressure, brake pads, bolt/quick-release tightness, overall secure attachment

Mike is a regular biking partner and neighbor.
