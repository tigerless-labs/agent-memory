Completed a solo day hike to Mission Peak in Fremont on April 10, 2023. Enjoyed the experience significantly and reported plans to do another hike there.

Key details:
- Location: Mission Peak, Fremont (Bay Area)
- Offers stunning views of the Bay Area from the summit
- Good solo hiking destination
- Can be hot during summer months—requires plenty of water (2-3 liters recommended) and sun protection

For future hikes: Check weather beforehand, let someone know route/return time, bring water/snacks/map/GPS/first aid kit/sun protection/extra layers/headlamp, hike during daylight hours, avoid 11am-3pm peak sun to minimize heat-related illness risk, wear bright/reflective clothing.
