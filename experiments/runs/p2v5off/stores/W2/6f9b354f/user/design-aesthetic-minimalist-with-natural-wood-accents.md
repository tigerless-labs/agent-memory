Prefers minimalist aesthetic with natural wood accents for home spaces. This style creates a clean, calming atmosphere. Examples:
- Home office nook features natural wood floating desk with built-in shelving
- Choosing wooden desk lamp with simple shade to complement wood accents
- Natural materials (wood, glass, metal) preferred over plastic
- Clean lines and simple geometric shapes for lighting fixtures

Minimalist approach reduces visual clutter and promotes focus and productivity.
