Has established a daily cleaning routine with quick tasks like making bed and wiping down kitchen counters. Also does weekly tasks (vacuuming, tidying living room) and tackles one deeper cleaning task per week (e.g., organizing closet, cleaning fridge).

Benefits experienced: improved mental clarity and overall well-being. Finds evening tidying challenging when exhausted but persists because it creates calm and relaxation before bed.

Approach: breaking tasks into manageable daily and weekly chunks to make it sustainable.
