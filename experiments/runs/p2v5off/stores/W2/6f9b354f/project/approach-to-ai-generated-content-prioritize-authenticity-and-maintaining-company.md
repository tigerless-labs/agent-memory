User wants to use AI for content creation but with strong emphasis on authenticity. Key principles:
- Define brand voice/tone guide before using AI tools
- Train AI with existing company content (blog posts, social posts, website copy)
- Always review and curate AI output before publishing
- Edit and refine to sound more human—add anecdotes, humor, emotional connection
- Treat AI as starting point, not final product
- Mix AI-generated with human-created content
- Consider AI tools for research/optimization rather than pure content generation when concerned about robotic tone
- Disclose when content is AI-generated if significant portion

Concern: ensuring AI output sounds authentic to the company, not robotic.
