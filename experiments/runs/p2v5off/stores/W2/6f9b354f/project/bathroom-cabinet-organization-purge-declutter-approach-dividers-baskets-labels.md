Project: organize messy bathroom cabinets (planned May 2023).

**Approach:**
1. Start with purging and decluttering—remove expired products and unused items
2. Categorize items (skincare, haircare, oral care, medications, etc.)
3. Use dividers and baskets to separate categories
4. Use back of cabinet door for additional storage (over-the-door racks, hooks, adhesive pockets)
5. Label everything
6. Place heavy items (hair dryer, curling iron) on lower shelves
7. Keep frequently used items front and center
8. Consider lazy Susan for hard-to-reach corners

**Key principle:** Create a functional, visually appealing system that adapts as needs change.
