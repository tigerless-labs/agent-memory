Definition and legal concepts for building contract repudiation:

Repudiation occurs when a party demonstrates through conduct either:
1. No longer intends to be bound by the contract, OR
2. Intends to fulfill only in a way substantially inconsistent with their obligations

Homeowner repudiation examples: significant delay, refusing to build scope of work, demanding excess payment

Builder repudiation examples: owner demands more work than contracted, demands lower payment than contracted, owner conduct prevents builder's performance

Wrongful termination = repudiation if party has no right to terminate

Party rights when other party repudiates:
1. Accept repudiation and terminate contract, OR
2. Affirm contract and insist on continued performance

Practical note: Repudiation requires more than ordinary breach; must show clear intent to abandon agreement and requires compelling evidence. Mere abandonment of site may not constitute repudiation if contractor intends to return.
