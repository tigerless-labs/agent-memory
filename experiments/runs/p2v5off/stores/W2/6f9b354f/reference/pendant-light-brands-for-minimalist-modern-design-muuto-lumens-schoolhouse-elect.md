Recommended pendant light brands for modern minimalist home office:

1. **Muuto** - Known for Scandinavian-inspired designs, range of modern pendant lights
2. **Lumens** - Wide selection in various styles including industrial-chic and minimalist designs
3. **Schoolhouse Electric** - Combines modern design with industrial flair, diverse pendant light options

Selection criteria:
- Size proportional to desk and space
- Geometric shapes (sphere, cylinder, rectangle) for modern look
- Materials: metal (copper, brass, chrome), glass, or acrylic
- Matte or satin finish to complement natural wood accents
- 300-400 lumens per square foot for task lighting
- Warm white color temperature (2700K-3000K)
- Dimmer compatible
