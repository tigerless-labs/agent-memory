Ayn Rand's Challenge to Altruism:
- Argues that nature does not guarantee survival; prioritizing self-interest is moral
- Criticizes altruism as manipulable for authoritarian control
- Central claim: 'selfishness' (self-prioritization) is necessary for survival, not immoral
- Historical concern: altruism-based ideology misused by regimes (Nazi example mentioned)
