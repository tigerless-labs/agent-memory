**ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) Care Guide**

**Watering:**
- Summer: Every 1-2 weeks (only when soil feels dry to touch)
- Winter: Every 4-6 weeks
- Avoid overwatering; can lead to root rot
- Don't get water on leaves to prevent fungal disease

**Fertilizing:**
- Use balanced water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20)
- Fertilize once per month during growing season (spring & summer)
- Dilute to half strength to avoid burning roots
- Skip fertilizing in winter when dormant

**Other notes:**
- Prefers well-draining soil
- Tolerates extreme low light
- Avoid direct sunlight
- Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
