Took an Art of Communication course that covered:

**Core skills:**
- Building confidence and self-esteem
- Overcoming glossophobia (fear of public speaking)
- Different confidence frameworks
- Understanding audience structure and analysis
- Articulation frameworks for clear speech
- Persuasion techniques

**Professional application:**
- Delivering speeches effectively
- Personal branding
- LinkedIn profile maintenance
- Networking strategies
- Active listening techniques
- Conversation skills

**Key insights gained:**
- Communication is a learnable skill, not just a natural talent
- Effective communication is two-way (speaking + active listening)
- Nonverbal communication (body language, tone of voice) is essential
- Communication requires effort, patience, and practice
- Understanding your audience is crucial to effectiveness
- Building relationships through effective communication applies in both personal and professional contexts
- Different communication frameworks can be applied situationally
