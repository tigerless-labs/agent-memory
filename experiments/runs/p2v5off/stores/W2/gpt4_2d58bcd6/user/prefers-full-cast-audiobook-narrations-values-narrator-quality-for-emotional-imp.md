Strongly prefers full-cast narrations, particularly for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes. Believes full-cast narration adds to the immersive experience and helps track who is speaking. Also appreciates skilled single narrators who bring unique tone and style to stories.\n\nBelieves narrator quality is crucial to the audiobook experience. A good narrator can elevate the story, convey emotional depth and complexity, and amplify emotional impact through tone, pace, and inflection. Poor narration can detract significantly from the listening experience. Narrator's interpretation influences listener imagination but listener's own experiences still play a significant role in shaping their understanding.
