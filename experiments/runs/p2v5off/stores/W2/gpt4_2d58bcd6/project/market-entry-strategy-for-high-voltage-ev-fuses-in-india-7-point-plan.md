Context: Company manufactures high-voltage fuses for EVs in Japan, seeking to enter Indian market.

Key market differences: India is price-sensitive with diverse demographics, varied infrastructure, and distinct regulatory environment.

7-Point Strategy:
1. Market Research - Understand Indian EV industry needs, competitors, infrastructure
2. Indian Standards Compliance - Obtain local certifications and regulatory approvals
3. Localized Production/Partnership - Partner with Indian company to reduce costs and navigate regulations
4. Competitive Pricing - Price competitively while maintaining quality (price-sensitive market)
5. Distribution Network - Establish robust distribution through distributors, retailers, and e-commerce across diverse geography
6. Targeted Marketing - Digital/social media marketing emphasizing safety, reliability, performance; tailor to local preferences
7. After-Sales Service - Establish service centers and train local technicians for brand loyalty and trust

Success depends on compliance, competitive pricing, accessible distribution, and strong service reputation.
