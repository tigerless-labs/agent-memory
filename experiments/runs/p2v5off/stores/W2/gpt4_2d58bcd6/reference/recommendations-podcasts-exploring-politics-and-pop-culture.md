Podcast recommendations for politics and pop culture intersection:

**News/politics focus:**
- Pod Save America (Crooked Media, hosted by Obama aides)
- The Daily (New York Times daily news podcast)
- Slate's Political Gabfest (weekly discussion of politics and culture)
- The Axe Files with David Axelrod (CNN, interviews with newsmakers)
- The Rachel Maddow Show (MSNBC podcast)

**Satire/comedy:**
- The Bugle (Times of London, humorous take on news and politics)

**Current events digest:**
- TheSkimm (witty breakdowns of current events)
- Politico's Playbook (daily coverage of politics and policy)

**TV shows exploring politics and pop culture:**
- The Circus: Inside the Greatest Political Show on Earth (Showtime)
- The Daily Show with Trevor Noah (Comedy Central)
- Last Week Tonight with John Oliver (HBO)
- Patriot Act with Hasan Minhaj (Netflix)
