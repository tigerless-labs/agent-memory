Strava is a popular mobile app for tracking outdoor activities like bike rides and hikes. Features include:
- Tracking distance, speed, heart rate, and other metrics
- Route mapping and history
- Social features to compete with friends
- Challenges and community engagement
- Can create clubs (user plans to create 'The Spikers' club)

User plans to use Strava to track their new weekend outdoor activities.
