Sample 3-day home strength training routine using household items (water bottles, cans, backpacks with books):

**Monday - Upper Body:**
- Warm-up: 5-min jog in place or jumping jacks
- Push-ups: 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Water bottle bicep curls: 3 sets × 12 reps
- Tricep dips (chair/bench): 3 sets × 12 reps
- Cool-down: 5-min stretching

**Wednesday - Lower Body:**
- Warm-up: 5-min jog in place or jumping jacks
- Squats: 3 sets × 12 reps
- Lunges: 3 sets × 12 reps per leg
- Calf raises (using step/bench): 3 sets × 12 reps
- Cool-down: 5-min stretching

**Friday - Core:**
- Warm-up: 5-min jog in place or jumping jacks
- Plank: 3 sets × 30-60 sec hold
- Russian twists (with weight/medicine ball): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Leg raises (lying on back): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Bicycle crunches: 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Cool-down: 5-min stretching

**Key principles:** Start with lighter weights, gradually increase intensity, focus on proper form, mix compound and isolation exercises.
