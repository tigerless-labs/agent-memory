**12 tactics for increasing daily physical activity:**

1. Take stairs instead of elevator
2. Do desk exercises: chair squats, desk push-ups, leg lifts, wrist extensions
3. Walk to coworker's desk instead of emailing/messaging
4. Park car farther away from destination
5. Walk while taking phone calls
6. Turn household chores into workout (music, increased intensity, squats/lunges while vacuuming)
7. Suggest walking meetings with colleagues/friends
8. Use fitness tracker to monitor steps and set daily goals
9. Take short breaks to stretch throughout the day
10. Try active commuting (bike, bus, train to work/school)
11. Play active games with kids (tag, hide-and-seek, soccer)
12. Take fitness classes during lunch break near work

**Principle:** Every bit counts; find activities that work best for your lifestyle and consistency is key.
