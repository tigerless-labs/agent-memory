## Audiobooks with Great Narration\n\n- **'The Hate U Give'** by Angie Thomas, narrated by **Bahni Turpin** — Powerful and emotive performance\n- **'The Night Circus'** by Erin Morgenstern, narrated by **Jim Dale** — Known for incredible range and ability to bring characters to life\n- **'The Power'** by Naomi Alderman, narrated by **Adjoa Andoh** — Commanding and thought-provoking performance\n- **'The Song of Achilles'** by Madeline Miller, narrated by **Frazer Douglas** — Brings lyrical and emotive quality to the story\n- **'The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August'** by Claire North, narrated by **Peter Kenny** — Masterful performance, brings complex story and characters to life\n\n## Notable Narrators\n\n**Juliet Stevenson** — Known for nuanced and emotive performances; has narrated 'The Girl on the Train', 'The Power'\n\n**Scott Brick** — Distinctive voice and style; has narrated 'The Da Vinci Code', 'The Bourne Identity'\n\n**George Guidall** — Veteran narrator, recorded 1000+ audiobooks including 'The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo', 'The Lincoln Rhyme' series\n\n**Khristine Hvam** — Brings youthful energy to her performances; has narrated 'The Hunger Games', 'The Divergent' series\n\nAudie Awards are a good source for discovering great audiobook narrations.
