**Injury Prevention Strategies:**

1. Warm up and cool down (10-15 min each with cardio and stretching)
2. Regular stretching and foam rolling to improve flexibility and reduce tension
3. Strengthen core and ankle muscles (planks, side planks, ankle circles)
4. Use proper gear and equipment suited to activity
5. Listen to body - stop and rest if fatigued or experiencing pain
6. Gradually increase intensity and volume to avoid overuse injuries

**Injury Recovery Strategies:**

1. **RICE principle** for acute injuries: Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation
2. Gentle stretching and mobilization exercises to promote recovery
3. Use compression wraps and elevation to reduce swelling
4. Take rest days and modify workout routine as needed
5. Seek professional help for chronic or severe injuries

**Specific for Ankle Sprains:**

1. Strengthen ankles with exercises: ankle circles, calf raises, single-leg balancing
2. Use ankle braces/supports during high-impact activities
3. Improve balance and proprioception with single-leg squats and balance boards

**General principle:** Proactive prevention is better than recovering from injury and losing training time.
