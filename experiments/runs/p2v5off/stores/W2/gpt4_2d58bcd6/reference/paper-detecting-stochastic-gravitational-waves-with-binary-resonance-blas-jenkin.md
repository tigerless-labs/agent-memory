**Paper Details:**
- Title: Detecting stochastic gravitational waves with binary resonance
- Authors: Diego Blas and Alexander C. Jenkins
- Institutions: Departament de Física (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona), IFAE (Barcelona), King's College London
- Published: Phys. Rev. D [closed access]

**Key Innovation:**
Proposes using binary systems to detect gravitational waves by measuring resonant oscillations when gravitational wave frequency is an integer multiple of the binary's orbital frequency. Effects accumulate over time for persistent stochastic backgrounds.

**Application - Earth-Moon System:**
- The Earth-Moon system (28-day orbital period) is proposed to detect microhertz gravitational waves
- Fills gap in frequency coverage between pulsar timing arrays and LISA
- Requires measuring Earth-Moon distance to ~0.1mm accuracy
- Technology already exists: Moon retroreflectors placed by Apollo astronauts and Soviet landers have enabled lunar ranging for 50+ years
- Current measurement accuracy may be sufficient with improved sensitivity

**Source:** Article 'Detecting Gravitational Waves with the Moon' by Emma Clarke (Daily Paper Summaries, Feb 8, 2023)
