Movie musical recommendations blending jazz, pop, and hip-hop:

**Jazz-influenced:**
- Chicago (2002)
- Moulin Rouge! (2001)
- The Great Debaters (2007)

**Pop-influenced:**
- Hairspray (2007)
- Mamma Mia! (2008)
- Rock of Ages (2012)
- Enchanted (2007)

**Hip-hop/rap-influenced:**
- Get Rich or Die Tryin' (2005, based on 50 Cent)
- Hustle & Flow (2005, about small-time pimp becoming rapper)
- Wild Style (1983, early hip-hop film)

**Other genre-bending:**
- The Bodyguard (1992, with Whitney Houston)
- Scottsboro (2001, musical drama with blues/jazz/gospel)
