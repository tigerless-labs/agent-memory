## Authors and Books Recommended\n\n**Jason Reynolds**: 'Long Way Down', 'Look Both Ways' — Known for powerful voice in YA, complex characters, layered storytelling about social justice and identity\n\n**Nicola Yoon**: 'The Sun is Also a Star', 'Everything, Everything' — Feature lyrical prose, complex characters, blend of realistic and speculative elements, explore themes of identity and family\n\n**Renee Watson**: 'Piecing Me Together', 'Betty Before X' — Strong female protagonists, character-driven narratives focused on Black girls and women, social justice and identity themes\n\n**Ibi Zoboi**: 'American Street', 'Pride' — Beautifully written, explore experiences of Haitian-American teenagers, identity and family themes\n\n**Dhonielle Clayton**: 'The Belles', 'Shattered Midnight' — Lush and imaginative fantasy/speculative fiction exploring themes of identity, power, and social justice\n\n**Tomi Adeyemi**: 'Children of Blood and Bone' — Blends African mythology, fantasy, and social commentary\n\n**Sabaa Tahir**: 'An Ember in the Ashes' — Blends ancient Roman-inspired world-building, fantasy, and social commentary\n\n**Nnedi Okorafor**: 'Who Fears Death', 'Binti' — Blend African culture and mythology with speculative fiction\n\nThese authors are recommended for readers who enjoy a mix of genres and styles, with strong emphasis on character development, identity exploration, and social justice themes.
