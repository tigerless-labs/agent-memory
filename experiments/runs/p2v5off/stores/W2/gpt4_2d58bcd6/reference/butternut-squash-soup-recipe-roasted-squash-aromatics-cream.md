Simple butternut squash soup recipe (serves 4-6).

**Ingredients:**
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs (parsley, sage) for garnish

**Method:**
1. Roast squash at 400°F (200°C) for 45-50 min, cut-side up with olive oil
2. Sauté chopped onion (5 min), add garlic (1 min)
3. Add cumin, paprika, salt, pepper (1 min)
4. Pour in broth, bring to boil
5. Scoop roasted squash into pot
6. Blend smooth with immersion or regular blender
7. Stir in cream if desired
8. Season to taste
9. Serve with fresh herbs

**Tips:** Use pre-cut squash to save time. Add diced apple or chopped kale for variation. Substitute heavy cream with coconut cream or Greek yogurt. Experiment with nutmeg or cinnamon. Store 3-5 days refrigerated or 3-6 months frozen in portions.
