Key musical recommendations given for hip-hop and theater fusion:

**Lin-Manuel Miranda works:**
- In the Heights
- Freestyle Love Supreme

**Hip-hop musicals:**
- Passing Strange (by Stew and Heidi Rodewald)
- Holler If Ya Hear Me (inspired by Tupac Shakur)
- The Bomb-itty of Errors (hip-hop adaptation of Shakespeare)
- Stagger Lee (hip-hop musical thriller)

**Contemporary musicals:**
- Dear Evan Hansen
- Rent
- The Great Comet
- Hadestown

**Artists and producers:**
- Kendrick Lamar (storytelling-driven hip-hop)
- The Roots (hip-hop group)
- Lauryn Hill (hip-hop + soul/R&B)
- J. Period (producer, R&J: The Hip-Hop Version)
- A Tribe Called Quest

**Organizations:**
- The Hip-Hop Shakespeare Company (UK, led by Akala)
- The Remix Project
- Rhymes Over Reason (The Realness: hip-hop musical about Tupac)
