Common spreads for daily tarot readings:
1. **Single Card Spread** — One card for insight into the day ahead
2. **Three-Card Spread** — Past, Present, Future structure
3. **Celtic Cross** — Ten-card spread for in-depth readings; can be adapted for daily focus
4. **Daily Focus Spread** — Three cards: overall energy, what to focus on, expected outcome
5. **Mind, Body, Spirit Spread** — Three cards addressing mental, physical, and spiritual aspects of the day
