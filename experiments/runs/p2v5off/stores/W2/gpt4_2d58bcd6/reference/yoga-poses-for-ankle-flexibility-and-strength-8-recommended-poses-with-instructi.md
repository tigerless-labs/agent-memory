Recommended yoga poses for improving ankle flexibility and strength:

1. **Ankle Circles** - Sit with legs straight, lift one foot and draw circles (small to large), 5-10 reps each direction, then switch
2. **Toe Spreads** - Sit with legs straight, spread toes as far apart as possible then release, 10-15 reps each foot
3. **Ankle Alphabet** - Sit with legs straight, lift one foot and draw alphabet A-Z with toes, repeat on other leg
4. **Calf Raises** - Stand on edge of stair/step with heels hanging off, raise onto tiptoes then lower, 10-15 reps
5. **Downward-Facing Dog** - Yoga pose: hands forward, hips lifted back, straighten arms and legs, ankles in line with shoulders, hold 30 sec-1 min
6. **Tree Pose** - Balance on one leg with other foot on inner thigh, engage core, hold 30 sec-1 min, switch legs
7. **Eagle Pose** - Bend knees slightly, cross one leg over wrapping ankle around calf, hold 30 sec-1 min, switch
8. **Seated Forward Fold** - Sit with legs extended, fold forward reaching for toes/shins, hold 30 sec-1 min

**Important:** Listen to body, modify if discomfort, warm up before practice.
