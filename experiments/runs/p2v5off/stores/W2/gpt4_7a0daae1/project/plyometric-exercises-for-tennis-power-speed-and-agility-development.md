Adding plyometric exercises to tennis training routine 2-3 times per week to improve explosive power, speed, and court agility.

**Core plyometric exercises:**
- **Box jumps**: 3 sets of 10-15 reps on 12-18" box, rest 30-60s between sets
- **Depth jumps**: Jump from box then immediately jump up; 3 sets of 10-15 reps
- **Lateral bounds**: Sideways jumping with quick turnaround; 3 sets of 10-15 reps
- **Tuck jumps**: Jump tucking knees to chest; 3 sets of 10-15 reps
- **Medicine ball throws**: Rotational explosive throws; 3 sets of 10-15 reps

**Integration approach:**
- Combine with strength training (squats, lunges)
- Alternate with cardio sessions (running, cycling)
- Always warm up with 5-10 min light cardio + dynamic stretching
- Focus on proper technique over speed
- Include 5 min cool-down stretching after each session

**Sample weekly schedule:**
- Monday: Plyometric + strength (box jumps, squats, lateral bounds)
- Wednesday: Cardio + agility drills (20-30 min steady jogging/cycling)
- Friday: Plyometric + strength (depth jumps, lunges, ladder drills)

Goal: Build explosive power and court agility to complement running/cycling endurance base.
