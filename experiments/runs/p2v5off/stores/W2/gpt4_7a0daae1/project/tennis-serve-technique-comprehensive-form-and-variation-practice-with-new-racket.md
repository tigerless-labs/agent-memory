After receiving new racket on 2023-03-10, working on serve technique improvements covering all aspects of the motion.

**Grip and setup:**
- Continental grip (index finger on second groove of racket handle)
- Feet shoulder-width apart, body slightly turned right (right-handed)
- Weight shifted onto back foot

**Toss technique:**
- Toss 6-8 feet high
- 6-8 inches in front of body, slightly to the right
- Use non-dominant hand

**Racket positioning and contact:**
- Hold racket at 90° angle to body
- Racket face pointing toward net
- Contact at peak of toss using wrist and forearm for power
- Racket face slightly closed for optimal contact

**Follow-through and body mechanics:**
- Follow through with racket close to body for control and power
- Rotate body to generate momentum
- Transfer weight from back foot to front foot

**Serve variations to practice:**
- **Flat serve**: Fast, straight, minimal spin
- **Topspin serve**: Forward spin causing ball to dip into court
- **Slice serve**: Sidespin causing ball to curve away from opponent

**New racket specific:**
- Experiment with sweet spot locations on racket face
- Adjust technique if new racket has higher power level than previous
- Test different string tensions and types if adjustable

**Practice approach:**
- Record self serving to analyze technique
- Practice regularly to develop muscle memory
- Mix grips, tosses, and contact points to find optimal combinations

Focus: Consistency and muscle memory development.
