Incorporating regular stretching into tennis training, especially after Saturday lessons. Focus areas:

- **Warm-up/Pre-play stretches** (5-10 min): neck, shoulders, chest, arm circles
- **Lower body stretches** (10-15 min): calf, hamstring, quad, hip flexor
- **Tennis-specific stretches** (5-10 min): forearm, rotator cuff, glutes
- **Post-lesson stretching** (10-15 min): major muscle groups

Key principles:
- Hold each stretch 30 seconds
- Breathe deeply and naturally
- No bouncing or forcing
- Make stretching a daily habit, not just post-Saturday lessons

Goal: Improve flexibility and reduce injury risk for continued tennis improvement.
