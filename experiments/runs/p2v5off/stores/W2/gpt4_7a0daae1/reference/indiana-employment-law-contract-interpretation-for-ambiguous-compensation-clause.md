In Indiana, when an ambiguous compensation clause leads to an employment dispute, courts apply standard contract interpretation principles:

- **Against the drafter**: ambiguity is construed against the employer (typically the contract drafter)
- **Reasonable interpretation favoring employee**: courts prefer interpretations favoring the employee when genuine ambiguity exists
- **Evidence of intent**: courts examine written/oral communications, negotiations, industry practices
- **Parol evidence**: extrinsic evidence (testimony, prior drafts) may clarify ambiguous clauses
- **Course of performance**: how the parties acted under the contract historically indicates intent
- **Custom and usage**: industry standards and practices inform interpretation

Indiana also has specific wage payment statutes governing timing and manner of payments, which may apply depending on the dispute nature.
