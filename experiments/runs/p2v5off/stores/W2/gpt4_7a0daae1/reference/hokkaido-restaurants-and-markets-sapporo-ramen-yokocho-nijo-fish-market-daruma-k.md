Recommended Hokkaido food destinations:

**Markets & Streets:**
- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) — fresh seafood including crabs, sea urchin, squid
- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho — alley famous for ramen shops, try different local varieties
- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) — local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs

**Restaurants:**
- Daruma (Sapporo) — Jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) — Jingisukan
- Yurigakuro (Sapporo) — Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)
- Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru) — Ishikari Nabe

**Transportation in Hokkaido:**
- Rental cars available with easy-to-follow road network
- JR Hokkaido trains connect major cities (regular service, comfortable)
- Extensive bus network covering major tourist spots
- Scenic biking routes for cycling enthusiasts
- Guided food tours available with tastings, sightseeing, and transportation
