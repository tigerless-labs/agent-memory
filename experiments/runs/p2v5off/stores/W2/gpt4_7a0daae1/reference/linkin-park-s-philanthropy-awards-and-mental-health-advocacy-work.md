Linkin Park founded Music for Relief in 2015 for disaster relief and mental health awareness campaigns.

**Awards:**
- Lifesaver Award (2017) from the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention for suicide prevention and mental health awareness
- Change Award (2018, first-ever award) from Unite the United Foundation's inaugural fundraising dinner, recognizing work with Music for Relief and other charities

**Advocacy work:**
- Partnered with American Foundation for Suicide Prevention
- Worked with Campaign to Change Direction
- Promoted environmental sustainability and education initiatives
- Lead singer Chester Bennington died in 2017; remaining members continued advocacy work

Band used their platform to encourage fans to seek help and get involved in communities.
