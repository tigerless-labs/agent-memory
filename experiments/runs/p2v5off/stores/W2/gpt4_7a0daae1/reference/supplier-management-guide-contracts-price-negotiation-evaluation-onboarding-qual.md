## Key Topics Covered

1. **Supplier Contracts**: Template with 14 sections including intro, scope, pricing, delivery, quality/warranty, IP, term/termination, liability, dispute resolution, confidentiality, governing law, amendments, entire agreement, and signatures.

2. **Price Negotiation Tips**: Research market rates, know requirements, build relationships, be transparent about budget, ask directly for better prices, look for value-added services, be willing to walk away, use competition as leverage, consider alternative pricing models (tiered, cost-plus, value-based).

3. **Supplier Evaluation Criteria**: Quality standards, reliability/consistency, pricing/cost transparency, customer service/support, financial stability/reputation, supply chain visibility, certifications/compliance, innovation/continuous improvement. Red flags: poor communication, lack of transparency, unwillingness to provide references, late delivery history, inadequate insurance, unprofessional behavior, unclear contract terms.

4. **Supplier Onboarding**: 9 key steps including contract finalization, supplier profile creation, communication setup, onboarding meeting, training/support, testing/QA, payment terms, and performance metrics. Tools: supplier management systems (SAP Ariba, Coupa), contract management software (ContractWorks, Concord), project management tools (Asana, Trello).

5. **Quality Control**: Define standards, identify critical control points, develop QC plan, implement checks, establish corrective action process, monitor performance. Use KPIs for defect rates, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction. Standards: ISO 9001, AS9100, Six Sigma, Lean Manufacturing.
