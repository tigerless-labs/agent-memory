**Recommended Madras Curry Powder Brands:**
- Penzeys Spices: 12-spice blend including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom
- The Spice House: 15-spice blend including fenugreek and fennel
- McCormick: Affordable option with good balance of spices

**Madras Curry Sauce Recipe (from scratch):**

Ingredients: 2 tbsp vegetable oil, 1 diced onion, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp cumin, curry leaves (optional), 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder, 1/2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional), 1 can diced tomatoes (14 oz), 1 cup vegetable broth, 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional), fresh cilantro

Instructions: 
1. Heat oil, sauté onions until translucent
2. Add all spices, cook 1-2 min until fragrant
3. Add tomatoes, broth, coconut milk
4. Simmer 10-15 min until thickened
5. Add cooked protein, simmer 5-7 min more
6. Serve over rice or with naan

**Tips:** Can customize spice blend, add vegetables like potatoes/carrots, substitute coconut milk with yogurt/cashew cream, add lemon juice for brightness
