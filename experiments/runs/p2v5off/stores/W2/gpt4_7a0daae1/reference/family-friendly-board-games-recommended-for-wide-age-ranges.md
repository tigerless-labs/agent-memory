Games recommended for family game nights with mixed ages:\n\n- **Ticket to Ride** — train-themed, ages 8+, easy to learn, fun to play\n- **Pictionary** — drawing game, ages 8+, classic family favorite\n- **Scattergories** — word game, ages 10+\n- **Cranium** — combines drawing, acting, trivia, wordplay, ages 10+\n- **Settlers of Catan** — strategy game, ages 10+, simple to learn with depth\n- **Clue** — detective mystery game, ages 8+\n- **Uno** — card game, ages 6+, quick play\n- **Forbidden Island/Forbidden Desert** — cooperative games, ages 10+\n- **Apples to Apples** — party game, ages 10+\n- **Guess Who** — guessing game, ages 6+\n\nAll suitable for 2-6 players; gameplay can be adjusted for age and attention span.
