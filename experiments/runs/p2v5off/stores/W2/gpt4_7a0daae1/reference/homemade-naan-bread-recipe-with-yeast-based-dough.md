**Simple Yeast-Based Naan Bread Recipe:**

Ingredients: 1.5 cups all-purpose flour, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp active dry yeast, 1/2 cup lukewarm water, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, for tenderness)

Instructions:
1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, yeast in bowl
2. Gradually add lukewarm water, mix until shaggy dough
3. Add olive oil, mix until dough comes together
4. Knead 5-7 min until smooth and elastic
5. Place in greased bowl, cover, let rise 1-2 hours until doubled
6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat
7. Divide dough into 6-8 portions, shape into balls
8. Roll each into thin circle (6-7 inches)
9. Cook in skillet 1-2 min per side until puffed and golden brown
10. Brush with melted butter or ghee
11. Serve hot

**Tips & Variations:**
- Use stand mixer for easier kneading
- Add garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds to dough
- Use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crispier crust
- Try whole wheat flour or higher-protein flour blends
- Practice makes perfect; adjust recipe to preference
