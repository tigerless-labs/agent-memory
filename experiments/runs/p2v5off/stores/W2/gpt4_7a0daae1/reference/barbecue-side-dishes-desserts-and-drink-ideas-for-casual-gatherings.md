**Side Dishes:**\n- Coleslaw (creamy with cabbage, mayo, vinegar)\n- Baked beans (sweet and smoky)\n- Grilled vegetables (peppers, zucchini, onions)\n- Potato salad (with mayo and herbs)\n- Corn on the cob (buttered)\n\n**Desserts:**\n- S'mores station (marshmallows, chocolate, graham crackers)\n- Watermelon (fresh and refreshing)\n- Brownies or cookies\n- Fruit salad (strawberries, grapes, pineapple)\n- Watermelon patriotic parfaits\n- Ice cream sandwiches (various flavors)\n\n**Drinks:**\n- Lemonade (classic, can add fruit flavors)\n- Iced coffee (with milk/cream/syrup)\n- Fruit infused water (cucumber, lemon, lime, berries)\n- Sparkling water with fruit\n- Popsicles (various flavors)\n- Beer and wine (for adult gatherings)\n- Agua fresca (watermelon, cantaloupe, or hibiscus)\n- Virgin piña coladas (pineapple juice, coconut cream, lime)\n- Cucumber lime refresher\n- Ginger ale spritzer\n\n**Other ideas:**\n- Chips and dips (guacamole, salsa, queso)\n- Fresh fruit infused water station\n\nKey tip: Set up DIY stations to let guests customize.
