Wants to visit Hokkaido to experience its distinctive cuisine, particularly:
- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton)
- Kaisendon (sashimi rice bowl)
- Jingisukan (Japanese-style Mongolian barbecue)
- Uni ikura don (sea urchin and salmon roe)
- Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)
- Local Hokkaido dairy products and ice cream

Interested in exploring restaurants, markets, and using trains or car rental to get around the region.
