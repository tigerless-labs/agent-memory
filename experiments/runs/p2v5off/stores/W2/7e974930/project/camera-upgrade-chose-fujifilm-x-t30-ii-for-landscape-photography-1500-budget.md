Decided to upgrade from DSLR to mirrorless. Chose Fujifilm X-T30 II after weighing Sony vs Fujifilm options.

**Specs & features valued:**
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- Compact size with vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- Fast autofocus: 425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time, performs well in low light
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed, but adequate for light rain/mist)
- Battery life: approximately 380 frames per charge with EVF

**Kit lens option:** Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ (~499 dollars)

**Budget:** 1500 dollars total for body plus lens

**Photography focus:** Landscape and nature photography. Does not yet own lenses; open to building kit over time.
