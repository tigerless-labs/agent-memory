Planning to add gift baskets to product line as new revenue stream and upselling opportunity.

**Downtown Farmers Market Themed Gift Basket** — Feature best-selling soaps and candles from the Downtown Farmers Market events. This theme taps into customer nostalgia and emotional connection to the market and appeals to both local regulars and tourists seeking a piece of the community to take home.

**Key benefits:** Increased average order value, new revenue stream, cross-selling opportunities, and strong marketing potential during holidays and special occasions.

**Suggested enhancements:** Personalized note/card about the market, small memento representing the market, custom packaging reflecting market aesthetic, and discount codes for market or online use.

**Implementation strategy:** Start with limited selection to test market demand, choose products complementing the soap and candle brand, price strategically for profitability, and cross-promote with the Downtown Farmers Market community.
