A creative writing project developed collaboratively. 

**Main characters:**
- MK: A human captive held by Wukong
- Wukong: The Monkey King, cruel captor searching for a 'diamond snare'

**Plot arc developed:**
1. Wukong interrogates MK about the location of the diamond snare; MK claims ignorance
2. MK convinces Wukong not to kill him by offering himself as 'insurance'
3. Wukong agrees to keep him alive 'for now'
4. MK tries to get Wukong's attention by complaining about hunger—backfires
5. Another monkey delivers meager rations (minimal fruit and water)
6. MK internally struggles reconciling the heroic Wukong of legend with his cruel captor
7. MK hears monkeys partying and feasting, attempts escape to steal food—fails
8. MK begs Wukong for food/water, arguing he can't be 'insurance' if he starves
9. Wukong reluctantly provides stale bread and water, warns against asking for more

**Themes:**
- Captivity and survival
- Contrast between myth and cruel reality
- Desperation and manipulation
- Power imbalance between captor and captive

**Story status:** Multiple scenes written; narrative ongoing with potential for continuation
