Arctic cargo loading: preparation → transportation (helicopter/planes/boats) → securing with ropes/chains → crane lift → designated stowage per management plan. Unloading: retrieve from storage → secure → transport to destination via multiple modes. Safety: specialized gear, cargo securing, detailed management plans, crew communication, training/drills, weather monitoring. Crew typically stay 2-6 months (up to 1 year), work shifts with breaks, trained in survival/isolation/extreme conditions.
