Bass Lures: Topwater (Poppers, Spooks, Frogs), Soft Plastics (Curly Tail Grubs, Worms, Lizards), Crankbaits (Shad-imitating, Square-bill, Deep-diving), Jigs (Jig and Pig, Swim bait, Finesse jig), Other (Spinnerbaits, A-rigs).

Fly Patterns: Streamers (Woolly Bugger in black/olive/brown, Muddler Minnow, Baitfish Streamers in silver/gold/chartreuse), Poppers & Sliders (Poppers in white/yellow/chartreuse, Sliders, Gurgler), Nymphs (Crayfish Patterns, Mayfly Nymphs like Hare's Ear and Pheasant Tail, Leech Patterns), Dry Flies (Frog Patterns, Terrestrials like Ant, Beetle, Grasshopper).

Fishing Knots: Improved Clinch (widely used, high breaking strength), Palomar (strength and reliability), Loop Knot (secure, won't slip).

Fly Rod Setup: Rod 9-10 feet, weight 6-8, medium to fast action. Reel with 150-200 yards 20-lb backing. Line weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking, weight 6-8, 90-foot length. Leader 9-12 feet, 15-20 lb breaking strength. Tippet 1-2 feet, 12-15 lb breaking strength.

Presentation: Retrieves (Strip steady or varied, Twitch short quick, Drag-free). Action (vary sink rate, match fly to prey, incorporate pauses). Casting (parallel to structure, 45-degree angles, into weed edges). Depth varies with water temperature and feeding patterns.

Structure Targeting: Rocks (cast parallel, use streamers, quick retrieves). Weed Beds (weedless flies, cast into edges, slow retrieve). Sunken Logs (cast near, vary depth). Lake Tips (find baitfish schools, focus on drop-offs and weed lines).
