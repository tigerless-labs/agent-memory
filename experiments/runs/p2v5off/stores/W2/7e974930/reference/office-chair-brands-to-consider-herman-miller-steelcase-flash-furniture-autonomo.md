Office chair options and recommendations:

ERGOhuman: Already satisfied user (has one in home office)

Herman Miller: High-quality, ergonomic designs ranging from budget-friendly to high-end

Steelcase: Well-established brand with variety of ergonomic, task, and executive options

Flash Furniture: Affordable options with modern designs and decent ergonomics

Autonomous: Newer brand offering affordable ergonomic chairs with adjustable lumbar support and ventilation features

When choosing, prioritize: adjustability (lumbar, armrests, height), materials and build quality, comfort and cushioning, style fit, and budget.
