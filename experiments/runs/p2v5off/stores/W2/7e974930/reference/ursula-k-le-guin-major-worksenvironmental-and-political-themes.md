Key works by Le Guin explored for ecocritical analysis:
- *The Dispossessed* (1974): Anarchism, critique of capitalism, human-environment relationship
- *The Left Hand of Darkness* (1969): Gender, sexuality, power, environmental critique
- *The Word for World is Forest* (1976): Imperialism, colonialism, human-environment conflict
- *The Lathe of Heaven* (1971): Environmentalism, science and technology misuse, environmental destruction
- *Always Coming Home* (1985): Post-apocalyptic, environmentalism, ecology, human-nature relationship

Le Guin known for exploring social/political issues and critiques of capitalist ideologies through compelling character-driven narratives.
