**Eligibility:** Non-UK citizens can be appointed as life peers in the UK if they are British citizens, Commonwealth citizens, or citizens of the Republic of Ireland with permanent residence in the UK. Appointments are rare.

**Examples discussed (2023):**
- Baroness Boothroyd — served as Speaker of the House of Commons (1992–2000), appointed to House of Lords in 2000
- Baroness Hayman — born in Canada, appointed to House of Lords after stepping down as Speaker in 2000
- Lord Patten of Barnes — born in Sudan, served as last British Governor of Hong Kong, appointed as life peer in 2005
- Baroness Manningham-Buller — Director General of MI5 (2002–2007), appointed to House of Lords in 2008

The House of Lords has become increasingly diverse with appointments of women, ethnic minorities, and non-traditional backgrounds, bringing diverse perspectives and expertise.
