Examples of successful government initiatives to reduce mental illness stigma and promote mental health care:

- **HeadsUp** (Australia, 2016) — Government awareness campaign increasing mental health literacy and reducing stigma; focuses on mentally healthy workplaces
- **Bell Let's Talk** (Canada) — Multi-year program raising awareness and combating mental illness stigma; supports organizations developing innovative programs and treatments  
- **ReachOut** (Ireland) — Online support service for ages 16-25; provides information, advice, and tools for mental health management
- **NAMI** (National Alliance on Mental Illness, US) — Major grassroots mental health organization providing awareness, education, and resources
- **Dealing with Depression** (New Zealand) — Government program combining public awareness campaigns with online resources and face-to-face counseling

These programs demonstrate effectiveness of prioritizing mental health in public policy.
