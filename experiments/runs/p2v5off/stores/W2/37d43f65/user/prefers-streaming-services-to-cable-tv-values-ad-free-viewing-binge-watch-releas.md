Values streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) over cable TV for:
- Ad-free, uninterrupted viewing experience
- Ability to pick up a show where they left off, even after days away
- Access to shows not available on cable and previously aired episodes
- Multi-device viewing (phone, tablet, on the go)
- Full-season releases enabling binge-watching instead of waiting weekly

Hasn't watched cable TV in months. Appreciates the flexibility and convenience streaming provides.
