Research interest in mental health care systems worldwide, particularly:

- Disparities in mental health care access between developing and developed countries
- Stigma surrounding mental illness and its impact on care-seeking
- Government policies and individual actions to improve access
- Statistics on prevalence of mental health disorders globally

Concerned about lack of resources and trained professionals in low-income countries, especially sub-Saharan Africa (1 professional per 100,000 people vs 70 per 100,000 in high-income countries).
