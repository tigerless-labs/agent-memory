Coach offers 1-year limited warranty on handbags (covers defects in materials and workmanship). Coach Promise adds 1 additional year for around 20 dollars. Monogramming available on many bags: 2-3 weeks turnaround, costs 10-30 dollars depending on bag and options. Gift wrapping available free or for small fee (5-10 dollars). Register bags on Coach website for warranty coverage.
