### Canvas Sneakers (Converse Chuck Taylors)\n- Soft brush to remove loose dirt\n- Mild soap + warm water solution\n- Wipe gently with damp cloth\n- Air dry at room temperature\n- Condition rubber toe cap with rubber conditioner or silicone lubricant\n- Avoid harsh chemicals\n\n### Leather Boots\n- Regular conditioning with leather conditioner or oil\n- Waterproofing spray/cream (especially in wet climates)\n- Clean regularly with soft dry cloth\n- Store in cool, dry place away from sunlight\n- Avoid extreme temperatures (near heaters or freezing)\n- Use leather protector on high-wear areas\n- Avoid over-conditioning\n- Professional maintenance by cobbler for polishing/conditioning\n\n### White Sneakers (Adidas Superstars)\n- Soft-bristled brush or cloth to remove loose dirt\n- Gentle cleaning solution: mild soap + warm water (or equal water + white vinegar)\n- Dampen cloth and wipe entire surface including tongue, laces, sole\n- Gentle scrubbing for stubborn scuff marks\n- Rinse with clean damp cloth\n- Air dry at room temperature\n- Avoid harsh chemicals and rough cloths\n- Consider fabric protector or waterproofing spray\n- Use sneaker cleaning products like Sneaker Cleaner or Jason Markk for tough stains
