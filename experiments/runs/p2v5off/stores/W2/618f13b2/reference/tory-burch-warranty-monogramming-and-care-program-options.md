Tory Burch offers 2-year limited warranty on handbags (covers defects in materials and workmanship). Tory Burch Care program adds 1 additional year for around 30 dollars. Monogramming available on many bags: 2-3 weeks turnaround, costs 10-30 dollars depending on bag and options. Care program includes priority customer service and dedicated concierge service. Register bags on Tory Burch website for warranty coverage.
