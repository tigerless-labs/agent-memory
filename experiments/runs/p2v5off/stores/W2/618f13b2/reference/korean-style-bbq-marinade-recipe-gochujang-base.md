Basic Korean BBQ marinade for 1-2 pounds of meat.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp gochugaru (optional)
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (optional)
- Black pepper to taste

**Instructions:** Blend until smooth, taste and adjust, coat meat in marinade, massage to ensure even coating, refrigerate 2+ hours or overnight (6-8 hours for more intense flavor).

**Variations:** Bulgogi (sweet/savory with soy and sugar), galbi (spicy/savory with gochujang and garlic), dak galbi (spicy/sweet). User plans to experiment and adjust to personal taste.
