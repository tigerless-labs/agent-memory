## Companies implementing blockchain/IoT

- **Walmart**: blockchain for tracking food origins (traceability, safety)
- **Coca-Cola**: blockchain for bottling equipment tracking (cost reduction, transparency)
- **IBM & Maersk**: TradeLens platform (blockchain-based global shipping, improved visibility)
- **Nestle**: IoT sensors for product quality/safety monitoring during transport (waste reduction)
- **Provenance**: blockchain platform for consumer product origin tracing (transparency, trust)

## Industries with high potential
Healthcare, Finance, Agriculture, Energy, Manufacturing

## Key benefits
Enhanced transparency, real-time monitoring, sustainability tracking, automation, reduced errors

## Key risks to manage
Security vulnerabilities, interoperability issues, regulatory uncertainty, privacy concerns
