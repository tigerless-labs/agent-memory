As of 2021, ~3,000 languages classified as endangered. Classification criteria: number of speakers, age of speakers, intergenerational transmission, language prestige, political/economic factors. Preservation methods include documentation, revitalization programs, digital archives, immersion schools, community-driven efforts. Challenges: limited resources, lack of written materials, social/cultural barriers, language attrition, cultural misappropriation risks. Preservation value: maintains diversity, preserves traditional knowledge, strengthens identity, cognitive benefits of multilingualism.
