Planning to organize garage storage for bikes. Considering multiple storage solutions: wall/ceiling-mounted hooks, free-standing or wall-mounted bike racks, ceiling hoists/lifts, floor-standing racks, padded bike shelves, or lockable storage cabinets. Factors to consider: space constraints, bike types (road, mountain, hybrid), protection from dust/moisture, budget. Popular brands: Saris, Thule, Topeak, Delta Cycle, Feedback Sports.
