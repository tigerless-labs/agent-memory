**Everlane**: Transparent sustainable brand with minimalistic aesthetic, timeless designs, detailed production info. Good men's clothing selection (t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, shoes).

**Pact**: Affordable organic cotton clothing for men, women, and children with fair labor practices.

**H&M Conscious**: H&M's sustainable collection using organic cotton, recycled polyester, and Tencel.

**Patagonia**: Sustainable outdoor brand with mid-range/affordable options in organic cotton, recycled polyester, and hemp.

**Levi's**: Denim brand with affordable sustainable options using recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton.

**Premium sustainable brands**: Stella McCartney, Eileen Fisher, Honest By.
