**Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) measurement guide (per 2 cups vegetables):**
- Mild: 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon
- Medium: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon  
- Hot: 1 to 2 teaspoons
- Extremely Hot: 2+ teaspoons

**Garlic preparation:**
- Use 2-3 fresh cloves per 2 cups vegetables
- Mince or crush to release oils and flavor
- Mix with other ingredients (gochugaru, fish sauce, ginger)

**Ginger preparation:**
- Use 1-2 inches of fresh ginger per 2 cups vegetables (peeled and grated or sliced)
- Ginger can be potent, so adjust to taste
- Mix with other ingredients

**Key principles:**
- Gochugaru has balanced medium-to-hot heat and good flavor profile for kimchi
- Start with small amounts, taste as you go
- Measure by weight when possible for accuracy
- Rinse vegetables after salting to remove excess salt
- Different brands of gochugaru vary in heat level
