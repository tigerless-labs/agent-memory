## Recommended Graphic Novels (similar to Saga)

Given on 2023-05-22 when user was seeking recommendations similar to **Saga** by Brian K. Vaughan and Fiona Staples.

1. **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu & Sana Takeda — Fantasy epic with stunning art, complex characters, moral ambiguity
2. **The Wicked + The Divine** by Kieron Gillen & Jamie McKelvie — Mythology meets pop culture; gods as pop stars
3. **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire & Dean Ormston — Superhero deconstruction, heroes on rural farm
4. **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way & Gabriel Bá — Dysfunctional superhero family, action/drama/humor
5. **Descender** by Jeff Lemire & Dustin Nguyen — Sci-fi epic; young robot boy hunted by humans
6. **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron & R.M. Guéra — Dark, gritty biblical/mythological reimagining
7. **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan & Pia Guerra — Last human male after mysterious event
