Snake Plant: Balanced 20-20-20 NPK, pH 6.0-7.0, water-soluble. Brands: Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food, Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer, Schultz All Purpose Plant Food.

Succulents: High phosphorus 10-20-10, low nitrogen, water-soluble diluted half-strength. Brands: Miracle-Gro Cactus/Palm/Citrus Food, Espoma Organic Cactus Fertilizer, Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food.

Orchids: High phosphorus 10-20-10 or 15-30-15, low nitrogen, pH 5.5-7.0. Use orchid-specific formulas only. Fertilize monthly at half strength. Brands: Miracle-Gro Orchid Food 30-10-10, Espoma Organic Orchid Fertilizer 15-30-15, Schultz Orchid Food 20-20-20.

All: Dilute to half strength, fertilize spring/summer only, use water-soluble formulas.
