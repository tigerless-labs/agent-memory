## Recommended Contemporary Fiction (May 2023)

Books recommended alongside **The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo**, featuring strong female protagonists and complex relationships.

1. **The Devil Wears Prada** by Lauren Weisberger — Demanding boss/assistant dynamic, female-focused relationships
2. **The Mothers** by Brit Bennett — Coming-of-age, themes of motherhood and identity in Southern California
3. **The Girls** by Emma Cline — 1960s, teenage girls in cult setting, female dynamics and power
4. **The Interestings** by Meg Wolitzer — Group of friends from arts camp; complex relationships over decades
5. **Conversations with Friends** by Sally Rooney — Complex relationships between two young women and a couple; power, identity, desire
6. **The Female Persuasion** by Meg Wolitzer — Young woman's complex relationship with famous feminist mentor
7. **The Last Romantics** by Tara Conklin — Four siblings; complex relationships shaping life over years
8. **The Age of Light** by Whitney Scharer — Based on Lee Miller; model/photographer in 1920s-30s Paris; complex relationships with men and identity

All feature strong female protagonists with relationship complexity and character development.
