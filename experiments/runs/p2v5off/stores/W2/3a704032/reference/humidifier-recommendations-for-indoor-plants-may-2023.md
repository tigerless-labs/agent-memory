Humidifier models recommended:
1. Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Humidifier - cool/warm mist, adjustable humidistat, 6-liter tank
2. Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier - compact, 1-liter tank, easy-to-use
3. Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier - ultrasonic, 2.5-liter tank, built-in humidistat, sleek design
4. Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier - compact, 1-liter tank, whisper-quiet, easy cleaning

Placement: 3-5 feet from plants for general plants, 1-2 feet for African violets.
Humidity target: 50-60% for African violets, higher for ferns.
Best type: Cool mist for most plants, especially African violets.
Key features: adjustable mist output, humidistat, easy cleaning, low noise.
