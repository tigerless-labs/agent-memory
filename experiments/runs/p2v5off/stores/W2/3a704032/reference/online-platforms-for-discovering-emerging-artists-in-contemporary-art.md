Platforms recommended for discovering emerging artists and contemporary art:

**General platforms:**
- Artsy — emerging artists section, exhibitions, filters by medium/style
- Saatchi Art — focuses on emerging artists, curated collections
- Behance — creatives showcase work including artists, designers, illustrators
- Instagram — hashtags: #emergingartist, #artoftheday, #contemporaryart, #artistsofinstagram
- Juxtapoz — art magazine with artist profiles and interviews
- Hyperallergic — art blog with news, exhibitions, artist profiles
- New American Paintings — emerging painters from US, bi-monthly magazine
- ArtNet — news, exhibitions, artist profiles, emerging artists section
- The Artist's Institute — exhibitions, interviews, essays
- Platform — showcases emerging artists worldwide

**Curated platforms:**
- Artspace — contemporary art, emerging artists, curated selection, direct purchase
- 1stdibs — curated contemporary art, design, antiques
- Uprise Art — contemporary art, emerging artists, purchase available
- Minted — contemporary art and design
- Collecteurs — contemporary art, emerging artists, curated experience

Many offer personalized recommendations, artist advisory, virtual events, and artist spotlights.
