## Music Programming Languages (sorted by beginner-friendliness)

**Easy to Learn:**
- **Sonic Pi** — Live coding environment, simple syntax, real-time feedback, designed for music education
- **ChucK** — Free and open-source, designed specifically for music programming, simple syntax with powerful audio tools
- **Python** — General-purpose language, large community, extensive documentation, good for music production libraries

**More Advanced:**
- **Max/MSP** — Visual programming language, widely used in electronic music, custom instruments and effects, more complex syntax but visual interface
- **Pure Data (PD)** — Free and open-source, similar to Max/MSP, interactive music and multimedia, smaller community than Max
- **SuperCollider** — Real-time audio synthesis and algorithmic composition, supports many synthesis methods, used for experimental music
- **Csound** — Software synthesis system, C-like language, 30+ years of development, powerful for complex musical structures
- **Swift** — Apple's language, growing popularity for iOS/macOS music apps

Each language has different strengths; choice depends on specific goals and learning style.
