Recommended tools for interactive discussion and reflection:
- **Padlet**: Collaborative platform for sharing thoughts and ideas in virtual space
- **Nearpod**: Interactive platform with polls, open-ended questions, and student participation features
- **Flipgrid**: Video discussion platform where students respond to prompts with short video responses
- **Google Jamboard**: Virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual idea-sharing

All tools enable engaging, interactive reflection and discussion.
