Music room organization approach:
- Prefers vertical storage (wall-mounted shelves, hooks) to maximize floor space
- Likes flexibility in layout for future additions
- Values categorizing and grouping similar items

Recording and content creation:
- Prefers starting with cover songs over tutorials for confidence building
- Plans to use phone camera rather than investing in expensive equipment initially
- Interested in learning as they go (beginner approach)
- Values practical tips for audio/video quality
- Plans to share content online (likely YouTube or similar platforms)
