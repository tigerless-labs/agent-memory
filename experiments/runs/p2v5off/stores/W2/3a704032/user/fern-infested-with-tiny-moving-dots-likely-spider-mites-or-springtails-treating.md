**Plant:** Fern
**Pest identification:** Tiny moving dots on leaves and stems, likely spider mites or springtails
**Treatment plan:** 
- Applying neem oil per product instructions
- Misting 2-3 times weekly with distilled/filtered water (morning preferred)
- Improving air circulation
- Monitoring closely for effectiveness

**Misting tips:**
- Mist undersides of leaves where pests hide
- Avoid misting crown (where leaves meet stem)
- Target humidity: 40-60% relative humidity
- Timing: Morning to allow drying before nightfall

**Follow-up:** Monitor plant response and adjust treatment as needed; may need repeat applications
