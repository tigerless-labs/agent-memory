**Location:** Balcony (east or west-facing preferred)

**Solution:** Relocating to shadier spot with filtered/indirect sunlight; adjusting watering schedule for reduced soil drying

**Care routine:** Regular pruning to encourage bushy growth, monitoring for healthy response to shade adjustment
