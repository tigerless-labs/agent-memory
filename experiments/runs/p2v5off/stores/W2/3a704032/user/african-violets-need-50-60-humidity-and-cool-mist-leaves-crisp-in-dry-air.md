African violets in collection are sensitive to low humidity. Leaves become crispy and brittle when air is too dry. Preference to maintain 50-60% relative humidity using cool mist humidifier positioned 1-2 feet away from plants. Avoid over-humidification to prevent root rot. Require good air circulation to prevent fungal diseases while maintaining humidity.
