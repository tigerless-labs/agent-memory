Repotting spider plant (potting mix aging, needs refresh).

Potting mix recommendations for spider plants (well-draining, rich in organic matter):
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix (general purpose)
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix (organic, peat/compost/perlite blend)
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix (nutrient-rich, eco-friendly)
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix (professional-grade)

Repotting steps:
1. Choose pot only 1-2 sizes larger (spider plants prefer slight root-binding)
2. Gently remove plant, inspect roots for rot or damage
3. Trim damaged roots
4. Fill new pot with fresh potting mix
5. Firmly settle mix around roots
6. Water thoroughly after repotting
7. Keep soil consistently moist but not waterlogged

Key ingredients to look for: peat moss/coconut coir, compost, perlite/vermiculite, worm castings.
