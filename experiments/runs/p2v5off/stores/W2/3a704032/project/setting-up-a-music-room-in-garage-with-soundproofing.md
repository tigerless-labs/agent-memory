Planning to convert garage into a dedicated music room. Key focus areas for soundproofing: thin walls and ceilings, gaps around doors and windows, hollow doors, uninsulated floors. Considering acoustic panels, mass-loaded vinyl, resilient channels, and drop ceiling. Goal is to practice without disturbing others and improve sound quality.
