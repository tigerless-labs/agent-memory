For reflection and discussion time to have maximum educational effect, use these methods:
- Ask open-ended questions requiring thoughtful answers, not yes/no responses
- Paraphrase and summarize student contributions to validate their thinking
- Encourage active listening—ask peers to summarize what they heard
- Use visual aids like diagrams or graphic organizers to help students organize thoughts
