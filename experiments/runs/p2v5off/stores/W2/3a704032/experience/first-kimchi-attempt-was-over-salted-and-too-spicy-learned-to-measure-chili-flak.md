Made first kimchi batch on or around 2023-05-15. The batch turned out over-salted and too spicy, which prompted research into proper measurement and flavor balancing.

**Key learnings:**
- Started with too much gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
- Added too much salt
- Found that measuring by weight is more accurate than volume
- Learned to taste as you go and adjust gradually

**Resolution plan:**
- Rinse kimchi under cold running water to remove excess salt
- Reseason with measured amounts of gochugaru, garlic, and ginger
- Apply measurement guidelines going forward
