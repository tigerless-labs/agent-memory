Peace lily acquired May 6, 2023, experiencing leaf drop after being brought home. This is normal plant stress during environmental transition. Addressed through: proper lighting (bright, indirect; avoid direct sun), careful watering (soil dry to first knuckle before watering), increasing humidity above 50%, maintaining temperature 65-80°F, fertilizing with balanced formula during spring/summer at half strength, removing dead leaves, monitoring for pests. Plant expected to recover within a few weeks of adjustment.
