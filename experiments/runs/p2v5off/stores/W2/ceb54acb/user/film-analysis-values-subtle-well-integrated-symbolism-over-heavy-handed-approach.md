Values film symbolism and cinematographic techniques. Appreciates how filmmakers use visual language (lighting, color, objects) to convey deeper meaning — e.g., oranges in The Godfather foreshadowing danger.

Prefers symbolism that enhances rather than overwhelms the story. Finds heavy-handed or overused symbolism distracting and pretentious. Believes the best symbolic use is thoughtful and sparse, creating emotional connection without taking away from narrative.

Interested in analyzing how technical elements (direction, cinematography, editing, music) combine to create thematic depth.

Films discussed: The Godfather, The Shining, Blade Runner, Get Out, The Matrix, Casablanca, The Shawshank Redemption, Star Wars.
