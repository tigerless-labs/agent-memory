Films cited as examples of effective symbolism:

The Shining (1980) — Stanley Kubrick horror. Symbolic imagery includes maze representing characters' minds, color red symbolizing danger and violence.

Blade Runner (1982) — Ridley Scott sci-fi. Uses symbolism like eyes, animals, and nature to explore themes of identity, humanity, and nature of existence.

Get Out (2017) — Jordan Peele thriller. Color white symbolizing oppressive nature of racism, repeated deer imagery symbolizing victimhood.

The Matrix (1999) — Wachowskis sci-fi. Red and blue pills symbolize choice and free will; explores nature of reality and human perception.

Films considered great without relying heavily on symbolism:

Casablanca (1942)
The Shawshank Redemption (1994)
Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope (1977)

Note: Symbolism can enhance a film but is not necessary for greatness. However, when overused or heavy-handed, it can be distracting and seem pretentious.
