Key elements: Direction by Francis Ford Coppola (detail, pacing, character development); Acting by Brando and Pacino; Score by Nino Rota; Cinematography by Gordon Willis (light and shadow); Screenplay by Coppola and Puzo; Seamless editing.

Horse head scene: Iconic moment showing brutal nature of Corleone family business. Pivotal to the film.

Oranges: Symbolic of impending danger and death in scenes before violent events (Don Corleone at fruit market, garden assassination attempt). Creates foreshadowing and inevitability. Coppola and Willis have not officially confirmed this interpretation.

Overall: Technical mastery combined with emotional impact makes it a masterpiece.
