Vinyl: Discogs, MusicStack, Reverb LP, local record stores. Rare Books: Abebooks, Biblio, Alibris, Strand, Powell's, auction houses. Cameras: eBay, Etsy, KEH, Leica Store, Camera West. Typewriters: eBay, Etsy, Typewriter Collector, Gramercy. Jewelry: Find appraisers via ASA/NAJA/ISA. Coins: NGC, APMEX, Liberty Coin, Heritage Auctions. Tips: research comps, get professional appraisals for valuable items, provide quality photos, include provenance, package carefully, expect negotiation.
