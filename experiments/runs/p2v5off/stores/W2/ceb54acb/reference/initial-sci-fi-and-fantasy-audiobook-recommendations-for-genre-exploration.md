Initial audiobook recommendations for expanding from romance/mystery into sci-fi and fantasy:

Science Fiction:
- The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin (narrated by Luke Daniels) — Hugo Award-winning, first contact with alien civilization. User already listened and enjoyed.
- Ready Player One by Ernest Cline (narrated by Wil Wheaton) — nostalgic, fast-paced, dystopian future with OASIS virtual reality
- The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude) — character-driven, diverse crew, tunneling ship journey

Fantasy:
- The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August by Claire North (narrated by Peter Kenny) — reincarnation, time travel, complex plot
- The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (narrated by Simon Vance) — con artists in fantasy world, witty dialogue
- The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison (narrated by Kyle McCarley) — young goblin emperor navigating court politics

All selected for engaging stories, relatable characters, and quality narration.
