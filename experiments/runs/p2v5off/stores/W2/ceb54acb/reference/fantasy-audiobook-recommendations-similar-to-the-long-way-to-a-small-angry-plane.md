Character-driven fantasy recommendations with similar tone/style to The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet:

1. The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard (narrated by Anne Flosnik) — dark fantasy set in post-apocalyptic Paris
2. The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker (narrated by George Guidall) — historical fantasy in early 20th-century NYC
3. The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty (narrated by Soneela Nankani) — Middle Eastern/South Asian mythology, young con artist protagonist
4. The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) — fantasy novella with god training
5. The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller) — inspired by Chinese history and mythology, military academy setting

All share: character-driven narratives, strong world-building, emphasis on character development and relationships.
