## Context
Preparing for promotion interview to manager of information desk role at a small private college (May 2023).

## Interview Questions Prepared
Closing questions to ask during interview:
- Questions about concerns/reservations about qualifications
- Questions about role fit within department/college goals  
- Questions about upcoming initiatives
- Skill development opportunities
- Department challenges and opportunities
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Available support and resources
- Communication/collaboration approaches
- Employer expectations and alignment
- Timeline for decision

## Approach
Asked employees about:
- Most important responsibilities of the role
- Role in serving students, faculty, staff
- Challenges faced and college support
- Cross-departmental collaboration
- Essential qualities for success
- Work culture and values alignment
- Professional development opportunities
- Success measurement and metrics
- Feedback from campus community
- Upcoming initiatives the role would be involved in
