Visited the Science Museum's 'Space Exploration' exhibition with colleague David, spending approximately two hours exploring the interactive exhibits and viewing actual moon rocks on display. The museum also has permanent Space Galleries featuring artifacts like the Soyuz T-1 spacecraft and a full-size replica of the Eagle lunar module.
