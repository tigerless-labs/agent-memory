Attended guided tour at Modern Art Museum led by Dr. Patel focusing on abstract expressionism. The tour explored Jackson Pollock and Mark Rothko's work in depth, with insights into their emotional expression and meaning. Was deeply moved by a large-scale installation by Anselm Kiefer. This reinforced broader interest in understanding abstract expressionism and the techniques these artists used to convey emotion through non-representational means.
