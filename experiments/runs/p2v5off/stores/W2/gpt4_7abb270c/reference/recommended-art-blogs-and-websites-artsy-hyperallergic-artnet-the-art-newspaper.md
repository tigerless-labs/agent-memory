**Recommended art blogs and websites for staying updated on latest trends and exhibitions:**\n\n1. **Artsy** — Comprehensive online platform for art, featuring news, exhibitions, artist profiles, and market trends\n2. **Hyperallergic** — Leading art blog covering news, reviews, and opinion pieces on contemporary art, with focus on New York City\n3. **Artnet** — Source for art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists and trends\n4. **The Art Newspaper** — Daily online publication covering international art news, exhibitions, and market analysis\n5. **Contemporary Art Daily** — Resource for discovering new artists and exhibitions, focus on contemporary art\n6. **Art in America** — Magazine covering contemporary art with in-depth features, reviews, and interviews\n7. **Frieze** — Leading art magazine covering contemporary art, culture, and politics, focus on international exhibitions and events\n8. **Apollo Magazine** — British art magazine covering art, architecture, and collecting, focus on historical and contemporary art\n9. **Art21** — Non-profit organization producing documentaries and online content on contemporary art and artists\n10. **Culture Trip** — Website covering art, culture, and travel, with focus on emerging artists and underground scenes
