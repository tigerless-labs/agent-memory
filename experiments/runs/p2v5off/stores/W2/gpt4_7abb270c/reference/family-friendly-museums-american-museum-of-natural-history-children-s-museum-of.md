Museum recommendations for family visits with children:

Science and Interactive:
- American Museum of Natural History - family tours, discovery guides
- New York Hall of Science - interactive physics, chemistry, biology exhibits; 3D theater
- Intrepid Sea Air and Space Museum - aircraft carrier, spacecraft, maritime history

Art Museums for Kids:
- Children's Museum of the Arts - interactive exhibits for ages 10 and under
- Guggenheim Museum - Frank Lloyd Wright architecture, modern and contemporary art
- Whitney Museum of American Art - American art from 20th-21st centuries
- Brooklyn Museum - art history spanning 5000 years
- Studio Museum in Harlem - family programs, African American art focus

Metropolitan Museum exhibits:
- Temple of Dendur - ancient Egyptian temple from 10 BC
- The American Wing - American art colonial to early 20th century
- The Cloisters - medieval art and architecture
- Roof Garden - Central Park views with rotating installations

Design focused:
- Museum of Arts and Design (MAD) - art, craft, and design intersection
