**Recommended books on feminist art:**\n\n1. **"The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard** — Comprehensive history of feminist art from 1960s to 1990s\n2. **"Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss** — Explores relationship between feminist art and the maternal, featuring artists like Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles\n3. **"Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin** — Collection of essays covering topics like representation of women in art and feminism's role in art history\n4. **"The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard** — Detailed examination of feminist art movement in US during 1970s\n5. **"Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls** — Collection of essays and artworks by the anonymous feminist art collective, known for bold and humorous critiques of the art world
