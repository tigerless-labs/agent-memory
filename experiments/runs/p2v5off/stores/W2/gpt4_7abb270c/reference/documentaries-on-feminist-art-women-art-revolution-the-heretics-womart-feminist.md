**Recommended documentaries on feminist art:**\n\n1. **"!Women Art Revolution" (2010)** — Film by Lynn Hershman Leeson, featuring interviews with over 40 feminist artists including Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, and Carolee Schneemann\n2. **"The Heretics" (2009)** — Documentary about the Heresies Collective feminist art collective, featuring interviews with artists like Nancy Azara and Miriam Schapiro\n3. **"Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991)** — Documentary series by filmmaker Kate Horsfield, featuring interviews with artists like Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, and Yvonne Rainer\n4. **"Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010)** — Panel discussion featuring artists like Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, and Howardena Pindell, moderated by art historian Huey Copeland\n5. **"Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014)** — Episode of PBS series Art:21, featuring in-depth interview with Judy Chicago, pioneer of feminist art
