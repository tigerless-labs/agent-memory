Comprehensive comparison of major sustainability and climate reporting frameworks and regulations.

Key frameworks: GRI Standards, SASB Standards, TCFD Recommendations, IR Framework, EU Taxonomy Regulation, SFDR, CSRD, and CDP.

NFRD applicability: Large public-interest entities with more than 500 employees and balance sheet total over 20M euros or net turnover over 40M euros.

NFRD format: No mandatory format; encourages use of recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD).

CSRD format: European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) using Inline XBRL (iXBRL). Introduced 2024; reporting required from 2026 onwards. Expands to cover all large companies in EU.

CDP: Voluntary framework for climate disclosure, complementary to other frameworks, aligned with TCFD recommendations.
