## 4K OLED TV Research: LG vs Sony\n\n### LG OLED TVs\n- **Panel Quality**: Perfect blacks, infinite contrast, excellent color accuracy\n- **Color Gamut**: Wide color range for vivid, lifelike images\n- **HDR**: Supports Dolby Vision, HDR10, HLG\n- **Smart Platform**: webOS - user-friendly with broad app support\n- **Gaming**: Low input lag\n- **Price**: Slightly more affordable than Sony\n\n**Popular Models**:\n- LG C9 Series (2019)\n- LG B9 Series (2019)\n- LG CX Series (2020)\n- LG GX Series (2020)\n\n### Sony OLED TVs\n- **Panel Quality**: Perfect blacks, wide viewing angles, excellent processing\n- **Processor**: X1 Ultimate with object-based HDR remastering\n- **Audio**: Acoustic Surface Audio uses screen as speaker\n- **Smart Platform**: Android TV with Google Assistant built-in\n- **Gaming**: Advanced features including HDMI 2.1, variable refresh rate (VRR)\n- **Price**: More expensive than LG\n\n**Popular Models**:\n- Sony A9G Series (2019)\n- Sony A8G Series (2019)\n- Sony A1E Series (2020)\n- Sony A9S Series (2020)\n\n### Key Differences\n- Panel Quality: Both excellent; LG slight edge on color accuracy and viewing angles\n- Smart Platform: webOS generally more user-friendly\n- Gaming: Sony has more advanced gaming features\n- Audio: Sony has Acoustic Surface Audio feature\n- Price: LG more affordable\n\n### Status\nResearch in progress as of December 22, 2022. Not yet decided between brands/models.
