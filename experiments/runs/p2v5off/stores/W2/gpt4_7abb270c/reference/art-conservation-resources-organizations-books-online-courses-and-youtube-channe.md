## Organizations\n- **American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC)**: Professional organization with online courses, webinars, publications\n- **International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC)**: Features articles, news, and events on art conservation\n- **Conservation Wiki**: Collaborative platform with info on techniques, materials, and best practices\n\n## Books\n- *The Conservation of Art and Architecture* by James C. Y. Watt\n- *The Art of Conservation: Principles and Theory* by Caroline K. Keck\n- *Conservation of Cultural Heritage: A Practical Guide* by John H. Stubbs\n\n## Online Platforms\n- YouTube channels: Art Conservation, Conservation Framing, The Getty Conservation Institute\n- Coursera, edX, Udemy offer online courses on art conservation and cultural heritage\n\n## Key Topics Covered\n- Adhesives: Acrylic polymers, animal glues, cellulose-based adhesives, epoxy resins, natural waxes\n- Consolidants: Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98, Parylene, Silanes\n- Papers: Cotton paper, linen paper, rag paper, kozu paper, gampi paper\n- Mounting techniques: Hinging, window mounting, float mounting\n- Framing: Conservation framing, reversible framing, specialized glazing
