## Dell XPS vs MacBook Air Comparison\n\n### Dell XPS\n- **OS**: Windows 10\n- **Design**: Compact, lightweight aluminum and carbon fiber\n- **Display**: InfinityEdge with minimal bezels; 4K touch and OLED options\n- **Performance**: Up to 11th Gen Intel Core i9, 64GB RAM, 2TB SSD\n- **Battery**: Claimed up to 18 hours 49 minutes\n- **Ports**: 2x Thunderbolt 3 (USB-C), 1x USB-A, HDMI, headphone jack\n- **Gaming**: Suitable with configurable graphics options\n- **Price**: More affordable than MacBook Air\n\n### MacBook Air\n- **OS**: macOS\n- **Design**: Sleek aluminum unibody, slightly larger\n- **Display**: Retina display with high resolution and excellent color accuracy\n- **Performance**: Apple M1 chip; suitable for productivity, web development, creative tasks\n- **Battery**: Claimed up to 18 hours\n- **Ports**: 2x Thunderbolt 3 (USB-C), headphone jack only (no USB-A or HDMI)\n- **Gaming**: Lower performance for gaming\n- **Price**: More expensive than Dell XPS\n\n### Key Differences\n- Operating system preference (Windows vs macOS)\n- Gaming capability importance\n- Ecosystem compatibility (Apple ecosystem)\n- Port availability and flexibility\n- Price and affordability\n\n### Decision Made (December 22, 2022)\nChose Dell XPS for Windows OS preference, gaming potential, and better pricing.
