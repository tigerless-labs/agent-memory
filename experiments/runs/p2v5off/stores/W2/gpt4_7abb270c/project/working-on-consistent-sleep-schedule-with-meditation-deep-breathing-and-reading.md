Goal: establish a consistent sleep schedule for better time management. Wind-down routine approach: 30-60 minutes before bed with meditation, deep breathing exercises (using apps like Headspace or Calm), gentle yoga/stretching, journaling, and reading. Prefers prioritizing reading as part of the routine while remaining flexible about fitting it in throughout the day. Dark, cool bedroom (60-67°F), consistent bedtime/wake time.
