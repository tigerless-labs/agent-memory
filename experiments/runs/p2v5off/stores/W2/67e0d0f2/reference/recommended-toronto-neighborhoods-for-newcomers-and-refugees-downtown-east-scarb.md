Recommended neighborhoods for refugees and newcomers in Toronto:

**Downtown East (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown)**
- High concentration of newcomer communities
- Many refugee organizations, community centers, language schools
- Close to city center; easy access to services

**Scarborough**
- Large immigrant population
- Refugee communities from Africa, Asia, Middle East
- Scarborough Village Recreation Centre: newcomer services, language classes, cultural programs

**North York**
- Significant refugee population
- Support organizations: North York Community House, Centre for Information and Community Services
- Close to York University (strong refugee support program)

**Etobicoke**
- Growing refugee community
- Support organizations: Etobicoke Newcomer Centre, West Toronto Newcomer Services
- Close to Toronto Pearson International Airport

**Parkdale**
- History of welcoming refugees and immigrants
- Strong sense of community, many social services
- Close to city center; vibrant arts and cultural scene

**Selection factors to consider:**
- Proximity to community centers, language schools, support services
- Public transportation access
- Grocery stores and healthcare services
- Safety and walkability
- Affordable housing and amenities (parks, recreation)
- Cultural and linguistic diversity
