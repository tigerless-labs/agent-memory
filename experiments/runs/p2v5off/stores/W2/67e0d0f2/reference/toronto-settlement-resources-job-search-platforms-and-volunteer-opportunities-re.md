**Settlement Organizations (Toronto):**
- The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO)
- COSTI Immigrant Services
- WoodGreen Community Services
- The Refugee Sponsorship Support Program (RSSP)
- Toronto Refugee Centre
- North York Community House
- Etobicoke Newcomer Centre
- West Toronto Newcomer Services

**Housing:**
- Toronto Affordable Housing Office
- City of Toronto Rent Supplement Program
- YMCA and Salvation Army subsidized housing

**Language Programs:**
- Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada (LINC)
- Community center English classes

**Job Search:**
- Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca)
- Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor
- Settlement agencies for job search support

**Volunteer Opportunities:**
- Volunteer Toronto
- Idealist
- LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace
