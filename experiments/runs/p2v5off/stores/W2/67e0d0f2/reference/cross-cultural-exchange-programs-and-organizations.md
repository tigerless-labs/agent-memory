## Organizations & Resources for Cross-Cultural Exchange

### AFS Intercultural Programs
- Website: https://afs.org/
- One of the largest non-profit organizations for intercultural learning
- Offers: student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering opportunities
- Coverage: 50+ countries

### Experiment in International Living
- Website: https://www.experiment.org/
- Immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students
- Format: live with host families, attend local schools, community service projects

### Youth for Understanding (YFU)
- Website: https://www.yfu.org/
- International educational exchange organization for students and young adults
- Options: year-long exchanges, semester programs, summer programs
- Coverage: 40+ countries

### Cross-Cultural Solutions
- Website: https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/
- Volunteering opportunities for all ages
- Locations: Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
- Focus: community development projects and cross-cultural exchange
