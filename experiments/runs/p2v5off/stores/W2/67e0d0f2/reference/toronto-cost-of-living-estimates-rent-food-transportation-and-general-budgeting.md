Rent (Monthly):
- Bachelor/1-bed shared: $1,200–$1,600
- 1-bedroom apartment: $1,600–$2,000
- 2-bedroom: $1,800–$2,500
- 3-bedroom: $2,500–$3,500

Food (Monthly):
- Groceries: $500–$750
- Casual dining: $10–$15/meal
- Mid-range restaurants: $20–$30/meal
- Delivery/takeout: $10–$20/meal

Transportation:
- TTC monthly pass: $156.00 (adult)
- Single fare: $3.00 (cash), $2.90 (Presto card)
- Bike Share Toronto: $15–$20/month
- Car ownership: gas, parking, maintenance significant

Other Monthly Expenses:
- Utilities (hydro, water, internet): $100–$200
- Phone + internet: $50–$100
- Entertainment: $200–$500

Income Reference:
- Ontario minimum wage (2022): $15.00/hour
- Entry-level job average: $35,000–$50,000/year

Budgeting Tips:
- Budget 10–20% savings
- Consider roommates to split rent
- Public libraries, parks, community events are free/low-cost

Government Support:
- Ontario Works program available for eligible individuals
