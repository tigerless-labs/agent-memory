## Books for Cultural Learning

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri - Identity, family, and culture through a Bengali-American family lens
2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini - Childhood friends in Afghanistan; friendship, betrayal, and redemption
3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan - Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers
4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe - Nigerian tribe's life before and after British colonization
5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi - Graphic novel following an Iranian girl during the Islamic Revolution

## Movies for Cultural Learning

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** - Martial arts epic in ancient China; loyalty and honor themes
2. **The Lives of Others** - German film set in East Berlin during Cold War; surveillance and human connection
3. **Lion** - True story of an Indian boy lost and adopted by an Australian family; journey of discovery
4. **The Motorcycle Diaries** - Based on Che Guevara's travel journals across South America; social injustice
5. **City of God** - Brazilian film about life in a favela; poverty, violence, and survival themes
