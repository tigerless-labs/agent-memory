Planning to make cold brew coffee using dark roasted beans. Preferred brewing parameters:
- Ratio: 1 part coffee to 4 parts water (classic; can adjust to 1:3 for stronger or 1:5 for weaker)
- Grind: Coarse grind using burr grinder
- Steeping time: 12-24 hours (can experiment with shorter times for lighter extract)
- Optional: bloom with small amount of hot water first
- Consider adding flavorings like vanilla, cinnamon, or citrus zest for variations

Dark roast chosen for its bold, rich flavor with chocolate/caramel/smoky notes and lower acidity.
