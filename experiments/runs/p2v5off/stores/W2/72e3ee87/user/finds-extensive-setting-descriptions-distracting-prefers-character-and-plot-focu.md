While reading *Wuthering Heights*, found the detailed descriptions of moors, weather, and landscape excessive and distracting from the story and characters. Tends to skim over heavy setting descriptions. Prefers prose where setting is integrated organically into character development and plot, rather than presented as separate descriptive passages. Values efficient storytelling that prioritizes character dynamics and narrative momentum over atmospheric detail.
