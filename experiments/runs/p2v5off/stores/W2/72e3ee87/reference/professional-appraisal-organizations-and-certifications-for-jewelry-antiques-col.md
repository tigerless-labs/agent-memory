For jewelry: NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers), AGTA (American Gem Trade Association), ASA (American Society of Appraisers). For antiques/collectibles: AAA (Appraisers Association of America), ISA (International Society of Appraisers), NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers). Ensure appraiser is certified, experienced with similar items, impartial (not affiliated with dealers), has professional affiliations, and recognized by insurance industry.
