**Band**: The Postal Service — indie-electronic duo of Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

**Album**: Give Up (2003)

**Standout tracks to listen to**:
- "Such Great Heights" — dreamy, synth-driven anthem about new love
- "The District Sleeps Alone Tonight" — melancholy, introspective about disconnection and insomnia
- "Clark Gable" — quirky, upbeat with charming romantic lyrics
- "We Will Become Silhouettes" — somber, reflective about nuclear apocalypse aftermath
- "Recycled Air" — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar, propulsive mood
- "Sleeping In" — multiple vocal tracks in harmony/counterpoint, orchestral complexity

**What makes them notable**: Blended indie rock with electronic elements; used acoustic instruments alongside synthesizers; employed vocal layering and harmonies to create textured sound. Genre-defining album that appealed to both indie and electronic music scenes.
