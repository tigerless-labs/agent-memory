Specialty Insurance: Jewelers Mutual (jewelry, vintage, heirloom pieces), Chubb (high-value jewelry/art/collectibles), AXA ART (art, antiques, collectibles). Standard companies with specialty departments: State Farm, Allstate Valuable Items, Liberty Mutual Valuable Possessions. Look for: agreed value coverage, low/no deductible, worldwide coverage, mysterious disappearance coverage. Consider independent brokers for better rates.
