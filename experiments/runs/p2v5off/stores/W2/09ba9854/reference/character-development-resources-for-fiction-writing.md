Recommended online resources and books for character development in fiction:

**Online Resources:**
- Writers Digest: Character Development
- The Creative Penn: Character Development  
- Helping Writers Become Authors: Character Development category
- The Write Practice: Character Development Exercises
- Novel Writing Help: Character Development Exercises
- 16 Personalities: Free personality test for character insights

**Books:**
- 'Characters & Viewpoint' by Orson Scott Card — comprehensive guide to creating believable characters
- 'The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers' by Christopher Vogler — explores hero's journey and character archetypes
- 'How to Write Short Stories and Use Them to Further Your Writing Career' by James Scott Bell — includes character development section
