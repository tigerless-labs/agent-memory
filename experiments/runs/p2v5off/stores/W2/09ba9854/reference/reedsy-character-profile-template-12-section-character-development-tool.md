The Reedsy character profile template is a comprehensive, free downloadable tool with 12 sections:

1. Basic Info (name, age, occupation)
2. Physical Appearance (height, hair, distinguishing features)
3. Personality (traits, values, goals, flaws)
4. Backstory (childhood, family, education, key events)
5. Motivations (what drives the character)
6. Conflict (internal and external challenges)
7. Relationships (family, friends, rivals, romantic)
8. Skills and Abilities (talents, expertise)
9. Fears and Phobias
10. Habits and Quirks (mannerisms, peculiarities)
11. Emotional Landscape (emotional strengths/weaknesses)
12. Evolution (character growth through the story)

Provides good balance between brevity and depth; includes example questions for brainstorming. Recommended for developing protagonists and supporting characters alike.
