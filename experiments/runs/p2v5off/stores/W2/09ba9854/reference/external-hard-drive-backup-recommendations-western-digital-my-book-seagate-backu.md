Recommendations for external drives to back up photos:
- **Western Digital My Book**: Popular, reliable, capacities up to 16TB, Mac/PC compatible, compact with built-in power management
- **Seagate Backup Plus**: Well-known, up to 5TB, sleek design, 2-year warranty
- **LaCie Rugged**: Rugged/durable design, up to 5TB, withstands rough handling
- **G-Technology G-Drive**: High-performance, up to 10TB, fast transfer speeds, designed for creative professionals

Key considerations: capacity (2-3x current needs), USB 3.0/Thunderbolt 3/USB-C interface, speed (7200rpm or SSD), durability, bus vs external power, built-in encryption/security, warranty support, portability, and price balance.

Backup strategy: Use 3-2-1 rule (three copies, two media types, one offsite); set up automatic backups; verify backups periodically.
