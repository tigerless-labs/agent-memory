**Lens Cleaning Kits:**
- Canon Lens Cleaning Kit: Brush, microfiber cloth, cleaning solution
- Nikon Lens Cleaning Kit: Suitable for Canon lenses too
- Zeiss Lens Cleaning Kit: High-quality microfiber cloth, solution, brush

**Cleaning Best Practices:**
- Use microfiber cloths (lint-free, soft)
- Apply cleaning solution to cloth, not directly to lens
- Clean in circular motion from center outward
- Store lenses with caps on, upright
- Avoid stacking lenses
- Clean before storage
- Use breathable storage to prevent moisture buildup
