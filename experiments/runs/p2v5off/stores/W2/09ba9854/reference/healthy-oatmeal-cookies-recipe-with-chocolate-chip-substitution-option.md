**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins (can substitute dark chocolate chips for chocolate option)

**Directions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease.
2. Whisk together rolled oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in large bowl.
3. In separate bowl, whisk together melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla extract.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients, mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold in raisins (or chocolate chips).
6. Using cookie scoop or spoon, drop dough onto prepared baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
7. Bake 10-12 minutes or until cookies are golden brown.
8. Let cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely.

**Note:** Dark chocolate chips (higher cocoa %, less sugar) are preferred over regular chocolate chips for healthier option.
