Interested in understanding:

- Methods for incorporating political changes and uncertainty into economic forecasts (scenario analysis, time series analysis, regression analysis, survey data, expert judgment, game theory)
- How economists account for political biases and agendas in forecasts
- Methods for determining which scenarios are most likely (statistical analysis, market expectations, polling, scenario modeling)
- Limitations and inherent uncertainty in economic prediction despite methodological sophistication
- Role of behavioral/psychological factors in economic outcomes

Recognizes that forecasting always involves uncertainty due to economy complexity, unforeseen events, and hard-to-predict behavioral factors.
