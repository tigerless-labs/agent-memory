**Setup Process:**
1. Convert Instagram account to Business Account
2. Connect Facebook Page (required for ads)
3. Use Facebook Ads Manager to create campaigns
4. Choose 'Awareness' objective to increase project visibility
5. Set up target audience based on demographics, interests, behaviors
6. Select ad format (photo, video, carousel, stories)
7. Set budget (daily or lifetime) and schedule
8. Monitor and adjust based on performance

**Metrics to Track:**
- **Reach**: Number of people who saw the ad
- **Impressions**: Number of times ad was displayed
- **Frequency**: Average times each person saw the ad (avoid ad fatigue)
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, saves, shares
- **CPM** (Cost Per Thousand Impressions): Efficiency of ad spend
- **Ad Recall Lift**: How well people remember the ad/brand (surveys/polls)
- **Brand Awareness Lift**: Increase in brand awareness (surveys/focus groups)
- **Social Media Engagement**: Followers, likes, comments, shares on organic posts

**Tools to Use:**
- Facebook Ads Manager (primary)
- Instagram Insights (organic performance)
- Third-party: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, or Agorapulse (additional analytics)

**Questions to Ask When Reviewing:**
- Is reach and impressions increasing?
- Is engagement rate improving?
- Is CPM decreasing?
- Are people remembering the brand?
