## Project: Untitled Sci-Fi Romance (Memory Thief Story)

**Protagonist:** Lyra
- Skilled memory thief with no recollection of their own past
- Has fragmented, unclear memories
- Identity and sense of self are mysterious, even to themselves

**Core Plot:**
- Lyra is hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO
- While accessing the CEO's memories, Lyra begins uncovering clues about their own past
- Must confront the possibility that their own memories may be fabricated or altered

**Themes Explored:**
- Identity and self-discovery
- Nature of memory and authenticity of experience
- What it means to be human in a world of memory manipulation

**Sci-Fi Elements:**
- Advanced neurotechnology
- Memory extraction, storage, and manipulation
- Memory marketplace (black market and regulated)

**Key Plot Twists Planned:**
1. Discovery of Lyra's true past and the reason behind their memory loss
2. Revelation of CEO's true intentions and consequences of memory theft
3. Both twists challenge Lyra's understanding of their role and relationships

**Character Arc:**
- Lyra begins as solitary, skilled thief
- Gradually opens up to others, forms connections
- Must decide whether to continue as memory thief or forge new path
- Learns to trust and accepts new/true identity

**Related Inspirations:**
- 'The Memory Thief' writing prompt (from 2023-05-20 session)
- Interest in character-driven narratives exploring identity
