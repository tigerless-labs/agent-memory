Planned a trip to Japan in May 2023 with:
- Hotel booked in Tokyo
- Arriving with significant luggage
- Budget-conscious approach to airport transportation
- Researched options: train (Narita Express, Keisei Skyliner, Tokyo Monorail), Airport Limousine Bus, taxi, private car services
- Decided to contact hotel for transportation recommendations and preferred provider partnerships
- Was quoted approximately $60 USD for taxi from airport (likely from Haneda based on pricing)
- Interested in cost-effective options around $20-30 using train + subway combination
- Noted Suica/Pasmo cards for Tokyo transit
