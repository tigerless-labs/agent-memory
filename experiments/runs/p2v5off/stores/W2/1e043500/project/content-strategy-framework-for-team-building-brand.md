Seven-step framework for building a brand around hiring and team building philosophy.

**1. Develop brand voice**
- Decide on tone and language to communicate the message
- Convey values about prioritizing human qualities in tech hiring

**2. Identify target audience**
- Who wants to hear this message
- Focus on hiring managers, team leads, company founders who care about team quality

**3. Create valuable content**
- Blog posts: explore importance of hiring humans first, identify key traits, build effective teams
- Podcasts/videos: interviews with industry experts and successful team leaders
- Webinars/workshops: practical tips and strategies for hiring and team building
- E-books/guides: in-depth exploration of psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation, collaboration culture
- Social media: share content, connect with audience, highlight case studies and team member profiles

**4. Promote content**
- Use social media, email marketing, and other channels
- Build brand awareness

**5. Engage with audience**
- Respond to comments, questions, feedback
- Build relationships and foster engagement

**6. Collaborate with thought leaders**
- Connect with industry experts
- Collaborate on content and events
- Build network and amplify message

**7. Measure results**
- Use analytics to track content performance
- Adjust strategy as needed to optimize reach and engagement
