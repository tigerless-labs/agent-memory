Research question: How are social media usage patterns associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults?

Key research directions being explored:
- Mediating roles of self-esteem and social support in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes
- Implications for mental health interventions and policies
- Leveraging social media platforms for mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, and peer-to-peer support initiatives
