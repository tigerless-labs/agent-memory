Core brand philosophy for team building and hiring initiative.

**Core principle:** Prioritize hiring great human beings first, then great technologists second. This inverts the typical tech hiring priority.

**Key positions:**
- Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just amazing coders
- Value EQ (emotional quotient) more than IQ when hiring software developers
- Focus on building effective teams through better hiring practices
- Emphasize collaboration, communication, and soft skills

**Brand position:** Establish thought leadership in the space of building effective teams and organizations that prioritize human qualities alongside technical skills.
