Asked for recommendation between Lisbon and Porto for a 3-day trip in early April.

Chose Lisbon because:
- Wider range of activities and attractions (Belem Tower, Jeronimos Monastery, National Museum of Ancient Art)
- Vibrant nightlife, especially in Bairro Alto neighborhood
- Good base for day trips: Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica beaches
- Porto is beautiful but smaller and quicker to explore
