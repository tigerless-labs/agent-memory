**Error:** I just saw a tweet expressing that the TV show was interesting.

**Correct:** I just saw a tweet that said the TV show was interesting.

Usage: When referring to what a tweet or quote said, use "that said" (relative clause), not "expressing that". More natural and idiomatic for native speakers.

The phrase "that said" in this context is different from the contrasting "that said" (e.g., "I like parks, but that said, crowds are annoying").
