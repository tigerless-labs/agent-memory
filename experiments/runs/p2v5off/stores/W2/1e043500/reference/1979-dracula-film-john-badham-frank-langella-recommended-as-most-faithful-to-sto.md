Film: Dracula (1979)\nDirector: John Badham\nStar: Frank Langella\n\nDescribed as the most accurate film adaptation of Bram Stoker's original novel, praised for staying true to the characterization in the book while including original plot, symbolism, and themes. Langella's portrayal emphasizes the character's charm and menace.
