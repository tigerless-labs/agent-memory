## Day 1 - Alfama & Downtown Lisbon
- Morning: Alfama neighborhood, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, tram rides
- Afternoon: Baixa district, Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square, Chiado, A Brasileira café

## Day 2 - Sintra Day Trip & Belém  
- Morning: Day trip to Sintra – Pena Palace, Moorish Castle, gardens, hilltop views
- Afternoon: Belém neighborhood – Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém

## Day 3 - River & Bairro Alto
- Morning: Walk along Tagus River, MAAT Museum
- Afternoon: Bairro Alto neighborhood, Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara viewpoint, dinner

## Additional attractions
National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum, LX Factory
