## Traditional Gambian Visual Art Forms

**Batik Printing**: Technique using wax resist dye to create traditional patterns unique to Gambian culture on fabric.

**Woodcarving**: Traditional Gambian technique for creating wooden statues, masks, and utensils representing cultural traditions.

**Pottery**: Rich historical craft using coiling and throwing techniques to create culturally significant everyday utensils.

**Photography**: Documents cultural ceremonies (weddings, naming ceremonies), daily life, and cultural celebrations. Creates visual archives for educating younger generations about cultural heritage.

**Painting**: Depicts traditional clothing, food, dance, historical events, and significant places in The Gambia.

## Recent Developments (as of 2023)

- **Contemporary Art**: Emergence of installations, performance art, conceptual art connecting Gambian artists with global art scene
- **Digital Technology**: Artists creating digital paintings and animations exploring climate change, social justice, human rights
- **Pan-African Collaborations**: Gambian artists collaborating with other African artists on shared themes
- **Youth Programs**: Growing youth involvement with art education programs emphasizing cultural heritage preservation and social change
