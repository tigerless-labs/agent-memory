User prefers detailed Midjourney prompts written in a professional photographer/illustrator/animator voice. Style includes:

- Rich, descriptive language
- Specific camera equipment (Canon EOS R5, Nikon D850, RF lenses, Nikkor lenses, etc.)
- Detailed settings: aperture f-stops, ISO, shutter speed
- Lighting descriptions (natural light, neon, dappled sunlight, etc.)
- Aspect ratios (16:9, 4:5, 3:2)
- Quality parameters (--q 1.5, --q 2, --v 5)
- Composition and mood descriptions
- Background and environment details

Example: Female Shiba Inu dressed as grandma gangster playing poker in casino (16:9, whimsical/playful tone, high-energy scene)
