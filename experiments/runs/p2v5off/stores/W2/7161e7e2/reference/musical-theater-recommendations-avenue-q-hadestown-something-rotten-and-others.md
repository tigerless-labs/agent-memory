Recommended musicals based on interest in irreverent comedies like The Book of Mormon:\n\n- **Avenue Q** — Irreverent musical about puppets in NYC with similar humor to Book of Mormon\n- **Hadestown** — Folk-opera retelling of Orpheus/Eurydice myth; won 8 Tony Awards in 2019 including Best Musical; features Eva Noblezada, Reeve Carney, Patrick Page, Amber Gray\n- **Something Rotten!** — Hilarious musical comedy set in 1590s about brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare; original Broadway cast recording from 2015 available on streaming platforms\n- **The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee** — Quirky musical comedy about spelling bee competition\n- **Hamilton** — Groundbreaking musical blending hip-hop, jazz, blues, R&B; diverse cast\n- **The Producers** — Mel Brooks musical about scheming producers\n- **Urinetown** — Satirical musical commentary on capitalism and government\n- **Be More Chill** — Sci-fi comedy about high school student taking pill for popularity\n- **Dear Evan Hansen** — Musical about social media, mental health, human connection\n- **The Band's Visit** — Poignant musical about Egyptian police band's visit to Israel\n\nCast recordings available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and other streaming platforms.
