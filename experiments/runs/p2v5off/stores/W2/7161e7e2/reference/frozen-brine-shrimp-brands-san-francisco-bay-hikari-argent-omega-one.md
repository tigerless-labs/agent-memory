Recommended brands for frozen brine shrimp supplementary feeding:

**San Francisco Bay Brand**: Popular, well-respected; harvested from Great Salt Lake; offers plain, with garlic, with omega-3

**Hikari**: Established brand; offers plain, with spirulina, with krill

**Argent**: Sustainable sourcing; offers plain, with omega-3, with vitamins  

**Omega One**: High-nutrient specialty brand; Great Salt Lake harvest; rich in omega-3 and essential nutrients

Criteria: sustainable sourced, nutrient/omega-3 rich, no artificial preservatives, airtight packaging

Feeding tip: thaw properly before feeding; feed only what fish consume in a few minutes
