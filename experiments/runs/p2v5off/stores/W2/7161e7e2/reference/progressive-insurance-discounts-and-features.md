Progressive Discounts

Safe Driving: Snapshot program (OBD-II tracking, up to 30% discount), Accident Forgiveness, Good Driver Discount

Multi-Policy: Multi-Car, Bundle with home/life, Homeowner, Continuous Insurance, Good Student, Professional Association

Vehicle Safety: Advanced Safety Features discount (lane departure, blind spot, collision warning), Vehicle Safety Discount (4-5 star NHTSA), Anti-Theft Device

Purchasing: Online, Paperless, Name Your Price Tool, Price Match, Roadside Assistance

Coverage Features:
- Loan/Lease Gap Coverage (covers amount owed if vehicle totaled)
- Custom Equipment Coverage
- Deductible Savings Bank
- Disappearing Deductible (reduces over time if accident-free)
- Deductible range: 0 to 1500 (higher deductible = lower premium)
