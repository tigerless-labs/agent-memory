Contemporary soapstone sculptors: Susan Taylor Glasgow (delicate organic forms), Mark Lundeen (realistic animal/human sculptures), Tim Lewis (abstract and surreal pieces). Historical traditions: Inuit artists (Kenojuak Ashevak, Kiawak Ashoona) with centuries of soapstone heritage. African sculptors: Nicholas Mukomberanwa (Zimbabwean).
