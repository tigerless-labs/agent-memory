Content strategy expansion:

**Instagram Stories**: Behind-the-scenes from #BookClubChat prep and post-discussion reflections; Q&A sessions with followers

**IGTV Videos**: In-depth book reviews and author interviews

**Hashtag Strategy**: Using #bookrecs (short, 12M+ uses) instead of #bookrecommendations; diversifying hashtags per post instead of using same set for all content

**Audience Polling**: Planned Instagram Story poll asking followers to vote on preferred content type: behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, or book reviews

**Quarterly Post**: Title "Quarterly Faves & Wants" — featuring favorite books of the quarter with call for follower recommendations using #bookrecs
