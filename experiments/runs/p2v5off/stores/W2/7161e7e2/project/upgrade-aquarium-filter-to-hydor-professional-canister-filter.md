Decision: Upgrade filtration to Hydor Professional Canister Filter for 20-gallon tank

Chosen over: Eheim Classic, Fluval 206, API SuperClean

Why Hydor:
- Noise: Very quiet (silent pump + noise-reducing system) vs Eheim hum, Fluval louder
- Maintenance: Easy with modular design and built-in primer vs Eheim bulky, Fluval tricky to prime  
- Design: Compact, sleek, modular compartments for pump/mechanical/biological stages
- Media: Customizable, includes coarse foam, fine pad, biological basket
- Price: 150-200 dollars (vs Eheim/Fluval 100-150 dollars)

Specifications: 20-40 gal capacity, recommend 100-200 GPH flow, modular media (ceramic rings, bio-balls, sintered glass, pre-colonized)

Setup: Follow manufacturer instructions; may add biological supplement to establish cycle in new filter
