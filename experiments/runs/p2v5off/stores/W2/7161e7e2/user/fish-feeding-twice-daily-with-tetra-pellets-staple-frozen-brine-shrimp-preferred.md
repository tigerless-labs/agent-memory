Feeding frequency: Twice daily

Staple food: Tetra pellet food  
Supplements: Frozen brine shrimp and frozen bloodworms  
Fish preference: Strong preference for frozen brine shrimp ('go crazy for it'), more finicky about bloodworms

Approach: Intentionally rotates diet for balanced nutrition  
Goal: Prevent deficiencies, promote healthy growth/coloration/vitality  
Result: Fish appreciate Tetra pellets as regular meal
