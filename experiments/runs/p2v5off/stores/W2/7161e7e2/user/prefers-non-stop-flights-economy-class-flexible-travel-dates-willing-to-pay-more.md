When planning transatlantic flights (e.g., New York to Frankfurt), values:
- Non-stop options when possible, but open to layovers if price difference is significant
- Economy class travel
- Flexibility with travel dates to find better deals
- Established carriers like Lufthansa over budget airlines, despite higher cost
- Prioritizes airline stability and financial reliability over rock-bottom prices
- Values in-flight amenities and service quality (food, seat comfort, entertainment)
