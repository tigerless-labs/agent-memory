Transatlantic flight comparison (May 2023):

Lufthansa: 850 USD return, non-stop NY to Frankfurt, complimentary meals, better seat pitch (31-32 inches), full amenities, financially stable, includes checked bag.

Norwegian Air: 600-750 USD return, budget option, limited amenities, smaller seats (29-30 inches pitch), pay-for-drinks service, extra baggage fees, bankruptcy concerns in 2023.

User preference: willing to pay 100+ USD more for Lufthansa to avoid financial risk, poor service, and flight cancellations. Stability and service quality valued more than lowest price.
