## Nutrients and Foods

**Omega-3 fatty acids:** Salmon, tuna, mackerel — improve memory and cognitive function

**Antioxidants:** Berries, spinach, kale — reduce oxidative damage in brain

**B vitamins (B6, B9/folate, B12):** Leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals — improve cognitive function

**Caffeine:** Coffee or tea in moderate amounts — associated with improved cognitive function

**Curcumin:** Found in turmeric — anti-inflammatory, may improve cognitive function

## Easy-to-Make Healthy Snacks

1. Apple slices or celery with almond/peanut butter
2. Roasted chickpeas (paprika, garlic powder, salt)
3. Greek yogurt with berries and honey
4. Hummus with carrots, celery, or pita chips
5. Trail mix (nuts, dried fruit, dark chocolate)
6. Whole-grain crackers with avocado/guacamole
7. Smoothies (frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, spinach/kale)
8. Energy balls (dates, nuts, coconut)

## Tips for Making Healthy Foods Appealing

- Use creative recipes and new flavor combinations
- Incorporate into dishes already enjoyed
- Present on colorful, visually appealing plates
- Consider texture needs (puree, steam, or roast until soft for easier eating)
- Involve her in meal planning and preparation

Note: Always consult healthcare professional before starting new supplements.
