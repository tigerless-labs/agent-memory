Three beginner-friendly meditation practices:

1. **Mindfulness Meditation**: Focus on present moment without judgment. Sit quietly, close eyes, observe breath/thoughts/emotions, refocus when distracted.

2. **Guided Meditation**: Listen to recorded guidance (via app or audio). Helpful for maintaining focus.

3. **Loving-Kindness Meditation**: Cultivate compassion toward self and others. Visualize someone you care for, silently repeat phrases like 'may you be happy, healthy, safe, at peace.'

For beginners: start with 5-10 minutes daily, gradually increase. Finding the right type requires experimentation.
