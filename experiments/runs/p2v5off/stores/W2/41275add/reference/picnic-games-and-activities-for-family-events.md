Curated suggestions for family picnics:

**Games:** Scavenger hunt, outdoor badminton/cornhole, tag, hide-and-seek, capture the flag, treasure hunt, bingo, kite flying

**Quiet activities:** Nature crafts, storytelling, nature walks, photo booth

**Music activities for kids:** Call-and-response with instruments, movement activities (marching/dancing/clapping), freeze dance, musical chairs

**Kid engagement:** Provide simple shaker instruments (maracas, tambourines), let kids try different instruments, incorporate movement, storytelling through music, improvisation

**Baby comfort at events:** Designate quiet shaded area, prepare diaper kit, provide soft toys, take breaks, assign baby buddy, accommodate nap schedule
