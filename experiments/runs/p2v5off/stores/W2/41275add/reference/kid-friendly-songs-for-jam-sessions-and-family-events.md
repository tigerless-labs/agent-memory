Song suggestions for engaging kids during jam sessions or group music activities:

**Children's classics:** Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, The Wheels on the Bus, Old MacDonald Had a Farm

**Fun pop songs:** Happy (Pharrell Williams), Can't Stop the Feeling! (Justin Timberlake), We Will Rock You (Queen)

**Action songs:** If You're Happy and You Know It, The Chicken Dance

These work well for call-and-response, movement activities, and singing along with acoustic instruments.
