Recommended herbal tea options for evening relaxation and sleep:\n\n- **Chamomile**: Classic relaxation tea; soothes nervous system, calms anxiety\n- **Lavender**: Known for calming properties, reduces stress and anxiety\n- **Valerian root**: Natural sedative, improves sleep quality\n- **Peppermint**: Despite being stimulating, can calm mind and body for sleep\n- **Tart cherry**: Contains melatonin, helps regulate sleep-wake cycles\n- **Passionflower**: Natural sedative, reduces anxiety and insomnia\n- **Lemon balm**: Calming herb, reduces stress, improves sleep quality\n- **Rooibos**: Caffeine-free, contains antioxidants, promotes relaxation\n- **Hibiscus**: Calming, helps lower blood pressure\n- **GABA tea**: GABA neurotransmitter helps regulate sleep and relaxation\n\nRecommendation: Brew 30-60 minutes before bed, use high-quality organic brands without added sugars or artificial flavorings. Experiment with blending different herbs.
