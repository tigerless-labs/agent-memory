Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat is made 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac and 20% Folle Blanche. The 2021 vintage saw severe frost which prevented him from using his usual full Folle Blanche blend. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes. Imported and sold by Under The Bonnet. Served by the glass at 161 in Sydenham as a spring wine.
