Music genres and specific recommendations for sleep:\n\n**Genres:**\n- Classical (Mozart, Chopin, Bach)\n- Instrumental piano (slow, gentle)\n- Acoustic guitar (soft, slow)\n- Ambient (soft textures, minimal melodies)\n- New age (flutes, harps, gentle percussion)\n\n**Specific Albums:**\n- Brian Eno - *Ambient 1: Music for Airports* — classic ambient, peaceful atmosphere\n- Max Richter - *Sleep* — 8-hour album designed for sleep\n- Ludovico Einaudi - *Nightbook* — beautiful, calming piano\n- Enya - *Watermark* — soothing vocals with gentle instrumentation\n- Steve Reich - *Music for 18 Musicians* — ambient with gentle percussion\n\nFind on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, or iTunes. Experiment to find what resonates with you.
