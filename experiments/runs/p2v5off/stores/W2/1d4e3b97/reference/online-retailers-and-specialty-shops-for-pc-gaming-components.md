## Major Retailers
- Amazon.com
- Newegg.com  
- Best Buy.com (also pre-built PCs)
- Micro Center (physical stores + online)
- B&H Photo Video

## Specialty/Niche Retailers & Communities
- **PCPartPicker.com** — Component selector and price comparison tool across retailers
- **Superbiiz.com** — Specialty retailer for computer hardware, including hard-to-find and discontinued items
- **Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales** — Community dedicated to sharing PC component deals
- **Performance-PCs.com** — Specializes in high-performance hardware, water cooling, and modding parts
- **FrozenCPU.com** — Computer hardware and custom PC modding components

**Tips:** Compare prices across multiple retailers; check return policies and warranties before purchasing.
