## Requirements
Trainees can answer a survey at beginning and end of course. At course end, trainees can view:
- Their own progress (survey results change over time)
- Whole class results (aggregated survey responses)

## User Stories Generated

**Trainee stories:**
1. Access survey at beginning of course for initial feedback
2. Access same survey at end of course for final feedback
3. View progress over the course (track own improvement)
4. Compare end-of-course results with initial results (see attitude changes)
5. View class results at end of course (compare with peers, see group performance)

**Instructor stories:**
6. Collect survey responses at beginning and end of course (track progress, evaluate training)
7. View individual trainee survey results (provide personalized feedback)
8. View class survey results (evaluate overall training effectiveness, identify improvements)
