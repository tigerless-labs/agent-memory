## Bathroom Renovation Project

**Design Chosen**: Spa-Inspired Retreat
- Freestanding tub (keeping existing tub)
- Large walk-in shower
- Goal: create relaxing oasis

**Bathroom Dimensions**: 5x8 feet

**Materials Decided**:
- **Tile**: Glazed porcelain (walls and floor)
  - Chosen for: glossy finish, protection against scratches and stains, easy cleaning
- **Grout**: Cement-based
  - Selected for: all-purpose suitability, affordability
  - Plan: apply grout sealer for water/stain protection

**DIY Approach**: Willing to tackle portions of renovation work personally, given recent home improvement experience. Will hire professionals for specialized work (plumbing, electrical).

**Phase**: Planning and material selection stage
