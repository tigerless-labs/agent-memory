# Work Overload Incident

**What happened (week of 2024-02-20):**
- Multiple nights staying up until 1am to finish work projects
- Work commitments interrupted evening screen-time reduction goals
- Recognized this as exception, not normal pattern
- Now focused on recovering regular sleep schedule this week

**Lessons & plans:**
- Need better task prioritization at work to avoid late nights
- Interested in strategies to prioritize tasks effectively (Eisenhower Matrix, realistic deadlines, avoiding multitasking, breaking large tasks into chunks, learning to say no)
- Plans to communicate with team about workload and set boundaries around work hours
- Taking regular breaks during workday to recharge and avoid burnout
