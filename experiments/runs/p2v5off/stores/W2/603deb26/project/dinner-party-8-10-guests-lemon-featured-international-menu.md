**Guests:** 8-10; accommodate vegetarians + 1 gluten-free

**Menu:**
- Appetizer: Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas/hummus
- Main: Vegetarian sushi rolls (grilled portobello mushroom, carrot-ginger tempura, avocado, cucumber), lemon-ginger dipping sauce, lemon-herb quinoa with pine nuts
- Dessert: Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available)

**Garnish:** Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint), lemon slices, edible flowers (violas/pansies)

**Timing:** Prep ahead, cook in batches; main 30-45min after appetizer, dessert 30-60min after main
