Rod recommendations for light trout fishing (2-5 weight lines):

Orvis Clearwater: 198-298 USD (entry-level, lightweight, sensitive)
Sage Igniter: 400-500 USD (high-performance, fast action)
Winston Pure: 300-400 USD (premium, smooth casting)
Redington Crux: 150-250 USD (budget-friendly)
TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series: 200-300 USD (high-quality for smaller fish)

Preferences: 9-foot length standard, fast or medium-fast action, graphite for sensitivity.
