**Lavender Syrup Recipe**
- 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, 1/4 cup dried lavender buds
- Heat sugar and water until dissolved; add lavender; steep 10-15 min; strain and refrigerate up to 2 weeks

**Lavender Cocktails**
- Lavender Gin Fizz: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, top with soda
- Lavender Dream: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup
- Lavender Bee's Knees: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz honey syrup, serve on rocks

**White Negroni**: 1.5 oz Hendrick's, 0.5 oz Suze, 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth; stir 20-30 sec, strain, garnish with citrus twist

**Bitter Liqueur Alternatives for Negroni**: Cynar (artichoke, vegetal), Aperol (lighter, citrusy), Luxardo Bitter (herbal), Suze (gentian, crisp), Meletti (sweet, citrus)

**Citrus for Gin + Lavender**: Orange/blood orange (classic), yuzu (Japanese), kumquat (small, sweet), satsuma (mandarin), bergamot (subtle, tea-like)
