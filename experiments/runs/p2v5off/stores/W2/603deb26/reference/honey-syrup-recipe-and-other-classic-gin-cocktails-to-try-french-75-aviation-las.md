## Honey Syrup (1:1 ratio)\n- Combine 1 part honey to 1 part water\n- Heat gently until honey dissolves (do not boil)\n- Cool before use\n- Store in fridge up to 2 weeks\n- Can infuse with citrus peel, herbs, or spices\n\n## Classic Cocktails to Try\n- **French 75**: Gin, lemon juice, sugar, champagne - elegant and citrusy\n- **Aviation**: Gin, maraschino liqueur, crème de violette, lemon juice\n- **Last Word**: Gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur, lime juice\n- **Corpse Reviver No. 2**: Gin, Lillet Blanc, Cynar, Cointreau, absinthe - from Savoy Cocktail Book\n- **Gin Rickey**: Gin, lime juice, soda water - simple and refreshing\n- **Gin-Gin Mule**: Gin, lime juice, ginger beer (like Moscow Mule)\n- **Old Fashioned**: Bourbon/rye, sugar, bitters, citrus twist\n- **Bee's Knees**: Gin, honey syrup, lemon juice
