## Lavender Gin Fizz\n- 1.5 oz gin\n- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice\n- 0.5 oz lavender syrup\n- 0.5 oz simple syrup\n- Top with soda water\n- Garnish: lavender sprig, lime wheel\n\n## Grapefruit Basil Martini\n- 2 oz gin\n- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice\n- 0.5 oz lime juice\n- 0.5 oz basil syrup\n- Garnish: grapefruit twist, basil leaf\n\n## Lavender Grapefruit Spritz\n- 2 oz prosecco\n- 1 oz grapefruit juice\n- 0.5 oz lavender syrup\n- Splash of soda water\n- Garnish: grapefruit slice, lavender sprig\n\n## Lavender Syrup\nSteep 0.25 cup dried lavender buds in 1 cup sugar and 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes. Strain and refrigerate up to 2 weeks.
