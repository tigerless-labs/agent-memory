2. **Unknown Mortal Orchestra** — Album: Multi-Love (2015); Song: "Can't Keep Checking My Phone"
3. **Temples** — Album: Sun Structures (2014); Song: "Shelter Song"
4. **Mild High Club** — Album: Timeline (2015); Song: "Windowpane"
5. **King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard** — Album: I'm in Your Mind Fuzz (2014); Song: "Am I in Heaven?"
6. **Foxygen** — Album: ...And Star Power (2014); Song: "How Can You Really"
7. **The Black Angels** — Album: Phosphene Dream (2010); Song: "Black Grease"
8. **The Horrors** — Album: Skying (2011); Song: "Still Life"
9. **Moon Duo** — Album: Circles (2012); Song: "Sleepwalker"
10. **A Place to Bury Strangers** — Album: Worship (2012); Song: "You Are the One"

All feature psychedelic rock with emphasis on atmosphere, synths, and layered production.
