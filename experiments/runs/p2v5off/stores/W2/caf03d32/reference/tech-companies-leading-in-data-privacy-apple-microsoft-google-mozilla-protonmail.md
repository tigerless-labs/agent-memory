### Companies Known for Data Privacy Leadership

- **Apple**: strict privacy policies, default end-to-end encryption for iMessage/FaceTime, advanced privacy in Safari
- **Microsoft**: privacy controls, two-factor authentication support, Windows Hello biometric authentication
- **Google**: limiting third-party app data access, privacy controls across products, Privacy Checkup feature
- **Mozilla**: privacy-first approach via Firefox, built-in privacy protections, blocking tracking cookies by default
- **ProtonMail**: privacy-focused email service, end-to-end encryption, zero-access encryption

Discussed in context of 2023 tech privacy landscape.
