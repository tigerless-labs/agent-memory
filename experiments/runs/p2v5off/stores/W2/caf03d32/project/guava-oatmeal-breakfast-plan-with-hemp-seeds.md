Decided to implement guava oatmeal as morning breakfast for protein and fiber boost.

**Recipe (per serving):**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1-2 guavas depending on size)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds (provides ~5-10g protein, omega-3s)
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- Optional: honey or maple syrup, nuts, cinnamon, protein powder

**Preparation tips:**
- Grind hemp seeds for better texture
- Mix hemp seeds with liquid first to distribute evenly
- Use fresh guava for best flavor
- Can add walnuts, almonds, or pecans for extra crunch
- Toast nuts/seeds at 350°F for 5-10 minutes to enhance flavor

**Nutritional target:** ~15-20g protein per serving when including hemp seeds and chia seeds
