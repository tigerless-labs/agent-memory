Beginner-level guitarist looking to learn.

Recommended YouTube channels for guitar tutorials:
- Justin Guitar (Justin Sandercoe) — comprehensive, beginner-friendly
- Marty Music (Marty Schwartz) — popular songs focus
- GuitarLessons365 (Bill) — acoustic guitar structured course
- GuitarJamz (Martyn) — popular songs and riffs
- Andy Guitar (Andy Crowley) — electric guitar focus

Tips suggested:
- Start with acoustic guitar
- Practice regularly (10-15 min daily minimum)
- Use a metronome for rhythm development
- Find a practice buddy for accountability
