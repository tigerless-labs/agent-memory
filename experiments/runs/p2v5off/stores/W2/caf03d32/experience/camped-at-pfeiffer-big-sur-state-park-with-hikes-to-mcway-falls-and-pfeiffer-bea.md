Stayed at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park for two nights. Hiked to McWay Falls on the first day and Pfeiffer Beach on the second day. Used a portable power bank to keep devices charged, which was helpful for navigation and capturing memories. First time cooking camping food went okay but looking to improve. Interested in improving astrophotography skills—attempted night photography during this trip but results were mediocre; still learning the techniques.
