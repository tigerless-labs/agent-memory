Chose WriteTrack as a writing progress dashboard tool to track:
- Daily word count across all projects
- Number of finished poems
- Overall writing progress metrics

Reasoning: WriteTrack's simplicity and ease of use appealed to the user as a way to consolidate progress tracking separate from the individual project management in Scrivener and Google Docs.
