Uses morning pages as a primary journaling technique:
- **Method**: Free-form writing, letting thoughts flow naturally
- **Frequency**: Daily, as part of journal practice
- **Topics**: Sometimes specific topics, often stream-of-consciousness
- **Purpose**: Clearing mind, processing emotions, reflecting on experiences and writing goals

**Creative impact**: Morning pages directly influence creative writing. Ideas developed in morning pages are revisited and expanded into poems or short story scenes. Has been helpful for exploring character thoughts, feelings, and motivations in a freeform way.
