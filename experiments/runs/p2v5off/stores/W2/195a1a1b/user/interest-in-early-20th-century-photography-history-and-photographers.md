Learning about early photography movements and key photographers:

**Straight Photography Movement**
- Early 20th century style emphasizing objectivity and truthfulness
- Pioneered by Alfred Stieglitz and photographers like Edward Weston, Paul Strand, Ansel Adams
- Influenced documentary and landscape photography

**Key Photographers:**
- **Paul Strand** (1890–1976): Modernist pioneer; documentary work; famous Mexico series (started 1932)
- **Francis Benjamin Johnston** (1864–1952): Portraits and architectural photography; documented historic buildings; one of first women professional photographers in US
- **Charles Dudley Arnold** (1865–1946): Aerial photography pioneer (balloons and airplanes); nighttime photography innovator with artificial lighting techniques

**Photography Genres Explored:**
- Architectural photography
- Aerial photography
- Straight/documentary photography
- Nighttime photography
- Portrait photography
