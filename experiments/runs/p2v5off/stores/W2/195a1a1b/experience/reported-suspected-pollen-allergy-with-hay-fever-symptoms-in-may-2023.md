In May 2023, reported symptoms consistent with pollen allergy (hay fever):
- Sneezing
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Itchy nose, throat, or ears
- Watery, red, or swollen eyes
- Cough
- Postnasal drip
- Fatigue and weakness
- Headache

Planned to consult with doctor for proper diagnosis and treatment. Also expressed interest in natural remedies and lifestyle modifications for managing allergy symptoms, including air filtration, saline rinses, anti-inflammatory diet, hydration, and avoiding high pollen count days.
