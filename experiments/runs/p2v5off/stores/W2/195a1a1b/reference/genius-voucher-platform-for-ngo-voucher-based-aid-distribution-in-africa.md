Genius Voucher is a platform designed to help NGOs manage voucher-based humanitarian projects with real-time tracking and accountability.

**Core features:**
- Hardware: POS machines for authorized vendors, smart cards for beneficiaries
- Software: Portal for project teams to create and monitor distribution projects in real-time
- Transactions recorded with receipts confirming items/cash received
- All transactions monitored in real-time by project teams

**Why suited for Africa:**
- Ensures accountability and transparency (prevents corruption/mismanagement common with manual systems)
- Works offline in remote areas with poor internet connectivity (NFC cards hold beneficiary data; devices sync locally and sync to database after each round)
- User-friendly redemption: beneficiaries tap smart card on POS terminal, select items, enter PIN to finalize

**Problem solved:** NGOs traditionally face challenges with accountability systems, manual data entry, and operating in areas with limited infrastructure. Genius Voucher eliminates manual data entry and provides real-time monitoring/reporting.
