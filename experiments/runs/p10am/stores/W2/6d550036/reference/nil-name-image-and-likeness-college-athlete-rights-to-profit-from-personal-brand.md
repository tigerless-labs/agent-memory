NIL stands for Name, Image, and Likeness. It refers to the rights of college athletes to profit from their personal brand, such as their name, image, and social media following.

History: Until 2021, NCAA rules prohibited college athletes from profiting off their NIL. In 2021, the NCAA adopted new rules allowing college athletes to profit from their NIL without losing eligibility.

Benefits: Provides opportunities to earn money while in college; gives control over personal brand; enables monetization of social media and marketable skills.

Considerations: Athletes must manage finances, protect intellectual property, avoid conflicts of interest, and follow NCAA rules.
