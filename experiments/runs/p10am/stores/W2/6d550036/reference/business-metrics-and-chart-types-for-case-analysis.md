Financial: Revenue growth, profit margin, ROI, break-even, cash flow, cost savings. Operational: Capacity utilization, cycle time, throughput, defect rate, inventory turnover. Customer: CAC, retention rate, lifetime value, satisfaction, NPS, conversion rate. Market: Market share, growth rate, competitive landscape, pricing analysis. Innovation: R&D investment, patents, time-to-market, product adoption. Chart types by use: performance dashboard for overview, scatter/bubble for competitive landscape, treemap for problem analysis, Sankey for impact, waterfall for cost-benefit, Gantt for roadmap. Tips: focus on few key metrics that tell story, use mix of leading/lagging indicators, provide context and benchmarks.
