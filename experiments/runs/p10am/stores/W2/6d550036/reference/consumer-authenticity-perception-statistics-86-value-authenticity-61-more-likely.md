Key research findings on consumer perception of authenticity in influencer marketing:

- Stackla: 86% of consumers say authenticity is important when deciding what brands to support
- Influencer Marketing Hub: 61% of consumers more likely to trust influencer recommendations if influencer perceived as authentic
- Edelman: 70% of consumers believe authentic brands are more reliable
- Edelman: 63% of consumers believe authentic brands are more trustworthy

These statistics demonstrate the critical importance of authenticity in influencer marketing effectiveness.
