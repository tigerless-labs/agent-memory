Collectives (group licensing programs) are groups of athletes who come together to collectively license their NIL rights to third parties such as brands or marketing agencies.

Benefits to athletes:
- Pool NIL rights for better deal negotiation and leverage
- Receive legal and financial education and support
- Get assistance with deal negotiation
- Avoid NCAA rule violations
- Receive fair compensation

Benefits to athletic programs:
- Help athletes navigate NIL complexities
- Centralized platform for brand connections
- More opportunities to monetize NIL
- Attract and retain top talent
