Collectives connect college athletes with businesses through these methods:

1. Sponsorship Deals: Negotiate athlete-brand partnerships involving product/service promotion on social media, at events, or other marketing channels.

2. Social Media Campaigns: Connect brands with athletes who have strong social media following; coordinate campaigns where athletes promote products/services to their followers.

3. Endorsements: Secure endorsement deals involving appearances in advertisements, product launches, or service promotion.

4. Events and Appearances: Book athletes for store openings, product launches, and promotional events.

5. Product Collaborations: Facilitate athlete involvement in product design, promotion, or new product development.

These arrangements help businesses reach athlete audiences while helping athletes monetize their NIL rights.
