Know your audience and tailor to their needs. Keep simple: avoid clutter, focus on key info. Use clear structure with sections/tabs, headings, whitespace. Choose appropriate charts: bars for categories, lines for time series, scatter for correlations. Use color strategically to draw attention and highlight trends. Tableau tips: use dashboards not worksheets, leverage interactivity with filters/parameters, encode data with size/color, avoid 3D charts, use Data Stories feature for narrative.
