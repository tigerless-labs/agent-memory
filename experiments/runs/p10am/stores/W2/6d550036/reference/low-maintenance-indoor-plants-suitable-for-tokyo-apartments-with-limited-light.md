Recommended plants for Tokyo apartments with limited natural light:

1. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)** - Survives with minimal light and water, nearly impossible to kill
2. **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)** - Tolerates low light and infrequent watering
3. **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)** - Versatile climber, good for air purification
4. **Dracaena** (40+ varieties) - Small, tolerates low light
5. **Succulents** (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) - Perfect for Tokyo's hot summers and dry winters
6. **Peace Lily** - Elegant white blooms, good air purification
7. **Air Plant (Tillandsia)** - Minimal watering needs, absorbs moisture through leaves

**Tokyo-specific care tips:**
- Water plants sparingly (Tokyo tap water is quite hard)
- Avoid direct sunlight, especially during summer months
- Keep plants away from drafts and air conditioning vents
- Fertilize sparingly (Tokyo soil tends to be nutrient-rich)
- Provide good air circulation
