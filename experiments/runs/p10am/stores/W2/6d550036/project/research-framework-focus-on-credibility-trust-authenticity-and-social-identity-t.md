Key frameworks from research conversation:

**Credibility factors:** expertise, relevance, authenticity, consistency, social proof
**Trust factors:** reliability, integrity, benevolence, communication
**Authenticity strategies:** genuine voice, personal storytelling, transparency with sponsored content, engagement, UGC, avoiding over-production, consistency across platforms
**Social identity theory application:** group membership, social categorization, in-group favoritism, social influence, self-categorization

Research focus: How influencers leverage these factors to increase brand awareness and drive consumer purchases, particularly among different demographic groups.
