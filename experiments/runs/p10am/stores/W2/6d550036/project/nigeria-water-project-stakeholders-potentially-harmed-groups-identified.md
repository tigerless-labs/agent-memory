Stakeholders potentially harmed:
- Landowners and residents (displacement risk)
- Traditional water sources (contamination or obsolescence)
- Environmental organizations (environmental impact concerns)
- Communities relying on traditional sources for religious/cultural practices
- Small-scale water vendors (business loss from free/subsidized running water)
