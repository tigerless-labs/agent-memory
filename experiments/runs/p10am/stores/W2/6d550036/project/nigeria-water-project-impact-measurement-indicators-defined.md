Measurement indicators to assess project impact:

Access and Health Indicators:
- Increase in access to safe and clean water sources
- Reduction in water-borne illnesses
- Reduction in health care costs related to water-borne diseases

Social Impact Indicators:
- Reduction in time spent collecting water
- Increase in time available for education and employment
- Improved health due to reduced physical strain

Economic Indicators:
- Changes in agricultural crop yields and revenue
- Tax revenue increases from improved economic activity
- Revenue changes for local businesses and industries
- Livelihood impacts on water vendors

Environmental Indicators:
- Water quality changes in traditional water sources
- Wildlife and habitat impacts
- Contamination levels of traditional sources

Displacement and Equity Indicators:
- Number of people displaced by infrastructure
- Adequacy and sufficiency of compensation for displaced persons
- Property value changes in affected areas
