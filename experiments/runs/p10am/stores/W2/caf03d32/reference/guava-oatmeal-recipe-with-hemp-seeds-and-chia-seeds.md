Ingredients: 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/4 cup diced fresh guava, 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk, 1 tablespoon chia seeds, 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds, optional honey or maple syrup. Optional toppings: sliced banana, nuts, shredded coconut, cinnamon, protein powder. Hemp seeds: start with 1-2 tablespoons per serving for 5-10g protein and 1-2g omega-3s. Can grind seeds or mix with liquid first for better incorporation. Store in refrigerator or freezer to prevent rancidity. Total nutritional value approximately 15g protein, 12g fiber, rich in omega-3 fatty acids.
