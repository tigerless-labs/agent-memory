Recommended YouTube channels for beginner guitar learning:

1. **Justin Guitar** (Justin Sandercoe) - Gentle, encouraging approach with wide range of topics from basics to advanced
2. **Marty Music** (Marty Schwartz) - Great for learning popular songs quickly, breaks down complex songs into simple lessons
3. **GuitarLessons365** (Bill) - Comprehensive beginner course focused on acoustic guitar, well-structured and patient
4. **GuitarJamz** (Martyn) - Fun approach to learning popular songs and riffs, good at breaking down complex parts
5. **Andy Guitar** (Andy Crowley) - Perfect for electric guitar, covers basics to advanced with engaging teaching style

**Key tips for practice:**
- Start with acoustic guitar (easier than electric)
- Practice regularly, even 10-15 minutes daily
- Use a metronome for rhythm development
- Find a practice buddy for motivation
- Be patient—learning takes time
