Recommended music documentaries on Netflix:

1. **Homecoming: A Film by Beyoncé** (2019) - Concert film documenting Beyoncé's 2018 Coachella performance
2. **Quincy** (2018) - Documentary about music legend Quincy Jones with interviews from Frank Sinatra, Michael Jackson, and more
3. **Travis Scott: Look Mom I Can Fly** (2019) - Follows Travis Scott's rise to fame and Astroworld tour
4. **Gaga: Five Foot Two** (2017) - Explores Lady Gaga's life, music, struggles with chronic pain, and creation of album "Joanne"
5. **The Black Godfather** (2019) - About music executive Clarence Avant with interviews from Quincy Jones and Snoop Dogg
6. **The Apollo** (2019) - Documentary about the legendary Apollo Theater in Harlem with performances and interviews
7. **The Remix: Hip Hop x Fashion** (2019) - Explores intersection of hip-hop and fashion with Kanye West and Pharrell Williams
8. **I'll Sleep When I'm Dead** (2016) - Documentary about DJ Steve Aoki's life and tours
9. **What Happened, Miss Simone?** (2015) - Documentary about Nina Simone's life and career
10. **The Art of Rap** (2012) - Explores history and evolution of hip-hop with interviews from Ice-T, Dr. Dre, Eminem
