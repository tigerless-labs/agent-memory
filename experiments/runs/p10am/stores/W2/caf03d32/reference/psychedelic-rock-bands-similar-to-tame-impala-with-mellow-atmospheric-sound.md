Recommended psychedelic rock bands with mellow and atmospheric qualities similar to Tame Impala:

- **Pond**: Australian psychedelic rock, more laid-back and dreamy
  - Album: *Man It Feels Like Space Again* (2015)
  - Song: "30000 MEGATONS"

- **Unknown Mortal Orchestra**: New Zealand-based psychedelic rock with funk and soul
  - Album: *Multi-Love* (2015)
  - Song: "Can't Keep Checking My Phone"

- **Temples**: British psychedelic rock with neo-psychedelia
  - Album: *Sun Structures* (2014)
  - Song: "Shelter Song"

- **Mild High Club**: Psychedelic rock with jazz, folk, and electronic elements
  - Album: *Timeline* (2015)
  - Song: "Windowpane"

- **King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard**: Mellow tracks like "Am I in Heaven?"
  - Album: *I'm in Your Mind Fuzz* (2014)

- **Foxygen**: American indie rock duo with psychedelia and folk
  - Album: *...And Star Power* (2014)
  - Song: "How Can You Really"

- **The Black Angels**: Dark, atmospheric psychedelic rock
  - Album: *Phosphene Dream* (2010)
  - Song: "Black Grease"

- **The Horrors**: Post-punk/psychedelic with moody atmosphere
  - Album: *Skying* (2011)
  - Song: "Still Life"

- **Moon Duo**: Hypnotic psychedelic rock with krautrock influences
  - Album: *Circles* (2012)
  - Song: "Sleepwalker"

- **A Place to Bury Strangers**: Noise rock/psychedelic with dark atmosphere
  - Album: *Worship* (2012)
  - Song: "You Are the One"
