Discovering guava and durian as novel high-protein fruits to incorporate into daily diet. Guava about 4.2g protein per cup, pairs well with smoothies, salads, and oatmeal. Durian about 3.6g protein per cup, tropical option with strong flavor. Both are sources of fiber, vitamins, and antioxidants.
