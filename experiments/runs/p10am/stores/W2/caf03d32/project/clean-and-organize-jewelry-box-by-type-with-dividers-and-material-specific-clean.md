User plans to clean and organize jewelry box. Will sort by category (earrings, necklaces, rings), use dividers and compartments, and keep frequently worn items accessible. Cleaning approach: lint-free cloth for gentle wipe, mild soap and water soak for 15-30 minutes with soft-bristled toothbrush for intricate pieces, silver polish for tarnished silver, specialized solutions for pearls/opals/emeralds, ultrasonic cleaners for delicate pieces.
