Options for trying out a Mellotron:

1. **Rental** — Contact local musical instrument rental companies to check for Mellotron availability
2. **Studio sessions** — Contact recording studios with vintage gear; book studio time to play
3. **Music stores** — Contact local instrument stores to see if they have Mellotrons available for testing  
4. **Virtual/software** — Online virtual Mellotron websites and digital plugins
