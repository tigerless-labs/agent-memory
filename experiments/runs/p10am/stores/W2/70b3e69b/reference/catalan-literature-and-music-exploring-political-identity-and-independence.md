Works recommended for nuanced perspective on Catalan political situation and independence debate:

**Novels:**
- On The Line by Joseph Piqué — Catalan family struggles with national identity and loyalty to Spanish state
- The Impostor by Javier Cercas — Explores identity, truth, memory through case of Enric Marco
- Patria by Fernando Aramburu — Explores Spanish/Basque nationalism with pro- and anti-separatist viewpoints

**Music:**
- Marina Rossell — Explores Catalan identity and politics with emphasis on humanity and poetry

**Visual Art:**
- Francesc Torres — Multimedia artist exploring history, memory, identity from Franco-era Catalonia experience

**Context:** Independence themes dominate mainstream Catalan literature/music; diverse perspectives exist in independent outlets.
