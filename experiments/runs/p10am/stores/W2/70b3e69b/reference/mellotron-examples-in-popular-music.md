Songs featuring Mellotron sounds:

- **Strawberry Fields Forever** (The Beatles) — Mellotron flute loop in intro
- **Kashmir** (Led Zeppelin) — Mellotron strings in chorus  
- **Nothing Else Matters** (Metallica) — Mellotron strings for haunting effect
- **Champagne Supernova** (Oasis) — Mellotron flute in instrumental break
- **Fluorescent Adolescent** (Arctic Monkeys) — Mellotron choir in intro and choruses
