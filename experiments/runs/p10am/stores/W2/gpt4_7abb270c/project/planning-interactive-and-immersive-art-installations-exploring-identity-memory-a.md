Developing interactive and immersive art installations to engage audiences and create empathy and connection. Specific ideas include: (1) Multi-sensory environment installations using sound, light, and texture to recreate specific environments or memories (e.g., forest with bird sounds, rustling leaves, damp earth scent); (2) Collective memory installations incorporating found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods to explore shared human experience and how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts.
