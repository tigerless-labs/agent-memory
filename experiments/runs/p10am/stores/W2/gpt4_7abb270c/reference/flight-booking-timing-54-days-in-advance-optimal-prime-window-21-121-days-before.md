Optimal time to book flights: approximately 54 days before departure.\n\nPrime booking window: 21-121 days ahead (prices typically within 5 percent of lowest point).\n\nDays to avoid: Tuesday, Wednesday, Saturday are peak booking days; instead book on Monday, Thursday, or Friday.\n\nOff-peak travel seasons: mid-January to mid-March, April-May, September-November.\n\nStrategies:\n- Early morning or late evening flights often cheaper\n- Use price alerts: Airfarewatchdog, The Flight Deal, Secret Flying\n- Clear browser cookies to avoid airline price tracking\n- Look for error fares (act quickly, usually short window)\n- Check multiple airports\n- Consider budget airlines\n- Use Google Flights calendar view to find cheapest days\n- Use Skyscanner Everywhere search for flexible dates
