Artists: Carsten Höller (German artist, interactive installations with slides, Ferris wheels, playful elements); Anicka Yi (Korean-American, scent-based immersive installations exploring art, science, technology); Rafael Lozano-Hemmer (Mexican-Canadian, interactive installations using biometrics and robotics to explore surveillance and control). Exhibitions/Collectives: Meow Wolf (Santa Fe, immersive interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, storytelling); The Color Factory (San Francisco, interactive installations exploring emotional and psychological effects of color); TeamLab (Japan, immersive exhibitions using technology and light to create interactive environments).
