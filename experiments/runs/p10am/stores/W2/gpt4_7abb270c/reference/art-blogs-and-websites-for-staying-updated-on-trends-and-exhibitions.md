Artsy: Comprehensive online platform with news, exhibitions, artist profiles, market trends. Hyperallergic: Art blog covering news, reviews, opinion on contemporary art. Artnet: Art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists. The Art Newspaper: Daily publication on international art news and exhibitions. Contemporary Art Daily: Discovering new artists and contemporary exhibitions. Art in America: Contemporary art magazine with features and reviews. Frieze: International contemporary art and culture coverage. Apollo Magazine: British art magazine covering art, architecture, collecting. Art21: Documentaries and online content on contemporary art. Culture Trip: Art, culture, travel focusing on emerging artists and underground scenes.
