Books: The Feminist Art Movement by Broude and Garrard (comprehensive history 1960s-1990s); Feminist Art and the Maternal by Andrea Liss; Women Art and Power by Linda Nochlin (essays on women's representation); The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s by Broude and Garrard; Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly by Guerrilla Girls. Documentaries: !Women Art Revolution (2010) by Lynn Hershman Leeson, featuring 40+ feminist artists; The Heretics (2009) on the Heresies Collective; Womart: The Women's Art Movement (1991) by Kate Horsfield; Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations (2010); Art:21 - Judy Chicago (2014) PBS series episode.
