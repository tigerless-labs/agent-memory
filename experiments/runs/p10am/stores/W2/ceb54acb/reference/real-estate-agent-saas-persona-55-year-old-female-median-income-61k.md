Performance marketing persona for typical US real estate agent. Female, age 55, suburban or rural location. Median income 61,140 dollars (2019 data). Bachelor degree in real estate. Detail-oriented, organized, strong customer service skills. Works independently with brokerage support. Builds and maintains relationships with clients and colleagues. Active in local real estate community. Knows market trends and regulations.

Main challenges are limited marketing resources, difficulty measuring marketing performance, heavy competition from other agents and brokerages, and keeping up with technology changes.

Wants to increase visibility and reputation, generate more leads and close more deals, stay competitive, and improve marketing efficiency.

Uses CRM software, email marketing tools, and social media. Open to adopting new technology. Focuses on online listings and advertising, networking and referrals, direct mail, and social media ads. Balances cost with quality results. Continuously monitors ROI. Uses brokerage marketing resources.

Based on NAR statistics from 2023.
