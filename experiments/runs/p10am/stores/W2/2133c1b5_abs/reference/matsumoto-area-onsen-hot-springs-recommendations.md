**Recommended onsen facilities in Matsumoto area:**

1. **Asama Onsen** — Popular resort area with public and private baths; 30 minutes from Matsumoto City by bus or taxi
2. **Utsukushigahara Onsen** — Scenic location with stunning views of Japanese Alps; 45 minutes from Matsumoto City by bus or taxi  
3. **Hirata Onsen** — Historic facility with traditional atmosphere; indoor and outdoor baths; 20 minutes from Matsumoto City by bus or taxi
4. **Matsumoto Onsen Hotel** — Located in heart of Matsumoto City; public onsen facility with scenic mountain views

**Important tips:**
- Many onsen have specific customs: wash body before entering, some don't accept tattooed individuals, some may not accept foreigners—check in advance
- Private bath rentals available at some facilities for those uncomfortable with public bathing
- Confirm hours, prices, and specific rules before visiting
- Public baths (sentō) offer more local and affordable experience for mingling with locals
