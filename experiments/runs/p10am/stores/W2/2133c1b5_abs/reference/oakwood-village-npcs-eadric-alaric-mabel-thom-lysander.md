Location: Oakwood - small farming/artisan village at edge of Whispering Woods

Key NPCs:
- **Eadric**: Village elder, old friend of Abraham, white beard, wise
- **Alaric**: Innkeeper at Silver Stag Inn, stout with bushy beard  
- **Mabel**: Older villager, encountered aggressive wolves with unnatural glowing eyes
- **Thom**: Burly woodsman, witnessed trees uprooting and moving to block forest paths
- **Lysander**: Traveling merchant, spotted mysterious figure from afar; sensed powerful magical energy
