Research explored potential of AI-powered adaptive learning systems to promote equity and inclusion, finding they can help reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students.

However, identified significant concern: potential for bias in the algorithms used to drive these systems could exacerbate existing inequities if not addressed. This is a critical area requiring ongoing research and development.
