**Must-visit historical and cultural attractions in Matsumoto:**

1. **Matsumoto Castle** — National Treasure of Japan, 16th-century castle with unique blend of Japanese and Western architecture; well-preserved; features museum and exhibits on samurai era and regional history
2. **Nawate Street** — Charming traditional street with shops, tea houses, restaurants; good for souvenirs and local snacks
3. **Matsumoto Folkcraft Museum** — Showcases traditional Japanese crafts (woodcarvings, pottery, weaving); housed in beautiful historic building
4. **Kaichi School Museum** — Historic school building from Meiji period; provides insight into Japan's educational history
5. **Zenko-ji Temple** — Founded in 7th century; famous for beautiful garden and traditional architecture; popular for cherry blossom viewing; best visited late afternoon/early evening
6. **Matsumoto Shrine** — Shinto shrine near castle; dedicated to god of war and agriculture; known for beautiful torii gate and peaceful surroundings
7. **Nakamachi Street** — Historic street with traditional warehouses; glimpse into mercantile past
8. **Matsumoto City Museum** — City history, art, and culture exhibits
9. **Agata no Mori Park** — Beautiful park with pond, walking trails, plants and flowers; peaceful atmosphere

**Local specialties to try:**
- Shinshu salmon
- Hakuba apples and apple pie
- Matsumoto coffee
