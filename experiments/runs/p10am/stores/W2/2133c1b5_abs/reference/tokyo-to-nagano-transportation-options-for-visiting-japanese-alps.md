**Public transportation options from Tokyo to Nagano prefecture:**

**Train:**
- **Hokuriku Shinkansen** — Bullet train from Tokyo Station to Nagano Station; ~1 hour 45 minutes
- **Nagano Shinkansen** — From Tokyo Station to Nagano Station; ~2 hours
- From Nagano Station: local trains or buses to final destination

**Bus:**
- **Highway Bus** — From Tokyo's Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal or Tokyo Station to Nagano City; 3-4 hours depending on traffic
- **Nagano Snow Shuttle** — Connects Tokyo's Shinjuku area to Hakuba Valley; 4-5 hours

**Flight + Train:**
- Fly from Haneda or Narita to Matsumoto Airport, then local train or bus to Nagano City or destinations in prefecture

**Tips:**
- Book train tickets in advance for best prices and availability, especially during peak seasons
- Online bus bookings usually available with advance discounts
- Japan Rail Pass can be cost-effective for multiple train trips
- Narita Express (N'EX) available to Tokyo Station for connections
