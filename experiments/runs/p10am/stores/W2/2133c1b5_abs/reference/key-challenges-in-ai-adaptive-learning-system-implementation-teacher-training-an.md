**Common challenges identified across case studies:**

1. Teacher professional development and training: Many teachers felt they lacked necessary skills and knowledge to effectively use AI-powered adaptive learning systems, which hindered successful implementation.

2. Curriculum alignment: When there was clear alignment between the AI system and the school's curriculum and learning goals, teachers were more likely to adopt and use the systems effectively.

**Recommendations:**

- Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and support as systems are implemented
- Developers should work closely with educators to ensure systems are designed with their needs in mind
- Provide clear guidance on how systems align with curriculum and learning goals
- Conduct ongoing evaluation and assessment of system effectiveness
