**Reasoning:** Selected Matsumoto over Hakuba Valley because:
- Primary interest in traditional Japanese culture and history (not winter sports)
- Closer proximity to Japanese Alps Visitor Center
- More laid-back, less touristy atmosphere
- Better public transportation access (fewer car rental needs)
- Richer cultural heritage with Matsumoto Castle, temples, and traditional streets

**Planned itinerary:**
1. Japanese Alps Visitor Center
2. Matsumoto Castle
3. Nawate Street stroll
4. Matsumoto Folkcraft Museum
5. Zenko-ji Temple (if time permits, preferably late afternoon/evening)

**Group:** Planning to invite friends from Tokyo to join for a few days.
