Taking English language proficiency classes to prepare for naturalization test. Eligibility: must be permanent resident (green card holder) for at least 5 years, or 3 years if married to a U.S. citizen. Benefits of early preparation: head start, improved English skills, increased confidence, familiarity with the process. Will require Form N-400 application when eligible.
