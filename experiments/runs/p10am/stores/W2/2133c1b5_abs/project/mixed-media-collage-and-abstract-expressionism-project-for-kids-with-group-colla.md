Planning a mixed media collage project combining abstract expressionism for kids. Structure: small groups of 3-4 kids working collaboratively, specific themes like cityscape or forest to guide creativity. Materials include construction paper, scrapbook paper, fabric, yarn, found objects, paint, markers, glitter, buttons, bottle caps, and cardboard tubes. Process: (1) introduce theme with visual inspiration and storytelling, (2) group brainstorming with open-ended questions about the theme, (3) quick sketches of ideas, (4) create large-scale collaborative collage. Key approach: emphasize process over product, encourage experimentation and imagination. Group management: assign group facilitator, establish clear expectations, circulate for guidance, set up materials station for rotation.
