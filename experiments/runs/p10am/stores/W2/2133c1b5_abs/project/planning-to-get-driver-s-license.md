Wants to get driver's license but has delayed due to long DMV wait times. Strategies to smooth the process: check state DMV website for requirements, gather documents, study for written test, schedule appointment, arrive early, bring entertainment and snacks, use DMV wait time apps, consider offices with shorter lines.
