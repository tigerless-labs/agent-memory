When asking for descriptions, wants vivid sensory details (sights, sounds, smells, textures) and emotional resonance rather than overview-style narrative. Dislikes brief, surface-level summaries. References the aesthetic of museum sets from 'How the Grinch Stole Christmas!' (2000, Jim Carrey film) as an example of the lived-in, detailed, immersive style preferred.
