The philosophical view that the only person you can trust is conscious is yourself is **solipsism**.

Solipsism holds that only one's own mind is certain to exist, and that all external realities—including other minds—are uncertain and may not exist. According to this view, the only person you can trust to have truly conscious and real existence is yourself. Other people and external objects may be mere illusions or figments of your imagination.

This is primarily a thought experiment and epistemological position in philosophy rather than a practical worldview most people adopt.
