Recipe: Brew strong coffee, mix with heavy cream, sugar, and eggs for custard base. Pour into ramekins, chill until set. Top with caramelized sugar crust before serving.

Caramelizing sugar technique:
- Use granulated sugar
- Heat skillet over medium-high heat until almost smoking  
- Sprinkle sugar in thin, even layer
- Don't stir; gently tilt pan for even cooking
- Look for deep amber or reddish-brown color
- Remove from heat immediately
- Let cool completely for crunchy glass-like crust
- Apply just before serving for best adhesion
