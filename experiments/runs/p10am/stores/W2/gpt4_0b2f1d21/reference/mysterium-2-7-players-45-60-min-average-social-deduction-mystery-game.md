**Mysterium Board Game Details**

- **Players:** 2-7 players recommended
- **Age:** Suitable for 12+ with adult guidance
- **Playtime:** 
  - Average: 45-60 minutes
  - First playthrough: 60-90 minutes  
  - Experienced players: 30-45 minutes
- **Gameplay:** One player is the Ghost, others are Psychic Mediums
- **Objective:** Ghost uses abstract vision cards to communicate with Psychics to identify the correct suspect, location, and murder weapon for a murder mystery
- **Structure:** 7 rounds total (7 nights in haunted mansion)
- **Strengths:** Encourages creative thinking, imagination, communication, and teamwork
- **For 12-year-olds:** Works well with guidance; benefits from simpler visions at first and collaborative approach
