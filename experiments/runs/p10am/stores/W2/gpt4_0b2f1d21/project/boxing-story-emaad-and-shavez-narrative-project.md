Creative fiction narrative featuring:

**Main Characters:**
- **Emaad**: Boxer who retires at age 46 after losing matches; trains CrustiChezz (Romanian fighter)
- **Shavez**: Emaad's younger brother; becomes biggest boxer in world; wins 7 Boxing World Cup trophies for Pakistan; faces Emaad in 8th World Cup final ("fight of the century")
- **Mustafa**: Arch-nemesis of Emaad; arrested, escaped after 2 days; challenges Emaad again; later shoots Shavez
- **CrustiChezz**: Fighter who seeks training from Emaad after their fight

**Plot Arc:**
1. Mustafa arrested, but escapes after 2 days and challenges Emaad again
2. Shavez has car accident while planning Eid surprise for Emaad
3. Emaad continues boxing but loses matches; retires at 46
4. Shavez rises to become dominant boxer, wins 7 World Cup trophies
5. Emaad defeats Shavez in 8th World Cup final ("fight of the century")
6. Mustafa shoots Shavez after the match
7. Shavez survives despite having no heartbeat; Emaad performs CPR and saves him

**Key Details:**
- Shavez's trophies won for Pakistan
- Emaad's age when retiring: 46
- Mustafa's prison escape: 2 days
- Eid subplot: Shavez planning surprise meal delivery
- Mustafa later killed by Emaad after shooting Shavez
