Closest star to our Solar System, approximately 4.24 light years away. NASA Parker Solar Probe would take over 6,000 years to reach it with current technology. Future propulsion concepts for interstellar travel include fusion-based systems, antimatter engines, warp drives, and theoretical wormholes.
