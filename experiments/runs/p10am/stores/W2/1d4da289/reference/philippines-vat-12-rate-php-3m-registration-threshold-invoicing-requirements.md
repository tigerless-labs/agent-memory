## Philippines VAT System

**VAT Rate:** 12%

**Calculation:** Multiply total cost by 0.12  
Example: PHP 1,000 × 0.12 = PHP 120 VAT; total with VAT = PHP 1,120

**VAT Registration:**
- **Threshold:** Annual sales or gross receipts exceeding PHP 3 million
- **VAT-registered businesses (>PHP 3M):**
  - Must charge VAT on sales
  - Must include VAT in invoices
  - Must file VAT returns with Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- **Non-VAT-registered businesses (<PHP 3M):**
  - Not required to charge VAT
  - Not required to include VAT in invoices
