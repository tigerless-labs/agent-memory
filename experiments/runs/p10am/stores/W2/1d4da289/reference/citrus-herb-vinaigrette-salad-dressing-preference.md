Prefers Citrus-Herb Vinaigrette as a salad dressing. Recipe: Mix together olive oil, freshly squeezed lemon or orange juice, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, and chopped fresh herbs like parsley, basil, or cilantro. Finds this option refreshing and light, perfect for weekday lunches with leftover chicken.
