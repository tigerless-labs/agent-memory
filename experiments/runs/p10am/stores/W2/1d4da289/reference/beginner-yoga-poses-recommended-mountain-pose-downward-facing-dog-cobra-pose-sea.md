Essential beginner poses with English and Sanskrit names:

1. **Mountain Pose (Tadasana)** - Foundational standing pose for posture and balance
2. **Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana)** - Stretches hamstrings, calves, and spine
3. **Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)** - Gentle chest opener and back strengthener
4. **Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana)** - Stretches hamstrings, calves, and spine
5. **Child's Pose (Balasana)** - Resting pose that stretches back and hips
6. **Tree Pose (Vrksasana)** - Balancing pose that improves focus and stability
7. **Seated Twist (Bharadvajasana)** - Promotes spinal flexibility and detoxification

**Tips for practicing:** Listen to your body, start slow with 20-30 minute sessions, focus on breathing, use props like blocks and straps, and practice at least 2-3 times per week.
