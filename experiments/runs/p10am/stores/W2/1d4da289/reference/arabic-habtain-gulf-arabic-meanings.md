**حبتين** (habtain/hubtain) in Gulf countries:

1. **Quantifier**: Two pieces, units, or items (e.g., two tablets of medicine, two candies)
2. **Affectionate slang**: Term of endearment in informal settings between close friends or family; similar to 'dear,' 'buddy,' or 'sweetheart'

Context determines the intended meaning.
