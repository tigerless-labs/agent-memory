Wearable device that provides real-time feedback and correction during yoga practice.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time feedback on form, technique, and breathing
- Recognizes and tracks 100+ yoga poses
- Breathing guidance synchronized with movements
- Personalized feedback based on practice style and goals
- Tracks progress with analytics on frequency, duration, and improvement
- Detects deviations from proper form and offers corrections
- Suggests alignment adjustments and movement modifications
