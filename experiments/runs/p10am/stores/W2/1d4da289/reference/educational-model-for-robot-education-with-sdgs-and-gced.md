Model for non-majors in robot education combining Global Citizenship Education and SDGs using Convergence D-SteamRobot. Emphasizes books for empathy about real-world problems, simple mechanical mechanisms, and student prototyping. Constructivist learning with exploration, peer-learning, divergent thinking. Develops computational and critical thinking skills.
