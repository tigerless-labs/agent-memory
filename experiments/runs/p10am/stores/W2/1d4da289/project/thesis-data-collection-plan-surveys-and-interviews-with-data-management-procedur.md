Data collection plan for thesis research on social media use and mental health.

**Quantitative Data Collection:**
- Method: Online surveys
- Platform: Secure online platform
- Content: Validated measures of social media use and mental health outcomes
- Sample: 500 young adults aged 18-25
- Sampling strategy: Online convenience sampling through social media platforms

**Qualitative Data Collection:**
- Method: Semi-structured interviews
- Platform: Video conferencing software
- Topics: Social media behaviors and mental health experiences
- Sample: Subset of survey participants or additional qualitative participants

**Data Quality and Management:**
- Data cleaning: Statistical software for standardization and outlier detection
- Data coding: Systematic application of thematic analysis framework
- Data storage: Secure server with restricted access
- Confidentiality: Anonymization of participant identifiers
- Analysis software: NVivo or Atlas.ti (pending selection)

**Data Analysis Timeline:**
- Quantitative: Descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, regression modeling
- Qualitative: Thematic analysis as per agreed framework
