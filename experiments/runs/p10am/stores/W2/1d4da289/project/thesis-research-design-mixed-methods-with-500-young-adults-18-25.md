## Methodology

**Design:** Mixed methods approach

**Target Population:** Young adults aged 18-25

**Sample Size:** 500 participants

**Quantitative Component:**
- Online surveys with validated measures of social media use and mental health outcomes
- Administered through secure online platform

**Qualitative Component:**
- Semi-structured interviews exploring social media behaviors and mental health experiences
- Conducted via video conferencing software
- Data analysis using thematic analysis

**Key Research Questions:**
1. How does social media use affect mental health outcomes in young adults?
2. What are the specific social media behaviors associated with negative mental health outcomes?
