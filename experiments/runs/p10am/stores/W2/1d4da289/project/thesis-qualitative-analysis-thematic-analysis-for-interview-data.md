Using thematic analysis to analyze semi-structured interview data.

**Analysis Process:**
- Familiarize with data through repeated reading of transcripts
- Initial coding of meaning units and concepts  
- Development of coding framework with categories and subcategories
- Apply framework across full dataset and refine
- Theme refinement and naming
- Create visual theme maps showing relationships
- Interpret themes in context of research questions

**Expected Themes:**
- Social media use habits (frequency, duration, platform preferences)
- Mental health experiences (anxiety, depression, self-esteem, body image)
- Social comparison and envy
- Social support and connection
- Self-presentation and identity formation
- Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) and anxiety
- Cyberbullying and online harassment
- Self-regulation and boundary-setting
- Parental and peer influence
- Coping mechanisms and strategies
