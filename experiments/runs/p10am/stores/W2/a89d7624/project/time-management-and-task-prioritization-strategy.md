Learned strategy for managing multiple commitments (studies + part-time/freelance work):

1. Create master calendar for entire semester (deadlines, exams, commitments)
2. Set clear goals and objectives, break into manageable tasks
3. Use task list or planner (Trello preferred), check off completed tasks
4. Allocate dedicated time for each type of task, stick to schedule
5. Learn to say no to avoid overcommitment
6. Take regular breaks and practice self-care (exercise, socializing, relaxation)
7. Review and adjust schedule regularly

Recommended tools: Google Calendar, Trello, Asana, Todoist, RescueTime, Toggl

Key principle: Prioritize quality over quantity. Set clear work/life boundaries.
