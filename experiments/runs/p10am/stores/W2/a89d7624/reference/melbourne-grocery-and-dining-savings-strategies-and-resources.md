Strategies and resources for saving money on food in Melbourne:

**Local Markets:**
- Queen Victoria Market
- South Melbourne Market

**Supermarkets:**
- Aldi (most budget-friendly)
- Coles (sales and loyalty programs)
- Woolworths

**Savings Strategies:**
- Plan weekly meals to reduce waste
- Buy non-perishables in bulk (rice, pasta, canned goods)
- Use cashback apps: Cashrewards, Checkout 51
- Avoid shopping when hungry

**Dining Out:**
- Follow restaurants on social media for promotions
- Use food delivery apps sparingly (Uber Eats, Foodora, Deliveroo)
- Try ethnic restaurants (Vietnamese, Chinese, Indian) for affordability
- Pack lunch instead of buying

**Budget Tips:**
- Allocate 50% income to essential expenses (including food)
- Set weekly food budget and track it
- Limit eating out to social occasions

Target food savings: 500-700 AUD/month possible.
