**New music:** Streaming platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal), music blogs (Pitchfork, NME, Stereogum, Indie Shuffle), music newsletters (The Fader, Pigeons & Planes, The Music Ninja), social media, local music communities, radio stations, artist websites
