**Ancient Greece**
- The Song of Achilles (Madeline Miller): Trojan War from Patroclus's perspective  
- Circe (Madeline Miller): Story of the witch Circe from Homer's Odyssey
- The Penelopiad (Margaret Atwood): Odyssey retold from Penelope's perspective
- The Odyssey (graphic novel, Gareth Hinds): Illustrated Homer adaptation
- The House of Nicholaos (Nick Brown): Daily life of young Athenian noble
- The Oracle of Delphi (D.J. Niko): Life of priestess at Delphi

**Ancient Rome**
- Rubicon (Tom Holland): Fall of Roman Republic and rise of Empire
- Lustrum (Robert Harris): Rise of statesman and orator Cicero
- Daughters of Rome (Kate Quinn): Lives of four women in ancient Rome
- The Memoirs of Hadrian (Marguerite Yourcenar): Life of Emperor Hadrian
- Romanitas (Sophia McDougall): Daily life and politics of Roman Empire

**Ancient Mesopotamia and Other Civilizations**
- The Red Tent (Anita Diamant): Story of Dinah in ancient Mesopotamia
- The Egyptian (Mika Waltari): Life of Egyptian physician in 14th century BCE
- The Golden Mean (Annabel Lyon): Aristotle and Alexander the Great
