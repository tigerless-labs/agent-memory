**Within walking distance:**
- The Ship Rock Grille (across street)
- The Village Tavern (5 min walk, pub-style fare)
- Morrison Inn (10 min walk, American comfort food)

**Short drive/Uber:**
- The Fort (2 miles, Southwestern cuisine)
- Bear Creek Tavern (2.5 miles, American classics)

Note: Food trucks and vendors operate during concert season.
