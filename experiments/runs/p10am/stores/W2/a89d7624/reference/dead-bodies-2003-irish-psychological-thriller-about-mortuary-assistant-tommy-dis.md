**Dead Bodies** is a 2003 Irish psychological thriller film.

**Plot:** Follows a hospital mortuary assistant named Tommy (played by Andrew Scott). He discovers a dead body with money in its mouth, which sets him on a course of dangerous and sudden events.

**Themes:** Grief, mental health, desperation

**Tone:** Intense with a grim edge
