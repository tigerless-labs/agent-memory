Platforms researched for freelancing and online tutoring opportunities:

**Freelancing:**
- Upwork
- Fiverr
- Freelancer

**Online Tutoring:**
- TutorMe
- Chegg
- Varsity Tutors

Benefits of freelancing: flexibility, build portfolio, potential for higher rates.
Benefits of tutoring: set own schedule/rates, remote work, positive student impact.

Considerations: visa restrictions, required skills, time commitment balance with studies.
