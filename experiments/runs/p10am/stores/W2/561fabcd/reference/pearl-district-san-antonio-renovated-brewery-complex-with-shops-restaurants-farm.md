Vibrant, upscale neighborhood at north end of River Walk; historic Pearl Brewery complex converted to shopping, dining, and entertainment hub.\n\n**Must-See Attractions**\n- The Pearl Brewery building (shops and restaurants)\n- Hotel Emma (stunning architecture, Sternewirth bar)\n- La Gloria (Mexican cuisine, craft cocktails)\n- The Boiler House (gastropub, impressive beer selection)\n- Elise (curated women's boutique)\n- The Twig Book Shop (independent bookstore with book clubs and events)\n- Jasmine Thai (authentic Thai, cozy atmosphere)\n\n**Pearl Farmers Market** - Saturdays 9am-1pm\n- Fresh produce (organic, heirloom varieties)\n- Artisanal breads, pastries, cakes, cookies\n- Ready-to-eat items: tacos, barbecue, sandwiches, international cuisine\n- Handmade jewelry, pottery, textiles, artwork\n- Gourmet foods: artisanal cheeses, charcuterie, honey, spices\n- Fresh-cut flowers, succulents, potted plants\n- Local craft brews and wines in beer and wine garden\n\n**Tips**\n- Arrive early for best selection\n- Bring reusable bags\n- Cash helpful for small vendors\n- Pet-friendly\n- Parking via garage or street parking\n- Popular for weekend brunch (expect crowds)\n- Explore surrounding neighborhoods: Olmos Park, Alamo Heights
