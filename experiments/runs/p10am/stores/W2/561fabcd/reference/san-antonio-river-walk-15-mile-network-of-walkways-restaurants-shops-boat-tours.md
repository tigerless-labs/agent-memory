Network of walkways along the San Antonio River lined with cypress trees, restaurants, bars, shops, and hotels. Stretches from Pearl District to Missions Trail (15 miles total).\n\n**Open Hours**: 24/7 (individual businesses have varying hours)\n\n**Things to Do**\n- Boat tours from multiple companies\n- Dining: La Gloria, Boudro's, The Esquire Tavern\n- Shopping: Boutiques, art galleries, souvenir shops, River Walk Mall\n- Pearl District at north end: upscale shops, restaurants, Farmers Market (Saturdays 9am-1pm)\n\n**Events Throughout Year**\n- Ford Holiday River Parade and Lighting Ceremony (late November)\n- Fiesta San Antonio (April)\n- Jazz & Blues Festival (September)\n\n**Amenities**\n- Free shuttle service available\n- Multiple parking garages and lots nearby\n- Public transportation and ride-share access
