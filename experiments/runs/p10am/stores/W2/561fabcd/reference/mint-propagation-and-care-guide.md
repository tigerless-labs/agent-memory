Mint propagation: stem cuttings (4-6 inches with lower leaves removed, roots in 1-2 weeks), division in spring/fall, layering. Pruning: pinch tips to encourage branching, cut back 1/3-1/2 every 4-6 weeks, divide every 6-12 months as it is invasive. Watering: every 2-3 days summer, 4-5 days winter. Fertilize every 1-2 weeks with balanced 10-10-10 at half strength. Regular pruning prevents flowering.
