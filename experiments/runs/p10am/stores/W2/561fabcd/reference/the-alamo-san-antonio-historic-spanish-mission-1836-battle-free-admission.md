Historic Spanish mission and symbol of Texas independence. In 1836, defenders including Davy Crockett and Jim Bowie fought against Mexican General Santa Anna; they lost but inspired the eventual Texas independence movement.\n\n**Location & Hours**\n- Address: 300 Alamo Plaza, San Antonio, TX 78205\n- Hours: Daily 9:00 AM-5:30 PM (extended March-October)\n- Admission: Free; guided tours available for fee\n\n**What to See**\n- Alamo Church\n- Long Barrack Museum\n- Alamo Gardens\n- 17-minute documentary: "The Battle of the Alamo"\n\n**Photography**\n- No photography allowed inside the Alamo Church\n- Photos permitted in gardens and other areas\n\n**Tips**\n- Visit early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds\n- Wear comfortable shoes\n- Bring sunscreen, hat, and water (San Antonio gets hot)
