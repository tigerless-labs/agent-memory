Hotel recommendations for San Antonio:\n\n**Omni La Mansión del Rio** (4-star, River Walk)\n- Spanish colonial-style architecture\n- Beautifully landscaped courtyard\n- Outdoor pool and fitness center\n- Tex-Mex restaurant and poolside bar\n\n**Hotel Contessa** (4-star, River Walk)\n- Modern rooms\n- Rooftop pool and fitness center\n- Contemporary American cuisine\n\n**Hotel Valencia Riverwalk** (4-star, downtown)\n- Chic, sophisticated atmosphere\n- Rooftop pool and fitness center\n- Gourmet cuisine\n\n**Hotel Emma** (4-star boutique)\n- Historic 19th-century brewhouse conversion\n- Beautiful courtyard and outdoor pool\n- Farm-to-table restaurant\n\nAll are within walking distance to River Walk and major attractions.
