Cilantro propagation: from seed (1/8 inch deep, 1-2 inches apart at 70F, transplant at 2-3 leaves) or stem cuttings (2-3 inches, roots in 1-2 weeks). Growth pattern: bolts quickly, short lifespan, sow new seeds every 1-2 weeks for continuous harvest. Harvesting: pinch flower buds regularly, harvest individual leaves/stems to encourage foliage. Watering: every 1-2 days (frequent). Fertilize every 1-2 weeks with balanced 10-10-10 at half strength.
