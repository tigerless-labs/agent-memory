Parsley propagation: stem cuttings (4-6 inches, roots in 1-2 weeks) or division in spring/fall. Growth: can bolt quickly, prefers partial shade and consistent moisture. Harvesting: pinch flower buds to encourage leaf growth, harvest outer (older) leaves first, cut back 1/3 every 4-6 weeks. Watering: every 2-3 days summer, 4-5 days winter. Fertilize every 1-2 weeks with balanced 10-10-10 at half strength.
