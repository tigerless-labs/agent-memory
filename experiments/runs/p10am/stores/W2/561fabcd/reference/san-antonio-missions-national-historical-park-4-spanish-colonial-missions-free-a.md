Preserves the largest collection of Spanish colonial architecture in the United States, along the San Antonio River.\n\n**The Four Missions**\n\n1. **Mission Concepción** (founded 1716)\n   - Known for stunning frescoes and ornate architecture\n\n2. **Mission San José** (1720)\n   - Called the "Queen of the Missions"\n   - Famous for beautiful stone carvings and ornate decorations\n\n3. **Mission San Juan** (1716)\n   - Known for tranquil atmosphere\n   - Picturesque surroundings\n\n4. **Mission Espada** (1731)\n   - Southernmost of the four\n   - Features beautiful chapel and peaceful grounds\n\n**Visiting Information**\n- Hours: Daily 9am-5pm (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day)\n- Admission: Free (some special events may require fee)\n- Ranger-led tours available\n- Self-guided tour brochures and maps provided\n\n**Scenic Mission Trail**\n- Connects all four missions\n- Offers stunning views of San Antonio River and surrounding landscape\n\n**Tips**\n- Wear comfortable shoes for walking and exploration\n- Bring sunscreen and water (Texas heat can be intense)\n- Respect the historical significance and cultural sensitivity of each site
