## Sources for environmental engineering datasets

Key data sources for environmental engineering research and projects:

- **NOAA** — Climate, weather, and oceanography datasets
- **EPA** — Air and water quality, hazardous waste
- **USGS** — Water resources, geology, natural hazards
- **Data.gov** — Multi-agency datasets including environmental topics
- **World Bank** — Environmental and development datasets (water, sanitation, air pollution, climate change)
- **Kaggle** — Community-hosted datasets including environmental engineering datasets

Additional sources available through web research.
