Major festivals in North Wales:\n\n- **National Eisteddfod of Wales**: Annual, held in different location each year. Week-long festival celebrating Welsh language, literature, music, folk traditions. Attracts thousands of visitors.\n- **Conwy Feast**: Annual food festival in Conwy (historic coastal town). Local food/drink with music, arts, crafts.\n- **Royal Welsh Show**: Annual agricultural show near Builth Wells. Showcases Welsh farming, livestock competitions, agricultural exhibits, traditional crafts.\n- **Llangollen International Musical Eisteddfod**: Annual music and dance festival in Llangollen. International artists and performers celebrate diverse musical and cultural traditions.\n- **Anglesey Agricultural Show**: Two-day annual show on Isle of Anglesey. Welsh livestock, farm produce, family-friendly entertainment.
