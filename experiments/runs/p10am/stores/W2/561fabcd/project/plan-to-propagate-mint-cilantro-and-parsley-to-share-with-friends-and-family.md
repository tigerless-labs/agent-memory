Goal: propagate mint, cilantro, and parsley plants to share with friends and family. Wait until parent plants established before propagating. Choose fresh, healthy parent material. Mint/parsley: stem cuttings or division preferred. Cilantro: mostly from seed (bolts too quickly for reliable cuttings). Key requirements: keep soil consistently moist, bright indirect light, 65-75F day/55-65F night. Roots develop in 1-2 weeks typically. Transplant gently to new pots when ready.
