## Visual Design

- Humanoid host in radiated environment with fused protective suit
- Body bloated with tumors and blisters, sickly green tint
- Green parasite visible under skin, writhing and pulsating
- Parasite protrudes from gash in neck seeking new host
- Milky or glowing eyes, hunched lurching gait
- Jerky spasmodic movements, low guttural growl
- Leaves trail of green glowing slime

## Mechanics

- AoE radiation damage: closer = more damage over time
- Vision distortion warns players; partially negated by Cosmic Cleanse
- Radiation poisoning from attacks
- Parasite attack: tries to worm into mouth, slowing player
- Death effect: radioactive burst transforms nearby zombies
- Slime trail causes damage
- Lingering radiation fields from attacks
- Environmental objects become irradiated

## Rationale

- Slow lumbering threat, stress from passive radiation not direct damage
- Fused suit traps parasite, explains why it cannot escape
- Visible parasite struggling adds horror and telegraphs attacks
- Multiple overlapping damage forces constant player adaptation
- Zombie transformation keeps gameplay fresh
