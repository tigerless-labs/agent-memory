Key Beatles albums recommended from May 2023 discussion:

- **Revolver** (1966): Turning point in their career; features psychedelic rock and Indian-influenced styles
- **Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band** (1967): Landmark concept album exploring wide range of musical styles and themes
- **Abbey Road** (1969): Final album, widely regarded as a masterpiece featuring 'Come Together', 'Something', 'Here Comes the Sun'
- **Rubber Soul** (1965): Significant departure from straightforward rock and roll; includes 'Norwegian Wood', 'Drive My Car', 'In My Life'
- **The White Album** (1968): Double album, sprawling and eclectic with wide variety of styles

Note: These were recommended as starting points for deeper exploration of the Beatles catalog beyond radio hits.
