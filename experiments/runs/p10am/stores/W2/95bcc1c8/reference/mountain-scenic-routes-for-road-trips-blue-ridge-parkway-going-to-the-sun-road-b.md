General mountain scenic route recommendations:

**Scenic Drives:**
- Blue Ridge Parkway (North Carolina/Virginia): 469 miles through Blue Ridge Mountains
- Going-to-the-Sun Road (Montana): 50 miles through Glacier National Park
- Beartooth Highway (Montana/Wyoming): 68 miles through Beartooth Mountains
- Million Dollar Highway (Colorado): 25 miles through San Juan Mountains (Silverton to Ouray)
- Kancamagus Highway (New Hampshire): 34 miles through White Mountains

**Mountain Attractions:**
- Grand Teton National Park (Wyoming)
- Asheville, North Carolina (arts scene, breweries, outdoor activities)
- Glacier National Park (Montana)
- Estes Park, Colorado (Rocky Mountain National Park entrance)
- Mount Rainier National Park (Washington)
