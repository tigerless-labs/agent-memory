## Use cases for hockey teams

1. **Player scouting**: Analyze statistics (goals, assists, plus-minus) to identify top-performing talent
2. **Game strategy**: Identify opposing team patterns (e.g., power-play) and suggest defensive adjustments
3. **Player performance**: Track metrics like save percentage and suggest positioning/technique improvements
4. **Injury prediction**: Detect increased injury risk based on training load, recommend preventive measures
5. **Fan engagement**: Personalize in-game experiences (food, merchandise, seat recommendations)
6. **Social media**: Generate pre-game hype posts with player stats and matchup previews
7. **Ticket pricing**: Dynamically adjust prices based on opponent, weather, demand
8. **Merchandise**: Predict demand for specific player jerseys post-performance, manage inventory
9. **Sponsorship**: Identify local retailers/businesses, create tailored partnership proposals
10. **Game scheduling**: Optimize schedules to avoid conflicts with local events, maximize attendance
11. **Travel planning**: Find cost-effective flights/accommodations considering team comfort
12. **Nutrition**: Create personalized meal plans (e.g., high-protein, low-carb for muscle building)
13. **Fitness monitoring**: Track VO2 max, recommend personalized cardio for endurance
14. **Fan profiling**: Segment fans (die-hard, casual, youth families) for targeted communication
15. **Venue management**: Schedule ice resurfacing and maintenance for efficiency
16. **Virtual coaching**: Offer sessions with real-time feedback on techniques like wrist shots
17. **Fan sentiment analysis**: Monitor social media reactions, especially during losing streaks
18. **Financial forecasting**: Predict playoff ticket revenue to inform investment decisions
19. **Competitive analysis**: Analyze rival teams to identify performance advantages
20. **Marketing optimization**: A/B test promotional graphics and messaging
21. **Contract negotiation**: Analyze player performance and comparable contracts for fair pricing
22. **Community outreach**: Identify local charity events for team participation and publicity
23. **Media relations**: Draft and distribute press releases to journalists
24. **Mental health**: Monitor player stress, recommend relaxation and support resources
25. **E-sports**: Develop virtual hockey tournament to generate fan excitement
26. **Content generation**: Create recap blogs with quotes and highlights
27. **Event planning**: Coordinate fan meet-and-greets, manage logistics
28. **Brand development**: Analyze fan data to design logos appealing to new and long-time supporters
29. **Video analysis**: Analyze game footage for coaching insights and promotional content
30. **Sustainability**: Recommend sustainable practices for operations and facilities
