Ibis Paris Gare de Lyon Reuilly (12th arr): 80 euros/night, 3-star with bar and breakfast. Hotel Eiffel Kensington (15th): 60 euros/night, 10-min walk from Eiffel Tower. Hotel des Archives (3rd arr, Marais): 70 euros/night. Hotel Le Walt (9th): 60 euros/night, near Opera. Hostel Les Piaules (11th): 30 euros/night for dorms. Neighborhoods: Latin Quarter (5-6th) with cafes and nightlife, Le Marais (3-4th) trendy with galleries, Montmartre (9th-18th) bohemian.
