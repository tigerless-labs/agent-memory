Used homemade cleaning solution (1 tsp baking soda + 2 tsp warm water) on diamond ring on February 2, 2023. Applied gently with soft-bristled toothbrush, rinsed thoroughly, dried with soft cloth. Ring looks sparkling new. Method works for diamond rings but NOT for pearls, opals, or porous stones.
