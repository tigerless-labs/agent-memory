Modified Tahoe to Yosemite bike trip planned for beginner-friendly pace:

**Route**: Carson City, NV (start, lower elevation 4,697 ft) → Gardnerville → Markleeville → Bridgeport → Tuolumne Meadows

**Itinerary**:
- Day 1: Carson City to Gardnerville (20 mi, 1,000 ft gain)
- Day 2: Gardnerville to Markleeville (25 mi, 1,500 ft gain)
- Day 3: Markleeville to Bridgeport (30 mi, 2,000 ft gain)
- Day 4: Bridgeport to Tuolumne Meadows (40 mi, 2,500 ft gain)

**E-bike rental**: First time riding e-bike; renting from Lake Tahoe area (Tahoe City or South Lake Tahoe shops)

**Battery concerns addressed**: 
- Expect 20-40 mi range on flat/easy terrain with minimal assist
- 10-20 mi on hilly terrain with moderate assist
- 5-10 mi on steep terrain with high assist
- Charging available at campgrounds, RV parks, lodges, general stores, hotels along route

**Next steps**: Research cyclist-friendly accommodations (camping or lodges) with showers and laundry; identify must-see attractions
