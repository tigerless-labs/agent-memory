Recognized pattern of impulse purchases and committed to being more intentional with money. Strategies include:
- Setting a budget before shopping
- Waiting 24 hours before purchases
- Researching and reading reviews
- Comparing prices across retailers
- Looking for sales and second-hand options
- Focusing on value over price tags

Goal: Find quality products at affordable prices without sacrificing durability.
