Approach for engaging in book club discussions: Use I-statements (I felt, I think) rather than absolute claims. Provide specific examples from the book to support opinions. Use respectful disagreement phrases like I see it differently instead of You are wrong. Prepare 2-3 key points or questions beforehand but keep notes brief. Use notes as a guide, not rigid requirements. Practice active listening and respond thoughtfully to others. Arrive early to introduce yourself. Focus on building genuine connections rather than winning arguments. Be open-minded to differing perspectives.
