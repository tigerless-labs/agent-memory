#BreakTheSilenceMH is the chosen branded hashtag for the mental health awareness social media campaign (May 2023). Straightforward approach that encourages people to speak up and break silence surrounding mental health issues. Used across Instagram and Twitter to track conversations, create sense of community, and encourage user-generated content from followers sharing their own mental health experiences and stories.
