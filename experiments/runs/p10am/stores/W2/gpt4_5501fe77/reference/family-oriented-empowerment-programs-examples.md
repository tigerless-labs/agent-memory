Programs that successfully incorporated family-oriented approaches in their design:

**Strong Families, Safe Communities (SFSC)**
- Evidence-based initiative for low-income families affected by chronic poverty
- Emphasizes comprehensive family-based interventions including parent education, family support, and family book groups

**Parent-Child Home Program (PCHP)**
- Targets families living in poverty to support early childhood development and school readiness
- Uses trained home visitors who work with families twice a week in their homes
- Delivers educational materials and supports parent-child interaction

**Strengthening Families Program: For Parents and Youth 10-14 (SFP 10-14)**
- Family-based preventive intervention program for youth aged 10-14
- Designed to build family competencies and enable successful transitions
- Emphasizes family dynamics, social competencies, and relationship enhancement
