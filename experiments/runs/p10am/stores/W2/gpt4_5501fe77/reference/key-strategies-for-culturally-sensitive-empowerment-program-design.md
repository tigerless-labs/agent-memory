Strategies discussed in May 2023 conversation:

- **Involve diverse community members in program design** — ensure perspectives, preferences, and values are represented
- **Develop clear understanding of cultural context** — understand cultural norms, values, and beliefs to ensure program relevance
- **Tailor communication strategies** — consider language, literacy level, and cultural preferences (e.g., use recognizable images and simple text)
- **Adapt delivery methods to the community** — consider access barriers (internet access, transportation); may need paper-based, DVD, or other non-digital options
- **Build on existing community strengths** — e.g., if storytelling is valued, use narrative-based or visual program delivery

These strategies help ensure empowerment programs are culturally appropriate and meet the unique needs of the communities they serve.
