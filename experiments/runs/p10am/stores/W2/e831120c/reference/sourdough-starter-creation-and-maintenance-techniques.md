Key sourdough starter practices:

**Creating a starter:**
- Mix 1/2 cup flour and 1/2 cup water (1:1 ratio)
- Use high-protein bread flour or all-purpose flour
- Use filtered or bottled water (avoid chlorine)
- Keep at room temperature 75-80°F (24-27°C)
- Let rest 24-48 hours initially

**Daily maintenance:**
- Feed once daily with 1:1 ratio (flour to water)
- Discard half before feeding
- Monitor activity, smell, and appearance

**Refrigerator storage:**
- Store at 39-45°F (4-7°C)
- Feed once before storing
- Feed weekly when stored long-term

**Reviving from refrigeration:**
- Allow 24-48 hours at room temperature (75-80°F)
- Feed once or twice during revival
- Use when active, bubbly, and frothy

**Long-term storage options:**
- Freeze: mix with equal parts water and flour, store in airtight container
- Dehydrate: spread on parchment, dehydrate at 150°F for 1-2 hours

**Feeding schedule example:**
- Once daily: Feed at 8am daily
- Twice daily: Feed at 8am and 8pm
