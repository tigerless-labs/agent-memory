Museums and sites in Paris related to French Resistance during WWII:
- Musée de la Résistance: dedicated to the history of the French Resistance
- Musée Jean Moulin: dedicated to Jean Moulin, a key figure in unifying Resistance groups
- Mont Valérien Memorial: honors French Resistance fighters executed by the Nazis
- Musée de l\"Armée: extensive section on Resistance and Liberation
- Musée Carnavalet: includes Occupation and French Resistance section
- Cimetière de Montparnasse: final resting place of many Resistance fighters including Jean Moulin

Guided tours available: Paris Resistance Tour (2 hours), WWII in Paris Tour (3 hours), French Resistance and Liberation Tour (4 hours). Many offer audio guides or guided tours in English.
