Recommended restaurants in Paris:
- Le Comptoir du Relais (6th arr.): classic French dishes, steak tartare, escargots
- Chez L\"Ami Jean (11th arr.): organic, locally sourced, duck confit
- Le Grand Vefour: historic restaurant (since 1784), foie gras, wild game, roasted scallops
- La Réserve (8th arr.): seasonal menu, fresh ingredients
- Le Petit Châtelet (5th arr.): roasted chicken, vegetables, seafood platter
- Breizh Café: seasonal crepes, organic ingredients
- Le 6 Paul Bert (11th arr.): seasonal menu, creative dishes
- La Dame de Pic (11th arr.): Michelin-starred, seasonal focus

Tip: Make reservations in advance; consider lunch menus (menu du jour) for better prices.
