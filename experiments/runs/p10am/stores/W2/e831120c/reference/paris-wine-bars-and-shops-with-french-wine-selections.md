Wine venues in Paris:

Wine Bars:
- La Closerie des Lilas (6th arr.): extensive selection, rare and vintage bottles
- Le Barav (11th arr.): natural and organic wines from small producers
- Septime La Cave (11th arr.): rotating selection, natural/organic wines, small plates

Wine Shops:
- La Maison du Vin: multiple locations, wide selection
- Nicolas: chain with multiple Paris locations
- Le Wine Shop (6th arr.): curated selection, wine tastings and workshops

Wine Markets:
- Marché aux Vins (12th arr.): taste and purchase from producers
- Marché d\"Aligre (12th arr.): variety of wines including natural/organic options

Tips: Ask staff for recommendations; try wine flights or tastings; wine prices vary significantly.
