How to toast walnuts to enhance flavor:

**Benefits of toasting:**
- Brings out natural oils
- Enhances fragrance and flavor
- Reduces bitterness
- Improves texture and crispness

**Oven method (preferred):**
- Preheat to 350°F (180°C)
- Spread walnuts in single layer on baking sheet
- Toast 5-7 minutes
- Stir halfway through
- Cool completely before using

**Pan-toasting method:**
- Dry skillet over medium heat
- Stir frequently
- Toast until fragrant and lightly browned

**Microwave method:**
- Use short 30-45 second intervals
- Stir between intervals
- Risk of burning if not careful

**Bread baking tips:**
- Chop coarsely (about 1/4 inch pieces)
- Distribute evenly throughout dough
- Consider mixing toasted and untoasted for complex flavor
- Optional: add walnut oil or extract to enhance flavor
