Best practices for adding nuts to bread dough:

**Preparation:**
- Chop nuts coarsely (about 1/4 inch pieces)
- Avoid over-chopping which creates uneven distribution

**Incorporation timing:**
- Add during mixing phase
- After autolyse but before bulk fermentation
- Use gentle mixing to prevent crushing

**Mixing techniques:**
- Use stand mixer with dough hook (gentle setting)
- Or mix by hand
- Avoid overmixing which breaks down nuts

**Folding and fermentation:**
- Gently fold dough to distribute nuts evenly
- This is especially important in first few folds
- Use gentle touch during shaping
- Avoid over-proofing (causes uneven distribution or sinking)

**Special considerations for spelt flour:**
- Spelt is more delicate than other flours
- Be gentle when handling dough
- Avoid overdeveloping gluten
- Spelt absorbs more liquid than other flours
- Spelt breads may be denser than wheat breads

**Scoring:**
- Use gentle, shallow cuts when scoring
- Avoid cutting into nuts to prevent them from becoming exposed
