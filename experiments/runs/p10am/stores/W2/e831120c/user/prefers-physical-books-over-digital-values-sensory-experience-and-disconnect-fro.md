Strongly prefers reading physical books over digital formats. Values the tangible experience: the feel of the book in hands, the smell of pages, and the sound of pages turning. Appreciates that physical books don't require battery life or internet connectivity. Notes that reading paper reduces eye strain compared to screens and helps create mental distance from the digital world. Sees physical books as a way to disconnect and immerse in reading without device distractions.
