When inviting speakers from companies, maintain informal friendly tone. Instead of saying 'avoid sales pitch', use alternative framings like 'focus on knowledge and insights' or 'promotion or advertisement of your products'. Emphasize community values: knowledge sharing, discussions, and community building.
