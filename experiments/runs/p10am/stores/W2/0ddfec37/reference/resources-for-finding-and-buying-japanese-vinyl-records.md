Specialty stores: Jet Set Records (jetsetrecords.com), HMV Japan (hmv.co.jp), Disk Union (diskunion.net)

Online communities: r/vinyl, r/JapaneseMusic, Japanese music forums

In-person Tokyo stores: Tower Records Shibuya, HMV Shibuya, Disk Union Shibuya, Meditations (meditations.jp)

Auction sites: Yahoo Japan Auctions (auctions.yahoo.co.jp), Rakuten
