General marketplaces: eBay, Etsy, Amazon

Photography-specialized: Fred Miranda (forum with marketplace), Rangefinder Forum, Photography Stack Exchange

Social platforms: Facebook groups (vintage cameras), Instagram (#vintagecameras #leica), Reddit (r/photography, r/vintagecameras, r/leica)

Specialized dealers: KEH Camera, WestLicht Auctions

Selling tips: research comparable prices, clean camera, take high-quality multi-angle photos, write detailed condition descriptions, use secure payment methods
