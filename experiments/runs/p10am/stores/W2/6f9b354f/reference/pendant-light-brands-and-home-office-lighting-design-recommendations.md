**Pendant Light Brands for Modern Home Offices**

1. **Muuto** - Scandinavian-inspired designs, minimalist aesthetic
2. **Lumens** - Wide selection of pendant lights in industrial-chic and minimalist styles
3. **Schoolhouse Electric** - Modern design with industrial flair and personality

**How to Choose the Right Pendant Light**

**Size & Scale:**
- Consider scale of room and desk
- Larger lights can overwhelm small spaces
- Smaller lights may get lost in larger rooms

**Shape & Design:**
- Geometric shapes work well for modern spaces: sphere, cylinder, rectangle
- Complement overall aesthetic of workspace
- Choose based on minimalist vs. industrial vs. Scandinavian preference

**Material & Finish:**
- Metal options: copper, brass, chrome
- Glass or acrylic for transparency
- Matte or satin finishes avoid harsh reflections
- Should complement natural wood accents

**Lighting Direction:**
- Downward-directed: better for task lighting over desk
- Upward-directed: more ambient, general room lighting
- Consider your primary lighting needs

**Technical Specifications:**
- Lumen output: 300-400 lumens per square foot (calculate based on room size)
- Color temperature: Warm white (2700K-3000K) for cozy atmosphere; cool white (3500K-4100K) for energizing effect
- Dimmer compatibility: Check if compatible with dimmer switches for flexibility
- Energy efficiency: LED options recommended

**Complementary Lighting:**
- Combine with table lamps for focused task lighting
- Add sconces for soft ambient lighting
- Consider recessed lighting for clean, minimalist look
- Under-cabinet lighting for shelving
- Floor lamps for pools of light in workspace
