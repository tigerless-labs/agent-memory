Low-light indoor plants suitable for kitchens or offices with limited natural light:

1. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)** - Purifies air, survives on neglect, low-maintenance, can go weeks without watering

2. **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)** - Easy care, can grow long, trainable on trellis or as climbing vine

3. **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)** - Extreme low-light tolerance, very infrequent watering needed, great for busy people

4. **Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii)** - Elegant white blooms, excellent air purifier, thrives in low light

5. **Dracaena (Dracaena spp.)** - Over 40 varieties available, diverse shapes and sizes, adaptable

6. **Philodendron (Philodendron spp.)** - Range of shapes available, compact to trailing varieties

7. **Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema modestum)** - Beautiful variegated leaves, low-maintenance, infrequent watering

8. **Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)** - Easy care, air purifier, produces cute white flowers, very forgiving

9. **Parlor Palm (Chamaedorea elegans)** - Slender and elegant, space-efficient, prefers low to medium light

10. **Bromeliad (Bromeliaceae family)** - Includes pineapples and air plants, low-maintenance and adaptable

**General Tips:**
- Even low-light tolerant plants benefit from indirect sunlight
- North-facing windows are ideal
- Grow lights can help them thrive
- Position plants near windows when possible to maximize available light
