**ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) Complete Care Guide**

**Watering Schedule:**
- **Summer:** Every 1-2 weeks (water when soil feels dry to the touch)
- **Winter:** Every 4-6 weeks (plant enters dormancy)
- Allow soil to dry between waterings
- Avoid overwatering to prevent root rot
- Water sparingly; do not get water on leaves to prevent fungal diseases

**Fertilization:**
- **Frequency:** Once per month during growing season (spring and summer only)
- **Fertilizer Type:** Balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20 ratio)
- **Application:** Dilute to half the recommended strength to avoid burning roots
- **Winter:** Skip fertilizing during dormant months

**Light & Environment:**
- Avoid direct sunlight (causes leaf scorch and damage)
- Prefers low to medium indirect light
- Extremely tolerant of low-light conditions
- Can adapt to various light conditions

**Soil & Potting:**
- Prefers well-draining soil
- Tolerates various potting mixes
- Good drainage is essential to prevent root rot

**Maintenance:**
- Prune to maintain desired shape
- Remove dead or damaged leaves promptly
- Clean leaves occasionally to remove dust

**Safety:**
- Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
- Keep out of reach of children and curious pets
- Wear gloves when handling to avoid skin irritation

**Summary:** ZZ Plants are ideal for busy people or beginners due to their ability to tolerate neglect and survive in extreme low-light conditions with minimal watering.
