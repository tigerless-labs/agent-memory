Key scholars and resources for feminist art study:

**Scholars:**
- Linda Nochlin - pioneering art historian, feminist scholar; seminal essay Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists (1971)
- Lucy Lippard - art critic, curator, and feminist activist; wrote From the Center: Feminist Essays on Women's Art (1976) and Get the Message?: A Decade of Art for Social Change (1984)
- Griselda Pollock - British art historian and feminist scholar; wrote Old Mistresses: Women, Art and Ideology (1981) and Vision and Difference: Feminism, Femininity and the Histories of Art (1988)
- Cornelia Butler - curator and art historian; worked on WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution exhibition (2007) catalog and essays

**Key Exhibition:**
- WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution (2007-2008) at Museum of Contemporary Art, Los Angeles - surveyed feminist art from the 1960s to 1980s

**Organizations:**
- The Feminist Art Base - online archive founded by Brooklyn Museum with 200+ artists and 1500+ artworks
- The Women's Art Library (MAKE) - London-based online archive and library collecting materials on women's art
- The Guerrilla Girls - anonymous collective of feminist artists active since 1980s, producing posters, exhibitions, and performances
