## Cilantro Lime Dressing

Ingredients:
- Fresh cilantro, finely chopped
- Lime juice
- Olive oil
- Garlic, minced
- Salt to taste

Whisk together and drizzle over quinoa and black bean salad. Works especially well with grilled chicken.

Flavor pairing ideas for quinoa salad:
- Spicy roasted vegetables (bell peppers, onions, jalapenos)
- Grilled pineapple chunks
- Avocado crema (mashed avocado + lime + salt + cumin)
- Feta cheese crumbles
- Chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- Hard-boiled eggs, diced tomatoes, or black olives
