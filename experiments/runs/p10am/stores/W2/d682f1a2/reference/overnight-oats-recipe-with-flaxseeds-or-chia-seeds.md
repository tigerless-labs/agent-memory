## Overnight Oats with Seeds

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds OR 1 tablespoon ground flaxseeds
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
- Pinch of salt

Toppings: sliced banana, berries, shredded coconut, cinnamon, Greek yogurt

Instructions:
1. Combine oats, milk, seeds, and sweetener in jar
2. Stir well to coat seeds evenly
3. Add salt and stir
4. Cover and refrigerate 4+ hours or overnight
5. Stir in morning, add more milk if needed, top and serve

Tips:
- Chia seeds absorb liquid and expand, thickening the mixture
- Flaxseeds should be ground just before use (use coffee grinder)
- Can add vanilla extract, cinnamon, or protein powder
- Pre-ground flaxseeds available at health food stores if grinding is inconvenient
