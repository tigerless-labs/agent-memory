**Abstract Expressionism:**
- The New York School: The Painters and Sculptors of the Fifties by Irving Sandler
- Pollock by Kirk Varnedoe (MoMA curator)
- Mark Rothko: The Decisive Decade: 1940-1950 by Todd Herman
- Abstract Expressionism by David Anfam
- Art Since 1940: Strategies of Being by Fred S. Kleiner
- Theories of Modern Art: From Impressionism to Abstract Expressionism edited by Herschel B. Chipp

**Anselm Kiefer:**
- Anselm Kiefer: Heaven and Earth by Anselm Kiefer and Lisa Dennison
- Anselm Kiefer: The High Priestess by Anselm Kiefer and Klaus Dermutz

**Art Conservation:**
- The Conservator's Handbook by Valerie Dorge and Florian K. P. Horenburg
- Art Conservation: Principles and Practice by Andrew Oddy and Steven Smith
- The Art of Conservation: Preserving the Past for the Future by David Bomford

**Feminist Art:**
- The Feminist Art Movement: Images and Ideologies by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard
- Feminist Art and the Maternal by Andrea Liss
- WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution edited by Cornelia Butler and Lisa Gabrielle Mark
