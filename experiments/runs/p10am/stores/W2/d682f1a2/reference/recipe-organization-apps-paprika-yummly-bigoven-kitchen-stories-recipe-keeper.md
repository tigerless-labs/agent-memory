Paprika Recipe Manager (iOS/Android/Windows): Comprehensive organization, tagging, search, meal planning, grocery lists, nutritional analysis. Yummly (iOS/Android): Discover and save recipes; meal planning, grocery lists. BigOven (iOS/Android): 200,000+ recipe database; organize, create cookbooks. Kitchen Stories (iOS/Android): Visually appealing; organize recipes, create cookbooks, watch videos. Recipe Keeper (iOS/Android): Simple, intuitive; organization, tagging, meal planning, lists. Also consider: Evernote, OneNote, Google Keep.
