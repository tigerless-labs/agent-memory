## Tips for Juicy Grilled Chicken Breast

Key Techniques:
1. Marinate: Olive oil + acid (lime juice/vinegar) + spices for 30+ minutes before grilling
2. Brine: Soak in saltwater (1 tablespoon salt per 1 cup water) for 30-60 minutes
3. Pound evenly: Gently pound breast to consistent thickness
4. Low heat: Grill at medium-low (325-350F) to avoid burning outside before inside cooks
5. Dont press: Never press down with spatula - squeezes out juices
6. Temperature check: Use meat thermometer; pull at 165F internal temp
7. Rest: Let rest 5-10 minutes after cooking before slicing

Flavorful Marinades:
- Chipotle lime: lime juice + olive oil + chipotle peppers + cumin
- Herby: olive oil + garlic + fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, parsley) + salt + pepper
- Korean-style: soy sauce + brown sugar + garlic + ginger + gochujang

Alternative Cooking Methods if Grilling Remains Dry:
- Pan-searing in hot skillet
- Oven roasting with aromatics (onions, carrots, celery)
- Poaching in chicken or vegetable broth
