Glass containers (Pyrex, Anchor Hocking, OXO): Non-toxic, eco-friendly, suitable for hot/cold foods. Stainless steel (S'well, Hydro Flask, Klean Kanteen): Durable, rust-resistant, keeps temperature for hours. BPA-free plastic (Rubbermaid, Glad, Ziploc): Affordable, various durable materials. Budget options: Mason jars, reusable takeout containers. Key features: Portion control markings, airtight seals, stackability, microwave-safe, dishwasher-safe.
