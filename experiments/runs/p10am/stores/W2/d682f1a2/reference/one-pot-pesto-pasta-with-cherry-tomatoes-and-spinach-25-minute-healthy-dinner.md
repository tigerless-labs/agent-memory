## Recipe

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Total Time:** 25 minutes

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup whole-grain pasta
- 1/4 cup pesto sauce
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1/2 cup grated mozzarella cheese (optional)
- Salt, pepper, olive oil

**Why it's healthy:** Whole-grain pasta provides fiber and complex carbs. Pesto is rich in healthy fats and antioxidants from basil. Cherry tomatoes have vitamin C and lycopene. Spinach adds iron and vitamins A and K.
