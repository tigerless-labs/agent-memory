Two options being considered for 20-gallon tank: Lemon Tetras - can school peacefully with existing neon tetras, similar size and temperament. Zebra Danios - hardy and active, coexist peacefully with current fish. Both should be introduced in groups of 6-10+. Decision not yet made.
