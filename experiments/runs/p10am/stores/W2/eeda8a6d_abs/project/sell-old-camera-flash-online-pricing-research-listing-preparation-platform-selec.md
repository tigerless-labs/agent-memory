Selling used photography equipment strategy (May 2023):

Research Phase:
- Check eBay, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace for comparable flashes
- Note condition, features, and prices of similar items

Preparation:
- Document complete specifications (make, model, condition, features like TTL, bounce head)
- Clean flash thoroughly
- Take clear photos from multiple angles
- Gather original packaging and manuals if available

Listing:
- Include detailed condition description
- List all features and camera compatibility
- Note any included accessories
- Set competitive price based on research

Platform Choice:
- eBay: larger audience but fees apply
- Craigslist: free, local sales, requires in-person safety precautions
- Consider bundling with other unused gear

Safety: Meet potential buyers in public locations, follow standard safety protocols.
