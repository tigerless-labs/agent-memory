## Key Contract Law Principles
- DLP creates contractual obligation for contractor to rectify defects post-practical completion
- Expiry of DLP does NOT end contractor's common law liability
- Common law claim period: 12 years (deed) or 6 years (agreement)
- Specific performance (defect rectification) rarely granted; contractual DLP clause is critical
- All content to be in Australian English
