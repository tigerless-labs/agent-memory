Popular Swedish dishes to try in Stockholm:\n- Swedish meatballs with cream sauce and lingonberry jam (Pelikan, Meatballs for the People, Rosendals Trädgård)\n- Gravlax: Cured salmon appetizer (Nystekt Strömming, Östermalms Saluhall, Wedholms Fisk)\n- Smörgåsbord: Traditional buffet with pickled herring, cold cuts, cheeses (Kvarnen, Grand Hôtel Veranda, T/Bar)\n- Toast Skagen: Shrimp, mayo, dill on bread (Restaurang AG, Sturehof, Krog Karl Johans Torg)\n- Falukorv: Swedish sausage with mashed potatoes and lingonberry jam (Tranan, Tradition Gastrobar, Pane Vino)\n- Raggmunk: Potato pancakes (Bakfickan, Tennstopet, Hasselbacken)\n- Janssons Frestelse: Potato and anchovy casserole (Tysta Mari, Prinsen, Fem små hus)
