Swedish cinnamon buns widely available in Stockholm bakeries and cafes. Recommended locations: Fabrique, Bullar och Bröd, Café Saturnus. Known for warm, comforting cinnamon taste and fluffy texture. Best enjoyed with coffee or tea.
