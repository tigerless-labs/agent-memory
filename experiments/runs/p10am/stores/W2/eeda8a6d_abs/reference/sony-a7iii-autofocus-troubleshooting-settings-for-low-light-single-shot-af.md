Troubleshooting Sony A7III autofocus in low-light indoor conditions with 100-400mm lens:

Settings to try:
- AF Micro Adjustment: Camera Settings > AF2 > AF Micro Adj., set to On
- AF Drive Speed: Set to Fast or Faster
- AF Area: Use Zone or Flexible Spot instead of Wide
- AF Illuminator: Camera Settings > AF2, set to On

Additional steps:
- Clean lens and sensor thoroughly
- Update camera firmware to latest version
- Verify subject is beyond lens minimum focus distance
- Try increasing ISO to assist autofocus in dim light
- Consider using remote shutter release to minimize camera shake
