**Key treaties:**

1. **Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT)** - Prevents the spread of nuclear weapons
2. **Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty (START)** - Reduces strategic nuclear arsenals
3. **Anti-Ballistic Missile Treaty (ABM Treaty)** - Limits missile defense systems
4. **Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty (CTBT)** - Bans all nuclear explosions
5. **Intermediate-Range Nuclear Forces Treaty (INF Treaty)** - Eliminates medium-range missiles
6. **Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC)** - Prohibits chemical weapons
7. **Biological Weapons Convention (BWC)** - Prohibits biological weapons
8. **Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW)** - Comprehensive nuclear ban
9. **New Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty (New START)** - Reduces deployed nuclear warheads
10. **Treaty on Open Skies** - Permits aerial surveillance for transparency

These treaties aim to prevent nuclear proliferation, reduce the number of nuclear weapons, limit certain weapon types, and promote transparency and confidence-building measures among nations.
