Popular Swedish desserts to explore in Stockholm:\n- Kladdkaka: Rich chocolate cake at Vete-Katten or Bageri Petrus\n- Pannkakor: Thin pancakes with whipped cream and lingonberry jam (Pom & Flora, Café Pascal)\n- Semla: Traditional pastry with almond paste and whipped cream, eaten around Lent\n- Chokladbollar: Chocolate and coconut balls (Drop Coffee, Kafé Esaias)\n- Marängsviss: Chocolate sauce, whipped cream, meringue (Pelikan, Tennstopet)\n- Lingonberry pie: Tart berry pie (Riche, Operakällaren)
