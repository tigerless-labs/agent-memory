**Price Ranges (as of 2023):**

The Cosmopolitan:
- Deluxe Room: $150-300/night
- City View: $200-400/night  
- Strip View: $300-600/night
- Suites: $500-1500/night

Bellagio:
- Deluxe Room: $180-350/night
- City View: $250-450/night
- Strip View: $350-700/night
- Fountain View: $400-1000/night
- Suites: $600-2000/night

Peak season (Fri-Sun, holidays, summer): 20-50% higher
Off-peak (Mon-Thu, winter): 10-30% lower

**Fitness Centers:**
- The Cosmopolitan: 2000 sq ft, 24/7, personal training, group classes
- Bellagio: 6000 sq ft, 24/7, personal training, group classes  
- Wynn Las Vegas: 6000 sq ft, 5am-8pm (Mon-Thu), 5am-9pm (Fri-Sun)
- Encore at Wynn: 5000 sq ft, similar hours

**Key Features:**
- The Cosmopolitan: rooftop pool, Marquee dayclub/nightclub, Qua Baths & Spa
- Bellagio: Mediterranean pool complex, Canyon Ranch SpaClub, Fountains of Bellagio
- Wynn: private golf course, luxury pool, high-end shopping esplanade
- Encore: XS nightclub, luxury pool, shopping
