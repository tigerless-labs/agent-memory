Based on user's interest in The 1975, Arctic Monkeys, and The Neighbourhood:

First round: Pale Waves, Imagine Dragons, Panic! At The Disco, The Killers, Twenty One Pilots, Bleachers, Walk The Moon, COIN

Second round for indie/alternative rock specifically: The Black Keys, The Strokes, Interpol, The Vaccines, Foals, Glass Animals, Cage the Elephant

Third round focused on crowd interaction and mixed setlists: The Libertines, Kings of Leon, The Wombats, The Xcerts, Catfish and the Bottlemen

All recommended for their high-energy live shows, crowd interaction, and mix of old and new material.
