During the Victorian era, Worcester Royal Porcelain had significant international export presence:

**Primary markets:**
- **Europe**: France, Germany, Italy - aristocrats and wealthy merchants
- **North America**: Significant US market; highly sought by wealthy Americans emulating European luxury; exported dinner services, vases, figurines
- **Asia**: India (valued by British colonial elite) and China (treaty ports including Canton and Shanghai); exported fine bone china and ornamental pieces
- **Other regions**: Australasia, Africa, South America facilitated by British trading companies

**Marketing strategies:**
- Participated in international exhibitions (Great Exhibition 1851, Paris Exposition 1855)
- Maintained network of agents and distributors in key markets
- Produced catalogs and promotional materials
