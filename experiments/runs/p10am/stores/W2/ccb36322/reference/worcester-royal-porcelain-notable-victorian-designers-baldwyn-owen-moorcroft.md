Notable Worcester Royal Porcelain designers and artists from the Victorian era:

- **Charles Baldwyn (1821-1898)**: Known for Japanese-inspired designs and Aesthetic Movement pieces
- **George Owen (1845-1914)**: Designed Renaissance Revival and Moorcroft-style vases; skilled potter and modeler known for pieces with raised pomegranates
- **William Moorcroft (1855-1917)**: Renowned ceramic artist who worked at Worcester; developed the Moorcroft-style featuring intricate hand-painted designs in blue, green, and yellow with distinctive "flambe" glaze

These artisans were part of Worcester's hierarchical workshop system with master craftsmen overseeing apprentices and specialists.
