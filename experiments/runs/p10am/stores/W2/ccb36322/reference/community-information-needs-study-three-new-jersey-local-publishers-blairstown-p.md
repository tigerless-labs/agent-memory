## Study overview
Research conducted with three New Jersey communities to assess information needs of local news audiences:
- **Ridge View Echo** in greater Blairstown
- Paterson
- Trenton

## Key findings

**Common across all three communities:**
- Desire for 'service journalism': municipal meeting locations/times, service provider directories, local leader contact info
- Heavy reliance on Facebook for news and community information
- Want more feel-good, solutions-oriented local news

**Paterson and Trenton specifically:**
- Demand for more local crime and safety news

**Paterson and Blairstown specifically:**
- Could grow audience with multilingual content (languages other than English)

## Recommended improvements

- Keep up-to-date, reliable service directories and contact lists
- Increase publisher visibility on Facebook as news breaks there first
- Expand language options for content

## Lessons learned

- Difficult to 'parachute' into communities and expect robust research response
- Benefit from extended face time in communities and creative outreach
- Early-stage audience research provides foundation for iterative feedback loop
- Recommendation: conduct similar research in 6-12 months with another round of improvements

## Research notes

- Publishers deeply rooted in communities helped with participant recruitment
- Publishers were appreciative of understanding their audiences at formative stage
- Success depends on continuous connection and iterative improvement based on audience feedback
