Beginner harmonica recommendations: Hohner Special 20, Lee Oskar, Suzuki (diatonic); Hohner Super Chromonica, Suzuki Chromatic, Huang (chromatic); Seydel, Bushman (other). Price range: 20 to 50 dollars. Consider material, reed quality, tuning, comfort. Plastic models are more affordable and durable. Metal models offer brighter tone.
