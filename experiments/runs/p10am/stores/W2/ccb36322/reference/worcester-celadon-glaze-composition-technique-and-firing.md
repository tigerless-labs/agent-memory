Worcester Royal Porcelain's distinctive celadon glaze developed in mid-19th century to evoke coveted Chinese celadon wares.

**Composition:**
- Small amounts of cobalt oxide (rare and expensive) for blue color
- Feldspathic glaze made from feldspar, quartz, and kaolin

**Application:**
- Brushing, pouring, and spraying techniques developed by artisans
- Often featured subtle crackle pattern

**Firing:**
- Relatively low temperature (~1200°C)
- Critical to developing characteristic crackle effect

**Characteristics:**
- Soft pale blue with slight greenish or greyish undertone
- Subtle crackle pattern for depth and texture
- Matte or semi-matte finish
- Delicate, translucent quality

The crackle was both natural (thermal expansion/contraction) and intentionally enhanced through glaze composition and firing control.
