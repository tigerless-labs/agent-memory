Currently experimenting with pre-mixed glazes on earthenware pieces. Goal is to transition to creating own glaze recipes from scratch. Interested in achieving specific colors (subtle blues, vibrant reds) and effects (textured matte finishes) and ensuring food-safe finishes. Recommended resources: The Potter's Bible by Marylin Scott, Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter by Ian Gregory, Craftsy, Ceramic Arts Network, The Ceramic School.
