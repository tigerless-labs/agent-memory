Trip details:
- Location: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp, Yosemite
- Duration: 3-4 days
- Distance: approximately 20 miles
- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet
- Difficulty: moderate
- Access: 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows or shuttle bus from visitor center
- Camp elevation: 10,300 feet

Accommodations:
- Four-person tent cabins with wooden floors and canvas walls
- Bunk beds with mattresses and pillows
- Full meal service (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Modern restrooms with flush toilets and showers
- Potable water and dishwashing station
- Camp store with limited snacks

Permits: Required through Yosemite National Park; book early through Yosemite Conservancy

Context: Chosen after reassessing fitness level. Initially considered High Sierra Camp Loop (40 miles, 7,000 ft elevation gain) but decided this moderate route better matched current preparedness.
