**Tilden Regional Park (Berkeley Hills)**
- Nimitz Way Trail: 4.5 miles out-and-back (9 miles round-trip), 1,000 ft elevation gain, moderate, 3-4 hours. Graded trail through forests and grasslands with Bay Area and Golden Gate Bridge views.
- Wildcat Gorge Trail: 5.5 miles out-and-back (11 miles round-trip), 1,200 ft elevation gain, moderate-challenging, 4-5 hours. Shaded canyon with Wildcat Creek, stream crossings, ridge views.

**Other Bay Area options considered:**
- Muir Woods and Dipsea Trail (redwoods, steep ravine)
- Mount Diablo State Park (3,849 ft summit, 360-degree views, 4-10 mile options)
- Sam McDonald County Park (Santa Cruz Mountains, Heritage Grove redwood trail 4.5 mi)
- Purisima Creek Redwoods (San Mateo, 4.5-6.5 mile routes)
- Mission Peak Regional Preserve (Fremont, challenging 6.5 mile out-and-back)
