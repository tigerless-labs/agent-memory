Festivals: Timkat (Jan 19), Meskel (Sep 27), Enkutatash (Sep 11), Fasika (Apr). Dishes: Injera, Doro Wot, Tibs, Kitfo, Shiro, coffee ceremony. Sites: Lalibela, Axum, Addis Ababa. Ethiopia has 3000+ years history, strong Orthodox traditions, unique cuisine and coffee culture.
