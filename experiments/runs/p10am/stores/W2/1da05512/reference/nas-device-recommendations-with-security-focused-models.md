High-end (8+ bays): Synology DS1819+, QNAP TS-1685, Netgear ReadyNAS RN628X. Mid-range (4-bay): Synology DS418play, QNAP TS-453D, Asustor AS6404T. Budget (2-bay): Western Digital My Cloud EX2 Ultra, Seagate Personal Cloud. All support AES-256 encryption. Priority features: 2FA, granular access control, regular updates. Compatible with laptop (Time Machine/Backup & Restore), phone apps (DS file/Qfile), cloud service sync.
