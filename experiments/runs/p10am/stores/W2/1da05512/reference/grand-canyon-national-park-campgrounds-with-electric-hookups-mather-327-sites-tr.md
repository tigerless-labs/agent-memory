Mather Campground (South Rim): 327 sites with hookups, flush toilets, showers, laundry; recreation.gov

Trailer Village RV Park (South Rim): 84 sites with hookups, Wi-Fi, laundry, camp store; grandcanyonlodges.com

Desert View Campground (South Rim): 50 sites with hookups, flush toilets, potable water; recreation.gov

Critical: Fill up quickly during peak season—reserve well in advance.
