Apps for carbon offsetting: Carbon Footprint, Offset, EcoPassenger. Platforms for sustainable accommodations: Ecobnb, BookDifferent, Eco Hotels of the World, Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, Glamping.com. Airlines with carbon offset programs: Qantas, Air New Zealand, KLM, Lufthansa, SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United. Transportation apps: Citymapper, Moovit, Waze Carpool, Rome2rio, Google Trips. Zero-waste apps: Refill, iRecycle, Zero Waste Travel, HappyCow, EcoChallenge, Susty. Gear recommendations: reusable water bottles, beeswax wraps, rechargeable power banks, solid toiletries, cloth towels, merino wool, solar chargers, reusable straws, reef-safe sunscreen. Certifications to look for: LEED, EarthCheck, Bluesign, GOTS, OEKO-TEX.
