Folk horror and atmospheric horror films with elements of paganism, ritual, unease, and slow-burn tension:

- The Devil's Backbone (2001)
- Kill List (2011)
- A Dark Song (2016)
- November (2017)
- Apostle (2018)
- The Sacrament (2013)
- The Invitation (2015)
- Martha Marcy May Marlene (2011)
- The Blackcoat's Daughter (2015)
- The Autopsy of Jane Doe (2016)

Note: The Wicker Man (1973) is also folk horror but user has already seen it.
