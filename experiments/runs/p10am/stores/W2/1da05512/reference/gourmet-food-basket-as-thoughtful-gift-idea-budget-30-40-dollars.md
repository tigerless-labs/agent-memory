Gift basket can include: savory items (artisanal cheeses, cured meats, nuts, crackers, olives, antipasto), sweet treats (gourmet chocolates, dried fruits, cookies, biscotti), spreads (artisanal jams, honey, maple syrup, chutneys), beverages (specialty tea, coffee, wine or champagne optional). Can be personalized with customized serving items like cheese knives or cutting boards. Consider friend preferences and theme around specific cuisine.
