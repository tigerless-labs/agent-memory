**Routes evaluated for September 2023 trip:**

**High Sierra Camp Loop** (40 miles, 7,000 ft elevation) — CONSIDERED BUT NOT CHOSEN
- Difficulty: moderate-challenging
- Terrain: Rocky/uneven, stream crossings, possible snow/ice on passes
- Route: Tuolumne Meadows → Glen Aulin → May Lake → Vogelsang → return to Tuolumne Meadows
- Permits: Yosemite National Park
- Decision: Assessed as beyond current fitness level

**Rae Lakes Loop** (45 miles, 6,000 ft elevation) — MODERATE
- Route: Road's End → Rae Lakes → Glen Pass → Paradise Valley → return
- Permits: Kings Canyon National Park
- Features: Rae Lakes, Fin Dome, Kings River Canyon views

**Ansel Adams Wilderness – Thousand Island Lake** (30 miles, 4,000 ft elevation) — MODERATE
- Trailhead: Agnew Meadows
- Features: Thousand Island Lake, Minarets, Ritter Range views
- Permits: Ansel Adams Wilderness

**Emigrant Wilderness – Emigrant Lake** (35 miles, 3,500 ft elevation) — MODERATE
- Trailhead: Crabtree
- Features: Secluded, lesser-visited option with surrounding lakes and peaks

**Vogelsang High Sierra Camp** (20 miles, 3,000 ft elevation) — CHOSEN
- Difficulty: moderate (good introduction to high-elevation backpacking)
- Features: Full camp amenities, tent cabins, meal service, established infrastructure
- Permits: Yosemite National Park
- Reason for selection: Best balance of scenic alpine experience with manageable difficulty and camp support
