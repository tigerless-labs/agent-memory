Cloud Gate (also called The Bean) is a large-scale stainless steel sculpture by Anish Kapoor installed in Millennium Park, Chicago in 2004. Composed of 168 stainless steel panels, weighs over 110 tons. Dimensions: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long. Features mirror-like polished surface that reflects and distorts surrounding environment. Interactive public sculpture - visitors can walk around it and take photos with their reflections.
