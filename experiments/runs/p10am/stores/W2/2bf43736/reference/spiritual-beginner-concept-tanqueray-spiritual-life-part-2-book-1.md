Spiritual beginner: someone newly begun spiritual journey in early purgative way. Characterized by desire to please God and recognition of need for detachment from sin and worldly things. May struggle with temptations, weaknesses, lack of knowledge. Tanqueray's guidance: emphasize humility and obedience; seek spiritual director/mentor; practice mental prayer, spiritual reading, meditation; persevere patiently with God's grace.
