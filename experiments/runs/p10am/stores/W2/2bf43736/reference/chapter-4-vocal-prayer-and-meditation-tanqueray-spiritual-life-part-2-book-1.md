Prayer of beginners focuses on vocal prayer and meditation. Vocal prayer (Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be) provides structure, focuses mind/heart on God. Requires sincerity and devotion. Meditation is more advanced: reflection on God's truth, requires concentration. Beginners can engage in simple forms. Recommendations: set specific prayer times, use prayer books, practice spiritual reading to deepen understanding and provide prayer inspiration.
