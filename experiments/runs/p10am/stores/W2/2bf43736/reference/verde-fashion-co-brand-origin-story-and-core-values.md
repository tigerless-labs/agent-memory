**Origin story:** Brand inspired by grandmother's handmade dress discovered in attic - representing quality, craftsmanship, and timelessness of previous generation. This sparked realization about fast fashion's environmental and social devastation.

**Core mission:** Merge passion for fashion with commitment to sustainability. Create beautiful, high-quality clothing that respects planet and resources.

**Sustainable practices:**
- Eco-friendly materials: organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel
- Waste reduction: pattern-making optimization, fabric scrap recycling  
- Supply chain transparency and fair labor practices
- Focus on durability and timelessness to encourage buying less and wearing more
- Making sustainable fashion accessible and stylish

**Philosophy:** Sustainability is a way of life, not just a buzzword. About slowing down, being intentional, making choices for healthier, more equitable world.
