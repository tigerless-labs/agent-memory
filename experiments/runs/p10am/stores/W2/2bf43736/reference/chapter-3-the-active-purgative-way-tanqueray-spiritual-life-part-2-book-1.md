First step of purgative way where soul actively detaches from sin and worldly things. Covers mortification, cultivation of virtues, avoidance of occasions of sin. Mental prayer, spiritual reading, and meditation deepen God relationship. Sacraments (confession, Eucharist) provide grace and strength. Emphasizes humility and trust in God's providence. Process is difficult and challenging.
