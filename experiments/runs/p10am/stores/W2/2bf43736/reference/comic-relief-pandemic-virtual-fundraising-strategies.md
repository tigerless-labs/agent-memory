Adapted during COVID-19 by:

1. **Digital strategy**: Live-streamed events and remote challenges
2. **Partnerships**: Celebrity and influencer engagement
3. **Content**: High-quality shareable videos and social media posts  
4. **Community**: Supporter-generated content with branded hashtags
5. **Donation systems**: Easy, secure online payment platforms
6. **Communication**: Regular supporter updates on campaign progress
7. **Mission focus**: Prioritized funding for disadvantaged communities

Demonstrated agility as a nonprofit model for innovation during disruption.
