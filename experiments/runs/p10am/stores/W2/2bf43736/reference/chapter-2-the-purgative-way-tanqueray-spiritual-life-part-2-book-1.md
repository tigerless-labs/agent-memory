First stage of spiritual life. Soul is purified of attachment to sin and worldly things through mortification, self-denial, and detachment. Emphasizes cardinal virtues: prudence, justice, temperance, fortitude. Prayer and sacraments (confession, Eucharist) are essential. Draws on St. John of the Cross and St. Teresa of Avila. Perseverance required through long, difficult process.
