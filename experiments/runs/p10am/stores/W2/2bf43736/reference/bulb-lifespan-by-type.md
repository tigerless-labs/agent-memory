Expected lifespans for different bulb types:\n- Incandescent bulbs: 1,000 to 2,000 hours\n- Halogen bulbs: 2,000 to 4,000 hours\n- Compact Fluorescent Lamps (CFLs): 8,000 to 10,000 hours\n- LED bulbs: 15,000 to 25,000 hours or more\n\nLED bulbs are the most energy-efficient and longest-lasting option.
