Room shape: rectangular, 1.5 to 2x width ratio preferred\nSound treatment: acoustic panels on walls, ceiling, corners\nBass traps: place in corners to absorb low frequencies\nFlooring: area rugs to reduce echo (hardwood/tile can be reflective)\nCeiling height: 10-12 feet ideal\nWindows: position away from listening area to minimize reflections
