**Primary platforms:** Instagram and Facebook

**Instagram focus:**
- Sustainability series highlighting eco-friendly practices
- Behind-the-scenes content (design process, material sourcing, production)
- Customer showcase and user-generated content campaigns
- Educational content on sustainable fashion practices
- Stories and IGTV for interactive, in-depth experiences
- Consistent aesthetic, high-quality visuals, relevant hashtags
- Regular posting for brand identity

**Facebook focus:**
- Longer-form content (blog posts, videos, educational pieces)
- Promotions, sales announcements, new product launches
- Event promotion and Facebook Events for webinars/workshops
- Facebook Live sessions for real-time connection
- Community building and group engagement
- Mobile-optimized content

**Cross-promotion:** Share Instagram content on Facebook and use Facebook to drive Instagram traffic.
