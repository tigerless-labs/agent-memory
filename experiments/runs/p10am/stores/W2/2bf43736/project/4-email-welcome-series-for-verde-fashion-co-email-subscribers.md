Welcome series for new email subscribers:

1. **Email 1** (immediate): Introduction and Brand Story - personal story about grandmother's dress, mission to counter fast fashion, warm welcome
2. **Email 2** (3-4 days later): Our Sustainable Approach - eco-friendly materials (organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel), waste reduction, supply chain transparency  
3. **Email 3** (7-10 days later): Featured Products and Collections - showcase 2-3 best-selling items with sustainable features, customer testimonials
4. **Email 4** (10-14 days later): Exclusive Offer and Community Invitation - first-purchase discount (15% suggested), social media follow invitation, product sneak peek

Each email has one main focus with clear call-to-action. Keep concise and visually appealing.
