Currently doing strength training three times per week to improve overall fitness and support running performance. Routine focuses on lower body and core strength:

**Monday:** Squats, lunges, calf raises, plank
**Wednesday:** Deadlifts, step-ups, leg press, Russian twists
**Friday:** Glute bridges, leg extensions, leg curls, side plank

Goals include injury prevention, improved running efficiency, and increased power for cycling and running.
