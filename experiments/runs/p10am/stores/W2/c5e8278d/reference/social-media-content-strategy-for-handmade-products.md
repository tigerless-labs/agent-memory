Key content types for selling candles and soaps on Facebook and Instagram:

- Product showcases: High-quality photos from multiple angles
- Behind-the-scenes content: Making process, pouring, packaging
- Customer testimonials and user-generated content: Build social proof
- Lifestyle shots: Products in use or in home settings
- Hashtag strategy: Branded hashtag plus 5-10 relevant hashtags per post
- Engagement tactics: Calls-to-action, questions, contests, exclusive offers
- Tools: Planoly or Later for Instagram scheduling

Key principles: Consistent aesthetic, conversational tone, mobile-friendly captions, quick response to comments and messages.
