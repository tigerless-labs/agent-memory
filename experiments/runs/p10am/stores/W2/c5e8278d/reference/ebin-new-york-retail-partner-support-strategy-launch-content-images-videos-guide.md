Launch Phase Support:
- Product images and videos for web/social/in-store use
- How-to guides (in-store signage, product packaging)
- Clear product descriptions (labels, website, in-store signage)

Promotion Phase Support:
- Social media content: images, videos, targeted ads
- Email marketing campaigns with product info and purchase links
- In-store promotional signage highlighting features and benefits

Measurement Metrics:
- Sales: Before/during/after campaign comparison
- Social Engagement: Likes, shares, comments on content
- Email Performance: Open rates and click-through rates to partner sites/stores
- Retail Impact: Sales lift, foot traffic increase, brand awareness building
