Songs: "I'm Yours" (Mraz), "Ho Hey" (Lumineers), "Riptide" (Vance Joy), "Here Comes the Sun" (Beatles), "Sunny Afternoon" (The Kinks)

Albums: "Ambient 1: Music for Airports" (Eno), "Sleep" (Richter)
