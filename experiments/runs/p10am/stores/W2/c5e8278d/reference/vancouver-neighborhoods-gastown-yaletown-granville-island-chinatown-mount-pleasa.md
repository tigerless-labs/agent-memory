Popular neighborhoods to explore before classes start.

**Gastown** - Oldest neighborhood with cobblestone streets, independent boutiques, trendy restaurants, Steam Clock. Transit: SkyTrain Waterfront or bus #4, #7

**Yaletown** - Upscale shops, restaurants, bars, Seawall waterfront. Transit: SkyTrain Yaletown-Roundhouse or bus #6, #17

**Granville Island** - Public market, artisan shops, waterfront restaurants, street performers. Transit: SkyTrain Granville + bus #50, or ferry from False Creek

**Chinatown** - Historic Chinatown with food and shops, Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden. Transit: SkyTrain Stadium-Chinatown or bus #3, #8

**Mount Pleasant** - Vintage shops, craft breweries, street art, Vancouver Flea Market. Transit: SkyTrain Main Street-Science World or bus #3, #9

**Commercial Drive** - Independent shops, cafes, Rio Theatre. Transit: SkyTrain Commercial-Broadway or bus #9, #20

**Kitsilano** - Beachy vibe, Kitsilano Beach, Vanier Park, outdoor pool. Transit: SkyTrain Granville + bus #4
