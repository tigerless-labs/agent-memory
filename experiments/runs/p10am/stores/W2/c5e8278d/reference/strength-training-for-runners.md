Aim for 2-3 strength sessions per week on non-consecutive days.

Key muscle groups: Glutes (side lunges, clam shells, glute bridges, leg press, step-ups), Hip Flexors (step-ups, lunges, leg press), Core (planks, side planks, Russian twists).

Sample exercises: Squats 3x8-12, Lunges 3x8-12 per leg, Deadlifts 3x8-12, Calf raises 3x12-15, Clam shells 3x12-15, Leg press 3x8-12, Planks 3x30-60sec, Russian twists 3x12-15.
