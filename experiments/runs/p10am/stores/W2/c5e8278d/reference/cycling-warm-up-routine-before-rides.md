Duration: 15-30 minutes total

1. Light cardio (5-10 min): Easy pedaling to gradually raise heart rate
2. Dynamic stretching (5-10 min): Leg swings forward/backward, hip circles both directions, knee lifts, ankle rotations
3. Muscle activation (5-10 min): Glute bridges, calf raises, core engagement
4. Final spin (5-10 min): Gradually increase to moderate pace, focus on pedaling technique and cadence

This prepares muscles, heart rate, and mind for comfortable and effective riding.
