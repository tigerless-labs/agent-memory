Perform 2-3 sets each, 30-60 seconds per area. Apply moderate pressure, roll slowly and smoothly.

IT Band Roll: Lie on side with foam roller under leg above knee. Roll toward ankle, focusing on outside leg above knee.

Lateral Leg Roll: Lie on side with foam roller under leg above ankle. Roll toward knee, focusing on outside leg.

Hip Flexor Roll: Kneel on all fours with foam roller under hip flexor (front of hip). Roll forward/backward, focusing on hip flexor to quad junction.

Piriformis Roll: Sit on floor with foam roller under glutes. Cross one leg over other, roll under glutes focusing on piriformis muscle.

Tips: Start light and increase pressure gradually. Stop immediately if experiencing pain. Breathe deeply and relax muscles.
