Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- Sales: Track new product sales during campaign vs. baseline period
- Foot Traffic: Count store visitors during campaign period  
- Customer Engagement: Track product trials, questions asked, interactions

Measurement Process:
1. Baseline: Establish pre-campaign measurements for sales, foot traffic, engagement
2. Campaign Implementation: Execute with in-store signage, demos, samples
3. Analysis: Compare campaign period results to baseline
4. Evaluation: Assess effectiveness and adjust tactics (messaging, demos, promotions, targeting)

Campaign Tactics:
- Eye-catching in-store signage
- Staff product demonstrations
- Free product samples
- Personalized promotions by customer segment
