### Local Business Support Programs in Hull

1. **Business Support Programs** — Mentorship, training, and funding opportunities provided to help local businesses compete with larger chains and sustain themselves.

2. **Start-Up Support** — Funding, space, and resources made available for new entrepreneurs to establish themselves in the local market.

3. **Local Procurement Policy** — Public sector organizations encouraged to purchase goods and services from local businesses wherever possible. Helps keep money circulating in local economy.

4. **Grant Schemes** — Financial assistance available to small businesses meeting certain criteria, such as hiring local employees or operating sustainably.
