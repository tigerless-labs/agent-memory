### Long-Term Affordable Housing Protections in Hull

1. **Rent Caps** — Limits set on landlord charges for affordable housing. Rents kept significantly lower than market rate to ensure access for low-income earners.

2. **Long-Term Contracts** — Affordable homes held as leasehold with long-term contracts to prevent excessive future rent increases and maintain affordability.

3. **Quality Standards** — Minimum standards enforced for insulation, heating, and general living conditions. Ensures affordable homes remain habitable and meet basic needs.

4. **Monitoring & Enforcement** — Local government oversight to ensure landlord compliance with regulations and prevent excessive charges.
