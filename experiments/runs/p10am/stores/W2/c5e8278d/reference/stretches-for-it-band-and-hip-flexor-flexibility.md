Hold each stretch 15-30 seconds, 2-3 repetitions per side.

IT Band Stretch: Stand with affected side to wall. Cross opposite leg over affected leg at ankle toward knee. Bend knee and lean toward wall.

Hip Flexor Stretch (Kneeling): Kneel on all fours, bring one knee forward with foot flat. Lean forward to stretch front of hip.

Hip Flexor Stretch (Lying): Lie on back with legs straight. Lift affected leg toward ceiling keeping knee straight. Hold and repeat.

Lateral Leg Stretch: Stand feet shoulder-width apart. Step large to one side, bend front knee, lower body stretching outside of leg.

Piriformis Stretch: Sit on floor with affected leg crossed over other. Pull knee toward opposite shoulder. Feel stretch in back of leg.

Pigeon Pose: Kneel on all fours. Place one foot forward on opposite side of body. Lean forward over bent leg.

Additional: Leg swings forward/backward, hip circles, dynamic stretching before rides.
