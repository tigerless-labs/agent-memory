## Workouts for 5K Speed

- **Fartlek Intervals**: 20-30 min alternating 1-2 min fast pace with 2-3 min easy pace
- **400m Repeats**: 3-5 sets of 400m fast pace with 200m recovery jog
- **800m Repeats**: 2-3 sets of 800m fast pace with 400m recovery jog
- **Hill Repeats**: 3-5 sets of uphill sprints with downhill jog recovery
- **Pyramid Intervals**: Build up from 200m to 600m then back down
- **Proportional Intervals**: 1 min hard/2 min easy, then 2 min hard/4 min easy, then 3 min hard/6 min easy

All workouts should include 10-15 min warm-up and cool-down.
