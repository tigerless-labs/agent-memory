Top e-commerce platforms for selling handmade products:

- Etsy: Marketplace focused on handmade and vintage items
- Shopify: User-friendly platform with templates and integrations  
- WooCommerce: WordPress plugin that turns sites into online stores
- Big Cartel: Simple platform focused on creative entrepreneurs

Good for next phase after establishing social media presence.
