Popular attractions and activities to explore.

**Must-see attractions:**
- Stanley Park: 1,000-acre park with trails, beaches, city/mountain views
- Capilano Suspension Bridge Park: Scenic park with bridges, cliff walks, forest views
- Grouse Mountain: Winter skiing, summer hiking, wildlife refuge
- Museum of Anthropology: Indigenous art and culture
- Vancouver Aquarium: 50,000+ marine animals
- VanDusen Botanical Garden: 55-acre garden with 7,500+ plant species
- English Bay: Popular beach with city/mountain views

**Activities:**
- Walking tours of Gastown, Yaletown, Chinatown
- Hiking: Lynn Canyon, Cypress Falls, Quarry Rock
- Cycling: Seawall, Central Valley Greenway
- Local markets, breweries, wineries
- Canucks hockey or Whitecaps soccer
- Street art in Mount Pleasant
- Day trips to Bowen Island, Squamish, Whistler
