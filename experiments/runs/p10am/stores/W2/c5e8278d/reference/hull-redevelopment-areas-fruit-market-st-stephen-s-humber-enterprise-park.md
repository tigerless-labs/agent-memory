### Hull Redevelopment Areas

1. **Fruit Market** — Transformed into cultural, residential, and commercial hub with modern apartments, galleries, bars, restaurants. Attracted young professionals and students; created new job opportunities.

2. **St. Stephen's** — Large shopping center built on site of former Hull Royal Infirmary. Created retail, food, entertainment opportunities; generated jobs and boosted local economy.

3. **Humber Enterprise Park** — Large industrial estate on outskirts; converted former industrial buildings into modern office spaces for business establishment and job creation.

### Mixed Impacts
- New investment, job opportunities, increased property values
- Displacement of long-standing residents
- Rising house prices limiting affordability
- Complaints about homogenization and negative impact on small local businesses
