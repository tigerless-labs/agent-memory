### Government Mitigation Measures for Displacement

1. **Affordable Housing Requirement** — All new developments required to include percentage of affordable homes to keep displaced residents able to remain in area.

2. **Tenancy Protection** — Long-term residents in social housing offered special protection against eviction due to regeneration projects.

3. **Community Engagement** — Local government involves community in planning and decision-making for regeneration projects to address resident concerns.

4. **Compulsory Purchase Orders** — Enables regeneration to proceed by acquiring land/property from unwilling sellers; property owners compensated for loss.
