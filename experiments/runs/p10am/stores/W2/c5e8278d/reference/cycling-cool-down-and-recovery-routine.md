Duration: 15-30 minutes post-ride

1. Easy pedaling (5-10 min): Gradually bring heart rate down with light spinning
2. Static stretching (10-15 min): Quad stretch, hamstring stretch, hip flexor stretch, calf stretch - hold each 15-30 sec
3. Foam rolling (5-10 min): Focus on tight/sore areas like IT band, quads, hamstrings - roll slowly with moderate pressure
4. Hydration and refueling (10-15 min): Drink water/sports drink, consume carbs+protein within 30-60 min

Additional recovery: warm bath/shower, compression garments, massage, adequate rest.
