Weather patterns and packing guidance for Vancouver.

**August (Summer)**
- Daytime: 23-25°C (73-77°F)
- Nights: 13-15°C (56-59°F), pack layers for cool evenings
- Sunshine: ~9 hours/day
- Humidity: ~64%

**September (Early Fall)**
- Daytime: 20-22°C (68-72°F)
- Nights: 10-12°C (50-54°F)
- Sunshine: ~7 hours/day
- Rainfall: ~12 rainy days; light to moderate
- Beginning of wet season

**Winter (Dec-Feb)**
- Temperatures: 3-8°C (37-46°F)
- Rainfall: 12-15 rainy days per month
- Snowfall: Rare in city, possible in nearby mountains
- Weather unpredictable; use weather app for forecasts

**Packing essentials:**
- Waterproof jacket (Gore-Tex/eVent) with hood
- Insulated or fleece jacket, water-resistant
- Warm sweaters and thermal layers
- Waterproof pants for outdoor activities
- Waterproof boots with good grip
- Gloves, hat, scarf (wool, fleece, synthetics)
- Moisture-wicking socks
- Comfortable walking shoes

**Tips:** Invest in quality water-resistant materials; layering key for temperature changes; breathable fabrics prevent overheating. Vancouver winters milder than other Canadian cities. Check thrift stores for deals on gear.
