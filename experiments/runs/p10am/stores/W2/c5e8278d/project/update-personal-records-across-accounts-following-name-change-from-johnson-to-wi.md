Following recent name change from Johnson to Winters (May 2023), systematically update personal information across:

**Financial & Government:**
- Health insurance provider (update address + name)
- DMV/Driver's license (state requirement)
- Credit card companies (request new cards with updated name)
- Credit bureaus: Experian (866-200-6020), TransUnion (800-680-7289), Equifax (800-685-1111)

**Social Media:**
- Facebook (preserve friends and memories)
- Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn

**Timeline:**
- Phone/online updates: 1-3 business days typically
- Mail/fax updates: 5-7 business days
- Credit bureau updates: may require documentation

**Notes:**
- May need certified marriage certificate or court order as proof
- Consider backing up Facebook data before changes
- Maintain consistent name/username across all profiles
- Monitor credit reports after updates
- Update automatic payment information where applicable
