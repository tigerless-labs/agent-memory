Fairview neighborhood details for accommodation search.

**Location:** South-central Vancouver
- Bounded by: Granville St (west), Cambie St (east), 16th Ave (south), Broadway (north)
- Walking distance to UBC and Emily Carr University
- Close to Langara College and Vancouver Community College

**Housing options:**
- Apartments, condos, houses, basement suites available
- Competitive rental market during peak season

**Amenities:**
- Restaurants/cafes: Diverse options (Asian, vegan, trendy)
- Groceries: Save-On-Foods, Safeway, Whole Foods Market
- Parks: Charleson Park (playground, tennis courts, community garden)
- Public transit: Well-connected with bus routes including B-Line rapid transit on Broadway
- Shopping: Independent boutiques, vintage shops, Canadian Tire, BC Liquor Store
- Safety: Generally safe with low crime rates
- Community: Strong sense of community with residents and growing student population; farmers markets, street festivals

**Nearby highlights:** Fairview Village, Granville Island (just across bridge), Fairview Slopes neighborhood

**Housing search tips:**
- Research thoroughly to find best fit for needs and budget
- Visit neighborhood in person before committing to rental
- Be prepared for competitive rental market, especially during peak season
