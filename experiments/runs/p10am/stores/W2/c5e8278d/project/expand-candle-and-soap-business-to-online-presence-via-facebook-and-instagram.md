Planning to transition from local market sales to online selling by:

1. Setting up Facebook and Instagram business pages
2. Creating product showcases and behind-the-scenes content
3. Creating a branded hashtag to encourage user-generated content
4. Adding calls-to-action to packaging and website
5. Running photo contests and giveaways to build engagement
6. Eventually collaborating with micro-influencers and complementary small businesses

Will start with social media before committing to a full e-commerce platform (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, etc.).
