Accommodations: Canyon Lodge & Cabins (heart of park, near Grand Canyon of Yellowstone), Grant Village (shores of Yellowstone Lake), Yellowstone Gateway Inn (West Yellowstone). Geysers: Old Faithful (iconic), Grand Geyser (Upper Basin, 7-10 hours, 200 feet), Castle Geyser (10-12 hours), Daisy Geyser (3-4 hours, 80 feet). Hot Springs: Mammoth Hot Springs (travertine, stroller-friendly), Norris Geyser Basin, Boiling River (near Mammoth). Wildlife: Lamar Valley (prime bear viewing), Slough Creek (grizzlies), Tower-Roosevelt Area, Mammoth area. Trails: Brink of Lower Falls (0.75 mi, easy), Seven Mile Hole (10 mi, challenging), South Rim (6 mi, easy). Keep 100+ yards from wildlife, use binoculars, visit dawn/dusk, book early for peak season.
