Believes governments and healthcare institutions should prioritize proactive measures to address healthcare inequalities in marginalized communities, rather than waiting for crises like COVID-19. Emphasizes that systemic change is needed to address root causes of health disparities, including poverty, discrimination, and lack of education. Frustrated with reactive, crisis-driven policy responses and advocates for addressing inequalities through sustained, coordinated efforts from multiple stakeholders.
