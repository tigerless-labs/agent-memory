### Ideas Journal\nKeep a running journal (notebook or app) to capture content ideas throughout the day. Review regularly to identify which ideas can be developed into live streams.\n\n### Repurposing Content\n- Take popular blog posts or videos and turn into live streams\n- Share behind-the-scenes looks at content creation\n- Host Q&A sessions based on popular topics\n\n### Platform Tailoring\n**Instagram Live:** 10-30 min, visually-focused, behind-the-scenes content, use polls/questions/quizzes, mobile-first (vertical format)\n\n**YouTube Live:** 30-60 min, in-depth content, high-quality production, evergreen content, SEO optimization with keywords/tags/descriptions\n\n**Facebook Live:** 30-90 min, community-focused, personal stories, promote events, use call-to-action buttons\n\n### Cross-promotion\n- Share snippets/teasers on other platforms to drive traffic\n- Use platform-specific features (Reels on Instagram, Super Chat on YouTube)\n- Maintain consistent tone/style/schedule across all platforms
