Loves *The Grand Budapest Hotel* for its use of color grading to create mood and atmosphere. Particularly enjoyed the candy-colored scenes in the hotel for their playful and whimsical tone, and the contrasting darker/desaturated colors in tense scenes for the emotional depth they added. Interested in visual storytelling and how filmmakers use color to enhance narrative.
