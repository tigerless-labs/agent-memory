Observation from recent family reunion: Female cousins tend to initiate and dominate group conversations and decision-making processes. Male cousins appear more laid-back and less assertive in group settings. Gender ratio (17 female, 8 male cousins) may contribute to this dynamic, though individual personality differences could also play a significant role. Possible factors: females often socialized to be more communicative/empathetic; males may perceive group norms as shaped by female majority and feel less inclined to assert themselves.
