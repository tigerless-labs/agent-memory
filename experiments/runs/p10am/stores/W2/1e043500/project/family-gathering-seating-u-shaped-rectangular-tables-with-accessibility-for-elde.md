Seating layout for 25-30 people:\n- Main gathering: 2-3 rectangular tables (8-10 ft) arranged in U-shape or gentle curve\n- Seat elderly members at table ends near walls for easier access and support\n- 2-3 smaller rectangular tables (4-6 ft) around perimeter for smaller groups\n- Leave 3-4 ft clearance between tables and walls for movement\n- Pathways between tables should be 4-5 ft wide for comfortable passage\n- Reserve a few quiet chairs near entrance for anyone needing breaks\n- Plan for flexibility: adjust layout on gathering day based on actual attendance and needs
