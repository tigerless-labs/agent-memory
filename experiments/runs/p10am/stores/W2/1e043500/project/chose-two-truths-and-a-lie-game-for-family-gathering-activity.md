Selected 'Two Truths and a Lie' as the group activity for the family gathering. Game is easy to understand and good for learning about family members. Implementation plan: encourage people to prepare truths and lie in advance, set timer for each turn, emphasize fun over competition, consider small prize for most convincing lie.
