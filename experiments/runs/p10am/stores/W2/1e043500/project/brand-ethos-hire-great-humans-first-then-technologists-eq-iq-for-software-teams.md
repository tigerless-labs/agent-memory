## Brand Philosophy

Prioritize hiring great human beings first, then great technologists second. Specific emphasis on:
- Psychometric testing for critical thinking ability, not just coding skill
- Emotional Intelligence (EQ) valued higher than IQ for software developer hiring
- Team building culture focused on collaboration and communication

## Content Strategy

Planning multi-format content:
- Blog posts: explore importance of hiring humans first; identify key traits
- Podcasts/videos: interviews with industry experts on team building
- Webinars/workshops: practical tips for effective team hiring and building
- E-books/guides: in-depth on psychometric testing, soft skills, collaboration culture
- Social media: share content, case studies, team member profiles

## Target Positioning

Establish thought leadership in team building and hiring; position as expert in balancing human skills with technical ability.
