Tips: Choose music you're interested in, arrive early for good seat, dress formally, silence devices, listen attentively, embrace the emotions, applaud between movements, talk to musicians afterward. Prepare by: listening to pieces beforehand on Spotify/YouTube, reading about composers and historical context, familiarizing yourself with the program, arriving early, coming with an open mind.
