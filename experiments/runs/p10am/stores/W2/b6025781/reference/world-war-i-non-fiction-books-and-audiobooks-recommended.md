**Non-fiction Books:**
- 'The Guns of August' by Barbara W. Tuchman (Pulitzer Prize winner, detailed account of events leading to WWI and first month of war)
- 'A World Undone: The Story of the Great War, 1914-1918' by G.J. Meyer (comprehensive, well-researched)
- 'The First World War: A Complete History' by Martin Gilbert (thorough and accessible)
- 'Women in the War Zone: Hospital Service in France, 1914-1918' by Sharon Ouditt (explores experiences of women in WWI including nurses, ambulance drivers, support roles)

**Audiobooks (good for listening during runs):**
- 'The World Remade: America in World War I' by G.J. Meyer (narrated by Malcolm Hillgartner)
- 'World War I: The Great War' by S.L.A. Marshall (narrated by Grover Gardner)
- 'A World Undone: The Story of the Great War, 1914-1918' by G.J. Meyer (narrated by Simon Vance)

**Online Resources:**
- BBC World War I Centenary Website
- National World War I Museum and Memorial
- Imperial War Museums (UK-based)
- Library of Congress World War I Collection

**Documentaries:**
- 'The Great War' (PBS, 2017, 6-part series) - comprehensive history with stunning visuals and engaging storytelling
- 'World War I: The Centenary' (BBC, 2014, 6-part series) - explores causes, course, and consequences with historian interviews
