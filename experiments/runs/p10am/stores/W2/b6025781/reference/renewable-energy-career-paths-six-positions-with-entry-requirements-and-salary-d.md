**Six Career Paths in Renewable Energy:**

1. **Solar Design Engineer** - Bachelor's in mechanical or electrical engineering; further renewable energy training may be required
2. **Wind Turbine Technician** - Associate degree or technical training in wind energy technology; median salary $56,230
3. **Energy Efficiency Specialist** - Environmental science or engineering degree preferred
4. **Biomass Scientist** - Degree in biochemistry or chemical engineering
5. **Geothermal Engineer** - Mechanical or civil engineering degree; geology background helpful
6. **Hydropower Engineer** - Mechanical, civil, or electrical engineering

**Salary Comparisons vs Traditional Energy:**
- Wind Turbine Technician: $56,230 (renewable) vs Oil/Gas Rotary Drill Operator: $54,940 (traditional)
- Solar PV Installer: $44,890 (renewable) vs Natural Gas Plant Operator: $69,910 (traditional)

**Career Entry Strategy:**
- Obtain relevant degree or certification in engineering/science/technology
- Seek internships or apprenticeships with renewable energy companies
- Network with professionals in the industry
- Attend conferences and events
- Stay current with industry news and trends

**Industry Growth Outlook:**
- Renewable energy sector employed 11.5 million globally in 2019
- Expected continued growth and job creation
- Many renewable energy occupations projected to have faster than average growth rates
