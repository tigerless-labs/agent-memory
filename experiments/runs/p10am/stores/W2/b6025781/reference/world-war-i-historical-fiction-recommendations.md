**WWI Historical Fiction:**
- 'The Soldier's Wife' by Pam Jenoff (young German woman living in France during WWI, struggles to survive and resist occupation)
- 'Those Who Save Us' by Jenna Blum (mother-daughter story in Nazi-occupied Germany during WWII, explores post-WWI events including Treaty of Versailles and rise of fascism)
- 'The War Bride' by Pam Jenoff (American woman travels to Europe to marry British officer during WWI, caught up in war effort and British aristocratic intrigue)
- 'The Winter Guest' by Pam Jenoff (set in Poland during WWI, young woman embroiled in Polish resistance movement)
- 'The Poppy War' by R.F. Kuang (fantasy world inspired by Chinese history and WWI mythology, follows orphan in military academy who becomes embroiled in war between rival nations)

**Other Historical Fiction Also Recommended:**
- 'The Song of Achilles' by Madeline Miller (reimagines Trojan War from perspective of Achilles' lover)
- 'The Red Tent' by Anita Diamant (biblical story of Dinah, explores lives of women in ancient Mesopotamia)
- 'The Historian' by Elizabeth Kostova (sweeping novel spanning centuries, explores Dracula legend)
- 'The Lost Girls of Paris' by Pam Jenoff (WWII-based, follows woman discovering suitcase of photographs of women in resistance network)
