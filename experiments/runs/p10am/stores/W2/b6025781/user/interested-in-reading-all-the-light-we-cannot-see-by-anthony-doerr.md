Pulitzer Prize-winning novel set during World War II. Tells the story of a blind French girl and a German boy whose paths intersect during the war. Known for lyrical and descriptive writing with character-focused, introspective style. Pacing is deliberate and measured, building tension and suspense. Features short episodic chapters that alternate between two main characters, creating urgency and momentum. Described as a beautiful, emotional, and thought-provoking read.
