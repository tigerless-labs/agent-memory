**Schedule**: Every Sunday evening from 8:30 pm to 9:30 pm

**Agenda**:
1. Review tasks and goals (15–20 minutes): reflect on accomplishments, review progress, adjust priorities
2. Plan meals for the week (20–25 minutes): decide on 3–5 dinner meals, list ingredients needed
3. Plan grocery shopping (10–15 minutes): create shopping list, check pantry/fridge/freezer

**Purpose**: Reflect on past week, prepare for next week, support cooking at home goal.
