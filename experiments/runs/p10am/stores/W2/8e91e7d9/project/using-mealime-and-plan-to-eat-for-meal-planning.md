Decided to try both Mealime and Plan to Eat apps for organizing weekly meal plans and grocery shopping lists.

**Mealime**: Offers healthy recipes, automated grocery lists, and meal planning for special diets (vegan, gluten-free).

**Plan to Eat**: Comprehensive meal planning app with recipe saving, meal planning, grocery list generation, and nutritional tracking.

Both apps support the goal of cooking dinner at home more often.
