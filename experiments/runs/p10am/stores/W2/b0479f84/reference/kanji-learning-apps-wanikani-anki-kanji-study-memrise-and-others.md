Popular apps for practicing kanji reading and writing:

- **WaniKani** — Comprehensive kanji learning using mnemonics and radicals with spaced repetition
- **Anki** — Customizable flashcard app with downloadable kanji decks
- **Kanji Study** — Dedicated app with large database, stroke order, and example sentences
- **Memrise** — Language learning platform with user-generated kanji content
- **Joyo Kanji** — Free app focusing on standardized kanji for everyday writing
- **KanjiQ** — Gamified app for kanji readings and meanings (iOS)
- **Learn Japanese with Yomiwa** — Comprehensive with kanji dictionary and flashcards
- **Kanji Grid** — Practice kanji readings by filling grids (Android)
- **Kanji Senpai** — Free app with large database and practice exercises
- **NHK WORLD-JAPAN's Kanji Master** — Free online tool from Japan's national broadcaster
