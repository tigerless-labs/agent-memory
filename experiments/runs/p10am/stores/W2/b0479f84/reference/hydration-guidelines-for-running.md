Before run: Drink 16-20 oz of water or sports drink 1-2 hours prior

During run: 
- For runs longer than 30-45 minutes: drink 4-6 oz every 10-15 minutes
- For runs longer than 60 minutes: use sports drink with electrolytes (sodium, potassium) and carbohydrates
- For shorter runs: water is sufficient

After run: Drink 16-20 oz of water or sports drink within 30 minutes

Monitoring: Pale yellow or clear urine indicates good hydration; dark yellow or amber indicates dehydration

Warnings: Avoid overhydration which can cause hyponatremia
