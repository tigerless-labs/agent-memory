**Jhana meditation** is a Buddhist meditation practice that cultivates concentration, mindfulness, and insight.

**The eight levels**:

1. **First Jhana** — Strong, sustained focus on single object (breath, mantra); mind fully absorbed; distractions suppressed
2. **Second Jhana** — Deep joy and contentment; focused on object of meditation but with less effort required
3. **Third Jhana** — Equanimity and balance; deeper inner peace and tranquility; mind free from distractions
4. **Fourth Jhana** — Pure awareness and detachment; complete freedom from distractions; blissful serenity
5. **Fifth Jhana** — Sense of infinite space; consciousness expands beyond body boundaries
6. **Sixth Jhana** — Sense of infinite consciousness; separateness dissolves; unity with all things
7. **Seventh Jhana** — Sense of infinite bliss; pure unbounded joy; freedom from negative thoughts
8. **Eighth Jhana** — Sense of infinite peace; completely still mind; profound inner peace and serenity

Each level builds on the previous, leading to deep inner peace and spiritual insight. Progressive deepening of concentration.
