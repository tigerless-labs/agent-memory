Prevention strategies:
- Gradual progression: Avoid sudden changes in mileage, frequency, or intensity
- Proper footwear: Wear shoes designed for your foot type; replace every 300-400 miles or 3-4 months
- Ankle strengthening: Calf raises, ankle circles, single-leg balancing
- Running form: Land midfoot or forefoot (not heel strike), upright posture, engaged core, avoid overstriding
- Strength training: Target calves, tibialis anterior, and peroneals
- Stretching and foam rolling: Regular stretching and release work on lower legs
- Softer surfaces: Prefer trails, grass, or treadmill over concrete or asphalt
- Orthotics: Consider if you have flat feet, high arches, or other foot issues
- Cross-training: Incorporate low-impact activities like cycling, swimming, or elliptical
- Listen to your body: Rest and ice if pain occurs
