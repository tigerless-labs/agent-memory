Popular productivity apps for staying organized on a new laptop:

**Task Management:**
- **Todoist** — Powerful task management with prioritization, deadlines, and project templates
- **Trello** — Visual project management using boards, lists, and cards for team collaboration
- **Asana** — Comprehensive task platform with workflows, deadlines, and reporting

**Note-taking and Organization:**
- **Evernote** — Popular note-taking with tagging, searching, and notebook organization
- **OneNote** — Microsoft-owned, integrates with Office tools and Microsoft ecosystem
- **Simplenote** — Minimalistic note-taking focused on simplicity and ease of use

**Time Management:**
- **RescueTime** — Tracks laptop time usage, provides productivity insights and optimization
- **Focus@Will** — Background music specifically designed for concentration and focus
- **Pomodoro Timer** — Time management using 25-minute work increments with breaks

**Calendar and Scheduling:**
- **Google Calendar** — Popular calendar with Google tools integration, scheduling and reminders
- **Microsoft Outlook** — Email client with calendar and scheduling features
- **Fantastical** — Calendar app with natural language input and clean interface

**Additional Tools:**
- **StayFocusd** — Browser extension for blocking distracting websites
- **Forest** — Gamifies work sessions by growing virtual forest during focus time
- **IFTTT** — Automates tasks by connecting different apps and services
