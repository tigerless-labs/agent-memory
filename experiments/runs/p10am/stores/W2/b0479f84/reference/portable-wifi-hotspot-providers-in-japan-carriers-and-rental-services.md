Options for portable WiFi connectivity in Japan:

**Major Carriers:**
- **NTT Docomo** — Wi-Fi STATION series, speeds up to 1 Gbps
- **au by KDDI** — Wi-Fi WALKER series, speeds up to 440 Mbps
- **SoftBank** — 4G LTE Wi-Fi series, speeds up to 150 Mbps

**MVNOs (Mobile Virtual Network Operators):**
- **UQ WiMAX** — Popular MVNO, speeds up to 220 Mbps
- **Y!mobile** — Speeds up to 150 Mbps
- **LINE Mobile** — Speeds up to 150 Mbps

**Rental Services:**
- **Japan WiFi** — ¥540/day, unlimited data
- **Wi2** — ¥648/day, unlimited data
- **Pupuru** — ¥698/day, unlimited data

**Where to Buy:** Yodobashi Camera, Bic Camera, Amazon Japan, or airports/shopping districts
