Running Technique Tips:
- Posture: Maintain alignment (ears, shoulders, hips, ankles)
- Footstrike: Land midfoot or forefoot, not heel strike
- Cadence: Aim for 160-170 steps per minute
- Arm swing: Relaxed, 90-degree bend, elbows close to body
- Breathing: 2-3 steps inhale, 2-3 steps exhale
- Core: Engage to support posture and stability
- Glutes and hips: Activate to drive stride
- Ankle mobility: Keep relaxed and flexible
