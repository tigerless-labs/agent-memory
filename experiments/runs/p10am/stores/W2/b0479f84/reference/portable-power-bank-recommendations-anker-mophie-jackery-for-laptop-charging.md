Compact and reliable portable power banks for laptop charging on-the-go:

**Compact Options:**
- **Anker PowerCore 10000** — 10,000mAh, 220g, slim and lightweight
- **Mophie Powerstation Mini** — 5,000mAh, compact design
- **Jackery Titan 20000** — 20,000mAh, sleek design with multiple USB ports

**Laptop-Specific Options:**
- **Anker PowerCore Fusion** — 20,000mAh, designed specifically for laptops
- **Mophie Powerstation AC** — 22,000mAh, includes built-in AC outlet
- **Eggtronic Power Bank** — 20,000mAh, compact with USB-C fast charging

**Reliable Brands:**
- **Anker** — Known for high-quality, fast charging, compact designs
- **Mophie** — Stylish, reliable, advanced features like wireless charging
- **Jackery** — Durable construction, high-capacity options

**Key Considerations:** Capacity, size/weight, charging speed (QuickCharge/PowerDelivery), multiple ports, safety features (overcharge/short-circuit protection)
