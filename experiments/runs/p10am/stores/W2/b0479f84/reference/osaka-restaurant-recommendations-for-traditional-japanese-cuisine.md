**Ramen:** Ichiran Osaka or Ramen Kuroda (rich, savory tonkotsu)\n**Okonomiyaki:** Okonomiyaki Mizuno (classic, savory) or Okonomiyaki Chitanda (modern, creative variations)\n**Kushikatsu:** Kushikatsu Tanaka (wide variety of deep-fried skewers) or Kushikatsu Daruma (classic, affordable)\n**Udon:** Sanuki Udon (authentic, handmade) or Tsurutontan (modern, creative dishes)\nAlso try: takoyaki (octopus dumplings) and other local specialties
