1. **Be friendly and polite** - Greet vendors and engage in small talk to build rapport
2. **Know the value** - Research what other vendors are selling similar items for
3. **Start with a low offer** - Give yourself room to negotiate; vendor will counter with higher offer
4. **Be willing to walk away** - Sometimes vendor will call you back with better price
5. **Have fun with it** - Approach bargaining positively and enjoy the process

**Important**: Bargaining is common practice in Tanzania, but remember vendors rely on sales for their livelihood. Negotiate for fair price while still paying reasonable amount.
