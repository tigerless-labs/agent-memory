**Meister Eckhart** was a Christian mystic whose teachings are used to bridge Eastern and Western spiritual traditions.

**Key insight used**: "The eye with which I see God is the same eye with which God sees me."

This quote reframes God not as an object of doctrine or external authority, but as a mutual mystery experienced through awareness and perception. It emphasizes:
- God as not graspable through intellect alone but through experience
- Intimate connection between observer and observed
- Resonates with Buddhist concepts of non-dual awareness and Jhana meditation's expanded consciousness

Useful for addressing concerns that non-doctrinal, experience-based spirituality is anti-Christian. Shows Christianity has mystical, introspective traditions that value direct experience over rigid doctrine.
