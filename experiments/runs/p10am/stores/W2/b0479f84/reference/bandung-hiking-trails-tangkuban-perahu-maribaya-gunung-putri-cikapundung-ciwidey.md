Popular trails near Bandung:

Tangkuban Perahu Volcano: 25 km north, 1.5 hrs, lush rainforest, crater views
Maribaya Waterfall: 15 km northeast, 45 mins, moderate difficulty
Gunung Putri: 20 km outside city, 4-5 hrs, challenging, 360-degree panoramic views
Cikapundung River: 5 km leisurely trail along river
Ciwidey Valley: 1 hour south, 2-3 hrs, tea plantations and rice paddies
