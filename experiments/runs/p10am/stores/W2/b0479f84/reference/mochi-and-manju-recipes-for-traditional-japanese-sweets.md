**Mochi:**\n\nTips:\n- Use glutinous or sticky rice flour (mochigome)\n- Soak rice flour in water for at least 4 hours or overnight\n- Use 2:1 ratio (2 parts flour to 1 part water), adjust as needed\n- Knead dough until smooth and pliable like playdough\n- Shape and fill with preferred sweet filling\n\nSimple Recipe:\n- 2 cups glutinous rice flour\n- 1 cup water\n- 1/2 cup granulated sugar\n- Filling of choice (red bean paste, chestnut paste, or ice cream)\n\n**Manju:**\n\nTips:\n- Use all-purpose flour or combination of all-purpose and bread flour\n- Steam to cook evenly and prevent density\n- Don't overmix dough\n- Handle gently to prevent becoming too dense or developing gluten\n\nSimple Recipe:\n- 2 cups all-purpose flour\n- 1/2 cup granulated sugar\n- 1/2 cup warm water\n- 1/4 teaspoon salt\n- Filling of choice (sweet bean paste made from azuki beans)
