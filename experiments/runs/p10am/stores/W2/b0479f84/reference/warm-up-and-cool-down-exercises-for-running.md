Warm-up (10-15 minutes):
- Light cardio: 5-10 minutes of jogging or walking
- Leg swings: 30-60 seconds
- Hip circles: 30-60 seconds
- Knee lifts: 10-15 reps each leg
- Calf raises: 10-15 reps
- Arm circles: 30-60 seconds

Cool-down (10-15 minutes):
- Easy walking: 5-10 minutes
- Static stretches (hold 15-30 seconds each): hamstrings, quads, calves, hip flexors, glutes
- Foam rolling: IT band, quadriceps, hamstrings
