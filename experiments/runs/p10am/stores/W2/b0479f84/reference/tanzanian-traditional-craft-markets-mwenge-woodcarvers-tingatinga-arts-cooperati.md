**Mwenge Woodcarvers Market** (Dar es Salaam)
- Known for intricate woodcarvings
- Home to many skilled craftsmen

**Tingatinga Arts Cooperative Society** (Dar es Salaam)
- Founded by Edward Tingatinga
- Known for distinctive Tingatinga painting style

**Cultural Heritage Center** (Arusha)
- Large selection of art and crafts from different regions of Tanzania

**Arusha Maasai Market** (Arusha)
- Specializes in Maasai beadwork and other crafts by Maasai artisans

**Zawadi House** (Stone Town, Zanzibar)
- Boutique offering high-quality crafts
- Features wood carvings, textiles, and jewelry
