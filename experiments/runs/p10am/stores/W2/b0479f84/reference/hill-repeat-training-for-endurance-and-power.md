Hill repeat guidelines:

Hill selection: Choose a hill that takes 1-3 minutes to climb, steep enough to challenge but maintainable

Progression: Start with 3-5 repeats and gradually increase as endurance builds

Form: Maintain upright posture, engaged core, quick turnover, light footstrikes, relaxed arms and shoulders

Pace: Try to maintain consistent pace throughout each repeat

Recovery: Walk or jog back down the hill between repeats to conserve energy and reduce joint impact

Frequency: Incorporate once per week with 2-3 rest days between sessions

Variations:
- Short, steep hills (1-2 minutes): Target explosive power
- Medium hills (2-3 minutes): Build lactate tolerance
- Longer hills (3-5 minutes): Improve endurance and stamina

Warm-up and cool-down: 10-15 minutes of easy running before and after hill repeats

Breathe consciously to stay relaxed and maintain pace

Mental focus: Hill repeats build mental toughness as well as physical strength
