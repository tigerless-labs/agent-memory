Strength training for runner power and endurance (2-3x per week):

Glutes:
- Squats: Feet shoulder-width apart, lower until thighs parallel to ground, push back up
- Lunges: Step forward, lower until back knee nearly touches ground, alternate legs
- Deadlifts: Bend and lift weight from ground to hip level, keep back straight
- Glute bridges: Lie on back with knees bent, lift hips toward ceiling, squeeze glutes at top

Hamstrings:
- Deadlifts: (see above)
- Leg curls: Curl legs toward glutes on leg curl machine
- Leg press: Push platform away with feet, extending legs
- Romanian deadlifts: Bend down to lift weight, keeping back straight

Quads:
- Squats: (see above)
- Leg press: (see above)
- Leg extensions: Straighten legs against resistance on leg extension machine
- Step-ups: Step up onto bench with one foot, bring other foot up, step back down

Guidelines:
- Start with lighter weights and progress gradually
- Focus on proper form and technique
- Incorporate exercises for other muscle groups: core and calves
- Allow 2-3 rest days between strength sessions
- Pair with running routine (Tuesday/Thursday) for balanced training
