**Ingredients:**\n- 1 cup red bean paste (canned or homemade)\n- 1 cup granulated sugar\n- 2 cups water\n- 2 tablespoons agar powder\n- Food coloring (optional)\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Prepare red bean paste - drain and rinse if canned, ensure smooth and creamy\n2. Combine sugar and water in saucepan, heat over medium until sugar dissolves\n3. Mix agar powder with water to make paste, add to sugar syrup and stir\n4. Bring to boil, reduce heat to low, simmer about 10 minutes until thickened and agar fully dissolved\n5. Add red bean paste and stir well to combine\n6. Optional: add food coloring\n7. Pour into rectangular mold or individual molds, cool at room temperature\n8. Refrigerate until set, cut into cubes or slices and serve\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use high-quality agar powder specifically for yokan\n- Yokan is quite sweet; adjust sugar amount to taste\n- Experiment with flavors: green tea, sesame, or yuzu for variations
