Writing fictional episodes about a character called 'the Teacher' who engages with people from different backgrounds.

**Core teachings**: curiosity, resilience, openness to new perspectives

**Episodes completed**:
1. **Journalist** — Teacher questions whether journalism captures all untold stories; advocates for questioning assumptions beyond professional norms
2. **LLM Computer Scientist** — Teacher admires LLM technology but argues it has limits in fostering resilience and curiosity; warns against over-reliance on technology at the expense of human ingenuity
3. **Fundamentalist Christians** — Teacher addresses concerns that his Eastern-sounding message is anti-Christian by invoking Meister Eckhart's Christian mysticism ("The eye with which I see God is the same eye with which God sees me") to reframe God as mystery experienced through awareness, not doctrine

**Approach**: The Teacher navigates between worldviews without dismissing them; uses silence and questions to invite deeper thinking; bridges Eastern meditation concepts with Western Christian mysticism
