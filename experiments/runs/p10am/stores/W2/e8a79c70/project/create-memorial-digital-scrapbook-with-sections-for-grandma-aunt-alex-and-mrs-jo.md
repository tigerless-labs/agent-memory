Digital memorial scrapbook to organize and preserve photos, stories, and memories in three sections:

**Grandma**: Funeral photos, memorial stories, life memories

**Aunt Alex**: Hospital visit photos, stories from interactions

**Mrs. Johnson**: English class memories, classroom photos, lessons learned, anecdotes about teaching impact

Content includes: photos, written stories and anecdotes, audio recordings, quotes and testimonials. Each section organized chronologically or by theme.

Privacy: Requires password protection and private/invite-only sharing. Accessible only to specific trusted family members and friends.

Platforms: Considering Google Photos (free, supports private albums with invite-only sharing), Adobe Spark (free web design app), or Canva (design templates)
