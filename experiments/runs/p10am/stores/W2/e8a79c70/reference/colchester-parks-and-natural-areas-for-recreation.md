## Colchester Parks and Natural Areas

**Castle Park**
Town centre's largest public park with beautiful gardens, a large pond, and sports facilities including skateboard park, tennis courts, and cricket ground.

**Highwoods Country Park**
Large nature reserve (350+ acres) on the outskirts featuring woodlands, open meadows, and several ponds. Good for hiking, wildlife watching, and picnics.

**Wivenhoe Trail**
5-mile walking and cycling route following the River Colne between Colchester and Wivenhoe, passing through woodlands, wildlife habitats, and old industrial sites.

**Cudmore Grove Country Park**
Beautiful country park located on Mersea Island featuring a large beach, woodlands, picnic areas, and various walking and cycling routes.

**Castle Point Wildlife Reserve**
Nature reserve near the coast featuring salt marshes, mudflats, and tidal creeks. Home to numerous waterfowl and waders, ideal for bird watching.
