**Time requirements:**
- Full-time programs: approximately 15-20 hours of coursework per week
- Part-time programs: lower weekly hours but longer completion timeline

**Course components:** assignments, readings, videos, online discussions, assessments

**Flexibility advantage:** programs allow self-paced completion and personalized scheduling, enabling better balance with work, family, and personal commitments. Faculty use digital technologies for active learning, interactive discussions, and group projects.
