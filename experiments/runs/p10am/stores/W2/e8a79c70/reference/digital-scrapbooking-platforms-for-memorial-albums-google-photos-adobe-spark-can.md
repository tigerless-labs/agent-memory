Platforms recommended for creating digital memorial scrapbooks:

**Google Photos**
- Free and user-friendly
- Supports private album creation
- Invite-only sharing with password protection
- Good for organizing photos with captions and stories

**Adobe Spark**
- Free web-based design application
- Supports private sections with restricted access
- Includes design templates for scrapbooks and albums
- Good for creating polished, designed layouts

**Canva**
- Popular graphic design platform
- Extensive library of scrapbook and album templates
- Customizable design tools
- Good for creating visually cohesive memorial albums

**Key features across platforms:**
- Password protection and private/invite-only settings
- Ability to add captions, written stories, and organize chronologically or by theme
- Audio recording embedding and media support
- Cloud backup and automatic syncing across devices
- Search, tagging, and keywording for easy access to specific memories
