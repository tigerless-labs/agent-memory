## Colchester Historical Sites

**Colchester Castle**
Norman castle with impressive museum featuring exhibits on Roman, medieval, and Civil War history.

**St. Botolph's Priory**
11th-century religious site featuring impressive ruins highlighting Colchester's religious heritage.

**Hollytrees Museum**
Georgian townhouse (18th century) preserving daily life of middle-class families, with exhibits of furniture, clothing, and decorative arts.

**Tymperleys Clock Museum**
Grade I listed house featuring a fascinating collection of clocks, including lantern clocks, long-case clocks, and a clock with a small garden.

**Firstsite Art Gallery**
Contemporary art gallery featuring modern art exhibitions and hosting various events throughout the year.
