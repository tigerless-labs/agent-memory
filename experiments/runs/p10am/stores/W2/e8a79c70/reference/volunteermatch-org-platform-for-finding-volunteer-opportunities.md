VolunteerMatch.org (www.volunteermatch.org) - Platform for discovering local volunteer opportunities filtered by location, cause, and interests. Useful for finding both food security and music-related volunteering opportunities. Search using keywords like food bank, food pantry, hunger relief, music therapy, seniors.
