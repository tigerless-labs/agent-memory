**Recommended evaluation approaches:**

- Review university website and individual faculty profiles for academic background, research interests, publications, and professional experience
- Verify professional accreditations and endorsements from field-specific professional organizations (quality indicator)
- Check university rankings (U.S. News and World Report and similar sources)
- Contact current or former students for firsthand feedback on instruction quality and support level
- Verify faculty hold relevant credentials, certifications, or licenses typical for their profession
- Verify faculty tenure or appointment status at accredited institutions as indicator of qualification standards
