**Multiple interaction methods available:**

- **Discussion boards** — ask questions, share information, gain insights from faculty and peers
- **Email** — direct communication with faculty members about assignments, questions, feedback
- **Group projects** — collaborative work with peers and faculty interaction
- **Chat and messaging platforms** — real-time communication (Slack, Zoom, WebEx)
- **Social media** — program announcements and community engagement (Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn)
- **Online communities** — networking and professional connections with peers and alumni
