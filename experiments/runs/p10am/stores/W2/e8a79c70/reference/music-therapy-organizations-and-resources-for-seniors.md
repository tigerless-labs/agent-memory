Key organizations for music therapy programs and resources:

**Music Therapy Associations:**
- American Music Therapy Association (AMTA) - directory of programs and professionals
- Music Therapy Association of America (MTAA)
- National Association for Music Therapy (NAMT)

**Senior-focused organizations:**
- AARP
- National Council on Aging (NCOA)
- LeadingAge

**Common music therapy activities for seniors:**
- Singing sessions (familiar songs, group singing)
- Instrument playing (tambourines, maracas, drums)
- Music-based exercise (chair yoga, dance, stretching)
- Memory recall/reminiscence sessions
- Lyric analysis and discussion
- Music-assisted relaxation and guided imagery
- Improvisation activities
- Music combined with storytelling
