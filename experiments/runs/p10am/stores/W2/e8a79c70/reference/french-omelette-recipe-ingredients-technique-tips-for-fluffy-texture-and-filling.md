## Classic French Omelette

**Ingredients:**
- 2–3 eggs
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- Optional: 1 tablespoon finely chopped herbs

**Steps:**
1. Whisk eggs with salt until frothy and well combined
2. Heat nonstick skillet over medium-high heat
3. Add butter and swirl until it covers the surface
4. Pour egg mixture in and spread evenly with fork/spatula
5. Let cook ~30 seconds until eggs begin to set
6. Gently fold one edge to center, then opposite side
7. Tilt skillet and slide omelette onto plate
8. Garnish with herbs if desired

**Tips for fluffy, light texture:**
- Whisk eggs thoroughly until frothy
- Use nonstick skillet to prevent sticking/tearing
- Cook over medium-high (not too high or low)
- Don't overfill – just a few tablespoons of filling
- Fold gently without pressing down
- Serve immediately after folding

**Filling recommendations:**
- Cheese (Swiss, cheddar, Gruyere)
- Cooked ham, diced and sautéed
- Sautéed mushrooms (earthy flavor)
- Cooked spinach with sautéed onions and garlic
- Diced, cooked tomatoes
- Fresh/dried herbs (parsley, thyme, chives)

**Popular combinations:**
- Ham and cheese
- Mushroom and cheese
- Spinach and feta
- Tomato and basil
- Smoked salmon and dill
