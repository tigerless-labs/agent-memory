Privacy preferences for memorial and tribute albums:

- Password protection required
- Private/invite-only sharing only, no public access
- Control over who can view sensitive family memories and stories  
- Enable two-factor authentication on accounts storing memorial content
- Importance of security for emotional, personal family memories
