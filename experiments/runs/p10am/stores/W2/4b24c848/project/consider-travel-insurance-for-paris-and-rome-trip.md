Evaluating travel insurance for Paris and Rome trip.

Coverage to consider:
- Trip cancellation or interruption due to illness, injury, natural disasters
- Medical emergencies while abroad
- Flight delays, cancellations, lost luggage
- Theft or loss of personal belongings
- 24/7 emergency travel assistance

Expected cost: 5-10% of total trip cost

Recommendation: Purchase early (soon after booking) to ensure coverage for cancellations. Research and compare policies from reputable providers. Understand terms, conditions, and exclusions. Consider if travel arrangements are expensive enough to justify insurance.
