Simmer 1 cup lentils (brown or green) with ginger, garlic, and spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala) for 20-25 minutes. Can blend to desired consistency. Optional: cayenne pepper. Variations include vegetables, coconut milk, or yogurt.
