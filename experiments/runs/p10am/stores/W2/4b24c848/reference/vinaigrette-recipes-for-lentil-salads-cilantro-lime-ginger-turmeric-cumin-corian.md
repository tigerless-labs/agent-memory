Basic ratio: 2 tbsp acid (vinegar/citrus) to 1 tbsp oil to 1 tsp flavoring. Cilantro-lime with cumin; ginger-turmeric with apple cider vinegar; cumin-coriander with red wine vinegar and cayenne; lemon-garlic. Use mild oil, adjust acid to taste, do not over-dress.
