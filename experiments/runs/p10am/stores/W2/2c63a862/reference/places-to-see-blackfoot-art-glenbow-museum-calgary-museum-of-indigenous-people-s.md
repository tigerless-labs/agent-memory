**Glenbow Museum** (Calgary, Canada): Extensive collection of Blackfoot art, artifacts, and culture. The Blackfoot Gallery features traditional and contemporary Blackfoot art including beadwork, paintings, and sculptures.

**Museum of Indigenous People** (Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA): Showcases contemporary Blackfoot art along with works of other indigenous artists in rotating exhibitions.

**Blackfoot Crossing Historical Park** (Alberta, Canada): Cultural center celebrating Blackfoot culture and history with various exhibits and showcases of Blackfoot artistry.
