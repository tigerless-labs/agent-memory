**Jason Heavy Runner** (Blackfoot fashion designer): Incorporates traditional Blackfoot beadwork techniques into contemporary fashion pieces—dresses, jackets, accessories combining traditional designs with modern cuts and fabrics.

**Adrian Stimson**: Creates paintings combining traditional Plains Indian ledger art with modern pop culture images. Example: 'Parked Pow Wow Princess' depicts a young woman in traditional Native regalia on a pile of tires in a parking lot.

**Lauren Crazybull**: Works across painting, embroidery, and digital art. Incorporates traditional Blackfoot motifs into contemporary pieces. Series 'Everything is a Ceremony' explores themes of spirituality and mindfulness in the modern world.
