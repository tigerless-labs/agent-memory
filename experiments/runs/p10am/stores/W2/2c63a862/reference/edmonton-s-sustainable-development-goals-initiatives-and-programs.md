Edmonton, Canada has multiple sustainability initiatives aligned with UN SDGs:

**Energy (SDG 7):**
- Community Energy Transition Strategy (2015) - targets 35% GHG reduction by 2035, net-zero by 2050
- Solar installations: Edmonton Valley Zoo, recreation centers, LRT maintenance facilities
- Biomass energy project at Edmonton Waste Management Centre (landfill gas to electricity)

**Water (SDG 6):**
- Source Water Protection Plan
- Drinking Water Quality monitoring
- North Saskatchewan River water quality protection

**Waste (SDG 12):**
- Edmonton Waste Management Centre - state-of-the-art facility with composting, anaerobic digestion, materials recovery
- Zero Waste Framework - targeting 90% residential waste diversion from landfills
- Waste Strategy (2020)

**Food Systems:**
- Edmonton Food Council - supports local food system, urban agriculture, community gardens, farmers markets

**Regional Coordination:**
- Edmonton Metropolitan Region Board (EMRB)
- North Saskatchewan Watershed Alliance
- Edmonton Community Foundation

**Programs:**
- Change Homes for Climate Solar Program (resident/business incentives)
- Building Energy Benchmarking Program (commercial/institutional buildings)
- Sustainable Purchasing Guide
