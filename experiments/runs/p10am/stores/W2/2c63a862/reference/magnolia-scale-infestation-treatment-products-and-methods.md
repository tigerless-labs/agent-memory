## Prevention & Early Detection
- Regularly inspect for scale insects or sticky honeydew on leaves and branches
- Prune and dispose of infested branches promptly to prevent spread

## Recommended Horticultural Products
- **Neem oil**: natural oil extracted from neem tree; suffocates and repels scales; mix with water per label
- **Insecticidal soap**: potassium salts of fatty acids; spray directly on tree but rinse with water after to avoid leaf damage
- **Dormant oil**: heavier oil for dormant season; used winter/early spring to smother eggs before hatch
- **All Seasons Spray Oil**: year-round option for scale control; mix with water per label

## Integrated Control Methods
- Encourage natural predators: ladybugs, lacewings
- Proper tree nutrition and care
- Deep, infrequent watering (avoid water stress)
- Avoid over-fertilizing (creates favorable scale conditions)
- **Rotate treatment methods** to prevent resistance development

## Ant Management (Critical)
- Ants are attracted to honeydew excreted by scales
- **Ants DO NOT help control—they harm control efforts** by protecting scales from predators
- Ants transport scales to other tree parts and adjacent trees, spreading infestation
- Control ants with sticky barriers or baits

## Severity if Untreated
- Can last indefinitely and spread to other trees in yard
- Weakens tree by feeding on sap; stunts growth
- Causes premature leaf yellowing and drop; branch die-back
- **Can ultimately kill the tree**, especially if tree is already stressed (drought, heat, poor nutrition)
- Easier to control if caught early; prevention/early intervention critical
