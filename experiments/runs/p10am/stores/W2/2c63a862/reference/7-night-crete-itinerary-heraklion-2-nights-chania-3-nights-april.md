## Days 1–2: Heraklion

**Restaurants:** Peskesi (traditional Cretan), Karavitis Taverna (grilled meats/seafood), Erganos (Cretan breakfast), Arismari (organic, vegetarian options)

**Attractions:** Heraklion Archaeological Museum (Minoan artifacts), Palace of Knossos, Old town (Venetian Fortress Koules, Agios Titos Church), Cretaquarium, Watercity Waterpark

## Days 3–6: Chania

**Restaurants:** Ta Chalkina (traditional taverna), Tamam (creative Cretan), Almyra (beachfront seafood), Salis (contemporary Greek/Mediterranean), Chrisostomos (grilled meats/fish), Thalassino Ageri (seafood), To Spitiko (traditional Greek)

**Attractions:** Venetian Harbor/old town, Maritime Museum, Botanical Park, Agia Triada Monastery (17th-century), Samaria Gorge, Elafonisi Beach (pink sand), Chania Lighthouse, Ancient Aperta archaeological site

**Trip details:** April visit, 7 nights total, departing from UK
