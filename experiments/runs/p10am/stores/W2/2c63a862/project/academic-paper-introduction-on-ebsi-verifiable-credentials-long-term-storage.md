Writing academic paper introduction addressing long-term storage of EBSI-issued verifiable credentials using TR-ESOR middleware. Paper tackles three challenges:

1. **Long-term storage**: designing reliable and efficient solutions for EBSI-issued verifiable credentials, ensuring availability and integrity over extended periods
2. **Privacy preservation**: protecting VC holder privacy while maintaining system security
3. **TR-ESOR integration**: feasibility and benefits of integrating TR-ESOR middleware as long-term storage solution

## Key concepts
- **Verifiable credentials (VCs)**: cryptographically verifiable digital credentials enabling issuers to grant credentials to holders for presentation to verifiers as proof of identity/qualification/attributes
- **EBSI**: European Blockchain Service Infrastructure, decentralized platform fostering secure cross-border digital public services
- **Trust in decentralized systems**: traditional PKI insufficient; VCs establish trust while preserving privacy
