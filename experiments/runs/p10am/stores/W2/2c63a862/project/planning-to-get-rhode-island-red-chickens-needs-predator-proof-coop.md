Plans to get Rhode Island Red chickens requiring predator-proof coop with: hardware cloth (max 1-inch mesh), buried foundation (1-2 feet deep), secure latches on doors/windows, roosting bars 2-3 feet high, covered run to prevent aerial predators, electric fencing around perimeter (optional), regular inspections, located near house within fenced yard.
