Mirrors have been streaked and foggy for weeks. Wants long-term prevention strategy to avoid weekly cleaning. Solutions discussed: anti-fog coatings, squeegee after showers, improved ventilation with exhaust fan or dehumidifier, vinegar-water cleaning solution, microfiber cloths, and regular 2-3 week cleaning schedule.
