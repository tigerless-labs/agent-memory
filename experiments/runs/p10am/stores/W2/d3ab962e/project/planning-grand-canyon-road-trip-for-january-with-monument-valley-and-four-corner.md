User planning Southwest road trip for January. Route: Las Vegas → Williams or Flagstaff → Grand Canyon South Rim → Monument Valley → Four Corners. Suggested accommodations: Bright Angel Lodge or Yavapai Lodge (Grand Canyon); Monument Valley Inn or Goulding's Lodge (Monument Valley); Cortez Colorado area (Four Corners). Tradeoff between Williams (closer ~30mi, rustic) and Flagstaff (more amenities, ~65mi from park). Additional stops mentioned: Hoover Dam, Petrified Forest National Park. Note: January is peak season; recommendation to book accommodations in advance.
