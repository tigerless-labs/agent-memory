Merkur HD safety razor: approximately 40 USD. Uses double-edge (DE) blades. Blade costs: 0.25–1 USD per blade depending on brand and quality. Blade lifespan: typically 5-10 shaves per blade, or 1-2 weeks depending on shaving frequency and hair type. Good beginner option for wet shaving.
