## Structure for Community-Focused Startup Newsletter

1. **Why community matters**: How community helps with feedback, validation, and customer connection
2. **How to build community**: Tips including social media engagement, blogging, hosting events; emphasis on authenticity, transparency, approachability  
3. **Success stories**: Examples of Airbnb, Dropbox, Tesla building strong communities around their products
4. **Leverage community for growth**: Using feedback to improve product, user-generated content, referral incentives
5. **Conclusion & call-to-action**: Summarize key takeaways, invite participation

Framing: Opening with greeting and newsletter welcome, closing with invitation to join community and follow on social media.
