Landmarks: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Champs-Élysées, Arc de Triomphe, Montmartre/Sacré-Cœur, Palace of Versailles, Sainte-Chapelle, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, Palace of Fontainebleau, Pont des Arts, Les Invalides, Place des Vosges, Panthéon

Cafes near Eiffel Tower: Café de l'Homme (Eiffel Tower view, creative French), Café Gustave (15th arr., excellent brunches), Carette (pastries and coffee)

Restaurants near Louvre: Le Grand Véfour (Michelin-starred, exquisite French), Le Comptoir du Louvre (classic bistro), Verjus (modern, inventive tasting menus)
