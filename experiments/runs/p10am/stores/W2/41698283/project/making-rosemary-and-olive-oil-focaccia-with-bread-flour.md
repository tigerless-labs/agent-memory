Ingredients: 1.5 cups warm water, 2 tsp active dry yeast, 3.5 cups bread flour, 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 sprigs fresh rosemary (chopped), coarse sea salt for topping

Instructions:
1. Combine warm water and yeast in a bowl. Let sit 5-10 minutes until frothy.
2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix until shaggy dough forms.
3. Knead 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Place in lightly oiled bowl, cover, rise 1 hour until doubled.
5. Preheat oven to 425F (220C).
6. Punch down dough, shape into rectangle on parchment-lined baking sheet.
7. Dimple with fingers, sprinkle chopped rosemary and sea salt.
8. Drizzle with olive oil, bake 20-25 minutes until golden brown.

Notes: Using bread flour will produce chewier crumb and stronger gluten. May need to adjust water content as bread flour absorbs more than all-purpose.
