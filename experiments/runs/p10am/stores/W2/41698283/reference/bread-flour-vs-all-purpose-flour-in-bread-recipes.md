Key differences when substituting bread flour for all-purpose flour:

Protein Content:
- Bread flour: 12-14% protein
- All-purpose flour: 10-12% protein

Effects on Texture and Flavor:
- Produces chewier, more robust crumb
- Creates larger holes (more open crumb)
- Enhances gluten development for tender yet crispy crust
- Imparts slightly sweeter, nuttier flavor

Practical Adjustments Needed:
- Bread flour absorbs more water than all-purpose
- May need to increase water content slightly
- Start with same amount, add more as needed for shaggy dough
- Monitor dough development more closely (may develop faster)
- Handle gently when shaping (dough can be more delicate)

When using bread flour in focaccia or sourdough:
- Expect better oven spring and more open crumb
- May need slightly longer hydration
- Adjust proofing times based on dough development
- Results in heartier, more substantial bread
