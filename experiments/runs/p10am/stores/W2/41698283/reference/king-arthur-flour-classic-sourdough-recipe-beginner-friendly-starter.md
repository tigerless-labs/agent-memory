A recommended beginner sourdough recipe from King Arthur Flour. Known for:
- Simple ingredients: sourdough starter, bread flour, salt, water only
- Clear, well-written instructions
- Flexible schedule allowing for different proofing times
- Great for learning sourdough basics

Key proportions:
- 1 cup active, bubbly sourdough starter
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp water

Process: Mix starter and flour until combined, add water, knead 10 minutes, ferment 4-5 hours until nearly doubled, shape, proof 2-3 hours, bake at 450F (230C) for 25-30 minutes with steam.
