Tips for working with fresh rosemary in bread recipes:

Selection and Storage:
- Choose fresh, fragrant sprigs with vibrant green color
- Avoid wilted or brownish sprigs (can be bitter)
- Store in plastic bag or wrap in plastic wrap in refrigerator (lasts up to 1 week)
- Chop just before use to release essential oils and flavors

Usage in Bread:
- For focaccia, use about 2-3 sprigs (releases 2-3 tbsp of leaves)
- Adjust amount to taste, starting smaller
- Distribute evenly throughout dough during initial mixing
- Rosemary is potent - start with smaller amount and add more as needed
- Can pair with other herbs like thyme, oregano, or parsley for more balanced flavor

Application:
- Mix into dough during initial mixing for even distribution
- Can also sprinkle on top before baking for aromatic effect
