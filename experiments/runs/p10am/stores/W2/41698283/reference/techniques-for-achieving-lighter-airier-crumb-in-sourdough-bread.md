Steps to improve sourdough crumb structure:

Starter and Ingredients:
- Use a healthy, active sourdough starter that is bubbly and robust at peak activity
- Use bread flour (12-14% protein) for better gluten development
- Higher protein supports more open, airy crumb

Mixing and Handling:
- Use gentle mixing and folding techniques
- Avoid overmixing - this creates dense, tight crumb
- Mix ingredients just until shaggy mass forms
- Use gentle folding technique to develop dough

Temperature Control:
- Maintain dough temperature of 75-78°F (24-25°C)
- Ideal for yeast and bacteria to work together
- Produces more open crumb through proper fermentation

Hydration and Proofing:
- Use higher water content: 70-80% hydration
- Enables more open, airy crumb structure
- Proof slowly and gently (not aggressively)
- Avoid over-proofing which leads to dense, flat crumb

Baking Technique:
- Score bread correctly just before baking to control expansion
- Bake with steam (use Dutch oven or pan of water)
- Steam allows better expansion during baking for open crumb

General:
- Monitor dough progress during fermentation and proofing
- Practice and adjustment needed - this improves over time
- Keep experimenting with techniques to develop skills
