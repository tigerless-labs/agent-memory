Equation: ln(1+e^(-3x)) = 2

Solution steps:
1. Exponentiate both sides: e^(ln(1+e^(-3x))) = e^2
2. Simplify: 1 + e^(-3x) = e^2
3. Subtract 1: e^(-3x) = e^2 - 1
4. Take natural log: -3x = ln(e^2 - 1)
5. Divide by -3: x = -(1/3) ln(e^2 - 1)

**Answer:** x = -(1/3) ln(e^2 - 1)
