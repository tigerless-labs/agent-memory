On 2023-05-22, organized and clarified notes on Greek mythology. Studied:

**Jason & Medea:** Medea tricks Pelias's daughters into slicing him up; Jason ultimately leaves Medea, who kills their children in revenge.

**Hercules (Heracles/Alcides):** Son of Zeus and Alcmene. Hera, jealous of his birth, sends snakes to kill him as a baby. After killing his wife Megara in a fit of madness caused by Hera, he performs 12 Labors assigned by King Eurystheus (designed by Hera to kill, humiliate, and exhaust him). On return home, attacked by centaur Nessus; Nessus gives Deianeira a love potion that actually poisons Hercules.

Key characters organized with definitions including: Zeus, Amphitryon, Alcmene, Iphicles, Thespius, Eurystheus, Hera, Theseus, Megara, Deianeira, Nessus.
