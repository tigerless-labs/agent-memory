Planned road trip for beach activities and water sports, departing from Outer Banks area. Route: ~2 hours to Topsail Island, then ~4-5 hours to Tybee Island (~7-8 hours total). Restaurant options on Topsail Island: The Fish Factory (seafood platters, covered outdoor deck with marsh/waterway views), The Shrimp Haus, The Beach Shop & Grill, and Ocean's Edge Restaurant at Topsail Island Beach Club (ocean views).
