## Nero: Skincare Brand for Men

**Market & Opportunity**
- Target market: men 25-45, appearance-conscious, time-constrained
- Global men's grooming market: 166 billion dollars projected
- Gap: lack of men-specific skincare products

**Business Model**
- Funding sought: 500,000 dollars
- Year 1 revenue projection: 1 million dollars
- Year 1 net profit projection: 500,000 dollars

**Product Line**
- Cleanser (invigorating)
- Moisturizer (hydrating)
- Sunscreen (protective)
- Formulation: natural ingredients, easy-to-use
- Key differentiation: specifically formulated for men's skin, uncomplicated routines

**Brand Positioning**
- Tagline: Uncomplicated skincare for the modern man
- Mission: empower men to achieve healthy appearance without sacrifice or fuss

**Go-to-Market**
- Marketing channels: social media, influencer partnerships, events
- Sales: online plus in-store retail partnerships

**Personas**
- John: 25, construction worker, practical/hardworking, research-driven buyer
- David: 40, CEO, success-oriented, willing to pay premium for quality
