**Study Abroad Programs:**
- 320+ programs in 60+ countries
- Locations: Europe, Asia, Latin America, Australia, China, Japan, South Korea
- Internship and research opportunities

**Financial Aid:**
- Federal/state financial aid (Pell Grants, Stafford Loans)
- Rutgers scholarships (Global Experience, Need-Based, International Public Service)
- External scholarships (Gilman, Fund for Education Abroad)
- Work-study opportunities

**Language Resources:**
- Spanish courses at beginner to advanced levels
- Conversation Cafés (informal practice with native speakers)
- Language Exchange Program (peer language practice)
- Global Ambassadors Program (student-to-student cultural exchange)

**Application Process:**
- Research through Rutgers Global website
- Meet with Rutgers Global advisor
- Complete online application
- Submit supporting documents (letters, transcripts, statement of purpose)
- Requirements vary by program
