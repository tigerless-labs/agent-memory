Restaurant recommendations for Topsail Island, NC:
- The Fish Factory: Seafood platters, covered outdoor deck, marsh/waterway views (sound side)
- The Shrimp Haus: Seafood restaurant
- The Beach Shop & Grill: Seafood restaurant
- Ocean's Edge Restaurant at Topsail Island Beach Club: Ocean views, seafood menu with platters and sandwiches

Local specialties: shrimp and grits, fish tacos. Tip: Check tide times before beach walks.
