Scenic road through Beartooth Mountains connecting Cooke City, Montana to Red Lodge, Montana (68 miles).

**Summer conditions (June–September):**
- Fully paved, 2 lanes with occasional passing lanes
- Speed limit 45–65 mph
- Elevation over 10,000 feet; mild weather (60s–70s°F) with occasional thunderstorms
- Well-maintained road

**Planning notes:**
- Limited gas stations — fill up in Cooke City or Red Lodge before driving
- Watch for wildlife (bears, elk, deer, bighorn sheep); most active dawn/dusk
- High altitude may cause altitude sickness — stay hydrated, take breaks
- High water pressure at potable water stations; RVs need pressure regulator
- Multiple pull-outs and scenic viewpoints for stops
