Garmin Dash Cam 66W: 1440p QHD at 30fps, 140-degree wide-angle lens, GPS with GLONASS, 2-inch LCD screen. Impact detection saves footage automatically. Lane Departure Warning and Forward Collision Warning. Wi-Fi and voice commands. Parking mode with lithium-ion battery up to 24 hours, records 1-minute segments, wakes on motion or impact. Optional hardwire kit (sold separately) enables continuous 12V power from car electrical system for multi-day recording and reduced battery drain. MicroSD storage up to 512GB with loop recording. Recharge battery every 1-2 weeks without hardwiring. Adhesive mount, compact low-profile design. Strengths: excellent video quality, comprehensive GPS tracking, safety features, parking capability, hardwiring option. Considerations: no cloud storage, frequent recharging required without hardwiring, complex menu system, hardwiring requires professional installation.
