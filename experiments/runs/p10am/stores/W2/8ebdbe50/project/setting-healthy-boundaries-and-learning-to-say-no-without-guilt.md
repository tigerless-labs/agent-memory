Recognizes that learning to say no and setting healthy boundaries could help reduce overwhelm. Plans to practice boundary-setting starting with low-stakes requests and gradually building confidence. Goal is to communicate boundaries clearly without apologizing or justifying, using I statements, avoiding guilt. Will focus on identifying capacity limits, knowing priorities, and staying firm but polite when others push back. Recognizes this as an important self-care skill to develop.
