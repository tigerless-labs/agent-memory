Plan to contact current students and alumni from both University of Florida and Arizona State University MBA programs to gather insider information about:

- Program strengths and weaknesses
- Valuable skills and knowledge gained
- Academic environment and faculty support
- Internship, networking, and career development opportunities
- Challenges faced during the program

This outreach is intended to help inform the final decision between the two programs.
