Project steps:

1. Photograph: Take high-quality photos of each item from multiple angles, close-ups of distinctive features
2. Spreadsheet catalog: Create spreadsheet with columns for item description, value, date acquired, photos
3. Research: Consult online collector forums and communities
4. Get appraisals: Contact local appraisers specializing in vintage toys/collectibles; prioritize Boba Fett figure
5. Insurance: Obtain specialized collectibles insurance (Chubb, State Farm, Collectibles Insurance Services) with comprehensive documentation
6. Storage: Keep items in cool, dry place; use archival materials; handle with care to preserve condition

Goal: Accurate valuation and proper insurance coverage.
