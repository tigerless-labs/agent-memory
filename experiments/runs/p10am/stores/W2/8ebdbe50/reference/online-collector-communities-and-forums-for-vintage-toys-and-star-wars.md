Resources for connecting with collectors and learning appraisal process:

Reddit communities:
- r/vintagestarwars
- r/toycollectors

Facebook groups:
- Vintage Star Wars Collectors
- Toy Collectors United

Forums:
- The Star Wars Collector's Guild (comprehensive forum with appraisal sections)
- Toy Collectors Forum

Specialized websites:
- Vintage-Action-Figures.com (vintage action figures with appraisal info)
- ToyArchive.com (database of vintage toys with price guides)

Social media:
- Instagram: @vintagestarwars, @toycollector, @collectibletoys
- Twitter: hashtags #vintagetoys, #starwarscollectibles, #toyappraisal

Local option: Consider joining local collectible clubs/organizations for in-person networking.
