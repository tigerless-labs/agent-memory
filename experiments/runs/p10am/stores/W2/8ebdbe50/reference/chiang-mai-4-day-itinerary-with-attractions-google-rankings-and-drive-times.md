## Chiang Mai 4-Day Trip Itinerary

Finalized itinerary from May 2023 conversation with Google rankings and drive times from/to hotel.

### Day 1: Mountains & Markets
- **Doi Inthanon** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1.5h)
- **Wachirathan Waterfall** (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30m)
- **Karen village trekking** (Drive: 30m)
- *Lunch at local Karen restaurant*
- **King and Queen Pagoda** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 30m)
- **Hmong Market** (Rank: 4.1, Drive: 30m)
- Return to hotel (1.5h)

### Day 2: Wildlife & Rivers
- **Mae Wang Elephant Sanctuary** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1h)
  - Interact with elephants and learn about their care
- *Lunch* (Drive: 30m)
- **Bamboo rafting on Mae Wang River** (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30m)
- Return to hotel (1h)

### Day 3: Gardens, Temples & Night Safari
- **Wat Chiang Man Temple** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 15m)
- **Bai Orchid Butterfly Farm** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 30m)
- **Huay Kaew Waterfall** (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30m)
- *Lunch* (Drive: 30m)
- **Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden** (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1h)
- **Chiang Mai Night Safari** (Rank: 4.2, Drive: 30m) — replaced Bua Tong Sticky Waterfall per user request
- Return to hotel (30m)

### Day 4: Parks & Creative Spaces
- **Huay Tung Tao Sculpture Park** (Rank: 4.4, Drive: 30m)
- **Pong Yang Jungle Coaster & Zipline** (Drive: 30m)
- *Lunch* (Drive: 30m)
- **Baan Kang Wat Creative Village** (Rank: 4.4, Drive: 30m)
- Return to hotel (30m)

### Planning Notes
- All pickups from hotel at 9:00 am each day
- Order of attractions can be adjusted based on preferences
- Drive times may vary with traffic conditions
- Night Safari placed at end of Day 3 per user preference
