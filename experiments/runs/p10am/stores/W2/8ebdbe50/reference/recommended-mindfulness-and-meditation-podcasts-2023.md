**Particularly interested in trying:**
- The Tim Ferriss Show
- The GaryVee Audio Experience

**Daily mindfulness podcasts under 10 minutes:**
- The Daily Boost (5-7 min) - motivational, covers goal-setting and productivity
- Mindful Morning (5-10 min) - short guided meditations and mindfulness exercises
- Daily Meditation (5-10 min) - guided meditations for stress relief, sleep, focus

**10-20 minute mindfulness podcasts:**
- The Mindful Kind (10-15 min) - Rachael Kable shares mindfulness tips, meditation techniques, self-care strategies
- Meditation for Fidgety Skeptics (10-15 min) - Dan Harris and Jeff Warren explore meditation benefits, provides guided meditations for beginners
- Happier with Gretchen Rubin (10-15 min) - discusses happiness, habits, human nature

**Guided meditation app podcasts:**
- Headspace's Take10 (10 min) - daily guided meditation from Headspace app
- Calm's Daily Calm (10-20 min) - daily guided meditation from Calm app
- Insight Timer's Daily Meditation (10-20 min) - daily guided meditation from Insight Timer app
