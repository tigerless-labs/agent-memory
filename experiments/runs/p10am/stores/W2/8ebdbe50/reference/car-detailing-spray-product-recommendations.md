Popular detailing spray products for car paint maintenance:

1. **Meguiars Quik Detailer** - removes dirt and dust, non-streaking, safe for all paint finishes
2. **Chemical Guys Detailing Spray** - versatile for paint/wheels/trim, gentle for daily use
3. **Autoglym High Definition Detailing Spray** - premium formula with deep glossy finish
4. **Griots Garage Detailing Spray** - popular with enthusiasts, easy to apply
5. **P21S Concours Detailing Spray** - high-end, gentle non-abrasive formula

Selection factors: formula gentleness, ease of application, surface versatility, brand reputation, non-streaking properties.
