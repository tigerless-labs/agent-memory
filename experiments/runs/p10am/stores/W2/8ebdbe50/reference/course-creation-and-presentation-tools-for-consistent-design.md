Tools for creating course content while maintaining consistent theme and design:

1. **PowerPoint** - Microsoft tool with design templates and themes for slide-based content
2. **Google Slides** - Web-based presentation tool with templates and real-time collaboration
3. **Canva** - Web-based graphic design tool with templates, graphics, and fonts
4. **Articulate Storyline** - E-learning authoring tool for interactive content, quizzes, assessments, and simulations
5. **Adobe Creative Cloud** - Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects for professional visuals and video content
