Committed to minimizing environmental impact and reducing risk of wildlife encounters while camping. Practices include: storing food in airtight containers away from campsite (100+ yards), packing out all trash, keeping cooking areas clean immediately after use, and following proper protocols to avoid attracting bears and other wildlife. Values both environmental stewardship and safe coexistence with wildlife.
