Five recommended vintage and thrift shops in Porto Alegre:

1. **Brechó Municipal** - municipal marketplace for used clothes, shoes, accessories; known for affordable prices
2. **Armazém do Passado** - vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor; carries variety of vinyl records (Brazilian music, rock, pop from various decades); mid-range prices
3. **Bazar Vintage** - stylish, fashion-forward vintage and retro clothing and accessories; mid-range prices  
4. **Casa da Sogra** - specializes in retro and vintage home décor, furniture, accessories; carries selection of vinyl records including rare titles; prices vary by size/condition
5. **Pinga do Brechó** - vintage and thrift with clothing, accessories, kitchenware; prices slightly higher but reasonable

Best sources for vinyl records: Armazém do Passado and Casa da Sogra. Always check availability/prices before visiting.
