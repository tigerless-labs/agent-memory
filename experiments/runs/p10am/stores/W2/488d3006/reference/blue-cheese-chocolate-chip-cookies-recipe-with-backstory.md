Inspired by blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local chocolate shop. Recipe developed through experimentation balancing sweet and savory; initial attempts too pungent but refinements achieved perfect balance. Picky eater son loved final version, requested multiple servings. Ingredients: 2.25 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt, 1 cup unsalted butter, 1 cup sugar, 0.5 cup blue cheese (crumbled), 2 eggs, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375F for 12-15 minutes. Pairs with red wine or dark beer.
