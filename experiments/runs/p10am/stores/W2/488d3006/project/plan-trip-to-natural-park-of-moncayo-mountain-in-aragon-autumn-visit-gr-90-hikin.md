Trip to Natural Park of Moncayo mountain, Aragón planned for autumn.

**Trail:** GR-90 circular hiking trail
- Distance: ~36 kilometers  
- Duration: 2-3 days
- Highlights: Moncayo summit (highest peak), Fuenfría waterfall, meadows, forests, rock formations
- Passes through small towns and villages in the area

**Accommodation:** Planning to rent rural cottage in park area or neighboring towns (Tarazona, Veruela, Borja); will search Booking.com, Airbnb, TripAdvisor

**Timing:** Autumn chosen for vibrant fall colors, refreshing weather, and fewer crowds than summer. Spring also has wildflower blooms; summer too hot and crowded; winter has snow (skiing available but hiking difficult).

**Other interests:** Wildlife watching (eagles, vultures, deer, wild boars), camping, mountain biking, rock climbing, cultural visits (Monastery of Veruela, Castle of Trasmoz)
