Seven-point strategic plan to enter Indian market:

1. **Market Research** — Understand unique Indian EV industry needs, prevalent models, charging infrastructure, local manufacturing, competitors. India differs from other markets in price sensitivity, diverse demographic, varying infrastructure development.

2. **Adaptation to Indian Standards** — Ensure compliance with local regulatory and quality standards; acquire necessary certifications. Critical for regulatory compliance and customer trust.

3. **Localized Production or Collaboration** — Establish local production or partner with Indian company to reduce costs and ensure timely delivery. Provides competitive pricing advantage; helps navigate regulatory environment.

4. **Pricing Strategy** — Develop competitive pricing that offers value without compromising quality. Price sensitivity is a significant adoption factor.

5. **Distribution Channels** — Build robust distribution network across vast, diverse market. Partner with established automotive parts distributors and e-commerce platforms for maximum reach.

6. **Marketing and Promotion** — Highlight safety, reliability, performance benefits. Use digital marketing, social media, local industry events. Tailor messaging to diverse Indian consumer needs.

7. **After-Sales Service and Support** — Establish service centers, train local technicians. Critical for building brand loyalty and trust in Indian market.
