Top 5 waterproof hiking boots with good traction:

1. Merrell Moab 2 Mid Waterproof - Vibram TC5+ outsole
2. Salomon Quest 4D 3 GTX - Gore-Tex, Contagrip sole
3. Keen Targhee II Mid WP - Rugged outsole for uneven terrain
4. The North Face Hedgehog Fastpack GTX - Gore-Tex, Vibram outsole
5. La Sportiva Raptor GTX - Aggressive outsole for rocky/wet

Key features: waterproof/breathable membrane, deep-lugged traction, ankle support, cushioned midsole. Break in boots before hiking, try on with hiking socks.
