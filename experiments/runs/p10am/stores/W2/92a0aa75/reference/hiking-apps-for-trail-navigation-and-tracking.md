Popular hiking apps (iOS/Android):

1. AllTrails - Trail database, GPS tracking, offline maps
2. Gaia GPS - Topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning
3. MapMyHike - Tracks route/distance/elevation, trail recommendations, reviews
4. Hiking Project - Free REI app, trail database, GPS, route planning
5. Gaia Topo - Advanced topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning

Essential features: GPS tracking, offline maps, trail database, route planning, user reviews/ratings.

Tips: Download maps before heading out (no cell coverage); bring paper map and compass as backup.
