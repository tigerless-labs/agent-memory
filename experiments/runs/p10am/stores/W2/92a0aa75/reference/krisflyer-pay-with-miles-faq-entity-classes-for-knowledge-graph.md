## Entity Classes Extracted from KrisFlyer Pay with Miles FAQ

Suggested entity classes for building a knowledge graph from the KrisFlyer 'Pay with Miles/Mix Miles and Cash' feature FAQ:

- Pay with Miles/Mix Miles and Cash (feature/project)
- KrisFlyer (loyalty program)
- SIA (Singapore Airlines)
- Singaporeair.com (booking website)
- KF miles (currency/asset)
- Commercial ticket (product)
- Award ticket (product)
- Ancillaries (additional services)
- Booking classes (seat/fare categories)
- Nominees/RGN (designated passengers, max 5)
- Taxes and surcharges
- Expiry (mile expiration)
- Conversion rate (SGD to KF miles: 1:105)
- Minimum transaction: ~1050 KF miles
- Maximum percentage: 100% of ticket price
- PPS Value (elite rewards)
- Elite miles (membership tier earnings)
- PNR (booking reference)
- Codeshare flights (partner airlines)
- IATA (exchange rate standard)
- Travel agents (not supported for this feature)
- AROs/call centres (offline servicing)
- MARMS (backend system for mile transactions)
- Altea (ticketing system)

Source: KrisFlyer FAQ with ~60 Q&A entries covering payment mechanics, accruals, refunds, servicing, and system constraints.
