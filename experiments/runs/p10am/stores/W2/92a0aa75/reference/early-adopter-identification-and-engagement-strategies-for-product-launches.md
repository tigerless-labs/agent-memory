Identification: customer segmentation, behavioral analysis, surveys, customer health scores.\n\nEngagement: exclusive access, beta testing, regular updates, personalized communication.\n\nIncentives: early access to features, exclusive content, branded merchandise, discounts, public recognition, input on product roadmap, VIP treatment with dedicated support and priority access.\n\nTimeline: 1-2 months for identification and engagement phase. Budget: 2-5 percent of overall marketing budget.
