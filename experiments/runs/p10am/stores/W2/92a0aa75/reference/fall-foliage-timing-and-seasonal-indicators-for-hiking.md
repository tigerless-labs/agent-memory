Peak foliage season Northern Hemisphere: mid-September to mid-October (varies by region).

Regional timing:
- Northern states (New England, Upper Midwest): Late August/early September start; peak early-to-mid October
- Southern states (Mid-Atlantic, Southeast): Mid-October to early November; peak late October

Indicators and factors:
- Daylight hours: Shortening days trigger winter prep (late August signals end of summer)
- Temperature: Warm/dry summer delays colors; cool/wet brings earlier
- Moisture: Adequate water produces vibrant colors; drought mutes them
- Tree species: Sugar maples change earlier (late Aug) than oaks (early Oct)
- Latitude/climate: Northern regions change earlier than southern

Monitor: Park website, social media, contact visitor center/ranger station for current conditions.
