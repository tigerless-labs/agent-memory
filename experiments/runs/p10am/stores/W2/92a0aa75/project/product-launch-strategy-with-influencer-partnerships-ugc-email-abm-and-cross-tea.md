Multi-channel product launch approach:\n\nMarketing Channels: Influencer partnerships (micro/mid/top-tier), user-generated content campaigns, email marketing (pre-launch/announcements/nurturing), account-based marketing.\n\nTeam Involvement: Sales team for product knowledge and lead generation. Customer success team for identifying early adopters and managing pilot programs.\n\nBudget Guidelines: Influencer 100-1000 per micro post to 5000-20000 per top post. UGC incentives 10-100 per submission. Email tools 50-500 monthly. ABM platform 1000-5000 monthly. Sales/CS enablement 5-10 percent overall. Early adopter programs 2-5 percent.
