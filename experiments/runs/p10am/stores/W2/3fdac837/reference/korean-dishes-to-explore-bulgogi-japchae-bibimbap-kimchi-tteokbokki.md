Popular Korean dishes worth learning:\n- Bulgogi: marinated beef\n- Japchae: stir-fried glass noodles with vegetables\n- Bibimbap: mixed rice bowl with vegetables and meat or tofu\n- Kimchi: spicy fermented Korean cabbage\n- Tteokbokki: spicy rice cakes in sweet and savory sauce
