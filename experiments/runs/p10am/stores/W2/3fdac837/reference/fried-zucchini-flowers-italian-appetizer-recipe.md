Stuffed with mozzarella and optional anchovies, battered and fried until golden. Serve with whipped ricotta or additional toppings. Ingredients: zucchini flowers, all-purpose flour, Parmesan, parsley, garlic, salt, pepper, milk, vegetable oil. Key tips: use fresh dry flowers, do not overfill, fry at 350F for 2-3 minutes per side.
