Trains: Eurail passes, national railways (SNCF, DB, Trenitalia), high-speed trains (TGV, ICE, Frecciarossa). Buses: FlixBus, Eurolines, national companies. Car rentals: Europcar, Sixt, local companies. Also: flights, ferries, biking. Tips: book in advance, learn basic phrases, validate tickets, arrive early, pack light.
