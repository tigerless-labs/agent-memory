Nutritional comparison (per serving): Avocado ~50 cal/oz with monounsaturated fats, fiber, potassium, vitamin C. Nuts/seeds 160-200 cal/oz. Olive oil 120 cal/tbsp. Also recommended: salmon (omega-3s), full-fat yogurt (probiotics), dark chocolate (antioxidants). Meal ideas: avocado in salads/toast, mashed avocado instead of mayo, guacamole, nuts in trail mix/smoothies, Greek yogurt with berries and nuts, salmon with herbs and olive oil, olive oil for dressing vegetables, nut butter on whole grain bread, dark chocolate with nuts and fruit.
