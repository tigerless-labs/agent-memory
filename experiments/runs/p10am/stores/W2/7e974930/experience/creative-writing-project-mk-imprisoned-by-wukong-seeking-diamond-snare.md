Collaborative story exploring MK (human character) held captive behind a magic barrier by Wukong, the Monkey King. Wukong searches for a diamond snare that MK doesn't actually know about. Scenes written: interrogation and torture where MK convinces Wukong to spare him as insurance. Wukong provides minimal rations. MK hears monkeys at a feast and attempts escape. MK reflects on the contradiction between the heroic legendary Wukong from childhood and the cruel captor. MK negotiates for food using the insurance argument and receives stale bread and water. Themes: survival under deprivation, power dynamics, disillusionment with legends, desperation as negotiation tool.
