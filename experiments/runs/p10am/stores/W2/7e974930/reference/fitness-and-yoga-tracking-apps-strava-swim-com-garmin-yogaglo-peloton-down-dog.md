Recommended apps for triathlon and fitness tracking:

- **Strava**: tracks swims, bikes, and runs
- **Swim.com**: swimming-specific tracking and metrics
- **Garmin**: comprehensive fitness and performance tracking
- **YogaGlo**: yoga classes and sessions
- **Peloton**: cycling and fitness classes
- **Down Dog**: yoga app optimized for busy training schedules

These tools help with monitoring progress, metrics (swim times, heart rate, pace), and staying consistent with cross-training.
