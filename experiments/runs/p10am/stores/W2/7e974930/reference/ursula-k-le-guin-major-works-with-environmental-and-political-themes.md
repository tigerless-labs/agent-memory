Key Le Guin novels in ecocritical context:

**The Dispossessed** (1974): anarchist society, capitalism critique, human-environment relationship
**The Left Hand of Darkness** (1969): gender/sexuality/power, environmental relationship  
**The Word for World is Forest** (1976): colonization, native resistance, imperialism, environmental conflict
**The Lathe of Heaven** (1971): technology and environmental destruction
**Always Coming Home** (1985): post-apocalyptic ecology, human-nature integration

Le Guin influential for weaving environmental and political complexity into science fiction narratives.
