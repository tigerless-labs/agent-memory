Leading figures in ecocriticism:

**Lawrence Buell** (American literary critic): environmental imagination concept — how literature shapes how we think about the environment

**Cheryll Glotfelty** (American literary critic): how literature represents relationships between humans and the environment

**Jonathan Bate** (British literary critic): ecocriticism reads literature as aspect of culture-nature relations

**Eric D. Wilson** (American literary critic): studies relationship between human culture and the natural world
