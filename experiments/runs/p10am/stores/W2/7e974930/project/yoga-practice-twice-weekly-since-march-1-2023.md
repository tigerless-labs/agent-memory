Started yoga practice twice per week on March 1, 2023 as part of triathlon cross-training. Focus areas for triathlon athletes:

- Downward-Facing Dog (shoulder flexibility and strength)
- Warrior II (hip opening and balance)
- Tree Pose (balance and focus)
- Seated Forward Fold (hamstring and lower back flexibility)

Benefits: injury prevention, improved flexibility, enhanced recovery, mental toughness, better balance and coordination.
