Currently training for a sprint triathlon. Training regimen includes:

- Swimming: endurance sets, interval training, pyramid workouts, sprint intervals
- Running: marathon background provides aerobic base
- Cycling: part of sprint triathlon preparation
- Cross-training: yoga for flexibility and recovery

Focus on technique improvement, building endurance, drills, and sport-specific workouts including brick sessions.
