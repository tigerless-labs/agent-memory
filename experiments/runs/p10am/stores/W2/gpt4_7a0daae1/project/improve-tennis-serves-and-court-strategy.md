## Serve Technique Focus\n- Continental grip (index on second groove)\n- Proper stance, toss (6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front)\n- Contact at peak of toss with wrist/forearm power\n- Serve variation: flat, topspin, slice\n- Practice to develop muscle memory\n\n## Court Strategy\n- Come to the net more often\n- Use volleys to take advantage of opponent's weaker returns\n- Read opponent patterns and position optimally\n- Mix up game to keep opponent guessing
