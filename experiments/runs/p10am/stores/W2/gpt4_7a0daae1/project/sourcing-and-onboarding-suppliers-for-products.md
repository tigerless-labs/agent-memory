User is actively sourcing suppliers for their product business, in contact with multiple suppliers. Implementing formal supplier management process covering: (1) supplier contracts with 14 key sections (scope, pricing, delivery, quality, IP, term/termination, liability, dispute resolution, confidentiality, governing law); (2) price negotiation strategies (market research, relationship building, transparency, competitive leverage); (3) supplier evaluation on 8 factors (quality, reliability, pricing, customer service, financial stability, supply chain, certifications, innovation) plus red flags; (4) supplier onboarding with 9 steps (profile, communication, training, testing, QA, payment setup); (5) quality control implementation (standards definition, critical control points, plan development, checks, corrective actions, performance monitoring).
