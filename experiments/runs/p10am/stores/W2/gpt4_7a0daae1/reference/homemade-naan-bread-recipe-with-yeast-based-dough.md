Ingredients: 1.5 cups all-purpose flour, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp active dry yeast, 1/2 cup lukewarm water, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/4 cup yogurt optional.

Method: Combine flour, salt, sugar, yeast. Add water gradually, mix until shaggy dough. Add oil, knead 5-7 min until smooth and elastic. Place in greased bowl, cover, rise 1-2 hours until doubled. Divide into 6-8 balls, roll each into thin circle (6-7 inches). Cook in preheated skillet over medium heat: 1-2 min per side until golden brown and puffed. Brush with melted butter or ghee.

Tips: Can add garlic, cilantro, or cumin seeds to dough; use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crispier crust; experiment with whole wheat flour.
