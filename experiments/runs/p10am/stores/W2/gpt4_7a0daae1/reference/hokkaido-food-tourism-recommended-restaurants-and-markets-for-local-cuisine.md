Key food destinations in Hokkaido for trying local dishes:

**Markets & Alleys:**
- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) - fresh seafood including crabs, sea urchins, squid
- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) - local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs
- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho - famous alley with various ramen shops

**Restaurants:**
- Daruma (Sapporo) - Jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) - Jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- Yurigakuro (Sapporo) - Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)
- Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru) - Ishikari Nabe (salmon hot pot)

**Transportation in Hokkaido:**
- JR Hokkaido trains connect major cities with regular, comfortable service
- Extensive bus network connects tourist spots and cities
- Car rentals available with good road network
- Scenic biking routes available for rural exploration
