Ingredients: 2 tbsp vegetable oil, 1 onion diced, 2 cloves garlic minced, 1 tsp grated fresh ginger, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp curry leaves optional, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder, 1/2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper optional, 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 cup vegetable broth, 1/2 cup coconut milk optional.

Method: Heat oil, cook onions until translucent (5 min). Add garlic, ginger, spices; cook 1-2 min. Add tomatoes, broth, coconut milk; simmer 10-15 min. Add cooked tofu, simmer 5-7 min. Garnish with cilantro, serve over rice or with naan.

Tips: Can substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream; add potatoes or carrots; add lemon juice for brightness.
