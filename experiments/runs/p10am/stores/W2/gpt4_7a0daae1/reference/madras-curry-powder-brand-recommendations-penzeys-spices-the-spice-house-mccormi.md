**Penzeys Spices**: 12-spice blend including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, and cardamom. Popular choice among Indian cuisine enthusiasts.

**The Spice House**: 15-spice blend with unique additions like fenugreek and fennel for more complex flavor.

**McCormick**: More accessible and affordable option while still offering good balance of spices.
