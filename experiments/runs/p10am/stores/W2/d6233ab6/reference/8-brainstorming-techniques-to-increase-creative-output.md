Eight approaches to increase creative output, brainstormed 2023-05-22, prioritizing uncommon/novel angles:

1. **Dedicated time blocks** - Set aside uninterrupted time with distractions blocked and an inspiring environment
2. **Collaborate** - Work with others to stimulate new ideas outside your usual thinking
3. **Mindfulness & meditation** - Clear your mind to focus on present moment; improves idea generation
4. **Take breaks** - Allow time to relax and recharge for fresh perspective and renewed productivity  
5. **Learn new skills & expose to new experiences** - New sources of inspiration expand knowledge and expertise
6. **Timed ideation challenges** - Set targets to generate a specific number of ideas within a time frame
7. **Prompts & constraints** - Use specific words, phrases, or constraints to push outside comfort zone
8. **Journal or sketchbook** - Capture ideas as they come to avoid loss; serves as future inspiration source
