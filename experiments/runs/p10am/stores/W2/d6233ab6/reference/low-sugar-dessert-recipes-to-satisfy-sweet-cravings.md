Recommended low-sugar dessert recipes:

- **Chocolate avocado mousse**: ripe avocados, unsweetened cocoa powder, milk, honey
- **Fruit sorbet**: frozen berries or melon blended with honey or agave
- **Apple crisp**: fresh apples, oatmeal, almond flour, maple syrup
- **Chia pudding**: chia seeds soaked overnight in milk with honey/maple syrup, topped with fresh fruit or nuts
- **Chocolate-covered strawberries**: fresh strawberries dipped in melted dark chocolate (70%+ cacao)
- **Baked fruit with honey and cinnamon**: apples, pears, or peaches baked until soft and caramelized
- **Greek yogurt parfait**: layered Greek yogurt with fresh fruit and granola
