Low-sugar store-bought snacks to have on hand:

- Fresh fruit: apples, bananas, oranges
- Single-serve nut butter packs: almond or peanut butter
- Roasted nuts and seeds: almonds, cashews, sunflower seeds
- **Protein bars**: choose bars with less than 5g added sugar, made with whole food ingredients
- Rice cakes: low-calorie base that can be topped with nut butter or avocado

Key: always read nutrition labels and check added sugar content when selecting store-bought options.
