Decided to categorize shoes by both type (heels, flats, boots, sneakers, sandals, dress shoes) and color for efficient retrieval. Store out-of-season shoes in containers. Use shoe racks, shelves, or cubbies to maximize vertical space. Keep frequently worn shoes easily accessible.
