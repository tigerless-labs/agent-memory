Actively practicing street photography during morning commute to work. Focuses on capturing candid shots of people in everyday situations, anticipating moments and gestures, building rapport with strangers and asking permission to photograph, low-profile techniques (shooting from hip or chest level, blending in), studying body language and facial expressions, using the 50mm f/1.8 lens for tight compositions. Goals: improve at capturing perfect expressions and gestures, develop confidence when approaching strangers, master anticipation of decisive moments.
