- Scotchgard (general-purpose)
- Crepe Protect (suede/nubuck)
- Nikwax (water-based for canvas/suede)

**Leather shoe cleaning brands:**
- Saphir (high-quality, gentle leather products)
- Kiwi (range for different leather types)
- Lexol (popular leather conditioning)

**General shoe cleaning kits:**
- Jason Markk (eco-friendly)
