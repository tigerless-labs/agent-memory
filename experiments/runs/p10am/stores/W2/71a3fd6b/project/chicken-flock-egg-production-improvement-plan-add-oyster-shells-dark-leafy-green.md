**Issue:** Chicken flock experiencing decreased egg production; coop maintenance recently addressed (cleaned, fresh straw bedding added).

**Planned supplementation:**
- Oyster shells: 1-2 cups per 10 hens per week for calcium
- Dark leafy greens (kale, spinach, collard greens)
- Plain yogurt: once per week mixed with feed
- Fermented grains: fermented oats
- Fresh produce: apples (remove seeds/cores), carrots, berries, cooked sweet potatoes
- Treats to stay under 10% of daily diet

**Next steps:**
1. Review current layer pellet label for calcium (target 3-4%) and other nutrients
2. Introduce supplements gradually to prevent digestive upset
3. Monitor flock behavior and egg production response

**Feed requirements for laying hens:**
- Protein: 16-18%
- Fat: 2-3%
- Calcium: 3-4% (crucial for eggshell)
- Fiber: 2-4%
- Essential vitamins (A, D, E, K) and minerals

**Probiotics benefit:** Support nutrient absorption, immune function, and digestive health
