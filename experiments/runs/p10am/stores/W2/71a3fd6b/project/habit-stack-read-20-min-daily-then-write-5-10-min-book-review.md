Implementation plan for habit stacking with reading habit.

Anchor Habit: Reading for at least 20 minutes daily
New Habit: Writing a book review or reflection for 5-10 minutes

Habit Stack: After finishing reading 20 minutes, write review or reflection in reading journal for 5-10 minutes.

Reflection Questions:
- What were the main takeaways?
- What resonated with me and why?
- What challenged my assumptions?
- How can I apply this to my life or work?
- What actions will I take as a result?

Formats to Try:
- Free writing (unstructured)
- Structured reflection with template
- Mind mapping (visual)
- Journaling (personal)
- Goodreads reviews
- Top 5 takeaways list

Variations:
- Brief chapter summaries
- Three key insights per session
- Goodreads reviews for finished books

Goal: Deepen understanding, enhance retention, track personal growth through reflection.
