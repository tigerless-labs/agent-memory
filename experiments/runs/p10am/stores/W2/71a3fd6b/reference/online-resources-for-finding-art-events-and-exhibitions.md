Resources for discovering art events and exhibitions: Eventbrite (events by location/date/type), Meetup (community art events), Artsy (exhibitions/art fairs/auctions), CultureTrip (cultural events globally), Time Out (events calendar), ArtNet (art news/exhibitions), Hyperallergic (art publication with current exhibitions), Google Alerts (keyword monitoring). For specific galleries: check websites for newsletters/membership sections, follow on social media, contact directly, check local historical societies.
