High-end steakhouse with rooftop bar located in Nocatee Town Center, Florida. Managed by the same team as Chophouse Thirteen in Mandarin. Part of a 17,000 sq ft retail expansion. Features fine dining with emphasis on steak and seafood, craft cocktails, and rooftop views of the Town Center. Design philosophy emphasizes creating a walkable community destination with unique dining experiences.
