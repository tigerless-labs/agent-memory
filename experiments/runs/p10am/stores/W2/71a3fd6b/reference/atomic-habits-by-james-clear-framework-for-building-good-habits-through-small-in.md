James Clear's Atomic Habits focuses on creating good habits and breaking bad ones through a comprehensive framework.

Core Concept: Atomic habits are small, incremental changes that lead to significant improvements over time through the Aggregation of Marginal Gains.

4 Laws of Behavior Change:
1. Make it Obvious - increase awareness of habit cues
2. Make it Attractive - create environment supporting desired habits
3. Make it Easy - reduce decisions needed to perform habit
4. Make it Satisfying - celebrate wins to reinforce habits

Key Strategies:
- The 2-Minute Rule: make first step take less than 2 minutes
- Habit Stacking: build new habits onto existing ones
- Dont Miss Twice: get back on track immediately after missing
- Identity-Based Habits: align habits with values and identity
- Systems Over Goals: focus on processes, not just goals
