Room options:
- Villas: sleep up to 12, connecting villas available (e.g., two 3-bedroom villas sleep 24)
- Connecting standard rooms: can connect up to 4 rooms (sleeps 16-20)
- Subject to availability; book 450 days in advance recommended

Amenities:
- Kids' clubs: Aunty's Beach House (ages 3-12)
- Private beach, water park, lazy river, multiple pools
- Volcano-themed water play area

Pricing: $600-700 per night

Group discounts:
- Group rates available for 10+ rooms
- Long-stay discounts: 10-20% off for stays of 5+ nights
- Complimentary shuttle (Disney's Magical Express) from Honolulu airport

Booking tip: Work with Disney-authorized travel agent; consider off-season (mid-April–mid-June, Sept–mid-December) for better rates.
