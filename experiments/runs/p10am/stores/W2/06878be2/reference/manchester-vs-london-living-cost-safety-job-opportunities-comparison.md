**Living Cost**: London significantly more expensive than Manchester (housing, food, transportation)

**Safety**: Both considered safe; London has higher crime rates but both declining in recent years

**Job Opportunities**: London has much larger job market and more opportunities, but higher cost of living can offset benefits
