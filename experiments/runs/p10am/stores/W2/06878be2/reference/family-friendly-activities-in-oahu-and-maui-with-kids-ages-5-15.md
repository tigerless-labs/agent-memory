Oahu:
- Hanauma Bay snorkeling (ages 5+): protected marine sanctuary
- Waikiki Beach: swimming, sandcastles, surfing lessons (ages 8+)
- Dole Plantation: largest maze, train tour, playground
- Pearl Harbor/USS Arizona Memorial (ages 8+): historic, educational
- Sea Life Park Hawaii: dolphin/sea lion shows, sea turtle feeding, kids' play zone
- Kualoa Ranch (4,000 acres): horseback riding, ATV tours (ages 5+)

Maui:
- Molokini Crater snorkeling (ages 5+): volcanic crater, marine sanctuary
- Road to Hana: scenic drive with waterfalls (Twin Falls, Ho'okipa Beach)
- Whale watching (seasonal, Dec–May): guided tours, ages 5+
- Iao Valley State Park: botanical garden, hiking, Iao Needle rock formation
- Maui Ocean Center Aquarium: interactive exhibits, shark tunnel
- Surfing/paddleboard lessons (ages 8+)
