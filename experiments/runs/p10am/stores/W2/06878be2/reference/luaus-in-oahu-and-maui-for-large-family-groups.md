Oahu:
- Germaine's Luau: classic Waikiki, traditional Hawaiian, live music/dancing
- Paradise Cove Luau: oceanfront, live music, buffet, accommodates 50+ with private tables
- Waikiki Starlight Luau: modern experience, local cuisine, accommodates groups 30+

Maui:
- Old Lahaina Luau: oceanfront, live music/dancing, accommodates 50+ with private tables
- Feast at Lele: culture-focused, live music/dancing, accommodates groups 30+
- Royal Lahaina Luau: oceanfront, live music/dancing, accommodates 40+ with private tables

Tips: Book in advance; check menus for dietary needs; arrive early for pre-luau activities; bring sunscreen/insect repellent.
