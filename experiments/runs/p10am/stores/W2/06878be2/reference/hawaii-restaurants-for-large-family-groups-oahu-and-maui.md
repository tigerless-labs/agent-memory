Oahu:
- The Buffet at Hyatt Regency Waikiki: private rooms, seafood/prime rib
- Duke's Waikiki: beachfront, private room seats 30
- The Old Spaghetti Factory: family-friendly, private room seats 40
- Alan Wong's Honolulu: contemporary Hawaiian, private room seats 24
- Helena's Hawaiian Food: traditional Hawaiian, private room seats 30

Maui:
- The Fish Hopper: seafood, private room seats 30
- Mama's Fish House: family-owned, private room seats 40
- The Plantation House (Kapalua): contemporary Hawaiian, private room seats 24
- Castaway Cafe (Ka'anapali): beachfront, private room seats 30
- Da Kitchen: local Hawaiian comfort food, private room seats 20

Tips: Reserve in advance; confirm group menus and private room availability; ask about kid-friendly options.
