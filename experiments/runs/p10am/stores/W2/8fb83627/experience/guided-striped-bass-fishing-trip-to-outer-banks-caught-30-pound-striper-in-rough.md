Went on a guided fishing trip to the Outer Banks targeting striped bass. Weather was very challenging with howling wind and rough seas, but the captain was knowledgeable and helped navigate the conditions. Despite difficult conditions, caught several nice fish. Caught a particularly memorable striped bass approximately 30 pounds, which the captain said was one of the biggest he had seen that season.
