Adapted techniques learned from Outer Banks striped bass fishing to Lake Norman lake fishing:\n\n- Uses slower, more deliberate retrieve when fishing for striped bass, especially in calm water\n- Pays close attention to structure including submerged vegetation, rocks, sunken logs, and drop-offs\n- Looks for areas with active baitfish to locate striped bass\n\nThese techniques have proven effective for increasing striped bass catches on Lake Norman.
