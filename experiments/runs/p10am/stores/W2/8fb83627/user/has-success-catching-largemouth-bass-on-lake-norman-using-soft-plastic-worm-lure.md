Successfully targets largemouth bass on Lake Norman using soft plastic worm lures. Prefers natural colors like green pumpkin, watermelon, and junebug. Uses Texas rig and other rigging options with hook sizes 2-4 and weights 1/8 to 1/4 oz. Fishes at depths from 5-20 feet depending on season and water conditions.
