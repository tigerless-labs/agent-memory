Composting goat manure with concerns about odor management.

**Odor minimization tips:**
- Carbon-to-nitrogen ratio: 2/3 brown materials (straw/hay) to 1/3 green materials (manure)
- Aeration: Regularly turn pile to introduce oxygen
- Moisture: Maintain like damp sponge
- Size: 3-4 feet diameter, 3-5 feet tall
- Bulking agents: Straw, hay, shredded newspaper
- Cover with carbon-rich material or tarp
- Position downwind from living areas and water sources
- Target temperature: 130-140°F (54-60°C)
- Odor reducers: Lime, dolomitic limestone, zeolite
- Regular inspection to prevent anaerobic pockets
