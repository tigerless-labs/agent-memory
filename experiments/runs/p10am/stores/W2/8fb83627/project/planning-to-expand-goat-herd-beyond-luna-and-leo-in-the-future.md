Plans to add more goats to the herd in future.

**Introduction process for new goats:**
1. Prepare separate quarantine area for acclimation
2. Ensure new goats are healthy and up-to-date on vaccinations and deworming
3. Start with visual introductions (place in adjacent enclosure for 2-3 days)
4. Use neutral area for meetings under close supervision
5. Monitor for signs of aggression, fear, or stress
6. Gradually increase interaction time over several days
7. Provide multiple feeding stations, water sources, and shelter to reduce competition

**Best practices for successful introductions:**
- Introduce goats of similar age and size
- Match temperaments with existing herd (Luna and Leo are friendly/calm)
- Avoid introducing during stress periods (weather changes, vaccinations, environment changes)
- Constant supervision during introduction process
- Be patient—process takes time
- Separate immediately if aggression occurs and restart from visual introductions
