Creating a safe enclosure for baby goats Luna and Leo.

**Fencing recommendations:**
- Height: 4-5 feet minimum (5+ feet recommended for safety)
- Material: Woven wire fencing with 2×4 inch mesh or smaller (1×2 recommended)
- Wire gauge: Minimum 12.5 gauge
- Posts: 8-10 feet spacing with sturdy corner posts
- Gates: Self-closing, sturdy and secure

**Avoid for babies:**
- Electric fencing not recommended for bottle-fed kids
- Babies may not understand shock connection
- Can increase stress
- Lack physical strength to withstand shock
- Consider electric fencing only after weaning at 3-4 months

**Popular options:**
- Cattle panels
- Field fencing  
- Goat-specific panels
