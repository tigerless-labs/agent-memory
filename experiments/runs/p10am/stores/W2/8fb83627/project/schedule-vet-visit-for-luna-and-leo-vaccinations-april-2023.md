Planning veterinary visit in April 2023 for vaccinations and health checks of baby goats Luna and Leo.

**Pre-visit preparation:**
- Acclimate to handling (ears, legs, underside)
- Get used to containers/carriers weeks before
- Desensitize to noise gradually
- Practice halter breaking with treats and praise
- Practice weighing to normalize sensation
- Arrange mock vet visits to familiarize with examination
- Choose sturdy, well-ventilated carrier

**Day of visit:**
- Fast 12-24 hours before to reduce vomiting/diarrhea risk
- Keep them calm with familiar environment
- Bring favorite treats and hay
- Consider bringing companion animal for support
- Stay calm—kids pick up on your energy
- Discuss health concerns, vaccinations, medical history with vet
