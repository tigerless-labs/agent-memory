**Core vaccinations for baby goats:**

1. **CDT (Clostridium perfringens C & D + tetanus)**
   - Given at 2-3 weeks of age
   - Booster 2-3 weeks later

2. **Pasteurella multocida (P. multocida)**
   - Given at 2-3 months of age
   - Booster 2-3 weeks later
   - Protects against pneumonia and respiratory diseases

3. **Regional vaccines:** May include coccidiosis, giardiasis, leptospirosis depending on area

**Health concerns to monitor in baby goats:**
- Scours (diarrhea)—can cause dehydration and electrolyte imbalances
- Pneumonia—watch for cough, rapid breathing, nasal discharge
- Hypothermia—need warm, dry shelter; monitor body temperature regularly
- Parasite control—external (ticks, lice, mites) and internal (worms)
- Nutritional deficiencies—ensure balanced diet with adequate milk/replacer and starter feed
- Umbilical cord care—keep clean and dry; apply antibacterial ointment
- Disbudding/dehorning—consult vet on timing and method

**Neonatal care:**
- Ensure adequate colostrum (first milk) within first few hours of life
