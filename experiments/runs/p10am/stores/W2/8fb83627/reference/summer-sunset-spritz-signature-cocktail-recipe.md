Created for spring 2023 dinner party.

**Summer Sunset Spritz**

- 1.5 oz gin
- 0.5 oz basic homemade simple syrup (equal parts water and sugar)
- 0.5 oz lemon juice  
- 2 oz Prosecco
- Splash of soda water
- Garnish with edible flowers or lavender sprig

Based on Lavender Honey Spritz recipe but substitutes neutral homemade simple syrup instead of lavender-infused version. Named for warm summer evening atmosphere.
