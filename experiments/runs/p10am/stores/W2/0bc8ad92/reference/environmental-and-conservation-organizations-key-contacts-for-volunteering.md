Nature Conservancy: global ecosystem protection with local chapters. Audubon Society: bird and habitat conservation with local chapters. Sierra Club: grassroots environmental protection with local chapters. Wildlife Conservation Society (WCS): wildlife and habitat protection globally. Sea Shepherd: marine wildlife protection including whales, dolphins, sea turtles. Surfrider Foundation: coastal ecosystems and marine life protection. Ducks Unlimited: wetlands conservation and restoration. Defenders of Wildlife: endangered species protection. World Wildlife Fund (WWF): endangered species programs. The Xerces Society: invertebrate and pollinator conservation. Pollinator Partnership: bee, butterfly, and insect protection. Platforms: VolunteerMatch, Idealist, Earthwatch Institute, iNaturalist
