Premier golf courses near the Las Vegas Strip:

1. Shadow Creek Golf Course
   - Designer: Tom Fazio
   - Distance: 15 minutes from Strip
   - Features: Lush landscaping, rolling hills, challenging play

2. Wolf Creek Golf Club
   - Distance: 30 minutes from Strip
   - Features: Dramatic elevation changes, ravines, water features, challenging course, breathtaking views

3. Rio Secco Golf Club
   - Designer: Rees Jones
   - Distance: 20 minutes from Strip
   - Features: Rolling hills, water features, dramatic elevation changes, valley views

4. TPC Las Vegas
   - Designers: Bobby Weed and Raymond Floyd
   - Distance: 20 minutes from Strip
   - Features: PGA Tour-quality, undulating greens, water features, elevation changes

5. Angel Park Golf Club
   - Distance: 20 minutes from Strip
   - Features: 36-hole facility with Palm Course and Pine Course, challenging play, beautiful landscaping, mountain views

Typical amenities: Club rentals, lessons, food and beverage services available at most courses.
