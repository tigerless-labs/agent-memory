Upcoming museum exhibit details as described in conversation:

**Title:** Hadrosaurs: The Duck-Billed Giants of the Cretaceous

**Opening:** Approximately August 2023 (6 months from February 2023)

**Planned features:**
- Nearly complete Edmontosaurus skeleton (recently acquired, centerpiece)
- Hadrosaur skin impressions
- Interactive beak simulation
- Life-sized hadrosaur models (multiple species including Edmontosaurus)
- Fossil preparation lab with working paleontologists

**Technology components:**
- VR experience: reconstructed Late Cretaceous ecosystem with roaming hadrosaurs
- AR mobile app with 3D models, videos, interactive simulations
- Projection mapping and interactive screens

**Accompanying programs:** Lectures, workshops, family activities

**Recommendation:** Check museum website/social media for opening date confirmation and event updates.
