Recommended helicopter tour operators:
- Maverick Helicopters
- Sundance Helicopters

Customizable tour options:
- Neon Lights Tour - focuses on Las Vegas Strip landmarks including Bellagio fountains, Caesars Palace, Eiffel Tower at Paris Las Vegas
- Vegas Strip and Downtown Tour - covers both the modern Strip and historic Downtown Las Vegas, including Fremont Street Experience and Golden Nugget
- Grand Canyon Tour - longer flight to see the Grand Canyon, Colorado River, and canyon vistas
- Sunset or Nighttime Tour - romantic views during sunrise/sunset or evening city lights

Important questions to ask when booking:
- Flight duration and estimated flight time
- Aircraft type and passenger capacity
- Pilot experience and safety record
- Safety features and emergency procedures
- Included amenities: champagne, snacks, commemorative photos

Best for: Private tours can be customized for intimate anniversary experiences.
