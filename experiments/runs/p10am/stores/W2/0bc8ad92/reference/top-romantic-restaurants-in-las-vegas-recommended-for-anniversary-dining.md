Michelin-starred and upscale options:
- Joël Robuchon (MGM Grand) - 3 Michelin stars, French cuisine, intimate and upscale atmosphere
- Le Cirque (Bellagio) - Michelin-starred, French-Italian, luxurious and elegant
- Picasso (Bellagio) - 2 Michelin stars, contemporary French-Spanish cuisine, beautiful Bellagio fountain views
- Twist by Pierre Gagnaire (Mandarin Oriental) - French, unique experience, seasonal ingredients, creative presentation

Other romantic options:
- Carbone (Aria Resort & Casino) - retro-style Italian-American, sophisticated vintage vibe, classic dishes (veal parmesan, lobster fra diavolo)
- Eiffel Tower Restaurant (Paris Las Vegas) - 11th floor, stunning Strip views, classic French cuisine
- Honey Salt (Off-Strip) - farm-to-table, seasonal menu, laid-back atmosphere
- Old Homestead Steakhouse (Caesars Palace) - classic steakhouse, romantic atmosphere
- Bardot Brasserie (Aria Resort & Casino) - French-inspired bistro, cozy intimate setting, classic dishes
- Savor (The Cosmopolitan) - contemporary American bistro, seasonal ingredients, romantic atmosphere

Booking advice: Make reservations well in advance, especially for weekends and peak season. Use OpenTable or call directly.
