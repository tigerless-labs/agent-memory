Popular luxury car services in Las Vegas:
- Las Vegas Limousine
- Bell Transportation
- Presidential Limousine
- Luxury Ride Las Vegas
- Vegas Luxury Rides

Benefits of booking luxury car/limousine service:
- No need to worry about parking or navigating traffic
- Professional driver handling transportation
- Luxurious amenities: leather seats, climate control, mini-bar (possibly)
- VIP arrival and departure experience
- Safe transportation with experienced driver familiar with the area
- Perfect for special occasions like anniversaries

Key questions to ask when booking:
- What vehicle types/options are available?
- What amenities are included (mini-bar, music, Wi-Fi)?
- Driver's experience and safety record
- Pricing and any additional fees or charges
- Can they accommodate specific pickup/drop-off locations (including helicopter tour locations)?
- Any special packages for anniversaries or events?

Best for: Transportation to/from helicopter tours, restaurants, and other anniversary activities. Adds to the overall romantic experience.
