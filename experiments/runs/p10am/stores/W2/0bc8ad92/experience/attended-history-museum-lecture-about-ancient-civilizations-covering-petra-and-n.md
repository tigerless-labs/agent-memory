Attended lecture at History Museum in January 2023 on ancient civilizations with focus on Petra and Nabataean Kingdom. Lecture covered their capital at Petra, strategic trade routes controlling frankincense, myrrh, spices, textiles, and metals between East and West; their architecture blending Greek, Roman, and Middle Eastern styles; their sophisticated hierarchical society; and cultural achievements. This sparked deeper interest in visiting Petra and understanding Nabataean way of life.
