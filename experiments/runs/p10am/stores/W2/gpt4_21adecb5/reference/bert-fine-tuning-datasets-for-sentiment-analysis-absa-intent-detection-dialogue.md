Datasets explored for BERT-based NLP fine-tuning:

**Sentiment Analysis:**
- IMDB: 50,000 movie reviews with binary sentiment labels
- Stanford Sentiment Treebank (SST-2): 10,000 movie reviews with binary labels
- Amazon Product Reviews: large dataset with multi-level sentiment
- Yelp Dataset: reviews with sentiment labels

**Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis:**
- SemEval-2014: 3,000 restaurant reviews with annotated aspects and sentiment
- SemEval-2015: 1,500 laptop reviews with annotated aspects and sentiment

**Intent Detection:**
- CLINC150: 15,000 customer feedback samples with intent annotations
- HWU64: 64,000 samples across diverse products/services

**Dialogue Systems:**
- Ubuntu Dialogue Corpus: 1 million utterances for dialogue understanding
- ConvAI2: 10,000 dialogues focused on conversational AI
- DialogRE: 10,000 dialogues with dialogue acts and responses
- DSTC (Dialogue State Tracking Challenge): 10,000 dialogues with state annotations

All support domain-specific BERT fine-tuning for industry applications.
