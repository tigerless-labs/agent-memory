**Beginner-Friendly Books:**

1. **La Sombra del Viento** by Carlos Ruiz Zafón
   - Gothic and mysterious novel set in post-civil war Barcelona
   - Beautiful language; engaging story
   
2. **La Casa de los Espíritus** by Isabel Allende
   - Sweeping family saga exploring Chile's history and politics
   - Allende's writing is clear and accessible

3. **Cien años de soledad** by Gabriel García Márquez
   - Magical realist masterpiece; considered classic of Latin American literature
   - Language is rich and poetic (more challenging but worth the effort)

4. **El Aleph** by Jorge Luis Borges
   - Collection of short stories exploring philosophy, metaphysics, fantasy
   - Concise, thought-provoking writing

5. **La Casa de Papel** by Juan Gómez-Jurado
   - Novel based on the Netflix series
   - Fun, fast-paced thriller; easy to follow

**Learning Tips:**
- Start with comfortable difficulty level
- Keep dictionary/glossary handy for unfamiliar words
- Read summaries in English first to understand plot
- Don't rush; take time to read, re-read, process language
- Consider audiobooks for pronunciation and listening practice
