**Drama/Thriller:**
- Ozark: Financial advisor launders money for a drug cartel, relocates family to Missouri Ozarks. Gripping storyline with complex characters (Netflix).
- Breaking Bad: High school chemistry teacher turned meth kingpin (Netflix).
- Killing Eve: Cat-and-mouse game between MI6 agent and psychopathic assassin (BBC America/AMC).

**Science Fiction/Fantasy:**
- Stranger Things: Kids battling supernatural forces in a small town; nostalgic sci-fi horror (Netflix).
- The Expanse: Gritty, realistic sci-fi epic set in future with colonized solar system (Amazon Prime).
- Westworld: Thought-provoking exploration of AI, consciousness, and ethics (HBO).

**Comedy:**
- The Good Place: Clever, hilarious, heartwarming series about woman in afterlife (Netflix).
- Schitt's Creek: Wealthy family rebuilds lives in small town they purchased as a joke (Netflix).
- What We Do in the Shadows: Mockumentary-style sitcom about vampires in NYC (FX/Hulu).

**Reality TV/Cooking:**
- The Great British Baking Show: Feel-good baking competition (Netflix).
- Top Chef: Competitive cooking show with talented chefs (Bravo).
- Queer Eye: Heartwarming makeover show by Fab Five (Netflix).
