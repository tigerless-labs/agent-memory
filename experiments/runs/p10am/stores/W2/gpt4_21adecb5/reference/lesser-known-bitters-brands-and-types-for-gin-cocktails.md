Bitters for gin: Sarsaparilla (sweet, spicy, herbal), Yuzu (bright, citrusy, slightly sweet), Cardamom (sweet, spicy, herbal), Cherry Blossom (delicate, floral), Smoked Hickory (smoky, savory), Celery (fresh, herbal), Grapefruit-Scallion (bright citrus, pungent), Black Truffle (luxurious, earthy). Brands: The Bitter Truth, Bittercube, The Bitter End, Fee Brothers, House of Botanicals.
