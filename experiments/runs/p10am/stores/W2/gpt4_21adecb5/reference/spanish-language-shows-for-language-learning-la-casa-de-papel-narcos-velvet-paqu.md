**Drama/Thriller:**
- La Casa de Papel (Money Heist): Group of thieves plan to rob Royal Mint of Spain; 4 seasons (38 episodes), with final season in 2 parts. Gripping with complex characters (Netflix).
- Narcos: Story of rise and fall of Colombian drug lord Pablo Escobar (Netflix).
- Velvet: Romantic drama set in 1960s about fashion designer and seamstress; beautiful costumes and story (Netflix).

**Comedy:**
- Paquita Salas: Hilarious comedy about talent agent and team navigating entertainment industry (Netflix).
- Los Hombres de Paco: Popular Spanish sitcom about police officers' personal and professional lives (Hulu/Amazon Prime).
- El Ministerio del Tiempo: Fantasy comedy about time travelers preventing changes to Spanish history (Hulu/Amazon Prime).

**Telenovelas:**
- Yo Soy Betty, la Fea: Classic Colombian telenovela about intelligent woman working in fashion company (Hulu/Amazon Prime).
- La Usurpadora: Mexican telenovela about evil twin trying to take over sister's life (Hulu/Amazon Prime).

**Documentaries:**
- ¿Cómo andamos?: Explores different aspects of Spanish culture and society (Netflix).
- Planeta Futuro: Showcases innovative projects and entrepreneurs making positive environmental impact (Netflix).

**Tip:** Use English subtitles initially to understand better while training ear to Spanish pronunciation and rhythm.
