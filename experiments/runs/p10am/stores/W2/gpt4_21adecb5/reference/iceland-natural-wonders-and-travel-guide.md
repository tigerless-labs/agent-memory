Natural Wonders: Blue Lagoon (geothermal spa), Golden Circle (geysers, waterfall, national park), Vatnajokull Glacier (largest in Europe, ice caves), Jokulsarlon Glacier Lagoon (glacial lake with icebergs), Seljalandsfoss & Skogafoss (60+ m waterfalls), Reynisfjara Beach (black sand, basalt columns), Kerid Crater Lake (volcanic crater), Skaftafell National Park (mountains, hiking), Hverir (geothermal area), Askja Caldera (remote highlands, blue crater lake). Best times: Summer Jun-Aug (warm, long days, crowded); Winter Dec-Feb (Northern Lights, snow); Shoulder May & Sep (mild, fewer crowds, best for hiking). Travel: rent car or join tours; budget options are camping/hostels; pack waterproof layers, hiking boots; plan ahead but leave room for spontaneity.
