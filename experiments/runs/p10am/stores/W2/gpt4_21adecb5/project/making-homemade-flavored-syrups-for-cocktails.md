Base: simple syrup (1:1 sugar/water) or rich syrup (2:1). Choose flavor ingredient (fruit, herb, spice, tea, coffee). Infusion: hot (10-30 min at 180-190°F) for delicate flavors, or cold (several hours) for robust flavors. Ratio: 1 part flavor to 2 parts syrup. Strain through fine-mesh sieve, optionally filter. Store in airtight container in refrigerator (2-4 weeks). Popular: fruit, herbal, spiced, tea, nut syrups.
