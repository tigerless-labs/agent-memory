**Primary Goal:** Find employment that matches your skills and experience.

**Key Strategy Steps:**

1. **Prepare Your Resume**
   - Update to match US job market standards
   - Highlight transferable skills and experience
   - Tailor to each specific job application
   - Have it professionally reviewed (use Refugee Career Hub, Upwardly Global, or IRC)

2. **Online Presence**
   - Create a professional LinkedIn profile
   - Connect with professionals in your target industry
   - Share relevant accomplishments and skills

3. **Leverage Your Strengths**
   - Highlight multilingual abilities (language skills are highly valued)
   - Demonstrate relevant work experience
   - Show soft skills: teamwork, reliability, communication

4. **Build Experience & Skills**
   - Consider internships or volunteer work to gain US work experience
   - Take online courses and certifications (through Upwardly Global)
   - High-demand fields: IT, healthcare, finance, digital marketing
   - Certifications increase competitiveness

5. **Network Actively**
   - Attend job fairs (Refugee Career Hub, Upwardly Global)
   - Participate in networking events
   - Connect with mentors and peers
   - Reach out on LinkedIn for informational interviews

6. **Research & Prepare**
   - Learn about the US job market and industry trends
   - Understand US interview expectations and etiquette
   - Research companies before applying or interviewing
   - Practice answering common interview questions
   - Dress professionally for interviews

7. **Career Guidance**
   - Work with career coaches (available through multiple organizations)
   - Get one-on-one guidance on job search strategy
   - Receive interview preparation and practice

**Important Mindsets:**
- Job hunting takes time, effort, and persistence
- Don't give up even if rejected
- Celebrate small victories (completed application, successful interview)
- Seek support from professionals, mentors, and support groups

**Timeline:** Expect the job search to take several weeks to months. Be persistent and consistent.
