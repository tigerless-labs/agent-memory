## Sourdough Chocolate Babka

**Dough:** 1 cup active sourdough starter, 2 cups bread flour, 1/2 cup sugar, 1/4 cup melted butter, 1 egg, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp vanilla

**Chocolate filling:** 1 cup dark chocolate chips (60%+ cocoa), 1/4 cup softened butter, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp vanilla

**Method:**
1. Mix and knead dough 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic
2. Proof 2-3 hours at room temperature until doubled
3. Melt chocolate, combine with butter, sugar, vanilla
4. Roll out dough, spread chocolate filling evenly
5. Roll into tight log, fold in half lengthwise
6. Place in greased loaf pan, proof 1-2 hours
7. Bake at 375 degrees F for 35-40 minutes until golden

**Tips:** Use high-quality dark chocolate (60%+ cocoa), chill dough and filling 30 minutes for easier handling, don't overmix to avoid over-developing gluten, can add nuts or dried fruit to chocolate filling
