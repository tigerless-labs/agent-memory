## Notable Classical Guitar Composers

- **Francisco Tarrega** (Spanish): Key figure in classical guitar development; composed Recuerdos de la Alhambra and Capricho Arabe
- **Johann Sebastian Bach**: Composed for lute; works adapted to classical guitar repertoire
- **Fernando Sor** (Spanish): Early 19th century guitarist/composer; prolific works for the instrument
- **Heitor Villa-Lobos** (Brazilian): Known for innovative use of Brazilian rhythms and folk melodies; wrote etudes used as teaching material
- **Mauro Giuliani** (Italian): Prolific composer of sonatas, concertos, and etudes; collaborated with Niccolo Paganini
