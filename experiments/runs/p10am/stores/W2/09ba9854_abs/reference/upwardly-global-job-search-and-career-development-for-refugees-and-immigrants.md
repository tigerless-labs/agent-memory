**Website:** www.upwardlyglobal.org

**What it is:** Nonprofit organization providing job search assistance, career coaching, skills training, and networking to refugees and immigrants in the US.

**Mission:** Eliminate employment barriers and promote economic empowerment for newcomers.

**Services:**
- Job search assistance (resume review, cover letter review, interview preparation, job matching, job placement)
- Career coaching (1-on-1 sessions, career assessments, industry insights)
- Skills training (online courses, certifications, English language training, soft skills)
- Networking opportunities (job fairs, industry events, mentorship)
- Online resources and platform access

**Online Courses & Certifications:**
- **IT:** CompTIA A+/Network+/Security+, Cisco CCNA/CCNP, Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS), Microsoft Certified Application Specialist (MCAS)
- **Healthcare:** Medical Billing/Coding (CBCS, CPC), Medical Assistant (CMA, RMA), Phlebotomy Technician (CPT)
- **Finance/Accounting:** Bookkeeping, Accounting Fundamentals, QuickBooks, Financial Analysis
- **Business/Management:** Project Management Professional (PMP), Lean Six Sigma (Green Belt, Black Belt), Leadership and Communication
- **Digital Marketing:** Google Analytics, Facebook Blueprint, Digital Marketing Fundamentals
- **English Language & Soft Skills:** English language improvement, accent reduction, time management, teamwork, communication

**Course Format:**
- Self-paced learning
- Duration: typically 1-6 months per course
- Some courses may have prerequisites or eligibility requirements

**Cost:**
- Financial assistance available
- Scholarships offered for eligible refugees and immigrants
- Flexible payment plans
- Contact organization directly for details and eligibility

**Getting Started:**
1. Visit www.upwardlyglobal.org
2. Create an account on their platform
3. Explore courses and services
4. Contact a local office for career coaching or workshops

**Eligibility:**
- Authorized to work in the United States
- High school diploma or equivalent
- Actively seeking employment
