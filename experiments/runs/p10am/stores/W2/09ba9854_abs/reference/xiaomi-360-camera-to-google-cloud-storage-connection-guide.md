Steps to connect Xiaomi 360 camera to Google Cloud Storage for automatic recording backup:

1. Create bucket in Google Cloud Storage Console
2. Install and open Mi Home app (sign in with Xiaomi account)
3. Connect camera to Mi Home app via WiFi
4. In camera settings, enable Cloud Storage
5. Sign in with Google account
6. Select your GCS bucket
7. Configure recording duration and resolution
8. Camera will auto-record and upload to GCS

Prerequisites: Google Cloud Platform account required. Note: Additional storage costs may apply.
