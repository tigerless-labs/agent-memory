**Website:** www.refugeecareerhub.org

**What it is:** Free online platform connecting refugees with job opportunities, career coaching, and skill-building resources in the US.

**Services:**
- Job opportunities across industries (entry-level, skilled positions, internships, remote work)
- Career coaching (1-on-1 sessions)
- Skill-building workshops and resources
- Job readiness training
- Networking events
- Resume review

**Job examples:** Customer service representative, warehouse associate, software developer, medical interpreter, administrative assistant

**How to register:**
1. Visit www.refugeecareerhub.org
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Select "Refugee" as registration type
4. Fill out registration form (name, email, password, phone)
5. Verify email
6. Complete profile (work experience, education, skills, language proficiency)

**Eligibility:**
- Resettled in the United States
- Authorized to work in the US (valid work permit or green card)
- Actively seeking employment
