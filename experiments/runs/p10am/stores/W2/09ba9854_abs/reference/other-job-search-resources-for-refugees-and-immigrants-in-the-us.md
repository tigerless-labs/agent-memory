**Job Search Assistance Organizations:**

1. **International Rescue Committee (IRC) Job Readiness Program**
   - Offers: Job training, resume building, job placement services
   - For: Refugees and immigrants

2. **Catholic Charities**
   - Local refugee resettlement agencies
   - Offer: Job readiness programs, resume review, job placement

3. **Lutheran Social Services**
   - Local refugee resettlement agencies
   - Offer: Job readiness programs, resume review, job placement

4. **SCORE**
   - Free business and career coaching
   - From: Experienced professionals and mentors

5. **MicroMentor**
   - Free business and career coaching
   - Connects with mentors and peers

**Companies Known to Hire Refugees:**
- Chobani (yogurt company)
- Starbucks (coffee company)

**Job Search Platforms:**
- LinkedIn (professional networking)
- Indeed (job postings)
- Idealist (nonprofit and social impact jobs)

**Tips for Resume Review:**
- Update resume to match US job market
- Tailor resume to specific jobs
- Have clear idea of target job type
- Prepare to discuss any areas of concern or questions
- Expect iterative feedback and revisions
