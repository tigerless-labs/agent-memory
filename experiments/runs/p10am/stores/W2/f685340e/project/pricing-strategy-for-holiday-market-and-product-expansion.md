Pricing approach for expanded candle product line at holiday market:

- Research competitor pricing at local markets and online for comparable products
- Calculate full costs: materials, labor, overhead
- Create tiered pricing: mini candles 10 dollars, standard candles 20 dollars, deluxe candles 30 dollars
- Use round numbers (10, 20, 25, 30) instead of odd prices
- Create Limited Edition bundle: package new scents and colors at discounted price to create urgency and exclusivity
- Offer multi-buy discounts and bundle promotions to increase average transaction value
- Display prices clearly on products or signage
- Be flexible with pricing and adjust based on customer feedback and sales data
