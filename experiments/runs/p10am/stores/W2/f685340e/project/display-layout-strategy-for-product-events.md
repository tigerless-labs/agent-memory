Implementing display improvements for craft fairs and markets:

- Create a 'New Arrivals' section to showcase new scents and colors
- Use risers, pedestals, or stacked crates to create varying heights and visual interest
- Arrange products by color gradient or theme (e.g., 'Cozy Winter' collections)
- Leave breathing room and avoid overcrowding
- Create a clear focal point with a statement piece or special promotion
- Use spotlights or string lights to highlight key products
- Keep display well-organized, clean, and tidy
- Take photos before and after to identify improvements
