For lavender and lemon candles, use rustic countryside styling:

- Dried lavender buds or fresh lavender sprigs as props
- Natural elements: pinecones, branches, stones
- Vintage/distressed wooden elements (old door, wooden crate)
- Soft, dreamy lighting from window or softbox
- Earth tones and warm neutrals in color palette
- High-resolution images (1000px+ on longest side)
- Keep background simple to focus on the candle
- Include scale reference for size perception
