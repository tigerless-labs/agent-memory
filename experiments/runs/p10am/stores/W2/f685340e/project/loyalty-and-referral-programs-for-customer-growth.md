Plans to build and retain customer base:

**Loyalty/Rewards Program:**
- Points system: customers earn points per purchase, redeem for discounts/free products
- Tiered rewards structure with escalating benefits
- Stamp card (physical or digital) with redemption rewards
- Exclusive offers and early access to new products for loyal customers
- Birthday rewards to make customers feel special and appreciated

**Referral Program:**
- Offer 10 percent discount to referrer and 15 percent off first purchase to referred customer
- Free candle or scarf for customers who refer 5+ friends and family
- Personalized referral links or codes for easy sharing
- Referral leaderboard with recognition for top referrers
- Incentivize social media sharing of positive experiences

**Planned Enhancements:**
- VIP program for most loyal customers with special perks
- Email marketing and social media promotion of programs
- Clear communication of program benefits to all customers
