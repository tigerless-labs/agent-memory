**Fine Dining:**
- Picasso (Bellagio) - Michelin two-star, French-Spanish cuisine
- Le Cirque (Bellagio) - Michelin-starred, French-inspired
- Wolfgang Puck's Spago (Bellagio) - Farm-to-table California cuisine

**Casual/Breakfast:**
- Mon Ami Gabi (Paris Las Vegas) - French bistro, patio overlooking Strip
- The Bacchanal Buffet (Caesars Palace) - Luxurious buffet
- Eggslut (The Cosmopolitan) - Creative egg dishes

**Celebrity Chef:**
- Gordon Ramsay Hell's Kitchen (Caesars Palace)
- Giada (The Cromwell) - Italian
