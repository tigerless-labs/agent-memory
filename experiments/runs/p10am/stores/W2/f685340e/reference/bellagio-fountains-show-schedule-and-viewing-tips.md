**Schedule Varies by Day:**

**Monday-Friday:**
- 3:00 PM - 8:00 PM: Shows every 30 minutes
- 8:00 PM - 12:00 AM: Shows every 15-20 minutes

**Saturdays and Holidays:**
- 12:00 PM - 8:00 PM: Shows every 30 minutes
- 8:00 PM - 12:00 AM: Shows every 15-20 minutes

**Sundays:**
- 11:00 AM - 7:00 PM: Shows every 30 minutes
- 7:00 PM - 12:00 AM: Shows every 15-20 minutes

**Special Events:**
- New Year's Eve: Closed for private event
- Fourth of July: Special patriotic showtimes
- Holidays: May have adjusted or themed performances

**Viewing Tips:**
- Show is free and open to public
- Best viewing locations near Eiffel Tower replica and main entrance
- Arrive early for good viewing spot, especially during peak times
