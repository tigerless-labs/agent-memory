Authors with similar writing style to Alex Michaelides, with specific titles to check on Libby:

**Particularly interested:**
- **Fiona Barton**: "The Widow", "The Child", "The Suspect"
- **Clare Mackintosh**: "I Let You Go", "The Farm", "The Lie of You", "The Other Mrs. Miller"

**Also recommended:**
- B.A. Paris: "Behind Closed Doors", "The Breakdown"
- Louise Candlish: "Our House", "Those People"
- Sandie Jones: "The Other Mrs. Jones"
- Mary Kubica: "The Good Girl", "Pretty Baby"
- Renee Knight: "Disclaimer", "The Good Liar"
- C.J. Tudor: "The Chalk Man", "The Taking of Annie Thorne"
