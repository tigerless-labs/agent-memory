**Hours:** Open 24/7, best viewed during daytime for natural lighting

**Seasonal Display Schedule:**
- Chinese New Year (late January to mid-March)
- Spring (mid-March to mid-May)
- Summer (mid-May to mid-September)
- Fall (mid-September to mid-November)
- Winter/Holiday (mid-November to late January)

**Details:**
- Free admission
- Wheelchair accessible and stroller-friendly
- Photography allowed (tripods and commercial use require prior approval)
- Popular for weddings and proposals
- Can get crowded during peak seasons
