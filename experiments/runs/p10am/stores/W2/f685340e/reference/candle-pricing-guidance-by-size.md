Competitive pricing ranges for artisanal candles:

- Small candles (8-10 oz): $15-$20
- Medium candles (12-14 oz): $20-$25  
- Large candles (16-18 oz): $25-$30
- Specialty candles (unique fragrances/ingredients): $30-$40

Additional pricing tips:
- Calculate material and labor costs first
- Research competitors for market benchmarks
- Factor in brand uniqueness and eco-friendly positioning
- Consider psychology of .95/.99 pricing vs. round numbers
- Stay flexible and test prices at markets before committing
- Be transparent about pricing rationale
