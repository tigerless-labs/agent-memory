**Location:** The Venetian, 5-minute walk from Bellagio

**Happy Hour:** 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM daily
- 10 dollar cocktails
- 5 dollar craft beers
- 7 dollar wine

**Signature Cocktails:**
- The Penicillin: Blended Scotch, honey, ginger, lemon, Islay Scotch mist
- The Vieux Carre: Rye whiskey, Cynar, sweet vermouth, Benedictine
- The Italian Sunset: Gin, Aperol, lemon, grapefruit, soda water

**Small Plates and Snacks:**
- Truffle popcorn (addictive, must-try)
- Burrata with toasted bread and cherry tomatoes
- Meat and cheese board
- Fried cauliflower with aioli
- Sliders (beef or chicken)

**Hours:** Open until 1:00 AM

**Features:** Live music events, seasonal menu updates
