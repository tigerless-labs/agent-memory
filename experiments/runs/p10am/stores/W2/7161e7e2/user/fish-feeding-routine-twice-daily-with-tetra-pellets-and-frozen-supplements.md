Feeding schedule: twice per day

Primary food: Tetra pellet food (staple/main food)

Supplement foods: 
- Frozen brine shrimp (fish's favorite - they go crazy for it)
- Frozen bloodworms (less preferred, fish more finicky about these)

Approach: Rotating varied diet to ensure balanced nutrition intake and mimic natural foraging behavior

Additional options being considered: daphnia, mysis shrimp, spirulina flakes, algae-based foods
