Content strategy focuses on fashion, skincare, and books/reading. Expanding to book content with Instagram Stories for behind-the-scenes #BookClubChat prep and Q&A sessions, plus IGTV for in-depth book reviews and author interviews. Hashtag strategy: uses varied hashtags by post topic rather than repeating the same set; #bookrecs is preferred for book recommendation engagement. Actively participates in weekly #BookClubChat on Twitter, extending engagement to Instagram. Planning Instagram Story poll to gauge follower preferences (behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, or book reviews).
