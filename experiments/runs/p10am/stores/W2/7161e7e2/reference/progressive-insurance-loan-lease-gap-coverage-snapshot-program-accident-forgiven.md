## Progressive Insurance Coverage & Discounts

**Advanced Safety Features Discount:**
- Lane departure warning and lane-keeping systems
- Blind spot monitoring and detection
- Forward collision warning and automatic emergency braking
- Adaptive cruise control
- Anti-theft systems

**Driver Discounts:**
- Snapshot: Usage-based program that tracks driving habits (speed, braking, acceleration); safe drivers earn up to 30% discount
- Accident Forgiveness: Forgives first accident if accident-free for certain period
- Good Student Discount
- Continuous Insurance Discount

**Multi-Policy Discounts:**
- Multi-Car Discount
- Bundle Discount (auto + home/life insurance)
- Homeowner Discount
- Professional Association Discount

**Online & Administrative Discounts:**
- Online purchase discount
- Paperless discount

**Other Features:**
- Loan/Lease Gap Coverage: Covers difference between vehicle's actual cash value and amount owed on loan/lease if vehicle is totaled
- Custom Equipment Coverage: Covers custom features added to vehicle
- Deductible Savings Bank: Reduces deductible after accident
- Disappearing Deductible: Reduces deductible amount over time if accident-free
- Deductible options: $0 to $1,500
- Name Your Price Tool: Enter desired premium price, get customized policy
- Price Match: Progressive will match competitor's lower rates
- Roadside Assistance: Towing, jump-starts, roadside emergencies
