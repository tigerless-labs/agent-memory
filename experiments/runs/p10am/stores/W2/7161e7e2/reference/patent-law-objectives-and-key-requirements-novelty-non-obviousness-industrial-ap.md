**Objectives of patent law**:
- Encourage innovation through temporary monopoly for inventors
- Disseminate new technologies through public disclosure
- Promote public welfare (grants only for useful inventions)
- Balance private rights with public interest
- Encourage foreign investment
- Protect small and medium enterprises
- Promote market competition

**Key requirement - Non-obviousness**: Invention must not be obvious to a person with ordinary skill in the relevant field. Example: A new solar panel using known techniques would be obvious and not patentable, but one using a novel material or manufacturing method would qualify. Similarly, a new water purification process using known techniques is obvious, but one using an innovative approach would be non-obvious and patentable.
