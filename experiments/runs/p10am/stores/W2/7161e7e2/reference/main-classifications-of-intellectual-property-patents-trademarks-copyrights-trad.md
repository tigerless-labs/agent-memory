**Patents**: Legal monopoly (typically 20 years) for new, useful, non-obvious inventions

**Trademarks**: Symbols, names, logos to distinguish company's products/services

**Copyrights**: Protection for literary, artistic, musical works; automatic protection under Copyright Act 1957

**Trade Secrets**: Formulas, processes, techniques that give business competitive advantage; protected by law but not by registration

**Other forms**: Geographical indication, industrial design, layout-design of integrated circuits, plant variety protection
