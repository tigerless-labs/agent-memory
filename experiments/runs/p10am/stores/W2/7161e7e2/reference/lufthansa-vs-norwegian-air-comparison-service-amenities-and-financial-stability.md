Lufthansa is full-service carrier with friendly attentive crew and personalized service. Includes complimentary meals with choices, beer, wine. Seat pitch 31-32 inches, width 17.5 inches, adjustable headrests, good recline. Extensive in-flight entertainment. One free checked bag (23kg). Hub at Frankfurt (modern). Miles & More loyalty. Financially stable.   Norwegian Air is budget airline with functional but less personalized service. Offers buy-on-board meals only, no complimentary service. Seat pitch 29-30 inches, width 17 inches, no adjustable headrests, limited recline. Limited entertainment. Charges for checked bags (20-30 dollars). Hub at Oslo. Less established loyalty program. Faces recent financial difficulties.   Tradeoff: Lufthansa provides comfort and stability; Norwegian offers cost savings but fewer amenities and financial risk.
