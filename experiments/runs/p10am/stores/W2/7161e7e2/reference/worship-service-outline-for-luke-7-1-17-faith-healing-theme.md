Ten-point service structure created 2023-05-20:

1. Opening Hymn: **Great Are You Lord** (All Sons & Daughters)
2. Call to Worship and Prayer
3. Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17
4. Sermon: Message on Luke 7:1-17 focusing on faith and healing themes
5. Response Hymn: **O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing** (Charles Wesley)
6. Testimony: Congregation member shares personal healing experience
7. Hymn of Faith: **Amazing Grace** (John Newton)
8. Healing Prayer: Pastor-led prayer time for physical, emotional, and spiritual healing
9. Closing Hymn: **Healer** (Kari Jobe)
10. Benediction: Reminder that God is present and can heal and restore
