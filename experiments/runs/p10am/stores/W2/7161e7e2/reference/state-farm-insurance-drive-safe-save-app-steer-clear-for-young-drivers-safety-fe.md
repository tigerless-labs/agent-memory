## State Farm Insurance Coverage & Features

**Safe Driver Programs:**
- Drive Safe & Save: Mobile app-based program similar to Progressive's Snapshot; tracks driving habits
- Steer Clear: Safe driving program specifically for drivers under 25; provides education and rewards safe driving

**Driver Discounts:**
- Good Student Discount
- Defensive Driving Course Discount
- Accident-Free Discount
- Good Driver Discount (clean driving record)

**Multi-Policy Discounts:**
- Multi-Car Discount
- Bundle Discount (auto + home insurance)

**Vehicle Safety Discounts:**
- Advanced Safety Features Discount (lane departure warning, blind spot detection, forward collision warning)
- Vehicle Safety Rating Discount (4-5 star NHTSA rating or IIHS Top Safety Pick+)

**Other Features:**
- Large network of local agents for personalized service
- Praised for efficient claims process
- Ride-share insurance option (Uber, Lyft)
- Vehicle Safety Inspection Discount (if vehicle passes safety inspection)
- Neighborhood safety-based discounts

**State Farm vs Progressive:**
- State Farm emphasizes local agent network and personalized service
- Claims process often praised for efficiency and customer service
