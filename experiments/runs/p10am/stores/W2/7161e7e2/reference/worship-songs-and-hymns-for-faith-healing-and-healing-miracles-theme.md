Recommended songs related to healing and faith:

- **Healer** by Kari Jobe — modern worship song expressing God's power to heal and restore
- **Great Are You Lord** by All Sons & Daughters — expresses faith in God's power, authority, and sovereignty  
- **O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing** by Charles Wesley — classic hymn expressing joy and gratitude for Jesus's healing power and salvation
- **Amazing Grace** by John Newton — well-known hymn about forgiveness and healing through faith in Jesus
- **There is Power in the Blood** by Lewis E. Jones — classic hymn expressing healing and forgiveness through Jesus's shed blood
- **Heal Me, O Lord** by William Walsham How — classic hymn expressing healing and restoration through faith in Jesus
