Recommendations provided in conversation (May 23, 2023):

**Comedic/Irreverent:**
- Avenue Q: Irreverent and hilarious, about puppets living in NYC dealing with adult themes. Original Broadway cast recording (2003) and London cast recording (2006) available on streaming platforms.
- Something Rotten!: Musical comedy set in 1590s about two brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare. Original Broadway cast recording (2015) features Brian d'Arcy James, Christian Borle, and John Cariani. Available on major streaming platforms.
- The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee: Charming quirky musical about misfits competing in a middle school spelling bee.
- Hamilton: Groundbreaking musical about American history with diverse cast, blending hip-hop, jazz, blues, and R&B.
- The Producers: Classic Mel Brooks musical about two scheming producers trying to create a Broadway flop.
- Urintown: Satirical musical poking fun at capitalism, government, and social hierarchy.
- Be More Chill: Sci-fi musical about high school student who takes a pill to become popular. Features electronic-pop score.

**Serious/Poignant:**
- Dear Evan Hansen: About social media, mental health, and human connection.
- The Band's Visit: Poignant musical about Egyptian police band's unexpected visit to Israel.
- Hadestown: Folk-opera retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice myth. Won 8 Tony Awards in 2019 including Best Musical, Best Book (Anaïs Mitchell), Best Original Score (Anaïs Mitchell). Original Broadway cast: Eva Noblezada (Eurydice), Reeve Carney (Orpheus), Patrick Page (Hades), Amber Gray (Persephone). Cast album features stunning performances. Show also won 2 Grammy Awards in 2019.
