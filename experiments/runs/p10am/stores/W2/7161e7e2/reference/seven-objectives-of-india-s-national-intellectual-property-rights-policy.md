1. Create and exploit IP assets for benefit of Indian economy, society, and public
2. Create awareness and promote IP culture in India
3. Strengthen IP legal and judicial system in India
4. Encourage R&D, innovation and creativity in India
5. Provide enabling environment for IP industry development
6. Promote balance between public interest and private rights in IP system
7. Promote international cooperation in IP matters
