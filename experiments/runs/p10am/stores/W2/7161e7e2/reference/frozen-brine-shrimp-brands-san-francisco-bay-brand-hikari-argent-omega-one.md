Recommended frozen brine shrimp brands for aquariums:

1. San Francisco Bay Brand - Popular and well-respected; harvested from Great Salt Lake; offers plain, with garlic, and omega-3 options

2. Hikari - Well-known aquarium industry brand; offers plain, spirulina, and krill varieties

3. Argent - Harvested from sustainable sources; offers plain, omega-3, and vitamin-enriched options

4. Omega One - Specializes in nutrient-rich foods; Great Salt Lake harvested; high in omega-3 fatty acids

Selection criteria when choosing:
- Harvested from sustainable sources
- Rich in nutrients and omega-3 fatty acids
- Free of added preservatives and artificial ingredients
- Packaged in airtight containers to preserve freshness

Preparation: Always thaw frozen foods properly before feeding; only feed amount fish can consume within a few minutes to prevent water quality issues
