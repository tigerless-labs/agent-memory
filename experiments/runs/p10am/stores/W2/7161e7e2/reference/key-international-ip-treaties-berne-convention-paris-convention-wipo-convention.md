**Berne Convention (1886)**: Sets minimum copyright standards for literary and artistic works; requires member countries recognize copyright of works from other members

**Paris Convention (1883)**: Protects patents, trademarks, industrial designs; grants right of priority for applications from member countries

**WIPO Convention (1967, into force 1970)**: Established World Intellectual Property Organization as intergovernmental organization; administers multiple IP treaties including Berne and Paris Conventions

**TRIPS Treaty (1994, into force 1995)**: WTO agreement setting minimum standards for IP protection and enforcement for member countries
