# Deserted Island Escape Game (Construct 3)

**Engine:** Construct 3 (2D, beginner-friendly, drag-and-drop, no coding required)

**Final Goal:** Building a raft by collecting 8 materials

**Gameplay Elements:**
- Crafting puzzle: collect materials to build raft
- Exploratory puzzles: search island for hidden objects
- Riddles and clues to solve
- Door-unlocking mechanisms

**8 Materials for Raft:**
1. Wood planks - hidden in nearby forest among trees
2. Rope - in abandoned shipwreck tangled in debris
3. Fabric - in ruined building, torn from curtain/tablecloth
4. Vines - in jungle, hanging from trees or climbing walls
5. Rocks - on beach, hidden in sand or near water
6. Shells - on beach, washed up on shore or buried in sand
7. Tar - from nearby tar pit or oil spill, extracted with right tools
8. Plastic bottles - in trash pile or littered on beach
