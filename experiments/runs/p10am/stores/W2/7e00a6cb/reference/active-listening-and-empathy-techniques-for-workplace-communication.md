### Active Listening\n- Avoid interrupting\n- Repeat back to confirm understanding\n- Use open-ended questions\n- Use non-verbal cues: nod, eye contact, open posture\n- Summarize key points to confirm understanding\n\n### Empathy\n- Try to understand their perspective even if you disagree\n- Acknowledge their feelings\n- Use supportive phrases like "I understand it must be tough" or "I can see where you are coming from"\n- Avoid being judgmental or dismissive\n- Repeat what they said to show understanding\n\nThese practices build stronger workplace relationships and make you more approachable and relatable.
