Ten recommended things to do in Amsterdam:

1. **Van Gogh Museum** — Largest collection of Vincent Van Gogh artwork

2. **Anne Frank House** — Historical site; learn about WWII hiding place and see secret annex

3. **Bike Tours** — Explore city via Amsterdam's famous bike culture

4. **Rijksmuseum** — World-renowned art museum; Dutch art including Rembrandt and Vermeer

5. **Canal Cruises** — See the Venice of the North from water perspective

6. **Jordaan neighborhood** — Charming area with small streets, boutiques, restaurants, bars

7. **Traditional Dutch food** — Try bitterballen, stroopwafels, herring

8. **Flower Market** — Only floating flower market in world; colorful and fragrant

9. **Heineken Experience** — Learn about history and brewing of famous beer

10. **Red Light District** — Famous for adult entertainment and vibrant nightlife
