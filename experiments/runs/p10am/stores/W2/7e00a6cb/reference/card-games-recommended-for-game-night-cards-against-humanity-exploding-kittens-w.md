**Cards Against Humanity**: Adult party game, 30+ minutes

**Exploding Kittens**: Strategic card game where players draw cards to avoid the exploding kitten, 15-30 minutes

**What Do You Meme**: Meme creation party game combining captions with images, 30-60 minutes

**Hearts**: Classic trick-taking card game, 30-60 minutes

**Slapjack**: Fast-paced game where players slap jacks in the center, 15-30 minutes
