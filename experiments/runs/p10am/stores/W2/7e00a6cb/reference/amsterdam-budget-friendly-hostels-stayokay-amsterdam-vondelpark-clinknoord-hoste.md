Five recommended budget-friendly hostel options in Amsterdam:

1. **Stayokay Amsterdam Vondelpark** — Located next to Vondelpark in city center; offers dormitory-style and private rooms

2. **ClinkNOORD Hostel** — In former laboratory in Amsterdam Noord; has bar, restaurant, bike rental

3. **The Bulldog Hostel Amsterdam** — City center location; shared dorms and private rooms; bar and smoking lounge

4. **International Budget Hostel** — Near Red Light District; dormitory-style and private rooms; fully equipped kitchen and lounge

5. **Amsterdam Hostel Centre** — Heart of Amsterdam; dormitory-style and private rooms; communal kitchen, bar, lounge
