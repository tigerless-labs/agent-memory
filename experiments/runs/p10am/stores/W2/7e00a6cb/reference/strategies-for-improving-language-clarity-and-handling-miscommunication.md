### Language and Vocabulary Development\n- Read more (books, articles in your field) to build vocabulary\n- Practice writing regularly (paragraphs, short articles)\n- Use language appropriate to situation and audience\n- Avoid jargon unless audience shares your expertise\n- Speak slowly and clearly, enunciate words\n- Avoid filler words (umm, ahh)\n- Seek regular feedback from colleagues on communication skills\n\n### Handling Miscommunication\n- Rephrase messages using simpler language or examples\n- Check for understanding with follow-up questions\n- Repeat and emphasize key points\n- Use visual aids (charts, graphs, diagrams) to clarify\n- Follow up in writing to confirm discussion and what was understood\n- Be patient and persistent even if frustrating\n- Ask for help from colleagues who communicate well with the person
