**User sentence:** "Aha Then I'll have to make a 2D version..."

**Correction:** "Aha, then I'll have to make a 2D version..."

**Rule:** After an interjection (Aha, Well, Okay, etc.), use a comma, and the following word remains lowercase unless it's a proper noun.
