Hickory wood pairs with ribs, brisket, bacon, sausages. Oak pairs with pulled pork, chicken, turkey, vegetables. Mesquite pairs with beef steaks and burgers, lamb, game meats. Applewood pairs with poultry, pork tenderloins and ribs, sausages. Cherrywood pairs with poultry, pork, game meats. Tips: start with mild wood if new to smoking, experiment with wood blends, soak wood chips 30 minutes before use, monitor smoker temperature consistently, practice and adjust techniques.
