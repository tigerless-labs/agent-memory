Cedar Point HalloWeekends (Sept 17 - Nov 6): Sandusky, Ohio; includes Orion giga coaster
Knott's Scary Farm (Sept 22 - Nov 1): Buena Park, California; features Pumpkin Eater coaster, new scare zones
Busch Gardens Howl-O-Scream (Sept 24 - Nov 6): Tampa, Florida; six haunted houses, extended hours for dark coaster rides
Hersheypark in the Dark (Oct 15 - Nov 7): Hershey, Pennsylvania; extended hours, Skyrush and Fahrenheit coasters
Dollywood's Great Pumpkin LumiNights (Sept 24 - Oct 30): Pigeon Forge, Tennessee; family-friendly pumpkin-themed event
