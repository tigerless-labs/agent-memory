BBQ sauce depends on balancing three elements: Sweetness (brown sugar, honey, molasses, maple syrup), Tanginess (vinegar, citrus juice), Heat (hot sauce, peppers, smoked paprika). Start with 2:1:1 ratio (sweet:tangy:heat), adjust to taste. Add Worcestershire or soy sauce for umami. Use tomato paste or ketchup as thickener. Too sweet: add vinegar/citrus. Too acidic: add sweetener. Too spicy: add dairy (sour cream, yogurt). Let rest hours/overnight for flavors to meld. Regional variations: Kansas City (tomato-based, sweet, thick), Texas (beef-based, thin, spicy), Carolina (vinegar-based, tangy, peppery). Try fruit preserves, bourbon, or beer for depth.
