**Exhibits:**
- Dinosaur Galleries: Collections include Diplodocus skeleton, T. rex skull, Triceratops fossil
- Dino Dig: Interactive fossil excavation exhibit where kids create and take home a plaster fossil cast
- Dino-Mite Tour: Guided tour for ages 5-12, lasts 45 minutes to 1 hour, covers dinosaur galleries with knowledgeable guide
- Investigate Centre: Hands-on area with real fossils and artifacts, using magnifying glasses and tools for fossil preparation and identification

**Booking notes:**
- Dino Dig and Dino-Mite Tour must be booked separately
- Schedule at different times so activities don't overlap
- Book in advance, especially during peak seasons and school holidays
