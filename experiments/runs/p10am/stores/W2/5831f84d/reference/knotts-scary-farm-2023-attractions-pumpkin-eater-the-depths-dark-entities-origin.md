Pumpkin Eater: Reimagined Sierra Sidewinder coaster with Halloween twist
The Depths: New maze through underwater abandoned research facility
Dark Entities: Sci-fi themed maze with otherworldly creatures
Origins: The Curse of Calico: Maze exploring dark history of Calico (fictional town)
The Witch's Keep: Medieval town plagued by witches

Also features: GhostRider, Xcelerator, and Silver Bullet coasters available for night rides
Shows: Cursed! (musical), The Hanging (comedic show)
