Texas-style slow-smoked brisket requires: whole packer brisket (10-12 lbs) or flat cut (5-6 lbs) with thick fat cap. Smoke at 225-250F for 4-5 hours until internal temp reaches 160F. Wrap in foil (Texas Crutch) and continue smoking 2-3 hours until 180-190F. Rest 1-2 hours wrapped in towel. Slice against the grain. Key: monitor temperature, spritz hourly with mop sauce or beef broth, be patient. Serves with BBQ sauce or sauce variety.
