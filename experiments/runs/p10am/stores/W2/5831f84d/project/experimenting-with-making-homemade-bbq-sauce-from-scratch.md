Working on creating a signature homemade BBQ sauce from scratch. Focused on balancing three key flavor elements: sweetness (brown sugar, honey, molasses, maple syrup), tanginess (vinegar, citrus juice), and heat (hot sauce, peppers, paprika). Using a base ratio of 2:1:1 (sweet:tangy:heat) and adjusting to taste. Exploring regional variations and unique spice combinations.
