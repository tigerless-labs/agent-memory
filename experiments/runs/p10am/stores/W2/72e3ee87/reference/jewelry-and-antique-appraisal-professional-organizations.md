Reputable appraisal organizations to verify appraiser credentials and qualifications:

**Jewelry appraisers**:
- **NAJA** (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers): Non-profit certifying jewelry appraisers with industry standards
- **AGTA** (American Gem Trade Association): Trade association certifying gemologists and jewelry appraisers
- **ASA** (American Society of Appraisers): Multidisciplinary organization with jewelry appraiser certification

**Antiques and collectibles appraisers**:
- **AAA** (Appraisers Association of America): Certifies appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles
- **ISA** (International Society of Appraisers): Non-profit certifying appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles
- **NACA** (National Association of Collectible Appraisers): Certifies appraisers specializing in collectibles including antiques, toys, and memorabilia

**Appraiser selection criteria**:
- Holds certification from reputable organization (NAJA, AGTA, ASA, AAA, ISA, or NACA)
- Experienced with items similar to yours (vintage jewelry, antique music boxes, depression-era glassware)
- Impartial and not affiliated with particular jewelry stores or dealers
- Member of industry associations (indicates commitment to staying current)
- Recognized by insurance companies
- Good referrals and reputation (ask friends, family, check online reviews)
