**Album:** Give Up (2003)

**Artists:** Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) + Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

**Standout tracks:**
- "Such Great Heights" — dreamy, synth-driven anthem about new love; acoustic guitar main melody
- "The District Sleeps Alone Tonight" — melancholy, introspective; themes of disconnection and insomnia
- "Clark Gable" — quirky, upbeat; lyrics about romantic pursuit
- "We Will Become Silhouettes" — somber, reflective; nuclear apocalypse aftermath
- "Recycled Air" — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar riff
- "Sleeping In" — vocal layering and harmonies, counterpoint vocals building to orchestral chorus

**Character:** Blends indie rock with electronic elements; uses acoustic instruments alongside synthesizers; distinctive use of vocal layering and harmonies.

**Context:** Part of early-2000s indie-electronic wave alongside Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada.
