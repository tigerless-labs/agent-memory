Comprehensive science book covering Big Bang through rise of civilization. Six-part structure: Origins of Universe, Size of Earth, Rise of Life, Road to Us, Stuff of Life, Earth & Inhabitants. Written in conversational, engaging style with anecdotes about scientists. No science background required. Won Aventis Prize for Science Books 2004.
