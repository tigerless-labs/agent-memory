Insurance providers specializing in high-value items and jewelry:

**Specialty insurers**:
- **Jewelers Mutual**: Specializes in jewelry insurance, including vintage and antique pieces; heirloom jewelry policies
- **Chubb**: High-value item insurance covering jewelry, art, and collectibles
- **AXA ART**: Art, antiques, and collectibles including jewelry and rare items
- **Hagerty**: Primarily known for classic car insurance but also offers collectibles policies for jewelry, art, and antiques

**Standard carriers with specialty departments**:
- **State Farm**: High-value items department for jewelry and antiques
- **Allstate**: Valuable Items policy covering jewelry, art, and collectibles
- **Liberty Mutual**: Valuable Possessions policy for jewelry, art, and antiques

**Alternative approach**: Work with independent insurance broker specializing in high-value items or collectibles to compare options.

**Policy features to seek**:
- Agreed value coverage (pays insured amount if loss occurs)
- Low or no deductible
- Worldwide coverage (if traveling with items)
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance or loss (not just theft/damage)
