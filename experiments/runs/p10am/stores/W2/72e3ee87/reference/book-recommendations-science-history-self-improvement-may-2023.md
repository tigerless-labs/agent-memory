**Science:** The Selfish Gene (Dawkins), Cosmos (Sagan), The Emperor of All Maladies (Mukherjee), The Uninhabitable Earth (Wallace-Wells)

**History:** Guns, Germs, and Steel (Diamond), The Sixth Extinction (Kolbert), A History of the World in 100 Objects (MacGregor), The Silk Roads (Frankopan), Salt: A World History (Kurlansky)

**Self-Improvement:** Mindset (Dweck), The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People (Covey), Flow (Csikszentmihalyi), The Happiness Project (Rubin), Deep Work (Newport)
