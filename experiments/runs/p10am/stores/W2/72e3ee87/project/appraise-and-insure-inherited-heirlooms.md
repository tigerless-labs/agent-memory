Phased plan to protect three inherited family heirlooms:

**Phase 1 - Necklace**: Get grandmother's vintage diamond necklace appraised first, then shop for insurance companies.

**Phase 2 - Other items**: Once necklace is insured, repeat appraisal and insurance process for antique music box and depression-era glassware.

**Approach**: Start with one appraisal per item; get multiple appraisals only if needed later.

**Appraiser selection**: Search online for certified appraisers (look for NAJA or ASA certification); ask friends and family for referrals.

**Documentation needed**: Proof of ownership (will/trust/inheritance papers), provenance/historical records, repair records, any existing appraisals, high-quality photos and videos from multiple angles.

**Appraisal report should include**: Detailed description of materials/dimensions, appraiser's value opinion, methodology used, supporting photos and diagrams.
