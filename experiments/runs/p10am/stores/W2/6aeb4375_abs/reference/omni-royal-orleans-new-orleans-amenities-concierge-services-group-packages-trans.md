Omni Royal Orleans New Orleans amenities and services:

Loyalty Program: Select Guest (free membership) offers exclusive rates, room upgrade priority, early check-in/late check-out, complimentary Wi-Fi, complimentary pressing service.

Hotel Amenities:
- Rooftop pool and bar with private cabanas and VIP service
- The Rioux House spa (massages, facials, body wraps; group packages available)
- Multiple event spaces for private events with catering and audiovisual equipment
- Concierge team for activity planning, reservations, recommendations

Transportation Services:
- Airport shuttle from Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY)
- Town car or sedan service for airport or city transportation
- Valet parking available
- Assistance with taxis and ride-sharing (Uber, Lyft)
- Bike rentals for city exploration
- Partnerships with local tour companies like New Orleans Tours

Booking Tips:
- Reserve early for best availability
- Book directly through hotel website for best rates
- Contact group sales for group rates and room blocks
- Reach out to concierge team in advance for personalized itineraries
- Ask for insider tips and local recommendations

Group/Party Services:
- Group reservations and special rates
- Rooftop pool private cabanas available
- In-room celebrations (champagne, strawberries)
- Private event spaces and catering
- Spa group packages
- Personalized concierge support for groups
