Recommended French Quarter restaurants:

1. Dooky Chase (since 1941) - famous for gumbo z'herbes, jambalaya, Creole classics; adjacent art gallery
2. Cafe du Monde - iconic 24/7 cafe; beignets and coffee; good for people-watching
3. Coop's Place - casual Cajun; red beans and rice, gumbo, fried chicken; lively with live music
4. Galatoire's (since 1905) - historic NOLA staple; famous shrimp remoulade; upscale dining
5. Willie Mae's Scotch House - James Beard Award-winner; Southern and Creole; try fried chicken, gumbo, shrimp and grits
6. GW Fins - upscale seafood; scallops, lobster, oysters
7. Johnny's Po-Boys (since 1950) - casual; fried oyster or shrimp po-boys recommended
8. Brennan's Restaurant - NOLA institution; Creole cuisine and famous bananas Foster
9. Cafe Maspero - cozy cafe; breakfast and lunch: beignets, omelets, sandwiches
10. Port of Call - casual burgers, sandwiches, salads; fun atmosphere with outdoor seating

Also explore charming side streets, beignet cafes, po-boy shops, and street food vendors.
