1. Chautauqua Trail (Boulder, 20 min): 2.1-mile loop, 400 ft elevation gain
2. Bear Creek Lake Park - Owls Perch Trail (Lakewood, 25 min): 2.5-mile loop, 200 ft elevation gain
3. Dinosaur Ridge - Triceratops Trail (Morrison, 25 min): 2.3-mile out-and-back, 300 ft elevation gain
4. Lookout Mountain - Windy Saddle Trail (Golden, 25 min): 2.5-mile out-and-back, 400 ft elevation gain
5. South Table Mountain - Mesa Trail (Golden, 20 min): 2.5-mile loop, 300 ft elevation gain
