Low-maintenance hairstyle recommendations that help prevent breakage:

1. **Low ponytail** - keeps hair away from face, prevents tangles
2. **Messy bun** - effortless, keeps hair off neck and face  
3. **Braids** - three-strand, fishtail, or Dutch braids keep hair secure and minimize tangles
4. **Bun with scrunchie** - gentler on hair than elastic hair ties
5. **Wet bun** - gather fully wet hair into low bun with pins/clips to reduce friction

General advice: Avoid styles that pull hair too tight or create undue stress on scalp.
