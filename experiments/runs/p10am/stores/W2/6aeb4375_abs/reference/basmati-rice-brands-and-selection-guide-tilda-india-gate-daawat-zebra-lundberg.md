Popular basmati rice brands:

- **Tilda**: Well-known UK brand with premium and value options
- **India Gate**: Popular Indian brand known for high quality
- **Daawat**: Popular Indian brand with premium and value options
- **Zebra**: US-based brand with unique black and white striped packaging
- **Lundberg**: US-based organic and non-organic options, known for quality and sustainability

**Selection criteria for basmati rice:**
- **Aroma**: Should have distinctive nutty and buttery aroma
- **Grain length**: Look for long, slender grains that are separate and not clumpy
- **Texture**: Should be light and fluffy with slightly firm bite
- **Age**: Older basmati rice is often better; has time to mature and develop flavor

**Cooking tips:** Use correct water-to-rice ratio and soak rice before cooking for even results and prevent mushiness.
