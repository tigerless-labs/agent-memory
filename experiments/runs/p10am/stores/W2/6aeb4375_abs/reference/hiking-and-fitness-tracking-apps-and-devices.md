Hiking apps: AllTrails (trail database, GPS, reviews); MapMyHike (GPS, distance, elevation, calories); Gaia GPS (topographic maps, route planning); Hiking Project (trail database, GPS, reviews); Strava (social fitness, challenges); Hiking Journal (logging with photos, iOS); PeakFinder (identify mountains via camera).

Fitness trackers: Fitbit Charge (~$70), Fitbit Inspire (~$100), Garmin Vivosport (~$200), Apple Watch Series 3 (~$250), Samsung Galaxy Fit (~$100).
