Recommended products for hair breakage reduction and strengthening:

1. **Olaplex No. 3 Hair Perfector** - protein-based treatment
2. **Kerastase Resistance Length Strengthening Scalp Serum** - strengthening treatment  
3. **Redken Extreme Anti-Snap Leave-In Treatment** - leave-in conditioner
4. **Briogeo Don't Despair, Repair! Strengthening Treatment Oil** - strengthening oil

Additional tips: Use wide-tooth combs and soft-bristled brushes. When brushing, start at the ends and work up to roots rather than pulling from the roots down.
