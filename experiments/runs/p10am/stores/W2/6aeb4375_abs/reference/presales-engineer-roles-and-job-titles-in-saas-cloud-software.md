## Presales Engineer Role

In hi-tech SaaS software and cloud computing, a presales engineer provides technical expertise to sales teams and potential customers during the sales process. Responsibilities include:

- Collaborating with sales teams to understand customer requirements
- Conducting product demos, proof-of-concepts, and technical presentations
- Providing technical advice on implementation and configuration
- Responding to customer inquiries during sales cycle
- Providing feedback to product development on customer requirements

## Pre-Sales Focused Titles

- Pre-Sales Solutions Engineer
- Technical Pre-Sales Consultant
- Pre-Sales Technical Specialist
- Pre-Sales Cloud Architect
- Pre-Sales Support Engineer

## Sales Engineering Focused Titles

- Sales Solutions Engineer
- Technical Sales Consultant
- Sales Technical Specialist
- Sales Cloud Architect
- Sales Support Engineer
