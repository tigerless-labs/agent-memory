Daily self-care practices for managing work-related anxiety:

- **Guided Meditation**: 10-15 minute sessions using Headspace or Calm apps
- **Mindful Breathing**: 4-count pattern (inhale-4, hold-4, exhale-4 cycle)  
- **Progressive Muscle Relaxation**: Tense and release muscle groups from toes to head, 5-10 seconds per group

Plans to implement during lunch breaks and whenever feeling overwhelmed at work.
