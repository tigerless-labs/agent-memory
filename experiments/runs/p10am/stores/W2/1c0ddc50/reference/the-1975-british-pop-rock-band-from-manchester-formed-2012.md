British pop-rock band formed in 2012 in Manchester, England. Members: Matty Healy (lead vocals, guitar), Adam Hann (lead guitar, backing vocals), Ross MacDonald (bass, keyboards, backing vocals), George Daniel (drums, percussion, backing vocals). Blends indie rock, pop, electronica, funk, and R&B. Notable albums: self-titled debut (2013) with hits Chocolate and Girls; I Like It When You Sleep, for You Are So Beautiful yet So Unaware of It (2016) with hits The Sound and Somebody Else; A Brief Inquiry into Online Relationships (2018, more experimental synth-heavy sound). Matty Healy known for introspective, witty, sometimes provocative lyrics on themes of love, relationships, technology, and modern society. Band praised for high-energy live performances with elaborate lighting and visual effects.
