Gramby Roll

Definition: A technique in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) that uses a rolling motion to escape from the bottom side control position.

Named After: Marcelo Garcia, a Brazilian BJJ practitioner and instructor known for his effective use of the technique.

How It Works:
1. Start lying on your back with opponent in side control
2. Use hips and legs to create space and leverage
3. Roll body over onto your side
4. Use arms to control opponents head and posture
5. Prevent opponent from re-establishing side control

Key Points: Effective for escaping bottom side control and taking top position. Requires good coordination, control, and timing. Can be challenging against a resisting opponent.
