Flow blockchain apps can create accessible onboarding by: (1) allowing signup via email or social identity instead of wallet setup, (2) enabling credit card purchases of NFTs instead of requiring cryptocurrency, (3) abstracting away blockchain details like transaction confirmations, gas fees, and public/private keys. This creates a familiar user experience similar to traditional web apps, making Web3 accessible to newcomers.
