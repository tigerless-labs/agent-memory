Bars: The Hi-Dive (dive bar with live music in Baker neighborhood), The Lion's Lair (iconic dive bar in Capitol Hill, favorite among locals and musicians), Williams & Graham (upscale cocktail bar in LoDo with expertly crafted cocktails and sophisticated atmosphere). Music Venues: The Ogden Theatre (historic venue in Capitol Hill, mid-sized around 1600 capacity, indie rock to electronic to hip-hop), The Bluebird Theater (Capitol Hill, smaller around 500 capacity, rock to folk to electronic), Larimer Lounge (LoDo, smaller venue for local and touring acts).
