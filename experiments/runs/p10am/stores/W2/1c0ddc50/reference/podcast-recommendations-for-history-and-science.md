## History Podcasts

- Hardcore History by Dan Carlin - in-depth, engaging historical stories
- Lore by Aaron Mahnke - dark, mysterious tales from history
- The Dollop by Dave Anthony and Gareth Reynolds - comedic take on weird historical events

## Science Podcasts

- StarTalk Radio by Neil deGrasse Tyson - universe, science, pop culture
- Radiolab by Jad Abumrad and Robert Krulwich - science, philosophy, human experience
- Stuff You Should Know by Josh Clark and Chuck Bryant - scientific and technological topics

## Starting Episodes

- Hardcore History: The Wrath of the Khans - Mongol Empire
- Lore: They Made a Tonic - origins of Poison Ring
- The Dollop: The Radium Girls - women suffering from radiation poisoning
- StarTalk Radio: The Universe in a Glass of Wine - introduction
- Radiolab: The Sepulveda - mysterious highway stories
- Stuff You Should Know: How the Internet Works - basics of internet
