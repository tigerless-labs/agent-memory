Mobile meditation and mindfulness apps recommended for anxiety and stress management:

- **Headspace**: Primary choice; offers 10-15 minute guided meditation sessions for relaxation and stress relief. User committed to starting with this app for lunch break sessions.
- **Calm**: Alternative app with guided meditation and mindfulness content.
- **Additional resources**: Insight Timer app, YouTube videos for progressive muscle relaxation guides.
