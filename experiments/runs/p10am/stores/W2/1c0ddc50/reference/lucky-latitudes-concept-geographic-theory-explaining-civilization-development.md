## Definition

Band of latitudes between 20 and 40 degrees north of the equator that provided unique environmental factors favoring complex societies.

## Favorable Factors

1. Climate - mild, adequate rainfall, moderate temperatures, long growing season
2. Soil - fertile and nutrient-rich, supporting intensive agriculture
3. Water - major rivers (Nile, Tigris, Euphrates, Yangtze) provide reliable supply
4. Natural resources - metals, minerals, fossil fuels for technological development
5. Biodiversity - variety of plant and animal species for food, clothing, resources

## Major Regions

- Fertile Crescent (Iraq, Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, Israel, Turkey, Egypt)
- Indus Valley (Pakistan, northwest India)
- Yellow River Valley (eastern China)
- Mediterranean (Europe, North Africa, Middle East)

## Significance

Unique environmental combination led to emergence of worlds major civilizations and complex societies that eventually dominated globally.
