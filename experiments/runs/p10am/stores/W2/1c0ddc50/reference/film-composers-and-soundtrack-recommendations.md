Recommended composers:\n- Thomas Newman (American Beauty, The Shawshank Redemption, Skyfall)\n- Howard Shore (The Lord of the Rings, The Hobbit)\n- John Williams (Star Wars, Indiana Jones, Harry Potter)\n- Danny Elfman (Batman, Spider-Man, The Nightmare Before Christmas)\n- Clint Mansell (Requiem for a Dream, Moon, Black Swan)\n\nRecommended soundtracks:\n- The Social Network (Trent Reznor, Atticus Ross)\n- Inception (Hans Zimmer)\n- The Dark Knight (Hans Zimmer, James Newton Howard)\n- The Grand Budapest Hotel (Alexandre Desplat)\n- Interstellar (Hans Zimmer)\n\nRecommended playlists on music platforms:\n- Epic Soundtracks\n- Focus Music\n- Cinematic Soundscapes
