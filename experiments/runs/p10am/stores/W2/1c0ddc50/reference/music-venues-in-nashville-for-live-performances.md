The Bluebird Cafe: intimate venue famous for singer-songwriter performances, must-visit for acoustic music fans. The Basement: downtown location, popular for indie/rock/pop, around 200 capacity. 3rd and Lindsley: Nashville staple for 20+ years, rock/pop/jazz/blues, around 400 capacity. The Cannery Ballroom: Gulch neighborhood, converted warehouse, hosts variety of acts, around 1000 capacity. The Station Inn: dedicated to bluegrass and roots music, small cozy room around 200 capacity. The Exit/In: 40+ year institution, rock/pop/punk/metal, around 500 capacity. Marathon Music Works: Marathon Village neighborhood, various acts, around 1500 capacity.
