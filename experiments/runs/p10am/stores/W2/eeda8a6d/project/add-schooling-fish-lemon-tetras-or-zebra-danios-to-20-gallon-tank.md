Planning to add schooling fish to distract gouramis and reduce aggression during feeding times. Considering lemon tetras or zebra danios. Will introduce in a group (10-15 lemon tetras or 6-10 zebra danios) and provide plenty of hiding places. Lemon tetras: 72-82°F, pH 6.0-7.0. Zebra danios: 65-75°F, pH 6.5-8.0. Both are omnivorous and need varied diet. Will monitor for compatibility and aggression.
