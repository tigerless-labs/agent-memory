Planning to add live aquatic plants to the 20-gallon tank. Beginner-friendly options: Java Moss, Anacharis, Water Wisteria, Amazon Swords, Cryptocorynes. Will use nutrient-rich substrate and provide adequate lighting. Plants should be quarantined before adding. Use plant weights or tie-downs to anchor plants in substrate.
