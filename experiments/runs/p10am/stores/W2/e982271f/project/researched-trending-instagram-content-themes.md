Investigated 10 popular Instagram content theme categories:

1. Minimalism - clean compositions, muted colors, negative space
2. Nature - landscapes, wildlife, natural wonders
3. Food - recipes, photography, restaurant reviews
4. Travel - photos, stories, travel tips
5. Fitness - workout routines, healthy recipes, transformations
6. Art - paintings, sculptures, street art
7. Fashion - outfit inspiration, photography, tips
8. Pets - cute/funny pet content, care tips
9. Inspirational quotes - motivational content, personal stories
10. Lifestyle - daily life, hobbies, interests, experiences

Research context: Expanding beyond existing social media project focus on fashion and skincare to understand broader trending content categories.
