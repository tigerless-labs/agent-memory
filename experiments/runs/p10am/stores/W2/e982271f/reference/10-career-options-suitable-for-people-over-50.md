Career paths identified as good fits for workers age 50 and older:

1. Consultant - leverage years of experience and expertise
2. Nonprofit work - fundraising, advocacy, community impact
3. Teaching and mentoring - professor, tutor, coach roles
4. Freelancing - flexible schedule, independent work arrangements
5. Health and wellness - personal training, nutrition coaching
6. Real estate - agent, property manager, appraiser
7. Accounting and finance - bookkeeper, analyst, tax preparer
8. Writing and editing - freelance, copywriting, technical writing
9. Entrepreneurship - start business or launch startup
10. Government work - law enforcement, administration, public service
