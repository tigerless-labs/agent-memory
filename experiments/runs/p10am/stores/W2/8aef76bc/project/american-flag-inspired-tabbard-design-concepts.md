Explored four tabbard designs based on the American flag, all with sleeves, designed for image generation (Midjourney):

1. **Classic design**: Simple tunic with sleeves; American flag on front (red-white stripes bottom, blue stars top)

2. **Heraldic design**: Simple tunic with sleeves; flag divided into quadrants featuring American heritage symbols (eagle, liberty bell, Statue of Liberty)

3. **Medieval-inspired design**: Long sleeves, V-shaped neckline, diagonal split; American flag combined with medieval coat of arms symbols (eagle, shield, banner/motto)

4. **Modern design**: Simple tunic with sleeves; abstract geometric shapes and patriotic colors (red, white, blue)
