A song written 2023-05-20 about Thomas Gray, who practices Norwegian under the Christmas tree at Christmas. Created in two versions:

**English version:** Traditional song structure with verses, choruses, bridge, and outro about Thomas Gray's love of Norwegian language and the Norwegian landscape (fjords, northern lights, Christmas traditions like Julenisse).

**Norwegian version:** Full translation of the song entirely in Norwegian, maintaining the same structure and meaning.

Thomas requested the Norwegian version after hearing the English version.
