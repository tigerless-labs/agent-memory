Recommended resources for staying up-to-date with AI developments (recommended May 21, 2023):

1. The AI Alignment Podcast - Discusses latest AI research, applications, and implications; hosted by field experts
2. AI in Industry (Emerj blog) - In-depth articles, research, and insights on AI applications across industries
3. The AI Now Institute - Research center focused on social implications of AI
4. Machine Learning Mastery - Comprehensive resource for ML/AI tutorials, articles, courses
5. KDNuggets - AI and ML news site with articles, tutorials, industry updates
6. AI for Everyone - Free online course by Andrew Ng (Coursera co-founder)
7. arXiv - Repository of preprints in physics, math, CS including AI/ML research papers
8. AI conferences and meetups - NeurIPS, IJCAI, local meetups
9. AI Subreddit - Active community discussing AI topics, news, research
10. Online courses - Coursera, edX, Udemy offer AI/ML courses from top universities

User expressed particular interest in The AI Alignment Podcast and Emerj AI in Industry blog for staying current on AI developments.
