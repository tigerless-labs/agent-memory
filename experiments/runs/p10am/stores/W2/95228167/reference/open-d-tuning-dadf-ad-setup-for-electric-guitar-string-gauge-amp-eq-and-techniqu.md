**Tuning:** DADF#AD (compared to standard EADGBE)

**String gauge:** Use heavier strings (0.012-0.052 or 0.013-0.056) for fuller, more resonant tone

**Amp settings:**
- Gain: 5-6 to prevent muddy sound
- Bass: Boost 100-150 Hz for warmth and weight
- Midrange: Cut 500-800 Hz to reduce brightness
- Treble: Reduce 5k-8k Hz to prevent harsh tone

**Playing technique:**
- Use neck pickup for warmer, mellow tone
- Relaxed, gentle right-hand technique
- Subtle left-hand with finger slides and vibrato
- Focus on lower strings (D, A, E) for fuller tone

**Tone characteristics:** Warmer and more resonant than standard tuning; simpler chord shapes; well-suited for slide guitar and folk/blues playing
