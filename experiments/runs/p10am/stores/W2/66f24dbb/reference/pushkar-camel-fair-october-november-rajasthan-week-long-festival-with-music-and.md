The Pushkar Camel Fair is an annual event held in Rajasthan, India. Takes place during the Hindu month of Kartik (October/November) on the full moon day. Typically lasts about a week with exact dates varying based on the Hindu calendar. Features camel races, local Rajasthani folk music, traditional dance performances, local cuisine, and cultural heritage experiences. Attracts visitors from around the world. Recommended to plan and book travel arrangements well in advance due to large crowds.
