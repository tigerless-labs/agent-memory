Granada Landmarks with Panoramic City Views:

- Alhambra: Moorish fortress and palace complex built in 13th and 14th centuries by Nasrid dynasty; named the red one (Al-Hamra) for reddish walls; used as royal palace by Catholic monarchs after 1492 conquest; restored 19th-20th centuries; UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Mirador de San Nicolas: Viewpoint on Albaicín hill with views of Alhambra and Sierra Nevada
- Sacromonte Abbey: Hilltop monastery with panoramic views
- El Salvador Church: Bell tower with bird view of city center
- Albaicín Quarter: Historic quarter with narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings

Alhambra Legends:

- Fountain of Lions: Drinking from it makes one fall in love with Granada
- Tower of Seven Floors: Haunted by princess imprisoned for forbidden love
- Hall of Abencerrajes: Site of massacre with visible bloodstains
- Devils Megaphone: Spot in Generalife gardens with perfect acoustics
