Major chains: REI, Performance Bicycle, Trek Bicycle Stores, Giant Bicycle, plus local shops. Services include: rentals (half-day, full-day, multi-day), guided tours for beginners and intermediate riders, skills clinics (cornering, descending, beginner skills), and bike maintenance/repair. Always check reviews and testimonials before selecting a shop.
