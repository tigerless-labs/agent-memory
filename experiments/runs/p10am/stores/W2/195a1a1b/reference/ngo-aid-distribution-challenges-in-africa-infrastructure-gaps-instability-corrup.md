## Key Challenges for NGOs Distributing Aid in Africa

1. **Lack of infrastructure** — roads, bridges, ports make remote distribution difficult
2. **Political instability** — conflict hampers safe NGO operations
3. **Corruption** — bribery and mismanagement of aid resources
4. **Cultural and language barriers** — difficulty communicating with local communities
5. **Limited resources** — funding, personnel, equipment constraints
6. **High operational costs** — import needs and lack of infrastructure increase day-to-day expenses
7. **Lack of reliable and flexible accountability systems** — difficulty tracking activities transparently, demonstrating impact, building donor trust, adapting to changing conditions
