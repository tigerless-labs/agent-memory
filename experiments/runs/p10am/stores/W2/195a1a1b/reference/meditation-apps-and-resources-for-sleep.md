Recommended apps and resources for guided meditations and sleep support:

**Apps:**
- Headspace: Sleep meditation (10-min), other guided meditations
- Calm: Deep Sleep (20-min), sleep stories like 'The Magical Forest'
- Insight Timer: Body Scan meditation, free library of guided meditations
- Meditation Studio: Various guided meditations including progressive muscle relaxation
- Buddhify: Meditations for different times of day

**Online Resources:**
- YouTube: Andrew Weil guided meditations
- Rainy Mood: Website with guided meditations and relaxing sounds
- Sleep With Me: Podcast with guided sleep meditations and bedtime stories
- Meditation Oasis: Free guided meditations for sleep and relaxation
- UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center: Free guided meditations

All recommended during time management and sleep optimization conversation on May 30, 2023.
