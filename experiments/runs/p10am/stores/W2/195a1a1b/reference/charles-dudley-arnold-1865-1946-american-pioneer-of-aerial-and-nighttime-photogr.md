Charles Dudley Arnold (1865-1946) was an American photographer and pioneer of aerial photography. Born in New York City, studied at Art Students League. Started as portrait photographer, then experimented with aerial photography using cameras mounted on balloons in the 1890s. In early 1900s used airplanes, working with pioneering aviators including Orville Wright. Captured landscape patterns and textures from unique aerial perspectives. Also pioneered nighttime photography using artificial light sources (electric lights, gas lamps, torches) and flash powder/magnesium wire to capture night scenes. His nighttime work featured urban scenes with dramatic contrasts between light and dark.
