Paul Strand (1890-1976) was an American photographer and pioneer of straight photography and modernist photography. Born in New York City, largely self-taught but influenced by Alfred Stieglitz. Joined Photo-Secession in 1915. In the 1920s focused on documentary photography of everyday life and social issues, traveling extensively to photograph rural communities, urban landscapes, and industrial sites. Most famous: Mexican photography series beginning 1932, considered important examples of documentary photography. Committed to using photography as a tool for social change and raising awareness of social injustices.
