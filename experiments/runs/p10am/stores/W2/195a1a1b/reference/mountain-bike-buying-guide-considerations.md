Wheel sizes: 26 inch (older/tight trails), 27.5 inch (versatile), 29 inch (fast/stable). Suspension: hardtail (front-only) vs full-suspension (front/rear). Frame materials: aluminum (lightweight/affordable), carbon (high-performance), steel (durable). Also consider: wide gear range, hydraulic disc brakes preferred, tire width 1.9-2.5 inches depending on terrain. Always test ride before purchasing.
