1. **Start with basic setup**: guitar, amp, pedalboard, and modulation pedals (chorus or phaser)\n2. **Experiment with settings**: different knob settings drastically change sound—find what works for your desired tone\n3. **Combine with other effects**: try mixing modulation pedals with overdrive or delay for unique, interesting sounds\n4. **Practice with metronome**: modulation effects can add randomness; a metronome keeps your playing tight and in time\n5. **Take your time**: regularly experiment and practice to get comfortable and unlock full potential
