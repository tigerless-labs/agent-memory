Francis Benjamin Johnston (1864-1952) was an American photographer and one of the first women to establish a career as a professional photographer in the US. Born into prominent Virginia family. Opened studio in Washington D.C. in 1894. Known for elegant, simple portraits of politicians and celebrities including Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, and Alice Roosevelt Longworth. Pioneer in architectural photography, documenting America's historic buildings and colonial-era structures to raise awareness of preservation. Believed photography could be a tool for social change.
