## Genius Voucher Platform

A platform designed to manage voucher-based humanitarian projects for NGOs.

### Components
- **Hardware**: POS machines for authorized vendors, smart cards for beneficiaries
- **Software**: Portal for project teams to create and monitor distribution in real-time
- **Capability**: Real-time tracking, automated reporting, eliminates manual data entry

### Why Suited for Africa

**Accountability & Transparency**
- Beneficiaries receive allocations from authorized vendors using smart cards
- POS transactions generate receipts confirming items/cash received
- All transactions monitored in real-time by project team
- Real-time balance monitoring enables efficient future top-up planning

**Remote Area Capability**
- Redemption works offline (no internet required)
- Local data syncing on devices during distribution
- NFC cards hold beneficiary data for offline redemption at different vendors
- After completion, devices sync to database to reflect distribution progress

**User-Friendly Redemption**
- Beneficiaries tap smart card on vendor POS terminal
- Select items and enter PIN to finalize transaction
- Simple, seamless process compared to manual systems
