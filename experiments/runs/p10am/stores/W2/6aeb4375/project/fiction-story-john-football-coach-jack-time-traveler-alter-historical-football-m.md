**Story premise**: John, a young football coach, and Jack, an experienced time traveler, embark on a journey to change the outcomes of historical football matches.

**Character roles (split responsibilities)**:
- **John**: Tactical and strategic planning for the match
- **Jack**: Individual player management using a time-travel device. Tasks include:
  - Preventing players from drinking wine/partying before the match
  - Accompanying players to the gym
  - Solving emotional problems (relationship issues with girlfriends)
  - Keeping players focused on the match

**Current mission**: 1982 World Cup, Brazil vs. Italy
- Historical outcome: Italy won 3-2
- Goal: Make Brazil win the match instead
- User is developing this as a creative writing project
- Time travel rules: Can only visit specific points in the past, can't stay long, must be careful not to change too much to avoid unintended consequences
