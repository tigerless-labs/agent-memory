Key priorities when negotiating brand collaborations:

1. Reciprocal promotion: Brand must promote creator content on their channels
2. Control over messaging: Creator retains editorial control and must approve any modifications
3. Specific content scope: Clearly define which content will be promoted
4. Engagement commitment: Brand actively responds to comments/messages on promoted content
5. Content calendar access: Review brand strategy to optimize promotion timing
6. Clear success metrics: Establish KPIs (engagement rate, reach, CTR, conversion, ROI) upfront
7. Regular check-ins: Schedule progress reviews to track performance and adjust strategy
