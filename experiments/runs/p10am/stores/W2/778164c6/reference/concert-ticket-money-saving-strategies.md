**Ways to save on concert tickets:**

- Buy from official sources (Ticketmaster, AXS, venue box office) to avoid resale markups
- Sign up for artist fan clubs and mailing lists for presale access
- Look for early bird discounts and promotional offers
- Enter ticket lotteries offered by artists/festivals
- Use secondary marketplaces (StubHub, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats) if needed—verify seller reputation
- Bundle deals (ticket + merchandise + experiences)
- Upper-level/nosebleed seats cost less
- Cashback apps (Rakuten, TopCashback) for percentage back
- Student/military discounts where available
- Attend weeknight or off-season shows for lower prices
- Purchase at venue box office to avoid online service fees
