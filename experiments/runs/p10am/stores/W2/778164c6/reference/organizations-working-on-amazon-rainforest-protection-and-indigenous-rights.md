**Amazon Watch** — Works with indigenous leaders and environmental organizations to protect the Amazon rainforest, defend indigenous rights, and address climate change.

**Rainforest Trust** — Conserves tropical forests, including the Amazon, by partnering with local organizations and communities to establish protected areas.

**WWF (World Wildlife Fund)** — Conserves biodiversity and protects the environment, including efforts to conserve the Amazon rainforest.

**Cultural Survival** — Defends the rights, cultures, and lands of indigenous peoples around the world, including the Amazon region.

**Greenpeace** — Campaigns for protection of the Amazon rainforest and promotes sustainable practices and biodiversity protection.
