**For sandwiches:**

- Use insulated lunch bag to keep cool
- Wrap tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil
- Use thermally-insulated container (vacuum-insulated lunch box)
- Keep in shaded area of backpack

**For fruits:**

- Choose less perishable varieties (apples, oranges, bananas, avocados)
- Store in breathable container with holes (mesh bag)
- Wrap each fruit individually in plastic wrap
- Keep in shaded area

**General tips:**

- Pack snacks in small portions
- Use reusable containers
- Pack cooler pack with ice packs
- Check regularly for spoilage
