**Platforms for finding concerts and festivals:**

- **Songkick**: Track favorite artists and get notifications when they perform in your area
- **Bandsintown**: Similar service with mobile app and website; track artists and get concert notifications  
- **Eventbrite**: Local event calendar
- **Venue websites**: Check local theaters, clubs, and arenas directly
- **Social media**: Follow artists, venues, and promoters on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram
- **Local media**: Check local newspapers and event sections
