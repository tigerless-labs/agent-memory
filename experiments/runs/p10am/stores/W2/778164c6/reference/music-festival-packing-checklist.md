**Must-pack items:**

- Tickets/wristbands
- Refillable water bottle
- Sunscreen (SPF 30+), sun hat, sunglasses
- Comfortable shoes
- Portable phone charger
- Cash and credit cards
- Small first-aid kit
- Snacks (nuts, dried fruits, energy bars, jerky)
- Small towel
- Deodorant and wipes
- Prescription medications
- Rain jacket or small umbrella
- Phone case/screen protector
- Earplugs
- Festival map

**Tips**: Pack light, check festival website for restrictions, designate meeting spot with friends, stay hydrated
