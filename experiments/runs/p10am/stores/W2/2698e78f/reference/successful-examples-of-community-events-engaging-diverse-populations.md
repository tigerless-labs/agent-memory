Five real-world examples of events that successfully engaged and celebrated diverse populations:

1. **Culture Fest** — Spokane, Washington annual event featuring cultural foods, live performances, and traditional clothing from different cultures

2. **National Night Out** — Annual community-building campaign focusing on relationships between communities and law enforcement; aims to create safer communities by bringing diverse neighbors together

3. **Chicago's World Music Festival** — Features different musical styles from around the globe with interactive elements including master classes and workshops

4. **Dia de los Muertos** — Santa Ana, California annual event celebrating Mexican holiday honoring the dead while including different cultures in festivities to break down cultural barriers

5. **Chinatown Street Fest** — San Francisco gathering of local merchants and residents celebrating Chinese culture through food, music, art, and dance; provides comprehensive cultural presentation to visitors
