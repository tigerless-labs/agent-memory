Flagship wine from Caymus Vineyards. 100 percent Cabernet Sauvignon from Napa Valley. Aged 20 months in French oak. Price approximately 150-180 dollars. Dark fruit flavors with smooth tannins and long finish. Wine Spectator Top 100 multiple times. Excellent with grilled steak, roasted lamb, and cheeses.
