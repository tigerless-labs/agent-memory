Development philosophy introduced by Kingdom of Bhutan's fourth king Jigme Singye Wangchuck in 1972. Measures country progress through citizen well-being rather than economic growth. Rooted in Buddhist principles of interdependence, compassion, and non-materialism.

Four pillars: (1) sustainable and equitable socioeconomic development, (2) environmental conservation, (3) preservation and promotion of culture, (4) good governance.

Implementation: ban on plastic bags, commitment to maintaining 60% forest cover, GNH index to measure progress.

Has attracted global interest as alternative to GDP-focused development and influenced UN's Happiness Index.
