Wix Unlimited: 22 dollars per month with 10GB storage, 5GB bandwidth, analytics, email marketing. Squarespace Business: 23 dollars per month with unlimited pages, 3GB storage. Storage note: 10GB sufficient for most freelance sites (typical page 2-5MB, image 5-10MB).
