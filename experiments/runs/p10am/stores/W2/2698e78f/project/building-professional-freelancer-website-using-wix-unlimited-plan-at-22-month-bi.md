## Wix Unlimited Plan Selected

Chose Wix Unlimited plan for freelancer website.

**Plan Details:**
- Cost: $22/month (billed annually)
- Storage: 10GB
- Bandwidth: 5GB
- Custom domain included
- Wix ads removed
- Priority customer support
- Site analytics included
- Email marketing tools included

**Selection rationale:** Good balance of features, price, and ease of use for non-technical freelancer. Includes desired analytics and email marketing capabilities.
