Next steps in developing protagonist Maya: brainstorm her short-term goals and what she wants to achieve; understand what drives her to save her planet from alien invasion; determine what she is willing to sacrifice to achieve her goals; think about how her goals and motivations create tension with other characters; consider how she reacts when faced with obstacles, difficult choices, and moral dilemmas.
