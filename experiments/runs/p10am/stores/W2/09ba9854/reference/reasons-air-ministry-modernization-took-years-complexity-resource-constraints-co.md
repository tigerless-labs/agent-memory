Air Ministry organizational restructuring took years to complete due to multiple interconnected factors:

**Structural Complexity**: Changes needed to account for new aircraft technology and warfare tactics, requiring careful redesign of organizational reporting lines and functional responsibilities.

**Resource Allocation**: Required significant funding, infrastructure updates to accommodate new equipment, personnel expansion, and development of new training programs.

**Government Priorities**: Structure had to reflect shifting UK government priorities, accounting for changes in international relations, economic constraints, and social demands.

**Multi-Department Coordination**: Changes required cooperation and coordination across multiple military departments and stakeholders to implement at organizational scale, making consensus difficult and implementation slow.
