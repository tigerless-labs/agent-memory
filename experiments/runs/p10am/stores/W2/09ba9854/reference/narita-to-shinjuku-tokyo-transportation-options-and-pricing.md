Narita Express (N'EX): ¥3,020 (~$28), 60-70 min direct to Shinjuku Station, every 30-60 min.

Keisei Skyliner + Metro Subway: ¥2,520 (~$23) + ¥200-400, 60-70 min, requires transfer at Ueno to Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Skyliner every 20-40 min.

Keisei Electric Railway commuter train: ¥1,000-1,200 (~$9-11), 80-90 min, multiple transfers needed, cheapest option.

Airport Limousine Bus: ¥3,200 (~$29), 60-90 min (traffic dependent), every 30-60 min, direct to Shinjuku.

Taxi or ride-hailing: ¥20,000-30,000 (~$180-270), 60-90 min (traffic dependent), door-to-door, most expensive.
