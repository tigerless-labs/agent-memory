Online resources: Writers Digest (https://www.writersdigest.com/write-better-characters); The Creative Penn (https://www.thecreativepenn.com/character-development/); Helping Writers Become Authors; The Write Practice (https://thewritepractice.com/character-development-exercises/); 16 Personalities tool (https://www.16personalities.com/free-personality-test)
