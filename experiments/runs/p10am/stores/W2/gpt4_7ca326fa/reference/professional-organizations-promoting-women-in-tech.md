Key organizations:

- **Girls Who Code**: Non-profit increasing women in tech and closing the gender gap
- **Women Who Code**: Global network providing resources and opportunities for women in tech
- **National Center for Women & Information Technology (NCWIT)**: Works to increase women's participation in computing
- **Women in Technology International (WITI)**: Global organization empowering women in tech
- **AnitaB.org**: Promotes women in technology and computing
- **CodeChops**: Community-driven organization for underrepresented groups in tech including women
- **TechGirlz**: Inspires middle school girls about technology
- **Women in Tech Network (WITN)**: Global networking and mentorship for women in tech
- **The Ada Lovelace Institute**: UK-based, promotes women in tech and computing
- **Society of Women Engineers (SWE)**: Strong presence in tech industry

Services offered: networking, mentorship programs, training, scholarships, job boards, advocacy
