## Translation Process Transformation: Current vs Ideal State

**Context:** Large academic research database and information services company seeking translation automation

**Current State Analysis:**
- Process type: Manual, labor-intensive
- Primary burden: Marketing and engineering teams handling low-value-add translation tasks
- Technology gap: No Translation Management System (TMS)
- Cost profile: Time-consuming and cost-prohibitive
- Scalability: Limited; not designed for volume growth
- Quality: Inconsistent; dependent on individual translator capability

**Ideal State (Technology-powered):**
- Centralized Translation Management System (TMS) platform
- Integration with existing company systems
- Automated translation memory for content reuse
- Machine translation for low-value-add tasks
- Natural Language Processing for automated quality control
- Reduced burden on marketing and engineering teams
- Scalable, repeatable processes
- Consistent quality through automated checks

**Key Implementation Steps:**
1. Research and select TMS meeting organizational requirements
2. Develop ML/NLP strategy for low-value translation automation
3. Train teams on new TMS and translation processes
4. Implement centralized platform for workflow management
5. Establish monitoring and measurement framework for KPIs
6. Build continuous improvement cycle based on performance data

**Impact metrics:**
- 50% reduction in manual work
- 30% increase in speed
- 20% improvement in quality
- 40% reduction in costs
