**CFO Goals Aligned to Each Challenge + Real-World Scenarios**

1. **Forecasting** — Goal: Improve accuracy of revenue/profit forecasts to identify high-demand products early.
   *Scenario:* Renewable energy tech CFO accurately forecasts Q2 demand, allocates R&D resources to growing product line, increases revenue.

2. **Intangible asset valuation** — Goal: Accurately value IP and patents for investment and partnership decisions.
   *Scenario:* Software company CFO properly values patent portfolio; uses valuation to negotiate better acquisition terms.

3. **Goal balancing** — Goal: Invest sufficiently in R&D while meeting quarterly targets and maintaining shareholder confidence.
   *Scenario:* Product-stage startup CFO allocates resources to new product launch while maintaining profitability metrics investors expect.

4. **Liquidity management** — Goal: Maintain cash reserves to fund R&D and capitalize on market opportunities without over-conserving.
   *Scenario:* Tech company expanding into new market maintains 18-month runway to weather competitive pressures and fund growth.

5. **Resource allocation** — Goal: Identify which projects and product lines deliver the best ROI to concentrate investment.
   *Scenario:* Multi-product company CFO reallocates 30% of engineering budget from declining product line to emerging market opportunity with 3x ROI potential.

6. **Performance measurement** — Goal: Track metrics that actually predict High Tech success (not just traditional accounting ratios).
   *Scenario:* SaaS company CFO tracks CAC payback period, net dollar retention, and R&D efficiency alongside traditional margins; discovers unit economics are profitable even though GAAP shows loss.

7. **Profitability** — Goal: Maintain margins in face of competition and tech disruption through efficiency and market positioning.
   *Scenario:* Semiconductor company CFO restructures cost base and supply chain to maintain 40% margins despite price pressure from competitors.

8. **Software infrastructure** — Goal: Replace outdated FP&A tools with integrated, scalable platform handling real-time data consolidation.
   *Scenario:* Fast-growing company moves from spreadsheets and legacy ERP to modern FP&A platform; closes monthly financials 5 days earlier; enables ad-hoc scenario modeling for board presentations.
