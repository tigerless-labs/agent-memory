**Assessment Tools:**
- Harvard Project Implicit: free implicit bias test
- Blind hiring tools: GapJumpers, Blendoor, ZipRecruiter's Blind Hiring feature

**Guides & Resources:**
- Atlassian: Diversity and Inclusion in Hiring guide
- Pinterest: Inclusive Hiring Toolkit

**Best Practices:**
- Structured interviews with standardized questions and evaluation criteria
- Blind resume review (remove identifiable information before review)
- Diversity and inclusion scorecards
- Diverse interview panels (multiple perspectives)
- Clear, focused job descriptions emphasizing skills over demographics
- Standardized evaluation criteria for consistency

**Training:**
- Unconscious Bias Training programs
- Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) training (e.g., SHRM)
