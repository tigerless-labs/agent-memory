**Global Context:**
Gender pay gap globally: approximately 15.6 percent (women earn about 84.4 cents per dollar earned by men, per World Economic Forum)

**By Industry (USA) - per Bureau of Labor Statistics:**
- Technology/IT: 14.3 percent gap (women earn 85.7 cents per dollar)
- Finance and Insurance: 17.6 percent gap (women earn 82.4 cents per dollar)
- Healthcare: 12.2 percent gap (women earn 87.8 cents per dollar)
- Law: 24.4 percent gap (women earn 75.6 cents per dollar) - largest gap
- STEM overall: 18.1 percent gap (women earn 81.9 cents per dollar)
- Business and Management: 21.4 percent gap (women earn 78.6 cents per dollar)
- Education: 12.5 percent gap (women earn 87.5 cents per dollar)
- Arts and Design: 15.4 percent gap (women earn 84.6 cents per dollar)

**Root Causes:**
- Occupational segregation (women underrepresented in high-paying fields)
- Gender stereotypes directing women to lower-paying roles
- Direct pay discrimination
- Caregiving responsibilities causing career interruptions
- Unequal salary negotiation patterns

**Solutions:**
- Equal pay for equal work enforcement
- Salary transparency initiatives
- Mentorship and sponsorship programs
- Flexible work arrangements
- Education and training for career advancement and negotiation
