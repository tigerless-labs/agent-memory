**The 8 Key Challenges High Tech Companies Face with FP&A:**

1. **Forecasting revenue and profits** — Unpredictable markets, rapid technological change, and intense competition make it difficult to predict future revenue and market share.

2. **Valuing intangible assets** — IP, patents, and brand value lack standard valuation methods, making it hard to assess true company value for investment decisions.

3. **Balancing short-term and long-term goals** — Pressure to meet quarterly targets conflicts with need to invest in R&D and long-term strategic initiatives.

4. **Maintaining adequate liquidity** — High investment required to stay competitive; need to fund R&D and capitalize on opportunities while meeting immediate needs.

5. **Managing and allocating resources** — Multiple projects and product lines create complexity in deciding where to invest for maximum ROI.

6. **Measuring financial performance** — Traditional FP&A metrics (GAAP, ROI, margins) don't capture what drives High Tech value (user growth, innovation velocity, market position).

7. **Maintaining profitability** — Intense competition and rapid technological obsolescence make it hard to protect margins and maintain long-term profitability.

8. **Software scalability and integration** — Outdated FP&A tools cannot handle the large data volumes High Tech companies generate; poor integration across financial systems prevents consolidated analysis.

**Root causes:** Outdated software, insufficient resources for maintenance and updates, fundamental mismatch between traditional FP&A practices and High Tech business dynamics.
