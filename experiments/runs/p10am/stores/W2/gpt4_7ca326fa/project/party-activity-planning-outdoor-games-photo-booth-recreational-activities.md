Considering outdoor activities and games for a celebration event:

**Planned activities:**
- Backyard obstacle course: hula hoops, cones, balance beams, foam blocks, crawling tunnels, jumping hurdles
- Water balloon toss: classic summer game with participants in two lines tossing and stepping back
- Badminton game with net and optional tournament setup
- DIY photo booth with multiple backdrops, props, smartphone/camera with tripod, on-site printing station

**Alternative activity ideas:**
- Lawn Twister
- Scavenger hunt (backyard or neighborhood)
- Outdoor karaoke with microphone and speaker
- Sidewalk chalk art
- Lawn games: cornhole, giant Jenga, ring toss
