Began practicing yoga to help with stress and flexibility; it has been transformative for overall mood and energy levels.

**Current practice:**
- 3 classes per week
- Even short 10–15 minute practices make a noticeable difference
- Committed to maintaining regular practice by scheduling it as an appointment

**Favorite poses:**
- Downward-facing dog (helps stretch hamstrings, calves, spine; strengthens arms and shoulders)
- Child's pose (relaxes back, neck, and shoulders; provides sense of calm)

**Benefits experienced:**
- Feels more relaxed and centered
- More grounded and calm, especially after long work days and commuting
- Improved flexibility
- Better stress management

**Challenge:**
- Finding time and energy on busy days, especially after work
- Strategy: prioritizing self-care by treating yoga like any other important appointment; focuses on consistency over duration

**Travel adaptation:**
- Can maintain practice with lightweight yoga mat, apps (Down Dog, Yoga Studio, Pocket Yoga), and hotel room stretches
