Interested in investing in yoga props to support ongoing practice and increase flexibility.

**Props to acquire:**
- Yoga blocks (for modifying poses, improving alignment)
- Yoga straps (for deepening stretches, maintaining proper alignment in shoulder and hip openers)

**Selection criteria:**
- Start with lighter props; can reduce reliance as flexibility improves
- Invest in quality, durable, eco-friendly materials
- Experiment with different materials (rubber vs. foam blocks) to find preference
- Use props to modify, not to force body into unnatural positions

**Next step:** Consult with yoga teacher/instructor for personalized recommendations based on current flexibility and practice style
