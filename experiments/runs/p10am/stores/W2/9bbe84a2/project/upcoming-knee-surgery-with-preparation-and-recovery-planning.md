Knee surgery scheduled. Pre-surgery preparation includes: following doctor instructions, stopping certain medications, fasting, arranging help and transportation, preparing home with safety measures. Post-surgery recovery strategy: pain management with prescribed medication, rest and ice, knee elevation above heart level, gradual mobility increase, attending all follow-up appointments with surgeon.
