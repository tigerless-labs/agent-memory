GAIL sustainability efforts include:
- Expanding natural gas pipeline network as cleaner fuel alternative
- 2020 joint venture with Carbon Clean Solutions for CO2 capture and utilization
- MOU with Indian Railways for solar projects on railway lands/buildings
- Bio-CNG plants converting agricultural/municipal waste to compressed natural gas
- On-site practices: energy consumption reduction, water conservation, waste management

Goal: reduce carbon footprint and support India's sustainability commitments.
