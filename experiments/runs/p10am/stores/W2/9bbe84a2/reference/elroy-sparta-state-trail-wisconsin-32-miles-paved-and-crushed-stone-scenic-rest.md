**Elroy-Sparta State Trail, Wisconsin**

- **Length**: 32 miles (Elroy to Sparta)
- **Surface**: Mix of paved (~10 miles) and compacted crushed stone (~22 miles)
- **Terrain**: Relatively flat with only gentle hills, no steep grades
- **Scenery**: Wooded areas, prairies, wetlands; includes historic trestle bridge over Baraboo River; wildlife includes deer, turkeys, birds
- **Rest stops**: Approximately every 5-7 miles with restrooms, picnic tables, water refill stations
- **Towns**: Several small towns along the trail with restaurants, cafes, convenience stores
- **Access points**: Multiple with parking, restrooms, picnic areas
- **Bike shops near trail**: 
  - Sparta Bike Shop (Sparta, WI)
  - Elroy Bike Shop (Elroy, WI)
  - Tunnel Trail Cycles (Wilton, WI)
  - Kendallville Cycles (Kendall, WI)
- **Accommodations**: Campgrounds and hotels available in the area
- **Tips**: Crushed stone can be rough on road bikes with skinny tires; wider tires or mountain bikes recommended. Bring water, sunscreen, insect repellent.
