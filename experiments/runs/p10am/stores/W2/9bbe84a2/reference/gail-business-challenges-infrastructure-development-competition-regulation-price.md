GAIL faces key challenges in executing its 2030 strategy:

1. **Infrastructure development**: acquiring land, obtaining environmental/permitting clearances, securing financing
2. **Competition**: pressure from domestic and international energy sector players
3. **Regulatory/policy changes**: subject to varying rules from multiple government bodies
4. **Price volatility**: business heavily influenced by natural gas, crude oil, and energy commodity price fluctuations
5. **Geopolitical risks**: exposure across multiple countries impacts operations

Despite these challenges, GAIL has positioned itself with robust financials, diverse capabilities, and R&D investment in new technologies.
