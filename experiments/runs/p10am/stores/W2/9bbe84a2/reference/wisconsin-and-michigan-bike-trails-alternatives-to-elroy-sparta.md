**Wisconsin trails:**
- **Wiouwash State Trail**: 22 miles, mostly paved with crushed stone sections, Fox River Valley views
- **Lake Wissota State Park Trails**: 20 miles total including 6-mile Lake Wissota Trail with lake views in Chippewa Falls
- **Blue Mounds State Park Trails**: 15 miles including 4-mile Pleasure Valley Trail through prairies and forests

**Michigan trails:**
- **Pere Marquette State Trail**: 30 miles, paved, Pere Marquette River and forest views (Midland to Clare)
- **Leelanau Trail**: 17 miles, mix of paved and crushed stone, Leelanau Peninsula and Lake Michigan views (Traverse City to Suttons Bay)
- **Sleeping Bear Heritage Trail**: 27 miles, paved, Sleeping Bear Dunes lakeshore and forest views (Traverse City to Sleeping Bear Dunes)
- **North Country Trail**: 460 miles spanning Michigan including Upper Peninsula; mix of paved and dirt trails with forest, lake, and river views
