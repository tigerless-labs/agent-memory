Popular hiking trails in Shenandoah National Park:

- Old Rag: 9 miles, challenging difficulty; offers beautiful views and unique rock formations
- Hawksbill Mountain: 2.9 miles; leads to the tallest peak in Shenandoah with stunning views  
- Dark Hollow Falls: 1.4 miles, easy; waterfall destination, good for summer swimming
- Marys Rock: 3.7 miles; summit with views of Shenandoah Valley
- Stony Man: 1.6 miles, easy; second-highest peak in park with panoramic views

Always check weather conditions and park regulations before hiking.
