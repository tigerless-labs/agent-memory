- Lore (Aaron Mahnke): They Made a Tonic (Ep 1), The Bloody Pit (Ep 5), Echoes (Ep 20)  
- The Dollop (Dave Anthony & Gareth Reynolds): The Radium Girls (Ep 255), The Donner Party (Ep 134), The Astor Place Riot (Ep 201)

- Radiolab (Jad Abumrad & Robert Krulwich): The Sepulveda (Ep 1), The Trust Engineers (Ep 13), The Bad Show (Ep 23)
- Stuff You Should Know (Josh Clark & Chuck Bryant): How the Internet Works (Ep 1), The Science of Dreams (Ep 23), The Short History of the Guillotine (Ep 54)
