UVA has 550+ buildings across academic, medical, and athletic grounds. Notable: Rotunda (iconic centerpiece, Thomas Jefferson design), The Lawn (UNESCO World Heritage Site with ten Jefferson-designed pavilions), Academical Village. Visitor Center offers guided tours by student guides and self-guided tours bookable online/by phone. Monticello (Jefferson's home) is a few miles away. John Paul Jones Arena hosts concerts and events.
