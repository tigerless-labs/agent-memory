## Pig Aircraft Design Specifications

**Configuration:** Six-rotor (hexacopter) design

**Dimensions:**
- Wing Span: 15.6 m
- Length: 10.5 m
- Height: 4.0 m

**Cabin Specifications:**
- Length: 2.5 m
- Width: 1.5 m
- Height: 1.9 m (requirement: tall enough for passengers to stand)

**MTOW (Maximum Takeoff Weight): 3200 kg**

**Capacity:** 6 people (includes pilot/engineer)

**Payload:**
- 6 passengers × 75 kg = 450 kg
- Baggage 6 × 40 kg = 240 kg
- Total payload: 630 kg
