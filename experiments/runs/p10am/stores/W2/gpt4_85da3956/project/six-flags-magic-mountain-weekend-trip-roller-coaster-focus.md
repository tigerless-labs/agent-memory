Planning a weekend visit to Six Flags Magic Mountain. Ticket choice: General Admission. Coaster priorities in visit order: Twisted Colossus (first), Full Throttle (second), X2 (third), with interest in West Coast Racers, Tatsu, and Riddler's Revenge. Strategy: use single-rider lines to minimize wait times, visit major coasters during low-crowd hours (morning or late afternoon), use Six Flags Magic Mountain app for real-time wait times and line reservations.
