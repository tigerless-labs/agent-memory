Three major Shakespeare plays studied together (May 2023):

**Hamlet** — tragedy about revenge; themes: nature of revenge, corrupting influence of power, human self-delusion

**The Tempest** — romance about magical manipulation and forgiveness; themes: power and control, possibility of reconciliation

**The Winter's Tale** — tragicomedy about jealousy and redemption; themes: destructive nature of jealousy, power of redemption and forgiveness

User combined information about all three into a unified essay.
