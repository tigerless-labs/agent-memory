## Key topics to address when drafting a constitution for a UK community user group:

1. **Purpose**: Group's mission, objectives, and planned activities
2. **Membership**: Criteria for joining, member rights and obligations
3. **Governance**: Decision-making structure, board/committee roles and responsibilities
4. **Meetings & decisions**: Meeting frequency, conduct, voting procedures
5. **Finances**: Funding sources, membership fees, fund management and spending rules
6. **Amendments**: Procedures for amending the constitution
7. **Legal compliance**: Ensure document meets UK legal requirements
