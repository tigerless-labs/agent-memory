Explored using non-deterministic matrix semantics to model a real-world situation: a person deciding whether to go to a party or stay home.

In this scenario:
- Truth-value 0 represents certainty that the proposition is false
- Truth-value 1 represents possibility that the proposition is true  
- Truth-value 2 represents possibility that the proposition is false

This models the person's uncertainty about attending the party as non-deterministic truth-value assignments, where multiple truth-values are possible for each proposition.
