**Chase Ultimate Rewards Portal Features:**
- Online platform for redeeming Chase Ultimate Rewards points
- Search and book: flights, hotels, car rentals, activities
- Points valued at 1.25 cents each when redeemed through portal (25% bonus)
- Can pay with combination of points and cash
- Book confirmation sent via email

**Travel Insurance & Assistance Coverage:**
- Trip Cancellation/Interruption Insurance: up to $10,000 per person, $20,000 per trip
- Travel Delay Reimbursement: up to $500 per ticket if flight delayed 12+ hours
- Trip Delay Reimbursement: up to $500 per ticket if trip delayed 6+ hours  
- 24/7 Travel Assistance: help with flight changes, hotel bookings, travel emergencies
- Medical and legal assistance through partner network
- Emergency transportation services
