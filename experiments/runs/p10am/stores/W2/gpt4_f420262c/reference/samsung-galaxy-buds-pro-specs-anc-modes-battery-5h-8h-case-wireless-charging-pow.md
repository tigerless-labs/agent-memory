**Active Noise Cancellation:** Dual mics, three modes (High/Medium/Low), ambient sound mode, wind noise reduction, up to 99% noise reduction. **Battery:** 5 hours with ANC on, 4.5 hours calls, 13 hours total with case, quick charge (5 min = 1 hour). **Charging:** Wireless charging (Qi), USB-C case, Power Share (charge from Samsung phone). **Battery tips:** Turn off ANC when not needed, adjust volume, use Power Save mode (activates at 30% battery).
