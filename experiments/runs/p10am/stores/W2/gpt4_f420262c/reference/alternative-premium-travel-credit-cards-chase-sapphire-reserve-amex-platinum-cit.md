**Chase Sapphire Reserve**
- Premium version of Sapphire Preferred
- Includes $300 annual travel credit
- Airport lounge access
- Higher points earning rate than Preferred

**American Express Platinum**
- 5X points on airline flights booked directly with airlines or through Amex Travel
- 1X points on other purchases
- $200 airline fee credit annually
- Lounge access

**Citi Premier**
- 3X points on travel purchases
- Transferable points to multiple airline partners
- No foreign transaction fees

**Capital One Venture**
- 2X miles on all purchases
- Flexible redemption for any travel purchase
- No blackout dates or restrictions
- No foreign transaction fees
