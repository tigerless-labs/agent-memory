**Nubya Garcia** — British saxophonist/composer; incorporates jazz, soul, afrobeat; strong improvisational skills and powerful tone.

**Joel Ross** — American vibraphonist; innovative jazz composition/performance approach; debut album 'KingMaker' (Blue Note, 2019) received widespread critical acclaim.

**Ashley Henry** — British pianist/composer; blends jazz, hip hop, R&B; praised for unique sound and melodic sensibility.

**Georgia Anne Muldrow** — American singer/songwriter/multi-instrumentalist; incorporates jazz, funk, experimental music; known for soulful vocals and organic approach.

**Marquis Hill** — Chicago-based trumpeter; innovative jazz composition/performance; released several albums on independent labels, collaborated with prominent jazz musicians.
