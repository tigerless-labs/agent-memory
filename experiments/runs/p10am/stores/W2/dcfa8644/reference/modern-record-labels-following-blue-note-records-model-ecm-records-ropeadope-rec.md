**ECM Records** — German label founded 1969; focuses on innovative jazz and classical music; artists include Keith Jarrett, Pat Metheny, Jan Garbarek.

**Ropeadope Records** — US label founded 1999; eclectic roster committed to creative/innovative musicians; artists include Christian McBride, Robert Glasper, Snarky Puppy.

**Revive Music Group** — US company founded 2006; started as concert promotion, expanded to record label and artist management; focuses on emerging jazz artists and new talent.
