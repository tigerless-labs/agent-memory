Weekly training template for triathlon (swimming, biking, running) with strength training and recovery.

Monday: Swim 30 min easy + Upper body strength (push-ups, pull-ups)

Tuesday: Bike 45 min easy + Run 20 min easy

Wednesday: Rest or active recovery (yoga, stretching)

Thursday: Swim 30 min intervals + Lower body strength (squats, lunges)

Friday: Bike 45 min intervals + Run 20 min intervals

Saturday: Brick workout - 30 min bike followed by 20 min run

Sunday: Long swim (60-90 min) OR long bike (2-3 hours)

Customization: Adjust intensity and duration based on personal strengths/weaknesses and available training time.
