Must-visit historical and cultural landmarks in Ahmedabad recommended for 4-5 day visit:\n\n**Cultural Heritage Sites:**\n- Sabarmati Ashram: Mahatma Gandhi's former residence, symbol of India's independence struggle\n- Akshardham Temple: Stunning example of Indian architecture with intricate carvings and evening light/sound show\n- Jama Masjid: One of India's largest mosques, beautiful example of Indo-Islamic architecture\n- Sarkhej Roza: Historic mosque and tomb complex\n- Bhadra Fort: Historic fort dating back to 15th century\n\n**Cultural Experiences:**\n- Old City Heritage Walk: Explore narrow lanes, discover Bhadra Fort, Teen Darwaza, historic pols\n- Navratri celebrations: Traditional garba and dandiya raas dances if visiting Sept-Oct\n- Kite Festival (Uttarayan): Jan 14 celebration with locals\n\n**Other Attractions:**\n- Kankaria Lake: Recreational spot with boating, walking trails, sunset views\n- Science City: Interactive exhibits, planetarium, IMAX theater\n- Ahmedabad Museum: City's history, art, and culture
