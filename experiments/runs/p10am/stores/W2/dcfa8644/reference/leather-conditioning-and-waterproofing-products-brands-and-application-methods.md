Leather Conditioning - oil-based (more nourishing) and water-based (better for waterproofing). Brands: Leatherology, Chambers Leather Milk, Bickmore, Kiwi. Application: small amount on clean dry cloth, rub gently in circular motions. Waterproofing - protects from water and damage. Types: sprays (convenient), creams/waxes (more protection). Brands: Nikwax, Sno-Seal, Scotchgard, Kiwi. Always test on small area first, avoid over-conditioning, reapply regularly per instructions.
