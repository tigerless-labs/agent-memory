Recipe planned: ground beef browned with onions and garlic first, canned tomatoes, kidney beans. Seasonings: chili powder, cumin (half tsp), paprika (quarter tsp), oregano (quarter tsp), salt (half tsp), pepper (quarter tsp), brown sugar (1 Tbsp to balance acidity). Considering optional additions: bell peppers, jalapenos for heat, tomato paste (1-2 Tbsp). Simmer 6-8 hours.
