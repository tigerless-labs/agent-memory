Working on improving grilling skills for chicken breasts: achieving perfect grill marks and better timing to avoid overcooking.

**Grill mark techniques:**
- Clean and oil grill grates; preheat to 400-425°F
- Pat chicken dry; place at angle for diagonal marks
- Rotate 90 degrees after 3-4 minutes for crisscross pattern
- Don't press down with spatula
- Cook to 165°F internal temperature; let rest after

**Target timing:** 5-6 minutes per side
