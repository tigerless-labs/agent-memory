Interested in Korean-Style BBQ Chicken. Marinade: soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, gochujang. Considering adding Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) for authenticity and heat. Marinate 30 min-1 hour (or overnight), grill 5-6 min per side at medium-high heat, finish with sweet and smoky BBQ sauce. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and chopped green onions.
