Japan's maritime security strategy (2013) has three key components:

1. Active contribution to peace - diplomacy, economic development, peacekeeping
2. Protecting territorial integrity and waters - defense capabilities, surveillance, amphibious defense  
3. Open and stable maritime order - rules-based cooperation, freedom of navigation, address piracy/terrorism

Balances deterrence and diplomacy while promoting regional cooperation.
