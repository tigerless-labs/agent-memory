Japan modernized naval capabilities across several areas:

- Maritime surveillance: Advanced network, UAVs, maritime patrol aircraft
- Anti-submarine warfare: Advanced sonar, specialized aircraft, submarines with advanced sensors
- Amphibious capability: New amphibious force, assault vehicles, aircraft for island defense
- Ballistic missile defense: Aegis-equipped destroyers, ground-based PAC-3 systems
- Cybersecurity: Dedicated cyber defense unit; joint exercises with US
