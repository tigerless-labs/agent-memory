Japan's alliance structure for maritime interests:

- United States: Most important ally; US military in Japan; advanced technology, intelligence sharing, military deterrent
- Australia, Canada: Important security partnerships  
- ASEAN countries: Regional security cooperation
- Quadrilateral Security Dialogue (Quad): US, Japan, India, Australia; defense, maritime security, counterterrorism cooperation

Japan also pursues diplomacy with China and South Korea despite territorial disputes (Senkaku/Diaoyu, Takeshima/Dokdo).
