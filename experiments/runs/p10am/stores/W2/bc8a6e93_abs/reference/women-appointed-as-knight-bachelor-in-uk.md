Women have been eligible for Knight Bachelor appointment in the UK since the early 20th century, though appointments only became common from the 1970s onward. As of 2021, over 100 women have been appointed to the title. Requirements are gender-neutral: British or Commonwealth citizen, significant contribution to field of expertise. Women appointed receive title 'Dame' before their name. Recent examples include Dame Mary Beard (2018), Dame Sarah Gilbert (2021, Oxford-AstraZeneca COVID-19 vaccine co-creator), Dame Theresa Marteau (2019), and Dame Rosemary Squire (2018). In recent years, numbers of women receiving knighthood roughly equal those of men.
