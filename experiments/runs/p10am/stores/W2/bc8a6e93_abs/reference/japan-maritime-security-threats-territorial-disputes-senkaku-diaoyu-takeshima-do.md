Main perceived threats to Japan's maritime interests:

- Territorial disputes: China disputes over Senkaku/Diaoyu islands; South Korea disputes over Takeshima/Dokdo islands in East/South China Seas - can escalate to conflicts
- North Korean threats: Nuclear and missile programs pose significant threats to Japan's maritime trade and security
- Natural disasters: Located in earthquake-prone region; vulnerable to tsunamis affecting maritime infrastructure and naval response
