Joint military exercises Japan participates in:

- RIMPAC: Biennial US-hosted with navies worldwide; maritime interoperability focus
- Cope North: US, Japan, Australia; coordinated air operations (air-to-air, air-ground, air-sea)
- Keen Sword: Biennial US-Japan; ground, naval, air forces; interoperability and readiness
- Blue Flag: Held in Israel; air-to-air, air-to-ground, multi-air-force coordination
- Malabar: US, Japan, India; maritime security, freedom of navigation, counterterrorism
