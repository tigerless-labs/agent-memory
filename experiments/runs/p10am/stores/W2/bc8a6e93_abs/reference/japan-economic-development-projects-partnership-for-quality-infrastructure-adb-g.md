Economic development projects Japan pursues to promote regional peace:

- Partnership for Quality Infrastructure: High-quality infrastructure in Asia-Pacific; port development
- Asian Development Bank (ADB): Major contributor; influences projects improving connectivity
- Greater Mekong Subregion Economic Cooperation: Supports SE Asian integration; ports, roads, bridges
- Japan-China-South Korea Free Trade Agreement: Ongoing negotiations to lower trade barriers, promote integration

These projects enhance Japan's commercial presence and regional trade while contributing to peace and security.
