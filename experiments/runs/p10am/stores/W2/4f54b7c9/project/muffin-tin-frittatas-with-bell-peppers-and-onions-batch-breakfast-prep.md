Quick breakfast recipe for meal prep (makes 6 individual frittatas)

Ingredients: 6-8 eggs, 1/2 cup diced bell peppers (red or yellow preferred), 1/2 cup diced onions, 1/2 cup shredded cheese, salt, pepper, cooking spray

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Whisk eggs with salt
3. Mix in diced veggies and cheese
4. Spray 6-cup muffin tin with cooking spray
5. Divide mixture evenly among muffin cups
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until golden and set
7. Cool for a few minutes before serving

Storage: Refrigerate up to 3 days or freeze up to 2 months

Reheating options:
- Microwave: 20-30 seconds
- Toaster oven: 350°F for 5-7 minutes
- Conventional oven: 350°F for 10-12 minutes
- Stovetop: Medium heat 2-3 minutes per side (wrapped in foil)
- Tip: Add splash of water or milk to keep moist
