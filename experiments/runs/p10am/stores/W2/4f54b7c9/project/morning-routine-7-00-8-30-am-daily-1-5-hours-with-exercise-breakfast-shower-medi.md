Chosen schedule for consistent morning routine:

- 7:00 AM: Wake up, stretch, water
- 7:15-7:45 AM: 30-minute exercise (yoga, jogging, weightlifting, bike ride)
- 7:45-8:05 AM: Shower and get dressed
- 8:05-8:20 AM: Prepare healthy breakfast
- 8:20-8:25 AM: Meditation, journaling, or read news (10-15 min)
- 8:25-8:30 AM: Finish getting ready for work

Needs to be ready for work by 8:30 AM.
