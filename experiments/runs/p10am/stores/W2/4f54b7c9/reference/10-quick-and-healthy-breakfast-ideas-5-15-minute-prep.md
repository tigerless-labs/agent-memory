Quick breakfast options for busy mornings:

1. Avocado Toast (5 min): Whole-grain toast + mashed avocado + sliced egg, salt, pepper
2. Overnight Oats (5 min): Rolled oats + milk + nuts/seeds/fruit prepared night before
3. Yogurt Parfait (5 min): Greek yogurt + fresh berries + granola + cinnamon
4. Peanut Butter Banana Toast (3 min): Whole-grain toast + peanut butter + sliced banana
5. Breakfast Smoothie (5 min): Blend fruits + yogurt + milk
6. Scrambled Eggs with Spinach and Feta (10 min): Eggs + chopped spinach + feta + whole-grain toast
7. Muffin Tin Frittatas (10 min prep): Egg cups with veggies, prepare in advance
8. Cottage Cheese and Fruit (5 min): Cottage cheese + berries or peaches + cinnamon or honey
9. Green Smoothie Bowl (10 min): Spinach + avocado + milk + fruit topped with granola and nuts
10. Hard-Boiled Eggs and Toast (5 min): Pre-boiled eggs + whole-grain toast

All options are nutrient-dense and easily customizable based on dietary preferences.
