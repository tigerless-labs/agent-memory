Reputable appraisal organizations to look for when selecting certified appraisers:

Jewelry appraisers:
- NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers)
- AGTA (American Gem Trade Association)
- ASA (American Society of Appraisers)

Antique and collectible appraisers:
- AAA (Appraisers Association of America)
- ISA (International Society of Appraisers)
- NACA (National Association of Collectible Appraisers)

When selecting an appraiser, look for: certification from these organizations, experience with similar items, impartiality, professional affiliations, insurance industry recognition, and good reputation with referrals.
