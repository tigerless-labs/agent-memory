Insurance companies specializing in or offering high-value jewelry coverage:

Specialty insurers:
- Jewelers Mutual: specializes in vintage and antique jewelry
- Chubb: high-value items including jewelry, art, collectibles  
- AXA ART: art, antiques, collectibles including jewelry
- Hagerty: classic car insurance plus collectibles including jewelry

Standard companies with specialty departments:
- State Farm: high-value items department
- Allstate: Valuable Items policy
- Liberty Mutual: Valuable Possessions policy

Consider independent insurance brokers specializing in high-value items.
