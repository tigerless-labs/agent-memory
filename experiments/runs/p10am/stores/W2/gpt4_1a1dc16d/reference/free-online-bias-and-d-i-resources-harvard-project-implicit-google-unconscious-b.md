Free online resources for learning about unconscious bias and creating inclusive cultures:

- **Harvard University's Project Implicit**: Free online tool that measures implicit biases and provides educational resources
- **Google's Unconscious Bias Training**: Free online training module introducing unconscious bias and strategies to overcome it
- **SHRM Diversity and Inclusion Toolkit**: Free online resource with tools, templates, and resources for creating a more inclusive culture
