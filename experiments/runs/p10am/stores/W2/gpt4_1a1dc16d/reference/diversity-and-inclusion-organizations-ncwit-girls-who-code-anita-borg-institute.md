Non-profit organizations supporting diversity and inclusion in tech:

- **National Center for Women & Information Technology (NCWIT)**: Resources, training, consulting for promoting D&I in tech
- **Girls Who Code**: Aims to increase women in tech; provides resources for companies to create inclusive cultures
- **Anita Borg Institute**: Resources, training, and consulting for diversity and inclusion in tech
