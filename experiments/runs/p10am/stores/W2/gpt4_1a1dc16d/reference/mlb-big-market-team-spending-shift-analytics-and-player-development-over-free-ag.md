Teams becoming cautious on free agent spending: Yankees (historically big spenders, reduced post-pandemic), Dodgers (efficient, homegrown focus), Red Sox (under luxury tax after 2018).

Counter-examples still investing: Mets (Trevor Bauer), Padres (Blake Snell, Yu Darvish).

Success through young talent: Houston Astros, Chicago Cubs.

Key drivers: analytics better identifying young talent; luxury tax penalties; post-pandemic financial stability focus; reduced game-day revenue.

Long-term outlook: trend likely continues post-pandemic; modest parity possible if big-market caution persists; wealthier teams retain structural advantages.
