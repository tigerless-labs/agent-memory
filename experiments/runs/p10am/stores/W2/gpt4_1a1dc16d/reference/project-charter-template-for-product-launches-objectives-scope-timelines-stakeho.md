**Essential Project Charter Sections:**

**Project Objective:**
- State the business goal and what success looks like
- Example: Launch new product line on June 1 to drive sales, increase brand awareness, enhance customer engagement

**Project Scope:**
- Clearly define what is included in the project
- Example: Marketing strategy, creative assets, e-commerce development, content creation, campaign execution

**Project Timeline:**
- Break down into phases with start/end dates
- Identify key milestones and deliverable deadlines

**Stakeholders:**
- List all key stakeholders with their roles
- Include internal team members, external partners, client representatives

**Deliverables:**
- Specify all tangible outputs and their acceptance criteria
- Examples: strategy documents, design files, content, website updates

**Project Budget:**
- Allocate budget across tasks
- Track spending against plan

**Risks and Assumptions:**
- Identify potential roadblocks
- Document assumptions about client feedback, resources, timelines
- Note dependencies on external factors
