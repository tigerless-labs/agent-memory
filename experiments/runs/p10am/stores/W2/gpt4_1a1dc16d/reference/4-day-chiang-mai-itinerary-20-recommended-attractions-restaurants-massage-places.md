Massage: Fah Lanna Spa (4.6★, 300 THB). Dessert: Bake & Bite (4.4★). Day 3: Doi Inthanon National Park (4.6★, 300-150 THB), Baan Tawai Village (4.3★), Mae Sa Elephant Camp (4.3★, 2400-1200 THB). Lunch: The Salad Concept (4.5★). Dinner: The Good View (4.3★).
