Instagram Live: Plan ahead, promote 1+ week in advance, start with engaging hook, interact with viewers in real-time, keep 10-30 minutes duration, use polls/questions/shopping tags, follow up with replay and summary.

IGTV: Best for long-form content (tutorials, behind-the-scenes, interviews, documentaries). Focus on production quality, use vertical video format, include captions/subtitles, create eye-catching thumbnails, optimize for sound-off viewing, repurpose into shorter clips.

Hybrid Q&A: Combine pre-submitted questions (thorough prep, structured flow) with real-time questions (authenticity, engagement, follow-ups). Designate time for each segment.
