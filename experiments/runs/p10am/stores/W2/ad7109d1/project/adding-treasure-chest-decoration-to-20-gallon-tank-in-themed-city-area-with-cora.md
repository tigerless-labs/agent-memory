Decided to add a treasure chest decoration placed near existing artificial coral and rocks to create a themed exploration area. Planned arrangement:
- Treasure chest at centerpiece (possibly at angle or partially buried)
- Coral and rocks surrounding it at varied heights
- Open swimming space maintained around structures

Chosen treasure chest over sunken ship due to better fit for 20-gallon tank size and to avoid obstruction. Design intent: create a fascinating environment for fish (especially neon tetras) to explore.
