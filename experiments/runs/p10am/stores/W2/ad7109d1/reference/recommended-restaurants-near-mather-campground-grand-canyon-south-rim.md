1. El Tovar Dining Room (~1 mile): upscale contemporary American, regional focus, prime rib, wild king salmon - reservations recommended
2. Bright Angel Restaurant (walking distance): American comfort food, breakfast/lunch/dinner, canyon views - reservations recommended  
3. Canyon Cafe (short walk): casual, omelets, sandwiches, salads
4. Maswik Food Court (~10 min walk): quick bites, deli, pizza, grill
5. Yavapai Lodge Restaurant (~1.5 miles): American steak and seafood, canyon views

Peak season reservations especially important for El Tovar and Bright Angel.
