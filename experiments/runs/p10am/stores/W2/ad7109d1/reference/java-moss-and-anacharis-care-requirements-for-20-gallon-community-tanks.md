**Java Moss:**
- Lighting: Low to moderate (1-2 watts/gallon)
- Temperature: 72-82°F, tolerates 65°F minimum
- pH: 5.5-8.5, prefers slightly acidic to neutral
- Attach to rocks/driftwood with weight or thread
- Prefers gentle water flow
- Fertilize with 5-5-5 NPK weekly
- Compatible with pleco catfish (may graze lightly)

**Anacharis:**
- Lighting: Moderate to high (2-4 watts/gallon)
- Temperature: 65-85°F, prefers 72-82°F
- pH: 6.5-8.5, wide tolerance
- Floating plant, no substrate needed
- Prefers moderate to strong water flow
- Fertilize with 5-5-5 NPK weekly
- Pleco catfish will graze on it
- Prune regularly for bushy growth
