Lake Tahoe CA-NV (3.5h from SF): alpine lake, Sierra Nevada peaks. Mono Lake, CA (2.5h from Tahoe): saline lake with tufa towers. Bodie State Historic Park, CA (1.5h): Gold Rush ghost town. Hoover Dam (confirmed stop): historic engineering landmark with tours. Valley of Fire State Park, NV (confirmed stop): red rock formations, petroglyphs, petrified trees. Hackberry, AZ (1.5h): Route 66 historic town. Wigwam Motels, Holbrook, AZ (1.5h): vintage 1950s structures. Desert View Drive: scenic viewpoints and Watchtower.
