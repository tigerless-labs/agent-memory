Affordable healthcare options in Queens:

1. NYC Health + Hospitals: (212) 788-4000; locations include Elmhurst Hospital and Queens Hospital
2. Community Health Centers (CHCs):
   - Queens Community House: 718-592-5757
   - Joseph P. Addabbo Family Health Center: 718-945-7150
3. Medicaid: Apply through New York State of Health Marketplace (nystateofhealth.ny.gov) or Queens Medicaid Office (718-883-6666)
4. Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplace Plans: nystateofhealth.ny.gov
5. Free or Low-Cost Clinics:
   - Queens Free Clinic: 718-670-1271
   - Community Healthcare Network: 718-943-8633
6. Refugee-specific services:
   - Refugee Health Alliance: 718-445-0111
   - NYC Refugee Health Clinic: 212-562-4141
