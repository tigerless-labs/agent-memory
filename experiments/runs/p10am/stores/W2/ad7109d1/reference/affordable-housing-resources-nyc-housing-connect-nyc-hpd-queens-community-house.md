Resources for finding affordable housing in Queens:

1. NYC Housing Connect: Online portal to search affordable housing opportunities and apply for lotteries
2. NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD): Rental assistance and homeownership programs
3. Queens Community House (QCH): Affordable rental apartments, homeownership programs, supportive services
4. Local non-profits:
   - Queens Housing Authority
   - Jamaica Housing Authority
   - Flushing Willets Point Corona Local Development Corporation
5. Section 8 Housing: Federal rental assistance program through NYC Housing Authority (NYCHA) or local housing authorities
6. Shared housing options: Craigslist, Facebook Groups, local online classifieds for roommate/shared apartment situations

Tips: Always research landlord, property, and neighborhood before signing lease. Beware of scams; never pay deposit/rent without seeing apartment in person.
