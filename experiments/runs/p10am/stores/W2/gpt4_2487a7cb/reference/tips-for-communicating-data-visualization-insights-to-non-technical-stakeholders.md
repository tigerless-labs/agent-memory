**Core Principles**:
1. Know your audience - tailor to their needs and interests
2. Focus on the story - identify key insights and structure around them
3. Use simple language - avoid technical jargon
4. Visual hierarchy - use design to guide attention
5. Provide context - explain data collection and limitations
6. Highlight insights - use annotations and labels
7. Avoid overload - focus on most important findings
8. Interactive visualizations - enhance engagement without overwhelming
9. Provide recommendations - actionable insights for decision-making
10. Practice - rehearse before presenting
11. Clear titles - summarize main finding concisely
12. Narrative structure - problem → analysis → recommendations
