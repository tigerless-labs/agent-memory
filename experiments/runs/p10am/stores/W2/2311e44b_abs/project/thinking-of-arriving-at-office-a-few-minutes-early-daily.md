Considering arriving at the office a few minutes early every day to use that time for:
- Task prioritization for the day
- Responding to urgent emails
- Reviewing schedule and meetings
- Organizing workspace
- Getting a head start on important tasks
- Brief meditation or stretching

This extra buffer time is intended to help set a productive, organized tone for the workday.
