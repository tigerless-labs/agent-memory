Onboarding a new team with planned comprehensive training plan including:

- Clear training objectives, outcomes, and expectations
- Modular training content broken into manageable sections
- Progress tracking via checklist (using Checkli, Todoist, Trello, Asana, or Google Sheets)
- Interactive content such as videos, simulations, gamification
- Mentorship/buddy system assigned to each team member
- Regular feedback and evaluation using START method
- Celebration of milestones and achievements
- Flexible approach to accommodate unexpected changes
