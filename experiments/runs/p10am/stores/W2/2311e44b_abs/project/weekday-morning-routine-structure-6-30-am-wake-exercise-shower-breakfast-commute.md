Goal to optimize morning routine for productivity and well-being. Current structure:
- 6:30 AM: Wake, stretch, meditate
- 6:45 AM: Exercise (30 min yoga or jog)
- 7:15 AM: Shower and get ready
- 7:45 AM: Breakfast and meal prep
- 8:00 AM: Ready to head out

Exploration areas:
- Maximize morning commute with podcasts, audiobooks, or planning
- Arrive at office early to prioritize tasks, review schedule, or organize workspace
- Consider same structure adapted for weekends with later wake time (8:00-8:30 AM)
