Ways to improve smartphone battery life:

- Adjust screen brightness or enable auto-brightness
- Turn off location services for apps that don't need them
- Limit background app refresh
- Turn off non-essential notifications
- Enable Power Saving Mode
- Turn off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when not in use
- Keep operating system updated
- Close unused apps
- Use battery saver apps
- Replace battery if old/degraded (if device has removable battery)

**Note:** A portable charger can supplement these optimizations by providing emergency power when away from outlets, but is not a substitute for good battery habits.
