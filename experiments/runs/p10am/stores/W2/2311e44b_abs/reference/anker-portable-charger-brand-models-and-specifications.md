Anker is a highly reputable brand for portable chargers, known for quality, affordability, and excellent customer service.

**Popular Anker models:**
- PowerCore: Compact and lightweight, capacities 5,000-20,000mAh
- PowerBank: Mid-range series, up to 32,000mAh, includes multiple USB ports and LED flashlight
- PowerCore Fusion: High-capacity series, up to 50,000mAh, features rapid charging and solar panel options

**Alternative brands mentioned:**
- Mophie
- Jackery
