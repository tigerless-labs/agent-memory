Recommended musicals with similar themes, styles, and emotional resonance: In the Heights (Lin-Manuel Miranda; Latinx community in Washington Heights NYC; hip-hop, jazz, Latin music); Spring Awakening (Duncan Sheik; rock musical about identity and rebellion); Passing Strange (rock musical about self-discovery in 1970s Europe); Next to Normal (Pulitzer Prize-winner about mental health and family; rock score); Dear Evan Hansen (contemporary musical about social media and identity); The Last Five Years (two-person musical; introspective, non-linear); American Idiot (Green Day punk rock about disillusionment); Fun Home (Alison Bechdel adaptation about identity and family); The Wild Party (dark jazz-infused 1920s musical); Bat Out of Hell (rock musical inspired by Meat Loaf album; post-apocalyptic).
