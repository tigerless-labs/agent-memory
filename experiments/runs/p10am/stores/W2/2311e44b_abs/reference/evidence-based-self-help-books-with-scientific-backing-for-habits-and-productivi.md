- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey (decades of research)
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck (growth mindset research)
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle (mindfulness and meditation)
- Flow by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi (flow state and optimal experience)
- The Happiness Advantage by Shawn Achor (happiness-success connection)
- Mini Habits by Stephen Guise (small habit formation)
- The Productivity Project by Chris Bailey (productivity research and experiments)
- The Willpower Instinct by Kelly McGonigal (willpower science and self-control)
