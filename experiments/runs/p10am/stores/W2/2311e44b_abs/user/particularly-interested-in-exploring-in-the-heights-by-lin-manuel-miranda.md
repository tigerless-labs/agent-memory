Strong interest in In the Heights because it is Lin-Manuel Miranda's first Broadway musical (second major work after Hamilton). Shares similar energy and spirit with Hamilton. Features strong community focus reflecting Latinx cultural heritage of Washington Heights NYC neighborhood. Score blends salsa, merengue, bachata, Latin jazz, hip-hop, R&B, and show tunes. Character-driven narrative with strong sense of neighborhood interconnectedness.
