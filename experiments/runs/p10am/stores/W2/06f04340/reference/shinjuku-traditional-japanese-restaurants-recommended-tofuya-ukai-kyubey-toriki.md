Tofuya Ukai: Tofu hot pot, skewers, kaiseki. 2-14-3 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku. 5-min walk from Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building. Kyubey Ginza: Edomae-style sushi. 3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku. 10-min walk. Toriki: Tempura, soba, udon. 2-14-1 Yoyogi, Shinjuku-ku. 5-min walk. Kappo Nakajima: Omakase course. 3-2-14 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku. 10-min walk. Han no Daidokoro Bettei: Kaiseki cuisine. 3-2-12 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku. 10-min walk. Tsurutontan: Udon, soba, tempura soba. 3-28-10 Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku. 15-min walk. Reserve via Chope, TableCheck, or hotel concierge.
