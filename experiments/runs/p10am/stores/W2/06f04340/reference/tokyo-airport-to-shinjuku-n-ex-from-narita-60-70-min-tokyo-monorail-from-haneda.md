From Narita: N'EX direct to Shinjuku 60-70 min (3000-4000 yen); Keisei Skyliner to Nippori plus transfer 60-70 min; Airport Limousine Bus 90-120 min (2000-3000 yen); Taxi 60-90 min (15000-20000 yen). From Haneda: Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho plus transfer 30-40 min (500-1000 yen); Keikyu Line to Shinbashi plus transfer 30-40 min; Airport Limousine Bus 30-60 min (1000-2000 yen); Taxi 30-60 min (5000-10000 yen). Get Suica or Pasmo card for transit. Use Hyperdia for schedules.
