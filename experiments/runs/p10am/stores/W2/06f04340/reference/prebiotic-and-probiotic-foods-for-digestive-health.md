Prebiotic-rich foods: chicory root, garlic, onions, asparagus, bananas, apples, oats, Jerusalem artichoke

Probiotic-rich foods: yogurt (with live and active cultures), kefir, fermented vegetables (sauerkraut, kimchi, pickles), tempeh, miso, kombucha, natto

These can be incorporated into meals in various ways: bananas/berries in yogurt, roasted garlic/onions in pasta, asparagus in omelets, salads with mixed greens and apples/onions, oat-based bowls, fermented vegetable sides, roasted sweet potato, green smoothies with banana and oats.
