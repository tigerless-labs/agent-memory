Personas: The Revolver (Med-High risk, High profit), The Big Spender (Med risk, High profit), The Cashback Extender (Low-Med risk, Med-High profit), The High Default Risk (Very High risk, Low-Med profit), The Balance Payer (Low risk, High profit), The Minimum Payer (Med risk, Low profit), The Partial Payer (Med-High risk, Med profit), The Low-Income Borrower (Med-High risk, Low profit), The High-Income Borrower (Low-Med risk, High profit), The Underutilized Borrower (Low risk, Low profit). Strategic levers: Interest rates, credit limits, fees, rewards programs, targeted promotions, payment flexibility options.
