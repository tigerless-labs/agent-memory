Resources for registering a bike and recovering if stolen:

**BikeIndex**: Free online bike registration platform at bikeindex.org. Allows theft reporting and searches by police and bike shops.

**National Bike Registry (NBR)**: Non-profit national database for bike registration.

Local police departments and Department of Transportation may also have registration programs.

Registration typically requires: bike make/model/serial number, color, distinctive features, contact info, and photo of bike.
