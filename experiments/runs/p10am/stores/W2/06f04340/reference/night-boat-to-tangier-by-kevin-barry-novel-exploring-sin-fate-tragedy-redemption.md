## Night Boat to Tangier

**Author:** Kevin Barry

**Protagonists:** Maurice Hearne and Charlie Redmond, aging Irish gangsters

**Central narrative:** The two men wait for a boat to Tangier in hopes of finding Maurice's missing daughter.

**Philosophical themes explored:**
- Sin and guilt from criminal past
- Fate and inevitability of past choices
- Tragedy and emotional scarring
- Redemption and failed attempts at making amends
- Hope vs. false hope
- Failure and despair
- Existentialism and confrontation with mortality and meaninglessness

**Key observation:** The novel presents these philosophical themes through the lens of two characters unable to escape their past actions and consequences.
