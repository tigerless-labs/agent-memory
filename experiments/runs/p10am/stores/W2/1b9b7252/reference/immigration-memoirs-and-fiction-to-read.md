**To read soon:**\n- The Devil's Highway by Luis Alberto Urrea (Mexican immigrants crossing Arizona desert in 2001)\n- The Distance Between Us by Reyna Grande (growing up in poverty in Mexico and immigrating to US)\n\n**Also recommended:**\n- Enrique's Journey by Sonia Nazario (Pulitzer Prize-winning memoir of Honduran boy)\n- The Brief Wondrous Life of Oscar Wao by Junot Díaz (Dominican immigrants in US)\n- Exit West by Mohsin Hamid (refugee and immigrant experiences)\n- Dear America by Jose Antonio Vargas (undocumented immigrant memoir)
