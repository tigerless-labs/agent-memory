Implementing healthy online presence practices:
- Set specific times of day to check social media accounts
- Use website blockers to limit access during work hours
- Unfollow accounts that make them feel bad about themselves or others
- Designate Sundays as a social media-free day to focus on reading and other activities

Goal is to avoid mindless scrolling and maintain intentional online engagement.
