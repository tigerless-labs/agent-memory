Seven actionable ways an individual employee can contribute to a more inclusive workplace:

1. Educate yourself - Read and learn about experiences of people from different backgrounds to build empathy and understanding
2. Speak up respectfully - If you witness discrimination, harassment or bias, address it in a constructive way
3. Listen and learn - Hear colleagues' perspectives on diversity and inclusion to gain insights
4. Be open-minded - Welcome ideas and perspectives that differ from your own
5. Use inclusive language - Be mindful of language that is sensitive to different backgrounds and experiences
6. Support diversity initiatives - Participate in and attend diversity and inclusion events, training sessions, and ERGs
7. Create a supportive atmosphere - Foster inclusivity through Employee Resource Groups, social events, and respectful daily interactions
