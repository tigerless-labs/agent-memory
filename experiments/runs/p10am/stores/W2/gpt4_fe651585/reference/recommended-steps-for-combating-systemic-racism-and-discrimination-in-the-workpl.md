Eight key steps:
1. Hire a diverse workforce with inclusive hiring process
2. Provide cultural competency training for employees
3. Develop and enforce anti-discrimination policies
4. Conduct regular diversity and inclusion assessments
5. Encourage open communication and safe reporting
6. Offer mentorship and sponsorship programs for underrepresented employees
7. Hold leadership accountable for equity and inclusion progress
8. Be transparent and report regularly on DEI initiatives

Tools and resources to support implementation:
- DEI consultants for strategy and training
- Online courses on cultural competency and diversity topics
- DEI toolkits with ready-to-use resources
- Surveys and assessments to measure current DEI level
- Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) for community and support
- Tailored diversity and inclusion training programs
