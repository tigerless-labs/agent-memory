Fished at Lake Michigan near a drop-off with friends Alex and Ryan in mid-March 2023.

Catch: One large walleye (impressive size; Alex was impressed) and three smallmouth bass, two of which were keepers.

Equipment: St. Croix Mojo Bass rod, jig with curly tail

Conditions: Perfect sunny day with light breeze and calm water

Activity: Grilled the caught fish on the shore. Very enjoyable and memorable trip. Plans to return for more fishing adventures.
