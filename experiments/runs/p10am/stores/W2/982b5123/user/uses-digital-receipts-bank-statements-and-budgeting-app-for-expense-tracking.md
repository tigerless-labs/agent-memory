Multi-system approach to tracking expenses decided May 2023:

1. Save digital receipts from online retailers
2. Review bank and credit card statements regularly  
3. Use budgeting app (considering Mint, YNAB, or Personal Capital)
4. Create email filters to organize receipts by retailer

Goal: Maintain awareness of spending patterns and make intentional purchasing decisions rather than impulse buys triggered by marketing. Committed to building tracking as a consistent habit.
