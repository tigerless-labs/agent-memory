Email from H&M around May 1, 2023 triggered month-long shopping spree.

Purchases over 3-4 weeks:
- H&M (May 1): $70 distressed denim jeans and white linen shirt
- Sephora (May 4): $120 skincare products  
- Outlet mall (May 6-7): $80 Adidas sneakers, $20 kitchen gadgets
- ASOS (May 14-15): $220 clothing
- Farmer's market (May 20-21): $50 candles and soaps
- Garage sale (April): $15 vintage denim jacket

Total: $575

Key insight: Marketing emails can trigger impulse purchases even when buyer is aware and consciously seeking deals.
