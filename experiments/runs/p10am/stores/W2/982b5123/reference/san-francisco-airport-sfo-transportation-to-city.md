Options from SFO:

1. **BART**: ~$9.65, 30-40 min, most affordable
2. **SuperShuttle**: ~$17-20, shared van
3. **Taxi**: ~$40-50
4. **Uber/Lyft**: ~$30-40
5. **Private car**: ~$60-70+

In-city: BART, buses, cable cars, or ride-hailing without a car.
