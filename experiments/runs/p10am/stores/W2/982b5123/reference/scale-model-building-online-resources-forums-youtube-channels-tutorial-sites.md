**Websites & Forums:**
- Hyperscale (comprehensive tutorials, articles, galleries)
- Scalemates (tutorials, reviews, galleries with 100k+ members)
- Model Mayhem (aircraft, armor, vehicles focus)
- The Modeling News (news and tutorials)
- Reddit r/ModelBuilding (130k+ members)

**YouTube Channels:**
- Mig Jimenez (advanced techniques)
- Scale Model Guide (beginner to advanced)
- Modeldaddy (weathering and detailing emphasis)
- The Scale Modeler (painting, weathering, detailing)

**Tutorial Sites:**
- Vallejo Model Color (official painting and weathering tutorials)
- AK Interactive (advanced painting and weathering tutorials)

**Topics Covered:**
- Non-Metallic Metal (NMM) Painting
- Weathering techniques
- Masking and camouflage patterns
- Resin and photo-etch work
- Airbrush techniques and maintenance
