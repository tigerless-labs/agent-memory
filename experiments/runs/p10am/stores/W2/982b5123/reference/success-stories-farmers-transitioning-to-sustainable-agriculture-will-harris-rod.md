Will Harris / White Oak Pastures: 3,200-acre farm in Georgia producing grass-fed beef, lamb, poultry using regenerative practices; transitioned 1990s; increased soil fertility, biodiversity, animal health; model for regenerative agriculture in US.

Rodale Institute: Nonprofit studying sustainable agriculture 70+ years; research shows regenerative farming improves soil health, reduces greenhouse gas emissions, increases crop yields; helps farmers transition to organic and regenerative practices.

La Riojana Cooperative: Small-scale grape growers in Argentina; transitioned to organic and Fairtrade practices; improved soil health, biodiversity, water conservation; Fairtrade market access increased incomes.

Sjoerd Miedema: Dairy farmer in Netherlands; uses agroforestry (trees and crops combined); produces more on same land; retains more soil water; saves irrigation costs.
