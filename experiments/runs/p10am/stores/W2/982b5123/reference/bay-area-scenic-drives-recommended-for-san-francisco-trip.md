Recommended scenic drives around the Bay Area:\n\n- **Pacific Coast Highway (Hwy 1)**: Drive south from San Francisco to Santa Cruz (about 1.5 hours), stunning Pacific coastline and beaches. Stops: Half Moon Bay, Pescadero State Beach, Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk\n- **17-Mile Drive**: Scenic detour through Pebble Beach, stunning coastal scenery, famous golf courses, Lone Cypress tree\n- **Golden Gate Bridge to Muir Woods and Sausalito**: Drive north across Golden Gate Bridge, Muir Woods National Monument (30 minutes) with towering redwoods, continue to Sausalito (15 minutes) charming waterfront town\n- **Napa Valley Wine Country**: Head east from San Francisco to Napa Valley (about 1 hour), rolling hills, vineyards, towns like St. Helena and Calistoga\n- **Skyline Boulevard (Hwy 35)**: Drive south through Santa Cruz Mountains, breathtaking views of Bay Area and Pacific Ocean, stops at Wunderlich County Park or Thomas Fogarty Winery\n- **Mount Tamalpais**: Drive north to Mount Tamalpais State Park (about 1 hour), scenic summit views of the Bay Area
