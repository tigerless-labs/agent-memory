Recommended shops for creating wine country gift baskets:

- **K&L Wine Merchants**: 40+ year institution, wide selection, gift department
- **Bi-Rite Wine & Spirits**: Mission District, craft beers, artisanal spirits, gift service
- **The Wine Thief**: 2 SF locations, Napa/Sonoma wines, gift basket service
- **Wine.com**: Online, gift basket service
- **SF Wine School**: SoMa, pairs wine with artisanal cheeses/chocolates

Complementary items: Cowgirl Creamery or Mt. Tamalpais cheeses, Bi-Rite or Fatted Calf cured meats, Ghirardelli or Recchiuti chocolates, Ferry Building Marketplace items.
