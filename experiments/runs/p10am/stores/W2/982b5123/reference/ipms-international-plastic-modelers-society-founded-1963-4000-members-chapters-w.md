**Organization:** International Plastic Modelers' Society (IPMS)

**History:** Founded in 1963 in the UK by a group of modelers. By May 2023, had grown to over 4,000 members worldwide with chapters in over 20 countries.

**Mission:** Promote the hobby of scale modeling, provide a platform for modelers to share knowledge and skills, and foster community among modelers.

**Activities:**
- Local chapters that meet regularly to share models, discuss techniques, participate in group projects
- Local chapters host model shows, contests, and workshops (open to members and non-members)
- Annual national convention (US) featuring model contests, workshops, seminars, and vendor room
- Regional meets (smaller gatherings with contests, group builds, social activities)
- Active online forums for discussion and sharing
- Model contests at local, regional, and national levels
- Public displays and exhibitions to promote the hobby
- Publishes IPMS/USA Journal (quarterly magazine with articles, tutorials, reviews)

**Membership Benefits:**
- Access to local chapters and networking with other modelers
- Discounts on model kits, tools, and supplies from participating manufacturers
- Access to online forums and community resources
- Eligibility to participate in national and regional contests
- Sense of community and support from fellow modelers

**Competition:** Model contests provide opportunities to showcase work, receive feedback from experienced judges, and see work of other modelers. Contests open to all skill levels.
