Lunch/dinner: Nopalito (organic Mexican), The Alembic (gastropub, farm-to-table)

Cafes: Coffee to the People, The Haight Street Coffee Shop, Ritual Coffee Roasters

Other: Amoeba Music (record store), Golden Gate Park nearby.
