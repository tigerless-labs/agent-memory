Sustainable farming methods:
- Organic farming: avoids synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, GMOs; focuses on compost, cover crops, crop rotation
- Integrated pest management (IPM): combines cultural, biological, and chemical control methods
- Crop rotation: alternates crop types to prevent pest/disease buildup and improve soil fertility
- Agroforestry: combines trees, shrubs, and crops on same land; soil conservation, water conservation, biodiversity, productivity
- Conservation tillage: reduces plowing/tilling to reduce erosion, conserve moisture, sequester carbon
- Biopesticides: pesticides from natural sources (plants, microorganisms, minerals); less toxic than synthetic alternatives

Economic feasibility: Long-term benefits offset initial costs through reduced input costs, improved soil health, higher yields, and consumer premium pricing.

Government/organizational incentives: subsidies, grants, loans, research/education/training programs, certification schemes, tax incentives, conservation programs (Conservation Reserve Program, Conservation Stewardship Program in US).
