Recommended tailors:

- **Martin Greenfield**: 60+ years, bespoke, dressed notable figures including President Barack Obama
- **Suitsupply**: SoMa, European-inspired, affordable made-to-measure
- **The Tailor's Keep**: Mission District, bespoke + alterations
- **Al's Attire**: Haight-Ashbury, sustainable, locally sourced
- **Bespoke by Curtis Eliot**: Financial District, modern bespoke

Suit rental: The Black Tux, Menguin. Book early; allow 2-3 fittings.
