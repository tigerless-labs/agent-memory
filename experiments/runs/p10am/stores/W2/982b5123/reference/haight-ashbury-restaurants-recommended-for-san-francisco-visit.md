Recommended restaurants in Haight-Ashbury neighborhood:\n\n- **Zuni Cafe**: Institution since 1979, famous for brick oven roasted chicken, house-made bread, Mediterranean-inspired dishes\n- **The Pork Store Cafe**: Casual breakfast/brunch spot, famous for Pork Store Sandwich (scrambled eggs, bacon, cheddar on sourdough), serves breakfast all day (Mon-Fri 8am-3pm, Sat-Sun 8am-4pm)\n- **Nopalito**: Organic, farm-to-table Mexican cuisine (tacos, burritos, grilled meats)\n- **The Alembic**: Gastropub with farm-to-table American cuisine, craft cocktails and beers\n- **Magnolia Gastropub**: Craft beer selection, pub fare (burgers, sandwiches, salads)\n- **Cafe Cole**: Cozy family-owned cafe, coffee, sandwiches, salads, baked goods
