Decided in May 2023 to establish structured approach to holiday shopping for December 2023 holidays to avoid last-minute rush and stress.

**Strategy components:**
- **Early start**: Begin planning in May/June several months in advance, rather than late November as in past
- **Budget structure**: Categorize expenses with larger allocation for gifts, smaller for decorations; set per-person budgets ($10-200 depending on relationship)
- **Tracking tools**: Use Mint budgeting app and spreadsheet for expense monitoring and organization
- **Research method**: Browse Amazon and eBay during lunch breaks at work; create gift lists for each person on shopping list
- **Price optimization**: Use comparison tools (PriceGrabber, Nextag, Google Shopping) and set up price alerts for tracked items
- **Black Friday strategy**: Research prices beforehand, identify deals, compare across retailers, plan shopping strategy in advance
- **Optimization tactics**: Look for bundle deals, consider refurbished/used items, use cashback apps (Rakuten, TopCashback), check price matching policies

Previous pattern: Started thinking about gifts around late November, causing last-minute stress. This approach spreads planning effort earlier and enables better capture of sales and deals.
