## Ground Rules and Structure
- Establish clear communication expectations; do not interrupt while others speak
- Use structured turn-taking and hand-raising for speaking order
- Keep agendas focused with time limits per item

## Communication Practices
- Encourage active listening and clarifying questions
- Use inclusive language; respect cultural communication differences
- Foster safe environment for sharing thoughts

## Virtual Meeting Techniques
- Use mute button to reduce background noise and interruptions
- Use visual cues (hand raising) to signal speakers

## Addressing Persistent Interrupters
- Address behavior respectfully without accusation
- Seek their perspective on why they interrupt
- Suggest concrete solutions: note-taking, designated time for questions
- Acknowledge and reinforce positive behavior changes

## Manager or Team Leader Interruptions
- Address privately and tactfully, not during meeting
- Explain impact on team communication constructively
- Suggest alternatives: note-taking, waiting until end to share
- Seek colleague or senior support if situation persists
