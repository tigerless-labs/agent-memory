Orange (classic floral pairing, try blood orange for deeper flavor). Yuzu (Japanese citrus, grapefruit-orange hybrid, tart and subtly sweet, exotic). Kumquat (small sweet citrus, use syrup or muddle fresh). Satsuma (sweet/tart mandarin, easy-to-peel). Bergamot (subtle citrus from tea/perfumery, adds complexity). Tips: Use fresh citrus for brightest flavor, start with small amounts (can overpower), use citrus peels/twists for aroma.
