Recommended classic cocktails:

Gin-based:
- Gin Martini: 2:1 or 3:1 gin to dry vermouth; garnish with olive or lemon twist
- Pimm's Cup: Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit
- Bee's Knees: Gin, honey, lemon juice (Prohibition-era)
- French 75: Gin, lemon juice, sugar, champagne
- Corpse Reviver No. 2: Gin, Lillet Blanc, Cynar, Cointreau, absinthe

Other spirits:
- Old Fashioned: Bourbon or rye, sugar, bitters, citrus twist
- Daiquiri: White rum, lime juice, simple syrup
- Sidecar: Cognac, Cointreau, lemon juice, sugar-rimmed glass
