Popular skincare trends for content creation:\n\n**Sustainable Skincare:** eco-friendly products, zero-waste routines\n\n**Customized Skincare:** routines tailored to skin types (normal, dry, oily, combination)\n\n**Natural & Organic Ingredients:** CBD, green tea, turmeric, plant-based products\n\n**Skincare for Specific Concerns:** acne-friendly products, anti-aging treatments, hyperpigmentation\n\n**K-Beauty Trends:** snail mucin, sheet masks, essences\n\n**J-Beauty:** yuki (snow-like skin) philosophy, sake, green tea products\n\n**Skincare for Different Skin Tones:** inclusive content for underrepresented skin types\n\n**Skincare Tools & Devices:** microneedling tools, facial steamers, LED face masks, facial rollers\n\n**Content Formats:** tutorials, product reviews, Q&A sessions, before-and-after transformations, influencer collaborations, Instagram Stories/IGTV, relevant hashtags
