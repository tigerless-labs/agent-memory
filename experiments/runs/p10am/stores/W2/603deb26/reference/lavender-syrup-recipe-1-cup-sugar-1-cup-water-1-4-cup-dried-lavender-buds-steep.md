Combine 1 cup granulated sugar and 1 cup water in saucepan, heat until sugar dissolves. Add 1/4 cup dried lavender buds and remove from heat. Steep for 10-15 minutes until cooled to room temperature. Strain through fine-mesh sieve into clean glass bottle, discard lavender buds. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
