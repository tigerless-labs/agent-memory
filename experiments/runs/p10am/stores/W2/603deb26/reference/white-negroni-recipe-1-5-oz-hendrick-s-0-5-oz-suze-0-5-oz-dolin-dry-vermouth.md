Ingredients: 1.5 oz Hendrick's gin, 0.5 oz Suze (dry bitter liqueur), 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth. Stir with ice 20-30 seconds, strain to chilled coupe, garnish with orange or lemon twist. Can adjust ratio to taste (2:1:1 or 3:1:1). Use dry bitter liqueur for crisp herbaceous flavor.
