Alternatives to Campari for Negroni experiments: Cynar (artichoke, vegetal, 0.5 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica), Aperol (sweet orange, 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica), Luxardo Bitter (intense herbal, 0.5 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica), Suze (French gentian, crisp earthy, 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica), Meletti (citrus-forward, 1 oz + 1 oz Carpano Antica). May need to adjust ratios to find perfect balance.
