Honey Syrup Recipe

Basic ratio: 1:1 honey to water

Method:
1. Combine honey and water in saucepan
2. Heat over low heat, whisking until honey dissolves
3. Do not boil or burn
4. Cool before using
5. Store in fridge for up to 2 weeks

Variations:
- Infuse with citrus peel, herbs (basil, rosemary, thyme), or spices (cinnamon, nutmeg, cardamom)
- Try different honey types: clover, manuka, or acacia honey
- Honey-ginger syrup: add sliced ginger during dissolving
- Adjust ratio for different sweetness levels

Use in: Bee's Knees and other cocktails
