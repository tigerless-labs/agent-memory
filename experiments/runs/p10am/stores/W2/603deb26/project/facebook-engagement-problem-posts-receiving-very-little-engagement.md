Analytics show Facebook posts are getting very little engagement. Potential solutions: optimize content (high-quality visuals, concise posts, questions, humor); optimize timing using Facebook Insights; engage with audience (respond to comments, use Groups, host Q&A); leverage features (Facebook Live, Stories, Polls); promote page (ads, cross-promotion); analyze and adjust strategy; maintain consistent authentic posting.
