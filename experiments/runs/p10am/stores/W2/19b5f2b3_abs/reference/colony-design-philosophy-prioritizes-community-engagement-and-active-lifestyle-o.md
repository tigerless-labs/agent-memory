The colony is intentionally structured to encourage off-duty rangers, workers, and children to spend the majority of their leisure time in community spaces engaging in active pursuits and socialization.

Approach:
- Wide range of accessible amenities: sports facilities, recreational centers, parks, cultural institutions
- Regular events: festivals, performances, community gatherings
- Infrastructure supports walking, cycling, and people mover use
- Emphasis on vibrant social life and shared identity

Result: Strong, connected community with improved health, well-being, and social bonds among residents
