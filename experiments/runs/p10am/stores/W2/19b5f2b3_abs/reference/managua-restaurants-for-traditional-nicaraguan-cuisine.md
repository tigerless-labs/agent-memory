Recommended restaurants: (1) Los Ranchos - local chain with multiple locations, serves steak, seafood, gallo pinto, nacatamales; (2) Mercado Carlos Roberto Huembes - bustling market for quesillo, vigorón, griddled gallo pinto; (3) Asados Don Pancho - family-owned, grilled meats, gallo pinto, plantains; (4) La Cocina de Doña Haydée - near airport, nacatamales, indio viejo, tostones; (5) El Caramanchel - fish specialties including fish soup, grilled fish, ceviche, sopa de mariscos (seafood soup).
