Premier fitness and training facility in the colony dedicated to combat sports and martial arts. Founded by a renowned Kodian martial artist known as Big Bear.

Programs: boxing, wrestling, and mixed martial arts styles for novices to advanced fighters

Instructors: Expert instructors including Big Bear himself, emphasizing technique, conditioning, discipline, safety, and sportsmanship

Activities:
- Regular classes and training programs
- Workshops and seminars with guest instructors and professional fighters
- In-house competitions and sparring events

Facilities: Boxing rings, wrestling mats, padded MMA areas, fully equipped gym, weight room, locker rooms, showers

Location: Near The Playing Field Multisport Arena
