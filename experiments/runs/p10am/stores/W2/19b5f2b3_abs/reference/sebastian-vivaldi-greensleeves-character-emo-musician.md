Sebastian Vivaldi Greensleeves is an emo at heart musician with a kind and adventurous soul. Despite his emotion-filled ballads and fierce riffs, he presents a quiet and nervous demeanor in everyday life. Cares deeply about people, prioritizes friends, and supports them through his music. Not physically strong but loves harder than most.
