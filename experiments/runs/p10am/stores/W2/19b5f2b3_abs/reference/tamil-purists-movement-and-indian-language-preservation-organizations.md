## Tamil Preservation
- **Tamil Purists Movement**: advocates for pure Tamil words, discourages foreign loanwords
- **Tamil Virtual University**: offers courses on classical Tamil literature and language
- **Tamil Nadu government**: mandated Tamil in official communication, encourages use in public spaces
- Key challenge: widespread English use in education, media, administration; educated/urban speakers use significant loanwords

## Other Indian Languages
- **Hindi Rakshak Samiti** (Hindi Protection Committee): Hindi language purist movement
- **Bangla Bhasha Bachao Samiti** (Save Bengali Language Committee): Bengali language preservation
- Similar movements exist for Kannada, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati

## Government Initiatives
- **National Translation Mission**: promotes use of native and classical languages
- **Central Institute of Indian Languages**: documents and preserves regional/cultural diversity
