## VAT Rate
The Value-Added Tax (VAT) rate in the Philippines is **12%**.

## Calculation
To calculate VAT on a purchase total, multiply by 0.12. Example: PHP 1,000 purchase → PHP 120 VAT → PHP 1,120 total.

## VAT Registration Requirement
Businesses must be VAT-registered if their annual sales or gross receipts exceed PHP 3 million.

## Invoice Requirements
- VAT-registered businesses: Required to charge VAT and include it in invoices; must file VAT returns and pay collected VAT to the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
- Non-VAT-registered businesses (below PHP 3 million): Not required to charge or include VAT in invoices
