The colony maintains sustainability and healthy lifestyles by avoiding highly processed foods.

Focus: Fresh, locally sourced, nutritious ingredients

Benefits:
- Health: Whole foods diet improves overall well-being
- Sustainability: Reduces transportation environmental impact
- Economics: Supports local food production and jobs
- Taste: Fresh local ingredients provide superior flavor
- Food Security: Reduces dependence on external supplies

Result: Culture of healthy eating with strong foundation for thriving residents
