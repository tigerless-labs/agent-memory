Must-try traditional dishes: (1) Gallo Pinto - rice and beans with onions, sweet peppers, sour cream, cheese, fried plantains (national dish); (2) Nacatamales - masa (corn dough) with pork/chicken, rice, vegetables, wrapped in plantain leaves, steamed; (3) Vigorón - street food with yuca topped with crispy chicharrones (fried pork skins) and cabbage slaw with sour orange sauce; (4) Quesillo - Nicaraguan cheese wrapped in tortilla with onions, cream, salt; (5) Indio Viejo - stew with ground corn, chicken/beef, vegetables, sour orange-based sauce; (6) Tostones - fried green plantains, often with garlic sauce.
