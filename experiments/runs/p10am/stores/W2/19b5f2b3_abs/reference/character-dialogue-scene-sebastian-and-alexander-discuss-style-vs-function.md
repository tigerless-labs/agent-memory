Creative writing scene: Sebastian and Alexander sit outside a pub waiting for their friend Pond. They discuss style versus function in clothing. Alexander advocates for bold self-expression through streetwear while Sebastian initially favors function and comfort. After encouragement from Alexander, Sebastian opens to being more adventurous with his style. They plan to go shopping together, and Alexander suggests Sebastian wear something eye-catching for his next performance. The conversation is interrupted when Pond arrives and they head into the pub together.
