Hospital in the colony personally designed by the Chief Medical Officer of the Frontier Service, incorporating the colony's guiding principles of sustainability and resident well-being.

Sustainable Features:
- Energy: Passive solar heating/cooling, geothermal systems, solar panels
- Green spaces: Green roofs and walls for air quality and patient environment
- Water: Advanced greywater and blackwater recycling systems
- Materials: Eco-friendly, locally sourced, recyclable construction materials

Design Philosophy:
- Patient-centered: Spacious rooms, ample natural light, access to communal areas
- Holistic health: Emphasis on preventive care, mental health services, healthy lifestyle promotion

Role: Demonstrates how healthcare facilities can operate sustainably within an eco-conscious society
