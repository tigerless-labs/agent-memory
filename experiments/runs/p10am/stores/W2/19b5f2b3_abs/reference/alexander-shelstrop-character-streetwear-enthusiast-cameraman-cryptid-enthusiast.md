Alexander Shelstrop is a streetwear enthusiast and professional cameraman for Hollywood movies. Has a chaotic, devil-may-care attitude about most things except his professional camera work and personal interests in aliens and cryptids. Focused on the prosperity of himself and those close to him, regardless of how that affects the world around him.
