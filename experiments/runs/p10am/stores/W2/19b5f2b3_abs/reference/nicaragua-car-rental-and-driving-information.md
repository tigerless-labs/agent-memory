Car Rental Companies: Avis, Hertz, Enterprise, local companies available in Managua.

Driving License: Foreign drivers can drive with valid home-country driver's license for up to 90 days. Documents needed: valid driver's license, credit card, passport.

Road Conditions: Narrow roads, heavy traffic, poorly maintained roads, limited signage. Recommendation: rent car with GPS or reliable map app; experience driving in developing countries recommended.
