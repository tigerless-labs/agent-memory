## Founding & Growth - Founded 2008 by Brian Chesky; $31B valuation in 2018 with 4M properties across 190 countries; 7M+ locations in 220+ countries by 2020; IPO planned for 2020. ## Business Model - Sharing economy platform; hosts list properties, company charges ~11% transaction fee; operating costs like tech company not real estate. ## Market Context - Hotels/motels: 70% recreation, 18% business, 12% other; bed & breakfasts: small owner-operated, cozy/rural; all subject to regulations, licensing, taxation. ## COVID-19 Crisis - H1 2020 pandemic threatened model; many hosts dependent on revenue for mortgages; travel restrictions created uncertain market. ## Financial Performance - First profitable 2016; 2017: $3B revenue, $90M+ profit
