Cocktail recipes with Hendrick's gin recommended 2023-05-21:

1. **Pimm's Cup with a Twist:** 1.5 oz Hendrick's, 0.5 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 0.5 oz Pimm's No. 1, splash ginger beer. Garnish: cucumber, mint, fruit

2. **Cucumber Gimlet:** 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lime juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 0.5 oz cucumber juice. Garnish: cucumber slice

3. **Rose Petal Spritz:** 2 oz Hendrick's, 2 oz Prosecco, 1 oz rose syrup/liqueur, 1 oz lemon-lime soda. Garnish: rose petal or edible flower

4. **Gin-Gin Mule:** 2 oz Hendrick's, 4 oz ginger beer, squeeze lime juice. Garnish: lime wheel, mint sprig

5. **Summer Solstice:** 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz peach puree, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup. Garnish: rosemary sprig, peach slice

6. **Hendrick's High Tea:** 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz hot water steeped with tea, 1 oz lemon juice, 0.5 oz honey syrup. Garnish: lemon slice
