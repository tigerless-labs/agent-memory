Simple syrup recipes and variations (reference 2023-05-21):

**Classic Simple Syrup:**
- 1 cup (200g) sugar + 1 cup (240ml) water
- Heat until dissolved, cool, strain, store 2 weeks in fridge
- Adjust ratio to taste preference

**Citrus-Flavored Variations:**

1. **Grapefruit:** 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + 0.5 cup fresh grapefruit juice. Optional: grapefruit peel slices. Steep 30+ min.

2. **Lemon-Ginger:** 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + citrus juice + 1-2 inches fresh ginger. Steep 30+ min.

3. **Orange-Blossom:** 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + 0.5 cup orange juice + 0.25 tsp orange blossom water. Steep 30+ min.

4. **Mint:** Add mint sprigs to simple syrup while cooling

5. **Lemon:** Add lemon slice while cooling

**Grapefruit Varieties for Simple Syrup:**
- **Ruby Red:** Deep red color, sweet-tart flavor, balanced, adds pink hue
- **White (White Lady):** Pale yellow skin, tart, slightly sweet, more pronounced bitterness
- **Pink:** Sweet, tangy, more flavorful and sweeter than Ruby Red

**Tips:** Use heavy fresh grapefruits with no mold/soft spots; adjust juice amount to taste; consider adding peel slices for more citrus aroma
