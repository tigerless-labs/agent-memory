Living room ceiling light flickers intermittently; need to diagnose and fix.

**Troubleshooting Steps:**

1. **Check bulb connection:**
   - Ensure bulb is tightened properly
   - Replace if loose, worn-out, or at end of lifespan
   - Incandescent bulbs especially prone to flickering near end of life

2. **Replace bulb:**
   - Try new bulb to rule out bulb-related issues
   - Ensure correct wattage and voltage

3. **Inspect fixture:**
   - Look for signs of wear, damage, or loose connections
   - Check wiring inside fixture if possible

4. **Check for faulty ballast:**
   - If fluorescent fixture, faulty ballast may cause flickering
   - Ballast located inside fixture, regulates power to bulb

5. **Consult professional electrician:**
   - If problem persists after above steps
   - May indicate serious electrical issue with circuit or wiring
   - Better to be safe with electrical issues

**Safety:** Always turn off power to fixture before attempting repairs.
