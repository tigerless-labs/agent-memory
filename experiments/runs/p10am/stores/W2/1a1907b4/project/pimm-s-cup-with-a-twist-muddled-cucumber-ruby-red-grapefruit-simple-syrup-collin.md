Key decisions from 2023-05-21 experimentation:

- **Muddling:** Use muddled cucumber slices (not juice) for more intense fresh flavor and texture
- **Simple syrup:** Ruby Red Grapefruit variety for balanced sweet-tart profile and pink hue
  - Recipe: 1 cup sugar + 1 cup water + 0.5 cup fresh grapefruit juice, steeped 30+ minutes
- **Glassware:** Collins glass (tall, slender) to showcase layers of color and garnishes
- **Garnishes:** Cucumber slice, grapefruit slice, mint sprig, edible flowers, or fresh fruit
- **Context:** Attended mixology class; prior experience making classic cocktails
