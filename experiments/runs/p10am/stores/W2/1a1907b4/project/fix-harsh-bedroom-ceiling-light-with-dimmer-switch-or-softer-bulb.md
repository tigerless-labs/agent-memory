Bedroom ceiling light is too harsh and disruptive to cozy ambiance goal.

**Options to Address:**

1. **Dimmer switch:**
   - Allows adjustable brightness control
   - Compatible with LED, halogen, and incandescent bulbs
   - Provides flexibility to adjust based on time of day and mood

2. **Softer bulb:**
   - Replace with warmer color temperature bulb (2700K-3000K)
   - LED bulbs generally gentler on eyes than incandescent/halogen
   - Creates more relaxing, cozy atmosphere

3. **Combined approach (recommended):**
   - Install both dimmer switch AND softer bulb
   - Maximum flexibility and control

**Next step:** Decide between single option or combined approach based on preference and budget.
