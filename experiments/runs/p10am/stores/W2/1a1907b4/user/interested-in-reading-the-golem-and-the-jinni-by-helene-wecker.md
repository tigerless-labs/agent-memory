Historical fantasy set in early 20th-century New York City featuring a golem named Chava and a jinni named Ahmad. Themes: identity, community, morality, and cultural heritage (Jewish and Middle Eastern traditions). Lyrical, evocative writing with focus on character development and emotional depth. Awards: Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature 2014, National Book Award finalist 2013, New York Times Notable Book 2013.
