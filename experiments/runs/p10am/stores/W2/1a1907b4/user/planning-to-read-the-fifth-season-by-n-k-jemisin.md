First book in the Broken Earth trilogy. Features unique magic system based on orogeny (controlling energy), set in world called the Stillness subject to catastrophic events. Complex characters including Essun, Damaya, and Syenite. Won Hugo Award for Best Novel 2016. Non-linear narrative structure. Trilogy continues with The Obelisk Gate and The Stone Sky.
