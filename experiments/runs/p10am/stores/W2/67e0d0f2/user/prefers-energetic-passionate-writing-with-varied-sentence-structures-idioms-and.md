When rewriting or drafting content, favors:
- Energetic and passionate tone with humor and creative analogies
- Varied sentence length and complexity (avoiding uniform, AI-generated-sounding sentences)
- Mix of short and long sentences for dynamic flow
- Common idioms and figurative expressions
- Descriptive language and vivid imagery
- Synonyms and antonyms for variety while maintaining original meaning
- Unique but accessible vocabulary
- Unpredictable, intriguing constructions

Avoids uniform/monotonous sentence structures that signal AI writing.
