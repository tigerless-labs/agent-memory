Current home office desk lamp is too dim and causes eye strain/headaches. Shopping criteria for replacement: Brightness (500-800+ lumens minimum, primary priority), Adjustability (flexible arm, swivel head, or tilting shade preferred as secondary), Features (USB port or built-in USB hub to charge devices and reduce clutter). Also consider warmer color temperature (2700K-3000K) if sensitive to blue light. Saw a suitable lamp at IKEA (weekend of May 18-19, 2023) but didn't have time to purchase.
