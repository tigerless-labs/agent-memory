**Rent (2023):**
- 1-bedroom shared/bachelor: CAD 1200-1600 per month
- 1-bedroom high-rise: CAD 1600-2000 per month
- 2-bedroom: CAD 1800-2500 per month
- 3-bedroom: CAD 2500-3500 per month

**Food:**
- Groceries: CAD 500-750 per month
- Casual dining: CAD 10-15 per meal
- Mid-range restaurants: CAD 20-30 per meal

**Transportation:**
- TTC monthly pass: CAD 156.00 adult
- TTC single fare: CAD 3.00 cash or CAD 2.90 Presto card
- Bike Share Toronto: CAD 15-20 per month

**Other monthly expenses:**
- Utilities hydro water internet: CAD 100-200
- Phone and internet: CAD 50-100
- Entertainment: CAD 200-500
- Recommended savings: 10-20 percent of income

**Income 2023:**
- Minimum wage Ontario: CAD 15.00 per hour
- Entry-level jobs: CAD 35000-50000 per year
- Government benefits: Ontario Works program for eligible individuals
