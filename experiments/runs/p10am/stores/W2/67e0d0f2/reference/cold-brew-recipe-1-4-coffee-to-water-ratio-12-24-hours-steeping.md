Standard cold brew recipe: 1 part coarse-ground coffee to 4 parts water (e.g., 1 cup coffee + 4 cups water). Steeping time is 12-24 hours. Variations: 1:3 for stronger brew, 1:5 for weaker. Optional: bloom with hot water first to enhance flavor extraction. For dark roasts, use shorter steeping time (12-18 hours) to prevent over-extraction and bitterness.
