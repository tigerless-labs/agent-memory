**Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) for employment:**

- CLB 5-6: Basic fluency - minimum for most jobs in Toronto
- CLB 7-8: Intermediate fluency - customer service, sales, administrative roles
- CLB 9-10: Advanced fluency - professional roles (healthcare, finance, law)
- CLB 11-12: Proficient fluency - near-native ability

**Language assessment options:** IELTS, TOEFL, CELPIP, LINC certificate

**Language improvement resources:** 
- Community center classes
- LINC programs
- English TV/movies with subtitles
- Podcasts, radio, audiobooks
- Reading books/articles/news websites
- Language learning apps (Duolingo, Babbel, Rosetta Stone)
- Conversation practice with native speakers
