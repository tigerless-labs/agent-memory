**Best neighborhoods in Toronto for refugees and newcomers:**

1. Downtown East (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown) - High concentration of newcomer communities, refugee organizations, community centers, language schools
2. Scarborough - Large immigrant population, Scarborough Village Recreation Centre with newcomer services
3. North York - Significant refugee population, North York Community House, Centre for Information and Community Services
4. Etobicoke - Growing refugee community, Etobicoke Newcomer Centre, West Toronto Newcomer Services
5. Parkdale - History of welcoming refugees/immigrants, strong community services, vibrant arts/cultural scene

**Factors to consider:** Proximity to services, public transportation access, safety/walkability, affordable housing, diversity.
