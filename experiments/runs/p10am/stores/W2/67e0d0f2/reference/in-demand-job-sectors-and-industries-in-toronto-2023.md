**High-demand industries in Toronto (2023):**

1. Technology and IT - Software development, data science, cybersecurity
2. Healthcare - Nurses, doctors, allied health professionals due to aging population
3. Finance - Banking, accounting, financial analysis
4. Skilled Trades - Electricians, plumbers, carpenters
5. Renewable Energy - Growth sector as Canada transitions to sustainable energy
