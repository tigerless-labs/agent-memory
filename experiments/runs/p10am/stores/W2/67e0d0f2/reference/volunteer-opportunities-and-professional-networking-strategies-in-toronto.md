**Volunteer platforms:**
- Volunteer Toronto
- Idealist
- LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace

**Networking strategies for building professional network:**
- Attend industry events
- Join professional associations
- Connect on LinkedIn
- Reach out to settlement agencies for job search support
- Participate in community groups and cultural organizations

**Benefits of volunteering:**
- Gain Canadian work experience
- Build professional network
- Gain field-specific relevant experience
- Valued by Canadian employers as legitimate experience
- Way to integrate into community
