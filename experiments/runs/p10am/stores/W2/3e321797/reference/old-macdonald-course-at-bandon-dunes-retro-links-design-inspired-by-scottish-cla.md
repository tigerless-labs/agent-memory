**Design:** Designed by Tom Doak and Jim Urbina as a tribute to Charles Blair Macdonald, the 'Father of American Golf.'

**Key Features:**
- Template holes inspired by famous Scottish links courses (e.g., hole 3 inspired by St. Andrews 11th hole, hole 14 inspired by North Berwick 17th)
- Blind shots over hills and ridges for strategic play
- Deep, revetted bunkers in traditional links style
- Fast, undulating greens requiring precision and control
- Wide, rolling fairways; rough is not overly punishing
- Designed to encourage strategic play

**Playability:**
- Five sets of tees available (4,275 yards to 6,944 yards)
- Accessible to mid-to-high handicappers while still challenging better players
- Generally playable from any set of tees
