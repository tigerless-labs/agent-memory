**Ingredients:**
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 large tomatoes, diced
- 2 cups spinach leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp oil
- Water as needed

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil, add cumin seeds and let splutter
2. Add chopped onion, saute until golden brown
3. Add diced tomato, cook until soft and mushy
4. Add coriander powder, turmeric powder, chili powder, and salt; mix well
5. Add chopped spinach, saute 1 minute
6. Add water to adjust consistency, cook 5 more minutes
7. Serve hot with rice or roti
