Located approximately 90 miles north of California border in Oregon.

**Four 18-hole courses:**
1. **Bandon Dunes** (David McLay Kidd, 1999) - Original links-style course with ocean views
2. **Pacific Dunes** (Tom Doak, 2001) - Dramatic elevation changes, rugged coastline views
3. **Old Macdonald** (Tom Doak & Jim Urbina, 2010) - Retro-style, inspired by classic Scottish courses
4. **Sheep Ranch** (Coore & Crenshaw, 2020) - Rugged natural design with ocean views

**Amenities:** Multiple dining options, practice facilities, golf lessons, junior golf programs, non-golfing activities (hiking, beachcombing), family packages available.

**Booking tip:** Reserve well in advance, especially during peak season (June-September).
