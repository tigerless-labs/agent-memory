**Ingredients:**
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup diced onion
- 1/4 cup diced tomato
- 1/2 cup chopped spinach
- Salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Whisk eggs, season with salt and pepper
2. Heat olive oil in non-stick pan over medium heat
3. Saute diced onion for 2 minutes
4. Add chopped spinach, saute 2 minutes until wilted
5. Pour whisked eggs over onion-spinach mixture, cook 1-2 minutes until edges set
6. Add diced tomato on top
7. Continue cooking until eggs are set
8. Fold in half and serve hot
