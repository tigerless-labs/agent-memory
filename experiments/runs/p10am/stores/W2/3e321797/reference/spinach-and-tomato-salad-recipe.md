**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups spinach leaves, chopped
- 1 large tomato, diced
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:**
1. Combine chopped spinach, diced tomato, and chopped onion
2. In separate bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper for dressing
3. Pour dressing over spinach mixture and toss to coat
4. Serve as side dish, or add protein like boiled egg or grilled chicken to make complete meal
