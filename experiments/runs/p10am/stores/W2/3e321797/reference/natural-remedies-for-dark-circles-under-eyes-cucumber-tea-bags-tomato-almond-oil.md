10 natural remedies for treating dark circles:

1. **Cucumber** - Chill slices and place on eyes for 10-15 minutes to reduce swelling and soothe skin
2. **Tea bags** - Use cool tea bags on eyes for 10-15 minutes to reduce inflammation and swelling
3. **Tomato** - Mix tomato juice with lemon juice, apply under-eye area, wash off after 10 minutes with cold water for lightening
4. **Almond oil** - Massage regularly on affected area for moisturization and skin lightening
5. **Rose water** - Dip cotton ball in rose water and place on eyes to soothe and hydrate skin
6. **Aloe vera** - Apply fresh gel under eyes to reduce puffiness and lighten dark circles
7. **Turmeric** - Mix turmeric powder with pineapple juice, apply for about 10 minutes, rinse with lukewarm water
8. **Coconut oil** - Massage under eyes before sleeping, wash off with warm water in the morning
9. **Lemon juice** - Mix lemon juice and honey, apply for 15 minutes, rinse with warm water for lightening
10. **Mint leaves** - Crush fresh mint leaves into paste, apply under eyes for about 10 minutes, wash off with warm water

Note: Consult with a doctor before trying any new remedies, especially if you have pre-existing conditions or allergies.
