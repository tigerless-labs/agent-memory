User is creating a digital recipe book to preserve grandma's recipes and culinary legacy.

**Organization Plan:**

Categories (by course):
- Appetizers, Soups, Main Dishes, Sides, Desserts, Beverages

Tags:
- Ingredients
- Cooking time  
- Difficulty level

Content per recipe:
- Notes and stories about each recipe
- Grandma's anecdotes and cooking tips
- Separate section: grandma's cooking philosophy, favorite ingredients, memories of cooking together, family traditions, personal anecdotes, and vintage photos

Goal: Preserve family culinary traditions and create a treasured family heirloom.
