## LiFePO4 Battery Cell Compression Fixture Specification

**Cell dimensions:**
- Height: 205mm
- Width: 174mm
- Thickness: 72mm

**Compression apparatus:**
- Two steel compression plates: 205mm × 174mm each
- Four Grade 5 steel 1/4" threaded rods (UNC, 20 TPI) at corners
- Total fixturing force: 300 kgf

**Calculated specifications:**
- Force per rod: 75 kgf (300 kgf ÷ 4 rods)
- Pressure per face: ~41,234.5 Pa (≈ 6 psi)
- **Required torque per rod: 17.27 ft-lbs**
  - (Based on mechanical advantage of UNC threads and even distribution across two faces)
  - Assumes UNC pitch of 0.05" and no friction losses

**Torque application notes:**
- Use calibrated torque wrench
- Consider thread lubrication for accuracy
- Real-world friction may affect actual force distribution
