**Amenities to Prioritize (when evaluating apartments):**
1. In-unit laundry (time-saver and convenience)
2. Gym or fitness center
3. Pool
4. Community garden
5. Parking (if car owner)
6. Security features (gated parking, cameras, on-site security)
7. Proximity to public transportation
8. Pet-friendly policy (if applicable)

Note: Prioritize based on personal needs rather than accepting every amenity.
