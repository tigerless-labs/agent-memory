**Websites Recommended:**
1. **Zillow** - Filter by price, location, amenities; has Rent Zestimate feature
2. **Trulia** - Similar to Zillow; includes crime maps and neighborhood insights
3. **HotPads** - Map-based search with commute time feature
4. **PadMapper** - Aggregates from Craigslist and local listings; includes Safety Score
5. **Walk Score** - Evaluates walkability and public transportation access
6. **Apartments.com** - Comprehensive search with ratings and reviews
7. **Rent Jungle** - Aggregates listings; includes Neighborhood Guide

**Mobile Apps:**
- Zillow Rentals (iOS/Android)
- Trulia Rentals (iOS/Android)
- PadMapper (iOS/Android)

**Search Tips:**
- Set budget filter to $800 or less
- Use location filters for public transportation proximity
- Read reviews from current and former residents
- Contact landlords directly to ask questions and schedule viewings
