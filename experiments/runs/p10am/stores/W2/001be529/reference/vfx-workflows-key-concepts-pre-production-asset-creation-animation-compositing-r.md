## VFX Workflow Concepts

**Pre-production:** Planning phase where VFX artists work with directors, producers, and cinematographers to determine VFX needs, look, and feel.

**Asset creation:** Creating digital assets (characters, props, environments) using 3D modeling and texturing software.

**Animation:** Animating digital characters, objects, and environments via keyframe animation, motion capture, or simulations.

**Compositing:** Combining visual elements (live-action, CG, effects) into a single cohesive image.

**Rendering:** Generating the final output by rendering individual elements, then compositing them together.

**Review and approval:** Reviewing work with director and stakeholders to ensure it meets creative vision.

**Delivery:** Final stage where finished VFX are delivered to client or integrated into the project.

All roles (artists, producers, directors) must understand these concepts to ensure the final product meets creative vision and technical requirements.
