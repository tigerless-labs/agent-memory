## Techniques to prevent soggy crust:
- Blind bake crust 10–12 minutes at 400°F before adding fruit
- Pat berries dry with paper towel
- Toss berries with small amount sugar (1–2 tbsp per cup) and cornstarch (1/4 tsp per cup)
- Let mixture macerate 15–30 minutes to intensify flavor
- Strain through fine-mesh sieve to remove excess juice
- Arrange berries in single layer with border around edges

## Flavor pairings for whipped cream or vanilla ice cream:
- Vanilla bean whipped cream
- Lemon zest whipped cream
- Honey whipped cream
- Creamy lavender ice cream
- Bourbon vanilla ice cream
- Fresh mint churned into ice cream
- Candied ginger ice cream
