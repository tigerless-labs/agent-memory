Orchid species recommended for beginners:

- **Phalaenopsis** (Moth Orchid): Common, adaptable, easy to care for, tolerates varied lighting
- **Dendrobium**: Long-lasting flowers, low-maintenance, hardy, tolerates some neglect  
- **Oncidium** (Dancing Lady Orchid): Small, easy to care for, bright showy flowers, forgiving of occasional missed watering
- **Cattleya**: Large showy flowers, relatively easy care, prefers bright indirect light and moderate watering
- **Paphiopedilum** (Venus Slipper Orchid): Unique slipper-shaped flowers, low-maintenance, prefers bright indirect light and consistent watering
