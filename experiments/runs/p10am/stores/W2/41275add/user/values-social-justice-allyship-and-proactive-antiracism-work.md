Emphasizes importance of being proactive about calling out harmful behavior rather than reacting after harm occurs. Interested in taking daily individual actions toward social justice and equity. Concerned about ongoing impacts of colonialism and slavery on marginalized communities. Values education, empathy, listening to marginalized voices, and being an active participant in creating more inclusive and equitable systems. Views allyship as ongoing work that requires continuous learning and uncomfortable growth.
