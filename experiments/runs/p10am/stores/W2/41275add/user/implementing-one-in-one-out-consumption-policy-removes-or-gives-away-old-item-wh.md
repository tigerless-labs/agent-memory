Purpose: Maintain mindfulness about purchases, avoid clutter buildup, encourage intentional consumption

How it works: Every new item acquired requires disposing of (selling, donating, or giving away) an old item from that same category

Expected benefits:
- Reduced clutter
- More thoughtful purchasing decisions
- Promotes sustainability
- Forces evaluation of true necessity of new purchases
