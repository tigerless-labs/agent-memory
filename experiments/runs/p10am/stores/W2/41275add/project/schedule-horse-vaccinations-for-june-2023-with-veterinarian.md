Needs to schedule vaccinations for horses next month (June 2023).

Core vaccines recommended by AAEP:
- Eastern and Western Encephalomyelitis (EEE/WEE): annually, spring
- Tetanus: annually or every 5-7 years
- West Nile Virus (WNV): annually, spring

Risk-based vaccines:
- Influenza: annually or bi-annually
- Rabies: annually or every 3 years
- Strangles: annually or bi-annually

Preparation steps:
1. Schedule appointment in advance
2. Confirm vaccination schedule with vet
3. Clean and disinfect vaccination area
4. Separate horses if needed to reduce stress
5. Ensure horses are in good health
6. Have medical records and identification ready
7. Be present during vaccination
8. Follow post-vaccination care instructions
