Resources for sheep production and care:

Organizations:
- National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP): guidance on nutrition, health, management, vaccination protocols
- American Sheep Industry Association (ASI): sheep health information including vaccination guidelines and parasite management
- University Extension Services: local resources (e.g., Purdue, Cornell, Texas A&M) with fact sheets on vaccinations, nutrition, health management
- Sheep 101: comprehensive online resource covering all aspects of sheep production

Estimated costs (as of May 2023):

Vaccination costs per dose:
- CD-T vaccine (Clostridium perfringens C and D, tetanus): 1.50 to 3.00 per dose
- CDT with selenium: 2.50 to 5.00 per dose
- Pneumonia vaccines: 3.00 to 6.00 per dose

Feed costs:
- Hay (alfalfa or grass): 100 to 200 per ton
- Grain (corn, oats): 150 to 300 per ton
- Minerals and supplements: 20 to 50 per month

Consult veterinarian for best vaccination protocol and accurate cost estimates.
