Supplements to support horse immune function before vaccinations:

Nutritional supplements:
- Vitamin E: antioxidant, supports immune function
- Omega-3 fatty acids: anti-inflammatory, reduces oxidative stress
- Probiotics: promotes healthy gut microbiome
- Beta-glucans: stimulates immune system, increases white blood cells

Herbal supplements:
- Echinacea: natural immunomodulator, enhances immune response
- Astragalus: Chinese herb, stimulates immune system, reduces stress
- Selenium: antioxidant, supports immune function

Other options:
- Commercial immune-boosting feed additives
- Electrolyte supplements: maintain hydration and balance

Important notes:
- Consult veterinarian before adding supplements
- Choose high-quality products from reputable manufacturers
- Follow recommended dosages
- Monitor horses for any adverse reactions
- Supplements support but do not replace vaccinations
