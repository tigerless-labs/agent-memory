Online suppliers for organic chicken feed:
- Amazon (various brands including Amazon Elements)
- Tractor Supply Co.
- ChickensForBackyards
- Meyer Hatchery (family-owned)

Recommended brands:
- Organic Valley (farmer-owned cooperative)
- Blue Seal (certified organic and non-GMO options)
- Modesto Milling (family-owned, organic and non-GMO, locally sourced)
- Scratch and Peck Feeds (family-owned, certified organic and non-GMO)

Local options: Check feed stores, co-ops, and farm supply stores in your area

Selection tips:
- Look for USDA Organic, Non-GMO, or Certified Humane certifications
- Read ingredient labels and nutrition information
- Compare prices and consider bulk ordering
- Transition chickens gradually to new feed
