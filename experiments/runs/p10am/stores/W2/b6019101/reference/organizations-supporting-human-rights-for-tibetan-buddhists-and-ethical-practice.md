**International Campaign for Tibet (ICT)** - promotes human rights and democracy for Tibetans in China; works with policymakers and grassroots supporters.

**Human Rights Watch (HRW)** - international NGO investigating human rights abuses worldwide, including against Tibetan Buddhists.

**Free Tibet** - non-profit promoting human rights of Tibetans; focuses on raising awareness and supporting cultural preservation.

**Fair Trade USA** - promotes ethical international trade practices, environmental sustainability, and social justice.

**China Labor Watch (CLW)** - improves working conditions and labor rights in China through investigations and corporate supply chain advocacy.
