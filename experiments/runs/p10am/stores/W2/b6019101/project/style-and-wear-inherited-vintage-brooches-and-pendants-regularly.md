Wearing grandmother's vintage jewelry more often.

Styling approaches:
- Brooches: pin to dress/blouse/coat, denim jacket, use as focal point on scarves/hats
- Pendants: pair with simple chains and minimalist outfits, layer different chain lengths
- Combine vintage pieces with modern elements to balance the look
- Experiment with different outfit combinations
