Family movie marathon over upcoming long weekend (planned from May 2023). 

**Final film selections:** The Lion King, Back to the Future, The Goonies. 

**Rejected:** The NeverEnding Story (deemed too intense for younger kids in family).

**Planned activities:**
- Goonies-inspired treasure hunt with hidden clues and small treasures
- Movie trivia game with questions about all three films
- Quote-along competition
- Guess-the-movie-poster game (mix of iconic and lesser-known posters)

**Snacks:** Popcorn and movie snacks during films
