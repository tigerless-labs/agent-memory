When planning family movie events with younger children present, carefully evaluates movies for intense themes, scary scenes, or mature content. Example: rejected The NeverEnding Story from May 2023 marathon despite it being a classic, due to concerns about intensity for younger kids. Plans to revisit excluded films at a later time when children are older.
