Barcelona has impressive public art and heritage displays spread throughout its outdoor public spaces.

**Iconic works by Antoni Gaudí:**
- Sagrada Familia cathedral
- Park Guell

**Street art and murals:**
- Found in El Raval and Poble Nou neighborhoods

**Major plazas and public spaces:**
- La Rambla — lined with historic buildings, statues, and fountains
- Placa de Catalunya — similar historic architecture and public art

**General character:**
The city is known for vibrant cultural heritage and flourishing arts scene reflected in public spaces, artworks ranging from contemporary to historical, and cultural to abstract forms.
