Form I-601 requests waiver of inadmissibility grounds, potentially applicable to user's parents' overstay situation. Requirements: demonstrate extreme hardship to qualifying relative (U.S. citizen or LPR spouse/parent) and demonstrate rehabilitation. Process: file Form I-601 with USCIS and supporting evidence, attend biometric appointment, USCIS adjudication, possible interview, decision on waiver. Alternative: Form I-601A (Provisional Waiver) may apply in certain circumstances. Note: waiver decision is discretionary and complex; attorney guidance essential.
