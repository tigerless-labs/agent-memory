Common scams to watch for in Thailand:

1. **Tuk-Tuk scams** - Drivers offer suspiciously cheap tours, then take you to overpriced shops for commission. Always agree on price before boarding.

2. **Street Vendor Scams** - Fake or non-working products sold. Check items carefully and compare prices elsewhere.

3. **Gemstone Scams** - Fake or low-quality gems at inflated prices. Only buy from reputable jewelers.

4. **Jet-Ski Scams** - Operators charge for pre-existing damages. Take photos before and after use; clarify rental terms in advance.

5. **Currency Exchange Scams** - Verify conversion rates and commissions before exchanging. Don't accept torn or damaged banknotes.

General safety: Use reputable services, research beforehand, trust your instincts.
