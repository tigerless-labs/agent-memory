**Sources:** AmazonFresh, FreshSeafoodDirect.com, SushiGradeFish.com, or local fish markets

**Selection:** Buy from reputable fishmonger or high-end grocery store with good seafood department. Look for 'sushi-grade' or 'sashimi-grade' labels. Check for:
- Pleasant smell
- Firm texture
- Glossy appearance

**Beginner-friendly options:**
- Salmon (fatty, easy to work with, less prone to drying)
- Tuna (classic choice, smooth texture)
- Yellowtail (mild flavor, easy to handle)

Alternatively, consider cucumber rolls if uncertain about raw fish.
