SOFA Score components: Respiratory (PaO2/FiO2), Cardiovascular (MAP, vasopressors), Hepatic (bilirubin), Coagulation (platelets, INR), Renal (creatinine, urine output), Neurologic (GCS). Example with PaO2/FiO2=200, MAP=65, bilirubin=2.0, platelets=150K, INR=1.3, creatinine=1.5, urine=800mL/24h, GCS=12 gives SOFA=3 (2+1+0+0+0+0).
