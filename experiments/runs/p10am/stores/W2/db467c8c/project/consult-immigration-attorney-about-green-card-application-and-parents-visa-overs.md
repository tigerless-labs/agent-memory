User plans to consult with immigration attorney about: (1) green card application requirements and process, (2) how parents' overstay impacts user's application, (3) waiver of inadmissibility options. Resources: AILA (www.aila.org), National Immigration Project (www.nationalimmigrationproject.org), Immigration Advocates Network (www.immigrationadvocates.org), Avvo (www.avvo.com), Martindale-Hubbell (www.martindale.com). Key questions: attorney's experience with similar cases, success rates, communication approach, fee structure, timeline, and strategy for addressing parents' nine-month overstay.
