Strategy for turning Event Marketing Summit connections into partnerships:

**Immediate follow-up (24-48 hours):**
- Send personalized emails referencing conversation topics
- Connect on LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
- Share relevant content aligned with discussed topics

**Short-term follow-up (1-2 weeks):**
- Schedule calls or meetings to explore collaborations
- Introduce contacts to your existing network
- Share additional resources (whitepapers, webinars)

**Long-term follow-up (1-3 months):**
- Check in regularly and provide value
- Explore collaboration opportunities (joint webinars, content, sponsorships)
- Invite to exclusive events and beta testing

**Best practices:**
- Use CRM to track interactions and follow-ups
- Personalize approach based on conversation and interests
- Maintain consistency in outreach
- Look for mutually beneficial opportunities
- Be patient; building trust takes time
- Focus on providing value, not pushing for immediate results
