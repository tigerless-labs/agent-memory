Strategy to highlight environmental benefits of AI tool based on sustainability webinar:

**Sales conversations:**
- Emphasize environmental benefits: reduce carbon footprint, minimize waste
- Use eco-friendly language: green, sustainable, eco-conscious
- Quantify impact: reduced paper usage, energy consumption
- Connect sustainability to cost savings
- Share case studies from environmentally-focused customers

**Marketing materials:**
- Create dedicated sustainability webpage
- Use eco-friendly visuals (leaves, trees, recycling symbols)
- Highlight sustainability certifications (e.g., ISO 14001)
- Develop sustainability-focused whitepaper/e-book
- Share eco tips on social media for thought leadership

**Additional tactics:**
- Develop sustainability-focused sales script
- Offer free sustainability assessments/audits
- Partner with eco-focused organizations
- Create customer success story highlighting environmental benefits
- Make sustainability core to brand identity and messaging

Target environmentally-conscious leads and customers.
