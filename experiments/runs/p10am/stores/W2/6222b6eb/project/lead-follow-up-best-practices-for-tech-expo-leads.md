Key strategies for converting 200+ Tech Expo leads:

1. **Speed**: Respond within 24-48 hours to keep company top of mind
2. **Personalization**: Address by name, reference specific expo conversations
3. **Segmentation**: Categorize by interest level, job function, company size
4. **Multi-channel**: Use email, phone, social media combination
5. **Value provision**: Share case studies, whitepapers, webinars
6. **Demonstrations**: Offer personalized demos to showcase features
7. **Clear next steps**: Define follow-up calls or meetings explicitly
8. **CRM tracking**: Use CRM software to track interactions and prevent lead loss
9. **Phone follow-up**: Re-engage non-responders with brief, focused calls
10. **Lead nurturing**: Develop strategy for leads not ready to buy immediately
11. **Measurement**: Track effectiveness and optimize approach based on data

Also offer free trials or pilots to give hands-on experience.
