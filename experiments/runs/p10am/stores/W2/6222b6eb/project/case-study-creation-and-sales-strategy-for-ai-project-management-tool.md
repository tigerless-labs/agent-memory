Strategy for developing engaging case studies to support sales:

**Creation best practices:**
- Use storytelling with challenges, triumphs, and benefits
- Focus on customer journey, not just tool features
- Include concrete metrics and data (charts, graphs, tables)
- Use high-quality visuals, videos, screenshots
- Keep scannable with headings and bullet points
- Offer multiple formats: PDF, video, podcast, interactive web
- Highlight customer voice with quotes and testimonials
- Establish consistent design template with company branding
- Make easily shareable with social buttons and CTAs
- Create series by industry or use case

**Sales application:**
- Share case studies that address prospect's specific pain points
- Highlight metrics relevant to lead's business goals
- Use to build credibility by featuring similar companies
- Tell stories to help leads visualize benefits
- Share relevant case studies at right time in sales process
- Use to overcome objections with past examples
- Showcase ROI with financial data
- Make interactive to encourage engagement
- Follow up with additional resources (whitepapers, webinars)

Practice delivery and use case studies to differentiate from competitors.
