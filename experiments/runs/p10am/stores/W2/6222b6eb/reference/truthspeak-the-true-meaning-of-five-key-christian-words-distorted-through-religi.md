Book by Michael D. Halsey examining how five key Christian words have been distorted through religious newspeak in contemporary Christian culture.

The five words covered:
- Grace
- Faith
- Sanctification
- Righteousness
- Salvation

The book argues that these terms have lost their original biblical meanings through cultural and societal influences, and aims to help readers understand their true significance.
