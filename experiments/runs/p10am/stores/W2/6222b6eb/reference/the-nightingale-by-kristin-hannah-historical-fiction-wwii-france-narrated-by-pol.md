Historical fiction set in France during World War II, following sisters Vianne and Isabelle Rossignol as they navigate German occupation and join the French Resistance. Themes include sisterhood, bravery, survival, and sacrifice. Narrated by Polly Stone. Runtime ~17.5 hours. New York Times bestseller.
