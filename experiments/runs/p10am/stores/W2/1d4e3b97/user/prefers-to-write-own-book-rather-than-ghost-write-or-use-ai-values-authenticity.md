When considering book authorship, prefers self-writing over ghost-writing or AI-generated content for several reasons:

- **Authenticity & credibility**: Self-written work is a true representation of thoughts and values; builds reader trust
- **Ownership & control**: Full control over content, tone, and message; ensures accuracy to original ideas
- **Personal fulfillment**: Empowering experience of sharing own unique perspectives and experiences
- **Skill development**: Writing process improves writing skills and refines personal voice
- **Emotional connection**: Creates deeper, more meaningful engagement with readers

Ghost-writing and AI alternatives risk losing authenticity, personal connection, and the fulfillment of authorship.
