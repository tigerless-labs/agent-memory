Benoit Landron's 'Naturlich' pet-nat is imported and served by the glass at 161. The 2021 vintage is 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac and 20% Folle Blanche. Previous vintages were made with higher Folle Blanche, but frost damage in 2021 forced the blend change. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the wine. It's a spring wine that was opened by the glass at 161 for the spring season.
