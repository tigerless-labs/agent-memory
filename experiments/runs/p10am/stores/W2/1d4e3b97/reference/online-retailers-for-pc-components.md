**Mainstream retailers:**
- **Amazon.com** - Wide range of PC components and accessories
- **Newegg.com** - Popular computer hardware and software retailer
- **Best Buy.com** - PC components, peripherals, and pre-built gaming PCs
- **Micro Center.com** - Physical stores in several US locations plus online ordering with in-store pickup
- **B&H Photo Video.com** - Full range of computer hardware and accessories

**Specialty/niche shops:**
- **PCPartPicker.com** - Price comparison and availability across multiple retailers; component compatibility checker
- **Superbiiz.com** - Specializes in hardware; carries hard-to-find and discontinued items
- **Performance-PCs.com** - High-performance hardware including water cooling and modding parts
- **FrozenCPU.com** - Computer hardware and custom modding parts

**Community resources:**
- **Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales** - Subreddit for finding and sharing PC component deals

**Notes:** Compare prices across retailers, check customer reviews, verify return policies and warranties. Specialty shops may have limited availability, higher prices, or longer delivery times.
