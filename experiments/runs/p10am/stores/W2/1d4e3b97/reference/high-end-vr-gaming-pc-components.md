**Graphics Card:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, RTX 3070, or RTX 3060 Ti; AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT or RX 6800

**Processor:** AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, or Intel Core i7-11700K

**Memory:** 16GB or more DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency

**Motherboard:** ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, or Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX

**Storage:** SSD recommended for faster load times - Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850

**Power Supply:** High-quality PSU with at least 750W output - EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X
