**Graphics Card:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580

**Processor:** AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K

**Memory:** 8GB DDR4 RAM with high clock speeds and low latency

**Motherboard:** MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, or Gigabyte GA-A320M-H

**Storage:** 500GB SSD - WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D

**Power Supply:** EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W

These components offer good VR gaming performance at lower cost than high-end options and allow for future upgrades.
