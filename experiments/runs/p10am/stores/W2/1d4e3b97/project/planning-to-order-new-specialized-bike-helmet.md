Planning to order a new Specialized bike helmet soon to replace current outdated helmet.

Considerations:
- MIPS (Multi-Directional Impact Protection System) technology for impact protection
- Adjustable fit system for secure, comfortable fit
- Good ventilation to keep head cool during rides
- Safety certifications: CPSC, Snell, or EN 1078
- Proper sizing based on head circumference
- Models to consider: Specialized Tactic 3.0, Evade, or similar with MIPS technology
