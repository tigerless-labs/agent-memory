Tabletop RPG campaign set in lost city with puzzle challenges. Puzzles include: Mirror Puzzle (etched symbols and reflections), Elemental Puzzle (levers/buttons controlling earth/fire/air/water), Musical Puzzle (instruments and sheet music sequences), Weight Puzzle (arranged weights on marked scales), Memory Puzzle (memorize locations then navigate dark room). Designed for multiple skill levels.
