Planned 20-mile bike ride for weekend of February 24-25, 2024.

Preparation strategy:
- Hydration: drink 16-20 oz water 1-2 hours before, carry 20-24 oz per hour, drink every 10-15 minutes during ride
- Use Garmin bike computer to track distance and speed
- Route planning: use Strava, MapMyRide, Ride with GPS, or Google Maps bicycling mode to find bike-friendly roads
- Safety: wear helmet, check bike condition (tire pressure, brakes, gears), use hand signals, ride defensively, watch for hazards
- Post-ride: hydrate and refuel with food for recovery
