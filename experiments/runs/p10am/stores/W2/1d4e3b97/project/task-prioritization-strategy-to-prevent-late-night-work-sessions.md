Committed to improving task prioritization at work to avoid future late-night overcommitments. Interested in implementing: Eisenhower Matrix (urgent/important classification), impact-based prioritization, breaking down large tasks, realistic deadline setting with 10-20 percent padding, time-blocking, learning to say no, and regular task review. Goal is to maintain work-life balance without sacrificing sleep or quality.
