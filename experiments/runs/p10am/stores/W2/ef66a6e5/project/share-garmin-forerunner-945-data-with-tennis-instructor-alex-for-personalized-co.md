Plan to integrate Garmin Forerunner 945 watch data with tennis lessons with instructor Alex. Goals:

1. Share heart rate and movement pattern data via Garmin Connect app
2. Set up customized workouts designed by Alex
3. Use video analysis alongside Garmin data for technique review
4. Track progress toward specific training goals (endurance, serve speed, footwork)

This will help Alex provide more personalized feedback on game improvement and optimize training sessions.
