Currently building fitness focused on agility, speed, and strength training to improve tennis and soccer performance.

Target areas:
- Agility: shuttle runs, cone drills, ladder drills, box jumps
- Speed: sprints, pro agility shuttle, resistance band training, hill sprints  
- Strength: lower body (squats, lunges, deadlifts, calf raises), upper body (shoulder rotations, lat pulldowns, rows, bicep/tricep work), core exercises, tennis-specific movements with medicine balls and resistance bands
- Frequency: agility 2-3x/week, speed 1-2x/week, strength 2-3x/week, plyometrics 2-3x/week

Working with tennis instructor Alex to track progress and customize workouts.
