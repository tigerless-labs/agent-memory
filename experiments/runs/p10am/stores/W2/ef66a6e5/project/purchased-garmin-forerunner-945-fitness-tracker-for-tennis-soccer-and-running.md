Selected Garmin Forerunner 945 to track runs, soccer games, and tennis matches. Plans to share data with tennis instructor Alex for personalized feedback and customized workout planning.

Key features for sports:
- Manual activity selection (no auto-detect for tennis)
- Tracks: duration, distance, calories burned, intensity, stress levels, recovery time
- Built-in GPS for runs and soccer
- Heart rate monitoring
- Performance condition tracking

Limitations:
- Does not automatically detect tennis activity
- Does not track serve speed or racket swing directly
- Third-party apps (Tennis Buddy, Zepp Tennis) available for more detailed tennis metrics but may require additional hardware
