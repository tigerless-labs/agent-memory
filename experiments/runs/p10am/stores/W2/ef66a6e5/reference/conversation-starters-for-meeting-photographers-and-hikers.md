**General Icebreakers:**
- Hi, I'm [Name]. What brings you to this event?
- I'm new to this group. Can you tell me a little bit about it?

**Photography-Specific:**
- I love your [camera/gear]. What kind of photography do you like to do?
- What do you think of [recent news/event related to photography]?
- Have you worked with [specific technique/software/genre]?

**Hiking-Specific:**
- Have you been on any exciting hikes recently?
- What's your favorite trail in the area?

**Tip:** Start with light, casual conversation about the event or shared interest. Listen actively and ask open-ended questions.
