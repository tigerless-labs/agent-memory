**Immediate Follow-up (24-48 hours):**
- Send personalized message via email, social media, or messaging app
- Mention something specific you discussed or a shared interest
- Keep it brief - rekindle connection, not write a novel
- Example: Hey [Name], great meeting you at the photography meetup! I loved your tips on landscape photography. Would love to grab coffee and discuss more?

**Follow-up Conversation (1-2 weeks):**
- Suggest specific activity (coffee, photography walk, hike)
- Show genuine interest with follow-up questions about their projects/goals
- Be respectful of their time and don't overwhelm with messages

**Long-term Connection (ongoing):**
- Check in every few weeks or months
- Celebrate their successes
- Offer help proactively with projects or connections
- Use templates to save time on repetitive follow-ups
