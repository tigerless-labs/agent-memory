**Participating in Discussions:**
- Be an active listener and show interest in others' thoughts
- Don't be afraid to ask questions or seek clarification
- Share your own experiences, but avoid dominating conversation
- Stay open to different perspectives - photography is subjective

**Giving and Receiving Feedback:**
- Be constructive: focus on specific aspects (composition, lighting, editing) with actionable suggestions
- Receive feedback graciously without getting defensive
- Ask for specific feedback on your own work
- Learn from feedback given to others

**Building Relationships with Members:**
- Attend meetings regularly to build connections
- Participate in club activities: photo walks, workshops, outings
- Be approachable and welcoming to new members
- Follow up with people after meetings to continue connection

**Tips for Success:**
- Be open-minded and willing to try new genres and techniques
- Share your knowledge if you have expertise in particular areas
- Respect club rules and guidelines
- Have fun and enjoy learning and growing with like-minded people
