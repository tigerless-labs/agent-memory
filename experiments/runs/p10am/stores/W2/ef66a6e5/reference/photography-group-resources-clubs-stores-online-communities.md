**Local Photography Clubs:** National Camera Club, local Photography Club organizations

**Camera Stores:** B&H Photo, Camera World - offer photography workshops, classes, and group activities

**Online Communities:** Flickr, Facebook groups dedicated to photography

**Outdoor Resources:** REI, Dick's Sporting Goods - offer outdoor adventure groups that often include photography focus

**Educational:** Local colleges and community centers offer photography classes and workshops
