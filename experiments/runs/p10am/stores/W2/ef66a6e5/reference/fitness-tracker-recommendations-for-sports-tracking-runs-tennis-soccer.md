Top fitness tracker recommendations reviewed:

1. **Garmin Forerunner 945** (Selected) — High-end GPS smartwatch for athletes. Tracks runs, cycling, swimming, tennis. Features: pace, distance, heart rate, stress tracking, recovery time, performance condition, requires manual activity selection.

2. **Apple Watch Series 7** — User-friendly smartwatch, tracks runs/walks/high-intensity exercise, built-in GPS, heart rate monitoring

3. **Fitbit Ionic** — GPS-enabled smartwatch, tracks runs and bike rides, built-in music storage, sleep/stress tracking

4. **Polar M430** — GPS running watch, heart rate monitoring, stride detection, running power metrics

5. **Suunto 3 Fitness** — Mid-range GPS watch, tracks runs/cycling, heart rate monitoring, pace and distance tracking

**Key features to look for:** GPS, heart rate monitoring, water resistance (50m+ if swimming), activity tracking, battery life, smartphone compatibility

**Tennis-specific apps for additional metrics:**
- Tennis Buddy: tracks serve speed, spin, racket swing, video analysis
- Zepp Tennis: tracks racket swing, technique insights, personalized coaching

Most smartwatches require manual activity selection for tennis and don't natively track serve speed or detailed racket metrics.
