**Focus on Process, Not Just Outcomes:**
- Celebrate small wins (attended event, had good conversation, sent follow-up)
- Set realistic expectations - building relationships takes time
- Emphasize the journey and learning, not just the end goal

**Track Progress:**
- Keep journal/log of events attended, people met, follow-ups made
- Use habit tracker for daily or weekly social activities
- Regularly reflect on progress and adjust strategy

**Stay Positive:**
- Remind yourself why you started this goal
- Focus on positive interactions rather than dwelling on negative experiences
- Don't take rejection personally - it's not a reflection of your worth

**Find Support & Community:**
- Find accountability partner to share goals and progress
- Join community with shared goals and support each other
- Celebrate successes with others

**Self-Care is Essential:**
- Prioritize activities that bring you joy and relaxation
- Manage energy levels - don't overcommit
- Take breaks when needed to avoid burnout
