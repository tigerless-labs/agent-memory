**Time Management:**
- Create calendar with reminders for events and follow-ups
- Batch similar tasks (follow-up messages, calls) to optimize time
- Set time blocks for specific activities and stick to them
- Leave buffer time between events for unexpected delays
- Learn to say no to opportunities that don't align with goals

**Energy Management:**
- Prioritize self-care activities that energize you
- Schedule events during your peak energy periods
- Take regular breaks to rest and recharge
- Set clear boundaries with others about availability
- Delegate tasks when possible

**Event Selection Strategy:**
- Be selective - choose events that align with goals and values
- Evaluate ROI: potential benefits, networking, learning opportunities
- Attend manageable number of events per month (quality over quantity)
- Focus on building meaningful connections rather than volume

**Follow-up Strategy:**
- Prioritize follow-ups with most promising connections
- Use templates to save time
- Space out follow-ups to avoid overwhelming yourself and others
- Track progress to identify improvements
