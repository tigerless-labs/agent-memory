Started May 20, 2023. Plans:

- Use simple background with Luna's favorite toys as props (laser pointer, sisal rope scratching post)
- Get Luna to look at camera using: treats near lens, squeaky toys, familiar voice, eye contact
- Capture action shots of laser pointer play with fast shutter speed (1/1000s+), burst mode, continuous autofocus
- Shoot from low angles to emphasize movement and agility
- Keep background simple to avoid clutter
- Session lengths: 5-10 minutes to maintain engagement
