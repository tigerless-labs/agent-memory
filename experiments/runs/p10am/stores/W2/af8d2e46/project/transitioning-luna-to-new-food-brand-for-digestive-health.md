Decision made May 20, 2023. 7-10 day transition plan:

- Week 1: Mix 25% new food with 75% current food
- Gradually increase proportion of new food every few days
- Monitor: behavior, appetite, stool quality
- Watch for digestive upset: diarrhea, vomiting, lethargy
- Maintain Luna's regular feeding schedule
- Consult vet if any adverse reactions occur
