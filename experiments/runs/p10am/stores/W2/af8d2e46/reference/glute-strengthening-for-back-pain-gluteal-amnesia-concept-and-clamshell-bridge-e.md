## Gluteal Amnesia and Back Pain

From Chapter 13: "Rule #5 - Use the Power in Your Posterior"

**Key concept:** Modern sedentary lifestyle causes glutes to atrophy ("gluteal amnesia"). Brain loses neural connectivity with these muscles, causing them to stop functioning properly.

**Why it matters:** When glutes don't do their job, spine takes over bearing loads it's not designed for. This causes:
- Strain on small spinal muscles
- Pressure on discs, joints, ligaments, tendons
- Psoas muscles reflexively tighten
- Cumulative micro-damage over time ("geologic time")

**Solution - three steps:**
1. Wake up the glutes
2. Make them strong
3. Learn to use them at appropriate times

**Exercises mentioned:**
- **Bridge** - helps activate glutes
- **Clamshell** - critical for waking up and strengthening glutes
  - Lie on side with hips and knees bent
  - Use thumb and fingers to locate and feel glute muscles
  - Keep connection with brain while performing movement
  - Find neutral spine and brace core

**Correlation:** High correlation between glutes "going to sleep" and back pain.
