When rewriting travel content, wants to minimize overused phrases like 'perfect spot,' 'ideal destination,' 'crystal clear waters,' 'soak up the vibes.' Prefers concrete details and removes promotional references. Responsive to friendlier, conversational tone while maintaining specificity.
