## Share Disposal & BADR Evaluation

Status: Information gathering

**Situation:**
- User and brother considering share disposal in trading company
- User holds position of company secretary
- Business continues to trade during share disposal
- Evaluating BADR eligibility to reduce CGT on gains

**Key questions resolved:**
- Individual vs company-level £1 million allowance: **Individual basis** (each person has separate limit)
- Business continuation during share disposal: **Yes, allowed** (business can continue trading)
- Company secretary as qualifying officer: **Yes, qualifies** (recognized as officer of company)

**Next steps:** Consult qualified tax advisor for specific circumstances and final eligibility determination
