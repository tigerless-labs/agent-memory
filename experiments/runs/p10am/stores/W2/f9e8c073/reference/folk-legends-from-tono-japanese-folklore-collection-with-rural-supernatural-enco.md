Japanese folklore source material featuring supernatural encounters, ghosts, monsters, and strange visitors in rural mountain areas (Mt. Hayachine region). Stories include iwana river trout fishing encounters, supernatural beings in caverns, strange apparitions, creatures carrying wisteria-vine bags with snakes. Stories span from 1870s to early 1920s, with scholarly attribution (Tetsuzo of Tsuchibuchi village, old man Tora, Kikuchi Eisaku, etc.).
