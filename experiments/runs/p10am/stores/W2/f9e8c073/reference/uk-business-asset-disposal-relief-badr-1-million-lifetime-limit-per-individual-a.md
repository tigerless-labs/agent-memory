## Business Asset Disposal Relief (BADR)

**Key structure:**
- Individual shareholder can claim BADR even if business continues to trade
- £1 million lifetime limit applies *per individual*, not per company
- Rate reduced to 10% on qualifying gains (vs standard CGT rates)

**Qualifying conditions:**
1. Company must be a trading company or holding company of trading group
2. Shareholder must hold ≥5% of ordinary shares and voting rights for ≥24 months before disposal
3. Shareholder must be an officer or employee of the company (or trading group member) for ≥24 months before disposal

**Note:** Company secretary position qualifies as 'officer' for BADR purposes.

**Important:** The £1 million is a *lifetime cumulative* limit — previous BADR claims reduce available relief.
