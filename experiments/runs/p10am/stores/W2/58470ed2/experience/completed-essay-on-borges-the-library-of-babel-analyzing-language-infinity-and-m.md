Wrote/completed essay analyzing Jorge Luis Borges 'The Library of Babel'. Three main themes:

1. Structure of the library and language — hexagonal galleries represent infinite symbol combinations
2. Paradox of infinite language and meaninglessness — vastness creates futility in searching for meaning
3. Impact on reality and knowledge — truth is relative, complete understanding impossible

Key insight: Infinite possibilities paradoxically create meaninglessness; language limitations prevent complete understanding.
