Covers wide range of topics: occult, parapsychology, spiritualism, magic, astrology, psychic phenomena. Contains entries on historical figures (Aleister Crowley, Helena Blavatsky), paranormal events (poltergeists, UFO sightings), demon entries, and other supernatural topics. Considered one of most comprehensive reference books on the subject.
