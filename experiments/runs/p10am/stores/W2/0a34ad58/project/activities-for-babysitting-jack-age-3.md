Indoor: treasure hunts, arts and crafts, obstacle courses, cooking/baking, music and dance parties. Outdoor: nature walks, backyard sports, scavenger hunts, bubble play, sidewalk chalk. Special: movie nights, story time, dress-up play, simple experiments, indoor picnics. Tips: follow Jack's lead, be present, encourage imagination, take breaks, communicate with parent.
