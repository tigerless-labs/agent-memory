Game where Jack predicts milestones like first smile, first laugh, first tooth. Create chart with milestones, have Jack make predictions and write them down, track actual dates as they happen, mark with stickers or checkmarks. Take photos and videos of milestones. Celebrate each one with Jack - special activity or small party. Goal: help Jack feel invested in baby's development, involved in family, and build bonding memories.
