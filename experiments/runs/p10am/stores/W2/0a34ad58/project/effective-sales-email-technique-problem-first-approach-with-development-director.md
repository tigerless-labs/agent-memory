Sales email that led to booking. Key technique: Lead with industry problem (fundraiser retention 16 months avg, 127k replacement cost) rather than solution. Elements: personalization, knowledge of org/role, data-backed problem statement, unusual alternative approach (postpone hire to build internal capacity), positions as prerequisite work. CTA: invitation to short conversation. Tone: consultative, acknowledges may not fit, reduces sales pressure. Successfully prompted prospect to book 30-minute conversation.
