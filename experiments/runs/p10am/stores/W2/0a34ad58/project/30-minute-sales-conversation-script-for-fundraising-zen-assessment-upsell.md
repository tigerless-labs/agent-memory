Structured conversation with five phases: Introduction (5 min) to set context, Listen Intently (10 min) to understand their challenges, Present Assessment (10 min) explaining three focus areas and deliverables, Upsell (5 min) connecting benefits to their needs, Conclusion (5 min) to confirm next steps. Key: frame as data-driven professional analysis and connect to their stated challenges.
