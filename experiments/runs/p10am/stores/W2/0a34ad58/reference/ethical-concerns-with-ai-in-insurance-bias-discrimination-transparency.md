**Key risks:**

1. **Bias in training data**: Algorithms inherit biases from historical data. Underrepresented groups in training data risk discriminatory outcomes.

2. **Reinforcement of societal biases**: AI based on historical claims may assume certain neighborhoods, occupations, or demographics are inherently high-risk, perpetuating discrimination.

3. **Lack of transparency**: Opaque black-box algorithms make it difficult to explain decisions, raising accountability and fairness concerns.

**Mitigation strategies**:
- Review and test training data for bias before deployment
- Invest in transparency and explainability measures
- Establish clear guidelines and protocols
- Involve diverse stakeholders in design and implementation
