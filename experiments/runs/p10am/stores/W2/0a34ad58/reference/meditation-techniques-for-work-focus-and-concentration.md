Techniques to improve focus and alertness during long work hours:

**Core techniques:**
- **Mindful breathing**: Focus on breath sensation; gently return attention when mind wanders; increases focus
- **Body scan**: Lie or sit comfortably, bring attention from toes to head; release tension in each area
- **Loving-kindness meditation**: Send kind thoughts to self and others; increases calmness, compassion, and focus
- **Walking meditation**: Pay attention to breath and foot sensation while walking slowly; increases alertness

**Recommended guided meditations:**
- **Focus and Concentration** by Headspace — specifically designed for improving focus and concentration
- **Mindful Work** by Calm — helps stay present and focused during work hours
- **Energize and Focus** by Insight Timer — uses breathwork and visualization for energy and focus
- **Productivity and Focus** by Meditation Studio — designed for staying focused during long work hours

**Tips for practice:**
- Start with short sessions (5-10 minutes) and build endurance
- Use a timer to stay on track
- Find a quiet space free from distractions
- Be consistent; practice at same time daily
- Use meditation as a short break to recharge during work
