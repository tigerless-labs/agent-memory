Strategies that build brand loyalty:
- Product alignment with customer values (sustainability, innovation, eco-friendly)
- Unique ownership experience: charging network, over-the-air software updates
- Customer engagement: social media, exclusive owner events, product previews
- Culture encouraging risk-taking and continuous improvement
- Creates emotional connection by tapping into customer values
