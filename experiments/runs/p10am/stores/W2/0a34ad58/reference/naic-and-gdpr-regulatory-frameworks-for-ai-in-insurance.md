**NAIC** (National Association of Insurance Commissioners) established working group on AI focusing on ethical standards, data privacy protection, transparency, and independent third-party oversight.

**GDPR** (EU General Data Protection Regulation) imposes strict rules on collection and use of personal data in AI training. Individuals have right to know when AI affects their decisions and right to challenge those decisions.

Both address bias, transparency, and accountability concerns in AI-based insurance decisions.
