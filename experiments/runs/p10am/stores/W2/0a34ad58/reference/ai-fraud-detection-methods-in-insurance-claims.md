1. **Anomaly detection**: Identify unusual patterns in claims data (multiple claims for same injury type, unusual gaps between incident and claim).

2. **Social media monitoring**: Scan for posts/videos contradicting claimant's reported condition.

3. **Network analysis**: Analyze connections between claim participants to detect collusion.

4. **Image and voice recognition**: Detect signs of fabrication, staged accidents, or tampering in submitted media.

Benefits: faster detection, reduced investigation costs, improved accuracy.
