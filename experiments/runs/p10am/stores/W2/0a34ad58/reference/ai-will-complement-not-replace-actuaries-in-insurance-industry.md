While AI automates routine tasks and improves decision-making, actuaries bring irreplaceable skills:

- Deep understanding of insurance products and market trends
- Knowledge of regulation and legal requirements  
- Judgment, creativity, and problem-solving in complex situations

Likely future: AI and actuarial expertise work together to create more efficient and effective insurance operations rather than one replacing the other.
