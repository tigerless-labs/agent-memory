Evaluated meditation apps for establishing a morning practice:
- **Headspace**: User-friendly, guided meditations for stress/anxiety/sleep/focus; Andy Puddicombe's soothing voice
- **Calm**: Wide range of guided meditations, sleep stories, relaxing music; known for high-quality content
- **Insight Timer**: Free app with vast library of guided meditations and timer for personal practice
- **Meditopia**: Personalized meditation plans based on goals and preferences; gentle, non-judgmental approach
- **Buddhify**: Offers meditations for different parts of the day (waking up, bedtime, work breaks)
- **10% Happier**: Geared toward skeptics and beginners; gentle, humorous approach to mindfulness
- **Pocket Mindfulness**: Simple app with guided meditations, breathing exercises, mindfulness tips
