Decision to enhance yard habitat to attract birds. Plants to consider: trumpet vine, honeysuckle, fuchsia, lantana, salvias, bee balm, cardinal flower, coral bells, coneflower, black-eyed susan, zinnias for hummingbirds. Also interested in winter berry-producing plants like holly, cotoneaster, winterberry holly, crabapple, and seed-producing plants like pine, coneflower, sunflower for year-round visitors.
