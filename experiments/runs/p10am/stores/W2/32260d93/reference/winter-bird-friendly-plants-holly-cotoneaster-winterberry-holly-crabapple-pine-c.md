Plants providing winter food for birds. Berry-producing: Holly (Ilex spp.), Cotoneaster (Cotoneaster spp.), Winterberry Holly (Ilex verticillata) with bright red berries, Crabapple (Malus spp.). Seed-producing: Pine (Pinus spp.) for goldfinches and siskins, Coneflower (Echinacea spp.), Black-eyed Susan (Rudbeckia hirta), Sunflower (Helianthus annuus). Nectar-rich: Camellia, Mahonia, Gaultheria, Winter honeysuckle (Lonicera fragrantissima). Prioritize native species suited to local climate.
