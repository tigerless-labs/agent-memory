Winter birds likely to visit feeders (December-February): American Goldfinch, Common Redpoll, Purple Finch for nyjer seed; American Tree Sparrow, Dark-eyed Junco, White-throated Sparrow for ground feeding; Downy Woodpecker, Hairy Woodpecker, Red-bellied Woodpecker for suet; Blue Jays year-round but more frequent in winter. Year-round residents: Northern Cardinal, Black-capped Chickadee, Carolina Chickadee, Tufted Titmouse (prefer sunflower seeds, suet, peanuts). Variation depends on location and natural food availability.
