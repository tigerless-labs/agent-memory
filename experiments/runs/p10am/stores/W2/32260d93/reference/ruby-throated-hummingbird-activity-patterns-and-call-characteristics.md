Activity patterns: Most active 6-10am (morning peak), rest 11am-1pm (heat of day), resume 2-5pm (afternoon), evening social behaviors 5-7pm. Hummingbirds consume 2x body weight daily in nectar and insects. Call: Short, sharp, high-pitched zik or chip sound, repeated in series of 2-5 notes. Distinctive once learned.
