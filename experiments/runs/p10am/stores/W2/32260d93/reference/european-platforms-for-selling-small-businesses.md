Five major platforms for small business sales in Europe:

- **BusinessesForSale.com**: UK-based online marketplace with valuation, marketing, negotiation services
- **Rightbiz**: UK-based marketplace with valuation, legal preparation, marketing
- **Enterprise Europe Network**: EU initiative providing business brokerage and transfer services
- **BusinessesBuySell**: European platform specializing in SMBs with valuation and marketing
- **Companymarketplace.de**: German online marketplace with valuation, legal, marketing services
