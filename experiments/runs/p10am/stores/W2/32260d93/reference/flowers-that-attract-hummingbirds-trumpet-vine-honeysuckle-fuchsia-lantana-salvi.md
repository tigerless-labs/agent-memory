Plants attractive to hummingbirds, especially Ruby-throated Hummingbird:

- Trumpet vine (Campsis radicans)
- Honeysuckle (Lonicera spp.)
- Fuchsia (Fuchsia spp.)
- Lantana (Lantana camara)
- Salvias (Salvia spp.)
- Bee balm (Monarda didyma)
- Cardinal flower (Lobelia cardinalis)
- Coral bells (Heuchera spp.)
- Coneflower (Echinacea spp.)
- Black-eyed susan (Rudbeckia hirta)
- Zinnias (Zinnia spp.)

Hummingbirds prefer tubular, nectar-rich flowers in bright colors (red, orange, purple). Plant varieties that bloom at different times to provide continuous nectar source throughout growing season. Prefer native plants suitable to local region.
