Squirrel deterrents: weight-activated perches that collapse or spin under heavy animals, cage-like enclosures for birds only, squirrel-resistant plastic or metal feeders, physical baffles above or below feeder, position feeder at least 10 feet from trees and fences. Raccoon deterrents: sturdy pole at least 6 feet tall anchored in ground, heavy-duty secure feeders with weight-activated perches, raccoon-specific baffles around pole, move feeder away from water sources, take down or cover feeder at sunset (nocturnal activity). General: keep area clean of spilled seed, use safflower seeds or hot pepper-infused seeds, visual deterrents like aluminum foil or CDs, rotate feeder types and locations.
