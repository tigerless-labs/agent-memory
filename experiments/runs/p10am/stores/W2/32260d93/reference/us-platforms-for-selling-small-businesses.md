Five major platforms for small business sales in the US:

- **BizBuySell**: Online marketplace with business valuation, marketing, negotiation services
- **ExitAdviser**: Platform for business selling with valuation, legal documents, marketing
- **Sunbelt Business Brokers**: National network of business brokers across US states
- **Transworld Business Advisors**: Business brokerage for buying/selling with valuation and marketing services
- **Empire Flippers**: Online marketplace for e-commerce, software, and content-based businesses
