## Low-Maintenance Plants for Bright Indirect Light

Ideal for a living room with big windows:

1. **Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)** — air purifier, survives low light but thrives in bright indirect light, can go weeks without watering

2. **ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)** — tolerates range of lighting, infrequent watering, excellent air purifier, can survive with neglect

3. **Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)** — easy care, white flowers, bright indirect light, consistent watering, air purifier, easily propagated via plantlets

4. **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)** — versatile, low-maintenance, thrives in various lighting, air purifier, can be trained to climb

5. **Succulents (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula)** — extremely low-maintenance, bright indirect light, water sparingly

6. **Dracaena (Dracaena spp.)** — versatile lighting, air purifier, can grow tall

7. **Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii)** — elegant white blooms, air purifier, well-draining soil, moderate watering, tolerates neglect

8. **Bromeliads (Aechmea, Guzmania, Nidularium)** — low-maintenance, beautiful flowers, bright indirect light

**Plant Care Tips:**
- Water when soil feels dry to touch (avoid overwatering)
- Fertilize sparingly
- Prune/trim regularly for shape and health
- Monitor temperature and humidity
