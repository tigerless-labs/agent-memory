## Eligibility
- Non-UK citizens can be appointed as life peers in the UK
- Requirements: must be a British citizen, Commonwealth citizen, or Republic of Ireland citizen with permanent UK residence
- Such appointments are rare

## Examples cited (2023-05-20)
- Baroness Boothroyd (Speaker of House of Commons 1992–2000; appointed to House of Lords 2000)
- Baroness Hayman (first elected Speaker of House of Commons; appointed to House of Lords after 2000)
- Lord Patten of Barnes (last British Governor of Hong Kong; appointed life peer 2005)
- Baroness Manningham-Buller (Director General of MI5 2002–2007; appointed to House of Lords 2008)
