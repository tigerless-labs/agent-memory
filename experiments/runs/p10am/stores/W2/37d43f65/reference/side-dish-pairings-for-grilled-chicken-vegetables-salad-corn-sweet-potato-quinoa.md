## Side dishes that pair well with grilled chicken:

1. **Grilled vegetables** — Bell peppers, zucchini, and eggplant grilled with salt, pepper, and olive oil

2. **Fresh salad** — Caesar salad or Greek salad with cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, feta cheese, and olives

3. **Corn on the cob** — Grilled corn brushed with butter, sprinkled with salt and paprika

4. **Baked sweet potato** — Topped with butter, cinnamon, and brown sugar for a sweet and savory combination

5. **Quinoa salad** — Cooked quinoa mixed with chopped vegetables (cucumbers, tomatoes, bell peppers) and lemony vinaigrette
