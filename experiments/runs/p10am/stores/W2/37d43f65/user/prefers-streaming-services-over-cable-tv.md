Established May 2023. Strongly prefers Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu over cable. Key values: ad-free uninterrupted viewing, ability to pick up exactly where left off even after days, large content catalog including international shows and classics not on cable, watch on mobile devices (phone/tablet) on the go, and binge-watch model where entire seasons release at once rather than waiting weekly for next episodes.
