## Living Room Redesign Project

**Setting:** Living room with big windows; recently tidied and organized.

**Goals:**
- Add a small, compact desk or console table near the window for a workstation/reading nook
- Create a defined, warm, and visually interesting workspace area
- Add low-maintenance plants suitable for bright indirect light
- Incorporate a circular rug to define the space
- Add a circular mirror above the desk to enhance light and create visual interest
- Add artwork or prints for personality

**Key Design Preferences:**
- Compact and stylish (will not clutter the room)
- Neutral color palette for harmonious look
- Multi-functional furniture with storage where possible
- Low-maintenance, warm ambiance

**Desk/Console Table Options Explored:**
- Wall-mounted floating desk
- Narrow console table with slim profile
- Drop-leaf table (space-saving)
- Ottoman with tray
- Foldable desk

**Circular Rug Specs:**
- Size: 3-4 feet diameter
- Materials: wool, jute, or synthetic (durable, low-maintenance)
- Colors: beige, gray, taupe (neutral) or bold accent color
- Pattern: subtle, minimalist geometric or Moroccan-inspired
- Thickness: approximately 1/4 inch

**Circular Mirror Specs:**
- Size: 12-18 inches diameter
- Mount height: 57-60 inches from floor
- Frame: choose to complement surrounding decor (minimalist to ornate)

**Artwork/Print Recommendations:**
- Size: 1/3 to 2/3 the width of desk/table
- Styles: botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, pop art
- Choose pieces that resonate personally and complement room's color palette

**Furniture Retailers to Explore:**
IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate and Barrel, AllModern, Amazon

**Artwork/Print Retailers:**
Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, Saatchi Art
