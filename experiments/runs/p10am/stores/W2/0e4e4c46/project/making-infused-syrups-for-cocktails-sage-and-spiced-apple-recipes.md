## Sage Syrup
- Use fresh sage leaves
- Infuse in simple syrup for 30 minutes to 1 hour
- Taste as you go to adjust infusion time
- Use in Sage Gimlet or Sage Spritz with Hendrick's gin

## Spiced Apple Syrup
- Infuse cinnamon sticks, nutmeg, and allspice in simple syrup
- Infuse for 2-3 hours
- Can add apple slices for additional flavor
- Use in gin-based cocktails

## General Syrup Infusion Tips
- Fresh herbs: vibrant flavor, infuse 30 min-1 hour
- Dried herbs: more concentrated, use less, infuse 2-3 hours
- Simple syrup base: combine water and sugar, bring to boil, simmer 5-7 minutes until dissolved
- Strain through fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth
- Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks
- Experiment with ratios and combinations for stronger or subtler flavors
