Convenient, energy-boosting snacks for sports:

- Fresh fruits: apples, oranges, bananas, berries (natural energy)
- Nuts and seeds: almonds, cashews, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds (healthy fats, protein)
- Energy bars: see separate entry
- Trail mix: homemade with nuts, seeds, dried fruits (easy to prepare, customizable)
- Energy chews: Clif Shot Bloks, Gu Chomps (quick energy burst)
- Dried fruit and nut butter: apricots, cranberries, raisins with peanut or almond butter
- Coconut water or sports drinks: Gatorade, Powerade (hydration)
- Homemade granola bites: rolled oats, nuts, dried fruits

Choose easily digestible options high in complex carbs, protein, and healthy fats for sustained energy.
