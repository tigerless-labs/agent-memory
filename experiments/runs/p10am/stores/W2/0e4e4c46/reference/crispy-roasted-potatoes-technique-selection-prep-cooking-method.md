Tips for achieving crispy outside and tender inside roasted potatoes:

1. Use high-starch potatoes (Russet or Idaho)
2. Cut into thin wedges or slices (about 1/4 inch thick)
3. Soak cut potatoes in cold water for 30+ minutes to remove starch; pat dry
4. Use high-heat oil: avocado or grapeseed oil
5. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F; heat baking sheet 5-7 minutes before adding potatoes
6. Spread in single layer with space between pieces (avoid overcrowding)
7. Flip and rotate after 20-25 minutes
8. Check at 30-35 minutes; roast 5-10 minutes more if needed
9. Optional: broil 2-3 minutes at end for extra crispiness (watch closely)
10. Flavor: mix minced garlic and herbs (rosemary, thyme, parsley) with olive oil before tossing
11. Optional: add grated Parmesan and fresh parsley for extra flavor
