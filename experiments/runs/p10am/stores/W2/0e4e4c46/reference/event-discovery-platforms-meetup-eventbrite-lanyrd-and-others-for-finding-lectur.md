Platforms and resources for finding lectures, workshops, and conferences:\n\n**General platforms:**\n- Meetup: Groups organized by shared interests\n- Eventbrite: Event ticketing and discovery\n- Lanyrd: Conference and meetup calendar\n- Facebook Events: Local event discovery\n- Craigslist Events: Community events section\n\n**Institutional:**\n- University/college websites: Public lectures and workshops\n- City/town websites: Cultural and educational events\n- Libraries: Author readings, workshops, lectures\n\n**Specialized:**\n- Industry-specific websites: Field-focused events and conferences\n- Local newspapers and magazines: Event listings\n- TEDx, Creative Mornings: Specific conference series
