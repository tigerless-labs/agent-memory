ARIA (Australian Recording Industry Association) represents and promotes the Australian music industry.

**Data collection:**
- Music sales, streaming, and digital downloads
- Concert attendance and music video views
- Sources: sales reports from retailers and online platforms, record labels, industry organizations, concert promoters

**Role and activities:**
- Provides market insights and trends
- Supports policy and advocacy initiatives
- Recognizes excellence through ARIA Awards and ARIA Charts
- Identifies growth opportunities and develops strategies to promote Australian music
- Promotes diversity and representation in the industry
- Provides education and development programs for music creators (including ARIA Music Teacher of the Year Award)

**Industry challenges addressed:**
- Competition from overseas artists
- Revenue leakage due to piracy and illegal downloading
