Australian artists recommended for listening:

**G Flip** - Melbourne-based singer, songwriter, and producer; catchy pop-rock anthems and impressive live performances

**The Teskey Brothers** - Melbourne blues and roots band; vintage sound gaining popularity

**Genesis Owusu** - Ghanaian-Australian rapper/songwriter blending hip-hop, funk, and soul

**Tia Gostelow** - Far North Queensland singer-songwriter; indie pop and poignant lyrics

**A.GIRL** - Sydney-based R&B artist; soulful vocals and emotional songwriting

Other artists mentioned with ARIA recognition:
- Tones and I (2019 ARIA Awards, hit single 'Dance Monkey')
- Amy Shark (2018 awards, achieved international success)
- Stella Donnelly (2019 Western Australian Music Awards Best Female Artist)
- Angie McMahon (2019 Independent Music Awards Best Independent Single/EP)
