Post-sports soreness prevention strategies:

1. Warm up and cool down with light cardio and stretching
2. Stay hydrated with water or sports drinks
3. Stretch and foam roll to improve flexibility and promote blood flow
4. Use compression gear (tights, sleeves) to reduce inflammation
5. Apply ice for 15-20 minutes after playing; use heat therapy later
6. Allow 1-2 rest days per week for recovery
7. Eat balanced diet with protein, complex carbs, healthy fats
8. Use massage or self-myofascial release (lacrosse ball, tennis ball)
9. Listen to your body and modify activities if fatigued or in pain
10. Gradually increase intensity to let body adapt
