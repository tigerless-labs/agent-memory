Affordable energy bar recommendations for sports:

- **Clif Bar**: 1.50-2.50 per bar; made with nuts, seeds, dried fruits
- **RXBAR**: 2-3 per bar; known for simple natural ingredients and high protein  
- **Quest Bar**: 2-3 per bar; high-protein, low-sugar option
- **PowerBar**: 1.50-3 per bar; classic tried-and-true option

All widely available at sports stores, online retailers, and some grocery stores. Can buy in bulk or variety packs to save money.
