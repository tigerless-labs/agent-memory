Tools for managing contacts from events and speakers:\n\n- **Contactually**: CRM for contact management, reminders for follow-ups, interaction tracking and analysis\n- **FullContact**: Contact storage and organization, business card scanning, social media integration\n- **LinkedIn**: Professional networking platform, useful for connecting with speakers and attendees, messaging capability\n- **CamCard**: Business card scanner app, digitizes cards and connects with others on social media\n\n**Related tools:**\n- Trello: Can be used for organizing notes and follow-up tasks on cards\n- RescueTime: Time tracking tool for monitoring productivity\n\nBest practice: Follow up with speakers and attendees within 48 hours to sustain connections and conversations.
