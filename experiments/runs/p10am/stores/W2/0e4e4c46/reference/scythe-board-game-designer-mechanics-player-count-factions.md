**Scythe** by Jamey Stegmaier. Setting: Alternate history 1920s. Mechanics: Worker placement, resource management (wood, brick, metal, oil, food), area control, engine-building. Play time: 90-120 minutes. Players: 1-5 (scales well to 4-5). Base factions: Agricola Republic, Crimean Khanate, Nordic Kingdom, Polania Republic, Saxony Empire. Expansions: Invaders from Afar, Wind Gambit, The Rise of Fenris.
