Day 1:
- Doi Inthanon (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1.5 hours)
- Wachirathan Waterfall (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30 min)
- Karen village trek with waterfall and viewpoints
- Lunch at local Karen restaurant
- King and Queen Pagoda (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 30 min)
- Hmong Market (Rank: 4.1, Drive: 30 min)
- Return to Chiang Mai (1.5 hours)

Day 2:
- Mae Wang Elephant Sanctuary (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1 hour)
- Bamboo rafting on Mae Wang River (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30 min)
- Return to Chiang Mai (1 hour)

Day 3:
- Wat Chiang Man Temple (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 15 min)
- Bai Orchid Butterfly Farm (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 30 min)
- Huay Kaew Waterfall (Rank: 4.5, Drive: 30 min)
- Queen Sirikit Botanic Garden (Rank: 4.6, Drive: 1 hour)
- Chiang Mai Night Safari (Rank: 4.2, Drive: 30 min)
- Return to hotel (30 min)

Day 4:
- Huay Tung Tao Sculpture Park (Rank: 4.4, Drive: 30 min)
- Pong Yang Jungle Coaster & Zipline (Drive: 30 min)
- Baan Kang Wat Creative Village (Rank: 4.4, Drive: 30 min)
- Return to hotel (30 min)

All days: 9:00 am hotel pickup. Drive times vary by traffic.
