Engaged deeply with Christian theology and comparative religion, exploring:

- Christian understanding of God's will and purpose compared to other religious worldviews
- Differences between monotheism (Christianity) and polytheism
- Whether belief in a single God limits spiritual diversity compared to polytheistic religions
- Concerns about whether a monotheistic God could be perceived as authoritarian or egotistical  
- The problem of evil and theodicy — reconciling an all-powerful, loving God with suffering and evil

Demonstrates a critical, questioning approach to religious philosophy. Interested in examining potential contradictions and philosophical objections within Christian theology rather than accepting claims uncritically.
