Starting hip-hop dance as a beginner. Primary interest: footwork techniques and basic movements. Not a hip-hop music fan, but attracted to the dance form.

Beginner-friendly entry points: hip-hop freestyle (improvisational), New Jack Swing (syncopated, simple footwork).

Recommended approach: find teacher, master fundamentals (two-step, body rolls, shuffles), practice regularly, watch dancers, have fun.
