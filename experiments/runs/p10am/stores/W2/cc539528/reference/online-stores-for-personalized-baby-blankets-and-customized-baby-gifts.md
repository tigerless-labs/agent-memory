Popular online stores for customized and personalized baby items:

**Etsy** - Handmade and customized items. Search for custom baby blanket with name. Filter by price, rating, shipping time. Read reviews and check seller policies.

**Amazon** - Customized baby blankets with embroidery services. Look for high-rated sellers with strong customer feedback.

**Personalization Mall** - Wide range of personalized gifts including baby blankets. Multiple fonts, colors, designs. Can preview before ordering.

**CafePress** - Customized gifts including baby blankets. Range of designs and fonts. Can upload own design.

**Zazzle** - Customized products marketplace. Various designs, fonts, colors. Can upload own image or design.

**When shopping, verify:**
- Turnaround time for production and shipping
- Material quality (soft and cozy fabric)
- Embroidery quality and durability
- Customer reviews and seller reputation
