Online resources for hip-hop dance beginners:

1. STEEZY Studio - online platform with beginner-friendly hip-hop classes, step-by-step footwork breakdowns
2. Hip Hop Dance Tutorial - YouTube channel with free tutorials, detailed move breakdowns
3. DancePlug - online platform with beginner classes, personalized feedback
4. Matt Steffanina - YouTube channel, free tutorials breaking down basic movements
5. Hip Hop International - global organization offering online tutorials and workshops

Notable dancers for footwork: Bboy Lilou, Phillip Chbeeb, Les Twins, Krump King Tight Eyez, Poppin John
