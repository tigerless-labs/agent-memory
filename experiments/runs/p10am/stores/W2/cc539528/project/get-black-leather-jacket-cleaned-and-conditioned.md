Plan to contact dry cleaners on Main St. about leather cleaning and conditioning service. Cost estimate for basic leather cleaning/conditioning: $20–$100. When calling, ask: (1) experience with leather jackets, (2) specific cleaning products/methods recommended, (3) conditioners or treatments available, (4) protectants or waterproofing treatments, (5) cost, (6) turnaround time, (7) any guarantees or warranties, (8) products to use at home for maintenance between cleanings.
