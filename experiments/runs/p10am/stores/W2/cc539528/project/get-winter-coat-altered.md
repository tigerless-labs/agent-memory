Find a tailor to alter winter coat. Cost estimates: hemming $10–$30, taking in/letting out $20–$50, sleeve alterations $15–$40, shoulder/collar adjustments $20–$50, zipper/button replacement $10–$30, lining repairs $20–$100. Total cost typically $50–$200 depending on complexity. When contacting tailor, ask for quote, check reviews, ask about experience with winter coats, and inquire about turnaround time.
