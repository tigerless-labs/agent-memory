Decided to start with a single Etsy shop selling both vintage art posters and original artwork rather than creating separate shops. Will reconsider splitting shops later if the collection grows significantly. Chose Etsy over Shopify due to lower upfront costs. Etsy fees: 0.20 per listing, 3.5% transaction fee, 3% payment processing. Shopify: 29-299 monthly subscription.
