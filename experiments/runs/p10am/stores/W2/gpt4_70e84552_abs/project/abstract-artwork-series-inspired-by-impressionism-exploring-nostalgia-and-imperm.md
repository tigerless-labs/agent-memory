Creating a series of abstract pieces inspired by impressionism with personal twist. Central themes: nostalgia, memory, and impermanence. Recontextualizing vintage posters (originally advertisements) to encourage viewers to reflect on the past and its relationship to the present. The worn, faded quality of vintage posters serves as metaphor for forgotten history.

Techniques under active experimentation:
- Layering colors and textures to achieve depth and luminosity
- Layering paint and ink
- Using gel medium to create texture and depth  
- Decoupaging vintage posters onto canvas
- Using vintage posters as base for abstract pieces

Goal: Create dialogue between old and new elements, blending past and present while exploring themes of time and memory.
