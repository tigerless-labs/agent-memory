Attending a 1920s-themed party as character research for Mary Sunshine role to experience era atmosphere, observe period styling, dancing, and social dynamics.

Preparation strategy:
- Dress in full 1920s-inspired attire
- Review Charleston, Foxtrot, and other 1920s dances
- Familiarize with jazz and blues music

Expected activities at party:
- Live jazz bands or DJs playing 1920s music
- Costumed guests in period attire
- Classic cocktails (Sidecar, French 75) and period hors d'oeuvres
- Games: poker, blackjack, roulette
- Contests: Charleston dance-off, flapper dress competition
- Activities: photo booth, tarot card readings, vintage car display
- Scavenger hunts for 'bootlegged' liquor
- Speakeasy password challenges
- Jazz-age trivia or charades
- Live performances (burlesque, cabaret)

Approach: Fully embrace the atmosphere, be respectful of historical context, let go of inhibitions, focus on fun and learning.
