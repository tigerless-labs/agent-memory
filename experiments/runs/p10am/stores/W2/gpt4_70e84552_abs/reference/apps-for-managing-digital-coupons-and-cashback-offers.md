Recommended apps for managing digital deals and coupons: Flipp (aggregates coupons/sales/cashback with loyalty integration and shopping list); Checkout 51 (cashback app with offer browsing and receipt upload); Fetch Rewards (cashback platform); Honey (browser extension and app for coupons and cashback with price tracking); AnyList (shopping list with coupon integration). Key strategy: load coupons to loyalty card before shopping, check apps before trips, stack offers for maximum savings, track expiration dates.
