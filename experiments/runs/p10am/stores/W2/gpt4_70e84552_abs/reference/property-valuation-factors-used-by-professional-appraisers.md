Professional appraisers consider these key factors when determining property value:

1. Location - proximity to schools, parks, public transportation, shopping, amenities
2. Size and layout - bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, overall layout
3. Condition - age of property, repairs/renovations completed, overall condition
4. Comparable sales - recent sales of similar properties (sales comparison approach)
5. Income potential - for commercial properties, rental income or revenue potential
6. Zoning - current and potential future zoning restrictions
7. Environmental factors - contamination, proximity to hazardous waste sites
8. Market trends - supply/demand, interest rates, economic factors
9. Unique features - swimming pool, views, distinctive characteristics
10. Legal issues - pending litigation, easements affecting use

Used in determining residential, commercial, and industrial property values.
