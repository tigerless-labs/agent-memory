Movie recommendations for Mary Sunshine character research:

1920s-1930s:
- 42nd Street (1933) — Ruby Keeler as Peggy Sawyer
- Gold Diggers of 1933 (1933) — Ruby Keeler, Joan Blondell, Aline MacMahon  
- Footlight Parade (1933) — James Cagney, Joan Blondell, Ruby Keeler

1940s:
- Cover Girl (1944) — Rita Hayworth as Rusty Parker
- Gilda (1946) — Rita Hayworth as nightclub performer
- Lady in the Dark (1944) — Ginger Rogers as Liza Elliott

1950s:
- Singin' in the Rain (1952) — Debbie Reynolds as Kathy Selden
- The Band Wagon (1953) — Fred Astaire, Cyd Charisse, Nanette Fabray
- I Love Melvin (1953) — Debbie Reynolds as Judy Schneider

Also recommended: films featuring Bette Davis, Joan Crawford, Barbara Stanwyck for strong, dynamic performances.
