**Asana Pricing:**
- Free: up to 15 users, limited features
- Premium: $9.99/user/month (billed annually)
- Business: $19.99/user/month (billed annually)  
- Enterprise: custom pricing
- 30-day free trial available
- Discounts: 10% for annual billing, 50% for non-profits and educational institutions

**Key Features:**
- Tasks and subtasks with descriptions
- Projects and pre-built templates
- Custom workflows and automation
- Due dates and reminder notifications
- Task assignments and role-based permissions
- Team collaboration (comments, @mentions, file attachments)
- Reporting and custom dashboards
- 100+ third-party integrations (Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, etc.)
- Native mobile apps (iOS and Android)

**Time Tracking:**
- Built-in time tracking feature (enable in Settings > Time Tracking)
- Start/stop timer button for real-time tracking
- Manual time logging option
- View time spent: in task details, task list columns, time reports, custom dashboards
