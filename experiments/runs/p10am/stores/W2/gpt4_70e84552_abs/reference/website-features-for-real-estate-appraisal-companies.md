A modern real estate appraisal company website should include these features:

1. Clean, professional design conveying brand and services
2. Responsive design optimized for mobile and all screen sizes
3. Services page describing residential, commercial, industrial appraisals, inspections, market analysis
4. About us page with company history, values, team, industry experience
5. Client testimonials for social proof and credibility
6. Contact page with phone, email, contact form
7. Online ordering system for appraisals and services
8. Blog to demonstrate expertise and provide valuable information
9. Property search tools filtered by location, price range, property type
10. Industry news and updates section

For residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
