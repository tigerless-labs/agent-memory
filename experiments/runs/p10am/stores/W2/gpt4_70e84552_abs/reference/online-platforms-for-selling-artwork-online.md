Platforms evaluated for selling artwork online:

**Etsy**: Marketplace for handmade, vintage, unique items. Best for original pieces and vintage posters. Fees: 0.20 per listing + 3.5% transaction fee + 3% payment processing. Example: 50 sale yields 3.70 in fees, net 46.30.

**Society6**: Sells prints and products (mugs, phone cases, tote bags). Handles production and shipping.

**Redbubble**: Enables artists to sell designs on stickers, apparel, accessories.

**Saatchi Art**: Online art gallery and marketplace for original paintings, sculptures, prints. Takes commission on sold pieces.

**Shopify**: E-commerce platform to create own store. Must handle production, shipping, marketing. Fees: 29-299 monthly subscription plus 2.4% plus 0.30 per transaction.

**DeviantArt**: Community-driven platform for artists to showcase work and sell prints and digital files.

**ArtPal**: Allows artists to sell prints, posters, and products with options for custom framing and mounting.

**Fine Art America**: Marketplace for original artwork, prints, and home decor with fine art focus.
