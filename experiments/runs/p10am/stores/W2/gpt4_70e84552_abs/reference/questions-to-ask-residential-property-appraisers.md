Key questions to ask when working with a residential property appraiser:

1. What is the current market value of the property?
2. How did you determine the market value?
3. What factors were considered in valuation?
4. What comparable properties were used?
5. How does the condition of the property impact its value?
6. How does the location impact its value?
7. How does the size impact its value?
8. How does the age impact its value?
9. How does the property type (single-family, townhouse, condo) impact its value?
10. Are there unique features that impact value?
11. How does the current real estate market impact the value?
12. What are current market trends and their impact?
13. Are there external factors like zoning or environmental issues?
14. What improvements or renovations could increase value?
15. How often should a property be appraised for accurate valuation?

These questions help understand the appraiser's methodology and reasoning.
