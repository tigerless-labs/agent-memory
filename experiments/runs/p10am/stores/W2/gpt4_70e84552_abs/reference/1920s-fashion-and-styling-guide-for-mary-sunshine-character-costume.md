1920s-inspired fashion elements for Mary Sunshine character:

Clothing:
- Flapper dresses with dropped waistline, flowing skirts, above-knee hemline
- Beaded or fringe dresses for glamour
- Cloche hats (felt, straw, decorated with feathers, flowers, or beads)
- High-waisted pants with loose-fitting tops or fitted jackets

Accessories:
- Pearl necklaces (single-strand or multi-strand)
- Art Deco jewelry with geometric patterns and bold designs (diamonds, rubies, emeralds)
- Long gloves in silk or kid leather
- Mary Janes or T-strap shoes with low to moderate heel and rounded toe

Hair and Makeup:
- Bobbed hair with center parting and loose, curly locks (Mary Sunshine's signature)
- Bold matte red lipstick
- Rouge for cheeks and powder for subtle matte finish

Style tips:
- Embrace the androgynous look with oversized clothing, hats, and accessories
- Mix and match different patterns, textures, and styles
- Feel free to interpret elements uniquely
- Have fun and embrace spirit of self-expression
