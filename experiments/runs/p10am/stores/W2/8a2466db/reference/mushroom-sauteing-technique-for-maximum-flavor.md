Pat mushrooms dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture. Use large hot skillet (stainless steel or cast iron). Heat pan over medium-high heat. Add oil and let shimmer. Add mushrooms in single layer - do not overcrowd; cook in batches if needed. Sear 2-3 minutes on first side until golden brown. Stir and continue cooking 5-7 minutes total until tender and caramelized. Add aromatics like onions, garlic, thyme during cooking. Cook until liquid evaporates. Do not overcook or mushrooms will become mushy.
