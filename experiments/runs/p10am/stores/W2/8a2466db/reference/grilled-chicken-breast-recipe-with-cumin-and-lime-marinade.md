**Ingredients:**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro leaves (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (about 400°F/200°C)
2. In small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lime juice, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper
3. Place chicken breasts in shallow dish and brush marinade on both sides
4. Marinate for at least 15-20 minutes, or up to 30 minutes in refrigerator
5. Grill chicken for 5-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C)
6. Let rest for a few minutes before slicing thinly against the grain
7. Garnish with chopped cilantro if desired

**Variations:** Add diced bell peppers to grill with chicken; substitute lime juice with lemon or orange juice; add cayenne pepper or red pepper flakes for spice; marinate longer (2-3 hours or overnight) for deeper flavor.
