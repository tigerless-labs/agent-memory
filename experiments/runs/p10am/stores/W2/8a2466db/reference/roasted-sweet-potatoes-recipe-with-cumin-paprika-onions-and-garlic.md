**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (about 1-inch cubes)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Diced onions (optional, for extra flavor)
- Minced garlic (optional, for extra flavor)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss sweet potato cubes with olive oil, salt, black pepper, cumin, and smoked paprika until evenly coated
3. Add diced onions and minced garlic if desired
4. Spread in single layer on parchment-lined baking sheet
5. Roast for 20-25 minutes until tender and caramelized, flipping halfway through
6. Cool slightly before adding to bowl

**Tips:** Can also cut as wedges or thin slices for different texture; add aromatics like thyme or rosemary for extra flavor; roast at higher temperature (450°F) for crispier exterior.
