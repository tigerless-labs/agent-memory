Use rustic Italian bread or baguette. Mix softened butter (high-quality, salted) with 2-3 minced garlic cloves and dried/fresh herbs like parsley, thyme, or oregano. Start with less garlic and adjust to taste. Spread thin even layer of garlic butter on 1-inch bread slices. Toast in oven at 350°F for 8-10 minutes until lightly browned and crispy. Season with salt and pepper. Optional: top with grated Parmesan or fresh herbs before toasting. Serve warm alongside pasta. Pairs beautifully with the earthy truffle flavors.
