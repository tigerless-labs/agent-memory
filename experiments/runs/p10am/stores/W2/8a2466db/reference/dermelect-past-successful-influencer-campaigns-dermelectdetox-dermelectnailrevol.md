Three past successful Dermelect influencer marketing campaigns:\n\n1. #DermelectDetox - Promoted detoxifying skincare products. Influencers shared daily skincare routines using Dermelect products with before-and-after results. Multi-platform content (Instagram posts, stories, YouTube videos, blog posts). Result: significant increase in website traffic and product sales.\n\n2. #DermelectNailRevolution - Highlighted nail care line including strengthening treatments and polishes. Content included nail care tips, tutorials, before-and-after transformations. Multi-platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Result: boosted brand awareness and generated buzz around nail care offerings.\n\n3. #DermelectSelfCare - Promoted brand values and self-care importance. Influencers shared personal self-care routines featuring Dermelect products, emphasizing investment in wellbeing. Multi-platform (Instagram posts, stories, YouTube videos). Result: sparked conversations around self-care and encouraged audience to explore products and prioritize wellbeing.\n\nCampaigns informed current influencer marketing strategy refinements.
