## Breakfast Spots in Riverside, CA (May 2023)

**The Breakfast Club** — Retro-style diner; outdoor patio (limited, first-come-first-served); typical wait 15-30 minutes on weekends/peak hours (9-11am); specialty dishes: Breakfast Club sandwich, waffle dishes; recommend visiting before 8am or after 11am to avoid waits

**Simple Simon's Bakery & Bistro** — Family-owned bakery and cafe; fresh pastries, sandwiches, breakfast dishes; try quiche or breakfast burrito

**The Salted Pig** — Trendy breakfast spot with locally sourced ingredients; try Pig Benedict or Chilaquiles

**Gram's Place** — Cozy breakfast spot with vintage vibe; fluffy pancakes, omelets, breakfast sandwiches

**Cupcakes & Breakfast** — Serves both breakfast dishes and cupcakes; try breakfast tacos or quiche
