**Ingredients:**
- 3 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Blend avocado, red onion, jalapeño, lime juice, garlic, salt, and black pepper until smooth
2. Taste and adjust seasoning if desired
3. Stir in chopped cilantro
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow flavors to meld

**Variations:** Add Greek yogurt or sour cream for tangier sauce; squeeze of orange or grapefruit juice for different flavor; use as dip for veggies, chips, or on tacos and grilled meats.
