Grilled Chicken: Season with thyme, rosemary, or parsley. Grill 5-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Let rest before slicing.

Pan-Seared Scallops: Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice. Sear in hot skillet with oil for 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown.

Crispy Bacon or Pancetta: Cook until crispy in skillet over medium heat. Chop into small pieces and sprinkle on pasta. Drain excess fat from pan.

Sautéed Shrimp: Heat butter or oil in skillet over medium heat. Cook shrimp 2-3 minutes per side until pink and cooked through. Season with salt, pepper, parsley.

Pan-Seared Steak: Use tender cuts like filet mignon or ribeye. Sear 3-5 minutes per side in hot skillet to desired doneness. Let rest before slicing thinly.

Roasted Turkey or Chicken Sausage: Slice sausage and roast at 400°F, or slice and add to pasta for additional spice and flavor.
