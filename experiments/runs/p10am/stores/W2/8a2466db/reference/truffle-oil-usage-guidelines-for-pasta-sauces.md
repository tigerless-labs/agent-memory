Choose high-quality truffle oil made with real truffle essence; avoid artificial flavorings. Use sparingly - start with 1-2 teaspoons and adjust to taste, as truffle oil is potent. Add truffle oil toward the end of sauce preparation to preserve the delicate truffle flavor, as high heat causes it to evaporate. Allow the oil to infuse into the sauce for 1-2 minutes before serving for flavors to meld. Balance the strong truffle flavor with other ingredients like cream, Parmesan, and herbs. Truffle oil should enhance, not overpower the dish.
