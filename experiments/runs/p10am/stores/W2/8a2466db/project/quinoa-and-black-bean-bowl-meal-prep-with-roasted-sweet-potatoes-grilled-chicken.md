Customized bowl project combining:
- Cooked quinoa and black beans as base
- Grilled chicken breast seasoned with cumin, paprika, and lime
- Roasted sweet potatoes with diced onions and minced garlic
- Diced bell peppers grilled with chicken
- Homemade avocado sauce with lime, cilantro, and jalapeño
- Optional toppings: cilantro, salsa, cheese, lime wedges

Also planning to use leftover quinoa for quick breakfasts with mixed berries, banana, nuts, and seeds.
