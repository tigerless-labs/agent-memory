Films to watch:

- **The Women (1939)** — classic Hollywood film about high-society women dealing with infidelity and betrayal
- **Annihilation (2018)** — sci-fi thriller with a team of female scientists on a dangerous expedition
- **Little Women (2019)** — modern retelling of the classic novel, received 6 Academy Award nominations including Best Picture
- **Bridesmaids (2011)** — comedy about female friends navigating competing interests and personalities
- **A League of Their Own (1992)** — classic sports film about the first professional all-female baseball league; starring Tom Hanks, Geena Davis, Madonna
- **The Heat (2013)** — action-comedy about an FBI agent and Boston detective teaming up
- **The Help (2011)** — drama about African-American maids working in white households in Jackson, Mississippi during the early 1960s
