Prevent tarnishing: store in cool dry place away from humidity, use anti-tarnish bags/cloths, keep away from direct sunlight, avoid chemicals (bleach, ammonia, chlorine), store separately from other metals, use silica gel packets, clean regularly with soft cloth and mild soap, use silver polishing cloth as needed, consult jeweler if needed.
