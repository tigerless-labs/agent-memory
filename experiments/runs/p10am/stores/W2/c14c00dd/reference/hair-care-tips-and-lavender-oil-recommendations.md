**General Hair Care:**
- Use hydrating conditioner and weekly hair masks with argan oil, coconut oil, shea butter, or keratin
- Use gentle, sulfate-free products to avoid stripping natural oils
- Use heat protectant spray; limit heat styling to 1-2x per week
- Get regular trims every 6-8 weeks to prevent split ends
- Use wide-tooth comb, starting from ends and working up
- Wash every other day or every 2-3 days, not daily
- Use microfiber towel or old t-shirt instead of regular towels
- Eat balanced diet with omega-3s, vitamins, minerals; foods like salmon, walnuts, spinach, eggs, nuts
- Reduce stress through meditation, yoga, breathing exercises
- Protect from sun with hat or SPF hair products

**Lavender Benefits:**
- Soothes itchy scalp and promotes relaxation
- Pre-shampoo treatment: mix lavender oil with olive oil
- Helps reduce dandruff and flakiness
