**Exfoliating Gloves:**
- Sugarbear Exfoliating Gloves: gentle, plush, for daily exfoliation
- Dermalogica Daily Microfoliant Gloves: unique texture, gentle enough for daily use
- Kiko Exfoliating Gloves: intense exfoliation, better for thicker skin

**Scrubbers:**
- Konjac Sponge: gentle, natural fibers, suitable for all skin types including sensitive
- Dr. Dennis Gross Skincare alpha beta peel Body & Face Scrub: physical and chemical exfoliants combined
- The Body Shop Himalayan Salt Scrub: with sweet almond oil, use 1-2 times per week

Gloves are better for daily use; scrubbers for 1-2x weekly. More gentle than loofahs.
