**Cleaning Schedule:**
- Daily: Quick pick-up of sink, toilet seat, faucet; wipe shower and bathtub after use
- Weekly: Clean mirrors/glass, wipe toilet handle and surrounding areas, clean sink/faucet, mop/sweep floor
- Bi-weekly: Deep clean shower, bathtub, walls, toilet (inside/out)
- Monthly: Deep clean bathroom including grout, caulk, stubborn stains

**Apps Recommended:**
- Habitica: Gamifies habits with reminders and reward system (chosen by user)
- Clean My Space: Customizable cleaning schedules and reminders
- Home Routines: Custom cleaning schedules, routines, and reminders
- AnyList: Task management with custom lists and reminders
- Google Keep or Apple Notes: Simple note-taking for schedule and reminders
