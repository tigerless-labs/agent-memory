## Laxmi Worship in Hindu Culture

Laxmi, the goddess of wealth, prosperity, and good fortune, is worshipped through several key rituals:

### Major Worship Occasions
- **Diwali**: Five-day festival in October/November; primary occasion for Laxmi worship with offerings of flowers, incense, and sweets
- **Laxmi Puja**: Special prayer ceremony performed on Diwali with lighting of lamps/candles, offerings of flowers/sweets/fruits, and chanting of mantras

### Key Rituals
- **Havan**: Sacred fire ritual burning special herbs and food items; used to seek Laxmi's blessings and purify the environment
- **Aarti**: Waving an oil lamp in front of the deity while singing devotional songs; expresses devotion and reverence
- **Vrat**: Fasting to show devotion; can abstain from food or certain foods for varying periods (day, week, month)

### Ritual Symbolism
- **Lamps/Candles**: Symbolize light dispelling darkness and ignorance; invite the goddess into the home; honor the fire element
- **Flowers**: Symbol of beauty, purity, and life; Laxmi's favorites include lotus, marigold, rose, hibiscus, jasmine, and chrysanthemum
- **Fasting**: Purifies mind and body; develops self-discipline and spiritual awareness; shows willingness to make sacrifices for blessings

### Benefits Sought
- Prosperity, abundance, and good fortune
- Purification and spiritual growth
- Self-discipline and deeper connection with the divine
