Panko breadcrumb mixture for chicken parmesan:

**Ratio per pound of chicken:**
- 1-2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 to 1 cup grated parmesan cheese

**Basic mixture example:**
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Tips:**
- Use freshly grated parmesan for best flavor
- Check store-bought Italian seasoning blend for added salt
- Don't overmix as it can become dense
- Chill breaded chicken for 30 minutes before frying for extra crispiness
- Optional additions: garlic powder, dried basil, oregano
