Cold brew herbal tea process:\n1. Use glass or plastic container with lid (Mason jar recommended)\n2. Add 1-2 tablespoons loose-leaf tea per 8 oz cold water\n3. Steep 8-12 hours or overnight in refrigerator\n4. Strain and enjoy\n\nBenefits: Prevents bitter compounds from hot water, results in smoother/more refreshing flavor, gentler on delicate herbs. Works well with peppermint, hibiscus, lemon balm, and rosehip.\n\nCan also make concentrates: steep larger amount of herbs in smaller water, then dilute to desired strength.
