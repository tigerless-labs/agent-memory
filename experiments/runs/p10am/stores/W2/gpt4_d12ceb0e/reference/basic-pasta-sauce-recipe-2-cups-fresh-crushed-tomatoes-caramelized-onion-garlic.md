Simple pasta sauce recipe for rich and tangy flavor:

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups fresh crushed tomatoes
- 1 large onion, caramelized
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup red wine (optional)
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- Fresh basil leaves, chopped (optional)

**Method:** Combine caramelized onions, garlic, olive oil, and red wine in a saucepan. Add tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Simmer over low heat for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
