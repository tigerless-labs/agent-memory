Popular coffee creamer flavors to explore:\n- Vanilla Sweet Cream: Classic, smooth, velvety texture\n- Caramel: Rich, buttery, indulgent\n- Hazelnut: Nutty, smooth, aromatic\n- Cinnamon: Warm, spicy\n- Peppermint Mocha: Refreshing, combines peppermint and chocolate\n- Pumpkin Spice: Seasonal, cozy cinnamon/nutmeg/ginger flavors\n- Irish Cream: Creamy, sweet, luxurious\n- Coconut Cream: Dairy-free option, tropical flavor\n- Smores: Playful blend of chocolate, marshmallow, graham cracker\n- Gingerbread: Warm, spicy, sweet\n\nCan also make custom creamer with coconut oil, heavy cream, and flavor extracts.
