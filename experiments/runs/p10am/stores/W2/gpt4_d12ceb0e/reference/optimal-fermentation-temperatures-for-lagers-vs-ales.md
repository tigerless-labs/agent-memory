**Lagers:** Ferment at 45–55°F (7–13°C) for crisp, clean, smooth beer; condition at 32–40°F (0–4°C) to mature and reduce harsh flavors.

**Ales:** Ferment at 60–75°F (15–24°C) for fruity, spicy, complex flavors; condition at 55–65°F (13–18°C) to clear and remove chill haze.
