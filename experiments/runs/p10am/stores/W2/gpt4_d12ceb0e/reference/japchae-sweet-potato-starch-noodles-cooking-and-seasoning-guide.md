Cooking technique and seasoning guide for japchae (Korean sweet potato starch noodles):

**Achieving perfect texture:**
1. Choose 100% sweet potato starch noodles (dangmyeon) or combination with potato starch
2. Soak noodles in cold water for at least 30 minutes to rehydrate
3. Cook in boiling water 5-7 minutes (follow package instructions)
4. Drain and rinse in cold water to stop cooking
5. Stir-fry with 1-2 tablespoons oil to develop chewy texture and prevent sticking

**Seasoning blend:**
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon gochujang (Korean chili paste, optional)
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil (added near end to preserve flavor)
- Garlic, ginger, and aromatics sautéed first

**Optional additions:**
- Mixed vegetables (spinach, carrots, bell peppers) for texture
- Chopped green onions and toasted sesame seeds for garnish

**Key principle:** Balance sweet and savory; add vegetables and umami elements for depth.
