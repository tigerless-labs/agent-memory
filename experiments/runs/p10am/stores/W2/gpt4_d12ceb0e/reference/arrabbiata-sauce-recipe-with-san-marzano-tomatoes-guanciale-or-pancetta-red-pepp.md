Classic spicy arrabbiata pasta sauce recipe:

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups San Marzano tomatoes, crushed by hand
- 6 slices of guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil
- 1 pinch of sugar (optional)

**Method:**
1. Cook guanciale or pancetta in olive oil until crispy; remove and set aside
2. Sauté garlic and onions in same pan until softened
3. Add crushed tomatoes, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper; stir well
4. Return cooked guanciale/pancetta to sauce
5. Simmer for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally
6. Taste and adjust seasoning
7. Serve with pasta, garnished with fresh basil and parmesan

**Tips:** Use high-quality San Marzano tomatoes; guanciale is preferable to pancetta for authentic flavor.
