Next pasta dish project: combining arrabbiata sauce with:
- Grilled chicken breasts (marinated in olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, thyme, rosemary; grill 5-7 min per side to 165°F internal temp)
- Roasted vegetables: zucchini and bell peppers (toss with olive oil, seasonings; roast at 425°F for 20-25 minutes, optionally brush with balsamic-honey glaze last 10 minutes)

**Assembly:** Slice grilled chicken, toss pasta with arrabbiata sauce, add chicken and roasted vegetables, top with parmesan and fresh basil.
