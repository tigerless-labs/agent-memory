Quick nutrient-dense snacks without excess sugar, salt, or unhealthy fats: apple or celery slices with almond or peanut butter; roasted chickpeas seasoned with paprika, garlic powder, salt; Greek yogurt with fresh berries and honey; hummus with carrots, celery, or pita chips; trail mix with nuts, dried fruit, dark chocolate chips; whole-grain crackers with avocado or guacamole; smoothies with frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, spinach or kale; energy balls made with dates, nuts, and coconut. Suitable for between-meal snacks or as healthy desserts.
