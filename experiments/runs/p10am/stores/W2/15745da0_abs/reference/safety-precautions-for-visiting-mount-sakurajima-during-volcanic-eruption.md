When visiting Mount Sakurajima during/after eruption:

- Stay alert for warnings and follow local authority guidance
- Wear protective gear: dust mask, goggles, long-sleeved clothing
- Avoid inhaling volcanic ash or gases (respiratory hazard)
- Stay away from lava, pyroclastic flows, hazardous areas
- Do not touch or eat volcanic ash
- Drive carefully; avoid low-lying areas and coastlines (flooding/tsunami risk)
- Prepare for power outages and transportation disruptions
- Follow local regulations and respect the environment
- Consult professional guides or photography experts for safe volcano photography
