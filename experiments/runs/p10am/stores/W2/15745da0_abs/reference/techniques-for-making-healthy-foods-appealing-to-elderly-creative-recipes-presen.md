Creative recipes with new preparations of foods they enjoy. Integrate healthy ingredients into familiar dishes (spinach in pasta sauce, berries in smoothies). Presentation matters: colorful plates, fun-shaped cuts, garnishes. Adapt texture for those with chewing/swallowing difficulty by pureeing into smoothies or steaming/roasting until soft. Involve elderly in meal planning and preparation for greater engagement. Be patient when introducing new foods, allow time for adjustment, introduce gradually in small amounts.
