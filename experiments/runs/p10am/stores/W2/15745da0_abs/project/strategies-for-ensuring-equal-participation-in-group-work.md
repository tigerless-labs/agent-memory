1. Set clear expectations for participation and contribution at the beginning
2. Provide individual assignments that contribute to the overall project
3. Assign specific group roles (leader, recorder, timekeeper)
4. Encourage active listening so all voices are heard
5. Monitor participation and provide feedback to address unequal participation early
