### Purpose
Define objective criteria to determine whether appraisal orders qualify as complex and justify higher service fees.

### Evaluation Categories
1. **Location** — Remote access, unique environmental features, crime rate, street layout challenges
2. **Property type and design** — Unusual/complex design, special features (pools, decks), complex systems (geothermal, radiant heat), outbuildings
3. **Age and condition** — Significantly older/newer than area norm, extensive repairs needed, unusual wear/damage, major alterations
4. **Zoning and land use** — Floodplain/conservation/historic district designation, HOA restrictions, wetlands, zoning variances
5. **Market conditions** — Rapidly appreciating/declining markets, vacation home areas, high-end properties, economic trends, supply/demand factors

### Key Principle
Appraisers' complexity assessments require scrutiny due to fee incentives and potential conflicts of interest. Team must apply objective criteria and independent analysis rather than accepting appraiser designation at face value.
