Values ethical storytelling in true crime that:
- Respects victims and families' dignity and privacy
- Focuses on victims' experiences rather than glorifying perpetrators
- Promotes awareness of systemic issues and prevention
- Prioritizes justice and education over sensationalization
- Recognizes the real people and real tragedies behind each story

Sees herself as a conscious consumer with responsibility to hold both creators and herself accountable for how these narratives are told and consumed.
