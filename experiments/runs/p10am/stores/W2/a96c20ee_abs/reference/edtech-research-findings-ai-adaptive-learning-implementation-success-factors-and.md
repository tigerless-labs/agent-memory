**Critical Implementation Challenges Across Case Studies**: Teacher professional development and ongoing training needed for effective system use; curriculum alignment with learning goals essential for teacher adoption and effectiveness; data privacy and security concerns; equity and access disparities; technical infrastructure investment required.

**Success Factors**: When systems clearly aligned with curriculum and learning goals, teachers more likely to adopt and use effectively; ongoing implementation support critical.

**Key Recommendations**:
1. Educators/policymakers: Prioritize ongoing teacher professional development and sustained implementation support
2. Developers: Collaborate closely with educators to ensure systems designed with teacher/student needs in mind; provide clear curriculum alignment guidance
3. General: Establish ongoing evaluation and assessment processes; address algorithmic bias to prevent exacerbating existing inequities

**Equity and Inclusion Implications**: Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students, but algorithmic bias poses significant risk if not carefully addressed.

**Stakeholder Tensions Identified**: Debate over teacher role in AI systems—some educators feared replacement, others viewed systems as valuable teaching support tools.
