Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) per 2 cups vegetables: Mild 1/4-1/2 tsp, Medium 1/2-1 tsp, Hot 1-2 tsp. Garlic: 2-3 cloves per 2 cups. Ginger: 1-2 inches grated per 2 cups. Tips: measure by weight for accuracy; taste as you go; combine with garlic, ginger, fish sauce, gochujang; handle gently; rinse over-salted kimchi under cold water.
