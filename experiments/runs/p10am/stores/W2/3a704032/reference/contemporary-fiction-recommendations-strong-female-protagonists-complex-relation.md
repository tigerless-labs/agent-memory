The Devil Wears Prada by Lauren Weisberger - Boss-assistant relationship, female characters

The Mothers by Brit Bennett - Coming-of-age in Southern California, motherhood and identity

The Girls by Emma Cline - 1960s, teenage girls in cult-like group, female relationships and power dynamics

The Interestings by Meg Wolitzer - Friends from summer camp for the arts, relationships over time

Conversations with Friends by Sally Rooney - Complex relationships between young women and couple, power and identity

The Female Persuasion by Meg Wolitzer - Young woman with famous feminist mentor, identity and power dynamics

The Last Romantics by Tara Conklin - Four siblings, complex relationships shaping each other lives

The Age of Light by Whitney Scharer - Based on true story of Lee Miller (model/photographer, 1920s-30s Paris), relationships and identity
