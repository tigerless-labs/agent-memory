Custom basil-friendly potting mix recipe:
- 2 parts peat moss or coconut coir
- 1 part perlite or vermiculite
- 1 part compost or worm castings
- 1 part general-purpose potting soil

Basil prefers:
- Well-draining soil (to prevent root rot)
- pH balance 6.0-7.0 (slightly acidic to neutral)
- Soil rich in organic matter
- Good aeration
- Nutrient-rich environment (basil is a heavy feeder)

Alternatively, look for pre-made potting mixes specifically designed for herbs or vegetables.
