Monstress by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda - Fantasy epic, young woman with supernatural abilities, stunning art, moral ambiguity

The Wicked + The Divine by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie - Mythology meets pop culture, gods as pop stars

Black Hammer by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston - Superhero deconstruction, heroes stranded on rural farm

The Umbrella Academy by Gerard Way and Gabriel Ba - Dysfunctional superhero family, action and humor

Descender by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen - Sci-fi, young robot boy hunted by humans, explores identity and humanity

The Goddamned by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guera - Biblical and mythological figures in dark reality

Y: The Last Man by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra - Last human male on Earth, explores gender and power
