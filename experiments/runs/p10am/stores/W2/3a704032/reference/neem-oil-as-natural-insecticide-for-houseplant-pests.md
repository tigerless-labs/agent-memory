Neem oil is a gentle and effective natural insecticide for controlling a wide range of household pests.

Pests it controls:
- Spider mites
- Mealybugs
- Springtails
- Whiteflies
- Fungus gnats
- Scale insects
- And many others

How it works:
- Disrupts the life cycle of pests
- Natural origin, less harsh than chemical pesticides

Application:
- Mix with water according to product label instructions
- Spray thoroughly on plant leaves and stems
- Apply all-over coverage for best results
- May need repeat applications (follow product guidance)

Advantages:
- Non-toxic and safe for plants
- More gentle than chemical alternatives
- Good for organic pest management

Note: Always follow product label instructions for proper dilution and application.
