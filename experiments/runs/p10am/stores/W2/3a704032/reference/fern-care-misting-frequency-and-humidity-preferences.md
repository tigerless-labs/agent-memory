Ferns thrive in humid environments with 40-60% relative humidity.

Misting recommendations:
- Frequency: 2-3 times per week (adjust based on temperature and local humidity)
- Water type: Use distilled or filtered water to avoid mineral buildup
- Timing: Mist in the morning so leaves can dry slightly before nightfall
- Technique: Use a fine mist; avoid strong jets that can dislodge pests
- Coverage: Mist undersides of leaves thoroughly (pests often hide there)
- Avoid: Don't get water on the crown (where leaves meet stem) to prevent rot

Benefits:
- Maintains humid microclimate ferns prefer
- Helps prevent fungal diseases
- Can help dislodge light pest infestations

Note: Good air circulation also helps prevent fungal diseases and pest problems.
