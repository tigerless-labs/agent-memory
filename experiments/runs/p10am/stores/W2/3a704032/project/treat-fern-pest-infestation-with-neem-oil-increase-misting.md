Fern has tiny moving dot pests (likely spider mites or springtails) on leaves and stems.

Treatment plan:
1. Isolate fern from other plants to prevent pest spread
2. Prune any heavily infested leaves or stems
3. Apply neem oil treatment:
   - Mix neem oil with water per product instructions
   - Spray thoroughly on all leaf surfaces, especially undersides
   - Spray stems and soil surface
4. Allow neem oil to take effect (typically several days)
5. Begin misting routine:
   - Mist 2-3 times per week with distilled/filtered water
   - Mist in morning for best results
   - Use fine mist on leaf undersides where pests hide
6. Monitor plant closely for:
   - Signs of continued pest activity
   - Plant recovery
   - Possible need for repeat neem oil application
7. If problem persists, consult local nursery or garden expert

Pest identification clues to watch:
- Spider mites: move quickly, may see fine webbing
- Springtails: may jump or hop, attracted to moist environments
