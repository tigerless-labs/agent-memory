Recommended platforms for facilitating student discussion and reflection in digital literacy:

1. Padlet — Collaborative virtual platform for sharing thoughts and ideas

2. Nearpod — Interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions for engagement

3. Flipgrid — Video discussion platform where students respond with short videos

4. Google Jamboard — Virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual idea-sharing

Discussion and Reflection Methods:
- Open-ended questions (require explanation, not yes/no answers)
- Paraphrase and summarize student responses
- Active listening exercises (students summarize peers, ask follow-ups)
- Visual aids (diagrams, graphic organizers)
