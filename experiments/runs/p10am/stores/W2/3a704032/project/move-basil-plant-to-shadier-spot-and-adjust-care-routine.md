Basil plant currently struggling with too much direct sunlight on the balcony.

Steps to relocate and adjust care:
1. Move to shadier location on balcony or provide filtered sunlight (east/west-facing preferred)
2. Can use sheer curtain or shade cloth to filter intense sunlight
3. Adjust watering: soil will dry more slowly in shade, so check moisture before watering
4. Maintain humidity: consider placing pot on tray with water and pebbles or use humidifier
5. Fertilize sparingly with balanced, water-soluble fertilizer at half strength
6. Prune regularly: pinch off flower buds to encourage leaf growth; trim leggy stems
7. Monitor temperature: keep between 65°F-75°F (18°C-24°C)
8. Observe plant response and adjust care accordingly

Expected improvement: Plant should recover as it adjusts to reduced sunlight stress.
