Snake plant (gift from sister, arrived late April 2023) is thriving with current watering and fertilizer routine as of May 2023.

Decision: Hold off on repotting until clear signs of need.

Repotting only when:
- Roots are truly root-bound (circling pot or growing out of drainage holes)
- Soil is noticeably depleted or breaking down
- Plant has significant new growth requiring more space

Current status: No signs of repotting need at this time. Plant is healthy and happy.

Snake plant repotting preferences (if/when needed):
- Use pot only 1-2 sizes larger (these plants prefer slight root-bound conditions)
- Use well-draining potting mix (susceptible to root rot if soil too wet)
- Optionally prune circling/tangled roots
- Water sparingly after repotting; monitor for shock
- Best timing for repotting: spring or summer during active growth period
