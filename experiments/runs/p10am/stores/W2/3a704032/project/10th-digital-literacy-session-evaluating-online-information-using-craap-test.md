Session Focus: Teaching 6th-grade students to evaluate credibility and reliability of online sources.

Key Evaluation Method: CRAAP Test
- Currency: Is the information up-to-date?
- Relevance: Does it relate to the topic?
- Authority: Who is the author? What are their credentials?
- Accuracy: Is the information supported by evidence?
- Purpose: Why does this source exist?

Lesson Activities:
1. Intro to source evaluation
2. Teach CRAAP Test with sample articles
3. Students research, find two sources, evaluate
4. Peer presentations on credibility
5. Class discussion of evaluation tips
6. Conclusion and application
