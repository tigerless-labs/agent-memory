Has a fern plant with pest infestation. Pests appear as tiny, moving dots crawling around on leaves and stems. Pest type not definitively identified but likely spider mites or springtails. Decided to treat with neem oil and increase misting frequency to 2-3 times per week. Will monitor plant closely for improvement.
