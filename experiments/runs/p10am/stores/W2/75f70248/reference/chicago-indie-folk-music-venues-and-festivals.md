**Venues:**
- The Hideout (Logan Square) - weekly Tuesday open mic
- Schubas Tavern (Lakeview)
- The Empty Bottle (Ukrainian Village)
- Lincoln Hall (Lincoln Park)
- The Whistler (Logan Square) - weekly Wednesday singer-songwriter showcase
- Uncommon Ground (Wrigleyville) - coffee shop with open mics
- The Elbo Room (Lakeview)

**Festivals:**
- Chicago Folk Festival (June, Grant Park, free)
- Windy City Smokeout (July, country/folk/Americana)
- Lollapalooza (July-August, features indie folk acts)
- Taste of Randolph (June, West Loop street festival)
