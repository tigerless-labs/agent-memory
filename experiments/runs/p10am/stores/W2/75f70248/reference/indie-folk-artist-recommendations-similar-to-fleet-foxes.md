**Similar to Fleet Foxes:**
- Beirut (eclectic folk, world music, indie rock)
- The Tallest Man on Earth (Swedish folk-rock)
- Grizzly Bear (harmony-rich indie rock)
- The Lumineers (Denver-based folk-rock trio)
- First Aid Kit (Swedish folk-pop duo with lush harmonies)

**Other related artists:**
- The Head and the Heart (Seattle indie folk)
- The Avett Brothers (folk-rock storytelling)
- Mumford & Sons (British folk-rock)
- The Decemberists (Portland indie folk)
- Lord Huron (Michigan-based dreamy indie folk)
