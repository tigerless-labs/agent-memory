**Similar to Bon Iver:**
- S. Carey (former Bon Iver band member)
- Volcano Choir (Justin Vernon project with electronic elements)
- The Antlers (introspective, emotive soundscapes)
- James Blake (soulful, electronic-infused indie)
- Ben Howard (British singer-songwriter, introspective acoustic)
