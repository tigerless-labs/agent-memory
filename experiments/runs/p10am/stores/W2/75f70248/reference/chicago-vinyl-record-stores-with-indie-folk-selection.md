**Independent Record Stores:**
- Reckless Records (Wicker Park, Lakeview, Logan Square) - extensive new/used vinyl, strong indie folk section
- Permanent Records (Ukrainian Village) - vast selection, indie/folk/singer-songwriter focus
- Laurie's Planet of Sound (Lincoln Square) - curated new/used vinyl
- Saki (Logan Square) - record store and art gallery

**Used Record Stores:**
- The Exchange (Lakeview)
- Crossroads Music (Avondale)
- Dave's Records (Lincoln Square)

**Specialty:**
- The Vinyl Conflict (Logan Square) - punk/indie/underground, some indie folk
- Bric-a-Brac Records (Avondale) - record store and vintage shop
