**Music Schools/Lessons:**
- Old Town School of Folk Music (Lincoln Square) - folk and acoustic guitar focus
- Guitar Center (multiple locations) - private and group lessons
- The Music Gallery (Lakeview) - indie and folk music focus
- The Guitar Workshop (Logan Square) - indie, folk, rock focus
- Fretboard Music (Avondale) - folk, rock, indie music
- The Music Room (Ravenswood) - folk, indie, acoustic focus
- Folk Guitar Lessons Chicago (North Center) - specialized in folk
- Chicago Guitar Lessons (multiple locations) - indie and folk specialists
