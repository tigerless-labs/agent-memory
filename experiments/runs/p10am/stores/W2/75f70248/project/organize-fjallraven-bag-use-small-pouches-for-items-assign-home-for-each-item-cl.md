Organization strategy for brown Fjallraven cross-body bag:\n\n- Use small pouches or bags to group similar items (earbuds, sunscreen, makeup, etc.)\n- Assign a specific compartment or pocket as 'home' for each item type\n- Utilize front pocket for frequently accessed items (phone, wallet)\n- Use water bottle pocket for water bottle\n- Keep main compartment organized with dividers\n- Purge unnecessary items regularly\n- Clean bag exterior with soft dry cloth\n- Spot clean stains with appropriate cleaner\n- Air out bag regularly to prevent musty smells
