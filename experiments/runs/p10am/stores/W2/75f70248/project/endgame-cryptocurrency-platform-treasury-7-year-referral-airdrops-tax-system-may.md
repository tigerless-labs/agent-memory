# #Endgame Platform Project

Documented: May 22, 2023

## Treasury Management
Calculated buys/sells to maintain token growth; rebalance validator investments for consistent buybacks; buyback or burn tokens for sustainable growth.

## 7-Year Referral System
- Referred users earn rewards on friends transactions for 7 years
- Referred friends get discount for 7 years  
- Non-participants pay full price + taxes
- dApp: dapp.endgame.black

## Airdrop Raffles
Periodic drops for newcomers with Endgame or Reward token rewards.

## Sales Tax Structure
Base 20 percent (minus 10 percent with referral)

- Reward token reflection: 3 percent (minus 1 percent if referred)
- Liquidity/RFV/Treasury/Champion/Chart Management/Burning: 14 percent (minus 8 percent if referred)
- Battle Royal Champion: 2 percent (minus 1 percent if referred)
- Rebaser wallet: 1 percent

## Dynamic Whale Tax
1–10 percent range (scales with transaction size); proceeds to reward token purchases.

## Long-term Vision
Approach zero-tax structure through community-decided adjustments as infrastructure builds.
