## Event
- Family reunion planned for next summer (2024)
- Coincides with nephew's high school graduation
- Format: backyard BBQ + slideshow

## Slideshow Specifications
- Duration: 5–10 minutes max
- Structure: chronological from childhood through high school graduation
- Music: mix of instrumental tracks and popular songs sentimental to nephew; mellow and concise to avoid overpowering photos
- Suggested instrumental artists: Thomas Newman, Hans Zimmer, John Williams, Ludovico Einaudi, Max Richter, Olafur Arnalds

## Content Mix
**Photo types to include:**
- Sports achievements and team photos
- School events (prom, homecoming, plays, concerts)
- Family memories (vacations, holidays, everyday moments)
- Personal milestones and hobbies
- Humorous moments and inside jokes
- Graduation day photos

**Captions/quotes approach:** heartfelt but not cheesy; include mix of personal captions, inspirational quotes, and humorous ones

## Design Approach
- High-quality, well-lit, in-focus photos only
- Vary pace between fast and slow sections
- Use animations/transitions sparingly (fade-ins, dissolves, slide-ins acceptable; avoid overwhelming effects)
- Include captions for context and personal anecdotes
- Get feedback from family before finalizing

## Tools mentioned
- Google Photos
- Adobe Spark
- iMovie or DaVinci Resolve
- Smilebox

## Notes
- Involve nephew in planning to ensure celebration reflects his preferences
- Balance between quantity and quality of photos
- Test and refine before presentation
