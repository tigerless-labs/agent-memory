From Narita Airport (NRT):
- N'EX (Narita Express): 60 min, 3000-4000 yen
- Keisei Skyliner: 45 min, 2500-3000 yen
- Airport Limousine Bus: 1-2 hours, 2000-3000 yen
- Taxi: 1-2 hours, 15000-20000 yen (140-180 USD)

From Haneda Airport (HND):
- Tokyo Monorail: 15 min, 500-600 yen
- Keikyu Railway: 30-40 min, 400-600 yen
- Airport Limousine Bus: 30-60 min, 1000-2000 yen
- Taxi: 30-60 min, 6000-10000 yen daytime (55-90 USD), 8000-14000 yen late night

Tips: Suica/Pasmo cards work on trains/buses/subways; use luggage services on trains for multiple bags; book taxis in advance for fixed prices; hotels often have discounted partnerships.
