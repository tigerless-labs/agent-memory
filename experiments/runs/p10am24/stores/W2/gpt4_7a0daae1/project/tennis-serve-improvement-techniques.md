Key techniques to develop: Continental grip (index finger on second groove); Stance (feet shoulder-width apart, body slightly turned right for right-hander); Toss (6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front, slightly right); Contact (at peak of toss with wrist/forearm for power); Follow-through (racket close to body); Serve variations (flat serve for speed, topspin for dip, slice for curve); Find new racket's sweet spot.
