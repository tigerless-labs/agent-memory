Pre-play warm-up (5-10 min): neck stretch, shoulder rolls, chest stretch, arm circles. Lower body (10-15 min): calf stretch, hamstring stretch, quad stretch, hip flexor stretch. Tennis-specific (5-10 min): forearm stretch, rotator cuff stretch, glute stretch. Post-play: repeat major muscle groups. Guidelines: hold 30 seconds, breathe naturally, don't bounce, stretch to comfort not pain.
