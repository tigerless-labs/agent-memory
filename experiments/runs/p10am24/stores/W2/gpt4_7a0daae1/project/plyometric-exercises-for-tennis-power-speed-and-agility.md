Exercises: Box jumps (start 12-18 inches, 3 sets 10-15 reps); Depth jumps (jump from box then immediately up, 3 sets 10-15 reps); Lateral bounds (jump sideways with quick turnaround, 3 sets 10-15 reps); Tuck jumps (jump and tuck knees to chest, 3 sets 10-15 reps); Medicine ball throws (explosive rotational throws, 3 sets 10-15 reps). Schedule: 2-3 times per week with rest days. Approach: warm up 5-10 min, start lower intensity, combine with strength training (squats, lunges), focus on proper technique. Rest 30-60 seconds between sets.
