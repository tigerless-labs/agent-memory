Goal: improve endurance and stamina for Saturday lessons. Currently doing: running and cycling. Frequency: 2-3 times per week with rest days between. Types: HIIT (short bursts), aerobic (steady-state 20-30 min), plyometric, agility drills. Sample schedule: Monday HIIT (burpees, jump squats 20-30 min), Wednesday aerobic (jog/cycle 20-30 min), Friday plyometric + agility.
