Steps to implement quality control with suppliers:

1. Define clear quality standards and specifications for products/services
2. Identify critical control points in supplier's production process
3. Develop quality control plan with monitoring procedures and frequency
4. Implement regular inspections and testing to verify compliance
5. Use statistical process control methods (e.g., Six Sigma)
6. Establish corrective action process with root cause analysis
7. Regularly evaluate supplier performance using established KPIs

Checks and balances to implement:
- Regular audits and inspections of supplier operations
- Product testing and validation procedures
- Certification compliance verification (ISO 9001, AS9100)
- Supplier certification programs
- Third-party audits
- Customer feedback and complaint monitoring

Tools and methodologies:
- Quality management software: Qualityze, ETQ
- Quality control templates and checklists
- Defect rate tracking and analysis
- ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management Systems)
- AS9100 (Aerospace Industry QMS)
- Six Sigma for statistical process control
- Lean Manufacturing for process optimization
