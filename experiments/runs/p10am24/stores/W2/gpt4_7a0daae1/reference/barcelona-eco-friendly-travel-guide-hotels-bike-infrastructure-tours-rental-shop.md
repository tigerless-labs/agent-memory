Eco-friendly hotels in Barcelona city center: Hotel Omm (solar panels, efficient lighting, local breakfast), Hotel Casa Fuster (Biosphere certified, sustainable practices), Yök Casa + Cultura (solar, green roof, composting), Hotel España Ramblas (waste reduction), Hostal Orleans (budget option, eco measures).

Bike infrastructure: BiciLlanes network (200+ km designated lanes), Passeig de la Mar (7 km coastal path with Mediterranean views), Carrer de la Diagonal (11 km dedicated bike lane), Ciclopeatonal (connecting parks), Superblocks (pedestrian/cyclist priority zones), Bicing bike-sharing system with day passes.

Bike rental shops: Barcelona Bike Tours, Fat Tire Tours, Bicitours Barcelona, Donkey Republic. All offer baskets/racks and comfort bikes with upright handlebars, cushioned seats, and options for riders with back concerns.

Eco-friendly tours and activities: 3-hour bike tours visiting La Sagrada Família/Park Güell/Gothic Quarter, Barcelona Sail solar-powered boat tours, Park Güell guided visits (4 hours via GetYourGuide/Viator), Montserrat mountain hikes with wine tastings, Huerto Urbano urban gardening workshops, ecological walking tours, Barceloneta beach clean-ups with snorkeling, Hop-on Hop-off buses.

Bike-friendly cafes and restaurants: Can Culleretes (Gothic Quarter, outdoor seating), El Xampanyet (tapas, pan con tomate), La Pubilla (Eixample pastries), Bicioci (bike-themed café with repair station), Can Solé (Catalan cuisine, Barceloneta).

Transportation: Hola BCN Card for unlimited bus/metro/tram access. Water refill stations throughout city. Helmets recommended but not mandatory.
