Linkin Park created Music for Relief (focused on disaster relief and mental health awareness). Worked with American Foundation for Suicide Prevention and Campaign to Change Direction. Received Change Award (2018) and Lifesaver Award from AFSP (2017). Also involved in environmental sustainability and education initiatives. Music dealt with depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation themes.
