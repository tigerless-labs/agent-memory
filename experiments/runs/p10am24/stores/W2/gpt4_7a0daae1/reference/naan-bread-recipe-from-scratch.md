**Ingredients:**
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup lukewarm water
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional)

**Instructions:**
1. Combine flour, salt, sugar, and yeast
2. Gradually add lukewarm water; mix into shaggy dough
3. Add olive oil; mix until dough comes together
4. Knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth; let rise 1-2 hours until doubled
6. Preheat non-stick skillet or tava over medium heat
7. Divide dough into 6-8 portions; shape into balls
8. Roll each into thin circle (6-7 inches)
9. Cook 1-2 minutes per side until puffed and golden brown
10. Brush with melted butter or ghee

**Tips:** Use stand mixer for dough hook, add garlic/cilantro/cumin seeds for flavor, use cast-iron skillet or pizza stone for crisper crust, experiment with whole wheat flour
