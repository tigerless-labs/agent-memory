Non-alcoholic options: lemonade, iced coffee, fruit-infused water, sparkling water with fruit, popsicles, agua fresca, virgin pina coladas, cucumber lime refresher, ginger ale spritzer. Alcoholic: beer and wine (crisp white wine, cold lager). Staples: soda, water, iced tea. Consider setting up DIY drink station with mixers, garnishes, and flavorings.
