Games suitable for wide age ranges: Uno and Guess Who (ages 6+), Ticket to Ride/Pictionary/Clue (ages 8+), Scattergories/Cranium/Settlers of Catan/Forbidden Island/Forbidden Desert/Apples to Apples (ages 10+). All easy to learn, playable with 2-6 players, engaging for mixed ages.
