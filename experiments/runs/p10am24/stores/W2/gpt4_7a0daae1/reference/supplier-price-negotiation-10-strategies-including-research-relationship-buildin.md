10 supplier price negotiation strategies:

1. Research market rates and competitor pricing thoroughly
2. Clearly define your needs and identify non-negotiable items
3. Build professional and respectful relationships with suppliers
4. Be transparent about your budget constraints
5. Politely ask for better pricing with specific phrases
6. Look for value-added services (free shipping, priority delivery, technical support)
7. Know your walk-away point and be willing to use it
8. Use competition as leverage to negotiate better terms
9. Consider alternative pricing models (tiered discounts, cost-plus, value-based)
10. Verify and confirm final terms in writing before proceeding

Key principles: stay calm and professional, avoid threats or demands, focus on mutual benefit, ensure clear communication.
