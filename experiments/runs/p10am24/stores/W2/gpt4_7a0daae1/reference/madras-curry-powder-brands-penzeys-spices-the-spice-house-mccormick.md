Popular brands for Madras curry powder:

- **Penzeys Spices**: Blend of 12 spices including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, and cardamom
- **The Spice House**: Blend of 15 spices including fenugreek and fennel
- **McCormick**: More accessible and affordable option
