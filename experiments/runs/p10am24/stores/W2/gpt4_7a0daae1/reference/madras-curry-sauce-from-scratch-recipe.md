**Ingredients:**
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon curry leaves (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon Madras curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Heat oil in large pan, add onions until translucent (5 min)
2. Add garlic, ginger, spices; cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant
3. Add tomatoes, broth, coconut milk; stir well
4. Simmer 10-15 minutes until sauce thickens
5. Taste and adjust seasoning
6. Add cooked tofu and simmer 5-7 more minutes
7. Garnish with cilantro and serve over rice/naan

**Variations:** Add potatoes/carrots, substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream, add lemon juice/vinegar for brightness
