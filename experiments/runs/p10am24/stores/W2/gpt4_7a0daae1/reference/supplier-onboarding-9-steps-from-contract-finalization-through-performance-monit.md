Key steps for successful supplier onboarding:

1. Create comprehensive onboarding checklist
2. Finalize and sign contract with all agreed terms
3. Gather supplier profile information (company details, certifications, insurance, product specs)
4. Designate primary contact points and establish communication channels
5. Schedule onboarding meeting to review expectations and discuss processes
6. Provide training on your business processes and quality standards
7. Conduct testing and quality assurance checks
8. Establish payment terms and invoicing procedures
9. Set up KPIs to measure and monitor supplier performance

Tools to consider:
- Contract management: ContractWorks, Concord
- Supplier management systems: SAP Ariba, Coupa
- Project management: Asana, Trello
- Quality management software: Qualityze, ETQ

Use templates for: supplier onboarding checklists, supplier information forms, performance evaluation.
