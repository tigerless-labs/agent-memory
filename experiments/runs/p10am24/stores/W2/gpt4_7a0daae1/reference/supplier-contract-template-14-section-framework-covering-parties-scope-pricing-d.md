Comprehensive supplier contract sections:

1. Introduction: parties and purpose
2. Scope of work: products/services, specifications, quantity, delivery schedule
3. Pricing and payment terms
4. Delivery and shipping
5. Quality and warranty
6. Intellectual property rights
7. Term and termination
8. Liability and indemnification
9. Dispute resolution process
10. Confidentiality and NDA
11. Governing law and jurisdiction
12. Amendments and waivers
13. Entire agreement clause
14. Signatures

Key tips: clearly define scope of work, specify quality standards, establish clear payment schedule, include warranty and return policies, address IP protection, define term and termination procedures, cover liability allocation, establish dispute resolution process, protect confidential information, carefully review all terms before signing.
