Key supplier evaluation factors:

1. Product/service quality and industry certifications
2. Reliability and consistency in delivery
3. Pricing competitiveness and transparency
4. Customer service and technical support quality
5. Financial stability and industry reputation
6. Supply chain visibility and risk management
7. Relevant certifications (ISO, CE, FDA) and regulatory compliance
8. Investment in innovation and continuous improvement

Red flags to watch for:
- Poor communication or unresponsiveness
- Lack of transparency in pricing, quality, or supply chain
- Unwilling to provide references or case studies
- History of late deliveries, defects, or quality issues
- Inadequate insurance coverage or liability protection
- Unprofessional behavior or unqualified staff
- Unclear or unfair contract terms
- Unwilling to adapt to changing business needs
