Past back issues require careful attention to bike ergonomics when cycling or renting bikes for tours. Preferences: upright handlebars (not dropped racing bars) and well-cushioned seats to reduce strain on back and neck. When booking bike tours or rentals, specifically requests comfort-focused bikes. Interested in bikes with wider tires and suspension to absorb bumps. Comfortable riding position is important for longer rides.
