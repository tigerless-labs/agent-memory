Popular platforms for audiobook listening:

- **Audible** - subscription-based service with vast library
- **Libro.fm** - partners with independent bookstores
- **Scribd** - subscription-based access to e-books, audiobooks, and documents
- **OverDrive** and **Hoopla** - free audiobook access through many public libraries
