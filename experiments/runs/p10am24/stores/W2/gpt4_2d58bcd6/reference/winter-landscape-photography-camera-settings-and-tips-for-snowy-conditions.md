Recommended settings for winter landscape photography: Shoot in Manual mode; ISO 100-400 to minimize noise; Aperture f/8-f/11 for deep depth of field; Shutter speed 1/125-1/250s; White Balance set to Cloudy or Shade. Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop to capture snow detail. Use polarizing filter to reduce glare. Shoot during golden hour or overcast conditions. Bracket shots for HDR merging. Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility. Use wide-angle lens (10-24mm) for landscape vistas. Look for leading lines, patterns, textures, and interesting compositions.
