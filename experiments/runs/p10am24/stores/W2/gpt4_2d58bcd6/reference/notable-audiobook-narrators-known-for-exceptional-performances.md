Narrators recognized for quality performances:

- **Bahni Turpin** - "The Hate U Give"; powerful and emotive narration
- **Jim Dale** - "The Night Circus"; known for incredible range and ability to bring characters to life
- **Adjoa Andoh** - "The Power"; commanding and thought-provoking performance
- **Frazer Douglas** - "The Song of Achilles"; lyrical and emotive quality
- **Peter Kenny** - "The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August"; masterful performance bringing complex story to life
- **Juliet Stevenson** - "The Girl on the Train", "The Power"; nuanced and emotive performances
- **Scott Brick** - "The Da Vinci Code", "The Bourne Identity"; distinctive voice and style
- **George Guidall** - veteran narrator with 1000+ audiobooks; includes "The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo", "The Lincoln Rhyme" series
- **Khristine Hvam** - "The Hunger Games", "Divergent" series; youthful energy and enthusiasm
