Simple recipe: roast 2-3 lb butternut squash at 400F for 45-50 min, sauté onion and garlic, add cumin and smoked paprika, blend with 4 cups broth, finish with heavy cream or coconut cream. Serves 4-6. Optional: add apple or kale, use nutmeg or cinnamon for variations.
