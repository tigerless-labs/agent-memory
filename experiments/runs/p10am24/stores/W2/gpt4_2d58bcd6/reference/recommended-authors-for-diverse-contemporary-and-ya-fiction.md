Authors recommended for eclectic tastes with focus on blended genres, experimentation with form, and social justice themes:

**Contemporary/YA Social Justice:**
- **Jason Reynolds** - "Long Way Down", "All American Boys"; powerful YA voice writing about social justice, identity, Black experience
- **Nicola Yoon** - "The Sun is Also a Star", "Everything, Everything"; explores identity, family, social justice; lyrical prose, complex characters
- **Renee Watson** - "Piecing Me Together", "Betty Before X"; experiences of Black girls/women; identity, family, social justice themes
- **Ibi Zoboi** - "American Street", "Pride"; beautifully written Haitian-American perspectives
- **Dhonielle Clayton** - "The Belles", "Shattered Midnight"; lush, imaginative fantasy and speculative fiction; identity and power themes

**Fantasy/Speculative:**
- **Tomi Adeyemi** - "Children of Blood and Bone"; blends African mythology, fantasy, social commentary
- **Sabaa Tahir** - "An Ember in the Ashes"; fantasy with ancient Roman-inspired world-building and social commentary
- **Nnedi Okorafor** - "Who Fears Death", "Binti"; blends African culture, mythology, social commentary
