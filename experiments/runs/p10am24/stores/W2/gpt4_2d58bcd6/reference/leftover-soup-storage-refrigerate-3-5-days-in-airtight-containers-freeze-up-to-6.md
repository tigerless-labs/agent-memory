Cool soup to room temperature within 2 hours of cooking. Refrigerate in airtight, non-reactive containers (glass or BPA-free plastic) and label with date. For freezing: divide into individual portions, use freezer-safe containers or bags, remove excess air before sealing. Reheat gently to 165F minimum for food safety. Frozen soup can be thawed overnight in fridge or reheated from frozen.
