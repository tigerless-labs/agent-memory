Thrive is a feature on Higher Logic for creating online communities. Activation: 1) Log in and go to Admin, 2) Click Features tab, 3) Search Thrive and click Activate, 4) Follow prompts (agree to terms, billing, settings), 5) Start setting up community. Note: May not be available to all customers or pricing tiers; contact Higher Logic support for confirmation.
