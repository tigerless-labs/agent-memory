Common tarot spreads for daily readings:

1. **Single Card Spread** — one card for insight into the day
2. **Three-Card Spread** — first card = past, second = present, third = future
3. **Celtic Cross** — ten-card spread for in-depth readings
4. **Daily Focus Spread** — three cards: overall energy, what to focus on, outcome
5. **Mind, Body, Spirit Spread** — three cards representing mental, physical, and spiritual aspects of the day
