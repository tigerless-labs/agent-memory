Full-cast narrations enhance immersion and help identify speakers clearly. Also enjoys single narrators with unique tone and style depending on the book. Views narration as crucial to audiobooks—good narrator elevates story while poor narration detracts. Narrator's tone, pace, and inflection greatly impact listener's interpretation. Believes skilled narration can amplify emotional impact and empathy.
