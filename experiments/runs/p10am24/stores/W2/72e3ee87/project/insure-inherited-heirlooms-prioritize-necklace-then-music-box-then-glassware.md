Plan to insure three inherited items:

**Priority order:**
1. Grandmother's vintage diamond necklace (most valuable)
2. Antique music box from great-aunt
3. Depression-era glassware from mother

**Process for each item:**
1. Get professional appraisal from certified appraiser
2. Gather documentation: proof of ownership, provenance, any repair records, photos/videos
3. Shop for insurance company that specializes in valuable items
4. Obtain insurance policy

**Appraisal organizations to look for (certified appraisers):**
- Jewelry: NAJA, AGTA, ASA
- Antiques: AAA, ISA, NACA

**Approach:**
- Search online for certified appraisers in local area
- Ask friends and family for referrals
- Get one appraisal per item initially; get multiple appraisals later if needed
