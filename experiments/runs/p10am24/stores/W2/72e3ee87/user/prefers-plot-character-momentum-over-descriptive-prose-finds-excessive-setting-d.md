Dislikes novels with heavy, detailed descriptions of setting that don't directly advance plot or character development. Prefers setting to be woven organically into story and characters rather than presented as separate descriptive passages. Despite appreciating that literary techniques have value, when reading for enjoyment prefers narrative momentum and character focus over atmospheric descriptive prose.
