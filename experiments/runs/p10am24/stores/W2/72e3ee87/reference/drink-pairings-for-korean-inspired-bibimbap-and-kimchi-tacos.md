Drink pairing suggestions for Korean-inspired dishes:

For Bibimbap:
- Soju Spritz: Soju, lime juice, ginger beer (crisp, cuts through richness)
- Cucumber-Lime Refresher: variation with ginger liqueur
- Korean Iced Tea: black tea, sugar, citrus

For Kimchi Tacos:
- Spicy Mango Mule: mango puree, ginger beer, lime, chili flakes
- Ginger-Grapefruit Collins: ginger liqueur, grapefruit juice, soda water
- Cucumber-Kiwi Cooler: cucumber, kiwi puree, lime, sparkling water

General pairing tips:
- For spicy dishes like kimchi, balance with sweet or creamy elements
- For savory dishes like Bibimbap, use bright citrusy flavors to cut through richness
- Echo ingredients from the dish in the drink (e.g., ginger-forward drinks with ginger-heavy dishes)
