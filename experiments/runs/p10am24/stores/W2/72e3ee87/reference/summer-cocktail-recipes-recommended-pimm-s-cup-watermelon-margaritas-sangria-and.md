Summer cocktail recipes to complement Cucumber Gimlets:
- Pimm's Cup: gin-based liqueur with lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit
- Watermelon Margaritas: watermelon puree, tequila, lime, agave nectar
- Summer Sangria: white wine with peaches, strawberries, citrus, sparkling water
- Cranberry Lime Sparkler: cranberry juice, lime, vodka, sparkling water
- Gin-Gin Mule: ginger beer, lime, gin in copper mug
- Spicy Pineapple Collins: pineapple juice, tequila, lime, cayenne pepper
- Rosé Spritz: rosé wine, lemon-lime soda, grenadine
- Blackberry Basil Martini: blackberry puree, vodka, lime, basil
- Citrus-Ginger Fizz: citrus juices, ginger liqueur, sparkling water
- Elderflower Collins: elderflower liqueur, gin, lemon, soda water
