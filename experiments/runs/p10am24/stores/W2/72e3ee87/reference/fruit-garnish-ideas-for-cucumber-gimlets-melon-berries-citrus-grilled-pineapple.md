Fruit garnish options that pair well with cucumber and lime in Cucumber Gimlets:
- Lime wedges with mint sprig
- Cantaloupe or honeydew melon balls
- Strawberry slices
- Kiwi slices or wedges
- Grapefruit slices or wedges
- Orange slices or wedges
- Peach slices
- Mango slices or wedges
- Grilled pineapple (already tested successfully at BBQ)

Tip: Consider marinating fruits in lime juice, sugar, and spices to enhance flavors and add complexity.
