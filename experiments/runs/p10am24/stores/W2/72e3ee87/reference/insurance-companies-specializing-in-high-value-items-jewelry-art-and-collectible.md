**Specialty Insurance Companies:**
- Jewelers Mutual: specializes in jewelry including vintage and antique pieces
- Chubb: high-value item insurance including jewelry, art, collectibles
- AXA ART: art, antiques, collectibles, including jewelry and rare items
- Hagerty: known for classic cars; also offers collectibles policies (jewelry, art, antiques)

**Standard Insurance Companies with Specialty Departments:**
- State Farm: specialized department for high-value items including jewelry and antiques
- Allstate: Valuable Items policy for jewelry, art, and collectibles
- Liberty Mutual: Valuable Possessions policy for jewelry, art, and antiques

**Policy Features to Look For:**
- Agreed value coverage (pays insured amount in case of loss/damage)
- No deductible or low deductible
- Worldwide coverage (if traveling with items)
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance/loss (not just theft/damage)
