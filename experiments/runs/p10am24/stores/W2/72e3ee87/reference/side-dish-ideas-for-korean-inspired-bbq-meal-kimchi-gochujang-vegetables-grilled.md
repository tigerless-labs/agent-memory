Side dish recommendations for Korean-inspired BBQ party:

Korean-Inspired Sides:
- Kimchi Coleslaw: kimchi, shredded cabbage, carrots, soy sauce, lime juice
- Gochujang Roasted Vegetables: broccoli, Brussels sprouts, or sweet potatoes tossed with gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil
- Korean-Style Corn: grilled or boiled corn with spicy sauce (gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)
- Cucumber Kimchi Salad: thinly sliced cucumbers, kimchi, red onion, cilantro, soy sauce, lime juice

Other Sides:
- Pickled Ginger: thinly sliced ginger pickled in vinegar, sugar, salt
- Cilantro Lime Rice: rice with cilantro, lime juice, garlic, hint of sesame oil
- Grilled Pineapple: grilled pineapple slices with cilantro, lime juice, honey
- Spicy Edamame: steamed edamame with chili flakes, soy sauce, sesame oil

User planning to serve: kimchi and spicy pickled carrots as key sides.
