BBQ sauce recipes for Korean-inspired flavors:

Sauces:
- Gochujang BBQ Sauce: gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil (umami-rich)
- Soy-Ginger BBQ Sauce: soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice vinegar
- Korean-Style BBQ Sauce with Gochugaru: gochugaru, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil (bold, spicy kick)

Marinades:
- Sesame-Ginger Marinade: sesame oil, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar (for chicken, beef, or pork)
- Korean-Style BBQ Marinade with Gochujang: gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil
- Cilantro-Lime Marinade: cilantro, lime juice, garlic, olive oil (bright, for chicken, shrimp, or vegetables)

Tips: Balance bold flavors with acidity (lime or vinegar); adjust sweetness, spiciness, and umami to taste.
