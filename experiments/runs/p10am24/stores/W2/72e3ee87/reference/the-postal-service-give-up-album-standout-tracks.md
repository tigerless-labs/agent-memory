Album: Give Up (2003) by The Postal Service (Ben Gibbard + Jimmy Tamborello)

Recommended standout tracks:
- Such Great Heights - synth-driven anthem, dreamy
- The District Sleeps Alone Tonight - melancholic, introspective  
- Clark Gable - quirky and upbeat
- We Will Become Silhouettes - somber, reflective
- Recycled Air - acoustic guitar with electronic elements
- Sleeping In - vocal layering and harmonies

Distinctive features: Blends acoustic instruments with electronic; rich vocal layering; postal imagery in lyrics
