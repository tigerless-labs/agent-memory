Current lightweight materials: aluminum, carbon fiber, reinforced plastics. Structural materials: steel, titanium. Manufacturing strategy mixes materials by function - steel for structure, aluminum for panels. Emerging materials researched: nanocellulose, graphene as advanced composites for strength and lightness. New technologies: 3D printing enables complex shapes. Electric vehicles prioritize lightweight materials for range; sports cars use advanced composites. Adoption constraints: rigorous testing, regulatory approval, and cost-effectiveness mean new materials take years to reach mainstream use.
