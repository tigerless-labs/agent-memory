DIY Cocktail Bar Setup for Cucumber Gimlets and summer party:

Mixers:
- Ginger Beer
- Lime Juice
- Grapefruit Juice
- Cucumber-Lime Juice
- Soy Sauce-Infused Simple Syrup

Garnishes:
- Cucumber Slices
- Lime Wedges
- Mint Leaves
- Kimchi (for Korean-inspired cocktails)
- Pickled Ginger
- Sesame Seeds
- Fresh Herbs (basil, cilantro, parsley)

Bar Essentials:
- Ice
- Variety of glasses (highball, old fashioned, martini)
- Muddler (for crushing fruits, herbs, spices)
- Jigger (for accurate measuring)
- Garnish Picks (small skewers)

Setting up a DIY bar allows guests to get creative and customize their drinks while complementing the food.
