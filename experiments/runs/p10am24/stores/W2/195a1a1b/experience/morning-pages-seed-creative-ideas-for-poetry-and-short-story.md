Discovered that morning pages function as a creative seedbed for polished work. Pattern: writes ideas and themes in morning pages, then revisits entries and develops them into full poems or short story scenes. Uses morning pages to explore character thoughts, feelings, and motivations in a free-form way that feeds storytelling. Example: poem from February 2, 2023 (inspired by a winter walk in nearby park) originated as a morning page entry, then was revised and expanded into a complete poem.
