Challenges NGOs face when extending support to vulnerable communities in Africa:

Infrastructure gaps:
- Lack of roads, bridges, ports in many areas
- Difficult to transport aid to remote regions

Political and security issues:
- Political instability and conflict in some countries
- Difficult for NGOs to operate safely and effectively

Corruption:
- Pervasive problem affecting aid distribution
- NGOs may face bribery when distributing aid

Communication barriers:
- Language differences with local communities
- Cultural barriers to effective collaboration

Resource constraints:
- Limited funding, personnel, and equipment
- High operational costs due to lack of infrastructure and need to import goods/services

Accountability system gaps:
- Many NGOs lack systems to track and report activities transparently
- Difficult to demonstrate impact to donors
- Leads to lack of trust from donors and communities
- Difficult to ensure aid reaches intended recipients
