A solution designed to help NGOs manage voucher-based humanitarian projects in real-time.

Hardware: POS machines for authorized vendors and smart cards for beneficiaries.

Software: Portal for NGO project teams to create and monitor distribution projects in real-time.

Key Features:
- Real-time accountability through smart card transactions and POS receipts
- Offline capability: redemption works without internet connection, devices sync data locally
- NFC cards hold beneficiary data enabling redemption from different vendors
- User-friendly process: beneficiaries tap card, choose items, enter PIN

Why suited for Africa: addresses corruption and accountability gaps, works in areas with limited internet, real-time balance monitoring, eliminates complex manual processes
