Resources for finding mountain biking trails: Websites - Trailforks, MTB Project, TrailLink; Apps - Trailforks, MapMyRide. Organizations: IMBA (International Mountain Bicycling Association) designates Epic trails. Look for trails 20-30 miles, moderate difficulty (blue/green IMBA rating), elevation gain 1000-2000 feet, mix of singletrack and rocky sections.
