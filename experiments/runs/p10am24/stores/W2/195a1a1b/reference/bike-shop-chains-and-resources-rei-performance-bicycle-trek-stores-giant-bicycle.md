Bike shop resources for rentals, guided tours, and lessons: REI (Recreational Equipment Inc.), Performance Bicycle, Trek Bicycle Stores, Giant Bicycle, and local bike shops. Look for shops offering: guided tours for beginners/intermediate, half-day/full-day/multi-day rentals, lessons (beginner skills, cornering, descending), maintenance services.
