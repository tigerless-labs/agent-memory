Francis Benjamin Johnston (1864-1952) was an American photographer and one of the first women to establish a professional photography career in the United States.

**Background:**
- Born into prominent Virginia family
- Began career in 1890s as apprentice, opened studio in Washington D.C. in 1894
- Quickly established reputation as talented and innovative photographer

**Major Contributions:**
1. **Architectural Photography:** Pioneer in documenting America's historic buildings, traveled extensively photographing colonial-era structures. Helped raise awareness of importance of preserving architectural heritage.
2. **Portraiture:** Renowned for portraits of politicians, celebrities, socialites including Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, Alice Roosevelt Longworth. Characterized by elegance, simplicity, and attention to detail.

**Legacy:**
Committed to photography as tool for social change. Work celebrated for technical excellence, artistic innovation, and influence on American photography.
