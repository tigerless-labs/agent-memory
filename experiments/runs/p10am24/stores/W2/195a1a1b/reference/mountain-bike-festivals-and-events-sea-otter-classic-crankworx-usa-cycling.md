Major mountain biking festivals and events: Sea Otter Classic (California) - four-day festival with racing, demos, exhibitions; Crankworx (Whistler BC and other locations) - mountain bike festivals with racing, slopestyle; USA Cycling National Championships (various locations) - national championships in different cycling disciplines. Check USA Cycling website, local cycling organizations, bike shops, and social media for event updates.
