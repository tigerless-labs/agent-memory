Diagnosis: skin prick testing, blood tests (allergen-specific antibodies), patch testing.

OTC Medications: antihistamines (reduce sneezing/itching/runny nose), decongestants (nasal congestion), nasal corticosteroid sprays (reduce inflammation/swelling), cromolyn sodium nasal spray (prevents histamine release), saline nasal rinses.

Consult doctor if symptoms persist or worsen despite OTC treatment.
