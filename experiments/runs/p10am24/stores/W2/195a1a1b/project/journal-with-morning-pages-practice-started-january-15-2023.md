Journaling practice started January 15, 2023, using journal for morning pages and occasional poetry. Morning pages are free-form, unstructured writing without specific prompts or constraints. Uses journaling to clear mind, organize thoughts, process emotions, reflect on experiences, and reflect on writing progress and goals. Morning pages have become a valuable creative practice that fuels other writing projects.
