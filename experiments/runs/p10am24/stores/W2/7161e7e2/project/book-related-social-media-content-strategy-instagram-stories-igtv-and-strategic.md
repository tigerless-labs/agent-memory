Expanding social media presence into book lover community content.

**Platform strategy:**
- Instagram Stories: behind-the-scenes content (prep for #BookClubChat, post-chat thoughts, Q&A sessions, creative process sharing)
- IGTV: in-depth book reviews, author interviews, discussion videos, bookish challenges

**Hashtag approach:**
Mixing popular (#bookstagram, #booklover) and niche hashtags (#bookrecs, genre-specific) by post content (moved away from using same hashtags for all posts). Prefers #bookrecs over #bookrecommendations for brevity and reach.

**Specific plans:**
- Create post titled "Quarterly Faves & Wants" about favorite books of the quarter + ask followers for recommendations
- Launch Instagram Story poll asking which content followers prefer: behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, or book reviews
- Use book-related engagement questions with followers (favorite books, reading formats, genres, etc.)
