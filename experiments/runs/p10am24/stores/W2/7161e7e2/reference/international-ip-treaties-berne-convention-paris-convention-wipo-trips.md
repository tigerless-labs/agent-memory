**Berne Convention (1886):** Protection of literary and artistic works; requires member countries recognize copyrights of other members

**Paris Convention (1883):** Protection of patents, trademarks, industrial designs; provides right of priority for applications

**WIPO Convention (1967/1970):** Established World Intellectual Property Organization as intergovernmental body; administers international IP treaties

**TRIPS (1994/1995):** Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of IPR; WTO member requirement for minimum standards in patent, trademark, copyright, and other IP protection with legal enforcement procedures
