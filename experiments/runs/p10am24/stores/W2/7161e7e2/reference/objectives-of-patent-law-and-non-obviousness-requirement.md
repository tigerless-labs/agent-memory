**Patent law objectives:**
- Encourage innovation through temporary monopoly
- Disseminate new technologies via public disclosure
- Promote public welfare (only socially beneficial technologies)
- Balance private inventor rights with public interest
- Encourage foreign investment in India
- Protect small and medium enterprises
- Promote market competition

**Non-obviousness:** Invention must not be obvious to person with ordinary skill in the field. Example: a new solar panel design using known techniques = obvious (not patentable); same design using novel materials or manufacturing method = non-obvious (patentable). Similarly, water purification using new bacteria strain instead of known techniques = non-obvious.
