- Comprehensive legal framework (patents, trademarks, copyrights, geographical indications)
- First-to-file patent system with novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability criteria
- First-to-use trademark system under Trade Marks Act 1999
- Automatic copyright protection under Copyright Act 1957
- Civil, criminal remedies, specialized courts/tribunals for IPR disputes
- Signatory to TRIPS Agreement and Paris Convention
- Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB) hears appeals on patents, trademarks, copyrights
- National IPR Policy (2016) provides overall development framework
