10-Part Worship Service Structure:

1. Opening: Great Are You Lord (All Sons & Daughters)
2. Call to Worship and Prayer
3. Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17
4. Sermon on Luke 7:1-17
5. Response Hymn: O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing (Charles Wesley)
6. Congregation Testimony
7. Hymn of Faith: Amazing Grace (John Newton)
8. Healing Prayer Time
9. Closing: Healer (Kari Jobe)
10. Benediction

Theme: Faith in Gods power to heal; salvation open to all; connection between physical and spiritual healing.
