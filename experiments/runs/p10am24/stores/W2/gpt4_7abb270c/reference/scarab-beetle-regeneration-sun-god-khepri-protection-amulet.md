Revered for life cycle as symbol of regeneration, transformation, and eternal life. Rolling dung-ball and emerging as adult beetle metaphor for sun god Khepri's daily cycle. Symbolizes rebirth, transformation, renewal, fertility. Depicted pushing sun disk representing Khepri's role in rolling sun across sky. Used in amulets for protection and as funerary protector symbols.
