Duration: approximately 70 days. Steps: (1) Remove internal organs except heart (kept as seat of soul and ka); (2) Dehydrate with natron salt for about 40 days; (3) Treat with resin; (4) Wrap in linen bandages with amulets placed between layers; (5) Cover with shroud and place mask or coffin over face. Purpose: preserve body for soul's use in afterlife. Techniques: desiccation prevents decay, disinfection via natron and resin, physical protection via wrappings, spiritual protection via amulets and symbols.
