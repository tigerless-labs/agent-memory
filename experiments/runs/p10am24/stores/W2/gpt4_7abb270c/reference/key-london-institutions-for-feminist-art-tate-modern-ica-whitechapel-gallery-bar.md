Museums and galleries in London with strong collections and exhibitions of feminist art:\n\n- **Tate Modern**: impressive collection of feminist art from the 1970s\n- **Institute of Contemporary Arts (ICA)**: strong history of showcasing feminist art and experimental works\n- **Whitechapel Gallery**: supports women artists, hosted exhibitions on feminist art and Women's Liberation Movement\n- **Barbican**: strong program on feminist art including Nancy Spero exhibition (2020)\n- **Photographers' Gallery**: collection of feminist photography from the 1970s
