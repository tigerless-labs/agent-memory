Located at Egyptian Museum in Cairo (not the Metropolitan Museum of Art). Discovered 1922 by Howard Carter in Valley of the Kings tomb KV62. Weighs 11 kg solid gold, adorned with lapis lazuli, carnelian, and turquoise. Represents divine kingship, protection of pharaoh's identity in the afterlife, and demonstration of wealth and power.
