Museums and exhibitions focused on the history of space exploration:\n\n- **National Space Centre** (Leicester): dedicated space museum with interactive exhibits, planetarium, Soyuz spacecraft, piece of the Moon's surface\n- **Science Museum Space Galleries** (London): permanent exhibits exploring history of space travel, Soyuz T-1 spacecraft, Eagle lunar module replica\n- **Royal Observatory, Greenwich** (London): astronomy and space exploration history, telescopes, navigation exhibits\n- **Imperial War Museum Duxford** (near Cambridge): aircraft and spacecraft including Concorde and SR-71 Blackbird\n- **RAF Museum London**: history of British spaceflight, Blue Streak rocket, Black Arrow launch vehicle
