## Sustainability & ESG Reporting Frameworks Overview

### CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) — EU
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for all large companies operating in the EU
- **Scope (as of 2023 proposal):** Reporting format expected introduction 2024, mandatory reporting from 2026 onwards
- **Format requirement:** European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) using Inline XBRL (iXBRL)
- **Purpose:** Standardize and make sustainability information more comparable and accessible for investors/stakeholders

### NFRD (Non-Financial Reporting Directive) — EU
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for large public-interest entities with:
  - More than 500 employees, AND
  - Balance sheet total over €20 million, OR net turnover over €40 million
- **Public-interest entities include:** Companies with securities on regulated EU markets, credit institutions, insurance undertakings
- **Format requirement:** None prescribed; companies encouraged to use recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) for structured reporting
- **Content required:** Business model description, policies, outcomes, risks/impacts (ESG), diversity policy for management/supervisory bodies

### Voluntary/Recommended Frameworks
**GRI Standards** (Global Reporting Initiative)
- Voluntary, comprehensive sustainability reporting
- Recommended approach for detailed disclosure

**SASB Standards** (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)
- Voluntary, industry-specific sustainability reporting

**TCFD Recommendations** (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures)
- Voluntary but increasingly adopted by governments/investors
- Recommends disclosure in mainstream financial filings
- Aligned with CSRD approach

**IR Framework** (International Integrated Reporting Council)
- Voluntary integrated reporting approach

**EU Taxonomy Regulation**
- Mandatory for NFRD/SFDR companies
- Climate-related classification system for economic activities

**SFDR** (Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation) — EU
- Mandatory for financial market participants and advisers
- Disclosure in pre-contractual documents and periodic reports

**CDP Climate Disclosure Framework**
- Voluntary, climate-focused
- Complements other frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD, IR)
- Provides detailed climate risk/opportunity information

## Key Relationships
- CDP framework aligns with TCFD recommendations
- CSRD builds on/expands NFRD requirements
- NFRD/SFDR subject to EU Taxonomy compliance
- International frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) used by EU companies to meet NFRD requirements
