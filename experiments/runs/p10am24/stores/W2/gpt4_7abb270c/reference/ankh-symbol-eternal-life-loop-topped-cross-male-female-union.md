Hieroglyphic character: cross with loop at top. Represents eternal life, vitality, and union of male and female principles. Loop symbolizes cyclical nature of life; cross symbolizes physical and spiritual balance. Used in Egyptian religion, art, architecture, and daily life as amulets. Often paired with was (power) and djed (stability) symbols. Associated with pharaonic power to grant eternal life.
