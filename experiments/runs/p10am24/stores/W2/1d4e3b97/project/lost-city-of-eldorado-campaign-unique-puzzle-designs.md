## Campaign overview
Game campaign set in the lost city of Eldorado with puzzle-based progression.

## Unique puzzle designs explored

### 1. Mirror Puzzle
Players find a large mirror with symbols etched on its surface. Their reflections appear reversed. Must position themselves and manipulate symbols in a specific way to reveal hidden passages.

### 2. Elemental Puzzle
Room with levers and buttons controlling elemental forces (earth, fire, air, water). Players activate correct sequence to manipulate elements and reveal hidden paths. Example: air lever + water button creates mist revealing a path.

### 3. Musical Puzzle
Room with musical instruments and sheet music. Symbols on music correspond to different instruments. Players must play correct sequence (e.g., piano → drum → trumpet) to unlock doors.

### 4. Weight Puzzle
Series of weights (different sizes/shapes) and scales with marked weights. Players must weigh objects and arrange them in specific order on scales to reveal passages.

### 5. Memory Puzzle
Objects arranged in a room that players memorize before lights go out. Players navigate dark room to retrieve specific objects and bring them to designated spots to unlock passages.

## Design philosophy
Puzzles designed to challenge different skill levels, mix logic/observation/physical challenges, offer unique solutions for progression, and keep players engaged throughout the campaign.
