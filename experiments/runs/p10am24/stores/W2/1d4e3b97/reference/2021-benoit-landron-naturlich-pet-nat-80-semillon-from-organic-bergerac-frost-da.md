Due to terrible frost in 2021, Benoit Landron made Naturlich with 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac, rather than his usual Folle Blanche. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes to the wine. Described as a spring wine. Served by the glass at 161 (natural wine bar in Sydenham, South East London).
