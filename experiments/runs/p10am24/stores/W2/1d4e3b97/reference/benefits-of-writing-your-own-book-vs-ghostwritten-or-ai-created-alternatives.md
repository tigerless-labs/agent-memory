Self-authored books offer personal fulfillment and empowerment, complete ownership and control over content/tone/message, authenticity that builds reader credibility and trust, improved writing skills and refined voice, and emotional connection leading to deeper reader engagement. Ghostwriting or AI alternatives result in lack of authenticity, reduced personal connection with audience, and less sense of fulfillment and ownership.
