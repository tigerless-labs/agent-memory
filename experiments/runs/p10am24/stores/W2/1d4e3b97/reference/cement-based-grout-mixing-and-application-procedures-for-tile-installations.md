Mixing: Follow manufacturer ratio, use clean potable water, mix in ventilated area, achieve toothpaste/thick batter consistency, mix small batches for consistency.

Application: Clean and dry tile surface, remove spacers, use grout float at 45-degree angle, work in 4 sq ft sections, fill joints completely without overfilling, allow 10-15 min setting time, clean with damp sponge and rinse thoroughly.

Additional: Apply grout sealer after curing. Avoid over-grouting. Work in well-lit area. Use specialized grout sponge. Maintain clean work area.
