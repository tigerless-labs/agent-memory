## Errors from 2026-09-02 session

**Spelling/Name errors:**
- Wrote: 'Ayn Land'
- Correct: 'Ayn Rand'

- Wrote: 'somewaht'
- Correct: 'somewhat'

**Sentence correction:**
- Wrong: 'Ayn Rand thinks that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must take care of ourselves to live in a somewaht "selfish" manner.'
- Correct: 'Ayn Rand believed that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests in order to live, which some might consider a somewhat "selfish" way of thinking.'

**Changes made:**
- Tense: 'thinks' → 'believed' (more formal when discussing historical figures' established views)
- Vocabulary: 'take care of ourselves' → 'prioritize our own interests' (more precise)
- Structure: Restructured to clarify that this is how others might perceive it, not how Rand herself would frame it
