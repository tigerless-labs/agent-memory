Intelligentsia Coffee: Chicago-based coffee roaster at 53 E Randolph St, 2 minutes from Hilton Hotel downtown. Known for expertly brewed coffee and minimalist vibe.

Willis Tower Skydeck: 103rd floor observation deck at 233 S Wacker Dr, 10 minutes walk from Hilton. Offers breathtaking views of city and Lake Michigan.

Chicago's First Lady Cruises: 90-minute architectural cruise along Chicago River. Tours iconic buildings and city history.

Lakefront Trail: 18.5-mile path along Lake Michigan with stunning views of city skyline. Can be accessed from Millennium Park or Navy Pier areas.
