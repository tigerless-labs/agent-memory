**ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) Care Guide**

**Watering Schedule:**
- Summer: every 1-2 weeks
- Winter: every 4-6 weeks  
- Water when soil feels dry to the touch
- Avoid overwatering (risk of root rot)
- Do not get water on leaves (prevents fungal disease)

**Fertilization:**
- Feed monthly during growing season (spring and summer)
- Use balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20)
- Dilute to half recommended strength to avoid root burn
- Skip fertilizing during winter dormancy

**Additional Care:**
- Prefers well-draining soil
- Avoids direct sunlight; low to medium indirect light ideal
- Prune to maintain shape and encourage growth
- Remove dead/damaged leaves to prevent disease
- Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
