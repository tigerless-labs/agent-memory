Research findings on AI-powered adaptive learning systems:

**Effectiveness**: Systems can effectively improve student learning outcomes in math and reading comprehension.

**Critical Implementation Factors**:
- Ongoing teacher professional development and training essential; many teachers lack skills and knowledge to effectively use systems
- Clear alignment with curriculum and learning goals crucial; when alignment exists, teachers more likely to adopt and use effectively

**Equity and Inclusion Considerations**:
- Systems have potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students
- Significant concern: potential for algorithmic bias in systems, which could exacerbate existing inequities if not addressed

**Stakeholder Tensions**:
- Disagreement on role of teachers: some educators worried systems would replace teachers, others viewed them as valuable support tools
