Popular diners in Fresno recommended for breakfast and brunch stops:

- **Max's Bistro & Bar** — generous portions, creative breakfast dishes, signature Max's Breakfast burrito with scrambled eggs and chorizo
- **The Breakfast Club** — retro-style, breakfast tacos, waffle sandwiches, omelets
- **Pete's Breakfast House** — established 30+ years, hearty breakfast dishes, giant pancakes, omelets, breakfast combos
- **Frank's Place** — operating since 1947, no-frills diner, famous Frank's Favorite breakfast plate (eggs, bacon, sausage)
- **Denny's** — chain locations in Fresno, wide variety of breakfast options, Grand Slam
