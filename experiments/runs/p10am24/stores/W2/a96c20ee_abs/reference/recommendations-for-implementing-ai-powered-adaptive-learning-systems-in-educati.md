Based on thesis research exploring implementation challenges and opportunities:

1. **Teacher Development**: Educators and policymakers should prioritize teacher professional development and provide ongoing support for teachers implementing AI systems

2. **Developer Engagement**: Developers must work closely with educators to ensure systems are designed with the needs of teachers and students in mind

3. **Curriculum Alignment**: Provide clear guidance on how systems align with curriculum and learning goals

4. **Ongoing Evaluation**: Continuous evaluation and assessment of AI-powered adaptive learning systems to ensure effectiveness

5. **Address Bias**: Systems must be designed to prevent algorithmic bias and ensure equitable outcomes for all students
