Techniques to enhance learning during discussion/reflection segments:

- **Open-ended questions**: encourage sharing that requires substantive answers
- **Paraphrase and summarize**: validate student input and ensure comprehension
- **Encourage active listening**: have students summarize peer contributions or ask follow-ups
- **Use visual aids**: diagrams and graphic organizers help students organize and communicate ideas

These methods promote deeper engagement and learning outcomes.
