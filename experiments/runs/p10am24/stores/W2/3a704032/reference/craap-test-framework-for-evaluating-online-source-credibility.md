Method for assessing reliability of online sources:

- **Currency**: How recent is the information?
- **Relevance**: Does it fit your research needs?
- **Authority**: Who authored it? What are their credentials?
- **Accuracy**: Is information supported by evidence?
- **Purpose**: Why does this source exist?

Use to help students verify credibility and reliability of online sources.
