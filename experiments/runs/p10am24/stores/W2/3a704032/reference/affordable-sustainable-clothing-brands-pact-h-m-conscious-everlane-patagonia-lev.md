Recommended brands balancing affordability with sustainability and ethical production:

**pact** — Organic cotton clothing for men, women, and children with fair labor practices

**H&M Conscious** — H&M's sustainable collection using organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel

**Everlane** — Transparent sustainable brand; minimalist aesthetic with timeless designs; recycled synthetic fibers and organic cotton; strong men's selection (t-shirts, shirts, sweaters, jackets, pants, shoes); detailed transparency about production and materials

**Patagonia** — Sustainable outdoor clothing; mid-range/affordable; organic cotton, recycled polyester, hemp

**Levi's** — Denim using recycled materials and sustainably sourced cotton

**Premium leaders** — Stella McCartney, Eileen Fisher, Honest by
