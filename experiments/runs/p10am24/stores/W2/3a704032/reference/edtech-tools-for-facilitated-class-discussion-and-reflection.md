Recommended interactive platforms for discussion/reflection in digital literacy classes:

1. Padlet - collaborative space for students to share thoughts/ideas
2. Nearpod - interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions  
3. Flipgrid - video discussion platform for short video responses
4. Google Jamboard - virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual sharing

All support active engagement and deeper learning during reflection time.
