As of May 22, 2023, in the middle of the book and really enjoying it. Drawn to character development, interconnectedness of characters, and Evelyn's backstory as a Cuban immigrant. Appreciates how Monique functions as an active narrator. Loves the book's exploration of identity, personas, and how public and private selves conflict. Loves the portrayal of complex relationships between women - both empowering and limiting. Book balances glamour with serious themes about intersectionality and power.
