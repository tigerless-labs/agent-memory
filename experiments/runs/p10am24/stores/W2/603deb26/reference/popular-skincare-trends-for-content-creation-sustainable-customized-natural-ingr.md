Popular skincare content trends:\n\n**Sustainable Skincare**: eco-friendly products, recyclable packaging, zero-waste routines, reusable cotton pads, refillable products\n\n**Customized Skincare**: skin typing (normal, dry, oily, combination), personalized formulas\n\n**Natural/Organic Ingredients**: CBD, green tea, turmeric, DIY exfoliants (sugar, salt, coffee)\n\n**Specific Concerns**: acne, blackheads, hyperpigmentation, anti-aging, fine lines, wrinkles\n\n**K-Beauty Trends**: snail mucin, sheet masks, essences\n\n**J-Beauty Inspiration**: yuki/snow-like skin philosophy, sake, green tea\n\n**Diverse Skin Tones**: inclusive skincare routines for underrepresented skin types\n\n**Tools & Devices**: microneedling tools, facial steamers, LED face masks, silicone cleansing brushes, facial rollers
