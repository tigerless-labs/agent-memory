Recommended fly fishing rods for trout fishing (lighter rods for smaller fish):

1. Orvis Clearwater ($198-$298): Entry-level to intermediate, lightweight, balanced, excellent sensitivity
2. Sage Igniter ($400-$500): High-performance, incredibly light, accurate, fast action
3. Winston Pure ($300-$400): Premium, smooth and precise casting
4. Redington Crux ($150-$250): Budget-friendly, lightweight, easy to cast
5. TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series ($200-$300): High-quality, lightweight, accurate, smooth action

Recommended specs for trout: 2-5 weight lines, 9-foot length or 7-8 feet for smaller streams, fast or medium-fast action, graphite preferred for sensitivity.
