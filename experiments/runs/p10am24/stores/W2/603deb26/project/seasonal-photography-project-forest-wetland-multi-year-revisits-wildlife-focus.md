Decided to pursue seasonal photography project combining seasonal changes with wildlife/nature themes. Plans to:

- Choose a specific natural location (forest or wetland)
- Visit regularly to document transformation through seasons
- Capture same scene at same time each year to track multi-year changes
- Focus on light and color changes throughout the year
- Experiment with different techniques and compositions
- Combine with wildlife and nature photography themes

Motivation: interested in how light and colors change seasonally; opportunity to experiment with techniques while improving skills. Plans to stay motivated by: setting realistic goals, creating a schedule, tracking progress in journal, sharing work, experimenting with new techniques, celebrating milestones, staying inspired by other photographers.
