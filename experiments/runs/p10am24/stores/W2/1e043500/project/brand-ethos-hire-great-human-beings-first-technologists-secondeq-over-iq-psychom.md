Core philosophy for brand and content strategy:

- **Primary value**: Hire great human beings (character, collaboration, communication) before great technologists (coding skills)
- **Key hiring approach**: Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers
- **Cultural emphasis**: Value EQ (emotional intelligence) significantly higher than IQ when evaluating software developers
- **Market positioning**: Position as thought leader in team building and hiring practices that prioritize human qualities

This ethos guides all content strategy and brand messaging around team building, hiring, and organizational culture.
