**Approach to building brand around hiring/team ethos:**

1. **Develop brand voice** — Define tone and language that conveys values around human-first hiring
2. **Identify target audience** — Reach people interested in alternative hiring and team building practices
3. **Create valuable content** — Align with brand message using multiple formats
4. **Promote content** — Use social media, email, other channels for brand awareness
5. **Engage audience** — Build relationships through comments, feedback, community interaction
6. **Collaborate with thought leaders** — Partner with industry experts to amplify message
7. **Measure results** — Track performance and optimize strategy using analytics

**Recommended content formats:**
- **Blog posts**: Importance of hiring great humans first, identifying key traits, building collaborative teams
- **Podcasts/videos**: Interviews with team leaders sharing insights on effective team building
- **Webinars/workshops**: Practical tips for hiring right people and building effective teams
- **E-books/guides**: In-depth exploration of psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation, collaboration culture
- **Social media**: Share content, case studies, team member profiles embodying brand values

**Content pillars to focus on:**
- Team building practices prioritizing human qualities
- Hiring strategies beyond technical skills
- Psychometric testing and assessment
- EQ/soft skills evaluation
- Building collaborative team culture
