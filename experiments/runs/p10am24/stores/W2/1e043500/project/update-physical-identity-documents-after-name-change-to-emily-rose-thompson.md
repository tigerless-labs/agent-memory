Checklist for updating essential government and identification documents after name change from John Wilson:

**Social Security Card:**
- Form SS-5 (Application for Social Security Card)
- Proof of citizenship (birth certificate or U.S. passport)
- Proof of identity (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Certified copy of divorce decree/court order
- Submit to local SSA office or mail

**U.S. Passport:**
- Form DS-11 (Application for U.S. Passport)
- Proof of citizenship (birth certificate or naturalization certificate)
- Proof of identity (driver's license, government ID, or current passport)
- Certified copy of divorce decree/court order
- One passport photo
- Take to passport acceptance facility or mail

**Driver's License/State ID:**
- Check state DMV requirements (vary by state)
- Certified copy of divorce decree/court order
- Proof of identity (current driver's license or state ID)
- Proof of residency (utility bill, lease, bank statement)
- Visit local DMV office

**Other Important Updates:**
- Voter registration
- Vehicle registration
- Property deeds
- Bank accounts and credit cards
- Insurance policies
- Loans and credit accounts
- Employer records (HR)
- Professional licenses

**Note:** Processing can take several weeks or months; keep photocopies of updated documents.
