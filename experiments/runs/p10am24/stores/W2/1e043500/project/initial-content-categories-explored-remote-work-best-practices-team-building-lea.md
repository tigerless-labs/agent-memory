**Six content categories initially developed:**

1. **Remote Work Best Practices**
   - Tips for managing remote teams effectively
   - Strategies for staying productive while working remotely
   - Best practices for communication in remote environments

2. **Remote Team Building**
   - Virtual team building activities and games
   - Strategies for building and maintaining team culture remotely
   - Best practices for managing team dynamics and resolving conflicts

3. **Remote Leadership**
   - Best practices for leading remote teams effectively
   - Strategies for developing and mentoring remote team members
   - Techniques for providing feedback and performance evaluations

4. **Remote Hiring and Onboarding**
   - Best practices for hiring and onboarding remote employees
   - Techniques for assessing candidates' fit for remote work
   - Strategies for creating effective remote onboarding programs

5. **Remote Work Tools and Technology**
   - Reviews and recommendations for remote work tools
   - Best practices for using video conferencing, chat, collaboration tools
   - Tips for maintaining data security and privacy

6. **Remote Work and Work-Life Balance**
   - Strategies for achieving work-life balance in remote environments
   - Tips for establishing boundaries and avoiding burnout
   - Guidance on maintaining healthy habits and routines

**Note**: These were used as initial exploration before narrowing focus to the specific brand ethos around hiring great humans first.
