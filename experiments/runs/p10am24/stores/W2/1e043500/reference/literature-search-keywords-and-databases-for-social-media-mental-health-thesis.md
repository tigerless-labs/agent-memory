Databases: PsycINFO, PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar. Core keywords: social media, mental health, young adults, depression, anxiety, usage patterns, online social networking. For mediating variables: self-esteem, social support, mediating role, indirect effect. For interventions: social media intervention, digital mental health, e-mental health, randomized controlled trial. For awareness: mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, peer support, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, TikTok. Search strategy: Boolean operators, wildcard, exact phrases, filter 2015+, systematic reviews.
