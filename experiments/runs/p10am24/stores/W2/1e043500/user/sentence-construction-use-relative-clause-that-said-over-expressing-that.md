**Error:** I just saw a tweet expressing that the TV show was interesting.

**Correct:** I just saw a tweet that said the TV show was interesting.

**Note:** The relative clause construction (that + verb) sounds more natural to native speakers than using 'expressing that'. Discussed 2023-05-20.
