Decision date: March 27, 2023

Camera: Fujifilm X-T30 II
- 26.1MP APS-C sensor
- 425 phase-detection AF points with 0.02s response time
- Compact size with vari-angle touchscreen LCD
- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)
- ~380 frames per charge battery life
- Price: ~$899 body only

Kit Lens: Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ
- Compact, versatile focal range
- Price: ~$499

Total Budget: ~$1,500 for body + lens

Photography Focus: Landscape and nature photography

Context: User has old Canon EOS 60D DSLR, new to mirrorless cameras, chose X-T30 II over Sony a6400 and a7 II due to compact size and vari-angle LCD screen.
