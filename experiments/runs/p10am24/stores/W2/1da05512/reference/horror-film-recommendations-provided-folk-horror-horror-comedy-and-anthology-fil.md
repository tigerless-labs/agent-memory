Folk horror: The Wicker Man (1973), The Devil's Backbone (2001), Kill List (2011), A Dark Song (2016), November (2017), Apostle (2018), Midsommar (2019). Horror-comedies: The Evil Dead (1981), Tucker & Dale vs Evil (2010), Scream (1996), The Faculty (1998), Ready or Not (2019), Shaun of the Dead (2004), Zombieland (2009), What We Do in the Shadows (2014), Cooties (2014), Tragedy Girls (2017). Anthologies: Creepshow (1982), Tales from the Darkside: The Movie (1990), Trick r Treat (2009), V/H/S (2012), Scare Package (2019).
