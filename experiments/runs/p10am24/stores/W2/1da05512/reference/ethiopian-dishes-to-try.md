Essential Ethiopian cuisine experiences:

- Injera: Sourdough flatbread used as a utensil to scoop up stews and curries
- Doro Wot: Spicy chicken stew made with berbere spice (blend of chili powder, ginger, coriander, and other spices)
- Tibs: Spicy sautéed meat dish with beef or lamb, onions, peppers
- Kitfo: Raw beef dish seasoned with spices and chili powder
- Shiro: Thick stew made with ground chickpeas or lentils, served with injera
- Ethiopian Coffee: Famous for traditional coffee ceremony involving roasting beans, grinding, and brewing in a special pot
