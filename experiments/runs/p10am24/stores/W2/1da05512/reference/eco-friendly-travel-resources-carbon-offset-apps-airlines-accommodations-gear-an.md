## Eco-Friendly Travel Gear
- Refillable water bottles (BPA-free)
- Beeswax wraps
- Rechargeable power banks
- Travel-sized/refillable toiletries, shampoo bars, toothpaste tablets
- Cloth travel towels
- Merino wool clothing
- Quick-drying fabrics
- Solar-powered chargers
- Reusable straws (bamboo, stainless steel, glass)
- Travel laundry detergent sheets
- Reef-safe sunscreen
