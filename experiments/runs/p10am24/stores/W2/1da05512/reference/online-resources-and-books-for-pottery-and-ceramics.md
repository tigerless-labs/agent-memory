Websites and Online Courses: Craftsy (pottery and ceramics courses), Pottery Making Illustrated (comprehensive tutorials and articles), The Potter's Council (pottery resources and tutorials), Ceramic Arts Network (online community with tutorials and articles). YouTube channels: Simon Leach Pottery (renowned potter with tutorials on techniques), Pottery By Osa (variety of pottery tutorials), The Ceramic School (pottery tutorials by experienced ceramic artists). Recommended books: 'The Potter's Bible' by Marylin Scott (comprehensive guide to ceramics), 'Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter' by Ian Gregory (detailed guide to glaze formulation and application).
