Popular festivals in Ethiopia to experience local culture:

- Timkat (January 19): Religious festival marking baptism of Jesus; features processions, music, dancing, water splashing
- Meskel (September 27): Religious festival commemorating the finding of the True Cross; includes lighting of bonfires, feasting, traditional dancing
- Enkutatash (September 11): Ethiopian New Year; celebrated with feasting, dancing, exchange of gifts
- Fasika (April): Ethiopian Orthodox Easter celebration; includes fasting, prayer, processions, feasting, gift exchange
