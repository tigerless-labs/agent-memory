Recommended modern sculptors for further study:

1. **Anish Kapoor** - large-scale abstract installations with reflective surfaces, voids, and geometric forms
2. **Rachel Whiteread** - intricate large-scale installations exploring space, memory, and human experience
3. **Richard Serra** - large-scale site-specific installations with massive steel plates and curves
4. **Jeff Koons** - hyper-realistic sculptures on popular culture and consumerism themes
5. **Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen** - playful large-scale installations transforming everyday objects
6. **Ursula von Rydingsvard** - large-scale abstract organic forms exploring nature and human experience
7. **Barbara Hepworth** - modernist sculptor with abstract biomorphic forms
8. **Tony Cragg** - abstract colorful surreal works with found objects and industrial materials
9. **Eva Rothschild** - large-scale installations with found objects, textiles, and mixed materials
10. **Phyllida Barlow** - large-scale colorful playful installations with found objects and fabric
