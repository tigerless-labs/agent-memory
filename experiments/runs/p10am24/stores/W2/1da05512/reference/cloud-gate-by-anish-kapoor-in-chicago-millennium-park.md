Cloud Gate is a large-scale stainless steel sculpture by Anish Kapoor installed in Chicago's Millennium Park in 2004. Also known as 'The Bean' due to its bean-like shape. Made of 168 stainless steel panels weighing over 110 tons, standing 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, and 66 feet long. The mirror-like polished surface reflects and distorts the surrounding environment, creating a sense of infinity. The curvature creates movement and fluidity, and the concave shape allows visitors to stand inside and experience the reflection from within. It is an interactive piece inviting visitors to walk around, touch, and photograph themselves. The surface is cleaned daily to maintain its mirror-like finish. Originally intended as temporary but became a permanent icon of Chicago. One of the most photographed landmarks in Chicago with over 10,000 photos taken daily.
