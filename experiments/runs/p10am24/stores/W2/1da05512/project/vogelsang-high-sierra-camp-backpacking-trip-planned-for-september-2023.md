Planning a 20-mile backpacking trip to Vogelsang High Sierra Camp in the Sierra Nevada mountains (September 2023). Route difficulty: moderate, elevation gain: 3000 feet, camp elevation: 10300 feet. Camp amenities include four-person tent cabins with wooden floors and canvas walls, full meal service, modern restrooms with showers, potable water, dishwashing station, and solar-powered lighting. Access via 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows or shuttle from visitor center. Reservations required through Yosemite Conservancy; permits required through Yosemite National Park. Chosen for being more manageable than 40-mile High Sierra Camp Loop while still offering high-elevation backpacking experience.
