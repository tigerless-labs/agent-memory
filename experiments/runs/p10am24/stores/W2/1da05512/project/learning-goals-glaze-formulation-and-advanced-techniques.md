Ryan wants to develop expertise in glaze formulation including: creating glaze recipes from scratch with understanding of material selection and ingredient roles; achieving specific colors (subtle blues, vibrant reds) and effects (textured, matte finishes); mixing and testing glazes for consistency while avoiding common mistakes; and mastering application techniques including brushing, pouring, and spraying. He recognizes this is complex and nuanced and is seeking guidance through online resources, books, and tutorials.
