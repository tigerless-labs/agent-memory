Solo day hike planned for late May 2023 (weekend of May 27-28) to Tilden Regional Park in Berkeley Hills. Considering Nimitz Way Trail (4.5 miles out-and-back, 1000 feet elevation gain, moderate, 3-4 hours) through forests and grasslands with Bay Area views, or Wildcat Gorge Trail (5.5 miles out-and-back, 1200 feet elevation gain, moderate to challenging, 4-5 hours) along Wildcat Creek with ridge views. Chosen for panoramic Bay Area views as alternative to Mount Tamalpais.
