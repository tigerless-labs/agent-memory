**Recipe:** 1 part coarsely ground coffee to 4 parts water (e.g., 1 cup coffee + 4 cups water)

Can adjust to 1:3 (stronger) or 1:5 (weaker) to taste.

**Grind:** Coarse (burr grinder preferred)

**Steeping time:** 12-24 hours; with dark roasts targeting 12-18 hours to avoid over-extraction

**Coffee choice:** Dark roasted beans for bold, smooth flavor with lower acidity and full body. Expect chocolate, caramel, smoky/toasted notes.

**Tips:** Use fresh high-quality beans; optionally bloom with hot water first; can experiment with flavorings (vanilla, cinnamon, citrus)
