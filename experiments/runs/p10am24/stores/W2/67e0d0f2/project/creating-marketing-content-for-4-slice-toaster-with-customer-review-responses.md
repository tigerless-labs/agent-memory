Toaster marketing project based on 4 customer reviews: Linzi Schwartz, Edward Farrey, Kindle Customer, and Tim S.

Key issues from reviews:
- Uneven toasting (top of bread doesn't toast as much as rest of slice)
- Crumbs spilling beyond clean-out tray
- Loud and jarring eject function
- Large size and weight, short cord

Customer positives:
- Sleek, modern design appreciated
- Wide slots accommodate thick bread, 4 slices, or English muffins
- Digital timing controls
- Ability to toast multiple items at once

Marketing content delivered:
- Individual positive response templates for each reviewer
- Blog post promoting toaster as sleek kitchen essential emphasizing design, wide-slot functionality, and multi-slice capacity
