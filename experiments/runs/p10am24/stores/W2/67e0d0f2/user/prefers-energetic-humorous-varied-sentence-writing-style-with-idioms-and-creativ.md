When rewriting content, prefers:
- Energetic and passionate tone with humor and creative analogies
- Varied sentence lengths and complexity to create dynamic, engaging flow
- Common idioms and figurative expressions
- Synonyms and antonyms to convey meaning in multiple ways
- Unique but accessible vocabulary
- Descriptive language and vivid imagery to paint pictures
- Unpredictability and intrigue through creative phrasing and constructions
- Short and long sentences interspersed
- Avoidance of uniform, AI-sounding sentences
- Retention of original meaning and factual accuracy
