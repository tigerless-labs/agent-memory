Planning to sell a rare first edition book. Key marketplaces: AbeBooks, Biblio, Alibris. Can also consult The Strand (NYC), Powell's City of Books (Portland), or Peter Harrington Rare Books (UK). May want to get professional appraisal first and consider auction houses like Christie's or Sotheby's for extremely valuable books.
