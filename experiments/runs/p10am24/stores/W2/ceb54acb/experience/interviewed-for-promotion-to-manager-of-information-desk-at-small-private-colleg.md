Prepared for a promotion interview to Manager of Information Desk at a small private college. Developed interview strategy with questions to ask employees and closing questions for hiring team.

**Key questions prepared for employees:**
- Most important responsibilities of the role
- Information desk's role in serving campus community
- Biggest challenges and college support
- Interdepartmental collaboration
- Essential qualities for success
- College work culture and values
- Professional development opportunities
- Success metrics and performance evaluation
- Feedback from students, faculty, staff
- Upcoming initiatives the role will handle

**Closing questions for hiring team:**
- Concerns about qualifications
- Role fit within department and organization goals
- New initiatives and contributions
- Professional development in role
- Anticipated challenges and opportunities
- Success metrics and KPIs for position
- Support and resources available
- Cross-departmental collaboration approach
- How strengths align with desired qualities
- Timeline and next steps
