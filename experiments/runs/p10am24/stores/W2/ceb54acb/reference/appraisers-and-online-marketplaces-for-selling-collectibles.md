Appraisers for vintage jewelry: ASA (American Society of Appraisers), NAJA (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers), ISA (International Society of Appraisers).

Vinyl records: Discogs, eBay, MusicStack, Reverb LP, Vinyl Me Please, The Vinyl Factory, r/vinylcollectors.

Rare books: AbeBooks, Biblio, Alibris, The Strand (NYC), Powell's City of Books (Portland), Peter Harrington Rare Books (UK), Christie's, Sotheby's, r/rarebooks.

Typewriters: eBay, Etsy, Ruby Lane, The Typewriter Shop (UK), Typewriter Collector (US), Gramercy Typewriter (NYC), The Typewriter Forum, r/typewriters.

Vintage cameras: eBay, Etsy, KEH Camera, Leica Store, Camera West, The Camera Shop (UK), r/vintagecameras, Leica Forum.

Rare coins: eBay, NGC (Numismatic Guaranty Corporation), CoinWeek, APMEX, JM Bullion, Liberty Coin, Heritage Auctions, Stack's Bowers, r/coincollecting.
