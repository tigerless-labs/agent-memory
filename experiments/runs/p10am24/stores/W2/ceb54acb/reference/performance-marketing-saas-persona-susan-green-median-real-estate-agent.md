Refined buyer persona for real estate agent SaaS tools, based on NAR statistics.

**Demographics:**
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female
- Location: suburban/rural
- Median income: $61,140

**Professional Profile:**
- Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field
- Works independently with brokerage support
- Active in local real estate community

**Pain Points:**
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking/analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents
- Keeping up with new technology and trends

**Goals:**
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion rates
- Stay competitive in market
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

**Technology:**
- Uses: CRM software, email marketing, social media
- Open to new tools that improve efficiency
- ROI-focused adoption

**Marketing Channels:**
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking and referrals
- Direct mail and email
- Social media advertising

**Buying Behavior:**
- Cost-conscious; focuses on demonstrable ROI
- Monitors and adjusts budget based on performance
- Leverages brokerage resources and support
