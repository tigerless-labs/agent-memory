The FOMC reviews GDP, inflation, unemployment, consumer spending, and other economic indicators when deciding whether to adjust interest rates. Decisions aim to balance the Fed's mandate of stable prices, maximum employment, and moderate long-term interest rates. Interest rate changes can significantly affect borrowing costs for mortgages and credit cards, returns on savings accounts, and overall inflation in the economy.
