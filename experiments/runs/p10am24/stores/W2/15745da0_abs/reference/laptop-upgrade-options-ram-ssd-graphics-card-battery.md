Other laptop upgrades to consider beyond RAM upgrade:
- **SSD upgrade**: If laptop has traditional hard drive, upgrading to SSD provides significant performance improvement in loading times and responsiveness
- **Graphics card upgrade**: For gaming or graphics-intensive applications
- **CPU upgrade**: For older processors; provides noticeable performance boost
- **Battery upgrade**: Higher-capacity battery or external battery options for better battery life
- **Check compatibility**: Ensure motherboard and BIOS support upgrades before purchasing
- **Priority**: Generally SSD upgrade provides more noticeable improvement than RAM for general users
