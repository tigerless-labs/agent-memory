## Safeguard Against Appraiser Bias in Complexity Assessment

An appraisal professional's designation of a property as complex does not conclusively establish its complexity. Although appraisers possess expertise and experience in evaluating property complexities, it is imperative to meticulously consider their opinion and accompanying evidence in conjunction with our own research and analysis.

**Key concern:** Appraisers may have a vested interest in securing the highest fee possible, and may therefore be inclined to search for justifications of complexity.

**Team responsibility:** Exercise caution in judgment and ensure that our decisions are informed by objective criteria and a comprehensive examination of the property and market conditions.

This guidance was developed to maintain consistency in fee decisions and protect against fee inflation driven by appraiser incentive misalignment.
