## Property Complexity Assessment Categories

**Location:**
- Remote area or limited access
- Unique environmental/geographical features (hillside, water, wooded)
- High crime rate or environmental hazards
- Unusual street layout (cul-de-sac, steep driveway)

**Property type and design:**
- Unusual/complex design (multi-unit, multiple levels, attached garage)
- Special features (pool, hot tub, large deck)
- Complex mechanical systems (geothermal, radiant heat)
- Outbuildings (barn, shed, guesthouse)

**Age and condition:**
- Significantly older/newer than comparable market properties
- Extensive repairs/renovations required
- Significant/unusual wear and tear (water damage, pest issues)
- Structural alterations or conversions

**Zoning and land use:**
- Unique restrictions (floodplain, conservation area, historic district)
- Complex regulatory environment (strict HOA rules, wetlands)
- Zoning variances or conditional use permits

**Market conditions:**
- Rapidly appreciating/declining market
- Unique demographics (vacation homes, high-end properties)
- Unusual trends (high unemployment, foreclosures)
- Supply/demand imbalances

**Purpose:** Enable team to consistently, objectively assess whether properties meet complexity criteria for fee justification. Reduces errors and maintains lender client confidence.
