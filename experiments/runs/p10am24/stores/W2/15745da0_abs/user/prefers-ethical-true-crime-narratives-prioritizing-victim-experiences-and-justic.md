Values conscious, mindful consumption of true crime narratives that:
- Prioritize victims' experiences and dignity over graphic details
- Focus on justice and preventing future crimes
- Challenge normalization of violence
- Raise awareness of social issues rather than glorifying perpetrators

Believes consumers should:
- Hold themselves accountable for how they consume these narratives
- Hold media creators and outlets accountable for ethical storytelling practices
- Remember the real people and real tragedies behind stories
- Support narratives that promote justice, empathy, and education

Frustrated with exploitative, sensationalized true crime stories that re-traumatize families and prioritize entertainment over justice.
