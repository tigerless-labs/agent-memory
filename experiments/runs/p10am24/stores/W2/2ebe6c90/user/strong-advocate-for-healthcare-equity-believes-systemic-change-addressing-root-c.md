Values healthcare equity and addressing healthcare disparities as moral and economic imperative. Believes marginalized communities should be prioritized for healthcare funding. Advocates for proactive systemic change rather than reactive crisis response. Emphasizes that root causes (poverty, discrimination, lack of education) must be addressed through coordinated, sustained effort from governments, institutions, and communities. Opposes waiting for crises like pandemics to spur action on healthcare inequalities.
