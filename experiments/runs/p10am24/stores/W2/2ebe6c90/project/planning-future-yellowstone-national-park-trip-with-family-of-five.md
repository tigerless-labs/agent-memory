Accommodations: interested in Canyon Lodge & Cabins.

Geysers: Old Faithful, Grand Geyser, Castle Geyser, Daisy Geyser.

Hot springs: Mammoth Hot Springs, Norris Geyser Basin, Boiling River.

Wildlife viewing: especially bears. Best areas—Lamar Valley, Slough Creek, Tower-Roosevelt Area, Mammoth Hot Springs.

Grand Canyon of Yellowstone River—priority to explore and hike.

Hiking trails: Brink of Lower Falls Trail (3/4 mi, easy), Seven Mile Hole Trail (10 mi), South Rim Trail (6 mi, easy).

Scenic drives: Grand Loop Road, Upper and Lower Yellowstone Falls scenic drives.

Needs: strategies for keeping children occupied on long RV drives.
