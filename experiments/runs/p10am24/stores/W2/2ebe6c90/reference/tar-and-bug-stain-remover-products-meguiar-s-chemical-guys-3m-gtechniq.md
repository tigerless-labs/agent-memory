Recommendations for tar and bug stain removers for car paint:

1. **Meguiar's Tar & Bug Remover** - Popular and well-reviewed, safe for most paint finishes, easy to apply and wipe off
2. **Chemical Guys Tar & Bug Remover** - Favorite among detailing enthusiasts, gentle yet effective, safe on wheels and trim
3. **3M Tar & Bug Remover** - More aggressive formula, designed for tough stains and overspray, requires testing on small area first
4. **Gtechniq Tar & Bug Remover** - Premium product, gentle yet effective

**Application Tips:**
- Always read instructions first
- Test on inconspicuous area before applying
- Spray onto microfiber cloth, not directly on paint
- Use gentle rubbing motion to avoid scratching
- Follow up with wash and wax to protect paint
