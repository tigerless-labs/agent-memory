Recommended car waxes for protecting and polishing car paint:

1. **Meguiar's Gold Class Carnauba Plus Premium Wax** - Classic, easy-to-use, deep glossy finish. Higher price but high quality
2. **Chemical Guys Car Wax** - Popular, affordable, easy application, high-gloss finish, safe for most paint
3. **Turtle Wax Carnauba Premium Car Wax** - Well-known brand, good quality at affordable price, deep glossy finish
4. **P21S Concours Carnauba Wax** - Premium wax for enthusiasts, expensive, showroom-quality finish

**Application Tips:**
- Apply in thin layers and allow each layer to haze before buffing
- Avoid direct sunlight during application
- Wax car every 2-3 months for best results
- Use clean, soft cloth to avoid scratching
- Best for after tar/bug removal and cleaning
