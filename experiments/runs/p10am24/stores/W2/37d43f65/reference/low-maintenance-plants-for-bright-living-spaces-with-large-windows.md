## Recommended low-maintenance plants for bright indoor spaces:

- **Snake Plant** — air purifying, survives low light, tolerates weeks without watering
- **ZZ Plant** — tolerates range of lighting, infrequent watering, air purifying
- **Spider Plant** — easy care, white flowers, good air purifier, easily propagated
- **Pothos** — versatile, works in various light conditions, trainable to climb walls
- **Succulents** (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) — very low-maintenance, variety of shapes/colors, minimal watering
- **Dracaena** — tolerates range of conditions, air purifying, grows tall
- **Peace Lily** — elegant white blooms, air purifying, tolerates neglect
- **Bromeliads** — low-maintenance, colorful flowers, thrives in bright indirect light
