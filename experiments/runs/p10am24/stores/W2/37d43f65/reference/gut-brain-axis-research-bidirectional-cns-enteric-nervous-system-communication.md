Research area in neuroscience examining the gut-brain axis—bidirectional communication pathway between central nervous system and enteric nervous system in gastrointestinal tract. Emerging evidence suggests gut microbes play a role in neurological and psychiatric diseases; regulating gut microbiome may impact mental health and cognitive function.
