Planning a dedicated workspace/reading nook in living room with big windows. Setup includes: compact desk or console table (considering wall-mounted, narrow, drop-leaf, ottoman with tray, or foldable options), small circular rug (3-4 feet diameter) to define space, small circular mirror (12-18 inches) above desk for visual interest and light reflection, and artwork/prints above desk for personality. Also considering adding low-maintenance plants to the space.
