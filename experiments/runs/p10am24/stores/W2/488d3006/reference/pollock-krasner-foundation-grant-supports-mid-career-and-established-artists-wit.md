Established in 1985 by Lee Krasner. Open to artists who have been actively working for at least 5 years. Provides monetary awards for art supplies, studio rent, and expenses. Award amount determined by individual needs and selection committee evaluation. Eligibility requires artistic merit through exhibition history and demonstration of financial need. Foundation maintains archives about Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner. Notable recipients: Mark Bradford, Kara Walker, Robert Gober, David Salle, Elizabeth Murray, Frank Stella, Chuck Close, Julie Mehretu, Kiki Smith, Jasper Johns, Joan Mitchell.
