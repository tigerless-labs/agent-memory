The GR-90 is a circular hiking trail in the Natural Park of Moncayo mountain in Aragon.

Route details:
- Distance: approximately 36 kilometers
- Duration: 2-3 days to complete
- Type: Circular trail passing through small towns and villages

Highlights:
- Summit of Moncayo mountain (highest peak in the park)
- Fuenfria waterfall
- Meadows, forests, and rock formations

Best time to visit: Spring and autumn (autumn recommended for vibrant forest colors and refreshing weather without heavy summer crowds).

Note: Winter snowfall may make hiking impossible; summer can be very hot and overcrowded.
