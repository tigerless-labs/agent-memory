**Must-see Attractions:**
- **The Narrows**: Scenic hike along Virgin River through narrowest part of Zion Canyon; expect wading through water
- **Angels Landing**: Iconic rock formation with spectacular views; steep, narrow final ascent
- **The Emerald Pools**: Three pools/waterfalls along Kayenta Trail; refreshing swim opportunity
- **Weeping Rock**: Rock formation with dripping springs and lush vegetation; short, easy hike

**Popular Hikes:**
- **Angel's Landing Hike**: 5 miles, strenuous, steep drop-offs and narrow ridges
- **The West Rim Trail**: 10 miles along west rim with canyon views
- **The Observation Point Trail**: 8 miles to viewpoint with spectacular canyon views
- **The Kayenta Trail**: 2 miles to Emerald Pools and Weeping Rock
- **The Watchman Trail**: 3 miles to viewpoint with views of Springdale

**Park Tips:**
- Free shuttle service throughout park
- Check website for permits/restrictions
- Bring plenty of water and sunscreen
- Wear sturdy hiking shoes with good traction
- Stay on designated trails, respect wildlife, pack out all trash
