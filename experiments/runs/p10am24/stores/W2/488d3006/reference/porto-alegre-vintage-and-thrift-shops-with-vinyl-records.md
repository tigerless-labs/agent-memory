## Porto Alegre Vintage/Thrift Shops

**Shops with vinyl records:**
- **Armazém do Passado** — vintage clothing, accessories, decor; carries Brazilian music, rock, and pop vinyl records from various decades
- **Casa da Sogra** — retro and vintage home decor, furniture, accessories; selection of vinyl records including rare and hard-to-find titles

**Other shops:**
- **Brechó Municipal** — affordable municipal marketplace for used clothes, shoes, and accessories
- **Bazar Vintage** — stylish, fashion-forward store selling vintage and retro clothing and accessories
- **Pinga do Brechó** — vintage and thrift store with clothing, accessories, and kitchenware

**Price ranges:** Brechó Municipal most affordable; Pinga do Brechó slightly higher but still reasonable; Armazém do Passado and Bazar Vintage more curated with varying prices.
