2 1/4 cups flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt, 1 cup butter, 1 cup sugar, 1/2 cup blue cheese (crumbled), 2 eggs, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375F for 12-15 minutes.
