Recommended bear-resistant storage options for camping:

**Containers:**
- **Garcia Bear-Resistant Container**: Durable plastic, meets IGBC standards
- **BearVault**: Heavy-duty plastic, easy to use and secure
- **Ursack**: Lightweight, puncture-resistant material for backpackers

**Bags:**
- **Ursack Major**: Durable, puncture-resistant material
- **Counter Assault Bear Bag**: Heavy-duty, secure design
- **Sea to Summit Ultra-Sil Bear Bag**: Lightweight, compact, puncture-resistant

Selection criteria: durable/puncture-resistant material, bear-resistant design, IGBC certification, ease of use, appropriate size.
