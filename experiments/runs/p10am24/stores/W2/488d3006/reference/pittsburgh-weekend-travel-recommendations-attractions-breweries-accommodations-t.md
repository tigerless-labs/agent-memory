Attractions: Strip District (street food, markets, Primanti Bros), Andy Warhol Museum, Mount Washington (Duquesne/Monongahela inclines), Phipps Conservatory, Three Rivers Heritage Trail, Carnegie Science Center, PNC Park, North Shore. Neighborhoods: Oakland, Shadyside. Breweries: Penn Brewery, East End Brewing Company, Grist House Brewing. Hotels: Hotel Indigo ($150/night), The Oaklander ($120/night), Hotel Shadyside ($120/night), The Inn on Walnut ($100/night), Airbnb ($80–$150/night). Transit: Port Authority Day Pass $5.50, Healthy Ride bike share, Free Transit Zone downtown. Flights: $150–$250 round trip from NYC.
