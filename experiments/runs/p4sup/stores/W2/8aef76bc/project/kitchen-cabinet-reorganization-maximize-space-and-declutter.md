## Goal\nReorganize kitchen cabinets to maximize space and eliminate clutter.\n\n## Approach\n1. Purge and declutter items\n2. Categorize and group similar items (baking, cooking, dinnerware, utensils)\n3. Assign a home for each group\n4. Use vertical space with stackable containers and baskets\n5. Optimize shelf layout with frequently used items on accessible shelves\n6. Utilize back of doors for storage\n7. Install dividers or inserts\n8. Store heavy items on lower shelves\n9. Use adjustable shelves for flexibility\n10. Maintain organization every 6-12 months\n\n## Decluttering Criteria\n- If not used in a year, likely safe to get rid of\n- Assess if item is loved or needed\n- Check if item is in good condition\n- Consider if item can be easily replaced\n- Ask if it brings joy or serves a purpose\n- Remove duplicates, keep only favorites\n- Get rid of broken or outdated items\n- Take photos of sentimental items before letting go
