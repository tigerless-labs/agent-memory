Goal: Gain visibility into spending habits and make intentional purchasing decisions.\n\n**Approach:**\n- Record all transactions (including small purchases like coffee)\n- Create clear categories: Housing, Food, Transportation, Clothing, Entertainment, Health, Personal Care, Debt, Savings, Misc\n- For clothing: subdivide into Luxury (designer brands, high-end stores like Saks Fifth Avenue) and Budget (fast fashion, discount stores)\n- For groceries: use subcategories like produce, meat, dairy, specialty items\n- Track spending monthly and analyze patterns\n\n**Key insight:** Wants to balance luxury purchases with budget consciousness rather than going to extremes in either direction. Recognizes oscillating pattern and wants to create spending awareness through categorization.
