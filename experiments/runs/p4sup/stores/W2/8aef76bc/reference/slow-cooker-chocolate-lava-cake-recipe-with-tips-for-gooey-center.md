**Slow Cooker Chocolate Lava Cake**

Ingredients:
- 1 can chocolate cake mix
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:
- Cook on low for 2-3 hours
- Serve with vanilla ice cream

**Tips for perfect gooey center:**
- Use high-quality chocolate cake mix (at least 30% cocoa content)
- Don't overmix - blend ingredients gently
- Use 2-quart slow cooker (prevents cooking too quickly)
- Cook on low and slow, never rush
- Check doneness at 2 hours with toothpick - if clean or few moist crumbs, it's done. If not, cook 30 more minutes
- Let rest 10-15 minutes after cooking to retain gooeyness
- For extra gooeyness: add higher ratio of oil, extra tablespoon chocolate chips, or spoonful of Nutella/peanut butter to batter
- Serve warm with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream for best contrast
