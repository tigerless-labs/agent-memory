Online retailers: Greenington, Vivaterra, Joybird. Other sources: thrift stores, consignment shops, antique stores for secondhand/vintage pieces; custom furniture makers using sustainable materials; local artisans creating from reclaimed or salvaged materials; DIY upcycling. Sustainable elements: LED light bulbs, sustainable fabrics (organic cotton, bamboo, hemp), indoor plants, FSC-certified furniture, natural cleaning products.
