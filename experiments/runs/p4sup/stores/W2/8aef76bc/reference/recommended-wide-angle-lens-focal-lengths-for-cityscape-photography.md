Recommended wide-angle lens focal lengths for cityscape photography by sensor type:

**APS-C format:**
- 10-22mm
- 12-24mm

**Full-frame:**
- 14-24mm
- 16-35mm
- 14mm or 15mm prime lenses

These focal lengths are ideal for capturing expansive urban landscapes, emphasizing the grandeur of buildings and streets, and providing a unique perspective for cityscape compositions.
