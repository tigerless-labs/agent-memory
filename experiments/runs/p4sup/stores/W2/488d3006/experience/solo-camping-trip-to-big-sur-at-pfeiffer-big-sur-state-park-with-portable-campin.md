Took a solo camping trip to Big Sur and stayed at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park campground. This was the maiden voyage for a portable camping stove. Properly stored food in airtight containers kept in car and packed out all trash when leaving campsite. Experienced hiking in similar terrain to what's found at Zion National Park. Focused on minimizing environmental impact and wildlife safety during the trip.
