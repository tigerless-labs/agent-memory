GR-90 is a circular hiking trail in Natural Park of Moncayo mountain in Aragón, approximately 36 kilometers long, taking 2-3 days to complete. Key highlights:\n\n- Reaches the summit of Moncayo mountain (highest peak in the park)\n- Features the beautiful Fuenfría waterfall\n- Passes through beautiful meadows, forests, and rock formations\n- Passes through several small towns and villages offering cultural and historical landmarks (includes Monastery of Veruela and Castle of Trasmoz)\n\nBest seasons: spring (wildflower blooms, mild climate) or autumn (vibrant forest colors, refreshing weather, fewer crowds).
