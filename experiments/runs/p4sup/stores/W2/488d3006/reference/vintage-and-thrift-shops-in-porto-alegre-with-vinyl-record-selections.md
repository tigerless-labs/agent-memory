Shops recommended for Porto Alegre:

**Armazém do Passado** — vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor, and vinyl records (Brazilian music, rock, pop from various decades); curated/trendy styles

**Casa da Sogra** — retro/vintage home decor, furniture, accessories, and rare vinyl records; prices vary by size/condition

**Brechó Municipal** — municipal marketplace for used clothes, shoes, accessories; known for affordable prices

**Bazar Vintage** — stylish, fashion-forward vintage and retro clothing/accessories; varying prices

**Pinga do Brechó** — vintage/thrift store with clothing, accessories, kitchenware; prices higher than Brechó Municipal but still reasonable

Armazém do Passado and Casa da Sogra specifically known for vinyl record selections.
