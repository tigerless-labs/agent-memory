Resources for back pain management and recovery from herniated disc:

Websites: American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (www.aaos.org), American Physical Therapy Association (www.moveforwardpt.com), NIAMS (www.niams.nih.gov), Spine-Health (www.spine-health.com), Mayo Clinic (www.mayoclinic.org).

Books: 'Treat Your Own Back' by Robin McKenzie, 'The Back Pain Bible' by Dr. Todd Sinett, 'Back Pain: A Movement Problem' by Josephine Key.

YouTube: Dr. Berg, Physical Therapy Videos, Yoga With Adriene.

Podcasts: The Back Pain Podcast, The Physical Therapy Podcast.

Apps: Back Pain Exercises and PT-Helper (both iOS/Android).
