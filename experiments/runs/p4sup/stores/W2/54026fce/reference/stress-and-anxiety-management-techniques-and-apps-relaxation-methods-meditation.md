Relaxation techniques for managing stress and anxiety:

Deep Breathing (4-7-8 pattern: inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec), Progressive Muscle Relaxation, Mindfulness Meditation, Visualization, Yoga.

Meditation and wellness apps: Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer (free), Breethe, Happify.

Other tools: Guided meditation videos (YouTube/Vimeo), essential oils (lavender, chamomile, bergamot), nature sounds, journaling, grounding techniques using five senses.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR) practice: Systematically tense and relax muscle groups from toes to face, 5-7 seconds tense, 10-15 seconds release. Can be practiced 2-3 times weekly. Start with 10-15 minute sessions. Helpful for reducing muscle tension, pain, and anxiety, and improving sleep quality.
