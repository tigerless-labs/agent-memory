## Dinner Party Planning (May 2023)

**Theme:** Spring-inspired entertaining with pastels, fresh flowers, potted plants, and lavender accents

**Signature Cocktail:** Lavender Gin Fizz
- Gin, lemon juice, simple syrup, lavender syrup
- Garnished with lavender sprig and lemon wheel
- Chosen for light, refreshing quality matching spring aesthetic

**Desserts:** Mix of floral and fruit-inspired options
- Lemon Lavender Panna Cotta
- Rose Petal Macarons  
- Strawberry Rhubarb Crisp
- Lemon Poppy Seed Cake
- Edible flower garnishes

**Sourcing:** Committed to sustainable materials
- Fresh flowers from farmers markets or local sources
- Potted plants from local nurseries or plant communities
- Plan to donate/repurpose plants after event

**Style Notes:** Prefers lavender and floral elements, local/sustainable sourcing, spring pastels and earthy tones, Instagram-worthy presentation
