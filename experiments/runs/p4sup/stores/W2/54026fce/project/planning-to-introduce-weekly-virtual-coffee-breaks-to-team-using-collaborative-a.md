**Approach:** Frame as collaborative suggestion; discuss with team first; encourage participation in shaping format; stay attentive to quieter members; adapt based on feedback.

**Ground rules:** No work talk; keep light and casual; respect 30-min timeframe; use video; minimize distractions; rotate conversation starters; practice active listening.

**Challenges to address:** Technical reliability; time zone coordination; scheduling conflicts; cultural/language barriers; home distractions; low engagement risk.

**Additional ideas:** Dedicated virtual social platform for informal hangouts; consistent scheduling; start small and test; gather regular feedback.
