**Hadestown**: Modern retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice myth set in post-apocalyptic world.

**Creative Team:**
- Composer/Lyricist: Anaïs Mitchell
- Director: Rachel Chavkin
- Musical Director/Orchestrator: David Lai

**Music Style:** Folk-opera, Americana, blues, soul; mixture of solo numbers, duets, and ensemble pieces.

**Key Info:**
- Concept album released 2010
- Off-Broadway premiere 2016
- Broadway transfer 2019
- Won 8 Tony Awards in 2019, including Best Musical
- Themes: love and sacrifice, power and corruption, hope and redemption
- Notable for emotional resonance and integration of music into storytelling

**Notable Songs:**
- "Way Down Hadestown"
- "It's a Deal"
- "All I've Ever Known"
- "Why We Build the Wall"
