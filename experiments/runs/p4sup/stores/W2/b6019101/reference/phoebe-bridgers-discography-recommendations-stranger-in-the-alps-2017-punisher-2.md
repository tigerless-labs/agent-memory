## Phoebe Bridgers Discography

**Albums to explore:**
- **Stranger in the Alps** (2017) — Debut album, critically acclaimed for introspective songwriting and indie-rock/folk elements. Recommended as starting point.
- **Punisher** (2020) — Sophomore album, more experimental and introspective. Explores themes of love, loss, and introspection.
- **Boygenius** (2018) — Self-titled EP collaboration with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus. Features harmony-rich vocal arrangements.

**Popular tracks:**
- Scott Street
- Killer
- Motion Sickness
- Funeral
- Kyoto

**Other work:**
- Better Oblivion Community Center (2019) — Collaborative album with Conor Oberst
- "7" (2020) — Single

Known for emotive and introspective live performances (NPR Tiny Desk, KCRW sessions available on YouTube).
