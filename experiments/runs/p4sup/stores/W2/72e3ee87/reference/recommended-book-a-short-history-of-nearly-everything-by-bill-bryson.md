**A Short History of Nearly Everything** by Bill Bryson

Overview: Comprehensive and entertaining survey of sciences covering Big Bang to rise of civilization; makes science accessible to non-experts.

Structure (6 parts):
1. The Origins of the Universe
2. The Size of the Earth
3. A New Age Dawns
4. The Road to Us
5. The Stuff of Life
6. The Earth and Its Inhabitants

Writing style: Engaging, witty, concise; uses anecdotes, historical examples, vivid descriptions; conversational tone. Includes stories about scientists themselves.

Complexity level: Clear explanations accessible to readers without science background.

Awards: Aventis Prize for Science Books (2004); shortlisted for Samuel Johnson Prize.

Fits interests: Science and history; recommended for daily 30-minute reading sessions.
