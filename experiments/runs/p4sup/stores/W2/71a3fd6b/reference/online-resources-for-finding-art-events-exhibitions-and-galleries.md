Resources for discovering art events and exhibitions in your city:

**Event Listing Platforms:**
- Eventbrite
- Meetup
- Lanyrd

**Art-Specific Websites:**
- Artsy — exhibitions, art fairs, auctions worldwide
- Artnet — news, exhibitions, events
- Hyperallergic — articles about exhibitions
- Artforum — reviews and articles about exhibitions
- CultureTrip — cultural events worldwide
- Time Out — events, exhibitions, things to do

**Other Methods:**
- Local arts council or tourism board websites
- Social media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) — follow galleries directly
- Google Alerts — set up for "art events in [city]" or "art exhibitions in [city]"
- Local art newspapers or magazines
- Gallery websites — "Newsletter" or "Stay Connected" sections

**For Gallery Information:**
- Gallery websites — "About", "History", "Past Exhibitions" sections
- Wikipedia — often has articles on notable galleries
- Online archives through libraries and cultural institutions
- Contact gallery directly for comprehensive information
