Ms. Johnson highlighted several memorable exhibitions at the City Art Gallery:

- **2015**: Retrospective on Emily Wilson, local abstract expressionist pioneer (50+ works from private collections and museums worldwide)
- **2018**: Contemporary street art show, partnership with local graffiti collective transforming the entire gallery into immersive environment
- **2012**: History of photography in the city exhibition, partnership with local historical society covering the city's development over past century
- **2019**: Sustainable art practices show featuring local artists using recycled materials, found objects, and eco-friendly methods

The gallery is committed to showcasing the local art scene and supporting various artistic movements and practices.
