## Reflection Approaches\n\n**Question Prompts:**\n- What were main takeaways?\n- What resonated and why?\n- What challenged my assumptions?\n- How can I apply this to my life/work?\n- What do I still wonder about?\n- How did it make me feel?\n- What was most interesting or surprising?\n- How does it relate to current goals?\n- What actions will I take?\n\n## Structured Formats\n\n1. **Free Writing** — Stream of consciousness without editing\n2. **Structured Template** — Intro, Takeaways, Applications, Questions, Conclusion\n3. **Mind Mapping** — Visual connections between ideas\n4. **Journaling** — Personal, introspective entry format\n5. **Book Club Style** — Conversational review as if discussing with friends\n6. **Top 5 List** — Most important insights\n7. **Letter Writing** — To author or character\n8. **Visual Reflection** — Sketch, collage, or diagram\n\n## Digital Tools\n- Goodreads reviews\n- Blog posts\n- Note-taking apps (Evernote, OneNote, Simplenote)\n- Custom templates
