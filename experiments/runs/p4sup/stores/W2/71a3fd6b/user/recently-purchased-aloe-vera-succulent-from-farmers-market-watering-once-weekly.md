Acquired aloe vera succulent from farmers' market approximately 3 weeks ago (around May 1, 2023). Currently watering once per week. Care approach: well-draining cactus/succulent soil required, prefers bright indirect light (east or west-facing window), optimal temperature 65-75°F, monthly fertilization during growing season (spring/summer) at half-strength. N-P-K fertilizers like Miracle-Gro Cactus/Palm/Citrus, Schultz Cactus & Succulent, or Espoma Organic Cactus. Sensitive to overwatering—monitor soil moisture before each watering.
