Reads a mix of dense non-fiction and graphic novels/manga. Alternates between them to maintain motivation and manage varying reading speeds. Graphic novels and manga are quicker to get through, helping boost progress on ambitious reading goals. Example pattern: 2-3 days of dense non-fiction, then 1-2 days of graphic novels/manga.
