Platform Workflow (6 steps):

1. YouTube Viewers → entry point
2. AI Chatbot Health Assessment — personalized assessment for cancer, depression, anxiety, chronic kidney disease (from Diabetes/Hypertension), with free health risk assessment
3. Free Music Therapy — listening to music therapy songs for anxiety, depression, and anger management
4. NFT Token Purchase — buy NFT tokens to support cancer patients and innovations
5. Mental Health Screening — PHQ 2 or PHQ 10 screening questionnaires
6. Group Counseling — joining group counseling with counselors and specialists via Calendly platform or custom EHR booking calendar platform

Key Elements:
- AI-driven health risk stratification
- Music therapy as core therapeutic component
- NFT tokens as funding/support mechanism for cancer research
- Structured mental health screening (validated PHQ tools)
- Professional counseling with specialist coordination
- Multiple booking options: Calendly or EHR platform
