Tips for starting a blog as a freelancer:

**Strategy:**
- Choose a specific niche and establish authority in that area
- Define blog tone (formal/professional or casual/conversational)
- Develop a content calendar for consistency
- Build community around shared interests

**Content:**
- Write attention-grabbing, relevant headlines
- Keep posts concise and scannable using headings, bullet points, short paragraphs
- Incorporate SEO keywords naturally
- Use optimized images (high-quality, web-optimized)

**Promotion:**
- Share posts on social media, LinkedIn, and relevant platforms
- Include blog link in email signatures and website
- Engage with readers in comments

**Benefits for freelancers:**
- Establish thought leadership and expertise
- Attract potential clients searching for related information
- Build trust through experience sharing
- Improve website SEO and search visibility
- Create community engagement

**File size guidelines (Wix storage 10GB context):**
- Typical webpage: 2-5MB
- High-resolution image: 5-10MB
- Short video (1-2 min): 100-500MB
- Large PDF: 10-50MB

Can use external services (YouTube for video, Google Drive for files) to conserve storage space.
