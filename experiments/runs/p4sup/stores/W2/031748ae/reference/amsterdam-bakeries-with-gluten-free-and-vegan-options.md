Recommended bakeries in Amsterdam catering to dietary preferences:

**Gluten-free & Vegan:**
- Sharp Sharp — vegan and gluten-free bakery offering cakes, energy bars, sweet treats
- Bakken met Passie — gluten-free and vegan bread and pastries
- Koffie ende Koeck — plant-based bakery with vegan cakes and pastries

**General favorites:**
- Van Stapele Koekmakerij — known for chocolate cookie with melted white chocolate filling
- Lanskroon — traditional Dutch oliebollen and poffertjes, stroopwafels
- Patisserie Holtkamp — French patisserie, cakes, croissants, macarons
