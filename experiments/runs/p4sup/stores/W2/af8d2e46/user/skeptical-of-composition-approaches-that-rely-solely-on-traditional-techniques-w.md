Interested in understanding classical music and composition, but questions whether composers should be limited to traditional techniques and structures. Concerned that traditional approaches alone can become repetitive and boring for audiences. Values both pushing boundaries and building on tradition, but leans toward wanting innovation and new possibilities explored alongside or within traditional frameworks.

Context: Discussion about Brahms' embrace of traditional techniques sparked reflection on whether tradition-only approaches limit artistic expression and audience engagement.
