Interested in journaling as a way to: process emotions, identify patterns and triggers, track progress, and practice mindfulness. Writes without focusing on perfection - it's about expressing thoughts and feelings honestly. Uses journal prompts like: What am I grateful for today? What's been challenging? What self-care do I want to prioritize? What are my goals and intentions? Also practices daily gratitude journaling by writing three things grateful for each day to help shift focus toward the positive.
