Decision: Build a skincare routine combining products for both efficacy and affordability.

Plan:
1. Purchase Dr. Jart+ Dermaclar Clearing Solution Set (65 dollars) as the core routine. This set includes cleanser, toner, essence, serum, and moisturizer, focusing on acne control and brightening with niacinamide and salicylic acid.
2. Add Cetaphil Moisturizing Cream (15 dollars) as an additional moisturizer to ensure sufficient hydration, especially if skin tends toward dryness.
3. Consider purchasing a facial steamer (Neutrogena at 25 dollars or Dr. Jart+ at 40 dollars) to enhance product absorption by opening pores before treatment.

Rationale: Sister's success with expensive La Mer set motivated finding quality alternatives at lower cost. Dr. Jart+ targets acne-prone skin with proven ingredients, while Cetaphil adds hydration layer. Steamer enhances the overall routine effectiveness.
