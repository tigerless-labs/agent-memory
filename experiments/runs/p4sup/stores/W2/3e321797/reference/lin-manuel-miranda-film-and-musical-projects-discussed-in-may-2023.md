**tick, tick... BOOM! (Netflix Film)**
- Director: Lin-Manuel Miranda (directorial debut)
- Original release date discussed: November 12, 2021
- Cast: Andrew Garfield as Jonathan Larson, Alexandra Shipp as Susan, Robin de Jesús as Michael, Vanessa Hudgens as Karessa, Joshua Henry as Roger, MJ Rodriguez as Carolyn, Ben Ross as Freddy, Judith Light as Clara
- Based on Jonathan Larson's semi-autobiographical rock monologue
- Explores themes of creativity, ambition, and pressure to succeed

**Encanto (Disney Animated Film)**
- Release year discussed: 2023
- Composer: Lin-Manuel Miranda
- Story: Magical family in Colombia
- Expected to blend Lin-Manuel Miranda's style with Disney storytelling and Latinx culture

**Freestyle Love Supreme Projects**
- Documentary: 'We Are Freestyle Love Supreme' (2020, Hulu)
- PBS Special: 'Freestyle Love Supreme: The Musical' (2020)
- Improvisational hip-hop group featuring Lin-Manuel Miranda
- Broadway run in 2019
