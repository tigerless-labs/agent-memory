**Spinach and Tomato Omelette**

Ingredients:
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup diced onion
- 1/4 cup diced tomato
- 1/2 cup chopped spinach
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions:
1. Whisk eggs and season with salt and pepper
2. Heat olive oil in non-stick pan over medium heat
3. Add diced onion and sauté 2 minutes until softened
4. Add chopped spinach and sauté 2 minutes until wilted
5. Pour whisked eggs over the mixture, cook 1-2 minutes until edges set
6. Add diced tomato on top and continue cooking until eggs set
7. Fold in half and serve hot

Quick, simple meal combining all three ingredients.
