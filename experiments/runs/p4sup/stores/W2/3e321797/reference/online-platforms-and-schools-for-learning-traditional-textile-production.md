Platforms and schools recommended for online textile workshops and classes:

- **Slow Fiber Studios** — Classes on traditional weaving, natural dyeing, Japanese shibori for all skill levels
- **Maiwa School of Textiles** (Vancouver, Canada) — Online classes on natural dyeing, block printing, textile design taught by artisans worldwide
- **Fibershed** (nonprofit) — Classes on spinning, weaving, natural dyeing focusing on sustainable and regenerative practices
- **Udemy** — Wide range of textile classes from traditional techniques to digital design
- **Skillshare** — Classes on natural dyeing, embroidery, quilting for all skill levels

Also noted: UNESCO recognizes several traditional textile techniques as Intangible Cultural Heritage. Organizations like the Ethical Fashion Initiative and Mayan Women's Weaving Cooperative support traditional textile producers.
