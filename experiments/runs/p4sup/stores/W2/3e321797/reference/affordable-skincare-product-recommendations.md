Budget-friendly skincare sets and products recommended as alternatives to La Mer luxury skincare:

SETS:
Dr. Jart+ Dermaclar Clearing Solution Set, price 65: Cleanser, toner, essence, serum, moisturizer with niacinamide and salicylic acid. Best for acne-prone and oily skin.
Neutrogena Hydrating Facial Skincare Set, price 40: Includes cleanser, moisturizer, serum with hyaluronic acid and vitamin E.
The Ordinary Skincare Set, price 25-40: Mix-and-match individual products.

MOISTURIZERS:
Cetaphil Moisturizing Cream, price 15: Non-comedogenic, fragrance-free
Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel, price 17: Lightweight, oil-free
Laneige Water Bank Moisturizing Cream, price 35: 24-hour hydration
Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cream, price 38: Non-greasy, anti-wrinkle
Olay Regenerist Micro-Sculpting Cream, price 25: Plumping formula

FACIAL STEAMERS:
Dr. Jart+ Dermaclar Facial Steamer, price 40: Multiple steam settings
Neutrogena Facial Steamer, price 25: Includes timer
Conair Facial Steamer, price 30: Multiple settings, compact

Tips: Use steamer 1-2 times weekly for 5-10 minutes, follow with toner and moisturizer.
