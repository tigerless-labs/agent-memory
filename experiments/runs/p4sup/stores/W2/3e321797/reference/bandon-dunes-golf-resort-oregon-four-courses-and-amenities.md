**Bandon Dunes Golf Resort** is located on the Oregon coast, about 90 miles north of the California border.

**Four 18-hole courses:**
- **Bandon Dunes** (David McLay Kidd, 1999) — links-style with ocean views
- **Pacific Dunes** (Tom Doak, 2001) — dramatic elevation changes, rugged coastline
- **Old Macdonald** (Tom Doak & Jim Urbina, 2010) — inspired by classic Scottish courses, retro-style design
- **Sheep Ranch** (Coore & Crenshaw, 2020) — rugged natural design with ocean views

**Amenities:** Luxury accommodations, driving range, putting green, short-game area, restaurants (McKee's Pub, Gallery Restaurant), shuttle service.

**Activities:** Golf lessons, clinics, junior programs, hiking, beachcombing, scenic tours.

**Tips:** Book accommodations well in advance (peak season June–September). Prepare for coastal weather with layers. Multiple tee boxes available (4,275–6,944 yards).
