Planning to establish a consistent journaling practice to reflect on day, week, and month. Key strategies for building the habit: set specific frequency (daily/weekly/monthly), designate quiet space, select a journal that feels right, establish a routine (e.g., after waking or before bed), start small with short entries, make it a ritual (tea, candle, music), stay flexible about missed days, use guided prompts (gratitude, learnings, challenges), write without editing, periodically review entries, connect journaling to existing habits, and consider digital or analog options (apps: Evernote, Day One, or physical journal).
