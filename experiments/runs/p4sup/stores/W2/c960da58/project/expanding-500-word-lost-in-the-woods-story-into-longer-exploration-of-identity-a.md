**Writing Challenge Prompt:** 'Lost in the woods'

**Initial Work:** Wrote a 500-word story for the challenge

**Expansion Plan:** Exploring the prompt on multiple levels—not just physical being lost, but emotional and mental struggles

**Character Development:** Creating a character struggling to find their way in life, with the woods serving as a symbol of their inner journey. 

**Thematic Focus:** 
- Identity and purpose after major life change
- Resilience and self-discovery
- The connection between external journey (woods) and internal journey (emotional/mental state)
- Personal growth and finding one's way

Drawing personal inspiration from own experiences with major life change and uncertainty.
