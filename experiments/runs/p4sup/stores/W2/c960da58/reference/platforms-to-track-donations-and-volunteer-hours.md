Software and services for tracking charitable activities:\n\n- **GivingGrid**: track donations, volunteer hours, fundraising efforts; discover new charities\n- **DonorPerfect**: donor management software; track donations, receipts, charitable activities; set reminders for deadlines\n- **Network for Good**: donate to charities, track donations, receive receipts and tax deductions\n- **VolunteerMatch**: connect with nonprofits; track volunteer hours, log activities, earn badges\n- **Bright Funds**: donate to multiple charities through single platform; track donations; earn rewards and badges\n- **Charity Navigator's Giving Basket**: track donations, set up recurring gifts, receive receipts and tax deductions
