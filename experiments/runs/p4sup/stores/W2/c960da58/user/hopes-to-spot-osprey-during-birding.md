Still hoping to see an Osprey someday. In Illinois, Osprey typically arrive in late March or early April and stay until September or October. Best locations to spot them near large bodies of water: Lake Michigan, Illinois River, Starved Rock State Park, Emiquon National Wildlife Refuge.
