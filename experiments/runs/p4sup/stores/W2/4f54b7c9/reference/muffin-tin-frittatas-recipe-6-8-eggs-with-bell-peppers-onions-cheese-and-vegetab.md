**Basic Muffin Tin Frittatas Recipe**

**Ingredients:**
- 6-8 eggs
- 1/2 cup diced vegetables (user preference: diced bell peppers and onions)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or feta)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cooking spray or oil for muffin tin

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Whisk eggs with salt
3. Add diced vegetables and cheese, mix well
4. Spray 6-cup muffin tin with cooking spray or brush with oil
5. Divide egg mixture evenly among muffin cups
6. Bake 10-12 minutes until edges are golden and centers are set
7. Cool for a few minutes before serving

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate up to 3 days
- Freeze up to 2 months

**Reheating Options:**
- Microwave: 20-30 seconds (or 10-15 more if needed)
- Toaster oven: 350°F for 5-7 minutes
- Conventional oven: 350°F for 10-12 minutes
- Pan with foil: medium heat, 2-3 minutes per side
- Tip: Add water or milk while reheating to keep moist

**Variations:**
- Add diced ham, bacon, or sausage
- Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, chives)
- Different cheeses (feta, goat cheese, Parmesan)
- Dried oregano, thyme, or paprika

**Yield:** 6 individual frittatas (easily doubles or triples)

**Recommended vegetables:** Bell peppers (any color, user likes red/yellow for sweetness), onions, mushrooms, spinach, cherry tomatoes, zucchini, broccoli, asparagus, carrots
