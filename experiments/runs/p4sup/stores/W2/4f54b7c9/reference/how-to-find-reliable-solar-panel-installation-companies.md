Methods for researching solar installers:

1. **Personal referrals**: Ask friends, family, neighbors who've installed panels
2. **Online reviews**: Check customer feedback on review sites
3. **Industry associations**: Solar Energy Industries Association (SEIA) has accredited installer lists
4. **Search**: Google local providers
5. **Compare quotes**: Get multiple bids and compare:
   - Installation cost
   - Warranty terms
   - Associated fees

Ask questions and review ratings before committing.
