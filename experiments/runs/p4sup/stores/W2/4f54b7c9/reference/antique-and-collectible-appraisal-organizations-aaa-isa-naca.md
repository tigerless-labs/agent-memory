**Organizations that certify appraisers for antiques and collectibles:**

- **AAA** (Appraisers Association of America) — Professional organization certifying appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles
- **ISA** (International Society of Appraisers) — Non-profit certifying appraisers in antiques, art, and collectibles
- **NACA** (National Association of Collectible Appraisers) — Specializes in collectibles including antiques, toys, and memorabilia

**For specialized items:**
- **Antique music box** — Look for appraisers with experience in antique mechanical devices or musical instruments
- **Depression-era glassware** — Look for appraisers specializing in antique glassware or ceramics

**Value factors for antiques and collectibles:**
- Rarity and uniqueness
- Historical significance
- Condition and any repairs/maintenance
- Provenance (history of ownership)
- Current market demand
- Comparable sales of similar pieces

**Tips for finding qualified appraisers:**
- Check certification from AAA, ISA, or NACA
- Verify experience with items similar to yours
- Check professional affiliations and insurance industry recognition
- Get referrals from trusted sources
- Research reputation online
