Teaching digital literacy course to 6th-grade elementary school students. Session 10 focuses on **Evaluating Online Information**, covering:

- Introduction to evaluating credibility and reliability
- CRAAP Test methodology (Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose)
- Research activity: students evaluate sources on assigned topics using the CRAAP Test
- Reflection and discussion: class discussion on tips for verifying source credibility
- Assessment via worksheets and student presentations

Curriculum also includes earlier sessions (at least through session 4) with reflection and discussion components requiring active learning strategies.
