Basil plant on the balcony is struggling with too much direct sunlight. Currently using general-purpose potting mix. Considering moving to a shadier spot with filtered/indirect sunlight. Ideal conditions: partial shade, soil pH 6.0-7.0, well-draining mix with organic matter. Prefers watering when soil feels dry to touch.
