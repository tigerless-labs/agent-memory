Planning a trip to the Japanese Alps/Nagano prefecture in November 2023. Will base accommodations in Matsumoto rather than Hakuba Valley because interested in traditional Japanese culture and history, and closer to Japanese Alps Visitor Center. Friends from Tokyo may join for a few days.

**Planned itinerary:**
- Japanese Alps Visitor Center
- Matsumoto Castle (National Treasure - priority visit)
- Nawate Street (traditional shops and tea houses)
- Matsumoto Folkcraft Museum
- Zenko-ji Temple (if time permits)
- Onsen visits (considering Asama Onsen, Utsukushigahara Onsen, or Hirata Onsen)

**Travel logistics:**
- Friends in Tokyo can reach Nagano via Hokuriku Shinkansen (1h 45min from Tokyo Station)
- Will explore public transportation rather than car rental

**Preferences for trip:**
- Values traditional architecture, history, cultural experience
- Not interested in winter sports activities despite November timing
