Eight day road trip route from London to Nice via continental ferries. Outbound: April 3 London to Harwich, April 4 Hook of Holland to Amsterdam, April 5 Amsterdam to Luxembourg City, April 6 Luxembourg to Lucerne, April 7 Lucerne to Lake Como, April 8 Lake Como to Nice. Return: April 11 Nice to Lyon, April 12 Lyon to Reims, April 13 Reims to Calais, April 14 Calais to London via Eurotunnel. Total distance 2059 miles.
