Comic Relief adapted its fundraising and operational strategy during the pandemic through several key approaches:

**Strategy shifts:**
- Pivoted from traditional in-person events to digital fundraising campaigns
- Prioritized virtual engagement and online donor accessibility
- Reevaluated long-term strategy to focus on supporting disadvantaged communities

**Specific tactics for successful virtual events:**
- Leveraged social media platforms for awareness and engagement
- Partnered with celebrities and influencers to attract broader audiences
- Produced high-quality, shareable content (videos, social media posts, live streams)
- Encouraged user-generated content from supporters using hashtags
- Made online donations seamless through multiple digital payment systems
- Maintained regular communication with supporters about campaign progress and impact

**Key insight:** The pandemic forced the organization to become more agile and flexible, with a emphasis on long-term sustainability. Their willingness to embrace technology and experiment with new digital strategies served as an example for other charities facing operational disruptions.

**Broader lesson:** Charities that remained flexible and adaptive during the pandemic were able to continue fundraising and supporting their mission despite traditional channels being disrupted.
