Rearranging office space to take better advantage of natural light. Key preferences: desk positioned near window but with light from side/angle to avoid glare and screen reflections; minimize obstructions in front of windows; use mirrors to reflect and bounce light deeper into room; light color scheme for walls/floors/furniture to amplify light; consider task-oriented zones to use different natural light levels effectively.
