4-email automated welcome series to introduce new email subscribers to Verde Fashion Co. and build initial engagement.

## Email 1: Introduction and Brand Story
- **Timing**: Sent immediately after subscription
- **Subject**: Welcome to Verde Fashion Co.!
- **Content**:
  - Brief introduction to brand mission (making sustainable fashion accessible and stylish)
  - Personal founder story about grandmother's dress and commitment to sustainability
  - Warm welcome message expressing excitement for subscriber
  - CTA: Explore website and social media channels

## Email 2: Our Sustainable Approach
- **Timing**: 3-4 days after Email 1
- **Subject**: Our Sustainable Approach to Fashion
- **Content**:
  - Highlight sustainable practices (eco-friendly materials, waste reduction, fair labor)
  - Share commitment to supply chain transparency and accountability
  - Introduce sustainable fabric options (organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel)
  - CTA: Learn more about sustainable materials and practices on website

## Email 3: Featured Products and Collections
- **Timing**: 7-10 days after Email 2
- **Subject**: Explore Our Sustainable Fashion Collections
- **Content**:
  - Showcase 2-3 best-selling or newest products with sustainable features highlighted
  - Introduce product categories (tops, dresses, outerwear)
  - Include customer testimonials and reviews for social proof
  - CTA: Shop website and explore collections

## Email 4: Exclusive Offer and Community Invitation
- **Timing**: 10-14 days after Email 3
- **Subject**: Join Our Sustainable Fashion Community
- **Content**:
  - Exclusive discount for first purchase (e.g., 15% off)
  - Invitation to join social media communities (Instagram, Facebook)
  - Sneak peek of upcoming products, collections, or collaborations
  - CTA: Shop with exclusive discount and follow social media

## Key Principles
- Keep each email concise and visually appealing
- Focus on one main topic per email
- Track open rates, click-through rates, conversion rates to optimize
