Verde Fashion Co. is a sustainable fashion brand founded to merge passion for fashion with commitment to sustainability.

## Current Status
- Website launched a few weeks ago (as of May 2023)
- First few email subscribers acquired
- Building engaged community

## Brand Mission & Story
Founder started Verde Fashion Co. after finding a handmade dress in grandmother's trunk, which sparked reflection on fashion industry's environmental impact. Committed to countering fast fashion with high-quality, sustainable pieces that are timeless and crafted with care.

## Sustainable Practices
- Uses eco-friendly materials: organic cotton, recycled polyester, Tencel
- Reduces waste in production through pattern-making optimization and fabric scrap recycling
- Emphasizes durability and timelessness to encourage buying less and wearing more
- Committed to supply chain transparency and accountability
- Promotes slow fashion approach

## Product Categories
- Tops
- Dresses
- Outerwear
