Social media strategy for Verde Fashion Co. using Instagram and Facebook to showcase sustainability commitment and connect with audience.

## Instagram Strategy
**Focus**: Visual-centric platform ideal for fashion brand

### Content Types:
- **Sustainability Series**: Share sustainable practices with engaging visuals, infographics, videos
- **Behind-the-Scenes (BTS)**: Design process, material sourcing, production process
- **Customer Showcase**: Encourage customers to share photos wearing Verde pieces (incentivize with discounts/free merchandise)
- **Educational Tips**: Tips on sustainable fashion practices, care instructions, styling sustainable clothes
- **Stories & IGTV**: Sneak peeks, exclusive deals, interactive experiences

### Best Practices:
- High-quality, well-lit, visually appealing photos/videos
- Consistent aesthetic to build brand identity
- Research and use mix of popular + niche relevant hashtags
- Write engaging, story-driven captions that provide value
- Respond to comments and DMs promptly
- Use polls and quizzes to encourage engagement
- Collaborate with influencers aligned with brand values
- Post consistently to maintain engagement

## Facebook Strategy
**Focus**: Long-form content and community building

### Content Types:
- **Blog Posts**: In-depth articles about sustainability, fashion, brand updates
- **Events**: Promote product launches, webinars, workshops, online events
- **Promotions**: Sales announcements, special offers, limited-time deals
- **Facebook Live**: Real-time Q&A sessions, behind-the-scenes content, live events
- **Facebook Groups**: Create or join communities to connect with audience and encourage discussions

### Best Practices:
- Use targeted ads to reach specific audiences by demographics/interests/behaviors
- Optimize all content for mobile viewing
- Share longer-form, in-depth content
- Use Facebook Insights to track performance and adjust strategy
- Cross-promote with Instagram content

## Cross-Platform Strategy
- Share Instagram posts to Facebook to reach broader audience
- Use Facebook-exclusive content to drive traffic to Instagram
- Consistent messaging across both platforms
- Track analytics on both to optimize content strategy

## Implementation Focus
- Start with both platforms simultaneously
- Track engagement, reach, conversion metrics
- Adjust content strategy based on audience response
- Build community around sustainable fashion mission
