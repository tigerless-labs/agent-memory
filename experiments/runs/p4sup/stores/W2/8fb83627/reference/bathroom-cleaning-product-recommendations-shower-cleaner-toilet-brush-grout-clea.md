Shower Cleaners: Scrubbing Bubbles, Lysol, Clorox, Seventh Generation (natural), Ecover (natural), Tilex Mold & Mildew Remover. Toilet Brushes: Clorox, Scotch-Brite, OXO Good Grips, long-handled (Clorox/Libman), angled (Mr. Clean/Casabella), Seventh Generation (eco). Grout Cleaners: Lysol, OxiClean, Scrubbing Bubbles, CLR (acid-based), Seventh Generation (natural), Ecover (natural).
