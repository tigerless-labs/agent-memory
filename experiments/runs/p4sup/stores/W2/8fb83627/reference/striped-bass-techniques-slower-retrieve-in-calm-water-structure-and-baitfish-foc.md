**Effective striped bass techniques on Lake Norman:**\n- Slower, more deliberate retrieve when water is calm\n- Focus on structure (submerged humps, points, drop-offs)\n- Look for areas with baitfish activity\n- Temperature preferences: 50-75°F optimal, 60-70°F ideal\n- Fish during dawn, dusk, night (especially summer)\n- Medium to heavy rod recommended for their fighting ability
