Common team-building activities recommended for strengthening relationships and improving morale:\n\n1. **Escape rooms** - Problem-solving under pressure; teamwork to achieve common goal\n2. **Volunteer activities** - Meaningful connection while giving back (soup kitchen, charity fundraiser)\n3. **Scavenger hunts** - Themed hunts encourage teamwork, problem-solving, creativity\n4. **Group cooking classes** - Participants work together to plan, prepare, and enjoy meal together\n5. **Sports activities** - Bowling, mini-golf, softball; friendly competition and relationship building\n\n**Key principle**: Organize based on team member interests; ensure all feel included and valued.
