## Recommended for bottle-fed baby goats (Luna and Leo's age):

**Height:** Minimum 4 feet, ideally 5+ feet — baby goats are escape artists and excellent climbers

**Material options:**
- **Woven wire fencing:** 2-inch x 4-inch mesh minimum (smaller mesh like 1-inch x 2-inch preferred for safety)
- Use 12.5 gauge wire minimum
- Difficult for goats to get heads/horns caught

**Posts:** Sturdy posts spaced 8-10 feet apart; corner posts with anchor system to prevent sagging

**Gates:** Sturdy, secure, easy to operate; consider self-closing gates

**Visibility:** Add flags or strips so goats see boundaries

**NOT recommended for bottle-fed baby goats:**
- Electric fencing — babies don't understand shock association, causes stress, unsuitable when bottle-feeding and already vulnerable
- Can introduce electric fencing at 3-4 months when kids understand the connection better

**Additional considerations:**
- Provide multiple feeding/water stations to reduce stress
- Ensure adequate shelter
- Supervise when introducing to new enclosure
