Scheduling veterinary appointment for Luna and Leo in April 2023 for vaccinations (CDT, Pasteurella multocida, and any regional vaccines recommended by vet).

Preparation tips: Acclimate goats to handling weeks before visit, get them comfortable with carriers, practice halter breaking, practice weighing them, consider mock vet visit with a friend, fast them 12-24 hours before visit, bring favorite treats and hay, stay calm during visit.
