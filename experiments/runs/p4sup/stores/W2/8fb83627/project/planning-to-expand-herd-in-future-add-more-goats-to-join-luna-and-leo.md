Plans to introduce additional goats to the existing herd (Luna and Leo) in the future.

Introduction process: Use separate quarantine area for new goats; ensure they are healthy and up-to-date on vaccinations; start with visual introductions over 2-3 days; gradually increase interaction in neutral areas; provide multiple feeding stations and water sources.

Best practices: Introduce goats of similar age, size, and temperament; avoid introducing during stressful times; always supervise interactions; be patient as process can take time.
