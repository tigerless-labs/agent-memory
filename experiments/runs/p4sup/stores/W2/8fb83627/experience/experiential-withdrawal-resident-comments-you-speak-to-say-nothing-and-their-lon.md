Resident comments such as 'you speak to say nothing' have profound direct impacts on mood and work capacity. The experience involves:

- **Immediate effects**: Becoming entangled in self-related thought processes, disconnection from shared experience
- **Somatic dimension**: Situational impacts on interoceptive abilities
- **Motivational erosion**: Affects drive to help and empathy capacity
- **Phenomenological state**: 'Experiential withdrawal' - a state that can be mentalized and sometimes verbalized
- **Temporal mystery**: Situational acknowledgment is possible in-the-moment, but long-term emotional effects remain unclear

This connects to growing fear of not finding words to connect with residents, creating a cyclical dynamic where the lack of formal training and community positioning legitimizes these fears.
