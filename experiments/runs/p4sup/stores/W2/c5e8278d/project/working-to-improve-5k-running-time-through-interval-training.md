Goal: Improve your 5K running time.

Recommended interval training workouts to integrate into your routine:
- Fartlek Intervals: 1-2 min hard (5K pace) alternating with 2-3 min easy recovery
- 400m repeats: 400m fast with 200m jog recovery, 3-5 sets
- Hill repeats: Run up gradual incline at high intensity, walk/jog down, 3-5 sets
- 800m repeats: 800m fast with 400m jog recovery, 2-3 sets
- Pyramid intervals: 200m, 400m, 600m, 400m, 200m with recovery between each
- Proportional intervals: 1 min hard / 2 min easy, then 2 min / 4 min, then 3 min / 6 min

Start with 1-2 interval sessions per week, gradually increasing frequency. Always warm up 10-15 min and cool down 10-15 min.
