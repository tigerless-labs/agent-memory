Complete cool-down takes 15-30 minutes after cycling:

Easy Pedaling (5-10 min):
- Gradually bring heart rate and breathing back to normal
- Spin in low gear to flush waste products and promote blood flow

Static Stretching (10-15 min):
Hold each stretch for 15-30 seconds, breathe deeply:
- Quad stretch: stand with hand on wall, lift leg behind you
- Hamstring stretch: sit with legs extended, lean forward toward toes
- Hip flexor stretch: kneel on all fours, bring one knee forward, lean
- Calf stretch: stand facing wall, step one foot back with heel on ground

Foam Rolling (5-10 min):
- Focus on tight areas: IT band, quadriceps, hamstrings
- Roll slowly and smoothly with moderate pressure

Hydration and Refueling (10-15 min):
- Drink water or sports drink to replenish fluids and electrolytes
- Consume balanced snack or meal with carbs and protein within 30-60 minutes
- Examples: chocolate milk, banana with peanut butter, energy bar

Additional recovery tips:
- Warm bath or shower to relax muscles and promote recovery
- Compression garments to improve blood flow if available
- Massage or recovery tools (NormaTec, Hyperice) if available
- Prioritize rest and recovery time to allow body to repair
