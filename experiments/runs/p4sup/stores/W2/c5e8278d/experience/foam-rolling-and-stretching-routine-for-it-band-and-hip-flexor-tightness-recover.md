Foam Rolling Exercises (30-60 seconds each, 2-3 times):
- IT Band Roll: Start above knee, roll toward ankle with moderate pressure
- Lateral Leg Roll: Roll from ankle toward knee on outside of leg  
- Hip Flexor Roll: Position foam roller under front of hip, roll forward/backward
- Piriformis Roll: Sit on roller with affected leg crossed, roll under glutes

Stretches (hold 30 seconds, repeat 2-3 times per side):
- IT Band Stretch: Stand with affected side near wall, cross opposite leg over, lean toward wall
- Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch: Kneel on all fours, bring one knee forward, lean to stretch front of hip
- Lateral Leg Stretch: Wide stance, step to one side, lower body while keeping back straight
- Piriformis Stretch: Sit with affected leg crossed over other leg, pull knee toward opposite shoulder
- Calf Stretch: Stand facing wall, step one foot back with heel on ground, bend front knee

Tips:
- Start with light pressure, gradually increase as tolerated
- Never bounce or force stretches
- Do not push through sharp pain
- Consistent practice is key for lasting improvement
