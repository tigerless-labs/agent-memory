Diatonic harmonicas: Hohner Special 20 (classic, affordable, versatile), Lee Oskar (rich tone, easy playability), Suzuki. Chromatic harmonicas: Hohner Super Chromonica, Suzuki Chromatic, Huang Chromatic. Other brands: Seydel (German, handcrafted), Bushman (US-based). Beginner price range: $20-$50. For beginners, focus on diatonic harmonicas in a key matching the song.
