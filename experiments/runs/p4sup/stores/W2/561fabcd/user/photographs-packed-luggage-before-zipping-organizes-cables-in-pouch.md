Organizes travel packing with a photo-first system:
- Takes a clear, well-lit photo of packed luggage before zipping it up to quickly remember what's packed and where it is in the suitcase
- Packs all chargers and cables together in a single small dedicated pouch for easy access when needed
- Plans to extend this to emergency/medical bags (first-aid kit): photographs contents so can quickly identify what's inside without digging through

Finds this system saves time finding items and reduces stress when in a hurry.
