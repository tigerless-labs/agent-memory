Sources for environmental engineering and related datasets:

1. **NOAA** - climate, weather, and oceanography datasets
2. **EPA** - air and water quality, hazardous waste, and other environmental topics
3. **USGS** - water resources, geology, and natural hazards datasets
4. **Data.gov** - government-run website with datasets from various federal agencies, including environmental data
5. **World Bank** - environmental and development datasets (water, sanitation, air pollution, climate change)
6. **Kaggle** - various datasets including environmental engineering and science
