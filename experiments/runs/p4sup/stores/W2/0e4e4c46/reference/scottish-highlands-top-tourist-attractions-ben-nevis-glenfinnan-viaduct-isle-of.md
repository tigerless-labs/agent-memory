## Top Scottish Highlands attractions

1. **Ben Nevis** — UK's highest mountain at 1,345m; sprawling Highlands views
2. **Glenfinnan Viaduct** — Harry Potter filming location; picturesque valley views
3. **Isle of Skye** — Picturesque island with stunning Highlands views
4. **Loch Ness** — Surrounded by forests and rolling hills; scenic views
5. **Glencoe** — Dramatic landscape; hiking trails for all levels
6. **Cairngorms** — UK's largest National Park (1,748 sq mi); extensive scenery
7. **The Three Sisters** — Three mountains in Glen Coe with lake views
8. **Bealach na Bà** — Mountain pass with spectacular views; challenging drive
9. **Scottish Coastline** — White-sand beaches, hidden coves, rugged cliffs
10. **Drumossie Moor** — Moorland near Inverness; accessible Highlands views
