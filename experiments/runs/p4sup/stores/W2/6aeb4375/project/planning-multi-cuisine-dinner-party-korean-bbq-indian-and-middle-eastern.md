Menu planning for a dinner party combining three cuisines:

**Korean BBQ dishes:**
- Bulgogi (marinated beef)
- Galbi (short ribs)
- Dak Galbi (spicy chicken)
- Pork Belly BBQ
- Japchae (glass noodles)
- Kimchi Jjigae (stew)

**Indian dishes:**
- Chana Masala
- Palak Paneer
- Samosas
- Tandoori Chicken
- Vegetable Biryani
- Rogan Josh
- Sag Aloo

**Middle Eastern dishes:**
- Falafel (special focus on texture)
- Shawarma
- Hummus
- Tabbouleh

Strategy: Set up DIY BBQ station, prepare dishes in advance, offer variety of banchan (side dishes), pair with Korean beers.
