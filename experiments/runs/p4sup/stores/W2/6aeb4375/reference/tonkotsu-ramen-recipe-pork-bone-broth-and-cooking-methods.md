Rich pork bone broth ramen. Roast 2 lbs pork bones (or neck/rib bones): 30 min at 400°F, then 1-2 hours at 350°F. Simmer with 4 cups chicken broth, 2 cups water, 2 tbsp sake, 2 tbsp mirin, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp white pepper for 4-5 hours (or overnight). Strain. Alternative methods: slow cooker 8-10 hours low, or Instant Pot 30-40 min high pressure with natural release. Serve over noodles with pork belly, boiled egg, green onions.
