Napoleon Hill's *Think and Grow Rich* (published 1937). User requested summary tailored for Gen Z high school students.

**Key points covered:**
- Positive mindset and clear goals help overcome obstacles and achieve dreams
- Importance of taking action and persisting through challenges
- Power of visualization and law of attraction to attract success into one's life

Author emphasizes both mindset work and concrete action as complementary forces.
