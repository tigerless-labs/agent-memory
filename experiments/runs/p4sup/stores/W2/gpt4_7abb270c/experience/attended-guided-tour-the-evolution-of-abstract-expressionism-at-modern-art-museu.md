Attended a guided tour at the Modern Art Museum led by Dr. Patel focusing on 'The Evolution of Abstract Expressionism.' The tour provided enlightening insights into Jackson Pollock and Mark Rothko's work, helping develop a deeper appreciation for how abstract art conveys emotion and meaning. Also saw a large-scale installation by Anselm Kiefer that was particularly moving.
