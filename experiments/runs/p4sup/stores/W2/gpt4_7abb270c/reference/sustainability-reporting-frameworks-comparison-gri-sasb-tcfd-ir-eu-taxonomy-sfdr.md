## Sustainability Reporting Frameworks Overview

| Framework | Regulatory Body | Focus | Applicability | Required Format |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| GRI Standards | Global Reporting Initiative | Sustainability | Voluntary | None, but recommends use of GRI Standards for comprehensive reporting |
| SASB Standards | Sustainability Accounting Standards Board | Sustainability | Voluntary | None, but recommends use of SASB Standards for industry-specific reporting |
| TCFD Recommendations | Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures | Climate-related risks and opportunities | Voluntary, but increasingly adopted by governments and investors | None, but recommends disclosure in mainstream financial filings |
| IR Framework | International Integrated Reporting Council | Integrated Reporting | Voluntary | None, but recommends integrated reporting framework for comprehensive reporting |
| EU Taxonomy Regulation | European Union | Climate-related risks and opportunities | Mandatory for companies subject to NFRD and SFDR | Must be aligned with EU Taxonomy using common classification system |
| SFDR | European Union | Sustainability of financial products | Mandatory for financial market participants and financial advisers | Requires disclosure in pre-contractual documents and periodic reports |
| CSRD | European Union | Sustainability reporting | Mandatory for all large companies operating in the EU | European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) with Inline XBRL (iXBRL) in annual reports |

## Key Details

**NFRD Applicability**: Applies to large EU public-interest entities with:
- More than 500 employees AND
- Balance sheet total >€20 million OR net turnover >€40 million
- Public-interest entities include: listed companies, credit institutions, insurance undertakings

**CSRD**: Proposed expansion of NFRD requirements
- Expected adoption: 2022
- Reporting required from: 2026 onwards
- Will extend mandatory reporting to all large EU companies (not just listed)
- Uses ESEF format with Inline XBRL for standardized reporting

**CDP Climate Disclosure**: Voluntary reporting framework
- Focused on climate-related risks and opportunities
- Aligned with TCFD recommendations
- Can be used alongside GRI, SASB, TCFD, and IR for comprehensive reporting

## Framework Interoperability

Frameworks can be used in conjunction with each other for more comprehensive reporting. EU regulators encourage use of internationally recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) to improve quality and comparability of non-financial reporting.
