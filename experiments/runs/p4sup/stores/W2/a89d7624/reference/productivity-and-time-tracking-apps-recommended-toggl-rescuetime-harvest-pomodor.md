Recommended apps and tools for productivity and focus:

**Time Tracking:**
- Toggl: Simple time tracking with task creation and reporting
- RescueTime: Automated tracking of computer/mobile time usage with insights
- Harvest: Time tracking and invoicing for understanding task time allocation

**Focus and Productivity:**
- Pomodoro Timer apps (Pomofocus, Tomato Timer): 25-min work + 5-min break cycles
- Freedom: Blocks distracting websites/apps during work sessions
- StayFocusd: Chrome extension to limit access to distracting sites

**Task Management:**
- Todoist: Task creation, deadlines, progress tracking
- Any.do: Daily planner, to-do lists, collaboration features

**Habit and Exercise Tracking:**
- Habitica: Gamified habit tracking for daily routine (including yoga)
- Streaks Workout: Fitness app to track exercise routine consistency
- HabitBull: Habit insights and consistency tracking

Particularly relevant for user's morning yoga and early morning productivity focus.
