**Budget allocation (50/30/20 rule):**
- Essential expenses: 50% (1,250 AUD from 2,500 scholarship)
- Non-essential expenses: 30% (750 AUD)
- Savings: 20% (500 AUD)

**Grocery and food cost-saving strategies:**
- Shop at affordable supermarkets (Aldi, Coles, Woolworths)
- Visit local markets: Queen Victoria Market, South Melbourne Market for fresh produce
- Plan weekly meals to avoid waste
- Buy non-perishables in bulk
- Use cashback apps (Cashrewards, Checkout 51)
- Avoid shopping when hungry
- Cook at home most nights
- Pack lunch instead of buying
- Explore affordable ethnic restaurants (Vietnamese, Chinese, Indian)

**Time management tools mentioned:**
- Digital: Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Microsoft Outlook
- Task management: Trello (user has previous experience), Asana, Todoist, Wunderlist
- Planners: Passion Planner, Erin Condren, Bullet Journal
- Time tracking: RescueTime, Toggl, Harvest

**Estimated food budget:** 500-700 AUD per month with smart shopping strategies
