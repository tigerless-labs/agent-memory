Influencer Discovery Platforms: AspireIQ, HYPR, Upfluence, Grin, IZEA, Crunchbase, Influencer Marketing Hub, Ahrefs, Moz, SEMrush, Google Search

Influencer Categories by Size:
- Nano-influencers (1,000–10,000 followers): High engagement, authentic, cost-effective
- Micro-influencers (10,000–100,000 followers): Balance of reach and engagement  
- Mid-tier (100,000–500,000 followers): Broader reach, affordable
- Macro-influencers (500,000+ followers): High reach, expensive

ROI Tracking Methods: Unique promo codes/affiliate links, Google Analytics, engagement metrics, surveys/polls, A/B testing

Attribution Models: Last-touch (final influencer), First-touch (initial introduction), Multi-touch (multiple influencers), Linear (equal credit)

Influencer Performance Tiers: Tier 1 (high-performing), Tier 2 (mid-performing), Tier 3 (low-performing)
