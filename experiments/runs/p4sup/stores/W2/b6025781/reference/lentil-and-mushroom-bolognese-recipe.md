A plant-based pasta sauce made with:
- 1 cup dried green or brown lentils
- 2 cups mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, button), sliced
- Aromatics: onion, garlic, carrot, celery
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- Herbs: dried basil, dried oregano
- Optional: 1/4 cup red wine
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Sauté aromatics (onion, garlic, carrot, celery) in olive oil until tender
2. Add mushrooms and cook until they release liquid and brown
3. Add lentils and spices, toast for 1-2 minutes
4. Add crushed tomatoes and optional red wine
5. Simmer covered for 20-25 minutes until lentils are tender
6. Adjust seasoning and serve over quinoa, pasta, or with roasted vegetables

**Cooking time:** 30-40 minutes for 4-6 servings

**Tips:** Can use different lentil varieties for complex flavor, add bay leaves or thyme, include tomato paste for richness, or add bell peppers and zucchini in the last 10 minutes of simmering.
