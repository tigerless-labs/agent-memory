Key books recommended for navigating spiritual crisis and integrating values of kindness and compassion:\n\n**On grief and faith crisis:**\n- *A Grief Observed* by C.S. Lewis\n- *The Dark Night of the Soul* by St. John of the Cross\n- *When Bad Things Happen to Good People* by Harold S. Kushner\n\n**On spirituality and personal growth:**\n- *The Power of Now* by Eckhart Tolle (mindfulness, living in present)\n- *The God Delusion* by Richard Dawkins (atheism perspective)\n- *The Undefended Heart* by Jett Psaris (spiritual growth)\n- *The Art of Possibility* by Rosamund Stone Zander and Benjamin Zander\n- *The Gifts of Imperfection* by Brené Brown\n\n**Podcasts:**\n- The RobCast (faith, doubt, spirituality with Rob Bell)\n- The Mindful Kind (mindfulness, self-care, spiritual growth)\n- The Happiness Lab with Dr. Laurie Santos\n- The Daily Boost (motivation and inspiration)
