**Non-fiction books recommended:**\n- 'The Guns of August' by Barbara W. Tuchman — Pulitzer Prize winner; detailed account of events leading up to WWI and the first month\n- 'A World Undone: The Story of the Great War, 1914-1918' by G.J. Meyer — comprehensive history; also available as audiobook narrated by Simon Vance\n- 'The First World War: A Complete History' by Martin Gilbert — thorough and accessible\n- 'Women in the War Zone: Hospital Service in France, 1914-1918' by Sharon Ouditt — focuses on women's experiences in WWI\n\n**Documentaries:**\n- 'The Great War' (PBS, 2017) — 6-part series; comprehensive history\n- 'World War I: The Centenary' (BBC, 2014) — 6-part series; explores causes, course, and consequences\n\n**Online resources:**\n- BBC World War I Centenary Website\n- National World War I Museum and Memorial\n- Imperial War Museums (UK)\n- Library of Congress WWI Collection
