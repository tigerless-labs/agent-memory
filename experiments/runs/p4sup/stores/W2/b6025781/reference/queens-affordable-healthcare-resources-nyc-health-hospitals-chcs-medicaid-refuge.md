**NYC Health + Hospitals**: (212) 788-4000 — Low-cost/free healthcare, locations include Elmhurst Hospital and Queens Hospital

**Community Health Centers (CHCs)**:
- Queens Community House: (718) 592-5757
- Joseph P. Addabbo Family Health Center: (718) 945-7150

**Medicaid**: Apply via New York State of Health Marketplace (nystateofhealth.ny.gov) or Queens Medicaid Office (718) 883-6666

**ACA Marketplace Plans**: nystateofhealth.ny.gov

**Free/Low-Cost Clinics**:
- Queens Free Clinic: (718) 670-1271
- Community Healthcare Network: (718) 943-8633

**Refugee-Specific Healthcare**:
- Refugee Health Alliance: (718) 445-0111
- NYC Refugee Health Clinic: (212) 562-4141
