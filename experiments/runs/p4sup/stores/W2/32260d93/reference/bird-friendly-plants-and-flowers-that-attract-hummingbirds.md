Tubular flowers: trumpet vine, honeysuckle, fuchsia, lantana, salvias, bee balm, cardinal flower, coral bells, coneflowers, black-eyed susans, zinnias. Hummingbirds prefer red, orange, purple flowers with nectar-rich content and long tube-shaped blooms. Most active 6-10 AM, 2-5 PM; lull 11 AM-1 PM. Eat 2x body weight daily. Winter plants: Holly, cotoneaster, winterberry holly, crabapple (berries); Pine, coneflower, black-eyed susan, sunflower (seeds); Camellia, mahonia, gaultheria, winter honeysuckle (nectar).
