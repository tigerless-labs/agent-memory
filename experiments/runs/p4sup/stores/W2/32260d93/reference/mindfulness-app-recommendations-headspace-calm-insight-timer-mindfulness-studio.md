Seven mindfulness apps recommended for complementing a podcast routine:

1. **Headspace** — Popular, user-friendly; free: Take10 (10-day intro program), Everyday Headspace (daily 3-5 min meditations), SOS sessions (3-5 min stress relief), limited Basics Pack. Premium: full meditation library, progress tracking, themed collections (stress, sleep, focus). Basics Pack includes Focus, Calm, Clarity, Motivation, and Rest meditations (10-20 min each).

2. **Calm** — Well-known for soothing atmosphere; free: 7 Days of Calm (intro program), Daily Calm (3-10 min daily meditation), limited sleep stories, limited music/nature sounds. Premium: full meditation library, sleep stories, music library.

3. **Insight Timer** — Free app with large meditation library and customizable timer; flexible approach to personal practice.

4. **Mindfulness Studio** — Variety of guided meditations for stress, anxiety, sleep; includes 'Mindful Moments' short daily exercises.

5. **Buddhify** — Unique approach with meditations tailored to specific times of day (waking, working, bedtime).

6. **10% Happier** — Caters to skeptics and beginners with humorous, approachable style; good for building practice gradually.

7. **Simple Habit** — Bite-sized 5-minute meditations for busy schedules.

Integration strategies: Start day with short meditation, use timer between podcast episodes, listen to guided meditations before bed, incorporate mindfulness during breaks or chores.
