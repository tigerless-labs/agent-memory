Recommended pizza chains with BBQ chicken pizza options:\n\n1. **California Pizza Kitchen (CPK)** - BBQ Chicken Pizza with grilled chicken, red onion, cilantro, BBQ sauce\n2. **Mellow Mushroom** - Holy Shiitake Pie with grilled chicken, shiitake mushrooms, BBQ sauce; also BBQ Chicken pizza\n3. **Papa Murphy's** - Take-and-bake model; BBQ Chicken pizza\n4. **Marco's Pizza** - Fresh never-frozen crust; BBQ Chicken and Chicken Bacon BBQ with caramelized onions\n5. **Jet's Pizza** - Square-cut crust; BBQ Chicken pizza\n6. **Pieology** - Fast-casual; customizable BBQ Chicken pizza\n7. **Local pizzerias** - Often have unique BBQ chicken variations\n\nAlso check local independent pizzerias for hidden gems with unique flavor profiles.
