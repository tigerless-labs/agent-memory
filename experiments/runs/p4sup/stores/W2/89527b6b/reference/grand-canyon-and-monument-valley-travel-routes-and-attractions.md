Recommended restaurants near Mather Campground: El Tovar Dining Room (upscale Southwestern), Bright Angel Restaurant (comfort food), Canyon Café (casual). Scenic stops: Lake Mead, Kingman (Route 66 Museum), Hackberry General Store, Williams (Williams Depot), Oak Creek Canyon. Desert View Watchtower: 70-foot Mary Colter tower with Native American art, 360-degree canyon views, best at sunset. Route to Monument Valley: Hwy 64 E to Hwy 89 N through Navajo Nation, 4-5 hours from Grand Canyon. Cameron Trading Post historic stop (1916). Hoover Dam worth 2-4 hours visit.
