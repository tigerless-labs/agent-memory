User is planning to get Rhode Island Red chickens and needs to build a predator-proof chicken coop. Coop should use hardware cloth (1 inch or smaller mesh), be buried 1-2 feet underground, have secure doors/windows with predator-proof latches, and roosting bars 2-3 feet off ground. Consider electric fencing around perimeter and covering the run to protect from flying predators.
