## Key Artists

- **Jason Heavy Runner** — Fashion designer incorporating traditional Blackfoot beadwork into contemporary fashion pieces (dresses, jackets, accessories with traditional designs and modern cuts/fabrics)
- **Adrian Stimson** — Creates paintings combining traditional Plains Indian ledger art with modern pop culture; example: "Parked Pow Wow Princess" depicts young woman in traditional regalia on pile of tires in parking lot
- **Lauren Crazybull** — Works across painting, embroidery, digital art; incorporates traditional Blackfoot motifs with contemporary urban inspiration; "Everything is a Ceremony" series explores spirituality and mindfulness in modern world

## Approach
Blackfoot artists successfully blend traditional and modern elements due to:
- Cultural history of adaptation and resilience
- Influence of both traditional teachings and contemporary world
- Creating art that is both relevant and rooted in cultural identity

## Where to See Blackfoot Art

- **Glenbow Museum** — Calgary, Canada; extensive collection of traditional and contemporary Blackfoot art (beadwork, paintings, sculptures)
- **Museum of Indigenous People** — Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA; features contemporary indigenous art
- **Blackfoot Crossing Historical Park** — Alberta, Canada; cultural center with exhibits on Blackfoot artistry and history
