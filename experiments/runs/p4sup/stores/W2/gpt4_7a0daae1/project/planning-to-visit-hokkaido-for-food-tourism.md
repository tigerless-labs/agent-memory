User wants to visit Hokkaido to explore the region's distinctive cuisine shaped by its northern climate, cold-water seafood, and dairy production.

## Must-try dishes
- Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton with savory sauce)
- Kaisendon (rice bowl with assorted sashimi)
- Jingisukan (Japanese Mongolian barbecue, grilled lamb/mutton)
- Uni ikura don (rice bowl with sea urchin and salmon roe)
- Ishikari Nabe (salmon and vegetable hot pot)
- Local ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors)
- Hokkaido dairy products (milk, butter, cheese)
