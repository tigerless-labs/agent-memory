**Bike Rental Companies** (offering baskets/racks & comfort bikes):

- **Barcelona Bike Tours** – 3-hour guided tours (La Sagrada Família, Park Güell, Gothic Quarter); bikes with baskets/racks; comfort bikes available
- **Fat Tire Tours** – guided bike tours; comfortable, well-maintained bikes with baskets/racks
- **Bicitours Barcelona** – guided tours; bikes with baskets/racks; special requests available (child seats, trailers)
- **Donkey Republic** – bike-sharing platform; daily rentals or hourly; bikes with baskets available

Comfort options: comfort bikes with upright handlebars and cushioned seats; hybrid bikes; e-bikes with adjustable suspension and electric assist.

**Bike Infrastructure**:

- **BiciLlanes** – 200+ km network of dedicated bike lanes separated by physical barriers or markings
- **Barcelona Bike Path** (Passeig de la Mar) – 7 km seaside path along Mediterranean coast
- **Diagonal Bike Lane** (Carrer de la Diagonal) – 11 km dedicated bike lane running diagonally across city
- **Ciclopeatonal** – network connecting parks and green areas
- **Bicing** – city bike-sharing system (day pass or subscription available)

**Bike-Friendly Cafes & Restaurants**:

- **Can Culleretes** (Gothic Quarter) – historic cafe, coffee & snacks, bike parking
- **El Xampanyet** (Gothic Quarter) – pan con tomate specialty, outdoor seating, bike parking
- **La Pubilla** (Eixample) – pastries and coffee
- **Bicioci** (Poblenou) – bike-themed cafe, healthy snacks, bike repair station, bike parking
- **Can Solé** (Barceloneta) – traditional Catalan cuisine, outdoor seating, bike parking

**Tips**: Request bikes with comfortable seats and upright handlebars for back support. Always bring reusable water bottle (refill at fountains throughout city). Helmets recommended. Follow traffic rules, yield to pedestrians. Hola BCN card covers unlimited public transport.
