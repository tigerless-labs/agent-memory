## Recommendations Given

**Similar to The Book of Mormon (irreverent humor, catchy music):**
- Avenue Q (puppets in NYC, adult themes)
- The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee (quirky comedy about middle school spelling bee)
- Something Rotten! (1590s, brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare)
- The Producers (Mel Brooks, scheming producers creating Broadway flop)
- Urinetown (satirical, pokes fun at capitalism and government)
- Be More Chill (sci-fi, high school student takes pill to be popular)

**Other genres:**
- Hamilton (groundbreaking, diverse cast, hip-hop/jazz/blues)
- Dear Evan Hansen (heartwarming, about social media and mental health)
- The Band's Visit (poignant, Egyptian police band visits Israel)
- Hadestown (folk-opera retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice myth, won 2019 Tony for Best Musical)

## Where to Find Recordings
Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Google Play Music, YouTube Music

## Hadestown Details
- Original Broadway cast album released 2019
- Features Eva Noblezada (Eurydice), Reeve Carney (Orpheus), Patrick Page (Hades), Amber Gray (Persephone)
- Music by Anaïs Mitchell (also book and lyrics)
- Blend of folk, blues, jazz, Americana with ancient Greek influences
- 2019 Tony Awards: 8 wins including Best Musical
- 2019 Grammy Awards: 2 wins
- Also performed in London West End and US tour
