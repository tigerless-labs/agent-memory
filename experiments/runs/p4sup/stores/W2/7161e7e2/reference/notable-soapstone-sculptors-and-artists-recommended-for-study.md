**Historical and Contemporary Soapstone Sculptors:**

**Inuit Artists (Canada, Greenland, Alaska)**
- Kenojuak Ashevak (1927-2013) — pioneering Inuit artist, colorful graphic prints and soapstone sculptures
- Kiawak Ashoona (1933-2014) — Inuit sculptor and printmaker, worked with soapstone, whalebone

**African Soapstone Sculptors**
- Nicholas Mukomberanwa (Zimbabwe, 1940-2002) — prominent Zimbabwean sculptor, figurative and abstract pieces
- Joram Mariga (Zimbabwe, b. 1927) — Zimbabwean sculptor, intricate figurative sculptures

**Contemporary Western Sculptors**
- Susan Taylor Glasgow (USA, b. 1950) — delicate, intricate soapstone carvings of flowers, leaves, organic forms
- Mark Lundeen (USA, b. 1958) — Colorado-based, realistic detailed soapstone sculptures of animals, humans
- Tim Lewis (USA, b. 1952) — works with soapstone, wood; abstract, figurative, surreal pieces
- Clare Leighton (UK, 1898-1989) — British sculptor, printmaker, illustrator; worked with soapstone, wood
