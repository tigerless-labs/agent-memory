## Worship Service Structure (Luke 7:1-17, Faith Healing Theme)

1. Opening Hymn: "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters
2. Call to Worship and Prayer
3. Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17
4. Sermon on Luke 7:1-17 (faith healing theme)
5. Hymn of Response: "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley
6. Testimony from congregation
7. Hymn of Faith: "Amazing Grace" by John Newton
8. Healing Prayer
9. Closing Hymn: "Healer" by Kari Jobe
10. Benediction

## Recommended Songs & Hymns (Faith Healing Theme)

**Modern:**
- "Healer" by Kari Jobe
- "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters

**Other:**
- "I Believe in Miracles" by Jackson Browne

**Classic Hymns:**
- "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley
- "Amazing Grace" by John Newton
- "There is Power in the Blood" by Lewis E. Jones
- "Heal Me, O Lord" by William Walsham How

## Key Biblical Connections

**Primary passage:** Luke 7:1-17 (centurion's servant healed)

**Related themes:**
- Matthew 8:10: Faith not limited to particular groups
- Hebrews 11:1: Faith defined (substance of things hoped for)
- Mark 9:23: All things possible to those who believe
- John 14:12-14: Authority and power of believers
- James 2:14-26: Faith without works is dead
- Isaiah 53:5: Healing through Christ's wounds
