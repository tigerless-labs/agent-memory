**Crusty Vegan Bread with Oat Flour and Sesame Seeds**

Creates crusty exterior with soft, airy interior.

Ingredients:
* 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
* 1/2 cup oat flour
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1 teaspoon sugar
* 1 packet active dry yeast (vegan)
* 1 cup warm water
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 tablespoon sesame seeds

Instructions:
1. Mix dry ingredients (flour, oat flour, salt, sugar, yeast)
2. Gradually add warm water until shaggy dough forms
3. Add olive oil and mix until comes together
4. Knead on floured surface 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in oiled bowl, cover with damp cloth, rise 1 hour until doubled
6. Preheat oven to 425°F
7. Shape dough into round/oblong loaf on parchment-lined baking sheet
8. Brush top with water, sprinkle sesame seeds
9. Bake 25-30 minutes until golden brown (tap bottom to check for hollow sound)
10. Cool on wire rack 10-15 minutes before slicing

Tips: For crustier bread, bake additional 5-10 minutes. Brush with olive oil/water mixture for more golden crust. Can add other toppings like nuts, seeds, or herbs.
