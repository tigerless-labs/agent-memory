**Tank size:** 20 gallons

**Current fish:**
- 10 neon tetras
- 5 golden honey gouramis
- 1 small pleco catfish

**Existing decorations:**
- Artificial coral
- Rocks

**Planning to add:**
- Live plants (Java Moss and Anacharis)
- Treasure chest decoration (to create a 'city' area with coral and rocks)
- Possibly schooling fish (lemon tetras or zebra danios) in a school of 6-10+

**Care considerations:**
- Java Moss: Low to moderate lighting, 72-82°F, attach to rocks/driftwood
- Anacharis: Moderate to high lighting, 65-85°F, floating plant, natural food for pleco
- All fish compatible with the current community
