Planning gift package for cousin John's engineering degree graduation (June 22, 2022):

1. **Customized briefcase or laptop bag** - personalized with his name or initials for his new job
2. **Congratulatory card** - handwritten message with a funny joke about engineers (e.g., 'Why did the engineer cross the road? Because it was a structurally sound decision!')
3. **Tech store gift card** - $25-$100 to Best Buy or Micro Center to help set up his new workspace

The combination provides both a professional, personalized keepsake and practical support for his career start.
