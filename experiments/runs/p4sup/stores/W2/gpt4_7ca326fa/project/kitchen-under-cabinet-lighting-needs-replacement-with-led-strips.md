User has been meaning to replace under-cabinet lighting in kitchen. Researched LED strip lifespan: typical quality lasts 20,000-30,000 hours (10-15 years at 8 hrs/day), premium strips 50,000+ hours (30+ years). Factors affecting lifespan: temperature, humidity, usage patterns. Recommendation: high-quality strips with CRI 80+.
