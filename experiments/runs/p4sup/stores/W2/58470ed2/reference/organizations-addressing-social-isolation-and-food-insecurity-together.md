Several organizations specifically address both social isolation and food insecurity together: Meals on Wheels America, Feeding America, Senior Corps, Little Brothers - Friends of the Elderly, Catholic Charities USA, The Salvation Army, and local faith-based organizations. These organizations provide meal delivery, companionship, social services, counseling, case management, and other support to vulnerable populations including seniors and individuals with disabilities.
