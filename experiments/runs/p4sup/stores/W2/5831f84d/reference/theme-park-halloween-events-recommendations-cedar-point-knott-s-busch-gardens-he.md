**Recommended Halloween theme park events for September-November:**\n\n1. **Cedar Point HalloWeekends** (Saturdays and Sundays, Sept 17 - Nov 6) — Sandusky, Ohio. Features haunted houses, scare zones, trick-or-treating, and nighttime coaster rides including Orion giga coaster.\n\n2. **Knott's Scary Farm** (Select nights, Sept 22 - Nov 1) — Buena Park, California. Known as "granddaddy of all Halloween events" with new scare zones, mazes, shows, and Pumpkin Eater coaster.\n\n3. **Busch Gardens Howl-O-Scream** (Fridays and Saturdays, Sept 24 - Nov 6) — Tampa, Florida. Features six haunted houses, scare zones, live shows, and extended hours for dark coaster rides (SheiKra, Montu).\n\n4. **Hersheypark in the Dark** (Weekends, Oct 15 - Nov 7) — Hershey, Pennsylvania. Trick-or-treating, costume contests, themed areas, extended hours for nighttime coaster rides (Skyrush, Fahrenheit).\n\n5. **Dollywood's Great Pumpkin LumiNights** (Fridays and Saturdays, Sept 24 - Oct 30) — Pigeon Forge, Tennessee. Family-friendly Halloween event with pumpkin-themed activities, shows, attractions.
