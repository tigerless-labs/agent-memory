When styling winter clothing, prioritizes:
- **Balance over bulk**: Pairs voluminous winter pieces (puffy jackets, chunky sweaters) with fitted graphic tees and fitted bottoms
- **Strategic layering**: Layers graphic tee → thinner fitted sweater → winter coat to add texture without excessive bulk
- **Proportion play**: Mixes different silhouettes (fitted + flowy, bulky top + fitted bottom)
- **Defined silhouettes**: Uses belts to cinch waist and create feminine shape
- **Minimalist accessories**: Opts for simple hats, scarves, gloves rather than over-accessorizing
- **Fabric quality**: Prefers lightweight winter fabrics (merino wool, cashmere, cotton blends) over heavy materials
- **Tailored coats**: Gravitates toward fitted silhouettes (tailored coat, pea coat) rather than oversized puffy jackets

Goal: Create stylish, cohesive winter looks without feeling weighed down or overwhelmed by bulk.
