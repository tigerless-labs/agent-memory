**Core vaccinations for baby goats:**
- CDT (Clostridium perfringens type C and D, and tetanus): Given at 2-3 weeks of age, booster 2-3 weeks later
- Pasteurella multocida (P. multocida): Given at 2-3 months, booster 2-3 weeks later (protects against pneumonia and respiratory diseases)
- Regional variations may include: Coccidiosis, Giardiasis, Leptospirosis

**Key health concerns for baby goats:**
- Scours (diarrhea): Monitor stool quality and hydration
- Pneumonia/respiratory issues: Watch for coughing, rapid breathing, nasal discharge
- Hypothermia: Especially in cold/wet conditions; ensure warm, dry shelter
- Parasite control: Both external (ticks, lice, mites) and internal (worms)
- Nutritional deficiencies: Ensure balanced diet with adequate milk/milk replacer and starter feed
- Umbilical cord care: Keep clean and dry; apply antibacterial ointment or iodine
- Colostrum: Critical to receive adequate colostrum within first few hours of life

**Preparation for vet visits:**
- Acclimate to handling (ears, legs, underside)
- Get used to containers/carriers
- Desensitize to clinic noises
- Practice halter breaking
- Consider mock vet visit beforehand
