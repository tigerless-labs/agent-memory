1.5 oz Hendrick's gin (or preferred gin), 0.5 oz Suze (or other dry bitter liqueur like Luxardo Bitter), 0.5 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth. Stir in mixing glass with ice for 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass. Garnish with orange or lemon twist. This variation emphasizes gin and bitter liqueur with a crisp, herbaceous profile. Can adjust ratios; some prefer 2:1:1 or 3:1:1 gin-to-bitter-to-vermouth.
