Ingredients: 1 cup granulated sugar, 1 cup water, 1/4 cup dried lavender buds. Instructions: (1) Combine sugar and water in saucepan, heat while stirring until sugar dissolves. (2) Add dried lavender buds and remove from heat. (3) Let steep 10-15 minutes until cooled to room temperature. (4) Strain through fine-mesh sieve into clean glass bottle and discard lavender buds. (5) Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Use 1 oz in cocktails; pairs well with Hendrick's gin. Start with small amounts as lavender can overpower quickly.
