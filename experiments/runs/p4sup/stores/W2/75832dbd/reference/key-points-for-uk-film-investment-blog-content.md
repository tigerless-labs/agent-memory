## UK Film Investment Arguments

Box Office Performance
- 2019: UK films grossed over £1.3 billion at global box office
- Successful recent examples: 'Rocketman', 'Yesterday'
- Consistent strong domestic and international performance

Industry Strengths
- Awards success: numerous Academy Awards and BAFTAs
- Talent pool: diverse filmmakers, writers, actors
- Strong film schools and institutions
- Government funding and support initiatives

Investment Appeal
- Lucrative opportunity with proven track record
- Thriving, innovative industry
- Fertile ground for new and original ideas

Target audience: Investors considering film industry opportunities
