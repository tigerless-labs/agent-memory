Evidence-based strategies for staying motivated during weight loss:

**Goal Setting & Tracking**
- Set small, achievable goals reachable each week for sense of accomplishment
- Keep track of progress to see how much you've achieved
- Keep food and exercise diary to identify patterns

**Celebration & Rewards**
- Celebrate non-scale victories: fitting into clothes, increased energy, improved mood, better sleep
- Reward successes with treats like massage, new workout gear, night out
- Celebrate every small victory, no matter how small it seems

**Support & Mindset**
- Find a support system—surround yourself with supportive people
- Don't compare yourself to others; everyone's journey is different
- Focus on the bigger picture and why you started the journey
- Be kind to yourself; setbacks are normal
- Practice self-care: enough sleep, reduce stress, include enjoyable activities
- Focus on the process, not just the end result; enjoy the journey

**Problem-Solving**
- Identify obstacles standing in your way and brainstorm strategies
- Mix up workouts if not seeing results; try new exercises
- Find healthier alternatives to favorite foods
- Incorporate favorite foods in moderation—practice portion control

**Realistic Expectations**
- Healthy weight loss is gradual: 1-2 pounds per week is safe and achievable
- Weight loss is not linear; it takes time for progress to become visible
- Remember that making healthy lifestyle changes is a journey, not a destination
