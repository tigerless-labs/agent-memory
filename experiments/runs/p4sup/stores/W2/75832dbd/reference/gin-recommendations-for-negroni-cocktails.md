## Gins That Pair Well with Campari in a Negroni

- **Plymouth Gin** — slightly sweeter, subtle flavor profile; fruit and spice notes complement Campari's bitterness
- **Bombay Sapphire** — crisp, citrusy, moderate juniper; citrus notes cut through Campari's richness
- **Beefeater London Dry Gin** — bold juniper, subtle fruit notes; stands up to Campari's bitterness well
- **G'Vine Floraison** — French, grape-based spirit; floral notes (grapeflower, jasmine) add unique dimension
- **Sipsmith London Dry Gin** — juniper-forward with sweetness and citrus; bold but balanced
- **The Botanist** — 22-botanical blend; complex with subtle fruit and floral notes
- **Death's Door Gin** — Wisconsin; crisp, clean flavor with juniper, coriander, citrus; refreshing with hint of sweetness
- **Ransom Old Tom Gin** — sweeter, botanical-driven; historical take on classic cocktail

**Key principle:** Balance is essential; experiment with different gin styles and ratios to find harmony.
