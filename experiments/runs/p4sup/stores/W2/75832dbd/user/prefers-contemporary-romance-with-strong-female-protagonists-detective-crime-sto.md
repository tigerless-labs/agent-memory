Reading preferences across Romance genre:

**Core interests:**
- Romance novels by female authors
- Strong female protagonists with goals/agency beyond finding love
- Contemporary settings preferred
- Crime/detective storylines blended with romance
- Steamy, intimate scenes (spicy Romance)

- Crime-solving Romance combinations
- Female detective protagonists (e.g., In Death series by J.D. Robb, Rizzoli & Isles by Tess Gerritsen)

- Steamy Romance (e.g., Sylvia Day's work)

**Dislikes:**
- 'The Help' by Kathryn Stockett (not recommended again)

**Characteristics valued:**
- Strong, badass female leads
- Dual plot threads (crime/mystery + romance)
- Mature/spicy content acceptable
