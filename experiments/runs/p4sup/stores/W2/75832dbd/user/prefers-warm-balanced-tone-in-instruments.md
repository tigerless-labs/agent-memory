Based on ukulele research, prefers warm, rich, balanced sound with strong mid-range presence over brighter, trebly tones. Drawn to mahogany wood (warm, velvety tone) rather than koa (brighter, more trebly). Interested in instruments with full-bodied resonance and sustain.
