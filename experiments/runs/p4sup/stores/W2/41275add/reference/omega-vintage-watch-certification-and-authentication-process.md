Omega offers services for vintage watch owners:\n\n**What Omega provides:**\n- Authentication: Verify genuine Omega vs. replica\n- Certification of Origin: Certificate stating production date, model, serial number\n- Condition Report: Assessment of watch condition and restoration needs\n- Service and Restoration: Professional servicing using original parts when possible\n- Certificate of Authenticity: Unique certificate number for verification\n\n**Benefits of certification:**\n- Increased resale value\n- Enhanced buyer credibility and confidence\n- Better market reception\n\n**Process:**\n- Contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service\n- Cost varies by model, age, and condition\n- Keep all documentation (certification, service records)\n- Store certificate securely\n\nRecommended for high-value vintage watches before selling to maximize value.
