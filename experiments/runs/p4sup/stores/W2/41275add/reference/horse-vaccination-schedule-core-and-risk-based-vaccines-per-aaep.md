Core vaccines (annual): EEE/WEE (spring), Tetanus (annually or 5-7 yrs), West Nile Virus (spring) | Risk-based: Influenza (annual/biannual), Rabies (annually or 3 yrs), Strangles (if high risk) | Foals: Initial at 6-8 weeks, boosters at 10-12 and 14-16 weeks | Preparation: schedule ahead, confirm vaccination history, clean area, consider separating horses, ensure good health, have medical records ready | Consult veterinarian for individual schedule based on age, health, and local disease risks
