Mindfulness Meditation: Focus on present moment. Sit quietly, close eyes, become aware of breath and thoughts, gently redirect attention when distracted. Guided Meditation: Listen to recorded sessions for structure and focus. Loving Kindness Meditation: Cultivate compassion by visualizing someone you care about and silently repeating phrases of kindness toward them.
