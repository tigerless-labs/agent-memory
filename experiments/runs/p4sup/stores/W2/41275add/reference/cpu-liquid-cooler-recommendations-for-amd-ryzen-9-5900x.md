Recommended liquid coolers: NZXT Kraken X73 (360mm), Corsair Hydro Series H115i RGB Platinum (280mm), EKWB EK-AIO 360 D-RGB (360mm), Thermaltake Floe Riing 360 (360mm), Noctua NH-U14S TR4-SP3 (air cooler alternative). Factors: radiator size, noise, RGB, budget 100-200 dollars, warranty.
