Using improvisation techniques from workshop to prepare for next audition.\n\nTechniques to practice:\n- Improvise scenes and characters from the audition script\n- Explore different character choices: accents, mannerisms, emotional responses\n- Practice responding to unexpected moments in scenes\n- Work on listening and reacting skills to stay present\n- Find own unique interpretation of the character\n- Record and review improvisation sessions to identify areas for improvement\n\nAlso practicing:\n- Getting familiar with the script through repeated reading\n- Exploring character motivations and goals\n- Trying different interpretations before finalizing choices\n- Staying loose and flexible rather than over-rehearsing\n\nUses improvisation as supplement to traditional audition prep (script study, character research, materials preparation).
