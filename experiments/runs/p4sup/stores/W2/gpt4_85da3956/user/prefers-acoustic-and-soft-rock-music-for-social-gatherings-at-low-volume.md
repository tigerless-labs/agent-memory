For dinner parties and social gatherings, enjoys acoustic and soft rock music as background ambiance.

**Artists**: Jack Johnson, Jason Mraz, Ed Sheeran, James Taylor, Fleetwood Mac, The Eagles, Crosby Stills & Nash, and classic artists like Frank Sinatra and Ella Fitzgerald.

**Setting**: Music should be kept at low volume so guests can have conversations easily without being drowned out.

**Context**: Creates a warm, inviting, relaxed atmosphere for family gatherings.
