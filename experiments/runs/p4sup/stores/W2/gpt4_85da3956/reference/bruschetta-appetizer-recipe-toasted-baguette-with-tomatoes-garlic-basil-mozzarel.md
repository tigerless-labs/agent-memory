## Bruschetta Recipe

**Ingredients**:
- Baguette, sliced and toasted
- Fresh diced tomatoes
- Minced garlic
- Fresh basil
- Mozzarella cheese
- Olive oil
- Balsamic glaze (or balsamic vinegar)

**Preparation**: Top toasted baguette slices with the tomato mixture, drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze, and finish with basil and mozzarella.

**Serves**: Works well for appetizers for 6-8 people at a dinner party.

**Notes**: A classic Italian-style appetizer that's easy to make and always popular with guests.
