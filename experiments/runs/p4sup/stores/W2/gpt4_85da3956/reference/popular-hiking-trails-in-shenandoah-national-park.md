**Old Rag** — 9 miles, challenging hike with beautiful views and unique rock formations\n\n**Hawksbill Mountain** — 2.9 miles, hike to tallest peak in Shenandoah with stunning views\n\n**Dark Hollow Falls** — 1.4 miles, easy hike to waterfall, good for summer swimming\n\n**Marys Rock** — 3.7 miles, hike to summit with views of Shenandoah Valley\n\n**Stony Man** — 1.6 miles, relatively easy hike to second-highest peak with panoramic views
