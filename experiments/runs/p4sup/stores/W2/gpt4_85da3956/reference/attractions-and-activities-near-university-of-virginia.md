**Historical/Cultural:**\n- Monticello (Thomas Jefferson's home, a few miles from campus)\n- The Rotunda (iconic centerpiece designed by Jefferson)\n- The Lawn (UNESCO World Heritage Site with 10 pavilions by Jefferson)\n- Downtown Charlottesville Historic Mall (pedestrian area with shops and restaurants)\n\n**Outdoor:**\n- Shenandoah National Park (north of Charlottesville)\n- Vineyards and breweries in Charlottesville area\n\n**Events & Entertainment:**\n- UVA sports games\n- John Paul Jones Arena (concerts and events)\n- Virginia Discovery Museum\n\n**Tourist Information:**\n- UVA Visitor Center offers guided tours, self-guided maps, parking info, and lodging help
