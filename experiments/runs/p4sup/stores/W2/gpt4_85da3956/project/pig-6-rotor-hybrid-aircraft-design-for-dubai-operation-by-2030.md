Project Goal: Develop Pig, a multirotor hybrid electric/fuel-cell aircraft for operation in Dubai by 2030.

Design Requirements:
- Capacity: 6 people (including pilot/engineer)
- Cabin height: 1.90 m (allows standing room)
- Propulsion: 6-rotor configuration with hybrid fuel cell + battery system
- Range: 400 km

Current Specifications (as of 2023-06-18):

MTOW: 3200 kg
- Structure: 950 kg
- Avionics and systems: 450 kg
- Other equipment: 150 kg
- Payload (6 people + baggage): 630 kg (75 kg/person + 40 kg baggage each)
- Fuel (hydrogen): 30 kg

Dimensions:
- Wingspan: 15.6 m
- Length: 10.5 m
- Height: 4.0 m
- Cabin: 2.5 m (length) x 1.5 m (width) x 1.9 m (height)

Design Phase Status: Initial design point established. Next steps include detailed engineering drawings, manufacturing planning, system specifications, component selection, aerodynamic optimization, and control algorithms.
