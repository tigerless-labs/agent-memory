## Budget Hostels in Amsterdam

1. **Stayokay Amsterdam Vondelpark** — Located next to Vondelpark in the city center; offers dormitory and private rooms
2. **ClinkNOORD Hostel** — In Amsterdam Noord (former laboratory); has bar, restaurant, and bike rental
3. **The Bulldog Hostel Amsterdam** — City center location; shared dorms and private rooms; bar and smoking lounge
4. **International Budget Hostel** — Near Red Light District; dormitory and private rooms; equipped kitchen and lounge
5. **Amsterdam Hostel Centre** — Heart of Amsterdam; dormitory and private rooms; communal kitchen, bar, lounge

Note: Recommendations are from May 2023; verify current availability, pricing, and reviews before booking.
