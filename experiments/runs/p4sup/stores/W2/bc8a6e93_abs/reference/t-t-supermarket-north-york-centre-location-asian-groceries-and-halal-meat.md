T&T Supermarket at 5300 Yonge St, North York Centre, Toronto

Asian grocery store chain specializing in:
- Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Thai, and Southeast Asian ingredients
- Rice, noodles, sauces, spices, frozen foods
- Indian and Pakistani spices
- Middle Eastern ingredients
- Some Latin American and European items

Dedicated halal meat section certified by Halal Monitoring Authority (HMA) and Islamic Society of North America (ISNA):
- Fresh meat: beef, chicken, lamb, goat
- Frozen meat products: burgers, sausages, kebabs
- Poultry products
- Seafood: shrimp, fish, squid

Additional features:
- Large fresh produce section with Asian specialties
- In-store bakery with Asian breads and pastries
- Small food court with Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese restaurants
- Loyalty program with occasional sales

Tips: Can be crowded on weekends, bring shopping list, ask staff for assistance, check website for promotions
