Has had a lot of success with honey in lemon desserts. Finds that the rich, caramel-like flavor of honey complements the bright, tangy flavor of lemon well. Favors using honey in combination with other sweeteners (like granulated sugar) to balance out both flavor and texture. Interested in exploring different types of flour (prefers all-purpose over bread flour for lemon cakes and tart crusts) and techniques for incorporating lemon zest into baking.
