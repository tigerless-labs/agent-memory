Cultural orientation program available for newcomers/refugees arriving in Canada

Program objectives:
- Understand Canadian culture, customs, and values
- Learn rights and responsibilities as permanent resident
- Receive job search assistance and employment integration support
- Connect with community resources and services
- Build network of contacts and friendships

Content covered:
- Canadian culture, customs, values, communication styles
- Settlement information (housing, healthcare, education, services)
- Job search strategies, resume writing, interview techniques
- Language training or referrals for English/French
- Community resources, cultural organizations, recreation

Format:
- Typically several weeks
- Multiple sessions per week
- Group sessions, workshops, one-on-one meetings
- Cultural events, field trips, social activities

Preparation tips:
- Be open-minded and willing to learn
- Ask questions and seek help
- Take advantage of offered resources and services
- Be prepared to share own experiences and perspectives
