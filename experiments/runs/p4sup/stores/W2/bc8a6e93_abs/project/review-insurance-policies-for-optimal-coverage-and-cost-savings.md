Plan to review health, auto, and home insurance policies to ensure adequate coverage and identify cost savings opportunities. Key review areas:
- Health insurance: coverage adequacy, network providers, deductibles, copays, HSA eligibility
- Auto insurance: liability and comprehensive coverage, deductible amounts, available discounts
- Home insurance: coverage for home value and contents, deductible, available discounts, inventory of belongings
- General: compare rates with other providers, check for bundling discounts, adjust coverage as needs change
