Set up spreadsheet-based budget tracking with columns for:
- Income sources (salary, disability benefits, freelance work, part-time jobs)
- Fixed expenses (rent, utilities, insurance)
- Variable expenses (food, transportation, entertainment, hobbies)
- Debt repayment (credit cards, loans)
- Annual/quarterly expenses divided by 12 and added to monthly budget

Irregular income from freelance work and online sales is tracked separately, with 25-30% set aside for taxes.
