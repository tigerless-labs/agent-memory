Websites recommended for apartment search on limited budget:
- Zillow (rental search with price/location/amenities filters; Rent Zestimate feature)
- Trulia (price/location filters + crime maps + neighborhood insights)
- HotPads (map-based search with commute time feature)
- PadMapper (aggregates from Craigslist and local listings; has Safety Score)
- Walk Score (evaluates walkability and public transportation access)
- Apartments.com (comprehensive search with resident ratings & reviews)
- Rent Jungle (aggregates listings; has Neighborhood Guide feature)

Mobile apps:
- Zillow Rentals (iOS/Android)
- Trulia Rentals (iOS/Android)
- PadMapper (iOS/Android)

User currently using Zillow and HotPads.
