Orchid species recommended for beginners:

1. **Phalaenopsis (Moth Orchid)** - Most common and adaptable, relatively small, easy to care for, thrives in various lighting conditions
2. **Dendrobium** - Long-lasting flowers, low-maintenance, relatively hardy, tolerates some neglect
3. **Oncidium (Dancing Lady Orchid)** - Small, easy to care for, produces bright showy flowers, forgiving of occasional missed watering
4. **Cattleya** - Large showy flowers, relatively easy to care for, prefers bright indirect light and moderate watering
5. **Paphiopedilum (Venus Slipper Orchid)** - Unique slipper-shaped flowers, low-maintenance, prefers bright indirect light and consistent watering

All are relatively easy to find at nurseries or online and tolerate a range of conditions.
