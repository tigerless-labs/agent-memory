Recommended productivity apps for organization on new laptop:\n\n**Task Management:**\n- **Todoist**: Prioritization, deadlines, project templates\n- **Trello**: Visual boards, lists, cards, team collaboration\n- **Asana**: Workflows, deadlines, reporting\n\n**Note-taking:**\n- **Evernote**: Tagging, searching, organization into notebooks\n- **OneNote**: Microsoft integration, tagging, searching\n- **Simplenote**: Minimalistic, focus on simplicity\n\n**Time Management:**\n- **RescueTime**: Time tracking and productivity insights\n- **Focus@Will**: Background music for concentration\n- **Pomodoro Timer**: 25-minute focused work intervals with breaks\n\n**Calendar/Scheduling:**\n- **Google Calendar**: Integrates with Google apps, easy scheduling\n- **Microsoft Outlook**: Email + calendar + scheduling\n- **Fantastical**: Natural language input, clean interface\n\n**Additional Tools:**\n- **StayFocusd**: Browser extension to block distracting sites\n- **Forest**: Gamified productivity, grow virtual forest while focused\n- **IFTTT**: Automate tasks across apps and services
