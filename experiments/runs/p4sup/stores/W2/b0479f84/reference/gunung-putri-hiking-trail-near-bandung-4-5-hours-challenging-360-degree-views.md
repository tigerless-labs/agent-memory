Located about 20 kilometers outside Bandung. Challenging hike taking 4-5 hours to complete roundtrip. Offers unobstructed 360-degree views of Bandung city, including Tangkuban Perahu Volcano, and surrounding lush rainforests. Recommended to start early to avoid heat and crowds, bring water, food, sun protection, first aid kit, hiking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, and map/GPS device.
