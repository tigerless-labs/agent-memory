## Bandung Hiking Trails

**Tangkuban Perahu Volcano** — 25 km north of city center, 1.5 hours to crater. Popular volcano hiking through lush rainforests. Offers panoramic views of surrounding mountains and Bandung city; visible sulfuric smoke from crater.

**Maribaya Waterfall** — 15 km northeast of Bandung, moderate hike taking about 45 minutes.

**Cikapundung River** — Leisurely 5 km trail following the river, popular local spot for relaxation and nature.

**Ciwidey Valley** — About 1 hour drive south of Bandung. 2-3 hour hike through scenic valley with tea plantations and rice paddies, ends at a waterfall. Serene atmosphere with bird and waterfall sounds.

Each trail offers different difficulty levels and scenic experiences suitable for different preferences.
