Eight progressive levels of concentration in Buddhist practice:
1. First Jhana — sustained focus on meditation object (breath, mantra)
2. Second Jhana — joy and contentment, effortless focus
3. Third Jhana — equanimity and inner peace
4. Fourth Jhana — pure awareness, blissful detachment
5. Fifth Jhana — infinite space, expansive consciousness
6. Sixth Jhana — infinite consciousness, sense of unity with all things
7. Seventh Jhana — infinite bliss, pure unbounded joy
8. Eighth Jhana — infinite peace, complete stillness

Each level builds on the previous, ultimately leading to spiritual insight.
