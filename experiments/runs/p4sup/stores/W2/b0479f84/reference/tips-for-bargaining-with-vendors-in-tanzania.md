Bargaining is a common practice in Tanzanian markets. Key tips: (1) Be friendly and polite—greet vendors and make small talk to build rapport. (2) Know the value—check what other vendors charge for similar items to understand fair pricing. (3) Start with a low offer—offer below asking price to create negotiation room. (4) Be willing to walk away—vendors sometimes call you back with a lower price if you disengage. (5) Have fun with it—approach bargaining as an enjoyable process with a positive attitude. Important: Remember vendors rely on sales for their living, so negotiate fairly without undercutting too severely.
