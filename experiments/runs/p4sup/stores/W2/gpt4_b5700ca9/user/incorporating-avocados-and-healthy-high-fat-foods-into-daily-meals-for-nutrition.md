From March 2023 discussion:

Interested in adding avocados and other healthy high-fat foods to daily diet for better nutrition without excess calorie gain.

**Healthy high-fat foods identified:**
- Avocados (~50 cal/oz) - source of monounsaturated fat, fiber, potassium, vitamin C
- Nuts & seeds (almonds, cashews, chia, flaxseeds, pumpkin seeds) - 160-200 cal/oz, healthy fats, protein, fiber
- Salmon & fatty fish - omega-3 fatty acids, anti-inflammatory
- Olive oil (~120 cal/tbsp) - Mediterranean diet staple, heart health benefits
- Full-fat yogurt - healthy fats, protein, probiotics for gut health
- Dark chocolate - antioxidants, heart/brain health benefits (in moderation)

**Meal incorporation ideas:**
- Avocado slices in salads or grain bowls
- Mashed avocado instead of mayo on sandwiches/wraps
- Homemade guacamole
- Nuts/seeds in trail mix, smoothie bowls, oatmeal
- Greek yogurt with berries, honey, chopped nuts
- Salmon cooked with olive oil and herbs
- Olive oil for dressing salads/cooking vegetables
- Avocado on breakfast toast or with eggs
- Nut butter on whole grain bread
- Dark chocolate with nuts, berries, or fruit as snack

**Key principle:** Moderation is essential; balance with other healthy choices to avoid excess calorie intake.
