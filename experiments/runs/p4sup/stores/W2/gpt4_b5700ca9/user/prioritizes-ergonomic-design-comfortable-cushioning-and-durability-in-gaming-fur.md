When selecting gaming chairs and desks, prioritizes: 1) Ergonomic design for proper posture and support, 2) Comfortable cushioning for extended sessions, 3) Durability for long-term use given frequent 5-hour gaming sessions. Budget-conscious but willing to invest in quality.
