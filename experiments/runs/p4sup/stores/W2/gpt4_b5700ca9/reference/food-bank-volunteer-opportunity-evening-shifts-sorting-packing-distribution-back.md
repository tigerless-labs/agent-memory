**Location:** 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345 (accessible by public transit, parking available)

**Contact:**
- Phone: 555-555-5555
- Email: volunteer@foodbank.org
- Website: foodbank.org

**Tasks:**
- Sorting and packaging donated food
- Inventory management (receiving, stocking)
- Food distribution (setup, distribution, cleanup)
- Special events and administrative support

**Schedule Options:**
- Monday–Friday, evening shifts typically 4:00 pm–7:00 pm
- One evening/week, two evenings/week, or flexible (minimum 2 hours/month)

**Requirements:**
- Age: 18+
- Volunteer application form
- 30-minute orientation session
- Waiver and release form
- Background check (standard procedure)
- On-the-job training provided

**Group Volunteering:**
- Group volunteer program available for groups of 5–20 people
- Flexible scheduling
- Dedicated staff member supervision
- Can work together on sorting, packing, distributions, special events
