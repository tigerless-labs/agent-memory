Implementing healthy social media habits with these boundaries:\n\n1. **Specific check times** - Check social media at designated times only to avoid mindless scrolling\n2. **Website blockers** - Use blockers during work hours to stay focused\n3. **Unfollow negative accounts** - Remove accounts that cause stress, anxiety, or feelings of inadequacy\n4. **Sunday social media-free day** - Designate Sundays as completely screen-free from social media to focus on reading and other enjoyable activities\n\nThese strategies build on the success of the February 2023 social media break.
