## Economics Resources\n- Khan Academy Economics — comprehensive introductory economics courses (microeconomics, macroeconomics)\n- Coursera Economics Courses — various courses including Principles of Economics by University of Illinois\n- edX Economics Courses — Introduction to Economics from MIT and Harvard\n- MIT OpenCourseWare Economics — free course materials for range of economics subjects\n- Investopedia Economics — articles and tutorials on basic and advanced economic concepts\n\n## Introductory Data Science Courses\n- IBM Data Science Professional Certificate (Coursera) — hands-on projects, beginner-friendly\n- Data Science MicroMasters (UC San Diego on edX) — covers essential data science skills\n- DataCamp — interactive courses and career tracks (Data Scientist with Python, Data Scientist with R)\n- Udacity Data Scientist Nanodegree — real-world projects and mentorship\n- Kaggle Learn — free courses on data science topics and competitions
