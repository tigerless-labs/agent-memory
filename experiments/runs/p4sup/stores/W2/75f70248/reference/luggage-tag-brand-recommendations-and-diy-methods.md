Pre-made luggage tag brands: Tumi (durable, stylish, various colors/designs), Leatherology (premium leather, monogrammable), Travelon (affordable, durable, various designs). DIY options: fabric tag with cardboard/leather backing, painted plastic/metal tag using acrylic paint or nail polish, washi tape decorated plain tag, laminated card with name/contact info attached with ribbon. DIY tips: use durable materials, keep design simple and readable, secure attachment to bag, protect with clear plastic sleeve or laminate to resist weather.
