Best practices: Always use planters with drainage holes. Well-draining potting mix is essential to prevent root rot. Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) only, once monthly. Use balanced fertilizer with N-P-K ratio 20-20-20, diluted to half-strength. Skip fertilization fall/winter. Popular fertilizers: Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food, Schultz Indoor Plant Food, Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer. Overwatering is the most common mistake. Most indoor plants tolerate underwatering better than overwatering. Optimal temperatures: 65-75F (18-24C). Most prefer bright indirect light.
