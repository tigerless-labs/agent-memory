**Certification Overview**
- Offered by Financial Modeling Institute (FMI), founded 2015
- Validates expertise in financial modeling and ability to build robust, accurate, scalable models
- Suitable for finance professionals, analysts, and modelers

**Eligibility Requirements**
- Bachelor's degree in relevant field
- 2+ years work experience in finance or related field
- Strong understanding of financial modeling concepts

**Exam Details**
- Duration: 4 hours
- Format: 120-question exam
- Covers: financial modeling fundamentals, modeling techniques, model building, financial analysis, model review and audit

**Costs**
- Application Fee: $200
- Exam Fee: $1,200
- Annual Maintenance Fee: $200

**Benefits**
- Demonstrates expertise and knowledge in financial modeling
- Enhanced career prospects, higher salaries, job security
- Improved modeling skills
- Access to CFM professional community and networking
- Ongoing professional development requirements

**Maintenance**
- Annual renewal with maintenance fee of $200
- Continuing professional development required
- Adherence to FMI code of ethics
