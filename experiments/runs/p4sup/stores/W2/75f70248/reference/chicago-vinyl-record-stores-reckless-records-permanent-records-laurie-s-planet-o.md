Reckless Records (Wicker Park, Lakeview, Logan Square): Extensive new/used vinyl with strong indie folk section. Permanent Records (Ukrainian Village): Vast selection with indie/folk focus. Laurie's Planet of Sound (Lincoln Square): Curated indie/folk/singer-songwriter vinyl. Saki (Logan Square): Record store and gallery with indie folk. The Vinyl Conflict (Logan Square): Punk/indie/underground with indie folk selection. Crossroads Music (Avondale): Large used vinyl selection. Dave's Records (Lincoln Square): Vast used vinyl collection including indie folk.
