Exhibition critique of capitalist and consumerist culture. Centerpiece: handmade rug with IKEA logo altered to MERDA. Features 100 artists across video art, installations, performances, and ready-made projects. Artists include Ai Wei Wei, Banksy, Yoko Ono, Felix Gonzalez-Torres, Jenny Holzer critiquing labor exploitation, environmental waste, and consumption.
