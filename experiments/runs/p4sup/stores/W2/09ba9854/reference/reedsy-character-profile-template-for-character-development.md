Free downloadable template for creating well-rounded, believable characters. Divided into 12 sections:

1. Basic Info (name, age, occupation, brief description)
2. Physical Appearance (height, weight, hair, distinguishing features)
3. Personality (traits, values, goals, flaws)
4. Backstory (childhood, family, education, significant life events)
5. Motivations (what drives the character, what they want, obstacles)
6. Conflict (internal and external conflicts, responses to challenges)
7. Relationships (family, friends, romantic relationships, enemies)
8. Skills and Abilities (talents, expertise, limitations)
9. Fears and Phobias (what scares them, potential reactions)
10. Habits and Quirks (mannerisms, peculiarities)
11. Emotional Landscape (emotional strengths and weaknesses)
12. Evolution (character growth throughout the story)

Provides balanced level of detail—comprehensive but not overwhelming. Can be customized and expanded as needed.
