Lowepro Lens Case - High-quality materials, durable, excellent protection, multiple sizes\n3. Lenscoat Lens Pouch - Size-specific, soft padded interior, water-resistant and breathable\n3. Tamrac Lens Pouch - Protection and convenience, zippered closure, multiple sizes\n\nSelection criteria: appropriate size for snug fit, durable high-quality materials, thick soft padding for shock absorption, secure closure (zipper or Velcro), breathability for humid storage environments.\n\nStorage tips: Keep lens caps on, store upright to prevent oil flow to mounts, avoid stacking lenses, clean lenses before storage.
