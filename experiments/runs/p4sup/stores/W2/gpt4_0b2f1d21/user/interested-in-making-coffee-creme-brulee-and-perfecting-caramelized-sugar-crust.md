Specifically interested in Coffee Crème Brûlée as a dessert option for dinner parties. Wants to master the technique for creating a perfect caramelized sugar crust.

Learned key techniques:
- Use granulated sugar for even caramelization
- Heat skillet to medium-high (almost smoking) before adding sugar
- Sprinkle sugar gradually in thin, even layer
- Don't stir—let dissolve undisturbed for 2-3 minutes, gently tilt skillet for even cooking
- Target deep amber color (reddish-brown), not light
- Remove from heat immediately when desired color reached
- Cool and set completely before serving for glass-like crust

Also interested in other coffee-based desserts like Mocha Trifle.
