**Platforms for printing business cards:**

1. **Vistaprint** — Well-known, wide templates, frequent promotions
2. **Canva** — Design platform with built-in printing services
3. **MOO** — Stylish, modern, focuses on business cards and print
4. **FedEx Office** — Reliable, extensive templates
5. **GotPrint** — Budget-friendly option

**Considerations when ordering:**
- Paper quality and finish (matte, glossy, recycled)
- Card size and shape options
- Minimum order quantity
- Turnaround time and shipping costs
- Design upload/editing capabilities
- Consider local print shops for personalized service
