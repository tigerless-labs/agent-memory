**Why separate business accounts:**
- Establishes professional online presence
- Maintains brand consistency, tone, and voice
- Allows targeted content relevant to business
- Provides access to analytics and insights
- Builds customer trust and credibility

**Recommended platforms for handmade business:**

**Instagram** — Visually-driven platform
- Showcase products, behind-the-scenes moments
- Share recipes, tutorials, lifestyle content
- Ideal for artisanal/handmade aesthetic

**Facebook** — Community-focused
- Build customer community
- Share updates and announcements
- Run targeted ads

**Pinterest** — Discovery and lifestyle
- Showcase products in lifestyle settings
- Create thematic boards
- Use keywords for search visibility

**Best practices:**
- Use consistent business name, logo, bio across platforms
- Complete all profile sections (picture, cover, bio, contact info)
- Post high-quality, engaging, visually appealing content
- Mix promotional, educational, and entertaining content
- Engage with audience: respond to comments, answer questions
- Share user-generated content
