**Online marketplace strategy:**
Starting with Etsy and Amazon Handmade (both focused on handmade products). Eventually expand website as central hub.

**Social media strategy:**
Create separate business accounts (not personal) on:
- **Instagram:** Showcase products, behind-the-scenes, recipes/tutorials
- **Facebook:** Build community, share updates, run targeted ads
- **Pinterest:** Lifestyle product showcase, boards, keyword visibility

**Brand consistency:** Maintain consistent name, logo, bio, contact info across all platforms
