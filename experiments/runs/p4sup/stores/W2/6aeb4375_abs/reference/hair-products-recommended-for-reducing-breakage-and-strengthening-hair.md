**Protein-based conditioners & strengthening treatments:**
- Olaplex No. 3 Hair Perfector
- Kerastase Resistance Length Strengthening Scalp Serum
- Redken Extreme Anti-Snap Leave-In Treatment
- Briogeo Don't Despair, Repair! Strengthening Treatment Oil

**General recommendations:**
- Leave-in conditioners for nourishment and protection
- Hair masks for deep hydration
- Heat protectants for styling tools

**Tools:** Wide-tooth combs and soft-bristled brushes for detangling; start combing from ends and work up to roots.
