When preparing Indian cuisine, chooses traditional Indian basmati rice over Japanese short-grain rice. Basmati's long, slender grains and nutty flavor pair well with Indian spices and seasonings. Short-grain rice is too sticky and clingy for these applications—better reserved for sushi and other Japanese dishes where the sticky texture is desired.
