Downtown coffee shop recommendations for quick grab-and-go:

**The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf**
- Price: 2-4 dollars per drink
- Style: Popular chain with multiple downtown locations
- Highlights: Wide variety of coffee drinks, reasonable prices

**Peet's Coffee**
- Price: 2-4 dollars per drink
- Style: Well-known chain with downtown location
- Highlights: High-quality beans, quick service model

**7-Eleven**
- Price: 1-2 dollars
- Style: Convenience store
- Highlights: Improved coffee quality, very quick and convenient, most affordable option

**Blue Bottle Coffee**
- Price: 4-5 dollars per drink
- Style: Premium specialty coffee
- Highlights: High-quality sourced beans, expertly brewed coffee
