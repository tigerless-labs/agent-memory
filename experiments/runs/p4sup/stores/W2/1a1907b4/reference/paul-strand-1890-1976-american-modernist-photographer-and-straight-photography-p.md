American photographer and pioneering figure in modernist and documentary photography.

**Life & Career:**
- Born 1890 in New York City; died 1976
- Largely self-taught, strongly influenced by Alfred Stieglitz
- Joined Stieglitz's Photo-Secession group in 1915
- Exhibited work in their gallery

**Work & Contributions:**
- Pioneer of straight photography movement
- Transitioned to documentary photography in 1920s
- Traveled extensively: rural communities, urban landscapes, industrial sites
- Characterized by simplicity, directness, and social purpose
- Deeply committed to using photography as tool for social change and raising awareness of injustices

**Major Projects:**
- Series photographing people and landscapes of Mexico beginning in 1932 (considered some of the most important examples of documentary photography from the period)

**Legacy:**
- One of the leading photographers of his generation
- Work continues celebrated for innovative style, social relevance, and enduring influence on modern photography
