Online marketplaces and forums for vintage camera sales:
- **Fred Miranda** (fredmiranda.com) - Popular forum for photography enthusiasts with dedicated marketplace for cameras and equipment
- **Rangefinder Forum** - Community focused on rangefinder cameras including Leica, with "For Sale" threads
- **eBay** - Large collector base, good for detailed listings with photos
- **Etsy** - Marketplace for vintage and unique items
- **KEH Camera** - Reputable dealer in used cameras; may purchase cameras or help with sales
- **WestLicht Auctions** - Leading auction house for photography equipment, holds regular auctions and accepts consignments
- **Photography Stack Exchange** - Q&A platform with buying/selling section

Tip: Include original lens and accessories to increase value; take high-quality photos; provide detailed condition descriptions.
