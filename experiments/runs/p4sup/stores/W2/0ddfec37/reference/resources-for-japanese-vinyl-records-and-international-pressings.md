Online stores and marketplaces for Japanese vinyl and international pressings:
- **Discogs** (discogs.com) - Vast database and marketplace; use "Country" filter for Japanese pressings; wantlist feature
- **eBay Japan** (ebay.co.jp) - Large selection; use Google Translate for navigation
- **Jet Set Records** (jetsetrecords.com) - Tokyo-based online store specializing in Japanese vinyl, rare and hard-to-find pressings
- **HMV Japan** (hmv.co.jp) - Japanese music retailer with selection of rare and imported vinyl
- **Disk Union** (diskunion.net) - Japanese online store with vast vinyl selection including rare and second-hand records
- **Yahoo! Japan Auctions** (auctions.yahoo.co.jp) - Auction site for rare records (watch for language barriers)
- **Rakuten** (rakuten.co.jp) - Japanese e-commerce platform with auction section for rare vinyl

Physical stores in Tokyo to visit: Tower Records Shibuya, HMV Shibuya, Disk Union Shibuya, Meditations (meditations.jp)

Communities: r/vinyl and r/JapaneseMusic on Reddit, Japanese-language music forums and Facebook groups
