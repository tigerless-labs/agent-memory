**Why kosher salt is preferred for pickling:**
- Coarser texture: easier to sprinkle, harder to over-salt
- Creates more even brine distribution
- Usually no additives (no iodine or anti-caking agents)
- Better flavor profile; milder than table salt
- Traditional choice for centuries

**Using table salt as substitute:**
- Use less table salt than kosher salt (table salt is more dense)
- May contain additives that affect flavor and texture
- Results in denser brine, potentially softer pickles
- Start with smaller amount and adjust to taste

**Bottom line:** Kosher salt is preferred but not strictly necessary; table salt works if you adjust quantities.
