Goals:\n1. Improve serve technique and consistency (serves have been inconsistent lately)\n2. Take online lessons to improve technique and strategy\n3. Balance practice: ~20-30% serve, remainder on groundstrokes/volleys/other shots\n4. Explore online resources: Tennis.com, Essential Tennis, Tennis Warehouse University\n5. Consider instructors: Brett Hobden, Jeff Salzenstein, Ian Westermann\n6. Coaching platforms: CoachTube, Tennis Coach Pro\n\nApproach: Demo Wilson Blade 98, take lessons, practice with purpose, film serves to track progress, work on core strength.
