**Title:** The Immortalists\n**Author:** Chloe Benjamin\n\n**Summary:** Novel about four Gold siblings who, in 1969, visit a fortune teller named Madame Rosa who predicts the exact date of each sibling's death. The narrative then jumps forward in time, exploring each sibling's life and how this knowledge affects their relationships, life choices, and understanding of mortality.\n\n**Structure:** Organized by each of the four siblings (Varya, Daniel, Klara, and Simon), with individual sections exploring their unique perspectives and experiences.\n\n**Themes:**\n- Mortality and the human condition\n- Family dynamics and bonds\n- Identity and purpose\n- Love, loss, and grief\n\n**Why recommended:** Strong character-driven narrative that explores complex family relationships. Lyrical, evocative writing style. Thought-provoking exploration of human experience. Aligns with user's interest in mythology-inspired books and character-driven fiction.
