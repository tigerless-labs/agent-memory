Trip Details:
- Location: Tilden Regional Park, Berkeley Hills, East Bay
- Date: Weekend of May 20, 2023
- Type: Solo day hike
- Trail options: Nimitz Way Trail or Wildcat Gorge Trail

Nimitz Way Trail:
- Distance: 4.5 miles out-and-back (9 miles round-trip)
- Elevation gain: 1,000 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate
- Duration: 3-4 hours
- Terrain: Gentle, graded trail through forests and grasslands
- Features: Panoramic views of Bay Area, San Francisco, Golden Gate Bridge

Wildcat Gorge Trail:
- Distance: 5.5 miles out-and-back (11 miles round-trip)
- Elevation gain: 1,200 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate to challenging
- Duration: 4-5 hours
- Terrain: Lush canyon along Wildcat Creek with stream crossings
- Features: Wildlife viewing, ridge views of Bay Area

User's Choice: Tilden Regional Park for its panoramic views of the Bay Area.
