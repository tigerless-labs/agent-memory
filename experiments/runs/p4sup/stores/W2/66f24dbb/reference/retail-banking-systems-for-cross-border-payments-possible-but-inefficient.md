Retail banking systems *can* be used for cross-border payments, but are not efficient or cost-effective. Designed primarily for domestic transactions and lack infrastructure for international payments.\n\nFor cross-border: use SWIFT, correspondent banking, or specialized international payment processors for better speed, security, and efficiency.
