## Jamaican Dishes Recommended to Explore\n\n1. **Escovitch Fish** - Jamaican fried snapper topped with spicy pickled vegetable sauce (carrots, onion, bell peppers, vinegar)\n2. **Curry Goat** - Flavorful curry with tender goat meat and Jamaican curry spices\n3. **Festival and Fried Fish** - Festival (fried cornbread/dumpling) served with fried fish\n4. **Brown Stew Chicken** - Chicken marinated in sweet and savory sauce, cooked with onion, garlic, and bell peppers\n5. **Oxtail Stew** - Slow-cooked oxtail in rich savory stew with vegetables and beans\n6. **Ackee and Saltfish** - Jamaica's national dish, made with ackee fruit and salted codfish\n7. **Jerk Chicken** - Spicy grilled chicken marinated in Jamaican spice blend\n\nAlso worth exploring: Fish Curry (Trinidadian), Conch & Snapper Stew (Bahamian), Grilled Snapper with Mango Salsa, Fish and Fungi (Virgin Islands).
