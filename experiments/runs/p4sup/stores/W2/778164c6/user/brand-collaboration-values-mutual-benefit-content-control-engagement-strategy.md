When negotiating brand collaborations, prioritizes:

**Structural fairness:**
- Reciprocal promotion: brand promotes user's content on their channels (not just one-way)
- Clear scope: defined content types, frequency, posting times for maximum visibility
- Contract clarity: formal agreement outlining all terms and conditions

**Content control & integrity:**
- No unwanted modifications: brand cannot edit or alter user's content without approval
- Brand voice consistency: brand must adhere to user's tone, messaging, and aesthetic guidelines
- Content ownership: user retains ownership; brand use is limited to collaboration period

**Engagement & community:**
- Brand must actively respond to comments/messages on promoted content (not just post and ignore)
- Escalation process for complex interactions requiring user's direct involvement
- Engagement timeline: responses within 2-24 hours depending on agreement

**Measurement & accountability:**
- Clear KPIs: engagement rate, reach, click-through rate, conversion rate, ROI
- Regular check-ins to assess progress and adjust strategy
- Success benchmarks defined upfront
- Analytics shared and discussed

**Due diligence:**
- Research the brand thoroughly before committing
- Understand brand's goals, target audience, previous collaborations
- Evaluate if opportunity aligns with personal values and brand identity
- Willing to walk away if terms aren't favorable
