Has a rabbit named Benny. Benny needs:

**Toys for enrichment:**
- Chewing toys: untreated wood (apple, willow, birch), hay balls, cardboard rolls
- Interactive toys: tunnels, tents, food puzzle toys, bell toys
- Recommended brands: Kaytee, Oxbow, Pet Zone, Small Pet Select, Ware Manufacturing

**Hiding places:**
- Tunnels and tents (durable, ventilated)
- Cardboard boxes or wooden crates with multiple entrances
- Huts and houses (sturdy, easy-to-clean)
- Multiple hiding places help Benny feel secure and reduce stress

Rotate toys regularly to keep Benny engaged.
