Framework for drafting constitution covering:

- **Purpose and objectives**: group's mission and activities
- **Membership criteria**: who can join, rights and obligations
- **Governance structure**: decision-making bodies, roles and responsibilities
- **Meetings and decision-making**: meeting frequency, voting procedures
- **Finances**: funding sources (membership fees, etc), fund management rules
- **Amendments**: procedures for amending the constitution
- **Legal compliance**: ensure constitution meets UK legal requirements
