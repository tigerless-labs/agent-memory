Visited Chicago from approximately May 21, 2023. Stayed at Hilton Hotel downtown Chicago, 14th floor with a small balcony overlooking the city. Visited the fitness center on two occasions during the stay and was impressed by the range of equipment.

Activities planned/recommended:
- Intelligentsia Coffee (53 E Randolph St) for morning coffee
- Willis Tower Skydeck (103rd floor) for city views
- Lakefront Trail for running (18.5-mile scenic path along Lake Michigan)
- Chicago's First Lady Cruises architectural cruise along the Chicago River
- Art Institute of Chicago and other museums

Enjoyed the views from the room, especially at night.
