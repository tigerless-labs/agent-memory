Lighting for Home Office:

Types: natural light (position desk near window, perpendicular to minimize glare), task lighting (under-cabinet, desk lamps, floor lamps), ambient lighting (table lamps, floor lamps, string lights).

Color temperature: Warm (2700K-3000K) for calming, cool (5000K-6500K) for energizing. Use layered lighting combining multiple sources.

Pendant Light Selection: Consider size (scale to room/desk), shape (geometric for modern), material (metal, glass, acrylic), finish (matte/satin to avoid reflections), lighting direction (downward for task, upward for ambient), lumen output (300-400 per sq ft), dimmer compatibility.

Popular styles: Industrial-chic (exposed bulbs, metal), Scandinavian (simple geometric, natural materials), Minimalist (sleek, clean lines).

Recommended brands: Muuto, Lumens, Schoolhouse Electric.

Home Office Design Essentials:

Ergonomic: comfortable adjustable chair with lumbar support, desk with appropriate height and space, standing desk options.

Minimize distractions: quiet area away from bed/TV, room dividers, proper layout (corner desk, floating desk, built-in shelving, cozy nook).

Storage: file cabinets, shelving, desk organizers, cable management.

Atmosphere: calming colors (blue, green, yellow), plants for air purification, inspiring artwork, good acoustics with sound machines or acoustic panels.
