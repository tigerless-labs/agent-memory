Toronto cost of living estimates (2023):

**Rent** (monthly):
- Bachelor/1-bed shared: 1,200-1,600
- 1-bed high-rise: 1,600-2,000
- 2-bed: 1,800-2,500
- 3-bed: 2,500-3,500

**Food**:
- Groceries: 500-750/month
- Casual dining: 10-15 per meal
- Mid-range restaurants: 20-30 per meal
- Delivery/takeout: 10-20 per meal

**Transportation**:
- TTC monthly pass: 156.00 (adult)
- TTC single fare: 3.00 (cash), 2.90 (Presto card)
- Bike Share Toronto: 15-20/month
- Car ownership: significant gas, parking, maintenance costs

**Other monthly expenses**:
- Utilities (hydro, water, internet): 100-200
- Phone/internet: 50-100
- Entertainment/leisure: 200-500

**Income reference** (2022-2023):
- Minimum wage Ontario: 15.00/hour
- Entry-level jobs: 35,000-50,000/year

**Budgeting strategy**: Target 10-20 percent savings, consider shared housing to split costs, explore affordable housing programs through Toronto Affordable Housing Office and Rent Supplement Program
