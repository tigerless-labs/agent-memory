Actively seeks ways to reduce waste from coffee consumption rather than discarding grounds and filters. Interested in: composting grounds, using as natural pest repellent and plant fertilizer, creating natural dyes or skincare exfoliants. For filters: reusing as cleaning cloths, DIY air fresheners, fire starters, garden markers, and craft materials. Aligns with broader eco-conscious values.
