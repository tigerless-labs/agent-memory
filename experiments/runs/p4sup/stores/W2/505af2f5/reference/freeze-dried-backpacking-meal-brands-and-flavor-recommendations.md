Popular freeze-dried food brands: Mountain House (Chicken Teriyaki flavor), Backpacker's Pantry (Pad Thai), Good To-Go (Mushroom Risotto), AlpineAire (Spicy Black Bean Chili). All brands offer lightweight options suitable for backpacking. Tips: try samples before trip, check reviews, consider dietary needs, pack utensils. User has experience with freeze-dried meals from Yosemite trip.
