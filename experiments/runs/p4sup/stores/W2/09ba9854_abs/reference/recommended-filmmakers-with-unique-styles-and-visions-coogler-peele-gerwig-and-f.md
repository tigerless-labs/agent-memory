**Up-and-coming directors:**
- Ryan Coogler (Black Panther, Fruitvale Station)
- Jordan Peele (Get Out, Us)
- Greta Gerwig (Lady Bird, Little Women)

**Lesser-known filmmakers with distinct visions:**
1. Anna Rose Holmer — Coming-of-age drama; explores identity and conformity through a young female boxer (The Fits)
2. Barry Jenkins — Moonlight, If Beale Street Could Talk; explores race, sexuality, and family
3. Chloé Zhao — The Rider, Nomadland; soulful character explorations with poetic visual style
4. Céline Sciamma — Portrait of a Lady on Fire, Water Lilies; emotionally rich dramas exploring desire, identity, and coming-of-age
5. Robert Eggers — The Witch, The Lighthouse; horror films with distinct visual styles exploring isolation, madness, and the supernatural
