## Steps

1. Create GCS bucket in Google Cloud Storage Console with desired location/class
2. Install Mi Home app, sign in with Xiaomi account
3. Connect Xiaomi 360 camera to Wi-Fi and pair with Mi Home app
4. Enable Cloud Storage in camera settings; sign in with Google account
5. Configure GCS settings: select bucket, choose recording duration/resolution
6. Camera automatically records and uploads to GCS bucket

## Requirements
- Google Cloud Platform account (incurs storage costs)
- Mi Home app
- Xiaomi 360 camera
