Prioritizes luggage accommodation when selecting airport transportation, but remains cost-conscious. When planning airport transfers, researches multiple available options (train, bus, taxi, private car services) and asks hotels for local recommendations on balancing convenience with pricing.

Example: For Tokyo airport transfer (2023), evaluated Narita Express, Keisei Skyliner, Tokyo Monorail, buses, taxis, and private car services—weighing luggage capacity, journey time, and fares together.
