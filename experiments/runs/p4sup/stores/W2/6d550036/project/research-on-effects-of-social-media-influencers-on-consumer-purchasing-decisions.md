Conducting academic research investigating how social media influencers affect consumer purchasing decisions. Research is being expanded from initial poster presentation at a consumer psychology conference.

**Focus areas for research expansion:**
- Credibility and trust factors in influencer-consumer relationships
- Authenticity: how influencers establish and demonstrate authenticity
- Social identity theory: how shared identity between influencer and audience drives effectiveness

**Key research insights collected:**
- 86% of consumers say authenticity is important when deciding what brands to support
- 61% of consumers more likely to trust influencer recommendations if they believe influencer is authentic  
- 70% of consumers believe authentic brands more reliable; 63% believe more trustworthy
