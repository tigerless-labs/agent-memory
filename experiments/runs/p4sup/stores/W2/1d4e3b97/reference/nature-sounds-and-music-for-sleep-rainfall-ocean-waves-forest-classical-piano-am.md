Nature sounds recommended for sleep: rainfall, ocean waves, forest sounds (birdsong, rustling leaves), babbling brook, wind chimes.

Music genres: Classical (Mozart, Chopin, Bach), instrumental piano, acoustic guitar, ambient music, new age music (flutes, harps, gentle percussion).

Recommended artists and albums: Brian Eno - Ambient 1 Music for Airports, Max Richter - Sleep (8-hour album), Ludovico Einaudi - Nightbook, Enya - Watermark, Steve Reich - Music for 18 Musicians.

Available on: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, iTunes, Google Play Music.
