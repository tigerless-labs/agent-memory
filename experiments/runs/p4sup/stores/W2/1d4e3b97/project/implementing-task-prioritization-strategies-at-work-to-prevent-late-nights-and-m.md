Methods adopted:\n- Eisenhower Matrix: categorize tasks by urgency/importance quadrants\n- Prioritize by impact: identify tasks with greatest effect\n- Break down large tasks into manageable chunks\n- Use task lists and cross off completed items\n- Avoid multitasking - focus on one task at a time\n- Set realistic deadlines with 10-20% buffer for interruptions\n- Learn to say no to non-aligned or impossible tasks\n- Time-blocking: schedule specific times for tasks\n- Take regular breaks to avoid burnout\n- Review and adjust priorities regularly\n\nGoal: Manage workload so work commitments don't interfere with sleep schedule (target 10 pm weekday bedtime) and screen time reduction goals.
