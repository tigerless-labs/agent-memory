Genres suitable for dynamic, emotionally-driven instrumental compositions:\n\n- **Film Score/Soundtrack**: Purpose-built for emotional enhancement of visual media\n- **Classical**: Inherently epic and dynamic, wide instrumental range\n- **Ambient & Cinematic Electronic**: Instrumental, creates epicness through texture and space\n- **Post-rock**: Guitar-based melodies with complex rhythms, strong emotional impact\n- **Instrumental Hip Hop**: Heavy basslines, dynamic instrumentation, epic beats\n- **Jazz & Jazz Fusion**: Improvisational, conveys emotion through instrument interplay, wide dynamic range\n\nThese genres share characteristics of evolution, emotional depth, and orchestral or complex arrangements without relying on vocals to carry meaning.
