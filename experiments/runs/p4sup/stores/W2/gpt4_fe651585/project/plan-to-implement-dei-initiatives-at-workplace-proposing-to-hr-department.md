Decided to suggest systemic anti-racism and discrimination measures to HR:

- Hire diverse workforce with inclusive hiring
- Provide cultural competency training
- Develop and enforce anti-discrimination policies
- Conduct regular diversity assessments
- Encourage open communication
- Offer mentorship and sponsorship programs
- Hold leadership accountable
- Report transparently on progress

Planning to explore resources:
- DEI consultants
- Online courses on cultural competency and diversity
- DEI toolkits
- Diversity surveys and assessments
- Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
- Diversity and inclusion training programs

Aware this requires ongoing cultural change and collective effort, but committed to making positive impact.
