Research into song composition that merges:\n- Orchestral pop instrumentation (strings, horns, woodwinds with synths)\n- Adult contemporary vocal treatments and emotional focus\n- EDM context and production techniques\n- Dynamic, constantly evolving structure rather than traditional verse-chorus\n- Electronic elements (synthesizers, samplers, processors) alongside orchestral instruments\n\nSong structure template explored: intro → verse → pre-chorus → chorus → bridge → outro, with focus on building momentum and emotional arc. Interested in how this applies to instrumental or heavily instrumental pieces.
