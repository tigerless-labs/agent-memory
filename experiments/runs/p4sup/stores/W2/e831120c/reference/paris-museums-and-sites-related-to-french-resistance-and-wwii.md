## Museums\n- Musée de la Résistance — dedicated to French Resistance history\n- Musée Jean Moulin — dedicated to Jean Moulin, key Resistance figure who united various groups\n- Musée de l'Armée — extensive section on French Resistance and Liberation of Paris\n- Musée Carnavalet — includes section on Occupation and Resistance\n- Musée de la Contrefaçon — quirky museum on counterfeiting history\n\n## Memorials & Sites\n- Mont Valérien Memorial — honors Resistance fighters executed by Nazis\n- Cimetière de Montparnasse — final resting place of many Resistance fighters including Jean Moulin\n- Place de la République — monument to Resistance and Liberation\n- Archeological Crypt of Notre-Dame — Gallo-Roman artifacts and history\n- Catacombs of Paris — underground ossuary with remains of millions of Parisians\n\n## Tours\n- Paris Resistance Tour (2 hours)\n- WWII in Paris Tour (3 hours)\n- French Resistance and Liberation Tour (4 hours)
