Building a gift basket for cousin with wine theme, ~$40 budget.

**Components planned:**
- Wine bottle ($10-30 range) — recommendations: Chardonnay, Pinot Grigio, Merlot, Sauvignon Blanc, or Rosé
- Gourmet cheese
- Wine opener
- Cheese crackers or breadsticks
- Gift card to local wine and cheese shop

**Wine brand suggestions:** Apothic, La Crema, Kim Crawford, Cupcake Vineyards, Masciarelli

**Pairing notes:**
- Chardonnay with Brie/Camembert
- Pinot Grigio with Goat Cheese/Feta
- Merlot with Cheddar/Gouda/Provolone
- Sauvignon Blanc with Blue Cheese/Parmesan
- Rosé with soft-ripened cheeses
