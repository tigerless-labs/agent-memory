**Truthspeak: The True Meaning of Five Key Christian Words Distorted Through Religious Newspeak**

By: Michael D. Halsey
ISBN-10: 0979963729
ISBN-13: 978-0979963728

The book explores how five important Christian words have been distorted from their original biblical meanings through 'religious newspeak' and contemporary misinterpretation.

**Five words covered:**
- Grace
- Faith  
- Sanctification
- Righteousness
- Salvation

The book aims to help readers gain a deeper understanding of these concepts and their true meanings in Christian theology.
