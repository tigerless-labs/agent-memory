Wine types and pairings for cheese gifts:

**Wine varieties:**
- Chardonnay: buttery, oaky; pairs with Brie or Camembert
- Pinot Grigio: crisp, refreshing; pairs with Goat Cheese or Feta
- Merlot: smooth; pairs with Cheddar, Gouda, or Provolone
- Sauvignon Blanc: zesty, citrusy; pairs with Blue Cheese or Parmesan
- Rosé: dry; pairs with soft-ripened cheeses like Brie or Chevre

**Recommended brands:**
- Apothic
- La Crema
- Kim Crawford
- Cupcake Vineyards
- Masciarelli

**Price range:** 10-30 dollars per bottle
