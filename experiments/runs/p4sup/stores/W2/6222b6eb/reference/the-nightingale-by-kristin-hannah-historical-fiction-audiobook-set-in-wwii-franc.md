**The Nightingale** by Kristin Hannah (2015)\n\n**Narrator:** Polly Stone\n**Runtime:** Approximately 17 hours 30 minutes\n\n**Setting:** France during World War II\n\n**Plot:** Follows two sisters, Vianne and Isabelle Rossignol, as they navigate the German occupation of France during WWII. Vianne, the older sister, tries to maintain normalcy while becoming involved in the French Resistance. Isabelle, the younger sister, is rebellious and takes on dangerous resistance missions. The novel explores their relationship, sacrifices, and bravery through the war.\n\n**Themes:** Sisterhood, bravery, survival, love, sacrifice, women's resilience during wartime\n\n**Recommendation:** Highly acclaimed historical fiction with engaging narration. New York Times bestseller.
