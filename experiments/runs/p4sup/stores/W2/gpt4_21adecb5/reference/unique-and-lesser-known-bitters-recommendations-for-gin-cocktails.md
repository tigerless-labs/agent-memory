Collection of unique bitters to explore with gin:

**Recommended bitters:**
1. **Sarsaparilla Bitters:** Sweet, slightly spicy, herbal. Pair with London Dry Gin in citrus-forward cocktails.
2. **Yuzu Bitters:** Japanese citrus, bright, citrusy, slightly sweet.
3. **Cardamom Bitters:** Sweet, spicy, herbal. Works with botanical-forward gin and honey/sugar sweeteners.
4. **Cherry Blossom Bitters:** Delicate, floral. Pairs with Plymouth Gin and fruit-forward cocktails.
5. **Smoked Hickory Bitters:** Smoky, savory. Use with robust, juniper-forward gin (Smoked Gin Old Fashioned, Gin-Gin Mule).
6. **Celery Bitters:** Fresh, herbal. Complements gin's botanicals in classic G&T or Celery-Gin Fizz.
7. **Grapefruit-Scallion Bitters:** Bright grapefruit with pungent scallions (Grapefruit-Gin Fizz, savory Gin-Grapefruit Martini).
8. **Black Truffle Bitters:** Luxurious, earthy. Use with rich, complex gin (Black Truffle Negroni, Gin-Truffle Martini).

**Recommended brands:**
- The Bitter Truth
- Bittercube
- The Bitter End
- Fee Brothers
- House of Botanicals

**Dosing tip:** Start with 2-3 dashes and adjust to taste to balance flavors.
