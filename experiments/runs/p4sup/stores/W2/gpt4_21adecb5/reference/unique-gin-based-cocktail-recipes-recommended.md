Collection of gin cocktails with creative flavor combinations:

**Exotic ingredients:**
1. **Saffron Gin Fizz:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz saffron-infused simple syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup, egg white, dash of orange bitters. Dry shake first, then add ice. Garnish with saffron thread.
2. **Gin-Ginger-Kaffir Lime Collins:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz ginger liqueur (e.g., Domaine de Canton), 1 oz kaffir lime leaf-infused simple syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, splash soda water. Muddle leaves to release oils.
3. **Cucumber-Rose-Gin Refresher:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz cucumber juice, 1 oz rose syrup, 1 oz lime juice, splash soda water. Garnish with cucumber slice and rose petal.
4. **Smoked Cherry Gin Sour:** 2 oz gin, 3/4 oz smoked cherry puree, 3/4 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup, dash Angostura bitters.
5. **Persimmon-Gin Spritz:** 2 oz gin, 2 oz persimmon puree, 1 oz Prosecco, 1 oz lemon-lime soda. Garnish with persimmon slice and rosemary.

**More accessible:**
1. **Cucumber-Lime Refresher:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz lime juice, 1 oz cucumber juice, 1/2 oz simple syrup.
2. **Blackberry-Basil Martini:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz blackberry puree, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz simple syrup. Muddle berries and basil.
3. **Grapefruit-Rosemary Fizz:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz grapefruit juice, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup, top with soda water.
4. **Spiced Apple Cider Gin Toddy:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz apple cider, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz cinnamon syrup. Serve warm.
