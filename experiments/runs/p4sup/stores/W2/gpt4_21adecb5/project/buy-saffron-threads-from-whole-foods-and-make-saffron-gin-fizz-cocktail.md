Plan to purchase saffron threads from Whole Foods to make Saffron Gin Fizz cocktail.

**Recipe:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz saffron-infused simple syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup, egg white, dash of orange bitters. Dry shake without ice first, then add ice and shake. Strain into Collins glass, garnish with saffron thread.

**Saffron infusion:** Steep 1/2 tsp saffron threads in 1 cup hot water for 10-15 minutes.

**What to look for when buying saffron:** Deep red-orange color, strong sweet earthy aroma, whole threads (avoid powder), prefer Spanish origin (Crocus sativus.
