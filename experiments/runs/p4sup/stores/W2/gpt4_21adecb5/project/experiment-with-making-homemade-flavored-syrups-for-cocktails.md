Plan to make custom flavored syrups to use in cocktails and elevate drinks with personal touches.

**Base syrup options:** Simple syrup (1:1 sugar and water) or rich syrup (2:1 sugar and water, thicker and more concentrated).

**Infusion methods:**
- Hot infusion: steep ingredient in 180-190°F water for 10-30 minutes (best for delicate flavors like fruits and herbs)
- Cold infusion: steep in cold water for several hours or overnight (better for robust flavors like spices and roots)

**General ratio:** Start with 1 part flavor ingredient to 2 parts syrup.

**Process:** Strain through fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth, filter through coffee filter if clearer syrup desired, taste and adjust.

**Storage:** Airtight container in refrigerator, lasts 2-4 weeks.

**Popular varieties:** Fruit (raspberry, strawberry, orange), herbal (mint, basil, lemongrass), spiced (cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger), tea (Earl Grey, green tea, chai), nut (almond, hazelnut, pecan).

**For Cardamom Old Fashioned:** Try cardamom-honey syrup by infusing crushed cardamom pods in hot honey syrup (1:1) for 10-15 minutes.
