Plant selections: Java Moss and Anacharis for 20-gallon tank with 10 neon tetras, 5 golden honey gouramis, 1 pleco catfish. Java Moss: low-moderate light (1-2W/gal), 72-82F, pH 5.5-8.5, attach to rocks/driftwood, gentle flow, fertilize weekly. Compatible with fish - hideout for tetras, gouramis may nibble, pleco may graze occasionally. Anacharis: moderate-high light (2-4W/gal), 65-85F, pH 6.5-8.5, floating plant, moderate-strong flow, fertilize weekly. Provides food for pleco, tetras/gouramis may nibble. Both sensitive to copper medications. Clean decaying matter regularly. Selected for ease of care and water quality improvement.
