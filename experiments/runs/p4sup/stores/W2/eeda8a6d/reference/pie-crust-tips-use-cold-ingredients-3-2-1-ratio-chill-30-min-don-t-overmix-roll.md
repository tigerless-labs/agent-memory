**Homemade pie crust techniques:**

**Key principles:**
- Use cold ingredients (butter, water, flour)
- Follow 3:2:1 ratio: flour to fat to water
- Don't overmix — mix just until dough comes together
- Chill dough at least 30 minutes before rolling (allows gluten to relax)
- Roll gently to avoid overdeveloping gluten (aim for ~1/8 inch thickness)

**Baking:**
- Bake crust until lightly golden (15-20 minutes for single-crust pie)
- Use pie shield or weights to prevent shrinking or burning

**Helpful tools:**
- Food processor can efficiently cut butter into flour
- Refrigerate if dough becomes warm or sticky

**Make-ahead option:**
- Make dough up to 2 days ahead and refrigerate
- Can freeze up to 2 months
