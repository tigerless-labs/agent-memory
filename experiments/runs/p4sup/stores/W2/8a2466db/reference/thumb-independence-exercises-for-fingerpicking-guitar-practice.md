Five targeted exercises to develop independent thumb movement separate from fingers:

1. **Thumb-only exercises** — Play steady rhythm with thumb on low E or A string, fingers lifted. Start slow (5-10 min), gradually increase tempo.

2. **Thumb-finger opposition** — Alternate thumb and index finger (then middle, ring) on different strings, focusing on independence. 5-10 min per finger.

3. **Thumb-glissando exercise** — Slide thumb up/down string without lifting, keeping fingers still and relaxed. 5-10 min, gradually increase distance and speed.

4. **Thumb-tap exercise** — Tap thumb on string in staccato rhythm, fingers still. Start slow, 5-10 min, increase tempo gradually.

5. **Metronome practice** — Perform all exercises with metronome at 60-80 BPM, playing thumb on beat and fingers on off-beat.

Key principles: regular daily practice even if brief, start slow and increase gradually, prioritize relaxation and control over speed, be patient with development.
