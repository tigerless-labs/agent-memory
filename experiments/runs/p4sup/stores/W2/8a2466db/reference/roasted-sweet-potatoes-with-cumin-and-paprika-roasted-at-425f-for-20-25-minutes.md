**Simple Roasted Sweet Potatoes Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 2-3 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (about 1-inch cubes)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
- Diced onions and minced garlic (optional additions)

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a large bowl, toss sweet potato cubes with olive oil, salt, black pepper, cumin, and smoked paprika until evenly coated.
3. Spread in single layer on parchment-lined baking sheet.
4. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender and caramelized, flipping halfway through.
5. Let cool slightly before adding to bowl.

Tips: Use high-starch sweet potatoes for fluffiest interior and crispiest exterior. Cut into wedges or thin slices for different texture. Add diced onions and minced garlic for extra flavor. Add cayenne pepper or red pepper flakes for spicy kick. Roast at 450°F for crispier exterior.
