**Simple Grilled Chicken Breast Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro leaves, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (about 400°F/200°C).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lime juice, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
3. Place chicken breasts in shallow dish and brush marinade on both sides.
4. Marinate for at least 15-20 minutes, or up to 30 minutes in refrigerator.
5. Grill chicken for 5-7 minutes per side, until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
6. Let rest a few minutes before slicing thinly against the grain.
7. Add to bowl, garnished with chopped fresh cilantro if desired.

Tips: Use meat thermometer for food safety. Can substitute lime juice with lemon or orange juice. Add diced bell peppers, onions, or mushrooms to grill with chicken. Add cayenne pepper or red pepper flakes for spicy kick. Marinate 2-3 hours or overnight for more flavor.
