Dermelect skincare/nail care brand influencer marketing strategy. Past campaigns: #DermelectDetox (Instagram, YouTube blogs), #DermelectNailRevolution (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), #DermelectSelfCare (Instagram, YouTube). Influencer tiers: micro (10K-50K), mid (50K-250K), macro (250K-1M). Compensation: product gifting, monetary (individually negotiated), affiliate program with promo codes, performance bonuses for KPIs. Geographic preference: US-based. Ideal traits: authenticity, engaging personality, high engagement rate, relevant niche (beauty/skincare/self-care), creative storyteller, professionalism, brand alignment (cruelty-free/eco-friendly). Suggested influencers: Susan Yara (@susanyara), Hyram Yarbro (@skincarebyhyram), Kelli Marissa (@kellimarissa), Chriselle Lim (@chrisellelim), Liah Yoo (@liahyoo). Example promo code: INFLUENCER10 (10% off).
