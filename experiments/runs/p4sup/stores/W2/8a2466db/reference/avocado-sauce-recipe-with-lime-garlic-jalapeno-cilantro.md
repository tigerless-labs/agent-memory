**Avocado Sauce Recipe**

Ingredients:
- 3 ripe avocados, peeled and pitted
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. In a blender or food processor, combine the avocado, red onion, jalapeño, lime juice, garlic, salt, and black pepper.
2. Blend until smooth and creamy, scraping down sides as needed.
3. Taste and adjust seasoning if desired.
4. Stir in chopped cilantro if using.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

Tips: Use ripe avocados for best flavor. Adjust heat with more/less jalapeño. Mix in Greek yogurt or sour cream for tangier sauce. Use as dip or topping for tacos, grilled meats, or vegetables.
