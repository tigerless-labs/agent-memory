A decadent pasta sauce combining sautéed mushrooms with cream, truffle oil, and Parmesan.

**Mushroom Preparation**: Use a mix of cremini, shiitake, and button mushrooms. Pat dry to enable browning. Use large hot skillet over medium-high heat, don't overcrowd. Sear 2-3 minutes per side until golden, then stir and cook 5-7 minutes until tender and caramelized. Add aromatics (onions, garlic, thyme) during cooking.

**Building the Sauce**: After mushrooms caramelize, add garlic and thyme (1 min). Deglaze with dry white wine, reduce almost completely. Add heavy cream and bring to simmer. Reduce heat and simmer 2-3 minutes. Stir in 1-2 teaspoons truffle oil toward end (high heat destroys flavor). Let infuse 1-2 minutes. Season with salt, pepper, and Parmesan.

**Truffle Oil Tips**: Use high-quality oil with real truffle essence. Start with small amount (potent). Add toward end to preserve flavor. Balance with cream, Parmesan, and herbs. Taste as you go.

Pairs well with garlic bread and proteins like grilled chicken, scallops, shrimp, or bacon.
