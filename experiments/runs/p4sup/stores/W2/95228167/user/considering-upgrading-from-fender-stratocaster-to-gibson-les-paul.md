Exploring upgrading to a Gibson Les Paul from a Fender Stratocaster. Interested in the warmer, fuller tone and sustain that the Les Paul offers, particularly for heavier music and open tuning experimentation. Aware of the differences: Les Paul has humbucker pickups (vs Stratocaster's single-coils), set-in neck, heavier weight (9-10 lbs), and different tonal characteristics.
