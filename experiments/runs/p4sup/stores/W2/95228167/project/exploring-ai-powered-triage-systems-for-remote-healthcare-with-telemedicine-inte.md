Project direction: develop AI-powered triage systems to support telemedicine initiatives in resource-constrained areas where access to specialized medical professionals is limited.

**Key goals:**
- Enable remote diagnosis and triage in underserved areas
- Reduce healthcare costs through automation and remote access
- Improve patient outcomes through early detection

**Integration scope:**
- Integrate AI triage system with existing telemedicine platforms
- Seamless data exchange between systems via APIs
- Real-time triage scoring and patient prioritization

**Critical data requirements:**
- EHRs (electronic health records)
- Vital signs and medical data
- Patient demographics and medical history
- Clinical notes and treatment plans

**Key challenges to address:**
- Data quality and standardization in remote settings
- Data security and HIPAA compliance
- Algorithmic bias and accuracy
- Clinical validation in real-world remote environments
- Interoperability between systems
- Workflow integration with existing clinical processes
