**Recommended resources for learning about AI developments:**

1. **The AI Alignment Podcast** - podcast covering latest AI research, applications, and implications
2. **AI in Industry** - blog by Emerj with in-depth articles on AI applications across industries
3. **The AI Now Institute** - research center on social implications of AI; features research papers and event recordings
4. **Machine Learning Mastery** - comprehensive online resource for ML/AI tutorials and articles
5. **KDNuggets** - popular AI/ML news site with articles, tutorials, industry updates
6. **AI for Everyone** - free online course by Andrew Ng covering AI basics and applications
7. **arXiv** - repository of preprints including AI/ML research papers
8. **Conferences & meetups** - NeurIPS, IJCAI, and local meetups for networking with experts
9. **AI Subreddit** - active community discussing AI topics and research
10. **Online courses** - Coursera, edX, Udemy offer AI/ML courses from top universities

**User interest:** Particularly interested in AI Alignment Podcast and AI in Industry blog by Emerj
