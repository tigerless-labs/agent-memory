Considering purchasing the Chase Sapphire Preferred card for travel rewards. Key features: 60,000 bonus points after 4000 dollars spend in 3 months; 2X points on travel and dining, 1X on other purchases; 95 dollar annual fee; no foreign transaction fees; 25 percent point boost when redeeming through Chase portal (60,000 points equals 750 dollars travel value); travel and purchase insurance included; requires 700+ FICO score. Points can be redeemed through Chase portal, transferred to airline and hotel loyalty programs (United, American, Delta, Marriott, Hyatt, others), or booked through travel partners.
