Private guided tour platforms for Paris:

**Viator** (established, well-reviewed)
- Large network of local guides
- Customized tours available
- 10 percent first-time customer discount
- Price Match Guarantee
- Package deals and bundles available

**GetYourGuide** (established, well-reviewed)
- Connects travelers with local guides
- Variety of tour types (classic, food, art, etc.)
- 10 percent discount for new customers
- Best Price Guarantee
- Regular special deals and discounts

**Paris Walks** (specialty)
- Specializes in guided walking tours
- Expert guides passionate about history, art, culture
- Private tour options available

**Context Travel** (specialist)
- Private tours led by experts in art, history, architecture
- In-depth cultural and historical focus
- Higher-end option

**Local Operators**
- Can search TripAdvisor, Google, Yelp for local Paris tour companies
- Often provide authentic experiences and support local businesses

**Explore Earth** (UK-based)
- Customized private tours in European cities
- Basic website, limited online presence
- Requires direct contact for quotes
- Recommend checking reviews before booking
