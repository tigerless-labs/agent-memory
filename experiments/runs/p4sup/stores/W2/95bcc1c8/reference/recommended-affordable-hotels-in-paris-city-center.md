Recommended options for affordable Paris city center accommodation:

1. Ibis Paris Gare de Lyon Reuilly (12th arr.): 3-star, 80 euros/night, 10-min walk to Gare de Lyon, buffet breakfast, 24-hour bar

2. Hotel Eiffel Kensington (15th arr.): 2-star, 60 euros/night, 10-min walk to Eiffel Tower, continental breakfast

3. Hotel des Archives (3rd arr., Marais): 2-star, 70 euros/night, stylish rooms, close to Picasso Museum and Seine

4. Hotel Le Walt (9th arr.): 2-star, 60 euros/night, 5-min walk to Opera Garnier, continental breakfast

5. Hostel Les Piaules (11th arr.): Budget, 30 euros/night for dorm beds, 10-min walk to Place de la Republique

Also recommended neighborhoods: Latin Quarter (5th-6th), Le Marais (3rd-4th), Montmartre (9th-18th)
