Madewell handbag options recommended as quality mid-range alternative:

**Transport Tote**: Canvas construction with vegan leather trim, water-resistant coating, structured tote style, approximately 50-70 dollars. Care: spot clean stains with damp cloth, use fabric conditioner every 2-3 months, store away from direct sunlight.

**Mini Transport Crossbody**: Pebbled leather construction, compact crossbody style, approximately 50-70 dollars. Care: condition regularly with leather conditioner, protect from water/moisture, avoid extreme temperatures, handle gently to avoid scratches.

Both bags balance quality and affordability, with classic timeless designs compared to fast-fashion alternatives.
