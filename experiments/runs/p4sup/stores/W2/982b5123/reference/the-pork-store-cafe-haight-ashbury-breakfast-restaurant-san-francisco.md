Casual, no-frills breakfast and brunch spot in the Haight-Ashbury neighborhood of San Francisco.\n\n**Famous dishes:**\n- Pork Store Sandwich (scrambled eggs, bacon, cheddar on sourdough)\n- Omelets, pancakes, waffles, breakfast burritos\n\n**Hours:**\n- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 3:00 PM\n- Saturday and Sunday: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM\n\n**Note:** Serves breakfast all day during operating hours.
