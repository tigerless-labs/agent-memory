Must-see scenic drives around the San Francisco Bay Area:\n\n1. **Pacific Coast Highway (Hwy 1):** South to Santa Cruz (1.5 hours). Stops: Half Moon Bay, Pescadero State Beach, Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk.\n\n2. **17-Mile Drive:** Through Pebble Beach, coastal scenery, Lone Cypress tree landmark.\n\n3. **Golden Gate Bridge to Muir Woods and Sausalito:** North across Golden Gate Bridge. Muir Woods National Monument (30 min) - towering redwoods. Sausalito (15 min) - charming waterfront town with Bay views.\n\n4. **Napa Valley Wine Country:** East from SF (1 hour). Rolling hills, vineyards, towns like St. Helena and Calistoga. Wine tours and gourmet dining.\n\n5. **Skyline Boulevard (Hwy 35):** South from SF through Santa Cruz Mountains. Stunning views of Bay, Pacific Ocean, hills. Stops: Wunderlich County Park, Thomas Fogarty Winery.\n\n6. **Mount Tamalpais:** North from SF (1 hour). Mount Tamalpais State Park summit with panoramic Bay Area views. Hiking, biking, scenic drive.
