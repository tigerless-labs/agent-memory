User suspects they have a pollen allergy (hay fever) based on experiencing symptoms including sneezing, runny/stuffy nose, itchy nose/throat/ears, watery/red/swollen eyes, cough, postnasal drip, fatigue, and headaches.

Interested in managing through natural remedies and lifestyle changes rather than just medications. Specific interests include:
- Using air filters (home, HVAC, portable air purifier)
- Anti-inflammatory diet (omega-3s, fruits, vegetables)
- Staying hydrated
- Saline nasal rinses
- Avoiding outdoor activities on high pollen count days
- Keeping indoor air clean (washing clothes/hair, closing windows/doors)

Plans to consult with doctor for proper diagnosis and medical guidance.
