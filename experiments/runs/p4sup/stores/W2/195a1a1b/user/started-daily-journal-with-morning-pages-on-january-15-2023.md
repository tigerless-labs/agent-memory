Established a daily journaling practice starting January 15, 2023, filling it quickly with consistent writing. Uses the journal for:

- **Morning pages**: Free-form writing to clear mind and let thoughts flow naturally (sometimes with specific topics)
- **Occasional poetry**: Creative expression through the journal
- **Reflection**: Processing emotions, reflecting on experiences, and tracking progress on writing projects and goals

Finds morning pages particularly valuable for emotional processing and getting thoughts out of her head and onto paper.
