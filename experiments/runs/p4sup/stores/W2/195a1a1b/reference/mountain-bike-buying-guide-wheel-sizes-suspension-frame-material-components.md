**Key factors when purchasing a mountain bike:**

**Wheel Size:**
- 26" (older standard, tight trails)
- 27.5" (versatile, all-around)
- 29" (fast, efficient, stable)

**Suspension Types:**
- Hardtail (front suspension only): smooth trails, cross-country
- Full-suspension (front and rear): technical trails, rough terrain

**Frame Materials:**
- Aluminum (lightweight, affordable)
- Carbon fiber (lightweight, high-performance)
- Steel (durable, comfortable)

**Components:**
- Gearing: 1x or 2x chainring for wide range
- Brakes: Disc brakes mandatory (mechanical or hydraulic)
- Tires: Aggressive tread for technical, smoother for cross-country; width 1.9-2.5"
- Dropper post: Lowers saddle 100-150mm for technical descents and control

**Beginner Recommendations:**
- Hardtail or entry-level full-suspension
- Forgiving geometry: relaxed head angle, lower standover height, shorter top tube
- Always test ride before purchasing
- Visit local bike shops for expert guidance and sizing

**Dropper Post Benefits:**
- Increased control and confidence on technical terrain
- Improved clearance from obstacles
- Enhanced descending capabilities
- Efficient climbing when raised
- Convenient remote lever for on-the-fly adjustment
- Requires regular maintenance
