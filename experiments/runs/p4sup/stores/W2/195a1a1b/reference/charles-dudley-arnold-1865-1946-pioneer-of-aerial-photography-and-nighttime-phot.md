American photographer (1865-1946) who pioneered innovative techniques in aerial and nighttime photography.

**Aerial photography:**
- One of the first photographers to experiment with cameras mounted on balloons and airplanes (1890s)
- Early 1900s: began using airplanes as photography platform
- Worked with pioneering aviators including Orville Wright
- Used various cameras and techniques to capture landscape patterns and textures from above
- Photographs characterized by innovative composition and unique perspectives

**Nighttime photography:**
- Pioneered techniques for low-light photography when most work was daylight-only
- Used artificial light sources: electric lights, gas lamps, torches
- Developed special equipment like flash powder and magnesium wire for bright bursts of light
- Captured urban scenes (city streets, buildings) with dramatic, moody, atmospheric effects
- Work characterized by sharp light-dark contrasts and sense of mystery

**Legacy:** Established aerial photography as important new genre; influenced photographers worldwide. Nighttime photographs recognized as important historical documents and continue to inspire modern photographers.
