American photographer (1864-1952) and one of the first women to establish a professional photography career in the United States.

**Career:**
- Born into prominent Virginia family
- Opened studio in Washington, D.C. in 1894
- Established reputation as talented and innovative photographer
- Worked extensively as portrait photographer

**Notable contributions:**
- Pioneered architectural photography, documenting America's historic buildings
- Photographed colonial-era structures and other historic architecture
- Subjects included Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, Alice Roosevelt Longworth

**Style:** Portraits characterized by elegance, simplicity, and attention to detail.

**Philosophy:** Believed photography could be a powerful tool for social change; committed to raising awareness of important social issues.

**Legacy:** Recognized for technical excellence, artistic innovation, and lasting influence on American photography development.
