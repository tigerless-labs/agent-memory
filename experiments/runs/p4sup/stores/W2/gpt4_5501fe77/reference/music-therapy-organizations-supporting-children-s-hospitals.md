Organizations that provide music therapy services to children's hospitals:\n\n1. **Music Therapy Inc.** - Provides music therapy services to children's hospitals, hospices, and other healthcare settings\n2. **The Children's Music Fund** - Provides music therapy to children with serious illnesses, disabilities, or in need of emotional support\n3. **The Nathaniel Anthony Ayers Foundation** - Provides music therapy and education to children and adults with mental and physical disabilities\n4. **Melodic Caring Project** - Provides music therapy and live music performances to children's hospitals and hospices\n5. **Songs for Kids** - Provides music therapy and songwriting programs to children's hospitals, schools, and communities\n\nThese organizations often work with healthcare providers to develop and implement music therapy programs, offer training for music therapists, and advocate for the benefits of music therapy in healthcare.
