Three evidence-based programs that successfully incorporate family-oriented approaches in their empowerment program design:

1. **Strong Families, Safe Communities (SFSC)** — Evidence-based initiative for low-income families affected by chronic poverty. Emphasizes comprehensive family-based interventions including parent education, family support, and family book groups.

2. **Parent-Child Home Program (PCHP)** — Works with families in poverty to support early childhood development and school readiness. Trains home visitors to work with families twice weekly in their homes, delivering educational materials and supporting parent-child interaction.

3. **Strengthening Families Program: For Parents and Youth 10-14 (SFP 10-14)** — Family-based preventive intervention for youth aged 10-14. Focuses on family dynamics, social competencies, and relationship enhancement.

These programs demonstrate how integrating family-oriented approaches, parent education, and community support creates effective empowerment interventions.
