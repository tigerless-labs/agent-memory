**Sample two-week content calendar structure** with mix of content themes designed to drive engagement and followers:

**Week 1 content themes:**
- Mon: Instagram Story—morning routine
- Tue: TikTok Video—hobby tips (e.g., photography)
- Wed: Facebook Post—TV show update ("What I'm watching this week")
- Thu: Twitter Tweet—movie/TV show fun fact
- Fri: Instagram Story—weekend plans preview
- Sat: TikTok Video—"A day in the life" vlog
- Sun: Facebook Post—weekly wrap-up of favorite moments

**Week 2 content themes:**
- Mon: Twitter Tweet—TV show/movie quote
- Tue: Instagram Post—hobby-related photo
- Wed: TikTok Video—morning routine/get-ready
- Thu: Facebook Post—behind-the-scenes daily life
- Fri: Instagram Story—new hobby/interest preview
- Sat: TikTok Video—Q&A with followers
- Sun: Facebook Post—personal achievement/milestone

**Content mix strategy:**
- Behind-the-scenes content (builds audience connection)
- Hobby-related content (attracts like-minded followers)
- TV/movie updates (engages around shared interests)
- Interactive content (Q&A, sneak peeks, prompts—encourages engagement)
- Personal content (builds personality and connection)

**Template columns:** Platform | Date | Content Theme | Post Type | Time

**Additional growth tactics:**
- Use relevant hashtags on Instagram and TikTok
- Engage actively with audience (respond to comments/DMs)
- Collaborate with other creators/influencers
- Run contests or giveaways
- Share user-generated content (UGC)
