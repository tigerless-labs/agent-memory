Instagram: 12pm-1pm and 5pm-6pm are peak hours. Best days: Tue-Thu. Facebook: 12pm-3pm peak hours, best days Thu-Fri-Sun. TikTok: post during after-school, lunch breaks, evenings. Use Instagram Stories stickers (polls, quizzes, question sticker), Facebook Groups for community building, user-generated content, and consistent posting schedules.
