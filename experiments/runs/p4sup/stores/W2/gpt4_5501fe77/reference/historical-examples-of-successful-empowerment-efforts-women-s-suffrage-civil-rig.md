Historical empowerment efforts with significant social justice impact: (1) Women's Suffrage Movement — secured 19th Amendment (1920), gave women voting rights and political voice, advanced gender equality; (2) Civil Rights Movement — secured Civil Rights Act (1964) and Voting Rights Act (1965), ended segregation, prevented discrimination, promoted equal opportunities; (3) Microfinance — small loans for marginalized communities, enables business creation, financial independence, and control over lives, addresses poverty cycles; (4) Youth Empowerment Programs — provide knowledge and skills for youth leadership, give marginalized youth agency and voice. These demonstrate how empowerment efforts give marginalized communities tools and voice for positive change.
