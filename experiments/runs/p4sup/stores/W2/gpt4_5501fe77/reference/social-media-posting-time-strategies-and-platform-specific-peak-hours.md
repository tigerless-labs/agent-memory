**Peak posting times by platform:**
- **Instagram:** 12:00 PM–4:00 PM (primary); secondary peaks vary by audience
- **Facebook:** 12:00 PM–3:00 PM, and 8:00 PM
- **Twitter:** 12:00 PM–3:00 PM, and 5:00 PM
- **TikTok:** 3:00 PM–5:00 PM, and 8:00 PM

**Tools for analysis:**
- Instagram Insights (native—shows when followers are most active)
- Third-party tools: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Buffer (provide more detailed analytics)

**General best practices:**
- Post 3–5 stories per day, spaced out to avoid audience fatigue
- Maintain consistent posting schedule to build audience expectations
- Experiment with different times and track performance via analytics
- Consider audience demographics and time zones
- Adjust strategy based on audience feedback and engagement patterns

**Question Sticker tips:**
- Ask open-ended questions instead of yes/no
- Keep questions relevant to content and audience interests
- Use attention-grabbing language with emojis or humor
- Make questions easy to answer (avoid overly complex or personal queries)
- Respond to answers promptly to show engagement and build community
- Pin question to top of story for visibility
