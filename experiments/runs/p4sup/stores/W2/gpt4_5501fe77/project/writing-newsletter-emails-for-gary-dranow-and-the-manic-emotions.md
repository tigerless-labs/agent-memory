Writing a series of newsletter emails for Gary Dranow and The Manic Emotions band. Each email follows the band's story chronologically:

- Email about the album 'Destiny Road' (fourth email): album written in 90s, intended summer 1998 release, recorded on Jefferson Airplane's old 2" 16 Track machine owned by producer Jerry Manfredi, Gary moved to Park City, Utah and focused on ski racing/coaching for 10 years instead of mastering the tracks
- Email about band reformation (sixth email): Gary got back into music in 2008, re-established The Manic Emotions in Salt Lake City area by 2021, band started playing shows immediately
- Email about 'Made it Another Day' song (seventh email): song speaks to experience of personal/professional life disrupted by sensitivity to others; 'Like a rat in a wheel' lyric represents never-ending struggle to get life in order; Gary thrilled to be performing and sharing music with fans again in 2023 and beyond

Each email is formatted as a newsletter to subscribers with opening greeting, story content, closing call-to-action, and signature from the band.
