**Engagement strategy:** Use Instagram's Question sticker to encourage follower interaction around shared interests.

**Planned questions:**
- "What's your favorite TV show to binge-watch on a lazy Sunday?"
- "What's the best movie you've seen recently?"

**Approach:**
- Keep questions open-ended and easy to respond to
- Make questions relevant to content (TV shows/movies—a personal passion area)
- Engage actively with followers who respond (likes, comments, sharing their answers)
- Pin questions to top of story for visibility
- Monitor engagement using Instagram Insights

**Related goal:** Identify audience interests and build community around shared TV/movie preferences while driving engagement metrics.
