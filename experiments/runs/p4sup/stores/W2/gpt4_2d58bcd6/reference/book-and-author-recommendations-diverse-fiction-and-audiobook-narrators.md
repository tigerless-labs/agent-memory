- 'The Poet X' by Elizabeth Acevedo

- 'The Sun is Also a Star' by Nicola Yoon

- 'The Power' (Naomi Alderman), narrated by Adjoa Andoh
- 'The Song of Achilles' (Madeline Miller), narrated by Frazer Douglas
- 'The First Fifteen Lives of Harry August' (Claire North), narrated by Peter Kenny
- 'The Hate U Give' (Angie Thomas), narrated by Bahni Turpin
