**Active fitness routine as of May 30, 2023:**

- **Morning yoga**: Light yoga poses focused on flexibility and ankle strength (ankle circles, toe spreads, ankle alphabet, downward-facing dog, tree pose, eagle pose)
- **Gym membership**: Joined gym for structured guidance on strength training exercises
- **Home strength training**: Bodyweight exercises including push-ups, squats, lunges, planks; considering using household items as weights
- **Daily walks**: Taking short walks during lunch break at work
- **Weekend biking**: Riding bike to grocery store instead of driving
- **Goal**: Supplement gym routine with more structured home strength training; improve ankle flexibility and strength to prevent future injuries
