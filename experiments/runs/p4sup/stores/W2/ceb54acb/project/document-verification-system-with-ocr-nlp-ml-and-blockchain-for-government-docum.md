AI-powered document verification system for government documents (tax returns, business permits). Features: online submission, blockchain storage check, approval workflow via BIR and Office of Permits and Licenses, OCR/NLP/ML-based authentication, automatic storage in blockchain upon agency approval, fraud detection via anomaly patterns.
