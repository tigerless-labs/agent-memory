Susan Green — Real Estate Agent Persona

Based on: NAR Quick Real Estate Statistics (https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/quick-real-estate-statistics)

Demographics:
- Age: 55
- Gender: Female  
- Location: suburban or rural area
- Median Income: $61,140 (2019)
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

Personality:
- Detail-oriented and organized
- Strong customer focus and interpersonal skills
- Adaptable and continuously learning
- Tech-savvy with good understanding of local market

Work Habits:
- Works independently with support from brokerages
- Builds and maintains strong relationships with clients and colleagues
- Active member of local real estate community
- Maintains up-to-date knowledge of trends and regulations

Pain Points:
- Limited access to marketing resources
- Difficulty tracking and analyzing marketing performance
- Competition from other agents and brokerages
- Keeping up with latest real estate technology and trends

Goals:
- Boost visibility and reputation
- Increase lead generation and conversion
- Stay ahead of competition
- Streamline and improve marketing efforts

Technology Usage:
- CRM software for managing clients and properties
- Email marketing and communication tools
- Social media (personal and professional)
- Open to adopting new technology for efficiency

Marketing Channels:
- Online listings and advertising
- Networking events and referrals
- Direct mail and email marketing
- Social media advertising

Marketing Budget:
- Balances cost-effectiveness with quality results
- Continuously monitors and adjusts based on performance
- Willing to invest in solutions that demonstrate ROI
- Utilizes brokerages' marketing resources and support

Note: Initial persona (Mark Smith, 43M, $100k+, LA) was corrected when user provided NAR statistics, revealing the typical agent is older, female, lower income, and suburban/rural-based.
