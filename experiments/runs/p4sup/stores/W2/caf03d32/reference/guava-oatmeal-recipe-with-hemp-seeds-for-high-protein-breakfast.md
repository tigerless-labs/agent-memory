Base: 1/2 cup rolled oats + 1/4 cup diced fresh guava + 1/2 cup almond milk. Add 1-2 tbsp hemp seeds (provides ~5-10g protein) + 1 tbsp chia seeds (~2g protein). Optional toppings: sliced banana, walnuts/almonds, cinnamon, honey/maple syrup. Ratio: 1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal. Hemp seeds can be ground before adding or mixed with liquid first. Store hemp seeds in airtight container in fridge/freezer. Total protein per serving: ~10-15g.
