Building a comprehensive tracking system for managing sports equipment across multiple activities.

**Tracked Categories:**
- Running (shoes, socks, water bottle, headphones)
- Cycling (road bike, mountain bike, helmet, shoes)
- Tennis (racket, balls, shoes)
- Skiing (skis, boots)
- Yoga and Fitness Equipment (mat, weights, resistance bands)

**Tracking Columns:**
- Equipment name
- Last Used (date)
- Last Serviced (date)
- Service Due (date)
- Lent To (person name, for tracking gear loaned to friends)
- Expected Return (date for returned items)
- Notes (including mileage trackers, maintenance reminders, storage location)

This system helps stay organized and ensures timely maintenance, particularly important for high-mileage items like running shoes and bikes.
