## Must-visit historical sites
- **Colchester Castle**: Norman castle with museum covering Roman, medieval, and Civil War history
- **St. Botolph's Priory**: 11th-century religious site with impressive ruins
- **Hollytrees Museum**: Georgian townhouse (18th century) showcasing middle-class Georgian life, furniture, clothing, and decorative arts
- **Tymperleys Clock Museum**: Grade I listed house with collection of lantern clocks, long-case clocks, and other timepieces
- **Firstsite Art Gallery**: Contemporary art gallery with rotating exhibitions

## Parks and natural areas
- **Castle Park**: Town center's largest public park with gardens, pond, skateboard park, tennis courts, cricket ground
- **Highwoods Country Park**: 350+ acre nature reserve with woodlands, meadows, ponds—good for hiking, wildlife watching, picnics
- **Wivenhoe Trail**: ~5 mile walking/cycling route along River Colne between Colchester and Wivenhoe, through woodlands and wildlife habitats
- **Cudmore Grove Country Park**: On Mersea Island with beach, woodlands, picnic areas, walking/cycling routes
- **Castle Point Wildlife Reserve**: Salt marshes and tidal creeks near coast, excellent for bird watching
