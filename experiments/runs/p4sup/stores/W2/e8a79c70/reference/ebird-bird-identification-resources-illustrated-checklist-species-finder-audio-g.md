eBird is the user's primary tool for bird identification and tracking. Key features:\n\n- **eBird Illustrated Checklist** - Comprehensive guide with descriptions, range maps, and photos for 10,000+ bird species\n- **eBird Species Finder** - Helps identify birds based on location, date, and observation details\n- **eBird Audio Guide** - Free app with audio recordings, photos, and range maps by species or location\n- **eBird Species Accounts** - Each species has a 'Sounds' section with call and song recordings\n- **eBird Forums** - Community for discussing bird identification, including bird songs\n\nAccuWeather is used for weather forecasts before birding trips.
