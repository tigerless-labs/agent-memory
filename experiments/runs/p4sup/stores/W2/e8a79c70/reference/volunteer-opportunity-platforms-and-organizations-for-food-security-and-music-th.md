## Platforms\n- **Volunteer Match** (VolunteerMatch.org): Search by location and interests for food security, music therapy, and other causes\n\n## Organizations for Food Security\n- Feeding America\n- Food Bank for New York City\n- Food Pantry Network\n- Catholic Charities\n- Meals on Wheels\n- Salvation Army\n- Society of St. Andrew (gleaning)\n- Food Rescue US\n\n## Music Therapy Organizations\n- **American Music Therapy Association (AMTA)**: Directory of music therapists and programs\n- **Music Therapy Association of America (MTAA)**: Directory of music therapists and programs\n- **National Association for Music Therapy (NAMT)**: Resources for music therapists\n- Local senior centers, nursing homes, and assisted living facilities\n- Rehabilitation centers and hospitals\n\n## Finding Opportunities\n- Use keywords: "music therapy for seniors," "music therapy for disabilities," "food bank volunteer," "hunger relief"\n- Contact local organizations serving seniors or people with disabilities\n- Search AMTA/MTAA directories for local music therapists\n- Check local hospitals, rehabilitation centers for music therapy programs
