Recommended books and resources for learning to draw birds:\n\n- **The Sibley Guide to Birds** by David Allen Sibley - Comprehensive guide with detailed illustrations and descriptions\n- **Birds of a Feather** by Stan Tekiela - Focuses on basics of drawing birds with step-by-step instructions\n- **Drawing Birds** by John Busby - Covers basics with emphasis on capturing movement and gesture\n\nKey tips for drawing birds:\n- Observe birds in person and use reference images\n- Break down into simple shapes (spheres, cylinders, rectangles)\n- Focus on gesture and proportions\n- Use a range of values (light/dark) to capture texture\n- Draw from life when possible\n- Practice with different media and angles
