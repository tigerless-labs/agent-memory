Apps recommended for tracking workout progress and meal planning:

**Workout Tracking:**
- MyFitnessPal: Tracks exercises, sets, reps, weight; large exercise database; allows setting goals and tracking progress
- Strava: Popular for tracking runs, rides, cardio activities; social features and challenges
- Fitbit Coach: Personalized workouts based on fitness goals and level; tracks progress with reminders

**Meal Tracking:**
- MyFitnessPal: Tracks daily food intake with macronutrients and calories; large food database; set daily goals
- Lose It!: Calorie tracking app with barcode scanning; allows setting goals and tracking progress
- YAZIO: Nutrition app for tracking food intake and macronutrients; customizable daily goals

**Other Tools:**
- Spreadsheets: Manual tracking customized to specific needs
- Progress photos: Visual tracking at regular intervals
- Body measurements: Track changes in body composition over time
