For product photos of eco-friendly candles, use natural light from windows, neutral backgrounds, and props like dried flowers, shells, reclaimed wood, and potted plants. Style summer candle photos with light woods, pastel colors, summer fruits like lemons, and beachy elements. Keep styling simple and light to highlight the candle. Use earth tones and natural textures for the color palette.
