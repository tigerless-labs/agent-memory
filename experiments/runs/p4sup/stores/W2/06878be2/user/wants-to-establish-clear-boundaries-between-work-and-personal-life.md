Interested in strategies to maintain work-life balance, including:
- Setting defined working hours
- Creating dedicated workspace
- Establishing transition routines between work and personal time
- Limiting work-related technology use during personal time
- Communicating boundaries clearly to colleagues and family
- Prioritizing personal time with non-negotiable activities
- Learning to decline work tasks that interfere with personal time
- Practicing effective time management during work hours
- Building a support network for maintaining boundaries
- Regularly reviewing and adjusting boundaries as needed

Goal is to prevent burnout and maintain healthy balance.
