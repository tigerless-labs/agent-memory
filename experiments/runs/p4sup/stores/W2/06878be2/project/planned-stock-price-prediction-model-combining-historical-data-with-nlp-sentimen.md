**Project**: Build predictive model to forecast stock prices using historical data and news sentiment

**Status**: In planning phase as of May 2023

**Approach**:
- **Data collection**: News API and Quandl for historical news articles and stock prices
- **Text preprocessing**: NLTK and spaCy for cleaning, tokenization, text representation (TF-IDF or word embeddings)
- **Sentiment analysis**: Combination of rule-based approaches and ML models (Naive Bayes, SVMs), plus deep learning (CNNs, RNNs)
- **Feature engineering**: Sentiment scores, named entity recognition (NER), topic modeling, part-of-speech (POS) tagging
- **Model evaluation**: MAE, MSE, RMSPE metrics

**Key insight**: NLP sentiment and tone analysis of news articles is expected to improve stock price prediction accuracy.

**Motivation**: Combines interest in NLP with predictive modeling to apply practical ML skills to financial domain.
