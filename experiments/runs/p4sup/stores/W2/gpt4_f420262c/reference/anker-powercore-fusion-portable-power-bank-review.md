**Anker PowerCore Fusion** — Portable power bank combining wall charger and battery.\n\n**Specs:**\n- Capacity: 5,000mAh\n- Dual USB-A ports (no USB-C)\n- Compact design\n- Can charge 2 devices simultaneously\n- Fast charging support\n- Functions as wall charger when needed\n\n**Pros:**\n- Compact and portable\n- Dual charging ports\n- Wall charger functionality\n- Decent 5,000mAh capacity\n\n**Cons:**\n- May be insufficient for heavy users\n- USB-A only (no USB-C)\n- Limited capacity vs larger power banks\n\n**Assessment:** Solid value for compact, fast, convenient portable charging. Worth considering if prioritizing portability.
