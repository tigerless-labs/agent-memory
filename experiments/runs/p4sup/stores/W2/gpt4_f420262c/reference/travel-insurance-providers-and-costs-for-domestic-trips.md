Providers: Allianz Travel Insurance, TravelGuard Insurance, Travelex Insurance Services, Squaremouth Travel Insurance. Approximate costs for 4-day domestic trip: Trip Cancellation 20-50, Trip Interruption 15-30, Travel Medical 10-25, Travel Delay 10-20 per person (in dollars). Available from providers directly or travel agencies. Many credit cards offer benefits.
