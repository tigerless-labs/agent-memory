Key attractions and experiences in Ahmedabad:

**Historical Landmarks:**
- Sabarmati Ashram: Mahatma Gandhi's former residence, museum of his life and teachings
- Akshardham Temple: Intricate carvings, ornate pillars, beautiful lake, evening light and sound show
- Jama Masjid: One of India's largest mosques, Indo-Islamic architecture
- Bhadra Fort: Historic 15th-century fort
- Sarkhej Roza: Historic mosque and tomb complex, Islamic architecture

**Cultural Experiences:**
- Old City Heritage Walk: Narrow lanes, Bhadra Fort, Teen Darwaza, historic pols (neighborhoods)
- Navratri garba and dandiya raas celebrations
- Kite festival (Uttarayan) with locals
- Lal Darwaja Market: Traditional clothing, handicrafts, souvenirs
- Manek Chowk: Historic square for local snacks, drinks, souvenirs

**Other Attractions:**
- Kankaria Lake: Boating, walking trails, sunset views
- Science City: Interactive exhibits, planetarium, IMAX theater
- Ahmedabad Museum: City's history, art, culture

Suggested 5-day itinerary focuses on family history (day 1), cultural heritage (day 2), historical landmarks (day 3), local culture and traditions (day 4), modern Ahmedabad (day 5).
