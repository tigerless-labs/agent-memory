**Traditional Gujarati Dishes:**
- Undhiyu: Mixed vegetable dish with variety of vegetables, beans, and spices
- Khaman: Sweet and sour snack made from chickpea flour, served with green chutney
- Handvo: Savory cake made from lentils and rice, served with butter
- Gujarati Thali: Traditional meal plate with dal, rice, roti, vegetables
- Local street food: Pani puri, sev usal, masala chaas

**Recommended Restaurants:**
- Vishalla: Popular restaurant serving traditional Gujarati cuisine in rustic setting
- Agashiye: Vegetarian restaurant with wide range of Gujarati dishes, some unique and innovative options
- Gordhan Thal: Popular eatery serving traditional Gujarati snacks and meals
- Toran Dining Hall: Restaurant offering Gujarati and Indian dishes in traditional setting

Gujarati cuisine is known for sweet and savory flavors. Be sure to explore local street food vendors for authentic experiences.
