Recommended festivals and events in Gujarat:

**Major Festivals:**
- **Navratri** (September-October): Famous garba and dandiya raas dances in Ahmedabad and Vadodara
- **Uttarayan** (January 14): Kite-flying festival marking sun's journey north
- **Modhera Dance Festival** (January): At Sun Temple in Modhera, UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Rann Utsav** (November-February): In Rann of Kutch white salt desert, cultural performances and handicrafts
- **Vibrant Gujarat** (January, biennial): Business and cultural summit celebrating heritage and entrepreneurship
- **Bhavnath Mahadev Mela** (February): Fair at temple in Junagadh with traditional music and dance
- **Gujarat Festival** (November): Showcases cultural heritage through music, dance, theater, handicrafts

Best timing depends on festival schedule and personal interests.
