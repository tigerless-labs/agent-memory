## Leading Ecocritics

- **Lawrence Buell** - Introduced concepts of 'environmental imagination' (the capacity of literature to shape how we think about the environment) and cultural restoration through literature.
- **Cheryll Glotfelty** - Focuses on how literature represents the relationship between humans and the environment, and how representations shape societal attitudes.
- **Jonathan Bate** - Emphasizes reading literature as an aspect of relations between culture and nature (not just reading nature into literature).
- **Eric D. Wilson** - Studies the relationship between human culture and the natural world through literature.

## Research Methods in Ecocritical Scholarship

1. Close reading (analyzing language, imagery, literary techniques in texts)
2. Historical and cultural context analysis
3. Environmental science and ecology analysis
4. Comparative analysis across time periods and cultures
5. Ethnographic fieldwork and interviews
6. Discourse analysis of cultural representations
