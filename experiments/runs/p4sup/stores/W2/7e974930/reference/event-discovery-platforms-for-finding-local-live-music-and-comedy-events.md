Platforms and websites recommended for discovering local live music and comedy events:

**Event Discovery Apps/Websites:**
- **Eventbrite** — search by location and event type/interests
- **Meetup** — find local groups and events
- **Songkick** — specialized for finding concert events and touring artists
- **Bandsintown** — music-focused event discovery for artists and venues

**Other discovery methods:**
- Follow local venue social media accounts for event announcements
- Check local newspaper event calendars or city websites
- Join Facebook groups focused on local music/comedy communities
