**Interdisciplinary scholarship**: Combines different disciplines to study a problem. Synthesizes insights and methods from multiple fields for comprehensive understanding. Example: climate change studied through atmospheric science, economics, sociology, political science.

**Transdisciplinary scholarship**: Goes beyond interdisciplinarity by actively involving stakeholders and practitioners from various fields. Collaborative approach to co-create knowledge and solve real-world problems. Bridges gap between academic research and practical application. Aims for deeper understanding and implementable solutions.

*Simple distinction*: Interdisciplinary = combining disciplines to study; Transdisciplinary = combining disciplines to solve.

Ecocriticism can be seen as both interdisciplinary (literary studies + environmental studies + cultural studies) and transdisciplinary when it engages stakeholders to address environmental issues.
