**Fujifilm X-T30 II** - Mirrorless camera recommended for landscape/nature photography.\n\n**Key specs:**\n- 26.1MP APS-C sensor\n- Hybrid autofocus with 425 phase-detection points\n- Vari-angle touchscreen LCD\n- Compact and lightweight body\n- Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)\n- Battery: ~380 frames per charge (EVF), ~440 frames (LCD)\n- Price: ~899 USD (body only)\n\n**Recommended kit lens:**\n- Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ (~499 USD) - compact, lightweight, versatile focal range\n\n**Autofocus highlights:**\n- 0.02 second response time\n- Advanced subject tracking with AI prediction\n- Face and eye detection\n- Works down to -3EV in low light\n- Customizable autofocus modes (zone, wide/tracking, single-point)
