Weekend meal prep recipe for freezer-friendly breakfast burritos (makes multiple servings).

**Basic Recipe Components:**
- Scrambled eggs
- Sausage or bacon (cooked)
- Black beans
- Cheese
- Whole-grain tortillas
- Salt and pepper

**Filling Variations:**
- Roasted sweet potatoes, Brussels sprouts, or broccoli
- Chorizo or Korean chorizo (gochujang) for spicy kick
- Mushroom duxelles (sautéed mushrooms with onions and herbs)
- Black beans with cumin and coriander
- Grilled pineapple (sweet and savory)
- Diced bell peppers, onions, mushrooms

**Topping Suggestions:**
- Avocado salsa (mashed avocado with lime juice, salt, and spices)
- Pickled onions
- Cilantro lime crema (Greek yogurt, lime juice, cilantro, salt)
- Sliced radishes
- Feta cheese
- Kimchi (spicy and probiotic)
- Sliced jalapeños
- Fresh cilantro
- Sliced scallions
- Guacamole

**Meal Prep Tips:**
- Batch cook eggs, sausage/bacon, and beans on the weekend
- Use variety of fillings to stay interested throughout week
- Choose whole-grain tortillas
- Portion fillings into individual portions
- Wrap individually in plastic wrap or foil and freeze for up to 3 months
- Label and date containers

**Reheating:**
- **Microwave:** Wrap in paper towel, microwave on high 1-2 minutes, check after 1 minute and rotate
- **Oven:** Wrap in foil, bake at 350°F for 15-20 minutes; for crispy tortilla add 1-2 minutes under broiler
- Ensure internal temperature reaches 165°F for food safety
