## Key LHC Discoveries
- **Higgs boson** (2012) — confirmed Higgs field and mechanism for how particles acquire mass
- **Standard Model validation** — extensive confirmation of particle physics predictions
- **Dark matter searches** — dark matter estimated at ~85% of universe's matter; searches ongoing but no definitive evidence yet
- **Quark-gluon plasma** — recreation of matter state shortly after Big Bang

## Planned LHC Experiments
- **High-Luminosity LHC** (mid-2020s upgrade) — new magnets, increased collision frequency for rare phenomena studies
- **Future Circular Collider (FCC)** — proposed larger, more powerful accelerator at existing LHC site
- **Precision Higgs studies** — detailed investigation of Higgs boson properties and role in universe
- **Continued dark matter searches** — sensitive detectors and advanced analysis techniques

## Potential Real-World Applications
- New energy sources from dark matter research
- Medical imaging via detector technology (gamma ray detection)
- New materials with unique properties
- Space exploration advancement
