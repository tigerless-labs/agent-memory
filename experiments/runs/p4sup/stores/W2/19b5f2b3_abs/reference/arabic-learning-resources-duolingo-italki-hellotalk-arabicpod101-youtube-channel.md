Recommended resources for Arabic practice: Online platforms include Duolingo, Al-Kitaab, ArabicPod101, italki, HelloTalk. YouTube channels: Learn Arabic with Maha, Arabic Alphabet, Arabic with Nadia. Apps: Memrise, Anki. Audio: Arabic Radio (Radio Sawa, Monte Carlo Doualiya), Podcasts (Coffee Break Arabic, Arabic Pod). Communities: r/LearnArabic on Reddit, Facebook groups. Key points: Arabic written right-to-left in cursive, 28 letters with position-dependent forms, short vowels indicated by diacritics.
