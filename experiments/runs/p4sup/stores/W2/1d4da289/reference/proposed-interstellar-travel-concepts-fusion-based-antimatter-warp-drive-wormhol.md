Proposed technologies for future interstellar travel (all theoretical, not yet viable):

- **Fusion-based propulsion** — propulsion systems using nuclear fusion
- **Antimatter engines** — propulsion using antimatter reactions
- **Space-time warping** — hypothetical method to contract space in front of a vessel and expand it behind
- **Wormholes** — hypothetical shortcuts through space-time

Status: Require significant research and development before viable for human space travel.
