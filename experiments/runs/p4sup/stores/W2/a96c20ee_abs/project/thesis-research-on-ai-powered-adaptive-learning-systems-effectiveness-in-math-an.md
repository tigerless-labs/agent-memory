Conducted thesis research on the impact of AI on student learning outcomes using AI-powered adaptive learning systems in math and reading comprehension contexts.

Approach: Broader conceptual focus with case studies of specific platforms/systems to illustrate implementation challenges and opportunities.

Key findings: Systems can be effective, but implementation and integration are crucial. Teacher professional development is a critical barrier. Clear curriculum alignment increases adoption. Systems could promote equity but risk algorithmic bias exacerbating inequities.

Recommendations: Prioritize teacher PD, encourage developer-educator collaboration, ensure curriculum alignment guidance, conduct ongoing evaluation.

Conference: Presented poster at first research conference summer 2022. Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers. Some developers reached out interested in applying findings.

Stakeholder tensions: Disagreement on teacher role (replacement vs. support tool).
