Recommended platforms for selling different types of vintage collectibles:

**Luxury Watches (Omega Seamaster):**
- Reverb (specialized marketplace)
- Chrono24 (watch-focused community)
- 1stdibs (high-end luxury items)
- The RealReal (luxury consignment model)
- eBay (with Authenticity Guarantee program)
- WatchUseek, Timekeeper Forums, Omega Forums (specialized communities)
- Local watch dealers and collectors
- Auction houses: Christie's, Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions

**Vintage Guitars (Fender Jazzmaster):**
- Reverb (primary platform for musical instruments)
- eBay
- The Gear Page, TDPRI (Dallas Pedal and Guitar Ranch), GuitarPlayer forums
- Vintage Guitar Exchange and specialist marketplaces
- Facebook Marketplace and local selling groups

**Comic Books (The Avengers #1):**
- ComicConnect (comic-focused marketplace)
- eBay
- Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, r/comicbooks
- Local comic book stores
- Note: Use CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) grading service for certification

**Action Figures (Batman 1960s):**
- eBay
- Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk forums
- ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room
- Local toy and collectible stores
- Note: Can use Wata Games or PSA (Professional Sports Authenticator) for grading

**Vintage Audio Equipment (Sony Walkman):**
- eBay
- Gearslutz, Resonance FM, vintage audio forums
- Facebook Marketplace, local audio enthusiast groups

**Currency (Cuban Peso Note):**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG) approved appraisers
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD)
- Specialized numismatic dealers
- eBay (for currency collectibles)

**Key tips for all items:**
- Provide high-quality photos and detailed descriptions
- Get professional authentication/certification when available
- Research fees for each platform
- Set competitive prices based on market research
- Be prepared to negotiate and answer buyer questions
- Keep all documentation and certificates of authenticity
