Recommended silver jewelry cleaning solutions:\n\n1. **Godfrey's Silver Dip** — Popular, easy-to-use solution (dip, rinse, dry)\n2. **Weiman Silver Jewelry Cream** — Gentle, non-abrasive cream that removes tarnish\n3. **Hagerty Silver Foam** — Ammonia-free foam cleaner\n4. **Connoisseurs Silver Jewelry Cleaner** — Non-toxic solution\n5. **DIY Baking Soda Paste** — Mix 1 tbsp baking soda with 2 tbsp water, apply for 15-30 minutes\n\nGeneral silver care tips:\n- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight\n- Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive materials, and hot water\n- Dry thoroughly to prevent water spots\n- Use silver polishing cloths and anti-tarnish storage bags\n- Have pieces professionally maintained by a jeweler as needed
