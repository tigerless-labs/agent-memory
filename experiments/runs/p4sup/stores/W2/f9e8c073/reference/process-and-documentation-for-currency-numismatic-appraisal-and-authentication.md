Appraisal process for rare currency like the 1950s Cuban peso note:

**Finding an Appraiser:**
- Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG): directory of qualified appraisers
- International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD): specialized currency dealers
- Research appraisers with expertise in Cuban currency specifically

**Appraisal Process:**
1. Research and locate reputable numismatic expert
2. Submit note with any existing documentation/provenance
3. Appraiser examines authenticity, rarity, condition
4. Appraiser researches note, verifies existence and market demand
5. Receives appraisal report and documentation

**Documentation Provided:**

*Appraisal Report (types available):*
- Verbal appraisal: informal, casual assessment
- Written appraisal: more detailed formal assessment
- Formal appraisal report: comprehensive analysis

*Should include:*
- Description: denomination, series, condition
- Assessment of authenticity and rarity
- Estimated value based on current market
- Historical and provenance information
- Detailed analysis of market demand

**Certificate of Authenticity:**
- Some appraisers provide separate certificate
- Verifies note's genuineness
- Useful for proving authenticity to buyers

**Recommendations:**
- Choose reputable appraiser with specialty in currency
- Be transparent about history and provenance
- Keep appraisal report and supporting documentation for insurance/sales
- Consider multiple appraisals for more accurate value estimate
