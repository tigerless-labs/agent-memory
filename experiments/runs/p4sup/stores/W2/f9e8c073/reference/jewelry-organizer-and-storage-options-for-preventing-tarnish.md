Jewelry storage and organization options:\n\n1. **Stackable Jewelry Trays** (e.g., Songmics, Whitmor) — Individual compartments, soft fabric lining\n2. **Jewelry Armoires** (e.g., Mirrored, Standing by SEI) — Large multi-drawer cases with compartments and locks\n3. **Anti-Tarnish Jewelry Boxes** (e.g., Stackers, Mele & Co.) — Special lining that absorbs moisture and prevents tarnishing\n4. **Travel Jewelry Cases** (e.g., Case Elegance, Mark and Graham) — Compact, portable options for travel or small collections\n5. **Over-the-Door Organizers** (e.g., Whitmor, InterDesign) — Space-saving options with hooks, pockets, and compartments\n\nSelection criteria:\n- Material: Velvet, soft fabric, or anti-tarnish lining\n- Compartments: Separate sections for different jewelry types\n- Size: Fits current collection and available storage space\n- Portability: Consider if traveling with jewelry\n- Security: Look for locks/secure closures for valuable pieces
