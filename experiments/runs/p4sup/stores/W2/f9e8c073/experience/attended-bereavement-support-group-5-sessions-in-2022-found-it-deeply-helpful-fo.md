Attended 5 sessions of a bereavement support group in 2022. Found the experience very helpful for processing emotions related to loss and grief. What worked well:
- Sense of community and shared understanding with others going through similar experiences
- Kind and understanding facilitator who created psychological safety
- Safe space to share personal story without fear of judgment
- Opportunity to listen to others and feel less alone in grief

Experienced this as a major support during a difficult period. Values the principles of this experience: community, safety, being heard, and guidance from a trusted facilitator.
