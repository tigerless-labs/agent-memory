Decided to pursue multiple avenues for ongoing healing and support after difficult period:

1. **Therapy/Counseling**: Planning to seek out a therapist or counselor for professional guidance and support

2. **Journaling**: Starting a journaling practice as a therapeutic outlet. Writing has historically been therapeutic. Plans to use it for processing emotions, reflecting on experiences, and gaining clarity.

3. **Online Communities**: Considering joining online forums and social media communities focused on grief, loss, and mental health to connect with others who understand similar experiences.

4. **Personal Network**: Planning to reach out to friends and family members who have gone through similar experiences to connect and receive support.

Motivated by positive experience from bereavement support group and desire to continue building supportive practices.
