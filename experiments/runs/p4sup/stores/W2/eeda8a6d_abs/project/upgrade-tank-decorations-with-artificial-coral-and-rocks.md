User plans to upgrade 20-gallon tank decorations with artificial coral and rocks to create more hiding places and improve aquascape aesthetics. Recommendations: establish a focal point, create asymmetrical balance, arrange for varied water flow, provide multiple hiding places (caves, overhangs, crevices), leave open swimming space, consider reef or nature-inspired themes.
