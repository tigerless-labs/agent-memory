Since ~May 1, 2023: bathroom sink drains slowly after washing hands/face. Tried drain cleaner twice (ineffective). Used plunger + plumbing snake on May 21 (Sunday) without success.

**Plan to troubleshoot:**
- Clean faucet aerator with hot water + white vinegar soak (30-60 min), then soft brush
- Check P-trap is filled with water; pour baking soda + hot water, let sit overnight
- Clean bathroom trash can (likely contributing to odors in sink area)
- If still slow after these steps, call professional plumber

Bathroom also has bad odor from trash can, which may be masking or related to drain issues.
