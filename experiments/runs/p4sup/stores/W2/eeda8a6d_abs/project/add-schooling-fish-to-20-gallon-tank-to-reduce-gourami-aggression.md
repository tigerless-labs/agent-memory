User is considering adding schooling fish (lemon tetras or zebra danios) to the 20-gallon tank to distract the gouramis and reduce their feeding-time aggression. Key plan: introduce 10-15 fish as a group, provide plenty of hiding places, increase water changes. Lemon tetras prefer 72-82F, pH 6.0-7.0; zebra danios prefer 65-75F, pH 6.5-8.0. Both need open swimming space and compatible tank mates.
