User plans to add live aquatic plants (Java Moss, Anacharis, Water Wisteria, Amazon Swords, Cryptocorynes) to improve water quality. Will use nutrient-rich substrate, provide adequate lighting, and anchor plants to prevent floating. Recommended to start with a few plants and gradually add more.
