Plans to incorporate role-playing activities and collaborative group projects into classroom teaching to develop students' leadership skills (communication, decision-making, confidence, empathy, creativity).

Specific interests:
- Simulating workplace leadership scenarios
- Boardroom discussion role-plays
- Conflict resolution role-plays
- Collaborative learning activities where students work in groups

Key techniques for implementation:
- Set clear expectations and individual assignments within groups
- Assign group roles (leader, recorder, timekeeper)
- Encourage active listening and equal participation
- Monitor participation and provide feedback
- Provide real-life examples and clear objectives
- Allow flexibility and creativity in role-playing scenarios
