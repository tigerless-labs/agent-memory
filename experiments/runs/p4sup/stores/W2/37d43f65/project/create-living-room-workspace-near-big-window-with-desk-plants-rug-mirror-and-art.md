Redesigning living room workspace area near big window with functional and decorative elements to create a workspace and reading nook.

LOCATION: Near big window in living room

DESK OPTIONS: Wall-mounted floating desk, narrow console table, drop-leaf table, ottoman with tray, or foldable desk. Considerations: measure space carefully, choose comfortable height, opt for multi-functionality with storage, select neutral color, add task lighting. Retailers: IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, Amazon.

CIRCULAR RUG: Size 3-4 feet diameter to define workspace and add warmth. Material: wool, jute, or synthetic (durable, low-maintenance). Color: neutral (beige, gray, taupe) or bold accent. Pattern: simple, subtle or Moroccan-inspired designs. Thickness around 1/4 inch.

CIRCULAR MIRROR: Size 12-18 inches diameter to add visual interest and make space feel larger. Frame style to match room decor. Hang at 57-60 inches from floor. Beveled edge or textured glass for visual interest.

ARTWORK/PRINTS: Scale 1/3 to 2/3 width of desk/table. Styles: botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, pop art. Consider personal connection, color palette match, lighting, and frame style. Budget-friendly options available on Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, Saatchi Art.

PLANTS: Low-maintenance options for bright window light: Snake Plant, ZZ Plant, Spider Plant, Pothos, Succulents, Dracaena, Peace Lily, Bromeliads. Care: water when soil feels dry, fertilize sparingly, prune regularly, monitor temperature/humidity.
