Stated preference as of May 2023: does not watch cable TV. Values streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) for:

- **Ad-free viewing** — no commercials, uninterrupted binge-watching
- **Content catalog** — wider selection of shows/movies, including content unavailable on cable and previously aired episodes
- **Resume feature** — ability to pause and resume days or weeks later without forgetting progress
- **Release model** — prefers entire seasons released at once (binge-watch) over weekly episode rollouts
- **Multi-device access** — can watch on smartphones and tablets while traveling or on-the-go
- **Personalized recommendations** — algorithms suggest content based on viewing history
- **Convenience** — no long-term contracts required; lower cost

Streaming has become primary entertainment consumption method.
