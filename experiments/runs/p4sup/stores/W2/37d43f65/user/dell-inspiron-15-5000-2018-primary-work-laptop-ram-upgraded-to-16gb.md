Primary work laptop: Dell Inspiron 15 5000 series from 2018.

Recently upgraded RAM to 16GB, which provided significant performance boost.

Battery life issue after upgrade:
- Before: 6-7 hours
- After RAM upgrade: 4-5 hours
- Planning to calibrate battery (drain to 0%, charge to 100%, repeat 2-3 times) to troubleshoot

Battery life decrease likely due to:
- Battery calibration need
- Power consumption from additional RAM
- More resource-intensive apps running
- Power management settings needing adjustment
