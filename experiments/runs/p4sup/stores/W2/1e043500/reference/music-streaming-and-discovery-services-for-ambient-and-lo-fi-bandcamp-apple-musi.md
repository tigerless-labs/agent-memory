Recommended music streaming services with good discovery features for ambient and lo-fi genres:\n- **Bandcamp**: Platform for discovering indie artists and labels\n- **Apple Music**: 'Moods' section, 'New Music Mix' playlist\n- **Tidal**: Human-curated 'Discover' feature, 'Rising' section for emerging artists\n- **Deezer**: AI-powered 'Discover' and 'Mood' section\n\nSpotify playlists worth exploring: Lo-Fi Girl, Ambient Universe, Chillhop Music, Coffee Shop Jazz, Discover Weekly, Release Radar
