Traditional Gambian visual art forms:
- Batik printing: wax-resist dyeing for traditional patterns
- Woodcarving: statues, masks, utensils
- Pottery: coiling and throwing techniques
- Photography: documenting weddings, naming ceremonies, everyday life
- Painting: depicting traditional clothing, food, dance, historical events

Recent developments (as of 2023):
- Emergence of contemporary art forms (installations, performance, conceptual art)
- Increased use of digital technology (digital paintings, animations on climate/social justice themes)
- Pan-African art collaborations
- Youth engagement programs teaching cultural heritage preservation
