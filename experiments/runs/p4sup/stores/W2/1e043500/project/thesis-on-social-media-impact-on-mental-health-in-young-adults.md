## Research Question
How are social media usage patterns associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults?

## Primary Focus
- Relationship between social media usage patterns and mental health outcomes (depression, anxiety) in young adults/emerging adults/adolescents

## Extended Research Interests
- Mediating roles of self-esteem and social support in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes
- Effectiveness of social media-based interventions and policies for promoting mental health in young adults
- Social media-based mental health awareness campaigns
- Online support groups on social media platforms
- Peer-to-peer support initiatives leveraging social media
- Strategies to leverage social media platforms to promote mental health awareness and reduce stigma

## Relevant Databases for Literature Review
- PsycINFO (American Psychological Association)
- PubMed (National Library of Medicine)
- Scopus (Elsevier)
- Web of Science (Clarivate Analytics)
- Google Scholar

## Key Search Terms and Strategies
- Core: Social media, mental health, young adults, depression, anxiety
- Mediation analysis: Self-esteem, social support as mediating variables
- Interventions: Social media-based interventions, digital mental health, e-mental health, online programs
- Policies: Social media regulation, online safety, digital health policy
- Awareness: Mental health awareness campaigns, mental health promotion, reducing stigma
- Support: Online support groups, peer-to-peer support, online peer support, peer-led initiatives
