Bathroom mirrors have been streaky and foggy for several weeks. User wants to prevent this long-term rather than clean weekly.

**Solutions discussed:**
- Vinegar and water (1:2 ratio) spray with lint-free cloth
- Glass cleaner with newspaper
- Microfiber cloth with warm water
- Rain-X glass treatment (from auto parts stores)

**Prevention strategies:**
- Apply anti-fog coating or mirror treatment (creates hydrophobic surface)
- Regular cleaning every 2-3 weeks
- Squeegee after showers to remove water
- Dry with microfiber cloth after cleaning
- Improve bathroom ventilation (open windows, exhaust fan, dehumidifier to reduce moisture)
- Use mirror-friendly cleaning products designed to reduce streaks/fog
- Clean top to bottom motion

Goal: reduce cleaning frequency through preventive measures.
