Spider plant in the collection developed a pest infestation as of May 2023.

Treatment approach:
- Using neem oil as primary treatment method
- Applying treatment every 3-4 days
- Spraying all parts of plant including undersides of leaves and stems
- Isolating plant from other houseplants to prevent spread

Additional planned interventions:
- Monitoring for pest eggs and larvae
- Continuing treatments for 4-6 weeks to ensure complete eradication
- May consider repotting in sterilized soil if infestation is severe
- Improving air circulation around the plant
- Considering insecticidal soap or horticultural oil as supplementary treatments
