Traveled solo in Japan previously. Accommodation: mixed hostels and hotels. Found hostels excellent for meeting fellow travelers. Transportation: relied heavily on public transportation, found it really efficient and convenient. Travel style: was able to change plans on a whim and explore whatever caught interest, which felt liberating and empowering. This flexibility and spontaneity is important to future trips.
