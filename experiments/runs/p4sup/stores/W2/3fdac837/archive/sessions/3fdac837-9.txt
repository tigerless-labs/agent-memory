user: How do you make a kubernetes daemonset tolerate any taints?
assistant: To make a Kubernetes DaemonSet tolerate any taints, you can add a wildcard toleration in the DaemonSet's PodSpec. This will allow the DaemonSet's pods to be scheduled on any node, regardless of the taints present on those nodes.

Here's an example of a DaemonSet manifest with a wildcard toleration:
```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: DaemonSet
metadata:
  name: my-daemonset
spec:
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: my-daemonset-app
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: my-daemonset-app
    spec:
      tolerations:
      - operator: Exists
        effect: NoSchedule
      - operator: Exists
        effect: PreferNoSchedule
      - operator: Exists
        effect: NoExecute
      containers:
      - name: my-daemonset-container
        image: my-container-image
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
```
In the `tolerations` section, we've added three tolerations with the `operator` field set to `Exists`. This means that the pods will tolerate any taint, regardless of its key or value. We've also specified all three possible taint effects: `NoSchedule`, `PreferNoSchedule`, and `NoExecute`. This ensures that the DaemonSet's pods will be scheduled on nodes with any taint effect.
