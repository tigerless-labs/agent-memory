Ingredients: 12-15 zucchini flowers (male flowers preferred), 1 cup flour, 1/2 cup Parmesan, 1/4 cup parsley, 2 cloves garlic, salt, pepper, 1 cup milk, vegetable oil, mozzarella (optional), anchovies (optional).

Preparation: Mix Parmesan, parsley, garlic, salt, pepper for filling. Rinse flowers, remove stamens, pat dry. Stuff each with 1-2 teaspoons filling. Whisk flour and milk into batter (slightly lumpy). Heat oil to 350F. Dip flowers in batter, fry 2-3 minutes per side until golden. Drain on paper towels. Serve with parsley, whipped ricotta, or mozzarella.

Tips: Use fresh dry flowers, don't overfill, optional powdered sugar for contrast.
