Recommended Korean dishes to learn about and explore: Bulgogi (marinated beef), Japchae (stir-fried glass noodles with vegetables), Bibimbap (mixed rice bowl with vegetables and meat or tofu), Kimchi (spicy fermented Korean cabbage), Tteokbokki (spicy rice cakes in sweet and savory sauce).

Several of these dishes have plant-based variations using tofu or tempeh instead of meat, which aligns with user's interest in exploring those proteins in Korean cuisine.
