Tokyo recommendations for deeper exploration beyond typical tourist spots:\n1. Shinjuku's Kabukicho, Ikebukuro's Gyoza Stadium, Kichijoji's Harmonica Yokocho (neon districts)\n2. TEAMLAB Borderless (digital art museum in Odaiba)\n3. Kagurazaka (traditional architecture, quaint shops, gardens)\n4. Yanaka (historic district with shops, cafes, restaurants; Yanaka Ginza shopping street; Tennoji Temple)\n5. Sumo Tournament at Ryogoku Kokugikan (January, May, or September seasons)\n6. Golden Gai (tiny bars and yakitori restaurants in Shinjuku)\n7. Todoroki Valley (peaceful river walk with greenery)\n8. Ghibli Museum (Studio Ghibli works, garden, theater)\n9. Ameya Yokocho (Ueno marketplace with produce, snacks, souvenirs)\n10. Tsukishima (monjayaki restaurants, man-made island in Tokyo Bay)
