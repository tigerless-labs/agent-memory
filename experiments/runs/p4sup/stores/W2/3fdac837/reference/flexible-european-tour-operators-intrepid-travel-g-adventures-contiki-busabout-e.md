Tour operators and services offering flexibility for European travel:\n\n1. **Intrepid Travel** - Offers Flexi option allowing changes to plans or skipping activities\n\n2. **G Adventures** - Independent Style (mix of guided activities and free time) and Spontaneous travel style (change plans on the fly)\n\n3. **Contiki** - Free Time policy (opt out or explore on own); Flexi option on some trips\n\n4. **Busabout** - Hop-on hop-off bus network in Europe; create own itinerary, change plans as you go\n\n5. **Eurail** - Train passes offering flexibility to create own itinerary and change plans; can add/remove travel days\n\n6. **Local tour operators** - GetYourGuide, Viator, Airbnb Experiences often more flexible than large operators, shorter cancellation notice
