As of 2023-10-20, user is planning to apply for a green card and consulting an immigration attorney about addressing parents' visa overstay. Parents have been in US household for 9 months (since ~2023-01-16), exceeding typical B-2 tourist visa limit of 6 months. This overstay could affect both parents and user's immigration status. User will inquire about waiver of inadmissibility (Form I-601/I-601A), extreme hardship claims, and attorney's experience with similar cases. Resources for finding qualified attorney: AILA (aila.org), National Immigration Project, Immigration Advocates Network, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell.
