**Seat Selection:**\n- Access via Delta.com (My Trips > View/Change > Seat Map) or mobile app\n- Can select immediately after booking for most fares\n- Filter by window, aisle, middle seats\n- Basic Economy: selection available only at 24-hour check-in\n- Group bookings: restrictions may apply\n\n**Special Meals:**\n- Request via website (Meal Preferences tab), check-in, phone, or email\n- Requires 24+ hours advance notice\n- Options: vegetarian, gluten-free, kosher, halal, Hindu, vegan\n- Availability varies by flight and cabin\n- Some special meal options may not be available on all flights
