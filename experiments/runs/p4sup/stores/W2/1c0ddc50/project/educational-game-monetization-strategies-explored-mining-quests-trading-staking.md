Evaluated five monetization approaches for the Flow blockchain educational game:

1. **Mining**: Players earn cryptocurrency by contributing mining power
2. **Quests and challenges**: Reward players with crypto or NFTs for completing educational tasks (coding challenges, educational questions)
3. **Trading and investing**: Players buy/sell/trade NFTs on open market; collect and trade rare NFTs tied to game theme
4. **Staking**: Allow players to stake cryptocurrency to earn interest/transaction fee percentage
5. **Referral programs**: Reward players for bringing new users to the game with crypto or NFTs

Rationale: Multiple earning paths create more engaging and rewarding experience, tap into growing blockchain gaming market
