Popular hiking apps for navigation, trail discovery, and progress tracking:

1. **AllTrails** - Most popular hiking app with vast trail database, GPS tracking, offline maps. iOS and Android.
2. **Gaia GPS** - Powerful app with detailed topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning. iOS and Android.
3. **MapMyHike** - User-friendly, tracks route/distance/elevation gain, trail recommendations and reviews. iOS and Android.
4. **Hiking Project** - Free app from REI with comprehensive trail database, GPS tracking, route planning. iOS and Android.
5. **Gaia Topo** - Advanced version with detailed topographic maps, GPS tracking, route planning. iOS and Android.

Key features to look for: GPS tracking, offline maps, trail database, route planning, reviews and ratings.
