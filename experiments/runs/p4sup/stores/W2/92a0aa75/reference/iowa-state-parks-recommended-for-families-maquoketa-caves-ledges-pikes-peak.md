Recommended parks: Maquoketa Caves (caves, hiking, trails, playground, picnic areas, campground); Ledges State Park (high cliffs, scenic views, hiking, nature center with kids' programs, picnic areas, campground); Pikes Peak State Park (Mississippi River overlooks, hiking trails, Driftless Area Education and Visitor Center with interactive exhibits, picnic areas, campground). Dog policy: leash required (max 6 feet), owners clean up after dogs, some trails/areas have restrictions, check individual park regulations.
