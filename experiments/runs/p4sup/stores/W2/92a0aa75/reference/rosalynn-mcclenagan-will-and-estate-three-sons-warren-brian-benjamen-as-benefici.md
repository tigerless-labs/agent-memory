## Estate of Rosalynn McClenagan

**Beneficiaries:** Three sons
- Warren McClenagan (primary executor)
- Brian McClenagan (alternate executor)
- Benjamen McClenagan

**Real Property:**
- Home at 305 Chase Ave, Gruver, TX 79040 — valued ~$120,000, owned outright
- One-third (1/3) interest in Ayres farmland

**Personal Property:**
- Car — valued ~$11,000
- Annuity
- Life insurance policy

**Distribution:**
- All property divided equally among three sons
- Per stirpes: if any son predeceases, his share passes to his surviving children in equal shares
- Tangible personal property (household items, keepsakes, photos, pets) also equally divided

**Executor:**
- Warren McClenagan (primary)
- Brian McClenagan (alternate if Warren unable to serve)

**Contests Clause:** Any beneficiary who contests the will forfeits their share

**Draft Date:** May 2023
