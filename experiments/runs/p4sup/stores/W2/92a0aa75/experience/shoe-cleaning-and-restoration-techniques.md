Cleaning: Remove loose dirt with soft brush, identify material, use warm water with mild soap. For tough stains use equal parts water and white vinegar. Dry with cloth and air or low-heat dryer. Avoid harsh chemicals and machine washing. Restoring shape: Stuff with paper or use shoe trees, apply gentle heat, use shoe stretchers, apply leather conditioner to leather, try freezer for a few hours. For professional help: Cobblers can reshape shoes, repair seams, and offer reinforcements.
