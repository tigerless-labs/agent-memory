Identification: Customer segmentation by adoption rate/usage/satisfaction; behavioral analysis of feature adoption and support requests; surveys on pain points and preferences; customer health scoring.

Engagement: Exclusive early access; beta testing; regular updates; personalized communication.

Incentives: Early access; exclusive content; swag; discounts; recognition; product roadmap input; VIP treatment (dedicated support, priority features, exclusive events).

Timeline: 1-2 months. Budget: 2-5% of overall marketing budget.
