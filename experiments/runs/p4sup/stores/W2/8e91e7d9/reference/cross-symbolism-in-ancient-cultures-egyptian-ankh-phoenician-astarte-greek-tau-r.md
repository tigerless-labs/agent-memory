Research on the cross symbol across ancient civilizations:\n\n**Egyptian**: Ankh symbolized life, immortality, balance between earthly (horizontal) and spiritual (vertical) realms; associated with regeneration and eternal life.\n\n**Phoenician**: Cross linked to goddess Astarte (fertility, sexuality); represented four directions and balance between earthly and spiritual realms.\n\n**Greek**: Tau symbol associated with god Tammuz; represented resurrection and regeneration; used in architecture and art as protective symbol.\n\n**Roman**: Initially symbol of punishment/crucifixion; later adopted by Christianity.\n\n**Christian**: Represents sacrifice and resurrection of Jesus Christ; vertical arm = divine, horizontal arm = human nature; central icon of Christianity.\n\n**Jewish**: Represents Tree of Life in Kabbalah tradition.\n\n**Hindu**: Represents intersection of physical and spiritual realms; associated with god Vishnu.\n\n**Buddhist**: Crossed arms of Buddha represent four immeasurables: love, compassion, joy, equanimity.\n\nCommon themes: balance, harmony, regeneration, divine nature, connection between earthly and spiritual realms.
