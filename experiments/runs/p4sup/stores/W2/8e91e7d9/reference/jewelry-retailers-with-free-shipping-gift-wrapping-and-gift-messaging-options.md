7. Jared - free shipping, gift message at checkout
8. Net-A-Porter - complimentary gift wrapping and gift message
9. Saks Fifth Avenue - free shipping, free gift wrapping
10. Tiffany & Co. - complimentary gift wrapping (luxury option)

All support 50-100 dollar silver jewelry range and offer expedited/fast shipping options.
