Practice plan: short 10-15 minute sessions at home to reinforce learning between classes. Focus areas: footwork patterns (forward/back, side to side), arm movements, body isolations. Approach: practice to salsa music, record progress, use YouTube tutorials as reference. Plan to master basics before learning full choreographies.
