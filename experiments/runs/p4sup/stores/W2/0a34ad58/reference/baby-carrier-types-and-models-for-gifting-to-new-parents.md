**Soft-structured carriers:** Ergobaby Omni 360, Lillebaby Complete, Tula Explore

**Wrap-style carriers:** Moby Wrap, Solly Baby Wrap, Boba Wrap

**Ring slings:** Maya Wrap, Sleeping Baby Productions (SBP) Ring Sling, Tula Ring Sling

**Mei Tai carriers:** Babyhawk Mei Tai, DidyTai

**Key considerations:** comfort (padded straps, supportive seat), adjustability for multiple parents, ease of use with intuitive design, breastfeeding support capability, appropriate weight range, machine washability.

**Tips:** try before buying if possible, read online reviews from other parents, check warranty/return policy.
