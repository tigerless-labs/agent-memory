Conversation script structure for 30-minute sales call: (1) Introduction 5 min — introduce self/org, brief overview of assessment, ask about goals/challenges; (2) Listen intently 10 min — let prospect share challenges, take notes, ask clarifying questions; (3) Present Fundraising Zen Assessment 10 min — explain three focus areas, outline deliverables (historical analysis, KPIs, revenue growth plan, campaign design, delegation calendar); (4) Upsell 5 min — discuss benefits, offer follow-up to discuss details; (5) Conclusion 5 min — thank them, confirm next steps. Heavy weighting on listening (10 min) and presentation (10 min) to earn buy-in before asking.
