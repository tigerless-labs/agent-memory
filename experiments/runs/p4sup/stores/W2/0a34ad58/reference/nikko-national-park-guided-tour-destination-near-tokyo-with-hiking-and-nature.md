## Nikko National Park\n\n**Overview:** Popular nature destination accessible from Tokyo, known for hiking trails and scenic beauty.\n\n**Reaching Nikko from Tokyo:**\n- JR Tohoku Shinkansen to Utsunomiya Station\n- Transfer to JR Nikko Line to reach Nikko Station\n- Can use Suica card for train fares\n\n**What to Prepare for Guided Tour:**\n- Weather-appropriate clothing, layers recommended\n- Sturdy, comfortable walking shoes for trails\n- Reusable water bottle and snacks\n- Sunscreen and hat\n- Camera or smartphone for photos\n- Tour booking confirmation and documents\n- Small amount of cash for purchases/emergencies\n\n**Tips for Visit:**\n- Guided tours handle logistics and transport from meeting point\n- Check weather forecast before packing\n- Bring waterproof jacket if rain expected\n- Some tours may provide meals; verify with provider\n- Ask concierge (e.g., Park Hyatt) for tour booking assistance
