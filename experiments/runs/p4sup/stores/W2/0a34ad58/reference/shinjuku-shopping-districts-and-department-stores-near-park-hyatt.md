## Shopping in Shinjuku (near Park Hyatt Tokyo)\n\n**Major Shopping Destinations (5-10 minute walk):**\n- Shinjuku Isetan: Massive department store with fashion, electronics, everything\n- Shinjuku Odakyu Shinjuku Mylord: Multiple stores, fashion, beauty, souvenirs\n- Shinjuku Lumine: Stylish mall focusing on fashion, accessories, lifestyle\n- Tokyu Hands: Wide range from daily essentials to unique souvenirs\n- Shinjuku Takashimaya: Large department store with fashion, beauty, food\n\n**Nearby Districts (10-25 minutes by taxi/subway):**\n- Harajuku: Fashion-forward, trendy boutiques, vintage shops, unique souvenirs\n- Omotesando: Stylish shopping street with high-end boutiques, art galleries, restaurants\n- Ginza: Luxury shopping with designer brands, jewelry, upscale department stores\n- Ikebukuro Parco: Trendy mall with fashion, entertainment, lifestyle goods\n\n**Tips:**\n- Shinjuku is one of Tokyo's premier shopping districts\n- Most shops within easy walking distance of Park Hyatt\n- Use Suica card at many stores\n- Peak shopping hours tend to be midday and evening
