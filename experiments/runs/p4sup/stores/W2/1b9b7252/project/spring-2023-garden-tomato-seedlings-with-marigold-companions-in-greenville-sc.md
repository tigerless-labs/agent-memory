Planning to plant tomato seedlings outside in spring 2023 (May) in Greenville, SC (29651). Planting marigolds as companions to deter nematodes.

**Climate expectations for Greenville, SC (spring):**
- Daytime temp: 68–75°F (20–24°C)
- Nighttime temp: 48–58°F (9–14°C)  
- Sunlight: 7–8 hrs (Mar), 8–9 hrs (Apr), 9–10 hrs (May)
- Plant when soil ≥55°F and air consistently >60°F

**Marigold spacing:** 6–12 inches from tomato plants. Mix French (Tagetes patula) and African marigold (Tagetes erecta) varieties for better pest deterrence. Plant marigolds same time or weeks before tomatoes.
