Spring 2023 garden planning includes multi-year crop rotation to break pest/disease cycles and optimize soil health.

**3-year rotation example:**
- Year 1: Tomatoes (Solanaceae)
- Year 2: Beans/peas (Legumes) — nitrogen-fixing to enrich soil
- Year 3: Cucumbers (Cucurbitaceae)

**Key families to rotate:**
- Solanaceae: tomatoes, peppers, eggplants, potatoes
- Legumes: beans, peas, lentils
- Cucurbitaceae: cucumbers, squash, melons
- Brassicas: broccoli, cauliflower, kale, cabbage
- Alliums: onions, garlic, leeks

**Best practices:**
- Avoid planting same crop in same bed for consecutive seasons
- Use cover crops to fill gaps and add organic matter
- Track crop performance and pest issues year-to-year
- Be flexible; adjust based on soil/pest changes
