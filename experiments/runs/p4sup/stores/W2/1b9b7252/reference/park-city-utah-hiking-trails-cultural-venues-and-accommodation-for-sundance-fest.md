**Popular Hiking Trails:**
- Jordanelle Reservoir Trail: 3.5-mile out-and-back, reservoir and mountain views
- Mid-Mountain Trail: 13.5 miles, aspen forests and meadows
- Guardsman Pass: 4.5-mile out-and-back, panoramic views
- Rob's Trail: 4.5-mile out-and-back, good for hiking and mountain biking

**Cultural Events & Attractions:**
- Sundance Film Festival: annual January festival, largest independent film festival in US
- Park City Museum: mining history exhibits
- Kimball Arts Festival: three-day festival in August with live music, food, 200+ artists
- Egyptian Theatre: historic theater with concerts, plays, musicals
- Park City Institute: cultural events including concerts, theater, lectures

**Hotels:**
- The Grand Summit Hotel: base of Park City Mountain, indoor pool, free shuttle to venues
- The Chateau at Deer Valley: luxury hotel at Deer Valley base, outdoor pool, free shuttle
- The Park City Peaks Hotel: downtown location, indoor pool, free shuttle

**Vacation Rentals:**
- Park City Mountain Resort Condos: fully equipped kitchens, fireplaces
- Deer Valley Resort Condos: private hot tubs, luxury amenities
- Park City Vacation Rentals: company offering cabins to townhomes

**Getting Around:** Free bus system connects resorts and town. Salt Lake City airport ~30-40 minutes away.

**Tips:** Book early as prices increase closer to Sundance dates. Properties with kitchens can reduce meal costs.
