Considering two camera bags for Nikon D5600 + lenses (18-55mm kit, 50mm prime):\n\n- **Lowepro ProTactic 450 AW**: Recommended for portrait/low-light work. More rugged, better weather protection, customizable compartments, fits 15-inch laptop. Heavier, bulkier.\n- **Think Tank Urban Disguise 40L**: Sleek urban design, lightweight, compact, fits 13-inch laptop. Smaller capacity, less weather protection, more fixed layout.\n\nRecommendation: ProTactic 450 AW for better gear protection given portrait/low-light focus.
