Plans to add a wide-angle lens to complement the 70-200mm zoom lens. Two focal lengths under consideration:\n- **14-24mm**: Ultra-wide angle, creates dramatic perspective distortion, ideal for sprawling landscapes, architecture, interior shots\n- **16-35mm**: Slightly less extreme, more versatile, good for landscapes, cityscapes, and environmental portraits\n\nMotivation: Wants to capture sweeping landscapes and cityscapes, and expand creative possibilities beyond the telephoto reach of the 70-200mm. Notes that wide-angle lenses require attention to distortion, vignetting, and flare.
