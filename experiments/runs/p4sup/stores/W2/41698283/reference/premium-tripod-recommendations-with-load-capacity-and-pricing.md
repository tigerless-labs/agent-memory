Evaluated tripods for Nikon D5600 and future Z6 upgrade:

**High-end options:**
- **Gitzo GT3543LS Systematic Series 3**: Carbon fiber, 39.7 lbs (18 kg) capacity, approximately 800-900 USD
- **Really Right Stuff TVC-34L**: Anodized aluminum, 50 lbs (23 kg) capacity, approximately 1000-1100 USD (top tier)
- **Manfrotto MT057CXPRO4**: Carbon fiber, 35.3 lbs (16 kg) capacity, approximately 600-700 USD (recommended mid-tier option)

**Budget option:**
- **Davis & Sanford Travel Tripod**: Aluminum, 25 lbs (11 kg) capacity, approximately 200-300 USD

Note: MT057CXPRO4 recommended for future-proofing with Z6; supports sufficient weight and offers good stability despite being lighter than RRS option.
