## Program Overview
- 320+ study abroad programs in 60+ countries
- Durations: from a few weeks to full academic year
- Available disciplines: language studies, anthropology, business, engineering, and more
- Popular regions: Europe, Asia, Latin America, Australia, China, Japan, South Korea

## Language Support
- Beginner, intermediate, and advanced Spanish language courses offered
- Department of Spanish and Portuguese Studies
- Rutgers Language Institute available

## Language Exchange Programs
- Conversation Cafés: Casual practice sessions with native speakers in multiple languages (including Spanish)
- Language Exchange Program: Matches fluent speakers with learners for regular practice
- Global Ambassadors Program: Pairs international students with American students for language practice and cultural exchange

## Financial Resources
- Federal financial aid: Pell Grants, Stafford Loans, PLUS Loans
- New Jersey state aid: Tuition Aid Grant, Educational Opportunity Fund
- Rutgers scholarships: Global Experience Scholarship, Study Abroad Need-Based Scholarship, International Public Service Award
- External scholarships: Gilman Scholarship, Fund for Education Abroad, Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship
- Work-study opportunities abroad

## Application Process
1. Research programs on Rutgers Global website
2. Meet with Rutgers Global advisor
3. Complete online application
4. Submit required documents (letters of recommendation, transcripts, statement of purpose)
5. Receive decision and preparation materials
