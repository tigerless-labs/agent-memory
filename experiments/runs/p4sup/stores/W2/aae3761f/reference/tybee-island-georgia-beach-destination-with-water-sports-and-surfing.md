Laid-back beach town in Georgia, ~7-8 hours from Outer Banks. Known for gentle waves suitable for surfing, paddleboarding, and kayaking. Has multiple water sports rental shops and lesson options. Lively downtown with restaurants, bars, and shops. Chosen over Chincoteague Island for better wave conditions and more water sports infrastructure.
