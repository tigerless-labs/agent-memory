**Preparation:**
- Brown beef in Dutch oven on medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes per side until deep brown crust forms
- Use chuck roast or round cut, cut into bite-sized pieces
- Season with salt, pepper, thyme, rosemary before browning

**Potatoes:**
Preferred varieties: Yukon Gold or Russet (hold shape well)
- Cut into 1-2 inch cubes
- Add to slow cooker for last 2-3 hours for tender texture or last 1-2 hours for firmer texture
- Optional: soak in cold water 30 minutes before adding to reduce starchiness

**Slow cooking:**
- Low: 8-10 hours
- High: 4-6 hours
- Add beef broth and optional red wine, tomato paste, diced tomatoes

**Serving:**
- Crusty bread (baguette, ciabatta, rustic) for dipping
- Simple green salad with light vinaigrette for contrast
