North Carolina: Topsail Island (seafood, turtle center), Wilmington (Riverwalk, USS North Carolina). South Carolina: Georgetown (historic waterfront, antiques), Hunting Island State Park (lighthouse, nature trails), Edisto Beach State Park. Georgia: Savannah (historic district, Forsyth Park). Drive times: Outer Banks to Topsail 2h, Topsail to Tybee 4-5h (7-8h total with stops).
