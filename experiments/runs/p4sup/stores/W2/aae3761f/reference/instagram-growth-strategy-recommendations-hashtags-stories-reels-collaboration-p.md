Hashtag strategy: use 5-10 relevant hashtags per post. Key hashtags: #photography, #landscapephotography, #travelphotography. Use hashtag research tools like Hashtagify or RiteTag.

Instagram Stories: post 3-5 Stories per day. Content ideas include behind-the-scenes, Q&A sessions, polls/surveys, swipe-up links, and themed content (e.g. Motivation Monday). Use Highlights to showcase best Stories on profile.

IGTV: create long-form tutorials, gear reviews, vlogs, interviews, and photography challenges. Optimize with captions and graphics for sound-off viewing.

Reels: create 60-second videos showcasing photography skills, gear, and behind-the-scenes content. Participate in challenges and use relevant hashtags.

Live: host Q&A sessions, workshops, tutorials, and real-time engagement with followers.

Finding collaborators and brands: join 500px, Flickr, photography forums. Use platforms AspireIQ, Grin, Upfluence for brand collaborations. Join Instagram and Facebook photography groups. Attend photography workshops, conferences, meetups. Reach out directly to photographers and brands in niche.

Content calendar: plan 2-4 weeks in advance using Google Calendar, Excel, Hootsuite, or Buffer. Suggested content themes: Gear Reviews, Photography Tips, Behind-the-Scenes. Track analytics and refine strategy based on results. Leave room for spontaneous content.
