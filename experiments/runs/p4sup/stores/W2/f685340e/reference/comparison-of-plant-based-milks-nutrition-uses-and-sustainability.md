**Almond milk:** Low calories, rich in vitamins, neutral slightly nutty flavor. Good for cereal, smoothies, desserts, baking (cakes, muffins). High water usage (~1.1 gallons per almond), grown mainly in drought-prone California, pesticide use.\n\n**Soy milk:** High protein, isoflavones, slightly nutty earthy flavor. Excellent for savory dishes, creamy sauces, Asian-inspired. Major deforestation contributor (Amazon), high water and fertilizer use.\n\n**Cashew milk:** Creamy texture, rich in magnesium, mild slightly sweet flavor. Ideal for creamy sauces (Alfredo, hollandaise), desserts (cheesecake, ice cream). Lower water use than almonds, fair trade concerns, pesticide use.\n\n**Oat milk:** Low calories, high fiber, naturally lactose-free, neutral slightly sweet flavor. Good for cereal, smoothies, desserts, baking. Most sustainable: low water use, fewer pesticides, local production possible, crop rotation benefits.\n\n**Choosing factors:** Allergies, flavor profile, nutritional needs, desired texture, environmental impact. Look for eco-certifications (Fairtrade, Organic, Regenerative), local brands, research company practices.
