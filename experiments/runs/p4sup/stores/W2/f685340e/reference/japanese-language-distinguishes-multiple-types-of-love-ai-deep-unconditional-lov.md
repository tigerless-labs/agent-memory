The Japanese language has multiple words for 'love' that reflect cultural values and levels of emotional intensity. This contrasts with English, which often uses a single word 'love' for multiple contexts.\n\nExample words:\n- **ai** (愛): deep and unconditional love\n- **koi** (恋): romantic love  \n- **daisuki** (大好き): liking or affection\n\nThe existence of these distinct terms suggests a cultural emphasis on precision in emotional expression and the importance of context when discussing love.
