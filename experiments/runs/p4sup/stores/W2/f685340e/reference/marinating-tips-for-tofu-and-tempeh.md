**For Kung Pao Tofu:**\n- Press tofu 30+ minutes to remove excess liquid (helps marinade penetrate)\n- Use acid + oil mixture: soy sauce, rice vinegar, cornstarch, oil (peanut/vegetable)\n- Add aromatics: minced garlic, ginger, scallions\n- Don't over-marinate: max 30 min to 1 hour (tofu becomes salty/mushy)\n- Fry in hot wok with flavorful oil (peanut/avocado) after marinating\n\n**For Tempeh 'Fish' Tacos:**\n- Crumble tempeh and soak in flavorful liquid: lime juice, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika (30 min to 1 hour)\n- Use citrus-based marinade (breaks down bitterness, adds brightness)\n- Add umami: nutritional yeast or miso paste\n- Don't over-soak: max 1-2 hours (prevents sogginess)\n- Pat dry with paper towels after marinating, then pan-fry/bake until crispy\n\n**General tips:**\n- Use ziplock bag or shallow dish; ensure even coating\n- Massage marinade into tofu/tempeh to help absorption\n- Marinate in refrigerator (prevents bacterial growth)\n- Experiment with flavor combinations
