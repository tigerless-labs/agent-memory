**Dark Sky** — minute-by-minute forecasts for exact location; highly accurate; especially useful for hikers to predict precipitation.

**Mountain Weather Forecast** — specifically designed for mountain weather; includes snowfall, avalanche risk, and wind conditions.

Also mentioned: Weather Underground, OpenSnow, The Weather Channel, AccuWeather, National Weather Service (NWS).
