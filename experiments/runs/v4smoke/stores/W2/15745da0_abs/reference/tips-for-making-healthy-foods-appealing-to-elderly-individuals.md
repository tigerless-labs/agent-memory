Strategies for getting elderly individuals to eat nutritious foods:

1. **Get creative with recipes** — find new ways to incorporate brain-healthy foods they enjoy (e.g., fruit salads with berries, roasted vegetables with herbs)

2. **Incorporate into familiar dishes** — add spinach/broccoli to pasta sauce; add berries or flaxseed to smoothies

3. **Presentation matters** — use colorful plates, cut fruits/veggies into fun shapes, add garnishes

4. **Consider texture** — those with chewing/swallowing difficulties may prefer pureed foods, steamed or soft roasted vegetables

5. **Involve them in the process** — ask what they enjoy, involve in meal planning and preparation to increase engagement and investment

6. **Be patient** — allow time to adjust to new flavors/textures, introduce in small amounts
