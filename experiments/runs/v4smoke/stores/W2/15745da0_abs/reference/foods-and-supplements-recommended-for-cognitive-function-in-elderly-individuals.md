Foods and supplements with evidence for improving cognitive function in elderly:

**Omega-3 fatty acids:** salmon, tuna, mackerel — improve memory and cognitive function

**Antioxidants:** berries, spinach, kale — reduce oxidative damage in brain

**B vitamins:** B6, B9 (folate), B12 — found in leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals

**Other:** caffeine (coffee/tea), curcumin (turmeric with anti-inflammatory properties)

**Easy healthy snacks:**
- Apple slices or celery with almond/peanut butter
- Roasted chickpeas (paprika, garlic powder, salt)
- Greek yogurt with berries and honey
- Hummus with carrots, celery, or pita chips
- Trail mix (nuts, dried fruit, dark chocolate)
- Whole-grain crackers with avocado
- Smoothies (frozen fruit, Greek yogurt, spinach/kale)
- Energy balls (dates, nuts, coconut)
