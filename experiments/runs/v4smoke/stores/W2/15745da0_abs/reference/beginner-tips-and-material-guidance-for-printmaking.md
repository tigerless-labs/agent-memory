Five key tips for beginners: (1) Start with relief printing, minimal equipment; (2) Experiment with different materials to understand their effects; (3) Don't fear mistakes, adjust and try again; (4) Take a class/workshop for feedback; (5) Practice consistently. Brayer selection depends on: ink type (soft for water-based, hard for oil-based), surface texture, print size, design detail level, and personal experience. Material impacts: ink affects effects/textures; paper thickness affects boldness; different plate materials produce different textures; different presses produce different effects.
