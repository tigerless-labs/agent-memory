Organizations: American Institute for Conservation (AIC), International Institute for Conservation (IIC), Conservation Wiki, YouTube channels (Art Conservation, Conservation Framing, Getty Conservation Institute). Books: The Conservation of Art and Architecture by Watt, The Art of Conservation by Keck, Conservation of Cultural Heritage by Stubbs. Online platforms: Coursera, edX, Udemy. Key adhesives: PVA glue, Acryloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV, hide glue, rice/wheat starch paste, epoxy resins. Consolidants: Paraloid B-72, Butvar B-98, Parylene, silanes. Conservation papers: cotton, linen, rag, kozu (Japanese mulberry), gampi.
