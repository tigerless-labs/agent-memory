LG OLED: webOS platform, exceptional panel quality, wide color gamut, Dolby Vision support, good for gaming. Sony OLED: X1 Ultimate processor, Acoustic Surface Audio, Android TV, HDMI 2.1 support, stronger gaming features. LG slightly more affordable; both have excellent picture quality.
