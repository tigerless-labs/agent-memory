Currently exploring themes of identity, memory, and the human experience through interactive and immersive art installations.

**Specific Installation Concepts:**

1. **Sensory Environment Installation**: Creating an installation that recreates the sensation of being in a specific environment or memory. Example concept: simulating the experience of walking through a forest with sounds of birds and rustling leaves, plus the scent of damp earth. Uses multiple sensory channels (sound, light, texture).

2. **Collective Memory Installation**: Exploring how individual experiences are shaped by shared cultural and historical contexts. Plans to incorporate found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods to create a sense of shared human experience.

**Technologies of Interest:**
- 3D spatial audio
- Projection mapping
- Scent diffusion
- Haptic feedback
- Data visualization for representing cultural/temporal connections
- Augmented reality
- Interactive storytelling platforms
- Programming tools: Unity, Max/MSP, OpenFrameworks, Arduino

**Goals:**
- Use interactive and immersive installations to engage audiences and create empathy and connection
- Get work seen by a wider audience (per curator advice received at 2023-01-22 event)
