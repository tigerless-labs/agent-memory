**Decision:** Will acquire a fiddle leaf fig (FLF) for the living room as part of the living room redesign.

**Motivation:** Loves the unique, violin-shaped leaves and willing to provide the extra care required.

**Care Commitment:** Acknowledged that FLFs are finicky but willing to invest the effort. Plans to:
- Start with a smaller plant (1-2 feet tall)
- Provide bright, indirect light (room has good natural light)
- Monitor soil moisture carefully and water when top 1-2 inches feel dry
- Maintain humidity with pebbles and water tray or humidifier
- Research and join online communities for guidance

**Companion Plant:** Will complement the existing snake plant already in the living room.
