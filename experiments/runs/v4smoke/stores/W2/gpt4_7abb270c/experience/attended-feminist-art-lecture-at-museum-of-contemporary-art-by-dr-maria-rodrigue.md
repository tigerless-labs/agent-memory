Attended a lecture series on the role of feminist art in the 1970s at the Museum of Contemporary Art. The speaker was Dr. Maria Rodriguez. During the reception afterward, made professional connections with several artists working on feminist art projects and exchanged contact information for potential future collaboration. Also met a curator who offered advice on getting artistic work seen by a wider audience. The event was enriching for understanding the historical context and continued relevance of feminist art.
