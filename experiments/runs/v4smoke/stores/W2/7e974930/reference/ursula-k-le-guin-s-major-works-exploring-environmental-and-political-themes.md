Discussed Le Guin's major works including:
- **The Dispossessed** (1974) - anarchism and human-environment relationship
- **The Left Hand of Darkness** (1969) - gender, sexuality, and environmental themes
- **The Word for World is Forest** (1976) - imperialism, colonialism, and human-environment relations
- **The Lathe of Heaven** (1971) - science fiction and environmentalism
- **Always Coming Home** (1985) - post-apocalyptic exploration of environmentalism and ecology

Le Guin known for exploring ecological, social, and political issues in her fiction; critique of capitalist ideologies and destructive human impact on the environment.
