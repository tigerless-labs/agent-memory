**Fujifilm X-T30 II Specs & Features:**\n\n- Sensor: 26.1MP APS-C\n- Autofocus: Intelligent Hybrid AF with 425 phase-detection points, 0.02s response time, 99% success rate\n- Display: Vari-angle touchscreen LCD\n- Viewfinder: Electronic\n- Weather sealing: Moisture-resistant (not fully weather-sealed)\n- Battery life: 380 frames per charge (EVF), 440 frames (LCD); 45 min video (EVF), 60 min (LCD)\n- AF subject tracking with AI prediction\n- Face and eye detection\n- Low-light AF: -3EV minimum\n- Customizable AF zones (zone, wide/tracking, single-point)\n- Body only: USD 899\n- Kit lens (XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ): USD 499\n\nKey advantage over higher-end models (X-T3, X-H1): More compact and affordable while maintaining excellent AF performance and image quality for landscape photography.
