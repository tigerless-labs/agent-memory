**Swimming Workouts for Triathlon:**\n- Endurance sets: 4-6 sets of 200-400m at moderate pace (60-70% effort), 20-30s rest\n- Interval training: 25-50m high intensity (80-90%), 15-25s rest, 8-12 reps\n- Pyramid workout: progressive distance increases/decreases (25m → 100m → 25m)\n- Sprint intervals with kickboard: 25m high intensity, 15-25s rest, 8-12 reps\n- Long steady-state: 10-20 minutes at moderate pace (60-70% effort)\n\n**Triathlon-Specific Training:**\n- Brick workouts: swim+bike or swim+run transitions to simulate race conditions\n- Open-water practice: build comfort with wetsuit, sighting, navigation\n- Focus on technique: body position, core engagement, hip power generation\n\n**Cross-Training:**\n- Yoga: 1-2x weekly on rest/easy days for flexibility, injury prevention, recovery\n- Strength: shoulders, back, core (lat pull-downs, planks, rotations)\n- Drills: kicking drills, arm circles, catch-up drill\n\n**Motivation & Tracking:**\n- Log all workouts with distance, time, effort level, and notes\n- Set specific, achievable goals and break into smaller milestones\n- Use tracking apps (Strava, Swim.com, Garmin) or spreadsheets\n- Find a swim buddy or join training group for accountability\n- Celebrate small wins and progress markers
