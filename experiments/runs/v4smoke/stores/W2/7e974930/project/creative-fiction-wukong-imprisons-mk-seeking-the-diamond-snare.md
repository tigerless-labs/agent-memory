# Wukong/MK Fantasy Story

## Setup
A dark fantasy story where the legendary Monkey King Wukong has captured and imprisoned a human character named MK behind a magical barrier.

## Characters
- **MK**: Human who knows legends about Wukong from childhood; imprisoned, desperate, hungry, scared
- **Wukong**: The Monkey King; cruel and merciless; searching for a magical artifact called the diamond snare; views humans as weak and pathetic

## Plot Arc (Chronological)
1. Wukong interrogates MK about the diamond snare's location—MK genuinely doesn't know, only knows old stories
2. Wukong considers the question aloud: why keep MK alive if he's useless?
3. MK argues he should be kept as "insurance"—won't reveal Wukong's plans if released
4. Wukong reluctantly agrees to spare MK's life for now
5. Days later, MK tries to get Wukong's attention by complaining about hunger/thirst—backfires badly
6. A subordinate monkey brings MK minimal food and water (some fruit, small container)
7. MK reflects on the contradiction: the heroic Wukong of legend vs. the cruel captor reality; he's confused and scared
8. MK hears monkeys having a loud feast nearby; desperate to escape and steal food, but his attempts fail
9. MK begs Wukong for food/water again, using logic: "You can't use me as insurance if I starve to death"
10. Wukong is annoyed but convinced by the practical argument; gives MK a bowl of water and stale bread; warns him not to be greedy

## Tone & Themes
- Dark fantasy with extreme power imbalance
- Physical and psychological suffering
- Desperation and survival
- Cognitive dissonance (legendary hero vs. actual cruelty)
- Wukong's pragmatism overriding his disdain, barely

## Key Dynamics
- Wukong is irritated by MK's pleas but sometimes swayed by practical reasoning
- MK combines respect/admiration for the legend with fear of the man
- Wukong occasionally shows calculation (keeping MK alive as potential leverage, sustaining him minimally)
