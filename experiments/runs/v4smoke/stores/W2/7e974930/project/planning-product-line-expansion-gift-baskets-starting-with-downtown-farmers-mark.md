Considering expanding product line to include gift baskets as a revenue stream and cross-selling opportunity.

**Planned first offering:** Downtown Farmers Market themed gift basket
- Feature best-selling soaps and candles
- Appeal to locals and tourists with emotional connection to the market
- Possible additions: personalized note, market memento/sticker, custom packaging, discount code for future market purchases

**Rationale:** Increases average order value, attracts new customers, creates unique offering with strong local appeal.
