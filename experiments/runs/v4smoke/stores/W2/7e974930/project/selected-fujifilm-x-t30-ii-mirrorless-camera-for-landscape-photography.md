Decided to purchase Fujifilm X-T30 II mirrorless camera for landscape and nature photography. Budget: approximately 1,500 USD for body and lens. Key features valued: compact size, vari-angle touchscreen LCD, fast autofocus (0.02 second response, 425 phase-detection points), hybrid AF system. Does not require full weather sealing but appreciates moisture-resistant design. Planning to purchase XF 15-45mm kit lens initially (approximately 499 USD) and add additional lenses later. No existing lens collection to migrate.
