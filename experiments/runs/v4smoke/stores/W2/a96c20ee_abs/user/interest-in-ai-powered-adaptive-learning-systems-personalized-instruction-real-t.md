## AI in Education Interests

Focused interests in how AI can enhance learning:

- **Personalized learning systems:** How AI tailors instruction to individual students' needs, abilities, and learning styles
- **Adaptive learning & real-time feedback:** Mechanisms for immediate feedback in AI systems and their impact on student outcomes
- **Critical thinking & problem-solving:** AI's role in helping students develop these essential skills through argumentation, inquiry-based learning, and simulations
- **Collaborative problem-solving:** How AI can facilitate teamwork, role assignment, communication analysis, and resource allocation in group problem-solving contexts

Particularly interested in research evidence and effectiveness data for these applications.
