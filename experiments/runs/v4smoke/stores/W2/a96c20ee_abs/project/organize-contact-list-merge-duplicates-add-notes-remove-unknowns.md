Plan to organize phone contacts:
1. Go through contact list and merge duplicate entries
2. Add notes to contacts (how met, relationship, important dates)
3. Delete unknown contacts or those no longer needed
4. Categorize contacts (friends, family, work, services)
5. Use consistent naming convention across contacts

Has many numbers and names in contact list that they're unsure about.
