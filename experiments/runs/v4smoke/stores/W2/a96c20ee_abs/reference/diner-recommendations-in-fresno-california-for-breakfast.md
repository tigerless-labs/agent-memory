**Max's Bistro & Bar** — Known for generous portions and creative breakfast dishes. Signature "Max's Breakfast" burrito with scrambled eggs, chorizo.\n\n**The Breakfast Club** — Retro-style diner. Menu features breakfast tacos, waffle sandwiches, omelets.\n\n**Pete's Breakfast House** — Local institution for 30+ years. Famous for giant pancakes, omelets, breakfast combos.\n\n**Frank's Place** — No-frills diner operating since 1947. Popular for breakfast/brunch. Famous "Frank's Favorite" breakfast plate with eggs, bacon, sausage.\n\n**Denny's** — Chain diner with multiple Fresno locations. Wide variety of breakfast options including Grand Slam.\n\nUser planning to stop at diner in Fresno on way back from future hikes, especially interested in breakfast burritos.
