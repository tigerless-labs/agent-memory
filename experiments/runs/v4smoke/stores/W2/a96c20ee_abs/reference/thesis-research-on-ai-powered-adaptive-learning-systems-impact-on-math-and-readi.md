Research on effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes in math and reading comprehension. Found that systems can be effective but implementation and classroom integration require careful consideration.

Key challenges: teacher professional development and skills gaps, curriculum alignment, algorithmic bias risk, teacher concerns about replacement, technical infrastructure needs, data privacy, and equity/access disparities.

Recommendations: prioritize ongoing teacher professional development, ensure developer-educator collaboration during design, provide clear curriculum alignment guidance, implement ongoing evaluation, address algorithmic bias.

Approached with broad conceptual focus supported by several specific platform/system case studies. Presented as poster at first research conference (summer 2023), received positive feedback from educators and policymakers; developers reached out afterward.
