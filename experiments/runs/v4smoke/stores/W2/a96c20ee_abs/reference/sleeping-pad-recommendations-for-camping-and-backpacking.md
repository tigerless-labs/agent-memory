**Inflatable pads** (lightweight, compact, comfortable):\n- Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite: 11.8 oz (335g), 2.5 in thick\n- Therm-a-Rest NeoAir XLite: 12.5 oz (354g), 2.8 in thick  \n- Big Agnes Insulated AXL: 9.8 oz (278g), 2.5 in thick\n- NEMO Equipment Astro: 10.5 oz (298g), 2.5 in thick\n- Sea to Summit Ether Light XT: 13.9 oz (394g), 3.5 in thick\n\n**Foam pads** (durable, affordable, good insulation):\n- Therm-a-Rest Z Lite\n- RidgeRest Classic\n\n**Hybrid pads** (comfort + durability):\n- Therm-a-Rest Trail Pro\n- Sea to Summit Ether Light XT\n\nConsider: insulation (R-value), comfort (thickness), weight, packability, durability, and personal preferences.
