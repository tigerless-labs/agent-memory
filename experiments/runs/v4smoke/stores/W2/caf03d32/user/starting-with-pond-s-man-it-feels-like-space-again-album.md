Decided to begin exploration of psychedelic rock recommendations with Pond's 2015 album 'Man It Feels Like Space Again'.

Plan to pay attention to:
- How Pond uses synthesizers and keyboards for atmosphere and depth
- Standout tracks: 'Waiting Around for Grace', 'Hand Mouth Dancer', 'Sweep Me Off My Feet'
- Comparison with Billie Eilish's use of atmospheric soundscapes

This album was chosen as a starting point after learning about multiple psychedelic/atmospheric artists.
