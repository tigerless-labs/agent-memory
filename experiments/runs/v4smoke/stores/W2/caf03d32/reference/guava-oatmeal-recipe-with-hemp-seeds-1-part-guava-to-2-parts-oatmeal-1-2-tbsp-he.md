**Recipe proportions:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1 part guava to 2 parts oatmeal)
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- Optional: honey or maple syrup, pinch of salt

**Hemp seed additions:**
- 1-2 tablespoons per serving
- Provides 5-10g protein and 1-2g omega-3s per serving
- Can grind in coffee grinder for better incorporation with oatmeal
- Mix with small amount of liquid first to create paste, then mix into cooked oatmeal
- Start with 1 tablespoon if new to hemp seeds (nutty flavor can be overpowering)
- Store in airtight container in fridge or freezer

**Optional toppings:** sliced banana, chopped walnuts/almonds, shredded coconut, cinnamon, protein powder

**Nutritional content per serving:**
- Oatmeal (1 cup cooked): 6g protein, 4g fiber
- Guava (1/2 cup diced): 2g protein, 4g fiber
- Chia seeds (1 tbsp): 2g protein, 4g fiber
- Hemp seeds (1 tbsp): 2.6g protein, 1.2g omega-3s
