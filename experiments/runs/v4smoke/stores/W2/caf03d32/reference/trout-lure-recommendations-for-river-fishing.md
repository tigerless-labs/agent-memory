For trout fishing in rivers, recommended streamers include Woolly Bugger (versatile, good for moderate-to-fast currents with sink-tip line) and Muddler Minnow (slim profile, good for rocky bottoms). Also recommended: dry flies (Elk Hair Caddis, Stimulator, Blue Wing Olive), nymphs (Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail, Copper John), spinners/spoons (Mepps Aglia, Rooster Tail, Acme Kastmaster), soft plastics (Curly Tail Grub, PowerBait). Key brands: Simms, Orvis, Umpqua, Rio Products, Mepps, Acme.
