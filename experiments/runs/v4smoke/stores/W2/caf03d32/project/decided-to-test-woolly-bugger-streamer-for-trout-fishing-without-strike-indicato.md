Plans to start testing Woolly Bugger streamer for trout fishing in rivers without using a strike indicator. Will rely on feeling takes through the marabou tail action and line sensitivity. Reasoning: the marabou tail has sufficient action to detect takes, and a strike indicator would reduce natural presentation.
