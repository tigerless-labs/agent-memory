Camped for two nights at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. Day 1: Hiked to McWay Falls. Day 2: Shorter hike to Pfeiffer Beach. Brought portable power bank which was a lifesaver for navigation and capturing memories with camera/devices. Attempted astrophotography shots but results weren't as great as hoped - still learning night photography. First time cooking camping food; experience was okay, looking to improve skills.
