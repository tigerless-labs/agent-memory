Uses Libro.fm for audiobooks. Listens during daily commute (approximately 1 hour each way). Has been impressed with the quality of narrators on Libro.fm.

**Genre interests:**
- Currently enjoys: romance, mystery novels
- Exploring: science fiction, fantasy audiobooks
- Recently branching into: non-fiction on self-improvement and productivity

**Listening preferences:**
- Open to different speeds; prefers to start at comfortable speed (1x) and adjust as needed
- Values well-narrated audiobooks with good character work and pacing
- Interested in character-driven narratives with strong world-building
