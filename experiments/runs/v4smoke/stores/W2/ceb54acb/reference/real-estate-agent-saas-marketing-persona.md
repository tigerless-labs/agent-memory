## Persona: Susan Green
Demographics: 55-year-old female in suburban/rural USA, median income $61,140
Education: Bachelor's degree in Real Estate or related field

Goals: Boost visibility, increase lead generation, stay competitive, streamline marketing

Tech usage: CRM, email marketing, social media, open to new solutions

Marketing channels: Online listings, networking, direct mail, social media ads
