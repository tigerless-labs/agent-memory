Films using symbolism effectively per discussion:

**The Godfather**: Oranges symbolize impending danger and death; appear before violent events or character deaths (Don Corleone fruit market shooting, assassination attempt in garden)

**The Shining** (1980, Kubrick): Maze represents the mind; red color symbolizes danger and violence

**Blade Runner** (1982, Scott): Eyes, animals, nature symbolize themes of identity, humanity, existence

**Get Out** (2017, Peele): White color represents oppressive racism; deer imagery symbolizes victimhood  

**The Matrix** (1999): Red and blue pills symbolize choice and free will; explores nature of reality and human perception

Counter-examples of great films without heavy symbolism: Casablanca (1942), The Shawshank Redemption (1994), Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope (1977)
