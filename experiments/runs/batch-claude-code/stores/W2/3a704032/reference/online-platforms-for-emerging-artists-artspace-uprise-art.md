Artspace and Uprise Art are curated online platforms specializing in emerging contemporary artists. Both platforms feature artist profiles, online exhibitions, and artworks available for purchase. They offer personalized recommendations and curated collections to help discover new artists based on interests. Also recommended: Artsy, Saatchi Art, Behance, Instagram (artist hashtags), Juxtapoz, Hyperallergic, New American Paintings, ArtNet.
