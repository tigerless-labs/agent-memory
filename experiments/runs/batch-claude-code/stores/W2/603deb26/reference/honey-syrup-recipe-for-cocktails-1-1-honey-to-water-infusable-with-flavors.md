Honey Syrup Recipe: Use 1:1 ratio of honey to water. Heat over low heat until honey dissolves, whisking gently. Cool before using; store in fridge up to 2 weeks. Can add citrus peel, herbs, spices during dissolving. Try honey-ginger variation with sliced ginger. Experiment with different honey types (clover, manuka, acacia) for distinct flavors. Used in Bee's Knees cocktail (gin, honey syrup, lemon juice).
