Recipes being tried: Lavender Gin Fizz (1.5oz gin, 0.5oz lime, 0.5oz lavender syrup, 0.5oz simple, soda water), Grapefruit Basil Martini (2oz gin, 1oz grapefruit juice, 0.5oz lime, 0.5oz basil syrup), Pimm's Cup (Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit), Bee's Knees (gin, honey syrup, lemon), Lavender Grapefruit Spritz, Grapefruit Lavender Collins. Other classics to explore: Gin Rickey, Gin-Gin Mule, Aviation, Monkey Gland, Last Word. Interested in trying London Dry Gin, Plymouth Gin, and New Western/New World gin styles.
