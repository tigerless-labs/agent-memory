Soak 1 cup cashews 4+ hours, drain and rinse. Blend with 1/2 cup water for 2 minutes until smooth. Strain through cheesecloth, nut milk bag, or fine-mesh sieve. Less water = thicker; more water = thinner. Chill 2+ hours.
