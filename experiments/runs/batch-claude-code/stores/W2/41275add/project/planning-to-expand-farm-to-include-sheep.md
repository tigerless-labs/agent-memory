Researching sheep care costs including vaccinations and feed. Resources reviewed: National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP), American Sheep Industry Association (ASI), University Extension Services, Sheep 101. Estimated vaccination costs: CD-T vaccine 1.50-3.00 per dose; CDT with selenium 2.50-5.00; pneumonia vaccines 3.00-6.00. Feed costs: hay 100-200 per ton, grain 150-300 per ton, minerals/supplements 20-50 per month.
