Core vaccinations to consider: Eastern and Western Encephalomyelitis (EEE/WEE) annually in spring; Tetanus annually or every 5-7 years; West Nile Virus (WNV) annually in spring. Risk-based: Influenza annually or bi-annually; Rabies annually or every 3 years; Strangles annually or bi-annually. Need to consult veterinarian for customized schedule and preparation: clean area, separate horses if needed, ensure good health, have medical history ready, allow veterinarian access on day of appointment.
