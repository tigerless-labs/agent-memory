Key feminist artists explored: Judy Chicago (The Dinner Party), Miriam Schapiro, Carolee Schneemann (performance art), Guerrilla Girls (feminist activist collective), Barbara Kruger (graphic text-based works), Cindy Sherman (photographer and filmmaker), Laurie Anderson (multimedia artist and performer). These artists challenged traditional representations of gender, identity, and power through various media.
