Key abstract expressionist artists: Jackson Pollock (gesture and abstraction), Mark Rothko (color fields and emotion), Willem de Kooning (gestural abstraction), Franz Kline (bold gestures), Clyfford Still (abstract forms, muted palette), Ad Reinhardt, Barnett Newman. Movement emphasized scale, materiality, emotional intensity, and the artist's gesture and subconscious expression. Artists explored color, abstraction, and the viewer's emotional response.
