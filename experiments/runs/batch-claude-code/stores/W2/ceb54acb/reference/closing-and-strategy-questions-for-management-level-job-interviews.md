## Strong Closing Questions for Job Interviews (Promotion Level)

Use these when interviewing for Manager, supervisor, or leadership roles to demonstrate strategic thinking and readiness.

**Addressing Concerns** - Is there anything that concerns you about my qualifications or experience for this role?

**Strategic Fit** - How does this role fit into the broader goals and objectives of the department and organization?

**Upcoming Initiatives** - Can you describe upcoming initiatives this manager role will be involved in?

**Professional Development** - How can I continue developing skills to excel in this role?

**Future Challenges** - What challenges or opportunities do you foresee for the department?

**Success Metrics** - How does the department measure success for this manager role? What are the KPIs?

**Support & Resources** - What support and resources will be available to help me succeed?

**Collaboration** - How can I best communicate and collaborate with other departments and stakeholders?

**Values Alignment** - What qualities are most important in this manager, and how do my strengths align?

**Timeline** - What is the timeline for the decision, and when will I hear about next steps?

These questions show strategic thinking, genuine interest in organizational context, and commitment to success. Especially effective for information desk management and similar supervisory promotions.
