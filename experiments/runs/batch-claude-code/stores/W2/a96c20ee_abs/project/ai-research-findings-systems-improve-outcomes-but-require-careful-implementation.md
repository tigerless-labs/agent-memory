Key findings from research: Teacher professional development is essential. When AI systems clearly align with curriculum and learning goals, teachers are more likely to adopt them. Algorithms carry risk of bias that could exacerbate inequities if not addressed. Systems work best when designed with teacher and student needs in mind.
