Unique recipe combining sweet and savory flavors. Inspired by blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local chocolate shop. Ingredients include butter, sugar, crumbled blue cheese, eggs, vanilla, flour, baking soda, salt, semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375°F for 12-15 minutes.
