OTC medication options for pollen allergy symptom management:
- Antihistamines: reduce sneezing, itching, runny nose
- Decongestants: relieve nasal congestion
- Nasal corticosteroid sprays: reduce inflammation and swelling in nasal passages
- Cromolyn sodium nasal spray: prevents symptoms by stopping histamine release
- Saline nasal rinses: clear mucus and irritants from nasal passages

Important: Read instructions carefully, check for side effects and drug interactions. Consult healthcare provider if symptoms persist despite OTC treatments.
