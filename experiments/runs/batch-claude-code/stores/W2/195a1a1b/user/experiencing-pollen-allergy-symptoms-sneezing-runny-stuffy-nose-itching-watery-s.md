Suspects pollen allergy based on symptoms experienced. Symptoms include: sneezing, runny or stuffy nose, itchy nose/throat/ears, watery/red/swollen eyes (allergic conjunctivitis), cough, postnasal drip, fatigue/weakness, headache. May be seasonal or year-round depending on pollen type. Planning to consult doctor for diagnosis and treatment.
