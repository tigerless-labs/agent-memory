Suggestions given on 2023-05-23: eBay, Etsy, Ruby Lane, The Typewriter Shop, Typewriter Collector, Gramercy Typewriter, The Typewriter Forum, and Reddit's r/typewriters. Suggested researching comparable sales, cleaning or restoring appropriately, documenting condition and flaws, testing the machine with a test page or video, including original cases, ribbons, or manuals, and packaging carefully.
