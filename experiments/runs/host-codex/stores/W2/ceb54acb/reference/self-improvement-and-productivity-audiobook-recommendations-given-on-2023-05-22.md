Recommendations: The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey, narrated by Stephen Covey; Daring Greatly: How the Courage to Be Vulnerable Transforms the Way We Live, Love, Parent, and Lead by Brené Brown, narrated by Brené Brown; Mindset: The New Psychology of Success by Carol S. Dweck, narrated by Bernadette Dunne; Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World by Cal Newport, narrated by Jeff Bottoms; Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less by Greg McKeown, narrated by Greg McKeown; Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones by James Clear, narrated by James Clear; Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind by Yuval Noah Harari, narrated by Derek Perkins; The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle, narrated by Eckhart Tolle; and Shoe Dog: A Memoir by the Creator of Nike by Phil Knight, narrated by Norbert Leo Butz and Phil Knight. Playback advice was to start at 1x or 1.25x, experiment up to 1.5x, pause for notes and reflection, and revisit key sections.
