On 2023-05-23, the assistant explained that the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meets eight times per year and considers GDP, inflation, unemployment, consumer spending, current conditions, and forecasts when setting the federal funds rate. Rate changes were described as affecting mortgages, credit cards, savings-account returns, inflation, spending, and financial markets. Suggested ways to stay informed were following financial news, checking with one's bank or financial institution, monitoring mortgage rates, and consulting a financial advisor. Historical crisis examples discussed were the Great Depression (1929-1933), the 1970s and 1980s, the Global Financial Crisis (2007-2009), and the COVID-19 Pandemic (2020).
