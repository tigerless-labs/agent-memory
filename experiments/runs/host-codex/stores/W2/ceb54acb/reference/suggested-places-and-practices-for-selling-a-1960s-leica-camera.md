Suggestions given on 2023-05-23: eBay, Etsy, KEH Camera, Leica Store, Camera West, The Camera Shop, Reddit's r/vintagecameras, Leica Forum, Fred Miranda, Photography-on-the-Net, and DPReview. Suggested researching comparable sales, cleaning carefully, documenting camera, lenses, accessories, condition and flaws, testing with a test roll or sample photos, considering professional service, and packaging carefully.
