Suggestions given on 2023-05-23: Abebooks, Biblio, Alibris, The Strand, Powell's City of Books, Peter Harrington Rare Books, Reddit's r/rarebooks, Book Collectors' Forum, Christie's, and Sotheby's. Suggested obtaining an appraisal, researching comparable prices, documenting condition and edition with photos, considering auction consignment for a very valuable book, preserving provenance, and packaging carefully.
