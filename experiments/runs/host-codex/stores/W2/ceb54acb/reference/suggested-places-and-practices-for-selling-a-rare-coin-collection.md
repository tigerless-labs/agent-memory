Suggestions given on 2023-05-23: eBay, NGC (Numismatic Guaranty Corporation), CoinWeek, APMEX (American Precious Metals Exchange), JM Bullion, Liberty Coin, Reddit's r/coincollecting, Coin Forum, CoinTalk, and CoinCommunity. Suggested checking comparable prices; considering grading and certification by NGC or PCGS; photographing obverse, reverse, and certificates; preserving provenance; and considering Heritage Auctions or Stack's Bowers for extremely rare or valuable coins.
