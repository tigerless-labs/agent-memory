On 2023-05-23, examples given were ‘The Shining’ (1980), ‘Blade Runner’ (1982), ‘Get Out’ (2017), and ‘The Matrix’ (1999). Films cited as great without necessarily relying on symbolism were ‘Casablanca’ (1942), ‘The Shawshank Redemption’ (1994), and ‘Star Wars: Episode IV - A New Hope’ (1977). The discussion also covered ‘The Godfather’: its oranges were described as a commonly analyzed symbol of impending danger and death, while noting that Francis Ford Coppola and Gordon Willis had not confirmed or denied that interpretation.
