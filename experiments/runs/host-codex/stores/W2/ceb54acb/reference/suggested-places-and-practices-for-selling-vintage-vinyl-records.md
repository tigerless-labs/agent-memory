Suggestions given on 2023-05-23: Discogs, eBay, MusicStack, Reverb LP, Vinyl Me, Please, The Vinyl Factory, Reddit's r/vinylcollectors, Vinyl Collective, local record stores, and Half Price Books. Suggested researching completed sales, grading accurately, providing detailed photos and descriptions, considering bundles, negotiating, and packaging carefully.
