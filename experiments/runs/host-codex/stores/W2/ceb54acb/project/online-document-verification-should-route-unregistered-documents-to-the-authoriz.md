Required workflow: documents may be submitted online; the system checks whether each document is already stored in the blockchain; documents not yet stored are sent for approval to the concerned authorized government agency, such as the BIR for an income tax return or the office of permits and licenses for a business permit document; after the agency approves and verifies a document, the system automatically stores the verified document in the blockchain and notifies the user.
