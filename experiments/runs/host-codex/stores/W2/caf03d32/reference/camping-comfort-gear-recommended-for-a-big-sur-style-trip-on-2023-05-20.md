Portable stoves recommended: **MSR PocketRocket 2** and **Jetboil Flash Cooking System**. Lanterns: **Black Diamond Apollo Lantern** and **Coleman Classic Camp Lantern**. Other gear: **Therm-a-Rest Trail Lite**, **REI Co-op Trailbreak**, **REI Co-op Camp X Chair**, **Coleman Camping Chair**, **LifeStraw**, **Sawyer Mini**, **Teva Terra-Float**, **Sanuk Beaumont**, **MSR Trail Towel**, and **REI Co-op Camp Towel**. Portable-power advice was to favor **20,000mAh or higher** when multiple charges are needed, conserve device power, protect the bank, charge strategically, and consider solar charging or campsite hookups.
