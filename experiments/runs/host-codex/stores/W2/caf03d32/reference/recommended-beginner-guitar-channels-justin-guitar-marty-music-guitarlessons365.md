Beginner-friendly YouTube recommendations: Justin Guitar (Justin Sandercoe), Marty Music (Marty Schwartz), GuitarLessons365 (Bill), GuitarJamz (Martyn), and Andy Guitar (Andy Crowley). Specific tutorials: "Guitar for Beginners" by Justin Guitar, "Acoustic Guitar for Beginners" by GuitarLessons365, "5-Minute Guitar Lessons" by Marty Music, "Guitar for Absolute Beginners" by Andy Guitar, and "Beginner Guitar Lessons" by GuitarJamz.
