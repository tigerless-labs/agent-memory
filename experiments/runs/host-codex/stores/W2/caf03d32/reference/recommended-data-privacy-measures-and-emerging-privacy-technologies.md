Measures discussed: encryption; two-factor authentication; granular privacy settings; data minimization; regular security audits; transparency reports; and third-party access controls. Emerging technologies discussed: homomorphic encryption, differential privacy, federated learning, and blockchain technology. Companies cited as privacy leaders were Apple, Microsoft, Google, Mozilla, and ProtonMail. The assistant concluded that no perfect protection is likely because threats and technology continue to evolve, so strong encryption, access controls, audits, and ongoing vigilance remain necessary.
