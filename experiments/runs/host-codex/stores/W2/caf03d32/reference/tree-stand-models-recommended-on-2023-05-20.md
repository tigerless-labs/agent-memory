Recommended tree stands: budget-friendly **Summit Solo Pro**, **Summit Viper SD**, **Millennium M150**, and **Millennium M250**; mid-range **Lone Wolf Assault II**, **Lone Wolf Alpha II**, **Muddy Nexus**, and **Muddy Skybox**; high-end **Trophyline Ultra**, **Trophyline Ambush**, **Hawk Helium**, and **Hawk UltraLite**. Special mentions were the climbing stands **Summit Cobra SD** and **Lone Wolf Climbing Stand**, and the hang-on stands **Muddy Skybox** and **Trophyline Ultra**. Selection factors included hunting style, tree type and size, weight capacity, comfort, and safety features such as a harness and fall arrest system (FAS).
