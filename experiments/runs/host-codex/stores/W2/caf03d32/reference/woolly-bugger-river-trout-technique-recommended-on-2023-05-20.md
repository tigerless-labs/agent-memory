For river trout, the assistant recommended fishing a **Woolly Bugger** with a **9-10 foot leader** and **1-2X tippet**, using a sink-tip line or weighted leader, casting upstream and across, letting it swing downstream, and varying the retrieve. A strike indicator was described as potentially useful in fast water, low light, or for beginners, but potentially detrimental in slow or clear water because it can reduce natural action or spook fish. If used, the advice was to choose a small, thin indicator.
