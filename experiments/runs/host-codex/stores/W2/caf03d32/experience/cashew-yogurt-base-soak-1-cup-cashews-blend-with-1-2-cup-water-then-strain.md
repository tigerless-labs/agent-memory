Rinse 1 cup of cashews, soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight, then drain and rinse. Blend with 1/2 cup fresh water on high for about 2 minutes, scraping down the sides as needed, until smooth and creamy. Adjust water for thickness. Strain with clean cheesecloth or a nut milk bag for the smoothest result; a fine-mesh sieve works but may leave a slightly grainy texture. Squeeze gently to extract liquid without forcing particles through. For slow-cooker yogurt, use a vegan starter or probiotic powder and incubate 8-12 hours or overnight.
