On 2023-05-21, the assistant recommended contacting an authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service about manufacturer service and authentication. The advice said to ask about authentication, production/model/serial documentation, a condition report, service/restoration records, and any certificate or unique certificate number; retain all documents safely because provenance and manufacturer records could improve buyer confidence and resale value. This was conversational guidance and should be verified against Omega’s current official services before relying on it.
