The checklist advised scheduling the veterinarian in advance; reviewing vaccination history and required vaccines; preparing a clean, safe area; separating horses if the veterinarian considers it necessary; ensuring the horses show no illness or stress; fasting or restricting food and water only if the veterinarian recommends it, especially if sedation is required; and having medical and vaccination records ready. On appointment day: have each horse haltered, groomed, and ready; have identification such as a Coggins test, microchip, or tattoo accessible; be present; and follow post-vaccination instructions. Supplement discussions named omega-3 fatty acids, antioxidants including vitamin C, vitamin E, and beta-carotene, probiotics, beta-glucans, L-glutamine, L-arginine, L-carnitine, turmeric (curcumin), ginger, and boswellia, but repeatedly advised consulting the veterinarian before adding anything and emphasized that supplements do not replace vaccination.
