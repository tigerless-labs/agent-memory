Prior recommendations on 2023-05-21:\n\n- 1960s Omega Seamaster: eBay, Chrono24, 1stdibs, The RealReal, WatchUseek, Timekeeper’s Forums, Omega Forums, local watch dealers/collectors, Christie’s, Sotheby’s, Heritage Auctions, Instagram, Facebook, and Facebook Marketplace.\n- 1980s Sony Walkman Professional: eBay, Gearslutz, Resonance FM, vintage-audio forums, Facebook Marketplace, and local selling groups.\n- The Avengers #1 (1963): ComicConnect, eBay, Comic Book Resources, Comic Vine, Reddit’s r/comicbooks, and local comic-book stores; CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) grading was suggested.\n- 1960s Batman action figure: eBay, Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk, ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room, and local toy/collectible stores; Wata Games or Professional Sports Authenticator (PSA) certification was suggested.\n- 1960s Fender Jazzmaster: Reverb, eBay, The Gear Page, GuitarPlayer, TDPRI (The Dallas Pedal and Guitar Ranch Internet Forum), Vintage Guitar, Guitar Collector, Vintage Guitar Exchange, Facebook Marketplace, and local groups.\n\nGeneral advice was to research fees and comparable prices, supply detailed descriptions and high-quality images, retain appraisal/authentication documents, answer buyer questions, negotiate patiently, and transact cautiously.
