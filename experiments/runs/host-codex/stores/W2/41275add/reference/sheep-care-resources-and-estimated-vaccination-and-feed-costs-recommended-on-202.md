Recommended resources: National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP), American Sheep Industry Association (ASI), local University Extension Services such as Purdue, Cornell, or Texas A&M, and Sheep 101. Estimated costs given: CD-T vaccine (Clostridium perfringens type C and D, and tetanus) $1.50 to $3.00 per dose; CDT vaccine with selenium $2.50 to $5.00 per dose; pneumonia vaccines such as Pasteurella or Mannheimia $3.00 to $6.00 per dose; alfalfa or grass hay $100 to $200 per ton; grain such as corn or oats $150 to $300 per ton; minerals and supplements $20 to $50 per month. A veterinarian was recommended for a flock-specific protocol and accurate costs.
