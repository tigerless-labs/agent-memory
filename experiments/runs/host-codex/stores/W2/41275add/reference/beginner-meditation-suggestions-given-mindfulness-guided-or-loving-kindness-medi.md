Advice given on 2023-05-20: beginners can try mindfulness meditation, guided meditation (including Headspace or Calm), or loving-kindness meditation. Start with five or ten minutes and increase gradually; consistency matters more than occasional long sessions, and practicing a few times a week can still be worthwhile.
