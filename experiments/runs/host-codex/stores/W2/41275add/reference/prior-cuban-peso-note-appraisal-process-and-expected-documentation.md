The assistant suggested finding a Cuban-currency specialist through the Professional Numismatists Guild (PNG) or International Association of Paper Money Dealers (IAPMD), submitting the note with provenance/context, and requesting examination of condition, authenticity, rarity, and market demand. Expected documentation: a written appraisal describing denomination, series, condition, authenticity/rarity opinion, estimated current-market value, and provenance/history; some appraisers may also issue a Certificate of Authenticity. Multiple appraisals were suggested, with reports retained for sale or insurance.
