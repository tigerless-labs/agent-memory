CPU cooler options named for the AMD Ryzen 9 5900X: NZXT Kraken X73, Corsair Hydro Series H115i RGB Platinum, EKWB EK-AIO 360 D-RGB, and Thermaltake Floe Riing 360. Noctua NH-U14S TR4-SP3 was also mentioned as an air-cooling alternative. The advice was that 32GB RAM is sufficient for most gaming, while 64GB can help with multiple 4K streams, high-resolution video editing, RAM-intensive effects, or several heavy applications. Gameplay recorder options named: OBS Studio, XSplit, ShadowPlay, Fraps, Bandicam, Camtasia, and Windows 10 Game Bar. OBS Studio was characterized as free, open-source, lightweight, highly customizable, supported by a large community and plugins, and a good fit for the user hardware.
