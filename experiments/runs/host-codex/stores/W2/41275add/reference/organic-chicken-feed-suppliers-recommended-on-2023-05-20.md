Online stores recommended: Amazon, Tractor Supply Co., ChickensForBackyards, and Meyer Hatchery. Suppliers recommended: Organic Valley, Blue Seal, Modesto Milling, and Scratch and Peck Feeds. Local feed stores, co-ops, and farm supply stores were also suggested. Buying guidance was to check USDA Organic, Non-GMO, or Certified Humane certifications; read ingredients and nutrition labels; compare prices; consider bulk orders; and transition chickens gradually to new feed.
