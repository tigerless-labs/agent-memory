Use diffused natural light or a softbox, avoid direct flash, and optionally layer a side light to reveal texture. Use simple neutral backgrounds, varied angles, overview and close-up detail shots, a tripod, RAW capture, accurate focus, and occasional in-use lifestyle images. In editing, preserve accurate color and condition, use restrained noise reduction and sharpening, remove only distracting dust/minor flaws, avoid deceptive over-retouching, and maintain a consistent visual treatment across listings.
