The user felt their online-shopping spending had been adding up and planned to cut down. Their chosen approach was to track expenses more closely, set a budget, and follow a "one in, one out" policy: get rid of an old item whenever bringing a new one into their life, to encourage mindful purchases and ensure the new item is really needed.
