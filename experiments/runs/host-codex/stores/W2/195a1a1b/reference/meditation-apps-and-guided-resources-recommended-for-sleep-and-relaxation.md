Recommended meditation apps: Headspace (free trial, then subscription), Calm (free trial, then subscription), Insight Timer (free), Meditation Studio (free with in-app purchases), and Buddhify (one-time purchase). Other named resources: Guided Meditation by Andrew Weil on YouTube, Rainy Mood, the Sleep With Me podcast, Meditation Oasis, and the UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center.

Specific guided sessions named were: "Sleep" by Headspace (10 minutes), "Deep Sleep" by Calm (20 minutes), "Sleep Meditation" by Andrew Weil (10 minutes), "Guided Sleep Meditation" by Meditation Studio (15 minutes), "Sleep Story: The Magical Forest" by Calm (20 minutes), "Relaxation Response" by Dr. Herbert Benson (10 minutes), "Body Scan" by Insight Timer (10 minutes), "Loving-Kindness Meditation" by Jack Kornfield (10 minutes), "Progressive Muscle Relaxation" by Meditation Studio (10 minutes), and "Rainy Night" by Rainy Mood (10 minutes).
