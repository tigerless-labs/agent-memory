Suggested schedule: exercise 8:00 am-8:30 am; shower/get ready 8:30 am-9:00 am; breakfast/relaxation 9:00 am-9:30 am; urgent email check 9:30 am-10:00 am; focused project/report work 10:00 am-12:00 pm with a 10-15 minute break each hour; lunch 12:00 pm-1:00 pm; email management 1:00 pm-2:00 pm; meetings 2:00 pm-3:00 pm; project work 3:00 pm-4:00 pm; break 4:00 pm-4:30 pm; project work 4:30 pm-6:00 pm; review and next-day planning 6:00 pm-6:30 pm. Recommended a 90-120 minute focus timer and limiting social media/email checks. Suggested Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook for scheduling/reminders and Trello or Asana for task management.

Sample bedtime wind-down: finish work/stimulating activities at 8:30 pm; relaxation at 8:45 pm; put away electronic devices at 9:15 pm; prepare the sleep environment at 9:20 pm; get ready for bed at 9:25 pm; sleep at 9:30 pm.
