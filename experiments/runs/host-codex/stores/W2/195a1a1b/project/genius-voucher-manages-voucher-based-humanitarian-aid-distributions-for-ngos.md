Genius Voucher is an all-in-one hardware and software platform for NGO voucher-based humanitarian projects. Authorized vendors use POS machines and beneficiaries use NFC smart cards to receive items or cash. The NGO project team uses a portal to create and monitor distribution projects in real time and generate periodic accountability reports. Transactions issue receipts, balances can be monitored for future card top-ups, and the system reduces repeated manual data entry. Redemption can work without internet in remote areas: devices sync locally during distribution and sync completed offline rounds to the database afterward. Beneficiaries tap a smart card at a contracted vendor's POS terminal, choose items, and enter a PIN. Its stated benefits for Africa are transparency, accountability, offline operation, and a user-friendly redemption process.
