The assistant summarized GRI Standards, SASB Standards, TCFD Recommendations, the IR Framework, EU Taxonomy Regulation, SFDR, and CSRD by regulatory body, focus, applicability, and format. Source URLs supplied: https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/what-are-the-gri-standards/ ; https://www.sasb.org/standards/ ; https://www.fsb-tcfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/FINAL-2017-TCFD-Report-11052018.pdf ; https://integratedreporting.org/resource/international-ir-framework/ ; https://ec.europa.eu/info/business-economy-euro/banking-and-finance/sustainable-finance/eu-taxonomy-sustainable-activities_en ; https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R2088 ; https://ec.europa.eu/info/business-economy-euro/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/non-financial-reporting_en . The session characterized GRI, SASB, TCFD, and IR as voluntary; EU Taxonomy as mandatory for companies subject to NFRD and SFDR; SFDR as mandatory for financial market participants and financial advisers; and CSRD as proposing ESEF/Inline XBRL for sustainability information. These were January 2023 statements and should be reverified before current use.
