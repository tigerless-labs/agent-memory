The assistant stated that NFRD did not prescribe a particular reporting format and allowed relevant non-financial information in management reports or a separate sustainability report, while encouraging recognized frameworks such as GRI, SASB, and TCFD. It described applicability as large public-interest entities with more than 500 employees and either a balance-sheet total over €20 million or net turnover over €40 million; public-interest entities included EU-regulated-market issuers, credit institutions, and insurance undertakings. This is a historical January 2023 explanation and should be checked against primary law before reliance.
