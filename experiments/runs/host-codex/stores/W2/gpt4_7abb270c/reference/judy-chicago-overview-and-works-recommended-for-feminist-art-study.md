The assistant identified Judy Chicago (born Judith Sylvia Cohen in 1939) as a key feminist artist. Works discussed: "The Dinner Party" (1974–1979), a triangular installation honoring 39 women and now in the Brooklyn Museum; "Atmospheric Sculptures" (1960s–1970s); "Birth Project" (1980–1985); and "PowerPlay" (1982–1987). The assistant also mentioned her teaching at CalArts, UCLA, and Duke University and stated that she received the National Medal of Arts in 2018.
