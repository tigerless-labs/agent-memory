Recommendations given for relaxing reading:\n\n- Nature writing: **Walden** by Henry David Thoreau; **Pilgrim at Tinker Creek** by Annie Dillard; **The Hidden Life of Trees** by Peter Wohlleben.\n- Light fiction: **The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society** by Mary Ann Shaffer; **The Elegance of the Hedgehog** by Muriel Barbery; **The Unlikely Pilgrimage of Harold Fry** by Rachel Joyce.\n- Poetry: **Leaves of Grass** by Walt Whitman; **The Complete Poems of Emily Dickinson**; **The Poetry of Mary Oliver**.\n- Reflection: **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle; **Mindset: The New Psychology of Success** by Carol S. Dweck; **The Miracle of Mindfulness** by Thich Nhat Hanh.\n- Essays/stories: **The Essays of George Orwell**; **The Complete Short Stories of Ernest Hemingway**; **Me Talk Pretty One Day** by David Sedaris.
