Recommended resources: American Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (AIC); International Institute for Conservation of Historic and Artistic Works (IIC); Conservation Wiki; YouTube channels Art Conservation, Conservation Framing, and The Getty Conservation Institute; and courses on Coursera, edX, and Udemy. Recommended starting only on non-valuable items, prioritizing reversibility and material compatibility, working in a well-ventilated area, wearing gloves, goggles, and masks as appropriate, and seeking experienced conservator guidance when unsure. Materials discussed included PVA glue, Acryloid/Paraloid B-72, Lascaux 360 HV, rice starch paste, wheat starch paste, BEVA 371, Butvar B-98, Parylene, silanes, epoxies, waxes, varnishes, cotton/linen/rag paper, and Japanese kozu and gampi papers.
