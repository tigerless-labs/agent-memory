On 2023-05-28, the user reported that their 2018 Dell Inspiron 15 5000 series had provided around 6-7 hours of battery life before a RAM upgrade to 16GB, but afterward provided around 4-5 hours. They had made no other recent hardware or software changes and planned to try battery calibration.
