As of 2023-05-20, the user said they had not watched cable TV in months and preferred streaming services because they offer more variety and convenience; avoid cable commercials for uninterrupted binge-watching; resume shows where they left off after a few days; provide shows and movies unavailable on cable or missed during their original run; work on a phone or tablet while on the go; and often release whole seasons at once instead of requiring a one-week wait between episodes.
