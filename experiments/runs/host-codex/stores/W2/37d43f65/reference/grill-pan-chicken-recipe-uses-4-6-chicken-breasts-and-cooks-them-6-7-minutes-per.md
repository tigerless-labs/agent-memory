Ingredients: 4-6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts; 1/4 cup olive oil; 1/4 cup lemon juice; 2 cloves garlic, minced; 2 teaspoons dried oregano; 1 teaspoon salt; 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Whisk the marinade, coat the chicken, cover, and marinate for 20-30 minutes at room temperature. Heat a grill pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the breasts for 6-7 minutes per side until no longer pink and at least 165°F internally. Rest for 5-10 minutes, then slice and serve.
