The proposed 10th session is titled "Evaluating Online Information." Its objective is to teach students to evaluate online credibility and reliability using the CRAAP Test: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose. The lesson includes evaluating sample and researched sources, presenting reasoning, and compiling verification tips.
