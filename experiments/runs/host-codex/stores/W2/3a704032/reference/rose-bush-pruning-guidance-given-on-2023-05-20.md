The assistant recommended deadheading dead or wilted flowers at any time and only light pruning while new buds are forming to remove weak, spindly, crossing, or rubbing growth. More extensive pruning should wait until blooming is over; for most roses, major pruning is best during dormancy in winter or early spring. Cuts were recommended clean at a 45-degree angle.
