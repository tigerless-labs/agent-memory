On 2023-05-24, recommendations for the snake plant were Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20), Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer (2-2-2), and Schultz All Purpose Plant Food (20-20-20). For succulents: Miracle-Gro Cactus, Palm & Citrus Food (10-20-10), Espoma Organic Cactus! Fertilizer (5-2-6), and Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10). For the orchid: Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10), Espoma Organic Orchid! Fertilizer (15-30-15), and Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20). The assistant advised water-soluble fertilizer diluted to half strength and sparing use.
