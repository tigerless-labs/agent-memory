For a milder retry, the assistant suggested starting with 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon gochugaru per 2 cups of vegetables, then adjusting to taste. It also suggested about 2-3 cloves of fresh minced or crushed garlic and 1-2 inches of peeled, grated or thinly sliced fresh ginger per 2 cups of vegetables.
