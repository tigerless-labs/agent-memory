The assistant recommended exploring the live-coding interface; using the built-in Help > Tutorial and Help > Examples; joining the Sonic Pi community on Twitter, the Sonic Pi forums, and the unofficial Discord server; attending a workshop or class; and watching video tutorials such as the "Sonic Pi Live Stream" series on Twitch.
