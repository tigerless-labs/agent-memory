On 2023-05-23, the assistant recommended a Blue Yeti or Rode NT-USB condenser microphone, placing it 6-12 inches from the ukulele slightly off-center and angled toward the sound hole, and using Audacity, GarageBand, or Adobe Audition for audio. Video-editing options mentioned were iMovie, Adobe Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve.
