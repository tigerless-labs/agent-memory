Recommendations featuring strong female protagonists and complex relationships: "The Devil Wears Prada" by Lauren Weisberger; "The Mothers" by Brit Bennett; "The Girls" by Emma Cline; "The Interestings" by Meg Wolitzer; "Conversations with Friends" by Sally Rooney; "The Female Persuasion" by Meg Wolitzer; "The Last Romantics" by Tara Conklin; and "The Age of Light" by Whitney Scharer.
