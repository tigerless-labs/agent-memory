Guidance given: bright indirect light; water when the top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry (often every 7-10 days in spring/summer and every 10-14 days in fall/winter); ensure drainage; mist 1-2 times a week in average indoor conditions or every 3-4 days in dry environments; use room-temperature water in the morning; keep at 65°F (18°C) to 75°F (24°C), ideally 68°F (20°C) to 72°F (22°C); avoid below 55°F (13°C), above 85°F (29°C), and drafts. A balanced water-soluble fertilizer such as 20-20-20 or 20-15-15 was suggested, diluted to half strength, with advice to confirm and follow the nursery mixture's label. Fertilization guidance was every 1-2 weeks in spring/summer and once a month in fall/winter.
