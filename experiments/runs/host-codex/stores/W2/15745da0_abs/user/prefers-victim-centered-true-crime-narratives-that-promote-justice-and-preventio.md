The user is frustrated by true crime stories that glorify perpetrators and ignore victims. They prefer narratives that respect victims and families, promote justice, encourage prevention, address systemic causes of violence, and avoid sensationalism and exploitation. They also believe consumers should hold themselves accountable for how they engage with true crime.
