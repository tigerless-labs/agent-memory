For appraisal orders, the team should independently evaluate whether a property is complex and whether a higher fee is justified. An appraiser's assessment does not conclusively establish complexity; weigh the opinion and supporting evidence against internal research and analysis. Consider location, property type and design, age and condition, zoning and land use, and market conditions. Exercise caution because an appraiser may have a vested interest in securing the highest fee possible. Decisions should rest on objective criteria and a comprehensive examination of the property and market.
