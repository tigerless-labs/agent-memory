Guidance given on 2023-05-22: follow local-authority warnings and regulations; wear a dust mask, goggles, and long sleeves; avoid volcanic ash, gases, lava, pyroclastic flows, low-lying areas, and places close to the sea when flooding or tsunamis are possible; prepare for power and transport disruptions; drive carefully; and do not copy visitors who breach safety boundaries. Photograph lava only from a safe distance with suitable equipment, preferably after consulting a professional guide or volcano-photography expert. Report dangerous breaches to the nearest authority or emergency service.
