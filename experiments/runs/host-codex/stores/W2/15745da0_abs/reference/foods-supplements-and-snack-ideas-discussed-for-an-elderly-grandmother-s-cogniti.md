Suggestions given on 2023-05-22 included omega-3 sources such as salmon, tuna, mackerel, and flaxseed; antioxidant-rich berries, spinach, and kale; B6, B9 (folate), and B12 sources such as leafy greens, nuts, and fortified cereals; moderate coffee or tea; and curcumin from turmeric. Supplements should not replace a balanced diet, and a healthcare professional should be consulted before starting them. Appeal strategies included favorite recipes, adding foods to familiar dishes, colorful presentation, adapting texture by steaming, roasting, or pureeing, involving the grandmother in planning, and offering small amounts patiently. Snack ideas: apple or celery with almond or peanut butter; seasoned roasted chickpeas; Greek yogurt with berries and honey; hummus with carrots, celery, or pita chips; trail mix with nuts, dried fruit, and dark chocolate chips; whole-grain crackers with avocado or guacamole; frozen-fruit smoothies with Greek yogurt and spinach or kale; and energy balls with dates, nuts, and coconut.
