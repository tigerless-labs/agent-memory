Recommendations given on 2023-05-28: store vinyl upright in sturdy acid-free record crates, storage cubes, or dedicated LP shelves; display favorites on a record wall, shelves with a top display area, a rotating display, or acrylic stands. Display the rare 1978 pressing of Fleetwood Mac's Rumours prominently, possibly with Fleetwood Mac records, album artwork, lyrics, or band memorabilia. Clean records regularly, handle them by the edges, and keep them in a clean, dry environment.
