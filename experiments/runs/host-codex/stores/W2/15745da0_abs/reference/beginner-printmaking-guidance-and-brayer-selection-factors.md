Begin with relief printing, experiment with papers and inks, treat mistakes as part of learning, take a class or workshop, and practice. Choose a brayer based on ink, plate texture, surface size, and detail: the advice given was softer for water-based ink or rougher surfaces, harder for oil-based ink or smooth surfaces, smaller for small surfaces, and smaller/firmer for fine detail; personal experience and trial-and-error also matter.
