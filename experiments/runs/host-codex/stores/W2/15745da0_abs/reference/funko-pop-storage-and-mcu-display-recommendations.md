Recommendations given on 2023-05-28: use dedicated slotted shelves, glass-door cases, grid displays or pegboards, or rotating displays; store figures in figure-specific compartment bins, labeled stackable bins, or individual protector cases. For the entire Marvel Cinematic Universe set, group by movie or series such as Avengers, Guardians of the Galaxy, or Iron Man; recreate iconic scenes; feature Iron Man or Captain America prominently; add miniature props; dust regularly, handle carefully, choose a cohesive theme, and rotate the display.
