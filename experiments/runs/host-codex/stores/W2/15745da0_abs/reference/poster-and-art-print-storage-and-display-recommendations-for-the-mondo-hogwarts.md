Recommendations given on 2023-05-28: store posters in sturdy acid-free poster tubes, flat files or portfolios, or acid-free folders and sleeves. Display with acid-free mats and UV-filtering glass or acrylic, poster hangers or clips, art-print racks, or shadow boxes. Feature the Mondo Hogwarts castle poster prominently, group it with Harry Potter or fantasy art, optionally add a quote or related item, handle it carefully, and periodically rotate displayed works.
