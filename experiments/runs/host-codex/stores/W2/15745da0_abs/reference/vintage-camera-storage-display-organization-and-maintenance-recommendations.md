Recommendations given on 2023-05-28: store vintage cameras around 65°F to 70°F and around 50% humidity; use original cases or acid-free boxes with padded dividers; avoid stacking, direct sunlight, moisture, and extreme temperatures; use silica gel or a dehumidifier; dust with a soft brush or blower bulb; clean lenses with microfiber and lens solution; inspect seals; inventory make, model, serial number, condition, purchase date, repairs, and maintenance; categorize by type, era, or brand; keep manuals and a camera log; create a dedicated maintenance workspace and quarterly inspection schedule. Display suggestions included tiered or compartmented shelves, rotating shelves, glass-front or wall-mounted cases with UV-filtering glass, themed groupings, soft indirect light, security, and periodic rotation. The Brownie Hawkeye could be highlighted centrally with original packaging, instructions, accessories, or cameras of the same era. The prior advice also mentioned occasionally lubricating moving parts with light machine oil or silicone lubricant; because vintage mechanisms vary, that specific step should be verified with a qualified camera technician before use.
