The assistant recommended the Fujifilm X-T30 II for landscape and nature photography, quoting $899 body only and the Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ at $499. It described a 26.1MP APS-C sensor, Intelligent Hybrid AF, 425 phase-detection points, claimed 0.02-second autofocus, face/eye detection, and autofocus down to -3EV. Battery figures given were up to 380 frames with EVF, 440 with LCD, 45 minutes movie recording with EVF, and 60 minutes with LCD. The assistant said it was not fully weather-sealed but described it as moisture-resistant and advised caution in harsh weather. These were conversational recommendations/specifications and were not independently verified.
