Recommended yoga 1–2 times per week, ideally on rest or easy-training days, starting with 30–45 minute sessions. Suggested Hatha, Vinyasa, or Yin initially; focus areas included shoulders, hips, and IT band, with Downward-Facing Dog, Warrior II, Tree Pose, and Seated Forward Fold. Suggested online resources were YogaGlo, Peloton, and Down Dog.
