Dining-table options mentioned were IKEA LERHAMN, MALM, and BJURSTA, with the caveat that IKEA inventory changes and these were not explicitly reclaimed-wood tables. Office-chair suggestions were ERGOhuman High-Back Executive Chair, Mid-Back Mesh Chair, and Big & Tall Chair, plus Herman Miller, Steelcase, Flash Furniture, and Autonomous. Coffee-table forms suggested were ottoman, nesting, reclaimed wood, and industrial-chic; shopping sources discussed were West Elm and Crate & Barrel.
