Recommendations included Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, baitfish streamers, poppers, sliders, Gurglers, crayfish patterns, Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail Nymph, leech patterns, frog patterns, ants, beetles, and grasshoppers. Suggested setup: a 9-foot 6-inch to 10-foot, 6–8 weight, medium-to-fast-action fly rod; reel holding at least 150–200 yards of 20-pound-test backing with reliable drag; 90-foot weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking 6–8 weight line; 9–12 foot monofilament or fluorocarbon leader rated at least 15–20 pounds; and a 1–2 foot, 12–15-pound tippet. Presentation guidance: vary strip, twitch, and drag-free retrieves; add pauses; adjust sink rate and depth; target rocks, weed edges, sunken logs, drop-offs, weed lines, structure changes, and baitfish schools; and adapt aggression to water clarity.
