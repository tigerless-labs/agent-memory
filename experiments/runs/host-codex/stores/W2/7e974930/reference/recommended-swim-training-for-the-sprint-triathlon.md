Recommendations included technique work; 25–50 meter drills and sprint intervals; 200–400 meter moderate endurance sets; open-water practice for wetsuit use, sighting, and navigation; shoulder, back, and core strength work; and swim-to-bike or swim-to-run brick workouts. Suggested tracking apps were Strava, Swim.com, and Garmin, with swimming at least 2–3 times a week.
