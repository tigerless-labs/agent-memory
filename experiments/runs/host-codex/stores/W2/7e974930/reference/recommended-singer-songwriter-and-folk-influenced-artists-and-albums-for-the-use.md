Recommendations given on 2023-03-16 included Phoebe Bridgers, Julien Baker, Lucy Dacus, Bon Iver, Fleet Foxes, Courtney Marie Andrews, Vagabon, Adrianne Lenker, Sharon Van Etten, and The Tallest Man on Earth. Albums included Bon Iver — For Emma, Forever Ago; Phoebe Bridgers — Stranger in the Alps; Julien Baker — Turn Out the Lights; Fleet Foxes — Fleet Foxes; and Sufjan Stevens — Carrie & Lowell. Lucy Dacus starting points: Night Shift, Addictions, Nonbeliever; albums No Burden (2016), Historian (2018), and Home Video (2021), starting with Historian. Phoebe Bridgers starting points: Scott Street, Killer, Motion Sickness, Funeral, Kyoto; albums Stranger in the Alps (2017), Punisher (2020), Boygenius (2018), and Better Oblivion Community Center (2019). Julien Baker starting points: Appointments, Turn Out the Lights, Rejoice, Souvenir, Hurt Less; albums Sprained Ankle (2015), Turn Out the Lights (2017), and Boygenius (2018).
