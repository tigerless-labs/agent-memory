In the planned scenes, MK’s attempts to get food initially backfire. Later Wukong sends another monkey with only a few pieces of fruit and a small container of water; in another scene MK begs that he cannot serve as “insurance” if he starves, and receives a small bowl of water and a piece of stale bread. MK also unsuccessfully tries to escape and steal food while the monkeys hold a loud party and feast.
