In the user’s fiction project, Wukong has trapped MK behind a magic barrier while seeking the diamond snare. Wukong becomes convinced MK does not know its location, questions why MK should remain alive, and decides to keep him alive “for now” as possible “insurance.” MK is frightened, hungry, thirsty, and confused by the gap between the heroic Wukong of childhood stories and his cruel captor.
