The assistant recommended gentle water-based screen cleaners and avoiding harsh chemicals, ammonia, alcohol, paper towels, tissues, and abrasive materials. Named spray brands were iKlear, Whoosh!, and Ecolife. It also mentioned a 50/50 mix of water and white vinegar or diluted dish soap as DIY options, while advising checking device compatibility, ingredients, reviews, and manufacturer instructions.
