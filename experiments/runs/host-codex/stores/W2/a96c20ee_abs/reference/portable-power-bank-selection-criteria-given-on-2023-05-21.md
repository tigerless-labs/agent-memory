The assistant suggested evaluating capacity from 5,000mAh to 20,000mAh or more, output compatibility, input charging speed, Quick Charge (QC), USB-C Power Delivery (PD), size, weight, build quality, overcharge and short-circuit protection, temperature control, UL or CE certifications, brand reputation, wireless charging, solar charging, lights, and carrying cases.
