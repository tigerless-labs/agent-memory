The assistant advised refrigerating cooked ground beef and chicken breasts at 40°F (4°C) or below within 2 hours and using within 3 to 4 days. It suggested freezing cooked ground beef for 3 to 4 months and cooked chicken breasts for 4 to 6 months, labeling and dating packages, thawing in the refrigerator, and reheating to 165°F (74°C).
