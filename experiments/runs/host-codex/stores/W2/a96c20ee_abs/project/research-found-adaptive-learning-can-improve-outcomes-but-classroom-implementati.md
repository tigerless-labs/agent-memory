The user's research found that AI-powered adaptive learning systems can improve student learning outcomes, but successful classroom integration requires careful implementation. Recurring case-study findings were the need for **ongoing teacher professional development and training** and a clear alignment between the systems, the **curriculum**, and **learning goals**. Teachers who lacked the needed skills and knowledge struggled to implement the systems; clear alignment increased adoption and effective use.
