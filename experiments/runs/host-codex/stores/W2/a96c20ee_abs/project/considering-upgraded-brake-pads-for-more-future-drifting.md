The user is considering another brake-pad upgrade because they plan to do more drifting. The options discussed were high-performance pads for spirited driving, track days, and occasional drifting versus racing-specific pads for frequent extreme heat and repeated heavy braking. Racing-specific pads were suggested if pushing the car hard regularly; high-performance pads were presented as sufficient for occasional drifting or a tighter budget. Compatible pads, manufacturer bedding-in, and high-performance or racing brake fluid were also recommended.
