As of 2023-05-21, before their next bike ride the user needs to **oil the chain** and **adjust the derailleur**. Suggested preparation also included cleaning the chain before applying condition-appropriate lube, checking derailleur limit screws and alignment, tire pressure, brake pads, bolts, and quick releases.
