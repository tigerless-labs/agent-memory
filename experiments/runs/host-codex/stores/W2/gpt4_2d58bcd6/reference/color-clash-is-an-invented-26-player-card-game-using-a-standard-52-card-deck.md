Invented game: Color Clash. Deal 7 cards each to 2–6 players and use the rest as a draw pile. On a turn, draw one, play one face up, and declare a suit (the original description mistakenly said color, then corrected this to the four suits: clubs, diamonds, hearts, and spades). Other players play all cards matching the declared suit. Proposed bonuses included most cards of a suit and a "rainbow" containing all 4 suits. A later meld variant defined melds as three or more cards of the same rank, required a base meld before further melds, allowed adding a matching-rank card to an opponent meld, and ended when someone ran out of cards or no further melds could be made.
