Fiction recommendations included **Daisy Jones and The Six** by Taylor Jenkins Reid, **The Royal We** by Heather Cocks and Jessica Morgan, **The Address** by Fiona Davis, **Gone Girl** by Gillian Flynn, **The Girl on the Train** by Paula Hawkins, **The Last Time I Lied** by Riley Sager, **All the Light We Cannot See** by Anthony Doerr, **The Alice Network** by Kate Quinn, and **The Lost Girls of Paris** by Pam Jenoff. Suggested authors included Colleen Hoover, Emily Giffin, and Liane Moriarty. Non-fiction/self-help recommendations included **The Power of Now** by Eckhart Tolle, **Daring Greatly** and **The Gifts of Imperfection** by Brené Brown, **The Happiness Project** by Gretchen Rubin, **Attached** by Amir Levine and Rachel Heller, **Nonviolent Communication** by Marshall B. Rosenberg, **Hold Me Tight** by Sue Johnson, **Mindset** by Carol S. Dweck, **Presence** by Amy Cuddy, **Wild** by Cheryl Strayed, **The Year of Magical Thinking** by Joan Didion, and **Man's Search for Meaning** by Viktor E. Frankl.
