Platforms recommended: Audible (over 500,000 titles; free trial then 4.95/month for one credit), Libro.fm (free trial then 4.99/month for one credit and support for independent bookstores), Scribd (free trial then .99/month for unlimited access), Hoopla Digital (free with a library card or student ID), OverDrive (free borrowing with a library card), Google Play Books, Audiobooks.com (free trial then 4.95/month for one credit), Hibooks (free trial then 2.99/month for one credit), Kobo Audiobooks, and Audiobooksync (two free audiobooks per week for 13 weeks in summer). Suggested listens included **The Nightingale** by Kristin Hannah, **The Power** by Naomi Alderman, **The 7 1/2 Deaths of Evelyn Hardcastle** by Stuart Turton, **The Song of Achilles** by Madeline Miller, and **Educated** by Tara Westover.
