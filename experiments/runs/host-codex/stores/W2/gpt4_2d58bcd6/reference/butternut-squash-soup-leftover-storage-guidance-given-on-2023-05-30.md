Cool soup to room temperature within 2 hours and refrigerate promptly in a covered airtight container labeled with contents and date. Refrigerate for 3 to 5 days. For longer storage, freeze in 1-2 cup portions in freezer-safe containers or bags with excess air removed; freeze for 3 to 6 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator or reheat from frozen, and reheat to at least 165°F (74°C).
