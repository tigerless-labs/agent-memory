Recommended settings on 2023-05-30: Manual mode; ISO 100-400; f/8-f/11 for broad depth of field; approximately 1/125s-1/250s where motion blur is wanted; Cloudy or Shade white balance; expose snow by +1/3 to +1 stop; shoot RAW; bracket exposures where useful; use a polarizing filter and optionally a graduated ND filter; and favor a 10-24mm wide-angle lens. For freezing snowflakes: about 1/1000s and f/2.8. Golden-hour or overcast light was recommended.
