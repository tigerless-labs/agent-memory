Advice given on 2023-05-30: conduct India-specific market research; adapt to Indian automotive standards and obtain certifications; localize production or collaborate with an Indian company; set price-sensitive, value-oriented pricing; build broad distribution through established automotive-parts distributors, retailers, and possibly e-commerce; market safety, reliability, and performance through digital channels, social media, and industry events; and establish after-sales service with local technicians. The cited Indian-market differences were price sensitivity, demographic diversity, uneven infrastructure, and a distinct regulatory environment.
