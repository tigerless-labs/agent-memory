The article **Detecting Gravitational Waves with the Moon** by Emma Clarke (Feb 8, 2023) summarizes **Detecting stochastic gravitational waves with binary resonance** by Diego Blas and Alexander C. Jenkins, published in *Phys. Rev. D.* The proposal uses resonant orbital perturbations in binary systems to probe stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds, potentially filling the microhertz (10-6 Hz) gap. The Earth–Moon system is suitable because the Moon orbits in about 28 days; existing lunar retroreflectors have enabled ranging for over fifty years, and about 0.1 mm distance accuracy could make microhertz fluctuations detectable.
