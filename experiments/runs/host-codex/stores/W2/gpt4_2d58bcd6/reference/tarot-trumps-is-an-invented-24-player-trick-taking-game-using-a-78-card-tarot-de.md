Invented game: Tarot Trumps. Deal the entire 78-card tarot deck among 2–4 players, putting any remainder in a kitty. The player left of the dealer leads; players follow suit if possible or play a trump. Highest trump wins and leads next. The proposed ascending Major Arcana order was The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Emperor, The Hierophant, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, The Hermit, Wheel of Fortune, Justice, The Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement, The World. Tricks containing a tarot trump score by rank: The Fool 4 points, The Magician 5 points, continuing up to The World at 25 points; tricks without a tarot trump score zero. Highest total wins.
