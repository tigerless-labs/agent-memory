Servings: 4-6. Ingredients: 1 large butternut squash (about 2-3 lbs), 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 chopped onion, 3 minced garlic cloves, 1 teaspoon ground cumin, 1 teaspoon optional smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth, 1/2 cup optional heavy cream or coconut cream, and parsley or sage. Roast halved, seeded squash at 400°F (200°C) for 45-50 minutes; sauté onion about 5 minutes and garlic 1 minute; cook spices 1 minute; add broth and bring to a boil; scoop in roasted squash and blend; optionally add cream; season and garnish.
