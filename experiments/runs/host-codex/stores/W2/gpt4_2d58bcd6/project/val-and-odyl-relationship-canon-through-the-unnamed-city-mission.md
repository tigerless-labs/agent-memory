In the Val/Odyl story, Odyl is one of the Xirin. During a mission to retrieve important data from a hostile ship, Odyl devised a ruse in which Val allowed herself to be captured and tied up while Odyl played a double agent. Odyl leaned close, whispered "good girl," called Val "Dearie," and kissed her before leaving. Though planned as part of the ruse, Val did not anticipate her reaction; the moment established years of flirtation and connection. They later had one brief hallway encounter in which Odyl pulled Val into a more passionate kiss, smiled coyly, and disappeared. They met again for a month-long mission to the Unnamed City on the abandoned homeworld of the Xirin, which was sad and powerful for Odyl because of her people and heritage. Working together through danger, shared trust, hopes, fears, and vulnerability made them genuinely close. Their final night was fiery and passionate, although their feelings were still unspoken.
