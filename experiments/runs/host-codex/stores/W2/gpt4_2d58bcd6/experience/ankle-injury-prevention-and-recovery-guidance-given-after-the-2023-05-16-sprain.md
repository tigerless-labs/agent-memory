Guidance given: warm up with 10–15 minutes of light cardio and dynamic stretching; cool down with static stretching; increase training intensity and volume gradually; use proper shoes and gear; strengthen ankles and core with ankle circles, calf raises, planks, balance work, and single-leg exercises; stop for pain or excessive fatigue. For an acute ankle sprain, use Rest, Ice, Compression, and Elevation (RICE), then gentle mobility as tolerated. Seek a healthcare or sports-medicine professional for severe injury, chronic pain, or concerning symptoms.
