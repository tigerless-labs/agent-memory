A Monday/Wednesday/Friday home routine was suggested. Upper body: push-ups, presses or curls and bent-over rows using water bottles or cans, and chair tricep dips, generally 3 sets of 10–12 reps. Lower body: squats, lunges, calf raises, and glute bridges, generally 3 sets of 10–12 reps. Core: planks for 3 sets of 30–60 seconds, Russian twists, leg raises, and bicycle crunches. Use a 5–10 minute warm-up and cool-down, prioritize form, and apply progressive overload by gradually adding weight, reps, or sets.
