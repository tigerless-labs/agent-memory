The user prefers full-cast narrations, especially for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes, because they are immersive and make it easier to track who is speaking. They also enjoy skilled single narrators with a distinctive tone and style. They consider narration crucial: tone, pace, and inflection strongly shape interpretation and emotional engagement.
