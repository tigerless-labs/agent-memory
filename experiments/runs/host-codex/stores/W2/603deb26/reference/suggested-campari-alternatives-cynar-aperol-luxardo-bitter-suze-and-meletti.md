For Negroni experiments, the assistant recommended Cynar, Aperol, Luxardo Bitter, Suze, and Meletti in place of Campari, adjusting the bitter-to-vermouth ratio to taste. Suze was described as dry and herbaceous and suitable for a White Negroni.
