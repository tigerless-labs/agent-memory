Recommendations given 2023/11/30:\n\n- **Lavender Gin Fizz:** 1 1/2 oz gin, 1/2 oz fresh lime juice, 1/2 oz lavender syrup, and 1/2 oz simple syrup; shake, strain into a Collins glass, top with soda water; lavender sprig and lime wheel. Lavender syrup: steep 1/4 cup dried lavender buds in 1 cup granulated sugar and 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes; strain; refrigerate up to 2 weeks.\n- **Lavender Dream:** 2 oz vodka, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup; shake into coupe; lavender garnish.\n- **Grapefruit Basil Martini:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, 1/2 oz lime juice, 1/2 oz basil syrup; shake into chilled martini glass; grapefruit twist and basil leaf. Basil syrup: steep 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves in 1 cup granulated sugar and 1 cup water for 10-15 minutes; strain; refrigerate up to 2 weeks.\n- **Grapefruit Rosemary Sparkler:** 2 oz tequila, 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice, 1/2 oz lime juice, 1/2 oz rosemary syrup; shake over ice in highball, top soda; grapefruit and rosemary garnish.\n- **Lavender Grapefruit Spritz:** 2 oz prosecco, 1 oz grapefruit juice, 1/2 oz lavender syrup, splash soda over ice; grapefruit and lavender garnish.\n- **Grapefruit Lavender Collins:** 2 oz gin, 1 oz grapefruit juice, 1/2 oz lavender syrup, 1/2 oz lemon juice; shake over ice in Collins, top soda; grapefruit and lavender garnish.
