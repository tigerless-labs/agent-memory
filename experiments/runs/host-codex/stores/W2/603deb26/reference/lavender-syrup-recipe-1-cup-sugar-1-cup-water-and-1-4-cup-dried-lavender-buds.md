Recommended recipe: dissolve 1 cup granulated sugar in 1 cup water, add 1/4 cup dried lavender buds, remove from heat, steep 10-15 minutes or until room temperature, strain, and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks. Start with a small amount in cocktails because lavender can overpower other flavors.
