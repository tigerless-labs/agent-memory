Cocktails recommended on 2023/11/30 included **Classic Gin Martini** (2:1 or 3:1 gin to dry vermouth), **Pimm's Cup**, **Bee's Knees**, **French 75**, **Corpse Reviver No. 2**, **Old Fashioned**, **Daiquiri**, **Sidecar**, **Gin Rickey**, **Gin-Gin Mule**, **Aviation**, **Monkey Gland**, and **Last Word**. Gin styles suggested for exploration were **London Dry Gin**, **Plymouth Gin**, and **New Western or New World Gin**. Note: the earlier Corpse Reviver No. 2 suggestion listed gin, Lillet Blanc, Cynar, Cointreau, and absinthe; treat that as the historical recommendation from the conversation, not a verified canonical formula.
