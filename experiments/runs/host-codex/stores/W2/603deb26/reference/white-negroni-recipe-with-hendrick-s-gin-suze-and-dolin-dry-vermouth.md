Recommended White Negroni: 1 1/2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1/2 oz Suze (or Luxardo Bitter), and 1/2 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth. Stir with ice for 20-30 seconds, strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass, and garnish with an orange or lemon twist. Suggested testing 2:1:1 or 3:1:1 ratios and avoiding over-dilution.
