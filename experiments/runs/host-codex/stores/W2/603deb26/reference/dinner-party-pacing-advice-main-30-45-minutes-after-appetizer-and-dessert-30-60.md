The assistant recommended creating a realistic padded timeline, preparing ingredients and sauces ahead, cooking in batches, serving courses sequentially, aiming for the main course 30-45 minutes after the appetizer and dessert 30-60 minutes after the main course, keeping the atmosphere relaxed, and communicating any delay to guests.
