Guidance given 2023/11/30:\n\n- Lavender gin: 1/4 cup dried lavender buds per 1 liter gin, infuse 2-3 days cool and dark. Lavender vodka: same ratio, 1-2 weeks.\n- Grapefruit gin: 1-2 sliced grapefruits per 1 liter gin, 2-3 days. Grapefruit vodka: same ratio, 1-2 weeks.\n- Cucumber gin: use thin-skinned English/hothouse cucumber, thinly sliced; 1-2 cups per 1 liter gin; infuse 2-5 days cool and dark, tasting regularly.\n- Mint gin: 1/4 cup to 1 cup fresh peppermint or spearmint leaves per 1 liter gin; infuse 2-5 days and taste regularly.\n- For cucumber-mint gin, infuse separately, strain/filter, then begin blending at 50/50 and adjust; age the blend a few days.\n- Honey syrup: begin at 1:1 honey to water; warm gently over low heat while whisking until dissolved without boiling; cool and refrigerate up to 2 weeks. Optional citrus peel, herbs, spices, or sliced ginger can be infused during dissolving.\n- General advice: use sanitized equipment, a neutral base spirit, cool/dark storage, taste regularly to prevent over-infusion, and strain through cheesecloth or a coffee filter.
