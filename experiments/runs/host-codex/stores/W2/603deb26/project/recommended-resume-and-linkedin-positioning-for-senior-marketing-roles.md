Recommendations given on 2023/08/16: quantify accomplishments rather than list responsibilities; add a summary or objective; use job-description keywords for ATS; emphasize relevant achievements and soft skills; include relevant tools such as Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Google Analytics, or Adobe Creative Cloud; list Google Analytics and HubSpot Inbound Marketing under resume Certifications/Skills and LinkedIn Licenses & Certifications; and expand and engage the LinkedIn network. Suggested workload phrasing included: ‘Demonstrated flexibility and adaptability by working extended hours (up to 50 hours/week) during peak campaign seasons to ensure timely and successful project delivery.’ Suggested positioning: ‘Proven ability to work effectively in fast-paced environments, prioritizing tasks and managing multiple projects simultaneously to meet tight deadlines.’ Any claimed metrics such as 500%, 200%, 50%, 30%, or 25% were examples to replace with genuine results, not established user achievements.
