Grand Canyon National Park campgrounds with electric hookups:\n\n1. **Mather Campground** (South Rim area)\n   - 327 campsites with electric hookups\n   - Amenities: flush toilets, showers, laundry facilities\n   - Booking: recreation.gov\n\n2. **Trailer Village RV Park** (South Rim area)\n   - 84 sites with electric hookups\n   - Amenities: Wi-Fi, laundry, camp store\n   - Booking: grandcanyonlodges.com\n\n3. **Desert View Campground** (South Rim area)\n   - 50 campsites with electric hookups\n   - Amenities: flush toilets, potable water\n   - Booking: recreation.gov\n\nImportant: Book well in advance during peak season. Dispersed camping outside park available but typically without hookups.
