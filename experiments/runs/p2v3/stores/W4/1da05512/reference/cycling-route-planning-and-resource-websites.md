Recommended tools for route planning and bike resources:\n\n- **MapMyRide** - Online route planning tool for cyclists; allows filtering by distance, terrain, and bike type\n- **Strava** - Cycling app with route planning and community features\n- **Ride with GPS** - Route planning platform for cyclists\n- **Bikemap** - Online bike route mapping tool\n- **TrailLink** - Resource for finding rail trails and bike paths\n- **Rails-to-Trails Conservancy** - Organization providing information on rail trails and bike paths\n\nThese tools are useful for finding pre-made routes, creating custom routes, or discovering local cycling infrastructure.
