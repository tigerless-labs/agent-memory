Ideas for maintaining romance and intimacy in long-distance relationships:

1. **Cook and eat together over video chat** — Agree on a recipe, shop separately, cook together while on video call, share wine or dessert for a remote romantic dinner

2. **Virtual movie night** — Watch the same movie simultaneously and chat about it during/after using Netflix Party or Zoom

3. **Play online games together** — Chess, trivia, virtual puzzles, or multiplayer video games

4. **Take virtual tours together** — Museums, galleries, tourist attractions offering online tours

5. **Surprise date nights** — Send flowers, favorite food, or care package with romantic items; arrange surprise video call or online game; send love letter by mail

**Key principle:** Show your partner you care through commitment and effort; whatever activity, ensure it comes from the heart.
