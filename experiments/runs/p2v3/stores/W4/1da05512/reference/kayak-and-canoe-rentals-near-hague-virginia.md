**Westmoreland State Park**
- Location: 145 Cliff Road, Montross, VA
- Offers: kayak, canoe, and paddleboard rentals
- Water: Potomac River
- Life jackets: included with rentals

**Northern Neck Kayaking & Outdoor Rentals**
- Location: 126 Jessie duPont Memorial Highway, Burgess, VA
- Offers: kayak and canoe rentals and tours
- Waters: Great Wicomico River, Chesapeake Bay, and other Northern Neck waterways
- Life jackets: included with rentals

**Wildlife likely to see**: bald eagles, ospreys, blue herons, egrets, waterfowl, river otters, muskrats, beavers, turtles, fish (striped bass, largemouth bass, bluegill, catfish), deer, foxes, raccoons

**Best times**: Early mornings and late afternoons/early evenings for wildlife spotting and pleasant paddling conditions
