Recommended films with humor-horror blend: The Evil Dead (1981), Tucker & Dale vs Evil (2010), Scream (1996), Gremlins (1984). Folk horror: The Wicker Man (1973), Kill List (2011), Apostle (2018), November (2017). Recent: Barbarian (2022), X (2022), The Black Phone (2022). TV series: Ash vs. Evil Dead, What We Do in the Shadows, Santa Clarita Diet, iZombie, Chucky, The Haunting of Hill House, Lovecraft Country. Anthologies: Creepshow (1982), Trick r Treat (2009), V/H/S (2012).
