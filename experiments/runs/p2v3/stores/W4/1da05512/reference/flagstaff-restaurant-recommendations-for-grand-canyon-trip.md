Recommended restaurants in Flagstaff, Arizona:\n\n1. **Late for the Train Cafe** - Breakfast/lunch institution with vegan and gluten-free options. Signature: "Train Wrecks" (breakfast burritos)\n2. **Proper Meats + Provisions** - Gourmet deli and meat market with sandwiches, salads, and charcuterie boards\n3. **Fat Olives** - Italian cuisine: pasta, pizzas, risottos in warm atmosphere\n4. **The Toasted Owl** - Brunch spot with creative dishes (maple bacon waffles, breakfast tacos)\n5. **Big Foot BBQ** - BBQ with tender meats and generous portions\n\nNote: Book in advance, especially during winter holiday peak season.
