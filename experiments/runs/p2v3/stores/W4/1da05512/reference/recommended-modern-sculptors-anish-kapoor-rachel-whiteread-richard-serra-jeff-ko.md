List of modern sculptors recommended on 2023-05-20:\n- **Anish Kapoor**: Large-scale abstract installations with reflective surfaces and voids\n- **Rachel Whiteread**: Large-scale installations exploring space, memory, and human experience\n- **Richard Serra**: Site-specific steel installations altering perception of space\n- **Jeff Koons**: Hyper-realistic sculptures exploring popular culture and consumerism\n- **Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen**: Playful large-scale transformations of everyday objects\n- **Ursula von Rydingsvard**: Abstract organic forms in wood and metal\n- **Barbara Hepworth**: Modernist sculptor with biomorphic abstract forms\n- **Tony Cragg**: Abstract colorful surreal works with found objects\n- **Eva Rothschild**: Large-scale installations with found objects and textiles\n- **Phyllida Barlow**: Colorful playful installations with found objects and fabric
