"Cloud Gate" by Anish Kapoor in Millennium Park, Chicago\n\n**Installation**: 2004\n**Also known as**: "The Bean"\n**Material**: 168 stainless steel panels\n**Weight**: Over 110 tons\n**Dimensions**: 33 feet tall, 42 feet wide, 66 feet long\n**Surface**: Polished mirror-like finish\n\n**Key features**:\n- Reflective surface distorts and warps surroundings\n- Interactive experience; visitors can walk around, touch, take photos\n- Concave shape creates sense of intimacy\n- Reflects viewer's surroundings and self\n\n**Symbolism**: Represents Chicago's connection to sky and clouds; reflects city's vibrant, dynamic energy\n\n**Maintenance**: Surface cleaned daily to maintain mirror finish\n\n**Popularity**: Over 10,000 photos taken daily; iconic symbol of Chicago\n\n**History**: Originally intended as temporary installation but became permanent due to instant popularity
