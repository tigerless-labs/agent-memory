Synology DiskStation models: DS218j (2-bay, around 150), DS418play (4-bay, around 300), DS1819+ (8-bay with 2FA). QNAP models: TS-453D (4-bay, around 350), TS-1685 (12-bay). Western Digital My Cloud EX2 Ultra (2-bay, around 150). Asustor AS6404T (4-bay). All support AES-256 encryption. Prioritize models with 2FA, granular access control, and regular security updates.
