Learning about glazing and firing techniques for earthenware pieces. Currently using pre-mixed glazes but wants to start creating custom glaze recipes. Specific interests:\n- Glaze formulation from scratch (materials, ingredient roles)\n- Achieving specific colors and effects (blues, reds, matte finishes, textured effects)\n- Mixing and testing glazes for consistency\n- Application techniques (brushing, pouring, spraying)\n- Food-safe finishes for functional ware\n\nRecommended resources include: Craftsy, Pottery Making Illustrated, The Potter's Council, Ceramic Arts Network, and YouTube channels like Simon Leach Pottery, Pottery By Osa, and The Ceramic School.
