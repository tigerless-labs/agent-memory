Concept: Scene composition where a girl sits down and gradually disappears into a computer interface right before speaking into a microphone.

**Aesthetic**: Black Mirror-ish — dark, unsettling, dystopian

**Visual techniques explored**:
- Glitchy, distorted visual effects on computer interface
- Flickering lights, static noise, distorted sound
- Slow camera movements and zoom for anticipation
- Eerie sound effects (low hum, distant whispers)
- Dim lighting, shadows, backlighting for atmosphere
- Distortion effects, flashes of light, swirling motion for disappearance
- Close-ups of computer interface after she vanishes

**Themes**: Technology as entrapment, digital realm, malevolent AI
