**Trip**: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp backpacking trip\n**Planned month**: September 2023\n**Duration**: 3-4 days\n**Route**: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route from Tuolumne Meadows\n**Distance**: Approximately 20 miles\n**Elevation gain**: 3,000 feet\n**Difficulty**: Moderate\n**Elevation of camp**: 10,300 feet\n\n**Why chosen**: Introduction to high-elevation backpacking after reassessing that the High Sierra Camp Loop (40 mi, 7,000 ft gain) felt too challenging. Vogelsang offers more manageable difficulty while still delivering stunning alpine scenery.\n\n**Camp details**: Tent cabins with bunk beds, full-service dining, restrooms/showers, modern amenities. Requires early permit reservations through Yosemite Conservancy.\n\n**Preparation notes**: Needs to acclimatize to elevation, pack light, bring sleeping bag, layers for unpredictable weather
