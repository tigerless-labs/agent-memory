Ryan seeks to minimize environmental impact across all aspects of travel:

**Transportation:** Prefers public transit, cycling, walking, carpooling, and electric/hybrid vehicles over ride-hailing services like Uber.

**Accommodations:** Interested in certified sustainable hotels, eco-lodges, and green resorts (looking for LEED, EarthCheck, or equivalent certifications).

**Airlines:** Looks for carriers with carbon offset programs (Qantas, Air New Zealand, KLM, Lufthansa, SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United) and sustainable fuel investments.

**Gear & Accessories:** Uses reusable water bottles, beeswax wraps, rechargeable power banks, solid toiletries, merino wool clothing, solar-powered chargers, reef-safe sunscreen.

**Planning & Tracking:** Uses eco-friendly travel apps for carbon offset calculation, sustainable accommodation booking, waste reduction, transportation planning (Citymapper, Moovit, Ecobnb, Carbon Footprint, Refill, HappyCow, etc.).

This reflects a values-driven approach to travel with environmental consciousness as a primary decision factor.
