Collaborative scene-writing project exploring MK (a human character) imprisoned by Wukong from a Journey to the West-inspired universe. Scenes focus on:
- MK's desperation for food/water
- Psychological tension and moral complexity
- MK reconciling the heroic legends about Wukong with the cruel reality
- Detailed dialogue and character introspection
- Dark captivity dynamics where Wukong holds power and MK attempts survival/bargaining

Writing style preference: detailed scenes with dialogue, character-driven narrative, internal monologue exploring MK's fear and confusion.
