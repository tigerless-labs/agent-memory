**Leading Ecocriticism Scholars:**

- **Lawrence Buell** — Study of literature and environment as cultural restoration; environmental imagination as capacity to shape thinking about environment and relations to it
- **Cheryll Glotfelty** — Ecocriticism examines how literature represents human-environment relationship; representation reflects and shapes societal attitudes
- **Jonathan Bate** — Ecocriticism involves reading literature as aspect of culture-nature relations, not just reading nature into literature
- **Eric D. Wilson** — Study of literature and environment as study of human culture and natural world relationship

**Core Methodologies in Ecocritical Scholarship:**
- Close reading of language, imagery, literary techniques in natural world representation
- Historical and cultural context analysis
- Environmental science and ecology analysis
- Comparative analysis across periods/cultures
- Ethnographic fieldwork and interviews
- Discourse analysis of environmental construction in cultural productions
