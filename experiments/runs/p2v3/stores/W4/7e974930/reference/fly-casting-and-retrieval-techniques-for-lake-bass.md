Retrieves: Strip retrieve (steady, consistent for streamers/nymphs, vary speed/length), Twitch retrieve (short quick movements for poppers/sliders/dry flies, imitates injured baitfish), Drag-free retrieve (natural sink/float without imparting movement for nymphs/dry flies). Action: Control sink rate with weights/leaders/tippet, vary fly movement to match natural behavior (streamers glide smoothly, dry flies twitch/skitter), incorporate pauses to allow bass to react. Presentation: Cast parallel to rocks not directly at them, use weedless flies for weed beds, cast into weed bed edges not thick center, vary depth with water conditions, experiment with 45-degree casting angles, use subtle presentation in clear water and aggressive retrieve in murky water. Lake tips: Look for baitfish schools, focus on transitional zones (drop-offs, weed lines), be prepared to move to different locations as day progresses.
