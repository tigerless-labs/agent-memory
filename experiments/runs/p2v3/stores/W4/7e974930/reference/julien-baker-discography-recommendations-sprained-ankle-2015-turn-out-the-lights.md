## Julien Baker Albums & Tracks\n\n**Albums:**\n- Sprained Ankle (2015) - Debut, introspective songwriting, soaring vocals, indie-rock and folk\n- Turn Out the Lights (2017) - Sophomore, more experimental and introspective\n- Boygenius (2018) - EP collaboration with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocal arrangements\n\n**Popular Tracks:**\n- Appointments - Critically acclaimed, introspective songwriting, soaring vocals\n- Turn Out the Lights - Title track, poignant lyrics, hauntingly beautiful melody\n- Rejoice - Themes of love, loss, introspection\n- Souvenir - Emotive, introspective storytelling\n- Hurt Less - Poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic elements\n- Red Door (2019) - Single\n- Favor (2020) - Single, emotive introspective storytelling\n\n**Live:** Known for emotive, introspective performances (NPR Tiny Desk, KCRW)
