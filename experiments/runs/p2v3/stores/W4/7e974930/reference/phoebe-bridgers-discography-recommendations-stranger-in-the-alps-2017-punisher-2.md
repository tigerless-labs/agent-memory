## Phoebe Bridgers Albums & Tracks\n\n**Albums:**\n- Stranger in the Alps (2017) - Debut, introspective songwriting, soaring vocals, indie-rock and folk elements\n- Punisher (2020) - Sophomore, more experimental and introspective\n- Boygenius (2018) - EP collaboration with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus, harmony-rich vocal arrangements\n\n**Notable Collaborations:**\n- Better Oblivion Community Center (2019) - with Conor Oberst\n\n**Popular Tracks:**\n- Scott Street - Hauntingly beautiful, showcases introspective songwriting\n- Killer - Poignant lyrics, catchy indie-rock melody\n- Motion Sickness - Themes of love, loss, introspection\n- Funeral - Emotive, introspective storytelling\n- Kyoto - Poignant lyrics, soaring vocals, indie-rock and electronic elements\n- 7 (2020) - Single\n\n**Live:** Known for emotive, introspective performances (NPR Tiny Desk, KCRW)
