Streamers: Woolly Bugger (black/olive/brown, baitfish/leech), Muddler Minnow (versatile, baitfish/crawdad/frog), Baitfish Streamer (silver/gold/chartreuse, shad/shiner). Poppers and Sliders: Poppers (white/yellow/chartreuse, surface), Sliders (subsurface, slow retrieve), Gurgler (gurgling sound on surface). Nymphs: Crayfish Patterns (Crayfish Hopper, Crawdad Nymph), Mayfly Nymphs (Hare's Ear, Pheasant Tail), Leech Patterns (Woolly Leech). Dry Flies: Frog Patterns, Terrestrials (ant, beetle, grasshopper). Recommended rod: 9-10 ft, weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking line, 9-12 ft leader.
