Recommendations for office chair brands beyond ERGOhuman:\n- **Herman Miller**: High-quality, ergonomic designs; range from budget-friendly to high-end\n- **Steelcase**: Well-established; offers ergonomic, task, and executive chairs\n- **Flash Furniture**: Affordable options with modern designs and decent ergonomics\n- **Autonomous**: Newer brand; affordable ergonomic chairs with sleek designs, adjustable lumbar support, cooling/ventilation
