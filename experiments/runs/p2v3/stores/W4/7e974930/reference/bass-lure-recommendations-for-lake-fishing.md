Topwater: Popper (surface commotion), Spook (walk-the-dog, wounded baitfish), Frog (heavy cover). Soft Plastics: Curly Tail Grub (jighead/Texas/Carolina rig), Worm (Texas/Carolina rig), Lizard (crawdad imitation). Crankbaits: Shad-imitating (medium-dive), Square-bill (shallow-dive), Deep-diving (10-20ft). Jigs: Jig and Pig (heavy cover), Swim bait (baitfish imitation), Finesse jig (tight spaces/clear water). Other: Spinnerbait (blade + trailer), A-rig (school of baitfish). Selection: Warm water = baitfish/crawdad; Cool = shad. Clear water = natural; Murky = strong action.
