## Related Singer-Songwriter & Folk-Influenced Artists\n\n**Core Recommendations:**\n- Bon Iver - For Emma, Forever Ago (pioneering album)\n- Fleet Foxes - Self-titled debut (harmony-rich vocal arrangements)\n- Sufjan Stevens - Carrie & Lowell (themes of love, loss, folk-rock)\n\n**Additional Artists to Explore:**\n- Courtney Marie Andrews\n- Vagabon - Cameroonian-American, blends indie-rock, folk, electronic\n- Adrianne Lenker (Big Thief)\n- Sharon Van Etten - Blends indie-rock, folk, electronic\n- The Tallest Man on Earth - Swedish singer-songwriter\n- Soccer Mommy - Lo-fi indie-rock with introspective lyrics\n- Clairo - Singer-songwriter, indie-rock, pop, electronic\n- Wallows - Indie-rock with catchy hooks, introspective lyrics\n- Mild High Club - Psych-pop with synths, jazz elements\n\n**Indie Rock & Indie Pop:**\n- Tame Impala\n- Arctic Monkeys\n- The 1975\n- Pale Waves\n\n**Alternative & Dream Pop:**\n- The Neighbourhood\n- Billie Eilish\n- MUNA\n\n**Chillwave & Synth-Pop:**\n- Glass Animals\n- MGMT\n- Passion Pit
