**Common Methodologies in Landscape Architecture:**

1. **Site Analysis** — Evaluating physical, ecological, cultural characteristics to understand project opportunities and constraints; foundational step providing context-responsive design information

2. **Conceptual Design** — Creating initial design ideas responding to site context; iterative exploration of options before final design

3. **Master Planning** — Comprehensive plan for site development; provides framework for project implementation over time

4. **Detailed Design** — Creating detailed drawings and specifications for construction; provides construction information

5. **Construction Management** — Overseeing project construction to ensure design implementation

**Landscape Architecture's Influence on Ecocriticism:**
- Representation of nature shapes public perceptions and can reflect holistic understanding of environment
- Environmental justice considerations highlight impacts on marginalized communities
- Sustainability focus influences how literature's portrayal of human-environment relationship is interpreted
- Aesthetic representation of nature shapes environmental perceptions
