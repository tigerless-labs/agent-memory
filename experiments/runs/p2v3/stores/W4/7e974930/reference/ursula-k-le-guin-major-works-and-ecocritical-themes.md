**Le Guin's Major Works:**

- **The Dispossessed (1974)** — Anarchist commune on distant planet; explores political systems, relationship between humans and environment, critique of capitalism
- **The Left Hand of Darkness (1969)** — Genderless inhabitants; themes of gender, sexuality, power; human-environment relationship
- **The Word for World is Forest (1976)** — Human colonizers vs. native inhabitants; imperialism, colonialism, human-environment relationship
- **The Lathe of Heaven (1971)** — Dreams change reality; intersection of science fiction and environmentalism; critique of destructive human impact and science/technology misuse
- **Always Coming Home (1985)** — Post-apocalyptic California; environmentalism, ecology, human-environment relationship; collection of stories, songs, poetry

Le Guin known for exploring complex social and political issues, critiquing capitalist ideologies, and examining destructive impact of humans on environment.
