**Interdisciplinary Scholarship** — Combining different disciplines to study a problem; synthesizes insights and methods from multiple fields for comprehensive understanding. Example: climate change study drawing on atmospheric science, economics, sociology, political science.

**Transdisciplinary Scholarship** — Goes beyond interdisciplinarity by actively involving stakeholders and practitioners; collaborative co-creation of knowledge aimed at solving real-world problems. Bridges gap between academic research and practical application.

**Simple distinction:** Interdisciplinary = combining disciplines to study a problem; Transdisciplinary = combining disciplines to solve a problem.

Ecocriticism can be seen as interdisciplinary field involving literary studies, environmental studies, cultural studies — or transdisciplinary when engaging stakeholders to address environmental issues.
