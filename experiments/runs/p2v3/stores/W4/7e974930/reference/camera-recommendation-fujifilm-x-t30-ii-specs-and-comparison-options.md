Fujifilm X-T30 II recommended for landscape photography. Key specs: 26.1MP APS-C sensor, intelligent hybrid autofocus, compact size, vari-angle touchscreen LCD. Body approximately 899 USD, XF 15-45mm kit lens approximately 499 USD. Alternative options considered: Fujifilm X-T3 (1099 USD, weather-sealed), Sony a6400 (998 USD), Sony a7 II (1398 USD, full-frame). All provide good image quality for landscape/nature work.
