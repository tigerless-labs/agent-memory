Rod: 9-10 ft length, weight 6-8, medium to fast action. Reel: capacity 150-200 yards of 20-lb test backing, reliable drag system. Fly Line: weight-forward floating or intermediate sinking, weight 6-8, 90-foot length standard. Leader: 9-12 ft monofilament or fluorocarbon, 15-20 lb breaking strength. Tippet: 1-2 ft, 12-15 lb breaking strength. Accessories: tippet ring (prevents line twist), swivel. Tips: Use longer leader in clear water (reduce visibility), heavier leader for larger flies, sinking line for deeper water. Practice knots like Blood Knot and Surgeon's Knot.
