Fern has pest infestation of tiny moving dots crawling on leaves and stems. Likely spider mites or springtails based on appearance. 

**Treatment plan:**
- Treat with neem oil spray following product instructions
- Mist fern 2-3 times per week to maintain humidity and help prevent pest return
- Use distilled or filtered water for misting (not tap water)
- Mist in the morning to allow leaves to dry before nightfall
- Focus misting on undersides of leaves where pests hide
- Avoid misting the crown (where leaves meet stem) to prevent rot
- Isolate plant from other plants to prevent pest spread
- Prune any heavily infested leaves or stems
- Monitor plant closely and repeat treatment as necessary
