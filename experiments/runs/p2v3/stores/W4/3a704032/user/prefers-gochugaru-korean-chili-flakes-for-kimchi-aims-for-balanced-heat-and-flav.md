Chose gochugaru as the primary chili flake for kimchi-making. Appreciates its medium-to-hot heat level and balanced flavor. Starting measurements: 1/4 to 1/2 tsp per 2 cups vegetables for mild results, up to 1-2 tsp for hotter. Always starts small and adjusts to taste.
