**Core interests:**
- Historical fiction, mythology retellings, character-driven narratives
- Contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists
- Complex relationships between characters (especially between women)
- Intersectional narratives exploring identity across multiple dimensions (gender, race, class, sexuality, immigrant experience)
- Themes of public vs. private selves, power dynamics, privilege
- Stories where relationships are central to character development and identity

**Why it matters:** Values narratives that explore the nuanced ways personal experience shapes identity, and how different social positions (as woman, person of color, immigrant, etc.) intersect to create complex lived realities. Appreciates when books balance escapism with emotional and intellectual depth.
