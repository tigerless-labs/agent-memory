Basil plant on balcony is struggling from excessive direct sunlight. Currently using general-purpose potting mix. Plan to relocate to shadier spot with filtered sunlight (east- or west-facing area or using shade cloth).

**Care needs identified:**
- Well-draining soil with pH 6.0-7.0
- Organic matter content
- Good aeration
- Nutrient-rich environment
- Humidity maintenance
- Reduce fertilizer frequency (half-strength balanced mix)
- Regular pruning to prevent flowering
- Temperature range 65-75°F (18-24°C)
- Adjust watering to account for less evaporation in shade
- Consider upgrading to specialized herb/vegetable potting mix or custom mix
