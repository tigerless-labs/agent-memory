First kimchi batch (around 2023-05-14) failed due to excessive chili flakes and salt. Too much heat and saltiness made it inedible. Recovery approach: rinse under cold running water to remove excess salt; add complementary aromatics (garlic 2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables, ginger 1-2 inches per 2 cups vegetables) to balance flavors. Key lesson: measure chili flakes carefully and taste as you go — easier to add more than to remove excess heat.
