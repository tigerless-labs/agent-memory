**Garlic preparation:**\n- Use fresh garlic (2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables)\n- Mince or crush to release oils and flavor\n- Avoid old or sprouted garlic (bitter)\n\n**Ginger preparation:**\n- Use fresh ginger (1-2 inches per 2 cups vegetables)\n- Peel and grate or slice thinly to release oils\n- Young ginger preferred but mature ginger works\n\n**Best practices:**\n- Handle gently to avoid bruising/bitterness\n- Use separate cutting board/knife to prevent flavor transfer\n- Optional: sauté in oil before adding to mellow pungency\n- Mix with gochugaru, fish sauce for balanced flavor
