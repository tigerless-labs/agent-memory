Recommendations for graphic novels with similar blend of sci-fi/fantasy, complex characters, and stunning art:

1. **Monstress** by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda — Fantasy epic with supernatural abilities, complex characters, moral ambiguity
2. **The Wicked + The Divine** by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie — Mythology and pop culture intersection, gods as pop stars
3. **Black Hammer** by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston — Superhero deconstruction, heroes stranded on farm, exploration of human side of superheroism
4. **The Umbrella Academy** by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá — Dysfunctional superhero family, action/drama/humor blend
5. **Descender** by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen — Sci-fi epic with young robot boy, explores identity and humanity
6. **The Goddamned** by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra — Biblical figures and mythological creatures in dark gritty reality
7. **Y: The Last Man** by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra — Last human male on Earth, explores gender and power
