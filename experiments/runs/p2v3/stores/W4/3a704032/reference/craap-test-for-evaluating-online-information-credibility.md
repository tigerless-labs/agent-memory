The CRAAP Test is a framework for assessing the credibility and reliability of online sources:

- **Currency**: Is the information up-to-date?
- **Relevance**: Does the source relate to your research topic?
- **Authority**: Who is the author? What are their credentials?
- **Accuracy**: Is the information supported by evidence?
- **Purpose**: Why does the source exist? What is the author's intent?

Used in digital literacy instruction to teach students how to verify online sources before using them for research.
