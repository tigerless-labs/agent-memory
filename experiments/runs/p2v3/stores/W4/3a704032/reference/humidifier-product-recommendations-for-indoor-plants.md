## Recommended Humidifiers

### Levoit LV600HH Hybrid Humidifier
- Tank: 6 liters (large capacity)
- Type: Cool and warm mist settings
- Features: Adjustable humidistat
- Good for: General plant care, larger spaces

### Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate Humidifier
- Tank: 1 liter
- Type: Cool mist
- Features: Compact, easy to use
- Good for: Small to medium-sized spaces, African violets

### Deneve Minimal Aroma Humidifier
- Tank: 2.5 liters
- Type: Ultrasonic
- Features: Built-in humidistat, adjustable mist output, sleek design
- Good for: Stylish, easy to clean

### Crane Drop Ultrasonic Cool Mist Humidifier
- Tank: 1 liter
- Type: Ultrasonic cool mist
- Features: Whisper-quiet operation, easy cleaning
- Good for: Plants sensitive to noise, African violets

## Usage Tips
- Position within 3-5 feet of plants (1-2 feet for African violets)
- Use hygrometer to monitor humidity levels
- Clean regularly to prevent bacterial growth
- Ferns prefer humid environments; African violets thrive at 50-60% humidity
- Combine with good air circulation to prevent fungal diseases
