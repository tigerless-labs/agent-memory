AWS Escalation Engineer II focuses on troubleshooting complex customer issues, technical guidance, content creation, and collaborating across product/operations/security teams. A Computer Network and Systems Engineer plans, develops, and optimizes network services with focus on multiple OS configurations and operational readiness. Skills directly transfer: troubleshooting, technical depth, configuration management, operational rigor. Gaps: Network/Systems is infrastructure-focused (not customer-facing) and requires on-premises/hybrid/multi-OS experience beyond cloud. Assessment: Strong fit if candidate has prior Windows/Linux infrastructure experience.
