Online platforms recommended for discovering emerging artists and contemporary art: Artsy, Saatchi Art, Behance, Instagram, Juxtapoz, Hyperallergic, New American Paintings, ArtNet, The Artist's Institute, Platform, Artspace, 1stdibs, Uprise Art, Minted, Collecteurs. Many offer curated collections, virtual events, artist talks, and direct purchase options.
