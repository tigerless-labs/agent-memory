## Music Programming Languages

**Beginner-Friendly:**
- **Sonic Pi** — Live coding environment; simple syntax, real-time feedback, free/open-source
- **ChucK** — Music-specific language with built-in audio synthesis tools, free/open-source
- **Python** — General-purpose; large community and documentation, used in music production

**Visual Programming:**
- **Max/MSP** — Visual language for electronic music; create custom instruments and effects via patching
- **Pure Data (PD)** — Free/open-source alternative to Max/MSP; for interactive music and multimedia

**Advanced:**
- **SuperCollider** — Real-time audio synthesis and algorithmic composition
- **Csound** — 30+ year old software synthesis system for complex musical structures
- **Swift** — Apple language for iOS/macOS music apps

## Getting Started with Sonic Pi

- Built-in tutorials and examples in Help menu
- Active community: Twitter, Sonic Pi forums, Discord
- Workshops/classes at music schools and community centers
- Video tutorials; Sonic Pi Live Stream series on Twitch
