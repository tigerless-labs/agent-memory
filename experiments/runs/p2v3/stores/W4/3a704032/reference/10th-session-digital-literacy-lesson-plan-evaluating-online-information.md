**Objective**: Students learn to evaluate credibility and reliability of online information

**Materials**: Computers/laptops, whiteboard, CRAAP Test worksheet, online resources (news articles, academic papers)

**Lesson Schedule** (100 minutes total):
- 10 min: Introduction to evaluating online information importance
- 20 min: Teach CRAAP Test framework with examples, partner practice
- 30 min: Individual research activity - students find 2 sources, evaluate using CRAAP Test, present findings
- 10 min: Break
- 20 min: Create class list of evaluation tips (checking author credentials, finding alternative sources), discuss application
- 10 min: Conclusion and recap

**Assessment**:
- Review CRAAP Test worksheets for accuracy/completeness
- Short quiz or class discussion
- Monitor group participation
