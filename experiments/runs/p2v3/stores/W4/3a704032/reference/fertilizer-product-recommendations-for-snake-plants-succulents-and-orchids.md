## Snake Plant Fertilizers
Prefer balanced fertilizers (20-20-20 NPK) with slightly acidic pH (6.0-7.0):
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Plant Food (20-20-20)
- Espoma Organic Indoor Plant Fertilizer (2-2-2)
- Schultz All Purpose Plant Food (20-20-20)

Dilute to half strength; apply during growing season only.

## Succulent & Cactus Fertilizers
Require higher phosphorus for root growth and flowering (10-20-10 NPK):
- Miracle-Gro Cactus, Palm & Citrus Food (10-20-10)
- Espoma Organic Cactus! Fertilizer (5-2-6)
- Schultz Cactus and Succulent Food (10-20-10)

Low nitrogen preferred. Dilute to half strength.

## Orchid Fertilizers
Require high phosphorus (15-30-15 or higher) to promote blooming:
- Miracle-Gro Orchid Food (30-10-10)
- Espoma Organic Orchid! Fertilizer (15-30-15)
- Schultz Orchid Food (20-20-20)

Feed sparingly (monthly), avoid high-nitrogen formulas. Dilute to half strength.

## General Tips
- Always dilute to half strength to avoid root burn
- Fertilize during growing season (spring/summer) only
- Avoid general-purpose fertilizer for orchids and succulents
