Active listening and questioning techniques to promote student engagement:

1. **Ask open-ended questions** - Require more than yes/no answers to encourage deeper thinking. Examples: "What did you learn?" "How can you apply this in your daily life?"

2. **Paraphrase and summarize** - Show student thoughts are valued, ensure mutual understanding

3. **Encourage active listening** - Ask students to summarize peers' points or ask follow-up questions

4. **Use visual aids** - Diagrams and graphic organizers help students understand and organize thoughts

These techniques are effective during reflection and discussion time in digital literacy instruction.
