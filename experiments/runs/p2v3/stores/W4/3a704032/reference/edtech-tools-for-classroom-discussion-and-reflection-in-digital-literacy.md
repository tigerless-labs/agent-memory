Tools recommended for facilitating student engagement during reflection and discussion:

1. **Padlet** - Collaborative platform for students to share thoughts and ideas in a virtual space
2. **Nearpod** - Interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions for participation
3. **Flipgrid** - Video discussion platform for short video responses to prompts
4. **Google Jamboard** - Virtual whiteboard for visual collaboration and idea sharing

All can be used to facilitate discussion and reflection in digital literacy instruction.
