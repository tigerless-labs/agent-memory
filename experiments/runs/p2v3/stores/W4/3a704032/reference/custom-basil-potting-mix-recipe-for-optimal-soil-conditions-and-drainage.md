**Custom Basil Potting Mix Recipe:**
- 2 parts peat moss or coconut coir
- 1 part perlite or vermiculite
- 1 part compost or worm castings
- 1 part general-purpose potting soil

Mix ingredients thoroughly and adjust pH if necessary (ideal range: 6.0-7.0). Can optionally add a balanced, slow-release fertilizer per manufacturer instructions.

**Key soil requirements for basil:**
- Well-draining (prevents waterlogging and root rot)
- pH range 6.0-7.0 (slightly acidic to neutral)
- Rich in organic matter for moisture retention and nutrient content
- Good aeration to ensure healthy root oxygen supply
- Nutrient-rich (basil is a heavy feeder)

**Alternative:** Look for pre-made potting mixes specifically labeled for herbs or vegetables, which typically contain the necessary ingredients for healthy basil growth.
