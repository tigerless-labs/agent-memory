## Rose Bush Pruning Guidance (from 2023-05-20 consultation)\n\n**During blooming season:** \n- Deadhead (remove dead/wilted flowers) at any time to encourage new blooms\n- Do light pruning to shape bush and promote air circulation\n- Remove weak, spindly growth and any crossing/rubbing stems\n\n**After blooming season:** \n- Best time for extensive pruning to rejuvenate plant and control size\n- Allows plant to complete flowering cycle and store energy for next season\n\n**General pruning technique:**\n- Make clean cuts at 45-degree angle\n- Remove weak or diseased wood\n- Helps prevent disease spread and encourage healthy growth\n- Best dormant-season pruning (winter/early spring) promotes healthy growth and encourages new blooms
