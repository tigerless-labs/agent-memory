## Spider Plant Potting Mix Needs
Prefer well-draining mixes rich in organic matter with good aeration.

## Recommended Potting Mixes
- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix (general-purpose, well-balanced)
- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix (high-quality, organic, peat moss/compost/perlite blend)
- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix (nutrient-rich, eco-friendly, compost/peat moss/perlite blend)
- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix (professional-grade, peat moss/vermiculite/perlite blend)

## Key Ingredients to Look For
- Peat moss or coconut coir (moisture retention, acidic pH)
- Compost or humus (nutrients, soil structure)
- Perlite or vermiculite (drainage, aeration)
- Worm castings or worm compost (added nutrients)

## Repotting Tips
- Use pot only 1-2 sizes larger (spider plants prefer being slightly root-bound)
- Gently remove from old pot, inspect roots for rot
- Trim damaged roots
- Water thoroughly after repotting
- Keep soil consistently moist but not waterlogged
