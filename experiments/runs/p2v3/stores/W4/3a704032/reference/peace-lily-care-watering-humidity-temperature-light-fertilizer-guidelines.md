## Peace Lily Care Summary (from 2023-05-20 consultation)\n\n**Light:** Bright, indirect light preferred; avoid direct sunlight. East/west-facing windows ideal.\n\n**Watering:** Water when top 1-2 inches of soil feel dry. \n- Spring/summer: every 7-10 days\n- Fall/winter: every 10-14 days\n- Critical: avoid overwatering and waterlogged soil; don't water the crown\n\n**Humidity:** Prefers humid environment but tolerates average humidity (40-50%). Mist 1-2 times per week. More frequent misting (every 3-4 days) if in dry climate. Mist in morning to prevent fungal issues.\n\n**Temperature:** Ideal range 68-72°F (20-22°C); acceptable range 65-75°F (18-24°C). Avoid below 55°F or above 85°F. Keep away from drafts and temperature extremes.\n\n**Fertilization:** Use balanced fertilizer (20-20-20 or similar). Apply every 1-2 weeks during growing season (spring/summer), reduce to monthly in fall/winter. If using concentrated formula, dilute to half strength to avoid root burn.\n\n**Potting mix:** Use well-draining mix designed for tropical plants or orchids.\n\n**Pruning/maintenance:** Remove dead leaves and flowers to prevent disease spread. Repot only when plant outgrows container or shows nutrient deficiency (spring/summer preferred).\n\n**Pest monitoring:** Check regularly for spider mites, mealybugs, scale. Treat with insecticidal soap or neem oil if found.
