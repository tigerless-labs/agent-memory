## Lens Cleaning Kit Recommendations

**Recommended Kits:**
- **Canon Lens Cleaning Kit** — designed for Canon lenses, includes soft brush, microfiber cloth, cleaning solution
- **Nikon Lens Cleaning Kit** — works on Canon lenses too, includes brush, microfiber cloth, solution
- **Zeiss Lens Cleaning Kit** — high-quality microfiber cloth, cleaning solution, soft brush

**Lens Cleaning Cloths:**
- **Microfiber cloths** — gentle, effective at removing smudges/fingerprints, lint-free
- **Pre-moistened lens cleaning tissues** — convenient for quick cleanings (Canon or Nikon branded)
- **Sensor cleaning swabs** — for thorough cleanings, removes dust/debris

**Safe Cleaning Practices for Canon EF Lenses:**
- Use soft, dry cloth (avoid paper towels, abrasive materials)
- Handle by edges to prevent skin oils transferring
- Apply cleaning solution to cloth, NOT directly to lens
- Clean in circular motion (center outward)
- Inspect and repeat if needed
- Test on small area first
- Always follow instructions with cleaning products
- Avoid touching lens contacts
