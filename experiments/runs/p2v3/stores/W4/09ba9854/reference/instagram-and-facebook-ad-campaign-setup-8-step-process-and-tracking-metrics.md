## Setup Process (8 steps)\n1. Create Instagram Business Account\n2. Create Facebook Ads Manager Account  \n3. Set Up Ad Account with payment method\n4. Choose Ad Objective (Awareness/Consideration/Conversion)\n5. Define Target Audience\n6. Create Ad (photo, video, carousel, or stories format)\n7. Set Budget and Schedule\n8. Launch and Monitor Campaign\n\n## Key Metrics for Awareness Campaigns\n- **Reach**: number of people who saw the ad\n- **Impressions**: total times ad was displayed\n- **Frequency**: average times each person saw ad\n- **Engagement**: likes, comments, saves, shares\n- **CPM**: cost per thousand impressions\n- **Ad Recall Lift**: how well people remember the ad\n- **Brand Awareness Lift**: increase in brand awareness among target audience\n- **Social Media Engagement Metrics**: followers, organic engagement\n\n## Recommended Analytics Tools\n- Facebook Ads Manager (built-in metrics)\n- Instagram Insights (organic performance)\n- Hootsuite (multi-platform analytics)\n- Sprout Social (campaign management)\n- Agorapulse (engagement tracking)
