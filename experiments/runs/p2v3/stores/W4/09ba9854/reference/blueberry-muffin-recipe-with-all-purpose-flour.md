**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C). Line 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners or grease with cooking spray.
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in large bowl.
3. Whisk together melted butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla extract in another bowl until smooth.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Do not overmix.
5. Gently fold in blueberries with spatula.
6. Divide batter evenly among muffin cups. Top with additional blueberries if desired.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.
8. Let muffins cool in pan for 5 minutes before transferring to wire rack to cool completely.

**Note:** Can substitute all-purpose flour with whole wheat flour for a healthier option; may result in denser, darker muffin and require slight baking time adjustment. May need to add honey or maple syrup for added sweetness since whole wheat flour has deeper flavor.
