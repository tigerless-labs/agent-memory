## Lens Case & Pouch Recommendations

**Lens Cases:**
- **Think Tank Photo Lens Case** — excellent protection/durability, soft padded interior, specific sizes
- **Lowepro Lens Case** — high-quality, durable, various sizes and styles
- **Nanuk Lens Case** — extreme protection, designed for rugged outdoor use, excellent shock absorption

**Lens Pouches:**
- **Op/Tech USA Lens Pouch** — soft padded, various sizes, Velcro closure
- **Lenscoat Lens Pouch** — specific lens sizes, water-resistant, breathable
- **Tamrac Lens Pouch** — protection focused, various sizes, zippered closure

**Key Features to Look For:**
- Snug fit to prevent movement/damage
- Durable, high-quality materials
- Thick, soft padding for shock absorption
- Secure closure (zippered or Velcro)
- Breathable materials (if storing in humid conditions)

**Lens Storage Best Practices:**
- Always store with caps on
- Keep lenses upright (prevent oil flow on mounts)
- Avoid stacking lenses
- Clean before storage
- Clean contacts gently with soft cloth
