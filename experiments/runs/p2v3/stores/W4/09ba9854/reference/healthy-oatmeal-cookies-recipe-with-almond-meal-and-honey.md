**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.
2. Whisk together rolled oats, almond meal, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon in large bowl.
3. Whisk together melted coconut oil, honey, egg, and vanilla extract in another bowl.
4. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
5. Gently fold in raisins.
6. Using cookie scoop or spoon, drop dough onto prepared baking sheet, spacing about 2 inches apart.
7. Bake for 10-12 minutes, or until cookies are golden brown.
8. Let cookies cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to wire rack to cool completely.

**Note:** Can substitute raisins with dark chocolate chips (higher cocoa percentage, less sugar) for variation while maintaining healthiness.
