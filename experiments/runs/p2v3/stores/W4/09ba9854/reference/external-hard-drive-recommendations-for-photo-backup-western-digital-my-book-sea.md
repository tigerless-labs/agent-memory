## External Hard Drive Recommendations for Photo Backup

**Popular Options Discussed:**
- **Western Digital My Book** — up to 16TB, compact, built-in power management, Mac/PC compatible
- **Seagate Backup Plus** — up to 5TB, sleek design, 2-year warranty, compact
- **LaCie Rugged** — rugged/durable design for rough handling, up to 5TB, Mac/PC compatible
- **G-Technology G-Drive** — high-performance, up to 10TB, designed for creative professionals

**Key Selection Factors:**
- Capacity (calculate 2-3x current storage needs)
- Compatibility (Mac or PC)
- Interface (USB 3.0, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C)
- Speed (7200rpm or SSD)
- Durability (rugged/waterproof if portable)
- Security features (encryption, password protection)
- Warranty and support
- Portability (size and weight)
- Price range (under $100 to over $1,000)

**Best Practice:** 3-2-1 backup rule — three copies of files, two different media types, one copy offsite
