**Reedsy Character Profile Template** — Free downloadable template for character development with 12 sections:\n\n1. Basic Info (name, age, occupation)\n2. Physical Appearance\n3. Personality (traits, values, goals, flaws)\n4. Backstory\n5. Motivations\n6. Conflict (internal and external)\n7. Relationships\n8. Skills and Abilities\n9. Fears and Phobias\n10. Habits and Quirks\n11. Emotional Landscape\n12. Evolution\n\nProvides good balance between brevity and depth. Available at: https://reedsy.com/character-profile-template
