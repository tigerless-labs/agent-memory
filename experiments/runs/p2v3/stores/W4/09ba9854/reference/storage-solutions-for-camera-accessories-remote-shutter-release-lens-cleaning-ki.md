## Storage Solutions for Small Photography Accessories

**Remote Shutter Release Storage:**
- Small, soft pouches or cases designed for remotes
- Keychain storage (if model has keychain hole)
- Small zippered compartment within camera bag or accessory pouch

**Lens Cleaning Kit Storage:**
- Dedicated lens cleaning pouch
- Small compact storage box with dividers/compartments
- Travel case specifically designed for lens cleaning kits

**Extra Batteries Storage:**
- Individual battery cases or pouches (belt/bag/keychain attachable)
- Battery storage box with dividers/compartments
- Designated compartments within camera bag

**General Accessory Organization:**
- Accessory pouches (soft cases for multiple items)
- Mini storage bags or cubes (zippered, for cables/adapters/small items)
- Travel organizer/accessory cases
- DIY storage (repurposed containers: Altoids tins, film canisters)

**Selection Criteria:**
- **Protection** — prevent damage/scratches
- **Accessibility** — easy to find items when needed
- **Organization** — keeps accessories organized
- **Portability** — compact, lightweight, easy to carry
