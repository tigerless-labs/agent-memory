Two compelling prompt concepts explored:

**The Memory Thief**: Dystopian future where memories can be extracted and sold. Protagonist is a master thief hired to steal a powerful CEO's memories. Explores identity through memory manipulation, with themes of discovering one's true past and questioning the authenticity of memories and self.

**The Royal Affair**: Futuristic intergalactic kingdom where a commoner/outsider falls for a royal bound by duty. Explores identity through forbidden love, duty vs. personal desire, and navigating complex societal/political structures that challenge the character's sense of self.

Key guidance provided:
- World-building: establish clear rules for tech/magic, develop rich history, create diverse settings, define cultural implications
- Characters: develop clear motivations, create multi-dimensional characters with contradictions, show emotional vulnerability, avoid insta-love with slow-burn connections
- Themes: use character arcs and plot twists to explore identity formation, question purpose, embrace change, incorporate symbolism for self-discovery journeys
