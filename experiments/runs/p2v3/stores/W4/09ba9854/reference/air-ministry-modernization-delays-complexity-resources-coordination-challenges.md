Air Ministry took significant time to modernize structure and adapt to technology/warfare changes due to: complexity of structural changes requiring accommodation of new aircraft technology and tactics; need for resource/funding allocation and infrastructure updates; need to reflect changing government priorities and international relations; requirement for coordination across multiple departments and stakeholders.
