Key strategies for live streaming engagement:\n\n**General:**\n- Plan engaging content with a content calendar\n- Interact with audience in real-time, use their names\n- Optimize stream setup (lighting, sound, camera)\n- Monitor analytics to track performance\n- Follow up after streams to continue conversation\n\n**Content creation tips:**\n- Keep ideas journal to track content ideas\n- Repurpose existing content across formats\n- Mix content types (Q&A, tutorials, behind-the-scenes, guest streams)\n- Ask audience what they want via polls/surveys\n- Stay authentic and consistent\n\n**Platform-specific formats:**\n- Instagram: 10-30 min, visually focused, behind-the-scenes, mobile-first\n- YouTube: 30-60 min, in-depth, evergreen content, good for tutorials\n- Facebook: 30-90 min, community-focused, personal stories, event promotion\n\n**For tutorials specifically:**\n- Choose specific theme/focus\n- Prepare products/tools beforehand\n- Plan content outline or script\n- Use good lighting to showcase details\n- Encourage real-time questions and interaction
