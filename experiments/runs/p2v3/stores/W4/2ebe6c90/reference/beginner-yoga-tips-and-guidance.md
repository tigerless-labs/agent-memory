## Starting Yoga as a Beginner\n- Seek beginner-friendly classes designed for new practitioners\n- Listen to your body; don't push too hard initially\n- Yoga is not competitive; focus on doing poses to your ability\n- Use props (blocks, straps) for support and proper alignment\n- Find an attentive, supportive teacher who offers modifications\n- Practice patience—yoga is a journey, not a destination\n\n## Keeping Up with Class Pace\n- Take breaks anytime (child's pose or seated breathing)\n- Use breath as a grounding and centering tool during challenging moments\n- Modify poses to fit your body and comfort level\n- Practice at home beforehand to build familiarity and confidence\n- Remember everyone's body and progress path differ\n- Consistency matters more than intensity—show up regularly to improve over time
