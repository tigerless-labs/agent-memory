## Getting to Tortuguero National Park from San José\n\n**Best options:**\n1. Domestic flight (SYQ to TTQ, ~1 hour) + boat transfer (1-2 hours)\n2. Shuttle bus to La Pavona (4-5 hours) + boat transfer (1-2 hours)\n3. Private transfer to La Pavona + private boat\n\n**Key tips:**\n- Book flights/shuttles well in advance, especially Dec-April peak season\n- Check weather before traveling (affects boat transfers)\n- Pack light for boat transfers\n- Many eco-lodges offer transportation packages from San José\n\n## Road conditions in Costa Rica\n- ~30% paved roads (urban/highway areas) in good condition\n- ~70% unpaved (rural/national parks)\n- Mountain roads: steep, winding, narrow\n- Rainy season (May-Nov): muddy, flooded, potential closures\n- 4x4 vehicle recommended for rural exploration\n\n## Recommended eco-lodges\n- Lapa Rios (Osa Peninsula)\n- Monteverde Lodge (Monteverde)\n- Arenal Observatory Lodge\n- Tortuga Lodge (Tortuguero)\n- El Remanso Lodge (Osa Peninsula)\n\n## Sustainable tour operators\n- Costa Rica Expeditions\n- Osa Conservation\n- Tortuguero Canals Tours (local, family-owned)
