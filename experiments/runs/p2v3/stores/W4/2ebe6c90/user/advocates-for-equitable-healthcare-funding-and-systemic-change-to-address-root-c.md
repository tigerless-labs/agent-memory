Believes healthcare inequalities must be addressed through systemic change targeting root causes: poverty, discrimination, and lack of education. Prioritizes healthcare funding for marginalized communities as both a moral and economic imperative. Advocates for bold, innovative approaches and proactive government action rather than crisis-driven responses. Frustrated that governments only prioritize healthcare equity during crises like COVID-19 rather than taking preventive action.
