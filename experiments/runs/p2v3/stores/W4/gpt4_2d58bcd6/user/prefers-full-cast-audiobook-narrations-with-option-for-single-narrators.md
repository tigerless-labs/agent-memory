Enjoys full-cast narrations especially for novels with multiple characters and dialogue-heavy scenes, as they enhance immersion and help track who's speaking. Also appreciates single narrators who bring unique tone and style. Believes narrator's performance is crucial to the audiobook experience - good narrators elevate stories while poor ones detract.
