Suggestions for increasing daily physical activity beyond structured gym/workout time:\n\n1. Take the stairs instead of elevators\n2. Do desk exercises (chair squats, desk push-ups, leg lifts, wrist extensions)\n3. Walk to coworker's desk instead of emailing\n4. Park farther away to get more walking in\n5. Take walks during phone calls (when not requiring full attention)\n6. Turn housework into a workout (music, increased intensity, squats/lunges while vacuuming)\n7. Take walking meetings with colleagues/friends\n8. Use a fitness tracker to track daily steps and set goals\n9. Take short stretching breaks throughout the day\n10. Try active commuting (bike, bus, train)\n11. Play active games with kids (tag, hide-and-seek, soccer)\n12. Take fitness classes during lunch break\n\nKey principle: Every bit counts and creative activity fitting into existing routines adds up.
