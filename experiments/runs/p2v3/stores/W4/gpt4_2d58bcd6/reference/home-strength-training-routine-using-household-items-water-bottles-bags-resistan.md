Sample 3-day home strength training routine recommended for supplementing gym work:\n\n**Monday (Upper Body):**\n- Push-ups: 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Incline dumbbell press (water bottles/cans): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Bent-over dumbbell rows (water bottles/cans): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Tricep dips (using chair/bench): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n\n**Wednesday (Lower Body):**\n- Squats: 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Calf raises (bodyweight or weights): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Lunges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg\n- Glute bridges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n\n**Friday (Core):**\n- Plank: 3 sets of 30-60 seconds\n- Russian twists (using weight/medicine ball): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Leg raises (lying on back): 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n- Bicycle crunches: 3 sets of 10-12 reps\n\nKey: Start with lighter weights, gradually increase intensity, focus on proper form.
