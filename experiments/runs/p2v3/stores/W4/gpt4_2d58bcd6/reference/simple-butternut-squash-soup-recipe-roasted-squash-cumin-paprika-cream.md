Simple Butternut Squash Soup (4-6 servings)

Ingredients:
- 1 large butternut squash (2-3 lbs)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (optional)
- Fresh herbs (parsley or sage) for garnish

Instructions:
1. Roast squash at 400F for 45-50 minutes until tender
2. Saute onion and garlic in olive oil
3. Add spices and broth, bring to boil
4. Add roasted squash flesh
5. Blend until smooth
6. Add cream if desired
7. Season to taste and serve

Storage: Refrigerate 3-5 days, freeze up to 6 months in portions. Reheat to 165F.
