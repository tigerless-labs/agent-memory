**Injury Prevention Strategies:**\n1. Warm up and cool down — 5-10 minutes light cardio and dynamic stretching before; static stretching after\n2. Stretch and foam roll regularly\n3. Strengthen core and ankles — exercises like planks, side planks, ankle circles\n4. Use proper gear and equipment for activity/sport\n5. Listen to your body — stop if fatigued or experiencing pain\n6. Gradually increase intensity and volume — avoid sudden changes\n\n**Injury Recovery (RICE principle):**\n- Rest: Stop activity and allow recovery time\n- Ice: Apply ice to reduce inflammation\n- Compression: Use wraps/sleeves to reduce swelling\n- Elevation: Raise the injured area to promote blood flow\n\n**Additional Recovery Tips:**\n- Gentle stretching and mobilization\n- Take rest days and modify workouts as needed\n- Seek professional help for chronic pain or severe injuries\n\n**Ankle-Specific Prevention:**\n- Ankle braces/supports during high-impact activities\n- Balance exercises like single-leg squats and balance boards\n- Proprioception training
