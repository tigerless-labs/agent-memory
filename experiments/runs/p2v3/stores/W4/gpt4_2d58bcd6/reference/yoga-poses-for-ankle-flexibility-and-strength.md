Recommended yoga poses for improving ankle flexibility and strength:\n\n1. **Ankle Circles** — Lift one foot off ground, draw circles with ankle (small to large), 5-10 reps clockwise and counterclockwise each leg\n2. **Toe Spreads** — Lift one foot, spread toes apart then bring together, 10-15 reps per leg\n3. **Ankle Alphabet** — Lift one foot, draw alphabet A-Z with toes\n4. **Calf Raises** — Stand on edge of step with heels hanging off, raise onto tiptoes, 10-15 reps\n5. **Downward-Facing Dog** — Hold 30 seconds to 1 minute, keeps ankles in line with shoulders\n6. **Tree Pose** — Stand on one leg, place other foot on inner thigh, hold 30 seconds to 1 minute per side\n7. **Eagle Pose** — Cross one leg over the other, wrap ankle around calf, hold 30 seconds to 1 minute per side\n8. **Seated Forward Fold** — Fold forward reaching for toes/shins, hold 30 seconds to 1 minute\n\nAdditional ankle mobility: ankle rotations, toe curls, heel raises.
