Authors: Diego Blas, Alexander C. Jenkins. Institutions: Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, IFAE Barcelona, King's College London. Published in Phys. Rev. D (closed access). Summary article: Detecting Gravitational Waves with the Moon by Emma Clarke (Feb 8, 2023). Key concept: Uses Earth-Moon binary system with retroreflectors to detect gravitational waves at microhertz frequencies, filling gap between pulsar timing arrays and LISA. Requires Moon distance measurements to 0.1 mm accuracy.
