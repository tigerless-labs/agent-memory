Winter Landscape Photography Camera Settings

Manual (M) Mode Settings:
- ISO: 100-400 (keep as low as possible to minimize noise)
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 (medium to small for large depth of field)
- Shutter Speed: 1/125s-1/250s
- White Balance: Cloudy or Shade

Key Techniques:
- Overexpose by +1/3 to +1 stop to capture snow details
- Use graduated ND filter to balance sky and land exposure
- Shoot in RAW format for post-processing flexibility
- Bracket shots for HDR merging for wide dynamic range
- Use polarizing filter to reduce snow glare and enhance colors

Best Light Conditions:
- Golden hour (before sunset) for warm, magical light
- Overcast days for soft, even lighting
- Avoid harsh midday sun

Composition Tips:
- Use wide-angle lens (10-24mm) for vastness
- Shoot from low angles for texture and intimacy
- Look for leading lines, patterns, and textures
- Capture details like snowflakes (1/1000s shutter, f/2.8 aperture) and animal tracks
- Consider using macro lens for close-up snow texture

Gear Considerations:
- Bring extra batteries (cold drains them quickly)
- Use weather-sealed camera or waterproof housing
- Backup power and warm storage for batteries
