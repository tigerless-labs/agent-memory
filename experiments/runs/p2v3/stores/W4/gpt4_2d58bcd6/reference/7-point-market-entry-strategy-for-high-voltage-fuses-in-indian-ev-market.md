Recommended entry strategy emphasizes: (1) Market Research on Indian EV needs and price sensitivity; (2) Adaptation to Indian regulatory standards and certifications; (3) Localized production or partnership for cost competitiveness; (4) Competitive pricing aligned with market sensitivity; (5) Robust distribution network across diverse regions; (6) Targeted marketing via digital channels and industry events; (7) After-sales service and local technician support. Key differences vs other markets: Indian market is price-sensitive, infrastructure varies by region, specific regulatory standards required, needs robust geographic distribution.
