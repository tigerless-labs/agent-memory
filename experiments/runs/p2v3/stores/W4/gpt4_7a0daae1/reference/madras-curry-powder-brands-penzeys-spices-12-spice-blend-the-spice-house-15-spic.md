Recommended brands for Madras curry powder:

Penzeys Spices: 12-spice blend including turmeric, coriander, cumin, cinnamon, cardamom. Popular with Indian cuisine enthusiasts.

The Spice House: 15-spice blend with unique additions like fenugreek and fennel for more comprehensive flavor.

McCormick: Most accessible and affordable option, still provides good balance of spices.

These were recommended for making Madras curry sauce from scratch.
