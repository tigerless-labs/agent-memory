Madras Curry Sauce Recipe from Scratch

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp curry leaves (optional)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp Madras curry powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup coconut milk (optional, for creaminess)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions: Heat oil, sauté onions until translucent (5 min). Add garlic, ginger, and spices, cook 1-2 min until fragrant. Add tomatoes, broth, and coconut milk. Simmer 10-15 min until sauce thickens. Add cooked tofu and simmer 5-7 min more. Garnish with cilantro. Serve over rice or with naan.

Tips: Can add potatoes/carrots, substitute coconut milk with yogurt or cashew cream, add lemon juice for brightness, experiment with different proteins.
