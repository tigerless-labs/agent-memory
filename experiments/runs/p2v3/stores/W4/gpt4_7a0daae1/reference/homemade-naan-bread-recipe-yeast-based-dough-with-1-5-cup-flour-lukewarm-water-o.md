Homemade Naan Bread Recipe (Yeast-Based)

Ingredients:
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp active dry yeast
- 1/2 cup lukewarm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup yogurt (optional, for tender crumb)

Instructions:
1. Combine flour, salt, sugar, and yeast
2. Gradually add water and mix until shaggy dough forms
3. Add olive oil and mix until dough comes together
4. Knead 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. Place in greased bowl, cover with damp cloth, let rise 1-2 hours until doubled
6. Preheat non-stick skillet/tava over medium heat
7. Divide dough into 6-8 portions, shape into balls
8. Roll each into thin circle (6-7 inches)
9. Cook in skillet 1-2 minutes until puffed and edges curl
10. Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until golden brown
11. Brush with melted butter or ghee if desired
12. Serve hot

Tips: Use stand mixer for kneading, add garlic/cilantro/cumin seeds for flavor, use cast-iron or pizza stone for crisper crust, experiment with different flour types.
