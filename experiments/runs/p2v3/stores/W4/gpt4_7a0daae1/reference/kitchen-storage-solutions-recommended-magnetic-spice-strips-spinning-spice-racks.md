Kitchen Storage and Organization Recommendations

Spice Racks:
- Magnetic spice strips (adhesive, wall/cabinet/fridge mounted): IKEA SPICE STRIP, Amazon Magnetic Spice Strip
- Spinning/carousel racks: Cuisinart Spice Rack, OXO Good Grips Spice Rack
- Tiered vertical racks: Rubbermaid Tiered Spice Rack, YouCopia SpiceStack

Cabinet Organizers:
- Adjustable shelf dividers (customizable): The Container Store, Whitmor
- Stackable baskets (for dry goods/snacks): Seville Classics, ClosetMaid
- Pull-out cabinet organizers: Rev-A-Shelf, Knape & Vogt

Other Solutions:
- Over-the-door storage (hooks, baskets, shelves)
- Drawer organizers and dividers
- Countertop airtight containers and canisters: OXO Good Grips, Anchor Hocking

Considerations: Measure spaces first, think about item types and frequency of use, choose durable/easy-to-clean materials, read reviews.
