From 2023 research question: factors determining outcomes when employment compensation clauses are ambiguous and lead to lawsuits in Indiana.

Key principles Indiana courts apply:
- Contract interpretation: construing ambiguous language against the drafter (employer)
- Evidence of intent from negotiations, communications, prior drafts
- Parol evidence (extrinsic evidence) if contract language alone is unclear
- Course of performance: how parties actually acted under the contract
- Industry custom and usage
- Indiana wage payment statutes may impact the case depending on nature of dispute
- Indiana case law and established precedents

General principle: ambiguous compensation clauses may be interpreted in employee's favor under Indiana law.
