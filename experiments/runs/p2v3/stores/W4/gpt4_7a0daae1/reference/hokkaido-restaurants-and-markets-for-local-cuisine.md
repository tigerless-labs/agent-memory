**Markets and Food Streets:**
- Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo) — fresh seafood including crabs, sea urchins, squid
- Sapporo Ramen Yokocho — alley famous for ramen shops
- Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo) — local Hokkaido cuisine, confectionery, souvenirs

**Restaurants:**
- Daruma (Sapporo) — jingisukan (grilled lamb)
- Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) — jingisukan
- Yurigakuro (Sapporo) — Ishikari Nabe (hot pot)
- Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru) — Ishikari Nabe

**Transportation in Hokkaido:**
- Car rental (excellent road network)
- JR Hokkaido trains (connects major cities)
- Bus network (connects major tourist spots)
- Bicycle (scenic routes in rural areas)
- Guided tours (include food tastings and transportation)
