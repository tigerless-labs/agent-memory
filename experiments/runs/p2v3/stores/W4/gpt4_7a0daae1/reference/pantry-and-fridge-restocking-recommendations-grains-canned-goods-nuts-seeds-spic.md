Pantry and Fridge Restocking Recommendations

Pantry Essentials:
- Grains: brown rice, quinoa, whole wheat pasta, oats
- Canned goods: black beans, chickpeas, diced tomatoes, tuna, coconut milk
- Nuts and seeds: almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds
- Spices and herbs: turmeric, cumin, basil, oregano, cayenne pepper
- Oils and vinegars: olive oil, avocado oil, balsamic vinegar, apple cider vinegar

Fridge Essentials:
- Eggs (protein, versatile)
- Greek yogurt (protein, calcium, good for smoothies/snacks)
- Leafy greens: spinach, kale, collard greens (salads, smoothies, sautéed)
- Protein sources: chicken breast, salmon fillets, tofu (plant-based)
- Fresh fruit: apples, bananas, berries (snacking, oatmeal)

Other:
- Beverages: water, unsweetened tea, coffee
- Snacks: nuts, dried fruits, energy balls (oats + nuts)

Note: Check existing pantry/fridge before shopping to avoid duplicates and reduce waste.
