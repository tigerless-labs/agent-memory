User has had back issues in the past. When selecting bikes or seating for activities, needs:\n\n- Comfortable, cushioned seats with gel inserts or extra padding\n- Upright handlebars (not drop bars) to reduce strain on back and neck\n- Suspension systems to absorb bumps and vibrations\n- Wider tires for smoother ride\n- May benefit from back support devices on longer activities\n\nEspecially important for multi-hour activities like bike tours.
