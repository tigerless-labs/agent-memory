Prefers to start with lighter weights and progress gradually. Focuses on proper form and technique to avoid injury. Incorporates exercises targeting multiple muscle groups (squats, deadlifts, lunges). Allows 48-72 hours recovery between strength training sessions. Balances strength training with cardio (running, tennis) and flexibility exercises (stretching, yoga).
