Working on multiple tennis skills systematically:

**Serve technique**: continental grip, proper stance (feet shoulder-width apart, turned to right), toss 6-8 feet high, contact at peak of toss, follow-through, body rotation for power. Experimenting with flat serve, topspin serve, and slice serve variations.

**Flexibility & injury prevention**: regular stretching routine after Saturday lessons — neck, shoulders, chest, calf, hamstring, quad, hip flexor, forearm, rotator cuff, glute stretches.

**Endurance & stamina**: incorporating running and cycling to improve cardiovascular fitness.

**Plyometric exercises**: box jumps, depth jumps, lateral bounds, tuck jumps, medicine ball throws — aiming for 3 sets of 10-15 reps, 2-3 times per week.

**Net play & volleys**: strategy to come to net more often, using volleys to take advantage of opponent's weaker returns. Reading opponent's return patterns to identify opportunities.
