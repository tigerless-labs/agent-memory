User is actively sourcing suppliers for their products and has been in contact with multiple companies. They are implementing a comprehensive supplier management process that includes:

1. **Supplier Contracts** - Using a contract template covering scope, pricing, payment terms, delivery, quality standards, IP protection, and dispute resolution
2. **Price Negotiation** - Researching market rates, building supplier relationships, asking for competitive bids, and leveraging competition for better pricing
3. **Supplier Evaluation** - Assessing quality, reliability, pricing, customer service, financial stability, supply chain visibility, certifications, and innovation
4. **Supplier Onboarding** - Establishing profiles, communication channels, contracts, training, quality checks, payment terms, and KPI monitoring
5. **Quality Control** - Defining quality standards, identifying critical control points, implementing regular inspections/testing, establishing corrective action processes, and monitoring performance

Key areas of focus: ensuring quality standards are met, getting competitive pricing, building reliable supplier partnerships, and establishing clear communication and expectations.
