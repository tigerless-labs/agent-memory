**Low-Maintenance Plants for Bright Indirect Light:**
- Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata) — air-purifying, tolerates low light, infrequent watering
- ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia) — tolerates variable light, infrequent watering
- Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum) — easy care, white flowers, easily propagated
- Pothos (Epipremnum aureum) — versatile light tolerance, air-purifying, can climb/trail
- Succulents (Aloe, Echeveria, Crassula) — extremely low-maintenance, sparse watering
- Dracaena spp. — variable light tolerance, air-purifying, can grow tall
- Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum wallisii) — elegant white blooms, moderate watering
- Bromeliads (Aechmea, Guzmania, Nidularium) — flowers, bright indirect light

**Plant Care Tips:**
- Water when soil feels dry to touch; avoid overwatering
- Fertilize sparingly
- Prune regularly to maintain shape
- Monitor temperature and humidity

**Design Principles for Small Spaces:**
- Use proportional sizing: mirror 1/3-2/3 width of desk surface, rug 3-4 ft diameter, mirror 12-18 in diameter
- Circular shapes (rug, mirror) create visual cohesion
- Neutral colors avoid visual clutter and blend with existing decor
- Frames: select style matching room aesthetic (minimal for modern, ornate for traditional)
- Hang mirror 57-60 inches from floor for comfortable viewing
- Mix materials and textures for warmth and interest
- Use storage solutions (drawers, shelves) to maintain organization
- Task lighting enhances workspace and coziness
- Measure carefully before purchasing to ensure proportional fit
