## Programs for mental health stigma reduction and care access

- **HeadsUp** (Australia, launched 2016): Awareness campaign by Australian Government aimed at increasing mental health literacy and reducing stigma; facilitates access to resources, tools, and advice for mentally healthy workplaces.

- **NAMI** (National Alliance on Mental Illness, United States): One of the largest grassroots mental health organizations in the US; provides awareness, education, and resources to individuals, families, and communities affected by mental illness.

- **ReachOut** (Ireland): Online support service for ages 16-25; provides information, advice, and tools to help young people manage mental health.

- **Bell Let's Talk** (Canada): Multi-year program raising awareness and combating stigma surrounding mental illness; supports major organizations in developing innovative programs, services, and treatments.

- **Dealing with Depression** (New Zealand): Government program combining public awareness campaigns with online resources and face-to-face counseling to reduce depression rates and stigma.
