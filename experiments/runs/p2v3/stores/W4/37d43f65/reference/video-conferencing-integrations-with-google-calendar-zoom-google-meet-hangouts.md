Tools and integrations explored for adding video conferencing to Google Calendar gaming sessions:

**Google Meet/Hangouts:**
- Native integration with Google Calendar
- Add directly from Calendar's 'Add conferencing' dropdown
- One-click join from calendar event
- Built into Google's ecosystem

**Zoom:**
- Cloud-based video conferencing platform
- Zoom add-on available for Google Calendar from G Suite Marketplace
- One-click meeting creation from Calendar
- Automatic meeting invitations sent to attendees with Zoom link and details
- Recording feature available
- Features include: virtual backgrounds, breakout rooms, polls
- Integrates with multiple calendar platforms (Outlook, iCal, Google Calendar)
- Customizable meeting settings for audio/video

**Alternative approach:**
- Can manually add any video conferencing link (including Discord, Skype) to Calendar events via 'Add conferencing link' option
