The House of Lords can appoint non-UK citizens as life peers, provided they are British citizens of Commonwealth nations or Ireland and have permanent residence in the UK.

Examples of non-UK-born life peers mentioned:
- Baroness Boothroyd: Speaker of House of Commons (1992-2000), appointed to House of Lords in 2000
- Baroness Hayman: Born in Canada, first elected Speaker of House of Commons, appointed to House of Lords after 2000
- Lord Patten of Barnes: Born in Sudan, last British Governor of Hong Kong, appointed as life peer in 2005
- Baroness Manningham-Buller: Director General of MI5 (2002-2007), appointed to House of Lords in 2008

The House of Lords has become increasingly diverse in recent years.
