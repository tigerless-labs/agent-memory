Jesse Owens won four Olympic gold medals at the 1936 Berlin Olympics—a historic achievement that challenged Nazi ideology.

**Key facts:**
- First African-American to win Olympic gold in 200m sprint (1936)
- Won four gold medals total at a single Olympic Games
- Competed in Berlin, where Hitler intended to showcase Aryan supremacy
- Proved athletic superiority despite facing severe racism and discrimination

**Personal factors that enabled his success:**
- Strong mental toughness and resilience
- Unwavering commitment to his sport
- Support system: family, friends, community, coach
- Devout Christian faith provided strength during adversity
- Refused to let external factors compromise his focus and determination

**Historical significance:**
- Became an icon of civil rights and symbol against racism
- Paved the way for future generations of athletes
- Demonstrated that hard work and dedication can overcome significant barriers
