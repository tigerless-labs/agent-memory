## Mental health care access statistics

**Prevalence:**
- 1 in 4 people worldwide affected by mental or neurological disorders in their lifetime
- 300+ million with depression globally
- 264 million with anxiety disorders globally
- 270 million with substance use disorders globally
- 20 million with schizophrenia globally
- 45 million with bipolar disorder globally

**Treatment gaps:**
- 75% of people with severe mental disorders in developing countries receive no treatment
- 90% in low-income countries with mental health disorders receive no treatment
- 76% in middle- and high-income countries receive no treatment due to lack of resources or accessibility

**Resource disparities:**
- Sub-Saharan Africa: 1 mental health professional per 100,000 people
- High-income countries: 70 professionals per 100,000 people
- Government expenditure on mental health: less than 2% in low-income countries; less than 6% in middle-income countries

**Stigma barriers:**
- Over 50% of Australians with mental health issues don't seek treatment due to fear of stigma, discrimination, or confidentiality concerns
