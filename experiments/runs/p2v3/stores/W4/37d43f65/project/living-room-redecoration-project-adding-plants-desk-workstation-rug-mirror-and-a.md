User recently tidied their living room and is planning a phased redecoration. Room features big windows with bright indirect light.

**Components being considered:**
1. Low-maintenance houseplants suited to bright indirect light
2. Small compact desk/console table (wall-mounted, narrow, drop-leaf, or foldable) near window as workstation/reading nook
3. Small circular rug (3-4 ft diameter) under desk to define space and add warmth  
4. Small circular mirror (12-18 in diameter) above desk for visual interest and sense of openness
5. Artwork or prints above desk for personality and visual interest

**Design goals:** Maintain organized, clutter-free aesthetic established after recent tidying, while adding warmth, texture, visual interest, and functional workspace.

**Key constraints:** Compact space, must not add clutter, needs to work with existing bright window lighting.
