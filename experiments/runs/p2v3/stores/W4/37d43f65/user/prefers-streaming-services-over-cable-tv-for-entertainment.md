User strongly prefers streaming services (Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu) over traditional cable TV for the following reasons:

- **No advertisements**: enjoys uninterrupted viewing without commercials
- **Binge-watch model**: appreciates full seasons released at once rather than weekly episode waits
- **Resume capability**: likes picking up shows where they left off, even after days away
- **Large catalog**: values access to wide variety of content, including international shows and missed episodes
- **Mobile access**: enjoys watching on phones/tablets while on the go
- **Convenience**: prefers flexible viewing at own pace on own devices without contracts

As of 2023-05-20, had not watched cable TV in months. Views streaming as significantly better user experience than traditional television.
