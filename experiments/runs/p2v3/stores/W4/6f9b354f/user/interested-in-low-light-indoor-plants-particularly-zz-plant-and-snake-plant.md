Researching low-light plants for home decor. Particularly interested in ZZ Plant and Snake Plant for low-light conditions. Recently added a vase with fresh greenery to kitchen countertop.

Key information:
- ZZ Plant watering: every 1-2 weeks in summer, every 4-6 weeks in winter; let soil dry between waterings
- ZZ Plant fertilization: balanced 20-20-20 fertilizer diluted to half strength once monthly during growing season (spring/summer only)
- Snake Plant: low-maintenance, can survive in low light, go weeks without watering
- Both suitable for air purification and low-light kitchen/bedroom settings
