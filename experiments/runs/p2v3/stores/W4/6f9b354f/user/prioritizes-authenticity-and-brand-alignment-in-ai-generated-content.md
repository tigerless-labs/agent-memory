Key concerns and preferences regarding AI content creation:\n\nConcerns:\n- Quality and authenticity of AI-generated content\n- Content sounding robotic rather than authentic to brand voice\n\nPreferred approach:\n- Train AI models on own existing content (blog posts, social media, website copy)\n- Always review and curate AI output before publishing\n- Use AI as starting point, then edit and refine with personal voice\n- Mix AI-generated content with human-created content\n- Consider disclosing when content is AI-generated for transparency\n- Use AI for research/inspiration rather than direct content generation when concerned about authenticity\n\nPhilosophy: AI should augment creative process, not replace human judgment and creativity.
