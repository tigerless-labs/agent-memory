Getting back on track with fitness after several months of dealing with health issues. Focus on gentle exercises to improve flexibility without overexertion.\n\nGentile exercises recommended:\n- Neck stretches\n- Shoulder rolls\n- Chest stretch\n- Quad stretch\n- Hamstring stretch\n- Hip flexor stretch\n- Calf stretch\n- Cat-cow stretch\n\nApproach: warm-up with light cardio and dynamic stretching, followed by flexibility exercises, then cool-down with static stretching and deep breathing.
