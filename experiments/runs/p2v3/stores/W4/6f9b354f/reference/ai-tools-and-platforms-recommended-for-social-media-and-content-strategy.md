Tools recommended for tech industry social media strategy (2023):\n\nContent Creation:\n- WordLift, Content Blossom, Snoring Cat (AI-generated content)\n- Adobe Fresco, Prisma, Deep Dream Generator (visual content)\n\nAnalytics and Research:\n- Hootsuite Insights, Union Metrics (predictive analytics)\n- BuzzSumo (trending topics, content performance, influencer identification)\n- Ahrefs (content explorer, keyword research, competitor analysis)\n- SEMrush (topic research, keyword analysis)\n- Google Keyword Planner, Moz Keyword Explorer, Ubersuggest (keyword research)\n\nEngagement:\n- ManyChat, Dialogflow, Microsoft Bot Framework (chatbots)\n- Brandwatch, NetBase (sentiment analysis)\n- AgilOne, Sailthru (personalization)\n\nSocial Listening:\n- Sprout Social, Brand24, Talkwalker (social listening)\n- AspireIQ, Upfluence, HYPR (influencer identification)\n\nContent Optimization:\n- Acrolinx, ContentStudio, Conductor\n\nOther:\n- Answer Engine (AI Q&A research)\n- Quora (community Q&A)\n- Google Trends (search trend analysis)
