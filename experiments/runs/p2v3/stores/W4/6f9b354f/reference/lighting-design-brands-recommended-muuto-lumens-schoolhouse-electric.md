Recommended modern lighting fixture brands with minimalist aesthetic:

1. **Muuto** - Known for Scandinavian-inspired designs, offers modern pendant lights
2. **Lumens** - Wide selection of pendant lights in various styles (industrial-chic, minimalist)
3. **Schoolhouse Electric** - Modern design with industrial flair, offers pendant lights

When choosing pendant lights, consider:
- Size relative to space and desk
- Geometric shapes (sphere, cylinder, rectangle) for modern look
- Materials (metal, glass, acrylic)
- Finish (matte or satin to avoid harsh reflections)
- Warm color temperature (2700K-3000K) for cozy atmosphere
- Dimmer compatibility
- Lumen output based on space size
