## Day 1
- **Niushou Mountain** — Serene Buddhist cultural park with pavilions and temples, refreshing winter air and peaceful atmosphere
- **Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan)** — Snow-covered landscape creating serene environment, Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Xuanwu Lake

## Day 2
- **Nanjing City Wall** — Ancient military fortifications and watchtowers take on unique winter quality, cool weather allows comfortable exploration
- **Confucius Temple Area (Fuzimiao)** — Historic district with temples, gardens, markets; peaceful and romantic atmosphere in winter

## Day 3
- **Ming Palace Ruins (Minggugong)** — Remains of Ming Dynasty imperial palace, mysterious quality in winter
- **Presidential Palace (Zongtong Fu)** — Historic building complex that served as residence of Sun Yat-sen, serene and tranquil atmosphere, explore gardens to learn modern Chinese history
