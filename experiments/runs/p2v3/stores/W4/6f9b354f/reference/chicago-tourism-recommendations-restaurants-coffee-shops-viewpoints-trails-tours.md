Chicago Restaurants: The Berghoff (17 W Adams St), The Gage (24 S Michigan Ave), Girl & the Goat (809 W Randolph St), Blackbird (619 W Randolph St), Portillo's (520 W Taylor St), The Purple Pig (500 N Michigan Ave), Au Chavel (809 W Randolph St). Coffee Shops: Intelligentsia Coffee (53 E Randolph St), Starbucks Reserve Bar (646 S Michigan Ave), La Colombe Coffee Roasters (1552 N Wells St), Peet's Coffee (444 S LaSalle St), The Coffee Studio (56 E Randolph St). Viewpoints: Willis Tower Skydeck (233 S Wacker Dr, 103rd floor), Millennium Park (201 E Randolph St), Navy Pier (600 E Grand Ave), 360 CHICAGO (875 N Michigan Ave), Chicago Riverwalk. Running Trails: Lakefront Trail (18.5 miles), Millennium Park (1.5 miles), Grant Park, Maggie Daley Park (1.5 miles). Walking Tours: Architecture Riverwalk Tour, Food Tours, Gangster Tour. Boat Tours: Chicago's First Lady Cruises, Shoreline Sightseeing, Little Lady Cruises. Museums: Art Institute of Chicago (111 S Michigan Ave), Field Museum (1400 S Lake Shore Dr), Museum of Contemporary Art (220 E Chicago Ave). Galleries: Driehaus Museum (40 E Erie St), Arts Club (201 E Ontario St), Carl Hammer Gallery (740 N Wells St).
