## Day 1
- **Niushou Mountain** — Buddhist cultural park with temples, pagodas, Buddha museum, beautiful scenery in summer
- **Qinhuai River** — Historic waterway with romantic boat rides, especially beautiful at night with illuminated buildings

## Day 2
- **Nanjing City Wall** — Ancient fortification with gates and watchtowers, great views of city and countryside
- **Purple Mountain (Zijin Shan)** — Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, beautiful gardens, hiking trails, Xuanwu Lake, Botanical Garden

## Day 3
- **Fuzimiao** — Historic district with ancient temples, shops, street vendors, local cuisine; beautiful at night with colorful lights
- **Xuanwu Lake** — Tranquil natural area with sculptures and monuments, peaceful for strolls
