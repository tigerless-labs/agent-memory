When choosing between Lisbon and Porto for a 3-day Portugal trip, the user selected Lisbon. Reason: Lisbon offers more attractions and activities to fill the time — historical sites (Belem Tower, Jerónimos Monastery), museums, vibrant nightlife in Bairro Alto, and nearby day-trip destinations (Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica beaches). Porto, while beautiful, is smaller and can be explored more quickly.
