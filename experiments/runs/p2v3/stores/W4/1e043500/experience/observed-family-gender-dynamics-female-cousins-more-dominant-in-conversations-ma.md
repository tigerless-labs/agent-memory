During family reunions, noticed that female cousins tend to dominate conversations and decision-making processes, while male cousins appear more laid-back and less assertive in group settings. This observation emerged while planning seating and activities for a 25-30 person family gathering. Uncertain whether this is due to the female-majority gender ratio (17:8) or primarily individual personality differences.
