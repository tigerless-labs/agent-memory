## Traditional Forms

1. **Batik printing** — technique using wax-resist dyeing to create traditional patterns on fabric
2. **Woodcarving** — creating traditional wooden statues, masks, and utensils
3. **Pottery** — coiling and throwing techniques to make everyday utensils and culturally significant pieces
4. **Photography** — documenting ceremonies (weddings, naming ceremonies) and everyday life to educate younger generations
5. **Painting** — depicting traditional clothing, food, dance, and historical events

## Recent Developments (noted 2023)

- Contemporary art forms emerging: installations, performance art, conceptual art
- Increased digital technology use: digital paintings and animations exploring climate change, social justice, human rights
- Pan-African collaborations with artists from other African countries
- Growing youth involvement with dedicated art programs focused on cultural preservation and social change
