The 1979 Dracula film directed by John Badham and starring Frank Langella is noted by literary scholars as the most accurate adaptation of Bram Stoker's original novel. It stays true to the characterization in the book, includes the original plot, and captures the symbolism and themes of the novel. Features a refined and seductive portrayal of Dracula that embodies the character's charm and menace.
