## Day 1: Alfama & Baixa\n- Morning: Alfama neighborhood, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, tram ride through narrow streets\n- Afternoon: Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square, Chiado streets, A Brasileira café, pastel de nata\n\n## Day 2: Sintra day trip & Belém\n- Morning: Day trip to Sintra — Pena Palace, Moorish Castle, gardens, hilltop views\n- Afternoon: Belém neighborhood, Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém\n\n## Day 3: River & Bairro Alto\n- Morning: Tagus River walk, MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology)\n- Afternoon: Tram to Bairro Alto, Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara views, traditional restaurants\n\n## Other attractions to consider\n- National Museum of Ancient Art\n- Calouste Gulbenkian Museum\n- LX Factory (trendy art/culture district)
