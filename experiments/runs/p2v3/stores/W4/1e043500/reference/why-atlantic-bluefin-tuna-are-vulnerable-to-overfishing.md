1. **Slow growth rate**: Takes long time to reach reproductive age; population recovery after decline is slow
2. **High commercial value**: Highly prized in sushi market; high demand pressures the fishery
3. **Long-distance migrations**: Cross multiple jurisdictions during spawn, feed, and migration; subject to different regulations and enforcement levels
4. **Non-selective fishing methods**: Longline, purse seine, and gill nets cause high bycatch including juvenile tuna, accelerating population decline
5. **Unregulated and illegal fishing**: Particularly prevalent in Mediterranean Sea; lack of enforcement compounds the problem

These combined factors make effective conservation measures essential, including improved fishing regulations, monitoring and enforcement, and consumer awareness.
