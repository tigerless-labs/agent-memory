## Life cycle and biology
- Adult Atlantic Bluefin tuna grow up to 10 feet long, weighing over 1,000 pounds
- Lifespan: typically 25–30 years, can reach up to 40 years
- Spawn in the Gulf of Mexico between April and June
- Eggs hatch into transparent larvae, carried by Gulf Stream to eastern Atlantic
- Juvenile tuna feed on plankton and small fish, found in Mediterranean and Atlantic
- Adults migrate long distances to feed and spawn, crossing the Atlantic from west to east

## Conservation status and habitat needs
- Listed as endangered species (as of 2023)
- Critical habitats: spawning grounds (Gulf of Mexico), migration routes, feeding areas
- Conservation focus: reducing commercial fishing, protecting spawning and juvenile habitats, reducing bycatch, restricting fishing methods
