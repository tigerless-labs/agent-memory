While aquaculture appears to be a solution to overfishing, significant barriers exist:

1. **Complex life cycle**: Reproductive and early life stages difficult to replicate in captivity; breeding in captivity is challenging and requires large, expensive facilities

2. **High cost**: Operations require large facilities, high-quality feeds, and maintenance; production cost far exceeds wild-caught tuna

3. **Environmental impact**: Discharge of waste and nutrients; feed requirements often sourced from wild-caught fish, perpetuating overfishing of other species

4. **Genetic diversity loss**: Captive breeding reduces genetic diversity, weakening species resilience to environmental changes

5. **Consumer preference**: Many consumers prefer wild-caught over farmed Bluefin tuna; difficult to shift market preferences

**Conclusion**: Not a straightforward or practical near-term solution; requires significant investment and research to overcome breeding and captive growth challenges.
