**Research Question:** How social media usage patterns are associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults.

**Research Interests:**
- Mediating roles of self-esteem and social support in the relationship between social media usage and mental health outcomes
- Effectiveness of social media-based interventions and policies aimed at promoting mental health
- Use of social media for mental health awareness campaigns, online support groups, and peer-to-peer support initiatives

**Status:** Actively researching and developing literature review.
