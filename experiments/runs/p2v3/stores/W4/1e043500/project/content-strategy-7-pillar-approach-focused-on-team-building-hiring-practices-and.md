## Content Strategy for Human-First Tech Hiring Brand

### Strategic approach:
1. Build thought leadership through education-focused content
2. Establish authority via expert interviews and case studies
3. Create actionable guides for hiring managers and engineering leaders

### Content pillars:

**1. Blog posts**
- Why hiring humans first matters in tech
- How to identify key traits in great candidates
- Building collaborative, communication-focused teams
- Case studies of successful human-centered hiring

**2. Podcasts/Videos**
- Interview successful team leaders about their hiring philosophy
- Expert discussions on psychometrics in tech hiring
- Stories of teams that prioritized EQ and saw results

**3. Webinars/Workshops**
- Practical training on conducting psychometric assessments
- How to evaluate soft skills and cultural fit
- Building cultures that value collaboration and communication
- In-person and online formats

**4. E-books/Guides**
- Deep-dive guide on psychometric testing for tech hiring
- How to evaluate emotional intelligence in candidates
- Building diverse, high-performing engineering teams
- Framework for shifting from IQ-first to EQ-inclusive hiring

**5. Social Media**
- Share content and brand messaging
- Highlight successful case studies and team profiles
- Engage with audience around team building challenges
- Build community of like-minded leaders

**6. Thought Leadership**
- Collaborate with industry experts
- Guest posts and contributions
- Speaking engagements at tech conferences
- Build network of aligned professionals

**7. Measurement & Iteration**
- Track engagement metrics on all content
- Use analytics to understand what resonates
- Adjust strategy based on audience feedback
- Continuously refine messaging and positioning
