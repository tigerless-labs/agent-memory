## Core Brand Ethos

Founding principle: Prioritize hiring great human beings first, then great technologists second.

### Key differentiators:
- Use psychometric tests to identify critical thinkers, not just coders
- Value EQ (emotional intelligence) more than IQ when hiring software developers
- Focus on collaborative, communication-first team building
- Challenge tech industry's over-emphasis on pure technical skills

### Brand positioning:
Thought leadership in building effective engineering teams through human-centered hiring and team development practices.

### Target audience:
- Engineering leaders and managers
- Tech company founders and C-suite
- HR professionals in tech
- People interested in building better engineering teams
