Leather Shoe Cleaning Kits: Saphir Medaille d'Or (premium, leather-specific), Kiwi Shoe Care (well-known, range of types), Lexol (popular for conditioning).

Waterproofing Sprays: Scotchgard (various materials), Crepe Protect (suede/nubuck), Nikwax (water-based, canvas/suede).

General Shoe Care: Jason Markk Shoe Cleaner (eco-friendly, versatile).

Key tips: Match product to shoe material, test on inconspicuous area first, follow manufacturer instructions, cedar shoe trees prevent creasing and maintain shape.
