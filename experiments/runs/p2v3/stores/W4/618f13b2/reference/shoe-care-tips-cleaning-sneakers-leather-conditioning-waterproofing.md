### Cleaning White Sneakers (Adidas Superstars)\n- Use soft cloth, mild soap solution (1 tbsp soap + 1 cup warm water)\n- Gently wipe surface, focus on scuff marks with light pressure\n- Rinse with clean water, air dry at room temperature\n- Avoid harsh chemicals, heat sources, direct sunlight\n- Optional: sneaker cleaners like Sneaker Cleaner or Jason Markk\n- Consider fabric protector/waterproofing spray for prevention\n\n### Leather Boot Care\n- Condition regularly with quality leather conditioner or oil\n- Apply waterproofing spray/cream\n- Clean with soft dry cloth, avoid harsh chemicals\n- Store in cool dry place with shoe trees or paper stuffing\n- Avoid extreme temperatures\n- Use leather protector on high-wear areas (toes, heels)\n- Don't over-condition (can leave greasy residue)\n- Professional maintenance: polishing and conditioning\n\n### Canvas Sneakers (Converse)\n- Use soft-bristled brush to remove dirt\n- Mild soap and water (diluted dish/hand soap)\n- Remove tough stains with baking soda solution\n- Condition rubber toe cap with rubber conditioner\n- Waterproofing spray for protection\n- Air dry naturally, avoid heat\n- Clean laces separately
