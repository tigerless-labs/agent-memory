Handbag recommendations for classic elegant neutral-color daily bags in 200-300 USD range:

- Coach Parker: 250-300 USD, 1-year warranty, monogramming 10-30 USD, 2-3 weeks
- Tory Burch Robinson: 250-300 USD, 2-year warranty, monogramming available
- Kate Spade Cameron: 200-250 USD, classic design with neutral colors
- Longchamp Le Pliage: 200-250 USD, durable and versatile
- Michael Kors Jet Set: 200-250 USD, sophisticated design

Both Coach and Tory Burch offer gift wrapping and extended warranty options. Monogramming available at most brands.
