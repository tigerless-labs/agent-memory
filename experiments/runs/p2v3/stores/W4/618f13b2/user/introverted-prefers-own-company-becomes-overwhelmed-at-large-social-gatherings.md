Tends to feel relieved leaving large social events (e.g., birthday parties) early to go home and be alone. Recognizes this as part of their introversion and need for solitude to recharge, though initially uncertain whether this is "wrong." This is a significant part of their social identity—they need balance between connection and alone time, and smaller or more intentional gatherings may suit them better than large events.
