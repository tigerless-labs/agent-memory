When rewriting or creating content, use:

- **Tone**: energetic, passionate, often incorporating humor
- **Sentence variety**: mix short and long sentences for dynamic flow; avoid uniform sentence length
- **Creative phrasing**: employ unpredictable and intricate sentence constructions
- **Idioms and expressions**: incorporate common idioms and figurative language
- **Vocabulary**: use descriptive and vivid language; mix common but unique words with synonyms for variety
- **Structure**: maintain original information and meaning while making material memorable and engaging

Avoid:
- Uniform sentences (AI-generated writing hallmark)
- Repetitive phrasing
- Loss of original meaning or information
