**Organization & Planning:**
- Create dedicated course calendar marking assignment due dates and quiz deadlines
- Develop a note-taking system (notebook, digital notes, or mind mapping)
- Keep a glossary of ML terms and concepts
- Create programming language cheatsheets for quick reference

**Time Management:**
- Create and stick to a dedicated study schedule
- Break large tasks into smaller, manageable chunks
- Prioritize the most important/challenging tasks for peak alertness
- Take regular breaks to avoid burnout
- Don't fall behind—stay current with coursework
- Allocate specific times for coursework vs. personal projects

**Personal Projects & Practical Application:**
- Start with small, manageable projects that don't require large time commitment
- Choose projects aligned with course topics to directly apply what's being learned
- Example project ideas: image classification, text analysis, recommendation systems, time series forecasting
- Use projects to build portfolio demonstrating skills
- Don't let personal projects compromise coursework

**Community & Support:**
- Join a study group or find a study buddy
- Use course discussion forums actively
- Ask for help when stuck (instructors, TAs, fellow students)
- Follow ML blogs and podcasts for industry trends

**Motivation & Well-being:**
- Celebrate milestones and progress along the way
- Take care of physical and mental health (exercise, sleep, nutrition)
- Reflect regularly on what's working and adjust approach as needed
- Keep perspective: learning ML is a continuous process
