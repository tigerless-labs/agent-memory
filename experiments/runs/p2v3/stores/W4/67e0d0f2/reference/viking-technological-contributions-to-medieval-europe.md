Key Viking technology transfers that spread across Europe:

**Shipbuilding**: Clinker-built ships were stronger and more flexible than traditional European vessels, adopted widely.

**Metalworking**: Vikings used bog iron from Scandinavia to create strong swords and axes; technology spread and influenced medieval weapon development.

**Woodworking**: Intricate Viking designs influenced craftspeople in Scotland, British Isles, and Normandy.

**Hygiene**: Vikings' emphasis on cleanliness and personal hygiene (combs, razors, tweezers) improved standards across Europe, which was uncommon at the time.

The Viking Age (793-1066 CE) impact extended to disruption of trade, formation of Viking kingdoms in England/Ireland/Normandy, centralization of defensive power, and influence on feudalism.
