## Recommended Programs

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** - https://afs.org/
   - Large non-profit offering student exchanges, gap year programs, internships, volunteering
   - Programs available in 50+ countries

2. **Experiment in International Living** - https://www.experiment.org/
   - Nonprofit offering immersive cultural exchange for high school and college students
   - Programs include living with host families, attending local schools, community service

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** - https://www.yfu.org/
   - International educational organization offering exchange programs
   - Programs in 40+ countries (year-long, semester, summer options)

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** - https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/
   - Nonprofit offering volunteering in Costa Rica, Ghana, India, Peru
   - Focus on community development and cross-cultural exchange
