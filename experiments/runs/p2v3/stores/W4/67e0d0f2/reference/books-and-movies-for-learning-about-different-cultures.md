## Books

- **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri - Bengali-American family, identity and culture themes
- **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini - Afghanistan, friendship and redemption
- **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan - Chinese-American women and their immigrant mothers
- **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe - Nigerian tribe before/after British colonization
- **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi - Graphic novel, Iranian girl during Islamic Revolution

## Movies

- **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** - Martial arts epic, ancient China, themes of loyalty/honor
- **The Lives of Others** - German film, East Berlin Cold War, surveillance and human connection
- **Lion** - True story, Indian boy lost and adopted by Australian family, journey to find birth family
- **The Motorcycle Diaries** - Travel diaries of Che Guevara, South America, exploring injustices of poverty
- **City of God** - Brazilian film, favela life, poverty/violence/survival themes
