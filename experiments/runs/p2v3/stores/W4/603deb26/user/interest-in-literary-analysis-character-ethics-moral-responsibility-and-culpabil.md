## Interest in Literary Analysis

Engages with close analysis of fictional characters' moral and legal responsibility, particularly:
- Exploring culpability and ethical complexity in character actions
- Considering how circumstance and coercion mitigate responsibility
- Writing essays analyzing character agency and consequences

Evidence: 2023-08-15 — analyzed Miss Stapleton's moral/legal responsibility in *The Hound of the Baskervilles*, weighing her active participation in crimes against her victimization by her brother's control.

This complements interest in character-driven narratives; extends to analytical/academic writing about character ethics.
