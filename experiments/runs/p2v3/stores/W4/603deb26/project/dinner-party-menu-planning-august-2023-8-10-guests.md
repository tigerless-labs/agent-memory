## Menu\n- **Appetizer:** Lemon-herb naan with spiced chickpeas or hummus\n- **Main:** Vegetarian sushi rolls (avocado, cucumber, grilled portobello mushrooms, carrot-ginger tempura bits) with lemon-ginger dipping sauce\n- **Side:** Lemon-herb quinoa with fresh herbs and pine nuts\n- **Dessert:** Lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available)\n\n## Guest accommodations\n- 8-10 guests\n- Several vegetarians\n- One gluten-free guest\n\n## Garnishes\n- Fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint) for naan and quinoa\n- Thinly sliced lemons or edible flowers (violas, pansies) for sushi\n\n## Timing tips\n- Create detailed timeline with prep/cooking/serving times\n- Prep ingredients ahead of time\n- Cook in batches to keep dishes hot\n- Serve courses 30-45 minutes apart\n- Keep atmosphere relaxed and communicate with guests
