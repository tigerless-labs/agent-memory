Lavender Gin Fizz: 1.5oz gin, 0.5oz lime juice, 0.5oz lavender syrup, 0.5oz simple syrup. Grapefruit Basil Martini: 2oz gin, 1oz grapefruit juice, 0.5oz lime juice, 0.5oz basil syrup. Honey Syrup: 1:1 honey to water, heat gently, cool, store in fridge. Spirit infusions: use cheesecloth or coffee filter for straining. Gin-based cocktails to try: Pimm's Cup, Bee's Knees, French 75, Corpse Reviver No. 2, Gin Rickey, Gin-Gin Mule, Aviation, Last Word.
