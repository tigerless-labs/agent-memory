Currently using Lightroom on desktop. Considering Lightroom Mobile for on-the-go editing. Mobile version has limited functionality compared to desktop (no Tone Curve, Split Toning, Lens Corrections) but good for quick adjustments to exposure and color.

Other mobile editing apps mentioned: VSCO, Snapseed (Google), Adobe Photoshop Express, PicsArt, Polarr, Afterlight.
