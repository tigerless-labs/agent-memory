**White Negroni Recipe:**
- 1 1/2 oz Hendrick's gin (or preferred gin)
- 1/2 oz Suze (or other dry bitter liqueur like Luxardo Bitter)
- 1/2 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth
- Orange or lemon twist for garnish

Instructions: Fill mixing glass with ice, add gin, Suze, and Dolin Dry Vermouth. Stir 20-30 seconds. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass. Garnish with twist.

**Flavor Balance Tips:**
- Use dry bitter liqueurs (Suze, Luxardo Bitter) rather than sweet ones
- Choose dry vermouth to avoid sweetness
- Adjust ratio: some prefer 2:1:1, others 3:1:1 for gin:bitter:vermouth
- Hendrick's floral/herbal notes pair well with dry, herbaceous Suze
- Stir just enough to chill/dilute without watering down
