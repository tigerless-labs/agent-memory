**Citrus fruits that pair well with gin and lavender:**

1. **Orange** (especially blood orange) - sweetness complements lavender, bitterness balances gin
2. **Yuzu** - Japanese citrus, cross between grapefruit and orange; tartness and sweetness pair beautifully
3. **Kumquat** - small, sweet citrus; works well as syrup or muddled fresh
4. **Satsuma** - mandarin orange type; sweet and tart, easy-to-peel
5. **Bergamot** - subtle citrus flavor (used in tea/perfumery); adds complexity without overpowering

**Working with citrus tips:**
- Always use fresh citrus for brightest flavor
- Experiment with different varieties to find perfect fit
- Balance citrus carefully - can quickly overpower other flavors
- Start small and adjust to taste
- Use citrus peels/twists for added aroma and depth
