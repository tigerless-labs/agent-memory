Six vegetarian sushi filling suggestions from dinner party planning:\n1. **Roasted sweet potato** - dice, roast with oil/salt/pepper\n2. **Grilled portobello mushrooms** - marinate in soy/sake/sugar, grill until charred\n3. **Carrot-ginger tempura bits** - shred carrots with ginger, coat in tempura batter, fry\n4. **Spinach and tofu** - sauté spinach, crumble tofu, season with soy/sesame oil/ginger/garlic\n5. **Pickled shiitake mushrooms** - slice thin, pickle in vinegar/sugar/soy\n6. **Crispy fried tofu** - cube extra-firm tofu, fry, season with soy and sesame oil\n\nFor this dinner party, planning to use grilled portobello mushrooms and carrot-ginger tempura bits as the substantial vegetarian options.
