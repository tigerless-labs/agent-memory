**Lavender Syrup Recipe:**
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water  
- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds

Combine sugar and water in saucepan, heat until sugar dissolves. Add lavender buds, remove from heat. Steep 10-15 minutes. Strain through fine-mesh sieve. Store in refrigerator up to 2 weeks.

**Suggested Lavender Cocktails:**
1. Lavender Gin Fizz: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz simple syrup, top with soda water
2. Lavender Dream: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup (1:1)
3. Lavender Bee's Knees: 2 oz Hendrick's gin, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz honey syrup
