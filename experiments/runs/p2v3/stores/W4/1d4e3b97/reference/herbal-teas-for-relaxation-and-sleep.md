Recommended herbal teas for evening relaxation and sleep quality:

**Top recommendations:**
- Chamomile tea — classic for nervous system relaxation, calms anxiety
- Valerian root tea — natural sedative, improves sleep quality
- Tart cherry tea — contains melatonin, regulates sleep-wake cycles
- Passionflower tea — natural sedative, reduces insomnia and anxiety
- Lemon balm tea — reduces stress and anxiety
- Lavender tea — calming properties, reduces stress
- Peppermint tea — calms mind and body
- Rooibos tea — caffeine-free, antioxidants
- GABA tea — promotes relaxation, reduces anxiety

**Tips:**
- Choose high-quality, organic brands free of added sugars and caffeine
- Brew 30-60 minutes before bedtime
- Can blend different herbs for unique effects
