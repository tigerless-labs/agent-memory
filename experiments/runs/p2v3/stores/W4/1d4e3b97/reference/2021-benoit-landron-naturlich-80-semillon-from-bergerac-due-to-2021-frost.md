Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat was made with 80% organic Semillon from Bergerac and 20% other varieties (typically Folle Blanche) because of severe frost damage in 2021. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes. Imported and served by the glass at 161 for spring.
