Recommendations from coaching session:

**Alternative relaxation techniques:**
- Reading, journaling, music/nature sounds, yoga/stretches, puzzles, board games, warm bath

**Screen-free zones:** bedroom, dinner table, living room

**Screen-free time blocks:** 30min morning, 1 hour afternoon, 2 hours evening

**Relaxation window concept:** Allocate 30-60 min to choose between Netflix or screen-free activity

**App limits:** Consider 30 mins/day for social media

**Task prioritization strategies:**
- Eisenhower Matrix (Urgent/Important quadrants)
- Break down large tasks into smaller chunks
- Use task lists and cross off completed items
- Avoid multitasking
- Set realistic deadlines (pad by 10-20%)
- Learn to say no to non-priority tasks
- Time-blocking for focused work
- Take regular breaks
- Review and adjust priorities weekly

**Tracking:** Use iOS Screen Time or Android Digital Wellbeing app
