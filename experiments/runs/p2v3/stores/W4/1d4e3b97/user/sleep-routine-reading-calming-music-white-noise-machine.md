Established bedtime routine to improve sleep quality:

- Reads for 20-30 minutes before bed
- Listens to calming music or nature sounds on phone while reading (finds rainfall, ocean waves, forest sounds, and classical/ambient music genres helpful)
- Uses white noise machine to block outside noise
- Gets ready for bed earlier than before
- Limits caffeine intake in afternoons (previously drank coffee/tea at 4-5 PM but stopped because it affected sleep)
- Takes melatonin supplement 30 minutes before bed (usually falls asleep within 15-20 minutes)

These practices have significantly improved sleep quality.
