## Requirements Converted to User Stories

Original requirement: Trainees answer survey at beginning and end of course. At course end, they can see their own progress and class-wide results.

Eight user stories generated covering trainee and instructor perspectives:

Trainee stories:
- Access survey at course start for initial feedback
- Access same survey at course end for final feedback
- View own progress to track improvement and identify development areas
- Compare end-of-course with initial results to track attitude/opinion changes
- View class survey results to compare progress with peers and see group performance

Instructor stories:
- Collect pre/post survey responses to track progress and evaluate training effectiveness
- View individual trainee results to provide personalized feedback
- View class-wide results to evaluate overall effectiveness and identify improvements

This covers pre-course baseline, post-course measurement, individual progress tracking, and peer comparison.
