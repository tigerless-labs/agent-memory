For the bathroom renovation shower: **Glazed porcelain tile** chosen for both walls and floor. Reasons: glossy finish aesthetic, added protection against scratches and stains, easy to clean and maintain.\n\n**Cement-based grout** selected for the project. Rationale: all-purpose option, relatively affordable, suitable for glazed porcelain tile installations.
