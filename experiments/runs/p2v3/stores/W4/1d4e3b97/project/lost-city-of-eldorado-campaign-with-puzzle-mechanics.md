Campaign: Lost City of Eldorado game with treasure objective. Brainstormed puzzle types: (1) Mirror Puzzle - symbols on mirrored surface to reveal passage; (2) Elemental Puzzle - activate levers/buttons for earth/fire/air/water to progress; (3) Musical Puzzle - play instruments in correct sequence from sheet music; (4) Weight Puzzle - arrange weights on scales; (5) Memory Puzzle - memorize object locations before darkness, retrieve specific item.
