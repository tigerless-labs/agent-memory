Basic Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe:

Ingredients:
- 1/2 gallon milk (whole, low-fat, nonfat, or plant-based)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures or vegan yogurt starter
- Optional: sweetener, flavorings, probiotic powder

Instructions:
1. Heat milk on low for 2-3 hours to 180-185F (82-85C) to kill bacteria and denature proteins
2. Cool milk to 110-120F (43-49C) - takes 1-2 hours
3. Add yogurt starter and whisk gently
4. Cover slow cooker, wrap with towel/blanket, incubate 8-12 hours or overnight
5. Chill and transfer to containers

Temperature Monitoring:
- Using a thermometer ensures accurate temperature control and consistent results
- Without thermometer: heat 2-3 hours until bubbles form around edges, watch carefully for consistency
- Proper heating kills unwanted bacteria and denatures proteins for better set
- Overheating can damage proteins and affect texture/flavor

Longer incubation = thicker, tangier yogurt.
