Dry flies: Elk Hair Caddis, Stimulator, Blue Wing Olive. Nymphs: Hare's Ear Nymph, Pheasant Tail Nymph, Copper John. Streamers: Woolly Bugger, Muddler Minnow, Black Stonefly. Spinners/Spoons: Mepps Aglia, Rooster Tail, Acme Kastmaster. Soft plastics: Curly Tail Grub, PowerBait. Key brands: Simms, Orvis, Umpqua, Rio Products, Mepps, Acme. Woolly Bugger particularly effective in rivers with moderate to fast current; can be fished with sink-tip line or weighted leader. Muddler Minnow good for rocky/boulder-strewn bottoms. Vary retrieves to imitate fleeing baitfish.
