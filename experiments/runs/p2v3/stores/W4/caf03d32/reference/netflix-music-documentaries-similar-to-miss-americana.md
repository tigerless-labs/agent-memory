Recommended music documentaries on Netflix for fans of behind-the-scenes artist stories:\n\n- **Homecoming: A Film by Beyoncé** (2019) - Beyoncé's Coachella 2018 preparation and performance\n- **Quincy** (2018) - Quincy Jones biography with interviews from Frank Sinatra, Michael Jackson\n- **Travis Scott: Look Mom I Can Fly** (2019) - Travis Scott's rise to fame and Astroworld tour\n- **Gaga: Five Foot Two** (2017) - Lady Gaga's life, music, chronic pain struggles, and album 'Joanne' creation\n- **The Black Godfather** (2019) - Clarence Avant's career with interviews from Quincy Jones, Snoop Dogg\n- **The Apollo** (2019) - Apollo Theater history with performances by Aretha Franklin, James Brown\n- **The Remix: Hip Hop x Fashion** (2019) - Hip-hop and fashion intersection with Kanye West, Pharrell Williams\n- **I'll Sleep When I'm Dead** (2016) - DJ Steve Aoki's life and tours\n- **What Happened, Miss Simone?** (2015) - Nina Simone biography\n- **The Art of Rap** (2012) - Hip-hop history and evolution with Ice-T, Dr. Dre, Eminem
