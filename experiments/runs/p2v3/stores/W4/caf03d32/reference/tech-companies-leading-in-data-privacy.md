**Apple**: iMessage/FaceTime end-to-end encryption, Safari privacy protections

**Microsoft**: Privacy controls, two-factor authentication, Windows Hello biometric auth

**Google**: Limits third-party app/service data access, privacy controls across products, Privacy Checkup feature

**Mozilla (Firefox)**: Privacy-first browser with built-in protections, default cookie blocking

**ProtonMail**: End-to-end encryption, zero-access encryption for email
