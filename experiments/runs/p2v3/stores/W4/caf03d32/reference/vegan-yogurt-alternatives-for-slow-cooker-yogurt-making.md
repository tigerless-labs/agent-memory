Options for plant-based yogurt bases:

1. Soy Yogurt - classic option from brands like Silk or So Delicious
2. Coconut Yogurt - creamy, rich from brands like So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo
3. Almond Yogurt - low-calorie option from Almond Breeze, Silk, So Delicious
4. Cashew Yogurt - creamy and indulgent, can be made from scratch or brands like So Delicious
5. Oat Yogurt - good for nut allergies, from Oatly or So Delicious
6. Rice Yogurt - hypoallergenic from Rice Dream or So Delicious
7. Hemp Yogurt - high protein from Good Karma or Elmhurst 1925
8. Flax Yogurt - low-calorie, high-fiber from Good Karma or homemade

Always choose unflavored, unsweetened varieties to avoid extra sugars.
