Recommended channels for learning guitar as a beginner:\n\n- **Justin Guitar** (Justin Sandercoe) - Comprehensive beginner's course covering basics to advanced techniques\n- **Marty Music** (Marty Schwartz) - Great for learning popular songs quickly with simple, easy-to-follow lessons\n- **GuitarLessons365** (Bill) - Comprehensive beginner's course focusing on acoustic guitar\n- **GuitarJamz** (Martyn) - Fun lessons on popular songs and riffs\n- **Andy Guitar** (Andy Crowley) - Engaging lessons for electric guitar basics to advanced techniques\n\nTips: Start with acoustic guitar, practice regularly (even 10-15 minutes daily), use a metronome, find a practice buddy, and be patient with the learning process.
