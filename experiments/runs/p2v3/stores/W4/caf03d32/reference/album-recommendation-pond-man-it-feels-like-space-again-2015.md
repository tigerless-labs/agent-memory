Recommended starting point for psychedelic rock with mellow and atmospheric sound. Album features a blend of psychedelic rock, indie, and electronic elements with strong focus on melody and atmosphere. Standout tracks include 'Waiting Around for Grace', 'Hand Mouth Dancer', and 'Sweep Me Off My Feet'. Also recommended song '30000 MEGATONS' as a fan favorite.
