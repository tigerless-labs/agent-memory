Soaking Cashews:
1. Rinse 1 cup of cashews
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight
3. Drain and rinse
4. Optionally soak in fresh water for another 4 hours

Blending:
1. Add soaked, drained cashews to blender with 1/2 cup fresh water
2. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
3. Adjust ratio to preference: less water for thicker consistency, more for thinner

Straining Options:
- Cheesecloth: high filtration but can be messy, squeeze to extract liquid
- Nut milk bag: designed for plant milks, effective but potentially pricey
- Fine-mesh sieve: easy to use and clean but may not catch all particles

Straining Tips:
- Use clean, sanitized straining tool
- Squeeze through the tool to extract maximum liquid
- With cheesecloth, twist and squeeze to get more liquid
- With nut milk bag, squeeze gently to avoid particles through mesh

For slow cooker yogurt: use low heat, incubate 8-12 hours or overnight, use vegan yogurt starter or probiotic powder.
