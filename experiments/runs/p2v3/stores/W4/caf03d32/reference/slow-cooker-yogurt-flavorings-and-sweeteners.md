Flavorings:
- Vanilla extract or vanilla bean
- Cinnamon powder
- Fresh berries, mango, peaches
- Nutmeg powder
- Fresh ginger
- Lemon juice or zest
- Cocoa powder or melted chocolate

Sweeteners:
- Honey
- Maple syrup
- Coconut sugar
- Date syrup
- Monk fruit sweetener (150-200x sweeter than sugar)
- Stevia powder

Other add-ins:
- Granola (store-bought or homemade)
- Nuts or seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia, flax)
- Cocoa nibs
- Dried fruit (cranberries, raisins, apricots)

Experiment with combinations to create preferred flavor profiles.
