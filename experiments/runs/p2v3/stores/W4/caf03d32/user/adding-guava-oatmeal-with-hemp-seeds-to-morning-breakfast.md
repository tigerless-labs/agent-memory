Planning to incorporate guava oatmeal into morning routine with the following approach:

**Base recipe:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds

**Protein boost:**
- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds per serving (provides 5-10g protein and omega-3s)
- Optional: scoop of protein powder

**Tips for hemp seeds:**
- Grind them first for better mixing
- Mix with liquid to create a paste before adding to oatmeal
- Start with 1 tablespoon and adjust to taste
- Store in fridge/freezer to preserve freshness

Additional topping options: banana, nuts (walnuts, almonds, pecans), coconut flakes, cinnamon.
