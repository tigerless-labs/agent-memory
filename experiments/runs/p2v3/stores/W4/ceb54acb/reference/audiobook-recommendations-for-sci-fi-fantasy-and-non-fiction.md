Recommended titles across sci-fi/fantasy: The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet (Becky Chambers, narrated by Rachel Dulude), The House of Shattered Wings (Aliette de Bodard), The Golem and the Jinni (Helene Wecker), The City of Brass (S.A. Chakraborty), The Black Tides of Heaven (J.Y. Yang), The Poppy War (R.F. Kuang). Non-fiction self-improvement: The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People (Stephen Covey), Daring Greatly (Brené Brown), Mindset (Carol Dweck). Non-fiction productivity: Deep Work (Cal Newport), Essentialism (Greg McKeown), Atomic Habits (James Clear). Other non-fiction: Sapiens (Yuval Noah Harari), The Power of Now (Eckhart Tolle), Shoe Dog (Phil Knight).
