Fascinated by cinematographic symbolism—e.g., the oranges in *The Godfather* as foreshadowing death/danger. Values symbolism as a depth-adding technique (examples: *The Shining*, *Blade Runner*, *Get Out*, *The Matrix*), but dislikes overuse. Believes balance is essential: heavy-handed symbolism becomes distracting and contrived, detracting from narrative. Thoughtful, sparse use enhances emotional impact; excessive use feels pretentious.
