Decided to get all three inherited items insured. Priority order: 1) diamond necklace (likely most valuable), 2) antique music box, 3) depression-era glassware.

**Appraisal approach:**
- Search online for NAJA or ASA-certified appraisers
- Ask friends and family for referrals
- Start with one appraisal report per item; get additional appraisals only if needed
- Gather documentation: proof of ownership, provenance, repair records, existing appraisals, photos/videos

**Documentation needed for appraisal:**
- Proof of ownership (will, trust, inheritance papers)
- Provenance and historical records
- Repair/maintenance records
- Any existing appraisals or certificates
- High-quality photos and videos from multiple angles

**Factors affecting appraisal value:** diamond quality (carat, cut, color, clarity), metal type and purity, craftsmanship, condition, rarity/uniqueness, market demand, comparable sales
