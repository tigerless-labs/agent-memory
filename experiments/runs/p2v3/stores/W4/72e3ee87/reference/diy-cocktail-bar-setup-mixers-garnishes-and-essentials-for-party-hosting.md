## Mixers
- Ginger Beer (pairs with spicy Korean dishes)
- Lime Juice (complements cucumber and lime in Cucumber Gimlet)
- Grapefruit Juice (tangy contrast to spicy flavors)
- Cucumber-Lime Juice (enhances Cucumber Gimlet)
- Soy Sauce-Infused Simple Syrup (adds umami depth for Korean-inspired drinks)

## Garnishes
- Cucumber Slices (refreshing, crunchy texture)
- Lime Wedges (classic citrus)
- Mint Leaves (fragrant, refreshing)
- Kimchi (spicy, savory)
- Pickled Ginger (tangy, crunchy)
- Sesame Seeds (nutty, textured)
- Fresh Herbs: basil, cilantro, parsley

## Essential Equipment
- Ice (must-have for chilling)
- Glasses: highball, old fashioned, martini glasses
- Muddler (for muddling fruits, herbs, spices)
- Jigger (for precise measuring)
- Garnish Picks (small skewers for fruit/herb garnishes)

## Tips
- Variety of mixers allows guests to create custom cocktails
- Pre-prep garnishes in advance for easy access
- Clearly label mixers and alcohol options
