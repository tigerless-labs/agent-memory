## Korean-Inspired Sides
1. **Kimchi Coleslaw**: kimchi, shredded cabbage, carrots, soy sauce, lime juice
2. **Gochujang Roasted Vegetables**: broccoli, Brussels sprouts, or sweet potatoes with gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil
3. **Korean-Style Corn**: grilled or boiled corn with gochujang sauce (gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)
4. **Cucumber Kimchi Salad**: thinly sliced cucumbers, kimchi, red onion, cilantro, soy sauce, lime juice

## Other Sides
1. **Pickled Ginger**: thinly sliced ginger pickled in vinegar, sugar, salt
2. **Cilantro Lime Rice**: cilantro, lime juice, garlic, sesame oil
3. **Grilled Pineapple**: grilled pineapple slices with cilantro, lime juice, honey
4. **Spicy Edamame**: steamed edamame with chili flakes, soy sauce, sesame oil
5. **Spicy Pickled Carrots**: complements BBQ and Korean flavors

## Key Considerations
- Use variety of textures and temperatures
- Balance spiciness, sweetness, and umami with other dishes
- Spicy sides pair well with cooling elements like cucumber or rice
