## Summer Cocktail Recipes
- Pimm's Cup (Pimm's No. 1 with lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit)
- Watermelon Margaritas (watermelon puree, tequila, lime juice, agave)
- Summer Sangria (white wine, peaches, strawberries, citrus, sparkling water)
- Cranberry Lime Sparkler (cranberry juice, lime, vodka, sparkling water)
- Gin-Gin Mule (ginger beer, lime, gin in copper mug)
- Spicy Pineapple Collins (pineapple juice, tequila, lime, cayenne)
- Rosé Spritz (rosé, lemon-lime soda, grenadine)
- Blackberry Basil Martini (blackberry puree, vodka, lime, basil)
- Citrus-Ginger Fizz (citrus juices, ginger liqueur, sparkling water)
- Elderflower Collins (elderflower liqueur, gin, lemon, soda water)

## Fruit Garnishes for Cucumber Gimlets
- Lime wedges with mint
- Cantaloupe or honeydew melon balls
- Strawberry slices
- Kiwi slices
- Grapefruit slices
- Orange slices
- Peach slices
- Mango slices
- Grilled pineapple (proven success)

## Drinks Pairing with Korean Dishes
**For Bibimbap:**
- Soju Spritz (Soju, lime, ginger beer)
- Cucumber-Lime Refresher with ginger liqueur
- Korean Iced Tea (black tea, sugar, citrus)

**For Kimchi Tacos:**
- Spicy Mango Mule (mango puree, ginger beer, lime, chili flakes)
- Ginger-Grapefruit Collins
- Cucumber-Kiwi Cooler
