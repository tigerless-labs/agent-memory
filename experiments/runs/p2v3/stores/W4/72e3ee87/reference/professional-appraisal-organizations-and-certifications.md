**Jewelry appraisers:**
- **NAJA** (National Association of Jewelry Appraisers) - Non-profit; certifies jewelry appraisers; sets industry standards
- **AGTA** (American Gem Trade Association) - Trade association; certifies gemologists and appraisers; focuses on gemstones and jewelry
- **ASA** (American Society of Appraisers) - Multidisciplinary organization; certifies appraisers in various fields including jewelry

**Antique and collectible appraisers:**
- **AAA** (Appraisers Association of America) - Professional organization; certifies appraisers in antiques, art, collectibles
- **ISA** (International Society of Appraisers) - Non-profit; certifies appraisers in antiques, art, collectibles
- **NACA** (National Association of Collectible Appraisers) - Specializes in collectibles including antiques, toys, memorabilia

**What to look for in an appraiser:**
- Certification from reputable organization
- Experience with similar items (vintage jewelry, antique music boxes, antique glassware)
- Impartial; not affiliated with jewelry stores or dealers
- Professional affiliations; commitment to industry standards
- Recognition by insurance companies (beneficial for claims)
- Good referrals and reputation (ask friends/family; check online reviews)
