**Title**: A Short History of Nearly Everything  
**Author**: Bill Bryson  
**Genre**: Science, history overview

**Structure**: Six parts covering:
1. Origins of the Universe (Big Bang, life origins)
2. Size of the Earth (geology, plate tectonics)
3. A New Age Dawns (rise of life on Earth)
4. The Road to Us (human evolution)
5. The Stuff of Life (chemistry, physics, matter)
6. The Earth and Its Inhabitants (weather, oceans, biodiversity)

**Style**: Engaging, witty, conversational tone with anecdotes about scientists and historical examples. Complex topics made accessible.

**Awards**: Won Aventis Prize for Science Books (2004), shortlisted for Samuel Johnson Prize

**Fit**: Good for daily 30-minute reading sessions; combines science and history interests.
