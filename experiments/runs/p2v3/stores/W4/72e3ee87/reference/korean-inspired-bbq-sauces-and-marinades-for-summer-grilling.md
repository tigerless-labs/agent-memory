## Korean-Inspired BBQ Sauces
1. **Gochujang BBQ Sauce**: gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil
2. **Soy-Ginger BBQ Sauce**: soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice vinegar
3. **Korean-Style BBQ Sauce with Gochugaru**: gochugaru, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil

## Marinades
1. **Sesame-Ginger Marinade**: sesame oil, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar (chicken, beef, or pork)
2. **Korean-Style BBQ Marinade with Gochujang**: gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil
3. **Cilantro-Lime Marinade**: cilantro, lime juice, garlic, olive oil (chicken, shrimp, or vegetables)

## Key Tips
- Balance bold Korean flavors with acidity (lime or vinegar) to cut through richness
- Adjust sweetness, spiciness, and umami to taste
- Match marinade/sauce intensity to protein type (delicate fish = lighter marinade; beef/pork = bolder)
