**Artist:** Indie-electronic duo, collaboration between Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel). Released Give Up album in 2003.

**Standout Tracks:**
- "Such Great Heights" — dreamy, synth-driven anthem about new love; acoustic guitar-based main melody
- "The District Sleeps Alone Tonight" — melancholy, introspective; themes of disconnection and insomnia
- "Clark Gable" — quirky, upbeat; about wooing a romantic interest
- "We Will Become Silhouettes" — somber, reflective; about coping with nuclear apocalypse
- "Recycled Air" — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar riff; propulsive but introspective
- "Sleeping In" — vocal layering and harmonies; vocal lines weave in counterpoint to orchestral-sounding chorus

**Distinctive Elements:**
- Blended indie rock with electronic elements
- Used acoustic instruments alongside synthesizers (unusual for indie-electronic at the time)
- Vocal layering and harmonies creating rich, textured sound
- Postal-related imagery and themes throughout (mailboxes, postcards)
- Genre-blending collaboration that appealed to both indie and electronic audiences
