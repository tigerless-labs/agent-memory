**Specialty insurers:**
- **Jewelers Mutual** - Known for jewelry, vintage and antique pieces; heirloom-specific policies
- **Chubb** - High-value items including jewelry, art, collectibles
- **AXA ART** - Specializes in art, antiques, collectibles, including jewelry and rare items
- **Hagerty** - Primarily known for classic cars; also offers collectibles policies (jewelry, art, antiques)

**Standard insurers with specialty departments:**
- **State Farm** - Specialized department for high-value items including jewelry and antiques
- **Allstate** - "Valuable Items" policy covering jewelry, art, collectibles
- **Liberty Mutual** - "Valuable Possessions" policy including jewelry, art, antiques

**Policy features to look for:**
- Agreed value coverage (pays insured amount in case of loss/damage)
- No or low deductible
- Worldwide coverage (if traveling with items)
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance/loss (not just theft/damage)

**Other considerations:**
- Consider working with independent brokers specializing in high-value items
- Research each option thoroughly
- Understand policy exclusions and limitations
