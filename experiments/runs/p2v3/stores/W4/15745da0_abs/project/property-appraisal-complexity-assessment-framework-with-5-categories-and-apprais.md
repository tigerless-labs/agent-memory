A framework for objectively assessing property appraisal complexity across five dimensions: Location, Property Type and Design, Age and Condition, Zoning and Land Use, and Market Conditions. Includes guidance that appraiser assessments of complexity should not be taken at face value, as appraisers have financial incentives to justify higher complexity ratings. The team must meticulously evaluate appraiser opinions against objective criteria and the team's own analysis.
