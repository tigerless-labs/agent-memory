User is actively incorporating cognitive-supporting foods into grandmother's diet. Focus areas: omega-3s (salmon, tuna, mackerel), antioxidants (berries, spinach, kale), B vitamins (leafy greens, nuts), moderate caffeine. Seeking to make healthy foods more appealing to grandmother and find easy-to-prepare nutritious snacks. Plans to involve grandmother in meal planning and adjust food texture for easier eating.
