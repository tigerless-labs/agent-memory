Values ethical approaches to true crime storytelling that:
- Center victim experiences and dignity over sensationalism
- Focus on justice and prevention rather than glorifying perpetrators
- Raise awareness of social issues enabling crime
- Respect families' privacy and protect them from re-traumatization

Believes consumer accountability matters: actively chooses narratives that promote social consciousness and challenges the industry to shift away from exploitative practices toward responsible storytelling.
