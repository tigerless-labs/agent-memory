## Choosing the Right Brayer

Key factors to consider:

1. **Ink type**: Water-based inks require softer brayers; oil-based inks require harder brayers
2. **Printing surface texture**: Smoother surfaces suit harder brayers; rougher surfaces need softer brayers  
3. **Size of printing surface**: Smaller surfaces use smaller brayers; larger surfaces use larger brayers
4. **Design detail level**: Fine details work better with smaller, firmer brayers
5. **Personal preference & experience**: Past success with specific brayers is a valid guide

Selection is iterative—experimentation and trial-and-error are normal parts of the process.
