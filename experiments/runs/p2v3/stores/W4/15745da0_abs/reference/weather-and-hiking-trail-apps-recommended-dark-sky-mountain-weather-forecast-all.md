**Weather Apps:**
- Dark Sky: Minute-by-minute forecasts, accurate for hiking planning
- Weather Underground: Hourly/10-day forecasts, weather radar, satellite imagery
- Mountain Weather Forecast: Specialized for mountain weather, includes snowfall and avalanche risk
- OpenSnow: Mountain forecasts (ski-oriented but useful for hiking)
- The Weather Channel: General weather forecasts
- AccuWeather: Minute-by-minute forecasts
- National Weather Service (NWS): Official forecasts, warnings, radar

**Trail-Finding Resources:**
- AllTrails: Search by location, distance, difficulty; reviews
- TrailLink: US trail information with descriptions, maps, reviews
- Hiking Project: Comprehensive trail guide with maps, elevation profiles, reviews

**Social Media Platforms for Outdoor Engagement:**
- Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, Strava (for athletes), Pinterest
