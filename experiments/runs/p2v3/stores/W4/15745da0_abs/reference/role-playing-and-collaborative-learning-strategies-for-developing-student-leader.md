## Role-Playing Activities for Leadership Development\n1. **Workplace Leadership Simulations** - Students manage teams, practice decision-making, communication, conflict resolution under deadline pressure\n2. **Leadership Styles Role-Play** - Students explore different leadership styles, their advantages/disadvantages, develop empathy\n3. **Boardroom Discussion** - Students take roles (CEO, CFO, CMO), practice contribution, listening, negotiation, defending positions\n4. **Decision-Making Scenarios** - Students assume leader role, debate problems, determine positions, make decisions\n5. **Conflict Resolution** - Role-play mediator or conflict participant, learn to recognize and address conflicts productively\n\n## Tips for Engaging Role-Playing Activities\n- Use real-life scenarios students can relate to\n- Provide clear objectives from the start\n- Encourage creativity and imagination\n- Allow flexibility for students to take ownership\n- Provide constructive feedback highlighting strengths and improvement areas\n\n## Additional Leadership Development Strategies\n- Mentorship programs (students mentor younger peers)\n- Service learning projects (community engagement developing teamwork)\n- Internship opportunities aligned with interests/goals\n- Leadership workshops on communication, teamwork, critical thinking, adaptability\n- Collaborative learning requiring group work on assignments\n\n## Ensuring Equal Group Participation\n- Set clear expectations for participation and contribution roles\n- Provide individual assignments within group projects\n- Assign specific group roles (leader, recorder, timekeeper)\n- Encourage active listening\n- Monitor participation and address inequalities early
