**Display and power settings:**
- Dim screen brightness to comfortable level or enable auto-brightness
- Enable Power Saver or Low Power Mode
- Turn off keyboard backlight and unnecessary peripherals

**Disable unnecessary features:**
- Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, location services when not in use
- Close background processes and unused apps
- Disable notifications and alerts for non-essential apps

**Maintenance:**
- Clean laptop vents regularly to prevent heat buildup
- Keep laptop in comfortable temperature range (32-95°F / 0-35°C)
- Monitor battery health with Windows Battery Report or third-party tools

**Software optimization:**
- Update OS and drivers for power-saving improvements
- Use power-efficient browsers (Edge, Chrome) or lightweight alternatives (Firefox)
- Limit resource-intensive tasks (video editing, gaming, streaming)

**System updates:**
- Update BIOS for potential power-saving improvements
- Consider battery-saving software (BatteryMon, BatteryBar, Greenify)

**Periodic maintenance:**
- Calibrate battery periodically for health and performance (drain to 0%, charge to 100%)
