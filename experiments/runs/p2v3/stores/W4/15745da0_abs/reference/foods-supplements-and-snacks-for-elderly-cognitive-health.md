Brain-Supporting Nutrients: Omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, tuna, mackerel), Antioxidants (berries, spinach, kale), B vitamins B6/B9/B12 (leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals), Caffeine (coffee, tea - moderate), Curcumin (turmeric - anti-inflammatory). Easy Healthy Snacks: apple/celery + nut butter, roasted chickpeas, Greek yogurt + berries + honey, hummus + veggies, trail mix, whole-grain crackers + avocado, smoothies, energy balls. Tips: creative recipes, incorporate into favorites, colorful presentation, adjust texture for ease, involve elderly in planning.
