## Getting Started in Printmaking

1. **Master basics first**: Begin with relief printing (easiest, minimal equipment), then advance to other techniques
2. **Experiment widely**: Try different papers and inks to learn how materials affect the final product
3. **Embrace mistakes**: Don't fear failed prints—adjust and try again; mistakes are part of learning
4. **Take a class**: Formal instruction or workshops teach techniques, provide feedback, and build community
5. **Practice consistently**: Printmaking is a skill that improves with repetition and hands-on experience

Core mindset: Printmaking is fundamentally about experimentation and creativity—enjoy the process and push boundaries.
