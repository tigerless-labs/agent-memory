Guidelines for ethical engagement with true crime:

**As consumers:**
- Be mindful of consumption patterns and their impact on victims and families
- Hold ourselves accountable for how we engage with narratives
- Seek out stories that prioritize victim experiences over sensationalism

**What to look for in narratives:**
- Center the victim's life and dignity
- Challenge normalization of violence
- Promote awareness of broader social and systemic issues
- Focus on prevention and justice

**Holding creators accountable:**
- Demand ethical storytelling practices from media outlets
- Support narratives that demonstrate respect and social consciousness
- Contribute to creating a more equitable culture around the genre

The true crime industry has faced increasing scrutiny in recent years for exploitative practices; consumer demand for ethical alternatives can drive positive change.
