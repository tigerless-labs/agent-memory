Genius Voucher is an all-in-one platform for NGOs to manage voucher-based humanitarian projects and aid distribution in real-time. Hardware: POS machines for authorized vendors and NFC smart cards for beneficiaries. Software: Portal for project teams to create and monitor distribution projects. Beneficiaries tap cards on POS terminals to redeem items or cash, with real-time transaction monitoring and offline sync capability. Addresses NGO challenges: prevents corruption through real-time transparency, works offline in remote areas, user-friendly for beneficiaries, enables efficient planning and provides full accountability.
