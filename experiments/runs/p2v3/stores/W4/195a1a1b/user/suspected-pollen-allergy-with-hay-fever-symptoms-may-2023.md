User reported experiencing symptoms consistent with hay fever/pollen allergy: sneezing, runny or stuffy nose, itchy nose/throat, watery/red/swollen eyes, cough, postnasal drip, fatigue, and headache. Planned to consult a doctor for diagnosis. Interested in learning about over-the-counter treatments (antihistamines, decongestants, nasal corticosteroid sprays, cromolyn sodium) and lifestyle modifications (keeping windows closed, using air filters, saline rinses, anti-inflammatory diet, staying hydrated).
