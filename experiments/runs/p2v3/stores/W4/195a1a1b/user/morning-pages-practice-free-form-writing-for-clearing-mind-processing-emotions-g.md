Practices morning pages by letting thoughts flow freely without specific prompts or structure. Uses journal writing for:
- Clearing mind
- Processing emotions
- Reflecting on progress and goals
- Generating creative ideas for short stories and poetry

Morning page entries often develop into full creative pieces later (poems, story scenes). Uses journal to explore character thoughts, feelings, and motivations in free-form way.
