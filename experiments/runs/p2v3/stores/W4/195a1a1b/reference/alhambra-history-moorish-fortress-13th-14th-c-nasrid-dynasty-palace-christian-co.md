**The Alhambra** — Granada's iconic fortress and royal palace:\n\n- **Etymology**: "Al-Hamra" (Arabic) = "the red one" from the reddish hue of walls and towers\n- **Construction**: 13th and 14th centuries during Moorish reign\n- **Original Use**: Fortress against Christian attacks; royal residence of Nasrid dynasty sultans\n- **Cultural Role**: Center of political and cultural activity during Nasrid period\n- **Christian Period** (post-1492): Used as royal palace by Catholic monarchs with additions made\n- **Restoration**: Fell into disrepair but was restored in 19th and 20th centuries\n- **Today**: One of Spain's most visited landmarks; UNESCO World Heritage Site
