Writing analytics tools recommended for tracking overall writing progress:
- WriteTrack: free web-based tool for tracking word count, time spent writing, streaks, and customizable goals
- Wordcounter: web-based tool with dashboard for daily word count, writing goals, streaks, statistics
- 750 Words: writing community platform with daily word count tracking and dashboard
- Habitica: gamified habit tracking for writing-related tasks
- NaNoWriMo writing tools: available year-round with word count tracking and dashboard

Also recommended: Google Sheets or Excel for custom spreadsheet-based progress tracking.
