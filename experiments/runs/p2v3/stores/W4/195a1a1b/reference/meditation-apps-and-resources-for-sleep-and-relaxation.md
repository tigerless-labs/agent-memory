## Popular Meditation Apps

**Headspace**
- 'Sleep' meditation (10 minutes) — highly recommended, effective for relaxation and sleep prep
- Guided by Andy Puddicombe
- Features: breathing, body scan, imagery/visualization, guided journey to sleep
- Pricing: Free trial, then subscription

**Calm**
- Offers guided meditations, sleep stories, and relaxing music
- 'Deep Sleep' meditation (20 minutes)
- Sleep stories available
- Pricing: Free trial, then subscription

**Insight Timer**  
- 'Body Scan' meditation (10 minutes) — focuses on releasing body tension
- Free app with vast library of guided meditations
- Sleep and relaxation-specific content available

**Other apps and resources:**
- Meditation Studio: Offers guided meditations for sleep and stress
- Buddhify: Unique app with meditations for different times of day, including bedtime (one-time purchase)
- Guided Meditation by Andrew Weil: Free on YouTube
- Rainy Mood: Website with guided meditations and rain sounds
- Sleep With Me: Podcast with sleep meditations and bedtime-story approach
- Meditation Oasis: Free website with guided meditations
- UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center: Free guided meditations on website
