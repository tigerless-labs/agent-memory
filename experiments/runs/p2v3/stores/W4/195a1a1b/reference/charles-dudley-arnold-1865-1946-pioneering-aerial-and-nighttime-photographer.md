Charles Dudley Arnold (1865-1946) was an American photographer known for pioneering work in aerial photography and early nighttime photography. Born in NYC, studied at Art Students League; started as portrait photographer before innovating in aerial photography. In the 1890s experimented with cameras mounted on balloons; in early 1900s pioneered aerial photography from airplanes, working with aviators including Orville Wright. Also pioneered nighttime photography using artificial light sources (electric lights, gas lamps, torches, flash powder, magnesium wire) to capture urban scenes with dramatic lighting effects. His work was characterized by innovative composition and capturing patterns/textures from new perspectives.
