Paul Strand (1890-1976) was an American photographer and pioneer of straight photography and modernist photography. Born in NYC, self-taught but influenced by Alfred Stieglitz; joined Photo-Secession group in 1915. In the 1920s shifted to documentary photography, capturing everyday life and social issues. Most famous for his Mexico series starting in 1932, considered important examples of documentary photography. Committed to using photography as a tool for social change and raising awareness of social injustices.
