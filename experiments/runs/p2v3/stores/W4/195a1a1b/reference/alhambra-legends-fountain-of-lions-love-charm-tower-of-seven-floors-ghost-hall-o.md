**Legends and myths associated with the Alhambra**:\n\n1. **Fountain of Lions** — Drinking from this fountain in the Palace of the Lions will make you fall in love with Granada and never want to leave\n2. **Tower of the Seven Floors** — Said to be haunted by the ghost of a princess imprisoned by her father for a forbidden love with a Christian knight  \n3. **Hall of Abencerrajes** — Believed to be the site of a massacre of the Abencerraje family; bloodstains allegedly still visible on the walls\n4. **Devil's Megaphone** — A spot in the Generalife palace gardens with perfect acoustics where the devil is said to speak through echoes
