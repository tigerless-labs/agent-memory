Granada's top landmarks for city views:\n\n1. **The Alhambra** (most-recommended) — Moorish fortress and palace complex with panoramic views of the city and Sierra Nevada Mountains\n2. **Mirador de San Nicolas** — Viewpoint on Albaicín hill with panoramic vistas of Alhambra and mountains\n3. **Sacromonte Abbey** — Hilltop monastery with panoramic views of Granada\n4. **El Salvador Church** — Bell tower provides bird's-eye view of city center\n5. **Albaicín Quarter** — Narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings offering various vantage points
