1. Start with basic setup: guitar, amp, pedalboard, and modulation pedals (chorus or phaser)
2. Experiment with different settings: modulation effects have wide range of settings that drastically change their sound
3. Combine with other pedals: try mixing modulation pedals with overdrive or delay for unique sounds
4. Practice with metronome: helps stay in time even with the randomness modulation effects can add
5. Take your time: takes time to get used to modulation effects; regular experimentation and practice unlock their full potential
