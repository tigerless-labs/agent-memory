- David Gilmour (Pink Floyd): used chorus and phaser effects on 'Comfortably Numb' and 'Shine On You Crazy Diamond'
- Eddie Van Halen: used flanger and phaser effects to create his signature 'brown sound' tone
- The Edge (U2): frequently uses delay and chorus effects for his signature ethereal guitar sound
- Andy Summers (The Police): frequently uses chorus and flanger effects to create complex, layered guitar parts
- Jimi Hendrix: used uni-vibe pedal to create phaser-like effect on 'Machine Gun' and 'Star Spangled Banner'
