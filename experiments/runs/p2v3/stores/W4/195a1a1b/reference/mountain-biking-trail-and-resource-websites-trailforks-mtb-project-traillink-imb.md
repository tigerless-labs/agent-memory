Resources for finding mountain biking trails and events:\n\n**Trail Finder Websites:**\n- Trailforks — trail discovery and tracking\n- MTB Project — mountain biking trail database\n- TrailLink — bike path and trail database\n- IMBA (International Mountain Bicycling Association) Epics — must-ride trails designation\n\n**Event Resources:**\n- USA Cycling — national cycling events and championships\n- Sea Otter Classic — California; four-day festival with racing, demos, exhibitions\n- Crankworx — series of mountain bike festivals (Whistler, BC and other locations) with racing, slopestyle\n- Mountain Bike Festivals — various locations with group rides, demos, live music\n\n**Bike Shop Chains:**\n- REI (Recreational Equipment Inc.)\n- Performance Bicycle\n- Trek Bicycle Stores\n- Giant Bicycle
