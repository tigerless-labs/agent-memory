Francis Benjamin Johnston (1864-1952) was an American photographer and one of the first women to establish a professional photography career in the US. Born in Virginia, opened her own studio in Washington DC in 1894. Known for: 1) Portraits of politicians, celebrities, and socialites (Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, Alice Roosevelt Longworth); 2) Pioneering work in architectural photography, documenting America's historic buildings and colonial-era structures to raise awareness of preservation. Her work was characterized by elegance, simplicity, and attention to detail. Believed photography could be a tool for social change.
