## Common Implementation Challenges

**Teacher Professional Development**
- Ongoing training and support was identified as a common challenge across all case studies
- Many teachers lacked necessary skills and knowledge to effectively use the systems
- Lack of training hindered successful implementation

**Curriculum Alignment**
- Clear alignment between adaptive learning systems and curriculum/learning goals was crucial
- When alignment was clear, teachers were significantly more likely to adopt systems and use them effectively
- Misalignment reduced buy-in and effectiveness

**Equity and Inclusion**
- Systems show potential to reduce achievement gaps and provide personalized support to struggling students
- Significant concern: algorithmic bias in system design could exacerbate existing inequities if not actively addressed
- Bias in algorithms is a critical risk area requiring ongoing attention

**Teacher Role and Adoption**
- Stakeholder disagreement: some educators worried systems would replace teachers; others saw them as valuable support tools
- Successfully framing systems as augmentation rather than replacement is important for adoption
