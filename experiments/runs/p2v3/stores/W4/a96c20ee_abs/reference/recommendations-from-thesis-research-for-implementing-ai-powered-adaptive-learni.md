## Recommendations for Educators and Policymakers

1. **Prioritize Teacher Professional Development**
   - Educators and policymakers should make teacher professional development and training a top priority
   - Provide ongoing support for teachers throughout implementation

2. **Developer-Educator Collaboration**
   - Developers should work closely with educators during system design to ensure alignment with actual teacher and student needs
   - Provide clear guidance on how systems align with curriculum and learning goals
   - Build systems with curriculum integration in mind from the start

3. **Address Algorithmic Bias**
   - Systems must be designed and evaluated to identify and mitigate potential bias in algorithms
   - Ongoing auditing needed to prevent exacerbation of existing educational inequities
   - This is a prerequisite for equity-focused implementation

4. **Ongoing Evaluation and Assessment**
   - Establish continuous evaluation processes for AI-powered adaptive learning systems
   - Use data-driven decision-making to identify areas for improvement
   - Assess impact on different student populations to ensure equitable outcomes
