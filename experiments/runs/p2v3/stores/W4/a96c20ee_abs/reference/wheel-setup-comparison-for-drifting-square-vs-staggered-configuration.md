**Square Setup** (same width/offset all four corners):
- Pros: Even weight distribution, better stability at high speed, forgiving for beginners, preferred by pros
- Cons: May lack traction/grip of staggered, more prone to understeer

**Staggered Setup** (wider rear than front):
- Pros: Increased rear traction, reduces understeer, improves turn-in, more aggressive stance
- Cons: Weight distribution affected, more challenging to handle, less ideal for high-speed competition

Recommendation: Square for high-speed/competition drifting. Staggered for aggressive low-speed drifting. User chose staggered after seeing professional drifters use it.
