**High-Performance Brake Pads:**
- For spirited driving, track days, occasional drifting
- Better stopping power and fade resistance than OEM
- More aggressive friction compound with better initial bite
- Higher dust/noise than OEM, more affordable than racing pads

**Racing-Specific Brake Pads:**
- For competitive racing, track days, extreme conditions
- More aggressive, high-temperature-resistant material
- Exceptional stopping power, higher heat tolerance
- More expensive, but unparalleled performance

For drifting: Choose based on frequency and intensity. Racing-specific better for serious/frequent drifting; high-performance adequate for casual drifting.
