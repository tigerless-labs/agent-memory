**Cat-Back Exhaust** (catalytic converter back):
- Pros: Less expensive, easier install, noticeable power/sound gain
- Cons: Limited power gain, limited by stock converter/downpipe

**Full Exhaust System** (engine to rear):
- Pros: Significant power gain especially mid/high-end, free-flowing design, custom-tailored, improved throttle response
- Cons: More expensive, complex installation, may need additional tune/ECU work

Recommendation: Cat-back for budget-conscious. Full exhaust for serious power gains and unlocking engine potential.

User chose: Full exhaust system to unlock GT-R's engine potential.
