Research examined the effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes, specifically in mathematics and reading comprehension. Used a broad conceptual approach supplemented by case studies of specific platforms and systems to illustrate real-world implementation challenges and opportunities.

**Key Finding**: Systems can be effective in improving outcomes, but careful consideration must be given to how they are implemented and integrated into classroom practice.
