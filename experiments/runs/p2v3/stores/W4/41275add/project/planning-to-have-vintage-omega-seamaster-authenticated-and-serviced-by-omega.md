- Authentication verification (genuine vs. replica)
- Certificate of Origin with production date, model, serial number
- Condition report
- Professional service using original parts
- Unique certificate number for verification
- Increased credibility with buyers
- Higher resale value

Benefits include guaranteed authenticity, enhanced provenance documentation, and peace of mind. Need to contact authorized Omega dealer or Omega Customer Service for cost and timeline.
