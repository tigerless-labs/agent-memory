Decision to sell multiple vintage items online to reach wider audiences of collectors. Key platforms identified by category:

**Watches:** eBay, Chrono24, 1stdibs, The RealReal, watch forums, local dealers, auction houses
**Comics:** ComicConnect, eBay, comic forums, local comic stores
**Action figures:** eBay, action figure collector forums, specialized toy marketplaces
**Guitars:** Reverb, eBay, guitar forums (The Gear Page, TDPRI), vintage guitar marketplaces
**Currency:** Professional numismatists (PNG, IAPMD recommended)

Key strategy: Get professional appraisals, certifications, high-quality photos, and set competitive prices based on market research.
