Values from discussion on colonialism and reconciliation:

- Believes in acknowledging personal biases and privilege; sees this self-awareness as essential for being an ally
- Committed to calling out harmful behavior proactively rather than waiting until harm is already done, despite discomfort
- Recognizes difficulty in changing others' behavior; focuses on personal reflection and change instead
- Wants to find daily, actionable contributions to social justice:
  - Educate self and others about marginalized histories and experiences
  - Speak up and use platform/voice for social justice issues
  - Be an active listener to underrepresented voices
  - Take direct action: volunteering, advocacy, community work
  - Regularly check own privileges and biases
  - Practice empathy in interactions with marginalized groups
- Feels the scale of work needed can be overwhelming, but believes small consistent actions have cumulative positive impact
