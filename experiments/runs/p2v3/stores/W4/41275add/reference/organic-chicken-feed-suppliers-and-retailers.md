**Online Retailers:**
- Amazon
- Tractor Supply Co.
- ChickensForBackyards (specializes in backyard chicken supplies)
- Meyer Hatchery (family-owned, offers chicks and poultry supplies too)

**Feed Suppliers:**
- Organic Valley (farmer-owned cooperative)
- Blue Seal (established feed company with organic/non-GMO options)
- Modesto Milling (family-owned, organic and non-GMO options)
- Scratch and Peck Feeds (small family-owned, USDA organic and non-GMO, locally sourced)

**Tips:** Check certifications (USDA Organic, Non-GMO, Certified Humane), read ingredient labels, compare prices, consider bulk orders. Transition feed gradually to prevent digestive upset.
