Amazon Return Policy: 30-day return window for most products including laptop bags. Go to Your Orders, find the order, click Return or replace items. Amazon provides return shipping label. Once received, refund processed to original payment method.\n\nTargus Warranty: Limited lifetime warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. Contact Targus directly with proof of purchase to make warranty claim. Targus will repair or replace at their discretion.\n\nImportant: Keep original packaging, tags, and accessories for returns or warranty claims.
