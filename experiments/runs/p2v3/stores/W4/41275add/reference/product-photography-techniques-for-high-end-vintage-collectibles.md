Lighting: Use natural light when possible. Shoot near a window with diffused light or during golden hour. Use a softbox or DIY diffuser. Avoid direct flash; bounce light instead. Consider layered lighting for depth and interest.

Composition: Use plain, neutral backgrounds (white, gray, black). Apply rule of thirds. Experiment with unique angles and perspectives. Take close-up shots of specific features to show condition and details.

Editing: Adjust color temperature and saturation for accuracy. Use noise reduction and sharpening tools carefully. Retouch minor imperfections like dust or scratches. Maintain consistent look across all images.

Additional tips: Use a tripod for steady images. Shoot in RAW format. Ensure sharp focus on the item. Include lifestyle shots showing items in use.
