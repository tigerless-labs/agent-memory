**Key Organizations & Resources:**
- National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP) — guidance on nutrition, health, management, vaccination protocols
- American Sheep Industry Association (ASI) — sheep health, vaccination guidelines, parasite management, nutrition
- University Extension Services — local resources (e.g., Purdue, Cornell, Texas A&M) with fact sheets on vaccinations, nutrition, health
- Sheep 101 — comprehensive online resource covering all aspects of sheep production

**Estimated Costs:**
