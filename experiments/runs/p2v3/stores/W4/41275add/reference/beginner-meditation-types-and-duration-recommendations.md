Mindfulness Meditation: focus on present moment without judgment in quiet place, return attention to breath when distracted. Guided Meditation: use apps like Headspace or Calm. Loving Kindness Meditation: cultivate compassion toward yourself and others using phrases like 'may you be happy, may you be healthy.' Start with 5-10 minutes daily; consistency matters more than duration; even a few times per week is beneficial.
