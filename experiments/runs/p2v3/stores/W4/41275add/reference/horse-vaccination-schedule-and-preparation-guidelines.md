**Core Vaccinations (per AAEP):**
- Eastern/Western Encephalomyelitis (EEE/WEE): Annual, spring
- Tetanus: Annual (or 5–7 years if robust immunity)
- West Nile Virus (WNV): Annual, spring

**Risk-Based Vaccinations:**
- Influenza: Annual or biannual (high-risk horses)
- Rabies: Annual or 3-year intervals
- Strangles: Annual or biannual (young horses, high-density settings)

**Foal/Young Horse Schedule:**
- Initial series: 6–8 weeks, 10–12 weeks, 14–16 weeks
- Additional boosters until 1–2 years old

**Pre-Vaccination Preparation:**
- Schedule well in advance
- Confirm vaccination history with vet
- Clean/disinfect vaccination area
- Separate horses if needed (reduce stress)
- Ensure good health (no illness/stress)
- Have medical history and vaccination records ready
- Have horse haltered, groomed, and ready
- Ensure ID current (Coggins, microchip, tattoo)

**Immune-Boosting Supplements (before vaccination):**
- Vitamin E, omega-3 fatty acids, probiotics, beta-glucans
- Herbal: echinacea, astragalus, selenium
- Antioxidants: vitamin C, E, beta-carotene
- Amino acids: L-glutamine, L-arginine, L-carnitine
- Herbal: turmeric, ginger, boswellia

*Always consult veterinarian before adding supplements or changing vaccination schedule.*
