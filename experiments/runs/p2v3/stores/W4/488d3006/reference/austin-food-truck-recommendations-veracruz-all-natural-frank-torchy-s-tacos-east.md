**Popular Austin food trucks recommended:**\n\n1. Veracruz All Natural — authentic Mexican cuisine (migas tacos, tortas, breakfast burritos)\n2. Frank — gourmet hot dogs (Chicagoan, Bacon mac)\n3. Torchy's Tacos — now a chain but originated as food truck (Trailer Park taco)\n4. East Side King — Asian-fusion (pork belly buns, kimchi fries, Thai chicken karaage)\n5. Chi'Lantro — Korean-Mexican fusion (BBQ tacos, burritos, kimchi fried rice)\n6. The Peached Tortilla — Asian-fusion (Korean BBQ tacos, banh mi, kimchi fried chicken)\n7. G'Raj Mahal — Indian-inspired (chicken tikka masala, saag paneer, naan wraps)\n8. Lulu's Fried Chicken — Southern comfort food (fried chicken, waffles, biscuits)\n9. Yume Burger — Japanese-inspired burgers (Tokyo Burger, K-Town Burger)\n10. The Good Seed — plant-based/vegan options\n\n**Finding resources:**\n- Austin Food Truck Finder\n- Food Truck Fiesta (Facebook group)
