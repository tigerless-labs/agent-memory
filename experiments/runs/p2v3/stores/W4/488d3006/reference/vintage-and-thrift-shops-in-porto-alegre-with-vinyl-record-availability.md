Recommended vintage/thrift shops in Porto Alegre:

1. **Brechó Municipal** — Municipal marketplace for used clothes, shoes, accessories. Known for affordable prices.

2. **Armazém do Passado** — Vintage store with clothing, accessories, décor. Carries vinyl records (Brazilian music, rock, pop from various decades). Prices vary depending on items.

3. **Bazar Vintage** — Stylish, fashion-forward vintage and retro clothing/accessories store. Prices vary.

4. **Casa da Sogra** — Retro and vintage home décor, furniture, accessories specialist. Also carries vinyl records (including rare/hard-to-find titles). Broad price range based on size and condition.

5. **Pinga do Brechó** — Vintage and thrift store with clothing, accessories, kitchenware. Prices slightly higher but still reasonable.

**Tips:** Availability and selection vary by shop and current inventory. Best to call or check social media/website before visiting to confirm specific inventory, especially for vinyl records.
