Abstract Art Resources: The Art of Abstract, Abstract Art Academy (online courses), YouTube channels (The Art Sherpa, Artists Network, Abstract Art with Deb Watson). Printmaking Resources: Printmaking 101, The Printmaking Council of New Jersey, YouTube channels (Printmaking with Zeke, The Printmaking Studio). Online Courses: Skillshare, Craftsy, Creativebug. Communities: Reddit (r/AbstractArt, r/Printmaking), Facebook groups. Blogs: The Jealous Curator, Printmaking Today, Abstract Art Magazine.
