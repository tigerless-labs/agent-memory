**Screenwriting workshops and events in Austin:**\n\n1. Austin Film Festival's Screenwriting Conference — annual event with panels, workshops, one-on-one meetings with industry professionals\n2. Writers Guild of America (East & West) — occasional workshops and seminars in Austin\n3. Austin Screenwriters Group — meetup group with regular meetups, workshops, script readings, writing exercises, industry panels\n4. The Screenplay Workshop — regular workshops on character development, dialogue, structure; one-on-one coaching and script consulting\n5. Moody College of Communication (UT Austin) — screenwriting courses, workshops, lectures throughout the year; features industry professionals\n6. Austin Film Society — screenings, workshops, lectures; offers filmmaking grants and resources\n7. SXSW Conference & Festivals — robust film program with panels, workshops, networking\n\n**How to stay updated:**\n- Follow local film organizations on social media\n- Search for online communities and Facebook groups dedicated to screenwriting in Austin
