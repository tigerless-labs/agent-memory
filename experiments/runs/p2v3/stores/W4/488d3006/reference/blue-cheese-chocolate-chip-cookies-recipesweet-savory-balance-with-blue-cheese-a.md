Recipe inspiration came from a blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle tried at a local chocolate shop. First attempt had too much blue cheese (too pungent and salty), but after adjustments found perfect balance of sweet and savory.\n\n**Ingredients:**\n- 2¼ cups all-purpose flour\n- 1 tsp baking soda\n- 1 tsp salt  \n- 1 cup unsalted butter, room temperature\n- 1 cup granulated sugar\n- ½ cup blue cheese, crumbled\n- 2 large eggs\n- 2 tsp vanilla extract\n- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper.\n2. Whisk together flour, baking soda, salt.\n3. Cream butter, sugar, and blue cheese until light and fluffy.\n4. Beat in eggs and vanilla.\n5. Gradually mix in flour mixture.\n6. Fold in chocolate chips.\n7. Drop by tablespoonfuls onto baking sheet.\n8. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges lightly golden.\n9. Cool on baking sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to wire rack.\n\nNote: Use high-quality blue cheese, crumble finely. Blue cheese flavor should be subtle and intriguing, not overpowering. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer.
