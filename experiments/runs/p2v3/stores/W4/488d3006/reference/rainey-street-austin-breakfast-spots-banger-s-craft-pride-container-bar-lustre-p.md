**Breakfast spots near Rainey Street (5-10 min walk):**\n\n1. Banger's Sausage House & Beer Garden — breakfast tacos, quiche, eggs benedict\n2. Craft Pride — weekend brunch (breakfast burritos, avocado toast, chilaquiles)\n3. Container Bar / Café No Sé — separate coffee shop breakfast menu (quiche, sandwiches)\n4. Lustre Pearl — weekend brunch (eggs benedict, breakfast tacos, build-your-own omelette)\n5. Emmer & Rye — farm-to-table seasonal breakfast\n6. Café José — traditional and Mexican-inspired breakfast\n\n**Further options (10-20 min walk):**\n- Magnolia Café\n- The Frisco Shop — classic diner breakfast\n- La Condesa — weekend brunch
