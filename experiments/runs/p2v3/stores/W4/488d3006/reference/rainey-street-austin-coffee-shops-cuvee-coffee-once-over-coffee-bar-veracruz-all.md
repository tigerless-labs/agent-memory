Café No Sé — stylish spot at Container Bar complex, local roasted beans, specialty drinks\n5. The Brew & Brew — coffee, beer, wine, pastries (10 min walk)\n\nBest for quick coffee to-go: Cuvee Coffee or Once Over Coffee Bar
