Art-Science Exhibitions: Wonderlab at Science Museum London (interactive installations exploring scientific principles), Design and the Elastic Mind at MoMA, Making Sense at Wellcome Collection London. Artists: Heather Dewey-Hagborg (uses DNA and biotechnology for portraits), Rafael Lozano-Hemmer (biometric data and sensors for interactive installations), Anicka Yi (biotechnology and living organisms). Art Movements: Bio-Art (uses biotechnology, genetics, living organisms), Sci-Art (combines science, technology, art for interactive experiences), Neuro-Art (brain-computer interfaces and neuroscience). Online Communities: ArtSci Salon (website and online community), SciArt Initiative (non-profit), Ars Electronica (festival and platform).
