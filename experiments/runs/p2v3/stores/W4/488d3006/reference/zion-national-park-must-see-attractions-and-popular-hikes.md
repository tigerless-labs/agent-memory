Attractions: The Narrows (scenic hike through narrow canyon along Virgin River), Angels Landing (iconic rock formation with steep final ascent), The Emerald Pools (series of pools and waterfalls), Weeping Rock (dripping springs with unique rock formation). Popular hikes: Angels Landing Trail 5 miles strenuous, West Rim Trail 10 miles, Observation Point Trail 8 miles, Kayenta Trail 2 miles, Watchman Trail 3 miles. Free shuttle service available. Bring water, sunscreen, sturdy hiking shoes.
