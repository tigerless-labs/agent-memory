Dinosaur-Themed Artists: Paleoart (paleontological illustrations and sculptures), Gary Staab (dinosaur sculptures using fossilized bones), Michael Whelan (dinosaur-inspired paintings combining science and art). Natural History Exhibitions: Hall of Saurischian Dinosaurs at American Museum of Natural History New York, Sue the T-Rex at Field Museum Chicago, Dino Jaws at Natural History Museum London. Collaborations: Art Evolves (brings scientists, artists, natural historians together), The Art of Nature (art pieces exploring natural world), SciArt Initiative (science-art intersection). Online Resources: DinoPit (dinosaur art, illustrations, sculptures), Paleoart online community, Natural History Museum Picture Library.
