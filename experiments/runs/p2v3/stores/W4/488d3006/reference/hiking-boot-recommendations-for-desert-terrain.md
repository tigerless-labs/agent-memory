Keen Targhee II Mid WP: Great ankle support, breathability, waterproof, rugged outsole. Salomon Quest 4D 3 GTX: Excellent ankle support, lightweight, breathable, waterproof. La Sportiva Raptor GTX: Great ankle support, traction, waterproof, comfortable and breathable. Key features to look for: water resistance (Gore-Tex or eVent), ankle support, aggressive tread for traction, breathability for hot climate.
