Popular calendar and task management tools suitable for improving time management:\n\n1. **Google Calendar** - Free, easy to use, syncs across devices, shareable, reminders, multi-attendee events\n2. **Outlook Calendar** - Corporate productivity tool, scheduling, group calendars, email integration\n3. **Apple Calendar** - Inbuilt on Apple devices, iCloud sync, color coding, drag-and-drop, invitations\n4. **Any.do** - To-do list and task manager with built-in calendar, create reminders linked to calendar\n5. **Trello** - Visual collaboration tool with boards and cards, deadline setting, calendar linking\n\nThe right choice depends on personal needs and device ecosystem.
