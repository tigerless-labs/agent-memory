**Resources for finding Austin film productions:**\n\n1. Austin Film Commission — list of current/upcoming productions (features, TV, commercials); can contact directly\n2. Texas Film Commission — searchable by production type, location, job category\n3. Film Austin — non-profit supporting local film industry; posts about productions, casting calls, job opportunities\n4. Austin Film Society — posts about local productions, casting calls, behind-the-scenes events\n5. Casting Websites:\n   - Backstage\n   - Casting Networks\n   - Mandy.com\n6. Production Lists — website aggregating film/TV production info by location and job category\n7. Social Media — follow local production companies, casting agencies, industry professionals\n\n**Getting involved as extra or production assistant:**\n- Create profiles on casting websites\n- Follow casting calls and apply for matching roles\n- Network at local film events, workshops, seminars\n- Volunteer on film sets or with film organizations\n- Be flexible and prepared for long hours at short notice\n- Be respectful and professional when approaching productions
