The Pollock-Krasner Foundation was established in 1985 by Lee Krasner to support individual artists of all nationalities.

**Eligibility:**
- Mid-career and established artists
- Minimum 5 years of active artistic practice
- Must demonstrate financial need (with proof of income, expenses, debts)

**Award:**
- Monetary grants determined by individual artist needs
- Intended for art supplies, studio rent, and related expenses
- Awarded by selection committee based on artistic merit and financial need

**Notable recipients:** Mark Bradford, Kara Walker, Robert Gober, David Salle, Elizabeth Murray, Frank Stella, Chuck Close, Julie Mehretu, Kiki Smith, Jasper Johns, Joan Mitchell

**Additional activities:** Maintains extensive archives on Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner; supports research and scholarly activities related to their work.
