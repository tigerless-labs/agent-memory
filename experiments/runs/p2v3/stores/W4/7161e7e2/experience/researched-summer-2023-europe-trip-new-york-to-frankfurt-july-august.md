**Trip parameters:**
- Route: New York to Frankfurt
- Timeframe: Summer 2023 (July/August), flexible dates
- Solo travel, economy class
- Preference for non-stop flights, open to layovers if not too long

**Research process:**
- Used Google Flights and Skyscanner for price tracking
- Found Lufthansa deal: $850 return (economy)
- Researched multiple airlines: Lufthansa, United, Norwegian Air, TAP Air Portugal, Swiss International, Austrian Airlines
- Considered budget options (Norwegian Air $650, WOW Air $620) but decided against them

**Decision factors considered:**
- Airline financial stability (rejected Norwegian Air due to financial concerns)
- Service quality and amenities
- Price vs. value tradeoff
- Total travel time and layover duration

**Outcome:**
Decided to pursue Lufthansa over cheaper alternatives due to preference for stability and service quality.
