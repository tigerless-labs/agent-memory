## Berne Convention
- Full name: Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works
- Adopted: 1886 in Berne, Switzerland (revised several times)
- Sets minimum standards for copyright protection (literature, music, art)
- Requires member countries to recognize copyright of works from other members as if they were their own

## Paris Convention
- Full name: Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property
- Adopted: 1883 in Paris, France (revised several times)
- Provides protection for patents, trademarks, industrial designs
- Requires member countries to grant rights to applicants from other members, including right of priority for patent/trademark applications

## WIPO Convention
- Full name: World Intellectual Property Organization Convention
- Adopted: 1967; came into force: 1970
- Established WIPO as intergovernmental organization to promote IP protection worldwide
- WIPO administers several international IP treaties including Berne and Paris Conventions

## TRIPS Treaty
- Full name: Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights
- Adopted: 1994; came into force: 1995
- Sets minimum standards for IP protection and enforcement for WTO member countries
- Requires members to protect patents, trademarks, copyrights, other IP forms
- Requires establishment of legal procedures for IP enforcement
