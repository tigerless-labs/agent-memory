## Main Types (4 categories)

**Patent**: Legal monopoly granted to inventor for ~20 years. Protects new, useful, non-obvious inventions (machines, processes, chemical compositions).

**Trademark**: Symbol, name, or logo identifying and distinguishing company's products/services. Can include words, logos, distinctive marks.

**Copyright**: Protects original literary, dramatic, musical, artistic works (books, plays, songs, paintings, software, films, photographs).

**Trade Secrets**: Information (formula, pattern, compilation, program, device, method, technique, process) giving business competitive advantage. Protected by law but not by registration like patents/trademarks.

## Other Forms
- Geographical indication
- Industrial design
- Layout-design of integrated circuits
- Plant variety protection
