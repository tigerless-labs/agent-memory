Progressive insurance discounts and features:\n- Snapshot: Usage-based program tracking driving habits (speed, braking, acceleration) with discounts up to 30%\n- Loan/lease gap coverage: Pays difference between actual cash value and remaining loan/lease if vehicle totaled\n- Custom equipment coverage: Covers custom accessories like stereo systems or rims\n- Deductible savings bank: Reduces deductible after accident\n- Disappearing deductible: Reduces deductible over time for accident-free driving\n- Name Your Price Tool: Enter desired premium and get customized policy\n- Safety feature discounts: Lane departure, blind spot monitoring, forward collision warning, adaptive cruise control\n- Multi-car and bundling discounts available
