Songs/hymns recommended for worship services on faith healing and Luke 7:1-17 theme:
- Modern: 'Healer' (Kari Jobe), 'Great Are You Lord' (All Sons & Daughters)
- Secular: 'I Believe in Miracles' (Jackson Browne)
- Classic hymns: 'O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing' (Charles Wesley), 'Amazing Grace' (John Newton), 'There is Power in the Blood' (Lewis E. Jones), 'Heal Me, O Lord' (William Walsham How)

All express themes of God's healing power, faith, and restoration through Christ.
