**Brands tested or recommended (2023-05-23):**\n\nStaple: Tetra pellet food - fish enjoy regularly\n\nSupplements:\n- Frozen brine shrimp (San Francisco Bay Brand, Hikari, Argent, Omega One recommended brands) - fish strongly prefer, rich in omega-3 fatty acids\n- Frozen bloodworms - fish less interested, but good source of iron and protein\n\nOther potential additions: daphnia, mysis shrimp, spirulina flakes, algae-based foods for balanced nutrition and fiber.
