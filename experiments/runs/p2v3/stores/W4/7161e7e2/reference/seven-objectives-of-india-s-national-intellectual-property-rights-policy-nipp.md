1. Create and exploit IP assets for benefit of Indian economy, society, and public
2. Create awareness and promote IP culture in India
3. Strengthen IP legal and judicial system
4. Encourage R&D, innovation and creativity
5. Provide enabling environment for IP industry development
6. Promote balance between public interest and private rights
7. Promote international cooperation in IP matters

These objectives aim to create an environment promoting innovation, creativity, and R&D while protecting public interest and strengthening the legal and judicial system.
