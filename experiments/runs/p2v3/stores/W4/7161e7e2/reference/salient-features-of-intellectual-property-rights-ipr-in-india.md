1. **Legal Framework**: Comprehensive framework for protection and enforcement (patents, trademarks, copyrights, geographical indications)

2. **Patentability**: First-to-file system; strict criteria include novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability

3. **Trademark registration**: First-to-use system governed by Indian Trade Marks Act, 1999; administered by Trade Marks Registry

4. **Copyright**: Automatic protection without registration; governed by Copyright Act, 1957

5. **Enforcement**: Multiple mechanisms including civil/criminal remedies and specialized courts/tribunals for IPR dispute resolution

6. **International treaties**: Signatory to TRIPS Agreement, Paris Convention, and other international IP treaties

7. **Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB)**: Established to hear appeals against decisions of Registrar of Trade Marks, Controller of Patents, and Registrar of Copyrights

8. **National IPR Policy (2016)**: Framework for overall development and strengthening of IPR system

**Overall**: Robust system balancing creator/owner rights with larger public interest
