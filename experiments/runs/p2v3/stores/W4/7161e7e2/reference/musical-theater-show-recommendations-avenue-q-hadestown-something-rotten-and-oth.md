Recommendations given in response to user's interest in musicals similar to The Book of Mormon:\n\n**Recommended shows:**\n- Avenue Q (irreverent comedy, similar to Book of Mormon)\n- Hadestown (folk-opera retelling of Orpheus and Eurydice myth; won 2019 Tony for Best Musical)\n- Something Rotten! (comedy about two brothers trying to outdo Shakespeare; original Broadway cast recording from 2015)\n- Hamilton (hip-hop/jazz/blues/R&B musical about American history)\n- The Producers (Mel Brooks musical comedy)\n- Urinetown (satirical musical about capitalism and government)\n- Be More Chill (sci-fi musical comedy with electronic-pop score)\n- Dear Evan Hansen (about social media, mental health, human connection)\n- The Band's Visit (Egyptian police band's visit to Israel; quiet, introspective)\n\n**Hadestown details:**\n- Original Broadway cast: Eva Noblezada (Eurydice), Reeve Carney (Orpheus), Patrick Page (Hades), Amber Gray (Persephone)\n- Music by Anaïs Mitchell; haunting folk/blues/jazz/Americana score\n- Premiered off-Broadway 2016, Broadway 2019\n- Available on streaming platforms\n\n**Something Rotten! details:**\n- Original Broadway cast recording from 2015\n- Features Brian d'Arcy James, Christian Borle, John Cariani\n- Available on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music\n- Standout tracks: 'Hard to Be the Bard', 'Right Hand Man', 'We Are the Brothers Wright'
