State Farm insurance discounts and features:\n- Drive Safe & Save: Mobile app tracking driving habits, rewards safe driving with discounts (similar to Progressive's Snapshot)\n- Steer Clear: Safe driving program for young drivers under 25\n- Car safety feature discounts: Lane departure warning, blind spot detection, forward collision warning\n- Multi-car discounts available\n- Bundling discounts for combining auto and home insurance\n- Good student discounts\n- Accident forgiveness programs\n- Defensive driving course discounts\n- Large network of local agents\n- Claims process praised for efficiency and customer service
