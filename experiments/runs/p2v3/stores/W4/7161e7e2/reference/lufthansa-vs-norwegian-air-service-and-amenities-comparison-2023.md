## Lufthansa
- **Service:** Friendly, attentive cabin crew; personalized service; experienced staff
- **Amenities:**
  - Complimentary meal service on most flights (choice of two meals)
  - Seat pitch: 31-32 inches; width: 17.5 inches; adjustable headrests; more recline
  - Wide selection of in-flight entertainment
  - One free checked bag (up to 23 kg) included
- **Hub:** Frankfurt Airport (FRA) - modern, efficient
- **Frequent flyer:** Miles & More program with good benefits

## Norwegian Air
- **Service:** Functional and efficient; more impersonal; focus on speed over comfort
- **Amenities:**
  - Buy-on-board food service (no complimentary meals)
  - Seat pitch: 29-30 inches; width: 17 inches; no adjustable headrests; limited recline
  - Limited in-flight entertainment selection
  - Checked baggage charged extra ($20-$30 per bag)
- **Hub:** Oslo Airport (OSL)
- **Financial concerns:** Facing financial challenges due to competition, rising fuel costs, uncertain future

## Price comparison (2023)
- Norwegian Air: $620-$750 (budget option, multiple layovers)
- Lufthansa: $850 (non-stop or minimal layovers)
