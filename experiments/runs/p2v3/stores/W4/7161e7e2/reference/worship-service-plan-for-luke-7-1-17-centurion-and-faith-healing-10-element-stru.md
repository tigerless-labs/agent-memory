**Worship Service Structure for Faith Healing Theme (Luke 7:1-17):**

1. Opening Hymn: 'Great Are You Lord' (All Sons & Daughters)
2. Call to Worship and Prayer
3. Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17
4. Sermon: Message on faith, healing, and God's power
5. Hymn of Response: 'O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing' (Charles Wesley)
6. Testimony: Congregation member shares healing testimony
7. Hymn of Faith: 'Amazing Grace' (John Newton)
8. Healing Prayer: Corporate prayer for those needing physical/emotional/spiritual healing
9. Closing Hymn: 'Healer' (Kari Jobe)
10. Benediction: Reminder of God's presence and healing power

Plan emphasizes themes of faith, authority of Christ, inclusivity of salvation (centurion as outsider/foreigner), and God's healing power available to all believers.
