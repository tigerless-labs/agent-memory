**Books on feminist art:**
- "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard (comprehensive history 1960s-1990s)
- "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss (relationship between feminist art and maternal; artists Mary Kelly, Mierle Laderman Ukeles)
- "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin (essays: representation of women in art, feminism in art history)
- "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s, History and Impact" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard (detailed US 1970s focus)
- "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls (essays and artworks from anonymous feminist collective; bold, humorous art world critiques)

**Documentaries:**
- "!Women Art Revolution" (2010) - Lynn Hershman Leeson; interviews with 40+ feminist artists including Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, Carolee Schneemann
- "The Heretics" (2009) - Heresies Collective documentary; interviews with Nancy Azara, Miriam Schapiro
- "Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991) - Kate Horsfield; interviews with Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, Yvonne Rainer
- "Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010) - Panel with Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell; moderated by Huey Copeland
- "Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014) - PBS series episode featuring in-depth interview with Judy Chicago
