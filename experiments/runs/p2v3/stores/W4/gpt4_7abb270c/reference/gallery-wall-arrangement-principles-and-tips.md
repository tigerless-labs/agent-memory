**Key steps:** 1) Start with a theme or unifying element (colors, shapes, subjects). 2) Choose similar frame style/color or mix of shapes. 3) Create focal point with statement piece. 4) Experiment with layouts using graph paper or digital tools. 5) Balance and harmonize - distribute for visual equilibrium. 6) Consider wall architecture (molding, windows, doors). 7) Mix large and small prints for depth. 8) Leave breathing room between prints. 9) Step back and adjust. 10) Gallery wall is personal expression - have fun.
