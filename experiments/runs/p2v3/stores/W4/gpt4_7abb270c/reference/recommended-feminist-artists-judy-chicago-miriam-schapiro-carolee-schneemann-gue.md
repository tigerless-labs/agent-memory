## Notable Feminist Artists (1970s and beyond)\n\n- **Judy Chicago**: Pioneer of feminist art; known for 'The Dinner Party'\n- **Miriam Schapiro**: Used fabric, textiles, and other materials to challenge traditional notions of art and femininity\n- **Carolee Schneemann**: Multi-disciplinary artist working in performance, film, and installation; explored gender, sexuality, and the body\n- **The Guerrilla Girls**: Anonymous collective using provocative, humorous tactics to challenge male-dominated art world power structures\n- **The Feminist Art Program**: Founded 1970 at California Institute of the Arts; provided platform for women artists to explore feminist themes\n- **Barbara Kruger**: Known for bold, graphic images and text-based works critiquing consumer culture and gender roles\n- **Cindy Sherman**: Pioneering photographer and filmmaker exploring construction of identity, gender, and representation\n- **Laurie Anderson**: Performance artist, musician, filmmaker exploring identity, politics, and human experience through multimedia
