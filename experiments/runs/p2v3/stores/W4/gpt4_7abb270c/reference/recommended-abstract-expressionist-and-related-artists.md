## Abstract Expressionists and Related Artists\n\n### Core Abstract Expressionists\n- Jackson Pollock\n- Mark Rothko\n- Willem de Kooning: Dutch-American painter; bold expressive brushstrokes, vibrant color palette\n- Franz Kline: Large scale, bold colors, gestural brushstrokes evoking energy and spontaneity\n- Clyfford Still: Large abstract forms, muted color palette; themes of sublime and unknown\n- Ad Reinhardt\n- Barnett Newman\n- Robert Motherwell\n\n### Related Artists\n- **Anselm Kiefer**: German artist; large-scale installations and sculptures exploring history, mythology, human condition; emphasizes scale, materiality, emotional intensity
