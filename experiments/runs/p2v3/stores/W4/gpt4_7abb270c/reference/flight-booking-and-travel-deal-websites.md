**Flight search engines:** Skyscanner, Google Flights, Kayak, Momondo, Airfarewatchdog

**Accommodation:** Booking.com, Airbnb, HotelsCombined, Trivago, Hostelworld

**Package deals:** Expedia, Orbitz, Priceline

**Booking strategy:** Optimal window 54 days before (21-121 day range advisable). Avoid peak days (Tue/Wed/Sat). Use price alerts. Clear cookies. Consider budget airlines, alternative airports.
