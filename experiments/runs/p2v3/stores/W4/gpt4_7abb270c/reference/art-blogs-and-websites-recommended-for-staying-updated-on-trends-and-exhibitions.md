Recommended platforms for art news, exhibitions, and contemporary art coverage:

**Art Blogs & News Sites:**
- Artsy (comprehensive art platform: news, exhibitions, artist profiles, market trends)
- Hyperallergic (contemporary art news, reviews, opinion; NYC focus)
- Artnet (art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists)
- The Art Newspaper (international art news, exhibitions, market analysis)
- Contemporary Art Daily (discovering new artists and exhibitions)
- Art in America (contemporary art features, reviews, interviews)
- Frieze (contemporary art, culture, politics; international focus)
- Apollo Magazine (British publication: art, architecture, collecting; historical and contemporary)
- Art21 (non-profit: documentaries and online content on contemporary art)
- Culture Trip (art, culture, travel; emerging artists and underground scenes)

**Artists for inspiration (interactive/immersive installations):**
- Carsten Höller (interactive installations with slides, Ferris wheels, playful elements)
- Anicka Yi (scent-based immersive installations exploring art, science, technology)
- Rafael Lozano-Hemmer (interactive installations using biometrics, robotics; themes of surveillance/control)

**Immersive exhibition spaces:**
- Meow Wolf (Santa Fe: immersive, interactive exhibitions blending art, technology, storytelling)
- The Color Factory (San Francisco: interactive installations exploring emotional/psychological effects of color)
- TeamLab (Japanese collective: immersive exhibitions using technology and light)
