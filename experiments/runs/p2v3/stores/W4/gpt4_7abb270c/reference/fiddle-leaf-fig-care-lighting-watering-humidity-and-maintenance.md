**Pros:** Beautiful unique leaves, air-purifying, can grow large. **Cons:** Finicky, requires bright indirect light (avoid direct sun which causes leaf burn), needs consistent moisture but easily overwatered, prefers humid environment, needs regular pruning. **Care tips:** Start with smaller plant (1-2 feet tall). Place near bright east- or west-facing window or use grow lights. Check soil daily; water when top 1-2 inches feel dry. Maintain humidity with pebble tray and water or humidifier. Regular pruning. Choose plant with dark green glossy leaves and sturdy stem.
