Developing interest in interactive and immersive installations as a medium for artistic practice. Particularly interested in:

- Themes of identity, memory, and human experience
- Multi-sensory engagement (sound, light, texture, scent)
- Recreating the sensation of specific environments or memories
- Collective memory and how individual experiences relate to shared cultural/historical contexts
- Using found objects, images, and sounds from different cultures and time periods

Technologies/techniques of interest:
- 3D audio and spatial sound design
- Projection mapping
- Scent diffusion
- Haptic feedback
- Augmented reality
- Interactive storytelling and branching narratives
- Collaborative platforms for viewer contributions
- Game engines (Unity) and visual programming (Max/MSP)
- Arduino and interactive objects
