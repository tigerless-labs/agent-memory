Research conducted May 2023 for July-August travel showed these return economy fares:

Non-stop flights: Lufthansa 820-950 USD, United 820 USD, Singapore Airlines 950-1100 USD

Flights with layovers: Norwegian Air (Oslo) 600-750 USD, TAP Portugal (Lisbon) 650-800 USD, Swiss (Zurich) 700-850 USD, Austrian (Vienna) 810 USD, WOW Air (Reykjavik) 620 USD

Booking strategies: Set price alerts on Google Flights and Skyscanner, be flexible with dates (off-peak days cheaper), book in advance, consider alternative airports (Amsterdam, Paris), watch for error fares.

Peak season (July-August) pricing is significantly higher than off-season. Day of week and advance booking time both affect pricing.
