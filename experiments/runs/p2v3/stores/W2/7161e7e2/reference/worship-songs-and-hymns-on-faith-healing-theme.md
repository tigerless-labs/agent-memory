Songs and hymns recommended for worship services on faith healing and Luke 7:1-17 passage:

**Modern Worship Songs:**
- "Healer" by Kari Jobe
- "Great Are You Lord" by All Sons & Daughters

**Classic Hymns:**
- "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" by Charles Wesley
- "Amazing Grace" by John Newton
- "There is Power in the Blood" by Lewis E. Jones
- "Heal Me, O Lord" by William Walsham How

**Other:**
- "I Believe in Miracles" by Jackson Browne (secular)
