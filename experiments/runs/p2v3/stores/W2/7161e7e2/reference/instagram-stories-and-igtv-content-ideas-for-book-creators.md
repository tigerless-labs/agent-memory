Instagram Stories: Sneak peeks of BookClubChat prep (reading, notes, questions). Post-chat thoughts and what was learned. Mini Q&A sessions answering follower questions. Behind-the-scenes of content creation. Story highlights to preserve key content. IGTV: Book reviews discussing thoughts and feelings with video footage. Author interviews (reach out to authors/publishers). In-depth discussion videos about themes, characters, plot twists. Bookish challenges (Read in a day? Summarize in 5 minutes?). Tips: Keep visually appealing with good lighting and sound. Use editing to make content stand out. Mix formats to keep audience engaged.
