Favorites: What's your favorite book of all time? Who's your favorite author and why? What book changed your perspective? Reading habits: Do you prefer e-books or physical? How many books monthly? What's your favorite reading spot? Genres: Favorite fantasy series? Scariest book read? Go-to genre in reading slump? Preferences: Happy vs realistic endings? Hardcover, paperback, or audiobook? Complex characters vs plot-driven? Standalone vs series? Discovery: How do you find new books? Best recommendation received? Trust reviews or word-of-mouth? Fun: Which fictional character would you have dinner with? Longest book read? Attend book signings? These questions help spark engagement and understand follower preferences.
