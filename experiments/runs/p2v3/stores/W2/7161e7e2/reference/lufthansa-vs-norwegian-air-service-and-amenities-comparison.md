Detailed comparison from flight research May 2023:

LUFTHANSA:
- Service: Friendly, attentive cabin crew, personalized experience, more experienced staff
- Meals: Complimentary meal service on most flights, choice of two meals, snacks and drinks included
- Seats: 31-32 inches pitch, 17.5 inches width, adjustable headrests, more recline
- Entertainment: Wide selection of movies, TV, music, games
- Baggage: One free checked bag (23 kg) included in economy
- Hub: Frankfurt Airport (modern, efficient)
- Program: Miles & More frequent flyer program

NORWEGIAN AIR:
- Service: Functional and efficient, friendly but more impersonal
- Meals: Buy-on-board only, no complimentary meals (can pre-order online)
- Seats: 29-30 inches pitch, 17 inches width, no adjustable headrests, limited recline
- Entertainment: Limited selection of movies, TV, music
- Baggage: Extra charge for checked baggage (20-30 USD per bag)
- Hub: Oslo Airport
- Concern: Financial instability, higher bankruptcy risk

Key trade-off: Lufthansa costs more but offers significantly better comfort and service; Norwegian Air is budget option with bare essentials.
