10-part worship service structure for faith healing theme based on Luke 7:1-17:

1. Opening Hymn: "Great Are You Lord" (All Sons & Daughters)
2. Call to Worship and Prayer
3. Scripture Reading: Luke 7:1-17
4. Sermon: Message on Luke 7:1-17 with focus on faith, healing, God's power
5. Hymn of Response: "O For a Thousand Tongues to Sing" (Charles Wesley)
6. Testimony: Congregation member shares healing experience
7. Hymn of Faith: "Amazing Grace" (John Newton)
8. Healing Prayer: Leader leads congregation in prayer for physical, emotional, spiritual healing
9. Closing Hymn: "Healer" (Kari Jobe)
10. Benediction: Reminder of God's presence and healing power

Notes: Structure can be adapted to context and congregation preferences.
