When creating Midjourney prompts, wants:
- Rich, descriptive language from POV of professional photographer/illustrator/animator
- Specific camera equipment details (model, lens, aperture, ISO, shutter speed)
- Detailed lighting descriptions
- Aspect ratio and quality parameters (e.g., --ar 16:9 --q 2 --v 5)
- Creative, whimsical scenarios
- Composition and subject details that capture mood and narrative

Example: detailed casino poker scene with Shiba Inu dressed as gangster grandma, complete with camera specs.
