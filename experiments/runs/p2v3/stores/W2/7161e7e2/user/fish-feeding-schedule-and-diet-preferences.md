Feeding: Twice daily. **Staple:** Tetra pellet food. **Favorites:** Frozen brine shrimp (fish go crazy for these). **Supplementary:** Frozen bloodworms (fish are more finicky about these). Diet varied for balanced nutrition with omega-3 and protein intake.
