Actively practices soapstone sculpture and recently started exploring the medium in depth. Particular interest in bird carvings—realistic, stylized, and whimsical forms. Learns through:
- Following contemporary sculptors on Instagram for techniques and inspiration
- Online tutorials and courses (Craftsy, Udemy, Instructables)
- YouTube sculpture channels
- Communities like Reddit's r/soapstonecarving

Techniques of interest:
- Creating intricate details and textures in soapstone
- Stippling, hatching, relief carving for feather and surface details
- Using specialized carving tools (gouges, chisels, files, rasps)
- Carving process: rough shaping → refining → detailing → texturing

Prefers learning from real sculptors' processes and hands-on experimentation with different tools and techniques.
