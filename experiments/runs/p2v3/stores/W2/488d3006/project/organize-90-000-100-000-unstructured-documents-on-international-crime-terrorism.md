Context: Unstructured documents corpus (1-5 pages each) on international crime, terrorism, and politics. Recommended organization workflow: (1) Text Cleaning - remove irrelevant chars, punctuation, stop words, normalize to lowercase; (2) Document Parsing - extract title, author, date, body; (3) Entity Extraction - identify names, organizations, locations; (4) Topic Modeling - identify main themes and patterns; (5) Sentiment Analysis - track tone and political sentiment; (6) Document Classification - categorize by content type for search and filtering.
