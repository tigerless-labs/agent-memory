**Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grant**

Established in 1985 by Lee Krasner, widow of Jackson Pollock.

**Purpose:** Support individual artists of all nationalities with financial assistance to promote the legacy of both artists.

**Eligibility:**
- Mid-career and established artists
- Minimum 5 years of active work in their artistic practice
- Must demonstrate financial need (proof of income, expenses, debts)

**Award:**
- Monetary grants intended for practical support
- Amount varies based on individual artist's specific needs
- Covers: art supplies, studio rent, other expenses

**Selection:**
- Competitive process based on artistic merit
- Committee evaluates work, exhibition history, and impact in field
- No preference by style, medium, or genre (painting, sculpture, printmaking, photography, multimedia all eligible)

**Archive:**
- Foundation maintains extensive archive about Jackson Pollock and Lee Krasner
- Supports research and scholarly activities related to their work
