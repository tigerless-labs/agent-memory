## Austin Film Festival\n- Hosts annual Screenwriting Conference with panels, workshops, and one-on-one meetings with industry professionals\n- Jim Uhls leads screenwriting workshops\n\n## Film Industry Resources in Austin\n- Austin Film Commission: provides list of current/upcoming productions\n- Texas Film Commission: state-level production listings\n- Film Austin: non-profit supporting local film industry\n- Austin Film Society: posts about productions, casting calls, behind-the-scenes events\n- Austin Screenwriters Group: regular meetups, script readings, writing exercises\n- The Screenplay Workshop: offers regular workshops on character development, dialogue, structure; one-on-one coaching\n- Moody College of Communication (UT Austin): screenwriting courses and industry lectures\n- SXSW: robust film program with panels and networking\n\n## Casting/Production Opportunities\n- Backstage, Casting Networks, Mandy.com: casting calls\n- Production Lists: aggregates production info by location and job type
