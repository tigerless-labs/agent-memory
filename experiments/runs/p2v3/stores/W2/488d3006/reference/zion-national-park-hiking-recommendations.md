Must-see attractions: The Narrows, Angels Landing, Emerald Pools, Weeping Rock. Popular hikes: Angels Landing (5 mi, strenuous), West Rim Trail (10 mi), Observation Point Trail (8 mi), Kayenta Trail (2 mi), Watchman Trail (3 mi). Tips: use free shuttle service, wear sturdy hiking boots with good traction, bring water and sunscreen, check for permits.
