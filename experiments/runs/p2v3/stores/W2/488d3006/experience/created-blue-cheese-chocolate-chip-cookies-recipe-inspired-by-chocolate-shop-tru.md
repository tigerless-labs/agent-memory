Developed a Blue Cheese Chocolate Chip Cookies recipe that combines savory and sweet flavors. The idea came from tasting blue cheese and dark chocolate truffles at a local chocolate shop. Initial attempts failed because blue cheese overwhelmed other flavors, but after several iterations found the right balance. Son (a picky eater) loved the final version and asked for multiple servings. Recipe includes 1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese mixed into standard chocolate chip cookie dough, baked at 375°F for 12-15 minutes. Wrote a blog post about the recipe and its origin story.
