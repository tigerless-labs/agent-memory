Cares deeply about healthcare disparities affecting marginalized communities. Believes healthcare inequalities stem from broader systemic issues: poverty, discrimination, and lack of education. Advocates for proactive government policies addressing root causes rather than crisis-driven responses. Emphasizes need for sustained, coordinated action from governments, healthcare institutions, and community organizations to ensure equitable access to quality healthcare regardless of socioeconomic status or geography.
