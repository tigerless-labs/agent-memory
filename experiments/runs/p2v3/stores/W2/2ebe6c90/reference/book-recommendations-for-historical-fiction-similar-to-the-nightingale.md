We Were the Lucky Ones by Georgia Hunter (WWII Polish family)\n2. The Lost Girls of Paris by Pam Jenoff (1946, WWII resistance)\n3. The Women in the Castle by Jessica Shattuck (WWII aftermath)\n4. The Soldier's Wife by Pam Jenoff (Nazi-occupied France)\n7. The Postmistress by Sarah Blake (WWII, 1940)\n8. The Girl You Left Behind by Jojo Moyes (WWI France)\n9. The Invisible Bridge by Julie Orringer (WWII Hungarian Jewish family)\n10. The Red Tent by Anita Diamant (Biblical mythology retelling)\n11. The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (Chinese history-inspired fantasy)\n12. The Gilded Wolves by Roshani Chokshi (late 1800s Paris fantasy)\n13. The Ten Thousand Doors of January by Alix E. Harrow (historical fantasy)\n\nUser showed particular interest in The Alice Network, The Song of Achilles, and The Kitchen House.
