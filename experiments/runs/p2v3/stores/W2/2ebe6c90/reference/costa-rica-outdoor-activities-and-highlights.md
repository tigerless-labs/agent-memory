# Costa Rica Outdoor Activities & Destinations

## Hiking & Trekking
- Corcovado National Park: Primary rainforest, monkeys, sloths, toucans (highly biodiverse)
- Arenal Volcano: Rainforest hikes with volcano views
- Monteverde Cloud Forest: Misty ecosystem, Sky Tram option

## Wildlife Watching
- Tortuguero National Park: Sea turtles, monkeys, sloths, 300+ birds
- Manuel Antonio National Park: Monkeys, sloths, toucans, quetzals, beaches, trails
- Osa Peninsula: Remote, jaguars, pumas, harpy eagles

## Water Activities
- Surfing: 800+ miles coastline; spots include Tamarindo, Nosara, Puerto Viejo
- Whitewater rafting: Class II-IV rapids with scenic views
- Waterfalls & swimming: Hidden waterfalls and natural pools

## Aerial/Canopy
- Zip-lining and canopy tours: Fly through jungle, spot wildlife from above

## Other
- Birdwatching: 850+ species including quetzals, macaws, toucans
- Hot springs: Natural hot springs surrounded by rainforest

## Climate Notes
- August: Green season (lush landscapes, fewer tourists, lower prices)
- Occasional rain showers but generally short and followed by sunshine
- High humidity, especially on coasts
- 12 rainy days typical for August
