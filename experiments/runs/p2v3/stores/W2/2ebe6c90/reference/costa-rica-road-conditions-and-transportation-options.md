# Costa Rica Travel Logistics

## Road Conditions
- 30% paved roads (generally good condition, urban/highway/tourist routes)
- 70% unpaved roads (rough, dusty, potholes in rural areas)
- Mountain roads: steep, winding, narrow, prone to fog and landslides
- Rainy season (May-Nov): muddy/flooded roads, potential closures

## Transportation Options
- **4x4 rental:** Essential for exploring rural areas; inspect before renting
- **Shuttle services:** Convenient option arranged by hotels/tour operators
- **Public buses:** Affordable but crowded and slow
- **Taxis:** Widely available; agree on price beforehand (not always metered)
- **Private transfers:** Arranged through hotels/tour operators
- **Domestic flights:** Convenient for longer distances or remote areas

## Driving Tips
- Drive during daytime only (poor night visibility on rural roads)
- Watch for pedestrians, cyclists, livestock
- Use headlights in low-light conditions
- Avoid driving when tired
- Keep spare tire and basic toolkit
- Regular vehicle maintenance (tire pressure, oil, brakes)
- Learn basic Spanish phrases

## Essential Gear
- Detailed road maps and GPS device or app
- Insect repellent and sunscreen
- Sturdy hiking shoes
- Refillable water bottles
- Binoculars and camera
