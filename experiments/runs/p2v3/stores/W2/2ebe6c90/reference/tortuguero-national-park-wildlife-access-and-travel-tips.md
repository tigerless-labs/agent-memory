# Tortuguero National Park, Costa Rica

## Location & Access
- Only accessible by boat or plane — no road access
- From San José: flight (1 hr) + boat (1-2 hrs), or shuttle to La Pavona (4-5 hrs) + boat (1-2 hrs)
- Book transportation well in advance (especially Dec-April peak season)
- Many eco-lodges include transportation in packages

## Wildlife (Over 300 bird species)
- Monkeys: Howler, white-faced capuchins, spider monkeys
- Sloths: Two-toed and three-toed (commonly seen hanging from branches)
- Large cats: Jaguars (rare), other predators
- Reptiles: American crocodiles, Morelet's crocodiles, iguanas, snakes (fer-de-lance)
- Birds: Toucans, quetzals, macaws
- Other: Caimans, 1,200+ butterfly species

## Travel Tips
- Pack light for boat transfers
- Best wildlife spotting: early morning and late afternoon
- Use guided tours to maximize sightings
- Be patient; wildlife requires keen observation
- Respect wildlife: maintain distance, don't feed, avoid disturbance
- Insect repellent essential (mosquitoes prevalent)
