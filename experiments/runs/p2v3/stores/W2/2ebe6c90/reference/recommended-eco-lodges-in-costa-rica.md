# Eco-Lodges in Costa Rica

**Osa Peninsula:**
- Lapa Rios Ecolodge — Pioneer in sustainable tourism; luxurious with community engagement
- El Remanso Lodge — Luxury accommodations with reforestation program

**Arenal Region:**
- Arenal Observatory Lodge — Near base of Arenal Volcano; strong conservation focus

**Monteverde:**
- Monteverde Lodge — Surrounded by cloud forest; strong sustainability focus

**Tortuguero National Park:**
- Tortuga Lodge — Accessible only by boat/plane; haven for wildlife enthusiasts with conservation focus

**Tour Operators (Sustainable/Certified):**
- Costa Rica Expeditions — Pioneer in eco-tourism; hiking, birdwatching, wildlife adventures
- Osa Conservation — Local operator with conservation programs in Osa Peninsula
- Tortuguero Canals Tours — Family-owned, prioritizes sustainability and community
- Selva Bananito Lodge and Reserve — Supports local conservation efforts
- Golfito Wildlife Refuge Tours — Supports conservation in the region

**Certifications to look for:**
- Certification for Sustainable Tourism (CST)
- International Ecotourism Society (TIES)
- Costa Rican Tourism Board (ICT) certification
