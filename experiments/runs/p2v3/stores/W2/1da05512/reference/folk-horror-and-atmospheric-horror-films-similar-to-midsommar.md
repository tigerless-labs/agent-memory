**Folk horror recommendations:**
- The Wicker Man (1973) — classic paganism/ritual sacrifice film
- The Devil's Backbone (2001) — ghost story set during Spanish Civil War
- Kill List (2011) — slow-burning paganism and occult
- A Dark Song (2016) — occult rituals and folk magic
- November (2017) — black-and-white Estonian folk horror
- Apostle (2018) — cults, paganism, charismatic leaders (Netflix)
- The Sacrament (2013) — found-footage cult horror

**Atmospheric horror with similar unease:**
- The Invitation (2015) — slow-burning psychological tension
- Martha Marcy May Marlene (2011) — cult aftermath, psychological drama
- The Blackcoat's Daughter (2015) — boarding school supernatural
- The Autopsy of Jane Doe (2016) — father-son supernatural mystery

All feature sense of unease, atmospheric tension, and folk/pagan elements or unsettling atmosphere.
