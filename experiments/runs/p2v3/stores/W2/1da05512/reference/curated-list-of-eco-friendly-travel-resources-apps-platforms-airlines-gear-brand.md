Transportation & Mobility: Moovit, Transit, Waze Carpool, BlaBlaCar, Citymapper, Rome2rio, Via, Juno, Gett, Green Taxi, Electric Cab, Bird, Lime, Spin.

Airlines with carbon offset: Qantas (Fly Carbon Neutral), Air New Zealand (FlyNeutral), KLM (CO2ZERO), Lufthansa (Compensaid), SAS, Virgin Atlantic, United, Norwegian Air, TAP Air Portugal, Finnair, Singapore Airlines.

Accommodation platforms: EcoLodges.com, Ecobnb, Booking.com (Sustainable Travel filter), Expedia (Sustainable Tourism), Airbnb (Eco-Friendly Stays), Glamping.com, National Geographic Unique Lodges. Hotel programs: IHG Green Engage, Marriott Sustainability, Hilton Travel with Purpose.

Carbon offset apps: Carbon Footprint, Offset, EcoPassenger.

Waste reduction apps: Refill, iRecycle, Zero Waste Travel, HappyCow, EcoChallenge, Susty, TripIt, Google Trips.

Travel gear: bags (recycled polyester, canvas), toiletries (shampoo bars, toothpaste tablets), clothing (merino wool, quick-dry fabrics), accessories (water bottles, beeswax wraps, power banks, solar chargers, reusable straws, reef-safe sunscreen).

Certifications: LEED, EarthCheck, Bluesign, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, Ecotourism Kenya.
