**Horror anthologies with humor and horror blend:**
- Creepshow (1982) — classic Stephen King + George A. Romero, five stories
- Tales from the Darkside: The Movie (1990) — based on TV series, three stories with wraparound
- Trick 'r Treat (2009) — interweaved Halloween night stories, folklore focus
- V/H/S (2012) — found-footage, six stories with different styles
- Scare Package (2019) — seven stories, genre tropes and clichés focus

**Other horror anthologies:**
- Asylum (1972) — British, four stories, atmospheric and suspenseful
- The Twilight Zone: The Movie (1983) — four stories, horror/sci-fi/social commentary
- Body Bags (1993) — Tobe Hooper/John Carpenter, three stories, EC Comics tribute
- XX (2017) — four stories directed by female directors, horror and drama

All offer variety of horror styles and storytelling approaches.
