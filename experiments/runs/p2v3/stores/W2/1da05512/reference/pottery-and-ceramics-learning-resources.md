Online platforms and resources for pottery/ceramics learning:

**Courses:**
- Craftsy — online courses on pottery, ceramics, glazing, firing
- Ceramic Arts Network — online courses, tutorials, articles, videos

**YouTube Channels:**
- Simon Leach Pottery — tutorials on pottery techniques including coiling, scoring
- Pottery By Osa — variety of pottery tutorials including coiling, scoring
- The Ceramic School — range of pottery tutorials by experienced ceramic artists

**Websites:**
- Pottery Making Illustrated — step-by-step tutorials, articles, videos on pottery techniques
- The Potter's Council — pottery tutorials, articles, videos
- Ceramic Arts Network — community and resource for ceramic artists

**Books:**
- 'The Potter's Bible' by Marylin Scott — comprehensive guide to ceramics
- 'Glazes for the Self-Sufficient Potter' by Ian Gregory — guide to glaze formulation and application
