**Similar tone to 'Cabin in the Woods':**
- The Evil Dead (1981) — classic horror comedy
- Tucker & Dale vs Evil (2010) — hilarious genre subversion
- Scream (1996) — self-aware, witty horror
- The Faculty (1998) — group trapped with sinister forces

**Other horror-comedies:**
- Shaun of the Dead (2004) — zombie comedy perfection
- Zombieland (2009) — fun, action-packed zombie film
- What We Do in the Shadows (2014) — mockumentary vampire comedy
- Cooties (2014) — dark comedy, zombie-like virus
- Tragedy Girls (2017) — dark comedy with social media angle
- Bad Times at the El Royale (2018) — horror-tinged thriller
- Gremlins (1984) — classic horror comedy
- The Frighteners (1996) — Peter Jackson, Michael J. Fox
- Slither (2006) — alien parasite invasion
- Zombeavers (2014) — campy, over-the-top

All blend humor with genuine scares and horror elements.
