**Horror-comedy TV series:**
- Ash vs. Evil Dead (2015-2018) — continuation of Evil Dead franchise, gore and humor
- What We Do in the Shadows (TV series, 2019-present) — mockumentary vampire comedy in NYC
- Santa Clarita Diet (2017-2019) — suburban couple, wife becomes zombie
- iZombie (2015-2019) — supernatural procedural, medical student-turned-zombie in coroner's office
- Scream Queens (2015-2016) — Ryan Murphy creation, satire and horror
- Stan Against Evil (2016-2018) — former sheriff vs supernatural forces
- Chucky (2021-present) — Syfy series, killer doll, campy and humorous
- NOS4A2 (2019-2020) — supernatural, based on Joe Hill novel

**Other atmospheric horror TV:**
- The Haunting of Hill House (2018-2019) — haunted house, psychological complexity
- Stranger Things (2016-present) — sci-fi horror, 1980s homage
- The Haunting of Bly Manor (2020) — Netflix, based on Henry James, atmospheric
- Lovecraft Country (2020) — HBO, 1950s racism + supernatural horror
- The Outsider (2020) — HBO, detective mystery with supernatural elements
- Resident Evil: Infinite Darkness (2021) — Netflix, action-packed zombie horror
- The Terror (2018-2019) — historical horror based on true events
