**Scene Concept**: Girl about to sit down and speak into microphone; slowly disappears into the computer on camera while the viewer watches.

**Style**: Black Mirror-ish — dark, unsettling, tech-dystopian; goal is to leave viewers feeling uneasy and curious about what's happening in the digital realm.

**Visual Techniques**:
- Glitchy and distorted computer interface
- Flickering lights, static noise, distorted sounds
- Distortion effects, flashes of light, swirling motion during disappearance
- Slow, deliberate camera movements to build anticipation
- Zoom in on face/hands to create intimacy and focus
- Quick cuts for jarring effect (optional)
- Cut to computer interface after disappearance, showing closeups of buttons/screens

**Sound Design**:
- Haunting or eerie melody for foreboding
- Discordant sounds, white noise
- Low hum or distant whispers
- Distorted sound effects

**Lighting**:
- Dim lighting or backlighting to create mystery
- Use of shadows for moody, atmospheric setting
