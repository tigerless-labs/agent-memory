**Trip details:**
- Type: 3-4 day backpacking trip
- Destination: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp, Sierra Nevada, Yosemite
- Planned for: September 2023
- Elevation: 10,300 feet
- Route: Vogelsang High Sierra Camp route (easier alternative to High Sierra Camp Loop)

**Route specifics:**
- Distance: ~20 miles total
- Elevation gain: 3,000 feet
- Difficulty: Moderate (good introduction to high-elevation backpacking)
- Access: 7-mile hike from Tuolumne Meadows, or shuttle bus available from Tuolumne Meadows Visitor Center

**Accommodations:**
- Tent cabins with wooden floors, canvas walls
- Each cabin sleeps 4 with two bunk beds, mattresses, pillows
- Full-service dining (breakfast, lunch, dinner included)
- Modern restrooms with flush toilets and showers
- Potable water, dishwashing station, camp store

**Requirements:**
- Permit required from Yosemite National Park (apply early)
- Reservations required through Yosemite Conservancy website
- Need to bring sleeping bag despite mattresses provided
- Pack layers for unpredictable high-elevation weather

**Why chosen:**
- More manageable than High Sierra Camp Loop (40 mi, 7,000 ft)
- Good confidence builder for high-elevation backpacking
- Still offers stunning alpine scenery and good conditioning opportunity
