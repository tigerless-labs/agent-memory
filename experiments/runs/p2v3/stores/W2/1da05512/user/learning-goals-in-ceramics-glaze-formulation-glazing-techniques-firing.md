Learning objectives:
- Glaze formulation: creating custom glaze recipes from scratch
- Understanding glaze ingredient roles and selecting materials
- Achieving specific colors and effects (subtle blues, vibrant reds, textured matte finishes)
- Glazing application techniques (brushing, pouring, spraying)
- Firing techniques and schedules
- Achieving consistent, food-safe finishes on functional pieces
