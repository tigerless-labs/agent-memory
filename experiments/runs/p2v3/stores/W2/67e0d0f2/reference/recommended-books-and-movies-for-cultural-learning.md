Books for cultural learning:

1. **The Namesake** by Jhumpa Lahiri — Explores identity, family, and culture through a Bengali-American family's story.

2. **The Kite Runner** by Khaled Hosseini — Story of two childhood friends in Afghanistan; themes of friendship, betrayal, and redemption.

3. **The Joy Luck Club** by Amy Tan — Follows four Chinese-American women and their relationships with immigrant mothers.

4. **Things Fall Apart** by Chinua Achebe — Explores a Nigerian tribe before and after British colonization.

5. **Persepolis** by Marjane Satrapi — Graphic novel about a young Iranian girl during the Islamic Revolution.

Movies for cultural learning:

1. **Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon** — Martial arts epic set in ancient China; themes of loyalty and honor.

2. **The Lives of Others** — German film set in East Berlin during Cold War; explores surveillance, loyalty, and human connection.

3. **Lion** — True story of an Indian boy lost and adopted by an Australian family; his journey to find his birth family.

4. **The Motorcycle Diaries** — Based on travel diaries of Che Guevara and Alberto across South America; explores injustices faced by the poor.

5. **City of God** — Brazilian film following characters in a violent favela; themes of poverty, violence, and survival.
