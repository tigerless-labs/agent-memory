## Overview
Hurricane Dorian devastated the Bahamas in September 2019. A multi-organizational task force was formed to coordinate relief efforts.

## Organizations Involved
- United Nations
- World Health Organization (WHO)
- International Red Cross
- Various national governments

## Relief Activities
- Distributed food, water, and medical supplies
- Deployed search and rescue teams
- Set up temporary shelters for displaced people
- Assessed damage and developed long-term recovery plans

## Safety Measures for Aid Workers
- Conducted safety briefings on hazards (contaminated water, heat exhaustion, debris injuries)
- Provided protective gear (helmets, gloves, safety glasses)
- Established security measures to prevent looting and violence
- Coordinated with local authorities
- Created safe zones and checkpoints
- Provided psychological support and counseling for relief workers

## Challenges Faced
- **Scale**: Widespread damage across multiple islands, difficult to reach remote areas
- **Logistics**: Mobilizing resources and personnel from multiple countries/organizations
- **Communication**: Disaster knocked out telecommunications infrastructure
- **Security**: Reports of looting and criminal activities in some areas requiring security protocols
