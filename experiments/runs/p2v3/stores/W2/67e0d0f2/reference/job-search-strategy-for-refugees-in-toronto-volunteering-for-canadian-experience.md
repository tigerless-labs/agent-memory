Job search strategy in Canada:

Volunteering: Valuable for gaining Canadian work experience, building professional network, and demonstrating commitment. Employers value volunteer work. Platforms: Volunteer Toronto, Idealist, LinkedIn Volunteer Marketplace

Settlement agencies for support: The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO), COSTI Immigrant Services, WoodGreen Community Services, Toronto Refugee Centre, North York Community House

Job search resources: Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca), Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor

Networking: Attend industry events, join professional associations, connect on LinkedIn

Tips: Build network over time, connect with others in field, leverage settlement agencies for job search guidance, consider credential recognition programs for international qualifications
