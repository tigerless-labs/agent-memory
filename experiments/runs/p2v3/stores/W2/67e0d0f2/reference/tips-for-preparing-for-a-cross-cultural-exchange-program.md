Preparation tips for cross-cultural exchange programs:

1. **Research the country and culture** — Learn about customs, traditions, history, and current events. Understanding the cultural background helps ensure a more meaningful and respectful experience.

2. **Learn the language** — Aim to learn basics of the local language before departure. Demonstrates care for the culture and improves communication.

3. **Pack appropriately** — Choose clothing and items suitable for local climate, culture, and activities. Be willing to leave behind familiar items.

4. **Be open-minded and flexible** — Living abroad brings exposure to new foods, social norms, and cultural expectations. Flexibility is essential for making the most of the experience.

5. **Develop relationships with locals** — Make effort to meet and build relationships. Great way to practice language, learn about culture, and make friends.

6. **Stay safe** — Be aware of surroundings, follow local customs and laws, and use common sense.
