Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) scale 0-12. Employment requirements vary by job:

CLB 5-6 (basic fluency): Customer service, sales, skilled trades
CLB 7-8 (intermediate): Administrative, office work
CLB 9-10 (advanced): Professional roles (healthcare, finance, law)
CLB 11-12 (proficient): Near-native ability

Language certificates for demonstration: LINC (Language Instruction for Newcomers to Canada), IELTS, TOEFL, CELPIP

In-demand sectors in Toronto (2023): Technology/IT (software development, data science, cybersecurity), Healthcare (nurses, doctors, allied health), Finance (banking, accounting), Skilled Trades (electricians, plumbers, carpenters), Renewable Energy
