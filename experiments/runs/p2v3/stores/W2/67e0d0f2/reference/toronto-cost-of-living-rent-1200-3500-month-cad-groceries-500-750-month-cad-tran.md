Toronto cost of living estimates (2023):

Rent per month: 1-bedroom shared CAD 1200-1600, 1-bedroom apartment CAD 1600-2000, 2-bedroom CAD 1800-2500, 3-bedroom CAD 2500-3500

Food: Groceries CAD 500-750/month, casual meals CAD 10-15, mid-range restaurants CAD 20-30, delivery CAD 10-20

Transportation: TTC monthly pass CAD 156, single fare CAD 3 (cash) or CAD 2.90 (Presto), Bike Share CAD 15-20/month

Other: Utilities CAD 100-200/month, phone/internet CAD 50-100/month, entertainment CAD 200-500/month

Income: Ontario minimum wage CAD 15/hour, entry-level salaries CAD 35000-50000/year
