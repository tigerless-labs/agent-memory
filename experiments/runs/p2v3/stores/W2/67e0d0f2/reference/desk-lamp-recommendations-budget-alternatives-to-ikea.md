Desk lamp alternatives recommended:

**Budget options (around 25-30 dollars):**
- AmazonBasics LED Task Lamp (approx 25 dollars)
- Target Threshold Adjustable Task Lamp (approx 30 dollars)  
- Walmart Mainstays Flexible Arm Desk Lamp (approx 25 dollars)

**Mid-range with features (35-50 dollars):**
- Anker LED Desk Lamp (approx 40 dollars) - adjustable, USB port, modern design
- TaoTronics LED Desk Lamp (approx 35 dollars) - touch-sensitive dimmer, USB port, compact
- BenQ e-Reading Desk Lamp (approx 50 dollars) - designed for reading and working, eye care focus

Key selection criteria: brightness (500-1000 lumens), adjustability, color temperature (2700K-3000K for warm tone), USB port/hub support.
