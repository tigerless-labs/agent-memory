Recommended neighborhoods in Toronto for newcomers and refugees:\n\n- **Downtown East** (St. James Town, Regent Park, Cabbagetown): High concentration of newcomer communities, refugee organizations, community centers, language schools\n- **Scarborough**: Large immigrant population, refugee communities from Africa, Asia, Middle East; Scarborough Village Recreation Centre offers newcomer services\n- **North York**: Significant refugee population; North York Community House, Centre for Information and Community Services\n- **Etobicoke**: Growing refugee community; Etobicoke Newcomer Centre, West Toronto Newcomer Services; close to Toronto Pearson Airport\n- **Parkdale**: History of welcoming refugees and immigrants; strong community sense; many social services; vibrant arts and cultural scene\n\nKey settlement organizations: The Neighbourhood Organization (TNO), COSTI Immigrant Services, WoodGreen Community Services, Refugee Sponsorship Support Program (RSSP), Toronto Refugee Centre
