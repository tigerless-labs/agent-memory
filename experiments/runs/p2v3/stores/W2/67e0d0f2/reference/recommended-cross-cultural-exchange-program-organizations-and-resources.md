Organizations and resources for cross-cultural exchange programs:

1. **AFS Intercultural Programs** (https://afs.org/) — Non-profit providing intercultural learning experiences for students. Offers youth exchanges, study abroad programs, and volunteering opportunities in 50+ countries.

2. **Experiment in International Living** (https://www.experiment.org/) — Non-profit offering immersive cultural exchange programs for high school and college students. Includes host family stays, local school attendance, and community service.

3. **Youth for Understanding (YFU)** (https://www.yfu.org/) — International educational organization providing exchange programs for students and young adults. Offers year-long exchanges, semester programs, and summer programs in 40+ countries.

4. **Cross-Cultural Solutions** (https://www.crossculturalsolutions.org/) — Non-profit providing volunteering opportunities for people of all ages in Costa Rica, Ghana, India, and Peru. Focuses on community development and cross-cultural exchange.
