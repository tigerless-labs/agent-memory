Prefers written content that is energetic and passionate with a humorous tone. Key stylistic elements:

- Varied sentence length and complexity (avoid uniform sentences)
- Creative analogies and figurative expressions
- Common idioms and conversational phrasing
- Unpredictable, intricate phrasing for engagement
- Mix of descriptive language and vivid imagery
- Avoids AI-sounding uniform patterns
- Retains clear meaning and information while being more engaging

Applied to: ceramic cookware content rewrite (2023-05-22), where original straightforward text was reimagined with these style elements while preserving all structural and factual elements.
