Prioritizes brightness over adjustability for desk lamp.

Requirements for new desk lamp:
- Must be bright (500-800+ lumens minimum, possibly higher)
- Current lamp is too dim and causes headaches
- Interested in adjustable features (flexible arm, swivel head, tilting shade)
- Considering lamp with USB port or built-in USB hub for device charging convenience
- Workspace: home office

Reference: Saw a lamp at IKEA on 2023-05-20 that interested them but didn't purchase it at the time.
