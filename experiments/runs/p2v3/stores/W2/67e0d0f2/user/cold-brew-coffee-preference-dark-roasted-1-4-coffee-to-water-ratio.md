Brewing method: Cold brew

Preferred recipe:
- Ratio: 1 part coffee to 4 parts water (e.g., 1 cup ground coffee to 4 cups water)
- Coffee type: Dark roasted (prefers bold, rich, full-bodied flavor with chocolate/caramel notes)
- Grind: Coarse grind
- Steeping time: 12-24 hours (experimenting to find preference)
- Technique: May bloom with small amount of hot water before adding rest of water

Rationale: Dark roasts work well with cold brew's low-acidity method, avoiding overpowering acidity while allowing bold flavors to shine.
