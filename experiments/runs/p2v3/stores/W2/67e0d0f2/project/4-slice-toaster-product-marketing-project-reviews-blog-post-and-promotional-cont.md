Creating marketing content for a 4-slice toaster product including:
- Promotional blog post
- Positive response templates to customer reviews
- Marketing messaging around product benefits

**Product specs:**
- 4-slice capacity, wide slots
- Digital settings with timing function
- Sleek design (matte black finish)
- Lightweight
- Can accommodate 2 long slices or 4 English muffins

**Known customer complaints:**
- Uneven toasting (top of bread doesn't toast as much)
- Gets very hot including sides
- Loud/jarring eject
- Short power cord
- Flimsy/lightweight construction
- Crumb spillage despite tray

**Customer praise:**
- Sleek design
- Wide slots
- Multiple slice capacity
- Easy-to-use digital controls
- Timing function useful for meal prep
