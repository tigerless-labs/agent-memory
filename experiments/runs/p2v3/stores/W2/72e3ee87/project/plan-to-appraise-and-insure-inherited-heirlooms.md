**Approach:**
1. Start by getting grandmother's vintage diamond necklace appraised first (likely most valuable)
2. Once necklace is appraised and insured, move on to antique music box and depression-era glassware
3. Get one appraisal per item initially; get additional appraisals only if needed later

**Appraisal Process Steps:**
1. Search online for certified appraisers in local area (NAJA, ASA certified)
2. Ask friends and family for referrals to reputable appraisers
3. Verify appraiser credentials and experience with similar items
4. Gather documentation: proof of ownership, provenance, repair/maintenance records, existing appraisals, photos/videos
5. Receive detailed appraisal report with value, methodology, photos, diagrams
6. Use appraisal reports to shop insurance companies

**Key Factors Affecting Appraisal Value:**
- Type and quality of diamonds (carat, cut, color, clarity)
- Metal type and quality
- Craftsmanship and design
- Condition (damage, wear, repairs needed)
- Rarity and uniqueness
- Market demand
- Comparable sales
