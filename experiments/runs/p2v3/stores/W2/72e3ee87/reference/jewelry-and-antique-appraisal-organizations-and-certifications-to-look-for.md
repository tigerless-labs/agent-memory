**Jewelry Appraisers:**
- National Association of Jewelry Appraisers (NAJA): Certifies jewelry appraisers
- American Gem Trade Association (AGTA): Certifies gemologists and appraisers
- American Society of Appraisers (ASA): Multi-disciplinary, includes jewelry

**Antique and Collectible Appraisers:**
- Appraisers Association of America (AAA): Certifies in antiques, art, collectibles
- International Society of Appraisers (ISA): Certifies in antiques, art, collectibles
- National Association of Collectible Appraisers (NACA): Specializes in collectibles, antiques, toys, memorabilia

**What to Look For:**
- Certification from reputable organizations
- Experience with similar items (vintage jewelry, antique music boxes, depression-era glassware)
- Impartial (not affiliated with particular jewelry stores/dealers)
- Insurance industry recognition
- Strong referrals and reputation
