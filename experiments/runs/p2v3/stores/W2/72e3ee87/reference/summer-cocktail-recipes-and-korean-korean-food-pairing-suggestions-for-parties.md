## Summer Cocktail Recipes

Classic options for summer entertaining:
- Pimm's Cup (Pimm's No. 1, lemonade, cucumber, mint, fruit)
- Watermelon Margaritas (watermelon puree, tequila, lime, agave)
- Summer Sangria (white wine, peaches, strawberries, citrus, sparkling water)
- Cranberry Lime Sparkler (cranberry juice, lime, vodka, sparkling water, mint)
- Gin-Gin Mule (ginger beer, lime, gin in copper mug)
- Spicy Pineapple Collins (pineapple juice, tequila, lime, cayenne)
- Rosé Spritz (rosé, lemon-lime soda, grenadine)
- Blackberry Basil Martini (blackberry puree, vodka, lime, basil)
- Citrus-Ginger Fizz (citrus juices, ginger liqueur, sparkling water)
- Elderflower Collins (elderflower liqueur, gin, lemon, soda water)

## Korean Dish Pairings

**For Bibimbap:**
- Soju Spritz (Soju, lime, ginger beer)
- Cucumber-Lime Refresher with ginger liqueur
- Korean Iced Tea (black tea, sugar, citrus)

**For Kimchi Tacos:**
- Spicy Mango Mule (mango puree, ginger beer, lime, chili flakes)
- Ginger-Grapefruit Collins (ginger liqueur, grapefruit juice, soda)
- Cucumber-Kiwi Cooler (cucumber, kiwi puree, lime, sparkling water)

## BBQ Sauces & Marinades

**Korean-Inspired Sauces:**
- Gochujang BBQ Sauce (gochujang, soy, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)
- Soy-Ginger BBQ Sauce (soy, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, rice vinegar)
- Korean-Style with Gochugaru (gochugaru, soy, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)

**Marinades:**
- Sesame-Ginger (sesame oil, soy, ginger, garlic, brown sugar)
- Korean BBQ with Gochujang (gochujang, soy, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)
- Cilantro-Lime (cilantro, lime juice, garlic, olive oil)

## Side Dishes

Korean-inspired:
- Kimchi Coleslaw (kimchi, cabbage, carrots, soy, lime)
- Gochujang Roasted Vegetables (broccoli/Brussels sprouts/sweet potatoes with sauce)
- Korean-Style Corn (gochujang, soy, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil)
- Cucumber Kimchi Salad (cucumbers, kimchi, red onion, cilantro, soy, lime)

Other:
- Pickled Ginger
- Cilantro Lime Rice
- Grilled Pineapple with cilantro, lime, honey
- Spicy Edamame
