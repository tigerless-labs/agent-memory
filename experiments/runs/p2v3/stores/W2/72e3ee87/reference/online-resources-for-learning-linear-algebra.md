Online resources for learning linear algebra:

**Free Resources:**
- Khan Academy's Linear Algebra Course
- MIT OpenCourseWare: Linear Algebra (free course materials with video lectures and notes)
- Linear Algebra and Its Applications by Gilbert Strang (free online textbook)
- 3Blue1Brown's Linear Algebra Series (animated video explanations on YouTube)

**Paid Courses:**
- Coursera's Linear Algebra Specialization (4-course specialization by University of Pennsylvania)
- edX's Linear Algebra and Matrix Theory (12-week course by UC Berkeley)
- Udemy's Linear Algebra for Machine Learning

**Practice:**
- Khan Academy practice problems and exercises
- MIT OpenCourseWare exercise sets
