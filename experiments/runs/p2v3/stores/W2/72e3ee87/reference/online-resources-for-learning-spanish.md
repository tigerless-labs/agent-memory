Online resources for learning Spanish:

**Platforms:**
- SpanishDict (grammar guides, verb conjugations, dictionary)
- Babbel (paid, interactive lessons for conversational skills)
- Rosetta Stone (paid, interactive lessons with audio/visual aids and speech recognition)

**Courses:**
- Coursera's Spanish for Beginners (5-course specialization by Universidad Carlos III de Madrid)
- edX's Spanish Language and Culture (4-course series by Universidad de Salamanca)

**Video & Podcasts:**
- SpanishDict YouTube Channel
- SpanishPod101 (YouTube and audio)
- Coffee Break Spanish (podcast and YouTube)
- News in Slow Spanish (podcast for intermediate learners)
- Spanish Obsessed (podcast)

**Language Exchange:**
- Conversation Exchange (find exchange partners)
- Tandem (language exchange app)
- HelloTalk (language exchange with native speakers)

**Apps & Tools:**
- Anki (flashcard app)
- Memrise (language learning app)
- Quizlet (study app with flashcards)
