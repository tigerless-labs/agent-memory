Science books recommended:
- A Short History of Nearly Everything by Bill Bryson - survey of sciences from Big Bang to civilization; accessible and witty
- The Selfish Gene by Richard Dawkins - evolutionary biology and concept of memes
- Cosmos by Carl Sagan - exploration of universe and humanity's place
- The Emperor of All Maladies by Siddhartha Mukherjee - comprehensive history of cancer research
- The Uninhabitable Earth by David Wallace-Wells - climate change and consequences

History books recommended:
- Guns, Germs, and Steel by Jared Diamond - Pulitzer Prize winner on how geography and biology shaped history
- The Sixth Extinction by Elizabeth Kolbert - account of current mass extinction
- A History of the World in 100 Objects by Neil MacGregor - human civilization through British Museum artifacts
- The Silk Roads by Peter Frankopan - world history from East-West trade connections
- Salt: A World History by Mark Kurlansky - salt's impact on human societies

Self-improvement books recommended:
- Mindset by Carol Dweck - growth mindset and personal development
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey - personal and professional development strategies
- Flow by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi - psychology of optimal experience
- The Happiness Project by Gretchen Rubin - personal experimentation in finding happiness
- Deep Work by Cal Newport - developing focus in distracted world
