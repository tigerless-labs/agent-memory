## Album: Give Up (2003)
**Artists:** Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) + Jimmy Tamborello (Dntel)

### Key Tracks to Start With
- **Such Great Heights** — dreamy, synth-driven anthem about new love
- **The District Sleeps Alone Tonight** — melancholic, introspective; about disconnection and insomnia
- **Clark Gable** — quirky, upbeat; charming lyrics about wooing someone
- **We Will Become Silhouettes** — somber, reflective; nuclear apocalypse theme
- **Recycled Air** — prominent drum beat and acoustic guitar
- **Sleeping In** — vocal layering and harmonies, orchestral complexity

### Distinctive Elements
- Acoustic instruments blended with electronic beats (guitar, drums + synthesizers)
- Rich vocal layering and counterpoint harmonies
- Postal-related imagery woven through lyrics
- Genre-defining indie-electronic sound

### Context
Contemporary artists experimenting with similar indie-electronic fusions: Death Cab for Cutie, The Shins, Modest Mouse, Aphex Twin, Four Tet, Boards of Canada.
