**Specialty Insurance Companies:**
- Jewelers Mutual: Specialized in jewelry, vintage and antique pieces
- Chubb: High-value items including jewelry, art, collectibles
- AXA ART: Art, antiques, collectibles, jewelry, rare items
- Hagerty: Classic car insurance plus collectibles (jewelry, art, antiques)

**Standard Companies with Specialty Departments:**
- State Farm: High-value items department (jewelry, antiques)
- Allstate: Valuable Items policy (jewelry, art, collectibles)
- Liberty Mutual: Valuable Possessions policy (jewelry, art, antiques)

**Policy Features to Look For:**
- Agreed value coverage (pays insured amount in case of loss/damage)
- No deductible or low deductible
- Worldwide coverage
- Coverage for mysterious disappearance/loss
- Read exclusions carefully
