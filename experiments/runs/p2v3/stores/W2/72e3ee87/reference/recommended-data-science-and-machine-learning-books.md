Books recommended for data science and machine learning learning path:

**Foundational:**
- 'Python Machine Learning' by Sebastian Raschka
- 'Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow' by Aurélien Géron
- 'Data Science for Dummies' by Lillian Pierson

**Math & Statistics:**
- 'Linear Algebra and Its Applications' by Gilbert Strang
- 'Probability and Statistics for Data Science' by Jim Henley
- 'Calculus for Data Science' by Stefan Waner and Steven R. Costenoble

**Deep Learning:**
- 'Deep Learning' by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville
- 'Deep Learning with Python' by François Chollet

**Specialized:**
- 'Natural Language Processing (almost) from Scratch' by Collobert et al.
- 'Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning' by Christopher Bishop
- 'Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction' by Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto

**Soft Skills:**
- 'Data Science Handbook' by Jake VanderPlas
- 'Building Data Science Teams' by DJ Patil
