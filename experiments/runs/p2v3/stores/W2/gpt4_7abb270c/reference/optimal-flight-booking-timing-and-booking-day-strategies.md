**Best booking window:**
- Cheapest: 54 days before departure (on average)
- Prime window: 21-121 days before departure (prices within 5% of lowest)

**Best days to book:**
- Avoid: Tuesday, Wednesday, Saturday (peak booking days)
- Choose: Monday, Thursday, Friday (off-peak)

**Other timing tips:**
- Book early morning or late evening flights for lower prices
- Consider off-peak seasons (mid-Jan to mid-Mar, Apr-May, Sep-Nov)
- Monitor price trends continuously
- Look for error fares/mistake fares
- Set up fare alerts (Airfarewatchdog, The Flight Deal, Secret Flying)
