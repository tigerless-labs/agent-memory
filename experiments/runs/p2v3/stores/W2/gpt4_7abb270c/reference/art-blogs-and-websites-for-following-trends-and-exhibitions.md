**Art platforms and publications:**\n- Artsy — comprehensive online platform for art, news, exhibitions, artist profiles, market trends\n- Hyperallergic — leading art blog covering contemporary art news, reviews, opinion (focus on NYC)\n- Artnet — art market news, auction results, exhibitions, emerging artists\n- The Art Newspaper — daily international art news, exhibitions, market analysis\n- Contemporary Art Daily — discovering new artists and exhibitions, contemporary focus\n- Art in America — contemporary art magazine with features, reviews, interviews\n- Frieze — art magazine covering contemporary art, culture, politics, international exhibitions\n- Apollo Magazine — British art magazine covering art, architecture, collecting\n- Art21 — nonprofit producing documentaries and online content on contemporary art\n- Culture Trip — art, culture, travel coverage with focus on emerging artists and underground scenes
