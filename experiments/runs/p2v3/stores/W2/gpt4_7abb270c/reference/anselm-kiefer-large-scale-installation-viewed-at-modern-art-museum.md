Anselm Kiefer is a German artist known for large-scale installations and sculptures exploring themes of history, mythology, and the human condition. Viewed one of his installations at the Modern Art Museum and was moved by the work. While his practice extends beyond abstract expressionism, it shares the movement's emphasis on scale, materiality, and emotional intensity.
