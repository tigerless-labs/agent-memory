**Packing strategies:**
- Roll clothes instead of folding to save space and reduce wrinkles
- Use packing cubes to organize and find items quickly
- Pack versatile pieces that mix and match
- Wear bulkiest items on the plane
- Fill every empty space in luggage
- Decant toiletries into travel-sized containers

**Staying productive while traveling:**
- Download content before flying (movies, podcasts, audiobooks)
- Divide work into small chunks with breaks
- Use layovers for power naps or meal breaks
- Research destination during flight time
- Keep work essentials (laptop, power bank) easily accessible

**Staying connected:**
- Video calls (Zoom, Skype, FaceTime) for holidays
- Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Messenger)
- Schedule calls in advance to coordinate timing
