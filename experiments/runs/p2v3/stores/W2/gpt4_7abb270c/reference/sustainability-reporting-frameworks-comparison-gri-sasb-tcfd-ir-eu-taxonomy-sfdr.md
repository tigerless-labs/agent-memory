Comparison of major ESG/sustainability reporting frameworks:

**Voluntary Frameworks:**
- **GRI Standards** (Global Reporting Initiative): Comprehensive sustainability reporting, no mandated format
- **SASB Standards** (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board): Industry-specific materiality, no mandated format  
- **TCFD** (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures): Climate-related risks/opportunities, voluntary but increasingly adopted by governments and investors, recommends disclosure in mainstream financial filings
- **IR Framework** (International Integrated Reporting Council): Integrated reporting approach, voluntary

**Mandatory Regulations:**
- **EU Taxonomy Regulation**: Mandatory for companies subject to NFRD and SFDR; requires alignment with EU Taxonomy classification system for climate-related risks
- **SFDR** (Sustainability Finance Disclosure Regulation): Mandatory for financial market participants and advisers; requires pre-contractual and periodic disclosures on sustainability of financial products
- **CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive): Mandatory for large EU companies; uses ESEF/Inline XBRL format; reporting from 2026 onwards

**Complementarity:** CDP's climate framework aligns with TCFD; NFRD encourages use of GRI, SASB, TCFD for quality/comparability
