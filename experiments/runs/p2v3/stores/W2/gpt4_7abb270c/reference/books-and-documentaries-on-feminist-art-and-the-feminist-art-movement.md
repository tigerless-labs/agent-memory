**Books:**\n- "The Feminist Art Movement" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard — comprehensive history from 1960s to 1990s\n- "Feminist Art and the Maternal" by Andrea Liss — explores relationship between feminist art and maternal, featuring Mary Kelly and Mierle Laderman Ukeles\n- "Women, Art, and Power" by Linda Nochlin — essays on women's representation in art and feminism in art history\n- "The Power of Feminist Art: The American Movement of the 1970s" by Norma Broude and Mary Garrard — detailed US 1970s focus\n- "Guerrilla Girls: The Art of Behaving Badly" by Guerrilla Girls — essays and artworks from the collective\n\n**Documentaries:**\n- "!Women Art Revolution" (2010, dir. Lynn Hershman Leeson) — interviews with 40+ feminist artists including Judy Chicago, Yoko Ono, Carolee Schneemann\n- "The Heretics" (2009) — Heresies Collective with Nancy Azara, Miriam Schapiro\n- "Womart: The Women's Art Movement" (1991, dir. Kate Horsfield) — interviews with Mary Kelly, Barbara Kruger, Yvonne Rainer\n- "Feminist Art in the 1970s: Conversations Across Generations" (2010) — panel with Martha Rosler, Mierle Laderman Ukeles, Howardena Pindell, moderated by Huey Copeland\n- "Art:21 - Judy Chicago" (2014) — PBS series episode on Judy Chicago
