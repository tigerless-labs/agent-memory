**Meta-search/comparison engines:**
- Skyscanner: powerful search across airlines and travel agencies
- Google Flights: user-friendly with price trends and fare comparisons
- Kayak: searches hundreds of sites for flights and hotels
- Momondo: specializes in error fares and sales
- Airfarewatchdog: fare sale alerts and exclusive deals

**Accommodation:**
- Booking.com, Airbnb, HotelsCombined, Trivago, Hostelworld

**Package deals:**
- Expedia, Orbitz, Priceline
