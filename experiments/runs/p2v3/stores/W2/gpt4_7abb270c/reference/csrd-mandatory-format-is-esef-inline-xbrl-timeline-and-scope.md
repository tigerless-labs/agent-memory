The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) is the EU's revised sustainability reporting requirement:

**Required Format:**
- European Single Electronic Format (ESEF) based on Inline XBRL (iXBRL)
- Same format used for financial reporting in EU
- Makes sustainability info comparable and accessible to investors/stakeholders

**Timeline:**
- Framework introduced: 2024
- Mandatory reporting required from: 2026 onwards

**Scope:**
- Proposed to be mandatory for ALL large companies operating in the EU (broader than NFRD)
- Expands beyond listed/public-interest entities to private large companies

**Alignment:**
- CSRD complements and expands upon NFRD requirements
- Details of specific data points still being developed through consultation
