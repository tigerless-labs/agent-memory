4K OLED TV comparison evaluated 2022-12-22\n\n**LG OLED**\n- Panel quality: Perfect blacks, infinite contrast, wide viewing angles\n- webOS platform: User-friendly, wide app selection\n- Color: Wide color gamut, vivid and lifelike\n- HDR: Dolby Vision, HDR10, HLG support\n- Gaming: Low input lag\n- Price: Generally more affordable\n- Popular models: C9, B9, CX, GX series\n\n**Sony OLED**\n- Panel quality: Excellent, perfect blacks, wide viewing angles\n- Android TV platform: Built-in Google Assistant\n- Audio: Acoustic Surface Audio (unique feature)\n- Processor: X1 Ultimate with object-based HDR remastering\n- Gaming: Low input lag + HDMI 2.1 + VRR support\n- Price: Generally more expensive\n- Popular models: A9G, A8G, A1E, A9S series\n\n**Key difference**: LG favored for ease-of-use and value; Sony favored for advanced gaming features.
