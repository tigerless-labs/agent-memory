The Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) applies to large companies that are public-interest entities with:
- More than 500 employees AND
- Balance sheet total over €20 million OR net turnover over €40 million

Public-interest entities include:
- Companies whose securities are admitted to trading on a regulated market in the EU
- Credit institutions
- Insurance undertakings

EU member states have discretion to extend scope to additional companies or add stricter requirements.

NFRD does not prescribe a mandatory reporting format, but encourages use of recognized frameworks (GRI, SASB, TCFD) for comparability and quality.
