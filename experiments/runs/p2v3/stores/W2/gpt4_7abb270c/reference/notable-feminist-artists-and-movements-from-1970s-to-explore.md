Key feminist artists and figures recommended for exploration following interest sparked by Dr. Maria Rodriguez's lecture:

**Pioneer Artists:**
- **Judy Chicago** - Known for 'The Dinner Party', pioneering feminist art exploring gender, identity, and female experience
- **Miriam Schapiro** - Incorporated fabric, textiles, and materials to challenge traditional notions of art and femininity
- **Carolee Schneemann** - Multi-disciplinary artist (performance, film, installation) exploring gender, sexuality, and the body

**Movements & Collectives:**
- **The Guerrilla Girls** - Anonymous collective of female artists using provocative and irreverent tactics to challenge male-dominated art world power structures
- **The Feminist Art Program** - Founded 1970 at California Institute of the Arts, first major platform for women artists to explore feminist themes

**Contemporary Feminist Artists:**
- **Barbara Kruger** - Bold, graphic images and text-based works critiquing consumer culture and gender roles
- **Cindy Sherman** - Pioneering photographer and filmmaker exploring construction of identity, gender, and representation
- **Laurie Anderson** - Performance artist, musician, filmmaker incorporating storytelling, technology, and multimedia exploring identity, politics, and human experience

The 1970s feminist art movement was pivotal in challenging traditional gender representations and power structures in the art world.
