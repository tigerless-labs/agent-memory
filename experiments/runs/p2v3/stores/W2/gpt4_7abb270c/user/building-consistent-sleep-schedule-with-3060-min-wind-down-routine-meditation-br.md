## Sleep & relaxation goal

**Evening wind-down ritual (30–60 min before bed):**
- Meditation or deep breathing exercises
- Reading (poetry/nature writing preferred)
- Warm herbal tea (chamomile, peppermint, valerian root)
- Dim lights; no screens 2–3 hours before bed

**Sleep environment:**
- Dark, quiet, cool (60–67°F)
- Consistent bedtime & wake time (including weekends)

**Also seeking:** Healthy lunch options (protein-focused) to avoid afternoon energy crashes—wants to maintain energy without post-lunch slump.
