## Reading preferences

**For wind-down/relaxation:**
- Nature writing: "Walden" (Thoreau), "Pilgrim at Tinker Creek" (Dillard), "The Hidden Life of Trees" (Wohlleben)
- Poetry: Mary Oliver (starting choice), Emily Dickinson, Walt Whitman  
- Light fiction: "The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society" (Shaffer), "The Elegance of the Hedgehog" (Barbery), "The Unlikely Pilgrimage of Harold Fry" (Joyce)
- Essays: Orwell, Hemingway, Sedaris

**Ongoing:** Historical fiction, mythology retellings, character-driven narratives (from previous sessions)

Incorporates 20–30 min reading into evening wind-down before bed.
