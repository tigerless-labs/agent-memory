Exploring themes of identity, memory, and the human experience through multi-sensory interactive installations. Interested in recreating sensations of specific environments or memories using sound, light, texture, and scent. Also exploring collective memory and how individual experiences connect to shared cultural and historical contexts. Technologies of interest: spatial audio, projection mapping, scent diffusion, haptic feedback, AR, data visualization.
