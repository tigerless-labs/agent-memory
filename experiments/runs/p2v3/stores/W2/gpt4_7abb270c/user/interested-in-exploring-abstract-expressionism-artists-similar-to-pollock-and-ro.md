Particularly interested in Jackson Pollock and Mark Rothko's works. Also wants to explore other abstract expressionists including:
- Willem de Kooning (known for bold, expressive brushstrokes and vibrant color)
- Franz Kline (large-scale, bold gestural brushstrokes evoking energy)
- Clyfford Still (large abstract forms, muted palette, exploring the sublime)
- Ad Reinhardt
- Barnett Newman
- Robert Motherwell

The movement's emphasis on large scale, emotional intensity, and the conveyance of meaning through abstract forms is particularly compelling.
