Essay project researching ancient Egyptian civilization. Topics explored include Pharaoh Tutankhamun's golden mask (discovery 1922 by Howard Carter, housed at Egyptian Museum Cairo, 11kg solid gold with lapis lazuli/carnelian/turquoise inlay), ankh symbol (eternal life, union of male/female, balance between physical/spiritual), mummification process (70 days, natron dehydration, organ removal, wrapping in linen), and scarab beetle symbolism (regeneration, transformation, protection, associated with sun god Khepri).
