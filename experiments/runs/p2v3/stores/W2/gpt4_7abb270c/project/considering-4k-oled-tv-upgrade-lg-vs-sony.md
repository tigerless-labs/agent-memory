Researched 4K OLED TVs from LG and Sony as of 2022-12-22.\n\n**LG OLED**: webOS platform (user-friendly), perfect blacks, wide color gamut, supports Dolby Vision/HDR10/HLG, low input lag for gaming, slightly more affordable.\n- Popular models: C9, B9, CX, GX series\n\n**Sony OLED**: Android TV platform, X1 Ultimate processor, Acoustic Surface Audio, HDMI 2.1 + VRR for gaming, excellent picture processing.\n- Popular models: A9G, A8G, A1E, A9S series\n\nLeaning slightly toward LG for webOS simplicity and affordability, but both are excellent options.
