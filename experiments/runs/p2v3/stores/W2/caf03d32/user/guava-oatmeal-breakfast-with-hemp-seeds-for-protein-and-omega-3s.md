Developed breakfast routine combining oatmeal with guava and hemp seeds for high protein and fiber content.\n\n**Recipe ratio:**\n- 1/2 cup rolled oats\n- 1/4 cup diced fresh guava (1-2 guavas)\n- 1-2 tablespoons hemp seeds per serving (5-10g protein, 1-2g omega-3s)\n- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk\n- 1 tablespoon chia seeds\n- Optional: honey, banana, nuts, cinnamon\n\n**Preparation:**\n- Mix ground hemp seeds with liquid first to create paste\n- Add to cooked oatmeal with guava and toppings\n- Store hemp seeds in airtight container in fridge/freezer\n\n**Nutritional benefits:**\n- Hemp seeds: complete protein, omega-3 fatty acids\n- Guava: high in protein and fiber for tropical fruit\n- Oatmeal: complex carbohydrates and fiber
