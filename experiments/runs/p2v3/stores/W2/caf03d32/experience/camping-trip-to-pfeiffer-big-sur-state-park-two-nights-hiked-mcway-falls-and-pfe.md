Spent two nights camping at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park. First day hiked to McWay Falls, second day did a shorter hike to Pfeiffer Beach. Tried cooking camping food for the first time on the trip - it was okay but wants to improve. Used a portable power bank which was very helpful for keeping devices charged for navigation and capturing photos. Attempted astrophotography at night but results were not as good as hoped - still learning night photography and wants to improve skills for future trips.
