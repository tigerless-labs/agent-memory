Soaking Cashews:
1. Rinse 1 cup of raw cashews in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water
2. Soak in water for at least 4 hours or overnight in a bowl or container
3. After soaking, drain and rinse cashews again
4. (Optional) Soak in fresh water for another 4 hours or overnight for extra softness

Blending Cashews:
1. Drain and rinse the soaked cashews
2. Add 1/2 cup fresh water to a blender with the drained cashews
3. Blend on high speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and creamy
4. Stop and scrape down sides as needed to ensure even mixing

Straining Options:
- Cheesecloth: High filtration but can be messy and requires squeezing
- Nut milk bag: Purpose-designed with fine mesh but can be pricey
- Fine-mesh sieve: Easy to use and clean but may not catch all particles

Tips for straining: Use clean, sanitized equipment. Squeeze mixture through strainer to extract maximum liquid. Twist cheesecloth or gently squeeze nut milk bag to avoid pushing particles through.

Consistency Guidelines:
- Use 1:1 ratio of cashews to water (adjust to preference)
- For thicker yogurt: use less water, blend shorter time
- For thinner yogurt: use more water, blend longer
- Chill in refrigerator for at least 2 hours to thicken and set
