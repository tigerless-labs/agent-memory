Popular vegan yogurt alternatives for slow cooker yogurt making:

1. **Soy yogurt** — Brands: Silk, So Delicious
2. **Coconut yogurt** — Brands: So Delicious, Coconut Bliss, Coyo (creamy and rich)
3. **Almond yogurt** — Brands: Almond Breeze, Silk, So Delicious (low-calorie, low-fat)
4. **Cashew yogurt** — Can find from So Delicious or make at home from blended cashews (creamy and indulgent)
5. **Oat yogurt** — Brands: Oatly, So Delicious (good for nut allergies)
6. **Rice yogurt** — Brands: Rice Dream, So Delicious (hypoallergenic, easy to digest)
7. **Hemp yogurt** — Brands: Good Karma, Elmhurst 1925 (high in protein and omega-3s)
8. **Flax yogurt** — Brands: Good Karma or homemade (low-calorie, high-fiber)

**Important:** Select unflavored and unsweetened varieties to avoid extra sugars. Always check ingredient lists and nutrition labels for dietary needs.
