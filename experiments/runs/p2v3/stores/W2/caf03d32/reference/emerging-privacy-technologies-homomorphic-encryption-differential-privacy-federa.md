**Homomorphic encryption**: Allows computations on encrypted data without decryption; still in early stages with high computational costs, expected to improve with computing power increases\n**Differential privacy**: Injects random noise into datasets to prevent re-identification attacks; already used by Apple and Google\n**Federated learning**: Trains ML models on distributed user/device data without moving data to central location\n**Blockchain**: Decentralized ledger for secure, transparent data sharing with authorized access only\nExpected to see increased adoption as privacy concerns grow and technologies mature.
