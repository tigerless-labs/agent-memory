Slow Cooker Yogurt Recipe

Ingredients:
- 1/2 gallon milk (dairy or plant-based: soy, coconut, almond, oat, cashew, rice, hemp, or flax)
- 1/4 cup plain yogurt with live cultures (store-bought or previous batch) or vegan yogurt starter/probiotic powder
- Optional: sweetener (honey, maple syrup, coconut sugar, date syrup, monk fruit, stevia) and flavorings (vanilla, cinnamon, fruit, nutmeg, ginger, lemon, cocoa)

Instructions:

1. **Heat the milk** (2-3 hours on low): Pour milk into slow cooker and heat until reaching 180-185°F (82-85°C). This kills unwanted bacteria and denatures proteins.
   - Using a thermometer is highly recommended for consistency and food safety
   - Without thermometer: Heat until milk starts to simmer or bubbles form around edges

2. **Cool the milk** (1-2 hours): Turn off slow cooker and let milk cool to 110-120°F (43-49°C) before adding cultures

3. **Add starter culture**: Whisk in plain yogurt or starter powder gently

4. **Incubate** (8-12 hours or overnight): Cover slow cooker, wrap with towel or blanket to maintain consistent temperature. Longer incubation = thicker and tangier yogurt.

5. **Chill and store**: Refrigerate yogurt to chill, then transfer to airtight containers

Additional Options:
- Make Greek yogurt: strain through cheesecloth to remove excess liquid
- Add flavorings: incorporate during incubation or after chilling
- Add toppings: granola, nuts, seeds, dried fruit, cocoa nibs
