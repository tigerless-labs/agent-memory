Flavorings for Slow Cooker Yogurt:
- Vanilla: use vanilla extract or vanilla bean
- Cinnamon: adds warm, spicy flavor
- Fruit: berries, mango, peaches create sweet and fruity taste
- Nutmeg: warm, aromatic flavor
- Ginger: fresh grated for spicy, invigorating taste
- Lemon: juice or zest for bright, citrusy flavor
- Cocoa: cocoa powder or melted chocolate

Sweeteners:
- Honey: natural sweetness
- Maple syrup: rich, velvety sweetness
- Coconut sugar: subtle, caramel-like sweetness
- Date syrup: sweet, molasses-like flavor
- Monk fruit sweetener: 150-200 times sweeter than sugar (use sparingly)
- Stevia: natural powdered option

Other Add-ins:
- Granola: store-bought or homemade for crunch
- Nuts or seeds: almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flaxseeds for texture and nutrition
- Cocoa nibs: chocolatey, crunchy texture
- Dried fruit: cranberries, raisins, apricots for natural sweetness and chewiness

Note: Can always experiment with different combinations to create perfect custom yogurt
