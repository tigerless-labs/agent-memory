Sample 3-day home strength training routine (no special equipment needed):

**Monday - Upper Body:**
- Push-ups: 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Incline dumbbell press (using water bottles/cans): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Bent-over dumbbell rows (using water bottles/cans): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Tricep dips (using chair or bench): 3 sets × 10-12 reps

**Wednesday - Lower Body:**
- Squats: 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Calf raises: 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Lunges: 3 sets × 10-12 reps per leg
- Glute bridges: 3 sets × 10-12 reps

**Friday - Core:**
- Plank: 3 sets × 30-60 seconds
- Russian twists (using weight or water bottle): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Leg raises (lying on back): 3 sets × 10-12 reps
- Bicycle crunches: 3 sets × 10-12 reps

**Household weights:**
- Water bottles or cans (1-2 liters) as dumbbells
- Backpack or bag filled with books or weights
- Resistance bands

**Key principles:**
- Always warm up 5-10 minutes before and cool down 5-10 minutes after
- Start with lighter weights, gradually increase intensity
- Focus on proper form and technique
- Treat workouts as non-negotiable appointments
- Vary routine every 4-6 weeks to avoid plateaus
- Use progressive overload (gradually increase weight, reps, or sets)
