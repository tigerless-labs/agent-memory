Audiobook Platforms and Services reviewed and recommended

Paid Subscription:
- Audible: 500,000 titles, 14.95/month for 1 credit
- Libro.fm: Supports independent bookstores, 14.99/month for 1 credit  
- Scribd: 8.99/month unlimited access to audiobooks, ebooks, documents
- Audiobooks.com: 14.95/month for 1 credit
- Hibooks: Curated selection, 12.99/month for 1 credit
- Kobo Audiobooks: Wide selection with deals

Free/Library-Based:
- Hoopla Digital: Free with library card; audiobooks, ebooks, music, movies, TV
- OverDrive: Free with library card; borrow from public libraries

Other:
- Google Play Books: Digital bookstore with deals
- Audiobooksync: Free summer program; 2 free audiobooks/week for 13 weeks

All have mobile apps for listening on-the-go.
