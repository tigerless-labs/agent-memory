**Injury Prevention Strategies:**
1. Warm up and cool down: 10-15 minutes light cardio + dynamic stretching before; static stretches after
2. Stretch and foam roll regularly to improve flexibility and reduce muscle tension
3. Strengthen core and ankles (planks, side planks, ankle circles) to improve balance and stability
4. Use proper gear and equipment appropriate for your activity
5. Listen to your body - stop if fatigued or in pain; don't push too hard
6. Gradually increase workout intensity and volume to avoid overuse injuries

**Injury Recovery Strategies (Acute Injuries):**
- **RICE**: Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation
- Gentle stretching and mobilization exercises for recovery
- Use compression wraps/sleeves to reduce swelling
- Elevation to promote blood flow and reduce inflammation
- Take rest days to allow body recovery
- Seek professional help for chronic pain or severe injuries

**Ankle Sprain Specific Tips:**
1. Strengthen ankles with ankle circles, calf raises, single-leg balancing
2. Use ankle braces or supports during high-impact activities
3. Improve balance and proprioception with single-leg squats and balance boards
4. Focus on proper form to avoid future injuries
