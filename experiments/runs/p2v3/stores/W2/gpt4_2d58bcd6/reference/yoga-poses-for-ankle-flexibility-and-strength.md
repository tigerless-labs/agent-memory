Recommended yoga poses to improve ankle flexibility and strength:

1. **Ankle Circles**: Sitting with legs straight; lift one foot and draw circles clockwise and counterclockwise (5-10 reps each direction)
2. **Toe Spreads**: Sitting with legs straight; spread toes as far apart as possible, then bring together (10-15 reps per foot)
3. **Ankle Alphabet**: Sitting with legs straight; lift one foot and draw alphabet with toes from A-Z
4. **Calf Raises**: Stand on edge of stair with heels hanging off; raise onto tiptoes and lower (10-15 reps)
5. **Downward-Facing Dog**: Start on all fours, walk hands forward, lift hips back and up; hold 30 seconds to 1 minute
6. **Tree Pose**: Stand on one leg with other foot on inner thigh; hold 30 seconds to 1 minute
7. **Eagle Pose**: Stand with feet hip-width apart, bend knees, cross one leg wrapping ankle around calf; hold 30 seconds to 1 minute
8. **Seated Forward Fold**: Sit with legs straight, fold forward reaching toward toes/shins; hold 30 seconds to 1 minute

**Ankle mobility exercises** to incorporate daily:
- Ankle rotations (both directions, 5-10 reps)
- Toe curls (10-15 reps)
- Heel raises (10-15 reps)

Key advice: Listen to body, warm up before yoga, modify poses if uncomfortable, be consistent and patient for improvements.
