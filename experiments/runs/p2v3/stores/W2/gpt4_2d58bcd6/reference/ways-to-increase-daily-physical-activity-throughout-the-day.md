Practical strategies to increase movement and activity during the day:

**At Work/Office:**
- Take stairs instead of elevator
- Do desk exercises: chair squats, desk push-ups, leg lifts
- Walk to coworker's desk instead of sending email/message
- Walk during phone calls (if feasible)
- Suggest walking meetings with colleagues

**Getting Around:**
- Park farther away from destination to add walking
- Try active commuting (bike, bus, train) when possible
- Bike to errands (like grocery shopping) instead of driving

**Home/Daily Life:**
- Turn household chores into workouts (music, higher intensity)
- Play active games with kids (tag, hide-and-seek, soccer)
- Take breaks throughout day to stretch

**Tracking & Motivation:**
- Use fitness tracker to monitor daily steps and set goals
- Look for lunchtime fitness classes near work
- Find activities that work for you personally

**Key principle:** Every bit counts; small increases in daily movement add up significantly over time.
