Common daily tarot reading spreads:
- **Single Card Spread**: Draw one card for insight into the day ahead
- **Three-Card Spread**: Past, Present, Future interpretation
- **Celtic Cross Spread**: 10-card complex spread, adaptable for daily focus
- **Daily Focus Spread**: Overall energy, what to focus on, expected outcome
- **Mind, Body, Spirit Spread**: Mental state, physical state, spiritual state

Card interpretations: reversed cards often indicate blockages or challenges; Two of Pentacles reversed suggests overwhelm/scattered thinking; Hierophant reversed suggests rebellion against routine; Empress reversed suggests creative blockage or lack of spiritual nourishment.
