- **Gym workouts**: Member of a gym, joined for structured guidance and accountability
- **Morning yoga**: Practices light yoga poses in the morning to improve flexibility
- **Home strength training**: Does bodyweight exercises (push-ups, squats, lunges, planks) at home to supplement gym routine
- **Daily activity**: Takes short walks during lunch breaks at work; rides bike to grocery store on weekends instead of driving
- **Injury prevention focus**: Very interested in injury prevention strategies and recovery techniques

Prefers using household items (water bottles, backpacks with books) as weights rather than investing in equipment. Values structured routines but also incorporates variety and consistency. Seeks guidance on proper form and evidence-based practices.
