Creative fiction exploring characters in a sci-fi universe.

## Characters
- **Val**: protagonist/narrator
- **Odyl**: Val's connection, from Xirin people

## Story arc
1. **Initial encounter**: Odyl has Val tied up during a covert mission (part of a ruse). Odyl whispers 'good girl' in Val's ear, calls her 'Dearie', and kisses her briefly—all ostensibly part of the scheme, but emotionally charged and unexpected.

2. **Brief follow-up encounter**: Later, Odyl pulls Val into a hallway kiss that's even more passionate than the first. She disappears afterward, leaving Val breathless and confused about what it all means.

3. **The Unnamed City mission**: Month-long mission to an abandoned city on the Xirin homeworld (Odyl's people). Working closely together, they grow emotionally intimate—sharing vulnerabilities, hopes, and fears. The final night together is intensely passionate, though feelings remain largely unspoken despite their depth.

## Narrative preferences
- Romance with emotional vulnerability and complexity
- Slow-burn development with underlying tension
- Unspoken feelings that resonate powerfully
- Character-driven storytelling focused on relationship depth
- Connection through shared danger and trust
