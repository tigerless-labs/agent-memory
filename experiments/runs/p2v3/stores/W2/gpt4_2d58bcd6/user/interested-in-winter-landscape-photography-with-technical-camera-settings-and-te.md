Focuses on technical aspects of winter photography including camera mode (manual), ISO 100-400, aperture f/8-f/11, shutter speed 1/125s-1/250s, white balance (Cloudy/Shade mode). Uses techniques: overexposure by +1/3 to +1 stop for snow, polarizing filters for glare reduction, graduated ND filters, bracketing for HDR, RAW format shooting. Interested in capturing snowflakes, animal tracks, texture details, and composition strategies like leading lines and low angles.
