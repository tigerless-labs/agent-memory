**Watering Schedule:**
- Summer: every 1-2 weeks
- Winter: every 4-6 weeks
- Water when soil feels dry to the touch
- Avoid overwatering to prevent root rot
- Avoid getting water on leaves to prevent fungal disease

**Fertilization:**
- Use balanced, water-soluble fertilizer (20-20-20)
- Fertilize once a month during growing season (spring and summer)
- Dilute to half-strength to avoid burning roots
- Skip fertilizing in winter when plant is dormant

**Additional care:**
- Prefers well-draining soil
- Thrives in low to medium indirect light; avoid direct sunlight
- Prune to maintain shape and encourage growth
- Toxic to pets and humans if ingested
