Buddhist cultural park located on the outskirts of Nanjing, Jiangsu Province.

**Features:**
- Large Buddhist temple complex with pagodas
- Modern Buddhist museum
- Giant statue of the Buddha
- Ancient temples and gardens
- Hiking trails and mountain walks

**Seasonal appeal:**
- Summer: Pleasant mountain scenery with lush vegetation
- Winter: Serene, peaceful atmosphere with clear views

**Visitor experience:** Educational and cultural visits, temple exploration, scenic walks suitable for couples and families. User has expressed specific interest in including this site in Nanjing itineraries.
