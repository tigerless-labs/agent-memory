When faced with disorganized spaces, starts by purging expired and unused items. Then applies organizational systems like dividers, baskets, labels, and strategic storage. Sees this as iterative: experiment with layouts and products until finding what works. Also interested in using often-wasted spaces like the back of cabinet doors for storage.
