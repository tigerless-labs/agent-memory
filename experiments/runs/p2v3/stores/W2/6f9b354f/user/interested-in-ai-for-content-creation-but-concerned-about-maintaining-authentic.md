**Interest:** Using AI-powered tools for social media content creation and topic research, especially to overcome idea generation challenges.

**Concern:** Quality and authenticity of AI-generated content; ensuring it sounds like coming from the company/brand, not robotic.

**Preferred approach:**
- Define brand voice and tone guide as the first step
- Use AI as a starting point/inspiration, then refine with human creativity
- Always review and curate AI-generated content before publishing
- Add personal anecdotes, humor, emotional connections to make content relatable
- Blend AI-generated content with human-created content
- Consider disclosing when content is AI-generated for transparency
- Use AI for research (topic suggestions, keyword research) rather than just final content generation

**Key principle:** AI should augment, not replace, human creativity and judgment.
