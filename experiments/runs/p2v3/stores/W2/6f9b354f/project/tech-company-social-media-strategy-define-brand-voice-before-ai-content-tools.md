Plan to improve company's online presence in tech industry.

**Upcoming meeting:** With marketing expert for social media strategy review.

**AI content approach:**
1. Define brand voice and tone guide first
2. Use AI tools as starting point, not final product
3. Always curate and review AI-generated content before publishing
4. Edit and refine to sound more human/authentic
5. Mix AI-generated content with human-created content
6. Disclose AI-generated content when appropriate

**Tools to evaluate:**
- BuzzSumo (topic suggestions, content performance)
- Ahrefs (content explorer, keyword research, competitor analysis)
- Answer Engine (AI Q&A insights)
- Quora (community-driven content inspiration)
- Google Keyword Planner, Moz Keyword Explorer, Ubersuggest

**Key concern:** Maintaining authenticity and brand voice when using AI content generation.
