## Word/spelling corrections
- somewaht → somewhat
- Ayn Land → Ayn Rand (proper noun)

## Sentence correction example
**Error:** Ayn Land thinks that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must take care of ourselves to live in a somewaht "selfish" manner.

**Correct:** Ayn Rand believed that nature does not guarantee our survival, so we must prioritize our own interests in order to live, which some might consider a somewhat 'selfish' way of thinking.
