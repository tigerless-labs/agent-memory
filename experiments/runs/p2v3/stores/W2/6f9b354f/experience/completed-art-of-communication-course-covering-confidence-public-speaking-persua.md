Completed an Art of Communication course that covered:

**Confidence & self-esteem:**
- Building confidence and self-esteem
- Overcoming glossophobia (fear of public speaking)
- Different confidence frameworks for managing nervousness

**Communication techniques:**
- Understanding audience structure and tailoring messages accordingly
- Articulation frameworks for clear and effective speaking
- Active listening and conversation skills
- Nonverbal communication (body language, tone of voice)

**Professional skills:**
- Persuasion techniques for presenting ideas convincingly
- Delivering speeches with impact and engagement
- Personal branding and LinkedIn profile maintenance
- Networking strategies

**Key insight:**
Learned that effective communication is a skill that can be developed with practice and effort, not just a natural talent. Communication is a two-way process requiring active listening and understanding, and success requires effort, patience, and practice.
