Planning to expand product line to include more artisanal soaps, based on:
- Recent sales performance and customer feedback
- Existing customer base appreciation for homemade products
- Strawberry jam as a best-seller indicates strong demand for artisanal goods

Areas to evaluate before expansion:
- Customer demand and feedback for specific soap types
- Market trends in artisanal soaps
- Competitor offerings
- Production capabilities and resources
- Equipment and ingredient sourcing
- Pricing strategy and profit margins
