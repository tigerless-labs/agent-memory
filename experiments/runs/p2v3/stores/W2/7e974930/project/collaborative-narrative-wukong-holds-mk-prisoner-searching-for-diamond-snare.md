## Story Setup
Character: MK — protagonist, imprisoned by Wukong; knows stories about the diamond snare but not its location
Character: Wukong — Monkey King, seeking the diamond snare; tortures then imprisons MK
Antagonistic dynamic: Wukong is cruel and dismissive; MK desperate to survive

## Plot Points Developed
- MK convinces Wukong to keep him alive by offering himself as 'insurance'
- Wukong responds with alternating cruelty (starvation) and minimal sustenance
- Another monkey brings MK fruit and water
- MK experiences cognitive dissonance: the heroic Wukong of legend vs. the cruel captor
- Wukong hosts feasts with other monkeys; MK tries unsuccessfully to escape and steal food
- MK negotiates for survival needs, reminding Wukong he's useless if starved

## Themes
- Heroism corrupted: legendary hero vs. present cruelty
- Survival under captivity: psychological and physical endurance
- Moral ambiguity in relationship between captor and captive
