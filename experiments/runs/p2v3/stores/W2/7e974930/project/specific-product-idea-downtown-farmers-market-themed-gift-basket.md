Creating a curated gift basket themed around the Downtown Farmers Market, featuring best-selling soaps and candles from that event. Benefits: taps into customer nostalgia/emotional connection, showcases top products, creates unique offering, appeals to both locals and tourists, cross-promotes the market. Enhancement ideas: personalized card with market story, market-themed packaging, small memento/token, discount code for market purchases.
