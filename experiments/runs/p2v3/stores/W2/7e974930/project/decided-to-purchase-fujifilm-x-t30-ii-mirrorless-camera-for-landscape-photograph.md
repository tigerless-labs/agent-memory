**Budget**: ~$1,500 for camera body and kit lens

**Camera Choice**: Fujifilm X-T30 II

**Decision Drivers**:
- Compact size and lightweight design
- Vari-angle touchscreen LCD for shooting flexibility
- Intelligent Hybrid AF system with 425 phase-detection points (0.02s response, ~99% accuracy)
- Strong image quality for landscape and nature photography
- Moisture-resistant construction
- New to mirrorless cameras; attracted to X-T30 II's feature set and value

**Kit Lens**: Fujifilm XF 15-45mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS PZ (~$499)
- Compact and lightweight
- Versatile focal range for landscape work

**Alternative Options Considered**: Fujifilm X-T3, Sony Alpha a6400, Sony Alpha a7 II
