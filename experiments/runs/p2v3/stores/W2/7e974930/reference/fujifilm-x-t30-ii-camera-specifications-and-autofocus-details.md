**Fujifilm X-T30 II Specifications**

**Autofocus System**: Intelligent Hybrid AF
- 425 phase-detection points covering ~100% of frame
- Response time: 0.02 seconds
- Accuracy: ~99% per Fujifilm specifications
- Face and eye detection with AI subject tracking
- Low-light AF minimum: -3EV

**Image Sensor**: 26.1MP APS-C

**Display**: Vari-angle touchscreen LCD

**Battery Life**:
- Real-world use: 300-400 frames per charge
- Official rating: 380 frames (EVF) / 440 frames (LCD)
- Video recording: 45 minutes (EVF) / 60 minutes (LCD)

**Durability**: Moisture-resistant design (not fully weather-sealed)

**Power Management Features**: Power Save mode, Economy mode, Auto Power Off

**Related Cameras**:
- Fujifilm X-T3: $1,099, more advanced, includes weather sealing
- Sony Alpha a6400: $998, compact, excellent autofocus
- Sony Alpha a7 II: $1,398, full-frame sensor
