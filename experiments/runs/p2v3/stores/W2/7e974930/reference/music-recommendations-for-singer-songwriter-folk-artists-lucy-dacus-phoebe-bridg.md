## Lucy Dacus
**Albums:**
- Historian (2018) — recommended as starting point; showcases storytelling and catchy hooks
- No Burden (2016) — debut album, lo-fi production, introspective songwriting
- Home Video (2021) — latest album, themes of nostalgia, identity, relationships

**Key tracks:** Night Shift, Addictions, Nonbeliever

## Phoebe Bridgers
**Albums:**
- Stranger in the Alps (2017) — debut, introspective with indie-rock and folk elements
- Punisher (2020) — sophomore album, more experimental and introspective
- Boygenius (2018) — collaborative EP with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus

**Key tracks:** Scott Street, Killer, Motion Sickness, Funeral, Kyoto

## Julien Baker
**Albums:**
- Sprained Ankle (2015) — debut album, introspective songwriting with soaring vocals
- Turn Out the Lights (2017) — sophomore album, more experimental sound
- Boygenius (2018) — collaborative EP with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus

**Key tracks:** Appointments, Turn Out the Lights, Rejoice, Souvenir, Hurt Less

## Other Recommended Artists
Bon Iver, Fleet Foxes, Sufjan Stevens, Courtney Marie Andrews, Vagabon, Adrianne Lenker, Sharon Van Etten, The Tallest Man on Earth, Soccer Mommy, Clairo, Wallows, Mild High Club
