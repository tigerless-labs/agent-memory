**The Dispossessed (1974)** — Explores anarchism as political system on distant planet; notable for human-environment relationship and critique of capitalist ideologies

**The Left Hand of Darkness (1969)** — Set on genderless planet; explores gender, sexuality, and power alongside human-environment relationship

**The Word for World is Forest (1976)** — Conflict between human colonizers and native inhabitants; themes of imperialism, colonialism, human-environment relations

**The Lathe of Heaven (1971)** — Intersection of science fiction and environmentalism; protagonist's dreams change reality; critiques destructive human environmental impact and science/technology misuse

**Always Coming Home (1985)** — Post-apocalyptic California; explores environmentalism, ecology, human-environment relationship through stories, songs, poetry

Le Guin influential for creating compelling stories exploring complex social/political issues, critiquing capitalist ideologies and destructive human environmental impact.
