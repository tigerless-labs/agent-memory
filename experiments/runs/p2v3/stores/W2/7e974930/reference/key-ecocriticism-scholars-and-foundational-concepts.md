**Leading figures:**
- Lawrence Buell: American literary critic; concepts of 'environmental imagination' and cultural restoration through literature
- Cheryll Glotfelty: American literary critic; founder of ecocriticism field
- Jonathan Bate: British literary critic; emphasizes reading literature as aspect of culture-nature relations
- Eric D. Wilson: American literary critic

**Core concepts:**
- Ecocriticism examines relationship between literature and environment; how literature reflects, shapes, and challenges perceptions of nature
- Ecocritical research methods: close reading, historical/cultural context analysis, environmental science analysis, comparative analysis, ethnographic fieldwork, discourse analysis
- Distinction: interdisciplinary = combining disciplines to study a problem; transdisciplinary = combining disciplines + stakeholders to solve real-world problems
