User wants to visit Hokkaido to try regional dishes and experience local food culture. Must-try dishes: Genghis Khan Yaki (grilled lamb/mutton with onions, vegetables, savory dipping sauce), Kaisendon (rice bowl with fresh sashimi—salmon, tuna, squid), Jingisukan (grilled lamb/mutton, Japanese Mongolian barbecue style), Uni ikura don (rice bowl with sea urchin and salmon roe), Ishikari Nabe (hot pot with salmon and vegetables, winter specialty), local ice cream (lavender, melon, cheese flavors). Recommended restaurants and markets: Nijo Fish Market (Sapporo), Sapporo Ramen Yokocho, Tanukikoji Shopping Street (Sapporo), Daruma (Sapporo) for jingisukan, Kitaichi Hall (Asahikawa) for jingisukan, Yurigakuro (Sapporo) for Ishikari Nabe, Kakizaki Shokudo (Otaru). Transportation: rental car, JR Hokkaido trains, bus network, bicycle, guided tours.
