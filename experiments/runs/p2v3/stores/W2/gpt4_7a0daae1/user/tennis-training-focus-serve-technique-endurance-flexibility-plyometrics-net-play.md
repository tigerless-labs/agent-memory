## Tennis Skill Development (2023-03)

Actively working to improve multiple tennis skills:

### Serve Technique
- Recently got new tennis racket (2023-03-10) to try out new serving techniques
- Focus areas: grip (continental grip), stance, toss consistency (6-8 feet high, 6-8 inches in front), racket position, contact point, follow-through, body rotation
- Practicing variation: flat serve, topspin serve, slice serve
- Working to develop muscle memory through consistent practice and self-recording for form analysis

### Endurance & Conditioning
- Running 30-minute runs regularly to build cardiovascular endurance
- Cycling Friday evenings along river
- Incorporating HIIT workouts: 30-second burpees and jump squats with 30-second rest intervals (20-30 min sessions)
- Aerobic training: 20-30 minutes of steady-state jogging or cycling
- Target: cardio sessions 2-3x per week with at least one rest day between

### Flexibility & Injury Prevention
- Stretching especially important after Saturday tennis lessons
- Pre-play stretches: neck, shoulders, chest, arm circles (5-10 min)
- Lower body: calf stretch, hamstring stretch, quad stretch, hip flexor stretch
- Tennis-specific: forearm stretch, rotator cuff stretch, glute stretch
- Post-play routine: repeat major muscle group stretches (10-15 min)
- Technique: hold 30 seconds, breathe naturally, no bouncing, stretch within comfortable range

### Plyometric Training
- Building explosive power and agility for improved court performance
- Exercises: box jumps (3 sets x 10-15 reps, start with 12-18 inch box), depth jumps (same), lateral bounds (quick turnaround), tuck jumps, medicine ball throws with rotation
- Rest between sets: 30-60 seconds
- Frequency: 2-3x per week
- Progression: start with lower intensity, gradually increase as strength improves
- Combine with strength training (squats, lunges)

### Court Strategy
- Coming to the net more often to take advantage of opponent's weaker returns
- Developing strong volley game
- Focus on: reading opponent's return patterns, positioning, footwork, racket face control, reaction speed
- Practice volleys and approach shots in drills before game play
- Mixing up strategies to keep opponent guessing
