Recommended approach for organizing large unstructured document collections: (1) Text cleaning - remove irrelevant chars, stop words, normalize to lowercase (2) Document parsing - extract title, author, date, body (3) Entity extraction - NER, POS tagging, dependency parsing for names/orgs/locations (4) Topic modeling - identify main themes and patterns (5) Sentiment analysis - understand tone and emotions (6) Document classification - categorize by topic (crime, terrorism, politics, etc.). Source: Discussion 2023-03-15 about organizing 90K-100K unstructured documents.
