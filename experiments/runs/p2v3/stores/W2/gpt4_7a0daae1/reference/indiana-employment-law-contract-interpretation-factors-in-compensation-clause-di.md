## Factors determining outcomes in ambiguous compensation clause litigation (Indiana)

When an employment contract has ambiguous compensation language and results in litigation, Indiana courts consider:

1. **Contract interpretation principles** — construing ambiguous language against the drafter (typically the employer); favoring reasonable interpretation that favors employee; examining entire contract context

2. **Evidence of intent** — written/oral communications, negotiations, industry practices when the parties entered the contract

3. **Parol evidence** — extrinsic evidence (witness testimony, prior drafts) when the contract language alone cannot resolve ambiguity

4. **Course of performance** — how the parties actually acted under the contract (e.g., consistent payment patterns may evidence intended compensation)

5. **Custom and usage** — industry standards and practices relevant to the disputed clause

6. **Indiana-specific factors**:
   - Indiana wage payment statutes regulate timing and manner of wage payments; could impact cases depending on nature of dispute
   - Indiana courts follow standard contract interpretation to determine and give effect to parties' intent
   - Relevant case law and precedent in Indiana may establish specific rules
