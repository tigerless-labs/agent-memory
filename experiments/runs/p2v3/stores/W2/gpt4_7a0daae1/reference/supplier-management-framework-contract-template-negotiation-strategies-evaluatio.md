## Supplier Contract Template (14 sections)

1. Introduction - parties and purpose
2. Scope of Work - products/services, specs, quality standards
3. Pricing and Payment Terms - unit price, payment terms
4. Delivery and Shipping - delivery schedule, shipping terms, lead time
5. Quality and Warranty - quality standards, warranty period, returns/refunds
6. Intellectual Property - IP ownership, confidentiality
7. Term and Termination - contract duration, termination notice, post-termination obligations
8. Liability and Indemnification - damage liability, indemnification
9. Dispute Resolution - arbitration/mediation, governing law
10. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure - confidentiality obligations
11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
12. Amendments and Waivers - amendment procedure
13. Entire Agreement clause
14. Signatures - authorized representatives

## Price Negotiation Tips
- Research market intelligence and competitor pricing
- Know your requirements and non-negotiables
- Build relationship with supplier
- Be transparent about budget constraints
- Ask directly for better price
- Look for value-added services (free shipping, priority delivery, support)
- Be willing to walk away
- Use competition as leverage
- Consider alternative pricing models (tiered, cost-plus, value-based)
- Verify and confirm in writing

## Supplier Evaluation Factors
- Product/service quality (certifications, compliance, QC process)
- Reliability and consistency (on-time delivery track record)
- Pricing and cost transparency
- Customer service and support quality
- Financial stability and industry reputation
- Supply chain visibility and risk management
- Certifications and compliance (ISO, CE, FDA, etc.)
- Innovation and continuous improvement culture

**Red Flags:**
- Poor communication or unresponsiveness
- Lack of transparency in pricing/quality/supply chain
- Unwillingness to provide references or case studies
- History of late deliveries or quality issues
- Inadequate insurance coverage
- Unprofessional behavior or unqualified staff
- Unclear or unfair contract terms
- Unwillingness to adapt to customer needs

## Supplier Onboarding Steps
1. Create comprehensive onboarding checklist
2. Finalize contract and get signatures
3. Gather supplier profile and information (company info, certifications, insurance)
4. Establish communication channels and designate primary contacts
5. Conduct onboarding meeting to introduce team and review expectations
6. Provide training on business processes and quality standards
7. Conduct testing and quality assurance checks
8. Establish payment terms, frequency, method, and invoicing procedures
9. Set up KPIs and performance monitoring

**Tools:** Excel/Google Sheets for checklists, contract management software (ContractWorks, Concord), supplier management systems (SAP Ariba, Coupa), project management tools (Asana, Trello), quality management software

## Quality Control Process Implementation
1. Define clear quality standards and specifications (document and communicate)
2. Identify critical control points that impact quality
3. Develop quality control plan with procedures, frequency, and methods
4. Implement regular inspections, testing, and monitoring
5. Establish corrective action process (root cause analysis)
6. Monitor and evaluate performance using KPIs (defect rates, on-time delivery, satisfaction)

**Checks and Balances:**
- Regular audits and inspections
- Product testing and validation
- Supplier certifications (ISO 9001, etc.)
- Third-party audits
- Customer feedback monitoring

**Methodologies:** ISO 9001:2015, AS9100, Six Sigma, Lean Manufacturing
