## Tennis Serve Technique Tips

Essential grip and positioning:
- **Grip**: Continental grip (index finger on the second groove of the racket handle)
- **Stance**: Feet shoulder-width apart, body facing net, turn slightly right (right-handers), shift weight to back foot
- **Toss**: 6-8 feet in air, 6-8 inches in front of body, slightly right (right-handers), use non-dominant hand
- **Racket position**: 90-degree angle to body, racket face pointing toward net
- **Contact**: Peak of toss, using wrist and forearm for power, slightly closed racket face
- **Follow-through**: Keep racket close to body for control and power
- **Body rotation**: Rotate to generate power, uncoil during contact, transfer weight from back to front foot

Serve types to practice:
- Flat serve: fast, straight, minimal spin
- Topspin serve: forward spin, dips down into opponent's court
- Slice serve: sidespin, curves away from opponent

New racket integration:
- Experiment with the racket's sweet spot (where ball responds best)
- Adjust technique if new racket has more power than previous one
- If adjustable strings, experiment with different tensions and types
- Record yourself serving to analyze technique and track progress
