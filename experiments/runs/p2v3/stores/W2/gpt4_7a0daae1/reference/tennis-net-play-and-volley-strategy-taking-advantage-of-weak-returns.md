## Net Play & Volley Strategy

Effective execution for volleys and net approach:

1. **Read your opponent**: Watch return of serve for patterns, especially weaker returns or short returns
2. **Positioning**: Position at right angle to cut off return; move forward and to the side for optimal volley position
3. **Footwork**: Continuously adjust with small steps forward/backward; don't just stand still waiting
4. **Watch the ball**: Focus eyes on ball, maintain concentration on contact point
5. **Racket technique**: Use slightly closed racket face to generate topspin and control
6. **Reaction speed**: Be prepared to react to strong returns and adjust volley accordingly
7. **Practice approach shots**: Drill volleys and approach shots before integrating into game play

Strategy notes:
- Mix up tactics to avoid being predictable
- Don't rely too heavily on one strategy
- Adapt to opponent's game plan and adjust accordingly
- Volleys are most effective against weak returns, so develop the ability to capitalize on those opportunities
