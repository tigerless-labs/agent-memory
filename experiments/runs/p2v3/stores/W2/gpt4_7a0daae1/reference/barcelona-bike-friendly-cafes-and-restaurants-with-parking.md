## Bike-Friendly Stops in Barcelona\n\n**Can Culleretes**\n- Historic café in Gothic Quarter\n- Outdoor seating, bike parking\n- Good for coffee/snack break\n\n**El Xampanyet**\n- Popular for quick bites/drinks\n- Outdoor seating, bike parking\n- Famous for pan con tomate (bread with fresh tomato)\n\n**La Pubilla**\n- Cozy café in Eixample neighborhood\n- Bike-friendly atmosphere\n- Delicious pastries\n\n**Bicioci**\n- Bike-themed café in Poblenou neighborhood\n- Healthy snacks and drinks\n- Has bike parking and repair station\n\n**Can Solé**\n- Family-run restaurant in Barceloneta neighborhood\n- Serves traditional Catalan cuisine\n- Outdoor seating and bike parking\n\n## Tips\n- Check opening hours before stopping\n- Most have outdoor seating areas where bikes can be locked\n- Ask tour guide for additional recommendations\n- Stay hydrated with reusable water bottle
