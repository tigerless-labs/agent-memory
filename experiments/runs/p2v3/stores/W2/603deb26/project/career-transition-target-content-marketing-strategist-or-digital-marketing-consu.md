## Target Roles
- Content Marketing Strategist
- Digital Marketing Consultant

## Key Experience Areas
- Managing social media presence
- Creating and scheduling posts
- Analyzing engagement metrics
- Peak workload: +10 hours/week during campaigns (working up to 50 hours/week total)

## Core Strengths to Highlight
- Works effectively in fast-paced environments
- Strong time management and prioritization
- Adaptable to changing requirements and workloads
- Delivers under tight deadlines

## Certifications
- Google Analytics certification
- HubSpot Inbound Marketing certification

## Application Materials Strategy
**Hybrid approach:**
1. Create core version of resume/LinkedIn with overall skills and achievements
2. Tailor specific versions to each job description
3. Use keywords from job descriptions in application materials
4. Quantify achievements with specific metrics (e.g., "Grew social media following by 500%")
5. Add summary/objective statement highlighting career goals
6. List certifications in dedicated section on resume
7. Add certifications to LinkedIn's Licenses & Certifications section

This demonstrates expertise in data-driven marketing strategies and commitment to industry best practices.
