Planned a dinner party for 8-10 guests with diverse, multi-cuisine menu featuring dishes from cooking classes. Menu needs to accommodate vegetarian guests and one gluten-free guest. Finalized menu: lemon-herb naan, vegetarian sushi rolls (with grilled portobello mushrooms and carrot-ginger tempura bits), lemon-ginger dipping sauce, lemon-herb quinoa (with toasted pine nuts and fresh herbs), lemon curd or lemon bars (gluten-free option available). Planned garnishes include fresh herbs (parsley, basil, mint), thinly sliced lemons, and edible flowers (violas or pansies). Emphasized importance of timing, prep-ahead work, and proper pacing between courses.
