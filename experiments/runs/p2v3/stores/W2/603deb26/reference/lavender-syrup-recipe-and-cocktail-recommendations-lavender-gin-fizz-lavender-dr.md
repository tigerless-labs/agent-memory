**Lavender Syrup Recipe:**\n- 1 cup granulated sugar\n- 1 cup water\n- 1/4 cup dried lavender buds\n1. Combine sugar and water, heat until sugar dissolves\n2. Add lavender buds, remove from heat\n3. Steep 10-15 minutes until cool\n4. Strain through fine-mesh sieve\n5. Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks\n\n**Cocktails:**\n- Lavender Gin Fizz: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz simple syrup, top with soda water\n- Lavender Dream: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz lemon juice, 1/2 oz honey syrup\n- Lavender Bee's Knees: 2 oz Hendrick's, 1 oz lavender syrup, 1 oz honey syrup
