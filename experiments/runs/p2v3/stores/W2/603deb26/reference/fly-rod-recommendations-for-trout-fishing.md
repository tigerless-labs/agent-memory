Recommended fly rods for lighter weight trout fishing:\n\n- **Orvis Clearwater**: Entry-level to intermediate, lightweight, balanced. ($198-$298)\n- **Sage Igniter**: High-performance, light, fast action. ($400-$500)\n- **Winston Pure**: Premium, smooth precise casting. ($300-$400)\n- **Redington Crux**: Budget-friendly, easy to cast. ($150-$250)\n- **TFO Lefty Kreh Signature Series**: High-quality, smooth action. ($200-$300)\n\nLook for 2-5 weight lines, fast or medium-fast action, 9-foot length or shorter (7-8 feet for tighter spaces). Graphite rods are lighter and more sensitive; fiberglass more durable.
