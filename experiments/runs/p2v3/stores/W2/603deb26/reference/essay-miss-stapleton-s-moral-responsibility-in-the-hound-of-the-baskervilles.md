# The Hound of the Baskervilles Essay

**Question:** Based on the ending, should Miss Stapleton be held responsible for the crimes? If so, how severely should she be punished?

## Analysis

Miss Stapleton should be held responsible for her actions, but her punishment should be tempered by her circumstances. She was both an active participant in the plot to harm the Baskervilles and a victim of her brother's cruel control.

### Active Role in Crimes
She pretended to befriend Sir Henry, lured him into danger, and helped her brother conceal his identity and carry out his plan. This violated moral principles and law, making her liable for criminal charges.

### Mitigating Circumstances  
She was raised by a cruel, abusive brother who controlled her completely. Her actions stemmed from desperation—a desire to escape his tyranny and gain independence—not malice.

### Balanced Culpability
Responsibility requires weighing both her participation and her coercion. Charges could range from conspiracy to attempted murder, but punishment should aim for reform rather than retribution.

### Conclusion
Moral responsibility must account for her dual role as both actor and victim. Justice should balance accountability with compassion for her circumstances.
