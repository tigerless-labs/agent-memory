**Citrus recommendations for pairing with gin and lavender:**\n\n- **Orange**: Classic with lavender; try blood oranges for deeper, complex flavor\n- **Yuzu**: Japanese citrus (cross between grapefruit and orange); tart with subtle sweetness; adds exotic note\n- **Kumquat**: Small, sweet citrus; particularly well-suited to lavender pairing; try kumquat syrup or muddled fresh\n- **Satsuma**: Mandarin variety; sweet and tart; loose, easy-to-peel skin; sweetness balances gin bitterness\n- **Bergamot**: Subtle citrus flavor (used in perfumery and tea); adds complexity; pairs nicely with lavender\n\n**General tips:**\n- Use fresh citrus for brightest, most vibrant flavor\n- Balance citrus carefully—it can overpower other flavors\n- Consider using citrus peels/twists for added aroma and flavor depth
