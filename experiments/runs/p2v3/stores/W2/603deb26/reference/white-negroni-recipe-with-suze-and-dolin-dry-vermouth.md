**White Negroni Recipe:**\n- 1 1/2 oz Hendrick's gin (or preferred gin)\n- 1/2 oz Suze (or other dry bitter liqueur like Luxardo Bitter)\n- 1/2 oz Dolin Dry Vermouth\n- Orange or lemon twist for garnish\n\n**Instructions:**\n1. Fill mixing glass with ice\n2. Add gin, Suze, and Dolin Dry Vermouth\n3. Stir 20-30 seconds to chill and dilute\n4. Strain into chilled coupe or cocktail glass\n5. Garnish with orange or lemon twist\n\n**Tips:**\n- Use dry bitter liqueur for crisp, herbaceous flavor\n- Dolin Dry Vermouth works well for the dry component\n- May need to adjust ratio; some prefer 2:1:1 or 3:1:1\n- Hendrick's floral notes pair well with Suze's dryness\n- Stir just long enough to chill without over-diluting
