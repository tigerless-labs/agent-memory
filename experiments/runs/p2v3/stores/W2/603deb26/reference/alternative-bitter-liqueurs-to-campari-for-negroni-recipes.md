**Bitter liqueur alternatives to replace Campari in Negroni:**\n\n1. **Cynar**: Italian bitter from artichokes; vegetal, slightly sweet flavor; try 1/2 oz Cynar + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica\n\n2. **Aperol**: Italian bitter; sweeter and more orange-forward than Campari; use 1 oz Aperol + 1 oz Carpano Antica for lighter, citrusy tone\n\n3. **Luxardo Bitter**: Italian; more intense, herbal flavor profile; try 1/2 oz Luxardo Bitter + 1/2 oz Carpano Antica for bold, complex result\n\n4. **Suze**: French gentian-based bitter; crisp, earthy flavor; use 1 oz Suze + 1 oz Carpano Antica for dry, herbaceous take\n\n5. **Meletti**: Italian bitter; slightly sweeter, more citrus-forward; use 1 oz Meletti + 1 oz Carpano Antica for smooth, refreshing variation\n\n**Note:** May need to adjust bitter-to-sweet vermouth ratio depending on intensity of chosen liqueur.
