Observation from family reunions: the female majority (17 of 25 cousins) tends to dominate conversations and decision-making processes during gatherings. Male cousins (8 of 25) tend to be more laid-back and less assertive in group settings. This may be influenced by the gender ratio, socialization patterns, or individual personalities.
