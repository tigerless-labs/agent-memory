Key academic databases for searching social media and mental health research: PsycINFO, PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar. Effective search strategies use Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) combined with keywords like: social media, mental health, young adults, depression, anxiety, usage patterns, self-esteem, social support, mediating/moderating roles, interventions, policies, awareness campaigns, online support groups, peer-to-peer support. Use citation tracking features and reference list exploration to find related sources. Filter by publication date to focus on recent studies.
