Inspirational stories: How I Built This (Guy Raz), The Tim Ferriss Show, Masters of Scale (Reid Hoffman), The GaryVee Audio Experience, The School of Greatness (Lewis Howes). Business news: The Hustle (daily), Planet Money (NPR), The $100 MBA (daily), HBR IdeaCast (Harvard Business Review), The Entrepreneur's Journey. Finance and marketing: The Dave Ramsey Show (personal finance), The Social Media Marketing Podcast (Michael Stelzner), The Marketing Companion (Mark Schaefer, Brooke Sellas), The Profit First Podcast (Mike Michalowicz), The Growth Lab (HubSpot).
