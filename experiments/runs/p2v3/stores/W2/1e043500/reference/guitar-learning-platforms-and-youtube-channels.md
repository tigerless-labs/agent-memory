Subscription Platforms: Guitar Tricks (11,000+ lessons, $19.95/month, 14-day free trial), TrueFire ($29.95/month or $299.95/year), Yousician ($19.95/month or $119.88/year), ArtistWorks ($29.95/month or $299.95/year), JamPlay ($29.95/month or $299.95/year). Free: Justin Guitar (beginner course), Ultimate Guitar (tabs/chords/lessons), YouTube (various teachers). YouTube Channels: Justin Guitar, Marty Music, GuitarLessons365, GuitarJamz, Andy Guitar, Ben Eller Guitar (advanced theory), Fingerstyle Guitar, GuitarJamz Acoustic, LickLibrary.
