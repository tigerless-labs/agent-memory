Services: Bandcamp (discover indie artists/labels by tags/genres), Apple Music (Moods section, New Music Mix), Tidal (human-curated Discover, Rising section), Deezer (AI Discover, Mood section). Recommended playlists: Lo-Fi Girl (2M+ followers, continuous lo-fi/chillhop), Ambient Universe (ambient/drone/experimental), Chillhop Music (lo-fi hip-hop, instrumental), Coffee Shop Jazz (relaxing, work/study), Spotify Discover Weekly and Release Radar (personalized).
