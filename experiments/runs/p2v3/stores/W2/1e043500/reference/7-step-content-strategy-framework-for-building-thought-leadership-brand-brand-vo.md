## Content Strategy Approach

**7-Step Brand Development:**
1. Develop brand voice and values
2. Identify target audience (team leaders, hiring managers, software dev professionals)
3. Create valuable content aligned with brand message
4. Promote via social media, email, and other channels
5. Engage with audience through community building
6. Collaborate with industry thought leaders
7. Measure and adjust strategy based on analytics

**Recommended Content Formats:**
- Blog posts: exploring importance of hiring humans first, identifying key traits, building collaborative teams
- Podcasts/videos: interviews with industry experts and successful team leaders
- Webinars/workshops: practical tips on team building, psychometric testing, soft skills evaluation
- E-books/guides: in-depth exploration of team building, soft skills assessment, culture development
- Social media: sharing content, case studies, team member profiles, building community

**Core Message:** Position as thought leader in building effective teams by prioritizing great human beings over pure technical skill, with emphasis on soft skills, emotional intelligence, and critical thinking.
