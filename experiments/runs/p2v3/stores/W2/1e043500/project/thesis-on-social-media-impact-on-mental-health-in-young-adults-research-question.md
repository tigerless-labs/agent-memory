Studying the impact of social media on mental health in young adults. Research question: How are social media usage patterns associated with symptoms of depression and anxiety in young adults? Extended interests include mediating roles of self-esteem and social support, effectiveness of interventions/policies, and use of social media for mental health awareness campaigns and peer support.
