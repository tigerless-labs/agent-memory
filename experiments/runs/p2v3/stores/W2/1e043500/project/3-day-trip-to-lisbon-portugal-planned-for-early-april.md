## Trip Decision

Chose Lisbon over Porto for a 3-day visit because of:
- Broader range of attractions and museums (Belém Tower, Jerónimos Monastery, MAAT, National Museum of Ancient Art, Calouste Gulbenkian Museum)
- Vibrant nightlife (Bairro Alto bars and restaurants)
- Easy day-trip access (Sintra, Cascais, Costa da Caparica)
- Can fill 3 days well; Porto is smaller and exploreable in less time

## Recommended 3-Day Itinerary

**Day 1: Alfama & Baixa**
- Morning: Historic Alfama neighborhood, Lisbon Cathedral, Castle of São Jorge, tram through narrow streets
- Afternoon: Baixa district—Praça do Comércio, Rossio Square, Chiado, Café A Brasileira (pastel de nata)

**Day 2: Sintra Day Trip & Belém**
- Morning: Sintra—Pena Palace, Moorish Castle, gardens, hilltop views
- Afternoon: Return to Lisbon, Belém neighborhood—Jerónimos Monastery, Monument to the Discoveries, Pastéis de Belém

**Day 3: Tagus River & Bairro Alto**
- Morning: Tagus River walk, MAAT museum (modern architecture)
- Afternoon: Bairro Alto—Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara viewpoint, narrow streets, traditional restaurants

## Other Attractions to Explore
- National Museum of Ancient Art
- Calouste Gulbenkian Museum
- LX Factory (art/culture district)
