Working on improving grandmother's diet to support cognitive function and brain health.

**Key recommendations being implemented:**
- Foods high in omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, tuna, mackerel)
- Antioxidant-rich foods (berries, spinach, kale)
- B vitamins (leafy greens, nuts, fortified cereals)
- Moderate caffeine (coffee/tea)
- Turmeric (curcumin)

**Practical strategies:**
- Finding recipes that incorporate healthy foods in appealing ways
- Adding healthy ingredients to dishes she already enjoys (e.g., spinach to pasta sauce, berries to smoothies)
- Using colorful, visually appealing presentation
- Adjusting texture as needed (pureeing, soft cooking for easier eating)
- Involving grandmother in meal planning and preparation
- Starting with small amounts to help her adjust to new flavors

**Easy healthy snacks being considered:**
- Apple/celery slices with nut butters
- Roasted chickpeas
- Greek yogurt with berries and honey
- Hummus with vegetables
- Trail mix with nuts and dark chocolate
- Whole-grain crackers with avocado
- Fruit and spinach smoothies
- Homemade energy balls with dates and nuts
