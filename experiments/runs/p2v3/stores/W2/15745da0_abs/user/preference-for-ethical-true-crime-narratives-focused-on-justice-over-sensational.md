Concerned about exploitation in true crime media when narratives sensationalize graphic details, glorify perpetrators, or re-traumatize families for entertainment. Prefers narratives that respect victims' dignity, center justice and prevention, examine systemic issues, and raise awareness responsibly. Holds self accountable as a consumer and believes audiences can shift media culture by supporting ethical creators. Core values: empathy, justice, education, human dignity.
