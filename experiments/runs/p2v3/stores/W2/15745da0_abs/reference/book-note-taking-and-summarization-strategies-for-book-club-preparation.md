**Summarizing techniques:**\n- Identify main plot points\n- Determine author's purpose and themes\n- Identify key characters and their motivations\n- Note setting and atmosphere\n- Focus on climax and resolution\n\n**Note-taking strategies:**\n- Use dedicated notebook or app\n- Create consistent system (colors, symbols, headings)\n- Record quotes with page numbers for later reference\n- Ask questions and make connections while reading\n- Summarize each chapter or section\n- Use sticky notes/tabs for important passages\n- Create character maps showing relationships\n- Research author background and historical context\n\nThese help with book club preparation and discussions.
