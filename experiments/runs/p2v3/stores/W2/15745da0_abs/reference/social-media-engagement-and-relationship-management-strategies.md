**Building connections on social media:**\n- Be authentic and share genuine thoughts\n- Use hashtags strategically\n- Engage in conversations, respond promptly\n- Share valuable content (tips, tutorials, inspiring stories)\n- Post consistently\n- Participate in online communities (Facebook groups, Reddit forums)\n- Monitor hashtags/keywords relevant to interests\n- Collaborate with others\n- Be respectful and open-minded\n- Monitor analytics and adjust strategy\n\n**Managing conversations and relationships:**\n- Create a social media calendar\n- Use management tools (Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social)\n- Set reminders for discussions/events\n- Keep a spreadsheet/document of conversations\n- Prioritize and focus on most important communities\n- Batch similar tasks\n- Take breaks and practice self-care\n\n**For book clubs specifically:**\n- Keep separate document for discussion topics and thoughts\n- Set reminders for discussion dates\n- Participate at consistent time weekly
