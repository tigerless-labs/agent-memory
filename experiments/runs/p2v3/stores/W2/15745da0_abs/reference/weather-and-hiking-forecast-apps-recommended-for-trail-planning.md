**Weather apps for hikers:**\n- Dark Sky (iOS, Android): Minute-by-minute forecasts, known for accuracy\n- Mountain Weather Forecast (iOS, Android): Mountain-specific, includes avalanche risk, wind, snowfall\n- Weather Underground (iOS, Android): Hourly/10-day forecasts, weather radar, personal weather stations\n- OpenSnow (iOS, Android): Ski-focused but good for mountain forecasts\n- The Weather Channel (iOS, Android): Detailed forecasts with radar\n- AccuWeather (iOS, Android): Minute-by-minute forecasts\n- National Weather Service (NWS) (iOS, Android): Official source, good for hazard warnings\n\n**Trail-finding resources:**\n- AllTrails: Search by location, distance, difficulty\n- TrailLink: US trails with descriptions, maps, reviews\n- Hiking Project: Maps, elevation profiles, user reviews
