Completed rewrites of Villa Gennaro Italian restaurant menu with enhanced descriptions for three major sections.

Parts rewritten: 
- Part 2 (The Classics): Chicken and veal dishes including Parmigiana, Piccata, Saltimbocca, Marsala, Francese at prices 22-26 dollars
- Part 3 (Entrees): Seafood, beef short ribs, lamb, chicken, and risotto dishes at prices 20-38 dollars  
- Part 4 (Pasta): Penne a la Vodka, Capellini Primavera, Rigatoni alla Gennaro, ravioli, lasagna, pappardelle at prices 20-32 dollars

Each dish included compelling descriptions highlighting flavors, preparation, and pairings.
