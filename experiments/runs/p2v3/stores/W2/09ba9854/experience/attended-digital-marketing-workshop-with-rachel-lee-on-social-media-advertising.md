Workshop covered effective social media ad campaigns:

**Facebook campaigns:** Define target audience (demographics, interests, behaviors); set clear goals (awareness, traffic, leads); use eye-catching visuals optimized for mobile; monitor and adjust targeting, budget, and creative based on performance.

**Instagram campaigns:** Visuals are critical to success; use Stories for casual, behind-the-scenes content; leverage relevant hashtags; partner with influencers; use interactive features (polls, quizzes, shopping tags).

**General best practices:** A/B test different ad creatives, targeting, and budgets; set and stick to a budget; track results with analytics tools; results take time — be patient.
