## Story: The Memory Thief

**Protagonist:** Lyra, a skilled memory thief with fragmented/absent memories of their own past.

**Setting:** Dystopian future where advanced neurotechnology allows memories to be extracted, stored, and sold on black markets. Memory trade is regulated by a government Memory Authority, but corruption and underground markets thrive.

**Core conflict:** Lyra is hired to steal memories from a powerful CEO. While doing so, they uncover clues about their own past and true identity—raising the question of whether their own memories and identity are authentic or fabricated.

**Major plot points:**
- Lyra's discovery of their true past and the reason for their memory loss
- The CEO's true intentions and hidden agenda
- Consequences of Lyra's memory theft that challenge their understanding of their role and relationships

**Key themes:** Identity and self-discovery, authenticity of memory, what it means to be human in a world where memories can be manipulated

**Related concept:** Also exploring 'The Royal Affair' (prince/commoner forbidden romance in intergalactic kingdom setting) but 'The Memory Thief' is the active focus.
