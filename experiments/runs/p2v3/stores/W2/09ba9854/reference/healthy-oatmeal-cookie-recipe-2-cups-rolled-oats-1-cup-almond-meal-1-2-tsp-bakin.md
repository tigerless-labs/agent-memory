## Healthy Oatmeal Cookie Recipe

Bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until golden brown.

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 cup almond meal
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 cup coconut oil, melted
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup raisins (or dark chocolate chips)

**Notes:** Can substitute raisins with dark chocolate chips for less healthy but still better-quality option. Dark chocolate chips have higher cocoa percentage and less added sugar than milk chocolate.
