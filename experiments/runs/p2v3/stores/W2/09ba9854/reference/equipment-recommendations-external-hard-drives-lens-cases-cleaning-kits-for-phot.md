**External Hard Drives for Photo Backup:**
- Western Digital My Book (up to 16TB, compact, Mac & PC compatible)
- Seagate Backup Plus (up to 5TB, 2-year warranty)
- LaCie Rugged (rugged/durable, up to 5TB)
- G-Technology G-Drive (high-performance, up to 10TB, for creative professionals)

**Lens Cases & Pouches:**
- Think Tank Photo Lens Case — excellent protection and organization
- Lowepro Lens Case — high-quality, various sizes
- Nanuk Lens Case — extreme protection, rugged outdoor use
- Op/Tech USA Lens Pouch — soft, padded, various sizes
- Lenscoat Lens Pouch — water-resistant and breathable
- Tamrac Lens Pouch — protection with zippered closure

**Lens Cleaning Products:**
- Canon Lens Cleaning Kit — official Canon kit with soft brush, microfiber cloth, solution
- Zeiss Lens Cleaning Kit — high-quality microfiber cloth, cleaning solution, soft brush
- Microfiber cloths — lint-free for safe cleaning
- Pre-moistened lens cleaning tissues

**Storage & Organization Tips:**
- Use memory card cases or wallets to prevent loss
- Label memory cards by capacity and identifier (color-coding helpful)
- Create card inventory list to track cards in use
- Store lenses with caps on and upright to prevent damage
- Use 3-2-1 backup rule: 3 copies on 2 different media types with 1 copy offsite
