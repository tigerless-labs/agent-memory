## Writing Craft: Sci-Fi Romance Development

**Character Development Approach:**
- Ground extraordinary circumstances in relatable emotional struggles (universal desires, fears, hopes)
- Build slow-burn relationships on mutual respect, trust, communication—avoid insta-love
- Use contradictory traits and flaws to create believable characters
- Show emotional vulnerability and how characters support/understand each other
- Make relationships a two-way partnership with both characters having agency

**Character Arcs:**
- Show authentic emotional journey with growth, change, or perspective shift
- Use character arcs to explore identity and self-discovery themes
- Create character-driven plots where plot stems from characters' desires/fears/motivations
- Use sci-fi elements to enhance character development and explore identity

**World-Building Foundations:**
- Establish clear vision: core concept, tone, themes before building
- Define rules of the world (laws of physics/technology/magic)—consistency is critical
- Develop world history including significant events, cultural development, tech advancement
- Consider implications of technology on daily life, relationships, societal structures
- Balance detail with ambiguity to let readers' imaginations fill gaps
- Create diverse settings reflecting culture, history, technology
- Invent unique language/slang specific to the world

**Thematic Depth (Identity & Self-Discovery):**
- Characters may question their purpose, identity, sense of self in extraordinary circumstances
- Sci-fi elements blur boundaries (human/non-human, self/others)—use to explore nature of identity
- Show how characters form/reform identity in response to experiences and relationships
- Consider intersectionality: how identity intersects with species, culture, gender, sexuality
- Use plot twists to challenge characters' identities and force re-evaluation
- Inner conflict drives plot and reveals growth
- Symbolism represents journey of self-discovery (transformation, hidden truths)

**Key principle:** Consistency is essential—stay true to world rules and character motivations while remaining flexible enough to adapt as story unfolds.
