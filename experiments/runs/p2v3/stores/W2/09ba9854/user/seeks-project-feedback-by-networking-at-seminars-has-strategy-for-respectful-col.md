When validating a project, attends entrepreneurship seminars and networking events to connect with like-minded individuals. Approach to asking for help or feedback:

- Be respectful of people's time; keep asks concise
- Clearly specify what help/feedback is needed (collaboration, advice, validation)
- Show genuine interest in the other person's work first
- Be open to constructive criticism and ready to learn
- Offer value in return (skill swap, connection, or genuine thanks)
- Be prepared to answer detailed questions about project, goals, and vision
- Never push if someone declines; accept gracefully and move on

Prepared with elevator pitch and clear articulation of project. Follows up with people after meetings to maintain connection and explore collaboration possibilities.
