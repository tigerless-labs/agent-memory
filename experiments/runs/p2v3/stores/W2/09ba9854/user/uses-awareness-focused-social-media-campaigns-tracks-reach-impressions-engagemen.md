When running social media ad campaigns, focuses on awareness objective to increase visibility and reach. Key metrics tracked:

- **Reach:** Number of people who saw the ad
- **Impressions:** Number of times ad was displayed  
- **Frequency:** Average times shown per person (monitors for ad fatigue)
- **Engagement:** Likes, comments, saves, shares
- **CPM:** Cost per thousand impressions (measures efficiency)
- **Brand awareness and recall lift:** Can be measured via surveys to confirm people remember the ad

Uses Facebook Ads Manager and Instagram Insights to monitor performance. Makes data-driven adjustments to targeting, ad creative, and budget based on these metrics. Patient approach — expects results to improve over time with optimization.
