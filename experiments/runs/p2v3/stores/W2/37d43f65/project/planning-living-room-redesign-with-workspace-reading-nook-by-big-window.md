## Living Room Workspace/Reading Nook Project

### Vision
Transform living room with a dedicated workspace/reading nook positioned near the big windows to take advantage of natural light.

### Design Elements (Committed)
- **Desk/Console Table**: Small, compact piece near the window
  - Options explored: wall-mounted desk, narrow console table, drop-leaf table, ottoman with tray, foldable desk
  - Preference: Something with minimal footprint that doesn't clutter the space
  
- **Circular Rug**: Small (3-4 feet diameter) positioned under desk/table
  - Purpose: Define the workspace, add warmth and texture
  - Material preference: Durable, low-maintenance (wool, jute, or synthetic)
  - Color: Neutral shades (beige, gray, taupe) or consider bold color for personality
  
- **Circular Mirror**: Small (12-18 inches diameter) hung above desk/table
  - Purpose: Add visual interest, create illusion of larger space, enhance light distribution
  - Frame style: Should complement the modern, minimal aesthetic
  - Mounting height: ~57-60 inches from floor
  
- **Artwork/Prints**: Above desk area
  - Styles under consideration: botanicals, abstracts, typography, landscapes, or pop art
  - Scale: 1/3 to 2/3 width of desk/table
  - Personal connection important; should inspire or resonate

### Plant Additions
Adding low-maintenance houseplants suited to bright indirect light:
- Snake Plant, ZZ Plant, Spider Plant, Pothos, Succulents, Dracaena, Peace Lily, Bromeliads

### Design Constraints
- Keep space compact and clutter-free
- Neutral color palette for furniture
- Complementary colors for rug, mirror, and artwork
- Task lighting over workspace

### Useful Retailers/Resources
Furniture & Decor: IKEA, West Elm, CB2, Crate & Barrel, AllModern, Amazon  
Artwork/Prints: Society6, Minted, Etsy, Redbubble, AllPosters, Art.com, Saatchi Art

### Living Room Maintenance Already Established
- One-Touch Rule (put items away immediately)
- Landing Strip for incoming items
- Regular 10-15 min daily or 30 min weekly tidying
- Home for Everything system with designated storage
- Decorative labeled containers/baskets
- Active paper clutter management
