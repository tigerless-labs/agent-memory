Worried that increased access to information hasn't led to more open-mindedness, but rather has entrenched people in filter bubbles and echo chambers. Observes that social media algorithms seem designed to reinforce existing beliefs and biases rather than expose users to diverse perspectives. Sees this as a significant societal problem that contributes to polarization and reduced empathy across groups.
