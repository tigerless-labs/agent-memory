Recommended calendar apps: Google Calendar (free, integrates Google services, time zones, recurring events, reminders), Microsoft Outlook (task management), Fantastical (premium, iOS/Mac), Any.do (task + calendar), Calendly (scheduling), World Time Buddy (time zones), Doodle (polls), TimeAndDate (free tools). Video conferencing: Google Hangouts (built into Google Calendar), Zoom (add-on for Google Calendar with one-click meeting creation and automatic invitations).
