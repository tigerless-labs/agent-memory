In discussion of future neuroscience breakthroughs mentioned:
- **Gut-brain axis**: Bidirectional communication between central nervous system and enteric nervous system in GI tract; gut microbiota may influence neurological and psychiatric conditions
- **Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs)**: Direct neural control of external devices for assistive tech, neuroprosthetics, treating neurological disorders  
- **Personalized genetic medicine**: Developing treatments based on individual genetic profiles, particularly for mental disorders
