**Side dish suggestions for grilled chicken:**

1. **Grilled vegetables** — Bell peppers, zucchini, eggplant seasoned with salt, pepper, and olive oil
2. **Fresh salad** — Caesar salad or Greek salad with cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, feta cheese, and olives
3. **Corn on the cob** — Grilled corn with butter, salt, and paprika
4. **Baked sweet potato** — Topped with butter, cinnamon, and brown sugar
5. **Quinoa salad** — Mixed with chopped veggies (cucumbers, tomatoes, bell peppers) and lemony vinaigrette
