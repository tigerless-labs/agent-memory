Was experiencing disconnect from real-world relationships due to excessive social media consumption. Scrolling feeds endlessly created fear of missing out (FOMO) and feelings of isolation despite being 'connected' online. Recognized the paradox: social media intended to connect, but made things feel more disconnected.\n\nInterested in strategies to limit social media while maintaining friendships—especially through phone calls, video calls, in-person meetups, and direct messaging rather than passive scrolling.
