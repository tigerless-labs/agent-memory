**Security risks** — vulnerable to hacking and cyber-attacks; requires proper security protocols

**Interoperability issues** — difficulty integrating systems built by different companies; affects data sharing

**Regulatory risks** — few established regulations; companies must ensure legal compliance

**Privacy concerns** — systems gather large amounts of sensitive/personal data; requires clear data management rules and compliance with privacy protection laws
