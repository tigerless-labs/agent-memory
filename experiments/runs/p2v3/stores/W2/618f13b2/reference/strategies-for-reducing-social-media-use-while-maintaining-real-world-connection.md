**Setting boundaries:**\n- Decide on specific times to check social media\n- Use website blockers (Freedom, SelfControl, StayFocusd)\n- Remove apps from phone home screen to reduce temptation\n- Implement 'no phone zones' during meals, conversations, time with friends/family\n- Schedule specific check-in times rather than constant checking\n\n**Content curation:**\n- Unfollow or mute accounts that trigger comparison/anxiety\n- Prioritize mental health over staying 'in the loop'\n- Find alternative news sources (newsletters, podcasts, news aggregators)\n\n**Reframing mindset:**\n- Instead of 'I'm missing out on fun' → 'I'm choosing to focus on my own life'\n- Instead of 'Everyone else is having more fun' → Remember social media shows curated highlights\n- Instead of 'I need constant connection' → Build meaningful relationships through in-person interaction\n\n**Alternatives to stay connected:**\n- Phone calls and video calls\n- Text/messaging apps (WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage)\n- In-person meetups (coffee, walks, restaurants)\n- Email newsletters\n- Snail mail (letters, postcards, care packages)\n- Online forums/group chats around shared interests (not social media)\n- Volunteer work and community involvement\n- Group activities (game nights, potlucks, movie nights)
