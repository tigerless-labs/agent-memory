**Walmart** — blockchain for traceability of food products

**Coca-Cola** — blockchain to track production and delivery of bottling equipment

**IBM and Maersk** — developed TradeLens, a blockchain platform for global shipping (reduces paperwork and delays)

**Nestle** — IoT sensors to monitor product quality and safety during transport and storage

**Provenance** — blockchain platform for consumer product origin tracing
