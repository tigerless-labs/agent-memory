**Transparency and traceability** — tamper-proof, immutable records of transactions; detect fraud and errors

**Efficiency** — automation and digitization reduce manual errors, speed up processing, lower costs

**Real-time visibility** — track location, temperature, humidity, quality factors; identify bottlenecks and optimize processes

**Sustainability** — verify ethical and sustainable production; monitor energy and water usage to reduce waste and emissions

**Improved compliance and accountability** — enhance trust with customers and stakeholders
