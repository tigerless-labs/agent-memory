## Canvas Sneaker Cleaning (Black Converse Chuck Taylor All Stars)\n- Use soft-bristled brush or clean cloth to remove loose dirt\n- Mix mild soap (diluted dish soap) with warm water\n- Wipe shoes with damp cloth, wring out thoroughly\n- Use baking soda + water or commercial stain remover for tough stains\n- Apply rubber conditioner to toe cap to prevent cracking\n- Use waterproofing spray to protect and repel water\n- Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners\n- Air dry at room temperature, avoid direct sunlight/heat\n- Clean laces with mild soap and water\n\n## White Leather Sneaker Cleaning (Adidas Superstars)\n- Remove loose dirt with soft-bristled brush or clean cloth\n- Use gentle cleaning solution: 1 tbsp mild soap + 1 cup warm water, OR equal parts water + white vinegar\n- Dip clean cloth into solution, wring thoroughly\n- Wipe entire surface including tongue, laces, sole\n- Use gentle scrubbing for scuff marks (avoid too much pressure)\n- Rinse with clean damp cloth to remove soap residue\n- Blot shoes with dry cloth\n- Air dry at room temperature\n- Consider sneaker cleaning products like Sneaker Cleaner or Jason Markk for tough stains\n- Apply fabric protector or waterproofing spray to prevent future stains\n- Avoid harsh chemicals and rough cloths\n\n## Brown Leather Boot Care & Maintenance\n- Regular conditioning with high-quality leather conditioner or oil to keep soft and supple\n- Apply waterproofing spray or cream (especially if rainy/snowy climate)\n- Clean regularly with soft dry cloth to remove dirt and debris\n- Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight\n- Use shoe trees or stuff with paper to maintain shape\n- Avoid extreme temperatures (near heater or freezing cold)\n- Handle with care to avoid scratches, scuffs, tears\n- Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive cleaners\n- Use leather brush for gentle dirt removal\n- Apply leather protector to high-wear areas (toes, heels)\n- Don't over-condition (can leave greasy residue)\n- Consider professional maintenance (polishing, conditioning) from cobbler\n\n## Finding a Cobbler\n- Google search: "cobblers near me" or "shoe repair near me" with city/zip code\n- Check online directories: Yelp, Google Maps, Bing Places\n- Ask friends, family, colleagues for recommendations\n- Ask local shoe store associates for recommendations\n- Check National Shoe Repair Association (NSRA) or Shoe Service Institute of America (SSIA) member directories\n- Check social media: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram for local cobblers\n- Review sites: Angie's List, Thumbtack, TaskRabbit\n- When evaluating: check reputation/reviews, experience with similar repairs, get quote, check turnaround time
