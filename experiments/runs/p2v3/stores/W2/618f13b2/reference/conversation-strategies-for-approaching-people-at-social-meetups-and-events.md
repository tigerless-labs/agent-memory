Strategies for making conversations at meetups more comfortable:\n\n**Opening**\n- Start with friendly greeting: 'hello' or 'hi, how's it going?'\n- Avoid diving into deep conversations right away\n- Example starters: 'What brings you to this event?', 'How did you get into [shared interest]?'\n\n**During conversation**\n- Listen actively and show genuine interest\n- Ask open-ended questions\n- Find common ground and shared interests\n- Be yourself—authenticity matters\n- Avoid dominating the conversation\n- Use positive body language (eye contact, smile, open posture)\n- Keep initial conversations brief, build gradually\n\n**Follow-up**\n- Exchange contact info if it feels natural\n- Follow up to show genuine interest in continuing\n\n**What not to do**\n- Don't try to be someone you're not\n- Don't monopolize conversation\n- Don't be pushy—pay attention to body language and verbal cues\n- It's okay to politely excuse yourself if someone seems uninterested
