Shopping pattern: Shops more on weekends when free time is available.

Spending concern: Noticed online shopping habits adding up and spending too much money.

Decision: Plans to cut down on online shopping.

Strategies:
1. Track expenses more closely
2. Set a personal budget
3. Implement "one in, one out" policy—get rid of an old item every time bringing a new one home. This maintains mindful consumption and prevents clutter.
