User decided to sell multiple vintage and rare items. Strategy includes:

**Platform recommendations by item type:**
- Watches: eBay (Authenticity Guarantee program), Chrono24, 1stdibs, specialized auction houses
- Guitars: Reverb, eBay, The Gear Page, TDPRI forums, Vintage Guitar Exchange
- Comics: ComicConnect, eBay, Comic Book Resources, local comic stores
- Action figures/toys: eBay, Action Figure Insider, The Fwoosh, ToyArk
- Electronics (Walkman): eBay, Gearslutz, vintage audio forums, Facebook Marketplace
- Currency: Professional Numismatists Guild, International Association of Paper Money Dealers

**Key approach:**
1. Get professional appraisals and certifications from relevant authorities (Omega, CGC for comics, etc.)
2. Use high-quality photography to showcase items
3. Provide detailed descriptions with documentation/provenance
4. Research competitive pricing before listing
5. Be prepared to answer buyer questions and negotiate
