Recommended models: NZXT Kraken X73 (360mm, 3 fans), Corsair Hydro H115i RGB Platinum (280mm), EKWB EK-AIO 360 (360mm, 3 fans), Thermaltake Floe Riing 360 (360mm, 3 fans), Noctua NH-U14S air cooler alternative. All compatible with Ryzen 9 5900X socket. Considerations include radiator size, noise level, RGB options, and warranty.
