## Specialized selling platforms by category

**Watch marketplaces:**
- Chrono24 (large watch collector community)
- 1stdibs (high-end luxury items marketplace)
- The RealReal (consignment model for luxury items)
- Specialized watch forums: WatchUseek, Timekeeper's Forums, Omega Forums

**Musical instruments:**
- Reverb (popular for new, used, and vintage instruments)
- The Gear Page (guitar enthusiasts)
- TDPRI (Dallas Pedal and Guitar Ranch Internet Forum)
- Vintage Guitar Exchange

**Comics and collectibles:**
- ComicConnect (specializes in comic book sales)
- Comic Book Resources
- Comic Vine

**Toys and action figures:**
- Action Figure Insider
- The Fwoosh
- ToyArk
- ToyCollector, FigureCollector, Vintage Toy Room

**General platforms:**
- eBay (categories: Vintage, Collectible, Authenticity Guarantee program)
- Facebook Marketplace (local buyers)

## Certification and grading services

- CGC (Certified Guaranty Company) - comics
- Wata Games or PSA - toys/action figures
- PNG (Professional Numismatists Guild) - currency
- IAPMD (International Association of Paper Money Dealers) - currency

## Authentication by manufacturer

Omega offers: authentication, certificate of origin with production date/serial, condition reports, and restoration services using original parts.
