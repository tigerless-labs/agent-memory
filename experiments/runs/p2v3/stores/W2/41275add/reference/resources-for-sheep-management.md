Key information sources for sheep farming:

- National Sheep Improvement Program (NSIP)
- American Sheep Industry Association (ASI)
- Sheep 101 online resource
- Local university extension services

Topics researched: vaccination protocols, feed and nutrition, cost management, health care. Veterinarian consultation recommended for herd-specific plans.
