**Online retailers:** Amazon, Tractor Supply Co., Meyer Hatchery, ChickensForBackyards

**Brands:** Organic Valley, Blue Seal, Modesto Milling, Scratch and Peck Feeds

**Tips:** Check for certifications (USDA Organic, Non-GMO, Certified Humane), compare prices, consider bulk orders, transition chickens gradually to new feed.
