OBS Studio: Free, open source, highly customizable with large community and plugins. XSplit: User friendly, subscription based, includes 4K recording and multi streaming. ShadowPlay: Free for NVIDIA users, lightweight, instant replay up to 30 mins at 1440p or 60 mins at 1080p. Fraps: Lightweight classic option. Bandicam: Free trial, paid features include 4K recording. Camtasia: Professional option with editing tools.
