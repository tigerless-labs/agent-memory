## Meditation Apps\n- **Headspace** — Guided meditation app with structured programs\n- **Calm** — Meditation and sleep app with various programs\n\n## Beginner Meditation Methods\n1. **Mindfulness Meditation** — Focus on present moment without judgment; sit quietly and observe breath, thoughts, emotions; refocus when distracted\n2. **Guided Meditation** — Listen to recorded meditations; helpful for maintaining focus\n3. **Loving Kindness Meditation** — Cultivate compassion by visualizing someone you love, silently repeating kindness phrases ("may you be happy, may you be healthy, may you be safe, may you be at peace")\n\n## Getting Started\n- Start with 5-10 minutes for beginners\n- Gradually increase duration as comfortable\n- Consistency matters more than duration/frequency\n- Regular practice helps internalize focus and calm skills into daily life
