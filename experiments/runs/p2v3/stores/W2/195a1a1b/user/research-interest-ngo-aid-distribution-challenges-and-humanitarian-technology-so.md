User researches challenges NGOs face when distributing aid in Africa, with focus on accountability systems and technology solutions.

## Key challenges explored
- Infrastructure gaps (roads, bridges, ports for remote delivery)
- Political instability and conflict barriers
- Corruption and resource diversion
- Cultural/language communication gaps
- Limited resources (funding, personnel, equipment)
- High operational costs in poorly-developed areas
- Lack of accountability systems:
  - No transparent tracking of aid
  - Difficulty demonstrating impact to donors
  - Hard to identify/address distribution issues
  - Lack of flexibility to adapt to changing conditions

## Solutions interest
- Examined Genius Voucher as example of digital solution for aid distribution
- Interested in how technology creates transparency and prevents corruption
- Focused on solutions that work in low-connectivity/remote environments
