Curious about Granada's historic landmarks with city views (particularly the Alhambra), its Moorish history, and the legends and myths surrounding it. This connects to broader interest in historical narratives and mythology. Impressed by the Alhambra's history (13th-14th century Nasrid dynasty fortress/palace, named from Arabic 'Al-Hamra' meaning 'the red one', UNESCO World Heritage Site, underwent Christian modifications after 1492 conquest). Interested in exploring local legends including the Fountain of Lions, Tower of the Seven Floors, Hall of Abencerrajes, and Devil's Megaphone at Generalife palace.
