American photographer born into prominent Virginia family. One of the first women to establish a professional photography career in the United States.

**Career:**
- Opened studio in Washington, D.C. in 1894
- Established reputation as one of city's most talented and innovative photographers
- Known for portraits of prominent figures: Theodore Roosevelt, Mark Twain, Susan B. Anthony, Alice Roosevelt Longworth

**Key contributions:**
- Pioneer in architectural photography - recognized importance of documenting America's historic buildings
- Traveled extensively photographing colonial-era buildings and historic structures
- Helped raise awareness of preserving America's architectural heritage

**Style & legacy:**
- Portraits characterized by elegance, simplicity, attention to detail
- Believed photography could be tool for social change
- Celebrated for technical excellence, artistic innovation, enduring influence on American photography
