American photographer and pioneer of modernist and straight photography. Born New York City, largely self-taught. Influenced by Alfred Stieglitz; joined Photo-Secession group in 1915.

**Key work:**
- Documentary photography focusing on everyday life and social issues
- Famous series: photographs of Mexico (began 1932), considered important examples of documentary photography
- Traveled extensively photographing rural communities, urban landscapes, industrial sites

**Style & legacy:**
- Simple, direct photography characterized by social purpose
- Believed photography could be a tool for social change to raise awareness of injustices
- Had profound influence on development of documentary photography
- Work celebrated for innovative style, social relevance, enduring influence on modern photography
