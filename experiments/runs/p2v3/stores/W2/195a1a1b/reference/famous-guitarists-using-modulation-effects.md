Key examples of guitarists known for modulation effects:

- **David Gilmour** (Pink Floyd): chorus and phaser on 'Comfortably Numb' and 'Shine On You Crazy Diamond'
- **Eddie Van Halen**: flanger and phaser for his signature 'brown sound' tone
- **The Edge** (U2): delay and chorus for ethereal guitar sound
- **Andy Summers** (The Police): chorus and flanger for complex, layered guitar parts
- **Jimi Hendrix**: uni-vibe pedal for phaser-like effect on 'Machine Gun' and 'Star Spangled Banner'
