American photographer born in New York City. Studied at Art Students League of New York.

**Career trajectory:**
- Started as portrait photographer
- 1890s: began experimenting with aerial photography using camera mounted on balloons
- Early 1900s: pioneer in using airplanes as platform for photography
- Worked with pioneering aviators including Orville Wright

**Key innovations:**
- Aerial photography: captured images from high above, revealing landscape patterns and textures from unique perspective
- Nighttime photography: experimented with low-light photography techniques unusual for his era
- Used artificial light sources: electric lights, gas lamps, torches, flash powder, magnesium wire for nighttime work
- Nighttime photographs characterized by dramatic, moody, atmospheric effects; sharp light/dark contrasts

**Legacy:**
- Helped establish aerial photography as new and important genre
- Aerial photographs recognized as important historical documents and works of art
- Work continues to inspire photographers exploring nighttime and aerial techniques
