Town on the Outer Banks of North Carolina, United States.

**Historical significance:**
- Site of Wright brothers' first powered flight: December 17, 1903
- Orville and Wilbur Wright chose location for consistent winds and sandy terrain
- Wright Brothers National Memorial located in town to commemorate achievement

**Attractions & activities:**
- Beautiful beaches: swimming, sunbathing, water sports
- Kitty Hawk Woods Coastal Reserve: nature preserve with walking trails, birdwatching, kayaking
- Watersports: surfing, kayaking, paddleboarding, fishing
- Golf courses: several in and around town
- Outer Banks history: chain of barrier islands with rich cultural heritage; historic lighthouses, museums
- Dining and shopping: local cuisine, seafood, souvenirs

**Character:**
- Known for small-town charm
- Part of broader Outer Banks region with significant historical and cultural heritage
