## Landmarks with Best City Views

**The Alhambra** — Ancient fortress and palace complex from the Moorish reign (13th-14th centuries). Offers breathtaking views of the city and Sierra Nevada Mountains. Named from Arabic 'Al-Hamra' ('the red one') for its reddish walls. Built by the Nasrid dynasty as both fortress and royal palace. After Christian conquest in 1492, modified as royal residence by Catholic monarchs. Currently a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

**Mirador de San Nicolas** — Viewpoint on Albaicín hill with panoramic views of the city, the Alhambra, and Sierra Nevada Mountains.

**Sacromonte Abbey** — Monastery on a hilltop offering panoramic views of Granada and surroundings.

**El Salvador Church** — Bell tower provides bird's eye view of Granada's city center.

**Albaicín Quarter** — Labyrinth of narrow alleys and whitewashed buildings with various vantage points for city views.

_Recommendation: The Alhambra is considered the must-visit landmark and top choice for views._
