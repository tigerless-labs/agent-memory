## Alhambra Legends & Myths

**The Fountain of Lions** — Legend says drinking from this fountain in the Palace of the Lions will make you fall in love with Granada and never want to leave.

**The Tower of the Seven Floors** — Said to be haunted by the ghost of a princess imprisoned there by her father due to forbidden love with a Christian knight.

**The Hall of Abencerrajes** — Believed to be the site of a massacre of the Abencerraje family. Bloodstains allegedly visible on the walls.

**The Devil's Megaphone** — A spot in the Generalife palace gardens with perfect acoustics; legend says the devil can be heard speaking through the echo.

_Note: These legends are significant to the cultural and historical heritage of Granada, though their historical accuracy varies._
