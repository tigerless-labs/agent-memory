## Recommended Meditation Apps for Sleep & Relaxation\n\n**Paid/Freemium:**\n- **Headspace**: Guided meditations for sleep, stress, focus (free trial, then subscription)\n- **Calm**: Meditations, sleep stories, relaxing music (free trial, then subscription)\n- **Meditation Studio**: Sleep and relaxation meditations (free with in-app purchases)\n- **Buddhify**: Meditations for different times of day including bedtime (one-time purchase)\n\n**Free:**\n- **Insight Timer**: Vast library including sleep and relaxation (free)\n\n**Online Resources:**\n- YouTube: Andrew Weil guided meditations\n- Rainy Mood: Guided meditations + relaxing soundscapes\n- Sleep With Me: Podcast with sleep meditations\n- Meditation Oasis: Free meditations\n- UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center: Free meditations\n\n**Tips:** Start with 5-10 min sessions; use headphones; find a quiet space; practice consistently at same time; experiment to find what resonates.
