## Avoiding Allergen Exposure
- Check pollen counts online and avoid outdoor activities on high pollen days
- Keep windows and doors closed during pollen season
- Wash hair and clothes when coming indoors
- Shower to reduce pollen exposure

## Indoor Air Quality
- Use air filters in heating/AC systems
- Use a portable air purifier

## Nasal Care
- Use saline nasal rinses or sprays to clear allergens from nasal passages

## Diet & Hydration
- Eat anti-inflammatory foods: omega-3s, fruits, vegetables
- Drink plenty of water to reduce skin irritation and clear nasal passages

## Note
These remedies may work for some people but are not guaranteed replacements for medical treatment. Always consult a doctor before trying new remedies.
