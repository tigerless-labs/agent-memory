Prefers to start listening to audiobooks at normal or slightly faster speed (1x or 1.25x) and adjusts based on comfort. For non-fiction books like self-improvement and productivity titles, willing to pause and take notes to help retain and apply concepts. Not interested in rushing through non-fiction at maximum speed — prioritizes comprehension and reflection.
