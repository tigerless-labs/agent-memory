## Audiobook recommendations provided (May 2023)

**To listen to soon:**
- The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers (narrated by Rachel Dulude) — character-driven sci-fi about diverse crew of a tunneling ship
- The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People by Stephen Covey (narrated by Stephen Covey) — classic self-help
- Atomic Habits by James Clear (narrated by James Clear) — building good habits

**Other fantasy/sci-fi recommendations:**
- The Lies of Locke Lamora by Scott Lynch (narrated by Simon Vance) — fantasy con artists
- The Goblin Emperor by Katherine Addison (narrated by Kyle McCarley)
- The House of Shattered Wings by Aliette de Bodard (narrated by Anne Flosnik)
- The Golem and the Jinni by Helene Wecker (narrated by George Guidall)
- The City of Brass by S.A. Chakraborty (narrated by Soneela Nankani)
- The Black Tides of Heaven by J.Y. Yang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller)
- The Poppy War by R.F. Kuang (narrated by Emily Woo Zeller)

**Other non-fiction recommendations:**
- Daring Greatly by Brené Brown
- Mindset by Carol S. Dweck
- Deep Work by Cal Newport
- Essentialism by Greg McKeown
- Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari
- The Power of Now by Eckhart Tolle
- Shoe Dog by Phil Knight
