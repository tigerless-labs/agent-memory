Methods and tools for tracking multiple loyalty programs: spreadsheet templates (Google Sheets, Excel), digital note-taking apps (Evernote, OneNote, Simplenote), physical loyalty card trackers, loyalty program aggregators (AwardWallet, LoyaltyLion, Stocard), phone reminders, habit-tracking apps (Habitica, HabitBull, Loop Habit Tracker), Google Alerts for specific stores, and loyalty program forums/communities. Staying informed: newsletters/email updates, social media following, mobile apps with push notifications, text message opt-ins, regular website checks, in-store signage, and asking employees about promotions.
