Methods to monitor and understand interest rate changes:

1. **Financial news sources** — Follow financial news websites, broadcasts, and podcasts for timely reporting of Fed decisions
2. **Bank/financial institution contact** — Call or visit your bank directly for information about how changes affect your specific accounts or loans
3. **Mortgage rate monitoring** — Track mortgage rates online (relevant when buying or managing existing mortgages)
4. **Financial advisor consultation** — Consider consulting a financial advisor for personalized guidance on how interest rate changes affect your financial situation

**FOMC meeting schedule:** The Federal Open Market Committee meets 8 times per year to review economic data (GDP, inflation, unemployment, consumer spending) and decide on interest rate adjustments.
