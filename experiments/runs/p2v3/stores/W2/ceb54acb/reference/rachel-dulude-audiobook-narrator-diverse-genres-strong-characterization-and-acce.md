**Rachel Dulude — Audiobook Narrator**

Growing audiobook narrator with strong following among audiobook enthusiasts. Known for:
- Versatility across multiple genres (sci-fi, fantasy, romance, mystery, non-fiction)
- Excellent characterization and distinct voices for different characters
- Strong accent work, particularly valuable for sci-fi/fantasy with diverse character origins
- Well-paced narration with good sense of tone and inflection for emotional moments vs action sequences
- Narrated The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet by Becky Chambers

Recommended as particularly well-suited to character-driven stories with humor and emotional depth.
