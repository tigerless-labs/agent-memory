Enjoys:
- Contemporary fiction with strong female protagonists and complex relationships (e.g., *The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo*)
- Graphic novels similar to *Saga* by Brian K. Vaughan
- Character-driven narratives exploring identity, intersectionality, female relationships
- Themes that resonate: public personas vs. private selves, societal expectations, power dynamics
- Appreciates narratives with unreliable narrators and layered storytelling
- Draws connections between fictional characters' experiences and universal human experiences (masks we all wear in different situations)
