Recommended platforms for facilitating interactive classroom discussion and reflection:\n\n- **Padlet**: Collaborative platform for students to share thoughts and ideas in a virtual space\n- **Nearpod**: Interactive platform with polls and open-ended questions for student participation\n- **Flipgrid**: Video discussion platform where students respond with short videos\n- **Google Jamboard**: Virtual whiteboard for collaborative visual idea-sharing\n\nEffective discussion facilitation methods: open-ended questions, paraphrasing, active listening, visual aids.
