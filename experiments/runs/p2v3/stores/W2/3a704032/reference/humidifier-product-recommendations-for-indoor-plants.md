**Recommended Humidifiers:**\n- **Levoit LV600HH Hybrid**: Both cool and warm mist, humidistat, 6-liter tank\n- **Honeywell HUL520W Mistmate**: Compact, 1-liter tank, easy to use\n- **Deneve Minimal Aroma**: Ultrasonic, 2.5-liter tank, built-in humidistat, adjustable mist\n- **Crane Drop Ultrasonic**: 1-liter tank, whisper-quiet operation, easy cleaning\n\nFor African violets: need low-noise models placed 1-2 feet away; maintain 50-60% humidity; ensure good air circulation to prevent fungal disease.\n\nFor ferns and other humidity lovers: place within 3-5 feet; monitor with hygrometer.
