## Contemporary Fiction with Strong Female Protagonists
- The Devil Wears Prada by Lauren Weisberger
- The Mothers by Brit Bennett
- The Girls by Emma Cline
- The Interestings by Meg Wolitzer
- Conversations with Friends by Sally Rooney
- The Female Persuasion by Meg Wolitzer
- The Last Romantics by Tara Conklin
- The Age of Light by Whitney Scharer (based on Lee Miller's life)

## Graphic Novels Similar to Saga by Brian K. Vaughan
- Monstress by Marjorie Liu and Sana Takeda
- The Wicked + The Divine by Kieron Gillen and Jamie McKelvie
- Black Hammer by Jeff Lemire and Dean Ormston
- The Umbrella Academy by Gerard Way and Gabriel Bá
- Descender by Jeff Lemire and Dustin Nguyen
- The Goddamned by Jason Aaron and R.M. Guéra
- Y: The Last Man by Brian K. Vaughan and Pia Guerra
