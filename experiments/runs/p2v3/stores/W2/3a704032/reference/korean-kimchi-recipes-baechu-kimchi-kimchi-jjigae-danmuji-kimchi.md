**Classic Baechu Kimchi**: Traditional recipe using napa cabbage, gochugaru, garlic, ginger, and fish sauce

**Kimchi Stew (Kimchi Jjigae)**: Spicy, savory stew made with gochugaru, kimchi, pork, tofu, and vegetables

**Radish Kimchi (Danmuji Kimchi)**: Refreshing recipe using daikon radish, gochugaru, garlic, and ginger

These recipes all use gochugaru as the base chili ingredient, adjustable to taste preference.
