**Recommended Potting Mixes:**\n- Miracle-Gro Indoor Potting Mix (general purpose)\n- Black Kow All Purpose Potting Mix (high-quality organic)\n- E.B. Stone Organics Potting Mix (nutrient-rich, eco-friendly)\n- Pro-Mix All Purpose Potting Mix (professional-grade)\n\nFor spider plants specifically: choose well-draining mix, repot into pot only 1-2 sizes larger (they prefer to be slightly root-bound), water thoroughly after repotting but keep soil consistently moist without waterlogging.
