Comprehensive list of platforms recommended for discovering contemporary art, emerging artists, and their work:

**Platforms featuring emerging artists:**
- Artsy — online platform with vast collection from emerging and established artists, dedicated emerging artist section, filterable by medium/style
- Saatchi Art — online gallery focused on emerging artists
- Artspace — contemporary art platform with curated artworks, artist profiles, exhibitions; direct purchase available
- Uprise Art — focused on emerging artists, offers curated selection, artist profiles, exhibitions; direct purchase available
- Behance — platform for creatives (artists, designers, illustrators) to showcase work; searchable by medium/style/location

**Art publications and blogs:**
- Juxtapoz — online art magazine featuring emerging and established contemporary artists
- Hyperallergic — online art blog covering news, exhibitions, artist profiles
- New American Paintings — platform featuring emerging painters from US, bi-monthly magazine

**Broader platforms:**
- ArtNet — features news, exhibitions, artist profiles with emerging artist section
- The Artist's Institute — online platform with emerging artist exhibitions, interviews, essays
- Platform — online platform showcasing emerging artists worldwide with curated artworks and exhibitions
- Instagram — many emerging artists showcase work; search hashtags like #emergingartist, #artoftheday, #contemporaryart
- 1stdibs — online marketplace featuring curated contemporary art, design, antiques from galleries and dealers
- Minted — curated contemporary art and design from emerging artists and designers
- Collecteurs — online platform focused on contemporary art and emerging artists
