On 2023-05-21, attempted first kimchi batch but added too much chili flakes and salt, making it inedible. Planning to rescue the batch by:
- Rinsing under cold running water to remove excess salt
- Re-seasoning with measured amounts of gochugaru, garlic, and ginger

**Chosen measurements:**
- **Gochugaru**: 1/2 to 1 tsp per 2 cups vegetables (medium heat)
- **Garlic**: 2-3 cloves per 2 cups vegetables
- **Ginger**: 1-2 inches grated per 2 cups vegetables

Key lesson: measure by weight when possible, taste as you go, start small with spice (can always add more, can't easily remove).
