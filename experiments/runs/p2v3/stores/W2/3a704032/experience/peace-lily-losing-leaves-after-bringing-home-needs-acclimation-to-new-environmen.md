Peace lily started dropping leaves after purchase. Advised to give it time to acclimate (2-3 weeks), ensure bright indirect light, check soil moisture before watering (finger test to first knuckle), maintain 50%+ humidity, keep at 65-80°F, avoid direct sunlight and temperature extremes.
