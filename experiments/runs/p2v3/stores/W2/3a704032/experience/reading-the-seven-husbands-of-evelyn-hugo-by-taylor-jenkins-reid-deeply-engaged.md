Started reading January 29, 2023. By May 2023, highly engaged with the novel.

## What resonated:
- Character development and interconnected storylines
- Evelyn's backstory as Cuban immigrant and how it shaped her
- Mystery of her seven husbands
- Monique as an active, non-passive narrator with her own biases
- Themes of public persona vs. private self (performing for public while vulnerable underneath)
- Relatability: the universal experience of wearing different masks/personas in different situations
- Intersectionality of Evelyn's identity (woman, Latina, immigrant) and how it shaped her experiences in Hollywood
- Complex, multifaceted relationships between women—simultaneously supportive and competitive, loving and complicated
- How female relationships are sources of both strength and pain
- Portrayal of power dynamics and privilege (celebrity, gender, race)

## Character appreciation:
- Found Monique an excellent narrator choice
- Appreciated how Monique's own life and biases influenced her perception of Evelyn
- Liked how Taylor Jenkins Reid balanced glamour and escapism with serious emotional themes
- Noted intersectionality in how women of color relationships were portrayed as sources of solidarity and tension
