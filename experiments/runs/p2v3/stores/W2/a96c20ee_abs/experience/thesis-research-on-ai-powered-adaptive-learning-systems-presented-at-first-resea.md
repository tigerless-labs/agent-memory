Researched effectiveness of AI-powered adaptive learning systems in improving student learning outcomes in math and reading comprehension. 

Key findings: Systems can effectively improve outcomes; implementation and classroom integration are critical. Potential to reduce achievement gaps and promote equity, but concerns about algorithmic bias. 

Common challenges: Teacher training gaps, need for curriculum alignment, role of teachers (replacement vs support). Recommendations: Prioritize teacher professional development, developer-educator collaboration on design, clear curriculum guidance, ongoing evaluation. 

Presented research poster at first conference (summer 2022). Received positive feedback from educators and policymakers; some developers reached out interested in findings.
