Experiencing rapid battery drain, especially with social media apps (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) and video watching. Tried closing background apps and adjusting screen brightness without significant improvement. Troubleshooting plan: disable location services, use app data restriction, try lite versions, enable auto-brightness, disable Bluetooth/GPS when not in use, consider power saving mode, check battery health. Opted to try troubleshooting steps before factory reset.
