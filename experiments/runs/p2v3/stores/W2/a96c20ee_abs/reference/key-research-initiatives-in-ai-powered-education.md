Harvard Project Zero explores AI and machine learning for teaching. NSF Cyberlearning and Future Learning Technologies program focuses on innovative STEM technologies. Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation supports personalized learning platforms and AI tools. Meta-analysis of 22 studies: adaptive learning systems effect size 0.34 (Raca et al. 2018). Real-time feedback improves accuracy, motivation, metacognition. Challenges: data quality, teacher adoption, equity gaps, transparency.
