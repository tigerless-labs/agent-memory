Inspiration: Blue cheese and dark chocolate truffle from local chocolate shop. Refined through iterations to balance flavors. Recipe yields crisp exterior, chewy center cookies. Key ingredients: 2.25 cups flour, 1 cup butter, 1 cup sugar, 0.5 cup crumbled blue cheese, 2 eggs, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips. Bake at 375F for 12-15 min. Pairs well with red wine or dark beer.
