User expressed interest in DJing after learning about it. DJing involves mixing and manipulating music tracks using equipment like turntables, CD players, or digital controllers.

**Getting started steps:**
1. Learn the basics (beatmatching, mixing tracks, equipment use) via online tutorials
2. Invest in equipment (DJ controller or turntables + software)
3. Practice regularly at home and at local events
4. Connect with other DJs through communities and events
5. Share music by recording and uploading mixes online

DJing is a rewarding hobby that takes time and practice to master.
