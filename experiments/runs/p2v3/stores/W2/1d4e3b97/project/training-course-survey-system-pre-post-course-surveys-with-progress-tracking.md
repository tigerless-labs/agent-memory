## Original Requirement
Trainees can:
- Answer survey once at beginning of course
- Answer same survey once at end of course
- See their own progress (survey results comparison)
- See entire class survey results

Instructors can:
- Collect survey responses at beginning and end
- View individual trainee results
- View aggregate class results

## 8 User Stories Generated
1. Trainee - access beginning survey for initial feedback
2. Trainee - access end survey for final feedback
3. Trainee - view personal progress over course duration
4. Trainee - compare pre/post results to see attitude/opinion changes
5. Trainee - view class results to compare with peers and see group performance
6. Instructor - collect responses from both survey points to track progress and evaluate training
7. Instructor - view individual trainee results for personalized feedback
8. Instructor - view class results to evaluate program effectiveness
