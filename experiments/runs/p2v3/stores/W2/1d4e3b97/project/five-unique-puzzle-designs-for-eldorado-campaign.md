## Puzzle Designs

1. **Mirror Puzzle**: Large mirror with etched symbols. Players see their reversed reflection and must position themselves and symbols correctly to reveal a hidden passage.

2. **Elemental Puzzle**: Series of levers and buttons controlling earth, fire, air, and water forces. Players must discover the correct sequence (e.g., air lever + water button = mist revealing hidden path) to progress.

3. **Musical Puzzle**: Room filled with instruments and sheet music with symbols corresponding to different instruments. Players must play the correct sequence (e.g., piano → drum → trumpet) to unlock a door.

4. **Weight Puzzle**: Series of weights (different sizes/shapes) and scales with marked weights. Players must weigh items and arrange them in specific order on scales to reveal hidden passage.

5. **Memory Puzzle**: Objects/items scattered in a room. Players memorize locations before lights go out, then navigate darkness to retrieve a specific object and bring it to a designated spot to unlock passage.
