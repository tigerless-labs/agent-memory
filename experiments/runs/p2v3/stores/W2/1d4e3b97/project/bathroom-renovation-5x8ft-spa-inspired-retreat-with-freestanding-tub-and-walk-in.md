Bathroom renovation project planned for 5x8 foot space in Spa-Inspired Retreat style. Keeping existing tub and adding freestanding tub, large walk-in shower, and double sink vanity for spacious, relaxing aesthetic.

Materials chosen: glazed porcelain tile for shower walls and floor (selected for durability, water-resistance, glossy finish, easy cleaning, scratch and stain protection); cement-based grout (chosen as affordable, all-purpose option, will apply sealer).

Estimated material costs: freestanding tub $1000-3000, pedestal $100-500, tub filler $200-1000, shower pan $500-2000, shower door $500-2000, showerhead/handheld $100-500, linear drain $100-500.

Approach: user will handle tile and grout installation themselves; will hire professionals for plumbing and electrical work.

Key considerations: waterproof membrane on shower surfaces, grout sealer application, verify floor weight capacity for tub, plan adequate ventilation and moisture control, check local building codes before starting.

Status: in planning phase with material selections finalized.
