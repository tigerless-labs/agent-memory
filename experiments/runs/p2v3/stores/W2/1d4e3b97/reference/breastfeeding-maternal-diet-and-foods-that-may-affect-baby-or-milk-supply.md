**Diet impact on breastfeeding:**
- Diet usually not a direct cause of low milk supply but can affect overall health and nutrition
- Breastfeeding mothers should eat a balanced diet with adequate nutrients

**Common food triggers (not all babies are sensitive):**
- Dairy
- Soy
- Wheat
- Nuts
- Caffeine

**Recommended diet components:**
- Plenty of fruits and vegetables
- Whole grains
- Protein sources: meats, poultry, fish, nuts, legumes
- Healthy fats: avocados, nuts, seeds
- Adequate hydration: water, milk, herbal teas

**Note:** Not all babies react to the same foods; individual responses vary. Breastfeeding mothers can usually eat a wide variety of foods without issue. If concerned about diet or baby's reactions, consult lactation consultant or healthcare provider.
