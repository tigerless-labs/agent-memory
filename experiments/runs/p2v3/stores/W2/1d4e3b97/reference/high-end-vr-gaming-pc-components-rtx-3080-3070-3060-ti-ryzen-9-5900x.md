**GPU options:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, RTX 3070, RTX 3060 Ti; AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT, RX 6800

**CPU options:** AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, Intel Core i7-10700K, Intel Core i7-11700K

**Memory:** 16GB+ DDR4 RAM (high clock speeds, low latency)

**Storage:** Samsung 970 EVO or WD Black SN850 (M.2 SSD)

**PSU:** EVGA SuperNOVA 750W or Corsair RM750X (750W minimum)

**Motherboard examples:** ASUS ROG Strix B550-F Gaming, MSI MPG B550 Gaming Edge WiFi, Gigabyte AORUS Pro AX
