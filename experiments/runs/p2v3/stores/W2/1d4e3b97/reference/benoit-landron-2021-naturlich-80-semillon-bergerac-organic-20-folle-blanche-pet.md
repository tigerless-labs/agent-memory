Benoit Landron's 2021 Naturlich pet-nat is made 80% Semillon from an organic grower in Bergerac and 20% Folle Blanche. The blend shift from previous vintages was due to terrible frost damage in 2021 that destroyed most of the Folle Blanche crop. The Semillon adds fleshy stone-fruit notes. 161 is opening it by the glass this weekend for spring.
