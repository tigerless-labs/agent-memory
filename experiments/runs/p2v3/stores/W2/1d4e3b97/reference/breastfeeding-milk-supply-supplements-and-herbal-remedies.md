Supplements and herbs discussed for increasing milk supply in new mothers:

- **Fenugreek**: herb, traditional use for boosting milk production, available in capsule or tea
- **Blessed thistle**: herb, traditionally used to increase milk supply, capsule or tea form
- **Milk thistle**: herb, supports liver function and may help increase milk supply
- **Brewer's yeast**: contains B vitamins and minerals for milk production, capsule or powder
- **Fennel**: herb traditionally used to increase milk production, tea or supplement form

**Important notes:**
- Consult healthcare provider or lactation consultant before taking supplements
- Check for interactions with other medications
- Supplements are not a substitute for frequent and effective breastfeeding or pumping
