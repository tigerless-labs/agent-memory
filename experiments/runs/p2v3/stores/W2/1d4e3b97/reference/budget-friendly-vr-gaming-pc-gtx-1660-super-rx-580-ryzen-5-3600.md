**GPU options:** NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super or AMD Radeon RX 580

**CPU options:** AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or Intel Core i5-10600K

**Memory:** 8GB DDR4 RAM (high clock speeds, low latency)

**Motherboard examples:** MSI B450M-A PRO MAX, ASRock B450M-HDV R4.0, Gigabyte GA-A320M-H

**Storage:** 500GB SSD — WD Blue SN550, Kingston A400, or SanDisk Ultra 3D

**PSU:** EVGA 500W 80+ Bronze or Corsair CX Series 650W
