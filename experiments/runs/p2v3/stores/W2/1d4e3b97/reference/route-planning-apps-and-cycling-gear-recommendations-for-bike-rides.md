## Route Planning Apps for Bike-Friendly Roads\n- Ride with GPS: Custom routes, turn-by-turn directions (Web, iOS, Android)\n- MapMyRide: Route planning, GPS tracking, performance analysis (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Strava: Social network, route browsing, performance tracking (Web, iOS, Android)\n- Google Maps: Bicycling mode for bike lanes and paths (Web, iOS, Android)\n\n## Bike Helmet Brands and Models\n- Bell: High-quality with MIPS technology (Zephyr model recommended ~$150-200)\n- Specialized: Tri-Fix web splitter, various models (Tactic 3.0, Evade ~$150-250)\n- Giro: MIPS technology, Roc Loc Air fit system (Synthe model ~$150-200)\n- Trek: In-Mold construction, ventilation (Vapor 3 model ~$100-150)\n\nBudget ranges: Entry-level $30-50, Mid-range $50-100, High-end $100-200, Premium $200-300
