**How stress affects breastfeeding:**
- Stress/anxiety increases cortisol production
- Cortisol inhibits oxytocin release (hormone that triggers let-down reflex and milk production)
- Milk composition can change with stress: higher cortisol, lower lactose
- This may impact taste and nutritional content

**Important context:**
- Occasional stress is normal and unlikely to significantly impact milk supply or quality
- Ongoing or severe stress warrants professional support

**Stress management strategies:**
- Regular exercise
- Getting enough sleep
- Seeking support from family and friends
- Speaking with healthcare provider or counselor if experiencing ongoing/severe stress
