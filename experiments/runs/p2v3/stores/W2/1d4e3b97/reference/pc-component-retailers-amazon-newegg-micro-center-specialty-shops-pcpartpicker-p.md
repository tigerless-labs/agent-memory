**Major retailers:**
- Amazon.com
- Newegg.com
- Best Buy (Bestbuy.com)
- Micro Center (Microcenter.com) — physical stores + online ordering with in-store pickup available
- B&H Photo Video (B&hphotovideo.com)

**Specialty/niche shops:**
- **PCPartPicker.com** — component selector with price comparison across retailers
- **Superbiiz.com** — specializes in hard-to-find and discontinued items
- **Reddit.com/r/buildapcsales** — community for finding deals on components and accessories
- **Performance-PCs.com** — high-performance hardware, water cooling systems, modding parts
- **FrozenCPU.com** — custom PC building components, modding parts, hard-to-find items

*Note: Specialty shops may have limited availability, higher prices, or longer delivery times compared to major retailers. Always compare prices and check return policies/warranties before purchase.*
