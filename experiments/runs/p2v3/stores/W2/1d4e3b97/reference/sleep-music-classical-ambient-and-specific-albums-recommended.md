**Music genres for sleep:**\n- Classical (Mozart, Chopin, Bach)\n- Instrumental piano (slow, gentle)\n- Acoustic guitar (soft, slow)\n- Ambient music (soft textures, minimal melodies)\n- New age music (flutes, harps, gentle percussion)\n\n**Specific albums recommended:**\n- Brian Eno - Ambient 1: Music for Airports\n- Max Richter - Sleep (8-hour album designed for sleep)\n- Ludovico Einaudi - Nightbook\n- Enya - Watermark\n- Steve Reich - Music for 18 Musicians
