**Signs baby is getting enough milk:**
- Weight gain: 5-7 ounces per week
- Wet diapers: 6-8 per day
- Dirty diapers: 6-8 per week
- Feeding frequency: every 2-3 hours
- Behavior: appears content after feeding, falls asleep or relaxed body language

**Common causes of low milk supply:**
1. Infrequent breastfeeding/pumping
2. Poor latch (lactation consultant can assess and correct)
3. Stress and fatigue
4. Health conditions: hypothyroidism, PCOS, previous breast surgery
5. Certain medications

**Ways to increase supply:**
- Pump between feedings (in addition to, not instead of, breastfeeding)
- Increase nursing frequency
- Ensure proper latch technique
- Practice stress management and get adequate rest
- Stay well hydrated (aim for pale yellow urine)
- Eat balanced diet with adequate nutrition

**Recommendation:** Consult with lactation consultant, doctor, or healthcare professional if concerned about milk supply
