**Nature sounds recommended for sleep:**\n- Rainfall: gentle rain is very calming\n- Ocean waves: soothing sound promotes sleep\n- Forest sounds: birdsong, rustling leaves, gentle wind\n- Babbling brook: sound of small stream is calming\n- Wind chimes: softly ringing creates peaceful atmosphere
